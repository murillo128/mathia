# Cross-source synthesis over the frozen pilot

This synthesis uses only the 24 frozen units and their revised analyses. Unit identifiers are evidence links, not category labels for a permanent ontology. The commonalities below are claims about performed moves; the limits prevent distinct RH programs from being merged into one metaphor.

## 1. Representation changes are useful when their new obligations are explicit

Several sources move the problem into a representation with a different tractable operation:

- `riemann1859_u02_mellin_bridge` turns multiplicative local factors into a weighted additive count and makes analytic inversion available.
- `bombieri2000_u02_explicit_trace_dictionary` aligns an analytic explicit formula with an exact cohomological trace/fixed-point dictionary in the function-field realization.
- `conrey2003_u01_positivity_reframing` turns zero location into inequalities, but explicitly leaves the mechanism making those inequalities positive unexplained.
- `baezduarte2003_u02_summability_repair` distinguishes pointwise/L1 success from failure in the required Hilbert norm, then changes the summation path rather than the target.
- `rodgers2020_u01_heat_flow_boundary` embeds a binary statement into a monotone deformation and splits equality of a threshold into two directional obligations.

The recurring move is not “use another viewpoint.” It is: name the operation gained in the new representation, retain the exact equivalence/conditional direction, and state the new hard obligation. This reading rejects two superficially similar cases as positive evidence. `lagarias2002_u01_elementary_equivalence` has an accessible statement form but locates the hard work in a deeper theorem and even lacks the displayed criterion inside the frozen span. `aim2004_u01_equivalence_map` catalogues interfaces but performs no mechanism that makes one equivalent easier.

Candidate reusable mechanism: when changing representation, require a three-part record—what is preserved, what new operation becomes available, and where the unsolved burden moves. A future transfer test can hide one of those consequences and ask the model to predict it.

## 2. Strong analogies select and reject models; resemblance alone does not

The strongest bridge units constrain an analogue through matched structure and failure:

- `bombieri2000_u01_finite_field_analogy` matches characteristic roots, Frobenius, cohomology, rationality, and the functional equation, while leaving the classical transfer absent.
- `montgomery1973_u02_unitary_analogy` uses the proved transform range to distinguish unitary/Hermitian statistics from orthogonal and symplectic alternatives, then makes conjectural predictions outside that range.
- `keating2000_u02_scale_and_arithmetic_split` fixes matrix size by mean density and separates exact CUE calculations from conjectural zeta transfer and prime corrections; CUE-plus-primes versus CUE alone supplies an ablation on the stated moment data.
- `connes1999_u02_periodic_orbit_constraints` extracts prime-labelled orbit, period, instability, and symmetry constraints, but also retains the wrong sign and finite-repeat mismatch.

The counterexamples are essential. `conrey2003_u02_spectral_false_friend` shows that a striking eigenvalue/zero list can be a computational artifact. `connes1999_u01_absorption_spectrum` offers an author-motivated response to a sign anomaly, but local compatibility cannot substitute for the open global trace formula. None of the statistical or term-matching evidence constructs a Hilbert-Polya operator.

Candidate reusable mechanism: an analogy becomes training-worthy when it fixes a parameter or role correspondence, rules out a nearby alternative, and predicts an observation not used to choose it. Pure resemblance remains style data.

## 3. Indirect objects are often chosen to amplify a hidden signal or isolate a bottleneck

Different techniques use very different mathematical objects, yet a narrower shared design move is visible:

- `conrey1989_u01_longer_mollifier_synthesis` makes mollifier length the leverage point and assembles main-term and error estimates around it.
- `lagarias2002_u02_extremal_thin_set` focuses on a thin set of divisor-sum maximizers where prime-distribution fluctuations should be amplified; the same mechanism predicts why finite testing can miss sparse, huge exceptions.
- `montgomery1973_u01_conditional_fourier_probe` replaces the spacing distribution by a Fourier observable whose range of control is explicit.
- `rodgers2020_u02_equilibrium_contradiction` replaces a search for rare close-pair witnesses by a collective relaxation consequence under a negative deformation margin.
- `platt2021_u01_certified_completeness` separates zero witnesses from an independent completeness count, optimizing computation for the bounded theorem actually claimed.

These are not instances of one mathematical method. Their common structure is experimental: identify a hidden signal, choose an auxiliary object that amplifies or certifies it, then expose the parameter/error/completeness condition that limits the inference. `conrey1989_u02_variational_freedom` could have been another good instance, but formula OCR is too damaged to verify the objective and admissible family; it is retained as an extraction failure, not supporting evidence.

## 4. Failure localization is a mathematical move, not merely negative sentiment

Four units show distinct repairs after a route fails:

- `baezduarte2003_u02_summability_repair`: the natural approximation fails specifically in the target norm, motivating summability rather than abandonment of the closure representation.
- `conrey2003_u02_spectral_false_friend`: pseudo cusp forms invalidate the numerical coincidence, not every possible trace-formula program.
- `connes1999_u02_periodic_orbit_constraints`: time reversal, sign, and finite-repeat behavior reject standard candidates while retaining explicit constraints on any replacement.
- `aim2004_u02_refuted_sufficient_conditions`: the sufficient premises are false, which kills those implications as proof routes without refuting RH or the full surrounding analytic/Hilbert-space languages.

Candidate reusable mechanism: report failure as a tuple `(target, failed premise or interface, counter-observation, surviving structure)`. This is more specific and transferable than generic “learn from failure” prose.

## 5. Evidence becomes useful by preserving its exact reach

The pilot repeatedly separates a useful result from a stronger RH claim:

- `riemann1859_u01_conjecture_limit` retains the xi product while marking all-root reality as conjectural.
- `montgomery1973_u01_conditional_fourier_probe` keeps both the RH assumption and the alpha-below-one range visible.
- `conrey1989_u01_longer_mollifier_synthesis` is a major partial-result mechanism, not a path shown to reach every zero.
- `rodgers2020_u01_heat_flow_boundary` proves the lower-bound half of a boundary equality, not RH's upper-bound half.
- `platt2021_u02_finite_evidence_utility` distinguishes a proved finite corollary, immediate improvements, potential/future work, and a case with little expected gain.

This supports a modest fertility hypothesis: evaluate a mathematical artifact by the downstream statements, tests, or design choices it legitimately changes, not by rhetorical proximity to the open conjecture. Platt-Trudgian supplies the clearest source-grounded instance. The analogy to future Mathia evaluation is ours, not source mathematics, and has not been experimentally validated here.

## What the synthesis does not establish

- It does not show that analytic transforms, geometry, random matrices, noncommutative geometry, heat flow, and computation are one program.
- It does not validate any candidate intuition as true, novel, or useful to a formal prover.
- It does not demonstrate out-of-domain transfer or distinguish learned capability from teacher style by itself.
- It does not turn a catalogue of concepts into evidence that a model can perform the moves.
- It does not repair two material context defects: unreadable formulas in `conrey1989_u02_variational_freedom` and the omitted Problem E display in `lagarias2002_u01_elementary_equivalence`. The clipped final-contradiction context in `rodgers2020_u02_equilibrium_contradiction` is another narrower boundary limitation.

The capability-vs-style audit must therefore test whether the revised outputs retain these operational discriminations when polished phrases and category names are ignored.
