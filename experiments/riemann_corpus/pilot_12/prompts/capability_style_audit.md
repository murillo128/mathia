Audit the complete frozen 12-source pilot after Pass 4 and cross-source synthesis. Actively test the alternative hypothesis that `source -> Codex prose -> Mathia` mainly teaches a recognizable explanatory style rather than reusable mathematical capability.

Check faithfulness, non-paraphrase value, specificity, transfer content, representation sensitivity, uncertainty discipline, recurring teacher templates, unit-boundary sufficiency, source diversity, and retention of concrete Riemann mathematics. Ignore prose polish as positive evidence. Identify strong accepted cases, impressive-looking rejected cases, context failures, and downgraded analogies/generalizations. Assess both evidence for and evidence against the style-only hypothesis. Recommend exactly one of the five issue exit decisions, but do not edit the executor's final decision or synthesize new source claims.
