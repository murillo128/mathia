# Pass 2 — Mathia-directed reading

Read exactly one frozen semantic unit and the frozen Pass 1 response. Analyze the unit using the distinctions in `AGENTS.md` and `docs/CONCEPTS_DIMENSIONS_INTUITION.md`:

- concepts or semantic relations present;
- conceptual moves actually performed, not merely named;
- one provisional candidate intuition, if warranted;
- epistemic status (definition, theorem, conditional result, conjecture, heuristic, analogy, computation, failed route, or open gap);
- one falsifiable expectation or downstream discrimination the mechanism suggests;
- relevance to a future Mathia extraction method;
- risk that the extraction is explanation-style imitation rather than mathematical capability.

Do not turn the categories into a permanent ontology. Do not upgrade an equivalence, analogy, computation, or teacher interpretation into proof.
