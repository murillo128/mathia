# Behavioral discriminator adversarial audit

## Scope

This audit covers the immutable pilot-v0 snapshot, three versioned source-unit repairs, three behavioral-candidate rounds, the held-out transfer candidates, and three fresh isolated reviews. It treats reviewer decisions as adversarial curation evidence, not mathematical truth or downstream capability evidence.

The final accepted subset has nine in-Riemann tasks across nine sources and six out-of-Riemann transfer tasks. It is frozen as evaluation-only evidence, but the in-Riemann subset does not meet Checkpoint K's intended 12-task minimum.

The first round rejected polished tasks that could be solved through option length, lexical repetition, generic finite-versus-global logic, or direct restatement. It also caught unsupported source imports, an ambiguity about Fourier support, and a mathematically false tensor-power amplification claim.

Round two retained six RH mechanisms: local-factor repetition, reciprocal Frobenius coordinates, extremal small-prime factor shape, target-topology control, density-matched matrix size, and witness-versus-completeness certification. It rejected module and artifact prompts that merely presented one favorable prose profile, direct curve/formula copying, a Rodgers-Tao prompt that overclaimed the repaired span, and more generic implication logic.

The bounded third round rechecked those six rather than grandfathering them. It accepted three replacements: the spectral coordinate `rho(1-rho)` and its symmetry axis, the incomplete-Kloosterman bottleneck for mollifier length, and the asymptotic frequency scale separating Montgomery's diagonal spike from the linear term. It rejected five other replacements, leaving the RH count at nine.

## What survived

The accepted RH tasks require exact symbolic or structural operations with discrete answers:

- expand the logarithm of two local encodings and detect the missing repeat term;
- distinguish a root of `det(I-tF)` from an eigenvalue of `F`;
- allocate multiplicative factors using reciprocal marginal gains;
- change the topology that controls a closure claim;
- match spectral densities before and after unfolding;
- separate a zero witness from exhaustive certified counting;
- derive a symmetry axis from reality of a quadratic spectral coordinate;
- identify which analytic module enlarges mollifier range versus optimizes inside a fixed range;
- select a frequency scale on which a diagonal transform term vanishes or remains visible.

All nine have cosmetic pairs with the same answer and structural pairs with a different answer. None requires subjective prose scoring.

The six accepted transfer tasks cover quotient factorization, topology-dependent closure, witness versus exhaustive root counting, the normality boundary for spectral structure, translation invariance as the boundary for Fourier diagonalization, and compactness upgrading weak to norm convergence. The same six survived all three isolated reviews.

## What remained weak

The final RH rejects are useful negative evidence:

- `rh3_b10_orbit_mismatch_isolation` maps each named mismatch almost verbatim to one option;
- `rh3_b11_local_statistic_selection` leaks the local/global distinction and offers noncompetitive distractors;
- `rh3_b12_heat_boundary_bracket` is generic threshold bookkeeping after the theorem is supplied;
- `rh3_b13_positivity_extension_obligation` reduces to a density-plus-continuity checklist;
- `rh3_b14_admissible_counterexample_boundary` is literal intersection of the quantified hypothesis with an option.

The transfer rejects remain stable negative controls. The recurrence item advertises its generating-function answer, finite exhaustion is generic epistemic calibration, and the tensor-power key is false: in the full tensor power, mixed tensors leave the second-to-first singular-value ratio at the original ratio rather than exponentiating it.

## Source sufficiency and style conclusion

All v0 unit hashes and all three repaired v1 hashes matched their metadata during the final review. The repaired Rodgers-Tao span reaches the Montgomery-based contradiction, the Lagarias span includes Problem E, and the Conrey transcription contains the formula-level variational setup. Those repairs removed the source blockers identified by v0, but a source repair does not automatically create a discriminating prompt.

The repeated high rejection rate is evidence that the process did not equate polished Codex prose with acceptance. The surviving tasks omit the nine-field explanation template and use exact perturbation behavior. However, nine accepted RH tasks are insufficient to claim that the requested compact discriminator has been built. No learner behavior, training effect, or transfer uplift was measured here.
