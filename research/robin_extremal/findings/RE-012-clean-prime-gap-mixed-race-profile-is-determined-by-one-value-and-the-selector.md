# RE-012 — clean prime-gap mixed-race profile is determined by one value and the selector

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + PRIME-FREE-INTERVAL-RECONSTRUCTION + SELECTOR-CONDITIONED-RIGIDITY + LOCAL-JET-NONINDEPENDENCE + NEGATIVE/OBSTRUCTION + PRIOR-ART-BOUNDED`. `RE-010` reduces the Robin sign at an unbounded clean regular self-tangent CA local peak to whether the selected mixed prime-race coordinate crosses `2 sqrt(2)`, and `RE-011` adds an exact source-selected value--slope relation at the intrinsic coordinate `X=log C`. Differentiating further might appear to produce a growing hierarchy of independent local constraints. It does not.

On the entire ordinary-prime-free interval containing `X`, the endpoint-subtracted Mertens remainder has an exact elementary reconstruction from its single value at `X` together with the already-known clean CA selector. Consequently every finite derivative of the normalized mixed-race coordinate at `X` is an affine function of that same value and the selector data. No higher local jet supplies an additional arithmetic observable once clean self-tangency has already been imposed.

More precisely, retain

\[
E(x)=\sum_{r\le x}-\log(1-r^{-1})-\gamma-\log\log x,
\qquad
\Delta(x)=\vartheta(x)-x,
\]

\[
h(x)=\frac{-\log(1-x^{-1})}{\log x},
\qquad
\mathcal M_*(x)=E(x)-\Delta(x)h(x),
\qquad
s(x)=\sqrt x\log x,
\qquad
\mathscr Y(x)=s(x)\mathcal M_*(x).
\tag{1}
\]

Let `C` be a sufficiently large clean regular self-tangent CA local peak, put `X=log C`, and let `p<X<q` be its consecutive first-layer boundary primes. Write

\[
\Phi:=\Phi_{\ge2}(X).
\tag{2}
\]

Then for every real `x` with `p<x<q`,

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal M_*(x)
=
\mathcal M_*(X)
-\log\frac{\log x}{\log X}
+(\Phi+x-X)h(x)-\Phi h(X).
}
\tag{3}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\boxed{
\mathscr Y(x)
=
\frac{s(x)}{s(X)}\mathscr Y(X)
+s(x)\left[
-\log\frac{\log x}{\log X}
+(\Phi+x-X)h(x)-\Phi h(X)
\right].
}
\tag{4}
\]

Thus the local mixed-race profile inside the fixed prime-free chamber contains exactly one scalar arithmetic degree of freedom beyond the clean selector: `mathscr Y(X)` itself. `RE-011`'s slope law is the first derivative of (4), not the first member of an independent hierarchy.

## 1. Prime-free intervals freeze both discontinuous arithmetic sources

Fix consecutive ordinary primes `p<q` and any `x,X` in `(p,q)`. The prime sum

\[
P(t):=\sum_{r\le t}-\log(1-r^{-1})
\]

is constant throughout this interval, as is `vartheta(t)`. Put

\[
\Theta:=\vartheta(p).
\tag{5}
\]

Then exactly

\[
E(x)-E(X)
=-\log\log x+\log\log X
=-\log\frac{\log x}{\log X},
\tag{6}
\]

and

\[
\Delta(x)=\Theta-x,
\qquad
\Delta(X)=\Theta-X.
\tag{7}
\]

Subtracting the two definitions of `mathcal M_*` gives the prime-free identity

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal M_*(x)-\mathcal M_*(X)
=
-\log\frac{\log x}{\log X}
-(\Theta-x)h(x)
+(\Theta-X)h(X).
}
\tag{8}
\]

This statement is independent of Robin geometry. It is merely the exact consequence of the two weighted prime sums being step functions with no jump between consecutive primes.

The clean CA selector now removes `Theta` as an independent parameter. `RE-011`, from the exact self-tangent balance of `RE-006`, gives

\[
X=\vartheta(p)+\Phi_{\ge2}(X),
\]

so

\[
\boxed{
\Theta-X=-\Phi,
\qquad
\Theta=X-\Phi.
}
\tag{9}
\]

Substituting (9) into (8) proves (3), and multiplying by `s(x)` proves (4). No asymptotic estimate, prime-gap bound, or hypothesis about the Robin sign is used.

## 2. Every local derivative is already encoded by the value and selector

Define the explicit selector remainder

\[
R_{X,\Phi}(x)
:=-\log\frac{\log x}{\log X}
 +(\Phi+x-X)h(x)-\Phi h(X).
\tag{10}
\]

Equation (4) is

\[
\mathscr Y(x)
=\frac{s(x)}{s(X)}\mathscr Y(X)+s(x)R_{X,\Phi}(x).
\tag{11}
\]

Both coefficient functions on the right are elementary and smooth near `X`. Therefore for every fixed integer `m>=1`,

\[
\boxed{
\mathscr Y^{(m)}(X)
=A_m(X)\mathscr Y(X)+B_m(X,\Phi),
}
\tag{12}
\]

where explicitly

\[
A_m(X)
:=\left.
\frac{d^m}{dx^m}\frac{s(x)}{s(X)}
\right|_{x=X},
\qquad
B_m(X,\Phi)
:=\left.
\frac{d^m}{dx^m}\bigl[s(x)R_{X,\Phi}(x)\bigr]
\right|_{x=X}.
\tag{13}
\]

Thus the whole Taylor jet at `X` is algebraically generated by the single value `mathscr Y(X)` and the exact selector datum `(X,Phi)`. In particular, higher derivatives cannot successively constrain a hypothetical clean counterexample after those data are already fixed.

For `m=1`, differentiating (10) gives

\[
R'_{X,\Phi}(X)
=h(X)-\frac1{X\log X}+\Phi h'(X).
\tag{14}
\]

Since

\[
\frac{s'(X)}{s(X)}
=\frac1{2X}+\frac1{X\log X},
\tag{15}
\]

(11) yields exactly

\[
X\mathscr Y'(X)
=\left(\frac12+\frac1{\log X}\right)\mathscr Y(X)
+Xs(X)\left[h(X)-\frac1{X\log X}+\Phi h'(X)\right],
\tag{16}
\]

which is the finite-`X` identity in `RE-011`. Its asymptotic `-sqrt(2)` source term follows there from `Phi=sqrt(2X)+o(sqrt X)`. This exact recovery is a normalization check on (3)--(4).

## 3. The full local germ is redundant, but the chamber boundaries are not

Equation (4) is stronger than the finite-jet statement. Conditional on knowing that `x` remains in the same ordinary-prime-free interval, the value of `mathscr Y(x)` at every such `x` is determined by `mathscr Y(X)`, `X`, and `Phi`. Any local norm, curvature statistic, finite-difference stencil, or fixed-radius Taylor functional that samples only points inside that same chamber is therefore a deterministic function of the same data.

This does **not** make the neighboring primes `p,q` redundant. Their locations determine the maximal domain on which the reconstruction is valid. In particular the gap width `q-p`, already important in `RE-007`, remains independent arithmetic information. Crossing an endpoint changes both the Mertens prime sum and `vartheta`; the jumps cancel in `mathcal M_*` itself, as `RE-011` proves, but the derivative law changes because the frozen value `Theta` changes. Matching successive prime-free chambers can therefore carry information that no single-chamber jet sees.

Nor does (12) say that a derivative is useless as a **diagnostic** of whether an arbitrary ambient point satisfies the clean selector. `RE-011` legitimately uses the value--slope law as a signature distinguishing selected coordinates from generic mixed-race excursions. The present result says something narrower: once the clean self-tangent selector and its exact higher-layer mass are admitted as hypotheses, the slope and all higher same-chamber derivatives are no longer independent source constraints.

A deliberately compressed approximation to the selector can of course leave higher-order terms recoverable from derivatives. That is recovery of information discarded from `Phi`, not a new arithmetic channel. The exact Research Watch frontier must be judged against the full selector already available in the canonical findings.

## 4. The Robin threshold remains one unresolved selected scalar

`RE-010` gives, along every unbounded clean regular self-tangent CA local-peak sequence,

\[
\sqrt X\log X\,\bigl(\log G(C)-\gamma\bigr)
=\mathscr Y(X)-2\sqrt2+o(1).

Combining (17) with (11)--(12) shows that enriching the local observation from the selected value to any fixed or even complete same-chamber smooth profile cannot change the first-order decision problem. A clean counterexample still requires

\[
\mathscr Y(X)\ge2\sqrt2-o(1),

This closes one natural continuation of `RE-011`: repeatedly differentiating the selected mixed race cannot produce a tower of independent constraints capable of excluding (18). ## 5. Prior art and falsification boundary

The algebra behind (8) is classical and elementary: weighted prime sums are step functions between consecutive primes, and partial summation gives the standard relation between Mertens-type and Chebyshev errors. No novelty is claimed for prime-free constancy, for differentiating a smooth expression between jumps, or for the global mixed prime-race coordinate itself.

Bhattacharya--Martin--Simpson, already anchored in `SOURCES.md`, study the global correlations of the normalized standard and reciprocal prime-counting errors and establish the RH-equivalence boundary for one-sided mixed-error control used in `RE-009`. Their global theory does not supply a CA self-tangent selector and is not used to prove (3). A targeted audit of the weighted-prime-counting/Mertens literature did not identify a theorem whose content is the selector-conditioned redundancy (11)--(12). This is not a blanket novelty claim: the Mathia-specific content is the consequence of splicing the exact CA balance (9) into the elementary prime-free reconstruction.

Several falsification boundaries are essential. First, (3) applies only while `x` stays in the same ordinary-prime-free interval as `X`; extending the same formula through another prime without updating `Theta` is false. Second, the conclusion about jet redundancy is relative to the **exact** selector `(X,Phi)`. If `Phi` is unknown or replaced by only its leading asymptotic, derivatives can encode the omitted selector error. Third, a nonlocal functional spanning a prime endpoint, or the entire future tail in `RE-008`, is not covered by the no-new-information statement. Finally, the clean first-layer/self-tangent hypotheses are needed only for the substitution (9); the generic prime-free identity (8) remains true without them.

No new source anchor is required. The external ingredients relevant to the interpretation are already recorded in `SOURCES.md`; the new claim is exact finite bookkeeping plus the existing canonical selector.
