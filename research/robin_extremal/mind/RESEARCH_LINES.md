## Find a selector-sensitive source invariant between one boundary charge and coefficient-by-coefficient Euler recovery

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps` through `MI-044-honest-multiplicity-prices-early-repair-by-deletion-support`.

RE-153--RE-168 identify the Pareto-weighted Chebyshev-deficit destination and quantify how much post-selector Robin motion remains possible under quantitative Korobov--Vinogradov source fidelity. The intrinsic decay length `ell_KV(T)=log T/V(T)` gives the spatial capacity scale: a bounded-multiplicative or subpower region can still carry order-one normalized displacement while enough future capacity remains.

RE-169 identifies Mertens normalization as an exact conservation law for the same Robin kernel. RE-170--RE-171 show that exact global charge does not localize it over flexible generalized sources. RE-172 adds finite ordinary-prime rigidity, but RE-173 proves that this is compactness rigidity: an infinite `{0,1,2}` delete/duplicate tail on ordinary prime labels can match `Gamma=gamma` exactly, stay below every fixed KV envelope and still create an order-one transient Robin excursion.

RE-174 closes the opposite extreme: an unbounded exact high-temperature Euler profile identifies every bounded multiplicity perturbation, but at exponentially bad Robin-scale conditioning. RE-175--RE-177 show that the first right-boundary Euler slope already forces and spatially prices sign changes in delayed repairs. RE-178 then shows that adding any subcritical scalar jet family `j=o(log p/log log p)` only buys logarithmic leverage and cannot improve the leading polynomial distance-versus-sign-complexity exponent.

RE-179 replaces the remaining raw sign-phase count by its intrinsic location-sensitive currency. With exact Mertens value and first Euler slope, ordinary-prime repair beyond `lambda p`, and the KV envelope, define the negative reset starts `z_j` and

`R_b^-(Y)=sum_j exp(-b V(z_j))`.

If `N_p^-(Y)` is the amount of forced negative centered repair already paid below `Y`, then

`(delta_(lambda,p) d_p-N_p^-(Y))_+ <<_b R_b^-(Y)+ell_KV(Y) exp(-bV(Y))`.

Hence, while less than half of the Robin-scale debt has been paid early,

`sqrt(p) log p [R_b^-(Y)+ell_KV(Y) exp(-bV(Y))] >> 1`.

Infinite sign count is therefore not an escape by itself. Very remote alternations have exponentially diminishing value in the source metric; a countably infinite tail may still have finite or negligible reset budget. Conversely, if the weighted remote budget is too small, a fixed fraction of the required negative repair must already occur near the selector. RE-177's raw `M exp(-bV(Y))` law is the finite-phase corollary of this sharper weighted statement.

RE-180 restores the honest multiplicity constraint `m_Q(q)>=0` that the relaxed signed-capacity model suppresses. Since a negative coefficient is then exactly one deleted ordinary prime, while the centered two-jet kernel satisfies `K_D(q)=O(1/p)` throughout the repair region, the forced negative debt requires at least `Omega(sqrt(p)/log p)` distinct repair deletions in total. More locally,

`N_del(Y)/p + R_b^-(Y) + ell_KV(Y) exp(-bV(Y)) >> d_p`.

Thus the early branch of RE-179 is no longer cost-free: avoiding the remote reset budget requires a second deletion set on the same cardinality scale as the original selector packet. This is genuinely source-specific one-sided granularity; it disappears if arbitrary negative signed coefficients are allowed.

The live matched-control escape is now a hybrid discrete/continuous dichotomy: **selector-scale many early repair deletions** or a sufficiently large/divergent KV-weighted reset budget. A stronger theorem must show that genuine CA/Robin selector structure cannot accommodate that second deletion set, exploit joint moment geometry across Euler jets, or obtain a source-native reason that the boundary coordinates are available. Merely adding more scalar derivatives, counting infinitely many sign changes, or treating early negative mass as an unpriced scalar no longer advances the boundary.

This still does not make any Euler boundary jet a free invariant of the original Robin problem. RE-175--RE-180 state what additional source coordinates would force and how their repair cost is conditioned. Any closure must explain why such information follows from genuine arithmetic input rather than being an extra hypothesis.

## Keep source capacity, exact identification, jet depth and usable conditioning distinct

Quantitative PNT/KV fidelity, exact Mertens normalization, ordinary-prime support, integer multiplicity, finite/growing Euler boundary jets, the full high-temperature Euler germ, weighted reset complexity and the zero-side packet obstruction answer different questions. RE-173 shows strong coarse fidelity plus one exact boundary charge is non-rigid; RE-174 shows the full exact Euler hierarchy is rigid but ill-conditioned; RE-175--RE-178 price delayed compensation and expose the saturation of shallow scalar jets; RE-179 identifies where sign resets occur as the right continuous oscillation currency; RE-180 adds the complementary discrete currency forced by honest nonnegative prime multiplicities.

Future matched controls must therefore state **what source ambiguity survives, where its sign resets occur, how much KV-weighted reset budget they carry, how many actual prime deletions are consumed near the selector, which joint source moments are genuinely available, and at what selector-normalized precision/placement cost** before source rigidity is credited as Robin leverage.
