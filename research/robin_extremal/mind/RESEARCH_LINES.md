## Resolve the vertical Euler continuum beyond the real single-center boundary regimes

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps` through `MI-055-separated-continuum-prediction-has-a-universal-unit-wiener-exponent-floor`.

The scalar Euler program separates positive-real compression from vertical-phase resolution. RE-196--RE-203 show that the positive real ray can be nearly blind and finite vertical panels can still be aliased. RE-204 moves to a whole vertical interval and proves that separated hiding has an exponential signed-prefix cost. RE-205--RE-208 construct ordinary-prime repairs in a small explicit range while respecting fixed KV envelopes.

RE-216--RE-218 improve the constructive continuum predictor and give

`1 <= C_sep <= 5.996390`.

RE-219 proves that the current one-positive-real-center negative-binomial architecture has Wiener exponent at least two whenever the discarded tail is certified termwise in absolute value. RE-220 shows that merely rotating the center into the complex plane cannot improve that certificate class: radialization weakly improves the contraction factor while preserving the exact coefficient Wiener norm.

RE-221 removes a different possible artifact. For fixed positive-real center, fixed arc and fixed truncation ratio, the exact incomplete-beta polynomial remainder may use complex cancellation, yet uniform prediction still forces normalized Wiener exponent at least two. The factor-two obstruction is therefore not created by the triangle inequality on the tail in this fixed-parameter real architecture.

RE-222 extends that exact-remainder obstruction to every **nondegenerate drifting real family**. On each compact subset `K` of the interior contracting parameter domain, uniform prediction forces

`liminf log ||F_m||_A/(m alpha_m) >= 2 + eta_K`

for some `eta_K>0`. A real single-center family can approach the factor-two boundary only by leaving every compact interior set. Combined with RE-219, any hypothetical sub-two real single-center route needs both a genuine parameter degeneration and exact finite-remainder cancellation that cannot be replaced by the absolute-tail certificate.

RE-223 rules out one such degeneration independently of the negative-binomial representation. If an arbitrary complex repair measure is supported in a finite corridor `[H,RH]` and approximates the constant one on `[-T,T]` with error `<1`, then

`||mu||_TV >= (1-epsilon) q_R^(-n)`,

where `q_R=cos(pi/(R+1))` and `n=floor((R+1)HT/pi)`. Hence the corridor-sensitive exponent satisfies

`C_sep(R) >= max(1,B(R))`,

`B(R)=((R+1)/pi) log sec(pi/(R+1))`.

`B(R)` diverges as `R->1+`, meets the global unit floor at `R=1.430264606...`, and for the single-center corridor `R=1+lambda+o(1)` any rate-two escape must satisfy

`liminf lambda >= 0.062422318...`.

Thus collapsing the repair corridor onto the first admissible delay cannot cross the factor-two barrier. In the fixed-corridor real single-center problem, `lambda` can be confined to a compact positive interval; the remaining exact-remainder escape must come from singular arc/center geometry such as `alpha->0`, approach to the contraction boundary, or an unbounded/endpoint center. A genuinely different multi-center/minimax basis remains a separate route toward the global unit floor.

## Keep exact identification, stable inversion, source capacity and prediction representation distinct

RE-174 gives exact source identification from rich Euler data, but the later results show why injectivity is not the selector-scale statement. Positive-real data can be nearly singular; finite vertical panels can be aliased; and a continuum interval forces an exponentially large signed-prefix primitive.

RE-217 separates the **global** prediction floor from prime-placement capacity. RE-219--RE-223 add representation-local and support-local lower bounds. The global floor remains one, while a fixed real single-center interior family pays strictly more than two and a narrow finite repair corridor has its own superoscillatory surcharge. None of these representation-specific floors may be promoted to a universal `C_sep>1` theorem.

The full ledger includes spectral interval width, logarithmic support gap, upper/lower delay ratio, global predictor/Wiener norm, representation-local approximation geometry, fixed versus boundary parameters, single- versus multi-center structure, tail certificate, total reciprocal variation, signed prefix primitive, prime-count capacity, Chebyshev discrepancy, window-tail norm, integer multiplicities and KV source fidelity. They remain independent until an exact theorem relates them.
