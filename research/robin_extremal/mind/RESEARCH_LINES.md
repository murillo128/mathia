## Reduce the selected-boundary problem to finitely many low CA layers

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps`, `MI-002-ordinary-sigma-rejects-composite-substitute-atoms-at-every-fixed-ca-layer`, `MI-003-fixed-threshold-cells-freeze-prime-source-data`, `MI-004-higher-layer-ca-switches-carry-quantized-residual-charge`, `MI-005-matched-first-layer-packets-conserve-the-endpoint-prime-multiset`, `MI-006-first-layer-timing-is-large-in-transport-but-summable-in-robin-metric`, `MI-007-matched-packets-convert-selector-dilation-into-chebyshev-underdensity`, `MI-008-reciprocal-mertens-recovery-forces-corrected-chebyshev-growth`, `MI-009-smooth-dilation-front-is-not-the-obstruction`, `MI-010-deep-support-boundaries-have-vanishing-threshold-measure`.

RE-076--RE-085 reconstruct the late selector geometry and remove bounded fixed-order mixed-event mass as a leading escape. RE-086 turns selection of a fixed-layer event as an adjacent support boundary into an anchored ordinary-prime-gap cost, while RE-087 shows that summing marginal one-sided gap capacity over all candidate primes is too generous to contradict the CA amplitude.

RE-088 now removes the opposite extreme. If `delta=1-b_1-theta` and `delta>1/r`, the total threshold measure of cells touching a depth-`>=r` adjacent support boundary tends to zero. For any hypothetical `Theta>theta`, one can choose a finite `r` with `1/r<Theta-theta`, so sufficiently deep CA layers are negligible in threshold measure. The argument cannot eliminate `r=2`, because `delta<1/2` always.

The live selection problem is therefore concentrated in a **finite low-layer chamber**, with the second CA layer an irreducible barrier for the present counting-plus-cell-width method. A successful theorem should exploit joint/tied resonances, rightmost-maximizer compatibility, skipped spans, or another nonlocal constraint among those low layers rather than summing marginal gap capacity or pushing depth farther.

## Keep event mass, selected-boundary depth and threshold measure separate

RE-085 says high layers carry vanishing normalized event mass; RE-088 says sufficiently deep selected boundaries occupy vanishing threshold measure under the off-RH exponent gap. Neither statement removes finitely many low layers. In particular, a mass-negligible low-layer boundary can still be structurally decisive when selected.

The remaining obstruction is finite-depth but selection-sensitive. Treating all candidate primes independently is too weak by RE-087, while treating arbitrarily deep layers is unnecessary by RE-088.
