## Exclude the threshold-selected opposite-sign standard prime-race cone

**Linked intuition:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps`.

RE-001--RE-025 reduce hypothetical Robin failure to self-tangent CA boundary events, a very sparse prime-power host set, and a coupled race/depth geometry in which the same frontier displacement controls both arithmetic race position and adjacent event-layer complexity.

RE-026--RE-029 convert that qualitative localization into a quantitative standard prime-race signature. A threshold-tangent Robin witness forces large selected excursions and super-square-root prime-layer defects; after eliminating the relocation ambiguity, RE-029 places the two relevant standard errors at the **same parameter**. Along the selected CA threshold sequence the Chebyshev error is forced negative while the ordinary Mertens log-product error is forced positive, with a quantitative asymptotic cone relating their magnitudes.

The live theorem is therefore no longer to show that several rare-looking CA conditions coincide. It is to prove that the threshold-selected sequence cannot sustain this same-parameter opposite-sign Chebyshev/Mertens cone with the required magnitude, or to derive a source identity that couples those two standard errors tightly enough to contradict the cone. The CA selector and deep event geometry remain essential because generic prime-race oscillation does not address the selected sequence.

## Keep host sparsity, event depth, and the same-parameter race cone logically distinct

The host set is thin and the event layers are deep, but neither fact alone excludes a Robin witness. The decisive current arithmetic surface is the selected standard-error pair at one common parameter. Future estimates should be conditioned on or transported to that CA-selected sequence rather than inferred from generic sign changes of either prime-race error separately.
