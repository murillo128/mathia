## Control matched first-layer fan runs through the one-sided Chebyshev--Mertens ledger

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps`, `MI-002-ordinary-sigma-rejects-composite-substitute-at-every-fixed-ca-layer`, `MI-003-fixed-threshold-cells-freeze-prime-source-data`, `MI-004-higher-layer-ca-switches-carry-quantized-residual-charge`, `MI-005-matched-first-layer-packets-conserve-the-endpoint-prime-multiset`, `MI-006-first-layer-timing-is-large-in-transport-but-summable-in-robin-metric`, `MI-007-matched-packets-convert-selector-dilation-into-chebyshev-underdensity`.

RE-068 makes every additive endpoint prime statistic exactly blind on one matched first-layer `0 -> 0` packet, and RE-069 shows that direct prime-to-event timing is summable after the exact Robin readout. RE-070--RE-071 nevertheless expose a pre-compression two-source saddle: selector dilation forces `H_vartheta(Z)=Z-vartheta(Z)` upward and the logarithmic Mertens error `E_l(Z)` downward, with both changes generated by the same selected packet geometry.

RE-072 upgrades the single-packet law to a consecutive matched fan run. A state lying between two matched switches has zero fringe bit at both cell endpoints, hence throughout the whole cell. Consequently `H_vartheta` increases and `E_l` decreases not only across every matched switch but through every intervening fixed-state cell. The total run ledger is a sum of positive switch and cell contributions; there is no internal cancellation while the fan stays in this branch.

The run also pays a threshold-width cost. If the selected matched fan spans `beta_m-beta_1` and begins at physical scale `X`, then

`Delta H_vartheta >>_J X^(1-b_1) log X (beta_m-beta_1)`.

The reciprocal error follows the same path measure with kernel about `1/(Z log Z)`. Thus a macroscopic threshold-width matched run consumes a monotone Chebyshev excursion even if it is decomposed into arbitrarily many individually small packets.

This is still a capacity law rather than a contradiction: `1-b_1<Theta` in the false-RH regime, so the forced Chebyshev scale remains below the natural off-critical PNT frontier. The live theorem should constrain how much threshold width or how many repeated matched runs the joint Chebyshev--Mertens error process can support, or prove that the fan must exit the matched first-layer branch and incur fringe/higher-layer charges.

## Treat source fidelity, selector geometry, destination compression, fan width, and joint error drift separately

Exact conservation of the prime multiset does not imply geometric neutrality. Direct timing can be summably erased while selector dilation survives; RE-072 shows that the survivor accumulates coherently across cells and switches. Higher-layer events and unmatched fringe transitions remain distinct interruption mechanisms rather than cancellation hidden inside the matched ledger.
