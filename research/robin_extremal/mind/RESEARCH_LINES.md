## Cross the fourth-root terminal source-capacity scale or use stronger arithmetic coupling

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps` through `MI-032-self-tangent-robin-height-is-a-pareto-weighted-chebyshev-deficit-average`.

RE-153 turns the source side of a regular self-tangent Robin counterexample into the canonical normalized average

`E_(nu_p)[(t-vartheta(t))/sqrt(t)] >= sqrt(2)-o(1)`.

Its kernel has a Pareto scaling law and places substantial mass far beyond the selector prime. RE-154 quantifies the resulting sensitivity at fixed multiplicative distance: only `sqrt(p)/log p` coherent post-selector relocations can move the normalized source coordinate by order one while preserving exact agreement below a fixed multiple of `p` and PNT-scale density.

RE-155 pushes that matched-source obstruction to any fixed terminal fraction of the full canonical Robin horizon `U_A(p)`. RE-156 now quantifies the shrinking-layer endpoint. For a terminal layer `[(1-epsilon_p)U,U]`, the maximal equal-cardinality relocation leverage is

`Cap_p(epsilon_p) asymp epsilon_p^2 sqrt(p) log p / log U`,

throughout the PNT-valid range, with order-one transition

`epsilon_*(p)=sqrt(log U/(sqrt(p)log p)) = sqrt(A) p^(-1/4)(log p)^(1/3)(log log p)^(1/6)`.

Thus the RE-154/RE-155 source surgery survives into shrinking layers whenever `epsilon_p/epsilon_*(p)->infinity`, and retains order-one capacity at the transition scale. Only for `epsilon_p=o(epsilon_*(p))` does this **specific equal-cardinality terminal relocation class** have vanishing leverage. That is a boundary of the matched control, not a positive Robin theorem.

The residual source theorem is therefore quantitative rather than merely “terminal.” It must propagate enough ordinary-prime/CA information to cross the fourth-root layer scale, derive stronger short-interval or signed information that forbids the coherent relocation near/beyond that scale, or bypass propagation with an exact source identity controlling the Pareto-weighted deficit directly. Below `epsilon_*`, a different structured perturbation could still carry order-one destination weight and must be ruled out separately.

## Keep the two source gates distinct

RE-153--RE-156 give a direct prime-side destination and a quantitative terminal-horizon matched-source sensitivity. RE-151--RE-152 give a zero-side matched obstruction to one reciprocal-scale observation route. Neither subsumes the other. Progress should be credited only when it changes the exact quantity consumed by its own destination: source control below the explicit `epsilon_*` relocation-capacity boundary (or a stronger arithmetic replacement) on the self-tangent branch, or target-conditioned zero geometry on the reciprocal-packet branch.
