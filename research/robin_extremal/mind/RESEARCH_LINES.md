## Resolve the vertical Euler continuum between constructive hiding and the sharp separated-source capacity boundary

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps` through `MI-054-continuum-vertical-hiding-pays-an-exponential-signed-prefix-tariff`.

The scalar Euler program now separates positive-real compression from vertical-phase resolution. RE-196--RE-199 show that the full positive real ray can remain exponentially almost blind at selector scale even when a fixed positive fraction of `log p` boundary jets are matched. RE-200 restores linear Euclidean dimension with vertical complex samples on one shell, but RE-201--RE-203 show that arbitrary finite vertical panels can still be aliased by multi-shell synthesis.

RE-204 moves from panels to a whole interval. Hiding a local selector-scale Euler atom from `|t|<=T` by repairs separated by a logarithmic gap `H` has an exponential spectral-gap cost in reciprocal variation. RE-205--RE-208 show that ordinary primes can pay a substantial part of that continuum cost while preserving every fixed Korobov--Vinogradov source envelope; the current explicit constructive range at `H=log 8` reaches only `c<0.0289079...` for `T_Q=c log Q`.

RE-209--RE-213 give the converse inside the same separated ordinary-prime architecture. The positive compact-window method has exact unit exponential rate, and under fidelity to every fixed KV envelope one obtains

`((1/2)log Q-H T_Q)/V(Q) -> +infinity`,

with `V(Q)=(log Q)^(3/5)/(log log Q)^(1/5)`. This excludes the endpoint `c=1/(2H)` and a whole near-endpoint corridor.

RE-214 now separates what is due merely to finite ordinary-prime carrying capacity from what genuinely uses KV fidelity. Two Kaiser factors already yield

`R >>_H (1-epsilon)e^(HT)/(HT)^2`.

For the `{0,1,2}` ordinary-prime repair, bounded edit multiplicity gives the independent upper bound `R<<sqrt(Q)` from prime-count capacity alone. Hence every such separated repair satisfies

`H T_Q-2 log(H T_Q) <= (1/2)log Q+O_H(1)`,

and therefore

`H T_Q <= (1/2)log Q+2 log log Q+O_H(1)`.

Thus every fixed `c>1/(2H)` is impossible **without any KV fidelity hypothesis on the perturbed source**. KV is needed only for the exact endpoint and the stronger `omega(V(Q))` subendpoint deficit of RE-213. At `H=log 8`, bounded prime capacity alone rules out every fixed `c>0.2404491734...`.

The remaining gap is no longer a window-shaping question, and the two converse layers should not be conflated. Explicit hiding exists only below `0.0289079...`; bounded ordinary-prime capacity forbids fixed heights above `0.240449...`; KV fidelity closes the endpoint and much of the near-endpoint region. Progress must either improve the constructive source architecture while respecting signed-prefix **cardinality** as well as KV, prove a stronger source-independent converse inside the remaining gap, or leave the fixed-gap separated bounded-multiplicity architecture.

## Keep exact identification, stable inversion, source capacity and fidelity distinct

RE-174 still gives exact source identification from sufficiently rich Euler data. The newer results show why exact injectivity is not the selector-scale statement. Positive-real data can be nearly singular; finite vertical panels can be aliased; and a continuum interval forces an exponentially large signed-prefix primitive.

RE-214 adds a key resource separation. The half-logarithmic leading ceiling above the endpoint comes from **carrier cardinality**: there are only so many distinct ordinary-prime edits available under bounded multiplicity. The exact endpoint and its thicker exclusion require the finer KV discrepancy information. A proof should therefore state whether its source upper bound is combinatorial capacity, Chebyshev/PNT fidelity, or something stronger; using a fine arithmetic theorem where raw source capacity already suffices obscures the actual mechanism.

The full ledger includes spectral interval width, logarithmic support gap, total reciprocal variation, signed prefix primitive, prime-count capacity, Chebyshev discrepancy, moment-cancellation order, integer multiplicities and KV source fidelity. These are independent currencies until an exact theorem relates them.
