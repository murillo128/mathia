## Exclude the forced cancelling-doublet geometry on an attained finite edge

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps` through `MI-021-one-level-zero-density-cannot-exclude-reciprocal-cancelling-doublets-on-sublinear-windows`.

RE-112--RE-123 reduce the attained finite-edge branch to a signed subedge zero packet and transfer continuum excursions to regular CA states. RE-124--RE-129 show that hiding a dominant algebraically strong atom forces a distinct, logarithmically comparable companion at inverse-window spacing with the required cancelling target-demodulated phase.

RE-130 closes the separate diffuse-cloud escape and now does so with an off-critical-density-aware quantitative bound. If `M_K(U)` is the largest packet atom and `A_K(U)` the absolute packet mass, a zero-density estimate `N_(sigma_0)(T) << T^kappa (log T)^q` gives

`A_K(U) << M_K(U)^(1-kappa/2) (log(e/M_K(U)))^q`.

Ingham already has `kappa<1` on every fixed off-critical half-strip, so the exponent is strictly larger than `1/2`; Guth--Maynard can improve it further. Thus algebraic packet mass forces a quantitatively stronger algebraic atom than the earlier square-root interpolation.

RE-131 sharpens the strong-atom geometry when hiding persists on a fixed relative window. A dominant atom must have a distinct companion at polylogarithmic-reciprocal ordinate distance, amplitude at least a logarithmic fraction of the dominant one, cancelling demodulated phase, and horizontal lock `O(log log U/U)`.

RE-132 now closes one tempting way to exclude that geometry. A formal simple functional-equation-symmetric spectrum with only `O(log log T)` off-critical points can still contain globally dominant equal-amplitude `pi/U` doublets whose full packet hides uniformly on **every sublinear window**. Hence arbitrarily strong one-level zero-density upper bounds can strengthen atom extraction but cannot by themselves forbid the local cancelling pair required after extraction.

Inside the attained finite-edge branch, the live question is therefore genuinely local/source-specific: can actual off-critical zeta zeros support infinitely many algebraically dominant phase-cancelling doublets with the required spacing and horizontal lock, or can fixed-relative-window visibility be derived from arithmetic zero geometry? The nonattained edge and `Theta=1` remain separate.

## Keep density-aware atom extraction, observation-window strength and cancelling-pair exclusion distinct

RE-130 is an absolute-mass interpolation theorem whose exponent depends on the off-critical zero-density input. RE-129 turns sublinear-window hiding into a nearby cancelling companion. RE-131 uses the stronger macroscopic hiding hypothesis to sharpen that companion to reciprocal spacing up to polylogarithms and simultaneously lock the real parts. RE-132 shows that even an extraordinarily sparse simple spectrum can realize the sublinear-window hiding geometry, so global counting and local pair exclusion are independent resources.

Improving zero-density estimates strengthens the atom extracted from a given packet mass but does **not** itself exclude the required companion geometry. A successful closure must instead use local spacing/phase restrictions for actual zeros, obtain the macroscopic visibility regime where the `pi/U` control fails, or introduce another arithmetic compatibility absent from the formal matched spectrum.
