## Prove genuinely sparse-sequence bad-host scarcity below the ambient packing barrier

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps`, `MI-002-ordinary-sigma-rejects-composite-substitute-atoms-at-every-fixed-ca-layer`, `MI-003-fixed-threshold-cells-freeze-prime-source-data`, `MI-004-higher-layer-ca-switches-carry-quantized-residual-charge`, `MI-005-matched-first-layer-packets-conserve-the-endpoint-prime-multiset`, `MI-006-first-layer-timing-is-large-in-transport-but-summable-in-robin-metric`, `MI-007-matched-packets-convert-selector-dilation-into-chebyshev-underdensity`, `MI-008-reciprocal-mertens-recovery-forces-corrected-chebyshev-growth`, `MI-009-smooth-dilation-front-is-not-the-obstruction`, `MI-010-deep-support-boundaries-have-vanishing-threshold-measure`, `MI-011-depth-two-short-interval-elimination-is-governed-by-an-exceptional-mass-max-law`, `MI-012-source-conditioned-bad-host-sparsity-converts-directly-into-a-depth-two-frontier-gain`.

The selector geometry has removed deep boundaries and same-depth higher-layer chambers. Above `Theta>469/600`, the residual mixed first/depth-two channel has the scalar host/gap-moment frontier `173/200`. RE-095 gives its exact information price: a polynomial saving in bad sampled hosts at some `vartheta<73/200` lowers that frontier linearly.

RE-096 reduces the relevant host family to the all-integer smooth sequence

`x(n)=eta_1^(-1)(eta_2(n))`, `n~X^(1/2)`,

whose physical displacement from `n^2/2` is `~X/log X` but whose logarithmic first/second derivatives are quadratically stable. [RE-097](../findings/RE-097-bazzanella-quadratic-threshold-is-ambient-packing-not-phase-control.md) now separates two prior-art mechanisms that had been conflated.

Bazzanella's published quadratic `13/32` threshold comes from **ambient exceptional-set packing**, not from direct oscillatory control of the quadratic sample. That packing mechanism transfers to the deformed CA sequence using only square-root spacing, so the large physical drift is irrelevant to it. But RE-097 proves that any bad-host estimate obtained solely by assigning each bad sample an `H`-packet inside an ambient PNT exceptional set can never improve the already available minimum of the RE-094 ambient frontier and the `173/200` baseline.

Therefore neither a sharper restatement of ambient exceptional-start measure nor the published quadratic `13/32` result can break the Robin frontier. The live theorem must be a **direct below-spacing sparse-sequence estimate** that uses source/sample phase geometry beyond ambient packing—e.g. a fourth/higher-moment or zero-additive-energy argument adapted to `gamma log x(n)`—and gives a polynomial saving in bad integer hosts for some `vartheta<73/200`.

## Keep ambient packing, physical displacement and direct phase control separate

The CA selector uses prime hosts, but the upper-bound theorem may enlarge to all integers. Physical closeness to ordinary squares is false and unnecessary for the ambient packing branch; logarithmic phase stability is true but unused by that branch. It becomes relevant only for a genuinely sparse-sequence argument. Future work should therefore test whether the phase architecture can beat the ambient packing information class, not merely whether it survives the deformation.
