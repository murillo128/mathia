# Robin-extremal research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable robin-extremal intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Separate the ordinary-supply threshold from the coarse-PNT ambiguity threshold

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps` through `MI-032-self-tangent-robin-height-is-a-pareto-weighted-chebyshev-deficit-average`.

RE-153 turns the source side of a regular self-tangent Robin counterexample into the canonical normalized average

`E_(nu_p)[(t-vartheta(t))/sqrt(t)] >= sqrt(2)-o(1)`.

Its Pareto kernel places substantial mass far beyond the selector prime. RE-154--RE-155 show that coherent post-selector source changes can move this exact destination by order one while preserving exact lower-source agreement and PNT-scale density.

RE-156 quantifies the shrinking-layer endpoint for ordinary-prime equal-cardinality relocation. In `[(1-epsilon)U,U]`, its capacity is

`Cap_p(epsilon) asymp epsilon^2 sqrt(p) log p / log U`,

with transition

`epsilon_*(p)=sqrt(log U/(sqrt(p)log p))`.

RE-157 shows that this fourth-root transition includes a local ordinary-prime supply constraint and is therefore not a coarse-PNT identifiability threshold. If matched generalized-prime controls instead add `delta_p U/log U` labels with `delta_p->0` and vary only their terminal placement, their leverage is linear in `epsilon` and the lower ambiguity scale is

`epsilon_PNT(p)=log U/(sqrt(p)log p)=epsilon_*(p)^2`.

Whenever `epsilon/epsilon_PNT -> infinity`, order-one separation of the Robin source coordinate is compatible with a perturbation intensity tending to zero. At `epsilon=epsilon_*`, this ambiguity already survives with `delta_p asymp epsilon_* ->0`. Only at `epsilon=O(epsilon_PNT)` does this vanishing-budget insertion-placement class itself become subcritical.

The residual source theorem has two distinct jobs. Crossing `epsilon_*` only outruns the known ordinary-prime relocation architecture. A positive conclusion from coarse PNT information requires either propagation through the parametrically thinner `epsilon_PNT` scale or a genuinely local ordinary-prime/colossally-abundant constraint that rules out the generalized-source placement freedom exposed by RE-157. Stronger short-interval or signed information, or an exact source identity controlling the Pareto-weighted deficit, may provide that missing rigidity.

## Control one-sided vertical reciprocal escape without overstating the symmetric obstruction

RE-144--RE-152 put the zero-side packet into reciprocal-scale language and show that one-sided vertical phase geometry can hide a scale-maximal target at lacunary stages while fixed-order global local-correlation statistics remain unchanged. A closure theorem on this branch must still control target-conditioned exceptional geometry rather than typical-center averages.

## Keep the two source gates distinct

RE-153--RE-157 give a direct source-side destination and separate the ordinary-local-supply threshold `epsilon_*` from the smaller coarse-PNT ambiguity threshold `epsilon_PNT=epsilon_*^2`. RE-151--RE-152 give a zero-side matched obstruction to one reciprocal-scale observation route. Neither subsumes the other. Progress should be credited only when it changes the exact quantity consumed by its own destination: local arithmetic control strong enough to rule out the RE-157 PNT-small placement ambiguity on the self-tangent branch, or target-conditioned zero geometry on the reciprocal-packet branch.
