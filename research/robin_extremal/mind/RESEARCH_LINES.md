RE-076--RE-085 reconstruct the late selector geometry and remove bounded fixed-order mixed-event mass as a leading escape. RE-086 turns selection of a fixed-layer event as an adjacent support boundary into an anchored ordinary-prime-gap cost, while RE-087 shows that summing marginal one-sided gap capacity over all candidate primes is too generous to contradict the CA amplitude.

RE-088 removes sufficiently deep selected boundaries: under a positive off-RH exponent gap, some finite depth cutoff makes all deeper layers threshold-negligible. Its ambient counting mechanism cannot eliminate depth `2`.

RE-089 resolves an important part of that low-depth obstruction above the `3/5` zero frontier. If both adjacent support boundaries come from the same fixed higher layer `j>=2`, the corresponding physical chamber pulls back to a square-root-sized ordinary prime gap. Heath-Brown's total large-gap length estimate makes the total threshold measure of every fixed `j/j` chamber family vanish. In particular, pure second-layer chambers are threshold-negligible when `Theta>3/5`.

Combining RE-088 and RE-089, outside vanishing threshold measure every sufficiently late selected cell either touches a first-layer atom on at least one boundary or has genuinely different higher-layer depths inside a finite range. In the simple untied case the remaining higher-layer alternative is `2<=j_-!=j_+<r`.

The live theorem must therefore exploit **joint low-depth selection compatibility**: first-layer contact, mixed-depth resonances, tied power-related events, simultaneous boundary constraints, skipped spans, or rightmost-maximizer dynamics. Neither marginal gap counting, deeper-layer asymptotics, nor same-depth higher-layer scarcity addresses that surviving carrier.

## Keep event mass, selected-boundary depth, same-depth chamber scarcity and mixed-depth compatibility separate

RE-085 says high layers carry vanishing normalized event mass; RE-088 says sufficiently deep selected boundaries occupy vanishing threshold measure; RE-089 says fixed same-depth higher-layer chambers also occupy vanishing threshold measure above `Theta>3/5`. None of these proves that mixed-depth low-layer chambers or first-layer-touching cells are rare.

The remaining obstruction is finite-depth and relational. The next estimate must couple the two selected boundaries rather than sum candidate capacity one side or one layer at a time.
