## Prove reciprocal-scale tightness for the attained finite-edge packet

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps` through `MI-023-bounded-reciprocal-box-packets-have-a-uniform-visibility-floor`.

RE-112--RE-123 reduce the attained finite-edge branch to a signed subedge zero packet and transfer continuum excursions to regular CA states. RE-124--RE-129 show that hiding a dominant algebraically strong atom forces a distinct, logarithmically comparable companion at inverse-window spacing with the required cancelling target-demodulated phase.

RE-130 closes the diffuse-cloud escape quantitatively. If `M_K(U)` is the largest packet atom and `A_K(U)` the absolute packet mass, a zero-density estimate `N_(sigma_0)(T) << T^kappa (log T)^q` gives

`A_K(U) << M_K(U)^(1-kappa/2) (log(e/M_K(U)))^q`.

RE-131 sharpens the strong-atom geometry under fixed-relative-window hiding: the required companion has polylogarithmic-reciprocal ordinate distance, logarithmically comparable amplitude, cancelling demodulated phase and horizontal lock `O(log log U/U)`. RE-132 shows that even a very sparse simple formal spectrum can realize the reciprocal doublet on every sublinear window.

RE-133 is now accepted current evidence: a growing formal reciprocal cluster can hide the dominant atom even on a fixed-relative window while preserving simplicity, functional-equation symmetry and arbitrarily sparse one-level density. Its construction has both growing cardinality and growing scaled diameter.

RE-134 proves a positive local compactness statement when both reciprocal-box diameter and cardinality are bounded. RE-135 removes the cardinality requirement entirely. Inside any fixed scaled reciprocal box, the physical Robin coefficient law aligns the atoms into an empirical positive measure, giving a visibility floor independent of occupancy:

`sup_((1-kappa)U<=u<=U)|S_(C_U,K)(u)| >= c_R #C_U |a_(rho_0,K)(U)|`.

Consequently unbounded local occupancy is **not** an escape. If the full packet hides at `o(M_K(U))`, then for every fixed scaled radius `R` the complement must supply order-`M_K(U)` cancellation. The remaining noncompact direction is escape in scaled reciprocal diameter.

Inside the attained finite-edge branch, the live source theorem is therefore sharper: can actual off-critical zeta zeros satisfy a reciprocal-scale tightness estimate that prevents order-`M_K(U)` cancelling mass from living at scaled radii tending to infinity? Equivalently, can one quantify the cost of exterior cancellation as `R->infinity` strongly enough to combine it with the cardinality-free fixed-box floor? The nonattained edge and `Theta=1` remain separate.

## Keep density-aware atom extraction, observation-window strength, local positive-measure compactness and exterior escape distinct

RE-130 transfers the off-critical zero-density exponent into atom extraction. RE-129/RE-131 turn hiding into a nearby strongly constrained cancelling companion. RE-132 proves that one-level sparsity alone does not exclude the sublinear doublet. RE-133 proves that even fixed-relative windows can be defeated by a formal cluster whose scaled diameter diverges.

RE-134--RE-135 identify what **cannot** be responsible for that hiding under the Robin coefficient law: arbitrary finite occupancy inside one fixed reciprocal box remains visible because its normalized local spectrum is a probability measure on a fixed compact set. The relevant compactness variable is therefore scaled diameter/tightness, not cardinality.

Improving zero-density estimates may strengthen the atom extracted from a given packet mass but does not by itself control exterior cancellation. A closure argument must prevent order-one normalized mass from escaping every fixed reciprocal box, or exploit another arithmetic compatibility that makes such a multiscale cancellation cascade impossible.
