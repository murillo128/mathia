RE-111 shows that recovery at the unconditional Korobov--Vinogradov minimum would require a positive-mass packet of ordinary first-layer primes saturating the favorable side of that envelope. RE-112 now separates when that packet question is actually relevant. In the false-RH finite-edge regime

`1/2 < Theta = sup Re(rho) < 1`,

a fixed off-critical strip has summable reciprocal-ordinate mass by the persisted zero-density estimate. The truncated explicit formula therefore sharpens the textbook bound to

`psi(x)-x = O_Theta(x^Theta)`.

The full CA event sweep inherits the same source scale. RE-110's exact workload identity then forces

`V-Y >>_Theta h(C) Y^(2-Theta) log Y`,

and on adaptive witnesses of exponent `b`,

`V-Y >>_(Theta,b) Y^(2-Theta-b) log Y`.

For `b=1-Theta+eta`, the fixed-`eta` quantitative block family has dyadic packing `O_(Theta,eta)(T^eta/log T)`. Thus the finite-edge frontier is no longer about removing logarithmic slack from a textbook PNT estimate. The next theorem must exploit information beyond the scalar edge—zero density/phase or another source coordinate conditioned on the selected corridor—to rule out **CA-selected one-sided saturation of the true `Y^Theta` source envelope**.

If instead `Theta=1`, the spectral power improvement disappears. Only in this extreme branch do the RE-109--RE-111 Korobov--Vinogradov corridor and first-layer anti-saturation problem remain the live unconditional frontier. A useful theorem here would show that same-sign first-layer saturation packets have too little selected mass, or that a coupled reciprocal-Mertens/source coordinate cannot coexist with them.
