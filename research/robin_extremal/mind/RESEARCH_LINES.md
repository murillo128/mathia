## Resolve the vertical Euler continuum beyond the fixed-parameter real single-center Wiener floor

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps` through `MI-055-separated-continuum-prediction-has-a-universal-unit-wiener-exponent-floor`.

The scalar Euler program separates positive-real compression from vertical-phase resolution. RE-196--RE-203 show that the positive real ray can be nearly blind and finite vertical panels can still be aliased. RE-204 moves to a whole vertical interval and proves that separated hiding has an exponential signed-prefix cost. RE-205--RE-208 construct ordinary-prime repairs in a small explicit range while respecting fixed KV envelopes.

RE-216 first improved the constructive side by moving the analytic expansion center, reducing the proven separated-predictor Wiener exponent to at most `6.081`. RE-218 retunes the same single-center negative-binomial architecture to

`C_sep <= 5.996390`.

At `H=log 8`, the corresponding ordinary-prime hiding range improves to `c<0.0400989...`. The arithmetic realization is unchanged; the improvement is entirely in the pure continuum predictor.

RE-209--RE-215 establish the source-independent converse/capacity side. The endpoint-zero Kaiser--Bessel test gives

`R >>_H (1-epsilon) e^(HT)/(HT log(HT))`,

so bounded prime capacity forces `HT_Q <= (1/2)log Q + log log Q + log log log Q + O_H(1)`. RE-217 packages the same coercivity as a universal separated-prediction Wiener floor and yields the global interval

`1 <= C_sep <= 5.996390`.

RE-219 proves that scalar retuning inside the one-positive-real-center negative-binomial architecture cannot approach the unit floor when the discarded tail is certified termwise in absolute value. Uniformly vanishing tail error forces the leading Wiener exponent to be at least two:

`2 <= C_(1c,abs,R+) <= 5.996390`.

RE-220 closes the corresponding **complex single-center absolute-tail** escape. For every nonzero center `a`, radialization to `rho=|a|` weakly improves the worst-case contraction on the symmetric frequency arc while preserving the exact coefficient Wiener norm. The phase of `a` factors out at each output degree, so it creates no hidden coefficient cancellation, and the same radial comparator also has no worse termwise absolute-tail certificate. Hence

`2 <= C_(1c,abs,C) <= 5.996390`.

RE-221 now removes the concern that the factor-two floor for a fixed positive-real center was merely an artifact of termwise absolute tail domination. For fixed `0<alpha<pi/2`, `a>0` and `N=lambda m+O(1)`, the exact truncated predictor is the regularized incomplete-beta polynomial `I_(z/a)(m,N+1)`. If its **actual complex polynomial remainder**, after exact DC normalization, converges uniformly to one on the symmetric arc, then

`liminf log ||F_m||_A/(m alpha) >= 2`.

Thus exact cancellation in the finite Taylor remainder does not lower the fixed-real single-center floor. The absolute-tail argument of RE-219 was sufficient but not responsible for the exponent-two obstruction in that fixed-parameter real architecture.

The constructive frontier is therefore sharper than after RE-220. Merely rotating one center does not help under absolute-tail certification, and merely retaining exact remainder cancellation does not help for fixed real center/arc/truncation ratios. A serious route toward `C_sep=1` must use **genuinely multi-center/minimax geometry, a different approximation basis, asymptotically drifting or degenerate single-center parameters, or genuinely complex-center cancellation outside the RE-220 absolute-tail reduction**. The global floor remains one; the factor-two theorem remains representation-local.
