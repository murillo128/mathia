# Robin-extremal research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Robin-extremal intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Leave the fixed-corridor complex single-center negative-binomial class

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps` through `MI-060-complex-single-center-drift-cannot-escape-a-fixed-prediction-corridor`.

RE-204--RE-218 establish the continuum prediction problem and the global interval `1 <= C_sep <= 5.996390`. RE-219--RE-227 close the positive-real single-center fixed-corridor architecture with a uniform factor-two-plus Wiener obstruction.

RE-228 shows that one fixed genuinely complex center does not exploit DC cancellation to escape: shrinking the normalization denominator only increases Wiener cost. RE-229 then handles bounded complex center drift in the vanishing-arc regime. Arbitrary phase motion does not remove the divergent normalized Wiener rate while the center modulus remains bounded.

RE-230 closes the complementary unbounded-center escape inside a fixed delay corridor. If `|a_m|->infinity` but `N_m/(m|a_m-1|)->0`, the normalized predictor converges on the microscopic scale to the raw delay `e^(-iu)` and cannot approximate the constant target on arcs with `m alpha_m->infinity`. Any successful unbounded-center family must therefore have `N_m/m` grow at least proportionally to `|a_m|`, which is impossible in a fixed corridor.

The constructive frontier has consequently left drifting complex single centers **within fixed corridor geometry**. Viable changes include widening corridors in the transition regime `N/(m|a-1|)=Theta(1)`, multi-center/minimax cancellation, rational or different approximation bases, or another representation whose microscopic normal form is not the raw delay. These line-specific obstructions still do not improve the universal separated-measure lower bound by themselves.

## Keep source capacity, center motion, corridor width and predictor class distinct

Allowing a parameter to drift is only a genuine escape if the drift changes the physical resource budget. Bounded center motion remains Wiener-expensive; unbounded center motion can suppress the off-center correction so strongly that the raw delay reappears, and avoiding that forces the delay corridor itself to widen.

A future prediction argument should therefore state which new geometry invalidates the single-center Wiener/microscopic probes rather than treating scale dependence alone as enrichment. The zero-side packet obstruction remains separate and still needs target-conditioned exceptional geometry rather than average-center statistics.
