`x(n)=eta_1^(-1)(eta_2(n))`, `n~X^(1/2)`,

[RE-098](../findings/RE-098-resonant-four-zero-energy-already-clears-the-robin-host-window.md) now isolates the fourth-moment bottleneck much more sharply. The known near-resonant four-zero additive-energy bound already gives a power saving whenever

For nonresonant quartets let `Omega=gamma_1+gamma_2-gamma_3-gamma_4` and

`S_X(Omega)=sum_(n~X^(1/2)) exp(i Omega log x(n))`.

The live target is therefore a genuinely **shifted four-zero/oscillatory-kernel estimate** that exploits the phase geometry of the deformed CA sample and yields a power-saving bad-host count for some `vartheta` in `(0.341216...,0.365)`.

## Keep resonant energy, shifted energy and sample-phase cancellation separate

The resonant calculation does not use the deformation at all; the same bound holds for exact quadratic starts. The source-specific content enters only in the nonresonant kernel `S_X(Omega)`. Consequently an improvement to unshifted `N^*(sigma,T)` is not automatically useful, and a good exponential-sum estimate is not enough unless it is integrated against the correct shifted-zero population.

Future work should price the complete weighted nonresonant sum directly. The stable derivatives from RE-096 are relevant only insofar as they buy cancellation in `S_X`, enable a stronger shifted-energy decomposition, or couple the two. Ambient packet counting and ordinary resonant energy are now classified as insufficient or already adequate, respectively.
