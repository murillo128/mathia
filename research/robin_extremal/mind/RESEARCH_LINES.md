## Resolve the vertical Euler continuum in the two remaining real single-center boundary regimes

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps` through `MI-056-fixed-corridor-real-single-center-prediction-has-two-singular-rate-two-regimes`.

The scalar Euler program separates positive-real compression from vertical-phase resolution. RE-196--RE-203 show that the positive real ray can be nearly blind and finite vertical panels can still be aliased. RE-204 moves to a whole vertical interval and proves that separated hiding has an exponential signed-prefix cost. RE-205--RE-208 construct ordinary-prime repairs in a small explicit range while respecting fixed KV envelopes.

RE-216--RE-218 improve the constructive continuum predictor and give

`1 <= C_sep <= 5.996390`.

RE-219 proves that the current one-positive-real-center negative-binomial architecture has Wiener exponent at least two whenever the discarded tail is certified termwise in absolute value. RE-220 shows that merely rotating the center into the complex plane cannot improve that certificate class: radialization weakly improves the contraction factor while preserving the exact coefficient Wiener norm.

RE-221 removes a different possible artifact. For fixed positive-real center, fixed arc and fixed truncation ratio, the exact incomplete-beta polynomial remainder may use complex cancellation, yet uniform prediction still forces normalized Wiener exponent at least two. The factor-two obstruction is therefore not created by the triangle inequality on the tail in this fixed-parameter real architecture.

RE-222 extends that exact-remainder obstruction to every **nondegenerate drifting real family**. On each compact subset `K` of the interior contracting parameter domain, uniform prediction forces

`liminf log ||F_m||_A/(m alpha_m) >= 2 + eta_K`

for some `eta_K>0`. A real single-center family can approach the factor-two boundary only by leaving every compact interior set.

RE-223 rules out truncation collapse in a fixed repair corridor. If an arbitrary complex repair measure is supported in `[H,RH]` and approximates the constant one on `[-T,T]` with error `<1`, then the corridor-sensitive exponent satisfies

`C_sep(R) >= max(1,B(R))`,

with `B(R)` diverging as `R->1+`. For the single-center corridor `R=1+lambda+o(1)`, any rate-two escape must keep `lambda` bounded away from zero.

RE-224 removes the other elementary geometric escapes for an exact positive-real single-center predictor in a fixed corridor. Uniform prediction forces

`(((m-1)-N_m/(a_m-1))_+) / (m+N_m/(a_m-1)) -> 0`.

When `lambda_m=N_m/m` is bounded above this gives `limsup a_m<=1+limsup lambda_m`; the expansion center cannot run to infinity. Together with contraction, the observed arc cannot approach `pi/2`. Since RE-223 also bounds `lambda_m` away from zero at rate two, the only remaining real single-center degeneracies are now

`alpha_m -> 0`  or  `r(alpha_m,a_m) -> 1`.

The live exact-remainder question is therefore sharply localized: derive a uniform incomplete-beta normal form in those two singular geometries, including their coupled limits, and decide whether genuine cancellation can keep the normalized Wiener rate at or below two. If both regimes retain a strict gap, the entire fixed-corridor positive-real single-center route closes. A genuinely different complex, multi-center/minimax or non-binomial basis remains a separate route toward the global unit floor.

RE-174 gives exact source identification from rich Euler data, but the later results show why injectivity is not the selector-scale statement. Positive-real data can be nearly singular; finite vertical panels can be aliased; and a continuum interval forces an exponentially large signed-prefix primitive.

The global floor remains one, while a fixed real single-center interior family pays strictly more than two, a narrow finite repair corridor has its own superoscillatory surcharge, and exact prediction localizes any rate-two fixed-corridor escape to `alpha->0` or the contraction boundary. None of these representation-specific floors may be promoted to a universal `C_sep>1` theorem.
