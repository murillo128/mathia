## Constrain assembly of the continuous selector-dilation front on the genuine CA staircase

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps`, `MI-002-ordinary-sigma-rejects-composite-substitute-atoms-at-every-fixed-ca-layer`, `MI-003-fixed-threshold-cells-freeze-prime-source-data`, `MI-004-higher-layer-ca-switches-carry-quantized-residual-charge`, `MI-005-matched-first-layer-packets-conserve-the-endpoint-prime-multiset`, `MI-006-first-layer-timing-is-large-in-transport-but-summable-in-robin-metric`, `MI-007-matched-packets-convert-selector-dilation-into-chebyshev-underdensity`, `MI-008-reciprocal-mertens-recovery-forces-corrected-chebyshev-growth`, `MI-009-smooth-dilation-front-is-not-the-obstruction`.

RE-076--RE-079 reduce the late positive fan to one transport measure. RE-080 proves complementary power fronts on long same-block fans. RE-081 then removes the discrete higher-layer prime-power events and first-layer births from the leading moments, leaving continuous selector dilation as the carrier.

RE-082 closes the next natural escape: the smooth common-amplitude selector law itself already realizes the required front. Its dilation density is asymptotically `s^(-b) log s`; the unweighted moment is upper-endpoint dominated, the Robin-kernel moment is lower-endpoint dominated, and the effective selector sits at exponent `1-b`. Even the two one-sided tail bounds have the required polynomial decay.

The live theorem has therefore moved one level deeper. It is not enough to show that a positive continuous measure, an absolutely continuous measure, or even the exact local selector ODE cannot realize the front—they can. One must constrain how the **actual CA staircase assembles** those smooth arcs with fixed-state threshold cells and admissible switches. Useful inputs include supporting-slope capacity, allowable tied-span length, ordinary-prime first-layer conservation, or a source estimate limiting how much long smooth dilation can be concatenated through genuine CA transitions.
