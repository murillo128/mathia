## Resolve the terminal hierarchy in the sharp Chebyshev coordinates, not raw moments

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps` through `MI-035-chebyshev-modes-are-the-sharp-approximate-terminal-coordinates`.

RE-162 sharpens that scale to an asymptotically exact capacity theorem. Exact matching through degree `r-1` turns terminal discrepancy into degree-`r-1` uniform polynomial approximation. Chebyshev interpolation and opposite Chebyshev level-set packets meet at

`D_(r,U)(L)=(4+o(1))(L/4)^r/r!`,

so the sharp transition is `L_crit=4 tau_r`.

RE-163 identifies the correct approximate coordinates. After rescaling the terminal interval, the kernel has positive Chebyshev coefficients

`A_j=(2+o(1))(L/4)^j/j!`,

and the terminal discrepancy is the signed spectral sum `sum_j A_j chi_j`. Exact lower-moment matching kills the low Chebyshev modes and leaves precisely the RE-162 tail. Approximate matching pays through the low-mode leakage

`C_r=sum_(j<r)(L/4)^j |chi_j|/j!`,

with the unresolved tail still at the sharp `4^{-r}` scale. The generalized-source obstruction from RE-161 can have every normalized raw monomial discrepancy tend to zero while a growing Chebyshev mode remains order one, so qualitative raw-moment closeness is not a sharp stability coordinate.

The live ordinary-prime/CA question is now specific: does arithmetic control the weighted Chebyshev leakage `C_r`, force cancellation in the signed kernel-weighted low-mode sum, or prevent endpoint spectral migration at growing degree? More raw moments or unweighted approximate moment control are exhausted unless they imply one of these kernel-adapted statements.
