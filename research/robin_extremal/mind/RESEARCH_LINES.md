RE-102--RE-104 reduce the typical local geometry to ordinary consecutive-prime-gap chambers and force either near-square-root fan complexity or large physical stretch. RE-106--RE-107 then close the internal fan problem: corrected Chebyshev transport telescopes across the whole compact threshold fan, and the corresponding raw endpoint debt remains positive even at the super-stretched terminal branch.

RE-108 prices the first genuinely inter-block resource exactly. If `C<D` are colossally abundant, `h(C)>0`, `h(D)<=0`, `Y=log C`, `V=log D`, and `R_C=e^(h(C))-1`, monotonicity of `sigma(n)/n` along the CA sequence forces

`V>Y^(1+R_C)`,

hence

`V-Y >= R_C Y log Y`.

For the common quantitative false-RH blocks with threshold fan `J=[b_0,b_1]`, this yields

`X_(k+1)-X_k >>_J X_k^(1-b_0) log X_k`

between successive quantitative block starts and therefore

`N_J(T) <<_J T^(b_0)/log T`.

This is a real recurrence constraint but not a contradiction. More importantly, because `b_0<b_1`, the forced normalization corridor `X^(1-b_0) log X` is polynomially larger than the intra-fan Chebyshev debt scale `X^(1-b_1) log X` from RE-107. The corridor therefore provides more than enough room, at the level of current endpoint information, for the source phase to reorganize before the next positive block.

The live theorem is now **inside the corridor**, not at its endpoints. Couple the reciprocal-Mertens coordinate, raw/corrected Chebyshev deficit or another source phase observable while `h` falls from a quantitative positive value through zero, and prove that the standard prime source cannot both pay the forced normalization corridor and regenerate the next `b_0`-scale positive witness. A proof that merely sums the already-positive RE-107 fan increments across block endpoints cannot close the gap exposed by RE-108.
