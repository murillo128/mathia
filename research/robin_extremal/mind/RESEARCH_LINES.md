## Leave the fixed single-center negative-binomial class and change geometry

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps` through `MI-059-fixed-complex-single-center-cancellation-still-pays-the-factor-two-wiener-floor`.

The scalar Euler program separates positive-real compression from vertical-phase resolution. RE-204--RE-218 establish the continuum prediction problem and the global interval `1 <= C_sep <= 5.996390`. RE-219--RE-222 show that the positive-real single-center architecture has Wiener exponent strictly above two throughout every compact interior parameter set.

RE-223 excludes truncation collapse inside a fixed delay corridor, RE-224 excludes unbounded real centers and the upper arc edge, and RE-225 reduces every remaining positive-real rate-two degeneration to a vanishing observed arc `alpha_m->0`. RE-226 closes that singular regime by evaluating the same normalized polynomial at the anti-aligned point `z=-1`. For bounded positive real center `a<=A`,

`||F_(m,N,a)||_A >= e^(-1)(1+1/(2A))^N`,

so if `N/m` stays bounded below while `alpha_m->0` and `m alpha_m->infinity`, the normalized Wiener rate diverges.

RE-227 removes the last family-wise caveat on the positive-real fixed-corridor class. For each fixed finite corridor ratio `R>1`, diagonal extraction gives one class-level constant `eta_R>0` such that every `R`-admissible positive-real single-center family satisfies

`liminf log ||F||_A /(m alpha) >= 2+eta_R`.

RE-228 closes a different escape: exact complex cancellation at one **fixed genuinely complex center**. For fixed `alpha`, `a=rho e^(i phi)`, and `N=lambda m+O(1)`, assuming the whole symmetric arc is contracting and the true normalized polynomial predicts uniformly, exact control of the DC denominator forces `lambda` past the upper negative-binomial root `lambda_*`, while the normalized Wiener norm obeys

`liminf m^(-1) log ||F_m||_A >= lambda_* log(nu/r) > 2 alpha`, `nu=1+rho^(-1)`.

The argument does not replace complex cancellation by an absolute remainder estimate: cancellation in `Q(1)` can only shrink the normalization denominator and therefore increase the Wiener norm. Radial endpoint geometry then shows complex phase is no cheaper than the positive-real comparator at fixed modulus. At the explicit RE-218 center the resulting lower exponent is `5.995948...`, within about `4.33e-4` of the explicit `5.996380...` construction, so the near-six cost there is not mainly an artifact of termwise tail control.

The fixed-parameter complex result is not yet a class closure for drifting complex centers. The constructive frontier is therefore narrower: genuinely scale-dependent complex centers, multi-center/minimax cancellation, rational or different approximation bases, or a quantitative determination of the remaining class gaps. Merely adding one fixed complex phase or exploiting its exact DC cancellation does not leave the factor-two obstruction.

## Keep exact identification, source capacity and prediction representation distinct

RE-174 gives exact source identification from rich Euler data, but injectivity is not the selector-scale statement. Positive-real data can be nearly singular; finite vertical panels can be aliased; and a continuum interval forces an exponentially large signed-prefix primitive.

RE-217 separates the global prediction floor from prime-placement capacity. RE-219--RE-227 prove a uniform fixed-corridor obstruction for the positive-real single-center negative-binomial representation, while RE-228 proves the corresponding fixed-parameter obstruction for every contracting complex single center with linear truncation depth. These results still do **not** improve the universal separated-measure lower bound `C_sep>=1`.

A future prediction argument should therefore state which new geometry invalidates the single-center Wiener probe or leaves the exact normalized negative-binomial class before claiming progress toward the universal floor. Drifting complex parameters remain a genuine quantifier change; a fixed phase does not.
