RE-076--RE-084 reduce the late positive fan to a selector-dilation carrier and reconstruct the complete first-layer birth geometry from selected endpoints. RE-085 removes bounded fixed-order mixed-event mass as a leading escape: higher-layer normalized event mass vanishes on stretched same-block fans.

RE-086 isolates a singular survivor. If a fixed-layer event `eta_j(p)` is selected as an adjacent support boundary, its generating base prime must carry a chamber-facing neighboring gap satisfying the layer-independent normalized requirement `G_C(p,j)/(p log p) >= (1-o(1)) a(C)|I_C|`; tied boundaries impose simultaneous conditions at power-related scales.

RE-087 now rules out the simplest aggregate contradiction. Summing ordinary one-sided prime-gap capacity over a dyadic base-prime shell gives only `Theta(1/log P)` normalized gap mass, but the CA small-height law gives `a(C) log P -> 0` at fixed layer. After converting gap length into threshold width, the **marginal available capacity diverges**. There is asymptotically more than enough candidate prime-gap capacity to cover any fixed threshold interval if selection compatibility is ignored.

The live theorem must therefore be selection-sensitive. It should show that the rightmost-maximizer fan can access only a thin subset of the candidate primes, exploit joint/tied resonances across power-related scales, or use skipped-span/nonlocal adjacency constraints that cannot be represented by independent marginal gap inequalities. “There is not enough total neighboring prime-gap length” is no longer a viable route.

## Keep event mass, boundary selection, marginal capacity and joint compatibility separate

RE-085 proves vanishing higher-layer mass, not vanishing structural influence. RE-086 converts boundary selection into a local arithmetic cost. RE-087 shows that the sum of those local costs over all candidate primes is too generous to force a contradiction because the selected CA amplitude is even smaller.

Any genuine scarcity theorem must therefore constrain the **selected configuration**, not the marginal pool. Fixed-layer marginal abundance says nothing about growing depth, exact tied events, compatibility of both chamber boundaries, skipped spans or discontinuous rightmost-maximizer dynamics.
