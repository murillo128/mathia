The full prime-layer event staircase obeys the unconditional Vinogradov--Korobov PNT envelope `Phi(x)=x+O(x E(x))`, and the same event atoms generate both numerator increments and physical logarithmic mass. Robin recovery is therefore an exact quadrature defect. A positive height `h(C)` requires

`V-Y >> h(C) Y log Y / E(Y)`

unless `V` is already beyond a fixed multiple of `Y`. On the false-RH quantitative block family this strengthens the inter-block corridor to

`X^(1-b_0) log X / E(X)`
