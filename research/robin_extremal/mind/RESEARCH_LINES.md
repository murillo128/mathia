# Robin-extremal research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable robin-extremal intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Use the source-fidelity capacity matched to the spatial class of the missing source

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps` through `MI-036-quantitative-pnt-fidelity-rigidifies-the-terminal-source-before-moment-complexity`.

RE-153--RE-163 identify the exact Pareto-weighted Chebyshev-deficit destination and quantify how coarse-PNT generalized sources can retain order-one terminal ambiguity even under many exact or approximate moment constraints. RE-164 then introduces quantitative Korobov--Vinogradov source fidelity.

RE-165--RE-166 make the **remote-tail** capacity sharp. For a lower cutoff `T`,

`K_b(p,T)=sqrt(p) log p exp(-b V(T))/V(T)`,

with `V(T)=(log T)^(3/5)/(log log T)^(1/5)`. Remote-tail rigidity holds when `K_b->0`; generalized insertions staying in the same PNT exponent class attain the same scale when the capacity stays positive. The capacity-one center is a thin moving inversion contour in the intrinsic `V` coordinate, not the leading fixed-`B` exponent. The nominal equality `b kappa_B=1/2` lies on the ambiguous side.

RE-167 shows that a perturbation confined to one fixed multiplicative shell has a **different and smaller capacity**,

`L_b(p,T)=sqrt(p) log p exp(-b V(T))/log T`.

This local-shell upper bound is sharp under exact mass-balanced generalized-prime relocations that return the Chebyshev discrepancy to zero above the shell while preserving the same KV exponent class. Since `V(T)<<log T`, local shell capacity dies before the full remote tail. Its capacity-one center lies below the remote center by

`(2/(3b)) log log p + (1/(3b)) log log log p + O(1)`

in the `V` coordinate, far wider than either individual `O(1)` transition layer.

At every bounded-multiplicative post-selector scale `T=Bp`, however, `L_b(p,Bp)=p^(1/2-o(1))->infinity`. Thus appending the full unconditional KV PNT remainder to a bounded-multiplicative selector does **not** repair the RE-154 ambiguity: the required square-root-scale Chebyshev relocation still occupies a vanishing fraction of the allowed envelope.

The live ordinary-prime/CA question must therefore specify the spatial support class of the source information it actually controls. If the unresolved source can move across the whole remote tail, the relevant rigidity target is `K_b`; if the selector localizes the uncertainty to proportional shells, the lower `L_b` center is the correct gate. Moment complexity should be spent only after the appropriate capacity has been shown to remain on the ambiguous side. The missing theorem is still a source-valid coupling between the actual CA selector and one of these moving capacity contours, or a non-terminal channel that bypasses them.

## Control one-sided vertical reciprocal escape without overstating the symmetric obstruction

RE-144--RE-152 put the zero-side packet into reciprocal-scale language and show that one-sided vertical phase geometry can hide a scale-maximal target at lacunary stages while fixed-order global local-correlation statistics remain unchanged. A closure theorem on this branch must control target-conditioned exceptional geometry rather than typical-center averages.

## Keep source capacities, source-spectral ambiguity and zero-packet gates distinct

The coarse terminal moment hierarchy, quantitative local/remote source capacities and zero-side packet obstructions answer different questions. A generalized-source comparison must declare whether it preserves only `theta_Q(x)=x+o(x)` or a quantitative PNT remainder, which perturbation support class is allowed, which cutoff is used, and whether the corresponding `L_b` or `K_b` capacity actually tends to zero. Neither a sharp source-rigidity capacity nor a zero-side matched obstruction supplies the missing ordinary-prime selector theorem by itself.
