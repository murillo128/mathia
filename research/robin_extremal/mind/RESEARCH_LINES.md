## Control the CA-selected mixed race on its finite intermediate annulus

**Linked intuition:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps`.

RE-001--RE-013 reduce clean hypothetical counterexamples to CA-selected boundary primes and show that locating the selector itself consumes logarithmically growing prime-power depth. RE-014 then proves that the direct mixed-race contribution of every fixed initial window `[p,p+p^alpha]`, `alpha<3/4`, is invisible at first Robin order. RE-015 closes the opposite escape: unconditional PNT decay makes the tail beyond a finite stretched-logarithmic horizon negligible. Any clean counterexample excursion must therefore be carried by a broad but finite **intermediate annulus**.

RE-016 separates selection complexity from height calibration. Once a clean CA event is selected, its deterministic CA tax has an all-orders inverse-logarithmic normal form governed by the second prime-power layer; higher layers and event discreteness are beyond all fixed log orders. The deep tower is needed to locate the event, not to supply ordinary logarithmic corrections to its Robin height.

The live theorem should exploit the exact CA selection law to control the mixed prime race on the RE-014/015 annulus, or compress the growing selector into a statistic sufficient for that selected-annulus test. It must remain weaker than a global one-sided race bound.

## Keep selector resolution, tax calibration, and race excursion distinct

Fixed-depth layers cannot locate the selected event at its natural resolution, but this does not mean every selected height correction depends on all depths. Conversely, precise all-orders calibration of the CA tax does not control the random-looking mixed-race excursion. A useful continuation must couple the nonlocal selector to the finite annulus where the target fluctuation is now known to live.
