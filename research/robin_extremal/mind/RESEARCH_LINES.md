## Control the Pareto-weighted Chebyshev deficit with source coupling stronger than fixed-cutoff PNT data

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps` through `MI-032-self-tangent-robin-height-is-a-pareto-weighted-chebyshev-deficit-average`.

RE-153 turns the source side of the self-tangent obstruction into a canonical scale-invariant average. For a regular self-tangent counterexample with largest active first-layer prime `p`, the normalized height kernel converges under `t/p` to the Pareto law `(1/2)y^(-3/2)dy`, and the counterexample must satisfy

`E_(nu_p)[(t-vartheta(t))/sqrt(t)] >= sqrt(2)-o(1)`.

The kernel has median `4p` and a `B^(-1/2)` tail beyond `Bp`; every sublinear additive selector neighborhood of `p` carries vanishing mass. RE-154 makes the residual sensitivity quantitative. A prime-like source may agree **exactly** with the ordinary primes below any fixed cutoff `Bp`, remain PNT-dense, and differ by only `O(sqrt p)` in its Chebyshev source on the affected shell, yet relocating only

`sqrt(p)/log p`

labels after the selector changes the normalized Pareto source average by order one. The moved fraction of labels in a fixed proportional shell is only `p^(-1/2)`. The exact single-label influence at `q=yp` is asymptotically

`Lambda_p(yp) = (log p)/(2 sqrt p) * y^(-1)`.

Thus a bounded multiplicative selector plus qualitative PNT control is not merely incomplete; it leaves an explicit order-one instability channel. The live source theorem must bind the post-selector shell back to the selected CA state through genuinely ordinary-prime/CA structure, propagate control across multiplicative scales tending to infinity, or establish a signed/constant-level Chebyshev estimate strong enough to defeat this coherent `sqrt(p)/log p` surgery.

This is not a request for a global pointwise square-root Chebyshev bound. The exact kernel has already compressed the historical source dependence to one signed average; the remaining opportunity is to exploit the self-tangent host and the arithmetic coupling of the actual prime sequence rather than strengthen PNT everywhere.

## Control one-sided vertical reciprocal escape without overstating the symmetric obstruction

RE-144--RE-148 put the local packet and sufficiently-rightward actual-zeta shell into the same reciprocal-scale language. RE-151 proves that pure vertical phase geometry can hide a scale-maximal target by `exp(-cR)` on the one-sided pre-anchor window `[(1-kappa)U,U]`; the earlier symmetric formulation is not valid and the post-anchor half remains open.

RE-152 shows that such bad stages can be inserted lacunarily while preserving Riemann--von Mangoldt-scale counting, local `O(log T)` occupancy and every fixed-order globally averaged local-correlation statistic. Typical-center GUE information therefore does not exclude the exceptional target-conditioned packet.

A closure theorem on this branch must still reverse the local-vs-right-shell exponent ordering or change that gate, control the near-left/vertical phase geometry with a target-conditioned theorem, and determine whether the post-anchor half supplies a symmetric-window obstruction that the matched control cannot evade. Global fixed-order averages are too weak unless upgraded to genuinely uniform or sufficiently strong large-deviation control.

## Keep the two source gates distinct

RE-153--RE-154 give a direct prime-side condition and its sharp matched-source sensitivity: the self-tangent Robin kernel consumes a Pareto-weighted Chebyshev deficit and is order-one sensitive to a vanishing fraction of coherent post-selector relocations. RE-151--RE-152 describe a zero-side matched obstruction to one reciprocal-scale observation route. Neither subsumes the other. Progress should be credited only when it changes the exact source quantity consumed by its own destination: arithmetic coupling of the selected Chebyshev-deficit average on the self-tangent branch, or target-conditioned zero geometry on the reciprocal-packet branch.
