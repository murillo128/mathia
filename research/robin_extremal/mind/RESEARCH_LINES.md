## Control the Pareto-weighted Chebyshev deficit inside a shrinking terminal layer or with stronger arithmetic coupling

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps` through `MI-032-self-tangent-robin-height-is-a-pareto-weighted-chebyshev-deficit-average`.

RE-153 turns the source side of a regular self-tangent Robin counterexample into the canonical normalized average

`E_(nu_p)[(t-vartheta(t))/sqrt(t)] >= sqrt(2)-o(1)`.

Its kernel has a Pareto scaling law and places substantial mass far beyond the selector prime. RE-154 quantifies the resulting sensitivity at fixed multiplicative distance: only `sqrt(p)/log p` coherent post-selector relocations can move the normalized source coordinate by order one while preserving exact agreement below a fixed multiple of `p` and PNT-scale density.

RE-155 now pushes that matched-source obstruction to the full canonical Robin horizon `U_A(p)`. Equal-cardinality relocation cancels the leading upper-endpoint term in the exact influence, leaving

`Lambda_p(yU)-Lambda_p(zU) ~ [sqrt(p) log p/(2U)](1/y-1/z)`

for fixed `0<y<z<1`. Consequently `U/(sqrt(p)log p)` relocations confined to a fixed terminal fraction of the horizon can shift the source average by order one while the source agrees exactly with the ordinary primes and generalized CA event history through `cU` and remains PNT-indistinguishable.

Since `c<1` is arbitrary, **exact propagation through every fixed fraction `(1-epsilon)U_A(p)` is still insufficient at PNT information level**. Merely letting the propagation radius tend to infinity no longer addresses the live gate.

The residual source theorem is therefore genuinely terminal. It must either control a layer whose relative width tends to zero, derive stronger short-interval/signed information that forbids the coherent relocation inside that layer, or bypass propagation with an exact ordinary-prime/CA identity controlling the Pareto-weighted deficit directly. The matched controls do not claim that ordinary primes admit the surgery; they specify the information a successful theorem must use to rule it out.
