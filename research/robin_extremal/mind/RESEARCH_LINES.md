## Find a selector-sensitive source invariant stronger than prime support, integer multiplicity, exact Mertens matching and KV fidelity

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps` through `MI-037-mertens-normalization-turns-local-robin-shifts-into-tail-debt`.

RE-153--RE-168 identify the Pareto-weighted Chebyshev-deficit destination and quantify how much post-selector Robin motion remains possible under quantitative Korobov--Vinogradov source fidelity. The intrinsic decay length `ell_KV(T)=log T/V(T)` gives the spatial capacity scale: a bounded-multiplicative or subpower region can still carry order-one normalized displacement while enough future capacity remains.

RE-169 identifies Mertens normalization as an exact conservation law for the same Robin kernel. If two sources agree below a selector, their Mertens-constant difference equals the entire future Robin-kernel debt. RE-170--RE-171 show that over flexible generalized sources even exact `Gamma(Q)=gamma` does not localize that debt: finite continuously movable shells can create an order-one intermediate excursion and later repay it exactly while respecting the KV envelope.

RE-172 finds a real but finite-support arithmetic rigidity. A finite perturbation on ordinary prime labels with rational/integer multiplicity changes cannot preserve exact Mertens normalization unless it is trivial; unique factorization gives an injective finite coordinate.

RE-173 shows that this is **compactness rigidity, not selector rigidity**. With infinite ordinary-prime support and multiplicities only in `{0,1,2}`, one can delete about `sqrt(p)/log p` primes just beyond the selector, create an order-one normalized Robin excursion, and repay the Mertens debt exactly by an absolutely summable infinite tail of duplicated ordinary primes. The resulting Chebyshev discrepancy is only `O(sqrt(p)+(log x)^2)`, hence smaller than every fixed KV envelope. Prime support, integer multiplicity, exact `Gamma=gamma` and strong PNT fidelity still do not determine the selected finite prefix.

The surviving source-faithful gate is therefore stronger than all of those aggregate conditions. The actual prime source has **multiplicity exactly one at every prime**, not merely integer multiplicity on the prime lattice. A useful theorem must consume a selector-sensitive invariant that the delete/duplicate infinite tail cannot fake: exact multiplicity-one support, another nonlocal arithmetic relation tied to the ordinary Euler product, or a zero-side condition incompatible with delayed compensation. Merely improving scalar Mertens precision or KV decay is closed as a route.

## Keep source capacity, global normalization, support arithmetic, multiplicity fidelity and zero-packet gates distinct

Quantitative PNT/KV fidelity, exact Mertens normalization, ordinary-prime support, integer multiplicity, multiplicity-one fidelity and the zero-side packet obstruction answer different questions. RE-172 shows finite prime-lattice injectivity; RE-173 shows that allowing a noncompact `{0,1,2}` multiplicity tail reopens exact compensation without paying KV scale. Future matched controls must state every one of these quantifiers explicitly before a source condition is treated as arithmetic leverage.
