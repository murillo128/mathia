RE-112--RE-118 reduce the attained finite-edge branch to the almost-periodic edge profiles `F_Theta`, `G_Theta` and `J=F+G`; endpoint, first recovery and block maximality all become one weighted stable-filter compatibility packet.

RE-119 closes the leading-order placement interpretation. For every sufficiently large regular colossally abundant state `C`, with `Y=log C` and `nu=log Y`,

`Y^(1-Theta) nu h(C) = J(nu)+o(1)`,

while consecutive regular-CA phases have mesh finer than every inverse power of `nu`. The actual CA staircase therefore samples the full continuous `J` profile at leading edge resolution.

RE-120 now closes every **fixed algebraic refinement** of that idea under a horizontally isolated attained edge. For each fixed `K`,

`B_Theta(C)=J(nu)+sum_(k=1)^K (-1)^k k! J_k(nu)/nu^k+O(nu^(-K-1))`,

and each `J_k` is a deterministic stable-filter iterate of `J`. Thus neither the `1/nu` correction nor any fixed power of `1/nu` supplies an independent CA-placement coordinate when the edge has a fixed horizontal gap below it.

The live finite-edge question has therefore bifurcated. Under horizontal isolation, fixed-order CA-height asymptotics are exhausted as a source of new arithmetic selection; a contradiction must use information beyond that stable-filter hierarchy. Without isolation, the first unresolved source is a shrinking near-edge spectral boundary layer: influence at algebraic order `nu^-K` can only come from zeros with

`Theta-Re(rho) <=~ K log(nu)/nu`.

The natural continuation is to control that near-edge population, its phases or its interaction with CA selection, rather than compute more fixed algebraic terms. If the edge is not attained, RE-113's separate obstruction remains. The `Theta=1` branch remains separate.

## Keep the exact binary tie local

The surviving local first/depth-two tie remains a Four-Exponentials contingency. The edge-profile and all-orders isolated-edge expansions do not require resolving it. A global argument should remain robust to that binary alternative unless exact transcendence becomes load-bearing.

## Keep stable-profile universality, spectral-edge structure and discrete arithmetic selection distinct

RE-119 shows that the regular CA mesh is already finer than every algebraic correction scale, and RE-120 shows that under edge isolation the complete fixed-order correction hierarchy is reconstructible from the same leading profile. Therefore finer asymptotic resolution alone does not restore discrete arithmetic information. A genuinely new selector must come from near-edge spectral accumulation, a source coordinate outside the stable-filter closure, or another mechanism not encoded by the isolated edge profile.
