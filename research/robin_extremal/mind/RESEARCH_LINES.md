## Use prime-gap capacity only above its sharp exponent threshold

**Linked intuition:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps`.

RE-049--RE-050 show that the isolated nonzero fringe/base-two channel is lacunary, summable, and threshold-negligible.

RE-051--RE-052 give a stronger capacity law: every positive-width exposed cell forces a proportionally long same-scale prime-free interval, and those costs add across the fan. Combined with a prime-in-short-interval exponent `theta`, this yields fan complexity when `Theta>theta`.

RE-053 proves that this threshold is sharp for the entire information class. When `Theta<=theta`, one-cell generalized-prime controls can satisfy the exact adaptive and first-layer formulas, PNT-scale density, and gaps `o(T^theta)`. No rearrangement of support geometry plus the same metric gap exponent can close the near-critical band.

The live near-critical theorem must therefore exploit a **genuinely ordinary-prime common-source relation** absent from the generalized controls, rather than seek a stronger bookkeeping form of the existing fan-capacity splice.

## Treat fringe capacity, metric prime-gap capacity, and ordinary-prime source identity as separate currencies

The fringe channel is too sparse; metric prime-gap capacity is decisive only above its exponent threshold; below it the missing discriminator must use arithmetic structure that generalized prime labels cannot reproduce.
