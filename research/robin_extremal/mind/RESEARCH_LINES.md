## Find a selector-sensitive source invariant between one boundary charge and coefficient-by-coefficient Euler recovery

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps` through `MI-038-high-temperature-euler-rigidity-is-exact-but-exponentially-conditioned`.

RE-153--RE-168 identify the Pareto-weighted Chebyshev-deficit destination and quantify how much post-selector Robin motion remains possible under quantitative Korobov--Vinogradov source fidelity. The intrinsic decay length `ell_KV(T)=log T/V(T)` gives the spatial capacity scale: a bounded-multiplicative or subpower region can still carry order-one normalized displacement while enough future capacity remains.

RE-169 identifies Mertens normalization as an exact conservation law for the same Robin kernel. RE-170--RE-171 show that exact global charge does not localize it over flexible generalized sources. RE-172 adds finite ordinary-prime rigidity, but RE-173 proves that this is compactness rigidity: an infinite `{0,1,2}` delete/duplicate tail on ordinary prime labels can match `Gamma=gamma` exactly, stay below every fixed KV envelope and still create an order-one transient Robin excursion.

RE-174 closes the opposite extreme. For any bounded multiplicity perturbation on ordinary primes, the Euler-product discrepancy

`D_Q(sigma)=log Z_Q(sigma)-log zeta(sigma)`

is an absolutely convergent Dirichlet series for `sigma>1`. Equality on **any unbounded sequence** `sigma_j->infinity` forces every prime multiplicity to be one. If `P` is the first modified prime, then

`D_Q(sigma)=c_P P^(-sigma)(1+o(1))`.

Thus the noncompact Mertens null direction of RE-173 cannot preserve an unbounded high-temperature Euler profile. Infinite prime support is not an obstruction to exact source identification once the observation hierarchy exposes the earliest changed Euler factor.

The price is conditioning. A selector-scale defect at prime `P` is visible at temperature `sigma` only at scale `P^(-sigma)`. Exact high-temperature data therefore provide a complete multiplicity fingerprint by almost coefficient-by-coefficient recovery, but not a robust Robin-scale selector. The live source-fidelity question lies between the two extremes: determine whether a **finite or otherwise economically conditioned Euler/source family** can rule out the RE-173 delayed compensation, or whether any such finite-dimensional summary can still be matched by a KV-faithful infinite prime control.

If finite fixed Euler temperatures remain matchable, the search should move toward a selector-conditioned occupancy relation, a local prime-count invariant, or another source coupling whose stability scale is commensurate with the Robin destination rather than exponentially smaller.

## Keep source capacity, exact identification and usable conditioning distinct

Quantitative PNT/KV fidelity, exact Mertens normalization, ordinary-prime support, integer multiplicity, multiplicity-one fidelity, finite-temperature Euler data, the full high-temperature Euler germ and the zero-side packet obstruction answer different questions. RE-173 shows strong coarse fidelity plus one exact boundary charge is non-rigid; RE-174 shows an unbounded exact Euler hierarchy is fully rigid but exponentially ill-conditioned. Future matched controls must state both **what is identified** and **at what precision/temperature cost** before source rigidity is credited as Robin leverage.
