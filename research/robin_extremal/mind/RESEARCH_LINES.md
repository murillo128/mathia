## Exclude the bounded-packet escape architectures of the higher-layer charge law

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps`, `MI-002-ordinary-sigma-rejects-composite-substitute-atoms-at-every-fixed-ca-layer`, `MI-003-fixed-threshold-cells-freeze-prime-source-data`, `MI-004-higher-layer-ca-switches-carry-quantized-residual-charge`.

RE-049--RE-058 progressively separate metric capacity from source fidelity. Gap geometry, synchronization, and integer labels can be reproduced by controls; ordinary `sigma` forces local matched atoms back to genuine prime coordinates, but that alone does not make one fixed chamber information-rich. RE-059 shows that after endpoint correction a single threshold cell contains only finite source data.

RE-060 identifies the first source-bearing cross-cell observable: one fixed higher-layer event leaves a nonzero quantized residual charge. RE-061 composes this law across any fixed finite event packet. If the higher-layer depths are `j_1,...,j_m`, then after normalization the leading charge is

\[
-\sum_{i=1}^m\frac1{j_i}+\chi_+-\chi_-.
\]

Interior first-layer events disappear at leading order; only the two endpoint fringe bits survive. Cancellation with bounded fixed depths is possible only for fringe change `0 -> 1` and an exact Egyptian fraction `sum 1/j_i=1`.

RE-062 removes the fixed-depth assumption uniformly for every bounded packet size. If the normalized charge tends to zero, then after subsequence extraction exactly two architectures remain: either all higher-layer depths escape to infinity with no net fringe change, or a finite bounded-depth Egyptian core sums exactly to one with fringe `0 -> 1`, while every remaining depth escapes to infinity. For fixed packet cardinality there are only finitely many possible Egyptian cores.

The live theorem is therefore no longer “understand how charges compose.” Their bounded-packet composition is classified. The next source/geometric step is to show that the actual exposed fan switches on the false-RH blocks cannot persist entirely through the exceptional architectures, or that sufficiently many nonresonant packets are forced. A complementary route may quantify what diverging layer depths imply for the physical CA event geometry and selector exposure.

## Treat metric capacity, local sigma fidelity, chamber source dimension, packet charge, and exceptional resonance as separate currencies

Genuine primality removes composite local substitutes but does not enrich a fixed chamber. New source information enters through event transitions. For bounded packets, generic higher-layer transitions carry reciprocal-depth charge; only a finite Egyptian catalogue plus subpower deep-layer tails can asymptotically hide it.

This classification is still local-in-packet. It does not prove abundance, sign coherence, or a contradiction along the global adaptive fan. The remaining progress must constrain which packet architectures the actual CA source permits, not resample the same chamber or re-derive the packet algebra.
