## Resolve the terminal moment hierarchy through the growing-order threshold

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps` through `MI-032-self-tangent-robin-height-is-a-pareto-weighted-chebyshev-deficit-average`.

RE-153 turns the source side of a regular self-tangent Robin counterexample into the canonical normalized average `E_(nu_p)[(t-vartheta(t))/sqrt(t)] >= sqrt(2)-o(1)`. Its Pareto kernel places substantial mass far beyond the selector prime. RE-154--RE-155 show that coherent post-selector source changes can move this exact destination by order one while preserving exact lower-source agreement and PNT-scale density.

RE-156 quantifies the shrinking-layer endpoint for ordinary-prime equal-cardinality relocation. In `[(1-epsilon)U,U]`, its capacity is `Cap_p(epsilon) asymp epsilon^2 sqrt(p) log p / log U`, with transition `epsilon_*(p)=sqrt(log U/(sqrt(p)log p))`. RE-157 removes the deletion-supply cap with PNT-small generalized insertions; when only cardinality is matched, first-order placement freedom survives down to `epsilon_PNT(p)=log U/(sqrt(p)log p)=epsilon_*(p)^2`.

RE-158 matches terminal count and total Chebyshev mass exactly. This cancels the linear placement moment and leaves quadratic spread, moving the ambiguity threshold back to `epsilon_*` for that more informative observation class.

RE-159 proves the full **fixed-order terminal moment hierarchy**. If two equal-cardinality insertion packets match logarithmic-position power sums through order `r-1`, the first unresolved Robin influence is the `r`-th moment and the sharp fixed-order transition is `epsilon_r(p)=epsilon_PNT(p)^(1/r)`. Prouhet--Thue--Morse matched controls show the resulting `m epsilon^r` separation is sharp for each fixed `r`.

RE-160 supplies the missing uniform growing-order correction. Put `eta_p=epsilon_PNT(p)` and let `r=r(p)->infinity`, `r=o(log U)`. Exact exponential-series control gives the universal stability scale

`tau_r(p)=(r! eta_p)^(1/r)=(1+o(1))(r/e) eta_p^(1/r)`.

Any vanishing-budget matched family is stable when its logarithmic terminal width `L` is at or below this scale in the precise sense `delta_p(L/tau_r)^r->0`. Conversely an exact subset-sum parity construction produces order-one Robin separation once `L` exceeds a universal constant multiple of `r eta_p^(1/r)`. Thus the growing-order crossover is bracketed, up to universal constants, at **`L asymp r eta_p^(1/r)`**, not at the fixed-order root law with an `r`-independent constant. The factorial gain and the explicit matched lower construction show that this dependence on `r` is structural.

The source-side question is therefore no longer merely whether finitely many moments can be added. A positive Robin route must show that the actual arithmetic source controls terminal information strongly enough to beat the **growing-order** matched-control threshold, or leave the moment hierarchy entirely through short-interval, analytic, CA/ordinary-prime, or other source-native structure. Retaining more moments is meaningful only with a theorem that prices their order and resolution uniformly; counting the number of retained moments without the factorial scale is not a fidelity statement.

## Keep the source-moment and zero-packet gates distinct

RE-153--RE-160 give a direct source-side destination and expose a hierarchy of terminal resources: ordinary local supply, first placement moment, endpoint Chebyshev mass, every fixed higher placement moment, and now the factorial resolution law when the matched order itself grows. RE-151--RE-152 give a zero-side matched obstruction to one reciprocal-scale observation route. Neither subsumes the other. Progress should be credited only when it changes the exact quantity consumed by its own destination: terminal arithmetic control strong enough to escape the growing-order moment hierarchy on the self-tangent branch, or target-conditioned zero geometry on the reciprocal-packet branch.
