RE-102--RE-107 close the relevant local/fan geometry and force positive raw Chebyshev debt across every sufficiently late quantitative fan. RE-108 shows that a positive state must traverse a normalization corridor of order `Y^(1-b) log Y` before any later nonpositive CA state can occur.

RE-109 restores the source coupling omitted by that normalization-only bound. The full prime-layer event staircase obeys the unconditional Vinogradov--Korobov PNT envelope `Phi(x)=x+O(x E(x))`, and Robin recovery becomes an exact quadrature defect. A positive height `h(C)` requires

`V-Y >> h(C) Y log Y / E(Y)`

unless `V` is already beyond a fixed multiple of `Y`.

RE-110 makes the missing sign information quantitative. Recovery carries an exact positive one-sided workload `W_+` satisfying

`W_+ >= (1+o(1)) h(C) Y^2 log Y`,

so over a corridor of length `L=V-Y` some crossed event must have forward phase defect at least of order `h(C)Y^2 log Y/L`. Near the RE-109 lower corridor scale this forces the maximal positive defect to be of order `Y E(Y)`: a fast recovery must **saturate the favorable side of the global PNT discrepancy envelope** on a CA-selected interval.

This identifies the next theorem precisely. Prove a selection-conditioned anti-saturation statement such as `M_+=o(YE(Y))`, or a coupled Chebyshev/reciprocal-Mertens analogue strong enough to rule out repeated one-sided envelope saturation on the selected recovery corridors. A global improvement to the two-sided PNT sup norm is not the issue; the required information is sign and phase conditioned by the CA selector.
