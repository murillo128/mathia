This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Robin-extremal intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Find a selector-sensitive source invariant beyond deep scalar Euler interpolation

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps` through `MI-047-growing-scalar-euler-depth-below-the-kv-shell-scale-remains-non-rigid`.

The matched-control program has progressively removed apparent scalar rigidity thresholds. Ordinary-prime `{0,1,2}` controls can preserve every fixed KV source envelope and an order-one transient Robin excursion while matching exact Mertens/Euler data at fixed depth, growing depth and separated temperatures.

RE-184--RE-186 showed that interpolation conditioning and coarse repair transport were artifacts: an adapted Chebyshev frame plus microstepped shell tracks reaches every `K=o(log p/loglog p)`. RE-187 now crosses that former scalar capacity boundary. Exact prime-power resummation permits the interpolation window to widen with depth, making the prime-power remainder exponentially small once

`W_p ~ K^2/log p`.

The repair remains ordinary-prime-supported, bounded-multiplicity and invisible to every fixed KV envelope throughout the sufficient range

`K=o((log p)^(4/3)(loglog p)^(1/6))`,

while still preserving an order-one transient Robin ambiguity. The earlier `log p/loglog p` crossover was therefore not a source-rigidity threshold.

The live scalar question has changed. The relevant tariff is now **spatial**: how wide a logarithmic prime reservoir is intrinsically necessary to match a depth-`K` Euler jet while remaining within the source-fidelity envelope? RE-187 pays `W~K^2/log p`; no matching lower bound is known. A sharper repair frame may push farther, while a lower bound on unavoidable spatial width could finally produce an honest capacity barrier.

More importantly, scalar depth itself is increasingly implausible as the missing selector. A positive route should identify a joint/nonlocal source invariant tied to the Robin destination that shellwise prime repair cannot reproduce, rather than enlarge another scalar family whose cost can migrate into a different repair coordinate.

## Keep source fidelity, exact identification and usable conditioning distinct

Finite ordinary-prime rigidity, full exact Euler-germ identification, fixed/growing jet matching, KV fidelity, weighted reset complexity and transient Robin displacement answer different questions. RE-187 shows especially clearly that a dimension threshold can disappear after the representation is reconditioned and the cost is allowed to move into spatial support.

Future proposals should therefore state the surviving source ambiguity, exact invariant family, repair width, source-fidelity cost at the furthest repair location, integer-multiplicity constraints, conditioning, and selector-normalized destination effect. A scalar family should be credited as Robin leverage only after matched controls are ruled out in **all** of those currencies, not merely because its dimension exceeds a previously observed crossover.
