RE-083 now closes the simplest ordinary-prime assembly repair. The endpoint first-layer support identity is exact on **arbitrary mixed fan segments**, not only first-layer-only packets. For any two selected states, the ordinary primes crossed by the selector equal the first-layer births between the CA states up to at most one pending endpoint prime on each side. Higher-layer events cancel because `Y-P_C=log rad(C)`.

The remaining first-layer timing mismatch is also too weak in the direct Robin workload. Although its flat area is asymptotically half the first-layer radical growth and is macroscopically large on a stretched fan, its contribution against the exact kernel derivative `g'(x)` is `O(1/Z_0)` and therefore negligible relative to the long-fan reciprocal transport scale.

The live theorem has therefore moved past both additive first-layer conservation and linear delay accounting. It must use information that survives these exact collapses: the **nonlinear placement/order of first-layer and higher-layer thresholds**, how those events constrain fixed-state chambers and tied spans, supporting-slope capacity, or another joint functional that does not factor through the endpoint prime multiset plus the summable linear timing tax.
