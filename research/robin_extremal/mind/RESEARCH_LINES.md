## Control matched first-layer packets through the coupled Chebyshev--Mertens saddle

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-robin-failure-requires-self-tangent-half-mass-event-gaps`, `MI-002-ordinary-sigma-rejects-composite-substitute-at-every-fixed-ca-layer`, `MI-003-fixed-threshold-cells-freeze-prime-source-data`, `MI-004-higher-layer-ca-switches-carry-quantized-residual-charge`, `MI-005-matched-first-layer-packets-conserve-the-endpoint-prime-multiset`, `MI-006-first-layer-timing-is-large-in-transport-but-summable-in-robin-metric`, `MI-007-matched-packets-convert-selector-dilation-into-chebyshev-underdensity`.

RE-068 makes every additive endpoint prime statistic exactly blind on a matched first-layer `0 -> 0` packet, and RE-069 shows that direct prime-to-event timing is summable after the exact Robin readout. RE-070 nevertheless finds a pre-compression geometric survivor: the tied selector interval is longer than the logarithmic state interval while carrying exactly the same Chebyshev mass, so `Z-vartheta(Z)` must increase across every sufficiently late matched packet.

RE-071 adds an independent arithmetic coordinate on the same packet. For the logarithmic Mertens-product error `E_l`, the tied common-amplitude geometry forces the opposite drift: `E_l(Z_+) - E_l(Z_-) < 0`. Quantitatively the Chebyshev increase and Mertens decrease are driven by the same selector dilation with different kernels; on sublinear packets the Mertens change is the Chebyshev change divided, to first order, by `Y log Y` and with opposite sign. The positive prime-square correction is uniformly lower order, even for packets of unbounded cardinality.

The matched branch is therefore a **two-source saddle**, not merely a Chebyshev underdensity condition. The live theorem should exploit joint restrictions on these two classical error processes along the adaptively selected packet intervals, or show that repeated packets cannot accumulate the required opposite drift while the false-RH common-amplitude regime persists. A one-coordinate short-interval estimate that is weaker than the forced scale remains insufficient.

## Treat source fidelity, selector geometry, destination compression, and joint error drift separately

Exact conservation of the prime multiset does not imply geometric neutrality. Direct timing can be summably erased while selector dilation survives, and the same dilation is now visible in two differently normalized prime error functions with opposite signs. Higher-layer switches and packet-history constraints remain distinct channels.
