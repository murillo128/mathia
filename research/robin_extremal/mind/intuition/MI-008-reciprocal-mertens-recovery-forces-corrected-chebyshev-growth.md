# MI-008 — Robin's long-fan transport fronts are carried by continuous selector dilation, not discrete higher-layer atoms

**Evidence level:** exact on sufficiently late common positive rightmost CA fans through RE-081 under the stated false-RH/long-same-block hypotheses. No contradiction with false RH, exclusion of long fans, or source-specific impossibility of the continuous front is proved.

RE-076--RE-079 show that corrected Chebyshev growth and fringe-corrected reciprocal/Mertens decrease are two moments of one canonical positive transport measure `mu`. First-layer births are the sole exact mismatch and have summable late tail. RE-080 then proves that if a selected block persists while `r=Y_1/Y_0->infinity`, the two moments are forced onto complementary moving power fronts: the effective selector lies between the exponents `1-b_1` and `1-b_0`, almost all unweighted mass lies above the lower front, and almost all kernel-weighted mass is spent below the upper front.

RE-081 identifies which part of the transport actually carries that law. The canonical measure splits as

`mu = mu_dil + mu_(>=2)`,

where `mu_(>=2)` is the complete sum of discrete higher-layer prime-power event atoms and `mu_dil` is the continuous selector-dilation transport, including the smooth tied-switch contribution. On a long false-RH fan with `b_1<1/2`, square-root prime-power ceilings give

`M_(>=2)/M -> 0`

and

`W_(>=2)/W -> 0`.

The first-layer correction is smaller still. Hence `mu_dil` carries both corrected Lyapunov changes up to relative `o(1)` and inherits the same RE-080 effective-selector window and two one-sided front estimates.

This rules out a natural but misleading source strategy. The long-fan obstruction is not generated at leading order by a sparse collection of discrete deep CA switches. Proving those switches rare, lacunary, low-rank, or small cannot remove the front. The leading source-facing object is the **continuous adaptive selector path over the actual CA staircase**.

The next leverage must therefore constrain how ordinary-prime source data drives selector dilation between and through state transitions, or independently exclude the long same-block stretch. Any contradiction that charges only discrete higher-layer atoms is asymptotically aimed at the wrong carrier.

**Boundary.** RE-081 is conditional on the same long-stretch false-RH fan regime as RE-080 and uses `b_1<1/2` critically. It does not show that `mu_dil` is source-free, that an abstract continuous positive measure cannot realize the fronts, or that long same-block fans must occur. The durable gain is the carrier classification: the forced two-front profile survives after every discrete higher-layer atom is deleted.
