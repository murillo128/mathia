# MI-008 — Reciprocal-Mertens recovery necessarily buys additional corrected-Chebyshev growth

**Evidence level:** exact on the sufficiently late positive rightmost CA fan under the hypotheses of RE-075--RE-077; no contradiction with the available false-RH prime-error envelope is proved.

RE-075 identifies the only genuine positive reset currency for the logarithmic Mertens source after a matched first-layer descent: recovering a fixed fraction at scale `W` and threshold width `w` requires `>> W^(1-b_1) w` new first-layer prime supports and physical CA span `>> W^(1-b_1) w log W`, once `W^(1-b_1)w -> infinity`.

RE-076 supplies a different global coordinate that packet complexity cannot reset,

`Hhat(C,b)=Z_b(C)-log rad(C)`.

It is monotone through cells and exposed-state switches, including arbitrary mixtures of first and higher layers.

RE-077 couples the two ledgers quantitatively. Along the positive fan, corrected Chebyshev growth is coercive in physical span:

`Hhat_1-Hhat_0 >= c_J int_(Y0)^(Y1) t^(-b_1) log t dt`.

Therefore the physical span forced by a fixed-fraction Mertens recovery carries the additional surcharge

`Hhat_* - Hhat_R >> W^(1-2b_1) w (log W)^2`.

The key point is directional: **the mechanism that repairs the reciprocal source necessarily pushes the corrected standard-prime coordinate farther in its monotone direction.** The two escape channels can no longer be budgeted independently. Higher-layer events do not subsidize the bill; their contribution to `Hhat` is nonnegative.

This does not yet contradict false RH. Since `b_1>1-Theta`, the forced exponent `1-2b_1` remains below the natural off-critical source exponent `Theta`. The next theorem must therefore be a genuine source-capacity statement: cap the accumulated corrected-Chebyshev growth available on the same positive fan, or show that repeated reset cycles force total excursion beyond that capacity.

**Boundary.** The reset surcharge uses the RE-075 divergence regime and a matched first-layer descent as the deficit being recovered. It does not prove that recovery occurs, bound the number of cycles, or supply the missing upper envelope. Its durable content is that any substantial recovery, if it occurs, has an unavoidable second-source price.
