# MI-005 — Matched first-layer fan packets conserve the endpoint prime multiset, not merely one scalar charge

**Evidence level:** exact for complete first-layer-only packets by RE-068, building on the endpoint-corrected source invariant RE-059 and bounded charge classification RE-067; no restriction on packet cardinality or contradiction with RH failure is claimed.

RE-067 shows that a bounded first-layer-only fan packet has asymptotic selected-residual charge

\[
Q=\chi_+-\chi_-+o(1),
\]

so the scalable bounded branch is the fringe-free matched transition `0 -> 0`. RE-068 shows that this cancellation is not an accident of the Chebyshev/Mertens normalization.

Let `C_-<C_+` be consecutive exposed fan states tied at the same adaptive amplitude, with tangent selectors `Z_-<Z_+`. Assume the **complete physical packet** between them is first-layer-only. Let

\[
\mathcal E=\{p:(p,1)\text{ occurs in the packet}\}
\]

and let

\[
\mathcal S=\{q\text{ prime}:Z_-<q\le Z_+\}.
\]

With the endpoint fringe indicators `chi_\pm` and their possible boundary primes `r_\pm`, RE-068 proves the exact prime-multiset identity

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal S\uplus\chi_-\{r_-\}
=
\mathcal E\uplus\chi_+\{r_+\}.
}
\]

The proof first obtains equality of the corresponding prime products from the endpoint-corrected Chebyshev invariant and frozen higher-layer mass, then upgrades that product equality to prime-multiset equality by unique factorization. No PNT error term, logarithmic-independence theorem, or bounded packet-size estimate is involved.

In the matched `0 -> 0` branch,

\[
\boxed{\mathcal S=\mathcal E.}
\]

Therefore every additive prime statistic agrees exactly between the adaptive selector interval and the physical packet. For any function `w` on the primes,

\[
\sum_{Z_-<q\le Z_+}w(q)
=
\sum_{p\in\mathcal E}w(p).
\]

Changing from `w(p)=log p` to reciprocal-prime weights, prime counts, residue-class indicators, logarithmic powers, or another additive decoration cannot reveal a mismatch that is absent at the set level. **The charge-silent packet is endpoint-source-invisible to the entire additive algebra generated by the crossed prime set.**

This remains true for an arbitrarily large pure first-layer packet. Unbounded first-layer cardinality is therefore not by itself a source discriminator. What can still distinguish the packet is information not determined by the endpoint prime multiset: the delayed physical locations `eta_(p,1)`, selector positions inside surrounding prime gaps, ordering/spacing across successive packets, higher-layer history, or a non-additive/global functional.

The first-layer-only hypothesis is essential. If a higher-layer event occurs, the frozen higher-layer source mass changes and the exact multiset law acquires a genuine source defect. The endpoint fringe corrections are also essential: they are precisely the one-prime mismatch behind the `0 -> 1` and `1 -> 0` nonzero charge classes.

The reusable lesson is stronger than “one normalized statistic can cancel.” **When source labels are genuine primes, an exact logarithmic source balance can rigidify to exact prime-set conservation by unique factorization.** Once that happens, every additive reweighting is information-equivalent at the packet endpoints; progress requires relational or positional information rather than another scalar source weight.

**Boundary.** RE-068 does not show matched packets are sparse or impossible, and it does not make them invisible to delayed event geometry, higher-layer structure, or non-additive observables. Mixed packets with higher-layer events fall outside the conservation law. The result narrows the admissible readout; it does not close the off-critical fan scenario.
