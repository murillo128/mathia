# MI-015 — Terminal source feedback makes whole-fan raw Chebyshev transport positive

**Evidence level:** supported by [RE-104](../../findings/RE-104-two-sided-gap-injectivity-forces-near-square-root-fan-complexity-or-huge-stretch.md), [RE-106](../../findings/RE-106-whole-fan-chebyshev-debt-forces-super-stretched-base-two-fringe-reset.md) and [RE-107](../../findings/RE-107-terminal-amplitude-floor-eliminates-whole-fan-chebyshev-resets.md) on the stated sufficiently late false-RH fan regime. No contradiction between different counterexample blocks or RH consequence is claimed.

RE-106 shows that the corrected Chebyshev coordinate `widehat H=Z-log rad(C)` accumulates a power-scale positive rise across every fixed compact threshold fan. Returning to the raw deficit telescopes all intermediate fringe corrections, apparently leaving one escape: a sufficiently large terminal fringe atom could erase the whole accumulated debt, at the price of super-stretch and a deep base-two host endpoint.

RE-107 closes that escape by feeding the required terminal scale back into the same false-RH selector law. For every selected endpoint with physical logarithmic scale `Y`, the tangent equation and quantitative amplitude floor force

`Z-Y >= c_J Y^(1-b_1) log Y`.

Since the raw Chebyshev deficit satisfies

`H_vartheta(Z)=(Z-Y)+P_C-delta_vartheta`,

with `P_C>=0` and `delta_vartheta<=log Z`, every sufficiently late selected endpoint carries its own power-scale raw deficit. A terminal fringe large enough to threaten the fan debt forces the endpoint scale `W` to be enormous; but then the new endpoint floor `W^(1-b_1) log W` dominates both that logarithmic fringe and the entire left-end deficit.

Consequently, uniformly in the fan's physical stretch,

`H_vartheta(Z_R)-H_vartheta(Z_L) >= c_J X^(1-b_1) log X > 0`.

The super-stretched base-two reset branch is therefore empty as a **whole-fan reset mechanism**. RE-063's classification of individual fringe hosts remains true; such a host simply cannot erase the accumulated raw debt of a sufficiently late compact fan.

This shifts the live scale from intra-fan bookkeeping to inter-block source geometry. A false-RH construction must accommodate repeated power-scale positive raw Chebyshev transport on late compact selected fans while its source oscillation still creates the next positive Robin-counterexample block. The unresolved theorem must compare those block-to-block source phases or couple the raw Chebyshev rise to the reciprocal-Mertens coordinate; another refinement of local fringe switching no longer attacks the surviving mechanism.

**Boundary.** The raw Chebyshev deficit is not globally monotone in its argument, and RE-107 does not compare the right endpoint of one positive block with the left endpoint of a later block. It eliminates internal fan reset, not global source oscillation.
