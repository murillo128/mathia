# MI-001 — Robin failure prices fan cells in prime-gap capacity, but the short-interval exponent barrier is sharp

**Evidence level:** supported under the false-RH common-block setup

## Core intuition

The isolated nonzero fringe channel is too sparse to carry a Robin contradiction: its hosts lie on a deep base-two ladder, its extracted contributions are summable, and its recurrent threshold set has Hausdorff dimension zero. Every positive-width exposed threshold cell has a stronger generic cost: it forces a proportionally long ordinary-prime-free selector interval at the same physical scale.

However, the newest matched control proves that this **support-capacity + prime-gap exponent** mechanism is already sharp. It cannot be rearranged to enter the near-critical chamber below the available short-interval exponent; a different ordinary-prime common-source relation is required there.

## Strongest justified claim

RE-049--RE-050 classify nonzero fringe exits as lacunary base-two events with summable extracted mass and threshold-dimension-zero recurrence.

RE-051 combines adaptive selector speed with a prime-in-short-interval exponent `theta`: if the rightmost zero frontier `Theta>theta`, compact intervals inside `(1-Theta,1-theta)` must fragment into many exposed states. With the currently anchored `theta=0.52`, this gives the strong off-critical chamber.

RE-052 removes the earlier half-unit loophole and factors the argument before inserting a prime-gap theorem. For every compact `J subset (1-Theta,1/2)`, an exposed cell of width `|I_C|` forces a prime-free interval of length `>> |I_C| Y^(1-b_1) log Y`; chosen intervals from different cells are disjoint, so the burden is additive in total threshold width.

RE-053 shows the exponent barrier is intrinsic to this splice. For every `theta>1/2` and `b_0>1-theta`, synthetic one-cell blocks can satisfy the exact adaptive breakpoint laws, exact first-layer CA mass/slope formulas for increasing real generalized-prime labels, PNT-scale density, and gaps `o(T^theta)`. Thus support geometry + first-layer formulas + a gap exponent `theta` cannot force fan fragmentation when `Theta<=theta`.

## Synthesis of evidence

In the chamber `Theta>theta`, prime-gap capacity remains a clean route. But the near-critical band `1/2<Theta<=theta` cannot be reached by a more careful accounting of the same metric gap input. The missing information must distinguish the **actual ordinary-prime common source** from the generalized-prime matched control—for example a relation tying the same prime labels simultaneously to the standard/reciprocal races or to another source-sensitive event statistic.

## Counterevidence / boundary cases

RE-053 is a matched generalized-prime control, not an ordinary-prime construction. It proves sharpness of an information class, not compatibility of actual primes with Robin failure.

The sparse fringe channel remains separate and too small by itself; the needed near-critical input must add information rather than merely count fringe events.

## Epistemic status

**Supported sharp capacity boundary:** each fan cell has a prime-gap cost, but the short-interval exponent threshold is sharp for any argument compressed to support geometry, first-layer formulas, gap exponent, and PNT density. Near-critical progress requires a new source-sensitive ordinary-prime relation.

## Novelty/prior-art status

CA event identities, Robin's criterion, selector geometry, prime-gap inputs, and generalized-prime controls retain their finding-level audits.

## Falsification criterion

Invalidate the RE-052 cell-to-gap law or the RE-053 one-cell matched controls. Strategically, identify an actual-prime invariant absent from the generalized controls and prove that it prevents bounded fan capacity in the near-critical chamber.
