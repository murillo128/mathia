# MI-001 — Robin failure requires deep event selection and an intermediate-scale mixed-race excursion

**Evidence level:** exact/literature-backed CA selection, logarithmic-depth selector resolution, finite-annulus localization, and all-orders tax calibration through RE-016

## Core intuition

A hypothetical clean Robin counterexample has two different nonlocal requirements. First, the CA mechanism must select the event using a prime-power tower whose depth grows logarithmically. Second, once the event is fixed, the decisive mixed prime-race excursion is neither a local gap effect nor an arbitrarily remote tail: it is forced into a broad finite intermediate annulus.

The deterministic CA height correction behaves differently. At every fixed inverse-logarithmic order it is already governed by the second layer. Deep selection complexity and height calibration are therefore distinct currencies.

## Strongest justified principle

RE-001--RE-013 tie clean counterexamples to self-tangent CA boundary primes, reduce the sign to a mixed standard/reciprocal prime race, close local-jet freedom, and show that the selector cannot be resolved at its natural scale from any fixed layer depth.

RE-014 proves that deleting `[p,p+p^alpha]` for every fixed `alpha<3/4` changes the normalized clean height only by `o(1)`. RE-015 uses unconditional PNT decay to truncate the opposite tail at a finite stretched-logarithmic horizon. Thus any threshold-crossing excursion must reside between those scales.

RE-016 resolves the CA tax itself to all inverse-logarithmic orders. Its expansion is controlled by the second-layer frontier and exponent-one Euler tail; layers `j>=3`, prime discreteness, the clean gap displacement and the small positive correction enter only beyond every fixed log order.

## Program consequence

Exploit the **joint selector-plus-annulus law**: prove that CA-selected boundary primes cannot support the required mixed-race mass on the finite intermediate annulus, or identify a compressed statistic of the growing selector that still controls that selected excursion. Do not replace this by a global race bound already at RH strength.

## Counterevidence / boundary

The annulus localization is a necessary condition, not an exclusion theorem, and the upper horizon is set by current unconditional PNT technology rather than claimed sharpness. The all-orders tax expansion does not simplify the event-selection problem and applies only in the clean regular chamber.

## Epistemic status

**Exact separation of deep event selection, all-orders deterministic tax calibration, and a finite intermediate fluctuation window; Robin's inequality is not proved.**

## Falsification criterion

Find a clean counterexample violating the selector/height identities, show a first-order threshold contribution from a forbidden short or remote region, or invalidate the all-log-orders tax expansion. A positive continuation should control the mixed race specifically on the CA-selected annulus.
