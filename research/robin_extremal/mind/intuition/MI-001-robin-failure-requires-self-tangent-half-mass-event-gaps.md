# MI-001 — Robin failure is forced onto a sparse CA-selected prime-race ray

**Evidence level:** exact CA event localization and Diophantine/prime-race reductions through RE-024

## Core intuition

The Robin route has moved from a global inequality to an exceptionally selective event problem. A hypothetical counterexample must occur at self-tangent CA boundary states, use a finite intermediate mixed-race excursion, and lie on increasingly sparse prime-power event coincidences. The remaining signal is now an explicit prime-race ray evaluated on that selected host set.

## Strongest justified principle

RE-017--RE-019 force bounded ordinary-prime envelopes into deep fixed-base event ladders. RE-020 upgrades their spacing using linear forms in logarithms: repeated close collisions require power-scale jumps, making bounded-envelope hosts extremely sparse. RE-021--RE-022 show that the self-tangent height and finite-annulus reductions survive exceptional regular boundary chambers, so the argument no longer depends on a clean first-layer configuration.

RE-023 turns the frontier displacement into a quantitative Chebyshev/Mertens race coordinate and isolates a square-root-scale dichotomy. RE-024 shows that slowly growing envelopes still leave a log-subpower selected host set. Thus both the location and the required race direction are sharply constrained.

## Program consequence

Seek a theorem on the **selected sequence**: a sign-change, large-deviation, repulsion, or incompatibility statement for the mixed race at CA event hosts. Do not spend effort proving further sparsity unless the improvement enters such a selected-event theorem.

## Counterevidence / boundary

Sparse exceptional sets can support rare arithmetic events, so sparsity is not a sign theorem. The forced prime-race ray is necessary for failure but not known impossible. Growing envelopes beyond the current regime also remain a separate case.

## Epistemic status

**Exact localization of hypothetical failure to a very sparse CA-selected mixed-race ray; exclusion of that selected arithmetic event remains open.**

## Falsification criterion

Construct an admitted Robin counterexample sequence outside the self-tangent finite-annulus/event-host geometry, or invalidate the RE-020/RE-024 sparsity bounds or RE-023 race-coordinate identity.
