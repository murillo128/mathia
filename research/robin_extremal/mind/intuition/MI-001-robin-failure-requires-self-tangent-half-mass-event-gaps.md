# MI-001 — Robin failure would require a deeply resolved CA selector plus an exceptional mixed prime-race excursion

**Evidence level:** exact/literature-backed CA selection, mixed-race reduction, local-jet closure, and logarithmic prime-layer resolution boundaries through RE-013

## Core intuition

A hypothetical Robin counterexample is not free to occur at an arbitrary extremal profile. Exact `log log n` normalization selects regular self-tangent colossally abundant states, and in the clean first-layer chamber the boundary prime, Euler-product height, and mixed prime-counting errors are tied to the same source.

The newer evidence shows that this source selection is itself highly nonlocal. The local clean mixed-race jet is already determined once the selector and one value are fixed, while the selector cannot be approximated at its natural logarithmic resolution from any fixed number of prime-power layers. The missing information lives in a growing hierarchical event tower rather than another local derivative.

## Strongest justified principle

RE-001--RE-009 force clean counterexample sequences, when they exist, onto CA-selected boundary primes and reduce the height condition to a positive square-root-scale excursion of a classical mixed prime-race coordinate. A global one-sided bound for that race is already RH-equivalent, so the useful theorem must exploit the selected subsequence.

RE-010--RE-012 close the local-jet escape. In the clean chamber the self-tangent selector fixes the sharp height threshold, imposes a value--slope relation, and determines the local prime-gap mixed-race profile from one value. Additional local derivatives do not provide an independent source coordinate.

RE-013 prices the selector's own resolution. For every fixed prime-power layer `j`, the layer mass has polynomial frontier `(jX)^(1/j)`, and any fixed truncation leaves omitted mass much larger than the logarithmic scale consumed by the clean selector. The maximal active depth is

`J_*(X)=log_2 X + log_2 log X + O(1)`.

Once the tail is purely `2`-adic its remaining mass is quantized in units of `log 2`; bounded selector error needs `J=J_*(X)-O(1)`, and error below `log 2` eventually forces exact recovery.

## Program consequence

Exploit the **joint hierarchical selection law** rather than globally control the mixed race or append fixed-depth layer corrections. A plausible intermediate theorem should either compress the growing prime-power tower into a genuinely sufficient noninvertible selector statistic, control how the selected boundary primes sample the mixed race, or force hypothetical counterexamples into the sparse exceptional event chamber.

The required compression must be proved at the selector's actual resolution. A low-depth asymptotic for the dominant layers is not enough.

## Counterevidence / boundary

RE-013 does not prove that the full selector tower is impossible to control, nor that every useful compressed statistic must reconstruct all layers. It concerns resolution of the clean CA selector, not a global impossibility theorem for other source-selected observables. The current findings also do not rule out counterexamples concentrated on higher prime-power/tied transitions.

## Epistemic status

**Exact counterexample narrowing plus an exact logarithmic-depth selector-resolution barrier; not a proof of Robin's inequality or RH.**

## Falsification criterion

Find a clean counterexample violating the RE-006--RE-012 selector/height identities, or approximate the RE-013 clean selector at logarithmic resolution from a fixed finite layer depth. A positive continuation should exploit the growing source hierarchy while remaining weaker than a global mixed-race bound.
