# MI-001 — Robin failure requires resolvable prime-power/common-race coupling across the selector fan

**Evidence level:** supported

## Core intuition

Finite spectral sign geometry and adaptive selector geometry are not selective enough on their own. A hypothetical Robin failure must carry an entire same-block adaptive selector family together with a translated standard/reciprocal prime-race law, and RE-040 now proves that this arithmetic calibration is uniform across every threshold exponent in a compact interval.

The remaining discriminator is even narrower: the selector staircase and the race coordinates must come from the **same quantized prime-power events**, and their common-source signature must be resolved at the scale supplied by the race approximation. Abstract support geometry can reproduce the fan and capacity budget; the first physical prime/event staggering is real but currently smaller than the known uniform error.

## Strongest justified claim

RE-030--RE-032 show that finite dominant off-critical packets can realize the required fixed-threshold sign cones. RE-034--RE-037 derive the adaptive selector and the exact translated nonlinear standard/reciprocal race relation for each fixed threshold.

RE-038 places all thresholds in a compact interval on the same finite counterexample block. RE-039 then proves that the convex-envelope fan, exact secant identities, small-height regime, and logarithmic intrinsic spacing can all be manufactured synthetically without arithmetic.

RE-040 removes the earlier fixed-parameter extraction gap. On sufficiently late common blocks, every maximizer is regular simultaneously for every threshold `b` in the compact interval, and the translated nonlinear prime-race residual tends uniformly to zero. The same geometry yields an exact threshold-cell capacity bound in terms of adjacent CA supporting slopes.

RE-041 shows that this stronger support geometry is still universal. Synthetic concave support staircases can realize the full chamber inequalities and exact event-selector equation. What they do not reproduce automatically is the physical quantization: one CA atom has `Delta Y=log p` and a supporting mass/slope tied to the same prime-power label `(p,j)` that also contributes to the race coordinates.

RE-042 tests the simplest first-layer manifestation. The first CA event for prime `p` occurs at

`eta_p=p+1/2+o(1)`,

while the standard/reciprocal race coordinates jump at `p`. The resulting nonlinear residual jump is positive and of size `asymp (log p)/(b sqrt p)`, but the uniform RE-040 theorem gives only an unspecified `o(1)` error. Thus the exact one-prime staggering is presently below the available resolution.

## Synthesis of evidence

The earlier “uniform arithmetic realization of the fan” gate is now crossed: RE-040 supplies it. The new boundary is a **resolution-matched common-source theorem**. Either sharpen the uniform remainder enough to see individual quantized events and control where the adaptive selector samples them, or aggregate many prime-power events into a signal that survives the square-root normalization.

Switch counts, secant geometry, support capacity, and an abstract event staircase remain controls. Even the physical half-unit first-layer staggering is only useful once the error theorem can resolve its `log p/sqrt p` signature.

## Counterevidence / boundary cases

RE-041 is synthetic and does not show that the full prime-power quantization plus the RE-040 race law can be reproduced by a non-zeta model. RE-042 addresses only the first layer and only the most local jump comparison; deeper layers or collective event statistics may have a larger or more coherent signature.

Conversely, improving the `o(1)` remainder without controlling selector placement may still fail to compare the two sides of an individual prime event. The arithmetic signal and the sampling geometry must be matched.

## Epistemic status

**Supported resolution boundary:** the adaptive fan and its translated race law are uniformly coupled on genuine counterexample blocks, abstract support/capacity geometry remains universal, and the first prime-quantized local mismatch sits at `log p/sqrt p`, below the current uniform error scale. The remaining gate is quantitative common-source resolution or aggregation.

## Novelty/prior-art status

This synthesis inherits the finding-level prior-art boundaries for CA event formulas, Robin's criterion, prime-race normalizations, and the synthetic controls. It makes no independent novelty claim.

## Falsification criterion

Construct a synthetic/non-zeta source satisfying the same prime-power quantization/common-race relation and the RE-040 uniform residual on growing blocks, or show that RE-042's first-layer staggering/jump scale is wrong. Strategically, prove a uniform remainder `o(log Z/sqrt Z)` with adequate selector-side control, or derive an aggregated quantized-event contradiction; either would cross the current gate.
