# MI-060 — Single-center drift must pay Wiener cost; same-ray serial composition does not create a new escape

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [RE-229](../../findings/RE-229-bounded-complex-single-center-vanishing-arcs-have-divergent-wiener-rate.md)--[RE-237](../../findings/RE-237-co-phased-single-center-cascades-have-exact-multiplicative-wiener-cost.md).

RE-229--RE-231 identify the microscopic transition: bounded complex drift and subcritical large-center load leave residual delay, while positive-real load past the unit threshold can flatten the local `u=m theta` profile. RE-232--RE-234 show that crossing this gate does not buy a competitive global predictor at finite load: the fixed arc can be predicted, but the normalized Wiener cost stays above the best existing separated construction.

RE-235 pushes the same audit into the super-broad regime `tau=N/(m|a|)->infinity`. Outside the inverse-center layer it proves that bounded normalized Wiener rate forces the observed arc to shrink exponentially in `tau`. RE-236 then shows that the apparent exception `|a| sin(alpha)<1` is only a proof artifact. Full contraction on the symmetric arc implies a uniform DC margin, so every contracting super-broad single-center predictor with bounded normalized rate is forced into the same exponentially thin destination regime.

RE-237 closes the most direct serial multi-center continuation of this family. Centers on one common complex ray induce the same relative coefficient phase character. Multiplying individually successful factors therefore creates no cancellation between convolution paths: the Wiener norm is exactly multiplicative, minimum delays add, and the normalized cascade rate is the delay-weighted average of the constituent rates. Adding co-phased centers cannot improve the one-center cost ledger.

The reusable audit now has five currencies: local normal-form threshold, effective predictive load, macroscopic destination width, coefficient-phase geometry and global Wiener cost. Parameter drift counts as a new mechanism only if it improves destination-visible approximation without paying the same or a worse global cost. Architectural multiplicity counts only if it changes the final coefficient interference; a same-ray serial cascade does not.

**Boundary.** RE-236 still assumes a contracting symmetric arc and the single-center truncated negative-binomial representation. RE-237 does not rule out different-phase serial products, parallel/additive/minimax multi-center synthesis, or coupled products whose factors are not individually predictors; it explicitly shows that different phases can make Wiener submultiplicativity strict. Those genuinely interfering architectures, rational predictors and other bases remain outside this obstruction. None of these results improves the universal separated constant by itself.
