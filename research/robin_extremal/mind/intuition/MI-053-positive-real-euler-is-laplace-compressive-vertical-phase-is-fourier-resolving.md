# MI-053 — Vertical Euler phase resolves within a shell, but commensurate grids alias shell index

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [RE-193](../../findings/RE-193-dyadic-laplace-compression-makes-euler-stable-dimension-logarithmic.md), [RE-199](../../findings/RE-199-full-real-euler-collapse-persists-through-sub-quarter-linear-depth.md), [RE-200](../../findings/RE-200-vertical-euler-phase-supports-linear-stable-dimension-on-one-prime-shell.md), and [RE-201](../../findings/RE-201-commensurate-vertical-euler-frames-admit-remote-shell-aliases-at-selector-debt-scale.md). The Laplace/Fourier and aliasing mechanisms are classical; the Mathia content is their exact realization by ordinary-prime Euler atoms at selector scale.

The same logarithmic prime coordinate behaves very differently along real and imaginary spectral directions. After selector-shell normalization, positive-real Euler sampling sees essentially `e^(-ux)`, so Laplace damping makes separated source locations redundant at finite precision. RE-193 quantifies the resulting low stable rank, while RE-196--RE-199 exploit the same geometry to make the complete positive real ray exponentially almost blind along honest matched controls carrying a Robin-scale transient.

Vertical motion replaces damping by phase. RE-200 places ordinary primes near an equally spaced logarithmic grid on one fixed multiplicative shell and samples dual frequencies. The exact Euler response is a diagonally weighted DFT plus a vanishing perturbation, so `K` prime atoms retain `K` uniformly conditioned Euclidean directions up to height `O(K)`. This proves genuine representation capacity inside one logarithmic fundamental domain.

RE-201 identifies the missing global coordinate. For the commensurate panel `t_k=2 pi k/W`, logarithmic position is observed only modulo `W`: multiplying a source location by `Lambda=e^(mW)` leaves every first-harmonic phase unchanged. The reciprocal-amplitude loss can be repaired by about `Lambda` ordinary remote primes. With `Lambda=Q^o(1)`, honest `{0,1,2}` multiplicities and the same KV source envelope, this creates a selector-scale packet whose debt is present between shells while every sampled exact Euler value is `o(d_Q)` after remote repair.

Thus **vertical Fourier rank is local support information, not automatically global source coercivity**. The observation must resolve both position within a shell and the shell index. A well-conditioned frame on a fixed support window can still have remote aliases once the admissible source is allowed to move across multiplicative scales.

The next complex-sampling question is therefore geometric rather than dimensional: can a noncommensurate or jittered `O(K)` height design force every approximate remote alias far enough away that prime availability, integer multiplicity or KV fidelity becomes too expensive? Two incommensurate panels lead naturally to a simultaneous Diophantine approximation problem coupling spectral irregularity to repair distance.

**Boundary.** RE-201 does not invalidate RE-200; it shows that its singular-value bound is conditional on one shell. The alias result is approximate at `o(d_Q)`, uses a multiplicatively remote shell, and does not cover a continuum of imaginary heights. It also does not by itself realize every exact Robin matching constraint or prove a destination displacement for that packet.
