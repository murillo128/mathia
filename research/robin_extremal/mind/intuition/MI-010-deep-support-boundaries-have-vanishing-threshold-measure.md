# MI-010 — Deep layers and same-depth higher-layer chambers become threshold-negligible; mixed low-depth selection remains

**Evidence level:** exact under the off-RH exponent-gap hypotheses of RE-088, strengthened by the same-depth chamber theorem RE-089 when `Theta>3/5`. The result narrows the positive-threshold-measure carrier but does not eliminate first-layer-touching or mixed-depth low-layer cells.

RE-086 turns selection of a CA layer-`j` event as an adjacent support boundary into an ordinary-prime-gap cost. RE-087 shows that the total marginal one-sided gap pool is too abundant to force a contradiction when selection compatibility is ignored. RE-088 instead weights selected cells by depth: if `delta=1-b_1-theta>1/r`, the total threshold measure of cells touching depth-`>=r` boundaries tends to zero. Under a hypothetical exponent gap `Theta>theta`, sufficiently deep layers are therefore negligible.

RE-089 attacks the low-layer obstruction that ambient depth counting cannot remove. If both adjacent support boundaries of an exposed chamber come from the same fixed higher layer `j>=2`, there is no first-layer event inside the physical chamber. Pulling that chamber back through the first-layer event map places it inside one ordinary consecutive-prime gap whose length is at least square-root scale. Heath-Brown's total-length bound for such large gaps then makes the total threshold measure of every fixed `j/j` chamber family vanish whenever `Theta>3/5`.

The important case is `j=2`: pure second-layer chambers are no longer a free square-root-density carrier above the `3/5` frontier. Combining RE-088 and RE-089, outside vanishing threshold measure the surviving finite-depth cells must either touch a first-layer atom on at least one boundary or have genuinely different higher-layer depths. In the untied case the residual carrier is a finite mixed-depth resonance `2<=j_-!=j_+<r`.

This changes the next theorem. Improving deep-layer counting is no longer the relevant move, and treating each low layer separately misses the surviving coupling. The useful target is joint left/right selection compatibility: first-layer contact, mixed-depth resonance, tied power events, skipped spans, and rightmost-maximizer constraints inside the finite low-depth chamber.

**Boundary.** RE-089 is conditional on `Theta>3/5` and on its stated late positive-block setup; it does not remove all higher-layer events or prove RH. Same-depth scarcity does not imply mixed-depth scarcity, and cells touching the first layer remain a separate carrier.
