# MI-009 — The long-fan front is native to smooth selector dilation; the obstruction must enter at CA assembly

**Evidence level:** supported by RE-080--RE-082 under the stated long same-block fan normalization. RE-082 is an exact/asymptotic negative control for the common-amplitude selector law, not a construction of a genuine false-RH CA fan.

RE-080 shows that a long same-block fan forces complementary power fronts in the positive transport measure: unweighted mass moves toward an upper front while the reciprocal-weighted moment stays near a lower front. RE-081 removes the obvious discrete explanation by proving that higher-layer prime-power atoms and first-layer births are negligible at leading order; the continuous selector-dilation component carries both moments.

RE-082 tests whether smoothness of that continuous component is itself too rigid. It is not. On the same late common-amplitude selector interpolation, with `0<b<1/2`, the pushed-forward dilation measure has density

`d nu_c/ds = b(1-b)c s^(-b) log s (1+o(1))`.

Its unweighted mass is upper-endpoint dominated, `M_c ~ b c T_1^(1-b) log T_1`, while its Robin-kernel moment is lower-endpoint dominated, `W_c ~ (1-b)c T_0^(-b)`. The effective selector therefore sits at exponent `1-b+o(1)`, exactly the complementary exponent required by RE-080. More strongly, both unwanted tails around the crossover are polynomially small.

Thus positivity, absolute continuity, the exact `g(s)=1/(s log s)` duality, the common-amplitude selector ODE, and its regularly varying density are all compatible with the forbidden-looking front. A density-only or smoothness-only contradiction cannot close the long-stretch branch.

The remaining information is discrete **assembly**, not continuous transport shape: genuine CA states must splice smooth tied arcs with fixed-state threshold cells while obeying supporting-slope admissibility, ordinary-prime labels, first-layer conservation where applicable, and the available transition-span/capacity budget. Any contradiction must show that the actual staircase cannot assemble enough of the smooth `s^(-b) log s` profile over a long fan, or independently rule out the long same-block branch.

**Boundary.** RE-082 extends the exact local selector equation over one formal long common-amplitude arc; it does not impose every integer CA state or prove such an arc occurs. The negative result is precisely that local smooth selector geometry is not the missing rigidity.
