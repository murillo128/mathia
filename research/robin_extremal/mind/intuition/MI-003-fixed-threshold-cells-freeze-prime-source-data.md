# MI-003 — A fixed Robin threshold cell carries frozen prime-source data

**Evidence level:** exact source identities from RE-059, within the established CA support-chamber framework.

A positive-width adaptive threshold cell does not provide a continuum of independent standard/reciprocal prime observations. Fix one selected colossally abundant state `C`, write `Y=log C`, and let `Z=Z_C(b)` move through the open support chamber as the threshold parameter `b` varies. The state and its higher-layer summaries remain fixed throughout that chamber.

RE-059 shows that after removing the unique possible first-layer endpoint mismatch, both prime-source coordinates are exactly constant:

\[
\vartheta(Z)-\delta_\vartheta(Z)=Y-P_C,
\]

and

\[
M(Z)-\delta_\ell(Z)
=\gamma+h+T(C)+\log\log Y.
\]

These are simply the standard and reciprocal statistics of the fixed active prime set of `C`. The support chamber contains at most one ordinary fringe prime, so without the correction the raw pair `(\vartheta(Z),M(Z))` has at most two values: one fixed source vector plus a single binary prime atom that can switch once as `Z_C(b)` increases.

Therefore the continuously varying normalized race coordinates inside one cell are a **reparameterization of frozen arithmetic data**, not new source samples. Their variation comes from the external coordinate `Z`, its normalization, and the tangent selector. Reusing many `b` values inside the same cell cannot amplify arithmetic information by sample count.

The source can genuinely change only at CA support events. After the local `sigma` fidelity results force admissible substitute atoms back to genuine primes, the surviving near-critical information is consequently cross-cell: the ordering and type of boundary events, cumulative first/higher-layer mass, dependence between successive standard and reciprocal updates, or another global relation among the genuine prime coordinates.

**Boundary.** Cell width still has geometric force: through the selector map it can imply a large prime-free interval. RE-059 only says that width is not additional *source dimension* inside the fixed chamber. It does not bound the number of cells or rule out information accumulated across their boundaries.
