# MI-062 — Finite Taylor cascades obey a max-plus error law even with DC-unsafe factors

**Evidence level:** exact geometric-tail synthesis from [RE-240](../../findings/RE-240-symmetric-mixed-phases-have-a-sharp-exponential-wiener-discount.md), [RE-244](../../findings/RE-244-fixed-multicenter-taylor-products-have-a-max-plus-failure-envelope.md), and [RE-245](../../findings/RE-245-dc-unsafe-fixed-taylor-cascades-have-a-rigid-normalization-envelope.md).

Different complex center phases can make the **coefficient Wiener norm** of a product exponentially smaller (RE-240). They do not enable cancellation of a geometrically failing factor into a uniformly successful *normalized* product for a fixed number of Taylor sections with fixed center and truncation parameters in the strict first-omitted-term regime.

Write `Q_(j,T)=1-E_(j,T)`, let `tau_j(theta)` be each factor's Taylor error exponent on the observed arc and `s_j>0` its normalized truncation load. Define `F(theta)=sum_j s_j[tau_j(theta)]_+`, `D=F(0)`, and `M=max_arc F`. RE-245 refines the DC-safe RE-244 result to the exact pointwise **normalized** growth exponent `F(theta)-D` and uniform-error exponential envelope `M-D>=0`. If `M>D`, the uniform error grows exponentially. If `M=D>0`, a finite positive logarithmic-potential rigidity argument still prevents convergence to one: the normalizing denominator can suppress magnitude but cannot flatten the entire nonconstant arc profile. Consequently `||G_T-1||_(infty,arc)->0` iff **every** `tau_j(theta)<=0` throughout the arc. The critical equality case has algebraically vanishing remainder, not exponential growth.

This is a **fixed finite Taylor/geometric-tail** obstruction, not a universal lower bound against changing numbers of factors, continuous center measures, nongeometric prediction, parallel addition or other architectures. In particular, Wiener submultiplicativity is not evidence of a better predictor until its *uniform functional error* survives DC normalization.
