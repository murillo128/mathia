# MI-054 — Continuum vertical hiding pays an exponential signed-prefix tariff

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [RE-203](../../findings/RE-203-arbitrary-finite-vertical-euler-panels-admit-multishell-aliases.md) through [RE-212](../../findings/RE-212-kaiser-bessel-powers-reach-every-subunit-separated-vertical-coercive-exponent.md). The compact-support Fourier/spectral-gap geometry is classical; the Mathia content is its exact pullback to separated ordinary-prime Euler repairs at selector scale under KV source fidelity.

Finite vertical Euler panels can be globally aliased even when they are well conditioned on one prime shell. The qualitative change occurs only when the destination asks to hide a whole interval `|t|<=T` while the repair source is separated from the local packet by a fixed logarithmic gap `H`. Then uncertainty is no longer a rank question: the gap forces an exponentially large weighted cumulative primitive.

RE-209 identifies the physical primitive after pulling the generic harmonic statement back to ordinary primes. The exponentially weighted primitive is exactly a signed prime-count prefix, and partial summation converts it into a Chebyshev-prefix excursion. Thus the continuum observation width is priced in a source currency that fixed Korobov--Vinogradov fidelity constrains.

RE-210 sharpens the repeated-uniform test to the optimized sinc-tail rate `gamma_*=0.5979019108...`; RE-211 shows that a shaped Blackman base improves this to `0.7415820315...`. RE-212 removes the dependence on either ad hoc constant. For the normalized continuous Kaiser--Bessel family,

`gamma_beta=log(sinh(beta)/beta)/beta -> 1`.

Hence, for every fixed `eta>0`, the same positive compact convolution mechanism forces

`log R >= ((1-eta)+o(1)) H T`.

For selector-scale ordinary-prime repairs preserving every fixed KV envelope, this excludes `T_Q=c log Q` for every `c>1/(2H)`. At the current gap `H=log 8`, every `c>0.2404491734...` is impossible, while explicit ordinary-prime hiding is constructed below `0.0289079...`. The endpoint `c=1/(2H)` is not excluded: the argument chooses a fixed Kaiser parameter after fixing a subunit target rate and does not justify taking that parameter to infinity with `Q`.

The reusable distinction is between **local spectral rank and global continuum source cost**. A finite panel asks whether source vectors are distinguishable at sampled phases; a continuum interval plus support separation asks how much weighted signed prefix must be generated to approximate an entire Fourier profile. The latter can be coercive even though every finite panel remains aliasable.

The obstruction-side constant is a window-design quantity, not a property of sinc or Blackman. For a nonnegative probability density `w` supported on `[-1,1]`, the convolution-power mechanism is governed by

`Gamma(w)=sup_a[-log sup_{|x|>=a}|w_hat(x)|]/a`.

RE-212 proves `sup_w Gamma(w)>=1` in the supremal sense because the Kaiser--Bessel rates approach one from below. It does not prove `Gamma(w)<=1`, equality of the supremum, or attainment by any fixed window. The next coercive question is therefore an upper-bound/extremal problem, not another small sidelobe optimization.

The remaining gap is already in the correct source currency. On the constructive side, a better repair must directly control signed weighted prefixes rather than merely total variation. On the obstruction side, an upper bound on `Gamma` would decide whether the unit rate is the true limit of the present positive compact-test method. Either improvement changes the physical source budget rather than the nominal number of vertical samples.

**Boundary.** The coercive theorem assumes a fixed pre-repair logarithmic gap, ordinary-prime edits with the stated integer multiplicities, selector-scale local mass and fidelity to every fixed KV envelope. The Kaiser parameter is fixed after choosing a target rate below one. The result does not prove Robin, eliminate repairs without a fixed separation gap, exclude the endpoint `c=1/(2H)`, or show that a hypothetical Robin counterexample necessarily supplies the required continuum vertical observation.
