# MI-004 — Bounded higher-layer CA packets have reciprocal-depth charge, and fringe cancellation is locked to lacunary base-two successors

**Evidence level:** supported by the exact adjacent/bounded-packet asymptotics RE-060--RE-063; global frequency/forcing of the surviving packet architectures remains open.

Inside a fixed CA support chamber, RE-059 shows that endpoint-corrected standard and reciprocal prime sources are frozen. RE-060 shows where new source information enters: one higher-layer boundary event produces a normalized nonzero residual charge.

RE-061 identifies the finite-packet composition law. For a bounded packet whose higher-layer event depths are `j_1,...,j_m`, the selected residual jump has leading charge

\[
-\sum_{i=1}^m\frac1{j_i}+\chi_+-\chi_-.
\]

Interior ordinary first-layer events disappear at leading order; only reciprocal higher-layer mass and the two endpoint fringe bits survive. RE-062 makes this uniform when the bounded number of depths move. If the charge tends to zero, subsequence compactness leaves only two possibilities: all depths diverge with zero fringe change, or a fixed finite Egyptian core has reciprocal sum one with fringe change `0 -> 1`, while every other depth diverges. For each fixed packet-size bound the Egyptian catalogue is finite.

RE-063 adds a source-geometric rigidity not present in that algebraic classification. Whenever a selected state has nonzero endpoint fringe correction, its immediate physical successor is forced to satisfy

\[
\frac DC=2.
\]

The outgoing event is one deep base-two layer `\eta_{2,j}`, and distinct selected fringe hosts inject into distinct depths. Their event coordinates are asymptotically lacunary with ratio at least two, so only `O(\log X)` occur below coordinate `X`; the corresponding recurrent threshold support has Hausdorff dimension zero.

The Egyptian cancellation branch is therefore not freely repeatable. Its required upper fringe bit `\chi_+=1` places the packet endpoint on this lacunary host family and hands the next physical motion a diverging-depth base-two atom. This turns a finite reciprocal-depth identity into a deterministic successor constraint. In the deep-layer zero-fringe branch no such endpoint lock is supplied, so it remains a genuinely different escape.

The global question is now to compose these facts along the exposed fan: either show that repeated Egyptian cancellations are incompatible with their forced base-two successors, or control the fringe-free deep-layer escape strongly enough to recover nonzero charge/coherence. Packet algebra alone is finished; the remaining information is in the source-selected successor order.

**Boundary.** RE-063 does not prove that Egyptian packets occur, that base-two successor locks yield a contradiction, or that the fringe-free deep-layer branch is impossible. The lacunarity and zero-dimensionality statements concern selected nonzero-fringe hosts on the late common fan, not arbitrary CA states.
