# MI-004 — Bounded higher-layer CA packets carry reciprocal-depth charge with two escape architectures

**Evidence level:** supported by the exact adjacent and bounded-packet asymptotics RE-060--RE-062; global frequency/forcing of the packet architectures remains open.

Inside a fixed CA support chamber, RE-059 shows that endpoint-corrected standard and reciprocal prime sources are frozen. RE-060 shows where new source information enters: one higher-layer boundary event produces a normalized nonzero residual charge.

RE-061 identifies the finite-packet composition law. For a bounded packet whose higher-layer event depths are the fixed multiset `j_1,...,j_m`, the selected residual jump satisfies

\[
\frac{b\sqrt Y}{\log Y}
\bigl(\mathcal R_b(Z_+)-\mathcal R_b(Z_-)\bigr)
=
-\sum_{i=1}^m\frac1{j_i}
+\chi_+-\chi_-+o(1).
\]

The mechanism is source-separated. Interior ordinary first-layer events cancel from the leading scale; only the total higher-layer mass and the two endpoint fringe bits survive. A fixed finite-depth packet can therefore cancel at leading order only when

\[
\chi_-=0,
\qquad
\chi_+=1,
\qquad
\sum_i\frac1{j_i}=1.
\]

The one-event case cannot cancel; the first resonance is `(2,2)`, with other examples such as `(2,3,6)` and `(3,3,3)`.

RE-062 makes the law uniform when the bounded number of layer depths themselves move. If `j_{i,n}` are the higher-layer depths, then

\[
Q_n=-\sum_i\frac1{j_{i,n}}+\chi_{+,n}-\chi_{-,n}+o(1)
\]

uniformly for fixed packet-cardinality bound. If `Q_n->0`, subsequence compactness leaves only two possibilities:

1. **deep-layer escape:** the fringe difference is zero and every higher-layer depth tends to infinity, so all reciprocal-depth charge disappears and the generating primes are `Y^{o(1)}`;
2. **Egyptian-core escape:** the fringe changes `0 -> 1`, a fixed finite subset of bounded depths has reciprocal sum exactly one, and every other depth tends to infinity.

For each fixed packet-size bound, only finitely many Egyptian cores exist. Thus bounded packets cannot hide charge by continuously tuning arbitrary finite layers: cancellation is confined to a discrete finite catalogue plus subpower deep-layer tails.

The global question is now geometric/source-selective rather than algebraic. One must show that actual exposed fan switches cannot remain entirely in these exceptional classes, prove that nonresonant packets occur with enough frequency/coherence, or extract a contradiction from what deep-layer escape forces on the CA event geometry.

**Boundary.** RE-061--RE-062 classify bounded packets only. They do not bound packet cardinality globally, prove that higher-layer switches occur often enough, align the signs of different packet charges, or derive RH. Egyptian resonance is an allowed leading cancellation architecture, not evidence that the actual source realizes it.
