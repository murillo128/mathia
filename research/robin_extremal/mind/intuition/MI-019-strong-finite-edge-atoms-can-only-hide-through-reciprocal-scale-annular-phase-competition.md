# MI-019 — Algebraically relevant finite-edge hiding reduces to an inverse-window cancelling pair

**Evidence level:** exact consequence of [RE-125](../../findings/RE-125-invisible-algebraic-subedge-atoms-require-a-reciprocal-scale-ordinate-annulus.md)--[RE-130](../../findings/RE-130-algebraic-finite-edge-packet-mass-always-contains-an-algebraically-strong-atom.md), building on the full-packet phase transfer of RE-123. No theorem excludes the required zeta-zero pairs, the nonattained edge, or `Theta=1`.

RE-125 gives a reinforcing inner radius: companions with `|gamma-gamma_0|<=c_*/U` lie in the same target-demodulated sector and cannot cancel the target. RE-128 localizes enough comparable mass to the inverse-window annulus, and RE-129 uses a maximal atom to show that the same narrow annulus contains a distinct companion with logarithmically comparable magnitude and cancelling target-demodulated phase.

RE-130 removes the last internal alternative. Because every coefficient is bounded by `C/(1+gamma^2)` and the zero count is `O(T log T)`, the full absolute packet mass satisfies

`A_K(U) <= C sqrt(M_K(U)) log(e/M_K(U))`.

Hence `M_K(U)` is superalgebraic iff the whole packet mass is superalgebraic. An algebraic signed residual or algebraic absolute mass necessarily contains an algebraically strong dominant atom. A diffuse cloud of only superalgebraic atoms cannot be an independent algebraic escape.

Therefore every algebraically relevant hiding scenario in the attained finite-edge branch reduces to the RE-129 zero-geometry configuration: a dominant atom plus a distinct inverse-window companion whose magnitude is within a logarithm and whose phase cancels at the center. Either such configurations persist, or the packet has an algebraic continuum excursion and the CA sampling theorem sees it.

**Boundary.** RE-130 is an absolute-mass interpolation and does not exclude the required pairs. The companion need not be consecutive, may depend on `U`, and the theorem remains conditional on the attained finite edge. The important closure is internal: there is no longer a separate algebraic many-weak-mode branch.
