# MI-006 — First-layer timing is endpoint-determined, asymptotically a half-translation, and summable in the Robin metric

**Evidence level:** proved for the first CA layer through RE-084. Endpoint support reconstruction is exact on arbitrary mixed fan segments; the half-translation and nonlinear delay collapse are uniform asymptotics at late scale. No conclusion is transferred to higher-layer/cross-layer assembly.

RE-068--RE-083 first remove the obvious first-layer currencies. Matched endpoint bookkeeping reconstructs the ordinary-prime birth multiset up to two fringe states even in the presence of arbitrary higher-layer events, while the exact Robin kernel turns the macroscopically large raw delay area into a summable `O(1/Z_0)` contribution.

RE-084 shows that first-layer timing itself contains no further hidden assembly coordinate. Once the endpoint selector primes and the two fringe bits are fixed, the complete birth set `B_01` is fixed. The threshold map `p -> eta_p` is a deterministic function of the prime label, and first-layer event order is exactly prime order. Therefore the whole labeled first-layer point configuration is reconstructible from endpoint data.

At late scale the map is uniformly

`eta_p = p + 1/2 + O(1/log Z_0)`.

Hence pure first-layer chamber widths are ordinary prime gaps plus `o(1)`, and the radical-mass-weighted distribution of delays `eta_p-p` collapses to the point mass at `1/2`. Every fixed Lipschitz one-prime timing functional has the same universal leading profile. Nonlinearizing a first-layer-only statistic therefore does not recover independent CA assembly information.

The reusable distinction is between a coordinate that is geometrically present and one that is independently informative after conditioning on the endpoints. First-layer event positions matter for physical chambers, but once their prime labels are known they are a deterministic near-half-translation of ordinary-prime geometry; the exact Robin readout suppresses their linear tail even further. A genuinely new obstruction must use cross-layer/state-selection coupling, not another functional of the first-layer timeline alone.

**Boundary.** Higher-layer thresholds can interleave with first-layer events, change exponent vectors, create tied spans, or become adjacent supporting boundaries. RE-084 does not make ordinary prime gaps regular and does not control functionals whose sensitivity itself grows with scale. It proves that first-layer-only timing contributes no independent source beyond endpoint prime data; the unresolved assembly problem is joint across layers and selected states.
