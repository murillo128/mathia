After the depth-at-least-three exceptional set is removed, a typical boundary contains either just `(p,1)` or `(p,1)` together with one distinct depth-two event `(q,2)`. A third prime at the same event coordinate would contradict Six Exponentials, so no larger typical co-event survives.

The binary tie is unusually rigid. It is the exact equality

`eta_1(p)=eta_2(q)`,

or equivalently an exact prime value of the RE-096 deformation `p=x(q)`, with `q=sqrt(2p)(1+O(1/log p))`. This is not another short-interval question: the successful host-sparsity machinery controls primes near `x(q)`, whereas the tie asks whether `x(q)` itself is a prime.

Together with multiplicative independence of the distinct primes, this is exactly a two-by-two counterexample configuration for the Four Exponentials conjecture. Thus Four Exponentials would remove every such binary decoration. The corresponding three-prime multiplicity is already excluded unconditionally by Six Exponentials.

This changes the research boundary. Further local depth-two density improvements cannot settle the exact tie unless they somehow cross that transcendence frontier.
