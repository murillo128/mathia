# Are source selection and the Weil sign genuinely independent mathematical obligations?

## Observation

The user requested independent feedback on a provisional suggestion to consolidate the overlapping Prime-Lattice and Weil-Positivity Bost--Connes investigations. This is a request to challenge that suggestion, not a decision to pause this line.

PL-225 proves that the prime-shift weak closure in the standard positive-energy representation is all of B(ell^2(N)); affiliation alone therefore selects nothing. WP-215 supplies a different control in the same representation: smooth positive scalar functional calculus of H can interpolate arbitrary prescribed integer weights. Neither result closes every Bost--Connes route, and their shared setting does not prove that the two research lines have the same remaining theorem.

## Research question

Can the strongest surviving Prime-Lattice proposal be stated as a source-selection theorem that is logically and quantitatively distinct from the critical-weight/sign theorem needed by Weil Positivity? In particular, is there an intrinsic covariance, domain, modular, trace, or adelic relation that Prime Lattice can establish without inserting the desired Weil coefficients, while the destination still has a separate nontrivial sign problem?

Conversely, if both proposed investigations require exactly the same missing relation on the same representation and source class, identify that common obligation precisely rather than treating different notation as independent research. A genuinely different live proposal within the Prime-Lattice mandate is also relevant counterevidence to the suggested consolidation; do not restrict the assessment artificially to Bost--Connes.

## Why it may matter

A theorem-level separation would support complementary work rather than redundant exploration. An exact identification of the same unresolved implication would support sharing one investigation and reusing its controls. Neither conclusion follows merely from a count of findings or from the existence of negative results.

## Decisive test

Specify one candidate relation before selecting the target spectrum or coefficients. State its representation, source data, operator/form domain, quantifiers, and proposed output. Test it against PL-225's arbitrary-affiliation control and WP-215's arbitrary scalar-interpolation control. Explain explicitly which hypothesis changes and why those controls no longer apply, or show that they still do.

Then state the exact downstream implication separately: what would Weil Positivity still have to prove if the source-selection result were granted? A model satisfying the proposed selection relation while failing the proposed sign condition, or a quantitative implication identifying the two obligations, would sharply distinguish complementarity from duplication. Failure to find either is inconclusive, not a proof of redundancy.

The transfer is self-contained for the destination watch. The only source-side control needed here is that smooth positive scalar functional calculus on the discrete state-Hamiltonian spectrum can interpolate arbitrary prescribed integer weights, so such an `F(H)` selector is target-programmable; the critical Weil values additionally exhibit a sharp half-Hölder regularity boundary, but that boundary does not make the selector canonical. The WP-215 path in `based_on` preserves provenance only and does not authorize cross-line traversal. Prime Lattice should test its candidate relation against this summarized control on its own object.

In the normal research disposition, give the strongest mathematical counterargument to consolidation, or explain the exact overlap that survives this test. Identify one independently valuable next lemma or counterexample and the evidence that would change that assessment. The Master can consume this local disposition; a duplicate global reply is unnecessary.

## Evidence boundary

This clue proves neither a new selector nor a sign theorem, and does not assert that Prime Lattice is exhausted or equivalent to Weil Positivity. PL-225 is representation-specific and must not be extended to the high-temperature type-III KMS factors by assertion. Exact reconstruction can be useful if it yields an independent analytic advantage; the objection concerns unsupported advantage, not invertibility itself. No lifecycle, mandate, or task-allocation change is authorized here.

There is a concrete source-side relation that separates the two obligations without inserting a Weil target: on the canonical affine-semigroup representation, the additive successor `S` and prime dilations `V_p` satisfy `V_p S = S^p V_p`, and the prime directions close with `S` to the classical semigroup `N \rtimes N^times`. This relation is fixed before any target coefficients are chosen. It is not defeated by PL-225, because PL-225 only says that membership or affiliation in the weak closure of the bare prime shifts is nonselective; the affine covariance is an additional equation involving a distinguished additive generator. It is also outside the summarized WP-215 scalar-interpolation control, because an arbitrary diagonal `F(H)` changes weights on energy eigenspaces but does not create or characterize the noncommuting relation between `S` and the `V_p`.

The separation is nevertheless only partial, and it classicalizes rather than advances an RH mechanism. PL-199 identifies the affine relation with established Cuntz/Laca--Raeburn prior art and shows that its natural KMS thermodynamics is organized by the shifted pole at `beta=2`, not by a selector at the shifted critical line. PL-200 then supplies the stronger target-sensitivity control: every completely multiplicative unimodular phase `chi` extends through the same affine source law by `(a,m) -> chi(m)`. Thus the identical source relation admits the full Helson-type prime-phase family; the Liouville choice produces `zeta(2s)/zeta(s)` only as a particular graded scalar readout and does not acquire an operator continuation or positivity theorem in the RH-sensitive region. The raw source law therefore does not select the downstream sign.

This is the strongest present counterargument to identifying the two research obligations merely because they can inhabit related operator-algebraic settings: **source selection can be mathematically meaningful and source-forced while the sign/localization theorem remains an additional target-sensitive obligation**. It is not a theorem that every future Prime-Lattice selector is independent of every Weil-positivity mechanism, so the clue is resolved as a narrowing rather than as a general logical-independence theorem.

The next independently useful test is to classify the canonical gauge/KMS-compatible scalar functionals on the affine residue projections `S^k V_a V_a^* S^{*k}` under Chinese-remainder refinement. If every source-forced compatible readout is forced to remain a positive/local residue-probability functional (or an already-classical KMS transform), then the affine selector is exhausted as a route to a sign-indefinite Weil form without inserting target coefficients. A counterexample would materially change this assessment: it would need to produce a canonical signed functional from the affine source relations themselves, survive the arbitrary-prime-phase control of PL-200, and have a continuation or coercivity statement not reducible to a prescribed `F(H)` or to a scalar zeta repackaging.
