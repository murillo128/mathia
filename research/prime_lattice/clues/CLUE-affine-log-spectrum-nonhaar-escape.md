# What source-forced affine statistic survives logarithmic two-point Haar flattening?

## Observation

The affine Liouville route has now separated several regimes sharply. Fixed-shift ordinary correlations retain the unresolved Chowla difficulty; canonical uniform shift averaging collapses or reduces to simpler summatory data; and PL-174 shows that every fixed finite second-order logarithmic shift filter has the universal Haar spectrum. Thus the specific second-order spectral carrier is exhausted even though additive coupling itself is not.

## Research question

Is there a canonical prime-lattice operation that retains a non-Haar, source-forced affine invariant outside PL-174's logarithmic two-point class—through higher-order or nonlinear data, a justified non-diffuse shift family, or a completed/target-relative coupling formed before the second-order logarithmic limit?

## Why it may matter

A survivor would identify arithmetic information that is genuinely present after additive coupling but not erased by the theorem-controlled second-order flattening. A negative result could close a broader family of affine spectral wrappers and redirect effort toward a different information carrier.

## Decisive test

Choose one concrete operation before inspecting its outcome and state exactly which hypothesis of PL-174 it leaves. Derive the resulting invariant from the prime-exponent/additive source rather than selecting weights from observed exceptional correlations. Compare it with a matched control and with the strongest applicable correlation theorem. The direction survives only if the observable is demonstrably non-Haar or otherwise nonuniversal under an independently justified estimate, and if its proposed RH-facing use needs information not already equivalent to the original unresolved correlation target.

If the candidate reduces to fixed logarithmic two-point correlations, a diffuse direct shift filter, a one-point summatory square, or a regularization that deletes the signed first-order carrier, reject it as already covered. For higher-order or completed candidates, keep the exact order, weights, topology, and target transport explicit.

## Evidence boundary

PL-174 proves a second-order logarithmic obstruction, not Bernoulli behavior, all-order Chowla, or the absence of useful completed couplings. No non-Haar survivor, analytic continuation, or RH implication is established here. Ordinary fixed-shift Cesaro correlations remain a harder arithmetic problem rather than a solved escape.
