# MI-017 — Zero-order shift transport needs source-structured BV coercivity, not ambient forcing-space smoothing

**Evidence level:** supported by [PL-297](../../findings/PL-297-renormalized-hadamard-stress-hhalf-decoupling.md)--[PL-304](../../findings/PL-304-suzuki-resolvent-no-lp-measure-bv-smoothing.md). The needed uniform BV/derivative-measure estimate for the actual completed-Weil ground branch remains open.

PL-297 separates the renormalized Hadamard boundary stress from the critical Fourier first moment: the stress can converge while the zero extensions diverge in `H^(1/2)`. PL-298 nevertheless gives `C^1` translation autocorrelations at every fixed positive fractional order, and PL-299 transports them to zero order on the pure principal branch using uniform convergence plus uniform bounded variation rather than half-Sobolev control.

PL-300 shows why ordinary Hilbert-space stability cannot supply that currency. Norm-small self-adjoint perturbations can preserve a unique simple isolated ground branch and uniform state convergence while injecting tiny-amplitude, huge-variation oscillations that make a prescribed shift derivative diverge.

PL-301 checks the exact Suzuki remainder against that adversary and removes one false obstruction. Compressed prime-power shifts are total-variation nonexpansive channel by channel, and the archimedean completion maps `L^1` uniformly into `BV`. The rough high-frequency packet mechanism of PL-300 therefore cannot be hidden in the actual lower-order remainder. PL-302 then proves that the finite arithmetic shift comb itself ceases to be a contraction once the `n=3` channel is active.

PL-303 closes the next shortcut using the **actual canonical operator**. At `a=0.8` the odd sector has a certified positive spectral gap from the tracked even ground level, so the odd resolvent is bounded on `L^2`. But it is not bounded from odd `L^2` into `BV_0`. High-frequency odd packets have completed-Weil graph cost only `O(log K)` because the principal symbol is logarithmic, while their total variation is `asymp K`. Thus no ordinary `L^2` resolvent estimate, however clean the spectral gap, can supply the derivative-measure compactness required at zero order.

PL-304 shows that this is not an `L^2` artifact. The same exact packets have `O(log K)` forcing cost in every ordinary `L^p`, `1<=p<=infinity`, and as finite Radon measures, while their `BV_0` cost is `Omega(K)`. Hence the canonical odd inverse has no ambient `L^p -> BV_0` smoothing route at any exponent and no bounded full-measure-space extension compatible with the Hilbert resolvent. Simply replacing the forcing norm by a stronger-looking standard Banach or measure norm cannot buy the missing spatial derivative.

The missing resource is therefore narrower than “a stronger forcing-space resolvent.” The differentiated eigen-equation must place its actual right-hand side in a **proper source-structured range** that excludes the packet family through cancellation, moment/boundary compatibility, commutator structure or another algebraic constraint; alternatively one needs a graph norm that already prices linear spatial frequency, or a direct variation inequality on the tracked eigenbranch. A generic `L^p` or finite-measure coercivity theorem is closed.

The reusable distinction is between spectral identification, BV transport and source-range BV coercivity. The gap says which odd equation is invertible in Hilbert space; the exact remainder says lower-order source terms do not manufacture arbitrary variation from nowhere; PL-304 says no unstructured standard forcing space supplies the missing order. Any viable zero-order transport theorem must use information specific to the differentiated source or explicitly pay first-order frequency.

**Boundary.** PL-304 does not say the actual aperture derivative or ground state has infinite variation. Its counterexamples are arbitrary odd graph-domain packets, not the source-forced derivative of the eigenbranch. A bounded inverse on a proper source-generated range, a cancellation identity for the exact differentiated forcing, a positive-order graph norm, or a direct branch-variation argument may still succeed. The independent source-specific sign gate also remains open.
