# MI-017 — Fixed-order stress is automatic; the small-order endpoint has a universal stretched Green profile but a source-specific amplitude

**Evidence level:** supported by PL-297--PL-305, sharpened by PL-306--PL-308, the explicit small-order boundary-layer obstruction in [PL-309](../../findings/PL-309-small-order-green-trace-boundary-layer.md), and the exact stretched Green limit in [PL-310](../../findings/PL-310-stretched-green-volterra-profile.md). Fixed-`s` Pohozaev admissibility and the principal stretched profile are exact; compactness for the actual completed-Weil branch, identification of its amplitude with the zero-order aperture derivative, and the rational-prime sign theorem remain open.

PL-297 separates renormalized boundary stress from the critical Fourier first moment. PL-298--PL-304 show that generic spectral stability and ordinary forcing norms do not make the logarithmic resolvent a `BV` smoother. PL-305 removes the kernel-side concern: once a fixed state lies in `C_0 cap BV_0`, every prime-power activation and the completed remainder are first-order differentiable in the aperture.

PL-306--PL-308 then remove fixed-order moving-boundary and high-regularity obligations. The completed-Weil lower operator preserves enough supported Sobolev regularity for a finite `mu`-transmission bootstrap at every fixed `s>0`, yielding the exact weak stress law

`Gamma(1+s)^2/(2s) (|tau_+|^2+|tau_-|^2) = E_s(w)+a^(2s)[b_a(w)+V_a(w)]`.

The translated source fronts are therefore not a fixed-`s` obstruction; transmission space is the right setting. The unresolved analytic currency is the singular limit.

PL-309 identifies the scale consumed by that limit. In the one-dimensional fractional Poisson model, the exact Green trace can be written in the stretched variable `r=-s log((1-x)/2)`. Fixed `r` samples forcing at physical distance `exp(-Theta(1/s))` from the boundary, and smooth forcings with `||f_s||_infty=O(sqrt(s))->0` can still prescribe an arbitrary finite limit of `tau_(s,+)/sqrt(s)`. Ordinary bulk convergence is therefore too weak.

PL-310 shows that the stretched layer is not geometrically arbitrary. With `x_s(r)=1-2e^(-r/s)`, the pushed Green measure converges to

`e^(-r-q) 1_(0<q<r)dq + e^(-2r) delta_r`,

so the limiting operator is

`(TF)(r)=e^(-2r)F(r)+e^(-r) integral_0^r e^(-q)F(q)dq`.

Its nonzero fixed points form the one-dimensional cone

`F(r)=C/sqrt(e^(2r)-1)`.

This profile simultaneously contains the logarithmic `r^(-1/2)` boundary face and the fixed-order `e^(-r)` tail. It is generated by the fractional principal operator and persists under bounded matched lower-order perturbations, so the shape itself carries no rational-prime specificity.

The endpoint has therefore split into two sharper gates. First prove that the actual completed-Weil rescaled profiles `U_s=s^(-1/2)w_s(x_s(r))` are compact/uniformly integrable enough for the Green representation to converge, or recover the same scalar through the weak virial. Once that succeeds, the principal limit has only one free datum: the amplitude `C`. Second prove that this amplitude/stress has the rational-prime-specific sign or first-crossing behavior needed by the destination theorem.

**Boundary.** PL-310 proves the Green-kernel limit, not compactness of the completed-Weil eigenbranch. Its conditional specialization assumes enough local compactness/uniform integrability and boundedness of the lower source term to pass to the limiting operator. The universal fixed profile does not identify `C`, its sign, or the zero-order aperture derivative, and it cannot be treated as arithmetic evidence merely because it is rigid.
