# MI-006 — The fixed-smooth second-scale Weil signal is an RH diagnostic, not yet a generative selector

**Evidence level:** supported by exact explicit-formula expansions and compact-group/Diophantine classifications

## Core intuition

A criterion can be mathematically equivalent to RH without supplying a new reason for RH. In the completed Weil boundary problem, the critical second amplitude on fixed smooth probes produces an exact exponential sum over the already-continued zeta zeros. Its boundedness can be RH-equivalent, but its subsequent spectral/dynamical packaging does not add rigidity: under RH the signal is recurrent rather than convergent, and its natural hull/coupling constructions only reorganize frequency data already present in the explicit formula.

## Strongest justified principle

PL-067 shows that after the canonical PNT cancellation, multiplying the fixed-smooth cross-end coefficient by `exp L` gives a zero expansion whose RH specialization is an absolutely convergent nonconstant Bohr almost-periodic function. Generic probes therefore have no limit even assuming RH.

PL-068 strengthens the criterion. There is a residual set of fixed smooth probe pairs that do not annihilate any zero, and for each such pair boundedness of the second-scale coefficient as `L->infinity` is equivalent to RH. Yet regular scalar renormalizations cannot turn this into a nonzero finite limit: under RH they either kill the signal, preserve nonconvergent recurrence, or make it unbounded.

PL-069 classifies the translation hull under RH as the compact group dual to the integer module generated by zero ordinates. The abstract flow remembers only that module; adding the evaluation observable restores precisely the Fourier coefficients already supplied by the zero expansion. PL-070 then shows that the canonical same-time coupling to the prime-log Kronecker flow is nontrivial exactly when the two frequency modules have an exact intersection; the joining construction does not create such a relation. PL-071 closes adaptive finite-horizon character resonance: any fixed finite real frequency pattern can be shadowed by prime-log lattice characters to accuracy `O(1/T)` with only logarithmic weighted exponent complexity. Near-unit finite-time prime/zero correlations are therefore universal Diophantine phenomena.

## What remains possible

The boundedness equivalence can still be useful as a target: a genuinely new proof would derive the needed uniform bound from rational-prime/completion structure **without** using a zero-location theorem or the explicit zero expansion as the decisive input. Such an upstream estimate would be substantive. Merely taking the hull, Koopman spectrum, same-time joining, or adaptive resonances of the zero signal is not.

## Status / novelty

The explicit-formula expansion, RH-equivalent boundedness, hull classification, joining criterion, and finite-horizon approximation are persisted findings with classical harmonic/dynamical ingredients. The “diagnostic versus generative” distinction is the local synthesis.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a natural dynamical invariant of the second-scale signal that forces all exponents to be purely imaginary without already assuming RH or importing their zero locations, or derive boundedness directly from upstream prime structure in a way not equivalent to a known zero-free/RH statement.

## Lean-formalizable core

- Compact-group hull of an absolutely summable almost-periodic Fourier series.
- Frequency-module intersection criterion for Kronecker joinings.
- Finite-frequency rational-log approximation with `O(log T)` height.
