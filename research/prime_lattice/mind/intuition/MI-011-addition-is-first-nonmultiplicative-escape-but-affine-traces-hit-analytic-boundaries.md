# MI-011 — Addition is a genuine missing variable, but canonical regularization and logarithmic affine observables delete or universalize it

**Evidence level:** supported through PL-175 by exact affine operator identities and published fixed-shift logarithmic Chowla theorems

## Core intuition

Ordinary addition is genuinely outside the multiplicative exponent-difference quotient: ratios one-swap terms and shifted Liouville correlations require relations `n` with `n+h`. But preserving the variable in the definition is not enough. The first canonical analytic and statistical completions of this affine channel either delete the difficult first-scale correlation or drive accessible finite-dimensional shift statistics to universal behavior.

The live question is therefore not “add affine shifts” but **retain a source-forced additive correlation through the exact analytic/limiting operation that is supposed to reach the critical regime**. Standard Hilbert--Schmidt regularization, uniform averaging, and fixed-shift logarithmic low-order statistics do not do this.

## Strongest justified principle

PL-169 identifies additive shifted correlations as information absent from pure multiplicative exponent differences. PL-170--PL-171 show that simple affine relative traces telescope to finite-head data and congruence channels recover classical Hurwitz/Dirichlet objects only where the relevant trace is already absolutely convergent.

PL-172 gives the first non-telescoping affine parity operator. Its ordinary trace is exactly the fixed-shift Liouville/Chowla Dirichlet series, while its singular values cross into `S_2` at `Re(s)>1/2`. But `det_2` achieves that extension by removing the `r=1` trace term itself; every retained determinant coefficient is already absolutely convergent. The critical-looking Hilbert boundary is therefore sign-blind regularization, not continuation of the hard correlation.

PL-173 shows that uniform shift averaging either converges strongly to zero, remains a finite combination of unresolved fixed-shift channels, or, when every finite-window displacement is included, collapses exactly to the classical Liouville summatory square. PL-174 then proves that fixed finite logarithmic second-order affine spectra are Haar-flat by Tao's two-point theorem. PL-175 extends the universality boundary: all odd Walsh sectors and degree two vanish under published logarithmic results, and every bounded function of three distinct fixed shifted Liouville signs has the independent fair-sign limiting law. The first Walsh degree not covered by these theorem inputs is even degree four.

## What remains possible

Ordinary fixed-shift Cesaro correlation remains outside these logarithmic theorems and may carry genuinely harder arithmetic, but no continuation mechanism is supplied. A source-forced exceptional/growing-shift construction may also escape if its weights are not chosen after inspection. Fixed even Walsh degree four or higher is merely an uncontrolled sector and should be pursued only when another source argument makes it relevant.

The most promising category change is to couple additive information to completed, target-relative, or explicit-formula structure **before** regularization or logarithmic averaging erases the source distinction.

## Status / novelty

Shifted correlations, Chowla conjectures, Schatten ideals, regularized determinants, logarithmic Furstenberg systems, and Walsh analysis are classical/prior-art ingredients. The persisted synthesis is the boundary: **addition supplies genuinely new arithmetic information, but the canonical affine completions currently available either remove its hard first-order trace or universalize the theorem-accessible low-order logarithmic sectors**.

## Falsification criterion

Recover the PL-172 fixed-shift first trace from `det_2` in `Re(s)>1/2` without independently continuing the Chowla series, produce a non-Haar fixed finite logarithmic sector covered by PL-174/PL-175, or derive a source-forced affine carrier outside those quotients whose critical analytic behavior retains a nonuniversal additive correlation.

## Lean-formalizable core

- Affine Liouville operator and Schatten thresholds.
- `det_2` deletion of the first trace.
- Uniform/all-shift averaging identities.
- Walsh-sector reduction from two-point and odd-order logarithmic correlation inputs.
