# MI-001 — The exponent lattice is scaffolding; arithmetic specificity lives in the exact global norm completion

**Evidence level:** supported

## Core intuition

The prime-exponent lattice is an exact and useful representation of multiplication, but the current evidence shows that its abstract combinatorics — even after natural topology is added — are too universal to determine Riemann zero geometry. What remains distinctive is not the free cone itself but the **entire rational-prime norm assignment together with global analytic structure capable of turning that norm into more than an RH-equivalent cancellation restatement**.

## Strongest justified principle

PL-015 supplies the decisive deformation control. Beurling generalized-prime systems can preserve the same free exponent cone, square-free Boolean sector, Euler-product architecture, square-root-scale generalized-integer counting regularity, and even an arbitrarily long exact initial segment of the ordinary rational primes, while having radically different RH-type zero behavior. Therefore none of those shared structures can force the Riemann zero divisor.

PL-022 closes a different escape: the energy cutoff of the square-free exponent cube is already Björner's number-theoretic simplicial complex, and the full exponent down-set already has a classical CW realization. RH becomes square-root cancellation of an Euler characteristic. For the canonical shifted square-free complex, the ordinary Hodge spectra are integral and the heat supertrace is identically the same Euler characteristic for every time. Thus raw exponent-lattice topology/Hodge theory does not create a zero-localizing spectrum; it repackages the Mertens/Liouville cancellation target unless additional structure is justified.

PL-014 identifies a classical boundary that the Beurling controls and bare cell complex generally do not preserve. The signed valuation lattice is

\[
\mathbf A_f^\times/\widehat{\mathbf Z}^\times\cong\bigoplus_p\mathbf Z,
\]

and the prime-lattice energy `log n` is the finite idelic log norm balanced by the archimedean place through the product formula. Tate's additive Fourier/Mellin theory then supplies analytic continuation and the functional equation on the **completed** adelic object. The bare Bohr torus is only the Pontryagin dual of a valuation quotient; it has forgotten the local-unit, additive, archimedean, and Poisson structure used by the functional equation.

Thus a credible lattice-based RH mechanism must exhibit an invariant of the full infinite rational-prime placement or completion that is destroyed by the Beurling controls and does more than restate Euler-characteristic cancellation. Merely increasing the finite cutoff, enriching the combinatorics of exponent vectors, or applying the ordinary unweighted Hodge Laplacian cannot do this.

## Evidence against overgeneralization

This does not say that Tate theory proves RH, nor that every successful mechanism must literally be adelic. Tate explains continuation and the self-dual functional-equation axis, not why every zero lies there. Likewise PL-022 does not rule out weighted, nonlocal, persistent, adelic, or asymptotic operators on the exponent complex. Those are legitimate only when the extra structure is canonical and supplies new control rather than encoding Mertens cancellation by hand.

The exponent lattice remains useful as a coordinate skeleton: it makes multiplicative semigroups, Möbius orientation, energy cutoffs, and log-prime frequencies transparent. The no-go concerns treating that skeleton or its most immediate topology as the zero-selecting mechanism itself.

## Status / novelty

The adelic identification, Beurling counterexamples, and Björner/Duval--Reiner topology are classical literature. The synthesis is a supported discriminator for this research line: **global norm/completion data and a genuine cancellation/selection mechanism, not free multiplicative combinatorics or ordinary Hodge topology, are the surviving variables**.

## Falsification criterion

Refute this intuition by giving a mechanism whose complete mathematical inputs are preserved by one of the contrasting Beurling systems or determined by the ordinary Björner exponent complex, yet which nevertheless logically forces the ordinary Riemann zero divisor without importing additional rational-prime, completion, or cancellation data.

## Lean-formalizable core

- Unique-factorization exponent-cone identities.
- Identification of the square-free energy down-set with the divisibility simplicial complex and Euler-characteristic identity.
- Quotient identification of finite-idelic valuations at the algebraic level.
- Product-formula relation between finite valuations and `log n`.
- Abstract control lemma: an invariant shared by systems with different target zero behavior cannot by itself determine that behavior.
