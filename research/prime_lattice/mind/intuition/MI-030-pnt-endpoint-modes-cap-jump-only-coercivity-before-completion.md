# MI-030 — PNT endpoint modes cap jump-only coercivity before it can cancel the scalar compensation

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [PL-339](../../findings/PL-339-outer-source-shell-carries-unit-gap-coercivity.md) and [PL-340](../../findings/PL-340-pnt-endpoint-jump-coercivity-ceiling.md), using the endpoint modes constructed in PL-051. The conclusion concerns the isolated prime/jump block, not the completed Weil operator.

PL-339 proved that the accumulated positive jump sector has the total-source lower bound

`J_a >= (1-o(1)) W_a I`.

Since the exact prime block is `P_a=J_a-2W_a I`, one remaining hope was that compatibility among many positive jump forms might drive the lower edge of `J_a` all the way toward `2W_a`, asymptotically neutralizing the scalar compensation.

PL-340 rules out that jump-only mechanism with explicit PNT endpoint states. For fixed endpoint depth `R`, normalized symmetric and antisymmetric states satisfy

`e^(-a) <K_a u_(a,R)^±,u_(a,R)^±> -> ±(1-e^(-R))`,

while `W_a~2e^a`. Hence the isolated prime block has positive and negative Rayleigh directions of order `W_a`, and the lower edge of the jump sector obeys the asymptotic bracket

`1 <= liminf inf sigma(J_a)/W_a <= limsup inf sigma(J_a)/W_a <= 3/2`.

The positive jump bulk is therefore genuinely coercive but cannot become strong enough, by compatibility alone, to cancel the full `2W_a` scalar subtraction. The missing resource is not “more of the same positive jumps.” Completion, centering or source-specific branch information must participate at leading order.

The endpoint witness is also a matched-control warning. The negative prime direction is generated by the universal first-order PNT endpoint mode, and PL-059 shows that the zeta-pole completion cancels precisely that universal mode. Thus PL-340 is **not** evidence that the completed Weil operator has a macroscopic negative direction. It instead identifies the exact point where the jump-only decomposition ceases to be destination-faithful: a mode that looks fatal before completion is designed to be removed by completion.

The reusable lesson is that **coercive positive pieces cannot be credited toward a signed destination until the compensation/completion at the same scale is included**. Improving a lower bound on one summand is irrelevant once an explicit witness proves that its best possible edge remains a fixed fraction below the cancellation threshold. The next theorem must work with the completed/centered object or with a rational-prime-specific branch invariant that survives the universal PNT cancellation.

**Boundary.** PL-340 does not determine the exact limiting spectral edges of `J_a` or `P_a`, prove branch persistence, or produce a negative direction for the completed Weil form. The `3/2` ceiling is enough only to kill the route “stronger joint jump coercivity alone neutralizes `-2W_aI`.” Completion remains an essential part of the live problem.
