# MI-041 — Every Venturini witness is Liouville up to a zero-free holomorphic gauge

**Evidence level:** literature-backed exact synthesis from [PL-369](../../findings/PL-369-venturini-completely-multiplicative-zero-free-witness.md) through [PL-373](../../findings/PL-373-infinite-alphabet-liouville-gauge-collapse.md), interpreted against the ambient filter controls PL-363--PL-368.

PL-363--PL-368 show that ambient linear filter classes can manufacture critical-looking convergence walls without selecting zeta zeros. PL-369 identifies a genuinely source-rigid nonlinear interface: if a bounded completely multiplicative `a` has `L(a,s)` holomorphic on `Re(s)>sigma_0` and `L(a,1)=0`, Venturini's theorem forces zeta to be zero-free there. Liouville gives the exact control `L(lambda,s)=zeta(2s)/zeta(s)`.

PL-370 shows that local pole neutrality already forces finite prime-harmonic distance from Liouville, while PL-371 shows that this local neighbourhood still admits arbitrary prescribed real continuation obstructions below one. PL-372 then proves that actual continuation forces weighted Liouville closeness at every crossed exponent and that finite-alphabet witnesses differ from Liouville only by a normally convergent zero-free Euler factor.

PL-373 removes the finite-alphabet loophole at the divisor level. In every RH-relevant half-plane `Re(s)>sigma_0`, `sigma_0>=1/2`, a genuine Venturini witness satisfies

`L(a,s) = [zeta(2s)/zeta(s)] C_a(s)`

with `C_a` holomorphic and zero-free throughout the half-plane. The first-order prime defect

`P_a(s)=sum_p (1+a(p))p^(-s)`

also extends holomorphically there. Conversely, if `P_a` extends holomorphically, then the resulting meromorphic continuation has the same zeta divisor and is holomorphic exactly when zeta is zero-free. Conditional first-order organization of an infinite alphabet can alter the gauge but cannot cancel, move or hide a zeta zero.

The durable point is stronger than finite-alphabet rigidity: **within the scalar bounded completely multiplicative Venturini class, coefficient freedom is divisor-free gauge freedom once the continuation hypothesis is present.** Infinite alphabet is not an independent escape from the Liouville obstruction.

This shifts the surviving route outside that witness class. A distinct mechanism must change the source object or theorem interface—e.g. a non-scalar/non-completely-multiplicative construction or a global trace/positivity relation whose divisor is not already fixed by `zeta(2s)/zeta(s)`—and must construct its analytic continuation independently rather than merely encode the desired zero-free region.

**Boundary.** The factorization is conditional on the genuine Venturini continuation and does not construct such a witness, prove a zero-free region, or imply RH. The zero-free gauge need not have an absolutely convergent relative Euler product for an infinite alphabet; what PL-373 fixes is its divisor and the holomorphic continuation of the first-order prime defect.
