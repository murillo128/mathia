# MI-004 — Prime-shift symmetry is scaffolding; one-dimensional unitary cocycles are pure gauge

**Evidence level:** supported by exact operator obstructions, gauge trivializations, and prior-art identifications through PL-195

## Core intuition

The multiplicative prime action is canonical on the exponent lattice, but spectral covariance is much easier to manufacture than a zero-sensitive invariant. The newest evidence makes the gauge problem exact: on a one-dimensional bilateral prime-shift base, scalar phases, arbitrary nonzero scalar resolvent weights, and even finite-dimensional noncommuting unitary cocycles are cumulative gauges. Matrix-valued transport does not create holonomy when the base itself has no loop.

A prime action becomes potentially RH-relevant only when an additional source-forced structure prevents that trivialization and survives the matched multiplicative controls.

## Strongest justified principle

Earlier results squeeze ordinary prime-shift covariance from both sides: exact commutation scalarizes, exact logarithmic covariance produces the wrong continuous spectral geometry, and sufficiently weak compact/Schatten resolvent covariance becomes automatic. Canonical GCD/Poisson, weighted Hasse, scalar cocycle, reciprocity, spherical-scattering, model-space, Clark, Sonine, and de Branges constructions likewise either reproduce classical data or remain universal until a zeta-specific target theorem is inserted.

PL-193--PL-195 sharpen the cocycle boundary. A scalar prime-shift phase is removed by rephasing the basis. Arbitrary nonzero scalar weighted-resolvent coefficients admit the same cumulative normalization. Most importantly, a finite-fiber unitary cocycle `U_n in U(d)` over the bilateral line is gauge-equivalent to the constant shift by recursively defining the fiber gauge. Noncommutativity of successive matrices does not help because there is only one path between sites and hence no gauge-invariant loop product.

## What remains possible

A target-relative nonnormal action, several genuinely independent shift directions, branching or loops with source-forced holonomy, an anchored boundary/reference condition that prevents cumulative gauge removal, an infinite ramified/adelic construction, or a nonunitary category with a replacement coercivity theorem can evade the current result. Such a construction must show that its surviving invariant cannot be reproduced by matched Beurling, arbitrary-inner, scalar-cocycle, or one-dimensional finite-fiber controls.

## Epistemic status

The covariance, cocycle, and cumulative-gauge statements are persisted exact mathematics; the synthesis is a supported restriction on what can count as a prime-action Hamiltonian principle.

## Falsification criterion

Construct a one-dimensional finite-fiber unitary prime-shift invariant that survives the explicit cumulative gauge of PL-195, or prove that the exact rational-prime source canonically fixes a gauge in a way that produces a nontrivial observable and constrains the zero divisor. Merely choosing a matrix cocycle or scalar phase does not falsify the intuition.
