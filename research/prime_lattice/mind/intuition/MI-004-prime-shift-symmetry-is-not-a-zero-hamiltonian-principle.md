# MI-004 — Prime-shift symmetry is scaffolding; flat unitary cocycles are gauge or programmable

**Evidence level:** supported by exact operator obstructions, gauge trivializations, arbitrary-Hilbert-fiber collapse, and state-dependent flat-connection collapse through PL-197

## Core intuition

The multiplicative prime action is canonical on the exponent lattice, but spectral covariance is much easier to manufacture than a zero-sensitive invariant. The current evidence closes increasingly rich exact-unitary routes. Scalar phases and nonzero scalar weights gauge away; fixed unitary fibers either gauge away or become inert multiplicity; and PL-197 shows that even arbitrary **state-dependent** unitary edge data carry no invariant content when the prime directions commute exactly.

Exact commutation is a flatness condition on the cubical exponent lattice, and every flat unitary connection there integrates to a global vertex gauge. Worse, self-adjoint resolvent covariance does not select an arithmetic gauge: arbitrary Boolean vertex fields can be programmed into such a flat model. Prime-shift symmetry becomes potentially RH-relevant only when an additional source-forced structure prevents both gauge removal and programmability and survives matched non-arithmetic controls.

## Strongest justified principle

PL-193 removes scalar prime-shift phase covariance by rephasing the basis, and PL-194 extends cumulative normalization to arbitrary nonzero scalar weighted-resolvent coefficients. PL-195 closes the finite-fiber unitary repair on a one-dimensional bilateral shift because the base has no loop on which holonomy can survive.

PL-196 removes internal-dimension freedom for exact pure-tensor unitary covariance of a genuine self-adjoint resolvent. On `l^2(N) tensor K`, the resolvent identity and mean-ergodic averaging force every fixed prime unitary to be the identity and the Hamiltonian to factor as `I tensor K_0`. Arbitrary internal spectrum is then only inert multiplicity.

PL-197 tests the relational loophole. Let the unitary on each prime edge depend on the whole exponent vertex. Pairwise commutation is exactly zero curvature on every elementary square; path independence then gives `U_p(alpha)=g_(alpha+e_p) g_alpha^*`, so all weighted prime shifts are a diagonal gauge of the canonical shifts. The same finding constructs self-adjoint two-point resolvents whose gauge encodes an **arbitrary Boolean field** while preserving exact covariance. Existence of a flat state-dependent intertwining is therefore not arithmetic rigidity.

## What remains possible

Nonzero curvature/noncommutation, target-relative compressions or quotients that create nontrivial topology, covariance modulo compact/Schatten ideals, source-target couplings, and nonunitary/nonnormal transfer are outside the exact flat-unitary class. But they are category exits, not positive evidence.

In particular, curvature itself must survive a programmability audit: PL-036 already shows that arbitrary scalar projective curvature can be assigned on the bare free prime semigroup. A useful relational action needs a source theorem tying its gauge-invariant obstruction to rational-prime/global-completion data and a replacement positivity/coercivity principle that cannot be reproduced by arbitrary vertex labels.

## Epistemic status

The gauge statements, arbitrary-Hilbert-fiber collapse, and state-dependent flat-connection trivialization are persisted exact mathematics. The synthesis is a supported restriction on what can count as a prime-action Hamiltonian principle.

## Falsification criterion

Construct an exactly commuting state-dependent unitary prime multishift on the bare exponent lattice that is not diagonally gauge-equivalent to the canonical shifts, or show that PL-197's Boolean resolvent programmability fails. A genuinely source-forced curved, relative, ideal-valued, or nonunitary category outside those hypotheses would instead be the intended escape and would need its own nontriviality/coercivity theorem.
