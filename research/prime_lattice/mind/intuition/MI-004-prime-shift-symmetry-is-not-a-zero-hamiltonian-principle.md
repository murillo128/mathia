# MI-004 — Prime-shift symmetry is scaffolding; exact unitary cocycles are gauge or inert multiplicity

**Evidence level:** supported by exact operator obstructions, gauge trivializations, and arbitrary-Hilbert-fiber collapse through PL-196

## Core intuition

The multiplicative prime action is canonical on the exponent lattice, but spectral covariance is much easier to manufacture than a zero-sensitive invariant. The current evidence now closes two increasingly strong unitary routes. On a one-dimensional bilateral base, scalar phases, arbitrary nonzero scalar weights, and finite-dimensional unitary cocycles are cumulative gauges. On the one-sided prime-shift lattice, PL-196 proves something stronger: exact self-adjoint-resolvent covariance with a fixed unitary acting on **any Hilbert fiber** forces that unitary to be the identity and pushes all remaining spectrum into an inert tensor factor.

A prime action becomes potentially RH-relevant only when an additional source-forced structure prevents both trivializations and survives matched multiplicative controls. Enlarging unitary multiplicity, even to continuous spectrum, is not such a structure.

## Strongest justified principle

PL-193 shows that scalar prime-shift phase covariance is removed by rephasing the basis, and PL-194 extends cumulative normalization to arbitrary nonzero scalar weighted-resolvent coefficients. PL-195 closes the finite-fiber unitary repair on a one-dimensional bilateral shift: even noncommuting `U(d)` values are gauge-equivalent to the constant shift because the base has no loop on which holonomy can survive.

PL-196 removes the remaining internal-dimension loophole for exact pure-tensor unitary covariance of a genuine self-adjoint resolvent. If

`R(S_p tensor I) = (S_p tensor U_p)R`

on `l^2(N) tensor K`, with arbitrary Hilbert `K`, then the resolvent identity `Im R = R*R` and mean ergodic averaging force every `U_p=I`. Commutation with the prime shifts and their adjoints then gives

`R = I tensor (K_0-i)^(-1)` and `L = I tensor K_0`.

Thus an arbitrary internal spectrum can survive only by being repeated independently of the exponent lattice. It is programmable multiplicity, not an arithmetic coupling selected by the rational-prime action.

## What remains possible

The exact no-go does not cover nonunitary or nonnormal transport, covariance only modulo compact/Schatten ideals, target-relative compressions, source-target couplings, actions in which the operator attached to one prime depends genuinely on other exponent coordinates, or carriers with loops/branching and source-forced holonomy. A completed global construction may also leave the pure-tensor category entirely.

These are category exits, not positive evidence. Each needs a replacement coercivity or gauge-obstruction theorem showing why the surviving structure cannot again be normalized away or parked in an inert factor.

## Epistemic status

The gauge statements and the PL-196 arbitrary-Hilbert-fiber collapse are persisted exact mathematics. The synthesis is a supported restriction on what can count as a prime-action Hamiltonian principle.

## Falsification criterion

Construct a nontrivial unitary `U_p` satisfying the exact PL-196 self-adjoint-resolvent covariance, or an internal spectrum under those hypotheses that couples nontrivially to exponent coordinates rather than factoring as `I tensor K_0`. A genuinely source-forced relational, relative, or nonunitary category outside those hypotheses would instead be the intended escape and would need its own nontriviality theorem.
