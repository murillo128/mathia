# MI-042 — Finite-dimensional complete multiplicativity filters through scalar characters and first jets

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from `PL-374`--`PL-375` plus standard simultaneous triangularization and Leibniz-additive arithmetic.

A finite-dimensional unital completely multiplicative matrix representation of the positive integers cannot make the prime directions noncommutative: `rho(p)rho(q)=rho(q)rho(p)` for every pair of primes. Over `C`, the entire image is therefore simultaneously upper triangularizable. The diagonal entries are scalar completely multiplicative characters.

This filtration is already decisive for ordinary scalarizations. Trace sees only the finite sum of the diagonal characters, and each local determinant Euler factor is the product of their scalar factors. In the normal/unitary case the filtration splits orthogonally, so the matrix representation is literally a finite sum of scalar prime channels.

The first nonnormal case does not yet escape. In dimension two, an extension between distinct characters splits by one fixed shear. A nonvanishing self-extension has the exact form

`rho(n)=chi(n)(I+d(n)N)`, `N^2=0`,

where `d(mn)=d(m)+d(n)`. Thus the off-diagonal datum is a linear functional on the exponent vector, equivalently the first tangent jet of `chi_t(n)=chi(n)e^(t d(n))`. For `d(n)=log n`, its Dirichlet series is `-L_chi'(s)` wherever termwise differentiation is justified.

The reusable distinction is therefore **matrix dimension versus genuinely new prime coupling**. Finite-dimensional complete multiplicativity gives a scalar composition series automatically, and the first nilpotent layer is only a tangent direction along that scalar family. A proposed matrix escape must identify an observable that genuinely uses higher extension data, or leave finite-dimensional exact complete multiplicativity altogether.

**Boundary.** Higher-dimensional nonnormal extensions, zero-valued diagonal characters, infinite-dimensional operator representations and non-completely-multiplicative source laws are not classified by this intuition. Derivatives of scalar Dirichlet series can have useful analytic structure, but the two-dimensional jet does not independently supply continuation or force zeta-zero localization. No RH consequence is claimed.
