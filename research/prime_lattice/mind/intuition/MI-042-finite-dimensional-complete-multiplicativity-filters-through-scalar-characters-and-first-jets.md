# MI-042 — Finite-dimensional complete multiplicativity is a finite scalar-jet source geometry

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from `PL-374`--`PL-376` plus standard finite-dimensional commutative Artin-algebra theory.

A finite-dimensional unital completely multiplicative representation `rho:N_(>0)->M_d(C)` cannot make prime directions noncommutative: all `rho(p)` commute. PL-374 therefore obtains a common triangular filtration with scalar completely multiplicative composition factors. Trace and local determinant observables see only those scalar channels, and normal/unitary representations split into them orthogonally.

PL-375 shows that the first nonnormal extension still does not create a new source geometry. Distinct scalar characters split by a fixed shear. A nontrivial two-dimensional self-extension has the form

`rho(n)=chi(n)(I+d(n)N)`, `N^2=0`,

with `d` completely additive, so it is exactly the first tangent jet of a scalar multiplicative deformation.

PL-376 upgrades that first-order picture to every finite dimension, including higher nilpotent order and zero-valued scalar blocks. The image generates a finite-dimensional commutative Artinian algebra, which decomposes into local factors with nilpotent maximal ideals. In each factor, every linear observable of `rho(n)` is an exact finite multivariate Taylor jet of a scalar completely multiplicative family assembled from the prime coordinates. Zero-valued residue characters do not open an infinite new direction: enough prime factors from their zero set push the local block into a nilpotent power and kill it.

Thus finite-dimensional exact complete multiplicativity has a stronger invariant than simultaneous triangularization: **for linear observables, the entire source is a finite infinitesimal thickening of scalar completely multiplicative channels**. Raising matrix dimension or nilpotent order adds only finitely many scalar derivatives. It does not by itself create irreducible cross-prime coupling.

The analytic boundary remains independent. The scalar deformation series admit Euler products only where normal/absolute convergence justifies them, and the finite-jet identity supplies no continuation into the critical strip. If a matrix construction gains analytic power, that power must enter through an additional global theorem or destination operation rather than through the finite-dimensional source algebra alone.

This leaves genuine escapes, but they are now category changes: infinite-dimensional or unbounded representations whose coefficient algebra is not Artinian, controlled violations of exact complete multiplicativity, global boundary/completion/scattering conditions, or nonlinear/target-relative observables. Nonlinear observables may exploit finite nilpotent geometry, but then the mathematical leverage belongs to that nonlinear observable and must be audited independently.

**Boundary.** The statement classifies finite-dimensional completely multiplicative source data as seen by linear matrix observables; it does not say that every nonlinear function of the matrices reduces to a single linear jet, nor does it rule out useful derivatives of independently continued scalar families. No continuation, positivity, zeta-zero localization or RH consequence follows from the algebraic reduction itself.
