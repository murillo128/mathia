# MI-044 — Countable Wold globalization needs a global hypothesis; positive energy supplies one but is universal

**Evidence level:** literature-backed and exact-derived synthesis from [PL-379](../../findings/PL-379-infinite-prime-wold-globalization-failure.md), [PL-380](../../findings/PL-380-cyclic-wandering-vacuum-log-covariance-rigidity.md), and [PL-381](../../findings/PL-381-semibounded-log-prime-covariance-global-wold-regularity.md), interpreted against the scalarization boundaries of PL-377--PL-378.

Unique factorization identifies the positive-integer exponent semigroup with the countably generated free commutative monoid `N_0^infinity` of finitely supported exponent sequences. For a fixed finite set of prime generators, doubly commuting isometries admit the familiar Wold decomposition into pure and unitary coordinate sectors. PL-379 shows why those finite classifications do not by themselves assemble into one global atomic type: a diffuse Bernoulli field can make every finite cylinder visible while every complete infinite type has zero mass.

This establishes a genuine **globalization gate**. Exact classification on every finite coordinate set does not determine the measure-theoretic organization of the countably infinite family. The missing resource is a global hypothesis that controls the projective limit, not another finite-prime Wold theorem.

PL-380 tests the strongest direct repair. If the joint wandering space is generating, the orbit sectors are automatically orthogonal and the family is the regular multishift `S_p tensor I_W`. In multiplicity one this is just the canonical coefficient model `V_p e_n=e_(pn)`. Exact vacuum covariance with weights `log p` then forces the diagonal energy `c+log n`. The diffuse pathology disappears, but no intermediate arithmetic structure is exposed: the repair closes back to the classical prime shift and logarithmic Hamiltonian.

PL-381 provides a more intrinsic sufficient condition. Suppose one semibounded self-adjoint generator `A` satisfies exact strong covariance `e^(itA) V_p e^(-itA)=e^(it log p)V_p`. The spectral lower edge forbids a unitary Wold sector for every prime. More importantly, every bounded-energy subspace can involve only finitely many prime directions because only finitely many `log p` lie below a fixed energy budget. Finite Wold theory then applies inside each energy window, while adjoints of all larger-prime shifts annihilate that window. Letting the energy bound grow proves that the global wandering space generates the whole Hilbert space.

The resulting operator still has only the regular prime dependence:

`A ~= N_log tensor I + I tensor B`,

where `B` acts on the joint wandering multiplicity space and is independent of the prime exponent coordinate. Thus positive energy solves the countable-globalization problem but leaves only an internal sector transverse to the prime lattice.

The reusable point is two-sided. **Finite-coordinate consistency is too weak, while semibounded locally finite covariance is strong enough; but the latter is a generic coercivity mechanism, not rational-prime arithmetic.** Replace `log p` by any positive locally finite countable weight family and the same proof globalizes the Wold decomposition. Therefore restoring the regular representation is not yet a zero-sensitive mechanism.

For Prime Lattice the live escape has moved past representation existence. A useful one-sided model must add a source-forced relation on top of the regular multishift/internal-sector normal form that fails for generic positive frequency tuples: Mellin/Nyman coupling, completed Weil data, a nonmultiplicative observable, or another target-relative global analytic constraint. Merely asking for a canonical vacuum or positive `log p` covariance has now been audited to completion.

**Boundary.** PL-379 remains the general obstruction without extra global hypotheses. PL-380 assumes a generating wandering space; PL-381 assumes one semibounded self-adjoint generator with exact strong covariance and doubly commuting isometries. These results do not classify arbitrary one-sided representations, and they do not imply analytic continuation, critical-line localization or RH. Their exact role is to identify when the infinite-coordinate Wold obstruction disappears and what representation remains afterward.
