# MI-044 — Prime-lattice operator topology must retain numerical weights, analytic representatives and the target simultaneously

**Evidence level:** literature-backed and exact-derived synthesis from [PL-379](../../findings/PL-379-infinite-prime-wold-globalization-failure.md)--[PL-390](../../findings/PL-390-besicovitch-b2-zero-blindness.md), interpreted against the scalarization boundaries of PL-377--PL-378.

PL-379--PL-382 locate the Wold boundary. Positive-energy covariance plus double commutativity gives a universal regular multishift, while the classical Nyman dilations satisfy the covariance but fail double commutativity and collapse in logarithmic coordinates to one ordered right-shift flow. PL-383--PL-385 then show that stronger ambient closures can erase the discrete sampling or preserve it only as a universal abstract positive semigroup.

PL-386 is the complementary positive result: if the selfadjoint energy structure is kept as part of the object, norm-closed information can recover the ordered frequency data. The relevant question is therefore not whether operator topology always forgets `log p`, but which distinguished structure carries those numerical values through the closure.

PL-387--PL-389 test one canonical answer using almost-periodic Wiener--Hopf index theory. The index sees additive log energy in its natural domain, yet the native vertical zeta symbol leaves the bounded Bohr AP algebra already at `Re(s)=1`. Value distribution makes it unbounded and arbitrarily close to zero throughout the open right half of the critical strip; even RH does not restore AP invertibility there.

PL-390 shows that changing only the completion does not solve the problem. Besicovitch `B^2` carries `zeta(sigma+it)` for every `sigma>1/2` with the correct prime-lattice frequencies, but it identifies functions modulo mean-square-zero changes. Isolated zero values are therefore not invariants of the `B^2` element, and `B^2` lacks the pointwise multiplicative algebra on which the earlier Fredholm index lived. The critical line appears as the divergence of `sum n^{-2sigma}`, a universal Hilbert threshold rather than a zero-localization mechanism.

The reusable audit is to keep four structures separate: the distinguished sampled generators and their numerical energies; the topology/closure in which they live; the analytic representative whose pointwise zeros matter; and the target/compression consumed by the RH criterion. Losing any one can produce an elegant universal object with the wrong information.

**Boundary.** Native uniform AP and native linewise `B^2` are the categories classified here. Unbounded/affiliated symbol theories, generalized analytic almost periodicity, Jensen/subharmonic data and Nyman-target compressions are not ruled out. Classical analytic almost-periodic zero theory is existing prior art and would need new source-specific structure to become a new Mathia route. None of PL-379--PL-390 proves RH.
