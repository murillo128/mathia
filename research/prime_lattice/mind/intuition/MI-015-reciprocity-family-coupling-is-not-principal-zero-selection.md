# MI-015 — Mixed-prime coupling matters only when global selection and purity survive the principal target channel

**Evidence level:** literature-backed exact reciprocity, principal-residue, affine-selector, Kac--Moody index-geometry, and geometric selection boundaries through PL-248

## Core intuition

Prime Lattice has genuine source-forced mixed-prime structure: quadratic reciprocity is not reducible to exchangeability or one-dimensional log geometry. But source richness is useful only if it survives the exact quotient/residue that exposes the Riemann divisor and if the surviving channel carries an independently proved localization principle.

The newer Kac--Moody evidence sharpens what counts as mixed-prime structure. Multivariable imaginary-root data in an indefinite root system can be completely genuine while living inside the **root-variable columns of one fixed rational-prime row**. That is not the same relation as coupling `v_p(n)` to `v_q(n)` for distinct rational primes. The known global constructions obtain cross-prime coupling elsewhere, through twisted multiplicativity/reciprocity, and obtain canonical selection through extra duality/purity axioms.

## Strongest justified principle

PL-240--PL-242 establish the reciprocity-family boundary. Quadratic reciprocity supplies authentic cross-prime data and multiple Dirichlet series give a classical family completion, but the clean `s=1` residue that isolates a rescaled Riemann zeta factor receives only the principal squarefree kernel; the nontrivial quadratic family carrying the mixed relation disappears.

PL-243--PL-245 show that further polar faces and higher affine residue structure do not repair this automatically: tested principal channels remain separable, and Weyl symmetry leaves freely selectable one-variable null-root data.

PL-246 tests an actual extra affine selector. In explicit `\widetilde A_1`, the non-Weyl symmetry fixes a scalar Pochhammer null-root factor that canonical normalization removes. The zero-bearing function-field block is centered using Weil purity/RH for curves; after that input, the extra action leaves it invariant. The symmetry is compatible with purity but does not derive it.

PL-247 shows that the standard correction factor remains one-variable in every affine type, while indefinite type has full-rank imaginary support and loses the simple affine coefficient positivity. PL-248 then resolves the index geometry: for an `r`-variable MDS the data are an array `a_(p,i)`, and the Kac--Moody root system acts on the `i`-coordinates within a fixed prime row `p`. Genuine interaction between distinct global prime rows enters through twisted multiplicativity/residue symbols, not from imaginary-root dimension alone.

PL-248 also identifies the strongest known global selector in the function-field setting. The axiomatic Sawin/Sawin--Whitehead construction is unique because it adds local-to-global duality and dominance/purity conditions; the geometric realization identifies these with Verdier duality and cohomological purity. Infinite Kac--Moody Weyl invariance alone leaves many local `p`-parts, and selecting those that recover the axiomatic object is open in general.

## Program consequence

A successful continuation must carry **cross-rational-prime relation, canonical global selection, principal-channel survival, and sign/purity in one construction**. Local indefinite multivariance is only one tensor factor. The number-field/Riemann route needs an independently justified analogue of the global selector and must prove that the twisted cross-prime relation remains nontrivial after extracting the ordinary zeta channel.

An extra functional equation that chooses a scalar correction, a local multivariable root factor at fixed `p`, or a selector defined using an already-proved purity theorem is a control rather than an ordinary-RH localization mechanism.

## Counterevidence / boundary

PL-248 does not prove that no number-field analogue of the geometric selector exists. Nor does loss of simple indefinite coefficient positivity rule out subtler operator/Hodge positivity. The function-field duality/purity package is legitimate theorem-level structure in its setting; the boundary is against transferring its conclusion to ordinary zeta without constructing the analogue.

## Epistemic status

**Exact source-relation, principal-projection, Kac--Moody index-geometry, and global-selector boundaries; no ordinary-zeta localization principle beyond scalar RH equivalence is established.**

## Falsification criterion

Find nontrivial reciprocity-family data surviving the PL-242 principal residue, derive the PL-246 critical purity from affine symmetry without importing Weil weights, or exhibit a canonical established Kac--Moody local correction whose multivariance directly couples distinct rational-prime rows contrary to PL-248. A positive continuation should build a source-derived global channel where cross-prime coupling and an independent localization principle survive together.
