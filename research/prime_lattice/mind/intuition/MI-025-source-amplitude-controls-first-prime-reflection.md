# MI-025 — Source amplitude, not qualitative spectral or all-log regularity, controls first-prime reflection

**Evidence level:** exact/source-selected synthesis from [PL-326](../../findings/PL-326-fixed-aperture-endpoint-laws-do-not-orient-the-first-prime-crossing.md) through [PL-331](../../findings/PL-331-all-log-fourier-control-misses-critical-first-prime-layer.md).

The first-prime activation isolates a shrinking reflected correlation

`H_delta(g)=int_0^(2delta) g(t)g(2delta-t) dt`

whose positive critical profile is only of size `delta/L_delta`, with `L_delta=log(a_delta/delta)->infinity`. PL-326--PL-327 show that fixed-aperture endpoint asymptotics and canonical half-log regularity do not determine its sign: an antisymmetric residual of state amplitude `L_delta^(-1/2)` on the moving layer can reverse the reflection while remaining small in natural endpoint norms.

PL-329 shows that the **exact source equation** sees that residual at the reciprocal scale. If `S_delta` is the endpoint-source amplitude and `C_delta` the selected critical coefficient, the source equation gives a remainder small enough to preserve the positive reflection whenever

`(1+S_delta)/(|C_delta| sqrt(L_delta)) -> 0`.

The threshold is sharp for the known counterfamily: its state amplitude is only `L_delta^(-1/2)` while the coercive source term magnifies it to `S_delta~sqrt(L_delta)`.

PL-330 proves that this source gate cannot be replaced by ordinary spectral continuity. Compact resolvent converts the shrinking-support source activation into norm-resolvent continuity of the full family, with continuous isolated spectral projections and graph-continuous simple eigenbranches, yet the hostile boundary layer can tend to zero in that graph norm and still reverse the much smaller moving sign reserve.

PL-331 strengthens the obstruction from one graph norm to the **entire qualitative all-log hierarchy**. For the same critical layer, `||r_delta||_(X_m)^2=O(delta L_delta^(2m-1))->0` for every fixed `m`. Thus the layer converges to zero in the projective Fréchet topology generated by all fixed logarithmic Fourier seminorms while retaining a leading-order negative reflected contribution and critical `sqrt(L_delta)` source cost.

The distinction is now sharp: branch transport, qualitative smoothness and destination orientation live at different scales. No finite or countable collection of fixed logarithmic regularity statements currently proved for the eigenstate controls the activation layer relative to the `delta/L_delta` sign margin. A replacement for the source-amplitude gate must be genuinely quantitative in the growth of the logarithmic seminorm constants so that `m` can increase with `L_delta`, provide positive-order regularity, or exploit the source equation directly.

The remaining question is therefore not whether the branch exists continuously or lies in every fixed logarithmic space. It is whether the **actual completed-Weil branch** obeys the sharp relative source condition, including non-collapse of `C_delta`. That is the first source-selected sign theorem still missing at the activation.

**Boundary.** PL-331 uses a matched perturbation and does not claim that the hostile layer is itself an eigenstate. The actual eigen-equation may exclude it; PL-329 identifies one sufficient quantitative mechanism. These results orient only the first prime channel and do not control later prime powers or prove global Weil positivity/RH.
