# MI-016 — Actual localized-Weil eigenstates need source-specific regularity and source-specific sign

**Evidence level:** exact for the source-static all-log hierarchy and its generic boundaries through PL-292--PL-295, conditional on the PL-283--PL-284 finite spectral certificate at `a=0.8` where that endpoint is invoked. [PL-296](../../findings/PL-296-volterra-ball-poisson-formula-boundary-contradiction.md) additionally rejects a recent explicit Volterra torsion formula as a model for the zero-exterior logarithmic-Laplacian boundary channel. Positive-order regularity and fixed sign of the actual selected ground state remain open.

PL-285--PL-292 separate generic graph regularity from the much stronger all-log regularity of actual source-static eigenstates. The latter gives a translation modulus faster than every fixed inverse-log power but still slower than every Hölder power. Generic cutoff costs prevent that bootstrap from automatically becoming positive-order Sobolev regularity.

PL-293 identifies the universal boundary obstruction: the sharp zero-exterior logarithmic-Laplacian torsion scale is `ell(delta)^(1/2)`, and a nonvanishing channel at that order excludes `H^s` for every `s>=1/2`. PL-294 separately ends ordinary Beurling--Deny positivity before the first prime-power translation. PL-295 then shows that fixed sign is not open in `H^log` or `H^s`, `s<=1/2`, and that eventual positivity strong enough to help already implies positivity of the limiting ground projection.

PL-296 is an important route guardrail. A published compactly supported Volterra candidate for `L_Delta u=1` on a ball has boundary order `ell(delta)`, contradicting the audited sharp `ell(delta)^(1/2)` two-sided torsion law on sufficiently small balls under the same Fourier normalization. The semigroup derivation also interchanges an indefinite whole-space multiplier with the restricted zero-exterior Dirichlet inverse. That formula therefore cannot be used to weaken the PL-293 boundary channel or as an explicit model for the localized-Weil eigenfunction boundary coefficient.

The two surviving resources remain separate. A source-specific `H^s`, `0<s<1/2`, gain can still improve non-absolute translation control but does not protect sign. The sign gate needs information from the **actual rational-prime eigen-equation**: a boundary-weighted lower law, source-specific oscillation/maximum principle, absolute-value defect identity, or another order mechanism unavailable to matched generic operators.

**Boundary.** PL-296 refutes one explicit inverse route, not the possibility of source-specific boundary cancellation. PL-295 is a method obstruction, not a sign-change theorem; its perturbations are not eigenfunctions.
