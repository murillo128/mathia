# MI-016 — Completed Weil order lives in prevention of one-sided residual crossings

**Evidence level:** proved observability/order/transport boundary through PL-280; completed arithmetic positivity remains open.

The complete Galerkin/cutoff family can recover the source, but source recovery and exact cutoff coherence are generic information properties rather than sign principles. PL-272--PL-276 show that every nonzero finite positive interior source is eventually sign-indefinite and no fixed compact self-adjoint correction repairs that high-frequency Morse defect.

PL-278 identifies what the canonical noncompact completion does buy. The localized completed Weil operator has compact resolvent and only finitely many negative eigenvalues, and eventual positivity of the high spectral tail survives arbitrary finite signed source replacement. The RH-facing information is therefore confined to the finite residual negative sector.

PL-279 then shows that an individual newly active prime-power shift is quadratically soft in the natural Sobolev form topology. The order-one edge shock seen in cutoff-rescaled finite models is not an intrinsic singularity of the canonical form.

PL-280 closes the possible healing mechanism. Because the localized form domains are nested under aperture growth, Courant--Fischer gives

\[
\lambda_k(b)\le\lambda_k(a)\qquad(a<b)
\]

for every ordered variational level. Hence the negative Morse index is nondecreasing. Once a level crosses below zero, later prime-power events cannot restore it to the nonnegative sector.

The source-specific problem is therefore **crossing prevention**, not post-crossing repair. A viable mechanism must constrain the accumulated low/mesoscopic source before any negative trial subspace appears, for example through a coercive completed-Weil inequality or another arithmetic restriction on the first variational levels. Individual threshold softness and later signed compensation are insufficient.

**Boundary.** PL-280 is min-max domain monotonicity, not differentiability or continuity of eigenbranches, and it supplies no source-specific positivity theorem. It only makes the residual index ledger one-sided.
