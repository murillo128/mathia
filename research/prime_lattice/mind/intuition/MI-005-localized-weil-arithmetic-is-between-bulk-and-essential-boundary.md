# MI-005 — Completed Weil localization has a wide collapse regime before essential prime-log recurrence

**Evidence level:** supported by exact localized-operator limits, completion cancellation, zero-sampling bounds, and inverse zero certificates

## Core intuition

The localized Weil operator has a topology-sensitive split, but the unexplored mesoscopic middle is now narrower. Completion cancels the universal PNT boundary mode. Fixed smoothing, spatial decompactification, and large moving frequency bands collapse, while essential prime-log recurrence survives only on very late moving states. The natural fixed-depth band itself now has an inverse interpretation: any order-one defect below the archimedean scale certifies a sufficiently rightward zero rather than producing an independent new limit.

## Strongest justified principle

PL-044 and PL-049--PL-054 establish the original split: finite localized spectral reality can be prime-free; fixed-depth strong boundary limits vanish or universalize; operator norm and essential norm retain order-one prime-power partial reflections through recurrence. PL-055--PL-058 close fixed Sobolev smoothing and spatial repairs, and PL-059 identifies the canonical pole/PNT cancellation.

PL-060--PL-065 push norm collapse into moving Dirichlet bands using classical zero-free sampling. PL-066 sharpens the same estimate before any zero-free region is inserted. If the naturally normalized fixed-depth compression has an order-one defect with `log N=o(exp L)`, then a nontrivial zeta zero must lie quantitatively near the right edge prescribed by `log log N/L`. In particular, under RH every such band collapses. The statement is a negative topology certificate: noncollapse would reveal a rightward zero, not a new RH-compatible boundary object.

The essential recurrence from prime logs remains outside these collapse estimates at ultra-high moving frequency. Reaching it is not itself RH evidence; the lower bound is generated by classical Diophantine recurrence and prime-log structure.

## What remains possible

A surviving topology must operate near or beyond the unresolved transition, or be genuinely different from the audited band/smoothing/spatial families. It must be defined before inspecting the zero divisor, prove convergence or a stable invariant, and survive Beurling controls. The fixed-smooth second-scale regime is handled separately by MI-006 because there the zero expansion itself becomes the object.

## Status / novelty

The completion cancellation, smoothing/spatial no-go results, moving-band bounds, recurrence-delay estimates, and PL-066 inverse certificate are persisted findings. The exact existence or nonexistence of a rational-prime-specific middle topology remains open.

## Falsification criterion

Construct an intrinsic completed-Weil topology inside one of the ruled-out regimes with a nonzero stable residual, or prove a canonical topology at the remaining transition whose invariant differs on matched generalized-prime systems for a reason independent of the known zero divisor.
