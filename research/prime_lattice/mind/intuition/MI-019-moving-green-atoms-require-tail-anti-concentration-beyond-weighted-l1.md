# MI-019 — Moving Green atoms require source-specific anti-concentration beyond global regularity and bounded forcing

**Evidence level:** exact moving-diagonal obstruction and sufficient tail criterion from [PL-314](../../findings/PL-314-moving-diagonal-tail-threshold.md), strengthened by [PL-315](../../findings/PL-315-moving-diagonal-survives-all-log-subcritical-sobolev.md), [PL-316](../../findings/PL-316-critical-half-derivative-still-misses-moving-diagonal.md), [PL-317](../../findings/PL-317-supercritical-zero-extension-forces-moving-tail-decay.md), and the bounded-forcing obstruction [PL-318](../../findings/PL-318-bounded-logarithmic-forcing-moving-diagonal-obstruction.md).

A weak integrated tail norm can miss the exact region sampled by a singularly moving kernel. In the Prime-Lattice stretched coordinate, the Green measure develops an atom at fixed `q=r`, while the corresponding physical logarithmic distance moves to `Q=r/s`. The normalized transfer therefore probes `m(r/s)/s`, not just total tail mass.

PL-314 constructs one fixed smooth nonnegative `m in L^1(dQ)` whose sparse bumps have vanishing total mass but height `Theta(s_n)` at `Q=r/s_n`. The moving atom sees each selected bump with order-one weight, so the rank-one limit predicted from the integral alone fails. The simple source-independent condition `Qm(Q)->0` is sufficient to kill that contribution but is not claimed necessary.

PL-315--PL-316 show how robust the obstruction is. It survives `C^infty` regularity, every `W^{k,1}(dQ)`, arbitrarily small physical `W^{1,1}`, all finite logarithmic Fourier moments, every zero-extension `H^alpha` with `alpha<1/2`, and even arbitrarily small critical `H^{1/2}` norm. PL-317 places the generic Sobolev success boundary just above that: any fixed `H^{1/2+eta}` zero-extension gain forces a true zero trace and exponential decay in `Q`, hence suppresses the moving sample.

PL-318 adds a different source-side negative result. The same sparse saturation can be arranged while the natural logarithmic zero-order forcing `L_Delta u` stays bounded. After scaling, that forcing can be arbitrarily small without removing the order-one moving transfer. Thus **bounded right-hand side is not the missing theorem either**: ordinary zero-order control of the source equation does not upgrade the tail to `Qm(Q)->0`.

The reusable diagnostic is now sharper. Global regularity, critical Sobolev control and bounded zero-order forcing all describe aggregate or local norm size; the moving observer asks for anti-concentration at a scale coupled to `s`. Success must come from a structural state--source relation that forbids sparse saturation where the observer moves, or from a genuinely supercritical boundary trace theorem.

For the completed-Weil branch, a useful theorem must force something such as `Qm(Q)->0`, an asymptotic expansion with an `o(1/Q)` remainder, monotone or one-sided tail structure, a cancellation law tied to the actual completed source, or a direct estimate of `m(r/s)` strong enough to replace pointwise decay. Merely proving a bounded forcing term is no longer progress toward this gate.

**Boundary.** PL-318 does not assert that the actual completed-Weil eigen-equation admits the constructed sparse mismatch; it shows only that bounded zero-order forcing does not exclude it. The actual state--source coupling may contain additional arithmetic or sign structure. Supercritical zero-extension regularity remains sufficient but is not claimed necessary.
