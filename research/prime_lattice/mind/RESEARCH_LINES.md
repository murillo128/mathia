## Keep exact-kernel transfer, regulator renormalization, endpoint localization and actual prime source distinct

The continuation kernel and critical model covariance are no longer the bottlenecks. Nor is any fixed fractional denominator interior a fundamental model-side barrier: PL-410 can suppress it below the zero-sensitive scale with finitely many exact cusp counterterms chosen for that fixed range.

The surviving model-side difficulty is the moving endpoint `q/H=Theta(1)`. The surviving arithmetic difficulty is still source replacement there, or an equivalent prime-variance theorem of sufficient precision. A future proof must specify whether it controls the full variance remainder or the single signed cubic functional, use the exact finite counterterms rather than only their leading asymptotics when that precision matters, and keep the model endpoint separate from the actual prime spectral measure. Failure of an absolute-value calibration identifies discarded sign information; it is not evidence that the underlying signed endpoint functional fails.
