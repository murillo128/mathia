## Find a genuinely global mechanism outside scalar characters and finite first-order jets

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-016-weil-form-observability-is-stronger-than-moment-observability` through `MI-042-finite-dimensional-complete-multiplicativity-filters-through-scalar-characters-and-first-jets`.

PL-345--PL-368 close the obvious completion, multiplicativity, functional-equation, coefficient-topology and positive-summation escapes. Finite filters give periodic continuations, unrestricted filters are programmable, ambient sequence geometry manufactures artificial critical walls, and scalar Dirichlet Hardy geometry produces a generic `1/2` boundary without selecting zeta zeros.

PL-369 identifies the source-forced nonlinear theorem interface: a bounded completely multiplicative `a` whose Dirichlet series extends holomorphically to `Re(s)>sigma_0` and vanishes at `1` forces zeta to be zero-free there. PL-370 makes the boundary zero locally Liouville-rigid, PL-371 shows local prime-harmonic closeness alone gives no continuation gain, and PL-373 fixes every genuine scalar Venturini witness to the Liouville divisor up to a zero-free holomorphic gauge.

PL-374 tests the most immediate matrix-valued escape. Any finite-dimensional completely multiplicative representation has commuting prime matrices and therefore a common triangular flag. Its composition factors are scalar completely multiplicative characters; trace is their finite sum and every local determinant factor is their product. If the prime matrices are normal or unitary, the representation is already a direct orthogonal sum of scalar channels.

PL-375 then classifies the first nonnormal escape in dimension two. Distinct diagonal characters force the extension to split by one fixed shear. A nonvanishing self-extension is exactly

`rho(n)=chi(n)(I+d(n)N)`, `N^2=0`,

with `d` completely additive. It is the first tangent jet of the scalar character family `chi_t(n)=chi(n)e^(t d(n))`. For the canonical lattice direction `d(n)=log n`, its Dirichlet-series coefficient is just `-L_chi'(s)`. Thus a `2x2` Jordan upgrade does not independently create continuation or a new zero divisor.

The live question has moved outside both scalar coefficient freedom and the first finite-dimensional nonnormal enlargement. A materially distinct mechanism must use higher extension structure that does not collapse to scalar jets, an infinite-dimensional operator algebra, break exact complete multiplicativity so prime directions genuinely interact, or introduce a target-relative/global observable that does not factor through the scalar composition factors. Merely replacing scalars by matrices is not a new source geometry.

## Treat coefficient algebra, scalar filtration, extension data and continuation as separate budgets

The divisor-convolution identity is coefficient algebra. Complete multiplicativity is a nonlinear prime-axis law. Pole neutrality is a local boundary condition. Holomorphic continuation is global analytic power. PL-373 shows that inside the scalar Venturini class the divisor is fixed up to a zero-free gauge; PL-374 shows that finite-dimensional complete multiplicativity still has only scalar composition factors for trace/determinant observables; PL-375 shows that the first nonsplit self-extension is a completely additive tangent direction.

A proposed Prime-Lattice mechanism should therefore identify exactly where it exits this hierarchy. Finite dimension plus complete multiplicativity does not by itself create irreducible cross-prime coupling. Nonnormality matters only if the destination observable actually sees extension data, and in dimension two the canonical logarithmic extension is still a derivative of the scalar series. Any claimed analytic gain must be derived independently rather than encoded in continuation of the underlying scalar channel.
