PL-237 shows that retaining only the abstract increasing order of the primes through spreadability does not help on the ordinary tensor product; spreadability collapses back to exchangeability.

PL-238 closes another generic repair. After group completion, any scalar projective curvature that is continuous in the canonical one-dimensional `log n` topology is trivial. PL-239 finally inserts genuinely source-specific prime order and consecutive log gaps before spectralization. The commutator is nontrivial and sits exactly at the `S_{1+}` endpoint, but its standard self-adjoint `det_2` divisor is tautologically the reciprocal local-gap list, not a zeta localization mechanism.

The surviving route must therefore use a **source-forced relation that couples actual prime labels beyond generic order type, a continuous scalar log-energy cocycle, or the bare nearest-neighbor gap determinant**. A twisted/nonlocal algebra, multiscale gap relation, mixed-prime incidence, or other coupling is admissible only when its defining relation is derived from rational-prime arithmetic before state evaluation.

## Treat symmetry, scalar log twists, and local-gap spectralization as controls

Completion, noncommutativity, phase transition, nonnormality, abstract order, or a real source-forced determinant divisor do not by themselves restore the missing global arithmetic relation. In the tested categories they yield scalar boundary data, generic product mixtures, coboundary curvature, or a determinant that merely restates local prime gaps.

A useful construction must show which nonlocal relation survives equilibrium/quotienting and why it carries parity-sensitive large-tail information rather than programmably reinserting prime labels.

## Keep Nyman target sensitivity separate from programmable model-space spectralization

Target sensitivity is not target selection. Any prime-lattice model-space, quotient, or twisted completion must derive its inner/quotient/coupling data from the source before inspecting a desired zero or sign.
