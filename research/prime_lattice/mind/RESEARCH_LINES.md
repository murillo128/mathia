This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-lattice intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Force a nonfactorizing and divisor-complete global relation for the principal zeta factor

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-011-addition-is-first-nonmultiplicative-escape-but-affine-traces-hit-analytic-boundaries`, `MI-014-prime-exchangeability-erases-normal-arithmetic-equilibrium`, `MI-015-reciprocity-family-coupling-is-not-principal-zero-selection`.

PL-240--PL-252 show that rich canonical arithmetic constructions can retain substantial global structure while the principal zeta block remains a direct factor. Finite representation functors and positive Rankin--Selberg self-correlation therefore do not constrain hypothetical off-line principal zeros merely by being canonical or positive.

PL-253 tests a stronger global object. Maaß--Selberg/Arthur truncation supplies genuine Hilbert-space positivity whose continued spectral decomposition contains the zeta explicit-formula block, but only together with a signed remainder; positivity of the whole object does not isolate the Weil zero form.

PL-254--PL-255 add a complementary selector warning. Designed pseudolaplacians can be self-adjoint and force every **selected** L-zero onto the critical line while still seeing only a sub-divisor. Squaring such a selector with a self-adjoint Dirac realization does not improve completeness unless the first-order domain/coupling itself changes.

The live construction must therefore be both nonfactorizing and **divisor-complete**: its source-forced relation must reach the full principal zeta divisor, not a selected subsequence, and its sign/spectral theorem must survive the decomposition without leaving an uncontrolled remainder equivalent to the target.

## Treat global positivity, self-adjoint selection, and formal Dirac squaring as controls

A positive completed distribution can hide the desired zero term behind a signed complement, while a real spectrum can encode only selected zeros. Any spectral route must prove completeness separately from reality; any positivity route must prove the target summand inherits the sign separately from positivity of the ambient form.
