## Extract arithmetic structure from the completed near-diagonal saddle--transition layer

**Linked intuition:** `MI-044-finite-prime-wold-classification-does-not-globalize-atomically`.

PL-391--PL-393 show that zero-faithful continuation can disappear from both `B^2` and fixed-`N` Jessen quotient averages. PL-394 shows that the frozen one-sided Gram at the Riemann--Siegel conductor still tends to Haar, while the continuation-faithful approximate functional equation retains reflected modes and the Gamma-phase cross channel.

PL-395 proves that the broad off-diagonal stationary shell is absent from the **sharp** moving Riemann--Siegel main sum: an off-diagonal pair reaches its nominal saddle before the larger mode is admitted. PL-396 shows why that conclusion cannot be promoted to completed zeta. In the exact Paris--Cang incomplete-gamma representation, fixed additive gaps `q=r+d` have order-one transition weights at `t=2 pi qr`; the sharp activation offset, incomplete-gamma transition width and stationary-phase width are all `Theta(r)`. The surviving object is therefore a coalescing saddle--transition layer, not a hard-cutoff endpoint.

This does not restore the broad shell of PL-394. Ratio-separated pairs remain suppressed, and the robust layer satisfies `log(q/r)=O(t^(-1/2))`. Moreover the local calculation still depends only on the scalar log-energy `log n`, the conductor and the archimedean smoothing kernel; rewriting it in prime-exponent coordinates adds no source-specific rigidity by itself.

The live question is now sharper: after the phase, incomplete-gamma transition weight and explicit archimedean correction are treated uniformly in an exact continuation-faithful representation, is there any invariant completed observable in the near-diagonal layer that retains factor labels or another genuinely prime-specific feature and remains individually zero-sensitive? A candidate should survive changes among exact smooth continuation charts or be tied to a representation-independent completed quantity. Otherwise the layer is classical continuation geometry with the prime lattice serving only as coordinates.

## Keep sharp-cutoff geometry, smooth continuation and arithmetic discrimination separate

PL-395 remains exact for the canonical sharp main sum, and PL-396 remains a representation refinement rather than an RH mechanism. Neither the disappearance of the sharp off-diagonal saddle nor the survival of fixed-gap smooth weights is itself arithmetic rigidity. Future constructions must state which completed quantity is invariant, why it is not determined solely by scalar energies, and how it avoids the scalar product/factor-label quotient while detecting individual zeros rather than only transition or density effects.
