`(1+S_delta)/(|C_delta| sqrt(L_delta)) -> 0`.

PL-330 proves norm-resolvent continuity and transports isolated eigenbranches continuously in `L^2`/graph topology, while PL-331 shows every fixed logarithmic Fourier seminorm still misses the hostile moving layer. Qualitative spectral or all-log regularity is therefore not a replacement for the source-amplitude estimate.

PL-332 now removes one of the two branch-specific unknowns **conditionally on that same uniform endpoint remainder**. If the PL-329 residual bound is family-uniform, any phase-aligned `L^2`-continuous branch satisfies `C_delta -> C_0`: a sufficiently remote but fixed logarithmic block recovers the asymptotic coefficient to `O(R^(-1/2))`, after which ordinary `L^2` continuity applies. Coefficient collapse is therefore not an independent moving-layer problem once the uniform source/remainder estimate has been established.

The first-prime frontier is correspondingly narrower. Prove the actual completed-Weil branch has the uniform endpoint/source control required by PL-329, and prove the **single fixed-threshold statement** `C_0 != 0`. If the source amplitude is uniformly bounded, PL-332 then gives `|C_delta|` bounded away from zero and PL-329 forces the first-prime reflected sign. No growing family noncollapse theorem is separately needed.
