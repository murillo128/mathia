# Prime-lattice research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Prime-Lattice intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Find a source-forced intermediate family that survives the completion topology

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-016-weil-form-observability-is-stronger-than-moment-observability` through `MI-036-ordinary-and-complete-multiplicativity-bracket-the-h2-rigidity-gap`.

PL-345--PL-347 classify the two obvious endpoint extremes. The centered same-state channel is the classical first logarithmic Riesz criterion, while the complete Fourier-modulation Gram family is a form core for the localized completed Weil form and therefore reconstructs ordinary Weil positivity. PL-348--PL-352 quantify how finite-axis sparsity loses its Beurling--Malliavin barrier once natural multiplicative closure is admitted.

PL-353 then shows that finite coefficient restrictions create a genuine closed intermediate family only when they are continuous in the destination Sobolev/form topology. Finite centering, finite logarithmic moments, finite real-phase jets and finite `p^(-sigma)` pole-cancellation relations disappear after closure. PL-354 gives the finite-support opposite extreme: finitely many exact positive-real Dirichlet evaluations remain topologically invisible, whereas an infinite distinct exact stack kills every finite Dirichlet polynomial.

PL-355 shows that the last statement is **not stable under the natural prime `l2` completion**. In the prime-supported Hilbert space `sum_p c_p p^(-s)` with `c in l2(P)`, point evaluation is continuous exactly for `Re s>1/2`, and classical Seip zero-set theory produces nonzero pure-prime series vanishing on bounded real Blaschke stacks accumulating at `1/2`. PL-356 makes the common zero-stack nullspace infinite-dimensional and shows that every finite family of bounded homogeneous coefficient repairs still leaves infinitely many solutions. Finite low-prime exclusions, finitely many Hilbert moments or finite orthogonality repairs therefore cannot restore the finite-support rigidity of PL-354.

PL-357--PL-358 now bracket the most canonical nonlinear Euler-product repair inside the native `H^2` topology. Complete multiplicativity forces every coefficient vector to be a joint backward-prime-shift eigenvector, equivalently a product Szegő reproducing kernel. Its Euler product is absolutely convergent and zero-free throughout `Re s>1/2`. Ordinary multiplicativity fails in the opposite direction: the Bohr lift tensorizes into independent one-prime factors, every native zero comes from one local factor, and any prescribed point of `1/2<Re s<1` can be made a zero with `|a_n|<=1` while keeping every degree-one prime coefficient nonzero.

The live intermediate-family problem is therefore sharper than “add nonlinear multiplicative structure.” A useful source law must constrain the complete prime-power strings strongly enough to prevent one-prime zero programming, while avoiding the geometric local law `a_(p^k)=a_p^k` that collapses the family to zero-free reproducing kernels. The missing structure must couple degrees or primes globally—possibly through completion, a functional equation, target-relative Nyman/Weil constraints, or another infinite relation whose effect survives the chosen completion topology. Each candidate still needs an exact nontriviality and destination-sufficiency test.

## Treat completeness radius, coefficient topology and boundary interpolation as separate budgets

Beurling--Malliavin density measures how far a restricted modulation family tests the localized form. PL-353 measures whether algebraic coefficient restrictions survive the destination topology. PL-354--PL-356 add a third budget: changing the source completion can turn an infinite exact evaluation stack from algebraically fatal into a large closed nullspace. PL-357--PL-358 add a fourth: an infinite nonlinear law can still be either too rigid or too local, depending on what it does to the one-prime factors in the completed space.

A proposed intermediate family should therefore state its closure law, effective log-frequency density, coefficient topology, evaluation/interpolation boundary, prime-power coupling and destination theorem together. Finite support, local Sobolev closure and native prime `l2`/Dirichlet `H^2` completion have genuinely different nullspaces and Euler-factor freedoms; a conclusion proved in one cannot be transferred to another by formal analogy.

## Keep centering, explicit-formula identity, family closure and source completion distinct

Raw endpoint recurrence is PNT-universal; centering introduces the classical zero expansion; same-state completion gives the Riesz criterion; complete modulation gives localized Weil positivity. Prime-log density and coefficient-dual results classify many nominally sparse repairs. PL-354--PL-356 show that exact evaluation rigidity itself depends sharply on the source topology. PL-357--PL-358 then show that even after choosing the standard `H^2` completion, multiplicativity alone does not select a useful middle family: ordinary multiplicativity leaves local zero programmability, while complete multiplicativity over-rigidifies the coefficients into zero-free kernels.

The next Prime-Lattice mechanism must therefore add canonical arithmetic coupling that is genuinely cross-prime or cross-degree and remains visible after completion, rather than treating the critical evaluation boundary, its Brownian kernel, or multiplicativity by itself as zero selection.
