# Prime-lattice research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-lattice intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Require a source-forced relation among prime channels, not merely richer operator transport

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-prime-shift-symmetry-is-not-a-zero-hamiltonian-principle`, `MI-007-pointing-breaks-gauge-but-exposes-additive-correlations`, `MI-011-addition-is-first-nonmultiplicative-escape-but-affine-traces-hit-analytic-boundaries`, `MI-012-one-point-target-complexity-still-scalarizes-under-equidistribution`.

PL-193--PL-207 close scalar phase gauges, state-dependent flat connections, broad fiberwise resolvent transports, fixed-edge exact covariance, and affine spectral reparameterization. PL-208--PL-210 show that making the transfer genuinely spectral-parameter-dependent or multichannel is not enough by itself. In particular, PL-210 constructs noncommuting finite-rank prime channels carrying the exact `log p` coefficients, with irreducible joint operator algebra, cyclic target vector, trace-class relative resolvent differences, and path-compatible determinants, while the target spectral measure remains arbitrarily programmable.

The surviving operator route therefore needs a **source-forced arithmetic relation among channels** whose spectral or trace consequence fails on these programmable controls. Noncommutativity, irreducibility, target cyclicity, exact prime weights, and finite-rank path compatibility are now controls rather than evidence of a Riemann selector.

## Treat Bost--Connes temperature geometry and scalar profiles as controls, not half-critical selectors

PL-211--PL-215 show that the canonical high-temperature Bost--Connes route has its intrinsic sector boundary at `beta=1`, not at `beta=1/2`: distinct canonical temperatures are disjoint whenever one parameter lies in `0<beta<=1`, and the adelic product-measure class changes at the thermodynamic threshold.

PL-216 makes the obstruction infinitesimal. The diagonal Fisher length of the canonical temperature direction is infinite throughout `0<beta<=1`, so there is no square-integrable global temperature tangent at the Riemann half. Prime-dependent same-measure-class perturbations do exist, but their admissible tangent cone is merely a freely selectable weighted `ell^2` family and supplies no arithmetic compatibility by itself.

PL-217 closes the scalar-profile escape from the other side. On every compact high-temperature interval, finite algebraic observables generated by a **single prime ray** have KMS expectation profiles dense in all continuous functions of `beta`; arbitrary finite patterns can be interpolated exactly. Rich dependence on temperature is therefore programmable before any cross-prime arithmetic enters.

A live Bost--Connes route must use genuinely non-diagonal same-sector data, a source-forced relation among prime channels, or a canonical global trace/positivity identity that survives both the sector/tangent obstruction and one-prime scalar-profile programmability. Neither the existence of a distinguished real temperature axis nor a striking profile near `1/2` is evidence of zero selection.

## Require a parity-sensitive large-tail coupling before assigning arithmetic meaning to the affine carrier

PL-184--PL-188 show that bounded-variation targets, polylogarithmic residue labels, and shifted-prime exponent vectors below any subpower cutoff are controlled by classical one-point information. The genuinely unresolved Möbius/Liouville content lies in the large-prime tail or in a coupling that reaches it. Enlarging the local coordinate block without a parity-sensitive cross-tail theorem is not enough.

## Keep Nyman target sensitivity but reject arbitrary-inner model-space spectralization

The off-line Blaschke factor is genuine target/model-space information that generator Gram geometry cannot see, but PL-204 shows that compressing the prime semigroup to an arbitrary model space faithfully encodes whichever inner divisor was supplied. Zero sensitivity is therefore not zero selection. A useful Nyman/prime-lattice construction must contain a source-forced relation unavailable for arbitrary inner functions and use it to derive a sign, coercivity, or localization theorem.
