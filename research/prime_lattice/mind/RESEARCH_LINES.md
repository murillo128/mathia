# Prime-lattice research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-lattice intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Require a source-forced relation among prime channels, not merely richer operator transport

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-prime-shift-symmetry-is-not-a-zero-hamiltonian-principle`, `MI-007-pointing-breaks-gauge-but-exposes-additive-correlations`, `MI-011-addition-is-first-nonmultiplicative-escape-but-affine-traces-hit-analytic-boundaries`, `MI-012-one-point-target-complexity-still-scalarizes-under-equidistribution`.

PL-193--PL-210 close scalar phase gauges, state-dependent flat connections, broad fiberwise resolvent transports, fixed-edge exact covariance, affine spectral reparameterization, and programmable noncommuting finite-rank prime channels. Noncommutativity, irreducibility, target cyclicity, exact `log p` weights, and finite-rank path compatibility are controls rather than evidence of a Riemann selector.

PL-219--PL-223 now close a large part of the canonical Bost--Connes semigroup escape as well. In particular, for every finite prime set `F`, the `C*`-algebra generated by the prime isometries is exactly a tensor product of unilateral Toeplitz algebras, and every Bost--Connes KMS state restricts to the corresponding product Toeplitz KMS state. Consequently **all bounded finite-order and multi-time correlations inside the finite-prime semigroup-isometry sector** have a matched non-arithmetic model obtained by replacing `log p` with generic rationally independent energies.

The surviving operator route therefore needs a source-forced arithmetic relation that leaves this finite-prime semigroup factorization — for example through a genuinely coupled cyclotomic/diagonal sector, an intrinsically infinite-prime completion, an unbounded/relative modular object, or another global relation — and must prove a spectral or trace consequence that fails on the generic Toeplitz controls. These are admissible categories to test, not positive evidence by themselves.

## Treat Bost--Connes temperature geometry, positive scalar profiles, and finite-prime semigroup correlations as programmable controls

PL-211--PL-218 show that the canonical high-temperature Bost--Connes route has its intrinsic sector boundary at `beta=1`, not `beta=1/2`; the diagonal Fisher direction is singular throughout the high-temperature sector; and finite-prime positive contractions already program arbitrary continuous `[0,1]` temperature profiles on compact subcritical intervals.

PL-219--PL-223 extend the control from scalar profiles to the bounded semigroup correlation algebra. Gauge/KMS structure does not rescue the half-line, finite-prime bounded correlations are pure product-Toeplitz data, and increasing correlation order inside that sector does not create an arithmetic coupling. Low-temperature arithmetic symmetry breaking and the cyclotomic diagonal live outside this restricted algebra, so they are not ruled out by the factorization.

A live Bost--Connes route must therefore use genuinely non-diagonal arithmetic data or a global infinite-prime/unbounded construction whose admissibility and sign are source-forced. Positivity, finite prime support, norm control, noncommutativity, arbitrary bounded correlation order, and a striking scalar profile near `1/2` are now matched controls rather than evidence of zero selection.

## Require a parity-sensitive large-tail coupling before assigning arithmetic meaning to the affine carrier

PL-184--PL-188 show that bounded-variation targets, polylogarithmic residue labels, and shifted-prime exponent vectors below any subpower cutoff are controlled by classical one-point information. The genuinely unresolved Möbius/Liouville content lies in the large-prime tail or in a coupling that reaches it. Enlarging the local coordinate block without a parity-sensitive cross-tail theorem is not enough.

## Keep Nyman target sensitivity but reject arbitrary-inner model-space spectralization

The off-line Blaschke factor is genuine target/model-space information that generator Gram geometry cannot see, but PL-204 shows that compressing the prime semigroup to an arbitrary model space faithfully encodes whichever inner divisor was supplied. Zero sensitivity is therefore not zero selection. A useful Nyman/prime-lattice construction must contain a source-forced relation unavailable for arbitrary inner functions and use it to derive a sign, coercivity, or localization theorem.
