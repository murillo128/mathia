## Transfer the exact cubic continuation kernel from model covariance to prime variance

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-044-finite-prime-wold-classification-does-not-globalize-atomically`, `MI-045-fixed-gap-meet-join-data-collapse-to-finite-congruence-decoration`, `MI-046-growing-gap-arithmetic-complexity-pays-a-reciprocal-continuation-tariff`, `MI-047-mesoscopic-continuation-phase-can-miss-the-prime-pair-parity-sector`, `MI-048-cubic-phase-can-reopen-arithmetic-support-yet-average-to-a-universal-control`, `MI-049-abel-cubic-singular-series-asymptotics-isolate-an-rh-equivalent-zero-layer-at-model-level`, `MI-050-the-zero-sensitive-exact-kernel-bridge-now-ends-in-a-signed-prime-variance-problem`.

PL-391--PL-403 separate frozen multiplicative geometry from the continuation-faithful transition layer, identify the cubic scale `d~r^(1/3)`, expose the zeta-zero layer of the pair singular series, and Abel-regularize it into an RH-equivalent `H^(-7/4+epsilon)` remainder after the explicit deterministic terms are removed.

PL-404 closes the exact-kernel transfer problem left by PL-403. A quadratic vanishing factor `(d/H)^2` suppresses the fixed/subcubic gap region without killing any zeta-zero Mellin pole, while the first omitted lower-transition term contributes an explicit universal `H^-1` correction. The resulting exact incomplete-gamma statistic differs from the shifted Abel singular-series average by `O_eta(H^-2(log H)^3)`, below the zero-sensitive scale. The continuation kernel is therefore no longer the live quantitative bottleneck.

PL-405 identifies the remaining model source as the critical off-diagonal Ramanujan covariance of `Lambda_1=(phi(n)/n)Lambda(n)`. Its lag-zero energy diverges logarithmically, so ordinary `B^2` Wiener--Khintchine passage to actual prime data fails at the endpoint. PL-406 then shows that this diagonal divergence is projected out by the cubic test itself: the even kernel vanishes to fourth order at zero and has exact additive-Fourier and triangular short-interval second-moment representations.

The live bridge is consequently a specific signed **prime short-interval variance / zero-pair-correlation** problem. Can the actual modified-Mangoldt quadratic statistic be compared with the singular-series source at error `o(H^-7/4)` in the cubic regime? Fixed-logarithmic almost-all-shift Hardy--Littlewood savings are asymptotically too weak when `H` is a fixed power of the ambient prime scale. A viable transfer must exploit cancellation in the declared sign-changing cubic kernel, a sufficiently quantitative zero-pair theorem, or another source identity that reaches the same power scale.

## Keep exact-kernel transfer, model covariance and actual prime source distinct

PL-404 materially strengthens PL-403: the RH-equivalent zero layer now survives the exact incomplete-gamma continuation kernel with room in the error budget. PL-405--PL-406 then move the remaining obstruction away from the continuation kernel and away from the divergent Ramanujan diagonal.

What is still missing is a theorem for the actual arithmetic source. The singular-series covariance is a critical model, and the cubic weight converts its replacement into one signed quadratic prime-variance functional. One such test is not the full strong pair-correlation conjecture, and generic averaged absolute-error bounds need not see its sign cancellation. The remaining Prime-Lattice theorem is therefore a quantitative source-replacement result at the `H^-7/4` layer, not another regulator, leading-average calculation or incomplete-gamma asymptotic.
