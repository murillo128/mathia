## Leave the KMS residue diagonal and require an off-diagonal source-forced relation among prime channels

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-prime-shift-symmetry-is-not-a-zero-hamiltonian-principle`, `MI-007-pointing-breaks-gauge-but-exposes-additive-correlations`, `MI-011-addition-is-first-nonmultiplicative-escape-but-affine-traces-hit-analytic-boundaries`, `MI-012-one-point-target-complexity-still-scalarizes-under-equidistribution`.

PL-193--PL-223 close scalar phase gauges, programmable finite-rank transport, finite-prime Bost--Connes semigroup correlations, and the bounded all-prime norm completion. The prime-isometry algebra over all primes is a countable tensor product of Toeplitz factors, so simply passing from finitely many primes to the bounded norm closure does not create an arithmetic coupling.

PL-224--PL-229 close several canonical infinite-prime and weak-completion repairs. Cyclotomic corners collapse by lcm; the standard prime-shift von Neumann closure is all of `B(ell^2(N))`; scalar renormalization of canonical all-prime tails has no nonzero KMS-GNS limit; lcm and coprimality conditioning return classical Bost--Connes boundary states; and a one-dimensional Dirichlet compression produces an RH-equivalent half-plane only by explicit shifted reciprocal-zeta repackaging.

PL-230 now classifies the most canonical affine KMS residue readout left by the source-selection clue. For the affine residue projections `E_{k,a}=s^k v_a v_a^* s^{*k}`, the KMS identity itself forces `phi(E_{k,a})=a^{-beta}` for every state. Nica covariance turns Chinese-remainder refinement into exact projection multiplication, so the divisibility diagonal has independent geometric prime-valuation tails. Even inclusion-exclusion by the all-prime complement remains a positive projection probability with boundary `1/zeta(beta-1)` and transition at `beta=2`.

Thus a genuinely source-forced relation **does** exist on the affine residue diagonal, but it is too rigid and positive/local to select the desired sign or critical line. Low-temperature KMS freedom lives elsewhere. The surviving affine route must leave this diagonal channel and use off-diagonal/additive correlations, a target-relative completed trace/positivity structure, or another source relation whose sign/coercivity is not fixed by the elementary KMS scaling law and is not a prescribed scalar zeta transform.

## Treat completion, diagonal conditioning, and scalar interpolation as controls rather than arithmetic mechanisms

Finite-prime correlations, all-prime bounded norm closure, full weak closure in the standard representation, scalar tail renormalization, lcm/coprimality conditioning, affine KMS residue probabilities, and one-dimensional Dirichlet transforms now provide a broad control catalogue. Each can look more global than the preceding category while still yielding generic Toeplitz data, maximal programmability, classical Bost--Connes boundary states, rigid positive product laws, self-similarity, or explicit zeta repackaging.

A category exit is meaningful only when it introduces a source-forced relation that fails on these controls. Infinite prime support, unboundedness, affiliation, noncommutativity, a phase transition, KMS covariance, or an RH-equivalent transform is not enough by itself.

## Require a parity-sensitive large-tail coupling before assigning arithmetic meaning to the affine carrier

PL-184--PL-188 show that bounded-variation targets, polylogarithmic residue labels, and shifted-prime exponent vectors below any subpower cutoff are controlled by classical one-point information. The unresolved Möbius/Liouville content lies in the large-prime tail or in a coupling that reaches it. PL-230 adds that the canonical KMS residue diagonal cannot supply a signed escape: its prime-valuation law is fixed and positive. Enlarging the local coordinate block without a parity-sensitive off-diagonal/cross-tail theorem is not enough.

## Keep Nyman target sensitivity but reject arbitrary-inner model-space spectralization

The off-line Blaschke factor is genuine target/model-space information that generator Gram geometry cannot see, but PL-204 shows that compressing the prime semigroup to an arbitrary model space faithfully encodes whichever inner divisor was supplied. Zero sensitivity is therefore not zero selection. A useful Nyman/prime-lattice construction must contain a source-forced relation unavailable for arbitrary inner functions and use it to derive a sign, coercivity, or localization theorem.
