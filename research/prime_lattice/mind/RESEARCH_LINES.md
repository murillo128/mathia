This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Prime-Lattice intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Leave finite-dimensional exact complete multiplicativity, not merely scalar characters

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-016-weil-form-observability-is-stronger-than-moment-observability` through `MI-042-finite-dimensional-complete-multiplicativity-filters-through-scalar-characters-and-first-jets`.

PL-345--PL-368 close the obvious completion, multiplicativity, functional-equation, coefficient-topology and positive-summation escapes. PL-369--PL-373 identify the scalar nonlinear theorem interface and then show that every genuine scalar Venturini witness is fixed to the Liouville divisor up to a zero-free holomorphic gauge.

PL-374 shows that any finite-dimensional completely multiplicative matrix representation has commuting prime matrices and therefore scalar composition factors. PL-375 classifies the first nonnormal `2x2` case: distinct-character extensions split, while a nontrivial self-extension is exactly a completely additive first tangent jet of a scalar multiplicative family.

PL-376 closes the remaining finite-dimensional source-representation loopholes. The image algebra of any finite-dimensional completely multiplicative representation is a commutative Artinian algebra. After decomposition into local factors, every linear matrix observable is an exact finite multivariate Taylor jet of finitely many scalar completely multiplicative deformations. Higher nilpotent order only adds finitely many jet directions, and scalar residue channels that vanish at primes have bounded nilpotent prime depth rather than an unbounded new exponent-lattice geometry.

This result is algebraic, not analytic. It supplies no continuation of the auxiliary scalar Dirichlet series into the critical strip; such continuation must be proved independently. It also does not rule out nonlinear matrix observables, which may see finite nilpotent geometry, but any gain there comes from the nonlinear destination operation rather than irreducibly new finite-dimensional completely multiplicative source data.

The live frontier has therefore moved outside **finite-dimensional exact complete multiplicativity itself**. A materially new mechanism must use an infinite-dimensional or unbounded global representation, a controlled failure of complete multiplicativity that creates genuine cross-prime interaction, a global completion/boundary/scattering condition, or a target-relative/nonlinear observable whose extra content is independently strong enough to constrain the scalar analytic problem.

## Keep source algebra, observable nonlinearity and analytic continuation as separate budgets

Finite matrix dimension is no longer evidence of new arithmetic geometry. The source algebra remains a finite infinitesimal thickening of scalar multiplicative channels for every linear observable, even when the matrices are singular or highly nonnormal. Enlarging nilpotent order therefore does not by itself buy continuation, positivity, zero localization or a new divisor.

A future Prime-Lattice proposal should state exactly where it leaves this classification: whether the source algebra ceases to be Artinian, whether exact complete multiplicativity is broken, whether the destination uses genuinely nonlinear operator data, or whether a global analytic boundary condition contributes information not present in the finite jets. Formal Euler products must not be carried beyond their proved normal-convergence domain merely because the matrix source has been rewritten as scalar deformations.
