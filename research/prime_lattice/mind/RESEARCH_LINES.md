# Prime-lattice research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Prime-Lattice intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Leave scalarizable exact complete multiplicativity, but do not infer a global Wold model from finite-prime restrictions

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-016-weil-form-observability-is-stronger-than-moment-observability` through `MI-044-finite-prime-wold-classification-does-not-globalize-atomically`.

PL-345--PL-373 close the obvious completion, multiplicativity, functional-equation, coefficient-topology and scalar nonlinear theorem interfaces. PL-374--PL-376 then classify every finite-dimensional completely multiplicative matrix source for linear observables as a finite Artinian thickening of scalar multiplicative channels.

PL-377 pushes scalarization beyond finite dimension. A bounded normal completely multiplicative representation generates an abelian C*-algebra; after Gelfand transform, every integer acts pointwise by a scalar semicharacter, and every bounded linear observable is a measure mixture of those scalar channels.

PL-378 narrows the apparent nonnormal escape. If an invertible completely multiplicative representation extends to the rational group `Q_(>0)^x` and that **full group representation is uniformly bounded**, Day--Dixmier unitarization supplies one bounded similarity that turns the whole family into commuting unitaries. The spectral theorem then again gives a measure of scalar prime-torus characters. Nonnormality that disappears under one uniformly controlled change of Hilbert metric is therefore not an irreducible arithmetic coupling mechanism.

PL-379 shows why the surviving one-sided case cannot be dismissed by checking every finite set of primes. The positive-integer exponent semigroup is the countably generated free commutative monoid `N_0^infinity`, and prior art gives doubly commuting isometric representations of this semigroup for which every finite-coordinate restriction has the ordinary Wold decomposition but the full family has no global atomic pure/unitary decomposition. A diffuse Bernoulli field of coordinate types is invisible to every finite-prime classification yet obstructs globalization.

This is a genuine infinite-coordinate boundary, but not yet arithmetic structure: the counterexample uses neither `log p` nor any property distinguishing rational primes from an arbitrary countable generator set. The load-bearing question is therefore whether a **source-forced global arithmetic condition** excludes such diffuse type fields while retaining genuinely one-sided information, without simply collapsing back to the classical vacuum/log-energy or Bost--Connes models.

## Keep source algebra, finite-prime classification, global type measure, metric covariance and analytic continuation separate

Finite nilpotent thickness, infinite normal spectral multiplicity and uniformly bounded nonnormal group representations are different enlargements, but all can remain scalar after the appropriate exact algebraic or similarity reduction. Conversely, PL-379 shows that finite Wold classifications need not assemble into an atomic global classification at countably many prime coordinates.

A future Prime-Lattice proposal should therefore state exactly where it leaves these boundaries: which one-sided/domain obstruction prevents group completion, what global condition controls the infinite prime-type field, which inverse growth blocks unitarization, which noncommutative or nonmultiplicative relation survives, or which metric-sensitive observable has source-canonical meaning not removable by equivalent renorming. Formal Euler products must not be carried past their proved normal-convergence domain merely because scalar channels or shift sectors have been packaged as operators.
