## Find a source-forced intermediate family that survives the completion topology

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-016-weil-form-observability-is-stronger-than-moment-observability` through `MI-037-conductor-one-functional-equation-overshoots-native-h2-to-zero`.

PL-345--PL-347 classify the two obvious endpoint extremes. The centered same-state channel is the classical first logarithmic Riesz criterion, while the complete Fourier-modulation Gram family is a form core for the localized completed Weil form and therefore reconstructs ordinary Weil positivity. PL-348--PL-352 quantify how finite-axis sparsity loses its Beurling--Malliavin barrier once natural multiplicative closure is admitted.

PL-353 then shows that finite coefficient restrictions create a genuine closed intermediate family only when they are continuous in the destination Sobolev/form topology. Finite centering, finite logarithmic moments, finite real-phase jets and finite `p^(-sigma)` pole-cancellation relations disappear after closure. PL-354 gives the finite-support opposite extreme: finitely many exact positive-real Dirichlet evaluations remain topologically invisible, whereas an infinite distinct exact stack kills every finite Dirichlet polynomial.

PL-355--PL-356 show that the last statement is not stable under the natural prime `l2` completion. In the prime-supported Hilbert space, classical boundary zero-set theory produces nonzero pure-prime series vanishing on bounded real Blaschke stacks accumulating at `1/2`; the common nullspace is infinite-dimensional and survives every finite family of bounded homogeneous coefficient constraints.

PL-357--PL-358 bracket the most canonical nonlinear Euler-product repair inside native Dirichlet `H^2`. Complete multiplicativity is too rigid: it forces product Szegő kernels and hence zero-free functions in `Re s>1/2`. Ordinary multiplicativity is too local: one one-prime factor can program an arbitrary zero in the native half-plane while all degree-one prime coefficients remain nonzero.

PL-359 now tests the most canonical global coupling suggested by that bracket: impose the exact conductor-one Riemann functional equation together with the standard finite-order continuation assumptions. Hamburger's converse theorem forces the Dirichlet series to be a scalar multiple of `zeta`. Native `H^2` then forces that scalar to vanish, because the zeta coefficient sequence is not square-summable and the pole at `1` is incompatible with the native class. Thus the exact Riemann functional equation does not provide the missing intermediate family either; in the unshifted native `H^2` topology it **overshoots all the way to zero**.

The surviving problem is therefore narrower. A useful source law must constrain prime-power strings globally enough to defeat one-prime zero programming, but it cannot simply impose the full conductor-one zeta symmetry inside the same Hilbert topology. Any completion/functional-equation route must alter at least one load-bearing ingredient—weight/topology, conductor/Gamma data, shift, source category or target-relative relation—while proving that the resulting class is nontrivial and still retains a meaningful critical boundary.

## Treat completeness radius, coefficient topology, boundary interpolation and global symmetry as separate budgets

Beurling--Malliavin density measures how far a restricted modulation family tests the localized form. PL-353 measures whether algebraic coefficient restrictions survive the destination topology. PL-354--PL-356 show that source completion can turn an infinite exact evaluation stack from algebraically fatal into a large closed nullspace. PL-357--PL-358 show that a nonlinear multiplicative law can still be either too local or too rigid.

PL-359 adds a distinct fourth failure mode: an exact global functional equation can be perfectly arithmetic and still be incompatible with the chosen native Hilbert class except at zero. “Global coupling” is therefore not enough; one must test **nontriviality after the coupling and completion are imposed simultaneously**.

A proposed intermediate family should state its closure law, coefficient topology, evaluation/interpolation boundary, prime-power coupling, global completion/functional equation and destination theorem together. A conclusion proved in the finite-support, local form, prime `l2`, native `H^2`, or completed zeta category cannot be transferred to another by formal analogy.

## Keep centering, explicit-formula identity, family closure and source completion distinct

Raw endpoint recurrence is PNT-universal; centering introduces the classical zero expansion; same-state completion gives the Riesz criterion; complete modulation gives localized Weil positivity. The later results classify several attempted intermediate source laws: exact stacks depend on topology, ordinary multiplicativity is locally programmable, complete multiplicativity is zero-free, and the exact conductor-one Riemann functional equation annihilates native `H^2`.

The next Prime-Lattice mechanism must therefore exhibit a genuinely nontrivial completed family first, then prove that its global arithmetic coupling survives the chosen topology and supplies a destination discriminator. Neither the critical evaluation boundary, its Brownian kernel, multiplicativity, nor the bare Riemann functional equation should be treated as zero selection in isolation.
