## Find a source-forced intermediate family that survives the completion topology

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-016-weil-form-observability-is-stronger-than-moment-observability` through `MI-038-diagonal-hilbert-reweighting-cannot-carry-zeta-continuation`.

PL-345--PL-347 classify the two obvious endpoint extremes. The centered same-state channel is the classical first logarithmic Riesz criterion, while the complete Fourier-modulation Gram family is a form core for the localized completed Weil form and therefore reconstructs ordinary Weil positivity. PL-348--PL-352 quantify how finite-axis sparsity loses its Beurling--Malliavin barrier once natural multiplicative closure is admitted.

PL-353--PL-356 show that finite coefficient restrictions survive only when continuous in the destination topology, and that exact evaluation stacks change character under completion: algebraically fatal stacks can reopen large nullspaces in prime `l2`. PL-357--PL-358 bracket the most canonical nonlinear Euler-product repair in native Dirichlet `H^2`: complete multiplicativity is zero-free while ordinary multiplicativity permits one-prime zero programming.

PL-359 tests exact conductor-one Riemann symmetry. Hamburger rigidity forces the Dirichlet series to be a scalar multiple of zeta, but native `H^2` excludes the nonzero zeta vector, so the exact functional equation overshoots the desired intermediate family all the way to zero.

PL-360 closes the most direct topological repair. In any positive **diagonal** coefficient Hilbert space `||sum a_n n^-s||^2=sum |a_n|^2 w_n`, bounded evaluation at `Re s=sigma` requires `sum n^(-2sigma)/w_n<infinity`. If the zeta coefficient vector `(1,1,...)` is also finite norm, Cauchy--Schwarz would force `sum n^-sigma<infinity`. Hence no diagonal reweighting can both contain zeta and extend ordinary bounded point evaluation anywhere with `sigma<=1`.

The surviving problem is therefore narrower. A viable completion cannot be obtained by merely changing monomial lengths. It must use genuinely **non-diagonal coefficient correlations**, a target-relative/model/quotient geometry, a rigged or distributional pairing, or another controlled continuation mechanism that does not reduce to diagonal RKHS duality. Any candidate must remain nontrivial after its source law and completion are imposed simultaneously and must still retain a critical zero-sensitive boundary.

## Treat completeness radius, coefficient topology, evaluation geometry and global symmetry as separate budgets

Beurling--Malliavin density measures how far a restricted modulation family tests the localized form. PL-353 measures whether algebraic coefficient restrictions survive the destination topology. PL-354--PL-356 show that source completion can turn an infinite exact evaluation stack from algebraically fatal into a large closed nullspace. PL-357--PL-359 show that nonlinear multiplicative or global symmetry constraints can be too local, too rigid, or incompatible with the chosen Hilbert class.

PL-360 adds a sharper duality obstruction: even when the topology is deliberately changed to admit the target coefficient vector, the **dual evaluation geometry** may retreat to the absolute-convergence half-plane. “Change the topology” is therefore incomplete unless one specifies both which target vectors become finite and how the destination functional remains continuous.

A proposed intermediate family should state its closure law, coefficient Gram geometry, evaluation/continuation mechanism, prime-power coupling, global completion/functional equation and destination theorem together. A conclusion proved in finite support, local form, diagonal weighted `l2`, native `H^2` or completed zeta categories cannot be transferred to another by formal analogy.

## Keep centering, explicit-formula identity, family closure and source completion distinct

Raw endpoint recurrence is PNT-universal; centering introduces the classical zero expansion; same-state completion gives the Riesz criterion; complete modulation gives localized Weil positivity. The later results classify several attempted intermediate source laws and topologies. The next Prime-Lattice mechanism must therefore exhibit a genuinely nontrivial completed family first, then prove that its global arithmetic coupling and its destination functional both survive that topology. Neither the critical evaluation boundary, multiplicativity, the bare Riemann functional equation nor diagonal coefficient reweighting should be treated as zero selection in isolation.
