# Prime-lattice research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Prime-Lattice intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Globalize one-sided prime shifts only with structure that does more than recover the regular model

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-016-weil-form-observability-is-stronger-than-moment-observability` through `MI-044-finite-prime-wold-classification-does-not-globalize-atomically`.

PL-345--PL-373 close the obvious completion, multiplicativity, functional-equation, coefficient-topology and scalar nonlinear theorem interfaces. PL-374--PL-376 then classify every finite-dimensional completely multiplicative matrix source for linear observables as a finite Artinian thickening of scalar multiplicative channels. PL-377--PL-378 extend scalarization to bounded normal representations and uniformly bounded invertible group completions.

PL-379 identifies the genuine countable-coordinate obstruction: every finite-prime restriction of a doubly commuting isometric family may have the usual Wold decomposition while the full `N_0^infinity` representation carries a diffuse non-atomic type field with no global atomic pure/unitary decomposition.

PL-380 shows that the most obvious global repair is too strong to create new arithmetic structure. If the joint wandering space is generating, the family is unitarily the regular prime multishift; in multiplicity one this is simply `V_p e_n=e_(pn)`. Adding exact vacuum covariance `[A,V_p]=(log p)V_p` then forces the standard diagonal energy `c+log n`.

PL-381 removes the need to assume a generating vacuum when a single semibounded self-adjoint generator has exact strong `log p` covariance. The lower spectral edge makes each coordinate pure, and bounded-energy subspaces see only finitely many prime directions because the positive weights `log p` are locally finite. Finite Wold theory therefore globalizes and the whole system becomes

`A ~= N_log tensor I + I tensor B`

on the regular multishift, with all residual spectral freedom confined to a prime-independent internal sector `B`.

This is a real solution to the representation-theoretic globalization problem, but still not an RH mechanism. The same argument works for any countable positive locally finite weight family, so semibounded covariance is a coercive positive-energy principle rather than rational-prime-specific rigidity. The live question is now to identify **additional source-forced arithmetic or analytic structure on top of the regular one-sided model** that is not reproducible for an arbitrary frequency tuple and is not merely the classical `log n` Hamiltonian or its multiplicity sector.

## Keep source algebra, finite-prime classification, global type measure, positive-energy covariance and zero-sensitive analytic data separate

Finite nilpotent thickness, infinite normal spectral multiplicity, uniformly bounded nonnormal group representations and countable Wold type fields are different enlargements. PL-379 proves that finite classifications alone do not control the last one; PL-380--PL-381 prove that cyclicity or semibounded locally finite covariance can control it, but they do so by selecting the universal regular representation.

The useful boundary is therefore no longer merely “find a hypothesis that globalizes Wold.” That can now be done. A future Prime-Lattice proposal should state what extra relation remains **after** this globalization: a target-relative Mellin/Nyman coupling, completed Weil relation, nonmultiplicative observable, boundary condition, or another arithmetic interaction that distinguishes the rational-prime weights from a generic positive frequency sequence.

Formal Euler products and partition factors should still be kept inside their proved convergence/topological regime. Recovering the canonical shift and the `log n` energy explains the ordinary zeta factor in positive temperature, but by itself supplies neither analytic continuation nor a selector for the nontrivial zeros.
