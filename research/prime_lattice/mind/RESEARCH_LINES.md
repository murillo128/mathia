## Force the zero-bearing variable itself into a source-determined self-adjoint relation

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-011-addition-is-first-nonmultiplicative-escape-but-affine-traces-hit-analytic-boundaries`, `MI-014-prime-exchangeability-erases-normal-arithmetic-equilibrium`, `MI-015-reciprocity-family-coupling-is-not-principal-zero-selection`.

PL-240--PL-256 separate three obligations that attractive global spectral constructions often conflate: source-forced coupling, positivity/self-adjointness, and two-way divisor completeness. Representation rings may leave the principal zeta factor algebraically separate; self-adjoint pseudolaplacians may localize only a selected sub-divisor; generic Weyl/boundary data may simply program the desired divisor.

PL-257--PL-258 close two further shortcuts. A self-adjoint/unitary dilation does not localize resonances when the zeta zeros live in a compressed non-self-adjoint interaction generator. Conversely, the canonical Farey/Gauss transfer family can encode the **full Riemann divisor** as complex parameter values for which an eigenvalue-1/admissibility condition holds, while related real-temperature operators are genuinely self-adjoint. Neither fact makes the zero-bearing complex parameter the spectrum of one fixed self-adjoint operator.

The live construction must therefore put the centered Riemann zero parameter itself inside a source-determined positive/self-adjoint spectral relation, prove that positivity without assuming RH or prescribing an RH-equivalent Herglotz function, and retain divisor completeness with multiplicity. Ambient self-adjointness, a self-adjoint dilation, a canonical determinant family, or a parameter-dependent eigenvalue condition are controls unless they transfer spectral reality to the complete zero-bearing variable.

## Treat positivity, ambient spectral reality, parameter-family completeness, and divisor completeness as separate obligations

A positive completed distribution can hide a signed target, an ambient self-adjoint dynamics can coexist with complex resonances, and a canonical operator family can be divisor-complete while leaving the divisor in external parameter space. A successful Hilbert--Polya-type route must make source forcing, sign/reality, and full divisor capture meet in the same zero-bearing object rather than distribute them across related constructions.
