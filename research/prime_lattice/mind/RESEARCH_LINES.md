## Transfer the repaired moving endpoint through the exact signed block statistic

PL-407--PL-415 isolate the `p=5` local alias, repair it at source level, and reduce the actual-prime target to one fixed signed supertrace of the four mod-5 character variances. PL-416--PL-421 separate that scalar target from matrix positivity and spectral reality. Positive residue energies can discard the off-diagonal coherence required by the signed source functional; exact local-factor deletion is not positive on the ambient PSD cone; and on the actual insertion image it is positive exactly when the normalized residue correlation matrix has lower spectral floor `lambda_min>=1/(p-1)`, hence `1/4` at `p=5`.

PL-424 shows that the obvious collections of scalar data still do not decide that image-cone condition. The four residue variances, the four character variances, and the multiplicative residue twirl admit positive-semidefinite matched controls with identical reported marginals but opposite `lambda_min>=1/4` verdicts. The missing resource is genuinely unsymmetrized cross-channel coherence.

PL-425 gives encouraging but model-level structure: the mod-5 singular-series covariance is nearly character diagonal, with square-root-scale off-diagonal suppression. This is not yet the deterministic source-subtracted prime covariance required by the endpoint argument. PL-426 correctly shows that the published all-residue mixed-zero observables recover only a Fourier-diagonal compression of the full mixed-character matrix and are not tomographically complete.

PL-427 refines where that information-loss obstruction actually lies. At fixed modulus `5`, the Kandhil--Languasco--Moree proof can be rerun with arbitrary fixed character coefficients and polarized, yielding under its GRH hypothesis the full operator-norm asymptotic `G(x,T)=(T log x/pi) I_4+o(T log x)`. Thus the analytic machinery is capable of controlling the unsymmetrized mixed matrix; the four published residue observables simply do not expose it. But the hypothesis is target-circular here: GRH for the principal character modulo `5` already contains RH for `zeta`, and the zero-side matrix estimate is still not the deterministic source-subtracted prime covariance needed by PL-421.

The live positive route is therefore narrower than a generic request for tomography. It needs an **unconditional, or genuinely weaker-than-RH, matrix-valued transfer** that controls the unsymmetrized character covariance in operator norm at a scale preserving the `1/4` image-cone margin, together with the source/model replacement and residual-order analysis. Reproving the KLM full-matrix asymptotic under GRH adds no mechanism. In parallel, the original actual-prime route remains the PL-407-scale power saving for the combined signed supertrace without silently importing critical-line control for auxiliary Dirichlet `L`-functions.

Future arguments must distinguish the signed supertrace, raw covariance positivity, full cross-residue Gram data, the exact local-factor inverse, membership in its restricted positive image, source/model subtraction and the assumptions used to control the matrix.
