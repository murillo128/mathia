## Preserve oriented additive information before phase-insensitive closure or diffuse averaging erases it

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-007-pointing-breaks-gauge-but-exposes-additive-correlations` and `MI-011-addition-is-first-nonmultiplicative-escape-but-affine-traces-hit-analytic-boundaries`.

PL-177 now shows that nonmultiplicativity alone is still insufficient: a prime-axis affine closure can become selector-flat under a phase-insensitive summary. PL-178 identifies the noncollapsed scalar remnant as shifted-prime correlation, exposing the familiar parity/analytic boundary. PL-179 shows that keeping the **oriented nonselfadjoint phase** before closure really does preserve shifted-prime arithmetic data.

The live additive carrier is therefore not generic addition but a source-forced oriented affine/nonselfadjoint statistic whose phase survives the final analytic operation. Its decisive test is a theorem that controls the resulting shifted-prime data without averaging it back into a universal pseudorandom quantity.
