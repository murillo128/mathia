This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-lattice intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Require a source-forced relation among prime channels, not a larger Bost--Connes completion

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-prime-shift-symmetry-is-not-a-zero-hamiltonian-principle`, `MI-007-pointing-breaks-gauge-but-exposes-additive-correlations`, `MI-011-addition-is-first-nonmultiplicative-escape-but-affine-traces-hit-analytic-boundaries`, `MI-012-one-point-target-complexity-still-scalarizes-under-equidistribution`.

PL-193--PL-223 close scalar phase gauges, programmable finite-rank transport, finite-prime Bost--Connes semigroup correlations, and now the bounded all-prime norm completion as well. The prime-isometry algebra over all primes is the countable tensor product of Toeplitz factors, so simply passing from finitely many primes to the bounded norm closure does not create an arithmetic coupling.

PL-224--PL-227 close several canonical infinite-prime and weak-completion repairs. Cyclotomic corners collapse by lcm; the standard prime-shift von Neumann closure is all of `B(ell^2(N))`, so weak membership/affiliation is maximally programmable; scalar renormalization of canonical all-prime tails has no nonzero KMS-GNS limit; and normalized lcm conditioning either collapses to the profinite-zero gauge state or, after the canonical moving-corner rescaling, reconstructs the original KMS state.

PL-228 identifies the complementary coprimality boundary. All-prime coprimality conditioning converges to the profinite-unit Haar state at and below the Bost--Connes phase transition and to the standard symmetry-broken unit-boundary measure above it. Its intrinsic transition is `beta=1`, not the Riemann half-line. PL-229 then shows that an additional one-dimensional Dirichlet compression of the unit-boundary coefficients produces `C(s)/zeta(s+1)` and an RH-equivalent half-plane only by explicit shifted reciprocal-zeta repackaging.

The surviving route must therefore add an exact **source-selection relation** not supplied by norm completion, weak affiliation, canonical corner limits, or order-collapsed Dirichlet transforms. A useful relation should couple prime channels before the target coefficients are inserted, survive matched product-Toeplitz/programmability controls, and produce a destination sign, coercivity, or localization consequence that is not merely a reconstruction of the zeta function in new coordinates. The current source-selection clue is the correct place to test whether such a relation is mathematically independent from the later Weil-sign obligation.

## Treat completion, conditioning, and scalar interpolation as controls rather than arithmetic mechanisms

Finite-prime correlations, all-prime bounded norm closure, full weak closure in the standard representation, scalar tail renormalization, lcm conditioning, coprimality conditioning, and one-dimensional Dirichlet transforms now provide a broad control catalogue. Each can look more global than the preceding category while still yielding either generic Toeplitz data, maximal programmability, classical Bost--Connes boundary states, self-similarity, or explicit zeta repackaging.

A category exit is meaningful only when it introduces a source-forced relation that fails on these controls. Infinite prime support, unboundedness, affiliation, noncommutativity, a phase transition, or an RH-equivalent transform is not enough by itself.

## Require a parity-sensitive large-tail coupling before assigning arithmetic meaning to the affine carrier

PL-184--PL-188 show that bounded-variation targets, polylogarithmic residue labels, and shifted-prime exponent vectors below any subpower cutoff are controlled by classical one-point information. The unresolved Möbius/Liouville content lies in the large-prime tail or in a coupling that reaches it. Enlarging the local coordinate block without a parity-sensitive cross-tail theorem is not enough.

## Keep Nyman target sensitivity but reject arbitrary-inner model-space spectralization

The off-line Blaschke factor is genuine target/model-space information that generator Gram geometry cannot see, but PL-204 shows that compressing the prime semigroup to an arbitrary model space faithfully encodes whichever inner divisor was supplied. Zero sensitivity is therefore not zero selection. A useful Nyman/prime-lattice construction must contain a source-forced relation unavailable for arbitrary inner functions and use it to derive a sign, coercivity, or localization theorem.
