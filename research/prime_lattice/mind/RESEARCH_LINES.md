## Prove a uniform source-selected orientation law on the shrinking first-prime layer

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-016-weil-form-observability-is-stronger-than-moment-observability` through `MI-023-the-first-prime-overlap-algebra-is-not-the-missing-rh-mechanism`.

PL-313--PL-323 close the moving Green endpoint transfer at each fixed aperture. The exact source equation, conservative singular-kernel flux identity and positive jump-kernel maximum principle force the primitive critical coefficient and `Qm(Q)->0`; there is no remaining generic analytic task to manufacture a finite endpoint limit.

PL-324 tests whether the RH-relevant odd Weil sector can supply the missing sign through standard positivity-preserving semigroup theory. Parity removes the full-space archimedean Beurling--Deny obstruction completely, so positivity survives exactly until the first rational-prime event `a=(1/2)log 2`; immediately afterward the prime translation has the wrong modulus sign.

PL-325 classicalizes the next obvious step. In the first-prime window the truncated `log 2` translation is exactly a two-edge flip with spectrum `{-1,0,1}` and infinite-dimensional `±1` sectors. Its operator norm jumps from zero to one at the threshold, but near threshold the same coupling is small relative to the singular endpoint coercive form. This exact overlap algebra, its Legendre compression and related finite-scale first-prime machinery are already public prior art and independently reconstructible.

PL-326 closes the naive bridge from the fixed-aperture endpoint law to first-crossing orientation. If `a_delta=(log 2)/2+delta`, the arithmetic reflection lives on a layer of width `2delta`. Two comparison families can have exactly the same critical endpoint germ and satisfy the pointwise `Qm(Q)->0` residual law at every fixed `delta`, while an antisymmetric bump supported inside the shrinking layer reverses the reflection sign. The bump is absolutely small in `L^2` and in the logarithmic endpoint-potential weight, yet it dominates the still smaller `delta/log(1/delta)` reflected correlation of the critical profile.

PL-327 identifies the sharp generic regularity threshold. After subtracting the canonical critical profile, logarithmic Hölder control with exponent `alpha>1/2` and the stated vanishing normalized seminorm is sufficient to preserve first-prime orientation. At the canonical exponent `alpha=1/2`, an antisymmetric residual can still reverse the sign while preserving the leading boundary coefficient and vanishing endpoint-potential energy.

The missing quantifier is therefore source-specific and uniform. A sufficient moving-layer scale is of order

`||r_delta||_2 = o(|C_delta| sqrt(delta/log(1/delta)))`,

or an equivalent estimate controlling the reflection functional directly. Generic endpoint regularity at its canonical exponent, norm-one overlap algebra, endpoint form-smallness and fixed-aperture asymptotics do not supply this estimate.

The live theorem must couple the **actual signed state selected by the full Weil operator** to the shrinking arithmetic prime-power channel uniformly across threshold: an aperture-uniform first-crossing/eigenstate-orientation law, a source/endpoint-flux estimate suppressing the antisymmetric moving-layer sector, or another signed identity that controls the first-prime reflection directly and survives beyond the single `log 2` block.

## Keep fixed-window certificates and scalable tail geometry separate

**Linked intuition:** `MI-024-fixed-window-certification-requires-frequency-dependent-tail-control`.

PL-328 records a recent non-peer-reviewed, not independently reproduced computer-assisted source claiming full complex-test Weil coercivity at `a=17/16`, with lower bound `2^-49162` and exact active prime-power list `2,3,4,5,7,8`. Through the already-audited common-form dictionary this would imply `A_(17/16)>0` and hence positivity for every smaller aperture. The numerical frontier is therefore materially deeper than the earlier `a=0.8` certificate **conditional on successful independent validation of the source package**; it is not promoted to reproduced Mathia evidence and has no RH consequence by itself.

The same finding contains an exact structural warning that is independent of trusting the large certificate: beyond the `8` threshold, a specific localization that drops the frequency-dependent positive tail develops a phase-locked negative high-frequency defect at the `log 8` channel, and no fixed bounded compact correction can repair that comparison for all tests. This is not negativity of the full Weil form. It says that a scalable source-exact proof cannot replace the difficult high-frequency positive remainder by a scalar floor plus a fixed compact patch once enough arithmetic channels are active.

The live fixed-window lesson is therefore not to chase isolated certificates at successive prime-power events. A scalable mechanism must retain enough **frequency-dependent positive information** to balance phase-locked arithmetic losses, while explaining that balance uniformly as aperture and source support grow. The reported `17/16` certificate is useful evidence that full positivity can survive several activations; its architecture does not yet provide the aperture-uniform sign mechanism.
