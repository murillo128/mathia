## Retain the functional-equation cross channel in an individual-zero-sensitive finite-height observable

**Linked intuition:** `MI-044-finite-prime-wold-classification-does-not-globalize-atomically`.

PL-391 shows that finite Dirichlet-polynomial Jessen zero density can concentrate on the critical half-line for an `L^2` coefficient-energy reason while remaining too coarse to track the zeta divisor. PL-393 shows that the standard fixed-`N` two-sided Jessen mean also forgets that tail because the corrected and uncorrected approximants have the same large-translation hull.

PL-394 closes the most canonical coupled-cutoff repair at quadratic level. At the Riemann--Siegel length `N asymp sqrt(T)`, Montgomery--Vaughan gives `||G_{T,N}-I||=O(T^(-1/2))`, so the entire frozen one-sided positive-cone Gram still converges to Haar. The continuation-faithful approximate functional equation changes the representation instead: it adds reflected negative modes multiplied by the archimedean chirp `chi(1/2+it)=e^(-2i theta(t))`. Its cross channel has stationary phase near `nm asymp t/(2 pi)`, a moving sum-energy shell in prime-exponent coordinates.

The live object must therefore retain that self-dual cross channel, or another demonstrably representative-sensitive continuation mechanism, **and** be sensitive to individual zeros. Merely growing the same-sign positive-cone cutoff at the natural conductor does not escape Haar washout. A finite-height/local statistic is useful only if it distinguishes the continuation-faithful representative while preserving the reflected-mode/Gamma-phase coupling rather than reducing again to translation-hull or same-sign Gram data.

## Keep self-duality, zero sensitivity and RH localization separate

The functional-equation cross channel is classical. Its critical-line balance and moving sum-energy shell explain what structure survives after the one-sided Gram classicalizes, but they do not force zeros onto `Re(s)=1/2`. Any bridge must state which distinguished representative is retained, where the opposite modes and archimedean phase enter, and why the observable sees an individual zero rather than only an averaged density or stationary-phase shell.
