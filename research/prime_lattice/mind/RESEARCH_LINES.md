# Prime-lattice research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-lattice intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they discriminate among mathematically different mechanisms.

## Distinguish the exact rational-prime norm completion from Beurling controls

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-the-exponent-lattice-is-scaffolding` and `MI-002-critical-line-is-boundary-not-selector`.

The free exponent cone, square-free hypercube, Euler-product architecture, square-root-scale integer-counting regularity, and arbitrary finite prefixes of the ordinary prime frequencies do not determine RH-type zero geometry. The surviving arithmetic variable must therefore use the full infinite rational-prime norm map or a completion, such as the product-formula/adelic structure, that the contrasting Beurling systems do not preserve.

The decisive test is to define a canonical invariant from the exact infinite norm/completion, prove that it distinguishes the ordinary primes from the PL-015 control class for a structural reason, and then show that it constrains more than the functional-equation symmetry axis. Re-deriving `Re(s)=1/2`, Euler factors, or finite-prefix statistics is not sufficient.

## Resolve the discrete Nyman defect with target-sensitive data

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-nyman-obstruction-is-target-data`.

The multiplicative dilation semigroup and its Mellin log-prime phases are classical, and the residual closed-span problem is already Balazard's discrete invariant-subspace question. Generator Gram geometry cannot see the common off-line Blaschke factor because inner multiplication is isometric.

A useful advance must therefore retain the distinguished target or equivalent model-space/divisibility data. The decisive outcomes are an unconditional classification of the de-Blaschke discrete span, a quantitative target-relative estimate strong enough to settle the missing converse, or a counterexample showing that zero divisibility does not exhaust the sparse-semigroup defect. Any proposed frame/Riesz/Gram estimate should first be checked for invariance under the common inner factor.

## Replace prime-shift covariance by a genuinely discriminating action

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-prime-shift-symmetry-is-not-a-zero-hamiltonian-principle`.

The natural operator-theoretic interpolation from exact prime-shift symmetry to weak covariance is now squeezed from both sides. Exact commutation is scalar, exact reversible logarithmic covariance has continuous full-line spectrum, compact/trace-class additive errors force the wrong eigenvalue-counting growth in the audited cases, while covariance only modulo compact or `S_q`, `q>1`, resolvents is automatic for a Riemann-density compact-resolvent operator. Translation-invariant GCD/Poisson and Hasse-Laplacian models likewise produce zero-blind boundaries or gauge phases.

The live question is to characterize a **prime action that is neither a spectral translation nor an automatic quotient relation**: for example a target-relative action, a boundary/defect observable, a one-sided nonnormal representation, or a relative construction against a noncompact reference. A decisive mechanism must have a nontrivial invariant forced by the exact rational-prime structure and survive the Beurling control audit; recovering only the Bost--Connes partition function or a universal `1/2` membership threshold does not qualify.
