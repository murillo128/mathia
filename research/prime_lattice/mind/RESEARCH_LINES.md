## Retain continuation information in the moving-cutoff boundary layer without quotienting away factor labels

**Linked intuition:** `MI-044-finite-prime-wold-classification-does-not-globalize-atomically`.

PL-391--PL-393 show that zero-faithful continuation can disappear from both `B^2` and fixed-`N` Jessen quotient averages. PL-394 then shows that the frozen one-sided Gram at the Riemann--Siegel conductor still tends to Haar; the continuation-faithful approximate functional equation differs by retaining reflected modes and the Gamma-phase cross channel.

PL-395 closes the naive bulk stationary interpretation of that cross channel. With the actual moving cutoff `M(t)=floor sqrt(t/(2 pi))`, an off-diagonal pair `(n,m)` reaches its nominal saddle `t approx 2 pi nm` before it is admitted at `t=2 pi max(n,m)^2`. Active interior stationary points therefore collapse asymptotically to the diagonal boundary `n=m`, `t approx 2 pi n^2`. After scalar pair summation the finite identity for `A_M(t)^2` also quotients ordered factor labels through the product `k=nm`, leaving only truncated divisor multiplicity.

The live continuation-sensitive object must therefore use what survives this collapse rather than the broad sum-energy shell itself. Can the near-diagonal cutoff-activation layer carry a nontrivial arithmetic signal once its endpoint terms are treated uniformly? Alternatively, can a finite-height observable keep `(n,m)` as separate labels, use a nonquadratic interaction, or couple to a global zero-count/argument-principle quantity before the product quotient, while remaining sensitive to individual zeros and to the reflected/Gamma-phase continuation data?

## Keep continuation structure, boundary resonance and RH localization separate

The functional equation and Riemann--Siegel moving cutoff are classical. The diagonal boundary saddle identifies where the canonical quadratic representation retains stationary continuation geometry after support is enforced; it does not force zeta zeros onto `Re(s)=1/2`. Off-diagonal near-boundary terms also remain possible without an interior saddle. Any bridge must specify which representative-sensitive quantity survives the moving support, why it is not only a divisor/product statistic or cutoff artifact, and how it detects individual zeros rather than an averaged density, boundary resonance or critical-line sign pattern.
