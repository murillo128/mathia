## Keep source projection, analytic continuation, block statistics and spectral localization distinct

The model/source de-aliasing, the endpoint meromorphic continuation and the actual-prime variance estimate are different gates. PL-416 adds another: first-order virtual-character scalarization need not preserve a second-order signed statistic. PL-417 adds a further gate: preserving that statistic in an indefinite metric does not imply the spectral reality needed for RH.

A future proof must therefore keep the away-from-5 source observable, its conditioned endpoint law, the zero-sensitive continuation, the exact signed mod-5 block variance, and any proposed spectral localization mechanism distinct until a theorem connects them. Replacing the four blocks by one scalar quotient, or replacing Hilbert positivity by indefinite selfadjointness without an extra arithmetic sign theorem, is a representation change rather than the missing transfer estimate.
