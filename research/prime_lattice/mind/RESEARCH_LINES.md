# Prime-lattice research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-lattice intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Introduce rational-prime relations before equilibrium; exchangeability is too coarse

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-prime-shift-symmetry-is-not-a-zero-hamiltonian-principle`, `MI-007-pointing-breaks-gauge-but-exposes-additive-correlations`, `MI-011-addition-is-first-nonmultiplicative-escape-but-affine-traces-hit-analytic-boundaries`, `MI-012-one-point-target-complexity-still-scalarizes-under-equidistribution`, `MI-013-kms-offdiagonal-freedom-is-boundary-data-not-prime-coupling`, `MI-014-prime-exchangeability-erases-normal-arithmetic-equilibrium`.

PL-230--PL-231 show that ordinary affine KMS evaluation scalarizes the source into positive residue probabilities or freely chosen boundary moments. PL-232--PL-235 close a different attempted repair: making prime directions symmetric does not manufacture the missing coupling. Full prime-permutation symmetry has no nontrivial normal finite-temperature equilibrium on the integer representation even with arbitrary interactions; the ordinary quasi-local completion reduces symmetric states to de Finetti mixtures of identical product states; and retaining only a fixed finite arithmetic coloring still leaves infinite permutation blocks whose normal state mass vanishes.

The surviving route must therefore introduce a **source-forced relation fine enough to distinguish the rational primes before state evaluation**: the actual norm `log p`, prime ordering/gaps, an injective or scale-refining arithmetic label, or a nonlocal relation whose defining algebra already couples sites. A fixed finite label followed by within-class exchangeability is closed.

## Treat ordinary KMS moments, full exchangeability, and fixed finite colorings as controls

Completion, noncommutativity, phase transition, interactions, or nonnormality do not by themselves restore rational-prime geometry. In the tested categories they produce scalar boundary data, vacuum collapse, or generic product-mixture structure.

A source relation is useful only if it remains visible after the chosen equilibrium/quotient and can be shown to carry parity-sensitive large-tail information rather than merely reinsert the prime labels programmably.

## Keep Nyman target sensitivity separate from programmable model-space spectralization

The Nyman/inner-function controls still show that target sensitivity is not target selection. Any prime-lattice model-space or quotient construction must derive its inner/quotient data from the source before inspecting a desired zero or sign.
