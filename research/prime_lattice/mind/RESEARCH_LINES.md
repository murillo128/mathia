## Find a genuinely global mechanism outside the scalar Venturini witness class

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-016-weil-form-observability-is-stronger-than-moment-observability` through `MI-041-complete-multiplicativity-can-make-a-filter-zero-sensitive-without-making-the-witness-easier`.

PL-345--PL-368 close the obvious completion, multiplicativity, functional-equation, coefficient-topology and positive-summation escapes. Finite filters give periodic continuations, unrestricted filters are programmable, ambient sequence geometry manufactures artificial critical walls, and scalar Dirichlet Hardy geometry produces a generic `1/2` boundary without selecting zeta zeros.

PL-369 identifies the source-forced nonlinear theorem interface: a bounded completely multiplicative `a` whose Dirichlet series extends holomorphically to `Re(s)>sigma_0` and vanishes at `1` forces zeta to be zero-free there. PL-370 makes the boundary zero locally Liouville-rigid, while PL-371 shows that local prime-harmonic closeness alone gives no continuation gain.

PL-372 initially showed that genuine continuation forces scale-by-scale weighted Liouville closeness and that every **finite-alphabet** witness is Liouville times a holomorphic zero-free Euler correction. PL-373 removes the finite-alphabet loophole at the divisor level. For every genuine Venturini witness in an RH-relevant half-plane `Re(s)>sigma_0`, `sigma_0>=1/2`, there is a holomorphic zero-free `C_a` such that

`L(a,s) = [zeta(2s)/zeta(s)] C_a(s)`.

Moreover the first-order prime defect `P_a(s)=sum_p (1+a(p))p^(-s)` itself extends holomorphically to the same half-plane. Conversely, if that prime defect extends there, the resulting continuation differs from the Liouville quotient by a zero-free exponential factor and is holomorphic **iff zeta is zero-free there**. Conditional organization of an infinite alphabet can change only a zero-free gauge; it cannot cancel, move or hide a zeta zero.

The live question has therefore moved outside “find a clever infinite prime alphabet.” Within the scalar bounded completely multiplicative Venturini class, every genuine witness has the Liouville divisor. A materially different route must change the theorem interface: a non-scalar or non-completely-multiplicative source object, a global trace/positivity relation whose divisor is not predetermined by `zeta(2s)/zeta(s)`, or another construction in which the required continuation is obtained independently rather than encoded as an RH-equivalent witness assumption.

## Treat coefficient algebra, local pole neutrality, divisor rigidity and continuation as separate budgets

The divisor-convolution identity is coefficient algebra. Complete multiplicativity is a nonlinear prime-axis law. Pole neutrality is a local boundary condition. Holomorphic continuation is global analytic power. PL-373 adds a stronger diagnostic: once all of those are present in the Venturini setting, the entire zero/pole divisor is already fixed to Liouville up to a zero-free gauge, even for an infinite alphabet.

A proposed Prime-Lattice mechanism should therefore state which assumption takes it outside this gauge-equivalence class and why that new object still produces an independently useful RH-facing statement. Infinite alphabet, conditional first-order organization and coefficient flexibility are no longer sufficient distinctions by themselves.
