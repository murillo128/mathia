# Prime-lattice research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Prime-Lattice intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Find an intermediate modulation family between one Riesz channel and form-core completeness

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-016-weil-form-observability-is-stronger-than-moment-observability` through `MI-032-complete-endpoint-modulation-gram-is-only-localized-weil-in-coordinates`.

PL-345--PL-347 classify the two obvious extremes of the endpoint state family. The centered same-state channel is the classical first logarithmic Riesz criterion, while the complete Fourier-modulation Gram family is a form core for the localized completed Weil form and therefore reconstructs ordinary Weil positivity. One scalar channel is too compressed; the complete family is exactly the old problem in coordinates.

PL-348 strengthens the completeness side in the arithmetic coordinates themselves: prime-log modulations already form a form core. PL-349 then gives the complementary Beurling--Malliavin interpretation. A frequency family with insufficient logarithmic density has only a finite form-core radius; the obstruction is quantitative rather than merely cardinal.

PL-350--PL-352 identify how fragile that sparse obstruction is under multiplicative closure. Once the modulation family is closed under the natural multiplicative combinations, the Beurling--Malliavin deficit collapses. A family supported on finitely many prime axes has a finite completeness budget, but freeing even one additional prime coordinate can already destroy that finite-axis barrier and restore arbitrarily large reach.

The live problem is therefore narrower than “find a sparse basis.” A useful family must be arithmetically source-forced **and** closed enough to carry a nontrivial zero-sensitive relation, while avoiding the immediate completeness collapse caused by unrestricted multiplicative generation. The right question is whether there is a constrained semigroup, bounded-complexity word family, or other source-defined subset whose Beurling--Malliavin budget grows enough to force the needed destination sign before it becomes a full form core.

## Treat Beurling--Malliavin radius as a sufficiency budget, not a new RH criterion

The new density results do not themselves prove positivity. They measure how far a restricted modulation family can test the localized form before Fourier completeness becomes available. Finite prime-axis families have a genuine finite budget; multiplicative closure can erase it; and one free prime coordinate already shows that nominal sparsity in the generating set is not the same as sparse information after closure.

A proposed intermediate family should therefore state its closure law, effective log-frequency density, form-core radius and destination theorem together. If its closure is dense enough to reconstruct the whole localized Weil form, the route has returned to the classical criterion. If its Beurling--Malliavin radius stays bounded, it must explain why that bounded testing window is nevertheless arithmetically sufficient.

## Keep centering, explicit-formula identity and family closure distinct

Raw endpoint recurrence is PNT-universal; centering introduces the classical zero expansion; same-state completion gives the Riesz criterion; complete modulation gives localized Weil positivity; prime-log form-core results and Beurling--Malliavin density now quantify where restricted families cross between those extremes. The next Prime-Lattice result must live genuinely between them rather than improving the bookkeeping of either endpoint.
