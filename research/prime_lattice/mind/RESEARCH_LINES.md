## Put arithmetic coupling into the completed near-diagonal transition layer rather than reading it from the transition kernel

**Linked intuition:** `MI-044-finite-prime-wold-classification-does-not-globalize-atomically`.

PL-391--PL-393 show that zero-faithful continuation can disappear from both `B^2` and fixed-`N` Jessen quotient averages. PL-394 shows that the frozen one-sided Gram at the Riemann--Siegel conductor still tends to Haar, while the continuation-faithful approximate functional equation retains reflected modes and the Gamma-phase cross channel.

PL-395 proves that the broad off-diagonal stationary shell is absent from the **sharp** moving Riemann--Siegel main sum: an off-diagonal pair reaches its nominal saddle before the larger mode is admitted. PL-396 shows why that conclusion cannot be promoted to completed zeta. In an exact incomplete-gamma continuation, fixed additive gaps `q=r+d` have order-one transition weights at `t=2 pi qr`; the sharp activation offset, incomplete-gamma transition width and stationary-phase width are all `Theta(r)`. The surviving object is therefore a coalescing saddle--transition layer, not a hard-cutoff endpoint.

PL-397 computes that layer to leading order and removes a remaining ambiguity about its arithmetic content. After writing `t=2 pi r(r+d)+r u`, the two incomplete-gamma weights converge to complementary error-function profiles and the stationary phase to a universal quadratic chirp depending only on `d` and `u`. The leading fixed-gap kernel is therefore additive and factorization-blind: it is reproduced by any energy-labelled system with the same consecutive integer spectrum, without using the prime-exponent vector of `r` or `r+d`.

The live question must consequently add a genuinely arithmetic coupling to the universal transition geometry rather than hoping the transition itself retains factor labels. Is there a completed observable carrying, for example, Möbius/von-Mangoldt weights, gcd/lcm incidence, divisor structure, cross-level data or another source-forced coupling `A(r,r+d)` whose interaction with the universal kernel survives exact continuation and remains individually zero-sensitive? Such a coupling must come with its own signed or positive control; merely decorating the coordinates after the scalar pair sum does not recover lost factor information.

## Keep sharp-cutoff geometry, smooth continuation and arithmetic discrimination separate

PL-395 remains exact for the canonical sharp main sum, PL-396 restores the smooth transition layer, and PL-397 shows that its leading fixed-gap kernel is universal rather than prime-lattice specific. None of these facts is itself arithmetic rigidity. Future constructions must state which completed quantity contains the extra arithmetic coupling, why it survives changes among exact continuation charts, and how it avoids collapsing back to scalar log-energy while detecting individual zeros rather than only transition or density effects.
