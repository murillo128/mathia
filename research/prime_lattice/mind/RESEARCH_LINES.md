This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Prime-Lattice evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Transfer the repaired moving endpoint through the exact signed block statistic

PL-407--PL-415 isolate the `p=5` local alias, repair it at source level, and reduce the actual-prime target to one fixed signed supertrace of the four mod-5 character variances. PL-416--PL-421 separate that scalar target from matrix positivity and spectral reality. Positive residue energies can discard the off-diagonal coherence required by the signed source functional; exact local-factor deletion is not positive on the ambient PSD cone; and on the actual insertion image it is positive exactly when the normalized residue correlation matrix has lower spectral floor `lambda_min>=1/(p-1)`, hence `1/4` at `p=5`.

PL-424 shows that the obvious collections of scalar data still do not decide that image-cone condition. The four residue variances, the four character variances, and the multiplicative residue twirl admit positive-semidefinite matched controls with identical reported marginals but opposite `lambda_min>=1/4` verdicts. The missing resource is genuinely unsymmetrized cross-channel coherence.

PL-425 gives encouraging but model-level structure: the mod-5 singular-series covariance is nearly character diagonal, with square-root-scale off-diagonal suppression. This is not yet the deterministic source-subtracted prime covariance required by the endpoint argument. What is needed is a matrix-valued transfer estimate whose operator error is small compared with the `1/4` image-cone margin, together with a separate treatment of positivity/order after source-model subtraction.

PL-426 shows why the most relevant mixed Dirichlet-zero pair-correlation literature does not already provide that theorem. The Yildirim/Kandhil--Languasco--Moree observable contains the mixed matrix `G_(chi,psi)` internally but then compresses it to the diagonal of a Fourier-conjugated matrix. Varying the residue recovers only cyclic diagonal sums, not the individual mixed entries. Positivity, conjugation symmetry and the individual self-correlations do not make this compression injective.

The live positive route is therefore a **tomographically complete matrix theorem**: control the individual mixed-character correlations, or another family of quadratic forms sufficient to bound the unsymmetrized covariance in operator norm at the scale required by the `1/4` cone margin. A scalar all-residue asymptotic, even one derived from a positive Gram representation, is not enough. In parallel, the original actual-prime route remains the PL-407-scale power saving for the combined signed supertrace without silently assuming critical-line control for auxiliary Dirichlet `L`-functions.
