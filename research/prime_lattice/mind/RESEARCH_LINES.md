# Prime-lattice research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-lattice intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Require a mixed-prime relation that survives principal-channel extraction

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-prime-shift-symmetry-is-not-a-zero-hamiltonian-principle`, `MI-007-pointing-breaks-gauge-but-exposes-additive-correlations`, `MI-011-addition-is-first-nonmultiplicative-escape-but-affine-traces-hit-analytic-boundaries`, `MI-012-one-point-target-complexity-still-scalarizes-under-equidistribution`, `MI-013-kms-offdiagonal-freedom-is-boundary-data-not-prime-coupling`, `MI-014-prime-exchangeability-erases-normal-arithmetic-equilibrium`, `MI-015-reciprocity-family-coupling-is-not-principal-zero-selection`.

PL-230--PL-239 close ordinary affine KMS scalarization, exchangeability/spreadability, fixed finite colors, scalar log-energy curvature, and bare consecutive-gap spectralization. PL-240 then supplies a genuine source-forced mixed-prime relation: quadratic reciprocity on a squarefree support produces the classical Rédei matrix and its class-group 4-rank geometry. This is a real category exit from generic symmetry, but its natural analytic completion opens a family of quadratic Dirichlet `L`-functions.

PL-241 identifies the established global completion: quadratic multiple Dirichlet series couple the reciprocity family to explicit zeta correction factors and Weyl-group functional equations. The visible zeta argument is not invariant under that Weyl action and its central geometry is not the Riemann critical symmetry. PL-242 then tests the cleanest principal residue. The `s=1` residue genuinely recovers `zeta_2(2w)`, but only because it projects to the trivial squarefree kernel; the nontrivial quadratic-family reciprocity has disappeared.

The live relation must therefore do more than be source-forced or return a zeta factor. It must retain **nontrivial mixed-prime arithmetic after the operation that isolates the principal Riemann divisor**, and that retained relation must yield an independent localization mechanism such as positivity, self-adjointness, or a trace/determinant constraint rather than merely a rescaled copy of RH.

## Treat generic symmetry, local gaps, quadratic family generation, and principal residue collapse as controls

Completion, noncommutativity, phase transition, abstract order, scalar log twists, and a real local-gap divisor do not restore global arithmetic relation. Quadratic reciprocity does restore such relation, but the first canonical analytic completions are classical family-valued objects; the first canonical principal residue recovers zeta only after discarding the nontrivial family.

A useful construction must identify which source relation remains visible in the **same lower-rank/principal object whose zero divisor is being localized**. Recovering `zeta(affine variable)` after quotienting away the relation is target reduction, not a new mechanism.

## Keep Nyman target sensitivity separate from programmable model-space spectralization

Target sensitivity is not target selection. Any prime-lattice model-space, quotient, residue, or twisted completion must derive its inner/quotient/coupling data from the source before inspecting a desired zero or sign, and must show that the source relation survives the final projection.
