# Prime-lattice research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-lattice intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Prove source-side positivity of a finite Weil core whose detectability is already complete

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-011-addition-is-first-nonmultiplicative-escape-but-affine-traces-hit-analytic-boundaries`, `MI-015-reciprocity-family-coupling-is-not-principal-zero-selection`, `MI-016-weil-form-observability-is-stronger-than-moment-observability`.

PL-240--PL-258 show that source forcing, self-adjointness/positivity, and divisor completeness can live in different objects; ambient self-adjointness or a divisor-complete parameter family does not by itself put the Riemann zero parameter into one fixed real spectrum.

PL-259 supplies an important positive alternative. A finite compression of Weil's Hermitian form, fed on the arithmetic side only by prime-power axis rays, combines zero-side inertia with arithmetic first/second moments to force at least `(2/3-o(1))` of zeros in a window to be simple and on the critical line, with the optimized constant about `0.6725`. Lamzouri's parallel argument shows that the robust mechanism is the completed explicit formula plus conjugation symmetry and pair-correlation second moments, not the particular finite matrix packaging.

PL-260 shows that bulk-moment blindness to sparse defects is not finite-form blindness: Bombieri's truncations eventually detect every finite-total off-line defect through negative index. PL-261 closes the broader existential observability gap for a different canonical family. Connes--Consani's semilocal Weil form has Laurent polynomials as a core, and the lower bound is the limit of smallest eigenvalues on nested finite Laurent spaces. Hence **any** RH failure, including an infinite zero-density off-line divisor, forces a negative eigenvalue in some finite semilocal compression and eventually in all larger compressions at one suitable scale.

The live theorem is therefore no longer finite detectability. For this semilocal core, detectability is already complete. The missing mechanism is a source-forced reason that every finite form is nonnegative, or a cheaper structural criterion that implies those signs without assuming the full Weil positivity statement. Mixed-prime exponent geometry is relevant only if it contributes such a sign law; it is not needed merely to make finite truncations complete.

## Treat moment observability, finite-form detectability, and sign coercivity as distinct obligations

Aggregate moments can force a positive proportion yet miss sparse defects. Full finite-form families can detect finite defects, and the Connes--Consani Laurent core detects every RH failure existentially. None of those completeness facts proves positivity. Future proposals should target the sign of the source-forced finite forms rather than construct yet another basis whose approximation completeness is already known.
