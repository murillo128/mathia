**Linked intuitions:** `MI-016-weil-form-observability-is-stronger-than-moment-observability` through `MI-023-the-first-prime-overlap-algebra-is-not-the-missing-rh-mechanism`.

This exact overlap algebra, its Legendre compression and related finite-scale first-prime machinery are already public prior art and independently reconstructible.

The live theorem is therefore not diagonalization of the single-prime shift, another operator-norm perturbation argument, or an isolated finite-scale positivity certificate. It must couple the **actual signed state selected by the full Weil operator** to the arithmetic prime-power channels: an aperture-uniform sign law, a signed first-crossing/eigenstate-orientation theorem, or the source/endpoint-flux bridge from PL-319--PL-324 in a form that survives beyond the first-prime block.

## Keep analytic transport, cone positivity, overlap algebra and arithmetic orientation separate

PL-322--PL-323 are real-variable/nonlocal transport results. PL-324 is an exact order-positivity classification on the odd sector. PL-325 is an exact single-shift algebra/prior-art boundary. None supplies the final arithmetic orientation.

The topology distinction in PL-325 is important: small overlap width is not small bounded-operator norm, while endpoint-weighted form control can still be small near threshold. A genuine first-crossing theorem must therefore use the selected state or a source-specific signed identity rather than infer spectral behavior from raw overlap size.

Future work should preserve the closed Green transfer and the now-classified one-prime algebra as interfaces, then work directly with the signed rational-prime contribution across apertures. Reproving generic endpoint regularity, the `±1` flip sectors, Legendre compression, or standard positive-semigroup continuation is no longer a live direction.
