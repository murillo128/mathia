PL-407--PL-411 move the model obstruction from fixed fractional Ramanujan ranges to the true endpoint and reorganize that endpoint exactly as an expectation over an odd square-free random integer whose prime coordinates are independent with `P(p|M)=1/(p-1)^2`. The law is heavy-tailed and its Mellin transform contains the zero-sensitive divisor `1/zeta(2s+4)`.

PL-412 audits cancellation in that Mellin factorization. Among the local Euler factors, `p=5` is the unique one whose zero locus can meet the RH-relevant half-strip; after deleting that coordinate, the only remaining cancellation mechanism is the numerator `zeta(rho/2)`. A descent under the functional equation rules out persistent numerator cancellation and yields the repaired model criterion

`RH iff N_5(s)-2/(5(s+1)) is holomorphic in Re(s)>-7/4`.

PL-413 closes the source/model arbitrariness of that repair: restricting the singular-series Ramanujan source to denominators coprime to `5` transports exactly to conditioning the endpoint Bernoulli law on `5` not dividing `M`.

PL-414 then pushes the repaired source to actual primes. The inverse local multiplier `g_5(h)` is a fixed additive Fourier projector, so the source-replacement error can be written as five mod-5 quadratic variance sectors. Separate PL-407-scale estimates for all five sectors are sufficient to retain the same power-saving threshold, but PL-414 does not show they are necessary.

PL-415 identifies the intrinsic target by changing to the multiplicative character basis. Up to explicit power-of-5 terms, the repaired source-subtracted variance is the single signed combination

`Delta T_(X,chi_0)(L) - (1/15)Delta T_(X,chi_2)(L) - (2/15)Delta T_(X,chi_4)(L)`,

where `chi_0` is the principal character, `chi_2` the quadratic character and `chi_4` one member of the quartic conjugate pair. The five additive-sector bounds of PL-414 are therefore an overcomplete sufficient condition: cancellation between the three distinct real Dirichlet-character variance channels is allowed by the exact source bridge.

The required exponent curve is unchanged. If that fixed signed supertrace satisfies

`<< X L (L/X)^mu (log X)^B`

uniformly on the effective kernel support, then for `H=X^theta` the PL-407 derivation still requires only

`mu > 3 theta / (4(1-theta))`.

The important new guardrail is spectral. The nonprincipal sectors are genuine primitive Dirichlet-`L` channels. Proving them independently through standard zero-pair technology can import GRH or comparable information for auxiliary `L`-functions, which is stronger than RH for zeta and cannot be silently used to close the bridge. The live actual-prime question is therefore to prove or refute the required power saving for the **combined signed character supertrace**, or to exhibit a structural explicit-formula cancellation specific to its coefficients. Treating the three sectors independently is now a control, not the intrinsic target.
