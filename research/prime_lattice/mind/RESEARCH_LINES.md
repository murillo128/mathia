## Treat spectral-parameter-independent exact resolvent transport as affine scalar geometry

**Linked intuition:** `MI-004-prime-shift-symmetry-is-not-a-zero-hamiltonian-principle`.

PL-193--PL-202 close scalar phase gauges, scalar nonunitary weights, state-dependent unitary flat connections, and invertible operator-valued fibers. PL-203 shows that even injective dense-range noninvertible edge weights can carry an arbitrary reducing spectral field, while PL-205 shows that exact bounded fiberwise covariance of genuine self-adjoint resolvents forces every edge range to be dense.

PL-206 proves that a single bounded left edge transporting two distinct spectral points at the same parameter must be the identity and the neighboring operators equal. PL-207 closes the obvious parameter-motion escape while keeping that edge fixed: if `R_1(z)=A R_0(phi(z))` with one bounded `z`-independent `A`, Hilbert's resolvent identity forces `A=aI`, and on a genuine parameter domain `phi(z)=az+b`. A homogeneous commuting prime-semigroup action therefore reduces to scalar translations or dilations; the canonical `log p` move is just the translation case.

The surviving exact-resolvent route must put arithmetic content outside this fixed-edge affine quotient: a genuinely spectral-parameter-dependent transfer operator, source-forced non-fiberwise coupling, domain/kernel structure outside bounded fixed-left transport, controlled approximate/ideal-valued covariance, left-right/conjugation architecture, or another noncommuting global completion. Each escape still needs a programmability and coercivity audit.

## Keep Nyman target sensitivity but reject arbitrary-inner model-space spectralization

**Linked intuition:** `MI-003-nyman-obstruction-is-target-data`.

The off-line Blaschke factor is genuine target/model-space information that generator Gram geometry cannot see. PL-204 tests the most canonical target-relative spectral repair: compress the exact prime semigroup to `K_B=H^2\ominus BH`. Zeros of `B` do become joint adjoint prime eigencharacters, but the construction works for an arbitrary Blaschke divisor, including a single freely chosen off-critical point.

Thus zero sensitivity is not zero selection. A useful Nyman/prime-lattice construction must contain a source-forced relation unavailable for arbitrary inner functions and use it to derive a sign, coercivity, or localization theorem. Merely passing to the model space of the already-supplied divisor is a faithful encoding, not an RH mechanism.

## Force completed arithmetic sign or one-sided coercivity upstream

A decisive theorem should derive an RH-level sign or checkpoint from exact rational-prime/global-completion structure, not from a representation whose critical boundary, phase law, flat covariance, singular decoration, fixed resolvent transport, affine spectral reparameterization, or inner divisor can be programmed independently of the Riemann source.
