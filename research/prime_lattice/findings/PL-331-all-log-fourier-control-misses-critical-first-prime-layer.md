# PL-331 — All fixed logarithmic Fourier seminorms miss the critical first-prime boundary layer

**Evidence:** `EXACT-DERIVED + DECISIVE-REGULARITY-NEGATIVE + ROUTE-NARROWING`

**Status:** `ALL-LOG-FRECHET-TOPOLOGY-INSUFFICIENT + SOURCE-AMPLITUDE-GATE-REMAINS`

## Precise claim

`PL-291` proves that every localized-Weil eigenstate belongs to every fixed logarithmic Fourier space

\[
X_m(I)=\left\{u:\int_{\mathbb R}\ell(\xi)^{2m}|\widehat{Eu}(\xi)|^2\,d\xi<\infty\right\},
\qquad \ell(\xi)=1+\log(2+|\xi|),
\]

and gives locally uniform bounds for each fixed `m` on source-static aperture intervals. `PL-327` and `PL-329` isolate a critical first-prime boundary layer that can reverse the newly active `p=2` reflection unless the actual source obeys the sharper amplitude gate.

The all-log hierarchy does **not** exclude that layer, even if one strengthens boundedness to convergence in every fixed logarithmic seminorm.

Put

\[
a_\delta=\frac{\log2}{2}+\delta,
\qquad
L_\delta=\log\frac{a_\delta}{\delta},
\]

choose a nonzero real `eta in C_c^infty((0,2))` with

\[
\eta(2-s)=-\eta(s),
\]

and define the physical endpoint layer

\[
r_\delta(t)=A L_\delta^{-1/2}\eta(t/\delta),
\qquad 0<t<2\delta,
\tag{PL331-1}
\]

extended by zero. Then, for every fixed integer `m>=0`,

\[
\boxed{
\|r_\delta\|_{X_m}^2
=O_{m,\eta,A}\!\left(\delta L_\delta^{2m-1}\right)
\longrightarrow0.
}
\tag{PL331-2}
\]

Hence `r_delta -> 0` in the projective Frechet topology generated by all fixed `X_m` seminorms.

Nevertheless the first-prime reflection sees this layer at leading order. For the canonical critical profile

\[
B_\delta(t)=C\left(\log\frac{2a_\delta}{t}\right)^{-1/2},
\qquad C\ne0,
\]

one has

\[
H_\delta(B_\delta)
=(2+o(1))C^2\frac{\delta}{L_\delta},
\]

whereas the exact antisymmetry gives

\[
H_\delta(r_\delta)
=-A^2\|\eta\|_2^2\frac{\delta}{L_\delta}.
\tag{PL331-3}
\]

The mixed term is `O(delta/L_delta^2)`, so choosing `A` with

\[
A^2\|\eta\|_2^2>2C^2
\]

forces

\[
\boxed{H_\delta(B_\delta+r_\delta)<0}
\]

for all sufficiently small `delta`, despite (PL331-2).

At the same time the exact endpoint source operator magnifies this Frechet-small layer to critical source size:

\[
\boxed{
\|\Delta F_\delta\|_\infty\asymp |A|\sqrt{L_\delta},
}
\tag{PL331-4}
\]

as already exposed at the pointwise level in `PL-329`. Thus the moving first-prime sign and the `PL-329` source-amplitude gate are discontinuous with respect to the entire qualitative all-log Fourier topology supplied by `PL-291`.

The consequence is sharp for the live route: **membership in every `X_m`, or even uniform control/convergence in every fixed `X_m` separately, cannot close the first-prime sign gate.** A successful branch argument must use quantitative control of the growth of the `X_m` constants that permits `m` to increase with `L_delta`, a genuinely positive-order regularity estimate, or the source equation/source amplitude directly.

## 1. Exact scaling in every logarithmic Fourier seminorm

Up to an irrelevant endpoint phase and Fourier-normalization constant, zero extension of (PL331-1) has transform

\[
\widehat{r_\delta}(\xi)
=A L_\delta^{-1/2}\delta\,\widehat\eta(\delta\xi).
\tag{PL331-5}
\]

Therefore

\[
\|r_\delta\|_{X_m}^2
=|A|^2\frac{\delta}{L_\delta}
\int_{\mathbb R}
\ell(y/\delta)^{2m}|\widehat\eta(y)|^2\,dy.
\tag{PL331-6}
\]

Since `a_delta` tends to the nonzero constant `(log 2)/2`,

\[
\log(1/\delta)=L_\delta+O(1).
\tag{PL331-7}
\]

For fixed `m`, Schwartz decay of `widehat eta` and

\[
\ell(y/\delta)
\le C\bigl(L_\delta+\log(2+|y|)\bigr)
\tag{PL331-8}
\]

give

\[
\int\ell(y/\delta)^{2m}|\widehat\eta(y)|^2dy
=O_{m,\eta}(L_\delta^{2m}).
\tag{PL331-9}
\]

Substitution in (PL331-6) proves (PL331-2). In fact the usual dominated-convergence argument away from the measure-zero point `y=0` gives the sharper asymptotic

\[
\|r_\delta\|_{X_m}^2
\sim c_{\mathcal F}|A|^2\|\eta\|_2^2
\delta L_\delta^{2m-1},
\tag{PL331-10}
\]

with `c_F` determined only by the Fourier normalization. The decisive feature is the exponential relation `delta ~ a_0 e^{-L_delta}`: no fixed power of `L_delta` can see a layer whose physical width is `delta`.

## 2. The same layer reverses the reflection at leading order

By scaled antisymmetry,

\[
r_\delta(2\delta-t)=-r_\delta(t),
\]

so (PL331-3) is exact. On the support of `eta(t/delta)`, write `t=delta s` with `s` in a compact subset of `(0,2)`. Then

\[
B_\delta(\delta s)
=C\left(L_\delta+\log\frac2s\right)^{-1/2}.
\]

The mixed reflection term is

\[
\int_0^{2\delta}
r_\delta(t)\bigl(B_\delta(2\delta-t)-B_\delta(t)\bigr)\,dt.
\tag{PL331-11}
\]

On that compact scaled support, the bracket is `O(|C|L_delta^{-3/2})`; multiplying by the layer amplitude `O(|A|L_delta^{-1/2})` and width `delta` gives

\[
O_{A,C,\eta}(\delta L_\delta^{-2}).
\tag{PL331-12}
\]

Combining this with the `PL-327` base asymptotic yields

\[
H_\delta(B_\delta+r_\delta)
=\left(2C^2-A^2\|\eta\|_2^2+o(1)\right)
\frac{\delta}{L_\delta}.
\tag{PL331-13}
\]

Thus the sign-reversing layer is invisible not only to the graph norm considered in `PL-330`, but to every fixed logarithmic Fourier norm used in the all-log bootstrap of `PL-291`.

## 3. Why the source still sees it

In logarithmic endpoint coordinate

\[
Q=\log\frac{2a_\delta}{t},
\]

the support of the layer lies at

\[
Q=L_\delta+O(1),
\]

and (PL331-1) becomes

\[
r_\delta(Q)=A L_\delta^{-1/2}\psi(Q-L_\delta)
\]

for a fixed smooth compactly supported `psi`. The exact endpoint identity of `PL-319`/`PL-329` contains the coercive term `2Qr_delta(Q)`. On this moving layer it has size `Theta(|A|sqrt(L_delta))`; the primitive, singular-translation remainder and bounded zeroth-order term are all `O(|A|L_delta^{-1/2})`. This proves (PL331-4).

Therefore the source equation measures a direction that is much stronger than the Frechet topology generated by fixed logarithmic moments. This is exactly why the source-amplitude criterion in `PL-329` is not a repackaging of `PL-291` regularity.

## 4. Adversarial checks

**The perturbation is not claimed to be an eigenstate.** This result is a matched-control obstruction against deriving the first-prime sign from qualitative all-log regularity alone. The actual completed-Weil eigen-equation may still exclude the layer; `PL-329` identifies one sufficient way it can do so through source amplitude.

**Uniform fixed-`m` bounds do not rescue the argument.** Because `||r_delta||_{X_m}->0` for each fixed `m`, adding this layer to any family with uniformly bounded fixed-`m` seminorms preserves those bounds. What could matter is a quantitative envelope in `m` strong enough to optimize `m=m(delta)`; `PL-291` explicitly does not control that growth.

**No coefficient collapse is used.** The layer is supported away from the endpoint at each fixed `delta`, so it leaves the critical coefficient `C` unchanged. The obstruction therefore persists even with a fixed nonzero leading coefficient.

**This is stronger than the graph-topology control in `PL-330`, not a contradiction of it.** The graph norm corresponds only to the first logarithmic level. Equation (PL331-2) shows simultaneous convergence at every fixed level.

**No RH conclusion follows.** The statement concerns only the first newly active prime channel in the localized-Weil program. It rules out a regularity shortcut; it does not prove or disprove positivity of the actual branch.

## 5. Prior-art and novelty audit

Function spaces with purely logarithmic smoothness and their sharp embeddings are classical subjects, so no novelty is claimed for the general fact that fixed logarithmic weights are much weaker than positive-order Sobolev control. A targeted search of logarithmic Sobolev/Besov embedding and concentration literature found standard embedding and growth-envelope theory, but no theorem needed for the calculation above: the decisive estimate (PL331-6)--(PL331-10) is a direct Fourier rescaling of the already constructed `PL-327` boundary layer.

The durable mathematical delta is line-specific. `PL-291` had left open whether its all-log eigenstate bootstrap could become useful near source activation if enough fixed logarithmic levels were combined. `PL-331` closes that qualitative route: the exact critical sign-reverser tends to zero in **all** those fixed seminorms while retaining a leading-order negative first-prime reflection and a critical `sqrt(L_delta)` source cost. Any use of the all-log hierarchy must therefore become quantitative in the growth of its constants, rather than merely adding more fixed moments.

## Consequence for the research line

The regularity side of the first-prime crossing is now exhausted at the level currently proved for actual eigenstates. The implication

\[
\text{all fixed logarithmic Fourier moments}
\Longrightarrow
\text{first-prime orientation}
\]

is false even in the strongest natural projective topology generated by those moments. The live branch-specific target remains the `PL-329` source gate: prove

\[
1+\|F_\delta\|_\infty
=o\!\left(|C_\delta|\sqrt{L_\delta}\right)
