# PL-011 — Bare prime Kronecker flow has pure-point rational-log spectrum and is non-mixing

## Claim

Let

\[
X=\mathbb T^{\mathcal P}
\]

be the infinite prime torus with Haar probability measure, and let the vertical prime flow act by

\[
\Phi_t(z)_p=e^{-it\log p}z_p.
\]

The standard Koopman representation of this flow on \(L^2(X)\) is completely diagonal in the character basis. Its eigenfrequencies are exactly

\[
\left\{\sum_p k_p\log p : k\in\mathbb Z^{(\mathcal P)}\right\}
=
\{\log q:q\in\mathbb Q_{>0}\}.
\]

Consequently the Haar Koopman dynamics has pure-point spectral type, is ergodic but not weakly mixing (hence not mixing), and has no correlation-decay mechanism of its own. Therefore a route in which the nontrivial Riemann zeros arise directly as a chaotic/mixing/resonance spectrum of the **bare prime Kronecker flow itself** is ruled out. Any such mechanism must add genuinely new structure: a distinguished analytically continued observable, a nonunitary/scattering construction, a different state space, singular boundary data, or another operator not equivalent to the Haar Koopman rotation.

**Evidence/status:** `LITERATURE+DERIVED + DECISIVE-NEGATIVE`.

The discrete-spectrum theorem for compact abelian rotations is classical. The Mathia-specific part is the exact specialization of the character lattice to prime-exponent differences and hence to the rational-log frequency module, together with the resulting obstruction for a bare-flow RH resonance mechanism.

## Exact character calculation

The Pontryagin dual of the infinite prime torus is

\[
\widehat X=\mathbb Z^{(\mathcal P)},
\]

the finite-support integer lattice. For

\[
k=(k_p)_p\in\mathbb Z^{(\mathcal P)},
\]

define the character

\[
\chi_k(z)=\prod_p z_p^{k_p}.
\]

The characters form an orthonormal basis of \(L^2(X)\). For the Koopman group

\[
(U_t f)(z)=f(\Phi_t z),
\]

we have

\[
U_t\chi_k(z)
=
\prod_p(e^{-it\log p}z_p)^{k_p}
=
e^{-it\sum_p k_p\log p}\chi_k(z).
\]

Thus every character is an eigenfunction with frequency

\[
\lambda_k=\sum_p k_p\log p.
\]

Unique factorization identifies the integer difference lattice with positive rationals:

\[
q(k)=\prod_p p^{k_p}\in\mathbb Q_{>0},
\qquad
\lambda_k=\log q(k).
\]

Hence the **point spectrum** of the self-adjoint Stone generator is

\[
\sigma_{\mathrm{point}}(L)=\log\mathbb Q_{>0}.
\]

Since \(\mathbb Q_{>0}\) is dense in \(\mathbb R_{>0}\), \(\log\mathbb Q_{>0}\) is dense in \(\mathbb R\). Therefore the operator spectrum of the unbounded generator is the closure, namely \(\mathbb R\), even though its spectral type is pure point. This distinction is important: the obstruction is not that the spectral set is sparse, but that the Haar dynamics decomposes entirely into exact almost-periodic eigenmodes.

## Ergodicity and lack of mixing

The flow-invariant characters are precisely those with

\[
\lambda_k=0
\iff
\prod_p p^{k_p}=1.
\]

By unique factorization this forces \(k=0\). Thus the only invariant \(L^2\) characters are constants, so the Haar flow is ergodic.

The same freeness of the frequency module is the special case relevant to the modern infinite-dimensional Kronecker-flow theory: the module generated by \(\{\log p\}\) is a free abelian group, and the prime flow has the corresponding minimal/uniquely-ergodic non-resonant behavior in the product topology.

But ergodicity here is not mixing. For every nonzero \(k\),

\[
\langle U_t\chi_k,\chi_k\rangle
=
e^{-it\lambda_k},
\]

whose modulus is identically one. It cannot converge to zero as \(|t|\to\infty\). The existence of these nonconstant eigenfunctions already rules out weak mixing, and therefore rules out strong mixing as well.

More generally, correlations of finite Fourier combinations are finite sums of pure phases and hence almost periodic rather than decaying. The complete character basis makes this a structural property of the whole Haar \(L^2\) representation, not an accident of one observable.

## Relevance to the prime-exponent lattice

The original positive-integer lattice is

\[
\mathbb N_0^{(\mathcal P)}.
\]

Passing to Fourier characters introduces its difference/group completion

\[
\mathbb Z^{(\mathcal P)},
\]

which is naturally the exponent-vector representation of \(\mathbb Q_{>0}\). The same logarithmic linear functional that gives integer size,

\[
\log n=\langle v(n),(\log p)_p\rangle,
\]

therefore diagonalizes the prime-flow Koopman representation on the entire character group:

\[
\langle k,(\log p)_p\rangle=\log q(k).
\]

This is a clean harmonic/spectral dictionary intrinsic to the lattice. It is exact, but it also shows why the most naive dynamical interpretation is too rigid: the bare flow is a compact almost-periodic rotation, not a mixing system with decay-generated resonances.

## Relation to PL-005 and Bagchi recurrence

This finding does **not** contradict `PL-005`.

Bagchi's RH equivalence concerns strong recurrence of the distinguished analytically continued zeta function in a space of holomorphic functions on the critical strip. The prime phases enter the limiting universality law, but the relevant object is not merely the Haar Koopman representation of translation on \(\mathbb T^{\mathcal P}\).

The distinction is therefore:

```text
bare prime torus rotation
    -> pure-point / almost-periodic Haar dynamics
    -> ergodic but non-mixing

zeta after analytic continuation as a function-space observable
    -> universality and strong recurrence
    -> RH-equivalent Bagchi condition
```

Thus a proposed explanation of Bagchi recurrence cannot come from generic mixing of the underlying prime rotation, because that mixing is absent.

## Prior art and novelty assessment

Compact abelian group rotations with discrete/pure-point spectrum are classical ergodic theory; Walters gives a standard treatment through character theory and group rotations. No novelty is claimed for that theorem.

Lorenzo Baroni's 2026 work gives a modern treatment of Kronecker flows on countably infinite tori and proves, in particular, that the finite-integer frequency module controls minimality and unique ergodicity; the prime frequency module is free by unique factorization.

The exact identification

\[
\mathbb Z^{(\mathcal P)}\cong\mathbb Q_{>0},
\qquad
k\mapsto\prod_p p^{k_p},
\]

and the frequency formula \(\lambda_k=\log q(k)\) are immediate arithmetic specializations, not a novelty claim. The durable Mathia consequence is the negative one: **the undistinguished prime Kronecker flow cannot simultaneously be treated as a hidden chaotic/mixing dynamical system whose ordinary Koopman resonances encode the Riemann zero divisor.**

## Boundary conditions and failure modes

- Pure-point spectral type is asserted for the standard Haar \(L^2(\mathbb T^{\mathcal P})\) Koopman representation. A different nonunitary operator or anisotropic/singular function space is additional structure and is not ruled out by this finding.
- The full operator spectrum of the Stone generator is \(\mathbb R\), because the pure-point eigenfrequencies \(\log\mathbb Q_{>0}\) are dense. One must not confuse pure-point spectral **type** with a discrete closed spectral set.
- Non-mixing of the base flow does not prevent a singular or analytically continued observable from having complicated value distribution. Bagchi universality is therefore compatible with this obstruction.
- This finding does not exclude every spectral approach to RH. It excludes only approaches that expect the bare Haar prime rotation itself, without extra analytic/operator structure, to supply chaotic decay or a nontrivial resonance spectrum.
- Finite-dimensional prime projections are already ordinary irrational Kronecker flows; increasing the number of primes does not create mixing, because the character eigenbasis survives in the infinite product.

## Audit / falsification test

The exact audit is the character correlation identity

\[
\langle U_t\chi_k,\chi_k\rangle=e^{-it\log q(k)}.
\]

A claim that the standard prime-torus Haar flow is weakly mixing or mixing is falsified by any nonzero \(k\).

A proposed RH mechanism genuinely escaping this finding must identify which additional mathematical structure changes the spectral problem. If the proposed operator is unitarily equivalent to the same Haar Koopman translation, or if its claimed resonance mechanism requires correlation decay of this base flow, the proposal fails this audit.

## Consequence for the research line

The prime lattice supplies an exact harmonic spectrum, but that spectrum is the rational-log frequency module of an almost-periodic compact rotation. The useful dynamical lesson is therefore negative:

\[
\boxed{
\text{prime-frequency Kronecker flow alone}
\;\not\Rightarrow\;
\text{mixing / decay / RH resonance mechanism}.
}
\]

Any credible dynamical or spectral RH construction built from the lattice must explain where the missing nontrivial analytic structure enters, rather than attributing it to the raw torus rotation.
