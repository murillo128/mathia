# PL-015 — Beurling systems show that exponent-lattice combinatorics and square-root counting do not force RH

## Claim

Beurling generalized prime systems preserve the same abstract exponent-vector architecture as the ordinary positive integers while allowing radically different zeta-zero behavior. This gives a decisive negative result for any proposed RH mechanism that uses only:

- the free commutative exponent lattice / positive cone;
- its square-free `{0,1}` subcube and prime-coordinate directions;
- coarse square-root-scale regularity of the generalized-integer counting function; or
- any fixed finite prefix of the ordinary prime directions/frequencies.

For a Beurling prime sequence `q=(q_j)` with `log q_j` linearly independent over `Q`, every generalized integer has a unique finite-support exponent vector

```text
alpha = (alpha_j) in N_0^(N),
nu(alpha) = product_j q_j^(alpha_j),
log nu(alpha) = sum_j alpha_j log q_j.
```

Thus the abstract lattice geometry is the same as for ordinary integers; what changes is the infinite norm/frequency assignment `e_j -> log q_j`.

Wen-Bin Zhang constructed two Beurling systems whose generalized-integer counting functions both have square-root-scale error

```text
N_q(x) = kappa x + O(x^(1/2) exp(c (log x)^(2/3))),
```

but whose zeta functions have opposite zero behavior: one satisfies the Beurling analogue of RH, while the other has infinitely many zeros on a curve approaching `Re(s)=1` and no zeros to its right. Zhang later strengthened the contrast by constructing, for any prescribed large cutoff, two systems whose generalized primes below that cutoff are **exactly the ordinary rational primes**, whose integer counting functions both satisfy the normalized estimate

```text
N_q(x) = x + x^(1/2) O(exp(c (log x)^(2/3))),
```

while one realizes Beurling RH and the other realizes only the classical de la Vallée Poussin zero-free-region behavior.

Therefore neither the abstract exponent lattice, nor square-root-order counting regularity, nor an arbitrarily long finite prefix of the ordinary prime-frequency data can determine RH-type zero geometry.

**Evidence/status:** `LITERATURE+DERIVED + DECISIVE-NEGATIVE` for the stated mechanism class.

The Beurling constructions are established literature. The Mathia-specific consequence is the adversarial use of these systems as controls for the prime-exponent research line: a lattice-based mechanism that cannot distinguish Zhang's pairs is not a mechanism for the Riemann zero divisor.

## Why Beurling systems are the correct control for the exponent lattice

A standard Beurling system begins with an increasing sequence

```text
1 < q_1 < q_2 < ...,
```

usually with the logarithms `log q_j` linearly independent over `Q` when one wants unique factorization as positive real numbers. Its generalized integers are the multiplicative semigroup generated by the `q_j`.

Under that independence hypothesis,

```text
alpha -> product_j q_j^(alpha_j)
```

is injective on finite-support `alpha in N_0^(N)`. Consequently all of the purely combinatorial geometry used in the prime-lattice representation survives unchanged:

- coordinate basis vectors are generalized primes;
- multiplication is exponent-vector addition;
- square-free generalized integers are the finite-support vertices of `{0,1}^N`;
- divisibility is the coordinatewise partial order;
- the logarithmic size is the linear functional

```text
E_q(alpha) = <alpha, (log q_j)_j>.
```

The corresponding Beurling zeta function begins in its absolute-convergence half-plane as

```text
zeta_q(s)
  = sum_alpha exp(-s E_q(alpha))
  = product_j (1-q_j^(-s))^(-1).
```

Broucke, Kouroupis, and Perfekt explicitly formulate modern Bohr/Hardy theory for exactly these multiplicatively generated frequency systems. Thus Beurling systems are not an unrelated analogy: they are a direct deformation class of the same exponent-lattice-plus-energy construction.

## Zhang's 2007 pair separates counting regularity from zero geometry

Zhang's 2007 construction gives two Beurling generalized number systems with

```text
N_q(x)=kappa x + O(x^(1/2) exp(c (log x)^(2/3)))
```

for positive density constant `kappa`.

The associated zeta function of the first system satisfies the Beurling RH condition. In the terminology used in later Beurling work, this means that, apart from the simple pole at `s=1`, the zeta function has an analytic nonvanishing extension to `Re(s)>1/2` with the required finite/zero-order control in smaller right half-planes.

The second system instead has infinitely many zeros on

```text
sigma = 1 - 1/log t
```

and no zeros to the right of that curve, together with the corresponding de la Vallée Poussin-scale oscillation in its prime-counting error.

The important comparison is not that the two systems are pointwise close. It is that they share:

```text
same abstract exponent cone
+ same multiplicative/Euler-product architecture
+ same square-root-scale class of N_q(x) regularity
```

while their zero-free half-planes are radically different.

Hence any derivation that tries to obtain RH from only those shared inputs is falsified by this pair.

## The 2013 normalization defeats finite-prefix mechanisms

Zhang's 2013 normalization strengthens the control in a way directly relevant to prime-lattice experimentation.

For **any assigned large number** `X_0`, the construction gives two Beurling systems such that the generalized primes not exceeding `X_0` are exactly the ordinary rational primes not exceeding `X_0`. Both systems have

```text
N_q(x)=x + x^(1/2) O(exp(c (log x)^(2/3))).
```

Nevertheless:

- the first realizes Beurling RH;
- the second realizes the classical zero-free region and de la Vallée Poussin error behavior rather than RH.

Therefore no mechanism that inspects only finitely many prime coordinates, finitely many `log p` frequencies, finite truncations of the exponent lattice, or any statistic determined by such a finite prefix can force the global RH conclusion. The cutoff can always be pushed beyond the entire inspected prefix while preserving the contrast.

This is especially relevant to numerical or finite-dimensional spectral experiments: agreement with the ordinary prime lattice to every tested finite scale is not evidence that a proposed mechanism is sensitive to the global arithmetic responsible for the Riemann zero divisor.

## What the obstruction does and does not rule out

The result rules out a broad but precise class of mechanisms:

```text
abstract free exponent lattice
+ coarse generalized-integer density
(+ any fixed finite prefix of ordinary prime frequencies)
    -> RH zero geometry.
```

It does **not** rule out a mechanism that uses the exact infinite frequency vector

```text
(log 2, log 3, log 5, ...)
```

through genuinely global invariants that Zhang's deformations do not preserve. In particular, it does not rule out mechanisms using:

- exact arithmetic relations specific to the rational primes beyond every finite cutoff;
- the global product formula and the archimedean place;
- Tate's adelic additive Fourier structure;
- the completed functional equation;
- exact Weil positivity / explicit-formula structure;
- another invariant proved to distinguish the ordinary prime norm map from the Beurling controls.

The Beurling examples generally do not carry the full arithmetic completion of the Riemann zeta function. Therefore this finding cannot be used to claim that the completed/adelic mechanisms in `PL-013` or `PL-014` are impossible.

## Relation to previous `prime_lattice` findings

This obstruction is complementary to, not redundant with, `PL-003`.

- `PL-003` keeps the **ordinary prime frequencies** fixed and varies unimodular multiplicative phases, showing that the ambient prime torus/frequency list alone does not determine the zero divisor.
- `PL-015` keeps the **abstract exponent lattice and positive Euler-product architecture** fixed while deforming the norm/frequency assignment, showing that the lattice combinatorics plus strong counting regularity do not determine RH either.
- `PL-014` identifies a classical structure that Beurling controls generally do not preserve: the adelic/global Fourier completion of the rational prime norm map.

Together they isolate the surviving target more sharply. A successful prime-lattice mechanism must use structure that is both:

1. specific to the full infinite arithmetic placement of the ordinary rational primes; and
2. strong enough to survive the passage from Euler-product data to the completed analytic object.

## Prior art and novelty assessment

No novelty is claimed for Beurling generalized primes or for Zhang's constructions.

- Beurling generalized prime systems are a classical framework for testing which prime-number phenomena depend only on multiplicative/counting axioms.
- Zhang's 2007 paper explicitly constructs one well-behaved system satisfying Beurling RH and another with zeros approaching the line `Re(s)=1` while retaining the same square-root-scale integer-counting error class.
- Zhang's 2013 paper normalizes the examples so that an arbitrarily long initial prime segment agrees exactly with the rational primes and both systems have density constant `1` with the same square-root-scale error form.
- Broucke, Kouroupis, and Perfekt give a modern Hardy/Bohr formulation for multiplicatively generated Beurling frequencies, confirming that this deformation class lies directly inside generalized Dirichlet-series/Bohr theory.

The only derived contribution recorded here is the use of these established examples as a **decisive control class** for the Mathia prime-exponent geometry: they identify precisely which information the abstract lattice and finite-dimensional approximations fail to retain.

## Boundary conditions and failure modes

- The two Zhang systems share an asymptotic **class** of integer-counting regularity; the finding does not assert that their counting functions are identical pointwise.
- The 2013 finite-prefix statement means that for each prescribed cutoff one can construct a suitable pair. It does not assert the existence of one fixed non-Riemann Beurling system agreeing with the rational primes up to every cutoff simultaneously.
- Beurling RH is a generalized zero-free/continuation condition for `zeta_q`, not evidence that a Beurling system possesses the Riemann functional equation or the same trivial-zero symmetry.
- If `log q_j` are not rationally independent, the map from exponent vectors to positive real norms can identify different formal products. The direct unique-exponent comparison uses the standard independent-log setting described in modern Beurling/Bohr theory.
- The obstruction does not apply to a proposed invariant that demonstrably depends on the entire exact rational-prime frequency vector or on completed adelic structure absent from the control systems.

## Audit / falsification criterion

The finding can be checked independently through four tests:

1. Verify from the modern Beurling/Bohr setup that rational independence of `{log q_j}` gives unique finite-product coordinates and the energy formula `log nu=<alpha,log q>`.
2. Verify Zhang's 2007 theorem/abstract: two systems satisfy the stated `N_q(x)` error scale while one satisfies Beurling RH and the other has zeros on `sigma=1-1/log t` with no zeros to the right.
3. Verify Zhang's 2013 normalization: for every assigned large cutoff, both systems agree with the rational primes below that cutoff and satisfy the normalized square-root-scale `N_q(x)` estimate, yet have the contrasting RH/classical-zero-free behavior.
4. For any claimed lattice mechanism, list its actual inputs. If those inputs are shared by Zhang's contrasting systems, the mechanism cannot logically imply the Riemann zero conclusion.

The negative result would cease to apply to a candidate only when the candidate exhibits a precise invariant that the ordinary prime system has and the Beurling controls provably lack. Merely increasing the finite cutoff or improving numerical agreement is not such an invariant.

## Consequence for the research line

The exponent lattice should now be treated as **universal multiplicative scaffolding**, not as the part of the construction likely to force RH. Even strong square-root-scale regularity of the induced generalized integers, and even arbitrarily long exact agreement with the ordinary prime directions, do not fix the zeta zero geometry.

What remains potentially distinctive is the exact infinite rational-prime norm map together with its global arithmetic completion. This reinforces the redirect already suggested by `PL-013` and `PL-014`: future geometric or spectral mechanisms must identify a genuinely global invariant of the ordinary prime placement, rather than an effect that persists for generic free multiplicative lattices or finite truncations.
