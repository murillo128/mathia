# PL-114 — Fixed-modulus congruence pair couplings scalarize into Dirichlet-character prime channels

## Claim

A genuinely arithmetic two-prime relation that survived the generic relative-energy obstruction in `PL-113` is to couple prime coordinates by congruence classes. For any **fixed** modulus `m>=2`, fixed exponent depth `l>=1`, and fixed symmetric kernel

`W : G_m x G_m -> C`, where `G_m=(Z/mZ)^*`,

define the full-lattice observable

`C_{l,m,W}(n)=sum_{p<q, p,q not dividing m, v_p(n)>=l, v_q(n)>=l} W([p],[q])`.

Then, for `Re(s)>1`, its Dirichlet series factorizes exactly as

`F_{l,m,W}(s)=sum_{n>=1} C_{l,m,W}(n)n^(-s)=zeta(s) Q_{m,W}(l s)`,

where

`Q_{m,W}(a)=sum_{p<q, p,q not dividing m} W([p],[q])(pq)^(-a)`.

Because the modulus and kernel are fixed, `Q_{m,W}` is a **finite bilinear form in residue-class prime-zeta sums**. If the kernel is translation-invariant on the finite abelian group,

`W(r,t)=kappa(r t^(-1))`,

then finite Fourier analysis diagonalizes the pair coupling completely into Dirichlet-character prime sums:

`Q_{m,kappa}(a)
 = (1/2) [ sum_{chi mod m} kappa_hat(chi) P_chi(a) P_bar_chi(a)
           - kappa(1) P^(m)(2a) ]`,

valid for `Re(a)>1`, with

`P_chi(a)=sum_{p not dividing m} chi(p)p^(-a)`,

`P^(m)(a)=sum_{p not dividing m}p^(-a)`.

In particular, the same-residue coupling `W(r,t)=1_{r=t}` gives

`Q_{m,=}(a)
 = (1/2) [ (1/phi(m)) sum_{chi mod m} P_chi(a)P_bar_chi(a)
           - P^(m)(2a) ]`.

Thus **fixed finite congruence labels do not preserve an irreducible two-prime geometry**. They distinguish rational primes from an unlabeled generic free monoid, but the interaction itself collapses under the ordinary character Fourier transform to finitely many scalar prime channels. Those channels are classical Dirichlet-prime/prime-zeta-modulo data and inherit their continuation singularities from Dirichlet `L`-functions rather than supplying a new operator, positivity principle, trace formula, or mechanism forcing `Re(rho)=1/2`.

**Evidence/status:** `LITERATURE+EXACT-DERIVED + DECISIVE-NEGATIVE` for the route

`fixed modulus + fixed finite residue-class kernel + fixed depth -> irreducible arithmetic two-prime RH geometry`.

The finding does **not** rule out moduli/conductors growing with the scale, relations not reducible to finitely many residue labels, or target-relative/nonlocal operators.

## 1. Exact full-lattice factorization

Fix distinct primes `p,q` not dividing `m`. The condition

`v_p(n)>=l` and `v_q(n)>=l`

is equivalent to `(pq)^l | n`. Hence for `Re(s)>1`, where the resulting nonnegative/absolutely convergent majorants justify exchanging sums,

`sum_{n:(pq)^l|n} n^(-s) = (pq)^(-l s) zeta(s)`.

Summing over pairs gives

`F_{l,m,W}(s)
 = zeta(s) sum_{p<q, p,q not dividing m} W([p],[q])(pq)^(-l s)
 = zeta(s) Q_{m,W}(l s)`.

No Euler product or analytic continuation is used in this derivation. It is only the exact incidence geometry of the exponent lattice: crossing depth `l` simultaneously in coordinates `p` and `q` selects the principal ideal generated by `(pq)^l`.

Since `G_m` is finite, define for each reduced residue class `r`

`P_r(a)=sum_{p not dividing m, [p]=r} p^(-a)`, `Re(a)>1`.

For symmetric `W`, ordered-pair symmetrization yields

`Q_{m,W}(a)
 = (1/2) [ sum_{r,t in G_m} W(r,t) P_r(a)P_t(a)
           - sum_{r in G_m} W(r,r)P_r(2a) ]`.

The subtraction is the exact `p=q` diagonal. Therefore an arbitrary fixed residue kernel already reduces to a finite matrix-valued coefficient acting on the finite vector `(P_r(a))_r`; there is no remaining infinite-dimensional prime-pair incidence after residue aggregation.

## 2. Convolution kernels are diagonalized exactly by Dirichlet characters

Suppose

`W(r,t)=kappa(r t^(-1))`

with `kappa(g)=kappa(g^(-1))` when a symmetric unordered-pair observable is desired. Normalize Fourier coefficients by

`kappa_hat(chi)=(1/phi(m)) sum_{g in G_m} kappa(g) overline(chi(g))`,

so that

`kappa(g)=sum_{chi mod m} kappa_hat(chi) chi(g)`.

Character orthogonality gives

`sum_{r,t} kappa(r t^(-1)) P_r(a)P_t(a)
 = sum_{chi mod m} kappa_hat(chi) P_chi(a)P_bar_chi(a)`.

The diagonal has `r=t`, hence `kappa(r t^(-1))=kappa(1)`, giving the formula in the claim.

For equality of residue classes, `kappa(g)=1_{g=1}` and every Fourier coefficient equals `1/phi(m)`. This produces the especially transparent identity

`Q_{m,=}(a)
 = (1/2phi(m)) sum_chi P_chi(a)P_bar_chi(a)
   - (1/2)P^(m)(2a)`.

So the most obvious arithmetic pair relation missing from `PL-113` does not resist scalar harmonic analysis: it is exactly a finite character decomposition.

## 3. The scalar channels are classical Dirichlet-L data

For a Dirichlet character `chi mod m`, the Euler product gives, only for `Re(z)>1`,

`log L(z,chi)=sum_{k>=1} (1/k) P_{chi^k}(k z)`.

Möbius inversion in the power index gives the exact converse

`P_chi(z)=sum_{k>=1} (mu(k)/k) log L(k z,chi^k)`,

again initially in the absolutely convergent half-plane, with logarithm branches normalized from large positive real part. Indeed, after substituting the first identity, the coefficient of `P_{chi^n}(n z)` is

`(1/n) sum_{k|n} mu(k)`,

which is `1` for `n=1` and `0` otherwise.

This is the character analogue of the classical prime-zeta identity used in `PL-009`, `PL-112`, and `PL-113`. It makes the novelty boundary explicit: continuation of the residue/character prime sums is controlled by logarithms of ordinary Dirichlet `L(kz,chi^k)` functions. Zeros and poles of those scalar `L`-functions therefore become branch singularities of the continued prime channels. Continuing the character formula does not derive a new zero-location law; it imports the corresponding Dirichlet-L divisor through classical one-variable analytic continuation.

Richard J. Mathar, *Table of Dirichlet L-Series and Prime Zeta Modulo Functions for Small Moduli*, arXiv:1008.2547v2 (2015), explicitly treats the character-weighted prime sums as **Dirichlet Prime L-functions** and their Fourier synthesis into **Prime Zeta Modulo functions**, and notes that the formulas/numerical techniques are standard. The use here is therefore not claimed as a new prime-zeta construction.

## 4. What genuinely survives into the critical strip

The original pair series `Q_{m,W}(a)` converges absolutely and is holomorphic for `Re(a)>1`. Consequently

`zeta(s)Q_{m,W}(l s)`

provides a meromorphic continuation of the lattice Dirichlet series from its defining half-plane `Re(s)>1` to

`Re(s)>1/l`.

This continuation uses the directly convergent pair factor at `l s`; it does **not** continue a divergent Euler product termwise.

The depth dependence makes the critical-line relevance precise:

- `l=1`: the pair factor itself requires `Re(s)>1`, so this route does not enter the critical strip.
- `l=2`: `Re(s)=1/2` is exactly the absolute-convergence boundary of the pair factor, giving a boundary phenomenon but no zero-localizing mechanism.
- `l>=3`: `Q_{m,W}(l s)` is already holomorphic on a neighborhood of `Re(s)=1/2`; any ordinary Riemann-zeta zero there enters through the explicit scalar factor `zeta(s)`, subject only to accidental cancellation by `Q`.

Thus the appearance of `1/2` at depth two is again a summability threshold, while deeper observables simply expose the existing zeta factor across the critical line. Neither case forces the zero divisor onto that line.

If one instead pushes the residue/character prime functions themselves farther left, the previous section shows that the singular input broadens from the Riemann zeta divisor to the finite family of Dirichlet-character channels and their power twists. That is closer to GRH data than to an explanation of ordinary RH.

## 5. Matched control: finite-label scalarization is not rational-prime rigidity

The congruence labels are arithmetically genuine, so this candidate does not survive the **unlabeled** free-monoid control used in `PL-113`. But the scalarization mechanism survives a sharper matched control.

Let a free commutative monoid have generators `j in J`, arbitrary energies `lambda_j>0`, and arbitrary labels `g_j` in a fixed finite abelian group `G`. Define

`P_chi(a)=sum_j chi(g_j) exp(-a lambda_j)`

and weight generator pairs by a fixed convolution kernel `kappa(g_j g_k^(-1))`. Whenever the sums converge absolutely, exactly the same finite Fourier calculation gives

`(1/2)[sum_chi kappa_hat(chi)P_chi(a)P_bar_chi(a)
       - kappa(1)sum_j exp(-2a lambda_j)]`.

Therefore the **structural** reduction to character channels follows only from a free set of coordinates carrying fixed finite abelian labels. Rational-prime arithmetic determines the special labels `p mod m` and the numerical scalar functions that result, but it does not create a new multidimensional geometry at this level.

This is the relevant falsification control: fixed finite congruence structure adds arithmetic data, but finite harmonic analysis exhausts that data before any RH-specific operator or positivity mechanism appears.

## Prior art and novelty audit

The nearest direct prior-art anchor found in the targeted audit is:

- **Richard J. Mathar**, “Table of Dirichlet L-Series and Prime Zeta Modulo Functions for Small Moduli,” arXiv:1008.2547v2 [math.NT] (revised 31 July 2015). It tabulates Dirichlet characters, Dirichlet `L`-series, character-weighted prime sums, and Prime Zeta Modulo functions, explicitly presenting this finite Fourier synthesis as established machinery.

Classical Dirichlet-character orthogonality and Euler products supply the rest of the reduction. The audit did not identify a source whose theorem is phrased as the exact exponent-depth pair observable above, but absence of identical wording is not a novelty claim. The durable contribution is the **route classification** relative to `PL-113`: one explicit class of genuinely arithmetic two-prime couplings still collapses to classical scalar one-variable data.

The finding is deliberately narrower than saying that all arithmetic prime-pair geometry scalarizes. Relations involving prime gaps, additive constraints such as `p+q`, conductors or moduli growing with the observation scale, nonlocal incidence, or target-relative operators are not covered.

## Adversarial boundaries

1. **Fixed modulus is load-bearing.** If `m` grows with a cutoff or spectral parameter, the number of character channels is not uniformly finite and this argument gives no uniform scalar-complexity bound.
2. **Finite residue labels are load-bearing.** A relation that cannot be factored through `(p mod m,q mod m)` for one fixed `m` is outside the result.
3. **Convolutional symmetry is needed only for diagonal character form.** An arbitrary fixed symmetric kernel `W(r,t)` still gives a finite bilinear form in the residue prime-zeta vector, but it need not be diagonal in the character basis.
4. **Prime-zeta continuation is multivalued around inherited zeros.** The `log L` formula describes branch singularities; it must not be advertised as a globally single-valued meromorphic continuation of `P_chi`.
5. **The critical-strip extension of `F_{l,m,W}` does not require that continuation.** For `Re(s)>1/l`, the pair series `Q(l s)` itself converges, and only the standard meromorphic continuation of `zeta(s)` is used.
6. **No GRH or RH equivalence is claimed.** Character channels expose known zero-sensitive scalar data; they do not constrain those zeros.

## Consequence for the research line

`PL-112` showed that fixed-degree symmetric depth couplings scalarize to ordinary prime-zeta polynomials. `PL-113` showed that fixed-depth pair kernels depending only on relative log-energy scalarize to vertical prime-zeta power spectra, leaving genuinely arithmetic two-prime relations as a plausible escape. This finding closes the cheapest such escape: **fixed congruence relations at fixed modulus are finite-label harmonic data and scalarize into Dirichlet-character prime channels.**

A next candidate must therefore use arithmetic relations whose complexity grows with scale or cannot be exhausted by a fixed finite label Fourier transform, or must introduce a target-relative operator/positivity structure that survives the line's Beurling, Helson, and generic labeled-monoid controls. Merely tagging prime coordinates by a fixed congruence class is not enough.
