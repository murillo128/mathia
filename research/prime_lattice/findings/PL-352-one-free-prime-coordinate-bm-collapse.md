# PL-352 — One free prime coordinate already forces form-core collapse at fixed Hamming degree

**Evidence:** `EXACT-DERIVED + CLASSICAL-PALEY-WIENER + CLASSICAL-BM + DECISIVE-BOUNDARY`

**Status:** `AFFINE-FREE-PRIME-FIBER-IS-ALREADY-A-FORM-CORE + FIXED-SQUAREFREE-HAMMING-DEGREE-DOES-NOT-PROVIDE-INTERMEDIATE-SPARSITY + PRIME-COORDINATE-THINNING-OR-COEFFICIENT-COUPLING-IS-NECESSARY`

## Precise claim

`PL-350` showed that multiplicative closure across two independent prime directions forces infinite Beurling--Malliavin density, while `PL-351` showed that finitely many unmixed complete prime axes can retain a finite BM budget. A natural remaining escape is to forbid arbitrary multiplication by bounding the interaction order in exponent space: for example, keep only square-free vectors of a fixed Hamming weight `k`.

That escape already collapses.

Let `S` be a set of exponent vectors and let

\[
E(\alpha)=\sum_p \alpha_p\log p
\]

be the Prime-Lattice energy. Suppose there is a fixed finite-support vector `\alpha_0` and a finite set of primes `F` such that

\[
\alpha_0+e_p\in S
\qquad\text{for every prime }p\notin F.
\tag{PL352-1}
\]

Then the energy image `E(S)` contains the affine prime-log fiber

\[
E(\alpha_0)+\{\log p:p\notin F\}.
\tag{PL352-2}
\]

For every finite aperture `A>0` and every `0<s<1/2`,

\[
\boxed{
\overline{\operatorname{span}\{e^{iE(\alpha)x}:\alpha\in S\}}^{\,H^s(-A,A)}
=H^s(-A,A).
}
\tag{PL352-3}
\]

Hence, when these modulations are inserted into the endpoint-envelope construction of `PL-347`, the resulting state family is already a form core for the localized completed Weil form on every finite window. Equivalently, by the BM classification of `PL-349`, any such energy set has infinite exterior BM density.

The important concrete corollary is that for every fixed integer `k\ge1`, the exact square-free Hamming layer

\[
\mathcal H_k^{\rm sf}
:=\left\{\alpha\in\{0,1\}^{(\mathcal P)}:
|\operatorname{supp}\alpha|=k\right\}
\tag{PL352-4}
\]

is already a form-core modulation source on every finite aperture. The same is therefore true for the full total-degree layer

\[
\mathcal D_k:=\{\alpha\in\mathbb N_0^{(\mathcal P)}:|\alpha|_1=k\},
\tag{PL352-5}
\]

because it contains `\mathcal H_k^{\rm sf}`.

Thus **bounding Hamming weight, square-free interaction order, or total exponent degree does not create an intermediate Prime-Lattice source if one prime coordinate is still allowed to range freely over all rational primes**. The obstruction is weaker than multiplicative closure: a single affine fiber in one fresh prime coordinate already contains enough frequencies to recover the full local Weil form.

## 1. Reduction to the prime-log core

The proof is immediate once the correct lattice slice is exposed. Put

\[
c:=E(\alpha_0).
\]

By (PL352-1), the frequency family generated by `S` contains

\[
\Lambda_{\alpha_0}
:=\{c+\log p:p\notin F\}.
\tag{PL352-6}
\]

`PL-348` proved that the prime-log exponentials

\[
\{e^{i(\log p)x}:p\text{ prime}\}
\]

are complete in `H^s(-A,A)` for every finite `A` and every `0<s<1/2`. Removing finitely many primes does not alter that proof: an annihilating compactly supported distribution would still have its Fourier--Laplace transform vanish at all but finitely many points `-\log p`, whose counting function remains superlinear by the prime number theorem, contradicting the linear zero-count bound for a nonzero entire function of exponential type.

Multiplication by the fixed phase `e^{icx}` is a bounded invertible multiplier on `H^s(-A,A)`. Therefore

\[
\overline{\operatorname{span}\{e^{i(c+\log p)x}:p\notin F\}}
=e^{icx}H^s(-A,A)
=H^s(-A,A),
\tag{PL352-7}
\]

which proves (PL352-3). Since `E(S)` contains this complete subsystem, enlarging the source cannot restore incompleteness.

The same statement can be phrased in BM language. Translation, deletion of finitely many frequencies, and passage to a superset preserve the property of having infinite completeness radius. Hence `PL-349` gives

\[
D_{BM}^*(E(S))=\infty.
\tag{PL352-8}
\]

No Euler product, analytic continuation, or assertion about zeta zeros is used here; this is purely a frequency-completeness obstruction.

## 2. Fixed Hamming layers contain an affine free-prime fiber

Fix `k\ge1`. Choose distinct primes

\[
q_1,\ldots,q_{k-1}
\]

(with the empty choice when `k=1`) and put

\[
\alpha_0=e_{q_1}+\cdots+e_{q_{k-1}},
\qquad
m=q_1\cdots q_{k-1}.
\tag{PL352-9}
\]

For every prime `p\notin\{q_1,\ldots,q_{k-1}\}`,

\[
\alpha_0+e_p\in\mathcal H_k^{\rm sf},
\]

and its energy is

\[
E(\alpha_0+e_p)=\log m+\log p=\log(mp).
\tag{PL352-10}
\]

Thus `\mathcal H_k^{\rm sf}` contains exactly the affine free-prime fiber required by (PL352-1). This proves the square-free claim. Since every square-free vector of Hamming weight `k` also has total degree `k`, inclusion `\mathcal H_k^{\rm sf}\subset\mathcal D_k` proves the total-degree claim.

The point is structural. These fixed-degree families are **not multiplicatively closed** and do not contain the rank-two additive semigroup used in `PL-350`. Nevertheless, they remain terminal because the source has infinitely many available prime coordinates. Free variation in only one coordinate position across the rational-prime labels recreates a translated copy of the already-overcomplete prime-log family.

## 3. Prior-art and novelty audit

The ingredients are classical and the conclusion is not claimed as a new harmonic-analysis theorem. `PL-348` derives prime-log completeness from the prime number theorem plus the Paley--Wiener--Schwartz/Jensen zero-count obstruction; `PL-349` identifies the corresponding infinite BM completeness radius. The present result is an exact Prime-Lattice consequence obtained by locating a translated prime-log subsystem inside an affine exponent slice.

A targeted literature audit on 18 September 2026 searched for Beurling--Malliavin or exponential-system completeness statements formulated for logarithms of square-free integers, fixed-Hamming-weight prime products, fixed `k`-almost-prime layers, and translated prime-log fibers. It did not locate this exact source-selection statement. That absence is **not** evidence of novelty. The durable content is the route restriction relative to `PL-350` and `PL-351`: forbidding multiplicative closure is not enough; even bounded interaction order collapses unless the set of available prime coordinates is itself thinned or the linear span is constrained by additional coefficient relations.

This also explains why asymptotics for the number of `k`-almost primes are unnecessary here. The full fixed-degree layer is much larger than required. One translated copy of the degree-one prime-log family already forces the result.

## 4. Adversarial boundaries

1. **The criterion requires a cofinite free-prime fiber.** It does not rule out selecting a genuinely thin infinite subset of primes whose log frequencies have finite BM density, nor aperture-dependent prime-coordinate sets of the type left open by `PL-351`.

2. **Frequency support is the only structure tested.** Nonzero scalar weights on the same frequencies do not change an unrestricted linear span, but coefficient constraints, coupled states, positivity cones, nonlinear relations, or target-relative selection rules can change the problem and are not excluded.

3. **Bounded degree alone is what fails.** The result does not say that every subset of a fixed Hamming layer is complete. It says that the natural full layer, and more generally any subset containing an affine free-prime fiber, is already terminal.

4. **There is no arithmetic selectivity in the completeness conclusion.** A generic frequency family containing a translated set with the same superlinear zero density would behave the same way. Rational-prime specificity enters through the canonical coordinate labels and the PNT growth inherited from `\{\log p\}`; the conclusion itself supplies no new RH mechanism.

5. **This does not contradict `PL-351`.** A finite union of complete coordinate rays fixes the prime labels and varies exponents, producing only linear frequency density. Here the exponent degree is fixed but the prime label ranges over essentially all primes, and that label freedom alone is already superlinear after taking logarithms.

## Research consequence

The source-selection frontier is now sharper. After `PL-350`, one could hope that keeping only low-order or square-free interactions would avoid the multiplicative-closure collapse while remaining genuinely arithmetic. `PL-352` shows that this is insufficient: every fixed nonzero Hamming layer across all prime coordinates contains a translated prime-log form core.

Therefore a genuinely intermediate exponent-lattice family must suppress **coordinate availability**, not merely mixed degree. Within the frequency-only endpoint-modulation framework, a viable source has to thin the prime labels strongly enough to keep finite completeness radius, make that thinning depend on aperture at a rate compatible with the `PL-351` budget, or impose source-forced coefficient/coupling laws that prevent unrestricted polarization. Merely bounding `\omega(n)` or `\Omega(n)` leaves the full localized Weil criterion hidden inside the selected family.
