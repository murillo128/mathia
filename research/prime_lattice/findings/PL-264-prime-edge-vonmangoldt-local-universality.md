# PL-264 — Prime-edge von Mangoldt shocks are locally universal, so Weil sign cannot come from single-edge spectral geometry

**Evidence/status:** `LITERATURE+DERIVED`; `PRIOR-ART-REDIRECT`; `NEGATIVE/OBSTRUCTION` for single-prime-edge sign mechanisms.

## Claim

In the finite Connes–van Suijlekom (CvS) Galerkin path obtained by varying the prime cutoff `c=e^u`, every zeta prime-power threshold `u_0=log q`, `q=p^a`, enters through the same matrix direction at first order. Akiva Groskin's corrected 2026 finite-path analysis proves

\[
\Delta Q_N'(\log q)
=Q_N'(\log q+0)-Q_N'(\log q-0)
=-a_q\,\mathbf 1_N\mathbf 1_N^t,
\qquad
a_q=\frac{2\Lambda(q)}{\sqrt q\,\log q}>0.
\]

More strongly, for every derivative order `r>=1`, the entire singular jet has the form

\[
\Delta Q_N^{(r)}(u_0)
=\frac{\Lambda(q)}{\sqrt q}\,B_{r,N}(u_0),
\]

where `B_{r,N}(u_0)` is universal: it depends on the derivative order, Galerkin level, and edge location, but has no further dependence on the arithmetic identity of `q`. Thus, once the edge is localized, the local singular event contains no independent arithmetic structure beyond the von Mangoldt source scalar (and for zeta the edge location itself already determines the prime power and hence its von Mangoldt weight).

Consequently, **single-edge rank-one negativity, velocity interlacing, coincidence-resolvent/Weyl identities, or higher singular-jet geometry cannot supply the missing RH sign rigidity by themselves**. They are exact local readouts of the source already inserted into the finite Weil path. Any source-side positivity mechanism capable of bearing on RH must use a genuinely nonlocal relation among multiple prime-power events, or their coupling to the pole/archimedean completion, rather than a property shared separately by every prime edge.

This is a restriction on *event-local* mechanisms only. The full event stream is the von Mangoldt measure and therefore carries global prime information; the claim does not say that the complete CvS path is arithmetically universal or incapable of encoding the zero side.

## Exact local derivation

At fixed Galerkin level `N`, Groskin writes the cutoff path as

\[
Q_N(u)=Q_{\mathrm{arch}}(u)+Q_{\mathrm{pole}}(u)
-\sum_{q=p^a\le e^u}\frac{\Lambda(q)}{\sqrt q}
A_N\!\left(1-\frac{\log q}{u}\right),
\]

with analytic archimedean and pole blocks. The single-source divided-difference matrix satisfies

\[
A_N(0)=0,
\qquad
A_N'(0)=2\,\mathbf 1_N\mathbf 1_N^t.
\]

For the entering atom at `u_0=log q`, its reduced coordinate is

\[
\omega_q(u)=1-\frac{\log q}{u},
\qquad
\omega_q(u_0)=0,
\qquad
\omega_q'(u_0)=\frac1{\log q}.
\]

The background is analytic across `u_0`, so the derivative jump comes only from this entering source and the chain rule gives the displayed rank-one formula. Distributionally,

\[
(D_u^2Q_N)_{\mathrm{sing}}
=-\sum_{q=p^a}
\frac{2\Lambda(q)}{\sqrt q\,\log q}
\mathbf 1_N\mathbf 1_N^t\,\delta_{\log q}.
\]

Thus the singular second derivative is literally a matrix-valued von Mangoldt measure, but its matrix direction is fixed and rank one at every event.

A useful line-specific corollary is the exact first-order blind hyperplane. For any coefficient vector `v`,

\[
\Delta\frac{d}{du}\langle v,Q_N(u)v\rangle\Big|_{u=\log q}
=-a_q\,|\langle v,\mathbf1_N\rangle|^2.
\]

Hence every `v\perp\mathbf1_N` is invisible to **every individual prime-edge first-derivative shock**. This does not make that hyperplane globally invisible: smooth evolution between thresholds and higher-order jets can act there. It does show that the eventwise negative-semidefinite shock cannot control the full finite Weil form at first order.

For a simple spectral velocity, the same rank-one update gives

\[
\Delta\lambda_j'(u_0)
=-a_q|\langle\phi_j,\mathbf1_N\rangle|^2\le0,
\]

with ordinary rank-one interlacing. If

\[
W_\pm(z)=\langle\mathbf1_N,(Q_N'(u_0\pm0)-z)^{-1}\mathbf1_N\rangle,
\]

Sherman–Morrison yields

\[
W_+(z)=\frac{W_-(z)}{1-a_qW_-(z)},
\qquad
\frac1{W_+(z)}=\frac1{W_-(z)}-a_q
\]

as a meromorphic identity with the usual pointwise restrictions at zeros/poles. The entire eventwise resolvent increment is therefore again generated by the same one scalar `a_q`; it is not an additional spectral invariant.

## Higher jets do not restore local arithmetic freedom

The negative conclusion is not limited to the first derivative. Groskin proves an arithmetic-rigidity theorem: every singular jet is `Lambda(q)/sqrt(q)` times a matrix determined universally by `(r,N,u_0)`. The single-source matrix is odd in its edge coordinate, and its Taylor coefficients are explicit divided-difference polynomial matrices. The resulting finite source-to-jet map has a confluent-Vandermonde description and the first `2N+1` scaled odd jets coordinatize the finite source-profile quotient.

That finite harmonic geometry is nontrivial, but at an actual zeta prime event it does **not** introduce new q-dependent data. Once the event weight is read from the first jump, the remaining singular jets reconstruct the universal source profile. Therefore replacing the rank-one first jump by a larger collection of same-edge derivatives cannot, by itself, create the missing arithmetic selector.

## Relevance to the current prime-lattice frontier

`PL-261` isolated a finite-core detectability fact: a genuine Weil-sign failure is visible on a finite Laurent-polynomial core, so the outstanding burden is not completeness but proving the sign from source data. `PL-262` then showed that fixed-window sign control does not automatically survive the expanding-window limit, and `PL-263` showed that enforcing positivity by a spectral shift can manufacture real spectrum even when off-line zero quartets are explicitly present.

The CvS event law sharpens that frontier from the source side. Prime powers do provide canonical negative-semidefinite local shocks, but that sign is *too local and too universal*: it occurs independently at every prime power and only in one first-order matrix direction. The conditional positive-cell determinant barriers in Groskin's analysis explicitly assume positivity of the ambient finite form; they do not derive it from the shocks. Therefore the missing theorem cannot be obtained by chaining the phrases “each prime event is rank-one negative”, “the velocity spectrum interlaces”, or “the event Weyl function is Nevanlinna” without an additional global argument that controls how the events accumulate relative to the completed background.

A viable continuation of the source-side program must therefore produce at least one of the following kinds of genuinely global input: a matrix inequality coupling distinct prime-power thresholds; a completion identity tying the accumulated von Mangoldt shocks to the pole and archimedean blocks with the correct unshifted sign; or a source-computable transfer theorem whose constants remain stable on the expanding Fourier cores required by `PL-262`. Merely refining the local event dictionary does not cross that gate.

## Prior-art and novelty audit

The finite CvS framework and its source matrices are prior art. The specific matrix-valued von Mangoldt measure, arithmetic-rigidity theorem, finite jet dictionary, rank-one velocity deflation/interlacing, and coincidence-Weyl increment are all explicitly developed by Groskin; none is claimed here as new. The paper itself is careful that it proves no positivity and no RH, and that its “spectral barrier” statement is conditional on positive definiteness of the ambient finite matrix.

The durable delta for this line is a **prior-art redirect**: these results close the tempting local branch of the present source-side search. In particular, they show that a single-edge spectral-flow analysis cannot be the missing mechanism behind unshifted Weil positivity, because all of its q-dependence has already collapsed to the defining von Mangoldt atom. This fits the canonical `prime_lattice` exclusion against reformulations that merely reread arithmetic already inserted by definition.

As an adversarial control, the same event algebra is source-template universal: Groskin notes that, conditional on constructing corresponding finite paths, replacing the zeta source coefficient by an `L`-function coefficient leaves the matrix direction and finite event geometry unchanged. Sign-definite conclusions then follow only when that coefficient is positive. This confirms that the local geometry itself is not a rational-prime RH selector.

## Boundaries and falsification

This finding would be falsified by a single-edge invariant derived from the same finite path that (i) is not a function of the edge location/source scalar and the universal matrices, (ii) is mathematically defined without importing the zero divisor or an assumed positive ambient form, and (iii) yields a zeta-specific sign/localization statement not shared by matched source-template controls. The arithmetic-rigidity theorem rules out such an invariant if it depends only on the singular jet at one edge, so any counterexample must use information outside that local singular jet.

The claim does **not** rule out multi-edge interference, nonlocal dependence on the full path, completion effects, growing-window limits, or source statistics involving the entire von Mangoldt measure. It also does not promote the finite numerical zero-recovery program to a convergence theorem. Those remain separate questions.
