# PL-354 — Exact real Dirichlet-evaluation stacks are either locally invisible or algebraically fatal

**Evidence:** `EXACT-DERIVED + CLASSICAL-EXPONENTIAL-POLYNOMIAL + DECISIVE-NEGATIVE`

**Status:** `FINITE-REAL-EVALUATION-STACKS-REMAIN-FORM-CORES + INFINITE-EXACT-STACKS-KILL-EVERY-FINITE-PRIME-SUM + NO-INTERMEDIATE-NYMAN-LIKE-EVALUATION-FAMILY`

## Precise claim

`PL-353` leaves essentially infinite coefficient coupling as one possible escape from the collapse of finitely many coefficient constraints. The most direct continuation of its Nyman-like pole-cancellation test is to impose many exact Dirichlet evaluations

\[
\sum_p c_p p^{-\sigma}=0
\]

on finite prime-log modulation sums. That continuation has an exact **dense-or-zero dichotomy** and therefore does not produce an intermediate family.

Fix `A>0`, `0<s<1/2`, `I=(-A,A)`, and

\[
\mathcal E
:=\operatorname{span}_{\rm alg}\{e^{i(\log p)x}:p\text{ prime}\}
\subset H^s(I).
\tag{PL354-1}
\]

For `\sigma>0`, define on a finite sum

\[
P(x)=\sum_{p\in F} c_p e^{i(\log p)x}
\]

the coefficient functional

\[
L_\sigma(P):=\sum_{p\in F} c_p p^{-\sigma}.
\tag{PL354-2}
\]

For a set `\Sigma\subset(0,\infty)` of distinct real parameters put

\[
K_\Sigma:=\bigcap_{\sigma\in\Sigma}\ker L_\sigma
\subset\mathcal E.
\tag{PL354-3}
\]

Then:

\[
\boxed{
|\Sigma|<\infty
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\overline{K_\Sigma}^{\,H^s(I)}=H^s(I),
}
\tag{PL354-4}
\]

where the empty set gives the unconstrained prime-log span, while

\[
\boxed{
|\Sigma|=\infty
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
K_\Sigma=\{0\}.
}
\tag{PL354-5}
\]

Thus no fixed collection of exact positive-real Dirichlet-evaluation cancellation laws gives a proper nonzero closed modulation family in the local topology used by the completed Weil form. Finitely many constraints disappear after closure; infinitely many annihilate every nonzero algebraic finite-prime state before closure.

The same conclusion survives a moving finite schedule. If every aperture `A` is assigned an arbitrary **finite** set `\Sigma_A`, even with `|\Sigma_A|\to\infty` as `A\to\infty`, then at each fixed aperture the endpoint-envelope states generated by `K_{\Sigma_A}` are still a form core for the localized completed Weil form by `PL-353`. If one instead imposes an infinite exact stack at any fixed aperture, there are no nonzero finite-prime states at that aperture.

So the most literal attempt to turn the finite `p^{-\sigma}` relations left open by `PL-353` into an infinite coherent Nyman-like relation fails at both ends:

\[
\boxed{
\text{finite exact evaluation stack}\Rightarrow\text{full local closure},
\qquad
\text{infinite exact evaluation stack}\Rightarrow\text{zero algebraic family}.
}
\tag{PL354-6}
\]

This is a route restriction, not an RH mechanism.

## 1. Finite stacks are still invisible in the local form topology

For a finite set

\[
\Sigma=\{\sigma_1,\ldots,\sigma_m\}\subset(0,\infty),
\]

`PL-353` already proves the required closure statement once the functionals `L_{\sigma_j}` are algebraically independent. That independence is automatic for distinct real parameters.

Indeed, suppose

\[
\sum_{j=1}^m a_j L_{\sigma_j}=0
\quad\text{on }\mathcal E.
\tag{PL354-7}
\]

Evaluating on the basis vector `e^{i(\log p)x}` gives

\[
\sum_{j=1}^m a_j p^{-\sigma_j}=0
\qquad\text{for every prime }p.
\tag{PL354-8}
\]

Order the parameters so that

\[
0<\sigma_1<\cdots<\sigma_m.
\]

Writing `y=\log p` and dividing (PL354-8) by `e^{-\sigma_1 y}` gives

\[
a_1+\sum_{j=2}^m a_j e^{-(\sigma_j-\sigma_1)y}=0
\]

for an unbounded sequence `y=\log p`. Letting `p\to\infty` forces `a_1=0`. Repeating the argument forces every `a_j=0`. Hence the finite family of coefficient functionals is independent.

`PL-353` proves that no nonzero finite linear combination of the corresponding weight families

\[
w_p=\sum_{j=1}^m a_jp^{-\sigma_j}
\tag{PL354-9}
\]

is the trace on `{\log p}` of a continuous dual functional in the subcritical local Sobolev topology. Its finite-kernel theorem therefore applies and yields (PL354-4).

Multiplication by the endpoint envelope `u_A` then gives exactly the same form-domain consequence as `PL-353`:

\[
\overline{u_AK_\Sigma}^{\,\mathrm{form}}
=D(Q_W^A)
\qquad(|\Sigma|<\infty).
\tag{PL354-10}
\]

The number of constraints can be arbitrarily large. Finiteness at the **fixed aperture** is the only property used here, so choosing a sequence `m(A)\to\infty` does not create an intermediate closure if `m(A)<\infty` for each `A`.

## 2. An infinite exact stack kills every finite Dirichlet polynomial

Take now an infinite set `\Sigma\subset(0,\infty)` and suppose

\[
P(x)=\sum_{p\in F}c_p e^{i(\log p)x}\in K_\Sigma.
\tag{PL354-11}
\]

Associate to its coefficient vector the finite Dirichlet polynomial

\[
D_P(z):=\sum_{p\in F}c_p p^{-z}
=\sum_{p\in F}c_p e^{-(\log p)z}.
\tag{PL354-12}
\]

By definition of `K_\Sigma`,

\[
D_P(\sigma)=0
\qquad(\sigma\in\Sigma).
\tag{PL354-13}
\]

A nonzero finite exponential polynomial with distinct positive real exponents has only finitely many zeros on the real axis. For completeness, no zero-count theorem is needed here. List the primes with nonzero coefficients as

\[
p_1<\cdots<p_r,
\qquad
D_P(\sigma)=\sum_{j=1}^r c_j e^{-a_j\sigma},
\qquad
a_j=\log p_j,
\]

with `0<a_1<\cdots<a_r` and every `c_j\ne0`. As `\sigma\to+\infty`,

\[
e^{a_1\sigma}D_P(\sigma)
=c_1+\sum_{j=2}^r c_j e^{-(a_j-a_1)\sigma}
\longrightarrow c_1\ne0.
\tag{PL354-14}
\]

Therefore `D_P(\sigma)` is nonzero for every sufficiently large positive `\sigma`.

If the infinite set `\Sigma` is unbounded, (PL354-13) contradicts (PL354-14). If `\Sigma` is bounded, it has a finite accumulation point; since `D_P` is entire, the identity theorem forces `D_P\equiv0`. Distinct real exponentials are linearly independent, so all `c_j=0`. In either case `P=0`, proving (PL354-5).

This argument is stronger than a dimension count. It does not matter how slowly the evaluation parameters escape, whether they cluster, or how the prime support is chosen. Exact vanishing at infinitely many distinct positive real points is incompatible with any nonzero **finite-support** coefficient vector.

## 3. Why this closes the literal infinite Nyman-like extension of PL-353

The special relation

\[
\sum_p c_p p^{-\sigma}=0
\tag{PL354-15}
\]

was singled out in `PL-353` because it resembles the scalar pole-cancellation conditions that occur in Nyman--Beurling/Balazard formulations. `PL-353` showed that transporting finitely many such relations into the local endpoint-modulation model does not transport the Hardy/Mellin geometry that makes Nyman's criterion sensitive to analytic continuation: their joint kernel stays dense.

The obvious repair would be to require the same cancellation for many values of `\sigma`, hoping that an infinite coherent stack retains enough analytic information to define a proper closed family. Equations (PL354-4)--(PL354-5) show that the literal version of this repair has no intermediate regime. On algebraic prime-log sums, the coefficient data are a finite Dirichlet polynomial. A finite number of its real values is locally invisible after prime-log overcompleteness, while infinitely many exact zero values determine the polynomial so rigidly that only the zero vector remains.

This also explains why an aperture-dependent **finite** stack is not a loophole. At each fixed aperture, closure is taken after imposing only finitely many discontinuous coefficient functionals, so `PL-353` still absorbs all of them. Letting the number of constraints grow only after changing the aperture does not retroactively create a proper closed subspace at any fixed aperture.

The result therefore removes one concrete interpretation of the surviving phrase “infinite coherent relation.” A useful infinite relation must involve a topology or completion in which infinite-support coefficient objects are present, or it must be approximate/asymptotic, nonlinear, target-relative, cross-aperture, or otherwise not equivalent to exact zeros of each finite Dirichlet polynomial at an infinite real set.

## 4. Prior-art and novelty audit

The analytic fact used in Section 2 is classical exponential-polynomial/Dirichlet-polynomial theory. Finite sums of real exponentials form one of the basic Chebyshev-type systems, and their real-zero rigidity is much stronger than what is required here. The proof above deliberately uses only elementary dominance at `+infinity` plus the identity theorem, so no external zero-count theorem is load-bearing.

A targeted literature audit on 18 September 2026 searched for real-zero theorems for finite Dirichlet polynomials, exponential polynomials with distinct real exponents, Chebyshev systems of exponentials, and coefficient constraints of the form `sum c_p p^{-sigma}=0` in Nyman/Paley--Wiener settings. It found the classical general exponential-polynomial and Chebyshev-system literature, but no reason to regard the elementary real-zero statement or its use here as novel. No novelty claim is made.

The durable content is the **route classification relative to `PL-353`**. `PL-353` explicitly left infinite systems of discontinuous coefficient relations outside its finite-dimensional kernel lemma. `PL-354` closes the most canonical exact infinite system generated by positive-real Dirichlet evaluations and shows that it fails in the opposite direction: rather than defining a nontrivial proper closed subspace, it leaves no algebraic finite-prime vector at all.

No claim is made that all infinite coefficient systems have this dichotomy. The result uses the analyticity and one-sided dominance of the finite Dirichlet polynomial associated with the specific weights `p^{-sigma}`.

## 5. Adversarial boundaries

1. **Finite-support coefficient vectors are essential on the zero side.** The algebraic modulation space `mathcal E` consists of finite prime sums. A completed coefficient space may contain infinite Dirichlet series with a substantially richer real zero set, and the convergence/domain questions then become load-bearing. `PL-354` does not classify that setting.

2. **Exact homogeneous constraints are essential.** Approximate conditions such as `|L_\sigma(P)|<=epsilon`, asymptotic cancellation as `sigma` or `A` varies, or inhomogeneous interpolation laws are not covered. Those can have nontrivial finite-support solutions and require quantitative stability rather than uniqueness.

3. **Triangular support/constraint schemes are not reduced to (PL354-6).** If the allowed prime support is simultaneously truncated and the number or location of constraints depends on that truncation, finite-dimensional conditioning may matter before the local closure is taken. The theorem only says that removing the support cutoff and then taking the fixed-aperture closure gives the finite/full versus infinite/zero dichotomy above.

4. **Other infinite relations may survive.** Recurrences between coefficients, operator-domain equations, positivity cones, nonlinear constraints, cross-aperture compatibility, or continuous-dual trace conditions are not equivalent to a stack of evaluations (PL354-2) and remain outside this negative.

5. **This does not transport Nyman--Beurling into the local model.** The Nyman/Bagchi criteria live in a half-plane Hardy/Mellin geometry in which analytic continuation and the zeta divisor are part of the ambient space. The present result concerns only the prime-log endpoint-modulation topology of `PL-347`--`PL-353`.

6. **No zero localization follows.** The only zeros studied here are zeros of a finite auxiliary Dirichlet polynomial in the coefficient parameter `sigma`, not zeros of the Riemann zeta function.

## Research consequence

The coefficient-coupling frontier after `PL-353` can be narrowed once more. Exact Nyman-like evaluation constraints do not interpolate between one discontinuous coefficient law and a genuine closed arithmetic subsystem:

\[
\text{any finite stack at fixed aperture}
\longrightarrow
\text{form-core collapse},
\]

whereas

\[
\text{an infinite exact positive-real stack}
\longrightarrow
\text{no nonzero finite-prime state}.
\]

A viable intermediate source must therefore change a load-bearing feature: use a completed infinite-support coefficient topology, an approximate or quantitative relation, a continuous-dual trace genuinely visible to the form topology, a nonlinear/positive constraint, or a cross-aperture law. Merely adding more exact scalar `p^{-sigma}` cancellation equations does not escape the two terminal regimes already identified by the local Prime-Lattice modulation program.
