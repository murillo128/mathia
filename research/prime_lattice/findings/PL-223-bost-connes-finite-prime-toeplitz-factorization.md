# PL-223 — Bost–Connes semigroup correlations are tensor-product Toeplitz KMS data, including the bounded all-prime norm closure

## Claim

The higher-order semigroup-isometry escape left open by `PL-222` disappears not only for each finite set of primes, but also after taking the full **bounded norm closure over all prime directions**.

For a finite set `F` of rational primes let

\[
\mathcal T_F:=C^*(\mu_p:p\in F)
\]

be the `C*`-subalgebra of the rational Bost–Connes algebra generated only by those prime isometries. Then

\[
\boxed{\mathcal T_F\cong \bigotimes_{p\in F}\mathcal T,}
\]

where `\mathcal T` is the ordinary unilateral Toeplitz algebra, with `\mu_p` corresponding to the unilateral shift in the `p`-coordinate.

Moreover, for **every** Bost–Connes `KMS_beta` state `phi_beta` with `beta>0`, its restriction to `\mathcal T_F` is the tensor product of the one-coordinate Toeplitz KMS states. Explicitly, for multiindices `a,b in N_0^F`, writing

\[
\mu^a=\prod_{p\in F}\mu_p^{a_p},
\]

one has

\[
\boxed{
\phi_\beta\!\left(\mu^a(\mu^b)^*\right)
=\mathbf 1_{a=b}\prod_{p\in F}p^{-\beta a_p}.
}
\]

Now let

\[
\mathcal T_{\mathcal P}:=C^*(\mu_p:p\in\mathcal P).
\]

Every `*`-polynomial uses only finitely many prime generators, hence

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal T_{\mathcal P}
=\overline{\bigcup_{F\Subset\mathcal P}\mathcal T_F}
\cong
\bigotimes_{p\in\mathcal P}^{\min}\mathcal T,
}
\]

where the countable tensor product is the unital inductive limit of the finite tensor products. Under this identification every Bost–Connes `KMS_beta` state has the same restriction

\[
\boxed{
\phi_\beta|_{\mathcal T_{\mathcal P}}
=
\bigotimes_{p\in\mathcal P}\psi_{\log p,\beta},
}
\]

the unique product state determined by its finite-prime marginals.

Consequently, arbitrary bounded elements of the all-prime semigroup-isometry `C*`-algebra and all of their finite equilibrium multi-time correlations are norm limits of finite-prime tensor-product Toeplitz correlations. Passing from finitely many prime channels to the full **quasilocal/norm-completed** prime semigroup therefore creates no new cross-prime algebraic relation: the subsystem remains the countable product of independent unilateral Toeplitz directions, with the arithmetic entering through the chosen energy list `log p`.

This strengthens the previous finite-prime version of the finding and closes the specific “genuinely infinite-prime completion” loophole **only at the bounded `C*` norm-completion level**. It does not cover weak/von-Neumann limits that are not norm approximable by local observables, unbounded affiliated observables, global Gibbs traces or partition functions, renormalized infinite products/determinants, or couplings to the cyclotomic/adelic sector.

## 1. Distinct prime isometries are doubly commuting

The classical Bost–Connes relations give

\[
\mu_m\mu_n=\mu_{mn},\qquad
\mu_n^*\mu_n=1,
\]

and, when `(m,n)=1`,

\[
\mu_m^*\mu_n=\mu_n\mu_m^*.
\]

Hence for distinct primes `p!=q`, the isometries `mu_p,mu_q` commute and doubly commute. The universal `C*`-algebra generated by finitely many doubly commuting isometries is the spatial tensor product of one-variable Toeplitz algebras, so there is a canonical surjection

\[
\bigotimes_{p\in F}\mathcal T\twoheadrightarrow \mathcal T_F.
\]

There are no hidden extra relations in the Bost–Connes algebra. To see this concretely, use the standard Bost–Connes representation on `ell^2(N)` in which

\[
\mu_p\varepsilon_n=\varepsilon_{pn}.
\]

Every `n` decomposes uniquely as

\[
n=r\prod_{p\in F}p^{k_p},
\qquad (r,\prod_{p\in F}p)=1.
\]

Therefore

\[
\ell^2(\mathbb N)
\cong
\bigoplus_{(r,\prod_{p\in F}p)=1}\ell^2(\mathbb N_0^F),
\]

and each `mu_p` becomes an amplification of the unilateral shift in the `p`-coordinate. The standard tensor-product Toeplitz representation on `ell^2(N_0^F)` is faithful, so the displayed surjection is injective. This proves

\[
\mathcal T_F\cong\bigotimes_{p\in F}\mathcal T.
\]

This is operator-algebraic structure, not an RH input: it is simply the finite free-abelian positive cone of the prime-exponent lattice represented by doubly commuting unilateral shifts.

## 2. The KMS restriction factorizes exactly

The Bost–Connes time evolution satisfies

\[
\sigma_t(\mu_p)=p^{it}\mu_p.
\]

Thus

\[
\sigma_t\!\left(\mu^a(\mu^b)^*\right)
=
\exp\!\left(
 it\sum_{p\in F}(a_p-b_p)\log p
\right)
\mu^a(\mu^b)^*.
\]

Every KMS state is invariant under the time evolution. If `a!=b`, unique factorization gives

\[
\sum_{p\in F}(a_p-b_p)\log p
=\log\!\left(\frac{\prod p^{a_p}}{\prod p^{b_p}}\right)\ne0,
\]

so invariance forces

\[
\phi_\beta\!\left(\mu^a(\mu^b)^*\right)=0.
\]

If `a=b`, put `n=prod_(p in F) p^(a_p)`. The KMS identity and `mu_n^*mu_n=1` give

\[
\begin{aligned}
\phi_\beta(\mu_n\mu_n^*)
&=\phi_\beta\!\left(\mu_n^*\sigma_{i\beta}(\mu_n)\right)\\
&=n^{-\beta}\phi_\beta(\mu_n^*\mu_n)\\
&=n^{-\beta}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Hence

\[
\phi_\beta\!\left(\mu^a(\mu^b)^*\right)
=\delta_{a,b}\prod_{p\in F}p^{-\beta a_p}.
\]

For one unilateral Toeplitz shift `S` with dynamics `alpha_t(S)=e^{itE}S`, the standard `KMS_beta` state has

\[
\psi_{E,\beta}(S^aS^{*b})
=\delta_{a,b}e^{-\beta Ea}.
\]

Taking `E=log p` shows that

\[
\boxed{
\phi_\beta|_{\mathcal T_F}
=\bigotimes_{p\in F}\psi_{\log p,\beta}.
}
\]

The formula is independent of which Bost–Connes KMS state is chosen when `beta>1`; the low-temperature arithmetic symmetry breaking lives outside the prime-isometry-only subalgebra.

## 3. Arbitrary bounded higher-order finite-prime correlations add no arithmetic coupling

The monomials `mu^a(mu^b)^*` span a dense `*`-subalgebra of `\mathcal T_F`. Since the state and dynamics are norm-continuous on bounded observables, the factorization above determines the restriction on the full finite-prime `C*`-algebra, not only on algebraic polynomials.

Therefore, for bounded `X_1,...,X_k in \mathcal T_F`, any finite multi-time correlation

\[
C(t_1,\ldots,t_k)
=
\phi_\beta\!\left(
\sigma_{t_1}(X_1)\cdots\sigma_{t_k}(X_k)
\right)
\]

is exactly the corresponding correlation in the tensor product Toeplitz system

\[
\bigotimes_{p\in F}
(\mathcal T,\alpha^{(\log p)},\psi_{\log p,\beta}).
\]

This is stronger than `PL-222`, which programmed arbitrary finite prime weights only in a particular two-point function. Here **the entire bounded finite-correlation hierarchy** of the finite-prime semigroup sector is identified with a generic product of unilateral shifts.

The matched control is immediate. Choose any positive rationally independent numbers `E_1,...,E_d` and define

\[
\alpha_t(S_j)=e^{itE_j}S_j
\]

on `\mathcal T^{\otimes d}`. Its product KMS state satisfies

\[
\psi_\beta(S^a(S^b)^*)
=\delta_{a,b}\exp\!\left(-\beta\sum_j a_jE_j\right),
\]

which is structurally identical after `E_j=log p_j`. Thus every finite-prime higher-order readout in this sector survives replacement of rational primes by a generic free-abelian energy set.

No analytic continuation of an Euler product is used anywhere in this derivation. The formulas are real-temperature KMS identities valid inside the `C*`-dynamical system itself.

## 4. The all-prime bounded norm completion is the inductive-limit tensor product

The finite-prime factorization is coherent under inclusion. If `F subset F'`, then the inclusion

\[
\mathcal T_F\hookrightarrow\mathcal T_{F'}
\]

corresponds under the finite tensor-product identifications to

\[
x\longmapsto x\otimes1
\]

on the extra prime factors. This is immediate in the standard exponent-vector representation.

Indeed unique factorization gives a canonical unitary

\[
U:\ell^2(\mathbb N)
\longrightarrow
\bigotimes_{p\in\mathcal P}^{\varepsilon_0}\ell^2(\mathbb N_0),
\qquad
U\varepsilon_n
=
\bigotimes_p\varepsilon_{v_p(n)},
\]

where the Hilbert tensor product is taken with vacuum reference vector `varepsilon_0`; every basis tensor has finite nonzero support. Under `U`,

\[
U\mu_pU^*
=1\otimes\cdots\otimes S_p\otimes\cdots,
\]

the unilateral shift in exactly the `p`-coordinate.

By definition, every element of `C^*(mu_p:p in P)` is a norm limit of `*`-polynomials in finitely many generators. Therefore

\[
\mathcal T_{\mathcal P}
=
\overline{\bigcup_{F\Subset\mathcal P}\mathcal T_F}.
\]

Since each finite representation is faithful, the coherent inductive-limit representation is faithful as well. Hence

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal T_{\mathcal P}
\cong
\varinjlim_F\bigotimes_{p\in F}\mathcal T
=
\bigotimes_{p\in\mathcal P}^{\min}\mathcal T.
}
\]

There is no convergence hypothesis on an infinite Euler product in this statement. It is simply the standard unital `C*` inductive limit of local tensor products.

The KMS restrictions are compatible on overlaps. Thus the finite product states determine a unique state on the inductive limit,

\[
\phi_\beta|_{\mathcal T_{\mathcal P}}
=
\bigotimes_{p\in\mathcal P}\psi_{\log p,\beta}.
\]

This remains well-defined for every `beta>0`, including `beta<=1`: one is constructing a quasilocal product state from consistent finite marginals, not a global Gibbs trace with partition function `prod_p(1-p^(-beta))^(-1)`. The divergence of that product at `beta<=1` is therefore not an obstruction to the bounded `C*`-state.

Now let `X_1,...,X_k in T_P` and fix real `t_1,...,t_k`. Given `epsilon>0`, choose a common finite prime set `F` and local elements `Y_j in T_F` with `||X_j-Y_j||` arbitrarily small. Since each `sigma_t` is isometric and every state has norm one, a telescoping product estimate shows

\[
\phi_\beta\!\left(\prod_j\sigma_{t_j}(Y_j)\right)
\longrightarrow
\phi_\beta\!\left(\prod_j\sigma_{t_j}(X_j)\right).
\]

Thus every bounded all-prime finite-time correlation is a norm limit of finite-prime product-Toeplitz correlations. No new cross-prime relation appears merely by completing the local semigroup algebra in operator norm.

The same construction supplies a countable generic control. For any positive energy sequence `(E_j)` one obtains an inductive-limit dynamics

\[
\alpha_t(S_j)=e^{itE_j}S_j
\]

and the consistent product `KMS_beta` state `otimes_j psi_(E_j,beta)`. If the energies are chosen with the same finite rational-independence/nonresonance property as the prime logarithms, all of the free-abelian semigroup and local KMS structure above persists without rational primes. What may still distinguish `E_j=log p_j` is a **separately source-forced global use of the energy sequence**; the tensor-product `C*` structure itself supplies no such coupling.

Any genuinely new infinite-prime effect must therefore use a topology, functional, or coupling not already exhausted by this norm closure.

## 6. Adversarial boundaries and failure modes

**The cyclotomic diagonal is excluded.** The relation between `mu_n` and `e(r)` is where the genuine arithmetic Hecke structure lives. A correlation mixing the semigroup isometries with cyclotomic observables is not represented by the bare tensor-product Toeplitz model. `PL-214` constrains fixed algebraic correlations of that larger core, `PL-219`--`PL-220` constrain bounded scalar/correlation channels, and `PL-224` collapses the canonical finite cyclotomic corner hierarchy, but none of those statements makes the full Bost–Connes algebra a product of independent Toeplitz factors.

**“Infinite prime” is not synonymous with “outside the result.”** Every bounded element of `T_P=C*(mu_p:p in P)` is, by definition, norm approximable by finite-prime elements and is covered. What remains outside are genuinely non-quasilocal limits: weak/operator-topology limits in a GNS von Neumann algebra that need not converge in norm, unbounded affiliated observables, distributional limits, or renormalized global functionals.

**Global traces and partition functions are excluded.** The free primon partition function `zeta(beta)=prod_p(1-p^(-beta))^(-1)` for `beta>1` is precisely an all-prime global trace, not the expectation of a fixed bounded quasilocal observable. Its existence shows why the present result must not be overstated: the exact energy sequence `log p` can matter to global nonlocal functionals even though the bounded semigroup `C*`-algebra factorizes.

**Norm-convergent infinite products do not evade the factorization.** If a source-forced product or series converges in `C*` norm, its finite-prime approximants converge to the same element in the inductive-limit tensor product, and its KMS correlations are limits of the corresponding local product correlations. A proposed escape therefore needs more than the phrase “uses infinitely many primes”; it must identify a non-norm topology, a divergent/renormalized functional, an unbounded object, or an additional arithmetic coupling.

**Generic energy matching is a control, not a deformation of zeta.** The comparison shows that the tensor-product/KMS architecture itself does not distinguish rational primes. It does not assert that replacing `log p` by generic energies preserves the numerical value of a specially engineered global readout, nor that the generic energy system has a zeta function, functional equation, or RH analogue.

**Low-temperature symmetry breaking is not contradicted.** Extremal `KMS_beta` states for `beta>1` differ on the arithmetic/cyclotomic sector. Their restrictions to `\mathcal T_{\mathcal P}` all coincide because the finite-prime restrictions coincide and the local union is norm dense.

## Falsification / audit tests

The claim would be materially falsified or narrowed if any of the following failed:

1. distinct prime isometries in the Bost–Connes algebra did not doubly commute;
2. the standard representation restricted to `C*(mu_p:p in F)` imposed an extra relation beyond the coordinate Toeplitz shifts;
3. the finite tensor-product identifications were not coherent under `F subset F'`;
4. `C*(mu_p:p in P)` contained bounded elements not norm approximable by `*`-polynomials involving finitely many prime generators;
5. a Bost–Connes KMS state were not invariant under `sigma_t`;
6. unique factorization failed to make every off-diagonal finite-prime multiindex a nonzero time grade;
7. the KMS identity failed to give `phi_beta(mu_n mu_n^*)=n^(-beta)`;
8. the finite product KMS states failed to be compatible under the inductive-limit embeddings; or
9. finite multi-time correlations failed to pass to the norm limit by continuity of the state, multiplication, and dynamics.

All nine tests are independent of RH and of analytic continuation of `zeta`.

## Consequence for the research line

The Bost–Connes branch now has a sharper boundary. `PL-214` shows that fixed finite algebraic correlations in the full Hecke core are finite Dirichlet polynomials in the high-temperature sector; `PL-219`--`PL-222` remove several bounded one-point, modular, and correlation selectors; `PL-224` collapses the canonical finite cyclotomic corner hierarchy. The strengthened `PL-223` removes **all bounded finite- and infinite-prime norm-completed structure in the pure semigroup-isometry sector** as an independent source of cross-prime arithmetic coupling: it is exactly a countable product of ordinary Toeplitz KMS systems.

A surviving Bost–Connes mechanism must therefore use structure absent from that quasilocal tensor-product control: a source-forced coupling to the cyclotomic/adelic sector, a weak or von-Neumann infinite-prime limit not induced by norm convergence, an unbounded/global trace or determinant, a renormalized divergent tail, or another invariant whose arithmetic content cannot be reduced to independent shift energies plus a chosen global readout. Merely increasing prime support or correlation order inside the bounded semigroup-isometry `C*`-algebra is no longer a live escape.
