# PL-223 — Finite-prime Bost–Connes semigroup correlations are exactly tensor-product Toeplitz KMS data

## Claim

The higher-order finite-prime escape left open by `PL-222` disappears if one stays inside the canonical Bost–Connes semigroup-isometry sector.

Let `F` be a finite set of rational primes and let

\[
\mathcal T_F:=C^*(\mu_p:p\in F)
\]

be the `C*`-subalgebra of the rational Bost–Connes algebra generated only by the prime isometries. Then

\[
\boxed{\mathcal T_F\cong \bigotimes_{p\in F}\mathcal T,}
\]

where `\mathcal T` is the ordinary unilateral Toeplitz algebra, with `\mu_p` corresponding to the unilateral shift in the `p`-coordinate.

Moreover, for **every** Bost–Connes `KMS_beta` state `phi_beta` with `beta>0`, its restriction to `\mathcal T_F` is the tensor product of the one-coordinate Toeplitz KMS states. Explicitly, for multiindices `a,b in N_0^F`, writing

\[
\mu^a=\prod_{p\in F}\mu_p^{a_p},
\]

one has

\[
\boxed{
\phi_\beta\!\left(\mu^a(\mu^b)^*\right)
=\mathbf 1_{a=b}\prod_{p\in F}p^{-\beta a_p}.
}
\]

Consequently, for arbitrary bounded `X_1,...,X_k in \mathcal T_F` and real times `t_1,...,t_k`, every finite equilibrium correlation

\[
\phi_\beta\!\left(\sigma_{t_1}(X_1)\cdots\sigma_{t_k}(X_k)\right)
\]

is **exactly** a correlation in a finite tensor product of ordinary Toeplitz shift systems with one-particle energies `log p`. All nonlinear and higher-order bounded correlation data in this finite-prime semigroup sector therefore have a matched non-arithmetic control: replace the prime labels by any finite rationally independent positive energies `E_j` and use the same tensor-product Toeplitz system.

Thus neither noncommutativity, arbitrary correlation order, nor using the full bounded `C*`-completion of finitely many prime-isometry channels can create a rational-prime-specific RH mechanism. The arithmetic input in this sector is exhausted by the chosen energies `log p` and unique factorization/nonresonance.

**Evidence/status:** `LITERATURE+EXACT-DERIVED + DECISIVE-NEGATIVE + PRIOR-ART-AUDITED + NO-NOVELTY-CLAIM`.

The negative is deliberately restricted to the semigroup-isometry algebra generated by **finitely many** primes. It does not cover observables coupling the `mu_p` to the cyclotomic diagonal `e(r)`, genuinely infinite-prime completions, unbounded affiliated observables, relative modular constructions outside this subalgebra, renormalized traces/determinants, or adelic/Weil scattering.

## 1. Distinct prime isometries are doubly commuting

The classical Bost–Connes relations give

\[
\mu_m\mu_n=\mu_{mn},\qquad
\mu_n^*\mu_n=1,
\]

and, when `(m,n)=1`,

\[
\mu_m^*\mu_n=\mu_n\mu_m^*.
\]

Hence for distinct primes `p!=q`, the isometries `mu_p,mu_q` commute and doubly commute. The universal `C*`-algebra generated by finitely many doubly commuting isometries is the spatial tensor product of one-variable Toeplitz algebras, so there is a canonical surjection

\[
\bigotimes_{p\in F}\mathcal T\twoheadrightarrow \mathcal T_F.
\]

There are no hidden extra relations in the Bost–Connes algebra. To see this concretely, use the standard Bost–Connes representation on `ell^2(N)` in which

\[
\mu_p\varepsilon_n=\varepsilon_{pn}.
\]

Every `n` decomposes uniquely as

\[
n=r\prod_{p\in F}p^{k_p},
\qquad (r,\prod_{p\in F}p)=1.
\]

Therefore

\[
\ell^2(\mathbb N)
\cong
\bigoplus_{(r,\prod_{p\in F}p)=1}\ell^2(\mathbb N_0^F),
\]

and each `mu_p` becomes an amplification of the unilateral shift in the `p`-coordinate. The standard tensor-product Toeplitz representation on `ell^2(N_0^F)` is faithful, so the displayed surjection is injective. This proves

\[
\mathcal T_F\cong\bigotimes_{p\in F}\mathcal T.
\]

This is operator-algebraic structure, not an RH input: it is simply the finite free-abelian positive cone of the prime-exponent lattice represented by doubly commuting unilateral shifts.

## 2. The KMS restriction factorizes exactly

The Bost–Connes time evolution satisfies

\[
\sigma_t(\mu_p)=p^{it}\mu_p.
\]

Thus

\[
\sigma_t\!\left(\mu^a(\mu^b)^*\right)
=
\exp\!\left(
 it\sum_{p\in F}(a_p-b_p)\log p
\right)
\mu^a(\mu^b)^*.
\]

Every KMS state is invariant under the time evolution. If `a!=b`, unique factorization gives

\[
\sum_{p\in F}(a_p-b_p)\log p
=\log\!\left(\frac{\prod p^{a_p}}{\prod p^{b_p}}\right)\ne0,
\]

so invariance forces

\[
\phi_\beta\!\left(\mu^a(\mu^b)^*\right)=0.
\]

If `a=b`, put `n=prod_(p in F) p^(a_p)`. The KMS identity and `mu_n^*mu_n=1` give

\[
\begin{aligned}
\phi_\beta(\mu_n\mu_n^*)
&=\phi_\beta\!\left(\mu_n^*\sigma_{i\beta}(\mu_n)\right)\\
&=n^{-\beta}\phi_\beta(\mu_n^*\mu_n)\\
&=n^{-\beta}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Hence

\[
\phi_\beta\!\left(\mu^a(\mu^b)^*\right)
=\delta_{a,b}\prod_{p\in F}p^{-\beta a_p}.
\]

For one unilateral Toeplitz shift `S` with dynamics `alpha_t(S)=e^{itE}S`, the standard `KMS_beta` state has

\[
\psi_{E,\beta}(S^aS^{*b})
=\delta_{a,b}e^{-\beta Ea}.
\]

Taking `E=log p` shows that

\[
\boxed{
\phi_\beta|_{\mathcal T_F}
=\bigotimes_{p\in F}\psi_{\log p,\beta}.
}
\]

The formula is independent of which Bost–Connes KMS state is chosen when `beta>1`; the low-temperature arithmetic symmetry breaking lives outside the prime-isometry-only subalgebra.

## 3. Arbitrary bounded higher-order finite-prime correlations add no arithmetic coupling

The monomials `mu^a(mu^b)^*` span a dense `*`-subalgebra of `\mathcal T_F`. Since the state and dynamics are norm-continuous on bounded observables, the factorization above determines the restriction on the full finite-prime `C*`-algebra, not only on algebraic polynomials.

Therefore, for bounded `X_1,...,X_k in \mathcal T_F`, any finite multi-time correlation

\[
C(t_1,\ldots,t_k)
=
\phi_\beta\!\left(
\sigma_{t_1}(X_1)\cdots\sigma_{t_k}(X_k)
\right)
\]

is exactly the corresponding correlation in the tensor product Toeplitz system

\[
\bigotimes_{p\in F}
(\mathcal T,\alpha^{(\log p)},\psi_{\log p,\beta}).
\]

This is stronger than `PL-222`, which programmed arbitrary finite prime weights only in a particular two-point function. Here **the entire bounded finite-correlation hierarchy** of the finite-prime semigroup sector is identified with a generic product of unilateral shifts.

The matched control is immediate. Choose any positive rationally independent numbers `E_1,...,E_d` and define

\[
\alpha_t(S_j)=e^{itE_j}S_j
\]

on `\mathcal T^{\otimes d}`. Its product KMS state satisfies

\[
\psi_\beta(S^a(S^b)^*)
=\delta_{a,b}\exp\!\left(-\beta\sum_j a_jE_j\right),
\]

which is structurally identical after `E_j=log p_j`. Thus every finite-prime higher-order readout in this sector survives replacement of rational primes by a generic free-abelian energy set.

No analytic continuation of an Euler product is used anywhere in this derivation. The formulas are real-temperature KMS identities valid inside the `C*`-dynamical system itself.

## 4. Prior-art and novelty audit

The ingredients are classical. Bost and Connes' 1995 construction (source 5 in `research/prime_lattice/SOURCES.md`) supplies the isometry relations, the evolution `sigma_t(mu_n)=n^(it)mu_n`, the standard representation, and the KMS framework. Laca--Raeburn's semigroup-crossed-product treatment and the general Nica--Toeplitz/product-system KMS literature place exactly this sort of free-abelian Toeplitz/KMS factorization in established operator-algebraic machinery.

A targeted search for finite-prime Bost–Connes correlation hierarchies did not expose a theorem that should be claimed as a new arithmetic result. Absence of an exact search hit is not used as novelty evidence. The tensor-product identification and KMS moment formula above are elementary consequences of the classical relations, and **no novelty claim is made**.

The durable Mathia delta is a negative closure of an explicit surviving branch. `PL-222` left a possible finite-prime higher-order compatibility after eliminating arbitrary two-point weights. The present result shows that if the observables remain inside the semigroup-isometry sector generated by finitely many primes, increasing the correlation order or passing from algebraic polynomials to arbitrary bounded elements cannot create that compatibility: the whole subsystem is already a generic tensor-product Toeplitz KMS model.

## 5. Adversarial boundaries and failure modes

**The cyclotomic diagonal is excluded.** The relation between `mu_n` and `e(r)` is where the genuine arithmetic Hecke structure lives. A correlation mixing the semigroup isometries with cyclotomic observables is not represented by the bare tensor-product Toeplitz model. `PL-214` constrains fixed algebraic correlations of that larger core, but the present factorization does not claim to classify all bounded nonalgebraic cyclotomic couplings.

**Finite prime support is essential.** The result does not prove that a source-forced infinite-prime observable is a tensor product element with harmless correlation data. Infinite tensor products, convergence thresholds, non-summable tails, and global modular/trace structures can introduce genuinely new analytic issues.

**Generic energy matching is a control, not a deformation of zeta.** The comparison only shows that the finite-prime correlation hierarchy itself does not distinguish rational primes. It does not assert that the generic energy system has a zeta function, functional equation, or RH analogue.

**The tensor-product state does not make the full Bost–Connes system a tensor product.** Only the subalgebra generated by the selected `mu_p` is being classified. The cyclotomic algebra and its semigroup action are precisely what prevent the full system from reducing to independent Toeplitz factors.

**Low-temperature symmetry breaking is not contradicted.** Extremal `KMS_beta` states for `beta>1` differ on the arithmetic/cyclotomic sector. Their restrictions to `\mathcal T_F` coincide because the monomial values above are forced by time invariance and the KMS relation alone.

## Falsification / audit tests

The claim would be materially falsified or narrowed if any of the following failed:

1. distinct prime isometries in the Bost–Connes algebra did not doubly commute;
2. the standard representation restricted to `C*(mu_p:p in F)` imposed an extra relation beyond the coordinate Toeplitz shifts;
3. a Bost–Connes KMS state were not invariant under `sigma_t`;
4. unique factorization failed to make every off-diagonal finite-prime multiindex a nonzero time grade;
5. the KMS identity failed to give `phi_beta(mu_n mu_n^*)=n^(-beta)`;
6. bounded finite correlations were not determined by norm-density from the monomial `*`-algebra; or
7. a generic tensor-product Toeplitz system with matched rationally independent energies failed to reproduce the same moment formula.

All seven tests are independent of RH and of analytic continuation of `zeta`.

## Consequence for the research line

The Bost–Connes branch now has a sharper boundary. `PL-214` shows that fixed finite algebraic correlations in the full Hecke core are finite Dirichlet polynomials; `PL-219`--`PL-222` remove several one-point, modular, and two-point finite-channel selectors. `PL-223` additionally removes **all bounded higher-order finite-prime structure in the pure semigroup-isometry sector** by identifying it with a generic tensor product of ordinary Toeplitz KMS systems.

A surviving Bost–Connes mechanism must therefore use structure absent from that tensor-product control: a source-forced coupling to the cyclotomic/adelic sector, a genuinely infinite-prime relation with nontrivial convergence or renormalization, an unbounded/global trace or determinant, or another invariant whose arithmetic content cannot be reduced to finitely many independent shift energies. Merely increasing correlation order among finitely many `mu_p` channels is no longer a live escape.
