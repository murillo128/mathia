# PL-169 — The first ratios off-diagonal correction imports additive shifted correlations, not new exponent-lattice geometry

## Claim

The most immediate beyond-support escape left open by `PL-168` is already classical at the first non-diagonal layer of the zeta ratios machinery. Let

`prod_(alpha in A) zeta(s+alpha) / prod_(gamma in C) zeta(s+gamma) = sum_(n>=1) I_(A,C)(n) n^(-s)`

in a half-plane of absolute convergence. The coefficients `I_(A,C)` are multiplicative, so in prime-exponent coordinates they factor intrinsically as

`I_(A,C)(n) = prod_p I_(A,C)(p^(v_p(n)))`.

The ordinary vertical average supplies the harmonic detector `m=n`, equivalently `v(m)=v(n)`. However, when the corresponding truncated ratio Dirichlet polynomials are long enough for a first off-diagonal contribution to survive, Conrey--Keating show that the relevant arithmetic data are shifted correlations

`I_(A,C)(n) I_(B,D)(n+h)`.

More precisely, their 2017 ratios/divisor-correlation theorem identifies the arithmetic contribution of these shifted correlations exactly with the `|U|=|V|=1` **one-swap** terms of the ratios conjecture. For truncation length `X=T^lambda`, their comparison explicitly separates the regime `lambda<1`, where the mean is diagonal-dominated, from `lambda>1`, where these off-diagonal correlations enter. Thus the first standard arithmetic correction beyond multiplicative diagonal matching does not reveal an irreducible tensor or circuit internal to the exponent lattice: it imports the additive relation `m=n+h` on the ordinary integers.

This is structurally important for `prime_lattice`. The abstract free commutative monoid of exponent vectors remembers multiplication perfectly, but it does not carry the addition law `n+h`. The map

`v(n) -> v(n+h)`

is highly nonlocal in prime coordinates and is not generated by coordinate translation, the energy functional `sum_p v_p(n) log p`, or a finite exponent-conservation law. Consequently the first classical route that recovers arithmetic-specific lower-order zero statistics after the Rudnick--Sarnak multi-diagonal range uses **extra semiring structure of Z**, not the multiplicative lattice alone.

**Evidence/status:** `LITERATURE+DERIVED + PRIOR-ART/REDIRECT + NEGATIVE/OBSTRUCTION` for the route

`leave the Rudnick--Sarnak diagonal-pairing support range -> recover a new intrinsic cross-prime exponent-lattice invariant`.

The Conrey--Keating identification is classical prior art and is conditional at the stated theorem/conjecture interfaces. The line-specific deduction is that its first off-diagonal arithmetic variable is additive shift, which is external to the intrinsic exponent-lattice operations. No novelty is claimed for the ratios conjecture, swap expansion, shifted divisor correlations, delta method, or zero-correlation formulas.

## The ratio coefficients are native multiplicative-lattice data

For finite shift sets `A` and `C`, define `I_(A,C)(n)` by the Dirichlet series of the zeta ratio in a region where the numerator and reciprocal denominator expansions converge absolutely. Since both zeta coefficients and reciprocal-zeta coefficients are multiplicative, so is `I_(A,C)`. Hence

`I_(A,C)(n) = prod_p a_p(v_p(n))`

for explicit local prime-power coefficients `a_p(j)` depending on the shifts.

This part is completely natural in the prime lattice. A vertical average of products of two such Dirichlet polynomials contains phases

`exp(-it(log m-log n))`,

and the infinite-time harmonic detector gives

`log m=log n <=> m=n <=> v(m)=v(n)`.

That is exactly the same multiplicative/exponent-vector diagonal mechanism audited repeatedly in this line and, at higher fixed zero-correlation order, in `PL-168`.

The important question after `PL-168` is what replaces this diagonal when the support or polynomial length is enlarged enough that non-diagonal arithmetic survives.

## Conrey--Keating identify the first surviving layer with `n` versus `n+h`

Brian Conrey and Jonathan Keating study averages of ratios

`R_(A,B,C,D)(T)`

on `s=1/2+it` and compare two evaluations of long truncated ratio polynomials. One evaluation uses the ratios conjecture. The other expands the Dirichlet coefficients and analyzes their correlations.

Their paper states the key routing result explicitly: the arithmetic contributions arising from averages of

`I_(A,C)(n) I_(B,D)(n+h)`

coincide with the ratios-conjecture terms having `|U|=|V|=1`, the one-swap terms. Their Theorem 2 shows that, for `X=T^lambda` with some `lambda>1`, a uniform ratios conjecture or a uniform conjecture for the coefficient correlations leads to the same asymptotic. They contrast this with `lambda<1`, where the average is dominated by diagonal terms.

The off-diagonal calculation is not a disguised prime-coordinate equality. After expansion it is organized by additive shifts `h`, Ramanujan sums `r_q(h)`, and a delta-method analysis. Prime Euler products re-enter when the resulting Dirichlet series are evaluated, but the coupling that made the off-diagonal term possible was already

`m-n=h`.

This is the first load-bearing distinction from the `PL-168` multi-diagonal sector. There, exact multiplicative equality reduces to exponent-vector conservation and then to universal pairings. Here, surviving arithmetic corrections require comparing *different integers at an additive displacement*.

## The additive shift is not an intrinsic operation of the exponent lattice

The exponent lattice is the free positive cone

`N_0^(P)`

with addition of exponent vectors representing multiplication of integers. Its canonical scalar functional is

`E(alpha)=sum_p alpha_p log p = log n(alpha)`.

Neither operation determines `v(n+h)` locally from `v(n)`. Even for `h=1`, changing `n` by one can replace essentially the entire prime support. There is no identity of the form

`v(n+h)=v(n)+c_h`

or a finite fixed collection of coordinate moves independent of `n`. Writing

`v(n+h)=v(n(alpha)+h)`

is only re-importing the ordinary integer addition law through the inverse factorization map; it adds no intrinsic lattice geometry.

This is also a useful falsification control. A generic Beurling/generalized-prime multiplicative system may have a free exponent monoid and an energy map analogous to `sum alpha_p log p`, but it has no canonical operation corresponding to adding the ordinary integer `h` to the represented generalized integer. The one-swap channel therefore can distinguish the rational integers from generic multiplicative frequency systems, but it does so by using **additional additive structure**. It is not evidence that the bare exponent lattice itself has acquired RH-sensitive rigidity.

## Relation to `PL-075`, `PL-076`, and `PL-168`

`PL-075` found the same structural escape in the simplest pointed coefficient channel: at fixed additive lag, the half-weight von Mangoldt Gram term is exactly Hardy--Littlewood prime-pair data `Lambda(n)Lambda(n+h)`. `PL-076` then showed that a canonical long smoothed aggregate is routed, under RH, to Montgomery zero pair correlation.

The present result is not another fixed-`Lambda` calculation. It says that the **general shifted-zeta ratio apparatus itself** has the same boundary at its first beyond-diagonal swap. The coefficient system now consists of the Möbius/divisor convolutions `I_(A,C)`, and Conrey--Keating identify the one-swap ratios term with their additive shifted correlations.

This also sharpens the frontier left by `PL-168`. Rudnick--Sarnak prove that fixed-order n-level correlations in their restricted Fourier-support region reduce at leading order to exponent-conservation pairings and the universal GUE determinant. Conrey--Snaith later show, assuming the Conrey--Farmer--Zirnbauer ratios conjecture, how to obtain all lower-order terms of arbitrary-order zero correlations without the same compact-support restriction. The first arithmetic correction exposed by the Conrey--Keating coefficient analysis is not a hidden non-pairing exponent circuit but an additive shifted-correlation layer.

Conrey--Keating's later divisor-correlation series goes further for shifted zeta moments: Type-II convolution machinery reconstructs all swap strata for long divisor-polynomial mean squares. That broader literature reinforces the routing conclusion, but it is not used here to claim a theorem that every arbitrary-support zero n-correlation is equivalent to a finite list of additive divisor correlations.

## Analytic-continuation boundary

The coefficient identity defining `I_(A,C)` is used only in a half-plane where the relevant Dirichlet expansions converge. The critical-line ratios problem is not obtained by formally substituting `s=1/2+it` into an Euler product.

The ratios recipe uses the zeta functional equation and analytic continuation. In Conrey--Keating's arithmetic comparison, contour shifts, the zeta functional equation, polar analysis of Dirichlet series, and delta-method/Ramanujan-sum input are part of the bridge. Their Theorem 1 is stated assuming GRH; Theorem 2 is conditional on either a suitable uniform ratios conjecture or a suitable uniform coefficient-correlation conjecture. These hypotheses are inputs, not outputs.

Accordingly this finding supplies no unconditional critical-line localization. It classifies where the first standard beyond-diagonal arithmetic information comes from.

## Adversarial controls and limitations

1. **One-swap is the exact main scope.** The theorem-level identification from the 2017 Conrey--Keating paper is for the `|U|=|V|=1` layer. This finding does not claim a proved additive-correlation formula for every swap in every zero n-correlation.
2. **Conditional interfaces remain conditional.** GRH and uniform ratios/correlation conjectures appear in the cited results. They cannot be used as evidence for RH.
3. **Addition is extra structure, not forbidden structure.** The conclusion is not that additive correlations are irrelevant to `prime_lattice`; it is that they are not generated by the multiplicative exponent monoid alone. A successful extension may deliberately couple the lattice to the additive/adelic structure of the rational integers.
4. **Euler-factorization after the shift does not make the shift intrinsic.** Ramanujan sums and local Euler products can factor the answer prime by prime once `h` is fixed, but the relation connecting the two coefficient arguments remains `n` and `n+h`.
5. **No universal Beurling analogue is asserted.** The control is structural: a bare generalized-prime monoid has no distinguished ordinary additive translation. A specially enriched generalized-prime semiring could of course be supplied with more structure and would require a separate audit.
6. **No new spectrum is produced.** One-swap terms and shifted correlations are terms in conjectural/asymptotic mean-value formulae, not eigenvalues of a self-adjoint operator.
7. **No formal Euler-product continuation occurs.** All critical-line statements rely on the established functional equation/analytic framework or the stated conjectural inputs.

A falsification would require showing that Conrey--Keating's one-swap terms are not generated by the displayed shifted coefficient correlations, or exhibiting a canonical operation internal to the free exponent monoid plus its energy functional that recovers `v(n+h)` without re-importing ordinary addition. Neither occurs in the audited sources.

## Consequence for the research line

The higher-correlation frontier now has a sharper structural split. Inside the Rudnick--Sarnak support window, the multiplicative lattice supplies exact conservation but collapses to universal pairings (`PL-168`). At the first standard beyond-diagonal ratios layer, arithmetic specificity returns, but through additive shifted correlations of multiplicative coefficients rather than through a new intrinsic exponent-lattice circuit.

Therefore “go beyond diagonal pairings” is still too weak as a research instruction. A viable `prime_lattice` mechanism must now do one of two harder things: either derive an RH-sensitive invariant from the multiplicative lattice without importing the ordinary additive law, or make the **coupling between multiplication and addition** itself into the new precise object and show why the rational-integer semiring/adelic completion supplies rigidity that Beurling and Helson controls do not.

The latter possibility is not closed by this finding. It is simply no longer legitimate to call classical shifted divisor/prime correlations an emergent property of the bare exponent lattice.
