# PL-374 — Finite-dimensional completely multiplicative matrix witnesses scalarize in trace/determinant data

**Evidence:** `EXACT-DERIVED + STANDARD-SIMULTANEOUS-TRIANGULARIZATION + PRIOR-ART-CALIBRATED + DECISIVE-NARROWING`.

**Parent finding:** `PL-373`.

**Related findings:** `PL-023`, `PL-034`, `PL-039`, `PL-369`, `PL-373`.

**Status:** `FINITE-DIMENSIONAL-MATRIX-VALUED-COMPLETE-MULTIPLICATIVITY-DOES-NOT-BY-ITSELF-ESCAPE-THE-SCALAR-PRIME-CHANNEL; TRACE-AND-DETERMINANT-EULER-OBSERVABLES-FACTOR-THROUGH-FINITELY-MANY-SCALAR-COMPLETELY-MULTIPLICATIVE-CHANNELS, WHILE ONLY-NONNORMAL-EXTENSION-DATA-REMAINS-OUTSIDE-THOSE-OBSERVABLES`.

## Claim

Let

\[
\rho:\mathbb N_{>0}\longrightarrow M_d(\mathbb C)
\]

be a unital completely multiplicative matrix representation,

\[
\rho(1)=I,
\qquad
\rho(mn)=\rho(m)\rho(n).
\tag{PL374-1}
\]

Write `A_p=rho(p)`. Because multiplication of positive integers is commutative, the entire family `{A_p}` is pairwise commuting. Hence, in finite dimension over `C`, it is simultaneously upper-triangularizable. In one common basis there are scalar functions `chi_1,...,chi_d` such that

\[
\operatorname{diag}\rho(n)
=
\bigl(\chi_1(n),\ldots,\chi_d(n)\bigr),
\qquad
\chi_j(mn)=\chi_j(m)\chi_j(n).
\tag{PL374-2}
\]

Thus every composition factor of the finite-dimensional prime-semigroup representation is a one-dimensional completely multiplicative scalar channel.

Two natural RH-facing scalar observables then lose all genuinely matrix-valued coupling:

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Tr}\rho(n)=\sum_{j=1}^d\chi_j(n)
}
\tag{PL374-3}
\]

and, prime by prime,

\[
\boxed{
\det(I-\rho(p)x)=\prod_{j=1}^d(1-\chi_j(p)x).
}
\tag{PL374-4}
\]

Consequently any trace Dirichlet series is a finite sum of scalar completely multiplicative Dirichlet series on every common domain where the termwise series are legitimate,

\[
\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{\operatorname{Tr}\rho(n)}{n^s}
=
\sum_{j=1}^d
\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{\chi_j(n)}{n^s},
\tag{PL374-5}
\]

while every finite-prime determinant Euler product factors exactly as

\[
\prod_{p\in P}
\det(I-\rho(p)p^{-s})^{-1}
=
\prod_{j=1}^d
\prod_{p\in P}(1-\chi_j(p)p^{-s})^{-1}.
\tag{PL374-6}
\]

The infinite version of (PL374-6) is asserted only in a region where the corresponding products converge; no Euler product is being transported formally into the critical strip.

If the matrices `A_p` are normal (in particular if they are unitary), the conclusion is stronger: the commuting family is simultaneously unitarily diagonalizable, so `rho` itself is a direct orthogonal sum of scalar completely multiplicative channels. There is then no finite-dimensional irreducible non-scalar prime coupling at all.

This is a no-go result only for the stated interface. For non-normal commuting matrices, the common upper-triangular form can contain nonzero off-diagonal extension data. Such data is invisible to trace and determinant but can affect norms, resolvents, pseudospectra, matrix coefficients, and other target-relative observables. Infinite-dimensional representations and representations that break complete multiplicativity are also outside the claim.

## 1. Complete multiplicativity forces a commuting prime family

For primes `p,q`,

\[
A_pA_q
=\rho(pq)
=\rho(qp)
=A_qA_p.
\tag{PL374-7}
\]

More generally every pair `rho(m),rho(n)` commutes. The matrix image of the positive-integer multiplicative monoid is therefore a commutative matrix semigroup.

Over an algebraically closed field, every commuting family of finite-dimensional matrices is simultaneously triangularizable. One way to see this without any finiteness assumption on the set of primes is to take the finite-dimensional commutative algebra `A` generated by all `A_p`. A nonzero finite-dimensional `A`-module has a common eigenvector; its span is invariant under the whole algebra. Passing to the quotient and inducting on `d` produces a common invariant flag and hence one basis triangularizing every `A_p`.

This is standard linear algebra/operator-theory prior art. A convenient literature anchor is H. Radjavi, “A Trace Condition Equivalent to Simultaneous Triangularizability,” *Canadian Journal of Mathematics* **38**(2) (1986), 376–386, DOI `10.4153/CJM-1986-018-1`, whose introduction explicitly records the classical fact that a commutative set of matrices over an algebraically closed field is triangularizable. No novelty is claimed for that theorem.

## 2. The diagonal entries are scalar completely multiplicative channels

Choose a common triangularizing basis and write

\[
T^{-1}A_pT=
\begin{pmatrix}
\chi_1(p)&*&\cdots&*\\
0&\chi_2(p)&\cdots&*\\
\vdots&&\ddots&\vdots\\
0&\cdots&0&\chi_d(p)
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{PL374-8}
\]

Unique factorization gives

\[
\rho(n)=\prod_p A_p^{v_p(n)}.
\tag{PL374-9}
\]

Products of upper-triangular matrices are upper triangular and multiply their diagonal entries coordinatewise. Hence

\[
\chi_j(n)
=
\prod_p\chi_j(p)^{v_p(n)}.
\tag{PL374-10}
\]

Equation (PL374-10) is exactly complete multiplicativity. In exponent-lattice language, a finite-dimensional linear representation of the positive cone generated by the basis directions `e_p` therefore has a finite flag whose one-dimensional graded pieces are scalar prime-axis characters.

This is the load-bearing algebraic collapse. The matrix representation can be a nontrivial extension of those scalar graded pieces, but it cannot have a higher-dimensional irreducible graded piece while retaining complete multiplicativity over `C`.

## 3. Trace cannot see the extension data

For every upper-triangular matrix, the trace is the sum of the diagonal entries. Applying this to (PL374-10) gives (PL374-3) exactly:

\[
\operatorname{Tr}\rho(n)=\sum_j\chi_j(n).
\]

Therefore, wherever absolute convergence permits rearrangement,

\[
D_\rho(s)
:=
\sum_n\operatorname{Tr}\rho(n)n^{-s}
=
\sum_j L(\chi_j,s).
\tag{PL374-11}
\]

No off-diagonal matrix extension can change this trace series. Any zero, pole, continuation, or positivity statement extracted only from `D_rho` must therefore arise from a finite scalar combination of the channels `chi_j`, not from a genuinely matrix-valued prime interaction.

The finite sum in (PL374-11) is not itself completely multiplicative, so `PL-373` cannot simply be applied to the sum. Nor do the hypotheses of `PL-373` automatically hold for each `chi_j`. The conclusion here is more limited and algebraic: finite-dimensional matrix-valued complete multiplicativity has not created an irreducible non-scalar source object at the level of trace.

## 4. Determinant Euler data also sees only the scalar channels

For any upper-triangular `A_p`, the matrix `I-xA_p` is upper triangular with diagonal entries `1-x chi_j(p)`. Therefore

\[
\det(I-xA_p)
=
\prod_j(1-x\chi_j(p)),
\]

which proves (PL374-4).

For a finite set of primes `P`, multiplying these exact local identities yields (PL374-6). If an infinite product is used, its factorization into scalar Euler products is justified only in a common convergence region. For example, under a uniform bound on the local matrices, ordinary absolute convergence holds sufficiently far to the right. Any meromorphic continuation beyond that region requires an independent continuation theorem and cannot be inferred from the formal Euler product.

Thus a determinant built solely by multiplying the finite-dimensional local factors

\[
\det(I-\rho(p)p^{-s})^{-1}
\]

contains no determinant-level cross-prime matrix invariant beyond the finite list of scalar channels. The off-diagonal extension data disappears before primes are globally assembled.

## 5. The normal/unitary case collapses completely

Suppose every `A_p` is normal. A commuting family of normal matrices is simultaneously unitarily diagonalizable. Hence for one unitary `U`,

\[
U^*\rho(n)U
=
\operatorname{diag}
\bigl(\chi_1(n),\ldots,\chi_d(n)\bigr)
\qquad(n\ge1).
\tag{PL374-12}
\]

If each `A_p` is unitary then `|chi_j(p)|=1`, so the channels are ordinary unimodular completely multiplicative prime phases of exactly the Helson-type scalar kind that the Prime-Lattice falsification controls require us to distinguish from rational-prime-specific structure.

This rules out a particularly tempting escape from `PL-373`: replacing a scalar completely multiplicative witness by a finite-dimensional **unitary** completely multiplicative witness does not introduce a new joint prime geometry. It is exactly a finite orthogonal sum of scalar phase twists.

## 6. What the result does not kill

The non-normal case retains genuine extension data. For instance two commuting upper-triangular prime matrices can share nonzero nilpotent parts, and products may encode polynomial factors in the exponents `v_p(n)`. Such information can affect

\[
\|\rho(n)\|,
\qquad
\langle u,\rho(n)v\rangle,
\qquad
(zI-\rho(n))^{-1},
\]

and other non-spectral or non-normal observables even though it is absent from trace and determinant.

Accordingly this finding does **not** establish that every finite-dimensional matrix-valued construction is scalar in every sense. It establishes the narrower statement needed for the live research fork: if the proposed escape from scalar Venturini witnesses keeps exact complete multiplicativity and then returns to a scalar RH-facing object through ordinary trace or local determinant Euler factors, the matrix layer has added no irreducible cross-prime information.

Nor does the argument address infinite-dimensional operator representations. Simultaneous triangularization is a finite-dimensional theorem, and infinite-dimensional commutative operator algebras can carry much richer invariant-subspace and spectral behavior. Likewise a construction that deliberately breaks `rho(mn)=rho(m)rho(n)` can couple different prime directions in ways excluded here.

## 7. Adversarial controls

### Cross-channel cancellation is not being ruled out

A finite sum

\[
\sum_jL(\chi_j,s)
\]

can have zeros produced by cancellation between channels. The claim is not that the trace series has the divisor of one scalar channel, nor that every `chi_j` is Liouville-like. The claim is that this cancellation is finite scalar-channel cancellation; it does not use noncommuting or irreducible matrix geometry.

### Jordan data is real but trace/determinant-blind

For a non-normal commuting family, simultaneous triangularization does not diagonalize the matrices. Nilpotent upper-triangular terms can survive and can interact under products. Any argument that uses those terms through matrix coefficients, singular values, resolvent growth, or a target-relative positivity relation is outside this no-go theorem and must be audited separately.

### No analytic continuation is smuggled through the Euler product

The determinant identity is local and algebraic. The finite-prime product is exact. Infinite Euler products and Dirichlet-series rearrangements are invoked only in their actual convergence domains. Nothing in simultaneous triangularization supplies continuation into `Re(s)<=1`.

### Finite dimension is essential

The argument uses a finite common invariant flag. It does not classify commuting bounded operators on an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space and does not imply that an infinite-dimensional trace-class/Fredholm construction scalarizes in the same way.

## Prior-art and novelty assessment

The simultaneous-triangularization theorem is classical. The literature audit found no reason to treat the matrix reduction itself as new mathematics. The durable Prime-Lattice delta is the exact application to the current post-`PL-373` fork: a finite-dimensional completely multiplicative matrix witness is not yet a genuinely non-scalar escape when the final arithmetic observable is a trace Dirichlet series or a product of local determinants.

This is therefore a **derived narrowing result**, not a theorem-priority claim. It removes a natural but superficial enlargement of the scalar witness class while leaving precisely identified non-normal, infinite-dimensional, non-completely-multiplicative, and target-relative possibilities open.

## Research consequence

`PL-373` moved the live question outside bounded scalar completely multiplicative Venturini witnesses. The most immediate finite-dimensional matrix upgrade still does not buy the required new global structure if it preserves complete multiplicativity and reads the result through trace or determinant: the commutative prime semigroup forces a finite scalar filtration before those observables are formed.

A genuinely different next mechanism must therefore do at least one of the following:

- use non-normal extension data through an observable that does not factor through the diagonal characters;
- use an infinite-dimensional operator algebra where the finite common-flag argument fails;
- break exact complete multiplicativity so different prime directions can interact nontrivially;
- introduce a target-relative/global trace or positivity relation whose arithmetic content is not already a finite scalar combination/product.

The useful boundary is now explicit: **matrix-valued is not by itself non-scalar**. For the rational-prime lattice, finite-dimensional complete multiplicativity plus trace/determinant scalarization remains inside a finite collection of scalar prime channels rather than producing the missing global RH mechanism.
