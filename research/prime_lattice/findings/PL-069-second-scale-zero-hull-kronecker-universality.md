# PL-069 — The RH second-scale zero hull is a generic compact-group rotation, not new spectral rigidity

The translation-hull escape left open by `PL-067`--`PL-068` can be classified exactly, and the classification is negative for new RH rigidity.

```text

sum_omega |b_omega| < infinity.
```

Here the active frequencies are doubled nontrivial zero ordinates

```text
omega=2 gamma,
```

and multiplicity is absorbed into `b_omega`. Let

```text
Gamma=<Omega>_Z subset R
```

be the additive subgroup generated by the active frequencies, equipped with the discrete topology, and let

```text
K_Gamma=dual(Gamma)
```

be its compact Pontryagin dual.

Then the sup-norm translation hull

```text
X_F=closure_{||.||_infinity}{F(.+tau): tau in R}
```

is canonically topologically conjugate to the compact-group rotation on `K_Gamma`. Under this conjugacy, real translation is the dense one-parameter homomorphism

```text
iota(t)(gamma)=exp(i gamma t),
```

and the hull dynamics is minimal, uniquely ergodic with Haar measure, and has pure-point Koopman spectrum exactly `Gamma`.

For the residual non-annihilating probe pairs constructed in `PL-068`, every nontrivial zeta zero contributes a nonzero Fourier coefficient. Hence the abstract second-scale hull flow remembers the **integer frequency module generated by the zero ordinates**, but it does not remember individual zero multiplicities, amplitudes, or even which generating subset of that module was used. If one retains the canonical evaluation observable on the hull, those missing data reappear as its Fourier coefficients — exactly the zero coefficients already inserted by the completed explicit formula.

Therefore the bare translation hull gives a sharp dichotomy:


It supplies no additional mechanism that can force the frequencies to arise from zeros on `Re(s)=1/2`, and no rational-prime discriminator beyond the already-resolved zero data.

**Evidence/status:** `LITERATURE+EXACT-DERIVED + DECISIVE-NEGATIVE` for the route

## Exact identification of the hull with the dual frequency group

For each character

```text
chi in K_Gamma=Hom(Gamma,T),
```

define

```text
F_chi(t)=sum_(omega in Omega)
           b_omega chi(omega) exp(i omega t).
```

Absolute summability gives uniform convergence for every `chi`, and

```text
||F_chi-F_psi||_infinity
 <= sum_omega |b_omega| |chi(omega)-psi(omega)|.
```

Hence `chi -> F_chi` is continuous from the compact group `K_Gamma` into `C_b(R)` with the sup norm.

It is injective. If `F_chi=F_psi`, uniqueness of Bohr Fourier coefficients gives

```text
b_omega chi(omega)=b_omega psi(omega)
```

for every active frequency. Since `b_omega != 0` and `Omega` generates `Gamma`, the characters agree on all of `Gamma`.

Now define

```text
iota: R -> K_Gamma,

iota(t)(gamma)=exp(i gamma t).
```

The annihilator in `Gamma` of `closure(iota(R))` is trivial: if

```text
exp(i gamma t)=1 for every real t,
```

then `gamma=0`. Pontryagin duality therefore gives

```text
closure(iota(R))=K_Gamma.
```

Finally,

```text
F_iota(tau)(t)=F(t+tau).
```

Thus the image of `K_Gamma` under `chi -> F_chi` is precisely `X_F`. Compactness plus injectivity into the Hausdorff sup-norm function space makes this map a homeomorphism, and it intertwines translation with multiplication by `iota(tau)`.

No arithmetic input enters this argument beyond the already-specified frequencies and coefficients.

## The hull has only generic compact-rotation spectral structure

Because `iota(R)` is dense, the rotation on `K_Gamma` is minimal. Any probability measure invariant under `iota(R)` is invariant under its closure, hence under every translation of `K_Gamma`; the unique invariant probability measure is Haar measure.

The characters of `K_Gamma` are indexed by `Gamma`. For `gamma in Gamma`, put

```text
e_gamma(chi)=chi(gamma).
```

Then

```text
U_t e_gamma
 = exp(i gamma t) e_gamma.
```

The characters form an orthonormal basis of `L^2(K_Gamma,Haar)`. Therefore the Koopman representation is pure point and its eigenfrequency group is exactly `Gamma`.

This is classical compact-abelian rotation theory, the same general mechanism already used for the **prime-log** Kronecker flow in `PL-011`. The present frequency group is different: it is generated by zero ordinates that entered only after completed analytic continuation. The same dynamical theorem therefore does not create a bridge from prime-log geometry to zero localization; it merely diagonalizes a frequency set already supplied by the explicit formula.

## The abstract flow loses divisor data

The abstract conjugacy class of the hull rotation depends on the embedded additive group `Gamma`, not on the coefficients `b_omega`.

More strongly, different active frequency sets can generate the same group and hence have the same abstract hull flow. For example,

```text
F_1(t)=exp(i t)+exp(i sqrt(2) t),

F_2(t)=7 exp(i t)
       +3 exp(i sqrt(2) t)
       +exp(i(1+sqrt(2)) t)
```

both have frequency module

```text
Gamma=Z + sqrt(2) Z
```

and both hull flows are the same irrational two-torus rotation up to topological conjugacy, despite different Fourier support and amplitudes.

For the zeta signal this means that the unpointed hull/Koopman system cannot recover zero multiplicities or the detailed zero list from which `Gamma` was generated. It sees only their integer-relation closure.

This information loss matters for RH: a mechanism that is supposed to constrain the precise zeta divisor cannot rely only on a dynamical system that identifies many different divisors with the same frequency module.

## Adding the natural observable only re-encodes the explicit formula

The function-space realization of the hull comes with a natural evaluation observable

```text
E(h)=h(0).
```

Under the compact-group identification,

```text
E(chi)=F_chi(0)
      =sum_omega b_omega chi(omega).
```

Thus the Fourier coefficients of `E` on `K_Gamma` are exactly the coefficients `b_omega` of the original second-scale zero series. The orbit sampling identity is

```text
E(iota(t))=F(t).
```

So retaining the observable restores the detailed information that the abstract flow discarded, but it restores it by storing exactly the Fourier data from which the hull was constructed. Any invariant derived solely from this pointed observable/hull pair is therefore a functional of the already-continued explicit-formula zero signal; it is not an independent operator or positivity mechanism selecting `Re(s)=1/2`.

This is the relevant no-reencoding test from the line mandate: the hull is a mathematically clean representation of the second-scale signal, but it does not add a new constraint on that signal.
