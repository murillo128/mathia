## Claim

\[
\sigma_t(s)=s,
\qquad
\sigma_t(v_a)=a^{it}v_a.

For `0<=k<a`, define the affine residue range projection

\[
E_{k,a}:=s^k v_a v_a^* s^{*k}.

Then every `KMS_beta` state `phi` satisfies the state-independent identity

\[
\boxed{

Thus a signed scalar readout **cannot be manufactured on these canonical residue projections while retaining the KMS covariance law**. The freedom of the low-temperature KMS simplex for `beta>2` lives in other, off-diagonal/additive data; all KMS states agree on this residue diagonal.

Nica covariance turns Chinese-remainder refinement into an exact projection law. If `E_{k,a}` and `E_{l,b}` impose incompatible congruences modulo `gcd(a,b)`, their product is zero. If the congruences are compatible and `r` is the simultaneous residue modulo `lcm(a,b)`, then

\[
\boxed{
E_{k,a}E_{l,b}=E_{r,\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)}.
}
\]

For coprime `a,b`, therefore,

\[
\phi(E_{k,a}E_{l,b})
=(ab)^{-\beta}
=\phi(E_{k,a})\phi(E_{l,b}).
\]

Writing

\[
Q_a:=\sum_{k=0}^{a-1}E_{k,a},
\]

one has

\[
Q_aQ_b=Q_{\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)},
\qquad
\boxed{
\phi(Q_a)=a^{1-\beta}.
}
\]

Hence for every prime `p` and integer `r>=0`,

\[
\phi(Q_{p^r})=p^{-r(\beta-1)}.

For `beta>1`, the restriction of any KMS state to the commuting algebra generated by these divisibility projections therefore has independent prime valuations with the geometric tail law

\[
\boxed{
\Pr(N_p\ge r)=p^{-r(\beta-1)}.

Equivalently, with `q_p=p^{1-\beta}`,

\[
\Pr(N_p=r)=(1-q_p)q_p^r.

\[
R_F:=\prod_{p\in F}(1-Q_p).

Since the `Q_p` commute and are projections,

\[
\boxed{

For real `beta>2`, this converges to

Thus the most canonical signed-looking inclusion-exclusion operation on the affine residue diagonal is still a **positive projection probability** and its infinite-prime boundary is again the shifted zeta pole at `beta=2`. Meromorphically continuing `1/zeta(beta-1)` toward the shifted critical line `Re(beta)=3/2` would be precisely the scalar-continuation import already ruled out by `PL-199` and `PL-200`; the KMS projection law itself does not provide that continuation.

## 1. The KMS identity fixes every affine residue mass

Laca and Raeburn's natural dynamics assigns energy `log a` to the multiplicative isometry and zero energy to the additive isometry:

\[
\sigma_t(v_a)=a^{it}v_a,
\qquad
\sigma_t(s)=s.
\]

Thus

\[
\sigma_{i\beta}(v_a)=a^{-\beta}v_a.
\]

Let `phi` be a `KMS_beta` state. Since `v_a` is an isometry,

\[
\begin{aligned}
\phi(v_av_a^*)
&=\phi\!\left(v_a^*\sigma_{i\beta}(v_a)\right)\\
&=a^{-\beta}\phi(v_a^*v_a)\\
&=a^{-\beta}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Because `s^k` is fixed by the dynamics and is also an isometry,

\[
\begin{aligned}
\phi(E_{k,a})
&=\phi(s^k v_av_a^*s^{*k})\\
&=\phi(v_av_a^*s^{*k}s^k)\\
&=\phi(v_av_a^*)\\
&=a^{-\beta}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Nothing in this computation uses positivity except the conventional definition of a KMS state. Consequently, if `omega` is merely a normalized bounded linear functional satisfying the same KMS identity on these analytic generators, the same calculation gives

\[
\omega(E_{k,a})=a^{-\beta}.
\]

This is stronger than saying that the known KMS states happen to be positive on the residue diagonal. The covariance equation itself fixes the value before one chooses a state inside the KMS simplex.

For `beta>2`, Laca--Raeburn show that the KMS states are parametrized by probability measures on the circle, and their explicit formula on the spanning monomials `s^m v_a v_b^* s^{*n}` exhibits genuine state dependence off the diagonal. Setting `a=b` and `m=n` in that formula gives exactly `a^{-beta}`, consistent with the elementary derivation above. Hence the low-temperature symmetry breaking does not create any freedom on the projections `E_{k,a}`.

## 2. Chinese-remainder refinement is Nica projection multiplication

For a Nica-covariant isometric representation `w_x` of a quasi-lattice ordered semigroup, its range projections satisfy

\[
(w_xw_x^*)(w_yw_y^*)
=
\begin{cases}
 w_{x\vee y}w_{x\vee y}^*,&x\vee y<\infty,\\
 0,&x\vee y=\infty.
\end{cases}
\]

Laca--Raeburn prove that `N rt N^times` is quasi-lattice ordered inside `Q rt Q_+^*` and that its Toeplitz algebra is the universal Nica-covariant completion. For

\[
x=(k,a),\qquad y=(l,b),
\]

a common upper bound exists exactly when the congruences

\[
r\equiv k\pmod a,
\qquad
r\equiv l\pmod b
\]

are compatible, equivalently `k=l mod gcd(a,b)`. In that case the least upper bound has multiplicative coordinate `lcm(a,b)` and additive coordinate the least simultaneous residue `r`. Therefore

\[
E_{k,a}E_{l,b}=E_{r,\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)}.
\]

When `(a,b)=1`, the Chinese remainder theorem gives one `r mod ab` for every pair `(k,l)`, so the KMS expectation factors exactly:

\[
\phi(E_{k,a}E_{l,b})
=(ab)^{-\beta}
=a^{-\beta}b^{-\beta}.
\]

This is not a new arithmetic interaction. It is the classical CRT/Nica structure of the affine semigroup, now used as a matched control for the source-selection question.

## 3. The aggregate divisibility projections carry a product-geometric law

For fixed `a`, the projections

\[
E_{0,a},\ldots,E_{a-1,a}
\]

are mutually orthogonal: distinct residues modulo `a` have no common upper bound. Hence

\[
Q_a=\sum_{k=0}^{a-1}E_{k,a}
\]

is a projection. Its KMS mass is

\[
\phi(Q_a)
=\sum_{k=0}^{a-1}a^{-\beta}
=a^{1-\beta}.
\]

Positivity now gives the classical necessary lower-temperature bound immediately:

\[
a^{1-\beta}=\phi(Q_a)\le1
\]

for all `a`, so `beta>=1`. This is one elementary face of the established Laca--Raeburn theorem that no KMS states exist for `beta<1`.

The CRT multiplication also gives

\[
Q_aQ_b=Q_{\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)}.
\]

In particular, if `(a,b)=1`,

\[
\phi(Q_aQ_b)
=(ab)^{1-\beta}
=\phi(Q_a)\phi(Q_b).
\]

For a prime `p`, the projections form a decreasing chain

\[
Q_p\ge Q_{p^2}\ge\cdots,
\]

with

\[
\phi(Q_{p^r})=p^{-r(\beta-1)}.
\]

Thus, on the abelian algebra they generate, a KMS state induces for `beta>1` a geometric valuation variable `N_p` with

\[
\Pr(N_p\ge r)=p^{-r(\beta-1)}.
\]

Coprime factorization makes these valuation variables independent across distinct primes. This description is fully compatible with the adelic/Nica-spectrum parametrization developed by Laca and Neshveyev; no novelty is claimed for the existence of an adelic product description of the KMS system.

Two boundary values help calibrate the geometry. At `beta=1`, every `Q_{p^r}` has expectation `1`, so the KMS state kills the additive-boundary defect and the valuation tail is concentrated at infinity. At `beta=2`,

\[
\phi(Q_{p^r})=p^{-r},
\]

which is the familiar Haar valuation tail on `Z_p`. Neither point is `beta=3/2`, the affine shift of the Riemann critical line.

## 4. The all-prime sieve remains a positive pole-boundary observable

The residue-diagonal analogue of an inclusion-exclusion sieve is

\[
R_F=\prod_{p\in F}(1-Q_p).
\]

For finite `F`, this is a positive projection. Because the `Q_p` are independent under every KMS state,

\[
\phi(R_F)
=\prod_{p\in F}(1-p^{1-\beta}).
\]

For `beta>2`, ordinary Euler multiplication is legitimate and gives

\[
\lim_F\phi(R_F)
=\prod_p(1-p^{-(\beta-1)})
=\frac1{\zeta(\beta-1)}.
\]

For `1<=beta<=2`, the product tends to zero. No analytic continuation is used in either statement: the first is the absolutely convergent Euler product, and the second follows from divergence of the prime harmonic-type sum in the corresponding exponent range.

This gives an adversarial control against reading Möbius cancellation into the residue diagonal. The finite products have the formal inclusion-exclusion expansion

\[
R_F
=\sum_{d\mid \prod_{p\in F}p}\mu(d)Q_d,
\]

but the operator itself is positive and its expectation is a positive probability. The Möbius signs are bookkeeping for complements, not a source-forced indefinite form. Continuing the scalar `1/zeta(beta-1)` below `Re(beta)>2` does not turn `R_F` into a KMS observable with a continued expectation.

Thus the infinite-prime sieve reproduces the same shifted pole boundary already seen thermodynamically in `PL-199`, rather than creating a new continuation mechanism at `Re(beta)=3/2`.

## 6. Adversarial boundary conditions

### Gauge invariance alone is not enough

The conclusion must not be enlarged from `KMS-compatible` to merely `gauge-compatible`. The projections `E_{k,a}` are gauge fixed, so gauge invariance by itself does not determine a scalar functional on them. An arbitrary gauge-invariant signed functional on the diagonal can still be programmed. The rigidity comes from the KMS scaling identity.

### Off-diagonal KMS data remain nontrivial

For `beta>2`, distinct KMS states really do differ, and Laca--Raeburn's explicit formula shows that additive/off-diagonal monomials can retain the circle-measure parameter. This finding does not say that the entire affine KMS simplex is classical noise; it says only that **the canonical residue projection channel is state-independent and positive**.

### The unilateral integer model must not be conflated with the universal Toeplitz algebra

The concrete operators `S e_n=e_(n+1)` and `V_a e_n=e_(an)` used in `PL-199` derive the affine semigroup law, but they impose stronger residue-partition equalities than the universal Nica-Toeplitz algebra. In the Toeplitz algebra one has

\[
\sum_{k=0}^{a-1}E_{k,a}\le1,
\]

not equality in general. This defect is exactly why `phi(Q_a)=a^{1-beta}` can be strictly less than `1` for `beta>1`. Any argument that silently replaces the universal Toeplitz system by the unilateral integer representation would incorrectly eliminate the known low-temperature KMS states.

### Scalar continuation remains external

The identity

\[
\lim_F\phi(R_F)=1/\zeta(\beta-1)
\]

is an operator-state statement only for real `beta>2`, where the Euler product converges. Its meromorphic continuation is a scalar number-theoretic continuation. No KMS state at complex inverse temperature, no continued projection expectation, and no RH localization theorem follows from it.

### A target-relative or off-diagonal selector is still open

A future mechanism can evade this negative by using correlations involving `s`, a Nyman/Weil target, a completed trace formula, a nonpositive form with independently justified domain/continuation, or another relation that is not determined by the residue projections alone. Such a mechanism must explain why its signs are source-forced and survive the arbitrary-prime-phase control of `PL-200`.

## 7. Decisive audit tests

This finding should be withdrawn or materially narrowed if any of the following occurs:

1. the natural affine dynamics does not satisfy `sigma_t(v_a)=a^(it)v_a` and `sigma_t(s)=s`;
2. a `KMS_beta` state can have `phi(s^k v_a v_a^* s^{*k}) != a^(-beta)`;
3. Nica covariance fails to give the stated compatible-congruence intersection law for the range projections;
4. the projections `Q_a` fail to satisfy `phi(Q_a)=a^(1-beta)` or `Q_aQ_b=Q_lcm(a,b)`;
5. distinct KMS states at a fixed `beta` give different values on this residue diagonal;
6. an established theorem derives a sign-indefinite RH/Weil localization mechanism from these **same KMS residue projection values** without adding target coefficients, off-diagonal data, or an external meromorphic continuation.

Items 1--5 are exact consequences of the established affine-semigroup/KMS framework. Item 6 is the substantive novelty boundary relevant to this research line.
