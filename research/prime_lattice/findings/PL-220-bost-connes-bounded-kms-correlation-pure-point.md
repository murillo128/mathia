# PL-220 — Bost–Connes bounded KMS correlations are prime-gauge almost periodic with generic detailed balance

## Claim

The full bounded Bost–Connes `C*`-completion does not create a new continuous or zero-ordinate spectral carrier at the level of equilibrium time correlations. Once the prime-gauge action from `PL-219` is retained, every bounded finite-time correlation is the restriction of a continuous function on a compact prime torus, hence is Bohr almost periodic. \[
\log \mathbb Q_+^\times
=
\left\{
\sum_p k_p\log p:
 k\in\mathbb Z^{(\mathcal P)}
\right\}.

Let

\[
G=\widehat{\mathbb Q_+^\times}\cong\mathbb T^{\mathcal P}
\]

be the compact prime-gauge group, let `gamma` be its action on the rational Bost--Connes algebra `A_BC`, and let `phi` be any Bost--Connes KMS state. By `PL-219`, `phi` is `G`-invariant and the physical time evolution is the dense one-parameter subgroup

\[
\sigma_t=\gamma_{z(t)},
\qquad
z_p(t)=p^{it}.
\]

For arbitrary bounded `X,Y in A_BC`, the mixed two-point function

\[
C_{X,Y}(t)=\phi(X^*\sigma_t(Y))
\]

has an absolutely and uniformly convergent expansion

\[
\boxed{
C_{X,Y}(t)
=
\sum_{q\in\mathbb Q_+^\times}
 c_q(X,Y)\,q^{it},
\qquad
\sum_q |c_q(X,Y)|<\infty.

For `X=Y`,

\[
c_q(X,X)\ge0,
\qquad
\sum_q c_q(X,X)=\phi(X^*X),
\]

so the correlation spectral measure is a finite positive atomic measure supported at the frequencies `log q`. Each coefficient is itself a diagonal KMS scalar:

\[
\boxed{
c_q(X,Y)=\phi(X_q^*Y_q),
\qquad
X_q^*Y_q\in\mathcal D=C^*(\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z),
}
\]

where `X_q,Y_q` are the `q`-gauge spectral components.

For self-adjoint `X`, the KMS condition imposes the exact atomwise detailed-balance law

\[
\boxed{
c_{q^{-1}}(X,X)=q^{-\beta}c_q(X,X)}

for a `KMS_beta` state. This is a genuine source-forced relation between opposite signed lattice grades, but it is **generic KMS detailed balance**, not a rational-prime or Riemann-specific rigidity law. For every `beta>0` it can be symmetrized by the tautological reweighting

\[

More generally, for fixed bounded `X_1,...,X_k`,

\[

Thus the bounded correlation hierarchy does contain exact cross-channel constraints, but at this level they are compact-gauge charge conservation plus ordinary KMS detailed balance. They do not supply analytic continuation of `zeta`, a Riemann-zero divisor, or a Hilbert--Pólya spectrum.

## 1. Compact prime-gauge symmetry forces almost periodicity

`PL-219` identifies the physical Bost--Connes time flow as the restriction of the compact gauge action

\[
\gamma:G\curvearrowright\mathcal A_{BC},
\qquad
G\cong\mathbb T^{\mathcal P},
\]

to the one-parameter orbit `z(t)=(p^{it})_p`. Let `phi` be any KMS state. Since every KMS state is time invariant and the physical orbit is dense in `G`, `PL-219` proves

\[
\phi\circ\gamma_z=\phi
\qquad(z\in G).
\]

Take bounded `X_1,...,X_k`. Define on the compact group `G^k`

\[
F(z_1,\ldots,z_k)
=
\phi\!\left(
\gamma_{z_1}(X_1)\cdots\gamma_{z_k}(X_k)
\right).
\]

The action is point-norm continuous, multiplication is continuous, and `phi` is bounded, so `F` is continuous on the compact group `G^k`. Characters of `G` are indexed by

\[
\widehat G\cong\mathbb Q_+^\times
\cong\mathbb Z^{(\mathcal P)}.
\]

Finite linear combinations of characters are uniformly dense in `C(G^k)`. Therefore `F` is a uniform limit of finite character polynomials. Restricting along

\[
(t_1,\ldots,t_k)
\longmapsto
(z(t_1),\ldots,z(t_k))
\]

shows that the physical correlation is a uniform limit of finite exponential sums with coordinate frequencies `log q`. Hence it is Bohr almost periodic.

This conclusion uses compactness of the gauge completion, not the analytic continuation of any Dirichlet series. No Euler product is being evaluated outside `Re(s)>1`.

## 2. Gauge invariance enforces exact neutrality of multi-time Fourier modes

There is an additional constraint. For any common `w in G`, gauge invariance of `phi` gives

\[
F(z_1w,\ldots,z_kw)
=
F(z_1,\ldots,z_k).
\]

A character of `G^k` indexed by `(q_1,...,q_k)` transforms under this diagonal action by

\[
w(q_1\cdots q_k).
\]

Consequently a nonzero Fourier coefficient must satisfy

\[
q_1\cdots q_k=1.
\]

In exponent coordinates this is simply

\[
\sum_{j=1}^k
\bigl(v_p(q_j)\bigr)_p
=0,
\]

and after applying the logarithmic energy,

\[
\sum_{j=1}^k\log q_j=0.
\]

Thus bounded equilibrium multi-time data does carry an exact prime-lattice conservation law. The adversarial point is that this is **charge neutrality for a compact abelian gauge action**. It follows before any Riemann-specific arithmetic enters, and the same statement holds for a generic free multiplicative system with rationally independent generator energies.

## 3. Two-point functions have an absolutely summable pure-point spectrum

The two-point case is stronger than mere almost periodicity. Because `phi` is `G`-invariant, its GNS triple `(pi,H_phi,Omega)` carries a unitary representation `U_z` of `G` satisfying

\[
U_z\,\pi(a)\Omega
=
\pi(\gamma_z(a))\Omega,
\qquad
U_z\Omega=\Omega.
\]

A unitary representation of a compact abelian group decomposes as a Hilbert direct sum of its character subspaces. Let `P_q` be the orthogonal projection onto the character `q in Q_+^times`, so

\[
U_z P_q=z(q)P_q.
\]

For

\[
\xi_X=\pi(X)\Omega,
\qquad
\xi_Y=\pi(Y)\Omega,
\]

write

\[
\xi_X=\bigoplus_q P_q\xi_X,
\qquad
\xi_Y=\bigoplus_q P_q\xi_Y.
\]

On the physical subgroup,

\[
U_{z(t)}P_q=q^{it}P_q.
\]

Orthogonality therefore gives

\[
C_{X,Y}(t)
=
\langle \xi_X,U_{z(t)}\xi_Y\rangle
=
\sum_q
\langle P_q\xi_X,P_q\xi_Y\rangle q^{it}.
\]

The coefficients satisfy

\[
\sum_q
\left|
\langle P_q\xi_X,P_q\xi_Y\rangle
\right|
\le
\left(\sum_q\|P_q\xi_X\|^2\right)^{1/2}
\left(\sum_q\|P_q\xi_Y\|^2\right)^{1/2}
<\infty
\]

by Cauchy--Schwarz. Hence the Fourier series converges absolutely and uniformly in real time.

For `X=Y`, the coefficient

\[
c_q(X,X)=\|P_q\xi_X\|^2
\]

is nonnegative and the total mass is

\[
\sum_q c_q(X,X)=\|\xi_X\|^2=\phi(X^*X).
\]

So the ordinary bounded autocorrelation has a finite positive pure-point spectral measure. Its possible frequencies are fixed **a priori** by the signed exponent lattice; they are not the ordinates of the Riemann zeros.

This reconciles rather than contradicts `PL-213`. A countable pure-point set `log Q_+^times` is dense in `R`, so the closure of the spectrum can be all of `R` while individual bounded correlation vectors still have atomic spectral measures. The type-`III_1` ratio-set statement of `PL-213` is a different, coarser von Neumann-algebra invariant.

## 4. Every two-point Fourier coefficient is already diagonal

For `q in Q_+^times`, define the compact-gauge Fourier projection

\[
E_q(X)
=
\int_G \overline{z(q)}\,\gamma_z(X)\,dz.
\]

Then

\[
\gamma_z(E_q(X))=z(q)E_q(X),
\]

and in the GNS representation

\[
P_q\xi_X=\pi(E_q(X))\Omega.
\]

Therefore

\[
c_q(X,Y)
=
\phi(E_q(X)^*E_q(Y)).
\]

The product has zero total gauge degree:

\[
\gamma_z(E_q(X)^*E_q(Y))
=E_q(X)^*E_q(Y).
\]

By the fixed-point theorem proved in `PL-219`,

\[
\mathcal A_{BC}^{G}=\mathcal D=C^*(\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z).
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
E_q(X)^*E_q(Y)\in\mathcal D

\]

for every `q`. The full two-point frequency profile of an arbitrary bounded noncommuting observable is thus a sequence of **diagonal scalar KMS evaluations** indexed by signed rational scales.

This is the precise completion of the gap left in `PL-214` and `PL-219`. The finite algebraic core gave finite trigonometric polynomials; the full `C*`-completion can indeed have infinitely many grades, but the completion changes `finite atomic` to `countable absolutely summable atomic`, not to a new continuous spectral channel.

The result does **not** say that the whole coefficient sequence is arbitrarily programmable inside the Bost--Connes algebra. Joint constraints among the diagonal coefficients may exist. It says only that any surviving arithmetic content of the bounded two-point process must reside in those weights, not in a new set of frequencies or a hidden continuous component.

## 5. KMS gives a generic atomwise detailed-balance law

Now let `phi=phi_beta` be a `KMS_beta` state and let `X=X^*`. Write

\[
X_q=E_q(X).
\]

Self-adjointness gives

\[
X_{q^{-1}}=X_q^*.
\]

The time evolution acts on a spectral component by

\[
\sigma_t(X_q)=q^{it}X_q,
\]

so it is entire analytic and

\[
\sigma_{i\beta}(X_q^*)=q^{\beta}X_q^*.
\]

Apply the KMS identity to `a=X_q^*`, `b=X_q`:

\[
\phi(X_q^*X_q)
=
\phi\!\left(X_q\sigma_{i\beta}(X_q^*)\right)
=q^{\beta}\phi(X_qX_q^*).
\]

Since

\[
c_q(X,X)=\phi(X_q^*X_q),
\qquad
c_{q^{-1}}(X,X)=\phi(X_qX_q^*),
\]

we obtain

\[
\boxed{
c_{q^{-1}}(X,X)=q^{-\beta}c_q(X,X).}
\]

This is the atomic version of the standard KMS spectral detailed-balance relation. It is mathematically real cross-grade structure, but it does not distinguish the ordinary primes. Any KMS system with the same energy labels obeys the same Boltzmann reciprocity.

It also does not select the Riemann critical half. For every `beta`, define

\[
s_q=q^{-\beta/2}c_q(X,X).
\]

Then

\[
s_{q^{-1}}=s_q.
\]

Thus every temperature admits the same canonical reflection-symmetric normalization. At `beta=1/2` the exponent happens to be `1/4`; no new algebraic or positivity condition appears.

The detailed-balance identity also explains the KMS strip without importing zeta continuation. Pairing `q` with `q^{-1}` shows that the atomic series is absolutely controlled throughout the ordinary KMS strip by the endpoint `ell^1` mass. The strip is thermodynamic KMS analyticity, not analytic continuation of the Euler product.

## 6. Matched controls and why this is not an RH mechanism

Three controls are decisive.

**Compact-gauge control.** The entire almost-periodic conclusion follows from a continuous action of a compact abelian group and an invariant state. It is not special to the Bost--Connes arithmetic algebra. Classical compact-group spectral-subspace theory and ordinary pure-point dynamics already supply the abstract mechanism.

**Generic-energy control.** Replace the prime energies `log p` by any countable set of rationally independent positive energies `lambda_j` and let a compact torus rotate the corresponding free multiplicative grades. The same proof gives almost-periodic bounded correlations supported on the integer module generated by the `lambda_j`, the same neutrality law, and the same KMS Boltzmann reciprocity. The ordinary rational primes therefore fail the required source-discrimination test at this structural level.

**Frequency-versus-weight control.** The spectral carrier is fixed to `log Q_+^times`. Riemann-zero ordinates are not generated as its eigenfrequencies. Any proposal that extracts zeta-zero information from these correlations must prove a theorem about the **weights or a further source-forced global functional of them**. Merely observing dense signed prime-log frequencies, almost periodicity, KMS reflection, or positive atomicity does not provide that theorem.

These controls do not prove that every admissible weight sequence occurs in the rational Bost--Connes system. Such a programmability theorem would be stronger and is not claimed here.

The finding would need correction or narrowing if any of the following failed:
