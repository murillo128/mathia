# PL-272 — Positive Weil source and projective coherence do not imply matrix positivity

**Evidence:** `LITERATURE+DERIVED`

**Status:** `DECISIVE-NEGATIVE + EXACT-TWO-MODE-CONTROL + PRIOR-ART-ADJACENT`

## Claim

`PL-271` shows that the full finite-Weil source tower is projectively identifiable: apart from the intrinsically invisible zero-frequency atom, all Galerkin bands together recover the source measure. That injectivity does **not** upgrade the source map to an order embedding. In fact the obstruction already occurs for one positive source atom and persists coherently through every band.

Let `Q_N[mu]` be Groskin's level-`N` source matrix for a finite signed measure `mu` on `[0,1]`. For every `N>=1`, take the positive measure

\[
\mu=\delta_{1/2}.
\]

On the first two even Galerkin modes, with coordinates `v=(v_0,v_1)`, Groskin's normalization gives

\[
T_v(t)=v_0+\sqrt2\,v_1\cos(2\pi t).
\]

His exact source calculus says

\[
\langle v,Q_N[\delta_\omega]v\rangle
=K_v(\omega),
\qquad
K_v(\omega)=2\int_0^\omega T_v(t)T_v(\omega-t)\,dt.
\]

At `omega=1/2`, `cos(2 pi(1/2-t))=-cos(2 pi t)`, so the cross terms cancel and

\[
\boxed{
K_v(1/2)=v_0^2-v_1^2.
}
\]

Hence the compression of `Q_N[delta_{1/2}]` to the span of the constant and first cosine modes is exactly

\[
\boxed{
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix}.
}
\]

Thus a **positive source atom maps to an indefinite quadratic form**. The same two-dimensional compression sits inside every level `N>=1`, so passing from one finite quotient to the complete projective tower does not repair the sign failure. The full source tower can be injective while remaining non-positive as a map from the cone of positive measures to the cone of positive-semidefinite matrices.

This is directly compatible with the rational-prime source geometry. If `q=p^a` is any prime power and the prime cutoff is chosen as `c=q^2`, then its normalized source frequency is

\[
\omega_q=1-\frac{\log q}{\log c}=\frac12.
\]

The actual zeta prime source carries mass `-Lambda(q)/sqrt(q)` at this point, so this one prime-power event contributes the opposite signature

\[
\frac{\Lambda(q)}{\sqrt q}
\begin{pmatrix}-1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}
\]

on the same two modes. Therefore even an individual canonical prime-power event is not a positive-semidefinite matrix contribution. Any proof of Weil positivity by decomposing the positive von-Mangoldt source into positive atomic matrix contributions is impossible in this source calculus.

The obstruction is not a single finely tuned cutoff. For the pure first-cosine mode, Groskin's formula gives

\[
K_{(0,1)}(\omega)
=2\omega\cos(2\pi\omega)+\frac{\sin(2\pi\omega)}{\pi},
\]

which equals `-1` at `omega=1/2` and is therefore negative on an open neighborhood of `1/2`, while the constant mode has `K_{(1,0)}(omega)=2 omega>0` for every `omega>0`. The source-to-form map is genuinely sign-indefinite around the mid-frequency region, not only at one algebraic coincidence.

## 1. Exact two-mode derivation

Groskin's Lemma 2.3 attaches to a positive atomic source `alpha delta_omega`, `alpha>0`, the quadratic value

\[
\langle v,Q_{\alpha,\omega}v\rangle=\alpha K_v(\omega).
\]

For `N>=1`, restrict to the two-dimensional even subspace generated by the constant mode and `cos(2 pi t)`. At `omega=1/2`,

\[
T_v(1/2-t)=v_0-\sqrt2\,v_1\cos(2\pi t),
\]

and therefore

\[
\begin{aligned}
K_v(1/2)
&=2\int_0^{1/2}
(v_0+\sqrt2v_1\cos 2\pi t)
(v_0-\sqrt2v_1\cos 2\pi t)\,dt\\
&=2\left(\frac12v_0^2
-2v_1^2\int_0^{1/2}\cos^2(2\pi t)\,dt\right)\\
&=v_0^2-v_1^2.
\end{aligned}
\]

No zero information, analytic continuation, asymptotic approximation, or numerical spectral calculation enters this identity.

The conclusion is stronger than merely saying that the prime term has an overall negative sign in the explicit formula. Multiplying an indefinite matrix by `-1` leaves it indefinite. The failure is internal to the oscillatory sine/divided-difference transfer from source frequency to Galerkin form.

## 2. Consequence for the post-PL-271 source-positivity route

`PL-270` showed that one fixed band sees only a finite-dimensional quotient of the positive prime source, while `PL-271` removed the corresponding all-band ambiguity: the complete projective family recovers a finite signed source modulo `delta_0`. A natural surviving hope was that **positive source + coherent all-band recovery** might carry an order/Herglotz structure strong enough to force Weil positivity.

The atom above is a matched control against that hope. The family

\[
\bigl(Q_N[\delta_{1/2}]\bigr)_{N\ge1}
\]

is generated by one fixed positive source and is perfectly coherent under increasing Galerkin bands. It is also identifiable by the full tower, yet every level contains the same positive and negative two-mode directions. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\text{source positivity + projective coherence + exact identifiability}
\not\Longrightarrow
\text{Galerkin positivity}.
}
\]

In operator-language terms, the linear source map is not a positive map of ordered spaces; consequently it cannot be made completely positive merely by remembering all finite compressions. Likewise, there is no representation of this *same* source map of the form `Q_N[mu]=int X_N(omega)^*X_N(omega) dmu(omega)` with a positive matrix density valid for all positive `mu`, because `mu=delta_{1/2}` is an explicit counterexample.

This does **not** rule out zeta-specific positivity mechanisms. The completed Weil form also contains the pole and archimedean terms, and the canonical rational-prime source has highly constrained support and weights across both band `N` and cutoff `c`. A surviving mechanism may use cancellations among different source atoms, completed/global identities, cross-cutoff arithmetic laws, or a transformed object whose positivity is not the positivity of `Q_N[mu]` itself. What is closed is the simpler cone-preservation route: positivity of the von-Mangoldt masses cannot be pushed through Groskin's source map atom-by-atom or by projective completion alone.

## 3. Prior-art and novelty audit

The load-bearing source identity is not new: it is exactly Groskin's Lemma 2.3, and his Corollary 2.4 supplies the finite quotient used in `PL-270` and `PL-271`. The formula for the pure cosine mode is also already implicit in the kernel basis displayed in the proof of that corollary.

The sign-indefinite nature of the prime contribution is additionally close to explicit prior art. Breno Wilson de Andrade Silva's June 2026 Loewner note studies the same localized prime block and proves a parity-dependent sign law: a distinguished even vector receives termwise non-positive prime contributions, whereas the odd vector has per-prime contributions of mixed sign and positivity, when it occurs, is a competition among primes. That paper is therefore strong prior-art evidence against treating the arithmetic source as an automatically positive operator measure.

The exact `omega=1/2` two-mode signature above was derived independently as the minimal control needed for the current line. The audit did not establish that this particular `diag(1,-1)` observation is absent from the literature, so no novelty is claimed. Its durable role is narrower: it decisively separates **projective source identifiability** from **order preservation** at the precise frontier left by `PL-271`.

An adversarial escape would have to use a property not shared by arbitrary positive source measures and not reducible to the cone structure of the von-Mangoldt masses. Merely replacing `delta_{1/2}` by the complete zeta source is not such an explanation; one must identify the extra rational-prime/global relation and prove that it changes the sign mechanism.
