# PL-235 — Finite arithmetic coloring does not rescue prime-symmetric normal equilibrium

## Claim

`PL-233` showed that exact permutation symmetry of **all** prime coordinates collapses every normal state on the finite-support integer Hilbert space to the vacuum. A natural repair after `PL-234` is to break that symmetry only by a fixed amount of genuine arithmetic information — for example, keep the residue class `p mod m` or the Frobenius conjugacy class of `p` in one fixed finite Galois extension — and allow permutations only among primes carrying the same arithmetic label.

That repair still fails at the level of normal equilibrium, for a stronger orbit reason that does not depend on additivity, diagonality, or the detailed Hamiltonian.

Let the rational primes be decomposed as

\[
\mathcal P=E\sqcup\bigsqcup_{j\in J}C_j,
\]

where `E` is any exceptional set and each `C_j` is an infinite block. Let

\[
G=\bigoplus_{j\in J}S_{\rm fin}(C_j)
\]

act on the exponent lattice by finite permutations inside each block while fixing `E`, and let `U_g` be the induced unitary action on

\[
\mathcal H_{\rm int}
=\ell^2\!\left(\mathbb N_0^{(\mathcal P)}\right)
\cong\ell^2(\mathbb N).
\]

If `T>=0` is trace class and

\[
U_gTU_g^*=T\qquad(g\in G),
\]

then

\[
\boxed{
T=P_E T P_E,
}
\]

where `P_E` projects onto the closed span of exponent vectors supported entirely in `E`. Equivalently, every basis state whose support meets even one infinite color block is annihilated by `T`.

Consequently, if at least one infinite block is present, **no self-adjoint Hamiltonian that is strongly invariant under these within-color permutations can have a trace-class finite-temperature Gibbs operator**. Indeed `e^{-\beta H}` would be positive, trace class and `G`-invariant, hence would annihilate all states using a prime in an infinite block, while a heat exponential of a self-adjoint operator is injective.

Two canonical arithmetic applications are immediate.

For a fixed modulus `m>=2`, take

\[
E=\{p:p\mid m\},
\qquad
C_a=\{p\nmid m:p\equiv a\pmod m\},
\quad a\in(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^*.
\]

Dirichlet's theorem makes every `C_a` infinite. Therefore any normal state invariant under arbitrary relabelling of primes **within each fixed residue class** is supported only on integers generated by the finitely many prime divisors of `m`; and no fully defined self-adjoint Hamiltonian with that symmetry has a finite-temperature Gibbs trace on the full integer Hilbert space.

For a fixed finite Galois extension `L/\mathbb Q`, let `E` be the finite ramified set and color every unramified prime by its Frobenius conjugacy class. Chebotarev gives positive density, hence infinitude, for every conjugacy-class block. The same conclusion holds: a normal state invariant under arbitrary relabelling inside each Frobenius class can see only the finitely ramified prime sector, and no invariant finite-temperature Gibbs density exists on the full exponent lattice.

Thus a **fixed finite arithmetic label is not enough symmetry breaking**. It can distinguish rational primes from an unlabeled generic free monoid, as `PL-114` and `PL-115` already show, but if the subsequent operator/state construction forgets the individual primes inside each infinite label class, normality forces essentially all of the infinite-prime sector to disappear.

**Evidence/status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + LITERATURE-DELIMITED + DECISIVE-NEGATIVE + PRIOR-ART-AUDITED + NO-NOVELTY-CLAIM`.

The orbit/trace lemma is elementary and is recorded only as a line-specific strengthening/application of `PL-233`. Dirichlet distribution in reduced residue classes, Chebotarev density, permutation-invariant states, and exchangeability are classical prior art. No novelty is claimed for any of those ingredients.

## 1. Infinite within-color orbits are enough

Write the canonical basis as `e_alpha`, with

\[
\alpha=(\alpha_p)_p\in\mathbb N_0^{(\mathcal P)}
\]

of finite support. Suppose that `supp(alpha)` meets one infinite block `C_j`; choose `p in C_j` with `alpha_p>0`. Since the support is finite while `C_j` is infinite, there are infinitely many primes

\[
q\in C_j\setminus\operatorname{supp}(\alpha).
\]

For each such `q`, let `g_q` exchange `p` and `q` inside `C_j` and fix all other coordinates. The exponent vectors `g_q alpha` are pairwise distinct. Therefore

\[
|G\cdot\alpha|=\infty
\]

for every finite-support configuration that uses at least one coordinate from an infinite color block.

This is the only group-theoretic input. Full transitivity on the whole prime set, used in the statement of `PL-233`, is not load-bearing. What matters for a positive normal density is whether the relevant basis configuration belongs to an infinite orbit.

## 2. Positive trace class kills every infinite orbit

Let `T>=0` be trace class and `G`-invariant. Set

\[
d_\alpha=\langle e_\alpha,Te_\alpha\rangle\ge0.
\]

Invariance gives

\[
d_{g\alpha}=d_\alpha
\qquad(g\in G).
\]

For a positive trace-class operator,

\[
\operatorname{Tr}T
=\sum_\gamma d_\gamma<\infty.
\]

If `alpha` has an infinite orbit and `d_alpha>0`, that orbit contributes the same positive number infinitely often, contradicting trace finiteness. Hence

\[
d_\alpha=0
\]

whenever `supp(alpha)` meets an infinite block.

Positivity converts zero diagonal weight into complete annihilation:

\[
d_\alpha
=\|T^{1/2}e_\alpha\|^2=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
Te_\alpha=0.
\]

Thus `T` annihilates the closed span of all basis vectors using any prime outside `E`, and therefore

\[
T=P_E T P_E.
\]

Notice that the theorem does **not** require `E` to be finite. The finite-arithmetic applications below happen to have finite exceptional sets. Nor does it require finitely many color blocks: any family of infinite blocks is covered.

The diagonal probability statement is the same. A probability measure on finite-support exponent vectors that is invariant under within-block permutations assigns zero mass to every configuration whose orbit is infinite; it is therefore supported on the coordinates in `E`.

## 3. Gibbs repair is impossible as soon as one infinite color block remains

Let `H` be self-adjoint on `H_int`, with its domain preserved by `U_g` and

\[
U_gH=HU_g
\]

in the strong operator sense. Functional calculus gives

\[
U_ge^{-\beta H}U_g^*=e^{-\beta H}
\]

for every `beta>0` for which the heat operator is bounded. If `e^{-\beta H}` were trace class, Section 2 would force it to annihilate every basis vector whose support meets an infinite block.

But for a self-adjoint operator,

\[
\ker(e^{-\beta H})=\{0\}.
\]

The scalar spectral multiplier `e^{-beta x}` is strictly positive for every finite real spectral value. Therefore a trace-class heat operator cannot coexist with even one infinite color block on the full integer Hilbert space.

This is stronger than saying that a block contains infinitely degenerate one-particle energies. The Hamiltonian may be non-diagonal, may mix different occupation sectors, and may contain arbitrary many-body interactions. Exact within-color symmetry plus normal finite-temperature equilibrium is already incompatible with the finite-support representation.

## 4. Fixed congruence coloring still leaves infinite symmetry blocks

Fix `m>=2`. The rational-prime label

\[
p\longmapsto p\bmod m
\]

is genuinely arithmetic and was already audited in `PL-114`. For primes not dividing `m`, the label lies in the finite group

\[
G_m=(\mathbb Z/m\mathbb Z)^*.
\]

If one keeps only this label and declares primes with the same residue interchangeable, the symmetry blocks are

\[
C_a=\{p:p\equiv a\pmod m\},
\qquad a\in G_m.
\]

Dirichlet's theorem on primes in arithmetic progressions says that every reduced class contains infinitely many primes. Hence every nonexceptional prime belongs to an infinite orbit under the color-preserving permutation group.

The classification above gives

\[
T=P_{E_m}TP_{E_m},
\qquad
E_m=\{p:p\mid m\}.
\]

The surviving subspace is the occupation space generated by only the finitely many prime divisors of `m`. In particular, a normal color-symmetric state does not retain the infinitely many Dirichlet-character prime channels that appear analytically in `PL-114`; it has quotiented much more aggressively, all the way down to the exceptional finite-prime sector.

This separates two logically different uses of congruence data. A **congruence observable** may still retain nontrivial scalar Dirichlet-`L` information, as `PL-114` records. A **state or Hamiltonian symmetric under arbitrary relabelling within congruence classes** cannot retain the individual infinite-prime sector normally. The latter is the route closed here.

## 5. Fixed Frobenius coloring behaves the same way

Let `L/Q` be a fixed finite Galois extension with group `G_L`, and let `S` be the finite set of ramified rational primes. For an unramified prime, the canonical local label is the Frobenius conjugacy class

\[
C_p\subset G_L.
\]

`PL-115` already showed that fixed pair observables depending only on these classes scalarize into finitely many Artin-character channels. The present question is different: could one use the fixed Galois label to break full prime exchangeability enough to support a nontrivial normal symmetric equilibrium?

No. Chebotarev's density theorem gives, for every conjugacy class `C`, asymptotic density

\[
\frac{|C|}{|G_L|}>0
\]

for unramified primes with `C_p=C`. In particular each Frobenius color block is infinite. Taking

\[
E=S
\]

in the orbit theorem yields

\[
T=P_S T P_S
\]

for every positive trace-class state operator invariant under within-Frobenius-class permutations. Only the finitely ramified prime sector survives.

Again, this does not say that Frobenius data are arithmetically trivial. Artin `L`-functions and compatible Galois representations contain deep information. The no-go concerns the act of retaining only one **fixed finite** Frobenius color and then declaring all primes of the same color interchangeable in the normal state geometry. That symmetry is still infinitely transitive inside each color and therefore too large.

## 6. Matched control and prior-art audit

The abstract theorem is generic. Replace rational primes by any countably infinite coordinate set, partition it into infinite blocks, and the same orbit/trace proof holds. This universality is the falsification control: the normal-state collapse is a representation-theoretic consequence of infinite block symmetry, not a signature of zeta or RH.

The arithmetic applications are nevertheless useful because they test two canonical attempts to add source information before equilibrium. Fixed congruence labels and fixed Frobenius labels are not arbitrary decorations: they are standard rational-prime data, and `PL-114`/`PL-115` already establish their analytic character decompositions. Dirichlet's theorem and Chebotarev then show that each fixed label still leaves infinitely many coordinates interchangeable.

A bounded literature audit found only classical ingredients, exactly as expected. Dirichlet's theorem gives infinitude of primes in every reduced residue class. Chebotarev gives positive density `|C|/|G|` for each Frobenius conjugacy class in a fixed finite Galois extension. Størmer/de Finetti theory, already used in `PL-234`, supplies the broader context that infinite permutation symmetry strongly restricts states after quasi-local completion. None of this supports a novelty claim for the orbit lemma or its finite-color specialization.

The durable mathematical delta relative to `PL-233`--`PL-234` is the route classification: **one cannot evade the all-prime symmetry obstruction merely by inserting a fixed finite arithmetic coloring and then symmetrizing within colors**. The fixed labels distinguish arithmetic channels at the observable level, but they do not make the normal equilibrium representation fine enough to preserve the infinite prime geometry.

## 7. Exact boundaries and decisive tests

The conclusion uses **exact permutation invariance inside an infinite block**. A construction that retains the actual prime value `p`, the exact norm `log p`, prime ordering/gaps, or another injective label breaks these block symmetries and is outside the theorem.

A scale-dependent coloring is also outside the fixed statement. If `m=m(X)` grows, or a tower of Galois extensions is refined with the observation scale, the equivalence classes may shrink and no single fixed infinite-block symmetry need remain. Such a route must still show that the growing label complexity produces a canonical zero-sensitive limit rather than merely approaching the full prime labels by hand.

Finite blocks are not obstructed by the orbit argument. Symmetry among finitely many primes can coexist with normal Gibbs states. What fails is any exact symmetry block containing infinitely many prime coordinates.

The result is specific to **normal trace-class states on the finite-support integer Hilbert space** and their Gibbs heat operators. Nonnormal KMS states or completed representations can evade the trace argument, just as `PL-234` explains for full exchangeability. In a completion, however, the state must still justify how the retained finite arithmetic labels and any additional nonlocal relations recover the actual rational-prime norm data rather than a generic colored exchangeable system.

The theorem should be withdrawn or narrowed if any of the following fails: (1) a nonexceptional finite-support configuration does not have an infinite orbit under within-color permutations; (2) invariant positive trace class does not force equal nonnegative diagonal weights along that orbit; (3) trace finiteness does not force those weights to zero; (4) positivity does not force annihilation of the corresponding basis vectors; (5) a self-adjoint heat exponential can have nontrivial kernel; (6) a reduced residue class can be finite; or (7) a Frobenius conjugacy class occurring in a fixed finite Galois extension can have only finitely many unramified rational primes. The first five are elementary operator facts; the last two are excluded by Dirichlet and Chebotarev.

## Consequence for the research line

`PL-232`--`PL-234` show that bare full prime exchangeability is too large both before and after the ordinary quasi-local completion. A tempting next repair is to preserve a small amount of genuine arithmetic information while retaining a large symmetry: color primes by a fixed congruence or fixed Frobenius datum and only permute within colors.

That repair is now closed for normal equilibrium on the integer occupation representation. **Any fixed finite arithmetic coloring leaves infinite color classes, and exact relabelling symmetry inside even one such class is incompatible with a trace-class Gibbs operator.** At the state level, all sectors using those classes are annihilated.

A surviving source-before-equilibrium mechanism must therefore retain finer information than a fixed finite prime color, or replace block exchangeability by a genuinely nonlocal arithmetic relation. Scale-growing conductors/Galois data, exact norm weights, additive relations, reciprocity constraints, target-relative Nyman/Weil observables, or nonnormal arithmetic completions remain outside this no-go. What is no longer live is the idea that a finite arithmetic label plus within-label prime symmetry can preserve enough of the infinite exponent lattice to support a normal RH-sensitive equilibrium.
