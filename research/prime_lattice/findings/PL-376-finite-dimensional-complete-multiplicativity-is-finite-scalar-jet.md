# PL-376 — Every finite-dimensional completely multiplicative representation is a finite scalar jet

**Evidence:** `EXACT-DERIVED + STANDARD-COMMUTATIVE-ARTIN-ALGEBRA + FINITE-JET-PRIOR-ART + DECISIVE-NARROWING`.

**Parent finding:** `PL-375`.

**Related findings:** `PL-201`, `PL-202`, `PL-369`, `PL-373`, `PL-374`, `PL-375`.

## Claim

Let

\[
\rho:\mathbb N_{>0}\longrightarrow M_d(\mathbb C),
\qquad
\rho(1)=I,
\qquad
\rho(mn)=\rho(m)\rho(n).
\tag{PL376-1}
\]

No invertibility hypothesis is imposed on the matrices `rho(n)`. Let `A` be the unital subalgebra of `M_d(C)` generated by the image of `rho`. Since multiplication of positive integers is commutative,

\[
\rho(m)\rho(n)=\rho(n)\rho(m),
\]

so `A` is a finite-dimensional commutative `C`-algebra.

Every such algebra is Artinian and decomposes as a finite product of local Artinian algebras,

\[
A\simeq A_1\times\cdots\times A_r.
\tag{PL376-2}
\]

Write `e_j` for the corresponding central idempotents. Because the base field is algebraically closed, the residue field of each local factor is `C`; hence there is a quotient character

\[
\lambda_j:A_j\to\mathbb C,
\]

with nilpotent maximal ideal

\[
\mathfrak m_j=\ker\lambda_j,
\qquad
\mathfrak m_j^{N_j}=0
\tag{PL376-3}
\]

for some finite nilpotence index `N_j`.

Define

\[
\chi_j(n)=\lambda_j(e_j\rho(n)).
\tag{PL376-4}
\]

Then every `chi_j` is a scalar completely multiplicative arithmetic function. It is important that it is allowed to vanish at primes.

Choose a vector-space basis

\[
x_{j1},\ldots,x_{jr_j}
\]

of `m_j`. For every prime `p` write uniquely

\[
e_j\rho(p)
=
\chi_j(p)e_j+
\sum_{a=1}^{r_j} c_{ja}(p)x_{ja}.
\tag{PL376-5}
\]

Introduce commuting formal parameters `t=(t_1,...,t_{r_j})` and the affine scalar prime symbol

\[
a_{j,p}(\mathbf t)
=
\chi_j(p)+\sum_a c_{ja}(p)t_a.
\tag{PL376-6}
\]

Unique factorization then defines, for every fixed `t`, the scalar completely multiplicative function

\[
\boxed{
f_{j,\mathbf t}(n)
=
\prod_p a_{j,p}(\mathbf t)^{v_p(n)}.
}
\tag{PL376-7}
\]

Let

\[
\pi_j:\mathbb C[t_1,\ldots,t_{r_j}]\to A_j,
\qquad
\pi_j(t_a)=x_{ja},
\qquad
\pi_j(1)=e_j.
\tag{PL376-8}
\]

Equation (PL376-5) gives

\[
\pi_j(a_{j,p})=e_j\rho(p),
\]

and complete multiplicativity therefore gives the exact identity

\[
\boxed{
\pi_j\bigl(f_{j,\mathbf t}(n)\bigr)=e_j\rho(n)
}
\tag{PL376-9}
\]

for every positive integer `n`.

Now every monomial of total degree at least `N_j` is sent into `m_j^{N_j}=0`. Thus

\[
(t_1,\ldots,t_{r_j})^{N_j}\subseteq\ker\pi_j.
\tag{PL376-10}
\]

Consequently, for every linear functional `L_j:A_j -> C`, the functional `L_j o pi_j` depends only on the finite Taylor polynomial of degree `<N_j` at the origin. There are constants `C_{j,alpha}`, independent of `n`, such that

\[
\boxed{
L_j(e_j\rho(n))
=
\sum_{|\alpha|<N_j}
C_{j,\alpha}
\left.
\partial_{\mathbf t}^{\alpha}
 f_{j,\mathbf t}(n)
\right|_{\mathbf t=0}.
}
\tag{PL376-11}
\]

Summing the local factors, every linear matrix observable `L:M_d(C)->C` restricted to the image algebra has an exact representation

\[
\boxed{
L(\rho(n))
=
\sum_{j=1}^{r}
\sum_{|\alpha|<N_j}
C_{j,\alpha}
\left.
\partial_{\mathbf t}^{\alpha}
 f_{j,\mathbf t}(n)
\right|_{\mathbf t=0}.
}
\tag{PL376-12}
\]

Hence every finite-dimensional completely multiplicative matrix representation is, as seen by all linear observables, a **finite multivariate infinitesimal thickening of finitely many scalar completely multiplicative channels**. Higher-dimensional nonnormal extensions add only finitely many jet directions and finite nilpotent order.

This is stronger than the trace/determinant scalarization of `PL-374`: it applies to every matrix coefficient and every linear observable, and it includes the higher-order and singular cases left open by `PL-375`.

## Zero-valued scalar blocks have bounded prime-depth

The vanishing case excluded from the normalization argument of `PL-375` is particularly rigid here. Define

\[
Z_j=\{p:\chi_j(p)=0\}
\]

and

\[
\Omega_{Z_j}(n)=\sum_{p\in Z_j}v_p(n).
\tag{PL376-13}
\]

For `p in Z_j`, equation (PL376-5) puts `e_j rho(p)` entirely inside `m_j`. Therefore any product containing at least `N_j` prime factors from `Z_j`, counted with multiplicity, lies in `m_j^{N_j}=0`. Thus

\[
\boxed{
\Omega_{Z_j}(n)\ge N_j
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
e_j\rho(n)=0.
}
\tag{PL376-14}
\]

So a zero-valued scalar block cannot support arbitrarily deep motion in those prime directions. The singular escape is a bounded-thickness nilpotent neighborhood of the scalar residue channel, not an unbounded new exponent-lattice geometry.

## `PL-375` is exactly the first-order case

For a two-dimensional repeated-character block with a single square-zero nilpotent direction,

\[
A_j\simeq \mathbb C[\varepsilon]/(\varepsilon^2),
\]

so `N_j=2`. Only the zeroth and first derivatives in (PL376-11) survive. If the scalar character is nonzero-valued, the first derivative normalized by the scalar channel is precisely the completely additive cocycle

\[
d(n)=\sum_pv_p(n)d(p)
\]

of `PL-375`. The canonical choice `d(p)=log p` is therefore the tangent direction obtained by differentiating a scalar multiplicative deformation. Higher nilpotent layers produce higher mixed derivatives, but remain finite jets of scalar channels rather than a new type of finite-dimensional multiplicative source.

## Dirichlet-series consequence and validity domain

For a linear observable `L`, set

\[
F_L(s)=\sum_{n\ge1}L(\rho(n))n^{-s}.
\tag{PL376-15}
\]

If, for a given local block and a neighborhood of `t=0`, the scalar deformation series

\[
D_{j,\mathbf t}(s)
=
\sum_{n\ge1}f_{j,\mathbf t}(n)n^{-s}
\tag{PL376-16}
\]

converge normally in both `s` and `t` on the domain under discussion, termwise differentiation is legitimate and (PL376-12) yields

\[
F_L(s)
=
\sum_{j,\alpha}C_{j,\alpha}
\left.
\partial_{\mathbf t}^{\alpha}
D_{j,\mathbf t}(s)
\right|_{\mathbf t=0}.
\tag{PL376-17}
\]

In a region of absolute convergence, unique factorization gives the scalar Euler products

\[
D_{j,\mathbf t}(s)
=
\prod_p
\left(1-a_{j,p}(\mathbf t)p^{-s}\right)^{-1}.
\tag{PL376-18}
\]

Nothing in the finite-dimensional algebra argument supplies analytic continuation of (PL376-16) or (PL376-18). In particular, (PL376-18) must **not** be transported formally into the critical strip. If a scalar deformation family has a continuation there, that continuation has to be established independently; the matrix thickening contributes only finite derivatives of that independently continued scalar family.

## Prior-art and novelty audit

The algebraic ingredients are standard prior art, not a novelty claim. A finite-dimensional algebra over a field is Artinian; an Artinian ring is a finite product of local Artinian rings; and the maximal ideal of an Artinian local factor is nilpotent. See the Stacks Project, Section 10.53, especially Lemmas 10.53.2, 10.53.4 and 10.53.6, and Proposition 10.60.7:

- https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00J4
- https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00JB
- https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00KJ

The interpretation of a finite local algebra as a finite infinitesimal neighborhood, whose dual linear functionals are finite-order differential/jet data, is likewise classical Macaulay-duality / inverse-system territory. A convenient modern summary is the Macaulay2 inverse-systems documentation:

- https://macaulay2.com/doc/Macaulay2/share/doc/Macaulay2/InverseSystems/html/index.html

`PL-374` already used the classical simultaneous-triangularization theory of commuting matrices, and `PL-375` identified the two-dimensional nonzero self-extension with the classical Leibniz-additive/completely-additive first jet.

A targeted literature search for matrix-valued completely multiplicative arithmetic functions, commuting finite-dimensional representations of the free abelian prime monoid, higher Leibniz/Hasse-Schmidt extensions, and Artinian/Macaulay duality did not locate an RH/prime-lattice result claiming this exact line-specific reduction. The **new content asserted here is therefore only the derived consequence for this research frontier**: once the source is assumed finite-dimensional and completely multiplicative, the two open finite-dimensional escape routes left by `PL-375` — higher nilpotent order and zero-valued scalar blocks — both remain finite scalar-jet phenomena. No theorem priority is claimed for the underlying commutative-algebra classification.

## Adversarial audit

This result closes less than an unrestricted statement that “matrix-valued approaches cannot help”. Several possible sources of additional structure are outside the theorem.

First, nonlinear observables such as norms, pseudospectra, condition numbers, operator-valued boundary limits, or optimization over vectors can see finite nilpotent geometry in ways a single linear coefficient does not. What (PL376-12) says is that any such gain would come from the **added nonlinear observable**, not from irreducibly new finite-dimensional completely multiplicative source data.

Second, infinite-dimensional or unbounded representations need not generate an Artinian coefficient algebra and are not covered. Neither are constructions in which the arithmetic action ceases to be completely multiplicative, or where global relations couple primes only after a completion, boundary condition, quotient, scattering construction, or target-relative projection.

Third, the finite-jet formula is algebraic. It provides no new zero-free region, functional equation, positivity statement, spectral localization, or analytic continuation. In particular it does not single out `Re(s)=1/2`.

Finally, the scalar families `f_{j,t}` are auxiliary deformations assembled from the prime coordinates of the finite algebra. Calling them “scalar channels” does not make their analytic behavior classical or easy; it says only that the finite matrix source has no additional algebraic depth beyond finitely many derivatives of such scalar multiplicative data.

## Consequence for `prime_lattice`

The finite-dimensional completely multiplicative matrix branch is now exhausted at the source-representation level:

- `PL-374` showed that commuting finite matrix representations triangularize and that trace/Euler-determinant observables scalarize;
- `PL-375` showed that the first nonnormal two-dimensional extensions split or are first-order scalar jets;
- the present result shows that **all** finite-dimensional local factors, including higher nilpotent order and singular/zero-valued residue channels, are finite multivariate scalar jets.

Accordingly, a genuinely new RH-relevant mechanism cannot consist merely of choosing a larger finite matrix at each prime while preserving exact complete multiplicativity. A surviving route must add structure not captured by this Artinian source algebra: for example an infinite-dimensional/unbounded global representation, a controlled failure of complete multiplicativity, a genuinely global completion or boundary condition, or a target-relative/nonlinear operation whose mathematical content is independently strong enough to constrain the scalar analytic continuation.
