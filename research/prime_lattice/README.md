# Prime Lattice

## Research mandate

### Primary object

The line studies the prime-exponent geometry of the positive integers through the unique-factorization vector

```text
n = product_p p^{v_p(n)}
v(n) = (v_2(n), v_3(n), v_5(n), ...)
```

with finite support. Multiplication becomes vector addition, primes are coordinate basis directions, square-free integers form the finite-support `{0,1}` hypercube, and logarithmic size is the linear functional

```text
log n = sum_p v_p(n) log p.
```

Under the classical Bohr correspondence, Dirichlet series become power series in prime coordinates. The ordinary one-complex-dimensional slice is

```text
s = sigma + i t  ->  z(s) = (p^{-s})_p
                     = (p^{-sigma} e^{-i t log p})_p,
```

so vertical translation is a Kronecker-type flow with frequencies `log p`.

### Objective

Determine whether this arithmetic lattice and its natural harmonic/dynamical completions admit a nontrivial geometric, operator, spectral, or cancellation mechanism relevant to the Riemann zeta function beyond the standard exponent-vector representation, Euler product, and classical Bohr transform.

A successful mechanism should explain genuinely global zeta structure rather than merely re-encode a Dirichlet series in infinitely many coordinates.

### Priority questions

Study in particular:

- the Bohr lift of Dirichlet series and Hardy spaces on the infinite polydisk/polytorus;
- vertical limit functions and Kronecker/Weyl flows generated by `log p`;
- the role of `log n = <v(n),(log p)_p>` as an energy or generator;
- analytic continuation from `Re(s)>1` into the critical strip;
- why `Re(s)=1/2` appears as a natural Hilbert, unitary, or self-dual boundary when it genuinely does;
- the functional equation and the extra global structure required to produce it;
- Möbius inversion and cancellation on the square-free sector;
- Euler-product breakdown or meaningful renormalization;
- explicit-formula, trace, determinant, resonance, operator, or function-space mechanisms that preserve arithmetic information through continuation;
- Fourier/spectral duality capable of distinguishing the exact rational-prime norm map from generic multiplicative frequency systems.

For mixed additive/multiplicative candidates, derive the averaging weights and their scale dependence from a declared arithmetic operation. Seek an independently controlled signed correlation that survives the resulting operator or limiting construction; an affine shift or a Hilbert-space boundary alone does not supply cancellation.

### Scope and exclusions

Treat the exponent-vector representation, square-free Boolean hypercube, Euler-product factorization, classical Möbius inversion, and classical Bohr lift as baseline prior art rather than discoveries.

Do not transport identities valid only in `Re(s)>1` into the critical strip without a valid continuation mechanism. Operators, determinants, or geometric language that reproduce zeta by definition without additional structure are outside the target.

### Line-specific falsification controls

For candidate mechanisms, compare specifically against:

- Helson-type phase twists and other prime-coordinate deformations;
- Beurling/generalized-prime systems with matched coarse counting or finite-frequency data;
- generic Kronecker-flow or infinite-torus phenomena;
- regularizations that remove exactly the zero-sensitive term they are supposed to explain;
- operators whose spectral behavior is universal or determined by a classical boundary singularity rather than by the Riemann zero divisor.

Identify which target-relevant information singles out the rational-prime application from broader multiplicative systems. A universal analytic estimate can contribute when an independent arithmetic input supplies that distinction; ambient lattice structure alone does not.

### Prior-art domains

- Bohr's theory of Dirichlet series;
- Hedenmalm-Lindqvist-Seip theory and Hardy spaces of Dirichlet series;
- Hardy spaces on the infinite polytorus;
- Kronecker/Weyl equidistribution and vertical-limit theory;
- Helson-type constructions and multiplicative harmonic analysis;
- Beurling/generalized primes;
- adelic/idèlic harmonic analysis when the missing global structure points there;
- dynamical and recurrence formulations of zeta;
- Hilbert-space, Hankel/operator, determinant, trace, or spectral formulations of RH.

### Relationship to other lines

This line is not derived from the prime-circle or prime-flute geometries. Any bridge to those constructions must therefore identify additional shared mathematical structure rather than assuming a common geometric origin.
