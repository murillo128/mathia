# Ambient Bohr structure is insufficient, but scalar energy coherence can still force zeros

- [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-003-ambient-prime-torus-zero-flexibility]] uses [[research/prior_art/incremental/PA-helson-zeta-divisor-and-continuation-flexibility|Helson-zeta flexibility]] to show that the undistinguished prime torus / `log p` frequency list permits radically different zero, pole, and maximal-continuation behavior under multiplicative twists.
- [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-004-prime-exponent-gas-prior-art]] shows that the bare exponent-lattice / energy-`log p` statistical-mechanics picture is already classical.
- [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-006-random-bohr-boundary-gmc]] shows that the standard randomized critical boundary exists as a GMC-related generalized distribution but has no ordinary pointwise zero set.

[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-108-large-lattice-coherence-near-one-zero-forcing|PL-108]] adds an important positive literature redirect without reversing those ambient obstructions. The uniform energy-ball Bohr polynomial is exactly `sum_{n<=x} n^{it}`, and the new Dong--Wang--Wang--Zhang inverse theorem proves that an extremely large value forces a cluster of ordinary zeta zeros near the same height. This is genuine zero-detection from an unweighted finite lattice Fourier amplitude, not a programmed zero readout.

The same finding also identifies its information boundary: both the cutoff and phase factor completely through the scalar energy `E(alpha)=log n`, while the analytic inverse theorem works through the shifted Dirichlet series and neighborhoods of the Euler-product/pole edge `Re(s)=1`. It does not use multidimensional exponent-coordinate relations or derive a mechanism localizing zeros on `Re(s)=1/2`.

Derived curation statement: ambient frequency geometry alone still does not rigidly determine the Riemann continuation or critical-line location. Scalar energy coherence is now known to carry real inverse information about ordinary zeta zeros, so a stronger Prime-Lattice mechanism should state what additional coordinate/global structure it uses beyond `sum_{n<=x} n^{it}` and why that information changes zero **location**, not merely detection near the analytic edge.
