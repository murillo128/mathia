# Positive-cone and growing-depth Gram data reduce to classical sampling controls

[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-072-positive-cone-dirichlet-resolution-universality|PL-072]]--[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-087-prime-power-axis-bulk-vanishing-rank|PL-087]] establish the earlier boundary: fixed-lag pointing redirects to classical additive correlations, the local sharp kernel is prolate, the macroscopic unweighted bulk is classical sampling geometry, bounded prime clusters destroy sublinear extreme rigidity, the mean-gap bulk routes to Poisson sinc statistics under its stated Hardy--Littlewood hierarchy, and ordinary von-Mangoldt/prime-power population weighting does not create a new empirical bulk variable.

The growing-depth escape is now substantially narrower. [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-088-fixed-depth-prime-power-time-dilation|PL-088]]--[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-094-growing-depth-loguniform-shell-envelope|PL-094]] identify the depth scaling and log-uniform shell envelope, while [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-095-growing-depth-pnt-envelope-gram-universality|PL-095]] supplies the deterministic PNT envelope. [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-096-growing-depth-all-horizon-s2-universality|PL-096]] then proves that the naturally normalized coherent covariance is Hilbert--Schmidt universal at every observation horizon throughout the first growing-depth regime, so the earlier inverse-PNT-error scale is not a genuine `S_2` spectral transition.

The stronger trace norm has a sharp sampling boundary rather than a new arithmetic phase. [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-097-super-nyquist-s1-rank-defect-universality|PL-097]] and [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-098-critical-s1-nyquist-rank-floor|PL-098]] identify the super/critical Nyquist rank obstruction, while [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-099-subnyquist-midpoint-s1-control|PL-099]] gives a deterministic midpoint cloud that attains the same continuum prolate comparator in `S_1` below Nyquist.

The source-backed conclusion is scoped but broad: ordinary bulk, fixed moments, the first growing-depth coherent spectrum, and its raw `S_2`/`S_1` sampling transitions are matched by PNT/log-uniform/prolate controls. The live frontier is the **excess beyond this sampling model**—microscopic spacing, hard-edge or inverse statistics, condition numbers, target-relative Schur complements, singular/growing tests, depth-selective observables, or completed indefinite Weil data. Any positive claim must separate rational primes from these deterministic/generalized-prime controls in the same statistic and norm.
