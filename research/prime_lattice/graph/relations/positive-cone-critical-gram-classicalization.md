# Positive-cone finite-horizon Gram data redirect to classical resolution, sampling, and local prime statistics

[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-072-positive-cone-dirichlet-resolution-universality|PL-072]]--[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-078-sharp-critical-gram-prolate-universality|PL-078]] establish the first layer: the `N~T` transition is universal sinc/Dirichlet-polynomial resolution, unpointed Möbius orientation is a torus gauge, pointing redirects fixed-lag data to Mirsky/Chowla or Hardy--Littlewood correlations, smoothed growing lags lead to pair correlation/short-interval variance, and the local sharp kernel is the classical prolate time-bandlimiting operator. [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-079-global-sharp-gram-kms-spectral-law|PL-079]]--[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-081-prime-support-gram-scale-split|PL-081]] extend this through the macroscopic integer-band law, Nyquist/Ingham determinant transition, and prime-support scale split.

The prime-support hard edge is not a stable escape below the macroscopic scale. [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-082-bounded-prime-cluster-two-sided-gram-instability|PL-082]] combines bounded prime clusters with the sharp kernel to force arbitrarily large nearly rank-one prime blocks for every `T=o(X)`, driving the lower edge to zero and the upper edge to infinity along subsequences. Thus extreme-eigenvalue rigidity at sublinear horizons is already vulnerable to classical prime-cluster geometry even when the empirical bulk is close to the identity.

The exact mean-gap bulk also redirects to classical local statistics under its stated conjectural input. [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-083-critical-prime-gram-poisson-second-moment|PL-083]] identifies the second moment at `T=cX/log X` with the Poisson sinc constant under local Hardy--Littlewood pairs; [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-084-critical-prime-gram-poisson-moment-hierarchy|PL-084]] extends this to every fixed moment under the corresponding finite tuple hierarchy; and [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-085-critical-prime-gram-full-poisson-bulk-law|PL-085]] upgrades the conditional statement to the full empirical bulk law under the full local Hardy--Littlewood hierarchy. These are routing statements, not evidence for the conjectural Hardy--Littlewood inputs.

Natural von-Mangoldt half-weighting does not restore an ordinary bulk selector in this regime. [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-086-prime-vonmangoldt-weight-deterministic-grading|PL-086]] shows that on prime support `Lambda(p)=log p` contributes only an asymptotically deterministic macroscopic diagonal grading. [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-087-prime-power-axis-bulk-vanishing-rank|PL-087]] then restores the full prime-power axis and shows that although exponent depth survives pointwise, all higher-prime-power indices form a vanishing-rank sector on a fixed multiplicative shell, so they cannot change the ordinary dimension-normalized weak bulk law.

The source-backed boundary is therefore substantially narrower than an unresolved mean-gap bulk phase. Remaining possibilities include rare-sector/hard-edge or `log det` statistics, moments whose order grows with scale, depth-conditioned or canonically reweighted prime-power sectors, target-relative/Nyman observables, and completed explicit-formula structures that do not average the prime-power axis by ordinary matrix dimension. None is promoted here without its own matched-control and source-fidelity audit.
