# Positive-cone finite-horizon Gram data redirect to classical resolution, sampling, and correlations

[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-072-positive-cone-dirichlet-resolution-universality|PL-072]]--[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-075-critical-half-weight-hardylittlewood-lags|PL-075]] establish the first layer: the `N~T` transition is universal sinc/Dirichlet-polynomial resolution, unpointed Möbius orientation is a torus gauge, and pointing redirects fixed-lag data to Mirsky/Chowla or Hardy--Littlewood correlations rather than a new zero selector.

The global/growing-lag escape is now substantially narrower. [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-076-long-vonmangoldt-mean-square-pair-correlation|PL-076]] shows that a natural smoothed long von-Mangoldt mean square reorganizes its off-diagonal content into the classical zero pair-correlation channel. [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-077-gallagher-mellin-resolution-trichotomy|PL-077]] gives an exact positive Mellin/Gallagher localization: sub-resolution bands are diagonal, critical boxes expose finitely many additive lags, and wider boxes are multiplicative short-interval variance; the half-weight produces the classical Selberg/Goldston--Montgomery `x^{-2}dx` geometry.

The sharp rectangular kernel also classicalizes at the unweighted Gram level. [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-078-sharp-critical-gram-prolate-universality|PL-078]] identifies the local critical sinc kernel with the classical discrete prolate time-bandlimiting operator. [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-079-global-sharp-gram-kms-spectral-law|PL-079]] upgrades the macroscopic bulk to a locally Toeplitz/Kac--Murdock--Szegő mixture shared by a non-arithmetic `log(n+theta)` control, and [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-080-sharp-logarithmic-gram-nyquist-determinant-phase|PL-080]] routes the first lower-frame/per-site determinant transition to Nyquist/Ingham density geometry.

Restricting to prime basis directions changes the scale but not yet the source of arithmetic significance. [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-081-prime-support-gram-scale-split|PL-081]] proves bulk convergence to the identity whenever `T >> X/log X`, while bounded prime gaps can still drive the extreme lower edge to zero along subsequences. The first unresolved prime-support bulk scale is therefore `T~X/log X`, the classical mean-gap/short-interval scale.

The source-backed conclusion is scoped: local, smoothed-global, unweighted macroscopic bulk, first determinant scale, and prime-support bulk above the mean-gap transition all redirect to classical resolution/correlation/sampling theory. Exact mean-gap-scale prime-support laws, finer edge/determinant asymptotics, arithmetic `Lambda(p)p^{-1/2}` weighting, target-relative/Nyman data, and other structures forced by the full rational-prime completion remain outside this relation.
