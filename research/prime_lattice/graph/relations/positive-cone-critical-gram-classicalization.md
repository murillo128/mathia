# Positive-cone critical Gram data redirect to classical resolution and correlations

[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-072-positive-cone-dirichlet-resolution-universality|PL-072]] shows that forbidding inverse prime directions restores finite-horizon rigidity, but its first transition is the classical Dirichlet-polynomial resolution threshold `N ~ T`: below it the positive characters are asymptotically orthogonal, above it adjacent logarithmic frequencies become unresolved, and the critical local kernel is universal sinc geometry.

[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-073-mobius-torus-gauge-statistics|PL-073]] then separates square-free support from Möbius orientation. On the square-free cube the Möbius sign field is exactly a prime-torus translation, so all unpointed Haar laws and finite-time Gram spectral invariants are unchanged by the orientation.

Pointing breaks that gauge but redirects the first surviving fixed-lag data to established additive-correlation problems. [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-074-pointed-mobius-gram-mirsky-chowla-split|PL-074]] splits the critical terms into unconditional Mirsky square-free-pair density and the open fixed-shift Chowla correlation. [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-075-critical-half-weight-hardylittlewood-lags|PL-075]] shows that the `sigma=1/2` fixed-lag scale is universal for any coefficient sequence with a nonzero correlation density; for von Mangoldt coefficients the arithmetic constant is the classical Hardy--Littlewood prime-pair singular series.

Thus the positive cone and coefficient weighting do reveal real arithmetic data, but the first finite-horizon/pointed mechanisms do not supply a new RH selector: they are governed by ordinary frequency resolution, gauge symmetry, Mirsky/Chowla, and Hardy--Littlewood correlation structure. Growing-lag, mesoscopic and explicit-formula regimes remain outside this scoped relation.
