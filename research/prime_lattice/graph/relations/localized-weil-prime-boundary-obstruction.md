# Localized Weil prime operator: exponential norm and boundary escape

[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-049-weil-prime-shift-norm-exponential-obstruction|PL-049]] closes the proposed subexponential-norm escape for the localized non-archimedean Weil prime operator by proving its norm remains of exponential order.

[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-050-rescaled-weil-prime-boundary-escape|PL-050]] explicitly builds on that operator and shows that after natural `exp(-L)` normalization and dilation to a fixed interval the operators converge strongly to zero while positive and negative spectral edges remain macroscopic in shrinking endpoint layers.

The source-backed chain is:

```text
localized Weil prime translations
    -> exponential operator-norm scale
    -> natural fixed-window rescaling
    -> zero bulk strong limit + escaping boundary spectrum
```

This closes the specific bulk-limit route stated in PL-050. It does not close boundary-blow-up limits or the full localized Weil operator after archimedean and pole terms are restored.
