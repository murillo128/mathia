# Localized Weil boundary: topology, second scale and mesoscopic obstruction

[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-049-weil-prime-shift-norm-exponential-obstruction|PL-049]]--[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-065-zero-free-prime-log-recurrence-delay|PL-065]] establish a sharp topology/scale split. Completion cancels the universal PNT rank-one boundary mode strongly; norm/Calkin topology retains prime-log recurrence; fixed smoothing, growing depth, canonical recentering and broad moving bands collapse; zero-free sampling delays coherent recurrence beyond a very large frequency scale.

The newest chain closes or reclassifies the most natural escapes above that barrier:

- [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-066-natural-weil-band-right-edge-zero-certificate|PL-066]] turns natural-band noncollapse into an inverse rightmost-zero certificate: an order-one band defect already forces a quantitatively near-right-edge zeta zero.
- [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-067-second-scale-weil-boundary-almost-periodic-no-limit|PL-067]] identifies the fixed-smooth second amplitude, under RH, as a nonconstant uniformly almost-periodic zero signal rather than a convergent operator coefficient.
- [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-068-fixed-smooth-second-scale-boundedness|PL-068]] shows that for residual non-annihilating smooth probes, boundedness of that second-scale coefficient is RH-equivalent.
- [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-069-second-scale-zero-hull-kronecker-universality|PL-069]] classifies its translation hull as the compact Kronecker rotation generated by the zero-ordinate frequency module; retaining the evaluation observable simply restores the already-present zero coefficients.
- [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-070-prime-zero-kronecker-joining-intersection|PL-070]] shows the canonical same-time prime-log/zero-hull joining is controlled exactly by the intersection of their frequency groups.
- [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-071-finite-horizon-prime-zero-resonance-universality|PL-071]] closes adaptive finite-horizon character resonance as a selector: arbitrary finite target frequencies admit prime-log approximants with logarithmic weighted exponent complexity.

These findings do produce a sharp RH-equivalent diagnostic at second scale, but they do not yet give an upstream zero-selection mechanism. The surviving route must derive the decisive bound or topology directly from rational-prime/completion structure and matched controls, rather than repackaging the continued zero expansion, a zero-location theorem, or generic Diophantine recurrence.
