# Critical Hilbert boundary does not locate zeros

- [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-001-bohr-hardy-half-boundary]] identifies `Re(s)=1/2` as the exact bounded-evaluation / `ell^2` boundary of the standard Bohr–Hardy Hilbert geometry.
- [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-002-standard-h2-kernel-zero-obstruction]] immediately narrows that observation: the standard reproducing kernel is evaluated in a zero-free half-plane and therefore cannot encode nontrivial zeta zeros by kernel zeros or evaluation-state orthogonality.

Derived curation statement: the critical line is geometrically natural in this Hilbert space, but the standard `H^2` kernel route is decisively insufficient for zero location.
