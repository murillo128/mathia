# MI-028 — Exact-zero incidence is thinner than stable access and algebraic separation

**Evidence level:** exact for the ambient saturated equal-weight incidence geometry through [AF-308](../../findings/AF-308-exact-zero-frequency-incidence-is-a-null-cone.md)--[AF-309](../../findings/AF-309-exact-zero-frequency-incidence-is-dense-and-nowhere-stable.md), and for the eight-prime two-observable boundary through [AF-328](../../findings/AF-328-prime-singular-incidence-is-a-coprime-exponential-sum-common-zero-problem.md)--[AF-329](../../findings/AF-329-dense-prime-flow-collapses-joint-margin-and-blocks-bezout-separation.md). Exact eight-prime incidence itself remains open.

For saturated equal-weight Dirichlet sums, AF-308--AF-309 separate exact incidence from stable access. The exact-hit frequency set is dense but Lebesgue-null, while rational independence makes the value orbit approach the ambient zero set arbitrarily closely. Exact zero-freeness can therefore coexist with zero value margin and zero frequency margin.

The eight-prime middle-singular target sharpens this distinction. AF-328 rewrites `s_1=e_4=0` as `F(it)=G(it)=0` for two finite exponential sums and proves that the prime-frequency sum is irreducible and the pair is coprime. AF-329 then observes that the prime-log orbit is dense in `T^8` and that the regular-octagon configuration lies in `V(e_1,e_4)`. Consequently

`inf_t ||(F(it),G(it))|| = 0`,

and every fixed positive tolerance is reached with relatively dense return times. Thus even the **joint** retained defect has no positive stability margin.

AF-329 also closes the next algebraic escape. Although `(F,G)` is coprime in the finite exponential-sum ring, it is not comaximal: `(F,G)` is a proper ideal, so no finite exponential-sum Bézout identity can certify separation from zero. The ambient torus zero blocks such a certificate without implying that the one-dimensional prime orbit ever hits that zero exactly.

The reusable hierarchy is therefore strict: common-factor exclusion, unit-ideal separation, positive joint margin and exact pointwise incidence are different currencies. In the present prime problem the first three are exhausted in the relevant direction: there is no common factor, no Bézout certificate, and no positive joint margin. What survives is only the exact placement of the prime-log subgroup inside `V(e_1,e_4)`.

A successful next theorem must therefore use pointwise arithmetic/transcendence information, an exact source identity forcing a forbidden multiplicative relation, or an actual construction of a hit. Conditioning, perturbation stability, near-zero numerics, ambient topology and finite exponential-sum factor/ideal theory cannot decide the remaining predicate.

**Boundary.** Dense near-incidence is not exact incidence. Properness of `(F,G)` is an algebraic character-space statement and does not produce a common analytic zero. Nothing here settles unequal weights, nonzero real part, infinite supports, or the exact eight-prime hit.
