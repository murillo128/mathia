# MI-019 — Faithful translation lifts cannot nonlinearly stretch the prime tail for free

**Evidence level:** exact and stable rigidity through AF-221--AF-223

## Core intuition

If a representation is required to preserve the dense source translation law and the observation remains injective, then the apparent freedom to repair a collapsing prime-tail mesh by nonlinear or higher-dimensional coordinates is illusory. The representation is forced onto an affine line; approximate fidelity is forced near that affine model.

## Strongest justified principle

AF-221 proves scalar affine rigidity from the prime-tail difference source. AF-222 shows that vector-valued faithful translation lifts do not gain extra geometric directions: all source points lie on one affine line. AF-223 makes the conclusion stable in Banach space under approximate translation fidelity, with deviation controlled by the admitted representation error.

## Program consequence

A proposed coordinate repair should state which hypothesis it breaks. Genuine improvement must come from source-specific side information, a non-translation relation, controlled loss of injectivity, or an error/conditioning tradeoff that is quantitatively favorable. Dimension and nonlinear parametrization alone are not new arithmetic information.

## Counterevidence / boundary

The rigidity applies to the faithful translation category generated by the dense difference source. It does not classify representations with additional labels, nonlinear group laws, non-injective quotients used deliberately, or observation maps whose error is paid elsewhere.

## Epistemic status

**Exact category boundary with a stable approximate form; no claim is made that every arithmetic representation is affine.**

## Falsification criterion

Construct a faithful source translation lift satisfying the AF-221/AF-222 hypotheses whose image is not affine, or an approximate lift violating the AF-223 stability bound. A valid escape should instead identify the extra relation or error budget that leaves those hypotheses.
