# MI-029 — Prime phase relation obstructions exhaust their linear power at the pentagon

**Evidence level:** proved for common-time phases of distinct rational primes through AF-311. Exact prime-support zero incidence remains open for saturated supports of size `k>=5`.

AF-310 identifies the elementary relation barrier. For distinct primes `p_1,...,p_k` at a common nonzero time, normalize by the last phase and write `u_j=(p_j/p_k)^(-it)`. The integer character-relation lattice

`L_(p,t)={a in Z^(k-1): product_j u_j^(a_j)=1}`

injects into `Z` by its winding number, so it is cyclic and has rank at most one. This exactly excludes the forced rank-two zero geometries at `k=3,4`.

AF-311 shows that replacing integer characters by classical linear-forms-in-logarithms theory does not restore separation. For five distinct primes, the frequencies `log(p_j/p_5)` are linearly independent over the algebraic numbers by Baker's theorem. Consequently a common-time prime phase point can satisfy at most one independent algebraic-affine lifted-angle relation `a·theta=2 pi b` with algebraic coefficients. The relation rank is invariant under integer `2 pi` changes of lift.

The pentagon zero manifold is already compatible with that stronger restriction. Near the regular pentagon it contains a real-analytic two-dimensional patch that is not contained in any affine hyperplane. Because the algebraic numbers are countable, a Baire argument gives a dense residual subset of exact pentagon lifts with **no nontrivial algebraic-affine relation at all**. Thus the first unresolved zero geometry generically satisfies a stronger linear-independence condition than a prime phase point is required to satisfy.

The mechanism boundary is therefore sharper than “integer characters stop at five.” Standard algebraic-linear information about prime logarithms also stops there. The next exact-incidence theorem must detect something genuinely nonlinear or source-specific about the prime-log direction inside the nontorsion polygon-zero cone; more generic topology, density, torsion classification, character rank, or a routine Baker upgrade cannot recover the `k=3,4` contradiction.

**Boundary.** Compatibility is not existence. AF-311 supplies no five-prime exact zero, measure lower bound, Diophantine incidence rate, nonlinear algebraic-independence theorem, zeta zero count, or RH implication. Baker theory constrains algebraic-coefficient linear relations among logarithmic frequencies; it does not decide the nonlinear exponential incidence equation defining pentagon closure.
