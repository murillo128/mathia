# MI-057 — Paired-cube completion defect transfers Banach type ceilings into source-relative mixture fidelity

**Evidence level:** exact geometric synthesis from [AF-458](../../findings/AF-458-rademacher-type-controls-sparse-mixture-fidelity-through-paired-secants.md), [AF-459](../../findings/AF-459-average-cube-completion-defect-controls-type-p-fidelity.md), [AF-460](../../findings/AF-460-finite-rank-source-laws-have-zero-cube-completion-defect.md), and [AF-461](../../findings/AF-461-regular-finite-dimensional-nonlinear-source-laws-inherit-zero-cube-defect.md), interpreted against AF-456--AF-457. The AF-460 conclusion applies to normalized kernels of bounded finite-rank linear laws; AF-461 transfers that geometry locally to regular `C^1` finite-dimensional fibers. In both cases, transfer to a physical carrier requires the stated carrier inclusion.

For a bounded barycentric marking `phi:A->Y` with `sup_a ||phi(a)||<=M`, Banach type limits source-relative TV fidelity through the geometry of the normalized completed-secant carrier `C`. For a paired block `B=((a_1,b_1),...,(a_k,b_k))`, write `q_epsilon^B=(1/(2k)) sum_j epsilon_j(delta_(a_j)-delta_(b_j))` and let `D_k(C)` be the least mean `ell^1` distance from such a sign cube to `C`. AF-459 gives

`kappa_C(F_phi) <= M [T_p(Y) k^(1/p-1) + D_k(C)]`.

AF-460 supplies an exact source-side closure theorem for finite-rank linear constraints. If `L:ell^1_0(A)->E` is bounded and finite-rank on an infinite alphabet, then its normalized kernel carrier `C_L={h in ker L: ||h||_1=1}` satisfies `D_k(C_L)=0` for every fixed `k`. Finite-dimensional compactness clusters many atomic images, and a bounded right inverse corrects the residual without destroying paired-cube normalization.

AF-461 shows that this is not merely a linear-model pathology. Let `G:U->E` be `C^1` with finite-dimensional `E`, and let `x_0` be a regular point where `DG(x_0)=L` is onto. The local level set has the form `x_0+v+g(v)` with `v in ker L` and `Dg(0)=0`. After normalization, arbitrarily small same-fiber secants approach every unit vector of `ker L`; hence the local same-fiber carrier inherits `D_k=0` for every fixed `k`. Smooth curvature does not remove the tangent kernel at first order strongly enough to protect cube completion.

Combined with AF-459, any physical completed-secant carrier containing either the full normalized finite-rank kernel or one of these regular local fibers has zero bounded-affine lower gain into every Banach target of nontrivial type. The obstruction is therefore not just that a source has many degrees of freedom: **finite-dimensional regular equality constraints leave the same paired-cube tangent geometry unless the physical source deletes it**.

This sharpens the contrapositive resource. Stable bounded affine fidelity cannot be protected merely by finitely many bounded linear conservation laws, nor by replacing them with a regular finite-dimensional `C^1` nonlinear law. A surviving restriction must make the actual carrier tangent-incomplete: singular fibers, positivity or boundary constraints, hard support, genuinely infinite-dimensional laws, an unbounded observation category, or a different source metric may do so, but the separation from paired cubes has to be proved on that carrier.

**Boundary.** AF-461 is local and regular-point specific. At singular points the derivative kernel can overstate the true fiber dramatically, and simplex positivity or hard support may destroy the open-neighborhood hypothesis. The physical-carrier inclusion remains load-bearing, as do the infinite alphabet, finite-dimensional target law, TV/`ell^1` geometry and bounded affine observation. Large `D_k(C)` is still only a necessary escape from this obstruction, not a sufficient inverse theorem.
