# MI-006 — Interaction fidelity has support and collective stability layers

**Evidence level:** supported by exact product-space, arbitrary-source, and multichannel Hilbert classifications

## Core intuition

Perfect marginal or channel data can fail in two different ways. A target interaction may be absent from the closed information space generated by the retained channels, or it may belong to that space but be recoverable only through an unbounded or increasingly ill-conditioned inverse. The first is an exact support-fidelity failure; the second is a stability failure. With more than two channels, stability is genuinely collective: good pairwise angles do not control the full fusion operator.

## Strongest justified principle

AF-031--AF-033 identify the finite Boolean prototype: marginal scenarios retain exactly the Walsh faces of a downward-closed simplicial complex, and missing faces are the exact kernel. AF-034 extends this to independent product spaces through the Hoeffding decomposition, while AF-035 shows that the simple meet law for coordinate conditional expectations is equivalent to the relevant conditional-independence structure rather than a generic projection identity.

AF-036--AF-038 separate linear and multiplicative completion and then remove product structure. For arbitrary sub-sigma-fields, degree-`r` information is exactly the closed fusion span of joins of at most `r` channels. The support projection of the positive fusion operator gives exact recoverability without any commuting-projection hypothesis; dependence merely makes the grading source-dependent.

AF-039 supplies the two-channel stability gate: for closed channel subspaces `M,N`, exact recovery on `closure(M+N)` is automatic, while stable recovery holds exactly when the Friedrichs cosine is below one. AF-040 shows that this does not reduce pairwise when several channels are present. For closed `M_1,...,M_m`, the analysis map `h -> (P_1h,...,P_mh)` is stably invertible on the generated support exactly when the algebraic sum `M_1+...+M_m` is closed, equivalently when the full fusion operator has a positive lower frame bound. Three channels can have uniformly good pairwise Friedrichs gaps while the collective sum is dense and nonclosed.

The reusable lesson is therefore sharper than “check the angles.” Exact interaction support is a closure question, while robust use of that support is a **global lower-frame/closed-sum question for the whole retained family**. Pairwise collision or conditioning tests can all pass while the joint decoder still loses every uniform margin.

## What remains possible

For mathematically forced nonlinear, operator-valued, or asymptotic channel families, compute both the source-dependent retained support and a uniform collective stability modulus. A proposed minimal lift is incomplete if it restores algebraic support but its fusion lower bound collapses in the intended limit.

For arithmetic applications this matters directly: a discriminator can survive every finite truncation and every pairwise comparison yet become unusable after completion. Conversely, adding a genuinely new interaction channel can help only if it enlarges the retained support or improves the collective lower bound rather than merely duplicating pairwise information.

## Status / novelty

Hoeffding decompositions, conditional expectations, fusion frames, closed sums, and Friedrichs angles are classical. The persisted Mathia contribution is their exact fidelity organization across Boolean support, arbitrary-source fusion support, multiplicative degree, and the separation between pairwise and collective stability.

## Falsification criterion

Refute the principle by producing a target outside the stated fusion support that is nevertheless determined by the admitted observations, or a multichannel family with nonclosed algebraic sum whose analysis map is uniformly bounded below on the generated support. A positive extension should compute a sharp collective lower bound in the actual source category.
