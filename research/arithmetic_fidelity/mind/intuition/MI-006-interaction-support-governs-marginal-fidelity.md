# MI-006 — Interaction fidelity has support and stability layers

**Evidence level:** supported by exact product-space, arbitrary-source, and two-subspace Hilbert classifications

## Core intuition

Perfect marginal or channel data can fail in two different ways. A target interaction may be absent from the closed information space generated by the retained channels, or it may belong to that space but be recoverable only through an unbounded/ill-conditioned inverse. The first is an exact support-fidelity failure; the second is a stability failure. Treating both as “information loss” hides a mathematically sharp distinction.

## Strongest justified principle

AF-031--AF-033 identify the finite Boolean prototype: marginal scenarios retain exactly the Walsh faces of a downward-closed simplicial complex, and missing faces are the exact kernel.

AF-034 extends this to independent product spaces through the Hoeffding decomposition

\[
L^2(Q)=\bigoplus_{S\subseteq[d]}\mathcal H_S.
\]

Coordinate conditional expectations retain exactly the interaction faces contained in the observed coordinate set. AF-035 shows that the Boolean meet law for these raw conditional-expectation projections is not generic: it is equivalent, pairwise, to the corresponding conditional-independence statement and, for the whole coordinate family, to mutual independence.

AF-036--AF-037 then separate linear and multiplicative completion. The Hilbert-space join of retained channels contains only interaction faces already present in one channel, while the generated sigma-field fills the whole simplex on the covered coordinates. Allowing products of at most `r` channel observables yields an exact filtration by channel cover number; the minimal multiplicative degree needed for a target is the maximum cover number of its nonzero interaction faces.

AF-038 removes product structure entirely. For arbitrary sub-sigma-fields, the degree-`r` information space is exactly the closed fusion span of all joins of at most `r` channels,

\[
W_r=\overline{\sum_{|I|\le r}L^2(\bigvee_{i\in I}\mathcal F_i)}.
\]

Its invisible space is the common kernel of the corresponding conditional expectations, and the support projection of the positive fusion operator gives exact recoverability without any commuting-projection hypothesis. The price is that dependence destroys the source-independent simplicial grading: the relevant interaction layers are properties of the actual source law.

AF-039 adds the second gate. For two closed channel subspaces `M,N`, exact recovery on `\overline{M+N}` is automatic, but stable recovery holds exactly when their Friedrichs cosine is strictly below one. The sharp lower fusion-frame bound is `1-c(M,N)`. Thus finite-stage injectivity can survive while the infinite-dimensional decoder becomes unbounded.

## What remains possible

The next useful generalization is not another Boolean marginal example. It is to classify source-dependent interaction grades and **uniform stability constants** for mathematically forced families of nonlinear, operator-valued, or asymptotic channels. A minimal lift should be judged twice: does it put the target in the retained support, and does it keep that target uniformly separated from the annihilator under the intended limit?

For arithmetic applications this matters directly. A representation can preserve a rational-prime discriminator algebraically yet become useless after completion if the reconstruction constant collapses, while a multiplicative or generated-sigma lift may restore interactions that a linear span provably cannot see.

## Status / novelty

Hoeffding/ANOVA decompositions, conditional expectation, fusion frames, and Friedrichs angles are classical. The persisted Mathia contribution is the exact fidelity interpretation across these categories: Boolean support, product-space cover degree, arbitrary-source fusion support, and the separation between exact and stable recovery.

## Falsification criterion

Refute the principle by producing a target outside `W_r` that is nevertheless determined by every degree-`r` channel observation, or a two-channel system with `c(M,N)=1` whose inverse observation map is uniformly bounded on `\overline{M+N}`. A positive extension should compute both the exact retained support and a sharp stability modulus in the actual source category.

## Lean-formalizable core

- Hoeffding interaction-face projection identities.
- Conditional-independence characterization of projection products.
- Channel-cover filtration for bounded multiplicative degree.
- Arbitrary-source fusion-span/support-projection theorem.
- Friedrichs-angle formula for the two-channel stability constant.
