# MI-033 — Parity and product phase control harmonic recovery, and the half-spectrum endpoint is globally rigid

**Evidence level:** exact local/global harmonic-fibre geometry through AF-313--AF-321, AF-335 and [AF-336](../../findings/AF-336-half-spectrum-vanishing-rigidifies-the-unit-circle-fiber.md). No unconditional theorem is claimed that the one-parameter prime-time orbit hits or avoids the remaining even middle-singular target.

For a unitary `n`-point configuration, self-inversive symmetry makes harmonic recovery parity-sensitive. In odd size, the low consecutive harmonics determine the configuration up to the expected product phase. In even size, the middle coefficient is singular: imposing `e_(n/2)=0` removes only one real source degree while the low-harmonic image loses two, leaving exactly one real phase of local ambiguity. AF-335 makes this dimension statement sharp near a regular `2m`-gon: adjoining the product phase `E=prod z_j in S^1` is dimension-minimal as a continuous exact lift on that singular patch.

AF-336 identifies the opposite endpoint. If `q=floor(n/2)` and the full consecutive half-spectrum vanishes,

`s_1=...=s_q=0`,

then the entire unit-circle configuration is exactly a rotated regular `n`-gon. For every shorter consecutive block `s_1=...=s_r=0` with `r<q`, positive shape dimension remains after quotienting global rotation. Thus the transition is not a vague increase in rigidity: **the consecutive half-spectrum is the exact point at which the zero fibre collapses from positive-dimensional shape freedom to one global phase orbit.**

The product phase remains the surviving coordinate on that endpoint fibre. This also makes the prime-source obstruction exact: for distinct rational primes, a nonzero real time cannot place the prime phases on a rotated regular `n`-gon, because ratios of vertices would be roots of unity and hence force a forbidden multiplicative relation among distinct primes. So the maximally singular consecutive-half-spectrum branch has no nonzero prime-time incidence.

This does not close the current eight-prime middle-singular target. The condition `e_1=e_4=0` is much weaker than vanishing the four consecutive harmonics, and AF-335 shows precisely why one real phase remains missing after incidence. The durable separation is therefore: **half-spectrum vanishing is globally rigid and arithmetically impossible for the prime-time orbit, whereas the weaker even middle-singular fibre retains genuine shape freedom and still needs an independent joint-dependence obstruction.**

**Boundary.** AF-336 is an exact zero-fibre classification, not a conditioning theorem. Near the rigid endpoint, quantitative inverse stability can still degenerate, and nothing here supplies a uniform margin for near-incidence.
