# MI-027 — One-sided source constraints are governed by reachable bodies; exact and stable zero fidelity split at discrete saturation

**Evidence level:** exact for positive coordinate deletion, simplex normalization, fixed source-independent coordinate caps, fixed support cardinality, their joint cap/support law through AF-305, and the saturated equal-weight stability/no-zero-margin statements of AF-306. The exact zero classification of general saturated supports remains open outside the proved three-prime imaginary-axis case.

AF-300 shows that a complex-affine source family of intrinsic dimension `r` lowers the leverage bill from ambient dimension to source dimension, but every surviving complex direction remains target-visible. AF-301--AF-302 change category: forgotten positive coordinates generate a cone, and normalized fibers generate a convex body. Fidelity is therefore a target-relative question about whether the reachable fiber body meets the downstream failure set.

AF-303--AF-305 make the source-law boundary sharp. Fixed coefficient caps and fixed support cardinality do not separately protect zero-sensitive coordinate deletion. For normalized nonnegative sources with support at most `k` and coefficient cap `alpha`, the class is empty when `k alpha<1`; every strict-slack regime `k alpha>1` still admits recurrent same-fiber zero/safe collisions. Only exact saturation `k alpha=1` changes category: every admissible source is the uniform measure `(1/k)1_A` on a `k`-subset, so continuous barycentric freedom disappears.

AF-306 shows what that category change does and does not buy. For every `k>=2`, every fixed vertical line, and arbitrarily large height, two saturated uniform prime supports in one deletion fiber can have normalized pointwise zero margins arbitrarily close to `0` and `1`. Thus saturation does **not** restore a positive quantitative zero margin or a compression-only stability modulus.

At the same time exact zero behavior can genuinely improve. For three distinct primes on `Re(s)=0`, the equal-weight polynomial never vanishes: an exact zero would force an equilateral phase relation and hence a forbidden rational relation among prime logarithms. Kronecker density nevertheless drives the same polynomial arbitrarily close to zero at arbitrarily large heights. The zero set can therefore be empty while the distance of the value set to zero is zero.

The reusable lesson is stronger than “discrete restrictions matter.” **Exact failure membership and stable distance from failure are separate currencies.** Saturation can remove continuous exact-cancellation mechanisms without creating any conditioning buffer. A proposed RH bridge must declare which currency its destination theorem consumes before source simplicity is credited as fidelity.

**Boundary.** AF-306 gives a family-level stable-fidelity obstruction for all saturated `k>=2` and an exact zero-free theorem only for three equal prime terms on the imaginary axis. It does not classify exact zeros for `k>=4`, for general `Re(s)`, prescribed support systems, multiplicative/nonlinear source laws, vector observations, or compact height windows. Those cases still require their own reachable-set and target-margin analysis.
