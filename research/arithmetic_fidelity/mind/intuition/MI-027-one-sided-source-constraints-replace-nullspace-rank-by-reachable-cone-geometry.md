# MI-027 — One-sided and normalized source restrictions are governed by fiberwise reachable bodies

**Evidence level:** exact for positive coordinate deletion through AF-302. Extensions to discrete, sparse, multiplicatively coupled, nonlinear, support-constrained, or fixed-window source classes remain open.

AF-300 shows that a complex-affine source family of intrinsic dimension `r` really lowers the leverage bill from the ambient `N^(-1/2)` scale to `r^(-1/2)`, but every complex direction that remains admissible is still target-visible. AF-301 changes category: with nonnegative coefficients, forgotten coordinates no longer expose a linear correction space but the positive cone generated by their Dirichlet response rays. A retained state can reach failure exactly when its negative response lies in that cone.

AF-302 identifies the normalized version exactly. On the coefficient simplex, for retained state `y`, remaining mass `M(y)=1-||y||_1`, retained response `r_S(y;z)`, and discarded response hull `K_D(z)=conv{d^{-z}:d in D}`, the whole fiber is

`r_S(y;z) + M(y) K_D(z)`.

Thus normalization is a real resource bound, not a cosmetic restriction: every failure completion must satisfy `|r_S| <= M R_D`, while if `0` lies in the interior of `K_D` then every retained response inside the corresponding `M rho_D` ball is failure-completable. But total mass alone does not restore exact fidelity. If three discarded indices have Q-independent logarithms—three distinct primes suffice—Kronecker-Weyl recurrence gives arbitrarily large heights where `0` lies in the interior of their response hull. The `y=0` fiber then contains both a zero-producing normalized source and a safe monomial source with the same compressed image.

The reusable invariant is therefore **the target-relative reachable set of each compressed fiber together with its available source budget**. Signed linear spaces, positive cones and normalized convex bodies are different geometries, but the fidelity test is the same: does the fiberwise reachable set intersect the target failure set? A source restriction becomes materially stronger only when its intrinsic constraints prevent that intersection at the scale and observation family actually used downstream.

**Boundary.** AF-302 treats coordinate deletion of a finite positive simplex and exact point/zero-count discrimination over unbounded height. It does not rule out coefficient caps, integrality, sparsity, multiplicative relations, nonlinear source varieties, support restrictions, or a prescribed finite contour. Those cases require their own reachable body, not extrapolation from positivity or normalization alone.
