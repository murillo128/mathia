# MI-042 — Finite-prefix and scalar-counting fidelity both need a structural source class

**Evidence level:** exact-derived boundary statement from [AF-366](../../findings/AF-366-finite-prefix-observations-need-tail-class-for-dirichlet-pole-fidelity.md), strengthened inside positive generalized-prime categories by [AF-367](../../findings/AF-367-finite-prime-prefix-plus-square-root-beurling-regularity-does-not-determine-zero-geometry.md) and [AF-368](../../findings/AF-368-logarithmic-integer-counting-proximity-does-not-identify-rational-primes.md), complementary to the global Riesz principal-part theorem AF-365.

AF-366 isolates unrestricted completion freedom. A finite observation depending on the first `N` Dirichlet coefficients is blind to tails that vanish on that prefix yet insert prescribed poles in the critical strip. Arbitrarily long exact finite observations therefore do not determine meromorphic principal parts without an admissible tail class.

AF-367 shows that this is not merely an artifact of arbitrary signed coefficients. For every finite cutoff, positive Beurling generalized-prime systems can agree exactly with the ordinary rational primes and induced integers below that cutoff, satisfy near-square-root generalized-integer counting, and nevertheless have different Beurling-RH zero geometry. Finite exact arithmetic data plus positivity, multiplicativity and that global counting class remain target-mixed.

AF-368 moves the source-side obstruction much closer to the ordinary integers. For every `c>0`, Olofsson's theorem supplies a nonordinary Beurling prime system `Q` with

`|N_Q(x)-floor(x)| < c log x`.

Thus **no fixed positive logarithmic scalar counting envelope identifies the rational-prime source** inside the Beurling category, even when its constant is arbitrarily small. This is a different discriminator from AF-367: AF-368 does not prove different RH status or zero geometry for the matched system; it proves that extraordinarily accurate global scalar counting still fails to recover ordinary-prime provenance.

The complementary recovery currency remains structural. A coefficient-tail bound `|a_n-b_n| <= M n^(theta-1)` makes the Dirichlet-transform difference holomorphic on `Re s>theta`, so principal parts there agree. On the arithmetic side, exact support, factorization structure, cancellation laws, functional-equation coupling or some genuinely stronger rigidity theorem can carry information that scalar `N(x)` proximity does not. Lagarias' Delone classification illustrates the qualitative jump: exact integer support plus Delone structure collapses Beurling freedom to finite arithmetic modifications, even though it still does not by itself force the exact ordinary system.

The reusable rule is therefore stricter than “preserve many primes and tighten the counting error.” Before inferring a pole, zero or source discriminator, specify the admissible source category and prove that the retained data are constant on the relevant completion fibres. Progressively smaller fixed multiples of `log x` are still on the wrong side of that gate. A scalar-counting route would need a genuinely stronger regime, such as a proved sublogarithmic rigidity theorem, rather than merely a better constant.

**Boundary.** AF-368 is a source-identity obstruction, not an RH counterexample. It does not show that exact generalized-integer support, the rational-prime Euler product, a zeta functional equation, or a stronger cancellation/continuation law can be varied independently. Those are precisely the structural source resources that remain live.
