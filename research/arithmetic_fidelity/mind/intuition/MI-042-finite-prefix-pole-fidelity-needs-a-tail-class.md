# MI-042 — Fixed-source fidelity is governed by completable secants, not by the barycenter of the residual Radon circuit

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from AF-449, the correction in [AF-451](../../findings/AF-451-common-backgrounds-transport-radon-collisions-across-source-law-fibers.md), and the conditioning identity in [AF-452](../../findings/AF-452-difference-norm-conditioning-descends-to-completed-source-law-secants.md).

AF-451 corrects an important overreach in AF-450. If two positive sparse measures in the same prescribed source-law fiber have equal observations, cancelling their common mass produces disjoint positive Jordan sides with the same observation barycenter. Their source-law barycenter, however, need not equal the barycenter of the original measures. The cancelled common background carries part of the source statistic, so a residual Radon circuit cannot be assigned to a source fiber from its own label alone.

The exact source-relative object is a **completed Radon core**. A disjoint probability pair `alpha,beta` with the same observation image is relevant to source law `u` only when there exist a common background `rho` and a mixing weight `tau` such that `tau alpha+(1-tau)rho` and `tau beta+(1-tau)rho` both satisfy the declared source law and sparsity budget. Completion can move a core between source-law fibers and can consume atoms, so the background is part of the admissibility geometry even though it cancels from the observed difference.

AF-452 shows why this correction is also the right conditioning language. For difference-homogeneous source norms, normalization of those completed cores gives exactly the normalized secant carrier of the physical source fiber, and the inverse lower Lipschitz modulus is the minimum observation gain on that carrier. Exact nonidentifiability is zero gain; poor conditioning is an approaching zero-gain sequence. The geometry relevant to stability is therefore not “all circuits with barycenter `u`” but “all circuits that can actually be completed into two admissible points of the `u` fiber.”

The metric hypothesis is load-bearing. The cancellation/completion quotient behaves cleanly for homogeneous difference norms such as total variation, bounded-Lipschitz, MMD and `W_1`, but AF-452 gives an explicit `W_p`, `p>1`, scaling failure. A fidelity claim must therefore state the source metric before importing this secant reduction.

**Boundary.** These findings classify the exact collision and conditioning carrier; they do not prove a positive arithmetic lower gain for the observation map. Establishing such a gain, or constructing a sequence of admissible completed secants with gain tending to zero, is the live quantitative problem.
