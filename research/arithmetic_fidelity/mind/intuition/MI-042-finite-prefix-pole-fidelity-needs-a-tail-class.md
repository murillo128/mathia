# MI-042 — Finite-prefix pole fidelity is source-class and provenance dependent before conditioning can even be priced

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [AF-439](../../findings/AF-439-finite-moment-data-do-not-identify-movable-positive-stieltjes-supports.md)--[AF-443](../../findings/AF-443-finite-moment-families-cannot-protect-remote-marks-against-equal-latent-support-dimension.md), interpreted against the exact reciprocal-square carrier of AF-436--AF-438.

AF-439 shows that finite moment vectors do not identify a nearby positive Stieltjes source when support locations may move. AF-440 gives the complementary rigid theorem: on the exact reciprocal-square skeleton, a separated finite mark alphabet makes one sufficiently deep moment superseparating enough to recover a linearly deep mark prefix. AF-441 shows that small geometric perturbations do not destroy this when the realized support coordinate is retained as provenance.

AF-442 proves that coarse provenance is not enough for the one-moment mechanism. A remote mark flip can be hidden exactly by an exponentially smaller motion of an earlier latent support point while that point remains in the same shell cell. High order therefore improves separation on a fixed skeleton while simultaneously making latent support compensation cheaper.

AF-443 generalizes the obstruction from one nuisance coordinate to a finite family. For any fixed `K` distinct reciprocal-power moments, choose `K` independently movable earlier supports. Their local moment Jacobian is a nonsingular generalized Vandermonde matrix, so the implicit-function theorem gives a local fibre that compensates an arbitrarily remote mark flip while preserving all `K` moments and all shell labels. The required support displacements vanish as the target shell recedes.

The durable lesson is a **rank-versus-latent-dimension gate**. Several independent moments count as new fidelity only when they outrun or structurally constrain the nuisance degrees of freedom admitted by the source class. Observation rank equal to effective latent support dimension can still leave the target mark completely nonidentified. Realized relational provenance, a lower-dimensional source deformation law, an observation family whose rank grows faster than the nuisance class, or another source-derived constraint can evade this obstruction; merely adding a fixed number of moments cannot.

**Boundary.** AF-443 is a local smooth nonidentifiability theorem for a source class with independently movable supports. It does not prove that observation-rank surplus is sufficient, does not cover every constrained or discrete deformation model, and does not rule out growing moment families. Conditioning remains a separate question after identifiability has actually been established.
