# MI-042 — Fixed-source fidelity is governed by completable secants and a topology-compatible source metric

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from AF-449, the correction in [AF-451](../../findings/AF-451-common-backgrounds-transport-radon-collisions-across-source-law-fibers.md), the conditioning identity in [AF-452](../../findings/AF-452-difference-norm-conditioning-descends-to-completed-source-law-secants.md), and the topology obstruction in [AF-453](../../findings/AF-453-weakly-collapsing-secants-force-zero-total-variation-conditioning.md).

AF-451 corrects the residual-circuit picture: cancelling common mass from two equal-observation measures exposes a Radon core, but the core need not itself carry the prescribed source-law barycenter. A core is relevant to a fixed source law only when a common positive background and mixing weight complete both Jordan sides into admissible sources without violating the atom budget. AF-452 then identifies the inverse lower Lipschitz modulus, for difference-homogeneous source metrics, with the minimum observation gain on the normalized carrier of those completable secants.

AF-453 adds a topological gate before any arithmetic lower-bound search. If the admissible fiber contains secants `mu_n-nu_n` that converge weakly to zero while their total-variation norm stays bounded below, every finite continuous observation has vanishing normalized gain on that sequence. The elementary witness is a moving atom inside a non-discrete exact source-law level set: `delta_{x_n}-delta_x` has TV norm `2` while every continuous observation difference tends to zero. Exact injectivity on each pair does not help; the uniform TV conditioning constant is still zero.

Hence a positive fidelity margin requires both **completion admissibility** and **topological compatibility** between the declared source metric and the observation class. Total variation remains viable only when the physical source law excludes weakly collapsing motions, for example through a genuinely discrete/quantized support class. If continuous location perturbations are admissible, a weak metric such as `W_1` or bounded-Lipschitz can avoid this automatic obstruction; for Dirac masses the relevant gain becomes the lower-Lipschitz ratio `||Phi(x)-Phi(y)||/d(x,y)` on the admissible source set.

**Boundary.** AF-453 is not arithmetic evidence and does not prove a positive gain in any weak metric. It removes an impossible target: finite continuous measurements cannot uniformly control unrestricted moving atomic sources in total variation. The remaining arithmetic problem is to fix the physically justified source topology and then estimate the observation gain on the corresponding completed source-law secant carrier.
