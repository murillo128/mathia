# MI-042 — Fixed-source TV fidelity requires completable secants and source-relative noncompactness throughout the chain

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from AF-449, the correction in [AF-451](../../findings/AF-451-common-backgrounds-transport-radon-collisions-across-source-law-fibers.md), the conditioning identity in [AF-452](../../findings/AF-452-difference-norm-conditioning-descends-to-completed-source-law-secants.md), the topology obstruction in [AF-453](../../findings/AF-453-weakly-collapsing-secants-force-zero-total-variation-conditioning.md), the discrete-output capacity theorem [AF-454](../../findings/AF-454-bounded-finite-observations-cannot-be-tv-coercive-on-infinite-discrete-alphabets.md), and the compact-postprocessing obstruction [AF-455](../../findings/AF-455-compact-postprocessing-erases-bounded-infinite-tv-marks.md).

AF-451 corrects the residual-circuit picture: cancelling common mass from two equal-observation measures exposes a Radon core, but that core matters for a fixed source law only when a common positive background and mixing weight complete both Jordan sides into admissible sources. AF-452 then identifies the inverse lower Lipschitz modulus, for difference-homogeneous source metrics, with the minimum observation gain on the normalized carrier of those completable secants.

AF-453 adds the source-topology gate. Weakly collapsing admissible secants of nonvanishing TV size force every finite continuous observation to have zero normalized TV gain. AF-454 adds the output-capacity gate: on an infinite one-atom alphabet, positive TV conditioning is equivalent to a uniformly discrete observation image. A bounded finite-dimensional image is totally bounded, so its uniform margin vanishes; finite windows pay the corresponding packing bound.

AF-455 shows that the bounded infinite-dimensional escape is not stable under arbitrary downstream processing. If `Phi(A)` is bounded and `K` is compact, then `K Phi(A)` is precompact and `kappa_A^TV(K Phi)=0`. For an orthonormal one-hot carrier and bounded `L:H->Y`,
`kappa_e^TV(L) <= ||L||_e/sqrt(2)`, so a TV margin `c` forces `||L||_e >= sqrt(2)c`. This is only a necessary resource: projection onto the even coordinates has essential norm one but zero one-hot margin because all odd labels collide.

The reusable point is therefore stronger than “infinite dimension can rescue discreteness.” Uniform TV fidelity on an infinite bounded source requires non-precompactness to survive every downstream stage **and** the surviving noncompact directions to separate the actual completable source secants. Essential norm measures distance from compact collapse for the one-hot model, but it is not a source-relative restricted minimum modulus.

**Boundary.** AF-455 does not identify a sufficient noncompact operator class for arithmetic fidelity, and it does not rule out compact processing under weaker source metrics or on finite source windows. The remaining problem is to identify a physical source class and metric together with a downstream operator geometry that retains a positive lower gain on its completed secants rather than merely retaining generic infinite-dimensional capacity.
