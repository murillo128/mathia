# MI-042 — Finite-prefix pole fidelity is governed by source-law-filtered collision circuits before conditioning can be priced

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [AF-439](../../findings/AF-439-finite-moment-data-do-not-identify-movable-positive-stieltjes-supports.md)--[AF-450](../../findings/AF-450-fixed-source-laws-localize-affine-radon-collisions.md), interpreted against the exact reciprocal-square carrier of AF-436--AF-438.

AF-439--AF-443 show the basic rank obstruction. A fixed finite moment family can be locally absorbed by enough movable positive support coordinates, even while coarse shell labels are preserved. Rigid support or retained realized provenance can restore fidelity, so the obstruction belongs to the admitted source class rather than to moments in isolation.

AF-444--AF-447 sharpen the positive finite-atomic side. Unit-weight atoms, unknown positive weights, known total mass and more general Chebyshev observation systems have different exact thresholds because the source laws change the available deformation and the algebraic certificate exposed by surplus observations. In particular, known mass is not merely another number in a dimension count: it changes the collision geometry and can save one positive-moment channel.

AF-448--AF-450 identify the invariant behind those examples. Positive fidelity depends on which signed kernel relations have admissible positive and negative parts. With fixed mass the problem is affine/Radon rather than conic. With additional affine source statistics, every balanced collision carries a barycentric label `u`, and only circuits with the prescribed label can destroy fidelity in that source-law fiber. Global augmented rank or circuit tests are therefore worst-fiber statements; they can be strictly too pessimistic for one fixed source law.

The durable gate is consequently **source class -> oriented collision geometry -> source-law fiber -> exact certificate -> quantitative margin**. Rank versus latent dimension remains a useful warning, but it is only a preliminary surrogate for the actual admissible circuit geometry.

The remaining difficulty is robustness. AF-444--AF-446 already show exact class separation whose margin collapses as a remote atom disappears. AF-450 is exact/noiseless and deliberately does not turn distance from the source-law collision locus into an intrinsic condition number. A useful arithmetic continuation must define the physical metric, quantify near-collisions within the fixed fiber and show that the resulting margin survives transport to the destination scale.

**Boundary.** These results cover positive finite-atomic sources with linear measurements and affine source laws. Nonlinear constraints need not admit a barycentric circuit description. A target-dependent source law can trivially encode the answer and receives no arithmetic credit. Exact exclusion of sparse Radon circuits does not imply stable recovery, and none of the results identifies a rational-prime discriminator or proves RH.
