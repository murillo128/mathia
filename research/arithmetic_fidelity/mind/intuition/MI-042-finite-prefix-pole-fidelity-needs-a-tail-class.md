# MI-042 — Full-simplex TV fidelity exposes a target-geometry gate beyond atomwise noncompactness

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from AF-449, the correction in [AF-451](../../findings/AF-451-common-backgrounds-transport-radon-collisions-across-source-law-fibers.md), the conditioning identity in [AF-452](../../findings/AF-452-difference-norm-conditioning-descends-to-completed-source-law-secants.md), the topology obstruction in [AF-453](../../findings/AF-453-weakly-collapsing-secants-force-zero-total-variation-conditioning.md), the discrete-output capacity theorem [AF-454](../../findings/AF-454-bounded-finite-observations-cannot-be-tv-coercive-on-infinite-discrete-alphabets.md), the compact-postprocessing obstruction [AF-455](../../findings/AF-455-compact-postprocessing-erases-bounded-infinite-tv-marks.md), and the full-simplex classification [AF-456](../../findings/AF-456-full-simplex-tv-fidelity-requires-l1-geometry.md).

AF-451--AF-452 identify the fixed-source stability carrier: completable source-law secants, with inverse lower Lipschitz modulus equal to the minimum observation gain on their normalized carrier. AF-453 adds the source-topology gate, AF-454 the output-packing gate for an infinite discrete alphabet, and AF-455 the downstream gate: compact post-processing destroys any bounded infinite atomwise TV margin, while positive essential norm alone still does not guarantee separation of the physical secants.

AF-456 shows that even perfect atomwise separation is not the end of the story once the admissible source class contains arbitrary mixtures. For a countably infinite alphabet `A` and a bounded linear/barycentric observation `T:l1(A)->Y`, the full-simplex TV modulus is exactly the lower gain of `T` on the zero-mass subspace `l1_0(A)`. For a given `T`, positive full-simplex TV fidelity therefore means that `T|l1_0(A)` is bounded below. Such an observation exists into `Y` exactly when `Y` contains an isomorphic copy of `l1`; every reflexive target, in particular every Hilbert space, has zero uniform full-simplex TV margin.

This separates three different resources that should not be conflated. A non-precompact carrier can keep infinitely many atoms uniformly separated. A fixed `k`-sparse mixture class can still be stable in Hilbert geometry, with the canonical one-hot map having the sharp modulus `1/sqrt(2k)`. But uniform TV fidelity over mixtures of unbounded complexity requires `l1`-type target geometry, not merely infinite dimension or noncompactness.

The reusable audit is therefore source-class relative. First identify the completed secants generated by the actual admissible mixtures; then ask whether the retained target/operator category can be bounded below on that carrier through the whole downstream chain. Atomwise noncompactness, essential norm and injectivity are only partial resources unless they control those source-law secants at the required complexity.

**Boundary.** AF-456 is a bounded linear/barycentric TV statement on the full countable probability simplex. It does not exclude nonlinear encoders, weaker source metrics, finite alphabets, or bounded-sparsity families. The remaining arithmetic problem is to state the physical source class and metric precisely, then prove a positive restricted lower gain in a target geometry that is not already ruled out by these category constraints.
