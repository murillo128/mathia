# MI-042 — Finite-prefix pole fidelity is source-class and provenance dependent before conditioning can even be priced

**Evidence level:** exact-derived boundary synthesis from [AF-366](../../findings/AF-366-finite-prefix-observations-need-tail-class-for-dirichlet-pole-fidelity.md), [AF-367](../../findings/AF-367-finite-prime-prefix-plus-square-root-beurling-regularity-does-not-determine-zero-geometry.md), [AF-368](../../findings/AF-368-logarithmic-integer-counting-proximity-does-not-identify-rational-primes.md), the category-specific recovery theorems [AF-369](../../findings/AF-369-integer-supported-delone-zero-divisors-universal-residue-restores-prime-identity.md) and [AF-370](../../findings/AF-370-hamburger-source-class-functional-equation-rigidity.md), and the finite-moment boundary [AF-439](../../findings/AF-439-finite-euler-moment-prefixes-have-local-pole-moving-fibers.md)--[AF-442](../../findings/AF-442-cell-labels-do-not-protect-single-moment-mark-fidelity.md).

AF-366--AF-368 isolate completion freedom: arbitrarily long exact finite observations and strong ambient regularity can still leave the source or zero geometry undetermined. AF-369 and AF-370 show how that ambiguity can disappear once the admissible source category is narrowed enough.

AF-439 gives the negative finite-moment side directly in the Euler/Stieltjes language. For every fixed `N`, the first `N` moments are locally noninjective among nearby positive finite unit-residue measures when support positions are latent: perturbing the constant elementary-symmetric coefficient of `N+1` nodes preserves the first `N` power sums while moving every pole.

AF-440 supplies the complementary positive theorem on the exact reciprocal-square support with a separated finite mark alphabet. Equality of a sufficiently deep moment forces a linearly deep prefix of marks to agree and gives an explicit dominance margin, separating exact identifiability from numerical conditioning.

AF-441 shows that the real boundary is finer than “fixed support versus jitter.” If the displaced support is retained with its realized source coordinate, the first differing mark remains detectable to linear depth under the finding's exact bounds. If support positions become latent and only their unmarked moment prefix is retained, a local Prony/Newton fibre reappears.

AF-442 rules out a natural intermediate shortcut for the **single high moment**. Retaining only the shell/cell containing each support point is not enough: a later mark change at shell `m` can be cancelled exactly by moving one earlier latent support point at shell `j<m` inside its unchanged cell. The required movement is exponentially small, of order `(j/m)^(2N)/N`. Thus increasing moment order can strengthen discrete mark separation on a shared skeleton while simultaneously making latent-coordinate compensation cheaper.

The reusable lesson is: **finite-prefix fidelity depends on the source coordinates that remain fixed or observable, not merely on perturbation size or nominal sampling depth.** Identifiability asks whether the admissible fibre contains one target; conditioning asks how perturbations are amplified only after that fibre is target-pure. A high-order observable can improve the first question in one source category and worsen it in another.

The next useful boundary theorem should therefore test provenance lifts that genuinely remove the support/mark trade rather than merely localize it: several independent moments, a constrained low-dimensional deformation law, relational support data that determines realized positions, or another canonical source-derived constraint. Each candidate should first be checked against explicit compensated collisions before inverse conditioning is priced.

**Boundary.** AF-442 is an exact noninjectivity result for one scalar high moment with one latent support coordinate; it does not show that cell provenance fails for every richer compression. AF-439 and the latent half of AF-441 allow movable finite positive supports, while AF-440 and the positive half of AF-441 retain stronger support information. None settles noisy moments, continuous residue amplitudes, or the minimal richer observation needed for recovery. No zero selection or RH consequence follows.
