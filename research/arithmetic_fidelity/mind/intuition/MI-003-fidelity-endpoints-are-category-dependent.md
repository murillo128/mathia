# MI-003 — Exact source category, local depth, global breadth, and metric fidelity are different claims

**Evidence level:** supported by exact counterseparations

## Core intuition

Exact analytic-source membership can be rigid without being a robust metric certificate. Even exact Bohr recurrence, Riemann-type reflection symmetry, exact ordinate counts, and support on a single rational-prime-power ray can coexist with an infinite off-critical transport. The decisive information lies in the **coefficient/divisor law inside the category**, and its cost splits further into local depth, global prime breadth, and stability.

The correction forced by the current evidence is important: finite-prefix complexity belongs to the **cancelled quotient being audited**, not separately to each representation.

## Strongest justified claim

AF-266--AF-270 show that every nontrivial finite-total zero surgery exits the exact Bohr almost-periodic source category, while an infinite periodic reflection-symmetric transport can remain inside it and preserve one prime-power frequency ray.

AF-272 supplies the corrected finite certificate. If the cancelled quotient has `R` distinct signed divisor nodes, its first `R` prime-power coefficients detect every nontrivial divisor change. If two controls each have complexity at most `r`, their quotient can have complexity `2r`, so agreement through depth `2r` rather than `r` is the general pairwise guarantee. Exact detection still has no uniform metric gap without node separation.

AF-273 shows that reflection symmetry and exact ordinate matching do not cap this complexity. Cyclotomic splits of orbit size `M` can hide every coefficient defect through depth `M-1`; no fixed source prefix is universally faithful while local divisor degree may grow.

AF-274 identifies the complementary positive boundary. Inside a fixed local Euler degree class `E_d`, the first `d` prime-power coefficients determine the complete local factor by Newton--Girard, and depth `d` is sharp. For the zeta degree-one local category, one coefficient at each prime fixes the entire local ray. This finite **depth** result does not remove the separate need for infinitely many prime locations: an unobserved prime remains a free global modification.

## Synthesis of evidence

Fidelity now has separable currencies: exact category membership, complexity of the difference object, local coefficient depth, global prime breadth, and quantitative stability. The AF-273 hidden-prefix construction pays unbounded local Euler degree, while AF-274 shows that bounded degree collapses that depth bill exactly.

A useful zeta-source theorem should therefore state which complexity is bounded and where. If the intended category is genuinely degree one at every prime, the hard problem is no longer deep prime-power recovery; it is enforcing the correct law across the unbounded prime breadth without assuming the Euler product conclusion.

## Counterevidence / boundary cases

AF-270 and AF-273 are engineered controls, not zeta models. They prove insufficiency of recurrence, reflection, counts, and ray support, not existence of an off-critical object satisfying the full zeta coefficient law.

Exact finite-depth fidelity is also not automatically stable: nearby divisor nodes can make a nontrivial quotient arbitrarily small on every fixed coefficient window unless geometric separation is supplied.

## Epistemic status

**Supported category/depth/breadth/stability boundary:** exact recurrence excludes finite-total transport but not infinite periodic transport; bounded cancelled-quotient complexity gives a finite certificate; fixed local Euler degree gives a sharp depth certificate; global Euler fidelity still requires unbounded prime breadth; stable recovery needs a quantitative separation modulus.

## Novelty/prior-art status

The almost-periodic, exponential-polynomial, Vandermonde/Prony, cyclotomic, and Newton--Girard ingredients retain their finding-level prior-art classifications. This note records their current information hierarchy.

## Falsification criterion

Construct a nontrivial cancelled quotient of support `R` whose first `R` source coefficients vanish, invalidate the AF-273 reflection-symmetric hidden-prefix family, or find two degree-`d` local Euler factors agreeing through depth `d` but differing as rational functions.
