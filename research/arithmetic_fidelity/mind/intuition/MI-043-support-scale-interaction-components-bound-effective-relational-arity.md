# MI-043 — Support-scale interaction components bound effective relational arity

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [AF-372](../../findings/AF-372-finite-order-total-positivity-does-not-determine-laguerre-polya.md), [AF-376](../../findings/AF-376-uniform-separation-defeats-e-polya-frequency-determination.md) through [AF-379](../../findings/AF-379-vanishing-row-mesh-determines-continuous-translation-total-positivity.md), and the two-sided/local-generator refinements [AF-381](../../findings/AF-381-prime-ratio-minors-determine-translation-total-nonnegativity.md) and [AF-382](../../findings/AF-382-adjacent-prime-ratio-minors-determine-order-two-total-nonnegativity.md).

Nominal access to determinants of every order does not imply genuinely unbounded relational information. For a compact/local translation kernel with support width `w`, the intrinsic graph `G_w(E)` joins sampled rows only when their translates can interact. If its connected components have size at most `r`, every sampled Toeplitz determinant factors into blocks of order at most `r`; all nominal determinant orders are then decided by bounded-order data. AF-377 makes this obstruction survive even when the minimum spacing of `E` vanishes.

The converse resource is not merely small gaps but **finite-pattern closure**. AF-379 shows that density of normalized sampled row patterns determines every continuous one-sided translation minor. AF-381 strengthens this to joint normalized row/column patterns: if arbitrary finite row and column configurations can be approximated after one common translation, fully sampled `E x E` minors determine the entire real translation target. Vanishing tail mesh is a clean sufficient condition for both closures, and `E={log p}` has that property by the prime number theorem. Thus fully prime-ratio sampling is target-complete even though the displayed coordinates remain arithmetic.

AF-382 exposes a second, independent notion of relational complexity: **placement-generator complexity at a fixed determinant order**. For strictly positive sampled profiles, adjacent order-two inequalities become additive mixed differences after taking logarithms. Summing those local differences over a rectangle reconstructs every nonadjacent sampled `2x2` inequality exactly. On a vanishing-mesh tail, continuity then promotes the local successor-prime tests to global `TN_2`, equivalently log-concavity.

This creates a useful two-axis audit. Determinant order measures how many points participate in one target relation; generator complexity measures how many placements must actually be checked once composition laws inside that order are used. AF-372 says the first axis is genuinely unbounded for unrestricted `PF_infinity`. AF-382 says the second axis collapses dramatically at order two: arbitrary prime-ratio rectangles carry no more target information than adjacent successor-prime rectangles.

The reusable hierarchy is therefore: dense generated differences may kill a separable gauge; unbounded support-scale components prevent bounded-block aliases; dense normalized finite patterns remove geometric sampling aliases; and, *within a fixed order*, algebraic composition may compress the placement family even further. None of these resources implies the next. In particular, adjacent `2x2` recognition must not be extrapolated to all-order Pólya-frequency structure without a separate theorem handling higher minors and their zero/support patterns.

For sampled positivity arguments ask, in order: what source relation and localization scale are intrinsic; whether a coordinate change transports that source category or replaces it; what interaction graph the source induces; what determinant order the destination truly requires; whether normalized row/joint patterns are dense enough to determine that order; and whether the remaining placement family has an intrinsic local generating set. Only after those questions are settled is it meaningful to ask where arithmetic specificity survives.

For prime logs, AF-381--AF-382 relocate that specificity entirely into **provenance of the inequalities**. Prime symbols in a target-complete grid do not make the final positivity arithmetic, and at order two even the large prime-pair family is redundant. A source-selective route must explain why the required local inequalities are forced by arithmetic in a way matched vanishing-mesh controls cannot fake, or use source restrictions that change the determining problem itself.

**Boundary / falsification.** The AF-382 compression uses strict positivity to take logarithms and continuity only for the final grid-to-continuum passage. It is an order-two theorem. To overturn the present hierarchy, either exhibit a sampled all-order certificate whose intrinsic support components stay bounded yet still determines unrestricted translation `PF_infinity`, or prove a higher-order local generator theorem under the actual nonnegative/zero-pattern hypotheses of the RH-facing profile. Coordinate densification counts only when the declared source category and translation relation are preserved rather than redrawn.
