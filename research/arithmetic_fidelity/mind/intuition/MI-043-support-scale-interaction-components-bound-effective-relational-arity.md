# MI-043 — Support-scale interaction components bound effective relational arity

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [AF-372](../../findings/AF-372-finite-order-total-positivity-does-not-determine-laguerre-polya.md), [AF-376](../../findings/AF-376-uniform-separation-defeats-e-polya-frequency-determination.md) through [AF-379](../../findings/AF-379-vanishing-row-mesh-determines-continuous-translation-total-positivity.md), and the two-sided/generator refinements [AF-381](../../findings/AF-381-prime-ratio-minors-determine-translation-total-nonnegativity.md), [AF-382](../../findings/AF-382-adjacent-prime-ratio-minors-determine-order-two-total-nonnegativity.md), and [AF-383](../../findings/AF-383-vanishing-lattice-meshes-reduce-continuum-total-nonnegativity-to-one-axis-toeplitz-generators.md).

Nominal access to determinants of every order does not imply genuinely unbounded relational information. For a compact/local translation kernel with support width `w`, the intrinsic graph `G_w(E)` joins sampled rows only when their translates can interact. If its connected components have size at most `r`, every sampled Toeplitz determinant factors into blocks of order at most `r`; AF-377 makes this obstruction survive even when the minimum spacing of `E` vanishes.

The converse resource is finite-pattern closure. AF-379 shows that density of normalized sampled row patterns determines every continuous one-sided translation minor. AF-381 strengthens this to joint normalized row/column patterns: if arbitrary finite row and column configurations can be approximated after one common translation, fully sampled `E x E` minors determine the entire real translation target. Vanishing tail mesh is a sufficient condition, and `E={log p}` has it.

Placement complexity is a different axis from determinant order. AF-382 gives the strongest collapse at order two: for strictly positive profiles, logarithms convert adjacent `2x2` inequalities into mixed differences whose rectangle sums generate every sampled order-two inequality, so successor-prime rectangles already determine global `TN_2` on a vanishing mesh.

AF-383 gives the robust higher-order replacement. For every fixed order `k` and every lattice mesh `h`, a Toeplitz minor decomposition expresses **every** order-`k` minor as a nonnegative integer combination of minors whose rows are one consecutive `k`-block while the columns remain arbitrary. Translation invariance removes the starting row index, and a sequence `h_m -> 0` plus continuity closes the resulting lattice certificate to continuum `TN_k`. Therefore one entire spatial tuple is algebraically redundant at every order; the other tuple is not.

This yields a three-resource decomposition for unrestricted translation total positivity:

- **relational arity:** unbounded determinant order remains necessary by AF-372;
- **placement generators:** at each fixed order one row tuple can be fixed to a consecutive stencil by AF-383;
- **continuum resolution:** a vanishing mesh is still needed to recover unsampled placements for a general continuous profile.

At order two strict positivity adds a fourth simplification: arbitrary columns also collapse to nearest-neighbor generators by AF-382. That extra collapse should not be extrapolated upward. The fixed-order Toeplitz decomposition used in AF-383 explicitly retains arbitrary columns, and fully consecutive higher-order minors are not a complete nonnegative certificate without additional strictness hypotheses.

For sampled positivity arguments the useful audit is now concrete: determine the destination order, the support-scale interaction components, the normalized-pattern closure, and the smallest **positive generator cone** for placements at that order. Raw inequality count is not an information measure. An omitted family carries no extra target information when every omitted relation lies in the positive cone generated by retained ones, but unbounded order can remain essential even after enormous spatial compression.

For prime logs this relocates arithmetic specificity into the provenance of the generator inequalities. Prime symbols in a target-complete vanishing grid do not make the final positivity arithmetic. A source-selective route must explain why the RH-facing source forces the one-axis generator family, or prove source hypotheses strong enough to compress the remaining arbitrary-column geometry.

**Boundary.** AF-383 is source-independent and uses continuity only for grid-to-continuum closure. It does not reduce determinant order, does not justify fully consecutive higher-order tests, and does not provide the arithmetic sign of any generator. AF-382's two-axis adjacency reduction additionally uses strict positivity/logarithmic Monge structure and remains an order-two phenomenon unless a separate higher-order theorem is proved.
