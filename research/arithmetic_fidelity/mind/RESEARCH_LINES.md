AF-328 reduces the unresolved middle-singular target to the exact common-zero question `F(it)=G(it)=0`; AF-329 then proves that dense near-incidence destroys every positive joint margin and that coprimality does not yield a finite exponential-sum Bézout certificate.

AF-330 shows that any exact eight-prime hit forces the common parameter `-it` into `ecl_C(empty)`. AF-331 closes the tempting stability/genericity use of that restriction: the corresponding real exceptional-time set is countable but dense, contains `2*pi*Q`, has dense complement, and has zero distance from every real time. AF-332 adds that at every nonzero real time no three distinct prime phases can all be algebraic, so a hypothetical eight-prime hit has at least six transcendental coordinates.

AF-333 gives the first joint algebraic-dimension closure, but only conditionally. Assuming Schanuel, every nonzero common-time phase tuple from multiplicatively independent positive algebraic bases has algebraic codimension at most one. On the unit torus, however, `e_1=0` also forces the independent reciprocal relation `e_(n-1)=0` for `n>=3`. A zero therefore consumes at least two algebraic codimensions, contradicting the Schanuel budget. For the eight-prime target, Schanuel already forbids the first equation `e_1=0`; `e_4=0` is unnecessary.

AF-335 independently closes the information-size ambiguity of the even middle-singular fibre. Near a regular `2m`-gon, `e_m=0` removes one real source degree, while the low-harmonic image loses two real output degrees because the product phase `E` has been forgotten. Hence the harmonic compression has one-real-dimensional local fibres on a nonempty singular patch, and adjoining `E in S^1` is dimension-minimal as a continuous exact lift there. This does **not** address whether the one-real prime-time orbit hits that singular stratum; it makes the incidence problem cleaner by proving that post-incidence information loss is exactly one real phase, not the missing arithmetic obstruction.

The unconditional question is correspondingly sharper: **can one rule out two independent polynomial relations on the common prime-phase tuple without Schanuel, or obtain another source-specific theorem strong enough to exclude the unitary zero?** Exceptional-parameter provenance and coordinatewise transcendence are not enough. The missing currency is joint algebraic dependence of a mostly transcendental tuple tied together by one prime-log parameter.

Stable conditioning, ambient topology, measure/category genericity, proximity to the exceptional set, algebraic/torsion target classification, near-zero numerics, common-factor theory and finite exponential-sum ideal separation remain exhausted for this predicate. If exact hits survive unconditionally, the separate downstream question is still whether the finite-height Gram/eigenvalue margin is strong enough in the metric consumed by the final theorem, or which richer provenance than maximum-prime height must be retained.

## Derive an equicoercive quartic profile restriction from source structure

**Linked intuition:** `MI-034-a-sub-boundary-profile-cap-makes-quartic-euler-minimization-equicoercive`.

AF-319 closes the optimizer-transfer problem once one assumes a homogeneous profile cap `Q<=C<3`: the cap is projectively compact, the normalized full Euler objective converges uniformly to the quartic homogeneous objective with `O(delta)` error, and its minimizers converge to the unique certified homogeneous optimizer. The unrestricted problem still admits escaping source shapes.

The remaining question is provenance of the non-escape resource. Can the arithmetic/source construction force a cap below the certified boundary level, or another condition strong enough to give projective equicoercivity, without inserting that restriction solely to recover the desired optimizer?
