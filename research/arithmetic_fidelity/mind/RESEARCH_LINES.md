## Classify exact zero membership on saturated supports, but do not confuse it with stable fidelity

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-023-local-completeness-can-preserve-a-root-rate-discriminator-at-zero-density`, `MI-024-periodic-completeness-turns-li-cancellation-into-profinite-leakage`, `MI-025-regular-sampling-separates-aliasing-from-finite-window-stability`, `MI-026-zero-count-fidelity-is-distance-to-the-boundary-zero-discriminant`, `MI-027-one-sided-source-constraints-replace-nullspace-rank-by-reachable-cone-geometry`.

AF-282--AF-305 replace deleted linear directions by the exact positive cone or normalized convex body reachable inside each compression fiber and isolate exact saturation `k alpha=1` as the only categorical cap/support boundary. AF-306 then resolves the stable question negatively: every fixed saturated support with `k>=2` can approach zero arbitrarily closely at arbitrarily large imaginary heights, so equal-weight discreteness supplies no positive conditioning margin.

AF-307 sharpens the exact problem. Saturated two-prime supports have exact imaginary-axis zeros and already give same-fiber zero/safe collisions, while every saturated three- or four-prime support is exactly zero-free on the imaginary axis despite having zero margin. Exact recovery can therefore become **vacuously faithful** because the admissible source class contains no failures at all.

The live exact classification now starts at `k>=5` on `Re(s)=0`, together with nonzero-real-part, structured-support, discrete-alphabet, nonlinear/multiplicative and vector-observation variants. Every such result must state a non-vacuity condition before treating exact discriminator preservation as useful. Stable transport remains a separate quantitative problem and is already obstructed by saturation alone.
