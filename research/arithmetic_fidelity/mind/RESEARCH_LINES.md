## Make any effective-dimension reduction source-relative and target-geometric

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-023-local-completeness-can-preserve-a-root-rate-discriminator-at-zero-density`, `MI-024-periodic-completeness-turns-li-cancellation-into-profinite-leakage`, `MI-025-regular-sampling-separates-aliasing-from-finite-window-stability`, `MI-026-zero-count-fidelity-is-distance-to-the-boundary-zero-discriminant`, `MI-027-one-sided-source-constraints-replace-nullspace-rank-by-reachable-cone-geometry`.

AF-282--AF-300 separate acquisition stability from zero-sensitive fidelity and show that a genuine complex-affine source dimension `r` lowers the leverage bill to `r^(-1/2)` without making any remaining complex direction dispensable. AF-301--AF-302 then replace deleted linear directions by the exact positive cone and normalized convex body reachable inside each compression fiber.

AF-303 closes a fixed source-independent coefficient cap as a standalone repair: enough comparable discarded primes still admit recurrent capped barycentric zero polygons at arbitrarily large height. AF-304 now closes support cardinality by itself as well. On a `k`-sparse simplex fiber, the forgotten response is exactly a bounded-cardinality convex hull. Three unused support slots already recover the full planar convex hull by Carathéodory, while an explicit two-term retained/forgotten prime cancellation gives same-fiber zero/safe collisions at arbitrarily large height for every `k>=2`. The `k=1` boundary is faithful only because monomials have no finite zeros.

The surviving source-relative escape must therefore change the feasible fiber more essentially than positivity, normalization, a universal coefficient cap, or a fixed nontrivial support bound. Geometry-adaptive constraints, a discrete/integral alphabet, prescribed support patterns, multiplicative or nonlinear coupling, joint cap/support laws, or a finite observation window remain legitimate categories. Each must be tested against the actual target failure set rather than inferred from a nominal reduction in source size.

## Treat the information bills separately

Finite local degree, prime breadth, mixed-channel coupling, sample count, frame conditioning, nuisance gauge, residual jitter, projective distance to failure, linear condition number, nonlinear relative distortion, radial metric singularity, observation leverage, admissible-source dimension, quotient geometry, source-derived anisotropy, and fiberwise reachability are distinct resources. AF-304 adds a sharp sparsity lesson: support count only matters relative to the real dimension and geometry of the downstream response; for one complex scalar, three available forgotten slots already have the full convex-hull reach, and even two total source terms suffice for recurrent cancellation.
