## Classify nontorsion prime incidence inside the polygon-zero cone

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-023-local-completeness-can-preserve-a-root-rate-discriminator-at-zero-density`, `MI-024-periodic-completeness-turns-li-cancellation-into-profinite-leakage`, `MI-025-regular-sampling-separates-aliasing-from-finite-window-stability`, `MI-026-zero-count-fidelity-is-distance-to-the-boundary-zero-discriminant`, `MI-027-one-sided-source-constraints-replace-nullspace-rank-by-reachable-cone-geometry`, `MI-028-exact-zero-incidence-is-thinner-than-stable-access`, `MI-029-prime-phase-character-rank-exhausts-the-small-polygon-obstruction-at-the-pentagon`, `MI-030-five-prime-harmonic-rigidity-constrains-recurrence-not-first-incidence`, `MI-031-two-harmonics-recover-five-point-zero-fiber-except-torsion`.

AF-282--AF-305 replace deleted linear directions by the exact positive cone or normalized convex body reachable inside each compression fiber and isolate exact saturation `k alpha=1` as the categorical cap/support boundary. AF-306 then resolves the stable-value question negatively: every fixed saturated support with `k>=2` can approach zero arbitrarily closely at arbitrarily large imaginary heights, so equal-weight discreteness supplies no positive conditioning margin.

AF-307 classifies the first exact prime-support cases: saturated two-prime supports have exact imaginary-axis zeros, while every saturated three- or four-prime support is exactly zero-free there despite having zero value margin. AF-308 identifies the ambient exact-hit geometry as a radial cone `E_k` of Hausdorff dimension at most `k-2` inside `R^(k-1)`, hence Lebesgue-null. AF-309 proves the opposite topological extreme: `E_k` is dense, its complement is dense, and the exact-hit predicate is discontinuous everywhere in frequency space.

AF-310 isolates the strongest obstruction obtainable from exact integer phase characters. For distinct rational primes at one nonzero time the normalized character-relation lattice is cyclic, hence has rank at most one. This excludes the forced rank-two zero geometries at `k=3,4`, but the normalized pentagon zero surface already has a dense residual rank-at-most-one stratum.

AF-311 pushes the same mechanism through Baker's theorem. The prime logarithmic frequencies are linearly independent over the algebraic numbers, so a common-time prime phase point can satisfy at most one independent algebraic-affine angle relation. Yet a real-analytic patch of the exact pentagon zero manifold contains a dense residual set with **no** nontrivial algebraic-affine relation at all. Thus the first unresolved pentagon geometry is generically compatible not only with integer-character rank but with the full standard algebraic-linear logarithm obstruction.

AF-312 finds the first genuinely nonlinear rigidity after a hypothetical five-prime hit, but it acts on recurrence rather than incidence. If `F_P(t)=0`, self-inversive five-point geometry and Newton identities force `F_P(mt) != 0` for `m=2,3,4,5`; and on every fixed integer time lattice `{n t_0}` the exact-zero set is finite by the finite-rank unit-equation theorem.

AF-313 shows that the same low harmonics carry substantially more information than mere non-recurrence. On the five-point unit-circle zero fiber, `(s_2,s_3)` determines the whole unordered phase multiset except for the rotated-regular-pentagon fiber; distinct rational-prime phases exclude exactly that exceptional fiber at nonzero time. Thus a hypothetical five-prime hit is **exactly recoverable from `(F_P(2t),F_P(3t))` after incidence**. The recovery becomes ill-conditioned as `s_2` approaches zero, so this is exact fidelity rather than a uniform stability theorem.

The live `k>=5` problem is therefore specifically **first nontorsion arithmetic incidence beyond algebraic-linear relations and post-incidence recovery/recurrence obstructions**. Decide whether vectors `(log(p_j/p_k))` can meet the exact polygon-zero cone at all, or prove a genuinely nonlinear/source-specific obstruction to that first meeting. More Kronecker--Weyl density, generic dimension/category arguments, root-of-unity classification, perturbative continuity, near-zero numerics, integer-character rank, a standard Baker upgrade, low-harmonic repetition/recovery arguments, or finite-lattice recurrence bounds cannot decide first incidence. Nonzero-real-part, structured-support, discrete-alphabet, nonlinear/multiplicative and vector-observation variants remain separate questions, and every exact classification must state its non-vacuity condition.
