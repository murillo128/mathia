# Arithmetic-fidelity research lines

This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Arithmetic-Fidelity evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Separate finite-sample recoverability from rational-prime provenance

AF-483--AF-484 show that nonintegral Beurling-prime controls can preserve prime density, free numerical factorization, the square mass, the sharp Gibbs boundary law, and any prescribed finite family of exact prime-zeta samples. AF-485--AF-487 identify a genuinely different exact-fidelity regime: samples escaping to the right determine a discrete Dirichlet source, and a positive depth-`J` source observed at its critical scale has sharp local generator resolution `Theta(1/t)`. AF-488--AF-490 then price the observation channel itself. Under coherent pointwise critical noise, accumulated history inside a horizon does not beat the `Theta(1/H)` barrier; a fixed global finite-`p` noise budget can make sample accumulation useful; and nuisance directions must be quotiented before raw depth or cardinality is credited as sensitivity.

The quantitative frontier therefore remains growing-depth conditioning under explicit noise/correlation laws and structured nuisance classes. The provenance frontier is separate: a rational-prime discriminator must use information that the AF-483/AF-484 generalized-prime deformation family cannot preserve, such as genuinely integral/additive or congruence incidence, an unbounded tied family of exact analytic data, or another source restriction whose effect survives the destination quotient. Candidate snapping after localization is side information, not provenance.

## Classify when asymptotic contact retains threshold information

AF-495 exposes a second fidelity issue that is invisible in the scalar inverse-modulus story. For a task-truncated contact observable, the critical threshold bit can depend on whether bounded translated residuals recur at equality before an outer asymptotic limit is taken. The limiting asymptotic fibre alone may lose that boundary-side information.

AF-496--AF-498 identify the dimensional obstruction sharply. Universal norm-contact fidelity holds in finite dimension and fails in every infinite-dimensional real normed space, including after passing to difference sets. The failure is not repaired by algebraic difference-set structure; what finite dimension was silently providing is compactness of bounded returning residuals.

AF-499 separates the two compactness resources that must not be conflated. Vanishing tail noncompactness of the escaping residual family restores norm-precompactness and hence identifies the asymptotic distance with an attained contact point, while recurrence of every strict superlevel still needs the corresponding attainment/proximinality hypothesis. Thus finite dimensionality was packaging both **tail norm-precompactness** and **distance attainment**.

The live question is to characterize natural arithmetic/source classes in which these two properties hold uniformly at growing depth, or to identify a task-level observable that legitimately keeps the threshold-boundary bit before the information-destroying outer limit. Any claimed asymptotic discriminator must state whether it uses the raw translating family, a truncated contact set, the post-limit fibre, or only its distance. Moving between those objects can erase exactly the equality information being tested.
