## Distinguish relational order from placement-generator complexity

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-canonical-relational-lifts-can-restore-gauge`, `MI-019-faithful-translation-lifts-are-affine`, `MI-043-support-scale-interaction-components-bound-effective-relational-arity`.

AF-371--AF-378 separate target-faithful translation positivity from gauge elimination, sampling density and coordinate display. Uniformly discrete rows admit compact-support false positives; even `inf gap=0` can leave every support-interaction component uniformly bounded; faithful nonlinear coordinate transport preserves the sampled fibre rather than creating new information.

AF-379 and AF-381 give the complementary determining regime. Dense normalized finite row patterns determine one-sided continuous translation minors, while dense joint row/column patterns determine the fully sampled target. Vanishing tail mesh forces both closures. Since `log p_(n+1)-log p_n -> 0`, the fully prime-indexed matrices `f(log(p_i/q_j))` are target-complete for continuous translation total nonnegativity.

AF-382 adds an independent compression axis at determinant order two. For strictly positive continuous `f`, it is enough to test adjacent successor-prime rectangles in any far tail. Taking logarithms turns each adjacent `2x2` determinant inequality into a nonnegative mixed difference; exact rectangle telescoping generates every sampled order-two inequality, and the vanishing prime-log mesh then closes to global `TN_2`, equivalently log-concavity of `f`.

The resulting hierarchy is sharp enough to prevent two opposite mistakes. **Unbounded determinant order remains load-bearing for unrestricted `PF_infinity` by AF-372, while at fixed order two the enormous family of spatial placements is redundant and has a nearest-neighbor generating family.** The live arithmetic question is therefore not to retain more order-two prime pairs, but to explain why the required successor-prime inequalities hold for the source-derived profile, and to determine whether higher-order nonnegative translation positivity has comparably small generating families under hypotheses actually available in the RH-facing source class. Strict-TP finite-matrix recognition cannot simply be imported across zero-pattern and closure issues.
