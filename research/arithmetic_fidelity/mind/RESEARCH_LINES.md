## Replace scalar canonical-center budgets by full likelihood-ray geometry

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-015-whole-experiment-fidelity-needs-a-common-recovery-profile` and `MI-016-shtarkov-rays-separate-radial-reset-from-full-experiment-loss`.

AF-149--AF-150 identify the Shtarkov/NML envelope as a source-selected common reference and show that stagewise recanonicalization need not follow approximate recoverability. AF-151 makes the reset exact: the propagated and recomputed centers differ by the normalized local max-sum conflict profile `kappa`, so different divergence geometries see different moments or tails rather than only the scalar maximal-leakage drop.

AF-152--AF-153 then separate path composition from endpoint relevance. Old mismatch is conditionally averaged before each new reset, while the endpoint effect of a local reset is exactly its covariance with the chosen discriminator pulled backward through the remaining channels. AF-154 identifies the full geometric object: max-normalized likelihood rays are conditionally barycentered by compression; `kappa` records only their radial retreat, whereas the Shtarkov Pearson profile records the full conditional ray variance and vanishes exactly at finite-experiment sufficiency.

## Derive a source-natural reference path with destination-calibrated conflict control

A useful arithmetic certificate should propagate one source-selected dominating reference through the compression chain and control the part of likelihood-ray loss that the final theorem actually consumes. A small mean leakage drop is enough for some weak metrics but not for strong likelihood-ratio geometry; a large local reset may also be irrelevant if later compression annihilates the corresponding discriminator. The next source theorem should therefore bound either the full conditional likelihood-ray dispersion or the transported reset covariance in the declared destination topology.

## Classify retained interaction support before proposing repair

Linear span, sigma-algebra, interaction depth, family recovery, center reset, radial conflict, tangential likelihood-ray loss, and endpoint observability are distinct information layers. Repairs should target the first layer that fails instead of treating one canonical center or one divergence as a complete fidelity metric.
