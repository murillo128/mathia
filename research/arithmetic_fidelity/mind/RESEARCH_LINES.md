## Separate exact zero membership from stable zero margin on saturated discrete sources

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-023-local-completeness-can-preserve-a-root-rate-discriminator-at-zero-density`, `MI-024-periodic-completeness-turns-li-cancellation-into-profinite-leakage`, `MI-025-regular-sampling-separates-aliasing-from-finite-window-stability`, `MI-026-zero-count-fidelity-is-distance-to-the-boundary-zero-discriminant`, `MI-027-one-sided-source-constraints-replace-nullspace-rank-by-reachable-cone-geometry`.

AF-282--AF-302 separate acquisition stability from zero-sensitive fidelity and replace deleted linear directions by the exact positive cone or normalized convex body reachable inside each compression fiber. AF-303--AF-305 then close every strict-slack cap/support regime: whenever normalized nonnegative sources with support at most `k` and coefficient cap `alpha` are nonempty with `k alpha>1`, recurrent same-fiber zero/safe collisions survive. Exact saturation `k alpha=1` is the only categorical boundary because every source becomes the uniform measure on a `k`-subset.

AF-306 resolves the **stable** part of that saturated boundary negatively. For every `k>=2` and every fixed vertical line, uniform prime supports in one deletion fiber can have normalized zero margins arbitrarily close to `0` and `1` at arbitrarily large common heights. Discreteness therefore supplies no positive conditioning margin. For three equal prime terms on the imaginary axis, however, the polynomial is exactly zero-free while its values approach zero arbitrarily closely. Exact discriminator fidelity and stable discriminator fidelity are genuinely different resources.

The live saturated-source question is now narrower: classify when prescribed discrete support geometry preserves or destroys **exact** zero membership, and state explicitly whether the downstream RH mechanism needs exact membership or a quantitative margin. For stable transport, equal-weight saturation alone is already ruled out. For exact transport, the general `k>=4` and nonzero-real-part support problem remains open, as do more structured support systems, discrete coefficient alphabets, multiplicative/nonlinear coupling, vector observations, and compact vertical windows.

## Treat the information bills separately

Finite local degree, prime breadth, mixed-channel coupling, sample count, frame conditioning, nuisance gauge, residual jitter, projective distance to failure, linear condition number, nonlinear relative distortion, radial metric singularity, observation leverage, admissible-source dimension, quotient geometry, source-derived anisotropy, fiberwise reachability, continuous versus saturated-discrete source geometry, and **exact failure membership versus stable failure margin** are distinct resources. AF-306 shows that a source restriction can improve the first while completely failing the second.
