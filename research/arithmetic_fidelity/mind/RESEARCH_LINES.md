# Arithmetic-fidelity research lines

This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Arithmetic-Fidelity evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Separate finite-sample recoverability from rational-prime provenance

AF-483--AF-484 show that nonintegral Beurling-prime controls can preserve prime density, free numerical factorization, the square mass, the sharp Gibbs boundary law, and any prescribed finite family of exact prime-zeta samples. AF-485--AF-487 identify a genuinely different exact-fidelity regime: samples escaping to the right determine a discrete Dirichlet source, and a positive depth-`J` source observed at its critical scale has sharp local generator resolution `Theta(1/t)`. AF-488--AF-490 then price the observation channel itself. Under coherent pointwise critical noise, accumulated history inside a horizon does not beat the `Theta(1/H)` barrier; a fixed global finite-`p` noise budget can make sample accumulation useful; and nuisance directions must be quotiented before raw depth or cardinality is credited as sensitivity.

The quantitative frontier therefore remains growing-depth conditioning under explicit noise/correlation laws and structured nuisance classes. The provenance frontier is separate: a rational-prime discriminator must use information that the AF-483/AF-484 generalized-prime deformation family cannot preserve, such as genuinely integral/additive or congruence incidence, an unbounded tied family of exact analytic data, or another source restriction whose effect survives the destination quotient. Candidate snapping after localization is side information, not provenance.

## Classify quotient-visible contact through admission, temporal attainment and near-fiber compactness

AF-495 exposes a second fidelity issue that is invisible in the scalar inverse-modulus story. For a task-truncated contact observable, the critical threshold bit can depend on whether bounded translated residuals recur at equality before an outer asymptotic limit is taken. The limiting asymptotic fibre alone may lose that boundary-side information.

AF-496--AF-500 identify the first compactness and order-of-operations gates. Tail norm-precompactness and distance attainment are separate resources, and for a closed nuisance space `V` the relevant separation and compactness test live in the quotient `Q=Y/V`. Raw tail compactness in `Y` is neither necessary nor sufficient for quotient-visible compactness.

AF-501 decomposes that noncommutation into two independent mechanisms. Source saturation `cl(C)+V=cl(C)` controls whether quotienting admits new bounded aliases that were not legitimate upstream source states; finite-dimensional nuisance fibres give the exact universal bounded compact-lifting property. These are different gates: saturation can hold while an infinite-dimensional fibre hides raw noncompact motion, and finite-dimensional fibres can coexist with new quotient aliases when the source is not saturated.

AF-502 then replaces the universal finite-dimensional requirement by the exact fixed-family diagnostic. Once the quotient image of a bounded residual family has noncompactness below a tube width `rho`, the full upstream Hausdorff noncompactness is already attained inside one `rho`-tube of a quotient fibre. In the quotient-compact regime, this localization holds at every positive `rho`; compactness of each exact fibre separately is insufficient because noncompactness can drift through fibres whose quotient labels converge.

AF-503 closes the fully saturated infinite-dimensional lift strictly above threshold. If the closed source `D=cl(C)` satisfies `D+V=D`, `V` is infinite-dimensional, and the asymptotic separation `lambda=liminf_(t->infinity) dist(ts,D)` is finite, then every radius `R>lambda` has a positive raw and near-fibre noncompactness floor. AF-504 resolves the Hilbert equality case exactly. For a closed nuisance subspace of a Hilbert observation space,

`nu_C^V(s;R) <= nu_C^0(s;R) <= sqrt(nu_C^V(s;R)^2 + R^2-lambda^2)`

for `R>=lambda`; with infinite-dimensional `V`, the raw defect is also at least `sqrt(R^2-lambda^2)`. Hence at the critical radius the nuisance term collapses and

`nu_C^0(s;lambda)=nu_C^V(s;lambda)`.

If the quotient-visible tail is compact, the raw defect is therefore exactly zero at `R=lambda` and exactly `sqrt(R^2-lambda^2)` for every fixed `R>lambda`. The infinite-dimensional nuisance fibre is harmless for this compactness test only at the critical truncation itself; it immediately reappears above it.

AF-505 now isolates the equality-contact gate itself under convex quotient geometry. For `f(t)=dist_Q(tu,F)` with nonempty convex `F` and finite `lambda=liminf_(t->infinity) f(t)`, convexity forces `f(t)` to decrease to `lambda`; the critical sublevel is either empty or a terminal ray. Thus exact contact occurs if and only if the distance profile eventually plateaus at `lambda`. Proximinality and critical-tail compactness do not supply that temporal witness. AF-505 gives closed convex planar controls with the same asymptotic threshold, recession cone, bounded-offset translation fibre and post-limit compactness data, while one reaches `lambda` and the other approaches it strictly from above.

The live question is now ordered rather than merely compactness-based. Admission still needs a natural arithmetic nuisance equivalence whose quotient does not create illegitimate source states. At exact threshold one must then prove temporal attainment/eventual plateau in the actual quotient; only after such a contact exists do pointwise source attainment and compactness of the retained contact fibre become relevant. If temporal attainment is unavailable, an equality argument must retain the critical-contact bit or move to controlled radii `R>lambda`. Any argument that needs such a fixed neighborhood must stay in the quotient, use a compactly controlled transversal/restricted representative class, or otherwise avoid restoring an entire infinite-dimensional saturated nuisance orbit. Any claimed asymptotic discriminator must state the nuisance quotient, admission rule, representative/lift class, truncation radius, compactness test, and whether it uses actual contacts, the post-limit fibre, or only its distance.
