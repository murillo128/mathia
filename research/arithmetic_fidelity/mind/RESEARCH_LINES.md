## Separate source category, local depth, prime breadth, coupling, cadence, and observation horizon

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-fidelity-endpoints-are-category-dependent`, `MI-023-local-completeness-can-preserve-a-root-rate-discriminator-at-zero-density`, `MI-024-periodic-completeness-turns-li-cancellation-into-profinite-leakage`, `MI-025-regular-sampling-separates-aliasing-from-finite-window-stability`.

AF-244--AF-276 show that coarse symmetry, zero counting, endpoint jets, compact-open proximity, and periodic transport do not replace an exact source-category argument. Bounded Euler degree makes local prime-power depth finite and sharp, but global Euler fidelity still requires information at infinitely many prime locations. AF-275--AF-276 further show that finite collections of exact values or jets leave positive-dimensional tail fibers even inside degree-one Euler products.

AF-278--AF-280 now separate three acquisition losses. Continuous vertical Bohr averaging can recover each declared Dirichlet coefficient by paying unbounded phase-time breadth. A single regular lattice first quotients frequencies through `lambda -> e^{-ih lambda}`: rational resonance creates exact aliases, while nonresonance gives infinite-data injectivity but no fixed-window inverse modulus on the unrestricted weighted `ell^1` ball. A **finite family** of regular lattices does not repair several resonant quotients: it is injective iff at least one cadence is already nonresonant, because an explicit group-algebra interaction lies in the common kernel when every cadence resonates.

Even an injective joint phase tuple is insufficient if acquisition retains only the separate lattice marginals; mixed phase coupling can be annihilated in every channel. Finite windows also remain unstable for every finite cadence family by simultaneous torus recurrence.

The live theorem must therefore specify a source category and an acquisition geometry whose **joint coupling**, cadence diversity, horizon, precision, or structural constraints provide a uniform recovery modulus at the prime breadth actually consumed downstream. Exact infinite-data uniqueness and the mere number of cadences are insufficient.

## Treat the information bills separately

A finite local degree determines how much prime-power depth is needed at one prime. It does not bound how many primes must be seen. Multiple marginal channels do not automatically retain their joint coupling, an injective infinite channel can still have no fixed-window inverse modulus, and a finite observation vector can still leave an infinite source fiber. Future fidelity claims must state separately the difference-object complexity, local depth, prime breadth, observation dimension, coupling geometry, cadence/fiber geometry, horizon, and target metric modulus.
