## Quantify observation gain on the completed source-law secant carrier in the physically correct source topology

**Linked intuition:** `MI-042-finite-prefix-pole-fidelity-needs-a-tail-class`.

AF-451--AF-452 identify the exact fixed-source stability object: a residual Radon core matters only when a common positive background completes both sides into the prescribed source-law fiber, and for a difference-homogeneous source metric the lower Lipschitz modulus is the minimum observation gain on the normalized carrier of those completable secants.

AF-453 now rules out one large class of targets before arithmetic enters. If the admissible fiber contains weakly collapsing secants whose total-variation norm does not vanish—already true for moving Dirac atoms inside a non-discrete exact source-law level set—every finite continuous observation has zero uniform TV gain. Exact injectivity can coexist with this zero margin.

The live quantitative problem must therefore begin by fixing the physical source topology. If the arithmetic source class is genuinely discrete, determine whether total variation avoids the weak-collapse obstruction and then estimate its gain against matched non-prime controls. If locations are continuously perturbable, use a topology-compatible metric such as `W_1` or bounded-Lipschitz and estimate the corresponding lower gain on the completed secant carrier. For `W_p`, `p>1`, the AF-452 homogeneous completion quotient cannot be imported unchanged.

## Keep algebraic identifiability, topology and stable physical inversion separate

Rank, spark and Radon criteria can prove exact injectivity without yielding a quantitative inverse margin. Conversely, failure of TV conditioning for moving atoms is a topology mismatch rather than evidence that the observation has lost exact information. A finite-prefix fidelity theorem should therefore identify the admissible completed secants, the source metric, the observation norm and a positive lower gain in that geometry; otherwise it is only an exact decoding statement or an impossible stability target.
