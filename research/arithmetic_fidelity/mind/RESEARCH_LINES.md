# Arithmetic-fidelity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable arithmetic-fidelity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Require one family-level reverse channel, then classify certificate calibration separately

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-regular-recovery-orders-the-full-multiscale-information-profile`, `MI-014-target-relative-spectral-fidelity-is-projection-geometry`, and `MI-015-whole-experiment-fidelity-needs-a-common-recovery-profile`.

AF-142--AF-145 establish the categorical layer: tangent and pairwise fidelity do not imply recovery, while a common-reference Bayes/Petz reverse kernel does, and coherent references make those reverse kernels compose with additive chi-square loss.

AF-146--AF-148 classify the quantitative family-size pathology on the canonical private-label control. Pearson and KL are instances of one exact `f`-divergence phase diagram: the common-reference loss is the AF-050 reverse-support penalty, and dimension-free calibration against recovery on this family occurs exactly when the `f`-divergence endpoint diameter is finite. Infinite endpoint diameter permits vanishing deficiency with order-one or infinite raw profile loss.

A useful arithmetic certificate must therefore prove both a source-natural common recovery map and a quantitatively appropriate divergence/profile for the effective family complexity. Finite endpoint diameter removes this particular dilution obstruction but does not itself construct a reverse kernel in a general experiment.

## Derive a source-natural coherent reference or equivalent composable compatibility object

AF-145 solves composition once a coherent reference family is supplied. The remaining burden is to derive that reference from source structure, or replace it by another family-wide object with an explicit common reverse channel and composition law.
