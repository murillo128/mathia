# Arithmetic-fidelity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable arithmetic-fidelity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Make signed-source complexity and observation-kernel sign regularity explicit

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-007-stable-fidelity-is-distance-from-collision`, `MI-017-exact-sufficiency-geometry-does-not-fix-approximate-recovery-scale`, `MI-018-signed-complexity-only-transfers-through-sign-regular-observations`.

AF-195--AF-206 identify the exact fixed-order transfer geometry. Minimal-support moment-flat controls are Peano B-spline/divided-difference carriers; inside the positive Peano cone the fixed-band transfer is an exact positive expectation. AF-207--AF-213 then show that unrestricted signed carriers are qualitatively different: exact injectivity can coexist with an exponentially collapsing BV lower modulus, and Hobby--Rice signs reach the exponent-one near-null scale.

AF-214--AF-215 close provenance-only repairs. Arbitrary-order moment flatness survives with unit multiplicity and simple integer support, and even with support restricted to actual rational primes: Green--Tao prime progressions carry Prouhet partitions of every prescribed order. Primality by itself therefore does not bound cancellation complexity.

AF-216 supplies a genuine source-complexity gate. A signed atomic source annihilating `m` polynomial moments must have at least `m` ordered sign changes; likewise, a Dirichlet sum vanishing at `m` equally spaced real samples must have at least `m` ordered sign changes. Bounded ordered variation can therefore prevent arbitrary exact first-harmonic cancellation.

AF-217 shows why that gate is observation-relative rather than universal. The full Euler-log kernel is not totally nonnegative of all orders: its bilateral transform contains the nontrivial zeta zeros, and the Schoenberg/Pólya-frequency criterion therefore forbids all-order variation diminution. The live problem is to determine, for a declared prime-derived source class and sign budget, the **largest finite Euler order/band with a quantitative sign-regular transfer modulus**, and to identify the source-forced law that actually bounds oscillation complexity. Neither primality nor all-order Euler positivity can be assumed to supply it.

## Solve extremals only inside the declared transfer category

The homogeneous Peano shape functional remains useful for classifying minimal-support source geometry, but AF-200 and the later fixed-band results show that shape optimization without the observation/localization and source-category gates can optimize the wrong problem. Positive Peano carriers, signed BV classes, prime-supported Prouhet controls, bounded-sign-variation classes, and genuinely prime-derived sources have fundamentally different admissible cancellation laws.

Future extremal statements should therefore optimize the actual transfer resource inside a declared source class. A sharp `Q_m` constant, a transport-distance bound, exact finite-dimensional sufficiency, primality of support, or a sign-count bound is not by itself a stable-recovery theorem unless the observation kernel preserves enough of that structure quantitatively.

## Keep provenance and endpoint sufficiency separate from conditioning

A representation can be exactly sufficient yet lose source identity after quotienting, or preserve provenance while becoming arbitrarily ill-conditioned on an expanding source class. Recovery claims should state separately what is reconstructible, which equivalences are harmless, which source carrier remains localized, which arithmetic restrictions exclude generic near-null controls, which sign-regularity order the observation actually preserves, and at what quantitative cost the inverse stays stable.
