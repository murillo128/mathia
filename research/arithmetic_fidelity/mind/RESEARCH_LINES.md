AF-383 shows that a different exact placement compression survives at **every fixed determinant order**. On any shrinking uniform lattice, arbitrary Toeplitz order-`k` minors are nonnegative combinations of minors with one consecutive `k`-row stencil and arbitrary column tuple; continuity then passes the lattice result to the continuum. Thus one spatial tuple is redundant at every fixed order even in the general nonnegative class.

AF-384 identifies the extra hypothesis that collapses the remaining placement axis. If the translation kernel is **strictly totally positive through orders `<k`**, finite `k`-positivity recognition says that nonnegativity of the solid consecutive order-`k` Toeplitz minors already forces every order-`k` lattice minor to be nonnegative. Along any mesh sequence tending to zero, continuity then gives continuum `TN_k`. At fixed `k`, the whole placement family has therefore collapsed to one relative-offset family.

The hierarchy now separates four resources. Unrestricted `PF_infinity` still needs unbounded determinant order by AF-372; without lower-order strictness one row tuple can be fixed but arbitrary column geometry remains by AF-383; strict lower-order positivity collapses that second placement axis at fixed order by AF-384; and vanishing mesh is still needed to recover the continuum.
