# Arithmetic-fidelity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable arithmetic-fidelity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Quantify observation gain on the completed source-law secant carrier

**Linked intuition:** `MI-042-finite-prefix-pole-fidelity-needs-a-tail-class`.

AF-451 retracts the raw-circuit source-law localization asserted in AF-450. Cancelling the common mass of two equal-observation measures exposes a Radon core, but that core need not have the original source-law barycenter. A collision belongs to a fixed source-law fiber only after the core is completed by a common positive background that restores the prescribed source statistic and respects the atom budget.

AF-452 gives the corresponding stability object for difference-homogeneous source metrics. For a fixed mass, source law and sparsity budget, the lower Lipschitz modulus of the observation map is exactly the infimum observation gain on the normalized secant carrier generated by those completable cores. Exact collisions are the zero-gain points; instability can also occur through a sequence of admissible completed secants whose gain tends to zero without an exact collision.

The next quantitative problem is therefore source-relative and metric-specific: estimate that gain for the actual arithmetic observation family and the physical source norm, with the common background and sparsity budget kept explicit. The exact quotient is valid for norms such as total variation, bounded-Lipschitz, MMD and `W_1`; it must not be silently transferred to nonlinear transport metrics such as `W_p`, `p>1`, where the completion scale changes the ratio.

## Keep algebraic identifiability and stable physical inversion separate

The augmented affine/Radon criteria remain useful for exact global injectivity, but a rank or spark statement does not price the inverse problem on a constrained source fiber. Any proposed finite-prefix fidelity theorem should identify the admissible completed secant set, the source metric, the observation norm and a positive lower gain on that set. If the argument instead reconstructs an unrestricted source from enough measurements, that is generic decoding rather than an arithmetic stability gain.
