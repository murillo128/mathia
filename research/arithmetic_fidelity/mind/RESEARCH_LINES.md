## Reduce target-relevant dimension or enrich the observable beyond scalar boundary evaluations

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-023-local-completeness-can-preserve-a-root-rate-discriminator-at-zero-density`, `MI-024-periodic-completeness-turns-li-cancellation-into-profinite-leakage`, `MI-025-regular-sampling-separates-aliasing-from-finite-window-stability`, `MI-026-zero-count-fidelity-is-distance-to-the-boundary-zero-discriminant`.

AF-282--AF-289 separate stable finite-prefix acquisition from the final zero-sensitive target. AF-290 identifies the exact zero-count robustness radius as distance to the boundary-zero discriminant; AF-291 shows that its normalized eta margin collapses on fixed RH-relevant contours despite correct analytic boundary convergence.

AF-292--AF-295 close positive diagonal reweighting, bounded-condition dense Hilbert mixing, uniformly quasisymmetric transport, and the obvious global complex-scalar quotient as free repairs. AF-296--AF-297 show that a target-radial conformal repair either preserves collapse, sends failure to an infinite-distance boundary, or explicitly pays reciprocal raw-margin scale.

AF-298 closes the most canonical nonradial escape generated by the target observations themselves. For every full-rank boundary-observation Gram metric, the leverage identity `int K dmu=N` forces normalized zero-count margin at most `O(N^(-1/2))`, while `D`-optimal weighting attains that scale. The anisotropic metric genuinely removes the extra `H_N(alpha)` evaluation bill, but the `sqrt(N)` effective-dimension bill remains sharp.

The live question is therefore no longer merely “find a nonradial metric.” A successful representation must explain why the target-relevant perturbation dimension is smaller than `N`, prove that discarded directions are target-null, use observables stronger than positive averages of scalar boundary evaluations, or introduce source-derived arithmetic information that changes the failure geometry.

## Treat the information bills separately

Finite local degree, prime breadth, mixed-channel coupling, sample count, frame conditioning, nuisance gauge, residual jitter, projective distance to failure, linear condition number, nonlinear relative distortion, radial metric singularity, observation leverage, effective target dimension, quotient geometry, and source-derived anisotropy are distinct resources. AF-298 adds a sharp warning: even optimally conditioned target-aligned observation geometry can remain asymptotically fragile solely because the observable spans `N` independent target-relevant directions.
