## Make any effective-dimension reduction source-relative or genuinely nonlinear

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-023-local-completeness-can-preserve-a-root-rate-discriminator-at-zero-density`, `MI-024-periodic-completeness-turns-li-cancellation-into-profinite-leakage`, `MI-025-regular-sampling-separates-aliasing-from-finite-window-stability`, `MI-026-zero-count-fidelity-is-distance-to-the-boundary-zero-discriminant`, `MI-027-one-sided-source-constraints-replace-nullspace-rank-by-reachable-cone-geometry`.

AF-282--AF-289 separate stable finite-prefix acquisition from the final zero-sensitive target. AF-290 identifies the exact zero-count robustness radius as distance to the boundary-zero discriminant; AF-291 shows that its normalized eta margin collapses on fixed RH-relevant contours despite correct analytic boundary convergence.

AF-292--AF-295 close positive diagonal reweighting, bounded-condition dense Hilbert mixing, uniformly quasisymmetric transport, and the obvious global complex-scalar quotient as free repairs. AF-296--AF-297 show that a target-radial conformal repair either preserves collapse, sends failure to an infinite-distance boundary, or explicitly pays reciprocal raw-margin scale.

AF-298 closes the canonical full-rank anisotropic escape generated by positive averages of scalar boundary evaluations: the leverage identity `int K dmu=N` leaves a sharp normalized margin `asymp N^(-1/2)` even after optimal weighting. AF-299 then closes the most literal lower-dimensional linear escape on the unrestricted coefficient space: every nontrivial additive linear quotient fills with boundary-failure representatives.

AF-300 classifies the complex-affine source-restriction escape exactly. If admissible coefficients lie in an independently justified complex-affine family `a+U` of intrinsic dimension `r`, the optimal observation bill really drops from `N^(-1/2)` to `r^(-1/2)`. But the restricted evaluation family still has trivial common kernel on `U`; every rank-`<r` complex-linear compression again gives zero distance to failure. Source restriction buys only the dimensions actually removed from the admissible perturbation model, not extra target-null directions inside that model.

AF-301 now tests the first non-complex-linear escape. For the full positive orthant, coordinate deletion is governed not by a deleted linear subspace but by the positive cone generated by the discarded Dirichlet response rays. This can protect a fixed observation when the negative retained response lies outside that cone. But if three discarded indices have Q-independent logarithms—three distinct primes suffice—Kronecker-Weyl recurrence produces arbitrarily large heights where those forgotten positive rays span the full complex plane, and every retained state again has a boundary-zero completion.

The live route is therefore sharper than “use positivity.” A useful arithmetic admissibility must bound the **fiberwise reachable correction set** itself: normalization, coefficient budgets, discreteness, multiplicative coupling, sparsity, nonlinear constraints, or genuine symmetries must prevent recurrent phase mixing from recreating arbitrary complex corrections while retaining enough source breadth for the RH-relevant carrier. For a fixed finite contour the exact test is local conic reachability, not the asymptotic recurrence statement.

## Treat the information bills separately

Finite local degree, prime breadth, mixed-channel coupling, sample count, frame conditioning, nuisance gauge, residual jitter, projective distance to failure, linear condition number, nonlinear relative distortion, radial metric singularity, observation leverage, admissible-source dimension, quotient geometry, source-derived anisotropy, and fiberwise conic reachability are distinct resources. AF-298 gives the ambient `sqrt(N)` bill; AF-300 replaces it by `sqrt(r)` only after an actual `r`-dimensional source restriction has been proved. AF-301 shows that a one-sided source can change the correction category again, but multiplicatively independent phase recurrence can refill the entire target cone unless additional magnitude or structural constraints limit what each compressed fiber can reach.
