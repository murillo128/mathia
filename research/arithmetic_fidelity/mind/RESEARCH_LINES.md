# Arithmetic-fidelity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable arithmetic-fidelity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they separate genuinely different mechanisms.

## Classify the maximal admissible quotient before constructing a repair

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-fidelity-is-quotient-relative`.

Given a compression together with a mathematically forced observable class, determine the joint indistinguishability relation generated by all admissible observables and test whether the target discriminator is constant on its fibers. The substantive problem is to derive the admissible class from symmetry, locality, naturality, geometry, or operator structure before optimization begins; allowing arbitrary marks makes the problem vacuous.

A decisive result is either an exact factorization theorem through the maximal admissible quotient or a conflicting pair that survives every admissible observable. Downstream optimization or spectralization cannot count as a repair unless it receives genuinely new information.

## Characterize canonical relational lifts that remove exactly the intended gauge

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-canonical-relational-lifts-can-restore-gauge`.

Use higher-order couplings, monomial lattices, or matrix-valued marked spectral data to identify when relational information restores a discriminator without selecting an arbitrary absolute coordinate. The exact test is whether the remaining ambiguity group equals the intended symmetry group, including finite aliases invisible to rank-only arguments.

A decisive positive example should derive the relational observable canonically and prove completeness modulo the intended gauge. A matched control with the same relational data but a different discriminator would close the proposed lift.

## Prove bridges between differential, average, formal, and zero-error fidelity only under explicit hypotheses

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-fidelity-endpoints-are-category-dependent`.

Determine when vertical-rank, conditional-variance, full-jet, and support-confusability criteria imply one another after adding connectedness, quasianalyticity, support separation, or other natural assumptions. The aim is to replace informal category changes by exact bridge theorems or exact counterexamples.

A decisive result must state the topology/category and show either equivalence under sharp hypotheses or a persistent obstruction. Small average error, formal agreement, or infinitesimal injectivity alone does not establish exact recoverability.
