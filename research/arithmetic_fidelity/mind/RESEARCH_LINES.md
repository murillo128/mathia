## Price source information after separating discriminator survival from completion freedom

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-fidelity-is-quotient-relative`, `MI-004-prime-specificity-depends-on-the-retained-analytic-layer`, `MI-018-signed-complexity-only-transfers-through-sign-regular-observations`, `MI-041-one-riesz-order-crosses-conditioning-without-moving-the-zero-discriminator`, `MI-042-finite-prefix-pole-fidelity-needs-a-tail-class`.

AF-351--AF-365 separate exact discriminator survival from target-relative inversion cost. Every fixed real Riesz order has a zero-free Mellin multiplier on `Re s>0`, so a **global eventual** profile error `O(X^theta)` preserves Dirichlet principal parts in `Re s>theta`, even though finite-band coordinate recovery through the same channel can be exponentially ill-conditioned.

AF-366 then closes the unrestricted finite-horizon loophole: a finite coefficient prefix is blind to arbitrarily small raw tails that insert prescribed critical-strip poles. AF-367 strengthens this inside a positive multiplicative source class: arbitrarily long exact ordinary prime/integer prefixes plus near-square-root Beurling integer counting still do not determine zero geometry.

AF-368 now removes a different tempting repair. For every `c>0` there is a nonordinary Beurling prime system with `|N(x)-floor(x)|<c log x`. Hence even **global scalar integer-counting accuracy at an arbitrarily small fixed logarithmic scale does not identify the rational-prime source**. This does not assert different RH status for the matched system; it says that source provenance itself is not carried by such a scalar envelope.

The live question is therefore structural: identify an independently justified **ordinary-arithmetic completion law** that excludes both pole-carrying coefficient tails and the Beurling matched controls. Exact arithmetic support, genuinely arithmetic cancellation, a rigid continuation/growth class, functional-equation coupling or another nonlocal relation remain legitimate candidates. Tightening a fixed `O(log x)` counting envelope is not enough; a purely scalar-counting route would need a genuinely stronger rigidity theorem, such as a proved sublogarithmic boundary.
