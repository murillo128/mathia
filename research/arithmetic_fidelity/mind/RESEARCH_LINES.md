## Separate source category, local depth, prime breadth, coupling, cadence, and observation horizon

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-fidelity-endpoints-are-category-dependent`, `MI-023-local-completeness-can-preserve-a-root-rate-discriminator-at-zero-density`, `MI-024-periodic-completeness-turns-li-cancellation-into-profinite-leakage`, `MI-025-regular-sampling-separates-aliasing-from-finite-window-stability`.

AF-244--AF-276 show that coarse symmetry, zero counting, endpoint jets, compact-open proximity, and periodic transport do not replace an exact source-category argument. Bounded Euler degree makes local prime-power depth finite and sharp, but global Euler fidelity still requires information at infinitely many prime locations. AF-275--AF-276 further show that finite collections of exact values or jets leave positive-dimensional tail fibers even inside degree-one Euler products.

AF-278--AF-280 separate continuous time breadth, one-lattice aliasing, marginal coupling loss, and finite-window instability. AF-281 now shows exactly what genuine mixed acquisition buys. If all mixed samples `F(c+i(k_1h_1+...+k_Jh_J))` are retained, the channel is the Fourier transform of the joint torus pushforward, so ordinary Dirichlet coefficient fidelity is equivalent to `cap_j R_(h_j)={1}` rather than requiring one individually nonresonant cadence.

That repair is not free. Commensurable cadences reduce to one finer lattice; if every individual cadence resonates, a strict repair is possible only when their generated time subgroup is dense. Even then every fixed mixed-index box has remote near-collisions on the unrestricted weighted `ell^1` source class. Finite known source breadth is recoverable by finite mixed polynomial degree, but the required degree and conditioning grow with breadth.

The live theorem must therefore specify a source category and acquisition geometry whose joint coupling, cadence group, mixed-mode budget, horizon, precision, or structural constraints provide a uniform recovery modulus at the prime breadth actually consumed downstream. Exact infinite-data uniqueness and the mere number of cadences are insufficient.

## Treat the information bills separately

A finite local degree determines how much prime-power depth is needed at one prime. It does not bound how many primes must be seen. Separate marginal channels can destroy joint interaction; complete mixed channels can restore exact separation by paying dense timing or mixed-word complexity; neither yields a fixed-budget inverse modulus on an unrestricted infinite source. Future fidelity claims must state separately the difference-object complexity, local depth, prime breadth, observation dimension, coupling geometry, cadence/fiber geometry, mixed-mode budget, horizon, and target metric modulus.
