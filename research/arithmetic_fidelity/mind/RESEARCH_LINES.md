# Arithmetic-fidelity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable arithmetic-fidelity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

**Linked intuition:** `MI-018-signed-complexity-only-transfers-through-sign-regular-observations`.

AF-215--AF-220 force ordered sign complexity but leave the quantitative transfer into the shrinking Euler-log observation window open. The next theorem must relate the source sign budget to a separation or curvature modulus at the resolution actually consumed by the target.

## Price the actual target metric before calling a representation compressed

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-019-faithful-translation-lifts-are-affine`, `MI-020-prime-tail-localization-has-an-information-conservation-law`.

AF-221--AF-233 show that localization has a real information bill and that contraction can lower it only in the metric actually used downstream. A useful compression claim still needs both a cheaper target geometry and a theorem that the final arithmetic argument consumes only that target.

## Build cancellation-coherent source access to the Li root-rate class

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-021-coarse-rh-endpoints-can-still-require-deep-source-access`, `MI-022-prefix-truncation-must-respect-cancellation-orbits`.

AF-234--AF-243 show that the RH root-rate endpoint tolerates large output error but is extremely sensitive to breaking the binomial cancellation orbit in the source representation. The live bridge must assemble the required cancellation orbit before the root-rate quotient is formed.

## Make any effective-dimension reduction source-relative or genuinely nonlinear

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-023-local-completeness-can-preserve-a-root-rate-discriminator-at-zero-density`, `MI-024-periodic-completeness-turns-li-cancellation-into-profinite-leakage`, `MI-025-regular-sampling-separates-aliasing-from-finite-window-stability`, `MI-026-zero-count-fidelity-is-distance-to-the-boundary-zero-discriminant`.

AF-282--AF-289 separate stable finite-prefix acquisition from the final zero-sensitive target. AF-290 identifies the exact zero-count robustness radius as distance to the boundary-zero discriminant; AF-291 shows that its normalized eta margin collapses on fixed RH-relevant contours despite correct analytic boundary convergence.

AF-292--AF-295 close positive diagonal reweighting, bounded-condition dense Hilbert mixing, uniformly quasisymmetric transport, and the obvious global complex-scalar quotient as free repairs. AF-296--AF-297 show that a target-radial conformal repair either preserves collapse, sends failure to an infinite-distance boundary, or explicitly pays reciprocal raw-margin scale.

AF-298 closes the canonical full-rank anisotropic escape generated by positive averages of scalar boundary evaluations: the leverage identity `int K dmu=N` leaves a sharp normalized margin `asymp N^(-1/2)` even after optimal weighting.

AF-299 now closes the most literal lower-dimensional linear escape on the unrestricted coefficient space. The boundary-zero failure set is a union of evaluation hyperplanes with trivial common kernel. For any linear quotient with nonzero kernel, its image of the failure set is the entire quotient; equivalently every rank-deficient positive-semidefinite coefficient metric has zero distance to failure. There is no nontrivial additive linear target-null direction to discard globally.

The live route is therefore narrower. A lower effective dimension must be proved **before** quotienting by restricting the admissible source family so discarded directions cannot reach the discriminant, or arise from a genuine nonlinear/projective symmetry under which failure is saturated. Otherwise the observable must be enriched—derivative/vector/nonlocal/coupled or source-derived information that changes the failure geometry rather than merely deleting coefficient directions.

## Treat the information bills separately

Finite local degree, prime breadth, mixed-channel coupling, sample count, frame conditioning, nuisance gauge, residual jitter, projective distance to failure, linear condition number, nonlinear relative distortion, radial metric singularity, observation leverage, effective target dimension, quotient geometry, and source-derived anisotropy are distinct resources. AF-298 shows that full-rank target-aligned geometry still pays a `sqrt(N)` bill; AF-299 shows that deleting an unrestricted linear direction does not reduce that bill but collapses exact zero-count robustness to zero.
