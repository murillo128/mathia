# Arithmetic-fidelity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable arithmetic-fidelity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they separate genuinely different mechanisms.

## Classify the maximal admissible quotient before constructing a repair

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-fidelity-is-quotient-relative`.

Given a compression together with a mathematically forced observable class, determine the joint indistinguishability relation generated by all admissible observables and test whether the target discriminator is constant on its fibers. In stochastic models the analogous exact object is the likelihood-ratio statistic of the whole experiment: zero loss of one strictly convex multidistribution divergence already means statistical sufficiency for every compared model.

A decisive result is either an exact factorization/recovery theorem through the maximal admissible quotient or a conflicting pair that survives every admissible observable. Downstream optimization, spectralization, or garbling cannot count as a repair unless it receives genuinely new information.

## Characterize canonical relational lifts that remove exactly the intended gauge

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-canonical-relational-lifts-can-restore-gauge`.

Use higher-order couplings, monomial lattices, Gram/compound-minor data, or matrix-valued marked spectral data to identify when relational information restores a discriminator without selecting an arbitrary absolute coordinate. The exact test is whether the remaining ambiguity group equals the intended symmetry group, including finite aliases invisible to rank-only arguments.

A decisive positive example should derive the relational observable canonically and prove completeness modulo the intended gauge. Gram compression is an important control: at full rank it already retains all relative maximal-minor signs and loses only the global `O(d)/SO(d)` orientation torsor.

## Prove bridges between differential, average, formal, and zero-error fidelity only under explicit hypotheses

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-fidelity-endpoints-are-category-dependent`.

Determine when vertical-rank, conditional-variance, statistical-sufficiency, full-jet, and support-confusability criteria imply one another after adding connectedness, quasianalyticity, support separation, or other natural assumptions. The aim is to replace informal category changes by exact bridge theorems or exact counterexamples.

A decisive result must state the topology/category and show either equivalence under sharp hypotheses or a persistent obstruction. Small average error, formal agreement, infinitesimal injectivity, or base-model symmetry breaking alone does not establish exact recoverability.

## Separate rational-prime norm fidelity from divisor fidelity

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-prime-specificity-depends-on-the-retained-analytic-layer`.

The bare multiplicative monoid retains prime type but forgets rational-prime identities and the norm `p -> p`; a chosen norm restores a weighted prime system, while the exact Euler-product function can recover the unordered norm multiset. Passing only to a meromorphic zero/pole divisor can lose that information again through zero-free factors.

The live problem is category-indexed: identify independently forced enrichments and analytic normalizations that retain ordinary rational-prime specificity after the actual downstream compression, and test them against Beurling, arithmetic-equivalence, and Grosswald--Schnitzer-type matched controls. A positive result must prove fidelity of the destination, not merely break visible prime-permutation symmetries.
