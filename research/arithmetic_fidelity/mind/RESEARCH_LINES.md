# Arithmetic-fidelity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable arithmetic-fidelity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Separate common recovery, reference provenance, and certificate calibration

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-regular-recovery-orders-the-full-multiscale-information-profile`, `MI-014-target-relative-spectral-fidelity-is-projection-geometry`, and `MI-015-whole-experiment-fidelity-needs-a-common-recovery-profile`.

AF-142--AF-145 establish the categorical layer: tangent and pairwise fidelity do not imply one family-wide reverse channel, while a common dominating reference supplies a Bayes/Petz reverse whose chi-square losses compose when that reference is propagated coherently. AF-146--AF-148 show that in-family mixture references can be badly calibrated as family complexity grows.

AF-149--AF-150 sharpen the frontier. The source-selected Shtarkov/NML envelope is the order-infinity information-radius center, escapes the private-label mixture dilution, and still supplies the same composable recovery inequality when propagated. But recomputing that center after each compression is not approximately functorial merely because the experiment itself is approximately recoverable: center drift is controlled instead by the drop in maximal leakage. Recovery quality, source-reference provenance, and numerical calibration are therefore three distinct gates.

## Derive a source-natural reference with a small propagated information-loss budget

A useful arithmetic certificate should derive a source-selected dominating reference or equivalent family-wide object, propagate it through the compression chain, and prove both a common reverse channel and a quantitatively appropriate loss profile. If stagewise recanonicalization is desired, it additionally needs a source theorem making the maximal-leakage drop small enough to control the resulting center drift.

## Classify retained interaction support before proposing repair

Linear span, sigma-algebra, interaction depth, family recovery, canonical-center drift, and certificate calibration are distinct information layers. Repairs should target the first layer that actually fails rather than treating any one divergence or canonicalization rule as a complete fidelity metric.
