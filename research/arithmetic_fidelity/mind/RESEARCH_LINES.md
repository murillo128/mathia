## Classify arithmetic incidence with the exact-zero cone, not generic access to it

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-023-local-completeness-can-preserve-a-root-rate-discriminator-at-zero-density`, `MI-024-periodic-completeness-turns-li-cancellation-into-profinite-leakage`, `MI-025-regular-sampling-separates-aliasing-from-finite-window-stability`, `MI-026-zero-count-fidelity-is-distance-to-the-boundary-zero-discriminant`, `MI-027-one-sided-source-constraints-replace-nullspace-rank-by-reachable-cone-geometry`, `MI-028-exact-zero-incidence-is-thinner-than-stable-access`.

AF-282--AF-305 replace deleted linear directions by the exact positive cone or normalized convex body reachable inside each compression fiber and isolate exact saturation `k alpha=1` as the categorical cap/support boundary. AF-306 then resolves the stable question negatively: every fixed saturated support with `k>=2` can approach zero arbitrarily closely at arbitrarily large imaginary heights, so equal-weight discreteness supplies no positive conditioning margin.

AF-307 classifies the first exact prime-support cases: saturated two-prime supports have exact imaginary-axis zeros, while every saturated three- or four-prime support is exactly zero-free there despite having zero stable margin. AF-308 now identifies the ambient geometry behind the split. After quotienting common phase, exact-zero frequency vectors for `k>=3` lie in a radial cone of Hausdorff dimension at most `k-2` inside the `(k-1)`-dimensional frequency-difference space, hence form a Lebesgue-null exceptional incidence set. Rationally independent frequency vectors nevertheless have dense torus orbits and zero stable margin.

The live `k>=5` prime-support problem is therefore specifically arithmetic incidence: decide whether vectors `(log(p_j/p_k))` can lie in that exceptional cone, or prove an arithmetic obstruction. More Kronecker--Weyl density, near-zero numerics, or generic dimension counting cannot decide membership of this special arithmetic family. Nonzero-real-part, structured-support, discrete-alphabet, nonlinear/multiplicative and vector-observation variants remain separate questions, and every exact classification must state its non-vacuity condition.
