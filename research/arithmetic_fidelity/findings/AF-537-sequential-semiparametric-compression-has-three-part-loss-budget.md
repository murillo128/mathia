# AF-537 — Sequential semiparametric compression has a three-part loss budget

## Status

`EXACT-DERIVED` · `LITERATURE+DERIVED` · `CLASSICAL-INGREDIENTS` · `ARITHMETIC-FIDELITY` · `SEMIPARAMETRIC` · `MARKOV-COMPOSITION` · `NUISANCE-EXPANSION` · `NO-NOVELTY-CLAIM`

## Claim

AF-535 gives the exact one-step loss of nuisance-robust Fisher information under a parameter-independent Markov channel, while AF-536 gives a sharper source-space composition law for nested deterministic statistics. The general Markov setting also composes exactly, but there is a third failure mode whenever the downstream model declares **more nuisance directions than those merely pushed forward from upstream**.

Let stage `j` of a regular local experiment have tangent Hilbert space

\[
H_j=L_0^2(P_j),
\]

target score `s_j`, closed nuisance tangent space `\Lambda_j\subset H_j`, efficient score

\[
e_j=(I-\Pi_{\Lambda_j})s_j,
\qquad
I_j^{\rm eff}=\|e_j\|^2.
\tag{1}
\]

Let a parameter-independent Markov kernel send stage `j` to stage `j+1`, and write its score contraction as

\[
C_j:H_j\to H_{j+1},
\qquad
C_j f=\mathbb E[f(X_j)\mid X_{j+1}].
\tag{2}
\]

Assume the downstream target score is `s_{j+1}=C_j s_j` and that its actual nuisance tangent space contains the pushed-forward upstream nuisance:

\[
N_{j+1}:=\overline{C_j\Lambda_j}
\subseteq
\Lambda_{j+1}.
\tag{3}
\]

Define the genuinely new downstream nuisance increment

\[
D_{j+1}:=\Lambda_{j+1}\cap N_{j+1}^{\perp},
\qquad
\Lambda_{j+1}=N_{j+1}\oplus D_{j+1}.
\tag{4}
\]

Then the efficient score propagates recursively by

\[
\boxed{
e_{j+1}=(I-\Pi_{\Lambda_{j+1}})C_j e_j.
}
\tag{5}
\]

Consequently the one-stage efficient-information loss has the exact nonnegative decomposition

\[
\boxed{
I_j^{\rm eff}-I_{j+1}^{\rm eff}
=
\underbrace{\bigl(\|e_j\|^2-\|C_j e_j\|^2\bigr)}_{\text{direct channel erasure}}
+
\underbrace{\|\Pi_{N_{j+1}}C_j e_j\|^2}_{\text{pushed-nuisance recontamination}}
+
\underbrace{\|\Pi_{D_{j+1}}C_j e_j\|^2}_{\text{new-nuisance reclassification}}.
}
\tag{6}
\]

For a chain of `m` such stages, the total loss telescopes exactly:

\[
\boxed{
I_0^{\rm eff}-I_m^{\rm eff}
=
\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}
\left[
E_j+R_j+Q_j
\right],
}
\tag{7}
\]

where `E_j`, `R_j`, and `Q_j` are the three terms in (6). Thus every loss can be assigned to the first stage where it occurs, and no later parameter-independent post-processing can cancel an earlier positive contribution.

The equality boundary separates data loss from semantic loss. Since conditional expectation is an `L^2` contraction,

\[
E_j=0
\iff
e_j(X_j)\text{ is measurable with respect to }X_{j+1}
\tag{8}
\]

under the baseline joint law. When (8) holds, upstream nuisance orthogonality transfers automatically to `N_{j+1}`, so `R_j=0`. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
I_{j+1}^{\rm eff}=I_j^{\rm eff}
\iff
\begin{cases}
e_j\text{ survives the channel exactly},\\
e_j\perp D_{j+1}.
\end{cases}
}
\tag{9}
\]

If there is no downstream nuisance enlargement, `D_{j+1}=\{0\}`, and (9) reduces to AF-535's efficient-score measurability criterion. If the observation is lossless but `D_{j+1}` overlaps the efficient direction, information is lost solely because the declared equivalence relation has changed. No observation-side lift can repair that loss while the enlarged nuisance semantics are kept fixed.

## Representation declaration

This is a local regular statistical representation used to study fidelity under sequential compression; it is not an intrinsic arithmetic model.

- **Source at stage `j`:** the tangent experiment `(H_j,s_j,\Lambda_j)`.
- **Target carrier:** the efficient score `e_j`, the orthogonal representative of the target direction modulo nuisance.
- **Forward operation:** a parameter-independent Markov kernel represented on scores by conditional expectation `C_j`.
- **Propagated equivalence:** `N_{j+1}=\overline{C_j\Lambda_j}`.
- **Additional equivalence change:** `D_{j+1}` records nuisance directions declared downstream that are not generated by pushing forward upstream nuisance paths.
- **Fidelity quantity:** local nuisance-robust Fisher information `\|e_j\|^2`.

The three terms in (6) therefore distinguish three mathematically different operations that can otherwise look like one generic "compression loss": deleting target score, mixing surviving target score with already legitimate nuisance, and changing the downstream notion of which directions count as nuisance at all.

## Derivation

Write

\[
v_j=\Pi_{\Lambda_j}s_j,
\qquad
s_j=e_j+v_j,
\qquad
v_j\in\Lambda_j.
\tag{10}
\]

The downstream target score is

\[
s_{j+1}=C_j s_j=C_j e_j+C_j v_j.
\tag{11}
\]

By (3), `C_jv_j\in N_{j+1}\subseteq\Lambda_{j+1}`. Orthogonal projection away from the downstream nuisance therefore removes the entire pushed nuisance component:

\[
\begin{aligned}
e_{j+1}
&=(I-\Pi_{\Lambda_{j+1}})s_{j+1}\\
&=(I-\Pi_{\Lambda_{j+1}})(C_j e_j+C_jv_j)\\
&=(I-\Pi_{\Lambda_{j+1}})C_j e_j,
\end{aligned}
\tag{12}
\]

which proves (5). This is the compositional point: once the efficient score has been formed at one stage, the raw target score and the already-removed nuisance component need not be carried further to compute the next efficient score, provided downstream nuisance contains the propagated upstream nuisance.

Pythagoras gives

\[
\|e_{j+1}\|^2
=
\|C_j e_j\|^2
-
\|\Pi_{\Lambda_{j+1}}C_j e_j\|^2.
\tag{13}
\]

Subtracting (13) from `\|e_j\|^2` yields

\[
I_j^{\rm eff}-I_{j+1}^{\rm eff}
=
\bigl(\|e_j\|^2-\|C_je_j\|^2\bigr)
+
\|\Pi_{\Lambda_{j+1}}C_je_j\|^2.
\tag{14}
\]

Since `N_{j+1}` is a closed subspace of the closed space `\Lambda_{j+1}`, equation (4) is an orthogonal direct sum. Hence

\[
\|\Pi_{\Lambda_{j+1}}C_je_j\|^2
=
\|\Pi_{N_{j+1}}C_je_j\|^2
+
\|\Pi_{D_{j+1}}C_je_j\|^2,
\tag{15}
\]

which proves (6). Summing (6) over the chain immediately gives (7).

For the equality criterion, `E_j=0` is equality in the conditional-expectation contraction. Under the joint realization this occurs exactly when `e_j(X_j)` is already measurable with respect to the downstream observation. In that case `C_je_j=e_j` as a downstream random variable. For every `\lambda\in\Lambda_j`,

\[
\langle e_j,C_j\lambda\rangle_{H_{j+1}}
=
\mathbb E[e_j(X_{j+1})\,\mathbb E(\lambda(X_j)\mid X_{j+1})]
=
\mathbb E[e_j(X_j)\lambda(X_j)]
=0.
\tag{16}
\]

Thus `e_j\perp N_{j+1}` automatically. The only remaining obstruction to equality is its component in the new nuisance increment `D_{j+1}`, proving (9).

For a genuine coarsening chain all summands in (7) are nonnegative. If the first positive contribution occurs at stage `r`, then

\[
I_r^{\rm eff}<I_0^{\rm eff},
\qquad
I_k^{\rm eff}\le I_r^{\rm eff}
\quad(k\ge r),
\tag{17}
\]

so later stages cannot restore the original first-order information. They may preserve the already-reduced efficient score, but they cannot subtract a previous loss term from the budget.

## Matched control: information loss with no observation loss

The third term in (6) is not a disguised channel defect. It can be positive even when the observation is unchanged.

Let

\[
X\sim N(\theta e_1+\eta e_3,I_3),
\tag{18}
\]

with target parameter `\theta` and upstream nuisance `\eta`. At the origin the target score is `X_1`, the nuisance tangent is

\[
\Lambda_0=\operatorname{span}\{X_3\},
\tag{19}
\]

and the efficient score is

\[
e_0=X_1,
\qquad
I_0^{\rm eff}=1.
\tag{20}
\]

Use the identity observation, so `C_0=I`. There is no direct channel erasure and no pushed-nuisance recontamination. Now enlarge the downstream model by an additional nuisance parameter `\zeta` in the unit direction

\[
u=\cos\alpha\,e_1+\sin\alpha\,e_2,
\qquad 0\le\alpha\le\frac{\pi}{2},
\tag{21}
\]

so that the downstream family has mean

\[
\theta e_1+\eta e_3+\zeta u.
\tag{22}
\]

Then

\[
N_1=\operatorname{span}\{X_3\},
\qquad
D_1=\operatorname{span}\{\cos\alpha X_1+\sin\alpha X_2\}.
\tag{23}
\]

The first two terms of (6) are zero, while

\[
Q_0
=
\|\Pi_{D_1}X_1\|^2
=
\cos^2\alpha.
\tag{24}
\]

Therefore

\[
I_1^{\rm eff}=\sin^2\alpha.
\tag{25}
\]

At `\alpha=0` the target direction is newly declared nuisance and all efficient information disappears although the full observation has been retained. At `\alpha=\pi/2` the new nuisance direction is orthogonal to the target and no information is lost. Intermediate angles give a continuous semantic-loss scale.

This control separates **representation/equivalence change** from **data compression**. If an arithmetic construction changes its notion of admissible controls, gauge directions, or equivalence classes between stages, a loss attributed to a downstream transform may actually come from the changed nuisance relation. Conversely, adding more observations cannot repair a target direction that the destination problem itself has declared equivalent to nuisance.

AF-535 already proves the one-channel efficient-score formula and two-term loss decomposition when the downstream nuisance is exactly the closure of pushed-forward nuisance. AF-536 specializes deterministic statistics further to the source-space closed sum `\overline{\Lambda+K}` and derives an orthogonal composition budget for nested deterministic compressions.

AF-537 does not claim that sequential efficient-information monotonicity is new. Its line-local contribution is the exact **recursive efficient-score propagation** (5), the chain budget (7), and the separation of downstream loss into observation erasure, propagated-nuisance recontamination, and genuinely new nuisance reclassification. The third term makes explicit a distinction that generic data-processing language can hide: changing what counts as nuisance is itself an information quotient, even when the observation channel is lossless.

## Boundary conditions and falsification tests

1. **The downstream nuisance inclusion is essential.** Equation (5) assumes `\overline{C_j\Lambda_j}\subseteq\Lambda_{j+1}`. If a purported downstream model excludes score directions generated by valid upstream nuisance paths, it is not the same nuisance-preserving transformation problem and the recursion must be re-derived for that different model.

2. **The channel must be parameter-independent.** Parameter-dependent observation mechanisms have their own score contributions; conditional expectation of the upstream score is then incomplete.

3. **This is local first-order information.** The budget concerns efficient Fisher information at the chosen baselines. It does not prove Blackwell equivalence, global identifiability, finite-sample recovery, or an arithmetic rigidity theorem.

4. **Closures matter.** `N_{j+1}` is the closed propagated nuisance tangent. Infinite-dimensional nuisance images need not be closed before taking closure, as AF-534 and AF-536 already illustrate in related settings.

5. **The third term is only meaningful when the nuisance enlargement is independently justified.** One may force any desired loss by declaring the target direction to be nuisance. An arithmetic application must derive the destination equivalence class, gauge freedom, control family, or admissible deformation independently of the desired conclusion.

6. **Observation repair and semantic repair are different.** When `Q_j>0`, retaining additional source observables can remove `E_j` and perhaps `R_j`, but it cannot eliminate overlap with a fixed enlarged nuisance space. Repair then requires changing or refining the target/nuisance relation itself, not merely attaching a richer mark.

7. **No resurrection assumes a genuine forward chain.** A later experiment supplied with a new parameter-bearing observation not generated through the preceding Markov channel is additional data, not post-processing, and need not obey the monotone budget from the original chain.

8. **Vector targets need the operator/matrix version.** Equations (1)–(9) are stated for one scalar target direction. A vector target replaces squared norms by efficient-information operators/matrices and requires the corresponding Loewner-order formulation.

## Consequence for Arithmetic Fidelity

The line's compression question has two logically independent axes:

\[
\text{what the transformation forgets}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\text{what the destination declares irrelevant}.
\tag{26}
\]

AF-535 showed that one Markov channel can both erase efficient score and recontaminate what survives. AF-536 showed that deterministic nested compressions admit an exact orthogonal source-space loss budget. AF-537 extends the compositional picture to general Markov chains and isolates a further semantic quotient whenever nuisance classes grow along the route.

For later arithmetic use, a proposed path through several representations should therefore audit each arrow with three questions: whether the discriminator survives the observation channel, whether surviving components become indistinguishable from already admitted controls, and whether the destination introduces new equivalences that absorb the discriminator. A failure of the third kind is not cured by a richer observation unless the destination equivalence itself is refined.
