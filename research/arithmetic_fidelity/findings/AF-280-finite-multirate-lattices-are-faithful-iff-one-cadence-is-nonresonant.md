# AF-280 — Finite multirate lattices are faithful iff one cadence is nonresonant

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED`, `LITERATURE+DERIVED`, `CLASSICAL-STRUCTURE`, `ARITHMETIC-FIDELITY`, `DIRICHLET-SERIES`, `MULTIRATE-SAMPLING`, `ALIAS-INTERACTION`, `STABILITY-OBSTRUCTION`, `NO-NOVELTY-CLAIM`

## Claim

Let

\[
F(s)=\sum_{n\ge1}a_n n^{-s}
\]

and fix a real `c` with

\[
\sum_{n\ge1}|a_n|n^{-c}<\infty.
\]

For finitely many sampling cadences `h_1,\ldots,h_J>0`, retain every bilateral sample on every lattice,

\[
\mathcal S_{\mathbf h}(F)
=
\bigl(F(c+ikh_j)\bigr)_{1\le j\le J,\ k\in\mathbb Z}.
\]

For one cadence define its rational resonance subgroup

\[
R_h
=
\{r\in\mathbb Q_{>0}:h\log r\in2\pi\mathbb Z\}.
\]

AF-279 implies that the single-lattice channel at `h` is coefficient-faithful exactly when `R_h=\{1\}`. For a **finite family** there is no new exact-fidelity regime:

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal S_{\mathbf h}\text{ is injective on the weighted }\ell^1
\text{ ordinary-Dirichlet coefficient class}
\iff
R_{h_j}=\{1\}\text{ for at least one }j.
}
\]

The forward direction is the nontrivial part. If **every** cadence is resonant, choose `r_j\in R_{h_j}\setminus\{1\}`. There is then a nonzero finite-support Dirichlet polynomial whose values vanish on every one of the complete lattices. Thus finitely many individually aliased regular samplers cannot repair one another on the unrestricted coefficient class, no matter how different their spacings are.

Moreover, finite cadence diversity does not repair AF-279's finite-window instability. For every finite cadence family, every target coefficient `N`, every fixed collection of windows `|k|\le K_j`, and every `\varepsilon>0`, there are two finite-support Dirichlet series at fixed weighted `\ell^1` distance whose weighted coefficient at `N` differs by one while

\[
\max_{1\le j\le J}\max_{|k|\le K_j}
|F(c+ikh_j)-G(c+ikh_j)|<\varepsilon.
\]

Hence finite multirate acquisition has two sharp limits on this source class:

1. with complete infinite lattices, exact fidelity occurs only if one cadence is already nonresonant by itself;
2. with any preassigned finite observation windows, no finite family has a uniform inverse modulus for a fixed coefficient.

The missing resource is therefore not merely “more cadences.” A useful multirate recovery theorem must add a source restriction, a genuinely joint/mixed observable, or an acquisition resource that grows with the source breadth being resolved.

## Exact invisible interaction from the rational resonance subgroups

Write weighted coefficients

\[
b_n=a_n n^{-c},
\qquad
\sum_n|b_n|<\infty.
\]

At imaginary height `t`,

\[
F(c+it)=\sum_{n\ge1}b_n e^{-it\log n}.
\]

Assume every `R_{h_j}` is nontrivial and choose `r_j\in R_{h_j}\setminus\{1\}`. Consider the finite group-algebra element in the multiplicative group `\mathbb Q_{>0}`,

\[
P=\prod_{j=1}^J(1-[r_j])
\in\mathbb C[\mathbb Q_{>0}].
\]

This element is nonzero. Indeed, the finitely generated subgroup of `\mathbb Q_{>0}` containing all `r_j` embeds in a finite-rank free abelian group generated by the finitely many primes occurring in their factorizations; its group algebra is therefore a Laurent polynomial ring over `\mathbb C`, hence an integral domain.

Write

\[
P=\sum_{q\in E}c_q[q]
\]

with finite `E\subset\mathbb Q_{>0}` and not all `c_q` zero. Choose an integer `M` clearing the denominators of all `q\in E`, and define a finite weighted coefficient perturbation by

\[
\Delta b_{Mq}=c_q,
\qquad q\in E,
\]

with all other coordinates zero. Distinct rational `q` give distinct integer indices `Mq`, so this perturbation is nonzero.

For cadence `h_j` and any `k\in\mathbb Z`, define the character

\[
\chi_{j,k}(q)=e^{-ikh_j\log q}.
\]

Since `r_j\in R_{h_j}`,

\[
\chi_{j,k}(r_j)=1.
\]

Therefore evaluation of `P` under `\chi_{j,k}` kills its `j`th factor:

\[
\sum_{q\in E}c_qe^{-ikh_j\log q}
=\chi_{j,k}(P)=0.
\]

Consequently

\[
\begin{aligned}
\Delta F(c+ikh_j)
&=\sum_{q\in E}c_qe^{-ikh_j\log(Mq)}\\
&=e^{-ikh_j\log M}\chi_{j,k}(P)\\
&=0
\end{aligned}
\]

for every `j` and every integer `k`.

Equivalently, in the original coefficients one may write the invisible Dirichlet polynomial as

\[
\boxed{
\Delta F(s)
=M^{c-s}
\prod_{j=1}^J\bigl(1-r_j^{\,c-s}\bigr),
}
\]

understanding the expanded rational scales after the common denominator `M` as ordinary integer Dirichlet monomials. At `s=c+ikh_j`, the `j`th factor vanishes exactly.

This proves noninjectivity whenever every cadence has a nontrivial rational alias. Conversely, if some `R_{h_j}=\{1\}`, AF-279 says that cadence alone recovers every coefficient, so the whole multirate channel is injective.

## Two-prime example: the joint phase tuple can be injective while the lattices are not

Take

\[
h_1=\frac{2\pi}{\log2},
\qquad
h_2=\frac{2\pi}{\log3}.
\]

For any integer `N\ge1`, the weighted four-point perturbation

\[
\Delta b_N=1,
\qquad
\Delta b_{2N}=-1,
\qquad
\Delta b_{3N}=-1,
\qquad
\Delta b_{6N}=1
\]

has transform

\[
\Delta F(c+it)
=e^{-it\log N}
(1-e^{-it\log2})(1-e^{-it\log3}).
\]

It vanishes for every `t=kh_1` and every `t=kh_2`.

Yet on these four source indices the **joint** phase map

\[
n\longmapsto
\bigl(e^{-ih_1\log n},e^{-ih_2\log n}\bigr)
\]

is injective. Equality of both phase coordinates for two of the four indices would force their ratio to be simultaneously an integral power of `2` and an integral power of `3`, hence equal to `1`.

So the failure is not that the combined phase tuple lacks enough labels. The acquisition keeps the two complete one-coordinate pushforwards separately and never observes their joint coupling. The four-term alternating perturbation is exactly the `2\times2` interaction cycle predicted abstractly by AF-031/AF-032.

This distinction matters operationally: concatenating complete aliased channels is not the same compression as measuring the joint phase law. Additional post-processing of the separate sample sequences cannot recreate a coupling component already annihilated in every channel.

## Finite windows remain nonuniformly conditioned for every finite cadence family

The exact theorem above concerns the complete bilateral lattices. Even if one cadence is nonresonant and therefore makes the infinite channel injective, no fixed finite collection of sample windows gives uniform coefficient recovery on the unrestricted weighted `\ell^1` source ball.

Fix `N`. Put

\[
\alpha_j=\frac{h_j}{2\pi}.
\]

The cyclic subgroup generated by

\[
\alpha=(\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_J)
\]

in the compact torus `\mathbb T^J` returns arbitrarily close to the identity along an unbounded sequence of positive integers `q_\ell`. Equivalently,

\[
q_\ell\alpha_j\to0\pmod1
\qquad\text{for every }j.
\]

This is the standard simultaneous Diophantine-recurrence statement: if the orbit is finite, exact returns repeat periodically; if it is infinite, compactness of its subgroup closure gives arbitrarily close returns to the identity.

Choose an integer `m_\ell` nearest to `Ne^{q_\ell}`. Then

\[
\log(m_\ell/N)=q_\ell+o(1),
\]

and therefore, simultaneously for every cadence,

\[
e^{-ih_j\log m_\ell}
\longrightarrow
e^{-ih_j\log N}.
\]

Now set

\[
\Delta b_N=1,
\qquad
\Delta b_{m_\ell}=-1.
\]

Its weighted `\ell^1` norm is exactly `2`, its target displacement at `N` is exactly `1`, and for every fixed finite set of windows,

\[
\max_j\max_{|k|\le K_j}
\left|
 e^{-ikh_j\log N}
-e^{-ikh_j\log m_\ell}
\right|
\longrightarrow0.
\]

Thus adding finitely many cadences can improve phase separation at any fixed finite source cutoff, but it cannot yield a source-breadth-independent finite-window inverse modulus on the full ordinary-Dirichlet `\ell^1` class.

## Prior art and novelty assessment

The surrounding sampling theory is classical, and no general multirate-sampling novelty is claimed.

- A. Papoulis, **“Generalized Sampling Expansion,”** *IEEE Transactions on Circuits and Systems* 24 (1977), 652–654, introduced the classical multichannel extension of Shannon sampling for structurally restricted band-limited signals. R. F. Hoskins and J. de Sousa Pinto, **“Generalized Sampling Expansions in the Sense of Papoulis,”** *SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics* 44(3) (1984), 611–617, DOI `10.1137/0144043`, gives a rigorous extension and records the classical multichannel framework.
- Michael Fleyer, Alexander Linden, Moshe Horowitz, and Amir Rosenthal, **“Multirate Synchronous Sampling of Sparse Multiband Signals,”** *IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing* 58(3) (2010), 1144–1156, DOI `10.1109/TSP.2009.2034906`, reconstruct signals from several aliased sampling rates by exploiting sparse multiband structure. Their reconstruction mechanism explicitly relies on source sparsity and on enough spectral content being unaliased across channels.
- Moshe Mishali and Yonina C. Eldar, **“Blind Multiband Signal Reconstruction: Compressed Sensing for Analog Signals,”** *IEEE Transactions on Signal Processing* 57(3) (2009), 993–1009, DOI `10.1109/TSP.2009.2012791`, likewise obtains sub-Nyquist recovery only after restricting the admissible source family by multiband sparsity/width structure.
- AF-031 classifies the difference between retaining a joint feature law and retaining only complete coordinate marginals; AF-032 identifies higher-order interaction components invisible to lower-arity marginals. AF-280 is their arithmetic-sampling specialization together with AF-279's exact rational resonance criterion.

The derived group-algebra counterexample is therefore not a claim that multiple-rate sampling cannot work. Classical multirate and multicoset results show that it can work under bandlimit, sparsity, bounded spectral support, or related structural assumptions. The exact Arithmetic Fidelity result is narrower: **on the unrestricted weighted `\ell^1` ordinary-Dirichlet frequency class, finitely many complete regular lattices are injective only when one lattice was already injective by itself.**

A targeted prior-art search found the expected multichannel/multirate aliasing and sparse-recovery literature, but not this exact rational-resonance subgroup classification for ordinary Dirichlet frequencies. That is not an exhaustive novelty proof; the theorem is presented as an exact specialization assembled from classical group-algebra, harmonic-analysis, and sampling ideas.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- The theorem is for a **finite** number of cadences. A countably infinite cadence family can have different separation and stability properties.
- The source class is the unrestricted weighted `\ell^1` class on the known ordinary frequencies `\log n`. Sparsity bounds, finite source cutoffs, positivity, Euler multiplicativity, coefficient decay, or another nonlinear arithmetic category can remove the explicit kernel.
- The exact noninjectivity construction uses signed/complex coefficient perturbations. It does not by itself give two positive Dirichlet series, two Euler products, or two admissible zeta-like objects with the same multirate samples.
- The finite-window obstruction concerns preassigned bounded windows. It does not rule out recovery when the observation horizon grows with the requested source breadth, nor does it quantify the optimal growth law.
- The separate-lattice channel keeps each cadence's one-dimensional sample sequence. A genuinely joint acquisition mechanism that measures mixed functions of several wrapped phases is a different channel and is not covered by the noninjectivity theorem.
- No analytic continuation, zero-location theorem, functional equation, or RH implication follows from this result.

## Decisive audit test

When several regular samplers are proposed as a cure for aliasing in an arithmetic analytic carrier, compute each rational resonance subgroup

\[
R_{h_j}=\{r\in\mathbb Q_{>0}:h_j\log r\in2\pi\mathbb Z\}.
\]

If every subgroup is nontrivial and the source category contains unrestricted finite signed coefficient perturbations, the group-algebra product above gives an exact invisible interaction before any downstream analysis. Cadence diversity has not restored fidelity.

If one cadence is nonresonant, exact infinite-data fidelity is available, but the next question is quantitative: what source restriction or growing observation budget prevents simultaneous remote phase recurrence from destroying the inverse modulus? In either case, the audit must distinguish **exact separation**, **joint-coupling retention**, and **finite-acquisition stability**.

## Consequence for the research line

AF-279 left cadence diversity as one possible resource for resolving wrapped logarithmic frequencies. AF-280 closes the simplest version of that escape. On the unrestricted ordinary-Dirichlet coefficient class, finitely many complete regular lattices do not combine several lossy quotients into a faithful one: if every cadence has an exact rational alias, a finite multiplicative interaction survives in the common kernel. If one cadence has no rational alias, exact recovery was already available from that cadence alone.

Finite cadence diversity also does not remove the source-breadth/horizon bill: any fixed finite windows admit simultaneous remote near-aliases. The remaining live routes must therefore use a genuinely different resource—source-category rigidity, mixed/joint observations, nonregular sampling, a cadence family that grows with the target breadth, or an explicit quantitative horizon/precision law.
