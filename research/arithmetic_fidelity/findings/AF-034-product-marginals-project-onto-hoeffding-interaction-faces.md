# AF-034 — Product marginals project exactly onto Hoeffding interaction faces

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED`, `LITERATURE+DERIVED`, `CLASSICAL-IDENTITY`, `STRUCTURAL-CLASSIFICATION`

## Claim

Let

\[
(X_i,\Sigma_i,q_i),\qquad i=1,\ldots,d,
\]

be probability spaces and let

\[
Q=\bigotimes_{i=1}^d q_i
\]

be their product measure on `X=\prod_i X_i`. Write

\[
L_i^2=L^2(q_i),
\qquad
L_{0,i}^2=\left\{f\in L_i^2:\int f\,dq_i=0\right\},
\qquad
C_i=\operatorname{span}\{1\}.
\]

For every `S\subseteq[d]`, define the Hoeffding interaction subspace

\[
\mathcal H_S
=
\widehat\bigotimes_{i\in S}L_{0,i}^2
\;\widehat\otimes\;
\widehat\bigotimes_{i\notin S}C_i
\subseteq L^2(Q),
\]

with `\mathcal H_\varnothing=C_1\widehat\otimes\cdots\widehat\otimes C_d` the constants. Then

\[
\boxed{
L^2(Q)=\bigoplus_{S\subseteq[d]}\mathcal H_S
}
\]

orthogonally. Write `f=\sum_S f_S` for this decomposition.

For `A\subseteq[d]`, let

\[
P_A f
=\mathbb E_Q[f\mid \sigma(X_i:i\in A)]
\]

be conditional expectation, identified with a function of `x_A`. Then

\[
\boxed{
P_A f=\sum_{S\subseteq A}f_S.
}
\]

Thus coordinate marginalization does not merely lower dimension: it deletes exactly those interaction subspaces whose support is not contained in the retained coordinate set.

Now let

\[
\mathcal M_2(Q)
=
\left\{\mu:\mu\text{ is a finite signed measure},\ \mu\ll Q,\ r_\mu:=\frac{d\mu}{dQ}\in L^2(Q)\right\}.
\]

For a family of retained coordinate marginals `\mathcal A\subseteq2^{[d]}`, define

\[
\mathsf M_{\mathcal A}(\mu)
=
\bigl((\pi_A)_*\mu\bigr)_{A\in\mathcal A},
\]

and its downward closure

\[
\Delta(\mathcal A)
=
\{S\subseteq[d]:S\subseteq A\text{ for some }A\in\mathcal A\}.
\]

Assume `\mathcal A` is nonempty, so `\varnothing\in\Delta`. Then:

1. **Marginal densities are conditional expectations.** For every `A`, the density of `(\pi_A)_*\mu` with respect to `Q_A=\bigotimes_{i\in A}q_i` is `P_A r_\mu`. Consequently
   \[
   \boxed{
   \mathsf M_{\mathcal A}(\mu)=\mathsf M_{\mathcal A}(\nu)
   \iff
   (r_\mu-r_\nu)_S=0
   \quad\forall S\in\Delta.
   }
   \]

2. **The complete `L^2` marginal kernel is the missing-face interaction space.** Under the identification `h\leftrightarrow hQ`,
   \[
   \boxed{
   \ker\mathsf M_{\mathcal A}
   =
   \left\{hQ:h\in\bigoplus_{S\notin\Delta}\mathcal H_S\right\}.
   }
   \]
   A whole interaction subspace, not merely one coefficient, is lost for every missing face.

3. **The Boolean simplicial fidelity lattice survives unchanged.** If every coordinate is nontrivial in the sense `L_{0,i}^2\ne\{0\}`, then for two downward-closed scenarios `\Delta_1,\Delta_2`,
   \[
   \boxed{
   \mathsf M_{\Delta_1}
   \text{ factors deterministically through }
   \mathsf M_{\Delta_2}
   \iff
   \Delta_1\subseteq\Delta_2.
   }
   \]
   Hence coordinate-marginal information states are still ordered exactly by simplicial inclusion, even when the coordinate spaces are continuous or infinite.

4. **Every missing face contains genuine probability collisions.** Suppose `S\notin\Delta`, `S\ne\varnothing`, and for each `i\in S` choose a measurable event `E_i` with `0<q_i(E_i)<1`. Put
   \[
   \phi_i=\mathbf1_{E_i}-q_i(E_i),
   \qquad
   h_S(x)=\prod_{i\in S}\phi_i(x_i).
   \]
   Then `h_S\in\mathcal H_S` is bounded, centered, and nonzero. For
   \[
   0<\varepsilon\le\|h_S\|_\infty^{-1},
   \qquad
   \frac{dP_\pm}{dQ}=1\pm\varepsilon h_S,
   \]
   `P_+` and `P_-` are distinct probability measures but
   \[
   \boxed{
   \mathsf M_\Delta(P_+)=\mathsf M_\Delta(P_-).
   }
   \]
   Thus the missing-face kernel is not an artifact of signed measures or finite alphabets.

5. **Target-relative linear fidelity is exactly Hoeffding-support containment.** For `g\in L^2(Q)`, define
   \[
   D_g(\mu)=\int g\,d\mu=\langle g,r_\mu\rangle_{L^2(Q)}.
   \]
   Then on `\mathcal M_2(Q)`, and also on the probability measures in this class,
   \[
   \boxed{
   D_g\text{ is exactly recoverable from }\mathsf M_\Delta
   \iff
   \operatorname{supp}_H(g):=\{S:g_S\ne0\}\subseteq\Delta.
   }
   \]
   For a target family `\mathcal G`, exact recovery is equivalent to containing the union of the Hoeffding supports of all targets.

6. **The exact quadratic defect is the missing interaction energy.** Let
   \[
   V_\Delta=\bigoplus_{S\in\Delta}\mathcal H_S.
   \]
   This is the closed subspace of `L^2(Q)` generated by observables measurable with respect to the retained marginal channels. The best `L^2` approximation of `g` by retained interaction tests satisfies
   \[
   \boxed{
   \inf_{v\in V_\Delta}\|g-v\|_2^2
   =
   \sum_{S\notin\Delta}\|g_S\|_2^2.
   }
   \]
   The right-hand side is therefore an exact linear-test fidelity defect: it quantifies the target component orthogonal to everything the retained marginal scenario can measure.

7. **Minimal marginal lifts remain a covering problem.** For a family of target observables `\mathcal G`, let
   \[
   R=\bigcup_{g\in\mathcal G}\operatorname{supp}_H(g),
   \qquad
   U=R\setminus\Delta.
   \]
   If new admissible marginal channels are coordinate sets `A\in\mathcal A_{\rm adm}` with costs `c(A)>0`, adding `A` supplies every interaction face in `2^A`. Hence a lift family `\mathcal L\subseteq\mathcal A_{\rm adm}` recovers all targets exactly iff
   \[
   \boxed{
   \forall S\in U\ \exists A\in\mathcal L:\ S\subseteq A.
   }
   \]
   The minimum-cost exact marginal lift is therefore the weighted set-cover problem on the missing target interaction support, exactly as in the Boolean case.

8. **Finite nonbinary products acquire face multiplicities.** If each `X_i` is finite of size `m_i` and `q_i` has full support, then
   \[
   \dim L_{0,i}^2=m_i-1,
   \qquad
   \boxed{
   \dim\mathcal H_S=\prod_{i\in S}(m_i-1).
   }
   \]
   Therefore
   \[
   \boxed{
   \dim\ker\mathsf M_\Delta
   =
   \sum_{S\notin\Delta}\prod_{i\in S}(m_i-1).
   }
   \]
   AF-033 is the special binary case `m_i=2`, where every face carries exactly one Walsh interaction coordinate. In a general product space the same simplicial face may carry a high- or infinite-dimensional interaction subspace.

The reusable Arithmetic Fidelity conclusion is

\[
\boxed{
\text{under a product reference law, coordinate marginalization preserves exactly the Hoeffding faces contained in the retained scenario.}
}
\]

The simplicial combinatorics discovered in the Boolean model is therefore not a Boolean accident. What changes beyond the Boolean cube is the internal dimension and geometry attached to each retained or missing face.

## Orthogonal tensor decomposition

Each coordinate Hilbert space splits orthogonally as

\[
L_i^2=C_i\oplus L_{0,i}^2.
\]

Taking the finite Hilbert tensor product and distributing over these orthogonal sums gives

\[
\widehat\bigotimes_{i=1}^dL_i^2
=
\bigoplus_{S\subseteq[d]}
\left(
\widehat\bigotimes_{i\in S}L_{0,i}^2
\widehat\otimes
\widehat\bigotimes_{i\notin S}C_i
\right).
\]

The canonical product-space identification

\[
\widehat\bigotimes_{i=1}^dL^2(q_i)
\cong L^2(Q)
\]

therefore yields the displayed Hoeffding decomposition.

On an elementary tensor

\[
f(x)=\prod_{i=1}^df_i(x_i),
\]

conditional expectation onto coordinates `A` integrates every factor outside `A`:

\[
P_Af
=
\left(\prod_{i\in A}f_i(x_i)\right)
\left(\prod_{j\notin A}\int f_j\,dq_j\right).
\]

If an interaction tensor lies in `\mathcal H_S` and `S\not\subseteq A`, at least one centered factor is integrated out and the result is zero. If `S\subseteq A`, every factor outside `A` is constant and the tensor survives unchanged. Density of elementary tensors and continuity of conditional expectation extend this to the whole subspace, proving

\[
P_A|_{\mathcal H_S}
=
\begin{cases}
I,&S\subseteq A,\\
0,&S\not\subseteq A.
\end{cases}
\]

Summing over `S` gives `P_Af=\sum_{S\subseteq A}f_S`.

## Marginal densities and the exact kernel

Let `\mu\in\mathcal M_2(Q)` with density `r`. For every bounded measurable test function `\psi` on `X_A`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
\int_{X_A}\psi\,d(\pi_A)_*\mu
&=\int_X\psi(x_A)r(x)\,dQ(x)\\
&=\int_{X_A}\psi(x_A)\,P_A r(x_A)\,dQ_A(x_A).
\end{aligned}
\]

Thus

\[
\frac{d(\pi_A)_*\mu}{dQ_A}=P_A r.
\]

For `h=r_\mu-r_\nu`, equality of all retained marginals is therefore equivalent to

\[
P_Ah=0\qquad\forall A\in\mathcal A.
\]

But

\[
P_Ah=\sum_{S\subseteq A}h_S
\]

is an orthogonal sum. Hence all these equations hold iff `h_S=0` for every face appearing inside at least one retained `A`, namely every `S\in\Delta(\mathcal A)`. This proves both the equality criterion and the kernel formula.

It also explains why the downward closure is forced rather than chosen: retaining an `A`-marginal automatically retains every interaction supported inside `A`, and nothing supported outside it.

## Factorization order and positive collision witnesses

If `\Delta_1\subseteq\Delta_2`, the `\Delta_1` data are obtained from `\Delta_2` by ordinary marginalization, so deterministic factorization is immediate.

Conversely choose

\[
S\in\Delta_1\setminus\Delta_2.
\]

Because `\varnothing` belongs to every nonempty downward-closed scenario, `S` is nonempty. If each coordinate is nontrivial, choose centered bounded nonzero `\phi_i` as in the claim and form

\[
h_S=\prod_{i\in S}\phi_i\in\mathcal H_S.
\]

For sufficiently small `\varepsilon>0`, the two probability densities `1\pm\varepsilon h_S` are nonnegative and integrate to one. Every `\Delta_2` marginal agrees because `S\notin\Delta_2`, whereas any retained `A\supseteq S` in `\Delta_1` sees the nonzero `S` component. Hence no deterministic map from the `\Delta_2` destination can recover the `\Delta_1` destination.

This simultaneously proves the converse factorization statement and gives a probability collision in every missing interaction face.

## Target fidelity and missing interaction energy

The marginal kernel in density coordinates is

\[
K_\Delta=V_\Delta^\perp
=\bigoplus_{S\notin\Delta}\mathcal H_S.
\]

A linear target `D_g` factors through the marginal map exactly when it annihilates the kernel. By Hilbert-space duality this is equivalent to

\[
g\in K_\Delta^\perp=V_\Delta,
\]

which is precisely Hoeffding-support containment.

For necessity on genuine probability measures, suppose `g_S\ne0` for some missing `S`. Bounded centered simple functions are dense in each `L_{0,i}^2`, so bounded finite sums of elementary tensors are dense in `\mathcal H_S`. Choose bounded `h\in\mathcal H_S` with

\[
\langle g_S,h\rangle\ne0.
\]

Then for small `\varepsilon`, `1\pm\varepsilon h` are probability densities with identical retained marginals, while

\[
D_g(P_+)-D_g(P_-)
=2\varepsilon\langle g_S,h\rangle\ne0.
\]

Thus support containment is also necessary on the probability subclass, not only on signed perturbations.

Finally orthogonality gives the projection identity

\[
\|g-P_{V_\Delta}g\|_2^2
=
\left\|\sum_{S\notin\Delta}g_S\right\|_2^2
=
\sum_{S\notin\Delta}\|g_S\|_2^2.
\]

This turns the qualitative missing-face criterion into an exact quadratic defect. It should not be confused with a universal Bayes-risk or distributional-distance bound: it measures the part of one declared `L^2` target observable that lies outside the retained linear-test span.

The underlying mathematics is classical and no novelty is claimed for the Hoeffding/functional-ANOVA decomposition, conditional expectation, finite marginal inversion, or product-space Fourier characterizations.

Role: classical orthogonal ANOVA-style variance decomposition for functions of independent variables and a standard bridge from interaction components to additive `L^2` variance accounting.

Sobol', **“Global sensitivity indices for nonlinear mathematical models and their Monte Carlo estimates,”** *Mathematics and Computers in Simulation* 55(1–3), 271–280 (2001), DOI `10.1016/S0378-4754(00)00270-6`. Role: established functional-ANOVA/Sobol interaction language and variance components for independent inputs; direct prior art for interpreting `\|g_S\|_2^2` as interaction energy rather than a new quantity.

Role: especially close finite-product prior art; gives a complete analysis of complex measures on finite Cartesian products with prescribed families of marginals and effective inversion of the associated discrete Radon transform. The Arithmetic Fidelity contribution is therefore a **compression audit**, not a new marginal or ANOVA theory. It identifies the exact joint statement needed by this line: an arbitrary coordinate-marginal scenario selects precisely a downward-closed family of Hoeffding interaction subspaces; their orthogonal complement is the complete `L^2`-density collision space; every missing face admits genuine probability collisions; target recoverability is exact interaction-support containment; and the quantitative target defect is the sum of missing interaction energies.

The useful new organizational fact relative to AF-033 is that the simplicial information lattice is stable when each Boolean one-dimensional face is replaced by an arbitrary Hilbert interaction fiber. This supplies a reusable theorem template for continuous/nonbinary channels while remaining explicitly inside classical product-measure ANOVA mathematics.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- **Product structure is essential to the orthogonal theorem.** For dependent coordinates, the subspaces indexed by different `S` need not be mutually orthogonal and conditional expectation need not act as a simple face selector. Generalized Hoeffding/functional-ANOVA decompositions for dependent inputs use additional hypotheses or oblique/hierarchical constructions. The theorem above must not be transferred to that setting by analogy.
- **The source class is dominated `L^2`.** Singular measures with respect to `Q`, or dominated measures with densities only in `L^1`, are outside the Hilbert-space statement. Finite full-support alphabets automatically fit the framework, but arbitrary singular arithmetic measures require a different category.
- **The coordinate system is structural data.** A nonlinear reparameterization mixing coordinates can move a target between interaction faces. An application must justify why the product factors and allowed marginal channels are intrinsic before the simplicial support has invariant meaning.
- **Ambient collisions do not settle restricted models.** A structured source family can intersect the missing interaction kernel trivially. As in AF-030 and AF-033, a concrete application must test `(\mathcal S-\mathcal S)\cap K_\Delta`, not infer non-identifiability solely from the ambient theorem.
- **The result is exact, not a stability theorem.** Small missing interaction energy controls this particular `L^2` linear target approximation, but does not by itself bound total variation, Wasserstein distance, Bayes risk, or nonlinear reconstruction error.
- **Nontrivial-coordinate assumptions matter.** If some `q_i` is concentrated on one measurable atom modulo null sets, `L_{0,i}^2=\{0\}` and faces containing `i` carry no information. Remove such trivial coordinates before applying the iff factorization criterion.
- **Minimality remains admissibility-relative.** If arbitrary side information is allowed, a target can always be copied into a lift. The set-cover statement is meaningful only because the allowed repairs are declared coordinate marginals.
- **No rational-prime claim follows.** A prime application must first produce an intrinsic product/channel decomposition and show where a rational-prime-specific discriminator sits in the resulting interaction spaces. The theorem does not establish that such a decomposition exists.

## Consequences for the research line

AF-033 left open whether its exact simplicial fidelity calculus was a peculiarity of Boolean Walsh analysis. It is not. Under any independent product reference law,

\[
\boxed{
\text{Boolean Walsh face}
\quad\longrightarrow\quad
\text{Hoeffding interaction Hilbert space}
}
\]

preserves the same information-state lattice and the same deterministic composition law.

This sharpens the line's general rule for multi-channel compressions. It is insufficient to say that every local component or even a large family of subsystem marginals is retained. One must determine the downward-closed interaction support actually measured. Any target component living in a missing face is orthogonal to the complete retained linear-test span and cannot be manufactured by later deterministic spectralization, positivity, averaging, scalarization, or asymptotic processing.

The next genuine boundary is therefore no longer finite-versus-continuous or binary-versus-nonbinary. It is **product/orthogonal versus dependent/non-orthogonal structure**: determine which exact fidelity calculus survives when the natural source law or intrinsic channel geometry does not factor as a product.
