# AF-484 — Finite prime-zeta samples admit free density-matched Beurling controls

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED`, `LITERATURE+DERIVED`, `NEGATIVE/OBSTRUCTION`, `ARITHMETIC-FIDELITY`, `BEURLING-CONTROL`, `FINITE-SAMPLING`, `PRIME-ZETA`, `NO-BROAD-NOVELTY-CLAIM`

## Claim

Let

\[
1<s_1<\cdots<s_m,
\qquad m\ge 1,
\]

be any finite family of real exponents, let `p_n` be the ordinary rational primes, and let `\varepsilon_n>0` be an arbitrary prescribed tolerance sequence. Then there exists a strictly increasing Beurling-prime sequence

\[
Q=\{q_n\}_{n\ge1}
\]

such that all of the following hold simultaneously:

1. **Arbitrarily fine coordinatewise perturbation:**
   \[
   0<|q_n-p_n|<\varepsilon_n
   \qquad(n\ge1).
   \]

2. **Every generator is genuinely non-rational-prime:** every `q_n` is nonintegral.

3. **Ordinary prime density is retained:**
   \[
   N_Q(x):=\#\{n:q_n\le x\}\sim \frac{x}{\log x}.
   \]

4. **Any prescribed finite prime-zeta sample is retained exactly:** if
   \[
   P(s)=\sum_p p^{-s},
   \qquad
   P_Q(s)=\sum_n q_n^{-s},
   \]
   then
   \[
   \boxed{P_Q(s_j)=P(s_j)\qquad(1\le j\le m).}
   \]

5. **Free multiplicativity is retained:** the set `\{\log q_n\}` is linearly independent over `\mathbb Q`. Hence the generalized integers generated by `Q` form a free commutative multiplicative monoid and have unique factorization into the generators `q_n`.

Moreover the control can be chosen in every sufficiently small `\ell^\infty` neighborhood of the rational-prime source point in the logarithmic perturbation coordinates below.

Thus no finite family of exact real prime-zeta values, even when combined with prime-density asymptotics, arbitrarily accurate coordinatewise proximity to the ordinary primes, and free unique-factorization structure, can certify that the underlying generators are the rational primes.

The theorem deliberately concerns the **prime-zeta sampling layer**, not the full Euler product or the full analytic prime-zeta function. Complete analytic data remain a categorically stronger source representation.

## Exact derivation

### 1. Put an arbitrarily small independent perturbation scale at every prime

Set

\[
\eta_n:=\min(\varepsilon_n,1/4).
\]

Choose numbers `\delta_n>0` so small that

\[
p_n(e^{\delta_n}-1)<\eta_n,
\qquad
0<\delta_n\le 1.
\]

On the open unit ball

\[
B:=\{y=(y_n)\in\ell^\infty:\|y\|_\infty<1\},
\]

define

\[
q_n(y):=p_n e^{\delta_n y_n}.
\]

For every `y\in B`,

\[
|q_n(y)-p_n|<\eta_n\le\varepsilon_n.
\]

Because `\eta_n\le1/4` and consecutive rational primes differ by at least `1`,

\[
q_{n+1}(y)-q_n(y)
>
(p_{n+1}-1/4)-(p_n+1/4)
\ge \frac12.
\]

So every perturbed sequence is still strictly increasing.

### 2. Finite prime-zeta sampling is a smooth finite-codimensional map

Define `G:B\to\mathbb R^m` by

\[
G_j(y)
:=
\sum_{n\ge1}\left(q_n(y)^{-s_j}-p_n^{-s_j}\right).
\]

Since every `s_j>1`, the series and all derivatives needed below converge absolutely and uniformly on smaller bounded subsets of `B`; in particular `G` is smooth (indeed real analytic), and

\[
G(0)=0.
\]

Its derivative is

\[
DG_j(y)[h]
=
-s_j\sum_{n\ge1}\delta_n q_n(y)^{-s_j}h_n.
\]

Fix any `m` distinct coordinate indices `n_1,\dots,n_m`. The derivative restricted to those coordinates has matrix

\[
\left[-s_j\delta_{n_\ell}q_{n_\ell}(y)^{-s_j}\right]_{j,\ell=1}^m.
\]

This matrix is invertible. After removing nonzero row and column factors, the issue is the generalized Vandermonde matrix

\[
\left[x_\ell^{-s_j}\right]_{j,\ell=1}^m
\]

at distinct positive `x_\ell=q_{n_\ell}(y)` and distinct exponents `s_j`.

For completeness, write `t_\ell=\log x_\ell`. A nonzero linear combination of the functions `e^{-s_j t}` has at most `m-1` distinct real zeros. This follows inductively from Rolle's theorem after factoring out the exponential with the smallest exponent. Therefore `m` distinct evaluation points cannot annihilate a nontrivial coefficient vector, so the evaluation matrix is nonsingular. Equivalently, distinct real exponentials form a Chebyshev system.

Hence `DG(y)` is surjective for every `y\in B`. Because the target is finite-dimensional, its kernel is complemented. The Banach-space submersion theorem therefore gives, near `0`,

\[
M:=G^{-1}(0)
\]

as a smooth Banach submanifold of codimension `m`.

Every point of `M` satisfies all exact prime-zeta equalities in the claim.

### 3. Exact interpolation still leaves infinitely many source directions

Finite codimension alone does not yet guarantee the two source properties needed here: every coordinate must actually move, and no rational relation may appear among the perturbed logarithms. Both can be imposed simultaneously by a Baire-category argument *inside the exact interpolation manifold* `M`.

For each index `r`, let

\[
C_r:=\{y\in M:y_r=0\}.
\]

`C_r` is closed in `M` and has empty interior. To see this at a point of `C_r`, set a tangent vector coordinate `v_r=1`, choose any `m` compensating coordinates different from `r`, and solve

\[
DG(y)v=0
\]

for those compensating coordinates. The corresponding `m\times m` generalized Vandermonde block is invertible by the previous step. Thus the coordinate function `y\mapsto y_r` has a nonzero tangent derivative on `M` at every point where it vanishes, so its zero set cannot contain an open subset of `M`.

Next let `a=(a_n)` be any nonzero finitely supported integer sequence and define

\[
H_a(y)
:=
\sum_n a_n\log q_n(y)
=
\sum_n a_n(\log p_n+\delta_n y_n).
\]

Let

\[
R_a:=\{y\in M:H_a(y)=0\}.
\]

Again `R_a` is closed and has empty interior. At any `y\in R_a`, choose `r` in the finite support of `a`; set `v_r=1`; choose `m` compensating coordinates outside the support of `a`; and solve `DG(y)v=0` using those coordinates. Then

\[
dH_a(y)[v]=a_r\delta_r\ne0,
\]

because all compensating coordinates have zero `a`-coefficient. Thus the restriction of `H_a` to `M` is transverse to zero at every zero.

There are only countably many sets `C_r` and only countably many nonzero finitely supported integer sequences `a`. Every sufficiently small nonempty open neighborhood in the Banach manifold `M` is a Baire space. Therefore such a neighborhood contains a point

\[
y\in M\setminus
\left(
\bigcup_r C_r
\cup
\bigcup_{a\ne0}R_a
\right).
\]

For this `y`, every coordinate `y_n` is nonzero and there is no nontrivial finite integer relation among the numbers `\log q_n(y)`.

Consequently:

- `q_n\ne p_n` for every `n`;
- since `|q_n-p_n|<1/4`, the only possible integer in that interval is `p_n`, so every `q_n` is nonintegral;
- `\{\log q_n\}` is linearly independent over `\mathbb Q`;
- multiplicative equality between two finite generalized-integer products would give an integer relation among the logarithms, so factorization in the generators is unique.

### 4. Coordinatewise closeness automatically preserves prime density

The uniform bound

\[
|q_n-p_n|<1/4
\]

gives the elementary sandwich

\[
\pi(x-1/4)\le N_Q(x)\le\pi(x+1/4)
\]

for all sufficiently large `x`. The prime number theorem then yields

\[
N_Q(x)\sim\frac{x}{\log x}.
\]

No delicate generalized-prime counting theorem is needed for this part: the density match is inherited directly from the coordinatewise perturbation.

## What this adds beyond AF-483

AF-483 constructed a non-rational Beurling control matching the rational primes in ordinary prime density, exact square mass `P_Q(2)=P(2)`, and free multiplicativity. That already showed that the sharp Gibbs boundary constant studied in AF-479--AF-482 is not prime-selective.

The present result closes the obvious finite-dimensional strengthening of that control. The square mass is not special: **any finite family of exact prime-zeta moments can be matched simultaneously**, while preserving all of the AF-483 source-side controls and while keeping every generalized prime inside an arbitrarily prescribed neighborhood of its rational-prime counterpart.

In particular, adding finitely many exact real Dirichlet moments to density, one-crossing Gibbs geometry, partition singularity information, and free multiplicativity cannot by itself supply a rational-prime discriminator. Any successful discriminator of this type must use genuinely infinite analytic data, an independently rigid source category, or arithmetic structure not implied by these constraints.

It changes the source category and strengthens the matched-control package: the **generator locations themselves move**, the finite observations are exact prime-zeta samples at arbitrary prescribed real exponents, the control can be made coordinatewise arbitrarily close to the rational primes, every generator can simultaneously be forced nonintegral, and the entire logarithmic generator family remains `\mathbb Q`-independent.

The common mechanism is now explicit: a finite-dimensional exact observation map cuts out only finite codimension, while infinitely many latent generator directions remain available to enforce additional generic source properties.

## Relation to full analytic fidelity

This theorem must not be extrapolated from finite samples to complete analytic data. Equality of a prime-zeta or Euler-product function on an open domain, or on a uniqueness set with an interior accumulation point under the appropriate analytic hypotheses, invokes analytic rigidity unavailable to a finite sample family. AF-017 already places the exact Euler-product layer on the faithful side of that boundary in its source model.

So the useful fidelity split is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{finite exact prime-zeta samples: non-faithful}
\quad\text{vs.}\quad
\text{complete analytic source data: potentially faithful}.
}
\]

The remaining arithmetic question is not whether more *finite* scalar moments can repair the problem, but what intrinsically arithmetic structure first destroys these Beurling fibers.

## Prior art and novelty assessment

The ingredients are classical or directly adjacent to established literature.

- The generalized-prime framework and the role of `\mathbb Q`-linear independence of `\{\log q_n\}` in obtaining uniquely represented Beurling integers are standard. Frederik Broucke, Athanasios Kouroupis, and Karl-Mikael Perfekt, **“A note on Bohr's theorem for Beurling integer systems,”** *Mathematische Annalen* 389 (2024), 4319–4333, DOI `10.1007/s00208-023-02756-x`, explicitly works with this multiplicatively generated setting and proves that small perturbations of Beurling-prime sequences can preserve or improve significant Dirichlet-series properties.
- Nonsingularity of the matrix `x_\ell^{-s_j}` for ordered distinct positive nodes and distinct real exponents is classical Chebyshev-system / strict-total-positivity mathematics. The proof above is included so that the finding does not depend on a black-box interpolation theorem.
- The regular-level-set/submersion mechanism and Baire-category avoidance used to retain generic properties inside a finite-codimensional fiber are standard functional/differential topology tools.

A targeted literature search located substantial work on small perturbations of Beurling primes, Beurling zeta functions, Bohr-type conditions, and classical generalized Vandermonde/Chebyshev systems, but did not locate this exact finite-interpolation package: arbitrary finitely many prime-zeta values matched at the rational-prime source while simultaneously imposing arbitrary coordinatewise closeness, nonintegrality of every generator, ordinary prime density, and free multiplicativity. No broad novelty is claimed; the Arithmetic Fidelity contribution is the specific **matched-control boundary** and its consequence for finite arithmetic compression.

## Boundaries and failure modes

- The exponents are finitely many distinct real numbers strictly larger than `1`; this is the absolute-convergence region of the prime-zeta sums used directly here.
- The theorem matches `P_Q(s_j)`, not `\zeta_Q(s_j)`. Matching finitely many prime-zeta values does not automatically match all prime-power terms entering `\log\zeta_Q`.
- No functional equation, analytic continuation, Euler-factor arithmetic degree, integer support, congruence law, additive incidence structure, or automorphic source axiom is preserved.
- The construction is existential. It does not provide an effective algorithm for selecting one Baire-generic point satisfying all countably many avoidance conditions.
- `\mathbb Q`-linear independence gives free multiplicative factorization but is not itself a rational-prime fingerprint; AF-484 is precisely a control showing that the property survives non-prime perturbation.
- Coordinatewise closeness, even arbitrarily strong nonuniform closeness `|q_n-p_n|<\varepsilon_n`, does not imply equality of the generator sets. Any proposed discriminator based only on finite continuous observations must confront this local fiber explicitly.

## Research consequence

The AF-483 control family survives every **finite** strengthening by exact real prime-zeta moments. Therefore the next useful arithmetic-fidelity question should not be “which extra finite moment should be added?” but rather:

> Which genuinely arithmetic restriction first destroys the finite-codimensional Beurling deformation fiber — integer support, congruence/additive structure, a rigid global functional equation/source class, or a complete analytic observation family?

That is a sharper boundary question than further refinement of the Gibbs margin itself.
