# Relational data can restore structure lost by scalar compression

Two current exact models show, without asserting a universal theorem, how relational observables can restore information discarded by lower-order or separately scalar summaries.

- [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-004-third-order-coupling-repairs-quadratic-phase-loss|AF-004]] shows that Fourier magnitude/autocorrelation loses phase on finite abelian groups, while the bispectrum plus power spectrum generically restores the signal modulo the intended translation symmetry.
- [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-006-marked-hermitian-spectra-classified-by-eigenspace-gram-data|AF-006]] shows that separately marked scalar spectral measures can lose the geometry between marks, while the full per-eigenspace Gram matrices give a complete invariant of the pointed self-adjoint system on the finitely cyclic locus.

In both models the recovered information is carried by coupling between retained coordinates rather than by attaching arbitrary labels. No direct transfer to Prime Circle, Prime Lattice, or another research line is asserted here.
