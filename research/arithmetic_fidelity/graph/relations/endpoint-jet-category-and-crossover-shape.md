# Endpoint-jet category and crossover shape

The sources distinguish exact integer rigidity, exact real repair and rational approximation for simple unit-weight finite edits of the ordinary primes. The discriminator is the turning geometry of the inverse-source crossover, not its nontrivial zero divisor.

- [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-591-matched-prime-endpoints-admit-arbitrarily-many-crossover-minima|AF-591]] supplies distinct nonintegral rational replacements above any prescribed cutoff, with exactly matched reciprocal mass and arbitrarily many separated strict crossover minima. It does not match the nonconstant endpoint jet.
- [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-592-remote-unit-weight-edits-repair-finite-endpoint-jets|AF-592]] uses that construction and repairs any fixed finite positive-order endpoint jet by remote **real** generator moves. The correction has zero mass and arbitrarily small positive-axis source norm. Only finitely many strict high/low crossover values are transported through the inverse; no uniform inverse estimate or rationality guarantee follows.
- [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-593-integer-first-jet-rigidity-does-not-give-a-rational-precision-gap|AF-593]] rationalizes AF-592 inside the exact mass hyperplane. Every fixed finite nonconstant jet can be arbitrarily close to the prime jet while arbitrarily many separated minima survive. There is no bound on denominator, edit count or generator range. Exact mass and first-derivative matching instead force an **integer** replacement collection to equal the removed prime set, including multiplicities. Signed denominator valuations invalidate that nonnegative-summand argument for rationals; they do not prove rational feasibility.

For fixed removed primes `S` and common denominator `D`, AF-593 expresses exact rational feasibility as
`sum n_g=sum (D/p)` and `product n_g^(n_g)=product (D/p)^(D/p)`, where `n_g=D/g`. Distinct nonintegral generators above `Y` require distinct integers `1<=n_g<D/Y` that do not divide `D`; their prime factors must divide `D`. The [[research/prior_art/incremental/PA-rational-entropy-self-power-identity|classical equal-entropy identity]] supplies the algebraic conversion, not solutions with these restrictions. Rational density on the mass hyperplane does not imply rational points on the exact logarithmic level set. Failure for finitely many denominators cannot settle the unbounded question.

[[research/arithmetic_fidelity/mind/intuition/MI-082-integer-jet-rigidity-does-not-supply-rational-precision|MI-082]] synthesizes this category/precision distinction. The existing rational first-jet clue remains unresolved; its lifecycle is not evidence. All three constructions preserve the nontrivial zeta zeros with multiplicity through a finite Euler factor analytic and nonzero on `Re s>0`. Ordinary-prime crossover uniqueness, exact rational jet matching, and an RH conclusion remain unproved. The graph records analytic source derivations, not formal verification, independent adversarial approval or a new general entropy/moment theorem.
