# Interaction support and collective stability are separate fidelity gates

Current Arithmetic Fidelity findings extend the earlier joint-versus-marginal examples into a source-dependent support and stability calculus.

- [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-031-complete-marginals-forget-coupling-joint-tests-recover-feature-law|AF-031]]--[[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-033-marginal-scenarios-form-a-simplicial-fidelity-lattice|AF-033]] classify the exact information lost by separate marginals and recovered by joint interaction data in finite models.
- [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-034-product-marginals-project-onto-hoeffding-interaction-faces|AF-034]]--[[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-038-dependent-multiplicative-fidelity-is-fusion-span-of-channel-joins|AF-038]] generalize this to Hoeffding faces, generated sigma-field joins, interaction cover number and dependent-source fusion spans.
- [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-039-friedrichs-angle-controls-two-channel-stable-fidelity|AF-039]] gives the two-channel stability modulus, while [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-040-pairwise-stability-does-not-imply-multichannel-stability|AF-040]] shows that uniformly good pairwise angles do not prevent the full multichannel sum from losing closedness and its collective lower bound.
- [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-041-stable-composition-is-range-kernel-transversality|AF-041]] identifies stable composition with transversality between the upstream range and downstream kernel.

The source-backed relation is therefore two-stage: recovering every target interaction algebraically is not enough; the complete channel family must also retain a positive collective stability modulus in the destination category. Pairwise conditioning cannot substitute for the global fusion/closed-sum test.

No direct claim about another Mathia line follows unless that line's persisted source model is explicitly placed in this framework.
