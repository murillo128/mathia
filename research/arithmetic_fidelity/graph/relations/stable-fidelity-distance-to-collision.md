# Stable fidelity is measured by distance from collision or repair geometry

[[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-041-stable-composition-is-range-kernel-transversality|AF-041]]--[[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-045-lower-lipschitz-modulus-is-exact-nonlinear-collision-distance|AF-045]] turn robust deterministic fidelity into exact transversality and distance-to-collision moduli in operator, smooth, linear-secant and nonlinear Lipschitz categories.

The stochastic zero-error results add a complementary boundary rather than a universal extension of that theorem. [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-046-zero-error-fidelity-has-zero-tv-robustness|AF-046]] shows that exact support-disjoint recovery can have zero robustness even where output laws remain separated by a positive TV margin. [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-047-zero-error-tv-repair-is-bayes-error-plus-hall-coverage|AF-047]]--[[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-051-quadratic-zero-error-repair-is-clone-granularity-sensitive|AF-051]] give exact projection distances to the zero-error set in several row-separable geometries, while quadratic repair retains representation-sensitive concentration/cardinality data.

[[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-052-reversible-output-cloning-removes-hall-repair-penalty|AF-052]] and [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-053-null-symbol-stabilization-collapses-zero-error-tv-repair-to-bayes-risk|AF-053]] sharpen that distinction at the representation boundary. Blackwell-equivalent output refinements can preserve the experiment and TV geometry while changing the unrestricted structural target enough to remove the Hall penalty entirely; after null-symbol stabilization the minimal zero-error TV repair is exactly Bayes risk. An exact ambient distance therefore becomes intrinsic only after the target family itself is required to descend through the declared information-preserving equivalence.

The positive counterpart is the maximal safe-envelope calculus. [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-054-maximal-safe-target-envelope-under-isometric-refinement|AF-054]] characterizes the unique largest target enlargement that preserves every repair radius under an isometric refinement. [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-055-singleton-safe-envelopes-are-best-coapproximation-fibers|AF-055]] redirects singleton envelopes to classical best coapproximation, [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-056-hilbert-set-target-envelopes-are-convex-roof-delaunay-radius-epigraphs|AF-056]] identifies Hilbert set-target envelopes with convex-roof/Delaunay radius epigraphs, and [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-057-product-norm-exponent-sharp-safe-lift-threshold|AF-057]] shows that even equivalent finite-dimensional norms can lie on opposite sides of a sharp safe-lift threshold.

[[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-058-near-isometric-renorming-can-destroy-safe-lift-existence|AF-058]] makes the representation boundary quantitative. It defines the exact multiplicative safety modulus, shows its bi-Lipschitz covariance, and proves that finite Hilbert safe lifts can sit at zero uniform margin: arbitrarily near-isometric renormings that leave the source embedding exactly isometric can eliminate every finite safe lift on the unbounded source. Exact existence is therefore not stabilized by saying only that two presentations have equivalent, or even almost identical, metrics; a positive uniform conditioning margin is an additional datum.

Across these source-backed categories, exact recovery, robust recovery, minimum repair, target semantics and harmless representation change are distinct questions. A downstream arithmetic claim that needs stability must specify both the perturbation geometry and the equivalence-compatible target class, and must supply a margin appropriate to that class; neither a margin proved in one metric nor an exact repair distance or lift in one presentation can be silently transferred to another.
