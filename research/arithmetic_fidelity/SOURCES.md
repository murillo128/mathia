# Sources

## AF-001 — Fiberwise recoverability and unconstrained lifts

- David Blackwell, **“Equivalent Comparisons of Experiments,”** *The Annals of Mathematical Statistics* 24(2), 265–272 (1953). DOI: `10.1214/aoms/1177729032`. Role: classical comparison-of-experiments / garbling background for the principle that deterministic or stochastic post-processing cannot increase available decision information.
- William W. Armstrong, **“Dependency Structures of Data Base Relationships,”** *Information Processing 74*, 580–583 (1974). Role: classical functional-dependency language; equality of determinant values forcing equality of dependent values is the relational-data analogue of a discriminator being constant on compression fibers.
- H. S. Witsenhausen, **“The Zero-Error Side Information Problem and Chromatic Numbers,”** *IEEE Transactions on Information Theory* 22(5), 592–593 (1976). DOI: `10.1109/TIT.1976.1055607`. Role: zero-error side-information prior art; when side information is also known at the transmitter, the alphabet-minimization problem becomes trivial, closely paralleling the unrestricted fiberwise lift construction in AF-001.
- Alon Orlitsky and James R. Roche, **“Coding for Computing,”** *IEEE Transactions on Information Theory* 47(3), 903–917 (2001). DOI: `10.1109/18.915643`. Role: function-computation with side information; shows that nontrivial compression questions arise once the admissible coding/observation model is constrained rather than allowing an arbitrary target-carrying mark.

The elementary set-theoretic factorization statements in AF-001 are derived directly. These sources locate their closest established languages and prevent treating the basic fiber criterion or unconstrained side-information phenomenon as novel.

DOI: `10.1007/978-94-015-7975-9_21`. AF-002 derives the conditional hitting-set statement directly from AF-001, but the finite fixed-library mechanism is classical rough-set reduct/discernibility mathematics. The source bridge is used to prevent a false novelty claim and to redirect future Arithmetic Fidelity work toward intrinsically constrained observable families and non-table-like settings.

## AF-003 — Invariant-observable quotients and orbit-closure fidelity

Role: standard source for reductive-group invariant theory, affine categorical quotients, orbit separation by invariants, and the distinction between quotient points and arbitrary group orbits.
- T. A. Springer, ***Invariant Theory***, Lecture Notes in Mathematics 585, Springer (1977). DOI: `10.1007/BFb0095644`. Role: classical invariant-ring background and finite-generation/separation context for algebraic group actions.
- **“Invariants, theory of,”** *Encyclopedia of Mathematics*. Role: concise authoritative summary of the affine reductive quotient statement used here: the invariant ring defines a quotient whose fibers contain exactly one closed orbit, and invariant functions alone do not generally retain complete information about nonclosed orbits.

AF-003 derives the maximal-observable joint-evaluation criterion and the `\mathbb G_m` weight-`(1,-1)` example directly. The orbit-closure description of the full invariant algebra is classical GIT; the Arithmetic Fidelity contribution is to use that quotient as a no-go test for every lift constrained to the same invariant-observable class.

DOI: `10.1364/JOSAA.9.000388`. Role: systematic uniqueness and non-uniqueness results for higher-order autocorrelations; establishes both the power of triple correlation and important degeneracy/band-limit failures.
- Ramakrishna Kakarala and Geoffrey J. Iverson, **“Uniqueness of Results for Multiple Correlations of Periodic Functions,”** *Journal of the Optical Society of America A* 10(7), 1517–1528 (1993). DOI: `10.1364/JOSAA.10.001517`. Role: periodic/discrete-Fourier setting; shows that triple correlation is not universally complete when spectral support is degenerate and studies recovery from higher moment spectra.
- Ramakrishna Kakarala, **“The Bispectrum as a Source of Phase-Sensitive Invariants for Fourier Descriptors: A Group-Theoretic Approach,”** *Journal of Mathematical Imaging and Vision* 44(3), 341–353 (2012). DOI: `10.1007/s10851-012-0330-6`. Role: group-theoretic bispectrum and completeness results on compact groups/homogeneous spaces under nondegeneracy conditions; places AF-004's finite-abelian argument inside established invariant theory for correlations.
- Tamir Bendory, Robert Beinert, and Yonina C. Eldar, **“Fourier Phase Retrieval: Uniqueness and Algorithms,”** in *Compressed Sensing and its Applications*, 55–91, Birkhäuser/Springer (2017). DOI: `10.1007/978-3-319-69802-1_2`. Role: modern survey of Fourier phase retrieval and the generic non-uniqueness of one-dimensional Fourier-magnitude data without additional information.

AF-004 derives the finite-abelian power-spectrum counterexample and ratio-character proof directly. The completeness mechanism is classical bispectral mathematics. Its role in Arithmetic Fidelity is to exhibit an exact order-sensitive quotient refinement: quadratic translation invariants leave excess phase freedom, while third-order compatibility can reduce the generic fiber to the intended translation orbit.

## AF-006 — Marked Hermitian spectra and matrix spectral measures

- Roger A. Horn and Charles R. Johnson, ***Matrix Analysis***, 2nd ed., Cambridge University Press (2012; digital edition 2013). DOI: `10.1017/CBO9781139020411`. - Marcin Moszyński, **“Spectral Theory of Self-adjoint Finitely Cyclic Operators and Introduction to Matrix Measure L2-spaces,”** arXiv:`2212.13953` (2022). - Constanze Liaw and Sergei Treil, **“Matrix Measures and Finite Rank Perturbations of Self-adjoint Operators,”** *Journal of Spectral Theory* 10(4), 1173–1210 (2020). DOI: `10.4171/JST/324`. Role: authoritative modern use of matrix-valued measures as the natural spectral language for finite-rank/multiple-vector self-adjoint theory, including spectral representations.

AF-006 derives the finite-dimensional pointed classification and the two-mark counterexample directly. The theorem is a finite atomic specialization of classical spectral/Gram theory and finitely cyclic matrix-measure representations. Its Arithmetic Fidelity role is to show exactly which relational information a marked spectral lift must retain: diagonal scalar spectral weights can preserve each mark separately while losing cross-mark geometry, whereas the full per-eigenspace Gram matrix closes that quotient fiber up to unitary gauge.

Role: classical transverse-geometry source for basic functions, i.e. smooth functions constant along leaves, which is the established foliation-theoretic language behind the criterion `dD(ker dT)=0` on connected fibers.

AF-007 derives the vertical-rank lower bound, local sharpness construction, composition monotonicity, and the disconnected-fiber counterexample directly from classical submersion/constant-rank and foliation facts. No novelty is claimed for the descent theorem or the differential-topological ingredients. The Arithmetic Fidelity contribution is to organize them into a two-layer audit: a computable infinitesimal defect for continuous loss plus a separate global fiber test for discrete or disconnected identifications.

## AF-010 — Full-jet projection fidelity needs quasianalyticity plus cylindrical closure

- Vincent Thilliez, **“On Quasianalytic Local Rings,”** *Expositiones Mathematicae* 26(1), 1–23 (2008). DOI: `10.1016/j.exmath.2007.04.001`. Role: authoritative survey identifying quasianalyticity with injectivity of the Borel/Taylor map and giving the Denjoy-Carleman criterion; supplies the uniqueness half of the full-jet projection criterion while standard Denjoy-Carleman settings carry additional closure structure.
- André Belotto da Silva, Edward Bierstone, and Michael Chow, **“Composite Quasianalytic Functions,”** *Compositio Mathematica* 154(9), 1960–1973 (2018). DOI: `10.1112/S0010437X18007339`. Role: decisive stronger prior art: formal compositeness at a single point with a generically submersive quasianalytic Denjoy-Carleman map implies local composition, with a possible shift/loss of regularity. This places AF-010's regular projection/submersion mechanism inside established composite-function theory rather than as a novelty claim.
- Edward Bierstone and Pierre D. Milman, **“Composite Differentiable Functions,”** *Annals of Mathematics* 116(3), 541–558 (1982). DOI: `10.2307/2007022`. Role: classical smooth composite-function background; establishes that differentiable factorization/composition is an existing theory and helps delimit AF-010's narrower pointwise-jet obstruction.

AF-010 derives the finite-jet polynomial counterexample, analytic projection criterion, flat-germ smooth counterexample, and the corrected quasianalytic projection theorem directly. The theorem requires not only quasianalytic uniqueness but also dimensional closure under coordinate restriction, cylindrical pullback, and subtraction so that the comparison germ remains admissible. The Arithmetic Fidelity use is a two-gate audit for formal recovery: uniqueness of the representation and closure under the reconstruction operations must both hold before infinite-jet data can certify actual descent.

## AF-015 — Abstract multiplication and rational-prime norm fidelity

- John Knopfmacher, ***Abstract Analytic Number Theory***, North-Holland Mathematical Library 12, North-Holland (1975); corrected and enlarged republication, Dover (1990), ISBN `0-486-66344-2`. Role: foundational abstract-analytic-number-theory source for arithmetical semigroups: unique factorization into abstract primes together with an additional multiplicative norm, with the positive integers as the prototype.
- Stephen D. Cohen, **“The function field abstract prime number theorem,”** *Mathematical Proceedings of the Cambridge Philosophical Society* 106(1), 7–12 (1989). DOI: `10.1017/S0305004100067943`. - Wolfgang Schwarz, **“John Knopfmacher, [Abstract] Analytic Number Theory, and the Theory of Arithmetical Functions,”** *Quaestiones Mathematicae* 24(3), 273–290 (2001). DOI: `10.1080/16073606.2001.9639218`. Role: historical/technical overview confirming that Knopfmacher's theory studies semigroups with countably many generalized-prime generators together with norm/degree maps rather than deriving the size structure from bare multiplication.

AF-015 derives the full automorphism group of the bare positive-integer multiplicative monoid, its factorization-shape orbit quotient, preservation of prime type, and loss of individual rational-prime norm identity directly from unique factorization and the universal property of the free commutative monoid. No novelty is claimed for those algebraic facts or for normed arithmetical semigroups. The Arithmetic Fidelity contribution is the exact boundary they impose: abstract multiplicativity can preserve prime-generator structure perfectly while erasing every distinction between the ordinary rational-prime norms, so any prime-specific mechanism must identify independently justified extra structure that breaks prime-generator permutation symmetry and survives the downstream compression.

The Arithmetic Fidelity contribution is the exact category boundary: full Euler-product value data are faithful to the unordered generator-norm multiset under the stated hypotheses, while compression to the meromorphic zero/pole divisor is not because a zero-free analytic factor can carry the changed norm information.
