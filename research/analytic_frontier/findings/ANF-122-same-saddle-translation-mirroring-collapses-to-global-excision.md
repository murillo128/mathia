# ANF-122 — same-saddle translation mirroring collapses to global excision

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + PROFILE-TRANSVERSE-COMPANION-GATE + SAME-SADDLE-MIRROR-EXCISION + BAND-TO-GLOBAL-TRANSFER + MATCHED-PHYSICAL-CONTROL`. `ANF-118` controls the full mesoscopic source profile of positive translation clouds of the interior-saddle packet from `ANF-115`, while `ANF-119`--`ANF-121` leave a physical completion problem: a bounded source-heavy packet can remain essential only if the remainder mirrors it in its off-notch saddle band while preserving enough central-notch efficiency. The most direct physical way to realize that mirror is to use translated copies of the same packet. That mechanism cannot be fatal.

Let `P_A` be the exact `ANF-115` packet, let

\[
a=\alpha_\gamma\in(0,1),\qquad

E_A=E_0(P_A),\qquad m_A=|P_A|,

\[
T_A=igsqcup_{j=0}^{J_A}w_{j,A}(P_A+\tau_{j,A}),

q_A(\alpha)=\sum_{j=0}^{J_A}w_{j,A}e^{-2\pi i\alpha\tau_{j,A}},
\]

where `w_{j,A}` are positive integers, `tau_{j,A}` are real, and one distinguished copy is the original packet (`w_{0,A}=1`, `tau_{0,A}=0`). Put

\[
V_A=\sum_jw_{j,A}.
\]

Assume the same growth envelope as the uniform comparison in `ANF-118`,

\[
\log(e+V_A)=o(A^{1/3}).
\tag{1}

\[
B_A:=\left\{\alpha\in[-1,1]:

\[

An essential mirror must therefore be profile-transverse (carry source not generated by the same saddle packet under positive translations), or leave the subexponential envelope strongly enough to move the saddle itself.

## 1. Saddle-band cancellation already controls the complete source norm

Normalize the source measure of the base packet as in `ANF-115`--`ANF-118`,

\[
d\nu_A(\alpha)
=\frac{J_0(\alpha)|S_{P_A}(\alpha)|^2}{E_A}\,d\alpha.
\tag{7}
\]

The translation-cloud factorization is exact:

\[
S_{T_A}(\alpha)=q_A(\alpha)S_{P_A}(\alpha),
\qquad
\frac{E_0(T_A)}{E_A}
=\int|q_A|^2\,d\nu_A.
\tag{8}
\]

The uniform saddle analysis in `ANF-118` gives constants `C,c_*>0` such that, whenever `1<=U<=kappa A^{1/6}`,

\[
\nu_A\!\left(
\bigl\{\bigl||\alpha|-a\bigr|>U/\sqrt A\bigr\}
\right)
\le Ce^{-c_*U^2}.
\tag{9}
\]

Because all cloud weights are positive,

\[
|q_A(\alpha)|\le V_A
\tag{10}
\]

pointwise. Splitting (8) over `B_A` and its complement therefore gives the cancellation-safe estimate

\[
\boxed{
\frac{E_0(T_A)}{E_A}
\le
\frac{E_{B_A}(T_A)}{E_A}
+C V_A^2e^{-c_*U_A^2}.
}
\tag{11}
\]

Condition (2) makes the second term tend to zero. This proves (4). No pairwise control between translated copies and no finite saddle jet is used: the only input is that every same-family translate is multiplied by the **same** base spectral density, whose mass outside `B_A` is Gaussian-small.

The Gram equivalence in (5) follows from `ANF-118`:

\[
\frac{E_0(T_A)}{E_A}
=\mathcal G_A(q_A)(1+o(1))+o(1)
\tag{12}
\]

throughout (1). Thus a same-saddle mirror that kills the physical band norm is exactly a vanishing-Gram cloud. There is no hidden source reservoir elsewhere in the spectrum that can keep the aggregate essential after the saddle profile has been cancelled.

## 2. Fixed-notch mass vanishes for the whole mirror aggregate

Fix `0<eta<a`. `ANF-115` gives a positive rate

\[
d_{\eta,\gamma}=F_\gamma(a)-F_\gamma(\eta)>0
\]

with

\[
\frac{E_\eta(P_A)}{E_A}
=\exp[-d_{\eta,\gamma}A+o(A)].
\tag{13}
\]

Using (10),

\[
\boxed{
\frac{E_\eta(T_A)}{E_A}
\le
V_A^2\frac{E_\eta(P_A)}{E_A}
=
\exp[-d_{\eta,\gamma}A+o(A)]
\longrightarrow0,
}
\tag{14}
\]

because (1) is much stronger than `log V_A=o(A)`. Hence the same-family companion cannot compensate for its own excision by carrying hidden central-notch mass. Both the source norm of the cancelled aggregate and its notch observable disappear on the ambient scale.

This is the point not supplied by the abstract Hilbert adversary in `ANF-120`. Abstractly, a vector may cancel a packet in one band and retain unrelated norm in another. A positive translation cloud of `P_A` cannot: multiplication by `q_A` modulates one already-concentrated spectral measure, so cancelling its saddle band removes essentially all of its source energy.

## 3. In a global near-extremizer the mirrored cloud can be deleted

Let a global physical configuration decompose as

\[
W_A=T_A\sqcup R_A
\]

and suppose

\[
\frac{E_0(W_A)}{L_A}=1+o(1),
\qquad
0<\lambda\le\frac{E_A}{L_A}\le\Lambda.
\tag{15}
\]

The exact packet asymptotic from `ANF-115` is

\[
\frac1A\log\frac{E_A}{m_A^2}\to f_\gamma>0,
\qquad
\frac1A\log m_A\to\gamma\log2.
\tag{16}
\]

Since `|T_A|=V_Am_A`, (1), (6), and (16) give

\[
\boxed{|T_A|=o(L_A).}
\tag{17}
\]

If the `ANF-119` mirror condition holds on `B_A`, equation (4) and (6) give

\[
E_0(T_A)=o(L_A).
\tag{18}
\]

The Montgomery--Taylor norm of the remainder is then automatically controlled:

\[
\|S_{R_A}\|_0
\le
\|S_{W_A}\|_0+\|S_{T_A}\|_0
=O(\sqrt{L_A}),
\]

so Cauchy--Schwarz yields

\[
E_0(R_A)=E_0(W_A)+o(L_A)=L_A+o(L_A).
\tag{19}
\]

Every point of `T_A` is nonreal, hence removing it changes the affine size by exactly `2|T_A|`; by (17),

\[
L(R_A)=L_A+o(L_A).
\tag{20}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\frac{E_0(R_A)}{L(R_A)}\to1.
}
\tag{21}
\]

The same argument applies to the notch Hilbert norm. Since `0<=phi_eta<=J_0`, the global notch energy is `O(L_A)`, while (14), (6) give `E_eta(T_A)=o(L_A)`. The notch cross term is therefore `o(L_A)`, and

\[
\boxed{
\frac{E_\eta(R_A)-E_\eta(W_A)}{L_A}\to0.
}
\tag{22}
\]

Thus deleting the entire mirrored same-saddle aggregate preserves both Montgomery--Taylor near-extremality and the asymptotic fixed-notch exposure. Such a mirror is not merely small in its local band after cancellation; it is irrelevant to the fatal envelope of `ANF-099`.

## 4. A two-copy mirror realizes the cancellation physically and shows why it is harmless

The obstruction is not that positive physical translations cannot anti-align. They can do so exactly at the saddle. Take

\[
\tau=\frac1{2a},
\qquad
T_A=P_A\sqcup(P_A+\tau).
\tag{23}
\]

Then

\[
q_A(\alpha)=1+e^{-2\pi i\alpha\tau},
\qquad
q_A(\pm a)=0.
\tag{24}
\]

The Gaussian Gram form is explicit:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal G_A
&=2+2e^{-2\pi^2\tau^2/(cA)}\cos(2\pi a\tau)\\
&=2-2e^{-2\pi^2\tau^2/(cA)}\\
&\sim\frac{4\pi^2\tau^2}{cA}
=\frac{\pi^2}{a^2cA}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{25}
\]

Using `ANF-118` with a polynomially small tail error gives

\[
\boxed{
\frac{E_0(T_A)}{E_A}
\sim\frac{\pi^2}{a^2cA}\to0.
}
\tag{26}
\]

Each copy separately has source energy exactly `E_A`, so their source cross term satisfies

\[
\frac{\mathcal C_0(P_A,P_A+\tau)}{E_A}
=-1+O(A^{-1}).
\tag{27}
\]

This is an explicit positive, conjugation-invariant, same-cardinality physical realization of almost perfect `ANF-119` mirroring. It does **not** create a fatal completion: the two copies together have become source-negligible and are excised by (21)--(22). The example is a matched control against the opposite overstatement that physical positivity itself prevents near-perfect saddle anti-alignment.

## 5. Scope, prior-art boundary, and the new live gate

The proof is exact once the uniform saddle tail and Gram comparison already canonicalized in `ANF-118` are granted. It permits arbitrary real translations, arbitrary positive integer multiplicities, growing translation ranges, and arbitrarily many copies under (1). It does not require the translations to be separated or the resulting multiset to have distinct sites.

The growth boundary is real. `ANF-118` already exhibits clouds with `V_A=exp(Theta(A))` that move the dominant saddle; for such clouds the fixed `a` band need not control the complete source norm. Nor does the finding exclude a companion built from a different vertical profile, a different interior-saddle family, or a more general physical subconfiguration whose source carries macroscopic mass outside the `P_A` saddle band. Those are precisely the profile-transverse mechanisms left open.

A targeted prior-art audit was made against general concentration/profile-decomposition results for translated profiles, including Solimini--Tintarev, *Concentration analysis in Banach spaces* (arXiv:1502.00414), and Okumura, *Profile decomposition in Sobolev spaces and decomposition of integral functionals I* (arXiv:2109.08176). Those works provide broad functional-analytic language for translated profiles and vanishing remainders, but no theorem found there supplies (11), (14), or the Mathia-specific affine excision consequence. No external result is load-bearing: (11)--(27) use only the exact physical factorization and canonical `ANF-115`/`ANF-118` estimates, so `SOURCES.md` is unchanged.

The live physical gate after `ANF-121` is therefore narrower than mere realizability of off-notch anti-alignment. **Same-saddle anti-alignment is easy to realize but self-destructs.** A fatal near-extremizer containing a bounded-mass `ANF-115` packet must obtain its essential companion from source structure transverse to that packet's positive translation orbit, or from an exponentially large cloud capable of changing the saddle. The next useful test is to quantify how much affine/source budget such a profile-transverse mirror must spend while simultaneously supporting the central-notch-efficient component required by `ANF-099`.
