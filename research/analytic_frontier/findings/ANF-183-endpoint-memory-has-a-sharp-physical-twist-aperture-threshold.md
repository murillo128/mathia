# ANF-183 — Endpoint memory has a sharp physical twist-aperture threshold

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + MATCHED-CONTROL + SHARP-NONLOCAL-APERTURE-THRESHOLD + POSITIVE-DISCRIMINATION-BOUNDARY`. `ANF-181` and `ANF-182` identify

\[
a_X:=\sqrt{\delta X\log X},
\qquad
K=X^{1-2\varepsilon+o(1)},
\]

and show that the exact-log derivative jet of a dangerous prime-scale endpoint-memory packet is asymptotically flat through every order `r_X=o(K/a_X)`, while some derivative at order `Theta(K/a_X)` must detect every sector-aligned target-sized packet in the matched-control class.

The derivative hierarchy is not the only way to resolve that packet. There is a sharp physical **twist-aperture** threshold. After a deterministic rephasing at the first active site, a dangerous packet of physical width `Theta(a_X)` can remain `o(a_X)`-close to a single coherent mode throughout every twist window

\[
|\tau|\le T_X,
\qquad
T_X=o\!\left(\frac{X}{a_X}\right).
\]

Conversely, every sector-aligned target-sized packet made from `log X` atoms on `log X`-separated sites and completed before `K=o(X)` is detected by a symmetric three-point twist probe inside an aperture

\[
\boxed{
T_X\asymp \frac{X}{a_X}
=\sqrt{\frac{X}{\delta\log X}}.
}
\]

Thus `X/a_X` is the sharp nonlocal frequency aperture, up to constants and the `o`/`Theta` boundary, for the same endpoint-memory obstruction. Unlike the derivative-order threshold `K/a_X`, this physical aperture is independent of the endpoint horizon `K`: the `K` dependence in the derivative formulation comes from the local exact-log normalization, not from the physical twist resolution required to distinguish a target-sized packet.

This is a sharpness theorem for the sector-aligned matched-control obstruction. It is not a theorem that the actual source `Lambda-Lambda^sharp` can be controlled with absolute error `o(a_X)` at the required shifted twists.

## 1. Rephase physical twist shifts at the first active site

Use the prime-scale alphabet from `ANF-175`--`ANF-182`:

\[
A_X:=\log X,
\qquad
q_X:=\lceil\log X\rceil,
\qquad
a_X:=\sqrt{\delta X\log X}.
\tag{1}
\]

Let

\[
0<J=j_1<j_2<\cdots<j_m\le K
\tag{2}
\]

be active offsets with

\[
j_{s+1}-j_s\ge q_X,
\tag{3}
\]

and let `zeta_s` denote their carrier phases at the distinguished twist `U`. Assume, as in the six-sector matched-control construction, that all `zeta_s` lie in one angular sector of width `pi/3`. After a common rotation,

\[
\operatorname{Re}\zeta_s\ge \cos(\pi/6)=\frac{\sqrt3}{2}.
\tag{4}
\]

A physical twist displacement `tau` replaces the carrier at `U` by that at `U+tau`. Remove the known common phase generated by the first active site and define

\[
\ell_s
:=
\log\frac{X+j_s}{X+J}
\ge0,
\tag{5}
\]

\[
H_X(\tau)
:=
A_X\sum_{s=1}^{m}\zeta_s e^{-i\tau\ell_s}.
\tag{6}
\]

Thus `H_X(0)` is the distinguished packet load, while `H_X(tau)` is exactly the shifted-twist packet after multiplication by a deterministic unit phase. No Taylor approximation is involved.

## 2. Every subcritical aperture can still be blind to a dangerous packet

Take the dangerous construction in its minimal prime-scale form. Choose `M=Theta(a_X/log X)` candidate sites on the progression

\[
J+r q_X,
\qquad 0\le r<M,
\tag{7}
\]

inside a physical interval of length

\[
B_X=\Theta(a_X).
\tag{8}
\]

Partition their distinguished carrier phases into six sectors of width `pi/3` and retain a most-populated sector. With the implied constant in `M` large enough, the retained set contains `Theta(a_X/log X)` sites and, by (4), has

\[
|H_X(0)|\ge a_X.
\tag{9}
\]

Because `a_X=o(K)` in the bow regime `epsilon<1/4`, the packet can be placed so that `J+B_X<K`. Its total absolute mass is `O(a_X)`.

For every retained site,

\[
0\le\ell_s
=\log\left(1+\frac{j_s-J}{X+J}\right)
\le \frac{B_X}{X+J}
\ll \frac{a_X}{X}.
\tag{10}
\]

Hence, uniformly for `|tau|<=T_X`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
|H_X(\tau)-H_X(0)|
&\le
A_X\sum_s |e^{-i\tau\ell_s}-1|\\
&\le
A_X m\,T_X\max_s\ell_s\\
&\ll
 a_X\,T_X\frac{a_X}{X}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{11}
\]

Therefore, whenever

\[
T_X=o\!\left(\frac{X}{a_X}\right),
\tag{12}
\]

we have the uniform flattening relation

\[
\boxed{
H_X(\tau)=H_X(0)+o(a_X)
\qquad (|\tau|\le T_X).
}
\tag{13}
\]

So enlarging a local twist probe from finitely many derivatives to an entire continuum of physical twist samples does not by itself defeat endpoint memory if its aperture remains `o(X/a_X)`. Any fixed bounded linear compression of those samples can still see an asymptotically rank-one packet.

## 3. A symmetric three-point probe detects every packet at aperture `Theta(X/a_X)`

Now take **any** sector-aligned prime-scale packet satisfying (2)--(4) and the dangerous-load condition

\[
|H_X(0)|\ge a_X.
\tag{14}
\]

The trivial bound `|H_X(0)|<=A_Xm` gives

\[
m\ge\frac{a_X}{A_X}.
\tag{15}
\]

Let `s_0=ceil(m/2)`. By the spacing (3), every `s>=s_0` satisfies, for all sufficiently large `X`,

\[
j_s-J
\ge (s_0-1)q_X
\ge \frac{a_X}{3}.
\tag{16}
\]

Since `K=o(X)` and `j_s,J<=K`, the elementary bound `log(1+u)>=u/2` for small positive `u` gives

\[
\ell_s
=
\log\left(1+\frac{j_s-J}{X+J}\right)
\ge c_0\frac{a_X}{X}
\qquad(s\ge s_0)
\tag{17}
\]

with an absolute constant `c_0>0`.

Consider the rephased symmetric second difference

\[
C_X(\tau)
:=
2H_X(0)-H_X(\tau)-H_X(-\tau).
\tag{18}
\]

It has the exact representation

\[
C_X(\tau)
=
2A_X\sum_{s=1}^{m}
\zeta_s\,[1-\cos(\tau\ell_s)].
\tag{19}
\]

Every scalar weight in (19) is nonnegative. After the common sector rotation in (4), destructive cancellation is therefore impossible in the real projection. Average over `0<=tau<=T`:

\[
\frac1T\int_0^T [1-\cos(\tau\ell)]\,d\tau
=
1-\frac{\sin(T\ell)}{T\ell}.
\tag{20}
\]

Choose

\[
T=C\frac{X}{a_X}
\tag{21}
\]

with `C` large enough that `T ell_s>=2` for every `s>=s_0`. For such atoms,

\[
1-\frac{\sin(T\ell_s)}{T\ell_s}\ge\frac12.
\tag{22}
\]

Using (4), (15), and the fact that at least `m/2` atoms lie in the outer half,

\[
\begin{aligned}
\frac1T\int_0^T
\operatorname{Re} C_X(\tau)\,d\tau
&\ge
c_1 A_X m\\
&\ge c_1 a_X.
\end{aligned}
\tag{23}
\]

Consequently there exists some `tau_X` with

\[
0\le\tau_X\le C\frac{X}{a_X}
\tag{24}
\]

such that

\[
\boxed{
|C_X(\tau_X)|\gg a_X.
}
\tag{25}
\]

Thus three physical twist samples, at `U` and `U+-tau_X` with their deterministic first-site rephasings, necessarily resolve every dangerous packet in this matched-control class once the aperture reaches a sufficiently large constant multiple of `X/a_X`.

## 4. Relation to the `K/a_X` derivative threshold

The two sharp thresholds measure the same packet width in different coordinates. `ANF-181` uses

\[
\xi_s=W_X\log(1+j_s/X),
\qquad
W_X=\frac{\xi_*}{\log(1+J/X)}
\asymp \frac{X}{J}.
\tag{26}
\]

A normalized exact-log displacement `y` corresponds to a physical twist displacement

\[
\tau=yW_X.
\tag{27}
\]

The hardest packets are placed late, with `J` comparable to the available endpoint horizon `K`. At that placement, the derivative-resolution scale from `ANF-182`,

\[
y\asymp\frac{K}{a_X},
\tag{28}
\]

maps to

\[
\tau
\asymp
\frac{X}{K}\frac{K}{a_X}
=
\frac{X}{a_X}.
\tag{29}
\]

This explains why `K` disappears from the physical threshold. The packet has physical width `Theta(a_X)` inside a base scale `X`; resolving that width by direct twist displacement costs reciprocal aperture `Theta(X/a_X)`. The factor `K` in the derivative statement enters only because the exact-log coordinate was normalized at a packet placed as late as `J~K`.

Numerically in exponent language,

\[
\boxed{
\frac{X}{a_X}
= X^{1/2+o(1)}(\log X)^{-1/2},
}
\tag{30}
\]

independent of `epsilon`, whereas

\[
\frac{K}{a_X}
=X^{1/2-2\varepsilon+o(1)}(\log X)^{-1/2}.
\tag{31}
\]

The nonlocal route therefore replaces a polynomially huge derivative hierarchy by a three-sample observable whose samples are separated by a polynomial physical twist distance.

## 5. Evidence boundary and analytic consequence

Equation (25) is a discrimination theorem, not the missing arithmetic estimate. The actual residual `Lambda-Lambda^sharp` is not free to select sites after inspecting the distinguished carrier, and the present argument does not prove that the three shifted source sums can be evaluated with absolute error `o(a_X)`. Existing all-interval discorrelation above the `X^(5/8+eta)` activation scale supplies relative errors of the form `H log^{-A}X`; `ANF-175`--`ANF-176` already show why such errors can remain much larger than the endpoint-memory load scale.

The result nevertheless changes the form of one viable escape. To defeat the matched-control obstruction, it is not necessary in principle to estimate `Theta(K/a_X)` derivatives. It would suffice to obtain source-faithful absolute control of a **rephased symmetric twist curvature** at three heights separated by

\[
|\tau|\asymp\sqrt{X/\log X},
\tag{32}
\]

or another observable with comparable physical frequency aperture. Because `|tau|=o(U)` when `U~X^2`, all three heights remain in the same macroscopic distinguished-twist regime; what is missing is norm strength and source-specific cancellation, not access to a different height scale.

This does not supersede the direct route through the signed dyadic statistic / Fejer slope of `ANF-169`--`ANF-172`. It supplies a second, now quantitatively sharp, nonlocal target against which future source theorems can be tested.

## 6. Prior-art audit and next gate

Classical super-resolution theory has an analogous reciprocal relation between Fourier bandwidth and point separation. In particular, Moitra's sharp Vandermonde/super-resolution threshold compares cutoff frequency with the inverse minimum node separation, and Candès--Fernandez-Granda recover separated point sources from band-limited Fourier data under an inverse-bandwidth spacing condition. That literature is conceptually adjacent to (12)--(25), but it is not load-bearing here: the present proof uses only the exact logarithmic carrier, the prime-scale mass/spacing model, sector positivity, and the elementary averaged identity (20). No external theorem is invoked, so no `SOURCES.md` update is required.

The next source-side gate can now be stated concretely. Either prove that prime-minus-Ramanujan placement forbids the carrier-selected packet, control the signed `ANF-169`/`ANF-172` Fejer-slope observable directly, or derive an absolute `o(a_X)` estimate for a rephased three-height curvature with aperture `Theta(X/a_X)`. Merely increasing the number of local twist samples while keeping total aperture `o(X/a_X)` remains vulnerable to the same endpoint-memory construction.
