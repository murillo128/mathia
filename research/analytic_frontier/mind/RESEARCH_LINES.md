`D_{2H}-D_H = 2 Re S_H`.

`|D_{2H}-D_H| = o(X log X / log K)`

uniformly on the surviving dyadic endpoint scales. Estimating `F_H` and `F_{2H}` independently is not enough unless the errors survive the discrete scale differences; the target is adjacent-scale stability, not another standalone positive square-function bound.
