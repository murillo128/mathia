## Control the endpoint Fejer scale-slope defect with the actual prime/rough placement law

**Linked intuition:** `MI-007-bow-variance-needs-source-specific-coherence-at-the-distinguished-twist`.

ANF-150--ANF-172 reduce bow variance to a phase-sensitive endpoint problem. Target-sized completion forces a large positive real dyadic shifted correlation, and that signed shell is exactly a dyadic defect in the scale slope of a positive endpoint Fejer family. A single moving-window energy is only a necessary shadow because phase orthogonality can make the energy critical while killing the real completion.

ANF-173 removes the earlier upper-range artifact: by raising the fixed Taylor degree, available exact-twist discorrelation extends through the full endpoint horizon `X^(5/8+eta) <= H <= K`. It also shows that arbitrary fixed-log savings, qualitative fixed-order Gowers uniformity, and bounded-test relative decorrelation can coexist with target-sized endpoint charge through a tiny carrier-aligned drift.

ANF-174 shows that the tiny-amplitude freedom is not essential. A vanishing-density subset of a coarse grid, chosen only where the **exact** logarithmic carrier lies in one angular sector, can use amplitudes restricted to `{0,1}` to store charge `asymp sqrt(X log X)` inside `o(K)` sites and then hold it for `Theta(K)` prefixes. Every bounded-test correlation on long intervals is nevertheless power-small, and every fixed-order Gowers norm is power-small. Hence coefficient quantization, a nonzero amplitude floor, nonnegative signs, sparsity, and generic relative pseudorandomness still do not anchor the endpoint.

ANF-175 removes the remaining one-point-scale mismatch in that control. Atoms of size `log X` on candidate sites spaced by `log X` obey a local total-variation envelope `O(H+log X)`, matching the natural prime-sized magnitude/density scale. Such an envelope forces any critical charge `sqrt(X log X)` to require physical loading length `Omega(sqrt(X log X))`, and exact carrier-sector selection attains that lower bound. The whole packet then has only `O(sqrt(X log X))` total mass, so on every interval with `H>=X^(5/8+eta)` it satisfies correlation `o(H log^{-A} X)` against **every bounded test**, for every fixed `A`, while still forcing bow-scale completion and the target Fejer defect. Thus the placement obstruction is sharply localized at the near-square-root loading scale below the current all-interval activation barrier.

ANF-176 turns that scale obstruction into an activation-saving phase diagram. If a long-interval theorem first activates at `H_0=X^(theta+o(1))` and is used only through an allowance `B(H)=H X^(-sigma+o(1))`, the ANF-175 packet automatically passes whenever `theta-sigma>1/2`, because its entire absolute mass is only `O(sqrt(X log X))`. The exact comparison is `B(H_0)` versus `sqrt(X log X)`; the power line `theta-sigma=1/2` is the boundary, with logarithmic factors deciding equality. Consequently fixed-log improvements are powerless at **every** fixed `theta>1/2`, not just at `5/8`. At `5/8` one needs roughly an `X^(-1/8)` relative power saving; even a hypothetical source-faithful exact-carrier theorem activated at the Guth--Maynard ordinary-prime exponent `17/30` would still need roughly `X^(-1/15)`. At `H_0 asymp sqrt(X log X)`, by contrast, any genuine `o(1)` relative saving falls below the critical charge.

ANF-177 shows that local averaging in the twist variable does not provide a coefficient-generic escape either. The ANF-175 packet remains `Omega(a_X)` for `a_X=sqrt(X log X)` throughout its natural coherence window `W_Xasymp X/a_Xasymp sqrt(X/log X)`. Its forced local twist mean square is therefore `Omega(Xa_X)`, while the classical separated-frequency Montgomery--Vaughan bound from its `log X` site spacing and `l^2` mass is only `O(Xa_X)`. Thus the packet already saturates the generic local-twist `L^2` envelope before its frequencies reach their much larger orthogonality scale `X/log X`.

ANF-178 closes the obvious higher-moment escape from that result. On the whole coherence window the same packet satisfies the pointwise two-sided bound `|P_X(t)|asymp a_X`; hence for every fixed `p>0` its local `L^p` mass is `asymp X a_X^(p-1)`, and the level set `|P_X|>=a_X/2` occupies the entire `Theta(X/a_X)` window. Fourth moments, higher fixed moments, generic large-value estimates, and any fixed nondegenerate convex/Orlicz magnitude penalty therefore see the same obstruction at full coherence scale. The issue is not weakness of `L^2`; it is a carrier-aligned packet which is uniformly large on the complete dual window.

The live theorem must therefore control the exact Fejer scale-slope defect, or a source-faithful equivalent, using the **joint placement of the actual prime/rough residual mass relative to the distinguished carrier**. Quantitatively, a long-interval exact-carrier estimate with activation `H_0` and relative error `rho_X` becomes relevant only once `rho_X H_0=o(sqrt(X log X))`, equivalently once its activation-saving profile crosses the ANF-176 boundary. A local-twist route is equally source-specific: already `p=2` requires an arithmetic `o(Xa_X)` mean square on the `X/a_X` coherence window, and ANF-178 shows that merely increasing the moment order does not weaken this burden. Reaching `H asymp sqrt(X log X)` with any genuine relative saving, beating the generic local-twist envelope arithmetically, or directly controlling the signed dyadic/Fejer statistic are the remaining interfaces.

## Treat low-spectrum abundance, carrier resonance, polylogarithmic discrepancy, phase-blind uniformity, amplitude quantization, and generic local-twist magnitude statistics as controls

The distinguished twist is a unitary gauge, low positive directions are abundant, the bare carrier fails at the endpoint threat scale, arbitrarily strong fixed-log long-interval cancellation can coexist with target-sized completion, and target-scale moving-window energy can coexist with negligible real completion through phase orthogonality. ANF-173 adds that full-endpoint fixed-order relative uniformity can coexist with a dangerous accumulated charge. ANF-174 adds that even a nonnegative `{0,1}` amplitude alphabet and vanishing support density can coexist with the same dangerous memory while all long-interval bounded-test and fixed-order Gowers statistics decay by powers. ANF-175 further matches prime-sized atom magnitude and density and shows that the dangerous packet can be fully assembled on the sharp `sqrt(X log X)` scale while its total mass is already below every fixed-log error allowance on the current `H>=X^(5/8+eta)` range. ANF-176 generalizes that invisibility to every activation-saving profile with `theta-sigma>1/2`, showing that exponent-only progress above one half cannot close the endpoint. ANF-177--ANF-178 then show that the same matched packet remains target-sized across its maximal generic twist-coherence window and saturates not only the local `L^2` envelope but every fixed local magnitude norm and the critical large-value tail there. A viable source theorem must cross the absolute-error boundary, obtain a strict arithmetic saving below the generic local-twist magnitude scale, constrain the arithmetic placement law at the near-square-root loading scale, or directly control the signed scale-difference statistic consumed downstream.
