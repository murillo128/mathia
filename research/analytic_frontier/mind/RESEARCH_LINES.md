# Analytic-frontier research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable analytic-frontier intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they separate genuinely different mechanisms.

## Make horizontal zero information survive the microscopic target scale

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-global-conjugation-geometry-carries-horizontal-information`.

ANF-001 shows that a power saving proved only at fixed horizontal displacement can collapse to a constant-factor statement when the displacement is `O(1/log T)`. ANF-002 shows, conversely, that support-one pair data can carry horizontal information when the full conjugation-symmetric global quadratic geometry is retained.

A decisive advance must therefore produce a near-line estimate whose coercive gain remains nontrivial at the actual microscopic scale, or prove that a proposed fixed-scale theorem cannot be made uniform enough. A visually or algebraically nonzero horizontal signal is not enough if its exponent budget vanishes after the required rescaling.

## Solve the signed support-one certificate problem with its normalization slack exposed

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-finite-pair-enrichment-dualizes-to-one-signed-profile`.

ANF-003--ANF-005 show that common-translation vector features and finite convex families of support-one pair observables scalarize, after affine separation, to one signed support-one profile. Allowing signs does not remove the cost: tiny configurations force an explicit normalization slack, and zero slack returns the termwise-nonnegative Montgomery--Taylor class.

The remaining finite-pair question is therefore a concrete extremal problem for the signed profile together with its compulsory slack. A positive must beat the known ceiling after including that slack; adding finitely many moments or channels without escaping the scalarized affine witness is not a new mechanism.

## Seek genuinely pre-scalarization matrix, higher-support, or higher-correlation structure

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-global-conjugation-geometry-carries-horizontal-information` and `MI-002-finite-pair-enrichment-dualizes-to-one-signed-profile`.

Lamzouri's Hilbert-inequality route demonstrates that global organization can recover information invisible to termwise-positive extraction. The next distinct categories are therefore objects whose sign or coercivity is established before reduction to one common-character scalar density: a true matrix order, support beyond one with justified arithmetic input, or a higher-correlation observable not affinely equivalent to one pair profile.

A decisive result must identify the exact extra information and show that matched support-one scalar controls cannot reproduce it.
