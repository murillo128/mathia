## Treat a distinguished bow saving as source-forced signed cancellation, not averaged variance

Any proof that takes absolute values or replaces the distinguished twist by an average before exposing that sign cannot reach the target. The live analytic question is to identify and control the actual source-fixed cancellation, or rule it out.
