This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable analytic-frontier intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they separate genuinely different mechanisms.

## Resolve the residual unequal-height Montgomery--Taylor zero-freeness problem

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-global-conjugation-geometry-carries-horizontal-information`, `MI-002-finite-pair-enrichment-dualizes-to-one-signed-profile`, and `MI-004-complete-scalar-control-is-diffraction-realizability`.

ANF-034--ANF-053 reduce complete scalar cardinality-five stability to one compact two-pair defect problem. The central-notch perturbation is now understood sharply: on bounded windows its leading `eta^3` term universally decreases the defect, so a genuine zero of the Montgomery--Taylor base defect would make every sufficiently narrow notch fail, while base zero-freeness would make all sufficiently narrow notches positive.

ANF-054 closes the entire equal-height diagonal. ANF-055 gives an explicit unequal-height tube, and ANF-056--ANF-057 strengthen it to a support-free relative-height exclusion. Under the general curvature gate every zero must satisfy `|y_1-y_2|/(y_1+y_2) >= 0.129209...`; for the fixed Montgomery--Taylor profile the certified strict margin improves this to `>0.1409`, equivalently `y_max/y_min>1.3280`. The horizontal-free comparison is small-frequency sharp at this stage.

The live scalar frontier is therefore no longer an arbitrary compact neighborhood of the diagonal. It is the residual intermediate-mismatch region outside the fixed cone, still constrained by the earlier height/separation bounds. Further progress must retain horizontal phase/separation information, use a stronger profile-specific diagonal margin, or analyze the fixed Montgomery--Taylor integral directly; merely re-optimizing the support-free scalar envelope cannot cross its small-frequency coefficient.

## Use unconditional out-of-band information only through a genuinely different zero-side carrier

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-global-conjugation-geometry-carries-horizontal-information` and `MI-002-finite-pair-enrichment-dualizes-to-one-signed-profile`.

The BGSST form factor is nonnegative beyond support one, but universal affine scalar certificates cannot exploit that region through a negative spectral tail. A live continuation must change the carrier through matrix/inertia order, nonlinear configuration information, source-specific inequalities, or genuine higher correlations, and then survive finite-cluster and multiplicity controls.

## Change local information rather than only block size

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-ordered-local-consistency-escapes-pair-scalarization`.

Ordered local consistency escapes global pair scalarization, but the present pressure architecture has a bounded gain and returns toward baseline as point order grows. A useful extension must alter the local state, compatibility law, nonlinear defect, or assembly rule rather than merely increase block size.
