# Analytic-frontier research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable analytic-frontier intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they separate genuinely different mechanisms.

## Solve or close the signed support-one affine extremal problem

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-finite-pair-enrichment-dualizes-to-one-signed-profile`.

Finite global pair enrichments scalarize to one signed support-one profile with compulsory normalization slack. The remaining scalar question is exact: determine whether the ANF-005 finite-configuration and copositivity constraints force the Montgomery--Taylor floor after slack, or exhibit a strict improvement that also satisfies the universal counting inequality.

This is the last live scalar-affine support-one branch. Adding finitely many already-global pair moments without leaving the affine scalar witness is not a new mechanism.

## Use unconditional out-of-band pair information only through a non-scalar or non-affine zero-side carrier

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-global-conjugation-geometry-carries-horizontal-information` and `MI-002-finite-pair-enrichment-dualizes-to-one-signed-profile`.

The BGSST form factor is nonnegative beyond support one, but ANF-010--ANF-011 show that neither positive scalar Gram kernels nor universal indefinite scalar affine pair certificates can exploit a nontrivial negative out-of-band dual tail. A decisive continuation must therefore identify a zero-side carrier that leaves that category: matrix/inertia order, a source-specific inequality, a nonlinear configuration functional, or genuine higher-correlation structure.

The test is not whether the analytic tail exists; it is whether the new carrier converts it into horizontal coercivity without violating conjugate-pair controls.

## Change the local information carrier rather than only increasing block size or point order

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-ordered-local-consistency-escapes-pair-scalarization`.

Ordered local consistency is a demonstrated escape from global pair scalarization, but the current pressure architecture is now structurally bounded. Two points cannot improve the baseline, the first useful layer is consecutive triple consistency, the optimal block size is forced once the certificate is fixed, and the `n`-point pressure envelope returns to the baseline as `n` grows.

A decisive new gain must therefore alter the local state, compatibility law, nonlinear defect, window/assembly rule, or analytic bridge. Merely taking larger blocks or more points inside the unchanged `F/Phi_n` carrier cannot provide a fixed asymptotic advantage.

## Make horizontal zero information survive the microscopic target scale

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-global-conjugation-geometry-carries-horizontal-information`.

A fixed-horizontal gain can collapse after displacement is rescaled to `O(1/log T)`. Any near-line theorem intended for RH-adjacent use must retain a quantitative margin at the actual microscopic scale and under the final zero detector. A nonzero fixed-scale signal is only diagnostic unless its complete exponent/support budget survives that rescaling.
