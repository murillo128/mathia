## Decide whether the surviving same-profile cascade is fatally non-excisable

**Linked intuition:** `MI-005-one-pair-collapse-is-curvature-seeded-before-higher-order-effects`.

ANF-134--ANF-142 progressively remove raw depth, span, slack-spend, coefficient-spread, and fixed exponential zero-pinching as existential obstructions to a same-profile cascade. ANF-143 goes further: source-chosen odd dilations can preserve the exact annihilation while making residual slack positive, total translated span summable, support only `O(A)`, and all finite-depth conditioning costs absorbable by slow diagonalization.

The live gate is therefore no longer whether the geometry can be kept under control. A useful theorem must show either that this cloud necessarily carries non-negligible source energy/coherence, or that it can always be excised while preserving the source objective.

Positive residual slack excludes a fixed positive exponential zero-pinching loss on active late layers, and finite-`A` quantization confines active unbounded tails to a mesoscopic window. Slow diagonalization can absorb every finite collection of local conditioning constants. These facts should now be used as controls rather than repeatedly reopened as generic failure modes.

A new obstruction has to survive the ANF-143 dilation freedom and attack the actual source-energy distribution, finite-prefix interaction, or excisability of the late cloud. Conversely, a positive continuation must quantify the source norm on that cloud; support count or coefficient concentration alone is not enough.
