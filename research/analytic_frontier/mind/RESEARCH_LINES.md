This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable analytic-frontier intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Bound fixed-notch near-extremizer exposure after removing fake escape tails

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-separable-near-nullity-is-not-physical-instability`, `MI-005-one-pair-collapse-is-curvature-seeded-before-higher-order-effects`.

ANF-097--ANF-099 show that dangerous destination defect cannot be read from a raw Gram condition number and that convexifying the separable comparator class destroys the distinction. For a declared notch `phi_eta`, improvement exists exactly when the limiting physical near-extremizer exposure `beta_eta` is strictly below the target ratio `B_eta`, and that exposure is recoverable from the source tensor up to the extremality defect.

ANF-100--ANF-103 now narrow the noncompactness mechanisms sharply. Every growing near-extremizer needs at least linear horizontal span; linearly spanned families have exponentially tight normalized vertical bulk; and an `o(N)` remote tail whose Montgomery--Taylor source norm is negligible can be trimmed without changing either near-extremality or limiting notch exposure. A sub-square-root tail that remains source-heavy must cross the universal endpoint scale

`4 pi H >= log(L/m^2) + 2 log log(L/m^2) + O(1)`.

The remaining fixed-notch counterexamples must therefore live in a linearly spanned tight bulk with genuine microscopic organization, a genuinely diffuse superlinear bulk that cannot be reduced to a linear core, or a source-heavy escaping tail satisfying the endpoint budget. The decisive test is still `beta_eta < B_eta`: prove it on all surviving chambers, or construct a physical near-extremizer in one of them whose exposure reaches `B_eta`.

## Treat a distinguished bow saving as resonant signed cancellation, not uniform variance control

ANF-102 closes the uniform form of the transferred bow variance target. Averaging the exact MRSTT residual over any fixed multiplicative-scale twist window `U asymp X^2` leaves a diagonal contribution `(1+o(1)) X K log X`, so no bound `o(X K log X)` can hold uniformly throughout that regime.

A distinguished bow twist can still be exceptional, but then the off-diagonal covariance must cancel the prime diagonal at the same macroscopic scale. Any proof that takes absolute values before exposing that sign cannot reach the target. The live analytic question is therefore to identify the exact bow twist/weight and prove its source-forced negative off-diagonal correlation, or to show that such a resonance is impossible.

## Keep relative Xi reconstruction separate from destination conditioning

The relative Xi source reconstruction remains extremely accurate on its admitted moving lines, but that forward accuracy does not itself control a destination observable whose safe and dangerous directions differ. Use the exact destination quotient and source tensor before translating analytic approximation into coercivity.

## Treat diffraction realizability and source geometry as independent gates

A formally admissible positive or spectral profile is not automatically realized by the Montgomery source configuration. Whenever a proposed analytic carrier is defined through a transform, isolate the inverse/realizability constraint before interpreting its destination sign or near-extremal geometry.
