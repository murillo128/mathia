# Analytic-frontier research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable analytic-frontier intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they separate genuinely different mechanisms.

## Prove or break the complete finite-configuration/diffraction ceiling for the universal scalar carrier

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-finite-pair-enrichment-dualizes-to-one-signed-profile` and `MI-004-complete-scalar-control-is-diffraction-realizability`.

ANF-018--ANF-019 identify the scalar floor with free-density finite-particle stability: a finite bound cluster can be replicated at arbitrarily large particle number, so fixed-density bulk optimality is not decisive. ANF-020 gives the exact dual problem: determine whether the convex diffraction body of finite real configurations meets the Montgomery--Taylor budget. ANF-021 shows that even a Pfaffian/symplectic candidate with the right local hyperuniform slope fails the full support-one domination.

The decisive scalar result is therefore either a universal finite-cluster/diffraction theorem proving the ceiling, or one admissible separating spectrum/realizable diffraction witness that breaks it. More periodization, small-frequency slope matching, or thermodynamic optimization is not enough.

## Use unconditional out-of-band information only through a genuinely different zero-side carrier

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-global-conjugation-geometry-carries-horizontal-information` and `MI-002-finite-pair-enrichment-dualizes-to-one-signed-profile`.

The BGSST form factor is nonnegative beyond support one, but universal affine scalar certificates cannot exploit that region through a negative spectral tail. A live continuation must change the carrier through matrix/inertia order, nonlinear configuration information, source-specific inequalities, or genuine higher correlations, and then survive finite-cluster and multiplicity controls.

## Change local information rather than only block size

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-ordered-local-consistency-escapes-pair-scalarization`.

Ordered local consistency escapes global pair scalarization, but the present pressure architecture has a bounded gain and returns toward baseline as point order grows. A useful extension must alter the local state, compatibility law, nonlinear defect, or assembly rule rather than merely increase block size.

## Preserve horizontal information at the microscopic target scale

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-global-conjugation-geometry-carries-horizontal-information`.

Any fixed-horizontal gain must be re-audited after displacement is rescaled to `O(1/log T)`. A RH-adjacent theorem needs a quantitative margin in the actual microscopic normalization and in the final zero detector, not only a nonzero fixed-scale effect.
