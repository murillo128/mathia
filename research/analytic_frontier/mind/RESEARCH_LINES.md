## Control the endpoint Fejer scale-slope defect with the actual prime/rough placement law

**Linked intuition:** `MI-007-bow-variance-needs-source-specific-coherence-at-the-distinguished-twist`.

ANF-150--ANF-172 reduce bow variance to a phase-sensitive endpoint problem. Target-sized completion forces a large positive real dyadic shifted correlation, and that signed shell is exactly a dyadic defect in the scale slope of a positive endpoint Fejer family. A single moving-window energy is only a necessary shadow because phase orthogonality can make the energy critical while killing the real completion.

ANF-173 removes the earlier upper-range artifact: by raising the fixed Taylor degree, available exact-twist discorrelation extends through the full endpoint horizon `X^(5/8+eta) <= H <= K`. It also shows that arbitrary fixed-log savings, qualitative fixed-order Gowers uniformity, and bounded-test relative decorrelation can coexist with target-sized endpoint charge through a tiny carrier-aligned drift.

ANF-174 shows that the tiny-amplitude freedom is not essential. A vanishing-density subset of a coarse grid, chosen only where the **exact** logarithmic carrier lies in one angular sector, can use amplitudes restricted to `{0,1}` to store charge `asymp sqrt(X log X)` inside `o(K)` sites and then hold it for `Theta(K)` prefixes. Every bounded-test correlation on long intervals is nevertheless power-small, and every fixed-order Gowers norm is power-small. Hence coefficient quantization, a nonzero amplitude floor, nonnegative signs, sparsity, and generic relative pseudorandomness still do not anchor the endpoint.

The live theorem must therefore control the exact Fejer scale-slope defect, or a source-faithful equivalent, using the **joint placement of the actual prime/rough residual mass relative to the distinguished carrier**. A useful equivalent target would be an absolute discrepancy estimate against carrier-sector selectors at the `sqrt(X log X)` charging scale. A relative-uniformity-only route would need roughly `rho_X K=o(sqrt(X log X))`; replacing tiny amplitudes by sparse unit occupancy does not evade that information barrier. Extending the same fixed-order uniformity theorem, discretizing amplitudes, or imposing sparsity is no longer the missing step.

## Treat low-spectrum abundance, carrier resonance, polylogarithmic discrepancy, phase-blind uniformity, and amplitude quantization as controls

The distinguished twist is a unitary gauge, low positive directions are abundant, the bare carrier fails at the endpoint threat scale, arbitrarily strong fixed-log long-interval cancellation can coexist with target-sized completion, and target-scale moving-window energy can coexist with negligible real completion through phase orthogonality. ANF-173 adds that full-endpoint fixed-order relative uniformity can coexist with a dangerous accumulated charge. ANF-174 adds that even a nonnegative `{0,1}` amplitude alphabet and vanishing support density can coexist with the same dangerous memory while all long-interval bounded-test and fixed-order Gowers statistics decay by powers. A viable source theorem must constrain the absolute signed/scale-difference statistic actually consumed downstream, or the arithmetic placement law that forces it small.
