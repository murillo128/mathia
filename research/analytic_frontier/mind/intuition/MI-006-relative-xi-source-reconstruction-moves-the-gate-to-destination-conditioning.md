# MI-006 — Relative Xi source reconstruction moves the live gate to destination conditioning

**Evidence level:** proved source-side relative periodization through ANF-084; destination transport remains unproved

## Core intuition

A moving zero-free Euler-product contour no longer appears to be the limiting information channel for the Xi handoff. ANF-084 constructs the actual relative Xi source on `sigma_T=1+1/log T` with Gaussian width `log T` and period `(log T)^3` and obtains superpolynomially small relative periodization error, including every fixed logarithmic derivative needed by a local interface.

The remaining question is therefore not whether the source can be represented accurately enough. It is whether the **normalized positive-time/reference transport and destination selector preserve that accuracy**. A bridge that loses an exponential margin through an ill-conditioned quotient, divisor map, or guarded resource has failed at the destination even when source reconstruction is essentially exact.

## Strongest justified principle

ANF-084 works entirely on a reflected `Re s>1` rectangle, where the Euler product and logarithmic derivatives are legitimate. Gaussian image separation gives a relative Xi error bounded by a polylogarithmic factor times `exp(-(log T)^4/8)`, and the finite-prime leakage is `exp(-c(log T)^2)` on the declared scale. No RH assumption and no positive-time Euler product enter.

This eliminates two previous generic objections: moving the contour toward `1` does not by itself consume the source margin, and differentiating the relative image does not force a fatal loss. The source theorem is already stronger than the precision required by the current Xi guarded-mode range.

The decisive quantity is now a condition number: how much the subsequent heat/reference transport, admissible-carrier construction, and normalized selector can amplify the source error. That question belongs to the actual destination coordinates rather than to the source periodization norm.

## Counterevidence / boundary

ANF-084 does not construct a real-rooted periodic carrier, prove positive-time quotient stability, control Vieta normalization, or show that a hypothetical positive-`Lambda` state has nontrivial guarded mass. It therefore does not solve the Xi-flow problem or produce an upper bound for the de Bruijn--Newman constant.

The already persisted Xi clue `CLUE-relative-xi-source-to-guarded-selector-stability` states the precise speculative bridge; this intuition records only the established relocation of the source/destination boundary.

## Epistemic status

**Proved source-side, open destination-side.** The analytic ingredients are classical; the durable Mathia conclusion is the conditioning boundary created by their exact specialization.

## Falsification criterion

Invalidate the ANF-084 relative/logarithmic periodization estimates in their stated moving-line regime, or prove that the required destination transport has a uniform condition number small enough to consume the source margin and enter the guarded Xi resource. The latter would retire the conditioning gate rather than strengthen it.
