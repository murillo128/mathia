# MI-005 — Fixed-notch success is controlled by physical near-extremizer exposure

**Evidence level:** supported by exact quotient/interference identities and exact near-extremizer localization through ANF-101

## Core intuition

The relevant analytic instability is not a raw Gram condition number and, for a fixed destination notch, it is not even the full nonconvex distance from the separable cone. ANF-097 shows that a fatal destination defect must be paid for by destructive source interference against compatible separable comparators, while ANF-098 shows that convexifying those comparators fills the whole physical conjugation-invariant cone and therefore erases the distinction one is trying to measure.

ANF-099 isolates the correct fixed-direction quantity. For a source-forced notch `phi_eta`, the derivative of the optimized envelope at zero is the limiting exposure `beta_eta` of physical near-extremizers, and there exists a positive notch amplitude improving the ratio exactly when `beta_eta < B_eta`. The same exposure is represented by a contracted physical source tensor up to an error vanishing with the extremality defect. Thus fixed-notch existence can be decided by one quantitative concentration theorem without solving the stronger global selector problem.

## Strongest justified principle

A dangerous near-extremizer cannot be diagnosed by conditioning of an arbitrary separable representation. The safe quotient must first be taken at the destination, and the surviving source question is how much a physical near-extremizer can align with the declared notch.

ANF-100--ANF-101 make this exposure problem geometrically tight. Any growing near-extremizer has horizontal diameter at least a fixed positive fraction of the source scale. If the diameter remains `O(N)`, its vertical mass is exponentially tight after the natural scaling. Bounded/sublinear horizontal supports and diffuse linear-span vertical escape therefore cannot supply the missing exposure.

The remaining failure modes are specific: nonconvex microscopic organization of a tight linear-scale bulk, a sparse high-height tail that still contributes to the source tensor, or superlinear horizontal escape. These are falsifiable geometric alternatives rather than generic warnings about noncompactness.

## Counterevidence / boundary

ANF-099 does not prove `beta_eta < B_eta` for any source-forced notch, and the abstract signature/rank constraints alone admit controls whose exposure is too large. ANF-100--ANF-101 do not rule out superlinear horizontal escape or a sparse tail with disproportionate source effect. The stronger problem of selecting finite-amplitude comparators across the whole nonconvex safe class also remains open.

## Epistemic status

**Exact fixed-notch reduction plus exact geometric restrictions on near-extremizers; the decisive physical exposure inequality is open.** No zero-location theorem follows yet.

## Falsification criterion

Construct physical near-extremizers for which the ANF-099 exposure reaches or exceeds the target ratio for every proposed source-forced notch, or violate the exact fixed-ray envelope derivative. A positive result is a source theorem proving `beta_eta < B_eta` while controlling the linear-span bulk, sparse-tail, and superlinear-escape alternatives.
