# MI-005 — One-pair vertical geometry reduces to growing heterogeneous real multiplicity

**Evidence level:** exact through ANF-076, with classical positive-spectrum Gram geometry

## Core intuition

The six-point one-pair obstruction is no longer primarily a complex-height problem, and it is no longer localized at three real support sites. ANF-072 shows that whenever a central notch creates a dangerous one-pair configuration, collapsing the conjugate pair to the real axis cannot improve the affine inequality: any counterexample already appears in a real multiplicity configuration.

ANF-073--ANF-074 then separate shape from normalization. The minimum-slack amplitude fails an exact doubled-site gate, but after amplitude reoptimization every sufficiently small viable notch shape survives the entire positive-spectrum class supported on at most two real sites. ANF-075 pushes this much further: **every fixed finite support cap can be defeated by narrowing the notch**. ANF-076 independently proves that unbounded support is harmless whenever all occupied sites have the same multiplicity.

The first unresolved scalar information is therefore not “three support sites” but **heterogeneous occupancy whose support cardinality grows with the inverse notch scale**.

## Strongest justified principle

ANF-070 screens the complete base-profile six-point collapse-reversal branch by a positive affine floor, ANF-071 confines any notch-created reversal to the collapsed boundary, and ANF-072 proves that vertical one-pair displacement adds no new danger before the collapsed real problem is solved.

For the central-notch family, ANF-075 gives a support-count-uniform estimate at every fixed `R`: after paying a slack of order `R beta eta`, the pair-functional gain remains order `beta`, so sufficiently small notch width `eta` makes every prescribed finite support cap affordable. No fixed number of distinct real support sites can therefore be a shape-level no-go.

ANF-076 identifies a complementary exact closure. The finite-real floor `q_real` gives the sharp intercept for every real multiset with **uniform occupancy**, with no support-cardinality bound at all. The central-notch separator has the favorable `C/q_real` ratio needed to beat Montgomery--Taylor on this entire class with one optimized amplitude.

Thus a surviving real-multiplicity obstruction must combine two features simultaneously: support size cannot remain bounded as the notch narrows, and the multiplicities cannot remain uniform across that growing support. Reopening complex height before resolving this support-uniform heterogeneous envelope would reintroduce a variable already shown not to be load-bearing for the first obstruction.

## What remains possible

A heterogeneous family with increasingly many occupied sites may accumulate negative off-diagonal contributions faster than the fixed-cap slack argument can absorb them, and `q_real` does not classify nonuniform replication. The unresolved problem is whether one common amplitude/intercept can control that full heterogeneous envelope or whether a genuinely new growing-support obstruction appears.

Separately, total cardinality eleven remains the first one-pair size where the higher-order coefficient argument is no longer automatically curvature-seeded. Ordered or multi-pair information also remains a genuinely different category. These frontiers should not be collapsed into the real-multiplicity question.

## Status / novelty

The Gram positivity, collapse comparison, amplitude optimization, support-count estimate, and finite-real-floor reduction are exact line evidence built from classical positive-definite kernel geometry. The durable synthesis is the strengthened reduction: **the first unresolved scalar notch mechanism is support-uniform heterogeneous real multiplicity with growing support, not complex height and not any fixed support count.**

## Falsification criterion

Produce a one-pair central-notch counterexample whose collapsed real multiset satisfies the same affine inequality, invalidate ANF-075's fixed-support-cap estimate or ANF-076's uniform-occupancy extremal reduction, or find a fixed support cap that obstructs every sufficiently narrow admissible notch after amplitude optimization. Otherwise the next scalar gate must be attacked through genuinely growing heterogeneous occupancy.
