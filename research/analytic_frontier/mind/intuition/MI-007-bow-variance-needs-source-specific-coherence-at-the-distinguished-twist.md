# MI-007 — Bow completion is an absolute, phase-sensitive endpoint-memory problem

**Evidence level:** supported

## Core intuition

The bow endpoint obstruction is not controlled by generic local regularity. Target-sized completion consumes a **positive real** dyadic shifted correlation at the distinguished logarithmic twist, equivalently a scale-slope defect of a positive Fejer family. A matched packet can store the required `sqrt(X log X)` memory using prime-sized atoms at prime-scale density while remaining invisible to long-interval bounded tests.

The newer evidence sharpens this further: the same packet can be target-sized throughout its full twist-coherence window and can saturate every fixed local magnitude norm and every fixed naturally normalized twist derivative. Finite jets and fixed Sobolev order therefore do not recover the missing arithmetic information.

## Strongest justified claim

ANF-157 and ANF-169--ANF-172 identify the exact endpoint currency. Completion forces a signed shifted-correlation shell, and `D_(2H)-D_H=2 Re S_H` recovers it as an adjacent-scale defect of positive Fejer energies. One-scale energy and complex coherence can be large with the wrong phase.

ANF-173--ANF-176 construct increasingly source-shaped controls. Fixed-log exact-twist cancellation can hold across the endpoint horizon while target charge survives; the charge can use `{0,1}` amplitudes or `log X` atoms at `log X` spacing; and the critical memory is assembled on the sharp physical scale `Theta(sqrt(X log X))`. If a theorem first controls scale `H_0` with allowed absolute error `rho_X H_0`, it cannot rule out this packet while that allowance is asymptotically larger than `sqrt(X log X)`.

ANF-177--ANF-178 show that local twist averaging does not help generically. The packet stays comparable to the critical charge throughout the coherence window `W_X asymp X/sqrt(X log X)` and saturates every fixed `L^p` magnitude statistic and large-value tail there.

ANF-179 removes the finite-jet escape. After demodulating the common carrier and moving the packet into a fixed positive rescaled frequency band, for every fixed derivative depth `r` one can arrange

`|W_X^k G_X^(k)(t)| asymp sqrt(X log X)`

for every `0<=k<=r` throughout a full coherence-width interval. Thus fixed-order derivative-sensitive and Sobolev estimates are matched controls too.

## Synthesis of evidence

The remaining information is not ordinary smoothness, local bandwidth, support density, coefficient size, or finite-order pseudorandomness. It is the **source-specific placement law** of the physical residual relative to the distinguished carrier, or an equivalent nonlocal/growing-complexity statistic that directly controls the signed endpoint memory.

A credible next theorem should therefore target the Fejer scale difference itself, prove an exact-carrier estimate whose absolute error crosses `sqrt(X log X)`, or exploit arithmetic structure that forbids the carrier-adapted packet geometry. Increasing a fixed moment or derivative order does not change the information class.

## Counterevidence / boundary cases

ANF-171 and ANF-173--ANF-179 are matched synthetic controls, not models of the actual prime/rough residual. They show that broad analytic hypotheses are insufficient; they do not show that the physical source realizes the bad packet. The controls are deliberately chosen after seeing the carrier, which is precisely the freedom a source theorem must remove.

The absolute-error boundary is one-way. Beating `sqrt(X log X)` at the first controlled scale rules out the persisted packet but does not by itself prove the complete bow estimate. Likewise ANF-179 treats every **fixed** derivative depth; a predeclared growing-order or genuinely nonlocal twist observable remains outside that no-go.

## Epistemic status

**Supported information boundary:** bow completion is a phase-sensitive absolute-memory problem. Prime-scale matched packets can saturate all fixed local magnitude and derivative hierarchies while retaining target-sized memory. The unresolved gate is arithmetic placement or a genuinely nonlocal/growing-complexity control.

## Novelty/prior-art status

No independent novelty claim is made here. This intuition synthesizes the persisted Mathia reductions and matched controls; the underlying Fejer, Fourier, Sobolev, Gowers, and short-interval ingredients retain their finding-level audits.

## Falsification criterion

Invalidate the exact Fejer scale-difference identity, the ANF-175 prime-scale loading estimates, or the ANF-179 normalized derivative bounds. Strategically, prove a source-faithful theorem that forbids carrier-sector-biased placement or controls the signed scale difference below the critical memory scale; that would close rather than falsify the stated boundary.
