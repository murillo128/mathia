# MI-007 — Bow completion is an absolute, phase-sensitive endpoint-memory problem

**Evidence level:** supported by exact matched-control boundaries

## Core intuition

The bow endpoint obstruction is not controlled by generic local twist regularity. Target-sized completion consumes a **positive real** dyadic shifted correlation at the distinguished logarithmic twist, equivalently a scale-slope defect of a positive Fejer family. Prime-scale matched packets can store the required `sqrt(X log X)` memory while remaining invisible to broad source-blind tests.

The newest evidence makes the complexity threshold explicit. Fixed finite local probe families collapse to one effective scalar on a sufficiently narrow carrier-adapted band, and even a derivative jet whose order grows with `X` remains asymptotically rank one throughout the full range `r_X a_X/K -> 0`, where `a_X=sqrt(delta X log X)`. For the sector-aligned matched-control class this is sharp up to constants: at order `r_X ~ K/a_X`, endpoint geometry itself forces a derivative discrepancy of order `a_X`.

## Strongest justified claim

ANF-157 and ANF-169--ANF-172 identify the exact endpoint currency: completion forces a signed shifted-correlation shell and `D_(2H)-D_H=2 Re S_H` recovers it as an adjacent-scale Fejer-energy defect. ANF-173--ANF-179 then show that prime-scale endpoint memory can coexist with fixed-log cancellation, source-sized sparse atoms, coherence across the natural twist window, every fixed local magnitude norm, and every fixed naturally normalized derivative hierarchy.

ANF-180 removes arbitrary fixed finite local tomography. After exact carrier selection, every fixed finite family of continuous twist multipliers is asymptotically constant on an admissible dangerous packet, so the complete measurement vector is rank one up to `o(a_X)`.

ANF-181 upgrades this to growing derivative depth. Exact log-frequency centering makes every derivative through order `r_X` asymptotically rank one whenever `r_X a_X/K -> 0`; this includes polylogarithmic depth and a large polynomial range. ANF-182 proves that `K/a_X` is the sharp geometric boundary for this matched-control compression architecture: a sufficiently large constant multiple of that order must detect every sector-aligned target-sized packet.

## Synthesis of evidence

The unresolved information is therefore not fixed smoothness, finite tomography, or merely “more derivatives.” A source theorem must either control the signed Fejer scale difference directly, constrain the actual prime/rough placement relative to the distinguished carrier, or access nonlocal/growing-complexity information at least as strong as the critical `K/a_X` resolution scale.

The sharpness theorem is a boundary, not a positive arithmetic result. It says where this synthetic flattening mechanism stops; it does not show that critical-order derivatives are analytically available for the true source or sufficient to close the bow endpoint.

## Counterevidence / boundary cases

ANF-173--ANF-182 are matched controls, not models of the actual prime/rough residual. Their role is to rule out source-blind hypotheses. The sector alignment used in ANF-182 is part of that control class, so the critical derivative threshold should not be promoted to a universal reconstruction theorem.

The absolute-error barrier remains one-way: beating `sqrt(X log X)` at the first controlled scale kills the persisted packet but does not by itself prove the full bow estimate.

## Epistemic status

**Supported sharp information boundary:** dangerous prime-scale packets defeat every fixed local probe family and every derivative hierarchy of subcritical complexity `o(K/a_X)`; `K/a_X` is the matched-control transition scale. The surviving burden is source-specific placement or equally strong nonlocal complexity.

## Novelty/prior-art status

No independent novelty claim is made here. This intuition synthesizes the persisted Mathia reductions and matched-control results; finding-level audits retain the relevant Fourier, Fejer, Sobolev, and approximation-theoretic boundaries.

## Falsification criterion

Invalidate the exact Fejer scale-difference identity, the ANF-180 rank-one probe collapse, the ANF-181 growing-jet estimate, or the ANF-182 sharpness bound. Strategically, prove a source-faithful theorem controlling the signed scale difference below `a_X`, or a source-accessible critical-complexity observable that cannot be matched by the packet construction.
