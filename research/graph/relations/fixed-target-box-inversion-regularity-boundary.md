## Attainable side and exact excluded certificate

[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-474-nonquasianalytic-tests-close-the-iterated-log-window-boundary|PL-474]] and [[research/mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-530-nonquasianalytic-tests-reach-logarithmic-mobius-windows|MC-530]] independently verify the stronger derivative envelope `C A^n n! log(e+n)^(q n)`, with `1<q<r`, through zero isolation, logarithmic composition and the growing finite inverse. The [[research/prior_art/incremental/PA-nonquasianalytic-infinite-convolution-cutoff|cutoff construction]] is classical. The resulting inverse error is bounded by `C exp(-c/[h log(e+1/h)^q])`, with common compact support, zero mean and bounded combined `L^2` norm.

Given a hypothetical selected zero with real part `beta>1/2` (of multiplicity `m` for reciprocal zeta), for every fixed `r>1` this gives the full empirically centered variance floors on every sufficiently large real block, uniformly for integer `1<=H<=X/[log X (loglog X)^r]`. PL-474's floor is `c H^2 X^(2 beta-1)`; MC-530 retains the additional factor `(log X)^(2m-2)`. Their coefficient-side errors cost respectively `O(X log X)` and `O(X)` times the uniform inverse error. Neither arithmetic explicit formula is applied to the moving inverse.

[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-473-box-fourier-zeros-obstruct-logarithmic-window-test-inversion|PL-473]], Sections 1–2, uses the [[research/prior_art/incremental/PA-paley-wiener-ingham-logarithmic-integral|classical Fourier logarithmic integral]] at both signs of the box zeros. PL-474 and MC-530 construct the previously undecided `r>1` side; convergence of the logarithmic integral alone was not a construction.

## Surviving limits

This closes one fixed-target norm-certificate family, not the variance problem. A variance ceiling with the source's stated strength would imply RH or joint mod-5 GRH even on an unbounded selected scale set, but no such ceiling is acquired. In contrast, the inverse obstruction requires all large scales and does not prohibit specially selected widths. Moving targets, multiple kernels and signed arithmetic sampling-error estimates remain outside it.

Constants and tests encode the full hypothetical zero set. Growing derivative order is not a fixed finite measurement bank, and bounded combined norm is not effective acquisition. No uniform onset near `beta=1/2` or `r=1`, external novelty, independent adversarial approval, formal verification, or unconditional zero-free gain is asserted. PL-474 and MC-530 settle complementary arithmetic instances of the same inspected regularity question; graph links and classical-method registration are maintenance.

## Two kernels change the operator

[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-475-two-box-lengths-force-paired-macroscopic-prime-variance|PL-475]], Sections 1–4, uses the classical [[research/prior_art/incremental/PA-multichannel-compact-distribution-deconvolution|compact-distribution multichannel identity]] for boxes of lengths 1 and sqrt(2). Its explicit construction has finite distributional order. Dilation and convolution with PL-471's fixed smooth mean-zero target give uniformly bounded smooth tests on a common compact subset of (1,2). Rounding K=floor(sqrt(2)H) contributes O(1/X) in the target sup norm and O(log X) to the prime pairing.

For every sufficiently large real X and integer 1<=H<=X/20, an off-critical zero with real part beta forces V₁(X,H)/H² + V₁(X,K)/K² >= c X^(2 beta−1). The two variances have separate empirical means. Both upper bounds are needed for the stated joint mod-5 GRH criterion; one small variance does not suffice, and no independence is used. This changes the measurement operator and leaves PL-473's single-box obstruction intact. Rational ratios retain common zeros; Liouville ratios can require reciprocal samples beyond every polynomial, preventing a compact finite-order inverse. The rounded pair itself is not inverted exactly.

## Bounded jumps recover omitted starts

[[research/mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-531-bounded-jumps-recover-trimmed-mobius-variance|MC-531]], Sections 1–3, uses MC-530's complete linear pairing with its uniformly bounded mean-zero test. For integer starts, any deleted set E of size B<X/2 and any one constant center c, the exact jump bound |ΔS_H|<=2 gives sum |S_H−c| <= 2 sqrt(X V_E(c)) + 4 B². Consequently sqrt(X V_E(c)) + B² >= C H X^beta (log X)^(m−1) in MC-530's single-box range. The reciprocal pole's full multiplicity m remains essential.

At H=floor(X/[log X (loglog X)^r]), fixed r>1, simultaneous bounds B_X <<_ε X^(3/4+ε) and V_E(c) <<_ε X^(1+ε)H for every ε>0 on one unbounded integer scale set imply RH. MC-520 supplies the converse with empty masks. The scale set, masks and constant centers are fixed as families before the epsilon quantifier. These upper estimates remain unproved. The B² term is sharp for generic bounded-jump absolute-mass recovery, not proved optimal for Möbius data or signed test-specific recovery. Qualitative density one, fractional-cell deletions and start-dependent centers are outside this statement.
