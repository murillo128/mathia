# Surviving structure must satisfy a quantitative non-escape condition

[[research/mind/intuition/MI-007-survival-requires-quantitative-non-escape-not-just-nonzero-structure|Global MI-007]] records a cross-line synthesis that is now supported by several independent source-backed mechanisms: a finite-scale invariant can remain exactly nonzero while becoming useless in the completion, limit topology, assembled operator, or admissible reference class.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the compactness side precise. [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-071-normalized-excess-fidelity-characterizes-finite-dimensionality|AF-071]]--[[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-077-canonical-compact-disk-is-minimal-functorial-witness-envelope|AF-077]] replace isolated finite witnesses by coherent compact witness geometry, precompact witness pools, Kolmogorov-width decay and a canonical compact envelope. Exact finite recoverability therefore does not automatically survive a cross-scale limit without a compactness or margin theorem.

Weil Inertia supplies the coercivity analogue. [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-104-simultaneous-full-packed-prime-defects-have-trivial-shared-source-intersection|WI-104]] shows that simultaneous full-packed defects can have trivial common kernel, yet [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-105-full-packed-defect-kernels-can-be-asymptotically-parallel|WI-105]]--[[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-110-arbitrary-linear-rescue-obeys-a-sharp-conditioning-transmission-product-law|WI-110]] show that the lower singular scale can still collapse and that bounded pre/post-processing cannot restore both conditioning and transmission. [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-111-bombieri-vinogradov-forces-polynomial-dimensional-full-packed-near-nullity|WI-111]]--[[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-114-maynard-fixed-residue-pushes-near-nullity-to-11-over-21|WI-114]] make the near-null sector polynomial-dimensional using established prime-distribution input.

Prime Circle separates average survival from uniform survival. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-139-primorial-hessian-bulk-collapse-hides-mesoscopic-macroscopic-defect-modes|PC-139]] and [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-142-gap-two-matching-has-an-exact-macroscopic-spectral-cliff|PC-142]] isolate a macroscopic mesoscopic top band. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-143-gap-two-top-band-locks-on-average-but-not-in-operator-norm|PC-143]]--[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-145-bounded-chord-windows-recover-mertens-scale-top-band-projector|PC-145]] then show density/Frobenius locking and finite-local recovery at the Mertens scale while a sparse worst-angle defect remains at operator-norm scale. The topology in which a signal survives is therefore part of the claim.

Prime Flute supplies the reference-stability version. [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-162-connected-ruelle-cusp-coefficient-is-shift-reference-dependent|PF-162]]--[[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-165-interval-filling-compact-reference-residuals-kill-analytic-scalar-normalization|PF-165]] show that the selected scalar cusp residual changes through natural reference choices, and [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-166-all-tail-marked-lengths-are-asymptotically-composite-blind|PF-166]] proves uniform asymptotic blindness of all tail marked lengths under the all-composite shift clone. A surviving value theorem must therefore be genuinely global/nonlocal and reference-stable rather than merely nonzero locally.

Visual Exploration provides matched falsification controls for the same principle. [[research/visual_exploration/findings/VIS-004-dyadic-midpoint-centering-keeps-sigma-one-threshold|VIS-004]] shows that a natural centering changes local geometry without improving the critical summability threshold, [[research/visual_exploration/findings/VIS-005-gap-two-local-leakage-obstructs-uniform-eigenspace-locking|VIS-005]] exposes the sparse worst-angle defect directly, and [[research/visual_exploration/findings/VIS-006-modulus-functional-equation-defect-forced-critical-line|VIS-006]] proves that a visually striking critical-line trench can be forced by symmetry after phase information is discarded.

**Derived curation statement:** once a candidate mechanism reaches a finite-scale nonzero invariant, the next load-bearing question is whether that invariant has a quantitative lower bound, compactness/coercivity theorem, reference stability, or other non-escape certificate in the exact category used by the final RH implication. This is a research-graph synthesis, not a theorem transferring estimates between the cited lines and not evidence for RH by itself.
