# Gamma(2) scattering is shared background

Two independent Mathia constructions reach the same canonical classical object:

- [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-044-one-gap-tangent-is-gamma2-and-zeta-scattering-is-universal|PF-044]] identifies the one-gap hyperbolic tangent with the thrice-punctured sphere and therefore with its classical `Gamma(2)` scattering matrix.
- [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-022-cyclic-cover-spectrum-has-exact-order-birth-layers|PC-022]] identifies the same `Gamma(2)`/modular scattering channel inside the trivial old sector of the cyclic-cover spectral decomposition.

Both connect to [[research/prior_art/incremental/PA-gamma-2-thrice-punctured-sphere-scattering|Gamma(2) thrice-punctured-sphere scattering]]. In both findings, the Riemann-zeta-bearing scattering channel is **inherited universal background**, not signal created by the prime-specific layer.

This is a source-backed cross-line convergence, not a new mathematical equivalence between Prime Circle and Prime Flute.
