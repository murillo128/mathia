# Classify the universal boundary model before calling an escape arithmetic

Central intuition: [[research/mind/intuition/MI-006-classify-the-boundary-model-before-calling-it-arithmetic]]. Current source-backed lines repeatedly show that the boundary of a failed category can carry a canonical model of its own; observing noncompactness, a singular limit, a stronger norm or strong local cancellation is therefore not yet source specificity.

- Prime Circle: [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-109-prime-conductor-microlocal-remainder-is-universal-carleman-hilbert-defect|PC-109]]--[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-119-bounded-two-sided-hardy-preconditioning-cannot-retain-a-finite-fredholm-divisor|PC-119]] take the canonical conductor escape from Hilbert/Carleman/Ramanujan boundary models through the arbitrary-conductor limit: the scalar-normalized corrector has a zero-free Gaussian determinant, similarities preserve its divisor exactly, and uniformly bounded operator-changing preconditioners still send every finite Fredholm zero to infinity.
- Prime Flute: [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-147-square-resolvent-S1-forces-first-resolvent-S2|PF-147]]--[[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-158-full-canonical-separator-selberg-cocycle-has-sharp-one-quarter-abscissa|PF-158]] classify both the resolvent/heat boundary and a matched relative-orbit boundary. Relative short-orbit packets can be summed after subtraction, but their canonical Selberg cocycles are zero-free on half-planes containing the critical line.
- Prime Lattice: [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-096-growing-depth-all-horizon-s2-universality|PL-096]]--[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-105-ihara-quotient-ramanujan-universality|PL-105]] identify raw growing-depth and near-Nyquist behavior with PNT/prolate/Poisson sampling controls, while the Bruhat--Tits and Ihara half-axis is classical local/graph temperedness reproduced by abstract regular graphs.
- Weil Inertia: [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-079-signed-scalar-large-sieve-needs-ramanujan-operator-cancellation|WI-079]]--[[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-087-close-prime-ramanujan-cross-grams-have-an-exact-loewner-bezout-rank-family|WI-087]] show that the signed scalar escape is an exact finite-window Ramanujan Toeplitz quotient: below the window the blocks form a basis, super-window moduli alias into an exact finite quotient, and residual pairwise defects can themselves be macroscopically large classical Loewner--cyclotomic phenomena.
- Weil Positivity: [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-093-finite-band-cover-covariance-forces-a-weighted-dirichlet-cone-with-continuous-dual-hahn-critical-ray|WP-093]]--[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-097-mixed-prime-product-completion-gives-sharp-finite-diagonal-threshold|WP-097]] enlarge the exact cover-positive boundary from finite-band Dirichlet/Cesàro geometry to the full multiplicative prime-torus Gram cone. Mixed-prime terms can restore positivity with finite diagonal mass, but that positive completion still lacks the global archimedean/sign theorem and loses positivity if the mixed interactions are simply projected away.
- Möbius Cancellation: [[research/mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-001-local-to-global-exceptional-mass-barrier|MC-001]] and [[research/mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-002-prime-harmonic-ceiling-single-scale-pretentiousness|MC-002]] identify two elementary information budgets at the cancellation boundary. The black-box local-to-global transfer is controlled by an `eta X+B+H`-type budget, while one-scale pretentiousness has only `O(log log x)` prime-harmonic mass; neither by itself reaches the polynomial global scale demanded by RH-level Mertens cancellation.

The source-backed cross-line rule is conditional rather than universal: before interpreting a boundary residual as arithmetic, identify and match the canonical boundary model in the same category. A positive mechanism must exhibit a residual that changes after that model is removed or matched; a negative result may show that the entire proposed escape factors through the universal model. This relation does not assert that the listed boundary models are mathematically equivalent to one another.
