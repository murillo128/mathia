# Classify the universal boundary model before calling an escape arithmetic

Central intuition: [[research/mind/intuition/MI-006-classify-the-boundary-model-before-calling-it-arithmetic]]. Current source-backed lines repeatedly show that the boundary of a failed category can carry a canonical model of its own; observing noncompactness, a singular limit or a stronger norm is therefore not yet source specificity.

- Prime Circle: [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-109-prime-conductor-microlocal-remainder-is-universal-carleman-hilbert-defect|PC-109]]--[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-114-new-prime-hardy-two-scale-corrector-is-ramanujan-reflection-tensor|PC-114]] identify canonical conductor and two-scale escapes with Hilbert/Carleman defects, trace-class dilation data and a Ramanujan reflection tensor rather than new spectral locations.
- Prime Flute: [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-147-square-resolvent-S1-forces-first-resolvent-S2|PF-147]]--[[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-155-local-heat-invariant-tower-is-area-only|PF-155]] classify the `S_1`/`S_2` resolvent boundary and show that local residue/heat regularization is universal area data; only a globally assembled nonlocal value remains potentially discriminating.
- Prime Lattice: [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-096-growing-depth-all-horizon-s2-universality|PL-096]]--[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-099-subnyquist-midpoint-s1-control|PL-099]] identify the growing-depth `S_2` and `S_1` boundary with PNT/log-uniform/prolate sampling and a sharp Nyquist rank phase reproduced by a deterministic control.
- Weil Inertia: [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-079-signed-scalar-large-sieve-needs-ramanujan-operator-cancellation|WI-079]]--[[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-081-pairwise-lcm-boundary-rank-controls-finite-window-ramanujan-leakage|WI-081]] show that the surviving signed scalar escape is an exact finite-window Ramanujan operator whose cross-modulus coupling is localized to an LCM boundary-rank effect.
- Weil Positivity: [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-089-cover-coherent-finite-global-couplings-freeze-to-one-collective-block|WP-089]], [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-092-refinement-coherent-fixed-infinite-auxiliary-sectors-still-freeze-to-one-collective-block|WP-092]] and [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-091-schiffer-finite-certificate-is-generic-compact-positivity-not-pole-structure|WP-091]] classify fixed coherent global coupling and finite positivity certificates as repetition/generic-positivity models rather than new pole/sign mechanisms.

The source-backed cross-line rule is conditional rather than universal: before interpreting a boundary residual as arithmetic, identify and match the canonical boundary model in the same category. A positive mechanism must exhibit a residual that changes after that model is removed or matched; a negative result may show that the entire proposed escape factors through the universal model. This relation does not assert that the listed boundary models are mathematically equivalent to one another.
