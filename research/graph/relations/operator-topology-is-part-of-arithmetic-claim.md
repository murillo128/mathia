# Operator topology is part of the arithmetic claim

Central intuition: [[research/mind/intuition/MI-005-topology-is-part-of-the-arithmetic-claim]]. Current source evidence shows that both excessively weak and excessively strong categories can erase, universalize, or make discontinuous the arithmetic signal.

- Prime Lattice: [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-066-natural-weil-band-right-edge-zero-certificate|PL-066]]--[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-071-finite-horizon-prime-zero-resonance-universality|PL-071]] make one second-scale topology an explicit-zero/RH-equivalent diagnostic rather than a generative selector. [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-078-sharp-critical-gram-prolate-universality|PL-078]]--[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-081-prime-support-gram-scale-split|PL-081]] show that local and macroscopic sharp-Gram bulk/determinant behavior is classical sampling geometry, while prime support can move only the extreme/mean-gap scales.
- Prime Flute: [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-112-first-relative-resolvent-is-not-trace-class|PF-112]] makes first-resolvent trace class too strong, whereas [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-125-shift-clone-has-compact-relative-resolvent|PF-125]] makes compact-resolvent class too coarse. [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-138-zero-twist-reflection-exhausts-margulis-short-closed-geodesics|PF-138]]--[[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-142-zero-twist-reflection-removes-short-collar-phase-gauge|PF-142]] further move the live wave/scattering gate to one globally coherent thin-collar/body comparison rather than local collapse or constant phase.
- Prime Circle: the natural solenoid scalar calculi fail to provide compact-resolvent arithmetic scale, while finite Hardy essential structure is universal and arithmetic survives only in relative/trace-class or still-unclassified higher coupling data.
- Weil Inertia: [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-059-fixed-polylog-conductor-truncation-misses-w-local-energy|WI-059]], [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-060-retained-w-spectrum-wiener-norm-forces-square-function-control|WI-060]], [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-063-lossless-spectral-sparsification-forces-superpolylog-diagonal-cost|WI-063]] and [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-064-mikawa-errors-form-exact-divisor-martingale-but-one-refinement-still-costs-superpolylog|WI-064]] separate a manageable collective `L^2` conditioning spectrum from prohibitively expensive diagonal/Wiener or one-common-refinement assembly.
- Weil Positivity: [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-069-positive-hardy-extensions-cannot-carry-unbounded-mangoldt-anchor-at-finite-energy|WP-069]]--[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-071-rotation-invariant-hilbert-completions-cannot-bound-mangoldt-boundary-anchor|WP-071]] close broad regular positive completions, while [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-072-base-point-local-dirichlet-energy-regularizes-mangoldt-anchor-but-is-not-global-weil-form|WP-072]] exhibits a pointed positive topology where the anchor becomes continuous. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-073-pointed-dirichlet-root-cover-isometry-forces-critical-half-weight|WP-073]]--[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-075-positive-shifted-resolvent-cover-defects-mix-log-degree-with-digamma-but-exact-finite-weights-force-zero-shift|WP-075]] then show that this topology forces the finite half-weight/log-degree data but still hits a finite-versus-archimedean compatibility obstruction.

No topology is privileged a priori. An RH-facing claim must use a category forced by the construction, make the relevant selector continuous there, and distinguish the strongest matched controls without merely repackaging classical correlation or explicit-zero data.
