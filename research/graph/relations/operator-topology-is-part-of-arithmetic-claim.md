# Operator topology is part of the arithmetic claim

Central intuition: [[research/mind/intuition/MI-005-topology-is-part-of-the-arithmetic-claim]].

The current source tree contains a cross-line topology boundary that is explicitly synthesized by the global `mind/` state:

- [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-051-weil-boundary-rank-one-pnt-model|PL-051]] through [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-055-fixed-sobolev-weil-boundary-smoothing|PL-055]] separate strong, norm, Calkin, and fixed compact-smoothing limits of the localized Weil boundary: weak/strong topologies universalize, norm/Calkin retain prime-log recurrence, and fixed compact Sobolev smoothing erases that recurrence again.
- [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-112-first-relative-resolvent-is-not-trace-class|PF-112]] closes first-resolvent trace class for the nonisometric metric comparison on local pseudodifferential grounds, while [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-118-shift-clone-pants-are-arc-lipschitz-close|PF-118]] and [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-121-ideal-lambert-shift-comparison-is-asymptotically-bilipschitz|PF-121]] leave weaker compact/`S_p`, heat, wave, or relative-scattering categories open after increasingly strong local geometric comparison.
- [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-068-regular-commuting-leaf-fiber-calculus-cannot-be-compact|PC-068]] through [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-070-additive-solenoid-dilation-covariance-also-forbids-compact-resolvent|PC-070]] show that regular scalar leaf/fiber and exact affine-dilation compact-resolvent formulations are incompatible with the natural solenoid carrier.
- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-040-canonical-solenoid-sobolev-trace-is-only-l2-and-cannot-host-weil-haar-tangent|WP-040]] and [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-041-compatible-profinite-cycle-energy-is-bounded-and-leaf-time-derived|WP-041]] show that canonical regular Sobolev/transverse positive scales can be too weak to host the singular arithmetic tangent even when they are geometrically natural.

The supported program-level lesson is not that one topology is privileged a priori. It is that an RH-facing operator claim must specify a topology/category forced by the construction **before** interpreting the surviving singularity as arithmetic. A useful next mechanism must retain a stable residual after the universal background is removed without merely choosing a weak topology that erases the arithmetic or a strong topology that turns it into an essential non-Fredholm artifact.
