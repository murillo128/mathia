# Operator topology is part of the arithmetic claim

Central intuition: [[research/mind/intuition/MI-005-topology-is-part-of-the-arithmetic-claim]]. Current source evidence shows that both excessively weak and excessively strong categories can erase or universalize the arithmetic signal.

- Prime Lattice: [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-051-weil-boundary-rank-one-pnt-model|PL-051]]--[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-065-zero-free-prime-log-recurrence-delay|PL-065]] separate strong, norm/Calkin, smoothing and moving-band regimes. [[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-066-natural-weil-band-right-edge-zero-certificate|PL-066]]--[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-071-finite-horizon-prime-zero-resonance-universality|PL-071]] show that the natural fixed-smooth second scale becomes an explicit-zero almost-periodic/RH-equivalent diagnostic, while its hull, same-time prime joining and finite-horizon resonances reduce to generic frequency-module/Diophantine structure.
- Prime Flute: [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-112-first-relative-resolvent-is-not-trace-class|PF-112]] makes first-resolvent trace class too strong locally, while [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-125-shift-clone-has-compact-relative-resolvent|PF-125]] makes compact-resolvent class too coarse to distinguish the all-composite clone. [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-131-lambert-split-ray-trace-mismatch-is-summable|PF-131]]--[[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-134-lambert-scalar-tail-is-log-weight-summable|PF-134]] further remove the full one-dimensional split-ray mismatch as the obvious missing middle obstruction.
- Prime Circle: [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-068-regular-commuting-leaf-fiber-calculus-cannot-be-compact|PC-068]]--[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-070-additive-solenoid-dilation-covariance-also-forbids-compact-resolvent|PC-070]] exclude regular scalar compact-resolvent formulations on the natural solenoid carrier, while its finite Hardy Calkin algebra is universal and the arithmetic survives only in relative trace-class data.
- Weil Positivity: [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-058-prym-hodge-determinant-norm-exports-log-p-but-polarization-spectrum-is-flat|WP-058]]--[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-060-prym-untwisted-analytic-torsion-completion-erases-determinant-line-prime-scale|WP-060]] show an integral prime scale can survive in a determinant-line norm yet disappear under a canonical full-complex completion; [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-061-q2-hardy-gram-is-positive-but-full-root-channel-is-indefinite|WP-061]]--[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-063-q2-hardy-krein-repair-is-uniquely-the-polar-absolute-value|WP-063]] show that changing the positive/Krein category can also replace an independent sign theorem by a tautological absolute-value repair.

No topology is privileged a priori. An RH-facing operator claim must specify a category forced by the construction before interpreting the surviving singularity as arithmetic, and it must distinguish the strongest matched controls without merely repackaging the explicit zero divisor.
