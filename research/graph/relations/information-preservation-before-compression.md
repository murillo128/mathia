# Information preservation before compression

Central intuition: [[research/mind/intuition/MI-001-preserve-structure-before-compressing]]. The current global synthesis now has an exact transversal vocabulary from Arithmetic Fidelity for several older branch-specific losses.

Arithmetic Fidelity evidence:
- [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-003-invariant-observable-quotients-impose-orbit-closure-fidelity-barrier|AF-003]] identifies the maximal quotient carried by an admissible invariant-observable algebra.
- [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-012-strictly-convex-f-divergence-equality-is-binary-statistical-fidelity|AF-012]] and [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-013-finite-experiment-fidelity-is-vector-likelihood-ratio-sufficiency|AF-013]] give exact stochastic sufficiency analogues through scalar/vector likelihood ratios.
- [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-015-abstract-multiplication-forgets-rational-prime-norms|AF-015]] separates prime type from rational-prime norm information, while [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-017-exact-euler-product-recovers-prime-norms-zero-divisor-does-not|AF-017]] shows the exact Euler-product function can retain that norm multiset even when the meromorphic divisor does not.

Prime Circle supplies concrete compression boundaries: [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-019-unanchored-birth-shell-geometry-cannot-distinguish-n-from-2n|PC-019]] loses information under unanchored quotienting, while [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-081-finite-hardy-shell-algebra-is-a-wedge-of-hilbert-bands|PC-081]] and [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-084-all-finite-mixed-hardy-shell-words-converge-in-trace-norm|PC-084]] separate a universal finite essential core from a well-defined trace-class relative sector; [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-085-common-repeated-prime-depth-is-universal-mixed-hardy-tensor-inflation|PC-085]] then removes common repeated-prime depth as a new finite mixed-shell variable.

Prime Flute gives the complementary inverse example: [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-048-small-spectrum-has-a-gap-determinant-but-is-not-inverse-unique|PF-048]] loses ordered gap data under a scalar determinant, whereas [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-049-canonical-endpoint-spectral-measure-recovers-the-relative-gap-path|PF-049]] retains it in a marked spectral measure.

The supported cross-line statement is therefore category-specific: before optimizing or spectralizing, identify the complete admissible statistic/quotient actually retained and test whether the intended discriminator factors through it. A later computation cannot be credited with information already lost upstream.
