# Information preservation before compression

Central intuition: [[research/mind/intuition/MI-001-preserve-structure-before-compressing]]. Arithmetic Fidelity supplies exact language for distinguishing information that is absent from a retained statistic from information that is merely hard to extract.

[[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-003-invariant-observable-quotients-impose-orbit-closure-fidelity-barrier|AF-003]], [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-012-strictly-convex-f-divergence-equality-is-binary-statistical-fidelity|AF-012]] and [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-013-finite-experiment-fidelity-is-vector-likelihood-ratio-sufficiency|AF-013]] identify deterministic/stochastic maximal-statistic gates. [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-020-full-weil-test-family-is-norm-faithful-fixed-support-is-finite-scale|AF-020]]--[[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-023-regular-weil-test-points-have-positive-dimensional-beurling-fibers|AF-023]] then separate Weil support horizon from finite-test resolution.

The latest finite-test audit adds a necessary adversarial control. [[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-024-unconstrained-weil-tests-program-local-separable-geometry|AF-024]]--[[research/arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-027-double-positive-compact-tests-retain-full-dimensional-finite-weil-jet-freedom|AF-027]] show that local isolation can be programmed around a chosen source and that finite linear constraints, Fourier positivity, and simultaneous pointwise/Fourier positivity do not by themselves make finite Weil jets rigid. A finite statistic is therefore not validated merely because a distinguished source is isolated; the admissible class must be fixed independently of that source.

The branch-specific examples remain consistent with this gate. Prime Circle distinguishes universal finite essential data from relative trace data, while Prime Flute loses ordered neck information in scalar determinants and recovers it in marked spectral measures. The supported cross-line statement is category-specific: identify the complete admissible statistic/quotient first, then ask whether the claimed arithmetic discriminator factors through it and survives matched controls. Downstream optimization, diagonalization or spectralization cannot be credited with information that the retained statistic never contained.
