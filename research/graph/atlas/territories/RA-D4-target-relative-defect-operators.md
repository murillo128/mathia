The localized completed-Weil boundary is now sharply topology- and scale-dependent. Completion cancels the universal PNT rank-one mode strongly, but an essential prime-log recurrence defect survives. Fixed smoothing, growing depth, canonical recentering, broad moving low-frequency bands, and moving Sobolev orders above the zero-free sampling threshold all collapse back to the universal/zero limit. Coherent recurrence is forced to frequencies at least of order `exp(c L^(3/2)/(log L)^2)` by the current zero-free estimate.

The territory remains open only beyond these proved regimes: ultra-high-frequency or non-band relative topologies, borderline moving smoothing, or a different full-Weil form-domain scaling that is intrinsically selected rather than tuned to the zeros.
