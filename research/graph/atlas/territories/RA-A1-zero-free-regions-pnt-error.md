# A1 — Zero-free regions / PNT and Möbius-summatory error

This coarse v1 leaf covers the classical analytic route in which RH-strength information is sought through zero-free control or through primary summatory error terms whose scale is governed by the zeta divisor, including prime-counting and Möbius/Mertens cancellation.

- [[research/mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-001-local-to-global-exceptional-mass-barrier|MC-001]]
- [[research/mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-002-prime-harmonic-ceiling-single-scale-pretentiousness|MC-002]]

The new Möbius Cancellation line directly targets the RH-scale Mertens bound rather than using a zero-free estimate only as a downstream tool. MC-001 proves that current almost-all short-interval magnitude plus exceptional-set size cannot be black-box aggregated to polynomial global cancellation; MC-002 proves that the standard single-scale pretentious distance has only `O(log log x)` prime-harmonic mass and therefore cannot by itself drive a generic Halász exponential to polynomial saving.

The territory is `active`, not `open`: these are exact obstruction audits that identify missing quantitative information, but no surviving positive mechanism has yet been established by canonical findings. The earlier Prime-Lattice use of PNT and Vinogradov--Korobov estimates remains auxiliary and does not strengthen this disposition.
