The category-changing audit now reaches genuine higher-genus cohomology. Ordinary `P^1` divisor/correspondence routes are principal or class-trivial; a granted cyclotomic double cover has nontrivial positive Hodge theory but normalized transfer is degree-flat. Its Prym polarization does retain `log p` in an integral discriminant, yet that carrier is torsion and cannot itself support a nonzero real quadratic/height pairing.

The territory remains active because WP-057 explicitly leaves a narrower mechanism: make the integral/torsion carrier act on a larger non-torsion space before positivity, or supply new non-cover correspondence data with an independent sign theorem.
