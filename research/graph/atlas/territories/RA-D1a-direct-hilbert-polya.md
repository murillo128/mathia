# D1a — Direct Hilbert–Pólya

Construction of a canonical self-adjoint operator whose spectrum directly realizes the Riemann zero ordinates and proves their line placement.

## Boundary

Mathia has tested many operator wrappers and known spectral programs, but no current line owns this direct construction as a viable primary route. PL-048 reinforces that distinction: Hedenmalm's completed theta/Mellin spectral pencil realizes zeros as generalized eigenparameters, while the decisive positive Hilbert metric/self-adjointness remains missing.
