Regular positive Hardy extensions, the canonical antipodal correction, rotation-invariant Hilbert completions and polar metric repairs are strongly constrained or closed. But WP-072--WP-074 produce a real positive survivor: pointed local Dirichlet/root-cover geometry makes the Mangoldt anchor continuous, forces the critical half-weight, and produces positive `log n` scale defects whose primitive prime-ray coefficients equal `(log p)p^{-k/2}`.

The remaining obstruction is now a compatibility/sign problem rather than finite-weight recovery. The positive Poisson lift is indefinite at the Weil subtraction, and WP-075 shows that the direct shifted-resolvent route cannot expose the digamma profile while preserving the exact finite coefficients. The territory remains `open` for a genuinely coupled finite--archimedean/polar construction with an independent sign theorem; no atlas mass or state change is justified.
