The full exact-cover-positive cone remains genuinely larger than the sparse selector: mixed-prime Fourier coefficients restore positivity with finite sharp diagonal mass. Those terms cannot be deleted by a positive unital quotient preserving the prime coordinates, nor by arbitrary passive positive auxiliary elimination, without returning to the divergent sparse obstruction.

The natural independent product completion is singular to product Haar at the critical exponent, but that is not a universal measure-class obstruction. WP-101 constructs correlated positive completions that retain the sharp mass and exact one-prime Weil moments while becoming Haar-equivalent. This is a real positive survivor, albeit necessarily rough below the classical Zygmund endpoint. WP-102 supplies a correlation-robust boundary: every exact critical completion has infinite cylindrical Fisher/square-root Dirichlet energy.

WP-103 shows that mixed-prime sparsification itself is not the missing theorem. Exponentiating the exact one-prime score produces a positive Gibbs carrier whose log density has exactly the Weil prime-power rays and no mixed-prime modes. But carrier positivity is tautological for arbitrary signed bounded potentials, and both relative entropy and the canonical multiplicative-flow energy diverge at the critical all-prime exponent. Thus this nonlinear selector succeeds algebraically while failing as an independent finite-energy sign mechanism.

The territory remains `open` with a narrower survivor. A correlated nonseparable positive completion exists, and nonlinear logarithmic readout can recover sparse finite-place data; what is still missing is a genuinely transforming finite--archimedean/polar operation that ties that data to a nontrivial independent positive form, remains meaningful at the critical global scale, retains the exact finite coefficients, and proves the sign rather than importing it.
