# C2 — Ordinary positive-calculus closures

The newest Schiffer audit strengthens rather than changes this closure. WP-091 reconstructs the finite positivity certificate without pole data and places its existence in the general classical fact that strict one-dimensional compact positivity with finite-order contact is finitely certifiable after factoring the contact. The low-order certificate is therefore not a new finite shadow of the global meromorphic pole structure.

The broader Weil-positivity program remains open elsewhere because these findings explicitly leave singular/domain-changing, higher-cohomological, nonperiodic, refinement-dependent and genuinely nonseparable finite--archimedean mechanisms outside this ordinary/regular positive-calculus territory.
