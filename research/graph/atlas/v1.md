# Riemann Atlas v1 — model and calibration

## Denominator

Atlas v1 partitions the identifiable RH approach space into **10 macro-families and 37 leaf territories**. The leaf masses sum to 1.0. Raw paper, finding, prior-art, or graph-node counts never contribute directly to the denominator.

When a territory is subdivided within one atlas version, child mass must sum to the parent mass. A material macro-taxonomy or denominator change creates a new atlas version; coverage is allowed to decrease when the map expands.
