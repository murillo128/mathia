# Riemann Atlas v1 — model and calibration

## Denominator

Atlas v1 partitions the identifiable RH approach space into **10 macro-families and 37 leaf territories**. The leaf masses sum to 1.0. Raw paper, finding, prior-art, or graph-node counts never contribute directly to the denominator.

When a territory is subdivided within one atlas version, child mass must sum to the parent mass. A material macro-taxonomy or denominator change creates a new atlas version; coverage is allowed to decrease when the map expands.

## Calibration evidence

The taxonomy is calibrated against the frozen issue-63 prior-art projection and its pinned corpora, including the qwen-lean RH atlas (234 entries), the Riemann–Mathia corpus, and the OpenAlex identity/citation graph. The frozen projection reports 423 relevant bibliographic records inspected in its main bootstrap, 11,753 accepted OpenAlex discovery candidates, and 45 emitted canonical prior-art notes. Current findings and local/global `mind/` are then used only to decide whether Mathia has traversed a territory and what disposition the surviving evidence supports.
