Analytic Frontier moves beyond the fixed-support escape. `ANF-078`--`ANF-081` close progressively larger real-multiplicity families, `ANF-082`--`ANF-086` open and stabilize a genuinely complex Hilbert tube, and `ANF-087` sharpens Montgomery--Taylor equality to an explicit two-site real zero-set packing. The surviving question is no longer whether real multiplicity alone defeats the sharp floor, but how a source-specific complex/separable certificate assembles with quantitative destination conditioning.

Arithmetic Fidelity reaches a positive task-relative endpoint. `AF-164`--`AF-170` expose decoding-capacity, quotient and multiplicity limits for divisor recovery, while `AF-171`--`AF-174` identify the right pseudohyperbolic/output-gauge geometry and prove sharp fixed-degree recovery of the full finite-Blaschke divisor. Exact sufficiency therefore needs to be paired with a quantitative stability scale rather than treated as stable transport automatically.

Möbius Cancellation now has both a stronger radial-filter boundary and a sharper moment-transfer boundary. `MC-109`--`MC-114` show that Hamming-shell regularization retains an unavoidable critical/supercritical floor even for growing radial filters. `MC-115`--`MC-119` then isolate mean-absolute Mertens information and show that sub-`L^1` fractional-moment control, even uniformly across polynomial windows and with matched multiplicative support, does not generically recover the needed first-absolute-moment scale. The deleted review sidecar for surviving `MC-107` means that earlier adversarial challenge is no longer a quarantine condition.

Prime Circle obtains a sharp Fourier classifier on reciprocal cyclotomic shells in `PC-191`--`PC-196`, but the subsequent global character constructions classicalize. `PC-197` is exactly the ordinary Dirichlet-`L` packet, `PC-198` rules out new mixed-root poles from finite holomorphic products, and `PC-199` shows that the natural sesquilinear two-shell fusion collapses to the composite-character packet. Mixed conductor creation alone is therefore insufficient; a survivor must retain genuinely nonseparable cross-shell information before character multiplication and radial-semigroup collapse.

Prime Flute reaches weak-trace-class control for the complete short-collar central sector in `PF-189`, then identifies the remaining endpoint obstruction. `PF-190`--`PF-194` separate exact area transport, determinant-one lifting and rigidity from the strong-`L^1` failure, while `PF-195` shows that generic critical one-sided weak-`S_2` localization cannot be controlled by endpoint mass alone. The next step requires a Prime-Flute-specific mass-sensitive nonconcentration/localization theorem or a direct symmetrized weak-`S_1` argument.

Prime Lattice closes the fixed-complexity covariance escape. `PL-190`--`PL-192` distinguish shrinking and escaping affine windows from genuine global recovery; `PL-193` and `PL-194` collapse scalar prime-shift covariance for self-adjoint resolvents; `PL-195` shows that a fixed finite-dimensional unitary prime cocycle collapses as well. Any operator-theoretic continuation must therefore change more than scalar weights or finite internal matrix multiplicity.

Visual Exploration makes the prime-phase controls more exact while narrowing what they can prove. `VIS-065`--`VIS-071` identify deterministic resonance geometry and the full shared-phase Haar law behind random-prime controls. `VIS-072`--`VIS-074` then give exact sinc-window averaging corridors and show that coordinate-separable additive log-Euler observables have no cross-prime small-divisor barrier. A useful visual discriminator must therefore involve mixed-coordinate/nonlinear structure rather than mistaking additive one-prime averaging difficulty for arithmetic coupling.

Weil Inertia closes the most direct drifting-bow localization escape. `WI-185`--`WI-189` expose a square-root resolution/reservoir tradeoff and a count-saturating B-process alias family; `WI-190` shows that the available almost-all short-interval prime-uniformity theorem can hide that entire deterministic alias locus inside its exceptional set. The remaining route needs uniformity on the structured alias family, a joint bilinear/spectral estimate, or cancellation across the dual family itself.

Weil Positivity pushes the Gamma obstruction through still broader bounded-analytic completion classes. `WP-178`--`WP-182` separate the exceptional `det_2` passive phase behavior from higher modified determinants and dissipative completions; `WP-183`--`WP-185` show that the exact Gamma relative phase cannot survive bounded analytic linear mixing, bounded-characteristic Möbius repair, or finite rational/projective decoding. A continuation must genuinely leave bounded-characteristic projective coordinates or obtain a new boundary sign theorem before scalar quotienting.

Xi Flow first closes several tempting global Gaussian/Vieta repairs in `XF-076`--`XF-082`: global finite-band quotients are trivial or seam-limited, and center-local Vieta state is ill-conditioned/nonidentifiable even with exact heat compatibility. `XF-083`--`XF-086` then recover a positive route: real-rooted carriers stabilize the visible logarithmic data, the source bridge becomes an equal-weight trigonometric moment problem, and bounded guarded-selector resource automatically enters the exact realization cone. The static real-divisor realization gate is therefore closed positively; what remains is a dynamic/source-to-destination transport theorem in this finite selector state.

Across the portfolio, the sharpest discriminator is increasingly **stable transport under source-constrained geometry**. Exact representation, exact recovery, finite-dimensional enlargement or local positivity is useful only when the same quantitative resource survives the actual destination map; otherwise classicalization, aliasing, gauge freedom, endpoint concentration or self-adjoint-resolvent rigidity removes the apparent gain.
