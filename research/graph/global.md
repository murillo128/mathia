At six points, the first one-pair reversal is shown to be curvature-seeded and the entire base-profile branch is screened; only a thin notch-induced curvature/height boundary layer remains before the line must move to higher-cardinality, multi-pair, ordered, or non-scalar information. Positive degree-two and degree-zero shells replace the hoped-for square-root cancellation transition, while exact Chebyshev conditioning and moving-window width/amplitude tradeoffs make degree-only reconstruction too expensive; a survivor must use a source-specific signed cross-degree relation or a different observable. Prime Circle similarly closes the finite local cyclotomic refinement algebra: finite positive-depth kernels, radial jets, refinement differences, weighted order defects and anchored angular/radial jets all reduce to one shell-dependent Mellin carrier. Any live route must introduce genuinely nonlocal mixed-prime or multi-carrier structure before scalarization.

Small exact symplectic strain alone does not force that chart entry, so the remaining theorem must use source-specific nonlinear control. This can certify a fixed-table residual distance or orientation separation; only a later across-height/population claim needs a zeta-side stochastic model.

A scalable continuation therefore has to preserve a genuinely joint nonlinear source constraint rather than compress the new surplus back into a universal scalar or affine tax.
