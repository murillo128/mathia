## Current integration boundary

The current source window materially expands all ten active research lines and adds four durable line intuitions. The Riemann Atlas denominator and territory states remain unchanged, and no new cross-line semantic relation is promoted: the synthesis below only summarizes surviving canonical evidence.

**Analytic Frontier** turns the complex/separable tube into a sharper near-extremizer program. `ANF-088`--`ANF-107` isolate projection, conditioning and source-heavy endpoint geometry; `ANF-108`, `ANF-109`, `ANF-111` and `ANF-112` then repair the matched-critical sparse cascade at packet level. An old endpoint packet is either source-negligible and excisable or forces new disjoint source-heavy height mass, so pairwise non-recycling is no longer the gate, although renewed matched-critical sparsity is still required to iterate.

**Arithmetic Fidelity** moves from fixed-degree Blaschke recovery to source-geometry optimization. `AF-175`--`AF-194` make inverse conditioning separation-controlled and map the Euler-cluster bandwidth/saddle profile; `AF-195`--`AF-197` identify minimal moment-flat controls with divided differences and show that nonuniform cubic and mirror-symmetric quartic source stencils beat equispaced parity at fixed Wasserstein scale.

**Möbius Cancellation** closes a broad finite/local state family. `MC-120`--`MC-137` show that bounded memory, modular state, polynomial clocks and related finite summaries remain excursion-blind; `MC-138`--`MC-144` then push the Bost--Connes route into diagonal prime independence, finite-character collapse and finally profinite-continuous number-state diagonals for bounded observables. The line still needs genuinely excursion-coupled signed information rather than a richer bounded local readout.

**Prime Circle** pushes finite scalar and mixed-shell fusion into a classical/low-complexity closure. `PC-200`--`PC-213` reduce power-map, Haar, Hardy and refinement constructions to Dirichlet-character, Mordell--Tornheim, rational-cone, Toeplitz or profinite prime-local data; `PC-214` shows that even the first binary mixed-composite coefficient-mask compression has a bounded Kronecker-sum spectrum. `PC/MI-012` records that a continuation must preserve a genuinely nonclassical relational degree of freedom.

**Prime Flute** makes strong local endpoint progress but meets a screened Schur-transport gate. `PF-196`--`PF-213` close several critical-Orlicz, localization, seam and reassembly costs; `PF-214`--`PF-217` show that normalized adjacent hopping diverges while boundary shorting screens the raw pant-mass floor. `PF-218` passes that screening through the exact inverse and excludes tail-uniform raw-`q` damping with summable low transport, so the live route must retain the physical seam coefficient or a weaker screened weight inside the recoupling word. `PF/MI-006` records the corresponding endpoint-currency lesson.

**Prime Lattice** extends covariance collapse well beyond finite fibers. `PL-196`--`PL-210` show that unitary, nonunitary, model-space and multichannel transports are flat, programmable or forced into trivial affine/resolvent geometry; `PL-211`--`PL-215` add Bost--Connes KMS thresholds, cyclotomic harmonics, high-temperature correlation collapse and temperature-sector disjointness. Enlarging the operator model still does not create an arithmetic zero-selector.

**Visual Exploration** narrows several attractive controls to exact null models. `VIS-075`--`VIS-091` characterize signed Euler-product and Gram-clock behavior, including Haar/freezing and finite-order null hierarchies; `VIS-092`--`VIS-095` show that one-dimensional persistence loses window order and simple delay signatures recover only bounded local block order. A discriminating visualization must therefore retain genuinely order-sensitive mixed-coordinate structure.

**Weil Inertia** isolates the bow-scale mismatch. `WI-191`--`WI-194` identify Atkinson/B-process self-dual phase geometry, while `WI-195`--`WI-197` reduce the live transfer to a multiplicative Gallagher `L^2` gate and show that twist-averaged diagonal information plus the published Type-II module do not reach the required bow scale. The continuation needs a genuinely joint structured-locus/bilinear estimate.

**Weil Positivity** finds a viable category beyond the finite regular passive no-go and then sharpens its source-forcing problem. `WP-198`--`WP-202` show that rough infinite centered baths can universally stabilize finite positive `4k` payloads, exactly when a reciprocal Fourier floor is present. `WP-203` shows that even the exact completed-Gamma determinant of the canonical number operator does not determine that roughness resource, so a survivor needs additional canonical geometry fixing the fractional functional calculus or a nonseparable finite-prime coupling.

**Xi Flow** closes endpoint-discretization mechanics positively and exposes a deeper transition-identifiability barrier. `XF-087`--`XF-097` obtain the corrected Volterra/trapezoid real carrier; `XF-098`--`XF-105` push nonidentifiability through growing prime bands and a fixed fraction of the `q` scale; `XF-106`--`XF-109` show that any surviving Toeplitz obstruction must be collective, `q`-scale and macroscopically rough because PNT flattening cancels smooth witnesses. `XF/MI-009` and `XF/MI-010` separate the positive static realization and source-fixed endpoint renormalization from that still-open dynamic gate.

Across the portfolio, the strongest recurring discriminator is whether retained structure survives source-constrained transport after regular, finite-state, scalar and smooth controls are quotiented out. Positive movement appears when the representation changes category in a source-backed way — packet-level endpoint geometry, optimized nonuniform source stencils, or rough infinite baths — while many finite-dimensional or regular enrichments continue to lose the arithmetic degree of freedom at the destination.
