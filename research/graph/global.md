The local nonlinear depth changes, but the output remains a classical cyclotomic period class; a live route must use genuinely nonlocal, growing-depth, shell-dependent, mixed-prime, or otherwise nonclassical structure before scalarization.

Bounded-window uniqueness is therefore not global arithmetic recovery; a survivor needs a genuinely joint signed or bilinear large-tail/small-block relation.
