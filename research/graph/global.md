## Current integration boundary

Ten source-backed research lines are represented, with Farey Discrepancy, Nyman–Beurling, and Robin Extremal additionally materialized as initialized zero-finding lines. The evidence-gated cross-line relations above remain unchanged; the current source delta advances several line-local frontiers without establishing a new cross-line theorem or changing the Atlas denominator.

Analytic Frontier now removes the finite-real Montgomery–Taylor ceiling rather than merely bounding it. The sharp extremizer's zero set kills stationary Palm realizations, but the finite-diffraction order interval is disjoint from the sharp budget, yielding an explicit central-notch separator. Complex tests then collapse through four points and most five-point geometry; the remaining two-conjugate-pair layer has a sharp one-self-energy floor and an explicit finite-height danger band, leaving cross-frequency phase coherence as the unresolved scalar obstruction. Arithmetic Fidelity extends the category/resource hierarchy through regularized-determinant summability, relative spectral scale, finite multiscale repair, entropy-constrained exact description, information dimension, and full conditional quantization profiles under a recovery modulus.

Möbius Cancellation sharpens the moving quadratic-character route. Absolute transfer still pays Burgess/conductor floors; signed squarefree-character feedback removes the conductor-zero `L1` floor but replaces it with a positive split-prime budget. Classical Page–Siegel and Landau–Page structure then excludes small stretched-exponential conductors and collapses the quasi-subpower positive-feedback family to at most one exceptional primitive character, without producing an improved Mertens estimate. Prime Circle closes a broad local refinement escape: full power-refinement covariance rigidifies local metrics and differential operators, while bounded generalized Robin repair is incompatible with exact refinement and strong unbounded Robin covariance collapses to one fixed four-real-parameter first-order block, leaving no intrinsic shell-dependent arithmetic slot in that category.

Prime Lattice replaces the withdrawn second-scale item with a sharper Suzuki checkpoint boundary. One-sided boundedness on prime-power checkpoints is already RH-equivalent, broad fine meshes can retain the completed signal, but any single fixed step has an exact off-line aliasing control. The surviving fixed-ray loophole is correspondingly narrow: under RH failure, one-sided boundedness phase-pins any rightmost zero frontier to the sampling identity, and lower boundedness forces an unattained high-height phase-pinned frontier. The unresolved input is therefore zeta-specific anti-aliasing or an additional incommensurable/source coupling, not more samples on the same ray.

Visual Exploration further classicalizes the Farey endpoint signal: permutation and reflection controls remove ordering/symmetry artifacts, the endpoint fan forces the `n`-scale modal transition, and the full fixed-`nx` profile is exactly a first-order Riesz mean of `phi(a)/a`, equivalently an integrated summatory-totient remainder with an exact Möbius decomposition. Weil Inertia closes much of the support-one positive-compression route: bounded-depth scalar universality and pointwise-PSD matrix compression reduce to Fourier-positive/Montgomery–Taylor extremals, while the published BGSTB pointwise form-factor error is not uniform enough on singular near-extremizers to justify an escape. Any surviving improvement needs genuinely new arithmetic error control, sign-indefinite joint structure, higher correlations, or source-restricted geometry.

Weil Positivity pushes the resultant route through its positive completion boundary. Mixed-prime resultant chains have extensive inertia; the all-prime graph Laplacian has divergent fixed-shell degree and spectator-prime bypasses collapse effective resistance, while every vertex-local positive renormalization that keeps fixed basis energies finite erases the fixed arithmetic edges. Xi Flow reaches a complementary local-to-source boundary: collision-safe normalized discriminants and finite-range scale-invariant shape assemblies still have geometric-ramp null modes, but super-mesoscopic Xi counting plus the borderline flux-variation hypothesis flattens those ramps and in fact forces the total logarithmic gap variation to vanish. The remaining obligation is to derive the required flux control from the Xi dynamics or exhibit a source-valid near-null escape.
