## Current integration boundary

The current source window adds **59 canonical findings across all thirteen research lines**. No durable `MI-*` node is added or removed, canonical prior-art membership is unchanged, no new cross-line semantic relation is promoted, and the Riemann Atlas v1 denominator and territory states remain unchanged. The summaries below integrate only persisted canonical evidence.

**Analytic Frontier** materially strengthens the positive saddle-annihilator cascade. Slow diagonalization absorbs arbitrary finite-depth conditioning losses, while odd dilation of the annihilator shifts preserves exact cancellation and makes both slack spend and density-weighted physical span summable. Positive residual slack and linear support span are therefore no longer existential obstructions inside this class; the remaining question is whether the resulting source-heavy infinite family is genuinely fatal rather than merely constructible.

**Arithmetic Fidelity** gets a small finite-order positive result and a stronger global rigidity boundary. The Euler-log kernel is strictly sign-regular at low finite orders, but exact scalar/vector translation representations on the prime-tail mesh collapse to one affine direction, and the approximate version is Hyers-stable whenever the retained profile has a quantitative inverse modulus. A nonlinear coordinate escape must therefore pay in observation error, conditioning, unresolved side information, or leave the one-difference translation category.

**Farey Discrepancy** turns the classical Farey criterion into an exact multiscale Mertens energy. The discrepancy is a Ramanujan–Mertens frame and the Franel quadratic discrepancy is an exact normalized power-GCD form, but the generic scalar dual bound for extracting `M(N)` is asymptotically sharp. Positivity, conditioning or diagonalization of that GCD energy alone cannot improve the critical scalar constant without using the actual staircase structure or pre-cumulative Farey ordering.

**Möbius Cancellation** tests Bost–Connes phase information more directly but finds no inexpensive statistic. The sigma twist and phase selector reduce to familiar arithmetic data, the heat orbit gives an RH-equivalent formulation, and stationary or sufficiently long finite-time mean-square readouts wash out the prime phases. The surviving route must retain genuinely nonstationary phase relationships rather than another bounded scalar heat statistic.

**Nyman–Beurling** establishes a target-awareness barrier inside the canonical semigroup itself. Complete generator Gram data do not determine distance to the fixed target; multiplicative semigroup copies preserve local Gram geometry while their global target mass decays by scale. Even finitely many target-character covariance probes have zero quantitative gap because high Mellin frequencies can alias any fixed finite set of logarithmic scales. A positive compression must retain target pairings or use a growing, quantitatively anti-aliased scale family.

**Prime Circle** narrows the fresh-prime escape to genuinely squarefree/internal-fiber information. Fixed-base fresh-prime leakage has finite residue-class limits and mode transfer converges to finite cyclotomic Cauchy packets; repeated-prime depth then factors exactly through the squarefree radical and adds only deterministic aliasing plus kernel directions. Repeated conductor powers do not create new mode-resolved arithmetic degrees of freedom.

**Prime Flute** sharpens the high-high shear gate rather than closing the route. More than one total scaled tangential derivative is sufficient, the natural blowup converts the corridor into a uniformly elliptic long-strip family, direct shear is exponentially local in Pöschl–Teller mode index, and a fixed-seam Robin homotopy reduces the full Schur difference to one-sided separated Poisson smoothing. An abstract countermodel proves that one-sided form domination alone cannot supply that smoothing, so a positive proof must now exploit actual seam microlocal/spectral structure or the finite physical trace realization.

**Prime Lattice** closes another symmetry-based equilibrium family. Exact prime permutation symmetry collapses normal states to the vacuum, exchangeable/de Finetti completion does not restore a normal arithmetic state, and preserving only a fixed finite arithmetic coloring still collapses mass outside the exceptional finite sector. Source-relevant equilibrium must break prime symmetry by unbounded arithmetic information or leave the normal-state category.

**Robin Extremal** turns the first canonical findings into a quantitative local gate. Hypothetical Robin failure forces infinitely many regular self-tangent colossally abundant peaks; exact neighboring comparisons require secant-clear transition gaps. Classical consecutive-CA jump structure makes the asymptotic half-jump regime uniform, so such peaks must occur in event gaps large enough to absorb asymptotically half of the logarithmic mass of both boundary jumps. The live problem is now an event-spacing versus event-mass comparison, not an unconstrained jump-size regime.

**Visual Exploration** improves the third-order chronology carrier and its null dictionary. Exact cyclic permutation covariance, Haar-CUE low-degree identities and collision removal separate self-collision terms from the genuine third factorial channel. The statistic survives as a prospective carrier, but a finite zero-window comparison still needs the same collision-free finite-window CUE calibration; raw centering or removing repeated indices is not enough.

**Weil Inertia** closes every fixed finite reciprocal-harmonic bootstrap. Dense bows cannot be screened by critical-line mass alone, but a bounded number of additional off-line functional-equation pairs can exactly cancel any fixed finite harmonic packet while remaining within the critical strip in the relevant scaling. A viable bootstrap must therefore charge radial depth, use a continuum or a number of frequencies growing with population, or impose source-specific restrictions on the screen.

**Weil Positivity** narrows the Bost–Connes test-space escape. Every nonzero jointly shift-invariant Hardy submodule retains both signs of the critical defect, while finite-dimensional coinvariant compressions can be programmed positive or negative by choosing the quotient. Quotient positivity is therefore not source-forced evidence unless arithmetic/KMS/archimedean data select the quotient before the sign is inspected.

**Xi Flow** strengthens the zero-delay conditioning obstruction. Central matched packets hide progressively larger logarithmic heat jets from exterior observations; after optimization, every sublinear raw logarithmic jet can remain exponentially transition-blind at a height whose macroscopic fraction tends to zero. Exact observability at specially informative traces therefore remains sharply separated from stable local transport.

Across the portfolio, the current window yields four narrow viable extensions (Analytic Frontier, Prime Flute, Robin Extremal and Visual Exploration), one explicit prior-art classicalization (Prime Circle), and eight new barriers. The common positive pattern is a sharper source-backed next gate, not a completed RH mechanism; the common negative pattern is that finite scalarization, fixed-scale probing, symmetry averaging or generic positive geometry discards precisely the information required by the destination criterion.
