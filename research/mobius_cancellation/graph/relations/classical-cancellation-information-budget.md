# Classical Möbius cancellation needs polynomial-strength information beyond generic local or scalar inputs

Central synthesis: [[research/mobius_cancellation/mind/RESEARCH_LINES|Möbius Cancellation Mind]].

[[research/mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-001-local-to-global-exceptional-mass-barrier|MC-001]] gives an exact generic transfer inequality from short-window magnitude and exceptional-set measure to a dyadic global sum. Inserting the currently available almost-all short-interval parameters yields only logarithmic global saving, so an RH-scale route needs polynomially stronger local magnitude, exceptional-mass control, or signed/coherent relations among windows.

[[research/mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-002-prime-harmonic-ceiling-single-scale-pretentiousness|MC-002]] closes a different black-box shortcut. The standard one-scale pretentious distance has only `O(log log x)` total prime-harmonic mass, so feeding that one scalar into the generic Halász exponential cannot yield the `Theta(log x)` exponent needed for a polynomial global saving. A viable pretentious route must retain additional multiscale, correlation, factorization or source-specific structure.

These findings are complementary information-budget obstructions, not a proof that short-interval or pretentious methods cannot contribute to RH. They identify the missing quantitative currency that any continuation must add before it can improve the global Mertens scale.
