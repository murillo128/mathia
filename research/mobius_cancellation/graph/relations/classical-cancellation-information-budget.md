# Classical Möbius cancellation needs polynomial-strength information beyond generic local or scalar inputs

Central synthesis: [[research/mobius_cancellation/mind/RESEARCH_LINES|Möbius Cancellation Mind]].

[[research/mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-001-local-to-global-exceptional-mass-barrier|MC-001]] gives an exact generic transfer inequality from short-window magnitude and exceptional-set measure to a dyadic global sum. Inserting the currently available almost-all short-interval parameters yields only logarithmic global saving, so an RH-scale route needs polynomially stronger local magnitude, exceptional-mass control, or signed/coherent relations among windows. [[research/mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-006-averaged-chowla-vdc-logarithmic-ceiling|MC-006]] reaches the same quantitative wall from averaged two-point correlations: the proved Matomäki--Radziwiłł--Tao scale gives only logarithmic saving through black-box van der Corput.

[[research/mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-002-prime-harmonic-ceiling-single-scale-pretentiousness|MC-002]] closes the one-scale prime-only shortcut, and [[research/mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-003-mobius-liouville-square-convolution-threshold|MC-003]] shows that the first natural prime-power repair has its own exact square-root boundary. Möbius and Liouville agree at every prime, their prime-power-sensitive transfer starts at the square layer, and the corresponding absolute convolution/pretentious thresholds converge only strictly above exponent `1/2`.

Qualitative pseudorandomness does not supply the missing polynomial budget. [[research/mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-004-qualitative-chowla-support-matched-bias|MC-004]] constructs exact-square-free-support qualitative Chowla controls with near-linear anchored bias, while [[research/mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-005-exact-support-multiplicativity-slow-mean-cancellation|MC-005]] restores multiplicativity and mean cancellation yet permits arbitrarily slow logarithmic decay. These controls leave quantitative uniformity and the exact multiplicative relations used by Möbius as the unresolved information source.

The transfer picture nevertheless has a sharper surviving distinction. [[research/mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-007-same-prime-comparator-squarefull-kernel|MC-007]] factors every bounded multiplicative same-prime comparator through a squarefull kernel and localizes absolute reverse-transfer obstructions to small-prime factors. [[research/mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-008-analytic-nonmasking-vs-absolute-inversion|MC-008]] then gives an explicit dyadic comparator whose absolute inverse is too large below exponent `1`, yet whose RH-scale summatory bound remains equivalent to RH because the analytic transfer factor does not mask hypothetical zeta zeros in the open right critical half-strip.

[[research/mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-009-pintz-mean-absolute-zero-boundary|MC-009]] adds a conditional literature-facing endpoint rather than new cancellation input. Subject to the fresh Pintz preprint theorem surviving its explicitly requested audit, the global mean-absolute Mertens statistic has logarithmic power exponent equal to the rightmost zeta-zero boundary; an RH-scale bound for that mean absolute statistic would therefore already imply RH. This weakens the required *output* from pointwise Mertens control to a mean-absolute endpoint, but none of MC-001--MC-008 currently supplies the polynomial-strength input needed to reach it.

The live frontier is therefore narrower than either "more pseudorandomness" or "a stronger scalar distance": identify a source-faithful signed/multiscale mechanism with a polynomial information budget, or exploit an analytically nonmasking transfer whose required comparator estimate is independently provable. The MC-009 endpoint may be used only conditionally until its fresh external theorem is audited; it is not itself evidence that the missing arithmetic estimate is easier.
