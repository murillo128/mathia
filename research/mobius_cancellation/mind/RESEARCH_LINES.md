# Möbius-cancellation research lines

This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Möbius-cancellation evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Move fixed-rank source geometry to a genuinely retained arithmetic resource

MC-423--MC-445 separate source diversity, representation fidelity, Möbius top-interaction and natural-source measure. Fixed-rank normalized prime-gap geometry is dense in the ambient simplex and polynomially realizable, while the most direct bounded-height natural ensemble concentrates on a terminal simplex vertex. Continuous fixed-rank normalized observables therefore do not acquire robust prime specificity merely from support richness or from switching to the canonical counting measure.

The surviving resources are qualitatively different: growing rank, another justified source measure, absolute scale, discontinuous arithmetic data, cross-rank structure, or coordinates that retain enough factor identity to survive the Möbius projection. Any such carrier still has to produce an analytic cancellation estimate after the exact top-interaction quotient.

## Use residual factor variables before anchor compression and positive closure erase them

MC-446--MC-453 close generic fixed-lcm occupancy, bare frequency synchronization, final beta-sieve sign variation, raw truncated Möbius as an all-splits well-factorable sequence, and bounded component multiplicity after separate positive quadratic closure. A conductor-power gain must therefore enter through a genuine auxiliary factor representation before generic recombination, majorization or squaring destroys the retained variables.

MC-458--MC-462 now locate that resource more precisely. Staged factor conditioning leaves a residual divisor-sum kernel whose divisibility concentration is governed by the normalized source shift. Expanding one residual divisor promotes that shift into the character source, but the apparent family of starts is compressed: the starts fall into only bounded anchor fibres determined by a reduced residue class. On a fixed anchor the character kernel has an exact shift-energy identity, so a gain is possible only through enough **effective weighted participation inside the anchor fibre**, not through the nominal number of residual divisors.

MC-462 adds a necessary support gate. The fibre participation itself is bounded by the residual range, giving the rough condition `S H ≳ p r_2` before the fixed-anchor square-root mechanism can even be numerically competitive. Passing this gate is not sufficient: coefficient spread, interval variation, aligned amplitude and the signed transfer back to Möbius remain unproved.

The live q-vdC question is therefore to prove a weighted dispersion theorem for the actual residual coefficients on the anchor fibres that pass the range gate, with enough target-aligned participation to obtain a conductor power **before** positive closure, and then to propagate that signed saving through the remaining recombination to `M(x)`. Conversely, a sharp upper bound showing that the actual coefficient weights never have sufficient effective participation would close this staged factorability route. Nominal factor count, anchor count, or residual support size alone is no longer a valid resource.
