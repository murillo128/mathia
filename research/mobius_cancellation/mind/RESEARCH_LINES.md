Can the source produce at least two **tangent or otherwise shell-compatible** translation directions with enough joint bandwidth, stationary phase, reconstruction and off-diagonal control before positive quadratic closure? Ordinary factor coordinates, ambient rank and displayed higher-order variables are not free bandwidth.
