If `d^perp(W)>=5`, the exact coordinate signs are four-wise independent, and their second/fourth moments force `Omega(H)` source differences to have correlation at least the critical frame scale `1/sqrt(2R)`. Hence the desired `o(R^(-1/2))` good-mode theorem outside `O(H/R)` exceptions is impossible in that regime.

MC-391 sharpens the surviving structural class. Quotient the coordinate characters by `C=W^perp` and merge duplicate characters exactly. If the distinct character classes have masses `w_a`, define

`sigma_A^2=sum_a w_a^2`.

The exact fourth moment is controlled by genuine four-character collisions. An `O(H/R)` exceptional family with all other modes `o(R^(-1/2))` requires normalized quartic collision mass growing linearly in `R`; in a balanced rank-linear regime this means `Omega(R^3)` genuine four-character relations. A single low-weight dual word is therefore nowhere near enough.

MC-392 then prices the entire collision budget by the number of independent source relations. Let `c=dim C`. Because the projected support is a quotient of coordinate vertices, every nonzero translate pairs at most `c+1` support points, giving the weighted fourth-moment ceiling

`E|g|^4 <= (2c+3)(E|g|^2)^2`.

If all but `B` satisfy `|g|<=epsilon` and `R epsilon^2<=1`, then

`|B|+1 >= H/(2c+3)(1-R epsilon^2)^2`.

Thus at `epsilon=o(R^(-1/2))`, a fixed exceptional budget `|B|<=K H/R` forces

`c >= (1-o(1)) R/(2K)-3/2`.

Sublinear source codimension cannot support any fixed `K`. For the fixed-power source cutoff of MC-381, `c<=A/beta` with `beta=1/100`, so the unavoidable exceptional population is at least `(1-o(1))H/(200A+3)`.

The immediate arithmetic target is therefore more primitive than raw quartic-collision counting: determine whether the actual source-incidence system can supply **linear codimension `c=Omega(R)` at admissible conductor/source cost** in the regime where the desired analytic theorem would leave only `O(H/R)` exceptional modes. If the physical source architecture forces `c=o(R)`, MC-392 already closes the sparse-exception route before any finer additive-energy optimization.
