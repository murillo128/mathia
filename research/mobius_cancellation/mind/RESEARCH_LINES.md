## Control blind-sector dimension or its signed source coupling, not only minimum support

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category`, `MI-014-centered-parity-and-zero-mode-are-separate-currencies`, `MI-015-sign-support-covariance-gives-a-conditioned-quadratic-bridge`, `MI-016-source-aligned-shifts-face-a-defect-or-mertens-dichotomy`, `MI-017-first-shell-crt-common-mode-is-a-source-coupled-energy-coordinate`, `MI-018-nonnegative-transference-does-not-create-fixed-power-sign-balance`, `MI-019-shell-square-blindness-is-a-cross-prime-common-quotient-problem`.

MC-238 identifies smooth-dilation blindness with the kernel of the Legendre-symbol matrix generated by small primes. MC-239 removes prime-square lift defects and one-coordinate blindness. MC-240--MC-242 progressively force any surviving blind vector to have support at least

`(1/log 2-o(1)) log log y`.

MC-243 turns this exact kernel into its natural coding-theory object. The support floor is a minimum-distance bound for a binary linear code, and the Hamming sphere-packing inequality gives the actual Legendre matrix the rank lower bound

`R_y(S) >= (1/(2(log 2)^2)-o(1)) log y log log y`

at the active shell scaling `n asymp y/log y`.

The same coding viewpoint also closes minimum support as a route to annihilator triviality. Matched Gilbert--Varshamov controls show that abstract binary kernels with the same double-logarithmic minimum distance can still have dimension `n-O(log y log log y)`. More generally, even a one-dimensional kernel can have a unique nonzero vector of full support. Thus increasing a scalar minimum-support bound, by itself, cannot determine whether the blind sector is tiny or enormous.

The live algebraic theorem must therefore use structure that minimum distance discards: arithmetic dependencies of the Legendre matrix, a direct rank/kernel-dimension estimate, a count of surviving blind characters, or a source-specific theorem showing that every surviving mode has negligible coupling to the Möbius progression vector. Even annihilator triviality would still leave the fixed-power signed-amplitude/Fourier-decay gate.

## Keep support size, blind-sector dimension, arithmetic rank, Fourier decay, and signed amplitude separate

MC-243 gives a genuine rank gain from MC-242, but only on the coding scale `log y log log y`, far below the full-rank scale `y/log y`. The matched-code control shows that this gap is informational, not merely a weak constant in the current support estimate. Future credit should go to a theorem that controls the **number/dimension or source coupling** of collective blind modes, not to another support lower bound unless it imports new arithmetic structure beyond minimum distance.
