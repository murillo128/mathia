## Control the blind endpoint before aggregation, or find a signed/source-native inverse

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category`, `MI-014-centered-parity-and-zero-mode-are-separate-currencies`, `MI-015-sign-support-covariance-gives-a-conditioned-quadratic-bridge`, `MI-016-source-aligned-shifts-face-a-defect-or-mertens-dichotomy`, `MI-017-first-shell-crt-common-mode-is-a-source-coupled-energy-coordinate`, `MI-018-nonnegative-transference-does-not-create-fixed-power-sign-balance`, `MI-019-shell-square-blindness-is-a-cross-prime-common-quotient-problem`, `MI-020-explicit-large-sieve-uniformity-stops-before-blind-support-scale`, `MI-021-support-matched-high-moments-cross-the-single-blind-shell-threshold`, `MI-022-positive-shell-envelopes-erase-the-phase-cancellation-needed-below-the-blind-atom`, `MI-023-negative-witness-collapse-trades-rowwise-simplicity-for-source-depth`.

MC-266--MC-277 show that the existing degree-`m` row representation still carries the blind bit with exact margin `delta_(k,m)~2m/k`; pointwise polynomial normalization is expensive, while L2 normalization is cheap precisely because it subtracts the target atom. The main loss is created when row identities are normalized or aggregated and exceptional-row provenance disappears.

MC-278 shows that one low-rational-degree decoder does not escape this source-depth bill: its positive Walsh mass concentrates near degree `k/2`. MC-279 removes the dependence on that particular formula. For **every positive-definite Boolean endpoint decoder** that is asymptotically concentrated on the blind atom, the total nonnegative Fourier mass below degree `(1/2-delta)k` tends to zero. The half-depth obstruction is therefore a positivity/localization theorem, not an accident of the explicit resolvent.

At the live arithmetic depth `m=Theta(log log y)=o(k)`, changing polynomial degree, rational degree, radial shape or the exact positive decoder cannot make a pointlike endpoint selector source-cheap. Two genuinely different routes remain. First, control the existing row scalar at its natural margin before provenance is discarded, or derive **signed** cross-row cancellation that cannot mask one blind contribution. Second, find a source-native operator/resolvent identity whose useful action is not represented by a positive scalar endpoint decoder on the full Boolean cube. Such an escape must exploit localized arithmetic structure or sign, not generic positive-definite approximation.

## Keep algebraic degree, source depth, positivity, normalization and aggregation separate

A negative witness simplifies one row; overlapping witnesses remains low-degree; the raw row scalar has a polynomially small but exact margin; polynomial normalization can reconstruct OR at high degree; rational normalization can have low numerator/denominator degree while importing macroscopic Walsh depth; MC-279 shows that any positive-definite pointlike endpoint decoder pays the same half-depth barrier; and L2 aggregation can erase the target atom by design. These are different resources.

MC-252 remains the matched control: once tight prime-shell localization is removed, separated-prime Legendre data can realize the full Boolean cube. Any successful escape must therefore identify where the localized arithmetic source or signed structure supplies information unavailable to this generic cube geometry.
