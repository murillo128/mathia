# Möbius-cancellation research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable Möbius-cancellation intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Recover first-absolute excursion from information that survives divisor-topology and coefficient-algebra controls

**Linked intuition:** `MI-004-mean-absolute-cancellation-needs-excursion-coupled-information`.

The endpoint is already sharp: square-root-scale first-absolute Mertens control on any unbounded checkpoint sequence is RH-complete. MC-122--MC-132 close broad finite-state, path-type, periodic-clock, and controlled moment summaries. MC-133--MC-137 then push the topological route further: the divisibility complex and its ordinary persistence already encode the hard Möbius sign in target-equivalent form, while the natural dyadic star/coning extensions do not manufacture a cheaper long-range excursion carrier.

The surviving source must therefore retain order or arithmetic relations that are neither finite/quasi-polynomial summaries nor a topological recoding whose signed observable is already `M`. A useful theorem must connect such a relation quantitatively to first-absolute excursion at a cost genuinely below the RH-complete endpoint.

## Seek an intermediate transverse Bost--Connes category, not canonical energy or raw semigroup regularity

**Linked intuition:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category`.

MC-138--MC-147 classify the two topology extremes. Canonical coefficient and fixed-rational-band scalarizations remain profinite-continuous and cannot uniformly recover Möbius orientation, while unrestricted strong/weak completion reaches all bounded diagonals and, for the represented full algebra, all of `B(l^2(N))`. The latter is target-complete rather than target-selective.

MC-148 closes the most immediate source-natural regularization of that weak completion. In the canonical number-state representation the time-flow fixed algebra, the coefficient von Neumann closure, and the canonical low-temperature Gibbs centralizer are all the same diagonal algebra `l^infty(N)`. Every bounded arithmetic diagonal has zero canonical energy derivative to all orders, so Hamiltonian smoothness or analyticity is completely blind to Möbius.

MC-149 then kills the raw transverse repair. For the canonical semigroup isometries, `[D_a,S_m]` is always bounded for every bounded diagonal `a`; requiring compactness or finite Schatten decay instead excludes the Möbius diagonal for every nontrivial dilation. The raw family therefore has an exact **too-weak / wrong-target** dichotomy.

The live Bost--Connes question is now genuinely intermediate: identify a source-forced weighted, twisted, averaged, representation-sensitive, or relational transverse structure that is stricter than arbitrary bounded multiplicative differences but still admits Möbius, is far smaller than arbitrary weak completion, and yields a non-tautological quantitative bridge from multiplicative variation to anchored additive Mertens excursion. Merely invoking dynamics, smoothness, noncommutativity, or commutator boundedness no longer changes the information class.

## Treat finite-state vocabulary growth as closed until it changes order topology

Adding context length, modular height, finitely many future steps, periodic phases, or finitely/moderately many polynomial timestamp moments enlarges the state description but still admits exact matched controls. A local enrichment should first be tested for whether it factors through a finite product-state/path-type summary before cancellation meaning is assigned to it.

## Require the source theorem to be Möbius-specific but not target-equivalent

Generic matched controls need not satisfy Möbius multiplicativity, square-free support, prime values, or divisor identities, so exact arithmetic remains a legitimate escape. The converse warning is equally important: MC-133--MC-149 show that a representation can be fully Möbius-specific or cyclotomic and still store the hard sign only as an equivalent encoding, lose it under a natural continuity quotient, admit every bounded target after completion, or impose a regularity class that rejects Möbius itself.

A positive theorem must identify an arithmetic relation that forbids the known rearrangement/product/profinite/target-complete/raw-commutator controls and yields an independently controlled quantity strictly cheaper than the Mertens endpoint.
