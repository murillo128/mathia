## Test restricted CRT-idempotent source-frame moments, not ambient cancellation

**Linked intuition:** `MI-089-ambient-cancellation-helps-a-source-family-only-after-its-weighted-image-pays-the-occupancy-tariff`.

MC-446 computes the exact full-box second moment of the incomplete translated character correlations left by MC-413. The ambient `(u,a)` family has genuine `sqrt(H)` RMS cancellation, but plain Cauchy transfers it to weighted source frames only when their effective occupancy exceeds `E_q(H)/H^2`, asymptotically about `q^2/H` in the rough squarefree regime. A sparse source image therefore cannot inherit the ambient gain for free.

MC-447 classifies the first proposed arithmetic escapes from that occupancy tariff. In the separated range `d,e<=D`, `D^2<q`, `(h,q)=1`, the frame determines the exact lcm and CRT residue. Collisions are confined to the elementary three-way assignment at primes common to `L` and `h`; for shifts coprime to the sieve support the map is injective. Fixing the constrained shift coordinate is simply fixing `L`, not gaining another averaging dimension.

The accepted q-vdC clue is consequently narrow and concrete: compute a restricted weighted second moment or dispersion form on the distinct CRT-idempotent `u`-set, inserting the well-factorable coefficients before Cauchy. The branch survives only if the actual arithmetic image has a diagonal/off-diagonal cost parametrically below the ambient tariff, if the outer weights cancel, if modulus factorization reduces the conductor before scalarization, or if a useful wraparound/nonunit-shift regime lies outside MC-447. More ambient moments, hidden fibre counts, or treating `a` as free are closed.

## Separate description sparsity, source measure, quotient entropy, interaction depth and analytic usefulness

Small alphabets, rich support, high nominal pair count and favorable ambient averages are all representation-level quantities until the relevant quotient is taken. MC-445 shows that support and natural-source measure can point in opposite directions; MC-446/447 show that ambient moment strength and source-image occupancy can do the same.

A surviving mechanism must state the resource after scale quotienting and Möbius interaction extraction, specify the source measure or weighted image actually used, and prove that this carrier reaches the analytic destination. Representation richness or collective cancellation outside the admissible arithmetic image does not count by itself.
