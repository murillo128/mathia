**Linked intuition:** `MI-073-source-mode-averaging-collapses-to-a-signature-projector`.

Extra axes count only after the mechanism that creates them and its diagonal geometry have been audited.

## Do not count channel multiplicity, ambient coordinates or higher-order variables as free bandwidth

Scalar filter banks, positive Toeplitz forms and operator-valued channels stay inside the MC-417--MC-418 cone. MC-419--MC-420 add that formal tensorization earns no bandwidth unless the physical Fourier support has the corresponding integer rank. MC-421 adds a different failure mode: an axis may be genuinely independent yet arrive through a higher-order positive closure whose coordinate-zero faces cost reciprocal one-axis bandwidth. A viable escape must therefore certify both effective support geometry and the closure-specific diagonal ledger; neither ambient rank nor the number of displayed variables is sufficient.
