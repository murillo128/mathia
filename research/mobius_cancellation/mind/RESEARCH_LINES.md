MC-281--MC-292 reduce the collision-rich odd branch to finite Fourier, odd-girth, common-cell and entropy constraints. MC-293 adds source cost: below `D_y=exp(kappa log y/loglog y)` the near-negative spectrum contains at most one Landau--Page exceptional functional. MC-294 then shows that many independent near-negative directions cannot hide behind a cheap subspace: after deleting at most one direction, their images in the high-cost quotient have no short dependencies, so Hamming sphere packing forces a quantitative quotient-dimension bill.

MC-295 proves that this whole generic bill can still be saturated. A fixed-weight defect-shell model can make every nonzero cheap direction perfectly balanced, place `R` independent quotient directions at defect `tau=1/R`, attain effective participation `eta=R2^-R`, avoid every nonempty selected relation landing in the cheap subspace, and have no common preferred-sign cell. At `k=1`, even `R`, the entropy/common-cell boundary is met at the live scale `R tau=1`, while the high-cost quotient has full dimension `R`.

Therefore finite Fourier geometry, participation, defect budgets, Page-balance of the cheap sector, entropy and generic quotient-girth/Hamming constraints are **jointly consistent** with the endpoint escape. High-cost quotient dimension is not merely an unproved nuisance; there is now an explicit matched control showing that the audited abstract constraints cannot exclude it.

The remaining theorem must use arithmetic structure of the actual Legendre shell that MC-295 does not encode. Viable routes are an arithmetic upper bound or structural restriction on the high-source-cost quotient, a lower bound on effective occupancy incompatible with the fixed-weight shell, a source-cost/multiplicity relation, or a proof that the possible Landau--Page singleton cannot carry the endpoint mechanism. Sharpening the same generic coding/entropy inequalities without new arithmetic input cannot close the branch.

## Keep parity, occupancy, entropy complexity, source cost and quotient realizability as different currencies

Even weights are killed by equal-cell pairing. Odd weights require an odd nucleus plus pair capacity. MC-289--MC-292 constrain non-affine escape using odd moments, common-cell geometry and entropy. MC-293 constrains the minimum lower-prime product cost of individual generated shell functionals. MC-294 charges many directions to dimension and girth after quotienting by a uniformly cheap subspace.

MC-295 separates one more currency: **whether the expensive quotient geometry is arithmetically realizable by the actual shell**. The finite model pays every previously identified generic bill simultaneously. Thus raw support density, `eta`, independent rank, defect profile, entropy cost, source cost, cheap-subspace dimension, quotient girth and actual arithmetic realizability must not be conflated.

## Preserve matched controls and classical exceptional-character/coding theory as falsifiers

The Geelen threshold, entropy inequalities and Hamming sphere-packing bounds are sharp or near-sharp finite controls, not arithmetic impossibility theorems. MC-293 imports classical Landau--Page/character-PNT input only into the quasi-subpower source-cost sector. MC-294 combines that input with generic coding geometry; MC-295 now exhibits a finite matched control saturating the resulting escape.

Further progress must therefore exploit properties of the actual Legendre shell unavailable to the generic fixed-weight model: arithmetic restrictions on the expensive quotient code, occupancy/multiplicity forced by source incidence, or a mechanism excluding the exceptional mode. The next useful theorem should explicitly identify which of those arithmetic coordinates the matched control fails to reproduce.
