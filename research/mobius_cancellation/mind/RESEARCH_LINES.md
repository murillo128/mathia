This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Möbius-cancellation intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Control the quantitatively collective blind sector or prove the required signed amplitude directly

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category`, `MI-014-centered-parity-and-zero-mode-are-separate-currencies`, `MI-015-sign-support-covariance-gives-a-conditioned-quadratic-bridge`, `MI-016-source-aligned-shifts-face-a-defect-or-mertens-dichotomy`, `MI-017-first-shell-crt-common-mode-is-a-source-coupled-energy-coordinate`, `MI-018-nonnegative-transference-does-not-create-fixed-power-sign-balance`, `MI-019-shell-square-blindness-is-a-cross-prime-common-quotient-problem`.

MC-238 identifies smooth-dilation blindness with characters annihilating the subgroup generated by small primes. MC-239 removes prime-square lift defects and one-coordinate blindness. MC-240 first forced every surviving blind character to involve at least seven shell primes, and MC-241 eliminated every bounded support size asymptotically.

MC-242 now gives a quantitative floor. By using the explicit growing-depth q-van der Corput inequalities, every nonprincipal blind shell character satisfies

`liminf m(chi)/log log y >= 1/log 2`,

or `2^m(chi) >= (log y)^(1-o(1))`. Thus a surviving exact blind mode must already couple a double-logarithmically growing number of shell factors; the former qualitative phrase “unbounded support” is no longer the sharp frontier.

This still does not give annihilator triviality or signed cancellation. The live algebraic obstruction is a genuinely collective mode above that support floor. One must push the explicit character-sum control into the regime `m >= (1/log 2-o(1)) log log y`, prove eventual product generation by another source-specific argument, or show that every surviving high-support mode couples too weakly to the actual Möbius progression vector. Even triviality of the blind sector would still leave the fixed-power signed-amplitude/Fourier-decay gate.

## Keep support size, global generation, Fourier decay, and signed amplitude separate

Local factor generation and every support size below the quantitative MC-242 floor are closed resources. A lower bound on `m(chi)` is not a bound on the number of surviving modes, their Fourier coefficients, or their source coupling. Future credit should go only to a theorem controlling the genuinely growing collective sector or the source-selected signed amplitude at the required power scale.
