## Resolve the logarithmic shell-generation exit or the surviving blind pretender at the actual source modulus

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category`, `MI-014-centered-parity-and-zero-mode-are-separate-currencies`, `MI-015-sign-support-covariance-gives-a-conditioned-quadratic-bridge`, `MI-016-source-aligned-shifts-face-a-defect-or-mertens-dichotomy`, `MI-017-first-shell-crt-common-mode-is-a-source-coupled-energy-coordinate`, `MI-018-nonnegative-transference-does-not-create-fixed-power-sign-balance`, `MI-019-shell-square-blindness-is-a-cross-prime-common-quotient-problem`, `MI-020-explicit-large-sieve-uniformity-stops-before-blind-support-scale`, `MI-021-support-matched-high-moments-cross-the-single-blind-shell-threshold`.

The conductor and fixed-order algebraic escapes are largely exhausted. The wild prime-square direction descends to the squarefree shell radical, but projector normalization preserves the support exponent. Generic smooth-modulus variance leaves at most one blind pretender and is quantitatively too weak at the live scale. Full shell generation is exactly an all-class smooth-representative theorem with generator cutoff `y=(1+o(1))log R`, far below the polynomial generator scales supplied by current generic results.

The black-box growing-order quadratic-large-sieve route also stops too early: its explicit uniformity reaches only `o(log^(4)y)`, while the first possible blind support has size `t~(1/log 2)log log y`. Embedding the sparse shell-product family into all squarefree moduli spends the saving before this support scale.

[MC-264](../findings/MC-264-support-matched-high-moment-blind-threshold.md) sharpens the remaining analytic exit. Second moment is genuinely blind to one exceptional weight-`t` subset, and even moment order `2t` remains above the single-atom threshold at natural diagonal size. But one additional tensor power, `m=t+1`, changes the scale: the correct quadratic-character diagonal is `(2m-1)!! k_y^m`, and after normalization the diagonal cost is only about `2^(t+1)sqrt(t)/k_y`, which tends rapidly to zero at the first unresolved support.

The missing theorem can therefore be stated concretely. Prove a sparse fixed-weight shell estimate for the `2(t+1)`-th moment with loss `L_y=o(k_y/(2^t sqrt(t)))`. Equivalently, use the exact Krawtchouk expansion to control the off-diagonal Hamming-distance distribution of Legendre signatures of `m`-fold lower-prime squarefree kernels. Polylogarithmic loss would already be more than sufficient. Ordinary all-moduli tensorized large sieve does not have the right coefficient-length/family-size balance.

The alternative exit remains logarithmic all-class generation or a direct source-scale theorem for the single surviving blind character. These are genuinely different mechanisms; success in one should not be inferred from generic pseudorandomness in another.

## Keep conductor descent, support order, family entropy and moment amplification separate

A smaller conductor does not improve the source exponent if the projector pays the same factor. Pairwise or low-moment balance does not determine whether a single support-`t` blind relation survives. Conversely, MC-264 shows that the shell family is not doomed merely by its cardinality: support-matched high moments can cross the single-atom detection threshold before any new arithmetic estimate is supplied. The next analytic result must exploit that exact fixed-weight family rather than hide it inside a larger modulus ensemble.
