MC-423--MC-430 show that tangent dimension, factor multiplicity and fixed-dimensional additive summaries cannot manufacture polynomial state capacity without paying retained precision, while full labelled prime-block incidence has Bell-number capacity `N_r^(1-o(1))` along primorials.

MC-431--MC-433 price finite-type color quotients and then general relational symmetry. If a retained relation `R` has automorphism group `G`, then `Q_R>=B_r/|G|`, and `log|G|=o(r log r)` already preserves near-full primorial-scale Bell capacity. A sparse bounded-degree relation can therefore retain every factor state; relation count and local degree are not information budgets.

MC-434 shows that even **canonical rigidity is not yet source information**. The directed successor order on the prime divisors is intrinsic and rigid, so it preserves the full Bell family internally, but at fixed `r=omega(n)` every such relation has the same isomorphism type. The scaffold provides perfect coordinate addressability with zero conditional source-type diversity, and ordinary factorwise Möbius sign then erases the rank positions anyway.

The live capacity/analytic audit is therefore three-stage. First compute the automorphism quotient of the datum that actually survives. Then ask how its isomorphism type varies among arithmetic sources after coarse invariants are fixed. Finally trace that source-varying residue through coefficient formation/projection and show that it contributes to a signed cancellation estimate rather than merely decoding the source.

## Separate description sparsity, quotient entropy, source diversity and analytic usefulness

A small alphabet is meaningful for color quotients because the quotient action is explicit. A sparse general relation can individualize every prime coordinate; conversely, a canonical rigid relation can still be universal up to isomorphism across sources. Neither incidence count nor rigidity alone prices arithmetic information.

The surviving relational escape is therefore a canonical decoration whose **source-varying part** is explicit: for example gaps, ratios, residues, weights or another arithmetic relation that varies among equal-`omega` sources. That variation must be priced for precision and symmetry and must survive the Möbius-facing projection. Passing the Bell-capacity or canonical-generation gate alone is representation capacity, not cancellation.
