# Möbius-cancellation research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Möbius-cancellation intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Preserve principal information before centering, then couple it with cheaper source structure

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category`, `MI-014-centered-parity-and-zero-mode-are-separate-currencies`.

MC-188--MC-194 show that canonical equivariant, CRT, character, and additive-image rearrangements remain sector-diagonal and cannot lend transverse cancellation to the rough principal mean. MC-195--MC-198 then test nonlinear escape routes. Pointwise polynomial mixing collapses back to the original Mertens scalar, while residue aggregation produces genuine higher interactions only by exposing same-residue pair/triple source correlations; centering does not algebraically erase those interactions.

MC-199 identifies a stronger boundary that precedes all such nonlinear processing. The centered residue profile is invariant under a constant shift, so the signed principal sum is not identifiable from that quotient at all. Explicit ternary matched controls can have identical centered data and support but different principal sums. Therefore **no linear or nonlinear functional of the centered profile can recover the discarded zero mode**.

The live route must retain an uncentered/source-sensitive degree of freedom before the quotient, or import an inter-site, inter-scale, factorization, or conservation relation that couples that retained principal coordinate to transverse structure at a cost strictly below the target Mertens bound. If the proposed mechanism first centers away the principal mode, later nonlinear sophistication cannot repair the information loss.

## Treat transverse dispersion, pointwise nonlinear closure, and centered-profile processing as controls

Better cancellation in nonprincipal sectors does not control the rough zero mode. Scalar pointwise nonlinearities add no new ternary information, aggregated nonlinearities pay explicit growing-modulus correlation bills, and arbitrary processing after the centered quotient is information-theoretically blind to the principal sum. Any viable escape must state what principal/source information survives before centering and why the relation controlling it is cheaper than the original cancellation problem.
