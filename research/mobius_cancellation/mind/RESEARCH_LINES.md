# Möbius-cancellation research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Möbius-cancellation intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Convert forced source-incidence cost into an arithmetic realizability obstruction beyond generic half-rate coding

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category` through `MI-041-smooth-modulus-zero-free-control-stops-above-the-dyadic-shell-scale`.

MC-307--MC-317 reduce the generic endpoint to a constant-defect generalized-Hamming/code problem and then close it in the permissive direction: sufficiently large full binary `[2R,R]` codes realize the required profile. Length, dimension, generalized weights, projective occupancy, erasure list size and systematic submatrix ranks therefore do not distinguish the arithmetic source from matched binary controls.

MC-319 supplies the first concrete source-specific analytic property of the forced common-radical regime. At balanced rank `2^R asymp log y` and the natural source scale, the modulus `q=4P` is smooth enough for collective Dirichlet-`L` zero-free control, up to the usual possible exceptional real character. This is genuinely absent from arbitrary half-rate codes.

However, the known conversion is too coarse. The Rodosskii-type prime-sum output is only `O((loglog q)^(-1/2))` on broad power intervals, whereas the endpoint needs a dyadic-shell signal of order `1/log y` at exponent resolution `Theta(1/log y)`. The live theorem is therefore not merely “prove a zero-free region”; it is to turn the smooth common modulus into **dyadic-resolution character cancellation** strong enough to exclude the arithmetic endpoint, or to find another source invariant whose endpoint cost survives the MC-317 controls.

## Repair probabilistic Möbius comparators only after exact-stratum and algebraic-collapse gates

**Linked intuition:** `MI-040-probabilistic-mobius-proxies-must-survive-exact-arithmetic-consistency`.

MC-318 shows that a recent probabilistic Möbius route fails before any delicate asymptotic estimate: conditioning on `Omega(n)=1` forces squarefreeness, contradicting the asserted independence, and the proposed auxiliary sum collapses exactly to `lambda(n)M(x)`. A repaired route must use a dependence law valid on a non-degenerate stratum and an auxiliary observable whose exact multiplicative simplification still leaves information beyond `M(x)`.

## Treat analytic rigidity and endpoint resolution as separate source currencies

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-039-generic-half-rate-codes-realize-the-constant-defect-endpoint`, `MI-041-smooth-modulus-zero-free-control-stops-above-the-dyadic-shell-scale`.

MC-317 says generic matrix existence is not the obstruction. MC-319 says a promising arithmetic replacement can still stop one scale too early. A source restriction is useful only after it is converted into the exact shell/precision norm consumed by the endpoint. Broad zero-free control, determinism, Legendre provenance or structural sparsity are not coercivity by themselves.

Future arguments should therefore simplify source observables first, preserve the random-linear matched control at the coding endpoint, identify the extra arithmetic theorem, and then price the theorem at dyadic endpoint resolution before claiming a gain.
