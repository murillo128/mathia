## Separate description sparsity, quotient entropy, source diversity, interaction depth and analytic usefulness

A small alphabet is meaningful only after the actual quotient is specified. Sparse incidence can rigidify every coordinate; a universal rigid scaffold can still carry zero source diversity; global ordinal relations can recover factorial variation only by paying identity-scale information; and Möbius convolution can erase that information if it lives below full prime interaction order or in a low-complexity carrier.

The path-distance sequence adds four audits. Universal coefficient signs are not cancellation; fixed-rank continuous shape sensitivity is not arithmetic separation when prime-derived shapes are dense in the ambient positive cone; polynomial fixed-rank realization cost removes a generic source-height excuse for that density; and none of these representation facts supplies destination cancellation. A surviving mechanism must therefore state which resource remains after scale quotienting—frequency, measure, growing-rank complexity, absolute scale, integrality or another arithmetic relation—and prove that this resource both survives the exact divisor interaction and reaches the destination at a useful scale.
