## Beat the extremal Fejér diagonal with source-specific off-diagonal cancellation, or leave the positive stationary cone

**Linked intuition:** `MI-073-source-mode-averaging-collapses-to-a-signature-projector`.

MC-414 localizes the surviving character information in the thin divisor-dependent endpoint domain. MC-415 shows that ordinary positive differencing re-squares the sieve on the zero-shift diagonal. MC-416 optimized that diagonal among single filters; MC-417 extends the result to every scalar positive translation-invariant quadratic kernel of bandwidth `H` by Fejér--Riesz factorization. After DC normalization, `K(0)/P_K(0) >= 1/H`, and the ordinary Fejér kernel is the unique extremizer.

The live estimate must therefore either extract incomplete bilinear-character gain from the thin endpoint domain large enough to compensate for this unavoidable diagonal, or change architecture so that the quadratic closure is not a scalar positive finite-band Toeplitz kernel. Indefinite correlations, nonstationary/modulus-factorized differencing or higher-dimensional arithmetic objects are relevant only when their replacement control and resulting diagonal are explicit.

## Do not count a new positive-kernel presentation as a new escape

A filter bank, positive Toeplitz form, sum of positive shift energies or nonnegative trigonometric multiplier does not enlarge the scalar finite-band cone: Fejér--Riesz refactors it into one filter. Any claimed diagonal saving must therefore come from arithmetic off-diagonal terms or from genuinely leaving that cone, not from repackaging the same positivity.
