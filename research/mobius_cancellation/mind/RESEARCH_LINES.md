This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Möbius-cancellation evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Move fixed-rank source geometry to a genuinely retained arithmetic resource

MC-423--MC-445 separate source diversity, representation fidelity, Möbius top-interaction and natural-source measure. Fixed-rank normalized prime-gap geometry is dense in the ambient simplex and polynomially realizable, while the most direct bounded-height natural ensemble concentrates on a terminal simplex vertex. Continuous fixed-rank normalized observables therefore do not acquire robust prime specificity merely from support richness or from switching to the canonical counting measure.

The surviving resources are qualitatively different: growing rank, another justified source measure, absolute scale, discontinuous arithmetic data, cross-rank structure, or coordinates that retain enough factor identity to survive the Möbius projection. Any such carrier still has to produce an analytic cancellation estimate after the exact top-interaction quotient.

## Use residual factor variables only after removing the redundant gcd-sector coordinate

MC-446--MC-453 close generic fixed-lcm occupancy, bare frequency synchronization, final beta-sieve sign variation, raw truncated Möbius as an all-splits well-factorable sequence, and bounded component multiplicity after separate positive quadratic closure. A conductor-power gain must therefore enter through a genuine auxiliary factor representation before generic recombination, majorization or squaring destroys the retained variables.

MC-458--MC-462 locate that resource more precisely. Staged factor conditioning leaves a residual divisor-sum kernel whose divisibility concentration is governed by the normalized source shift. Expanding one residual divisor promotes that shift into the character source, while the starts fall into bounded anchor fibres. On a fixed anchor the character kernel has an exact shift-energy identity, so a gain is possible only through enough effective weighted participation inside the fibre, not through nominal residual-divisor count.

MC-463 removes a redundant coordinate from that picture. For a fixed outer pair, write `G=(h_0,r_2)` and `T=r_2/G`. Every compatible residual gcd sector `d=(s,r_2)` normalizes to the same anchor modulus `T`; after writing `s=d s_0`, the source pair, interval and still-unexpanded residual-amplitude trajectory depend on `s_0` but not on `d`. The original coefficients must therefore be aggregated first into `\widetilde\beta(s_0)`. In the clean regime `T<p`, one anchor contains at most `1+S/T` reduced source points, so the bare fixed-anchor mechanism can only help after the outer-gcd gate `1+S/T>p/H`, equivalently at scale `S H (h_0,r_2) \gtrsim p r_2` away from endpoints.

The live q-vdC question is therefore not whether favourable internal gcd sectors can be averaged. It is whether outer pairs/shifts with sufficiently large `G` carry enough total weight, whether the **aggregated** coefficients `\widetilde\beta(s_0)` have effective reciprocal-shift participation above the `p/H` tariff inside the surviving anchor fibres, and whether a joint dispersion/completion estimate retains a conductor power with the aligned amplitude `B_\beta(C_{s_0}+r_1s_0j)` still unexpanded. Conversely, proving that the outer-gcd configurations are too sparse or that aggregation/aligned amplitude destroys the required participation would close this staged factorability route. The accepted q-vdC clue already records this narrowed test; no independent gcd-sector resource remains.
