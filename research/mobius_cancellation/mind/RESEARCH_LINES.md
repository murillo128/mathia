## Test structure beyond generic lcm synchronization and Fourier recombination

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-089-ambient-cancellation-helps-a-source-family-only-after-its-weighted-image-pays-the-occupancy-tariff`, `MI-090-fixed-lcm-beta-sieve-signs-remain-coherent-after-recombination`, `MI-091-well-factorability-must-be-carried-by-an-auxiliary-mobius-representation`.

MC-446 computes the exact full-box second moment of the incomplete translated character correlations left by MC-413. The ambient `(u,a)` family has genuine `sqrt(H)` RMS cancellation, but plain Cauchy transfers it to weighted source frames only when their effective occupancy exceeds about `q^2/H`.

MC-447 shows that in the separated range `d,e<=D`, `D^2<q`, `(h,q)=1`, the frame determines the exact lcm and CRT residue. Collisions are elementary and disappear for shifts coprime to the sieve support; fixing the constrained shift coordinate is simply fixing `L`.

MC-448 then prices that fixed-coordinate problem correctly. A mixed-Weil Fourier envelope plus Parseval lowers the generic occupancy tariff to order `p/H`, but a fixed-lcm CRT slice has only `2^omega(L)=p^(o(1))` distinct starts. This closes every fixed-power incomplete regime `H<=p^(1-delta)` for that generic method.

MC-449 removes the near-complete caveat. After centering away the universal complete-period scalar, length `H` is exactly dual to complementary length `p-H` up to translation of the source weights. The generic bound is controlled by `m(H)=min(H,p-H)`, so the occupancy tariff is `p/m(H)`. Near both endpoints the source slice is polynomially deficient; in the central range its subpolynomial occupancy can yield at most a subpolynomial conductor saving. The entire **one-fixed-lcm, uniform Fourier-envelope plus generic Parseval/Cauchy route is therefore power-closed for all interval lengths**.

MC-450 tests the next apparent escape directly. The exact dilation covariance `widehat g_(h/L)(mL)=widehat g_h(m)` really does synchronize fixed-lcm slices on a common frequency variable, but after full recombination the synchronized dual expression is exactly the Fourier representation of the original centered additive-difference autocorrelation. Generic dual Cauchy is therefore the same estimate as physical-space Cauchy, not a new cancellation mechanism. Moreover a fixed-lcm CRT start set has a deterministic low-frequency resonant band unless the relevant coefficient zero mode is cancelled.

MC-451 shows that the final classical beta-sieve coefficients cannot provide that zero-mode cancellation. For `lambda_d=mu(d)rho_d`, `rho_d>=0`, every compatible squarefree pair on a fixed lcm `L` has `de=L` and hence `mu(d)mu(e)=mu(L)`. All fixed-lcm weights have one sign, so the recombined zero mode is maximally coherent: `|A_L|=V_L`. The visible Möbius signs in the divisor variables do not survive this quotient as a cancellation resource.

MC-452 closes a different but closely related shortcut: the raw truncated Möbius sequence is not a standard all-splits well-factorable weight. For the balanced split `Q=(p-1)^2=(p-1)(p-1)`, any convolution of factors supported on `[1,p-1]` vanishes at the prime `p`, whereas `mu(p)=-1`. Thus the retained factorable variables used by a dispersion estimate must come from an auxiliary representation before scalarization; they are not latent in the raw finite Möbius prefix.

The accepted q-vdC clue must now specify both where its retained variables come from and how their estimate transfers back to the signed Möbius target. Surviving possibilities include an auxiliary signed/factorable decomposition, conductor factorization, higher-order CRT/cycle geometry, structured cancellation across several lcm values, genuinely collective variable-length structure, or weaker/approximate/multistage factorization that is proved for the actual transformed coefficients. A positive sieve majorant alone is insufficient unless a quantitative signed transfer to `M(x)` is established.

More ambient moments, hidden fibre counts, treating `a` as free, moving toward a complete interval, bare frequency synchronization, final beta-sieve sign variation, or simply declaring raw truncated Möbius well factorable are closed as conductor-power mechanisms in the present framework.

## Separate description sparsity, source measure, quotient entropy, interaction depth and analytic usefulness

Small alphabets, rich support, high nominal pair count and favorable ambient averages are all representation-level quantities until the relevant quotient is taken. MC-445 shows that support and natural-source measure can point in opposite directions; MC-446--MC-451 show that ambient moment strength, constrained-coordinate improvement, source-image occupancy, synchronized frequencies and nominal coefficient signs can do the same; MC-452 adds that a useful auxiliary convolution structure must be verified for the exact coefficient object to which the analytic theorem is applied.

A surviving mechanism must state the resource after scale quotienting and Möbius interaction extraction, specify the source measure or weighted image actually used, remove universal periodic modes before pricing the remainder, identify any auxiliary factorable variables before lossy recombination, and prove that their signed information reaches Möbius **before a generic recombination or majorization erases it**. Representation richness, frequency synchronization, sign variation or factorability of a neighboring surrogate does not count by itself.
