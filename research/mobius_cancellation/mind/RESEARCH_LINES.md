## Control the low-order primary multiplicities that survive the shell occupancy tradeoff, or cancel the exact rough Möbius coset

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category`, `MI-014-centered-parity-and-zero-mode-are-separate-currencies`, `MI-015-sign-support-covariance-gives-a-conditioned-quadratic-bridge`, `MI-016-source-aligned-shifts-face-a-defect-or-mertens-dichotomy`, `MI-017-first-shell-crt-common-mode-is-a-source-coupled-energy-coordinate`, `MI-018-nonnegative-transference-does-not-create-fixed-power-sign-balance`, `MI-019-shell-square-blindness-is-a-cross-prime-common-quotient-problem`.

MC-238--MC-246 identify smooth-dilation blindness with the annihilator of the subgroup `H` generated by the small primes and invert the complete annihilator: the whole blind source is one rough Möbius multiplicative coset `-H`. Blind-character count cancels exactly against coset density. The structural target is the quotient `Q=G/H`.

MC-247--MC-249 show that generic witness information first constrains active support and then, through shell progression occupancy, prime-power torsion order. Large torsion is increasingly expensive, but small exponent alone does not force generation.

MC-250 now couples **torsion level and primary multiplicity**. For `d=ell^a`, the order-`ell` shadow of the level-`a` blind torsion is an `F_ell` code supported on shell primes `r=1 mod d`, of dimension exactly `m_(ell,a)` and minimum distance at least the active-support floor `D_y`. Singleton gives

`D_y + m_(ell,a) - 1 <= #{r in S : r=1 mod ell^a}`.

Thus the exact occupancy slack bounds the multiplicity at each torsion level. Combined with Brun--Titchmarsh this yields an order--rank tradeoff `ell^a <= (4+o(1)) y/(R log R)`, `R=D_y+m_(ell,a)-1` (with leading `2` for large prime `ell`). High torsion order and high primary multiplicity cannot coexist freely.

The surviving obstruction is concentrated at **fixed low-order torsion**, where the compatible shell remains large and generic coding theory still permits high dimension at small relative distance. The next theorem must use simultaneous arithmetic relations among those shell coordinates, sharpen selected-shell occupancy far beyond coarse progression counting, or bypass generation altogether by proving fixed-power signed cancellation for the exact rough Möbius coset / complete-annihilator aggregate.

## Keep support, torsion order, occupancy slack, primary multiplicity, quotient index and signed amplitude separate

MC-250 closes the previous statement that primary rank is wholly uncoupled from the support information: at each torsion level, support plus exact order-compatible shell occupancy gives a sharp Singleton multiplicity bound. But this is not full generation. Large low-order rank can still survive when the progression shell has large slack, and neither the resulting quotient index nor the signed rough-coset amplitude is controlled. Future work must attack that residual arithmetic structure rather than treating a squeezed exponent or a coding bound as near-triviality.
