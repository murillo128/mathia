# Möbius-cancellation research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Möbius-cancellation intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Convert primitive source-conductor cost into dyadic prime-shell cancellation beyond common-modulus controls

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category` through `MI-041-smooth-modulus-zero-free-control-stops-above-the-dyadic-shell-scale`.

MC-307--MC-317 reduce the generic endpoint to a constant-defect generalized-Hamming/code problem and then close it in the permissive direction: sufficiently large full binary `[2R,R]` codes realize the required profile. Length, dimension, generalized weights, projective occupancy, erasure list size and systematic submatrix ranks therefore do not distinguish the arithmetic source from matched binary controls.

MC-319 supplies a genuine arithmetic property of the forced common-radical regime. At balanced rank `2^R asymp log y` and the natural source scale, the modulus `q=4P` is smooth enough for collective Dirichlet-`L` zero-free control, up to the usual possible exceptional real character. But its known Rodosskii conversion is too coarse: it controls broad power intervals at a scale far larger than the one-dyadic-shell `1/log y` signal consumed by the endpoint.

MC-320 now shows that replacing this by stronger **ordinary interval** character cancellation still attacks the wrong observable. A character induced from primitive conductor `4` to a source-scale-matched smooth common modulus has polylogarithmically bounded sums on every polynomial-length integer interval, yet it is identically `+1` on a full `1 mod 4` prime shell and therefore has the maximal harmonic signal of order `1/log y`. Common-modulus smoothness plus almost-perfect unweighted interval pseudorandomness is not a dyadic prime-shell discriminator.

The remaining arithmetic feature is therefore more specific: the endpoint characters are primitive quadratic characters built from costly lower-prime source products, whereas the MC-320 control has tiny primitive conductor and only an inflated ambient modulus. The live theorem is to convert that **primitive conductor/source provenance**, possibly together with the smooth common radical and family structure, into a genuinely prime-sensitive dyadic estimate for at least one endpoint-biased nonexceptional mode. A theorem that depends only on the ambient modulus or on `sum chi(n)` over ordinary intervals cannot close the gate.

## Repair probabilistic Möbius comparators only after exact-stratum and algebraic-collapse gates

**Linked intuition:** `MI-040-probabilistic-mobius-proxies-must-survive-exact-arithmetic-consistency`.

MC-318 shows that a recent probabilistic Möbius route fails before any delicate asymptotic estimate: conditioning on `Omega(n)=1` forces squarefreeness, contradicting the asserted independence, and the proposed auxiliary sum collapses exactly to `lambda(n)M(x)`. A repaired route must use a dependence law valid on a non-degenerate stratum and an auxiliary observable whose exact multiplicative simplification still leaves information beyond `M(x)`.

## Treat analytic rigidity, primitive provenance and endpoint resolution as separate source currencies

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-039-generic-half-rate-codes-realize-the-constant-defect-endpoint`, `MI-041-smooth-modulus-zero-free-control-stops-above-the-dyadic-shell-scale`.

MC-317 says generic matrix existence is not the obstruction. MC-319 says source-generated smoothness and zero-free control can stop one scale too early. MC-320 says even much stronger integer-interval cancellation can coexist with maximal prime-shell bias after primitive-conductor information is discarded. A source restriction is useful only after it is converted into the exact prime-weighted shell/precision norm consumed by the endpoint.

Future arguments should therefore simplify source observables first, preserve the random-linear matched control at the coding endpoint, identify the extra arithmetic theorem at the **primitive** source level, and then price that theorem at dyadic prime-shell resolution before claiming a gain. Broad zero-free control, common-modulus smoothness, determinism, Legendre provenance, structural sparsity or unweighted interval cancellation are not coercivity by themselves.
