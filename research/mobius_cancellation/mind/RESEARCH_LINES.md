# Möbius-cancellation research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Möbius-cancellation intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they separate genuinely different mechanisms.

## Exploit coupled signed feedback without isolating the inverse kernel or its first reciprocal block

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-008-moving-comparators-need-uniform-family-coherence`.

MC-066 identifies the nonnegative quadratic feedback kernel. MC-067--MC-069 exclude ordinary low-conductor motion and reduce the quasi-subpower positive corridor to at most the Landau--Page exceptional character. MC-070 then uses the kernel mean and Siegel's `L(1,chi)` lower bound to close that residual positive route throughout every fixed range `q<=X^A`, `A<2`; together with the Munsch comparator estimate, the exact positive-triangle package has a method-specific certification floor `theta>=7/8`.

The natural signed inverse is not a cheap replacement. MC-071 shows that standalone power cancellation of its partial sums already forces zero-free regions for both `zeta` and `L(s,chi)`, while MC-072 shows that even the first dyadic reciprocal annulus carries the same burden by telescoping. A decisive continuation must therefore prove cancellation in the **coupled recovery** `sum k_chi(d)F_chi(X/d)`, between reciprocal blocks, or in an equivalent signed recurrence without assigning RH-scale bounds to the inverse pieces separately.

## Derive an iterable strict contraction from the exact signed annular residual

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-005-scale-doubling-is-exponent-neutral-without-new-signed-information`.

Repeated scale doubling can amplify a genuine subunit contraction, but the contraction is not supplied by algebraic degree. A positive theorem must control the exact Huxley--Watt product-annulus residual with summable iteration losses and global scale coverage without splitting off an RH-equivalent coarse mode.

## Keep comparator turnover and scale coverage in any alternative moving-family theorem

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-008-moving-comparators-need-uniform-family-coherence`.

The quadratic positive-feedback corridor is now closed far beyond the earlier exceptional-character range, but alternative comparator families can still move with scale. Any such route must quantify how the chosen family persists or turns over, how transfer constants enter the exponent, and which signed coupling survives across the whole scale range. Per-scale existence remains insufficient.

## Treat asymptotically fixed Mellin drift as information-neutral unless the residual coupling changes

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-006-asymptotically-fixed-mellin-drift-is-information-neutral`.

Small deterministic Mellin shifts or slowly varying normalizations do not create new signed information when they can be absorbed into the existing annular/coarse decomposition. A useful deformation must alter the coupled residual rather than reparameterize the same scalar carrier.
