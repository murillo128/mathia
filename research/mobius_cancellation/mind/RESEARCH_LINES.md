# Möbius-cancellation research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Möbius-cancellation intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Find a localized bipartite invariant beyond bounded-order parity and full Rédei rank, or cancel the exact rough Möbius coset

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category`, `MI-014-centered-parity-and-zero-mode-are-separate-currencies`, `MI-015-sign-support-covariance-gives-a-conditioned-quadratic-bridge`, `MI-016-source-aligned-shifts-face-a-defect-or-mertens-dichotomy`, `MI-017-first-shell-crt-common-mode-is-a-source-coupled-energy-coordinate`, `MI-018-nonnegative-transference-does-not-create-fixed-power-sign-balance`, `MI-019-shell-square-blindness-is-a-cross-prime-common-quotient-problem`.

MC-238--MC-246 reduce complete blindness to one rough Möbius coset and the quotient `Q=G/H`. MC-247--MC-251 translate support, prime-power torsion and shell occupancy into coding constraints, but generic fixed-low-order rank pressure stays subcritical.

MC-252 shows that quadratic reciprocity without localization is universal enough to realize arbitrary finite binary matrices. MC-253 then proves that the actual moving shell has genuine fixed-order Legendre pseudorandomness, but a logarithmic-rank Walsh control satisfies the same qualitative bounded-order parity laws. Fixed-order moments are therefore still rank-blind.

MC-254 now closes ordinary Rédei/class-group rank as a shortcut as well. The lower-prime/shell-prime Legendre matrix is exactly an off-diagonal Rédei block `A`, but the full Rédei kernel sees only `ker A ∩ ker E`, where `E` is the shell-shell block. Genuine prime-discriminant path examples have maximal full Rédei rank, hence zero narrow `4`-rank, while the chosen bipartite cross-block has rank one. Full class-group `4`-rank can therefore be perfect while the shell generation problem remains almost completely unresolved.

The live algebraic theorem must preserve the **localized bipartite interaction** that both controls lose: a shell-specific restriction directly on `A`, a theorem controlling `E` on `ker A`, higher Rédei/Kummer data that provably constrains this cross-block at moving-shell scale, or growing parity order deep enough to see possible blind relations. The alternative remains to bypass generation and prove signed cancellation for the exact rough Möbius coset.

## Keep localization, parity order, cross-block rank, full class-group rank and signed amplitude separate

MC-252 says residue-symbol arithmetic without localization is too flexible. MC-253 says localization creates bounded-order Fourier structure but that structure does not determine code rank. MC-254 says even maximal rank of the **completed** Rédei matrix does not determine the lower/shell cross-block rank. A useful invariant must fail all three controls: separated-prime universality, low-rank bounded-order shell-like pseudorandomness, and genuine-prime maximal-full-rank/low-cross-rank Rédei completion. Pairwise near-orthogonality, fixed-order moments, ordinary `4`-rank, bipartite generation and signed Möbius cancellation are distinct currencies.
