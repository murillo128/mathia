# Möbius-cancellation research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Möbius-cancellation intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Match compulsory reverse discrimination to the actual endpoint channel

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category` through `MI-059-fixed-component-mixtures-pay-their-source-tariff-in-the-mixing-law`.

The reciprocal endpoint problem has been reduced from syntactic complexity to invariant source discrimination. Bounded coefficient energy with nontrivial all-mode dephasing forces linear joint source information. On the natural source graph, destination-relative reliability charges only edges that cross the relevant quotient, while the canonical channel-law quotient itself must have an extensive exact boundary.

MC-351--MC-353 identify the compulsory local currency: neighboring source states must carry a nontrivial half-Jeffreys/privacy-loss budget. MC-354 prices that budget for additive log-smooth readouts through mean displacement and noise curvature. MC-355 closes the Gaussian location-scale side-channel escape: source-dependent covariance contributes an exact positive affine-invariant Jeffreys term in addition to the symmetric whitened mean displacement.

MC-356 shows that this accounting is not Gaussian-specific. For any finite-dimensional regular exponential-family intermediate readout with source-independent support and downstream kernel, half-Jeffreys divergence before post-processing is exactly one half of the integrated Fisher action along the natural-parameter edge,

`F_e = int_0^1 Delta_e^T I(theta_e(t)) Delta_e dt`,

and downstream half-Jeffreys is no larger. Combining this with the endpoint lower budget forces `E_e F_e >= 2 eta^2/C-o(1)` under bounded coefficient energy and fixed nontrivial dephasing. Changing Bernoulli probabilities, Poisson rates, categorical weights, Gaussian natural parameters or other regular sufficient-statistic laws therefore does not create a free smooth non-Gaussian side channel.

MC-357 closes a further mixture loophole that MC-356 deliberately left open. If a source-dependent output mixture is generated by first drawing a latent law `nu_x` and then applying a **source-independent** component kernel, data processing gives `b_e(Y)<=b_e(Theta)`. The endpoint lower tariff therefore transfers directly to the mixing law. For finite categorical mixtures this becomes an explicit weighted-simplex motion budget, while exact neighboring transcript singularity forces singular latent laws and hence disjoint categorical supports. A complicated fixed component bank cannot provide source discrimination for free merely because the resulting output density is not an exponential family.

The live bridge is now more sharply channel-specific. For an actual Möbius endpoint architecture, either prove that its transcript factors through one of the priced regular/fixed-component classes and show that the corresponding Fisher/Jeffreys or mixing-law action is too small, or derive an equally invariant reverse-divergence upper theorem for the class it actually occupies. The remaining mixture escape is no longer “non-exponential output density” by itself; it requires genuinely source-dependent components, singular/support-changing latent laws, adaptive structure or an additional source-dependent transcript path.

## Repair probabilistic Möbius comparators only after exact-stratum and algebraic-collapse gates

Probabilistic proxies remain secondary until their conditioning is arithmetically consistent and their observables survive exact multiplicative simplification. A model that becomes degenerate after conditioning on an exact arithmetic stratum, or whose auxiliary statistic collapses algebraically to `M(x)`, has not created an independent cancellation mechanism.

## Keep the resource ledger explicit

The current invariant chain distinguishes source loading, coefficient energy, exact source quotient, joint transcript information, destination-cut geometry, aggregate edge KL, privacy-loss distribution, inverse distinguishability, mean displacement, covariance deformation, Fisher action, latent mixing-law divergence, support singularity, score curvature/noise scale and side-channel structure. These are different resources.

A useful Möbius endpoint theorem must close the loop in both directions: the destination must force enough neighboring-state discrimination, and the concrete analytic channel must be shown unable to afford that discrimination at the relevant scale. MC-354--MC-357 supply that second direction for progressively broader channel classes; the remaining problem is to justify one of those classes for the arithmetic architecture or replace them by a channel-specific upper bound that survives the actual singularities, source-dependent components, adaptation or side channels.
