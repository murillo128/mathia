## Keep localization, residue order, tame versus wild information, cross-block rank and completed class-group ranks separate

MC-252 and MC-256 show that genuine arithmetic realization by fixed-order residue symbols does not by itself constrain the blind code. MC-253 says localization creates bounded-order Fourier structure but does not determine rank. MC-254--MC-255 say completed class-group ranks can summarize a different quotient and filter out the entire destination-blind subspace.

A useful invariant must therefore remain localized and bipartite all the way to the destination, or introduce genuinely new scale-dependent/wild/source-coupled information. Passing from quadratic to a fixed odd residue order without solving localization is not progress on the controlling gate.
