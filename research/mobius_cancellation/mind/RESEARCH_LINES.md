**Linked intuitions:** `MI-073-source-mode-averaging-collapses-to-a-signature-projector`, `MI-074-thin-hyperbolic-shells-tax-independent-coordinate-bandwidth`, `MI-075-exact-product-tangent-fibers-have-subpolynomial-state-volume`, `MI-076-fixed-depth-factor-lifts-cannot-amplify-the-short-shell-state-exponent`, `MI-077-factor-multiplicity-does-not-survive-a-mobius-sign-projection`, `MI-078-unbounded-additive-factor-data-buys-state-capacity-only-through-retained-precision`, `MI-079-permutation-invariant-factor-size-needs-inverse-logarithmic-precision-for-polynomial-state-capacity`, `MI-080-fixed-dimensional-symmetric-factor-data-obeys-a-dimension-precision-law`, `MI-081-exact-factor-identity-has-near-linear-orbit-capacity-but-mobius-sign-erases-it`, `MI-082-prime-mark-entropy-controls-factor-quotient-capacity`, `MI-083-sparse-relational-asymmetry-can-preserve-the-full-factor-state-exponent`, `MI-084-global-ordinal-prime-relations-can-saturate-source-capacity-only-by-paying-identity-scale-information`, `MI-085-mobius-divisor-convolution-sees-only-the-full-prime-interaction`, `MI-086-additive-consecutive-chain-statistics-lose-every-interior-motif-under-mobius-projection`, `MI-087-full-order-determinant-interaction-can-collapse-to-fixed-dimensional-additive-moments`, `MI-088-path-distance-determinants-retain-gap-order-only-below-two-permutation-blind-scale-layers`.

MC-423--MC-435 show that tangent dimension, factor multiplicity and bounded/local factor decorations do not provide polynomial conditional source diversity unless their retained information budget reaches the `Theta(r log r)` scale of prime identity. MC-436 shows that this threshold is attainable as a representation budget: the global ordinal pattern of adjacent selected-prime gaps realizes all `(r-1)! = N_r^(1-o(1))` types.

MC-437 adds the downstream survival gate. For a squarefree source `n=p_1...p_r`, a divisor statistic `F_n(T)` passes through arithmetic Möbius convolution only through its top Boolean coefficient

`I_F(n)=sum_(T subset [r]) (-1)^(r-|T|) F_n(T)`.

Every bounded-order interaction expansion is annihilated once `r` exceeds its interaction degree, even if the representation is injective on all `2^r` divisor states. Raw source diversity, representation fidelity and Möbius interaction depth are therefore independent resources.

MC-438 closes the most immediate adjacency-dependent loophole. If a divisor statistic is a sum of a fixed kernel over consecutive `k`-blocks of the selected prime chain, then its full interaction keeps only blocks spanning both global endpoint primes. For `k=2` the entire statistic collapses to the single endpoint term `(-1)^(r-2)W(p_1,p_r)`. Selection-dependent adjacency can generate high Boolean degree, but additive local chain structure still loses every interior motif under the Möbius projection.

MC-439 then closes a different apparent escape: a globally nonadditive determinant can survive the full-interaction test and still collapse to a fixed-dimensional carrier. For the principal-minor statistic `A(T)=det K[T]`, the top interaction is `(-1)^r det(K-I_r)`. If `K=UV^T` has fixed separable feature rank `d`, Sylvester reduction gives `(-1)^r det(I_d-V^T U)`, whose entries are additive prime-feature moments. Full Boolean degree and nonlinear global dependence therefore do not by themselves certify retention of factorial gap-order information.

MC-440 tests the first canonical growing-rank escape. For the path-distance kernel on ordered prime locations, gap order genuinely survives at finite scale, but after common gap dilation `g->Xg` the `X^r` and `X^(r-1)` determinant coefficients depend only on the multiset through `S=sum g_i`, `P=prod g_i`, and `Q=sum 1/g_i`. Two permutations of the same fixed gap shape therefore first separate at `O_g(X^(r-2))`, a relative `O_g(X^(-2))` effect. Growing rank clears the MC-439 algebraic collapse but can still bury the ordinal carrier below permutation-blind leading layers.

The live gap-order branch must now satisfy three independent downstream tests. Its divisor statistic needs a nontrivial full interaction that does not collapse to endpoint motifs; its effective relational rank must genuinely grow rather than factor through fixed additive features; and the order-sensitive component must survive at a usable quantitative scale after universal leading layers are removed. Plausible exits are an intrinsically normalized or subtracted path-distance statistic that stably exposes the `r-2` layer, another growing-rank/set-dependent kernel with better leading sensitivity, or a different global functional. Only after that carrier survives should actual prime-gap realization and signed analytic control be investigated.

## Separate description sparsity, quotient entropy, source diversity, interaction depth and analytic usefulness

A small alphabet is meaningful only after the actual quotient is specified. Sparse incidence can rigidify every coordinate; a universal rigid scaffold can still carry zero source diversity; bounded local decorations can carry too little conditional variation; a global ordinal relation can recover factorial variation only by paying `Theta(r log r)` information; and Möbius divisor convolution can erase all of that information if it lives below full prime interaction order, only in additive local adjacency, inside a fixed-rank determinant wrapper, or only in a quantitatively subleading determinant layer.

The surviving relational escape is therefore not “use gaps”, “use a canonical order”, “use growing rank” or “use a determinant” in the abstract. It is a source-forced relation with an explicitly priced type budget and realization scale whose induced divisor statistic has a nontrivial full-order coefficient with demonstrably growing effective relational complexity **and** sufficient separation after the universal scale layers are removed, followed by an analytic estimate that turns that coefficient into cancellation. A different downstream operation remains possible, but then its own information-loss and conditioning map must be identified explicitly.
