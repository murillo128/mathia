This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Möbius-cancellation intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Prove an arithmetic estimate for the endpoint-optimal Christoffel detector

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category`, `MI-014-centered-parity-and-zero-mode-are-separate-currencies`, `MI-015-sign-support-covariance-gives-a-conditioned-quadratic-bridge`, `MI-016-source-aligned-shifts-face-a-defect-or-mertens-dichotomy`, `MI-017-first-shell-crt-common-mode-is-a-source-coupled-energy-coordinate`, `MI-018-nonnegative-transference-does-not-create-fixed-power-sign-balance`, `MI-019-shell-square-blindness-is-a-cross-prime-common-quotient-problem`, `MI-020-explicit-large-sieve-uniformity-stops-before-blind-support-scale`, `MI-021-support-matched-high-moments-cross-the-single-blind-shell-threshold`.

The conductor and fixed-order algebraic escapes are largely exhausted. MC-266--MC-267 show that the radial support hierarchy is just the Krawtchouk transform of the Hamming-weight distribution of the actual lower-prime Legendre sign vectors; universal recurrence and association-scheme algebra are information-neutral.

MC-268 sharpens the positive detection problem. A blind relation is the endpoint event `b_y(T)=0`, and the Christoffel extremal polynomial gives the minimum-binomial-mass degree-`m` square certificate for that endpoint. At the first unresolved support `t~(log log y)/(log 2)`, degree `2t` already reaches the natural one-atom scale, while degree `2(t+1)` leaves a baseline of order `(t+1)/k_y`, substantially more forgiving than the old monomial moment.

The live theorem is therefore not “obtain one more moment.” Prove arithmetic control of the explicit Christoffel-weighted combination of `H_0,...,H_(2m)`—or a stronger source-specific positive detector—using the actual Legendre sign image. At `m=t`, only a near-binomial relative error can work; at `m=t+1`, the admissible loss is much larger.

## Keep basis changes, detector efficiency and arithmetic information separate

MC-267 says Krawtchouk coordinates do not create information. MC-268 says the choice of positive detector still matters greatly: the monomial was wasting the available information. These are compatible statements. A detector can be optimal **conditional on the same low-support data** while the decisive estimate of that data remains entirely arithmetic.

Future work should therefore distinguish three layers: universal transform identities, optimal positive use of the truncated hierarchy, and source-specific control of the hierarchy itself. Only the third can exclude the blind support.
