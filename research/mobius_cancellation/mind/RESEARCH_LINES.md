# Möbius-cancellation research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Möbius-cancellation intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Prove shell-specific full generation or signed rough-Möbius cancellation on the exact multiplicative coset

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category`, `MI-014-centered-parity-and-zero-mode-are-separate-currencies`, `MI-015-sign-support-covariance-gives-a-conditioned-quadratic-bridge`, `MI-016-source-aligned-shifts-face-a-defect-or-mertens-dichotomy`, `MI-017-first-shell-crt-common-mode-is-a-source-coupled-energy-coordinate`, `MI-018-nonnegative-transference-does-not-create-fixed-power-sign-balance`, `MI-019-shell-square-blindness-is-a-cross-prime-common-quotient-problem`.

MC-238--MC-246 identify smooth-dilation blindness with the annihilator of the subgroup `H` generated by the small primes and then invert the complete annihilator: the entire blind source is one rough Möbius multiplicative coset `-H`. Blind-character count cancels exactly against coset density. The structural target is the actual quotient `G/H`; quadratic full rank only makes `[G:H]` odd.

MC-247 tests generic subgroup-witness information at the GRH/ERH `O(log^2 q)` scale. It forces surviving blind characters to have support `>>sqrt(y)/log y` but leaves that scale far below shell dimension `asymp y/log y`.

MC-248 makes the exponent obstruction exact. Any generic witness theorem of the form `W(q)<=C(log q)^A` with fixed `A>1` yields only `k >> y^(1/A)/log y = o(y/log y)`. Matched binary-code controls show that minimum distance at this scale is compatible with parity-check rank only `O(y^(1/A))=o(n)`, so the failure is in the information type, not merely a loose Hamming estimate. Improving constants, or even replacing `log^2 q` by `log^(1+delta) q`, cannot close shell generation by support alone.

The first generic power scale that can even reach linear support is `A=1`, but constant-fraction minimum distance still does not imply a trivial kernel. Moreover a uniform all-moduli `O(log q)` witness theorem is blocked already by classical lower bounds for least quadratic nonresidues. The generation exit must therefore use arithmetic special to the simultaneous moving shell family, not a progressively sharper generic least-witness theorem.

The two exact exits are now sharply separated: prove `H=G` from simultaneous shell arithmetic, or prove fixed-power signed cancellation for the one source-selected rough Möbius coset / complete-annihilator aggregate.

## Keep witness scale, support, quadratic rank, quotient index and signed amplitude separate

MC-244--MC-246 remove mode-count and smooth-weight proxies. MC-247--MC-248 show that even very strong generic witness information can make every blind mode highly nonlocal while leaving quotient capacity essentially unconstrained. Minimum support, quadratic rank, full quotient index, coset density, and signed rough-coset amplitude remain distinct currencies. Future work must control the quotient itself, simultaneous arithmetic dependencies among shell columns, or the final signed coset sum.
