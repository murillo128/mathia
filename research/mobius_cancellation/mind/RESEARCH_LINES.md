MC-293 collapses the low-source-cost near-negative spectrum to at most one Landau--Page exceptional functional. MC-294 forces many independent near-negative directions into a high-cost quotient, while MC-295 shows that a generic fixed-weight defect-shell model can still saturate the resulting coding bills.

MC-296 uses the endpoint defect geometry to price the **entire selected subset spectrum** rather than only a basis. In the disjoint equal-defect model, all noncentral subset sums are forced above the Page threshold except possibly the single exceptional mode.

MC-297 identifies the exact stability variable and corrects one boundary of that argument. Coverage of the shell is not needed for the MC-296 Fourier identity: pairwise disjointness alone gives it. With overlapping defect sets,

`x_(lambda_I)=(-1)^|I| (1-2d(I)+4 Omega_I)`,

where the nonnegative parity-folding correction satisfies `Omega_I<=B_I`, with `B_I` the total pair-overlap mass inside `I`. Below half defect mass, overlap cannot rescue a mode at all; above half mass, a Page-cheap mode must spend enough overlap to cancel the negative disjoint-profile bias. Layer averaging converts a global pair-overlap budget into an explicit bound on the multiplicity of cheap upper-half subset modes.

The live arithmetic gate is now twofold but concrete. Either prove that the actual Legendre-shell defect family has sufficiently small pair/parity overlap to force almost all selected subset modes Page-high-cost, or prove directly that an exponentially large family of such high-cost modes cannot be realized collectively by the lower-prime source. The first route needs an arithmetic overlap theorem; the second needs a source-cost/multiplicity, occupancy or quotient-capacity theorem. More generic Fourier, entropy or coding inequalities do not address either gate.

## Keep parity, overlap, individual source cost, subset-spectrum cost and collective realizability separate

MC-297 shows that incomplete coverage is harmless for the exact disjoint formula, whereas overlap is the genuine finite-harmonic escape. But overlap control alone would still only force many expensive modes; it would not say that their arithmetic realization is impossible. Conversely, a collective source-capacity theorem could close the branch without first proving near-disjoint defects.

Future arguments must therefore state which of these resources they control: defect mass, pair/multi-overlap, Page cost of individual functionals, multiplicity of expensive subset modes, or dimension/capacity of the arithmetic source. None can be substituted for another without a theorem.

## Preserve matched controls and classical Page/coding theory as falsifiers

The Geelen threshold, entropy inequalities, Hamming/Sperner bounds and Landau--Page input remain sharp controls, not arithmetic impossibility theorems. MC-295 remains the warning that a generic finite model may assign the whole selected span as expensive. MC-297 narrows the missing source input to actual overlap geometry or collective high-cost realizability in the Legendre shell.
