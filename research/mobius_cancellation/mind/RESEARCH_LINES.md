# Möbius-cancellation research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable Möbius-cancellation intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Recover first-absolute excursion from information that survives both depth and scalarization controls

**Linked intuition:** `MI-004-mean-absolute-cancellation-needs-excursion-coupled-information`.

The endpoint remains sharp: square-root-scale first-absolute Mertens control on any unbounded checkpoint sequence is RH-complete. MC-122--MC-153 close broad finite-state, topological, scalar-weighted, and every fixed finite prime-factor-depth summary. The parity-truncated controls show that exact local multiplicative structure through any prescribed finite depth can coexist with linear summatory bias.

MC-154--MC-155 remove the simplest proposed escape from that theorem. Passing from every fixed depth to the **entire all-depth hierarchy of scalar commutator magnitudes, row norms, and standard scalar aggregations** still does not recover cancellation: one support-matched biased control reproduces the whole hierarchy. Unbounded depth is therefore not by itself a new information category.

MC-156--MC-162 now classify the signed scalar direction much more sharply. A single-prime pulled-back signed commutator reconstructs the Möbius diagonal; tail-centering removes the eventual constant prime direction but every nonzero algebraic linear readout of the remaining `c_00` prime-divisor charge is still Mertens-complete across scales. The natural unit charge `mu(n)omega(n)` even has an unconditional source-side asymptotic while its inverse is power-unstable. Whenever the explicit absolute decoder contraction does hold at exponent `theta`, the transformed power bound is equivalent at the **same exponent** to `M(x)=O(x^theta)` and the transfer factor is already analytically nonmasking.

Thus scalar commutator information has no demonstrated intermediate quantitative regime: magnitude readouts are cancellation-blind; signed linear readouts are target-complete; and the available absolute-stability certificates, when strong enough to transfer a power bound back, give no weaker exponent than Mertens itself. A scalar transform could still matter if it admits an independent source theorem plus a genuinely signed arithmetic inverse beyond absolute contraction, but the contraction constant alone is not the advantage.

The live source should therefore be **genuinely relational and non-scalar**, unless such an independent signed inverse theorem is found. It should retain an interaction among prime channels that survives the all-depth magnitude controls but does not simply reconstruct the complete Möbius sign field or a same-exponent Mertens statement. Quantitative excursion transfer, not representational richness, is the decisive gate.

## Treat scalar Bost--Connes commutator regularity as a classified boundary

Prime-semigroup row weights, arbitrary `ell^q` aggregation, divisibility splitting, fixed-depth mixed differences, all-depth scalar magnitudes, signed scalar filters, and tail-centered algebraic linear functionals now form a nearly complete scalar control ladder. Increasing depth, changing scalar norms, quotienting the constant tail, or preserving orientation in a one-dimensional readout does not create a demonstrated intermediate arithmetic category.

A useful Bost--Connes continuation must make its non-scalar relation explicit before assigning cancellation meaning to it: for example a source-forced joint state geometry, operator-valued correlation, noncommuting module relation, or another representation-sensitive object whose retained information can be compared against matched biased controls. Merely hiding a target-complete decoder in a richer operator notation does not qualify.

## Treat finite-state vocabulary growth and scalar prime-factor vocabulary as closed until they change order topology

Adding context length, modular height, finitely many future steps, periodic phases, polynomial timestamp moments, scalar prime weights, any prescribed finite number of mixed prime-difference layers, the complete scalar norm hierarchy, or a linear tail-centered prime charge enlarges the description without establishing a cheaper excursion theorem. A local enrichment should first be tested for factorization through one of these classified summaries or for exact target reconstruction.

## Require the source theorem to be Möbius-specific but not target-equivalent

Generic controls need not satisfy Möbius multiplicativity, square-free support, prime values, or divisor identities, so exact arithmetic remains a legitimate escape. The converse warning is now stronger: a representation can encode arbitrarily deep Möbius-local structure and still miss additive cancellation, while a signed scalar commutator can cross directly to complete target recovery and an absolutely stable scalar decoder can collapse to same-exponent equivalence. The desired theorem lies strictly between those extremes.
