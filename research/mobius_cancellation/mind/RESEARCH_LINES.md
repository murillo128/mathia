## Close the explicit collective-capacity gap at the blind-support frontier

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category` through `MI-031-common-ramification-shifts-capacity-into-radical-size-or-roughness`.

MC-293--MC-297 move the problem from individual character cost to the selected subset spectrum. MC-298 shows that no fixed hierarchy of overlap marginals determines Page parity. MC-299--MC-300 price the pointwise arithmetic route: a strong Page exception triggers Deuring--Heilbronn repulsion, but with the available exceptional-zero input the uniform fixed-accuracy source band has a Lambert-W ceiling of order `log y log log log y/log log y` in log-conductor.

MC-301 supplies the distinct generic collective theorem that MC-300 left open. The multiplicative large sieve bounds selected source energy through conductor `sqrt(y)`. MC-302 exploits quadraticity and moves the natural collective conductor horizon to `y`, but the reciprocal endpoint-partition extremizer carries only about `log y/R` energy at the blind-support rank `R~log_2 log y`. Even an ideal loss-free quadratic large sieve of the same shape leaves an `O(log y)` capacity and therefore a factor-`R` gap.

MC-303 packages the independent selected modes into a degree-`2^R` multiquadratic field. The Page-forced source-cost spectrum makes the field discriminant enormous, so generic effective Chebotarev loses shell-scale uniformity. MC-304 then uses structure that generic field complexity forgets: all selected characters have conductors dividing one common squarefree ramification radical `P`, with exact discriminant identity

`|D_K|=P^(2^(R-1))`.

The fixed-modulus and sparse-character large sieves convert the reciprocal endpoint energy into a sharper dichotomy. Every reciprocal endpoint partition must pay either

`phi(P)/P >> 2^R/(R log y)`

or

`sqrt(P) log(2P) >> y/(R log y)`.

At the blind-support floor the second branch forces `P>=y^(2-o(1))`; the first requires the common radical to remain sufficiently totient-rough, at roughly the `1/R` scale up to the stated subpower slack. The live theorem is therefore source-specific and explicit: rule out one or both of these radical escape currencies for the actual selected family, or prove that the actual obstruction carries more energy/rank than the reciprocal extremizer.

## Keep pointwise exceptional-zero control, generic family energy, quadratic-family energy, field complexity and ramification capacity separate

Page/Deuring--Heilbronn controls individual modes strongly but only in a smaller conductor band. The generic multiplicative large sieve reaches square-root conductor collectively. The quadratic large sieve reaches linear conductor collectively, but its black-box mean-square capacity remains `O(log y)` at the relevant polynomial horizon. Multiquadratic packaging couples the modes algebraically, but generic Chebotarev pays through a discriminant inflated by degree replication.

MC-304 adds a different currency. A huge field discriminant does not imply a huge ramification radical: the Page-induced discriminant lower bound rewritten through `P` forces only `P=y^(o(1))` at the critical rank, far below the `P>=y^(2-o(1))` sparse-capacity branch. Field discriminant, radical size and reduced-residue density must therefore not be substituted for one another.

MC-300 is a pointwise certificate ceiling; MC-301--MC-302 are family-capacity ceilings; MC-303 is a black-box field-complexity obstruction; and MC-304 is a necessary source-specific radical dichotomy. None proves that the selected source family lacks a stronger arithmetic theorem.

## Preserve matched controls and exact energy accounting as falsifiers

The exchangeable controls of MC-295/MC-298 show that low-order combinatorial regularity can coexist with forbidden parity. The reciprocal endpoint partition gives the sharp finite-geometric energy profile against which collective arithmetic bounds should be tested. MC-303 adds the test that algebraic packaging may simply move source complexity into degree/discriminant. MC-304 sharpens that audit further: once the actual common radical is exposed, a successful closing theorem must change the `phi(P)/P` or `P` capacity balance, raise the forced endpoint energy, or supply a source coupling absent from the sparse-character bound. Repackaging the same selected modes without changing one of those currencies is not progress.
