## Beat the Fejér diagonal with source-specific off-diagonal cancellation, or leave one-variable stationary positivity

**Linked intuition:** `MI-073-source-mode-averaging-collapses-to-a-signature-projector`.

MC-414 localizes the surviving character information in the thin divisor-dependent endpoint domain. MC-415 shows that ordinary positive differencing re-squares the sieve on the zero-shift diagonal. MC-416--MC-417 optimize that diagonal over every scalar positive finite-band stationary kernel: after DC normalization the zero shift is at least `1/H`, with the Fejér kernel extremal.

MC-418 shows that positive matrix/operator-valued channel lifting does not change the floor. For every positive operator-valued bandwidth-`H` symbol, `K_0 >= P(0)/H` in Loewner order, independently of rank or channel dimension. Thus merely retaining more source coordinates through a one-variable stationary positive quadratic form cannot dilute the diagonal. The live estimate must either extract enough source-specific incomplete endpoint cancellation from the off-diagonal terms to pay this forced cost, or use an architecture genuinely outside this cone: nonstationary/modulus-factorized, multivariable, or indefinite with independent control.

## Do not count channel multiplicity or positive-kernel repackaging as a new escape

A scalar filter bank, positive Toeplitz form, sum of positive shift energies or nonnegative trigonometric multiplier stays inside the MC-417 cone. Replacing the scalar sequence by a finite- or infinite-dimensional source vector and using a positive stationary operator kernel stays inside the MC-418 cone as well. A claimed gain must therefore identify the arithmetic cross-channel off-diagonal theorem or the hypothesis of stationary one-variable positivity that has actually been left; adding channels alone is not a new resource.
