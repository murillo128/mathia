## Price factor-lift information after source projection and symmetry quotient

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-073-source-mode-averaging-collapses-to-a-signature-projector`, `MI-074-thin-hyperbolic-shells-tax-independent-coordinate-bandwidth`, `MI-075-exact-product-tangent-fibers-have-subpolynomial-state-volume`, `MI-076-fixed-depth-factor-lifts-cannot-amplify-the-short-shell-state-exponent`, `MI-077-factor-multiplicity-does-not-survive-a-mobius-sign-projection`, `MI-078-unbounded-additive-factor-data-buys-state-capacity-only-through-retained-precision`, `MI-079-permutation-invariant-factor-size-needs-inverse-logarithmic-precision-for-polynomial-state-capacity`.

MC-423--MC-424 show that fixed-depth exact-product fibers and whole short factor shells cannot manufacture an additional polynomial state exponent. MC-425 applies the source projection at arbitrary active depth: retaining each factor only through its Möbius sign leaves fewer than `2^omega(n)=X^(o(1))` source-sign words even when depth reaches its arithmetic maximum.

MC-426 closes every fixed-dimensional bounded additive prime-local summary. MC-427 then shows that simply making the additive weights unbounded does not remove the compression: for scalar factor log-size, every mesh with `eta^(-1)=(log X)^(o(1))` still gives only `X^(o(1))` ordered states, and polynomial ordered capacity already requires a genuinely growing precision budget.

MC-428 shows that ordered capacity can itself be a representation artifact. If the downstream mechanism is invariant under factor-slot permutations, the correct log-size state is an unordered multiset. Partition counting then gives `N_sym <= (r+1)(L+1)p(L)` with `L<=log X/eta`; every `eta^(-1)<=(log X)^(delta+o(1))` for fixed `delta<1` remains subpolynomial. Polynomial symmetric state capacity forces the much sharper threshold `eta^(-1)=Omega(log X)`.

The live question is consequently not whether a high-resolution factor label can be generated. A viable factor lift must identify which retained distinctions are physically observable after **both** arithmetic projection and every symmetry quotient. If factor slots are genuinely asymmetric, the asymmetry must be intrinsic and survive the analytic architecture; if the readout is symmetric, scalar size must reach inverse-logarithmic precision before polynomial orbit-state capacity is even combinatorially possible. Passing that threshold remains necessary, not sufficient, for cancellation.

## Audit labels, state count, retained precision and closure faces in the same coordinates

MC-423--MC-428 show that tangent dimension, raw factor multiplicity, Möbius-sign words, bounded additive summaries, coarsely quantized log-size and named factor slots are not independent bandwidth currencies.

Any proposed escape should remove unit padding, project to the datum actually consumed, quotient factor permutations unless the slots have a proved asymmetric role, price retained dimension and precision, and only then count independent channels. Exact factor identity, inverse-logarithmic scalar resolution, vector/nonadditive relations or intrinsic ordered roles may evade the newest counts, but none earns analytic credit until its information is shown to survive subsequent coefficient, shell and positivity/closure operations and to contribute to Möbius cancellation.
