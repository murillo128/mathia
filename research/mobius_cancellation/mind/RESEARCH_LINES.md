## Prove signed source discrepancy from actual arithmetic or leave the lcm semilattice frame

**Linked intuition:** `MI-073-source-mode-averaging-collapses-to-a-signature-projector`.

MC-397--MC-403 progressively consume source-mode averaging, divisor-pair multiplicity, lcm phase, moving endpoints, complete endpoints, generic truncation and finally the whole source family. The surviving nonprincipal family energy is exactly the squared norm of a Mertens-weighted signed signature-discrepancy vector. Formal family size is therefore not an independent cancellation resource.

MC-404 makes the representation boundary algebraic rather than merely quadratic. For any finite divisor set `D`, every finite Boolean or polynomial function of the incidence vector `(1_{d|n})_{d in D}` lies in the finite join-semilattice algebra generated by `e_d e_e=e_[d,e]`; equivalently it has a unique lcm-closure expansion. Higher-degree Selberg weights, threshold functions and other finite incidence-only nonlinearities can change coefficients and enlarge the lcm closure, but they cannot preserve a tuple variable through the downstream arithmetic.

The live route is correspondingly narrow. Either prove a source-specific theorem that makes the exact signed signature discrepancy small, or introduce information before the lcm quotient that genuinely changes a quotient, phase, congruence, translation, interval or other arithmetic object and is not constant on lcm fibres. Repackaging the same divisor-incidence algebra at higher degree is now ruled out as a source of new retained dimension.

## Keep formal dimension, semilattice normal form, retained arithmetic coupling and transferred difficulty distinct

A variable can survive notation while carrying no new information. Fourier mode labels can collapse to signature discrepancy; divisor tuples can collapse to lcm; endpoint truncation can invert to Mertens weights; and MC-404 shows that the entire finite divisor-incidence algebra already has the lcm semilattice as its exact normal form.

A future candidate should therefore be audited before any estimate is attempted: identify the proposed extra variable, reduce its incidence algebra exactly, apply the first correlation or regrouping that the proof will use, and ask whether two configurations with the same lcm data can still produce different downstream arithmetic. If not, the candidate is another coordinate system for the existing obstruction. Genuine progress requires a source relation that survives this quotient and then yields an estimate not equivalent to the Mertens/signature problem already present.
