## Earn near-tangent cross-fiber bandwidth before positive closure, not just factor coordinates

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-073-source-mode-averaging-collapses-to-a-signature-projector`, `MI-074-thin-hyperbolic-shells-tax-independent-coordinate-bandwidth`, `MI-075-exact-product-tangent-fibers-have-subpolynomial-state-volume`.

MC-414 localizes the surviving character information in the thin divisor-dependent endpoint domain. MC-415--MC-418 show that one-variable positive differencing re-squares the sieve on the zero shift and that finite-band stationary positivity pays the sharp Fejer `1/H` floor, unchanged by operator-valued channel lifting.

MC-419 identifies the genuine multivariable escape: independent translation axes can multiply positive-kernel bandwidth. MC-420 makes independence algebraic: if Fourier support lies in an injected rank-`r` lattice, the positive symbol factors through an `r`-torus, so ambient tensor coordinates do not increase effective dimension. MC-421 then shows that axes manufactured by iterated ordinary van der Corput bring coordinate-zero source-free square faces with reciprocal one-axis Fejer mass.

MC-422 adds the physical support budget. A full positive coordinate box inside a thin hyperbolic shell `X<prod u_j<=X+Delta` obeys `sum B_j/U_j <= Delta/P_0` and `prod B_j <= Delta^d/(d^d P_0^(d-1))`. For `P_0 asymp X` and `Delta=X^theta`, growing full `d`-axis volume requires `theta>1-1/d`. At the live `theta` near `0.55`, two-axis product bandwidth is at most `X^0.10`, and three independent positive integer widths cannot all grow.

MC-423 closes the most literal tangent escape left by that shell-normal obstruction. At fixed factor depth `d`, one exact product fiber `F_d(m)={u_1...u_d=m}` has exactly `d_d(m)` states and `d_d(m)=m^{o(1)}`. Any injective translation grid contained in one such fiber therefore has only subpolynomial total cell volume. Moreover, for a completely multiplicative source phase, `prod_j chi(u_j)=chi(m)` is constant across the entire fiber. Exact fixed-product redistribution can change coefficients and individual factors, but it cannot by itself provide polynomial independent bandwidth or a new total multiplicative phase.

The live question is therefore **near-tangent and cross-fiber**, not exactly tangent. Can a concrete sheared or tilted family move through neighboring product fibers while remaining inside the short shell, achieve polynomial distinct-state volume after collisions and boundary loss, and retain a genuinely nonseparable source phase before positive closure? Growing factor depth or a transformed coefficient/phase geometry are also not excluded, but their decomposition cost and effective state count must be paid explicitly.

## Audit support geometry and closure faces together

Scalar filter banks, positive Toeplitz forms and operator-valued channels stay inside the MC-417--MC-418 cone. MC-419--MC-420 show that formal tensorization earns no bandwidth without physical support rank; MC-421 shows that genuinely independent variables can still arrive with source-free codimension-one faces; MC-422 shows that algebraic factor axes may have too little admissible joint width inside the thin product shell; MC-423 shows that the exact fixed-product tangent fiber has only subpolynomial state volume at fixed depth and freezes the total completely multiplicative phase. A viable escape must certify the support lattice, cross-fiber shell-compatible bandwidth volume, transformed source phase and closure-specific diagonal ledger in the same coordinates. None of those can be inferred from factor count or continuous tangent dimension alone.
