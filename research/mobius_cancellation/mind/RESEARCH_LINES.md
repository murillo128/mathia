# Möbius-cancellation research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Möbius-cancellation intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Convert the forced high-source spectrum into nonshareable source incidence

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category` through `MI-036-a-cheap-subspace-rank-ceiling-forces-exponentially-many-expensive-directions-but-not-independent-source-cost`.

MC-293--MC-306 move from individual character cost through family energy, multiquadratic packaging, common radical and quotient occupancy, and show that the previous full-family scalar capacities can be simultaneously saturated by a matched disjoint-singleton control.

MC-307 forces complexity to survive every relevant target-coordinate projection. MC-308 dualizes this to source-prime cuts. MC-309 then uses Landau--Page to show that moderate-rank low-product quotients must have at least half occupancy apart from at most one exceptional frequency.

MC-310 makes the reciprocal equal-mass endpoint much more rigid. In the discrete-resolution regime `R epsilon_y<1`, every nonexceptional Page-cheap frequency lies exactly on the middle Hamming layer. Linear closure turns this into a one-weight binary-code constraint: a uniformly Page-cheap subspace of dimension `t` satisfies

`2^t | R`, hence `t<=nu_2(R)`

without the exceptional mode, and `t<=nu_2(R)+1` in general.

MC-311 closes the next purely combinatorial question. After removing the possible cheap Page exception, the cheap set is `PG(nu_2(R),2)`-free. Bose--Burton therefore forces

`|E_y| >= 2^(R-nu_2(R))-2 >= 2^R/R-2`

Page-expensive reciprocal directions, with the stronger `-1` bound when no cheap exception is present. For odd `R`, essentially the entire nonzero spectrum is expensive.

The live theorem is no longer to prove that the high-source complement is large. Its **cardinality is already exponentially large**. The missing arithmetic step is to show that this family cannot reuse one small common source package. A useful source-incidence theorem should force many distinct lower-prime coordinates, a large hereditary union radical, bounded representative overlap, a submodular conductor cost, or another nonshareable resource from a Bose--Burton-sized expensive set. The matched opposite test is an explicit source construction realizing a flat-like expensive complement with heavy reuse; such a construction would prove that direction density alone is still noncoercive.

## Keep target projection, source-cut quotienting, cheap-subspace closure and expensive-set incidence distinct

MC-307 asks what remains when endpoint coordinates are deleted. MC-308 asks what remains when source coordinates are deleted and their span is quotiented out. MC-309 gives a Fourier occupancy floor for low-cost source cuts. MC-310 imposes a separate linear-closure condition on Page-cheap frequencies. MC-311 then converts the absence of large cheap flats into a lower bound for the **number** of expensive directions.

These resources are not interchangeable. Half occupancy of a quotient does not imply a large cheap subspace; many cheap points need not close linearly; a small cheap-subspace rank can force a large expensive complement without making the corresponding source costs additive. Any closing argument must state which quotient or subspace is used, what source product is paid, whether the exceptional mode occurs, how representatives overlap, and which endpoint consequence consumes the resulting incidence resource.

## Preserve matched controls, but require them to match the full hereditary incidence profile

The exchangeable controls of MC-295/MC-298 show that low-order combinatorial regularity can coexist with forbidden parity. MC-306 proves aggregate capacities noncoercive; MC-307--MC-310 add hereditary projection/quotient and linear-subspace audits. MC-311 adds one more mandatory control: the expensive complement must match not only its size but also its source-incidence geometry.

A proposed theorem has discriminatory content only if the same audit kills the matched control or is independently verified on it. Recounting the exponentially many expensive directions, summing their individual Page thresholds as if independent, or assuming that a large complement must use many source primes is no longer a plausible route.
