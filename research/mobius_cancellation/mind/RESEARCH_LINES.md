## Price sparse source-frame exceptions by source rank before counting quartic collisions

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category` through `MI-071-exceptional-sets-must-be-measured-on-the-source-difference-frame`.

MC-385--MC-392 identify the correct endpoint resource. Ambient exceptional counts must be pulled back to the source-difference frame. The exact projected-coordinate simplex excludes generic Welch near-extremizers; high dual distance forces macroscopic critical-scale correlations; and the fourth moment is controlled by genuine quotient-character collisions. If `W` is the surviving mode space, `C=W^perp`, `c=dim C` and `H=|W|`, MC-392 gives

`E_W |g|^4 <= (2c+3)(E_W |g|^2)^2`

and hence, at `epsilon=o(R^(-1/2))`, any exceptional theorem `|B|<=K H/R` requires `c=Omega(R)`. Quartic collision mass is therefore not an independent combinatorial knob: the source quotient must first carry linearly many independent relations.

MC-393 feeds that codimension obstruction back into the physical source cost. For every fixed cutoff exponent `0<beta<1`, a trial source package `P<=y^A` leaves a mode subspace of codimension at most `A/beta` after all source primes above `y^beta` are removed, while the remaining rough-squarefree characters still admit the required power saving. Combining this with MC-392 yields, for every fixed `eta in (0,1)`,

`log P/log y >= min{(1-eta)R, a_eta log log y}`

for sufficiently large `y` in the rank-growing regime. Thus the rank-side source-cost coefficient can be pushed arbitrarily close to one. The older small fixed-power coefficient was not intrinsic; the exact codimension feedback shows that a sparse `O(H/R)` endpoint exception budget demands **near-full-rank source cost** until the independent `log log y` ceiling becomes active.

The live question is therefore no longer merely whether the source can supply `c=Omega(R)`. The fixed-power incidence architecture already prices that possibility almost at full rank. A surviving route must identify a mechanism that changes this resource ledger: a source relation not paid for by distinct large-prime incidence, a different endpoint quotient whose exceptional geometry needs less codimension, or an analytic estimate that succeeds with a materially larger exceptional set while still closing the destination theorem.

## Keep source incidence, conductor, exceptional geometry and relation codimension distinct

The current ledger separates finite-resolution quotienting, source-incidence rank, package conductor, theorem admission, terminal character saving, source-frame energy, additive collision mass and independent relation codimension. MC-393 adds the feedback loop between the last two and conductor cost: choosing the cutoff closer to one reduces the number of expensive source primes only by forcing the analytic block decomposition deeper, but for every fixed cutoff below one the argument still works.

A proposed endpoint theorem should therefore state both the exceptional-set target and the physical source cost needed to support its relation budget. Generic Gram improvements, abstract code-distance arguments, isolated low-weight relations or large quartic collision counts are not useful unless they survive the exact codimension inequality **and** the near-full-rank conductor lower bound.

## Repair probabilistic comparators only after exact-stratum and algebraic-collapse gates

Probabilistic proxies remain secondary until their conditioning is arithmetically consistent and their observables survive exact multiplicative simplification. The endpoint results make this quantitative: after duplicate-character collapse, a source with too little relation codimension cannot support the desired sparse exceptional geometry, and MC-393 shows that buying enough codimension inside the fixed-power source model already costs almost one full logarithmic source unit per endpoint rank.

A comparator that appears favorable only because it ignores that source-cost feedback is not modelling the physical endpoint problem.
