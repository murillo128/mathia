## Improve the linear-in-rank exact-endpoint source tariff only by separating combinatorial and analytic ceilings

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category` through `MI-069-fixed-global-modulus-powers-defeat-exponential-family-gains`.

MC-366--MC-376 separate exact-real source leakage, finite-resolution quotienting, average shell stability and the geometry of a hypothetical exact endpoint. MC-377 proves that every exact endpoint source package must escape every fixed-power common-radical regime: `log P/log y -> infinity`.

MC-378--MC-381 make the escape quantitative. Tiny source primes can be packed into an early exact factor rather than deleted, only source primes above one fixed shell power need to be removed, and the terminal frame contradiction needs only a fixed positive fraction of the endpoint dimensions. Consequently the common-source obstruction is already linear in rank until the analytic ceiling takes over:

`log P/log y >= c min{R, log log y}`.

MC-382 identifies one source of that `log log y` ceiling. In the present iterated q-van der Corput architecture, a source modulus `q=y^a` requires differencing depth at least `a-2-o(1)`, while even an ideal square-root terminal estimate leaves a saving exponent only of order `2^(-a+O(1))`. The frame argument needs that exponent times `log y` to diverge. Hence repacking factors or recovering more source rank inside the same architecture cannot push the supported source exponent beyond `a=O(log log y)` except by constants.

MC-383 closes one apparent collective escape without claiming that its cited prime-modulus theorem applies directly to the squarefree-composite endpoint. Even under a deliberately stronger hypothetical extension of that small-doubling family mean-value shape, with all subpower loss removed, a family of size `A<=exp(CR)` facing a fixed positive power of one global modulus can balance only at

`log q=O(log y+R)`

when the interval length is `y^(O(1))`. In the endpoint specialization tested there, the gain is only additive `O(R)` in `log P`, not the `Omega(R log y)` growth needed for a rank-linear source exponent. Exponential family breadth therefore does not by itself cure a theorem whose analytic tariff is a fixed positive global-modulus power.

The live frontier is now split more sharply. The absolute constant in the linear-in-`R` regime may still be sharpened within the current architecture. **Beating the `log log y` horizon requires analytic input whose conductor/modulus cost scales differently with rank**: a terminal estimate whose saving does not halve at every required q-vdC factor, a factor-local or multilinear estimate that avoids a fixed positive power of the full source modulus, a rank-decaying global-modulus exponent, a logarithmic gain of order `R log y`, or a different source-to-destination invariant altogether.

## Keep resolution, source incidence, conductor, common-radical cost, differencing depth, family breadth and bias amplitude distinct

The current resource ledger separates exact-real subset-sum conditioning, finite-resolution quotienting, `l1` worst-case shell displacement, `l2` average dephasing stability, source-incidence balance, incidence-signature delocalization, package conductor, minimum primitive conductor, common-radical size, q-vdC factor depth, collective family size, global-modulus exponent, terminal saving exponent and endpoint bias amplitude. These are not interchangeable.

MC-381 shows that an analytic contradiction driven by exponentially many rows need not preserve `R-o(R)` source dimensions: a fixed positive fraction can suffice. MC-382 supplies one complementary warning: once the source exponent itself forces linearly many differencing stages, eliminating rank waste does not move the analytic horizon if every stage exponentially dilutes the final saving. MC-383 supplies another: exponential family size is a real combinatorial resource, but a fixed positive global-modulus power converts it only into additive `O(R)` source range unless the analytic theorem couples rank and source scale more efficiently.

None of these source-cost bounds imply Möbius cancellation or establish an optimal source tariff. The endpoint argument must still specify which physical quotient is observed and how its source-cost obstruction reaches a destination quantity relevant to `M(x)`.

## Repair probabilistic comparators only after exact-stratum and algebraic-collapse gates

Probabilistic proxies remain secondary until their conditioning is arithmetically consistent and their observables survive exact multiplicative simplification. A model that becomes degenerate after conditioning on an exact arithmetic stratum, or whose auxiliary statistic collapses algebraically to `M(x)`, has not created an independent cancellation mechanism.

The source-cost results add another control: a plausible endpoint comparator must not hide the exact architecture inside a common source package whose cost violates MC-377--MC-381. MC-382--MC-383 further say that failure of the current q-vdC and fixed-global-modulus collective theorem shapes beyond their horizons is a method boundary, not evidence that the underlying source regime is impossible. Conditional finite-power models may still diagnose which modes or amplitudes become expensive, but they cannot be promoted to endpoint evidence merely because the current analytic inputs stop resolving them.
