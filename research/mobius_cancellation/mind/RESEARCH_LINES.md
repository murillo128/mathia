## Retain arithmetic variables that survive lcm collapse without self-coupling back to Mertens

**Linked intuition:** `MI-073-source-mode-averaging-collapses-to-a-signature-projector`.

MC-397--MC-403 progressively consume source-mode averaging, divisor-pair multiplicity, lcm phase, moving endpoints, complete endpoints, generic truncation and finally the whole source family. The surviving nonprincipal family energy is exactly the squared norm of a Mertens-weighted signed signature-discrepancy vector. Formal family size is therefore not an independent cancellation resource.

MC-404 makes the representation boundary algebraic. For any finite divisor set `D`, every finite Boolean or polynomial function of the incidence vector `(1_{d|n})_(d in D)` lies in the finite join-semilattice algebra generated by `e_d e_e=e_[d,e]`; equivalently it has a unique lcm-closure expansion. Higher-degree Selberg weights, threshold functions and other finite incidence-only nonlinearities can change coefficients and enlarge the lcm closure, but they cannot preserve a tuple variable through the downstream arithmetic.

MC-405 tests the most obvious escape outside that semilattice. The exact identity

`sum_(n<=x) mu(n) log n = -sum_(b<=x) Lambda(b) M(x/b)`

retains the prime-power source variable `b` through the quotient `x/b`, so it genuinely varies inside lcm fibres. But partial summation shows that the entire signed quotient family aggregates to

`-sum_(b<=x) Lambda(b) M(x/b) = M(x) log x - integral_1^x M(t) dt/t`.

Thus variable survival is necessary but not sufficient: this canonical quotient representation is an exact first-order self-coupling of `M`. If one inserts only a pointwise bound `|M(y)|<=C y^theta` and takes absolute values, then `sum Lambda(b)b^(-theta)` has size `x^(1-theta)`, so every fixed `theta<1` is blown back up to linear scale. Any useful quotient route must therefore prove signed interaction among the scales `M(x/b)` or introduce another observable that is not already an exact aggregate of `M`.

The live route is correspondingly narrower. Either prove a source-specific theorem that makes the exact signed signature discrepancy small, or leave the lcm quotient with arithmetic data whose **signed family has independently exploitable structure**. A retained quotient, phase, congruence, translation or interval variable earns no leverage merely by surviving notation; after exact aggregation it must not collapse to the original Mertens problem plus triangle-inequality loss.

## Keep formal dimension, retained arithmetic coupling, exact self-coupling and signed cross-scale information distinct

A variable can survive one quotient while still carrying no independent estimate. Fourier mode labels can collapse to signature discrepancy; divisor tuples can collapse to lcm; endpoint truncation can invert to Mertens weights; MC-404 collapses the entire finite divisor-incidence algebra to the lcm semilattice; and MC-405 shows that a genuine source quotient can survive all of that yet sum exactly back to `M`.

A future candidate should therefore be audited in two stages before any claimed bootstrap. First identify the extra variable, reduce incidence algebra and regrouping exactly, and verify that equal lcm data can still produce different downstream arithmetic. Then compute the aggregate transform and its unsigned mass: if the family is an exact self-coupling and pointwise bounds plus triangle inequality return `O(x)`, state the missing signed correlation explicitly. Genuine progress requires a theorem controlling that cross-scale phase/sign structure, not simply a representation in which the source index remains visible.
