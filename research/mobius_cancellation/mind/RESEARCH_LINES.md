## Prove full small-prime generation or signed rough-Möbius cancellation on the exact multiplicative coset

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category`, `MI-014-centered-parity-and-zero-mode-are-separate-currencies`, `MI-015-sign-support-covariance-gives-a-conditioned-quadratic-bridge`, `MI-016-source-aligned-shifts-face-a-defect-or-mertens-dichotomy`, `MI-017-first-shell-crt-common-mode-is-a-source-coupled-energy-coordinate`, `MI-018-nonnegative-transference-does-not-create-fixed-power-sign-balance`, `MI-019-shell-square-blindness-is-a-cross-prime-common-quotient-problem`.

MC-238--MC-243 identify smooth-dilation blindness with the annihilator of the subgroup `H` generated by the small primes, while the Legendre matrix sees only its elementary quadratic quotient. MC-244--MC-245 invert the quadratic blind family to one rough affine cell. MC-246 then inverts the **complete annihilator**: the entire blind source is one rough Möbius multiplicative coset `-H`, and blind-character count cancels exactly against coset density.

The structural target is therefore the actual quotient `G/H`. Quadratic full rank only makes `[G:H]` odd; it does not imply `H=G`.

MC-247 shows, conditionally on ERH, how far a generic strong witness theorem can push this route. Every nonprincipal blind character must have active support `>> sqrt(y)/log y`, and the quadratic code receives rank pressure `>> sqrt(y)`. But the shell dimension is `asymp y/log y`, and matched coding controls show that such a minimum-support bound is still parametrically insufficient to force full rank. Large support is not a surrogate for quotient triviality.

The live theorem therefore has two exact exits. Either exploit **simultaneous shell arithmetic** beyond generic least-witness/minimum-support information to prove `H=G`, or prove fixed-power signed cancellation for the one source-selected rough Möbius coset / complete annihilator aggregate.

## Keep support, quadratic rank, full quotient index and signed amplitude separate

MC-244--MC-246 remove mode-count and smooth-weight proxies. MC-247 sharpens the same lesson under ERH: even forcing every surviving blind relation to spread over `sqrt(y)/log y` shell coordinates does not determine the size or triviality of `G/H`. Future progress must control the quotient itself, arithmetic dependencies among shell columns, simultaneous character behavior, or the final signed coset sum—not merely the minimum support of individual blind modes.
