## Keep algebraic degree, source depth, coordinate conductor, parity, scalar accuracy, normalization and endpoint orientation separate

A negative witness can be low algebraic degree while requiring large source depth. Positive localization forces roughly half-cube Walsh depth; atom-sensitive signed accuracy kills low-depth coefficient variation; restricting to the arithmetic image turns the problem into directional row-span/synthesis cost; moderate-conductor shell shadows remain Boolean-complete; and the final support obstruction now splits by parity. Even weights face deterministic equal-cell pairing, while odd weights face affine-hyperplane concentration plus a finite multiplicity capacity condition.

These are different currencies. A proof that improves one does not automatically buy another. In particular, MC-285's large support requirement is necessary rather than sufficient for a Christoffel certificate, and MC-286's affine-span condition is weaker than full pattern realization but does not supply the needed character estimate.

## Preserve the earlier source-depth and shell controls as falsifiers

Separated-prime Boolean pattern realization, the structured blind-free simplex control, and the actual localized shell remain complementary. They rule out arguments based only on arithmetic provenance, reciprocity, rank deficiency, shell localization or generic pattern scarcity. Any successful theorem must identify the specific high-support collective relation that prevents a product-character direction from staying constant-negative, or explain why a different source-native operator bypasses the endpoint-row route entirely.
