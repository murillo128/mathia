# Möbius-cancellation research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Möbius-cancellation intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Prove shell-specific full generation or signed rough-Möbius cancellation on the exact multiplicative coset

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category`, `MI-014-centered-parity-and-zero-mode-are-separate-currencies`, `MI-015-sign-support-covariance-gives-a-conditioned-quadratic-bridge`, `MI-016-source-aligned-shifts-face-a-defect-or-mertens-dichotomy`, `MI-017-first-shell-crt-common-mode-is-a-source-coupled-energy-coordinate`, `MI-018-nonnegative-transference-does-not-create-fixed-power-sign-balance`, `MI-019-shell-square-blindness-is-a-cross-prime-common-quotient-problem`.

MC-238--MC-246 identify smooth-dilation blindness with the annihilator of the subgroup `H` generated by the small primes and then invert the complete annihilator: the entire blind source is one rough Möbius multiplicative coset `-H`. Blind-character count cancels exactly against coset density. The structural target is the actual quotient `G/H`; quadratic full rank only makes `[G:H]` odd.

MC-247--MC-248 show why generic least-witness information cannot determine that quotient. A fixed `O(log^A q)` witness scale with `A>1` yields only sublinear support, and matched code controls show that minimum support at that scale can coexist with large quotient capacity.

MC-249 adds a different constraint. If a prime power `d=ell^a` divides the quotient exponent, an order-`d` blind component can live only on shell primes `r=1 mod d`. Combining active-support lower bounds with Brun--Titchmarsh therefore squeezes **every prime-power divisor of the quotient exponent**: unconditionally `ell^a << y/(log log y log log log y)`, and under the ERH support bound `ell^a << sqrt(y)`. For a schematic witness scale `O(log^A q)`, the torsion order improves to `O(y^(1-1/A))`; at the formal `A=1` boundary it would become bounded.

This is a real quotient refinement but still not generation. Small exponent does not bound rank: an elementary abelian group can have exponent `2` and arbitrarily large dimension. The surviving structural problem is therefore more specific—control the **rank/multiplicity of low-order quotient torsion** from simultaneous shell arithmetic, or bypass quotient triviality by proving fixed-power signed cancellation for the one source-selected rough Möbius coset / complete-annihilator aggregate.

## Keep witness scale, support, torsion order, quotient rank/index and signed amplitude separate

MC-244--MC-246 remove mode-count and smooth-weight proxies. MC-247--MC-248 show that highly nonlocal blind modes can coexist with substantial quotient capacity. MC-249 now shows that support plus progression occupancy can squeeze the quotient's torsion orders without controlling its rank or size. Minimum support, prime-power exponent, primary rank, full quotient index, coset density, and signed rough-coset amplitude are distinct currencies. Future work must control simultaneous low-order relations or the final signed coset sum rather than treating a small torsion exponent as near-generation.
