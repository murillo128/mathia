# Möbius-cancellation research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Möbius-cancellation intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they separate genuinely different mechanisms.

## Find a transfer or moving-comparator theorem cheaper than the cancellation target

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-local-cancellation-needs-a-polynomial-information-budget`, `MI-002-single-scale-pretentiousness-has-a-prime-harmonic-ceiling`, and `MI-008-moving-comparators-need-uniform-family-coherence`.

MC-049--MC-054 close broad fixed real and complex comparator escapes: square-free-supported complex comparators and bounded completely multiplicative Liouville-close comparators still transfer their power cancellation into essentially the same zeta zero-free half-plane once the required global boundary relation is present.

MC-055 shows why scale dependence is genuinely different but not automatically useful. Exact quadratic-character interpolants can reproduce the whole Möbius prefix through any finite `X` while each frozen comparator has square-root cancellation; the missing difficulty is uniform control of the comparator-dependent constant. MC-056 then proves an unconditional coherence cost: two distinct exact-prefix quadratic interpolants have product conductor `X^{4-o(1)}` or larger.

The live route is therefore a source-forced moving family whose cancellation constants and inter-scale complexity are controlled uniformly enough to beat the target theorem. Replacing finite-prefix certificates arbitrarily as the scale grows is not such a theorem.

## Change smoothing only with a quantitative endpoint-visibility theorem

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-local-cancellation-needs-a-polynomial-information-budget`, `MI-005-scale-doubling-is-exponent-neutral-without-new-signed-information`, and `MI-007-growing-order-smoothing-can-hide-endpoint-information`.

Growing-order Riesz smoothing can manufacture favorable exponents while delaying a terminal defect beyond the observed scale. Any changing-order carrier must prove when endpoint information re-enters or replace endpoint-vanishing smoothing by a source-faithful localization/inverse theorem.

## Preserve signed cancellation in mean-absolute and scale-doubling arguments

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-mean-absolute-cancellation-needs-excursion-coupled-information` and `MI-005-scale-doubling-is-exponent-neutral-without-new-signed-information`.

Natural positive norms expose RH-equivalent coarse modes when pieces are bounded separately. The Huxley--Watt annular residual remains live only as a coupled signed object capable of producing an iterable strict contraction with all losses controlled.

## Keep power-aware fidelity distinct from comparator generation

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-single-scale-pretentiousness-has-a-prime-harmonic-ceiling`, `MI-003-analytic-nonmasking-is-weaker-than-absolute-convolution-inversion`, and `MI-008-moving-comparators-need-uniform-family-coherence`.

A metric may faithfully detect a target-scale defect while every known absolute transfer either overpays it or moves the RH burden into the comparator theorem. The quadratic-prefix family makes the distinction explicit: finite coefficients can be perfect while the global boundary zero, uniform cancellation constant, and conductor coherence remain invisible to that prefix.
