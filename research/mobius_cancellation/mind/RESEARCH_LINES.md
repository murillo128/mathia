## Prove fixed-power signed variance for the logarithmic rough parity block

**Linked intuition:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category`.

MC-156--MC-177 identify Gallagher localization as a genuine signed near-diagonal Möbius currency and price any power saving against a global Mertens consequence. MC-178--MC-179 show why the Chinis one-large-prime architecture does not reach polynomial windows by support counting: the polynomial smooth sector is dense, while lowering the cutoff to polylogarithmic size forces growing prime-by-prime extraction depth.

MC-180 gives a sharper source-specific decomposition. The live theorem is therefore a signed second-moment estimate for that rough block, uniformly over the divisor-scaled windows generated by the small-prime factor, with its exact induced Mertens exponent stated up front.

## Keep phase-sensitive local variance distinct from support and decomposition complexity

Phase-blind autocorrelation, support-only smooth-number estimates, bounded-depth repeated factor extraction, and long-window heat norms are controls. A successful rough-block argument must retain Möbius orientation rather than replace it by rough-number density or absolute coefficients.

Conversely, a local estimate whose pricing through MC-177 already gives the final desired global theorem is not a cheap intermediate bridge. The useful target is a source-attainable fixed-power gain whose exact global cost is understood.
