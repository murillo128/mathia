## Retain arithmetic variables that survive lcm and hyperbola-fiber collapse without self-coupling back to Mertens

**Linked intuition:** `MI-073-source-mode-averaging-collapses-to-a-signature-projector`.

MC-397--MC-403 progressively consume source-mode averaging, divisor-pair multiplicity, lcm phase, moving endpoints, complete endpoints, generic truncation and finally the whole source family. The surviving nonprincipal family energy is exactly the squared norm of a Mertens-weighted signed signature-discrepancy vector. Formal family size is therefore not an independent cancellation resource.

MC-404 makes the first representation boundary algebraic. For any finite divisor set `D`, every finite Boolean or polynomial function of the incidence vector `(1_{d|n})_(d in D)` lies in the finite join-semilattice algebra generated by `e_d e_e=e_[d,e]`; equivalently it has a unique lcm-closure expansion. Higher-degree Selberg weights, threshold functions and other finite incidence-only nonlinearities can change coefficients and enlarge the lcm closure, but they cannot preserve a tuple variable through the downstream arithmetic.

MC-405 tests the most obvious escape outside that semilattice. The exact identity

`sum_(n<=x) mu(n) log n = -sum_(b<=x) Lambda(b) M(floor(x/b))`

retains the prime-power source variable `b` through the quotient. But the complete signed family is an exact first-order self-coupling of `M`; taking absolute values after only a pointwise input `|M(y)|<=C y^theta` returns linear scale for every fixed `theta<1`.

MC-406 sharpens the quotient audit again. Once the downstream observable is only `M(floor(x/b))`, source labels in the same hyperbola fiber are indistinguishable. Writing `k=floor(x/b)` gives

`W_k(x)=psi(x/k)-psi(x/(k+1))`

and

`sum_b Lambda(b) M(floor(x/b)) = sum_k M(k) W_k(x)`.

Only `O(sqrt(x))` quotient coordinates survive. More importantly, after `psi(y)=y+E(y)`, the PNT main kernel is exactly

`x sum_k M(k)/(k(k+1)) = x(m(x)-M(x)/(x+1))`,

so it collapses to classical harmonic Möbius cancellation. The remaining fiber-total information is the signed pairing of `M(k)` with the increments `E(x/k)-E(x/(k+1))`. Even the first fiber has mass `W_1(x)~x/2`, so smallness of the total necessarily comes from cross-fiber cancellation rather than coordinate compression.

The live route is therefore narrower than “retain a quotient.” Either prove an independently useful signed relation between Mertens values and prime-counting error increments, or retain a discriminator **inside** the floor-quotient fibers—phase, congruence, translation or another source coordinate—that survives the `b -> floor(x/b)` compression and supports a non-tautological estimator. A retained variable earns no leverage merely by surviving notation or reducing dimension.

## Keep formal dimension, retained arithmetic coupling, exact self-coupling, quotient-fiber compression and signed cross-scale information distinct

A variable can survive one quotient while still carrying no independent estimate. Fourier mode labels can collapse to signature discrepancy; divisor tuples can collapse to lcm; endpoint truncation can invert to Mertens weights; finite divisor-incidence algebra collapses to the lcm semilattice; a genuine Mangoldt quotient can sum exactly back to `M`; and MC-406 shows that even that source variable is seen only through `O(sqrt(x))` floor-quotient masses whose deterministic main kernel is harmonic Möbius cancellation.

A future candidate should therefore be audited in three stages. First identify the extra variable and reduce incidence algebra/regrouping exactly. Second compute the aggregate transform and its unsigned mass to expose exact self-coupling. Third identify the **finest source statistic actually visible downstream** and test whether its signed interaction is independently controlled. Genuine progress requires a theorem on the cross-scale sign/phase structure or a within-fiber discriminator, not simply a larger representation or fewer quotient coordinates.
