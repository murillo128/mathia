## Exploit source-signature coefficients or a genuinely joint variable, not an invertible hyperbola coordinate

Keeping those labels visible does not make them independent cancellation coordinates.

MC-400 closes the remaining attempt to extract new information from the moving hyperbola endpoint alone. With `c_chi(ell)=C_g(ell)chi(ell)`, the twisted majorant is the exact convolution

`f_chi = chi * c_chi`,

hence `c_chi=(mu chi)*f_chi`. At summatory level

`F_chi(N)=sum_ell c_chi(ell) M_chi(N/ell)`

and

`A_chi(N)=sum_d mu(d)chi(d) F_chi(N/d)`.

The surviving routes are consequently narrower.
