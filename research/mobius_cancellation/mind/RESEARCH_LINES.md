# Möbius-cancellation research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Möbius-cancellation intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Convert forced source-incidence cost into an arithmetic realizability obstruction beyond generic half-rate coding

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category` through `MI-039-generic-half-rate-codes-realize-the-constant-defect-endpoint`.

MC-307--MC-312 turn endpoint projection and source cuts into a hereditary generalized-Hamming support law. MC-313 identifies the coarse coding boundary, MC-314 makes the natural equal-share source demand precise at constant defect, and MC-315--MC-316 show that projective occupancy, systematic submatrix-rank, primal/dual generalized-weight and erasure formulations are presentations of the same generic endpoint.

MC-317 closes that generic endpoint in the permissive direction: sufficiently large full binary `[2R,R]` codes realize the required constant-defect generalized-weight profile. Length, dimension, generalized weights, projective occupancy, ordinary erasure capacity and their dual recodings therefore do not distinguish the arithmetic source from matched binary controls.

The live theorem is source-specific. Either prove the source scale sharp enough to enter the constant-defect regime **and** identify a Legendre/reciprocal-prime incidence property arbitrary `[2R,R]` codes need not satisfy, or derive a different arithmetic invariant whose endpoint cost is not already realized by the MC-317 controls.

## Repair probabilistic Möbius comparators only after exact-stratum and algebraic-collapse gates

**Linked intuition:** `MI-040-probabilistic-mobius-proxies-must-survive-exact-arithmetic-consistency`.

MC-318 shows that a recent probabilistic Möbius route fails before any delicate asymptotic estimate is reached. Conditioning on `Omega(n)=1` forces squarefreeness, contradicting an asserted independence from `mu^2`; and the proposed auxiliary sum satisfies the exact identity

`Q_(1,n)(x)=lambda(n) M(x)`.

Thus an `n`-independent nonzero asymptotic for that object cannot supply independent information about `M(x)`. A repaired probabilistic route must first state its independence law on a range where deterministic strata do not already decide the event, and must use an auxiliary observable that does not algebraically collapse to the target.

The live question is narrow: can a corrected central/growing-`Omega` dependence statement be paired with a genuinely additional observable whose exact multiplicative simplification still leaves quantitative information beyond known Möbius cancellation? Until both gates are passed, probabilistic asymptotics cannot be treated as new source information.

## Identify the arithmetic property excluded by random-linear matched controls

**Linked intuition:** `MI-039-generic-half-rate-codes-realize-the-constant-defect-endpoint`.

MC-317 removes the matrix-existence problem itself. A source restriction is relevant only if it forces a stronger generalized-weight/support defect, forbids the required submatrix profile, or yields another endpoint inequality not implied by generic half-rate coding. Merely noting that the arithmetic matrices are deterministic, Legendre-derived or structurally sparse is not coercivity until that structure excludes the matched controls quantitatively.

## Keep target projection, source scale, exact identities and arithmetic realizability distinct

A large number of endpoint directions is not the same as expensive common support, and a probabilistically suggestive proxy is not a new information channel if exact algebra identifies it with the target. Future arguments should simplify source observables first, preserve the MC-317 matched control at the coding endpoint, and only then ask whether genuinely arithmetic structure forces a destination-relevant defect.
