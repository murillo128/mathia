## Match compulsory reverse discrimination to the actual endpoint channel

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category` through `MI-059-fixed-component-mixtures-pay-their-source-tariff-in-the-mixing-law`.

The reciprocal endpoint problem has been reduced from syntactic complexity to invariant source discrimination. Bounded coefficient energy with nontrivial all-mode dephasing forces linear joint source information. On the natural source graph, destination-relative reliability charges only edges that cross the relevant quotient, while the canonical channel-law quotient itself must have an extensive exact boundary.

MC-351--MC-353 identify the compulsory local currency: neighboring source states must carry a nontrivial half-Jeffreys/privacy-loss budget. MC-354 prices that budget for additive log-smooth readouts through mean displacement and noise curvature. MC-355 closes the Gaussian location-scale side-channel escape: source-dependent covariance contributes an exact positive affine-invariant Jeffreys term in addition to the symmetric whitened mean displacement.

MC-356 shows that this accounting is not Gaussian-specific. For any finite-dimensional regular exponential-family intermediate readout with source-independent support and downstream kernel, half-Jeffreys divergence before post-processing is exactly one half of the integrated Fisher action along the natural-parameter edge,

`F_e = int_0^1 Delta_e^T I(theta_e(t)) Delta_e dt`,

and downstream half-Jeffreys is no larger. Combining this with the endpoint lower budget forces `E_e F_e >= 2 eta^2/C-o(1)` under bounded coefficient energy and fixed nontrivial dephasing. Changing Bernoulli probabilities, Poisson rates, categorical weights, Gaussian natural parameters or other regular sufficient-statistic laws therefore does not create a free smooth non-Gaussian side channel.

MC-357 closes fixed-component mixtures: when all source dependence enters through a latent law `nu_x` followed by one source-independent component kernel, output half-Jeffreys is bounded by latent half-Jeffreys. MC-358 now closes the generic move-the-components escape for an actual common latent architecture. If

`Theta~nu_x`, `Y|Theta=theta~K_x(.|theta)`

and `Q_x(dtheta,dy)=nu_x(dtheta)K_x(dy|theta)`, then the KL chain rule and marginal data processing give the exact lifted ledger

`b_e(Y) <= (1/2)J(Q_x,Q_x') = b_e(Theta)+c_e(K)`.

The endpoint lower tariff therefore forces `E_e[b_e(Theta)+c_e(K)] >= eta^2/C-o(1)`. Source discrimination may move between the selector and the conditional components, but it cannot disappear. At the exact endpoint, shared positive-weight categorical labels are possible only when the paired source-dependent components themselves become mutually singular.

The live bridge is now architecture-specific rather than family-classification-specific. For an actual Möbius endpoint construction, expose its genuine lifted source graph and prove that the adjacent joint half-Jeffreys tariff is too small, or identify the singular/high-divergence source path that actually pays the compulsory budget. A post-hoc mixture decomposition is not enough: the latent labels and component pairing must have real common architectural meaning across source states. Hidden adaptive branches, random seeds, metadata or side channels must be included in the lifted transcript before any upper theorem is applied.

## Repair probabilistic Möbius comparators only after exact-stratum and algebraic-collapse gates

Probabilistic proxies remain secondary until their conditioning is arithmetically consistent and their observables survive exact multiplicative simplification. A model that becomes degenerate after conditioning on an exact arithmetic stratum, or whose auxiliary statistic collapses algebraically to `M(x)`, has not created an independent cancellation mechanism.

## Keep the resource ledger explicit

The current invariant chain distinguishes source loading, coefficient energy, exact source quotient, joint transcript information, destination-cut geometry, aggregate edge KL, privacy-loss distribution, inverse distinguishability, mean displacement, covariance deformation, Fisher action, latent mixing-law divergence, conditional component divergence, lifted joint divergence, support singularity, score curvature/noise scale and side-channel structure. These are different resources.

A useful Möbius endpoint theorem must close the loop in both directions: the destination must force enough neighboring-state discrimination, and the concrete arithmetic channel must be shown unable to afford that discrimination at the relevant scale. MC-354--MC-358 supply the second-direction accounting for progressively broader declared architectures; the remaining problem is no longer to name a more complicated generic statistical family, but to prove a source-edge upper theorem for the actual Möbius-derived lifted law or exhibit the source-dependent singular channel that defeats such an upper bound.
