This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Möbius-cancellation evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Move fixed-rank source geometry to a genuinely retained arithmetic resource

MC-423--MC-445 separate source diversity, representation fidelity, Möbius top-interaction and natural-source measure. Fixed-rank normalized prime-gap geometry is dense in the ambient simplex and polynomially realizable, while the most direct bounded-height natural ensemble concentrates on a terminal simplex vertex. Continuous fixed-rank normalized observables therefore do not acquire robust prime specificity merely from support richness or from switching to the canonical counting measure.

The surviving resources are qualitatively different: growing rank, another justified source measure, absolute scale, discontinuous arithmetic data, cross-rank structure, or coordinates that retain enough factor identity to survive the Möbius projection. Any such carrier still has to produce an analytic cancellation estimate after the exact top-interaction quotient.

## Keep first-exit progression, cutoff and source weights coupled after affine normalization

MC-484 removes the uncontrolled bracket selector without weakening the hard mask. MC-485 then rules out the complete coefficient-blind phase simplification: the bare inverse-shift character family has Gram `pI-J-vv*` and norm `sqrt(p)` on the codimension-two complement. MC-486 closes the sparse-subset escape: every incomplete family with at least three distinct nonzero `q` residues still has operator norm exactly `sqrt(p)`. Prime incompleteness is therefore not a new mechanism once the moving coefficients have been frozen.

MC-487 sharpens the representation itself. The common visible inverse shift `h q^(-1)` in the character phase and pair-sieve mask is **affine gauge**, not an independent arithmetic resource. On each first-exit root branch, returning to the intrinsic integer coordinate (`x=qm=n` or `x=qm=n+h`) simultaneously fixes the translated-ratio separation to `+/-h` and the pair-sieve polynomial to `x(x+/-h)`. What genuinely remains `q`-dependent is the arithmetic-progression support, exact endpoints, nested roughness cutoff `P(q)`, and the actual outer/staged coefficients.

MC-488 identifies the exact source incidence hidden in that normalized frame. The progression-plus-cutoff first-exit cells are a disjoint least-active-prime partition of the source support. Their incidence Gram is diagonal, and every common unitary transform preserves that orthogonality. Thus merely applying one shared transform to the exact first-exit rows cannot manufacture the cross-`q` interaction that the route needs; with common outer coefficients the rows simply refold to the original hard-mask difference.

MC-489 tests the first literal way to leave that common-transform obstruction. Rowwise dilation `n=qm` is genuinely `q`-dependent and can create cross-row terms after alignment in the `m` coordinate, but it does not remove the conductor-scale diagonal. In polynomial `q` ranges the normalized complete row still has `L^2` size `p^(1/2-o(1))`, and its complete cross-row interaction factorizes into the classical shifted-character correlation times an ordinary local sieve-overlap count. A `q`-dependent coordinate followed by coefficient-blind positive `L^2` closure is therefore not yet a conductor-power mechanism.

MC-490 closes the scalar favorable-subspace loophole left by that one-row obstruction. On a common complete period, any `k=o(p)` family of exact moving hard-mask rows with distinct conductor residues has normalized Gram matrix `I+E` with `|E_(q,r)|<=2/(p-2)`. Geršgorin therefore makes the whole row family asymptotically isometric in every scalar outer-weight direction. A clever scalar function of the least prime cannot create a conductor-power saving by aligning the completed rows with a hidden small singular direction.

MC-491 prices the repeated-residue pair branch. If `q!=r` but `q congruent r (mod p)`, the completed character phases coincide, yet their nested pair-sieve masks decorrelate the exact rows: in polynomial ranges the normalized pair coherence is `O(log log p/log p)`. Consequently arbitrary-residue completed families of size `o(log p/log log p)` are still Riesz-stable. An isolated conductor collision or a small repeated-residue block is therefore not a hidden scalar favorable direction.

MC-492 then removes the most direct dense repeated-residue continuation for the **native Buchstab coefficient** without any completion argument. Within one root branch, repeated-residue rows have identical character phase pointwise in the common `m` coordinate, while their exact masks and endpoint amplitudes are nonnegative. Their Gram entries are therefore nonnegative even on incomplete intervals, and the native common coefficient `-1` makes every same-root cross term constructive rather than cancelling. A small eigenvector of some completed dense repeated-residue Gram matters only if an earlier arithmetic step actually creates the required `q`-dependent signs or phases. Cross-root interference is different, but on complete conductor periods its pair correlation is only Jacobi-sum scale `O(p^(1/2))`, hence normalized `O(p^(-1/2))` (or `O(p^(-1))` for quadratic characters); genuinely collective cross-root accumulation and incomplete signed transforms remain open.
