MC-423--MC-430 show that tangent dimension, factor multiplicity and fixed-dimensional additive summaries cannot manufacture polynomial state capacity without paying retained precision, while full labelled prime-block incidence has Bell-number capacity `N_r^(1-o(1))` along primorials.

MC-431--MC-434 separate internal factor addressability from arithmetic source variation. A sparse rigid relation can preserve every factor state, but the canonical increasing-prime successor chain still has only one isomorphism type at fixed `r=omega(n)` and ordinary factorwise Möbius sign erases its rank scaffold.

MC-435 prices the missing source-varying decoration. If the retained labels on the canonical rank scaffold have total alphabet budget `B_r`, then the number of realized source types is at most `exp(B_r)`. Along the primorial scale, `B_r=o(r log r)` gives only `N_r^(o(1))` conditional source diversity. In particular, `O(r)` bounded or `r^(o(1))`-valued local marks are too small for polynomial source capacity.

MC-436 shows that the `Theta(r log r)` threshold is sharp as a representation budget. The global rank order of adjacent selected-prime gaps realizes every permutation in `S_(r-1)`, hence `(r-1)!=N_r^(1-o(1))` source types. But this does not beat the information budget: the ordinal permutation itself carries identity-scale combinatorial information, and its proof gives no uniform near-primorial source-height bound.

The live audit is therefore no longer another raw capacity count. A candidate must show that a source-varying part of an identity-scale relation is realizable at the source scale needed by the analytic argument and then survives coefficient formation, divisor aggregation or another Möbius-facing projection in a way that yields signed cancellation without effectively retaining the full factor data.

## Separate description sparsity, quotient entropy, source diversity and analytic usefulness

A small alphabet is meaningful only after the actual quotient is specified. Sparse incidence can rigidify every coordinate; a universal rigid scaffold can still carry zero source diversity; bounded local decorations can carry too little conditional variation; and a global ordinal relation can recover factorial variation only by paying `Theta(r log r)` information.

The surviving relational escape is therefore not “use gaps” or “use a canonical order” in the abstract. It is a source-forced relation with an explicitly priced surviving type budget, controlled realization scale, and a demonstrable Möbius-facing observable. Low numerical precision is not low information cost, and near-full representation capacity is not cancellation.
