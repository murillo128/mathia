## Treat every fixed finite radial parity filter followed by absoluteization as classified

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-011-source-forced-prime-deformation-is-a-polynomial-information-channel` and `MI-012-hamming-regularization-is-degree-two-damping-before-the-square-root-transition`.

MC-107--MC-110 carry the exact Hamming shell profile through the full fixed proportional Sathe--Selberg range and show that direct truncation and positive diagonal shell norms preserve almost-square bulk rather than the tiny Möbius endpoint. MC-111 first closed filters with nonzero or simple response at the locally flat shell frequency.

MC-112 removes the higher-order fixed-filter escape completely. For every fixed nonzero finite transfer polynomial `A`, some proportional shell has local ratio `beta^{-1}` with `A(beta^{-1})!=0`, and that shell still has size `N^{2-o(1)}`. If `A(-1)!=0` retains parity, any positive diagonal Hölder/absolute-value certificate after filtering inherits that floor. The order of the zero at `z=1` is therefore not a surviving resource.

The fixed local radial branch is closed. A viable radial mechanism must change scale with `N`, preserve signed nonlocal cancellation without absoluteizing, or use finer source coupling than one Hamming degree.

## If the filter grows, pay the logarithmic transfer range before claiming fixed-power attenuation

MC-113 classifies the first design budget for an `N`-dependent polynomial filter. After parity normalization `P(-1)=1`, Chebyshev minimax on the proportional ratio band `[1/2,2]` gives exact optimal attenuation `1/T_r(3)`. Uniform suppression by `N^{-delta}` therefore requires degree `Theta(log N)`; every `o(log N)` filter leaves some ideal proportional-shell response at `N^{-o(1)}`.

This is only a transfer-polynomial theorem. To turn logarithmic range into an actual source estimate one must still prove growing-shift Sathe--Selberg control, coefficient conditioning, and a signed reconstruction estimate for the true convolution. A mode-count design argument must not be substituted for that arithmetic uniformity.

## Derive a signed relation that carries parity across the full critical shell profile

The endpoint is the alternating evaluation at `z=-1`, while the positive shell bulk lives around degree `2 log log N` and remains almost square throughout every fixed proportional scale. A useful theorem must couple many degrees **before magnitudes are taken** and control the cancellation at endpoint scale.

A growing local filter is relevant only if its source convolution and reconstruction stay controlled across the required `Theta(log N)` range. A genuinely nonlocal recurrence, transfer operator, bilinear identity, or source-forced relation may instead avoid uniform local-frequency suppression altogether.

## Preserve finer source coupling when radialization discards the needed signs

Product-fiber, bilinear, prime-factor, or other source-coupled information can evade the radial shell quotient, but it must carry a falsifiable signed relation rather than merely enlarge the observable. Larger coordinate sets or longer filters are not new information unless the final theorem preserves the source coupling that creates the cancellation.
