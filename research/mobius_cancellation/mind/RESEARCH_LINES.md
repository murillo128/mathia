## Resolve the exceptional-character frontier or leave positive triangle feedback

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-008-moving-comparators-need-uniform-family-coherence`.

MC-066 identifies the positive signed-feedback budget, and MC-067 excludes slow conductor motion. MC-068 shows that below square-root scale only `O(log X)` prime quadratic characters can even satisfy the necessary terminal-prime bias. MC-069 is sharper in the quasi-subpower range `Q<=exp(kappa log X/log log X)`: every nonexceptional primitive character is excluded and at most the unique Landau--Page exceptional character can survive.

A decisive continuation must therefore confront that possible exceptional moving comparator, extend pointwise control beyond the current threshold, or abandon the positive triangle closure and prove genuine signed cancellation in the feedback sum. A broad search over ordinary nonexceptional quadratic characters in the current range is no longer a distinct mechanism.

## Keep comparator turnover and scale coverage in the theorem

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-008-moving-comparators-need-uniform-family-coherence`.

Even a rare admissible comparator at one scale is useless without coherent coverage. Any exceptional-character or alternative-comparator route must quantify how the chosen family persists or turns over across scales and how those changes enter the transferred Mertens exponent.

## Treat asymptotically fixed Mellin drift as information-neutral unless the residual coupling changes

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-006-asymptotically-fixed-mellin-drift-is-information-neutral`.

Small deterministic Mellin shifts or slowly varying normalizations do not create new signed information when they can be absorbed into the existing annular/coarse decomposition. A useful deformation must alter the coupled residual rather than reparameterize the same scalar carrier.
