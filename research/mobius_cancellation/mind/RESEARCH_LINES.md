# Möbius-cancellation research lines

This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Möbius-cancellation evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Move fixed-rank source geometry to a genuinely retained arithmetic resource

MC-423--MC-445 separate source diversity, representation fidelity, Möbius top-interaction and natural-source measure. Fixed-rank normalized prime-gap geometry is dense in the ambient simplex and polynomially realizable, while the most direct bounded-height natural ensemble concentrates on a terminal simplex vertex. Continuous fixed-rank normalized observables therefore do not acquire robust prime specificity merely from support richness or from switching to the canonical counting measure.

The surviving resources are qualitatively different: growing rank, another justified source measure, absolute scale, discontinuous arithmetic data, cross-rank structure, or coordinates that retain enough factor identity to survive the Möbius projection. Any such carrier still has to produce an analytic cancellation estimate after the exact top-interaction quotient.

## Price concrete staged sieve components before attacking residual dispersion

MC-446--MC-453 close generic fixed-lcm occupancy, bare frequency synchronization, final beta-sieve sign variation, raw truncated Möbius as an all-splits well-factorable sequence, and bounded component multiplicity after separate positive quadratic closure. A conductor-power gain must therefore enter through a genuine auxiliary factor representation before generic recombination, majorization or squaring destroys the retained variables.

MC-458--MC-463 locate that resource precisely enough to remove the apparent internal gcd-sector multiplicity. For a fixed outer pair, all compatible residual gcd sectors reduce to the same normalized anchor modulus `T=r_2/(h_0,r_2)` and must be aggregated into coefficients `widetilde beta(s_0)`. MC-464--MC-466 then show that standard positive q-vdC/Fejer weighting retains a square-root normalized-quotient mass barrier; concentrating nonnegative outer pair weight on large gcd does not remove it.

MC-467 couples that barrier to the actual source interval geometry. On a dyadic residual shell, the number of represented residual divisors grows like `X/T` while each source interval shrinks like `N/([r,r']X)`, so in the gainful bulk their product is at most `4N/([r,r']T)`. Away from near-complete source intervals, any nonvanishing favorable Fejer fraction is therefore confined to small outer scales, with the coarse necessary restriction `r' lesssim_eta (N/p)^(2/3)`. This is only a necessary geometric localization; it does not show that the surviving cells have negligible sieve coefficient mass.

MC-468 closes the tempting generic coefficient shortcut. Bare `k`-fold well-factorability is vacuous on support `n<=Q^(1/k)`: every `1`-bounded sequence on that range is automatically `k`-fold well-factorable by placing the whole sequence in the largest factor and using Dirichlet identities in the others. Hence no anti-concentration or cell-mass saving inside a root-scale region follows from the label “well-factorable” alone. Any mass theorem for the MC-467 cells must use construction-specific information from the actual sieve weight or decomposition.

MC-469 performs that construction-specific check for Lichtman's modified linear-sieve decomposition and exposes a sharper obstruction. Nonempty rough-block components do have an exact support filter: if their prescribed bin product exceeds the surviving outer cutoff, they cannot enter the small cell. But the decomposition also contains the `r=0` empty-vector component, for which the rough block is exactly `delta_1`; staged as `alpha=delta_1`, `beta=lambda^(0)`, it lands at the minimal outer cell `R=T=1`. Support shrinkage and root-scale pruning therefore cannot eliminate every component.

MC-470 determines the residual amplitude of that unavoidable base component exactly. Writing `m_z(y)` for the product of the small primes `q<z` dividing `y`,

`B_(lambda^(0))(y)=sum_(d|m_z(y), d in D^+(Q)) mu(d)`

is an integer-valued nonnegative upper-sieve boundary count. It is at least the `z`-rough indicator and equals one on genuinely `z`-rough values. For a nonrough value with `k=omega(m_z(y))`, it vanishes whenever the low-rank condition `epsilon(k+2)<=1` holds. This exact rank gate does not imply power sparsity, and the surviving rough population already has positive weight. In particular, the alternating signs of the underlying beta-sieve coefficients have recombined pointwise and cannot be credited as an internal oscillatory resource in the staged base amplitude.

MC-471 isolates the resulting rough-by-rough pair-sieve channel from the mixed and first-exit boundary pieces. MC-472 then closes its complete-period arithmetic interaction. For the translated-ratio phase `K_h(n)=chi(n+h)conjugate(chi(n))` and a square-free small-prime modulus `W` coprime to the conductor `p`, the complete joint-period sum is exactly `-V_W(h)`, where `V_W(h)` is the ordinary pair-sieve survivor count. Thus the normalized mean is simply `-delta_W(h)/p`: CRT makes the character phase and the small-prime sieve independent coordinates on a full period. If `2|W`, every odd shift has zero rough-by-rough population, while the complete shift-average of `delta_W(h)` is exactly `(phi(W)/W)^2`. Neither the local `q|h` enhancements nor complete-period completion supply an additional power saving.

MC-473 audits the most direct incomplete continuation and rules it out as a proof architecture. Exact Möbius expansion of both roughness constraints followed by separate Fourier/Weil completion of every divisor-residue branch gives the branch multiplicity

`B_W(h)=sum_(d|W)nu_d(h)=prod_(q|W)(1+nu_q(h))`.

For a primorial sieve this lies between `2^omega(W)` and `3^omega(W)`, so at a fixed-power cutoff the triangle-inequality tariff is superpolynomial even though every individual centered progression costs only `O(sqrt(p) log p)`. This does **not** show that the actual incomplete discrepancy is large; it shows that exact full inclusion-exclusion destroys the pair-sieve compression before the character estimate is used.

MC-474 closes the most natural exact Fourier compression of that branch explosion. Keeping the pair-sieve mask intact and Fourier expanding it modulo `W` gives a tensorized spectral norm

`||Ahat_(W,h)||_1 = prod_(q|W) L_q(h)`

with `L_q(h)>=7/5` for every sieve prime `q>=5`; hence every nonzero primorial channel has `||Ahat||_1 >= (7/5)^(N_5(W))`. Applying the uniform `O(sqrt(p) log p)` completion bound mode by mode and then taking absolute values therefore still pays a superpolynomial tariff. The obstruction is specifically `l^1` scalarization, not Fourier energy: Parseval gives `||Ahat||_2^2=delta_W(h)` and centered energy `delta_W(h)(1-delta_W(h))`.

MC-475 closes the obvious Hilbert-space repair of that `l^1` loss. For the centered mask `B=A-delta`, Cauchy--Schwarz pairs its small Fourier `l^2` energy with the response vector `S_r(I)=sum_(n in I)K_h(n)e_W(rn)`, but finite Parseval gives the exact identity

`sum_(r mod W)|S_r(I)|^2 = W sum_(a mod W)|F_a(I)|^2`.

When `|I|=H<W` and `H<=p`, the interval occupies distinct `W`-residue classes and the right side is essentially `WH`. Thus ordinary full-grid `l^2` coupling and the additive large sieve pay the full primorial frequency dimension; the cheap mask energy is exactly mismatched with a huge dual response norm. This is a proof-method obstruction, not a lower bound for the signed discrepancy.

MC-476 then audits the stronger modern restriction-theory suggestion. Bera--Viswanadham's sifted-set restriction theorem applies to this translated pair sieve in its stated `z<=H^(1/4)` range, but as a black-box zero-frequency bound it is coefficient-uniform and sees `K_h` only through its `l^2` mass. It therefore cannot distinguish the translated-ratio phase from constant positive coefficients on the same sifted support, and its dimension-two sieve normalization is only polylogarithmic. The missing conductor power cannot come from that restriction inequality alone.

MC-477 shows that the low-denominator enveloping-sieve representation is nevertheless compatible with the character phase at the level of individual rational modes: completion of a mode coupled to `K_h` costs `O(sqrt(p) log p)` independently of its denominator. MC-478 then preserves the exact local pair-sieve Fourier factorization instead of taking a generic envelope norm and lowers the aggregate `l^1` coefficient cost to `z^2 (log(2z))^5/G(z)`. Thus the low-denominator representation really does remove the full-primorial spectral explosion.

The remaining transfer error is not small in unsigned mass. MC-479 proves that the Selberg envelope agrees with the hard pair-sieve mask on survivors but can give order-one false-positive weight to a value with a single small-prime violation near the cutoff; over a complete period, total leakage is only logarithmically smaller than survivor mass. Positive majorization therefore cannot be converted back to the exact signed discrepancy by treating `beta-A` as a negligible `l^1` error.

MC-480 then exposes the exact phase structure of that leakage. On a complete joint period the leakage depends only on the sieve coordinate and

`sum_(n mod pW) K_h(n)L_(W,h)(n) = -sum_(b mod W)L_(W,h)(b)`.

So complete-period character oscillation does not cancel the leakage; it reproduces its mass with the same CRT mechanism as the hard mask. The corresponding centered leakage discrepancy is

`E_(W,h)(I)=sum_(n in I) K_h(n)L_(W,h)(n)+|I| ell_W(h)/p`.

MC-481 closes the interpretation of this quantity as a cheaper exact-to-envelope transfer target. Canonical centering is linear for every `W`-periodic sieve-coordinate weight, so if `A` is the hard mask, `beta` the envelope and `L=beta-A`, then exactly

`D_L(I)=D_beta(I)-D_A(I)`.

The low-denominator work of MC-477--MC-478 already gives a conductor-sensitive bound for `D_beta`. Hence any conductor-power bound for centered leakage is, up to that already-controlled comparison term, the original exact rough×rough discrepancy itself; conversely an exact-mask estimate controls centered leakage at the same scale. Centering removes the forced complete-period mean but creates no independent transfer resource.

MC-482 adds a quantitative barrier to replacing the exact signed target by a two-sided beta-sieve bracket. If `A^-<=A<=A^+` has the usual beta-sieve main terms `NV(z)F^-(s)` and `NV(z)F^+(s)`, then making the bracket width `o(N/log N)` requires `F^+(s)-F^-(s)=o(1)` and hence `s=log D/log z -> infinity`. At any fixed positive sieve level `D<=N^(theta+o(1))`, this forces `log z=o(log N)`, so the cutoff is only `z=N^(o(1))` (often polylogarithmic under quantitative convergence). A two-sided envelope cannot simultaneously reach a fixed power cutoff and transfer at the Mertens scale.

MC-483 shows that even an exceptionally narrow well-factorable bracket would not solve the signed transfer problem. The aggregate width controls the sum of positive bracket errors, while the Möbius target is a difference of signed components; cancellation in the bracket width does not imply cancellation in those component errors. Well-factorability is useful for linear prime detection, but it does not manufacture the selector-sensitive signed correlation needed here. Thus positive envelope tightness and signed Möbius transfer are distinct resources.

The live q-vdC route is therefore narrower than “control the envelope error.” A useful continuation must introduce **genuinely additional signed/factorable structure**—for example an exact finite factorization, a first-exit identity, or a bilinear/dispersion representation in which the translated-ratio phase and prime selector remain coupled before coefficientwise absolute values—or move to the mixed rough/boundary, nonempty rough-block, or pre-positive cross-component channels. The target theorem is a selector-sensitive signed correlation principle, not a still narrower positive bracket. Merely changing the majorant, centering, bracket width or norm on the same replacement error is closed as an independent mechanism. The accepted local clue `CLUE-averaged-qvdc-source-frame-variable-retention` has already been narrowed to this exact handoff.

For nonempty rough-block pieces, retain the exact bin-product support pruning and price only the surviving construction-specific coefficient mass. Redesigned non-Fejer architectures remain separate and must re-pay interval geometry, diagonal cost and favorable-set concentration rather than treating reweighting as free.
