# Möbius-cancellation research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Möbius-cancellation intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they separate genuinely different mechanisms.

## Identify the signed or multiscale datum that beats the exceptional-mass transfer barrier

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-local-cancellation-needs-a-polynomial-information-budget`.

MC-001 gives an exact black-box transfer bound: local size `eta H`, exceptional measure `B`, and trivial boundedness yield only `eta X+B+O(H)` for a dyadic global sum. Present almost-all short-interval inputs therefore do not approach the RH scale when their signs, overlaps, multiplicative organization, and proof-level structure are discarded.

A surviving local-to-global mechanism must identify the additional datum that prevents this triangle-inequality loss. Natural candidates are signed correlations between overlapping windows, multiscale compatibility of exceptional sets, bilinear/Type-I-II structure, or multiplicative constraints on coherent exceptional mass. A decisive result must produce a polynomial quantitative gain after all exceptional-set and scale-transition losses, or construct a source-faithful obstruction showing why the proposed extra datum still permits large global sums.

## Test power-cancellation-aware pretentiousness beyond the prime-harmonic ceiling

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-single-scale-pretentiousness-has-a-prime-harmonic-ceiling`.

MC-002 proves that the standard single-scale pretentious distance contains only `O(log log x)` prime-harmonic mass, while a generic Halasz exponential would need `Theta(log x)` information to yield a fixed polynomial saving. Trying to optimize the same scalar distance cannot bridge that gap.

The existing proposed clue `CLUE-power-cancellation-aware-pretentiousness` points to a precise possible enrichment: prime-power-sensitive notions designed to detect power cancellation. The decisive question is whether their specialization to Möbius retains genuinely new unconditional information — for example by distinguishing Möbius from Liouville beyond their identical prime values — without requiring a comparator estimate already as strong as the desired Möbius conclusion. Until that non-circular transfer is proved, the enrichment remains a clue rather than evidence.

## Require every global-cancellation statistic to expose its information budget and matched controls

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-local-cancellation-needs-a-polynomial-information-budget` and `MI-002-single-scale-pretentiousness-has-a-prime-harmonic-ceiling`.

Local pseudorandomness, logarithmic averages, correlation bounds, Fourier uniformity, and non-pretentiousness should be evaluated by the quantitative information they retain about the **uniform** summatory target. A statistic that has the correct qualitative symmetry but only logarithmic total mass, or that averages away rare coherent contributions, is diagnostic rather than generative.

A decisive candidate should state the transfer inequality before invoking RH-scale language and survive Liouville, generic multiplicative, random multiplicative, and Beurling/generalized-prime controls at the same retained-information layer. Using `1/zeta`, Perron shifting, or an explicit formula is admissible only when the needed zero-free information is not being imported circularly.
