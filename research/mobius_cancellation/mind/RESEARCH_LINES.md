## Match compulsory reverse discrimination to the actual endpoint channel

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-013-bost-connes-regularity-needs-an-intermediate-transverse-category` through `MI-065-hamming-shell-quantization-has-a-sharp-l1-resolution-tariff`.

The reciprocal endpoint problem has been reduced from syntactic complexity to invariant source discrimination. MC-351--MC-365 establish complementary finite currencies for regular, common-seed and product channels, then show that generic information/Hellinger/Bhattacharyya tariffs are admission conditions rather than generic impossibility theorems: a coordinatewise binary-symmetric channel can attain the forced endpoint scales.

MC-366 gives the first exact source-specific converse for adaptive noiseless coordinate-query transcripts. If `p_i` is the probability that the transcript reads its own source coordinate `X_i`, then for the even-rank punctured cube `rho_i^2 <= p_i + 1/(2^R-1)`. At unit coefficient energy, exact dephasing therefore requires average own-phase revealment at least `1-1/(2^R-1)`.

MC-367 closes the natural parity-query extension. A parity sketch predicts `X_i` only when its query span contains the target direction `e_i`, apart from the exponentially small puncture correction. For one common rank-`q` transcript,

`(1/R) sum_i rho_i^2 <= q/R + 1/((2^R-1)(2^(R-q)-1))`,

so unit-energy exact all-source dephasing requires full rank `q=R`.

MC-368 shows why a real arithmetic endpoint observable cannot be classified by ambient dimension alone. For cell masses `d_i` and source signs `X_i`, the scalar prime-shell bias is

`T=sum_i d_i X_i = 1-2d(J)`,

so exact observation of `T` is a subset-sum channel. At exact balance `d_i=1/R`, own-phase predictability is only `rho_i^2~1/R`, consistent with a large dephasing tariff. But arbitrarily near-balanced integer multiplicities can make every subset sum distinct, in which case the same one-dimensional real observable recovers the entire sign vector and `rho_i=1` for every coordinate.

MC-369 isolates the exact finite-resolution repair. Writing `d_i=1/R+epsilon_i`, the perturbation of the balanced Hamming level `b_K=1-2K/R` is `T-b_K=-2 epsilon(J)`, while `max_J |epsilon(J)|=||epsilon||_1/2`. Hence the nearest-Hamming-shell quotient is statewise stable under the sharp strict condition

`||d-R^(-1)1||_1 < 1/R`.

Inside that margin the quantized scalar sees only Hamming weight, so every coordinate returns to squared posterior predictability `(2^R-R)/(R(2^R-1))` and exact dephasing again costs `R+o(1)` coefficient energy. This does not make near-balance itself sufficient: it shows precisely which additional resolution quotient removes the subset-sum address bits.

The live bridge is now two-part. For the actual Möbius-derived endpoint statistic, prove an arithmetic `L^1` imbalance bound at the relevant rank **and** identify a canonical reason the coefficient architecture cannot resolve below the balanced-shell half-gap. If the observable really reduces to coordinate/parity access, MC-366--MC-367 price the provenance directly. If it has exact-real access, MC-368 remains live regardless of coefficient closeness. If it is canonically coarsened at shell resolution, MC-369 gives the exact admission tariff. A genuinely nonlinear/dependent channel needs its own exact provenance and resolution law rather than another architecture-free divergence bound.
