# MI-005 — Scale doubling can amplify only through iterable contraction; the remaining burden is coupled signed residual information

**Evidence level:** supported by MC-020--MC-027 and MC-031--MC-036; the current Pintz mean-absolute bridge remains externally audited rather than load-bearing here

## Core intuition

A one-step scale-doubling identity is exponent-neutral when read only through the sizes of its input factors, but this does **not** imply that repeated scale doubling can never improve an exponent. The correct distinction is dynamical: an iterable normalized recursion can turn a strict subunit contraction into a power gain along the square-scale tower.

For the Huxley--Watt branch, the hard question has therefore moved away from algebraic degree. It is whether the exact signed boundary/annular residual supplies a strict contraction that survives iteration and global scale coverage without isolating an RH-equivalent coarse mode.

## Strongest justified principle

MC-020--MC-026 remain the one-step controls: harmonic scale doubling exposes an RH-equivalent coarse component; finite jets do not close; higher product degree transports exponents by convex averaging; and unrestricted generalized Möbius convolution erases the cutoff-face information that could distinguish the recursion.

MC-027 supplies the missing iteration boundary. A recurrence of the normalized form `x_{k+1} <= c x_k^2` with `c<1` can amplify a subunit state into a strict power saving when the logarithmic contraction costs are summable with the dyadic weights generated by repeated squaring. Mild shrinking analytic radii of order `1/log N` do not by themselves destroy that mechanism.

MC-031--MC-034 then identify the source-faithful residual more sharply as a Huxley--Watt product annulus with nontrivial cutoff-face and prime-log-slab structure. MC-035--MC-036 show why scalar harmonic shortcuts are dangerous: the log-radial zero mode and fixed Mellin annulus modes expose RH-equivalent coarse Mertens information when controlled separately. The viable object is the **coupled signed annular functional**, not a norm or mode-by-mode absolute estimate.

## Evidence synthesis and boundaries

Iteration is not yet an RH proof. MC-027 is a transfer theorem conditional on obtaining the required strict contraction; it does not supply that contraction from Möbius arithmetic. Likewise the annular decomposition is information-preserving, but its useful cancellation has not yet been proved.

The durable rule is two-sided: do not dismiss a nonlinear recursion from one-step exponent bookkeeping, but do not credit repeated squaring with amplification until the contraction coefficient, additive residual, and interpolation across non-tower scales are all controlled.

## Status / novelty

The scale-doubling identities and analytic iteration mechanisms are classical in spirit; the product-annulus reductions are persisted exact specializations. The synthesis is the corrected separation between one-step neutrality, iterable contraction, and the source-specific residual needed to trigger it.

## Falsification criterion

Derive an unconditional strict normalized contraction for the exact Huxley--Watt annular residual with summable iteration losses and global scale coverage, or prove that every source-compatible residual decomposition necessarily retains an RH-equivalent uncoupled coarse component large enough to prevent such contraction.

## Lean-formalizable core

- One-step exponent transport.
- Iteration of `x_{k+1} <= c_k x_k^2` with weighted logarithmic costs.
- Exact annular/coarse-mode decomposition.
- Logical distinction between coupled cancellation and modewise absolute bounds.
