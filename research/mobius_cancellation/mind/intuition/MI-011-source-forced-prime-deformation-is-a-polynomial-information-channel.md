# MI-011 — Source-forced prime deformation is a polynomial information channel, not yet cancellation

**Evidence level:** exact finite deformation identities through MC-091--MC-093

## Core intuition

A deformation can be genuinely source-forced without creating new cancellation. Exclusive-prime symmetric-difference coordinates are not arbitrary labels: they give exact rectangular variation and an exact fixed-gap Hamming interpolation. But after factorization the apparent many-prime channel collapses to low-complexity product/degree data, and endpoint transport is polynomial.

The useful question is therefore not whether the deformation exists, but whether arithmetic supplies a norm, orthogonality, or signed estimate on that channel that reduces the target-bearing Mertens mode.

## Strongest justified principle

MC-091 factorizes rectangular variation over the exclusive-prime incidence inherited from the source. MC-092 shows that the corresponding product fibers collapse much of the apparent label richness. MC-093 identifies fixed-gap Hamming deformation as a bounded-degree interpolation/variation channel whose endpoint relation is exact.

These identities give a canonical finite-dimensional coordinate system for comparing source-related configurations, but no exponent gain follows from the algebra alone. Any strict contraction must enter as a source estimate on the coefficients/variation or as joint cancellation across the deformation, not as a consequence of having many prime directions.

## What remains possible

A weighted deformation norm may interact with Möbius signs or Euler-factor deletion in a way that changes the top-scale coefficient. A moving family may also expose orthogonality unavailable after taking absolute values. Both require uniform source estimates across the full scale range.

## Status / novelty

Finite differences, product factorizations, Hamming interpolation, and polynomial endpoint identities are elementary. The durable synthesis is the information boundary: **source-forced deformation gives a natural polynomial carrier, but cancellation must be proved on that carrier rather than inferred from its dimensionality**.

## Falsification criterion

Derive a strict Mertens contraction from MC-091--MC-093 without adding a new arithmetic norm/cancellation estimate, or exhibit a source deformation in the stated class whose exact variation cannot be represented by the persisted product/degree channel.
