# MI-044 — The quartic floor is shared by incidence geometry and fixed-modulus bias energy

**Evidence level:** exact/literature-backed synthesis from [MC-323](../../findings/MC-323-varying-modulus-prime-halasz-montgomery-obstruction.md) through [MC-330](../../findings/MC-330-signed-bias-postprocessing-cannot-amplify-quartic-horizon.md). The derivations use different summaries of the same endpoint package and neither proves RH.

The exponent four is no longer only the output of positive pair-incidence accounting. MC-323--MC-327 obtain it by charging common source primes through varying-modulus conductor costs and then show that saturation forces the actual logarithmic source mass into the half-loaded Hamming band. MC-329 reaches the same lower bound before that geometry is used: a fixed-common-modulus character-bias energy estimate already controls every fixed finite character family throughout `q<=y^(4-delta)`.

For the complete reciprocal endpoint family the squared-bias demand is exact, `sum_(a!=0)|x(a)|^2=2^R/R-1`. Under the mild growth condition `R y^(-c_delta) log y ->0`, the fixed-modulus estimate makes that demand impossible if `q=4P<=y^(4-delta)`. Hence the common radical again satisfies `liminf log P/log y>=4`.

The strengthened MC-329 calculation closes a tempting moving-order escape inside this same character input. At Burgess/Halász--Montgomery order `k`, the neutral power threshold is `alpha<4k/(k+1)`. At `alpha=4` the residual exponent remains positive (`1/k^2`), while every fixed `alpha>4` lies on the wrong side for all `k`. Letting `k` grow can approach four from below, but it cannot cross to a fixed power beyond four; the available saving degenerates quadratically near the boundary. Therefore better bookkeeping of the fixed-order constants, by itself, cannot turn this exponent family into a superquartic theorem.

MC-330 sharpens the placement of the remaining signed/joint escape. Let `beta` be the already-formed character-bias vector and `x` its exact reciprocal endpoint. For every coefficient vector `c` and matrix `K`,

`|c^* beta|^2 <= ||c||_2^2 ||beta||_2^2`,

`|beta^* K beta| <= ||K||_op ||beta||_2^2`.

At the endpoint, the normalized supremum in both classes is exactly `||x||_2^2`, attained respectively by `c` proportional to `x`, by `K=I`, and by the endpoint-matched rank-one projector. Thus arbitrary signs, off-diagonal couplings, low rank or endpoint matching **after** the bias family has been compressed to total `ell^2` energy cannot produce a stronger normalized contradiction than MC-329 already has.

The endpoint-matched signed combination is itself an exact Walsh projector onto the `R` endpoint atoms, so its saturation is not evidence of a hidden phase resource. The useful distinction is temporal in the proof architecture: a signed/joint method has to exploit arithmetic structure **before** generic energy compression. It must prove a source-natural estimate for a structured transform `BT` that is genuinely stronger than applying the operator norm of `B` after the existing theorem, while the endpoint retains a comparably large `||Bx||`; or it must use higher-order/nonlinear information whose estimate does not factor through the same `ell^2` energy.

The coincidence of the quartic exponents remains structurally useful because the incidence and fixed-modulus arguments discard different information. The incidence proof remembers which source primes load which modes but pays positive conductor bills. The fixed-modulus proof forgets the column geometry and compresses the whole family into one nonnegative bias-energy statistic. MC-330 now shows that linear/quadratic signed reassembly of that compressed statistic is not a third resource. Rearranging modes, moving the same Burgess order or postprocessing the same bias vector therefore remain inside the same fixed-power boundary.

The next resource must be genuinely new relative to all three operations: a stronger prime-character theorem with different exponent geometry; a structured pre-energy correlation/operator estimate; a higher-order statistic not controlled through the same energy; or a different endpoint/source representation. Within the incidence route, MC-327--MC-328 still say that any economical source lives in the central Hamming band and cannot be resolved by low Fourier degree.

**Boundary.** Equality of the two quartic exponents is a method-boundary diagnosis, not a theorem that exponent four is optimal for the underlying arithmetic problem. MC-330 is likewise a no-go only for downstream linear/quadratic amplification once the existing `ell^2` energy estimate is the analytic input. A stronger character theorem, an arithmetic structured transform controlled before energy collapse, a genuinely higher-order invariant, or a different source representation can escape. The intuition forbids treating another positive reweighting, moving the order inside the same Burgess exponent geometry, or endpoint-matched linear/quadratic postprocessing as independent evidence of fixed-power leverage beyond four.
