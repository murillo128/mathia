# MI-088 — Fixed-rank projective gap geometry is support-rich but natural-source measure can collapse

**Evidence level:** exact/classical determinant and source-geometry synthesis from [MC-440](../../findings/MC-440-path-distance-determinant-buries-gap-order-two-degrees-down.md), [MC-441](../../findings/MC-441-first-order-sensitive-path-coefficient-is-adjacency-endpoint-scalar.md), [MC-442](../../findings/MC-442-distance-polynomial-coefficients-have-universal-sign-rigidity.md), [MC-443](../../findings/MC-443-fixed-rank-prime-gap-shapes-are-projectively-dense.md), [MC-444](../../findings/MC-444-fixed-rank-prime-gap-shape-realization-has-polynomial-source-height.md), and [MC-445](../../findings/MC-445-natural-fixed-rank-sources-collapse-to-terminal-gap-shape.md). Classical determinant, prime-counting and reciprocal-prime inputs are used only inside the persisted reductions.

The path-distance kernel genuinely preserves fixed-rank gap order beyond two permutation-blind leading determinant layers. Its first order-sensitive coefficient factors through the endpoint-plus-nearest-neighbor scalar `J(g)`, but MC-442 shows that every nonconstant coefficient has a universal sign on the positive-gap cone and `J(g)<0`. Order sensitivity therefore does not manufacture a cancellation sign.

MC-443 then removes robust topological prime separation. After normalizing by total span, gap shapes from any fixed number of selected rational primes are dense in the positive simplex. Every continuous scale-invariant fixed-rank statistic consequently has the same image closure on prime-derived and generic positive shapes. MC-444 prices the immediate source-height loophole: on compact interior shape classes, every target shape has a selected-prime approximation of projective accuracy `eta` with squarefree source height polynomial in `eta^(-1)`.

MC-445 shows why support density still does not answer the natural-source question. If one samples uniformly from squarefree `n<=X` with fixed `omega(n)=r`, the normalized linear prime-gap shape converges in probability to the terminal simplex vertex: the largest prime factor absorbs essentially the full linear span while the earlier factors are negligible. Thus the same fixed-rank source family has dense support in the open simplex and a degenerate empirical probability limit at its boundary.

This is a durable separation between **support**, **realization cost**, and **measure**. A source can realize every interior shape at polynomial cost while almost all bounded-height sources concentrate on one boundary shape. Conversely, a nondegenerate distributional statistic cannot be inferred from topological density alone. The natural measure has to be specified and analyzed directly.

For continuous scale-invariant fixed-rank path/gap observables under the canonical uniform squarefree bounded-height ensemble, MC-445 is another no-go: every observable extending continuously to the closed simplex converges to its value at the terminal vertex. The remaining routes require a different ensemble or weighting, growing rank, logarithmic/other coordinates that resolve the smaller factors, absolute scale, discontinuous arithmetic data, or another carrier that survives Möbius full-interaction extraction.

**Boundary.** MC-445 is fixed-rank, uses uniform counting on squarefree integers of bounded height, and uses linearly normalized prime-value gaps. Other source measures, balanced-factor restrictions, logarithmic coordinates and growing rank can behave differently. MC-443/444 use selected primes and give support/existence information, not frequency. None of these representation results yields Möbius cancellation by itself.
