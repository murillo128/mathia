# MI-013 — Finite raw commutator algebra has only support and carried-target phase sectors

**Evidence level:** supported by the scalar hierarchy and exact finite commutator `*`-word classification through MC-165

## Core intuition

The Bost--Connes commutator route has a sharper boundary than “scalarization loses information.” Even before final scalarization, the entire finite raw algebra generated by prime commutators and their adjoints fails to create an intermediate Möbius phase category. Its noncommutative transport can be genuine while the arithmetic phase content remains only support information or a carried copy of the original Möbius orientation.

A viable relational escape must therefore alter the phase-transport law itself, introduce a source-forced relation outside the finite raw `*`-algebra, or use an analytic/infinite completion whose quantitative theorem cannot be reduced to the same two sectors. Merely using longer words, adjoints, Gram matrices, or Lie brackets is now a classified control.

## Strongest justified principle

MC-153 already shows that every fixed depth of scalar mixed prime differences can be matched by a linearly biased control, and MC-154--MC-162 extend the scalar classification through all-depth magnitudes and signed linear readouts. Magnitudes remain cancellation-blind, whereas signed scalar channels become target-complete or same-exponent Mertens-equivalent once the inverse estimate is strong enough.

MC-163 then enters the non-diagonal operator class. The cross-prime Gram relation `C_p^* C_q` is a support-replacement partial isometry multiplied by separable endpoint phase factors, and closed Gram cycles have trivial phase holonomy. MC-164 proves the corresponding raw-word parity law for distinct-prime adjoint-free products: even words are support/divisibility data, while odd words retain the input Möbius sign.

MC-165 closes the finite monomial boundary on square-free inputs. Repeated primes and arbitrary adjoint mixtures still carry one global phase charge; for Möbius it reduces to degree parity. Consequently every finite raw `*`-polynomial splits as a phase-free transport sector plus a sector containing `D_mu` linearly. No third cross-prime Möbius phase is generated by finite word algebra alone.

## Counterevidence / boundary

The theorem is about the canonical represented raw commutators on the square-free sector and finite words/polynomials. It does not classify coefficient-algebra insertions, source-forced twists, modular operations, unbounded affiliated operators, resolvents or norm/weak closures requiring genuine analytic limits, alternative representations, or state functionals that add structure before the two-sector reduction.

Nor does the two-sector normal form prove that every odd-sector scalar observable is globally Mertens-complete. A mask may lose information. The point is narrower: whatever Möbius orientation survives finite raw word algebra is the original orientation carried linearly, not a newly generated relational cancellation law.

## Epistemic status

**Exact closure of scalar commutator regularity and finite raw commutator `*`-word phase generation; a source-forced operation outside that algebra remains open.**

## Falsification criterion

Exhibit a finite raw commutator/adjoint word on square-free inputs whose Möbius phase dependence cannot be reduced to the MC-165 parity form, or a cross-Gram cycle with nontrivial intrinsic phase holonomy. A positive continuation should instead define an independently natural operation outside the classified algebra and prove that it creates a stable relational quantity with additive-excursion content cheaper than reconstructing `D_mu` or `M(x)` itself.
