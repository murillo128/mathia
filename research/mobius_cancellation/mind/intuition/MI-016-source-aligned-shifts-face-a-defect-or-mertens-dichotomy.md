# MI-016 — The surviving Möbius currency is gauge-invariant principal/transverse covariance

**Evidence level:** supported

## Core intuition

Preserving source order is necessary but not sufficient. The first source-aligned observables either concentrate new information on sparse defects, become principal/Mertens-equivalent after natural completion, or move toward pointwise control only by imposing invariant moment hypotheses that are themselves RH-complete. Even the visually source-specific character phase of the prescribed residue is removable by a residue-label gauge.

The surviving arithmetic channel must therefore be a **gauge-invariant signed relation** between principal and transverse source components, or an equivalent lifted integer relation, whose estimate is genuinely cheaper than controlling either component separately at Mertens strength.

## Strongest justified claim

MC-206--MC-208 show the first defect/completion dichotomy. Shifted squarefree support leaves only sparse defect sites; logarithmic prime incidence becomes an invertible Volterra transform of the rough Mertens prefix after prime-power completion; and the square-defect route pays a square-modulus level-doubling cost before cancellation technology begins.

MC-209--MC-213 reorganize the surviving branch. The square-defect tail becomes a prescribed-residue energy whose diagonal has the correct size, but centering removes a principal prefix that is already Mertens-complete. Stable aggregate principal-energy control is itself RH-hard. Small raw source energy in the presence of a large principal component therefore forces a signed principal-centered anti-alignment, making covariance rather than separate energies the live quantity.

MC-214 proves that higher centered moments do not supply a cheap substitute. Their sharp selected-coordinate leverage improves from `d^2` to `d^(2/k)`, but for any fixed `c<1/3`, sufficiently large fixed `k` plus raw prescribed-residue energy control already implies the critical Mertens exponent. Moment order interpolates toward pointwise access and toward RH-completeness at the same time.

MC-215 applies a source-coordinate gauge test. Multiplying residue labels by the distinguished unit moves the selected residue to the identity, preserves every all-residue norm and centered moment, and absorbs the explicit `chi(-q)` phase into the coefficient vector. Hence that phase is not invariant arithmetic information. The invariant object is the value at the distinguished coordinate and its signed covariance with the principal mode, or a relation that keeps the lift `n+q=d^2m` and interval order.

## Synthesis of evidence

The line now has a strong admission test for proposed escapes. Identify whether the observable survives support sparsification, natural completion, high-moment pricing, and residue relabeling. If it does not, it is another presentation of a known obstruction. If it does, show that its sign or cancellation is forced by the actual Möbius source rather than by a generic norm inequality.

The most concrete surviving route is to work with lifted square-defect solutions or another source-selected construction in which principal and transverse pieces are never separated before their cancellation is used.

## Counterevidence / boundary cases

MC-214 gives one-way implications only; RH for zeta alone does not control all centered character moments. MC-215 does not erase the arithmetic values of the translated coefficient vector and does not prove the live covariance is small. The square-defect lift remains a genuine source structure that a residue-label permutation cannot remove.

Other source-aligned observables may avoid square moduli or have a different completion. The synthesis is a gate on the audited branch, not a no-go for every Möbius construction.

## Epistemic status

**Supported route-narrowing:** sparse defects, principal completion, invariant moment upgrades, and bare character phases are now controlled. The remaining candidate information is signed, source-selected, and gauge invariant.

## Novelty/prior-art status

The analytic and Fourier ingredients retain their finding-level prior-art classifications. This intuition only synthesizes their role in the current Möbius line.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a successful estimate in the current square-defect setting using only all-residue norms or the explicit `chi(-q)` phase after the MC-215 gauge normalization, or show that a fixed higher-moment hypothesis below the stated MC-214 threshold yields the required source sign without Mertens-strength input. Either would reopen a controlled branch.
