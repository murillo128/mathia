# MI-047 — Low-degree mode dependence is an endpoint anti-dephasing resource

**Evidence level:** exact finite-endpoint synthesis from [MC-332](../../findings/MC-332-arbitrary-mode-dependent-prime-rows-exactly-dephase-the-reciprocal-endpoint.md) through [MC-335](../../findings/MC-335-low-degree-mode-dependence-cannot-approximate-dephasing.md).

MC-332 shows that arbitrary dependence of prime coefficients on the endpoint mode is too expressive: the conjugate-character matched filter erases the phase exactly, even though its evaluation matrix has rank only `R`. MC-333--MC-334 then show that rank, total Frobenius energy and centered variation are separate currencies.

MC-335 identifies another independent currency: **Fourier degree of the map from mode to coefficient row**. Write the reciprocal endpoint modes as the nonzero vectors of `G=F_2^R`. If every prime-coordinate function `a -> w_{a,r}` extends to a Walsh polynomial of degree at most `d`, then multiplication by the one-defect character geometry shifts its spectrum into an explicit reachable set `Omega_d`. The entire normalized output lies in the Walsh subspace generated by `Omega_d`; no choice of coefficient magnitudes can escape that subspace.

For `1<=d<=R-2`, the missing low-weight frequencies have exact cardinality

`q_d = sum_(k=1)^(R-2-d) binom(R,k)`,

and the best squared approximation error to the uniform all-mode dephasing target is

`N q_d/(q_d+1)`, with `N=2^R` in the finding's normalization.

In particular, if `d<=R-3` the normalized family RMS error is bounded away from zero, and when `d=o(R)` it approaches the full target scale. Genuinely low-degree mode dependence therefore cannot even approximately emulate the dangerous matched filter. The obstruction disappears sharply near the top degree: at `d=R-2` exact dephasing becomes possible.

This is not a reformulation of the rank or variation bounds. A function class can have large rowspace rank while remaining low Walsh degree, or small rowspace rank while containing highly nonlinear mode dependence. Likewise the centered variation of MC-334 measures distance from one common input, whereas MC-335 measures which mode frequencies the architecture is allowed to synthesize. They constrain different failure modes.

The live admission test for a structured joint coefficient class is therefore multi-axis. Before calling mode dependence “mild,” bound its Fourier degree in the natural endpoint mode coordinates, its distance from the common-input subspace, and the coefficient norm actually charged by the analytic theorem. A source-natural family that is provably low degree would exclude the exact programmable dephaser for a structural reason that rank alone cannot see.

**Boundary.** The degree theorem is exact for the finite reciprocal endpoint and its one-defect character geometry. It does not show that a useful arithmetic coefficient family has low degree, and near-maximal degree already restores exact programmability. Sparse target amplitudes, different mode sets, nonlinear output statistics or another analytic norm require a separate capacity calculation. Nothing here bounds `M(x)` or proves RH.
