# MI-002 — Standard single-scale pretentiousness has a prime-harmonic information ceiling

**Evidence level:** supported by MC-002 and classical Halasz/pretentious theory

## Core intuition

A scalar statistic cannot drive a polynomial cancellation theorem if its total available scale is only logarithmic in `log x` while the transfer theorem would require order `log x`. For the standard pretentious distance this mismatch is exact: every prime contributes at most `2/p`, so the full distance squared is only `O(log log x)`.

## Strongest justified principle

MC-002 shows that the standard Halasz parameter `M(f;x,T)` is universally bounded above by `2 log log x+O(1)`. A black-box exponential term `(1+M)e^{-M}` would need `M` of order `log x` to give any fixed power saving. For Möbius the untwisted distance to `1` already saturates the prime-harmonic scale, yet the resulting generic mean-value bound is only logarithmically small and far weaker than the known unconditional global bound.

Thus the obstruction is not failure to optimize the usual distance. An RH-relevant pretentious route must enlarge the information carrier or use additional structure outside the single scalar prime-only distance.

## What remains possible

Prime-power-sensitive or multiscale notions of pretentiousness are natural candidates because Möbius and Liouville agree on primes but differ on prime powers. The existing proposed clue on power-cancellation-aware pretentiousness isolates this possibility. It becomes a durable mechanism only if its transfer hypotheses can be verified unconditionally at a scale that improves Möbius cancellation without assuming an equally difficult comparator bound.

## Status / novelty

The prime-harmonic estimate and Halasz theory are classical. The durable synthesis is the scale mismatch between the total information available to the standard distance and the information demanded by a polynomial global conclusion.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a theorem using only the standard single-scale pretentious scalar that yields a fixed power saving without introducing another quantity carrying order-`log x` information, or show that a proposed enrichment merely re-encodes a power-cancellation hypothesis as strong as its desired conclusion.
