# MI-073 — Thin endpoint geometry survives, but every positive scalar finite-band stationary differencing scheme pays the Fejér diagonal

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from MC-409--[MC-417](../../findings/MC-417-fejer-riesz-closes-positive-finite-band-kernels.md), including the endpoint factorization of [MC-414](../../findings/MC-414-shift-position-kernel-is-endpoint-separable.md). No Möbius cancellation beyond the cited estimates is claimed.

The well-factorable frame creates a translated character before completion. MC-414 shows that in endpoint variables the phase factorizes as `chi(x) conjugate(chi(y))`; the genuinely source-sensitive object is the thin divisor-dependent endpoint domain. Full completion still fills that domain and collapses back to standard scalar character information.

MC-415 identifies the zero-shift cost of ordinary positive van der Corput/Cauchy differencing: the diagonal re-squares the sieve and recreates the lcm quadratic form before off-diagonal gain has been earned. MC-416 showed that signs or phases in one pre-square filter cannot lower its normalized diagonal below `1/H`.

MC-417 closes the entire scalar positive finite-band translation-invariant cone, not just the one-filter presentation. If a Hermitian bandwidth-`H` kernel has nonnegative symbol `P_K` and nonzero DC response, then `K(0)/P_K(0) >= 1/H`, with equality only for the Fejér kernel. Fejér--Riesz factorization writes every such positive symbol as the modulus square of one length-`H` filter. Consequently filter banks, positive Toeplitz forms and sums of positive shift energies are only different realizations of the same cone and cannot improve the normalized lcm diagonal.

The reusable audit is now **thin-domain off-diagonal gain versus the extremal Fejér diagonal, unless the method leaves scalar positive finite-band stationarity altogether**. A viable continuation must either prove collective incomplete endpoint cancellation strong enough to compensate for that diagonal, or use an indefinite identity with a separately controlled negative part, a nonstationary/modulus-factorized process, or a genuinely higher-dimensional arithmetic object whose positivity is not scalar finite-band Toeplitz positivity.

**Boundary.** MC-417 does not rule out off-diagonal arithmetic gain that compensates for a worse positive kernel, nor any method outside the scalar translation-invariant positive finite-band cone.
