# MI-073 — Positive stationary kernels pay for effective translation support, not ambient coordinates

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from MC-409--[MC-420](../../findings/MC-420-lattice-rank-controls-positive-kernel-fejer-floor.md), including the endpoint factorization of [MC-414](../../findings/MC-414-shift-position-kernel-is-endpoint-separable.md) and the multivariable Fejér theorem [MC-419](../../findings/MC-419-multivariable-positive-box-kernels-tensorized-fejer-floor.md). No Möbius cancellation beyond the cited estimates is claimed.

The well-factorable frame creates a translated character before completion, and MC-414 localizes the genuinely source-sensitive information in the thin divisor-dependent endpoint domain. MC-415--MC-417 then identify the cost of imposing one-variable finite-band stationary positivity: the zero-shift diagonal cannot fall below the Fejér `1/H` floor after DC normalization. MC-418 shows that replacing scalar coefficients by an arbitrary Hilbert/operator-valued channel does not change that bound. Rank and channel multiplicity are not independent averaging directions.

MC-419 classifies the genuine positive multivariable extension. If `P` is a positive operator-valued trigonometric polynomial on `T^d` supported in the rectangular box `|h_j|<H_j`, with zero coefficient `K_0`, then

`P(xi) <= (prod_j H_j) K_0`

in Loewner order for every `xi`, and the tensor-product Fejér kernel is sharp. Independent translation axes can therefore multiply positive-kernel bandwidth, but target dimension carried at each axis gives no further gain.

MC-420 identifies the exact effective dimension. If the Fourier support of `P` lies in `A Z^r` for an injective integer map `A:Z^r->Z^d`, then `P(theta)=Q(A^T theta)` for a positive operator-valued symbol `Q` on `T^r`; the torus map `A^T` is surjective and preserves the mean. The positive-kernel problem therefore factors through the support lattice before any ambient `d`-dimensional Fejér estimate is credited.

This kills a common formal escape. A two-coordinate symbol supported only on the anti-diagonal `h -> (h,-h)` is still rank one and pays `1/H`, not `1/H^2`. Likewise writing an endpoint kernel in variables `x,y` or carrying `chi(x) overline{chi(y)}` does not manufacture a second averaging direction unless the Fourier support actually contains two `Z`-linearly independent source translations.

The reusable audit is: compute the integer lattice generated by the physical translation support, reduce to that torus, and only then price positivity by its support geometry. For rectangular full-rank support this recovers the product Fejér volume. For sparse or non-product support, lattice rank alone need not be sharp and the extremal constant must follow the actual support set.

**Boundary.** MC-420 concerns finite stationary positive kernels and exact lattice-supported Fourier geometry. It does not rule out nonstationary kernels, controlled indefinite forms, infinite-support limits, or source-specific off-diagonal cancellation, and it does not prove that the Möbius architecture supplies more than one independent translation direction.
