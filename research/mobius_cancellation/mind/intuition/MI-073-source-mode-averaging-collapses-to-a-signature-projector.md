# MI-073 — Formal averaging, incidence algebra and quotient fibers collapse unless independently controlled signed structure survives

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [MC-397](../../findings/MC-397-source-mode-averaging-collapses-to-signature-projector.md)--[MC-406](../../findings/MC-406-mangoldt-quotient-fibers-collapse-to-harmonic-mobius.md), interpreted against the source-rank/codimension framework of MC-385--MC-396.

MC-397 shows that averaging squared source modes Fourier-collapses to a signature-subgroup projector. MC-398 shows that the Selberg divisor pair collapses through its lcm fibre coefficient, MC-399 removes the surviving lcm character scalar at first additive correlation, and MC-400--MC-402 consume moving endpoints, the complete endpoint and generic truncation through exact inversion/factorization.

MC-403 identifies what remains when the whole source family is retained. If `B_omega(X)` is the signed lcm coefficient mass in source-signature class `omega`, Parseval gives

`sum_(I!=0) |A_I(X)|^2 = H sum_omega |B_omega(X)-B_bar(X)|^2`.

After truncation inversion, the nonprincipal family energy is exactly the squared norm of a Mertens-weighted signed signature-discrepancy vector. Collective source averaging is therefore not a second cancellation resource beyond that vector.

MC-404 strengthens the lcm audit from a quadratic regrouping to an algebraic normal form. For any finite divisor set, the pointwise algebra generated by `1_{d|n}` is exactly the finite lcm join-semilattice algebra because `e_d e_e=e_[d,e]`. Every finite Boolean function, polynomial, higher-degree Selberg weight or threshold built only from those incidences has a unique expansion over the lcm closure. No finite incidence-only enrichment can manufacture a retained tuple variable before the analytic estimate begins.

MC-405 supplies a matched control outside the lcm fibres. The Möbius--Mangoldt identity

`sum_(n<=x) mu(n) log n = -sum_(b<=x) Lambda(b) M(floor(x/b))`

retains a genuine prime-power quotient variable, but the whole signed family aggregates exactly back to a first-order transform of `M`. Pointwise power control plus triangle inequality has linear unsigned mass, so quotient survival alone creates no bootstrap.

MC-406 identifies the next exact quotient. If `k=floor(x/b)`, then only the fiber total

`W_k(x)=psi(x/k)-psi(x/(k+1))`

is visible to the aggregate, and there are only `O(sqrt(x))` distinct quotient values. Splitting `psi(y)=y+E(y)` gives

`sum_b Lambda(b)M(floor(x/b)) = x(m(x)-M(x)/(x+1)) + sum_k M(k)(E(x/k)-E(x/(k+1)))`.

Thus the PNT main component of the retained quotient family is exactly classical harmonic Möbius cancellation. The first fiber itself has mass asymptotic to `x/2`, so reducing the family to `O(sqrt x)` coordinates does not create square-root cancellation; large cross-fiber cancellation is compulsory.

The reusable audit now has three gates. First ask whether a proposed index/tuple/family survives exact Fourier, lcm, inversion and incidence reductions. If it does, compute whether the retained family self-couples back to the original quantity after aggregation. Then identify the **finest statistic still distinguished by the downstream observable**: here it is the floor-quotient mass `W_k`, not the individual source label `b`. A variable counts only when source-specific signed/phase structure survives all three gates and yields control not already equivalent to the original Mertens problem.

For the current frame, the remaining possibilities are narrow: prove a useful signed relation between `M(k)` and the prime-error increments `E(x/k)-E(x/(k+1))`; retain phase/congruence/translation data inside a quotient fiber before it is summed to `W_k`; or prove signed near-equidistribution of the actual lcm/signature coefficients. Merely preserving `b`, `x/b`, family size or quotient-coordinate count is no longer sufficient.

**Boundary.** MC-403 is an exact `L^2` identity and lower-bound obstruction, not an upper bound. MC-404 is an algebraic collapse theorem for finite incidence-only observables. MC-405 rules out naive use of the canonical Möbius--Mangoldt quotient family with pointwise bounds/absolute values. MC-406 rules out interpreting its floor-quotient compression or PNT main kernel as new cancellation; it leaves prime-error cross-correlation and within-fiber discriminators open. None of these results proves a bound for `M` or RH.
