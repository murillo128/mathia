# MI-073 — Retained arithmetic variables can disappear at lcm reduction, scalarization or completion unless controlled first

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from MC-397--[MC-413](../../findings/MC-413-additive-differencing-complete-block-ramanujan-collapse.md), interpreted against the source-rank/codimension framework of MC-385--MC-396.

MC-397--MC-404 show that finite source averaging and divisor-incidence enrichment collapse through signature and lcm algebras. MC-405--MC-408 then demonstrate a different failure mode: a source variable can survive those reductions, remain finite-prefix invertible and still aggregate into a scalar correlation whose square-root estimate is RH-equivalent. Information retention and analytic leverage are separate properties.

MC-409 opens a representation outside the quadratic Selberg/lcm bottleneck. A well-factorable upper-sieve frame preserves nonnegativity, prime normalization and the correct diagonal scale. But MC-410--MC-412 show that factorability alone is not the desired interaction: the bare multiplicative phase is separable, plain Fourier completion only permutes dual frequencies, and a zero-residue divisor condition does not inherit the affine arithmetic of well-factorable progression theorems.

MC-413 supplies the first tested operation that genuinely creates the missing translation. After additive differencing, the inner phase is `chi(n+h) overline{chi(n)}` and the divisor pair enters through compatibility and CRT data. Yet a full conductor block has exact primitive-character autocorrelation `c_q(h)`, independent of that divisor geometry. Thus even a genuinely nonseparable variable can be destroyed by the **completion step** rather than by the earlier lcm or scalar quotients.

The reusable audit now has five gates. Ask whether a proposed variable survives Fourier/lcm/incidence reduction; whether aggregation self-couples it to the original target; whether the remaining scalar coordinate is analytically cheaper; whether a genuinely translated phase survives the completion actually used; and, if only incomplete pieces retain it, whether they can be controlled collectively before Cauchy or triangle inequalities discard the coupling.

For the current frame, the useful factorable object is therefore the sub-conductor family of translated character sums indexed by the divisor-pair CRT data. Its existence is not itself a saving. A positive result has to change the post-Cauchy diagonal or conductor tariff while keeping the joint family intact. The alternative truncated-lcm route likewise needs a theorem specific to the real source coefficients rather than another exact re-encoding.

**Boundary.** MC-413 gives an exact complete/incomplete decomposition, not an improved character-sum or Mertens estimate. Classical q-van der Corput theory shows that incomplete shifted correlations can be exploitable in other modulus factorizations; it does not automatically estimate this weighted family. None of MC-397--MC-413 proves RH.
