# MI-073 — Formal averaging and finite divisor incidence collapse to exact lcm/signature discrepancy

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [MC-397](../../findings/MC-397-source-mode-averaging-collapses-to-signature-projector.md)--[MC-404](../../findings/MC-404-divisor-incidence-algebra-collapses-to-lcm-semilattice.md), interpreted against the source-rank/codimension framework of MC-385--MC-396.

MC-397 shows that averaging squared source modes Fourier-collapses to a signature-subgroup projector. MC-398 shows that the Selberg divisor pair collapses through its lcm fibre coefficient, MC-399 removes the surviving lcm character scalar at first additive correlation, and MC-400--MC-402 consume moving endpoints, the complete endpoint and generic truncation through exact inversion/factorization.

MC-403 identifies what remains when the whole source family is retained. If `B_omega(X)` is the signed lcm coefficient mass in source-signature class `omega`, Parseval gives

`sum_(I!=0) |A_I(X)|^2 = H sum_omega |B_omega(X)-B_bar(X)|^2`.

After the truncation inversion, the nonprincipal family energy is exactly the squared norm of a Mertens-weighted signed signature-discrepancy vector. Collective source averaging is therefore not a second cancellation resource beyond that vector.

MC-404 strengthens the lcm audit from a quadratic regrouping to an algebraic normal form. For any finite divisor set, the pointwise algebra generated by the incidence predicates `1_{d|n}` is exactly the finite lcm join-semilattice algebra because `e_d e_e=e_[d,e]`. Every finite Boolean function, polynomial, higher-degree Selberg weight or threshold built only from those incidences has a unique expansion over the lcm closure. No finite incidence-only enrichment can manufacture a retained tuple variable before the analytic estimate begins.

The reusable test is therefore stronger than “expand the quadratic form.” Before crediting a formal index, tuple, cutoff, family average or nonlinear incidence feature as new information, perform exact Fourier/regrouping reductions, the first correlation intended to exploit it, explicit inversion when available, endpoint factorization, semilattice reduction of residual divisor incidence, and Parseval on any surviving source family. **A variable counts only if a source-specific relation survives these audits and makes the exact downstream discrepancy smaller.**

For the current frame, the remaining possibilities are correspondingly narrow: prove signed near-equidistribution of the actual lcm coefficient across source signatures, obtain a genuinely joint estimate for the Mertens-weighted discrepancy vector, or leave the divisor-incidence/lcm frame with a variable that changes downstream arithmetic inside an lcm fibre.

**Boundary.** MC-403 is an exact `L^2` identity and lower-bound obstruction, not an upper bound. MC-404 is an algebraic collapse theorem for finite incidence-only observables; it does not rule out arithmetic data carried by quotients, phases, congruences, translations, intervals or other variables not constant on lcm fibres. None of these results proves a bound for `M`, an RH equivalence, or uselessness of structured truncated lcm sums.
