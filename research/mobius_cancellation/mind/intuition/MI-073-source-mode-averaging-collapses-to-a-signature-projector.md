# MI-073 — Thin endpoint geometry survives, but every positive one-filter differencing scheme pays the diagonal

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from MC-409--[MC-416](../../findings/MC-416-weighted-vdc-filters-cannot-remove-diagonal.md), including the endpoint factorization of [MC-414](../../findings/MC-414-shift-position-kernel-is-endpoint-separable.md). No Möbius cancellation beyond the cited estimates is claimed.

The well-factorable frame creates a translated character before completion. MC-414 shows that after passing from shift/position variables to endpoints `x=m+j`, `y=m`, the phase factorizes as `chi(x) conjugate(chi(y))`; the genuinely source-sensitive object is the thin divisor-dependent endpoint domain, not a new irreducible two-variable character mode. Full completion still fills in that domain and collapses the problem back to standard scalar character information.

MC-415 identifies the zero-shift cost of ordinary positive van der Corput/Cauchy differencing: the diagonal energy `sum |w(n)chi(n)|^2` re-squares the linear sieve and recreates the lcm quadratic form before off-diagonal gain has been earned. MC-416 closes the simplest proposed escape. For an arbitrary linear shift filter `c_j`, the positive quadratic energy has autocorrelation kernel `K_c` with `K_c(0)=sum |c_j|^2>0`; after normalizing by the DC response `sigma_c=sum c_j`, Cauchy gives `K_c(0)/|sigma_c|^2 >= 1/H`, with equality only for the constant filter. Signs or phases before squaring cannot reduce the normalized diagonal below the standard Fejer window.

More generally a nontrivial positive-definite shift kernel cannot have zero diagonal: `K(0)=0` forces `K(h)=0` for every `h`. A genuinely diagonal-free shift identity must therefore be indefinite and obtain control from arithmetic structure rather than positivity alone.

The reusable audit is now **thin-domain off-diagonal gain versus an unavoidable positive diagonal**. A viable continuation must either prove collective incomplete endpoint cancellation strong enough to offset that diagonal, or change the architecture—an indefinite correlation identity, modulus-factorized/nonstationary `A`-process, or another operation whose diagonal is genuinely different. Merely reweighting the same positive sliding-window energy is closed.

**Boundary.** MC-416 optimizes only the diagonal ratio inside the one-filter/Cauchy architecture. It does not rule out a nonconstant filter whose off-diagonal arithmetic contribution compensates for a worse diagonal, nor an indefinite or modulus-changing argument.
