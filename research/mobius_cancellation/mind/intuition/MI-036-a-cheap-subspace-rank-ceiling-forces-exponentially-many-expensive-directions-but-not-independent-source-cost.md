# MI-036 — A cheap-subspace rank ceiling forces exponentially many expensive directions, but not independent source cost

**Evidence level:** exact/literature-derived finite-geometry consequence from [MC-311](../../findings/MC-311-bose-burton-page-expensive-direction-density.md), using the reciprocal Page-cheap rank obstruction [MC-310](../../findings/MC-310-reciprocal-endpoint-page-cheap-subspace-2-adic-rigidity.md) and the classical Bose--Burton extremal theorem

For the reciprocal one-defect endpoint, let `L=F_2^R` and put `v=nu_2(R)`. MC-310 shows that, after removing the possible unique Landau--Page exceptional mode, the Page-cheap spectrum cannot contain the nonzero points of any `(v+1)`-dimensional subspace.

MC-311 converts that local linear obstruction into a global density theorem. Bose--Burton gives

`|S_y^0| <= 2^R-2^(R-v)`

for the nonexceptional cheap set, so the Page-expensive set satisfies

`|E_y| >= 2^(R-v)-2`,

and `|E_y| >= 2^(R-v)-1` when no cheap exceptional direction is present. Since `2^v<=R`, at least `2^R/R-O(1)` directions are expensive uniformly in `R`; when `R` is odd, all but at most one nonzero direction are expensive.

The reusable step is **forbidden-flat geometry -> complement density**. A low-dimensional ceiling on every cheap linear sector can force a macroscopic or exponential complement even though an arbitrary large subset need not contain a large subspace. The endpoint's binary projective geometry is what makes the conversion exact.

But direction cardinality is not source cost. Bose--Burton is sharp with the expensive complement equal to one projective flat. Exponentially many Page-expensive functionals may therefore reuse the same lower-prime coordinates, the same common radical or another highly overlapping source package. Summing their individual source thresholds would double-count shared arithmetic information.

The next proof obligation is consequently an incidence/overlap theorem. It must turn a Bose--Burton-sized expensive family into a hereditary resource that cannot be shared away: many distinct source coordinates, a large union radical, bounded reuse multiplicity, a submodular conductor cost, or another source-native quantity. Conversely, a matched arithmetic construction in which an extremal expensive flat is generated by a small common source package would show that exponential direction density is still noncoercive.

**Boundary.** The density theorem is specific to the reciprocal equal-mass endpoint and the discrete-resolution/Page regime inherited from MC-310. It does not remove the possible Page exception, force disjoint representatives, estimate Mertens, or imply that source costs add across directions. The finite-geometry theorem closes the cardinality question, not the arithmetic incidence question.
