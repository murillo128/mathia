# MI-019 — Shell-square blindness is a localized bipartite problem; fixed-order tame residue algebra is universal once localization is removed

**Evidence level:** proved for the shell-square full-annihilator geometry through MC-256 under each finding's stated hypotheses. MC-247 supplies the conditional GRH/ERH support instance; MC-252 and MC-256 are exact nonlocalized universality controls; MC-253 gives unconditional fixed-order shell parity laws; MC-254--MC-255 give genuine-prime Rédei controls. No full shell generation, moving-shell higher-symbol theorem, wild prime-square rigidity or fixed-power signed cancellation is proved.

MC-238--MC-246 identify smooth-dilation blindness with the annihilator generated by lower primes and reduce the blind family to one rough Möbius multiplicative coset. At quadratic level the destination-visible object is a rectangular lower-prime/shell-prime code.

MC-252 shows that quadratic-residue algebra without localization can realize arbitrary finite binary cross-prime matrices. MC-253 shows that the actual moving shell has real bounded-order parity pseudorandomness, but logarithmic-rank controls can share the same qualitative moments. Fixed-order shell moments are therefore rank-blind.

MC-254--MC-255 show why completed class-group ranks do not repair this. The selected cross-block can have arbitrarily large kernel while the completed Rédei matrix has maximal rank and all higher `2^j` class-group ranks vanish; the shell-shell block can remove the destination-blind vectors before the classical higher-rank maps are even defined.

MC-256 now extends the nonlocalized universality obstruction to **every fixed tame prime residue order**. Kummer independence plus Chebotarev realize an arbitrary `F_ell` exponent matrix on prescribed row primes using arbitrarily large column primes carrying order-`ell` characters. Equivalently, every compatible `ell`-ary blind code can occur. Passing from Legendre symbols to fixed-order higher power residues therefore does not create generic rigidity.

The remaining algebraic resource must be absent from that construction: quantitative moving-shell localization, simultaneous distribution at the required scale, a source-coupled invariant that is not determined by the local characters, residue order growing with scale, or the wild order-`r` direction modulo `r^2`. The alternative is to bypass code generation and estimate the rough Möbius coset directly.

**Boundary.** MC-256 is qualitative and nonlocalized. It does not realize arbitrary matrices with all column primes in `(y,2y]`, does not control the actual Mathia shell quotient, does not treat growing residue order, and does not touch the wild prime-square character direction. Those are precisely the remaining categories rather than details hidden by the theorem.
