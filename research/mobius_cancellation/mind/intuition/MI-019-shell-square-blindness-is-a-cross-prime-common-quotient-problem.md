# MI-019 — Shell-square blindness reduces to one rough Möbius coset; support can squeeze torsion order without determining quotient rank

**Evidence level:** proved for the shell-square full-annihilator geometry through MC-249; MC-247 supplies the conditional GRH/ERH support instance, while MC-248--MC-249 give deterministic implications from a stated witness/support bound. No full generation or fixed-power signed cancellation is proved.

MC-238--MC-246 identify smooth-dilation blindness with the annihilator of the subgroup `H` generated by the small primes modulo the shell-square modulus and invert the complete blind family. Finite-group orthogonality turns the whole complete-annihilator contribution into one rough Möbius multiplicative coset `-H`; blind index and coset density cancel exactly. The true structural object is the quotient `Q=G/H`.

MC-247--MC-248 show that generic witness information acts first on **support**. A theorem `W(q)<=C(log q)^A` forces a nonprincipal blind character to involve `>>y^(1/A)/log y` shell primes. For fixed `A>1` this is sublinear relative to shell dimension, and matched linear-code controls show that such minimum distance is compatible with a large kernel. Support alone cannot prove `Q=1`.

MC-249 adds progression occupancy and thereby accesses a different invariant: **torsion order**. If `d=ell^a` divides `exp(Q)`, an order-`d` blind character requires active shell primes in the progression `1 mod d`. Brun--Titchmarsh bounds that occupancy from above, while MC-242/MC-247-type information bounds active support from below. The combination yields, unconditionally,

`ell^a << y/(log log y log log log y)`

for every prime-power divisor of `exp(Q)`, and under the ERH support input `ell^a << sqrt(y)`. More generally a schematic `O(log^A q)` witness scale yields `ell^a=O(y^(1-1/A))`; a formal linear-log scale would force bounded exponent rather than merely linear support.

This does not make the quotient small. Exponent and rank are independent: `(Z/2Z)^m` has bounded exponent and unbounded size. The obstruction has therefore changed shape from “possibly sparse high-order blind relations” to “possibly many-coordinate low-order relations.” A generation proof must control primary rank/multiplicity or exploit simultaneous shell dependencies; alternatively the analytic exit remains direct signed cancellation of the exact rough Möbius coset.

The durable separation is now three-stage: witness bounds constrain support; progression occupancy converts support into torsion-order bounds; neither controls quotient rank or signed amplitude. Treating the increasingly smooth torsion spectrum as approximate full generation would conflate distinct group invariants.

**Boundary.** MC-249 uses all primes in the progression as a safe occupancy upper bound and may be far from sharp for a sparse selected shell. Its symbolic witness implication is conditional on the stated witness theorem. No bound on `exp(Q)` alone bounds `|Q|`, no rank theorem is proved, and none of these algebraic constraints estimates the signed rough Möbius source term.
