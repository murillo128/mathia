# MI-019 — Shell-square blindness reduces to one rough Möbius coset; minimum support still does not determine the quotient

**Evidence level:** proved for the shell-square full-annihilator geometry through MC-246; MC-247 conditionally strengthens blind-support and quadratic-rank lower bounds under ERH, without proving full generation or fixed-power cancellation.

MC-238--MC-243 identify smooth-dilation blindness with the annihilator of the subgroup `H` generated by the small primes modulo the shell-square modulus. MC-244--MC-245 invert the quadratic blind family, showing that mode count cancels against affine-cell density and that the smooth source collapses to a rough Möbius source.

MC-246 performs the exact inversion for the **complete annihilator** `A=H^perp`. Finite-group orthogonality turns the whole blind contribution into one multiplicative coset rather than a family whose cardinality supplies a saving. For the physical smooth-inverse source the surviving term is a rough Möbius sum on `-H`; blind index and coset density cancel exactly.

This makes the true structural object the quotient `G/H`. Quadratic full rank sees only its elementary `2`-quotient: it says `[G:H]` is odd, not that `H=G`. Higher-order blind characters can therefore survive after every quadratic blind mode has vanished.

MC-247 tests whether generic strong zero information can bridge that gap. Under ERH, Bach's proper-subgroup witness theorem and the exact active conductor imply that every nonprincipal blind character must involve

`k(chi) >> sqrt(y)/log y`

shell primes. In the quadratic sector this gives minimum distance on the same scale and Hamming pressure only

`rank(A) >> sqrt(y)`.

The full shell has size `asymp y/log y`, and abstract coding controls still permit kernels with this minimum-distance scale and redundancy only `O(sqrt(y))`. Thus even the ERH-strengthened support floor is parametrically too weak to force full quadratic rank, much less `H=G`. **Minimum blind support, quadratic rank, full quotient index, coset density, and signed rough-coset cancellation remain distinct currencies even under ERH.**

The two exact exits therefore remain unchanged but sharper. A structural proof must exploit simultaneous shell arithmetic strongly enough to show `H=G`, not merely that every surviving relation has large support. The analytic alternative is fixed-power signed cancellation of the one source-selected rough Möbius coset `-H`, equivalently cancellation across the complete annihilator aggregate.

**Boundary.** MC-247 is conditional on ERH and does not bound `[G:H]`, count blind characters, prove full rank, prove `H=G`, or estimate the signed rough-coset sum. Its value is diagnostic: a generic least-witness theorem can make blind modes very nonlocal while still leaving far too much quotient capacity.
