# MI-019 — Shell-square blindness reduces to one rough Möbius coset; shell occupancy couples torsion order to primary multiplicity

**Evidence level:** proved for the shell-square full-annihilator geometry through MC-250; MC-247 supplies the conditional GRH/ERH support instance, while MC-248--MC-250 give deterministic implications from a stated witness/support bound. No full generation or fixed-power signed cancellation is proved.

MC-238--MC-246 identify smooth-dilation blindness with the annihilator of the subgroup `H` generated by the small primes modulo the shell-square modulus and invert the complete blind family. Finite-group orthogonality turns the whole complete-annihilator contribution into one rough Möbius multiplicative coset `-H`; blind index and coset density cancel exactly. The true structural object is the quotient `Q=G/H`.

MC-247--MC-249 separate the first currencies. A witness theorem constrains the support of every nonprincipal blind character. Progression occupancy then converts that support floor into an upper bound on every prime-power torsion order `ell^a` that can survive in `Q`. This squeezes large-order blind structure but does not by itself control multiplicity.

MC-250 supplies the missing first rank coupling. At level `d=ell^a`, take the order-`ell` shadow of the `ell`-primary blind subgroup. It is an `F_ell`-linear code on the order-compatible shell primes `r=1 mod d`, with dimension exactly the number `m_(ell,a)` of invariant factors whose order is at least `ell^a`, and minimum distance at least the universal active-support floor `D_y`. Singleton therefore gives the exact inequality

`D_y + m_(ell,a) - 1 <= n_d`,

where `n_d` is the number of selected shell primes congruent to `1 mod ell^a`. Equivalently, primary multiplicity is bounded by **occupancy slack**: `m_(ell,a) <= n_d-D_y+1`.

Combining this with Brun--Titchmarsh couples order and multiplicity quantitatively. With `R=D_y+m_(ell,a)-1`, one obtains `ell^a <= (4+o(1)) y/(R log R)` when `R->infinity`, and the leading constant improves to `2` for large prime divisors. High torsion order and high multiplicity cannot coexist at the same shell scale.

The obstruction has therefore narrowed again. The dangerous residual is not arbitrary quotient rank but **many low-order relations living in a large order-compatible shell**. For fixed small `ell`, the ambient shell can still be much larger than `D_y`, and generic linear codes with vanishing relative distance can have near-full dimension. Closing the quotient requires arithmetic structure beyond minimum support plus coarse progression occupancy—simultaneous residue dependencies, near-minimal selected-shell occupancy, or another constraint on the low-order primary layers. The analytic alternative remains direct signed cancellation of the exact rough Möbius coset.

**Boundary.** MC-250 does not prove `Q=1`, does not bound low-order rank sharply when occupancy slack is large, and does not estimate the signed rough-coset amplitude. Singleton is an exact information bound on the actual blind-character code, but generic coding controls show why it cannot alone force generation in the fixed-low-order regime.
