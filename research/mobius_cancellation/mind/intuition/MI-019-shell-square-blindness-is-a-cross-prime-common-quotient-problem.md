# MI-019 — Shell-square blindness reduces to one rough Möbius multiplicative coset after the full annihilator is inverted

**Evidence level:** proved for the shell-square full small-prime subgroup/annihilator geometry through MC-246; no fixed-power cancellation or full small-prime generation theorem follows.

MC-238--MC-243 identify the smooth-dilation blind family through the subgroup `H` generated by the small primes modulo the shell-square modulus. MC-244--MC-245 first invert its quadratic part, showing that quadratic blind-mode count cancels against affine-cell density and that the smooth weight collapses exactly to a rough Möbius source.

MC-246 performs the exact inversion for the **whole annihilator** `A=H^perp`. Finite-group orthogonality gives

`(1/phi(q)) sum_(chi in A) conjugate(chi(a)) S_f(N,chi) = (1/|H|) sum_(m<=N, a^(-1)m in H) f(m)`.

Thus the complete blind family is one multiplicative coset rather than a collection whose cardinality supplies a saving. For the smooth-inverse source, the entire blind contribution is exactly a logarithmically rough Möbius sum on `-H`. The blind index `[G:H]` cancels against coset density, so absolute support again retains the same fixed-power boundary and gains only the rough-density logarithm.

The important correction is that quadratic full rank is not full generation. If `G=(Z/qZ)^x`, then the quadratic Legendre cell is `-HG^2`, while the full source coset is `-H`. The quadratic blind dimension vanishes exactly when `[G:H]` is odd; a nontrivial odd-order quotient can therefore carry higher-order blind characters even when every nonprincipal quadratic blind character has disappeared.

The frontier is now the actual quotient `G/H`, not only its elementary `2`-quotient. A structural exit must prove `H=G`. The analytic alternative is signed cancellation of the one rough Möbius coset `-H`, equivalently cancellation across the complete annihilator family. Characterwise, reciprocal Dirichlet `L`-functions remain; aggregate cancellation is a distinct possibility but is not supplied by the projector identity.

The reusable separation is: **minimum support, quadratic rank, full quotient index, coset density, roughness, individual-character difficulty, cross-character cancellation, and final signed source amplitude are different currencies**. Exact source inversion removes mode-count and smooth-weight proxies and shows precisely which quotient charge survives.

**Boundary.** MC-246 does not prove `H=G`, does not prove signed rough-coset cancellation, and does not show that odd-order blind quotients occur for the actual moving shell family. It shows that ruling out only the quadratic quotient is logically insufficient and that the full source obstruction has a single exact coset form.
