# MI-019 — Shell-square blindness collapses to a tame squarefree moving-shell quotient

**Evidence level:** proved for the shell-square full-annihilator geometry through MC-257 under each finding's stated hypotheses. MC-252 and MC-256 are exact nonlocalized fixed-order universality controls; MC-253 gives unconditional fixed-order shell parity laws; MC-257 exactly removes the wild prime-square kernel from the actual shell quotient. No full tame shell generation or fixed-power signed cancellation is proved.

MC-238--MC-246 identify smooth-dilation blindness with the annihilator generated by lower primes and reduce the blind family to one rough Möbius multiplicative coset. MC-252--MC-256 show that fixed-order tame residue algebra does not generically constrain the corresponding bipartite code when realizing primes are allowed to escape the shell.

The remaining apparent source of genuinely new local information was the wild order-`r` factor inside `(Z/r^2Z)^times`. MC-257 eliminates it in the actual shell. Fermat-quotient additivity and the factor-of-two shell geometry show that for each shell prime `r` a lower prime has nontrivial wild component, while CRT isolates `U_r` as the unique global Sylow-`r` subgroup. Raising that lower-prime residue to the complementary group order produces a generator of `U_r` inside `H`. Thus the entire wild kernel `U` lies in `H`.

Consequently every blind character modulo the shell-square modulus factors through the squarefree shell `R`, and `G/H` is exactly a quotient of `(Z/RZ)^times`. **The blind problem is now tame but still localized.** The reduction removes a conductor layer; it does not create cancellation or rank rigidity.

The next useful distinction is between a smaller conductor and a smaller quotient. Re-running a localized character/projector estimate at squarefree conductor may buy analytic room, while proving actual shell generation requires new cross-prime structure that cannot be inferred from fixed-order residue laws alone.

**Boundary.** MC-257 does not prove `H=G`, control the tame quotient dimension, or bound the rough Möbius coset. No previous analytic estimate may be upgraded merely by replacing the modulus symbolically; its hypotheses and normalization must be rechecked at the descended conductor.
