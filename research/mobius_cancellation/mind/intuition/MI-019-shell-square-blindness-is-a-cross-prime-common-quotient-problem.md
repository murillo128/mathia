# MI-019 — Shell-square blindness descends to a tame conductor, but the support exponent is exactly compensated

**Evidence level:** proved for the shell-square full-annihilator geometry through MC-258 under each finding's stated hypotheses. MC-252 and MC-256 are exact nonlocalized fixed-order universality controls; MC-253 gives unconditional fixed-order shell parity laws; MC-257 removes the wild prime-square kernel; MC-258 determines the descended projector normalization. No full tame shell generation or fixed-power signed cancellation is proved.

MC-238--MC-246 identify smooth-dilation blindness with the annihilator generated by lower primes and reduce the blind family to one rough Möbius multiplicative coset. MC-252--MC-256 show that fixed-order tame residue algebra does not generically constrain the corresponding bipartite code when realizing primes are allowed to escape the shell.

MC-257 eliminates the wild order-`r` factor in the actual moving shell. The whole prime-square kernel lies in the lower-prime-generated subgroup, so every blind character modulo `q=R^2` factors through the squarefree shell modulus `R` and the residual quotient is tame.

MC-258 shows why this conductor descent is not a free support gain. The subgroup identity is exact, `H_q=pi^(-1)(H_R)`, but `|H_q|=R|H_R|`. Consequently the original blind source projector becomes

`B_S^(A)(X) = R^(-1) P_R(g_y;T,-1)`.

The smaller modulus is exactly accompanied by the exterior `1/R` normalization inherited from the square-modulus character expansion. Direct support counting therefore keeps the same main scale `T/R^2`; at `R=X^(alpha+o(1))`, support alone still reaches square-root size only at `alpha>=1/4`. The only elementary improvement is in the inclusion-exclusion error.

The surviving gain is analytic, not combinatorial. Every blind twist now has squarefree conductor dividing `R`, so an improvement must exploit cancellation available at that conductor, or exploit genuinely moving-shell tame cross-prime structure. Merely recounting the same coset at modulus `R` cannot improve the support exponent.

**Boundary.** MC-258 does not prove `H_R=G_R`, bound the tame quotient dimension, or estimate the required squarefree-conductor Möbius twists beyond support. Conductor size, projector normalization, quotient size and signed cancellation remain separate resources.
