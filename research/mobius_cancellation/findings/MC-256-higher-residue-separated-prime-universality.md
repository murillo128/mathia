# MC-256 — Fixed-order higher-residue matrices are universal once prime localization is removed

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED`, `NEGATIVE/OBSTRUCTION`, `MATCHED-ARITHMETIC-CONTROL`, `FRONTIER-REFINEMENT`, `CLASSICAL-MECHANISM`, `PRIOR-ART-AUDITED`, `NO-NOVELTY-CLAIM`.

## Claim

`MC-252` showed that the quadratic lower-prime / separated-upper-prime Legendre matrix can realize every finite binary matrix once the upper primes are allowed to escape the active shell. `MC-254`--`MC-255` then ruled out completed Rédei `4`-rank and the higher full `2`-class-rank tower as substitutes for the missing cross-block information, while leaving open the possibility that raw higher residue/Kummer data might be intrinsically more rigid.

For every **fixed prime order** `ell`, that escape also disappears without localization.

Fix a prime `ell`, distinct rational primes

\[
P=\{p_1,\ldots,p_m\},
\]

and any matrix

\[
B=(B_{ij})\in\mathbb F_\ell^{m\times n}.
\tag{1}
\]

Then there exist pairwise distinct rational primes

\[
r_1,\ldots,r_n,
\qquad r_j\equiv1\pmod\ell,
\]

which may be taken larger than any prescribed finite threshold, together with Dirichlet characters `chi_j` modulo `r_j` of exact order `ell`, such that after fixing a primitive `ell`-th root of unity `zeta_ell`,

\[
\boxed{
\chi_j(p_i)=\zeta_\ell^{B_{ij}}
\qquad(1\le i\le m,\ 1\le j\le n).
}
\tag{2}
\]

Equivalently, any finite `F_ell` matrix occurs exactly as the exponent matrix of order-`ell` residue characters evaluated on a prescribed finite set of rational primes.

Inflating each `chi_j` from modulus `r_j` to modulus `r_j^2` and using the Chinese remainder theorem, define the corresponding tame `ell`-primary blind code

\[
K_\ell(P,R)
:=
\left\{c\in\mathbb F_\ell^n:
\prod_{j=1}^n\chi_j(p_i)^{c_j}=1
\text{ for every }i
\right\}.
\tag{3}
\]

By `(2)`,

\[
\boxed{K_\ell(P,R)=\ker_{\mathbb F_\ell}B.}
\tag{4}
\]

Hence every `ell`-ary linear code `C\subseteq\mathbb F_\ell^n` with

\[
\operatorname{codim}C\le m
\tag{5}
\]

is realized exactly as such a separated-prime higher-residue blind sector.

The consequence for the current frontier is direct:

\[
\boxed{
\text{fixed-order Kummer/power-residue structure + primality alone}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{extra rank rigidity of the blind code}.
}
\tag{6}
\]

Thus the localization lesson of `MC-252` is not specifically quadratic. For every fixed prime `ell`, raw higher power-residue algebra remains flexible enough to encode an arbitrary finite linear code when the column primes can move sufficiently far away. Any useful higher-residue route must therefore use information destroyed by this construction: the moving shell `r\asymp y`, quantitative simultaneous distribution at that scale, a source-coupled invariant, order growing with the scale, or genuinely wild prime-square information not contained in characters inflated from modulus `r`.

This result does **not** realize arbitrary matrices with `r_j\in(y,2y]`, does not control the actual Mathia shell quotient, does not cover the wild order-`r_j` character direction of `(Z/r_j^2 Z)^times`, and gives no estimate for `M(X)`.

## 1. Kummer independence of the row primes

Let

\[
K=\mathbb Q(\zeta_\ell),
\qquad
L=K(p_1^{1/\ell},\ldots,p_m^{1/\ell}).
\tag{7}
\]

Because `K` contains the `ell`-th roots of unity, Kummer theory identifies `Gal(L/K)` with the dual of the subgroup generated by the classes of the `p_i` in

\[
K^\times/(K^\times)^\ell.
\]

Those classes are linearly independent over `F_ell`. Indeed, suppose

\[
\prod_{i=1}^m p_i^{a_i}=x^\ell,
\qquad 0\le a_i<\ell,
\qquad x\in K^\times.
\tag{8}
\]

For `p_i\ne\ell`, choose a prime ideal `mathfrak p_i` of `K` above `p_i`. The cyclotomic extension `K/Q` is unramified there, so

\[
v_{\mathfrak p_i}(p_i)=1,
\qquad
v_{\mathfrak p_i}(p_j)=0\quad(j\ne i).
\]

Taking valuations in `(8)` gives `a_i\equiv0 mod ell`.

If one of the row primes equals `ell`, let `mathfrak l=(1-\zeta_\ell)` be the unique prime above `ell`. Since

\[
v_{\mathfrak l}(\ell)=\ell-1
\]

and `gcd(ell,ell-1)=1`, the same valuation argument again forces its exponent to vanish modulo `ell`.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)\cong(\mathbb Z/\ell\mathbb Z)^m.}
\tag{9}
\]

For every vector

\[
b=(b_1,\ldots,b_m)\in\mathbb F_\ell^m
\]

there is consequently a unique element `sigma_b` of this Kummer Galois group satisfying

\[
\sigma_b(p_i^{1/\ell})
=\zeta_\ell^{b_i}p_i^{1/\ell}
\qquad(1\le i\le m).
\tag{10}
\]

## 2. Chebotarev realizes every column by a rational prime

The extension `L/K` is finite abelian, so each `sigma_b` is its own conjugacy class. Chebotarev's density theorem gives infinitely many prime ideals `mathfrak r` of `K`, unramified in `L`, with

\[
\operatorname{Frob}_{\mathfrak r}(L/K)=\sigma_b.
\tag{11}
\]

Moreover one may choose such `mathfrak r` of residue degree one over `Q`. Prime ideals of `K` of residue degree at least two and norm at most `X` lie above rational primes at most `X^{1/2}` and therefore contribute only `O_K(X^{1/2})`; they cannot exhaust a positive-density Chebotarev set. Thus infinitely many primes in `(11)` have

\[
N\mathfrak r=r
\]

for a rational prime `r`.

Since `K/Q` is cyclotomic and Galois, residue degree one means

\[
r\equiv1\pmod\ell.
\tag{12}
\]

For such `mathfrak r`, the `ell`-th power residue symbol defines a Dirichlet character

\[
\chi_{\mathfrak r}:(\mathbb Z/r\mathbb Z)^\times\to\mu_\ell,
\qquad
\chi_{\mathfrak r}(a)
=\left(\frac a{\mathfrak r}\right)_\ell.
\tag{13}
\]

It has exact order `ell`: the map `a\mapsto a^{(r-1)/ell}` on the cyclic group `F_r^times` has image exactly the `ell`-th roots of unity. The standard Kummer/Frobenius dictionary gives

\[
\frac{\operatorname{Frob}_{\mathfrak r}(p_i^{1/\ell})}{p_i^{1/\ell}}
=
\left(\frac{p_i}{\mathfrak r}\right)_\ell,
\tag{14}
\]

so `(10)`--`(14)` imply

\[
\chi_{\mathfrak r}(p_i)=\zeta_\ell^{b_i}.
\tag{15}
\]

This realizes one arbitrary column `b`. Apply the construction independently to the columns of `B`. Each relevant Chebotarev set is infinite, so the underlying rational primes may be chosen pairwise distinct and larger than any fixed threshold. This proves `(2)`.

For `ell=2`, the statement reduces to the separated-prime Legendre universality of `MC-252`; there the elementary CRT + Dirichlet proof is stronger and avoids the Kummer/Chebotarev machinery.

## 3. Exact code universality at every fixed tame primary order

Let `C\subseteq F_ell^n` have codimension `rho\le m`. Choose a full-rank parity-check matrix

\[
H_C\in\mathbb F_\ell^{\rho\times n},
\qquad
C=\ker H_C,
\]

and pad it with zero rows to obtain an `m\times n` matrix `B`. Realize `B` by `(2)`. Then for `c\in F_ell^n`,

\[
\prod_{j=1}^n\chi_j(p_i)^{c_j}
=
\zeta_\ell^{(Bc)_i},
\]

so the simultaneous blindness conditions are exactly `Bc=0`. This proves `(4)`--`(5)`.

Each `chi_j` factors through reduction modulo `r_j`; inflating it to `(Z/r_j^2 Z)^times` preserves its order and all values on the row primes. Thus the construction lives inside the same prime-square local groups used by the shell-square modulus, but only in their **tame** `ell`-primary quotient coming from `r_j-1`.

This gives a stronger matched-control interpretation than a merely abstract code comparison. Arbitrary `ell`-ary parity-check geometry is compatible with genuine rational primes, genuine order-`ell` Dirichlet characters, and genuine prime-square local factors. What it does not preserve is the simultaneous requirement that all column primes occupy one narrow moving shell.

## 4. Why this narrows the post-MC-255 Kummer frontier

`MC-255` ruled out the scalar completed higher `2`-class-rank tower but deliberately left open "raw higher residue/Kummer structure" that might constrain the bipartite lower/shell interaction directly. The present result separates two meanings of that phrase.

A **nonlocalized fixed-order residue matrix** cannot be the missing rigidity: `(1)`--`(5)` show that it can be completely arbitrary. Higher reciprocity identities or Kummer realization by themselves therefore do not improve the generic coding frontier any more than quadratic reciprocity did in `MC-252`.

A surviving higher-residue mechanism must use at least one feature absent here:

- quantitative localization `r\in(y,2y]` or a comparably tight moving range;
- simultaneous distribution of many Kummer Frobenius conditions at that moving scale;
- an invariant coupling the residue matrix to the exact Möbius source rather than only to its local character values;
- residue order `ell=ell(y)` growing with the scale, where the fixed-extension argument no longer supplies uniform information;
- or the wild order-`r` part of characters modulo `r^2`, which does not factor through the power-residue character modulo `r`.

The Chebotarev proof is intentionally qualitative. The degree

\[
[L:K]=\ell^m
\]

already grows exponentially with the number of row primes, and the discriminant grows as the row set grows. No effective Chebotarev estimate is being claimed at a scale remotely comparable with the active shell. That failure of quantitative localization is exactly the boundary, not a technical detail to suppress.

## 5. Prior art and novelty boundary

All mathematical ingredients are classical. Kummer theory over a field containing `mu_ell` identifies adjoining independent `ell`-th roots with an elementary abelian `ell`-extension; the power-residue symbol is the Frobenius action on the corresponding Kummer radical; and Chebotarev realizes every Galois element on a positive-density set of prime ideals. Standard references include Kenneth Ireland and Michael Rosen, *A Classical Introduction to Modern Number Theory*, 2nd ed., Springer GTM 84 (1990), especially the chapters on power reciprocity, and Jürgen Neukirch, *Algebraic Number Theory*, Springer Grundlehren 322 (1999), for Kummer/class-field/Frobenius and Chebotarev theory.

For the quadratic specialization, `MC-252` already cites Brandon Hanson, Robert C. Vaughan and Ruixiang Zhang, *The Least Number with Prescribed Legendre Symbols*, arXiv:1605.05584, as direct prescribed-sign prior art. No analogous novelty is claimed here for prescribing higher residue values: the theorem above is recorded as an explicit application of standard Kummer and Chebotarev machinery.

The durable Mathia delta is the **frontier exclusion**: after `MC-255`, passing from quadratic to any fixed odd prime residue order does not by itself create the missing cross-block rank rigidity. The only plausible higher-residue escape is one that is quantitatively shell-localized, source-coupled, growing-order, or wild at prime-square level.

## 6. Falsification checks and boundaries

- **Fixed order.** `ell` is fixed before the row set grows. No estimate uniform in `ell=ell(y)` is claimed.
- **No shell realization.** Chebotarev gives arbitrarily large realizing primes, not primes in `(y,2y]`. Claiming shell universality would reverse the conclusion.
- **Character orientation.** Choosing a prime ideal `mathfrak r` above the rational prime `r` fixes the embedding of `mu_ell` into the residue field and hence one of the order-`ell` characters modulo `r`. Changing that choice applies the expected Galois/basis automorphism. The blind-code rank question is invariant under such nonzero rescaling of a column.
- **Tame versus wild.** The characters used here inflate from modulus `r` to `r^2`. The order-`r` character direction coming from the kernel of `(Z/r^2 Z)^times -> (Z/r Z)^times` is not represented and remains a distinct possible source of structure.
- **Finite row set.** The construction applies to every finite set of row primes, including the row prime `ell`; it gives no uniform control as the number of rows tends to infinity.
- **Code codimension.** The code corollary requires `codim C<=m`, exactly because there are `m` row constraints.
- **No cancellation consequence.** Nothing here bounds the rough Möbius coset of `MC-246`, the full quotient `G/H`, or `M(X)`.
