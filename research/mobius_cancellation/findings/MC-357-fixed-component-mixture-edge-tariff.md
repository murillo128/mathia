# MC-357 — Fixed-component mixtures cannot hide source-edge divergence

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED` · `NEGATIVE/OBSTRUCTION` · `FRONTIER-ADVANCE` · `CLASSICAL-MECHANISM` · `PRIOR-ART-AUDITED` · `NO-NOVELTY-CLAIM` · `NON-RH`.

## Claim

`MC-356` prices every regular finite-dimensional exponential-family readout by its exact Fisher edge action, but leaves source-dependent mixtures outside the theorem because a mixture density need not itself remain in the same exponential family. For the important case in which the **components are source-independent and only the mixing law changes with the source**, that is not a genuine escape.

Let `X` be the reciprocal source state and let `Theta` be a latent mixing variable in an arbitrary measurable space. For each source state `x`, let `nu_x` be a probability law on `Theta`, and let `K(dy|theta)` be a Markov kernel that does **not** depend on `x`. The admitted transcript is generated by

\[
X=x\longrightarrow \Theta\sim\nu_x
\longrightarrow Y\sim K(\cdot\mid\Theta),
\tag{1}
\]

so

\[
P_x:=\operatorname{Law}(Y\mid X=x)=\nu_xK.
\tag{2}
\]

For a natural source edge `e={x,x'}`, write

\[
b_e(Y)
:=\frac12\left(D(P_x\|P_{x'})+D(P_{x'}\|P_x)\right)
\tag{3}
\]

and define the same half-Jeffreys cost for the latent mixing laws,

\[
b_e(\Theta)
:=\frac12\left(D(\nu_x\|\nu_{x'})+D(\nu_{x'}\|\nu_x)\right).
\tag{4}
\]

Then KL data processing through the fixed component kernel gives the exact architecture-level tariff

\[
\boxed{
 b_e(Y)\le b_e(\Theta).
}
\tag{5}
\]

Consequently, under the bounded-energy reciprocal dephasing hypotheses of `MC-353`,

\[
\mathcal E(W)\le C,
\qquad
\delta(W)\le1-\eta,
\qquad C<\infty,
\quad\eta>0,
\tag{6}
\]

the natural source-edge law satisfies

\[
\boxed{
\mathbf E_e\,b_e(\Theta)
\ge
\frac{\eta^2}{C}-o(1).
}
\tag{7}
\]

Thus a fixed bank of arbitrarily complicated component distributions cannot supply source discrimination for free. All separation available to the final transcript is bottlenecked by separation of the **mixing laws themselves**.

### Finite categorical mixtures

For a finite fixed component bank `K_1,...,K_m`, let

\[
w_x=(w_{x,1},\ldots,w_{x,m})\in\Delta_{m-1},
\tag{8}
\]

sample `J|X=x ~ Cat(w_x)`, and then sample `Y~K_J`. For neighboring weights `w=w_x` and `v=w_{x'}` with common positive support,

\[
\boxed{
 b_e(J)
 =
 \frac12\sum_{j=1}^m
 (w_j-v_j)\log\frac{w_j}{v_j}.
}
\tag{9}
\]

The mean-value theorem gives, componentwise,

\[
(w_j-v_j)\log\frac{w_j}{v_j}
\le
\frac{(w_j-v_j)^2}{\min(w_j,v_j)},
\tag{10}
\]

and hence

\[
\boxed{
 b_e(Y)
\le b_e(J)
\le
\frac12\sum_{j=1}^m
\frac{(w_j-v_j)^2}{\min(w_j,v_j)}.
}
\tag{11}
\]

Combining `(7)` and `(11)` yields the necessary average weight-motion budget

\[
\boxed{
\mathbf E_e
\sum_{j=1}^m
\frac{(w_{x,j}-w_{x',j})^2}
{\min(w_{x,j},w_{x',j})}
\ge
\frac{2\eta^2}{C}-o(1).
}
\tag{12}
\]

If all neighboring mixture weights obey a common interior floor

\[
\min_j w_{x,j}\ge a_R>0,
\tag{13}
\]

then

\[
\boxed{
\mathbf E_e\|w_x-w_{x'}\|_2^2
\ge
2a_R\left(\frac{\eta^2}{C}-o(1)\right).
}
\tag{14}
\]

Therefore a finite fixed-component mixture architecture whose adjacent mixing weights become too smooth in the weighted simplex metric in `(12)` cannot sustain fixed nontrivial bounded-energy dephasing.

### Exact endpoint

There is a sharper support statement at exact dephasing. Total variation also contracts through a Markov kernel:

\[
\|P_x-P_{x'}\|_{TV}
\le
\|\nu_x-\nu_{x'}\|_{TV}.
\tag{15}
\]

The exact reciprocal endpoint analyzed in the preceding source-discrimination chain requires neighboring complete-transcript laws to be mutually singular. Then the left side of `(15)` equals `1`, so the right side must also equal `1`:

\[
\boxed{
P_x\perp P_{x'}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\nu_x\perp\nu_{x'}.
}
\tag{16}
\]

For a finite categorical mixture this means

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{supp}(w_x)
\cap
\operatorname{supp}(w_{x'})
=\varnothing
}
\tag{17}
\]

on every natural source edge that must be exactly discriminated. In particular, an all-interior fixed-component mixture cannot realize the exact endpoint, regardless of how singular, multimodal, high-dimensional, or otherwise complicated the fixed components `K_j` are.

This closes the **fixed-component** part of the mixture loophole left explicit in `MC-356`. A surviving mixture mechanism must put source dependence into the component kernel itself, drive the latent mixing laws toward singular/support-changing behavior, or expose some additional transcript path not represented by `(1)`.

No estimate for `M(x)` follows. The result is a finite reciprocal-endpoint admission theorem: it identifies the latent mixing law as the compulsory discrimination resource for a broad class of non-exponential output mixtures.

## 1. Data processing prices every fixed-component mixture by its latent law

For `(1)`, the source-dependent objects are only `nu_x` and `nu_x'`; the kernel `K` is shared. Relative entropy contracts under a Markov kernel, so

\[
D(\nu_xK\|\nu_{x'}K)
\le
D(\nu_x\|\nu_{x'}),
\tag{18}
\]

and the same inequality holds after exchanging the two source states. Adding the two directed inequalities and dividing by two proves `(5)`.

This argument does not require a finite component family, densities on the output space, Gaussianity, an exponential-family form, or a finite-dimensional transcript. It is precisely the latent-variable representation of a fixed-component mixture that matters.

Averaging `(5)` over the same parity-appropriate natural source-edge law used in `MC-353` gives

\[
\mathbf E_e b_e(Y)
\le
\mathbf E_e b_e(\Theta).
\tag{19}
\]

The lower bound `E_e b_e(Y) >= eta^2/C-o(1)` from `MC-353` then proves `(7)`.

## 2. Categorical weights expose the tariff explicitly

For `J~Cat(w)` and `J'~Cat(v)`, direct substitution gives

\[
D(w\|v)=\sum_j w_j\log\frac{w_j}{v_j}.
\tag{20}
\]

Adding the reverse divergence yields

\[
D(w\|v)+D(v\|w)
=
\sum_j(w_j-v_j)\log\frac{w_j}{v_j},
\tag{21}
\]

which proves `(9)`.

For positive `w_j,v_j`, apply the mean-value theorem to `log`:

\[
\log w_j-\log v_j
=
\frac{w_j-v_j}{\xi_j}
\tag{22}
\]

for some `xi_j` between `w_j` and `v_j`. Hence

\[
(w_j-v_j)\log\frac{w_j}{v_j}
=
\frac{(w_j-v_j)^2}{\xi_j}
\le
\frac{(w_j-v_j)^2}{\min(w_j,v_j)},
\tag{23}
\]

proving `(11)`--`(14)`.

The categorical latent law is itself a regular exponential family on the simplex interior, so `(9)` is also the categorical specialization of the Fisher/Bregman identity in `MC-356`. The new point is architectural rather than representational: even when the **output mixture density** is not an exponential-family row, the actual generative graph factors through a categorical exponential-family latent variable whenever the components are fixed.

## 3. Exact output singularity forces singular mixing laws

Total variation is an `f`-divergence and contracts under the same source-independent Markov kernel. Therefore `(15)` holds. Mutual singularity of two probability measures is equivalent to total variation distance `1` under the convention

\[
\|P-Q\|_{TV}:=\sup_A|P(A)-Q(A)|.
\tag{24}
\]

So if `P_x` and `P_x'` are singular, `(15)` forces `||nu_x-nu_x'||_TV=1`, hence `nu_x perp nu_x'`, proving `(16)`. For categorical laws, singularity is exactly disjoint support, proving `(17)`.

Notice that no disjoint-support assumption is made on the component output laws `K_j`. The conclusion runs in the opposite direction: **even the most favorable fixed component bank cannot manufacture singular output laws unless the source dependence was already singular at the latent mixing layer.**

## 4. Prior art and novelty boundary

No novelty is claimed for relative-entropy data processing, total-variation contraction, the log-sum inequality, categorical relative entropy, or the fact that the categorical family is an exponential family.

Shields, *Information Theory and Statistics: A Tutorial*, Foundations and Trends in Communications and Information Theory **1** (2004), 417--528, DOI `10.1561/0100000004`, treats information divergence and its statistical/information-geometric role in the finite-alphabet setting; an author-hosted copy is available at https://www.renyi.hu/~csiszar/Publications/Information_Theory_and_Statistics%3A_A_Tutorial.pdf.

Yury Polyanskiy and Yihong Wu, *Strong Data-Processing Inequalities for Channels and Bayesian Networks*, in *Convexity and Concentration*, IMA Volumes in Mathematics and its Applications **161** (2017), 211--249, arXiv:1508.06025, surveys data-processing inequalities and channel-dependent strengthenings: https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.06025.

The exponential-family/Bregman side is already audited in `MC-356`; in particular, Banerjee, Merugu, Dhillon and Ghosh, *Clustering with Bregman Divergences*, JMLR **6** (2005), 1705--1749, records the KL/Bregman correspondence for regular exponential families.

A targeted prior-art audit on 17 September 2026 found the component inequalities and latent-channel contraction to be standard. No novelty is claimed for `(5)`, `(9)`, `(11)`, or `(15)` in isolation. The durable Mathia-specific delta is their composition with the independently derived reciprocal source-edge lower tariff of `MC-353`: the mixture loophole from `MC-356` splits cleanly into a closed fixed-component case and a genuinely source-dependent-component case.

## Boundaries and falsification tests

- **The component kernel must be source-independent.** If `K=K_x` changes with the source state, the Markov factorization `(1)` fails and component motion is an additional source channel that must be priced separately.
- **A latent-variable representation must be real, not post hoc.** Introducing an arbitrary mixture decomposition of an already-given output law does not help unless the architecture actually factors through that source-independent kernel.
- **The weighted simplex bound is the right finite-mixture local quantity.** Plain Euclidean weight motion is informative only with an interior floor such as `(13)`; vanishing weights can make the reverse divergence large at tiny Euclidean cost.
- **Support change is an explicit resource, not a contradiction.** Singular or nearly singular mixing laws escape the smooth-weight corollary precisely by paying the divergence/support tariff that the theorem identifies.
- **Growing component count is allowed by `(5)` but weakens simple interior-floor corollaries.** If `m=m_R` grows, a uniform floor can be at most `1/m_R`; use the weighted expression `(12)` rather than `(14)`.
- **No arithmetic upper bound has been proved.** A concrete Möbius endpoint construction still needs a theorem showing that its natural mixing-law edge cost is too small. The present result only says exactly where that cost must live for a fixed-component architecture.
- **No claim is made for source-dependent mixtures with hidden side channels.** If the mixture index, component law, random seed, adaptive branch, or auxiliary metadata carries additional source dependence outside `(1)`, that information is not priced by the stated latent law.

## Research consequence

The phrase “non-exponential mixture” is too coarse as an escape classification. A mixture can have a non-exponential output density while still factoring through a perfectly priced latent source channel. For a fixed component bank, the only source-sensitive object is the mixing law, and data processing makes its Jeffreys/TV geometry an unavoidable upper resource.

The remaining mixture frontier is therefore narrower: either prove an arithmetic upper bound on the adjacent mixing-law tariff in `(7)`/`(12)`, or study architectures whose **components themselves move with the source**. In the latter case the next useful theorem would have to split latent-weight divergence from source-dependent component divergence without double counting and determine whether either term can remain large under the reciprocal endpoint constraints.
