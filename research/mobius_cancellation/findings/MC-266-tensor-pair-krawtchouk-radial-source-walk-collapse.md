# MC-266 — Tensor-pair Krawtchouk moment collapses to a radial source walk

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED`, `FRONTIER-REFINEMENT`, `CLASSICAL-MECHANISM`, `PRIOR-ART-AUDITED`, `NO-NOVELTY-CLAIM`.

## Claim

Continue the notation of `MC-264` and `MC-265`. Let

\[
\mathcal P_y=\{p\le y:p\text{ odd prime}\},
\qquad k_y=|\mathcal P_y|,
\]

and identify square-free products of primes in `\mathcal P_y` with the Boolean group

\[
G_y=(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^{\mathcal P_y}
\]

under multiplication modulo squares. Write `e_p\in G_y` for the basis vector of a prime `p`, and let

\[
a_y=\sum_{p\in\mathcal P_y}\delta_{e_p}
\]

in the group algebra of `G_y`. The tensor coefficients from `MC-264` are exactly the convolution powers

\[
c_{m,y}(u)=[\delta_u]a_y^{*m}.
\tag{1}
\]

Consequently their pair autocorrelation collapses exactly:

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{u\in G_y}c_{m,y}(u)c_{m,y}(uq)=c_{2m,y}(q)
}
\qquad(q\in G_y).
\tag{2}
\]

Indeed `G_y` has exponent two, so `u^{-1}=u`, and the left side is simply `(c_{m,y}*c_{m,y})(q)=a_y^{*2m}(q)`.

Applying `(2)` to the exact Krawtchouk identity of `MC-264` gives

\[
\boxed{
S_{2m,y}(t)
=\sum_{q\in G_y}c_{2m,y}(q)
K_t\!\bigl(d_y(q);N_y\bigr),
}
\tag{3}
\]

where

\[
d_y(q)=\#\left\{r\in\mathcal R_y:\left(\frac qr\right)=-1\right\}.
\tag{4}
\]

Thus the weighted double distance distribution in `MC-264` is not intrinsically a two-source object. It is a **single `2m`-step parity walk on the lower-prime Boolean cube**, evaluated against the shell Krawtchouk kernel.

The identity preserves the natural diagonal exactly. Since

\[
c_{2m,y}(1)
=\sum_u c_{m,y}(u)^2
=D_{m,y},
\tag{5}
\]

the trivial source element contributes

\[
\binom{N_y}{t}D_{m,y}.
\tag{6}
\]

Therefore the candidate estimate from `MC-264`,

\[
S_{2m,y}(t)\le L_y\binom{N_y}{t}D_{m,y},
\tag{7}
\]

is equivalent to the one-source signed remainder bound

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{q\ne1}c_{2m,y}(q)
K_t\!\bigl(d_y(q);N_y\bigr)
\le (L_y-1)\binom{N_y}{t}D_{m,y}.
}
\tag{8}
\]

This sharpens the surviving target after `MC-265`: one does not need a theorem about arbitrary weighted pairs `(u,v)` as such. The missing arithmetic theorem may instead be sought as cancellation of shell Krawtchouk values over the **radial source-product mixture generated by a `2m`-step cube walk**.

No estimate `(8)` is proved here, no blind relation is excluded, and no bound for `M(X)` follows.

## 1. The source walk is exactly radial

Because `a_y` is invariant under every permutation of the `k_y` lower-prime coordinates, `c_{2m,y}(q)` depends only on

\[
s=\omega(q),
\]

the number of lower primes occurring to odd multiplicity in `q`. Write this common value as `C_{2m,k_y}(s)`.

There is an exact exponential generating function

\[
\boxed{
C_{2m,k}(s)
=(2m)![z^{2m}]
(\sinh z)^s(\cosh z)^{k-s}.
}
\tag{9}
\]

To see this, each coordinate belonging to `q` must occur an odd positive number of times in the ordered `2m`-tuple and each coordinate outside `q` must occur an even number of times. The factors `\sinh z` and `\cosh z` record exactly those parity constraints, while multiplication by `(2m)!` restores the multinomial count.

In particular,

\[
C_{2m,k}(s)=0
\qquad\text{unless}\qquad
0\le s\le2m,
\quad s\equiv0\pmod2.
\tag{10}
\]

Define the signed shell aggregate at source support `s` by

\[
H_{s,y}(t)
=\sum_{\substack{q\in G_y\\\omega(q)=s}}
K_t\!\bigl(d_y(q);N_y\bigr).
\tag{11}
\]

Then `(3)` becomes the finite radial hierarchy

\[
\boxed{
S_{2m,y}(t)
=\sum_{\substack{0\le s\le2m\\s\text{ even}}}
C_{2m,k_y}(s)H_{s,y}(t).
}
\tag{12}
\]

The `s=0` term is exactly `(6)`. All arithmetic difficulty is therefore concentrated in the finitely many signed aggregates `H_{s,y}(t)` for even `2\le s\le2m`.

At the live threshold `m=t+1\asymp\log\log y`, this reduces the source side of the missing theorem to only `m` nontrivial support layers, although each layer still contains a large family of source products.

## 2. Raw source mass forms an m-deep collision hierarchy

The radial reduction does **not** make the nontrivial layers negligible by counting alone. For

\[
s=2m-2j,
\qquad 0\le j\le m,
\]

let

\[
W_{m,j}(k)
=\sum_{\substack{q\in(\mathbb Z/2\mathbb Z)^k\\|q|=2m-2j}}
 c_{2m}(q)
=\binom{k}{2m-2j}C_{2m,k}(2m-2j).
\tag{13}
\]

There is the exact lower bound

\[
\boxed{
W_{m,j}(k)
\ge
\binom{k}{2m-2j}
\binom{k-(2m-2j)}{j}
\frac{(2m)!}{2^j}.
}
\tag{14}
\]

It counts only the configurations in which the `2m-2j` odd coordinates occur once and `j` additional coordinates occur twice. These use the maximum possible number `2m-j` of distinct coordinates among words with parity support `2m-2j`.

More generally, `W_{m,j}(k)` is a polynomial in `k` of degree exactly `2m-j`, with leading coefficient

\[
\boxed{
\frac{(2m)!}
{2^j(2m-2j)!j!}.
}
\tag{15}
\]

The degree statement follows because a word with parity support `s=2m-2j` can contain at most `j` additional even-positive coordinates; equality forces precisely the multiplicity pattern counted in `(14)`. Any other pattern uses at most `2m-j-1` distinct coordinate labels.

At the opposite end, `j=m` gives `s=0`, and `(15)` recovers the leading diagonal scale

\[
D_{m,y}=C_{2m,k_y}(0)
=(2m-1)!!\,k_y^m+O_m(k_y^{m-1}),
\tag{16}
\]

consistent with `MC-264`.

Hence every collision depth `j<m` has raw positive coefficient mass of strictly higher polynomial degree than the diagonal. The radial collapse therefore does not license discarding all but the maximal-support layer, nor treating repeated lower primes as a small absolute error. A successful proof of `(8)` must obtain signed Krawtchouk cancellation across the nontrivial source layers themselves, with enough strength to overcome their large raw masses.

This is the source-side counterpart of the obstruction in `MC-265`: taking absolute values too early destroys the scale needed by the high-moment route.

## 3. Why this changes the analytic target

`MC-265` left the live object as the weighted pair sum

\[
\sum_{u,v}c_{m,y}(u)c_{m,y}(v)
K_t(d_y(u,v);N_y).
\]

Identity `(3)` shows that its pair structure is algebraically redundant. The product `q=uv` already carries everything the shell kernel can see, while the tensor weights combine into the exact `2m`-step coefficient `c_{2m,y}(q)`.

This gives three sharper possibilities for the next analytic step:

1. prove cancellation separately for the signed layer sums `H_{s,y}(t)` and combine them with the explicit positive coefficients `C_{2m,k_y}(s)`;
2. exploit a relation among neighboring even source supports generated by the cube-walk/Krawtchouk recurrences before taking absolute values; or
3. derive a direct estimate for the mixture `(8)` using reciprocity or character-sum structure of products of exactly `s` lower primes.

What no longer needs to be treated as primitive is an arbitrary two-point geometry on the tensor indices. Any useful two-point theorem should descend to, or improve upon, the one-source radial formulation `(8)`.

The reduction also identifies a clean failure test: if available character-sum technology controls each fixed source support only after absolute values at a scale proportional to its raw mass `W_{m,j}(k_y)`, then all layers with `j<m` remain parametrically above the natural diagonal and cannot close `MC-264` by that route.

Krawtchouk convolution structures are also classical in the discrete-harmonic-analysis literature.

The durable Mathia delta is only the exact specialization of that classical Boolean convolution to the tensor coefficients already forced by `MC-264`: equations `(2)`--`(12)` collapse its double shell-distance sum to a radial `2m`-step source walk, while `(14)`--`(15)` show why the surviving signed theorem must control an entire collision-depth hierarchy rather than only generic distinct-prime products.

A targeted literature check through 13 September 2026 found the classical hypercube/Krawtchouk convolution machinery but no source asserting the specific Möbius shell identity `(3)` or the support-matched analytic estimate `(8)`. No novelty claim is made for `(3)` beyond this line-specific reduction.

## 5. Boundaries and falsification tests

- **No new moment estimate.** Equations `(3)` and `(12)` are exact reorganizations of the already-defined moment. They do not prove the missing inequality `(7)`.
- **No independence assumption.** The convolution identity is algebraic on the finite Boolean group; it does not model Legendre symbols as independent random signs.
- **Raw layer mass is not an arithmetic lower bound.** Equations `(14)`--`(15)` concern the positive tensor coefficients before multiplication by signed Krawtchouk values. Large layer mass can cancel in `H_{s,y}(t)`.
- **Radiality is source-side only.** `c_{2m,y}(q)` depends only on `\omega(q)`, but the arithmetic shell distance `d_y(q)` need not. The hard information remains in how Legendre signatures vary inside each source-support layer.
- **The reduction does not evade `MC-265`.** First-order shell balance is still too weak. The required cancellation is now localized more precisely to the signed aggregates `(11)`.
- **No RH-scale conclusion.** Even a proof of `(7)` at one support order would only exclude blind relations at that order; the rough-to-global Möbius transfer remains separate.

The support-matched high-moment route now has a more economical exact target. `MC-264` showed that moment order `2(t+1)` has enough amplification to detect one blind support; `MC-265` showed that pointwise first-order shell balance cannot prove the required estimate. The next useful theorem should therefore attack these **source-support-resolved shell Krawtchouk sums**, or exploit a recurrence/cancellation coupling them, at `m,t\asymp\log\log y`.
