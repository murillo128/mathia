# Riemann hypothesis

## What it is

The conjecture that every nontrivial zero of the Riemann zeta function has real part one half.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

This is the reference claim whose criteria, analogues, partial results, and proof programs are distinguished throughout the projection.

## Known scope and limits

RH remains open in the retained atlas. Equivalent criteria, finite-height checks, statistical evidence, formalized prerequisites, and proved function-field analogues do not constitute a proof of this conjecture.
