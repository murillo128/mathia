# Ideal-polygon cross-ratio moduli

## What it is

Ordered ideal polygons are naturally considered modulo Möbius transformations, and cross-ratios provide intrinsic coordinates on that moduli problem. The resulting moduli space has the expected finite-dimensional projective geometry; related cross-ratio dynamics descends to the moduli space of Möbius-equivalent polygons.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

[[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-097-finite-prime-tangents-are-moduli-complete-and-primality-blind]] proves that the projective positive gap vector of a finite prime tangent fills the full relevant ideal-polygon moduli cell. Thus a fixed normalized tangent is not sitting on a special prime-only intrinsic spectral locus: it is an ordinary point of classical polygon moduli.

## Known scope and limits

The classical moduli theory does not prove Mathia's project-specific CRT clone obstruction or say anything about how finite tangents recur along the actual prime sequence. It supports only the prior-art side of the moduli/cross-ratio identification.
