# Generalized cusp-scattering inverse rigidity

## What it is

Isozaki, Kurylev and Lassas define a generalized cusp S-matrix for asymptotically hyperbolic surfaces with cusps and conic singularities and prove inverse results in which the generalized scattering data determine the Riemannian metric and singularity structure. Their hyperbolic-orbifold setting includes the geometrically finite cusp geometry used by Mathia's finite tangents.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

[[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-067-generalized-cusp-scattering-already-solves-the-full-inverse-tangent-problem]] applies this theorem as a decisive novelty downgrade: recovery of a finite prime-derived tangent from the full generalized cusp response, followed by recovery of its gap ratios, is a specialization of established inverse scattering rather than a new prime-specific uniqueness mechanism.

## Known scope and limits

The result concerns rich operator-valued generalized scattering data. It does not imply inverse uniqueness from the much weaker ordinary physical scalar scattering coefficient, from only poles/residues, or from the resonance set alone. Those distinctions remain material in Prime Flute.
