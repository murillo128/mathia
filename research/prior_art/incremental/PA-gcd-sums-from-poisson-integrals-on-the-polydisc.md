# GCD sums from Poisson integrals on the polydisc

## What it is

Aistleitner, Berkes and Seip study GCD sums

`sum (gcd(m,n)^(2 alpha))/(mn)^alpha`

for `0 < alpha <= 1`. At the critical value `alpha=1/2` the kernel is exactly `gcd(m,n)/sqrt(mn)`. Their method identifies these sums with Poisson integrals on a polydisc and also studies the associated GCD matrices and their largest eigenvalues.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-006-critical-gcd-kernel-and-potential-theory-downgrade-PC005]] derives this same critical GCD kernel from the normalized prime-circle resultant geometry. The literature therefore makes the kernel itself established arithmetic harmonic analysis rather than a new prime-circle spectral object.

## Known scope and limits

The prior art controls GCD sums and matrices; it does not imply that Mathia's geometric derivation is identical in motivation or that the kernel yields an RH criterion. The exact project-specific reduction remains evidence about where Prime Circle loses novelty.
