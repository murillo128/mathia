# Gamma(2) thrice-punctured-sphere scattering

## What it is

The principal congruence subgroup `Gamma(2)` has index six in `PSL_2(Z)`, three cusp classes of width two, and `Gamma(2)\H` is the complete thrice-punctured sphere. Wolpert records its three-cusp Eisenstein/scattering matrix as the ordinary modular scattering factor multiplied by elementary level-two cusp factors; the modular factor contains the classical Riemann-zeta quotient.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

## Known scope and limits

The occurrence of zeta in modular scattering is classical background; it is not a Hilbert–Pólya realization and does not make either Mathia construction an explanation of RH. The exact assignment of every zero/pole in individual scattering eigenchannels requires the full level-two factors and normalizations.
