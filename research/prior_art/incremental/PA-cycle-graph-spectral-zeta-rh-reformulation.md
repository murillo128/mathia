# Cycle-graph spectral-zeta RH reformulation

## What it is

Friedli and Karlsson study spectral zeta functions of finite torus graphs and their limits. Their work proves that the Riemann hypothesis is equivalent to an approximate functional equation for the relevant graph spectral-zeta asymptotics in the critical strip.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-008-single-polygon-riesz-and-cycle-spectral-zeta-are-known-rh-reformulations]] observes that the cycle spectrum of a regular polygon lands directly in this known graph-spectral-zeta framework. Consequently, packaging a single regular polygon through its cycle Laplacian does not create a new RH mechanism merely by reproducing that criterion.

## Known scope and limits

The graph criterion is an RH reformulation, not a proof. It does not establish that the original prime-circle geometry explains why RH should hold, and it should not be confused with Ihara zeta.
