# Riesz energy of roots of unity

## What it is

Brauchart, Hardin and Saff give the complete large-`N` asymptotic expansion of the Riesz `s`-energy of equally spaced points on the unit circle, including analytic continuation in the energy parameter. Riemann-zeta values occur naturally in those asymptotics.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-008-single-polygon-riesz-and-cycle-spectral-zeta-are-known-rh-reformulations]] identifies single-polygon chord/Riesz energies with this established roots-of-unity energy theory. Their appearance of zeta is therefore prior art and cannot by itself be counted as a new geometric explanation of zeta zeros.

## Known scope and limits

This theory concerns energy sums and their asymptotics. The occurrence of the Riemann zeta function in the expansion is not an RH equivalence or a spectral realization of its nontrivial zeros.
