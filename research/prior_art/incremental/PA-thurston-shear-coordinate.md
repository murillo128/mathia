# Thurston shear coordinate

## What it is

For adjacent ideal triangles, the signed shear along their common diagonal is a standard Teichmüller coordinate. In an ideal quadrilateral it is represented by the logarithm of an appropriate positive cross-ratio; in decorated Teichmüller theory the same coordinate is expressed through logarithmic combinations of lambda lengths/renormalized horocycle distances.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

[[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-031-sojourn-gap-ratio-is-the-standard-shear-coordinate]] proves that its marked direct scattering-time difference is exactly the ideal-quadrilateral shear, up to orientation sign. The apparent prime/spectral observable therefore reads back an ordinary moduli coordinate already present in the geometry.

## Known scope and limits

This classical identification closes only the claim that the marked sojourn-time difference is a new spectral invariant. It does not imply that all scattering poles, resonances, eigenvalues, or other nonlinear spectral data are trivial functions of one shear coordinate.
