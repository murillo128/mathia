# Resultant potential theory on compact Riemann surfaces

## What it is

Gustafsson and Tkachev develop resultants for meromorphic functions on compact Riemann surfaces, give integral formulas for them, and relate those formulas to potential theory. This supplies a classical potential-theoretic interpretation for logarithmic resultant quantities rather than a new spectral structure.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-006-critical-gcd-kernel-and-potential-theory-downgrade-PC005]] uses this literature to downgrade the normalized resultant/discriminant sector of Prime Circle: its logarithmic interactions sit inside established resultant and potential theory. The prior art identifies the mathematical layer; it does not supply a new RH mechanism.

## Known scope and limits

The cited theory concerns resultants, integral representations, algebraic dependence, and potential theory on compact Riemann surfaces. It does not make the Prime Circle critical-line interpretation or any RH claim. Mathia's specific renormalization and arithmetic specialization remain project-level constructions.
