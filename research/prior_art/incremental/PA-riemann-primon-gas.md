# Riemann primon gas

## What it is

Bernard Julia's statistical-mechanical number-theory dictionary treats primes as elementary modes with logarithmic energies and integer prime exponents as occupation numbers. Unique factorization turns total energy into `log n`, so the bosonic partition sum reproduces the Riemann zeta function; square-free/fermionic variants encode the corresponding exclusion and sign structures.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-004-prime-exponent-gas-prior-art]] identifies the bare exponent-vector gas almost coordinate-for-coordinate with this classical model. Therefore the occupation-vector picture, `log p` energies and zeta partition function are baseline prior art, not a new Prime Lattice spectral mechanism.

## Known scope and limits

A partition function equal to zeta in its convergence half-plane does not provide analytic continuation or a spectral realization of nontrivial zeros. Richer arithmetic dynamical structures such as Bost–Connes or Connes's adelic trace program require additional data not present in the free gas.
