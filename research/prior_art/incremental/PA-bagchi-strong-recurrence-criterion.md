# Bagchi strong-recurrence criterion

## What it is

Bagchi's strong-recurrence result states that the Riemann hypothesis is equivalent to positive-lower-density self-approximation of the Riemann zeta function by its vertical translates on compact subsets of `1/2 < Re(s) < 1`. It upgrades the universality/translation-orbit viewpoint to an exact RH-equivalent recurrence property of the distinguished zeta function.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

[[research/prime_lattice/findings/PL-005-bagchi-prime-flow-recurrence-rh]] rewrites vertical translation in Bohr coordinates as the phase flow generated by the frequencies `log p`. The finding therefore lands on this exact classical criterion rather than discovering a new dynamical formulation of RH.

## Known scope and limits

Strong recurrence is an equivalent condition, not a proof of RH. Finite-dimensional Kronecker equidistribution of prime phases is much weaker and does not by itself imply the function-space recurrence theorem. The ordinary Euler product is not being asserted to converge pointwise in the critical strip.
