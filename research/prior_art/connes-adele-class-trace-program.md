# Connes adele-class trace program

## What it is

A noncommutative-geometric spectral program in which zeta or L-function zeros appear as missing states or an absorption spectrum for an idele-class action on adele-class spaces. Retained local and finite-place trace formulas recover corresponding explicit-formula terms, while noncritical zeros are represented as resonances.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

The program aims to make the explicit formula a genuine global trace formula and to explain the sign that obstructs a naive emitted-spectrum picture. It supplies concrete local constructions and a disciplined alternative to a bare Hilbert–Pólya analogy.

## Known scope and limits

The retained source explicitly leaves the decisive global trace formula open and does not claim Weil positivity or RH. Local trace results, a spectral interpretation, and a completed global proof are separate statuses.
