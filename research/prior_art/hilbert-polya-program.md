# Hilbert-Polya program

## What it is

A conditional spectral strategy seeking a self-adjoint operator whose real spectrum corresponds exactly to the ordinates of nontrivial zeta zeros. Retained statistical and periodic-orbit correspondences act as constraints on the missing system rather than as its construction.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

An exact self-adjoint realization with the required spectral identification would force the corresponding zeros onto the critical line. Mathia uses the program as a model of a potentially fertile representation whose proof value depends on the representation enforcing reality.

## Known scope and limits

No operator, domain, boundary conditions, or exact spectral identification is supplied by the retained evidence. Random-matrix statistics and prime/orbit analogies do not themselves prove RH.
