# Riemann zeta functional equation

## What it is

The reflection symmetry of the completed zeta function under s mapping to one minus s. Completion adds the archimedean factors needed to expose the symmetry.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

The equation organizes the critical line as the reflection axis and transfers information between the two sides of the strip. Mathia uses completion as a canonical representation change shared across L-function families.

## Known scope and limits

The functional equation is verified in pinned mathlib according to qwen, but symmetry alone does not place zeros on the reflection line or identify different L-functions.
