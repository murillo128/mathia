# Voronin universality translation orbit

## What it is

Voronin universality presents vertical translates of zeta as an infinite-dimensional approximation phenomenon; its probabilistic viewpoint studies the distribution of that translation orbit.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

It supplies a conceptual bridge between individual approximation shifts and a distribution over translated functions, a reusable move from pointwise witnesses to orbit-level behavior.

## Known scope and limits

Universality and recurrence do not by themselves prove RH. This note records only the retained source unit's denseness and translation-distribution viewpoints, not a stronger equivalence criterion.
