# Baez-Duarte strengthening of Nyman-Beurling

## What it is

A strengthening of the Nyman–Beurling closure criterion that restricts the generating family from all real dilation parameters at least one to natural-number dilations. The smaller arithmetic skeleton retains the target membership condition that is equivalent to RH.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

This result shows that the continuum of generators is not essential at the level of closed span. It is useful to Mathia as a precise example of a representation being compressed without losing the decisive property.

## Known scope and limits

The theorem statement does not provide a constructive approximating sequence or rate. It is not the Báez-Duarte sequence criterion based on zeta values. The qwen source label `baez-duarte-2002` and Mathia source `baezduarte2003_nyman` are retained as preprint/publication views of arXiv `math/0202141`, not as two mathematical nodes.
