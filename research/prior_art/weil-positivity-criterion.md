# Weil positivity criterion

## What it is

A reformulation of RH as nonnegativity of an explicit-formula zero functional for an admissible class of test functions. It changes the target from locating every zero to proving positivity of a structured family of quantities.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

The retained Mathia interpretation records the positivity statement as equivalent to RH and treats it as a central bridge between prime-zero duality and operator or geometric positivity programs.

## Known scope and limits

Equivalence does not explain why the functional should be nonnegative. Admissibility and normalization choices are mathematically substantive; the note does not generalize positivity beyond the retained test-function setting or claim a proof.
