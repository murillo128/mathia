# Spectral decomposition of normal operators

## What it is

For a finite-dimensional complex normal operator, an eigenvector and its invariant orthogonal complement permit induction to an orthogonal eigenspace decomposition.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

The proof architecture shows how a local invariant component can create a smaller problem of the same kind while preserving the structure required for iteration.

## Known scope and limits

A generic real operator may lack a real eigenline, and an arbitrary operator need not preserve the orthogonal complement. Eigenspaces are canonical, but bases inside repeated eigenspaces are auxiliary.
