# Levinson-Conrey mollifier method

## What it is

A method for proving lower bounds on the proportion of zeta zeros on the critical line by multiplying or combining zeta and its derivatives with a tunable Dirichlet-polynomial mollifier. The retained Conrey analysis identifies longer mollifiers, mean-value asymptotics, Kloosterman-sum control, and variational optimization as distinct components.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

It is a classical partial-progress route that demonstrates how representation choice and an auxiliary approximation can make a zero-location problem tractable without resolving all zeros.

## Known scope and limits

A positive critical-line proportion is not RH. Claimed improvements depend on the admissible mollifier length and all required twisted-moment or error estimates; an unproved supporting estimate cannot be counted as an achieved bound.
