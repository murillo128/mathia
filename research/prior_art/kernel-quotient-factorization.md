# Kernel-quotient factorization

## What it is

A map factors through the quotient by its kernel, separating the information it forgets from the injective map that faithfully retains what remains.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

It provides both a reusable compression move and a falsification test: a proposed equivalence is too coarse if it merges different images and unnecessarily fine if it preserves distinctions already killed by the map.

## Known scope and limits

The quotient is canonical relative to the chosen map, not an absolute simplification of the domain. This cross-domain note does not assert a new RH implication.
