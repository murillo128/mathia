# Robin criterion

## What it is

A universal upper bound for the divisor-sum function normalized by n log log n, required above a fixed finite threshold. The retained Mathia analysis places the same normalization beside Gronwall's limiting upper envelope and motivates extremal-number searches.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

The inequality is equivalent to RH. It gives a sparse arithmetic boundary where counterexamples would have to compete with the maximal-order scale.

## Known scope and limits

Restricting attention to extremal subsequences is useful only when a retained theorem justifies the reduction. Finite verification or asymptotic envelope information alone does not prove the universal inequality.
