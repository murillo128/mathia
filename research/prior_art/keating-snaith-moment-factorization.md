# Keating-Snaith moment factorization

## What it is

After matching mean densities, the zeta-moment model separates a universal random-matrix contribution from an arithmetic factor carrying prime-dependent information.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

The split is a useful example of decomposing a prediction into a transferable structural component and a domain-specific correction once the comparison scale is fixed.

## Known scope and limits

The retained evidence distinguishes proved CUE results, numerical or conditional zeta agreement, and conjectural zeta-moment extrapolation. Those statuses must not be collapsed.
