# Nyman-Beurling criterion

## What it is

A functional-analytic reformulation of zero exclusion as a closure or density statement for fractional-part functions. The retained Mathia object also records the wider L^p pattern: the ambient norm parameter tracks a corresponding zero-free half-plane, while a single characteristic-function target can compress the full density question.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

Both retained views classify the L² criterion as equivalent to RH. It is relevant to Mathia as a precise bridge from analytic zero location to approximation and representation selection.

## Known scope and limits

Closure membership is not a constructive approximation algorithm. The evidence supplies neither an effective convergence rate nor a Lean proof, and it does not imply that the discrete Baez-Duarte strengthening is automatic.
