# Dedekind-sum reciprocity in Farey correlations

## What it is

A finite correlation of periodic Bernoulli data is represented by a modified generalized Dedekind sum whose cyclic combination obeys a three-term reciprocity relation exposing gcd data.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

It converts oscillatory fractional-part correlations into an arithmetic object with a reciprocity law, illustrating how cyclic synthesis can remove directional dependence and reveal invariants.

## Known scope and limits

Exact coefficients are formula-sensitive and are not reproduced from the compact interpretation. The retained evidence supports generalized Dedekind-sum language, not an automatic identification with every Vasyunin-sum convention.
