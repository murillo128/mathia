# Sonin-space dual-localization positivity

## What it is

A Sonin space imposes matching localization gaps on a function and its Fourier transform; projection onto that space supplies a positive compressed-trace term.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

It exhibits an operator source for positivity at the archimedean place and a bridge from dual localization constraints to a trace inequality.

## Known scope and limits

The retained result applies to the stated compactly supported test class and specified transform vanishings. It does not include finite-prime terms or establish the global positivity criterion needed for RH.
