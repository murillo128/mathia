# Euler product for zeta

## What it is

The factorization of zeta into prime-indexed local factors in its half-plane of absolute convergence. Mathia's accepted view emphasizes that expanding local prime-power data organizes the global Dirichlet coefficients.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

The product is a foundational prime-to-function bridge and explains why prime-local information enters zeta-based research.

## Known scope and limits

The Euler product is verified in pinned mathlib according to qwen, but only in its stated convergence domain. Factorization alone does not supply analytic continuation, the functional equation, or critical-line zero location.
