# Montgomery pair correlation

## What it is

A conjectural fine-scale law for pairs of high zeta-zero ordinates after normalization by their local density. The retained Mathia material separates Montgomery's proved, RH-conditional restricted Fourier regime from the extrapolation that yields the full pair-correlation law and unitary symmetry prediction.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

The conjecture supplies a discriminating zero-statistics target and constrains spectral analogies. It also enters the retained de Bruijn–Newman argument as known local-statistical input against an overly rigid equilibrium consequence.

## Known scope and limits

The qwen atlas explicitly records no implication from the statistical similarity to RH. The full law is conjectural; restricted conditional results, numerical evidence, and a hypothetical exact operator are distinct epistemic claims.
