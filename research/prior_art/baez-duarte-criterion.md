# Baez-Duarte criterion

## What it is

A sequence criterion that concentrates zero information into coefficients formed from finite rational combinations of zeta values at positive even integers. The retained Mathia analysis records the RH-scale decay condition, an inversion formula, and an exponential generating function as different views of the same sequence.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

The qwen atlas records the asymptotic order condition as bidirectionally equivalent to RH. For Mathia it is a reusable example of compressing analytic zero information into a discrete, algebraically reversible observable.

## Known scope and limits

This is an equivalent criterion, not a proof of RH. Direct numerical checks can remain insensitive to hypothetical high off-line zeros, and the retained records report no Lean formalization. It is intentionally distinct from Báez-Duarte's strengthening of the Nyman–Beurling closure criterion.
