# Finite-height verification of RH

## What it is

A computer-assisted theorem that every nontrivial zeta zero up to a stated finite height lies on the critical line, supported by an explicit verification protocol.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

Finite checks calibrate zero-evaluation and counting machinery and constrain where a counterexample could first occur.

## Known scope and limits

A finite-height result is not evidence for all heights strong enough to prove RH. The qwen atlas classifies this as a computer-assisted literature result and records no Lean formalization of the complete verification.
