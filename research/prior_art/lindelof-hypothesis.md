# Lindelof hypothesis

## What it is

A conjectural sub-polynomial growth bound for zeta on the critical line.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

RH implies this hypothesis, and its growth control is often used as a conditional analytic resource in moment and approximation arguments.

## Known scope and limits

Lindelöf is not recorded as equivalent to RH here; the implication runs from RH to the growth statement. It remains an open literature-only conjecture in the qwen atlas.
