# Mertens conjecture

## What it is

The historical assertion that the absolute value of the summatory Möbius function stays below the square-root scale. The retained qwen atlas records it specifically to prevent a false stronger statement from being mistaken for a viable open criterion.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

The conjecture would imply RH, so it is a useful negative prior-art node when a Mathia route accidentally strengthens a legitimate Möbius-summatory RH criterion to this false bound.

## Known scope and limits

The Mertens conjecture is disproved. Its failure does not disprove RH, and this note does not conflate it with weaker Möbius-summatory conditions that are equivalent to RH.
