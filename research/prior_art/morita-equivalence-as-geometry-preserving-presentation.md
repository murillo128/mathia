# Morita equivalence as geometry-preserving presentation

## What it is

An endomorphism algebra can give a Morita-equivalent presentation of module geometry while an induced action preserves the stated trace structure.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

It illustrates comparing presentations at the module-category or Morita level, where geometric information may survive even when the concrete algebras differ.

## Known scope and limits

The retained interpretation is explicitly an inference from one source unit. It does not identify Morita-equivalent algebras literally or establish a new connection to the Riemann hypothesis.
