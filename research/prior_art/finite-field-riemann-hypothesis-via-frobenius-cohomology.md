# Finite-field Riemann hypothesis via Frobenius cohomology

## What it is

In the zeta theory of varieties over finite fields, reciprocal zeros and poles are interpreted as Frobenius eigenvalues on étale cohomology. For curves, the retained mechanism uses geometric positivity to constrain their magnitudes, while the retained overview presents Euler product, functional equation, and finite-field RH as a proved structural package.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

This is a proved analogue and a source of exact roles—Frobenius, cohomology, duality, and positivity—that a classical geometric proposal may try to reproduce. It is one of the strongest retained structural analogies for RH.

## Known scope and limits

The function-field theorem does not transfer a proof to the classical Riemann zeta function. The note does not infer that an analogous classical geometric object exists, and it preserves the distinction between zeros themselves and the reciprocal characteristic roots interpreted as eigenvalues.
