# Multiparameter persistence classification obstruction

## What it is

Moving from a one-direction persistence index to several independent directions destroys the interval decomposition that makes one-parameter persistence compressible as a barcode.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

It is a reusable warning that generalizing the geometry of an index can remove the canonical decomposition underlying a successful representation, creating a structural rather than merely computational obstruction.

## Known scope and limits

Changing arrow direction or broadening maps has different consequences from changing the index poset. The retained unit supports this distinction but no new RH application.
