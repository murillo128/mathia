# Dedekind zeta RH

## What it is

The conjecture that nontrivial zeros of a Dedekind zeta function lie on the critical line.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

It is a number-field generalization of RH and a comparison point for finite-field zeta theorems and explicit-formula programs.

## Known scope and limits

This is an open literature-only conjecture in the qwen atlas. The proved finite-field analogue and the Riemann-zeta special case must not be conflated with the number-field statement.
