# Li criterion

## What it is

A discrete positivity reformulation in which coefficients associated with the completed zeta function can be expressed through its zeros and through derivatives of log xi at a distinguished point.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

The qwen atlas records nonnegativity of every Li coefficient as equivalent to RH. Mathia's accepted analysis places the sequence beside Weil positivity as a compression from a functional family to discrete quantities.

## Known scope and limits

The criterion does not establish positivity, and the retained qwen record reports no located Lean proof. This note does not reconstruct formula-sensitive coefficient definitions beyond the accepted source descriptions.
