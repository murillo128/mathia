# Zero-density method

## What it is

A zero-detecting argument replaces zeros in small regions by a disjunction asserting largeness of one of several auxiliary analytic witnesses.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

Cauchy-Schwarz and mean-square estimates can then turn witness largeness into a zero-count bound, reframing a geometric counting problem as control of auxiliary quantities.

## Known scope and limits

The retained unit carries one branch of the method to an explicit upper bound. It is a partial-progress technique, not a proof that all nontrivial zeros lie on the critical line.
