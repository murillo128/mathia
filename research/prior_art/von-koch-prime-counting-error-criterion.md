# von Koch prime-counting error criterion

## What it is

A square-root-scale error bound comparing the prime-counting function with the logarithmic integral. The retained Mathia equivalence map treats this as a prime-side surface of the same zero-location claim.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

The qwen atlas records the bound as equivalent to RH. It supplies a standard target for any route claiming to turn zero control into prime-distribution accuracy.

## Known scope and limits

The logarithmic factor and choice of prime-counting observable are part of the statement. This criterion is not identical to the Chebyshev psi error formulation even though both are RH-equivalent.
