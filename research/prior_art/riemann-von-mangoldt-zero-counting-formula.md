# Riemann-von Mangoldt zero-counting formula

## What it is

An asymptotic formula for the number of nontrivial zeta zeros up to height T. Its main term determines the changing macroscopic zero density and therefore the natural scale for local statistics.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

The formula separates total zero abundance from the harder questions of critical-line proportion, multiplicity, and off-line density. It supplies the normalization used in pair-correlation and random-matrix comparisons.

## Known scope and limits

Counting all zeros does not show that they lie on the critical line. The qwen atlas records only partial formal components, not verification of the complete formula.
