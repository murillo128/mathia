# Mobius summatory criterion

## What it is

A square-root-scale cancellation condition for the summatory Möbius function: the retained qwen entry states the bound with an arbitrary positive epsilon, while Mathia contrasts this sign-cancellation observable with prime-power counting error.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

The condition is bidirectionally equivalent to RH and gives a prime-side cancellation interface that is structurally different from direct zero location.

## Known scope and limits

The criterion does not assert the false sharper Mertens bound. Its qwen status is foundations-only rather than a located formal proof of the full equivalence.
