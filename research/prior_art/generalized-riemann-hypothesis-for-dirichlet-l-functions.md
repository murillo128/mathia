# Generalized Riemann hypothesis for Dirichlet L-functions

## What it is

The conjecture that every nontrivial zero of every Dirichlet L-function has real part one half.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

It generalizes RH and provides the family-level target behind many conditional uniform arithmetic estimates.

## Known scope and limits

Dirichlet GRH remains open. A functional equation, verified local factors, or formalized prerequisites do not establish the conjecture for the family.
