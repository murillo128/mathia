# Operator-adapted Fourier diagonalization

## What it is

When an operator respects Fourier modes, expansion converts a coupled evolution equation into scalar coefficient evolutions, with boundary data helping select the admissible basis.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

It exemplifies choosing a representation adapted to the operator so that structural components decouple and scale-dependent behavior becomes predictable.

## Known scope and limits

Reconstruction remains a separate obligation; nonlinear terms mix frequencies, and weak convergence may not justify termwise differentiation. This note asserts no new RH application.
