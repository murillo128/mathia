# Prime number theorem

## What it is

The asymptotic law that the prime-counting function is equivalent to x divided by log x. Retained Mathia evidence contrasts analytic Tauberian routes with the Selberg–Erdős elementary proof architecture.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

PNT is weaker than RH and acts as a foundational checkpoint for prime-zero methods. It shows that the same arithmetic theorem can arise through substantially different representations.

## Known scope and limits

The qwen atlas records a verified proof in a pinned external Lean project, not a proof of RH. PNT corresponds to boundary zero-freeness and does not give the square-root-scale error required by RH-equivalent criteria.
