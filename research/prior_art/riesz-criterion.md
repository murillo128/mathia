# Riesz criterion

## What it is

A growth condition on the Riesz entire function constructed from zeta values. The retained Mathia comparison places its continuous growth behavior beside the discrete decay of the Báez-Duarte coefficients.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

The qwen atlas records the specified growth exponent as equivalent to RH. The criterion supplies a continuous representation of the same broad zeta-value information explored by sequence criteria.

## Known scope and limits

The direct relation to the Báez-Duarte sequence does not identify the two observables. Numerical oscillation and first-zero dominance are diagnostic representations, not proofs of the required asymptotic bound.
