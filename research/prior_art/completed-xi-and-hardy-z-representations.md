# Completed xi and Hardy Z representations

## What it is

Completion and reparameterization package zeta into an entire xi function with visible reflection symmetry and a real-valued Hardy Z function on the critical line.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

The representations expose symmetry, turn critical-line restriction into real-variable data, and support visualization and sign tests without changing the underlying zero information.

## Known scope and limits

Completion and reparameterization do not place off-line zeros on the line. The retained unit supports the roles of xi and Z, while damaged formula extraction makes exact normalization details unsuitable for reuse here.
