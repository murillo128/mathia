# Franel-Landau Farey discrepancy criterion

## What it is

A reformulation that orders Farey fractions, compares them with uniform grid positions, and aggregates the resulting discrepancy. The retained Mathia analysis keeps the quadratic and absolute-deviation norms distinct while identifying the common representation change from reduced-rational arithmetic to equidistribution error.
