# Random matrix model for zeta zeros

## What it is

A statistical analogy between locally unfolded zeta-zero ordinates and eigenvalues from unitary random-matrix ensembles. The retained Montgomery analysis uses an extrapolated form factor and Dyson's comparison to select the unitary symmetry class.

## Relation to RH / Mathia research

The model predicts local spacing and correlation behavior and constrains a prospective spectral realization. It provides a transfer laboratory for testing whether a proposed mechanism reflects universal statistics or arithmetic structure specific to zeta.

## Known scope and limits

Statistical agreement is not equality of spectra, an operator construction, or an implication of RH. Unfolding removes absolute height and global density information, and the full zeta pair-correlation law remains conjectural.
