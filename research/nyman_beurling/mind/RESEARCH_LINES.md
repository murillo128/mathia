# Nyman--Beurling research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Nyman--Beurling intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Connect the entire source-localized zeta-primitive pairing to zero-free-region data without decoupling its signed structure

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-target-aware-gram-geometry-must-carry-absolute-scale` through `MI-058-exact-zeta-primitive-reconstruction-loses-its-mellin-advantage-only-when-the-signed-convolution-is-decoupled`.

The positive-source program has progressively separated recurrence existence, source resolution, interval occupancy, atomic carrying capacity and integer-carrier discrepancy. NB-202--NB-212 derive the current log-squared corridor. NB-213--NB-217 then show that ordinary Mellin primitivization, signs, zero mean, bounded shell moments and support widening all repay apparent gains when reconstruction is estimated by absolute norms. For an arbitrary logarithmic support `E` and `j` primitive transfers, NB-217 isolates the exact absolute reconstruction currency `2pi/J_j(E)`.

NB-218 identifies where that tariff enters. On `Re(s)>1`, the Euler shell is exactly a signed convolution of the `j`-fold zeta primitive with its matched kernel; the factors `(log n)^j` and `(log n)^(-j)` cancel coefficientwise. Because the shell is supported away from zero, the kernel annihilates every finite polynomial jet of the primitive. The positive-power Mellin tariff is therefore not present in the exact algebra; it appears when kernel and primitive are decoupled by absolute values. The native observable is a carrier-frequency Fourier coefficient of the zeta primitive.

NB-219 first showed that the full vertical line can be smoothly localized on the natural `1+` source line with superalgebraically small source error. Its compact vertical cutoff produces a Schwartz, noncompact dual source tail; this removed the global vertical-domain cost but left a possible absolute-convergence obstruction when one tries to move the source representation left of `Re(s)=1`.

NB-220 removes that remaining **source-tail convergence** obstruction. Replacing exact compact vertical support by a Gaussian-type soft window gives Gaussian decay in the dual logarithmic source variable. For every prescribed algebraic accuracy one may choose a fixed window order so that the localized source differs from the original moving shell by `O_B(X^-B)`, while the corresponding Euler source series converges absolutely and locally uniformly for every complex `s`. The source-defined localized pairing is therefore entire. Finite-jet annihilation remains Gaussian-small rather than being lost.

This moves the bottleneck again. Neither the infinite vertical tail nor absolute summability of the localized source across `Re(s)=1` is now the live obstruction. What remains is an **analytic identification/transport theorem**: connect the entire source-defined continuation back to the zeta-primitive representation and Vinogradov--Korobov zero-free data on the left without importing remote zeta singularities, global acquisition or the NB-217 absolute reconstruction tariff. Entire source continuation by itself is not a bound for the primitive and does not justify a contour identity through its pole/branch structure.

The live target is therefore to prove that the soft-localized source pairing can be represented or deformed through the relevant zero-free corridor while preserving its signed carrier-frequency structure and quantifying all contour/remainder terms. A better pointwise bound for `log zeta`, another primitive, another sign pattern or another support widening does not address this target if the proof ultimately decouples the convolution or loses the source-defined continuation. A separate destination theorem remains load-bearing: a Nyman approximant resolving the target must still be connected to the source condition being estimated.

## Keep moving-complexity uniformity, source representation, conditioning, localization and analytic identification explicit

The relevant currencies now include source total variation, signed conditioning, requested return tolerance, integer-cell occupancy, zero-free contour width, reciprocal support mass `J_j(E)`, the destination-visible source functional, reconstruction cost, the exact carrier-frequency coefficient, soft vertical-window width/order, dual Gaussian source tails, the entire source-defined continuation, and the contour/identity terms required to relate that continuation back to zeta data across `Re(s)=1`.

NB-219--NB-220 give a useful separation principle. A large full-line Fourier domain need not be priced by the primitive's pointwise supremum, and source localization need not create a left-half-plane summability obstruction if the window class is chosen with analytic dual decay. The remaining price is no longer source-tail size but **faithful analytic transport**: proving that the entire source object still equals the intended zeta-primitive observable in the region where zero-free information is useful. Progress must be measured at that coupled identity/contour interface rather than by the window size or source continuation alone.
