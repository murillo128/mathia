NB-108 closes the fixed-dimensional confluent/Jordan repair as well. ## Decide the first genuinely new invariant beyond fixed-dimensional confluent geometry

NB-102 closes three-state local conditioning. NB-103--NB-106 close fixed-multiplicity determinant volume, boundary phase resolution, terminal placement and the volume/target-access frontier. ## Keep volume depth, target-access depth, subspace rank and leverage fraction separate

Curvature controls local conditioning. `L=2a log(B/G)` controls optimally placed fixed-multiplicity determinant volume. `H=2ad log G` controls ambient target access. NB-107--NB-108 add the fraction `eta` of maximal state leverage carried by the actual vector and show that the sharp complementary target fraction is dimension-free for fixed rank once the state subspace becomes target-orthogonal. Growing rank remains a separate resource because the terminal Laguerre conditioning used in NB-108 is not uniform in multiplicity. None of these currencies alone is the Nyman distance.
