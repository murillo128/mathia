**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-target-aware-gram-geometry-must-carry-absolute-scale`, `MI-003-low-gram-spectrum-is-cheap-target-occupation-is-the-signal`, `MI-004-weighted-tail-shell-quotient-is-a-conditioned-source-skeleton`, `MI-005-persistent-forward-memory-leakage-is-a-target-near-kernel-phenomenon`, `MI-006-boundary-zeros-force-reciprocal-depth-screening`, `MI-007-aliased-radial-prediction-has-a-positive-szego-floor`, `MI-008-finite-blaschke-defects-are-silent-in-terminal-scalar-energy`, `MI-009-summable-horizontal-zero-mass-keeps-full-terminal-gram-entropy-bounded`, `MI-010-growing-depth-mobius-annulus-exposes-off-critical-blaschke-amplification`, `MI-011-canonical-deflation-cancels-raw-cauchy-crowding-volume`, `MI-012-three-state-transverse-confluence-exposes-collective-shear`.

NB-092--NB-095 establish the growing-depth near-kernel, one-zero causal state and additive volume-repair exponent for every fixed finite set of distinct off-critical zeros. NB-096 removes the raw Cauchy determinant penalty exactly through canonical Blaschke deflation. NB-097 then proves every canonical leading pivot is one and every two-state transformed Gram is uniformly conditioned, even through distinct-point collision.

NB-098 shows that this pairwise stability does **not** extend dimension-free even at dimension three. A transverse family of three synthetic right-half-plane states has unit canonical pivots and uniformly bounded two-state subsystems, yet the canonical Cholesky shear grows like `epsilon^(-1)` and the full Gram condition number like `epsilon^(-4)`. The blow-up survives all six deflation orders. It is therefore a genuinely collective confluence effect, not a bad pair, determinant loss, collapsing pivot or unfortunate ordering.

The generic half-line geometry is now exhausted as a separation-insensitive conditioning route. Any uniform theorem for the actual zeta divisor must use additional source information: restrictions on admissible multi-zero configurations, zero-density/separation input strong enough to control collective shear, a representation adapted to crowded/confluent states, or a later target mechanism that does not require uniform conditioning of these canonical coordinates.

A separate route remains available: convert the sharp fixed-finite repair directly into a target-relevant Nyman obstruction before taking a growing-divisor limit.

## Keep collective half-line conditioning, true confluence, finite-window uniformity and target conversion separate

NB-098 uses distinct synthetic states approaching one point along a transverse path. It does not model a genuine repeated zeta zero, whose derivative/Jordan causal system is a different object. Nor does half-line conditioning settle the nonuniform finite-window/discretization errors or the final conversion into Nyman distance.

The remaining question is no longer whether pairwise crowding can be normalized away—it can—or whether collective shear can occur generically—it can. The source-specific problem is whether the actual zero divisor forbids or quantitatively limits the three-and-higher-state geometries that create that shear.
