NB-264--NB-267 force `Omega(m)` aggregate mesh response down to `O(log K)` rank-coupled coverage under bounded resolved cost. NB-268 then shows that this linear aggregate obstruction does not force diagonal quadratic energy: the legal uniform carrier keeps the aggregate mass while its damped diagonal diagnostic vanishes.

NB-269 resolves the actual Hardy-projected multi-shell diagnostic for the explicit matched packet. The resolved shell bands are disjoint after projection, so full-line Plancherel diagonalizes the shell index exactly. For the uniform positive carrier `c_j=1/K`, the projected energy is `Theta(1/K)` even though the center value remains order one on a spike of width `Theta(1/K)`. Thus the hard-window quadratic form itself can vanish; generic off-diagonal shell terms do not rescue this carrier.

NB-270 classifies every positive band-diagonal repair. If shell `j` is charged by `q_j`, the Euler anchor `sum c_j=1` has a rank-uniform quadratic floor exactly when `sum_j q_j^(-1)` remains bounded. Sobolev weights have the sharp threshold `s>1/2`; the critical `s=1/2` endpoint still leaks as `1/log K`, with the exact logarithmic refinement determined by reciprocal summability. The ordinary Hardy norm is the leaking `s=0` case.

NB-271 removes the generic off-diagonal loophole at the same abstraction level. For a positive Gram matrix `A_K`, the optimal anchored energy is `1/(1^* A_K^dagger 1)` when the anchor lies in the range, and zero if an anchor-visible null direction exists. Testing the uniform anchored vector shows that any rank-uniform positive floor requires operator strength at least `Theta(K)` relative to the canonical resolved Hardy norm. Uniformly bounded, finite-band or summably decaying positive corrections therefore cannot repair the leak.

NB-272 sharpens the remaining target-sensitive loophole by an exact finite-section identity. For source Gram `R_K=S_K^*S_K`, target pairing `b_K=S_K^*g`, and Euler anchor `e_K^*c=1`, the constrained residual splits into `delta_K^2 + |mu_K|^2/(e_K^*R_K^(-1)e_K)`, where `delta_K=dist(g,ran S_K)` and `mu_K=1-e_K^*R_K^(-1)b_K`. The target cross term changes the unconstrained projection and anchor mismatch but does not modify the source-source Hessian `R_K`; it cannot itself be the `Theta(K)` positive Gram repair sought after NB-271.

The live destination question is therefore a concrete fork. Estimate the actual canonical-target quantities `delta_K` and `mu_K` in the same rank-coupled resolved regime, or identify the smallest exact source component outside the NB-269 channel whose relative Gram has `Omega(K)` strength and passes the inverse-Gram anchor test. Merely invoking target cross terms, generic off-diagonal coherence, diagonal reweighting or local/summable corrections is closed at this abstraction level.

The separate source-side question of whether `O(log K)` aggregate coverage is minimal remains open, but improving that radius cannot by itself overcome the destination leak. Aggregate response, projected Hardy energy, target projection defect, anchor mismatch, inverse-Gram conditioning and the full Nyman norm are distinct currencies.

## Realize the Ford divisor through a legal source-native cost, not universal zero sampling

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-078-the-ford-current-is-a-singular-mellin-derivative-of-the-canonical-nyman-source`, `MI-079-finite-differentiated-nyman-sources-cannot-carry-interior-divisor-current`, `MI-080-finite-boundary-repair-is-exactly-hardy-zero-sampling-geometry`, `MI-081-first-free-zero-jets-have-sparse-support-but-unpriced-hilbert-conditioning`.

NB-276 gives the exact source identity `-s N[P_*'] = zeta'/zeta + zeta` for the canonical Möbius source `P_*=1/zeta`. The Ford divisor current is therefore encoded in a singular Mellin derivative, but differentiation inserts `log k`, multiplication by `s` is unbounded in the Hardy geometry, and the zero poles appear only after meromorphic continuation rather than a norm-continuous finite-source passage.

NB-277 closes the canonical Abel-shift repair: preserving Ford depth forces `X epsilon->0`, precisely the regime in which the differentiated shell coefficients retain their full logarithmic size. NB-278 closes direct finite differentiated truncation more strongly: every finite differentiated Nyman current is entire, so distributional continuity forbids convergence across a neighborhood containing a zero to the meromorphic divisor current.

NB-279 moves the poles legally into the dual functional. Every finite residual satisfies `r_P(rho)=1/rho`, giving a Hardy interpolation lower bound `||r_P||^2 >= v^*G^(-1)v`. NB-280 evaluates the canonical quadratic form exactly as the finite Burnol/Blaschke defect `1-|B_Z(1)|^2`; high-zero packets therefore carry only `O(1/G)` universal point-sampling energy even when matched Ford residue remains order one.

NB-281 closes the universal higher-jet escape. At a zero of multiplicity `m`, the only source-independent derivative data are the jets through order `m-1`, and their joint least Hardy cost is exactly the same multiplicity-counted Burnol defect. The first derivative of order `m` is source-dependent, through `P(rho)-P(1)`, and those first-free values can be prescribed independently on every finite zero packet.

NB-282 then removes support length as the simplest way to make those free jets expensive. For `N` distinct zeros, arbitrary first-free data can be realized by exactly `N` geometric integer atoms `q,q^2,...,q^N` for a suitable prime base, inside a natural cutoff with `log M=O(N log N)`. In particular, once `N log N=o(log M)`, existence of a finite-section neutralizer is too cheap to yield a persistent obstruction.

The live source-native question is now quantitative synthesis cost. For the specific first-free data that neutralize the Burnol/model-space minimizer, estimate the minimum Nyman/Hardy source norm under the natural cutoff. With section Gram `R_M` and zero-evaluation map `U`, the relevant object is the constrained inverse form built from `U R_M^(-1) U^*`, not another zero-only Hermite Gram and not support rank alone. A Ford-scale lower bound for that actual synthesis cost would be genuinely new; a matched-packet upper bound would close the first-free-jet repair and force movement to higher/global data or a nonlinear boundary observable.

This research line does not promote NB-273--NB-275 and does not use their pending claims as premises. NB-276--NB-282 do not establish `H^2` convergence to the singular current, an order-one Hardy lower bound, failure of Möbius cancellation, or any right-half zero. They classify where the legal finite boundary information lives and move the surviving cost from universal zero geometry and section existence to arithmetic conditioning in the actual finite synthesis map.

## Keep preassigned strips, rank-coupled coverage, certificate complexity, source Gram and target zero Gram distinct

A future theorem must state the order of quantifiers and every bounded resource. NB-269--NB-272 show that an order-one point residue can be hidden in a shrinking width while every positive quadratic observable continuously dominated by the resolved `H^2` channel pays the rank leak, and that the ordinary target pairing does not secretly alter the source Gram. A successful target mechanism must therefore locate either a genuine target mismatch that survives at order one or a new source-source component with the necessary rank-growing strength.

NB-276--NB-282 add a different source-native route rather than repairing that quadratic channel. The exact Ford current is present only after a singular derivative/continuation operation; the legal finite boundary route reduces all source-independent point and multiplicity-jet data to the Burnol defect; and even the first source-dependent jet can be realized with sparse finite support once the cutoff exceeds the explicit interpolation budget. Future work must therefore distinguish finite legal source approximation, singular dual weights, universal zero sampling, first-free source data, support/rank existence, interpolation conditioning, the actual source Gram, meromorphic continuation and Ford-scale localization. An ill-conditioned zero Gram or a large nominal derivative count is not extra Nyman coercivity until the corresponding cost is proved in the finite arithmetic synthesis norm.
