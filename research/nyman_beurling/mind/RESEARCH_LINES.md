This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Nyman--Beurling evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Keep aggregate Ford coverage separate from Hardy coercivity

NB-264--NB-272 show that large aggregate Ford response can coexist with a vanishing positive Hardy quadratic signal. Resolved shell bands can carry order-one point residue while their projected `H^2` energy leaks with rank, and positive band-diagonal repairs need rank-growing strength to restore a uniform anchored floor. Target pairings change the projection defect and anchor mismatch but do not secretly strengthen the source-source Gram. The live destination-side fork remains: either the canonical target quantities themselves stay order one in the moving regime, or a new exact source component supplies the missing rank-growing Gram strength.

## Price first-free boundary data through source-specific future sample-null innovations

NB-276--NB-283 move the source-native divisor route away from universal zero sampling. The Ford current is a singular Mellin derivative of the canonical Möbius Nyman source, but finite differentiated sources are entire and cannot carry the interior divisor current across a zero. Moving the pole into a legal dual functional reduces all source-independent point and multiplicity-jet data to the finite Burnol/Blaschke defect. The first source-dependent jet can be interpolated with sparse finite support, so support rank alone is not an obstruction.

NB-284--NB-287 isolate the genuine finite-section penalty. For a fixed zero packet the first-free synthesis cost converges to the full-space cost; any persistent obstruction must be a **moving-packet** phenomenon. After the first surjective section, each genuinely new source direction has a canonical neutralized innovation in the exact sampling nullspace, and the finite-section excess is exactly the Parseval tail of the current minimum-norm interpolant on the future sample-null innovations. The earlier leverage and target-alignment factors are one squared source-space angle after sample neutralization.

NB-288 closes a broad abstract shortcut. In a nested finite-section interpolation problem one can prescribe an arbitrary decreasing excess profile `a_M->0` while keeping every sampling Gram uniformly positive and uniformly conditioned, every optimal right inverse uniformly bounded, and every new source direction sample-visible. Moving families can keep a fixed excess floor at prescribed cutoffs with Gram condition numbers arbitrarily close to one. Thus generic Hilbert geometry, surjectivity, density, good conditioning and nonzero sample visibility impose no uniform decay rate on the target-aware null-innovation tail.

The decisive moving-packet question is therefore source-specific: for the actual Dirichlet atoms, zero-sampling map and natural sections `M_X`, does the minimum-norm first-free interpolant retain nonvanishing Parseval mass in future neutralized **Dirichlet** innovations, or does arithmetic localization force that tail to vanish? Any useful upper bound must exclude the NB-288 lethargy control by exploiting the structure of the orthogonalized Dirichlet atoms and their zero samples; generator-Gram decay or conditioning matters only if it transfers to those target-specific neutralized correlations. A lower bound would provide a genuine finite arithmetic-section obstruction; an upper bound would close the first-free repair route.
