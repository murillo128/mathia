NB-112 closes the remaining **true one-zero multiplicity rescue**. ## Keep volume depth, target-access depth, intrinsic Laguerre depth, true multiplicity and multi-zero rank separate

Curvature controls local conditioning. `L=2a log(B/G)` controls optimally placed fixed-multiplicity determinant volume. `H=2ad log G` controls ambient target access. NB-107--NB-111 add the principal-angle leverage fraction and the intrinsic one-zero extrapolation coordinate `T=2(1-a)log(m/r)`. In the exact weighted representation the decisive deterministic ratio is `d/T`; the `1/8` window comes from resolving about half of `T` against the Laguerre `4d` turning scale.

NB-112 adds a different source currency: the true multiplicity of a single off-critical zero is bounded by the same-ordinate zero-counting jump and falls strictly inside that deterministic window. This does **not** control the number or geometry of distinct zeros used simultaneously. Future claims must therefore distinguish derivative rank at one zero from multi-center rank across zeros; neither is the Nyman distance by itself.
