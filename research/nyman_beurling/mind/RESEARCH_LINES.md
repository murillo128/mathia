**Linked intuitions:** `MI-062-the-critical-ford-mark-is-a-growing-moment-observable`, `MI-063-demodulated-ford-energy-is-bandlimited-at-the-window-scale`.

NB-253 now closes the canonical positive fixed-`H` multi-shell comb. The effective selected-height population becomes `J/K`, and an order-one matched packet reappears at `d=-log K+O(1)`. Super-`H` convex support therefore does not count as independent averaging bandwidth when coherent recombination renormalizes the dangerous population at the same time.

The live alternative is narrower: either prove zeta-specific off-diagonal cancellation for the Hardy-projected current on the legal `O(H)` band, or construct a genuinely different multi-carrier operation whose energy spans `W>>H` while the Ford depth coefficients, selected-height capacity and projection-before-squaring geometry remain at the original scale. Signed/complex coefficients, a carrier family retained in `ell^2` until after projection, or a nonuniform design remain open only if their coherence kernel does not simply recreate a `1/W` peak with a shallower matched layer.
