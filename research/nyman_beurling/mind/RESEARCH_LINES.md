## Build an amplitude-balanced phase certificate without hiding the source cost

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-target-aware-gram-geometry-must-carry-absolute-scale` through `MI-049-fixed-prime-phase-rank-survives-density-one-acquisition-filters`.

The source-side problem has separated raw phase accessibility from physical Euler acquisition. Capped-Poisson transport, covering numbers and approximate linear complexity price how much a height-adapted template family can move. The natural scalar Euler functional escapes the bounded Hilbert dual but is still priced by an absolute Wiener/Dirichlet budget `W(a)~1/a`.

NB-184 shows that, after quotienting constants, the actual scalar Euler acquisition is cheap on density-one heights whenever `T a^2 -> infinity`. NB-185 preserves every fixed finite prime-phase code inside those good heights, and NB-186 obtains growing raw nuclear rank `N~1/a` using pairwise phase decorrelation and fourth-moment selection at the cubic simultaneous-selection scale.

NB-187 identifies why that raw code is not yet source-faithful. The first `O(1/a)` primes carry only `Theta(a log(1/a))` of the normalized Euler--Wiener mass. Any positive source fraction requires an exponentially larger population of Euler channels. Isolating the favorable Poisson-sized block therefore costs the reciprocal of this vanishing mass in Wiener coefficient currency.

NB-188 then prices overlapping aggregation of those many channels. In the same-frequency linear Wiener model, a Hadamard-scale `N x N` response forces square-root source amplification; at bounded row gain, the effective source fanout must remain `O(1)`. Broad reuse of many weak channels does not preserve dense phase nuclear complexity for free.

The live source-side target is therefore very concrete: construct a **positive-mass, bounded-fanout or otherwise explicitly paid aggregation** of true-amplitude Euler channels that preserves a growing phase certificate and remains affordable in the capped-Poisson/Wiener acquisition norm. A negative result could instead prove that every source-faithful aggregation capable of retaining positive Wiener mass necessarily loses the nuclear response or pays an acquisition gain too large to be useful.

The cubic selection scale and quadratic density-one acquisition scale remain different boundaries. Neither is automatically the final Nyman threshold.

## Keep source persistence and destination conditioning separate

The current results show that frequency membership, raw rank and density-one phase coherence are not enough. Any proposed certificate must retain the true Euler amplitudes, normalized source mass, acquisition norm, effective fanout and the conditioning of the final Nyman approximation problem.

Conversely, failure of the current same-frequency aggregation model would not prove that arithmetic source response is absent. It would show that one representation cannot compress exponentially many physical channels into Poisson-many effective observables at bounded cost. A successful continuation must either exhibit a different source-native compression with a fully charged resource ledger or derive a destination theorem that uses the available scalar source information without first manufacturing a dense phase code.
