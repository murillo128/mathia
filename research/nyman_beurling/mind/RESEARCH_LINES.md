# Nyman--Beurling research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Nyman--Beurling intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Force target-bearing geometry, not merely visibility or a well-conditioned coordinate chart

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-target-aware-gram-geometry-must-carry-absolute-scale`, `MI-003-low-gram-spectrum-is-cheap-target-occupation-is-the-signal` through `MI-028-global-density-rarity-is-not-worst-cell-coercivity`.

NB-123--NB-132 build the source-faithful recurrence/phase route and show that actual zero counting can put a narrow polynomial-height packet below a recurrence-visibility rank budget. NB-133--NB-136 then separate visibility, representation supply and conditioning: matched clustered packets can make scalar annihilators, finitely many geometric bases, and even the full natural-prefix evaluation matrix superpolynomially ill-conditioned under the same coarse source envelopes.

NB-137 shows that ordinary conditioning is not the endpoint quantity. A simple cluster may converge in the Grassmannian to a confluent jet state space while its raw power-basis condition number becomes arbitrarily worse, yet the **whole state span** has vanishing normalized projection of the canonical Nyman target. Divided-difference/confluent coordinates can remove a chart singularity without creating target relevance.

NB-138 puts that obstruction at an explicit polynomial scale for logarithmic packet rank. NB-139 removes all dependence on the internal vertical gap pattern: arbitrary pairwise-distinct nodes inside one small cell inherit the target-poor confluent limit with no inverse minimum-gap penalty.

NB-140 removes the remaining vertical-line restriction. The state family is holomorphic in complex displacement, so complex Newton divided differences control packets whose zeros move simultaneously in ordinate and horizontal depth. If

`Delta_G G^(c log(4/a-3)) (log G)^(25/2) -> 0`,

then every pairwise-distinct simple packet in the complex displacement disk `|z_j|<=Delta_G` has vanishing normalized target projection. In particular every arbitrary two-dimensional packet inside `|z_j|<=G^(-B)` with `B>c log(4/a-3)` inherits the barrier, independent of direction, anisotropy, ordering or internal pairwise gaps.

NB-141 tests the strongest already-imported near-critical density input against that exact cell geometry. Selberg density implies that, at fixed horizontal displacement `a`, the centers `t in [G,2G]` whose radius-`G^(-B)` cell contains `c log G` right-half zeros occupy only a relative height measure `O(G^(-B-a/8))`. Thus the forbidden cells are power-law rare. But a sparse logarithmic-rank matched control can still satisfy the explicit Riemann--von Mangoldt envelope, Selberg density and the Vinogradov--Korobov zero-free region while producing infinitely many such target-poor cells. **Average-height rarity is therefore not worst-cell coercivity.**

NB-142 identifies the exact scalar source budget consumed by such an exceptional cell. On every polynomially short vertical interval, the smooth Riemann--von Mangoldt main term changes by `o(1)`, so local zero occupancy is the positive increment of the remainder `R(T)=N(T)-M(T)` up to `o(1)`. A right-half cell containing `c log G` zeros forces at least `2c log G` total zeros after functional-equation reflection and therefore an order-`log G` positive jump of `R`, equivalently of the classical zeta argument `S(T)`. Thus `R(T)=o(log T)` — and in particular the classical Lindelöf consequence `S(T)=o(log T)` — rules out every fixed-positive logarithmic-rank `NB-140` packet. The present Bellotti--Wong `O(log T)` envelope stops exactly one order too early.

The geometric parameter remains the **total complex cluster diameter plus logarithmic occupancy**, not minimum spacing, vertical alignment or a preferred Vandermonde chart. The source frontier is now more exact: improving global density exponents only decreases the measure/frequency of dangerous cells, while the worst-cell route asks for sublogarithmic **positive polynomial-scale increments** of the zero-count remainder. A global `S(T)=o(log T)` bound is sufficient but stronger than necessary; a local increment theorem at the `NB-140` scales would already close this escape.

The live theorem must therefore do one of two genuinely different things: derive `o(log G)` positive increments of `R(T)` on every relevant polynomial window for the actual zeta divisor, or bypass occupancy by proving a direct lower bound on Nyman-target projection for an actually crowded cell. Coefficient occupation of target-bearing singular sectors or another representation whose margin is defined directly against the target remain possible versions of the second route. Proving merely that zeros are usually well distributed, controlling one minimum gap, stabilizing a coordinate basis, or sharpening a global zero-density exponent does not address the surviving worst-cell requirement.

The shell decomposition still reduces the rooted-mixing channel to a quantitative coefficient discrepancy. ## Keep recurrence order, translation resolution, coordinate conditioning, subspace geometry, target occupation and worst-cell source control separate

NB-128 buys local resolution for one common phase translation by growing the natural prefix. NB-132 restores rank visibility in a concrete actual-zero regime. NB-133--NB-136 show that those facts do not give a conditioned packet representation. NB-137--NB-140 show that catastrophic raw conditioning and arbitrary two-dimensional simple-node geometry can coexist with a target-poor confluent subspace. NB-141 adds that even strong global rarity of those cells does not remove a sparse exceptional sequence. NB-142 shows that such an exceptional sequence would necessarily create order-log upward oscillations of the zero-count remainder on polynomially shrinking intervals.

The surviving discriminator is representation-invariant, local and worst-cell. A proposal must state which subspace or quotient is physically meaningful, how the actual target occupies it, what **complex cluster-scale** source law prevents that occupation from vanishing, and why the law applies uniformly to actual zeta zeros rather than merely on average or in a chosen chart. Numerical conditioning, average gaps, one-dimensional spacing bounds, global density or small horizontal freedom are secondary unless they change the target-bearing state geometry or the local remainder increment budget.
