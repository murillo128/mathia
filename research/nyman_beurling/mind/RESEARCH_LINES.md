The relevant currencies now include source mass, source total variation, signed conditioning `kappa`, requested return tolerance, integer-cell occupancy, moving derivative order, zero-free contour width, logarithmic support width, reciprocal support mass `J_j(E)`, the norm of the destination-visible source functional, the exact prefactor multiplying the contour saving, whether the observable is prime-only or full von Mangoldt, and the reconstruction cost introduced when derivatives are moved between the arithmetic transform and Mellin carrier.
