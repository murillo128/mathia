The converse matched control is equally important. No fixed finite moment panel is therefore the missing theorem for the current information set.

The live source question is now precise: can actual zeta zeros satisfy sufficiently uniform cancellation for a **slowly growing local profiled depth-moment hierarchy** in each relevant `1/H` window, or can the equivalent Laplace-marked coefficient be controlled directly?
