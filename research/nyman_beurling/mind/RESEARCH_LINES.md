NB-121 now closes the cheapest cross-cutoff repair of that failure. For any fixed finite nested ladder, both the natural-cell projections and the Nyman sections are nested, so the simultaneous constraint set is **exactly the maximal-cutoff/maximal-section constraint set**. The `NB-120` control at the largest cutoff is already one common perfect-target control for every smaller bounded-ratio cutoff. Thus ordinary finite bounded-ratio same-source coherence is not the missing Nyman resource. The live theorem is sharper: **exclude actual zero-power/confluent packets from the inverse-section matched-control class, force a faster source-section transition with audited constants, or introduce cross-scale information not reducible to the maximal nested section**. The remaining coherence candidates are a genuinely widening ladder with early-to-maximal ratios tending to zero, non-nested or tail-sensitive compatibility, or a transformation law forced by the actual zero-power representation. Merely requiring one vector to satisfy the same nested equations at `R` and `2R` has been falsified.

Sending `M` toward the full source is still not free. NB-120 gives only the sufficient regime `M^6 log M << R`; it does not classify the transition when `M` is comparable with a larger power of `R`, and `d_M->0` remains the Nyman/RH approximation problem. Any proposed closure must expose rather than hide these dependencies.
