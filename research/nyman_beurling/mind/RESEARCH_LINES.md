# Nyman--Beurling research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Nyman--Beurling intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Restrict the acquisition surface, not merely its source-mass ledger

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-target-aware-gram-geometry-must-carry-absolute-scale` through `MI-050-paying-euler-source-mass-and-fanout-tariffs-still-does-not-make-a-phase-code-arithmetic`.

The source-side program has separated raw phase accessibility from physical Euler acquisition. The natural scalar Euler functional escapes the bounded Hilbert dual but is priced by an absolute Wiener/Dirichlet budget `W(a)~1/a`. NB-187 shows that the first `O(1/a)` favorable prime channels carry vanishing normalized source mass, and NB-188 shows that compressing a positive source fraction into a Hadamard-scale code forces square-root source-density amplification and bounded effective fanout at bounded row gain.

NB-189 now proves that those tariffs are sharp but not sufficient for arithmetic selectivity. A deterministic partition of a fixed positive-mass portion of the **true-amplitude** Euler--Wiener source into `K~1/a` disjoint contiguous log-frequency bands has fanout one and exactly the required `sqrt K` source-density gain. Yet on a Poisson-packed matched control the band responses form, up to a row envelope and rank-one correction, a DFT-scale matrix with `Theta(K^(3/2))` nuclear response.

The former live target—construct a positive-mass bounded-fanout aggregation preserving a growing phase certificate—is therefore resolved at the representation level, and it resolves in the wrong direction: such an aggregation exists even for a non-arithmetic matched control. The next discriminator must constrain **which coefficient-side decompositions are physically available**. A promising formulation is to compare independently normalized spectral-band access with the smaller operator family generated by the actual scalar Euler translates and the realized Nyman template range.

A positive route should prove that the Nyman architecture supplies an acquisition coupling unavailable to packed controls. A negative route could show that any admissible acquisition generated from the scalar Euler family factors through a low-complexity/common normalization that cannot reproduce the independent band code. In either case, source amplitudes, positive mass, gain and fanout remain part of the ledger, but they no longer constitute the final discriminator.

The cubic simultaneous-selection scale and quadratic density-one acquisition scale remain separate technology boundaries. Neither is automatically the final Nyman threshold.

## Keep source persistence and destination conditioning separate

NB-189 is deliberately a source/acquisition matched-control theorem. It does not establish that the actual Nyman realized range contains the Poisson-packed dipoles used to expose the DFT code. Therefore the next step must keep two questions distinct: which Euler acquisition operators the source architecture can actually realize, and whether the resulting observables remain well conditioned and destination-selective on the true Nyman approximation family.

Frequency membership, raw rank, true amplitudes, positive normalized source mass, bounded fanout and sharp amplification are all now known to be insufficient individually and jointly within the permissive band-routing model. A successful continuation must either restrict that model for a source-native reason or find a destination theorem that makes the true Nyman range distinguishable from its packed matched controls.
