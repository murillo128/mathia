NB-115 localizes the canonical finite-section escape to target-bearing cell-compression modes. NB-116 uses horizontal Burnol mass plus Selberg density to put every high-zero packet above height `G` on retention scale `O(1/G+1/R)`. NB-117 identifies finite target access exactly with weighted interpolation of the harmonic minimizer `C+D/n` by confluent actual-zero power sums.

NB-118 adds an independent source equation: every finite packet of actual zeta zeros is orthogonal to every fixed finite Nyman generator space `V_M`. Above the residual target baseline `d_M^2`, this forces a target-bearing witness into `O_M(1/R)` retained cell energy. NB-119 proves that the exponent is sharp for the broader class `V_M^perp`: for each fixed `M`, a minimum-norm invisible tail repairs the perfect visible target into exact Nyman orthogonality with retained fraction `1/(kappa_M R)+O_M(1/R^2)`.

NB-120 now closes the most immediate growing-section escape in a substantial mixed-tail regime. If `M=M(R)->infinity` with `M^6(1+log M)/R->0`, there are still exact controls `f_(M,R) in V_M^perp` with `Q_R f_(M,R)=e_R` and

`||Q_R f_(M,R)||^2/||f_(M,R)||^2 = (1+o(1))/(kappa_infty R)`,

where

`kappa_infty = 12 sum_p p^2(log p)^2/(p^2-1)^2`

is finite and positive. The periodic tail Gram admits an exact GCD/Jordan-totient factorization, and the repair constants converge rather than diverge as these Nyman equations are added.

Thus neither a fixed finite source section nor a slowly growing exact Nyman section automatically beats the inverse-section matched-control boundary. The live source theorem is sharper: **exclude actual zero-power packets from a matched-control class that survives growing Nyman orthogonality**, or leave the mixed-tail regime by proving that a faster-growing source section changes the repair geometry with controlled constants. Plausible inputs are the confluent power-sum representation itself, cross-cutoff coherence preventing the witness from being rebuilt independently for each `R`, or another identity specific to actual zeta zeros.

Sending `M` toward the full source is still not free. NB-120 gives only the sufficient regime `M^6 log M << R`; it does not classify the transition when `M` is comparable with a larger power of `R`, and `d_M->0` remains the Nyman/RH approximation problem. Any proposed growing-`M` closure must expose rather than hide these dependencies.

## Keep target baseline, zero height, continuum mass, cell retention and source coherence separate

NB-118 is not another Gram-conditioning estimate: it combines actual-zero annihilation of Nyman generators with finite target geometry. NB-119--NB-120 supply matched controls showing exactly how far that source information can go without using the actual zero-power representation. Even a growing family of exact source equations can permit perfect target alignment at `Theta(1/R)` retention when its periodic tails remain in the mixed regime.

Future claims should therefore distinguish available zero rank, horizontal zero mass, Nyman-section depth, continuum norm, retained cell energy, approximation of the harmonic minimizer, and **coherence of the source representation across cells, generators and cutoffs**. The unresolved object is whether the actual zero-power interpolation class can occupy the inverse-section mode that increasingly rich Nyman orthogonality itself still allows.
