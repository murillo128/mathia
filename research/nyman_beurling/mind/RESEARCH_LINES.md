## Control the normalized geometry of the Blaschke-deflated enrichment

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-coefficient-geometry-needs-target-coupling`, `MI-003-low-gram-spectrum-is-cheap-target-occupation-is-the-signal`, `MI-004-weighted-tail-shell-quotient-is-a-conditioned-source-skeleton`.

NB-049--NB-052 identify the finite repair gap, its closure-level limit `Delta_*`, and remove the common off-critical-zero Blaschke floor exactly.

NB-053 shows that the residual lives off the natural integer-log grid. NB-054 then rules out a simpler large-shift explanation: the distinguished deflated outer orbit is uniformly absorbed by the natural closure at large logarithmic shift, and the raw quotient energy of large-ratio finite-grid columns has a uniform `o(R^-2)` tail.

The repair gap can nevertheless survive because a span is unchanged by rescaling nonzero columns. The live bridge is therefore to control the **direction/conditioning of the tiny deflated quotient columns**, or isolate the bounded/moderate ratio sector that carries the enrichment, using source information available before solving the target projection.

## Treat raw quotient mass and normalized target geometry as separate gates

Large-ratio raw columns become uniformly negligible, but their normalized directions may remain valuable. Future work should target Gram/angle geometry or bounded-ratio structure rather than infer target irrelevance from small column norms.
