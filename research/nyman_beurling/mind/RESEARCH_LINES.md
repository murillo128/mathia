# Nyman--Beurling research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Nyman--Beurling intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Force target-bearing geometry, not merely visibility or a well-conditioned coordinate chart

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-target-aware-gram-geometry-must-carry-absolute-scale`, `MI-003-low-gram-spectrum-is-cheap-target-occupation-is-the-signal` through `MI-027-ordinary-near-confluent-conditioning-can-be-arbitrarily-bad-while-the-whole-state-space-is-target-poor`.

NB-123--NB-132 build the source-faithful recurrence/phase route and show that actual zero counting can put a narrow polynomial-height packet below a recurrence-visibility rank budget. NB-133--NB-136 then separate visibility, representation supply and conditioning: matched clustered packets can make scalar annihilators, finitely many geometric bases, and even the full natural-prefix evaluation matrix superpolynomially ill-conditioned under the same coarse source envelopes.

NB-137 shows that ordinary conditioning is not the endpoint quantity. A simple cluster may converge in the Grassmannian to a confluent jet state space while its raw power-basis condition number becomes arbitrarily worse, yet the **whole state span** has vanishing normalized projection of the canonical Nyman target. Divided-difference/confluent coordinates can remove a chart singularity without creating target relevance.

NB-138 puts that obstruction at an explicit polynomial scale for logarithmic packet rank. NB-139 removes all dependence on the internal vertical gap pattern: arbitrary pairwise-distinct nodes inside one small cell inherit the target-poor confluent limit with no inverse minimum-gap penalty.

NB-140 removes the remaining vertical-line restriction. The state family is holomorphic in complex displacement, so complex Newton divided differences control packets whose zeros move simultaneously in ordinate and horizontal depth. If

`Delta_G G^(c log(4/a-3)) (log G)^(25/2) -> 0`,

then every pairwise-distinct simple packet in the complex displacement disk `|z_j|<=Delta_G` has vanishing normalized target projection. In particular every arbitrary two-dimensional packet inside `|z_j|<=G^(-B)` with `B>c log(4/a-3)` inherits the barrier, independent of direction, anisotropy, ordering or internal pairwise gaps.

The geometric parameter is therefore the **total complex cluster diameter plus logarithmic occupancy**, not minimum spacing, vertical alignment or a preferred Vandermonde chart. The same coarse right-half zero-count and Vinogradov--Korobov envelopes still permit `Theta(log G)` formal points in such polynomially shrinking two-dimensional cells.

The live theorem must constrain a target-bearing invariant of the **actual** zero packet: a source law forcing `o(log G)` occupancy in every forbidden complex polynomial confluence cell, a direct lower bound on Nyman-target projection, coefficient occupation of target-bearing singular sectors, or another representation whose margin is defined directly against the target. Proving merely that zeros are not vertically aligned, controlling one minimum gap, or stabilizing a coordinate basis does not address NB-140.

## Push the canonical shell discrepancy toward a near-linear scale

**Linked intuition:** `MI-002-small-shell-discrepancy-can-destroy-rooted-visibility`.

The shell decomposition still reduces the rooted-mixing channel to a quantitative coefficient discrepancy. The live theorem is whether the canonical arithmetic coefficients can force near-linear discrepancy on the relevant shell family without assuming the Nyman distance estimate one is trying to prove.

## Keep recurrence order, translation resolution, coordinate conditioning, subspace geometry and target occupation separate

NB-128 buys local resolution for one common phase translation by growing the natural prefix. NB-132 restores rank visibility in a concrete actual-zero regime. NB-133--NB-136 show that those facts do not give a conditioned packet representation. NB-137--NB-140 show that catastrophic raw conditioning and arbitrary two-dimensional simple-node geometry can coexist with a well-defined target-poor confluent subspace.

The surviving discriminator is representation-invariant. A proposal must state which subspace or quotient is physically meaningful, how the actual target occupies it, what **complex cluster-scale** source law prevents that occupation from vanishing, and why the law applies to actual zeta zeros rather than only to a chosen chart. Numerical conditioning, average gaps, one-dimensional spacing bounds or small horizontal freedom are secondary unless they change the target-bearing state geometry.
