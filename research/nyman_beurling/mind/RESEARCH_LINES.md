# Nyman--Beurling research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Nyman--Beurling intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Force target-bearing geometry, not merely visibility or a well-conditioned coordinate chart

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-target-aware-gram-geometry-must-carry-absolute-scale`, `MI-003-low-gram-spectrum-is-cheap-target-occupation-is-the-signal` through `MI-029-logarithmic-confluence-is-exactly-a-short-scale-zero-count-remainder-budget`.

NB-123--NB-132 build the source-faithful recurrence/phase route and show that actual zero counting can put a narrow polynomial-height packet below a recurrence-visibility rank budget. NB-133--NB-136 then separate visibility, representation supply and conditioning: matched clustered packets can make scalar annihilators, finitely many geometric bases, and even the full natural-prefix evaluation matrix superpolynomially ill-conditioned under the same coarse source envelopes.

NB-137 shows that ordinary conditioning is not the endpoint quantity. A simple cluster may converge in the Grassmannian to a confluent jet state space while its raw power-basis condition number becomes arbitrarily worse, yet the **whole state span** has vanishing normalized projection of the canonical Nyman target. Divided-difference/confluent coordinates can remove a chart singularity without creating target relevance.

NB-138--NB-140 make that obstruction representation-invariant at polynomial scale. Arbitrary pairwise-distinct zeros inside a sufficiently small two-dimensional complex cell inherit the target-poor confluent limit, independent of direction, ordering or minimum gap.

NB-141 tests the strongest already-imported near-critical density input against that exact cell geometry. Selberg density makes logarithmic-rank forbidden cells power-law rare in height, but a sparse matched divisor can still obey the current global count, density and zero-free envelopes while producing infinitely many such cells. **Average-height rarity is not worst-cell coercivity.**

NB-142 identifies the exact scalar source budget consumed by an exceptional cell. On polynomially short vertical intervals the smooth Riemann--von Mangoldt main term changes by `o(1)`, so local zero occupancy is the positive increment of the remainder `R(T)=N(T)-M(T)` up to `o(1)`. A right-half cell containing `c log G` zeros forces an order-`log G` positive jump of `R`, equivalently of the classical zeta argument `S(T)`. Thus `R(T)=o(log T)`—and in particular the classical Lindelöf consequence `S(T)=o(log T)`—rules out every fixed-positive logarithmic-rank NB-140 packet. The current explicit `O(log T)` remainder envelope stops exactly one order too early.

The source frontier is therefore exact: improving global density exponents only decreases the frequency of dangerous cells, while the worst-cell route asks for sublogarithmic **positive polynomial-scale increments**

`Omega_B(G)=sup_(v-u<=O(G^-B)) (R(v)-R(u)) = o(log G)`.

A global sublogarithmic remainder bound is sufficient but stronger than necessary; a local increment theorem at the NB-140 scales would already close this escape.

The live theorem must do one of two genuinely different things: derive that short-increment law for the actual zeta divisor, or bypass occupancy by proving a direct lower bound on Nyman-target projection for an actually crowded cell. Proving merely that zeros are usually well distributed, controlling one minimum gap, stabilizing a coordinate basis, or sharpening a global zero-density exponent does not address the surviving worst-cell requirement.

## Push the canonical shell discrepancy toward a near-linear scale

**Linked intuition:** `MI-002-small-shell-discrepancy-can-destroy-rooted-visibility`.

The shell decomposition still reduces the rooted-mixing channel to a quantitative coefficient discrepancy. The live theorem is whether the canonical arithmetic coefficients can force near-linear discrepancy on the relevant shell family without assuming the Nyman distance estimate one is trying to prove.

## Keep recurrence order, translation resolution, coordinate conditioning, subspace geometry, target occupation and worst-cell source control separate

NB-128 buys local resolution for one common phase translation. NB-132 restores rank visibility in a concrete actual-zero regime. NB-133--NB-136 show that those facts do not give a conditioned packet representation. NB-137--NB-140 show that catastrophic raw conditioning and arbitrary two-dimensional simple-node geometry can coexist with a target-poor confluent subspace. NB-141 adds that strong global rarity of those cells does not remove a sparse exceptional sequence. NB-142 shows that such an exceptional sequence is exactly an order-log upward-oscillation event for the zero-count remainder on polynomially shrinking intervals.

The surviving discriminator is representation-invariant, local and worst-cell. A proposal must state which subspace or quotient is physically meaningful, how the actual target occupies it, what complex cluster-scale source law prevents that occupation from vanishing, and why the law applies uniformly to actual zeta zeros rather than merely on average or in a chosen chart. Numerical conditioning, average gaps, one-dimensional spacing bounds or global density are secondary unless they change the target-bearing state geometry or the local remainder-increment budget.
