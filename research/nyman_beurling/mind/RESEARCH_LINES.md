NB-118 adds an independent source equation: every finite packet of actual zeta zeros is orthogonal to every fixed finite Nyman generator space `V_M`. Above the residual target baseline `d_M^2`, this forces a target-bearing witness into `O_M(1/R)` retained cell energy. NB-119 proves that the exponent is sharp for the broader class `V_M^perp`: for each fixed `M`, a minimum-norm invisible tail repairs the perfect visible target into exact Nyman orthogonality with retained fraction `1/(kappa_M R)+O_M(1/R^2)`.

NB-120 closes the most immediate growing-section escape in a substantial mixed-tail regime. If `M=M(R)->infinity` with `M^6(1+log M)/R->0`, there are still exact controls `f_(M,R) in V_M^perp` with `Q_R f_(M,R)=e_R` and retention `(1+o(1))/(kappa_infty R)`, where `kappa_infty` is finite and positive. Increasing the number of exact Nyman equations is therefore not by itself the missing resource.

NB-121 closes the cheapest two-cutoff repair: for any fixed finite nested ladder, natural-cell projections and Nyman sections collapse algebraically to the maximal cutoff and maximal section. NB-122 sharpens that classification to the **entire growing prefix**. The same maximal `NB-120` repair satisfies `Q_r f=e_r` exactly for every `2<=r<=R`, remains orthogonal to every `V_(M_r)` with `M_r<=M(R)`, and has retained fraction `L_r/((kappa_infty+o(1))R)` uniformly across the prefix. Thus a widening ladder with `r/R->0` does not break the control; it pushes the early scales deeper to `o(1/r)` retention while preserving perfect target angle.

Ordinary nested same-source coherence is therefore exhausted as an independent resource, even when the number of cutoffs grows like `R` and the smallest-to-largest ratio tends to zero. The live theorem is sharper: **exclude actual zero-power/confluent packets from the full-prefix matched-control class, force a quantitatively stronger source-section transition outside the audited mixed-tail regime, or introduce cross-scale information that is non-nested or explicitly tail-sensitive**. The remaining source-faithful candidate is the transformation/interpolation law forced by the actual zero-power representation itself; merely adding more nested cutoffs is now classified as representation redundancy.

Sending `M` toward the full source is still not free. NB-120 gives only the sufficient regime `M^6 log M << R`; it does not classify the transition when `M` is comparable with a larger power of `R`, and `d_M->0` remains the Nyman/RH approximation problem. NB-122 only shows that every fixed generator equation is eventually satisfied along its diagonal controls, not that the full infinite source law holds at any finite cutoff. Any proposed closure must expose rather than hide these dependencies.

## Keep target baseline, zero height, continuum mass, cell retention and genuinely new source coherence separate

NB-118 is not another Gram-conditioning estimate: it combines actual-zero annihilation of Nyman generators with finite target geometry. NB-119--NB-120 supply matched controls showing exactly how far that source information can go without using the actual zero-power representation. NB-121--NB-122 complete the representation audit for nested cutoffs: one maximal repair already determines the target at every smaller natural-cell section, so even full-prefix widening does not create an independent source coordinate.

Future claims should therefore distinguish available zero rank, horizontal zero mass, Nyman-section depth, continuum norm, retained cell energy, approximation of the harmonic minimizer, and whether a proposed cross-scale law is **nonredundant under nesting**. The unresolved object is whether the actual zero-power interpolation class, a faster source regime, or genuinely non-nested/tail-sensitive multiscale information can exclude the inverse-section mode that increasingly rich nested Nyman orthogonality still allows.
