## Generalize the quadratic-tangency threshold to source-realistic local zero packets

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-target-aware-gram-geometry-must-carry-absolute-scale`, `MI-003-low-gram-spectrum-is-cheap-target-occupation-is-the-signal`, `MI-004-weighted-tail-shell-quotient-is-a-conditioned-source-skeleton`, `MI-005-persistent-forward-memory-leakage-is-a-target-near-kernel-phenomenon`, `MI-006-boundary-zeros-force-reciprocal-depth-screening`, `MI-007-aliased-radial-prediction-has-a-positive-szego-floor`, `MI-008-finite-blaschke-defects-are-silent-in-terminal-scalar-energy`, `MI-009-summable-horizontal-zero-mass-keeps-full-terminal-gram-entropy-bounded`, `MI-010-growing-depth-mobius-annulus-exposes-off-critical-blaschke-amplification`, `MI-011-canonical-deflation-cancels-raw-cauchy-crowding-volume`, `MI-012-three-state-transverse-confluence-exposes-collective-shear`, `MI-013-absolute-height-and-global-zero-counts-are-invisible-to-the-collective-shear-obstruction`, `MI-014-quadratic-horizontal-tangency-is-the-three-state-conditioning-threshold`.

NB-092--NB-097 remove fixed-finite determinant loss, raw Cauchy crowding and pairwise conditioning as the main issue. NB-098 gives a transverse three-state family with unit pivots and good pairs but `epsilon^-1` canonical shear and `epsilon^-4` full conditioning blow-up. NB-099 then proves that absolute height, zeta quartet symmetry, simplicity and arbitrarily sparse global zero counts do not exclude that local geometry.

NB-100 turns the remaining phrase “local zero geometry” into an exact scale law on the minimal symmetric bad family. For states `a+i epsilon`, `a+delta`, `a-i epsilon`, the full canonical condition number is comparable to `(1+L_a)^4`, where

`L_a(delta,epsilon)=|a delta-epsilon^2|/(delta^2+epsilon^2)`.

Along a true collision, bounded collective conditioning is equivalent to `delta=O(epsilon^2)`. First-order transverse bending is therefore genuinely singular; quadratic horizontal tangency is the threshold on this slice.

The live source theorem is now sharper. Either derive a comparable second-order local restriction for actual off-critical zeta-zero triples, extend the invariant to fully asymmetric near-confluent packets and test whether zeta structure enforces it, or change the representation/target conversion so that dimension-free canonical conditioning is unnecessary. Global zero-density bookkeeping and absolute height have already been shown to be the wrong variables for this obstruction.

A separate route remains available: convert the sharp fixed-finite repair directly into a target-relevant Nyman obstruction before taking a growing-divisor limit.

## Keep symmetric tangency, general confluence, actual zero geometry and target conversion separate

NB-100 classifies one exact symmetric three-state slice; it does not prove actual zeta zeros realize that slice or that `delta=O(epsilon^2)` is the coordinate-free condition for an arbitrary triple. A fully asymmetric local invariant may refine the threshold. Genuine repeated zeros require derivative/Jordan states rather than three distinct colliding kernels. And even a bad local canonical condition number matters for RH only if the destination argument truly requires uniform conditioning of this representation.

The next useful step should therefore either generalize the exact `L_a` law to a source-realistic local packet, prove a zeta-specific second-order restriction, or bypass the conditioning requirement through a target-aware conversion. Re-optimizing pairwise separation, height, or global zero counts cannot address the now-explicit collective scale.
