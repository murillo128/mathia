## Distinguish the actual Nyman Gram geometry from well-conditioned multiplicative-tree stable-tail controls

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-target-aware-gram-geometry-must-carry-absolute-scale`, `MI-003-low-gram-spectrum-is-cheap-target-occupation-is-the-signal`, `MI-004-weighted-tail-shell-quotient-is-a-conditioned-source-skeleton`, `MI-005-persistent-forward-memory-leakage-is-a-target-near-kernel-phenomenon`.

NB-066--NB-067 reduce a persistent late quotient to a global continuation mode and expose it through finite normalized Schur certificates. NB-068 showed that zero one-cell memory and finite causal bandwidth alone are insufficient. NB-069--NB-070 then impose the exact integer-dilation source law and show that every bounded nonorthogonal branch-compatible obstruction must carry persistent far Gram mass; rapidly decaying additive tails are impossible.

NB-071 proves that this nonlocal escape is real rather than merely formal. The explicit source `D_j=(I-rho V_m)e_j`, `rho>1`, obeys the same exact block-branching law and reproduces the same complete finite-window local flag as the flat control, while its Gram operator remains uniformly Riesz-conditioned. Nevertheless it has an infinite-dimensional stable tail, exponentially diverging causal continuation constants, and diverging finite Schur certificates. The price is a multiplicative parent-child Gram tree with fixed signed correlations at unbounded additive distance.

Thus exact branching, excellent global Gram conditioning, zero one-cell memory, and every finite local trace datum still do **not** determine the stable tail. The missing theorem must use a source property not reproduced by this tree control: a quantitative feature of the actual Nyman Gram entries, their signs/phases across multiplicative descendants, a causal trace norm tied to arithmetic rather than global Riesz conditioning, or another exact covariance identity strong enough to rule out the explicit stable-tail architecture.

## Treat local memory, branching covariance, far signed structure, global Gram conditioning, causal continuation cost, and finite certification separately

NB-071 sharpens the separation. A globally well-conditioned source can still have exponentially ill-conditioned continuation from a finite causal trace. Persistent far mass is not merely an adversarial possibility: a branch-compatible control realizes it with coherent signed multiplicative-tree couplings and a nonzero stable tail. Future progress must discriminate the canonical arithmetic Gram geometry from that matched control rather than strengthen generic locality or condition-number assumptions.
