## Resolve the one-Poisson-width interlaced signed-sampler regime

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-target-aware-gram-geometry-must-carry-absolute-scale` through `MI-037-high-ordinate-zeta-bounds-cap-the-boundary-approach-tariff`.

NB-148--NB-160 show that zero-mass signed exterior sampling removes the universal Hadamard logarithm only by exporting sign debt. Mesoscopic spreading, finite atomic repair and even arbitrary signed Borel samplers with Jordan supports separated by many Poisson widths still force an adverse `Omega(log G)` zero reservoir under bounded effective conditioning.

NB-161--NB-164 isolate the opposite, finely interlaced regime. If `ell_G` is mean optimal Jordan transport and `C_G` is total-variation conditioning, the scale-invariant first-order currency is

`Theta_G=C_G ell_G/a_G`.

Sub-Poisson transport suppresses target and signed Euler responses together. Fixed local moments do not create another currency, and growing moment order at bounded Poisson width pays an exponential target-capacity/conditioning bill.

NB-165 then inserts the actual high-ordinate zeta source. The pointwise bound for `zeta'/zeta` caps the absolute conservation tariff at

`C_G E_G`,  where  `E_G=min{a_G^(-1), log G/log log G}`.

Hence inverse-gap amplification saturates once `a_G << log log G/log G`.

NB-166 combines this source theorem with Jordan transport **before taking total variation**. Cauchy differentiation of the high-ordinate bound gives a vertical Lipschitz scale `E_G/a_G`; pairing it with the optimal Jordan coupling replaces `C_G` by `Theta_G` throughout the signed conservation budget:

`sum_rho Q_G(rho)=O(q_G Theta_G E_G)`.

For a logarithmic target packet, essentially full adverse debt is therefore forced whenever `Theta_G E_G=o(log G)`, regardless of how large total variation itself becomes. In the extreme boundary layer this reduces to `Theta_G=o(log log G)`. The adverse debt also localizes within ordinate radius `O(1+sqrt(a_G Theta_G))`, and the zero-free region converts it into a divergent adverse-zero population.

This changes the live compact escape. **Large conditioning is not itself a resource when the two Jordan signs are sufficiently interlaced; the source sees the transported signed measure through `Theta_G`, not through `C_G`.** The remaining question is whether one-Poisson-width samplers can exploit cancellation in the actual high-ordinate values beyond the available Lipschitz/pointwise theorem while retaining target gain, or whether a genuinely nonlocal geometry can do so with its support and transport costs controlled. Boundary motion, total-variation inflation and formal moment cancellation alone are no longer escapes.

## Keep the source and destination currencies separate

A future sampler must expose target charge, total variation, boundary gap, Jordan transport length, `Theta_G`, local moment order/normalized moment size, support growth, the actual high-ordinate logarithmic-derivative input, localization radius and adverse-zero population. `C_G` remains relevant to raw normalization, but NB-166 shows it need not govern conservation once signed transport is used before absolute values.
