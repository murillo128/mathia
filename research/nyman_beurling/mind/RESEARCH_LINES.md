## Derive the arithmetic finite-prediction rate instead of treating outerness as sufficient

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-target-aware-gram-geometry-must-carry-absolute-scale`, `MI-003-low-gram-spectrum-is-cheap-target-occupation-is-the-signal`, `MI-004-weighted-tail-shell-quotient-is-a-conditioned-source-skeleton`, `MI-005-persistent-forward-memory-leakage-is-a-target-near-kernel-phenomenon`.

NB-066--NB-079 reduce persistent continuation to the uncancelled charge `J` and cancel the common coarse-future prediction mode. NB-080 shows that fixed relative future depth has an extensive benign false positive, while NB-081 makes future screening exact and calibrates two explicit outer controls.

NB-082 replaces that example-specific conditioning language by one exact scalar invariant for every stationary branch filter `psi(V_m)`: the normalized finite prediction excess `a_(psi,L)`. At depth `L`, the global prediction-volume charge is comparable to `R a_(psi,L)` once `L->infinity`. Outerness is exactly the qualitative statement `a_(psi,L)->0`; bounded screening requires the much stronger quantitative scale `a_(psi,L)=O(1/R)`, and vanishing requires `o(1/R)`. The logarithmic-depth and order-`R` examples of NB-081 are simply different decay rates of this same quantity.

The live arithmetic theorem is therefore precise. Identify the corresponding nonstationary finite-prediction error for the actual Nyman innovations and prove that an affordable future horizon drives it below the `1/R` threshold, or construct a stable-mode-sensitive certificate that does not pay this prediction boundary layer. Qualitative outerness, zero stable tail, exact branching, or good global Gram conditioning alone do not determine the needed horizon.

## Keep stable modes, finite prediction rate and future cost separate

A nonzero stable tail still forces divergence for every finite horizon schedule, and boundedness on one unbounded schedule still excludes it. NB-082 shows that the converse cost is controlled by the decay of the finite prediction excess, not by a binary outer/non-outer label. The actual arithmetic source may have a very different rate from any stationary matched control, so transferring the stationary theorem requires an explicit source dictionary rather than analogy.
