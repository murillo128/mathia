\[

\[

Large-ratio raw columns can be small, and every sufficiently late individual shifted block can be invisible to the canonical residual. Neither statement controls the collective span. NB-060 further separates **existence** of finite-time defects from **abundance**: `ker B_2` decides the former, while the closed union of `ker B_2^k` decides the canonical tail.
