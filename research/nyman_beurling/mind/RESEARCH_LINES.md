## Quotient unresolved microscopic dipoles before pricing signed carrier cancellation

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-062-the-critical-ford-mark-is-a-growing-moment-observable`, `MI-063-demodulated-ford-energy-is-bandlimited-at-the-window-scale`, `MI-064-signed-scalar-coherence-shrinks-only-by-paying-total-variation`, `MI-065-signed-carrier-cost-must-be-measured-after-quotienting-unresolved-microscopic-dipoles`.

NB-245--NB-250 fix the representation and order of operations. The Ford-scaled Cauchy pole must be Hardy-projected through positive Mellin frequencies before Hilbert-space squaring; this exactly recovers the finite depth-marked zero current. The remaining second moment is a positive carrier-band form factor with natural width `Theta(H)=Theta(X/J)`.

NB-251 shows that independently available carrier width `W>>H` disperses the standard matched packet. NB-252 closes literal single-shell dilation because widening the shell also replaces `J` by `J/B_J`. NB-253 closes the canonical equal-weight translated-shell comb because its Dirichlet factor creates a `1/W` coherence peak and replaces the effective population by `R/W`. NB-255 extends the worst-case coherence estimate to signed or complex scalar mixtures. After Euler normalization, coefficient total variation `V` guarantees an order-one coherent disk of radius `c/(WV)`, so the corresponding compatibility population is `R/(WV)`. This is a valid universal lower bound from only span and variation.

NB-256 shows that raw `V` is not a physical conditioning cost. Adding a microscopic dipole `N delta_0-N delta_(H/N^2)` makes `V` diverge while changing both the synthesized smooth source and the carrier transform by `o(1)` at the relevant `1/W` resolution. The same construction leaves the first offset moment `M_1(lambda)=int |xi|d|lambda|(xi)` at `Theta(W)`, exposing the missing amplitude-times-separation information. Thus forcing `V \gtrsim R/W` does not by itself demonstrate either paid source cost or purchased carrier cancellation.

The live scalar question is now sharper: what **resolution-sensitive coefficient geometry** survives smooth-shell synthesis strongly enough that shrinking the actual Ford coherence window forces a comparable source/destination cost? Two concrete categories remain open without being promoted to conclusions: a transport/flat-type seminorm tied to shell resolution, or a stable carrier dictionary with center separation comparable to `H` on which the synthesis map is coercive. Any candidate must also survive the arithmetic error ledger and Hardy/Nyman projection; raw total variation has already failed before projection.

## Keep carrier coherence, source synthesis and the actual zero theorem separate

NB-250 gives a sufficient full-line carrier-energy route, not an equivalence. NB-251--NB-256 use matched packets and controls as adversarial compatibility tests rather than asserting actual zeta-zero configurations. NB-255 supplies a worst-case coherence radius from `W` and `V`; NB-256 proves that the bound can be arbitrarily non-sharp because coefficient representations with large unresolved dipoles can synthesize essentially the same source and carrier.

A future signed-scalar argument must therefore state the Euler normalization, carrier span, working resolution, coefficient quotient/seminorm or dictionary separation, smooth-shell synthesis map and destination projection before crediting either cancellation or conditioning cost. Macroscopic support and large signed variation are not independent bandwidth currencies. The missing object is a scale-sensitive source norm or restricted synthesis theorem that distinguishes carrier changes visible at Ford resolution from coefficient mass that cancels below that resolution.
