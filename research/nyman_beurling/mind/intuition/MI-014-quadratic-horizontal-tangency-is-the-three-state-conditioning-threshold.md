# MI-014 — Quadratic horizontal tangency is the three-state conditioning threshold

**Evidence level:** exact on the symmetric canonical three-state slice of [NB-100](../../findings/NB-100-quadratic-horizontal-tangency-is-the-local-three-state-shear-threshold.md). No source theorem for actual zeta-zero triples or fully asymmetric confluence is claimed.

After canonical Blaschke deflation, pairwise crowding is not the decisive variable. For the local packet

`lambda_1=a+i epsilon`, `lambda_2=a+delta`, `lambda_3=a-i epsilon`,

NB-100 isolates the collective bending parameter

`L_a(delta,epsilon)=|a delta-epsilon^2|/(delta^2+epsilon^2)`

and proves

`kappa_2(C_(epsilon,delta)) asy_a (1+L_a)^4`.

The divergent scale sits in a genuinely three-state triangular coefficient; the pairwise canonical interaction stays bounded. This makes the geometry much sharper than “crowded zeros are ill-conditioned.” What matters is whether the horizontal displacement of the middle state is first-order or second-order relative to the vertical collision scale.

Along any true collision, bounded conditioning is equivalent to

`delta(epsilon)=O(epsilon^2)`.

Thus a transverse path `delta=c epsilon` reproduces the quartic blow-up of NB-098, while every parabolic path `delta=k epsilon^2` stays bounded. The threshold is **quadratic horizontal tangency**, not exact vertical alignment.

Combined with NB-099, this prices the missing source information. Absolute height is gauge, quartet completion and simplicity do not help, and global zero-count sparsity can coexist with the bad synthetic packet. A source-specific rescue of this canonical representation therefore needs a local second-order law coupling horizontal displacement to neighboring ordinate gaps, or a different representation/target conversion that avoids uniform three-state conditioning.

The next mathematical question is whether the scalar `L_a` is the symmetric trace of a coordinate-free invariant for a general near-confluent triple. Such an invariant should distinguish harmless second-order bending from first-order transverse shear and be expressible in variables that actual zeta-zero geometry could constrain. If no such source law is available, that failure is evidence for changing the representation rather than strengthening global counting.

**Boundary.** The constants are local for fixed `a>0`; the result does not cover a simultaneous `a->0` limit. A fully asymmetric triple may have a different normal form, and a genuine repeated zero requires derivative/Jordan states. The theorem classifies conditioning of the canonical state system, not the Nyman target error itself.
