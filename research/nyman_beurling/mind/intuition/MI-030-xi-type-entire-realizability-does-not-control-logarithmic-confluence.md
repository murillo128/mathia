# MI-030 — Xi-type entire realizability does not control logarithmic confluence

**Evidence level:** exact source-realizability and method-boundary statement from [NB-143](../../findings/NB-143-xi-type-entire-realizability-still-permits-sparse-logarithmic-confluence-packets.md), building on the target-poor confluence geometry of NB-137--NB-142 and Hamburger's classical converse-theorem boundary cited in the canonical sources.

NB-140--NB-142 isolate a local obstruction: logarithmic-rank simple-zero packets in polynomially shrinking complex cells can have vanishing normalized projection on the Nyman target, and such a packet costs an order-`log G` positive short increment of the zero-count remainder. The remaining question was whether this matched divisor was an artifact of prescribing zeros without the analytic structure of `xi`.

NB-143 removes that escape. It constructs one entire function `Xi_*` of order exactly one with the standard reflection and conjugation symmetries, standard critical-line bulk count, and infinitely many sparse right-half logarithmic confluence packets. After the usual Gamma completion is undone, the associated `Z_*` has the zeta pole/trivial-zero pattern and satisfies the exact standard zeta functional equation. The divisor can simultaneously respect the explicit zero-count, imported zero-density and zero-free envelopes used by the line.

Those analytic constraints still permit the same target-poor packet geometry. Therefore the missing rigidity is not “be the zero divisor of some order-one xi-type entire function satisfying the functional equation.” Functional-equation symmetry and coarse zero laws do not supply the worst-cell occupancy/target-bearing estimate consumed by the Nyman argument.

The first classical boundary exposed by this construction is arithmetic half-plane structure. If the same `Z_*` also admitted an ordinary Dirichlet series absolutely convergent in `Re s>1` under Hamburger's hypotheses, the converse theorem would force it to be `zeta`; the synthetic control was chosen not to be zeta. This identifies a source distinction, not yet the quantitative theorem needed at the endpoint.

The reusable lesson is that **analytic realizability of the zero geometry and arithmetic realizability of the source are different gates**. A closing argument must extract from the actual Dirichlet-series/Euler-product source a local consequence that the xi-type matched control lacks—such as the sublogarithmic positive short-increment law already isolated by NB-142—or bypass zero occupancy with a direct target-projection lower bound.

**Boundary.** `Xi_*` and `Z_*` are matched controls, not alternative Nyman sources, and their synthetic zeros are not asserted to be Nyman orthogonality zeros. NB-143 does not prove a new property of the actual zeta zeros and Hamburger's theorem does not by itself provide the required local increment bound. The result only removes generic entire-function realizability as an explanation for why the obstruction might be impossible.
