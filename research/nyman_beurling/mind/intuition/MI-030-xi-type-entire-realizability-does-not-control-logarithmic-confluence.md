# MI-030 — Xi-type entire realizability does not control logarithmic confluence

**Evidence level:** exact source-realizability and method-boundary statement from [NB-143](../../findings/NB-143-xi-type-entire-realizability-still-permits-sparse-logarithmic-confluence-packets.md), now quantitatively separated from the actual zeta source by [NB-144](../../findings/NB-144-euler-product-poisson-mass-forces-logarithmic-confluence-away-from-the-one-line.md), building on the target-poor confluence geometry of NB-137--NB-142.

NB-140--NB-142 isolate a local obstruction: logarithmic-rank simple-zero packets in polynomially shrinking complex cells can have vanishing normalized projection on the Nyman target, and such a packet costs an order-`log G` positive short increment of the zero-count remainder. NB-143 then removes the escape that this geometry is impossible merely because it was prescribed synthetically.

It constructs one entire function `Xi_*` of order exactly one with the standard reflection and conjugation symmetries, standard critical-line bulk count, and infinitely many sparse right-half logarithmic confluence packets. After the usual Gamma completion is undone, the associated `Z_*` has the zeta pole/trivial-zero pattern and satisfies the exact standard zeta functional equation. The divisor can simultaneously respect the explicit zero-count, imported zero-density and zero-free envelopes used by the line.

NB-144 now shows that arithmetic realizability is not only a qualitative Hamburger boundary. The actual Euler product on `Re s>1` supplies a positive Poisson-mass budget for zeros. For suitable fixed right-half cells it quantitatively excludes logarithmic packet parameters that the NB-143 xi-type control realizes, and for cells whose center approaches `Re s=1` it forces the packet rank to be `o(log G)`.

Thus the reusable lesson has become two-sided: **analytic realizability of the zero geometry and arithmetic realizability of the source are different gates, and the difference can already be measured locally.** Functional-equation symmetry and coarse zero laws do not supply the needed target-bearing estimate; the Euler product does impose an additional occupancy tax. The remaining fixed-interior packet regime shows that even this source-specific law is weaker than the final Nyman requirement.

A closing argument must therefore build on the arithmetic source distinction rather than merely demand more xi-type structure. It may sharpen the one-point Poisson budget, combine several source-side observables, or bypass occupancy by proving that the actual packets still allowed by the budget necessarily have nontrivial Nyman-target projection.

**Boundary.** `Xi_*` and `Z_*` are matched controls, not alternative Nyman sources, and their synthetic zeros are not asserted to be Nyman orthogonality zeros. NB-144 does not rule out logarithmic-rank confluence at every fixed interior real part and does not itself control the Nyman target. The separation is a local source-realizability theorem, not a solution of the endpoint.
