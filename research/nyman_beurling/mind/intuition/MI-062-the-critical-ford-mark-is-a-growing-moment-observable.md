# MI-062 — The critical Ford mark is an R-normalized resonant current whose detector must resolve intra-cluster phase

**Evidence level:** exact positive reduction plus matched-control, classical logarithmic-derivative boundary and source-specific detector audit from [NB-234](../../findings/NB-234-growing-depth-moment-hierarchy-reconstructs-critical-ford-mark.md)--[NB-238](../../findings/NB-238-half-isolated-zero-theory-coarse-grains-the-carrier-locked-ford-packet.md), building on NB-230--NB-233.

NB-234 shows that on every fixed critical layer the residue can be reconstructed from profiled depth--phase moments with an order `K(Q)` that grows slowly with source capacity, while every predetermined fixed `K` remains blind in a matched spectral control. The nonlinear exponential depth mark is therefore not a fixed-moment statistic.

NB-235 identifies what that hierarchy approximates in classical zeta language. The critical coefficient is a microscopic Landau zero sum with an additional horizontal Ford-depth cutoff. The shell profile has zero total integral, so the ordinary Landau--Gonek prime-power diagonal disappears; black-box smoothing of the global cumulative formula pays the full cumulative remainder and does not exploit the microscopic window.

NB-236 changes representation again. For the depth-localized holomorphic carrier, Poincare--Lelong gives the weighted zero coefficient as a pairing with `log|zeta|` whose two transition-strip kernel has bounded `L^1` mass. NB-237 exposes the same coefficient at first order: distributional logarithmic-derivative calculus gives a kernel of total mass `O(1/R)`, so in Ford coordinates the unresolved source observable is a microlocal signed pairing with `R^(-1) zeta'(s)/zeta(s)`.

The factor `1/R` is the correct normalization, not an automatic gain. The matched critical packets of NB-231 have pole spacing `Theta(1/R)` and carrier phases locked. Their `Theta(J)` residues can therefore add coherently to order one despite the shrinking first-order tariff. Riemann--Lebesgue or integration-by-parts intuition fails because the source singularities themselves move on the carrier lattice scale.

NB-238 adds a resolution gate that generic “vertical zero information” does not satisfy automatically. The published Maynard--Pratt half-isolated-zero detector clusters same-line zeros whenever consecutive gaps are at most `(log T)^3`. In the conservative matched Ford regime the full carrier-locked packet has spacing `Theta(1/R)` and total diameter `o(1)`, so all `Theta(J)` resonant poles fall inside one such clump. The detector can retain only `O(1)` half-isolated representatives even though the Ford current sums all poles coherently.

The reusable audit therefore has five stages. Determine the source resolution needed to identify the mark; price localization; expose the natural normalized first-order current; test moving-source resonance at that normalization; then compare the **granularity of any source-specific detector** with the carrier period. A theorem can genuinely use vertical zero structure and still be irrelevant if its terminal observable coarse-grains the whole resonant packet before the Ford phase is evaluated.

A source theorem adequate for the present frontier must control cluster-internal geometry at approximately `1/R` vertical resolution, jointly with Ford depth and carrier phase, or prove an equivalent deterministic cancellation statement for the normalized logarithmic derivative. Counting clusters, isolated representatives or global exceptional populations is insufficient unless the theorem also controls the internal occupancy/phase that the current consumes.

**Boundary.** NB-236--NB-237 are exact representation/tariff results, not decay theorems. NB-238 is a scale audit of the published half-isolated/clump output, not a theorem that Maynard--Pratt machinery cannot be refined to microscopic scale. The resonant packet is an admissible adversarial control, not a claim about actual zeta zeros. No new bound for the optimal Nyman--Beurling distance or bridge from an optimal approximant to vanishing of this coefficient is proved.
