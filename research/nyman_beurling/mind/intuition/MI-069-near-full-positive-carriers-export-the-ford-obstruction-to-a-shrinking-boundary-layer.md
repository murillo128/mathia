# MI-069 — Near-full positive carriers export the Ford obstruction to a shrinking boundary layer

**Evidence level:** proven within the `H`-resolved positive scalar carrier model by [NB-261](../../findings/NB-261-near-full-positive-carriers-suppress-fixed-ford-depths-but-export-the-obstruction-to-a-shrinking-boundary-layer.md), sharpening the near-full-rank necessity of NB-260. The replacement zero packet is a matched compatibility control against the current zeta-information ledger, not a claim that actual zeta zeros realize it.

Let `A_J=Theta(log J)` and `N_J=J/A_J`. Choose `1<<m<<A_J` and `K=floor(J/m)`, so `K=N_J^(1+o(1))` while `K=o(J)`. The equal positive `H`-grid carrier

`C_J(w)=K^(-1) sum_(j=0)^(K-1) exp(i j w/J)`

has vanishing shell-Hilbert cost and, uniformly across the full hard ordinate window, satisfies

`sup_(|x|<=BJ, s_0<=s<=1) |C_J(x+i s A_J)| -> 0`

for every fixed `s_0>0`. Thus the `Theta(J)` Ford width does not restore coercivity on any depth rectangle whose lower edge is bounded away from the Euler anchor. NB-260's near-full effective rank is essentially sufficient at exponent level for that fixed-depth task.

The obstruction moves rather than disappears. At physical depth `u=X(1-beta)`, the same positive average obeys `C_J(iu/Y)=1+o(1)` whenever `u=o(m)`. More sharply, at the matched depth

`u=log m + O(1) = log(J/K)+O(1)`,

NB-261 constructs a Bellotti-compatible carrier-locked packet with order-one signed Hardy residue. The live scalar boundary therefore follows the carrier span toward the Euler edge as the rank increases.

The durable correction is that **a fixed two-dimensional destination rectangle is not the coercive object**. A closing theorem must control a shrinking Ford boundary layer down to depth `log(J/K)` (or another scale proved necessary), couple the Euler anchor to the interior, or leave the positive resolved scalar carrier category.

**Boundary.** The conclusion is architecture-specific. The carrier is positive, one-sided, `H`-resolved and scalar; vector-valued carriers, unresolved dictionaries, signed constructions with different cost, or a different legal Hardy/Nyman quantity remain outside it. The matched packet is only an adversarial control permitted by the current zero-free/counting inputs.
