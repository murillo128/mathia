# MI-084 — Multiplicative-band coercivity can be pure dilation-baseline repair

**Evidence level:** exact comparison of NB-294--NB-295 with the ambient Hardy-kernel countermodel [NB-296](../../findings/NB-296-ambient-hardy-kernel-saturation-realizes-full-off-critical-band-coercivity-with-zero-ordinary-enrichment.md).

NB-294 and NB-295 prove genuine coercivity and determinant growth for the multiplicative quotient band when the enlarged canonical section is compared with the old **dilated** section. NB-296 shows that this strength is not by itself evidence of ordinary Nyman enrichment. In the ambient Hardy RKHS generated by the full evaluation-kernel space, the undilated first-free interpolation problem is already saturated: the ordinary interpolation cost is constant and the ordinary finite-section decrement is exactly zero.

Nevertheless the dilated-old versus enlarged-section comparison remains fully active in that model. Its lower Gram is `A=DGD*`, its enlarged Gram is `G`, and the band increment `Gamma=G-DGD*` is positive with the same multiplicative cocycle. The determinant identity realizes the full mandatory factor `m^(2H_Z)`, and the off-critical relative gain can tend uniformly to one even though adding ordinary source directions produces no improvement whatsoever.

This makes the baseline issue structural rather than cosmetic. Multiplicative-band coercivity measures how much enlargement repairs a dilation-induced synthesis penalty. To become evidence for the Nyman approximation problem, that gain must survive subtraction of an ambient dilation-only baseline or be connected by a theorem to a decrement of the ordinary undilated distance. Large spectral floor, determinant growth and even target-uniform relative gain do not supply that bridge automatically.

The reusable audit is to separate **relative repair of a distorted comparator** from **new information in the target approximation problem**. A carrier may be perfectly source-geometric and strongly coercive while all of its gain is spent undoing the comparator transformation rather than enriching the original approximation space.

**Boundary.** NB-296 is an ambient Hardy-kernel countermodel, not a proof that the actual finite Ford/Nyman packet has zero ordinary enrichment. It does not invalidate NB-294 or NB-295 as exact statements about the multiplicative quotient band. It shows that any RH-relevant use of those statements needs an additional baseline-removal or ordinary-distance bridge.
