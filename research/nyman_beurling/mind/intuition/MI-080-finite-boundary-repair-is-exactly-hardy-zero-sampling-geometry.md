# MI-080 — Finite boundary repair is exactly Hardy zero-sampling geometry

**Evidence level:** exact finite-boundary and reproducing-kernel synthesis from [NB-279](../../findings/NB-279-finite-boundary-repair-collapses-to-hardy-zero-sampling.md), sharpened by the exact Burnol/Blaschke identification in [NB-280](../../findings/NB-280-canonical-zero-sampling-inverse-gram-is-exactly-the-finite-burnol-blaschke-defect.md).

For every finite legal Nyman source

`N[P](s)=zeta(s)/s (P(s)-P(1))`

with residual `r_P(s)=1/s-N[P](s)`, every nonzero zeta zero `rho` forces the exact value `r_P(rho)=1/rho`. A finite contour weighted by `s zeta'/zeta` can therefore see the enclosed divisor without making the finite source singular: the poles live in the dual functional, while the residual remains holomorphic.

Pricing this identity in `H^2(C_(1/2))` gives the zero-evaluation Gram

`G_(jk)=1/(rho_j+conj(rho_k)-1)`

and the canonical data vector `v_j=1/rho_j`. NB-279 yields the exact interpolation lower bound

`||r_P||_(H^2)^2 >= v*G^(-1)v`.

NB-280 resolves the apparent conditioning frontier. The vector `v` is not a generic prescribed direction: it is exactly the sample vector of the fixed Hardy kernel `k_1(s)=1/s`. Hence

`v*G^(-1)v = ||P_(K_Z) k_1||^2 = 1-|B_Z(1)|^2`

for the finite model space generated by the zero kernels. Equivalently,

`v*G^(-1)v = 1 - product_(rho in Z) (1-(2 Re(rho)-1)/|rho|^2)`.

Thus the canonical inverse-Gram quantity is exactly the finite Burnol/Blaschke target defect already present earlier in the line. Pairwise zero spacing, Cauchy-Gram condition number and Ford phase alignment do not provide an additional coercive currency for this data vector: the matrix can become ill-conditioned while the simultaneous motion of `v` cancels that instability in the quadratic form.

This also closes the high-zero point-sampling route quantitatively. NB-116 already proves that every finite packet of actual right-half zeta zeros above height `G` has Burnol defect `O(1/G)`, so the same bound holds for `v*G^(-1)v`. The matched Ford packets of NB-231 make the separation even sharper: they can retain order-one Ford residue while their canonical Hardy sampling energy tends to zero.

The legal finite boundary route is therefore classicalized more strongly than NB-279 alone showed. Zero counting, contour residues and generic Gram conditioning are not independent resources for the canonical point-value channel. A source-native escape must leave this finite point-evaluation projection—for example through a genuinely finite arithmetic-section interaction, higher jets at multiple zeros, or another nonlinear/boundary mechanism whose information is not equivalent to the same Blaschke defect.

**Boundary.** The exact collapse is specific to the canonical target data `v_j=1/rho_j` at distinct right-half-plane zero locations. Generic interpolation vectors need not satisfy it, and higher derivative constraints at multiple zeros require confluent/model-space jets rather than repeated point values. NB-280 does not rule out those channels or prove a new Nyman lower bound beyond the already-controlled finite Burnol defect.
