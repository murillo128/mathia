# MI-003 — Adjacent low-energy structure is universal, but the full adjacent family is the whole Nyman closure

**Evidence level:** exact Rayleigh, coefficient-separation, quotient, and telescoping-closure controls through NB-029

## Core intuition

Small Gram energy remains cheap and target-poor, but the latest evidence prevents a naive response: the adjacent differences that generate the obvious nuisance modes cannot be removed wholesale. Their closed Hilbert span is exactly the full canonical Nyman approximation space.

The only meaningful separator is therefore **energy- and target-aware**. One must distinguish the canonical coefficient vector from the low-Rayleigh portion of adjacent cancellation without quotienting the complete adjacent family that is itself capable of approximating the target.

## Strongest justified principle

NB-024 forces the normalized canonical coefficient vector into a low-Rayleigh regime when the Nyman approximation is good. NB-025--NB-026 construct many universal adjacent-cancellation directions with even cheaper energy and negligible target loading, so low spectrum alone is not arithmetic.

NB-027 shows that Möbius locking nevertheless produces real coefficient geometry: on a growing shell, the canonical vector has logarithmic normalized mass transverse to at least one of two disjoint adjacent matchings. NB-028 then warns that enlarging the nuisance space to all overlapping adjacent coefficient differences destroys that separation and leaves only a one-dimensional endpoint/Mertens scalar.

NB-029 closes the topological loophole completely. Since `||g_M||->0`, telescoping gives `g_n=lim_M sum_{k=n}^{M-1}(g_k-g_{k+1})`; hence the closed span of all adjacent differences equals the full Nyman closure. It also gives finite-section transfer with only an endpoint mode loss. Thus the complete adjacent family is not disposable redundancy even though many of its individual/local combinations are low-Rayleigh and target-poor.

## Program consequence

Define the nuisance sector spectrally, not topologically: for example by a low-Rayleigh threshold, a Gram spectral projector generated by local adjacent modes, or an equivalent energy-constrained closure. Then measure the canonical target-selected vector's projection/angle relative to that restricted sector in the normalization controlling the Nyman distance.

Any proposed quotient must first prove that it preserves the target-bearing closure; NB-029 makes this a nonnegotiable gate.

## Counterevidence / boundary

NB-029 does not say that every adjacent-difference combination is low-Rayleigh, nor that the low-energy adjacent sector is spectrally complete. A carefully energy-restricted subspace may still be genuinely target-poor and leave a distinguished canonical component. NB-027's coefficient separation is not by itself a Gram spectral theorem.

## Epistemic status

**Exact boundary: local adjacent cancellation supplies universal low energy, but the unrestricted adjacent family spans the entire Nyman closure; the remaining signal must live in target-selected spectral occupation rather than an adjacent-difference quotient.**

## Falsification criterion

Prove that the low-Rayleigh adjacent sector already exhausts the target-bearing Nyman closure at the relevant scale, or produce a target-independent spectral statistic that determines the Nyman distance despite the universal target-poor modes.
