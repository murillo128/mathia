# MI-082 — First-free jet cost splits into a reduced Burnol floor and restricted synthesis excess

**Evidence level:** exact Hardy-space factorization and finite-section cost decomposition from [NB-283](../../findings/NB-283-first-free-jet-neutralization-factors-exactly-through-reduced-zero-sampling.md), resolving the ambient-conditioning part left open by MI-081.

For a finite packet of distinct zeta-zero sites `rho_j` with actual multiplicities `m_j`, every legal Nyman source term `F_P` contains the full multiplicity Blaschke divisor `B_Z`. Writing

`F_P=B_Z h_P`

preserves the `H^2` norm. NB-283 proves that neutralizing the first source-dependent residual jet is then equivalent to the ordinary value constraints

`h_P(rho_j)=conj(B_Z(1))/rho_j`.

Multiplicity has disappeared from the interpolation geometry after the forced divisor is removed. The ambient minimum is exactly

`C_amb(Z)=|B_Z(1)|^2 (1-|B_red(1)|^2)`,

where `B_red` keeps each distinct zero once. Thus the unrestricted Hardy cost is only a scalar multiple of the reduced finite Burnol defect already identified by zero sampling; first-free jets do not create a new ambient coercive scale.

The genuinely source-native quantity is the excess above that floor. If `h_*` is the reduced model-space minimizer, every feasible finite Nyman source satisfies the exact Pythagorean identity

`||F_P||_2^2 = C_amb(Z) + ||h_P-h_*||_2^2`.

For a cutoff `M`, the optimal restricted cost therefore splits as

`C_M(Z)=C_amb(Z)+E_M(Z)`,

with `E_M(Z)>=0` equal to the squared distance from `h_*` to the affine quotient family generated by legal finite Dirichlet sources satisfying the first-free constraints. NB-282 already guarantees feasibility at an explicit sparse cutoff with `log M=O(N log N)`; NB-283 shows that any stronger obstruction must now be a lower bound for this **restricted synthesis excess**, not for the ambient zero-sampling problem.

This removes a possible false route from MI-081. Bad conditioning of the zero-only interpolation Gram cannot by itself supply the missing source obstruction, because the ambient problem factors exactly through the same reduced Burnol geometry. The only remaining linear first-free-jet leverage is the mismatch between the arithmetic finite-section source family and the ambient minimizer, measured in the actual Nyman/Hardy norm.

**Boundary.** NB-283 does not lower-bound `E_M(Z)`, does not show that it stays positive or large in a growing-zero regime, and does not classify higher free jets or nonlinear/global boundary observables. It also assumes only a hypothetical finite packet in the right half-plane and does not assert that such off-line zeros exist. The advance is the exact separation of universal zero geometry from the restricted arithmetic synthesis cost.
