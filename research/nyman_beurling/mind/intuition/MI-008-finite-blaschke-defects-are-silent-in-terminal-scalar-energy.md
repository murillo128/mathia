# MI-008 — Summable horizontal Blaschke mass is silent in the terminal scalar energy channel

**Evidence level:** exact for the dyadic terminal scalar distortion through NB-089, conditional in the infinite-product statement on finite total horizontal off-critical mass `A=sum_(Re rho>1/2)(Re rho-1/2)`. NB-088 gives the raw visible-norm identity and finite-product suppression; NB-089 upgrades the same finite-horizon mechanism uniformly to the full infinite product under that summability hypothesis. No conclusion is made about the complete terminal Gram entropy or RH.

NB-087 extracts from q-adic branching a compulsory scalar sibling-energy distortion. In the dyadic case NB-088 restores the common off-critical Blaschke factor and finds that the corresponding raw zeta innovation has the elementary terminal norm

`||J_X \widetilde D_j||^2 = 1 - 1/X`

whenever `j+1<=X<=2j`. All arithmetic deformation of the deflated visible norm is therefore carried by one nonnegative causal delay term,

`||J_X D_j||^2 = 1 - 1/X + Lambda_(j,X)(B)`.

NB-088 already shows that every fixed finite Blaschke divisor has `Lambda_(j,X)=O(j^-1)` and therefore contributes only `O(R^-1)` to each terminal sibling distortion. NB-089 identifies the correct quantity controlling the infinite limit. Finite-horizon inverse factors cost their horizontal displacement `a_rho=Re rho-1/2`, so if `sum a_rho=A<infinity`, the product estimates depend on `A` rather than on the number or heights of factors. Passing to the full inner factor yields uniformly

`Lambda_(j,X)(B)=O_A(j^-1)`, `eta_(j,R)^(2)=O_A(R^-1)`,

and hence total squared scalar distortion `O_A(R^-1)->0`.

The key distinction is now **infinitude versus nonsummable effective mass**. An infinite off-critical divisor can remain asymptotically invisible to this compulsory scalar average-direction channel. Thus proving only `B!=1`, or even proving that `B` has infinitely many factors, cannot make NB-087 RH-decisive. A scalar proof must first force the actual false-RH zero set outside the finite-horizontal-mass class and then show that the resulting nonsummable mass produces a nonsummable parent/child delay imbalance.

Even that escape is only necessary. Divergence of `sum (Re rho-1/2)` does not itself imply scalar entropy divergence; cancellation, height distribution and the three-delay combination may still suppress the observable. If the scalar channel remains silent, the available carriers are sibling contrasts, cross-block correlations, earlier indices and the positive nonterminal prediction floors that scalar branching discards.

**Boundary.** NB-089 assumes finite unweighted horizontal mass; the ordinary Blaschke condition is weaker and does not imply it. Small scalar distortion does not imply bounded radial charge because NB-087 is only a lower bound on the full entropy. No claim is made that false RH actually satisfies the summability hypothesis, or that its failure is sufficient for divergence.
