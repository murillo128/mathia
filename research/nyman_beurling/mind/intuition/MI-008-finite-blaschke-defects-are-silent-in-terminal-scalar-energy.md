# MI-008 — Finite Blaschke defects are silent in the terminal scalar energy channel

**Evidence level:** exact for the dyadic terminal scalar distortion through NB-088. The raw visible-norm identity and Blaschke-delay decomposition are exact; the finite-product suppression is uniform for any fixed finite Blaschke divisor. No conclusion is made about the full infinite Blaschke product, the complete terminal Gram entropy, or RH.

NB-087 extracts from q-adic branching a compulsory scalar sibling-energy distortion. In the dyadic case NB-088 restores the common off-critical Blaschke factor and finds that the corresponding raw zeta innovation has an elementary terminal norm independent of the sibling location:

`||J_X \widetilde D_j||^2 = 1 - 1/X`

whenever `j+1<=X<=2j`.

All arithmetic deformation of the deflated visible norm is therefore carried by one nonnegative causal delay term. If `B` is Burnol's off-critical inner factor,

`||J_X D_j||^2 = 1 - 1/X + Lambda_(j,X)(B)`,

where `Lambda_(j,X)(B)` measures how much multiplication by `B` pushes the visible prefix past the same physical cutoff. The dyadic parent/children distortion is exactly a three-term imbalance of these delay energies plus the universal `O(1/R)` raw mismatch.

This makes nontriviality of `B` far too weak as a scalar obstruction. On the RH branch `B=1` and `eta_(j,R)^(2)=-1/(R-1)`. More strongly, for any fixed finite Blaschke divisor `B_F`, Volterra inversion on the relative horizon `log 2` gives `Lambda_(j,X)(B_F)=O(j^-1)`, hence `eta_(j,R)^[F],(2)=O(R^-1)` uniformly for terminal blocks. Summing over `asymp R` siblings gives total squared distortion `O(R^-1)->0`.

Thus one or finitely many right-half zeros are invisible to this compulsory **scalar average-direction** entropy channel. If the full source is to violate the NB-087 scalar `l^2` budget, the effect must be collective: the number or effective strength of relevant off-critical Blaschke factors must grow with the terminal scale so that the parent/child delay imbalance fails to remain square-summable.

The next scalar theorem should therefore estimate the **nonuniform tail of the full Blaschke product across shrinking terminal cells**, not the footprint of a fixed zero. If that collective route stays silent, the remaining radial entropy can only be sought in sibling contrast directions, cross-block correlations, earlier indices or the positive nonterminal prediction floors that the scalar reduction discards.

**Boundary.** Small scalar distortion does not imply bounded radial charge because NB-087 is only a lower bound on the full entropy. Finite-product silence is a matched negative control, not evidence that an infinite off-critical product is silent. The result also does not turn a finite set of zeros into the actual source unless the full off-critical zero set is itself finite.
