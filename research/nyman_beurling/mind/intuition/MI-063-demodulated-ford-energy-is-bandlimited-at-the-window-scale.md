# MI-063 — Hardy-projected Ford energy is a narrow carrier-band form factor, and matched packets require only super-H bandwidth to disperse

**Evidence level:** exact harmonic/source-specific synthesis from NB-246--[NB-251](../../findings/NB-251-matched-ford-packet-energy-has-a-sharp-super-h-bandwidth-crossover.md), including the Hardy projection identity of [NB-249](../../findings/NB-249-positive-ford-frequencies-collapse-first-order-depth-localization-to-a-one-sided-cauchy-projection.md) and the carrier representation of [NB-250](../../findings/NB-250-hardy-projected-ford-energy-is-a-narrow-carrier-band-form-factor.md). The carrier-energy estimate is a sufficient route, and the matched-packet calculation is a control calculation, not a theorem for actual zeta zeros.

NB-248--NB-249 settle the order of operations. The Ford-scaled logarithmic-derivative field has a pole at the planar `L^2` endpoint, so it cannot be squared before the signed divisor pairing. Resolving the positive Mellin frequencies instead performs a one-sided Hardy projection, cancels the Ford depth factors and exactly recovers the finite marked zero current. The carrier phase and horizontal depth mark survive this projection; no decay is created by it.

NB-250 identifies what the remaining Hilbert-space problem measures. The projected moving-center current can be written as a Fourier integral whose amplitude is a depth-marked exponential sum over zeros. The natural Mellin shell samples a physical carrier band of width `Theta(H)=Theta(X/J)`. Expanding its energy gives a positive local pair form factor: the averaging envelope resolves ordinate differences on scale `1/H`, while the carrier phase itself oscillates on the finer `1/X` scale. Full-line carrier-band `L^2` control is therefore a sufficient way to prove the hard-window estimate without discarding the pair phase.

NB-251 separates two bandwidth thresholds that should not be conflated. To make adjacent `1/X` frequency cells orthogonal one would need width of order `X`. But the matched Ford packet from NB-231 already loses its normalized energy once the available carrier width `W` satisfies `W/H -> infinity`. Fixed `W=O(H)` retains a positive energy floor, whereas an intermediate scale such as `W=H sqrt(J)` disperses the matched packet even though `W/X -> 0`.

So the matched-packet obstruction is specifically a **natural-band obstruction**, not an argument that one needs full `X`-scale bandwidth. The catch is physical: the current legal Mellin shell has only `Theta(H)` width. Any claimed super-H escape must therefore be generated by a source-forced operation that preserves the depth marks, the selected center and the Hardy-projection order. Artificially enlarging the Fourier interval changes the observable rather than proving cancellation for the existing one.

**Boundary.** NB-250 does not prove the required carrier-energy estimate for zeta zeros, and NB-251 does not rule out tighter actual zero clusters or prove that `W/H -> infinity` suffices for the true marked current. The durable frontier is either zeta-specific off-diagonal cancellation on the legal `O(H)` band or a mathematically admissible source mechanism producing an intermediate `H << W << X` band.
