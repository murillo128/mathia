# MI-063 — Bellotti regularity upgrades Ford-scale observability from Schwartz tails to one hard box

**Evidence level:** exact harmonic/source-specific synthesis from [NB-246](../../findings/NB-246-demodulation-bandlimits-ford-pair-energy-to-the-window-scale.md) and [NB-247](../../findings/NB-247-bellotti-population-turns-hard-ford-window-energy-into-pointwise-control.md), interpreted against NB-230, NB-243 and NB-245. The required local mean is sufficient, not known for zeta zeros.

Freeze the Ford parameters at a selected center `t_0`, retain the full smooth shell current, and demodulate the common carrier `e^{-iXt}`. NB-246 shows that the moving-center current then has bandwidth `O(H)`, while the zero-pair expansion still retains the fine phase `e^{iX(gamma_j-gamma_k)}` and the horizontal depth marks. Band limitation alone gives a Schwartz-tailed local observability inequality on center scale `1/H`; it does not justify truncating the reproducing-kernel tails.

NB-247 supplies the missing source-specific regularity. Bellotti's weighted near-one zero-population estimate, combined with the smooth shell profile, gives a uniform Lipschitz bound for the demodulated current in the scaled coordinate `z=H(t-t_0)`: on every fixed compact `|z|<=A`, `sup |G'_{t_0}(z)| <= L_A` uniformly in the allowed critical regime. Therefore a large selected value cannot be a delta-like center spike.

For the hard Ford-window energy `I_A(t_0)=H int_{t_0-A/H}^{t_0+A/H}|R_{t_0}(s)|^2 ds`, the Lipschitz persistence argument gives `|R_{t_0}(t_0)| <<_A I_A(t_0)^(1/3)+I_A(t_0)^(1/2)`. Hence a uniform `o(1)` bound on one literal interval of length `O(1/H)` is already sufficient for uniform pointwise `o(1)` control. The gain comes from **band-scale motion plus the actual Bellotti population law**, not from band limitation by itself.

The matched coherent packet of NB-231 still has nonzero hard-box energy after this reformulation, so the stronger observability statement does not average away the adversary. The unresolved theorem remains arithmetic: prove the uniform depth-marked hard-window second moment for the actual zeta zeros while retaining the `1/X` carrier phase.

**Boundary.** NB-247 does not supply that mean-square theorem and does not turn macroscopic/random-height averaging into a substitute. The source window, frozen parameters, depth marks and uniform center quantifier remain load-bearing.
