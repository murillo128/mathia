# MI-004 — The Nyman repair defect is a Blaschke-deflated quotient-direction problem

**Evidence level:** proved structural identities with an open source-to-target bridge

## Core intuition

A source-faithful quotient can expose localized directional mass while still missing the target repair. After removing the common off-critical-zero Blaschke factor, the continuous-versus-natural Nyman gap is an **arithmetic enrichment defect inside the outer part**, not another zero-side obstruction.

The newest evidence narrows the residual further. The deflated natural closure misses off-grid continuous shifts, but the distinguished outer orbit itself is asymptotically swallowed by that closure and large-ratio raw quotient columns become uniformly tiny. Any persistent repair information must therefore survive through **directions/conditioning of tiny quotient columns or bounded/moderate ratios**, not through large raw off-grid mass.

## Strongest justified claim

NB-049--NB-051 show that ambient norms activate too late, the finite repair slack is exactly a distance gain from enriching the reciprocal-integer section by the `M/n` grid, and its two-scale first-order limit is `Delta_*=d_nat^2-d_cont^2`.

NB-052 applies Burnol's Blaschke factorization to both finite comparison spaces. The common off-critical-zero floor cancels exactly, and every finite repair numerator becomes `B(1)^2` times a distance difference between deflated arithmetic subspaces. Thus `Delta_*` is a deflated arithmetic enrichment defect.

NB-053 shows that the deflated natural closure is invariant under every integer-log shift while the full continuous shift hull generated by natural vectors is all of Hardy space. A nonzero orthogonal defect must therefore be visible at some off-grid shift.

NB-054 exploits the special form of the continuous Nyman generators as integrals of one fixed outer orbit. Exact zero-mean cancellation on each logarithmic cell implies
`sup_(t>=log N) dist(U_t O,A_nat)->0`.
It also gives a uniform finite-grid tail envelope: quotient columns with ratio `k/M>=R` have total raw quotient energy `o(R^-2)` after the natural scaling. This does **not** make their span irrelevant, because arbitrarily small nonzero columns can retain important directions after normalization or Gram whitening.

## Synthesis of evidence

The remaining target information cannot be attributed to persistent large-shift/off-grid amplitude. It must be encoded either at bounded/moderate ratio or in the directional geometry and conditioning of increasingly small deflated quotient columns.

A useful source theorem should connect the localized arithmetic direction to that normalized quotient geometry without reconstructing the target projection itself. Raw tail mass and another zero-side factorization are now matched negative controls.

## Counterevidence / boundary cases

NB-054 controls the distinguished outer orbit and raw quotient norms, not the operator norm of the whole quotient projection. Tiny columns can still span a substantial target direction after rescaling, so the repair gap need not vanish.

The results do not determine whether `Delta_*` is zero, positive, or how it behaves under RH.

## Epistemic status

**Exact target normal form and tail boundary:** the repair gap is Blaschke-deflated; large-ratio raw quotient mass vanishes uniformly; any surviving enrichment must live in normalized direction/conditioning or bounded/moderate ratios. The missing theorem is source control of that geometry.

## Novelty/prior-art status

Burnol factorization, Hardy shifts, outer/cyclic theory, projection geometry, and logarithmic-cell cancellation retain their finding-level prior-art boundaries.

## Falsification criterion

Invalidate the NB-052 deflated identity, the NB-054 logarithmic-cell cancellation, or its uniform outer-orbit/tail bounds. Strategically, derive a source-side control of the normalized quotient directions or prove they cannot sustain the closure-level gap.
