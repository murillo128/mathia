# NB-073 — stationary branch-inner defects are exactly one-step prediction gaps

**Status:** `CLASSICAL+EXACT-DERIVED + MATCHED-CONTROL-CLASSIFICATION + GRAM-SCHUR-PREDICTION + CAUSAL-FEEDTHROUGH + INNER-FACTOR-DETECTOR + METHOD-ADVANCE`.

`NB-072` classifies the stationary scalar branch-filter controls

\[
D_j^{(\psi)}=\psi(V_m)e_j,
\qquad
\psi=\theta g,
\]

by their inner factor: after the standard local defect sectors are adjoined,

\[
\mathcal T_\infty(\mathcal A_\psi)
\simeq K_\theta\otimes\ker V_m^*,
\]

so the stable tail vanishes exactly when `psi` is outer. It also shows why the complete **global** Gram operator alone cannot see that distinction: multiplication by the inner factor is isometric, hence

\[
H_\psi:=\psi(V_m)^*\psi(V_m)=g(V_m)^*g(V_m).
\tag{1}
\]

The present finding identifies the missing scalar datum. The inner ambiguity disappears as soon as the global Gram prediction energy is compared with the actual first-cell feedthrough.

Let

\[
\mathcal W:=\ker V_m^*,
\]

fix a unit vector `w in W`, and define the one-step Wold prediction energy

\[
\Pi(\psi)^2
:=
\inf_{x\in\overline{\operatorname{span}}\{V_m^rw:r\ge1\}}
\|\psi(V_m)(w+x)\|^2.
\tag{2}
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\Pi(\psi)=|g(0)|.
}
\tag{3}
\]

On the other hand the first causal cell in `NB-072` is

\[
C_R=\psi(0)e_R,
\]

so its scalar feedthrough magnitude is

\[
c(\psi):=|\psi(0)|=|\theta(0)|\,|g(0)|.
\tag{4}
\]

Consequently

\[
\boxed{
\frac{c(\psi)^2}{\Pi(\psi)^2}=|\theta(0)|^2\le1,
}
\tag{5}
\]

and, because `psi(0) != 0`,

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal T_\infty(\mathcal A_\psi)=\{0\}
\iff
c(\psi)^2=\Pi(\psi)^2.
}
\tag{6}
\]

Thus a stationary branch-inner defect is exactly a **strict prediction/feedthrough gap**. The global Gram matrix supplies the outer prediction scale `Pi`; the local causal diagonal supplies `c`. Neither alone detects the stable tail, but their ratio does.

This sharpens the live source question after `NB-072`. An exact Mellin/Paley--Wiener Gram formula by itself cannot prove the absence of a branch-inner factor in a stationary representation, because (1) erases that factor. What can work is a source-specific analogue of (6): compare the causal first-cell innovation with the prediction energy forced by the complete future Gram geometry. In the stationary class, that single comparison is already equivalent to inner-factor exclusion.

## 1. The prediction energy is a Gram Schur complement and equals the outer feedthrough

Under the Wold unitary from `NB-072`, the cyclic subspace generated by `w` is a scalar Hardy copy,

\[
\overline{\operatorname{span}}\{V_m^rw:r\ge0\}
\simeq H^2(\mathbb D),
\]

with `V_m` corresponding to multiplication by `z`. Therefore (2) becomes

\[
\Pi(\psi)^2
=
\inf_{h\in zH^2}
\|\psi(1+h)\|_{H^2}^2.
\tag{7}
\]

Write `psi=theta g`. Since multiplication by the inner function `theta` is an isometry,

\[
\Pi(\psi)^2
=
\inf_{h\in zH^2}
\|g(1+h)\|_{H^2}^2
=
\operatorname{dist}(g,z gH^2)^2.
\tag{8}
\]

The factor `g` is outer, so `gH^2` is dense in `H^2`; multiplying by `z` gives

\[
\overline{zgH^2}=zH^2.
\tag{9}
\]

Hence

\[
\operatorname{dist}(g,z gH^2)
=
\operatorname{dist}(g,zH^2)
=|g(0)|,
\tag{10}
\]

because the orthogonal complement of `zH^2` is the constants. This proves (3).

The same quantity is visible from the complete Gram operator without knowing the factorization. For `N>=1`, define

\[
\Pi_N(\psi)^2
:=
\inf_{a_1,\ldots,a_N\in\mathbb C}
\left\|
\psi(V_m)
\left(w+\sum_{r=1}^Na_rV_m^rw\right)
\right\|^2.
\tag{11}
\]

Each `Pi_N^2` is the scalar Schur complement of the `r=0` coordinate in the finite Gram matrix of

\[
\psi(V_m)w,\;
\psi(V_m)V_mw,\;\ldots,\;
\psi(V_m)V_m^Nw.
\]

The future spaces increase with `N`, so

\[
\boxed{
\Pi_N(\psi)^2\downarrow\Pi(\psi)^2=|g(0)|^2.
}
\tag{12}
\]

This is the stationary one-ray analogue of the finite-future Schur philosophy in `NB-067`: the outer prediction scale is the monotone limit of explicit finite Gram Schur complements.

## 2. One causal diagonal restores exactly the inner information lost by the Gram

Expand

\[
\psi(z)=c_0+c_1z+c_2z^2+\cdots,
\qquad c_0=\psi(0)\ne0.
\]

`NB-072` proves that the finite-window synthesis is triangular and that the newest-cell innovation is

\[
C_R=c_0e_R.
\tag{13}
\]

Therefore its normalized first-cell energy is exactly

\[
\|C_R\|^2=|c_0|^2=|\psi(0)|^2,
\tag{14}
\]

independent of `R`. Combining `psi(0)=theta(0)g(0)` with (3) gives (5).

If `theta` is constant unimodular, then `|theta(0)|=1` and equality holds in (5). Conversely, an inner function satisfies `|theta(z)|<=1` in the disk. If `|theta(0)|=1`, the maximum-modulus principle forces `theta` to be constant. Because `psi(0) != 0`, there is no zero-at-the-origin exceptional case. Hence

\[
\boxed{
|\theta(0)|<1
\iff
c(\psi)^2<\Pi(\psi)^2
\iff
\mathcal T_\infty(\mathcal A_\psi)\ne\{0\},
}
\tag{15}
\]

which proves (6).

This also makes precise what “Gram invisibility” means. The full operator `H_psi` in (1), all of its untruncated matrix entries, and all Wold-ray Schur complements `Pi_N` are unchanged if `psi` is replaced by its outer factor `g`. What changes is the **causal feedthrough** `psi(0)`. The inner factor is therefore invisible to global energy geometry but not to global energy geometry plus one time-oriented diagonal datum.

## 3. Equivalent Szegő/Jensen form and quantitative inner charge

The Wold model gives

\[
H_\psi
\simeq
T_{|\psi|^2}\otimes I_{\mathcal W}.
\tag{16}
\]

Thus the complete Gram operator together with the known branching shift determines the scalar boundary spectral density

\[
w_\psi(e^{it})=|\psi(e^{it})|^2
=|g(e^{it})|^2
\quad\text{a.e.}
\tag{17}
\]

Since `g` is outer,

\[
|g(0)|^2
=
\exp\left(
\frac1{2\pi}
\int_0^{2\pi}
\log w_\psi(e^{it})\,dt
\right).
\tag{18}
\]

Equation (18) is the classical Szego--Kolmogorov/geometric-mean form of the same prediction identity proved directly in (7)--(10). Hence the nonnegative scalar

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak J(\psi)
:=
\log\frac{\Pi(\psi)^2}{|\psi(0)|^2}
=-2\log|\theta(0)|
}
\tag{19}
\]

is an exact **inner charge** for this matched-control class:

\[
\mathfrak J(\psi)=0
\iff
\mathcal T_\infty=\{0\}.
\tag{20}
\]

For the canonical factorization of a nonconstant inner function with no zero at the origin,

\[
\theta=e^{i\gamma}
\prod_k b_{a_k}\,S_\mu,
\]

one has

\[
-\log|\theta(0)|
=
\sum_k\log\frac1{|a_k|}+\mu(\mathbb T)
\tag{21}
\]

under the standard normalization of the singular inner factor. Thus `J` sees both Blaschke zeros and singular inner mass. It is not merely a zero counter.

No novelty is claimed for (18)--(21): they are classical inner--outer/Jensen/Szego structure. The line-local advance is their exact identification with the `NB-072` stable-tail obstruction and with a Gram-Schur-versus-causal-feedthrough test adapted to the branching controls.

## 4. `NB-071` calibrates the gap and separates it from conditioning

For

\[
\psi_\rho(z)=1-\rho z,
\]

`NB-072` gives, when `rho>1`,

\[
\psi_\rho=b_{1/\rho}\,g_\rho,
\qquad
|g_\rho(0)|=\rho,
\qquad
|\psi_\rho(0)|=1.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\Pi(\psi_\rho)^2=\rho^2,
\qquad
\mathfrak J(\psi_\rho)=2\log\rho
\qquad(\rho>1).
}
\tag{22}
\]

The same parameter that controls the geometric decay of the model-space defect in `NB-071` is exactly the prediction/feedthrough gap.

At the boundary `rho=1`, however, `1-z` is outer but not boundedly invertible. Its Gram lower bound collapses, while

\[
\Pi(1-z)^2=|1-z|_{z=0}^2=1,
\qquad
\mathfrak J(1-z)=0.
\tag{23}
\]

For `0<=rho<1`, the filter is invertible outer and again `J=0`. Thus the new scalar detects the inner transition at `rho=1` without confusing it with the conditioning transition. This is the falsification that a condition-number surrogate fails.

A second stress test is a nonconstant singular inner factor with no zeros in the disk. The global Gram still equals that of the outer factor and a zero-counting test sees nothing, but `|theta(0)|<1`, so (19) remains strictly positive. The detector is genuinely inner-factor sensitive, not merely Blaschke sensitive.

## 5. Consequence for the actual Nyman frontier

The result closes one ambiguity in the frontier left by `NB-072`. In a stationary scalar branch representation, **the exact global Gram cannot by itself yield a no-inner-factor theorem**: replacing `psi` by `theta psi` with inner `theta` preserves that Gram. The correct stationary invariant is instead the comparison

\[
\boxed{
\text{causal feedthrough energy}
\quad\text{versus}\quad
\text{future-prediction energy forced by the Gram}.
}
\tag{24}
\]

Equality is equivalent to no stable tail; a strict deficit is exactly the inner obstruction.

For the actual Blaschke-deflated Nyman innovations, the ingredients analogous to the two sides already exist separately. `NB-062` supplies the genuine newest-cell vectors `C_R` and the complete causal innovation Gram data, while `NB-067` shows how finite future Schur complements encode continuation costs. What is **not** established is a stationary Wold-ray spectral density, a scalar `psi`, or a theorem identifying an arithmetic prediction limit with the local `||C_R||^2`.

The next source-specific target is therefore sharper than “use the Gram to exclude an inner factor.” One needs a nonstationary prediction theorem: construct from the actual descendant/future Gram geometry a canonical prediction energy `Pi_R^2`, then prove that its asymptotic ratio to the actual causal innovation energy satisfies

\[
\frac{\Pi_R^2}{\|C_R\|^2}\longrightarrow1
\tag{25}
\]

(or an appropriate uniform analogue). In the stationary matched-control class, (25) is exactly the no-inner-factor condition. A persistent gap would instead identify a concrete coefficient-side defect that Burnol's half-plane outerness does not automatically remove.

This finding does **not** prove (25), does not transfer Szego theory to the shrinking Nyman cells, and does not infer `T_infinity=0` for the arithmetic source. It narrows the required extra datum: global Gram structure is insufficient, but global Gram prediction plus the source's causal feedthrough is sufficient in the complete stationary control class.
