## Find genuine theta-remainder nonstationarity beyond phase-resolving Wang banks, then audit derivative repayment

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-041-wang-natural-window-is-a-taper-weighted-prime-ratio-occupancy-problem`, `MI-042-fixed-polynomial-smoothing-preserves-the-korobov-vinogradov-envelope-class`, `MI-043-finite-scalar-wang-dilation-banks-have-a-vandermonde-cancellation-ceiling`, `MI-044-translations-split-wang-tail-cancellation-into-independent-phase-fibers`, `MI-045-phase-resolving-wang-windows-inherit-the-fiberwise-moment-cost`.

VIS-330--VIS-340 establish the transform/source boundary. Wang smoothing attenuates high log frequency by one order and exact inversion charges one derivative. Proper prime powers are exponentially below the live envelope, while the surviving prime source and square-log summatory source are exact/invertible images of the same normalized Chebyshev-theta remainder `G(u)=e^(-u)theta(e^u)-1`.

VIS-341 shows that any fixed polynomial truncation power leaves the `u^(3/5)(log u)^(-1/5)` Korobov--Vinogradov class unchanged up to its leading constant. VIS-342 strengthens the gate for variable scalar order: `Omega_r(u)=r(u)Omega_1(u/r(u))`, so subpolynomial `r(u)` still cannot produce a fixed improvement in the `u`-power; a gain `delta>0` requires `r(u)=u^(5 delta/2+o(1))`.

VIS-343 turns that order requirement into a dilation-bank complexity bound. A nonzero scalar combination of `N` distinct pure Wang dilates can cancel at most `N-1` high-frequency moments, by Vandermonde independence of the inverse dilation powers, so its tail order is at most `N`. Consequently a fixed scalar power-class gain `delta` through this mechanism requires `N(u)>=u^(5 delta/2+o(1))`.

VIS-344 closes the **uniform translated-tail** escape. For `M(xi)=sum_j c_j exp(-i b_j xi)m(a_j xi)`, uniform decay of order `r` forces the first `r-1` inverse-scale moments to vanish separately inside every distinct translation fiber. Finite exponential-polynomial uniqueness prevents different translation phases from pooling their asymptotic cancellation. If a fiber contains `n_b` active scales then `r<=n_b`; with `B` active fibers and `N` total translated dilates, `N>=Br`.

VIS-345 now closes the same escape on any sufficiently phase-resolving moving window. If the fixed bank has minimum translation spacing `Delta` and a window has length `L`, then each moment polynomial has the lower frame factor `1-2(B-1)/(L Delta)`. In particular `L Delta>=4(B-1)` forces the same fiberwise moment cancellations as the uniform-tail argument whenever polynomial decay persists along windows tending to high frequency. Moving the window center alone therefore does not create a new smoothing resource.

The source question is consequently narrower. Does `G` have a controlled **short/unresolved-window** or scale-dependent signed structure for which `L_X Delta_X` stays too small relative to the active phase count, or for which translation gaps, coefficients or scales vary in a quantitatively favorable way? Alternatively, can frequency- or source-dependent coefficients exploit arithmetic cancellation that is not equivalent to a small scalar multiplier on a whole interval? Any such mechanism must keep coefficient conditioning and finite-frequency leakage explicit, and Wang's complete error decomposition must retain the smaller scale without requiring `Q'` or equivalent unsmoothed information that repays the gain.

## Quotient packet and summatory geometry through theta before crediting arithmetic structure

Proper prime powers are too small at the relevant scale. The centered prime measure and prime-only square-log summatory source are exact functionals of `G`. Any proposed packet, weighting, summatory organization or multiscale smoothing should first be translated back to a quantitative statement about `G`.

If the mechanism scalarizes to a Wang bank, VIS-341--VIS-345 supply the controls: fixed or subpolynomial order cannot change the power class, a pure-dilation realization of fixed gain needs polynomially many effective scales, fixed translation fibers cannot share the required asymptotic cancellations, and long moving windows that resolve their phase spacings inherit the same moment cost. A surviving translation mechanism must therefore be genuinely unresolved at its working window scale, have scale-dependent phase geometry/conditioning, be frequency- or source-adaptive, or use a signed estimate for `G` rather than multiplier smallness. In every case the signed estimate invalidating the scalar reduction must be explicit, baseline cancellation must be preserved, and the downstream derivative ledger must still close.
