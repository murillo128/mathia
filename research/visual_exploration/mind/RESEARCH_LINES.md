## Find genuine theta-remainder nonstationarity beyond scalar dilation-bank cancellation, then audit derivative repayment

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-041-wang-natural-window-is-a-taper-weighted-prime-ratio-occupancy-problem`, `MI-042-fixed-polynomial-smoothing-preserves-the-korobov-vinogradov-envelope-class`, `MI-043-finite-scalar-wang-dilation-banks-have-a-vandermonde-cancellation-ceiling`.

VIS-330--VIS-340 establish the transform/source boundary. Wang smoothing attenuates high log frequency by one order and exact inversion charges one derivative. Proper prime powers are exponentially below the live envelope, while the surviving prime source and square-log summatory source are exact/invertible images of the same normalized Chebyshev-theta remainder `G(u)=e^(-u)theta(e^u)-1`.

VIS-341 shows that any fixed polynomial truncation power leaves the `u^(3/5)(log u)^(-1/5)` Korobov--Vinogradov class unchanged up to its leading constant. VIS-342 strengthens the gate for variable scalar order: `Omega_r(u)=r(u)Omega_1(u/r(u))`, so subpolynomial `r(u)` still cannot produce a fixed improvement in the `u`-power; a gain `delta>0` requires `r(u)=u^(5 delta/2+o(1))`.

VIS-343 now turns that order requirement into a dilation-bank complexity bound. A nonzero scalar combination of `N` distinct pure Wang dilates can cancel at most `N-1` high-frequency moments, by the Vandermonde independence of the inverse dilation powers. Its tail order is therefore at most `N`. Consequently a fixed scalar power-class gain `delta` through this mechanism requires `N(u)>=u^(5 delta/2+o(1))`; finite, logarithmic, polylogarithmic and other subpolynomial dilation banks are excluded.

The source question is therefore more specific. Does `G` have a genuinely nonstationary, scale-migrating or arithmetic signed property at the live envelope that survives VIS-339--VIS-340 and **does not reduce to frequency-independent scalar tail cancellation among dilates**? Alternatively, can a polynomial-size effective scalar family be controlled with all leakage and conditioning costs explicit? The destination question remains separate: Wang's complete error decomposition must retain the smaller scale without requiring `Q'` or equivalent unsmoothed information that repays the gain.

## Quotient packet and summatory geometry through theta before crediting arithmetic structure

Proper prime powers are too small at the relevant scale. The centered prime measure and prime-only square-log summatory source are exact functionals of `G`. Any proposed packet, weighting, summatory organization or multiscale smoothing should first be translated back to a quantitative statement about `G`.

If the mechanism scalarizes to smoothing order, VIS-341--VIS-343 supply the control: fixed or subpolynomial order cannot change the power class, and a pure-dilation realization of fixed gain needs polynomially many effective scales. In either case the mechanism must preserve baseline cancellation and survive the downstream derivative ledger; scale count alone is not new source information.
