## Estimate the taper-weighted same-sign prime-gap lattice incidence mass

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-009-stable-decoding-is-a-collision-geometry-question`, `MI-010-weighted-null-needs-separate-concentration-and-shape-dimensions`, `MI-011-finite-start-dephasing-is-priced-by-weighted-local-spacing`.

VIS-192--VIS-195 reduce deterministic finite-start dephasing to the coefficient-weighted local-spacing resource `D_v` and show that `D_v=o(y^2)` is equivalent to vanishing taper-energy mass on every fixed top-scale bounded-determinant collision family.

VIS-196 now removes half of that geometry and reparametrizes the rest. Positive/negative prime-ratio frequencies are separated by `Omega(1/y)`, so replacing the full nearest-neighbor spacing by same-sign spacing changes `D_v` by only `O(y)`. Hence `D_v=o(y^2)` is equivalent to the same condition using same-sign neighbors only.

For same-sign frequencies `log(q/p)` and `log(s/r)`, writing the prime gaps `d=q-p` and `e=s-r` converts the dangerous bounded determinant to

`d r - e p = +/- h`,

with fixed nonzero even `h`, top-scale primes and `gcd(d,e)|h`. For fixed `(d,e,h)` all integer solutions lie on one common parameter and the four primality conditions become four affine forms. The equal-gap subfamily reduces further to finitely many fixed prime constellations indexed by divisors of `h`.

The live theorem is therefore more structured than a generic four-prime determinant count: estimate the **actual taper-weighted aggregate of these one-parameter same-sign affine prime-gap systems**, organized by `(d,e)`, or derive a weighted sieve/large-sieve inequality strong enough to force their coefficient-energy mass to zero. The coefficient weights and gap distribution remain essential; an unweighted tuple count is not automatically the target.

## Keep cross-sign separation, same-sign crowding and weighted incidence separate

Opposite-sign collisions can affect secondary terms but cannot carry the quadratic obstruction. VIS-196 does not prove `D_v=o(y^2)`; it identifies the exact remaining arithmetic geometry. The useful frontier is now a weighted prime-gap lattice incidence problem, while `D_v` remains a sufficient Hilbert-inequality horizon rather than a proved optimal observation time.
