## Price positive-Hilbert orientation on the actual similarity orbit

VIS-367--VIS-374 show that growing polynomial depth, bounded positive metric changes, one-Gram equivariant rules, commuting external operators and rotations hidden inside near-degenerate Gram clusters are all source-free conditioning resources unless an independently fixed geometry survives the exact destination budget. Formal noncommutation is not enough: across resolved gaps it is priced by the Gram commutator, while inside a collapsing cluster bounded rotations have only cluster-width-scale spectral effect.

VIS-379 corrects the remaining ambient-derivative overcount. Under simultaneous similarity, the admissible Gram tangent is not the full Hermitian tangent space but the commutator orbit generated by one common infinitesimal conjugation. The relevant amplification is therefore the derivative restricted to this actual orbit. A large unrestricted Frechet norm can be completely irrelevant if the physical/source orbit never reaches its extremizing direction.

VIS-380--VIS-382 sharpen that restriction spectrally. The joint commutator Laplacian controls orbit amplification: persistent bounded output orientation with vanishing Gram defect forces its positive spectral gap to collapse. Gap collapse alone is still insufficient. The Gram tangent itself must carry substantial mass in the collapsing low positive commutator modes, and VIS-382 quantifies the scale: an order-one oriented output with defect `epsilon` forces order-one tangent mass in modes whose commutator eigenvalues are `O(epsilon^2)`.

The live resource is therefore **source-aligned approximate-commutant mass at the defect-squared scale**, not a large full derivative, bare noncommutation, or a small commutator gap. The decisive question is whether the required low modes are forced by the arithmetic/source geometry and remain visible in the destination, or whether they are universal degeneracies reproducible by matched source-free controls. A successful mechanism must establish both the low-mode occupation and its provenance.

## Quotient universal compact geometry before crediting a Wang gain

For any proposed positive-Hilbert improvement, first reduce the source to the exact normalized statistic, price Vandermonde/capacity collapse, expose the full relative metric, restrict the differential to the true simultaneous-similarity orbit, and then measure the tangent against the joint commutator spectrum. Only orientation that survives this quotient and is independently source-forced counts as arithmetic leverage. Metric degeneration, cluster width, commutator gap and low-mode occupation are separate costs and should not be traded implicitly.
