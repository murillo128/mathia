## Resolve the critical `H~y/log y` short-gap zone or leave the smoothly tapered quadratic channel

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-009-stable-decoding-is-a-collision-geometry-question`, `MI-010-weighted-null-needs-separate-concentration-and-shape-dimensions`.

VIS-179--VIS-186 separate sparse close-pair visibility from diffuse full-prefix behavior. VIS-187 finds a deterministic logarithmic accumulation under a hard rectangular window, while VIS-188 removes that endpoint artifact with an endpoint-zero taper.

VIS-189 proves absolute diagonalization at linear observation scale. VIS-190 sharpens the same audited mechanism: for fixed `alpha<1/2` and fixed endpoint-zero `C^2` taper,

`O_v(y;H,T) << y/(H log y)` for `1<=H<=y`,

so the full quadratic prime-pair off-diagonal vanishes uniformly whenever `H >> y/log y`. The interesting boundary is therefore no longer `H~y` but `H~y/log y`.

At that boundary the absolute method leaves an `O(1)` unresolved contribution from top-scale gaps `d<=y/H`, where the kernel cannot use its decaying Fourier tail. Making the same fixed taper smoother only improves the resolved region and cannot eliminate this zone.

A future positive route inside this observable must therefore exploit **source-specific sparsity or signed cancellation in the unresolved short-gap population**. Otherwise the observation itself must change: higher/growing-order information, discrete or source-adapted sampling, nonlinear statistics, different normalization/support geometry, or another controlled functional.

## Keep weight geometry, observation order, sampling law, support breadth, window regularity, Fourier resolution, and signed short-gap cancellation separate

VIS-187--VIS-190 show that observation regularity first removes a deterministic false positive and then pushes complete quadratic diagonalization down to `H >> y/log y`. The remaining boundary is a resolution problem, not another gap-octave bookkeeping problem; below it, any claimed signal must identify the arithmetic or signed mechanism that survives the unresolved kernel zone.
