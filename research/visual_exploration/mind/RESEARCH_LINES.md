VIS-384 removes that entire generator-coordinate gauge relative to the fixed coefficient-space Hilbert geometry. VIS-385 then prices the first source-free subspace baseline. Thus an anomalous quadratic defect and an ordinary defect with anomalous low-mode spectral shape are distinct mechanisms.

VIS-386 removes one more representation freedom from the latter test. The raw self-defect window `P_(0,alpha epsilon_S(G)^2]^(Delta_S) G` is not invariant under rescaling the Gram tangent because the vector and the cutoff scale differently. After removing the exact commutant component `g=(I-P_0)G`, the scale-free object is the spectral probability measure of `Delta_S` seen by `g/||g||_F`, with mean `m_S(G)=<g,Delta_S g>/||g||_F^2`. Its cumulative profile

`F_(G,S)(alpha)=||P_(0,alpha m_S(G)] g||_F^2/||g||_F^2`

is tangent-scale invariant, but for every `alpha>1` it has the universal Markov floor `F_(G,S)(alpha)>=1-1/alpha`. Order-one mass below a fixed super-mean multiple is therefore automatic and is not source evidence.

The live resource is now **source-subspace-specific normalized spectral shape** after generator gauge, tangent-amplitude gauge and the isotropic rank baseline have all been removed. First decide whether the actual `(G,S)` is already exceptional at the quadratic defect-energy level; if not, compare the full normalized positive spectral distribution against stronger matched nonarithmetic controls, or use pre-registered sub-mean windows where no universal positive mass floor exists. Any surviving excess still needs a mathematical source mechanism and a Wang destination payoff after metric, rank-rule, Gram-gap and normalization costs are charged.
