# Visual-exploration research lines

This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Visual-Exploration evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Require an explicit source-to-response theorem before testing qutrit decoder capacity

VIS-367--VIS-389 reduce the intrinsic centered source-subspace problem to a scale-free commutator-Laplacian observable. In the minimal generic qutrit rank-one case, after source conjugacy and the scalar defect rate are fixed, the normalized positive spectrum has one residual shape coordinate. VIS-390--VIS-402 then calibrate that coordinate under progressively broader independently specified Gaussian/Gamma packet, exclusive-allocation, signed sector and finite common-factor nuisance mechanisms.

VIS-403--VIS-404 show why unrestricted latent language does not sharpen the null. Conditional independence given some finite latent is vacuous, and even a fixed scalar prior `U~Uniform(0,1)` plus an unrestricted monotone quantile decoder realizes every Borel residual law. Qualitative monotonicity or smoothness is therefore not a capacity restriction when the decoder or its constants are chosen after seeing the target.

VIS-405 identifies the first exact quantitative boundary for that transport escape. If the monotone decoder is required **in advance** to have `Lip(Q)<=L` on a residual interval `I` of length `ell`, then this is equivalent to the observable mass-floor condition

`nu((x,y]) >= (y-x)/L`

for every residual interval, or equivalently

`nu = (1/L) Leb_I + (1-ell/L) eta`, with `L>=ell`.

At `L=ell` the only admissible law is uniform; for larger fixed `L` the remaining excess law `eta` is arbitrary. Thus a numerical Lipschitz budget is genuinely falsifiable, but its mathematical content is exactly a uniform mass floor and nothing stronger.

VIS-406 closes the generic representation-to-capacity bridge. A fixed differentiable basis with coefficient budget `||c||_p<=B` gives `Lip(Q)<=B M_q` and hence a forced uniform component of mass at least `ell/(B M_q)`. A monotone polynomial decoder of degree at most `n` has the universal Markov bound `Lip(Q)<=n^2 ell`, forcing uniform mass at least `1/n^2`. In the ordered Bernstein subclass, the coefficient-gap geometry improves this to the exact floor `ell/(n Delta)`, and in particular to at least `1/n` when only degree and ordered endpoints are retained. These are source-free capacity consequences: they show exactly how a representation budget becomes a target-law restriction, but they do not derive any such budget from Wang arithmetic.

VIS-407 removes source conjugacy/support geometry itself as the missing justification for that budget in the rank-one qutrit test bed. The normalized source quotient has a regular Hilbert--Schmidt arc-length coordinate `theta`, but the complete algebraic invariant `x=tr(E^3)^2=(1/6)cos^2(3theta)` is metrically singular at both repeated-root boundaries: root gaps split like `sqrt(x)` or `sqrt(1/6-x)`. The source path and root spectrum remain Lipschitz in `theta` while derivatives with respect to `x` diverge. A numerical Lipschitz or derivative budget imposed only in the `x` chart is therefore a coordinate-dependent restriction, not an intrinsic source-complexity budget.

VIS-408 now closes the direct Wang-packet handoff. The actual final Wang test-packet interface admits an infinite-dimensional class of normalized smooth compactly supported packets with no packet-uniform derivative, finite-basis, polynomial-degree or Lipschitz budget. Its variationally selected cosine profile is a scalar zero-pair kernel; no source-derived dictionary identifies it with the qutrit tangent/response decoder of VIS-405--VIS-407. The resolved clue `CLUE-wang-fixed-source-packet-localization` therefore correctly records the direct decoder-capacity branch as refuted.

The surviving question is no longer whether a capacity bound can be read off from Wang packet admissibility. A revival requires **new mathematics**: an explicit intrinsic map from the Wang arithmetic source decomposition to the qutrit tangent/response object, together with a target-independent complexity bound on that map derived from packet resources or another operational source constraint. Only after such a theorem exists do the exact mass-floor tests of VIS-405--VIS-406 become relevant again. Choosing a finite decoder family, a convenient invariant chart, the Wang optimizer, or numerical constants after inspecting the candidate residual would recreate the representation restriction already ruled out.

No new Mind-owned clue is warranted at present. VIS-408 resolves the persisted clue negatively, and the remaining statement is a missing theorem rather than a precise new mathematical mechanism with independent evidence. If a concrete source-to-response map is later derived but its quantitative capacity is not yet established, that narrower object would be an appropriate `proposed` clue.

## Quotient universal compact geometry before crediting a Wang gain

Any positive-Hilbert improvement still has to survive the earlier representation gates: remove exact commutant directions, freeze the ambient Hilbert metric and numerical-rank rule, calibrate source-subspace and tangent-orientation nulls, and condition on the minimal source invariants before interpreting nonlinear low modes. A source-side residual anomaly is not yet a destination gain; its effect must propagate through the Wang map after metric scale, Gram-gap/cluster width, coefficient norm, normalization, rank selection and source-information costs are charged.

VIS-409 closes a natural nonlocal visualization escape from the one-shell boundary. In logarithmic coordinates the full Wigner distribution of a shell is exactly the Weyl symbol of its rank-one operator `|f><f|`; Fourier inversion recovers the complete rank-one kernel, and fixed-window spectrograms or fixed Cohen/localization portraits are deterministic downstream transforms. Cross-Wigner portraits of finitely many source-independent descendants are likewise fixed by the same shell and the declared descendant operators. Full phase space can therefore escape a finite-Mellin-jet truncation while still adding no independent arithmetic carrier.

VIS-410 classifies what changes when the displayed shells are genuinely separate source objects rather than descendants of one shell. The full matrix of cross-Wigner fields is equivalent to the rank-one state of the labelled vector `F=(f_1,...,f_K)` and determines that labelled vector up to one common phase. Separate auto-Wigner portraits forget the independent shell phases, while scalar trace aggregation is only the frame operator and is invariant under arbitrary `U(K)` mixing of shell labels. A matrix-valued portrait can therefore faithfully retain **already-present** cross-shell coherence, but neither the matrix nor its scalarizations manufacture an arithmetic coupling.

VIS-411 then takes the source-representation quotient seriously when each shell is specified only as a ray. Individual off-diagonal phases are vertex-phase gauge; the intrinsic phase data are closed-cycle Bargmann products. On each connected component of the nonzero-overlap graph, edge magnitudes plus a fundamental cycle basis reconstruct the Gram matrix up to independent shell phases. For complete overlap graphs, triangular Bargmann invariants suffice after a star-tree gauge. Thus a visually salient pairwise interference phase is not an intrinsic source observable unless the source fixes vector representatives; for projective shell data, the first admissible phase-sensitive carrier is cycle holonomy.

Higher dimension, rank or visually richer phase space may introduce useful coordinates, but nominal dimensionality is not evidence. After VIS-408--VIS-411, the first admissible escape is specifically a pre-specified **source-derived response mechanism or genuinely additional source coupling** whose gauge-invariant relation excludes some matched controls for an intrinsic reason and remains resolvable at the destination. If the source supplies projective shells, that relation must survive as a Bargmann cycle law or another representative-free invariant; if it supplies fixed relative phases, the full cross-Wigner matrix may retain those phases but still needs a source theorem showing why they are selective. Decoder complexity, one-shell Wigner geometry, scalar frame portraits, or raw pairwise phase patterns without that theorem are representation choices, not arithmetic discriminators.

The precise fertile direction exposed by VIS-410--VIS-411 is already established enough to state as this research-line question rather than as a weaker clue: determine whether the current Wang or neighboring arithmetic source constructions force nontrivial **gauge-invariant cross-shell cycle relations** that matched multi-shell controls cannot reproduce. The representation theory itself supplies no such law, so no Mind-owned `proposed` clue is created until an actual source-derived candidate relation is identified.
