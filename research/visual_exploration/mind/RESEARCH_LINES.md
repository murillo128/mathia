## Test intrinsic source-subspace localization beyond representation and universal spectral baselines

VIS-367--VIS-374 show that growing polynomial depth, bounded positive metric changes, one-Gram equivariant rules, commuting external operators and rotations hidden inside near-degenerate Gram clusters are all source-free conditioning resources unless an independently fixed geometry survives the exact destination budget. VIS-379 restricts sensitivity to the true simultaneous-similarity orbit, and VIS-380--VIS-382 show that persistent bounded output orientation with vanishing Gram defect requires the actual Gram tangent to carry substantial mass in positive joint-commutator modes at the `O(epsilon^2)` scale.

VIS-383 exposes a representation false positive: anisotropic reweighting of source generators can manufacture defect-normalized low-mode mass without changing their span or exact joint commutant. VIS-384 removes that entire generator-coordinate gauge relative to the fixed coefficient-space Hilbert geometry. After centering modulo the identity, the Hilbert-Schmidt source subspace `S` has an intrinsic commutator Laplacian `Delta_S`, equivalently obtained from any redundant frame by contracting with its Gram pseudoinverse. Invertible anisotropic reparameterizations, common scaling, permutations and redundant expansions of the same `S` no longer change the statistic.

VIS-385 then prices the first source-free subspace baseline. At fixed matrix size and source rank the total commutator spectral mass is exactly fixed, and under a Haar-uniform rank-`r` source subspace the defect energy `<G,Delta_S G>` has an exact mean and variance determined by `d`, `r` and the second/fourth moments of the fixed traceless Gram tangent. Thus an anomalous quadratic defect and an ordinary defect with anomalous low-mode spectral shape are distinct mechanisms.

VIS-386 removes one more representation freedom from the latter test. The raw self-defect window `P_(0,alpha epsilon_S(G)^2]^(Delta_S) G` is not invariant under rescaling the Gram tangent because the vector and the cutoff scale differently. After removing the exact commutant component `g=(I-P_0)G`, the scale-free object is the spectral probability measure of `Delta_S` seen by `g/||g||_F`, with mean `m_S(G)=<g,Delta_S g>/||g||_F^2`. Its cumulative profile

`F_(G,S)(alpha)=||P_(0,alpha m_S(G)] g||_F^2/||g||_F^2`

is tangent-scale invariant, but for every `alpha>1` it has the universal Markov floor `F_(G,S)(alpha)>=1-1/alpha`. Order-one mass below a fixed super-mean multiple is therefore automatic and is not source evidence.

VIS-387 closes the normalized spectral-shape escape completely in the qubit case. On `Herm_0(2)`, the Pauli/Bloch identification gives the exact identity

`Delta_S = 2(r I-P_S)`.

For source rank `r=1` or `3` the positive spectrum has a single normalized atom. For `r=2`, if `p=||P_S g||^2/||g||^2`, then the spectral measure is `p delta_2+(1-p)delta_4` while the scalar defect rate is `m=4-2p`; hence `p=(4-m)/2`. The complete positive spectral distribution is therefore already determined by the scalar defect rate. In `d=2`, normalized spectral shape contains no independent source-specific channel beyond the calibrated defect energy.

The live spectral-shape branch begins only at `d>=3`, where the qubit projector identity no longer proves collapse. First decide whether the actual `(G,S)` is exceptional at the quadratic defect-energy level. If not, any higher-dimensional shape claim must survive generator gauge, tangent-amplitude gauge, the isotropic rank baseline, the universal super-mean CDF floor and matched nonarithmetic controls; sub-mean windows remain legitimate because the Markov floor does not force mass there. None of VIS-387 establishes that an independent shape channel actually exists in `d>=3`, and any surviving anomaly still needs a source mechanism and a Wang destination payoff.
