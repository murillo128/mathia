This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Visual-Exploration evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Price positive-Hilbert orientation on the actual similarity orbit with a fixed source metric

VIS-367--VIS-374 show that growing polynomial depth, bounded positive metric changes, one-Gram equivariant rules, commuting external operators and rotations hidden inside near-degenerate Gram clusters are all source-free conditioning resources unless an independently fixed geometry survives the exact destination budget. Formal noncommutation is not enough: across resolved gaps it is priced by the Gram commutator, while inside a collapsing cluster bounded rotations have only cluster-width-scale spectral effect.

VIS-379 corrects the remaining ambient-derivative overcount. Under simultaneous similarity, the admissible Gram tangent is not the full Hermitian tangent space but the commutator orbit generated by one common infinitesimal conjugation. VIS-380--VIS-382 then sharpen that restriction spectrally: persistent bounded output orientation with vanishing Gram defect forces the Gram tangent itself to carry substantial mass in positive joint-commutator modes whose eigenvalues are `O(epsilon^2)`.

VIS-383 adds a representation gate to that defect-squared test. If source coordinates `H_j` are changed by `H'=AH`, then the commutator Laplacian becomes `Delta_(H')=delta_H^*(A^T A tensor I)delta_H`. Common scaling and orthogonal mixing are harmless because both `Delta_H` and the defect scale change by the same scalar, leaving the defect-normalized low-mode projector exactly invariant. A general anisotropic invertible change preserves the source-operator span and exact joint commutant but can create or remove order-one normalized low-mode mass. The source-index metric is therefore part of the observable, not free coordinates.

The live resource is **source-aligned approximate-commutant mass at the defect-squared scale after fixing the source-coordinate metric independently of the observed signal**. A successful mechanism must show both low-mode occupation and provenance, and either use a canonical metric or prove robustness over an explicitly admitted metric class. Same operator span, same commutant, a small commutator gap, or a low-mode signal obtained only after anisotropic reweighting is not enough.

## Quotient universal compact geometry before crediting a Wang gain

For any proposed positive-Hilbert improvement, first reduce the source to the exact normalized statistic, price Vandermonde/capacity collapse, expose the full relative metric, restrict the differential to the true simultaneous-similarity orbit, fix or price the source-coordinate index metric, and only then measure the tangent against the joint commutator spectrum. Metric degeneration, cluster width, source-index anisotropy, commutator gap and low-mode occupation are separate costs and should not be traded implicitly.

The decisive question remains whether excess defect-squared low-mode mass over matched controls survives these representation quotients because of arithmetic/source structure. VIS-383 closes the shortcut of treating arbitrary invertible generator reparameterizations as harmless: only the conformal-orthogonal gauge leaves the normalized localization statistic unchanged exactly.
