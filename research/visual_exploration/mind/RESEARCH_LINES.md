## Prove a coefficient-uniform weighted sieve over the same-sign prime-gap fibers

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-009-stable-decoding-is-a-collision-geometry-question`, `MI-010-weighted-null-needs-separate-concentration-and-shape-dimensions`, `MI-011-finite-start-dephasing-is-priced-by-weighted-local-spacing`.

VIS-192--VIS-195 reduce deterministic finite-start dephasing to the coefficient-weighted local-spacing resource `D_v` and show that `D_v=o(y^2)` is equivalent to vanishing taper-energy mass on every fixed top-scale bounded-determinant collision family. VIS-196 removes the opposite-sign branch at only `O(y)` cost and parametrizes every dangerous same-sign collision by `d r-e p=+/-h`; for fixed `(d,e,h)` the four primality conditions become affine forms in one parameter.

VIS-197 now shows that local congruence geometry is not the source of a power-scale loss. Outside the explicit finite conductor

`Q=d_0 e_0 g m (m^2-g^2 d_0^2 e_0^2)`,

every prime sees the generic four-root pattern, and the singular series of a prime-realized nondegenerate fiber is at most polylogarithmic in `y` at fixed normalized crowding scale. The degenerate `Q=0` branch collapses to finitely many fixed-shift three-prime progressions.

The live theorem is therefore a genuinely **global weighted sieve/summation problem**: obtain a coefficient-uniform upper bound for prime parameter values on the nondegenerate fibers and sum it over `(d,e,h)` with the actual taper weights, while treating the finite degenerate progression family separately. The number and length of fibers, overlap, and coefficient-energy distribution remain load-bearing; generic singular-series inflation does not.

## Keep local admissibility, tuple counts and weighted incidence separate

VIS-197 proves local admissibility and controls local-factor inflation, not the aggregate prime count. A polylogarithmic singular series cannot by itself establish `D_v=o(y^2)`, and an unweighted tuple bound need not control the taper-energy measure. Opposite-sign separation, same-sign affine parametrization, local conductor control, coefficient-uniform sieve estimates, and the final weighted incidence sum are distinct gates.
