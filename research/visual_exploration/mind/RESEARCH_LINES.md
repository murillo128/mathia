## Move beyond the smoothly tapered quadratic full-prefix prime-log channel

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-009-stable-decoding-is-a-collision-geometry-question`, `MI-010-weighted-null-needs-separate-concentration-and-shape-dimensions`.

VIS-179--VIS-186 progressively separate sparse close-pair visibility from diffuse full-prefix behavior and show that every individual polynomial mesoscopic gap octave is negligible at linear observation scale. VIS-187 then finds a deterministic `log M` accumulation under a hard rectangular window, while VIS-188 proves that an endpoint-zero taper removes that logarithm by improving the Fourier tail from `1/z` to `1/z^2`.

VIS-189 closes the remaining quadratic gap-scale escape for that admissible smooth observation. For every fixed `alpha<1/2`, every fixed endpoint-zero `C^2` taper, and `H>=c y`, the **entire absolute normalized prime-pair off-diagonal** is `O(1/log y)` uniformly in the interval start. Near pairs are summable because the taper supplies a `1/d^2` gap weight; far multiplicative pairs are smaller by fixed logarithmic separation.

Thus under the smoothly tapered linear-scale quadratic mean, bounded gaps, subpolynomial bands, polynomial mesoscopic octaves, cross-shell accumulation, and macroscopic multiplicative separations all collapse inside one vanishing envelope. Repeating a finer gap decomposition inside the same statistic cannot reopen the channel.

A future positive route must change a load-bearing feature: use higher/growing-order information, a different normalization/support geometry, discrete or source-adapted sampling, a genuinely different observation functional whose own leakage is controlled, or source information not reducible to this tapered quadratic prime-log field. The hard rectangular statistic remains mathematically different, but its endpoint leakage must be treated as part of the observable rather than as source evidence.
