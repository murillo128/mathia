## Find genuine theta-remainder nonstationarity beyond scalar dilation and translation-fiber cancellation, then audit derivative repayment

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-041-wang-natural-window-is-a-taper-weighted-prime-ratio-occupancy-problem`, `MI-042-fixed-polynomial-smoothing-preserves-the-korobov-vinogradov-envelope-class`, `MI-043-finite-scalar-wang-dilation-banks-have-a-vandermonde-cancellation-ceiling`, `MI-044-translations-split-wang-tail-cancellation-into-independent-phase-fibers`.

VIS-330--VIS-340 establish the transform/source boundary. Wang smoothing attenuates high log frequency by one order and exact inversion charges one derivative. Proper prime powers are exponentially below the live envelope, while the surviving prime source and square-log summatory source are exact/invertible images of the same normalized Chebyshev-theta remainder `G(u)=e^(-u)theta(e^u)-1`.

VIS-341 shows that any fixed polynomial truncation power leaves the `u^(3/5)(log u)^(-1/5)` Korobov--Vinogradov class unchanged up to its leading constant. VIS-342 strengthens the gate for variable scalar order: `Omega_r(u)=r(u)Omega_1(u/r(u))`, so subpolynomial `r(u)` still cannot produce a fixed improvement in the `u`-power; a gain `delta>0` requires `r(u)=u^(5 delta/2+o(1))`.

VIS-343 turns that order requirement into a dilation-bank complexity bound. A nonzero scalar combination of `N` distinct pure Wang dilates can cancel at most `N-1` high-frequency moments, by Vandermonde independence of the inverse dilation powers, so its tail order is at most `N`. Consequently a fixed scalar power-class gain `delta` through this mechanism requires `N(u)>=u^(5 delta/2+o(1))`.

VIS-344 closes the corresponding **uniform translated-tail** escape. For `M(xi)=sum_j c_j exp(-i b_j xi)m(a_j xi)`, uniform decay of order `r` forces the first `r-1` inverse-scale moments to vanish separately inside every distinct translation fiber. Finite exponential-polynomial uniqueness prevents different translation phases from pooling their asymptotic cancellation. If a fiber contains `n_b` active scales then `r<=n_b`; with `B` active fibers and `N` total translated dilates, `N>=Br`.

The source question is therefore more specific. Does `G` have a genuinely nonstationary, scale-migrating, finite-window, adaptive or arithmetic signed property at the live envelope that survives VIS-339--VIS-340 and **does not reduce to frequency-independent uniform tail cancellation among dilates or translation fibers**? Alternatively, can a polynomial-size effective scalar family be controlled with coefficient conditioning and finite-frequency leakage explicit? The destination question remains separate: Wang's complete error decomposition must retain the smaller scale without requiring `Q'` or equivalent unsmoothed information that repays the gain.

## Quotient packet and summatory geometry through theta before crediting arithmetic structure

Proper prime powers are too small at the relevant scale. The centered prime measure and prime-only square-log summatory source are exact functionals of `G`. Any proposed packet, weighting, summatory organization or multiscale smoothing should first be translated back to a quantitative statement about `G`.

If the mechanism scalarizes to smoothing order, VIS-341--VIS-344 supply the controls: fixed or subpolynomial order cannot change the power class, a pure-dilation realization of fixed gain needs polynomially many effective scales, and fixed translated fibers cannot share the required asymptotic cancellations. A surviving translation mechanism must therefore be genuinely nonuniform in frequency, frequency-dependent/adaptive, or source-coupled. In every case the signed estimate invalidating the scalar reduction must be explicit, baseline cancellation must be preserved, and the downstream derivative ledger must still close.
