## Test intrinsic source-subspace localization beyond calibrated qutrit nuisance nulls

VIS-367--VIS-386 progressively remove source-free conditioning, similarity-orbit, generator-coordinate and tangent-amplitude artifacts. The intrinsic centered source subspace `S` carries a commutator Laplacian `Delta_S`; after removing the exact commutant component, the scale-free observable is the positive spectral probability measure seen by the normalized Gram tangent. Its first moment is the scalar defect rate, and super-mean cumulative mass has a universal Markov floor, so neither raw low-mode mass nor an order-one super-mean CDF window is source evidence by itself.

VIS-387 closes the normalized spectral-shape escape completely for qubits: on `Herm_0(2)`, the full positive spectral distribution is already determined by source rank and scalar defect rate. VIS-388 then identifies the minimal higher-dimensional capacity. For `d=3`, source rank one, the three positive root-sector eigenvalues are fixed by the cubic source invariant `t^2=tr(E^3)^2`; generic tangents with the same first moment can nevertheless have different spectral measures. This is algebraic capacity, not arithmetic specificity.

VIS-389 shows that the generic qutrit capacity is only one-dimensional after the obvious source and energy coordinates are fixed. If the positive support is `q_1<q_2<q_3` and `m_1` is the scalar defect rate, then the second moment `m_2` determines all three spectral weights by the Vandermonde/Lagrange formula. Equivalently, conditional on `(t^2,m_1)`, every normalized positive spectral profile is a function of one residual shape scalar. At the symmetry-enhanced qutrit boundaries the residual degree disappears entirely.

VIS-390 supplies an exact source-free null for that last scalar under Haar-isotropic tangent orientation. The three two-dimensional sector energies are `Dirichlet(1,1,1)`, so conditional on the observed `m_1`, the normalized residual coordinate

`u=(m_2-L(m_1))/(U(m_1)-L(m_1))`

is exactly `Uniform(0,1)` once `t^2` fixes the support.

VIS-391 strengthens the nuisance class substantially. If the three root-sector tangent blocks are independent centered isotropic Gaussians with arbitrary fixed sector variances `sigma_i^2`, the normalized sector weights have the scaled-Dirichlet density proportional to `A(w)^(-3)`, where `A(w)=sum_i lambda_i w_i` and `lambda_i=(2 sigma_i^2)^(-1)`. Restricting to a fixed first moment makes `A` affine in the residual coordinate `u`, so the exact conditional density is proportional to an inverse cube. Its closed-form conditional CDF `F_lambda(u|m_1)` uniformizes the residual exactly. The isotropic VIS-390 null is only the equal-variance special case.

VIS-392 identifies the full generic source-stabilizer covariance freedom inside the Gaussian class, so the sector-scaled family is maximal only under Gaussianity. VIS-393 then shows why source symmetry cannot by itself extend that calibration beyond Gaussian laws. The stabilizer torus changes only root-sector phases; the normalized sector weights lie in its orbit quotient. For **every** Borel probability law on the weight simplex there is a torus-invariant tangent law realizing exactly that weight distribution, obtained by Haar-averaging phases over any prescribed weight law. Conditional on a fixed first moment, every Borel law on the residual coordinate `u` is therefore compatible with source-stabilizer invariance. The orbit quotient even retains an additional cubic phase invariant on the open stratum.

VIS-394--VIS-397 build an explicit mechanism-based nuisance ladder. Independent replicated Gaussian/Gamma sectors with arbitrary fixed counts and rates give exact normalized-Gamma residual laws, and one additive shared-Gamma block creates source-free positive dependence while remaining exactly calibratable. VIS-398 closes the finite extension of that mechanism: any fixed finite network of nonnegative additive shared-Gamma blocks has an exact simplex fiber integral, so unequal pairwise positive correlation and subset-specific common shocks remain calibrated nuisance when the architecture is specified independently.

VIS-399 adds a qualitatively different source-free mechanism. A fixed packet budget with exclusive sector assignment produces negative raw-energy covariance and an exact finite mixture of normalized-Gamma simplex laws. VIS-400 shows that Gamma amplitudes are not load-bearing: arbitrary independently specified positive packet-amplitude densities remain exactly calibratable by convolution and radial normalization, including boundary atoms from empty sectors.

VIS-401 closes the simplest signed-interference extension as well. If exclusively assigned packets carry arbitrary independently specified signed amplitudes but interfere only **within** their assigned sector before squaring, the sector totals still become conditionally independent positive variables after the signed convolution/square pushforward. Their normalized qutrit residual therefore remains exactly calibratable by the same radial construction. For centered signed packets, fixed-budget exclusivity still gives the explicit negative cross-sector energy covariance.

The nuisance frontier has therefore moved from amplitude law to **dependence architecture**. Nonuniformity, skew, concentration, boundary mass, positive or negative covariance, unequal counts/rates, non-Gamma tails, and sector-local signed cancellation can all be source-free under exact independently specified controls. A genuinely stronger test must introduce cross-sector coherent signed latents before squaring, phase locking, packet interactions that preserve dependence after conditioning on counts, a nontrivial infinite/continuum limit, or a Wang admissibility rule that derives a narrower coupled law from the source. The accepted clue `CLUE-wang-fixed-source-packet-localization` already records this handoff; it must not be weakened into a generic “try more flexible nuisance” program.

## Quotient universal compact geometry before crediting a Wang gain

For any proposed positive-Hilbert improvement, first reduce the source to the exact normalized statistic, price Vandermonde/capacity collapse, expose the full relative metric, restrict the differential to the true simultaneous-similarity orbit, replace arbitrary generator coordinates by the intrinsic centered source subspace, remove tangent-amplitude normalization, freeze the ambient Hilbert metric and numerical-rank rule, and calibrate both source-subspace and tangent-orientation nulls before interpreting nonlinear low modes.

The dimension-sensitive gate is now explicit. `d=2` has no independent normalized spectral shape. Generic `d=3,r=1` has exactly one residual shape coordinate after source conjugacy and scalar defect are conditioned out. Mechanism-fixed Gaussian/Gamma families, arbitrary finite additive shared-Gamma networks, fixed-budget exclusive positive amplitudes, and sector-local signed coherent amplitudes all admit exact conditional calibration when their nuisance parameters are fixed independently. But source-stabilizer symmetry by itself leaves the residual law arbitrary. Higher dimension/rank may have larger spectral support and additional shape degrees, but nominal dimensionality, isotropy, symmetry, residual nonuniformity, residual asymmetry, covariance sign or local interference alone is not evidence. The next genuine representation escape must derive a falsifiable source-free restriction on a **cross-sector coupled** surviving shape variable, show that the actual Wang/source family violates it for arithmetic rather than model-misspecification reasons, and then prove that the destination still resolves and rewards that direction.
