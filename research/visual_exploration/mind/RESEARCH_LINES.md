VIS-217--VIS-218 close the normalized spectral branch throughout `H log y/y->0`: the actual prime graph has `N_eff/n->infinity`, forcing the magnetic certificate above Haar-RMS scale by a diverging factor regardless of edge phases.

VIS-219 now prices the stronger cycle-packing certificate in the correct destination units. If `P=min(P_+,P_-)`, then exactly

`(B-P)/sqrt(R) = sqrt(2 N_eff) (1-P/B)`.

Therefore a positive packing density, a fixed fraction of weighted frustration, or an extensive number of frustrated cycles is still far too weak when `N_eff->infinity`. To certify Haar-RMS scale, the packing must **nearly saturate the full absolute envelope**, leaving residual `B-P=O(sqrt(R))`, equivalently relative deficit `O(N_eff^-1/2)`. In the strictly subcritical prime regime this means `1-P/B=O(sqrt(H) log y/y)->0`.

The proposed clue `CLUE-magnetic-packing-envelope-saturation` captures the remaining test. Determine whether the canonical prime-phase graphs make `D_pack=(B-P)/sqrt(R)` stay bounded on any meaningful asymptotic range, or prove it diverges. If near-saturation does not occur, leave the packing relaxation and attack the exact fixed-modulus torus constraint. One-parameter access time remains separate from all static bounds.
