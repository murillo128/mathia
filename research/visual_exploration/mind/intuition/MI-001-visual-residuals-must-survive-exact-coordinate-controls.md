# MI-001 — Visual residuals are meaningful only after exact quotients, matched nulls, and a common information geometry are explicit

**Evidence level:** supported through VIS-042 by exact reconstruction identities, finite-population controls, Hadamard factorization, Fisher-normal geometry, and common-reference residual comparisons

## Core intuition

Visual complexity is not an independent arithmetic resource when it can be reconstructed from ordinary coordinates, lower-order statistics, symmetry, deterministic boundary geometry, or the complete zero divisor itself. The meaningful object is the residual after the strongest control preserving the information already known to generate the picture.

The newer three-gap results add a second information-loss gate. Scalar residual scores can preserve the **size** of irreducible dependence while discarding its direction. Direction can be compared exactly only after the processes are embedded in one common predeclared metric/gauge. A common gauge makes orientation meaningful; it does not make that gauge canonical.

## Strongest justified principle

VIS-013--VIS-034 classify circular reconstruction, sampled statistics, Farey endpoint controls, same-gap/reflection nulls, and the complete-xi divisor quotient. In particular, diverging sublinear even Farey bands remove every fixed endpoint layer while retaining the exact same-gap/reflection null energy, and complete xi-field renderings are deterministic re-encodings of one normalized divisor channel.

VIS-035--VIS-039 show that CA, Pearson, LRT/CMI and nearby finite-size expansions are not independent confirmations when they act on the same three-gap residual. VIS-040 identifies the local geometry exactly: near a positive Markov law, CMI is one half of the squared Fisher norm of the perturbation component normal to the Markov manifold. It measures magnitude while quotienting the normal orientation.

VIS-041 restores that orientation only after fixing one common positive Markov reference. Both processes then lie in the same whitened interaction space and their Fisher overlap/angle is basis-invariant within that gauge. VIS-042 extends the construction to a whole predeclared panel: the weighted logarithmic pool of the panel's Markov closures remains Markov and supplies one common Fisher metric, producing a positive-semidefinite residual-orientation Gram. Equal weights remove panel-order dependence, but the answer still depends on the panel, weights, support, partition, and pooling rule.

## What remains possible

For Farey discrepancy, test pre-registered sublinear bands against the exact reflection-same-gap null and separate amplitude from redistribution before interpreting any residual.

For three-gap or other conditional residuals, decide first whether the claim concerns magnitude or direction. If direction matters, freeze a common external or panel-derived reference before inspection and challenge the result under reasonable source-compatible reference/partition controls. Separately whitening each process, or choosing the panel/reference after seeing the desired alignment, destroys the evidential meaning of the angle.

More broadly, useful visual independence should be organized by explicit information loss: finite prime versus zero truncations, local versus global windows, different conditional sigma-fields, residual directions versus norms, and other maps that provably discard different data.

## Status / novelty

Farey geometry, correspondence analysis, Fisher information, Markov models, logarithmic opinion pooling, and Hadamard factorization are classical ingredients. The persisted synthesis is the visual gate: **first quotient deterministic/statistical redundancy, then state exactly which residual magnitude or orientation survives, and compare orientation only in a common predeclared gauge whose own dependence is part of the claim**.

## Falsification criterion

Show that a fixed Farey endpoint hierarchy survives the VIS-031 band gate, that the reflection-same-gap null violates VIS-032, that scalar CMI locally determines Fisher-normal direction contrary to VIS-040, or that the VIS-041/042 common-gauge overlaps change under an allowed common basis relabeling rather than a genuine change of reference geometry.
