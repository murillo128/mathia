# MI-001 — Visual residuals are meaningful only after exact quotients, matched nulls, and quantitatively audited common information geometry

**Evidence level:** supported through VIS-046 by exact reconstruction identities, finite-population controls, Fisher-normal geometry, common-reference comparisons, KL-pooling controls, and exact gauge-stability classifications

## Core intuition

Visual complexity is not an independent arithmetic resource when it can be reconstructed from ordinary coordinates, lower-order statistics, symmetry, deterministic boundary geometry, or the complete zero divisor. The meaningful object is the residual after the strongest control preserving the information already known to generate the picture.

For higher-order residual direction there is an additional metric gate. Scalar scores can preserve the size of irreducible dependence while discarding its direction. Direction becomes comparable only inside one predeclared common positive metric, and the robustness of that direction must itself be quantified rather than inferred from agreement across a few convenient gauges.

Mixed local agreement/opposition permits positive, zero, or negative global orientation by gauge reweighting. Thus unrestricted gauge optimization is representation choice, not evidence.

## What remains possible

For Farey discrepancy, test pre-registered sublinear bands against the exact reflection-same-gap null and separate total amplitude from spectral redistribution before interpretation.

For conditional residual direction, freeze the primary gauge, panel, weights, support, partition, pooling rule, and KL direction before inspection. Predeclare a scientifically justified alternative gauge family and report its condition number or likelihood-ratio spread. If no bounded family is justified, claim gauge-independent sign only when coordinatewise sign compatibility gives the exact unrestricted certificate.

More broadly, useful visual independence should be organized by explicit information loss: finite prime versus zero truncations, localized windows, differently conditioned nulls, residual directions versus norms, and other maps that provably discard different data.
