# MI-001 — Visual residuals are meaningful only after exact quotients, matched nulls, and information-loss maps are explicit

**Evidence level:** supported through VIS-033 by exact reconstruction identities, classical statistical geometry, finite-population discrepancy controls, Farey endpoint/null reductions, and Hadamard factorization

## Core intuition

Visual complexity is not an independent arithmetic resource when it can be reconstructed from ordinary coordinates, lower-order statistics, symmetry, ordering baselines, deterministic boundary geometry, or the complete zero divisor itself. The meaningful object is the residual after the strongest control preserving the information already known to generate the picture.

The current evidence adds two complementary lessons. In Farey data one can choose a spectral regime where deterministic fixed endpoint layers disappear **without subtracting their arithmetic-bearing hierarchy**, while a strong same-gap/reflection null remains fully visible. At the opposite complete-data extreme, every exact xi-field rendering is already determined by the complete xi zero divisor and normalization. Independence among visualizations therefore comes from different information loss, not different rendering styles.

## Strongest justified principle

VIS-013--VIS-025 classify circular reconstruction, Gram occupancy, sampled `S`, parity, and the first three-gap Markov/Pearson/correspondence residuals. VIS-026 keeps the centered gap multiset fixed and randomizes only order, giving an exact Brownian-bridge baseline; VIS-027 conditions additionally on reflection symmetry and annihilates every odd Dirichlet mode.

VIS-028--VIS-030 identify the Farey endpoint control hierarchy. Fixed `nx` endpoint layers are deterministic totient geometry producing the natural `r=Theta(n)` scale, while the growing hierarchy is itself a normalized-totient Riesz/Mobius channel with Dirichlet series `zeta(s)/zeta(s+1)`. Unrestricted endpoint subtraction can therefore erase target arithmetic.

VIS-031 supplies a cleaner control: for every fixed endpoint cutoff `Y`, its contribution to any diverging sublinear even Dirichlet band `q=o(n)` vanishes at the natural `n`-scaled energy normalization. VIS-032 shows that the same regime is not vacuous. When `q->infinity` and `q=o(n)`, the reflection-preserving same-gap null places asymptotically all of its expected inverse-Laplacian/Green discrepancy energy in that band. The resulting normalized statistic has exact null mean one. Thus sublinear bands form a control sandwich: fixed endpoint geometry disappears while the strongest current exact same-gap/reflection null remains active.

VIS-033 gives the global analytic quotient. For order-at-most-one entire functions with the xi reflection symmetry, the complete zero divisor determines the whole field up to one constant, and standard normalization fixes that constant. Complete domain coloring, modulus/phase fields, critical-line traces, derivatives, critical points, and other exact xi-field renderings are therefore deterministic re-encodings of the same complete divisor. Cross-panel persistence among them is not independent evidence.

## What remains possible

For Farey discrepancy, a live residual should be measured in pre-registered diverging sublinear even bands and normalized against the exact reflection-same-gap expectation, then challenged by stronger local-order, denominator, mediant, and classical Franel--Landau/Mobius controls. VIS-031/032 remove fixed endpoints and baseline Green allocation; they do not prove that the actual Farey residual is nontrivial or novel.

For the visual atlas more broadly, useful independence must be an **information-loss atlas**: finite prime versus finite zero truncations, sparse versus dense samples, local versus global windows, differently conditioned nulls, or projections that discard provably different information. A feature is promotable only when the missing-information map and matched control are explicit and the residual becomes a rendering-independent mathematical statement.

## Status / novelty

Farey ranks, totient identities, Riesz means, path-Laplacian spectra, permutation nulls, Hadamard factorization, and xi uniqueness are classical or persisted reductions. The synthesis is the visual gate: **controls must remove known nuisance structure without subtracting target arithmetic, and exact full-field views count as one information channel once their common divisor quotient is recognized**.

## Falsification criterion

Show that a fixed Farey endpoint hierarchy contributes nonvanishing energy in a regime covered by VIS-031, that the reflection-same-gap null fails the VIS-032 exact expectation, or produce two normalized reflection-symmetric order-one entire fields with the same complete divisor but different exact field values, contradicting VIS-033.
