# MI-033 — Leading-null compensation can preserve an arithmetic carrier, but theorem coordinates must match its source scale

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from VIS-264, VIS-268, VIS-269, [VIS-270](../../findings/VIS-270-first-prime-translated-wang-null-packet.md), and [VIS-271](../../findings/VIS-271-wang-fixed-alpha-prime-scale-mismatch.md). The packet construction and Rodgers prime-power coefficient are exact; no lower-order Wang transfer to the same fixed-prime carrier is established.

Cancellation of the universal leading functional does not require retreating below the arithmetic spectrum. VIS-270 uses a fixed two-band translated packet: one band is centered at the first Rodgers prime frequency `xi_2=log 2/(2 pi)`, while a compensating band lies in a source-empty region. Their relative weight enforces `g(0)=0` and cancels the Wang diagonal/cusp functional, yet the Rodgers quadratic arithmetic coefficient remains a nonzero pure first-prime term.

This separates two representation resources: **leading-functional nulling** can be paid by compensation away from the carrier, while **arithmetic access** depends on support reaching the relevant prime-power frequency. But VIS-271 shows that this does not by itself identify the same arithmetic carrier across different asymptotic theorems.

In Wang's short-interval formula the Fourier variable is `alpha` with source scale `x=T^alpha`. Any fixed positive `alpha` therefore probes prime powers whose size grows like a power of `T`; a literal fixed prime contributes only negligibly on a band bounded away from `alpha=0`. In Rodgers' pair-correlation coefficient, by contrast, the same fixed prime sits at the fixed frequency `log p/(2 pi)`. Tracking that literal prime through Wang requires a shrinking regime `alpha~log p/log T -> 0`, not merely a sharper remainder for a fixed translated band.

The reusable rule is stronger than support crossing: **a test coordinate is meaningful only together with the theorem's source-coordinate map**. Two formulas can use superficially similar Fourier variables while assigning different arithmetic scales to a fixed location. Leading-null source-sensitive packets remain useful, but theorem transfer must preserve the identity of the source carrier, not just the shape of the test.

The live representation/theorem question is therefore either to derive a transform that relates Wang's collective moving prime scale to the fixed Rodgers carrier, or to obtain a controlled shrinking-`alpha` short-interval regime where a fixed prime remains visible. Simply improving the `O(H)` error at fixed positive `alpha` would not, by itself, recover the VIS-270 first-prime coefficient.

**Boundary.** This does not refute Wang's theorem or the VIS-270 Rodgers coefficient. It does not supply a shrinking-`alpha` expansion, a fixed-prime short-interval term, a zero-sensitive sign, or a multiband higher-order transfer. The obstruction is a source-scale mismatch, not a claim that no bridge between the two formulas can exist.
