# MI-011 — Finite-start dephasing is a weighted same-sign prime-gap incidence problem with only polylogarithmic local-factor inflation

**Evidence level:** exact deterministic weighted-Hilbert and same-sign gap-fiber reduction through VIS-197, combined with the VIS-192 Cesaro variance law. No coefficient-uniform aggregate sieve estimate proving `D_v=o(y^2)` or optimality of `D_v` is established.

VIS-192 writes the signed prime-ratio observable as a finite exponential polynomial and gives the long-start variance law. VIS-193 transfers it to deterministic starts at the coarse worst-gap horizon `O(y^2)`. VIS-194 replaces the single worst spacing by the coefficient-weighted local-spacing resource

`D_v = [sum_lambda |c_lambda|^2/delta_lambda] / sum_lambda |c_lambda|^2`,

so a tiny gap matters only in proportion to the coefficient energy carried by its frequency. VIS-195 converts `D_v=o(y^2)` into an exact weighted incidence condition: the taper-energy mass of every fixed normalized top-scale bounded-determinant collision family must vanish.

VIS-196 sharpens the geometry. Opposite-sign prime-ratio frequencies cost only `O(y)` in `D_v`, so the quadratic frontier is entirely same-sign. For positive frequencies `log(q/p)` and `log(s/r)`, writing `d=q-p`, `e=s-r` reduces every dangerous fixed normalized collision to

`d r - e p = +/-h`.

After `d=g d_0`, `e=g e_0`, `gcd(d_0,e_0)=1`, and `m=+/-h/g`, fixing `(d,e,h)` gives four affine prime forms in one common parameter.

VIS-197 now removes uncontrolled local congruence amplification from that remaining one-parameter problem. In the nondegenerate case the four-form local geometry has the explicit finite conductor carrier

`Q=d_0 e_0 g m (m^2-g^2 d_0^2 e_0^2)`.

Every prime not dividing `Q` sees exactly four distinct bad residues. A prime-realized fiber is locally admissible, and its singular series satisfies

`S(L) << (|Q|/phi(|Q|))^4 <<_A (1+log log(3+y))^4`

at fixed normalized crowding scale. Thus the singular series cannot create a power-scale loss. In the degenerate case `Q=0`, primality forces `d_0=e_0=1` and the fiber collapses to a fixed-shift three-prime progression with `h=g^2` and `6|g`; for fixed crowding scale only finitely many such branches occur.

The durable reduction is therefore sharper than “count close prime ratios.” The target is the **taper-weighted aggregate of admissible one-parameter same-sign prime-gap fibers**, with local-density inflation already known to be only polylogarithmic and the degenerate progressions separated. What remains is a coefficient-uniform upper-bound sieve and a summation over `(d,e,h)` that preserves the actual taper energy. A power loss attributed only to the singular series is no longer compatible with the available fiber structure.

**Boundary.** VIS-197 does not count prime parameter values uniformly as the coefficients vary, does not control overlap or the number/length of fibers, and does not prove `D_v=o(y^2)`. The singular-series bound is a local-density statement, not the desired weighted aggregate estimate. `D_v` remains a sufficient finite-start horizon rather than a proved necessary optimum.
