# MI-011 — Finite-start dephasing is a weighted same-sign prime-gap lattice incidence problem

**Evidence level:** exact deterministic weighted-Hilbert reduction through VIS-196, combined with the VIS-192 Cesaro variance law. No estimate proving `D_v=o(y^2)` or optimality of `D_v` is established.

VIS-192 writes the signed prime-ratio observable as a finite exponential polynomial and gives the long-start variance law. VIS-193 transfers it to deterministic starts at the coarse worst-gap horizon `O(y^2)`. VIS-194 replaces the single worst spacing by the coefficient-weighted local-spacing resource

`D_v = [sum_lambda |c_lambda|^2/delta_lambda] / sum_lambda |c_lambda|^2`,

so a tiny gap matters only in proportion to the coefficient energy carried by its frequency.

VIS-195 converts `D_v=o(y^2)` into an exact weighted incidence condition: the taper-energy mass of every fixed normalized top-scale bounded-determinant collision family must vanish. This already separates the true target from unweighted counts of close prime ratios.

VIS-196 sharpens the geometry. Opposite-sign prime-ratio frequencies are separated at scale `1/y`, not the dangerous `1/y^2` scale, and contribute only `O(y)` to `D_v`. Thus the quadratic dephasing problem is equivalent to the same-sign problem. For positive frequencies `log(q/p)` and `log(s/r)`, write `d=q-p` and `e=s-r`; then the bounded determinant is exactly

`q r-p s = d r-e p`.

At fixed normalized crowding, this becomes `d r-e p=+/-h` for one of finitely many fixed nonzero even `h`, with all primes at macroscopic scale and `gcd(d,e)|h`. After fixing `(d,e,h)`, all integer solutions are one-dimensional and the four primality conditions become four affine forms in a common parameter. Equal gaps collapse further to finitely many fixed-shift prime constellations.

The durable reduction is therefore not “prime ratios must usually be separated,” but **the taper-generated energy measure must avoid a weighted union of one-parameter affine prime-gap systems**. Generic four-variable determinant estimates waste the gap structure, while generic prime-tuple counts ignore the coefficient weights. A useful theorem should combine both.

**Boundary.** VIS-196 does not estimate the remaining weighted incidence mass and does not claim opposite-sign collisions are irrelevant below quadratic scale. The affine parametrization is exact for fixed `(d,e,h)` but supplies no prime-density theorem. `D_v` is still a sufficient finite-start horizon, not a proved necessary optimum.
