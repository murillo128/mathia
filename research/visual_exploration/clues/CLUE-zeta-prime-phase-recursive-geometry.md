# Can prime-by-prime zeta approximants be organized as a meaningful recursive geometry?

## Observation
The Euler product gives ζ a literal multiplicative hierarchy indexed by primes in its domain of convergence, while finite prime products and smoothed prime sums can still be formed as finite exploratory objects elsewhere. This suggests a natural source of recursion that is mathematical rather than decorative: add prime scales one by one or in logarithmic bands and inspect the resulting trajectory in value, phase, increment, or renormalized coordinates. The main danger is equally clear: below `Re(s)=1` an ordinary infinite Euler product does not converge, so any apparent attractor or self-similarity produced by raw partial products could be a truncation artifact rather than a property of ζ.

## Research question
Is there a mathematically justified recursive or multiscale representation of prime contributions — for example through finite Euler factors, logarithmic prime bands, smoothed explicit-formula terms, or a hybrid Euler–Hadamard style decomposition — whose geometry stabilizes after an explicit normalization and shows reproducible structure near zeta zeros? Can such a representation expose a scale-by-scale cancellation, phase-locking, forbidden region, or recurrence that is genuinely tied to the prime/zero correspondence rather than to the arbitrary order or truncation of factors?

## Why it may matter
This would connect the user's fractal intuition to one of ζ's intrinsic hierarchical structures: multiplicative prime scales. A useful representation could make the local/global tension between primes and zeros visible in a way that suggests a quantitative invariant or a new cancellation question. Even a negative result would sharply distinguish mathematically meaningful recursion from attractive but invalid "Euler-product fractals" inside the critical strip.

## Decisive test
Start in `Re(s)>1`, where the Euler product supplies a valid baseline, and define one or more recursive embeddings from successive prime factors or logarithmic prime bands. Test whether any candidate scaling law or geometric statistic survives changes of prime grouping, smooth cutoffs, truncation, and coordinate representation. Only then extend the visualization toward the critical strip using finite objects or a representation with an explicit analytic justification; do not infer convergence of the Euler product there. Compare with randomized prime phases, shuffled prime ordering/grouping, generalized-prime or density-matched controls, and with zero locations versus nearby nonzero ordinates. A surviving observation must be expressible as a quantitative statement about the finite/regularized construction and should undergo a serious prior-art audit before promotion.

## Evidence boundary
No convergent Euler product in the critical strip, fractal attractor, prime-zero locking law, or RH consequence is claimed. Raw finite products below `Re(s)=1` are exploratory constructions only, and any visual recursion that depends materially on truncation, ordering, or unjustified continuation kills the intended interpretation.
