# Can a zeta visual statistic leave the regular prime-torus representation class after the full Gram-clock quotient?

## Observation

The prime-phase program has closed a broad family of attractive visual signals as representation or sampling effects rather than source evidence. Fixed finite prime fields already have their shared-phase Haar population law (`VIS-071`); increasingly rich deterministic Gram selectors can reproduce every preassigned finite-order finite-prime Haar test (`VIS-160`); and ordinary omitted-prime controls already exhibit decoder failure, heavy collision tails, moment thresholds, and growing-support population barriers (`VIS-163`, `VIS-164`, `VIS-168`).

The weighted bulk is also representation-dependent. `VIS-172` and `VIS-173` separate the concentration scale `D_2` from the shape scale `D_4`: a natural prime-amplitude taper can concentrate while retaining a persistent non-Gaussian low-prime fingerprint. `VIS-174` then shows that, for slowly growing support, every preassigned fixed moment of the deterministic continuous prime-log field transfers to that same weighted Haar null.

`VIS-175` closes the corresponding full-distribution loophole for square-summable weights. If `sum_p w_p^2<infinity` and `yW_y^2/H->0`, the complete bounded-continuous empirical law of the normalized additive prime-phase field on an arbitrary interval `[T,T+H]` converges to the infinite weighted Haar law, uniformly in the interval start. For `w_p=p^(-alpha)`, every `alpha>1/2` satisfies this gate under any fixed polylogarithmic support cutoff. The resulting limit is generically non-Gaussian, so persistent weighted non-Gaussianity of this additive field is now a matched-null effect at the level of the whole weak law, not merely its first few moments.

## Research question

After quotienting the finite prime-torus population law, the deterministic Gram-clock hierarchy, regular-decoder/collision geometry, support-volume effects, coordinate weighting, fixed-moment transfer, and now the full square-summable weighted additive prime-phase law, does any zeta-facing visual statistic exhibit a **predeclared source-sensitive separation from the representation-matched deterministic/Haar control**?

A surviving mechanism must leave at least one hypothesis of the closed control class in a mathematically essential way. Plausible boundaries include a genuine hybrid prime/zero variable, Gram or other discrete sampling not covered by continuous vertical averaging, a nonsquare-summable growing-dimensional regime, a rare/shrinking or unbounded functional requiring stronger than weak convergence, or another source-dependent observable not reducible to the same additive prime torus.

## Why it may matter

The useful target is now much narrower than “find an unusual prime-phase picture.” Even a stable non-Gaussian distribution under a growing prime cutoff can be forced by the representation itself and inherited by the deterministic prime-log orbit. A positive result that survives the fully matched control would therefore isolate information not explained by ordinary prime-frequency geometry, coordinate weighting, or continuous vertical mixing.

Failure is also informative: it would continue converting visually compelling prime-phase phenomena into explicit null mechanisms and prevent downstream research from treating generic low-prime memory as evidence for a zeta-specific source.

## Decisive test

Freeze before inspection the support/support-growth law, coordinate weights or prewhitening, sampling rule, normalization, zeta-facing observable, matched control, statistic, and decision rule.

If the proposed observable is a fixed bounded continuous functional of an additive weighted prime-phase field with square-summable weights under continuous vertical averaging, first test whether the support/window law satisfies the `VIS-175` gate `yW_y^2/H->0`. If it does, its full weak population law is already the weighted Haar null and that route is closed unless an additional source variable is present.

For collision or decoder tails, shrinking events, moving thresholds, or unbounded statistics, do not infer a separation from `VIS-175`; use the matched rare-event/moment controls from `VIS-164`--`VIS-168` or prove the stronger quantitative control actually required. For Gram/discrete sampling, use the Gram-clock controls rather than importing the continuous theorem. For nonsquare-summable weights, derive the correct growing-dimensional normalization and null before interpreting any residual.

Accept a new direction only if it proves a predeclared separation from the relevant matched control in a regime where the control has nondegenerate mass and the proposed statistic is not already determined by the prime-torus representation. Kill it if the same effect is reproduced by the matched deterministic/Haar model, appears only after post-selected weights/windows/thresholds, or disappears once the representation and sampling geometry are matched.

## Evidence boundary

`VIS-071` controls fixed finite continuous prime fields. `VIS-160` shows that arbitrary fixed finite-order Gram-phase tests can be reproduced by deterministic clock selectors. `VIS-163`--`VIS-168` control regular decoding, collision tails, moment growth, and growing-support event mass. `VIS-172`--`VIS-173` show that weighted bulk concentration and shape are separate representation effects. `VIS-174` transfers fixed weighted moments to the deterministic continuous prime-log orbit. `VIS-175` strengthens that transfer to the entire weak law for additive square-summable weights under `yW_y^2/H->0`.

None of these results proves a zeta-versus-control source separation. `VIS-175` does not cover Gram-point/discrete sampling, hybrid Euler--Hadamard residuals, zeta-zero variables, nonsquare-summable weights, moving rare-event tests, decoder singularities, or unbounded observables. Its support/window condition is sufficient rather than sharp. These remaining boundaries are research opportunities, not established mechanisms, and no result here implies RH.

## Research disposition

Accepted in further narrowed form. Persistent non-Gaussianity or other bounded-continuous distributional structure of an additive square-summable prime-phase field under the `VIS-175` continuous-window gate is closed as a standalone positive discriminator, including every prime-power taper `p^(-alpha)` with `alpha>1/2` at polylogarithmic support.

Continue only through a predeclared zeta-facing separation that genuinely leaves this matched representation class and also survives the appropriate sampling, rare-event, weighting, and support-growth controls. The strongest current candidates are therefore source-bearing hybrid/zero observables or mathematically explicit boundary regimes excluded by the existing null theorems, not another remapping of the same continuous weighted prime torus.
