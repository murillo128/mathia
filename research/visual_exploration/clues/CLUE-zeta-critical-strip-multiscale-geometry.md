# Does the critical-strip geometry of zeta contain a nontrivial multiscale or fractal signature?

## Observation

The visual-exploration mandate permits fractal, multiscale, complex-plane, phase, zero, spectral, and topological representations, but the most obvious local, nested-scale, overlapping-disk, phase-topological, lowest-order zero-spacing, and elementary persistence structures have collapsed to exact analytic or established mathematical baselines.

`VIS-008` proves that after translation and leading-Taylor normalization, arbitrarily deep zoom around an isolated analytic zero converges to the universal multiplicity monomial. `VIS-011` gives the complete finite-radius reflection parity at a reflection-fixed zero. `VIS-012` identifies fixed finite log-residual jets of order at least two with reciprocal-power moments of the remaining zero configuration.

`VIS-013` through `VIS-015` classify complete concentric circular `log|F|` data as zero sources plus harmonic boundary data. `VIS-016` closes phase on one regular disk: the complete interior zero multiset plus one full boundary modulus determines the holomorphic field up to a single global phase.

`VIS-017` closes the easiest multi-center phase escape: on a connected overlapping disk atlas, local phase ambiguities glue to one global `U(1)` constant. `VIS-018` then closes topological phase circulation: by the argument principle, contour winding and domain-coloring vortex charge are exactly enclosed divisor count.

`VIS-019` closes the simplest source-statistics escape. The joint geometry of two consecutive unfolded zeta gaps and the derived gap-ratio distribution already have a finite-size CUE/arithmetic baseline in the literature. Raw adjacent-gap return maps, their anti-correlation, two-gap density plots, and ratio histograms therefore cannot serve as new source-sensitive geometry merely because they differ from Poisson or from the infinite sine-kernel limit.

`VIS-092` closes the standard one-dimensional persistent-topology escape. For any finite scalar point cloud on the real line, every Vietoris-Rips component is contractible at every scale and the complete `H_0` death multiset is exactly the unordered adjacent-gap multiset. Applied to raw or unfolded zero ordinates, an ordinary Rips/Cech barcode adds no positive-dimensional topology and discards gap order.

`VIS-093` audits the immediate higher-dimensional escape. A fixed-window delay embedding can create genuine loops or higher-dimensional geometry from local gap blocks, but ordinary point-cloud persistence forgets the visitation order of those delay vectors. It depends on at most their unordered window multiset, is exactly invariant under sequence reversal via coordinate reversal, and can identify distinct sequences with the same local `m`-block cloud.

`VIS-094` now closes the simplest static temporal-edge repair. Connecting consecutive `m`-windows and freezing the result as a value-quotiented directed transition multigraph is exactly equivalent to retaining the unordered `(m+1)`-block multiset. The missing datum is the distinguished Eulerian trail through those transition occurrences. If the construction forgets direction again and keeps only the polygonal trace, sequence reversal is once more invisible up to coordinate reversal. Static temporal adjacency therefore advances finite local memory by one block order but does not preserve the nonlocal sequencing of those transitions.

## Research question

After quotienting the universal local zero normal form, reflection parity, finite zero moments, the full Poisson-Jensen source-plus-boundary decomposition, phase/modulus conjugacy, overlap-phase gluing, phase-winding divisor count, the known finite-size two-consecutive-gap spectral statistics, scalar one-dimensional persistence, ordinary fixed-window delay-cloud order loss, and the finite-memory content of a static consecutive-window transition graph, is there a **mesoscopic geometry carrying information not already contained in the zero configuration plus regular holomorphic continuation and its established low-order random-matrix/arithmetic statistics**?

The live targets are now narrower. Plausible candidates are a higher-order or genuinely nonlocal statistic of the zero configuration that survives controls preserving the known one-gap/two-gap laws; an explicitly residual statistic after subtracting a finite-size CUE/arithmetic prediction; an incomplete or sparse measurement coupling whose missing information is quantifiable; a relation between genuinely separated regions requiring an explicit nonlocal bridge; or a topological construction that actually preserves a distinguished trajectory/order, uses a dynamically indexed filtration, adds an independent analytic mark/field value, or lets memory grow in a mathematically material way.

## Why it may matter

This sequence of negative controls turns the original fractal/multiscale intuition into a precise information-accounting question. Rich texture can be generated by local analytic normal form, reflection symmetry, reciprocal zero moments, Green/Poisson propagation, zero entry, harmonic conjugacy, ordinary analytic gluing, divisor topology, finite-size random-matrix spacing geometry, scalar persistence barcodes, higher-dimensional delay clouds that reorganize local windows, and static transition graphs that merely lift the local block inventory by one order.

A surviving visual invariant therefore has to live in **source-specific organization not exhausted by those baselines or in information deliberately absent from the regular local field and fixed finite-memory block statistics**, rather than in another rendering of already-known zero statistics. This makes matched higher-order point-process controls, incomplete-measurement constructions, genuinely path-aware/dynamic topology, independent analytic marks, and nonlocal filtrations the relevant next filters.

## Decisive test

Do not continue searching for independent information in complete concentric `log|F|` shells, local phase/domain coloring, phase relations among overlapping disks, contour/vortex winding numbers, standalone two-consecutive-gap return maps/ratio plots, ordinary one-dimensional Rips/Cech persistence of scalar zero ordinates, ordinary fixed-window delay persistence interpreted as though it retained delay-vector order, or a static value-quotiented consecutive-window transition graph interpreted as though it retained the distinguished trajectory: `VIS-013` through `VIS-019`, `VIS-092`, `VIS-093`, and `VIS-094` classify those channels.

Choose one genuinely different observable and state exactly what information it retains beyond the complete zero configuration, regular holomorphic continuation, established low-order spectral-statistics baseline, and fixed finite-memory block inventories. If it is a statistic of zeros, the control must preserve or explicitly model local density, reflection symmetry, the relevant lower zero moments, ordinary gap distribution, pair correlation, **and the finite-size joint law of two consecutive gaps including the known arithmetic correction when that scale matters**. A mere shuffled-gap or Poisson control is no longer strong enough for an adjacent-spacing claim.

If the candidate uses persistent topology or a trajectory construction, state the underlying metric/graph/path object, filtration, and information carrier explicitly. A scalar ordinate cloud with the ordinary metric is closed by `VIS-092`; an ordinary fixed-dimensional delay cloud is bounded by `VIS-093`; and a static directed graph obtained only by joining consecutive delay values is bounded by `VIS-094` to the `(m+1)`-block multiset. A genuinely nonlocal escape must therefore retain information not recoverable from a fixed local block inventory: for example occurrence-level time indices, a prefix-by-prefix or zigzag/dynamic filtration, an ordered path invariant, a memory scale growing materially with the observation window, an independent mark/analytic field, or a nonlocal relation whose value is not fixed by those local blocks.

If the candidate uses three or more neighboring gaps, long-lag structure, height coupling, conditioning, or a residual field, identify which lower-order information has been removed and which new degree of freedom remains. If it links separated regions, identify the bridge observable explicitly and test whether it is reducible to the union of zero coordinates or to ordinary analytic continuation through supplied intermediate data.

Keep the direction only if the statistic is stable under reasonable representation changes, survives the analytic and low-order-statistics quotients, and separates zeta from matched controls in a statement independent of the image. Kill it if it reduces to Poisson-Jensen bookkeeping, phase retrieval, overlap gluing, argument-principle winding, ordinary zero coordinates, known gap/crowding statistics, finite-size two-gap CUE geometry, the already-modeled arithmetic gap-ratio correction, one-dimensional Rips persistence, unordered fixed-window delay-cloud geometry, fixed-order transition-block graphs, standard point-process correlations, or rendering choices.

## Evidence boundary

`VIS-008`, `VIS-011`–`VIS-018`, `VIS-092`, `VIS-093`, and `VIS-094` are exact analytic/topological/information-loss reductions or negative controls. `VIS-019` is a literature-backed prior-art restriction based on finite-size random-matrix and high-zeta-zero spacing results; it does not prove equality of the zeta-zero process with CUE, nor does it eliminate higher-order arithmetic structure.

`VIS-092` classifies only standard one-parameter Rips/Cech persistence of a scalar real point cloud. `VIS-093` does **not** classify delay embeddings as useless or topologically trivial: it proves only that ordinary persistence of a fixed-window cloud is a function of the unordered window geometry and is reversal-invariant. `VIS-094` likewise does **not** classify temporal or directed methods as useless: it proves that the simplest static consecutive-window transition multigraph is exactly a finite-memory de Bruijn-style block representation and therefore still forgets the distinguished edge traversal.

None of these findings classifies intrinsically marked or field-based topology, occurrence-indexed trajectories, path signatures or other ordered-path invariants, time-varying/zigzag filtrations, nonlocal arithmetic graph filtrations, multiparameter persistence with an independent parameter, or growing-memory regimes that add information beyond a fixed empirical local-block inventory.

None of these results establishes a mesoscopic fractal dimension, a new zero statistic, an RH criterion, or a distinction between zeta zeros and suitably matched higher-order point-process controls. They progressively identify which visually rich constructions are information-preserving repackagings or information-losing quotients rather than independent arithmetic channels.

## Research disposition

Accepted in further narrowed form. **Complete regular holomorphic disk data are closed within one center and across connected overlapping covers; phase winding is closed as divisor count; raw two-consecutive-gap geometry is a finite-size RMT/arithmetic baseline; ordinary scalar Rips/Cech persistence collapses to the unordered adjacent-gap multiset; ordinary fixed-window delay persistence can add local block geometry but forgets delay-vector order; and freezing consecutive delay transitions into a static value-quotiented directed graph adds exactly one local block order while forgetting the distinguished Eulerian trail.** The live question is whether zeta's zero configuration has genuinely higher-order or nonlocal organization that survives controls preserving these known laws, whether an explicitly incomplete/separated measurement geometry exposes a nontrivial bridge, or whether a path-aware/dynamic/marked construction carries information not reducible to a fixed local block inventory.
