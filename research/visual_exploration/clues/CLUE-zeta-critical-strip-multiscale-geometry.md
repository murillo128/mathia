# Does the critical-strip geometry of zeta contain a nontrivial multiscale or fractal signature?

## Observation

The visual-exploration line has progressively closed many visually rich channels that do not add an independent arithmetic information carrier. Infinitesimal zero portraits collapse to the local analytic normal form; complete concentric `log|F|` shells reduce to Poisson-Jensen source plus boundary data; phase/modulus and overlapping-disk phase information collapse to ordinary analytic continuation up to global phase; phase winding is divisor count; raw two-consecutive-gap geometry has a finite-size RMT/arithmetic baseline; and ordinary one-dimensional persistence is exactly the unordered adjacent-gap multiset.

The delay-geometry branch gives a sharper information hierarchy. `VIS-093` shows that ordinary fixed-window persistence forgets visitation order. `VIS-094` shows that freezing consecutive delay transitions as a static value-quotiented graph retains exactly one extra local block order while forgetting the distinguished Eulerian traversal. `VIS-095` and `VIS-096` show that ordinary unaugmented delay-path signatures through level three are still determined by the same oriented local-edge inventory, and in the planar two-delay case level four is also determined by that inventory.

`VIS-097` proves that this boundary is sharp in three delay dimensions: two paths can share endpoints and the complete unordered length-four-block inventory yet differ at signature level four through an exact area-area commutator. Thus three-dimensional level four is a genuine chronology carrier in principle.

`VIS-098` adds the missing matched-null admission gate. If the fixed-length delay windows are pairwise distinct, the exact local-block transition graph is a directed simple path and the exact block-preserving Eulerian reassembly class is a singleton. More generally, repeated states are necessary but not sufficient for exact reassembly ambiguity. Therefore the exact local-four-block quotient is a valid theoretical information boundary, but it is not automatically a usable resampling ensemble for continuous-valued arithmetic data.

The live issue is now **source-specific chronology under a demonstrably nondegenerate matched null**, not merely whether a higher signature can remember order.

## Research question

Does a pre-registered fourth-level or log-signature observable of a zeta-derived three-delay path retain arithmetic organization after removing the local information already classified by `VIS-093` through `VIS-098`, while using a matched control that has genuine alternative realizations?

The first branch point is the exact quotient graph itself. If the source representation admits multiple endpoint-compatible Eulerian traversals with the same exact length-four-block inventory, an exact reassembly null is available. If it admits only one traversal, can a single pre-registered coarsened, approximate-block, distributional, or generative local model create a nondegenerate control without leaking enough lower-order information or representation freedom to manufacture the level-four residual?

The broader visual question remains open as well: after the classified analytic, spacing, persistence, finite-memory, and low-signature quotients, is there any mesoscopic or nonlocal geometry of the zeta zero configuration that survives appropriately matched source controls in a statement meaningful without the rendering?

## Why it may matter

`VIS-097` identifies an exact degree of freedom that is genuinely absent from the complete unordered local-four-block inventory: order-dependent interaction between nonparallel local area modes. That makes level four mathematically worth testing. `VIS-098` prevents a false next step: preserving the exact local block inventory may fix the entire observed path, in which case a nominal “matched ensemble” contains no counterfactuals and cannot test source specificity.

A successful construction would therefore separate three notions that are easy to conflate visually: generic chronology capacity, source-specific chronology, and freedom introduced by the control itself. If a zeta-derived statistic survives a null that is both nondegenerate and demonstrably matched at the admitted lower-information layers, it would expose a concrete higher-order organization not explained by the existing visual reductions. If every practical null either collapses to the original path or leaks the tested signal through its own coarsening/model freedom, that is itself a useful obstruction to this branch.

## Decisive test

Use one coherent source-specificity experiment and freeze its choices before confirmation.

First, fix the exact source representation: unfolding convention, three-delay coordinates, finite window, endpoint treatment, and numerical precision/identity convention. Build the value-quotiented three-window transition multigraph and determine whether the fixed endpoints plus exact edge-occurrence multiset admit more than one Eulerian trail. This is a hard admission gate, not a visualization.

If the exact class is nondegenerate, pre-register one fourth-level tensor coordinate, log-signature coordinate, invariant norm, or low-dimensional projection and compare the source only against exact endpoint- and length-four-block-preserving reassemblies. Also match or condition on the relevant lower spectral/source statistics needed for the claimed interpretation.

If the exact class is a singleton, choose **one** replacement null before inspecting confirmation data: for example a fixed quantization/coarsening, an explicit approximate block-matching tolerance, or a specified higher-order generative local model. Freeze its state identification/tolerance/model class and then measure the leakage it introduces into the exact local-four-block inventory, signature levels one through three, endpoint data, local density, and the lower spectral statistics being treated as nuisance structure. Reject the null if that leakage is large enough to provide an alternative explanation for the proposed level-four separation.

Only after the null passes this admission/calibration stage should the pre-registered level-four statistic be evaluated on untouched windows, heights, or scales. Keep the route only if the same direction-sensitive statistic separates the arithmetic source from the matched nondegenerate controls across confirmation regions and survives equivalent coordinate choices. Kill or narrow it if the effect is reproduced by generic chronology, disappears under confirmation, is determined by lower-order leakage, depends materially on the chosen coarsening/tolerance, or requires retuning the null after observing the residual.

For any alternative topological or multiscale route, state the metric/graph/path/filtration and the information carrier explicitly, then apply the analogous quotient test: identify what information the construction actually retains, construct a nondegenerate control preserving the admitted lower information, and test the residual rather than the visual texture.

## Evidence boundary

`VIS-008` and `VIS-013` through `VIS-019` are analytic, topological, information-loss, or prior-art controls; they do not classify every possible nonlocal zero statistic. `VIS-092` through `VIS-096` exactly bound several persistence, transition-graph, and low-signature constructions by local gap/block information. `VIS-097` proves only that fixed three-delay signature level four can distinguish some alternative assemblies with the same local edge inventory; it does not establish source specificity, reconstruction, or a zeta effect.

`VIS-098` proves a sufficient uniqueness condition for exact local-block reassembly and the corresponding almost-sure genericity statement under absolutely continuous finite-dimensional laws. It does **not** prove exact noncollision for the deterministic zeta gap sequence, invalidate quantized/approximate/generative controls, or show that every repeated-state graph has multiple Eulerian traversals. Any non-exact null must therefore be audited on its own terms; the exact equalities from `VIS-096` do not transfer automatically through approximation.

No finding in this chain establishes a mesoscopic fractal dimension, a new zeta-zero statistic, an RH criterion, or a distinction between zeta and a suitably matched higher-order point-process/control ensemble. This remains an accepted research direction, not evidence of such a distinction.

## Research disposition

Accepted in further narrowed form. The exact information boundary is now clear enough to state the next gate precisely: **before interpreting a three-delay level-four residual, prove that the proposed matched null is nondegenerate at the actual source representation.** Exact local-four-block reassembly is preferred when multiple compatible Eulerian traversals exist. When it is a singleton, one pre-registered approximate/coarsened/generative replacement may be tested only after its lower-level leakage and representation dependence are explicitly calibrated. The next positive result must separate source-specific chronology from both generic chronology capacity and control-induced freedom.
