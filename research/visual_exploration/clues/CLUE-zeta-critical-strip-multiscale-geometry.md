# Does the critical-strip geometry of zeta contain a nontrivial multiscale or fractal signature?

## Observation

The visual-exploration mandate permits fractal, multiscale, complex-plane, phase, zero, spectral, and topological representations, but the most obvious local, nested-scale, overlapping-disk, phase-topological, lowest-order zero-spacing, and elementary persistence structures have collapsed to exact analytic or established mathematical baselines.

`VIS-008` proves that after translation and leading-Taylor normalization, arbitrarily deep zoom around an isolated analytic zero converges to the universal multiplicity monomial. `VIS-011` gives the complete finite-radius reflection parity at a reflection-fixed zero. `VIS-012` identifies fixed finite log-residual jets of order at least two with reciprocal-power moments of the remaining zero configuration.

`VIS-013` through `VIS-015` classify complete concentric circular `log|F|` data as zero sources plus harmonic boundary data. `VIS-016` closes phase on one regular disk: the complete interior zero multiset plus one full boundary modulus determines the holomorphic field up to a single global phase.

`VIS-017` closes the easiest multi-center phase escape: on a connected overlapping disk atlas, local phase ambiguities glue to one global `U(1)` constant. `VIS-018` then closes topological phase circulation: by the argument principle, contour winding and domain-coloring vortex charge are exactly enclosed divisor count.

`VIS-019` closes the simplest source-statistics escape. The joint geometry of two consecutive unfolded zeta gaps and the derived gap-ratio distribution already have a finite-size CUE/arithmetic baseline in the literature. Raw adjacent-gap return maps, their anti-correlation, two-gap density plots, and ratio histograms therefore cannot serve as new source-sensitive geometry merely because they differ from Poisson or from the infinite sine-kernel limit.

`VIS-092` closes the standard one-dimensional persistent-topology escape. For any finite scalar point cloud on the real line, every Vietoris-Rips component is contractible at every scale and the complete `H_0` death multiset is exactly the unordered adjacent-gap multiset. Applied to raw or unfolded zero ordinates, an ordinary Rips/Cech barcode adds no positive-dimensional topology and discards gap order.

`VIS-093` audits the immediate higher-dimensional escape. A fixed-window delay embedding can create genuine loops or higher-dimensional geometry from local gap blocks, but ordinary point-cloud persistence forgets the visitation order of those delay vectors. It depends on at most their unordered window multiset, is exactly invariant under sequence reversal via coordinate reversal, and can identify distinct sequences with the same local `m`-block cloud.

`VIS-094` closes the simplest static temporal-edge repair. Connecting consecutive `m`-windows and freezing the result as a value-quotiented directed transition multigraph is exactly equivalent to retaining the unordered `(m+1)`-block multiset. The missing datum is the distinguished Eulerian trail through those transition occurrences.

`VIS-095` audits the weakest path-aware repair at signature depth two. For the ordinary unaugmented polygonal path through fixed-`m` delay windows, levels one and two are determined by the first/last windows plus the unordered `(m+1)`-block multiset.

`VIS-096` strengthens that obstruction. The complete signature through level three is determined by the same local edge inventory in every fixed delay dimension: a shuffle identity expresses every third-level coordinate through edge-additive polynomial line integrals. In the planar `m=2` case, even level four remains determined by that inventory after chord closure, because the oriented edge current fixes the winding-number function and Boedihardjo–Ni–Qian show that the first four terms of a closed planar log signature are winding determined.

`VIS-097` then proves that this local-edge boundary is sharp in three delay dimensions. It gives two explicit scalar sequences with the same endpoint three-windows and the same unordered length-four-block multiset whose ordinary three-delay paths agree through signature level three but differ at level four. The separating term is an exact area-area commutator: swapping two closed local cycles with nonparallel second-level area tensors changes a fourth-level tensor coordinate by `1/4` while leaving the full local edge inventory unchanged.

Thus **three-dimensional level-four path signature is now known to be a genuine chronology carrier in principle**, not merely an unclassified higher-order enrichment. What remains open is whether any pre-registered level-four/log-signature observable on an arithmetic path carries source-specific structure rather than generic path-order information.

## Research question

After quotienting the universal local zero normal form, reflection parity, finite zero moments, the full Poisson-Jensen source-plus-boundary decomposition, phase/modulus conjugacy, overlap-phase gluing, phase-winding divisor count, the known finite-size two-consecutive-gap spectral statistics, scalar one-dimensional persistence, ordinary fixed-window delay-cloud order loss, the finite-memory content of a static consecutive-window transition graph, and the local-edge collapse of low signature depths, is there a **mesoscopic geometry carrying information not already contained in the zero configuration plus regular holomorphic continuation and its established low-order random-matrix/arithmetic statistics**?

The path-signature branch is now narrower but genuinely open. For fixed three-dimensional delays, the question is no longer whether level four can remember assembly order at all: `VIS-097` proves that it can. The live question is whether an explicitly chosen fourth-level or log-signature statistic of a zeta-derived path detects arithmetic organization after controls preserve the endpoint windows, the relevant local four-block inventory, lower signature levels, local density, and the lower spectral statistics appropriate to the source.

Other plausible candidates remain a higher-order or genuinely nonlocal statistic of the zero configuration that survives controls preserving the known one-gap/two-gap laws; an explicitly residual statistic after subtracting a finite-size CUE/arithmetic prediction; an incomplete or sparse measurement coupling whose missing information is quantifiable; a relation between genuinely separated regions requiring an explicit nonlocal bridge; or a path/topological construction using a dynamically indexed filtration, independent time/analytic mark, growing memory, or another invariant whose dependence on long visitation order can be stated independently of the rendering.

## Why it may matter

This sequence of negative controls turns the original fractal/multiscale intuition into a precise information-accounting question. Rich texture can be generated by local analytic normal form, reflection symmetry, reciprocal zero moments, Green/Poisson propagation, zero entry, harmonic conjugacy, ordinary analytic gluing, divisor topology, finite-size random-matrix spacing geometry, scalar persistence barcodes, higher-dimensional delay clouds that reorganize local windows, static transition graphs that lift the local block inventory by one order, and several low path-signature levels that still integrate only the same oriented local-edge current.

`VIS-097` supplies the first exact escape in this delay-signature chain: in dimension three, fourth-level ordered interaction between local area modes survives quotienting by the complete unordered local edge inventory. This makes level four a legitimate carrier to test, but not evidence of arithmetic structure. The distinction now required is **generic chronology capacity versus source-specific chronology**.

A surviving visual invariant therefore has to live in source-specific organization not exhausted by those baselines or in information deliberately absent from the regular local field and fixed finite-memory block statistics. Matched higher-order point-process controls, occurrence-preserving local-block nulls, incomplete-measurement constructions, genuinely dynamic topology, independent analytic marks, growing-memory regimes, and nonlocal filtrations remain the relevant filters.

## Decisive test

Do not continue searching for independent information in complete concentric `log|F|` shells, local phase/domain coloring, phase relations among overlapping disks, contour/vortex winding numbers, standalone two-consecutive-gap return maps/ratio plots, ordinary one-dimensional Rips/Cech persistence of scalar zero ordinates, ordinary fixed-window delay persistence interpreted as though it retained delay-vector order, a static value-quotiented consecutive-window transition graph interpreted as though it retained the distinguished trajectory, or an **unaugmented fixed-delay signature through level three** interpreted as a nonlocal ordering statistic. In the planar `m=2` delay portrait, do not interpret **level four** that way either: `VIS-013` through `VIS-019` and `VIS-092` through `VIS-096` classify those channels.

For the now-open three-dimensional level-four branch, pre-register one concrete tensor, log-signature, invariant norm, or low-dimensional projection before confirmation. The control must preserve the first/last three-windows and the complete unordered length-four-block multiset, so that any separation necessarily lies beyond the local-edge quotient classified by `VIS-096`. It should also match the relevant lower-order source statistics rather than comparing only against a shuffled or Poisson baseline. Keep the branch only if the same statistic separates the arithmetic source from this matched ensemble across untouched windows/scales and survives equivalent coordinate choices; kill or narrow it if the effect is reproduced by generic Eulerian reassembly, finite-sample path geometry, or lower-order spectral differences.

If the candidate uses persistent topology or another trajectory construction, state the underlying metric/graph/path object, filtration, and information carrier explicitly. A scalar ordinate cloud with the ordinary metric is closed by `VIS-092`; an ordinary fixed-dimensional delay cloud is bounded by `VIS-093`; a static directed graph obtained only by joining consecutive delay values is bounded by `VIS-094` to the `(m+1)`-block multiset; `VIS-095`–`VIS-096` close the unaugmented signature through level three in every dimension and through level four when `m=2`; and `VIS-097` proves that level four for `m=3` escapes this local-block quotient in principle. Any positive arithmetic claim must therefore test source specificity, not merely rediscover this generic chronology capacity.

If the candidate uses three or more neighboring gaps, long-lag structure, height coupling, conditioning, or a residual field, identify which lower-order information has been removed and which new degree of freedom remains. If it links separated regions, identify the bridge observable explicitly and test whether it is reducible to the union of zero coordinates or to ordinary analytic continuation through supplied intermediate data.

Keep the direction only if the statistic is stable under reasonable representation changes, survives the analytic and low-order-statistics quotients, and separates zeta from matched controls in a statement independent of the image. Kill it if it reduces to Poisson-Jensen bookkeeping, phase retrieval, overlap gluing, argument-principle winding, ordinary zero coordinates, known gap/crowding statistics, finite-size two-gap CUE geometry, the already-modeled arithmetic gap-ratio correction, one-dimensional Rips persistence, unordered fixed-window delay-cloud geometry, fixed-order transition-block graphs, the low signature depths classified by `VIS-095`–`VIS-096`, generic level-four chronology already exhibited by `VIS-097`, standard point-process correlations, or rendering choices.

## Evidence boundary

`VIS-008`, `VIS-011`–`VIS-018`, and `VIS-092`–`VIS-097` are exact analytic/topological/information-loss reductions, negative controls, or exact sharpness results. `VIS-019` is a literature-backed prior-art restriction based on finite-size random-matrix and high-zeta-zero spacing results; it does not prove equality of the zeta-zero process with CUE, nor does it eliminate higher-order arithmetic structure.

`VIS-092` classifies only standard one-parameter Rips/Cech persistence of a scalar real point cloud. `VIS-093` does **not** classify delay embeddings as useless or topologically trivial: it proves only that ordinary persistence of a fixed-window cloud is a function of the unordered window geometry and is reversal-invariant. `VIS-094` likewise does **not** classify temporal or directed methods as useless: it proves that the simplest static consecutive-window transition multigraph is exactly a finite-memory de Bruijn-style block representation and therefore still forgets the distinguished edge traversal. `VIS-095` closes the unaugmented signature through level two. `VIS-096` extends the exact local-edge obstruction through level three for every fixed delay dimension and, using a classical planar winding/log-signature theorem, through level four for `m=2`.

`VIS-097` does **not** show that level four is sufficient to reconstruct a general three-delay sequence, that every distinct Eulerian assembly is separated, or that any zeta-derived level-four statistic differs from a matched non-arithmetic control. It proves only the sharper information statement needed here: fixed-`m=3` level four can retain traversal order not determined by the complete unordered length-four-block inventory and endpoints.

None of these findings classifies intrinsically marked or field-based topology, occurrence-indexed trajectories, time-varying/zigzag filtrations, nonlocal arithmetic graph filtrations, multiparameter persistence with an independent parameter, growing-memory regimes, or source-specific behavior of the newly open three-dimensional level-four signature channel.

None of these results establishes a mesoscopic fractal dimension, a new zero statistic, an RH criterion, or a distinction between zeta zeros and suitably matched higher-order point-process controls. They progressively identify which visually rich constructions are information-preserving repackagings, information-losing quotients, or generic chronology carriers rather than independent arithmetic channels.

## Research disposition

Accepted in further narrowed form. **Complete regular holomorphic disk data are closed within one center and across connected overlapping covers; phase winding is closed as divisor count; raw two-consecutive-gap geometry is a finite-size RMT/arithmetic baseline; ordinary scalar Rips/Cech persistence collapses to the unordered adjacent-gap multiset; ordinary fixed-window delay persistence can add local block geometry but forgets delay-vector order; freezing consecutive delay transitions into a static value-quotiented directed graph adds exactly one local block order while forgetting the distinguished Eulerian trail; the unaugmented fixed-delay signature remains determined by the same local oriented-edge inventory through level three in every dimension and through level four for the planar `m=2` portrait; but `VIS-097` proves that three-dimensional level four can genuinely recover assembly order beyond that local inventory.** The live path question is now source specificity: whether a fixed, pre-registered fourth-level/log-signature observable on a zeta-derived delay path retains arithmetic organization after matching the complete local four-block inventory and the relevant lower spectral statistics. The broader question remains whether zeta's zero configuration has any genuinely higher-order or nonlocal organization surviving all established analytic and statistical quotients.
