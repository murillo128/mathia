# VIS-080 — signed Euler-product energy has a positive coprime-ratio support-crowding floor

## Claim

Use the notation of `VIS-079`. For finite prime cutoff `X`, `sigma>0`, and window length `L>0`, let

`T_(X,sigma)(L)=V_(X,sigma)(L)/P_X(2 sigma,0)^2`.

Then the exact signed spectral energy admits the positive primitive-ratio representation

`T_(X,sigma)(L)`
` = sum_(a,b) (ab)^(-2 sigma) sinc^2((L/2) log(a/b))`,

where the sum is over ordered coprime positive integers `a!=b` whose prime factors are all at most `X`. Equivalently, the signed prime-exponent lattice from `VIS-078` is exactly the lattice of reduced ratios of `X`-smooth integers.

On the critical-line parameter `sigma=1/2`,

`T_(X,L):=T_(X,1/2)(L)`
` = sum_(a,b) [1/(ab)] sinc^2((L/2) log(a/b))`.

Every summand is nonnegative. Hence `T_(X,L)` is nondecreasing in the prime cutoff `X` for fixed `L`.

More importantly, ordinary near-diagonal coprime integer pairs already force a deterministic support-crowding floor. Put

`B=floor(X/2)`

and `kappa=sinc^2(1/4)>0`. There is an absolute constant `C` such that, whenever `L>=1` and `B>=2L`,

`T_(X,L)`
` >= [2 kappa/(3 zeta(2) L)] log(B/(2L)) - C/L`.

Consequently, for any joint law `X=X(L)`,

`T_(X(L),L) -> 0`

can hold only if

`(log X(L))/L -> 0`.

Thus exponential support growth `X(L)>=exp(eta L)` with fixed `eta>0` forces nonvanishing normalized signed window energy for all sufficiently large `L`. That persistence is already generated by the dense supply of ordinary reduced ratios near `1`; it is not evidence by itself for exceptional prime-log resonance geometry or a new zeta-specific channel.

**Evidence/status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + ELEMENTARY COPRIME-LATTICE LOWER BOUND + NEGATIVE CONTROL + NO-NOVELTY-CLAIM`.

No sharp asymptotic for `T_(X,L)`, matching upper bound, optimal cutoff threshold, zeta approximation theorem, or RH consequence is claimed.

## 1. Signed prime exponents are reduced smooth ratios

`VIS-078` and `VIS-079` give

`T_(X,sigma)(L)`
` = sum_(k!=0) w_k^2 sinc^2(L lambda_k/2)`,

with

`w_k=prod_(p<=X) p^(-sigma |k_p|)`

and

`lambda_k=sum_(p<=X) k_p log p`.

For an integer vector `k`, define

`a=prod_(k_p>0) p^(k_p)`,

`b=prod_(k_p<0) p^(-k_p)`.

The positive and negative supports are disjoint, so `(a,b)=1`. Both integers are `X`-smooth, and

`lambda_k=log(a/b)`,

`w_k^2=(ab)^(-2 sigma)`.

Conversely, unique factorization sends every ordered coprime `X`-smooth pair `(a,b)` to the unique signed exponent vector `k_p=v_p(a)-v_p(b)`. The zero vector is exactly `a=b=1`. This proves the displayed primitive-ratio sum.

This is an exact reindexing, not an approximation. It also makes positivity and monotonicity in `X` immediate: increasing the prime cutoff only adds admissible nonnegative terms.

## 2. A near-diagonal ordinary-integer family is already inside the support

Specialize to `sigma=1/2`. Take any integer

`1<=b<=B=floor(X/2)`

and any

`1<=h<=floor(b/(2L))`

with `(h,b)=1`. Put `a=b+h`. Then `(a,b)=1`, and for `L>=1`,

`a<=b(1+1/(2L))<=3b/2<=3X/4<X`.

Therefore both `a` and `b` are automatically `X`-smooth simply because they are positive integers below `X`; no special prime-factor construction is being selected.

For these pairs,

`0<(L/2) log(a/b)`
` =(L/2) log(1+h/b)`
` <=(L/2)(h/b)<=1/4`.

Hence their sinc-square factor is at least

`kappa=sinc^2(1/4)`.

Also `a<=3b/2`, so

`1/(ab)>=2/(3b^2)`.

Both ordered orientations `(a,b)` and `(b,a)` occur in the exact sum and have the same sinc-square weight. Their combined contribution is therefore at least

`4 kappa/(3b^2)`

for each admissible `h`.

It follows that

`T_(X,L) >= (4 kappa/3) S(B,L)`,

where

`S(B,L)`
` = sum_(b<=B) b^(-2)`
`   #{1<=h<=floor(b/(2L)) : (h,b)=1}`.

This lower bound uses only an explicitly selected subset of the positive exact spectrum.

## 3. Möbius inversion gives the support-crowding scale

For fixed `b`, elementary Möbius inversion gives

`#{1<=h<=floor(b/(2L)) : (h,b)=1}`
` = sum_(d|b) mu(d) floor(b/(2Ld))`.

Writing `b=dm` and interchanging the finite sums,

`S(B,L)`
` = sum_(m<=B) [floor(m/(2L))/m^2]`
`   sum_(d<=B/m) mu(d)/d^2`.

Because `sum_(d>=1) mu(d)/d^2=1/zeta(2)` converges absolutely,

`sum_(d<=Y) mu(d)/d^2 = 1/zeta(2) + O(1/Y)`.

The resulting truncation error in `S(B,L)` is `O(1/L)`: indeed

`sum_(m<=B) [floor(m/(2L))/m^2] O(m/B) = O(1/L)`.

For the main term,

`sum_(m<=B) floor(m/(2L))/m^2`
` = (1/(2L)) sum_(2L<=m<=B) 1/m + O(sum_(m>=2L) 1/m^2)`
` = (1/(2L)) log(B/(2L)) + O(1/L)`.

Therefore

`S(B,L)`
` = [1/(2 zeta(2) L)] log(B/(2L)) + O(1/L)`.

Combining this with the selected-family lower bound yields

`T_(X,L)`
` >= [2 kappa/(3 zeta(2) L)] log(B/(2L)) - C/L`

for an absolute `C`.

If `(log X(L))/L` fails to tend to zero, then along some subsequence `log X(L)>=eta L` for an `eta>0`. Along that subsequence `B/(2L)` grows exponentially, so the displayed lower bound stays bounded away from zero. This proves the necessary condition

`T_(X(L),L)->0  =>  (log X(L))/L->0`.

## 4. Interpretation and prior-art boundary

The product Poisson/Bohr representation behind `VIS-076`--`VIS-079` is classical harmonic analysis of finite Euler products. The use of Möbius inversion and `sum mu(d)/d^2=1/zeta(2)` is also standard elementary multiplicative number theory. No new general theorem about smooth numbers, coprime lattice points, Fejér kernels, or finite Euler products is claimed.

Gonek and Keating, **Mean values of finite Euler products**, *Journal of the London Mathematical Society* 82:3 (2010), 763--786, DOI `10.1112/jlms/jdq049`, remains the nearest direct analytic-number-theory prior-art boundary for mean-square finite-Euler-product behavior. The present claim does not import an asymptotic from that paper; it isolates an elementary positive subfamily inside Mathia's exact triangular/sinc energy.

The Mathia-specific consequence is a falsification control for the current visual route. Once support grows, persistence of the signed energy can arise from the sheer density of reduced ordinary ratios near `1`. A visual concentration of near-resonances or a nonvanishing `T_(X,L)` in an exponentially growing cutoff regime therefore does not distinguish exceptional prime structure from generic support crowding.

## Boundary and falsification

The floor concerns the exact normalized squared-modulus witness from `VIS-079` and the critical specialization `sigma=1/2`. It does not establish the behavior of other nonlinear witnesses, hybrid prime/zero residuals, adaptively chosen windows, or the same observable under the still-open subexponential regime `(log X)/L->0`.

The lower bound is deliberately non-sharp. It uses only pairs `a=b+h` with both integers at most `X` and `h<=b/(2L)`; many additional `X`-smooth reduced ratios are discarded.

Falsify the exact reindexing by exhibiting a signed exponent vector that does not correspond bijectively to a reduced `X`-smooth ratio, or falsify the floor by finding a step in the selected-pair inclusion, sinc bound, coprime count, or Möbius summation that fails under the stated `L>=1`, `B>=2L` hypotheses.
