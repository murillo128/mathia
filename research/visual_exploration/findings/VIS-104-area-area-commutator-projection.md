# VIS-104 — the three-dimensional area-area commutator subspace gives a canonical level-four chronology projection

## Claim

Let `V=R^3` with an orthonormal basis `e_1,e_2,e_3`, and let `L(V)` be the free Lie algebra embedded in the tensor algebra. Its homogeneous degree-two part is

`L_2(V)=Lambda^2 V`.

Write the standard bivector basis

`u_12=[e_1,e_2]`, `u_13=[e_1,e_3]`, `u_23=[e_2,e_3]`.

The degree-four subspace generated by commutators of degree-two area modes,

`C_AA = [L_2(V),L_2(V)] subset L_4(V)`,

is exactly three-dimensional. A basis is

`B_1=[u_12,u_13]`, `B_2=[u_12,u_23]`, `B_3=[u_13,u_23]`.

With the ordinary word-tensor inner product, these three tensors are mutually orthogonal and satisfy

`||B_1||^2=||B_2||^2=||B_3||^2=8`.

Therefore every fourth-level tensor `T` has a canonical orthogonal area-area projection

`P_AA T = q_1 B_1 + q_2 B_2 + q_3 B_3`,

where

`q_j=<T,B_j>/8`.

For two closed bounded-variation path pieces `A,B`, write their second signature levels as

`S^2(A)=a_1 u_12+a_2 u_13+a_3 u_23`,

`S^2(B)=b_1 u_12+b_2 u_13+b_3 u_23`.

Swapping the pieces changes the fourth-level signature by

`Delta_4 = S^4(A*B)-S^4(B*A) = [S^2(A),S^2(B)]`,

and hence

`Delta_4`
` = (a_1 b_2-a_2 b_1) B_1`
` + (a_1 b_3-a_3 b_1) B_2`
` + (a_2 b_3-a_3 b_2) B_3`.

Thus the first swap-sensitive interaction generated purely by nonparallel local area modes lies entirely in `C_AA`. After choosing an orientation to identify `Lambda^2 R^3` with `R^3`, the three coefficients are the wedge/cross-product coordinates of the two area vectors, and

`||Delta_4||^2 = 8 |a x b|^2`.

For the explicit delay-cycle collision in `VIS-097`, the coefficient vector is

`q(Delta_4)=(1/4,0,-1/4)`,

so `||Delta_4||^2=1`. The previously tested raw tensor coordinate `(1,2,1,3)` sees only the first component `1/4`; it is not the full area-area channel.

**Evidence/status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + CANONICAL-PROJECTION + INFORMATION-BOUNDARY + NO-NOVELTY-CLAIM`.

This supplies a pre-registration-ready low-dimensional degree-four statistic for the accepted chronology clue. It does not establish a zeta-specific effect.

## 1. The area-area subspace is three-dimensional

The degree-two free-Lie space in dimension three has basis `u_12,u_13,u_23`. Because the Lie bracket is alternating, `[L_2,L_2]` is spanned by the three pairwise brackets `B_1,B_2,B_3` above.

Expand, for example,

`B_1=[u_12,u_13]`
` = 1213-1231-2113+2131`
`   -1312+1321+3112-3121`,

where a word such as `1213` abbreviates `e_1 tensor e_2 tensor e_1 tensor e_3`.

Similarly,

`B_2`
` = 1223-1232-2123+2132`
`   -2312+2321+3212-3221`,

and

`B_3`
` = 1323-1332-3123+3132`
`   -2313+2331+3213-3231`.

The three supports are disjoint. Each basis tensor therefore has eight coefficients of magnitude one, so the `B_j` are mutually orthogonal with squared norm eight. In particular they are linearly independent, proving

`dim C_AA=3`.

This gives the explicit orthogonal-projection formula `q_j=<T,B_j>/8` without choosing a Hall/Lyndon coordinate convention.

## 2. Closed-loop swapping lands exactly in `C_AA`

For a closed path piece, `S^1=0`. Chen multiplicativity therefore gives at homogeneous degree four

`S^4(A*B)=S^4(A)+S^4(B)+S^2(A) tensor S^2(B)`,

`S^4(B*A)=S^4(B)+S^4(A)+S^2(B) tensor S^2(A)`.

Subtracting gives

`Delta_4=[S^2(A),S^2(B)]`.

Because a closed path has vanishing first level, the shuffle identity forces its second level to be antisymmetric, so `S^2(A),S^2(B)` lie in `L_2(V)=Lambda^2 V`. Bilinearity then yields the displayed coefficient formula in the `B_j` basis.

If `a=(a_1,a_2,a_3)` and `b=(b_1,b_2,b_3)`, the coefficient triple is

`(a_1b_2-a_2b_1, a_1b_3-a_3b_1, a_2b_3-a_3b_2)`.

Up to the conventional permutation/sign introduced by the Hodge-star identification, this is exactly `a x b`. Since the `B_j` are orthogonal with squared norm eight,

`||Delta_4||^2`
` = 8[(a_1b_2-a_2b_1)^2+(a_1b_3-a_3b_1)^2+(a_2b_3-a_3b_2)^2]`
` = 8 |a x b|^2`.

Hence this swap mechanism vanishes exactly when the two local area bivectors are parallel.

## 3. The projection is coordinate-covariant, while its norm is basis-independent

The construction of `C_AA` does not depend on the displayed basis. For any orthogonal map `g:V->V`, the induced tensor action preserves homogeneous degree, the free-Lie bracket, and the tensor inner product. Therefore

`g(C_AA)=C_AA`

and the orthogonal projector satisfies

`P_AA(gT)=g(P_AA T)`.

Consequently `||P_AA T||` is invariant under orthogonal changes of delay coordinates. The three displayed `q_j` are merely coordinates of this canonical projected tensor in one orthonormal frame.

This is useful for the visual chronology problem because it avoids choosing one raw fourth-order word coordinate after looking at the source. A source test can freeze the whole `C_AA` projection first and use a basis-independent scalar decision rule.

## 4. Relation to the previously tested `(1,2,1,3)` coordinate

For the explicit collision in `VIS-097`, the two closed cycles have area-coordinate vectors

`a=(-1/2,0,-1/2)`,

`b=(-1,-1/2,-1)`.

Therefore

`q(Delta_4)=(1/4,0,-1/4)`.

The raw word coordinate `(1,2,1,3)` equals `q_1=1/4` on this particular area-area commutator because only `B_1` contains the word `1213`.

But the full projection is not determined by that coordinate. For example `B_2` has zero `(1,2,1,3)` coordinate while `P_AA B_2=B_2` is nonzero. Thus the three-dimensional `P_AA` channel is algebraically different from the single scalar tested in `VIS-101` and `VIS-102`.

This does not prove that all three projected directions vary on every exact Markov type. A particular finite type class may still make `P_AA` degenerate; that is an exact admission check for any source experiment.

## 5. A pre-frozen exact-null statistic

Let `ell_4(X)` be the fourth homogeneous log-signature of the three-delay path associated with a word in one exact quantized Markov type from `VIS-099`/`VIS-100`. Define the conditional-null mean

`m_AA = E_null[P_AA ell_4]`.

A basis-independent scalar statistic is then

`T_AA(X)=||P_AA ell_4(X)-m_AA||^2`.

This statistic is fixed before looking at a new confirmation source once the delay embedding and quantizer have been fixed. Its exact conditional distribution can be obtained from exhaustive type-class enumeration or the exact completion-count sampler already validated in `VIS-100`.

By `VIS-103`, every admissible assembly in one exact type class has the same signature levels one through three, so `ell_4-s_4` is a fixed tensor on that type class. Therefore the centered `P_AA` vector, and hence `T_AA`, is identical whether computed from fourth-level signature or log-signature. The substantive new choice is the area-area subspace, not the logarithm.

The admission gate is also exact: if `P_AA ell_4` is constant across the type class, the statistic is degenerate and the representation must be rejected before any source interpretation.

## 6. Prior art and novelty assessment

Chen multiplicativity, the free-Lie structure of log-signatures, and the tensor/Lie bracket are classical. Christophe Reutenauer, *Free Lie Algebras*, Oxford University Press (1993), Chapter 3, is the existing reference used in `VIS-103` for logarithms, exponentials, free-Lie projections, and the Campbell-Baker-Hausdorff series. Ben Hambly and Terry Lyons, **Uniqueness for the signature of a path of bounded variation and the reduced path group**, *Annals of Mathematics* 171:1 (2010), 109–167, DOI `10.4007/annals.2010.171.109`, remains the Mathia source anchor for the bounded-variation signature framework.

A targeted literature check confirms that the log-signature as a Lie series and bracket-based coordinates are standard signature theory. No novelty is claimed for the free-Lie decomposition, the bracket of degree-two areas, the exterior/cross-product interpretation in dimension three, or orthogonal covariance.

The Mathia-specific value is organizational and experimental: the exact `VIS-097` chronology mechanism selects a canonical three-dimensional subspace before another source test, replacing post-hoc fourth-order coordinate search by one algebraically motivated projection with an exact null-degeneracy gate.

## Boundary and falsification

This finding does not establish that:

- all fourth-level chronology information lies in `C_AA`;
- `C_AA` is the only natural or orthogonally invariant degree-four subspace;
- the exact quantized Markov type leaves `P_AA` nondegenerate on every source window;
- the statistic `T_AA` separates zeta zeros from its conditional null;
- a separation from that null would be arithmetic-specific rather than generic higher-order spectral memory;
- any area-area statistic gives an RH criterion.

The algebraic claim is falsified by showing that `B_1,B_2,B_3` are not linearly independent, by exhibiting a closed-loop swap for which the displayed degree-four commutator formula fails, or by showing that the orthogonal action does not preserve `C_AA` and its projector.
