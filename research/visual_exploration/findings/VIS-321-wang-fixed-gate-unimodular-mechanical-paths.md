# VIS-321 — natural Wang branches are O(1)-perturbations of fixed-gate unimodular mechanical paths

## Claim

Keep the same-shell Bézout-strip setting of `VIS-319`--`VIS-320`:

`gcd(u,v)=1`, `u != v`,

`M/2 < u,v,q,r <= 2M`,

`h=qv-ur`, `|h|<=D`,

and choose a Bézout pair `a,b` with

`au-bv=1`.

Use the canonical representative with `0<a<v` and `0<=b<u`, and write

`q=ut-bh`, `r=vt-ah`.

Let `m=max(u,v)` and `n=min(u,v)`. Assume the subperiod regime

`2 floor(D)+1 < m`,

which holds eventually at the natural Wang scale `D asymp M/log log M`.

For the at-most-three candidate branches from `VIS-320`,

`t_j(h)=floor(U(h))-j`, `j=0,1,2`,

define

`delta(h)=t_j(h+1)-t_j(h)`.

Then `delta(h)` is independent of `j`, belongs to `{0,1}`, and is the binary rational mechanical word generated by the rotation `{U(h)}`. Consequently every candidate branch in `(q,r)`-coordinates uses only the two step vectors

`s_0=(-b,-a)`,

`s_1=(u-b,v-a)`,

with

`det(s_0,s_1)=1`.

The three candidate paths are parallel translates of one another by integer multiples of `(u,v)`. Over one full rotation period the path has unit lattice monodromy: if `v>u` the period contains exactly `a` copies of `s_1` and has total displacement `(1,0)`; if `u>v` it contains exactly `b` copies of `s_1` and has total displacement `(0,-1)`.

More importantly for the actual Wang strip, its slowly moving branch-admission threshold can be frozen at a fixed threshold at only `O(1)` cost. Put

`T_j(h)=clip_[0,1](W(h)-j)`

so that the exact branch mask is

`B_j(h)=1[{U(h)} < T_j(h)]`.

Since `VIS-320` gives `W(h)=W(0) +/- h/(uv)`, one has

`|T_j(h)-T_j(0)| <= |h|/(uv)`.

On any interval `|h|<=D` satisfying the subperiod condition, the number of indices for which the moving mask differs from the frozen mask

`B_j^0(h)=1[{U(h)} < T_j(0)]`

is at most

`1 + floor(2D/n)`.

At the natural Wang scale `D=O(M/log log M)` with `n>M/2`, this is eventually at most one mismatch per branch. Thus the complete admissible Wang strip differs from three fixed-gate translates of one rational mechanical lattice path by only `O(1)` points.

The remaining natural-window obstruction is therefore not motion of the strip width or branch threshold. Up to a bounded correction, it is simultaneous primality along a fixed interval coding of a subperiod rational mechanical path whose two step vectors form a unimodular basis.

**Evidence/status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + REPRESENTATION-REDUCTION + RATIONAL-MECHANICAL/CHRISTOFFEL PRIOR ART + NEGATIVE CONTROL + NO-NOVELTY-CLAIM`.

No prime-pair counting estimate, discrepancy theorem for the subperiod word, Wang natural-window estimate, or stronger RH criterion is proved here.

## 1. Canonical Bézout coordinates make the rotation increment positive

Because `gcd(u,v)=1`, there is a unique `a` with

`1<=a<v`, `au == 1 (mod v)`.

Then

`b=(au-1)/v`

is an integer with `0<=b<u`, and `au-bv=1`.

If `v>u`, `VIS-320` shows that the active upper boundary is

`U(h)=2M/v+(a/v)h`.

If `u>v`, it is

`U(h)=2M/u+(b/u)h`.

Hence in either case

`U(h)=alpha+(c/m)h`

for a constant `alpha`, where `m=max(u,v)`, `0<c<m`, and `gcd(c,m)=1`. Specifically `c=a` when `v>u` and `c=b` when `u>v`.

Therefore

`floor(U(h+1))-floor(U(h)) in {0,1}`.

Subtracting the same fixed `j` from both terms shows that the increment is the same for all three candidate branches.

## 2. The candidate branch is a two-step unimodular digital line

For any branch `j`, let

`(q_j(h),r_j(h))=(u t_j(h)-bh, v t_j(h)-ah)`.

If `delta(h)=0`, then

`(q_j(h+1),r_j(h+1))-(q_j(h),r_j(h))=(-b,-a)=s_0`.

If `delta(h)=1`, the increment is

`(u-b,v-a)=s_1`.

The determinant of these two steps is exactly

`det(s_0,s_1)`
` = (-b)(v-a)-(-a)(u-b)`
` = au-bv`
` = 1`.

So the two digital-line steps themselves form an oriented unimodular lattice basis. No arithmetic information has been projected away by passing from the thin strip to this path representation.

Moreover

`t_j(h)=t_0(h)-j`,

so

`(q_j(h),r_j(h))=(q_0(h),r_0(h))-j(u,v)`.

The at-most-three candidate branches are therefore not three unrelated moving sequences. They are parallel lattice translates of one and the same two-step mechanical path.

## 3. Full-period monodromy is exactly one lattice unit

The binary increment word

`delta(h)=floor(alpha+c(h+1)/m)-floor(alpha+ch/m)`

has exactly `c` ones in every complete period of length `m`.

When `v>u`, `m=v` and `c=a`. The total branch displacement across one period is

`a s_1+(v-a)s_0`
` = (1,0)`.

Indeed the first coordinate is

`a(u-b)-(v-a)b = au-bv = 1`,

while the second is

`a(v-a)-(v-a)a=0`.

When `u>v`, `m=u` and `c=b`. Then

`b s_1+(u-b)s_0=(0,-1)`.

This is the classical rational-mechanical/Christoffel geometry in the present Bézout basis: a period is a primitive digital-line word with unit lattice monodromy. The Wang problem does not observe a whole period, but only a subword of length `2D+1=o(m)`.

## 4. The moving strip mask freezes with a bounded symmetric difference

`VIS-320` gives the exact admission condition

`{U(h)}+j < W(h)`.

Equivalently, after clipping the threshold to the phase interval,

`B_j(h)=1[{U(h)}<T_j(h)]`,

where

`T_j(h)=clip_[0,1](W(h)-j)`.

Clipping is 1-Lipschitz. Since the width is affine with slope `+1/(uv)` or `-1/(uv)`,

`|T_j(h)-T_j(0)| <= |h|/(uv) <= D/(uv)`.

A mismatch between `B_j(h)` and the frozen-threshold mask

`B_j^0(h)=1[{U(h)}<T_j(0)]`

can therefore occur only if the phase `{U(h)}` lies within distance at most `D/(uv)` of `T_j(0)`.

Because `gcd(c,m)=1`, the phases of a complete period are a translated grid with spacing `1/m`. Under

`2 floor(D)+1<m`,

the phases for the consecutive indices `-floor(D),...,floor(D)` are all distinct points of that grid. An interval of length `2D/(uv)` contains at most

`1+floor(2Dm/(uv))`

such points. Since `uv=mn`, this becomes

`1+floor(2D/n)`.

This proves the claimed mismatch bound separately for every branch.

At the natural scale

`D <= C M/log log M`

for a fixed constant `C`, while `n>M/2`. Hence

`2D/n <= 4C/log log M <1`

eventually, and each branch has at most one moving-threshold mismatch. Across all three branches the complete strip differs from its frozen-mask model by at most three points for sufficiently large `M`.

The exact constant is not important for the Wang criterion. The important point is structural: the moving boundary contributes only a bounded perturbation, while the candidate occupancy itself has length `asymp M/log log M`.

## 5. What is still genuinely arithmetic

Freezing `T_j(h)` does not make the prime-pair problem random or equidistributed. The resulting fixed mask still selects a subperiod interval of a rational rotation whose denominator moves with the represented center `(u,v)`, and the selected lattice points must satisfy simultaneous primality of

`q_j(h)=u t_j(h)-bh`,

`r_j(h)=v t_j(h)-ah`.

The local two-prime factor from `VIS-319` remains present, but a theorem is still needed to control how these prime-coordinate exclusions interact with a subword shorter than one full mechanical period.

The present result removes one possible source of complexity: no proof needs to win a nontrivial discrepancy estimate merely to follow the `O(1/(uv))` drift of the branch width. Up to an `O(1)` correction, the gate may be fixed first and the arithmetic studied on the resulting subperiod digital path.

For the occupancy criterion of `VIS-315`--`VIS-316`, adding or deleting `O(1)` candidate points changes the local cell count by only `O(1)`, already at the scale of the explicit leading `1` in

`kappa_M(V) << 1 + A_M M^2/[V log^2 M]`.

Thus any failure large enough to obstruct the Wang natural window must come from the arithmetic occupancy/phase coherence of the fixed-gate path, not from the affine threshold drift itself.

## Prior-art and novelty boundary

Binary rational mechanical words and their geometric realization as Christoffel paths are classical. Jean-Pierre Borel and Christophe Reutenauer, **On Christoffel classes**, *RAIRO - Theoretical Informatics and Applications* 40:1 (2006), 15--27, DOI `10.1051/ita:2005038`, give geometric treatments of classical Christoffel and Sturmian word structure. The identification of a floor-increment word of coprime rational slope with a cyclic/conjugate Christoffel-type digital path is therefore prior art, not a Mathia discovery.

No novelty is claimed for Bézout coordinates, rational rotations, mechanical words, Christoffel words, or the fact that a rational floor-increment word contains a prescribed number of ones per period.

The durable contribution recorded here is narrower: in the exact Wang strip representation of `VIS-319`--`VIS-320`, the two branch steps form a unimodular basis, the three candidate paths are translates of one mechanical path, and the actual affine width drift can be replaced by a fixed branch gate with at most `1+floor(2D/min(u,v))` membership errors per branch. That exact reduction sharpens the remaining Wang proof obligation.

## Boundaries and falsification

The mismatch bound uses the subperiod condition `2 floor(D)+1<m` so that the observed phases are distinct points of one period grid. For wider windows, repeated periods must be counted and the `O(1)` natural-scale conclusion need not hold in this form.

The eventual one-mismatch statement uses the natural scaling `D=O(M/log log M)` with a fixed implied constant and the same-shell bound `min(u,v)>M/2`. It is asymptotic, not a uniform statement for small `M`.

The fixed-gate replacement controls only branch admission. It does not prove that the admitted prime coordinates have the expected `1/log^2 M` density, nor that their local excess is small enough after the Wang taper.

Falsify the exact claim by finding admissible `u,v,M,D` for which the candidate path has a step outside `{s_0,s_1}`, the step determinant is not one, the three branches fail to be lattice translates, the period monodromy differs from the displayed unit vector, or the moving/frozen mask symmetric difference exceeds `1+floor(2D/min(u,v))` on a subperiod interval.
