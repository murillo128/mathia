# VIS-254 — block heat-bath fiber variance certifies nonlinear autocorrelation slowdown

## Claim

Let `mu` be a probability law, let `B` range over a finite family of coordinate blocks with fixed state-independent weights `w_B>=0`, `sum_B w_B=1`, and let

`K_B f = E_mu[f | F_(B^c)]`

be the exact heat-bath update operator that resamples block `B` from its `mu`-conditional law given the complement. Put

`P = sum_B w_B K_B`.

For any nonconstant real `f in L^2(mu)`, define

`D_f = sum_B w_B E_mu[Var_mu(f | F_(B^c))]`

and the normalized fiber-variance ratio

`delta_f = D_f / Var_mu(f)`.

Then:

1. every `K_B` is an orthogonal projection in `L^2(mu)`, hence `P` is a self-adjoint positive-semidefinite contraction;
2. the exact Dirichlet identity is

   `<f-mu f,(I-P)(f-mu f)> = D_f`;

3. under stationarity,

   `Corr(f(X_0),f(X_1)) = 1-delta_f`;

4. the spectral gap, when defined on the mean-zero subspace, obeys

   `gap(P) <= delta_f`;

5. the integrated autocorrelation time of `f`, with value `+infinity` when the correlation series diverges, obeys the exact spectral lower bound

   `tau_int(f) >= 2/delta_f - 1`.

Equality in the last inequality holds whenever the centered `f` is supported on a single eigenspace of `P`; in particular it holds for the exact additive-coordinate eigenmodes of `VIS-253`.

For the uniform unordered-triple `VIS-249` heat-bath chain on the regular log-product level `S_lambda`,

`delta_f = [1/binom(m,3)] sum_(|B|=3) E_mu_lambda[Var(f | X_(B^c))] / Var_mu_lambda(f)`.

The ordinary and logarithmic coordinate contrasts from `VIS-253` have

`delta_linear = 1-rho_m = 2/(m-1)`

and therefore exactly

`tau_int = m-2`.

For the nonlinear cut-averaged tent statistic `bar T`, every inner conditional variance in `delta_(bar T)` is an expectation over the explicit one-dimensional three-coordinate conditional law of `VIS-248`--`VIS-249`. Consequently,

`delta_(bar T) < 2/(m-1)`

is an exact certificate that

`tau_int(bar T) > m-2`.

Such an inequality proves that `bar T` is necessarily slower in integrated autocorrelation than the universal linear scan modes, before any long-lag fit or prime-data comparison is attempted.

**Evidence/status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + CLASSICAL BLOCK-HEAT-BATH DIRICHLET FORM + NONLINEAR CALIBRATION CERTIFICATE + NO-NOVELTY-CLAIM`.

This does not prove the full spectral gap, give an upper bound on `tau_int(bar T)`, identify the slowest nonlinear eigenmode, validate a floating-point sampler, establish a prime residual, or imply RH.

## 1. Exact heat-bath updates are positive orthogonal projections

Fix a block `B` and write `G_B=F_(B^c)` for the sigma-algebra generated by the complement. Exact heat-bath resampling acts on observables as conditional expectation,

`K_B f = E_mu[f | G_B]`.

Conditional expectation is the orthogonal projection from `L^2(mu)` onto the closed subspace of `G_B`-measurable functions. Hence

`K_B^2=K_B`,

`K_B^*=K_B`,

and for every real `g in L^2(mu)`,

`<g,K_B g> = E_mu[(E_mu[g|G_B])^2] >= 0`.

Thus every `K_B` has spectrum in `{0,1}`. A convex combination with fixed nonnegative weights preserves self-adjointness, positivity, constants, and contraction, so `P=sum_B w_B K_B` has spectrum contained in `[0,1]`.

This positivity is stronger than generic reversibility and is what makes the one-step Rayleigh quotient informative about the whole autocorrelation spectrum.

## 2. The Dirichlet form is exactly averaged conditional variance

Let `g=f-mu f`. Centering does not change any conditional variance. For one block,

`<g,(I-K_B)g>`
` = E_mu[g^2] - E_mu[(E_mu[g|G_B])^2]`
` = E_mu[Var_mu(g|G_B)]`
` = E_mu[Var_mu(f|G_B)]`,

where the middle equality is the law of total variance.

Averaging over blocks gives

`<g,(I-P)g>`
` = sum_B w_B E_mu[Var_mu(f|G_B)]`
` = D_f`.

Dividing by `||g||_2^2=Var_mu(f)` gives the Rayleigh quotient

`delta_f = <g,(I-P)g>/<g,g>`.

Since the spectral gap is the infimum of this quotient over nonzero mean-zero observables in its domain,

`gap(P) <= delta_f`.

A small `delta_f` therefore certifies a slow direction carried by the chosen observable; it is not merely a heuristic local-sensitivity score.

## 3. One-step correlation and the integrated-autocorrelation lower bound

Under the stationary chain with transition operator `P`,

`Cov(f(X_0),f(X_1)) = <g,Pg>`.

Using the Dirichlet identity,

`<g,Pg> = Var_mu(f)-D_f`,

hence

`r_1(f)=Corr(f(X_0),f(X_1))=1-delta_f`.

Because `P` is self-adjoint and positive semidefinite, the normalized spectral measure `nu_f` of `g` is supported on `[0,1]`. Therefore

`r_t(f)=int_[0,1] z^t d nu_f(z)`.

If the integrated autocorrelation series converges, Tonelli/spectral summation gives

`tau_int(f)`
` = 1 + 2 sum_(t>=1) r_t(f)`
` = int_[0,1] (1+z)/(1-z) d nu_f(z)`.

If the integral diverges, set `tau_int(f)=+infinity`; the lower bound below is then automatic.

The function

`phi(z)=(1+z)/(1-z)`

is convex on `[0,1)`. Its `nu_f`-mean argument is

`int z d nu_f(z)=r_1(f)=1-delta_f`.

Jensen's inequality therefore yields

`tau_int(f)`
` >= phi(1-delta_f)`
` = (2-delta_f)/delta_f`
` = 2/delta_f - 1`.

Equality occurs when the spectral measure of `g` is concentrated at one eigenvalue. Thus the bound is exact on any nonzero eigenfunction and otherwise measures the minimum autocorrelation compatible with the observed one-step heat-bath dissipation.

## 4. The VIS-253 linear benchmarks are the equality calibration

For the uniform triple scan, `b=3`, and `VIS-253` proves that every admitted ordinary or logarithmic coordinate contrast is an exact eigenfunction with

`rho_m=(m-3)/(m-1)`.

For any such contrast `f`,

`delta_f=1-rho_m=2/(m-1)`.

The general lower bound becomes

`tau_int(f) >= 2/[2/(m-1)] - 1 = m-2`.

Because these contrasts are exact eigenfunctions, equality holds. The fiber-variance ratio therefore reproduces the already-proved `VIS-252`--`VIS-253` calibration exactly rather than introducing a second unrelated notion of mixing scale.

This supplies a useful implementation cross-check: a correct exact triple heat-bath implementation should agree simultaneously on the direct lag-correlation law and on the averaged conditional-variance Rayleigh quotient for the linear benchmark families.

## 5. `bar T` gets a nonlinear certificate from the exact three-gap fibers

For the current uniform unordered-triple chain,

`D_(bar T)`
` = [1/binom(m,3)] sum_(|B|=3) E_mu_lambda[Var_mu_lambda(bar T | X_(B^c))]`.

`VIS-249` shows that, once the complement is fixed, the selected triple has fixed sum and product and its exact normalized conditional is precisely the one-dimensional `VIS-248` discriminant law. Thus the inner conditional mean and second moment of `bar T` can be evaluated on the exact block fiber by one-dimensional integration over that law; no approximate high-dimensional conditional model is needed for the inner variance.

The outer `mu_lambda` expectation and `Var_mu_lambda(bar T)` still require global target integration or a validated stationary sampler at the intended high-dimensional parameters. The identity does not make the operational problem disappear. It does, however, turn one part of the nonlinear-mixing question into a scalar target-law diagnostic with an exact interpretation.

If

`delta_(bar T) < delta_linear = 2/(m-1)`,

then

`tau_int(bar T) >= 2/delta_(bar T)-1 > m-2`.

This proves a nonlinear slowdown relative to both exact additive-coordinate benchmark families. It does not identify whether the cause is narrow conductance, curvature of the conditioned level, a localized spectral mode, or another nonlinear mechanism.

Conversely, `delta_(bar T) >= 2/(m-1)` does **not** prove that `bar T` mixes on the linear `m-2` scale. A spectral mixture can have the same or larger one-step dissipation while retaining enough mass near one to make the full integrated autocorrelation substantially larger. The separated-start and long-lag checks in the accepted clue remain necessary.

## 6. Prior art and novelty boundary

The representation of block heat-bath Dirichlet forms by conditional covariances is classical. Pietro Caputo and Daniel Parisi, **Block Factorization of the Relative Entropy via Spatial Mixing**, *Communications in Mathematical Physics* 388 (2021), 793--818, DOI `10.1007/s00220-021-04237-1`, equation (1.5), writes the weighted block heat-bath Dirichlet form as a sum of conditional covariances; the `f=g` specialization is exactly the conditional-variance identity used above.

Random-scan Gibbs/heat-bath reversibility and spectral diagnostics are likewise classical and already anchored in `VIS-249`--`VIS-253` by Tierney and modern random-scan Gibbs literature.

No new general heat-bath, Poincare, spectral-gap, or autocorrelation theorem is claimed. The durable Mathia-specific point is the combination of the classical block Dirichlet identity with the explicit `VIS-248`--`VIS-249` one-dimensional triple fibers and the exact `VIS-253` linear equality calibration. That combination gives the active nonlinear `bar T` observable a directly interpretable pre-lag slowdown certificate inside the exact alpha-free quotient.

## Boundaries and falsification

The identities require **exact** heat-bath conditional expectation operators and fixed state-independent block weights. A Metropolis-within-Gibbs step, approximate root/CDF sampler, adaptive scan, or state-dependent triple-selection rule is a different operator and needs its own analysis.

The positivity argument is operator-specific. Reversibility alone does not force spectrum in `[0,1]`, so the Jensen lower bound in this form must not be transferred blindly to a generic reversible chain with negative spectrum.

The diagnostic is a statement under the stationary target `mu_lambda`. Estimating its outer expectations from an unvalidated or insufficiently mixed chain can bias `delta_f`; the exact inner fiber law does not cure that problem.

A small `delta_(bar T)` certifies large integrated autocorrelation for `bar T`, but a large value does not certify rapid mixing. The result supplies a lower bound, not a convergence theorem or effective-sample-size guarantee.

Falsify the core identity by producing a qualifying exact fixed-weight heat-bath block update for which `K_B` is not the conditional-expectation projection or for which `<f,(I-K_B)f>` differs from the corresponding conditional variance. Falsify the `VIS-249` specialization by showing that its selected-triple conditional is not the `VIS-248` law or that the implemented scan is not the exact uniform heat-bath operator assumed here.
