# VIS-381 — bounded output orientation requires low commutator-mode alignment

## Claim

Continue with the finite-dimensional source-packet setup of `VIS-380`. Let

`H_1,...,H_m`

be normalized Hermitian source coordinates, let

`K_H = {X : [X,H_j]=0 for every j}`

be their joint commutant, and let the joint commutator Laplacian on Hermitian matrices be

`Delta_H = sum_j ad_(H_j)^* ad_(H_j)`.

For a Hermitian Gram tangent `G`, write

`epsilon_G^2 = sum_j ||[G,H_j]||_F^2 = <G, Delta_H G>_F`.

Let `C=C(H_1,...,H_m)` be any bounded positive rational output admitted in `VIS-380`, and let

`omega(C)=lambda_max(C)-lambda_min(C)`

be its spectral spread.

For every threshold `tau>0`, let `P_0` be the orthogonal spectral projection of `Delta_H` onto its kernel `K_H`, let `P_(0,tau]` project onto the positive eigenvalues in `(0,tau]`, and let `P_(tau,infty)` project onto eigenvalues larger than `tau`. Then

`||[G,C]||_F`
` <= omega(C) ( ||P_(0,tau] G||_F + epsilon_G/sqrt(tau) ).`

Equivalently, whenever `omega(C)>0`,

`||P_(0,tau] G||_F`
` >= ||[G,C]||_F / omega(C) - epsilon_G/sqrt(tau).`

Thus the gap-collapse condition isolated in `VIS-380` is not by itself enough for a bounded output to retain order-one orientation. The Gram tangent must carry non-negligible mass in the collapsing low positive commutator modes.

In particular, for a sequence of source packets and outputs with

`omega(C_T) <= Omega < infinity`,

`||[G_T,C_T]||_F >= c > 0`,

and `epsilon_(G_T) -> 0`, choose any threshold sequence `tau_T -> 0` satisfying

`epsilon_(G_T)/sqrt(tau_T) -> 0`.

Then

`liminf_T ||P_(0,tau_T] G_T||_F >= c/Omega.`

So persistent bounded-output orientation forces not merely small positive eigenvalues of `Delta_(H,T)`, but asymptotically nonzero Gram-tangent projection onto their low spectral subspace.

**Evidence/status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + CLASSICAL SPECTRAL-PROJECTION/POINCARE REFINEMENT + NEGATIVE CONTROL + NO-NOVELTY-CLAIM`.

No sufficiency claim is made: low-mode alignment does not by itself imply useful arithmetic reconstruction, a new zeta criterion, or a mechanism relevant to RH.

## 1. Spectral decomposition separates the relevant collapse from irrelevant soft modes

Because `Delta_H` is positive semidefinite and self-adjoint for the Frobenius inner product, decompose

`G = P_0 G + P_(0,tau] G + P_(tau,infty) G`.

The commutator energy controls the high spectral part exactly in the usual Poincare way:

`epsilon_G^2`
` = <G,Delta_H G>_F`
` >= tau ||P_(tau,infty)G||_F^2`.

Hence

`||P_(tau,infty)G||_F <= epsilon_G/sqrt(tau)`.

This is the point missing from a bare minimum-gap statement. The smallest positive eigenvalue can collapse because some direction becomes an approximate joint commutant, while `G` remains almost orthogonal to that direction. Such a collapse does not automatically create a channel through which the Gram geometry can influence a bounded output.

## 2. The exact joint-commutant component cannot orient any rational source output

If `X in K_H`, then `X` commutes with every source coordinate `H_j`. It therefore commutes with every finite sum and product generated by the `H_j`, and with every inverse appearing in a rational expression whenever that inverse exists. Consequently

`[P_0 G, C]=0`.

Thus

`[G,C]`
` = [P_(0,tau]G,C] + [P_(tau,infty)G,C]`.

For Hermitian `C`, diagonalize `C`. In its eigenbasis,

`||[X,C]||_F^2`
` = sum_(a,b) |lambda_a-lambda_b|^2 |X_(ab)|^2`
` <= omega(C)^2 ||X||_F^2`.

Applying this bound to the two surviving spectral pieces and using the high-mode estimate gives

`||[G,C]||_F`
` <= omega(C)(||P_(0,tau]G||_F + ||P_(tau,infty)G||_F)`
` <= omega(C)(||P_(0,tau]G||_F + epsilon_G/sqrt(tau))`.

Rearranging proves the claimed alignment lower bound.

## 3. Why this is strictly stronger than gap collapse

`VIS-380` showed that when `omega(C)` stays bounded and the joint defect `epsilon_G` tends to zero, order-one output orientation requires the joint commutator gap `mu_H` to collapse. That criterion detects the existence of an approximate-commutant direction.

The present estimate identifies the additional coupling requirement. If for some useful threshold scale `tau_T -> 0` one has

`||P_(0,tau_T] G_T||_F -> 0`

and simultaneously

`epsilon_(G_T)/sqrt(tau_T) -> 0`,

then every bounded-spread rational output satisfies

`||[G_T,C_T]||_F -> 0`,

regardless of whether unrelated eigenvalues of `Delta_(H,T)` collapse below `tau_T`.

Therefore a soft commutator mode is relevant to the packet geometry only when the Gram tangent actually sees it. The correct obstruction is not simply

`mu_H -> 0`,

but a coupled statement involving the low spectral projector of `Delta_H` and the direction `G`.

This also supplies a sharper matched control for numerical or visual experiments. Two source families may have nearly identical minimum commutator gaps while differing completely in how much Gram-tangent mass lies in the corresponding low eigenspaces. A minimum-eigenvalue plot alone would conflate those cases; the projector mass separates them.

## 4. Threshold choice exposes a scale-dependent alignment profile

The estimate holds for every `tau>0`, so no canonical threshold is required. The quantity

`A_G(tau)=||P_(0,tau]G||_F`

is a monotone low-mode alignment profile, while

`epsilon_G/sqrt(tau)`

is the price for discarding the modes above the threshold.

For a sequence with `epsilon_G -> 0`, any choice satisfying

`epsilon_G^2 << tau << 1`

makes the high-mode error vanish while forcing the retained spectral window toward zero. Persistent orientation under bounded `omega(C)` then requires order-one `A_G(tau)` in a genuinely collapsing commutator band.

This scale formulation is more robust than selecting a single lowest eigenvector, whose identity can be unstable under degeneracy, crossings, or small perturbations. Spectral-projector mass is invariant under basis changes inside a low eigenspace and remains well defined when several approximate-commutant directions collapse together.

## 5. Sharpness against the `VIS-380` two-dimensional model

The two-dimensional sharpness example in `VIS-380` places the Gram tangent entirely in the nontrivial commutator mode whose eigenvalue is the collapsing gap. Taking `tau` to include that mode gives

`P_(0,tau]G=G`,

with no high-mode remainder. The new estimate then reduces to the same spectral-spread commutator bound that is attained in that model.

So the alignment refinement does not weaken the sharp example. It explains why that example succeeds: its collapsing commutator mode is not merely present; it is exactly the direction carrying the Gram tangent.

## Prior-art and novelty audit

The ingredients are standard finite-dimensional spectral theory: the positive commutator Dirichlet form from `VIS-380`, orthogonal spectral projections, the Poincare high-mode estimate, and the operator norm bound for `ad_C` under the Frobenius norm. The quantum-expander / noncommutative Poincare literature already cited by `VIS-380` is the appropriate prior-art family for interpreting a commutator gap.

This finding does **not** claim a new abstract theorem about approximate commutants, quantum expanders, or matrix Poincare inequalities. Its contribution inside Mathia is the exact control boundary for the current source-packet geometry: a collapsing joint gap can matter to bounded output orientation only through low commutator modes on which the Gram tangent has nonvanishing projection.

No external theorem beyond those standard spectral facts is imported, and no novelty is claimed for the abstract projector inequality itself.

## Research consequence

The next discriminating question is no longer whether the source tuple merely develops approximate joint-commutant directions. It is whether the collapsing low spectral subspace has a stable mathematical interpretation and whether the actual Gram tangent places persistent mass there under the admissible normalization and representation controls.

That is a separate research question. In particular, one should not yet interpret a large value of `A_G(tau)` as arithmetic signal: the low subspace may still be generated by a universal near-reducibility, duplicated coordinate, normalization artifact, or another source-packet degeneracy. The present result only identifies the coupling that any bounded-orientation mechanism must survive.
