# VIS-305 — a parity-matched Bernoulli support already has `x log log x` reciprocal-spacing cost

## Claim

The `x log log x` local-spacing norm in `VIS-304` is not, by itself, an arithmetic signature of the primes. A density- and parity-matched independent support already reproduces the same order.

Fix a large scale `x` and put

`p=2/log x`.

On the two-sided odd lattice, keep each odd integer independently with probability `p`. This gives mean density `1/log x` per integer, matching the prime-number-theorem density while preserving the parity restriction. For an occupied odd site `q`, let `d(q)` be the distance to the nearest other occupied site, and let

`delta(q)=min_(r occupied, r!=q) |log q-log r|`.

On the central shell `q in [x,2x]`, assign the Wang-scale weight

`b_q^2=((log x)^2/q) min(q/x,x/q)^2`.

Then

`E sum_(q occupied in [x,2x]) b_q^2/delta(q) = Theta(x log log x)`.

More precisely, if `M` is the nearest occupied distance in odd-lattice steps conditional on the origin being occupied, then with `r=(1-p)^2`,

`P(M=m | occupied)=(1-r) r^(m-1)`,

and therefore the physical nearest-neighbor distance `d=2M` satisfies

`E[1/d | occupied]`
` = ((1-r)/(2r)) log(1/(1-r))`
` = (p(2-p)/(2(1-p)^2)) log(1/(p(2-p)))`.

Hence

`E[1_(occupied)/d]`
` = (p^2(2-p)/(2(1-p)^2)) log(1/(p(2-p)))`
` = p^2 log(1/p)(1+o(1))`.

Since there are `(1+o(1))x/2` odd sites in `[x,2x]`,

`E sum_(q occupied in [x,2x]) 1/d(q)`
` = Theta(x log log x / log^2 x)`.

For all but an exponentially negligible event the nearest gap is `o(x)`, and on `q in [x,2x]` the logarithmic spacing obeys

`delta(q)^(-1) asymp q/d(q)`.

Because the Wang taper is bounded above and below by positive constants on this shell, `b_q^2 asymp (log x)^2/x`, so the previous expectation becomes

`E sum b_q^2/delta(q)=Theta(x log log x)`.

Consequently there also exist deterministic realizations of this matched support for which the absolute weighted local-spacing norm is `Omega(x log log x)`.

**Evidence/status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + MATCHED RANDOM CONTROL + NEGATIVE CONTROL + NO-NOVELTY-CLAIM`.

This does not estimate the actual signed endpoint Hilbert form for the primes and does not claim that the true prime-power spacing norm has a matching lower bound. It shows only that the `log log x` cost cannot be removed uniformly by an argument that remembers essentially only prime density, parity, Wang-scale coefficient size, and nearest-neighbor sparsity.

## 1. Exact inverse-gap expectation in the parity control

Condition on an occupied odd lattice site. Moving one odd-lattice step changes the integer by `2`. Let `L` and `R` be the numbers of odd-lattice steps to the nearest occupied site on the left and right. Independence gives geometric tails

`P(L>=m)=P(R>=m)=(1-p)^(m-1)`.

Thus for `M=min(L,R)`,

`P(M>=m)=(1-p)^(2(m-1))=r^(m-1)`,

so

`P(M=m)=(1-r)r^(m-1)`.

Therefore

`E[1/(2M)]`
` = ((1-r)/2) sum_(m>=1) r^(m-1)/m`
` = ((1-r)/(2r)) log(1/(1-r))`.

Substituting `1-r=p(2-p)` yields the exact displayed formula. For `p->0`, it is `p log(1/p)(1+o(1))` conditional on occupancy and `p^2 log(1/p)(1+o(1))` per odd site after multiplying by the occupancy probability.

With `p=2/log x`, the reciprocal-gap sum over a shell of `Theta(x)` odd sites therefore has expectation

`Theta(x log log x/log^2 x)`.

The logarithm comes from the harmonic contribution of the possible small nearest-neighbor gaps in an ordinary sparse Bernoulli process. No arithmetic correlation is needed to create it.

## 2. Conversion to Wang's logarithmic-frequency norm

For `q in [x,2x]`, suppose `d(q)<=x/2`. Monotonicity of `log` and the mean-value theorem give absolute constants `c,C>0` such that

`c d(q)/q <= delta(q) <= C d(q)/q`.

The complementary event `d(q)>x/2` has probability exponentially small in `px`, hence its expected contribution is negligible compared with the scale above.

On the same shell,

`1/2 <= min(q/x,x/q) <= 1`,

and `q asymp x`. Thus

`b_q^2/delta(q) asymp (log x)^2/d(q)`

on the overwhelmingly dominant event. Summing and taking expectation gives

`E sum_(q occupied in [x,2x]) b_q^2/delta(q)`
` = Theta(x log log x)`.

This is exactly the order of the support-sensitive upper norm in `VIS-304` at its central dyadic shell.

## 3. What this rules out

`VIS-304` left open whether its bound

`sum_(q prime power) a_q^2/delta_q << x log log x`

could be improved simply by a sharper treatment of nearest-neighbor sparsity. The Bernoulli control shows that **density plus parity cannot supply such a gain**: the same `log log x` order is already generated by independent sparse occupancy.

Equivalently, any uniform `o(x log log x)` theorem for a class broad enough to contain this control must use additional hypotheses absent here. For the actual prime-power support those hypotheses would have to encode genuinely arithmetic spacing correlations, stronger local congruence structure, or some other source-specific information.

This does not make the weighted Hilbert norm sharp for Wang's actual coefficients. More importantly, the mean-square remainder is a signed endpoint Hilbert form, whereas `sum a_q^2/delta_q` is an absolute majorant. The present control therefore redirects the live question toward either exact-prime arithmetic correlations or phase cancellation in the endpoint form itself.

## Prior art and evidence boundary

Harald Cramér, **On the order of magnitude of the difference between consecutive prime numbers**, *Acta Arithmetica* 2 (1936), 23–46, DOI `10.4064/aa-2-1-23-46`, is the classical source of the independent Bernoulli model for prime occurrence. The odd-lattice probability `2/log x` used here is only a local parity-matched variant chosen so that the mean density per integer is `1/log x`.

William Banks, Kevin Ford, and Terence Tao, **Large prime gaps and probabilistic models**, *Inventiones Mathematicae* 233 (2023), 1471–1518, DOI `10.1007/s00222-023-01199-0`, emphasizes that naive independent Cramér models miss genuine modular biases of the primes and develops a sieve-aware probabilistic model. That limitation is essential here: this control is deliberately too weak to reproduce prime-pair singular series or higher local congruence structure.

The geometric nearest-gap calculation above is elementary and no novelty is claimed for Bernoulli/geometric waiting-time identities. The durable Mathia conclusion is only the negative control: the `log log x` reciprocal-spacing scale survives after prime arithmetic has been replaced by parity-matched independent occupancy.

No lower bound for the true prime-power spacing sum, no Hardy–Littlewood prime-pair statement, no lower bound for Wang's signed Hilbert remainder, and no stronger RH criterion is claimed.

## Research consequence

The moving upper-source branch should no longer spend effort trying to remove the `log log x` factor from the absolute local-spacing norm using only density/parity-level spacing information. The live alternatives are narrower:

- exploit arithmetic correlations of the **actual** prime-power support that are absent from the Bernoulli control; or
- bypass the absolute spacing norm and prove cancellation in the signed endpoint Hilbert form.

A matched construction attaining `H` scale for the signed endpoint form near `H asymp x log log x` would still be decisive in the opposite direction; this finding does not provide one.
