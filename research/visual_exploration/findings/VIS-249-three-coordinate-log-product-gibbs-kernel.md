# VIS-249 — three-coordinate log-product Gibbs conditionals are explicit and span the regular tangent space

## Claim

Continue the symmetric-Dirichlet log-product quotient of `VIS-247` and the exact three-gap audit law of `VIS-248`. Let

`S_lambda={x_i>0 : sum_i x_i=1, sum_i log x_i=lambda}`

with `lambda<-m log m`, and let `mu_lambda` denote the alpha-free conditional law

`mu_lambda(dx)=Z(lambda)^(-1) J(x)^(-1) d sigma_lambda(x)`

from `VIS-247`.

Fix any coordinate triple `B={i,j,k}` and condition on all coordinates outside `B`. Write

`s=x_i+x_j+x_k`,

`r=x_i x_j x_k`,

`p=r/s^3`.

Then the full conditional law of the normalized block

`Y=(x_i,x_j,x_k)/s`

given the complement under `mu_lambda` is exactly the `m=3` law of `VIS-248`: if

`Q=Y_iY_j+Y_jY_k+Y_kY_i`,

then, for `0<p<1/27`,

`P(Q in dq | x_(-B), Lambda=lambda)`
` = Z_B(p)^(-1) Delta_p(q)^(-1/2) 1_(q_-(p)<q<q_+(p)) dq`,

where

`Delta_p(q)=q^2-4q^3-4p-27p^2+18pq`.

Conditional on `Q=q`, the unordered normalized block is the root multiset of

`t^3-t^2+q t-p=0`,

and the six label permutations have equal conditional weight away from repeated-root endpoints. Scaling the selected roots by `s` gives the exact block conditional on the original simplex.

Therefore an exact random-scan heat-bath kernel that chooses a triple with fixed state-independent positive probabilities and resamples it from this conditional is reversible with respect to `mu_lambda`, hence preserves the desired alpha-free conditional law.

There is also no local rank obstruction from using only three-coordinate blocks. At every regular point `x in S_lambda`, put `q_l=1/x_l`. Choose indices `a,b` with `q_a!=q_b`. For each `k notin {a,b}`, define a vector supported on `{a,b,k}` by

`d_a^(abk)=q_b-q_k`,

`d_b^(abk)=q_k-q_a`,

`d_k^(abk)=q_a-q_b`.

Then

`sum_l d_l^(abk)=0`,

`sum_l q_l d_l^(abk)=0`,

so `d^(abk)` lies in the tangent space

`T_x S_lambda={v : sum_i v_i=0, sum_i v_i/x_i=0}`.

The `m-2` vectors `d^(abk)` are linearly independent because their `k` coordinates all equal the same nonzero value `q_a-q_b` on distinct coordinates. Since `dim T_x S_lambda=m-2`, they form a basis. Thus the infinitesimal directions generated by regular three-coordinate sum/product-preserving moves span the entire conditioned tangent space.

**Evidence/status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + EXACT BLOCK-CONDITIONAL KERNEL + LOCAL TANGENT-SPANNING + CLASSICAL GIBBS SPECIALIZATION + NO-NOVELTY-CLAIM`.

This does not prove global irreducibility, Harris recurrence, a spectral gap, useful mixing time, numerical sampler accuracy, or a prime residual.

## 1. Conditioning a block fixes exactly its sum and product

Start from the ordinary symmetric Dirichlet density on the simplex,

`f_alpha(x) proportional to product_l x_l^(alpha-1)`.

Fix all coordinates outside `B={i,j,k}`. The simplex constraint fixes the remaining block sum

`s=1-sum_(l notin B) x_l`.

Conditioning additionally on the global log product

`Lambda=sum_l log x_l=lambda`

fixes the block product

`r=exp(lambda) / product_(l notin B) x_l`.

On this block surface the Dirichlet factor contributed by the three free coordinates is

`(x_i x_j x_k)^(alpha-1)=r^(alpha-1)`,

which is constant. The same cancellation that removes `alpha` globally in `VIS-247` therefore removes it from every exact block conditional.

Normalize by `x_l=sY_l` on the three selected coordinates. Then

`Y_i+Y_j+Y_k=1`,

`Y_iY_jY_k=p=r/s^3`.

The current state itself supplies a positive triple of sum one and product `p`, so AM-GM gives `0<p<=1/27`. Equality is the degenerate equal-block case; every non-equal block has `p<1/27` and lies on a regular one-dimensional product level.

Thus the conditional problem is not a new high-dimensional surface-integration problem. Every three-coordinate heat-bath step reduces exactly to the already-audited `m=3` quotient.

## 2. `VIS-248` supplies the complete block conditional

For the normalized three-block, define

`Q=Y_iY_j+Y_jY_k+Y_kY_i`.

`VIS-248` proves that fixed product `p` leaves one unordered coordinate and that its density is

`Delta_p(q)^(-1/2) dq`

between the two positive roots of the cubic discriminant. It also gives the nonsingular reparameterization

`q=c+h cos(theta)`,

under which the conditional measure is proportional to

`[c+h cos(theta)-q_0]^{-1/2} dtheta`,

where `q_0<0` is the third discriminant root.

This gives a one-dimensional numerical target for each block update. A practical implementation may sample `theta` or `q` by deterministic CDF construction, rejection, slice sampling, or another independently validated one-dimensional method; the mathematical target does not depend on that implementation choice.

After drawing `q`, the roots of

`t^3-t^2+q t-p=0`

recover the unordered normalized triple. Symmetry of the original block density makes all six label permutations equiprobable for distinct roots. Repeated-root endpoints have zero conditional mass under the continuous interior law, while `p=1/27` is the single equal-block configuration and yields a degenerate no-move conditional.

A correct block implementation can therefore be audited without high-dimensional reference integration: check the `VIS-248` support/CDF, root reconstruction, permutation symmetry, block sum, and block product.

## 3. The random-scan block kernel preserves the target law

Let `K_B` be the heat-bath update that leaves `x_(-B)` fixed and replaces `x_B` by an independent draw from its exact `mu_lambda` conditional distribution.

Write the disintegration of the target as

`mu_lambda(dx)=mu_(-B)(dz) mu_B(dx_B | z)`.

Then the joint measure for one update is

`mu_lambda(dx) K_B(x,dy)`
` = mu_(-B)(dz) mu_B(dx_B|z) mu_B(dy_B|z)`,

with `y_(-B)=x_(-B)=z`. This expression is symmetric in `x_B` and `y_B`, so `K_B` satisfies detailed balance with respect to `mu_lambda`.

If triples are chosen with fixed state-independent probabilities `w_B>0`, the random-scan kernel

`K=sum_B w_B K_B`

is a convex mixture of reversible kernels and is therefore itself reversible and invariant for `mu_lambda`.

This is only the standard Gibbs/heat-bath argument specialized to the exact conditional supplied above. It does not by itself imply that the chain reaches all of `S_lambda` from every starting point or that convergence is fast enough for the intended calibration.

## 4. Three-coordinate moves have full first-order access

The remaining structural concern is whether preserving the complement in each update confines the chain to a hidden lower-dimensional foliation even locally.

At a regular point, set

`q=(1/x_1,...,1/x_m)`.

Because `lambda<-m log m`, the point is not the barycenter, so `q` is not constant. Choose `a,b` with `q_a!=q_b`.

For every other index `k`, the vector

`d^(abk)=(q_b-q_k)e_a+(q_k-q_a)e_b+(q_a-q_b)e_k`

has zero ordinary sum and zero `q`-weighted sum. These are exactly the linearized constraints for preserving the simplex sum and `Lambda`.

The triple-restricted gradients of these two constraints are independent because `q_a!=q_b`. The implicit-function theorem therefore supplies a local one-dimensional curve varying only coordinates `{a,b,k}` while preserving their sum and product, with tangent proportional to `d^(abk)`.

There are `m-2` such vectors. In any linear combination

`sum_(k notin {a,b}) c_k d^(abk)=0`,

the `k` coordinate is `c_k(q_a-q_b)`, so every `c_k=0`. They are independent. Since the regular level surface has dimension `m-2`, these block directions span its whole tangent space.

The exact block family therefore has full local directional rank everywhere on a regular global level. This removes a simple geometric failure mode in which an exact conditional kernel could still be trapped infinitesimally because its permitted coordinate blocks miss some conditioned directions.

## 5. Representation audit

**Collapse.** Once the complement, block sum, and global `Lambda` are fixed, the symmetric-Dirichlet nuisance parameter disappears completely from the three-coordinate conditional. High-dimensional coarea weighting collapses to the one-dimensional discriminant law of `VIS-248`; no fitted `alpha` or same-sample plug-in parameter is needed for the block target.

**Survives.** The high-dimensional conditional geometry is not reduced to independent three-gap problems. The block parameters `s` and `p` change with the current complement, and the statistic `bar T` still couples many coordinates. The spanning calculation shows that the collection of these local block curves retains all first-order degrees of freedom of the full conditioned surface.

**Mixed.** Full tangent rank is a local statement. It rules out a differential rank defect but not disconnected components, narrow bottlenecks, metastability, poor conductance, or slow mixing. A visually smooth trajectory, stable short trace, or preservation of the constraints would not establish global calibration.

## 6. Prior art and novelty boundary

The Gibbs/heat-bath principle and detailed-balance argument are classical Markov-chain Monte Carlo. Luke Tierney, **Markov Chains for Exploring Posterior Distributions**, *The Annals of Statistics* 22:4 (1994), 1701–1762, DOI `10.1214/aos/1176325750`, is a standard general-state-space reference covering Gibbs and related Markov-chain methods. No new Gibbs theorem is claimed here.

The Dirichlet family and distributions of products of Dirichlet components are also established probability objects. Saralees Nadarajah and Samuel Kotz, **Exact and approximate distributions for the product of Dirichlet components**, *Kybernetika* 40:6 (2004), 735–744, is direct product-distribution prior art already used as the boundary in `VIS-248`.

A targeted literature check found no reason to treat the present construction as a new general MCMC method. The persisted value is Mathia-specific: the exact `VIS-247` quotient admits a concrete three-coordinate Gibbs conditional reducible to the `VIS-248` discriminant audit, and those same coordinate blocks have an elementary full-tangent spanning certificate.

## Boundaries and falsification

The construction assumes the one-parameter symmetric Dirichlet null and exact conditioning on a regular log-product level. It does not transfer unchanged to asymmetric Dirichlet families, mixtures, renewal/hard-core gap models, or another nuisance family.

Random-scan weights must be fixed independently of the current state for the simple mixture reversibility statement above. State-dependent block selection would require its own balance analysis.

The `m=3` discriminant density gives the exact mathematical conditional, not a guarantee that a particular floating-point root finder, inverse CDF, rejection scheme, or constrained sampler realizes it accurately.

Full tangent spanning does not prove global ergodicity or quantify mixing. Falsify the block law by deriving an `alpha`-dependent factor after fixing the complement, block sum, and global product. Falsify reversibility by showing that the proposed exact block conditional is not the regular conditional of `mu_lambda`. Falsify the spanning claim by producing a regular point for which the displayed `m-2` vectors fail either tangent constraint or linear independence.

## Research consequence

The accepted critical-logarithmic-occupancy clue no longer needs an unspecified high-dimensional conditional sampler as a matter of principle. There is an explicit candidate kernel: random-scan exact three-coordinate heat-bath updates, each reducible to the deterministic `VIS-248` one-dimensional audit law.

The next coherent thread is computational rather than another representation derivation. Before looking at a prime residual, implement the frozen block kernel, require the one-block sampler to reproduce the exact `m=3` distribution, then test high-dimensional stationarity and mixing from deliberately separated starting configurations at the actual `m` and `lambda`.
