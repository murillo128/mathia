# VIS-209 — one-cancellation prime-ratio shells form an anchor Gram kernel

## Claim

Keep the autocorrelation-taper setting of `VIS-203` and the exact shell classification of `VIS-204`. Let

`P_y={p<=y: p prime}`

and write the nonnegative prime-ratio amplitudes as

`c_lambda=exp(iHlambda/2)b_lambda`,

with

`b_(-lambda)=b_lambda>=0`

and

`R=R_v(y,H)=sum_(lambda in Lambda_y) b_lambda^2`.

Define the real symmetric anchor matrix `Psi` on `P_y x P_y` by

`Psi_(u,t)=b_(log(u/t))` for `u!=t`,

and

`Psi_(u,u)=0`.

Then every nonzero one-cancellation shell has an exact Gram representation. For distinct primes `u,v<=y`,

`B_(log(u/v))=2(Psi^2)_(u,v)`.

Equivalently, if

`G=Psi^2=Psi Psi^T`,

then `G` is positive semidefinite,

`tr G=R`,

and the complete one-cancellation shell energy is

`E_1`
` := sum_(u!=v) B_(log(u/v))^2`
` =4 sum_(u!=v) G_(u,v)^2`
` =4[tr(G^2)-sum_u G_(u,u)^2]`.

Thus the high-multiplicity prime/prime shells left outside the noncancelling regime are not an arbitrary collection of large fibers. They are exactly the off-diagonal entries of one positive semidefinite Gram kernel generated by the anchor profiles

`t -> Psi_(u,t)`.

Two immediate compactness reductions follow. Define the effective rank

`r_eff(G)=(tr G)^2/tr(G^2)`.

Then

`E_1/R^2`
` =4[1/r_eff(G)-sum_u (G_(u,u)/R)^2]`
` <=4/r_eff(G)`.

Also, since `G=Psi^2`,

`tr(G^2)<=||G||_op tr G=||Psi||_op^2 R`,

so

`E_1/R^2 <=4 ||Psi||_op^2/R`.

Consequently either of the conditions

`r_eff(G)->infinity`

or

`||Psi||_op^2=o(R)`

is sufficient to make the **entire one-cancellation shell energy** `o(R^2)`, before using any sinc attenuation from the finite start window.

For the finite-start Cesaro energy of `VIS-203`, the one-cancellation contribution is therefore bounded by the same quantity:

`sum_(u!=v) B_(log(u/v))^2 sinc^2((L/2)log(u/v))`
` <= E_1`.

The unresolved one-cancellation problem has thus become a concrete spectral-spreading question for `Psi`: whether the anchor profiles distribute their `l^2` mass across sufficiently many singular directions, rather than a problem of controlling the multiplicity of every prime/prime shell separately.

**Evidence/status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + GRAM/OPERATOR REDUCTION + STRUCTURAL FRONTIER + NO-NOVELTY-CLAIM`.

No lower bound on `r_eff(G)`, operator-norm estimate for `Psi`, one-cancellation decay theorem, or improvement of the finite-start horizon is claimed.

## 1. The one-cancellation representations are anchor profiles

`VIS-204` classifies every nonzero one-cancellation shell. If its reduced quotient is `u/v` with distinct primes `u,v`, every representation is obtained from an anchor prime `t`, distinct from `u,v`, in exactly the two orientations

`(lambda,mu)=(log(u/t),log(v/t))`

and

`(lambda,mu)=(log(t/v),log(t/u))`.

The autocorrelation taper of `VIS-203` has

`b_(-lambda)=b_lambda`.

Hence the two orientations have the same amplitude product:

`b_(log(u/t)) b_(log(v/t))`.

With `Psi_(u,u)=0`, anchors `t=u` or `t=v` contribute zero automatically, so the shell amplitude is exactly

`B_(log(u/v))`
` =2 sum_(t in P_y) Psi_(u,t)Psi_(v,t)`
` =2(Psi Psi^T)_(u,v)`.

Because `Psi` is symmetric, `Psi Psi^T=Psi^2`. This proves the claimed shell identity without an inequality and without losing the anchor variable.

The factor `2` is essential: it records the two nonzero orientations surviving the one-prime cancellation. The diagonal `u=v` is not a one-cancellation shell; it corresponds to the zero difference already removed from the finite-start error.

## 2. The normalization is exactly the Gram trace

Every ordered pair of distinct primes `(u,t)` determines exactly one signed prime-ratio frequency

`lambda=log(u/t)`

in `Lambda_y`, and every frequency in `Lambda_y` arises this way. Therefore

`tr G`
` =sum_u sum_t Psi_(u,t)^2`
` =sum_(u!=t) b_(log(u/t))^2`
` =sum_(lambda in Lambda_y) b_lambda^2`
` =R`.

This is important for the normalization: the Gram matrix introduces no auxiliary mass scale. Its trace is precisely the diagonal reference energy already used throughout `VIS-192`--`VIS-208`.

The one-cancellation question is consequently spectral redistribution of the same mass `R`, not a comparison between unrelated normalizations.

## 3. Exact Frobenius identity for one-cancellation shell energy

Distinct ordered prime pairs `(u,v)` correspond injectively to the signed shell frequencies `log(u/v)`. Thus summing over all nonzero prime/prime shells gives

`E_1`
` =sum_(u!=v) [2G_(u,v)]^2`
` =4 sum_(u!=v) G_(u,v)^2`.

Since `G` is real symmetric,

`||G||_F^2=tr(G^2)`.

Separating diagonal and off-diagonal entries yields the exact identity

`E_1=4[tr(G^2)-sum_u G_(u,u)^2]`.

This is stronger structurally than applying Cauchy--Schwarz independently to each high-multiplicity shell. All anchor multiplicities have already been assembled into one positive semidefinite object, and the total shell energy is its off-diagonal Frobenius mass.

The formula also gives a useful caution. Large `tr(G^2)` by itself need not imply large one-cancellation energy, because part of the Frobenius mass may sit on the diagonal. Accordingly the effective-rank condition below is sufficient, not necessary.

## 4. Effective rank and operator norm are sufficient compactness targets

Because `G` is positive semidefinite,

`tr(G^2)=(tr G)^2/r_eff(G)=R^2/r_eff(G)`.

Insert this into the exact identity and divide by `R^2`:

`E_1/R^2`
` =4[1/r_eff(G)-sum_u (G_(u,u)/R)^2]`.

In particular,

`E_1/R^2<=4/r_eff(G)`.

So effective rank tending to infinity is enough to suppress the whole one-cancellation family at once. No pointwise bound on an individual shell multiplicity or individual `B_xi` is then needed.

A second reduction uses the positive eigenvalues of `G`:

`tr(G^2)<=||G||_op tr G`.

Since `G=Psi^2`,

`||G||_op=||Psi||_op^2`.

Therefore

`E_1/R^2<=4||Psi||_op^2/R`.

This identifies an even sharper possible target: prove that no single singular direction of the anchor matrix captures a positive proportion of the total Hilbert--Schmidt mass.

The two criteria are related but should not be conflated with an equivalence. The exact quantity is the off-diagonal Frobenius mass of `G`; effective rank and operator norm merely give clean sufficient controls.

## 5. Explicit arithmetic form of the anchor matrix

For the autocorrelation tapers of `VIS-203`, one may write for distinct primes `u,t`

`Psi_(u,t)`
` =Q_y^(-1)(ut)^(-alpha) rho(H log(u/t))`,

where `rho>=0` is even. Hence the one-cancellation Gram entry is

`G_(u,v)`
` =Q_y^(-2)(uv)^(-alpha)`
`   sum_(t in P_y, t!=u,v)`
`     t^(-2alpha)`
`     rho(H log(u/t)) rho(H log(v/t))`.

Thus the spectral problem is not abstract: `Psi` is a localized kernel on the logarithmic prime coordinate, weighted by `(ut)^(-alpha)`. The one-cancellation shell `u/v` measures overlap between the two anchor profiles centered at `log u` and `log v`.

This representation makes the next obstruction precise. If many prime-centered profiles are nearly parallel, `Psi` can have low effective rank and one-cancellation shell energy may remain macroscopic. If the profiles spread through many approximately independent directions, the Gram energy is forced to dilute.

That dichotomy is a statement about overlap geometry of the actual prime-ratio kernel, not about the raw number of admissible anchors.

## 6. Relation to the sinc-weighted noncancelling result

`VIS-208` controls all noncancelling determinant shells below a fixed fraction of the first one-cancellation scale by combining cumulative incidence with the finite-window sinc kernel. It deliberately stops before prime/prime shells enter because their anchor multiplicity grows with the number of primes.

The present identity addresses exactly that next structural boundary. Once one-cancellation shells enter, their multiplicity can be retained rather than bounded away: the complete anchored sum is the Gram entry `2G_(u,v)`.

For Cesaro averaging over starts, distinct shell frequencies remain orthogonal exactly as in `VIS-203`. Therefore the one-cancellation contribution is

`sum_(u!=v) 4G_(u,v)^2 sinc^2((L/2)log(u/v))`.

Dropping the sinc factor gives `E_1`, so any effective-rank or operator-norm estimate above automatically controls the finite-window contribution. Conversely, sinc attenuation may provide additional gain and can be studied later without changing the Gram identity.

This cleanly separates two mechanisms: `VIS-208` uses geometric attenuation of many bounded-multiplicity four-prime shells, whereas the one-cancellation frontier can first be treated as spectral spreading of a high-multiplicity anchor kernel.

## Prior-art audit

The matrix facts used here are standard finite-dimensional linear algebra: a matrix of pairwise inner products is a positive semidefinite Gram matrix, its squared Frobenius norm is `tr(G^2)`, and `tr(G^2)<=||G||_op tr G` for `G>=0`. Effective rank in the form `(tr G)^2/tr(G^2)` is likewise a standard spectral participation ratio. No novelty is claimed for any of these identities or inequalities.

`VIS-204` already audits the arithmetic classification behind the anchor multiplicity against unique factorization and multiplicative-Sidon terminology. The present finding does not add a new product-representation theorem or prime-correlation theorem. Its Mathia-specific content is the exact assembly of the `VIS-203` autocorrelation-taper amplitudes and the `VIS-204` anchor classification into one Gram kernel whose off-diagonal Frobenius mass is precisely the full one-cancellation shell energy.

No external theorem is needed for that assembly beyond the standard linear-algebra identities above. Any future claim that `r_eff(G)` actually diverges, or that `||Psi||_op^2=o(R)`, requires its own arithmetic/analytic proof and prior-art audit.

## Boundaries and falsification

The exact Gram representation uses the autocorrelation-taper sign structure of `VIS-203`. For a general taper with signed or complex centered amplitudes, the anchor matrix need not be nonnegative or symmetric in this form, and the same positive-semidefinite reduction is not automatic.

The result concerns **exact** one-cancellation shells. Approximate shell bins can mix several distinct prime/prime ratios and require an additional incidence or spectral-resolution argument.

The effective-rank and operator-norm conditions are sufficient only. Small one-cancellation shell energy can also occur when `G` has substantial diagonal Frobenius mass, even if the displayed sufficient conditions are not sharp.

Nothing here proves that the prime anchor profiles are asymptotically orthogonal, weakly correlated, or high rank. The representation exposes that question; it does not answer it.

Falsify the finding by exhibiting a one-cancellation representation not captured by the two anchor orientations of `VIS-204`, by finding a missing factor or orientation in `B_(log(u/v))=2(Psi^2)_(u,v)`, by showing that `tr(Psi^2)` is not `R`, or by invalidating the Frobenius/effective-rank identities after the exact shell dictionary is applied.

## Research consequence

The natural next question is now narrower than "control the high-multiplicity one-cancellation shells." One should estimate the spectral concentration of the explicit prime-anchor kernel `Psi`.

A decisive positive route would prove, in a regime relevant to the finite-start problem, either

`||Psi||_op^2=o(R)`

or another estimate forcing the off-diagonal Frobenius mass of `Psi^2` to be `o(R^2)`. A decisive negative route would construct a coherent family of prime-centered anchor profiles carrying a positive fraction of `R` in bounded spectral rank.

That is a separate mathematical problem and should be attacked in a later invocation rather than extending this one into a new operator-norm campaign.
