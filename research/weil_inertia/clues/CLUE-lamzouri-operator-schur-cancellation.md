# Does the horizontal Schur complement of the Lamzouri operator exactly recover its positive retained Gram operator?

## Observation

The Lean proof of WI-137 deliberately treats the tensor matrix as an arbitrary block matrix, so its
Hilbert--Schmidt identity sees the `V-H` cross block and the `H-H` block only as separate squared
entries.  The source operator has more structure.  Writing the signed Lamzouri synthesis on
`W = V ⊕ H` as

\[
A_F=A_+-KK^*,
\]

where `A_+` is the positive Gram operator generated by the real and `g_z` columns and is supported
entirely on `V`, split the weighted odd synthesis as `K=(K_V,K_H)`.  Then

\[
A_{HH}=-K_HK_H^*,\qquad A_{VH}=-K_VK_H^*.
\]

WI-132/WI-138 imply that the odd residuals are independent modulo `V`; since their number equals
`dim H`, `K_H` is invertible for each finite configuration.  Formal block algebra therefore
suggests the exact operator identity

\[
A_{VV}-A_{VH}A_{HH}^{-1}A_{HV}=A_+.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
A_+=A_{VV}+A_{VH}(-A_{HH})^{-1}A_{HV}.
\]

Thus the cross block and horizontal block are not independent slack reservoirs: after normalizing
by the horizontal block, their contribution reconstructs precisely the negative odd Gram mass
hidden inside `V`.

## Research question

Is this operator-level Schur cancellation exact for the full weighted Lamzouri tensor, and can it
be combined with the WI-137 target `P_U+P_V`, the WI-132 quotient geometry, or source-level trace
and conditioning information to force an additional quantitative cost on a macroscopic collapsing
horizontal sector?

A useful outcome would be an invariant involving

\[
A_{VH}(-A_{HH})^{-1/2}
\]

or the positive Schur correction
`A_{VH}(-A_{HH})^{-1}A_{HV}` whose size cannot simultaneously collapse with the WI-137 slack under
a positive-density off-line population.

## Why it may matter

WI-138 fixes the number of negative directions and WI-139 fixes their asymptotic orientation, but
both still allow all negative eigenvalues to collapse toward zero through singular synthesis
geometry.  The proposed Schur identity attacks that remaining singular geometry at the level of
the original signed Gram factorization: making the horizontal block tiny may require a compensating
normalized cross interaction in `V` rather than allowing both blocks to shrink independently.

If a source-compatible lower bound on that normalized interaction exists, it would feed directly
into the current Lamzouri/inertia bootstrap.  If the identity is exact but admits near-sharp
families with arbitrarily singular `A_HH` and no additional cost, it would instead certify a sharper
barrier for this route.

## Decisive test

Prove the displayed Schur-complement identity directly from the weighted signed synthesis and the
invertibility of the residual odd map `K_H`.  Then rewrite the correction as a positive operator
and compare its trace and spectrum with the known positive retained Gram operator `A_+` and with
the target `2P_U+P_M`.

The direction survives only if this produces a quantitative constraint not already implied by
WI-132, WI-137, or WI-139.  An explicit finite or asymptotic family satisfying the exact Schur
identity while simultaneously making the WI-137 slack small, keeping positive-density off-line
pairs, and allowing the normalized Schur correction to carry no new lower bound kills the proposed
bootstrap.

## Evidence boundary

The displayed operator Schur identity is not proved by `WI137OperatorDistance.lean`; that file has
forgotten the signed synthesis structure needed even to state it.  WI-132 proves a Schur complement
for the odd quotient Gram matrix, WI-138 uses Gram/Schur factorization for determinant and inertia,
and WI-139 controls negative-eigenspace orientation, but none of those findings establishes this
operator-level cancellation or a stronger slack lower bound from it.  No new zero proportion,
uniform spectral gap, or novelty claim is established here.

## Research disposition
Outcome: refuted

Resolved by:
- [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-164-schur-normalization-cancels-horizontal-collapse.md]]

The Schur identity is exact, but the normalization cancels the horizontal singular scale: `A_VH(-A_HH)^(-1/2)` is unitarily equivalent to `K_V`, and the positive correction is exactly `K_V K_V^*`.  The isolated simple off-line pair from WI-140 has `K_V=0` identically while `A_HH` remains invertible for every nonzero depth and the full Lamzouri slack tends to zero under confluence.  Hence the proposed cross-block normalization is not an autonomous anti-collapse charge.  It could only become useful after adding an independent zeta-source lower bound on `K_V` or another invariant not algebraically cancelled by the normalization.
