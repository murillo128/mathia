# Welding boundary: the universal scalar Ramanujan interface is now sharply classified

[[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-041-mrt-endpoint-stability-gives-maximal-interval-l2|WI-041]]--[[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-081-pairwise-lcm-boundary-rank-controls-finite-window-ramanujan-leakage|WI-081]] establish the earlier boundary: conditioned pair dispersion exists, but truncation, positive scalar support/energy, common-refinement and ordinary pairwise-rank shortcuts do not close the exact Yang weighted changing-slope splice. The surviving signs produce an exact finite-window Ramanujan Toeplitz operator.

The scalar operator itself is much more rigid. [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-082-vandermonde-congruence-fixes-signed-scalar-inertia-before-overcompleteness|WI-082]] fixes signed inertia exactly before the primitive-frequency dictionary becomes overcomplete. [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-083-large-prime-ramanujan-blocks-admit-exact-saturated-cancellation|WI-083]] shows the opposite saturated extreme, while [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-084-subwindow-ramanujan-blocks-form-an-exact-toeplitz-basis|WI-084]] and [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-085-finite-window-ramanujan-scalarization-has-exact-alias-quotient|WI-085]] reconcile the regimes: every arbitrary super-window scalar law factors through an exact `N`-coordinate symmetric-Toeplitz alias quotient.

Pairwise transversality is now classified well beyond the existence of a one-third example. [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-086-pairwise-ramanujan-rank-defect-starts-past-both-totient-dimensions|WI-086]]--[[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-088-residual-prime-ramanujan-rank-defect-is-sharply-capped-at-one-third|WI-088]] localize residual prime rank loss and prove the sharp universal one-third ceiling. [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-089-sharp-prime-pairwise-rank-defect-is-confined-to-one-boundary-layer|WI-089]] and [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-090-exact-prime-pairwise-one-third-defect-is-an-opposite-residue-matching|WI-090]] confine exact extremizers to one boundary quotient and opposite mod-3 matchings, while [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-091-opposite-residue-prime-rank-defect-has-exact-triangular-boundary-layer|WI-091]] gives the exact triangular decay away from the extremal boundary.

Large rank defect is also metrically weak. [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-092-sharp-prime-rank-defect-has-bounded-metric-overlap|WI-092]] shows that the whole sharp boundary layer has uniformly bounded total squared canonical overlap, and [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-093-extensive-prime-rank-defect-forces-bounded-metric-overlap|WI-093]] extends this to every residual prime pair with positive-density rank defect. Only `O(1)` directions can retain any fixed nonzero canonical correlation even when the rank defect itself is `Theta(p)`.

[[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-094-extensive-prime-defect-has-vanishing-dyadic-cumulative-coherence|WI-094]] closes the remaining obvious collective rescue of that extensive-defect sector. On one dyadic prime scale each positive-density-defect pair has normalized Frobenius coherence `O_theta(P^{-1})`, and prime sparsity limits the entire adversarially signed edge contribution to `O_theta(1/log P)` of the diagonal Hilbert--Schmidt energy. Many individually weak extensive-defect couplings therefore cannot accumulate into a fixed cancellation fraction merely by summing over all comparable primes.

The territory remains open only for source-faithful information not exhausted by this universal scalar quotient, pairwise geometry and extensive-defect dyadic aggregation: the actual Yang coefficient/modulus restrictions, low- or zero-defect pairs, cross-scale structure, a direct weighted rectangle/incidence theorem, or the exact anisotropic multivariate polynomial representation. Neither large pairwise rank loss nor collective accumulation of its positive-density sector supplies the missing source-specific theorem.
