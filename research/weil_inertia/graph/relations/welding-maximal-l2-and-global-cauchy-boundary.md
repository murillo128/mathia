# Welding boundary: conditioned pair dispersion exists, but lossless cross-conductor assembly remains open

[[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-041-mrt-endpoint-stability-gives-maximal-interval-l2|WI-041]]--[[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-060-retained-w-spectrum-wiener-norm-forces-square-function-control|WI-060]] remove moving-endpoint loss, the invalid across-family Cauchy consumer, the deterministic four-prime local main, fixed-polylog coefficient regimes, pure quotient modes and ordinary marginal MRT as the missing conditioned interface. They also show that the `W`-local pair main is `L^2`-concentrated at subpolynomial conductors while fixed-polylog truncation and Wiener/triangle assembly are insufficient.

The review lifecycle has now changed materially for [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-061-mikawa-pair-ap-dispersion-is-the-conditioned-input|WI-061]]: its former review sidecar was deleted while the corrected canonical finding survived, so the conditioned-input identification is current evidence. Mikawa's classical pair-in-progressions dispersion supplies the residue-conditioned prime-pair square-function interface ordinary MRT lacked, but by itself only gives per-modulus / modulus-weighted control and does not solve the cross-conductor Yang splice.

The newest findings narrow that splice. [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-063-lossless-spectral-sparsification-forces-superpolylog-diagonal-cost|WI-063]] proves independently that no hard, sparse or soft mode selection can retain asymptotically all `W`-local `L^2` energy while keeping the diagonal conductor-weighted cost within any fixed logarithmic power. [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-064-mikawa-errors-form-exact-divisor-martingale-but-one-refinement-still-costs-superpolylog|WI-064]] then finds genuine cross-conductor structure: nested Mikawa errors form an exact divisor martingale and exact conductors become orthogonal coordinates under one common refinement. The obvious one-refinement use still fails because the residue-maximal Mikawa estimate pays the full refinement modulus, which is super-polylogarithmic for lossless retention.

[[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-062-lossless-conductor-truncation-forces-superpolylog-diagonal-hilbert-cost|WI-062]] remains under an open adversarial review. Its challenged specialization is therefore not load-bearing for this relation; the diagonal-sparsification and one-common-refinement boundaries above are supported independently by WI-063 and WI-064.

The Yang--Yang candidate remains neither certified nor refuted. The live source-backed gate is now more precise: exploit the actual divisor-martingale/cross-conductor structure **without** paying either diagonal Wiener/Hilbert cost or one full common-refinement modulus, for example through a vector-valued or residue-averaged pair-AP square function, blockwise refinement theorem, or an equivalent direct conditioned covariance estimate, together with the remaining moving-interval/local-main/parity bookkeeping.
