# Welding boundary: the universal scalar Ramanujan interface is now classified

[[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-041-mrt-endpoint-stability-gives-maximal-interval-l2|WI-041]]--[[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-081-pairwise-lcm-boundary-rank-controls-finite-window-ramanujan-leakage|WI-081]] establish the earlier boundary: conditioned pair dispersion exists, but truncation, positive scalar support/energy, common-refinement and ordinary pairwise-rank shortcuts do not close the exact Yang weighted changing-slope splice. The surviving signs produce an exact finite-window Ramanujan Toeplitz operator.

The scalar operator itself is now much more rigid. [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-082-vandermonde-congruence-fixes-signed-scalar-inertia-before-overcompleteness|WI-082]] fixes signed inertia exactly before the primitive-frequency dictionary becomes overcomplete. [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-083-large-prime-ramanujan-blocks-admit-exact-saturated-cancellation|WI-083]] shows the opposite saturated extreme: prime moduli larger than the window collapse to the two-dimensional span of `I` and `J` and can cancel exactly. [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-084-subwindow-ramanujan-blocks-form-an-exact-toeplitz-basis|WI-084]] and [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-085-finite-window-ramanujan-scalarization-has-exact-alias-quotient|WI-085]] reconcile those regimes: `B_1,...,B_N` form an exact symmetric-Toeplitz basis, and every arbitrary super-window scalar law has a unique `N`-coordinate alias determined by low divisor-Möbius marginals plus one tail moment.

Pairwise transversality is not a generic rescue. [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-086-pairwise-ramanujan-rank-defect-starts-past-both-totient-dimensions|WI-086]] localizes every exceptional rank loss to a precise cyclotomic excess-intersection regime, and [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-087-close-prime-ramanujan-cross-grams-have-an-exact-loewner-bezout-rank-family|WI-087]] constructs an infinite close-prime family where that defect occupies asymptotically one third of the smaller primitive-frequency space.

The territory remains open only for source-faithful information not exhausted by this universal scalar quotient: the actual Yang coefficient/modulus restrictions, a direct weighted rectangle/incidence theorem, genuinely many-modulus signed coherence after centering, or the exact anisotropic multivariate polynomial representation. Neither generic scalar inertia nor universally small pairwise leakage can supply the missing theorem.
