# Conditional moment density and critical-lattice screening

[[research/weil_inertia/graph/weil-inertia|Weil Inertia research graph]]

[[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-456-retracted-moment-transport-leaves-only-a-conditional-density-capstone|WI-456]] separates three obligations: actual arithmetic moment convergence, identification of a determinate positive model with no zero atom, and a source-valid positive-index counting bound. Its pinned primary-source correction withdraws the Yang--Yang unconditional higher-even-moment transport and the density-one consequences. The source notice reports Proposition R without its proof; this projection imports neither that reported theorem nor a general impossibility of higher-moment methods.

For Hermitian matrices of dimension `d_T ~ N_T`, with `N_T -> infinity`, convergence of every fixed empirical moment to a Hamburger-determinate probability measure `nu` with all moments finite and `nu((-infinity,0])=0` gives `n_(<=0)=o(N_T)`. The separate counting input `n_+ <= (N_T+S_T)/2 + o(N_T)`, with `0 <= S_T <= N_T`, then gives `S_T/N_T -> 1`. WI-456 proves this classical conditional implication by tightness, higher-moment tail control and the closed-set weak-convergence inequality. It does not establish these hypotheses for the arithmetic matrices. Its finite-matrix control `diag(-1,1,...,1)` retains one negative direction at every dimension, so density-one control does not exclude every exceptional block.

The sources supply distinct restrictions on that implication:

- [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-001-two-moment-bandwidth-one-barrier|WI-001]] supplies the independent baseline, the counting context and explicit first-two-moment extremizers. Those data alone do not furnish the full moment hypothesis.
- [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-003-cell-truncation-does-not-reduce-moduli-as-written|WI-003]] proves a literal cutoff defect in the pinned manuscripts: a global normalization cannot select cells by modulus. This obstruction survives withdrawal, but does not refute the conditional theorem or every possible arithmetic transport.
- [[research/weil_inertia/findings/WI-006-critical-lattice-screening-is-matrix-equivalent-to-doubles|WI-006]] identifies screened off-line pairs with on-line doubles through the same critical-band compressed operator, exactly on the infinite lattice and with sublinear trace-norm replacement cost for long finite blocks at bounded normalized depth. It obstructs distinguishing the two populations through that observation. It does not obstruct a hypothetical independent arithmetic moment theorem excluding their common aggregate spectral distribution.

[[research/weil_inertia/mind/intuition/MI-079-fixed-order-moment-convergence-removes-density-defect-not-every-negative-direction|The durable conditional synthesis]] retains the distinction between removing normalized nonpositive mass and excluding an individual negative direction. Fixed-order certificates, if independently valid at every order, need no uniform rate in the order for their density-one supremum argument. Neither model certificates nor this limit argument restore the missing arithmetic input.

This is a reconciliation of persisted evidence and scope, not a new moment theorem, an RH implication from density one, or a novelty claim.
