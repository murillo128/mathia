# WI-169 — positive odd-sector flag promotion is anti-aligned and cancels Lamzouri horizontal charge

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + LITERATURE+DERIVED + DECISIVE-NEGATIVE + STRUCTURAL-RIGIDITY + BARRIER`. WI-167 and WI-168 show metrically that extra positive integer-depth flag levels cannot be inserted into Lamzouri's `2/1/0` target for free. The source geometry gives a stronger obstruction for the most natural remaining candidate: promoting any subspace of the horizontal odd quotient `H=W\ominus V` from target depth `0` to positive depth is **strictly anti-aligned with the Lamzouri tensor itself**. The compression of `A_F` to `H` is negative definite, so every nonzero positive operator supported on `H` moves the target farther from `A_F`, not closer.

For an orthogonal projection `P_E` onto `E\subseteq H`, with `e=dim E` and

\[
H_E:=\sum_{z\in Z_+}m_z\|P_Eh_z\|^2,
\]

the target shift has the exact cost

\[
\boxed{
\|\mathcal A_F-(\mathcal D+P_E)\|_{\rm HS}^2
=
\|\mathcal A_F-\mathcal D\|_{\rm HS}^2+e+4H_E,
}
\tag{A}
\]

where `D=P_U+P_V`. Combining (A) with WI-137's complete slack identity gives

\[
\boxed{
\Delta
=
\|\mathcal A_F-(\mathcal D+P_E)\|_{\rm HS}^2
+(2B-e)+4(H_V-H_E).
}
\tag{B}
\]

For the full odd quotient `E=H`, `dim H=k` is exactly the number of distinct off-line conjugate pairs and `H_E=H_V`, so the whole horizontal transversality charge cancels:

\[
\boxed{
\Delta
=
\|\mathcal A_F-(\mathcal D+P_H)\|_{\rm HS}^2
+2B-k.
}
\tag{C}
\]

Thus the natural full off-line-counting `2/1/1` flag target is controlled by Lamzouri's existing deficit with a nonnegative remainder **if and only if** `k\le2B`. The horizontal term `4H_V` cannot fund this promotion: the exact increase in squared target distance consumes it. Any useful positive odd-sector depth therefore requires a population-sized source budget elsewhere, or a genuinely different mixed/signed observable. No unconditional zeta-zero proportion changes in this finding.

## 1. Exact source interface

Use the finite Hilbert-space notation reconstructed from Youness Lamzouri, *A new proof that more than 2/3 of the zeros of the Riemann zeta function are simple and on the critical line*, arXiv:2609.02882v1, Proposition 2.1. The source-generated spaces are

\[
U\subset V\subset W,
\qquad
H:=W\ominus V,
\]

where `V` is spanned by all real generators and the conjugation-even generators `g_z`, while `W` is obtained by adjoining the conjugation-odd generators `h_z`. WI-126 proves from finite exponential independence that

\[
\dim H=k,
\tag{1}
\]

where `k` is the number of distinct non-real conjugate pairs.

WI-137 identifies Lamzouri's two-variable tensor with the self-adjoint operator

\[
\mathcal A_F
=
\sum_{x\in R}m_x f_x\otimes f_x
+2\sum_{z\in Z_+}m_z
 \bigl(g_z\otimes g_z-h_z\otimes h_z\bigr)
\tag{2}
\]

on the canonical finite real Hilbert space, and defines

\[
\mathcal D:=P_U+P_V.
\tag{3}
\]

The exact complete deficit is

\[
\boxed{
\Delta
=
\|\mathcal A_F-\mathcal D\|_{\rm HS}^2+2B+4H_V,
}
\tag{4}
\]

with

\[
B:=A_U-2\dim U\ge0,
\qquad
H_V:=\sum_{z\in Z_+}m_z\|P_Hh_z\|^2\ge0.
\tag{5}
\]

The present result uses no new asymptotic input, no pair-correlation estimate, and no new test function. It is an exact consequence of the source decomposition (2) and the already established identity (4).

## 2. The odd compression has the opposite sign from a positive flag step

Let

\[
r_z:=P_Hh_z.
\tag{6}
\]

Every `f_x` and every `g_z` lies in `V`, hence is annihilated by `P_H`. Therefore the exact compression of (2) to `H` is

\[
\boxed{
P_H\mathcal A_FP_H
=-2\sum_{z\in Z_+}m_z\,r_z\otimes r_z.
}
\tag{7}
\]

The vectors `r_z` span `H`. Indeed, `W` is generated by `V` together with the `h_z`, so projecting the latter to `H=W\ominus V` spans the whole quotient. Since all multiplicities are positive, the operator

\[
C_H:=\sum_zm_z\,r_z\otimes r_z
\tag{8}
\]

is positive definite on `H`. Consequently

\[
\boxed{
P_H\mathcal A_FP_H=-2C_H\prec0
\quad\text{on }H.
}
\tag{9}
\]

This is a compression statement, not a claim that `H` is an invariant spectral subspace of `A_F`. WI-139 shows that the true negative eigenspace approaches `H` near saturation; equation (9) is the separate exact source-side fact needed here.

Now let `E\subseteq H` and let `P_E` be its orthogonal projection. Because `D` vanishes on `H`,

\[
\langle \mathcal A_F-\mathcal D,P_E\rangle_{\rm HS}
=\operatorname{tr}(\mathcal A_FP_E)
=-2\sum_zm_z\|P_Eh_z\|^2
=-2H_E.
\tag{10}
\]

Since `||P_E||_HS^2=e`, exact polarization gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
\|\mathcal A_F-(\mathcal D+P_E)\|_{\rm HS}^2
&=\|\mathcal A_F-\mathcal D\|_{\rm HS}^2
 +\|P_E\|_{\rm HS}^2
 -2\langle \mathcal A_F-\mathcal D,P_E\rangle_{\rm HS}\\
&=\|\mathcal A_F-\mathcal D\|_{\rm HS}^2+e+4H_E,
\end{aligned}
\]

which proves (A). In particular every nonzero positive depth-one shift supported in `H` increases the squared distance by **more than its rank** whenever it captures nonzero odd mass.

## 3. Exact cancellation of the horizontal charge

Subtract (A) from the complete identity (4). Since `H_E\le H_V`, one obtains exactly

\[
\Delta
=
\|\mathcal A_F-(\mathcal D+P_E)\|_{\rm HS}^2
+(2B-e)+4(H_V-H_E),
\]

which is (B). This identifies the available budget for an odd-sector promotion without a triangle inequality or a coarse midpoint estimate.

The full horizontal projection is decisive. Taking `E=H`, equations (1) and (5) give

\[
e=k,
\qquad
H_E=H_V.
\tag{11}
\]

Hence (B) collapses to (C):

\[
\Delta
=
\|\mathcal A_F-(\mathcal D+P_H)\|_{\rm HS}^2+2B-k.
\]

The target `D+P_H` has eigenvalues `2` on `U`, `1` on `V\ominus U`, and `1` on `H`. It is therefore the obvious positive flag-depth attempt to turn the previously unretained odd quotient into one additional retained level. Equation (C) shows that the entire horizontal charge `4H_V` is unavailable to pay for this step: exactly the same source mass appears with the opposite sign in the target-distance expansion.

There are two regimes and no hidden third one. If

\[
2B<k,
\tag{12}
\]

then the distance term alone exceeds `Delta`, so no nonnegative WI-137-type decomposition around the full positive odd target can be paid from the existing finite deficit. If

\[
2B\ge k,
\tag{13}
\]

then (C) itself supplies the nonnegative remainder `2B-k`. In that regime the flag is funded entirely by the `B` budget, not by horizontal transversality.

WI-126 gives the exact source content

\tag{14}

so condition (13) demands a population-sized amount of simple-real leakage, multiplicity excess, or odd mass outside the smaller exceptional span `U`. Merely proving a stronger lower bound for `H_V` cannot make (13) true.

## 5. General positive odd-depth operators are even more anti-aligned

The projection case is a special instance of an exact operator identity. Let `J\succeq0` be any self-adjoint operator supported on `H`, meaning

\[
J=P_HJP_H.
\tag{19}
\]

Define

\[
H_J:=\sum_{z\in Z_+}m_z
\langle Jr_z,r_z\rangle\ge0.
\tag{20}
\]

Equations (7)--(9) give

\[
\langle \mathcal A_F-\mathcal D,J\rangle_{\rm HS}
=-2H_J.
\tag{21}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\|\mathcal A_F-(\mathcal D+J)\|_{\rm HS}^2
=
\|\mathcal A_F-\mathcal D\|_{\rm HS}^2
+\|J\|_{\rm HS}^2+4H_J.
}
\tag{22}
\]

Because `C_H` is positive definite, `H_J>0` for every nonzero positive `J`. Thus `D` is the unique closest target along the entire positive cone supported on `H`: **any** positive odd-depth correction moves strictly uphill in Hilbert--Schmidt distance.

Combining (22) with (4) yields

\[
\boxed{
\Delta
=
\|\mathcal A_F-(\mathcal D+J)\|_{\rm HS}^2
+2B+4H_V-\|J\|_{\rm HS}^2-4H_J.
}
\tag{23}
\]

For a sum of nested odd-sector projections, the nonzero eigenvalues of `J` are positive integers as in WI-168, so `||J||_HS^2` additionally carries the quadratic depth cost. The new point is that the source polarization term has the **wrong sign as well**. Increasing the amount of odd residual mass seen by the added flag makes the available remainder smaller, not larger.

## 6. What this closes, and what remains live

WI-168 leaves open an interaction-triggered flag funded by genuinely new zeta information. \[
\boxed{k\le2B.}
\tag{24}
\]

A theorem implying (24) for every zeta configuration would indeed fund the `2/1/1` target, but (24) is not supplied by Lamzouri's Proposition 2.1 and is false in its abstract finite class by the shallow one-pair control. The result does **not** rule out every longer flag. A signed or non-quantized observable could also lie outside the positive-depth framework. Most importantly, an independent arithmetic/source inequality could add a genuinely new budget not already present in `Delta`. Those possibilities remain within the canonical mandate.

Nor does this result identify the uncertified complement with off-line zeros. Critical-line doubles and other multiplicity branches remain distinct, and `B` explicitly contains several sources of exceptional slack. No density-one or RH conclusion follows from (A)--(24).
