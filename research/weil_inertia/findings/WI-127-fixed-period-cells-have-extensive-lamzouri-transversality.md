# WI-127 — fixed-period density-one cells have extensive Lamzouri transversality

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + LITERATURE+DERIVED + STRUCTURAL-RIGIDITY + BOOTSTRAP-INTERFACE + PRIOR-ART-REDIRECT`. WI-126 isolates an exact nonnegative horizontal remainder in Lamzouri's Hilbert-space proof,

\[
R_H\ge 4\sum_{z\in Z_+}m_z\operatorname{dist}(h_z,V)^2,
\]

but leaves open whether a positive density of off-line pairs can make these distances collectively `o(N)` by global screening. The result below closes one important adversarial class: a **fixed-period, density-one, conjugation-symmetric cell with distinct reciprocal roots cannot screen this remainder when repeated globally**. Its Lamzouri `g/h` system fiberizes to a square Vandermonde matrix, so every omitted anti-invariant `h` direction stays a fixed positive distance from the global `V`-space. Repeating the cell for `M` periods therefore forces `R_H \gg M`, uniformly along Lamzouri's smoothing family approaching the Montgomery--Taylor optimizer.

This rules out the exact fixed-period compensated screens of WI-122--WI-125 as near-extremizers for the newly exposed WI-126 remainder, including the period-five motif that defeats ordinate-count and moving-edge extraction. It does **not** yet improve the unconditional zeta proportion: the constant can collapse for growing period, reciprocal-root collisions, critical-line multiplicity, or a periodic block embedded in an arbitrary external zero reservoir.

## 1. Primary-source interface

Lamzouri, *A new proof that more than 2/3 of the zeros of the Riemann zeta function are simple and on the critical line*, arXiv:2609.02882v1 (2 September 2026), proves Proposition 2.1 for a finite conjugation-invariant multiset `Z`. With `eta` real, even, supported in `(-lambda,lambda)` and normalized by `\widehat{\eta^2}(0)=1`, he sets

\[
f_z(u)=\eta(u)e^{-2\pi iuz},\qquad
 g_z=\frac{f_z+f_{\bar z}}2,\qquad
 h_z=\frac{f_z-f_{\bar z}}{2i}.
\]

The nested space `V` is generated by all real-point vectors together with every conjugation-even `g_z`; the anti-invariant `h_z` directions are added only in the larger space `W`. WI-126 reconstructs Lamzouri's discarded scalar/Bessel terms and proves the exact consequence

\[
\boxed{
R_H\ge4\sum_{z\in Z_+}m_z\operatorname{dist}(h_z,V)^2.
}
\tag{1}
\]

Lamzouri's optimizing family is supported in `I=(-1/2,1/2)`. Write

\[
f_0(x)=\frac{\cos(\sqrt2 x)}{\sqrt2\sin(1/\sqrt2)},
\qquad x\in I,
\]

so `f_0>0` on the closed interval. For an even cutoff `psi_delta` equal to one on `|x|<=1/2-delta`, put

\[
A_\delta=\int_I\psi_\delta(x)^2f_0(x)\,dx,
\qquad
\eta_\delta=\frac{\psi_\delta\sqrt{f_0}}{\sqrt{A_\delta}}.
\tag{2}
\]

Then `A_delta<=1` and Lamzouri lets `delta->0` to recover the Montgomery--Taylor constant. The statement below is uniform for all sufficiently small `delta`, so it survives this same approximation step at fixed cell geometry.

## 2. Fixed-period cell and fiberization

Fix an integer `P>=2`. Let

\[
\mathcal C=\{\zeta_1,\ldots,\zeta_P\}
\]

be a conjugation-invariant set of `P` **simple** labels, taken modulo real translation by `P`, with exactly `k>=1` non-real conjugate pairs. Assume the `P` reciprocal nodes

\[
\omega_j:=e^{-2\pi i\zeta_j/P}
\tag{3}
\]

are pairwise distinct. Equivalently, the labels are distinct modulo `P`. Repeat the cell over the whole real lattice,

\[
\mathcal Z_\infty
=\{\zeta_j+Pn:1\le j\le P,\ n\in\mathbb Z\}.
\tag{4}
\]

Let `V_infty` be the closed span of every repeated real `f` vector and every repeated conjugation-even `g` vector. For

\[
J=(-1/2,-1/2+1/P),
\]

the partition map

\[
(UF)(t)=\bigl(F(t+r/P)\bigr)_{r=0}^{P-1},\qquad t\in J,
\tag{5}
\]

is unitary from `L^2(I)` onto `L^2(J;C^P)`. A translation of the label by `Pn` gives

\[
U f_{\zeta+Pn}(t)
=e^{-2\pi iPn t}\,U f_\zeta(t),
\tag{6}
\]

because `e^{-2\pi inr}=1`. Thus every element of `U(V_infty)` lies almost everywhere in the pointwise span `mathcal V(t)` of the `P-k` base columns consisting of the real `f` columns and the `g` columns. No equality theorem for range functions is needed: this inclusion alone gives

\[
\operatorname{dist}(h,V_\infty)^2
\ge\int_J\operatorname{dist}\bigl(Uh(t),\mathcal V(t)\bigr)^2dt.
\tag{7}
\]

This is the standard finite-fiber mechanism for lattice-generated shift-invariant systems; the line-specific work is the exact `g/h` realization and its consequence for (1).

## 3. The full `g/h` fiber is an invertible Vandermonde system

Before replacing conjugate pairs by `g/h`, the `P x P` fiber matrix of the full base `f` family has entries

\[
F_{rj}(t)
=\eta(t+r/P)e^{-2\pi i\zeta_j(t+r/P)}
=\eta(t+r/P)e^{-2\pi i\zeta_jt}\omega_j^r.
\tag{8}
\]

Hence

\[
\det F(t)
=\left(\prod_{r=0}^{P-1}\eta(t+r/P)\right)
 \left(\prod_{j=1}^{P}e^{-2\pi i\zeta_jt}\right)
 \prod_{i<j}(\omega_j-\omega_i).
\tag{9}
\]

Conjugation invariance gives `sum_j Im(zeta_j)=0`, so the middle factor has modulus one. For each non-real pair, the column change

\[
(f_z,f_{\bar z})\longmapsto(g_z,h_z)
\]

is invertible with determinant modulus `1/2`. If `W(t)` denotes the resulting square matrix whose columns are all real `f` columns, all `g` columns and all `h` columns, then

\[
\boxed{
|\det W(t)|
=2^{-k}|\Delta(\omega)|
 \prod_{r=0}^{P-1}|\eta(t+r/P)|,
}
\tag{10}
\]

where

\[
\Delta(\omega)=\prod_{i<j}(\omega_j-\omega_i)\ne0.
\]

Thus the reciprocal-cell root geometry already used in WI-124--WI-125 is also the conditioning invariant for Lamzouri's even/odd Hilbert decomposition.

## 4. Uniform positive transversality along the Montgomery--Taylor smoothing

Choose

\[
0<\delta_0<\frac1{2P}
\]

and the compact positive-measure interval

\[
E=[-1/2+\delta_0,-1/2+1/P-\delta_0]\subset J.
\tag{11}
\]

For every `0<delta<=delta_0` and every `t in E`, all points `t+r/P` lie in the region where `psi_delta=1`. Therefore the fiber matrix built from (2) obeys

\[
W_\delta(t)=A_\delta^{-1/2}W_0(t),
\tag{12}
\]

where `W_0` is built from `eta_0=sqrt(f_0)`. Since `A_delta<=1`,

\[
\sigma_{\min}(W_\delta(t))
\ge\sigma_{\min}(W_0(t)).
\tag{13}
\]

Equation (10), positivity of `f_0`, and `Delta(omega) != 0` make `W_0(t)` continuously invertible on `E`. Hence

\[
\boxed{
\sigma_*:=\min_{t\in E}\sigma_{\min}(W_0(t))>0.
}
\tag{14}
\]

Now fix one anti-invariant base column `h_j`. The pointwise `mathcal V(t)` is contained in the span of every column of `W_delta(t)` except `h_j(t)`. For any invertible matrix, the distance of one column from the span of all the others is the reciprocal norm of the corresponding row of the inverse and therefore at least the smallest singular value. Consequently

\[
\operatorname{dist}\bigl(h_j(t),\mathcal V(t)\bigr)
\ge\sigma_{\min}(W_\delta(t))
\ge\sigma_*
\qquad(t\in E).
\tag{15}
\]

Combining (7) and (15) gives the fixed positive, smoothing-uniform bound

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{dist}(h_j,V_\infty)^2
\ge |E|\sigma_*^2.
}
\tag{16}
\]

## 5. Finite repeated blocks pay linear horizontal slack

Take `M` consecutive copies of the cell and let `V_M` be Lamzouri's finite `V`-space for those `PM` labels. Since

\[
V_M\subset V_\infty,
\]

distance to `V_M` is no smaller than distance to `V_infty`. Moreover multiplication by `e^{-2\pi iPn u}` is unitary and leaves `V_infty` invariant, so every translate of a fixed `h_j` has the same lower bound (16).

There are `kM` non-real representatives. Therefore WI-126's horizontal remainder satisfies

\[
\boxed{
R_H
\ge4kM|E|\sigma_*^2.
}
\tag{17}
\]

In particular the Lamzouri finite inequality cannot approach equality along longer and longer repetitions of one fixed off-line cell. If all real labels in the cell are simple, then the finite cell has `PM` labels and `(P-2k)M` simple real labels, and the exact WI-126 decomposition gives the explicit separation

\[
\boxed{
Q_M-\bigl(2PM-(P-2k)M\bigr)
\ge4kM|E|\sigma_*^2.
}
\tag{18}
\]

Other nonnegative WI-126 remainders may make the gap larger.

## 6. The WI-122 five-cell compensator is excluded from the Lamzouri equality geometry

WI-122 gives a density-one period-five motif with one mirror pair of normalized horizontal depth `y=arcosh(2)` at ordinate `0` and three simple critical-line labels at ordinates `1/2,3/2,5/2`. In Lamzouri's scaled coordinate, up to the harmless global real sign convention, its reciprocal nodes are

\[
e^{y/5},\quad e^{-y/5},\quad
 e^{-\pi i/5},\quad e^{-3\pi i/5},\quad -1.
\tag{19}
\]

They are pairwise distinct. Hence (17) applies with `P=5,k=1`: the exact periodic continuation has a fixed positive Lamzouri horizontal slack **per cell**.

This materially separates two mechanisms that previously looked aligned. The first-half Bragg packet of WI-125 and the Hilbert transversality charge are not yet quantitatively equated, but the common Vandermonde discriminant (10) identifies a precise algebraic bridge between the reciprocal-cell and Hilbert-space descriptions.

## 7. Boundaries and falsification controls

The theorem is deliberately narrower than a zeta bootstrap.

First, **the period is fixed**. The lower singular-value constant in (14) depends on the cell and on `P`; it may tend to zero along a growing-period sequence. No uniform-in-`P` estimate is proved.

Second, reciprocal-root separation is load-bearing. If an off-line conjugate pair approaches the critical line, its two reciprocal nodes collide and `Delta(omega)->0`; similarly a critical-line double is a repeated root. This is exactly compatible with WI-126: a real double can attain zero horizontal cost. The present result therefore does not collapse the multiple-critical and off-line parts of the uncertified complement into one population.

Third, (17) treats the **global periodic repetition** of the cell. An arbitrary set of additional outside zero vectors enlarges `V` and can reduce the distance of the selected `h` directions. Thus the result does not solve WI-125's selected-block extraction problem and does not rule out an embedded periodic packet canceled by a nonperiodic external reservoir.

Fourth, no pair-correlation support extension, higher trace moment, RH input, or conjectural zero statistic is used. Conversely, no unconditional zeta theorem says the actual exceptional zeros form a fixed-period cell of this type. The result is a structural exclusion of an extremal model, not a numerical improvement to the current simple-critical proportion.

A direct falsifier would be a fixed `P`, distinct-node cell for which the fiber matrix (8) is singular on a positive-measure subset of the common nonvanishing region, or a sequence of finite repetitions whose Lamzouri `h`-to-`V_M` distance per cell tends to zero despite (10). Equations (8)--(17) exclude both possibilities algebraically.

Christina Frederick and Kasso A. Okoudjou, **Finding duality for Riesz bases of exponentials on multi-tiles**, *Applied and Computational Harmonic Analysis* 51 (2021), 104--117, DOI `10.1016/j.acha.2020.10.006`, use square Vandermonde fiber matrices and their singular values in a closely related multi-tiling exponential-basis setting. The Vandermonde determinant, smallest-singular-value bound, and finite-dimensional distance identity used above are classical. No novelty is claimed for those tools.

A targeted search around Lamzouri's September 2026 preprint, periodic exponential systems, shift-invariant fiberization, Vandermonde Riesz bases and conjugation-even/odd decompositions located the classical machinery above but no statement applying it to Lamzouri's `g/h` split or to the fixed-period zeta-screening configurations of WI-122--WI-125. Absence from that search is not evidence of priority and no priority claim is made.

The durable line-specific deduction is the combination: Lamzouri's exact horizontal remainder from WI-126, the periodic density-one exceptional cells already isolated in this line, and classical fiber/Vandermonde theory imply the extensive lower bound (17). This redirects the bootstrap problem away from fixed-period exact screens. A surviving near-extremizer must instead use growing-period/aperiodic geometry, reciprocal-root near-collisions, critical-line multiplicity, or a genuinely nonperiodic external span capable of absorbing the anti-invariant directions.

## 9. Research implication

WI-126 turned off-line defect into the concrete quantity `dist(h_z,V)^2` but left global screening as the central uncertainty. WI-127 shows that the most explicit density-one compensated screens accumulated in WI-122--WI-125 do **not** realize that uncertainty once repeated at fixed period: their own global span cannot absorb the missing anti-invariant directions.

Either derive a period/conditioning estimate that combines the WI-125 uniform first-half alias-energy floor with (10)--(17) and survives `P->infinity`, or construct a growing-period/aperiodic family in which the reciprocal nodes become sufficiently ill-conditioned that the normalized Lamzouri horizontal charge is `o(1)` while the known zeta-side count and support-one constraints remain satisfied.
