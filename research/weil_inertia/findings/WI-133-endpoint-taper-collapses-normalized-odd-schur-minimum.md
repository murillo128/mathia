# WI-133 — admissible endpoint taper collapses the normalized odd Schur minimum on a transversely stable conjugate lattice

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + LITERATURE+DERIVED + DECISIVE-NEGATIVE + STRUCTURAL-RIGIDITY + PRIOR-ART-REDIRECT`. WI-132 converts Lamzouri's horizontal remainder into the square-depth estimate

\[
R_H\ge 4aD_2,
\qquad
a=\lambda_{\min}\!\left(U^*(I-P_V)U\right),
\]

where the normalized odd columns are first exponential divided differences. The exact construction below shows that the scalar minimum eigenvalue `a` is too strong to serve as a density-scale bootstrap invariant. For every fixed nonzero depth `b` and every one of Lamzouri's smooth compactly supported admissible tapers, the density-one conjugate lattice

\[
\{2n+ib,\ 2n-ib:n\in\mathbb Z\}
\]

has finite normalized odd Schur complements `S_M` with

\[
\boxed{\lambda_{\min}(S_M)=O_{b,\eta}(M^{-1})\to0,}
\]

even though WI-127's fixed-period fiber theorem gives a uniform positive lower bound for **every diagonal distance** and hence an extensive total Lamzouri horizontal remainder. Thus collective minimum-eigenvalue collapse can be created purely by the endpoint taper and critical modulation geometry without horizontal screening in the quantity that actually enters Lamzouri's slack.

This materially narrows the WI-132 program. Near-sharpness with macroscopic square depth still forces `a->0`, as WI-132 proves, but the converse heuristic is false: `a->0` can coexist with extensive horizontal charge. A successful defect-to-zero argument must retain an averaged/depth-weighted portion of the Schur spectrum or the actual diagonal trace, rather than lower-bounding every normalized odd direction by one global minimum eigenvalue.

## 1. Primary-source interface and the exact test family

Lamzouri, *A new proof that more than 2/3 of the zeros of the Riemann zeta function are simple and on the critical line*, arXiv:2609.02882v1 (2 September 2026), uses real even functions

\[
\eta\in C_c^\infty((-1/2,1/2)),
\qquad \widehat{\eta^2}(0)=1.
\]

His Lemma 3.2 constructs a family `eta_delta` of exactly this form approaching the Montgomery--Taylor optimizer. WI-126 reconstructs the discarded horizontal slack, and WI-132 defines for non-real representatives `z_j=x_j+iy_j`, `y_j>0`,

\[
u_j=\frac{h_{z_j}}{y_j},
\qquad
S=U^*(I-P_V)U,
\qquad
Uc=\sum_jc_ju_j.
\tag{1}
\]

Fix `b>0` and, for `M>=1`, take the finite conjugation-invariant multiset

\[
Z_M=\{2n+ib,\ 2n-ib:0\le n<M\}.
\tag{2}
\]

There are `M` off-real conjugate pairs, all simple and all at the same positive normalized depth `b`. Let `V_M` be Lamzouri's retained span generated by the `M` even vectors `g_n`, let `U_M` synthesize the normalized odd vectors `u_n=h_n/b`, and put

\[
S_M=U_M^*(I-P_{V_M})U_M.
\tag{3}
\]

Every finite `S_M` is positive definite by WI-132's linear-independence argument. The question is whether its smallest eigenvalue can be bounded below independently of `M`.

## 2. The normalized odd lattice is a weighted Fourier system

For `z_n=2n+ib`, Lamzouri's definitions give

\[
\begin{aligned}
f_{z_n}(x)
&=\eta(x)e^{-4\pi inx}e^{2\pi bx},\\
f_{\bar z_n}(x)
&=\eta(x)e^{-4\pi inx}e^{-2\pi bx},
\end{aligned}
\]

and therefore

\[
\boxed{
u_n(x)
=-\frac{i}{b}\,\eta(x)\sinh(2\pi bx)e^{-4\pi inx}.}
\tag{4}
\]

Write

\[
w_b(x):=\frac{\eta(x)\sinh(2\pi bx)}{b},
\qquad
q_b(x):=|w_b(x)|^2.
\tag{5}
\]

The common modulation spacing is two. Hence a coefficient vector `c=(c_0,...,c_{M-1})` has

\[
\|U_Mc\|_2^2
=\int_{-1/2}^{1/2}
q_b(x)\left|\sum_{n=0}^{M-1}c_ne^{-4\pi inx}\right|^2dx.
\tag{6}
\]

The decisive feature is that the weight vanishes at every concentration point of the spacing-two Dirichlet kernel relevant below. At `x=0`, the zero comes from `sinh(2 pi b x)`; near `x=+/-1/2`, it comes from the admissible compact support of `eta`.

## 3. One explicit unit vector forces `lambda_min(S_M)=O(1/M)`

Take the unit coefficient vector

\[
c^{(M)}=M^{-1/2}(1,\ldots,1).
\tag{7}
\]

Then

\[
K_M(x):=
\left|\sum_{n=0}^{M-1}c_n^{(M)}e^{-4\pi inx}\right|^2
=\frac1M
\frac{\sin^2(2\pi Mx)}{\sin^2(2\pi x)}
\tag{8}
\]

away from the removable lattice points. Since `eta in C_c^infty((-1/2,1/2))`, it vanishes on neighborhoods of the endpoints. At the only remaining zero of the denominator, `x=0`,

\[
q_b(x)
=\eta(0)^2\frac{\sinh^2(2\pi bx)}{b^2}+O(x^3)
=4\pi^2\eta(0)^2x^2+O(x^3),
\]

so

\[
\frac{q_b(x)}{\sin^2(2\pi x)}
\]

has a finite removable limit at zero and is integrable on `(-1/2,1/2)`. Define the finite constant

\[
C_{b,\eta}:=
\int_{-1/2}^{1/2}
\frac{q_b(x)}{\sin^2(2\pi x)}\,dx<\infty.
\tag{9}
\]

Using `sin^2(2 pi M x)<=1` in (6)--(8) gives the completely explicit estimate

\[
\boxed{
\|U_Mc^{(M)}\|_2^2
\le \frac{C_{b,\eta}}{M}.}
\tag{10}
\]

Because an orthogonal projection is a contraction,

\[
0\preceq S_M
=U_M^*(I-P_{V_M})U_M
\preceq U_M^*U_M.
\]

Therefore the same test vector yields

\[
\boxed{
\lambda_{\min}(S_M)
\le (c^{(M)})^*S_Mc^{(M)}
\le\frac{C_{b,\eta}}M.}
\tag{11}
\]

In particular, no positive lower bound for the WI-132 scalar `a` can hold uniformly along this fixed-depth density-one family for any fixed admissible Lamzouri taper.

The mechanism is the finite-section form of a classical weighted-Fourier/Toeplitz fact: a modulation block can concentrate coefficient energy where the multiplication symbol is small. No novelty is claimed for Dirichlet/Fejer concentration, Toeplitz compression, or weighted Fourier lower-bound failure. The line-specific content is what this does to the normalized Lamzouri odd Schur complement.

## 4. The same family has extensive horizontal transversality

The collapse in (11) is **not** a Lamzouri screen. The infinite continuation of (2) has the fixed fundamental cell

\[
\{ib,-ib\}\pmod 2.
\tag{12}
\]

It is exactly a `P=2`, `k=1` instance of WI-127. Its reciprocal nodes are

\[
e^{\pi b},\qquad e^{-\pi b},
\]

which are distinct for every `b>0`. WI-127 therefore supplies, for Lamzouri's smoothing family and all sufficiently small smoothing parameters, a constant

\[
d_b>0
\tag{13}
\]

independent of the lattice translate and of `M` such that

\[
\operatorname{dist}(h_n,V_\infty)^2\ge d_b.
\tag{14}
\]

Here `V_infty` is the retained span of all even lattice vectors. Since `V_M subset V_infty`,

\[
(S_M)_{nn}
=\operatorname{dist}(u_n,V_M)^2
\ge\operatorname{dist}(u_n,V_\infty)^2
\ge\frac{d_b}{b^2}.
\tag{15}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{tr}S_M
\ge \frac{Md_b}{b^2},}
\tag{16}
\]

while (11) simultaneously says `lambda_min(S_M)=O(1/M)`. The average Schur eigenvalue stays bounded below, but at least one collective mode collapses.

The actual WI-126 horizontal charge therefore remains extensive:

\[
\sum_{n=0}^{M-1}
\operatorname{dist}(h_n,V_M)^2
=b^2\sum_n(S_M)_{nn}
\ge Md_b,
\]

and

\[
\boxed{R_H\ge4Md_b.}
\tag{17}
\]

Thus the minimum-eigenvalue collapse is a false positive for horizontal screening. It comes from a collective coefficient mode adapted to the endpoint-tapered weighted Fourier system, even though every individual normalized odd direction stays uniformly transverse to the complete retained lattice span.

## 5. The WI-132 square-depth scalarization loses the whole density-scale charge here

For (2),

\[
D_2=\sum_{n=0}^{M-1}b^2=Mb^2.
\tag{18}
\]

If one keeps only the WI-132 scalar minimum eigenvalue, then (11) gives at best

\[
4\lambda_{\min}(S_M)D_2
\le4C_{b,\eta}b^2=O_{b,\eta}(1).
\tag{19}
\]

Since the multiset has `N=2M` labels, this lower-bound mechanism contributes only `o(N)` after normalization, whereas the exact diagonal sum in (17) is `Omega(N)`.

This separates two logically different statements in WI-132:

- the **necessary** stability conclusion remains valid: if Lamzouri's baseline is asymptotically sharp while `D_2` is macroscopic, then `lambda_min(S)->0`;
- the hoped-for **sufficient** route through a uniform scalar lower bound is too rigid: `lambda_min(S)->0` can occur in a family with an extensive horizontal remainder and fixed positive depth.

So the global minimum eigenvalue is not the right discriminator for near-extremal screening.

## 6. Order of limits and relevance to Lamzouri's zeta argument

The obstruction uses a fixed admissible `eta_delta` and lets the number of lattice pairs grow. This is the relevant order for Lamzouri's theorem: for each target accuracy `epsilon`, Lemma 3.2 first fixes a smooth compactly supported `eta_epsilon`; the zeta-height limit is then taken with that function fixed, and only afterward is `epsilon` allowed to tend to zero.

There is therefore no escape by observing that the taper region shrinks as `delta->0`. For every fixed `delta>0`, a sufficiently long modulation block produces (11). A diagonal choice of block length can outrun any prescribed sequence `delta->0`.

This is an adversarial Hilbert-space configuration, not a claim that actual zeta zeros contain long exact conjugate lattices. Its role is narrower and rigorous: **no theorem based only on a uniform lower bound for the full normalized odd Schur minimum can be proved from the generic Lamzouri window geometry**, because that lower bound already fails on the simplest fixed-depth lattice whose total horizontal transversality is known to be extensive.

## 7. Prior art, novelty boundary, and next invariant

Lamzouri's `C_c^infty((-1/2,1/2))` admissible family and the order of limits are from arXiv:2609.02882v1. WI-127 supplies the fixed-period extensive-transversality theorem, and WI-132 supplies the normalized divided-difference Schur formulation. The weighted modulation calculation above is the same classical multiplication-operator/critical-sampling phenomenon already represented in this line by Daubechies--Grossmann--Meyer, *Painless nonorthogonal expansions* (1986), and WI-005; the explicit bound (10) is just the elementary Dirichlet-kernel finite-section test. No novelty or priority is claimed for those harmonic-analysis ingredients.

A targeted literature check around weighted trigonometric Riesz systems, Toeplitz lower bounds, and exponential divided differences found classical results consistent with the obstruction: a weight touching zero cannot support a uniform full Fourier lower bound in the corresponding critical system, while grouped divided differences address frequency collisions rather than zeros of the common envelope. No external theorem is needed for (10)--(11), so the conclusion does not depend on importing a stronger black-box Riesz statement.

The research implication is a concrete redirection. The exact Lamzouri remainder is

\[
4\sum_jm_jy_j^2S_{jj},
\]

not `4 lambda_min(S) D_2`. A useful bootstrap should therefore target one of the quantities that survives the example above: a depth-weighted diagonal average, a positive-density lower spectral quantile, a localized/frame decomposition that discards only `o(N)` endpoint-concentration modes, or a zeta-specific theorem preventing macroscopic square-depth mass from aligning with a small-dimensional Schur near-null sector. Any proposed replacement should be tested first on the conjugate lattice (2): it should retain the extensive charge in (17) while still vanishing on genuine near-line collapse or critical-line doubles.
