# WI-136 — combined Lamzouri slack reduces free off-line screening from V to U

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + LITERATURE+DERIVED + STRUCTURAL-RIGIDITY + BOOTSTRAP-INTERFACE`. Lamzouri's Hilbert-space proof contains two separate nonnegative remainders. WI-126 used the horizontal remainder to charge the part of each off-line odd direction that survives after projection away from the full retained space `V`, while WI-132--WI-135 therefore studied the `V`-projected odd Schur complement. Recombining the exact `U`-remainder with the horizontal remainder shows that this leaves substantial information unused: projection of an off-line odd direction into the **simple-real layer** `V\ominus U` is itself charged by `R_U`. Simple-real zeros therefore cannot screen off-line odd directions for free.

With Lamzouri's finite notation, let

\[
\Delta:=n-(2N-Q)=R_B+R_U+R_M+R_H,
\qquad R_\bullet\ge0,
\]

let `U` be the span generated by repeated-real vectors and the off-line even vectors, and let `V` be obtained from `U` by adjoining the simple-real vectors. Put `d=\dim U=r+k`, let `A_U` be the coefficient sum from WI-126, and define

\[
B:=A_U-2d\ge0.
\]

For one representative `z=x+iy`, `y>0`, of each distinct off-line conjugate pair, with multiplicity `m_z`, define

\[
S_1:=\sum_{x\in R_1}\|P_Uf_x\|^2,
\qquad
E_{\mathbb R}:=\sum_{x\in R_2}(m_x-2),
\]

\[
E_{\mathbb C}:=\sum_{z\in Z_+}(m_z-1),
\qquad
H_U:=\sum_{z\in Z_+}m_z\|P_{U^\perp}h_z\|^2,
\]

and

\[
H_V:=\sum_{z\in Z_+}m_z\|P_{V^\perp}h_z\|^2.
\]

Then the exact identities reconstructed in WI-126 imply

\[
\boxed{
\Delta\ge
2S_1+2E_{\mathbb R}+4E_{\mathbb C}
+4H_U+4H_V+\frac{B^2}{d}
}
\tag{A}
\]

whenever `d>0`; for `d=0` the exceptional `U`-population is absent and the corresponding statement is vacuous. Since `U\subset V`, if `M:=V\ominus U` and `H:=W\ominus V`, then

\[
H_U=\sum_zm_z\bigl(\|P_Mh_z\|^2+\|P_Hh_z\|^2\bigr),
\qquad
H_V=\sum_zm_z\|P_Hh_z\|^2,
\]

so (A) is equivalently

\[
\boxed{
\Delta\ge
2S_1+2E_{\mathbb R}+4E_{\mathbb C}
+4\sum_zm_z\|P_Mh_z\|^2
+8\sum_zm_z\|P_Hh_z\|^2
+\frac{B^2}{d}.
}
\tag{B}
\]

Thus near saturation of Lamzouri's baseline forces off-line odd directions to approach `U`, not merely `V`. This removes the simple-real span as a zero-cost screening reservoir. No unconditional zeta-zero percentage changes in this finding.

## 1. Exact `U`-remainder expansion

WI-126 reconstructs Lamzouri's coefficient identity

\[
R_U
=\sum_{j\in U}\left(\alpha_j-\frac{A_U}{d}\right)^2
+\frac{A_U(A_U-2d)}d,
\qquad A_U\ge2d,
\tag{1}
\]

and the exact decomposition

\[
A_U-2d
=
\sum_{x\in R_1}\|P_Uf_x\|^2
+\sum_{x\in R_2}(m_x-2)
+2\sum_{z\in Z_+}
\left[(m_z-1)+m_z\|P_{U^\perp}h_z\|^2\right].
\tag{2}
\]

In the notation above, (2) says simply

\[
B=S_1+E_{\mathbb R}+2E_{\mathbb C}+2H_U.
\tag{3}
\]

Substitute `A_U=2d+B` into the second term of (1):

\[
\frac{A_UB}{d}
=2B+\frac{B^2}{d}.
\tag{4}
\]

The variance term in (1) is nonnegative, hence

\[
R_U\ge2B+\frac{B^2}{d}.
\tag{5}
\]

Using (3),

\[
\boxed{
R_U\ge
2S_1+2E_{\mathbb R}+4E_{\mathbb C}+4H_U+\frac{B^2}{d}.
}
\tag{6}
\]

The linear part already shows the structural point: every unit of squared off-line odd mass outside `U` costs at least four units in the finite Lamzouri deficit. The additional `B^2/d` term is an aggregate convexity penalty and can only strengthen this conclusion.

## 2. Add the independent horizontal remainder

WI-126 also reconstructs Lamzouri's horizontal estimate

\[
R_H\ge4H_V.
\tag{7}
\]

All four remainders in

\[
\Delta=R_B+R_U+R_M+R_H
\tag{8}
\]

are nonnegative. Combining (6)--(8) immediately proves (A).

The difference between the two distances is important. Because `U\subset V`, the orthogonal decomposition

\[
U^\perp=(V\ominus U)\oplus V^\perp
\tag{9}
\]

gives

\[
\|P_{U^\perp}h_z\|^2
=\|P_{V\ominus U}h_z\|^2+\|P_{V^\perp}h_z\|^2.
\tag{10}
\]

Thus the `V^\perp` component is charged once through `R_U` and once again through `R_H`, while the component captured by simple-real directions `V\ominus U` is still charged once through `R_U`. Equation (10) inserted into (A) gives (B).

This is the bookkeeping distinction that was not harvested in WI-126. Using `R_H` alone makes the simple-real layer look like a free screen because it disappears under `P_{V^\perp}`. In the full proof it is not free: moving an odd off-line direction from `V^\perp` into `V\ominus U` removes the horizontal charge but leaves the `U`-charge.

## 3. Near-sharpness forces distance to U to collapse

Suppose along a sequence of finite configurations

\[
\Delta=o(N).
\tag{11}
\]

Every term on the right of (A) is nonnegative, so necessarily

\[
\boxed{
H_U=\sum_{z\in Z_+}m_z\operatorname{dist}(h_z,U)^2=o(N).
}
\tag{12}
\]

This strictly strengthens the consequence extracted in WI-126,

\[
H_V=\sum_zm_z\operatorname{dist}(h_z,V)^2=o(N),
\tag{13}
\]

because `U\subset V` and therefore `dist(h_z,U)\ge dist(h_z,V)` termwise. Near equality also forces

\[
S_1=o(N),\qquad E_{\mathbb R}=o(N),\qquad E_{\mathbb C}=o(N),
\tag{14}
\]

and

\[
\frac{B^2}{d}=o(N).
\tag{15}
\]

The exceptional complement is therefore more rigid than the `V`-Schur picture alone suggests. A near-extremizer cannot use a macroscopic simple-real subspace to absorb off-line odd directions at zero cost; it must make those odd directions almost lie in the smaller space generated only by repeated-real and off-line-even data.

Equation (14) also keeps the populations separated as required by the research mandate. Critical-line doubles are included in `U` and remain a genuine exceptional branch; higher real multiplicity is charged. Off-line multiplicity is separately charged. Pure proof slack remains represented by the nonnegative remainders rather than being misidentified as off-line mass.

## 4. The correct normalized Schur quotient is smaller

For each distinct off-line representative `z_j=x_j+iy_j`, define the normalized odd divided difference from WI-132,

\[
u_j:=\frac{h_{z_j}}{y_j},
\qquad
\mathcal Ue_j=u_j,
\]

and now project only against `U`:

\[
\boxed{
T:=\mathcal U^*(I-P_U)\mathcal U.
}
\tag{16}
\]

Then

\[
H_U=\sum_jm_jy_j^2T_{jj}.
\tag{17}
\]

The matrix used in WI-132--WI-135 was

\[
S:=\mathcal U^*(I-P_V)\mathcal U.
\tag{18}
\]

and since `P_U\preceq P_V`,

\[
\boxed{T\succeq S.}
\tag{19}
\]

WI-132 proves `S\succ0` for distinct off-line pairs, so (19) also gives `T\succ0`. The finite-dimensional positivity is therefore automatic; the remaining question is quantitative lower-tail control.

The advantage is conceptual and dimensional. If `r` is the number of distinct repeated-real ordinates and `k` the number of distinct off-line conjugate pairs, then

\[
\dim U=r+k.
\tag{20}
\]

The exceptional multiplicity count satisfies

\[
N-n
=\sum_{x\in R_2}m_x+2\sum_{z\in Z_+}m_z
\ge2r+2k,
\]

hence

\[
\boxed{\dim U\le\frac{N-n}{2}.}
\tag{21}
\]

At a certified simple-real proportion near `0.67285`, this only indicates a dimension ratio below about `0.1636`; it is **not** a Riesz bound and does not by itself control clustering, separation, endpoint taper, or the least eigenvalue of `T`. The point is only that the exact proof reduces the potentially free screening space from all retained real directions to a space whose dimension is tied directly to the exceptional mass.

## 5. Bootstrap interface

Equations (12), (16), and (17) expose a stronger possible defect-to-zero mechanism than the `V`-projected Schur route.

If one could prove, for zeta-compatible exceptional configurations, a quantitative statement preventing a positive-density collection of normalized odd divided differences from becoming almost contained in `U`, then any positive off-line square-depth mass would contribute extensive `R_U` and force a strict improvement over the Lamzouri baseline. Iterating such an estimate as the exceptional mass decreases is the natural bootstrap target.

The relevant source-side object is now a grouped-exponential/divided-difference family projected against repeated-real exponentials and the corresponding off-line **even** divided differences. Classical nonharmonic Fourier theory, including Avdonin--Ivanov and Avdonin--Moran, is structurally relevant to such grouped exponential families. However no theorem located in the audit can be applied as a black box here: average dimension below critical density does not imply a uniform Riesz lower bound when ordinates can cluster, and WI-133 already shows that endpoint taper can destroy naive normalized lower bounds in the larger `V`-projected problem.

A valid next theorem therefore needs a genuinely quantitative hypothesis available for zeta zeros -- for example a local-density/separation decomposition, an averaged lower-tail estimate, or a cluster-aware divided-difference bound -- rather than merely the global count (21).

## 6. Relation to WI-132--WI-135

WI-132 correctly converted square depth into the `V`-projected Schur slack coming from `R_H`. WI-133 showed that endpoint taper can make the smallest eigenvalue of that matrix collapse. WI-134 converted low horizontal slack into a macroscopic `V`-Schur near-null sector, and WI-135 removed multiplicity-created automatic zero modes from that statement.

The present finding does not contradict any of those results. It identifies an additional remainder that changes the interpretation of the collapse. A vector that is near-null after projection away from `V` because it is well represented by **simple-real** directions need not be cheap in the full Lamzouri proof: its component in `V\ominus U` is measured by `R_U`. Therefore any source-compatible near-extremizer must pass the stronger `U`-projection test (12), not merely the `V`-projection test from `R_H`.

This also clarifies why the critical-lattice screening countermodels of WI-005--WI-007 remain relevant but are not the end of the story. Pairwise support-one aliases can make off-line and critical-line configurations look identical to a collapsed kernel, yet Lamzouri's conjugation-adapted coefficient bookkeeping retains a second orthogonal-layer invariant that charges how that screening is realized.

## 8. Research implication

The main consequence is a sharper characterization of what the uncertified complement would have to look like if the current lower bound were nearly sharp. Extensive off-line mass cannot merely hide behind the many simple critical-line vectors. Its odd conjugation directions must become collectively almost representable by a space built from the **exceptional population itself**: repeated-real directions and off-line even directions.

That is a substantially stronger self-consistency requirement and a more credible bootstrap interface. The next high-value route is to prove or refute quantitative lower-tail control for the `U`-projected matrix `T` under zeta-accessible local-density information. A successful bound would turn off-line square-depth mass directly into additional simple-real proportion; a countermodel would have to exhibit an exceptional family that self-screens both its even and odd divided-difference components while respecting the zeta zero constraints.
