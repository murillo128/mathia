# WI-268 — the archimedean sign change breaks unrestricted semigroup positivity before the first prime

## Status

- **Evidence:** `EXACT-DERIVED + LITERATURE+DERIVED + CLASSICAL-BEURLING-DENY + DECISIVE-NEGATIVE + PRIOR-ART-AUDITED`.
- **Scope:** an exact obstruction to the ordinary Perron--Frobenius/Beurling--Deny route for the **unrestricted** localized Weil operator. For every aperture `a>h_0/2`, with `h_0=\log\rho` and `\rho^3-\rho-1=0`, the semigroup generated by Suzuki's localized operator is not positivity preserving. The threshold is `h_0/2=0.1405997871...`, strictly before the first von-Mangoldt translation can become active at `(\log2)/2=0.3465735902...`.
- **What it does not claim:** this does not prove that a first-crossing ground/null mode changes sign, and it does not exclude a zeta-specific one-sign theorem obtained from the null equation or from a different cone/order structure. It closes only the standard order-preserving-semigroup shortcut.
- **Novelty boundary:** Suzuki proves positivity improvement for the limiting small-aperture logarithmic form used in his ground-state asymptotics; that is not a positivity-preservation theorem for the full localized Weil semigroup at arbitrary `a`. The first Beurling--Deny criterion is classical. A targeted audit found no source stating the full-form threshold below; search absence is not a priority claim.

## Claim

Let `Q_W^a` be Suzuki's real localized closed Weil form on `L^2(-a,a)`, and let `A_a` be its associated lower-bounded self-adjoint operator. Define, for `h>0`,

\[
\kappa(h):=\frac{e^{-5h/2}}{1-e^{-2h}},
\qquad
w(h):=\kappa(h)-e^{h/2}.
\tag{1}
\]

Let `\rho>1` be the unique positive root of

\[
\rho^3-\rho-1=0,
\tag{2}
\]

and put

\[
h_0:=\log\rho
=0.2811995743229618465\ldots .
\tag{3}
\]

Then for every

\[
\boxed{a>\frac{h_0}{2}=0.1405997871614809233\ldots}
\tag{4}
\]

the semigroup `e^{-tA_a}` is **not positivity preserving**.

Because `\rho<2`, one has `h_0<\log2`. Hence there is already a prime-free interval

\[
\boxed{
\frac{h_0}{2}<a<\frac{\log2}{2}
}
\tag{5}
\]

on which the obstruction is entirely archimedean: no von-Mangoldt translation is active, but positivity preservation of the full localized Weil semigroup nevertheless fails.

## 1. Suzuki's form reduces to an explicit continuous cross kernel off the diagonal

Suzuki, *Weil's quadratic form via the screw function*, arXiv:2606.09096v2 (revised 17 Aug 2026), gives on the form core `H_0^1(-a,a)` the exact decomposition (equation (2.5))

\[
Q_W^a(v)
=\mathcal L_a(v)-(2A+1)\|v\|_2^2
-\sum_{n\le e^{2a}}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}\,T_{\log n}(v)
-\iint r''(x-y)v(y)v(x)\,dx\,dy,
\tag{6}
\]

where

\[
\mathcal L_a(v)
=\frac14\iint_{(-a,a)^2}
\frac{|v(x)-v(y)|^2}{|x-y|}\,dx\,dy
-\frac12\int_{-a}^a\log(a^2-x^2)|v(x)|^2\,dx,
\tag{7}
\]

and `T_{\log n}` is the sum of the two translated autocorrelation integrals displayed in Suzuki's (2.5). The active arithmetic lag set is therefore finite:

\[
\mathcal P_a:=\{\log n:\Lambda(n)>0,\ \log n\le2a\}.
\tag{8}
\]

Take real nonnegative `f,g\in C_c^\infty(-a,a)` with disjoint supports. Polarizing the jump part of (7), all local terms vanish in the cross form and

\[
\mathcal L_a(f,g)
=-\frac12\iint\frac{f(x)g(y)}{|x-y|}\,dx\,dy.
\tag{9}
\]

The regular remainder contributes

\[
-\iint r''(x-y)f(y)g(x)\,dx\,dy.
\tag{10}
\]

`WI-255`, by simplifying Suzuki's explicit remainder, recorded the exact identity

\[
r''(h)=w(h)-\frac1{2h},
\qquad h>0.
\tag{11}
\]

Consequently the singular `1/(2h)` terms cancel **exactly** in the disjoint-support cross form:

\[
-\frac1{2h}-r''(h)=-w(h).
\tag{12}
\]

If the pairwise support distances avoid every active lag in `\mathcal P_a`, all discrete prime cross terms vanish as well. Under that support condition,

\[
\boxed{
Q_W^a(f,g)
=\iint[-w(|x-y|)]f(x)g(y)\,dx\,dy.
}
\tag{13}
\]

This identity is on the smooth form core, which is sufficient for a Beurling--Deny counterexample.

## 2. The continuous cross kernel changes sign at the plastic constant

For `h>0`, multiply `w(h)` by the positive quantity `e^{5h/2}(1-e^{-2h})`. Its sign is exactly the sign of

\[
1+e^h-e^{3h}.
\tag{14}
\]

Writing `x=e^h>1`, the right-hand side is `1+x-x^3`. Since `x^3-x-1` is strictly increasing for `x>1` and changes sign between `1` and `2`, it has the unique root `\rho` in (2). Therefore

\[
w(h)>0\quad(0<h<h_0),
\qquad
w(h_0)=0,
\qquad
w(h)<0\quad(h>h_0).
\tag{15}
\]

Thus the off-diagonal continuous kernel in (13) satisfies

\[
-w(h)>0\qquad(h>h_0).
\tag{16}
\]

The same sign threshold appeared earlier in `WI-254` and `WI-263` in the first-crossing autocorrelation flux. Here it has a different consequence: it directly violates the sign condition required of an order-preserving quadratic form.

## 3. Every aperture above `h_0/2` admits a positive disjoint-support cross term

Fix `a>h_0/2`. Then `(h_0,2a)` is a nonempty open interval. The set `\mathcal P_a` in (8) is finite, so choose

\[
d\in(h_0,2a)\setminus\mathcal P_a.
\tag{17}
\]

Choose an open interval `U` around `d` such that

\[
U\Subset(h_0,2a),
\qquad
U\cap\mathcal P_a=\varnothing.
\tag{18}
\]

Place two sufficiently small nonnegative smooth bumps `f,g` around the centers `-d/2` and `d/2`. Since `d<2a`, both supports can be chosen compactly inside `(-a,a)`. Shrink them so that they are disjoint and every distance `|x-y|` with `x\in\operatorname{supp}f`, `y\in\operatorname{supp}g` lies in `U`.

By (18), no arithmetic translation couples the two supports, so (13) applies. By (16), its integrand is strictly positive throughout the support rectangle. Hence

\[
\boxed{Q_W^a(f,g)>0.}
\tag{19}
\]

No asymptotic prime estimate, RH assumption, zero sum, or numerical eigenfunction enters this construction. The finite source set merely has to be avoided, which is possible at every aperture above the threshold.

## 4. Beurling--Deny rules out positivity preservation

For a lower-bounded real symmetric closed form `q`, add a scalar multiple of the `L^2` norm if necessary to make it nonnegative. The first Beurling--Deny criterion says that positivity preservation of the generated self-adjoint semigroup requires

\[
q(|u|)\le q(u).
\tag{20}
\]

For disjoint nonnegative `f,g`, take `u=f-g`. Then `|u|=f+g`, so

\[
q(f+g)-q(f-g)=4q(f,g).
\tag{21}
\]

The scalar shift cancels from (21), because `\|f+g\|_2=\|f-g\|_2`. Therefore positivity preservation requires `q(f,g)\le0` for every disjoint nonnegative pair.

Equation (19) gives a smooth-core counterexample. It follows that

\[
\boxed{
e^{-tA_a}\text{ is not positivity preserving for every }a>h_0/2.
}
\tag{22}
\]

A standard reference for the criterion is Reed--Simon, *Methods of Modern Mathematical Physics IV*, Theorem XIII.50; equivalent closed-form formulations are commonly called the first Beurling--Deny criterion.

## 5. The obstruction is already present before any prime interaction

The first active arithmetic lag is `\log2`. If

\[
a<\frac{\log2}{2},
\tag{23}
\]

then `2a<\log2`, and the sum over `n\le e^{2a}` in (6) contains no von-Mangoldt term. Since `\rho<2`,

\[
\frac{h_0}{2}<\frac{\log2}{2}.
\tag{24}
\]

Combining (22)--(24) gives the nonempty prime-free interval (5). This distinguishes the present obstruction sharply from `WI-252`: there the **odd-sector** half-line realization first loses semigroup positivity through the reflected `p=2` atom once `a>(\log2)/2`; here the **unrestricted** full form has already lost positivity preservation at the earlier archimedean threshold `h_0/2`.

This also clarifies Suzuki's small-`a` use of Dirichlet-form theory. His positivity-improving operator in the ground-state asymptotic analysis is associated with the limiting rescaled logarithmic form `\overline{\mathcal L}`, not a claim that the complete arithmetic-plus-archimedean localized Weil form generates an order-preserving semigroup for all sufficiently small fixed apertures up to the first prime.

## 6. Consequence for the current first-crossing program

The accepted `CLUE-first-global-crossing-sliding-autocorrelation-symbol` seeks a source-specific bridge from Suzuki's exact first-crossing/null equation to quantitative arithmetic overlap. A tempting shortcut would be to argue that the lowest localized eigenfunction is one-signed by ordinary Perron--Frobenius/Krein--Rutman theory and then extract signs for translated overlaps.

The present theorem rules out that shortcut throughout the regime relevant to the current first-crossing program. In particular, the clue's established search regime has hypothetical first crossing `a_*>0.8`, far above `h_0/2`. The full semigroup is therefore not even positivity preserving there.

This does **not** imply that the first-crossing null mode must change sign. It says that one sign cannot be obtained for free from the standard positive-semigroup cone. Any surviving one-sign or overlap theorem must use additional zeta-specific structure: the actual Suzuki null equation, firstness, a specialized invariant, or a genuinely different cone/order structure that is not contradicted by the positive off-diagonal cross term (19).

## 7. Prior-art and novelty audit

The audit separated three ingredients.

1. **Suzuki's localized Weil operator.** Masatoshi Suzuki, *Weil's quadratic form via the screw function*, arXiv:2606.09096v2, supplies the exact localized form (2.5), the Friedrichs realization of `A_a`, and the limiting logarithmic Dirichlet-form argument used for small-aperture ground-state asymptotics. The decomposition is literature input; the threshold conclusion (22) is not stated there.
2. **Classical form theory.** The implication from positivity preservation to `q(|u|)\le q(u)` is the classical first Beurling--Deny criterion. No novelty is attached to this step.
3. **Nearby Mathia results.** `WI-254`/`WI-263` already identify the zero `h_0` of the same continuous source weight, while `WI-252` proves a later odd-sector obstruction from the first reflected prime atom. The new deduction is that Suzuki's unrestricted full-form cross kernel is exactly `-w(h)` off the diagonal after the singular cancellation in (12), so the earlier sign change itself supplies a Beurling--Deny counterexample.

Targeted searches for combinations of Suzuki's localized Weil operator, Beurling--Deny positivity preservation, the plastic-constant threshold, and the archimedean kernel did not locate a source stating (22). This is recorded only as a prior-art audit boundary, not as a claim of priority.

## 8. Stress tests and boundaries

1. **Factor in the logarithmic jump form.** Polarizing the coefficient `1/4` in Suzuki's double integral gives exactly the cross kernel `-1/(2|x-y|)` in (9), not `-1/|x-y|`.
2. **Remainder sign.** Suzuki's form contains `-\iint r''(x-y)v(y)v(x)`. Together with `r''(h)=w(h)-1/(2h)`, this gives the exact cancellation (12). Reversing either sign would invalidate the theorem.
3. **Prime terms vanish exactly.** The active lag set at fixed `a` is finite. The interval `U` is chosen to avoid every one of those lags, and the two bump supports are then chosen so that all cross distances lie inside `U`; no limiting argument is needed to suppress the arithmetic cross terms.
4. **Boundary geometry.** Any `d<2a` can be realized by bump centers at `\pm d/2` with positive distance to the endpoints; supports can then be shrunk inside `(-a,a)`.
5. **Lower boundedness.** A scalar shift needed for Beurling--Deny has zero bilinear contribution on disjoint supports and equal norm on `f+g` and `f-g`, so it cannot repair the violation.
6. **No contradiction with small-radius ground-state asymptotics.** Suzuki's limiting logarithmic form is a Dirichlet form. The full form acquires the positive cross interaction only once `2a>h_0`; as `a\to0` that separation is geometrically unavailable.
7. **Not a nodal theorem.** Failure of positivity preservation does not imply that a particular ground state has a node. It only blocks the standard semigroup mechanism for proving constant sign.
8. **Not a first-crossing overlap theorem.** Nothing here lower-bounds `C_v(\log n)` or the sliding flux. The result is negative guidance: the live activity-to-overlap gate must exploit the actual equation or another source-specific structure.
