# WI-345 — fixed-degree polynomial bow carriers remain compressible

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + DECISIVE-NEGATIVE + CLASSICAL-MECHANISM + PRIOR-ART-AUDITED + STRUCTURAL-RIGIDITY + NON-RH`.

## Claim

WI-344 proves that a bow-height carrier with fixed logarithmic source aperture remains approximately finite-dimensional after every fixed height-independent **linear** bank. A natural escape is to use nonlinear source features. Fixed-degree polynomial nonlinearity does **not** escape that obstruction.

Let `S` be a finite set of positive integers contained in one logarithmic interval

\[
\log n\in[x_c-\Delta/2,x_c+\Delta/2]\qquad(n\in S),
\tag{1}
\]

let `a=(a_n)_{n\in S}\neq0`, and define the normalized carrier

\[
w_U(n):=\frac{a_n n^{iU}}{\|a\|_2},
\qquad
U\in[U_c-H/2,U_c+H/2].
\tag{2}
\]

Fix an integer polynomial degree `p>=1`. Put

\[
\mathcal H_p
:=
\bigoplus_{\substack{r,s\ge0\\r+s\le p}}
\ell^2(S)^{\otimes r}\otimes\overline{\ell^2(S)}^{\otimes s},
\tag{3}
\]

and define the canonical degree-`p` polynomial/tensor lift

\[
\Phi_p(U)
:=
\bigoplus_{\substack{r,s\ge0\\r+s\le p}}
w_U^{\otimes r}\otimes\overline{w_U}^{\otimes s}.
\tag{4}
\]

There are

\[
R_p:=\#\{(r,s):r,s\ge0,\ r+s\le p\}
=\frac{(p+1)(p+2)}2
\tag{5}
\]

bi-degree sectors, each of norm one, so `||Phi_p(U)||=sqrt(R_p)`.

For every integer `d>=0` there is a subspace `W_{p,d}\subset\mathcal H_p`, independent of `U`, with

\[
\boxed{
\dim W_{p,d}\le R_p(d+1)
}
\tag{6}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{dist}(\Phi_p(U),W_{p,d})
\le
\sqrt{R_p}\,\varepsilon_{p,d},
\qquad
\varepsilon_{p,d}:=
\frac{(pH\Delta/4)^{d+1}}{(d+1)!}.
}
\tag{7}
\]

Now let

\[
A:\mathcal H_p\longrightarrow\mathcal K
\tag{8}
\]

be any bounded linear map independent of `U`. This includes every vector-valued polynomial observable of total degree at most `p` in the carrier coordinates and their conjugates, with arbitrary height-independent coefficients: such a polynomial is a linear map applied to the finite direct sum of tensor monomials in (4).

For sampled heights `U_1,...,U_m`, assume the outputs are nonzero and define

\[
h_k:=\frac{A\Phi_p(U_k)}{\|A\Phi_p(U_k)\|},
\qquad
\eta:=
\min_k
\frac{\|A\Phi_p(U_k)\|}
     {\|A\|\sqrt{R_p}}
>0.
\tag{9}
\]

If `G_A=(\langle h_j,h_k\rangle)` has eigenvalues in decreasing order, then

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{j>R_p(d+1)}\lambda_j(G_A)
\le
m\left(\frac{\varepsilon_{p,d}}{\eta}\right)^2.
}
\tag{10}
\]

Hence at the exact Gallagher aperture `H\Delta=1`, every **fixed degree** `p` and every fixed lower transmission `eta>=eta_0>0` has bounded scale-preserving effective height dimension. Increasing the number of polynomial features or output channels does not create a growing family of independent height directions. Any bow route that specifically needs `K(T)->infinity` scale-preserving height directions therefore cannot obtain them from a fixed-degree polynomial algebra over one fixed-aperture carrier.

The remaining escapes are genuinely different: let the polynomial degree grow with height, let the total logarithmic source aperture grow, inject height-dependent coefficients/frequencies not generated by the common carrier, use a non-polynomial feature with unbounded effective frequency, or allow the transmission factor `eta` to collapse. The result does not rule out nonlinear observables in general and does not itself prove the signed `Lambda-Lambda^sharp` cancellation sought by the live bow clue.

## 1. Every fixed bi-degree sector has only `d+1` height modes

Fix a sector `(r,s)` with `q:=r+s<=p`. A coordinate of

\[
w_U^{\otimes r}\otimes\overline{w_U}^{\otimes s}
\tag{11}
\]

is indexed by `n_1,...,n_r,m_1,...,m_s` and has `U`-dependent phase

\[
\exp\!\left(
 iU\left[\sum_{j=1}^r\log n_j-\sum_{k=1}^s\log m_k\right]
\right).
\tag{12}
\]

Write

\[
\log n=x_c+\xi_n,
\qquad
|\xi_n|\le\Delta/2,
\tag{13}
\]

and similarly for each `m`. Then

\[
\sum_{j=1}^r\log n_j-\sum_{k=1}^s\log m_k
=(r-s)x_c+\Xi,
\tag{14}
\]

where

\[
\Xi:=
\sum_{j=1}^r\xi_{n_j}-\sum_{k=1}^s\xi_{m_k},
\qquad
|\Xi|\le\frac{q\Delta}{2}\le\frac{p\Delta}{2}.
\tag{15}
\]

Set `delta=U-U_c`; then `|delta|<=H/2`, and therefore

\[
|\delta\Xi|\le\frac{pH\Delta}{4}.
\tag{16}
\]

After extracting the harmless scalar phase `e^{iU(r-s)x_c}`, the remaining oscillation is

\[
e^{iU_c\Xi}e^{i\delta\Xi}.
\tag{17}
\]

Taylor's integral remainder for real `z` gives

\[
\left|
 e^{iz}-\sum_{k=0}^{d}\frac{(iz)^k}{k!}
\right|
\le\frac{|z|^{d+1}}{(d+1)!}.
\tag{18}
\]

Thus the degree-`d` approximation in this sector belongs to the span of the `d+1` fixed tensors whose coordinates are the static tensor amplitude times

\[
e^{iU_c\Xi}\Xi^k,
\qquad 0\le k\le d.
\tag{19}
\]

The sector-wide scalar `e^{iU(r-s)x_c}` does not change that subspace. Because the normalized tensor amplitude has squared coordinate sum one, (16)--(18) give sector error at most `epsilon_{p,d}` in Hilbert norm.

Taking the orthogonal direct sum over the `R_p` sectors produces a fixed subspace `W_{p,d}` of dimension at most `R_p(d+1)`. The sector errors add in squares, proving (6)--(7). No distributional property of primes or zeros is used.

## 2. Arbitrary fixed polynomial feature maps inherit the compression

The representation (4) is deliberately redundant. Symmetric tensor powers would be smaller, but the full tensor spaces make the containment immediate: every monomial

\[
\prod_{j=1}^{r}w_U(n_j)
\prod_{k=1}^{s}\overline{w_U(m_k)},
\qquad r+s\le p,
\tag{20}
\]

is a coordinate of one sector of `Phi_p(U)`. Any collection of polynomial features of total degree at most `p`, including polynomial combinations of finitely many fixed linear filters of `w_U`, is therefore `A Phi_p(U)` for a single height-independent linear map `A`.

From (7),

\[
\operatorname{dist}(A\Phi_p(U),AW_{p,d})
\le
\|A\|\sqrt{R_p}\,\varepsilon_{p,d}.
\tag{21}
\]

Under (9), division by `||A Phi_p(U_k)||` shows

\[
\operatorname{dist}(h_k,AW_{p,d})
\le
\frac{\varepsilon_{p,d}}{\eta}.
\tag{22}
\]

Let `P` be orthogonal projection onto the closure of `AW_{p,d}` and let `C:\mathbf C^m\to\mathcal K` be the column operator `Ce_k=h_k`. Since `rank(PC)<=R_p(d+1)`,

\[
\|C-PC\|_F^2
=
\sum_{k=1}^{m}\|(I-P)h_k\|^2
\le
m\left(\frac{\varepsilon_{p,d}}{\eta}\right)^2.
\tag{23}
\]

The best-rank approximation principle bounds the squared singular-value tail of `C` by the right side. The squared singular values of `C` are the eigenvalues of its Gram matrix, proving (10).

As in WI-344, the transmission condition is essential only after normalization. An ill-conditioned `A` can annihilate the dominant low-order Taylor modes and magnify the apparent role of the tiny residual once absolute scale is divided away. Without any lower bound on `eta`, one still has the unconditional unnormalized tail estimate

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{j>R_p(d+1)}s_j(C_A)^2
\le
m\|A\|^2R_p\,\varepsilon_{p,d}^2,
}
\tag{24}
\]

where `C_Ae_k=A Phi_p(U_k)`.

## 3. Fixed polynomial degree cannot supply growing effective rank at fixed aperture

Put `N=d+1`. The elementary bound `N!>=(N/e)^N` gives

\[
\varepsilon_{p,N-1}
\le
\left(\frac{e pH\Delta}{4N}\right)^N.
\tag{25}
\]

If

\[
N\ge\frac{epH\Delta}{2},
\tag{26}
\]

then `epsilon_{p,N-1}<=2^{-N}`. Therefore, for fixed `p`, fixed `H Delta`, fixed `eta_0>0`, and any prescribed tail tolerance `tau>0`, one may choose a **fixed** `N=N(p,H Delta,eta_0,tau)` such that every normalized polynomial feature family satisfying `eta>=eta_0` obeys

\[
\sum_{j>R_pN}\lambda_j(G_A)\le\tau m.
\tag{27}
\]

At the Gallagher scale `H Delta=1`, the right-hand rank cutoff `R_pN` is independent of the zeta height, the number of source coefficients, the number of sampled ordinates, and the number of output polynomial channels. Fixed-degree nonlinearity broadens the residual log-frequency aperture by at most the finite factor `p`; it does not change constant time--bandwidth product into growing time--bandwidth product.

This closes one precise escape left by WI-344. The relevant hierarchy is now:

- fixed-aperture linear banks are compressed by WI-344;
- fixed-aperture polynomial banks of any fixed total degree are compressed by (6)--(10);
- a nonlinear escape must have complexity that itself grows with height, inject genuinely new height frequencies, enlarge the source aperture, or pay vanishing transmission.

The statement is deliberately not extended to arbitrary nonlinear maps. A threshold, reciprocal, exponential, neural feature map, or polynomial whose degree tends to infinity can generate an unbounded Fourier algebra and is outside the theorem. Likewise, coefficients that explicitly depend on `U` need not factor through one fixed `A` and are outside the theorem.

## 4. Consequence for the BOW `L^2` gate

WI-195 isolates the missing bow step as a height-family `L^2` problem: one needs enough genuinely distinct height information to suppress the off-diagonal contribution rather than merely relabeling the same short Gallagher source. WI-344 rules out manufacturing that portfolio by taking arbitrarily many fixed linear views of one fixed logarithmic aperture.

The present result shows that replacing those views by a **fixed-degree polynomial feature bank** still does not manufacture the needed growing independent family. Any argument whose de-exceptionalization step requires an extensive normalized Gram tail, or equivalently a number of scale-preserving independent carrier directions tending to infinity with height, must violate at least one of the fixed-`p`, fixed-aperture, height-independent-coefficient, or nonvanishing-transmission hypotheses above.

This does not prove that the bow clue is impossible. In particular, a direct arithmetic covariance identity could yield cancellation without passing through a large generic feature rank at all. Nor does the theorem constrain cross-scale constructions whose union of source supports has growing logarithmic aperture. It only removes the seemingly cheap nonlinear escape of applying a large but fixed-order polynomial feature map to the same aperture-limited carrier.

## 5. Prior art and novelty boundary

The ingredients are classical. WI-344 already records the Slepian--Pollak/Landau--Pollak time--bandwidth literature and the modern complementary-low-rank viewpoint for restricted Fourier kernels. Finite-order polynomial and Volterra representations are likewise classical in nonlinear systems and signal processing: multiplication of band-limited factors produces only finite Minkowski sums of their frequency supports, and finite-order Volterra/polynomial models organize exactly such bounded-degree products.

The proof above does not require a theorem from that literature. It is the elementary centered Taylor expansion of the exact multiplicative carrier in each tensor sector, followed by a finite direct sum and the same Gram-tail argument as WI-344. The classical literature therefore fixes the mechanism's provenance rather than serving as an unproved bridge.

A line-local novelty audit checked the live BOW clue, WI-195, and WI-344 and searched the line for fixed-degree polynomial/tensor carrier compression and nonlinear-height-window variants. WI-344 explicitly left `nonlinear observables` as an escape; no existing canonical finding located in that audit closes the fixed-degree polynomial subclass. External searches across time--bandwidth concentration, polynomial signal processing, tensor powers, and finite-order Volterra nonlinearities found the classical mechanisms but not this specific BOW/Gallagher consequence. **No priority claim is made.** The durable Mathia delta is only the exact specialization (6)--(10) and its restriction of the fixed-degree nonlinear escape left open by WI-344.

## 6. Boundary conditions and falsification controls

The claim is exact under its stated hypotheses. The main ways to evade it are structural rather than numerical:

1. `p=p(T)->infinity`: the residual frequency width grows like `p Delta`, so the fixed-rank conclusion no longer follows.
2. `H Delta->infinity`: growing time--bandwidth product is intentionally not compressed to constant dimension.
3. Height-dependent coefficients: if the feature map changes with `U`, there need not be one fixed operator `A` and new oscillation may be inserted explicitly.
4. Vanishing transmission: a filter may kill the low-order Taylor space and expose the residual, but then `eta` tends to zero and (10) records the exact conditioning price.
5. Non-polynomial/unbounded-bandwidth observables: finite tensor degree no longer controls the generated frequency support.
6. Direct arithmetic cancellation: the BOW off-diagonal term might be controlled by a signed identity or covariance theorem that does not require a large feature-space rank.

A decisive audit of the theorem itself is finite: choose any `(r,s)` sector, expand its coordinate phase as in (12)--(17), verify the residual-frequency bound `|Xi|<=p Delta/2`, apply the scalar Taylor remainder, and then check the Hilbert direct-sum and Gram-tail inequalities. A counterexample with fixed `p,H Delta,eta_0>0` whose normalized Gram matrices retain a positive spectral mass beyond every fixed rank would contradict (10) and therefore falsify the claim.

## Consequence for the research line

The fixed-aperture BOW program can no longer treat `use nonlinear features` as a generic escape from the source-rank obstruction. **Every fixed-degree polynomial/tensor algebra generated from the same carrier is still aperture-compressed.** A credible nonlinear continuation must now identify where growing complexity or genuinely new arithmetic frequency enters: increasing degree, increasing logarithmic source aperture, justified height-dependent arithmetic structure, or a non-polynomial mechanism. This is a decisive route restriction, not a new zero-density theorem and not progress toward excluding every off-critical zero by itself.
