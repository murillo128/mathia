# WI-237 — Critical-line exponentials are complete but non-Bessel; Bombieri collapse is a coefficient-budget problem

**Status:** `LITERATURE+DERIVED + EXACT-DERIVED + PRIOR-ART-REDIRECT + DECISIVE-NEGATIVE`.

WI-236 left two possible ways to prevent Bombieri's finite negative eigensector from disappearing in the infinite limit: obtain a uniform negative spectral margin/tightness theorem, or exclude the limiting zero-eigenvalue linear relations. A primary-source rereading shows that the second formulation is too coarse. Bombieri explicitly records, crediting Bourgain, that linear relations among the zero exponentials exist on intervals of arbitrary length. Independently, the now-unconditional positive-density supply of **simple critical-line** zeros already implies a stronger ambient-space obstruction: their exponentials are complete in `L^2(E)` on every bounded positive-measure window `E`, while their frequency set is too dense for the exponential family to be Bessel.

Thus no positive `L^2` angle, raw-span separation, or ordinary Riesz lower bound between an off-line packet and the critical-line exponential span can rule out Bombieri's `lambda_N -> 0` alternative. The surviving quantitative object is the **coefficient cost** of such approximation. Bombieri's limiting eigenvectors have normalized `ell^2` coefficients and put at least half of that mass on the off-line coordinates, so any relation generated by spectral collapse is much more structured than mere membership in the closed critical span. A viable no-collapse theorem must charge that bounded coefficient budget, or exploit still finer structure of the eigenvector-selected relations.

## 1. Bombieri already warns that raw linear independence is false

Section 11 of Enrico Bombieri, *Remarks on Weil's quadratic functional in the theory of prime numbers, I* (2000), analyzes the limiting eigenvalue problem attached to a finite union of intervals `E`. With

\[
F(u)=\sum_{\gamma\in\Gamma} z_\gamma e^{-i\gamma u}\chi_E(u),
\]

Bombieri obtains, under the finite-off-line hypothesis used in that section, a normalized coefficient vector

\[
\sum_{\gamma}|z_\gamma|^2=1,
\qquad
\sum_{\gamma\notin\mathbb R}|z_\gamma|^2\ge \frac12.
\tag{1}
\]

His Lemma 14 identifies the collapse alternative `lambda=0` with `F=0` identically on `E`; Theorem 11 and its corollary retain this as a genuine alternative to a negative Weil witness. Immediately afterwards Bombieri notes Bourgain's observation that relations over intervals of arbitrary length also follow from the vanishing of gaps between consecutive zero ordinates, and emphasizes that the relations relevant to his argument are distinguished because their coefficients arise as limits of eigenvectors of well-behaved finite matrices.

This is a load-bearing prior-art correction to the wording after WI-236. One cannot hope to prove that **all** locally `L^2` zero-exponential relations are absent. If the `lambda=0` branch is to be excluded, it must be excluded through properties special to Bombieri's limiting eigenvectors: coefficient size, off-line mass, truncation geometry, spectral equations, symmetry, or some source-side constraint not shared by arbitrary relations.

## 2. The unconditional simple-critical set is already complete on every bounded window

There is an elementary strengthening of this ambient-space obstruction using the modern unconditional simple-critical theorem.

Let `Lambda_sc` be the set of ordinates of simple zeros on the critical line. Alpöge--Furman prove an unconditional positive lower proportion of zeros that are simultaneously simple and critical, so the Riemann--von Mangoldt formula gives

\[
\#\{\gamma\in\Lambda_{sc}:0<\gamma\le R\}\gg R\log R.
\tag{2}
\]

Let `E subset [-A,A]` be bounded, measurable, and of positive measure. Suppose `f in L^2(E)` is orthogonal to every critical exponential `e^{-i\gamma u}` with `gamma in Lambda_sc`. Since `E` has finite measure, `f in L^1(E)`, and

\[
\mathcal F_f(z)=\int_E f(u)e^{izu}\,du
\tag{3}
\]

is an entire function of exponential type at most `A`. Orthogonality says that every `gamma in Lambda_sc` is a real zero of `mathcal F_f`.

If `mathcal F_f` were nonzero, Jensen's formula together with

\[
|\mathcal F_f(z)|\le \|f\|_{L^1(E)}e^{A|\operatorname{Im}z|}
\]

would give only `O(R)` zeros in `|z|<=R`, counted with multiplicity. This contradicts (2). Hence `mathcal F_f` is identically zero, and Fourier uniqueness for compactly supported `L^1` functions gives `f=0` almost everywhere.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\overline{\operatorname{span}}\{e^{-i\gamma u}:\gamma\in\Lambda_{sc}\}
=L^2(E)
}
\tag{4}
\]

for every bounded positive-measure `E`. This uses only the unconditional positive-density simple-critical theorem, not RH and not a conjectural pair-correlation law. In fact, the `R log R` density is far more than the `O(R)` zero budget of a nonzero finite-exponential-type transform.

A direct consequence is that any finite off-line exponential packet `g in L^2(E)` admits finite simple-critical combinations `g_n` with

\[
\|g-g_n\|_{L^2(E)}\longrightarrow0.
\tag{5}
\]

Thus no inequality of the form

\[
\operatorname{dist}_{L^2(E)}
\bigl(g,\operatorname{span}\Lambda_{sc}\bigr)
\ge c_E\|g\|_{L^2(E)},
\qquad c_E>0,
\tag{6}
\]

can hold for nonzero `g`. Any route that tries to obtain Bombieri spectral margin from a positive ambient Hilbert-space angle between the off-line packet and the critical-line span is therefore closed.

## 3. The same exponential family is not Bessel

Completeness does **not** mean that the critical exponential synthesis map is a well-conditioned frame operator. Quite the opposite.

With the conventional frequencies `lambda=gamma/(2 pi)`, (2) implies that the upper Beurling density of `Lambda_sc/(2 pi)` is infinite: already centered intervals of radius `R` contain `gg R log R` frequencies. Jean-Pierre Gabardo and Chun-Kit Lai, *Frames of multi-windowed exponentials on subsets of R^d* (2014), Lemma 3.1(i)(a), prove that on a finite-measure set a Bessel family of nonzero windowed exponentials must have finite upper Beurling density. Taking the window to be `chi_E` gives

\[
\boxed{
\{e^{-i\gamma u}:\gamma\in\Lambda_{sc}\}
\text{ is not a Bessel sequence in }L^2(E).
}
\tag{7}
\]

Equations (4) and (7) are the useful combination: the critical exponentials have enough redundancy to approximate every `L^2(E)` target, but there is no bounded global synthesis/analysis geometry on unweighted `ell^2`. Consequently a standard frame/Riesz-basis argument is not merely unproved here; it is the wrong functional-analytic model for the raw zero family.

This also explains why completeness does not itself produce Bombieri's dangerous relation. The approximants in (5) may require coefficient `ell^2` norms tending to infinity. The question left by Bombieri is precisely whether an off-line packet can be cancelled **within the normalized coefficient budget inherited from the finite negative eigenvectors**.

## 4. Spectral collapse gives a sharp coefficient-cost target

Under Bombieri's finite-off-line hypothesis, split a limiting relation from the `lambda=0` alternative into its real and nonreal coordinate parts:

\[
0=F=F_{\rm crit}+F_{\rm off}.
\tag{8}
\]

Write the corresponding coefficient vectors as `a` and `b`. From (1),

\[
\|b\|_{\ell^2}^2\ge\frac12,
\qquad
\|a\|_{\ell^2}^2\le\frac12,
\]

so necessarily

\[
\boxed{\|a\|_{\ell^2}\le\|b\|_{\ell^2}.}
\tag{9}
\]

Because only finitely many off-line coordinates occur in this branch, `F_off` is a finite exponential packet. The critical series is the locally-`L^2` limit selected by the finite eigenvectors and represents `-F_off` with no larger coefficient norm.

This suggests the correct quantitative object. For a fixed finite off-line packet `g_b=F_off`, define the bounded-coefficient approximation cost

\[
\mathcal C_E(g_b)
:=
\inf
\left\{
\liminf_{n\to\infty}\|a^{(n)}\|_{\ell^2}:
\begin{array}{l}
 a^{(n)}\text{ finitely supported on critical-zero coordinates},\\
 S_Ea^{(n)}\to-g_b\text{ in }L^2(E)
\end{array}
\right\},
\tag{10}
\]

where `S_E` denotes finite exponential synthesis before the limit is taken. Completeness says only that the admissible set in (10) is nonempty; non-Besselness warns that the coefficient costs need not stay bounded.

A sufficient anti-collapse theorem for the finite-off-line branch would be

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal C_E(g_b)>\|b\|_{\ell^2}
\quad\text{for every nonzero admissible off-line packet }g_b.
}
\tag{11}
\]

Indeed, (9) would contradict (11). The inequality in (11) is a **research target**, not a theorem proved here. It is deliberately stronger and more specific than ordinary completeness/minimality: it asks for a lower bound on the coefficient price of cancelling the exact off-line packet, normalized in the same currency as Bombieri's eigenvectors.

For an infinite sparse off-line divisor, WI-236's additional height-tightness problem remains. Even a useful coefficient-cost theorem must be formulated so that off-line `ell^2` mass cannot migrate indefinitely to higher coordinates. WI-237 therefore does not solve the infinite sparse passage-to-limit problem; it removes a false escape route and identifies the quantitative currency that a successful theorem must control.

## 5. Relation to Burnol's complete/minimal systems

Jean-François Burnol, *Two complete and minimal systems associated with the zeros of the Riemann zeta function* (2004), proves sophisticated completeness and minimality statements for zero-indexed evaluator vectors in de Branges--Sonine spaces and for associated Mellin-side systems. Those are important neighboring results, but they are **not** a theorem that Bombieri's raw interval exponentials `e^{-i gamma u}` form a minimal or Riesz family.

This distinction matters because reading Burnol's title as a raw-exponential independence theorem would appear to contradict both Bombieri's Section 11 discussion and (4). There is no contradiction: the systems and Hilbert spaces are different. Burnol is therefore a guardrail for the prior-art audit, not evidence for (11).

## 6. What routes are now closed and what remains live

The following proposed repairs of WI-236 are decisively unavailable at the raw `L^2(E)` level:

- prove that the simple-critical exponential span misses off-line packets by a positive angle;
- obtain a Riesz lower bound for the full raw critical-zero exponential family;
- rule out spectral collapse merely by asserting linear independence of all locally `L^2` zero-exponential expansions.

The first is contradicted by exact completeness (4); the second is incompatible with the Bessel obstruction (7); the third conflicts with Bombieri's own prior-art discussion of interval relations.

The viable targets are correspondingly narrower: coefficient-budget coercivity such as (11), an eigenvector-specific uncertainty principle, a weighted synthesis model adapted to Bombieri's matrix, a quantitative analysis of his even/odd or Cramer-function formulation, or a source-side theorem that constrains the special finite-truncation eigenvectors. Any of these would have to preserve the `ell^2` normalization and off-line-mass information that arbitrary ambient-space approximation discards.

## 8. Adversarial boundaries and falsification

1. **Completeness is not a Bombieri relation.** Equation (4) provides finite combinations converging in `L^2`; it does not bound their `ell^2` coefficients and does not construct a `lambda=0` eigenvector limit.

2. **Non-Besselness is not failure of every restricted synthesis estimate.** Equation (7) rules out a global unweighted Bessel/frame model. A weighted, blockwise, eigenvector-selected, or source-constrained family may still have useful coercivity.

3. **Multiplicity coordinates must not be silently collapsed.** The simple-critical subset is used only to prove completeness and infinite upper density. Bombieri's coefficient vector is indexed by his full zero multiset, with repeated points carrying their own coordinates. Any proof of (11) must keep the exact coefficient convention it uses.

4. **The coefficient-cost inequality is not established.** Equation (11) is the sharpened theorem target. Its failure would not contradict (4) or (7); it would instead show that even the normalized coefficient budget permits critical screening of an off-line packet.

5. **Finite and infinite off-line cases remain distinct.** Bombieri's Section 11 compactness argument uses finiteness of the nonreal coordinate set. Infinite sparse off-line mass can additionally escape in height, as WI-236 already records.

6. **Burnol's systems are not raw exponentials.** His minimality/completeness theorems cannot be imported as a statement about `e^{-i gamma u}` on `E` without an explicit equivalence theorem.

## Consequence for the research line

The passage-to-limit program should stop trying to exclude arbitrary zero-exponential relations or to manufacture a positive ambient angle between critical and off-line modes. The simple-critical exponentials already densely span every bounded `L^2` window, and the same family is non-Bessel. The informative datum surviving from Bombieri is therefore not span membership but **how much `ell^2` coefficient mass is required to realize the cancellation**. The next useful theorem should price that coefficient mass in the eigenvector-selected class, or connect it to an arithmetic/source constraint, while keeping WI-236's independent height-tightness requirement for an infinite sparse defect.
