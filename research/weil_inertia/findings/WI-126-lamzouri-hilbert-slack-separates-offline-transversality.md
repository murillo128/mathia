# WI-126 — Lamzouri's Hilbert inequality has an exact slack decomposition that separates off-line transversality

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + LITERATURE+DERIVED + STRUCTURAL-RIGIDITY + BOOTSTRAP-INTERFACE + PRIOR-ART-REDIRECT`. Lamzouri's 2 September 2026 preprint gives a shorter Hilbert-space proof of the same unconditional Montgomery--Taylor baseline as Alpöge--Furman. Reconstructing the finite-multiset proposition without discarding its scalar/Bessel remainders yields an exact nonnegative slack decomposition. The new structural point is that one remainder is a basis-independent quantitative charge for the anti-invariant component of every non-real conjugate pair relative to the span generated by all real points and all conjugation-even components. Consequently, finite equality is impossible in the presence of any off-real pair, while a real double point can attain equality. This does **not** yet improve the zeta proportion: the horizontal charge can tend to zero under asymptotic screening. It identifies a precise transversality quantity whose extensive lower bound would turn off-line defect directly into a stronger simple-critical-zero bound without a new prime-side moment.

## Primary-source contract

Youness Lamzouri, *A new proof that more than 2/3 of the zeros of the Riemann zeta function are simple and on the critical line*, arXiv:2609.02882v1 (2 September 2026), proves the following finite statement as Proposition 2.1. Let `Z` be a nonempty finite multiset invariant under complex conjugation, let `eta` be real, even, supported in `(-lambda,lambda)`, and normalized by `\widehat{eta^2}(0)=1`, and put

\[
K(\xi)=\widehat{\eta^2}(\xi).
\]

If `N=|Z|` counts multiplicity and `n` is the number of simple real elements, then

\[
 n\ge 2N-Q,
 \qquad
 Q:=\sum_{z,s\in Z}K(z-s)^2.
\]

Lamzouri proves this by defining

\[
f_z(u)=\eta(u)e^{-2\pi iuz},\qquad
 g_z=\frac{f_z+f_{\bar z}}2,\qquad
 h_z=\frac{f_z-f_{\bar z}}{2i},
\]

so that

\[
\|g_z\|^2-\|h_z\|^2=1,
\]

and by introducing nested subspaces `U subset V subset W`: `U` is generated by the repeated-real vectors and all `g_z`; `V` additionally contains all simple-real vectors; `W` additionally contains all `h_z`. For an adapted orthonormal basis `(psi_j)` of `W`, the tensor

\[
F(u,v)=\sum_{z\in Z}f_z(u)f_z(v)
\]

has diagonal coefficients

\[
\alpha_j=\langle F,\psi_j\otimes\psi_j\rangle,
\]

with

\[
Q=\|F\|^2\ge\sum_j\alpha_j^2,
\qquad
\sum_j\alpha_j=N.
\]

On the last block `W\ominus V`, Lamzouri has exactly

\[
\alpha_j=-2\sum_{z\in Z_+}m_z\langle h_z,\psi_j\rangle^2\le0,
\]

where `Z_+` contains one representative of each non-real conjugate pair. The paper then applies coarse scalar inequalities to the three basis ranges. Its Section 3 combines the resulting proposition with the unconditional Baluyot--Goldston--Suriajaya--Turnage-Butterbaugh pair-correlation formula and recovers

\[
C_0=\frac32-\frac1{\sqrt2}\cot\!\left(\frac1{\sqrt2}\right)
=0.672500703679\ldots.
\]

The headline theorem and the finite Hilbert-space proposition are also formalized in `AxiomMath/ZetaZeros`; the public README states that its challenge has been locally checked with the Lean comparator. The stability decomposition below is **not** imported from that formalization.

## Exact dimensions: the middle block really has size `n`

Lamzouri only needs the inequalities `D_U<=r+k` and `D_V-D_U<=n`. For the stability bookkeeping one can sharpen these to equalities.

The family of vectors `f_s(u)=eta(u)e^{-2 pi ius}` attached to finitely many distinct complex numbers `s` is linearly independent. Indeed, if

\[
\eta(u)\sum_s c_s e^{-2\pi ius}=0
\]

almost everywhere, then `eta` is nonzero on a set of positive measure because `\|eta\|_2>0`. The exponential polynomial therefore vanishes on a positive-measure real set and hence, by analyticity, identically; distinct exponential frequencies then force every `c_s=0`. For each non-real pair the change of variables

\[
(f_z,f_{\bar z})\longleftrightarrow(g_z,h_z)
\]

is invertible. Therefore the full family consisting of all real `f_x` and all non-real `g_z,h_z` is linearly independent, and if `r` is the number of distinct repeated real points and `k` the number of distinct non-real conjugate pairs,

\[
\boxed{
\dim U=r+k,\qquad
\dim V=n+r+k,\qquad
\dim W=n+r+2k.
}
\]

In particular the middle block `M:=V\ominus U` has exactly `n` basis vectors and the horizontal block `H:=W\ominus V` has exactly `k`.

## Exact nonnegative slack decomposition

Let

\[
d=r+k,\qquad
A:=\sum_{j\in U}\alpha_j,
\]

and define the Bessel remainder

\[
R_B:=Q-\sum_{j\in W}\alpha_j^2\ge0.
\]

Since `sum alpha_j=N` and `|M|=n`, direct algebra gives the exact identity

\[
\boxed{
Q-(2N-n)=R_B+R_U+R_M+R_H,
}
\]

where

\[
R_U:=\sum_{j\in U}(\alpha_j^2-2\alpha_j),
\qquad
R_M:=\sum_{j\in M}(\alpha_j-1)^2,
\qquad
R_H:=\sum_{j\in H}(\alpha_j^2-2\alpha_j).
\]

Every term is nonnegative. For `d>0`,

\[
R_U
=\sum_{j\in U}\left(\alpha_j-\frac Ad\right)^2
 +\frac{A(A-2d)}d,
\]

and Lamzouri's argument proves `A>=2d`; for `d=0` set `R_U=0`. The middle term is visibly nonnegative. On `H`, every `alpha_j<=0`, so `alpha_j^2-2alpha_j>=0`.

Thus the published inequality is not merely bounded by several unrelated estimates: it is the result of discarding four explicit nonnegative remainders. Equivalently,

\[
\boxed{
n=2N-Q+R_B+R_U+R_M+R_H.
}
\]

This is the finite identity relevant to defect accounting.

## The horizontal remainder quantitatively charges non-real pairs

For `j in H`, put `b_j=-alpha_j>=0`. Lamzouri's exact formula gives

\[
b_j=2\sum_{z\in Z_+}m_z\langle h_z,\psi_j\rangle^2.
\]

Hence

\[
R_H=\sum_{j\in H}(b_j^2+2b_j)
\ge2\sum_{j\in H}b_j.
\]

Parseval on `H=W\ominus V` now removes the choice of basis:

\[
\sum_{j\in H}b_j
=2\sum_{z\in Z_+}m_z\|P_Hh_z\|^2
=2\sum_{z\in Z_+}m_z\operatorname{dist}(h_z,V)^2.
\]

Therefore Proposition 2.1 has the unconditional finite strengthening

\[
\boxed{
 n\ge 2N-Q
 +4\sum_{z\in Z_+}m_z\operatorname{dist}(h_z,V)^2.
}
\]

The full exact remainder is stronger than this displayed inequality; the point of the latter is that it isolates a geometrically interpretable off-real contribution.

Linear independence also implies `h_z notin V` for every non-real representative `z`. Thus every finite multiset containing a non-real pair has

\[
\operatorname{dist}(h_z,V)>0
\]

for each such pair, and consequently

\[
Q>2N-n.
\]

So exact equality in Lamzouri's simple-real inequality is impossible in the presence of non-real points.

This strictness genuinely distinguishes off-line defect from critical-line multiplicity. A single real point of multiplicity two has `N=2`, `n=0`, `Q=4`, and attains equality `Q=2N-n`. Critical-line doubles can therefore remain zero-cost even though non-real pairs cannot at finite dimension.

## The `U`-slack further separates multiplicity excess

The same reconstruction makes Lamzouri's estimate `A>=2d` exact. Because repeated-real `f_x` and all `g_z` lie in `U`, while simple-real vectors contribute only through their projection,

\[
A
=\sum_{x\in R_1}\|P_Uf_x\|^2
 +\sum_{x\in R_2}m_x
 +2\sum_{z\in Z_+}m_z\bigl(1+\|P_{U^\perp}h_z\|^2\bigr).
\]

Subtracting `2d=2r+2k` gives

\[
\boxed{
A-2d
=\sum_{x\in R_1}\|P_Uf_x\|^2
 +\sum_{x\in R_2}(m_x-2)
 +2\sum_{z\in Z_+}
 \left[(m_z-1)+m_z\|P_{U^\perp}h_z\|^2\right].
}
\]

This identifies exactly which multiplicity patterns are free at this stage: a real double has no intrinsic `A-2d` charge, whereas real multiplicity `>2`, non-real pair multiplicity `>1`, simple-real leakage into `U`, and anti-invariant leakage all create positive slack.

## Equality and near-equality geometry

Exact equality `Q=2N-n` requires all four remainders to vanish simultaneously. In particular:

1. Bessel must be exact: `F` lies in the closed span of the diagonal tensors `psi_j tensor psi_j` used by the adapted basis.
2. On `U`, the coefficients must saturate the variance/scalar step: `A=2d` and all `alpha_j=2`.
3. On `M`, every `alpha_j=1`.
4. On `H`, every `alpha_j=0`; by the preceding transversality argument this forces `k=0`, so there are no non-real points.

Thus any near-extremizer with substantial off-real mass must make the anti-invariant vectors `h_z` collectively almost contained in the global space `V`. The finite theorem converts the informal idea of "screening" into an exact subspace-angle condition.

## Zeta consequence and bootstrap interface

Apply the proposition to Lamzouri's scaled zeta-zero multiset. Real points in the scaled coordinate are precisely critical-line zeros; non-real conjugate pairs come from the functional-equation symmetry of off-line zeros. For any fixed admissible `eta`, the unconditional pair-correlation theorem evaluates `Q/N` asymptotically, while the identity above keeps the discarded geometric remainder:

\[
\frac{N_0^s(T)}{N(T)}
\ge
2-\frac{Q_T}{N(T)}
+\frac{4}{N(T)}
\sum_{z\in Z_{T,+}}m_z\operatorname{dist}(h_z,V_T)^2.
\]

Therefore any theorem of the form

\[
\liminf_{T\to\infty}
\frac1{N(T)}
\sum_{z\in Z_{T,+}}m_z\operatorname{dist}(h_z,V_T)^2
\ge c>0
\]

would give a strict additive gain `4c` over the corresponding Lamzouri pair-correlation baseline, with no higher prime-side trace moment. To beat the current WI-036 value `0.672852930121...` starting from the Montgomery--Taylor limit `0.672500703679...`, an MT-approaching family would need, after controlling the `eta_epsilon` limit, normalized horizontal transversality exceeding roughly

\[
\frac{0.6728529301211843-0.6725007036794116}{4}
\approx 8.806\times10^{-5}.
\]

This is a concrete defect-to-zero gate, not a proof that such a uniform `c` exists.

## Relation to the screening frontier

There is no contradiction with WI-005--WI-007, WI-119, or the more recent mesoscopic screening findings. The new statement is strictly finite-dimensional. As an off-line pair approaches the critical line, `h_z` itself tends to zero; and as the configuration grows, support-one critical sampling can make an anti-invariant vector increasingly well approximated by `V`. Hence finite strictness gives no count-only uniform lower bound for the distance term.

The formulation also clarifies the external-cancellation issue left by WI-125. WI-125 forces alias energy for a **selected periodic exceptional cell**. Here `V_T` is the **global** span generated by all real zero vectors and all conjugation-even components, including zeros outside a selected cell. Those outside vectors can in principle absorb the local alias. In this language, the unresolved extraction problem is exactly to prove that the WI-125 alias packet has a component transverse to the global `V_T`, not merely to a local screening span.

This distinction is useful: local Bragg/alias rigidity and global Hilbert transversality are not two unrelated obstacles. The latter is the precise quantity that must survive outside cancellation for the former to improve the zeta bound.

## Falsification controls and next tests

The result would fail if the exact dimension equalities above failed, if the adapted-basis coefficients on `H` were not real/nonpositive, or if Parseval did not identify the horizontal projection. Each point is directly checkable from Lamzouri's definitions: normalization makes `eta` nonzero on positive measure; the change `(f_z,f_barz)<->(g_z,h_z)` is invertible; Lamzouri proves the reality property of the adapted Gram--Schmidt basis; and `H=W\ominus V` by construction.

The main unresolved mathematical gate is not the finite inequality but **uniform transversality at zeta scale**. A useful next attack is to test whether the mesoscopic/Fujii constraints and the first-half alias energy of WI-121--WI-125 imply a lower bound on `dist(h_z,V_T)` after allowing the full outside-zero span. A decisive negative construction approximating all relevant `h_z` by the global `V_T` while respecting those arithmetic constraints would be equally substantive, because it would show that Lamzouri's newly exposed remainder cannot bootstrap the present support-one method by itself.
