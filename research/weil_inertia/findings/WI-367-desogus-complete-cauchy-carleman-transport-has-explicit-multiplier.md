# WI-367 — Desogus's complete Cauchy–Carleman transport has an explicit endpoint multiplier

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + PRIMARY-SOURCE-CRITICAL-AUDIT + REPAIR-ADVANCE + NON-ESTABLISHED-RH`.

## Claim

The diagonal multiplier isolated in WI-363 is a real term in the half-density transport of the **bare** Cauchy difference form, but it is not by itself a fatal obstruction to Marco Desogus's source-to-cell normalization. Once the source form is completed by the source Carleman term and one explicit endpoint multiplier, the transport is exact and lands on the normalized Legendre-plus-gamma block used by the preprint.

Let `A>0`, `Y=e^A`, and

\[
(U_Af)(u)=\sqrt{Ae^{Au}}\,f(e^{Au}),
\qquad 0<u<1.
\tag{1}
\]

On `C_c^\infty(1,Y)` define the singular Cauchy difference form

\[
q_C[f]
=
\frac14\int_1^Y\!\int_1^Y
\frac{|f(x)-f(y)|^2}{|x-y|}\,dx\,dy,
\tag{2}
\]

the source Carleman operator

\[
(K_{\rm Carl}f)(x)=\int_1^Y\frac{f(y)}{x+y}\,dy,
\tag{3}
\]

and the multiplier

\[
\boxed{
m_A(x)
=
\log x
-\frac12\log((x-1)(Y-x))
-\log(2\pi)-\gamma.
}
\tag{4}
\]

Then the completed source form

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak c_A[f]
=
q_C[f]
-\frac12\langle f,K_{\rm Carl}f\rangle
+\int_1^Y m_A(x)|f(x)|^2\,dx
}
\tag{5}
\]

satisfies the exact transported identity

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak c_A[U_A^{-1}g]
=
\mathfrak b[g]
-\bigl(\log A+\log(2\pi)+\gamma\bigr)\|g\|_2^2
-
A\!\int_0^1\!\int_0^1
\rho(A|u-v|)g(u)\overline{g(v)}\,du\,dv,
}
\tag{6}
\]

where

\[
\mathfrak b[g]
=
\frac14\int_0^1\!\int_0^1
\frac{|g(u)-g(v)|^2}{|u-v|}\,du\,dv
-
\frac12\int_0^1\log(u(1-u))|g(u)|^2\,du,
\tag{7}
\]

and

\[
\rho(z)=\frac{e^{-z/2}}{1-e^{-2z}}-\frac1{2z}.
\tag{8}
\]

Thus WI-363's missing diagonal bookkeeping is **repairable exactly**. However, equation (6) also shows that the regular gamma term has not disappeared: it survives with the target sign and coefficient. This repair therefore does not establish the later gamma-free one-cell collar asserted in Section 5 of the preprint, nor does it recover the fixed bare-Carleman OSPR reserve ruled out as a uniform comparison mechanism by WI-366. The remaining gate is narrower and more precise: justify the downstream passage from the exact full archimedean form (6) to the claimed gamma-free collar/reserve bookkeeping.

## 1. Bare Cauchy transport and the WI-363 multiplier

WI-363 derived the exact transport of (2):

\[
q_C[U_A^{-1}g]
=
\frac14\int_0^1\!\int_0^1
K_A(u,v)|g(u)-g(v)|^2\,du\,dv
+
\int_0^1W_A(u)|g(u)|^2\,du,
\tag{9}
\]

with

\[
K_A(u,v)
=
\frac{Ae^{-A|u-v|/2}}{1-e^{-A|u-v|}},
\tag{10}
\]

and

\[
\begin{aligned}
W_A(u)
&=
\frac{A(1-u)}2-2\log2
+\log\!\left(1+e^{-A(1-u)/2}\right)
+\log\!\left(1+e^{-Au/2}\right)\\
&=
\frac{A(1-2u)}4
+\log\cosh\frac{Au}{4}
+\log\cosh\frac{A(1-u)}4.
\end{aligned}
\tag{11}
\]

The point of WI-363 remains valid: the multiplier `W_A` is generated by transporting the bare difference form and cannot simply be erased by saying that `U_A` is unitary. The present finding instead asks the more precise question left implicit by Proposition 5.3: which **complete source form** would make the claimed normalized target identity true?

## 2. The source Carleman term fixes the off-diagonal kernel exactly

Under (1), the source kernel `1/(x+y)` becomes

\[
H_A(r)=\frac{Ae^{-Ar/2}}{1+e^{-Ar}},
\qquad r=|u-v|.
\tag{12}
\]

The off-diagonal operator kernel carried by the first term in (9) is `-K_A(r)/2`, while the transported term `-K_{\rm Carl}/2` contributes `-H_A(r)/2`. Their half-sum is elementary:

\[
\frac12\bigl(K_A(r)+H_A(r)\bigr)
=
A\frac{e^{-Ar/2}}{1-e^{-2Ar}}
=
\frac1{2r}+A\rho(Ar).
\tag{13}
\]

Hence the combined off-diagonal contribution is

\[
\boxed{
-\frac12\bigl(K_A(r)+H_A(r)\bigr)
=-\frac1{2r}-A\rho(Ar).
}
\tag{14}
\]

This is exactly the off-diagonal kernel of `\mathfrak b-K_{\gamma,A}` in (6)--(8). In particular, the gamma correction is encoded in the Cauchy--Carleman half-sum; it has not vanished.

## 3. Exact diagonal discrepancy

After matching the off-diagonal kernel, the remaining diagonal contribution before adding a source multiplier is

\[
D_A(u)
=
W_A(u)
+
\frac12\int_0^1
\left[
\frac{A}{2\sinh(A|u-v|/2)}-
\frac1{|u-v|}
\right]dv.
\tag{15}
\]

For `L>0`, direct integration gives the exact identity

\[
\boxed{
\int_0^L
\left[
\frac{A}{2\sinh(At/2)}-
\frac1t
\right]dt
=
\log\frac{4\tanh(AL/4)}{AL}.
}
\tag{16}
\]

Splitting (15) at `v=u` therefore yields

\[
D_A(u)
=
W_A(u)
+
\frac12\log\frac{4\tanh(Au/4)}{Au}
+
\frac12\log\frac{4\tanh(A(1-u)/4)}{A(1-u)}.
\tag{17}
\]

Using (11), this simplifies to

\[
\boxed{
D_A(u)
=
\frac{A(1-2u)}4
+
\frac12\log\frac{2\sinh(Au/2)}{Au}
+
\frac12\log\frac{2\sinh(A(1-u)/2)}{A(1-u)}.
}
\tag{18}
\]

The target multiplication-plus-scalar term in (6)--(7) is

\[
V_0(u)+c_A
=
-\frac12\log(u(1-u))
-\log A-\log(2\pi)-\gamma.
\tag{19}
\]

Therefore the source multiplier required to complete the transport is characterized by

\[
m_A(e^{Au})=V_0(u)+c_A-D_A(u).
\tag{20}
\]

Substituting (18), using `x=e^{Au}` and `Y=e^A`, and simplifying the hyperbolic functions gives exactly

\[
\boxed{
m_A(x)
=
\log x
-\frac12\log((x-1)(Y-x))
-\log(2\pi)-\gamma,
}
\tag{21}
\]

which is (4). Thus (6) follows by exact change of variables. No numerical quadrature, asymptotics, or conjectural zeta input enters this repair.

Among multiplication corrections added to the fixed source combination `q_C-K_{\rm Carl}/2`, equation (21) is forced up to almost-everywhere equality by requiring the target equality (6).

## 4. Relation to Desogus's printed argument

Equation (21) supplies exactly the missing diagonal completion needed for the **full normalized archimedean identity**. \tag{22}

Consequently the completed transport does **not** by itself imply the gamma-free collar claimed later in the same section. \[

## 7. Falsification and scope boundaries

The result is directly falsifiable on the common test domain. Starting from (2)--(5), apply (1); any sign or coefficient error must appear either in the off-diagonal identity (13) or in the diagonal identity (20)--(21). Conversely, if Desogus intended a different source definition of `\mathfrak c_A`, that definition must be shown exactly equivalent to (5) on the common form domain before Proposition 5.3 can inherit (6).

This finding does **not** prove or disprove RH, and it does not claim that the preprint's final theorem is false. It does not show that no later gamma cancellation is possible; it shows only that such a cancellation is an additional step not supplied by the exact source-to-cell transport reconstructed here. It also does not revive the uniform `J_A\succeq cK` repair excluded by WI-366.

The endpoint logarithms in (4) are locally integrable on `(1,Y)`. Extending (5)--(6) from `C_c^\infty(1,Y)` to a closed common form domain requires the standard domain/closure checks and should be made explicit in any theorem-level repair. The identity on the smooth compactly supported core is exact and sufficient for auditing the printed formal transport.
