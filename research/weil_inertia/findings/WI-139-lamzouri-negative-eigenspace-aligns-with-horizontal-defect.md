# WI-139 — near-sharp Lamzouri inertia forces the negative eigenspace into the horizontal defect block

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + LITERATURE+DERIVED + STRUCTURAL-RIGIDITY + BOOTSTRAP-INTERFACE`. WI-137 identifies Lamzouri's complete finite slack with the Hilbert--Schmidt distance of the self-adjoint tensor operator `A_F` from the quantized target `D=P_U+P_V`, and WI-138 proves that the negative index of `A_F` is exactly the number `k` of distinct off-line conjugate pairs. Those two facts imply a stronger rigidity statement that is not contained in the eigenvalue-only Hoffman--Wielandt estimate: the **negative spectral subspace itself** must align with Lamzouri's horizontal defect space `H=W\ominus V` whenever the finite inequality is near sharp.

Let

\[
\Delta:=n-(2N-Q),\qquad
\mathcal D:=P_U+P_V=2P_U+P_M,
\qquad M:=V\ominus U,
\qquad H:=W\ominus V,
\]

and put

\[
E:=\mathcal A_F-\mathcal D.
\]

Let `P_-` be the negative spectral projection of `A_F`. By WI-138,

\[
\operatorname{rank}P_-=k=\dim H.
\]

If `lambda_1,...,lambda_k<0` are the negative eigenvalues with orthonormal eigenvectors `v_1,...,v_k`, then the exact block identity below gives

\[
\boxed{
\Delta\ge
\sum_{j=1}^k\lambda_j^2
+4\sum_{j=1}^k(1-\lambda_j)\|P_Uv_j\|^2
+\sum_{j=1}^k(1-2\lambda_j)\|P_Mv_j\|^2.
}
\tag{A}
\]

In particular,

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{j=1}^k\lambda_j^2
+\frac12\|P_- - P_H\|_{\rm HS}^2
\le\Delta,
}
\tag{B}
\]

and the stronger weighted orientation bound

\[
\boxed{
4\operatorname{tr}(P_-P_U)+\operatorname{tr}(P_-P_M)
\le\Delta-\sum_{j=1}^k\lambda_j^2.
}
\tag{C}
\]

Thus a positive-density off-line population near saturation cannot hide its negative index in an arbitrary almost-zero spectral cloud. Its negative eigenspace must converge, in Hilbert--Schmidt density, to the **specific** `k`-dimensional quotient `H=W\ominus V` generated by the residual anti-invariant directions. Coupled with WI-136, this gives a singular two-scale picture: the original odd generators `h_z` must become almost contained in the smaller exceptional span `U`, while the negative eigenspace of the signed tensor simultaneously becomes almost orthogonal to all of `V` and aligns with the tiny residual quotient of those same odd generators.

## 2. Exact residual identity on each negative eigenvector

Let

\[
\mathcal A_Fv_j=\lambda_jv_j,
\qquad \lambda_j<0,
\qquad \|v_j\|=1.
\]

Because

\[
\mathcal D=2P_U+P_M
\]

and `U`, `M`, `H` are mutually orthogonal, the eigenvalue equation gives the three exact block relations

\[
P_UEv_j=(\lambda_j-2)P_Uv_j,
\]

\[
P_MEv_j=(\lambda_j-1)P_Mv_j,
\]

\[
P_HEv_j=\lambda_jP_Hv_j.
\tag{4}
\]

Taking squared norms and using

\[
\|P_Uv_j\|^2+\|P_Mv_j\|^2+\|P_Hv_j\|^2=1
\]

yields the exact identity

\[
\begin{aligned}
\|Ev_j\|^2
&=(2-\lambda_j)^2\|P_Uv_j\|^2
 +(1-\lambda_j)^2\|P_Mv_j\|^2
 +\lambda_j^2\|P_Hv_j\|^2\\
&=\lambda_j^2
 +4(1-\lambda_j)\|P_Uv_j\|^2
 +(1-2\lambda_j)\|P_Mv_j\|^2.
\end{aligned}
\tag{5}
\]

The vectors `v_j` are orthonormal, so summing (5) gives

\[
\|EP_-\|_{\rm HS}^2
=
\sum_{j=1}^k\lambda_j^2
+4\sum_{j=1}^k(1-\lambda_j)\|P_Uv_j\|^2
+\sum_{j=1}^k(1-2\lambda_j)\|P_Mv_j\|^2.
\tag{6}
\]

Since right multiplication by an orthogonal projection cannot increase Hilbert--Schmidt norm,

\[
\|EP_-\|_{\rm HS}^2\le\|E\|_{\rm HS}^2\le\Delta,
\]

which proves (A).

This is stronger than simply applying Hoffman--Wielandt to the negative eigenvalues. It retains the **orientation** of every negative eigenvector relative to Lamzouri's nested `2/1/0` target blocks.

## 3. Principal-angle form

Because every `lambda_j<0`, equation (A) implies

\[
\Delta\ge
\sum_j\lambda_j^2
+4\operatorname{tr}(P_-P_U)
+\operatorname{tr}(P_-P_M).
\tag{7}
\]

In particular,

\[
\operatorname{tr}(P_-P_V)
\le
\Delta-\sum_j\lambda_j^2.
\tag{8}
\]

By (3), `P_-` and `P_H` have the same rank `k`. Since `P_H=I-P_V` on `W`, the standard projection identity gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
\|P_- -P_H\|_{\rm HS}^2
&=2k-2\operatorname{tr}(P_-P_H)\\
&=2\operatorname{tr}(P_-P_V).
\end{aligned}
\tag{9}
\]

Combining (8) and (9) proves (B).

If `theta_1,...,theta_k` are the principal angles between `Ran(P_-)` and `H`, then

\[
\sum_{j=1}^k\sin^2\theta_j
=\operatorname{tr}(P_-P_V)
\le\Delta-\sum_j\lambda_j^2.
\tag{10}
\]

Thus the same finite slack budget simultaneously pays for negative-eigenvalue magnitude and for rotation of the negative eigenspace away from the horizontal quotient. There is no independent reservoir for the two effects.

A threshold consequence is immediate. For every `epsilon>0`,

\[
\#\{j:|\lambda_j|\ge\epsilon\}
\le\frac{\Delta}{\epsilon^2},
\tag{11}
\]

while

\[
\#\{j:\sin\theta_j\ge\epsilon\}
\le\frac{\Delta}{\epsilon^2}.
\tag{12}
\]

The first estimate was already implicit in WI-137/WI-138; the second is the new eigenspace constraint.

## 4. Near-extremizer consequence for off-line mass

Suppose a sequence of Lamzouri zeta configurations satisfies

\[
\Delta=o(N)
\tag{13}
\]

and contains a positive density of distinct off-line conjugate pairs,

\[
k\ge\delta N
\qquad(\delta>0).
\tag{14}
\]

Then (B) gives simultaneously

\[
\frac1k\sum_{j=1}^k\lambda_j^2=o(1)
\tag{15}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
\frac1k\sum_{j=1}^k\sin^2\theta_j=o(1).
}
\tag{16}
\]

Hence for every fixed `epsilon>0`, all but `o(N)` negative directions both have eigenvalue in `(-epsilon,0)` and principal angle at most `arcsin(epsilon)` from `H`.

This refines the WI-138 statement that extensive off-line mass requires a macroscopic near-zero negative spectral cloud. The cloud cannot be supported by a rotating collection of almost-null directions elsewhere in `W`: almost all of its subspace mass must lie in the canonical horizontal block.

The weighted estimate (C) adds an asymmetry that matches Lamzouri's target. Leakage of the negative eigenspace into `U`, where the target eigenvalue is `2`, costs at least four times as much at quadratic order as the same squared overlap with the simple-real layer `M`, where the target eigenvalue is `1`.

## 5. Coupling to WI-136: screening must become singular

WI-136 proves that near saturation forces the **generating odd vectors** themselves toward `U`:

\[
\sum_{z\in Z_+}m_z\operatorname{dist}(h_z,U)^2=o(N).
\tag{17}
\]

At first sight this points in the opposite geometric direction from (16), because `U\perp H`. There is no contradiction. The residual vectors

\[
r_z:=P_Hh_z
\tag{18}
\]

are nonzero for every finite off-line pair and span `H`, but they may have very small norms. WI-136 says the original columns can be almost swallowed by `U`; WI-139 says that after the signed tensor is diagonalized, its negative spectral subspace is nevertheless forced to align with the span of the small residuals `r_z`.

Therefore a positive-density off-line near-extremizer must exhibit a genuinely singular synthesis geometry: a macroscopic family of odd columns is almost represented by the exceptional positive span, while the linearly independent residual quotient still carries essentially the entire negative eigenspace and all its eigenvalues collapse toward zero.

This is stronger than either ingredient separately. Small negative eigenvalues alone permit arbitrary eigenspace rotation, and small odd residuals alone do not identify where the signed tensor's negative sector lives. The combination fixes both amplitude and orientation of the collapse.

## 6. Controls and limits

For one isolated off-line conjugate pair, `k=1` and `H` is one-dimensional. The negative eigenspace need not equal `H` at finite slack, but (B) measures exactly how far it may rotate. If the finite deficit is driven to zero along a screened family, both the negative eigenvalue and the angle to `H` must vanish.

A real double has `k=0`, so (A)--(C) are vacuous, as required. This finding therefore does not relabel critical-line multiplicity as off-line defect. Higher multiplicity and the other Lamzouri remainders remain separately charged exactly as in WI-126/WI-137.

The result does **not** supply a uniform spectral gap, a determinant lower bound, or a new unconditional zero proportion. It also does not rule out a construction in which the odd synthesis map becomes sufficiently ill-conditioned that a positive-density family of tiny residuals spans `H` while the corresponding negative eigenvalues tend to zero. Such a construction is now the correct falsifier for this orientation-aware bootstrap.
