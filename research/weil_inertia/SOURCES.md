# Weil-inertia literature anchors

This file records durable sources that support or constrain findings in `research/weil_inertia/`. It is not a reading log. Claims from recent unrefereed preprints are explicitly separated from the established baseline.

## Primary theorem and formal verification

- Youness Lamzouri, **A new proof that more than 2/3 of the zeros of the Riemann zeta function are simple and on the critical line**, arXiv:2609.02882v1 (2 Sep 2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.02882. Role for WI-126: new primary Hilbert-space proof of the same unconditional Montgomery--Taylor constant; Proposition 2.1 replaces the finite Weil-form matrix compression by a conjugation-adapted nested-subspace/Bessel inequality. WI-126 reconstructs the discarded nonnegative slack terms and isolates a quantitative horizontal transversality charge; that stability refinement is derived in Mathia, not claimed by the preprint.
