# MI-052 — A common-complement short can preserve source capacity and inherited-pivot surplus without double spending

**Evidence level:** exact/literature-backed synthesis from [WI-361](../../findings/WI-361-desogus-source-exact-cofinal-rooted-schur-induction.md) and the independent analytic reconstruction in [WI-362](../../findings/WI-362-desogus-common-cut-and-pivot-chain-is-exact-given-the-localized-form.md). The normalized one-cell localized form is a granted hypothesis here; no RH conclusion is inferred.

WI-361 identifies a potentially relevant feature of Desogus's Gate II architecture: several arithmetic source slots are eliminated through one common complement rather than by summing independently optimized one-source lower bounds. That distinction matters because the positive Cauchy--Carleman/exterior reserve is a shared physical resource and cannot be spent once per source.

WI-362 reconstructs this accounting directly inside the normalized one-cell form. For disjoint equal-length mean-zero source intervals, the source--source pair energy supplies exactly the logarithmic complement charge that would otherwise be missing from each source when another source occupies part of its complement. The resulting lower bound holds **before** minimization over one common extension. Taking one common infimum therefore preserves simultaneous source capacity without adding independently extracted reserves.

The same principle survives when source means are free. Splitting the one-cell form into a baseline rank-one part and a nonnegative residual separates the slot-ground budget from the within-slot mean-zero budget before the common infimum. The residual constant is controlled by the telescoping series `sum_j delta_j=1-gamma`, while the baseline short is an explicit rank-one Schur complement. Both pieces live in the same physical metric, so their combination is not reserve duplication.

Variational order then gives the key inherited-pivot transfer. If the exact form dominates the one-defect baseline for every extension variable, the short of the exact form dominates the short of the baseline on the inherited line. Because the actual and reference inherited source vectors are collinear there, the reference-to-actual difference produces a genuine positive comparator surplus. The later mixed constants and response-placement algebra are consistent with this same pivot rather than introducing an unrelated positive term.

The reusable lesson is that **shared reserve accounting must be established pointwise before elimination**. Independent lower bounds obtained after separate minimizations are unsafe to add; one common lower form plus one common short can be safe when source--source interactions explicitly replace the occupied complement and the remaining budgets arise from disjoint pieces of the same form. Monotonicity of the variational short then propagates the lower model without creating new positivity.

This moves the Desogus audit rather than completing it. The next load-bearing question is whether the full localized zeta Weil operator really enters this exact one-cell metric with every routed source, exterior reserve and fold term placed as assumed, and whether the global intertwining plus finite/analytic certificates preserve that placement cofinally.

**Boundary.** WI-362 validates the common-cut/source-capacity, baseline/residual, inherited-pivot and local constant subchain only after granting the normalized one-cell form. It does not establish the upstream source-to-cell identification, the full common-cut/fold intertwining, the `Y=7` interval certificate, MASTER sweeps, analytic tail, cofinal domain passage or RH. The intuition is therefore a reserve-accounting principle plus a partial audit result, not a positivity theorem.
