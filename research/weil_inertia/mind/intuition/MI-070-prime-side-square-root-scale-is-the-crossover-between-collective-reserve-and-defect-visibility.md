# MI-070 — The square-root prime scale is the crossover between collective reserve and sparse-defect visibility

**Evidence level:** exact RH-conditional phase-counting extension and RH-independent defect-envelope comparison from [WI-393](../../findings/WI-393-bandlimited-phase-counting-reaches-the-prime-defect-crossover.md), sharpening MI-068/MI-069.

The dilute-carrier positive floor survives much farther than the original `A=o(log log R)` phase argument suggested. A fixed Beurling--Selberg minorant inserted directly into the explicit formula gives an RH-side order-one reserve whenever

`e^(A/2)/log R -> 0`,

so one may approach `A=2 log log R-omega(1)`. The shifted Fourier bands created by the modulation sample prime powers near `n~e^A`, and absolute Chebyshev control prices their total contribution at the square-root scale `O(e^(A/2))` against a local zero-density main term of order `log R`.

The same ratio controls the worst sparse-defect envelope from WI-392. In the regime `e^(A/2)/log R -> 0`, every fixed number of co-moving off-critical zeros is still individually `o(1)` in the carrier channel even though the collective critical-line packet supplies an order-one RH floor. The two effects meet at

`e^(A/2) ~ log R`, equivalently `A=2 log log R+O(1)`.

At that crossover, the shifted prime-power term is itself order `log R` exactly when the worst critical-strip defect envelope first becomes order one. The obstruction is therefore not an arbitrary technical limit of the old phase estimate: **absolute prime-side control certifies the collective reserve only up to the same scale at which sparse defects can become visible**.

At the crossover the relevant prime polynomial is supported around `n~e^A~(log R)^2`. Moving beyond it in a defect-anchored argument requires new arithmetic information: cancellation of that short prime polynomial at the exceptional ordinate, a source-exact test with stronger per-defect response, or a multi-packet/global coupling that aggregates sparse defects before dilution erases them. Merely increasing the carrier separation is not a bootstrap.

For a fixed horizontal depth `|delta|<1/2`, the individual response scale `e^(A|delta|)/log R` reaches order one only after `A~(1/|delta|)log log R`, strictly beyond the square-root-prime crossover. Thus the prime-side cancellation problem becomes load-bearing before this scalar channel can robustly amplify an isolated fixed-depth defect.

The durable audit is to compare any moving-frequency converse mechanism against both sides of this same scale: how the on-line reserve is certified and how one off-line zero is amplified. A positive RH floor obtained by summing many `1/log R` contributions is not yet coercive if the arithmetic estimate needed to preserve that floor fails before one defect reaches order one.

**Boundary.** WI-393 is not an impossibility theorem beyond the crossover. The short prime polynomial may cancel, favorable carrier centers may exist when `R` is free, and other channels may be more defect-sensitive. The barrier concerns the uniform-in-`R`, defect-anchored dilute-carrier route using absolute prime-side estimates; it proves no unconditional positive Weil gap and no RH equivalence.
