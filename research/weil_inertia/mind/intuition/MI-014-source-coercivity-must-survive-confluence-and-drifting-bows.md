# MI-014 — Weil-inertia coercivity must convert source-weighted localization into a zero budget

**Evidence level:** exact rank/source tariffs, sharp compact-detector localization order, and current short-interval pair-statistics boundary through WI-226

## Core intuition

The bow obstruction has passed the point where better free screens or better scalar windows are likely to help. Remoteness has a proved source cost, and the best fixed-support scalar detector architecture has a sharp localization scale. What remains is a conversion theorem from that localized weighted source into something the actual zeta zero population cannot afford.

Generic local zero statistics do not automatically make that conversion. Their support/proportion range can miss the surviving bow regime and their scalar form can discard the very source orientation that screening exploits.

## Strongest justified principle

WI-218--WI-221 show that fixed-band cancellation costs linear channel rank and that remote cancellation forces rapidly growing weighted source mass. WI-222--WI-223 turn this into progressively strong compact-detector localization but identify an uncertainty obstruction to exponential localization with one fixed compact detector.

WI-224 allows `T`-dependent uniformly conditioned compact spline detectors and attains dimensionless cutoff `O(log T)`. WI-225 uses quantitative Logvinenko--Sereda to prove the matching `Omega(log T)` floor for every normalized fixed-support scalar detector that must beat the polynomial total-mass factor. The scalar architecture is therefore sharp at physical scale `Theta(M)`.

WI-226 checks Wang's new unconditional short-interval pair theorem. Its formula remains valid on bow-scale power intervals, but the simple-critical and distinct-zero lower bounds are nonpositive throughout the current surviving count-saturating exponent range. With Fourier support below that exponent, the generic scalar pair statistic also remains inside the previously screenable regime.

## Program consequence

Stop optimizing scalar localization constants. Derive a local source-admissibility inequality converting radial-weighted mass in the `Theta(M)` neighborhood into zero count/multiplicity/radial constraints, or retain coupled sign/phase/covariance information that scalar total-mass screening loses. If Wang's pair formula enters, it should enter through such a source-fixed observable rather than its standalone proportions.

## Counterevidence / boundary

Weighted mass can concentrate on few zeros with large coefficients, so localization alone is not contradiction. The Wang preprint is fresh and its failure here is numerical/structural, not a refutation of the theorem. Different source-aware observables or non-scalar detector architectures remain open.

## Epistemic status

**Sharp scalar source-localization scale with a verified gap to currently available generic short-interval proportion bounds; the final zero-budget conversion remains open.**

## Falsification criterion

Beat the Logvinenko--Sereda cutoff inside the same normalized fixed-support scalar architecture, or derive a bow contradiction from the current generic short-interval proportions in the surviving exponent range. A positive continuation should add the missing source-to-zero-budget relation.
