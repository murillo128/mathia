# MI-014 — Source coercivity must survive confluence and slowly drifting bows

**Evidence level:** exact source-geometry obstruction through WI-182 plus literature-backed zero-density redirect through WI-183

## Core intuition

The missing Weil-inertia source theorem cannot be obtained by merely keeping a more faithful low-rank correction from the existing Lamzouri decomposition. WI-182 proves that the natural source-specific proximal mismatch is trapped below the complete Lamzouri remainder and therefore vanishes on the same confluence near-extremizers that already saturate the scalar population budget.

The surviving source problem is also more demanding than excluding isolated shallow pairs or finitely many fixed vertical lines. WI-183 imports Maynard--Pratt's bow obstruction: a locally arithmetic vertical chain whose horizontal depth changes adiabatically from site to site and whose reflected arm is forced by the functional equation. A viable coercive observable must remain sensitive when local blocks look almost like screened vertical progressions but the horizontal coordinate drifts globally.

## Strongest justified principle

WI-182 gives an exact square-completion decomposition for the proposed source correction. If `Gamma_e` denotes the excess proximal mismatch beyond the optimal rank-`e` spectral tail, then

`0 <= Gamma_e <= R`,

where `R` is the full Lamzouri remainder. WI-140 supplies finite confluence configurations with any prescribed simple-real fraction, the corresponding scalar pair budget, and `R/N -> 0`. Therefore no theorem based only on the already retained finite Lamzouri geometry can make `Gamma_e` contribute a positive extensive density at the Montgomery--Taylor target. The route needs an additional source hypothesis that excludes those controls.

WI-183 identifies a canonical shape that such extra information should confront. Maynard--Pratt's unconditional half-isolated-zero method does not cover all off-line zeros; their paper isolates “bows of zeros” as a remaining obstruction. In local Weil coordinates, the vertical spacing is order one while adjacent normalized horizontal depths differ by `o(1)`. Near bounded-depth ends, fixed windows converge to constant-depth lattice blocks, so ordinary local screening is stable under the slow drift. In the deep interior, Maynard--Pratt independently explain why their source detector suffers Poisson cancellation.

The durable source criterion is therefore two-dimensional: **a successful anti-confluence theorem must charge something that is absent from isolated or fixed-depth controls but cannot be hidden by a slowly varying horizontal profile along a near-arithmetic vertical chain.** Candidate resources include horizontal variation/curvature, interaction of functional-equation-reflected arms, a source-controlled mixed moment, or a theorem forcing a detectable zero inside every sufficiently long bow.

## What remains possible

Local-density or separation information, prime-side mixed moments, longer relational observables, or a drift-sensitive source detector could rule out the abstract confluence families without contradicting WI-182. Likewise, Maynard--Pratt bows are a falsification control, not an asserted zeta-zero configuration; a theorem proving they cannot occur would itself be valuable source coercivity.

What is no longer justified is to infer a positive correction merely from preserving the exact proximal optimizer, or to regard exclusion of finitely many fixed vertical lines as the relevant endpoint. The source theorem must use information genuinely beyond the current finite pair/Hilbert geometry.

## Status / novelty

The proximal square completion and confluence domination are exact line results. Half-isolated zeros, the conditional finite-vertical-line theorem, and bows are Maynard--Pratt prior art. The durable Mathia synthesis is the combined falsification boundary: **free proximal mismatch dies on confluence, while source-level slow horizontal drift supplies a stronger canonical control that any replacement coercivity mechanism should survive.**

## Falsification criterion

Derive an extensive source-proximal mismatch from only the WI-182 finite geometry despite the WI-140 confluence sequence, or prove a source theorem whose hypotheses cover Maynard--Pratt-type slowly drifting near-AP chains and force a positive cost without introducing additional relational information. Either result would materially narrow this boundary.
