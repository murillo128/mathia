# MI-029 — Post-threshold compact positivity needs noncompact tail retention, not a canonical finite-rank correction

**Evidence level:** exact operator-level synthesis from [WI-303](../../findings/WI-303-finite-rank-tail-rank-is-noncanonical.md) and the independently audited prime-eight obstruction [WI-360](../../findings/WI-360-prime-power-eight-blocks-tail-dropping-localization.md), using the source-exact tail architecture recorded in [WI-302](../../findings/WI-302-liu-source-exact-certificate-claims-positivity-through-17-16.md). The external `17/16` certificate itself remains pending independent replay.

WI-302 suggested a tempting structural story: once the compact localization passes the `log 8 / 2` threshold where tail-dropping fails, perhaps the positive rank-two correction appearing in Liu's block-Schur certificate is the minimal new invariant. WI-303 rules out that interpretation. If `S>=0` and `V` spans any finite trial subspace with nonsingular Gram matrix `G=V^*SV`, then `0 <= S V G^-1 V^* S <= S`; a positive operator supplies finite-rank Loewner minorants of whatever rank the chosen trial subspace has. The displayed rank-two term is therefore a certificate-design choice, not an invariant of the post-threshold problem.

WI-360 independently reconstructs the stronger structural obstruction behind that conclusion. For every `L>log(8)/2`, a smooth two-bump test can isolate the `log 8` autocorrelation channel and a modulation sequence `T_k=2 pi k/log 8` locks that source phase exactly. Along the resulting weakly-null sequence, the tail-dropped localized lower comparison has the normalized limit

`1/16 - log(2)/(2 sqrt(2)) < -1/8`.

Any fixed compact self-adjoint correction has normalized quadratic value tending to zero on the same sequence. Consequently no fixed compact repair can restore positivity after the source/exterior tail has been dropped, and any successful compensating form must retain a nonvanishing asymptotic payment of at least `log(2)/(2 sqrt(2)) - 1/16` on this witness.

The relevant dichotomy is therefore **tail dropped + fixed compact repair** versus **source-exact noncompact information retained before finite-dimensional extraction**. Once the noncompact supplier is present, a finite trial subspace may be chosen for numerical or Schur efficiency without being mistaken for the mathematical mechanism. The exact obstruction is not “rank two is necessary”; it is that the discarded high-frequency/source channel cannot be recreated by a fixed compact operator.

This also clarifies the relation to the current first-crossing program. The prime-eight witness is an adversarial legal test, not an asserted Suzuki null state. A successful argument may still use the actual null equation or first-crossing geometry to exclude it. But a localization proof that drops the exterior/source tail and keeps only a fixed compact remainder has already lost too much information before that state selection can help.

**Boundary.** WI-360 begins only for `L>log(8)/2`, targets a fixed compact repair, and does not refute source-exact noncompact tail retention, source-adapted compensation, or a genuine null-state restriction. WI-303 does not validate or invalidate any specific finite certificate rank. The reported positivity through `17/16` remains a computational certificate pending independent replay and is not load-bearing for this intuition. The durable conclusion is only that post-threshold positivity cannot be explained by a canonical finite-rank correction once the noncompact/source tail has been discarded.
