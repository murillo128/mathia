# MI-017 — Suzuki null modes must be prime-translation support-dominating, but translated activity is still weaker than autocorrelation overlap

**Evidence level:** exact under the stated hypotheses through WI-267. WI-264 gives finite-prime screening, WI-265 gives an abstract first-crossing countermodel, WI-266 excludes the endpoint-bump extremizer from Suzuki's actual source equation, and WI-267 proves a support-cover theorem for arbitrary nonzero localized Weil null vectors. No theorem yet forces a nonzero active prime-lag autocorrelation or rules out every multi-component screened null mode.

WI-253--WI-258 reduce useful first-crossing information to aperture lower envelopes and phase-coupled source inequalities. WI-264 shows that finite source activation alone is not enough: narrow antisymmetric endpoint bumps can screen every active von-Mangoldt autocorrelation lag. WI-265 shows that exact zero-modehood, nested positivity and shorter-window positivity do not repair this generically.

WI-266 is the first source-specific rigidity result. The endpoint-bump support gap contains an interval where all active discrete translations vanish; there Suzuki's regular kernel gives a strict convolution sign, so the true localized Weil operator cannot annihilate the vector.

WI-267 upgrades that observation from one geometry to an exact theorem. For any nonzero `v` in the localized Weil nullspace, with essential support `S`, every `x` in `(-a,a)\S` must satisfy `x+log n in S` or `x-log n in S` for some active prime power `n` with `log n<=2a`. The proof uses Suzuki's full-domain distributional null equation; on a hypothetical translation-free gap, the regular kernel becomes a holomorphic exponential/Laurent expansion whose coefficient uniqueness forces all one-sided moments—and hence the whole vector—to vanish.

The durable distinction is now precise: **the arithmetic null equation forces pointwise prime-translation domination of support, but support domination is not yet a nonzero autocorrelation theorem.** Translated mass can be present everywhere while global lag overlaps cancel by sign or phase.

The next target is to combine this support cover with the null equation or sliding first-crossing inequalities strongly enough to produce a quantitative non-screenable overlap, preferably a lower bound on at least one active-lag autocorrelation or an equivalent symbol. A surviving countermodel would have to be multi-component and source-compatible at every gap point, not merely compatible with generic positivity.

**Boundary.** WI-267 gives no lower bound on support measure beyond a weak finite-cover corollary, no fixed active lag that works globally, and no sign/coherence of the translated pieces. It does not prove RH or a simple-zero proportion. Those missing quantitative/coherence properties are exactly the next gate.
