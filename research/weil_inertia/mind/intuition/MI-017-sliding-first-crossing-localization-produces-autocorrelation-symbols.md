# MI-017 — Suzuki null modes are support-dominating, but neither activity nor generic positivity supplies signed overlap

**Evidence level:** exact under the stated hypotheses through WI-268. WI-267 proves prime-translation support domination for arbitrary nonzero localized Weil null vectors; WI-268 proves failure of ordinary positivity preservation for the unrestricted localized semigroup above the archimedean threshold. No theorem yet forces a nonzero active prime-lag autocorrelation or a one-signed first-crossing mode.

WI-264--WI-265 show that finite source activation and abstract first-crossing positivity can be screened. WI-266 uses Suzuki's actual equation to exclude the endpoint-bump extremizer, and WI-267 upgrades this to an exact support theorem: every point outside the essential support must be reached by an active prime-power translation of the support.

That support cover is weaker than overlap. A gap point may see translated mass while `v(x)=0`, so it contributes to the pointwise null equation without contributing to the autocorrelation integrand at that lag. Multi-component signs and phases may still screen all global source-relevant autocorrelations.

WI-268 independently closes the standard route to sign coherence. After the singular logarithmic kernel cancels Suzuki's regular remainder, the unrestricted off-diagonal cross kernel is `-w(h)`, and `w` changes sign at `h_0=log rho`. For every `a>h_0/2` there are disjoint nonnegative test functions with positive cross form, violating the first Beurling--Deny criterion. This happens before the first prime lag activates.

Therefore a one-sign first-crossing mode cannot be assumed from Perron--Frobenius/Krein--Rutman theory. If sign or overlap rigidity exists, it must come from the exact Suzuki null equation, firstness, or a genuinely different cone/order structure. The live bridge is activity-to-overlap under phase cancellation.

**Boundary.** Failure of positivity preservation does not prove that a particular ground/null mode changes sign. Support domination does not prove any fixed lag has positive overlap. Those two missing statements require new source-specific mathematics.
