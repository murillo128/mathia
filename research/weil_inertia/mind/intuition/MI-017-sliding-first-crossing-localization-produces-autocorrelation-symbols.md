# MI-017 — Generic first-crossing screening survives abstract positivity, but Suzuki's exact null equation excludes the endpoint-bump extremizer

**Evidence level:** the phase-cone identities through WI-258, the finite-prime screening theorem WI-264, the abstract first-crossing countermodel WI-265, and the Suzuki source-specific exclusion WI-266 are exact under their stated hypotheses. No no-screening theorem for arbitrary localized Weil null vectors and no RH conclusion is proved.

WI-253--WI-258 reduce useful first-crossing information to aperture lower envelopes and phase-coupled source inequalities. WI-264 then shows that finite source activation is not enough: narrow antisymmetric endpoint bumps can screen every active von-Mangoldt autocorrelation lag simultaneously while placing the continuous source on favorable signs.

WI-265 shows that generic first-crossing operator structure is not enough either. The same screened vector can be made an exact terminal zero mode of a positive rank-one-defect operator while every proper centered and translated shorter-window compression remains strictly positive. Exact zero-modehood, nested positivity and localization reserves therefore do not force the missing arithmetic signal.

WI-266 is the first source-specific rigidity theorem against that countermodel. For every endpoint-bump vector from WI-264, the support gap contains a nonempty interval on which the local term and every active prime-power translation vanish. Suzuki's remaining regular kernel is strictly increasing with separation. On positive points in that interval the positive left endpoint bump is farther away than the negative right endpoint bump, so their antisymmetric contributions have a strict common sign. The true localized Weil operator is therefore nonzero on that open interval, contradicting the null equation.

The lesson is exact: **screening must be tested against the pointwise/distributional arithmetic null equation, not only against its quadratic or variational consequences.** The strongest explicit two-component screening extremizer is incompatible with the actual source even though it survives every generic first-crossing control previously imposed.

The next theorem should generalize the source-specific mechanism rather than strengthen abstract positivity. A useful target is a support-spreading/no-long-gap statement for arbitrary candidate null vectors under the combined prime translations and monotone continuous kernel. The decisive negative alternative is a genuinely source-compatible multi-component screened geometry in which discrete translations cancel the continuous convolution throughout every support gap.

**Boundary.** WI-266 excludes only the antisymmetric two-endpoint-bump family satisfying the WI-264 width conditions. It does not prove that every null vector has nonzero autocorrelation at an active prime-power lag, rule out three-or-more-component support geometries, or derive the required lower envelope from firstness alone. Those are the remaining source-specific questions.
