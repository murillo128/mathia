# MI-053 — Odd parity couples difference and image kernels before half-density transport

**Evidence level:** exact source-specific synthesis from [WI-363](../../findings/WI-363-desogus-half-density-transport-creates-a-jacobian-potential.md) and [WI-364](../../findings/WI-364-desogus-odd-fold-forces-gamma-image-term.md). The conclusions audit the current Desogus v1 source-to-cell derivation; they do not establish or refute RH.

For an even difference kernel `k(|x-y|)` on a symmetric interval, restriction to the odd sector is not the same kernel on the positive half-interval. The cross-sign quadrant creates an image channel. In the canonical unitary identification used by WI-364, the regular gamma operator becomes

`k_a(|x-y|)-k_a(x+y)`,

with `k_a(t)=a rho(at)`. The singular Legendre difference form has the same parity geometry: its cross-sign piece produces the Carleman image channel, while the accompanying endpoint potential supplies the exact diagonal correction needed for the collar form.

This makes the singular and regular image terms inseparable at the bookkeeping level. If one keeps the Carleman image generated by the singular kernel, the odd fold of the regular gamma kernel simultaneously produces `-a rho(a(x+y))`; after the sign in the localized operator this becomes a positive gamma image contribution. A later cancellation may remove or reassign it, but parity reduction itself does not.

WI-363 supplies the second transformation gate. Even after the complete odd-folded kernel is written correctly, conjugating a **quadratic difference form** by the exponential half-density map is not exhausted by conjugating its off-diagonal operator kernel. The unequal Jacobian factors create a nonconstant multiplication term `W_A`. Thus there are two distinct representation effects in sequence: parity creates image channels, then half-density form transport creates a diagonal Jacobian channel.

The reusable audit is therefore: when a proof passes from a symmetric source operator to a parity sector and then to a normalized coordinate cell, track **difference, image and multiplication channels as one closed quadratic form**. An off-diagonal identity for only the difference channel is insufficient, and a correct singular image term does not license dropping the regular image produced by the same symmetry reduction.

For the Desogus audit this localizes the repair obligation sharply. Starting from the full odd archimedean block, one must transport the difference gamma/Cauchy channel, the Carleman/gamma image channel, the scalar and endpoint potentials, and the `W_A` correction on a common core. Only an explicit cancellation or exact reassignment can justify the normalized one-cell form used by the downstream common-cut/rooted-Schur argument.

**Boundary.** The parity-image identity is elementary and source-specific; no general novelty claim is made for odd restriction of even kernels. WI-364 does not show that the advertised collar form is false, and WI-363 does not show that `W_A` survives the complete archimedean transport. A repaired derivation may cancel both corrections. WI-362 remains a valid conditional theorem once its normalized local form is granted.
