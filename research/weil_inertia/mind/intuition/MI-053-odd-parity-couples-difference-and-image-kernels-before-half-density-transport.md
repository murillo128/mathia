# MI-053 — Odd-fold and half-density transport separate sign-safe image terms from quantitative reserve

**Evidence level:** exact source-specific synthesis from [WI-363](../../findings/WI-363-desogus-half-density-transport-creates-a-jacobian-potential.md)--[WI-366](../../findings/WI-366-desogus-true-gamma-image-cannot-uniformly-dominate-bare-carleman.md). The conclusions audit the current Desogus source-to-cell derivation; they do not establish or refute RH.

Odd restriction of an even difference kernel produces both a difference channel and an `x+y` image channel. Applied to the regular gamma kernel, this creates the gamma image simultaneously with the Carleman image generated by the singular channel. The subsequent exponential half-density transform has a second effect: transporting a **quadratic difference form** creates a nonconstant diagonal Jacobian term `W_A` even when the off-diagonal kernel conjugation is correct.

WI-365 establishes that the exact gamma image enters the localized lower form with positive sign. This is enough to discard it when proving a sign lower bound. It is not enough to reuse the fixed quantitative rebound assigned to the normalized bare Carleman operator in the downstream OSPR argument.

WI-366 makes that distinction exact. Under the normalized cell scaling, the true gamma image is a boundary-layer Hankel operator. On vectors supported away from the folded endpoint its form decays exponentially with `A`, while the bare Carleman form remains scale invariant; even the constant-channel gamma-image mass decays like `1/A`. Consequently there is no `A`-independent positive coefficient with `J_A >= cK` throughout the cofinal regime.

The reusable audit is therefore to track **difference, image, multiplication and reserve channels separately but within one closed form**. A term can be positive enough to omit and still be quantitatively incapable of supplying the reserve booked elsewhere. Likewise, an off-diagonal transport identity does not determine the diagonal potential created by the same coordinate change.

For the Desogus audit, a valid repair must now do two things explicitly: settle the complete diagonal/scalar transport including `W_A`, and derive the later reserve from an exact surviving channel or an `A`-dependent source-aligned variational estimate. Treating the true gamma image as a scale-invariant Carleman copy is no longer available.

**Boundary.** WI-366 does not show that `J_A` has no useful coercivity or that the main theorem is false. Endpoint-concentrating test directions may support an `A`-dependent reserve, a separate exact Carleman channel may survive the full transport, or the induction may be reorganized. WI-362 remains valid conditional on its normalized local form and quantitative hypotheses.
