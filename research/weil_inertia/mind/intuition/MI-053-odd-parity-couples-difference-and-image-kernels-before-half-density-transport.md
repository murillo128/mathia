# MI-053 — Exact form transport repairs the diagonal but leaves a nonlocal gamma sign gate before any quantitative reserve

**Evidence level:** exact source-specific synthesis from WI-363--[WI-368](../../findings/WI-368-desogus-gamma-cancellation-has-form-sign-mismatch.md). The conclusions audit the current Desogus source-to-cell derivation; they do not establish or refute RH.

Odd folding and half-density transport create several channels that must be kept distinct: singular difference, image, multiplication and regular gamma terms. WI-365 shows that the exact gamma image is positive and may be omitted when only a sign lower bound is needed, while WI-366 proves that its boundary-layer scaling cannot uniformly dominate the scale-invariant bare Carleman operator strongly enough to inherit a fixed reserve.

WI-367 closes the separate diagonal question. The nonconstant Jacobian multiplier generated by transporting the bare Cauchy difference form is real, but an explicit source endpoint multiplier completes the Cauchy--Carleman form exactly. After that repair, the normalized target still contains the regular gamma kernel with its full nonlocal sign. The diagonal bookkeeping is therefore repairable and is no longer the decisive obstruction.

WI-368 identifies the remaining printed failure at the quadratic-form level. The unsigned Cauchy--Carleman kernel identity contains `+A rho`, but the off-diagonal kernel of the closed difference form is its negative half. The exact transported cross-kernel is consequently the gamma-corrected one, not the claimed gamma-free collar. A multiplication or scalar correction cannot alter this cross-polarization, so the missing cancellation would have to come from an additional genuine off-diagonal source term.

The reusable audit is to distinguish **sign, exact transport and reserve provenance**. A channel can be favorable enough to discard, yet too weak to finance the numerical reserve; a diagonal normalization can be repaired exactly while leaving a separate nonlocal sign mismatch untouched.

**Boundary.** WI-368 is a barrier to the printed Section 5 transport, not a no-go theorem for every rooted-operator argument. A repaired proof may exhibit the missing off-diagonal source identity or establish the necessary reserve directly for the true gamma-corrected operator. Neither has been established.
