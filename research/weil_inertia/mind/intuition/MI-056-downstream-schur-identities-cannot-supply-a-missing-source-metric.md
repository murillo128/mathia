# MI-056 — Downstream Schur identities cannot supply a missing source metric

**Evidence level:** exact dependency synthesis from [WI-362](../../findings/WI-362-desogus-common-cut-and-pivot-chain-is-exact-given-the-localized-form.md), [WI-368](../../findings/WI-368-desogus-gamma-cancellation-has-the-wrong-operator-sign.md), and [WI-375](../../findings/WI-375-common-cut-pivot-chain-remains-downstream-of-gamma-free-collar.md), with the corrected gamma profile in WI-369--WI-374 as context. The conclusion audits the current Desogus v1 dependency chain; it does not exclude a different gamma-retaining rooted-Schur repair.

WI-362 established an exact and useful conditional principle: once the normalized one-cell form is granted, simultaneous common-complement shorting can preserve source capacity without double-spending reserve, and monotonicity of the short can carry a valid lower operator model into the inherited pivot. The same-vector identity then places the response debit in that same Schur metric rather than treating comparator surplus as an unrelated positive term.

WI-368 changes the premise, not that algebra. The complete localized Weil form does not transport to the printed gamma-free collar because the closed difference form gives the opposite off-diagonal kernel sign; the regular gamma channel survives. WI-375 traces the later dependency and shows that Theorem 6.53 starts from the gamma-free split `b=l_0+r`, while Lemma 6.58 obtains its positive inherited pivot by monotonicity from that same lower form. No independent gamma-retaining source metric is inserted before the pivot is used.

The durable distinction is therefore between **response placement and metric creation**. A Schur/Feshbach identity can say exactly where a debit lies once a positive source metric or pivot exists. Monotonicity of variational shorts can propagate a valid upstream operator order. Neither operation can manufacture the missing operator inequality when the lower model itself was obtained after deleting a surviving negative channel.

For the corrected problem, the load-bearing theorem must come earlier: establish a common-cut lower bound directly for the gamma-retaining localized form, then prove that its mismatch short leaves a positive inherited pivot and enough transverse reserve for the downstream certificate. The cofinally positive fresh endpoint collars from WI-374 make source-conditioned old--new coupling a plausible place to look, but their positivity cannot simply be relabeled as the already-proved gamma-free pivot.

This also prevents two opposite bookkeeping errors. It is too pessimistic to charge every late collar with the full inherited gamma debt, because WI-374 proves those collars become positive. It is too optimistic to import the printed positive pivot unchanged, because WI-375 proves that pivot is downstream of the disputed source metric. The corrected mechanism must quantify how positive fresh reserve couples to inherited negative modes in the actual form.

**Boundary.** The conclusion does not say that common-cut shorting, same-vector response placement or rooted-Schur induction is invalid in itself; WI-362 remains exact under its stated localized-form hypothesis. Nor does it rule out an independent proof of a positive pivot for the corrected form. It isolates the missing dependency: positivity of the source metric must be established before the downstream Schur identities can use it.
