**Evidence level:** exact finite Lamzouri-model identity and sharp controls through WI-170

## Core intuition

A scalar deficit can charge several qualitatively different exceptional populations without distinguishing them. In the Lamzouri finite model the same pair-correlation surplus funds both off-real mass and multiplicity excess, so combining marginal percentage bounds does not create an independent bootstrap.

## Strongest justified principle

WI-170 gives the exact decomposition

`Q - N = O + 2M + R`, with `R>=0`,

where `O` is the non-real population counted with multiplicity and `M=N-D` is total multiplicity excess. The coefficient `2` on `M` is exactly saturated by a real double. The coefficient `1` on `O` is asymptotically saturated by a simple conjugate pair approaching the real axis. Thus the two exceptional mechanisms can spend the same scalar resource at their sharp leading costs.
