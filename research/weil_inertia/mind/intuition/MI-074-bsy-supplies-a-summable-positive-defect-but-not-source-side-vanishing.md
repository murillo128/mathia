# MI-074 — BSY supplies a summable positive horizontal defect but not source-side vanishing

**Evidence level:** classical Balazard--Saias--Yor identity as persisted and audited in [WI-409](../../findings/WI-409-bsy-already-supplies-summable-positive-horizontal-defect.md), contrasted with WI-405--WI-408.

WI-405--WI-408 explored whether a Widder/superzeta construction could globalize a positive horizontal-defect carrier. WI-409 changes that premise. The classical Balazard--Saias--Yor critical-line identity already writes the relevant logarithmic integral as a nonnegative sum over zeros with `beta>1/2`, with one-zero charge

`q_BSY(rho)=log |rho/(1-rho)| = artanh(d/(gamma^2+1/4+d^2))`

for `rho=1/2+d+i gamma`. This charge is strictly positive off the line, behaves like `d/gamma^2` for shallow defects, and the total sum is absolutely convergent from the ordinary zero-counting law. No zero-density hypothesis or matched superzeta finite part is needed merely to obtain **some** global positive summable defect observable.

The obstruction therefore moves. Constructing a positive carrier is no longer the missing theorem; forcing that carrier to vanish from the arithmetic/source side is. Summability, density information and even linear sensitivity to shallow displacement do not imply zero total charge. Defects can be arbitrarily shallow, and a positive summable family can carry arbitrarily small total mass without disappearing.

This also separates two research routes that should not be conflated. A matched superzeta subtraction may still be valuable if it produces a source-compatible identity with stronger determining information. But it is not a prerequisite for the existence of a positive off-critical defect functional. Any claimed advance must state what additional source relation proves exact vanishing, quantizes individual charges, or otherwise determines the positive defect measure.

**Boundary.** WI-409 does not provide a new proof of RH. The BSY identity is classical; the unresolved Mathia problem is to derive the required zero value or an equivalent coercive condition from source-side information under assumptions weaker than RH. Absolute convergence and positivity alone are not such a condition.
