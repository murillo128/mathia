# MI-073 — Critical superzeta defect charges need a determining globalization, not finite moment control

**Evidence level:** synthesis from [WI-405](../../findings/WI-405-signed-widder-mellin-averages-collapse-to-superzeta-and-require-renormalization.md)--[WI-408](../../findings/WI-408-finite-superzeta-jets-only-control-log-moments.md), with the Widder motivation in WI-399--WI-404 inheriting the stated recent non-peer-reviewed framework boundary. WI-406--WI-408 are exact consequences inside the superzeta comparison model described there.

WI-405 shows why the locally attractive critical signed Mellin weight cannot simply be summed globally: in the absolutely convergent half-plane the family collapses to classical Voros superzeta data, while at the critical exponent `sigma=-1` the desired local horizontal-defect charge lies outside absolute convergence and needs an explicit renormalization law.

WI-406 shows that analytic continuation alone is not that law. The canonical actual-spectrum continuation has the fixed value `Z(-1|0)=-9/32`; it is not a positive sum of horizontal displacements. The finite-cutoff positive comparison is actual spectrum minus a same-ordinate criticalized spectrum, so a global theorem would require a common matched subtraction for both sequences rather than the continuation of the actual sequence alone.

WI-407 strengthens the local carrier by differentiating with respect to the superzeta exponent. A same-ordinate off-line pair of horizontal depth `d` at height `gamma` then contributes positively, asymptotically `4d^2 log gamma`. This makes a hypothetical matched derivative identity more defect-sensitive, but a finite global value would imply only a weighted summability statement. Finitely many off-line zeros and sufficiently rapidly shrinking infinite depths both have finite cost.

WI-408 shows that taking more fixed exponent derivatives does not change that logical type. At order `k`,

`Q_k(gamma,d)=2^(k+1)d^2(log gamma)^k[1+O_k(1/log gamma+d^2/gamma^2)]`.

A finite jet therefore measures only finitely many logarithmic moments of the positive defect measure. More strongly, even finiteness of every fixed-order moment considered separately does not imply that the measure is zero: the hypothetical sparse configuration `gamma_n=e^n`, `d_n=e^(-n)` makes all such moments finite. A tail envelope that merely tends to zero is likewise noncoercive, because finite defects disappear from sufficiently high tails and sparse infinite defects can fit rapidly decaying envelopes.

The correct globalization target is consequently **measure determination**, not an ever longer finite jet. After establishing a source-compatible matched subtraction, the source side must force an exactly zero positive total, determine the defect measure from a rich transform family, supply large-order control strong enough for a determinate moment problem, or impose an independent lower/quantized horizontal-depth law that converts moment finiteness into absence. Increasing derivative order without such a coupling only raises the logarithmic weight attached to each defect; it does not change the existence of positive measures with all fixed moments finite.

This separates four currencies: local defect sensitivity, renormalized existence, moment strength, and global determinacy. A route can improve the first three while still failing the fourth. The RH-relevant theorem must state explicitly which source identity rules out every nonzero positive defect configuration, rather than inferring vanishing from finite regularized numbers.

**Boundary.** The Widder motivation still carries the evidence boundary of WI-399--WI-404. WI-408 does not prove that a matched superzeta transform family or a determinate large-order source identity is impossible; it only rules out the inference from finite fixed-order logarithmic moment budgets, including separate finiteness of all fixed orders. No RH implication follows without the missing matched and determining source theorem.
