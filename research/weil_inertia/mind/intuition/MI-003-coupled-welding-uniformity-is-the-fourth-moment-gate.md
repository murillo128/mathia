# MI-003 — Scalarizing the Yang lock moves the hard part into signed finite-window Ramanujan leakage, where rank is not metric cancellation

**Evidence level:** supported for the exact source reductions, alias quotient, complete residual pair classification, dyadic overlap bounds, and matched composite controls; the source-weighted many-modulus analytic bridge remains open

## Core intuition

After scalarization, genuine sign cancellation can occur only through finite-window interaction among otherwise orthogonal Ramanujan subspaces. The pairwise theory is now essentially classified: large residual rank defects are low-denominator finite-window resonances, but they are metrically weak, dyadically negligible even after adversarial aggregation, and not prime-specific. Pairwise rank saving is therefore not the missing fourth-moment mechanism.

## Strongest justified principle

WI-068--WI-085 establish the source/representation boundary. Positive pruning cannot recover the sparse two-dimensional source, complete-period scalar blocks are orthogonal, and every arbitrary-modulus scalar law on an `N`-window factors through exactly `N` divisor/tail coordinates with a unique subwindow alias. Much modulus provenance is already gone before any spectral estimate is made.

WI-086--WI-093 classify the first residual pair geometry: only a close-prime boundary strip has extra rank defect, with a one-third ceiling attained by a sparse Loewner--Bezout family. Yet the extremal triangular layer has uniformly bounded whitened projector overlap, and every positive-density defect has only `O(1)` total squared canonical overlap.

WI-094 turns this metric weakness into a collective theorem. On a dyadic prime block, even summing **all** positive-density defect edges with arbitrary signs gives only `O_theta(1/log P)` of the diagonal Frobenius energy. Any fixed-fraction signed Hilbert--Schmidt cancellation must therefore come from vanishing-density/full-rank pairs, cross-scale interactions, actual source-coefficient structure, or a pre-scalar object.

WI-095--WI-099 then identify the algebraic defect exactly. Residual defect is quantized by low-denominator boundary resonances; the true defect equals the number of free cycles minus one; and the recurrent partial map is cyclic-order preserving, so all free cycles for a given pair share one reduced rotation ratio and one common length. The defect is **phase-pure**, not a mixture of many Farey layers. The related sparse Loewner nullity is exactly a three-resonance, and the macroscopic one-third family extends to coprime composite moduli, including infinite prime-cube families. This makes the phenomenon an exact-period/cyclotomic control rather than a rational-prime-only source.

## What remains possible

The viable scalar route must use the actual centered signed Yang coefficients in a many-modulus theorem that survives the exact alias quotient and cannot be reduced to extensive pair defects. Plausible mechanisms are coherent interactions among many weak/full-rank blocks, cross-scale structure, a source-specific restricted region of the `N`-coordinate quotient, or a weighted incidence estimate before absolute values are taken.

Distinct source-faithful alternatives remain a labelled transform retaining reduced slopes, direct two-dimensional incidence, the base-aggregated multivariate polynomial representation, or a genuinely non-scalar finite-window object formed before Ramanujan scalarization.

## Status / novelty

The Ramanujan, Bezoutian, rotation, principal-angle, and prime-counting ingredients are classical or direct. The persisted Mathia synthesis is the exact closure of **pairwise residual rank** as an explanatory route: its algebra is a low-denominator periodic resonance and its metric mass is too small, even collectively, to provide the required source-independent cancellation.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a fixed-fraction dyadic Hilbert--Schmidt cancellation driven solely by positive-density residual defect edges despite WI-094, or show that the composite resonance controls fail for an invariant actually used by the Yang coefficients. A positive result must quantify the source-weighted many-body object rather than substitute support, pair rank, or unweighted coherence.
