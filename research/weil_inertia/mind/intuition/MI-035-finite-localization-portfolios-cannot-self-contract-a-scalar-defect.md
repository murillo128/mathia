# MI-035 — Bounded-complexity localization portfolios cannot self-contract a scalar defect

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [WI-315](../../findings/WI-315-nested-weil-windows-make-vanishing-cap-diagonals-inertia-complete.md), [WI-316](../../findings/WI-316-capped-comparison-bottoms-converge-to-global-weil-floor.md), [WI-317](../../findings/WI-317-finite-localization-portfolios-cannot-contract-scalar-defect.md), and [WI-318](../../findings/WI-318-bounded-h1-adaptive-localizers-cannot-contract-scalar-defect.md).

Once the capped comparison is reduced to a fixed nonzero scalar defect, source-exact localization does not automatically provide a bootstrap. Suppose an exact localization identity writes the global form as localized source terms plus an explicit error, and the only estimate supplied for every localized term is the same scalar lower bound `Q(h)>=-c||h||^2`. To improve the defect uniformly to `c-kappa`, the localization error would have to contribute a uniformly negative amount on every test vector after choosing a member of the portfolio.

WI-317 rules this out for every fixed finite portfolio of admissible smooth localizers at fixed aperture. High-frequency modulations preserve the scalar Rayleigh geometry while making the finitely many active prime-power phases simultaneously recurrent. Along a common weakly-null sequence, every active source error can be made nonnegative; in the prime-free regime the defects vanish instead. Thus even a best-after-seeing-`f` finite portfolio cannot force the negative correction needed for a uniform scalar contraction.

WI-318 shows that finiteness was not the essential resource limit for the unit-window architecture. Let an arbitrary finite or infinite family of normalized smooth multiwindow localizers have uniformly bounded total derivative energy. The `H^1` budget gives uniform `O(1/|t|)` Riemann--Lebesgue decay for the continuous localization error on high-frequency modulations. Independently, unit support makes translation by `log 2` square-zero, so the Haagerup--de la Harpe numerical-radius bound gives a family-independent autocorrelation ceiling `R(log 2)<=1/2`. A single Kronecker recurrence sequence then makes the active prime-power phases simultaneously favorable and defeats the infimum over the entire adaptive family.

Therefore **exact decomposition plus bounded localization complexity is not stronger information**, even when the portfolio is infinite and the localizer is selected after seeing the test vector. Within this source-exact unit-window scalar-feedback architecture, cardinality and adaptivity are not resources by themselves. If every localized term is still fed only the original scalar semibound, any genuine escape must contain a sequence whose total derivative energy—and equivalently aggregate frequency second moment—diverges.

That spectral noncompactness is necessary, not sufficient. WI-311 already uses large carrier frequencies to flatten a capped tail, while WI-312 shows that a particular anti-concentrating high-frequency limit transfers cost into archimedean compact complexity. An unbounded `H^1` family may therefore only move the obstruction. A cleaner fixed-defect bootstrap would import information absent from the original scalar premise: a stronger localized arithmetic estimate, prime/mode-dependent data, a vector- or matrix-valued invariant, a different support geometry, or another mechanism that breaks the common recurrence obstruction.

The reusable audit is to separate **portfolio size**, **adaptivity**, **localization regularity/frequency moment**, and **information content of the estimate fed into each piece**. A countably infinite family with bounded frequency second moment remains one bounded-complexity scalar channel for this purpose. Conversely, allowing the frequency moment to diverge changes the analytic resource class but does not itself prove contraction.

**Boundary.** The obstruction is exact for the source-exact unit-window smooth localization identities at fixed aperture when every localized piece is controlled only by the same scalar lower bound. WI-317 covers arbitrary fixed finite portfolios without a uniform derivative hypothesis; WI-318 covers arbitrary finite/infinite/adaptive families under a uniform total `H^1` budget. The chain does not rule out unbounded-`H^1` families, non-unit support geometries, stronger local arithmetic theorems, non-scalar information, or a direct proof of the exact comparison sign. It also does not say a fixed nonzero defect is RH-equivalent; WI-316 makes only the vanishing-defect cofinal limit RH-complete.
