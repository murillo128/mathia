# MI-035 — Finite localization portfolios cannot self-contract a scalar defect

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [WI-315](../../findings/WI-315-nested-weil-windows-make-vanishing-cap-diagonals-inertia-complete.md), [WI-316](../../findings/WI-316-capped-comparison-bottoms-converge-to-global-weil-floor.md), and [WI-317](../../findings/WI-317-finite-localization-portfolios-cannot-contract-scalar-defect.md).

Once the capped comparison is reduced to a fixed nonzero scalar defect, source-exact localization does not automatically provide a bootstrap. Suppose an exact localization identity writes the global form as localized source terms plus an explicit error, and the only estimate supplied for every localized term is the same scalar lower bound `Q(h)>=-c||h||^2`. To improve the defect uniformly to `c-kappa`, the localization error would have to contribute a uniformly negative amount on every test vector after choosing a member of the portfolio.

WI-317 rules this out for every fixed finite portfolio of admissible smooth localizers at fixed aperture. High-frequency modulations preserve the scalar Rayleigh geometry while making the finitely many active prime-power phases simultaneously recurrent. Along a common weakly-null sequence, every active source error can be made nonnegative; in the prime-free regime the defects vanish instead. Thus even a best-after-seeing-`f` finite portfolio cannot force the negative correction needed for a uniform scalar contraction.

The conceptual point is that **exact decomposition is not stronger information**. A localization formula can redistribute the unknown form among many pieces while preserving the same scalar premise on every piece. Finite portfolio size, smoother partitions, or adaptive choice among those fixed pieces do not create a new coercive resource when the input inequality is unchanged.

A viable fixed-defect bootstrap must therefore import something not contained in the original scalar semibound: a stronger localized arithmetic estimate, dependence on the active prime/mode, a vector- or matrix-valued invariant, a genuinely infinite/adaptive family whose additional coordinates carry source information, or another mechanism that breaks the simultaneous recurrence control. Otherwise the bootstrap is circular at the level of information even though the localization identity is exact.

**Boundary.** The obstruction is for a fixed finite portfolio at fixed aperture using only the same scalar lower bound on the localized pieces. It does not rule out stronger local theorems, non-scalar information, moving/infinite architectures with new source content, or a direct proof of the exact comparison sign. It also does not say a fixed nonzero defect is RH-equivalent; WI-316 makes only the vanishing-defect cofinal limit RH-complete.
