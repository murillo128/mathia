# MI-035 — Scalar localization self-bootstrap survives neither complexity nor Hilbert channel enrichment

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [WI-315](../../findings/WI-315-nested-weil-windows-make-vanishing-cap-diagonals-inertia-complete.md) through [WI-321](../../findings/WI-321-hilbert-valued-channelization-cannot-contract-scalar-weil-semibound.md).

Once the capped comparison has been reduced to a scalar defect, an exact localization identity does not automatically create a bootstrap. If every localized source term receives only the same premise `Q(h)>=-c||h||^2`, a uniform improvement to `c-kappa` would require the localization correction to contribute a uniformly favorable amount on every test vector after the localizer is chosen.

WI-317 proves that fixed finite portfolios cannot do this. WI-318 extends the recurrence obstruction to arbitrary adaptive unit-window families with uniformly bounded total `H^1`. WI-319 finds the complexity boundary of that particular falsifier, but WI-320 shows that defeating the recurrence witness does not reopen the architecture: one fixed short nonnegative test below the first arithmetic lag makes the exact localization defect nonnegative for every real multiwindow localizer.

WI-321 removes the representational escape completely at the level of Hilbert dimension. Let `K` be any complex Hilbert space and let `G` be a normalized `K`-valued compactly supported window. The scalar semibound amplifies componentwise to

`Q_K(F)>=-c||F||^2`.

The localization correction is controlled by the Hilbert autocorrelation `R_G(a)`. On the same short real nonnegative witness, all prime-power lags vanish and Cauchy--Schwarz gives `Re R_G(a)<=1`, so the defect is nonnegative. This argument does not depend on finite-dimensionality; the compact test generates only a separable range even inside a nonseparable ambient space.

The deeper point is that even apparently coupled positive matrix readouts may still contain no new premise. Polarization of the scalar semibound already gives the Loewner Gram inequality

`(B_Q(h_i,h_j)) >= -c(<h_i,h_j>)`.

Any fixed positive-semidefinite channel metric or positive contraction applied to this Gram matrix is therefore a consequence of the original scalar bound. It cannot be credited as extra arithmetic information merely because it introduces off-diagonal notation.

The reusable distinction is sharper than representation dimension versus information dimension: **the relevant information object is the cone of coupled inequalities not generated by scalar polarization**. Arbitrary Hilbert channels, phases, unitary mixing and fixed PSD recombination stay inside the scalar-generated Loewner cone and cannot contract the scalar constant.

A genuine non-scalar escape must change that cone: a matrix estimate with source-specific cross-channel terms not implied by polarization, prime- or mode-dependent admissible states, indefinite/source-breaking coupling, or a different exact decomposition whose correction has different sign geometry. Localization can organize such information but cannot manufacture it from repeated scalar copies.

The interpretation of WI-319 remains local to its witness: `t^2/log t` is the price for defeating one recurrence argument, not a threshold beyond which scalar self-bootstrap becomes possible. WI-320--WI-321 block the architecture after arbitrary real-window complexity and arbitrary Hilbert-valued diagonal amplification.

**Boundary.** WI-321 concerns Hilbert-valued channelizations whose form is the diagonal amplification of `Q` and whose coupled positive consequences are generated by the same scalar semibound. It does not rule out genuinely stronger matrix estimates, prime-dependent admissible sets, source-breaking localizations or a different coercivity/inertia mechanism. None of these no-go results proves Weil positivity.
