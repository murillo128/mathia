# MI-060 — Inherited arithmetic translations have rank but no sign certificate

**Evidence level:** exact operator synthesis from [WI-379](../../findings/WI-379-inherited-arithmetic-translations-defeat-source-rank-elimination.md), correcting the possible overextension of the source-rank obstruction in WI-378. The partial-translation representation is audited against the cited primary source; the two-cell sign decomposition is elementary.

An already-active prime-power branch acts on the old localized space as a truncated translation plus its adjoint. On two disjoint translated cells its compression is the flip operator

`[[0,U],[U*,0]]`,

so it has infinite-dimensional positive and negative subspaces. The same construction can be placed inside the current source-zero bulk for large support radius because the source slots and their fixed translations occupy vanishing measure there.

Therefore inherited old--old arithmetic is not limited by the rank of the current source map. WI-378 remains decisive for source-mediated Schur coupling, which vanishes on `ker R_k`, but it cannot be upgraded to eliminate the inherited arithmetic channel by source-rank counting. The channel has ample operator rank before elimination.

What remains unknown is precisely the harder property: **sign/coercivity on the certified gamma-negative source-zero band**. Each translation branch is itself indefinite and the arithmetic coefficients are signed by the exact Weil operator. Ambient infinite rank does not imply the lower spectral bound needed to pay the WI-377 depth tariff.

**Boundary.** A decisive next theorem must estimate the lower spectral edge of the inherited signed translation sum on the actual low-frequency/source-zero trial spaces, or prove an admissible-domain relation excluding those vectors. Rank, branch count and source dimension are no longer adequate substitutes for that restricted spectral estimate.
