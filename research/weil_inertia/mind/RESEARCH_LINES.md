## Keep feature resolution, aperture, transmission, global phase and covariance sign separate

Diagonal-free does not mean destination-sensitive. Scalar cube refinements can reduce a generic chaos floor without reaching the distinguished source sign. Likewise, nonlinear and phase-sensitive do not mean high-dimensional: at fixed aperture, WI-345--WI-348 show that every feature family with a height-uniform useful continuity modulus remains compressed at fixed output tolerance.

The projective and ordinary results price slightly different source geometries. After quotienting global phase, the carrier is essentially one-dimensional and WI-347 gives the stronger `rho=O(1/r)` scale. Retaining the phase produces a two-dimensional cylinder and WI-348 gives `rho=O(r^(-1/2))`. The weaker exponent is not free information; it is exactly the extra compact phase coordinate.

A proposed bridge should therefore state the source-start quantifier, exact carrier/feature family, normalization, physical logarithmic aperture, whether absolute phase is retained, the useful continuity/resolution modulus at height `T`, transmitted amplitude, and the exact signed off-diagonal covariance it forces. A large channel count or complicated feature map is not new arithmetic information unless its resolution on the physical carrier family scales strongly enough and the destination receives that information without losing the distinguished sign.
