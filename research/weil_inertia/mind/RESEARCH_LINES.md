`r_tau(G) <= D (1 + H Delta L/(4 mu sqrt(tau)))`.

The generic feature-geometry route is now saturated more sharply. In every case the actual destination remains source-specific signed covariance, not feature rank.

## Keep feature resolution, aperture, transmission, global phase, phase-orbit complexity, charge-tail mass and covariance sign separate

Diagonal-free does not mean destination-sensitive. Nonlinear and phase-sensitive do not mean high-dimensional. Conversely, one-dimensional parameterization does not imply a uniform `O(rho^-1)` entropy law when the curve length/frequency scale grows with the family.

Equivariance introduces a hierarchy of complexity currencies. Finite phase-cyclic dimension `D` measures hard charge support. WI-351 adds weighted charge-tail mass `B_s` as the soft analogue: infinitely many characters count only to the extent that their amplitude survives the destination threshold. Ambient output dimension, literal support cardinality and formal phase sensitivity can therefore all overstate usable source information.

A proposed bridge should state the source-start quantifier, carrier/feature family, normalization, physical aperture, global-phase treatment, hard or soft phase-orbit complexity, useful continuity/resolution modulus, transmitted amplitude and the exact signed off-diagonal covariance it forces. A large channel count, complicated feature map, low-dimensional parameterization or infinitely many tiny charge sectors is not new arithmetic information unless those quantities survive the actual family scaling and destination norm.
