## Keep bow de-exceptionalization on arithmetic cancellation, not reciprocal-scale sparsity or direct dual contraction

**Linked intuition:** `MI-036-exact-zero-density-locks-bow-reciprocal-scale-at-riemann-siegel-length`.

WI-322 corrects the reciprocal source scale for a source-compatible symmetrized Maynard--Pratt bow by retaining the exact local Riemann--von Mangoldt factor before exponentiation. Its reciprocal prime length satisfies

`X_* <= sqrt(U/(2pi))(1+o(1))`,

so `rho_T=U/X_*^2 >= 2pi(1-o(1))`. The distinguished logarithmic chirp therefore cannot become asymptotically sparse through an uncontrolled approach of the bow spacing to `4pi`; every growing coupled shift crosses at least linearly many stationary derivative levels.

WI-323 combines that scale with the exact two-dimensional logarithmic stationary map. Writing `A=U/(2pi)`, the direct dual length is `Y_*=A/X_*`, hence

`Y_*/X_* = A/X_*^2 >= 1-o(1)`.

At exact local count saturation the transform is asymptotically self-dual; on the shorter-source side the dual is longer. The stationary map also preserves the multiplicative ratio `p/q=n/m`, the relative bandwidth, and the square-root normalization `1/sqrt(mn) -> 1/sqrt(pq)` exactly. Direct approximate-functional-equation/Poisson/B-process geometry therefore supplies neither a hidden bandwidth contraction nor a normalized amplitude gain.

These are route-closing statements, not covariance estimates. The accepted distinguished-twist problem remains an arithmetic cancellation problem in the transformed von-Mangoldt coefficients. A viable advance must use signed source-specific cancellation, a stronger localized second-moment/dispersion theorem, a genuinely different source decomposition, spectral input, or another source-fixed observable with incompatible near-null directions. Reapplying reciprocal duality alone cannot turn the bow square into a shorter black-box Dirichlet object.
