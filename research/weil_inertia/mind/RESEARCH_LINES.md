## Close the remaining logarithmic gap between macroscopic screening cost and positive-density zero cost

**Linked intuition:** `MI-014-source-coercivity-must-survive-confluence-and-drifting-bows`.

WI-183--WI-215 reduce the count-saturating bow to a distinguished source-fixed von-Mangoldt covariance problem and progressively close bounded residue packets, window shaping, PSD multiwindow lifting, finite discrete screens, and fixed-rank continuum screens. A fixed number of exponential/zero channels cannot flatten even one natural Gallagher slab to diagonal scale.

WI-216--WI-217 now quantify how screen cost grows with observation size. On a slab widened only linearly with an `n`-channel screen, subdiagonal selected energy requires `n/log T -> infinity`, and the existing midpoint construction shows this logarithmic threshold is sharp inside the screen countermodel. On a **fixed multiplicative source slab**, the whole translating nullspace must be paid: subdiagonal energy forces effective channel rank `n >= (1-o(1))q` for every available translate budget `q`, hence `n = Omega(M/log T)` at macroscopic width.

This is still `o(M)`, so it does not force a positive-density exceptional-zero population. The live theorem is to remove the remaining logarithmic slack, derive an independent source restriction that makes `M/log T` screens inadmissible, or bypass the screen model with the full signed `Lambda-Lambda^sharp` covariance.

## Price observation scale, screen rank, radial depth, and zero count separately

A fixed finite screen can fit selected samples yet fail on a natural continuum slab. Superlogarithmic rank is enough on a screen-sized widened slab, while fixed multiplicative width charges almost one channel per available unit translate and raises the cost to `M/log T`. Neither statement by itself yields a positive fraction of the `M` bow zeros.

A useful theorem should state which observation width forces which complexity and why the **actual source** cannot supply or simulate the remaining screen budget. Channel count, pair count, physical depth, and zero density are different currencies.
