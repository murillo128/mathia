## Complete one-signed screening is closed; the remaining source currency is signed prime discrepancy

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-017-sliding-first-crossing-localization-produces-autocorrelation-symbols` through `MI-028-dyadic-screening-forces-a-uniform-archimedean-reserve-before-any-nonpositive-one-signed-weil-test`.

WI-298--WI-301 close complete one-signed dyadic screening. Rearrangement plus the independently replayed compact-window certificate gives

`q_arch(f) >= c_0 ||f||_2^2`,  `c_0=8.9e-18`,

for every nonzero real one-signed compactly supported test that screens all powers of two. Hence any such nonpositive Weil test needs a uniform unscreened arithmetic contribution.

WI-241 sharpens what that contribution must mean. For every compactly supported complex test, not only odd tests, the pole term and the density-one prime main term cancel exactly. The Weil form can be written schematically as

`Q_W = Q_gamma + R - 2 Delta_Lambda`,

where `R` is a bounded nonnegative density-one autocorrelation remainder and `Delta_Lambda` is the smoothed signed discrepancy of `d(psi-x)`. The total prime-power mass is therefore not the decisive currency: the universal main density has already been neutralized by the pole.

On the odd negative-index branch, Weil positivity would force a uniform signed deficit in `Delta_Lambda`. Thus the live source theorem is to control the **signed discrepancy relative to the density-one baseline**, not merely to prove that some unscreened prime-power mass exists.

## Audit the claimed `17/16` certificate while preserving the noncompact tail supplier

**Linked intuition:** `MI-029-post-threshold-compact-positivity-needs-noncompact-tail-retention-not-a-canonical-finite-rank-correction`.

WI-302 records a recent source-exact compact-positivity claim at apertures `1` and `17/16`, but its `N=448` package and analytic transfer remain author-certified rather than independently replayed. The positivity conclusion, claimed coercivity constant and conditional aperture bound therefore remain below accepted Mathia theorem-level evidence.

WI-303 nevertheless resolves the structural clue generated from that architecture. Liu's rank-two positive term is not a minimal post-threshold invariant. It is an optional finite-rank Loewner minorant extracted from the stronger positive tail operator `C(I-B_band)`. Classical positive-operator projection/shorting gives valid rank-zero, rank-one, rank-two and higher finite-dimensional minorants depending on the chosen trial subspace.

The source's own compact-repair obstruction makes the distinction sharper. Past `log 8 / 2`, a tail-dropped comparison cannot be repaired by any fixed compact self-adjoint correction; finite-rank terms are compact. The rank-two extraction therefore cannot be the structural cure. What changes the architecture is retention of a **noncompact source-exact positive tail supplier**, containing an identity component, before any finite-dimensional certificate is extracted.

The live tasks are now separate: independently replay and audit the `17/16` certificate; determine how much of the noncompact tail operator must be retained for a robust Schur margin; and optimize finite trial subspaces only as certificate design. Proving rank-two minimality is no longer a meaningful structural target.

## Keep structural reduction, compact positivity, noncompact tail retention, signed discrepancy and certificate provenance separate

WI-298 is an exact rearrangement theorem. WI-300 is a rigorous independently replayed computer-assisted compact-window certificate, not a formal Lean proof. WI-301 converts them into an aperture-independent reserve theorem on a nonlinear one-signed screened class. WI-302 remains at a different evidence level: a recent external source-exact certificate claim whose independent replay is pending. It does **not** validate the unreplayed `17/16` matrix.

None of these statements is unrestricted large-aperture Weil positivity. A lower bound on total unscreened interaction does not imply the signed `Delta_Lambda` estimate required after pole/main-density cancellation; retaining a noncompact tail supplier does not guarantee that a particular finite Schur certificate is positive; and an unreplayed external package does not extend the accepted compact aperture.
