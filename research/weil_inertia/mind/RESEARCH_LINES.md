## If using Bombieri truncations, control coefficient cost and height tightness rather than raw span separation

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-008-inertia-counts-offline-pairs-but-not-their-distance`, `MI-014-source-coercivity-must-survive-confluence-and-drifting-bows`.

The surviving Bombieri-side currency is coefficient price together with height tightness.

## Condition the finite-radius source estimate on the forced first odd zero mode

**Linked intuition:** `MI-016-finite-radius-source-localization-can-replace-zero-side-tightness`.

WI-238--WI-243 show why generic global signed-discrepancy control is not an intermediate theorem: subexponential growth of a natural diagnostic is already RH-equivalent.

WI-244--WI-247 produce and optimize the finite-radius odd-sector archimedean reserve and show that every RH failure forces a finite first odd zero mode, so the sharp reserve applies to an unavoidable first-crossing witness.

WI-248 closes uniform-symbol/triangle control of the prime source: recurrence forces the full positive coefficient mass and a doubly exponential resolution burden. WI-249 closes the stronger-looking all-odd discrepancy route even after density-one subtraction: smooth odd recurrent quasimodes force a uniform source scale `(2+o(1))e^a/a`, exponentially larger than the order-one first-crossing tariff.

The live theorem is therefore **eigenmode-conditioned and directional**. Show that the actual von-Mangoldt discrepancy cannot pay the joint reserve on the first odd zero mode by spending the zero-mode equation, prior positivity at smaller radii, localization of that mode's spectral mass, prime-phase nonalignment on that mass, or another bilinear invariant. No radius-only bound uniform over all odd tests can reach this target.

Global subexponential control is already RH. Finite support creates a calibrated `a^-2` reserve. Uniform prime-symbol and all-odd discrepancy bounds are exponentially too expensive. The remaining plausible leverage is a directional arithmetic constraint specific to the forced first-crossing odd mode.
