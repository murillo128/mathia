## Replace one-point phase cancellation by a signed two-point covariance mechanism

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-036-riemann-siegel-source-scale-makes-the-direct-logarithmic-dual-noncontracting` through `MI-041-one-point-phase-pseudorandomness-cannot-cancel-positive-diagonal-variance`.

WI-322--WI-329 progressively remove sparsity, direct-duality contraction, raw-positive resonance and bow-height phase diversity as generic escape mechanisms. The exact signed source `Lambda-Lambda_X^sharp` destroys the generic raw-positivity resonance on typical starts, but varying bow heights does not create independent source starts.

WI-331 then identifies a genuine regime split. When `0<theta<3/16`, the natural bow length lies inside an all-interval nilsequence theorem, so the distinguished-start exceptional-set problem disappears. For `3/16<=theta<=3943/12011`, the natural bow lies beyond that all-start range and the common exceptional-start issue remains.

WI-332 shows that the low-theta regime is not closed by inserting local `U^2` control. The bow phase is nonlinear at second order, and even an untwisted black-box conversion loses a factor of `K`: logarithmic `U^2` savings do not reach the `o(X K log X)` covariance scale.

WI-333 makes the missing structure categorical. Coefficients can exhibit essentially square-root cancellation against every fixed-degree polynomial phase on all short intervals and progressions while retaining the full diagonal sliding variance `~N K log N`. One-point pseudorandomness suppresses off-diagonal structure toward zero; it does not cancel the positive diagonal.

The live theorem below `theta<3/16` is therefore **signed two-point control**: an arithmetic identity, covariance theorem or source-fixed diagonal subtraction that forces a macroscopic negative off-diagonal contribution at the exact WI-195 scale. Above `3/16`, the same two-point problem remains together with the location/exceptional-start quantifier. Merely adding phase families, stronger one-point exponential-sum bounds or generic randomness cannot solve either regime by themselves.

## Keep source cancellation, start quantifiers and covariance sign separate

Density-one source cancellation, all-start source cancellation, one-point phase pseudorandomness and pointwise Weil covariance are different statements. WI-331 settles the start quantifier only in the low-theta regime. WI-332--WI-333 show that even much stronger one-point information need not imply the signed covariance needed after squaring.

A proposed bridge should therefore state separately: the source-start quantifier, the phase family, the one-point norm if any, the exact diagonal contribution, the off-diagonal covariance it can force, and the mechanism giving the required sign. The decisive question is no longer whether the source looks pseudorandom against many phases, but whether its two-point arithmetic structure can beat the diagonal variance on the distinguished bow.
