## Convert localized weighted source mass into an actual zero-count/source contradiction

**Linked intuition:** `MI-014-source-coercivity-must-survive-confluence-and-drifting-bows`.

WI-183--WI-220 show that fixed-band bow screening costs `Omega(M)` free channels, that this rank tariff is sharp in the relaxed class, and that support remoteness alone does not restore coercivity. WI-221--WI-223 recover a genuine source price from remoteness, while WI-224 shows that `T`-dependent uniformly conditioned compact spline detectors can improve the scalar screen to dimensionless cutoff `R_T=O(log T)`, i.e. physical radius `O(M)`.

WI-225 closes the adaptive scalar cutoff question in asymptotic order. Quantitative Logvinenko--Sereda applied to the detector transfer function forces every normalized fixed-support scalar detector to satisfy a stopband floor `sup_{|xi|>=R}|H_T(xi)| >= c_0 exp(-c_1 R)`. Any total-mass screen that must beat the polynomial factor `N_T/M` therefore needs `R_T=Omega(log T)`. Together with WI-224, the scalar global-supremum/total-mass architecture has the sharp order `R_T=Theta(log T)` and physical localization `Theta(M)`; it cannot reach `o(M)` by further window optimization alone.

The live theorem must therefore convert the already localized radial-weighted source into an incompatible **local zero-count/multiplicity/radial budget**, or retain structure discarded by scalar total-mass domination: coupled observables, phase/sign-sensitive cancellation, full signed source covariance, or arithmetic/zero-density restrictions on the remote reservoir.

## Price rank, coefficient mass, radial weight, support reach, and zero count separately

Free rank, remote completeness, weighted source localization, and raw zero count are distinct currencies. WI-221 closes the cheapest super-bow remote escape, and WI-224--WI-225 pin down the best possible scalar fixed-support localization scale, but the conclusion is still weighted source mass rather than an impossible number of actual zeros.

A useful theorem should state exactly which source constraint turns weighted mass in the `Theta(M)` neighborhood into a count/coefficient contradiction. Repeating fixed-profile Denjoy--Carleman decay estimates or adaptive scalar-window optimization past the Logvinenko--Sereda cutoff is now a control rather than a live route.
