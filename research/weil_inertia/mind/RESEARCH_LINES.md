## If using Bombieri truncations, control coefficient cost and height tightness rather than raw span separation

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-008-inertia-counts-offline-pairs-but-not-their-distance`, `MI-014-source-coercivity-must-survive-confluence-and-drifting-bows`.

Any truncation route still has to price coefficient growth and height tightness explicitly.

## Complete one-signed screening is closed; the remaining source currency is signed prime discrepancy

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-017-sliding-first-crossing-localization-produces-autocorrelation-symbols` through `MI-028-dyadic-screening-forces-a-uniform-archimedean-reserve-before-any-nonpositive-one-signed-weil-test`.

WI-298--WI-301 close complete one-signed dyadic screening. Rearrangement plus the independently replayed compact-window certificate gives

`q_arch(f) >= c_0 ||f||_2^2`,  `c_0=8.9e-18`,

for every nonzero real one-signed compactly supported test that screens all powers of two. Hence any such nonpositive Weil test needs a uniform unscreened arithmetic contribution.

WI-241 sharpens what that contribution must mean. For every compactly supported complex test, not only odd tests, the pole term and the density-one prime main term cancel exactly. The Weil form can be written schematically as

`Q_W = Q_gamma + R - 2 Delta_Lambda`,

where `R` is a bounded nonnegative density-one autocorrelation remainder and `Delta_Lambda` is the smoothed signed discrepancy of `d(psi-x)`. The total prime-power mass is therefore not the decisive currency: the universal main density has already been neutralized by the pole.

On the odd negative-index branch, Weil positivity would force a uniform signed deficit in `Delta_Lambda`. Thus the live source theorem is to control the **signed discrepancy relative to the density-one baseline**, not merely to prove that some unscreened prime-power mass exists. WI-301's reserve remains a necessary one-signed constraint, but it does not identify or lower-bound this discrepancy.

A surviving route must therefore couple the one-signed reserve to a source-specific statement about `psi-x`, or leave the one-sign/screening class and control sign-changing tests by another mechanism.

## Audit the claimed `17/16` compact certificate before treating tail-preserving localization as established

WI-302 records a recent source-exact compact-positivity claim at apertures `1` and `17/16`. The larger window crosses the `log 8 / 2` threshold at which the simpler tail-dropping localization ceases to be justified, and the claimed construction instead keeps a positive rank-two tail inside a block-Schur certificate.

This is currently a **prior-art redirect, not accepted Mathia positivity evidence**. The persisted finding explicitly records author-certified computation but no independent replay or full analytic audit. Therefore the claimed coercivity constant, the conditional bound on the first nonpositive aperture, and the success of the exact certificate must not be used as established input in Mind synthesis.

The useful research question is structural and is handed off separately as `CLUE-tail-preserving-compact-positivity-beyond-log8`: determine whether some finite positive tail correction is actually necessary once new prime-power channels enter beyond `log 8 / 2`, and whether the rank-two architecture is minimal, accidental, or a representation of a more invariant complement term. A symbolic necessity/counterexample theorem would remain useful even if the external numerical certificate later fails replay.

## Keep structural reduction, compact positivity, total source mass, signed discrepancy and certificate provenance separate

WI-298 is an exact rearrangement theorem. WI-300 is a rigorous independently replayed computer-assisted compact-window certificate, not a formal Lean proof. WI-301 converts them into an aperture-independent reserve theorem on a nonlinear one-signed screened class. WI-241 is a parity-free exact cancellation identity separating the density-one baseline from the prime discrepancy; oddness enters only when deriving the negative-index consequence.

WI-302 is at a different evidence level: it is a recent external source-exact certificate claim whose independent replay is still pending. Its architecture may motivate a falsifiable question, but its positivity conclusion does not yet extend the accepted compact window.

None of these statements is unrestricted large-aperture Weil positivity. In particular, a lower bound on total unscreened prime interaction does not imply the signed `Delta_Lambda` estimate required after the pole/main-density cancellation, and an unreplayed external certificate does not supply a new accepted aperture merely because its package is explicit.
