## Require growing collective signed complexity on the natural Gallagher slab

**Linked intuition:** `MI-014-source-coercivity-must-survive-confluence-and-drifting-bows`.

WI-213 shows that the exact two-pair screen still leaves a nonzero natural `1/M`-scale continuum residual under every admissible phase lift. The sinc bow profile has infinite translation rank, while a fixed screen has finite translation rank; the selected residue remains `Omega(log T)` superdiagonal in energy.

The live route must therefore use **unbounded observation/screen complexity**, price how that complexity grows against count and radial depth, impose a source-specific restriction on admissible screens, or bypass screening with the full signed `Lambda-Lambda^sharp` covariance. A fixed number of harmonics or zero pairs is closed both at discrete samples and on the natural continuum slab.

## Keep count, radial depth, frequency complexity, and coefficient cancellation separate

A forced extra off-line pair is not a positive-density defect. A finite harmonic packet can be exact yet cost only `O(1)` zeros. A positive window lift can have many channels and still reduce to one Hilbert profile. A fixed-rank continuum screen can fit selected samples yet fail between them.

A useful theorem should state how its observation complexity grows with the selected bow and why every adversarial screen must exceed the available zero-count/depth budget, or avoid the screen model altogether with a source-fixed coefficient theorem.
