## Match the gamma block's linearly growing negative sector with source-conditioned rank and singular-value reserve

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-010-pair-correlation-slack-is-a-shared-exceptional-budget`, `MI-029-post-threshold-compact-positivity-needs-noncompact-tail-retention-not-a-canonical-finite-rank-correction`, `MI-050-full-localized-weil-energy-turns-any-rh-failure-into-a-finite-radius-first-crossing`, `MI-051-local-threshold-continuity-does-not-bootstrap-cofinally-from-an-energy-budget-alone`, `MI-052-common-cut-short-preserves-inherited-pivot-without-double-spending`, `MI-053-odd-parity-couples-difference-and-image-kernels-before-half-density-transport`, and `MI-054-the-gamma-floor-carries-a-linearly-growing-negative-index`.

WI-369 gives the exact gamma-corrected archimedean decomposition with sharp lower floor `-C_Gamma I`, where `C_Gamma=pi/2+gamma+log(8 pi)`. WI-370 rules out the conservative scalar repair of the published MASTER budget: at `k=33` even the unspent direct target-ground coefficient is already below `C_Gamma` before later nonnegative debits.

WI-371 strengthens this from a scalar-floor warning to an inertia statement. Compactly supported bulk test spaces of dimension `m_A=floor(c_* A)` can be chosen so that the entire gamma block is uniformly negative on them, with `c_*` explicit. Hence the negative index grows at least linearly in the aperture parameter `A`. Any additive positive repair that makes the full block nonnegative must therefore carry rank `Omega(A)`; more generally a finite Schatten-`p` repair must spend `Omega(A^(1/p))` norm. A fixed finite-rank or fixed compact correction cannot finance the bulk sector.

The live source question is now quantitative rather than merely vector-valued. What effective rank and singular-value budget does the actual source network contribute on these interior bulk quasimodes as `A` grows? Either the source-conditioned subspace excludes a linear fraction of the negative directions, or its positive repair must provide comparable growing rank and enough eigenvalue mass on precisely those directions. Counting nominal source coordinates is not enough unless their coupling to the negative subspace is proved on the common form domain.

## Keep full-core inertia, source dimension and operator reserve distinct

The gamma block's linear negative index does not by itself disprove a source construction whose active source dimension also grows with `A`. Conversely, a growing number of source coordinates does not pay the debt automatically. Future arguments must identify the source-conditioned image, its overlap with the WI-371 bulk subspaces and the singular values of the positive correction there. Scalar reserve, nominal rank and actual coercive operator mass are separate currencies and cannot be substituted without a quantitative comparison.
