`(B_Q(h_i,h_j)) >= -c(<h_i,h_j>)`.

The live route therefore needs genuinely new non-scalar source information: a matrix lower bound not implied by polarization, prime- or mode-dependent admissible states, source-specific indefinite coupling, a stronger localized arithmetic estimate, a different exact decomposition, or direct coercivity/inertia control.
