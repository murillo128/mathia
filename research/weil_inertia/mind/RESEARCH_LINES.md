## Keep kernel transport, difference-form transport, conditional local algebra and global RH distinct

The current evidence now separates four layers sharply. Off-diagonal operator kernels can conjugate correctly while the corresponding closed difference form acquires a Jacobian multiplier. The normalized one-cell form may still be repairable after all scalar terms are transported. The common-cut/inherited-pivot algebra can be exact once that form is granted. And none of those local statements by itself establishes the finite-plus-analytic cofinal certificate chain or RH.

Future synthesis should therefore promote only the layer independently checked. The decisive next test is the explicit full-form identity containing the `W_A` contribution; only after it is settled should the audit return to the fold/intertwining and numerical/analytic globalization gates.
