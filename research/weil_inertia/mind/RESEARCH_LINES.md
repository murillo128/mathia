## Move beyond spectrally tight fixed-width source-zero coercivity

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-050-full-localized-weil-energy-turns-any-rh-failure-into-a-finite-radius-first-crossing` through `MI-064-fixed-width-source-zero-weil-trials-saturate-the-zero-coercivity-floor`, `MI-065-fixed-width-profile-freedom-cannot-open-a-weil-coercive-gap`.

WI-369--WI-385 progressively reduce the admitted fixed-width source-zero family to an exact three-layer ledger. The favorable critical arithmetic layer cancels the polar main term; prime-power sparsity removes the moving perforation reserve; and the classical explicit formula identifies the remaining stationary arithmetic reserve with the opposite of the archimedean debt. For the original cosine profile this gives `RH => liminf W[f_k]=0`, while non-RH gives arbitrarily negative excursions.

WI-386 shows that the cosine shape is not load-bearing. For every sufficiently regular real even compactly supported profile of fixed width, the separated odd pair has under RH a nonnegative uniformly almost-periodic spectral energy with recurrent near-zero returns. The endpoint mesh `A_k=log(k+1)` becomes fine enough to inherit them, exact source-slot perforation changes the form only by `o(1)`, and the profile-by-profile explicit formula locks the arithmetic-polar reserve to the archimedean debt. Choosing a different fixed lobe shape therefore cannot open a positive coercive gap.

WI-387 closes the next adaptive loophole. The profile may now vary arbitrarily with the endpoint, with no convergence or step-to-step regularity, provided the fixed-width family is uniformly spectrally tight at the zeta-zero frequencies. Finite-frequency Bohr/Kronecker recurrence is coefficient-independent, so one common low-frequency return works regardless of the changing profile coefficients; uniform spectral tightness controls the tail. A uniform fixed-width `H^1` bound is a concrete sufficient condition because it gives `|G_k(i gamma)|<<1/|gamma|` uniformly. The exact source-hole transfer remains uniformly `o(1)` on this class.

Thus endpoint-dependent shape adaptation with bounded Sobolev complexity also cannot create a positive Gate-II tariff under RH. To escape within fixed width, a family must at least lose uniform spectral tightness—within the natural Sobolev class this forces unbounded `H^1` complexity—or else change the trial geometry through growing width, multiscale/nonlocal structure, or an exact graph/domain restriction. That necessary complexity growth is not itself evidence of a successful mechanism.

The stronger non-RH divergence from the fixed-profile results does not automatically extend to adaptive families because the varying transforms may suppress selected off-line pole contributions. The durable barrier here is the RH-compatible failure of uniform coercivity, which is already enough to prune bounded-complexity fixed-width adaptation.

## Keep inherited core inertia, source restriction and elimination category distinct

The fixed-width quasimodes are now robust to both profile choice and arbitrary endpoint retuning inside uniformly spectrally tight families. Future claims should therefore state which resource actually changes: width, spectral complexity, locality, domain or operator category. A positive arithmetic reserve computed for an adaptive profile still cannot be credited independently of the full Weil form, and bounded-complexity shape retuning is not a new source mechanism.
