## Keep optimization loss separate from information loss

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-global-fenchel-dual-separates-losses`.

Global Fenchel coupling can remove artificial blockwise optimization loss, but no optimizer can reconstruct a discriminator already erased by the represented Gram/pressure data. Continue to audit the exact represented object before strengthening inequalities on it.

## Treat screening as an information-bandwidth obstruction

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-screening-is-an-information-bandwidth-obstruction`.

A decisive advance must enlarge the source-faithful information channel or prove that the existing channel already determines the missing witness variable.

## Work inside the exact finite-window alias quotient, or avoid scalarization before it forms

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-coupled-welding-uniformity-is-the-fourth-moment-gate` and `MI-004-w-conditioning-is-l2-compressible-but-l1-expensive`.

The universal signed scalar Ramanujan interface is now classified much more sharply. WI-082 shows that before overcompleteness a Vandermonde congruence fixes the positive and negative inertia exactly, so finite-window leakage cannot erase sign directions. WI-083 shows that after both sign dictionaries saturate, large-prime blocks collapse to two moments and admit exact nontrivial signed cancellation. WI-084 proves that subwindow blocks `B_1^(N),...,B_N^(N)` are an exact Toeplitz basis, and WI-085 completes the picture: **every** finitely supported arbitrary-modulus scalar law on an `N`-window factors through exactly `N` divisor/tail coordinates and has a unique subwindow alias.

Pairwise rank is therefore not the missing universal invariant. WI-086 identifies the only possible residual pairwise defect as an excess transversality intersection after the boundary exceeds both totient dimensions, while WI-087 gives an infinite close-prime family with asymptotic rank `2/3` and defect `1/3`. Large defects are real, but they do not prevent the full scalar alias quotient or the saturated cancellation examples.

The surviving fourth-moment routes must use information that the quotient does not erase. One route is a direct Yang-specific weighted incidence/rectangle estimate on the original non-Cartesian two-dimensional source. A second is to prove that the **actual source coefficients** occupy a restricted region of the `N`-coordinate alias quotient that forbids the universal cancellations. A third is the base-aggregated fixed-coefficient multivariate polynomial representation with its anisotropic boxes and outer prime/Mertens weights. A genuinely non-scalar finite-window object formed before Ramanujan scalarization is also admissible if it is forced by the source.

A decisive advance is a source-specific theorem on one of those retained structures. Generic many-modulus coherence, positive support/energy pruning, complete-period scalarization, pairwise rank saving, free independent shifts, fixed-`L^p` ambient bounds, and Cartesian reboxing are no longer distinct escape routes.
