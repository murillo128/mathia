## Move beyond spectrally tight fixed-width source-zero coercivity

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-050-full-localized-weil-energy-turns-any-rh-failure-into-a-finite-radius-first-crossing` through `MI-064-fixed-width-source-zero-weil-trials-saturate-the-zero-coercivity-floor`, `MI-065-fixed-width-profile-freedom-cannot-open-a-weil-coercive-gap`, `MI-066-dilute-high-frequency-sidebands-separate-gamma-margin-from-spectral-tightness`.

WI-369--WI-385 progressively reduce the admitted fixed-width source-zero family to an exact three-layer ledger. The favorable critical arithmetic layer cancels the polar main term; prime-power sparsity removes the moving perforation reserve; and the classical explicit formula identifies the remaining stationary arithmetic reserve with the opposite of the archimedean debt. For the original cosine profile this gives `RH => liminf W[f_k]=0`, while non-RH gives arbitrarily negative excursions.

WI-386 shows that the cosine shape is not load-bearing. For every sufficiently regular real even compactly supported profile of fixed width, the separated odd pair has under RH a nonnegative uniformly almost-periodic spectral energy with recurrent near-zero returns. The endpoint mesh `A_k=log(k+1)` becomes fine enough to inherit them, exact source-slot perforation changes the form only by `o(1)`, and the profile-by-profile explicit formula locks the arithmetic-polar reserve to the archimedean debt. Choosing a different fixed lobe shape therefore cannot open a positive coercive gap.

WI-387 closes the bounded-complexity adaptive loophole. The profile may vary arbitrarily with the endpoint, with no convergence or step-to-step regularity, provided the fixed-width family is uniformly spectrally tight at the zeta-zero frequencies. Finite-frequency Bohr/Kronecker recurrence is coefficient-independent, so one common low-frequency return works regardless of the changing profile coefficients; uniform spectral tightness controls the tail. A uniform fixed-width `H^1` bound is a concrete sufficient condition because it gives `|G_k(i gamma)|<<1/|gamma|` uniformly. The exact source-hole transfer remains uniformly `o(1)` on this class.

WI-388 shows that the missing tightness is not implied by the same negative archimedean margin used by the lobe construction. A sideband at carrier frequency `R` costs only `O(log R)` in the gamma difference form. Allocating `epsilon_R=c/log R` of the `L^2` mass to that sideband preserves a uniform strict negative `B_g` margin, but a suitable fixed-width frequency packet contains `Omega(log R)` zeta ordinates. The resulting `Omega(1/log R)` mass per ordinate therefore leaves `Omega(1)` sampled energy escaping to infinity. The profiles necessarily have `||g_R'||_2 >> R/sqrt(log R)`, so this is a genuine unbounded-complexity escape from the WI-387 hypothesis rather than a contradiction to it.

The same construction survives exact source perforation along a diagonal endpoint choice. Source exactness and negative gamma energy therefore do not recover spectral compactness once high-frequency profile complexity is allowed.

The live fixed-width question is now sharper than “let the profile become rough.” There are two distinct routes. A stronger no-go theorem would need a source-admissible norm, moment or arithmetic condition that directly controls the zeta-sampled tail beyond the negative gamma budget. A positive route would have to exploit the escaped high-frequency mass while also controlling the endpoint phase factors `sin^2(gamma A_k/2)` strongly enough to prevent the recurrent near-zero mechanism. WI-388 establishes the availability of spectral escape, not the phase-stable positive floor.

Growing width, multiscale/nonlocal structure, semidefinite use or an exact graph/domain restriction remain separate geometry changes.

## Keep inherited core inertia, source restriction and elimination category distinct

The fixed-width quasimodes are robust to both profile choice and arbitrary endpoint retuning inside uniformly spectrally tight families. WI-388 adds the converse warning needed at the frontier: a strict negative archimedean budget plus exact source slots can coexist with order-one spectral mass at frequencies tending to infinity. Future claims must therefore state which resource actually changes—spectral tightness, phase control, width, locality, domain or operator category—and must not treat gamma negativity as a substitute compactness estimate.

A positive arithmetic reserve computed for an adaptive profile still cannot be credited independently of the full Weil form. Conversely, destroying the compactness hypothesis is only removal of an obstruction; it is not evidence that the escaped mass lands with the sign and endpoint phases required for coercivity.
