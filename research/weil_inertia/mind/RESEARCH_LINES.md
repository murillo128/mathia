## Keep verified reductions, candidate architecture, reserve accounting, certificates and the RH conclusion distinct

The current evidence separates several layers that must not be merged. The odd restricted criterion can be correct while the positivity induction fails. Zero-extension compatibility can hold while a common-complement short double counts reserve. A scalar Schur identity can be algebraically valid while the inherited response is placed in the wrong physical metric. Printed positive margins can agree numerically while the interval certificate or analytic normalization is wrong.

Future synthesis should therefore promote components only as they are independently established. The strongest test is not whether the preprint's architecture resembles the source-exact mechanism the line wanted, but whether every common-cut, fold, inherited-response and certificate quantity can be reconstructed from the full localized form with the same reserve charged exactly once. Generic scalar envelopes and fixed compact repairs remain excluded by WI-359--WI-360 regardless of the outcome of this audit.
