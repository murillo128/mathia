## Keep optimization loss separate from information loss

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-global-fenchel-dual-separates-losses`.

Global Fenchel coupling can remove artificial blockwise optimization loss, but no optimizer can reconstruct a discriminator already erased by the represented Gram/pressure data. Continue to audit the exact represented object before strengthening inequalities on it.

## Close the fourth-moment gate on the actual weighted locked source

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-coupled-welding-uniformity-is-the-fourth-moment-gate` and `MI-004-w-conditioning-is-l2-compressible-but-l1-expensive`.

The local main and a genuine positive-power region are controlled, but the exact Yang lock now has a sharply constrained representation boundary. In free physical-shift coordinates the changing-slope union is `lcm`-sparse in two dimensions; every source-agnostic fixed finite `L^p` restriction pays a power, and separate Cartesian pruning cannot remove the long-shift large-GCD sparsity. Projecting to the scalar LCM does not rescue this through support cardinality: WI-076 proves from a fixed source slope and Bienvenu's prime-pattern theorem that the **effective weighted scalar support already has size at least `X/(log X)^4`**, so it is not power-sparse.

Three genuinely source-sensitive routes remain. One is a direct weighted incidence/rectangle estimate on the non-Cartesian two-dimensional source. A second is a scalar theorem using the actual weights, additive energy, or retained reduced-slope labels rather than support sparsity. The third is the base-aggregated fixed-coefficient multivariate polynomial representation of WI-070, with its anisotropic boxes and outer prime/Mertens weights.

A decisive advance is therefore a Yang-specific weighted rectangle/incidence theorem, a source-weighted scalar-energy/cancellation theorem, a labelled transform with acceptable factorization cost, or a quantitative multivariate polynomial von-Mangoldt theorem preserving the exact source normalization. Free independent shifts, fixed-`L^p` ambient bounds, Cartesian reboxing, and scalar support counting are now all ruled out as cheap interfaces.
