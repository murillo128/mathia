## If using Bombieri truncations, control coefficient cost and height tightness rather than raw span separation

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-008-inertia-counts-offline-pairs-but-not-their-distance`, `MI-014-source-coercivity-must-survive-confluence-and-drifting-bows`.

WI-231--WI-237 show that finite negative inertia detects off-line packets exactly but can lose coercivity as height grows. The unconditional simple critical-line exponentials are complete on every bounded positive-measure window and are not a Bessel family, so ordinary separation/Riesz arguments cannot exclude spectral collapse. The surviving Bombieri-side currency is coefficient price together with height tightness.

## Close the first odd crossing through its signed cutoff-flux constraints

**Linked intuition:** `MI-016-finite-radius-source-localization-can-replace-zero-side-tightness`.

WI-238--WI-243 show why generic global signed-discrepancy control is not an intermediate theorem: subexponential growth of a natural diagnostic is already RH-equivalent. WI-244--WI-247 calibrate the finite-radius odd-sector archimedean reserve and show that every RH failure forces a finite attained first odd zero mode, so the reserve applies to an unavoidable first-crossing witness.

WI-248--WI-249 close uniform-symbol/triangle and all-odd discrepancy routes: recurrent carriers force source scales exponentially larger than the first-crossing tariff. The remaining estimate must be eigenmode-conditioned.

WI-250 now supplies the exact localization interface. For every even cutoff `chi` supported at a strict interior radius `b<a`, the same first zero mode satisfies a signed von-Mangoldt cutoff-flux inequality whose positive side is `lambda_b^- ||chi v||^2` and whose archimedean localization loss is at most `(1/4) zeta(3,5/4)||chi'||_infty^2`. The source orientation survives; no absolute-value envelope is taken.

The live theorem is therefore precise: prove enough interior gap-times-mass to beat the IMS cost, bound the actual signed prime flux on this mode, or show that one mode cannot satisfy the full nested family of cutoff inequalities. The required arithmetic input must distinguish the von-Mangoldt source from a generic first-crossing operator.

## Treat global signed growth, finite-radius reserve, uniform odd-window discrepancy, and first-mode flux as separate gates

Global subexponential control is already RH. Finite support creates a calibrated `a^-2` reserve. Uniform prime-symbol and all-odd discrepancy bounds are exponentially too expensive. WI-250 converts prior interior positivity into a directional source constraint, but it does not yet provide a contradictory quantitative lower or upper bound. The frontier is now the compatibility of that signed flux family with one forced first-zero mode.
