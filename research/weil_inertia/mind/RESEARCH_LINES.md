## Make the finite-radius signed source fail the sharp joint `a^-2` tariff on the forced odd first crossing

**Linked intuition:** `MI-016-finite-radius-source-localization-can-replace-zero-side-tightness`.

WI-238--WI-243 show why generic global signed-discrepancy control is not an intermediate theorem: subexponential growth of a natural diagnostic is already RH-equivalent.

WI-244--WI-245 produce an explicit finite-radius odd-sector archimedean reserve. WI-246 then optimizes the entire positive digamma-resolvent form jointly: incompatible Robin ground states make the exact finite-radius reserve strictly larger than the termwise sum, but both have the same leading constant times `a^-2`. WI-247 closes the parity gap: every RH failure forces a finite first **odd** zero mode, so the source tariff applies to an unavoidable first-crossing witness rather than to a hypothetical parity choice.

WI-248 closes the phase-blind shortcut. The finite prime-power cosine comb has recurrent tail supremum equal to its full positive coefficient mass, and the resulting uniform-symbol envelope does not become cheaper at high frequency. In the one-stroke compact-window certificate this forces a cutoff of size `exp((4+o(1)) e^a)`. Thus operator-norm/triangle or uniform pointwise-envelope control of the prime source is not a scalable way to exclude the first crossing.

The live theorem is therefore directional and source-side. Show that the actual signed von-Mangoldt discrepancy cannot pay the **joint finite-radius reserve on the first odd zero mode**, using information the uniform envelope discards: the zero-mode equation, spectral-mass localization, prime-phase nonalignment on that mass, or another bilinear/averaged finite-radius invariant. Reoptimizing the same positive resolvents or the same uniform prime-symbol supremum cannot change the leading obstruction.

## Treat global signed growth, finite-radius mean reserve, and directional arithmetic source charge as separate gates

Global subexponential control is already RH. Finite support creates a classical `a^-2` reserve with a now-calibrated optimal leading coefficient. Uniform prime-symbol envelopes incur a doubly exponential resolution barrier. The remaining plausible leverage is a directional arithmetic constraint specific to the first-crossing zero mode, not a global norm bound on the source.
