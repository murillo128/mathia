## Force supercritical spectral shape beyond both screening rank and the unit-trace activation gate

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-global-fenchel-dual-separates-losses`, `MI-009-scalar-universality-collapses-to-fourier-positivity`, `MI-011-local-nonlinearity-is-lost-by-a-universal-scalar-tax`, `MI-012-affine-vector-retention-is-still-scalarization-at-the-witness`, and `MI-013-exceptional-populations-have-finite-spectral-screening-rank`.

WI-172--WI-180 establish that source placement matters but scalar pressure and finite additive vector storage are erased whenever the final proof consumes them through universal affine taxes on the same period-33 witness. WI-178--WI-180 retain a different kind of joint information: distinct exceptional elements bound the rank available to screen full-Gram modes above the clipping threshold `2`.

WI-181 adds the missing trace constraint. The simple-real Gram has unit diagonal and trace `n`, so every eigenvalue above `2` forces compensating subcritical defect. At a scalar budget `Q<=(1+c)N`, positivity of the WI-180 tail already requires `n/N>alpha_*(c)`; at the ideal Montgomery--Taylor budget the limiting activation threshold is about `0.79213`. This is not a zeta lower bound. It means the rank tail is a gated nonlinear mechanism, not an automatically available infinitesimal correction near the current scalar baseline.

The decisive source theorem must therefore force enough supercritical spectral multiplicity or participation beyond **both** the exceptional screening slots and the trace-budget activation threshold. Scalar pair energy or total Gram defect cannot substitute for this spectral-shape input.

## Treat free source-proximal mismatch and bounded-bandwidth confluence as insufficient controls

WI-182 closes the proposed source-structured proximal-mismatch correction as a free extensive gain from the existing Lamzouri geometry. Its exact nonnegative mismatch charge is bounded above by the complete remainder `R`, and WI-140 confluence near-extremizers make `R/N` and hence the mismatch density vanish at any prescribed simple-real fraction, including the Montgomery--Taylor target.

A strengthened mismatch route must therefore add genuinely new source information that excludes those confluence controls -- for example local-density/separation data, an independently controlled correlation observable, or a source-evaluable mixed moment. Repackaging the existing finite geometry cannot create the missing coercivity.

## Use symmetrized bows as the canonical slow-drift source control

**Linked intuition:** `MI-014-source-coercivity-must-survive-confluence-and-drifting-bows`.

WI-183 imports the Maynard--Pratt bow obstruction from peer-reviewed zero-density work. After Weil-inertia normalization, a bow is a locally arithmetic vertical chain whose horizontal depth changes by `o(1)` per site; functional-equation symmetry produces reflected off-line arms. Fixed bounded-depth blocks inherit the same local screening continuity as vertical-lattice controls, while Maynard--Pratt independently identify Poisson cancellation as the obstruction in the deep interior.

A viable zeta-specific anti-confluence theorem should therefore charge horizontal drift/curvature along a near-arithmetic ordinate chain, exploit interaction between the reflected arms, or force a detectable/half-isolated zero inside every sufficiently long bow. Excluding isolated shallow pairs or finitely many fixed vertical lines is not enough.

## Keep off-line mass, multiplicity, distinct exceptional count, and spectral activation separate

WI-178 improves the joint off-line/multiplicity budget, WI-179--WI-180 show that distinct exceptional count governs screening rank, and WI-181 shows that unit trace governs whether the supercritical tail can activate at all. Future source estimates should preserve these distinctions.
