## Separate off-line mass and multiplicity, then demand a new source budget to distinguish them

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-006-source-rigidity-can-eliminate-screening-extremizers`, `MI-008-inertia-counts-offline-pairs-but-not-their-distance`, and `MI-010-pair-correlation-slack-is-a-shared-exceptional-budget`.

WI-170 rewrites the complete Lamzouri slack as an exact joint budget `Q-N = O + 2M + R`, where `O` is multiplicity-weighted off-line mass, `M` is total multiplicity excess, and `R>=0` is source/operator slack. The coefficients on `O` and `M` are individually sharp: shallow simple off-line pairs and real doubles can spend the same scalar budget at leading order. The distinctness theorem therefore does not provide an independent bootstrap reservoir for the simple-critical theorem.

Together with WI-169, this rules out positive repartitioning of existing horizontal slack as a coercive mechanism. A surviving RH-facing theorem must derive source information that prevents the off-line/multiplicity exchange or supplies a genuinely different signed/mixed observable.
