## Turn first-crossing source order into unavoidable prime overlap

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-017-sliding-first-crossing-localization-produces-autocorrelation-symbols`, `MI-018-one-signed-first-crossings-pay-prime-overlap-or-short-range-mass`.

WI-253--WI-267 isolate the phase-coupled source cone, defeat finite-lag screening and generic first-crossing substitutes, and prove from Suzuki's actual null equation that every point outside a nonzero null mode's support is reached by at least one active prime-power translation. WI-268 closes the generic sign shortcut: the unrestricted archimedean cross kernel changes sign before the first prime lag, so ordinary positivity-preserving-semigroup theory is unavailable.

WI-269 derives a source-specific replacement. At a hypothetical first unrestricted crossing, the ground-state transform of every bounded Lipschitz multiplier is nonnegative. Polarization gives a PSD signed-source bilinear form, every finite multiplier partition produces a PSD signed Laplacian, and the nodal split forces positive-lag opposite-sign overlap to be financed by long-range negative archimedean overlap.

WI-270 extracts a quantitative consequence on the **one-signed branch**. For almost every cut, firstness gives a positive half-mode cost while the exact source decomposition writes the same cut energy as prime crossing overlap plus archimedean crossing overlap. Nonnegativity allows the long-range negative archimedean part to be discarded, and the short positive kernel is controlled by the sharp Hilbert--Carleman inequality. Every cut must therefore be paid by prime overlap or by bilateral short-range mass.

WI-271 converts a genuine support gap into direct arithmetic overlap. If an internal one-signed gap has width at least the exact archimedean sign-change length `h_0=log rho`, then every cross-gap pair lies where the continuous source weight is nonpositive. The archimedean part therefore cannot pay the strictly positive proper-aperture firstness cost, so the von-Mangoldt source must: the weighted prime-power cross-gap mass is positive, hence some active prime-power lag has `C_v(log n)>0`. Complete prime screening thus forces the support to be `h_0`-gap-connected, sharpening the `2h_0` Carleman corollary and replacing a topological exclusion by a positive autocorrelation statement.

WI-272 removes the hard threshold at `h_0`. For an internal one-signed gap of width `L`, the only archimedean currency that can help screening is the positive tail `B(L)=int_L^{h_0} w(h)dh`. Young's `L^1*L^2 -> L^2` inequality gives the exact tariff `P_gap >= max(lambda_L M_L,lambda_R M_R)-B(L)sqrt(M_L M_R)`. Complete screening therefore requires `B(L)^2 >= lambda_L lambda_R` and pins the side-mass ratio to a finite interval. Since `B(L)` vanishes quadratically as `L` approaches `h_0` from below, every proper-aperture margin has a strict screened-gap exclusion collar below the sign-change scale. The same scalar tail method cannot control arbitrarily narrow gaps because `B(L)` diverges logarithmically as `L` tends to zero.

The live theorem still splits by sign geometry. On a one-signed branch, the residual escape is now a quantitatively short-gap or gapless mode whose singular short-range archimedean network pays every proper-aperture cut; attack that regime with the full cut/null-equation family or a sharper source-conditioned operator norm rather than another hard `h_0` threshold. On sign-changing branches, combine the WI-269 PSD/nodal family with support domination strongly enough to control long-range archimedean compensation. Either route must remain source-specific; generic Perron--Frobenius structure is unavailable.

## Treat translated activity, scalar autocorrelation, nodal overlap, PSD multiplier rigidity, cutwise concentration and source-tail gap budgets as separate gates

WI-267 proves support domination, not `C_v(log n)!=0`. WI-268 proves failure of standard positivity preservation, not sign change of the particular mode. WI-272 extends that implication below `h_0` only when the positive source-tail budget is smaller than the proper-aperture firstness cost; it does not force a gap or control the narrow-gap singular regime.

Any successful next step must use the exact source-specific family appropriate to the mode's sign/support geometry. On the one-signed branch the residual escape is short-range connectivity strong enough to satisfy the WI-272 width--mass tariff at every gap/cut; on the sign-changing branch it remains long-range archimedean compensation.
