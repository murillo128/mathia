# Weil-inertia research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable Weil-inertia intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they separate genuinely different mechanisms.

## Keep optimization loss separate from information loss

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-global-fenchel-dual-separates-losses`.

Global Fenchel coupling can remove artificial blockwise optimization loss, but no optimizer can reconstruct a discriminator already erased by the represented Gram/pressure data. Continue to audit the exact represented object before strengthening inequalities on it.

## Treat screening as an information-bandwidth obstruction

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-screening-is-an-information-bandwidth-obstruction`.

When the represented test family cannot resolve the coordinates needed by an off-line witness, sharper optimization of the same screened object cannot recover them. A decisive advance must enlarge the source-faithful information channel or prove that the existing channel already determines the missing witness variable.

## Close the fourth-moment gate on the actual signed finite-window source

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-coupled-welding-uniformity-is-the-fourth-moment-gate` and `MI-004-w-conditioning-is-l2-compressible-but-l1-expensive`.

The source-agnostic exits are now sharply constrained. Free physical shifts are `lcm`-sparse in two dimensions and expensive in every fixed finite `L^p` ambient norm. Scalar LCM projection is near-linear rather than sparse, and WI-077--WI-078 show that both its unweighted and positive source-weighted additive energies have maximal exponent, even after mass-preserving positive pruning. Existing positive sparse-moduli large-sieve theory therefore cannot exploit the remaining Yang signs.

The signed scalar operator has a more rigid exact structure. WI-079 identifies a Ramanujan-sum Toeplitz kernel. WI-080 shows that distinct modulus blocks are orthogonal projectors on a complete common period, so signs cannot cancel there at all. WI-081 proves that finite-window cross-modulus coupling is controlled by pairwise LCM boundary rank and that this rank is already sharp in the small-boundary regime and generically maximal for sufficiently separated prime blocks.

Three genuinely source-sensitive routes remain. One is a direct weighted incidence/rectangle estimate on the non-Cartesian two-dimensional source. A second is the **centered signed finite-window Ramanujan operator**, using singular values or many-modulus coherence beyond pairwise rank and preserving the actual Yang coefficients. The third is the base-aggregated fixed-coefficient multivariate polynomial representation of WI-070, with its anisotropic boxes and outer prime/Mertens weights.

A decisive advance is therefore a Yang-specific weighted rectangle theorem, a signed finite-window Ramanujan cancellation theorem with the source coefficients explicit, or a quantitative multivariate polynomial von-Mangoldt theorem preserving the exact normalization. Positive support/energy pruning, complete-period scalarization, generic pairwise rank saving, free independent shifts, fixed-`L^p` ambient bounds, and Cartesian reboxing are now ruled out as cheap interfaces.
