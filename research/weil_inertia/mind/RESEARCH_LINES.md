## Find genuinely intermediate coercivity rather than an RH-equivalent threshold or scalar self-bootstrap

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-017-sliding-first-crossing-localization-produces-autocorrelation-symbols` through `MI-035-finite-localization-portfolios-cannot-self-contract-a-scalar-defect`.

WI-304--WI-316 build the source-exact comparison architecture, separate compact negative sectors from positive tail reserve, and show that cofinal vanishing-cap comparison bottoms converge to the global compact-support Weil floor. Consequently a comparison lower bound with `o(1)` threshold loss is already RH-equivalent; vanishing threshold error is not a softer intermediate theorem.

WI-317--WI-321 close scalar self-bootstrap through increasingly broad localization architectures. Feeding the same scalar lower semibound back through finite portfolios or adaptive windows cannot produce a contraction; a fixed short prime-free witness makes the exact localization defect nonnegative; and the scalar premise already amplifies dimension-free to Hilbert-valued diagonal forms and their Loewner Gram inequalities. Channel dimension, complex phase and fixed PSD mixing therefore add no information unless the premise itself contains a genuinely coupled source inequality.

The live route needs non-scalar source information outside the complete Loewner cone generated by the scalar semibound: prime- or mode-dependent admissible states, source-specific indefinite coupling, a stronger localized arithmetic estimate, a different exact decomposition, or direct coercivity/inertia control.

## Keep bow de-exceptionalization on arithmetic cancellation, not reciprocal-scale sparsity, direct dual contraction or endpoint nonresonance

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-036-exact-zero-density-locks-bow-reciprocal-scale-at-riemann-siegel-length`, `MI-037-riemann-siegel-endpoint-normalization-does-not-imply-carrier-nonresonance`.

WI-322 corrects the reciprocal source scale for a source-compatible symmetrized Maynard--Pratt bow by retaining the exact local Riemann--von Mangoldt factor before exponentiation. Its reciprocal prime length satisfies `X_* <= sqrt(U/(2pi))(1+o(1))`, so `rho_T=U/X_*^2 >= 2pi(1-o(1))`. The distinguished logarithmic chirp therefore cannot become asymptotically sparse merely through an uncontrolled approach of the bow spacing to `4pi`.

WI-323 combines that scale with the exact two-dimensional logarithmic stationary map. Writing `A=U/(2pi)`, the direct dual length is `Y_*=A/X_*`, hence `Y_*/X_* >= 1-o(1)`. At exact local count saturation the transform is asymptotically self-dual; on the shorter-source side the dual is longer. The stationary map also preserves the multiplicative ratio, relative bandwidth and square-root normalization. Direct approximate-functional-equation/Poisson/B-process geometry therefore supplies neither a hidden bandwidth contraction nor a normalized amplitude gain.

WI-324 now closes the remaining attempt to turn the sharper endpoint normalization itself into generic carrier cancellation. The explicit cofinal family `U_M=2pi M(M+1/2)`, `X_M=M` approaches `rho=2pi` from above while its direct dual is only `M+1/2`, yet the integer lattice aliases the linear and quadratic Taylor jets of `n^(iU_M)`. A block of length `asymp M^(1/3)` remains coherent. Thus arbitrarily accurate Riemann--Siegel endpoint saturation is compatible with short coherent resonance at the level of the exact scale information retained by WI-322--WI-323.

This is an information barrier, not a claim about the actual distinguished zeta height. The surviving bow problem is therefore still the signed arithmetic coefficient problem. A viable de-exceptionalization theorem must use information absent from the scalar ratio and bare logarithmic phase geometry: quantitative Diophantine information about the actual distinguished height, cancellation in the von-Mangoldt coefficients, a norm-matched dispersion theorem, a different source decomposition, spectral input, or another source-fixed observable.

## Track comparison coercivity, compact complexity and information content separately

Endpoint concentration, carrier frequency, compact correction size, cap defect, localization complexity and channel dimension remain implementation resources. The decisive currency is the new source information introduced beyond those universal transformations.

Finite-dimensional trial-subspace optimization remains downstream of source-valid information retention. Any compact certificate contributing to RH must import a nontrivial sign-producing estimate rather than rely on iteration, channelization or reciprocal geometric recoding.
