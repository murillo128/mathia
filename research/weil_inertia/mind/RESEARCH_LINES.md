## Turn first-crossing source order into unavoidable prime overlap

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-017-sliding-first-crossing-localization-produces-autocorrelation-symbols`, `MI-018-one-signed-first-crossings-pay-prime-overlap-or-short-range-mass`.

WI-253--WI-267 isolate the phase-coupled source cone, defeat finite-lag screening and generic first-crossing substitutes, and prove from Suzuki's actual null equation that every point outside a nonzero null mode's support is reached by at least one active prime-power translation. WI-268 closes the generic sign shortcut: the unrestricted archimedean cross kernel changes sign before the first prime lag, so ordinary positivity-preserving-semigroup theory is unavailable.

WI-269 derives a source-specific replacement. At a hypothetical first unrestricted crossing, the ground-state transform of every bounded Lipschitz multiplier is nonnegative. Polarization gives a PSD signed-source bilinear form, every finite multiplier partition produces a PSD signed Laplacian, and the nodal split forces positive-lag opposite-sign overlap to be financed by long-range negative archimedean overlap.

WI-270 now extracts a much sharper consequence on the **one-signed branch**. For almost every cut, firstness gives a positive half-mode cost while the exact source decomposition writes the same cut energy as prime crossing overlap plus archimedean crossing overlap. Nonnegativity allows the long-range negative archimedean part to be discarded, and the short positive kernel is controlled by the sharp Hilbert--Carleman inequality. Every cut must therefore be paid by prime overlap or by bilateral short-range mass. Under complete prime screening this becomes an explicit local concentration law; a median cut carries a positive bilateral mass product and an internal support gap of length at least `2h_0` is impossible.

The live theorem now splits by sign geometry. On a one-signed branch, determine whether the full family of Carleman cut tariffs propagates forced local concentration until an active prime-power translate must overlap, or construct a genuinely prime-screened mode satisfying all tariffs. On sign-changing branches, combine the WI-269 PSD/nodal family with support domination strongly enough to control long-range archimedean compensation. Either route must remain source-specific; generic Perron--Frobenius structure is unavailable.
