## Retain or neutralize every arithmetic channel whose omitted weight exceeds the high-frequency reserve

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-017-sliding-first-crossing-localization-produces-autocorrelation-symbols` through `MI-030-omitted-prime-power-atoms-create-essential-compact-invariant-bands`.

WI-298--WI-301 close complete one-signed dyadic screening and show that such screening leaves a uniform archimedean reserve. WI-241 separately identifies the source quantity after pole/main-density cancellation as signed prime discrepancy, not total prime-power mass.

WI-302--WI-303 study a different compact-positivity architecture. The external `17/16` finite certificate remains unreplayed, but the structural lesson is accepted: a finite-rank tail extraction is only a minorant of a stronger noncompact source-exact positive tail supplier, and a tail-dropped comparison cannot in general be repaired by a fixed compact correction.

WI-304 sharpens that obstruction atom by atom. If an active prime-power atom `q` can be isolated by the two-bump high-frequency construction, then the tail-dropped comparison has the essential numerical-range band

`[beta-w_q,beta+w_q]`,

`w_q=(Lambda(q)/sqrt(q))(1-rho_chi(log q))`,

where `beta` is the comparison's high-frequency reserve. Because the essential numerical range is invariant under compact perturbations,

`w_q>beta`

is an exact obstruction to positivity after **any** fixed compact self-adjoint repair. Liu's `q=8` example is the first numerical instance, not a special structural threshold.

A redesigned localization must therefore change something visible in this inequality: retain noncompact source-exact information for each dangerous channel, raise the high-frequency reserve, choose a localizer that reduces `1-rho_chi`, or add another noncompact term surviving the same weakly-null sequence. Literal retention of the entire infinite arithmetic tail is not forced, because `Lambda(q)/sqrt(q)->0`; only the channels exceeding the reserve are necessarily dangerous by this criterion.
