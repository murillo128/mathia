## Move beyond spectrally tight fixed-width source-zero coercivity

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-050-full-localized-weil-energy-turns-any-rh-failure-into-a-finite-radius-first-crossing` through `MI-064-fixed-width-source-zero-weil-trials-saturate-the-zero-coercivity-floor`, `MI-065-fixed-width-profile-freedom-cannot-open-a-weil-coercive-gap`, `MI-066-dilute-high-frequency-sidebands-separate-gamma-margin-from-spectral-tightness`, `MI-067-zeta-spectral-tightness-has-a-sharp-logarithmic-scalar-moment-boundary`, `MI-068-escaping-logarithmic-spectral-compactness-can-open-a-positive-rh-weil-floor`, `MI-069-dilute-carrier-floors-are-collective-and-sparse-defect-blind`, `MI-070-prime-side-square-root-scale-is-the-crossover-between-collective-reserve-and-defect-visibility`.

WI-369--WI-386 reduce the fixed-width source-zero family to a recurrent nonnegative spectral energy under RH and show that changing any one fixed profile cannot open a uniform positive Weil gap once exact source-slot perforation and the full explicit-formula ledger are retained.

WI-387 extends that obstruction to endpoint-dependent profiles whose zeta-frequency spectra are uniformly tight. WI-388 shows that a strict negative archimedean margin plus exact source slots does not imply that compactness: dilute high-frequency carrier mass can preserve the negative margin while a packet containing `Omega(log R)` zero ordinates retains order-one sampled energy.

WI-389 identifies the scalar-moment boundary. Fixed support gives a logarithmically weighted Fourier-tail criterion for zeta-spectral tightness. WI-390 supplies the source-transfer half at the same logarithmic scale: exact prime-power perforation changes both `L^2` norm and logarithmic difference-form energy by `o(1)` under uniform logarithmic integrability.

WI-391 proves that this compactness hypothesis is genuinely load-bearing. It constructs source-exact fixed-width trials with a dilute moving carrier and a uniform negative archimedean margin that, under RH, retain a positive Weil floor. WI-392 then identifies the reverse-direction limitation: each co-moving off-critical zero contributes only a small amount `q(A,R)`, so sparse packets can be invisible even while `Theta(log R)` critical-line samples collectively support the floor.

WI-393 sharpens both sides at once. A fixed Beurling--Selberg minorant inserted directly into the explicit formula extends the RH-side reserve to

`e^(A/2)/log R -> 0`,

equivalently up to `A=2 log log R-omega(1)`. The modulation exposes a shifted prime-power sum near `n~e^A` whose absolute size is `O(e^(A/2))`, while the local zero-density main term is order `log R`. The same ratio is the worst sparse-defect envelope from WI-392.

At the crossover `e^(A/2)~log R`, equivalently `A=2 log log R+O(1)`, absolute prime-side control becomes load-bearing exactly when the uniform sparse-defect envelope first becomes order one. The relevant short prime polynomial then lives around `n~(log R)^2`. For a fixed horizontal depth `|delta|<1/2`, the individual defect response becomes order one even later, at `A~(1/|delta|)log log R`.

The live fixed-width question is therefore no longer merely how to escape logarithmic compactness. That has been done. A useful converse/equivalence mechanism must cross the square-root-prime crossover with cancellation at defect-anchored ordinates, use a source-exact channel with stronger per-defect amplification, aggregate sparse off-line mass before dilution destroys it, or change category without reintroducing the compactness/no-gap obstruction. Simply increasing the existing carrier separation is not a bootstrap.

## Keep inherited core inertia, source restriction, sampling density, collective floor and sparse-defect sensitivity distinct

The fixed-width quasimodes are robust to profile retuning inside logarithmically compact families; WI-391 proves that controlled escape from that compactness can carry a phase-stable RH floor; WI-392 shows that the floor can remain sparse-defect blind; and WI-393 identifies the exact prime-side scale at which the two demands collide for the absolute-control route.

Future claims must therefore state which resource changes: continuous Fourier-tail compactness, zeta-sampled tightness, exact source-perforation stability, endpoint phase control, collective zero density, short-prime-polynomial cancellation, individual off-line response, width, locality, domain or operator category. A positive RH floor is not yet a coercive equivalence when the same arithmetic estimate that certifies the collective reserve fails before a sparse defect reaches order-one visibility.
