## Leave the scalar additive-cube hierarchy and retain source-specific signed covariance

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-036-exact-zero-density-locks-bow-reciprocal-scale-at-riemann-siegel-length` through `MI-045-unit-time-bandwidth-compresses-one-window-height-diversity`.

WI-331--WI-338 classify ordinary local Gowers smallness at the bow scale. Below the deterministic diagonal wall, matched-energy Rademacher sources can be small on every relevant interval while retaining full sliding variance; at the wall, the source's own energy prevents ordinary `U^s=o(1)`. There is therefore no remaining ordinary-Gowers order window in which generic smallness can force the distinguished WI-195 covariance saving.

WI-339 shows that deleting the literal zero-increment diagonal does not rescue the interface. The diagonal-deleted statistic is the centered Rademacher cube chaos, with `L^2` scale `K^(-(s+1)/2+o(1))`; all-start countermodels survive until the much higher transition `R_s=s+1`, apart from a bounded additive strip.

WI-340 closes the broader combinatorial-deletion repair. Repeated-vertex and other geometry-defined degeneracies form only a vanishing fraction of the cube family, and deleting any sparse deterministic set leaves the same renormalized chaos scale. Near the top transition, changing that floor by deletion alone would require retaining a superpolynomially tiny fraction of generic cubes.

WI-341 then classifies deterministic scalar reweighting. Every weighted injective-cube statistic factors through the Walsh character of its unordered vertex set. Positive or sign-coherent tapering cannot lower the normalized random floor; signed weights help only by cancelling duplicate copies of the **same** scalar monomial, a source-blind algebraic redundancy.

WI-342 closes the simplest mixed-order escape. Distinct injective cube orders live on disjoint Walsh levels and are exactly orthogonal in Rademacher `L^2`, so linear pooling cannot create destructive cross-order interference. The canonical multiplicative-derivative coupling of parallel faces is simply the next Gowers order. Ordinary scalar weighting, deletion and linear order mixing therefore remain inside the same quotient already defeated by the matched-energy control.

WI-343 closes a different multiplicity escape before arithmetic enters. On one natural Gallagher source window the bow-height span and logarithmic source aperture satisfy the exact unit time--bandwidth relation `H delta=1`. Consequently any two normalized carriers `n^(iU)` from the same bow have coherence at least `cos(1/2)`, and the whole height family lies within `1/(d+1)!` of a fixed `(d+1)`-dimensional subspace. In particular a power-growing number of bow ordinates does not give a power-growing supply of independent linear probes on one fixed source window.

The live theorem must therefore change the information carrier, not tune the scalar cube geometry or merely count more heights. Viable routes include a source- or carrier-dependent coefficient that retains the distinguished prime/twist structure, different source starts/windows, a wider logarithmic aperture with growing time--bandwidth product, a direct signed two-point covariance identity for `Lambda-Lambda^sharp`, or a genuinely nonlinear post-quotient observable whose Walsh/carrier structure is not merely the ordinary additive-cube hierarchy or one-window linear phase family in disguise. The accepted clue `CLUE-bow-distinguished-twist-offdiagonal-cancellation` remains the destination test: prove the needed negative off-diagonal contribution with `Lambda^sharp` retained in the same norm, or prove that the proposed statistic cannot force it.

## Keep diagonal removal, scalar quotienting, carrier dimension and covariance sign separate

Diagonal-free does not mean destination-sensitive. WI-339 removes the universal zero-increment floor but leaves generic chaos; WI-340 shows that broader sparse geometric deletion does not change that floor; WI-341 identifies the exact Walsh quotient seen by deterministic scalar weights; WI-342 shows that different ordinary orders do not cancel each other linearly; and WI-343 shows that spectral-height multiplicity inside one natural Gallagher window has bounded approximate carrier dimension even before the coefficient sequence is inspected.

A proposed bridge should therefore state the source-start quantifier, the exact monomial/character or carrier family, normalization, whether coefficients depend on the arithmetic source or carrier, the physical logarithmic aperture and resulting time--bandwidth product, which nonlinear operations create genuinely new support, and the exact off-diagonal covariance sign it forces. A lower generic scalar chaos norm or a larger count of highly coherent height samples is not enough unless the statistic preserves information that the matched control cannot reproduce.
