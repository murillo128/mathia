## If using Bombieri truncations, control coefficient cost and height tightness rather than raw span separation

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-008-inertia-counts-offline-pairs-but-not-their-distance`, `MI-014-source-coercivity-must-survive-confluence-and-drifting-bows`.

WI-231--WI-237 show that finite negative inertia detects off-line packets exactly but can lose coercivity as height grows. The unconditional simple critical-line exponentials are complete on every bounded positive-measure window and are not a Bessel family, so ordinary separation/Riesz arguments cannot exclude spectral collapse. The surviving Bombieri-side currency is coefficient price together with height tightness.

## Make the finite-radius signed source fail the sharp joint `a^-2` tariff

**Linked intuition:** `MI-016-finite-radius-source-localization-can-replace-zero-side-tightness`.

WI-238--WI-243 show why generic global signed-discrepancy control is not an intermediate theorem: subexponential growth of a natural diagnostic is already RH-equivalent.

WI-244--WI-245 produce an explicit finite-radius odd-sector archimedean reserve. WI-246 then optimizes the entire positive digamma-resolvent form jointly: incompatible Robin ground states make the exact finite-radius reserve strictly larger than the termwise sum, but both have the same leading constant times `a^-2`.

The live theorem is therefore source-side. Show that the actual signed von-Mangoldt discrepancy cannot pay the **joint finite-radius reserve** at the relevant first crossing, or find another finite-radius invariant that adds leading-order coercivity. Reoptimizing the same positive resolvents cannot improve the leading tariff.

## Treat global signed growth, finite-radius mean reserve, and arithmetic source charge as separate gates

Global subexponential control is already RH. Finite support creates a classical `a^-2` reserve with a now-calibrated optimal leading coefficient. Only the arithmetic charge remains capable of changing the leading-order balance.
