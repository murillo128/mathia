# Weil-inertia research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable Weil-inertia intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they separate genuinely different mechanisms.

## Keep optimization loss separate from information loss

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-global-fenchel-dual-separates-losses`.

Global Fenchel coupling can remove artificial blockwise optimization loss, but no optimizer can reconstruct a discriminator already erased by the represented Gram/pressure data. Continue to audit the exact represented object before strengthening inequalities on it.

## Treat screening as an information-bandwidth obstruction

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-screening-is-an-information-bandwidth-obstruction`.

When the represented test family cannot resolve the coordinates needed by an off-line witness, sharper optimization of the same screened object cannot recover them. A decisive advance must enlarge the source-faithful information channel or prove that the existing channel already determines the missing witness variable.

## Work inside the exact finite-window alias quotient, and measure leakage rather than counting rank

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-coupled-welding-uniformity-is-the-fourth-moment-gate` and `MI-004-w-conditioning-is-l2-compressible-but-l1-expensive`.

The universal signed scalar Ramanujan interface is now classified much more sharply. WI-082 shows that before overcompleteness a Vandermonde congruence fixes the positive and negative inertia exactly, so finite-window leakage cannot erase sign directions. WI-083 shows that after both sign dictionaries saturate, large-prime blocks collapse to two moments and admit exact nontrivial signed cancellation. WI-084 proves that subwindow blocks `B_1^(N),...,B_N^(N)` are an exact Toeplitz basis, and WI-085 completes the picture: **every** finitely supported arbitrary-modulus scalar law on an `N`-window factors through exactly `N` divisor/tail coordinates and has a unique subwindow alias.

Pairwise rank is now closed as a generic escape. WI-086--WI-088 show that the only residual defect occurs after the boundary exceeds both totient dimensions and is universally capped at one third of the smaller prime space; WI-087 attains that ceiling. WI-089--WI-091 show that the sharp scale is confined to one boundary quotient, exact ceiling pairs are precisely opposite residue classes modulo `3` and form a matching at fixed observation length, and the whole near-extremal region has an exact triangular rank profile.

Most importantly, WI-092--WI-093 show that this algebraic defect is metrically weak. The sharp triangular family has `tr(Pi_p Pi_q)<4` uniformly, and every positive-density residual rank defect has only `O(1)` total squared canonical overlap. Thus an extensive loss of cross-Gram rank does not provide an extensive family of strongly coupled directions. Pairwise rank saving alone cannot drive the signed Hilbert--Schmidt cancellation required downstream.

The surviving fourth-moment routes must use information that the alias quotient and pairwise metric controls do not erase. One route is a direct Yang-specific weighted incidence/rectangle estimate on the original non-Cartesian two-dimensional source. A second is to prove that the **actual source coefficients** occupy a restricted region of the `N`-coordinate alias quotient whose many-modulus interaction cancels despite weak pairwise overlaps. A third is the base-aggregated fixed-coefficient multivariate polynomial representation with anisotropic boxes and outer prime/Mertens weights. A genuinely non-scalar finite-window object formed before Ramanujan scalarization is also admissible if it is forced by the source.

A decisive advance is a source-specific theorem on one of those retained structures. Generic many-modulus coherence, positive support/energy pruning, complete-period scalarization, pairwise rank saving, free independent shifts, fixed-`L^p` ambient bounds, and Cartesian reboxing are no longer distinct escape routes.
