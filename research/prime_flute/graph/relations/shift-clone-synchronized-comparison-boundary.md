# Shift-clone comparison through compact relative resolvent

The accepted prime/shift-clone comparison has advanced from local closeness to a global essential-spectrum equivalence.

[[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-118-shift-clone-pants-are-arc-lipschitz-close|PF-118]] starts with two-sided local pant arc/Lipschitz closeness. [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-119-canonical-cusp-split-gluing-offset-has-summable-shift-clone-defect|PF-119]]--[[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-122-canonical-cusp-strip-gluing-cost-is-summable|PF-122]] remove the canonical gluing-offset and deep-cusp synchronization costs, and [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-124-lambert-cuff-trace-is-zero-twist-coherent|PF-124]] shows the explicit Lambert cuff traces are exactly compatible with zero-twist gluing.

[[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-123-asymptotic-metric-equivalence-forces-compact-relative-resolvent|PF-123]] identifies the classical operator bridge: asymptotic metric/volume equivalence is enough for compact relative resolvent. [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-125-shift-clone-has-compact-relative-resolvent|PF-125]] then supplies the missing global marked homeomorphism and proves that the exact prime flute and the all-composite shift clone have compact relative resolvent and the same essential Laplace spectrum.

The later findings move below that closed essential-spectrum gate:

- [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-126-shift-clone-metric-defect-is-Lp-above-one|PF-126]] gives global coefficient defect in every `L^r`, `r>1`, and weak `L^1`.
- [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-127-collapsing-canonical-collar-is-schatten-benign-above-trace-endpoint|PF-127]] shows the central collapsing-collar relative resolvent is locally `S_r` for every `r>1` but not `S_1`.
- [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-128-full-collar-wave-weight-is-collapse-benign|PF-128]] and [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-129-cusp-synchronization-has-summable-wave-weight|PF-129]] remove collapse and the cusp family as local inverse-volume scattering-weight obstructions.
- [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-130-lambert-shift-metric-defect-is-strong-L1-summable|PF-130]] gives a strong-`L^1` budget on the Lambert bodies themselves.

Thus essential-spectrum distinguishability by this explicit composite control is closed negatively. Global Schatten `S_r` for `r>1`, wave/scattering equivalence, interface terms, discrete spectrum and stronger relative invariants remain separate source-backed questions; first-resolvent trace class was already excluded by PF-112.
