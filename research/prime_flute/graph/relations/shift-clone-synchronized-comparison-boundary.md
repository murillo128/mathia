[[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-141-bounded-collar-phase-welding-is-wave-weight-collapse-suppressed|PF-141]] shows bounded angular phase is locally collapse-suppressed, and [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-142-zero-twist-reflection-removes-short-collar-phase-gauge|PF-142]] removes the constant rotation phase entirely under the canonical reflection marking.
