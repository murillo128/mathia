# Shift-clone comparison now has two stronger-than-compact wave routes

[[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-118-shift-clone-pants-are-arc-lipschitz-close|PF-118]]--[[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-125-shift-clone-has-compact-relative-resolvent|PF-125]] advance the explicit prime/shift-clone comparison from local pant closeness through boundary-coherent body/cusp gluing to compact relative resolvent and equality of essential Laplace spectrum. [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-126-shift-clone-metric-defect-is-Lp-above-one|PF-126]]--[[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-142-zero-twist-reflection-removes-short-collar-phase-gauge|PF-142]] then remove the canonical collar, cusp, Lambert-body, split-ray, scalar-tail, unclassified-short-core and constant-phase effects as independent local wave obstructions.

The remaining collar interface now has a sharp local norm. [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-143-nonconstant-collar-trace-is-not-collapse-suppressed|PF-143]] proves that a genuinely nonconstant angular boundary trace cannot be made cheap merely by pinching: its centered `L^1` amplitude gives a uniform lower bound on the natural inverse-unit-ball weighted welding cost. [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-144-reflection-odd-collar-trace-has-sharp-L1-angular-welding-cost|PF-144]] supplies the matching constructive upper bound for the canonically reflection-odd angular sector, and [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-145-reflection-even-collar-graph-has-sharp-L1-radial-welding-cost|PF-145]] gives the analogous sharp `L^1` currency for the radial shape mode together with an additive upper bound for simultaneous small radial-plus-angular traces. The local norm-selection problem is therefore solved; what remains is the actual trace sequence produced by one coherent global prime/shift body comparison and its summability.

A second operator-theoretic route is now source-backed. [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-146-squared-short-collar-relative-resolvent-is-trace-class|PF-146]] shows that although the first localized relative resolvent hits the two-dimensional trace-class endpoint obstruction, the **squared** relative resolvent on each fixed central matched short collar is trace class with a strongly pinching-suppressed norm. Classical invariance-principle scattering theory therefore makes a global trace-class squared-resolvent difference a sufficient alternative route to complete wave operators.

Essential-spectrum distinguishability remains closed negatively, but the territory is still open. The surviving gate is now global rather than another local collar estimate: either assemble the body/cusp/collar maps so that the actual angular and radial traces have summable `L^1` mass in the Güneysu--Thalmaier criterion, or prove trace class of the uncut global squared-resolvent difference under one coherent identification, including body, transmission, cutoff-commutator and infinite-tail terms. Neither route currently implies equality of scattering matrices, resonances, discrete spectrum, determinants, or any RH statement.
