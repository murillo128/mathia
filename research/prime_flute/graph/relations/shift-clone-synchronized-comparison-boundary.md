# Shift-clone comparison through the remaining global nonlocal-value gate

[[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-118-shift-clone-pants-are-arc-lipschitz-close|PF-118]]--[[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-146-squared-short-collar-relative-resolvent-is-trace-class|PF-146]] advance the exact prime/all-composite shift-clone comparison from local geometric closeness to compact relative resolvent, sharp local `L^1` interface currencies, and a fixed-collar squared-resolvent trace-class route. Essential-spectrum distinguishability and the obvious local collar/cusp/body obstructions are therefore already closed or localized to global assembly.

The newest findings classify what would and would not be gained by strengthening that assembly. [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-147-square-resolvent-S1-forces-first-resolvent-S2|PF-147]] and [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-150-square-resolvent-S1-is-sharp-at-S2-without-geometry|PF-150]] put the first relative resolvent at the abstractly sharp `S_2` boundary under the squared-resolvent `S_1` hypothesis. [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-148-square-resolvent-S1-recovers-krein-scattering-phase|PF-148]] shows that the same stronger hypothesis nevertheless carries one ordinary Krein/Birman--Krein phase by invariance, and [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-153-higher-even-resolvent-powers-share-one-scattering-phase|PF-153]] shows higher even powers do not create independent phases. [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-154-first-resolvent-det2-has-no-independent-zero-set|PF-154]] likewise rules out the natural first-resolvent `det_2` as a new zero divisor.

Local scalar regularization does not restore gap memory. [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-151-critical-first-resolvent-residue-is-pants-area-only|PF-151]] and [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-155-local-heat-invariant-tower-is-area-only|PF-155]] show that the critical residue and the complete local short-time scalar heat tower are area-only on curvature `-1` pants, while [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-152-critical-first-resolvent-finite-part-is-weight-dependent|PF-152]] shows residue subtraction alone does not canonize a finite part. [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-149-natural-volume-identifications-are-asymptotically-equivalent|PF-149]] removes the obvious choice of natural volume identification as an independent escape.

The territory therefore still has one source-backed bottleneck: prove an actual global body/collar comparison giving the required wave or squared-resolvent class, then prove a genuinely nonlocal **value theorem**—for the relative spectral-shift/scattering function, resonances, closed-geodesic data or another marked invariant—that separates the exact composite control. Merely changing the resolvent exponent, regularization or local heat coefficient is no longer a distinct route.
