[[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-033-no-absolute-or-standard-regularized-laplacian-determinant|PF-033]], [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-035-short-orbit-accumulation-kills-the-ordinary-selberg-ruelle-euler-product|PF-035]], [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-036-iterated-short-orbits-make-the-selberg-orbital-measure-infinite-on-every-positive-window|PF-036]], [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-069-prime-vector-limit-points-force-positive-interval-of-primitive-length-accumulation|PF-069]] and the related determinant/transfer findings establish that the standard absolute finite-type Selberg/Ruelle/Fredholm framework is structurally mismatched, not merely missing a better convergence estimate.

## Relative-sector boundary

Earlier selected relative sectors produced quarter-plane thresholds, while [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-088-quarter-threshold-is-one-dimensional-not-prime-specific|PF-088]] and [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-103-full-relative-ruelle-completion-restores-universal-cusp-half-threshold|PF-103]] showed that those exponents are universal propagation/parabolic effects rather than prime-specific evidence.

The exact shift-clone subtraction now resolves part of the absolute short-orbit pathology without reviving a zero divisor. [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-156-matched-short-orbit-packets-cancel-relative-trace-divergence|PF-156]] proves test-function-level absolute summability of the matched short-orbit packet difference. [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-157-matched-source-short-selberg-cocycle-is-zero-free|PF-157]] integrates the corresponding logarithmic derivative to a zero-free factor on `Re s>0`, and [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-158-full-canonical-separator-selberg-cocycle-has-sharp-one-quarter-abscissa|PF-158]] extends zero-freeness to the complete canonical-separator family on `Re s>1/4`, with the critical line strictly inside the proven region.

Thus the relative subtraction is analytically meaningful but does not make the controlled short/canonical orbit sectors into an RH divisor. A future full relative zeta/scattering object must obtain any nontrivial zero set from primitive classes or spectral sectors outside these explicit families and must still justify its global continuation/operator realization.
