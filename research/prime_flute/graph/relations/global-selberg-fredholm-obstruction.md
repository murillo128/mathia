## Relative-sector boundary

Earlier selected relative sectors produced quarter-plane thresholds, while [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-088-quarter-threshold-is-one-dimensional-not-prime-specific|PF-088]] and [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-103-full-relative-ruelle-completion-restores-universal-cusp-half-threshold|PF-103]] showed that those exponents are universal propagation/parabolic effects rather than prime-specific evidence.

The exact shift-clone subtraction resolves part of the absolute short-orbit pathology without reviving a zero divisor. [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-156-matched-short-orbit-packets-cancel-relative-trace-divergence|PF-156]] proves test-function-level absolute summability of the matched short-orbit packet difference. [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-157-matched-source-short-selberg-cocycle-is-zero-free|PF-157]] integrates the corresponding logarithmic derivative to a zero-free factor on `Re s>0`, and [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-158-full-canonical-separator-selberg-cocycle-has-sharp-one-quarter-abscissa|PF-158]] extends zero-freeness to the complete canonical-separator family on `Re s>1/4`, with the critical line strictly inside the proven region.

The newer boundary analysis identifies why those abscissae occur. [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-159-canonical-selberg-quarter-boundary-is-one-ended-propagation|PF-159]] subtracts the exact one-ended far-separator response and pushes the connected canonical derivative to `Re s>0`; the removed term alone carries the sharp one-quarter boundary. [[research/prime_flute/findings/PF-160-connected-selberg-zero-boundary-is-bottom-ruelle-layer|PF-160]] then shows that the remaining `Re s=0` natural boundary comes from the removable finite part of the standard bottom `m=0` Selberg/Ruelle layer combined with reciprocal-prime divergence, while all higher layers are already holomorphic across zero.

Thus the currently controlled convergence boundaries themselves factor through universal propagation/local-factor mechanisms rather than a hidden arithmetic divisor. A future full relative zeta/scattering object must obtain any nontrivial zero set from primitive classes or spectral sectors outside these explicit families and must still justify its global continuation/operator realization.
