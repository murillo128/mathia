# PF-291 — fixed-window physical high-pass annihilates compact response profiles

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + FOURIER-DENSITY + NEGATIVE/ROUTE-NARROWING`.

PF-290 rewrites the optimized PF-216/PF-227 local low/high correlation as a high-band shorting deficit. A tempting next mechanism comes from the exact thin-pant geometry: PF-215 gives a fixed-window transverse width proportional to `s_n cosh t`, while PF-231 shows in logarithmic coordinates that the PF-205 hypercycle trim replaces the constant conformal width by an explicit smooth variable width. One might therefore hope that the resulting reciprocal-width profile — `sech t` in the untrimmed first approximation — has enough nonconstant Fourier content to couple the PF-216 constant low mode to PF-227's physical-high witness band with a fixed normalized angle.

That fixed-profile mechanism is false for a structural reason that is easy to miss. PF-227 keeps a **fixed physical frequency cutoff** while the distinguished cuff length `L_n` tends to infinity. Therefore the global low Fourier grid

\[
\Xi_n=\left\{\frac{2\pi k}{L_n}:\left|\frac{2\pi k}{L_n}\right|\le\kappa\right\}
\]

becomes dense in the whole interval `[-\kappa,\kappa]`. On any fixed pant window `I=(-T,T)`, the restrictions of exponentials with frequencies in any nonempty interval are complete in `L^2(I)`. Consequently the exact PF-227 condition of being orthogonal to every frequency in `\Xi_n` forces the admissible fixed-window high profiles to become weakly invisible to **every precompact family of response profiles**.

In particular, the exact PF-231 reciprocal-width profiles of the trimmed corridor form such a precompact family on every fixed window. Hence the scalar variable-width conductance profile by itself cannot produce a positive tail lower bound for PF-290's local shorting deficit. A persistent local heavy angle, if it exists, must come from structure whose normalized response profile **escapes fixed-window `L^2` compactness** — for example increasing tangential oscillation, a growing-window/end effect, the full two-dimensional DtN response, physical normalization/projection mismatch, or more global Schur coupling.

This finding does **not** prove that the actual PF-290 deficits tend to zero. It removes the simplest local mechanism and turns the next test into a precise compactness-versus-frequency-escape question for the genuine mixed DtN response.

## Claim

Fix `T>0` and `\kappa>0`, and put

\[
I=(-T,T).
\]

Let `L_j\to\infty`, and define the finite low-frequency grid

\[
\Xi_j
:=
\left\{\frac{2\pi k}{L_j}:k\in\mathbb Z,
\ \left|\frac{2\pi k}{L_j}\right|\le\kappa\right\}.
\tag{1}
\]

Let

\[
E_j
:=\operatorname{span}\{e^{i\xi t}|_I:\xi\in\Xi_j\}
\subset L^2(I),
\qquad
V_j:=E_j^\perp.
\tag{2}
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{P_{V_j}\longrightarrow0\quad\text{strongly on }L^2(I).}
\tag{3}
\]

Equivalently, for every `g\in L^2(I)`,

\[
\boxed{
\sup_{0\ne\phi\in V_j}
\frac{|\langle g,\phi\rangle|}{\|\phi\|_2}
=\|P_{V_j}g\|_2
\longrightarrow0.
}
\tag{4}
\]

The convergence is uniform on compact subsets of `L^2(I)`: if `\mathcal C\subset L^2(I)` is compact, then

\[
\boxed{
\sup_{g\in\mathcal C}\|P_{V_j}g\|_2\longrightarrow0.
}
\tag{5}
\]

### PF-227 specialization

On the fixed PF-226/PF-227 corridor window, let `\Phi_n(\phi)` denote the two-sided physical boundary datum generated by `\phi\in S_n\subset H_0^1(I)`, and let

\[
f_n(\phi)=\Lambda_{Q,n}^{1/2}\Phi_n(\phi)
\]

be its normalized seam-boundary vector. Because the PF-205 strip metric is tangentially translation invariant, `\Lambda_{Q,n}^{1/2}` preserves every physical Fourier block and is strictly positive on the symmetric two-sided profile. Therefore

\[
P_{\kappa,n}f_n(\phi)=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\widehat\phi\!\left(\frac{2\pi k}{L_n}\right)=0
\quad
\text{for every }\left|\frac{2\pi k}{L_n}\right|\le\kappa.
\tag{6}
\]

Thus the source profiles used by PF-227's doubly-high witness space lie in `V_n`. The target high constraint only shrinks the admissible space further. Consequently, for every precompact family `\mathcal C` of fixed-window source response profiles,

\[
\boxed{
\sup_{g\in\mathcal C}
\sup_{\substack{0\ne\phi\in S_n\\
P_{\kappa,n}f_n(\phi)=0}}
\frac{|\langle g,\phi\rangle|}{\|\phi\|_2}
\longrightarrow0.
}
\tag{7}
\]

The same statement holds for source/target pairs in the direct sum of the two neighboring fixed windows. In particular it applies before the PF-290 coercive spectral cut `W_n^\sharp`; restricting from the whole PF-227 high witness span to `W_n^\sharp` cannot increase the optimized pairing.

## 1. Dense low-frequency restrictions on a finite window

Let

\[
\mathcal E_\kappa
:=
\operatorname{span}\{e^{i\xi t}|_I:|\xi|\le\kappa\}.
\tag{8}
\]

The closure of `\mathcal E_\kappa` is all of `L^2(I)`. Indeed, if `h\in L^2(I)` is orthogonal to every such exponential, then

\[
F(z)=\int_I h(t)e^{-izt}\,dt
\tag{9}
\]

extends to an entire function of `z`, because `I` is bounded. Orthogonality says that `F` vanishes on the real interval `[-\kappa,\kappa]`. The identity theorem gives `F\equiv0`, and Fourier uniqueness gives `h=0`.

Now fix `g\in L^2(I)` and `\varepsilon>0`. By density, choose a finite sum

\[
v(t)=\sum_{r=1}^m a_r e^{i\xi_rt},
\qquad |\xi_r|\le\kappa,
\tag{10}
\]

with `\|g-v\|_2<\varepsilon`. For each `r`, choose a grid point `\xi_{r,j}\in\Xi_j` with

\[
|\xi_{r,j}-\xi_r|\le\frac{\pi}{L_j}
\tag{11}
\]

for all sufficiently large `j` (at the endpoints use an inward grid point). On the fixed interval `I`,

\[
\|e^{i\xi_rt}-e^{i\xi_{r,j}t}\|_2
\le C_T|\xi_r-\xi_{r,j}|
=O(L_j^{-1}).
\tag{12}
\]

Hence the corresponding grid sum `v_j\in E_j` converges to `v` in `L^2(I)`. Therefore

\[
\operatorname{dist}(g,E_j)
\le\|g-v\|_2+\|v-v_j\|_2
<2\varepsilon
\tag{13}
\]

for large `j`. Since `\operatorname{dist}(g,E_j)=\|P_{V_j}g\|_2`, this proves (3)--(4).

For a compact set `\mathcal C`, take a finite `\varepsilon`-net and apply the same argument to each net point. Orthogonal projections have norm one, so (5) follows.

Two points are important. First, no quantitative rate is claimed: approximating an arbitrary fixed-window profile by a bounded physical-frequency band can be extremely ill-conditioned. Second, there is no contradiction with uncertainty principles. The approximating band-limited functions are controlled only **inside `I`** and may become very large outside it; this is exactly the classical time-band concentration setting.

## 2. PF-227's normalized high condition really imposes the grid orthogonality

PF-212 proves that the exact PF-205 seam metric

\[
g=\frac{dx^2}{1+x^2}+(1+x^2)dt^2
\]

is independent of the tangential coordinate. The two-sided strip DtN form, its positive square root, and the physical low/high projectors are therefore block diagonal in the Fourier frequencies `2\pi k/L_n`.

PF-226 uses the same profile `\phi` on both sides of a seam module. On each Fourier block this is the symmetric boundary direction. The shifted strip DtN form is strictly positive, so its square root multiplies that symmetric direction by a nonzero scalar. It can change the weight of a Fourier coefficient but cannot create, destroy, or move its frequency. This proves (6).

PF-227 additionally requires the mapped target profile to be high. That second condition is useful for the high--high crossing theorem, but it is not needed for the annihilation statement: the source condition already places every admissible source profile in `V_n`. Imposing the target condition or PF-290's later coercive spectral cut only takes a subspace of an already annihilated family.

## 3. The exact PF-231 reciprocal-width family is compact on fixed windows

PF-231 places the complete corridor between neighboring axes in logarithmic coordinates `(x,\theta)` and gives the exact PF-205 trimmed width

\[
a_n(\theta)
=s_n-f_{\rho_n}(\theta)-f_{\rho_{n+1}}(\theta),
\qquad
f_\rho(\theta)=\operatorname{arsinh}(\sinh\rho\,\sin\theta).
\tag{14}
\]

The PF-205 scale satisfies, after a finite head,

\[
0\le \rho_n+\rho_{n+1}\le\eta s_n
\quad\text{for one fixed }\eta<1,
\tag{15}
\]

and PF-231 proves

\[
(1-\eta)s_n\le a_n(\theta)\le s_n,
\qquad
|f_\rho'(\theta)|\le\sinh\rho.
\tag{16}
\]

Along the inner axis, Fermi arclength `t` and angle `\theta` satisfy

\[
\sin\theta(t)=\operatorname{sech}t,
\qquad
d\theta=\operatorname{sech}t\,dt
\tag{17}
\]

up to the harmless orientation choice. The scalar reciprocal-width conductance profile obtained by integrating a purely transverse logarithmic-coordinate interpolation is therefore, after removing its common `s_n^{-1}` scale,

\[
\boxed{
 g_n^{\rm width}(t)
 :=
 \frac{s_n\operatorname{sech}t}
 {a_n(\theta(t))}.
}
\tag{18}
\]

On every fixed `I=(-T,T)`, equations (15)--(16) and `\rho_j=O(s_n)` give uniform upper/lower bounds and a uniform Lipschitz bound for `g_n^{\rm width}`. Thus

\[
\{g_n^{\rm width}|_I:n\gg1\}
\]

is precompact in `C(I)` and hence in `L^2(I)` by Arzela--Ascoli. In the untrimmed case it is exactly

\[
g^{\rm width}(t)=\operatorname{sech}t.
\tag{19}
\]

Combining this precompactness with (7) yields

\[
\boxed{
\sup_{\substack{0\ne\phi\in S_n\\
P_{\kappa,n}f_n(\phi)=0}}
\frac{
\left|\int_I g_n^{\rm width}(t)\overline{\phi(t)}\,dt\right|}
{\|\phi\|_2}
\longrightarrow0.
}
\tag{20}
\]

Therefore the attractive heuristic

\[
\text{variable reciprocal width}
\;\Longrightarrow\;
\text{persistent constant-to-high coupling}
\tag{21}
\]

is false on PF-227's fixed window when the coupling is represented only by the scalar width profile. The profile is nonconstant and has ordinary high-frequency Fourier content, but PF-227 does not merely remove finitely many fixed Fourier coefficients. As `L_n\to\infty`, it removes an increasingly dense grid filling an entire physical band, and those restrictions can approximate the fixed profile arbitrarily well on `I`.

## 4. Consequence for the PF-290 shorting deficit

PF-290 shows that the optimized local correlation is

\[
\delta_{n,r}
=
\sup_{0\ne h\in W_n^\sharp}
\frac{|k[\ell_r,h]|^2}
{k[\ell_r]k[h]}.
\tag{22}
\]

PF-291 does not identify the full functional `h\mapsto k[\ell_r,h]` with (18), and therefore it does not conclude `\delta_{n,r}\to0`. Instead it gives a necessary diagnostic for any local proof of a positive limsup.

Parameterize the underlying PF-227 high witness span by its source/target fixed-window profiles and write the normalized mixed functional, after removing the known scalar energy scales, by its Riesz representative `G_{n,r}` in the corresponding direct-sum `L^2` profile space. If

\[
\{G_{n,r}:n\gg1,\ r\in\{n,n+1\}\}
\tag{23}
\]

is precompact, then (5)--(7) force the optimized pairing over the full PF-227 high witness span to tend to zero; a fortiori the PF-290 `W_n^\sharp` deficit tends to zero. Thus a positive PF-290 limsup requires failure of that compact-profile hypothesis.

This changes the immediate question. It is no longer enough to find a fixed nonconstant geometric weight such as `sech t`. One must determine whether the **actual energy-normalized mixed DtN response** develops non-precompact tangential structure on the tail. Plausible escape mechanisms include:

- frequencies increasing with `n` in the normalized response;
- response mass migrating from every fixed central window toward the growing corridor ends/cusp completion;
- a normalization or physical-projector effect not reducible to multiplication by a fixed smooth profile;
- multi-module/global Schur geometry absent from the local fixed-window model.

If none occurs and the actual normalized response family is precompact, the cheapest PF-289/PF-290 local noncompactness falsifier is closed. The investigation would then return to PF-287's full heavy-range essential-orthogonality problem rather than trying more fixed local weights.

The entire-function uniqueness argument used in (8)--(13) is the elementary Paley--Wiener/identity-theorem observation behind the completeness statement. No novelty is claimed for time-band concentration, completeness, Fourier uniqueness, or compact-set uniformity of strongly convergent contractions.

The project-specific deduction is the combination of that classical fact with PF-212/PF-227's exact physical-frequency projector and PF-231's exact trimmed-corridor width: **because the physical cutoff is fixed while `L_n\to\infty`, a fixed-window PF-227 high witness is asymptotically orthogonal to every compact family of normalized response profiles, including the exact scalar reciprocal-width family.** This removes a concrete candidate mechanism for a positive PF-290 local heavy angle.

A targeted literature check found the expected Slepian--Pollak/Landau--Pollak time-band concentration theory and no need for a stronger imported theorem: the exact grid-to-continuum density statement above is proved directly. Search absence is not used as a novelty claim.

## Falsification and boundary checks

1. `\kappa>0` is essential. If only the single mean mode were removed, the restricted low-frequency spans would not become dense on `I`.
2. The window `I` is fixed. A window whose length grows with `n` changes the time-bandwidth problem and is not covered by (3)--(7).
3. No convergence rate is claimed. The required low-band approximants may be violently ill-conditioned outside `I`; the theorem is qualitative.
4. The PF-227 source high condition is enough for (7). The target condition and the `W_n^\sharp` spectral cut can only shrink the admissible space.
5. Equation (20) concerns the scalar reciprocal-width profile. It is **not** an asymptotic theorem identifying the full pant DtN response with that profile.
6. Precompactness of the actual normalized response family in (23) is not proved here. Failure of precompactness is now a live geometric/operator question, not an established fact.
7. PF-217's unrestricted complementary-mode screening is distinct: PF-291 concerns exact physical high-pass constraints on the PF-227 fixed-window witness space.
8. PF-227's large raw high--high channel multiplicity is unchanged. Large dimension does not prevent all vectors in that space from becoming weakly orthogonal to a fixed compact family of low-response profiles.
9. No compactness of `T`, weak trace class, wave-operator result, determinant/resonance statement, zeta-zero correspondence, critical-line statement, or RH consequence is established.
