# PF-043 — prime-derived tangent spectrum has infinitely many positive essential points accumulating at zero

**Status:** `POSITIVE / LITERATURE-BACKED + DERIVED`.

PF-034 showed that every small Laplace eigenvalue of a recurrent isolated prime-pattern tangent `Y_H` is implanted into the essential spectrum of the fixed infinite prime-flute.  The new observation here is that one can force the cusp number of these tangents to infinity, and a deterministic theorem of Zograf then forces their first positive eigenvalues to zero.  Consequently `0` is not merely the bottom of the essential spectrum (PF-021): it is an accumulation point of **distinct positive** essential spectral values generated by prime-derived finite tangents.

This does **not** yet distinguish fine gap moduli; the bound used below is topological in the number of cusps.  It is nevertheless a stronger, unconditional spectral consequence of the prime geometry than PF-021/PF-034 separately.

## 1. Input from PF-034

For every prescribed cluster size `k0`, Pintz's isolated-cluster theorem together with the finite-pattern pigeonhole argument used in PF-034 gives an exact offset pattern

```text
H = {eta_1 < ... < eta_r},    r >= k0,
```

which occurs infinitely often as a bounded cluster of consecutive primes, with both exterior prime gaps tending to infinity.

The exact PF-004 separating-word geometry then gives an actual simple boundary geodesic `beta_j` around each occurrence with

```text
length(beta_j) -> 0.
```

PF-029/PF-034 identify the pointed limit of the enclosed finite subsurfaces as

```text
Y_H = double of an ideal (r+1)-gon,
```

a complete finite-area hyperbolic sphere with

```text
g(Y_H)=0,
number_of_cusps(Y_H)=r+1.
```

PF-034 proves the transplantation inclusion

```text
Spec_L2(Delta_{Y_H}) cap (0,1/4)
    subset sigma_ess(Delta_Xprime).
```

The proof uses the diverging collar of the actual separating geodesic, so this is a genuine pointed limit of the full prime-flute, not the invalid positive-generator limit of PF-025.

## 2. Zograf forces the first positive tangent eigenvalue to zero as the cluster size grows

Zograf proved a universal constant `C>0` such that for every complete finite-area hyperbolic surface `Y` of genus `g` with `n` cusps,

```text
lambda_1(Y) <= C (g+1)/n,
```

where `lambda_1` denotes the infimum of the non-zero spectrum.

For the prime tangents `Y_H`, `g=0` and `n=r+1`, hence

```text
boxed:
lambda_1(Y_H) <= C/(r+1).
```

For `r` sufficiently large the right-hand side is below `1/4`.  Since the continuous spectrum of a finite-area cusped hyperbolic surface starts at `1/4`, `lambda_1(Y_H)` is then a genuine positive discrete `L2` eigenvalue:

```text
0 < lambda_1(Y_H) < 1/4.
```

PF-034 therefore gives

```text
boxed:
lambda_1(Y_H) in sigma_ess(Delta_Xprime).
```

## 3. Infinitely many distinct positive essential spectral points accumulate at zero

Pintz allows the prescribed cluster size `k0` to be arbitrarily large.  Choose a sequence of recurrent exact patterns `H_j` with

```text
r_j := |H_j| -> infinity.
```

Set

```text
lambda_j := lambda_1(Y_{H_j}).
```

Then

```text
0 < lambda_j <= C/(r_j+1) -> 0,
```

and every `lambda_j` lies in the essential spectrum of the **same** prime-flute:

```text
boxed:
lambda_j in sigma_ess(Delta_Xprime) cap (0,1/4).
```

The set of values must be infinite: if it were finite, its positive minimum would contradict `lambda_j -> 0`.  Passing to a subsequence if desired gives strictly decreasing distinct values.

Hence

```text
boxed:
0 is an accumulation point of
sigma_ess(Delta_Xprime) cap (0,1/4).
```

This is strictly stronger than PF-021.  The statement

```text
inf sigma_ess(Delta_Xprime)=0
```

alone was compatible with `0` being an isolated essential point followed by a gap `(0,epsilon)`.  PF-043 rules out that possibility.

It is also stronger than the existence statement in PF-034, which produced at least one positive essential point below `1/4`.

## 4. Scattering interpretation on the finite tangents

A theorem of Huxley/Otal says that finite-area hyperbolic surfaces of genus `0` (and also genus `1`) carry no small cuspidal eigenpairs.  Thus every eigenvalue of `Y_H` in `(0,1/4)` is **residual**, i.e. arises from a pole of the finite-dimensional Eisenstein/scattering matrix.

Write

```text
lambda_j = s_j(1-s_j),
1/2 < s_j < 1.
```

Then `s_j` is a scattering pole of the finite tangent `Y_{H_j}`.  Since `lambda_j -> 0`,

```text
boxed:
s_j -> 1.
```

More quantitatively, for large `j`,

```text
1-s_j = lambda_j/s_j <= 2 lambda_j <= 2C/(r_j+1).
```

Thus arbitrarily large recurrent isolated prime clusters force a sequence of **tangent scattering poles approaching `s=1`**, while the corresponding spectral values are implanted into the global essential spectrum of the prime-flute.

Important limitation: this does **not** prove that `s_j` are poles of a global scattering matrix for the infinitely-cusped prime-flute.  PF-019 and later findings show that such a global standard scattering determinant is problematic.  The rigorous global statement is the essential-spectrum implantation of `lambda_j`; the scattering-pole statement belongs to the finite pointed tangents.

## 5. Relation to distinguished cuffs

This result reinforces the separation already seen in PF-028/PF-032/PF-037:

```text
individual distinguished cuffs ell_n
    -> local/collar spectral data are universal,
```

whereas

```text
multi-gap isolated blocks
    -> actual separating necks
    -> finite pointed tangents
    -> nontrivial sub-1/4 essential spectrum.
```

Inside a bounded prime cluster the distinguished positive-side cuffs still satisfy `ell_n -> infinity`; they are not the small necks responsible for the tangent.  The small separating neck is a multi-gap cross-ratio word.  Therefore PF-043 is not a new scalar formula `lambda=f(ell_n)`.  It is evidence that the genuinely prime-sensitive spectral channel is **relational geometry among several cuffs/cusps**, not a single cuff length.

Targeted searches for combinations of `prime gaps`, `isolated prime clusters`, `punctured sphere`, `Zograf first eigenvalue`, `essential spectrum`, and `hyperbolic flute` did not locate the composed statement above.  No novelty is claimed for any individual theorem.  The candidate new theorem is the specialization to the fixed prime-flute:

```text
arbitrarily large recurrent isolated prime patterns
 -> finite genus-zero pointed tangents Y_H
 -> lambda_1(Y_H) -> 0

    sigma_ess(Delta_Xprime) accumulating at 0.
```

Known constructions of infinite-type hyperbolic surfaces can have bottom essential spectrum zero or infinitely many small spectral phenomena; that general phenomenon is not new.  What appears specific here is that the **arithmetic prime-cluster theorem forces it on this exact deterministic prime-derived surface**.

## 7. Research gate

PF-043 is still partly topological: Zograf's upper bound uses only the cusp count, so it does not prove that exact internal prime-gap ratios determine the locations of the `lambda_j`.

The next genuinely fine-arithmetic gate remains:

```text
find recurrent H_1,H_2 with the same cardinality
and prove that their tangent scattering poles / small eigenvalues differ.
```

A second useful target is to determine whether the union of spectra of recurrent tangents is merely a countable set accumulating at zero or forces intervals/bands in `sigma_ess cap (0,1/4)`.  No such interval claim is made here.
