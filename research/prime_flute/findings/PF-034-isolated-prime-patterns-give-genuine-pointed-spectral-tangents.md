# PF-034 — recurring isolated prime patterns give genuine pointed spectral tangents

**Status:** `POSITIVE / LITERATURE-BACKED + EXACT-DERIVED`.

This closes the main geometric-limit gate left open by PF-029 and restores a corrected form of the spectral-implantation idea that PF-028 invalidated on the positive-endpoint side.

The key observation is that one does **not** need to prove Chabauty convergence of the entire conjugated infinite group.  An isolated prime block is enclosed by an actual simple separating geodesic whose length tends to zero.  The finite component on the block side has a finite generated Fuchsian representation whose cusp-side holonomies converge exactly to the ideal-polygon group of PF-029; the collar of the shrinking boundary then pushes the rest of the infinite flute to infinite pointed distance.  This gives pointed geometric convergence of the **full surface** to the finite tangent `Y_H`.

## 1. The PF-004 word is exactly the boundary of a consecutive cusp block

Use the symmetric ideal-polygon model of a zero-twist tight flute.  Let `g_i` denote the side-pairing generator for the consecutive positive/negative side pair indexed by `i`.  The cusp between two adjacent side pairs has the peripheral parabolic

```text
q_i = g_{i-1} g_i^(-1).
```

For consecutive cusps `i=1,...,r`, the peripheral product telescopes:

```text
q_1 q_2 ... q_r = g_0 g_r^(-1).
```

Topologically the prime-flute has genus zero, so the product of the peripheral loops of a finite consecutive cusp block is the boundary class of a regular neighborhood of exactly those cusps.  Hence, whenever `g_0 g_r^(-1)` is hyperbolic, its geodesic representative `beta` is a **simple primitive separating closed geodesic**.  Cutting along `beta` produces a finite component

```text
Omega = sphere with r cusps and one geodesic boundary beta.
```

This closes the topological audit that remained implicit in PF-005/PF-007: the PF-004 relative word is not merely a hyperbolic matrix with a formal translation length; it is the actual separating boundary geodesic of the corresponding consecutive prime block.

## 2. Exact neck length for an isolated block

Let the two side intervals adjacent to the block have ordered positive endpoints

```text
a < b < c < d,
```

where `[a,b]` is the left exterior side, `[c,d]` the right exterior side, and the cusps of the selected block lie between them.  Put

```text
X = b-a,
Y = c-b,
Z = d-c.
```

PF-004 gives exactly

```text
sinh(beta_length/4)^2
  = Y (X+Y+Z)/(X Z).
```

For a bounded prime cluster with bounded internal span `Y` and exterior prime gaps tending to infinity, `X,Z -> infinity`, so

```text
beta_length -> 0.
```

Thus every Pintz isolated cluster really is an embedded finite hyperbolic piece separated from the rest of the prime-flute by a pinching geodesic.

## 3. A fixed isolated offset pattern recurs infinitely often

Pintz, *On the ratio of consecutive gaps between primes* (arXiv:1406.2658), Theorem 2 and the construction in Section 4, proves the following form sufficient here:

- for every prescribed `k0`, there are arbitrarily large intervals of **bounded length depending only on `k0`** containing at least `k0` consecutive primes;
- the bounded cluster is flanked on both sides by prime-free intervals whose lengths tend to infinity on the Erdos-Rankin scale.

For fixed `k0`, only finitely many exact subsets of integer offsets can occur inside that bounded interval.  Passing to a subsequence therefore gives one exact pattern

```text
H = {eta_1 < ... < eta_r},   r >= k0,
```

that occurs infinitely often as

```text
P_j + H,
```

with both exterior prime gaps tending to infinity.

This fixes the finite tangent geometry rather than merely fixing the number of primes in the block.

## 4. The finite block converges to the PF-029 punctured sphere

Scale exact prime endpoints by

```text
v_p = pi cot(pi/p)
     = p - pi^2/(3p) + O(p^-3),
```

and use the cusp-side re-marking

```text
C_P(z) = pi z + P.
```

For an occurrence `P_j+H`, let `q_{j,i}` be the `r` peripheral parabolics of `Omega_j`.  PF-029 gives exact finite matrix limits

```text
C_{P_j} q_{j,i} C_{P_j}^(-1) -> Q_i,
```

where the `Q_i` are the standard parabolics of the ideal polygon with vertices

```text
{-eta_1,...,-eta_r,infinity}.
```

For internal cusps,

```text
Q_i = Q(c_i,D_i),
c_i = -eta_i,
D_i = 2(1/d_{i-1}+1/d_i),
d_i = eta_{i+1}-eta_i,
```

and the two end formulas are

```text
D_1 = 2/d_1,
D_r = 2/d_{r-1}.
```

Their product is the limiting peripheral parabolic at infinity:

```text
Q_1 ... Q_r
 = [[1, 2(eta_r-eta_1)],
    [0, 1]].
```

Thus the marked finite groups

```text
rho_j : F_r -> PSL(2,R),
```

generated by the `q_{j,i}` converge algebraically to the discrete faithful finite-area group `Gamma_H` uniformizing

```text
Y_H = double of the ideal (r+1)-gon
      with vertices {-eta_1,...,-eta_r,infinity}.
```

The boundary word

```text
q_{j,1}...q_{j,r}
```

is hyperbolic for finite `j` and becomes the parabolic cusp at infinity in the limit.  This is precisely a standard one-boundary pinching degeneration in the augmented Teichmuller space.  Standard convergence criteria for augmented Teichmuller space (equivalently, convergence of the hyperbolic metrics on compacta away from the pinched boundary) therefore give

```text
Omega_j -> Y_H
```

smoothly on compact subsets after the cusp-side markings.

This argument uses only the **finite block subgroup**.  It is not vulnerable to the long-word Chabauty issue raised in PF-029.

## 5. The collar lemma upgrades finite-block convergence to a pointed limit of the full prime-flute

Let

```text
L_j = length(beta_j) -> 0.
```

The standard embedded collar around `beta_j` has half-width

```text
w(L_j)
 = asinh(1/sinh(L_j/2))
 -> infinity.
```

On the `Omega_j` side, the pinching limit replaces `beta_j` by the cusp at infinity of `Y_H`.  Choose a point `y` in a fixed compact core of `Y_H` and corresponding points `x_j in Omega_j` under the compact convergence above.

Then

```text
dist_Xprime(x_j, beta_j) -> infinity.
```

Every path from `x_j` to the complement of `Omega_j` must cross `beta_j`.  Hence, for every fixed radius `R`,

```text
B_Xprime(x_j,R) subset Omega_j
```

for all sufficiently large `j`.  It follows that

```text
boxed:
(X_prime,x_j) -> (Y_H,y)
```

in pointed smooth/geometric topology.

This is the missing geometric statement from PF-029.  No global Chabauty classification of all long words is required: the shrinking **actual separating boundary** and its diverging collar isolate the finite tangent from the rest of the surface.

This also explains why PF-028 remains correct.  The distinguished positive-side cuffs inside the same block still have lengths tending to infinity.  The tangent is obtained from the finite **cusp-side relative geometry enclosed by `beta_j`**, not from convergence of those absolute cuffs.

## 6. Spectral transplantation is therefore rigorous

Let

```text
lambda in Spec_L2(Delta_{Y_H}),
0 < lambda < 1/4.
```

Take an `L2` eigenfunction `phi` on `Y_H`.  Since `Y_H` is complete finite area, choose compactly supported smooth truncations `phi_m` satisfying

```text
||phi_m||_2 -> 1,
||(Delta_{Y_H}-lambda) phi_m||_2 -> 0.
```

For each `m`, choose a sufficiently far occurrence `j_m` so that the compact support of `phi_m` lies in the region where `Omega_{j_m}` is `C^infinity`-close to `Y_H`, and transplant `phi_m` there.  Call the normalized transplanted function `u_m`.

The occurrences may be chosen pairwise disjoint and escaping every compact subset of `X_prime`.  Then

```text
u_m weakly -> 0,
||(Delta_Xprime-lambda) u_m||_2 -> 0.
```

The Weyl criterion gives the exact inclusion

```text
boxed:
Spec_L2(Delta_{Y_H}) cap (0,1/4)
    subset sigma_ess(Delta_Xprime).
```

So a recurring finite **multi-gap pattern** supplies a genuine finite-area spectral tangent, and every one of its small `L2` eigenvalues is implanted into the essential spectrum of the fixed infinite prime-flute.

This is not a scalar rewriting of the gaps.  `Y_H` is a standard finite-type hyperbolic surface whose moduli are determined by the gap ratios/cross-ratios.  For example, with three prime offsets and internal gaps `d_1,d_2`, PF-029 gives

```text
sinh(L_12/4)^2 = d_1/d_2.
```

The full small spectrum of the resulting four-punctured sphere is then an ordinary Laplace spectrum, not a generating function defined from the primes.

## 7. There is unconditionally at least one positive implanted point below 1/4

Hide and Thomas, *Small eigenvalues of hyperbolic surfaces with many cusps* (arXiv:2410.06093), prove constants `a,b>0` such that a genus-`g` finite-area hyperbolic surface with `n` cusps and

```text
(g+1) < a n/log n
```

has at least

```text
b (2g+n-2)/log(2g+n-2)
```

Laplacian eigenvalues below `1/4`.

For `Y_H`,

```text
g=0,
n=r+1.
```

For all sufficiently large `r`, their lower bound is greater than `1`.  Since the zero eigenvalue is simple on a connected finite-area surface, such a `Y_H` necessarily has

```text
0 < lambda_H < 1/4.
```

Pintz's theorem allows `k0`, hence `r`, to be chosen arbitrarily large.  Combining the two theorems with the geometric argument above yields the unconditional consequence

```text
boxed:
there exists lambda_H in (0,1/4)
such that lambda_H in sigma_ess(Delta_Xprime).
```

More strongly, for the selected recurrent pattern `H`, **all** small `L2` eigenvalues of its tangent `Y_H` belong to the essential spectrum of the prime-flute.

This is stronger than PF-021, which only established `inf sigma_ess = 0`, and independent of PF-024, which gives the universal cusp continuum `[1/4,infinity)`.

## 8. Novelty audit

The ingredients are classical separately:

1. explicit symmetric ideal-polygon uniformization of zero-twist tight flutes (Arredondo--Morales--Ramirez Maluendas);
2. Pintz's isolated bounded prime clusters;
3. standard pinching / augmented-Teichmuller convergence and the collar lemma;
4. the Weyl criterion and compact-support transplantation;
5. Hide--Thomas lower bounds for small eigenvalues of many-cusp finite-area surfaces.

The exact prime-flute matrix identities and cusp-side tangent algebra are PF-004/PF-018/PF-029.

Targeted searches for combinations of `prime gaps`, `isolated prime clusters`, `tight flute`, `punctured sphere`, `essential spectrum`, and `Laplacian` did not locate this composition.  No novelty is claimed for any of the five general ingredients above.  The potentially new statement is the prime-specific theorem

```text
recurrent isolated prime pattern H
 -> actual embedded pinching subsurfaces Omega_j
 -> genuine pointed tangent Y_H
 -> Spec_L2(Y_H) cap (0,1/4)
       subset sigma_ess(X_prime).
```

The result is also materially different from the invalid PF-025 construction: PF-025 tried to obtain a fixed surface by recentering the **absolute positive-side generators**, which PF-028 correctly showed retain divergent cuff traces.  PF-034 uses the actual finite subsurface enclosed by the PF-004 separating curve and the convergent **relative cusp holonomy** of PF-029.

## 9. Remaining research questions

This proves existence/implantation, but it does not yet show that two patterns of the same cardinality yield distinct small eigenvalues.  The next genuinely arithmetic-spectral gate is therefore:

```text
find recurrent H_1,H_2 with the same number of cusps
and prove that their Y_H small spectra differ.
```

That would separate the prime-gap **moduli** from the topological many-cusp mechanism of Hide--Thomas.

Other legitimate targets are spectral multiplicity of repeated tangents and whether several recurrent tangent types force a richer closed subset of `sigma_ess cap (0,1/4)`.
