\[
G:=F-I=(A_0^{1/2}-Q^{1/2})Q^{-1/2}.

On every finite section,

\[
\boxed{

=\frac1\pi\int_0^\infty
\mu^{1/2}(A_0+\mu)^{-1}
\Lambda_{LL}
(Q+\mu)^{-1}Q^{-1/2}
\,d\mu .

\tag{1}
\]

Thus the baseline seam square-root nonlocality cancels exactly: every non-identity term in `F` contains one explicit pant/core insertion `\Lambda_{LL}` dressed by the completed and seam resolvents. Equivalently, `G` is the unique finite-section solution of the Sylvester equation

\[
\boxed{
A_0^{1/2}G+GQ^{1/2}=\Lambda_{LL}Q^{-1/2}.

\tag{2}
\]

This is already useful for the spatial route. If `P` and `R` are separated packet/output projections with `RP=0`, then

\[
\boxed{

\mu^{1/2}R(A_0+\mu)^{-1}

(Q+\mu)^{-1}Q^{-1/2}P

The identity part contributes nothing. Any completed off-diagonal leakage must therefore be generated by a resolvent-dressed pant/core coupling; it is not an independent artifact of taking the seam square root.

\|[U_L,\tau_t]\|\le \|[F,\tau_t]\|,

## 1. Relative square-root identity

For a positive finite-dimensional operator `X`, the standard fractional-power representation is

\[
X^{1/2}
=\frac1\pi\int_0^\infty
\mu^{-1/2}X(X+\mu)^{-1}\,d\mu .
\tag{5}
\]

Use

\[
X(X+\mu)^{-1}=I-\mu(X+\mu)^{-1}.
\]

Subtract (5) for `A_0` and `Q`. The identity pieces cancel and the resolvent identity gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
A_0^{1/2}-Q^{1/2}
&=\frac1\pi\int_0^\infty
\mu^{1/2}
\bigl((Q+\mu)^{-1}-(A_0+\mu)^{-1}\bigr)
\,d\mu\\
&=\frac1\pi\int_0^\infty
\mu^{1/2}(A_0+\mu)^{-1}
(A_0-Q)(Q+\mu)^{-1}\,d\mu\\
&=\frac1\pi\int_0^\infty
\mu^{1/2}(A_0+\mu)^{-1}
\Lambda_{LL}(Q+\mu)^{-1}\,d\mu .
\end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]

Right multiplication by `Q^{-1/2}` proves (1). On a finite section the integral converges in operator norm: near zero all factors are bounded because `Q>0`, and at infinity the integrand is `O(\mu^{-3/2})`.

There is also a purely algebraic version. Put

\[
D=A_0^{1/2}-Q^{1/2}.
\]

Then

\[
A_0^{1/2}D+DQ^{1/2}=A_0-Q=\Lambda_{LL}.
\tag{7}
\]

Since `D=GQ^{1/2}`, right multiplication by `Q^{-1/2}` gives (2). The corresponding semigroup solution is

\[
G=\int_0^\infty
e^{-sA_0^{1/2}}
\Lambda_{LL}Q^{-1/2}
e^{-sQ^{1/2}}\,ds,
\tag{8}
\]

again on each positive finite section. Equations (1), (2), and (8) are three equivalent ways to keep the relative cancellation visible.

## 2. Translation defects contain no independent seam term

Because `Q` commutes with `\tau_t`,

\[
[F,\tau_t]=[A_0^{1/2},\tau_t]Q^{-1/2}.
\tag{9}
\]

Taking the commutator of (5), or equivalently differentiating the resolvent representation, gives for any bounded `V`

\[
[A_0^{1/2},V]
=\frac1\pi\int_0^\infty
\mu^{1/2}(A_0+\mu)^{-1}
[A_0,V]
(A_0+\mu)^{-1}\,d\mu .
\tag{10}
\]

For `V=\tau_t`,

\[
[A_0,\tau_t]=[Q+\Lambda_{LL},\tau_t]
=[\Lambda_{LL},\tau_t],
\]

and (4) follows.

This identifies a useful falsification control. Setting `\Lambda_{LL}=0` gives `F=I` exactly, even though `Q^{1/2}` and `Q^{-1/2}` separately can have nontrivial spatial kernels. Likewise, a translation-invariant `\Lambda_{LL}` gives `[F,\tau_t]=0`. Therefore a proof strategy that estimates

\[
[A_0^{1/2},X]Q^{-1/2}
\quad\text{and}\quad
A_0^{1/2}[Q^{-1/2},X]
\]

separately for a spatial cutoff `X` can lose an exact cancellation already present in the trivial-pant control. At the seam-critical scales of PF-326--PF-329 that can create artificial large terms. The relative object `G=F-I` is the safer locality target.

For a cutoff `\chi` that does not commute with `Q`, one no longer has the one-source formula (4), but still

\[
[F,\chi]=[G,\chi].
\tag{11}
\]

Expanding (1) shows that every term contains either an explicit `\Lambda_{LL}` or `[\Lambda_{LL},\chi]`; any seam commutator `[Q,\chi]` occurs only inside a term already carrying the pant insertion. Thus the baseline `\Lambda_{LL}=0` cancellation remains exact also for the cutoff formulation required by PF-331.

## Prior-art and novelty audit

The fractional-power/resolvent calculus behind (5)--(6) is classical; Balakrishnan's 1960 fractional-power theory already provides this type of representation. Likewise, commutator and Poisson-kernel estimates for Dirichlet-to-Neumann operators are an established PDE topic; recent work of ter Elst and Ouhabaz proves such bounds under regular bounded-domain hypotheses. No new general theorem about square roots, resolvents, or DtN commutators is claimed here.

Those general results do not supply the uniform statement needed in the present degeneration. The Prime-Flute sections have shrinking seam scales, a growing retained low rank, and the PF-324 finite-boundary/escaping-boundary alternatives. Generic bounded-domain commutator estimates do not by themselves show that (12) is `o(1)` uniformly in those parameters.

The project-specific contribution is the exact use of the PF-329 decomposition `A_0=Q+\Lambda_{LL}` together with PF-325 translation invariance of `Q`: the completed whitening defect has the relative formula (1), and its physical-translation defect has the one-source formula (4). This removes the seam square root as an independent source of translation nonlocality and isolates the remaining locality question in the actual pant/core DtN response.

The derivation above is self-contained on the finite sections, so no external theorem is imported as a proof dependency and `SOURCES.md` requires no new entry for this finding.

## Boundaries and adversarial checks

1. **No `o(1)` locality estimate is proved.** The resolvent identities expose the correct object but do not bound the integral uniformly in `n`.
2. **The factor `Q^{-1/2}` can still be singular in the degenerating scale.** Equation (4) is useful only if the resolvent-dressed commutator compensates that weight strongly enough.
3. **Cutoffs are harder than translations.** Physical translations commute with `Q`; spatial multiplication/cutoff operators generally do not. Equation (11) preserves relative cancellation, but no one-source cutoff formula is claimed.
4. **Off-diagonal representation is not off-diagonal decay.** The resolvents in (3) may themselves spread mass over the physical boundary. Their uniform kernels/commutators remain to be estimated.
5. **Finite sections are the safe level.** Passing (1)--(4) through the infinite boundary realization requires the same compatible limiting control already left open by PF-329--PF-331.
6. **No completed-angle lower bound or upper endpoint is proved.** The PF-318 Hilbert--Schmidt budget and the PF-328 positive-measure obstruction remain open.
7. **No scattering/determinant theorem, zeta-zero correspondence, critical-line result, or RH implication is established.**
