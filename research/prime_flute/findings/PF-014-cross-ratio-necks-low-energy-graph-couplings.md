# PF-014 — cross-ratio necks define low-energy graph couplings

> Retroactive persistence from the legacy `FINDINGS.md`; no new mathematical claim or evidence upgrade is introduced here.

**Status:** `LITERATURE+DERIVED`; application to the full fixed infinite flute remains a controlled-degeneration question.

## Claim

Burger's small-eigenvalue degeneration theorem associates pinched separating geodesics to a weighted graph whose edge weights are geodesic lengths and whose vertex masses are component areas, with the recorded normalization

```text
lambda_j(surface)/lambda_j(graph) -> 1/pi.
```

Combining this with PF-004 gives a canonical effective neck weight

```text
w(a,b,c,d)=L/pi
          =(4/pi) asinh(sqrt(chi(a,b,c,d))).
```

For an isolated prime cluster,

```text
w ~ (4/pi) sqrt(D(1/G_L+1/G_R)).
```

## Why it matters

This is a low-energy cluster graph driven by multi-gap cross-ratios rather than by one cuff length alone.

## Boundary

Burger's theorem concerns controlled degenerating families. It does not by itself prove spectral equivalence between an infinite graph assembled from all prime clusters and the fixed full prime flute.
