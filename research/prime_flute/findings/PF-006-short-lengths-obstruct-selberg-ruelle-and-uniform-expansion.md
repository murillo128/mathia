# PF-006 — short primitive lengths obstruct standard Selberg/Ruelle and uniform expansion

> Retroactive persistence from the legacy `FINDINGS.md`; no new mathematical claim or evidence upgrade is introduced here.

**Status:** `NEGATIVE/OBSTRUCTION`, conditional on the primitive-short-geodesic conclusion of PF-005.

## Claim

If primitive lengths `L_j->0`, then for fixed `Re(s)>0`

```text
1-exp(-s L_j) -> 0,
```

so the ordinary infinite Selberg product cannot converge in the usual finite-nonzero manner, and the corresponding Ruelle inverse factors diverge.

The same geometry blocks a faithful eventually uniformly expanding Bowen–Series-type coding: periodic multipliers associated with `L_j` tend to one, so no fixed iterate can retain all such periodic orbits and have a uniform expansion factor greater than one.

## Research consequence

Any useful dynamical object for the prime flute must accommodate genuine non-uniform hyperbolicity and renormalize the infinite short-orbit family rather than importing the standard finite-type absolute machinery unchanged.
