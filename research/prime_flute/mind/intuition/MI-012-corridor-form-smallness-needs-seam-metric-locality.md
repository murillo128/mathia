# MI-012 — Corridor form-smallness needs seam-metric locality before it controls the direct angle

**Evidence level:** exact as a two-metric obstruction through PF-319. PF-232 gives reciprocal-prime relative form control in the corridor energy metric, while PF-318 identifies the seam-normalized Hilbert--Schmidt leakage required by the direct physical angle. PF-319 proves that the former does not imply the latter without additional structure.

If positive forms `A` and `B` satisfy a small relative Loewner error, then `E=B^(-1/2)(A-B)B^(-1/2)` is small in operator norm. A logarithmic-rank mixed corner in that same `B` metric would therefore automatically have the mean-square scale required by PF-318.

The physical direct angle is normalized by a different seam form `Q`. In that metric the perturbation is

`D_Q=Q^(-1/2)(A-B)Q^(-1/2)=C^* E C`, with `C=B^(1/2)Q^(-1/2)`.

PF-319 gives an exact two-dimensional countermodel showing that a fixed small `||E||` places no bound on a mixed low/high corner of `D_Q` unless the relevant source/target pieces of `C` are controlled. The thin flat-corridor model confirms that the two energy metrics can differ by large geometry-dependent factors, so this is not a harmless uniform change of norm.

Thus PF-232 removes the apparent order-one hypercycle-straightening coefficient **inside its own corridor metric**, but it cannot be multiplied by low rank and promoted directly to the PF-318 seam-normalized endpoint estimate. The positive route must use source/target-restricted metric conversion, or exploit actual mode locality/almost-diagonality of the canonical shear in the physical-frequency seam basis, or estimate the seam-normalized mixed corner directly.

**Boundary.** The countermodel does not show that the real hypercycle shear has large seam-normalized leakage and does not refute the PF-318 target. It rules out only a proof based on Loewner-relative form smallness without the additional geometric localization that distinguishes the canonical operator from an arbitrary two-metric perturbation.
