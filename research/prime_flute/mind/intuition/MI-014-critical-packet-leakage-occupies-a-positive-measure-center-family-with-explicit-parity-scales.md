# MI-014 — Critical packet leakage occupies a positive-measure center family with explicit parity scales

**Evidence level:** supported by PF-325--PF-328 before the one-cusp-pant crossing and final intrinsic-angle normalization. No lower bound on the completed angle block is claimed.

PF-325 identifies the destination currency for the fallback argument: Hilbert--Schmidt mass is the integral, over physical packet center `t`, of the completed response to translated normalized low packets. PF-326--PF-327 show that one compactification-critical packet survives seam normalization and retains a positive transverse-energy floor before pant-side operations.

PF-328 removes the possibility that this witness is isolated in the integration variable. For any fixed `0<lambda_0<lambda_1<1`, the critical centers form an interval

`I_n=[tau_(n,lambda_0),tau_(n,lambda_1)]`

whose length tends to `log(lambda_1/lambda_0)>0`, and the shape-normalized critical-energy limit is uniform across that interval. Restoring the actual seam-energy normalization also exposes a parity asymmetry: the symmetric channel carries raw critical transverse energy at scale `w_n^-1`, while the antisymmetric channel carries scale `w_n`. These are pre-angle scales and may still be absorbed downstream.

The falsification burden is therefore measure-theoretic, not pointwise. If the completed local block satisfies the required Hilbert--Schmidt budget, then for every fixed response threshold `eta>0` the subset of this positive-length critical interval on which the completed packet response exceeds `eta` must have measure tending to zero. A pant-side argument that suppresses one distinguished critical center is structurally insufficient.

The next computation should propagate the whole weighted packet family, with the common seam amplitudes retained, through one-cusp-pant crossing, physical `L/H` projections, common completion and final intrinsic normalization, separately in the finite-boundary and escaping-boundary arithmetic regimes.

**Boundary.** PF-328 strengthens the upstream witness from one center to a positive-measure family but still stops before the completed angle. The explicit `w_n` parity scales are not themselves lower bounds after final diagonal normalization.
