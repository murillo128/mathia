# MI-014 — Critical packet leakage occupies a positive-measure center family, but final diagonal preconditioning is still decisive

**Evidence level:** supported by PF-325--PF-329. PF-326--PF-328 establish the positive-measure critical family before final intrinsic-angle normalization; PF-329 identifies the additional completed diagonal inverse-square-root acting on that family. No lower bound on the completed angle block is claimed.

PF-325 identifies the destination currency for the fallback argument: Hilbert--Schmidt mass is the integral, over physical packet center `t`, of the completed response to translated normalized low packets. PF-326--PF-327 show that one compactification-critical packet survives seam normalization and retains a positive transverse-energy floor before pant-side operations.

PF-328 removes the possibility that this witness is isolated in the integration variable. For any fixed `0<lambda_0<lambda_1<1`, the critical centers form an interval of nonzero limiting physical length, and the shape-normalized critical-energy limit is uniform across that interval. Restoring the actual seam-energy normalization also exposes a parity asymmetry: the symmetric channel carries raw pre-angle transverse energy at scale `w_n^-1`, while the antisymmetric channel carries scale `w_n`.

PF-329 shows exactly why those explicit amplitudes are not yet destination amplitudes. The seam-only pullback `Q_L^(-1/2) psi_t` is not the raw unit vector seen by the intrinsic angle. A unit packet in the completed normalized low coordinate corresponds instead to

`g_t = Q_L^(-1/2) A^(-1/2) psi_t`,

where `A` is the completed low diagonal block. This trace satisfies unit total low energy exactly. The full block-diagonal seam congruence changes the intrinsic angle only by left/right unitaries, so seam amplification itself cannot provide an angle gain.

The falsification burden remains measure-theoretic but is now operator-valued. If the completed local block satisfies the required Hilbert--Schmidt budget, then for every fixed response threshold `eta>0` the subset of the positive-length critical interval on which the **completed-energy-preconditioned** packet response exceeds `eta` must have measure tending to zero. Conversely, an obstruction requires proving that `A^(-1/2)` preserves enough of the critical family on positive measure, or estimating the equivalent raw total-energy correlation directly.

The key correction is that `A^(-1/2)` need not be scalar and need not commute with physical translations. Therefore the raw traces `g_t` need not remain translates of one fixed profile even though the normalized packets `psi_t` are. PF-328 remains a valid calibration of the pre-angle geometry; PF-329 locates the exact operator through which that calibration must pass.

**Boundary.** No endpoint estimate is proved. The positive-measure critical interval, seam localization and parity scales remain valid upstream facts, but neither large pre-angle energy nor one scalar normalization implies large completed angle response. The next theorem must control completed diagonal preconditioning or bypass explicit packet propagation with the total-energy variational correlation.
