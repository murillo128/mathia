# MI-015 — Completed packet locality should be priced at the normalized insertion, before the polar unitary

**Evidence level:** exact finite-section algebra from [PF-329](../../findings/PF-329-final-diagonal-normalization-turns-seam-pulled-packets-into-total-energy-unit-traces.md)--[PF-336](../../findings/PF-336-relative-polar-transport-removes-the-normalized-insertion-envelope.md). No Prime-Flute locality estimate or endpoint weak-trace-class theorem is claimed.

Let `A_0=Q+Lambda_LL`, `K=Q^(-1/2)Lambda_LLQ^(-1/2)>=0`, and let `U` be the completed polar unitary generated after the relative square-root step. PF-329--PF-332 show that completed normalization preserves the critical packet family and that the completion defect is generated by the actual pant/core insertion rather than by the seam square root itself.

PF-333 shows that generic operator-norm perturbation is the wrong currency: worst relative square-root sensitivity can grow like `log r`. PF-334 proves dimension- and condition-number-free Hilbert--Schmidt control under a normalized-insertion envelope, and PF-335 transfers physical translation commutators through that square-root map.

PF-336 sharpens the endpoint again. At the completed polar unitary, if `V` commutes with `Q`, then

`||[U,V]||_(S2) <= 1/sqrt(2) ||[K,V]||_(S2)`.

For the physical translations of PF-325,

`[K,tau_t]=Q^(-1/2)[Lambda_LL,tau_t]Q^(-1/2)`.

Thus translation locality of the **normalized pant insertion** transfers to the completed polar factor with no rank loss, seam-conditioning loss or uniform `||K||` envelope. The envelope that remained in PF-335 was a cost of the intermediate relative square-root estimate, not an intrinsic cost of the final polar transport.

The live translation endpoint is therefore simply to prove reciprocal-prime `S_2` commutator smallness for `K_n`. A separate physical concentration theorem is still required: translation locality is weaker than fixed-window locality because spatial cutoffs need not commute with `Q`, and a global `S_2` bound may hide exceptional packet directions.

**Boundary.** PF-336 does not prove `||[K_n,tau_t]||_(S2)=o(1)`, cutoff locality, a completed correlation lower bound, or the PF-318 low/high estimate. It removes an artificial envelope requirement from the completed polar step; it does not supply the geometric/PDE estimate on the insertion itself.
