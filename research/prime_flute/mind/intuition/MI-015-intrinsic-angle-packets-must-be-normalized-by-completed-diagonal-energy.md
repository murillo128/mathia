# MI-015 — Completed packet locality should be priced in the Hilbert--Schmidt currency before operator norm

**Evidence level:** exact finite-section algebra from [PF-329](../../findings/PF-329-final-diagonal-normalization-turns-seam-pulled-packets-into-total-energy-unit-traces.md)--[PF-335](../../findings/PF-335-relative-square-root-translation-commutators-inherit-hilbert-schmidt-control.md). No Prime-Flute locality estimate or endpoint weak-trace-class theorem is claimed.

Let `A_0=Q+Lambda_LL`, `K=Q^(-1/2)Lambda_LLQ^(-1/2)>=0`, and `F=A_0^(1/2)Q^(-1/2)`. PF-329--PF-332 show that completed normalization preserves the critical packet family and that the relative square-root defect is generated by the actual pant/core insertion rather than by the seam square root itself.

PF-333 shows that a generic operator-norm perturbation theorem is the wrong currency: the worst relative square-root sensitivity can grow like `log r`. PF-334 then proves dimension- and condition-number-free Hilbert--Schmidt control for the relative square-root map under a uniform `||K||` envelope.

PF-335 converts that finite-difference control into the exact commutator statement needed by the translation route. If `V` commutes with `Q`, then

`||[F,V]||_(S2) <= sqrt(1+||K||)/sqrt(2) ||[K,V]||_(S2)`.

For the physical translations of PF-325,

`[K,tau_t]=Q^(-1/2)[Lambda_LL,tau_t]Q^(-1/2)`.

Hence an `o(1)` Hilbert--Schmidt translation commutator for the **normalized pant insertion** transfers directly to the completed square root without rank or seam-conditioning loss, and PF-331 then transfers it to the completed polar unitary.

The live endpoint is therefore narrower than generic relative locality. Prove a uniform `||K_n||` envelope and reciprocal-prime `S_2` translation locality for `K_n`, then supply concentration of one completed reference profile. The relative square-root and polar steps no longer create an independent logarithmic loss along this route.

Translation locality remains weaker than fixed-window locality: spatial cutoffs need not commute with `Q`. A global `S_2` bound may also hide exceptional packet directions. The PF-328 positive-measure obstruction still requires either localized reference-profile control or a stronger cutoff/off-diagonal theorem before physical concentration is known after completion.

**Boundary.** PF-335 relocates the estimate but does not prove `||K_n||=O(1)`, `||[K_n,tau_t]||_(S2)=o(1)`, cutoff locality, a completed correlation lower bound, or the PF-318 low/high estimate itself.
