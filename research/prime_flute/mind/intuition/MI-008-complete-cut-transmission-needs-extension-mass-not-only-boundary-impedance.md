# MI-008 — The flute mixed-response gate is an accumulated source/escape balance, not a local contraction gap

**Evidence level:** proved local operator and source-space boundaries through PF-298; the factorization of the complete physical mixed response remains open.

For the fixed physical high-pass spaces, PF-292 gives a diverging local frequency floor. PF-296 then proves that the canonical fixed-axis synthesis is uniformly bounded from the explicit weighted source space `ell^1(q)` into positive Sobolev regularity, with `q_i asymp i+1`. Thus a complete normalized response with uniformly bounded `sum_i q_i|a_i|`, controlled transport, and negligible residual cannot sustain the PF-290 local high-band shorting deficit.

PF-297 identifies the exact behavior of one repeated local factor. Every fixed-axis Robin inverse is nonexpansive in the same `ell^1(q)` currency. Finite Galerkin sections are strictly contractive because `q`-mass leaks into omitted rows, but that loss is a truncation effect: the finite-section norms tend to one and the complete positive inverse preserves `q`-mass.

PF-298 removes a second possible uniform discount. On constant boundary data a tight one-cusp pant has response

\[
c_n\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}+\operatorname{diag}(\kappa_{n,L},\kappa_{n,R}),
\]

with `c_n \gtrsim s_n^{-1}`, nonnegative killing, and `\kappa_{n,L}+\kappa_{n,R}\le2\pi`. Hence relative killing is `O(s_n)` and the isolated constant-mode Schur transmission is `1-O(s_n)`. Local coercivity from `Delta+1` therefore does not produce a source-independent contraction bounded away from one.

The remaining gate is the **actual normalized mixed return/reassembly map** after the physical `P/H` split. A sufficient positive-majorant route remains a uniformly summable excessive vector for the physical forcing, but its escape must come from accumulated nonuniform pant killing, omitted support, nonconstant channels, source-specific forcing/cancellation, or a reassembly factor that changes the `q` currency. A direct signed estimate is equally admissible if absolute values destroy the useful structure.

**Boundary.** The PF-298 constant channel is a quadratic-form compression, not a proved invariant subspace of the full Schur problem. Because `sum s_n` is not summable, small per-pant losses may still accumulate substantially. Neither PF-297 nor PF-298 decides the global return potential, complete mixed compactness, or the separate heavy-range singular-value problem.
