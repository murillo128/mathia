# MI-004 — The clone comparison has reached a global-assembly and nonlocal-value boundary

**Evidence level:** supported; compact relative resolvent, local geometric/operator budgets, gauge equivalence, local regularization no-go results, and conditional functional-calculus consequences are exact, while the decisive global squared-resolvent/wave closure remains open

## Core intuition

The exact prime flute and the all-composite shift clone `q_n=p_n+1` are increasingly hard to distinguish in natural asymptotic operator categories. The remaining issue is no longer whether a large coordinate, a different `L^2` identification, a higher local heat coefficient, a different resolvent power, or a regularized determinant can manufacture arithmetic signal. It is whether the controlled local geometry assembles into a global comparison and, if it does, whether the **actual nonlocal relative spectral value** distinguishes the two surfaces.

## Strongest justified principle

PF-121--PF-125 give the baseline: a canonical marked asymptotically bilipschitz identification and compact first relative resolvent, hence equality of essential spectra. PF-112 fixes a hard local endpoint: the first relative resolvent of a genuinely nonisometric smooth two-dimensional metric pair is not trace class.

PF-126--PF-146 remove the main local geometric escape hatches and establish a complementary operator route. Body, cusp, split-ray, short-core, and handoff budgets are summable; deep cusps can be matched exactly; reflection removes the constant collar phase; and on fixed matched short collars the **squared** relative resolvent is trace class with a summable local budget. The open step is global assembly through all interfaces/localization commutators.

PF-147--PF-150 classify what a positive global square-resolvent result would and would not mean. It would place the first relative resolvent exactly at the abstractly sharp `S_2` boundary while still allowing ordinary Krein spectral shift and Birman--Krein scattering for the original Laplacians by invariance. The natural volume identifications give the same wave operators when either exists, and abstract functional calculus cannot improve below `S_2` without new geometry.

PF-151--PF-155 close several apparent scalar repairs around that boundary. The critical first-resolvent residue is hyperbolic area density. PF-152 shows that cancellation of the total residue on matched equal-area compact models does not canonize the finite part: weighted traces retain a classical regularization anomaly. PF-155 strengthens the locality obstruction: on curvature `-1` surfaces **every** local heat coefficient is area/topology only, so cuff-modulus information lies beyond all algebraic short-time orders in the nonlocal geodesic/scattering sector.

The conditional functional-calculus escapes also collapse. Under the still-open global square-resolvent `S_1` hypothesis, PF-153 shows that all higher even resolvent powers pull back to the same normalized spectral-shift/scattering phase; changing the exponent only changes the moment weight. PF-154 shows that the natural first-resolvent `det_2` creates no new zero divisor: its zeros on the reference resolvent set are just ordinary discrete eigenvalues of the numerator resolvent. Regularization changes normalization, not spectral locations.

## What remains possible

The decisive technical gate is still one global closure: either sum the reflection-compatible metric/interface criterion or globalize the PF-146 squared-resolvent trace-class estimate. If that succeeds, existence of wave operators, a spectral-shift function, or a scattering phase is no longer evidence by itself.

The arithmetic question then moves to the **value and continuation** of the nonlocal relative object: the actual spectral-shift/scattering function, relative resonance continuation, closed-geodesic contribution, or another stronger invariant. It must distinguish the prime flute from the exact composite clone for a reason not forced by generic trace-ideal theory, constant curvature, or the chosen regularization.

## Status / novelty

The geometric comparison, local Schatten estimates, invariance-principle implications, heat/residue universality, weighted-trace anomaly, and determinant factorization are persisted findings with classical ingredients. The synthesis is a narrowed boundary, not a proof of global wave operators, equality of scattering data, resonance continuation, or RH.

## Falsification criterion

A decisive advance should prove or obstruct the genuine global assembly or compute a relative nonlocal invariant that survives it and separates the clone. An alleged shortcut is falsified if it relies only on first-resolvent trace class, volume-identification gauge, higher even resolvent powers, the local heat tower, residue-subtracted weighted finite parts, or zeros of the natural `det_2` without new spectral input.
