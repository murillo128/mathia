## Resolve the endpoint conservative splice using structure stronger than generic incompressibility

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-standard-global-selberg-machinery-is-structurally-incompatible`, `MI-004-coordinate-amplification-is-not-operator-amplification`, and `MI-005-short-collar-multiplicity-reduces-to-one-local-splice`.

PF-171--PF-191 remove the collapsing-central-sector, long-body, artificial-interface, flux, multiplicity, and endpoint-body-integrability obstructions. The exact-area Lambert/body comparison has summable strong-`L^1` mass, so the remaining geometry is genuinely local to the conservative splice/reassembly.

PF-192 shows that this endpoint cannot be obtained by sending the generic `L^r`, `r>1`, rigidity theorem to `r=1`: strong `L^1` geometric rigidity fails even on a fixed regular domain, while only a weak-`L^1` substitute survives generically. PF-193 sharpens the boundary further. The Conti--Faraco--Maggi staircase admits an exact determinant-one lift preserving its rank-one lamination tree and arbitrarily bad strong-`L^1` rigidity ratio arbitrarily close to the identity.

Therefore **pointwise exact area is not the missing coercivity by itself**. A PF-specific strong endpoint theorem must use additional canonical structure -- reflection marking, exact/zero-flux global constraints, fixed-germ target confinement, boundary behavior, or special regularity of the actual PF maps -- and must explain why that structure forbids the determinant-one laminate mechanism. A map-level incompressible realization of the lifted laminate remains an open falsifier, not an established counterexample to the canonical germ.

## Develop the weak/Lorentz endpoint in parallel with any constrained strong theorem

PF-189 places the complete decoupled central short-collar sector in weak `S_1`, PF-190 identifies the possible logarithmic loss in full reassembly, and PF-191 supplies the strong endpoint body budget. PF-192's generic weak-`L^1` rigidity substitute now matches this operator frontier more naturally than an unsupported strong `W^{1,1}` interpolation.

A decisive route should either prove that the additional PF structure upgrades the local geometry to the strong endpoint with energy-linear cost, or show that weak/Lorentz geometric control is enough to build the exact-symplectic cutoff and obtain a true weak-`S_1` uncut resolvent without recreating the `log^2 n/n` loss. The two routes share the same body energy but make different claims about the critical local bridge.
