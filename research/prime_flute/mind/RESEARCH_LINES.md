# Prime-flute research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-flute intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should be merged, redirected, or removed when the mathematics changes.

## Resolve upstream spectral memory in recurrent prime blocks

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-relative-multigap-geometry-carries-the-spectral-signal`.

The finite graded-chain theorem shows that a resolved eigenvalue can retain the correction `w_j^2/w_{j-1}`, but the presently proved prime-isolation results do not force the moderate Burger window in which that term is visible on the true surface. There are two mathematically distinct ways to close this gap: prove recurrent consecutive-prime blocks with sufficient quantitative hierarchy control, or sharpen the multi-collar surface-to-graph error so that the stronger hierarchies already known to recur become spectrally resolvable.

A decisive result is either route yielding a recurrent prime-derived surface family for which an individual eigenvalue, resolvent pole, marked residue, or comparable resolved observable provably contains the upstream coefficient. Failure of every achievable prime hierarchy to clear any possible surface-resolution scale would weaken the arithmetic extension while leaving the finite-surface mechanism intact.

## Localized marked spectral data inside the essential background

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-localize-in-space-before-spectral-compression`.

Develop relative or localized Weyl/scattering objects for isolated finite tangents and determine how their marked spectral measures or residues are represented when recurrence turns the corresponding finite-tangent eigenvalues into essential global spectrum. The aim is not to recover ordinary isolated global poles, but to retain the finite ordered data without erasing the mark that made the inverse problem injective.

The decisive test is an operator-level localization statement that both converges to the finite-tangent marked object behind escaping collars and embeds that information canonically into the infinite-flute spectral representation. Any scalar compression proposed afterwards should have its fibers computed on the tangent family before it is used.

## Determine the relative operator class of the affine all-composite clone

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-standard-global-selberg-machinery-is-structurally-incompatible`.

PF-106 gives the strongest current matched control: after a canonical Möbius normalization, the all-composite sequence `p_n+1` has `ell^1` sampled-endpoint displacement from the exact prime flute, `O(P^-3)` all-span tail cross-ratio distortion, and a natural piecewise-affine boundary matching with finite `L^1` derivative defect. What is not known is whether this boundary comparison extends equivariantly to the quotient surfaces with enough analytic control to compare their Laplacians.

The decisive test is to prove either a canonical surface comparison implying compact/Schatten/trace-class control of an appropriate relative resolvent, heat kernel, or scattering operator, or a precise obstruction showing why the collapsing infinite-type geometry amplifies the summable endpoint defect into a noncompact operator difference. Either outcome would sharply locate the only exact-tail spectral information not already killed by the composite-clone controls.
