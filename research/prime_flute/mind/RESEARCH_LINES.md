# Prime-flute research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-flute intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Control low-output leakage after propagation rather than pre-propagation smoothing

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-standard-global-selberg-machinery-is-structurally-incompatible`, `MI-004-coordinate-amplification-is-not-operator-amplification`, `MI-005-short-collar-multiplicity-reduces-to-one-local-splice`, `MI-006-the-critical-endpoint-needs-structure-and-the-right-concentration-currency`.

PF-258--PF-268 establish the intrinsic-six tangent, the fixed-axis fractional smoothing window, the positive odd-mass seam decomposition, and the lowest-pole obstruction to applying a bare separated `R>1` weight before the correct composed geometry. That obstruction is real but it is not the final currency once spatial propagation is included.

PF-269--PF-271 sharpen the order of operations. Pre-propagation Robin smoothing is stronger than necessary; deterministic separated propagation may pay high-output modes before endpoint weighting. The exact surviving obligation is one-sided leakage from high input into **sublinear low output** of the conversion operator. If the far propagator has exponential spectral damping, boundedness forces and is naturally tested by logarithmic-output-window decay rather than by global graph-domain invariance.

The live theorem is therefore to estimate the actual Robin/reflection conversion in the strongly separated cone and prove the required high-to-low leakage bound in the endpoint normalization. A proportional high-high `N^-2` seam floor is no longer by itself fatal because propagation can suppress it; a fixed-low-output mixer remains fatal because the far propagator cannot pay a mode that has already fallen into the undamped window.

## Treat bare seam smoothing, full graph locality, and proportional high-high floors as stronger-than-needed controls

The fixed-axis fractional window and the positive lowest seam pole remain correct local facts, but the composed endpoint map consumes a narrower property. Future arguments should price only the spectral leakage that survives propagation and reaches the low-output endpoint window, rather than demand smoothing of the conversion operator in directions already killed by the deterministic far leg.
