## Apply normalized Robin or spatial propagation before demanding a supercritical endpoint weight

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-standard-global-selberg-machinery-is-structurally-incompatible`, `MI-004-coordinate-amplification-is-not-operator-amplification`, `MI-005-short-collar-multiplicity-reduces-to-one-local-splice`, `MI-006-the-critical-endpoint-needs-structure-and-the-right-concentration-currency`.

PF-258--PF-260 identify the physical relative-seam tangent as the intrinsic-six operator and prove a fixed-axis smoothing window with every strict `1<R<2`. PF-261--PF-267 then resolve the finite seam into a positive odd-mass resolvent ladder and obtain sharp fixed-mass and mass-uniform Green amplitude/decay control.

PF-268 changes the conclusion of that thread. Positivity makes the lowest mass pole a permanent lower bound, and on proportional separated blocks it already contributes an `N^-2` matrix floor. Consequently the bare finite-seam crossover followed by any separated input weight `R>1` is unbounded, even after a fixed high-mode cutoff. The hoped-for supercritical margin cannot be obtained by summing the mass ladder more carefully.

The surviving route must use extra structure **before** the `R>1` demand: the combined normalized Robin transform from PF-257, spatially separated Robin--Poisson propagation from PF-243, or another source-forced cancellation/normalization that removes the lowest-pole obstruction. The endpoint `R=1` remains a separate boundary and PF-268 does not establish boundedness below it.

## Treat fixed-axis smoothing and bare finite-seam weighting as distinct currencies

The fixed-axis fractional window is real, but it is not stably transported through the unnormalized finite seam. Fixed-mass asymptotics and odd-ladder summation are now controls rather than the main frontier. Any next theorem must estimate the actual composed Robin/corridor map in the endpoint norm, not infer its margin from the local seam operator.
