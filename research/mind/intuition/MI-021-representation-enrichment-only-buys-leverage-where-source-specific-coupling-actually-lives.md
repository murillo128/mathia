# MI-021 — Representation enrichment only buys leverage where source-specific coupling survives acquisition, closure and matched controls

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including FD-200--FD-202, MC-382--MC-383, NB-217--NB-218, PC-351, PL-361--PL-363, RE-216--RE-217, VIS-302--VIS-303, WI-351--WI-352 and WP-366. The quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific; the common content is the placement, independence and survival of information through the destination interface.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, compression can be legitimate when an exact theorem proves that discarded data are generated by retained data. The relevant question is not “how much representation is present?” but **what source-specific relation survives acquisition, canonical closure/globalization, topology, normalization and matched controls, and which apparently new resources remain genuinely independent after those operations?**

Farey Discrepancy now gives both the failure and the constructive repair inside one observation family. FD-200 proves that matched horizon-mode enlargement is exact divisor transport and adds no information. FD-201 finds a genuinely new fixed-mode cross-horizon source coordinate: prime-shell rows contain multiplicative Mertens increments. Squaring those rows independently into the positive half-Sobolev norm nevertheless imposes a random-multiplicative shell tariff of `H^(3/2+o(1))`, much stronger than the intended critical scale. FD-202 then applies an exact signed adjacent-prime derivative **before** squaring. The long overlapping Mertens intervals collapse to disjoint endpoint slabs, and the shell-normalized path-Laplacian energy has random-multiplicative mean `~(6/pi^2)(1+r)H`. The gain is therefore not “more data” but preservation of cross-row covariance through the compression interface. What remains is a one-dimensional constant-mode kernel, so the next price is a low-rank anchor rather than the original shellwide diagonal tariff.

Möbius Cancellation supplies both a method-depth and a family-breadth audit. MC-381 removes a fictitious square-root source-rank loss, but MC-382 shows that the same q-van der Corput architecture still has an intrinsic `log log` source horizon because source exponent forces linearly growing differencing depth while terminal saving decays exponentially. MC-383 then stress-tests an apparently more collective route: even an exponential small-doubling family cannot create a rank-linear source exponent if the theorem pays a fixed positive power of one global modulus. The family is genuinely larger, but its logarithmic gain is in the wrong currency relative to the conductor cost.

Nyman--Beurling now separates exact representation algebra from the proof norm used to estimate it. NB-217 proves that after `j` Mellin primitive transfers, every absolute-norm reconstruction pays the reciprocal log-support currency `2pi/J_j(E)`. NB-218 shows that this positive-power tariff is **not** present as an algebraic multiplier in the exact signed reconstruction. The shell is an exact convolution of the `j`-fold zeta primitive with its matched reconstruction kernel; the `(log n)^j` and `(log n)^(-j)` factors cancel coefficientwise, and the shell kernel annihilates every finite polynomial jet of the primitive. The loss appears when the two factors are decoupled by absolute values. Thus a representation may contain a cancellation mechanism that disappears not under acquisition but under the proof interface used to certify it. The live object is the carrier-frequency Fourier coefficient of the coupled zeta primitive, with contour/tail transport still unresolved.

Prime Circle supplies the quotient warning. PC-351 shows that the full noncommutative shell signature can reconstruct the centered source path, yet the canonical triangular decoder has trivial ordinary spectrum and a reciprocal sign-flip control is unitarily conjugate to the arithmetic decoder. A representation can therefore be faithful while its natural invariant quotient is blind. Source recovery and destination selectivity are different resources.

Prime Lattice sharpens the topology/continuation gate on both sides. PL-360--PL-362 show that diagonal Hilbert reweighting, non-diagonal completion with canonical coefficient cutoffs, and coefficientwise-positive smoothing all reconstruct the original convergence wall. PL-363 then allows finite signs and really crosses that wall: zero period mean gives ordinary convergence on `Re s>0`. But the resulting object is only a periodic Dirichlet series `P(s)zeta(s)` with classical continuation. **Changing the continuation mechanism is necessary, but an escape is not selective merely because it escapes a previous obstruction.** The sign pattern must be source-forced and retain a destination relation not reproducible by a chosen finite pole-cancelling filter.

Robin Extremal now cleanly separates spectral approximation cost from prime-carrier capacity. RE-216 changes only the analytic predictor and lowers the explicit coefficient-variation exponent from `12 log 2` to at most `6.081` while reusing the same ordinary-prime realization. RE-217 supplies the converse spectral floor: every separated continuum predictor must have leading Wiener exponent at least one, so the pure prediction constant satisfies `1<=C_sep<=6.081`. The arithmetic realization already accepts every strict coefficient exponent below `1/2`. Representation improvement therefore acts on a spectral-conditioning currency whose remaining constant-factor gap is independent of the known prime-placement ceiling.

Visual Exploration shows how proof packaging can manufacture an apparent channel more than once and how source support can then remove part of the newly exposed remainder. Exact gamma recombination removes one lower-source boundary; VIS-301 suppresses the isolated localization coherence; VIS-302 evaluates Wang's exact coefficient weight and removes another logarithm from the generic Montgomery--Vaughan bound. VIS-303 then uses the actual prime-power support: powers of two carry vanishing energy and bounded weighted mass, so at `x log x=O(H)` the entire odd-additive-shift sector is `o(H)`. The live upper object is therefore not the unrestricted off-diagonal remainder but its odd-prime-power/even-shift component. A claimed representation boundary is not a survivor until the theorem has been specialized to the exact source coefficients **and** the surviving source-support sectors have been separated at destination resolution.

Weil Inertia prices formal infinite-dimensionality and resource dependence. WI-351 shows that infinitely many tiny charge sectors become effectively finite at destination resolution when a charge-Sobolev moment is controlled. WI-352 then proves that the first such moment is not independent at all: `B_1<=L` follows from the same Lipschitz equivariance already used to price source sensitivity. A formal complexity coordinate deserves separate credit only after proving it is not already controlled by another budget in the architecture.

Weil Positivity adds the globalization warning. WP-365 already shows that an independent positive finite--archimedean tensor completion can fail at the critical divisor. WP-366 starts from a stronger fixed-height feature where finite and archimedean variables genuinely interact through `nY`; nevertheless positive scalar dilation-covariant globalization forces power-Haar averaging and Mellin-separates the coupling back into the failed Rankin--Selberg product. **Local non-product structure is not enough if the canonical operation that removes its auxiliary scale factorizes it again.**

These examples refine the audit into seven questions. First identify the minimal source carrier after exact algebraic generation. Second ask whether acquisition actually creates a new coordinate or merely transports old data. Third apply the destination's canonical closure, summation, reconstruction or scale-globalization and identify what it regenerates, annihilates or factorizes. Fourth state the topology and resolution seen by the destination, including which source correlations or support sectors survive the chosen norm or proof inequality. Fifth test whether the apparent resource budgets are genuinely independent. Sixth run the strongest matched control preserving the generic operations. Seventh state the source-specific theorem that leaves a nontrivial selective survivor after all six gates.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem transferring Farey path differencing, q-vdC depth, zeta-primitive convolution, reciprocal signatures, causal prediction, Dirichlet summation, Wang mean values, charge decompositions or Whittaker averaging between lines. Each quantitative statement remains line-specific. The cross-line content is the falsifiable audit: enrichment deserves credit only when newly acquired or newly retained structure survives the exact destination operations and the proof interface used to estimate them, remains visible at the requested resolution, is not merely a dependent resource, and defeats the relevant matched control.
