# MI-021 — Representation enrichment only buys leverage where source-specific coupling actually survives

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including AF-416--AF-433, FD-200--FD-213, MC-382--MC-393, NB-217--NB-227, PC-351--PC-358, PL-361--PL-373, RE-216--RE-225, VIS-302--VIS-314, WI-350--WI-354 and WP-366--WP-372. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, enrichment is useful only when a new relation has an exact source preimage, survives the destination operation and improves a load-bearing bottleneck without repaying the gain elsewhere. The relevant question is not how large the representation is, but **what source-specific relation survives acquisition, closure, inversion, symmetry, positivity and matched controls?**

Arithmetic Fidelity adds a provenance gate before any mesh audit. AF-433 shows that the current left-tail amplitude is a bijective coordinate on a freely chosen probe path. A rich shrinking-target geometry in that amplitude is therefore not source-discriminating until an independent source law selects it.

Farey Discrepancy now closes a stronger class than fixed-filter enrichment. FD-212 produces a supercritical Floquet blind mode from individually harmless bounded-depth filters, and FD-213 lifts it to a bounded source whose selected filter vanishes at **every integer**. The reason is exact: dyadic dilation preserves the mantissa coordinate, so a scale-index mode can be tensored with a boundary-vanishing profile and stitched across bands. All-integer sampling adds no new coupling when the operator remains fibrewise. The surviving discriminator must use genuinely arithmetic source structure or mix those fibres.

Prime Circle pushes the similarity audit beyond tensor metrics. PC-358 proves that on every finite signature truncation, **any** fixed positive Hilbert metric—nonfactorizing, shell-dependent, cross-word or cross-degree—still leaves source and reciprocal control exactly similar after whitening, because the algebraic parity conjugacy survives unchanged. At infinite depth the same holds whenever parity extends boundedly. Metric enrichment matters only if it changes the bounded domain/intertwiner structure, not merely its condition number.

Prime Lattice removes the infinite-alphabet divisor loophole. PL-373 shows that every genuine bounded completely multiplicative Venturini witness in an RH-relevant half-plane is

`L(a,s)=[zeta(2s)/zeta(s)] C_a(s)`

with `C_a` holomorphic and zero-free. Even conditional infinite-alphabet first-order organization changes only a gauge, not the zeta divisor. A distinct route must leave this scalar completely multiplicative witness class or construct a different global theorem interface.

Nyman--Beurling shows why a stronger-looking coordinate can remain separable from the true carrier. NB-227 extends the fixed-power phase no-go: multiplying `M^vartheta` by an arbitrary predetermined depth-only improvement `1/Q(D)` still fails, because a matched packet can let `D` diverge slowly enough that the depth penalty is neutralized by available population while the residue damping is exactly compensated. Useful information must couple population, depth and phase nonseparably or act directly on weighted residues.

Möbius Cancellation closes the loop between representation complexity and physical source cost. MC-392 says sparse source-frame exceptions require linear quotient codimension. MC-393 feeds that requirement back through large-prime incidence and shows that, inside the fixed-power source package, the logarithmic conductor cost has rank coefficient arbitrarily close to one until a separate `log log y` ceiling intervenes. Relation-rich enrichment is not free when the source must physically buy those relations.

Robin Extremal shows parameter enrichment collapsing into one genuine singular regime. RE-225 proves that in a fixed corridor a positive-real single-center predictor cannot approach the contraction boundary at fixed positive arc width. Any rate-two sequence must have `alpha->0`; if `r->1` as well, the center is forced to `a->1/2`. The remaining representation-specific question is the vanishing-arc asymptotic, not further interior parameter optimization.

Visual Exploration gives the constructive counterpart. VIS-313 replaces meaningless global gaps on a dense ratio spectrum by dyadic aggregate shells; VIS-314 then retains prime-pair sparsity inside each shell and removes one further logarithm by a classical upper-bound sieve. Enrichment is useful here because the aggregate representation keeps exactly the local arithmetic density that the previous pointwise-gap representation discarded.

Weil Positivity makes the completion audit global. WP-371's divergent diagonal tax is exact for independent sparse prime-ray completion, but WP-372 shows that arbitrary mixed-prime Fourier freedom collapses the global positive mass requirement from the divergent sum of local minima to their finite maximum. That mixed completion is nevertheless a universal matched control. The arithmetic question is not whether cross-prime coefficients can restore positivity, but whether the source **forces** a mixed-prime pattern whose positive sign and orientation are unavailable to the universal product completion.

Weil Inertia adds the complementary localization warning: shrinking the arithmetic aperture can simultaneously degrade defect certification and normalization. A more local representation is not automatically a stronger selector when its output remains population-normalized.

These examples refine one reusable audit. Identify the minimal source carrier after exact algebraic generation. Establish parameter provenance. Compute the physical inverse or nonautonomous transfer, including any preserved fibre coordinates. Solve the exact covariance/similarity equation before crediting a metric. If continuation is required, quotient by the canonical divisor and identify what freedom actually remains. If phase is used, couple it to the carrier weight. If sparse exceptions are used, price the required source relations through conductor. If finite-time averaging is used, aggregate dense spectrum before introducing pointwise gaps. If positivity is restored, solve the full global completion problem and then ask which mixed coefficients are source-forced.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem transferring Farey Floquet modes, Möbius codimension bounds, Nyman depth-phase controls, Prime-Circle similarities, Robin approximation geometry, Wang sieve bounds or Weil completion thresholds between lines. Each quantitative statement remains line-specific. The cross-line content is the falsifiable audit: enrichment deserves credit only when it introduces or preserves a source-selected relation that survives the exact downstream quotient and cannot be reproduced by the strongest matched control at the same quantifier.
