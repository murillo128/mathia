# MI-021 — Representation enrichment only buys leverage where source-specific coupling actually lives

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including [AF-384](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-384-strict-lower-order-positivity-collapses-order-k-to-solid-toeplitz-minors.md), [FD-159](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-159-truncated-auxiliary-down-sets-pay-average-fiber-leakage.md), [MC-335](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-335-low-degree-mode-dependence-cannot-approximate-dephasing.md), [NB-167](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-167-capped-poisson-transport-unifies-conditioning-and-wasserstein-conservation.md), [RE-169](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-169-mertens-normalization-turns-post-selector-ambiguity-into-remote-compensation-debt.md), [VIS-257](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-257-cusp-annihilation-exposes-finite-window-quadratic-conditioning.md), [WI-321](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-321-hilbert-valued-channelization-cannot-contract-scalar-weil-semibound.md), and [WP-335](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-335-max-divergence-and-channel-norm-convergence-still-miss-fixed-ucp-variance.md), together with the earlier line-specific chains they extend. The mechanisms differ; only the information-placement/resource-accounting relation is shared.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when the added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, an apparently drastic compression can be legitimate when an exact theorem proves that the discarded family lies in the closure/cone generated by retained data. The relevant question is therefore not “how much representation is present?” but **which carrier, metric, cone or invariant survives the source-to-destination map, and what hypothesis makes it complete?**

Arithmetic Fidelity now gives both sides in one line. AF-383 removes one Toeplitz placement tuple at fixed determinant order without losing target information. AF-384 removes the second tuple only after strict lower-order total positivity is assumed. The lesson is that generator compression has a price: a small test family is complete because algebraic generation or source-class interiority has already supplied the missing structure.

Farey Discrepancy shows the dual failure of apparent truncation. FD-159 replaces a complete auxiliary cube by any divisor-downward truncated core set and finds the exact invariant `bar f`, the average occupied primary fibre. The cut pressure survives as `P+L/|C|>=h bar f`. An incomplete representation is not weaker merely because coordinates are missing; it becomes weaker only when the missing coordinates reduce the effective fibre seen by the destination cut.

Möbius Cancellation makes programmability quantitative. Low rank and controlled total energy do not exclude the conjugate-character matched filter, and large centered variation is another independent cost. MC-335 adds Fourier degree of the mode-to-coefficient map: genuinely low degree cannot approximate the all-mode dephasing target, while near-maximal degree restores exact programmability. Structural complexity is useful only when it excludes the dangerous carrier in the coordinates natural to the source.

Nyman--Beurling shows that the right norm may be induced by the test class itself. Total variation overcharges locally paired signs and ordinary `W_1/a` overcharges distances beyond one Poisson width. NB-167's capped metric `min(1,|s-t|/a)` is exactly the resolution seen by the bounded/Lipschitz Poisson source kernels. Enrichment of the norm is useful only when it better matches what the destination can resolve.

Robin Extremal adds a global-invariant version of the same principle. RE-169 shows that the Robin kernel also measures the difference of Mertens normalizations. A local source modification that looks free under pointwise PNT fidelity becomes a future compensation debt once the Mertens constant is fixed at the critical `1/(sqrt p log p)` scale. Adding source information matters because it constrains the exact weighted carrier used by the destination, not because it is a generally stronger theorem about primes.

Visual Exploration shows that changing coordinates can expose the right obstruction without removing conditioning. VIS-257 annihilates the nonanalytic `|q|` cusp directly, but the sharp inequality `|m_2|<=(b^2/8)||nu||_TV` turns the surviving quadratic channel into a `b^(-2)` conditioning problem. A representation adapted to the singularity is valuable only after the first surviving smooth channel and its error amplification are priced.

Weil Inertia makes the representation/information distinction literal. WI-321 allows arbitrary Hilbert-valued diagonal amplification, yet polarization of the scalar semibound already generates the corresponding Loewner Gram inequality. More channels, phases and fixed PSD mixing remain inside the same admissible cone. A useful matrix state must contribute inequalities outside that scalar-generated cone.

Weil Positivity shows that even convergence topology can be representation-relative. WP-335 has transformed laws converging in two-sided max-divergence and channels converging in bounded-operator norm while the Kadison variance of a growing logarithmic observable stays fixed. The missing resource is not “stronger scalar convergence” but topology weighted by the actual observable energy.

These examples refine the audit into three steps. First identify the **minimal carrier** after exact algebraic generation, quotienting or polarization. Second identify the **metric/topology actually seen by the destination** on that carrier. Third state the **hypothesis cost** that makes a compressed carrier complete or excludes the matched control. Enrichment is substantive only if it changes one of these three objects.

This explains why some reductions are beneficial while some enrichments are not. AF-384 compresses because strict lower-order positivity activates a recognition theorem; FD-159 shows truncation without fibre thinning changes nothing; MC-335 shows low Fourier degree really does exclude the programmable endpoint filter; NB-167 and VIS-257 replace generic norms by destination-adapted ones but retain their exact conditioning bills; WI-321 and WP-335 show that coordinate/channel/topology enrichment can remain blind to the source-relevant energy.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem transferring Toeplitz recognition, Farey cuts, Walsh degree, capped transport, Mertens normalization, cusp duality, Weil localization or UCP stability between lines. Each quantitative statement remains line-specific. The cross-line content is the falsifiable resource test: before crediting a new representation, identify which effective carrier or destination norm it changes, what hypothesis pays for that change, and whether a matched construction can preserve the old carrier anyway.
