# MI-021 — Representation enrichment only buys leverage where source-specific coupling actually lives

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of exact/current results [AF-377](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-377-bounded-support-interaction-components-hide-finite-order-aliases.md), [MC-329](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-329-fixed-common-modulus-bias-energy-has-a-quartic-horizon.md), [WI-317](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-317-finite-localization-portfolios-cannot-contract-scalar-defect.md), and [WP-329](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-329-deterministic-amalgamated-lifts-make-operator-valued-free-divisibility-universal-before-scalarization.md). The four mechanisms are different; only the information-placement principle is shared.

Several current branches now exhibit the same structural failure in different mathematics: a construction can look richer by nominal dimension, arity, category or portfolio size while the source-sensitive interaction still factors through a much smaller object. The added degrees of freedom then rearrange or externalize information rather than create a new destination resource.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes this literal at the relation level. AF-377 shows that sampled total positivity of every nominal determinant order can factor through uniformly bounded blocks when the support-scale interaction graph has bounded components. Even `inf gap=0` does not imply growing effective arity. The information resource is the size and geometry of components that actually couple under the admissible source support, not the declared determinant order.

Weil Positivity gives the category/readout version. WP-329 places the exact Mangoldt law below a freely infinitely divisible operator-valued object whose enriched distribution is deterministic and identical for arbitrary scalar source laws; all specificity sits in the scalarization state. Enlarging the probability category therefore buys nothing unless arithmetic enters non-deterministic mixed data before readout.

Weil Inertia gives the portfolio version. WI-317 allows finitely many exact source localizations and even lets the best localizer be chosen after seeing the test vector, yet no scalar defect contracts if every localized piece is controlled only by the same original scalar semibound. Exact decomposition and portfolio choice do not strengthen the premise; simultaneous recurrence exposes a common extremizing sequence.

Möbius Cancellation gives the family-statistic version. MC-329 compresses the full reciprocal endpoint character family into one nonnegative bias-energy statistic and reaches the same quartic source floor as the independent incidence/conductor architecture. More modes do not create leverage when their contribution is summarized by a positive statistic whose analytic range already saturates at the destination exponent.

The reusable audit is therefore: **locate the smallest object in which source-specific coupling remains after all exact factorization, scalarization, localization and positive aggregation.** Count complexity there, not in the ambient representation. A proposed enrichment is potentially useful only if the new coordinates interact through a source-forced relation that survives to the destination and cannot be reproduced by bounded components, an arbitrary readout state, the same scalar premise on every piece, or a positive aggregate already at its analytic horizon.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem transferring AF interaction graphs to operator-valued probability, localization, or character families. Each obstruction is line-specific. The shared conclusion is only an audit principle about effective information placement: nominal richness is not a resource until a source-specific coupling across the added degrees of freedom is both proved and consumed by the destination.
