# MI-021 — Representation enrichment only buys leverage where source-specific coupling survives closure and matched controls

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including [AF-387](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-387-connected-support-does-not-repair-nonstrict-solid-pf3-recognition.md), [FD-162](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-162-first-omitted-anchor-prime-forces-squarefree-volume-closure-pressure.md), [MC-337](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-337-odd-source-parity-all-or-nothing-self-phase-barrier.md), [NB-170](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-170-bounded-variation-height-adaptation-pays-a-poisson-resolution-refresh-tariff.md), [PC-324](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-324-one-residue-green-controls-have-classical-hilbert-edge-zeros.md), [RE-171](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-171-exact-mertens-matching-still-permits-transient-post-selector-robin-excursions.md), [VIS-257](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-257-cusp-annihilation-exposes-finite-window-quadratic-conditioning.md), [WI-321](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-321-hilbert-valued-channelization-cannot-contract-scalar-weil-semibound.md), and [WP-338](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-338-quadratic-wasserstein-is-the-sharp-ucp-defect-threshold.md), together with the earlier line-specific chains they extend. The mechanisms differ; only the information-placement/resource-accounting relation is shared.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when the added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, an apparently drastic compression can be legitimate when an exact theorem proves that discarded data are generated by retained data under the destination's admissible closure. The relevant question is not “how much representation is present?” but **which carrier, metric, cone or invariant remains after the destination performs every free closure/reconstruction operation, and what source hypothesis makes the survivor selective?**

Arithmetic Fidelity gives both compression and its boundary. AF-383 removes one Toeplitz placement tuple at fixed determinant order, and AF-385 removes the second under a strict lower-order hierarchy. AF-386 shows that at order two connected support can replace strictness. AF-387 proves that support topology and lower-order interiority are separate at order three: a connected `PF_2` sequence can satisfy every solid `3x3` inequality and still fail a gapped `3x3` minor because zero lower-order minors inside the support block sign propagation. A compressed generator family is complete only after the exact zero-minor stratum needed by its recognition theorem has been paid for.

Farey Discrepancy shows the dual effect of intrinsic closure. FD-159 prices any divisor-downward truncated core by its average occupied primary fibre. FD-160 shows arbitrary sparse holes can be internalized by divisor closure. FD-162 then identifies a canonical volume already forced by an arbitrary finite prime anchor: if `ell_S` is the first omitted prime, half of every nontrivial principal closure sees all squarefree primary products below `ell_S`. Thus anchor families that exhaust every fixed prime accumulate `Theta(m log m)` closure pressure. High-prime holes do not remain holes under the destination's principal-ideal accounting.

Möbius Cancellation makes reconstruction cost quotient-relative. MC-335 shows low Fourier degree can exclude the matched filter in standard coordinates; MC-336 reveals that source coordinates make that filter degree one unless own-phase access is forbidden. MC-337 then shows literal exclusion is still insufficient as an invariant: for odd source rank the global parity relation determines `x_i` from the other phases. What protects the architecture below maximal degree is not non-observability but the `R-1` degree needed to reconstruct the hidden phase. Complexity must therefore be measured after exact source relations are quotiented out.

Nyman--Beurling shows that representation complexity itself must be measured at source resolution. NB-167 identifies capped Poisson transport as the metric seen by the bounded/Lipschitz source kernels; NB-169 charges each distinguishable height refresh. NB-170 quantizes a bounded-variation template path in that same metric and shows that its effective number of representations is only `1+V_T H_T/log T`. Continuous or infinitely frequent microscopic changes buy nothing until cumulative motion crosses the `q_T log T/H_T` source-resolution threshold. Adaptation is substantive only through the path length the source can actually resolve, not through nominal update count.

Prime Circle shows why moving from an absolute object to a relative one is not enough. PC-323 subtracts the universal Hilbert continuous core and leaves trace-class relative data, but PC-324 constructs genuine off-band relative-determinant zeros already in one-residue non-arithmetic controls. Those zeros are classical generalized-Hilbert edge eigenvalues controlled only by a residue offset. The source-sensitive carrier, if any, must therefore survive subtraction **and** the matched one-channel relative spectrum; multi-residue interference is the next unclosed object, not relative zeros in general.

Robin Extremal exhausts one scalar invariant completely. RE-169 identifies Mertens normalization as the exact total Robin-kernel charge. RE-171 shows that even exact `Gamma(Q)=gamma` can coexist with an order-one transient selected-prefix excursion and exact later repayment under negligible KV-relative perturbation. Adding precision to the invariant cannot help because the invariant is already exact; the missing carrier is spatial localization of its signed debt.

Visual Exploration shows that changing coordinates can expose the right obstruction without removing conditioning. VIS-257 annihilates the nonanalytic `|q|` cusp directly, but the sharp inequality `|m_2|<=(b^2/8)||nu||_TV` turns the surviving quadratic channel into a `b^(-2)` conditioning problem. A representation adapted to the singularity is valuable only after the first surviving smooth channel and its error amplification are priced.

Weil Inertia makes the representation/information distinction literal. WI-321 allows arbitrary Hilbert-valued diagonal amplification, yet polarization of the scalar semibound already generates the corresponding Loewner Gram inequality. More channels, phases and fixed PSD mixing remain inside the same admissible cone. A useful matrix state must contribute inequalities outside that scalar-generated cone.

Weil Positivity closes the analogous topology escalation. WP-336--WP-337 identify the geometric amplifiers that let tiny channel/barycentric errors retain fixed variance. WP-338 shows the invariant they are trying to control exactly: `W_2(P_x,delta_x)^2=K(x)+beta_x^2`. Every subquadratic `W_p` can vanish while `K` stays fixed on growing supports, whereas `W_2` works by directly measuring the desired second-moment quantity. A stronger topology is not a new mechanism when it merely assumes the destination defect in metric form.

These examples refine the audit into four steps. First identify the **minimal carrier** after exact algebraic generation, quotienting, polarization or universal-background subtraction. Second apply the **intrinsic closure/reconstruction** available to the destination and ask what information is regenerated for free. Third identify the **metric/topology and resolution actually seen by the destination** on the remaining carrier. Fourth state the **source hypothesis or matched-control separation** that makes that survivor selective rather than universal, tautological or cheaply reproducible.

This explains why some reductions are beneficial while some enrichments are not. AF compresses only after paying the correct interiority/zero-pattern hypothesis; FD closure can regenerate supposedly omitted anchor information; MC variable hiding matters only through reconstruction degree; NB continuous adaptation matters only through source-resolvable path variation; PC relative spectrum remains nonselective until one-channel Hilbert controls are removed; RE exact scalar normalization still lacks localization; WI channelization remains in a scalar-generated cone; and WP quadratic transport is the desired defect written as a metric.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem transferring Toeplitz recognition, Farey cuts, source-phase parity, Poisson-resolution variation, generalized-Hilbert spectrum, Mertens compensation, cusp duality, Weil localization or UCP transport between lines. Each quantitative statement remains line-specific. The cross-line content is the falsifiable resource test: before crediting a new representation, identify what survives the destination's free closure/reconstruction operations, what topology and resolution price it at the correct moving scale, and which matched control or source theorem proves that the survivor carries information unavailable to a cheaper model.
