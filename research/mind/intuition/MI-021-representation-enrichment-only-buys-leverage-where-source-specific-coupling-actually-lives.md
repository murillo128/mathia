# MI-021 — Representation enrichment only buys leverage where source-specific coupling survives the final projection and topology

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including AF-416--AF-434, FD-200--FD-216, MC-382--MC-396, NB-217--NB-229, PC-351--PC-359, PL-361--PL-375, RE-216--RE-228, VIS-302--VIS-317, WI-350--WI-356 and WP-366--WP-373. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, enrichment is useful only when a new relation has an exact source preimage, survives the destination operation and improves a load-bearing bottleneck without repaying the gain elsewhere. The relevant question is not how large the representation is, but **what source-specific relation survives acquisition, closure, inversion, symmetry, positivity, normalization, projection and the topology in which the final estimate is taken?**

Arithmetic Fidelity adds a provenance gate before any mesh audit. AF-433--AF-434 show that source-side realization and source-to-packet transport are different obligations: the square amplitudes can coincide with source singularity shells while the determinant still sums shell labels into zeta factors before partition packets form. A rich target geometry is not source-discriminating until selection and transport both survive.

Farey Discrepancy closes a stronger class than fixed-filter enrichment. FD-212 produces a supercritical Floquet blind mode from individually harmless bounded-depth filters, FD-213 lifts it to every integer because dyadic dilation preserves mantissa, and FD-214--FD-215 preserve squarefree ternary fidelity plus every fixed finite family of exact Euler sign laws. FD-216 then shows that even `Theta(log N)` exact prime relations can be absorbed **horizon by horizon** while the switched sensor remains sub-square-root. What finally changes the source class is not the number of constraints but same-source compatibility across scales: one fixed source obeying an exhaustive nested Euler family is forced to be Möbius.

Prime Circle pushes the similarity audit beyond tensor metrics. PC-358--PC-359 show that breaking bounded parity similarity is necessary but not sufficient: an infinite completion may make the reciprocal intertwiner unbounded while compactifying positive-degree transport so strongly that norm-convergent grade-raising multipliers have trivial spectrum. Metric or completion enrichment matters only if it both changes the bounded intertwiner structure and retains noncompact destination-visible transport.

Prime Lattice removes the first finite-dimensional matrix loophole. Complete multiplicativity gives scalar composition factors; distinct `2x2` extensions split and nonvanishing self-extensions are completely additive tangent jets. A distinct route must leave the scalar filtration in a way that changes the global theorem interface rather than merely thickening it by finite-dimensional extension data.

Nyman--Beurling shows why a stronger-looking coordinate can remain separable from the true carrier and why the formal parameter range can itself be misleading. The fixed-power phase no-go survives arbitrary predetermined depth-only improvements because matched packets can trade population against depth. NB-228 identifies the nonseparable object actually consumed by the residue: the nested cumulative depth-phase carrier paired with Ford damping. NB-229 then uses the smooth vertical carrier to prove that depths beyond `O(log Q)` are already absolutely negligible, so zeta-specific cancellation is needed only in the moving shallow range `1<<D<<log Q`. Useful information must therefore couple the variables in the same way as the destination functional **and be proved only on the part of that carrier the destination does not already suppress for free**.

Möbius Cancellation closes the loop between representation complexity and physical source cost, then shows why the internal representation of that cost must itself be audited. MC-392--MC-394 force essentially one source-cost unit per rank below the logarithmic horizon. MC-395 seemed to add an inverse-gap differencing depth, but MC-396 proves that factor was a greedy-packing artifact: pairing consecutive blocks leaves only `O(A)` stages and sharpens the unpaid rank to `O(log R)` up to a fixed positive fraction of `log log y`. Enrichment deserves no penalty merely because a lossy intermediate decomposition makes the proof look expensive.

Robin Extremal shows that phase enrichment and exact normalization cancellation can still be absorbed inside the same representation cost. RE-226--RE-227 close the positive-real fixed-corridor single-center class above rate two. RE-228 then allows one genuinely complex fixed center and treats the normalized polynomial with its exact complex cancellation; the factor-two Wiener floor still survives because cancellation in the DC denominator can only make the normalized coefficient norm larger. The remaining complex escape is therefore a quantifier/geometry change—drifting centers or a different predictor class—not a fixed phase added to the same single-center negative-binomial representation.

Visual Exploration gives both a constructive enrichment and a projection warning. Dyadic aggregate shells retain the arithmetic density discarded by meaningless pointwise gaps, and the Wang taper prevents remote shell maxima from being overpaid. But VIS-317 shows that the further projection `(q,r)->h=qv-ur` is too aggressive for the prime-pair sieve: it preserves ratio-cell geometry while every odd residue of `h` remains compatible with both prime coordinates nonzero. The scalar coordinate keeps the geometry and loses the sieve dimension. A useful representation must retain both.

Weil Positivity makes the completion audit global and topological. WP-371's divergent local tax disappears under the mixed-prime positive completion of WP-372, so local completion costs were not fundamental. WP-373 then shows that one cannot simply recover sparse prime-power support afterward with a continuous scalar common-line multiplier: the unwanted mixed frequencies are dense and force any such multiplier to vanish identically. Every finite truncation nevertheless admits a smooth exact selector. Thus both the completion class and the topology of the infinite quotient are load-bearing.

Weil Inertia adds the complementary topology lesson at an arithmetic activation threshold. WI-355 replaces a defect-normalized pair statistic by the full localized Weil ground energy, making every RH failure a finite-radius variational event. WI-356 then shows that a newly activated prime-power channel is maximally non-small in ambient `L^2` operator norm but tends to zero relative to Suzuki's endpoint logarithmic form. The right enrichment is not “more localization” abstractly; it is the topology that prices the concentration required to realize the bad ambient-norm witness.

These examples refine one reusable audit. Identify the minimal source carrier after exact algebraic generation. Establish parameter provenance. Compute the physical inverse or nonautonomous transfer, including preserved fibre coordinates. Before scalar projection, check which local arithmetic exclusions survive. Solve covariance/similarity before crediting a metric. Audit any normalization map before crediting cancellation. If continuation is required, quotient by the canonical divisor and identify what freedom remains. If phase is used, couple it to the carrier weight and use the carrier's own localization to discard inactive parameter ranges before demanding cancellation. If sparse exceptions are used, price source relations through conductor and audit the internal factorization that creates the analytic tariff. If finite-time averaging is used, aggregate dense spectrum before introducing pointwise gaps. If positivity is restored, solve the full global completion problem and then audit the topology of any selector back to sparse support. At threshold crossings, use the form in which the source channel is actually small rather than an ambient norm in which it is not.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem transferring Farey Floquet modes, Möbius source-cost bounds, Nyman depth-phase controls, Prime-Circle similarities, Robin approximation geometry, Wang sieve obstructions, Weil threshold estimates or positive-completion no-go results between lines. Each quantitative statement remains line-specific. The cross-line content is the falsifiable audit: enrichment deserves credit only when it introduces or preserves a source-selected relation that survives the exact downstream quotient **in the topology and normalization required by the final theorem** and cannot be reproduced by the strongest matched control at the same quantifier.
