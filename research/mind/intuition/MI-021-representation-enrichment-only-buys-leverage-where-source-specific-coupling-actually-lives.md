# MI-021 — Representation enrichment only buys leverage where source-specific coupling actually lives

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of exact/current results [AF-377](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-377-bounded-support-interaction-components-hide-finite-order-aliases.md)--[AF-380](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-380-prime-gap-bounds-give-finite-prime-log-negativity-witnesses.md), [FD-155](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-155-thinning-finite-prime-avoidance-frame-has-double-exponential-parent-load-cost.md), [MC-329](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-329-fixed-common-modulus-bias-energy-has-a-quartic-horizon.md)--[MC-331](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-331-common-coefficient-prefilters-cannot-cross-quartic-horizon.md), [NB-164](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-164-growing-moment-cancellation-forces-linear-poisson-width-support-or-exponential-conditioning.md), [WI-317](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-317-finite-localization-portfolios-cannot-contract-scalar-defect.md)--[WI-318](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-318-bounded-h1-adaptive-localizers-cannot-contract-scalar-defect.md), and [WP-329](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-329-deterministic-amalgamated-lifts-make-operator-valued-free-divisibility-universal-before-scalarization.md)--[WP-332](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-332-faithful-exact-law-ucp-readouts-force-multiplicative-domain-rigidity.md). The mechanisms differ; only the information-placement and resource-accounting principle is shared.

Several current branches exhibit the same structural distinction: a construction can become richer by nominal dimension, arity, category, coordinate geometry, filter complexity, moment order, noise content, portfolio size, or source coordinates while the source-sensitive interaction still factors through a smaller object, is killed by the destination map, or is purchased at a hidden resource cost. Added degrees of freedom become genuine leverage only when they change the effective source coupling **before** the destination-relevant compression and survive to the final observable with a controlled bill.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives both sides exactly. AF-377 shows that arbitrary nominal determinant order can factor through uniformly bounded support-interaction components; AF-378 shows that faithful nonlinear coordinate transport can make displayed rows look denser without changing the sampled fibre. AF-379 supplies the positive closure criterion: dense normalized row patterns really do determine continuous translation positivity. AF-380 then prices finite visibility of that determination by target margin, sample covering radius, kernel regularity, and determinant sensitivity. Representation richness is useful only when it changes the actual target relation, and even exact determination can remain badly conditioned at finite scale.

Farey Discrepancy gives a cost version. Adding avoided-prime coordinates can reduce anchored source mass, but FD-155 shows that in the direct coordinate-face frame the required depth grows essentially exponentially in the inverse exposure scale while the best parent load grows double exponentially. More coordinates alter the source fibre, but they are not cheap leverage unless the architecture's conditioning/load is priced simultaneously.

Möbius Cancellation now closes both downstream and the simplest upstream filter escapes around the same `ell^2` compression. MC-329 compresses the complete fixed-common-modulus character family into total bias energy and reaches the quartic source floor. MC-330 proves that arbitrary signed linear and quadratic processing **after** that compression has no normalized advantage over the energy itself. MC-331 moves one step earlier: even an arbitrary common complex coefficient vector on the shell primes, applied before the character-family transform, has endpoint operator norm `2^R A/R`, while the available prime Halász--Montgomery theorem charges exactly the same coefficient `ell^2` norm. The unweighted endpoint is already asymptotically extremal, so a common prefilter or any character-cube convolution still cannot move the quartic horizon.

The Möbius lesson sharpens “act before compression” into a stronger test: **acting earlier is not enough if the next theorem immediately quotients the new structure back to the same norm**. A surviving linear escape must retain mode-dependent or genuinely joint prime--character data and use an analytic estimate sensitive to that joint structure, rather than one common coefficient vector whose norm factors out. Higher-order information, different exponent geometry, or a changed representation are other possible currencies.

Nyman--Beurling gives the approximation-complexity version. NB-164 turns growing local moment order into an exact target-side capacity problem: after annihilating all polynomials below degree `r`, a signed sampler confined to a fixed number of Poisson widths sees the Poisson target only through a geometrically small Chebyshev quotient norm. Fixed target visibility then requires exponentially growing total variation; bounded conditioning forces support growth at least linear in `r` in Poisson units. More vanishing moments are therefore not a free source resource: before any arithmetic phase cancellation is consulted, the target itself charges either nonlocality or conditioning for the added formal order.

Weil Inertia gives the portfolio-complexity version. WI-317 shows that finitely many exact source localizations cannot contract a scalar defect when every localized term receives only the same scalar premise. WI-318 removes portfolio infinity and adaptivity as generic escapes for unit-window families with bounded total `H^1`: uniform Riemann--Lebesgue decay plus a family-independent first-prime autocorrelation gap yields one recurrence sequence defeating the entire family. Cardinality is not the resource; a reused scalar premise needs spectrally noncompact or genuinely new localized/non-scalar information.

Weil Positivity gives the readout-category version. WP-329 can store all arithmetic specificity in a scalar state below a universal deterministic operator-valued law. WP-330 can add genuine covariance while hiding it in a scalarization-null ideal. WP-331 shows that faithful exact-law visibility collapses conditional-expectation noise. WP-332 proves that conditional expectation was not essential: for any UCP readout, exact preservation of the scalar second moment under a faithful output state forces zero Kadison defect, puts the source observable in the multiplicative domain, and kills its off-diagonal Stinespring coupling to the dilation complement. A larger dilation or more general positive compression is therefore not source leverage when the same observable must remain an exact noiseless readout.

The reusable audit is: **locate the smallest object in which source-specific coupling remains before every factorization, norm bound, approximation quotient, compression, or readout; then ask what the next theorem can actually see of that object.** A proposed enrichment is useful when it creates a determining closure, a mode/source-dependent correlation, a new response observable, a genuinely stronger local invariant, or another destination-visible coupling whose cost is controlled. It is not useful merely because the enrichment occurs earlier, increases nominal complexity, or adds hidden geometry that the next positive/norm/readout/approximation step immediately erases.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem transferring AF pattern closure to Farey ancestry, character sums, Nyman samplers, Weil localization, or operator-valued probability. Each mechanism and quantitative cost is line-specific. The shared conclusion is an information-placement test: nominal richness becomes a resource only if source-specific coupling survives the exact map actually consumed downstream and the analytic estimate is sensitive to that coupling rather than to the same universal quotient or norm already known to saturate.
