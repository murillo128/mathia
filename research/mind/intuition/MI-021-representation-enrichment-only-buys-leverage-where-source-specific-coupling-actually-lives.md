# MI-021 — Representation enrichment only buys leverage where source-specific coupling survives acquisition, closure, inverse geometry and matched controls

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including FD-200--FD-204, MC-382--MC-385, NB-217--NB-219, PC-351--PC-353, PL-361--PL-366, RE-216--RE-217, VIS-302--VIS-305, WI-351--WI-352 and WP-366--WP-367. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, enrichment can be useful when new degrees of freedom have exact source preimages and improve a destination bottleneck without repaying the gain elsewhere. The relevant question is not “how much representation is present?” but **what source-specific relation survives acquisition, closure/globalization, topology, normalization, inverse recovery and matched controls?**

Farey Discrepancy gives the cleanest constructive example. FD-200 shows that matched horizon-mode enlargement can be exact divisor transport and add no information. FD-202 applies signed adjacent-prime differencing before squaring, collapsing common source bulk and reducing the random-multiplicative tariff to the critical `H` scale. FD-203 then shows that cheap kernel closure leaves large path resistance. FD-204 changes the graph rather than the source: a lowbit tree uses exact nonlocal source-slab differences, reuses each adjacent slab only logarithmically, and reduces anchored resistance from `Theta(m)` to `Theta(log m)` while the random cost remains `H^(1+o(1))`. Here representation enrichment is valuable because the added edges improve inverse geometry and their source cost is explicitly controlled.

Prime Circle supplies the automorphism-quotient warning in two stages. PC-351 shows that source-faithful noncommutative data can become blind under a spectral quotient. PC-352 shows that even a full semisimple target remains control-blind when reciprocal parity is inner. PC-353 then makes the parity outer and genuinely separates conjugacy classes, but the only forced spectral change is contragredient duality `D -> D^-T`, shared by every admissible reciprocal control. Escaping one quotient obstruction can therefore expose only a generic orientation rather than a source-specific discriminator.

Prime Lattice adds an ambient-norm warning. Finite signs can cross one convergence wall, unrestricted infinite signs can program arbitrary targets, and `ell^1` restores rigidity only as limit-periodicity. PL-366 shows that pole-neutral `ell^2` filters automatically produce square-root summatory control and the half-plane `Re(s)>1/2` by Cauchy--Schwarz. A richer function space can therefore generate a critical-looking exponent without adding any zero-selective arithmetic relation.

Visual Exploration shows the matched-control version. Exact coefficient and support specialization move the generic mean-value wall repeatedly, but VIS-305 constructs a parity- and density-matched Bernoulli support whose absolute reciprocal-spacing norm already has the same `x log log x` order as the prime-power upper bound. What remains source-specific is the signed endpoint form or arithmetic correlation absent from that control, not the sparse-spacing norm itself.

Nyman--Beurling separates exact representation algebra from the proof interface and now shows that some apparent global complexity can be removed without decoupling. NB-218 proves that the primitive/reconstruction factors cancel exactly inside a signed convolution. NB-219 multiplies its Fourier kernel by a smooth compact vertical window and transfers that operation back to a superalgebraically small source perturbation on `Re(s)>1`. The full vertical tail is therefore not an intrinsic representation cost; the live question is whether the localized coupling survives contour transport across `Re(s)=1`.

Weil Inertia shows that formal infinite-dimensionality can collapse at destination resolution and that apparent resource budgets can be dependent. Weil Positivity strengthens the globalization warning. WP-366 shows that fixed-height Whittaker `nY` incidence is factorized by canonical scalar dilation averaging. WP-367 broadens this: any common positive-semidefinite radial form with exact dilation homogeneity factors the finite cutoff into an archimedean scalar times a Rankin--Selberg kernel. Sophistication inside the radial form does not help when the same homogeneous operation is applied componentwise.

Möbius Cancellation adds the theorem-interface analogue. Exponential family size, strong terminal saving and small ambient exceptional volume can all fail to buy source reach if their conductor tariff, admission range or exceptional geometry is misaligned with the actual endpoint source-difference family.

These examples refine the audit into a compact sequence. Identify the minimal source carrier after exact algebraic generation. Ask whether acquisition creates a new coordinate or transports old data. Apply the destination's canonical closure, summation or globalization. State the topology and ambient norm and test whether they already explain the apparent critical scale. Price the inverse geometry needed to recover the witness. Pull theorem hypotheses and exceptional sets back to the actual source image. Determine whether apparent global domains can be localized as negligible source perturbations before estimation. Finally run the strongest matched control preserving all generic operations.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem transferring Farey graph design, character-family estimates, zeta-primitive convolution, reciprocal `SL_3` duality, lattice filtering, Wang mean values or Whittaker averaging between lines. Each quantitative statement remains line-specific. The cross-line content is the falsifiable audit: enrichment deserves credit only when newly acquired or retained structure survives the exact destination operations, has a controlled inverse/source cost, is not merely an ambient norm or automorphism artifact, and defeats the relevant matched control.
