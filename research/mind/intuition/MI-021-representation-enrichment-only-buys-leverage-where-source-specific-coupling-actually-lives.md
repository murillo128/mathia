# MI-021 — Representation enrichment only buys leverage where source-specific coupling actually lives

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including [AF-383](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-383-vanishing-lattice-meshes-reduce-continuum-total-nonnegativity-to-one-axis-toeplitz-generators.md), [FD-158](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-158-finite-auxiliary-prime-boxes-replicate-the-cross-core-leakage-obstruction.md), [MC-334](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-334-exact-dephasing-requires-matched-filter-scale-mode-variation.md), [WI-321](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-321-hilbert-valued-channelization-cannot-contract-scalar-weil-semibound.md), and [WP-334](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-334-finite-information-divergences-miss-bounded-likelihood-ucp-tail-defects.md), together with the earlier line-specific chains they extend. The mechanisms differ; only the information-placement/resource-accounting relation is shared.

Across several Mathia lines, nominal enrichment keeps failing for the same precise reason: the added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest object through which the destination-relevant source coupling factors. Dimension, arity, coordinate count, channel count or stronger-looking scalar topology becomes useful only when it changes that effective coupling **before** the decisive quotient/compression and survives in the norm the destination actually consumes.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives an exact positive example of legitimate compression rather than enrichment. AF-383 proves that, at each determinant order, every lattice Toeplitz minor lies in the nonnegative cone generated by minors with one consecutive row stencil and arbitrary columns. One entire placement tuple can therefore be discarded without information loss, while arbitrary columns and unbounded determinant order remain load-bearing. What matters is the generator cone of the target relations, not their nominal count.

Farey Discrepancy gives the complementary failure of finite coordinate enrichment. FD-157 says that cheap anchored expansion must cross the conserved `S`-free core. FD-158 then enlarges the vocabulary by any finite auxiliary prime set `U` and completes its Boolean box. The obstruction simply tensorizes: packet volume and available anchor states both acquire the same `2^|U|` factor, leaving `P+2^(-|U|)L>=h(2^|S|-1)`. More coordinates do not buy leverage when they only replicate the same conserved-fibre cut.

Möbius Cancellation shows why low-dimensional structure is also not enough. MC-332's dangerous conjugate-character matched filter already has rank only `R`. MC-333 prices rank against total Frobenius energy, while MC-334 proves that the uniform all-mode target lies almost entirely outside the image of a common prime vector: every exact dephaser needs `(1-o(1))A(2^R-1)` variation away from the best common input. The meaningful question is not whether a joint class is low-rank, but whether the matched filter is expensive under **all** source-natural restrictions charged by the analytic theorem.

Weil Inertia makes the representation/information distinction literal. WI-321 allows arbitrary finite complex Hilbert-valued channelization, phases and unitary channel mixing. On the same prime-free short witness as WI-320, Cauchy--Schwarz forces `Re R_G(a)<=1`, so every channelization still has nonnegative localization defect if each component receives only the same scalar semibound. More channels are just more copies of the old admissible set. A useful vector state must introduce cross-channel information not reducible to componentwise scalar bounds.

Weil Positivity shows that even a stronger-looking scalar topology can be the wrong enrichment. WP-334 keeps source and transformed laws mutually absolutely continuous with uniformly bounded likelihood ratios, while every fixed finite `f`-divergence and finite Renyi divergence tends to zero around an order-one UCP tail defect. The missing resource is worst-case control on the rare moving atom: max-divergence remains order one. Averaging the same hidden coupling with a more sophisticated scalar functional does not make it visible.

These examples refine the audit into two steps. First identify the **minimal carrier** of the source-sensitive relation after exact algebraic generation, conserved fibres, common-input projections, channel amplification or scalarization. Then identify the **cost/topology actually seen by the destination** on that carrier. Enrichment is substantive only if it changes one of those two objects. Otherwise it is a reparameterization, tensor replication, programmable matched filter, duplicated scalar premise or averaged shadow of the same unresolved coupling.

This also explains why some reductions are beneficial while some enrichments are not. AF-383 removes redundant relations by an exact positive-generation theorem, preserving the target. FD-158 and WI-321 add representational degrees of freedom that leave the obstruction invariant. MC-334 and WP-334 reveal hidden norms/topologies in which the dangerous coupling remains large even though rank or averaged divergence looks small. The correct resource ledger is therefore determined by invariants of the source-to-destination map, not by raw representation size.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem transferring Toeplitz generators to Farey graphs, character dephasing, Weil localization or UCP readouts. Each quantitative statement remains line-specific. The cross-line content is the falsifiable information-placement criterion: before crediting an enrichment, exhibit the exact source-sensitive coupling it changes and the destination norm/topology that detects that change; otherwise test whether a matched construction preserves the old effective carrier unchanged.
