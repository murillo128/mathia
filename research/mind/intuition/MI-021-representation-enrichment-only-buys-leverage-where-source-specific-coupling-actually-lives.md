# MI-021 — Representation enrichment only buys leverage where source-specific coupling survives the final projection, quotient and topology

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including AF-416--AF-436, FD-200--FD-218, MC-382--MC-397, NB-217--NB-230, PC-351--PC-361, PL-361--PL-377, RE-216--RE-230, VIS-302--VIS-319, WI-350--WI-358 and WP-366--WP-375. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, enrichment is useful only when a new relation has an exact source preimage, survives the destination operation and improves a load-bearing bottleneck without repaying the gain elsewhere. The relevant question is not how large the representation is, but **what source-specific relation survives acquisition, selection, transport, inversion, symmetry, positivity, normalization, projection and the topology/quantifier in which the final estimate is taken?**

Arithmetic Fidelity now distinguishes exact source information from downstream compression. Coefficient-root neutrality selects the Bernoulli radius, while AF-436 proves that the exact even coefficient sequence retains the whole radial shell ladder at exponentially separated residual scales. The current determinant nevertheless aggregates those shells into zeta factors before packet geometry. Information can therefore be present at the source and still disappear through a lossy representation, especially when stable recovery requires exponentially fine precision.

Farey Discrepancy makes overlap geometry part of the quantifier. One-horizon exact Euler coverage below any fixed fraction remains blind, and FD-218 shows that even one same source shared by horizons `N` and `2N` remains blind for every fixed cutoff fraction below `1/2`. Rigidity begins when the later exact-prime window reaches the entire earlier prefix. Persistence is useful only to the extent that moving source constraints actually overlap the object being tested.

Möbius Cancellation adds an averaging audit. Source rank/codimension is a real resource, but MC-397 shows that uniform averaging over the source-mode label can Fourier-collapse exactly to a signature subgroup projector. A formal representation dimension is not an extra cancellation variable if exact algebra has already dualized it into a nonnegative residue condition.

Prime Circle and Prime Lattice close two more “make it bigger” routes. PC-361 shows that a proper unbounded grade Hamiltonian plus a bounded strictly raising source term keeps exactly the diagonal eigenvalue spectrum: unboundedness does not help when source data remain triangular. PL-377 shows that bounded normal infinite-dimensional complete multiplicativity generates an abelian C*-algebra, so every bounded linear observable is a measure mixture of scalar semicharacters. Infinite dimension does not help when the source algebra remains abelian-normal.

Nyman--Beurling continues to show why enrichment must respect the destination's active capacity scale. After retaining the exact Ford carrier and damping, the dangerous horizontal depth is logarithmic in the vertical capacity `1+R/H`, not uniformly logarithmic in ambient height. A theorem over a larger formal range is not useful enrichment if the physical carrier has already paid it away.

Robin Extremal now closes scale-dependent complex single centers inside fixed corridor geometry. Bounded center drift remains Wiener-expensive; unbounded center drift makes the microscopic predictor revert to the raw delay unless the truncation/corridor width grows proportionally to the center modulus. A changing parameter is not a new resource when its only escape requires paying the same change in another physical budget.

Visual Exploration supplies the constructive counterpart. The Bezout chart of VIS-319 is unimodular and preserves both prime exclusions exactly, restoring the local dimension-two sieve factor while using the transverse determinant coordinate as the long direction. Here representation enrichment genuinely helps because the new coordinates preserve the source exclusions **and** the aggregation length; the remaining problem is an analytic thin-domain distribution theorem, not missing source information.

Weil Inertia sharpens the topology lesson. WI-357 gives threshold continuity on actual null states because the null equation controls logarithmic energy, but WI-358 shows that the same energy budget alone is too weak for cofinal threshold-by-threshold continuity. The useful topology is state-selected; global progress still needs source-specific coherence or positive reserve.

Weil Positivity sharpens the radical audit. WP-375 removes stationarity from the earlier radial no-go: exact mixed-shell nullity in any closable positive form on the canonical logarithmic-radial `L^2` topology propagates to the prime-power shells themselves. Positivity plus exact nullity and topology can erase the arithmetic carrier even when the form is nonstationary.

These examples refine one reusable audit. Identify the minimal source carrier after exact algebraic generation. Establish parameter provenance and the carrier's resolution scale. Verify that the representation transports rather than averages away that carrier. Before crediting a new dimension, check whether Fourier/Gelfand/triangular reduction scalarizes it. Before crediting an operator enlargement, locate the source term relative to invariant gradings and domains. Before crediting positivity, compute the radical generated by exact nullity in the actual completion topology. Before crediting persistence, quantify overlap of the source constraints on the destination object. Finally, price only the variables and ranges that survive those exact reductions.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem transferring Farey overlap thresholds, Möbius source-mode projectors, Nyman carrier-width estimates, Prime-Circle triangular spectral blindness, Prime-Lattice C*-scalarization, Robin prediction bounds, Wang thin-strip geometry, Weil threshold estimates or positive-radical no-go results between lines. Each quantitative statement remains line-specific. The cross-line content is the falsifiable audit: enrichment deserves credit only when it introduces or preserves a source-selected relation that survives the exact downstream quotient **with the arithmetic coordinates, topology, positivity structure, active capacity scale and quantifier required by the final theorem**, and cannot be reproduced by the strongest matched control.
