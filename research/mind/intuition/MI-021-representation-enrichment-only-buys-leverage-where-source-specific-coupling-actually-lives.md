# MI-021 — Representation enrichment buys leverage only when source-specific coupling survives identifiability, exact reduction and the destination quantifier

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including AF-416--AF-443, FD-200--FD-223, MC-382--MC-404, NB-217--NB-235, PC-351--PC-361, PL-361--PL-382, RE-216--RE-236, VIS-302--VIS-325, WI-350--WI-359 and WP-366--WP-378. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, enrichment is useful only when a new relation has an exact source preimage, is identifiable on the admissible source class, survives the destination operation, is recoverable at the needed precision/resolution, and improves a load-bearing bottleneck without repaying the gain elsewhere. The relevant question is not how large the representation is, but **what source-specific relation survives source restriction, acquisition, exact algebraic reduction, inversion, renormalization, globalization and the topology/quantifier of the final theorem?**

Arithmetic Fidelity now makes the identifiability gate dimensional. AF-439--AF-442 separate movable support, rigid reciprocal-square support, realized provenance and one-coordinate compensation. AF-443 proves that any fixed `K` reciprocal-power moments can still hide an arbitrarily remote mark when `K` earlier support coordinates remain independently movable. Observation richness therefore counts only when effective rank outruns or constrains the latent nuisance dimension; equal rank can still leave a local fibre.

Farey Discrepancy upgrades the quantifier audit from log-log to Vinogradov--Korobov scale. FD-223 extends switched blindness to `J<=kappa_beta Phi(N)`, with `Phi(N)=(log N)^(3/5)/(log log N)^(1/5)`: scheduled observations remain `N^(o(1))`, an earlier prefix defect remains `N^(1-o(1))`, and exact Euler laws hold through `beta N` for any fixed `beta<1`. More observations still do not create source coherence while the source can be rebuilt scale by scale. Failure beyond the certified range would be only a method boundary unless source freedom itself is shown to disappear.

Möbius Cancellation now carries the reduction audit from the whole source family to the full finite divisor-incidence algebra. MC-403 makes nonprincipal family energy exactly signed lcm-signature discrepancy. MC-404 proves that every finite Boolean or polynomial function of divisibility predicates lies in the lcm join-semilattice algebra. Higher incidence degree therefore cannot manufacture a retained tuple variable; a useful escape must control the exact discrepancy or introduce downstream arithmetic that varies inside lcm fibres.

Nyman--Beurling identifies the classical object behind its growing-resolution requirement. NB-234 shows that a slowly growing hierarchy of profiled depth--phase moments reconstructs the critical Ford mark while every fixed hierarchy remains blind. NB-235 rewrites the same coefficient as a horizontally truncated microlocal Landau--Gonek remainder. The shell kills the ordinary prime-power diagonal, and global cumulative control loses the microscopic window under bounded-variation smoothing. The missing information is genuinely local cancellation coupling horizontal depth to ordinate phase, not another fixed global statistic.

Prime Circle and Prime Lattice continue to close “make the operator larger” escapes, but PL-382 sharpens the boundary. PL-379--PL-381 show that double-commuting positive-energy prime shifts globalize to the regular multishift. The classical Nyman dilations satisfy the same covariance and have a generating wandering space yet fail double commutativity through an infinite-rank boundary defect; in logarithmic coordinates all prime directions lie on one right-shift semigroup. One cannot canonicalize away this RH-equivalent representation, but the defect itself is generic, so arithmetic must come from additional source/target structure in the overlap.

Robin Extremal now completes the single-center super-broad price audit outside the genuinely coupled thin-arc limit. RE-236 shows that the inverse-center exception left by RE-235 was a proof artifact: full symmetric contraction gives a uniform DC margin for every center scale. Bounded normalized Wiener rate therefore forces exponential destination thinning throughout the contracting super-broad regime. Crossing a local normal-form threshold is useful only if the global destination and coefficient budget remain competitive.

Visual Exploration adds a renormalization gate to the sample-size audit. VIS-324 closes every polynomially long first-level center-gap run with the classical two-linear-form sieve. VIS-325 then shows that the apparently singleton regime `k>L` is, up to bounded error, a lower-denominator rational mechanical path whose period monodromy is the first diagonal step. Fragmentation must therefore be measured after faithful continued-fraction/Christoffel renormalization, not only in the first period coordinates.

Weil Inertia and Weil Positivity retain the complementary source-structure and topology controls. WI-359 shows that generic graph-norm/zero-mode regularity is still too weak without actual Suzuki arithmetic. WP-378 shows that richer Hardy geometry can retain cross-prime structure while the exact Mangoldt selector survives only as unbounded boundary evaluation. More representation is not enough when the source relation or selector lives outside the topology used by positivity.

These examples refine one reusable audit. Identify the admissible source class and retained provenance; compare observation rank with latent dimension; establish carrier scale and price any inverse; perform exact Fourier/lcm/Gelfand/triangular/similarity and semilattice reductions; apply the first correlation intended to exploit a variable; test whether a family is merely Fourier-equivalent to one discrepancy vector; distinguish fixed, growing and cofinal quantifiers; and use faithful renormalization before declaring fragmentation final. For countable representations, identify the theorem that globalizes local structure and inspect its load-bearing hypotheses. Finally price the physical destination margin, aggregation length or coefficient cost after all representation artifacts are removed.

**Boundary.** This synthesis does not transfer quantitative thresholds between lines. AF rank/latent-dimension fibres, Farey VK-scale reservoirs, Möbius lcm semilattice, Nyman microlocal explicit-formula remainder, Prime-Lattice double-commuting boundary, Robin Wiener rates, Wang mechanical renormalization, Weil threshold estimates and selector-topology obstructions are different theorems. The cross-line claim is only the audit: enrichment deserves credit when it contributes source-selected information that is identifiable, survives exact reduction/inversion/renormalization, remains stably usable in the final topology and quantifier, and cannot be reproduced by the strongest matched control.
