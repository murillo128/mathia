# MI-021 — Representation enrichment only buys leverage where source-specific coupling survives acquisition, closure, inverse geometry and matched controls

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including FD-200--FD-208, MC-382--MC-389, NB-217--NB-222, PC-351--PC-355, PL-361--PL-370, RE-216--RE-220, VIS-302--VIS-309, WI-351--WI-352 and WP-366--WP-369. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, enrichment can be useful when new relations have exact source preimages and improve a destination bottleneck without repaying the gain elsewhere. The relevant question is not “how much representation is present?” but **what source-specific relation survives acquisition, closure/globalization, topology, normalization, inverse recovery, analytic identification and matched controls?**

Farey Discrepancy shows both constructive leverage and its price. FD-202--FD-205 build a genuinely better pre-norm geometry: signed source-slab differences, lowbit edges, logarithmic resistance and exact quotient-coordinate recovery. FD-206 then finds an RH-equivalent positive root anchor inside that geometry, and FD-207 shows that every fixed-depth signed dyadic difference of the root still has a physical inverse back to the same destination-complete scalar problem. FD-208 prices the first escape not already covered by that theorem: for depth `q=q(n)`, the squarefree-random cost grows by the central Delannoy factor `D_q=exp(Theta(q))`, so the benchmark remains critical-power only for `q=o(log n)`. Enrichment deserves analytic credit only inside the window where it can plausibly break the inverse without paying an equivalent power loss in transform cost or observation span.

Prime Circle supplies the automorphism-and-representation warning at increasing depth. Source-faithful noncommutative data can become blind under a spectral quotient; making reciprocal parity outer can expose only generic contragredient duality. PC-354 closes fixed finite-dimensional algebraic representation lifts as weight-monomial functions of the same reciprocal spectrum. PC-355 then closes the canonical free-Fock lift: second quantization implements reciprocal parity by unitary conjugacy on every finite truncation, while the left-regular signature operator is `I` plus a degree-raising nilpotent and hence has spectrum `{1}`. Moving to all tensor depths does not help if the richer representation remains functorially covariant under the same matched-control involution.

Prime Lattice separates ambient criticality, boundary source rigidity and continuation. PL-367 shows that `ell^p` topology manufactures every summatory exponent `1-1/p`; PL-368 shows that scalar Dirichlet Hardy geometry manufactures a generic `1/2` wall. PL-369 identifies a genuinely stronger nonlinear class from Venturini: bounded complete multiplicativity plus pole neutrality and continuation can force zeta zero-freeness, but at the critical half-plane witness existence is RH-equivalent through Liouville. PL-370 then narrows the admissible class already at `s=1`: a holomorphic pole-neutral witness has a simple zero and lies at finite prime-harmonic distance from Liouville. This is genuine source selection, yet the hard continuation theorem remains separate.

Robin Extremal gives a representation-parameter version of the same audit. RE-217 supplies a universal unit Wiener floor for separated continuum prediction, while RE-219 proves a stricter factor-two floor only for the single-positive-real-center negative-binomial construction with termwise absolute-tail control. RE-220 enlarges the formal parameter class to an arbitrary complex center and proves that the enlargement is illusory for the quantities the proof consumes: radialization to `|a|` weakly improves the symmetric-arc contraction and leaves the exact coefficient Wiener norm unchanged because the center phase factors out degreewise. Richer coordinates do not count as enrichment when the destination norm and certificate quotient them away exactly.

Visual Exploration supplies a progressively stronger matched-control warning. VIS-305--VIS-307 show that density, parity, exact prime-power support, spacing and coefficient magnitudes do not sustain the signed boundary once phases are randomized. VIS-308 preserves complete multiplicativity of the random phase field, including every same-base relation `p,p^2,p^3,...`, and cancellation still survives. VIS-309 then identifies the long-time quadratic mean of the **actual** common-time phase orbit with that same Haar model for fixed support. Hence generic cross-prime deterministic alignment is not enough either; any surviving pointwise effect must exploit exceptional finite heights, finite-window discrepancy or a moving support/frequency regime not captured by the Bohr limit.

Nyman--Beurling separates exact representation algebra from the final analytic class. NB-218--NB-221 remove absolute reconstruction, source-tail and remote-zero artifacts while preserving the signed source object, but every fixed primitive depth returns to the same local `-zeta'/zeta` carrier. NB-222 then proves that generic bounded strip analyticity is already sharp for that carrier: a matched analytic exponential saturates the contour scale and its Cauchy derivative envelope. Further gain has to use arithmetic spectral amplitudes of the actual logarithmic derivative.

Weil Positivity gives a three-stage scale warning. WP-366--WP-367 show that exact common homogeneous globalization destroys Whittaker `nY` incidence. WP-368 shows that bounded positive fixed scale preserves incidence only while exponentially suppressing large indices. WP-369 lets the scale move: the unique critical pure power is `Y_n=c/n`, exactly where `nY_n` freezes and finite--archimedean incidence factorizes; ordinary positive `L^2` then squares and diverges. Scale adaptation deserves credit only when the remaining transition coordinate stays source-specific and nonconstant.

Möbius Cancellation adds the extremizer/source-image audit and shows that source pullback can change the **numerical threshold**, not merely invalidate one model. MC-386 identifies the sharp ambient Welch scale; MC-387 rigidifies its minimally concentrated near-extremizers into coset/annihilator geometry; MC-388 proves that the exact high-dimensional endpoint Bochner support is a projected simplex and cannot approach that abstract extremizer. MC-389 converts the incompatibility into a fixed additive-energy deficit and, conditional on `epsilon_R=o(R^(-1/2))` good-mode control, a strict exceptional-count gap `|B|>=(1+c)H/R` above the generic Welch scale.

These examples refine the audit into a compact sequence. Identify the minimal source carrier after exact algebraic generation. Ask whether acquisition creates a new coordinate or transports old data. Apply the destination's closure, summation, globalization, norm, certificate or spectral quotient. State the ambient topology and test whether it already explains the critical scale. Price inverse geometry and ask whether a finite or uniformly controlled physical inverse restores the hard coordinate. Pull theorem hypotheses, exceptional sets and even sharp extremizer shapes back to the source image. If continuation is required, ask whether proving it is independent of the target. If a signed analytic carrier survives, compare it to the largest generic function class still allowed. Finally run the strongest matched control—including the actual deterministic orbit when available—and distinguish long-time/average agreement from the finite-height or pointwise statement the destination consumes.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem transferring Farey graph design, character-family estimates, zeta-primitive convolution, reciprocal matrix duality, prediction approximation geometry, lattice filtering, Wang mean values or Whittaker geometry between lines. Each quantitative statement remains line-specific. The cross-line content is the falsifiable audit: enrichment deserves credit only when newly acquired or retained structure survives exact destination operations, has controlled source/inverse cost, is not an ambient norm, automorphism, continuation, scale, certificate or averaging artifact, remains analytically identified with the intended destination observable, excludes the relevant ambient extremizers, and defeats the strongest matched control at the same quantifier and scale required by the target.
