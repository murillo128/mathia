# MI-021 — Representation enrichment buys leverage only when the escape survives the source class, quotient and destination category

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including AF-451--AF-456, FD-231--FD-235, MC-414--MC-418, NB-245--NB-249, PC-369--PC-373, PL-391--PL-395, VIS-335--VIS-340, WI-367--WI-370 and WP-381--WP-385, together with the earlier line-specific foundations they extend. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, “the representation contains more information” is only the first gate. The retained datum must survive the actual source class and its quantifiers, the exact quotient/topology and permitted operation order, distinguish the source from matched controls, reduce to a genuinely different mathematical resource rather than a richer presentation of a classical carrier, and retain the required margin or sign at the destination. The latest results sharpen that audit: an apparent escape can disappear when the source class is enlarged, when a limit is taken in the wrong order, when omitted moving support is restored, or when a richer positive representation is classified in its natural operator category.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the source-class dependence exact. AF-453--AF-455 show that weak collapse, finite output capacity and compact downstream processing can each destroy TV gain. AF-456 then separates atomwise separation from arbitrary-mixture stability: for the full countable probability simplex, a bounded linear/barycentric TV embedding must be bounded below on `l1_0`, so a suitable target must contain an isomorphic copy of `l1`. Hilbert geometry can separate all atoms and even retain `k`-sparse mixtures with sharp margin `1/sqrt(2k)`, while still having zero uniform margin on the full simplex.

Farey Discrepancy exposes a quantifier-order failure. FD-234 identifies the block-Mertens correction modulo rounding, but FD-235 proves that after freezing `N` the correction itself lies in the switched-blind null space, so the fixed-profile affine class contains zero. The nontrivial object must couple `N`, the available row depth and a uniform normalization. Möbius Cancellation supplies an operator-category control: MC-418 extends the Fejér diagonal floor from scalar positive kernels to arbitrary operator-valued one-variable stationary positive kernels, with no gain from channel dimension. A richer coefficient space is not an escape unless its arithmetic off-diagonal structure is used before that positive scalarization or the stationary one-variable hypothesis is genuinely left.

Nyman--Beurling separates a false analytic singularity from the real zero statistic. NB-248 forbids squaring the Ford-scaled `zeta'/zeta` area field before the signed divisor pairing, while NB-249 uses positive Mellin frequencies to Hardy-project the pole and exactly recover the finite marked zero current. Prime Circle supplies a cohomological classicalization control: PC-373 shows that the canonical Chebyshev Jacobian weight is only a degree scalar plus an endpoint coboundary.

Prime Lattice now adds a support-restoration control. PL-394 showed that the frozen one-sided Gram at the conductor remains Haar while the continuation-faithful approximate functional equation retains a reflected packet and Gamma-phase cross channel with nominal stationary product `nm approx t/(2 pi)`. PL-395 restores the actual moving Riemann--Siegel cutoff and shows that every off-diagonal pair reaches that saddle before it enters the sum; active stationary points collapse asymptotically to the diagonal boundary `n=m`, `t approx 2 pi n^2`. After scalar pair summation the product map also forgets ordered factor labels. The live continuation information is therefore in near-boundary activation, factor-label-retaining/nonquadratic structure, or a global zero-sensitive coupling—not in the broad bulk sum-energy saddle by itself.

Visual Exploration removes both natural source repackagings before Wang's filter. VIS-339 identifies the centered prime measure as `u(1+D)G` for the normalized theta remainder `G`; VIS-340 shows that the prime square-log summatory remainder is also an explicitly invertible partial-summation image of the same `G`, with proper prime powers exponentially lower order. Atomic packet geometry, square-log weighting and ordinary partial summation therefore do not create independent source leverage.

Weil Positivity strengthens the same reduction logic in a different category. WP-384 finds a genuine boundary layer but one that classicalizes to a finite-Euler-weighted Nyman Gram. WP-385 then shows that every closed positive dilation-invariant form is just a nonnegative Mellin spectral weight, while the finite Euler multipliers are boundedly invertible. Consequently no such stationary positive reweighting can null a mixed-base boundary packet without also nulling the prime packet. Boundary sensitivity is necessary there, but stationary positive boundary geometry is still too small a resource class.

Weil Inertia retains the scalar financing obstruction: WI-369 gives the sharp full-core gamma debit and WI-370 shows that the available direct target-ground scalar reserve is already smaller before later nonnegative penalties. Any repair must use vector-dependent positive gamma remainder, genuinely additional source coercivity, or exclude the bulk quasimodes.

The reusable audit is ordered: identify the physical source class and its coupled limits; track the exact quotient, topology, support and allowed operation order; classify alternate presentations, weights and channel enlargements in the correct mathematical category; apply the strongest matched control; then compare the surviving source-specific quantity with the destination inverse modulus, sign requirement or coercive reserve. Similar vocabulary across lines remains only analogy unless the objects and hypotheses are explicitly identified.

**Boundary.** This is an interface principle, not a theorem transferring estimates between lines. The `l1` simplex geometry, triangular switched quotient, operator Fejér floor, Hardy projection, Chebyshev coboundary, moving Riemann--Siegel boundary, theta partial-summation equivalence and Mellin boundary form live in different mathematical categories; their common role is only that each tests whether apparent information survives the next exact reduction.
