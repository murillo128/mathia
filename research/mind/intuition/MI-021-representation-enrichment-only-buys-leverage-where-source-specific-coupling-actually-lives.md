# MI-021 — Representation enrichment buys leverage only when source-specific coupling survives identifiability, exact reduction and the destination quantifier

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including AF-416--AF-439, FD-200--FD-221, MC-382--MC-400, NB-217--NB-232, PC-351--PC-361, PL-361--PL-378, RE-216--RE-233, VIS-302--VIS-321, WI-350--WI-359 and WP-366--WP-378. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, enrichment is useful only when a new relation has an exact source preimage, is identifiable on the admissible finite-data class, survives the destination operation, is recoverable at the needed precision, and improves a load-bearing bottleneck without repaying the gain elsewhere. The relevant question is not how large the representation is, but **what source-specific relation survives source-class restriction, acquisition, inversion, transport, exact algebraic reduction, correlation geometry, positivity, normalization and the topology/quantifier of the final theorem?**

Arithmetic Fidelity now separates three inverse layers. AF-438 gives an exact non-peeling Stieltjes carrier `F(z)=sum_m 1/(m^2-z)` whose poles expose the entire reciprocal-square shell ladder simultaneously, so AF-437's sequential peeling cascade is representation-specific. AF-439 then shows that finite-prefix recovery is not automatically a conditioning problem: for every fixed moment depth there are nearby positive unit-residue Stieltjes measures with the same observed moments and different poles. Before pricing Padé/Hankel/Prony stability, the admissible source/tail class must first make the finite observation map injective.

Farey Discrepancy upgrades the same lesson from finite data to a finite **quantifier**. FD-221 proves that every fixed block of the periodic switched schedule remains repairable below literal Euler-prefix coverage. A positive null cycle is killed by all positive-order switched differences, and triangular reservoir pivots repair the finitely many remaining observations. The earlier examples `1/2,1/4,1/8` were instances of a general fixed-`J` law. Any stronger discriminator must use growing-horizon/cofinal source coherence or uniformity that the finite construction does not provide.

Möbius Cancellation now has a four-stage dimension audit. MC-397 consumes the source-mode average by Fourier duality; MC-398 consumes divisor-pair multiplicity by lcm regrouping; MC-399 makes the standard lcm character phase disappear at first additive correlation; and MC-400 shows that the remaining moving hyperbola endpoints form an exactly Möbius-invertible convolution representation. A visible index is not a new arithmetic dimension if exact reduction, first correlation or exact inversion reconstructs it for free. The surviving resource must be independent structure in the signed lcm/signature coefficients, a genuinely joint bilinear estimate, or a pre-quadratic non-lcm coupling.

Nyman--Beurling keeps the theorem quantifier in view. NB-230--NB-232 show that the carrier transition is local and depth-conditioned, while sparse mesoscopic phase-aligned packets can remain invisible to a globally normalized Montgomery-type pair statistic and still create order-one Ford residue at selected heights. Strengthening the wrong global average does not control the exceptional local windows consumed by a uniform endpoint.

Prime Circle and Prime Lattice continue to close “make the operator larger” escapes. PC-361 shows that an unbounded graded Hamiltonian with a strictly raising bounded source term can remain spectrally triangular. PL-377--PL-378 show that bounded normal complete multiplicativity and uniformly bounded invertible nonnormal group completions both collapse to prime-torus character mixtures, the latter after one bounded similarity. New operator size or nonnormality is not information unless a source relation survives the invariant reduction.

Robin Extremal now demonstrates a full local-to-global price ledger. RE-231 identifies the microscopic coordinate `kappa=N/(m(a-1))`; positive-real `kappa>=1` can erase the compact residual delay. RE-232 then gives a sharp macroscopic arc `alpha_*(kappa)` but a normalized Wiener cost strictly above `6`, already worse than the existing finite-center construction. RE-233 resolves the equality arc in a fast-center regime and finds a sequence-sensitive `log(m)/m` tuning layer without lowering that cost. Crossing a local normal-form threshold is useful only if the global destination and coefficient budget remain competitive.

Visual Exploration supplies a constructive faithful representation. VIS-319 preserves both prime exclusions in a unimodular Bezout chart, VIS-320 reduces the cell to at most three subperiod mechanical branches, and VIS-321 proves that the moving branch gate can be frozen up to `O(1)` points while the two branch steps form a unimodular basis. The representation work is now essentially done at the natural scale: the unresolved resource is simultaneous-prime occupancy or destination-weighted cancellation on a fixed-gate short mechanical orbit.

Weil Inertia sharpens the source-structure requirement. WI-357 gives local threshold continuity on actual null states; WI-358 shows scalar energy control is too weak cofinally; WI-359 shows that even principal graph norm plus an exact **abstract** zero-mode equation and a scalar perturbation-norm envelope remains too weak. Progress must use relations specific to Suzuki's actual arithmetic perturbation, not another generic regularity class.

Weil Positivity supplies the complementary topology control. WP-377 collapses exact radial mixed-shell nullity to the invariant sector for any strongly continuous unitary radial action. WP-378 leaves the radial category entirely and tests the canonical cyclotomic `H^2` Gram: it has genuine cross-prime terms, yet Euler-factorizes, has full composite support and a radical-only diagonal, while the exact Mangoldt selector appears only as unbounded boundary evaluation. More geometry is not enough when positivity and the arithmetic selector occupy incompatible topological levels.

These examples refine one reusable audit. First identify the admissible source class and ask whether the finite observation is identifiable there. Then establish parameter provenance and carrier scale, price any inverse, verify transport, and perform exact Fourier/lcm/Gelfand/triangular/similarity reductions. Apply the first correlation/Cauchy step meant to exploit a variable, and test whether the surviving family is exactly invertible to the original data. For positive constructions, compute the radical and locate the arithmetic selector in the actual topology. For persistent families, distinguish fixed finite panels from growing/cofinal quantifiers. Finally price the physical destination margin or coefficient cost after all representation artifacts are removed.

**Boundary.** This synthesis does not transfer quantitative thresholds between lines. AF finite-moment fibres, Farey fixed-block coverage, Möbius convolution inversion, Nyman exceptional packets, Prime-Circle/Prime-Lattice scalarization, Robin Wiener rates, Wang short-orbit geometry, Weil threshold estimates and Hardy/radial positivity obstructions are different theorems. The cross-line claim is only the audit: enrichment deserves credit when it contributes source-selected information that is identifiable, survives exact reduction and inversion, remains stably usable in the final topology/quantifier, and cannot be reproduced by the strongest matched control.
