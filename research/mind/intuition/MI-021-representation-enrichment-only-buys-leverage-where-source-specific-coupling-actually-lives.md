# MI-021 — Representation enrichment only buys leverage where source-specific coupling actually survives

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including FD-200--FD-209, MC-382--MC-389, NB-217--NB-223, PC-351--PC-356, PL-361--PL-370, RE-216--RE-221, VIS-302--VIS-310, WI-351--WI-352 and WP-366--WP-370. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, enrichment can be useful when new relations have exact source preimages and improve a destination bottleneck without repaying the gain elsewhere. The relevant question is not “how much representation is present?” but **what source-specific relation survives acquisition, closure/globalization, topology, normalization, inverse recovery, naturality constraints, analytic identification and matched controls?**

Farey Discrepancy shows both constructive leverage and its price. FD-202--FD-205 build a genuinely better pre-norm geometry: signed source-slab differences, lowbit edges, logarithmic resistance and exact quotient-coordinate recovery. FD-206 finds an RH-equivalent positive root anchor, and FD-207 shows that every fixed-depth signed dyadic difference of the root still has a physical inverse back to the same destination-complete scalar problem. FD-208 prices growing depth through the central Delannoy factor `D_q=exp(Theta(q))`, leaving only `q=o(log n)` as a power-critical window. FD-209 then proves that one **common** sublogarithmic depth on a square-root scale window is still RH-complete: the binary-prefix Green kernel amplifies by only `(1+sqrt(2))^q`, which is subpower in precisely that regime. Growing transform depth becomes potentially new only through scale-dependent incoherence or a collective relation that never produces a common-depth pointwise bound.

Prime Circle supplies the covariance warning at increasing depth. Source-faithful noncommutative data can become blind under a spectral quotient; making reciprocal parity outer can expose only generic contragredient duality. PC-354 closes fixed finite-dimensional algebraic representation lifts, and PC-355 closes the canonical free-Fock lift because second quantization implements reciprocal parity by unitary conjugacy. PC-356 then closes the obvious source-metric repair: the actual radial-energy Gram matrix can be non-diagonal and fail to commute with parity, but when the same native construction is applied to the reciprocal control its metric is `J^*GJ`. The source and control Hilbert spaces remain isometric. A source-dependent enrichment is not discriminating merely because it is asymmetric internally; it must fail to be transported covariantly by the matched control for an independently arithmetic reason.

Prime Lattice separates ambient criticality, boundary source rigidity and continuation. PL-367 shows that `ell^p` topology manufactures every summatory exponent `1-1/p`; PL-368 shows that scalar Dirichlet Hardy geometry manufactures a generic `1/2` wall. PL-369 identifies a genuinely stronger nonlinear class from Venturini: bounded complete multiplicativity plus pole neutrality and continuation can force zeta zero-freeness, but at the critical half-plane witness existence is RH-equivalent through Liouville. PL-370 narrows the admissible class already at `s=1`: a holomorphic pole-neutral witness has a simple zero and lies at finite prime-harmonic distance from Liouville. This is genuine source selection, yet the hard continuation theorem remains separate.

Robin Extremal gives a representation-parameter version of the same audit. RE-217 supplies a universal unit Wiener floor for separated continuum prediction, while RE-219 proves a stricter factor-two floor only for one single-center construction under termwise absolute-tail control. RE-220 shows that making the center complex is illusory for that certificate: radialization weakly improves the symmetric-arc contraction while preserving the exact Wiener norm. RE-221 then removes a different artifact hypothesis: for fixed positive-real center, arc and linear truncation ratio, the **exact cancellation-sensitive truncated polynomial** still has Wiener exponent at least two whenever it predicts uniformly. Parameter enrichment and a sharper remainder treatment both fail if the consumed finite predictor remains in the same fixed single-center geometry.

Visual Exploration supplies a progressively stronger matched-control warning. VIS-305--VIS-307 show that density, parity, exact prime-power support, spacing and coefficient magnitudes do not sustain the signed boundary once phases are randomized. VIS-308 preserves complete multiplicativity of the random phase field, and VIS-309 identifies the long-time quadratic mean of the **actual** common-time phase orbit with that same Haar model for fixed support. VIS-310 then shows that the naive attempt to transfer the small Bohr energy to finite windows by hard uniform tail truncation plus one global ratio-frequency gap is far too lossy: at `H~x log log x` it yields a sufficient horizon only beyond `x^5` up to logs. The remaining finite-height route must preserve coefficient-weighted ratio geometry or control the infinite tail in energy rather than collapse both to worst cases.

Nyman--Beurling now supplies a positive example of what happens when the generic analytic class is replaced by genuine source spectral information. NB-218--NB-221 remove absolute reconstruction, source-tail and remote-zero artifacts while preserving the signed source object, and every fixed primitive depth returns to the same local `-zeta'/zeta` carrier. NB-222 proves that bounded strip analyticity alone is sharp for that carrier. NB-223 then shifts the smooth carrier across the actual zeta zeros and uses Bellotti's near-one zero-density bounds on the resulting weighted residue sum. The generic strip ceiling is beaten: an order-one positive Euler gap persists up to every fixed `log^epsilon` margin below Ford height. The remaining endpoint cost is the last `log log T`-deep near-one zero layer or phase cancellation inside it. This is precisely the kind of leverage enrichment is supposed to expose: not a new formal representation, but a source-specific restriction that excludes the generic matched extremizer.

Weil Positivity gives a scale-and-naturality warning. WP-366--WP-367 show that exact common homogeneous globalization destroys Whittaker `nY` incidence. WP-368 shows that bounded positive fixed scale preserves incidence only while exponentially suppressing large indices. WP-369 finds one critical power law, `Y_n=c/n`, exactly where `nY_n` freezes and finite--archimedean incidence factorizes. WP-370 proves that this collapse is not merely a bad scale guess: among diagonal point samplers, exact intertwining with every prime Hecke operator **forces** `Y_(pm)=Y_m/p`, hence `Y_n=c/n`. A source naturality constraint can select the critical exponent while simultaneously erasing the mixed coordinate that made the representation interesting.

Möbius Cancellation adds the extremizer/source-image audit and shows that source pullback can change the **numerical threshold**, not merely invalidate one model. MC-386 identifies the sharp ambient Welch scale; MC-387 rigidifies its minimally concentrated near-extremizers; MC-388 proves that the exact high-dimensional endpoint Bochner support is a projected simplex and cannot approach that abstract extremizer. MC-389 now makes the source gap explicit: affine dimension bounds every nonzero translate overlap, giving `E(S)<=3|S|^3/4` in the high-rank branch and, conditional on `epsilon_R=o(R^(-1/2))`, `|B|>=(1+1/4096)H/R`. The remaining difficulty is analytic good-mode control, not making the source gap effective.

These examples refine the audit into a compact sequence. Identify the minimal source carrier after exact algebraic generation. Ask whether acquisition creates a new coordinate or transports old data. Apply the destination's closure, summation, globalization, norm, certificate or spectral quotient. State the ambient topology and test whether it already explains the critical scale. Price inverse geometry and ask whether a finite or subpower physical inverse restores the hard coordinate. Solve any covariance or naturality equation imposed on source-adapted structure before crediting it with new information. Pull theorem hypotheses, exceptional sets and sharp extremizers back to the source image. If continuation is required, ask whether proving it is independent of the target. If a signed analytic carrier survives, compare it to the largest generic function class still allowed, then retain any source-specific spectral restriction strong enough to exclude that class's extremizer. Finally run the strongest matched control at the same finite-height or pointwise quantifier consumed by the destination.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem transferring Farey graph design, character-family estimates, zeta-zero density, reciprocal matrix duality, prediction approximation geometry, lattice filtering, Wang mean values or Whittaker geometry between lines. Each quantitative statement remains line-specific. The cross-line content is the falsifiable audit: enrichment deserves credit only when newly acquired or retained structure survives exact destination operations, has controlled source/inverse cost, is not an ambient norm, automorphism, covariance, naturality, continuation, scale, certificate or averaging artifact, remains analytically identified with the intended destination observable, excludes the relevant ambient extremizers, and defeats the strongest matched control at the target quantifier and scale.
