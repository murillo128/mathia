# MI-021 — Representation enrichment buys leverage only when source-specific coupling survives exact reduction with usable conditioning

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including AF-416--AF-437, FD-200--FD-219, MC-382--MC-398, NB-217--NB-231, PC-351--PC-361, PL-361--PL-378, RE-216--RE-231, VIS-302--VIS-320, WI-350--WI-358 and WP-366--WP-376. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, enrichment is useful only when a new relation has an exact source preimage, survives the destination operation, is recoverable at the destination's precision, and improves a load-bearing bottleneck without repaying the gain elsewhere. The relevant question is not how large the representation is, but **what source-specific relation survives acquisition, selection, inversion, transport, symmetry, positivity, normalization, projection and the topology/quantifier in which the final estimate is taken?**

Arithmetic Fidelity now distinguishes exact source retention from stable accessibility. AF-436 proves that the exact even coefficient sequence contains the whole shell ladder at residual scales `m^(-2k)`. AF-437 shows that sequential peeling of shell `m` amplifies an earlier shell-`j` weight/location error by roughly `(m/j)^(2k)`: exact information may survive while the inverse needed to expose it is exponentially ill-conditioned. A destination should not receive credit for retaining provenance merely because that provenance is formally reconstructible at infinite precision.

Farey Discrepancy makes overlap geometry part of the quantifier. FD-218 shows that one same source at `N,2N` remains blind below half-scale Euler coverage. FD-219 extends the control through the whole `(0,3,3)` period `N,2N,4N`: blindness persists below quarter-scale coverage, and prefix rigidity begins when the largest exact-prime window reaches the earlier prefix. Persistence and period completion are useful only to the extent that moving source constraints and observation support actually close the repair reservoir.

Möbius Cancellation adds two exact averaging audits. MC-397 turns the source-mode average into a signature subgroup projector. MC-398 shows that the two divisor labels in the already-squared Selberg majorant enter the arithmetic quantity only through their lcm, so they collapse to one lcm-fibre coefficient. A formal index is not an extra cancellation dimension if Fourier duality or exact regrouping can consume it without changing the arithmetic object. The surviving candidate is the lcm/shell variable itself or a genuinely non-lcm-factorable pre-quadratic coordinate.

Prime Circle and Prime Lattice close several “make it bigger” routes. PC-361 shows that a proper unbounded grade Hamiltonian plus a bounded strictly raising source term keeps exactly the diagonal eigenvalue spectrum: unboundedness does not help when source data remain triangular. PL-377 shows that bounded normal infinite-dimensional complete multiplicativity remains a measure mixture of scalar semicharacters. PL-378 adds the complementary nonnormal boundary: if the exact multiplicative representation extends to a uniformly bounded rational-group representation, Day--Dixmier removes the nonnormality by one bounded similarity and the spectral data again reduce to prime-torus character mixtures.

Nyman--Beurling shows why enrichment must respect the carrier's own capacity coordinate. NB-230 gives an absolute Ford cutoff once `YD-log J -> +infinity`, where `J=1+R/H`. NB-231 matches that upper boundary with a phase-aligned abstract packet carrying order-one mass at `YD-log J=O(1)` in the audited capacity regime. The open source theorem is therefore not “improve the whole Ford strip” but “rule out the bounded carrier-width transition for the actual zeta zeros.”

Robin Extremal turns parameter drift into an exact normal-form audit. RE-231 uses `kappa=N/(m(a-1))`: finite complex limits with `Re(kappa)<1` retain residual microscopic phase `e^(-i(1-kappa)u)`, while positive-real `kappa>=1` flattens the compact microscopic profile. A moving parameter becomes a real resource only when it crosses the transition seen by the destination; after that transition the same local probe must not be reused as though it still discriminated.

Visual Exploration supplies the constructive counterpart. VIS-319 finds a unimodular Bezout chart that preserves both prime exclusions and a long transverse direction. VIS-320 then identifies the actual remaining geometry: at most three simultaneous-prime rational mechanical branches sampled for less than one full rotation period. Here enrichment genuinely helps because the representation preserves the source exclusions and aggregation length; the next theorem is a concrete subperiod prime-distribution estimate, not another coordinate repair.

Weil Inertia sharpens the topology lesson. WI-357 gives threshold continuity on actual null states because the null equation controls logarithmic energy, but WI-358 shows that the same energy budget alone is too weak for cofinal threshold-by-threshold continuity. The useful topology is state-selected; global progress still needs source-specific coherence or positive reserve.

Weil Positivity sharpens the same audit from the spectral side. WP-375 shows that exact mixed-shell nullity in a closable positive form on logarithmic-radial `L^2` propagates to prime-power shells. WP-376 proves that every Sobolev order and every absolutely-continuous Fourier-weight translation topology containing the source profiles has the same weak-translation escape and radical closure. A zero-frequency trace changes the spectral type and evades the closure, but retains only the already-known `Lambda^2` zero-Mellin mode. Reweighting the same continuous spectrum is not a new arithmetic channel.

These examples refine one reusable audit. Identify the minimal source carrier after exact algebraic generation. Establish parameter provenance and the carrier's resolution scale. Price the conditioning of any inverse used to expose the carrier. Verify that the representation transports rather than averages it away. Before crediting a new dimension, check Fourier, lcm-fibre, Gelfand, triangular and similarity reductions. Before crediting an operator enlargement, locate source terms relative to invariant gradings, group completion and metric covariance. Before crediting positivity, compute the radical generated by exact nullity in the actual spectral topology. Before crediting persistence, quantify overlap of source constraints and observation support. Finally, price only the variables and ranges that survive all those reductions.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem transferring Farey overlap thresholds, Arithmetic-Fidelity peeling condition numbers, Möbius lcm compression, Nyman carrier-width estimates, Prime-Circle triangular spectral blindness, Prime-Lattice unitarization, Robin microscopic thresholds, Wang subperiod geometry, Weil threshold estimates or positive-radical no-go results between lines. Each quantitative statement remains line-specific. The cross-line content is the falsifiable audit: enrichment deserves credit only when it introduces or preserves a source-selected relation that survives exact downstream reduction **with stable enough recovery, the arithmetic coordinates, topology, positivity structure, active capacity scale and quantifier required by the final theorem**, and cannot be reproduced by the strongest matched control.
