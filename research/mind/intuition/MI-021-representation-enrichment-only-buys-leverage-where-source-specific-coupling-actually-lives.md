# MI-021 — Representation enrichment buys leverage only when the escape survives reduction, matched controls and the destination margin

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including AF-451--AF-455, FD-231--FD-234, MC-414--MC-417, NB-245--NB-249, PC-369--PC-373, PL-391--PL-394, VIS-335--VIS-339, WI-367--WI-370 and WP-381--WP-384, together with the earlier line-specific foundations they extend. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, “the representation contains more information” is only the first gate. The retained datum must survive the exact quotient, topology and permitted operation order; distinguish the source from a strong matched control; reduce to a genuinely different mathematical resource rather than a richer presentation of a classical carrier; and retain a positive margin or coercive reserve at the destination. The latest results show that these gates are sequential: escaping one obstruction can expose a second reduction that removes the apparent leverage.

The reusable audit is ordered: identify the physical source class and metric; track the exact quotient, topology and allowed operation order; reduce alternate presentations, weights and boundary limits to their true resource class; apply the strongest matched control; then compare the surviving source-specific signal with the destination inverse modulus, sign requirement or coercive reserve. Similar vocabulary across lines remains only analogy unless the mathematical objects are explicitly identified.
