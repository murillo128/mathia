# MI-021 — Representation enrichment buys leverage only when retained source information survives the quotient, breaks the control and keeps a usable margin

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including AF-451--AF-453, FD-231--FD-233, MC-414--MC-416, NB-245--NB-247, PC-369--PC-371, PL-391--PL-393, VIS-335--VIS-337, WI-367--WI-369 and WP-381--WP-383, together with the earlier line-specific foundations they extend. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, “the representation contains more information” is only the first gate. The retained datum must survive the quotient, completion or topology used downstream; distinguish the source from a strong matched control; and retain a positive margin or reserve at the destination scale.

Arithmetic Fidelity now makes the topology gate explicit. AF-451--AF-452 identify completed source-law secants as the exact stability carrier, while AF-453 proves that finite continuous observations have zero uniform total-variation gain whenever that carrier contains weakly collapsing secants of nonvanishing TV size. Exact injectivity is therefore insufficient unless the physical source metric is compatible with the observation topology.

Farey Discrepancy closes its coefficient-level positivity gate: FD-233 proves that vanishing aggregate negative-capacity density is not only necessary but sufficient for a switched-preserving positive lift, with the canonical lift `b^+`. The unresolved cost is now the affine search for such a representative modulo switched-blind directions, followed separately by discrete source realization.

Möbius Cancellation closes the cheap signed-filter escape. MC-416 proves that every nontrivial positive quadratic shift filter pays a positive diagonal and that the constant Fejer window minimizes its normalized coefficient. A useful continuation must win in the thin endpoint off-diagonal or replace positivity with another controlled mechanism.

Nyman--Beurling improves observability without supplying the missing zero theorem. NB-247 combines the NB-246 Ford-scale band motion with Bellotti population bounds to make one hard `1/H` box sufficient for pointwise control. The matched coherent packet still saturates that target; the remaining input is a uniform depth-marked shrinking-window second moment for actual zeta zeros.

Prime Lattice strengthens its quotient warning: PL-393 shows that the same Euler--Maclaurin continuation tail erased by `B^2` is also invisible to the standard fixed-`N` infinite-height Jessen logarithmic mean. A zero-faithful category must retain the distinguished continuation representative rather than only its translation hull.

Visual Exploration now locates arithmetic singular information exactly but also prices it. VIS-337 copies each prime-power jump into Wang's derivative channel, yet each individual jump is exponentially below the live Korobov--Vinogradov envelope. Any useful migration must be collective, and the destination must still avoid derivative repayment.

Weil Inertia replaces an ambiguous reserve question by a fixed quantitative debit. WI-369 decomposes the corrected odd archimedean block as `-C_Gamma I` plus positive pieces and proves the floor is asymptotically sharp. Any cofinal reserve must therefore come from source-conditioned channels or from excluding the bulk quasimodes that attain that floor.

The reusable audit is ordered: identify the physical source class and metric; track the exact quotient, completion and topology; apply the strongest matched control; then compare the surviving source-specific signal with the destination inverse modulus, positivity slack or coercive reserve. Similar vocabulary across lines is only analogy unless those objects are explicitly identified.

**Boundary.** This is an interface principle, not a theorem transferring estimates between lines. The line-specific mechanisms above remain mathematically distinct.
