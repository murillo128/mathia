# MI-021 — Representation enrichment buys leverage only when the escape survives the source class, quotient and destination category

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including AF-451--AF-457, FD-231--FD-236, MC-414--MC-419, NB-245--NB-251, PC-369--PC-375, PL-391--PL-396, VIS-335--VIS-340, WI-367--WI-371 and WP-381--WP-386, together with the earlier line-specific foundations they extend. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, “the representation contains more information” is only the first gate. The retained datum must survive the actual source class and its quantifiers, the exact quotient/topology and permitted operation order, distinguish the source from matched controls, reduce to a genuinely different mathematical resource rather than a richer presentation of a classical carrier, and retain the required margin or sign at the destination. The latest results sharpen that audit further: apparent leverage can disappear under optimal target geometry, a natural family normalization, formal tensorization, an inadmissible bandwidth enlargement, a canonical measure change, a universal weighted-isometry spectrum, a representation-dependent hard cutoff, pointwise-local positive selection, or a repair whose nominal dimension does not couple to the bad subspace.

Arithmetic Fidelity now has an optimal Hilbert benchmark. AF-456 shows that arbitrary-mixture TV fidelity requires `l1` geometry; AF-457 proves that even on `k`-sparse mixtures every bounded Hilbert marking has worst-case TV modulus at most `M/sqrt(2k)`, attained by orthogonal one-hot marks. More Hilbert dimension cannot beat the square-root tariff. A stronger arithmetic result must therefore come from the actual source-law secants excluding the extremizing sparse mixtures or from leaving the Hilbert/barycentric category.

Farey Discrepancy exposes the same audit at the quotient level. FD-235 shows that freezing `N` makes the block-Mertens correction switched-blind. FD-236 then couples `N` to the physical row depth and uses the natural reservoir scale `L_N=N/(D log N)`, yet the correction still has vanishing normalized coefficient envelope and switched response. The next quotient must therefore refine the response topology itself, and the positive-lift theorem must be re-established at that finer scale rather than inherited formally.

Möbius Cancellation distinguishes representation dimension from independent source geometry. MC-418 rules out gain from operator-valued channels in one translation variable; MC-419 proves that genuine independent translation axes do help, but exactly through the reciprocal bandwidth volume `1/prod H_j`. Formal tensor labels or diagonal embeddings do not earn that product. The source must actually provide independent translations and the arithmetic off-diagonal control needed to exploit them.

Nyman--Beurling separates an admissible representation repair from a bandwidth resource. NB-249 Hardy-projects the Ford pole before squaring and exactly recovers the finite marked zero current. NB-250 identifies its energy as a narrow carrier-band form factor. NB-251 shows that the standard matched packet ceases to obstruct normalized decay once carrier width is super-`H`, even if it remains `o(X)`. But the physical Mellin shell currently provides only `Theta(H)` width. The issue is therefore not whether a wider Fourier window would help, but whether the source construction can legally generate one while preserving depth marks, selected center and projection order.

Prime Circle gives a complementary matched-control warning. PC-373 turns the Chebyshev Jacobian into an arcsine coboundary, and PC-374 shows that the natural endpoint line `Re(s)=1/2` is exactly the half-density exponent of that measure change. PC-375 then retains arbitrary unit-modulus inverse-branch phase, including orientation, yet the transfer remains the adjoint of a proper weighted isometry and its normalized spectrum is the same closed disk for every integer degree. Pointwise phase information is therefore not spectral arithmetic leverage inside this canonical completion.

Prime Lattice shows that support itself must be audited at the level of exact continuation. PL-395 correctly eliminates off-diagonal interior saddles from the sharp moving Riemann--Siegel main sum. PL-396 evaluates an exact incomplete-gamma continuation at the same pair saddle and finds order-one weights for fixed additive gaps; the sharp activation offset, smoothing width and stationary-phase width all scale like `sqrt(t)`. The representation-faithful object is therefore a near-diagonal coalescing saddle--transition layer, not the literal hard boundary. Yet the local layer still depends only on scalar `log n` energies and the archimedean smoothing kernel, so it earns no prime-lattice leverage unless a completed invariant retains factor labels or another genuinely source-specific feature.

Weil Positivity now separates two boundary positivity classes and closes both as selection mechanisms. WP-385 shows that stationary positive Mellin geometry cannot create mixed-shell nullity because every finite Euler factor is boundedly invertible. WP-386 drops stationarity completely but keeps pointwise-local positivity: exact mixed-shell nullity forces the positive measure onto `(0,1)`, where every arithmetic boundary profile becomes `Lambda(m)/sqrt(m)` and both shell Grams and local-anchor incidences collapse to rank one in the shell variable. The surviving categories are genuinely nonlocal/off-diagonal boundary forms, finite-prelimit effects, or delayed finite--archimedean coupling before exact selection.

Visual Exploration retains its earlier reduction gate: the two natural prime observables are invertible reparameterizations of the same normalized theta remainder.

Weil Inertia strengthens its financing obstruction from a scalar floor to positive-density operator demand. WI-369--WI-370 identify the sharp gamma debit and show that the existing scalar MASTER reserve cannot pay it. WI-371 proves `liminf iota_0(A)/A >= c_bulk=0.183495...` using interior Dirichlet bands, and derives matching linear-rank and Schatten-growth requirements for additive repairs. A source network whose active dimension grows with `A` remains logically possible, but only its actual coupling and singular-value mass on those bulk directions count as reserve.

The reusable audit is ordered: identify the physical source class and its coupled limits; track the exact quotient, topology, support, representation choice and allowed operation order; classify alternate presentations, locality assumptions, weights, channels and tensor axes in the correct mathematical category; apply the strongest matched control; then compare the surviving source-specific quantity with the destination inverse modulus, sign requirement, bandwidth or coercive reserve. Similar vocabulary across lines remains only analogy unless the objects and hypotheses are explicitly identified.

**Boundary.** This is an interface principle, not a theorem transferring estimates between lines. Sparse-mixture Hilbert geometry, triangular Farey response topology, multivariable Fejér volume, Ford carrier bandwidth, Chebyshev weighted-isometry spectrum, Riemann--Siegel saddle/transition geometry, local boundary-measure rigidity and gamma negative-index density live in different mathematical categories. Their common role is only that each tests whether apparent information survives the next exact reduction.
