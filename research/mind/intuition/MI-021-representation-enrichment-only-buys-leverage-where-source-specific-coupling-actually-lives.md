# MI-021 — Representation enrichment buys leverage only when source-specific coupling survives exact reduction with usable conditioning and the right quantifier

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including AF-416--AF-438, FD-200--FD-220, MC-382--MC-399, NB-217--NB-232, PC-351--PC-361, PL-361--PL-378, RE-216--RE-231, VIS-302--VIS-320, WI-350--WI-358 and WP-366--WP-377. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, enrichment is useful only when a new relation has an exact source preimage, survives the destination operation, is recoverable at the destination's precision, and improves a load-bearing bottleneck without repaying the gain elsewhere. The relevant question is not how large the representation is, but **what source-specific relation survives acquisition, selection, inversion, transport, exact algebraic reduction, correlation geometry, positivity, normalization and the topology/quantifier in which the final estimate is taken?**

Arithmetic Fidelity now separates sequential inversion from exact joint representation. AF-437 proves that recursive peeling of shell `m` amplifies earlier shell-`j` errors by roughly `(m/j)^(2k)`. AF-438 then gives an explicit exact Stieltjes carrier

`F(z)=sum_(m>=1)1/(m^2-z)`

whose poles at `m^2` with residue `-1` carry all shell labels simultaneously. Thus the peeling cascade is not an information-theoretic obstruction: an exact representation change removes the forced sequential subtraction. But finite/noisy recovery of remote poles requires analytic continuation/rational reconstruction and may be badly conditioned. Enrichment deserves credit for exact transport only after the inverse needed by the actual destination is priced.

Farey Discrepancy makes reachable-cone geometry part of the quantifier. FD-218, FD-219 and FD-220 show that one same source remains blind through `N,2N,4N,8N`, including the first `(0,3,3,0)` wrap, until the latest exact-prime window literally covers the earlier prefix. In FD-220 the wrapped observation is repairable because the low-prefix response lies in the positive cone of four high-reservoir response vectors. Persistence, period completion and wraparound matter only through the source overlap and the positive observation-response cone they leave available. A general finite-block conclusion still requires a structural cone/rank theorem; the observed half/quarter/eighth thresholds must not be extrapolated by pattern alone.

Möbius Cancellation now shows that surviving one exact compression is not enough to create an averaging dimension. MC-397 turns the source-mode average into a signature subgroup projector. MC-398 collapses the two divisor labels in the squared Selberg majorant to one lcm-fibre coefficient. MC-399 then shows that the remaining standard lcm source phase separates by complete multiplicativity: `chi_I(ell m)=chi_I(ell)chi_I(m)`, and the `ell` scalar disappears at the first additive correlation. A formal index counts only if it survives both exact regrouping and the Cauchy/differencing geometry that is supposed to exploit it. The remaining arithmetic resource is collective lcm/signature hyperbola structure or a genuinely non-lcm-factorable pre-quadratic coupling.

Nyman--Beurling shows that enrichment must also respect the theorem's exceptional-set quantifier. NB-230--NB-231 make `YD-log(1+R/H)` the sharp source-side transition for the audited information. NB-232 constructs a matched-control family of phase-aligned packets whose spacing is much coarser than mean zero spacing and whose cumulative population can be only `O(sqrt(log T) log log T)`. Such packets can remain asymptotically invisible to the standard globally normalized Montgomery weighted pair statistic while still carrying order-one Ford residue at selected heights. Therefore a stronger global average need not constrain the sparse local configuration consumed by a uniform endpoint. The missing information must resolve local shallow-zero phase at carrier frequency `X`, conditioned on depth and the actual `1/H` window.

Prime Circle and Prime Lattice close several “make it bigger” routes. PC-361 shows that a proper unbounded grade Hamiltonian plus a bounded strictly raising source term keeps exactly the diagonal eigenvalue spectrum: unboundedness does not help when source data remain triangular. PL-377 shows that bounded normal infinite-dimensional complete multiplicativity remains a measure mixture of scalar semicharacters. PL-378 adds the complementary nonnormal boundary: if the exact multiplicative representation extends to a uniformly bounded rational-group representation, Day--Dixmier removes the nonnormality by one bounded similarity and the spectral data again reduce to prime-torus character mixtures.

Robin Extremal turns parameter drift into an exact normal-form audit. RE-231 uses `kappa=N/(m(a-1))`: finite complex limits with `Re(kappa)<1` retain residual microscopic phase `e^(-i(1-kappa)u)`, while positive-real `kappa>=1` flattens the compact microscopic profile. A moving parameter becomes a real resource only when it crosses the transition seen by the destination; after that transition the same local probe must not be reused as though it still discriminated.

Visual Exploration supplies the constructive counterpart. VIS-319 finds a unimodular Bezout chart that preserves both prime exclusions and a long transverse direction. VIS-320 then identifies the actual remaining geometry: at most three simultaneous-prime rational mechanical branches sampled for less than one full rotation period. Here enrichment genuinely helps because the representation preserves the source exclusions and aggregation length; the next theorem is a concrete subperiod prime-distribution estimate, not another coordinate repair.

Weil Inertia sharpens the topology lesson. WI-357 gives threshold continuity on actual null states because the null equation controls logarithmic energy, but WI-358 shows that the same energy budget alone is too weak for cofinal threshold-by-threshold continuity. The useful topology is state-selected; global progress still needs source-specific coherence or positive reserve.

Weil Positivity now shows that spectral singularity alone is not a new radial channel. WP-377 assumes only a strongly continuous unitary radial action and the exact spectator-prime difference identity. Shrinking gaps between prime logarithms plus uniform continuity of matrix coefficients and the mean ergodic theorem force every non-invariant prime-power component into the closed span of mixed shells. Exact positive mixed-shell nullity therefore factors through the invariant sector for arbitrary scalar spectral type; in the scalar realization that sector is only zero Mellin frequency and again returns `Lambda^2`. A successful escape must change a load-bearing structural hypothesis or provide genuinely arithmetic invariant multiplicity, not merely replace absolutely-continuous spectrum by a singular one.

These examples refine one reusable audit. Identify the minimal source carrier after exact algebraic generation. Establish parameter provenance and the carrier's resolution scale. Price the conditioning of any inverse used to expose the carrier. Verify that the representation transports rather than averages it away. Before crediting a new dimension, check Fourier, lcm-fibre, Gelfand, triangular and similarity reductions **and the first correlation/Cauchy step that is meant to exploit the variable**. Before crediting an operator enlargement, locate source terms relative to invariant gradings, group completion and metric covariance. Before crediting positivity, compute the radical generated by exact nullity and the invariant quotient of the actual translation action. Before crediting persistence, quantify overlap and positive response-cone geometry. Before crediting a global statistic, check whether the final theorem is vulnerable to sparse exceptional local configurations that the statistic averages away. Finally, price only the variables, scales and quantifiers that survive all those reductions.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem transferring Farey overlap thresholds, Arithmetic-Fidelity pole-recovery condition numbers, Möbius lcm compression, Nyman carrier-width/pair-correlation controls, Prime-Circle triangular spectral blindness, Prime-Lattice unitarization, Robin microscopic thresholds, Wang subperiod geometry, Weil threshold estimates or positive-radical no-go results between lines. Each quantitative statement remains line-specific. The cross-line content is the falsifiable audit: enrichment deserves credit only when it introduces or preserves a source-selected relation that survives exact downstream reduction **with stable enough recovery, surviving arithmetic coupling, the topology/positivity structure, active capacity scale and local-versus-global quantifier required by the final theorem**, and cannot be reproduced by the strongest matched control.
