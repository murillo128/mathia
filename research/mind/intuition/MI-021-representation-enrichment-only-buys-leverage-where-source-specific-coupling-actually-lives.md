# MI-021 — Representation enrichment only buys leverage where source-specific coupling survives acquisition, closure, inverse geometry and matched controls

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including FD-200--FD-207, MC-382--MC-388, NB-217--NB-222, PC-351--PC-354, PL-361--PL-369, RE-216--RE-218, VIS-302--VIS-308, WI-351--WI-352 and WP-366--WP-369. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, enrichment can be useful when new relations have exact source preimages and improve a destination bottleneck without repaying the gain elsewhere. The relevant question is not “how much representation is present?” but **what source-specific relation survives acquisition, closure/globalization, topology, normalization, inverse recovery, analytic identification and matched controls?**

Farey Discrepancy shows both sides. FD-202--FD-205 build a genuinely better pre-norm geometry: signed source-slab differences, lowbit edges, logarithmic resistance and exact quotient-coordinate recovery. FD-206 then finds an RH-equivalent positive root anchor inside that geometry. FD-207 goes further: every fixed-depth signed dyadic difference of the root still has a bounded physical inverse back to the same destination-complete scalar problem. Enrichment deserves analytic credit only when its signed transform is not finitely invertible at the same critical exponent on the actual source class.

Prime Circle supplies the automorphism-quotient warning. Source-faithful noncommutative data can become blind under a spectral quotient; making reciprocal parity outer can expose only generic contragredient duality. PC-354 then closes fixed finite-dimensional algebraic representation lifts as weight-monomial functions of the same reciprocal spectrum. Escaping one quotient obstruction matters only if the survivor is source-selective rather than a universal orientation.

Prime Lattice separates ambient criticality from source-rigid zero sensitivity. PL-367 shows that `ell^p` topology manufactures every summatory exponent `1-1/p`; PL-368 shows that scalar Dirichlet Hardy geometry manufactures a generic `1/2` wall. PL-369 then identifies a genuinely stronger nonlinear class from Venturini: bounded complete multiplicativity plus pole neutrality and continuation can force zeta zero-freeness. But at the critical half-plane the witness-existence problem is RH-equivalent. Extra source rigidity can therefore create a valid theorem interface without creating an easier theorem.

Visual Exploration supplies a progressively stronger matched-control warning. VIS-305--VIS-307 show that density, parity, exact prime-power support, spacing and coefficient magnitudes do not sustain the signed boundary once phases are randomized. VIS-308 preserves complete multiplicativity of the random phase field, including every same-base relation `p,p^2,p^3,...`, and cancellation still survives. The remaining source-specific candidate is collective deterministic alignment across distinct base primes, not merely “phase structure” in the abstract.

Nyman--Beurling separates exact representation algebra from the final analytic class. NB-218--NB-221 remove absolute reconstruction, source-tail and remote-zero artifacts while preserving the signed source object, but every fixed primitive depth returns to the same local `-zeta'/zeta` carrier. NB-222 then proves that generic bounded strip analyticity is already sharp for that carrier: a matched analytic exponential saturates the contour scale and its Cauchy derivative envelope. Further gain has to use arithmetic spectral amplitudes of the actual logarithmic derivative.

Weil Positivity gives a three-stage scale warning. WP-366--WP-367 show that exact common homogeneous globalization destroys Whittaker `nY` incidence. WP-368 shows that bounded positive fixed scale preserves incidence only while exponentially suppressing large indices. WP-369 lets the scale move: the unique critical pure power is `Y_n=c/n`, exactly where `nY_n` freezes and finite--archimedean incidence factorizes; ordinary positive `L^2` then squares and diverges. Scale adaptation deserves credit only when the remaining transition coordinate stays source-specific and nonconstant.

Möbius Cancellation adds the extremizer/source-image audit. MC-386 identifies the sharp ambient Welch scale; MC-387 rigidifies its minimally concentrated near-extremizers into coset/annihilator geometry; MC-388 proves that the exact high-dimensional endpoint Bochner support is a projected simplex of affine rank `Theta(R)` and therefore cannot approach that abstract extremizer. A matched control can be sharp in its ambient category and still be irrelevant after source pullback.

These examples refine the audit into a compact sequence. Identify the minimal source carrier after exact algebraic generation. Ask whether acquisition creates a new coordinate or transports old data. Apply the destination's closure, summation or globalization. State the ambient topology and test whether it already explains the critical scale. Price inverse geometry and ask whether a finite physical inverse restores the hard coordinate. Pull theorem hypotheses, exceptional sets and even sharp extremizer shapes back to the source image. If continuation is required, ask whether proving it is independent of the target. If a signed analytic carrier survives, compare it to the largest generic function class still allowed. Finally run the strongest matched control while progressively restoring the suspected source relation.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem transferring Farey graph design, character-family estimates, zeta-primitive convolution, reciprocal matrix duality, lattice filtering, Wang mean values or Whittaker geometry between lines. Each quantitative statement remains line-specific. The cross-line content is the falsifiable audit: enrichment deserves credit only when newly acquired or retained structure survives exact destination operations, has controlled source/inverse cost, is not an ambient norm, automorphism, continuation or scale artifact, remains analytically identified with the intended destination observable, excludes the relevant ambient extremizers, and defeats the strongest matched control.
