# MI-021 — Representation enrichment buys leverage only when retained source information survives the quotient, breaks the control and keeps a usable margin

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including AF-451--AF-452, FD-231--FD-232, MC-414--MC-415, NB-245--NB-246, PC-369--PC-371, PL-391--PL-392, VIS-335--VIS-336, WI-367--WI-368 and WP-381--WP-383, together with the earlier line-specific foundations they extend. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, “the representation contains more information” is only the first gate. The retained datum must survive the exact quotient, completion or topology actually consumed downstream; it must distinguish the source from a strong matched control; and it must remain visible with enough margin or reserve at the destination scale. The latest findings sharpen all three later failures.

Arithmetic Fidelity shows that even the **admissible source class** can be misidentified if cancellation is performed too early. AF-451 corrects the residual-circuit picture: a Radon core belongs to a fixed source-law fiber only through a common positive completion that restores the source statistic and respects sparsity. AF-452 then identifies the conditioning object as the normalized completed secant carrier. The richer affine description is useful only after it is restricted to physically completable source pairs and given a positive observation gain.

Farey Discrepancy shows that operator invisibility and physical realizability are different. FD-231 supplies large signed switched-blind profiles; FD-232 proves that macroscopic negative capacity forces comparable positive slack. The meaningful quotient is therefore the source-forced affine class modulo blind directions measured against the positive cone, not the dimension or norm of the signed kernel.

Möbius Cancellation separates retained phase from the operations used to estimate it. MC-414 factors the translated character into endpoint phases, leaving the arithmetic information in a thin divisor-dependent domain. MC-415 shows that ordinary positive differencing introduces a zero-shift square that recreates lcm-level sieve structure. Information can survive algebraically and still be destroyed—or made too expensive—by completion and Cauchy before it yields a saving.

Nyman--Beurling gives both a conditioning warning and a source-faithful repair of the averaging quantifier. NB-245 shows that finite rational Cauchy data are exactly injective while normalized fixed-interior probes can lose a growing depth mark. NB-246 then uses the actual smooth Ford current: after demodulation its center energy is `O(H)`-bandlimited, so a uniform local `L^2` bound on scale `1/H` suffices for the selected point even though the zero-pair phase still resolves `1/X`. The usable representation is the one whose topology and averaging scale are generated by the source window itself.

Prime Circle shows why the strongest control can change when a representation retains a new kind of structure. PC-369--PC-370 keep Fock, signature and centered-refinement data inside the reciprocal common-unitary equivalence. PC-371 retains radial order and thereby escapes that control, but the resulting cone is universal for generic conjugation-stable unit-circle roots and its canonical moments classicalize to Ramanujan data. Passing one control only exposes the next: retained order must still become arithmetic discrimination rather than generic geometry or source decoding.

Prime Lattice shows that the decisive information can lie in the **representative killed by the quotient**. PL-392's Euler--Maclaurin tail is exactly what turns raw cutoffs into locally zero-faithful approximants, but that tail is Besicovitch-null on fixed interior vertical lines. PL-391 simultaneously shows that a half-line limiting zero density can be too averaged to recover the analytic divisor. A useful category must keep the continuation representative and individual-zero sensitivity, not merely the correct mean-square class.

Visual Exploration gives the inverse-cost control in explicit form. VIS-335 and VIS-336 exhibit sources for which Wang smoothing makes the output small while the derivative channel stays order one. The representation has not destroyed the information; it has moved its scale. Only arithmetic frequency migration plus a destination that does not repay the derivative cost would turn that generic attenuation into leverage.

Weil Inertia distinguishes repairable representation bookkeeping from a true nonlocal obstruction. WI-367 repairs the diagonal half-density transport by restoring the missing source multiplier. WI-368 then shows that the gamma cancellation fails in the cross-kernel sign, where diagonal or scalar normalizations cannot help. Exact transport and quantitative reserve must be proved on the same closed form.

Weil Positivity moves the dimension audit into topology. WP-381 gives a bounded Mangoldt selector; WP-382 collapses every fixed finite exact-null interior packet; WP-383 extends the collapse to arbitrary Banach/Hilbert-valued compact-open-continuous responses. Infinite coordinates buy nothing if they are all controlled at one fixed interior radius. A genuine escape must change the topology, approach the boundary, vary with source scale or delay exact selection.

The reusable audit is therefore ordered. First identify the true source class and the exact datum retained. Then track the quotient, completion and topology actually used. Apply the strongest matched control before scalarization. Finally compare the surviving signal with the destination's inverse modulus, positivity slack or coercive reserve. Representation enrichment earns mathematical leverage only after all of those gates remain open.

**Boundary.** This is an interface principle, not a theorem transferring estimates between lines. Completed Radon secants, positive Farey lifts, thin character domains, Ford transform conditioning, unitary-equivariant signatures, Besicovitch-null continuation tails, Wang inverse derivatives, gamma cross-kernels and compact-open cyclotomic rigidity are different mechanisms. Their common content is the audit: retention, survival, discrimination and quantitative usefulness are distinct claims.
