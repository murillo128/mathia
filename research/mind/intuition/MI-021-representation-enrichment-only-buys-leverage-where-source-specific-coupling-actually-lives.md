# MI-021 — Representation enrichment buys leverage only when retained source information discriminates and stays quantitatively usable

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including AF-416--AF-450, FD-200--FD-231, MC-382--MC-413, NB-217--NB-244, PC-351--PC-368, PL-361--PL-390, RE-216--RE-237, VIS-302--VIS-334, WI-350--WI-366 and WP-366--WP-382. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, “the representation contains more information” is only the first gate. The retained datum must also distinguish the source from a strong matched control, survive the exact quotient or completion actually consumed, be resolved at the physical scale of the destination and retain enough margin or reserve after normalization. Recent results give exact examples of failure at each later gate.

Arithmetic Fidelity moves from rank to admissible collision geometry. Known mass, positivity and affine source laws change which signed circuits can actually occur; AF-450 localizes sparse Radon collisions by their source-law barycenter. A richer observation family earns exact fidelity only on the declared fiber, and exact fidelity still says nothing about the near-collision margin.

Farey Discrepancy shows why source realizability can be stricter than operator nullspace geometry. FD-230 gives one-sided coercivity for nonnegative linear-capacity occupancy, while FD-231 constructs macroscopic signed blind directions with uniformly small divisor response. The useful object is not a large signed kernel vector but one that can be embedded in a single physical nonnegative reservoir profile at acceptable slack cost.

Möbius Cancellation adds **completion** as an information-loss gate. The well-factorable frame of MC-409 is genuinely different from the squared Selberg frame, and additive differencing in MC-413 genuinely creates a translated source phase. Nevertheless every complete conductor block erases the divisor-dependent phase exactly into `c_q(h)`. Information that exists algebraically can disappear at the averaging step unless it is estimated while still incomplete.

Nyman--Beurling shows that one can also demand too much information. The signed Ford coefficient needs an absolute phase anchor for reconstruction, but its decay needs only the squared modulus. NB-243 reduces that norm to a depth-marked local carrier pair energy with vanishing diagonal. The remaining difficulty is not absolute phase but source-local resolution: available pair/clustering theorems still coarse-grain the selected Ford block.

Prime Circle supplies an especially clean retention-versus-discrimination control. Heterogeneous Fock channels retain cross-grade resonant contractions that homogeneous Gram classification loses, and those contractions can reach canonical spectral moments. Yet all canonical word-Gram resonances are preserved by the common one-particle unitary relating the source to its reciprocal control. Extra information that both source and control share is not a discriminator.

Prime Lattice separates retained numerical data from the analytic category carrying the target. The energy-enriched norm closure can recover ordered frequencies, while the bounded AP zeta symbol exits its algebra before the critical line. Weakening to Besicovitch `B^2` carries the vertical zeta function through `Re(s)>1/2`, but the mean-square quotient cannot remember isolated zero values and lacks the old multiplicative index structure. One category has the weights, another has the continuation, and neither native form yet has all target information.

Visual Exploration gives an inverse-cost version of the same principle. Wang's source transform has no fixed real-frequency null and only first-order high-frequency attenuation. VIS-334 factors the exact inverse through one derivative plus memory, so a smooth gain in both output and derivative transfers back to the source. A transform gain is real only if arithmetic nonstationarity moves the cost into a derivative that the final destination does not need to repay.

Weil Inertia separates positivity from quantitative financing. The parity-generated gamma image is positive, so omitting it is sign-safe. But its normalized boundary-layer geometry cannot uniformly dominate the bare Carleman operator, so the same term cannot automatically finance the fixed OSPR reserve. A channel can have the right sign and the wrong scale.

Weil Positivity supplies a source-response-dimension control. A bounded pointed Mangoldt selector exists, yet WP-382 shows that every fixed finite interior Riesz packet with exact mixed-shell nullity collapses on the shell family to one Mangoldt line. More ambient analytic coordinates do not imply more source coordinates after the support quotient.

These examples refine one reusable audit. Identify the admitted source class and the exact information retained by the representation. Determine the equivalence group and strongest matched control. Track every regrouping, completion, parity reduction and closure that the argument actually applies. Compare detector resolution with the destination scale. Finally, quantify the surviving margin, reserve or normalization strength. Enrichment buys leverage only if it passes **all** of those gates.

**Boundary.** This is an interface principle, not a theorem transferring estimates between lines. Fiberwise Radon geometry, positive Farey reservoirs, incomplete character correlations, Ford pair energy, Fock resonances, almost-periodic symbol categories, Wang transform inversion, odd-fold form transport and cyclotomic Riesz rigidity are different mechanisms. Their common content is the audit: retention, discrimination and quantitative usefulness are distinct mathematical claims.
