# MI-021 — Representation enrichment only buys leverage where source-specific coupling actually lives

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of exact/current results [AF-377](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-377-bounded-support-interaction-components-hide-finite-order-aliases.md), [AF-378](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-378-order-reparameterization-preserves-sampling-fibres-but-not-translation-geometry.md), [MC-329](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-329-fixed-common-modulus-bias-energy-has-a-quartic-horizon.md), [WI-317](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-317-finite-localization-portfolios-cannot-contract-scalar-defect.md), [WP-329](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-329-deterministic-amalgamated-lifts-make-operator-valued-free-divisibility-universal-before-scalarization.md), and [WP-330](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-330-nondeterministic-amalgamated-noise-remains-universal-when-scalarization-annihilates-the-fluctuation-ideal.md). The mechanisms are different; only the information-placement principle is shared.

Several current branches exhibit the same structural failure in different mathematics: a construction can look richer by nominal dimension, arity, category, coordinate geometry, moment/order, noise content or portfolio size while the source-sensitive interaction still factors through a much smaller object or is killed by the destination map. The added degrees of freedom then rearrange, externalize or hide information rather than create a new destination resource.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes both the factorization and coordinate versions exact. AF-377 shows that sampled total positivity of every nominal determinant order can factor through uniformly bounded blocks when the support-scale interaction graph has bounded components. AF-378 then shows that a nonlinear order reparameterization can make the displayed rows look denser without changing any sampled determinant or false-positive fibre when the kernel is transported faithfully. If translation geometry is reimposed after the nonlinear change, the source category has changed. Nominally richer geometry is therefore not leverage unless the new relation is intrinsic rather than a redrawing or model replacement.

Weil Positivity gives the category/readout version in two stages. WP-329 places the exact Mangoldt law below a freely infinitely divisible operator-valued object whose enriched distribution is deterministic and identical for arbitrary scalar source laws; all specificity sits in the scalarization state. WP-330 shows that even adding genuine centered noise and nonzero completely positive covariance is insufficient when the scalarization annihilates the fluctuation ideal. The relevant resource is not merely mixed data existing upstream but source-dependent mixed data whose ideal survives the actual destination readout.

Weil Inertia gives the portfolio version. WI-317 allows finitely many exact source localizations and even lets the best localizer be chosen after seeing the test vector, yet no scalar defect contracts if every localized piece is controlled only by the same original scalar semibound. Exact decomposition and portfolio choice do not strengthen the premise; simultaneous recurrence exposes a common extremizing sequence.

Möbius Cancellation gives the family/statistic version. MC-329 compresses the full reciprocal endpoint character family into one nonnegative bias-energy statistic and reaches the same quartic source floor as the independent incidence/conductor architecture. Its strengthened exponent audit shows that moving the Burgess/Halász--Montgomery order can approach the quartic boundary from below but cannot cross to a fixed power beyond four. More modes or higher order do not create leverage while the same positive summary and exponent geometry remain load-bearing.

The reusable audit is therefore: **locate the smallest object in which source-specific coupling remains after exact factorization, coordinate transport, scalarization, localization and positive aggregation, then verify that the destination map actually sees that object.** Count complexity there, not in the ambient representation. A proposed enrichment is potentially useful only if the new coordinates interact through a source-forced relation that survives to the destination and cannot be reproduced by bounded components, faithful redrawing, an arbitrary/readout-null state, the same scalar premise on every piece, or a positive aggregate already at its analytic horizon.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem transferring AF interaction graphs to operator-valued probability, localization, or character families. Each obstruction is line-specific. The shared conclusion is only an audit principle about effective information placement and visibility: nominal richness is not a resource until a source-specific coupling across the added degrees of freedom is both proved and consumed by the destination.
