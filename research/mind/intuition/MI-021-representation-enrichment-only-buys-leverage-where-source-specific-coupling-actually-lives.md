# MI-021 — Representation enrichment buys leverage only when source-specific coupling survives identifiability, exact reduction and the destination quantifier

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including AF-416--AF-442, FD-200--FD-222, MC-382--MC-403, NB-217--NB-234, PC-351--PC-361, PL-361--PL-381, RE-216--RE-235, VIS-302--VIS-324, WI-350--WI-359 and WP-366--WP-378. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, enrichment is useful only when a new relation has an exact source preimage, is identifiable on the admissible finite-data class, survives the destination operation, is recoverable at the needed precision, and improves a load-bearing bottleneck without repaying the gain elsewhere. The relevant question is not how large the representation is, but **what source-specific relation survives source-class restriction, acquisition, inversion, transport, exact algebraic reduction, correlation geometry, positivity, normalization, globalization and the topology/quantifier of the final theorem?**

Arithmetic Fidelity now separates not just rigid from movable support, but **realized support provenance from coarse provenance**. AF-438 removes sequential shell peeling as a representation artifact. AF-439 shows finite-moment noninjectivity under latent support motion; AF-440 gives deep mark recovery on the exact reciprocal-square skeleton; AF-441 preserves that fidelity under controlled jitter when the realized support remains marked. AF-442 then shows that merely retaining the shell/cell label does not suffice for one high moment: an earlier latent support displacement of size about `(j/m)^(2N)/N` can exactly hide a later mark change. High order strengthens mark separation on a fixed skeleton while making latent-coordinate compensation exponentially cheaper. The right gate is the finite observation fibre on the actual retained source coordinates, not nominal resolution alone.

Farey Discrepancy upgrades the quantifier audit. FD-221 proves every fixed block of the periodic switched schedule remains repairable below literal Euler-prefix coverage. FD-222 extends the same mechanism to every `J<=C log log N`: exact Euler laws can coexist with polylogarithmically small switched observations and a super-square-root earlier-prefix defect. The positive null cycle is dimension-free, divisor reservoirs retain enough capacity when `2^J` is polylogarithmic, and exponential triangular conditioning is absorbed by fixed-power logarithmic cancellation. The next discriminator must therefore live in faster-growing capacity, literal coverage, or one persistent/cofinal source; “growing `J`” by itself is no longer enough.

Möbius Cancellation now carries the reduction audit through the entire source family. MC-397 consumes source-mode averaging by finite Fourier duality; MC-398 consumes divisor-pair multiplicity by lcm regrouping; MC-399 removes the lcm phase at first correlation; MC-400--MC-402 consume moving endpoints, complete endpoints and generic truncation through exact inversion/factorization. MC-403 then shows that the remaining nonprincipal family energy is exactly the signed discrepancy norm of lcm coefficient masses across source-signature classes. After the truncation inversion this becomes a Mertens-weighted discrepancy vector. “Collective source averaging” is therefore not a residual dimension: the surviving theorem must control this exact source-specific vector or leave the lcm-factorable frame.

Nyman--Beurling keeps the theorem quantifier in view from a different direction. NB-230--NB-234 show that the carrier transition is local and depth-conditioned, while fixed finite statistics can remain blind to matched packets. The exact profiled Ford mark becomes reconstructible only through a hierarchy of local mixed depth--phase moments whose order grows with source capacity. Strengthening the wrong fixed global average does not control the exceptional local windows consumed by a uniform endpoint.

Prime Circle and Prime Lattice continue to close “make the operator larger” escapes. PC-361 shows that an unbounded graded Hamiltonian with a strictly raising bounded source term can remain spectrally triangular. PL-377--PL-378 scalarize bounded normal complete multiplicativity and uniformly bounded invertible group completions. PL-379 then shows that finite-prime Wold data need not globalize, but PL-380--PL-381 identify natural repairs: a generating wandering vacuum or semibounded exact positive-energy covariance forces the regular multishift, with the latter leaving only a prime-independent internal Hamiltonian. Because the positive-energy proof works for arbitrary positive locally finite frequencies, solving representation globalization still creates no rational-prime or zero-sensitive discriminator.

Robin Extremal now demonstrates a full local-to-global price ledger. RE-231 identifies the microscopic coordinate `kappa=N/(m(a-1))`; RE-232 gives a sharp macroscopic arc but a normalized Wiener cost already worse than the existing finite-center construction; RE-233 resolves a sequence-sensitive tuning layer without lowering that cost; and RE-235 closes the super-broad fixed-arc escape by showing bounded normalized rate requires exponential destination thinning. Crossing a local normal-form threshold is useful only if the global destination and coefficient budget remain competitive.

Visual Exploration supplies the complementary sample-size audit. VIS-319--VIS-323 find a faithful unimodular representation, reduce the cell to fixed-gap runs and prove aggregate singular-series neutrality. VIS-324 then shows that the classical dimension-two Selberg upper-bound sieve already reaches the Wang target whenever each effective run is polynomially long: for `k<=M^(1-delta)` at the natural scale, the required `L/log^2 M` bound follows. The remaining resource is not a stronger theorem on each run but a new aggregation direction across subpolynomial or singleton fragments when `k=M^(1-o(1))` or `k>L`.

Weil Inertia sharpens the source-structure requirement. WI-357 gives local threshold continuity on actual null states; WI-358 shows scalar energy control is too weak cofinally; WI-359 shows that even principal graph norm plus an exact **abstract** zero-mode equation and a scalar perturbation-norm envelope remains too weak. Progress must use relations specific to Suzuki's actual arithmetic perturbation, not another generic regularity class.

Weil Positivity supplies the complementary topology control. WP-377 collapses exact radial mixed-shell nullity to the invariant sector for any strongly continuous unitary radial action. WP-378 leaves the radial category entirely and tests the canonical cyclotomic `H^2` Gram: it has genuine cross-prime terms, yet Euler-factorizes, has full composite support and a radical-only diagonal, while the exact Mangoldt selector appears only as unbounded boundary evaluation. More geometry is not enough when positivity and the arithmetic selector occupy incompatible topological levels.

These examples refine one reusable audit. First identify the admissible source class and ask whether the finite observation is identifiable on the **actually retained** source coordinates. Establish provenance and carrier scale, price any inverse, and perform exact Fourier/lcm/Gelfand/triangular/similarity reductions. Apply the first correlation/Cauchy step meant to exploit a variable, test whether a surviving family is merely Fourier-equivalent to one discrepancy vector, and ask whether truncation is exactly invertible to an equally hard arithmetic weight. For persistent families, distinguish fixed, slowly growing and genuinely cofinal quantifiers. For countable representations, identify the theorem that globalizes local structure and then ask whether its hypotheses are arithmetic or universal. Finally price the physical destination margin, aggregation length or coefficient cost after all representation artifacts are removed.

**Boundary.** This synthesis does not transfer quantitative thresholds between lines. AF latent-support compensation, Farey `O(log log N)` reservoir capacity, Möbius signature discrepancy, Nyman growing local moments, Prime-Lattice positive-energy globalization, Robin Wiener rates, Wang subpolynomial fragmentation, Weil threshold estimates and Hardy/radial positivity obstructions are different theorems. The cross-line claim is only the audit: enrichment deserves credit when it contributes source-selected information that is identifiable, survives exact reduction and inversion, remains stably usable in the final topology/quantifier, and cannot be reproduced by the strongest matched control.
