# MI-021 — Representation enrichment only buys leverage where source-specific coupling survives closure and matched controls

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including [FD-168](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-168-prefix-mobius-rows-give-coordinate-minimal-stable-partial-farey-sampling.md), [MC-337](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-337-odd-source-parity-all-or-nothing-self-phase-barrier.md), [NB-215](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-215-signed-fixed-shell-preconditioning-can-only-export-the-mellin-tariff.md), [PC-350](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-350-full-inversion-defect-is-faithful-via-two-by-two-fourier-decoder.md), [PL-359](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-359-hamburger-functional-equation-h2-triviality.md), [RE-171](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-171-exact-mertens-matching-still-permits-transient-post-selector-robin-excursions.md), [VIS-298](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-298-wang-gamma-recentering-removes-moving-h-barrier.md), [WI-348](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-348-global-phase-sensitivity-still-needs-resolution-collapse.md), and [WP-365](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-365-completed-rankin-selberg-tensor-gram-still-fails-at-critical-zeros.md). The mechanisms differ; only the information-placement/resource-accounting relation is shared.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when the added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, drastic compression can be legitimate when an exact theorem proves that discarded data are generated by retained data or lie outside the support of the relevant inverse. The relevant question is not “how much representation is present?” but **which carrier, relation, metric or sign structure remains after the destination performs every free reconstruction, quotient, completion, normalization and matched-control comparison, and what source theorem makes that survivor selective?**

Farey Discrepancy gives a constructive example of legitimate compression. FD-168 identifies the coordinate-minimal raw Fourier subset needed by the exact localized inverse: other modes can be discarded without changing the recovered prefix. The later quadratic-energy results show the converse danger: compressing to a positive scalar norm can lose rowwise inverse geometry and simultaneously impose unintended collective source coercivity. Representation size is not the invariant; the destination norm and exact inverse are.

Möbius Cancellation makes reconstruction complexity quotient-relative. Low Fourier degree can exclude a matched filter in standard coordinates, while exact source relations can make the same filter trivial; global parity can reconstruct hidden phase at high degree. What protects an architecture is not literal non-observability but the cost after all exact source relations are quotiented out.

Nyman--Beurling gives a sharper preconditioning audit. NB-215 shows that even signed/complex fixed-shell enrichment cannot lower the global source-normalized absolute reconstruction cost below the carrier scale when the signal is a nonzero mean. A profile may shift mass out of the central contour, but then source conditioning or frequency tails inherit the tariff. A richer representation only counts after acquisition, normalization and reconstruction have all been priced.

Prime Circle now supplies a direct warning against equating nonlinear richness with selectivity. PC-350 proves that a small solvable all-depth subsector of the regularized inversion signature already reconstructs every centered reciprocal shell profile. The same decoder works for non-arithmetic reciprocal controls. Infinite noncommutative depth is therefore genuine extra information but still only source recovery. The missing object must be a strict quotient/invariant that destroys generic reciprocal freedom while retaining a zero-sensitive arithmetic relation.

Prime Lattice adds a topology-versus-global-law warning. Ordinary multiplicativity is too local, complete multiplicativity too rigid, and PL-359 shows that the exact conductor-one Riemann functional equation is stronger still inside native Dirichlet `H^2`: Hamburger uniqueness selects zeta, but the Hilbert topology excludes it, leaving only zero. A canonical arithmetic constraint can therefore annihilate the enriched class instead of creating a useful intermediate family.

Visual Exploration shows that apparent representational complexity can be created by proof decomposition itself. VIS-298 recombines the exact gamma and right-half-plane zeta pieces before norm inequalities and removes the entire moving lower-source `H` barrier. An “extra channel” deserves credit only after artificial proof splits and deterministic normalizations are undone.

Weil Inertia removes both projective and ordinary smooth feature richness as generic escapes. WI-347 prices projective source resolution; WI-348 restores absolute phase and still finds a fixed-resolution covering number `O(rho^(-2))` for the phase-saturated carrier. A feature can produce extensive normalized rank only by resolving progressively smaller physical source differences, becoming increasingly ill-conditioned, growing aperture, injecting height dependence or using a direct arithmetic covariance identity. Ambient channel count and rapid phase winding are not source information at fixed resolution.

Weil Positivity gives the strongest closure analogue. WP-365 does not merely append scalar Gamma factors: it tensors the finite Hecke Gram with an independent positive Bessel/Whittaker Hilbert Gram, producing genuinely nonseparable finite--archimedean geometry and the completed zeta factors automatically. Yet after continuation the normalized overlap still blows up and the Fubini--Study metric turns negative near critical zeros. Even canonical local-product enrichment can fail when the target divisor remains multiplicatively embedded in the completed global geometry. The surviving route must change the non-product correlation law itself.

These examples refine the audit into five steps. First identify the **minimal carrier** after exact algebraic generation and source relations. Second apply the destination's **intrinsic closure/reconstruction/normalization** and ask what is regenerated, annihilated or repaid by an inverse tariff. Third specify the **topology and resolution** actually seen by the destination. Fourth run the strongest **matched control** that preserves the generic representation while removing the claimed arithmetic selectivity. Fifth state the **source-specific theorem** that leaves a nontrivial selective survivor after all four operations.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem transferring Farey inversion, Möbius parity, Mellin reconstruction, reciprocal signatures, Hamburger uniqueness, Wang decomposition, bow metric entropy or completed Rankin--Selberg geometry between lines. Each quantitative statement remains line-specific. The cross-line content is the falsifiable audit: before crediting a richer representation, identify what survives free operations and matched controls, what resolution/reconstruction tariff it pays, whether the resulting category is nontrivial, and which source theorem makes the survivor unavailable to a cheaper generic model.
