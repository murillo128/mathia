# MI-021 — Representation enrichment only buys leverage where source-specific coupling actually survives

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including FD-200--FD-209, MC-382--MC-390, NB-217--NB-224, PC-351--PC-356, PL-361--PL-371, RE-216--RE-221, VIS-302--VIS-311, WI-350--WI-354 and WP-366--WP-370. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, enrichment is useful when a new relation has an exact source preimage, survives the destination operation and improves a load-bearing bottleneck without repaying the gain elsewhere. The relevant question is not “how much representation is present?” but **what source-specific relation survives acquisition, closure, topology, normalization, inverse recovery, localization, naturality constraints, analytic identification and matched controls?**

Farey Discrepancy shows both constructive leverage and its inverse price. Signed source-slab differences and low-resistance graph geometry improve the pre-norm representation, but fixed-depth roots and then every common sublogarithmic depth on a square-root scale window admit an explicit physical inverse with only subpower cost. Growing transform depth becomes potentially new only through scale-dependent incoherence or a collective signed relation that never produces a common-depth pointwise bound.

Prime Circle supplies the covariance warning. Free-Fock/full-signature lifts remain related to reciprocal control by second-quantized unitary conjugacy, and even the native radial-energy Gram metric transforms as `G~ = J^*GJ`. A source-dependent representation is not discriminating merely because it is internally asymmetric; it must fail to be transported covariantly by the matched control for an independently arithmetic reason.

Prime Lattice separates ambient criticality, local source rigidity and global continuation. `ell^p` and Hardy geometry manufacture critical-looking walls. Complete multiplicativity plus pole neutrality and continuation gives a genuine zero-sensitive theorem interface, and PL-370 proves that a local zero at `1` already forces finite prime-harmonic distance to Liouville. PL-371 then supplies the missing control: reciprocal-prime-summable Liouville perturbations can preserve that exact local zero while placing a real continuation obstruction at any prescribed `alpha in (1/2,1)`. Source selection is real, but it creates no continuation gain unless additional structure controls the sparse tail on the exponent scales actually crossed.

Robin Extremal gives a representation-parameter version of the same audit. The universal separated-prediction floor is one Wiener exponent, while the fixed single-center architecture carries a factor-two floor. Complexifying the center and then treating the finite truncated polynomial with exact cancellation do not remove that stricter cost. Parameter enrichment is not new information when the consumed predictor remains in the same approximation geometry.

Visual Exploration supplies progressively stronger matched controls. Exact prime-power support, magnitudes and same-base multiplicative phase relations still cancel under randomized cross-prime phases; the actual deterministic time orbit has the same long-time quadratic mean as the multiplicative Haar control. VIS-310 showed that hard tail truncation plus one global ratio gap manufactured a quintic finite-window horizon. VIS-311 preserves coefficient-weighted local ratio spacing and lowers that sufficient horizon to cubic. The remaining explicit loss is now the **uniform hard-tail representation**, so another finite-frequency spacing refinement is not automatically new leverage.

Nyman--Beurling gives a positive example and then prices it. Generic bounded strip analyticity is sharp for the transported `-zeta'/zeta` carrier. Retaining the actual zero spectrum and Bellotti's near-one density beats that ambient extremizer almost to Ford height. NB-224 reaches the exact Ford denominator after widening the logarithmic source shell to `Theta(X)`, which narrows the vertical carrier to `Theta(1/X)` and lets local zero counts replace a unit-window population bound. The gain is genuine but purchases height by sacrificing multiplicative source resolution: the source spans a power-sized interval `[e^X,e^((1+theta)X)]`.

Weil Positivity gives a scale-and-naturality warning. Fixed scale suppresses the tail; homogeneous globalization removes incidence; the unique critical pure-power scale freezes `nY`; and exact diagonal Hecke intertwining forces that same ray. A naturality law can select a critical exponent while simultaneously erasing the mixed coordinate that motivated the representation.

Möbius Cancellation shows that source pullback can change not only the ambient extremizer but the direction of a pseudorandomness heuristic. The projected endpoint simplex excludes the abstract Welch near-extremizer and yields an explicit fixed gap above `H/R`. But MC-390 proves that if the physical dual code has distance at least five, four-wise independence of coordinate signs forces `Omega(H)` correlations at scale `R^(-1/2)`. High dual distance is therefore hostile to the desired good-mode theorem. The viable branch requires explicit weight-`<=4` source-incidence parity relations.

Weil Inertia adds a localization-normalization warning. WI-354 shows that Wang's current short-interval pair-correlation theorem loses fixed Fourier support as the power interval shrinks, while its optimized simple-critical proportion also decreases and every fixed-power window still contains macroscopically many zeros. A more local representation is not automatically a stronger defect certificate when the arithmetic aperture narrows and the output remains normalized by local population.

These examples refine the audit into one sequence. Identify the minimal source carrier after exact algebraic generation. Ask whether acquisition creates a new coordinate or only transports old data. Apply the destination closure, norm, certificate or spectral quotient. State the ambient topology and test whether it already explains the critical scale. Price the physical inverse and every localization/resolution trade. Solve any covariance or naturality equation before crediting source-adapted structure. Pull theorem hypotheses, exceptional sets and sharp extremizers back to the exact source image. If continuation is required, test sparse source perturbations that preserve local diagnostics while changing the global domain. If finite-time averaging is required, preserve weighted spectral geometry and the infinite tail at the destination norm. Finally run the strongest matched control at the same pointwise, finite-height or individual-defect quantifier actually consumed downstream.
