# MI-021 — Representation enrichment only buys leverage where source-specific coupling survives closure and matched controls

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including [AF-387](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-387-connected-support-does-not-repair-nonstrict-solid-pf3-recognition.md), [FD-168](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-168-prefix-mobius-rows-give-coordinate-minimal-stable-partial-farey-sampling.md), [MC-337](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-337-odd-source-parity-all-or-nothing-self-phase-barrier.md), [NB-213](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-213-absolute-mellin-primitivization-preserves-a-positive-power-vk-tariff.md), [PC-324](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-324-one-residue-green-controls-have-classical-hilbert-edge-zeros.md), [RE-171](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-171-exact-mertens-matching-still-permits-transient-post-selector-robin-excursions.md), [VIS-263](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-263-subprime-band-weight-has-exact-quadratic-carrier.md), [WI-347](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-347-extensive-bow-feature-rank-requires-projective-resolution-collapse.md), and [WP-363](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-363-eisenstein-rankin-selberg-gram-collapses-at-critical-exponent-and-first-jet-is-indefinite.md). The mechanisms differ; only the information-placement/resource-accounting relation is shared.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when the added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, an apparently drastic compression can be legitimate when an exact theorem proves that discarded data are generated by retained data or are outside the support of the relevant inverse. The relevant question is not “how much representation is present?” but **which carrier, metric, cone or invariant remains after the destination performs every free closure/reconstruction/scalarization operation, and what source hypothesis makes the survivor selective?**

Arithmetic Fidelity gives both compression and its boundary. AF-383 removes one Toeplitz placement tuple at fixed determinant order, and AF-385 removes the second under a strict lower-order hierarchy. AF-386 shows that at order two connected support can replace strictness. AF-387 proves that support topology and lower-order interiority are separate at order three: a connected `PF_2` sequence can satisfy every solid `3x3` inequality and still fail a gapped `3x3` minor because zero lower-order minors inside the support block sign propagation. A compressed generator family is complete only after the exact zero-minor stratum needed by its recognition theorem has been paid for.

Farey Discrepancy gives a constructive version of legitimate compression. FD-167 proves a uniform `sqrt(30)` inverse modulus for the complete Farey field on the square-root source prefix. FD-168 then identifies the union of the nonzero Möbius inverse rows as the **unique coordinate-minimal subset of raw Fourier coordinates** that still recovers a smaller prefix over the full formal source space, with the same localized inverse modulus. Discarding the other raw Fourier modes costs nothing because the exact inverse never uses them. The compression is substantive precisely because support and stability are proved, not because a smaller representation was guessed.

Möbius Cancellation makes reconstruction cost quotient-relative. Low Fourier degree can exclude the matched filter in standard coordinates, yet source coordinates can make that filter degree one unless own-phase access is forbidden; global parity can then reconstruct the hidden phase at high degree. What protects the architecture is not literal non-observability but the reconstruction degree after exact source relations are quotiented out.

Nyman--Beurling now gives a particularly sharp representation audit. NB-212 shows that the prime-harmonic plateau is not load-bearing for the current log-squared Vinogradov--Korobov corridor: one smooth full-von-Mangoldt barycenter already exposes it. NB-213 then tests the opposite maneuver, replacing `-zeta'/zeta` by the smoother primitive `log zeta`. If the transferred contour is closed by absolute values, differentiating the Mellin carrier restores a factor `X=log x`; on the coupled diagonal this is `Q^(2/3+o(1))`, so the apparently cheaper representation remains in the same positive-power prefactor class. Enrichment or smoothing deserves credit only after the cost of reconstructing the actual source observable has been charged.

Prime Circle shows why moving from an absolute object to a relative one is not enough. PC-323 subtracts the universal Hilbert continuous core and leaves trace-class relative data, but PC-324 constructs genuine off-band relative-determinant zeros already in one-residue non-arithmetic controls. Those zeros are classical generalized-Hilbert edge eigenvalues controlled only by a residue offset. The source-sensitive carrier, if any, must survive subtraction **and** the matched one-channel relative spectrum.

Robin Extremal exhausts one scalar invariant completely. Exact Mertens normalization can coexist with an order-one transient selected-prefix excursion and exact later repayment under negligible KV-relative perturbation. Adding precision to that scalar invariant cannot help because it is already exact; the missing carrier is spatial localization of its signed debt.

Visual Exploration shows that changing coordinates can expose a real coefficient without making it source-selective. After removing a nonanalytic cusp, a surviving quadratic coefficient still carries a conditioning cost, and explicit subprime-band controls can produce the same coefficient entirely from zero-frequency pole compensation. A representation adapted to the singularity is useful only after both its conditioning and the source-selective part of its first surviving channel are isolated.

Weil Inertia now removes “nonlinear feature richness” as a generic escape much more broadly than an analytic-class argument. WI-343--WI-346 show fixed-aperture compressibility for linear, fixed-degree polynomial and uniformly analytic maps. WI-347 proves the underlying projective statement: any height-independent feature family with a height-uniform projective continuity modulus has bounded effective dimension when `H Delta<=1`. Extensive output rank forces the useful source-resolution radius to collapse like `O(H Delta/r)`. Ambient feature count and formal nonlinearity are therefore irrelevant unless the map resolves progressively finer differences of the physical carrier family at nonvanishing scale.

Weil Positivity supplies the closure analogue. Earlier Hecke/cusp constructions retained arithmetic labels before positivity but scalarized them to `1/p`, rank-one pullbacks or modulus squares. WP-363 shows that even a genuinely two-parameter positive Rankin--Selberg Gram does not survive the Weil half-density: the exact `1/zeta(2s)` factor makes the whole kernel vanish at `s=1/2`, and the first nonzero jet is indefinite. The logarithmic derivative recovers the desired linear Mangoldt carrier only after positivity has been lost and the zeta divisor has entered. Non-scalar enrichment therefore matters only if the **same positive category survives the critical specialization with the required linear arithmetic amplitude**.

These examples refine the audit into four steps. First identify the **minimal carrier** after exact algebraic generation, quotienting, polarization, scalarization, projective compression or universal-background subtraction. Second apply the **intrinsic closure/reconstruction** available to the destination and ask what information is regenerated for free, annihilated, or repaid by a reconstruction tariff. Third identify the **metric/topology and resolution actually seen by the destination** on the remaining carrier. Fourth state the **source hypothesis or matched-control separation** that makes that survivor selective rather than universal, tautological or cheaply reproducible.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem transferring Toeplitz recognition, Farey inversion, source-phase parity, Mellin carrier reconstruction, generalized-Hilbert spectrum, Mertens compensation, cusp duality, bow projective width or Rankin--Selberg collapse between lines. Each quantitative statement remains line-specific. The cross-line content is the falsifiable resource test: before crediting a richer representation, identify what survives the destination's free operations, what resolution and reconstruction tariff it pays at the moving scale, and which source theorem proves that the survivor carries information unavailable to a cheaper matched model.
