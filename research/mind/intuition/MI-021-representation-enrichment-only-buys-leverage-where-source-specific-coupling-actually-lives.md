# MI-021 — Representation enrichment only buys leverage where source-specific coupling survives closure and matched controls

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including [AF-387](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-387-connected-support-does-not-repair-nonstrict-solid-pf3-recognition.md), [FD-168](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-168-prefix-mobius-rows-give-coordinate-minimal-stable-partial-farey-sampling.md), [MC-337](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-337-odd-source-parity-all-or-nothing-self-phase-barrier.md), [NB-176](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-176-euler-weighted-fourier-fingerprints-certify-source-complexity.md), [PC-324](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-324-one-residue-green-controls-have-classical-hilbert-edge-zeros.md), [RE-171](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-171-exact-mertens-matching-still-permits-transient-post-selector-robin-excursions.md), [VIS-263](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-263-subprime-band-weight-has-exact-quadratic-carrier.md), [WI-321](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-321-hilbert-valued-channelization-cannot-contract-scalar-weil-semibound.md), and the Weil-positivity closure through [WP-356](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-356-hecke-truncation-commutator-is-positive-cusp-annulus-energy-but-does-not-isolate-weil-prime-weight.md). The mechanisms differ; only the information-placement/resource-accounting relation is shared.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when the added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, an apparently drastic compression can be legitimate when an exact theorem proves that discarded data are generated by retained data or are outside the support of the relevant inverse. The relevant question is not “how much representation is present?” but **which carrier, metric, cone or invariant remains after the destination performs every free closure/reconstruction/scalarization operation, and what source hypothesis makes the survivor selective?**

Arithmetic Fidelity gives both compression and its boundary. AF-383 removes one Toeplitz placement tuple at fixed determinant order, and AF-385 removes the second under a strict lower-order hierarchy. AF-386 shows that at order two connected support can replace strictness. AF-387 proves that support topology and lower-order interiority are separate at order three: a connected `PF_2` sequence can satisfy every solid `3x3` inequality and still fail a gapped `3x3` minor because zero lower-order minors inside the support block sign propagation. A compressed generator family is complete only after the exact zero-minor stratum needed by its recognition theorem has been paid for.

Farey Discrepancy gives a constructive version of legitimate compression. FD-167 proves a uniform `sqrt(30)` inverse modulus for the complete Farey field on the square-root source prefix. FD-168 then identifies, for a smaller prefix `r<=R`, the union of the nonzero Möbius inverse rows as the **unique coordinate-minimal subset of raw Fourier coordinates** that still recovers the prefix over the full formal source space, with the same localized inverse modulus. For `R<=H^(1/2-delta)` this support has size `R H^{o(1)}=o(sqrt H)`. Discarding the other raw Fourier modes costs nothing because the exact inverse never uses them. The compression is substantive precisely because support and stability are proved, not because a smaller representation was guessed.

Möbius Cancellation makes reconstruction cost quotient-relative. MC-335 shows low Fourier degree can exclude the matched filter in standard coordinates; MC-336 reveals that source coordinates make that filter degree one unless own-phase access is forbidden. MC-337 then shows literal exclusion is still insufficient as an invariant: for odd source rank the global parity relation determines `x_i` from the other phases. What protects the architecture below maximal degree is not non-observability but the `R-1` degree needed to reconstruct the hidden phase. Complexity must therefore be measured after exact source relations are quotiented out.

Nyman--Beurling shows that representation complexity must be measured at source resolution **and can sometimes be certified by the source's own coordinates**. NB-167 identifies capped Poisson transport as the metric seen by the bounded/Lipschitz source kernels; NB-174 defines approximate stationary linear complexity after quotienting residual motion below that resolution; NB-175 lower-bounds it with any jointly contractive nuclear-response witness. NB-176 then supplies a canonical witness: the actual Euler frequencies `log n`, weighted by `Lambda(n)n^(-1-a)`, form an order-one capped-Poisson Hilbert feature map, so the singular geometry of the resulting Euler response Gram directly certifies source-resolved template complexity. This is genuine representation enrichment because the extra coordinates are not arbitrary; they are the individual modes coherently summed by `zeta'/zeta`. The remaining question is whether their Hilbert diversity becomes useful scalar phase alignment.

Prime Circle shows why moving from an absolute object to a relative one is not enough. PC-323 subtracts the universal Hilbert continuous core and leaves trace-class relative data, but PC-324 constructs genuine off-band relative-determinant zeros already in one-residue non-arithmetic controls. Those zeros are classical generalized-Hilbert edge eigenvalues controlled only by a residue offset. The source-sensitive carrier, if any, must therefore survive subtraction **and** the matched one-channel relative spectrum; multi-residue interference is the next unclosed object, not relative zeros in general.

Robin Extremal exhausts one scalar invariant completely. RE-169 identifies Mertens normalization as the exact total Robin-kernel charge. RE-171 shows that even exact `Gamma(Q)=gamma` can coexist with an order-one transient selected-prefix excursion and exact later repayment under negligible KV-relative perturbation. Adding precision to the invariant cannot help because the invariant is already exact; the missing carrier is spatial localization of its signed debt.

Visual Exploration shows that changing coordinates can expose a real coefficient without making it source-selective. VIS-257 annihilates the nonanalytic `|q|` cusp directly, but the sharp inequality `|m_2|<=(b^2/8)||nu||_TV` turns the surviving quadratic channel into a `b^(-2)` conditioning problem. VIS-262 proves some admissible weights have nonzero quadratic coefficient. VIS-263 then constructs an explicit weight supported below the first nonzero prime frequency whose coefficient is nevertheless exactly `1/(2pi^2)`, entirely from zero-frequency pole compensation. Thus a representation adapted to the singularity is valuable only after both its conditioning **and the source-selective part of its first surviving channel** are isolated; nonzero amplitude alone can be a universal background.

Weil Inertia makes the representation/information distinction literal. WI-321 allows arbitrary Hilbert-valued diagonal amplification, yet polarization of the scalar semibound already generates the corresponding Loewner Gram inequality. More channels, phases and fixed PSD mixing remain inside the same admissible cone. A useful matrix state must contribute inequalities outside that scalar-generated cone.

Weil Positivity now shows that even **arithmetic coupling before positivity** can still collapse at the final closure. WP-338--WP-342 had already shown that stronger topology or operator-valued stationary transport buys leverage only when the desired arithmetic quadratic direction survives scalarization. WP-356 tests a genuinely source-native finite-prime insertion rather than an abstract operator enlargement. On the constant cusp mode, the self-adjoint prime Hecke operator becomes `S_(log p)+S_(-log p)` in log height, and its commutator with the sharp cusp projection satisfies

`[P_tau,T_p]^*[P_tau,T_p]=1_(tau-log p,tau+log p]`.

The Hecke label is real and enters before positivity, but the positive square retains it only as unsigned annulus width. The critical Eisenstein scattering phase survives only in a sign-indefinite interference term, while the natural Hecke normalization loses the desired `p^(-1/2)` attenuation after the cusp Jacobian and quadratic norm are applied. Continuous nonarithmetic translations and arbitrary unitary boundary phases preserve the same positivity architecture. Representation enrichment therefore buys leverage only if the **post-closure carrier**, not merely the pre-closure construction, remains source-selective with the required sign and weight.

These examples refine the audit into four steps. First identify the **minimal carrier** after exact algebraic generation, quotienting, polarization, scalarization or universal-background subtraction. Second apply the **intrinsic closure/reconstruction** available to the destination and ask what information is regenerated for free — or, as FD-168 shows, which ambient coordinates the exact inverse never uses at all. Third identify the **metric/topology and resolution actually seen by the destination** on the remaining carrier. Fourth state the **source hypothesis or matched-control separation** that makes that survivor selective rather than universal, tautological or cheaply reproducible.

This explains why some reductions are beneficial while some enrichments are not. AF compresses only after paying the correct interiority/zero-pattern hypothesis; FD sparse Fourier recovery is valid because the inverse support and modulus are exact; MC variable hiding matters only through reconstruction degree; NB source-native Euler features matter because they are contractive in the source metric and coincide with the actual source expansion; PC relative spectrum remains nonselective until one-channel Hilbert controls are removed; RE exact scalar normalization still lacks localization; VIS nonzero quadratic amplitude remains nonselective until pole background is removed; WI channelization remains in a scalar-generated cone; and WP Hecke enrichment remains universal annulus geometry when the positive square erases the divisor-sensitive orientation and critical half-density.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem transferring Toeplitz recognition, Farey inversion, source-phase parity, Poisson-resolution variation, Euler fingerprints, generalized-Hilbert spectrum, Mertens compensation, cusp duality, Weil localization or Hecke-truncation geometry between lines. Each quantitative statement remains line-specific. The cross-line content is the falsifiable resource test: before crediting a new representation, identify what survives the destination's free closure/reconstruction/scalarization operations, what topology and resolution price it at the correct moving scale, and which matched control or source theorem proves that the survivor carries information unavailable to a cheaper model.
