# MI-021 — Representation enrichment buys leverage only when the escape survives the source class, effective geometry and destination category

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including AF-451--AF-458, FD-231--FD-237, MC-414--MC-420, NB-245--NB-252, PC-369--PC-375, PL-391--PL-397, VIS-335--VIS-341, WI-367--WI-372 and WP-381--WP-386, together with the earlier line-specific foundations they extend. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, “the representation contains more information” is only the first gate. The retained datum must survive the actual source class and its quantifiers, the exact quotient/topology and permitted operation order, distinguish the source from matched controls, reduce to a genuinely different mathematical resource rather than a richer presentation of a classical carrier, and retain the required margin or sign at the destination. The newest results make **effective source geometry** an explicit part of that audit: ambient dimension, formal coordinates, smooth charts and rescaled windows can all overstate the resource actually available.

Arithmetic Fidelity supplies the source-secant version. AF-456--AF-457 classify full-simplex and Hilbert sparse-mixture geometry; AF-458 shows how the obstruction transfers to a restricted arithmetic source. A complete paired sign `k`-cube in the completed source-law secants already forces the Banach type-`p` ceiling `k^(1/p-1)`. The target cannot recover a margin that the source carrier presents in this extremal geometry, while absence of that cube is only an escape from the obstruction, not a lower-gain theorem.

Farey Discrepancy shows that some apparent costs can instead disappear after the right exact coordinates are chosen. FD-235--FD-236 prove that the natural switched quotients are too coarse, but FD-237 shows that the finite divisor-response map is unimodular. An interior real reservoir profile can therefore be rounded in response space and pulled back to integer occupancies with only `tau(n)` coefficient error and bounded switched-response error. This removes generic bulk integrality from the main ledger while leaving the finer-topology, capacity-boundary and source-coherence problems intact.

Möbius Cancellation gives the Fourier-support analogue of the source-secant audit. MC-419 prices genuine multivariable positivity by reciprocal bandwidth volume; MC-420 proves that a symbol supported on an injected rank-`r` integer lattice factors exactly through an `r`-torus. Thus a two-coordinate anti-diagonal or any other formal tensorization with rank-one support still pays the one-variable Fejér floor. Independent variables count only when the physical Fourier support has the corresponding integer rank, and sparse support may cost more than rank alone predicts.

Nyman--Beurling gives the scale analogue. NB-251 shows that bandwidth genuinely super-`H` would disperse the standard matched Ford packet, but NB-252 proves that dilating the same shell also renormalizes `J` and recreates the packet at the new `H`. The useful resource is therefore bandwidth added relative to a fixed source geometry, not a change of coordinates that enlarges both the window and its natural unit.

Prime Circle retains its canonical measure-change/weighted-isometry control. Prime Lattice now has an equally explicit matched control: PL-397 computes the leading fixed-gap completed saddle--transition kernel and finds a universal error-function/chirp profile depending on additive gap and transition coordinate, not factorization. Prime-exponent coordinates do not turn that archimedean kernel into arithmetic leverage. Any surviving mechanism must supply an additional source-specific coupling before scalarization and prove that it survives the completed sum.

Visual Exploration separates fixed smoothing from new arithmetic information. VIS-339--VIS-340 reduce the natural prime packet and summatory observables to invertible images of the same normalized theta remainder, and VIS-341 shows that any fixed polynomial improvement in spectral truncation changes only the Korobov--Vinogradov constant, not its exponent class. A new envelope therefore needs information beyond fixed-order smoothing and must still survive the inverse-derivative ledger.

Weil Positivity continues to rule out stationary and pointwise-local positive boundary selectors. Weil Inertia now sharpens the financing side from one scalar or rank condition to a full distributional requirement. WI-371 gives a positive-density negative sector; WI-372 shows that any additive repair must have linearly many positive eigenvalues above every depth threshold `t<C_Gamma`, yielding quantile and factorized singular-value lower bounds. Nominal source dimension or total Schatten mass is not the same resource as the spectral profile on the actual bad subspace.

The reusable audit is ordered: identify the completed physical source class and its coupled limits; compute the effective secant/support/scale geometry rather than ambient coordinates; track the exact quotient, topology, representation and allowed operation order; apply the strongest matched control; then compare the surviving source-specific quantity with the destination inverse modulus, sign requirement, bandwidth or coercive spectral profile. Similar vocabulary across lines remains analogy unless the objects and hypotheses are explicitly identified.

**Boundary.** This is an interface principle, not a theorem transferring estimates between lines. Banach type on source secants, unimodular response quantization, integer support rank, Ford shell scaling, universal Riemann--Siegel transition kernels, Korobov--Vinogradov optimization and gamma repair spectra live in different mathematical categories. Their common role is only to distinguish a real source resource from representation, rescaling or generic capacity.
