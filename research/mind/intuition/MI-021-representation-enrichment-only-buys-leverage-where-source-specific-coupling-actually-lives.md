# MI-021 — Representation enrichment buys leverage only when the escape survives source geometry, closure and destination category

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact/supported results including AF-451--AF-461, FD-231--FD-239, MC-414--MC-423 and MC-497, NB-245--NB-255, PC-369--PC-378, PL-391--PL-400, VIS-335--VIS-344, WI-367--WI-375 and WP-381--WP-388 plus WP-423. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific and no estimate is transferred between these categories.

Across Mathia, “the representation contains more information” is only the first gate. The retained datum must survive the actual source class and its quantifiers, exact quotient/topology and permitted operation order, support or coherence geometry, the closure used to obtain positivity or a norm, matched controls, complex phase where it is load-bearing, and the destination margin. The newest results increasingly turn that audit into exact resource ceilings.

Arithmetic Fidelity now shows that neither finite linear codimension nor regular finite-dimensional smooth nonlinearity protects paired-cube geometry by itself. AF-460 proves `D_k=0` for every fixed `k` on the normalized kernel carrier of any bounded finite-rank law over an infinite alphabet. AF-461 transfers that result locally to every regular point of a finite-dimensional `C^1` source law: normalized same-fiber secants recover the unit sphere of the derivative kernel in closure. Combined with AF-459, nontrivial-type target cancellation is unavoidable whenever the physical completed-secant carrier contains that local fiber. The source resource must therefore be actual tangent-incompleteness of the physical carrier, not merely the presence of nonlinear equations.

Farey Discrepancy makes the complementary observation budget quantitative beyond sparsity. FD-239 proves that arbitrary sample patterns with octave density `eta<9/pi^2-3/4=0.161890652...` still admit integer signed divisor-response null profiles carrying macroscopic capacity in both signs. The constant is a certified survival range of the construction, not a sharp coercivity threshold. Sparse placement is therefore not the missing resource, and even a sufficiently low positive density is insufficient without denser/structured observation or physical one-sided transversality.

Möbius Cancellation distinguishes ambient dimension, support rank, shell-compatible width, exact arithmetic state volume and now source occupation of exceptional geometry. MC-422 shows that a full positive coordinate box inside a thin product shell pays `sum B_j/U_j <= Delta/P_0`; factor axes can be algebraically independent yet have too little joint width to realize the multiaxis gain of MC-419. MC-423 then closes the most literal product-preserving tangent escape at fixed depth: an exact factorization fiber has only `d_d(m)=m^{o(1)}` states, so no injective grid inside it has polynomial cell volume, and a completely multiplicative total phase is constant there. Tangent language counts only after near-tangent cross-fiber state volume, source variation and closure geometry are proved.

MC-497 adds a different admission test for the surviving first-exit endpoint route. The quadratic short-overlap phase has sparse large-bias start classes by a deterministic second-moment bound, but the theorem is only average over physical starts. Those exceptional classes matter only if the **actual first-exit endpoint map with its true reconstruction weights** places substantial source mass on them. Enriching the ambient character family or displaying a sparse exceptional set is not leverage until source incidence into that set is proved.

Nyman--Beurling now prices the signed scalar escape as conditioning. NB-254 shows that positive scalar carrier span `W` forces a `1/W` coherence window. NB-255 normalizes an arbitrary signed/complex scalar mixture by its Euler return and shows that total variation `V` can shrink the guaranteed Fourier--Laplace coherence radius only to `c/(WV)`. The matched Ford population becomes `R/(WV)`, so signs do not create free bandwidth: destroying this source-side coherence mechanism requires variation on the scale `R/W`. What remains open is whether legal convolution/projection can hide that conditioning cost in the destination norm, or whether a genuinely non-scalar channel is needed.

Prime Circle and Prime Lattice show two further ways an apparent escape can collapse after assembly. PC-376 identifies every finite pairwise-coprime canonical Chebyshev transfer family with standard polydisk shifts. PC-377 classifies the direct scalar all-prime sum by `ell^2` closability and `ell^1` boundedness. PC-378 then shows that the canonical commuting Euler-product completion is exactly the classical weighted divisor transform, with Bohr symbol `zeta(w+conj(s)-1)`, and inherits the same movable summability thresholds. Explicit zeta syntax after the quotient is therefore not new spectral information. On Prime Lattice, PL-399 gives the `1/d` mesoscopic continuation tariff, and PL-400 shows that magnitude alone is insufficient: in the phase-pinned regime the leading real continuation tail vanishes on even gaps, precisely where von-Mangoldt pair bulk can live. An arithmetic coupling must match the complex channel phase to its source support, not merely attach a large arithmetic weight to a nonzero absolute tail.

Visual Exploration turns scalar multiscale and phase freedom into complexity lower bounds. VIS-343 proves that `N` distinct pure Wang dilates can cancel at most `N-1` tail moments. VIS-344 adds translations and shows that uniform asymptotic cancellation decouples by translation frequency: every active phase fiber must independently pay the full moment order, so `N>=Br` for `B` fibers and order `r`. Combined with VIS-342, subpolynomial scalar banks remain in the old asymptotic class unless a genuinely finite-window, adaptive, frequency-dependent or source-signed estimate enters.

Weil Positivity and Weil Inertia sharpen locality and dependency order in complementary ways. WP-388 closes the regular strongly local Markov sector: admitting the discontinuous mixed shells forces effective intervals into unit cells, where all shell profiles are constant and diffusion energy vanishes. WP-423 closes a later representation-enrichment escape at the equilibrium boundary. A decoupled product dynamics with finite or countable atomic type-I active fibre may contain every desired opposite Bohr frequency, but every positive-temperature KMS state is separately invariant and blockwise Gibbs-factorized; nonzero source time degrees therefore vanish against every fibre observable. Programmable spectral richness does not become source coupling merely because the total degree can be made zero. A surviving equilibrium route must change the source--fibre dynamics or fibre category and prove the nontrivial KMS joining, not just enlarge the auxiliary spectrum.

On Weil Inertia, WI-374 shows that sufficiently late endpoint collars are already gamma-positive, so the exact negative profile of WI-373 is inherited bulk debt. WI-375 then shows that the printed common-cut positive pivot is still inherited from the invalid gamma-free lower metric; the later same-vector Schur identity places response correctly only after that pivot exists. The repaired source metric must therefore be proved on the gamma-retaining form before downstream elimination can use it.

The reusable audit is ordered: identify the completed physical source and coupled limits; compute effective secant/support/coherence/state geometry; identify exact kernels and quotients; account for domain admissibility and the closure used to create new variables or positivity; retain complex phase when the destination projection is phase-sensitive; check whether exceptional or auxiliary geometry is actually occupied by the source; apply the strongest matched control; then compare the surviving source-specific quantity with the destination inverse modulus, sign condition or spectral profile. Similar vocabulary across lines remains analogy unless objects and hypotheses are explicitly matched.

**Boundary.** This is an interface principle, not a theorem transferring Banach type, divisor-response density thresholds, factorization-fiber counts, exceptional-start incidence, scalar-carrier variation taxes, Chebyshev graph-closure or Euler-product criteria, transition parity phases, Wang phase-fiber cancellation, KMS factorization or Weil/gamma operator facts between lines. The common conclusion is methodological and evidence-disciplined: a resource counts after representation artifacts, source-occupation gaps and closure-induced costs have been removed, not before.
