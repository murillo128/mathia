# MI-021 — Representation enrichment only buys leverage where source-specific coupling survives the final projection, quotient and topology

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including AF-416--AF-435, FD-200--FD-217, MC-382--MC-396, NB-217--NB-230, PC-351--PC-360, PL-361--PL-376, RE-216--RE-228, VIS-302--VIS-318, WI-350--WI-357 and WP-366--WP-374. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, enrichment is useful only when a new relation has an exact source preimage, survives the destination operation and improves a load-bearing bottleneck without repaying the gain elsewhere. The relevant question is not how large the representation is, but **what source-specific relation survives acquisition, selection, transport, inversion, symmetry, positivity, normalization, projection and the topology/quantifier in which the final estimate is taken?**

The difference is not representation size or prime count but **whether one source must remain compatible as the horizon moves**.

The right topology removes a false singularity without pretending to supply the missing positivity.

These examples refine one reusable audit. Identify the minimal source carrier after exact algebraic generation. Establish parameter provenance and transport. Compute the physical inverse or nonautonomous transfer, including preserved fibres and destination quantifiers. Before scalar projection, check which local arithmetic exclusions and averaging resources survive. Solve covariance/similarity before crediting a metric. In positive constructions, compute both the completion freedom and the radical generated by zero-energy source relations. Audit normalization before crediting cancellation. If phase is used, couple it to the carrier weight, discard parameter ranges already suppressed absolutely, and express the residual range in the carrier's own capacity variable. If source exceptions are used, price relation codimension through the actual conductor/analytic tariff rather than an artifact of decomposition. At activation thresholds, work in the form topology that contains the physical states, then keep coercive reserve conceptually separate from regularity.
