# MI-021 — Representation enrichment buys leverage only when source-specific coupling survives identifiability, exact reduction, closure and the destination quantifier

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including AF-416--AF-444, FD-200--FD-225, MC-382--MC-406, NB-217--NB-237, PC-351--PC-363, PL-361--PL-383, RE-216--RE-237, VIS-302--VIS-327, WI-350--WI-362 and WP-366--WP-378. [WI-361](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-361-desogus-source-exact-cofinal-rooted-schur-induction.md) remains a candidate/prior-art redirect; WI-362 validates only a conditional internal subchain, not RH or global Weil positivity. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, enrichment is useful only when a new relation has an exact source preimage, is identifiable on the admissible source class, survives the destination operation and chosen closure, is recoverable at the needed precision/resolution, and improves a load-bearing bottleneck without repaying the gain elsewhere. The relevant question is not how large the representation is, but **what source-specific relation survives source restriction, acquisition, exact algebraic reduction, inversion, renormalization, completion/closure, globalization and the topology/quantifier of the final theorem?**

Arithmetic Fidelity supplies both sides of a sharp finite-moment boundary. AF-443 proves that `K` reciprocal-power moments can hide an arbitrarily remote mark when `K` earlier support coordinates remain independently movable. AF-444 shows that for positive **unit-weight** atoms the first `K+1` consecutive moments expose an exact Newton--Girard certificate separating `K` from `K+1` atoms. The same repair has class margin only `Theta(y)` as the added atom disappears (`Theta(m^-2)` in the shell model). Observation surplus therefore earns leverage only through a source law that turns it into a discriminator, and exact identifiability must still be priced separately from robustness.

Farey Discrepancy now separates source fidelity, response inversion and physical capacity. FD-224 proves that a fixed positive fractional exact-Euler cutoff at every dyadic horizon covers the original prefix after finitely many steps and that cofinally exhaustive exact Euler windows identify the source pointwise with `mu`. The long blind blocks instead keep one absolute cutoff `beta N<N`. Within that model, FD-225 shows that the full divisor-reservoir response is exactly invertible: all sampled prefixes can be flattened up to unit error, leaving `O(1)` switched observations and an `N^(1-o(1))` earlier defect through the certified Vinogradov--Korobov-scale range. The previous triangular conditioning loss was representational. What remains is the actual reservoir/source budget and physical Mertens baseline.

Möbius Cancellation now distinguishes three levels of variable survival. MC-404 proves that every finite divisor-incidence observable collapses to the lcm semilattice. MC-405 deliberately leaves that quotient: the prime-power label survives in `M(floor(x/b))`, but the full Mangoldt family is an exact self-coupling of `M` and absolute values destroy every fixed sublinear exponent. MC-406 then identifies the finest statistic seen by that aggregate: only the floor-quotient masses `W_k(x)` survive, with `O(sqrt x)` coordinates, and their PNT main kernel is exactly harmonic Möbius cancellation. A retained arithmetic variable is therefore only a candidate resource; its signed/phase family must remain independently informative **after the final quotient actually consumed downstream**.

Nyman--Beurling now separates source resolution, localization tariff and source/carrier resonance. NB-234 needs growing depth--phase resolution; NB-236 shows that imposing the Ford horizontal mark through Poincare--Lelong costs only bounded kernel mass. NB-237 then exposes the same coefficient through a first-order `R^(-1) zeta'/zeta` current whose kernel mass is `O(1/R)`. This smaller tariff is still not cancellation: matched critical packets place poles at spacing `asymp 1/R` and are exactly phase-locked to the carrier. Enrichment into a first-order current is useful because it isolates the natural normalization and the missing source theorem, but generic high-frequency decay cannot replace a result controlling joint pole geometry.

Prime Circle and Prime Lattice now show two complementary closure hazards. PC-361--PC-362 prove that bounded or subcritical relatively bounded strictly grade-raising source terms can leave a proper-grade eigenvalue spectrum unchanged. PC-363 resolves the canonical critical weighted shift: its forced closure has a whole half-plane spectrum with an RH-looking edge at `1/2`, but the edge is universal `ell^2`/Hardy domain geometry and selects no zeta zeros. PL-382 places the classical Nyman dilations outside the doubly commuting multishift normal form; PL-383 then shows that adjoining adjoints and taking ambient strong closure of only two incommensurate dilations already gives `B(L^2(0,1))`. Source distinctions can therefore be lost both by a universal critical completion and by a forgetful self-adjoint closure.

Robin Extremal shows that even changing architecture from one center to several centers can be representation-neutral. RE-236 closes the contracting super-broad single-center escape outside the genuinely coupled thin-arc limit. RE-237 proves that a serial product of individually successful centers on one common complex ray has exactly multiplicative Wiener norm and a normalized rate equal to the delay-weighted average of the constituent rates. Multi-center enrichment buys nothing when all coefficient paths remain locked to one phase character; any surviving architecture must create genuine phase disagreement before absolute values, use parallel/minimax interference, or be coupled only at the final product level.

Visual Exploration now turns faithful renormalization into an explicit finite-window arithmetic estimate. VIS-325 rewrites the maximally fragmented Wang branch as a lower-denominator mechanical path; VIS-326 computes the two first Christoffel-parent monodromies. VIS-327 proves that a parent of length `s<L` yields actual affine copy runs with repeat scale `L/s` and resultant `|Delta|<=4s`. Every polynomially short nondegenerate first parent is therefore already controlled by the ordinary two-form sieve with only `O(log log M)` excess at the natural scale. Renormalization earns leverage because it simultaneously reduces block length, preserves two-prime dimension and controls conductor; the surviving obstruction is near-full-scale nonrepetition.

Weil Positivity retains the selector-topology obstruction: WP-378 produces genuine cross-prime Hardy geometry, but the exact Mangoldt selector is only unbounded boundary evaluation. Weil Inertia provides the converse lesson that a carefully shared physical reserve can survive elimination if the lower form is established before the common infimum. WI-362 independently validates the Desogus source-capacity/common-short/inherited-pivot subchain **given the normalized one-cell form**. This is real progress inside the candidate architecture, but the upstream source-to-cell/fold identification and the finite-plus-analytic cofinal certificate chain remain unverified. One validated internal representation step must not be promoted into the global theorem it was designed to support.

These examples refine one reusable audit. Identify the admissible source class and retained provenance; compare observation rank with latent dimension and identify any source law that converts surplus rank into an invariant; establish carrier scale and price inverse stability and localization separately; perform exact Fourier/lcm/Gelfand/triangular/similarity and semilattice reductions; then identify the finest statistic distinguished by the downstream observable and test it for exact self-coupling. If the representation introduces a moving oscillatory carrier, test whether source singularities can follow and resonate at exactly that scale. Test multi-component constructions for phase-locked coefficient paths. Distinguish fixed, growing, base-anchored, fractional and cofinal quantifiers. Use faithful renormalization before declaring fragmentation final, then price repetition and conductor. For operator constructions, classify the forced closed extension and every later algebra/topology closure: a distinguished generator tuple can become universal after completion or strong self-adjoint closure. For countable representations, identify the theorem that globalizes local structure and inspect its load-bearing hypotheses. Finally price the physical destination margin, signed source fluctuation, aggregation length or certificate cost after all representation artifacts are removed.
