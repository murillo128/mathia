# MI-021 — Representation enrichment only buys leverage where source-specific coupling survives acquisition, closure, inverse geometry and matched controls

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including FD-200--FD-205, MC-382--MC-386, NB-217--NB-220, PC-351--PC-353, PL-361--PL-367, RE-216--RE-218, VIS-302--VIS-306, WI-351--WI-352 and WP-366--WP-368. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, enrichment can be useful when new degrees of freedom have exact source preimages and improve a destination bottleneck without repaying the gain elsewhere. The relevant question is not “how much representation is present?” but **what source-specific relation survives acquisition, closure/globalization, topology, normalization, inverse recovery, analytic identification and matched controls?**

Farey Discrepancy gives the cleanest constructive example. FD-202 takes signed source-slab differences before squaring, FD-204 replaces a high-resistance path by a lowbit tree while keeping source reuse logarithmic, and FD-205 shows that the resulting auxiliary geometry is globally coherent even though the physical prime and horizon may change at every scale. The invariant is the quotient coordinate: local lowbit edges become exact dyadic Mertens increments, and binary quotient ladders telescope them back to arbitrary `M(N)`. Representation enrichment buys leverage because its added relations have exact source preimages, near-critical random cost, controlled inverse geometry and an exact recovery map to the destination.

Prime Circle supplies the automorphism-quotient warning. Source-faithful noncommutative data can become blind under a spectral quotient; even making reciprocal parity outer can expose only generic contragredient duality shared by controls. Escaping one quotient obstruction is useful only if the survivor is source-selective rather than a universal orientation.

Prime Lattice strengthens the ambient-norm warning from one critical exponent to a continuum. Finite signs cross one convergence wall only through classical periodic continuation; unrestricted infinite signs can program arbitrary targets; `ell^1` restores rigidity only as limit-periodicity. PL-366 showed that pole-neutral `ell^2` filters automatically manufacture square-root summatory control by Hilbert duality. PL-367 generalizes this: for every `1<p<infinity`, pole-neutral `ell^p` filters automatically satisfy the sharp generic exponent `theta=1-1/p`, and explicit examples attain ordinary abscissa exactly `theta`. Every wall in `(0,1)` can therefore be manufactured by coefficient topology. The RH-shaped `1/2` is not merely generic inside Hilbert space; it is one point on a full Holder ladder.

Visual Exploration supplies a two-stage matched-control warning. VIS-305 reproduces the `x log log x` absolute spacing norm with parity/density Bernoulli support. VIS-306 then evaluates the signed endpoint channel on the same control and finds `O_p(x)`, hence `o_p(H)` at `H~x log log x`. The critical absolute norm and the actual signed destination effect can therefore separate. Source-specific credit belongs only to phase/coherence that survives the signed matched control.

Nyman--Beurling separates exact representation algebra from the proof interface. NB-218 shows that exact primitive/reconstruction cancellation contains the source relation before absolute-value decoupling. NB-219 removes the global vertical-domain cost by smooth localization. NB-220 goes further: Gaussian-type soft windows give a dual source profile whose Euler series converges absolutely and locally uniformly for every complex `s`, while approximating the original moving shell to arbitrary algebraic order. The source-defined localized pairing is entire. The live cost is therefore no longer vertical acquisition or source-tail continuation but the **analytic identification** that connects this entire source object back to the zeta primitive and zero-free-region data across `Re(s)=1` without recreating the absolute Mellin tariff.

Weil Positivity gives a two-sided scale warning. WP-366--WP-367 show that exact common homogeneous globalization destroys Whittaker `nY` incidence by factorization. WP-368 shows that refusing globalization and using a bounded positive form on fixed `Y>=Y_*>0` preserves incidence only while exponentially suppressing large arithmetic indices. A useful positive representation must therefore couple Fourier index and scale in a way that survives both scalarization and tail extinction.

Möbius Cancellation adds the theorem-interface analogue. Exponential family size, strong terminal saving and small ambient exceptional volume can all fail if conductor, admission or exceptional geometry is misaligned with the source-difference frame. MC-386 further shows that the natural generic benchmark is Welch energy: source-specific progress must beat or exclude sharp annihilator-like frame controls, not merely impose stronger pointwise correlation than the endpoint needs.

These examples refine the audit into a compact sequence. Identify the minimal source carrier after exact algebraic generation. Ask whether acquisition creates a new coordinate or transports old data. Apply the destination's closure, summation or globalization. State the topology and ambient norm and test whether they already explain the critical scale or even an entire ladder of scales. Price inverse geometry and source reuse. Pull theorem hypotheses and exceptional sets back to the source image. Check whether scale treatment erases incidence or suppresses the required tail. If a source-side continuation is created, verify the analytic identity that ties it back to the intended zeta/zero-free observable. Finally run the strongest matched control on the actual signed/destination-visible channel.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem transferring Farey graph design, character-family estimates, zeta-primitive convolution, reciprocal `SL_3` duality, lattice filtering, Wang mean values or Whittaker geometry between lines. Each quantitative statement remains line-specific. The cross-line content is the falsifiable audit: enrichment deserves credit only when newly acquired or retained structure survives exact destination operations, has controlled source/inverse cost, is not an ambient norm, automorphism or scale artifact, remains analytically identified with the intended destination observable, and defeats the relevant matched control.
