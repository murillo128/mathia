# MI-021 — Representation enrichment buys leverage only when the escape survives source geometry, closure and destination category

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact/supported results including AF-451--AF-460, FD-231--FD-239, MC-414--MC-422, NB-245--NB-254, PC-369--PC-376, PL-391--PL-399, VIS-335--VIS-343, WI-367--WI-374 and WP-381--WP-388. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific and no estimate is transferred between these categories.

Across Mathia, “the representation contains more information” is only the first gate. The retained datum must survive the actual source class and its quantifiers, exact quotient/topology and permitted operation order, support or coherence geometry, the closure used to obtain positivity or a norm, matched controls, and the destination margin. The newest results increasingly turn that audit into exact resource ceilings.

Arithmetic Fidelity now shows that the source-side paired-cube defect is not protected by finitely many bounded linear laws. AF-460 proves `D_k=0` for every fixed `k` on the normalized kernel carrier of any bounded finite-rank law over an infinite alphabet. Combined with AF-459, nontrivial-type target cancellation is unavoidable whenever the physical completed-secant carrier still contains that kernel. The source resource must therefore be an actual completion restriction, not merely finite codimension.

Farey Discrepancy makes the complementary observation budget quantitative. FD-239 extends the dyadic nullspace of FD-238 to arbitrary response sampling with `o(2^k)` points per octave: macroscopic signed capacity remains invisible. Sparse coordinate placement is not the missing resource; the first unresolved point-sampling regime is positive density, unless the physical positive/source-coherent class is separately transverse to the signed nullspace.

Möbius Cancellation distinguishes ambient dimension, support rank and physically usable bandwidth. MC-422 shows that a full positive coordinate box inside a thin product shell pays `sum B_j/U_j <= Delta/P_0`; factor axes can be algebraically independent yet have too little joint width to realize the multiaxis gain of MC-419. This sits on top of MC-421's closure-face tax. Tangent or tilted directions remain possible only after their source stationarity and closure geometry are proved.

Nyman--Beurling removes an apparent geometric loophole in the opposite direction. NB-254 upgrades the equal-comb test to every positive scalar carrier mixture of span `W`: positivity itself forces a `1/W` coherence window, so the effective Ford population is `R/W` and the matched obstruction moves rather than disappears. Wide support counts only if scalar-positive recombination is genuinely escaped.

Prime Circle and Prime Lattice now close natural finite arithmetic enrichments and expose the price of making one of them grow. PC-376 identifies every finite pairwise-coprime canonical Chebyshev transfer family with countably many standard polydisk shifts, matching prime and composite tuples. PL-398 shows that fixed-gap meet/join data reduce to finite `d`-periodic congruence information because `gcd(r,r+d)=gcd(r,d)`. PL-399 then shows that allowing `d` to grow in order to enlarge that gap-supported arithmetic alphabet pushes the larger continuation mode into a `1/d` tail at the nominal saddle. More arithmetic labels count only after the analytic price of exposing them has been paid.

Visual Exploration turns scalar multiscale freedom into a complexity lower bound. VIS-343 proves that `N` distinct pure Wang dilates can cancel at most `N-1` tail moments; combined with VIS-342, a fixed scalar power-class gain `delta` needs `N(u)>=u^(5delta/2+o(1))`. Subpolynomial scale banks therefore remain in the old asymptotic class unless a genuinely non-scalar source estimate enters.

Weil Positivity and Weil Inertia sharpen locality in complementary ways. WP-388 closes the regular strongly local Markov sector: admitting the discontinuous mixed shells forces effective intervals into unit cells, where all shell profiles are constant and diffusion energy vanishes. Together with WP-386--WP-387, the canonical continuum Markov decomposition is exhausted. WI-374, by contrast, shows that sufficiently late endpoint collars are already gamma-positive; the exact negative profile of WI-373 is inherited bulk debt, so a common-cut construction must explain old-core coupling rather than budget fresh gamma debt against every new collar.

The reusable audit is ordered: identify the completed physical source and coupled limits; compute effective secant/support/coherence geometry; identify exact kernels and quotients; account for domain admissibility and the closure used to create new variables or positivity; apply the strongest matched control; then compare the surviving source-specific quantity with the destination inverse modulus, sign condition or spectral profile. Similar vocabulary across lines remains analogy unless objects and hypotheses are explicitly matched.

**Boundary.** This is an interface principle, not a theorem transferring Banach type, divisor-response nullspaces, hyperbolic-shell bandwidth, Ford coherence, Chebyshev shift models, gap-transition tariffs, Wang tail cancellation or Weil/gamma operator facts between lines. The common conclusion is methodological and evidence-disciplined: a resource counts after representation artifacts and closure-induced costs have been removed, not before.
