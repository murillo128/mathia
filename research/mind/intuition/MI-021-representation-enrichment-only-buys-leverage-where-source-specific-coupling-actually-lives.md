# MI-021 — Representation enrichment only buys leverage where source-specific coupling survives acquisition, closure and matched controls

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including FD-201, MC-382, NB-217, PC-351, PL-361--PL-362, RE-216, VIS-301, WI-351 and WP-366. The quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific; the common content is the placement and survival of information through the destination interface.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when added degrees of freedom do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, compression can be legitimate when an exact theorem proves that discarded data are generated by retained data. The relevant question is not “how much representation is present?” but **what source-specific relation survives acquisition, canonical closure/globalization, topology, normalization and matched controls, and what theorem makes that survivor selective at the destination?**

Farey Discrepancy now shows that acquisition and compression must be audited separately. FD-200 proves that one matched horizon-mode enlargement is exact divisor transport and adds no information. FD-201 then finds a genuinely new fixed-mode cross-horizon source coordinate: prime-shell rows contain multiplicative Mertens increments. But immediately squaring those rows into the positive half-Sobolev norm again imposes shellwide subpower RMS, much stronger than the intended pointwise target. **New source information can be acquired and then destroyed or overcoerced by the destination compression.**

Möbius Cancellation supplies the analogous method-depth audit. MC-381 removes a fictitious square-root source-rank loss, but MC-382 shows that the same q-van der Corput architecture still has an intrinsic `log log` source horizon because source exponent forces linearly growing differencing depth while the terminal saving decays exponentially with that depth. Recovering combinatorial breadth therefore does not help if the analytic transport channel itself attenuates the survivor.

Nyman--Beurling makes support geometry part of the representation cost. NB-217 replaces shell-width heuristics by the exact reciprocal log-support mass `J_j(E)=int_E u^(-j)du`. Signs, disconnected support and downward tokens do not make higher-primitivization cheap unless enough actual source mass reaches small logarithmic scales. Representation enlargement counts only after acquisition, source normalization and inverse reconstruction are all priced.

Prime Circle supplies the quotient warning. PC-351 shows that the full noncommutative shell signature can reconstruct the centered source path, yet the canonical triangular decoder has trivial ordinary spectrum and a reciprocal sign-flip control is unitarily conjugate to the arithmetic decoder. A representation can therefore be faithful while its natural invariant quotient is blind. Source recovery and destination selectivity are different resources.

Prime Lattice sharpens the topology/continuation gate. PL-360 rules out diagonal Hilbert reweighting; PL-361 shows that even non-diagonal Hausdorff completions cannot cross `Re s=1` if ordinary zeta partial sums converge in the space and point evaluation remains continuous. PL-362 then shows that replacing sharp cutoffs by coefficientwise-positive approximate identities still cannot cross the real convergence wall: positive recovery preserves the divergent coefficient-one mass. Merely enriching the Gram geometry or smoothing the approximation does not help if the **summation protocol plus destination functional** reconstruct the original convergence wall. A viable completion must change that interface and identify where source-forced signed/complex cancellation or renormalization enters.

Robin Extremal provides the complementary positive example: representation choice can matter without adding arithmetic information. RE-216 changes only the analytic predictor, lowering the constructive coefficient-variation exponent from `12 log 2` to at most `6.08073274` while reusing the same ordinary-prime realization. This is genuine leverage, but it is leverage on a representation/Wiener-norm currency, not evidence of greater source capacity. The distinction matters because source-independent carrier bounds remain unchanged.

Visual Exploration shows how proof packaging can manufacture an apparent channel. Exact gamma recombination removes one lower-source boundary; VIS-301 then uses classical zero-free information to suppress the isolated localization coherence and moves the first unresolved pointwise term to the interior `x log^2 x` mean-value remainder. A claimed boundary is not a surviving representation feature until all earlier deterministic and proof-localization artifacts have been removed.

Weil Inertia prices formal infinite-dimensionality. WI-350 shows that bounded phase-orbit dimension restores linear resolution cost. WI-351 extends this to infinitely many tiny charge sectors: a controlled charge-Sobolev moment makes the tail effectively finite at destination resolution, and generic square-root-rank behavior requires the weighted high-charge mass itself to grow. Infinite support is not usable information when closure below the resolution threshold compresses it away.

Weil Positivity adds the globalization warning. WP-365 already shows that an independent positive finite--archimedean tensor completion can fail at the critical divisor. WP-366 starts from a stronger fixed-height feature where finite and archimedean variables genuinely interact through `nY`; nevertheless positive scalar dilation-covariant globalization forces power-Haar averaging and Mellin-separates the coupling back into the failed Rankin--Selberg product. **Local non-product structure is not enough if the canonical operation that removes its auxiliary scale factorizes it again.**

These examples refine the audit into six questions. First identify the minimal source carrier after exact algebraic generation. Second ask whether acquisition actually creates a new coordinate or merely transports old data. Third apply the destination's canonical closure, summation, reconstruction or scale-globalization and identify what it regenerates, annihilates or factorizes. Fourth state the topology and resolution seen by the destination. Fifth run the strongest matched control preserving those generic operations. Sixth state the source-specific theorem that leaves a nontrivial selective survivor after all five gates.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem transferring Farey differencing, q-vdC depth, Mellin support duality, reciprocal signatures, Dirichlet completion/summation, Euler predictors, Wang localization, charge decompositions or Whittaker averaging between lines. Each quantitative statement remains line-specific. The cross-line content is the falsifiable audit: enrichment deserves credit only when the newly acquired structure survives the exact operations the destination is required to perform.
