# MI-021 — Representation enrichment buys leverage only when source-specific coupling survives exact reduction, detector resolution, symmetry, closure and destination scale

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current exact results including AF-416--AF-445, FD-200--FD-227, MC-382--MC-408, NB-217--NB-238, PC-351--PC-364, PL-361--PL-385, RE-216--RE-237, VIS-302--VIS-329, WI-350--WI-364 and WP-366--WP-379. WI-362 remains conditional on the normalized one-cell form; WI-363--WI-364 identify concrete proof gaps in the current derivation of that form rather than proving the final form false. Quantitative mechanisms remain line-specific.

Across Mathia, nominal enrichment keeps failing when added coordinates do not alter the smallest source-sensitive object actually consumed downstream. Conversely, enrichment is useful only when a new relation has an exact source preimage, is identifiable on the admissible source class, is resolved at the scale the destination consumes, survives the final quotient, symmetry reduction and closure, cannot be cloned by a matched control, and remains quantitatively visible at the destination. The relevant question is not how large the representation is, but **what source-specific relation survives source restriction, acquisition, detector coarse-graining, exact algebraic reduction, parity/normalization, completion/globalization and the final topology/quantifier?**

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the source-class dependence of rank explicit. Positive unit-weight `K`-atomic sources and variable-weight `K`-atomic sources have different exact moment thresholds because latent amplitudes double the continuous nuisance dimension. The same remote-atom limit leaves a shrinking class margin. Observation surplus earns leverage only through a source law, and exact identifiability is not robustness.

Farey Discrepancy now separates algebraic repair, analytic resolution and physical **shape-compatible** capacity. FD-225 removes switched-response conditioning by exact full-divisor inversion and FD-226 removes one power of `D` from prime-resolution error by scale-adapted exact floor cells. FD-227 then shows that smooth positive use of the extra capacity is not neutral: a power-law occupancy is mapped to a Jordan-totient main mode seen at full leading order by every fixed dyadic root difference. Representation cleanup exposed a stricter physical question rather than solving it: can an oscillatory switched-null profile remain positive and leave enough reservoir slack for the Mertens baseline?

Möbius Cancellation shows why survival, invertibility and analytic leverage are distinct gates. Finite divisor incidence collapses to lcm; the Mangoldt/floor-quotient family self-couples to Mertens; MC-407 Abel-collapses the residual prime-error increments to one scalar Möbius--PNT-error correlation. MC-408 then shows that this scalar coordinate is finite-prefix invertible **and** that its square-root bound is RH-equivalent. An observable can preserve essentially all finite source information and still be no easier to control at the destination. New leverage must enter through independently controlled structure before the scalar endpoint is formed.

Nyman--Beurling adds detector resolution as a separate gate. NB-237 identifies the unresolved first-order source current as a microlocal pairing with `R^(-1) zeta'/zeta`, while matched critical packets can place `Theta(J)` poles at spacing `Theta(1/R)` with locked carrier phase. NB-238 tests Maynard--Pratt half-isolated-zero theory, which is genuinely sensitive to vertical zero structure, but its published `(log T)^3` clustering scale places the entire resonant Ford packet inside one clump. A detector can therefore be source-specific and mathematically strong while still discarding exactly the intra-cell occupancy/depth/phase data consumed by the destination.

Prime Circle and Prime Lattice sharpen the closure audit. The canonical scalar one-sided grade-raising family has a universal critical half-plane and whole-plane residual spectrum above threshold. Ambient adjoint/SOT closure of incommensurate Nyman dilations becomes universal, while even the norm-closed positive algebra retains only the abstract free positive monoid/polydisk structure and forgets the numerical `log p` values. Source information can be lost both by overly strong closure and by a seemingly faithful algebra that preserves coordinates while discarding their physical weights.

Robin Extremal shows the analogous phase failure in coefficient space: adding centers on one common complex ray leaves Wiener cost multiplicative and the normalized rate a delay-weighted average. Several components buy leverage only if they create genuine phase disagreement or another coupling before absolute aggregation.

Visual Exploration gives the positive counterpart and a warning about intermediate coordinates. VIS-329 applies one Minkowski short vector directly to the original Wang period and simultaneously controls packet count, exceptional edges, two-prime step and resultant. That closes the entire natural subperiod branchwise occupancy channel without the center-gap case split left by earlier representations. A faithful simplification can therefore create real leverage by exposing the cost in source-native coordinates; more elaborate intermediate structure is not automatically closer to the load-bearing estimate.

Weil Inertia adds symmetry and form transport to the audit. WI-364 shows that odd restriction of an even difference kernel generates an image channel as well as the difference channel; the regular gamma image must be tracked together with the singular Carleman image. WI-363 then shows that the subsequent half-density transform of a quadratic difference form creates a nonconstant diagonal Jacobian term even when the off-diagonal kernel conjugation is correct. Representation equivalence must therefore be checked at the level of the complete closed form, not termwise only on the visible kernel.

Weil Positivity adds a destination-scale version of the same principle. WP-378 shows that a positive Hardy geometry can contain real cross-prime incidence while putting the exact Mangoldt selector on a singular boundary trace. WP-379 shows that canonical pointing can regularize the geometry and produce a nondegenerate positive mixed Gram, yet the critical normalized Gram is universal across odd prime and composite degrees while the arithmetic boundary contrast decays to zero. Selector regularity and positive incidence are not enough unless their coupling survives the destination normalization with nonvanishing strength.

These examples refine one reusable audit. Identify the admissible source class and effective latent dimension; determine what provenance and numerical weights survive; verify that the detector resolves the scale the destination actually consumes; perform exact Fourier/lcm/Gelfand/triangular/summation-by-parts reductions; identify the finest statistic actually consumed downstream and test it for self-coupling; distinguish invertibility from a genuinely cheaper norm estimate; track symmetry-generated image terms and diagonal corrections under coordinate changes; price localization and inverse stability separately; test moving carriers for source resonance; classify every completion/closure before using its invariants; and compare the normalized surviving signal with the strongest matched control.

**Boundary.** This synthesis transfers no quantitative threshold between lines. Moment/Hankel certificates, Farey reservoirs, Möbius Mellin/Abel identities, Ford zero detectors/currents, weighted-shift spectra, Nyman closures, Robin phase locking, Wang Minkowski packets, Weil parity/half-density transport and pointed selector-topology obstructions are different theorems. The cross-line claim is only the audit: enrichment is useful when it contributes source-selected information that is identifiable, resolved at the required scale, survives exact reduction and the actual symmetry/closure/topology consumed, retains any physical weights that matter, cannot be reproduced by the strongest matched control, and remains quantitatively coupled to the destination after normalization.
