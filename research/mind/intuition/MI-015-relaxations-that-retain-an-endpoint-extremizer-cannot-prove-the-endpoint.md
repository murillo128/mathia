# MI-015 — A relaxation is exhausted only at the destination scale; stronger nonredundant constraints can still change the game

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from MC-276--MC-277, PF-333--PF-334, WI-282--WI-283, Farey Discrepancy through [FD-123](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-123-polynomial-growth-forces-a-cesaro-fourier-skeleton-gap-on-bounded-ratio-chains.md), Nyman--Beurling through [NB-122](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-122-full-prefix-nested-coherence-still-admits-one-perfect-target-inverse-section-control.md), and Visual Exploration through [VIS-221](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-221-sdp-stress-certificate-retains-torus-interactions.md). This is a diagnostic about audited relaxed classes, not a universal impossibility theorem.

A proposed simplification is exhausted when its admissible class still contains an exact or asymptotic extremizer at the forbidden budget **in the norm or loss actually consumed by the endpoint**. No sharper estimate using only the retained hypotheses can then create the missing gap. The number of constraints is not the relevant measure: what matters is whether they are independent near the extremizer and what imposing them costs in the destination currency.

Möbius Cancellation makes the destination-functional test explicit. Imposing the correct blind-point value and minimizing `L^2` classification error gives exactly the Christoffel kernel, with a loss that subtracts the blind mass itself. MC-277 shows that the original scalar representation still has exact blind/nonblind margin; the relaxation is exhausted because its aggregation discards the endpoint atom, not because the representation lacks information.

Weil Inertia supplies a support-topology extremizer test. Complete prime-power screening plus nonnegativity allows the length-`log 2` packing optimizer; qualitative full span and source-specific support domination can be added with vanishing-mass repairs. A strict decrement must therefore force quantitative amplitude or operator balance that excludes those near-extremizers rather than adding more qualitative support facts.

Farey Discrepancy shows how a marginally exhausted class can acquire a real but still insufficient multiscale cost. FD-119--FD-120 preserve critical near-saturation under strong single-horizon constraints. FD-121--FD-122 make one common source incompatible with simultaneous equality-ray tracking along fixed squarefree dilation edges. FD-123 then adds a weak global source envelope and proves a positive **Cesàro deficit on every bounded integer-ratio chain**: squarefree steps pay pair gaps, while too many highly saturated nonsquarefree steps would force endpoint growth faster than any polynomial envelope. The relaxation is no longer average-saturable on bounded scales, yet the pointwise endpoint remains open because sparse exceptional horizons and unbounded-ratio escape can still avoid the average cost.

Nyman--Beurling gives the complementary warning: wording a condition as multiscale does not make it nonredundant. NB-118--NB-120 show that growing exact Nyman orthogonality still leaves perfect target capture at `Theta(1/R)`. NB-121 proves finite nested ladders collapse to the maximal section, and NB-122 extends this to the **entire prefix `2<=r<=R`**. One maximal repair simultaneously hits every smaller natural-cell target and every nested generator section in the audited mixed-tail regime. More nested scales cannot help because they do not shrink the feasible set; the next relation must be genuinely non-nested/tail-sensitive, use actual zero-power structure, or enter a faster source regime.

Prime Flute adds the necessary norm check. In operator norm, PF-333 shows the generic positive relative-square-root class already has logarithmic sensitivity, so positivity alone cannot produce a dimension-free theorem. PF-334 proves the same map is dimension-free in Hilbert--Schmidt norm, which is the currency the downstream theorem consumes. A bad extremizer in the wrong norm is irrelevant; exhaustion must be demonstrated in the destination norm.

Visual Exploration shows that a **certificate family** can be exhausted while a strictly stronger relaxation remains live. VIS-217--VIS-218 close the normalized magnetic certificate, and VIS-220 proves a universal ceiling for the simple-cycle packing LP. VIS-221 embeds the magnetic bound as one restricted dual ansatz inside the full correlation SDP, which retains heterogeneous vertex stresses and all pair correlations. The SDP therefore survives the old certificate no-go. It is still destination-priced: to prove Haar-RMS control its signed deficit must be `B-O(sqrt(R))`. If the SDP is large and high rank, only that relaxation has failed; if a stationary rank-one solution has PSD stress, the exact torus optimum is certified.

The reusable workflow is therefore sharper than “add more structure.” Identify the relaxed class and its endpoint extremizers; evaluate them in the exact destination functional; test whether a proposed source relation actually shrinks the feasible set; and distinguish failure of one certificate from a lower bound on the underlying object. A new relation is meaningful only when it is nonredundant **and** its induced cost survives the final normalization. An average/network cost is not automatically pointwise, and a stronger relaxation must be calibrated anew rather than condemned by a ceiling proved only for a weaker one.

**Boundary.** The mechanisms differ: target subtraction, support-topology saturation, horizon-dependent repair, bounded-scale Cesàro coercivity, full-prefix projection redundancy, inverse-section tail repair, norm dependence and certificate hierarchy. FD-123 does not give pointwise Farey coercivity; NB-122 does not model actual zero packets; VIS-221 does not prove SDP tightness. The diagnostic applies only to the specific relaxed class, source relation and destination currency that have been audited.
