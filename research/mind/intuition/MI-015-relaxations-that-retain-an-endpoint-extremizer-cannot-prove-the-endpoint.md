# MI-015 — A relaxation is exhausted only at the destination scale; stronger nonredundant constraints can still change the game

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from MC-276--MC-277, PF-333--PF-334, WI-282--WI-283, Farey Discrepancy through [FD-124](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-124-cesaro-fourier-skeleton-saturation-forces-superexponential-horizon-growth.md), Nyman--Beurling through [NB-123](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-123-perfect-target-capture-by-a-finite-zero-packet-requires-logarithmic-confluent-rank.md), and Visual Exploration through [VIS-222](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-222-symmetric-grothendieck-constant-factor-sdp-torus-equivalence.md). This is a diagnostic about audited relaxed classes, not a universal impossibility theorem.

A proposed simplification is exhausted when its admissible class still contains an exact or asymptotic extremizer at the forbidden budget **in the norm or loss actually consumed by the endpoint**. No sharper estimate using only the retained hypotheses can then create the missing gap. The number of constraints is not the relevant measure: what matters is whether they are independent near the extremizer and what imposing them costs in the destination currency.

Möbius Cancellation makes the destination-functional test explicit. Imposing the correct blind-point value and minimizing `L^2` classification error gives exactly the Christoffel kernel, with a loss that subtracts the blind mass itself. MC-277 shows that the original scalar representation still has exact blind/nonblind margin; the relaxation is exhausted because its aggregation discards the endpoint atom, not because the representation lacks information.

Weil Inertia supplies a support-topology extremizer test. Complete prime-power screening plus nonnegativity allows the length-`log 2` packing optimizer; qualitative full span and source-specific support domination can be added with vanishing-mass repairs. A strict decrement must therefore force quantitative amplitude or operator balance that excludes those near-extremizers rather than adding more qualitative support facts.

Farey Discrepancy shows how a marginally exhausted class can acquire a real but still insufficient multiscale cost. FD-119--FD-120 preserve critical near-saturation under strong single-horizon constraints. FD-121--FD-122 make one common source incompatible with simultaneous equality-ray tracking along fixed squarefree dilation edges. FD-123 adds a polynomial source envelope and proves a positive Cesàro deficit on every bounded integer-ratio chain. FD-124 removes the fixed ratio ceiling: if the average logarithmic horizon growth `Lambda_n=(1/n)log(H_n/H_0)` is bounded along a subsequence, a fixed positive average deficit survives there. Cesàro saturation can therefore occur only on a superexponential horizon schedule. The relaxation is no longer average-saturable at any finite logarithmic horizon entropy, yet the pointwise endpoint remains open because sparse superexponential horizons can still avoid assignment of the average cost.

Nyman--Beurling gives both sides of the nonredundancy test. NB-118--NB-122 show that growing exact Nyman orthogonality and even the full nested cutoff prefix still leave a perfect inverse-section target extremizer: all smaller constraints are projections of one maximal repair, so adding them does not shrink the relaxed class. NB-123 then imposes information not encoded by those nested equations—the finite annihilating recurrence forced by an actual zero-power packet. Perfect target capture makes the geometric block increments equal the missing harmonic root `q^-1`, while the packet recurrence has only roots `q^(-conj(rho))`; hence exact capture requires total confluent rank at least `floor(log_2 R)`. The broader matched-control class is therefore genuinely larger than the actual low-rank source class. What remains is to make that exact recurrence mismatch **stable** for near-perfect capture or high growing rank.

Prime Flute adds the necessary norm check. In operator norm, PF-333 shows the generic positive relative-square-root class already has logarithmic sensitivity, so positivity alone cannot produce a dimension-free theorem. PF-334 proves the same map is dimension-free in Hilbert--Schmidt norm, which is the currency the downstream theorem consumes. A bad extremizer in the wrong norm is irrelevant; exhaustion must be demonstrated in the destination norm.

Visual Exploration shows that a **certificate family** can be exhausted while a stronger relaxation remains live, and also that the stronger relaxation may later become a faithful surrogate rather than another unknown gap. VIS-217--VIS-218 close the normalized magnetic certificate, and VIS-220 proves a universal ceiling for the simple-cycle packing LP. VIS-221 embeds the magnetic bound as one restricted dual ansatz inside the full correlation SDP, which retains heterogeneous vertex stresses and all pair correlations. VIS-222 then proves, by the complex symmetric Grothendieck inequality,

`M_abs(A) <= U_abs(A) <= K_gamma^C M_abs(A)`.

Hence the SDP is constant-factor equivalent to the exact unit-modulus quadratic objective for every finite Hermitian matrix. High rank can prevent exact optimizer recovery, but it cannot produce an unbounded multiplicative objective gap. The destination question is no longer whether the SDP is asymptotically tight enough to transfer scale; it is the actual prime-specific value of `U_abs/sqrt(R_v)`. A bounded SDP ratio gives a bounded exact torus ratio, and a divergent SDP ratio forces the exact ratio to diverge as well.

The reusable workflow is therefore sharper than “add more structure.” Identify the relaxed class and its endpoint extremizers; evaluate them in the exact destination functional; test whether a proposed source relation actually shrinks the feasible set; and distinguish failure of one certificate from a lower bound on the underlying object. A new relation is meaningful only when it is nonredundant **and** its induced cost survives the final normalization. An average/network cost is not automatically pointwise; an exact source representation can exclude extremizers left alive by all nested source equations; and a stronger relaxation can sometimes be proved universally equivalent to the exact objective even when its optimizer geometry is different.

**Boundary.** The mechanisms differ: target subtraction, support-topology saturation, horizon-dependent repair, finite-entropy Cesàro coercivity, full-prefix projection redundancy, zero-power recurrence rank, inverse-section tail repair, norm dependence and certificate hierarchy. FD-124 does not give pointwise Farey coercivity or control superexponential schedules; NB-123 does not give a stable near-perfect capture bound or control high-rank packet conditioning; VIS-222 does not determine the prime-specific SDP scale, rank or access time. The diagnostic applies only to the specific relaxed class, source relation and destination currency that have been audited.
