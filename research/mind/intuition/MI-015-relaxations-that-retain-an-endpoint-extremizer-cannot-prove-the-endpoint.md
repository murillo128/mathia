# MI-015 — A relaxation is exhausted only at the destination scale; stronger nonredundant constraints can still change the game

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current canonical findings, including Arithmetic Fidelity through [AF-344](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-344-power-supported-backgrounds-trade-sign-density-for-amplitude.md), Möbius Cancellation through [MC-317](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-317-random-linear-erasure-augmentation-closes-generic-endpoint.md), Farey Discrepancy through [FD-125](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-125-square-root-common-sources-saturate-the-fourier-skeleton-on-repeated-square-horizons.md), Nyman--Beurling through [NB-124](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-124-weighted-annihilator-duality-gives-a-stable-target-angle-gap-for-finite-zero-packets.md), Prime Flute through PF-333--PF-334, Weil Inertia through WI-282--WI-283, and Visual Exploration through VIS-222. This is a diagnostic about audited relaxed classes, not a universal impossibility theorem.

A proposed simplification is exhausted when its admissible class still contains an exact or asymptotic extremizer at the forbidden budget **in the norm or loss actually consumed by the endpoint**. No sharper estimate using only the retained hypotheses can then create the missing gap. The number of constraints is not the relevant measure: what matters is whether they are independent near the extremizer and what imposing them costs in the destination currency.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives a clean resource-transfer countermodel. AF-342--AF-343 show that low ordered sign variation is expensive against broad low-exception and syndetic baseline classes, but AF-344 constructs a fixed prime-independent perfect-power background that preserves the zero-pole discriminator while reducing residual sign variation to `O(P^(1/k))`. The lost sign bill reappears as sparse support and amplitudes `asymp P^(1-1/k)`. Therefore the relaxation “zero-pole fidelity + small sign variation” is not coercive unless the destination also controls the amplitude/concentration coordinate into which the source can escape.

Möbius Cancellation now supplies a destination-scale matched control rather than only a qualitative generic escape. MC-314 turns the natural equal-share source scale into the constant-defect demand `d_(t_R)>=R-2+o(1)` at `t_R=ceil(2 log_2(R+1))`; MC-315--MC-316 show that projective occupancy, systematic ranks, erasure ambiguity and primal/dual generalized weights are the same generic frontier. MC-317 then proves that full binary `[2R,R]` codes satisfying `d_(t_R)>=R-2` actually exist for all sufficiently large `R`. The entire generic coding/projective relaxation therefore contains an endpoint-scale matched control. Any strict improvement must constrain arithmetic realizability of the reciprocal-prime/source-incidence matrices rather than recombine the same generic invariants.

Farey Discrepancy shows both a real multiscale cost and its exact audited boundary. FD-119--FD-120 preserve critical near-saturation under strong single-horizon constraints. FD-121--FD-124 show that one polynomially bounded common source cannot Cesàro-saturate the skeleton on schedules with finite average logarithmic horizon growth. FD-125 then constructs one square-root-bounded common source that does saturate along the repeated-square superexponential schedule. A pointwise theorem must add a physical Mertens/Farey law that excludes this source; more generic common-source coherence cannot do so.

Nyman--Beurling gives the complementary case where a stronger constraint really changes the destination. Growing exact Nyman equations and the full nested prefix can still leave a perfect inverse-section target extremizer. The finite annihilating recurrence forced by an actual zero-power packet adds information not encoded by those projections, and NB-124 bounds that annihilator directly in the reciprocal block weights of the Nyman target energy. The new relation is effective because its induced cost survives the final normalization.

Prime Flute adds the necessary norm check. In operator norm the generic positive relative-square-root class already has logarithmic sensitivity, while the same map is dimension-free in Hilbert--Schmidt norm, the currency consumed downstream. A bad extremizer in the wrong norm is irrelevant; exhaustion must be demonstrated in the destination norm.

Weil Inertia supplies a support-topology extremizer test. Complete prime-power screening plus nonnegativity allows the length-`log 2` packing optimizer; qualitative full span and source-specific support domination can be added with vanishing-mass repairs. A strict decrement must therefore force quantitative amplitude or operator balance that excludes those near-extremizers rather than adding more qualitative support facts.

Visual Exploration shows that a certificate family can be exhausted while a stronger relaxation remains live, and that the stronger relaxation may later become a faithful surrogate rather than another unknown gap. The full correlation SDP is constant-factor equivalent to the exact static torus objective by the complex symmetric Grothendieck inequality. High rank may obstruct optimizer recovery but cannot create a parametrically different objective scale.

The reusable workflow is concrete. Identify the relaxed class and its endpoint extremizers; evaluate them in the exact destination functional; test where a candidate can move cost without violating retained hypotheses; and add source structure only if it blocks that escape in the same normalization. MC-317 is the sharpest new instance: once a matched control reaches the **exact live endpoint**, further generic estimates in that same invariant family are not merely weak—they are the wrong place to search for arithmetic coercivity.

**Boundary.** The mechanisms differ and none transfers automatically between lines. AF-344 does not identify the final joint source norm; MC-317 is not an arithmetic Legendre/source-incidence realization; FD-125 is not the Mertens function; NB-124 does not control arbitrary growing-rank packets approaching `Re s=1`; VIS-222 does not determine the prime-specific SDP scale. The diagnostic applies only to the specific relaxed class, escape coordinate and destination currency that have been audited.
