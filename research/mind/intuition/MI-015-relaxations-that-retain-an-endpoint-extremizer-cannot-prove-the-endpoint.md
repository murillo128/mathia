# MI-015 — A relaxation is exhausted only at the destination scale; stronger nonredundant constraints can still change the game

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current canonical findings, including Arithmetic Fidelity through [AF-344](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-344-power-supported-backgrounds-trade-sign-density-for-amplitude.md), Möbius Cancellation through [MC-295](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-295-fixed-weight-defect-shell-saturates-quotient-escape.md), Farey Discrepancy through [FD-125](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-125-square-root-common-sources-saturate-the-fourier-skeleton-on-repeated-square-horizons.md), Nyman--Beurling through [NB-124](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-124-weighted-annihilator-duality-gives-a-stable-target-angle-gap-for-finite-zero-packets.md), Prime Flute through PF-333--PF-334, Weil Inertia through WI-282--WI-283, and Visual Exploration through VIS-222. This is a diagnostic about audited relaxed classes, not a universal impossibility theorem.

A proposed simplification is exhausted when its admissible class still contains an exact or asymptotic extremizer at the forbidden budget **in the norm or loss actually consumed by the endpoint**. No sharper estimate using only the retained hypotheses can then create the missing gap. The number of constraints is not the relevant measure: what matters is whether they are independent near the extremizer and what imposing them costs in the destination currency.

Arithmetic Fidelity now gives a particularly clean resource-transfer countermodel. AF-342--AF-343 show that low ordered sign variation is expensive against broad low-exception and syndetic baseline classes, but AF-344 constructs a fixed prime-independent perfect-power background that preserves the zero-pole discriminator while reducing residual sign variation to `O(P^(1/k))`. The lost sign bill reappears as sparse support and amplitudes `asymp P^(1-1/k)`. Therefore the relaxation “zero-pole fidelity + small sign variation” is not coercive unless the destination also controls the amplitude/concentration coordinate into which the source can escape.

Möbius Cancellation now has an explicit extremizer for the generic quotient escape. MC-293--MC-294 force low-cost near-negative modes to collapse to at most one exceptional character and charge many remaining independent directions to a high-cost quotient with large girth. MC-295 constructs a fixed-weight finite model that simultaneously pays the live defect, participation, cheap-sector balance, entropy and quotient-girth bills while keeping full expensive quotient dimension and no common preferred-sign cell. Hence the generic Fourier/coding relaxation still contains an endpoint-scale extremizer. Any strict improvement must constrain arithmetic realizability of that expensive quotient rather than recombine the same generic inequalities.

Farey Discrepancy shows both a real multiscale cost and its exact audited boundary. FD-119--FD-120 preserve critical near-saturation under strong single-horizon constraints. FD-121--FD-124 show that one polynomially bounded common source cannot Cesàro-saturate the skeleton on any schedule with finite average logarithmic horizon growth. FD-125 then constructs one square-root-bounded common source that does saturate along the repeated-square superexponential schedule. The former “superexponential loophole” is therefore an actual extremizer in the relaxed class. A pointwise theorem must add a physical Mertens/Farey law that excludes this source; more generic common-source coherence cannot do so.

Nyman--Beurling gives the complementary case where a stronger constraint really changes the destination. NB-118--NB-122 show that growing exact Nyman equations and the full nested prefix still leave a perfect inverse-section target extremizer. NB-123 adds information not encoded by those projections—the finite annihilating recurrence forced by an actual zero-power packet—and excludes exact perfect capture below logarithmic rank. NB-124 then tests the annihilator directly in the reciprocal block weights of the Nyman target energy. This yields a cutoff-uniform positive gap for every fixed finite packet and an `Omega(log R)` rank requirement for polynomially near-perfect capture in a fixed horizontal strip. The new relation is effective because its induced cost survives the final normalization.

Prime Flute adds the necessary norm check. In operator norm, PF-333 shows the generic positive relative-square-root class already has logarithmic sensitivity, so positivity alone cannot produce a dimension-free theorem. PF-334 proves the same map is dimension-free in Hilbert--Schmidt norm, which is the currency the downstream theorem consumes. A bad extremizer in the wrong norm is irrelevant; exhaustion must be demonstrated in the destination norm.

Weil Inertia supplies a support-topology extremizer test. Complete prime-power screening plus nonnegativity allows the length-`log 2` packing optimizer; qualitative full span and source-specific support domination can be added with vanishing-mass repairs. A strict decrement must therefore force quantitative amplitude or operator balance that excludes those near-extremizers rather than adding more qualitative support facts.

Visual Exploration shows that a certificate family can be exhausted while a stronger relaxation remains live, and that the stronger relaxation may later become a faithful surrogate rather than another unknown gap. VIS-217--VIS-218 close the normalized magnetic certificate and VIS-220 proves a universal ceiling for simple-cycle packing. VIS-221 passes to the full correlation SDP; VIS-222 then proves by the complex symmetric Grothendieck inequality that

`M_abs(A) <= U_abs(A) <= K_gamma^C M_abs(A)`.

The stronger relaxation is therefore constant-factor equivalent to the exact static torus objective. High rank may obstruct optimizer recovery but cannot create a parametrically different objective scale.

The reusable workflow is now concrete. Identify the relaxed class and its endpoint extremizers; evaluate them in the exact destination functional; test where a candidate can move cost without violating the retained hypotheses; and add source structure only if it blocks that escape in the same normalization. AF-344 moves cost from sign density to amplitude/support geometry. MC-295 moves it into arithmetic realizability of an expensive quotient. FD-125 moves it into superexponential scheduling plus omitted physical source laws. NB-124 succeeds because the annihilator is bounded directly in the destination energy. A new relation is useful only when it is nonredundant **and** the endpoint cannot route around its cost.

**Boundary.** The mechanisms differ and none transfers automatically between lines. AF-344 does not identify the final joint source norm; MC-295 is not an arithmetic Legendre-shell realization; FD-125 is not the Mertens function; NB-124 does not control arbitrary growing-rank packets approaching `Re s=1`; VIS-222 does not determine the prime-specific SDP scale. The diagnostic applies only to the specific relaxed class, escape coordinate and destination currency that have been audited.
