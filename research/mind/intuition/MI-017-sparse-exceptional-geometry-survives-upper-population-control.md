# MI-017 — Sparse exceptional geometry survives population control; the useful source theorem must change the local rate, orbit or gate

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis from [NB-141](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-141-selberg-density-makes-polynomial-confluence-cells-rare-but-not-forbidden.md), [NB-144](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-144-euler-product-poisson-mass-forces-logarithmic-confluence-away-from-the-one-line.md), the reflection-closure classification [NB-147](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-147-reflection-closure-exactly-interpolates-the-positive-poisson-occupancy-coefficient.md), [RE-145](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-145-reciprocal-box-visibility-has-an-exponential-scaled-radius-floor.md)--[RE-149](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-149-zero-horizontal-spread-product-packets-isolate-vertical-escape.md), and [PL-320](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-320-source-trace-full-tail-volterra-matching.md)--[PL-321](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-321-conditional-moment-green-transfer.md). The mathematical objects remain line-specific.

Nyman--Beurling shows why broad zero-density rarity is not a local exclusion theorem. The actual Euler product does charge an exceptional cell through a positive Poisson budget, but NB-147 identifies the exact limit of that resource for every tight interior packet. The charged object is the weighted **reflection closure**. Near the critical line, if `d_G` counts the packet and `b_G` counts occurrences whose reflected partner is absent, then `d_G+b_G <= (1/4+o(1))log G`. The old `1/8` one-sided and `1/4` self-dual coefficients are the endpoints of one orbit-exposure law. Positive exterior resampling cannot improve this closure budget within the same proof cone.

Robin Extremal supplies a different saturation mechanism. RE-145 gives the cardinality-free local floor `exp(-O(R))`; RE-148 compares the local witness and actual-zeta right-shell estimate at the same point but with incompatible proved exponents. RE-149 then shows that exponential deterioration is already sharp for pure vertical phase escape with zero horizontal spread. Better global counts or horizontal localization leave a matched exceptional family unless an actual-zeta theorem restricts its vertical occupancy/phase geometry.

Prime Lattice shows a third route: change the logical gate rather than the population estimate. PL-320 turns convergence of the source trace and one singular translation remainder into the boundary coefficient, `Qm(Q)->0` and a conditionally convergent residual moment. PL-321 proves that those weaker data already pass through the moving Green kernel. Thus the old absolute-`L^1` alternative disappears; the singular remainder trace is the single remaining analytic endpoint-transfer gate in that chain.

The reusable diagnostic is more specific than “global density is insufficient.” Identify the exceptional local configuration; express it in the destination's scale; determine whether the available source argument is already optimal in its proof class; track the symmetry/orbit closure actually charged by the estimate; and compare its rate with destination visibility loss. If those steps fail, progress needs a genuinely new source resource, a sharper destination theorem, or an identity that changes the gate itself.

For Nyman the missing resource lies outside positive Poisson mass plus the absolute Euler-product majorant. For Robin it must constrain vertical/near-left geometry or reverse the local-vs-shell exponent ordering. For Prime Lattice it is now the convergence of one source-coupled singular remainder, not stronger generic tail integrability.

**Boundary.** NB-147 is method-optimality for a positive proof architecture, RE-149 is a matched-control spectrum rather than an actual-zeta construction, and PL-320--PL-321 are one-dimensional endpoint identities. No coefficient or mechanism transfers automatically between lines.
