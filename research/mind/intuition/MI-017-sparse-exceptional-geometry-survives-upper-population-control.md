# MI-017 — Sparse exceptional geometry survives population control; useful source laws must change the local rate, not just the global count

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis from [NB-141](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-141-selberg-density-makes-polynomial-confluence-cells-rare-but-not-forbidden.md), [NB-144](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-144-euler-product-poisson-mass-forces-logarithmic-confluence-away-from-the-one-line.md), the positive-cone optimality theorem [NB-145](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-145-positive-exterior-poisson-sampling-cannot-beat-the-NB-144-coefficient.md), [RE-145](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-145-reciprocal-box-visibility-has-an-exponential-scaled-radius-floor.md)--[RE-148](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-148-early-window-dilation-removes-location-synchronization-but-exposes-a-rate-gap.md), and the moving-observer source-trace reduction [PL-319](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-319-endpoint-log-source-trace-tauberian.md). The mathematical objects and hypotheses remain line-specific.

Nyman--Beurling and Robin Extremal expose the same quantifier failure under broad upper population laws by different mechanisms. In Nyman, Selberg density makes logarithmically crowded polynomial confluence cells rare in height but does not rule out a sparse sequence of target-poor cells. In Robin, zero density can suppress a weighted exterior population while leaving a local reciprocal configuration capable of hiding a strong target.

NB-144 shows what changes when the source theorem charges the exact exceptional cell. The actual Euler product yields a positive local Poisson budget that excludes some xi-type matched packets and forces `o(log G)` occupancy as the cell approaches the one-line. NB-145 then closes an important escape: arbitrary positive exterior resampling cannot improve the fixed-interior coefficient if the proof uses only Poisson positivity and the pointwise absolute Euler-product majorant. The one-point argument is already minimax in that cone. **Once a local source budget is optimized inside its proof class, more instances of the same inequality are not a stronger source law.**

Robin supplies the complementary rate-comparison lesson. RE-145 gives a cardinality-free local visibility floor `exp(-O(R))`. Scale-maximal anchoring in RE-146 converts rightward horizontal advantage into coefficient/height cost; RE-147 improves the population theorem itself by replacing the near-edge Ingham tax `Theta(delta)` with Pintz's `Theta(delta^(3/2))` tax. RE-148 then removes the positional mismatch by placing the local witness on the same early window as the shell estimate.

Yet this stronger and synchronized source control still does not close the endpoint. The currently proved local floor has exponent constant above `19`, while the shell gain is below `1/2`, so increasing the common radius makes the certified comparison worse. This is a sharper failure mode than “global density is too weak”: **the source-side and destination-side rates can have the wrong ordering even after they are expressed in the same local currency.**

Prime Lattice gives a third possibility. PL-319 does not improve a population estimate at all. Instead the exact source equation supplies a convergent endpoint trace, and an operator identity turns that convergence into the moving-tail law `Qm(Q)->0` once the leading boundary profile is removed and the residual is integrable with vanishing singular remainder. Here the source law succeeds by changing the logical form of the local gate: anti-concentration becomes Tauberian rather than a separately imposed pointwise estimate.

The refined cross-line diagnostic is therefore: identify the exceptional geometry allowed by the current theorem; express it in the destination's scale; determine whether the available source argument is already optimal inside its proof cone; and compare its rate with the local visibility or stability loss. If that comparison fails, progress requires a genuinely new source resource, a sharper destination theorem, or an exact structural identity that changes the gate — not simply more averaging, more sampling or a stronger global count with the same local currency.

For Nyman the open resource lies outside the positive Poisson cone: signed/oscillatory zero-side control, prime correlations or a target-bearing theorem for surviving packets. For Robin it lies in a much sharper local floor, a stronger source-specific exterior exponent, or relational phase/sign structure, together with control of the near-left and vertically remote pieces. For Prime Lattice it is the actual boundary expansion and residual class needed to activate the source-trace identity.

**Boundary.** NB-145 is a method-optimality result for one positive proof architecture, not a universal impossibility theorem. RE-147--RE-148 concern the leading Robin packet near a finite zero edge and do not control all exterior components. PL-319 is a one-dimensional logarithmic-Laplacian identity. No coefficient or mechanism transfers automatically between the lines.
