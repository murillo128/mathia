# MI-017 — Sparse exceptional geometry survives upper population control

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [NB-141](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-141-selberg-density-makes-polynomial-confluence-cells-rare-but-not-forbidden.md) and [RE-142](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-142-upper-zero-density-envelopes-cannot-improve-the-rightmost-robin-gain.md), with the moving-observer quantifier warning independently reinforced by [PL-316](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-316-critical-half-derivative-still-misses-moving-diagonal.md). The mathematical objects and hypotheses remain line-specific.

Two current zero-based lines now expose the same quantifier failure by different mechanisms. In Nyman--Beurling, Selberg density makes logarithmically crowded polynomial confluence cells power-law rare in height, but the current global count/density/zero-free envelopes still permit a sparse sequence of target-poor cells. In Robin Extremal, upper zero-density information suppresses the weighted exterior tail, but the same class of upper envelopes still permits a sparse formal spectrum whose rightmost transfer loses the full two powers of truncation height even when retained-core complexity is `o(log T)`.

The shared mathematical lesson is narrower than “average estimates are weak.” **An upper population law does not control the relational geometry of the exceptional points that remain.** A destination that can fail because of one bad cell, one lacunary subsequence, or one badly placed rightmost atom needs a source theorem with the corresponding worst-case or relational quantifier.

For NB the missing relation is local occupancy/target geometry: either uniformly exclude `Theta(log G)` zeros from every forbidden complex cell or prove that an actually crowded cell cannot remain target-poor. For RE the missing relation is horizontal position/ordinate recurrence: a strong atom must force a sufficiently rightward companion below a controlled height, or the observability certificate must avoid the rightmost-transfer step. A better exponent in the same one-point upper count changes rarity but not either relation.

PL-316 supplies a useful independent control on the logic. A global critical norm can be arbitrarily small while a moving observer extracts order-one mass from a sparse sequence of scales. This is not the same zero-density phenomenon, but it confirms the endpoint principle: when the observer moves or takes a supremum, small total mass/energy is not enough unless the source law prevents concentration exactly where the observer looks.

The reusable discriminator is therefore: identify the set of exceptional configurations still allowed by the source theorem, then ask whether the destination is stable under an arbitrarily sparse sequence of them. If not, the next theorem must couple the source directly to the destination's local geometry rather than merely reduce the population of exceptions.

**Boundary.** This synthesis does not assert a common zero theorem behind NB and RE, nor that every upper-density estimate is useless. In RE the same density theorem is quantitatively useful for the exterior tail; in NB it gives a genuine measure rarity statement. The claim is only about the missing implication from upper population control to the worst-case relational structure required by these endpoints. PL-316 is an analogy at the quantifier/observer level, not a transfer of its Sobolev construction to zeta zeros.
