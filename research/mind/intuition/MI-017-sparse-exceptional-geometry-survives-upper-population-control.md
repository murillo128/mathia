# MI-017 — Target-conditioned thin channels can carry order-one leverage while typical-population controls see almost nothing

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from NB-147--NB-153, FD-147--FD-151, MC-319--MC-322, RE-145/RE-151--RE-157 and PL-321--PL-323. The mathematical objects and scales remain line-specific.

A recurring obstruction is now quantitatively clearer: **small ambient population is not small destination influence**, and conversely a broad or ill-conditioned observation can move a target-conditioned debt without removing it. A target may concentrate decisive mass on a thin shell, deep ancestry layer, signed lobe or exceptional stage that generic population/interval controls underweight. The relevant question is not how rare the source feature is globally, but how much mass the destination assigns to it and which conditioning, localization and population cost is paid to isolate it.

Robin Extremal now separates ambient PNT closeness from local ordinary-prime supply. RE-153 identifies the self-tangent source requirement as a Pareto-weighted average of `(t-vartheta(t))/sqrt(t)`. RE-156 finds an ordinary-prime relocation transition

`epsilon_*(p)=sqrt(log U_A(p)/(sqrt(p)log p))`,

because both the influence range and the number of locally available ordinary primes shrink with the terminal width. RE-157 removes the local-deletion cap using PNT-small generalized-prime insertion packets and pushes order-one destination ambiguity down to

`epsilon_PNT(p)=log U_A(p)/(sqrt(p)log p)=epsilon_*(p)^2`.

For every `epsilon/epsilon_PNT->infinity`, a perturbation intensity tending to zero can still move the destination by order one. Thus even a vanishing PNT-scale source discrepancy can retain full leverage long after the ordinary relocation control becomes subcritical; local source supply is itself a mathematical resource that must be justified rather than inferred from global density.

Farey Discrepancy gives the depth/anchor analogue. FD-149 shows bounded-distortion ancestry must reach essentially typical squarefree factor depth. FD-151 makes the anchor bill explicit: if `p_T` is the directly oriented anchor mass, parent distortion obeys `h<=P_T p_T/(1-p_T)`. Keeping parent expansion bounded therefore requires positive-density direct orientation; sparse anchors merely move the information cost into the same thin arithmetic population one hoped to infer.

Möbius Cancellation gives an observation-population analogue. MC-320 shows that strong control on ambient integer intervals can coexist with maximal bias on the exact dyadic prime shell. MC-321--MC-322 then attack the correct prime-supported population and obtain a family energy bound, replacing raw character count by an `ell^2` shell-bias budget below the cubic modulus scale. A theorem becomes relevant only when it is stated on the population the destination actually consumes.

Nyman--Beurling supplies both oversmoothing and conditioning versions. NB-150 proves that moving a signed two-point sampler to any growing mesoscopic horizontal scale `1<<L_G=o(G)` fills both lobes with `Theta(log G)` actual-zero mass while the narrow target is diluted. NB-151--NB-152 show that at fixed horizontal scale a logarithmic target packet instead forces a neighboring logarithmic adverse packet by completed-function conservation. NB-153 extends this to any fixed compact two-dimensional exterior cloud and quantifies the escape bill: with condition number `C_G=||mu_G||_TV/q_G=o(log G)`, a fixed fraction of the adverse charge remains within radius `O(C_G)` and is carried by at least `Omega(log G/C_G)` zeros. Ill-conditioning can trade population for displacement, but below logarithmic order it cannot erase the target-conditioned debt.

Prime Lattice gives the complementary structural escape. PL-322--PL-323 change the destination gate itself and remove the generic moving-diagonal obstruction, so no exceptional-population theorem is needed there; the residual problem becomes the rational-prime-specific sign/first-crossing law.

The reusable diagnostic is destination-weighted and quantifier-sensitive. Identify the exact set/mode/shell/depth/sign component the target consumes; compute its leverage before judging it negligible from ambient density; distinguish global source closeness from local source supply; state whether the source theorem is average, uniform, target-conditioned or prime-restricted; and price any conditioning or localization used to isolate the target. A vanishing source fraction, a PNT-small perturbation, an interval theorem on the wrong population, a typical-center law, a mesoscopic average that swamps a narrow packet, or a condition number that merely pushes adverse mass farther away has not closed the gate.

**Boundary.** The examples use unrelated measures, operators, graph depths and arithmetic families. No common exponent or estimate transfers between them. The synthesis is the logical requirement to measure source smallness, local supply, observation breadth and conditioning in the destination's own weighting/category before declaring a thin channel harmless or a broad/ill-conditioned observation informative.
