A recurring obstruction is quantitatively clearer: **small ambient population is not small destination influence**, but neither is syntactic sparsity automatically a surviving resource. A target may concentrate decisive mass on a thin shell, deep ancestry layer, signed lobe, exceptional height or structured source family that generic population controls underweight. The relevant questions are how much leverage the destination assigns to that set, whether it survives natural closure/completion, and how an ambient exceptional or extremal family intersects the exact source image.

These examples show that thinness must be measured after the destination's own closure and quantifier are fixed.

MC-385 adds an exact source-incidence version. Endpoint rows are indexed by an additive source group, so exceptional difference modes induce bad-neighbor geometry on a Cayley frame. An ambient exceptional count says little unless it stays sparse after pullback to that structured difference image.

MC-388 supplies the source-image gate that the abstract sharpness argument was missing. A high-dimensional endpoint source therefore cannot approach the low-dimensional coset required by the abstract near-extremizer. The ambient inequality remains sharp, but its sharpness mechanism is inadmissible on the actual source image.

The reusable diagnostic is therefore destination-weighted, closure-aware, quantifier-aware and **source-pullback-aware twice**. First pull exceptional populations to the destination-visible source family and measure them in the norm the endpoint consumes. Then, if an ambient bound is sharp, classify its near-extremizers and pull those shapes through the exact source map as well. Arithmetic structure can exclude an ambient extremizer without improving the ambient theorem itself.

**Boundary.** The examples use unrelated measures, operators, graph depths and arithmetic families. No common exponent or density conclusion transfers between them. MC-388 excludes the coset/annihilator near-extremizer only in the high-dimensional exact endpoint regime and does not yet improve the `H/R` exceptional-count scale quantitatively. The synthesis is the requirement to measure both exceptional size and extremal shape in destination-visible source coordinates.
