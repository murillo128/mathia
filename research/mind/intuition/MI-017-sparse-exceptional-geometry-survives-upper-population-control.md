# MI-017 — Sparse exceptional geometry survives upper population control, but local geometric budgets can bite

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis from [NB-141](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-141-selberg-density-makes-polynomial-confluence-cells-rare-but-not-forbidden.md), sharpened by the source-specific local law [NB-144](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-144-euler-product-poisson-mass-forces-logarithmic-confluence-away-from-the-one-line.md), and [RE-142](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-142-upper-zero-density-envelopes-cannot-improve-the-rightmost-robin-gain.md), quantitatively refined on the Robin side by [RE-145](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-145-reciprocal-box-visibility-has-an-exponential-scaled-radius-floor.md), with the moving-observer quantifier warning independently reinforced by [PL-316](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-316-critical-half-derivative-still-misses-moving-diagonal.md). Nyman--Beurling and Robin Extremal expose the same quantifier failure under broad upper population laws by different mechanisms. In Nyman, Selberg density makes logarithmically crowded polynomial confluence cells rare in height but does not by itself rule out a sparse sequence of target-poor cells. In Robin, upper zero-density information suppresses a weighted exterior tail while still allowing local reciprocal geometry capable of hiding a strong target.

NB-144 shows what kind of additional information changes this picture. The actual zeta Euler product yields a **positive local Poisson budget**: zeros in the exact right-half cell seen by the obstruction are charged according to their distance from the one-line. This excludes some xi-type matched packets and forces `o(log G)` occupancy for packets whose centers approach `Re s=1`. The fixed-interior coefficient remains positive, so the budget does not yet remove every logarithmic-rank exceptional cell.

RE-145 supplies a different local budget without source specificity. Positive empirical packets confined to reciprocal scaled radius `R` retain normalized visibility at least `exp(-O(R))`, independently of cardinality, and RE-144 shows this exponential order is sharp in the matched class. Thus even when a global population theorem does not exclude the cluster, local geometry can still price exactly how much cancellation it can buy. Closure then becomes a comparison between this local floor and the exterior error terms.

The refined lesson is therefore not merely that averages are weak. **An upper population law controls how many exceptions exist globally; a useful local law must charge the relational geometry of the exception at the scale actually consumed by the destination.** Such a law can turn a qualitative obstruction into an explicit rate comparison without yet supplying full target coercivity.

For Nyman the remaining relation is fixed-interior occupancy/target geometry: improve the source-side Poisson budget to a sublogarithmic law there or prove that surviving crowded cells cannot remain target-poor. For Robin the remaining relation is source control of scaled reciprocal radius versus the exterior budget: make `exp(-O(R(U)))` dominate the tail, or impose phase/sign/source structure that forbids the matched product organization.

PL-316 supplies an independent control on the quantifier logic. A global critical norm can be arbitrarily small while a moving observer extracts order-one mass from a sparse sequence of scales. This is not the same zero-density phenomenon, but it confirms that when the observer moves or takes a supremum, small total mass/energy is insufficient unless the controlling law prevents concentration exactly where the observer looks.

The reusable discriminator is: identify the exceptional configurations still allowed by the current source theorem, derive the sharpest local geometric budget available for them, and compare that budget in the destination's own normalization. Seek stronger global population estimates only when they alter that local comparison.
