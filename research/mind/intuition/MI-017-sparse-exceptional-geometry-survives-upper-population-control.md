Prime Lattice shows the complementary escape: change the destination gate rather than strengthen the population theorem. The reusable diagnostic is therefore quantifier- and sign-sensitive. Identify the exceptional target geometry; express it in the destination scale; determine the exact orbit/sign mass charged by the source theorem; ask whether the theorem is average, high-probability or uniform at target-bearing centers; and compare its rate with destination visibility. If an averaging theorem can ignore a lacunary exceptional set, or a signed cancellation merely moves equal mass into an uncontrolled negative lobe, it has not closed the target-conditioned gate.

For Nyman the missing resource must control the negative signed lobe or add prime-side/target-bearing information beyond the classified positive cone.
