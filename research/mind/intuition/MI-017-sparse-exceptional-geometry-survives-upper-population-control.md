# MI-017 — Sparse exceptional geometry survives upper population control, but source-coupled local budgets can bite

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis from [NB-141](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-141-selberg-density-makes-polynomial-confluence-cells-rare-but-not-forbidden.md), sharpened by the source-specific local law [NB-144](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-144-euler-product-poisson-mass-forces-logarithmic-confluence-away-from-the-one-line.md), and [RE-142](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-142-upper-zero-density-envelopes-cannot-improve-the-rightmost-robin-gain.md), quantitatively refined on the Robin side by [RE-145](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-145-reciprocal-box-visibility-has-an-exponential-scaled-radius-floor.md) and [RE-146](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-146-scale-maximality-turns-zero-density-into-an-exponential-rightward-shell-budget.md), with the moving-observer quantifier warning independently reinforced by [PL-316](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-316-critical-half-derivative-still-misses-moving-diagonal.md). The mathematical objects and hypotheses remain line-specific.

Nyman--Beurling and Robin Extremal expose the same quantifier failure under broad upper population laws by different mechanisms. In Nyman, Selberg density makes logarithmically crowded polynomial confluence cells rare in height but does not by itself rule out a sparse sequence of target-poor cells. In Robin, upper zero-density information suppresses a weighted exterior tail while still allowing local reciprocal geometry capable of hiding a strong target.

NB-144 shows what kind of additional information changes this picture. The actual zeta Euler product yields a **positive local Poisson budget**: zeros in the exact right-half cell seen by the obstruction are charged according to their distance from the one-line. This excludes some xi-type matched packets and forces `o(log G)` occupancy for packets whose centers approach `Re s=1`. The fixed-interior coefficient remains positive, so the budget does not yet remove every logarithmic-rank exceptional cell.

RE-145 supplies a different local budget without source specificity. Positive empirical packets confined to reciprocal scaled radius `R` retain normalized visibility at least `exp(-O(R))`, independently of cardinality, and RE-144 shows this exponential order is sharp in the matched class. Thus even when a global population theorem does not exclude the cluster, local geometry can still price exactly how much cancellation it can buy.

RE-146 shows how an actual source-side population theorem becomes useful once it is coupled to the **right variational anchor**. Choosing the zero whose Robin atom is maximal at scale `U` converts a rightward displacement `R/U` into an exponential coefficient penalty. Looking earlier turns that penalty into a fractional coefficient moment, after which ordinary Ingham zero density gives an `exp(-cR)` rightward-shell budget up to an explicit anchor-height factor. The gain is not a stronger density theorem; it comes from forcing the exceptional configuration to pay in the currency that the density estimate can sum.

The refined lesson is therefore stronger than “averages are weak.” **An upper population law controls how many exceptions exist globally; a useful local argument must first couple the exception to a source/variational constraint that prices its relational geometry at the destination scale.** Only then can a population estimate turn into a coercive rate. This can convert a qualitative obstruction into an explicit rate comparison without yet supplying full target control.

For Nyman the remaining relation is fixed-interior occupancy/target geometry: improve the source-side Poisson budget to a sublogarithmic law there or prove that surviving crowded cells cannot remain target-poor. For Robin the rightward shell is now priced, but the local witness must be synchronized with the earlier subwindow and the near-left plus vertically remote near-horizontal pieces remain. Closure requires one common `R(U)` and observation point where the local `exp(-O(R))` floor dominates all exterior budgets, or additional phase/sign/source structure that forbids the matched product organization.

PL-316 supplies an independent control on the quantifier logic. A global critical norm can be arbitrarily small while a moving observer extracts order-one mass from a sparse sequence of scales. This is not the same zero-density phenomenon, but it confirms that when the observer moves or takes a supremum, small total mass/energy is insufficient unless the controlling law prevents concentration exactly where the observer looks.

The reusable discriminator is: identify the exceptional configurations still allowed by the current source theorem, find a source-native or variational relation that converts their displacement into a summable cost, derive the sharpest local geometric budget, and compare that budget in the destination's own normalization. Seek stronger global population estimates only when they alter that local comparison.

**Boundary.** NB-144 is specific to the actual zeta Euler-product half-plane; RE-145 is a representation-level positive-measure visibility theorem; RE-146 is a leading-layer scale-maximal Robin estimate using Ingham density and does not control all exterior pieces; PL-316 concerns a different moving-tail operator. No coefficient or rate transfers automatically between the lines.
