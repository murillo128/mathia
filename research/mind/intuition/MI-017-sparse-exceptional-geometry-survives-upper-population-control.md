# MI-017 — Sparse exceptional geometry survives population control; the useful source theorem must change the local rate, orbit or gate

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis from [NB-141](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-141-selberg-density-makes-polynomial-confluence-cells-rare-but-not-forbidden.md), [NB-144](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-144-euler-product-poisson-mass-forces-logarithmic-confluence-away-from-the-one-line.md), the corrected positive-sampling classification [NB-146](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-146-separated-right-half-poisson-optimality-has-a-factor-two-self-dual-boundary-jump.md), [RE-145](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-145-reciprocal-box-visibility-has-an-exponential-scaled-radius-floor.md)--[RE-149](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-149-zero-horizontal-spread-product-packets-isolate-vertical-escape.md), and [PL-320](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-320-source-trace-full-tail-volterra-matching.md). The mathematical objects remain line-specific.

Nyman--Beurling shows why broad zero-density rarity is not a local exclusion theorem. The actual Euler product does charge the exceptional cell through a positive Poisson budget. NB-146 identifies the exact limit of that resource: arbitrary positive exterior resampling cannot improve the separated right-half coefficient when the proof uses only zero positivity and the pointwise absolute Euler-product majorant. At the critical line the coefficient changes because the reflection orbit changes, so even the **counting orbit** is part of the local source/destination contract.

Robin Extremal supplies a different saturation mechanism. RE-145 gives the cardinality-free local floor `exp(-O(R))`; RE-148 shows the local witness and actual-zeta right-shell estimate can be compared at the same point but with incompatible proved exponents. RE-149 then shows that exponential deterioration is already sharp for pure vertical phase escape with zero horizontal spread. Better global counts or horizontal localization leave a matched exceptional family unless an actual-zeta theorem restricts its vertical occupancy/phase geometry.

Prime Lattice shows a third route: change the logical gate rather than the population estimate. PL-320 turns convergence of the source trace and one singular translation remainder into an exact Volterra identity that forces both the boundary coefficient and `Qm(Q)->0`. The local anti-concentration condition becomes a consequence of structural coupling instead of a stronger pointwise population bound.

The reusable diagnostic is therefore more specific than “global density is insufficient.” Identify the exceptional local configuration; express it in the destination's scale; determine whether the available source argument is already optimal in its proof class; track any symmetry/orbit multiplicity used by the estimate; and compare its rate with the destination visibility loss. If those steps fail, progress needs a genuinely new source resource, a sharper destination theorem, or an identity that changes the gate itself.

For Nyman the missing resource is outside the positive Poisson cone. For Robin it must constrain the vertical/near-left geometry or reverse the local-vs-shell exponent ordering. For Prime Lattice the remaining analytic question is absolute pre-diagonal control or a weaker transfer theorem, not a stronger generic forcing norm.

**Boundary.** NB-146 is method-optimality for a positive proof architecture, RE-149 is a matched-control spectrum rather than an actual-zeta construction, and PL-320 is a one-dimensional endpoint identity. No coefficient or mechanism transfers automatically between lines.
