# MI-024 — Control subtraction must match the load-bearing source channel

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [PC-330](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-330-smooth-li-profile-subtraction-preserves-canonical-green-hilbert-band.md), [NB-179](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-179-poisson-separated-dipoles-realize-maximal-coordinatewise-phase-codes-without-arithmetic.md), [WI-327](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-327-signed-source-subtraction-suppresses-cubic-resonance-on-density-one-starts.md), and [VIS-305](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-305-cramer-parity-control-loglog-spacing-norm.md)--[VIS-306](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-306-bernoulli-signed-shell-hilbert-cancellation.md). No universal subtraction theorem is claimed.

Four current lines show that “subtract or match the obvious background” is meaningful only after identifying the representation and norm in which the alleged signal actually lives.

Prime Circle gives the failure mode. The smooth Li/PNT profile has the correct leading density and removes fixed low-frequency structure, but it is too diffuse in `L^2` to cancel the sparse prime source. The residual keeps asymptotically all of the source RMS and therefore preserves the dominant Green Hilbert channel. A control can be accurate in a weak/density sense while missing the channel that carries the spectral effect.

Nyman--Beurling gives the complementary matched-control failure. Hadamard-scale coordinatewise response looks like rich prime-phase reuse until the control is matched in capped-Poisson geometry. Generic Poisson-width dipole packing already realizes the same maximal response and exact complexity. Matching only source labels or the intended interpretation of the probes would have credited ambient metric coding as arithmetic structure.

Weil Inertia gives a successful subtraction at the correct source level. Raw positive `Lambda` weights preserve the snapped cubic resonance, but the actual Gallagher/BOW coefficient is the signed difference `Lambda-Lambda_X^sharp`. On density-one starts that signed source cancels the generic coherent block to a small fraction of its length. Here the source correction acts directly on the coefficient channel that created the apparent resonance, and the false positive disappears.

Visual Exploration adds an exact two-stage control. VIS-305 matches density and parity and reproduces the `x log log x` **absolute** reciprocal-spacing norm, but that still leaves open whether the signed endpoint form is large. VIS-306 evaluates that signed channel on the same Bernoulli control and finds only `O_p(x)`, hence `o_p(H)` at `H~x log log x`. The control becomes decisive only after it is applied to the signed observable actually consumed by the endpoint. A generic absolute critical norm can coexist with subcritical signed output.

The reusable rule is therefore **channel-matched control all the way to the destination-visible sign/norm**. Identify the coefficient, quotient, phase relation or norm through which the candidate reaches the destination; preserve that channel in the control; and evaluate the same signed or nonlinear observable before interpreting survival as arithmetic structure. A smooth first-order approximation, nominally source-free comparison, positive proxy or absolute majorant is insufficient if the load-bearing channel lives after another cancellation step.

Passing this audit still does not establish arithmetic selectivity. Prime Circle needs a residual after sparse-Hilbert-channel cancellation, Nyman--Beurling needs a probe/range restriction that beats generic Poisson packing, Weil Inertia still needs de-exceptionalization from density-one cancellation to the distinguished bow, and Visual Exploration still needs the actual prime-power signed endpoint form. But the audit prevents a mismatch in source channel, norm or sign from being mistaken for a source-specific signal.

**Boundary.** The four examples concern different representations and do not imply a common subtraction operator or common scale. The synthesis is source-grounded: controls must be matched to the exact mathematical channel carrying the claimed destination effect before survival or cancellation is interpreted.
