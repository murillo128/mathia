# MI-011 — After exact factorization, calibrate benign depth and endpoint channels before treating the remainder as global obstruction

**Evidence level:** supported by NB-066--NB-081 and RE-068--RE-084; no operator or estimate is transferred between the lines, and the Robin long-front conclusions retain their stated same-block hypotheses.

Nyman--Beurling and Robin Extremal both show why a residual can remain large or geometrically rich after obvious common structure is removed yet still fail to measure the target pathology. The extra step is to test the residual against a healthy matched control at the actual depth/kernel and ask whether the allegedly missing information was already reconstructible from boundary data.

In Nyman, NB-078--NB-079 cancel the common coarse-future prediction mode and express continuation as residual discovery minus screening. NB-080 shows that fixed relative future depth still leaves an extensive benign boundary layer on a boundedly invertible outer source with zero stable tail. NB-081 then turns future enlargement into an exact nonnegative screening charge and computes the calibration: for `D_j=(I-rho V_m)e_j`, fixed `0<rho<1`, the remaining charge is `J~R rho^(2L)`, so logarithmic descendant depth is enough to remove the false positive. At the outer noninvertible endpoint `rho=1`, the decay is only `R/(L+1)`. The same residual therefore changes meaning with depth and conditioning.

Robin supplies a complementary endpoint-identifiability collapse. RE-083 proves exact first-layer prime-set conservation on arbitrary mixed fan segments and shows that the raw first-layer delay area is summable under the Robin kernel. RE-084 strengthens this: endpoint selector data reconstruct the **entire** labeled first-layer timeline, whose late geometry is uniformly an ordinary-prime configuration shifted by one half. Even fixed nonlinear one-prime delay statistics therefore add no independent assembly coordinate once endpoints are known.

The surviving Nyman information has to persist after the future depth appropriate to healthy controls, or be explicitly normalized against that screening law. The surviving Robin information has to be joint across layers/states rather than another functional of the first-layer timeline. In both cases, the nominal residual must be tested for **independence from benign completion** before it is interpreted as the obstruction.

The reusable diagnostic is: after exact local/common structure is removed, determine the minimum completion depth or endpoint data needed to reconstruct healthy behavior, then quotient that resource out. A shallow boundary cost or endpoint-determined geometry can be extensive and still be destination-neutral.

**Boundary.** NB-081 calibrates explicit outer controls rather than the arithmetic Nyman source. RE-084 does not control higher-layer interleaving or state-selection constraints. The synthesis is a proof-allocation principle grounded in exact negative controls, not a shared operator theorem.
