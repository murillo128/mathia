# MI-011 — After exact factorization, remove benign boundary and endpoint channels before treating the remainder as global obstruction

**Evidence level:** supported by NB-066--NB-080 and RE-068--RE-083; no operator or estimate is transferred between the lines, and the Robin long-front conclusions remain conditional on the stated same-block stretch hypothesis.

Nyman--Beurling and Robin Extremal now both show why a large residual can remain mathematically real after obvious common structure is removed yet still be irrelevant to the intended destination. The useful move is not only to factor exact local structure, but also to test the surviving quantity against matched controls that have no target pathology.

In Nyman, NB-078--NB-079 write the q-adic change of uncancelled continuation as residual discovery `P` minus future screening `S` after the common coarse-future prediction mode `G` cancels. This makes `P-S` the right algebraic residual, but NB-080 shows that the fixed-ratio sum of this residual is still not tail-specific. A boundedly invertible outer control with zero stable tail and uniformly conditioned Gram has `J_(R,N)>=kappa R-O(1)` on every fixed-ratio refinement ray because a positive-density boundary layer receives only finitely many descendant generations. The remaining charge is a benign finite-depth horizon cost, not persistent memory.

Robin supplies the complementary endpoint collapse. RE-081 removes discrete higher-layer atoms from the leading long-fan transport, and RE-082 shows that the smooth selector-dilation law itself realizes the required power front. RE-083 then proves that arbitrary interleaved higher layers do not create an ordinary-prime endpoint mismatch: `Y-P_C=log rad(C)` upgrades to exact first-layer prime-set conservation on every mixed fan segment. The large raw first-layer delay area also becomes only a summable `O(1/Z_0)` tax after the exact Robin kernel is applied.

The surviving Robin information is therefore nonlinear assembly—event ordering, fixed-state chambers, tied spans and supporting slopes—not smooth density, endpoint support count or linear delay. The surviving Nyman information likewise has to be deeper than a fixed-ratio prediction-volume total: grow future depth, subtract the outer boundary layer, or isolate a target-sensitive mode that survives arbitrarily deep screening.

The reusable diagnostic is stronger than simple factorization: **after removing exact common modes, test the nominal residual on a matched healthy control and under the actual destination weighting/horizon.** A residual that stays extensive on an outer zero-tail source, or a source discrepancy that vanishes under the target kernel, is not yet the obstruction one wants to prove.

**Boundary.** NB-080 does not invalidate NB-079's one-way sufficient criterion, and RE-083 does not construct or exclude a genuine long CA fan. The synthesis is a proof-allocation principle grounded in exact negative controls, not a shared operator theorem.
