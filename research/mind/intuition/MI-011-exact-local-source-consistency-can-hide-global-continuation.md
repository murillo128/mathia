# MI-011 — After local structure is factored out, the surviving obstruction is a scale-distributed continuation or transport residue

**Evidence level:** supported by NB-066--NB-078 and RE-068--RE-081; no operator or estimate is transferred between the lines, and the Robin long-front conclusion is conditional on the stated same-block stretch hypothesis.

Nyman--Beurling and Robin Extremal expose complementary failures of “local matching implies global harmlessness.” In both, the useful move is to remove what exact local/source geometry already explains and then identify the residual budget by **where it is created or transported across scales**.

In Nyman, NB-074--NB-076 write the uncancelled continuation charge as `J=L-C`, with `C` a nonnegative cross-cut lattice of conditioned prediction charges. NB-077 proves that both raw continuation and total cross-cut information survive exact integer branching. NB-078 then resolves the *new* information created at each `q`-adic refinement into average/contrast coordinates. Both continuation gain `ell_m` and prediction gain `c_m` are sums of nonnegative conditional mutual informations, and

`J_m=J_0+sum_(r<=m)(ell_r-c_r)`.

A stable tail therefore requires a cumulative excess of new continuation over new prediction on every refinement ray; summable excess on one ray kills it. The missing arithmetic theorem can target this increment ledger directly, not only the final location of a large determinant charge.

Robin supplies a different factorization. RE-076--RE-080 reduce the late fan to one positive transport measure whose total and reciprocal-weighted moments are forced onto complementary power fronts on a long same-block fan. RE-081 then deletes the obvious discrete carrier: every higher-layer prime-power atom contributes only `o(1)` of both normalized moments, and first-layer births are smaller. The power-front law survives entirely in the continuous selector-dilation component `mu_dil`.

The common diagnostic is now stronger than “control a global residue.” **Once local/atomic structure is removed, ask which scale-distributed channel still carries the deficit.** In Nyman it is the imbalance between new continuation and new contrast-mediated prediction information. In Robin it is the continuous adaptive selector path after discrete event atoms disappear. A proof aimed at already-factored local structure cannot close either line.

This also clarifies the role of localization. Nyman may still close by proving enough prediction gain stays mesoscopically near the cut, but NB-078 permits any source theorem that controls the cumulative increment imbalance. Robin may close by a source theorem restricting the continuous selector path, not by tighter counting of discrete switches.

**Boundary.** No Nyman conditional-mutual-information identity transfers to Robin transport, and no Robin power-front theorem transfers to Nyman continuation. NB-078 does not bound `ell_m-c_m`; RE-081 does not make the continuous front impossible. The synthesis is residual carrier identification after exact factorization, not a shared mechanism.
