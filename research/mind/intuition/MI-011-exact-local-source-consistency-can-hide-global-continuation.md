The surviving Nyman information has to persist after the future depth dictated by the actual source's finite-prediction profile, or be explicitly normalized against that profile. The reusable diagnostic is: after exact local/common structure is removed, identify the quantitative recovery or regularity class of healthy behavior—finite prediction error, future depth, endpoint data, vanishing event-mass sectors—and quotient that resource out at the scale actually required by the destination. A shallow boundary cost or a structurally vivid but mass-negligible component can be extensive locally and still be destination-neutral for all regular observables.
