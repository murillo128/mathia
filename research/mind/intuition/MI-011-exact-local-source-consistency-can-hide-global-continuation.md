# MI-011 — Exact local source consistency can hide a cross-cut continuation deficit or a scale-forced transport ledger

**Evidence level:** supported by NB-066--NB-077 and RE-068--RE-080; no operator or estimate is transferred between the lines, and the Robin long-front conclusion is conditional on the stated same-block stretch hypothesis.

Nyman--Beurling and Robin Extremal expose complementary failures of “local matching implies global harmlessness.” In both cases the useful move is to subtract what local/source geometry already explains and isolate the exact global residue that still has to be controlled.

In Nyman, NB-074 introduces the renormalized continuation charge `J_(R,N)`. NB-075 writes it as raw continuation volume `L_R` minus sequential future-conditioned multiple-correlation charges. NB-076 resolves those charges into a nonnegative lattice of interval endpoint partial correlations:

`J_(R,N)=L_R-sum_(j<R<=n<=N) kappa_(j,n)`.

Each `kappa` is a contiguous-Gram determinant cross-ratio. NB-077 then proves that the rectangular cross-cut sum itself is a block log-determinant information charge and is monotone under exact integer branching: a coarse cross-cut charge cannot disappear through signed fine-scale cancellation. Raw continuation volume is monotone under the same dilation. The remaining Nyman datum is therefore more localized than “does predictive information survive?”—it is whether enough of the branch-stable charge can be forced into a finite/mesoscopic band near the moving cut to recover `L_R-O(1)`. Total preserved information may spread over descendant rectangles whose width grows proportionally with scale.

Robin supplies a different exact factorization. RE-076--RE-079 show that corrected Chebyshev growth and fringe-corrected reciprocal/Mertens decrease are not two independent debts. For every oriented fan segment there is one positive transport measure `mu` with

`Delta Hhat = int 1 dmu`,

`-Delta Ehat + B_1 = int (1/(s log s)) dmu`.

First-layer births are the sole exact mismatch and have summable late tail. On bounded selector dilation the two apparent capacities are one ledger viewed through two kernels.

RE-080 then makes that ledger geometrically rigid on a long same-block fan. If the selected logarithmic sizes stretch by `r->infinity` across a fixed threshold interval `[b_0,b_1]`, the effective kernel scale is forced into

`Z_0 r^(1-b_1-o(1)) <= Z_eff <= Z_0 r^(1-b_0+o(1))`.

More strongly, almost all unweighted transport mass lies above a lower complementary-power front, while almost all kernel-weighted mass is spent below an upper complementary-power front. Thus the Robin residue is not merely “some scale distribution”: on the long-stretch branch it must realize a moving two-front profile dictated by `1-b`.

The common discipline is therefore sharper: **factor local geometry until the remaining global datum has a location as well as a magnitude.** Nyman now knows total conditioned information survives branching but still needs its distance from the cut controlled. Robin now knows where two moments of its one transport measure must sit on a long fan but still needs ordinary-prime source geometry to rule that profile out.

**Boundary.** No Nyman partial-correlation theorem transfers to Robin fan transport, and no Robin transport-front theorem transfers to Nyman continuation. NB-077 does not localize the preserved charge to a bounded band or make `J` monotone. RE-080 does not prove that long same-block stretch must occur or that the forced positive-measure profile is impossible for ordinary primes. The synthesis is residual accounting plus localization, not a shared operator theorem.
