# MI-035 — Nominal multiplicity is leverage only if the destination still resolves it

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis. The shared claim is an accounting rule, not a theorem transferring estimates between the research lines.

Several Mathia frontiers now exhibit the same failure mode: a representation exposes many coordinates, blocks, frequencies, samples, matrix entries or auxiliary variables, but the exact destination map compresses them before they can become a coercive resource. The correct quantity to price is therefore the information that survives the final quotient, aggregation, neutralization or admissible tangent restriction—not the size of the representation before that reduction.

Farey Discrepancy now supplies a phase-sensitive version. FD-269 shows that the critical-zero packet has enough absolute reciprocal-derivative mass to cross the spectral complexity threshold under its stated negative-moment calibration, but FD-270--FD-271 show that typical and integer-sampled replicated mass is controlled by a reciprocal-square ledger. Under the reciprocal-square moment hypothesis used there, threshold coherence is polynomially sparse; FD-272 then shows that Pintz's Mertens-large family has full polynomial counting exponent and cannot all hide inside that exceptional set. Zero count and absolute spectral `l^1` mass are therefore not the destination resource; source-selected phase coherence is.

Möbius Cancellation supplies a quotient-by-anchor version. MC-458--MC-462 retain genuine residual factor variables, but their apparent start family collapses into bounded anchor fibres. On each fibre the relevant gain is governed by effective weighted participation, and even that participation is capped by the residual range. Nominal divisor count becomes useful only after anchor compression and only if the actual coefficients occupy the surviving shift family with enough aligned weight before positive closure.

Nyman--Beurling supplies a target-neutralization version. NB-284--NB-287 show that fixed-packet finite-section excess is a tail of target-aligned null-innovation gains rather than generic inverse-Gram conditioning. NB-292 then identifies a genuine positive multiplicative-band Gram beyond the matched scalar sample/pivot controls, but NB-293 shows exactly how much of that carrier reaches the destination. After neutralization the band becomes the new sample-null block `Q_(m,n,Z)`, and the whole multiplicative-section gain is

`Delta_(m,n,Z)(y)=||P_Q g_(m,n)(y)||^2=x^*M(I+M)^(-1)x`,

with `M=A^(-1/2)Gamma A^(-1/2)` and `x=A^(-1/2)y`. Thus trace, determinant, rank, operator norm and scalar dilation loss count only through spectral occupation by the actual transformed datum. A band component already sample-null is silent for this direct enrichment, and multiplicative gains telescope only after the datum is transported contravariantly. The remaining resource is therefore a source-specific lower bound on target occupation or an equivalent pairing such as `d^*A^(-1)y`, not another proof that the matrix carrier exists.

Prime Lattice supplies a tomography version. PL-424 shows that residue variances, character variances and multiplicative twirling can agree while the local dephasing image-cone verdict differs. PL-426 then shows that the strongest nearby mixed-zero pair-correlation observable is itself a Fourier-diagonal compression: all residues recover cyclic diagonal sums, not the unsymmetrized mixed-character matrix. Many scalar quadratic forms do not become matrix control unless they are tomographically complete for the operator property the destination needs.

Visual Exploration supplies a tangent-reachability version. VIS-379 restricts the relevant derivative to the actual simultaneous-similarity orbit, not the full Hermitian tangent space. VIS-380--VIS-382 show that persistent orientation with vanishing Gram defect requires actual Gram mass in positive commutator modes at the defect-squared scale. A large ambient derivative or a collapsing spectral gap is not leverage unless the source tangent occupies the low modes that the destination can reach.

The same audit explains several representation changes elsewhere. PC-400--PC-401 show that positive and indefinite shell-2 multiplication operators can have the same universal support spectrum even though their source measures differ. WI-397--WI-398 show that matrix dimension does not beat a scalar `log^2 T` budget unless the reference margin or source variation changes. Conditional on the recent non-peer-reviewed Widder source audited in WI-399--WI-400, the resulting root profile is an exact recoding of strip width, so inverting the same profile cannot contract that width. WP-405--WP-406 show that one-sided analytic frequencies are killed by ambient positivity, while a generic block dilation restores positivity only by adding a universal scalar bath. In each case the larger representation is real, but no new independent destination-resolved information has been created.

The reusable rule is: **write the exact map into the final quantity first, then price only the rank, phase coherence, matrix tomography, target occupation, tangent mass, source variation or order asymmetry that survives that map.** A resource erased by signed aggregation, anchor compression, sample neutralization, Fourier-diagonal projection, orbit restriction, spectral recoding or universal dilation is representation richness rather than mathematical leverage.

**Boundary.** This intuition does not say that high-dimensional representations, dense zero packets, factor decompositions, large matrices, ill-conditioned sections, low commutator modes or positive dilations are useless. Each can become decisive when an additional theorem proves that the source occupies the part retained by the destination. The point is only that this occupation/retention theorem is the mathematical resource and must be proved rather than inferred from nominal multiplicity.
