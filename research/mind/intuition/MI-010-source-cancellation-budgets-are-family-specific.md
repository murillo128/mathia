# MI-010 — Source cancellation must be priced after composition geometry, localization, support order and family entropy are fixed

**Evidence level:** supported by Farey results through [FD-093](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-093-dyadic-terminal-obstruction-collapses-to-the-single-row-two-head-atom.md) and Möbius shell results through [MC-264](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-264-support-matched-high-moment-blind-threshold.md). Their conditional hypotheses and literature inputs retain their stated boundaries, and no cancellation theorem or operator transfers between the two families.

Farey Discrepancy has passed the generic abundance ladder. Source-aware Jordan transport recovers positive bulk mass at exact lower anchors, canonical prime contractions remove rowwise predecessor entropy, and scale-normalized weighting makes the predecessor family summable. Odd repeated-prime channels can then restore nonsquarefree recursive eligibility exactly at lower scale.

FD-093 shows that the apparently broad dyadic exception is much thinner. The deleted FD-092 missed one physical-boundary coordinate. After correction, every nonhead dyadic terminal coordinate is again exactly repairable through an odd-prime/four-adic descent; the universal representability failure is concentrated in the single row-two/source-head atom. The remaining Farey bill is therefore **one source atom plus one composition modulus**: either prevent frontier mass from concentrating on that head value, or upgrade frontier-normalized repaired masks to fixed lower-state occupation.

Möbius Cancellation has the orthogonal family-order problem. Conductor descent, generic smooth-modulus variance and the ambient growing-order quadratic large sieve have all been priced at the actual shell scale. The first possible blind relation lives at support `t~log log y`, well beyond the black-box parity-balance range.

MC-264 changes what “family entropy” means at that support. A second moment is genuinely too weak to detect one exceptional shell subset, and even the natural `2t`-th moment remains above the one-atom threshold. But at `m=t+1` the correctly priced quadratic-character diagonal `(2m-1)!! k_y^m` falls below a single blind atom by a large margin. The missing analytic input is therefore not arbitrary stronger pseudorandomness: it is a **fixed-weight shell high-moment estimate**, equivalently off-diagonal Krawtchouk control of Legendre-signature distances for the support-matched tensor family.

The common lesson is now sharper. **First identify the exact residual carrier, then match the analytic order and normalization to that carrier before paying family size.** Farey discovers that almost an entire terminal sector was representationally repairable once the physical boundary atom was separated. Möbius discovers that a family-size obstruction at low moment disappears once the moment order tracks the possible blind support. In both cases, a generic estimate can fail because it prices the wrong object rather than because the desired source-specific phenomenon is absent.

**Boundary.** FD-093 is conditional on the false-RH frontier construction and still does not prove an iterable fixed-strength descent. MC-264 proves the threshold calculation and Krawtchouk identity, not the candidate sparse high-moment estimate. The synthesis concerns source-family accounting, not a transfer of their arithmetic mechanisms.
