# MI-010 — Source cancellation must be priced after recurrence strength, shell occupancy and quotient invariants are fixed

**Evidence level:** supported by Farey results through FD-079 and Möbius shell results through MC-250; the concrete GRH/ERH witness instance remains conditional, and no cancellation theorem or operator is transferred between the two families.

Farey discrepancy has passed several distinct abundance gates. FD-072--FD-076 give causally protected record episodes. FD-077 gives frontier-near source values on a set of full polynomial counting exponent. FD-078 shows that physical-energy weighting cannot introduce any fixed-power occupation loss. FD-079 then closes the remaining ubiquitous subpower escape at the annular level: the logarithmic entropy of the energy-weighted occupation is uniformly bounded, so its logarithmic geometric and arithmetic means have a fixed positive lower bound and every sufficiently small fixed threshold is exceeded on a positive lower logarithmic proportion of endpoint scales.

Farey's residual problem is therefore no longer a nonvanishing aggregate in logarithmic mean. The possible failure has been pushed into **sparse exceptional scales** or into a mismatch between the logarithmic recurrence now proved and the stronger pointwise/local recurrence consumed downstream.

Möbius cancellation has an algebraic rather than concentration bottleneck. MC-244--MC-246 invert the complete blind family to one rough Möbius coset and identify `Q=G/H` as the exact quotient. MC-247--MC-249 use witness support and progression occupancy to squeeze high torsion orders. MC-250 then turns each level-`ell^a` blind subgroup into an actual `F_ell` code and gives the exact multiplicity constraint

`D_y+m_(ell,a)-1 <= #{r in S:r=1 mod ell^a}`.

Thus primary multiplicity is not wholly independent of the support data: it is bounded by order-compatible shell occupancy slack, and high torsion order cannot coexist freely with high rank. What remains dangerous is **large low-order primary structure**, where the compatible shell is itself large and generic coding bounds allow high dimension.

The cross-line lesson has sharpened in opposite directions. Farey now has constant-strength occupation on logarithmically many energy-weighted annular scales but must control exceptional localization. Möbius now has quantitative order--rank coupling inside the exact quotient but must control the large low-order shell or the signed rough-coset amplitude. Neither needs a generic “more cancellation” estimate: each has a precisely located residual currency.

**Boundary.** FD-079 is not a pointwise horizon lower bound and permits deep zero-log-density dips. MC-250 does not force `Q=1` or bound fixed-low-order primary rank when occupancy slack is large. The synthesis compares where cancellation is still missing, not the underlying operators.
