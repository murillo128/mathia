# MI-010 — Source cancellation must be priced after exact observation geometry and exact source inversion are fixed

**Evidence level:** supported by Farey results through FD-076 and Möbius shell results through MC-247; the MC-247 strengthening is conditional on ERH, and no cancellation theorem or operator is transferred between the two families.

Farey discrepancy contains a source-selected normalization mechanism with increasingly rigid local and global arithmetic. FD-072 protects a `sigma`-record by choosing a causal horizon whose denominator is confined to the record-controlled prefix. FD-073 propagates protection to an amplitude-length block; FD-074 constrains the center; FD-075 forces `>>A^2/X` same-parity large-radical hosts inside the block.

FD-076 now supplies power-scale recurrence from the exact source. Pintz's fixed-power-window Mertens maximal order transfers through the inverse floor-harmonic identity, giving frontier-sized physical-source values in every fixed power window. Strict normalized records satisfy

`log X_(j+1)/log X_j -> 1`,

with amplitude `X_j^(Theta+o(1))`; under false RH their protected arithmetic swarms have size `X_j^(2Theta-1-o(1))`. Fixed power gaps are no longer available to a negative model. The remaining currency is **subpower-to-logarithmic recurrence** or another way to aggregate power-dense protected blocks whose relative widths still shrink.

Möbius cancellation has a different bottleneck. MC-244--MC-246 invert the complete blind family until the entire source obstruction is one rough Möbius multiplicative coset `-H`. The true algebraic charge is the quotient `G/H`; quadratic full rank only says its order is odd.

MC-247 conditionally tests how much generic strong zero information says about that quotient. Under ERH every nonprincipal blind character must spread over `>>sqrt(y)/log y` shell coordinates, and the quadratic code gets rank pressure `>>sqrt(y)`. Yet the shell has dimension `asymp y/log y`, and matched coding controls show that this minimum-support scale is still compatible with a large kernel. Generic nonlocality of each blind mode does not determine quotient triviality.

The cross-line lesson is precise: **Farey gains global information by forcing frontier-sized source episodes to recur at power resolution, while Möbius gains algebraic information by exact inversion until only one quotient charge survives.** Neither gain is the final cancellation theorem. Farey still needs finer recurrence/aggregation; Möbius still needs simultaneous shell generation or signed rough-coset cancellation.

**Boundary.** FD-076 gives no bounded multiplicative gaps or logarithmic density. MC-247 is ERH-conditional and gives no quotient-size or signed-amplitude estimate. Their operators and scales remain distinct; the synthesis is the discipline of identifying the exact source currency left after normalization and asking for the theorem that controls that currency itself.
