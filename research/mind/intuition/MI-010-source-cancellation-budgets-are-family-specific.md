# MI-010 — Source cancellation must be priced after recurrence density, parity order and localization are fixed

**Evidence level:** supported by Farey results through FD-082 and Möbius shell results through MC-253; the concrete GRH/ERH witness instance remains conditional, and no cancellation theorem or operator is transferred between the two families.

Farey discrepancy has passed the generic abundance ladder all the way to physical horizons. FD-077--FD-081 move from frontier-near source abundance through physical-energy weighting and annular recurrence to a good physical horizon in every sufficiently late fixed-power window.

FD-082 identifies the exact limit of that currency. Subpower first passage means only that the gaps vanish after taking two logarithms. It forces `N_eta(X)/log log X -> infinity`, but remains compatible with `N(X)~(log log X)^2` and divergent logarithmic horizon entropy. Therefore even a favorable fixed factor gained at every occupied horizon can accumulate to only `X^(-o(1))`. Farey's residual problem is no longer recurrence existence but **accumulation strength**: stronger distinct-horizon density, annular-energy anti-concentration, a gap-sensitive reward, or a genuinely nonlocal use of the bad intervals.

Möbius cancellation has an orthogonal order/localization bottleneck. MC-252 shows that without tight shell localization every finite binary matrix can be realized by genuine separated-prime Legendre symbols, so residue algebra alone is universal enough to model low-rank codes.

MC-253 proves that localization does create new information: every fixed-order parity projection of the actual shell columns is asymptotically balanced for almost all column subsets. But an explicit Walsh-character control has exact pairwise half-distance and the same qualitative fixed-order balance while its binary rank is only logarithmic. The missing shell theorem must therefore see relations whose order grows with scale, impose a direct shell-specific rank obstruction, or bypass generation and cancel the exact rough Möbius coset.

The cross-line lesson is concrete: **generic recurrence and generic pseudorandomness are not currencies until their density/order is high enough for the destination.** Farey needs enough scale occupation to accumulate a power gain; Möbius needs parity information deep enough to detect the global blind quotient. Another theorem that stays subcritical in those respective complexity variables can be mathematically true and still leave the RH-facing obstruction untouched.

**Boundary.** FD-082 does not say the actual good horizons are sparse; it says FD-081 alone permits sparse controls. MC-253 does not say the actual shell has low rank; it says fixed-order parity balance cannot exclude it. The synthesis compares the information budget each destination still lacks, not the underlying arithmetic mechanisms.
