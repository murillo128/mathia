# MI-010 — Residual cancellation should be priced only after representation and detector losses are removed

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis supported by Farey results through [FD-097](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-097-projective-occupation-defect-quality-reduces-growing-depth-loss-to-exponential.md) and Möbius results through [MC-268](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-268-christoffel-endpoint-detector.md). Their mechanisms remain line-specific; no cancellation theorem transfers between them.

Farey Discrepancy illustrates a **composition-coordinate loss**. FD-094--FD-096 close all current one-step branches and supply sharp bounded-defect entry states. FD-097 then shows that separately propagating occupation and defect had introduced a fictitious superexponential penalty. Both inherit the same normalized-energy ratio, so the projective quality `q=eta/K` cancels that ratio exactly and loses only a fixed branch factor per step. The residual is no longer constant blow-up but an effective theorem relating growing predecessor depth to actual physical descent and accumulated transport error.

Möbius Cancellation illustrates a **detector loss**. MC-266--MC-267 collapse tensor/radial structure to the Hamming distribution of the actual Legendre sign image, so universal Krawtchouk algebra is information-neutral. MC-268 then shows that the monomial high moment was nevertheless an inefficient positive detector of the blind endpoint. The Krawtchouk--Christoffel extremal square reaches the natural one-atom scale already at degree `2t` and gives substantially more error budget at degree `2(t+1)`. The remaining resource is an arithmetic estimate for that endpoint-optimal combination, not another universal transform.

The shared rule is sharper than “use source structure.” Before demanding stronger cancellation, remove losses created by the **coordinates of composition** and by the **test used to read the final carrier**. Farey preserved a common scale factor that independent bounds had thrown away; Möbius changed the positive detector without changing the underlying low-support information. Only after those losses are removed does the residual quantify arithmetic cancellation that must actually be proved.

This gives a practical adversarial test for other lines: if a threshold looks impossible, ask whether the proof has independently maximized two quantities that share a latent parameter, or committed to a non-extremal detector before using all target-relevant information. Such a repair is legitimate only when it is exact and information-neutral; it cannot substitute for the source theorem that remains afterward.

**Boundary.** FD-097 does not give an RH contradiction, and MC-268 excludes no blind relation. The synthesis concerns where artificial proof loss can enter, not a universal method for producing cancellation.
