# MI-010 — Residual cancellation should be priced only after composition, detector, inventory and positive-envelope losses are removed

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis supported by Farey results through [FD-100](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-100-source-neutral-predecessor-genealogies-drain-carrier-rows-to-the-four-adic-head.md) and Möbius results through [MC-272](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-272-raw-cauchy-circle-norm-blind-threshold.md). Their mechanisms remain line-specific; no cancellation theorem transfers between them.

Farey Discrepancy first removes a composition-coordinate loss. FD-097 shows that occupation and defect inherit the same normalized-energy ratio, so projective quality cancels that ratio and loses only a fixed branch factor. FD-098 then prices the strongest current uniform local Möbius regularity and shows what it can actually buy: only a submacroscopic source-head corridor.

FD-099 exposes a different hidden resource. Arbitrarily deep **finite** multiplicative horizon descent can preserve the sampled source quotient because repeated-square inventory prepays the contractions. FD-100 then proves that this source-neutral inventory is not inexhaustible on an exact carrier: `I(r)=log_2(r/4)` drops by at least one at every non-head source-neutral repair and terminates at the four-adic head. The live cancellation problem is therefore not “force any contraction”; it is lift this finite inventory charge to the adaptive energy packets and accumulate the genuinely source-moving steps.

Möbius Cancellation removes detector and ambient-family losses in a parallel sequence. MC-268 replaces an inefficient monomial detector by the endpoint-optimal Christoffel square; MC-269 reduces the residual to one flat sparse quadratic-character second moment; MC-270 shows arbitrary-coefficient operator control already costs one blind atom.

MC-271--MC-272 sharpen the last point. Even if the distinguished flat vector is preserved, replacing the shell family by a **positive scalar envelope** loses the needed scale: positive-real generating-function majorants and raw complex-circle `L^p`/Hardy norms stay above the blind-atom threshold for every tuning parameter. The residual resource is phase-sensitive extraction or signed off-diagonal cancellation, not another absolute norm of the full shell polynomial.

The shared rule is therefore more precise than “use source structure.” Before demanding stronger cancellation, identify losses introduced by the coordinates of composition, finite source-neutral inventory, detector choice, enlargement to an ambient coefficient family, and positive scalarization of a signed target. Remove only losses that can be removed by exact identities. What remains afterward is the true source-specific theorem.

A practical adversarial test is to ask whether a proof has hidden progress inside a quotient, allowed a finite inventory to masquerade as indefinite descent, enlarged one distinguished vector to a norm ball, or taken an absolute/positive envelope before the target coefficient was isolated. Any of those moves can spend the entire destination budget before arithmetic cancellation is even tested.

**Boundary.** FD-100 is a carrier-genealogy theorem, not yet an adaptive packet theorem. MC-271--MC-272 close positive-envelope routes but do not prove the required signed cancellation. The synthesis locates artificial proof loss; it does not supply a universal cancellation mechanism.
