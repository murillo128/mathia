# MI-010 — Source cancellation must be priced after composition geometry and quantitative localization are fixed

**Evidence level:** supported by Farey results through FD-085 and Möbius shell results through MC-256. The concrete GRH/ERH support witness in the Möbius line retains its stated conditional boundary, and no cancellation theorem or operator transfers between the two families.

Farey Discrepancy has passed the generic abundance ladder. FD-083 gives full counting exponent for the physical occupied set and FD-084 upgrades this to polynomial-length consecutive additive blocks in every sufficiently late fixed-power future window. FD-085 then proves the crucial negative: a synthetic set can satisfy stronger versions of both properties while every infinite integer-ratio chain has ratios tending to infinity and superexponential logarithmic growth. Coarse abundance and additive thickness therefore cannot supply the finite-entropy multiplicative geometry consumed by the existing GCD-duality mechanism.

The Farey bottleneck is now **source-specific composability**. A positive theorem must use the quotient-shell/Jordan-energy structure to constrain block placement, derive transport directly under additive motion, or replace integer-ratio chaining by a destination mechanism adapted to the proved blocks.

Möbius Cancellation has the orthogonal localization problem. MC-252 shows arbitrary quadratic cross-prime matrices are realizable when the upper primes may escape. MC-253 shows moving-shell fixed-order parity laws are real but rank-blind. MC-254--MC-255 show that completed Rédei and higher `2`-class ranks can filter out the destination-blind cross-block before the higher maps even begin.

MC-256 closes the obvious tame higher-residue repair without localization. For every fixed prime order `ell`, Kummer theory and Chebotarev realize arbitrary finite `F_ell` residue matrices at sufficiently remote genuine primes. Fixed-order higher reciprocity therefore supplies no generic code rigidity by itself. A survivor must use quantitative shell localization, growing-order or wild prime-square information, a source-coupled invariant, or direct signed cancellation of the rough Möbius coset.

The cross-line lesson is exact: **a source statistic must be organized in the same scale/composition geometry as the destination.** Farey has enough points but not the right composition theorem; Möbius has genuine residue algebra but, without quantitative localization, it can realize arbitrary destination-blind codes. Improving an aggregate that forgets the controlling geometry does not approach the RH-facing gate.

**Boundary.** FD-085 is a synthetic set control and does not prove the physical Farey set takes the superexponential escape. MC-256 is qualitative and nonlocalized and does not settle the actual moving shell. The synthesis identifies the missing destination-sensitive structure; it does not transfer arithmetic mechanisms between the lines.
