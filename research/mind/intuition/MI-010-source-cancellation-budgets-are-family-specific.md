# MI-010 — Source cancellation must be priced after the exact family, quantifier geometry, normalization, and blind modes are fixed

**Evidence level:** supported by Farey results through FD-070 and Möbius shell results through MC-241; no cancellation theorem or operator is transferred between the two families.

Farey discrepancy now has a strong source-to-numerator theorem. FD-069 makes every sufficiently late admissible horizon see a source spike; FD-070 uses the physical one-Lipschitz law to expand that spike into a growing four-adic packet with forced energy of order `T A_X^3/X^2` once packet cardinality diverges.

Yet the stronger quantifier does not improve the normalized scalar obstruction. For a zero-frontier spike, the generic same-horizon denominator makes the earliest packet onset optimal, and the resulting exponent reproduces the old `Theta=2/3` boundary. More rows and later horizons are therefore closed resources for this mechanism; the next gain must couple the forced packet to a sharper denominator estimate or change the target normalization.

Möbius cancellation has a different family geometry. MC-238--MC-240 reduce blindness to cross-prime common-quotient relations and first force a fixed support floor. MC-241 eliminates **every bounded support size** asymptotically. Any surviving blind character must involve an unbounded number of shell primes.

That is still not cancellation. MC-241 is qualitative in support and gives neither annihilator triviality nor Fourier decay. The live gate is uniform growing-support character control or a structural theorem showing that the actual Möbius progression vector couples weakly to all surviving collective modes.

The cross-line lesson is stronger than “abundance is not cancellation.” A favorable theorem must survive the exact downstream normalization and quantifier geometry of its family. Farey has ample persistent numerator mass but pays destination dilution; Möbius has eliminated every finite-size blind pattern but may retain genuinely collective blindness.

**Boundary.** FD-070 is not a normalized zero-free theorem and MC-241 is not a signed progression estimate. Their operators remain distinct.
