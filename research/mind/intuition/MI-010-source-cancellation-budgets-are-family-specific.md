# MI-010 — Residual cancellation should be priced only after structural collapse exposes the final carrier

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis supported by Farey results through [FD-095](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-095-bounded-record-defect-turns-the-source-head-into-an-amplitude-scale-predecessor.md) and Möbius shell results through [MC-266](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-266-tensor-pair-krawtchouk-radial-source-walk-collapse.md). Their hypotheses and arithmetic mechanisms remain line-specific; no cancellation theorem transfers between them.

Farey Discrepancy has now closed every current local one-step representability branch under one quantitative hypothesis. Exact prime-square/four-adic transports give lower nonsquarefree carriers for the nonhead branches. FD-094 shows that converting their frontier-normalized size into fixed lower-state occupation costs exactly the parent's backward normalized-energy record defect `R_sigma(H)`. FD-095 then removes the apparently different row-two/source-head obstruction: the source is one-Lipschitz, so backing up by a fixed fraction of its amplitude and reembedding on row `4` produces a genuine predecessor with fixed occupation and bounded inherited defect whenever the parent defect is bounded.

The Farey residual has therefore changed category. It is no longer “repair the last bad local branch.” The first gate is **historical coupling**: prove that some sufficiently sharp occupied frontier packets occur with `R_sigma(H)=O(1)`. Only after that comes the multistep stability question, because inherited occupation/defect constants may deteriorate under repeated descent even though every one-step branch closes.

Möbius Cancellation has undergone a different structural collapse. MC-264 shows that support-matched moment order `m=t+1` crosses the one-blind-atom entropy threshold; MC-265 shows first-order shell balance is far too weak if pair terms are absoluteized. MC-266 then proves that the apparent weighted tensor-pair geometry is algebraically redundant: Boolean-cube convolution turns the pair autocorrelation into a single `2m`-step source walk, whose weights are radial in the parity support `s`.

The Möbius residual is therefore the signed finite hierarchy

`H_(2,y)(t), H_(4,y)(t), ..., H_(2m,y)(t)`

of fixed-support Krawtchouk shell sums, combined with explicit radial coefficients. The raw mass of the nontrivial collision-depth layers is larger than the diagonal, so the exact reduction does not create a small error. It identifies where cancellation must happen: **inside or across source-support layers before absolute values are taken**.

The shared lesson is more specific than “use source structure.” Structural simplification should continue until the residual carrier cannot be reduced without losing the theorem. In Farey that carrier is now a scalar historical defect plus its iterative stability. In Möbius it is a finite signed radial family at the support-matched moment order. Paying abundance, local branch count, arbitrary pair geometry, or a stronger auxiliary norm after those objects have collapsed is paying for information the destination no longer asks for.

**Boundary.** FD-095 does not produce bounded-defect occupied packets or a uniform infinite descent. MC-266 proves no signed Krawtchouk estimate and excludes no blind relation. The synthesis is a discipline for locating the final cancellation currency, not a transfer of one line's mechanism to the other.
