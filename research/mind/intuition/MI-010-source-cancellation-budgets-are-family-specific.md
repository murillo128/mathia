# MI-010 — Exact simplification can relocate the endpoint into normalization, transport or relative locality

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis supported by Farey Discrepancy through [FD-103](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-103-power-dense-record-ladders-saturate-the-logarithmic-source-budget.md), Möbius Cancellation through [MC-275](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-275-witness-normalization-approximate-degree-barrier.md), Prime Flute through [PF-332](../../prime_flute/findings/PF-332-completed-square-root-defect-is-a-resolvent-dressed-pant-insertion.md), and Prime Lattice through [PL-299](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-299-uniform-bv-principal-shift-derivative-limit.md). No universal conserved complexity is claimed.

Several mature reductions show that solving the apparent local problem often changes the type of the residual rather than merely shrinking an error term.

Farey Discrepancy removes carrier identity and adaptive retagging from the deterministic source-neutral budget. FD-102 gives a carrier-free potential and FD-103 proves its logarithmic depth sharp on actual record ladders. The remaining cost is packet strength and source progress across `c log H` transport, not better carrier bookkeeping.

Möbius Cancellation now makes the relocation exact. MC-273 simplifies any nonblind row after a negative witness; MC-274 shows atomwise witness search can be deep; MC-275 then proves that **overlapping use of all witnesses is cheap** at degree `m+1`. The expensive step is normalization: converting overlap to one unit per nonblind row reconstructs OR, whose exact and approximate polynomial degrees exceed the live source depth on the generic cube. The cost moved from row algebra to endpoint classification.

Prime Flute removes another false cost. PF-330 preserves total packet mass and PF-331 removes polar instability. PF-332 shows that even the seam square-root nonlocality cancels in the relative object `F-I`; every remaining defect contains the actual pant/core insertion dressed by resolvents. The live cost is therefore relative source-generated locality through a degenerating resolvent family, not two independent square-root estimates.

Prime Lattice shows that some relocated costs can disappear after choosing the right compactness currency. PL-297 blocks a uniform `H^(1/2)` route, but PL-299 proves `C^1` zero-order shift transport on the pure principal branch using uniform BV instead. The residual moves to proving that weaker compactness for the arithmetic perturbation rather than forcing the failed Fourier norm to become finite.

The durable rule is to ask, after each exact reduction, **which operation is still nontrivial in the variables consumed by the final theorem**. The difficulty may sit in transport depth, normalization, source-restricted classification, relative locality, or compactness of a perturbed family. Optimizing the coordinate that was just algebraically eliminated is then irrelevant even if the estimate is correct.

**Boundary.** These relocations are line-specific. There is no theorem that complexity is conserved or that the new currency is easier. Each line still needs a quantitative comparison between its residual resource and its own endpoint budget.
