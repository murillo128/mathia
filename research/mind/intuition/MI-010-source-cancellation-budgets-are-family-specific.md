# MI-010 — Residual cancellation should be priced only after representation, detector and regularity losses are removed

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis supported by Farey results through [FD-098](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-098-uniform-short-interval-cancellation-thickens-source-head-descent-by-a-logarithmic-corridor.md) and Möbius results through [MC-269](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-269-christoffel-flat-sparse-quadratic-large-sieve-energy.md). Their mechanisms remain line-specific; no cancellation theorem transfers between them.

Farey Discrepancy illustrates a **composition-coordinate loss** followed by a **regularity ceiling**. FD-094--FD-096 close all current one-step branches and supply sharp bounded-defect entry states. FD-097 shows that separately propagating occupation and defect had introduced a fictitious superexponential penalty: both inherit the same normalized-energy ratio, so the projective quality `q=eta/K` cancels that ratio exactly and loses only a fixed branch factor per step.

FD-098 then charges the strongest currently anchored uniform local Möbius regularity against the residual all-head branch. Above the `11/20` threshold, a frontier spike of amplitude `A=X^(Theta+o(1))` remains protected for a backward corridor `A(log X)^kappa`, `kappa<1/3`, and every four-adic reembedding across that corridor preserves sharpness, occupation and bounded defect. This is a genuine arithmetic improvement, but it is still `o(X)`. The residual is therefore not another composition constant or generic endpoint-smoothing estimate; it is a theorem coupling branch type, source refueling or exact signed/divisibility structure to **macroscopic physical descent**.

Möbius Cancellation illustrates a **detector loss** followed by an **ambient-family loss**. MC-266--MC-267 collapse tensor/radial structure to the Hamming distribution of the actual Legendre sign image. MC-268 shows that the monomial high moment was an inefficient positive detector. MC-269 then writes the endpoint-optimal Christoffel detector exactly as a flat sparse quadratic-character second moment whose desired scale is its literal diagonal.

That reduction removes high-moment coefficient multiplicities but exposes the real arithmetic theorem. Taking absolute values in the radial/Krawtchouk layers requires implausibly fine per-layer precision because the comparison baseline is the tiny diagonal atom. Embedding the exact sparse row family into a generic all-moduli quadratic large sieve also pays a huge ambient penalty. The live resource is cancellation inside the precise fixed-weight shell-product by lower-prime-product matrix, not another universal basis identity or a generic large-sieve theorem oblivious to sparsity.

The shared rule is now sharper than “use source structure.” Before demanding stronger cancellation, remove losses created by the **coordinates of composition**, the **positive detector**, and any **generic regularity/ambient-family estimate** that discards the exact source geometry. Farey preserves a common scale factor and then shows what current uniform smoothing can actually buy. Möbius chooses the endpoint-optimal nonlinear lift and then shows why both layerwise absolute values and all-moduli embeddings throw away the remaining gain.

This gives a practical adversarial test for other lines: if a threshold looks impossible, ask whether the proof has independently maximized quantities sharing a latent parameter, committed to a non-extremal detector, or embedded a thin arithmetic family into an ambient class whose norm scale is much larger than the destination diagonal. Such repairs are legitimate only when exact and information-neutral; they cannot substitute for the source-specific theorem that remains afterward.

**Boundary.** FD-098 does not give a false-RH contradiction, and MC-269 excludes no blind relation. The synthesis concerns where artificial proof loss enters and how to identify the residual arithmetic currency, not a universal mechanism for producing cancellation.
