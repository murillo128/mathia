Möbius cancellation has a different bottleneck. MC-244--MC-245 invert the quadratic blind family to one rough affine Legendre cell and remove mode-count and smooth-weight proxies. This exposes a stronger algebraic boundary: quadratic full rank only kills the elementary `2`-quotient. It is equivalent to `[G:H]` being odd, not to `H=G`; higher-order blind characters can survive in an odd-order quotient. The true exits are therefore full small-prime generation `H=G` or signed cancellation of the one rough multiplicative coset / complete annihilator aggregate.

The cross-line lesson is precise: **Farey gains information by coupling causal normalization to a source episode and then forcing many arithmetic hosts inside its protected neighborhood; Möbius gains information by exact inversion until all blind modes collapse to the one quotient charge the source actually leaves.** Both routes reduce proxy complexity, but one leaves a global recurrence problem and the other a signed quotient-cancellation problem.

**Boundary.** FD-075 still gives no global density theorem; its local cloud can live in multiplicatively sparse blocks. MC-246 gives no full-generation or signed rough-coset theorem and does not assert that odd-order blind quotients occur. Their operators and scales remain distinct; the synthesis is the discipline of identifying the exact source currency that survives normalization.
