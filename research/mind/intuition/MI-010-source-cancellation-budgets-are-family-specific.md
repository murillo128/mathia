# MI-010 — Source cancellation must be priced after the exact family, coherence, normalization, and signed target are fixed

**Evidence level:** supported by the proved Farey transfer/normalization geometry through FD-066 and the exact first-shell representation/transference/variance reductions through MC-237; no cancellation theorem is transferred between the two families.

Two Möbius-facing lines expose complementary versions of the same quantitative lesson.

In Farey discrepancy, FD-062--FD-065 show that genuine short-interval cancellation can buy a host corridor and polynomial packet multiplicity, yet horizon normalization preserves the `Theta=2/3` crossover and Schur-head motion cannot repair it. FD-066 identifies the invariant source-side threshold. If a zero-frontier spike of size `X^{Theta+o(1)}` stays coherent on a window `X^{ell+o(1)}`, the direct packet improves the deterministic terminal obstruction exactly when

\[
\ell>2(1-\Theta).
\]

Host and head exponents cannot change that sign. The current all-short-interval input supplies only `ell<Theta`, so below two thirds the missing theorem is not merely a shorter-interval theorem: it needs source-relative variation `o(A_X)` on super-amplitude windows or a mechanism that avoids the direct horizon ledger.

In Möbius cancellation, MC-232--MC-236 reduce the hard first-shell coordinates to source-selected progression sums, show that both signs already occupy essentially the full progression exponent, and identify why the audited Linnik pipeline still misses the target: principal bias is preserved, the later argument is one-sided, and the published separate-sign transference error is only subpower where a fixed interior point needs fixed-power relative balance.

MC-237 shows that replacing one-sided transference by a strong **signed variance theorem on the exact smooth CRT modulus** still does not cross that scale. The individual smooth-modulus theorem applies directly, but its residual RMS is only `X^{-o(1)}` relative to trivial progression occupancy. It reaches the `X^{1/2}` target only where support already does. Thus the problem is not lack of smoothness or lack of a theorem adapted to the modulus; it is the mismatch between subpower resolution and a fixed-power signed deficit.

The cross-line lesson is stronger than “abundance is not cancellation.” **An input counts only after its gain is measured in the same power currency as the final target.** Farey packet multiplicity is the image of a source-coherence window and can be erased by horizon/leverage normalization. Möbius support, sign abundance, and even signed smooth-modulus dispersion can remain quantitatively useless when their relative precision is subpower.

Therefore coherence length, packet width, horizon cost, scalar leverage, sign abundance, smoothness, variance resolution, transference resolution, and signed amplitude are distinct currencies. The Farey escape requires source coherence beyond `2(1-Theta)`, source-coupled normalization, or another interaction. The Möbius escape requires fixed-power signed accuracy tied to `delta=1/2-2alpha-beta` or coherent recombination before componentwise errors are paid.

**Boundary.** FD-066 remains a conditional classification inside the direct reciprocal-floor/zero-frontier/Schur framework. MC-236--MC-237 audit particular published theorem surfaces and do not prove that stronger signed or source-coupled uses are impossible. The two source families are not identified and no estimate transfers from one to the other.
