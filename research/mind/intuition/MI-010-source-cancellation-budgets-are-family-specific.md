# MI-010 — Source cancellation must be priced after the exact family, quantifier geometry, normalization, and blind modes are fixed

**Evidence level:** supported by Farey results through FD-071 and Möbius shell results through MC-242; no cancellation theorem or operator is transferred between the two families.

Farey discrepancy has a strong source-to-numerator theorem. FD-069 makes every sufficiently late admissible horizon see a source spike, and FD-070 uses the physical one-Lipschitz law to expand that spike into a growing reciprocal packet with forced energy of order `T A_X^3/X^2` once packet cardinality diverges.

FD-071 shows why this stronger quantifier is still not normalized control. The complete reciprocal image of one coherent episode grows only linearly in the later horizon, while under `Theta>1/2` an arbitrarily later frontier spike sampled on one squarefree row can contribute denominator energy of order `T^(2Theta-o(1))`. One can pair two frontier episodes so that the earlier packet has diverging absolute mass but a vanishing share of the same-horizon denominator. The live Farey theorem must therefore be episode-adapted near capture onset or control later-source interference; arbitrary-horizon persistence is a closed resource.

Möbius cancellation has a different family geometry. MC-238--MC-241 reduce blindness to cross-prime common-quotient relations and eliminate every bounded support size. MC-242 strengthens this to the quantitative floor

`liminf m(chi)/log log y >= 1/log 2`.

Thus a surviving blind character must already involve a double-logarithmically growing number of shell primes. This is still not cancellation: the theorem controls support size, not annihilator triviality, number of modes, Fourier decay, or coupling to the actual Möbius progression vector.

The cross-line lesson is stronger than “abundance is not cancellation.” **A favorable source theorem must survive both the family's quantifiers and its destination normalization.** Farey has persistent absolute packet mass but later source events can dilute it; Möbius has excluded every small collective blind pattern up to an explicit growing floor but may retain larger blind modes. In neither family does more raw source structure automatically yield the signed normalized estimate needed downstream.

**Boundary.** FD-071 is not a lower bound for total nonsquarefree occupation and MC-242 is not a signed progression estimate. Their operators and scales remain distinct.
