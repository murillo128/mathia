# MI-010 — Source cancellation must be priced after exact observation geometry, abundance, energy weighting and quotient invariants are fixed

**Evidence level:** supported by Farey results through FD-078 and Möbius shell results through MC-249; the concrete GRH/ERH witness instance remains conditional, and no cancellation theorem or operator is transferred between the two families.

Farey discrepancy has moved through three distinct abundance currencies. FD-072--FD-076 produce causally protected record episodes. FD-077 transfers Pintz's average absolute Mertens order to frontier-near source values on a set of full polynomial counting exponent. FD-078 then rules out a stronger dilution mechanism: after horizons are weighted by their actual physical Schur energy, the aggregate nonsquarefree energy and total energy still have the same full exponent `2 Theta` in every fixed power annulus.

Thus Farey's residual loss is now **subpower even under physical-energy weighting**. The energy-weighted occupation ratio may tend to zero, but cannot lose any fixed positive power. What remains is a nonvanishing aggregate lower bound, a source-specific no-concentration theorem excluding subpower dilution, or another exact bridge from annular energy moments to the downstream criterion. Positive density is one route, but no longer the only missing currency.

Möbius cancellation has the opposite algebraic bottleneck. MC-244--MC-246 invert the complete blind family to one rough Möbius multiplicative coset `-H`; the exact algebraic object is `Q=G/H`. MC-247--MC-248 show that generic witness bounds first constrain character support and that fixed `O(log^A q)`, `A>1`, information cannot force quotient triviality.

MC-249 adds shell-progression occupancy and therefore constrains a new invariant: every prime-power divisor of `exp(Q)` is squeezed below `y/(log log y log log log y)` unconditionally and below square-root scale under the ERH support input. More generally a witness exponent `A` yields torsion order `O(y^(1-1/A))`. But small exponent does not bound primary rank or quotient size, so the surviving obstruction becomes many-coordinate low-order relations rather than sparse high-order ones.

The cross-line lesson is now: **Farey has source amplitude whose counting and energy-weighted survival have reached the zero-power-loss boundary; Möbius has exact source inversion plus increasingly restricted quotient torsion, but still lacks rank/index or signed-amplitude control.** Neither line needs a generic slogan like “more cancellation.” Farey needs to eliminate subpower concentration; Möbius needs simultaneous low-order generation or signed cancellation after exact quotient inversion.

**Boundary.** Equal annular exponents in FD-078 do not imply comparable constants or positive limiting occupation. MC-249's torsion bounds do not control quotient rank, index, or rough-coset amplitude. The operators, arithmetic families and normalizations remain distinct; the synthesis is exact currency separation before asking for a cancellation theorem.
