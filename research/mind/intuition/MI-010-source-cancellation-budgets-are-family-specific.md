# MI-010 — Source cancellation must be priced after compatible scale geometry and localized cross-block information are fixed

**Evidence level:** supported by Farey results through FD-083 and Möbius shell results through MC-254; the concrete GRH/ERH witness instance remains conditional, and no cancellation theorem or operator is transferred between the two families.

Farey discrepancy has now passed the generic abundance ladder completely. FD-077--FD-081 move from frontier-near source abundance through physical-energy weighting and annular recurrence to a good physical horizon in every sufficiently late fixed-power window. FD-082 shows that first passage alone permits a very sparse control, but FD-083 imports the physical atom cap and rules that control out for the actual occupation set: every fixed-power future window contains `X^(1+epsilon-o(1))` constant-strength occupied horizons, and cumulatively the good set has counting exponent one.

The Farey bottleneck is therefore no longer recurrence density. It is **composability**. Full-counting abundance does not by itself give the integer-ratio/multiplicative chain or transport law needed by the GCD/Schur accumulation mechanism, and fixed local gains at unrelated horizons cannot simply be multiplied. The next currency is a compatible scale geometry or a nonlocal inequality that can consume the dense occupied set.

Möbius cancellation has an orthogonal localization/bipartite bottleneck. MC-252 shows that without tight shell localization every finite binary cross-prime matrix can be realized by genuine Legendre symbols. MC-253 shows that localization creates fixed-order parity pseudorandomness, but a logarithmic-rank Walsh control has the same qualitative bounded-order balance.

MC-254 adds a second exact aggregation failure. The shell Legendre matrix is literally an off-diagonal block `A` of the classical Rédei matrix, yet full Rédei rank sees only the intersection of `ker A` with the shell-shell constraints. Genuine prime-discriminant examples can have maximal full Rédei rank and zero narrow `4`-rank while the relevant cross-block has rank one. The next quadratic invariant must therefore see the localized lower/shell interaction itself, grow to the order of possible blind relations, or be bypassed by direct signed cancellation.

The cross-line lesson is now more specific than “generic recurrence and pseudorandomness are subcritical.” **A source statistic must be organized in the same composition geometry as the destination.** Farey has enough points but not yet a theorem composing them; Möbius can have strong global/fixed-order quadratic summaries while the decisive bipartite block remains blind. Improving the wrong aggregate can leave the RH-facing obstruction unchanged even after its nominal density or rank looks maximal.

**Boundary.** FD-083 proves counting abundance, not positive density or a compatible chain. MC-254 proves blindness of ordinary full Rédei rank, not low rank of the actual moving shell. The synthesis compares the destination-sensitive information each family still lacks; it does not transfer arithmetic mechanisms between them.
