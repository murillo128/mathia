**Evidence level:** supported by Farey results through FD-091 and Möbius shell results through MC-262. Their conditional hypotheses and literature inputs retain their stated boundaries, and no cancellation theorem or operator transfers between the two families.

Farey Discrepancy has passed the generic abundance ladder. FD-086--FD-090 then restore the actual Jordan source, recover positive bulk transport, compress row-adaptive anchors to canonical prime contractions and remove the predecessor-count loss after each lower state is priced at its own frontier normalization.

FD-091 shows that even **recursive eligibility** has internal arithmetic structure. For every odd repeated-prime channel, stripping the second prime copy and reembedding by the universal square `4` preserves the quotient shell exactly, restores a nonsquarefree lower mask and descends to scale `4H/p^2`; the normalized carrier weights become `p^(-4 sigma)` and are summable already for `sigma>1/4`. The same operation is scale-neutral for `p=2`. The remaining Farey problem is therefore not an undifferentiated composition failure: it is fixed-strength mask inheritance in the odd branch or the dyadic terminal channel where the universal repair consumes the contraction.

Möbius Cancellation has the orthogonal family-entropy problem. MC-257--MC-261 remove wild conductor directions, audit the projector normalization, compress generic smooth-modulus obstruction to at most one blind pretender and identify full shell generation with an all-class `y`-smooth representation theorem at the extreme cutoff `y~log R`.

MC-262 then makes the growing-order analytic limit explicit. Beyond the fourth-iterated-log range, the explicit `epsilon` dependence plus the waste of embedding a sparse product family in all squarefree moduli destroys the saving before the first live support is reached. The missing theorem must therefore exploit the **actual shell-product family** at its cardinality/order scale, prove logarithmic generation, or attack the surviving blind source mode directly.

The common lesson is sharper than “use source-specific cancellation.” **First identify the exact composition/family on which cancellation must accumulate, then price its entropy at the destination scale.** Farey turns a large predecessor family into a summable normalized packet and discovers one scale-critical prime channel. Möbius discovers that a generic all-moduli theorem can be strong pointwise yet spend its gain on moduli the source never uses. In both cases, improving a bound before respecting the source family can miss the only remaining obstruction.

**Boundary.** FD-091 is conditional on the false-RH frontier construction and does not prove recursive descent. The synthesis concerns how to price source-family geometry, not a transfer of their arithmetic mechanisms.
