# MI-038 — Destination fibres and comparator baselines must be separated before crediting a resource

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis of [AF-509](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-509-log-prime-sum-curvature-classifies-simple-sources-up-to-dilation.md), [AF-548](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-548-finite-regular-jet-lifts-are-pure-gauge-under-unrestricted-calibration.md), [FD-281](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-281-classical-perron-error-pushes-repair-control-above-cluster-pruning-regime.md), [MC-471](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-471-empty-vector-base-is-two-sided-sifted-translated-ratio-sum.md), [MC-511](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-511-relative-state-recurrence-loses-collision-budget-before-reconstruction.md), [NB-296](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-296-ambient-hardy-kernel-saturation-realizes-full-off-critical-band-coercivity-with-zero-ordinary-enrichment.md), [VIS-392](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-392-source-stabilizer-gaussian-null-is-sector-scaled.md), [VIS-429](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-429-target-rarity-forces-pre-saturation-hitting-tail.md), [WI-409](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-409-bsy-already-supplies-summable-positive-horizontal-defect.md), and [WP-410](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-410-central-bost-connes-groupoid-twists-leave-critical-divisibility-gram-unchanged.md), sharpening MI-037.

MI-037 requires a proposed resource to survive the intrinsic destination budget. The new evidence adds two logically prior audits: identify the **fiber of the actual readout**, and separate genuine enrichment from **repair of the comparator baseline**. A quantity can be large, exact, positive or even coercive and still carry no new destination information because it is pure gauge, a reparametrization, an invariant nuisance direction, or compensation for a deliberately distorted baseline.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the positive classification case. AF-509 proves that the full open-interval curvature profile of a simple unit-weight source collapses its readout fiber exactly to dilation, and primitive integer support can remove that gauge. Visual Exploration supplies the nuisance analogue: VIS-392 proves that the sector-scaled Gaussian family is the entire centered Gaussian covariance fiber compatible with the generic qutrit stabilizer. In both cases, the right conclusion comes from classifying the exact surviving fiber rather than informally counting how much upstream structure was retained.

Möbius Cancellation and Weil Positivity show two forms of invisible upstream structure. MC-471 identifies the staged residual divisor coordinate in the empty-vector branch as a reparametrization of one two-sided translated pair-sieve sum rather than an independent phase resource. WP-410 shows that arbitrary central Bost--Connes groupoid twists can carry phase geometry while the canonical divisibility projection/KMS Gram remains exactly unchanged. A representation may therefore become richer without the readout fiber becoming smaller.

The newer quotient/collision/hitting results make both audits quantitative. AF-548 shows that retaining more finite regular derivatives does not shrink the calibration fibre at all when the observed branch values are separated: the unrestricted smooth calibration action remains transitive on each finite-jet order/sign chamber. MC-511 shows a different failure that occurs before exact source reconstruction: an exact relative-state fibre of size `L` contributes at most a fraction `pL/R_*` of the natural conductor collision scale, so a recurrent state can be real yet destination-negligible when effective participation `R_*` is large. VIS-429 supplies the corresponding null baseline for path persistence: rare superlevel targets force long pre-saturation hitting tails under Haar solely from target mass and the orbit-Lipschitz scale. In each case the apparently favorable object—more jets, repeated states, or a long quiet window—must first be compared with the fibre or baseline induced by the exact destination experiment.

Nyman--Beurling isolates the comparator-baseline trap. NB-296 realizes full multiplicative-band coercivity and the mandatory determinant gain in an ambient Hardy model whose ordinary finite-section enrichment is exactly zero. The gain is real relative to the old **dilated** comparator, but can be spent entirely repairing the dilation penalty. Farey Discrepancy supplies the corresponding tariff-order warning: FD-281 shows that a classical pointwise Perron remainder becomes small enough for the physical repair norm only after the spectral population has already left the regime where the existing cluster-pruning ledger closes. A valid resource must therefore arrive before the other proof tariffs it depends on invalidate the argument.

Weil Inertia gives a final separation between carrier existence and coercive use. WI-409 shows that a classical positive, absolutely summable off-critical charge already exists and is linearly sensitive to shallow horizontal displacement. The missing theorem is not another positive carrier but a source-side identity forcing exact vanishing or otherwise determining the defect measure. Positivity plus summability is a representation property until the source controls the value needed by the destination.

The combined gate is therefore: first compute what the final readout actually identifies modulo gauge and nuisance, including whether added coordinates reduce that fibre at all; second price the **weighted** contribution of any surviving fibre using the physical source participation rather than its raw cardinality; third subtract universal, comparator- or target-rarity-induced baselines; and only then verify that the residual is available at the quantitative scale before auxiliary tariffs consume it. Exact source identification, repeated-class population, unusual persistence, destination coercivity and proof closure are separate achievements and should not be credited to one another without an explicit bridge.

**Boundary.** These examples do not instantiate one universal quotient theorem: curvature dilation, spectral-cutoff tariffs, sieve reparametrization, Hardy dilation repair, stabilizer Gaussian covariance, BSY defect charge and central groupoid-twist blindness are mathematically different mechanisms. The synthesis is an evidence-backed proof discipline about where information can disappear or be mispriced. None of the cited findings supplies an RH consequence by itself.
