# MI-030 — Effective complexity is a geometry-weighted budget

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from exact/local results in Arithmetic Fidelity ([AF-474](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-474-best-s-term-tail-mass-gives-sharp-additive-hilbert-fidelity.md), [AF-479](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-479-prime-gibbs-coordinate-margin-collapses-logarithmically-at-the-prime-zeta-boundary.md), [AF-482](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-482-prime-density-controls-sharp-global-gibbs-margin.md)), Farey Discrepancy (FD-255--FD-257), Nyman--Beurling ([NB-271](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-271-inverse-gram-anchor-criterion-forces-k-scale-operator-growth-for-any-off-diagonal-ford-channel-repair.md)), Visual Exploration ([VIS-365](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-365-finite-weighted-vandermonde-subset-energy-spectrum.md)), Möbius Cancellation, and Prime Lattice. The component theorems remain line-specific; the cross-line claim is an audit principle about which quantitative resource survives the exact representation.

Nominal cardinality, rank or combinatorial richness are repeatedly replaced by a weighted quantity after the exact source-to-destination geometry is imposed. Arithmetic Fidelity prices sparse approximation by support, tail and downstream resolution, while the Gibbs results separate one-crossing sign breadth from logarithmically diverging norm breadth. AF-482 adds the provenance warning in its strongest current form: even the sharp worst-pair anchored half-line conditioning law is forced by the coarse density class `N_Q(x)~x/log x` and canonical coordinate geometry, so a quantitatively exact resource can still be arithmetic-generic.

Farey gives the reachability version. Restricted incidence conditioning is irrelevant unless the extremizer extends through all divisor rows. FD-255 closes macroscopic signed reachability for sublinear omissions; FD-256 shows that linear-density defects can recover full signed capacity by moving into a top divisibility antichain; FD-257 shows that positivity then changes the currency again, forcing a fixed power of relative capacity depletion under bounded switched response. Defect count, signed reachability and nonnegative source slack are different weighted budgets.

Nyman--Beurling gives an anchor-conditioned Gram budget. An order-one center value may cost only `1/K` quadratic mass when its bandwidth is `Theta(K)`. NB-271 shows that a positive quadratic repair has a uniform Euler-anchor floor only if its inverse Gram is controlled on the anchor, and any such repair needs at least `Theta(K)` operator strength relative to the canonical resolved Hardy channel. Off-diagonal count is not the resource; anchor-aligned operator strength is.

Visual Exploration now supplies a global finite branching ledger. For fixed positive weighted Vandermonde families, every `k`-volume is priced by a subset energy combining vertex mass valuations and all pair-separation valuations. The full singular spectrum is the sequence of marginal minimum subset costs. Branching complexity, changing active supports and several incomparable finite power scales therefore collapse to one tropical exterior-volume budget rather than creating a new free direction.

The same audit remains visible in Möbius and Prime Lattice. Möbius factor representations are priced only after the top Boolean interaction and carrier factorization survive, while Prime Lattice prices source replacement by the precision exponent needed to preserve the continued zero-sensitive layer. A resource can be numerous but geometrically cheap, globally sharp but provenance-generic, pointwise large but quadratically narrow, or structurally noncentral yet erased by the positivity map.

The common invariant is **effective complexity after geometry, symmetry, reachability, scale, conditioning, measure class and provenance are imposed**. This is not one universal formula. It is a falsification rule: identify the exact surviving carrier first, derive the weighted resource that the destination actually sees, include compensating and conditioning costs, and only then compare it with the scale required by the final theorem.

**Boundary.** Tail mass, Gibbs norm breadth, omission density, positive capacity, inverse-Gram anchor strength, Vandermonde subset energies, factor entropy and variance exponents are not numerically interchangeable. The reusable content is the order of operations, not a cross-line inequality.
