# MI-030 — Effective complexity is a geometry-weighted budget

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from exact/local results in Arithmetic Fidelity ([AF-474](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-474-best-s-term-tail-mass-gives-sharp-additive-hilbert-fidelity.md), [AF-482](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-482-prime-density-controls-sharp-global-gibbs-margin.md), [AF-483](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-483-prime-density-square-mass-free-multiplicativity-admit-nonprime-control.md)), Farey Discrepancy ([FD-257](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-257-bounded-switched-rows-force-polynomial-nonnegative-capacity-depletion.md), [FD-259](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-259-monotone-divisor-response-forces-sharp-inverse-linear-capacity-depletion.md)), Nyman--Beurling ([NB-271](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-271-inverse-gram-anchor-criterion-forces-k-scale-operator-growth-for-any-off-diagonal-ford-channel-repair.md), [NB-272](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-272-target-cross-terms-cannot-supply-the-missing-k-scale-gram-repair.md)), Möbius Cancellation ([MC-444](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-444-fixed-rank-prime-gap-shape-realization-has-polynomial-source-height.md)), Prime Lattice ([PL-415](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-415-away-from-5-dirichlet-character-variance-supertrace.md)), Visual Exploration ([VIS-366](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-366-fixed-depth-vandermonde-partition-sum-spectrum.md)), and Weil Inertia ([WI-388](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-388-dilute-high-frequency-sidebands-defeat-gamma-to-spectral-tightness.md)). The component theorems remain line-specific; the cross-line claim is an audit principle about which quantitative resource survives the exact representation.

Nominal cardinality, rank, support size, raw mass and combinatorial richness are repeatedly replaced by a weighted quantity after the exact source-to-destination geometry is imposed. Arithmetic Fidelity prices sparse approximation by support, tail and downstream resolution. AF-482 shows that even the sharp global Gibbs conditioning law can be fixed by the coarse density class and one scalar square mass; AF-483 strengthens the provenance warning by matching that scalar and free multiplicativity simultaneously with a nonprime Beurling control. Exact-looking resources are useful only if the destination sees something the matched source class cannot reproduce.

Farey gives the reachability and sign-structure version. FD-257 shows that nonnegative coefficient mass with bounded switched response loses a fixed power of relative dyadic capacity. FD-259 demonstrates that the currency changes again when the Möbius increments `mu*b` are also nonnegative: monotonicity of the divisor response sharpens aggregate relative capacity to the exact inverse-linear scale `O(1/x)`. Positive mass, signed reachability and monotone response are therefore distinct budgets even inside the same divisor architecture.

Nyman--Beurling separates quadratic strength from target mismatch. NB-271 shows that a positive quadratic repair needs `Theta(K)` operator strength relative to the resolved Hardy channel before it can support a rank-uniform Euler-anchor floor. NB-272 then writes the target-aware anchored residual exactly as a section defect plus `|mu_K|^2/(e_K^*R_K^(-1)e_K)`: the target pairing moves the unconstrained projection but does not alter the source Gram. In the resolved regime `R_K asymp I`, a bounded mismatch costs only `O(1/K)`. Target sensitivity, projection defect and source-source Hessian strength are therefore different resources; only the last can satisfy the NB-271 Gram-repair threshold.

Möbius Cancellation prices a different apparent resource: realization complexity. MC-443 makes fixed-rank selected-prime projective gap shapes dense in the ambient simplex, and MC-444 shows that on compact interior shape classes the source height needed for accuracy `eta` is at most polynomial, `n<=C eta^(-40r/19)`. At fixed rank, topological density is therefore not hidden behind a superpolynomial source-cost barrier. Any useful source-height obstruction must change the regime—growing rank, natural-source frequency, scale, or another constraint—not merely rename the cost of the same dense approximants.

Prime Lattice shows that representation dimension can overstate the actual quantitative target. PL-414 gives five additive mod-5 variance sectors for the away-from-five repair; PL-415 diagonalizes the same source into one signed supertrace of three distinct real Dirichlet-character variance sectors. Componentwise control is sufficient but stronger than the exact destination requires, and triangle inequalities can destroy cancellation that the source representation preserves. The effective object is the signed combination, not the number of coordinates used to express it.

Visual Exploration removes support count from fixed-depth conditioning entirely. VIS-366 packages every positive compact coefficient measure, including growing discrete and continuous support with non-power scales, into the fixed `q x q` moment Gram. Its singular products are priced by the Vandermonde partition sums `Z_k`; the finite subset-energy ledger of VIS-365 is only their tropical limit. Support cardinality can diverge while effective conditioning complexity remains fixed-depth and completely accounted for by `Z_1,...,Z_q`.

Weil Inertia supplies the complementary warning that a small raw mass can remain a large destination resource. WI-388 puts only `1/log R` of the profile mass into a carrier at frequency `R`. The archimedean form charges that carrier by `O(log R)`, so the negative gamma margin survives; but a suitable fixed-width packet contains `Omega(log R)` zeta ordinates, leaving `Omega(1)` sampled tail energy. Effective complexity is therefore not monotone in raw mass: a vanishing component can matter when destination sampling multiplicity compensates its geometric cost.

The common invariant is **effective complexity after geometry, symmetry, reachability, scale, conditioning, target projection, measure class, sampling density and provenance are imposed**. This is not one universal formula. It is a falsification rule: identify the exact surviving carrier first, derive the weighted resource that the destination actually sees, include compensating and conditioning costs, and only then compare it with the scale required by the final theorem.

**Boundary.** Gibbs norm breadth, positive-capacity depletion, target mismatch, inverse-Gram anchor strength, signed variance supertraces, prime-gap realization height, Vandermonde partition sums and zeta-frequency tail mass are not numerically interchangeable. The reusable content is the order of operations and the demand that the proposed resource be priced in the destination's own geometry, not a cross-line inequality.
