# MI-030 — Effective complexity is a geometry-weighted budget

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from exact/local results in Arithmetic Fidelity ([AF-474](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-474-best-s-term-tail-mass-gives-sharp-additive-hilbert-fidelity.md), [AF-479](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-479-prime-gibbs-coordinate-margin-collapses-logarithmically-at-the-prime-zeta-boundary.md)), Farey Discrepancy ([FD-251](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-251-ordered-factorization-chain-entropy-bounds-exact-omission-conditioning.md)), Möbius Cancellation (MC-431--MC-432), Visual Exploration ([VIS-358](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-358-dense-cluster-occupancy-width-floor.md), [VIS-364](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-364-nested-vandermonde-chain-cumulative-scale-spectrum.md)), and Prime Lattice (PL-407). The component theorems remain line-specific; the cross-line claim is an audit principle about which quantitative resource survives the exact representation.

Several current frontiers have moved beyond nominal cardinality. Arithmetic Fidelity prices approximate source concentration by the triple `(s,epsilon,delta)`: support complexity costs `sqrt(s)`, unresolved tail mass creates an additive floor `2epsilon`, and finite multiplicative conditioning returns only above the downstream resolution `delta`. AF-479 adds a complementary boundary effect. Along the prime Gibbs curve, every secant remains one-crossing, yet the canonical coordinate margin satisfies `rho(1+epsilon)~sqrt(P(2)/2)/log(1/epsilon)`. Combinatorial sign breadth stays simple while the tangent norm-breadth ratio `||mu'||_1/||mu'||_2` diverges logarithmically. Support/sign complexity and metric conditioning are therefore separate weighted resources.

Farey exact omissions are priced by the weighted incidence inverse `kappa(D)`, not by omission count alone. FD-251 makes even that weighted quantity geometric: strict divisibility-chain entropy injects into ordered factorizations and enforces the universal ceiling `kappa(D)<=N^(rho_*-1+o(1))`. Later reachability results further restrict which such directions are admissible, but the point already visible here is that defect count alone is not the physical budget.

Visual Exploration gives two explicit compensation ledgers. In the regular fixed-depth one-cluster Wang bank, the actual floor is `s_q asymp sqrt(N) epsilon^(q-1)`, equivalently the Gram budget `N epsilon^(2(q-1))`: occupancy and collision width compensate each other. VIS-364 shows that adding finitely many nested positive scales does not evade pricing. For nodes `0,epsilon^(kappa_1),...,epsilon^(kappa_(q-1))`, Newton interpolation gives singular exponents `K_r=sum_(i<=r)kappa_i`; each deeper direction pays every separation already crossed. Raw scale hierarchy is therefore replaced by a cumulative geometric budget.

The same audit remains visible in the older examples. Möbius factor quotients are priced by multinomial prime-mark entropy rather than the nominal number of colors, and Prime Lattice prices source replacement by the coupled exponent pair `(theta,mu)` rather than by calling an estimate a “power saving.” A resource can be numerous but cheap if its reachable footprint is small; it can use few labels but retain large entropy; it can have simple sign geometry while becoming norm-broad near a singular boundary; or it can contain many nested scales whose cost is exactly the product/cumulative valuation imposed by interpolation geometry.

The common invariant is **effective complexity after geometry, symmetry, scale and conditioning are imposed**. This does not identify one universal complexity formula across Mathia. It gives a falsification rule: before crediting cardinality, sparsity, defect count, rank, labels, interpolation points, sign changes, nested scales or named regularity, derive the weighted budget that the exact source-to-destination map actually sees, including compensating resources and any carrier-restricted condition number. Then ask whether that combined budget reaches the scale required by the final theorem.

**Boundary.** The weights are theorem-specific. Tail mass, downstream resolution, Gibbs norm breadth, ordered-factorization entropy, occupancy, Newton separation exponents, multinomial entropy and variance exponents are not interchangeable numerically. The reusable content is only the order of operations: quotient and localize first, price losses and compensations in the physical geometry, then compare the surviving budget with the destination scale.
