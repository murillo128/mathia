# MI-030 — Effective complexity is a geometry-weighted budget

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from exact/local results in Farey Discrepancy (FD-246--FD-247), Möbius Cancellation (MC-431--MC-432), Visual Exploration (VIS-352--VIS-353), Arithmetic Fidelity (AF-468--AF-469), and Prime Lattice (PL-407). The component theorems remain line-specific; the cross-line claim is an audit principle about which quantitative resource survives the exact representation.

Several current frontiers have moved beyond nominal cardinality. Farey exact omissions are priced first by divisor footprint and more sharply by divisibility incidence of active increments, not just their count. Möbius factor quotients are priced by multinomial prime-mark entropy, not the nominal number of colors. Wang phase collisions are priced by explicit Hermite powers of physical collision scales and then by a block Riesz floor, not raw Vandermonde condition numbers. Arithmetic Fidelity prices scalar Hilbert repair by its small-scale amplification law and, under exactness, by complete-alternation constraints rather than by the transform's name. Prime Lattice prices source replacement by the coupled exponent pair `(theta,mu)`, not by calling an estimate “power saving.”

The common invariant is **effective complexity after geometry, symmetry and scale are imposed**. A resource can be numerous but cheap if its reachable footprint is small; it can use few labels but still be expensive if those labels retain large entropy; it can look ill-conditioned in raw coordinates yet become uniformly stable after the correct quotient; or it can pass an abstract category test only by paying a singular small-scale modulus.

This does not identify one universal complexity formula across Mathia. It gives a falsification rule: before crediting cardinality, rank, labels, interpolation points or named regularity, derive the weighted budget that the exact source-to-destination map actually sees. Then ask whether that budget has the scale required by the final theorem.

**Boundary.** The weights are theorem-specific. Harmonic location mass, multinomial entropy, collision diameter and variance exponents are not interchangeable numerically. The reusable content is only the order of operations: quotient and localize first, then price the surviving resource in the geometry consumed downstream.
