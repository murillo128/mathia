# MI-030 — Effective complexity is a geometry-weighted budget

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from exact/local results in Arithmetic Fidelity ([AF-474](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-474-best-s-term-tail-mass-gives-sharp-additive-hilbert-fidelity.md)), Farey Discrepancy ([FD-251](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-251-ordered-factorization-chain-entropy-bounds-exact-omission-conditioning.md)), Möbius Cancellation (MC-431--MC-432), Visual Exploration ([VIS-358](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-358-dense-cluster-occupancy-width-floor.md)), and Prime Lattice (PL-407). The component theorems remain line-specific; the cross-line claim is an audit principle about which quantitative resource survives the exact representation.

Several current frontiers have moved beyond nominal cardinality. Arithmetic Fidelity now prices approximate source concentration by the triple `(s,epsilon,delta)`: support complexity costs `sqrt(s)`, unresolved tail mass creates an additive floor `2epsilon`, and finite multiplicative conditioning returns only above the downstream resolution `delta`. A small tail and a small structural budget are therefore different statements.

Farey exact omissions are priced by the weighted incidence inverse `kappa(D)`, not by omission count alone. FD-251 makes even that weighted quantity geometric: strict divisibility-chain entropy injects into ordered factorizations and enforces the universal ceiling `kappa(D)<=N^(rho_*-1+o(1))`. The effective response budget is consequently the coupled product `K kappa(D)` at the ambient coefficient scale, not either factor in isolation.

Visual Exploration gives a different but equally explicit compensation law. In the regular fixed-depth one-cluster Wang bank, square-anchor quality can collapse with cluster width `epsilon` while full rectangular occupancy contributes positive Gram mass; the actual floor is `s_q asymp sqrt(N) epsilon^(q-1)`, equivalently the Gram budget `N epsilon^(2(q-1))`. Raw scale count and raw collision width have opposite effects and only their geometry-weighted combination controls the quotient.

The same audit remains visible in the older examples. Möbius factor quotients are priced by multinomial prime-mark entropy rather than the nominal number of colors, and Prime Lattice prices source replacement by the coupled exponent pair `(theta,mu)` rather than by calling an estimate a “power saving.” A resource can be numerous but cheap if its reachable footprint is small; it can use few labels but retain large entropy; or it can look ill-conditioned in square coordinates while the full quotient is stable after multiplicity is charged.

The common invariant is **effective complexity after geometry, symmetry and scale are imposed**. This does not identify one universal complexity formula across Mathia. It gives a falsification rule: before crediting cardinality, sparsity, defect count, rank, labels, interpolation points or named regularity, derive the weighted budget that the exact source-to-destination map actually sees and include any compensating resource. Then ask whether that combined budget reaches the scale required by the final theorem.

**Boundary.** The weights are theorem-specific. Tail mass, downstream resolution, ordered-factorization entropy, cluster occupancy, multinomial entropy and variance exponents are not interchangeable numerically. The reusable content is only the order of operations: quotient and localize first, price losses and compensations in the physical geometry, then compare the surviving budget with the destination scale.
