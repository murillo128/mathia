# MI-002 — A compatibility equation can reduce independent phase freedom to one symmetry; target-faithful positivity needs the right relational geometry and arity

**Evidence level:** supported; the recovery models are exact/classical, and transfer to another source requires an explicit identification. The positivity synthesis is grounded in [AF-371](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-371-hankel-positivity-vs-total-positivity-target-fidelity.md), the finite-arity obstruction [AF-372](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-372-every-finite-toeplitz-minor-order-has-polya-frequency-false-positives.md), and the source-Laplace orientation audit [WP-324](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-324-direct-laplace-translation-of-mangoldt-herglotz-source-is-strictly-sign-regular-not-polya-frequency.md).

For a signal on a finite abelian group with every Fourier coefficient nonzero, retain `P_f(chi)=|fhat(chi)|^2` and

\[
B_f(\chi,\psi)=\widehat f(\chi)\widehat f(\psi)
\overline{\widehat f(\chi\psi)}.
\]

If two signals have the same `P` and `B`, their Fourier ratio `r=ghat/fhat` satisfies `|r(chi)|=1` and `r(chi psi)=r(chi)r(psi)`. Thus `r` is one character, i.e. the two signals differ only by translation. Magnitudes alone leave many nontranslation phase assignments. The operative datum is the multiplication compatibility equation, not an injunction to retain more information ([AF-004](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-004-third-order-coupling-repairs-quadratic-phase-loss.md)). Nonvanishing is essential to this argument; it supplies no uniform inverse bound near zero coefficients.

In the prime-ray Gram problem the missing relation is different. For logarithmic valuation differences `Delta_p`, independent Cauchy coordinates give `exp(-sum_p|Delta_p|/2)`, whereas one common Cauchy coordinate gives `exp(-|sum_p Delta_p|/2)`. Both have the same one-coordinate laws. Independence is an all-coordinate factorization identity, not a consequence of those marginal laws or of positivity ([WP-234](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-234-gamma-and-bost-connes-critical-grams-are-common-versus-independent-cauchy-couplings.md)).

AF-371 adds a target-property version of the same lesson. The complete reciprocal Taylor sequence of an entire function may determine the function, yet compressing that sequence to the infinite signature “every Hankel matrix is positive” still does not determine Laguerre--Pólya membership: Hamburger gives false positives. Schoenberg's Pólya-frequency characterization succeeds because it retains a different relation, total positivity of all ordered translation minors `det(Lambda(x_j-y_k))`. The distinction is not finite versus infinite positivity; it is **quadratic moment positivity versus ordered relational positivity with an exact target theorem**.

AF-372 shows that even the correct relational geometry has an independent arity bill. For every fixed order `r`, there are translation profiles in `TN_r\TN_(r+1)`. Exhaustively testing all locations at bounded determinant order therefore cannot replace the all-order Pólya-frequency condition on the unrestricted class. “More samples of the same relation” and “higher relational arity” are different resources.

WP-324 supplies the complementary source-construction warning. The direct Laplace transform of the positive Mangoldt Herglotz measure does not inherit Schoenberg positivity: its ordered translation minors have the universal alternating sign `(-1)^(m(m-1)/2)` for every nondegenerate positive source. Reversing orientation to the Hankel kernel makes all minors positive, but for every positive measure. Thus **having the right-looking ordered determinant family is still insufficient if the source map places it in a universal sign class or the wrong orientation**. The sign theorem itself must be source-selective and compatible with the target theorem's domain.

This sharpens the cross-line audit. When a scalar positivity route survives only by adding more points, derivatives, inequalities or determinant orders, ask separately: is the relation target-faithful, is its arity sufficient, and is its sign nonautomatic in the admissible source category? Quantity alone cannot repair a universal Gram/moment geometry; fixed arity cannot reproduce an all-order certificate; and a positive-measure construction can make ordered signs universal in either the wrong translation orientation or a nonselective Hankel orientation.

The concrete transfer question remains source-specific: which arithmetic identity couples the retained coordinates or minors and makes the stronger relational structure nonautomatic? The bispectral character equation answers that in AF-004, and Schoenberg answers the abstract Laguerre--Pólya target through translation total positivity; neither result says that a given arithmetic positive-source representation realizes the needed kernel. WP-324 proves that the most direct Mangoldt Laplace passage does not.
