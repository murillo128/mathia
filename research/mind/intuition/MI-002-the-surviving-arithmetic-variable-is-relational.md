# MI-002 — The surviving arithmetic variable is usually relational, not absolute

**Evidence level:** supported

## Core intuition

The strongest constructions repeatedly discard a large universal or gauge-like absolute scale and retain a **relative invariant**: a cross-ratio, gap ratio, cuff difference, shell interaction, or modulus. This suggests that the arithmetic content relevant to geometry is encoded less in absolute sizes than in relations that survive the natural symmetry or degeneration.

## Strongest current claim

In the prime-flute asymptotic regime,

`ell_i(P)=2 log(4P/d_i)+o(1)`,

so the common `2 log P` divergence disappears from differences and

`d_i/d_j = lim exp(-(ell_i-ell_j)/2)`.

Those ratios are exactly what enter multi-gap cross-ratios, separating lengths, capacities, systoles, Cheeger constants and weighted-path limits. On the prime-circle side, Möbius/projective normalization similarly kills absolute placement while preserving cross-ratios; cyclotomic inversion preserves a two-sided relation rather than an absolute interior/exterior labeling.

The important distinction is between **gauge removal** and **information destruction**. Removing a common scale can expose the true modulus. Removing the anchor, labels, or relative ordering can instead identify arithmetically different objects.

## Evidence and synthesis

PF-029/PF-047/PF-074/PF-076 show the same adjacent or cumulative gap ratios reappearing as hyperbolic invariants. PC-013/PC-014 show that absolute one-dimensional transport data are often endpoint/subdivision gauge. PC-019 shows that quotienting by too much symmetry destroys arithmetic distinction entirely.

## Boundary cases

Not every ratio is meaningful. A marked sojourn difference can merely be the standard shear coordinate, and a ratio inserted into an arbitrary generating function adds no mathematics. The relation must arise as an invariant of a canonical geometric construction.

## Status / novelty

Exact for the displayed asymptotic conversions and cross-ratio identities; heuristic as a program-wide principle.

## Falsification criterion

Find a robust prime-specific spectral mechanism in this program whose decisive variable is an absolute local scale and cannot be rewritten as a relative invariant after quotienting universal geometry.

## Most informative next move

Search for higher-dimensional families of canonical relative invariants whose joint geometry has curvature or nontrivial spectral response, rather than adding more scalar encodings of individual gaps.

## Lean-formalizable core

- Conversion from cuff differences to gap ratios under the established asymptotics.
- Cross-ratio invariance under Möbius normalization.
- Exact four-punctured formulas linking ratio, systole and Cheeger constant.
