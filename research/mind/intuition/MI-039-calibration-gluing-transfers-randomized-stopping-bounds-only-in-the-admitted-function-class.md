# MI-039 — Calibration gluing transfers randomized stopping bounds only in the admitted function class

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of [AF-575](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-575-unknown-calibration-slack-requires-unbounded-sampling-depth.md), [AF-576](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-576-randomized-calibration-recovery-has-a-sharp-error-stopping-tail.md), [AF-577](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-577-unknown-polynomial-calibration-separates-continuous-randomness-from-finite-certification.md), and [XF-203](../../xi_flow/findings/XF-203-unknown-jacobi-calibration-slack-admits-finite-certified-stopping.md)–[XF-204](../../xi_flow/findings/XF-204-randomized-jacobi-detection-has-a-sharp-error-stopping-tail.md), with the analytic noisy boundary in [XF-205](../../xi_flow/findings/XF-205-analytic-jacobi-calibration-retains-the-noisy-stopping-obstruction.md). The transfer identifies testing mechanisms, not the two sources or their noise geometries; it is not a new general recovery theorem.

Subtract a known reference source and write the binary observations as \(b\) or \(g+b\), with the nuisance seminorm strictly below \(d/2\) but no supplied slack. In AF-576, the seminorm is \(\|b^{(n)}\|_\infty\), the gap has \(g^{(n)}(0)=d>0\) and positive strictly decreasing \(g^{(n)}\), and only positive coordinates are queryable. In XF-204, it is \(\|B'\|_\infty\) on complex-valued functions, with \(g=F_{\tau_Q}\), \(d=D\), and the maximizing endpoint \(Q\) queryable. The AF slack \(d-2\|b^{(n)}\|_\infty\) equals twice the Xi convention \(D/2-\|B'\|_\infty\) after this dictionary; constants involving slack require that factor.

The reusable premise is a family of **fixed admissible pairs** \((J_u/2,g-J_u/2)\) agreeing with \(g/2\) outside a shrinking query region. AF obtains \(J_u\) from the order-\(n\) Taylor extension below \(u\). Xi requires the convex-velocity join of [local MI-014](../../xi_flow/mind/intuition/MI-014-endpoint-preserving-calibration-joins-force-sharp-randomized-stopping-tails.md), which preserves \(g(Q)\) as well as the tail values and matching derivative. Translation \(a=r-Q\) identifies the hidden regions \((0,u)\) and \((Q,Q+u)\); it does not make the Taylor and complex joins interchangeable.

For any \(N\)-query policy, let \(q_N(u)\) be the probability that its hypothetical midpoint transcript enters that hidden region. Every seed has only finitely many query positions, so \(q_N(u)\to0\). Shared-seed coupling gives
\[
\frac{\operatorname{err}(x_{0,u})+\operatorname{err}(x_{1,u})}{2}
\ge\frac{1-q_N(u)}2.
\]
Choose \(u\) after the policy and cap but **before** its seed. This yields capped minimax error \(1/2\), hence the sharp uniform stopping-tail obstruction \(1-2\alpha\) for per-input error \(\alpha<1/2\), using the pointwise finite exact procedures for attainability. The hard input can depend on \(N\); pointwise finite cost is consistent with infinite worst-case expected cost. Fixing a seed and then interpolating an adversary would not establish this randomized theorem.

This gluing proof stops at the nuisance function class. A nontrivial analytic gap cannot be joined analytically to a strictly subcritical replacement while preserving equality on a tail interval. AF-577 gives a concrete different outcome, retained in [local MI-079](../../arithmetic_fidelity/mind/intuition/MI-079-unknown-polynomial-calibration-separates-random-identification-from-certification.md): for \(m\ge2\) known analytic sources with nonpolynomial pairwise gaps and an unknown finite-degree polynomial nuisance, every finite deterministic transcript has an opposite-label extension, yet one independent continuously random exact validation coordinate almost surely rejects all false interpolants at every stage. Its false residuals have null zero sets, whereas the smooth hard pairs above agree on a whole interval.

In that same polynomial class, finite-bit randomness has a stronger obstruction than any fixed query cap. A **finite collection** of returning coin words has a finite union of queried coordinates; one polynomial per label matches every transcript simultaneously. If the collection has mass \(q\), some matched label has error at least \((1-1/m)q\). Exhaustion gives \(e\ge(1-1/m)\Pr_y(\text{return})\) on every input, where \(e\) is the worst-case unconditional probability of a wrong label return. No expected-cost or uniform resource bound is assumed. Thus almost-sure return has minimax error \(1-1/m\), even with input-dependent unbounded stopping. This does not transfer to the smooth derivative-bounded class, where arbitrary simultaneous interpolation need not preserve the derivative constraint and exact pointwise finite stopping exists. AF-577's capped minimax law for ideal continuous-real randomness remains unestablished.

Analyticity also does not remove the **noisy** Xi obstruction. [Local MI-015](../../xi_flow/mind/intuition/MI-015-positive-noise-retains-analytic-calibration-stopping-obstruction.md) retains XF-205's holomorphic scalar baselines \(B_{0,\varepsilon}= (1-\varepsilon)F/2\), \(B_{1,\varepsilon}=-(1-\varepsilon)F/2\), whose two observations differ by \(\varepsilon F\). For each fixed cap \(N\) and positive total-noise allowance \(\sigma\), bounded-energy priors on fixed noise sequences give transcript total variation at most \(\min\{1,N^{3/2}\varepsilon\|F\|_\infty/\sigma\}\). Sending \(\varepsilon\) to zero recovers capped minimax error \(1/2\) and the same sharp tail \(1-2\alpha\), without any tail gluing. This direction is one-query identifiable when supplied and noiseless, so the argument does not settle the noiseless full analytic class.

There is an exact observation-space correspondence, but a different noise consequence. AF-577's polynomial density brings the two classes \(F_i+\Pi\) and \(F_j+\Pi\) arbitrarily close in uniform norm; opposite supremum-bounded errors then alias the **whole curves**. Xi's scalar baselines likewise bring opposite-label curves arbitrarily close in uniform norm, but its total squared-noise constraint is imposed on one sequence across all rows. XF-205 converts that proximity into near-indistinguishable **finite transcript laws**, not a full infinite-observation alias. Distinct-row contrasts have coefficient norm \(\sqrt{2/n}/h\), so XF-203 can still stop exactly on each fixed positive-slack input. Neither intersection of uniform closures nor failure of analytic gluing alone determines recovery: the noise norm, allowed randomness and stopping quantifiers distinguish these outcomes. No physical Xi access, ordinary-prime provenance, or RH implication follows.
