# MI-039 — Calibration gluing transfers randomized stopping bounds only in the admitted function class

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of [AF-575](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-575-unknown-calibration-slack-requires-unbounded-sampling-depth.md), [AF-576](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-576-randomized-calibration-recovery-has-a-sharp-error-stopping-tail.md), [AF-577](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-577-unknown-polynomial-calibration-separates-continuous-randomness-from-finite-certification.md), and [XF-203](../../xi_flow/findings/XF-203-unknown-jacobi-calibration-slack-admits-finite-certified-stopping.md)–[XF-204](../../xi_flow/findings/XF-204-randomized-jacobi-detection-has-a-sharp-error-stopping-tail.md). The transfer identifies an exact testing mechanism, not the two sources or their noise geometries.

Subtract a known reference source and write the binary observations as \(b\) or \(g+b\), with the nuisance seminorm strictly below \(d/2\) but no supplied slack. In AF-576, the seminorm is \(\|b^{(n)}\|_\infty\), the gap has \(g^{(n)}(0)=d>0\) and positive strictly decreasing \(g^{(n)}\), and only positive coordinates are queryable. In XF-204, it is \(\|B'\|_\infty\) on complex-valued functions, with \(g=F_{\tau_Q}\), \(d=D\), and the maximizing endpoint \(Q\) queryable. The AF slack \(d-2\|b^{(n)}\|_\infty\) equals twice the Xi convention \(D/2-\|B'\|_\infty\) after this dictionary; constants involving slack require that factor.

The reusable premise is a family of **fixed admissible pairs** \((J_u/2,g-J_u/2)\) agreeing with \(g/2\) outside a shrinking query region. AF obtains \(J_u\) from the order-\(n\) Taylor extension below \(u\). Xi requires the convex-velocity join of [local MI-014](../../xi_flow/mind/intuition/MI-014-endpoint-preserving-calibration-joins-force-sharp-randomized-stopping-tails.md), which preserves \(g(Q)\) as well as the tail values and matching derivative. Translation \(a=r-Q\) identifies the hidden regions \((0,u)\) and \((Q,Q+u)\); it does not make the Taylor and complex joins interchangeable.

For any \(N\)-query policy, let \(q_N(u)\) be the probability that its hypothetical midpoint transcript enters that hidden region. Every seed has only finitely many query positions, so \(q_N(u)\to0\). Shared-seed coupling gives
\[
\frac{\operatorname{err}(x_{0,u})+\operatorname{err}(x_{1,u})}{2}
\ge\frac{1-q_N(u)}2.
\]
Choose \(u\) after the policy and cap but **before** its seed. This yields capped minimax error \(1/2\), hence the sharp uniform stopping-tail obstruction \(1-2\alpha\) for per-input error \(\alpha<1/2\), using the pointwise finite exact procedures for attainability. The hard input can depend on \(N\); pointwise finite cost is consistent with infinite worst-case expected cost. Fixing a seed and then interpolating an adversary would not establish this randomized theorem.

The transfer stops at the nuisance function class. A nontrivial analytic gap cannot be joined analytically to a strictly subcritical replacement while preserving equality on a tail interval. AF-577 gives a concrete different outcome, retained in [local MI-079](../../arithmetic_fidelity/mind/intuition/MI-079-unknown-polynomial-calibration-separates-random-identification-from-certification.md): for analytic sources with nonpolynomial pairwise gaps and an unknown finite-degree polynomial nuisance, every finite deterministic transcript has an opposite-label extension, yet one independent continuously random exact validation coordinate almost surely rejects all false interpolants at every stage. Its false residuals have null zero sets, whereas the smooth hard pairs above agree on a whole interval. Finite-bit zero-error termination still fails in the polynomial experiment because every terminating finite random word has positive mass.

Thus finite-transcript ambiguity does not alone determine probabilistic recovery. The source-backed distinction is whether the admitted nuisance supplies fixed opposite-label inputs agreeing on an increasingly hard-to-hit region's complement, or instead forces every fixed false residual into a null coincidence set independent of the validation coordinate. This is not a classification of all analytic or quasianalytic nuisance classes, and AF-577 gives no sharp capped minimax law for its polynomial class.

Noise does not transfer through this dictionary either. AF-575 has zero uniform full-curve margin for any positive supremum-norm noise. Xi's known total squared-noise budget permits the contrast coefficient norm \(\sqrt{2/n}/h\) to tend to zero through distinct-row replication, so XF-203 retains exact per-input stopping. Neither source identification result establishes physical Xi access, ordinary-prime provenance, or an RH implication.
