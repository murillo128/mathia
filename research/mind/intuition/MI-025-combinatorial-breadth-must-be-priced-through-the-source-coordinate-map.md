# MI-025 — Source breadth must be measured in the theorem's physical coordinate at the scale being probed

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of [AF-399](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-399-regular-variation-transfers-fixed-physical-walls-to-source-dependent-index-depth.md), [AF-400](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-400-regular-variation-does-not-control-shrinking-locality-walls.md), and [VIS-271](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-271-wang-fixed-alpha-prime-scale-mismatch.md). No numerical scale or detector estimate is transferred between the two objects.

Counts of indices, frequencies or samples are not intrinsic measures of how much source geometry an architecture reaches. The invariant question is first **which physical/source coordinate interval is traversed**, then how that interval is represented by the discrete source, and finally whether the theorem controlling that representation is uniform at the same scale.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the scale qualification explicit. For an increasing regularly varying source scale `a_N`, a fixed logarithmic reach `h` has successor depth asymptotic to `(exp(h/rho)-1)N`, with lower-order corrections inherited from the source-density law. But AF-400 shows that ordinary regular variation cannot be extrapolated blindly to a shrinking wall `h_N->0`: even smooth monotone regularly varying scales can give different subsequential values of `B_N(h_N)/(N h_N)`. A matching local law such as `x f'(x)/f(x)->rho` restores `B_N(h_N)~N h_N/rho`. **The source-coordinate theorem must resolve the same scale as the destination wall.**

Visual Exploration supplies the complementary coordinate-identity warning. In Rodgers' pair-correlation coefficient a literal prime `p` sits at fixed frequency `log p/(2pi)`. In Wang's short-interval formula a fixed positive Fourier parameter `alpha` corresponds instead to a moving prime scale `T^alpha`; the same literal fixed prime moves toward `alpha=0` like `log p/log T`. A fixed band in two formulas that use superficially similar Fourier coordinates can therefore probe entirely different arithmetic carriers.

Across these mechanisms the reusable rule is: **define source reach before counting source coordinates, preserve the source-coordinate map across theorem transfer, and demand asymptotic control at the actual moving scale.** A discrete breadth can be linear in index while the physical wall is fixed, or apparently fixed in a plotting variable while the arithmetic carrier moves with the ambient parameter.

**Boundary.** AF-399--AF-400 concern regularly varying index scales and their local refinement; VIS-271 concerns the arithmetic meaning of theorem Fourier coordinates. Their formulas are not interchangeable, and the principle supplies neither a new shrinking `PF_3` detector wall, arithmetic cancellation, nor a zero-sensitive theorem. Its role is to prevent combinatorial breadth or coordinate labels from being credited as source access before the physical map and scale are verified.
