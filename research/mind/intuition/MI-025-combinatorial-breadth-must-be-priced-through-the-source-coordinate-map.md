# MI-025 — Source breadth must be measured in the theorem's physical coordinate at the scale being probed

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of [AF-399](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-399-regular-variation-transfers-fixed-physical-walls-to-source-dependent-index-depth.md) through [AF-402](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-402-exceptional-set-inertia-transfers-short-interval-pnt-to-density-one-prime-starts.md), [VIS-271](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-271-wang-fixed-alpha-prime-scale-mismatch.md), [VIS-272](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-272-wang-source-green-kernel-recovers-rodgers-prime-measure.md), [VIS-274](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-274-wang-fixed-q-rescaled-laplace-limit.md), and [WI-343](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-343-natural-gallagher-window-has-bounded-height-carrier-dimension.md). No numerical scale or detector estimate is transferred between these objects.

Counts of indices, frequencies, spectral heights or samples are not intrinsic measures of how much source geometry an architecture reaches. The invariant question is first **which physical/source coordinate interval is traversed**, then how that interval is represented by the discrete source, and finally whether the theorem controls that representation at the same moving scale and sampling regime.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes both scale and quantifier explicit. For an increasing regularly varying source scale `a_N`, fixed logarithmic reach has a deterministic successor-depth conversion, but AF-400 shows that ordinary regular variation cannot be extrapolated to arbitrary shrinking walls. For primes, AF-401 uses uniform short-interval density to give all-start control for `h_N=p_N^(-beta)` throughout `beta<13/30`. AF-402 reaches every fixed `beta<13/15` on a relative-density-one set of prime starts by adding an exceptional-set inertia plus Brun--Titchmarsh packing argument. The two exponents price present theorem technology and sampling mode, not intrinsic geometry.

Visual Exploration supplies a complementary warning about **how theorem error is represented at the moving scale**. VIS-271 identifies that fixed positive Wang `alpha` corresponds to a moving prime scale while a literal fixed prime approaches `alpha=0`. VIS-272 repairs the source-coordinate mismatch exactly: in `q=log x/(2pi)`, Wang's diagonal source field is the exponential Green potential of the Rodgers weighted prime-power measure.

VIS-274 then corrects the apparent derivative-budget obstruction at the pulled-back fixed-`q` scale. A fixed physical packet becomes a Wang test of width `Theta(1/log T)` with derivative norm `Theta(log T)`, but the Wang exponential main term concentrates on that same scale. If that structured main term is retained and rescaled with the test, the normalized statistic has a finite Laplace limit and only `O_h(1/log T)` remainder. The earlier `O(1)` barrier arose from first replacing the concentrated main term by a fixed-test Taylor approximation and only then charging the moving-test seminorm.

Weil Inertia adds a different source-coordinate conversion: **spectral multiplicity need not become source-space dimension**. On one natural Gallagher window the selected bow has height span `H` while the source log-aperture is exactly `delta=1/H`. WI-343 proves directly that this unit time--bandwidth product forces pairwise carrier coherence at least `cos(1/2)` and places the entire linear height family within factorially small error of a fixed finite-dimensional span. A power-growing number of ordinates is therefore not a power-growing number of independent probes unless the construction also changes the source window, aperture, nonlinear coupling or arithmetic state.

The reusable rule is therefore stronger than “check the seminorm” or “count the samples.” **Map the physical source window into theorem coordinates, preserve any structured main term on the induced scale, compute the effective dimension or resolution actually available there, and only then measure the residual error and sampling uniformity.** Exact coordinate equivalence does not imply theorem transfer, a large derivative norm does not imply failure when it resolves the theorem's own boundary layer, and a large observation count does not imply large information dimension when all samples occupy one bounded time--bandwidth cell.

**Boundary.** AF-399--AF-402 concern source-density conversion on regular-varying/prime meshes; VIS-271--VIS-274 concern a coefficient-level coordinate dictionary and a specific rescaled Wang main term; WI-343 concerns linear phase carriers on one Gallagher window. Their formulas and exponents are not interchangeable. The `13/30` and `13/15` exponents are not detector thresholds, the Wang--Rodgers Green identity plus fixed-`q` Laplace limit is not a shrinking-support pair-correlation theorem, and the WI-343 bounded carrier dimension says nothing by itself about nonlinear/cross-window arithmetic decorrelation. The principle is an audit of physical scale, effective source breadth, sampling quantifier and proof stability, not a new zero-sensitive result.
