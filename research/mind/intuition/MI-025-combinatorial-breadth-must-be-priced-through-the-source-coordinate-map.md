# MI-025 — Source breadth must be measured in the theorem's physical coordinate at the scale being probed

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of [AF-399](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-399-regular-variation-transfers-fixed-physical-walls-to-source-dependent-index-depth.md) through [AF-401](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-401-prime-short-interval-fidelity-pushes-shrinking-locality-to-17-30-successor-depth.md), and [VIS-271](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-271-wang-fixed-alpha-prime-scale-mismatch.md) through [VIS-272](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-272-wang-source-green-kernel-recovers-rodgers-prime-measure.md). No numerical scale or detector estimate is transferred between the two objects.

Counts of indices, frequencies or samples are not intrinsic measures of how much source geometry an architecture reaches. The invariant question is first **which physical/source coordinate interval is traversed**, then how that interval is represented by the discrete source, and finally whether the theorem controlling that representation is uniform at the same scale.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the scale qualification explicit. For an increasing regularly varying source scale `a_N`, a fixed logarithmic reach `h` has successor depth asymptotic to `(exp(h/rho)-1)N`, with lower-order corrections inherited from the source-density law. AF-400 shows that ordinary regular variation cannot be extrapolated blindly to a shrinking wall `h_N->0`: even smooth monotone regularly varying scales can give different subsequential values of `B_N(h_N)/(N h_N)`. A matching local law such as `x f'(x)/f(x)->rho` restores `B_N(h_N)~N h_N/rho`.

AF-401 gives a stronger prime-specific moving-scale theorem. Uniform short-interval prime density yields `B_N(h_N)~N h_N` for every shrinking wall with `h_N>=p_N^(-13/30+epsilon)` eventually. Thus the current unconditional uniform source theorem prices a polynomial wall all the way down to the `p_N^(-13/30+epsilon)` regime, with successor breadth `N^(17/30+epsilon+o(1))` at the boundary. Those exponents are theorem-technology boundaries, not intrinsic geometry; a destination requiring a thinner wall needs a stronger source-density theorem, a justified exceptional-set transfer, or a different representation.

Visual Exploration supplies the complementary coordinate-identity warning and then its exact repair. VIS-271 shows that in Rodgers' pair-correlation coefficient a literal prime `p` sits at fixed frequency `log p/(2pi)`, whereas fixed positive Wang `alpha` corresponds to a moving prime scale `T^alpha`; the same literal fixed prime moves toward `alpha=0` like `log p/log T`. VIS-272 shows that this is not an intrinsic mismatch of arithmetic carriers. In the logarithmic source coordinate `q=log x/(2pi)`, Wang's diagonal source field is exactly the exponential Green potential of the Rodgers weighted prime-power measure, and Green inversion recovers that measure distributionally.

The remaining obstruction is therefore a theorem-scale one rather than a missing representation map. A fixed Rodgers `q`-window pulls back to a Wang `alpha`-window of width `O(1/log T)` centered at `log p/log T`. The exact coefficient dictionary does not imply that Wang's short-interval asymptotic is uniform on that moving family, nor does it authorize differentiating its error term. **An exact source-coordinate map and a theorem uniform at the pulled-back scale are separate resources.**

Across these mechanisms the reusable rule is: define source reach before counting source coordinates, preserve the source-coordinate map across theorem transfer, and demand asymptotic control at the actual moving scale. A discrete breadth can be linear in index while the physical wall is fixed, polynomially sublinear because a source theorem resolves a shrinking wall, or apparently fixed in a plotting variable while the arithmetic carrier moves with the ambient parameter. Even after an exact change of coordinates is found, the transfer remains invalid until the theorem controls the image of the intended source window.

**Boundary.** AF-399--AF-401 concern regularly varying/prime index scales and their local refinement; VIS-271--VIS-272 concern the arithmetic meaning and exact coefficient-level relation of two theorem coordinates. Their formulas and exponents are not interchangeable. The `17/30` successor exponent is not a detector threshold, and the Wang--Rodgers Green identity is not a shrinking-support pair-correlation theorem. The principle supplies neither a new `PF_3` detector wall, arithmetic cancellation, nor a zero-sensitive theorem; it prevents combinatorial breadth, coordinate labels, or exact representation equivalence from being credited as source access before the physical map and same-scale theorem are verified.
