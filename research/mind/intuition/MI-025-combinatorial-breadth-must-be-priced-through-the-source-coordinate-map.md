# MI-025 — Source breadth must be measured after pullback to the physical source coordinates and admissible source set

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current exact results including [AF-425](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-425-second-order-square-packet-detuning-exposes-source-lattice.md)--[AF-426](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-426-exact-square-amplitude-has-irreducible-integer-source-packet-gap.md), VIS-294--VIS-306, FD-200--FD-205, MC-383--MC-386, NB-216, and WI-343--WI-352. No numerical scale, conductor exponent or detector estimate is transferred between these objects.

Counts of indices, frequencies, spectral heights, samples, character families or nonlinear features are not intrinsic measures of how much source geometry an architecture reaches. The invariant question is first **which physical/source coordinate interval is traversed and which points of that interval are actually admissible**, then which algebraically independent coordinates survive exact relations, what analytic cost is charged to them, and at what destination resolution the theorem controls the result.

Arithmetic Fidelity now makes the admissible-set pullback exact on one critical fiber. AF-425 shows that continuous saddle tuning becomes an integer shrinking-target problem in `d_n=s_n-2n`. AF-426 proves that on exact square amplitude `a=m^2` this is not merely a sparse-hitting issue: bounded packet ratio forces `d_n=-1` eventually, where the ratio tends to `mK_m>1`, so the required cancellation target has no solutions. Formal continuous breadth can therefore collapse to an empty admissible source fiber at the destination precision. The remaining tuning problem is off-square, where the integer depth moves on the `n/log n` scale.

Visual Exploration gives a theorem-specialization and matched-control audit. Exact gamma structure, source-specific coefficients, support parity and prime-power frequency spacing successively move the proof wall. VIS-305 shows that the remaining `x log log x` absolute spacing norm is reproduced by parity/density Bernoulli support; VIS-306 then shows that the corresponding signed control cancels to `o_p(H)` at that scale. Nominal sparse-frequency breadth is not arithmetic information unless the actual signed channel beats the matched source model.

Farey Discrepancy shows that observation breadth can be illusory or productive. FD-200 proves that matched dilation families can be exact divisor transport. FD-204 adds genuinely useful nonlocal relations with explicit Möbius-slab preimages and controlled source overlap. FD-205 then shows that global coherence does not require one fixed physical mode: changing prime and horizon still recovers arbitrary `M(N)` when the exact quotient coordinate is preserved and its increments telescope. The source breadth that matters is breadth in the invariant recovery coordinate.

Möbius Cancellation gives the exceptional-set counterpart. MC-383--MC-385 show that family cardinality, theorem admission and ambient exceptional volume can all misprice the actual source frame. MC-386 identifies the sharp generic energy scale after pullback: the endpoint can tolerate good-mode correlation only at the natural `R^(-1/2)` scale and about `H/R` exceptional difference modes, with annihilator frames showing that the latter cannot be improved by abstract rank/PSD arguments alone.

Nyman--Beurling gives another case where widening enlarges physical support but must be paid through its reconstruction currency. Weil Inertia shows why observation count, nonlinear feature count and formal charge complexity can overstate independent source breadth when destination resolution or existing Lipschitz budgets already control them.

The reusable rule is therefore stronger than “count samples” or “specialize the seminorm.” **Map the physical source window into theorem coordinates, intersect it with the admissible source set, quotient exact algebraic redundancy, identify the coordinate that stays coherent under allowed transport, specialize the theorem to the actual source family, pull exceptional sets back to that family, price the result in the destination norm, and run a matched control on the signed channel actually consumed.** A continuous critical parameter may be unreachable; a huge family may add no source breadth; and changing physical sensors can still be coherent if the exact internal coordinate is preserved.

**Boundary.** The mechanisms remain line-specific: integer derivative-depth spacing, Wang frequency support, Farey quotient transport and graph overlap, Möbius conductor/frame geometry, Mellin-shell reconstruction and bow feature geometry are not quantitatively interchangeable. The synthesis is an audit of physical scale, admissible source geometry, algebraic independence, theorem specialization and destination resolution, not a new zero-sensitive theorem.
