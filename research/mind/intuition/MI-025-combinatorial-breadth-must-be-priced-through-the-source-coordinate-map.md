# MI-025 — Source breadth must be measured in the theorem's physical coordinate at the scale and resolution being probed

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current exact results including AF-399--AF-402, VIS-294--VIS-302, FD-200, MC-383, NB-216, and WI-343--WI-352. No numerical scale, conductor exponent or detector estimate is transferred between these objects.

Counts of indices, frequencies, spectral heights, samples, character families or nonlinear features are not intrinsic measures of how much source geometry an architecture reaches. The invariant question is first **which physical/source coordinate interval is traversed**, then which algebraically independent coordinates survive exact relations, which complexity class maps that interval into observations, what analytic cost is charged to that family, and finally at what destination resolution the theorem controls the resulting object.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the source-grid conversion explicit. For primes, current short-interval technology gives all-start control for shrinking physical walls `h_N=p_N^(-beta)` with `beta<13/30` and density-one control for every fixed `beta<13/15`. Those exponents belong to the source theorem and sampling quantifier; they are not detector constants. The later critical-window results add a second warning: once two large-complexity saddles collide, the coarse physical coordinate can cease to be sufficient on the collision fiber and a finer detuning coordinate becomes load-bearing.

Visual Exploration adds a resolution-sensitive moving-source example and a theorem-specialization audit. VIS-294--VIS-298 show that apparent lower-source boundaries depend on whether deterministic gamma structure is retained or subtracted. VIS-299--VIS-301 then move the pointwise upper wall away from localization. VIS-302 goes one step further: the generic Montgomery--Vaughan coefficient cost `O(x log^2 x)` overprices Wang's exact coefficients by one logarithm, because

`sum_n n a_n^2=(4/3)x log x+(8/9)x+o(x)`.

The physical upper source window therefore extends to `x log x=o(H)`. The theorem's nominal worst-case complexity is not the source cost until the exact coefficient class has been inserted.

Farey Discrepancy shows that observation breadth can be illusory even when both horizon and Fourier mode grow. FD-200 proves a universal divisor cocycle for matched dilations, and divisor-closed coprime matched families are Dirichlet-invertible to fixed-mode horizon coordinates. More measurements are not more source breadth when exact algebra makes them copies.

Möbius Cancellation gives the complementary case where a family is genuinely large but its analytic pricing still lives in the wrong coordinate. MC-383 stress-tests a collective character-family mean-value shape with family size `A<=exp(CR)`. Even granting a stronger composite-modulus version than the cited theorem and deleting subpower losses, a fixed positive power of the full source modulus converts the family gain only into `log q=O(log y+R)`. A rank-linear source exponent would require `Omega(R log y)`. Exponential family cardinality therefore does not buy multiplicative source breadth unless the conductor/modulus dependence itself scales compatibly with rank.

Nyman--Beurling gives another case where widening really enlarges the physical support, but the enlargement must be paid. NB-216 shows that a bounded shell signal after one Mellin primitive can reduce its global reconstruction tariff only by growing the logarithmic support width; after two or more primitives, even arbitrary upward widening leaves an irreducible polynomial tariff. Nominally richer support is useful only after its physical width and functional normalization are included in the cost model.

Weil Inertia shows why observation count, nonlinear feature count and formal charge complexity can all overstate independent source breadth. WI-343--WI-349 price the natural fixed-aperture carrier and its nonlinear feature images. WI-350 makes the phase-sensitive escape quantitative through the phase-cyclic dimension `D`; WI-351 replaces hard support by the charge-tail moment `B_s`. WI-352 then shows that at first angular order this apparent new currency is not independent: `B_1<=L` follows automatically from the same Lipschitz equivariance used to price source sensitivity, and no higher `B_(1+epsilon)` follows without additional regularity. A complexity coordinate should be counted separately only after its dependence on existing budgets is audited.

The reusable rule is therefore stronger than “check the seminorm” or “count the samples.” **Map the physical source window into theorem coordinates, quotient exact algebraic redundancy, specialize the theorem to the actual source class, express family gain and conductor loss in the same coordinate, declare the destination resolution, and audit whether each apparent complexity budget is genuinely independent.** A large feature bank need not provide new information when all outputs remain in one fixed-aperture image; a large measurement family need not provide new constraints when exact transport identifies its rows; and an exponential family need not buy a rank-linear source exponent when the global-modulus tariff consumes the gain.

**Boundary.** The underlying scales and mechanisms remain line-specific: prime-mesh density conversion, Wang's source/error budget, Farey divisor transport, Möbius family/conductor pricing, Mellin-shell reconstruction and bow feature geometry are not quantitatively interchangeable. The principle is an audit of physical scale, algebraic independence, theorem specialization, analytic pricing, sampling quantifier, requested resolution and dependency among resource budgets, not a new zero-sensitive theorem.
