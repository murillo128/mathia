# MI-025 — Source breadth must be measured in the theorem's physical coordinate at the scale and resolution being probed

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [AF-399](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-399-regular-variation-transfers-fixed-physical-walls-to-source-dependent-index-depth.md) through [AF-402](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-402-exceptional-set-inertia-transfers-short-interval-pnt-to-density-one-prime-starts.md), [VIS-294](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-294-wang-lower-moving-source-sqrtlog-crossover.md), [VIS-295](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-295-wang-gamma-subtracted-residual-relative-crossover.md), [FD-200](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-200-matched-horizon-mode-dilations-are-a-universal-divisor-cocycle.md), [NB-216](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-216-bounded-shell-signals-price-zero-mean-and-growing-width-escapes.md), [WI-343](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-343-natural-gallagher-window-has-bounded-height-carrier-dimension.md), [WI-345](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-345-fixed-degree-polynomial-carriers-remain-compressible.md), [WI-346](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-346-uniformly-analytic-bow-features-remain-compressible.md), and [WI-350](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-350-bounded-phase-orbit-dimension-restores-linear-bow-resolution-cost.md). No numerical scale or detector estimate is transferred between these objects.

Counts of indices, frequencies, spectral heights, samples or nonlinear features are not intrinsic measures of how much source geometry an architecture reaches. The invariant question is first **which physical/source coordinate interval is traversed**, then which algebraically independent coordinates survive exact relations, then which complexity class maps that interval into observations, and finally at what destination resolution the theorem controls the resulting family.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the source-grid conversion explicit. For primes, current short-interval technology gives all-start control for shrinking physical walls `h_N=p_N^(-beta)` with `beta<13/30` and density-one control for every fixed `beta<13/15`. Those exponents belong to the source theorem and sampling quantifier; they are not detector constants.

Visual Exploration adds a resolution-sensitive moving-source example. VIS-294 shows that Wang's statistic is already controlled to absolute `H` accuracy throughout `x>>sqrt(log T)` once the explicit gamma term is retained. VIS-295 asks for a different output resolution: after gamma subtraction, relative control of the `H log x` profile persists whenever `x^2 log x>>log T`, reaching below `sqrt(log T)`. The theorem-coordinate boundary therefore depends on the scale of the residual being requested. Merely saying `beta(T)->0` or naming one crossover without its normalization loses the relevant information.

Farey Discrepancy shows that observation breadth can be illusory even when both horizon and Fourier mode grow. FD-200 proves a universal divisor cocycle for matched dilations: `B_(pH)(pk)` is an explicit lower-triangular combination of `B_(pH)(k)`, `B_H(k)` and the lower mode `B_H(k/p)`, and divisor-closed coprime matched families are Dirichlet-invertible to fixed-mode horizon coordinates. The prime ridge `(pH,p)` simply repeats the same source value `A(H)`. More measurements are therefore not more source breadth when exact algebra makes them copies.

Nyman--Beurling gives the complementary case where widening really does enlarge the physical support, but the enlargement must be paid. NB-216 shows that a bounded shell signal after one Mellin primitive can reduce its global reconstruction tariff only by growing the logarithmic support width `Delta`; subpolynomial cost requires almost carrier-scale width. After two or more primitives, even arbitrary upward widening leaves an irreducible polynomial tariff. A nominally richer source profile is useful only after its support width and functional normalization are included in the cost model.

Weil Inertia shows why observation count and nonlinear feature count can both overstate source breadth. WI-343--WI-344 identify the unit time--bandwidth product `H Delta=1` on the natural Gallagher window and prove bounded effective linear height dimension. WI-345 shows that every fixed-degree polynomial/tensor feature family remains compressible on the same aperture. WI-346 extends this to uniformly analytic height-independent maps with fixed radius, coefficient budget and nonvanishing transmission: their normalized effective rank stays bounded at fixed tolerance, and forcing large rank requires deteriorating complexity/conditioning or enlarging the physical aperture. WI-350 makes the phase-sensitive escape quantitative: for an equivariant feature, the relevant algebraic complexity is the phase-cyclic dimension `D`, not ambient output dimension. Bounded `D` restores a linear rank-resolution tariff; the generic quadratic rank regime requires `D` itself to grow.

The reusable rule is therefore stronger than “check the seminorm” or “count the samples.” **Map the physical source window into theorem coordinates, quotient exact algebraic redundancy, preserve structured main terms on that induced scale, declare the requested destination resolution, and price any support width, orbit dimension, linear or nonlinear feature complexity together with its conditioning.** A large derivative norm need not signal failure when it resolves a theorem boundary layer; a large feature bank need not provide new information when all outputs remain in one fixed-aperture analytic image; and a large measurement family need not provide new constraints when an exact transport law identifies its rows.

**Boundary.** AF-399--AF-402 concern prime-mesh source-density conversion; VIS-294--VIS-295 concern one Wang error budget under two normalizations; FD-200 concerns exact divisor transport in Farey coordinates; NB-216 concerns Mellin-shell support and bounded source functionals; WI-343--WI-350 concern fixed-aperture carrier compression and phase-orbit complexity under declared feature classes. Their scales and constants are not interchangeable. The principle is an audit of physical scale, algebraic independence, effective breadth, feature complexity, sampling quantifier and requested resolution, not a new zero-sensitive theorem.
