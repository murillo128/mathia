# MI-025 — Source breadth must be measured after pullback to the physical source coordinates and admissible source set

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current exact results including [AF-425](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-425-second-order-square-packet-detuning-exposes-source-lattice.md), VIS-294--VIS-305, FD-200--FD-204, MC-383--MC-385, NB-216, and WI-343--WI-352. No numerical scale, conductor exponent or detector estimate is transferred between these objects.

Counts of indices, frequencies, spectral heights, samples, character families or nonlinear features are not intrinsic measures of how much source geometry an architecture reaches. The invariant question is first **which physical/source coordinate interval is traversed and which points of that interval are actually admissible**, then which algebraically independent coordinates survive exact relations, what analytic cost is charged to them, and at what destination resolution the theorem controls the result.

Arithmetic Fidelity now makes the admissible-set pullback explicit. On the tuned square-transition fibers, continuous saddle asymptotics produce a cancellation target in the derivative-offset coordinate, but the physical variable `d_n=s_n-2n` is integer. AF-425 computes that adjacent admissible values are separated by `~log n` in the corrected target function, while the cancellation window required for successive packet orders shrinks like inverse powers of `n` times `1/log n`. A continuously solvable critical equation is therefore not source breadth until one proves that the integer source lattice hits the required moving window.

Visual Exploration gives a theorem-specialization and matched-control audit. Exact gamma structure, source-specific coefficients, support parity and prime-power frequency spacing successively move the pointwise proof wall. VIS-305 then shows that the remaining `x log log x` absolute spacing norm is already reproduced by a parity- and density-matched Bernoulli support. The nominal source sparsity is not arithmetic information unless the theorem uses correlations or phases absent from that control.

Farey Discrepancy shows that observation breadth can be either illusory or productive. FD-200 proves that matched dilation families can be exact divisor transport. FD-204, by contrast, adds genuinely new nonlocal edge relations whose source preimages are explicit Möbius slabs. Their value is measured not by edge count but by the joint effect on source-slab overlap and inverse resistance.

Möbius Cancellation gives the exceptional-set counterpart. MC-383 shows that exponential family cardinality does not buy rank-linear source exponent when a fixed global-modulus power absorbs the gain. MC-384 shows that a theorem can have ample terminal saving yet reject the intended source scale in its admission hypotheses. MC-385 then shows that even a small ambient exceptional population is the wrong coordinate unless it remains small after intersection with the structured endpoint source-difference image. The relevant quantity is bad-difference degree on that frame, not `P^theta` in the ambient conductor interval.

Nyman--Beurling gives another case where widening enlarges the physical support but must be paid through its reconstruction currency. Weil Inertia shows why observation count, nonlinear feature count and formal charge complexity can overstate independent source breadth when destination resolution or existing Lipschitz budgets already control them.

The reusable rule is therefore stronger than “count samples” or “specialize the seminorm.” **Map the physical source window into theorem coordinates, intersect it with the admissible source set, quotient exact algebraic redundancy, specialize the theorem to the actual source family, pull exceptional sets back to that family, express family gain and conductor/source-overlap loss in the same coordinate, and declare the destination resolution.** A continuous critical parameter need not be physically reachable; a huge family need not provide new source breadth; and a small ambient exceptional set need not be small on the source image.

**Boundary.** The mechanisms remain line-specific: integer derivative-depth spacing, Wang frequency support, Farey divisor transport and graph overlap, Möbius conductor geometry, Mellin-shell reconstruction and bow feature geometry are not quantitatively interchangeable. The synthesis is an audit of physical scale, admissible source geometry, algebraic independence, theorem specialization and destination resolution, not a new zero-sensitive theorem.
