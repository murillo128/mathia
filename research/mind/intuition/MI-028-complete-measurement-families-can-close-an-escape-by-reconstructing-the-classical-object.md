# MI-028 — Complete exact families can fail by reconstruction, annihilation, topology-dependent reopening or invariant loss

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [PC-343](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-343-full-shell-mellin-measure-kernel-is-zeta-zero-support.md), [PC-350](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-350-full-inversion-defect-is-faithful-via-two-by-two-fourier-decoder.md), [PC-351](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-351-triangular-inversion-defect-readouts-are-spectrally-trivial.md), [PL-347](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-347-endpoint-modulation-gram-weil-core.md), [PL-354](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-354-real-dirichlet-evaluation-stack-dense-or-zero.md), [PL-355](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-355-prime-l2-boundary-zero-stacks.md), [PL-359](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-359-hamburger-functional-equation-h2-triviality.md), [PL-360](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-360-diagonal-weighted-hilbert-spaces-cannot-carry-zeta-continuation.md), and [VIS-293](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-293-wang-source-dirichlet-rh-half-plane.md). The closure mechanisms are line-specific; no theorem identifies these objects across lines.

Several mathematically different completeness mechanisms share one strategic danger: adding exact information, exact global relations or a more canonical quotient can remove ambiguity in the wrong way. The route may reconstruct the classical object, annihilate the chosen completed class, reopen a large nullspace after changing topology, import the target divisor through continuation, or erase the very source information that the richer representation had recovered.

Prime Circle now exhibits both reconstruction and **invariant loss**. Complete scalar shell observations recover the classical zeta-zero support. PC-350 shows that all-depth reciprocal data are faithful to the centered radial source and already admit a tiny `2x2` solvable decoder. PC-351 then shows that basis-free spectralization of that decoder is blind: triangular regularization has unit limiting spectrum, while an exact reciprocal sign-flip control is unitarily conjugate to the arithmetic decoder. Complete source information can therefore survive in an oriented coordinate and disappear when one applies the canonical basis/gauge quotient proposed for the destination.

Prime Lattice displays several topology outcomes. Complete Fourier modulations form a core for the localized Weil form. Infinite exact evaluation stacks annihilate finite Dirichlet polynomials but reopen an infinite-dimensional nullspace in prime `l2`. The exact conductor-one functional equation collapses native Dirichlet `H^2` to zero because zeta lies outside the space. PL-360 shows that a naive topology repair cannot fix this: any positive diagonal coefficient Hilbert space that contains zeta loses ordinary bounded point evaluation throughout `Re s<=1`. The source vector and destination functional must survive the same topology together.

Visual Exploration gives the analytic-continuation analogue. Exact source access plus first-half-plane holomorphy is not a new discriminator when that condition is already RH-equivalent and reinstates the original divisor.

The shared audit is therefore four-part. For every enriched family or exact source law ask: **what theorem makes the family sufficient; is the completed family nontrivial; what quotient/invariance is actually passed to the destination; and what selective information survives that quotient against matched controls?** Sufficiency without nontriviality can collapse to zero. Nontrivial completion can reopen interpolation freedom. Faithful decoding can disappear under invariantization. Analytic continuation can simply import the original target.

A potentially new mechanism must live between these automatic closures with a source-forced intermediate category that survives completion and matched controls. It must retain a nontrivial quotient or relation without becoming a lossless generic decoder, without collapsing to zero, without reopening arbitrary interpolation freedom, without losing its distinction under the destination's canonical gauge, and without making its decisive analytic condition equivalent to RH itself.

**Boundary.** PC-351 concerns simultaneously triangularizable finite-dimensional developments and the canonical decoder's unitary-equivalence class; PL-360 concerns positive diagonal coefficient Hilbert metrics with ordinary bounded evaluation; the other findings use different source spaces and topologies. The synthesis is a reusable sufficiency/nontriviality/quotient/selectivity audit, not a transfer of their theorems.
