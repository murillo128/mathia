# MI-028 — Complete measurement families can close an escape by reconstructing the classical object

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [PC-343](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-343-full-shell-mellin-measure-kernel-is-zeta-zero-support.md), [PL-347](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-347-endpoint-modulation-gram-weil-core.md), and [PL-353](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-353-finite-coefficient-constraints-dual-interpolation-gate.md). The completeness mechanisms are line-specific; no theorem identifies the Prime-Circle and Prime-Lattice objects.

Two closure mechanisms have the same structural shape even though their mathematics is different, and PL-353 now sharpens what counts as a genuine intermediate restriction.

In Prime Circle, semiprime-null scalar Mellin selectors leave an interpolation problem on the sparse zero set `{log p}` whose survivors depend on strip width and growth class. When the observation family is enlarged to **all shells**, divisor inversion forces zeros at every `{log m}`. For a finite Mellin-frequency measure, Gamma decay permits only polynomial boundary blow-up while that integer-log zero set would force essential-exponential blow-up. The transform must vanish, and Fourier uniqueness leaves only measures supported on the common zeta-zero factor (or the trivial kernel at `sigma=1`). The larger family closes the analytic escape, but what remains is exactly the classical universal factor already present in the shell formula.

In Prime Lattice, one completed endpoint state is only a classical logarithmic Riesz channel. Enlarging to **all Fourier modulations and all cross terms** genuinely adds information, but the endpoint envelope is an invertible multiplier and the modulation span is a form core for the localized Weil quadratic form. Therefore positivity of the complete Gram family is exactly positivity of the pre-existing localized Weil form, and over all apertures it is exactly Weil's RH criterion.

PL-353 shows that nominally restricting that complete family by finitely many coefficient equations need not create an intermediate object. On the overcomplete prime-log span, a finite coefficient relation survives completion only when it is continuous in the actual local Sobolev/form topology, equivalently when its weights are samples of a continuous dual Paley--Wiener functional. Finite centering, logarithmic moments, real-point jets and finite `p^(-sigma)` relations fail this gate, so their constrained spans still close to the full Weil form core. Algebraic codimension before completion can therefore become **zero topological codimension** at the destination.

The shared mechanism is not “more data are bad.” It is more precise: when the added observation family is complete for the ambient analytic/form object by a classical inversion or density theorem, the apparent new rigidity can consist entirely of **reconstructing that old object**. Conversely, deleting finitely many algebraic coefficient directions does not create a new family when the destination topology cannot see those deletions continuously. Completeness closes loopholes and is mathematically useful, but it does not by itself create a new source-selective law.

This gives a concrete test for intermediate proposals. Before crediting a richer or restricted family, ask whether its sufficiency is doing anything beyond one of these free closures: algebraic inversion may upgrade observed coordinates to a complete classical transform; density/form-core completion may upgrade the sampled family to the entire known quadratic form; and finite coefficient restrictions may disappear if they are discontinuous in the completion topology.

A potentially new mechanism must live strictly before those closures while still having a source-forced sufficiency theorem: for example a sparse family not automatically complete by density, a shell subfamily whose arithmetic relations imply more than generic interpolation, a coefficient law represented by a genuine continuous destination-dual trace, an infinite coherent restriction, or a coupling/noncommutative relation not invariant under arbitrary replacement by another complete frame.

**Boundary.** PC-343 concerns finite Mellin measures and all Prime-Circle shells; PL-347 concerns one endpoint envelope, its full modulation Gram family and the localized completed Weil form; PL-353 concerns finite linear coefficient constraints in a subcritical local Sobolev topology. The cross-line claim is only the reusable completeness/topology audit above, not a transfer of any theorem between settings.
