# MI-028 — Complete exact families can close an escape by reconstruction, completion or annihilation

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [PC-343](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-343-full-shell-mellin-measure-kernel-is-zeta-zero-support.md), [PL-347](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-347-endpoint-modulation-gram-weil-core.md), [PL-353](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-353-finite-coefficient-constraints-dual-interpolation-gate.md), [PL-354](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-354-real-dirichlet-evaluation-stack-dense-or-zero.md), and [VIS-293](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-293-wang-source-dirichlet-rh-half-plane.md). The completeness mechanisms are line-specific; no theorem identifies these objects across lines.

Several mathematically different closure mechanisms now show the same strategic danger: adding an exact family can remove ambiguity by returning to the classical target object, while accumulating exact constraints can jump from topologically invisible to algebraically trivial. Neither outcome is automatically an intermediate RH mechanism.

In Prime Circle, semiprime-null scalar Mellin selectors leave an interpolation problem on the sparse zero set `{log p}` whose survivors depend on strip width and growth class. When the observation family is enlarged to all shells, divisor inversion forces zeros at every `{log m}`. Gamma decay then makes the only surviving finite Mellin-frequency measures those supported on the common zeta-zero factor. The larger family closes the analytic escape, but what remains is precisely the classical universal factor already present in the shell formula.

In Prime Lattice, one completed endpoint state is only a classical logarithmic Riesz channel. Enlarging to all Fourier modulations and all cross terms genuinely adds information, but the endpoint envelope is an invertible multiplier and the modulation span is a form core for the localized Weil quadratic form. Positivity of the complete Gram family is therefore exactly ordinary localized Weil positivity.

PL-353 shows that nominally restricting this complete family by finitely many coefficient equations need not create an intermediate object. A finite relation survives completion only when it is continuous in the actual local Sobolev/form topology. Otherwise algebraic codimension becomes zero topological codimension and the constrained span still closes to the full Weil form core.

PL-354 closes the opposite exact-stack escape. A finite collection of positive-real Dirichlet evaluations remains in the PL-353 dense-kernel regime, but an infinite distinct exact stack annihilates every nonzero finite Dirichlet polynomial. The literal family therefore jumps from dense to zero rather than approaching a proper nonzero closed intermediate space.

Visual Exploration gives an analytic-continuation version. The exact weighted prime-power source has `D(s)=(log zeta)''(s)+A(s)` with `A` holomorphic on `Re(s)>1/2`. Asking the recovered source transform to be holomorphic there, after removing the elementary pole, is exactly asking for RH. Exact source access has not produced a new discriminator if the decisive analytic extension simply reinstates the target zero divisor.

The shared mechanism is not “more data are bad.” It is more precise: an enlarged exact family must be audited for what theorem makes it sufficient. Algebraic inversion may reconstruct a complete classical transform; density may reconstruct the whole known quadratic form; finite restrictions may disappear in the destination topology; an infinite exact constraint stack may annihilate the algebraic source class; and analytic continuation may import the target divisor as the very obstruction being tested.

A potentially new mechanism must live strictly between these automatic closures while still having a source-forced sufficiency theorem: for example a sparse family not automatically complete by density, a relation represented by a genuine continuous destination-dual trace, an approximate infinite law in a completed coefficient topology, a cross-aperture or nonlinear coupling, or another source-specific quotient whose sufficiency does not amount to reconstructing the classical object or demanding the target continuation directly.

**Boundary.** PC-343 concerns finite Mellin measures and all Prime-Circle shells; PL-347 the localized Weil form core; PL-353 finite linear coefficient constraints; PL-354 exact positive-real evaluation stacks on finite Dirichlet polynomials; VIS-293 the first-half-plane pole set of one weighted source transform. The synthesis is only the reusable closure audit, not a transfer of any theorem between settings.
