# MI-028 — Complete exact families can fail by reconstruction, classicalization, annihilation, topology-dependent reopening or invariant loss

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [PC-343](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-343-full-shell-mellin-measure-kernel-is-zeta-zero-support.md), [PC-350](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-350-full-inversion-defect-is-faithful-via-two-by-two-fourier-decoder.md), [PC-351](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-351-triangular-inversion-defect-readouts-are-spectrally-trivial.md), [PC-371](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-371-shell2-centered-order-cone-is-universal-ramanujan-moment-data.md), [PC-372](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-372-shell2-order-refinement-is-real-cyclotomic-chebyshev-tower.md), [PL-347](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-347-endpoint-modulation-gram-weil-core.md), [PL-354](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-354-real-dirichlet-evaluation-stack-dense-or-zero.md), [PL-355](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-355-prime-l2-boundary-zero-stacks.md), [PL-359](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-359-hamburger-functional-equation-h2-triviality.md), [PL-360](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-360-diagonal-weighted-hilbert-spaces-cannot-carry-zeta-continuation.md), and [VIS-293](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-293-wang-source-dirichlet-rh-half-plane.md). The closure mechanisms are line-specific; no theorem identifies these objects across lines.

Several mathematically different completeness mechanisms share one strategic danger: adding exact information, exact global relations or a more canonical quotient can remove ambiguity in the wrong way. The route may reconstruct the classical object, classicalize a seemingly new representation, annihilate the chosen completed class, reopen a large nullspace after changing topology, import the target divisor through continuation, or erase the very source information that the richer representation had recovered.

Prime Circle exhibits reconstruction, invariant loss and now **classicalization of a genuine order escape**. Complete scalar shell observations recover classical zeta-zero support. PC-350 shows that all-depth reciprocal data are faithful and admit a tiny decoder, while PC-351 shows that basis-free spectralization erases its distinction. PC-371 then finds a real escape from reciprocal parity: the shell-2 radial order cone. But its complete moment data already reconstruct the angular shell, and PC-372 goes further: the entire order polynomial is exactly an affine reciprocal maximal-real cyclotomic polynomial, while the source-native power map is exactly the Chebyshev quotient of ordinary cyclotomic refinement. The canonical order-plus-refinement enrichment therefore closes by returning to the classical cyclotomic/Bost--Connes tower.

Prime Lattice displays several topology outcomes. Complete Fourier modulations form a core for the localized Weil form. Infinite exact evaluation stacks annihilate finite Dirichlet polynomials but reopen an infinite-dimensional nullspace in prime `l2`. The exact conductor-one functional equation collapses native Dirichlet `H^2` to zero because zeta lies outside the space. PL-360 shows that a naive topology repair cannot fix this: any positive diagonal coefficient Hilbert space that contains zeta loses ordinary bounded point evaluation throughout `Re s<=1`. The source vector and destination functional must survive the same topology together.

Visual Exploration gives the analytic-continuation analogue. Exact source access plus first-half-plane holomorphy is not a new discriminator when that condition is already RH-equivalent and reinstates the original divisor.

The shared audit is therefore five-part. For every enriched family or exact source law ask: **what theorem makes the family sufficient; is the completed family nontrivial; what known object does complete recovery reconstruct; what quotient/invariance is actually passed to the destination; and what selective information survives that quotient against matched controls?** Sufficiency without nontriviality can collapse to zero. Nontrivial completion can reopen interpolation freedom. Faithful decoding can disappear under invariantization. A genuinely new-looking coordinate can classicalize once its complete polynomial and natural refinement law are written exactly. Analytic continuation can simply import the original target.

A potentially new mechanism must live between these automatic closures with a source-forced intermediate category that survives completion and matched controls. It must retain a nontrivial relation without becoming a lossless generic decoder, collapsing to zero, reopening arbitrary interpolation freedom, losing its distinction under the destination gauge, classicalizing to an already-known tower, or making its decisive analytic condition equivalent to RH itself.

**Boundary.** PC-372 concerns the shell-2 order polynomial and its natural power/Chebyshev refinement; it does not classify every nonlinear or nonlocal order operation. PL-360 concerns positive diagonal coefficient Hilbert metrics with ordinary bounded evaluation; the other findings use different source spaces and topologies. The synthesis is a reusable closure audit, not a transfer of their theorems.
