# MI-028 — Complete exact families can close an escape by reconstruction, annihilation or topology-dependent reopening

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [PC-343](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-343-full-shell-mellin-measure-kernel-is-zeta-zero-support.md), [PC-350](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-350-full-inversion-defect-is-faithful-via-two-by-two-fourier-decoder.md), [PL-347](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-347-endpoint-modulation-gram-weil-core.md), [PL-354](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-354-real-dirichlet-evaluation-stack-dense-or-zero.md), [PL-355](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-355-prime-l2-boundary-zero-stacks.md), [PL-356](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-356-boundary-zero-stacks-finite-constraint-robustness.md), [PL-359](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-359-hamburger-functional-equation-h2-triviality.md), and [VIS-293](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-293-wang-source-dirichlet-rh-half-plane.md). The closure mechanisms are line-specific; no theorem identifies these objects across lines.

Several mathematically different completeness mechanisms share one strategic danger: adding exact information or exact global relations can remove ambiguity by returning to the classical target object, annihilating the chosen completed class, or reopening a large nullspace after the topology changes. None of these outcomes is automatically an intermediate RH mechanism.

In Prime Circle, sufficiently complete scalar shell observations close interpolation but leave the universal zeta-zero factor: the enriched family reconstructs the classical analytic object rather than generating an independent selector. PC-350 shows that the nonlinear all-depth boundary is no safer. The complete reciprocity-regularized inversion defect is faithful to the entire radial path, and a tiny `2x2` solvable subsector already decodes it. Yet the same decoder works for broad non-arithmetic reciprocal controls. Complete nonlinear information has again produced source reconstruction, not zero selection.

Prime Lattice displays three different topology outcomes. Complete Fourier modulations form a core for the localized Weil form. Infinite exact real evaluation stacks annihilate finite Dirichlet polynomials, but after passing to the natural prime `l2` completion classical boundary interpolation produces an infinite-dimensional common nullspace. PL-359 supplies a different extreme: append the exact conductor-one Riemann functional equation with Hamburger regularity inside native Dirichlet `H^2`, and the admissible family collapses to zero because the unique nonzero classical solution, zeta, lies outside that completion.

Visual Exploration gives the analytic-continuation analogue. Exact access to the weighted source transform is not a new discriminator if demanding first-half-plane holomorphy simply reinstates the Riemann zero divisor; VIS-293 shows that this continuation condition is already RH-equivalent.

The shared audit is therefore stronger than “more data can reconstruct the answer.” For every enriched family or exact source law, ask three separate questions: **what theorem makes the family sufficient, is the resulting completed family nontrivial in the declared topology, and what selective information remains after sufficiency/nontriviality are applied?** A route can fail by reconstruction, by annihilation, by topological reopening, or by importing the target divisor through continuation.

A potentially new mechanism must live between these automatic closures with a source-forced intermediate category that survives completion and matched controls. It must retain a nontrivial quotient or relation without becoming a lossless decoder of generic source data, without collapsing to zero, without reopening arbitrary interpolation freedom, and without making its decisive analytic condition equivalent to the original RH statement.

**Boundary.** PC-350 concerns one reciprocal all-depth signature decoder; PL-359 uses the exact conductor-one Riemann functional equation with standard converse-theorem regularity in native unweighted Dirichlet `H^2`; the other cited findings use different source spaces and topologies. The synthesis is a reusable sufficiency/nontriviality/selectivity audit, not a transfer of their theorems.
