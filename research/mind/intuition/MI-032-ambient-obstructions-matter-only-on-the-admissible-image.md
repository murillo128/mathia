# MI-032 — Ambient obstructions matter only on the admissible image

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of [AF-476](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-476-compressibility-profiles-force-hamming-cube-lower-conditioning.md), [FD-253](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-253-companion-incidence-controls-paired-exact-omission-conditioning.md), [VIS-360](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-360-pooled-rescaling-colliding-center-floor.md), and [WP-393](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-393-unitality-collapses-woronowicz-affiliation-back-to-bounded-bost-connes-algebra.md). The four mathematical objects are different; the shared statement is a reachability audit, not a transfer of their constants or theorems.

Several current obstructions live in an ambient space larger than the states actually admitted by the source or workflow. Such an obstruction is relevant only when the bad direction survives the exact admission map and the downstream quotient/category restriction. Finding an ill-conditioned ambient family is not enough if the admissible image avoids it; enlarging an ambient category is not progress if the purported enlargement collapses back onto the old image.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes this distinction explicit. AF-476 places Hamming cubes inside the full compressibility envelope `C_phi` and obtains a minimax Hilbert lower bound there. A narrower arithmetic subclass inherits that obstruction only if it actually realizes comparable private-tail switches. Its upper approximation profile alone does not prove that reachability.

Farey Discrepancy gives a linear-image version. FD-252 can make the full increment support carry arbitrarily bad incidence conditioning, but FD-253 shows that the exact omission map restricts coefficients to adjacent opposite pairs. On large dilations the companion rows are private sensors, so the paired image has `O(K)` inverse cost even while the ambient support norm is super-polylogarithmically bad. The bad ambient row exists but is not reachable with admissible coefficients.

Visual Exploration gives a quotient-geometry version. Colliding cluster centers make many square coordinate descriptions look singular, yet after pooling at the correct outer scale a nondegenerate normalized moment Gram restores the ordinary `sqrt(N) rho^(q-1)` floor. The relevant bad direction begins only when the full rescaled sampling image itself loses rank/conditioning.

Weil Positivity gives a category version. “Allow Woronowicz-affiliated elements” sounds like an unbounded enlargement of the Bost--Connes source, but unitality gives `A_BC^eta=A_BC`. The admissible category has not enlarged at all, so the bounded obstruction remains unchanged.

The reusable test is therefore concrete: write the actual source/admission map, compute the image after the line's exact quotient or category restriction, and exhibit the obstruction **inside that image**. If the bad ambient direction is unreachable, record the thinner admissible geometry and move the frontier there rather than crediting the ambient obstruction. If a proposed escape enlarges the representation, verify that it enlarges the admissible image rather than only its description.

**Boundary.** This is not a universal theorem that all Mathia obstructions are image-restriction problems, nor a common numerical complexity measure. Hamming-cube realizability, paired incidence reachability, polynomial-sampling rank and C-star affiliation are distinct objects. The supported commonality is only the order of proof: ambient pathology or ambient freedom counts after admissible reachability is established.
