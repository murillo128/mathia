# MI-032 — Ambient obstructions matter only on the admissible image

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of [AF-476](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-476-compressibility-profiles-force-hamming-cube-lower-conditioning.md), [FD-255](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-255-global-mobius-rows-collapse-exact-omission-reachability.md), [VIS-360](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-360-pooled-rescaling-colliding-center-floor.md), [WP-393](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-393-unitality-collapses-woronowicz-affiliation-back-to-bounded-bost-connes-algebra.md), and [WP-395](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-395-critical-kms-centralizer-erases-exact-prime-power-orbit-support.md). The mathematical objects are different; the shared statement is a reachability/quotient audit, not a transfer of constants or theorems.

Several current obstructions live in an ambient space larger than the states actually admitted by the source or workflow. Such an obstruction is relevant only when the bad direction survives the exact admission map and the downstream quotient/category restriction. Finding an ill-conditioned ambient family is not enough if the admissible image avoids it; enlarging an ambient category is not progress if the enlargement collapses back onto the old image or quotients away the source datum one hoped to use.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the first distinction explicit. AF-476 places Hamming cubes inside the full compressibility envelope `C_phi` and obtains a minimax Hilbert lower bound there. A narrower arithmetic subclass inherits that obstruction only if it actually realizes comparable private-tail switches. Its upper approximation profile alone does not prove that reachability.

Farey Discrepancy now gives the sharpest extension example. FD-249--FD-254 exhibit large active-support and companion inverse norms, but FD-255 restores the rows discarded by those restricted systems. The global identities `g=mu*b` and `b=1*g`, together with `|b_n|<=C_b n`, force every realized active coordinate to satisfy `|g(d)|/d<=C_b sigma_{-1}(d)` and every sublinear exact-omission family to have only sparse double-log aggregate amplification. Polynomial restricted inverse extremizers therefore live outside the globally extendable coefficient image. The ambient singular direction is real, but it is not admissible data for the original problem.

Visual Exploration gives a quotient-geometry version. Colliding cluster centers make many square coordinate descriptions look singular, yet after pooling at the correct outer scale a nondegenerate normalized moment Gram restores the ordinary `sqrt(N) rho^(q-1)` floor. The relevant bad direction begins only when the full rescaled sampling image itself loses rank or conditioning.

Weil Positivity exhibits both category failure modes. WP-393 shows that “allow Woronowicz-affiliated elements” does not enlarge the original unital Bost--Connes algebra at all. WP-395 then tests a category that genuinely is larger: the critical modular centralizer is a diffuse `L^infinity(hat Z,m_Haar)` completion. But exact arithmetic point support on a standard countable orbit becomes null in that measure class. Literal prime-power support is therefore zero, while prime-power-versus-mixed-prime point values are not operator invariants. An enlargement can thus fail either because the admissible category never changed or because its quotient erased the datum of interest.

The reusable test is concrete: write the actual source/admission map, compute the image after every extension constraint and quotient/category identification, and exhibit the obstruction or resource **inside that image**. If the bad ambient direction is unreachable, record the thinner extendable geometry. If a proposed escape enlarges the representation, verify both that it truly enlarges the admissible category and that the source distinction remains an invariant after the new equivalence relation or measure class is imposed.

**Boundary.** This is not a universal theorem that all Mathia obstructions are image-restriction problems, nor a common numerical complexity measure. Hamming-cube realizability, global divisor-row extension, polynomial-sampling rank, C-star affiliation and diffuse von Neumann measure classes are distinct objects. The supported commonality is only the order of proof: ambient pathology or ambient freedom counts after admissible reachability and quotient survival are established.
