# MI-019 — Source identification, analytic access and zero selection are orthogonal gates

**Evidence level:** exact cross-line synthesis supported by [AF-370](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-370-hamburger-source-class-functional-equation-rigidity.md), [AF-406](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-406-finite-polya-frequency-positivity-is-not-zero-set-faithful-under-exponential-gauge.md), [AF-407](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-407-euler-transform-zero-obstruction-is-continuation-only.md), the Prime-Circle scalar-sector results [PC-314](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-314-fourier-symmetrized-canonical-packet-has-off-critical-zeros.md)--[PC-320](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-320-canonical-mordell-tornheim-off-critical-zeros-form-local-hypersurface.md), and the Gamma-strip classification through [PC-341](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-341-prime-log-uniqueness-extends-to-bounded-characteristic-on-gamma-strip.md). No transfer theorem between the source classes is claimed.

AF-370 gives a positive recovery theorem. In Hamburger's class of ordinary Dirichlet series with the required finite-order continuation, the exact completed Riemann functional equation forces `f=c zeta`; coefficient normalization removes the scalar gauge. Here the symmetry is genuinely faithful because the admissible source class already includes enough global analytic structure.

AF-406 sharpens the **zero-selection** side of the separation. For `xi_1(z)=xi(sqrt(z)+1/2)`, multiplication by `e^(nz)` preserves the complete zero divisor, but for every fixed finite `m` sufficiently large `n` forces `e^(nz)xi_1 in PF_m`. Thus a natural finite positivity verdict can vary while the entire RH-relevant zero geometry stays fixed. Positivity is not automatically a zero selector merely because all-order Pólya-frequency positivity is related to the Laguerre--Pólya target; the finite certificate must first descend through the zero-free exponential gauge or that gauge must be fixed independently.

AF-407 adds an orthogonal **analytic-access** gate. For the affine-tilted Euler-log profile, the bilateral Laplace transform is zero-free throughout its genuine convergence half-plane. The nontrivial zeta zeros appear only in the meromorphic continuation beyond that wall, and exponential gauge shifts the wall and continued zeros together. The all-order Pólya-frequency obstruction can use those zeros because Schoenberg supplies a reciprocal-entire continuation theorem. A finite-order translation-positivity argument confined to the native transform domain cannot: there are no transform zeros there. Therefore a certificate may identify the source and be gauge-invariant yet still fail to control the analytic continuation on which the desired zero obstruction lives.

Prime Circle gives the complementary zero-geometry theorem at progressively richer representations. PC-314--PC-315 show that complete finite-Fourier scalar symmetry, including orientation, is compatible with infinitely many zeros in `Re s>1`. PC-319 moves before scalar cyclic compression: the exact canonical `(5,7)` Mordell--Tornheim carrier itself inherits off-critical zeros for every sufficiently small positive coupling. PC-320 then removes the temptation to blame a frozen slice: joint holomorphy and Weierstrass preparation extend the off-critical zero to a complex-analytic hypersurface finite over an open bidisk of the other Mellin parameters.

PC-335--PC-341 sharpen the **analytic-domain** part of the audit in a different direction. Exact mixed-semiprime nulls force zeros at all prime logarithms in the scalar Mellin interpolation function. On a strip `S_a={|Im z|<a}`, conformal transport gives

`1-|phi_a(log p)| = 2/(p^(pi/(2a))+1)`.

Hence the prime logs are a uniqueness set for bounded-characteristic functions exactly when `a>=pi/2`. At the native Gamma half-width `pi/2`, even the full Nevanlinna class is too rigid: the semiprime-zero packet collapses to the same von-Mangoldt endpoint classification as the bounded/polynomial-growth cases. But on every narrower strip `a<pi/2`, a nonzero bounded Blaschke product can vanish at all prime logs. Analytic access is therefore not a binary statement that a continuation exists; **the width and function class of the controlled domain determine whether the zero set is coercive at all**.

The obligations are therefore distinct:

1. **Identification:** does the retained structure determine which analytic source/function or source-dependent object is present inside the declared category?
2. **Gauge fidelity:** does the proposed intermediate certificate descend to the quotient that leaves the target zero divisor unchanged, or has a canonical normalization genuinely fixed that freedom?
3. **Analytic access:** does the theorem control the domain and analytic class on which the relevant zeros or uniqueness set are visible, with enough width/growth control for the intended inference?
4. **Zero selection:** what independent theorem forces the relevant divisor or spectrum into the desired region?

A functional equation, exact coefficient vector, invertible source representation, nonseparable multivariable carrier, finite positivity hierarchy, or exact transform on its native domain can settle or enrich the first three questions while answering none of the fourth. PC-341 adds the converse warning: a zero set that is coercive in one analytic domain can cease to be coercive after a seemingly modest loss of strip width, even inside `H^infty`.

The practical audit is to demand separate certificates. First prove the source-class recovery statement and its normalization/gauge boundary. Then state the native analytic domain and the exact theorem that grants access to the continuation containing the relevant zero set, including width and growth class when uniqueness is used. Next check that the proposed sign/positivity/spectral statistic is intrinsic to the target quotient on that domain. Finally identify a genuinely zero-selecting mechanism—positivity, unitarity, sign, self-adjoint spectrum, a source-forced distinguished submanifold/divisor relation, or another theorem whose conclusion actually concerns the target zero set.

**Boundary.** AF-370 concerns Hamburger's ordinary-Dirichlet finite-order class; AF-406 concerns finite Pólya-frequency positivity under a specific zero-free exponential gauge; AF-407 concerns native versus continued bilateral Laplace information for the Euler-log profile; PC-314--PC-320 concern canonical periodic/Mordell--Tornheim objects; PC-341 concerns bounded-characteristic uniqueness from prime-log zeros and does not classify arbitrary analytic functionals. The synthesis does not say every positivity notion is gauge-dependent, every continuation is inaccessible, or every narrower domain is useless. It says that faithful identification, gauge fixing, analytic-domain control and RH-like zero confinement remain separate mathematical obligations until a theorem links them.
