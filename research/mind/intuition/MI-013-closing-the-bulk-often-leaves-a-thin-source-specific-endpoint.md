These examples support a common diagnostic: **after a bulk mechanism is closed, identify the exact carrier and the new currency before strengthening the old estimate.** The surviving obstruction may be first nontorsion incidence, recursively eligible normalized transport, logarithmic shell generation or one exceptional source character, microscopic scalar geometry, physical-metric transport of an already-local coefficient, sub-threshold fiber coupling, nonsummable delay geometry, conditioned cross-scale selector incidence, source-specific boundary regularity/sign, short-range first-crossing connectivity, or independently forced global coupling. Better constants in the exhausted bulk currency need not touch it.

**Boundary.** This synthesis does not identify a transfer theorem between these objects and does not imply that the residual carriers are easier or rare on the arithmetic source. It is a program-level warning against continuing to optimize a mechanism after the destination has moved to a mathematically different carrier.
