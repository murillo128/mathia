# MI-013 — Closing the bulk often changes the mathematical currency of the endpoint

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from the current local minds and canonical findings. The reductions below are exact only inside their owning lines and hypotheses; this is not a theorem that every RH route must have the same shape.

Several mature lines now show a stronger pattern than “the obstruction gets smaller.” Once a broad mechanism is closed, the surviving object often lives in a different mathematical category. Continuing to improve the old bulk estimate then attacks the wrong currency.

Arithmetic Fidelity is an exceptional-fiber example. AF-316 makes generic post-incidence recovery dimension-tight for every support size. [AF-317](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-317-antipodal-extension-recursion-breaks-six-point-singular-rigidity.md) proves that the six-point singular exclusion does not extend: at eight points `e_4=0` contains exact centered nontorsion configurations without global antipodal symmetry. The residual carrier is no longer generic harmonic recovery but **the integer-character rank of the source-accessible singular fiber**, together with first prime-log incidence and conditioning.

Farey Discrepancy has changed currency twice. Exact lower transports removed the predecessor-entropy/representation problem, and FD-094 priced fixed occupation by the backward normalized-energy record defect. [FD-095](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-095-bounded-record-defect-turns-the-source-head-into-an-amplitude-scale-predecessor.md) then absorbs the last source-head branch using source Lipschitz coherence. The one-step residual is now **historical alignment between sharp occupied packets and bounded record defect**; if that is obtained, the next currency becomes multistep stability of the inherited constants, not another local repair theorem.

Möbius Cancellation moves from entropy to signed radial arithmetic. MC-264 finds the support-matched moment order that can detect one blind atom, MC-265 kills first-order/absolute-value control, and [MC-266](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-266-tensor-pair-krawtchouk-radial-source-walk-collapse.md) collapses the entire tensor-pair geometry to a single `2m`-step Boolean-cube walk and then to support layers. The residual carrier is **signed Krawtchouk cancellation in the finite radial hierarchy `H_2,...,H_(2m)`**, not arbitrary pairwise shell geometry.

Nyman--Beurling is an inverse-geometry example. NB-092 moves from fixed-depth silence to a growing-depth near-kernel, and NB-093 reduces one zero to a rank-one causal-state leverage problem. [NB-094](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-094-zero-cancellation-keeps-coordinate-state-cost-bounded-while-leverage-grows.md) closes the normalization gate: the zero identity keeps every coordinate small while collective inverse-Gram leverage grows polynomially, so the full one-zero exponent survives normalization with the sign of a correlation-volume repair. The residual carrier is now **interaction among several causal zero states on successively deflated sources**, not whether a single growing-depth signal survives diagonal normalization.

Prime Circle supplies a symmetry-endpoint version. PC-278 shows positive single-source phase spectra are pure unitary gauge. [PC-279](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-279-full-dual-torus-anchor-nonlinearities-are-source-blind-fourier-data.md) closes the obvious nonlinear escape on the complete torus: even source-selected anchored profiles become permutation-equivalent and reduce spectrally to one-dimensional Fourier magnitudes. The residual carrier is now **finite-window boundary mismatch under source multiplication** or a genuinely relative/flux construction, not “more nonlinear phase data.”

Prime Flute shows that a proof-strategy endpoint can also migrate. PF-322 blocks uniform low-sector Taylor smallness near the receding seam, and [PF-323](../../prime_flute/findings/PF-323-interior-critical-low-packets-avoid-the-cuff-seam.md) moves the same critical scale onto a fixed window strictly inside the cuff. The residual is therefore **what the actual pant quotient, seam normalization, physical projections and completion do to an interior critical packet**, not whether seam identification caused the obstruction.

Robin Extremal changes the meaning of sparse-host scarcity. RE-095 shows that a source-conditioned bad-host saving would lower the depth-two frontier, and RE-096 identifies a phase-stable deformed quadratic sample. [RE-097](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-097-bazzanella-quadratic-threshold-is-ambient-packing-not-phase-control.md) proves that the published quadratic `13/32` mechanism and any analogous ambient exceptional-set packing cannot improve the existing Robin envelope. The residual carrier is therefore **direct sparse-sequence phase control below the host-spacing scale**, not a better ambient exceptional-start measure evaluated on sparse points.

Visual Exploration gives a resource-threshold example. VIS-203--VIS-205 identify the fixed-scale positive shell target with the two-sample crowding mass `Q(A)`. [VIS-206](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-206-fixed-scale-pair-crowding-bounded-degree.md) proves that this weaker target already vanishes for every fixed `A` in the full strictly subcritical regime, even though the stronger one-sided crowding route has a square-root-log method threshold. The residual carrier has moved to **growing normalized collision windows and the intermediate sinc-weighted shell range**.

Weil Inertia gives a relaxation endpoint. WI-274 turns all cuts into an integrated positive source reserve; [WI-275](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-275-a-harmonic-minimax-reserve-is-the-sharp-mass-only-cut-bound.md) computes the sharp pointwise minimax envelope of the same complementary masses. Since the equality mass profile is monotone, another mass-only rearrangement cannot help. The residual carrier is **paired-radius spectral shape or cross-cut null/source coupling**, not a better convex average of the two cut inequalities.

The durable rule is therefore: after a bulk mechanism is closed, re-identify the mathematical type of the residual before strengthening anything. A local branch can become a historical-record problem; a pair family can become a radial signed transform; a normalized Gram question can become a multi-state interaction problem; a spectral phase field can become a boundary mismatch; a sparse-host count can become a direct phase theorem; a seam obstruction can become a post-projection angle problem; a crowding threshold can move to a widening window; a positive reserve can become a sharp-relaxation barrier. Better constants in the exhausted currency need not move the new endpoint at all.

**Boundary.** The pattern does not imply that the residual carriers are small, easier, rare, or sufficient for RH, and it creates no transfer theorem between them. It is a program-level test for whether the next theorem is being asked in the right mathematical currency.
