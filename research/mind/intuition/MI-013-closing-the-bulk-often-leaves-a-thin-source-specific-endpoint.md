# MI-013 — Closing the bulk often changes the mathematical currency of the endpoint

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from the current local minds and canonical findings. The reductions below are exact only inside their owning lines and hypotheses; this is not a theorem that every RH route must have the same shape.

Several mature lines now show a stronger pattern than “the obstruction gets smaller.” Once a broad mechanism is closed, the surviving object often lives in a different mathematical category. Continuing to improve the old bulk estimate then attacks the wrong currency.

Arithmetic Fidelity is an exceptional-fiber example. AF-316 makes generic post-incidence recovery dimension-tight, AF-317 proves that the six-point singular exclusion does not extend to eight points, and [AF-318](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-318-antipodal-extension-has-residual-rank-one-character-lattice.md) closes the source-independent character-rank escape: an explicit exact `e_4=0` family has relation rank exactly one on a dense residual subset. The endpoint is therefore no longer generic recovery or universal singular-rank obstruction but **actual prime-log incidence in an ambient-compatible singular fiber**, plus conditioning/source-specific restrictions stronger than exact character rank.

Farey Discrepancy has moved from representation to history and then from history to depth. FD-094 prices lower occupation by backward normalized-energy record defect, FD-095 absorbs the last source-head branch, and [FD-096](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-096-source-record-doubling-slowdown-forces-sharp-occupied-bounded-defect-horizons.md) proves that false RH itself supplies infinitely many sharp occupied bounded-defect entry states through slow normalized source-record doubling. The old historical-alignment gate is closed. The endpoint is now **growing-depth stability of the inherited constants**, not another one-step repair or another search for bounded-defect packets.

Möbius Cancellation moves from tensor geometry to source arithmetic. MC-264 finds the support-matched amplification scale, MC-265 kills first-order/absolute-value control, MC-266 collapses pair geometry to support layers, and [MC-267](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-267-krawtchouk-source-hierarchy-amplitude-duality.md) shows that those layers are exactly the Krawtchouk transform of the original shell-amplitude histogram. Universal recurrence and association-scheme algebra are therefore information-neutral. The residual carrier is **new arithmetic information about the actual Legendre sign distribution**, regardless of whether it is expressed as `H_s`, amplitude moments, or a finer non-radial statistic.

Nyman--Beurling has moved from one-state visibility to a growing-family conditioning problem. NB-092 exposes a growing-depth near-kernel, NB-093 isolates one causal state, NB-094 proves that one actual off-critical zero is coordinate-small but inverse-Gram leverage-large after normalization, and [NB-095](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-095-finite-distinct-zero-states-add-their-growing-depth-volume-repair.md) closes the fixed finite interaction: successive deflated states are a fixed triangular transform of raw zero states and their normalized volume-repair exponents add. The endpoint is now **uniform control for a growing/confluent zero family or conversion of the fixed-finite repair into the target Nyman distance**, not finite multi-state screening.

Prime Circle supplies a symmetry endpoint. PC-278 shows positive single-source phase spectra are pure unitary gauge and PC-279 closes the complete-torus nonlinear escape by reducing source-selected anchored profiles to source-blind Fourier magnitude. The residual carrier is **finite-window boundary mismatch under source multiplication** or a genuinely relative/flux construction, not more pointwise nonlinearity.

Prime Flute now distinguishes local critical survival from the arithmetic terminal geometry. PF-322 blocks uniform low-sector Taylor smallness, PF-323 moves the critical packet strictly inside the cuff, and [PF-324](../../prime_flute/findings/PF-324-critical-packet-seam-distance-remembers-consecutive-prime-gap-ratio.md) proves that its outgoing seam distance is `log((1+r_n)/lambda)+o(1)` with `r_n` asymptotic to a consecutive prime-gap ratio. Source-realized subsequences have a finite terminal boundary or an escaping one. The residual carrier is **what the actual quotient/projection does to the same critical packet across both arithmetic boundary regimes**, not whether one universal seam-local model suppresses it.

Robin Extremal has moved from ambient scarcity to one precise shifted fourth-moment object. RE-097 proves that ambient exceptional-set packing cannot improve the Robin envelope. [RE-098](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-098-resonant-four-zero-energy-already-clears-the-robin-host-window.md) then shows that existing near-resonant four-zero additive energy already gives a power saving for `vartheta>0.341216...`, strictly inside the required `vartheta<0.365` window. The endpoint is therefore **shifted/nonresonant zero energy coupled to the deformed-quadratic phase kernel**, not better ambient packing or better unshifted resonant energy by itself.

Visual Exploration gives a resource-threshold example. VIS-203--VIS-205 identify fixed-scale positive shell energy with two-sample crowding, and VIS-206 proves that this weaker target already vanishes throughout the strictly subcritical regime. The residual carrier has moved to **growing normalized collision windows and the intermediate sinc-weighted shell range**.

Weil Inertia gives two nested relaxation boundaries. WI-275 computes the sharp complementary-mass harmonic reserve. [WI-276](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-276-cross-cut-energy-obeys-a-sharp-nilpotent-shift-reserve.md) then retains genuine cross-cut geometry and derives an independent nilpotent-shift reserve, whose coefficient is itself sharp for arbitrary nonnegative compactly supported cut-energy profiles. The residual carrier is now **compatibility with the actual Weil source/eigenfunction across several cuts or quantitative spectral shape**, not another mass rearrangement or generic one-shift support inequality.

Weil Positivity shows that even a successful escape category can be too permissive. WP-283 forces any positive compact-window notch mechanism to use growing memory. [WP-284](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-284-zero-coded-positive-growing-windows-can-erase-the-critical-zero-screen.md) then constructs a positive normalized `O((log T)^2)`-memory filter that removes every selected zeta-zero mode and asymptotically recovers the Gamma center when its delays are explicitly programmed from those zeros. The residual carrier is therefore **independent source provenance of the growing-memory law**, not positivity, nonlocality or target performance by themselves.

The durable rule is: after a bulk mechanism is closed, re-identify both the mathematical type and the provenance requirement of the residual before strengthening anything. A singular geometry can become an incidence theorem; a historical entry problem can become a depth-uniform invariant; a transformed hierarchy can become a source-distribution theorem; a fixed finite state problem can become a growing-family conditioning problem; a local boundary obstruction can become a two-regime destination test; an ambient sparse-host problem can become a shifted spectral-energy kernel; a positive reserve can become an exact-source compatibility problem; and a nonlocal positive completion can become a canonicality/source-forcing problem. Better constants in the exhausted currency need not move the endpoint at all.

**Boundary.** This synthesis does not imply that the residual carriers are small, easier, rare, mutually equivalent or sufficient for RH. It creates no transfer theorem between the lines. It is a program-level test for whether the next theorem is being asked in the mathematical currency that remains after the current exact obstructions and controls.
