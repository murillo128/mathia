# MI-013 — Closing the bulk often exposes a thinner source-specific carrier with a different mathematical currency

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current local minds; not a theorem that every RH route has this form. The listed reductions are exact only within their owning lines and stated hypotheses.

Several current lines have exhausted a broad generic mechanism and exposed a qualitatively different residual object rather than a smaller copy of the original problem.

Arithmetic Fidelity is now the sharpest topological example. AF-308 makes the exact-zero frequency cone Lebesgue-null; AF-309 proves the same cone is dense and has dense complement. Thus neither ambient prevalence nor perturbative stability decides the prime-log case: the residual carrier is exact arithmetic incidence of a structured vector in a dense null set.

Farey Discrepancy separates exact recoverability from normalized capacity. FD-086 makes frontier blocks projectively coherent, and FD-087 shows every integer dilation contains a uniformly conditioned exact lower-state copy. Yet every common anchor forced at or below the additive block length lives in one terminal sector whose normalized frontier energy vanishes. The residual carrier is bulk placement of transported mass, not existence or conditioning of an exact copy.

Möbius Cancellation gives a normalization analogue. MC-257 removes the wild square conductor, but MC-258 shows that the descended squarefree projector inherits an exact exterior `1/R` factor that preserves the support exponent. The residual carrier is analytic cancellation at the smaller conductor or genuinely localized tame coupling, not the conductor reduction itself.

Prime Circle closes two large normalized scalar regimes. PC-277 matches subexponential-band normalized near-resonance to a non-prime exact-`Li` denominator control and makes the far rational endpoint exactly resonant for every denominator-`q` source. The residual carrier is an intermediate rational-microscopic window, or non-scalar/singular information outside that observable.

Prime Flute provides an operator-side version. PF-317 localizes the direct angle to three local mixed-frequency offsets, and PF-318 shows weak trace needs only a Hilbert--Schmidt mean-square leakage bound at logarithmic target rank, not reciprocal-prime control of every singular direction. Once global reassembly is removed, the residual currency changes from worst-case operator norm to aggregate local leakage.

Nyman--Beurling turns an infinite-depth floor question into finite visible geometry. NB-085 isolates the radial Szegő floor charge; NB-086 lower-bounds it by the normalized Gram determinant deficit of the terminal visible innovations. The residual carrier is now terminal correlation entropy: divergence of normalized visible pair-correlation energy already kills a fixed-ray certificate without estimating the spectral floors individually.

Robin Extremal narrows the low-depth carrier relationally. RE-088 makes sufficiently deep selected boundaries threshold-negligible; RE-089 shows that, above the `3/5` frontier, every fixed same-depth higher-layer chamber family is threshold-negligible too, including pure second-layer chambers. What survives is first-layer contact or finite mixed-depth/tied selection compatibility, not another tail count.

Weil Inertia replaces a failed generic order structure by a source-specific one. WI-268 rules out ordinary positivity preservation; WI-269 nevertheless derives a PSD multiplier family and an uncancelled nodal compensation tariff from the actual first-crossing equation. The residual carrier is whether long-range archimedean compensation can screen all prime-lag overlap while satisfying that whole source-conditioned family.

Weil Positivity closes a superficially promising global scalar escape. WP-280 shows that retaining the Chebyshev primitive really recovers the missing Gamma-center scalar, but only through a normalized limit or eventual sign condition equivalent to RH. The residual carrier is therefore an independently selected non-scalar/global completion architecture, not a stronger scalar primitive asymptotic.

These examples support a common diagnostic: **after a bulk mechanism is closed, identify the exact carrier and the new currency before strengthening the old estimate.** The surviving obstruction may be arithmetic incidence, normalized sector capacity, analytic cancellation after compensation, a microscopic window, mean-square leakage, correlation entropy, mixed-depth selection, source-specific PSD overlap, or independently forced global coupling. Better constants in the exhausted bulk currency need not touch it.

**Boundary.** This synthesis does not identify a transfer theorem between these objects and does not imply that the residual carriers are easier or rare on the arithmetic source. It is a program-level warning against continuing to optimize a mechanism after the destination has moved to a mathematically different carrier.
