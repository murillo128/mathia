**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current local minds and canonical findings. Each reduction below is exact only in its owning line; this is not a theorem that all RH routes share one endpoint structure.

The durable rule is to re-identify the **type of the residual** after every exact reduction. In the current corpus the endpoint has moved from stable incidence to an unconditional joint algebraic-dependence problem; generic/higher-index/finite-order coherence to nonlocal or growing-complexity source structure; scalar decoding to localization-forced directional leverage; fixed-rank state geometry to a growing-rank endpoint-extrapolation transition; prime-specific separation to RH-conditional collapse; normalized translation algebra to one raw two-mode seam-width PDE comparison; generic spectral/ambient forcing coercivity to source-generated range or first-order forcing geometry; coarse finite-edge PNT recovery to sharp `Theta`-scale selected-source saturation; support topology to growing-family restricted spectral criticality; and pole cancellation/coarse scalar recovery to non-scalar recovery architecture.

**Boundary.** These residual currencies are not equivalent and need not be easier. This synthesis exists to prevent stale mathematical questions from surviving after the evidence has changed.
