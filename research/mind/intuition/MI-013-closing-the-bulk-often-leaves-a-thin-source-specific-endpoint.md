# MI-013 — Closing a universal bulk often leaves a thinner source-specific endpoint

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current local minds and canonical findings. The reductions are exact only in their owning lines; no theorem is asserted that the mechanisms transfer between objects.

A recurring pattern is sharper than “the endpoint gets thinner.” After a broad analytic, geometric or operator obstruction is closed, the remaining problem often changes category: from bulk regularity to a source-specific sign, from essential spectrum to finitely many modes, from local selector data to a canonical global average, or from weak-family calibration to an exact conditional law. Continuing to strengthen the solved bulk variable then attacks the wrong resource.

Prime Lattice is now the cleanest positive example. PL-322 uses conservative endpoint flux to force the critical coefficient and residual moment; PL-323 turns the source-coupled residual equation `2Qm+Hm->0` into `Qm->0` by coercive comparison; PL-321 completes the moving Green transfer. The analytic moving-diagonal gate is closed for the actual branch. The remaining problem is the **arithmetic orientation** of the rank-one endpoint datum: a rational-prime sign/first-crossing theorem, not more generic endpoint smoothness.

Weil Inertia shows a different reduction of obstruction dimension. For the frozen reserve, WI-304--WI-306 produce a compact-invariant essential negative band at the first active prime. WI-307 exploits the unbounded archimedean symbol to raise the admissible reserve; at each fixed aperture the essential spectrum can be moved positive and the residual negative sector becomes finite/discrete. The live question is no longer “can any compact repair remove the essential band?” but whether source-exact remainder information controls those finitely many modes and whether the reserve cost remains viable as aperture grows.

Robin Extremal turns historical source dependence into one canonical scalar average. RE-153 shows that the self-tangent height kernel converges on multiplicative scale to a Pareto law and forces a `sqrt(2)` lower bound on the corresponding Chebyshev-deficit average. Sublinear selector neighborhoods carry vanishing kernel mass. The endpoint is therefore not a stronger local prime estimate but a selected multiplicative propagation theorem for exactly that average.

Nyman--Beurling shows how a bulk cancellation can expose a sign endpoint rather than remove the cost. NB-148 cancels the universal Hadamard logarithm with a zero-mass sampler, but exports equal negative Poisson mass. NB-149 shows that generic Jordan/absolute-value control of that negative lobe restores the same positive logarithmic budget. The remaining resource is location-sensitive or genuinely signed control after cancellation.

Arithmetic Fidelity supplies the completion version. AF-366--AF-368 progressively remove finite-prefix and scalar-counting descriptions from the list of sufficient source identifiers. Even extremely accurate `O(log x)` generalized-integer counting does not identify the ordinary-prime source. The thin endpoint is structural arithmetic provenance: exact support, cancellation, continuation/functional coupling or a genuinely stronger rigidity regime.

Visual Exploration and Weil Positivity give calibration/category endpoints. VIS-245 completes the fixed-parameter covariance block but leaves the nonlinear same-sample conditional nuisance law. WP-322 closes exact finite scalar Pick positivity as universal Gram geometry, leaving only non-Pick support-sensitive scalar invariants or a change to matrix/operator/global structure.

The common diagnostic is therefore: **after a closure theorem, rewrite the frontier in the new minimal variable before investing in stronger estimates of the old one**. Ask what obstruction remains, whether it is lower-dimensional, sign-sensitive, source-selective or category-changing, and which theorem could actually move it.

**Boundary.** “Thin endpoint” is an organizational pattern, not a claim that the remaining gates are easy or equivalent. In several lines the thinner endpoint may be the genuinely RH-hard part.
