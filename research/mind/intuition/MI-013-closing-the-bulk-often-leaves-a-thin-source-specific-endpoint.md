# MI-013 — Closing a bulk mechanism changes both the endpoint currency and its required scale

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current local minds and canonical findings through AF-347, FD-128, MC-298, NB-127, PC-297, RE-129, VIS-225, WI-294 and WP-307, together with unchanged line evidence. Each reduction is exact only in its owning line; no theorem is asserted that these mechanisms transfer between lines.

A recurring pattern is now more precise than “the endpoint gets thinner.” After a broad representation or control is closed, the surviving resource often changes **and acquires a scale requirement**. A source property can be genuine yet useless because its order, density, resolution or transport margin does not grow with the destination.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the clearest composition example. AF-346 shows that sign variation and the forced prime-gap concentration trade can be saturated by prime-independent perfect-power backgrounds. AF-347 then shows that a visibly stronger raw locality contrast is still not enough: finite Riesz smoothing of order `k-1` can make the sparse weighted `k`-power background bounded-error indistinguishable from dense mass on exact-power cutoffs. The endpoint currency is not “more locality” but a source functional with a theorem showing that the actual downstream map preserves or consumes it.

Farey Discrepancy sharpens arithmetic-rank language. FD-127 makes every fixed finite set of exact Möbius prime directions cheap; FD-128 makes even an infinite ancestry set cheap when its primes are sparse enough that the free core and semigroup inverse remain controllable. The relevant variable is therefore **prime breadth at the active horizon**, not finite versus infinite cardinality.

Möbius Cancellation supplies the corresponding order barrier. MC-298 shows that for Page cardinality `m`, every fixed overlap order `r<m` can be moment-matched while parity changes. The endpoint cannot be reached by appending finitely many more overlap moments. It needs interaction order that scales with the selected family, an arithmetic identity collapsing parity, or a different collective source-capacity theorem.

Nyman--Beurling separates exact identifiability from stable inversion. NB-127 proves that finitely many multiplicatively independent geometric bases can remove exact ordinate aliases while still admitting arbitrarily high simultaneous near-aliases. Their finite recurrence data return arbitrarily close as Burnol absolute-height mass tends to zero. The missing currency is a quantitative phase modulus tied to height/cutoff, growing sampling complexity, or actual-zero rigidity—not merely “more bases.”

Prime Circle moves the same issue into operator resolution. PC-297 shows that under diffuse averaging the whole one-profile boundary-log matrix approaches a square-zero class in operator norm. Singular channels remain order one, eigenvalues collapse, and every fixed-scale pseudospectrum becomes universal. Any source-specific nonnormal observable must resolve the vanishing defect scale or retain relational information outside that one-profile quotient.

Robin Extremal is a positive example of successfully aligning currencies. RE-127 had signed cancellation in a wider annulus while RE-128 had magnitude at inverse-window scale. RE-129 uses the dominant packet atom to make the Gevrey Taylor error target-relative, forcing spacing, comparable magnitude and cancelling phase into the same inverse-window companion. The remaining question is now genuinely external zero geometry rather than an internal mismatch of estimates.

Visual Exploration similarly closes a scale question by preserving the right structure. VIS-225 does not need finer continuous phase optimization: unresolved near-ratio prime blocks already have one-signed couplings, and block-constant binary signs force energy above Haar RMS by a diverging factor throughout the strictly subcritical regime. The next currency is transition/optimizer/access information, not a stronger static relaxation.

Weil Inertia shows why constraints must survive relaxation. WI-294 applies the form to the **actual nonnegative null vector**. Its pole term has the opposite sign from the odd directions used by the unrestricted spectral-bottom relaxation, and Fourier uncertainty forces a positive support-measure floor. That one sign constraint is enough, with the PNT, to close the large symmetric two-island branch that rare-host spectral analysis left open. Enlarging the admissible class had created an escape unavailable to the real object.

Weil Positivity closes a coordinate family by finding its invariant. WP-307 passes from raw asymptotic coefficients to `J=[s^2]log(sF(s))`; translation leaves `J` unchanged and positive density-normalized dilation scales it by `a^2`. Since the Mangoldt source has `J>0`, the entire positive affine orbit has the wrong Gamma curvature sign. The new finite--archimedean structure must change the invariant, not retune the same coordinates.

The unchanged lines fit the same discipline without being forced into one mechanism: Analytic Frontier needs joint placement relative to its carrier; Prime Lattice needs finite-`s` corner matching; Prime Flute needs a new split beyond the noncompact hard cutoff; Xi Flow needs global support discovery/access rather than better local packet conditioning.

The common diagnostic is now: **what quotient did the theorem control, at what scale can cost still escape, which intervening map preserves the surviving coordinate, and does the endpoint charge it uniformly?** A stronger estimate in the old variable is useful only if those four links are proved. Otherwise the apparent progress can be absorbed by a matched control, a sparse/near-alias limit, a relaxation, or a downstream compression.

**Boundary.** The endpoint currencies above are not interchangeable. This is an evidence-backed research accounting principle, not a cross-line theorem or a claim that one line's construction should be transplanted into another.
