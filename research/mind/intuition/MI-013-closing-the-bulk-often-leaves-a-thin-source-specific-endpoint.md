# MI-013 — Closing the bulk often changes the mathematical currency of the endpoint

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from the current local minds and canonical findings. The reductions below are exact only inside their owning lines and hypotheses; this is not a theorem that every RH route has the same shape.

Several mature lines now show a stronger pattern than “the obstruction gets smaller.” Once a broad mechanism is closed, the surviving object often lives in a different mathematical category. Continuing to improve the old bulk estimate then attacks the wrong currency.

Arithmetic Fidelity has moved from exact singular repair to finite-height source conditioning. AF-320 repairs eight-prime middle-singular recovery and AF-321 shows relation rank has zero uniform margin. AF-322 now proves that each fixed prime support nevertheless has a polynomial finite-height exclusion modulus near the deeper singular geometry. The endpoint is exact incidence plus support-uniform/destination-sufficient conditioning, not another recovery identity.

Farey Discrepancy has moved from physical horizon descent to finite source-neutral inventory. FD-099 shows long finite contraction chains can preserve the sampled quotient; FD-100 proves every exact carrier genealogy spends a strict row potential and eventually reaches the source-moving head. The endpoint is lifting that inventory charge to adaptive energy packets and accumulating source refresh, not proving more contractive branches.

Möbius Cancellation has moved from tensor geometry to one phase-sensitive target coefficient. MC-270 closes arbitrary-coefficient operator bounds; MC-271--MC-272 show that even flat-vector-preserving positive generating majorants and raw circle norms remain above the blind atom. The endpoint is signed/angular coefficient extraction or direct off-diagonal cancellation, not another positive norm.

Nyman--Beurling has crossed from pairwise to genuinely local collective geometry. NB-098 exposes three-state shear; NB-099 proves that high ordinate, standard zeta symmetries, simplicity and global zero-count sparsity can all coexist with the synthetic obstruction. The endpoint is a local zeta constraint, a different symmetry/confluence representation or a target that avoids uniform conditioning—not stronger global counting.

Prime Circle has moved beyond both scalar boundary extremes. PC-282 makes the divergent logarithmic boundary term an exact selector; PC-283 shows the canonically selector-subtracted full-domain remainder is a classical digamma/Green kernel. The endpoint is critically coupled two-sided finite-section placement or architecture beyond one source-independent scalar profile, not a new scalar boundary multiplier.

Prime Flute has moved from one critical witness to a measure-theoretic destination test. PF-326--PF-327 preserve localization and pre-pant critical energy; PF-328 shows the critical centers occupy a positive-length interval with explicit parity seam scales. The endpoint is suppression or survival of **almost all** that family after pant/projection/completion/final normalization, not another statement about a distinguished center.

Robin Extremal has now closed the current local support-boundary depth hierarchy. RE-102 shows that above the new threshold, almost every surviving chamber has first-layer contact on both sides and pulls back to a consecutive-prime gap. The endpoint is nonlocal/source-conditioned assembly of those locally ordinary chambers, not another depth-two/depth-three host count.

Visual Exploration has moved twice inside the one-cancellation layer. VIS-209 first packages all shell multiplicity into one PSD anchor Gram kernel. VIS-210 then proves that in the thin-window cone `L_tri>5R-5` the anchor supports are disjoint and that Gram is exactly diagonal, with only an explicit `6/5` resonance surviving in the adjacent fringe. The endpoint there is diagonal shell-energy control, not shell multiplicity or even generic spectral spreading; outside the cone the explicitly stratified resonance geometry determines which broader Gram estimates are relevant.

Weil Positivity has been squeezed from both target and source ends. WP-288 shows any exact continuous Weil-square functional satisfying the forced quadratic identities linearizes to a translation-invariant distribution and equals the Weil distribution if it matches all squares. WP-289 shows the non-tempered critical Mangoldt covariance cannot be monotonically preserved as a positive summand inside any continuous positive completion. The endpoint is a signed/non-monotone source-native transformation before final positive quadratic readout, together with an exact target-identification theorem.

The durable rule is: after a bulk mechanism is closed, re-identify both the **mathematical type** and the **provenance/metric requirement** of the residual before strengthening anything. Exact recovery may become finite-height conditioning; horizon descent may become finite inventory plus source refresh; a large sieve may become phase-sensitive one-vector cancellation; global zero density may become local collective geometry; scalar boundary sharpness may become finite-section placement; a point witness may become a positive-measure destination test; local channel estimates may become nonlocal assembly; shell multiplicity may become a Gram problem and then exact support geometry; and positive completion may require signed source transformation before positivity can even be read.

**Boundary.** This synthesis does not imply that the residual carriers are easier, mutually equivalent or sufficient for RH. It creates no transfer theorem between lines. Its purpose is to prevent work from continuing in a mathematical currency that current exact reductions have already exhausted.
