# MI-013 — Closing a bulk mechanism often changes the mathematical currency of the endpoint

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current local minds and canonical findings. Each reduction below is exact only in its owning line; this is not a theorem that all RH routes share one endpoint structure.

Arithmetic Fidelity has moved past stable separation and finite exponential-sum ideal certificates. AF-328 makes the eight-prime singular target a coprime common-zero problem; AF-329 removes every positive joint value margin; AF-330 forces an exact hit onto the exponentially algebraic time locus; and AF-331 shows that locus is countable **and dense** with dense complement. The residual currency is therefore pointwise arithmetic incidence of the prime subgroup with `V(e_1,e_4)`, not conditioning, neighborhood separation, measure/category genericity or proximity to a thin exceptional set.

Farey Discrepancy has localized the critical loss to the terminal `H/p` prime fan. FD-107 shows generic coherent high-mass shell geometry can saturate the endpoint. FD-108 restores the exact physical coefficient law but first-order prime-divisibility energy is still power-critical. FD-109 then shows that raw higher-order simultaneous divisibility remains flat: the coefficient cube is tensor rank one and the cumulative hierarchy is only a commuting product-depth resolvent. The residual must use nonlinear, ordered, signed or cross-horizon physical source structure, or make actual source descents pay the selection tax.

Möbius Cancellation has separated algebraic normalization complexity, scalar accuracy and arithmetic source depth. MC-278 shows one low-rational-degree endpoint decoder hides its cost near Walsh depth `k/2`; MC-279 proves a positivity-driven half-depth barrier; MC-280 closes the signed full-cube pointwise scalar loophole at the live `1/C` atom-sensitive accuracy by forcing all available low-depth Fourier variation to `o(1)`. The residual is cross-row signed aggregation before scalarization, restriction to the actual arithmetic image, or a source-native operator/inverse.

Nyman--Beurling has split terminal geometry into volume depth, target-access depth, state-subspace rank and realized leverage. NB-107 gives the sharp rank-one tradeoff `sup_(eta>=theta) chi=1-theta+o(1)` after complete backfill. NB-108 extends both target-orthogonality and the same Pareto frontier to every fixed-dimensional confluent/Jordan state packet. The residual is no longer fixed repeated-zero geometry: one must force near-maximal state participation or enter genuinely growing-rank / several-distinct-zero state geometry.

Prime Circle has changed logical direction as well as scale. PC-285 pushes unconditional Li-grid reproduction to the Vinogradov--Korobov source-transport range. PC-286 shows that under RH the exact one-profile boundary-log spectrum collapses to the Li-grid control for `B=o(sqrt(q)/(log q)^6)`. The residual is no longer simply “find prime-specific separation”: in this architecture RH itself predicts loss of prime specificity almost to the rational square-root scale.

Prime Lattice has moved from generic perturbative instability to a coupled source-specific regularity problem. PL-300 shows spectral stability does not transport BV. PL-301 proves the actual Suzuki remainder is BV-stable, excluding the generic rough high-frequency injection. PL-302 proves the finite prime shift comb nevertheless ceases to be contractive once the `n=3` channel activates. The residual is therefore coupled principal-plus-shifts BV/derivative-measure coercivity on the tracked ground state, not Kato theory or a standalone shift norm.

Robin Extremal has moved from corridor length to selected one-sided **packet** workload. RE-109 prices recovery by the PNT-regular event source; RE-110 forces one-sided envelope-scale workload; RE-111 shows that near-minimal recovery cannot be paid by one extremal event. It requires a positive-mass packet of ordinary first-layer primes with the same favorable sign and PNT-envelope-order Chebyshev deficit. The residual is a CA-selection-conditioned packet anti-saturation theorem or a coupled source coordinate that destroys such packets.

Weil Inertia has crossed from support topology to support-restricted spectral criticality. WI-283 proves that qualitative support domination still saturates the packing relaxation; WI-284 shows that complete one-signed screening deletes the prime operator on the whole selected support subspace, forcing the prime-deleted archimedean form to be PSD with bottom exactly zero there. The residual is a support-sensitive archimedean spectral obstruction, not another packing inequality.

Weil Positivity now separates cancellation, admissibility, localization and recovery architecture. WP-291 shows every finite canonical dilation-coboundary product preserves Mangoldt localization but remains RH-equivalent in temperedness. WP-292 obtains unconditional category descent by counting convolution while erasing prime-power localization. WP-293 proves that exact scalar source-native relocalization is uniquely Möbius and therefore necessarily restores `1/zeta` and the zero screen. The residual must relax exactness, one-channel scalar arithmetic convolution, or both.

The durable rule is to re-identify the **type of the residual** after every exact reduction. In the current corpus the endpoint has moved from stability to pointwise algebraic incidence, generic/higher-index coherence to nonlinear ordered source structure, scalar decoder formulae to pre-scalar signed provenance, fixed-rank state geometry to growing-rank coercivity, prime-specific separation to RH-conditional collapse, generic functional calculus to explicit frequency-off-diagonal pant locality, spectral perturbation theory to coupled BV coercivity, corridor length to selected same-sign saturation packets, support topology to restricted spectral criticality, and pole cancellation to non-exact/non-scalar localization recovery.

**Boundary.** These residual currencies are not equivalent and need not be easier. This synthesis exists to prevent stale mathematical questions from surviving after the evidence has changed.
