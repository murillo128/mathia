# MI-013 — Closing the bulk often exposes a thinner source-specific carrier with a different mathematical currency

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current local minds; not a theorem that every RH route has this form. Each reduction below is exact only within its owning line and stated hypotheses.

Several mature lines now show the same program-level pattern: once a broad generic mechanism is audited hard enough, the surviving problem is often not a smaller instance of that mechanism. It becomes a thinner source-selected object whose relevant currency has changed.

Arithmetic Fidelity is an incidence example. Generic topology, integer-character rank and Baker-type algebraic-linear relations do not decide the first five-prime hit. Post-incidence structure is nevertheless rigid: five-point hits have exact low-harmonic recovery and a one-complex-coordinate shape quotient. [AF-315](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-315-six-point-zero-shapes-need-three-real-harmonic-coordinates.md) gives the next threshold: six-point hits are still recoverable from `(s_2,s_3)`, but their generic rotation quotient is real three-dimensional and needs one complex invariant plus one real modulus. Prime arithmetic removes the obvious low-harmonic singular strata without touching first incidence. The residual carrier is therefore **first nontorsion incidence of the prime-log orbit**, not missing post-hit coordinates.

Farey Discrepancy has moved beyond occupation geometry, exact lower representation and predecessor count. Odd repeated-prime channels are exactly reembedded into lower nonsquarefree states with summable normalized prime weights. [FD-093](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-093-dyadic-terminal-obstruction-collapses-to-the-single-row-two-head-atom.md) corrects the former dyadic terminal picture: every nonhead terminal coordinate is also exactly repairable, and the irreducible universal defect is one row-two/source-head atom. The residual carrier is now **persistent mass concentration on that single source value or failure of frontier-sized repaired masks to compose at fixed lower-state strength**, not a broad dyadic squarefree sector.

Möbius Cancellation has similarly moved past generic conductor descent and ambient large-sieve balance. The live blind support occurs at order `t~log log y`, outside the useful black-box growing-order range. [MC-264](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-264-support-matched-high-moment-blind-threshold.md) shows that this is specifically a low-moment obstruction: the correctly priced `2t`-th moment is still too weak, while the `2(t+1)`-th moment crosses the single-blind-atom threshold with large quantitative slack. The remaining analytic carrier is **the off-diagonal Krawtchouk distance distribution of the fixed-weight shell-product tensor family**, or the alternative logarithmic all-class generation/source-character route, not generic all-moduli pseudorandomness.

Weil Inertia gives an operator endpoint. On a one-signed first-crossing branch, source order converts an internal gap into a prime-overlap tariff. [WI-273](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-273-positive-tail-hankel-norm-sharpens-gap-screening-but-hits-the-carleman-wall.md) replaces the previous scalar positive-tail budget by the exact positive-source Hankel norm and visible collar masses, strictly sharpening every positive-width gap. But the norm tends exactly to `pi/2` as the gap closes, matching the half-Carleman wall. The residual carrier is therefore **structure discarded by a global mass-only `L^2` norm**—simultaneous cut constraints, local null-mode structure or the full distributional equation—not another refinement of the same norm.

Nyman--Beurling now shows the complementary transition from a silent fixed-depth observable to a source-sensitive growing-depth one. Fixed-ratio Gram bands can remain calibrated and source-silent under summable horizontal zero mass, but [NB-092](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-092-growing-depth-mobius-annulus-mode-forces-polynomial-blaschke-gram-gain.md) constructs an exact weighted-Möbius annulus direction on which false RH both creates a raw near-kernel and forces polynomial Blaschke amplification. The residual carrier has moved again: it is **whether that relative generalized Gram gain survives the diagonal normalization or enters a target-lower-bounding inter-scale statistic**, not whether growing depth contains source information at all.

Robin Extremal makes source conditioning quantitative. Ambient bad-start measure, exact mixed-depth incidence and paired gap magnitudes all stop at the depth-two `173/200` information frontier. [RE-095](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-095-source-conditioned-sampled-gap-sparsity-lowers-the-depth-two-moment-frontier.md) shows that the next carrier is much thinner: the prime hosts `q` whose deformed quadratic sample `x_q` has a prime-free one-sided interval. Any fixed power saving in the number of these bad hosts lowers the frontier, while repackaging an ambient exceptional set cannot. The residual currency is therefore **source-conditioned host sparsity**, not a stronger theorem about arbitrary interval starts.

Other lines show the same change-of-currency effect in different categories. Weil Positivity has ruled out finite stable response and completely monotone positive relaxation as ways to remove the zero field while retaining the Gamma/DC component, pushing the problem toward independently forced oscillatory or pre-scalar finite--archimedean coupling. Prime Flute separates raw seam-block amplification from the intrinsic positive-form angle, leaving the shear-deleted physical angle rather than the already-controlled shear perturbation. Prime Lattice has reached explicit generic regularity and positivity thresholds, leaving zeta-specific boundary cancellation or source-specific sign selection.

The durable rule is: **after a bulk mechanism is closed, identify the exact residual carrier and its new currency before strengthening the old estimate.** A broad terminal sector may collapse to one atom; a family-size barrier may disappear at the support-matched moment order; a divergent scalar bound may become finite under the exact operator norm yet still hit a sharp wall; a fixed-depth null signal may become a growing-depth relative gain; an ambient exceptional set may be irrelevant when only a sparse prime-indexed host family matters; exact reconstruction may remain possible while the minimal quotient dimension increases. Better constants in the exhausted bulk currency need not touch any of these residuals.

**Boundary.** This synthesis does not identify a transfer theorem between the listed objects and does not imply that the residual carriers are easier, rare, or sufficient for RH. It is a program-level discipline for deciding what mathematical object the next theorem must actually control.
