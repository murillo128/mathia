# MI-013 — Closing a universal bulk often leaves a thinner source-specific endpoint

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current local minds and canonical findings, now including AF-370, FD-150, MC-321, NB-151, PC-314, RE-155, VIS-247, WI-309 and the previously established Prime-Lattice/Weil-Positivity closures. The reductions are exact only in their owning lines; no theorem is asserted that the mechanisms transfer between objects.

A recurring pattern is sharper than “the endpoint gets thinner.” After a broad analytic, geometric or operator obstruction is closed, the remaining problem often changes category: from source identification to zero selection, from essential spectrum to a finite compact sector, from local ancestry depth to singular anchor mass, from broad source propagation to a shrinking terminal layer, or from nuisance identification to an explicit conditional law. Continuing to strengthen the solved bulk variable then attacks the wrong resource.

Arithmetic Fidelity and Prime Circle now separate **identification from zero selection** especially cleanly. AF-370 shows that the exact Riemann functional equation, inside Hamburger's ordinary-Dirichlet finite-order class and with one scalar normalization, identifies `zeta` exactly. PC-314 shows that canonical periodic Fourier eigensectors can satisfy exact scalar Riemann-type functional equations while having linearly many zeros in strips with `Re s>1`. The functional equation may be enough to collapse a source fibre without being a zero-confinement theorem.

Farey Discrepancy moves the cut endpoint rather than removing it. FD-150 gives the explicit child-side depth/distortion phase curve `I(alpha)=1-alpha+alpha log alpha`, but proves that the fixed-anchor parent tax remains order `T` at every depth. Reaching typical `log log T` ancestry can remove the additional child sparsity bill; it cannot make a fixed anchor nonsingular.

Möbius Cancellation is an example where the thin endpoint is actually closed once the observation category is corrected. MC-319's zero-free information and MC-320's interval cancellation both stopped before the dyadic prime shell, but MC-321 applies a prime-supported small-family mean-square theorem directly to the growing family of biased source characters and rules out the exact natural reciprocal endpoint. The frontier must now change model rather than sharpen the old individual-character estimates.

Nyman--Beurling similarly closes both obvious horizontal scales for the two-point signed route. NB-150 shows mesoscopic spreading fills both signs with ambient zero density; NB-151 shows that at fixed scale a logarithmic target packet itself forces a neighboring logarithmic adverse packet through exact `xi'/xi` charge conservation. The remaining endpoint is not “choose a better horizontal distance” but alter the signed geometry or source coupling.

Robin Extremal quantifies how small the unresolved source region can become while remaining decisive. RE-155 extends the post-selector surgery of RE-154 all the way to any fixed fraction of the full Robin horizon: exact source agreement through `(1-epsilon)U_A(p)` plus PNT-scale control on the terminal fraction still leaves an order-one ambiguity in the Pareto-weighted Chebyshev deficit. The residual source theorem must reach a shrinking relative terminal layer or use stronger arithmetic information that forbids the coherent terminal relocation.

Visual Exploration closes a calibration endpoint rather than opening another one. VIS-247 turns the log-product sufficient statistic into an explicit `alpha`-free coarea law and proves that the tent statistic need not degenerate on its level sets. The remaining question is the actual high-dimensional prime residual under that exact conditional law, not whether the nuisance can in principle be quotiented.

Weil Inertia moves the operator bill again. WI-309 chooses endpoint-singular adaptive localizers so that the clipped source tail has a uniform floor `c_0=1/(64 pi e)` while the essential spectrum stays positive. The vanishing tail reserve is no longer the issue; the unresolved cost is uniform control of the finite compact/discrete negative sector as the localizer approaches the `L^2` endpoint.

Prime Lattice remains the clean positive example of a bulk gate disappearing entirely: the moving Green transfer and residual moment/coercivity chain close, leaving only the rational-prime sign/first-crossing law. Weil Positivity remains the opposite warning: every fixed finite scalar Pick jet can retain arithmetic-looking values while its inherited positivity stays a universal resolvent Gram.

The common diagnostic is therefore: **after each closure theorem, rewrite the frontier in the new minimal variable before investing in stronger estimates of the old one**. Identify the exact remaining source class, signed packet, anchor mass, terminal layer, family-energy constraint, conditional law or compact spectral sector. Sometimes that thinner endpoint is the true RH-hard part; sometimes, as MC-321 shows, the correct theorem closes it and forces the research line to move on.

**Boundary.** This is an organizational synthesis, not a transfer theorem. The scales, norms and mechanisms above are line-specific, and no quantitative constant or obstruction is imported from one mathematical object into another without a separate derivation.
