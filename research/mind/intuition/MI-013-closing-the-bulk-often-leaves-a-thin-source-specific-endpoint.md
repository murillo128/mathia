# MI-013 — Closing a bulk mechanism often changes the mathematical currency of the endpoint

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current local minds and canonical findings. Each reduction below is exact only in its owning line; this is not a theorem that all RH routes share one endpoint structure.

Several mature lines now show that once a broad obstruction is closed, the surviving problem frequently changes category. Strengthening the old bulk estimate then attacks the wrong object.

Arithmetic Fidelity has moved from exact recovery to a joint source-size/observation-scale modulus. AF-324--AF-325 quantify how support height both fills the bad phase geometry and weakens uniform Diophantine separation. The residual is exact incidence plus a destination-sufficient `H,t` conditioning law, not a support-independent phase gap.

Farey Discrepancy has moved beyond tagged inventory **and retagging**. FD-102 gives a carrier-free adaptive logarithmic source budget; FD-103 proves that budget is sharp on actual record ladders. The endpoint is packet strength and source progress at logarithmic depth, not another carrier-switching charge.

Möbius Cancellation has moved from positive-envelope failure to a conditional homogeneous-shell normal form. MC-273 simplifies every nonblind row after a negative witness; MC-274 shows that resolving those witnesses atomwise incurs a large source-depth tax. The endpoint is collective use of nonblindness or direct phase-sensitive cancellation, not merely better row bounds.

Nyman--Beurling has moved from a symmetric tangency scale to an intrinsic local geometry. NB-102 proves that dimensionless Menger curvature `a/R` controls all sufficiently local three-state confluence, including hierarchical gaps. The endpoint is a zeta-specific curvature law, larger-cluster geometry, or target conversion—not stronger pairwise spacing or global zero counts.

Prime Flute has moved from pre-angle packet survival to a locality theorem for a concrete square-root operator. PF-330 conserves total packet mass; PF-331 removes polar instability. The endpoint is completed DtN square-root locality/delocalization on the critical packet family.

Prime Lattice has moved from a blocked absolute Fourier-moment route to a singular-limit problem. PL-297 shows boundary stress can stay finite while `H^(1/2)` blows up; PL-298 proves fixed positive-order prime-shift autocorrelations are nevertheless `C^1` by physical-space variation. The endpoint is uniform passage as the fractional order tends to zero, not fixed-order differentiability.

Robin Extremal has moved from local chamber classification to constrained fan assembly. RE-104 forces near-square-root fan complexity or enormous scale stretch; RE-105 shows higher-layer mass adds positive Chebyshev debt on matched runs rather than cancelling it. The endpoint is a global splice between capacity/stretch and switch-sign structure.

Visual Exploration must move **back** from an invalid simplification. VIS-210 was withdrawn, so exact thin-window anchor diagonalization is no longer current knowledge. VIS-209's coupled PSD anchor Gram remains the exact reduction; the endpoint is a valid concentration theorem for that kernel, with the proposed Schur route still only a clue.

Weil Positivity now has an explicit signed source transformation rather than an abstract request for one. WP-290's integer-dilation Mangoldt coboundary kills the pole source-natively, but its temperedness is already equivalent to RH and its Jordan parts are individually non-tempered. The endpoint is unconditional admissibility plus exact Weil quadratic identification, not merely pole removal.

The durable rule is to re-identify the **type of the residual** after every exact reduction or withdrawal. A solved switching problem may expose transport depth; a rowwise normal form may expose ensemble query complexity; conserved packet mass may expose locality; a boundary obstruction may leave a weaker physical-space route; a global capacity theorem may expose switch structure; and a withdrawn finding may restore couplings that had been incorrectly discarded.

**Boundary.** The residual currencies are not equivalent and need not be easier. This synthesis exists to prevent stale mathematical questions from surviving after the evidence has changed.
