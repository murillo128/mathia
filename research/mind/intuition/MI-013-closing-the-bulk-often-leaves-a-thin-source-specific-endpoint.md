# MI-013 — Closing a bulk mechanism often changes the mathematical currency of the endpoint

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current local minds and canonical findings. Each reduction below is exact only in its owning line; this is not a theorem that all RH routes share one endpoint structure.

Arithmetic Fidelity has separated an observation theorem from the source resource it consumes. AF-338 proves fixed-order full-Euler sign regularity once `sigma>r-1`, with linear rather than quadratic harmonic depth because tail terms spend collision factors only when harmonic labels differ. AF-339 then shows that the canonical zero-sensitive source `Lambda-1` has sign variation `Theta(P/log P)` on every fixed multiplicative window. The residual currency is therefore **zero-sensitive source compression with audited recovery**, a genuinely growing-order observation theorem, or another finite source certificate—not another fixed-order determinant improvement.

Farey Discrepancy has finished both the information and first coercivity audits of its cumulative field. FD-117 makes the whole field invertibly equivalent to quotient-Mertens data, FD-118 compresses it to the `2 sqrt(N)+O(1)` floor-quotient Fourier skeleton, and FD-119 shows that any fixed true Mertens prefix plus the exact global floor-quotient identity can be grafted onto the skeleton equality ray at only `O_R(1)` energy cost. At critical amplitude `~sqrt(N)` the residual currency is therefore **growing-depth physical source law with critical-scale coercive cost**, not more same-field coordinates or finitely many exact endpoint constraints.

Möbius Cancellation has reduced the collision-rich odd branch to a sharply one-sided arithmetic interface. MC-289 forces a large negative generated product-character bias unless effective occupancy is exponentially small. MC-290 shows that if analytic input controls all but a small exceptional family, the exact odd moments force that family toward the affine endpoint: for `epsilon_m=delta^(m-2)(eta^-1-1)` small and `log J=o(m)`, one exceptional character is `-1` on `1-o(1)` of weighted shell mass. The residual currency is **effective occupancy plus exceptional-character rigidity/conductor control**, not generic Boolean geometry.

Nyman--Beurling has moved twice beyond generic finite-section conditioning. NB-117 identifies finite target access with weighted interpolation of the unique harmonic minimizer `C+D/n` by confluent actual-zero power sums. NB-118 then uses exact orthogonality of every actual zero packet to each fixed finite Nyman generator space: once target capture exceeds the finite residual baseline `d_M^2`, a target-bearing witness can retain only `O_M(1/R)` of its continuum norm in the first `R` cells. For `M=3` the threshold is already about `9.6%`. The residual currency is therefore **source-specific exclusion of strong harmonic-profile interpolation at inverse-section retention**, not merely inverse-height packet cost or another Gram-spectrum estimate.

Prime Circle's one-profile observable has reversed its diagnostic under RH. PC-290 shows that once total source count is matched, exact prime locations give no order-one separation from the Li control for `B=o(sqrt(q))`. The residual is the denominator-microscopic `B~sqrt(q)` regime, the scalar count itself, or a richer observable that retains non-scalar/cross-level information before spectral collapse.

Prime Flute's sharp-cutoff completed-angle branch remains noncompact because adjacent modes straddling the cutoff retain order-one canonical correlations on infinitely many separated modules. The residual is a change of splitting or observable, not a sharper estimate inside the same compactness class.

Prime Lattice has successively removed two artificial analytic gates. PL-307 shows a fixed-domain Pohozaev identity suffices for scalar boundary stress without a mixed Hadamard theorem. PL-308 then proves fixed-`s` Pohozaev admissibility automatically for the actual completed-Weil eigenstate by `mu`-transmission bootstrap and produces an exact weak virial stress. The residual currency is now **uniform `s->0` convergence of the weak virial / source-structured derivative compactness**, followed separately by a rational-prime sign theorem—not fixed-order regularity.

Robin Extremal has closed both leading placement and every fixed algebraic correction under an isolated attained edge. RE-119 shows the regular CA mesh samples the full leading stable profile `J`; RE-120 proves that, with a fixed horizontal zero gap, every coefficient in the `1/log log C` hierarchy is a stable-filter descendant of that same `J`. The residual currency is therefore **near-edge spectral accumulation in a shrinking `O(log nu/nu)` boundary layer, or information outside the stable-filter closure**, not higher fixed-order CA asymptotics.

Weil Inertia has split symmetric two-island complete screening into a large lower-scale reservoir and a rare upper-scale host family. WI-290 gives `X^(3/8-o(1))` composite-safe components from published gap moments; WI-291 confines eligible ordinary-prime Lambert hosts to `X^(1/10+o(1))` gaps and summable logarithmic measure via Heath-Brown's large-gap theorem. The residual is a **cross-scale localization/correlation theorem** between those two arithmetically selected families; their count exponents alone do not interact by pigeonhole.

Weil Positivity has moved beyond “introduce curvature.” WP-300 shows prime-symmetric scalar projective curvature is only flat or universal fermionic geometry. WP-301 shows even arbitrary noncommuting local holonomies collapse under a positive frustration-free zero-mode readout to the intersection of local kernels, while the same projection erases nonzero archimedean spectrum. The residual is **arithmetic geometry whose informative spectrum survives the readout**: moving kernels, boundary/cohomological observables, or non-frustration-free finite--archimedean coupling with an independent sign theorem.

The durable rule is to re-identify the **type of the residual** after every exact reduction. A line may have solved its representation problem only to expose source complexity; solved source abundance only to expose placement; solved fixed-order regularity only to expose singular-limit compactness; or constructed genuine geometry only to discover that the final readout erases it. Continuing to optimize the closed currency is then mathematically inert.

**Boundary.** These residual currencies are not equivalent and need not be easier. This synthesis exists to prevent stale questions from surviving after the evidence has changed; it does not assert a common theorem connecting the line-specific endpoints.
