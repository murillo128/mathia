# MI-013 — Endpoint resources must scale, survive transport, and remain noncircular

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current local minds and canonical findings through AF-348, FD-129, MC-299, NB-128, PC-298, PL-313, RE-130, VIS-226, WI-294 and WP-308, together with unchanged line evidence. Each reduction is exact only in its owning line; no theorem is asserted that these mechanisms transfer between objects.

A recurring pattern is now sharper than “the endpoint gets thinner.” After a broad representation or control is closed, a surviving source resource can still fail in three distinct ways: it may be visible only below the destination's required **order/density/resolution**, it may be **erased by an intervening map**, or the hypothesis used to enforce it may already **contain the endpoint statement**. These failures must be audited separately.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the clean transport example. Perfect-power recodings already saturate sign/gap concentration. AF-348 then proves uniformly over all real cutoffs that finite Riesz smoothing of order `k-1` can make their much stronger raw locality profile bounded-error indistinguishable from dense unit mass. The endpoint currency is not “more source locality”; it is a source functional with a non-erasure theorem for the declared destination operation.

Farey Discrepancy adds the circularity boundary. FD-128 makes even infinite exact prime ancestry cheap when it is sparse at the active scale. FD-129 shows that complete initial-prime ancestry plus a stage-uniform critical prefix envelope goes too far in another sense: it reconstructs `mu` on the growing prefix and transfers the same envelope to `M(x)`. At RH scale the matched-control condition is already RH-equivalent. A useful breadth law must constrain the free source without importing the target.

Möbius Cancellation separates information order from arithmetic source cost. MC-298 proves that any bounded hierarchy of overlap moments can miss high-cardinality Page parity. MC-299 does not repair that information loss; instead, conditionally on a genuinely near-negative Page exception, Deuring--Heilbronn repulsion pushes every other low-bias mode beyond a source-cost band whose logarithmic width is larger by `log log log y`. Arithmetic feedback can therefore tighten the admissible family even when the parity representation itself remains unresolved.

Nyman--Beurling supplies a positive resolution theorem. Fixed finite multibase data are globally unstable, but NB-128 proves that the growing natural prefix has a deterministic inverse phase modulus through a height window of size `Theta(R)`, with `Theta(1/log R)` local resolution. The next failure is downstream: the phase witness is multiplied by the actual primitive amplitude, so phase coercivity must be coupled to target-bearing nonconcentration before it becomes a Burnol-mass resource.

Prime Circle makes the resolution threshold explicit in operator geometry. PC-298 gives a square-zero approximant with error `r_q` and proves that every pseudospectral scale `epsilon_q >> r_q` remains universal. Merely sending `epsilon` to zero does not evade the quotient. Arithmetic information, if present in the same profile, must live at the defect scale or below.

Prime Lattice identifies a complementary successful interface. PL-313 proves that a resolved physical corner reaches the outer Volterra cokernel through one weighted scalar `M_±`, exactly matching the one-dimensional regular range obstruction of PL-312. The channel is now known, but the actual eigenbranch still has to match its singular amplitude strongly enough for that scalar to be finite and source-sensitive. Identifying the right interface is not the same as proving the source crosses it.

Robin Extremal shows how an apparent branch can disappear once the correct norm is priced. RE-130 uses the summable coefficient envelope and zero counting to bound total packet mass by `sqrt(M_K) log(e/M_K)`. Hence an algebraic diffuse cloud without an algebraically strong atom cannot exist. The frontier collapses to the external zero-geometry pair already isolated by RE-129.

Visual Exploration closes a different scale by matching constructions from both sides. VIS-225's coherent binary blocks give the lower bound and VIS-226's absolute tapered envelope gives the matching upper bound `y/(H log y)`. The static optimization hierarchy can change constants but not the coarse subcritical order. The endpoint currencies are now transition behavior, optimizer geometry and dynamical access rather than a stronger relaxation.

Weil Inertia and Weil Positivity retain their previous lessons. WI-294 closes an escape by restoring the actual nonnegative null-vector constraint that the unrestricted spectral relaxation had discarded. WP-307 identifies an affine-covariant curvature invariant, and WP-308 shows the canonical scalar Herglotz projective output gauge cannot evade it: a non-affine Möbius map destroys the logarithmic end, while an end-preserving one is positive affine and keeps the curvature sign. In both Weil lines, respecting the exact admissible/gauge class changes what counts as a live degree of freedom.

The common diagnostic is therefore: **what source coordinate survives, at what scale is it visible, which map transports it, what admissible class is retained, and do the assumptions already imply the endpoint?** A stronger estimate in the old variable is useful only if those links remain independent and quantitative. Otherwise a route can be absorbed by a matched control, a sparse or near-alias limit, a universal quotient, a relaxation, downstream smoothing, or target reconstruction itself.

**Boundary.** These endpoint currencies are not interchangeable and none of the line-specific mechanisms is asserted to transfer automatically. This is an evidence-backed accounting principle for selecting proof obligations, not a cross-line theorem.
