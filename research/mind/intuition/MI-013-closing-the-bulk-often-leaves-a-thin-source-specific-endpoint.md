# MI-013 — Closing a universal bulk often leaves a thinner source-specific endpoint, whose remaining resource can often be priced explicitly

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current local minds and canonical findings, now including AF-371, FD-151, MC-322, NB-152, PC-314, RE-156, VIS-248, WI-310 and the previously established Prime-Lattice/Weil-Positivity closures. The reductions are exact only in their owning lines; no theorem is asserted that the mechanisms transfer between objects.

A recurring pattern is sharper than “the endpoint gets thinner.” After a broad analytic, geometric or operator obstruction is closed, the remaining problem often changes category: from source identification to zero selection, from generic positivity to target-faithful relational positivity, from essential spectrum to a bounded-tail finite compact sector, from local ancestry depth to explicit anchor mass/distortion, from broad source propagation to a quantified shrinking terminal layer, or from nuisance identification to an auditable conditional law. Continuing to strengthen the solved bulk variable then attacks the wrong resource.

Arithmetic Fidelity and Prime Circle separate **identification from zero selection** especially cleanly. AF-370 shows that the exact Riemann functional equation, inside Hamburger's ordinary-Dirichlet finite-order class and with one scalar normalization, identifies `zeta` exactly. PC-314 shows that canonical periodic Fourier eigensectors can satisfy exact scalar Riemann-type functional equations while having linearly many zeros in strips with `Re s>1`. AF-371 adds another separation: even an infinite Hankel-positivity hierarchy can remain target-mixed for real-zero structure, while Schoenberg's ordered translation total positivity is exact. The certificate must therefore be target-faithful, not merely large or positive.

Farey Discrepancy now prices both sides of its cut endpoint. FD-150 gives the child depth/distortion phase curve `I(alpha)=1-alpha+alpha log alpha`. FD-151 shows that for a growing divisor-closed anchor of mass `p_T`, parent expansion obeys `h<=P_T p_T/(1-p_T)`. Bounded parent distortion consequently requires a positive-density anchor; sparse rank anchors pay the same Sathe--Selberg factor as the child-depth tail. Growing the anchor does not remove the endpoint tax—it converts singular parent weighting into direct orientation of more source coefficients.

Möbius Cancellation is an example where the thin endpoint is closed once the observation category is corrected and then quantitatively strengthened. MC-321 applies a prime-supported small-family mean-square theorem directly to the biased source characters. MC-322 promotes that contradiction to an `ell^2` bias-energy budget and extends the fixed-power common-modulus range to every `q<=y^(3-delta)`. The frontier must now escape the energy hypotheses rather than sharpen individual-character zero-free or interval estimates.

Nyman--Beurling similarly removes the apparent many-point loophole. NB-150 handles mesoscopic horizontal separation; NB-151 handles one fixed two-point sampler conditioned on a logarithmic target packet; NB-152 shows that every zero-mass fixed-horizontal signed measure with bounded variation-to-target-charge ratio still forces `Omega(log G)` adverse actual zeros at bounded distance. The surviving resource is now explicit: diverging conditioning, a different scale/vertical geometry, signed prime cancellation, or earlier coupling to Nyman target data.

Robin Extremal quantifies the terminal source endpoint. RE-156 shows that equal-cardinality source relocation in a shrinking terminal layer has capacity

`Cap_p(epsilon) asymp epsilon^2 sqrt(p) log p / log U_A(p)`,

with order-one transition `epsilon_*(p)=sqrt(log U_A(p)/(sqrt(p)log p))`. The matched-source obstruction survives throughout `epsilon/epsilon_*->infinity`; below that scale only this relocation architecture loses order-one leverage. The residual theorem is no longer merely “reach `(1-o(1))U`,” but cross a specific fourth-root capacity boundary or introduce stronger arithmetic coupling.

Visual Exploration closes a calibration endpoint and adds an exact implementation audit. VIS-247 turns the log-product sufficient statistic into an explicit `alpha`-free coarea law and proves that the tent statistic need not degenerate on its level sets. VIS-248 reduces the three-gap quotient to the exact discriminant density `Delta_p(q)^(-1/2)`, giving deterministic one-dimensional quadrature. The remaining question is the actual high-dimensional prime residual, but any numerical conditional implementation should first reproduce that exact low-dimensional law.

Weil Inertia exposes a second kind of representation debt. WI-309 buys a uniform positive retained-tail floor by adapting the localizer. WI-310 shows that keeping the **entire** clipped excess makes that positive summand diverge and therefore forces the complementary comparison to acquire arbitrarily deep discrete negative modes by construction. Capping the retained tail at `1/2` preserves the source identity and uniform lower witness while preventing this overcharge. The honest endpoint is the finite negative sector of the bounded-tail comparison, not the artificial depth of the uncapped complement.

Prime Lattice remains the clean positive example of a bulk gate disappearing entirely: the moving Green transfer and residual moment/coercivity chain close, leaving only the rational-prime sign/first-crossing law. Weil Positivity remains the opposite warning: every fixed finite scalar Pick jet can retain arithmetic-looking values while its inherited positivity stays a universal resolvent Gram. AF-371 suggests the relevant contrast is not finite versus infinite testing but universal Gram/moment positivity versus a relation whose sign is target-selective.

The common diagnostic is therefore: **after each closure theorem, rewrite the frontier in the new minimal variable and price that variable before investing in stronger estimates of the old one**. The relevant currency may be an exact source class, ordered relational minors, anchor mass, family-energy budget, sampler conditioning ratio, terminal relocation capacity, exact null law or bounded-tail compact spectrum. Sometimes that thinner endpoint is the true RH-hard part; sometimes the correct theorem closes it and forces the line to move on.

**Boundary.** This is an organizational synthesis, not a transfer theorem. The scales, norms and mechanisms above are line-specific, and no quantitative constant, positivity criterion or obstruction is imported from one mathematical object into another without a separate derivation.
