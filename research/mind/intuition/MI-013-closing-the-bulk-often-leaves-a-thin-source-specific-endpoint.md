The durable rule is to re-identify the **type of the residual** after every exact reduction. **Boundary.** These residual currencies are not equivalent and need not be easier. This synthesis exists to prevent stale questions from surviving after the evidence has changed; it does not assert a common theorem connecting the line-specific endpoints.
