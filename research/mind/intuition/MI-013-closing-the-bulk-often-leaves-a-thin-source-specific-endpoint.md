# MI-013 — Closing a bulk mechanism often changes the mathematical currency of the endpoint

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current local minds and canonical findings through AF-346, FD-127, MC-297, PC-296, RE-128, VIS-224, WI-293 and WP-306, together with the unchanged line evidence summarized below. Each reduction is exact only in its owning line; this is not a theorem that all RH routes share one endpoint structure.

A recurring pattern is now sharper: once a broad class of representations or controls is closed, the remaining obstruction usually becomes **thinner and more source-specific**, and the currency itself changes. Counting more structure in the old currency then stops helping.

Arithmetic Fidelity is the cleanest concentration example. AF-345 proves that the residual sign statistic factors exactly through occupied prime gaps and obeys a sharp Holder mass-concentration law. AF-346 then constructs simple prime-independent weighted `k`-th-power backgrounds (`k>=3`) that retain the RH pole discriminator while attaining that law at the exponent level and asymptotically at the sharp constant on thin multiplicative windows. The endpoint currency is therefore no longer sign count or the unavoidable one-moment concentration bill; it must be a stronger normalized locality/concentration profile that the destination actually consumes, or a growing-order observation theorem whose constants charge it.

Analytic Frontier has crossed the analogous one-point-statistics boundary. Its deterministic critical high-pass has the correct physical width, yet source-matched controls can preserve alphabet, densities, mean, diagonal scale and local absolute mass while placing signs relative to the logarithmic carrier so as to retain endpoint memory. The residual currency is joint arithmetic placement relative to that distinguished carrier, not stronger marginals.

Farey Discrepancy now locates the surviving source resource at **growing arithmetic rank**. FD-125--FD-126 show that repeated-square horizons admit one permanent squarefree unit source with critical saturation. FD-127 adds the exact Möbius sign law in any prescribed fixed finite set of prime directions without destroying the escape. Thus “some multiplicativity” is still too cheap: the first plausible coercive input must expand with scale—`P=P(H)`, full coprime multiplicativity, or an equivalent growing exact identity family.

Möbius Cancellation has similarly moved from basis cost to subset-spectrum geometry. MC-296 forces almost all selected subset combinations Page-high-cost in the disjoint endpoint model. MC-297 shows that shell coverage was never the load-bearing condition: pairwise disjointness suffices exactly, and overlap enters through the explicit nonnegative parity-folding correction `4 Omega_I`. Lower-half modes cannot be rescued by overlap at all; upper-half cheap modes must spend pair/multi-overlap. The endpoint currency is now either arithmetic overlap control or a collective theorem limiting realization of the resulting exponential family of high-cost characters.

Nyman--Beurling remains the positive example of a source law that survives in the destination norm. Its zero-power annihilator produces a stable missing-root margin, and rootwise horizontal depth becomes a quantitative rank--height budget. Fixed-base aliasing then shows that the next endpoint must retain non-aliased absolute-height information before scalarization. The missing currency is not another exact source equation but a joint target-bearing compression statistic that preserves that non-aliased root identity.

Prime Circle now closes both obvious one-profile spectral compressions under diffuse averaging. PC-295 preserves a nonzero classical Green singular spectrum, while PC-296 proves that the raw eigenvalue spectral radius nevertheless collapses to zero because diffuse independent left/right dilations kill the cross-Gram loops. Strong singular channels and eigenvalue loops are different resources. Any surviving nonnormal signal must therefore live in pseudospectral/transient behavior, joint profiles, source-coupled left/right geometry, cross-level relations or a non-diffuse regime—not in another statistic of the singular or eigenvalue set.

Prime Flute and Prime Lattice exhibit two geometric versions of endpoint thinning. Prime Flute's fixed hard cutoff is already noncompact because adjacent lattice frequencies across the boundary retain order-one correlation, so the split or observable must change. Prime Lattice has solved the regular outer Volterra range up to one scalar endpoint condition and one singular amplitude; the missing information sits in the exponentially compressed `r=O(s)` corner and must enter through a finite-`s` matching law before any rational-prime sign theorem.

Robin Extremal now separates **very close strong mass** from **signed cancellation**. RE-128 forces a logarithmically comparable companion at essentially inverse observation-window spacing, while RE-127 forces a cancelling-phase companion only in the wider signed annulus. The remaining strong-atom endpoint is precisely to connect those two companions or exclude the narrow pair geometry; the diffuse cloud is a separate branch.

Weil Inertia is already localized to rare upper hosts. WI-293 adds a provenance boundary rather than a new endpoint: a current Cramér-scale prime-gap claim would close the two-island branch if valid, but its proof contains a false telescope, so the rare-host incidence problem of WI-292 remains the actual frontier. Here the thin endpoint includes **proof provenance** because importing an invalid global theorem would erase the genuine local arithmetic problem.

Weil Positivity has moved from mass class to an affine curvature obstruction. WP-304 reaches the correct logarithmic scale only by entering an infinite-mass class; WP-305 finds the exact Mangoldt source on the wrong side of the Gamma `y^-2` curvature; WP-306 proves that every support-preserving spectral translation stays strictly on that wrong side. The endpoint is no longer “find the right recentering” but derive genuinely new finite--archimedean structure with independent positivity before consulting the Gamma coefficient.

Visual Exploration has compressed its static optimizer hierarchy all the way to a binary problem. VIS-223 gauges midpoint-symmetric taper matrices to real signed ratio-shell form; VIS-224 proves that the binary Ising optimum, exact torus optimum and real SDP have the same bounded-versus-divergent Haar-normalized scale up to a universal constant. The endpoint is therefore the arithmetic scale of one explicit signed prime Hamiltonian, not continuous phase freedom or SDP rank.

Xi Flow gives a complementary conditioning lesson. Known packet windows admit uniformly conditioned linear and nonlinear holomorphic charts with only linear sample complexity. The missing endpoint is global support discovery, assembly and physical access to the complex samples, followed by transport to a zero-sensitive quantity; local packet conditioning is no longer the scarce resource.

The common diagnostic is therefore concrete: after every closure, ask **what quotient did the theorem actually control, where can cost still escape, and does the final destination charge that surviving coordinate?** The recent findings repeatedly rule out a merely stronger estimate in the old variable: AF-346 saturates the concentration bill, FD-127 absorbs fixed multiplicative breadth, MC-297 isolates overlap, PC-296 destroys eigenvalue loops while leaving singular channels, RE-128 separates magnitude from phase, VIS-224 removes continuous-phase scale freedom, and WP-306 closes support-preserving translation. Progress requires changing the mathematical currency only when persisted evidence shows that the previous one has genuinely become insufficient.

**Boundary.** These endpoint currencies are not interchangeable and no cross-line theorem is asserted. The synthesis is an accounting principle for choosing the next mathematical question, not evidence that one line's mechanism transfers to another.
