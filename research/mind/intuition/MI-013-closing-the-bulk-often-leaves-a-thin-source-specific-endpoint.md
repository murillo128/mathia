# MI-013 — Closing the bulk often exposes a thinner source-specific carrier with a different mathematical currency

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current local minds; not a theorem that every RH route has this form. The listed reductions are exact only within their owning lines and stated hypotheses.

Several current lines have exhausted a broad generic mechanism and exposed a qualitatively different residual object rather than a smaller copy of the original problem.

Arithmetic Fidelity is now the sharpest incidence example. AF-308--AF-309 make the exact-zero cone simultaneously Lebesgue-null, dense and nowhere stably classifiable. AF-310 then identifies the full elementary character obstruction: common-time distinct-prime phases have a cyclic relation lattice of rank at most one, which excludes the forced rank-two geometries at `k=3,4` but is already compatible with a residual subset of the pentagon zero surface. The residual carrier is therefore **nontorsion exact arithmetic placement**, not more topology, near-zero access, root-of-unity comparison or integer-character rank.

FD-088 nevertheless recovers a fixed positive share of the nonsquarefree frontier through exact unit-distortion row-adaptive anchors far above the block length. The residual carrier is now **multihorizon composability** of those anchors, not existence, conditioning, scale or normalized mass of an exact copy.

The residual carrier is the **single source-blind pretender or source-coset-scaled residual**, not conductor reduction or generic all-residue variance.

PC-277 matches subexponential-band normalized near-resonance to a non-prime exact-`Li` denominator control and makes the far rational endpoint exactly resonant for every denominator-`q` source. These examples support a common diagnostic: **after a bulk mechanism is closed, identify the exact carrier and the new currency before strengthening the old estimate.** The surviving obstruction may be nontorsion incidence, multihorizon composition, a single exceptional character, microscopic scalar geometry, seam-metric locality, aggregate fiber coupling, q-adic branch-energy balance, mixed-depth selection, source-specific boundary cancellation, cutwise source concentration, or independently forced global coupling. Better constants in the exhausted bulk currency need not touch it.

**Boundary.** This synthesis does not identify a transfer theorem between these objects and does not imply that the residual carriers are easier or rare on the arithmetic source. It is a program-level warning against continuing to optimize a mechanism after the destination has moved to a mathematically different carrier.
