# MI-013 — Closing the bulk often changes the mathematical currency of the endpoint

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from the current local minds and canonical findings. The reductions below are exact only inside their owning lines and hypotheses; this is not a theorem that every RH route has the same shape.

Several mature lines now show a stronger pattern than “the obstruction gets smaller.” Once a broad mechanism is closed, the surviving object often lives in a different mathematical category. Continuing to improve the old bulk estimate then attacks the wrong currency.

Arithmetic Fidelity separates generic recovery, exceptional incidence and variational non-escape. AF-316--AF-318 make generic harmonic recovery dimension-tight and show that the eight-point middle singularity is compatible with the strongest elementary prime-phase character-rank restriction. The endpoint is actual prime-log incidence/conditioning in that singular fiber. Independently, AF-319 shows that quartic Euler optimizer transfer becomes rigorous once a source profile cap supplies projective equicoercivity; the remaining issue is whether that non-escape resource is source-forced rather than imposed.

Farey Discrepancy has moved from representation to history, then from history to depth, and now from separate constants to physical descent. FD-096 supplies sharp occupied bounded-defect entry states, while FD-097 preserves the common scale ratio and reduces composition loss to a fixed exponential factor in projective quality. The endpoint is an effective depth-to-scale theorem plus transport-error control, not further improvement of independently propagated occupation/defect constants.

Möbius Cancellation has moved from tensor geometry to source arithmetic and then from a monomial moment to an endpoint-optimal detector. MC-267 proves the Krawtchouk hierarchy is only a change of basis for the Legendre-sign Hamming distribution. MC-268 shows that Christoffel extremality uses that same low-support information far more efficiently. The endpoint is now an arithmetic estimate for the endpoint-optimal weighted hierarchy, not more universal Krawtchouk algebra or a higher monomial moment.

Nyman--Beurling has moved from one-state visibility to fixed-finite interaction and then from raw volume crowding to true conditioning. NB-095 closes the finite distinct-state interaction, and [NB-096](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-096-canonical-deflation-cancels-raw-cauchy-crowding-volume.md) shows that the raw Cauchy determinant penalty is cancelled exactly by the reciprocal determinant of canonical Blaschke-deflation coordinates. PC-280 classifies the truncated escape exactly as `P_(p,B) D_f P_(r,B)`: the source changes only the placement of two dilated coefficient windows around one fixed multiplier. The endpoint is therefore **source-forcing of the operation itself**, not positivity, target independence, nonlocality or successful scalar extraction.

The durable rule is: after a bulk mechanism is closed, re-identify both the mathematical type and the provenance requirement of the residual before strengthening anything. A singular geometry can become an incidence theorem; a composition problem can become a depth-to-scale invariant; a moment problem can become an endpoint-optimal arithmetic detector; a finite-state interaction problem can become a condition-number problem; a symmetry escape can become finite-section placement; a local packet can become an integrated destination budget; an ambient sparse-host problem can become a joint shifted-energy kernel; and a successful nonlocal readout can become a source-canonicality problem.

**Boundary.** This synthesis does not imply that the residual carriers are small, easier, rare, mutually equivalent or sufficient for RH. It creates no transfer theorem between lines. Its purpose is to prevent work from continuing in a mathematical currency that the current exact reductions have already exhausted.
