# MI-013 — Closing a bulk mechanism often changes the mathematical currency of the endpoint

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current local minds and canonical findings. Each reduction below is exact only in its owning line; this is not a theorem that all RH routes share one endpoint structure.

Arithmetic Fidelity has now separated sign order from the resource used to achieve it. AF-338 proves fixed-order full-Euler sign regularity once `sigma>r-1`. AF-339--AF-343 show that after pole cancellation the prime layer keeps the RH pole discriminator and `Theta(P/log P)` sign variation against broad low-exception and periodic/syndetic baselines. AF-344 then constructs a fixed prime-independent perfect-power baseline that remains zero-pole-blind and residue-one while reducing residual sign variation to `O(P^(1/k))`. The bill moves into sparse support and amplitudes `asymp P^(1-1/k)`. The residual currency is therefore **a joint source certificate coupling sign order to amplitude/concentration/support geometry, or a different representation-invariant certificate**—not non-syndetic structure or description complexity by itself.

Analytic Frontier has crossed the analogous one-point-statistics boundary. ANF-185 turns the critical bow detector into a fixed one-height high-pass at the exact physical width `a_X`. ANF-186 then matches the natural prime/rough coefficient alphabet, densities, mean, diagonal scale and local absolute-mass envelope while still producing critical endpoint memory by placing signs after reading the logarithmic carrier. The residual currency is **joint arithmetic placement relative to that carrier**, or direct control of the deterministic weighted residual—not stronger marginal statistics.

Farey Discrepancy has moved from an entropy loophole to an explicit matched control. FD-117--FD-120 show that reversible representation enlargement and rich single-horizon source truth can remain endpoint-cheap. FD-121--FD-124 prove a positive average cost for every polynomially bounded common source on schedules with finite average logarithmic horizon growth. FD-125 then constructs one square-root-bounded common source on the repeated-square schedule `H_(j+1)=H_j^2` with `S_(H_j)->1`. The residual currency is no longer “control the superexponential schedule” in the same abstract class; it is **a physical Mertens/Farey arithmetic law that the synthetic nested-carrier source fails and that creates pointwise cost at those sparse horizons**.

Möbius Cancellation has reduced the collision-rich odd branch beyond one-character bias, common-cell union bounds, individual source cost and generic quotient coding. MC-293 collapses the low-source-cost near-negative spectrum to at most one Landau--Page mode. MC-294 forces many remaining directions into a high-cost quotient with no short dependencies. MC-295 then constructs a fixed-weight finite model satisfying the live defect, participation, Page-balance, entropy and quotient-girth constraints simultaneously, with full high-cost quotient dimension and no common preferred-sign cell. The residual currency is therefore **arithmetic realizability of the expensive quotient**—an actual Legendre-shell restriction on quotient code, occupancy/multiplicity or the exceptional mode—not another generic Fourier/coding inequality.

Nyman--Beurling supplies the opposite outcome: a source-representation law that survives in the destination metric. NB-118--NB-122 show that increasingly many exact Nyman equations and the full nested prefix still admit a perfect inverse-section extremizer. NB-123 uses the finite zero-power annihilating recurrence to exclude exact perfect capture below logarithmic rank. NB-124 makes that separator stable without Prony inversion: the annihilator is tested as a bounded dual functional in the same reciprocal block weights as the Nyman energy, giving every fixed finite packet a cutoff-uniform target-angle gap and forcing `Omega(log R)` rank for polynomially near-perfect capture in a fixed horizontal strip. The residual currency is now **the normalized missing-root annihilator margin under growing rank and horizontal approach to `Re s=1`**, together with height/horizontal-mass control.

Prime Circle has separated five square-root-scale notions that previously looked like one boundary. PC-291 shows microscopic residue relations are destination-negligible; PC-292 gives an order-one fixed-pair local Hankel band; PC-293 washes every local `T=O(N)` band out under diffuse source averaging. PC-294 then proves that the full source-averaged Hilbert--Schmidt mass itself tends to the same `pi^2/12` benchmark for the prime and count-matched diffuse controls. The residual currency is therefore **spectral shape at fixed total mass**—higher moments, spectral edges, small singular values or richer pre-spectral structure—not microscopic resonance, worst-case local amplitude or aggregate energy.

Prime Flute's sharp-cutoff completed-angle branch remains noncompact because adjacent modes straddling the cutoff retain order-one canonical correlations on infinitely many separated modules. The residual is a change of splitting or observable, not a sharper estimate inside the same compactness class.

Prime Lattice has moved beyond universal profile recovery to a singular interface problem. PL-308 gives exact fixed-`s` weak virial stress; PL-309 locates the renormalized trace on an `exp(-Theta(1/s))` layer invisible to bulk convergence; PL-310 computes the universal stretched Green profile `C/sqrt(e^(2r)-1)`. PL-311 proves that a regular endpoint-integrable outer correction can absorb only continuous forcings with zero endpoint value, while every fixed physical bulk point collapses into the `r=O(s)` corner. The residual currency is therefore **corner-to-outer matching and its endpoint solvability condition for the scalar amplitude `C`, then the rational-prime sign**—not another fixed-`r` perturbation or generic compactness theorem.

Robin Extremal has progressively converted a cancellation cloud into pairwise zero geometry. RE-122 shows that an algebraic anomaly requires a subedge zero within `O(log U/U)` of the attained edge at polynomial height. RE-123 proves the regular-CA mesh samples the full signed packet. RE-124 localizes compensating mass to `U^(-1+eta)` in ordinate, and RE-125 removes the sub-reciprocal core because same-sector neighbors reinforce. RE-126 adds the local zero-count budget: a hidden strong atom forces a distinct companion with coefficient at least a `1/log U` fraction of the target and `c_*/U<|gamma_1-gamma_0|<=U^(-1+eta)`. Along unbounded targets the normalized spacing `|gamma_1-gamma_0| log gamma_0` tends to zero. The residual currency is therefore **algebraically strong near-edge pairs with vanishing normalized spacing and the required signed phase, or a genuinely diffuse many-mode cloud**—not generic annular mass.

Weil Positivity has moved beyond “introduce curvature” and “make the kernel move.” WP-302 traps free projector motion between homogeneous rigidity and programmability. WP-303 shows that a moving spectral cut of one fixed source operator is instead too singular: commuting projectors are norm-discrete and have zero strong derivative wherever differentiable. WP-304 shows bounded source resolvents have finite spectral mass and decay, while the first renormalized infinite-mass logarithm is universal and orientation-sensitive. The residual currency is a genuinely source-forced noncommuting/nonlocal law whose orientation, subtraction and subleading response survive the readout.

Visual Exploration has separated weak certificate failure from exact static objective scale. VIS-217--VIS-218 close the normalized magnetic route and VIS-220 closes simple-cycle packing. VIS-221 introduces the full correlation SDP; VIS-222 applies the complex symmetric Grothendieck inequality to prove `M_abs<=U_abs<=K_gamma^C M_abs`. The residual currency is therefore **the actual prime-specific correlation-SDP scale**: high rank can obstruct exact optimizer recovery but cannot create an unbounded objective gap.

The newest reductions sharpen a common diagnostic in two directions. AF-344, FD-125, MC-295 and PC-294 are matched controls showing that a previously plausible scalar resource can be made cheap while cost escapes into another coordinate: amplitude/support geometry, omitted physical arithmetic law, expensive quotient realizability, or spectral shape. NB-124 is the positive counterpart: it chooses a source functional whose norm is controlled in the exact destination geometry and therefore obtains a real stable gap. PL-311 and RE-126 show a second kind of narrowing: once the bulk or cloud is controlled, the endpoint can collapse onto a much thinner compatibility object—an `r=O(s)` solvability corner or an actual near-edge zero companion pair. The question after every reduction should be **where can the missing cost still escape, and does the destination theorem actually charge that coordinate?**

**Boundary.** These residual currencies are not equivalent and need not be easier. This synthesis prevents stale questions from surviving after the evidence changes; it does not assert a common theorem connecting the line-specific endpoints.
