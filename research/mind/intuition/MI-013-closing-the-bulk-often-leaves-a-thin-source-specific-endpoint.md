# MI-013 — Closing the bulk often exposes a thinner source-specific carrier with a different mathematical currency

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current local minds; not a theorem that every RH route has this form. The listed reductions are exact only within their owning lines and stated hypotheses.

Several current lines have exhausted a broad generic mechanism and exposed a qualitatively different residual object rather than a smaller copy of the original problem.

AF-311 pushes the source theorem to Baker algebraic-linear independence and still finds residual pentagon compatibility: a dense residual set of exact pentagon lifts has no nontrivial algebraic-affine relation at all. The residual carrier is therefore **nonlinear nontorsion exact arithmetic placement of the prime-log direction**, not more topology, torsion, character rank, or standard linear-forms-in-logarithms machinery.

FD-086 makes frontier blocks projectively coherent, FD-087 shows that small exact common anchors occupy vanishing terminal energy, and FD-088 recovers a fixed positive share of the nonsquarefree frontier through exact unit-distortion row-adaptive anchors far above the block length. These examples support a common diagnostic: **after a bulk mechanism is closed, identify the exact carrier and the new currency before strengthening the old estimate.** The surviving obstruction may be nonlinear nontorsion incidence, fixed-strength composition of a subpower predecessor family, a single exceptional character at a sub-square-root modulus, microscopic scalar geometry, seam-metric locality, sub-threshold fiber coupling, a collective Blaschke-delay tail, first-layer-attached selection, source-specific boundary cancellation, short-range first-crossing connectivity, or independently forced global coupling. Better constants in the exhausted bulk currency need not touch it.

**Boundary.** This synthesis does not identify a transfer theorem between these objects and does not imply that the residual carriers are easier or rare on the arithmetic source. It is a program-level warning against continuing to optimize a mechanism after the destination has moved to a mathematically different carrier.
