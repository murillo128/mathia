# MI-013 — Closing the bulk often changes the mathematical currency of the endpoint

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from the current local minds and canonical findings. The reductions below are exact only inside their owning lines and hypotheses; this is not a theorem that every RH route has the same shape.

Several mature lines now show a stronger pattern than “the obstruction gets smaller.” Once a broad mechanism is closed, the surviving object often lives in a different mathematical category. Continuing to improve the old bulk estimate then attacks the wrong currency.

The durable rule is: after a bulk mechanism is closed, re-identify both the **mathematical type** and the **provenance/metric requirement** of the residual before strengthening anything. Exact recovery may become fixed-component conditioning plus support control; horizon descent may become finite tagged inventory plus switching cost; a large sieve may become phase-sensitive one-vector cancellation; global zero density may become second-order local geometry; scalar boundary sharpness may become finite-section placement; a positive-measure packet family may become completed-energy preconditioning; local analytic channel estimates may end at an exact transcendence contingency while the RH obstruction becomes nonlocal assembly; shell multiplicity may become exact support geometry; and positive completion may require signed source transformation before positivity can even be read.

**Boundary.** This synthesis does not imply that the residual carriers are easier, mutually equivalent or sufficient for RH. It creates no transfer theorem between lines. Its purpose is to prevent work from continuing in a mathematical currency that current exact reductions have already exhausted.
