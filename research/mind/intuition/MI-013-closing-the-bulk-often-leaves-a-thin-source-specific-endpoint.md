# MI-013 — Closing a bulk mechanism often changes the mathematical currency of the endpoint

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current local minds and canonical findings. Each reduction below is exact only in its owning line; this is not a theorem that all RH routes share one endpoint structure.

Several mature lines now show that once a broad obstruction is closed, the surviving problem frequently changes category. Strengthening the old bulk estimate then attacks the wrong object.

Arithmetic Fidelity has moved from exact recovery to a joint source-size/observation-scale modulus and then to a target-margin transfer. AF-324--AF-325 price the `H,t` source window; AF-326 shows that, on the exact middle-singular stratum, the source `|s_3|` margin forces a second-harmonic gap. The residual is exact incidence plus a destination-sufficient quantitative margin, not a support-independent phase gap.

Farey Discrepancy has moved beyond tagged inventory and retagging. FD-102 gives a carrier-free adaptive logarithmic source budget and FD-103 proves it sharp on actual record ladders. The endpoint is packet strength/source progress at logarithmic depth, not another carrier-switching charge.

Möbius Cancellation has refined the witness endpoint twice. MC-273 gives a homogeneous-shell collapse after a negative witness; MC-274 prices atomwise conditioning; MC-275 shows overlapping all-witness aggregation is actually cheap, while normalization to a nonblind classifier is an OR approximate-degree barrier on the generic cube. The endpoint is source-restricted or signed aggregate cancellation, not better generic witness selection.

Nyman--Beurling now separates two previously conflated local resources. NB-102 makes Menger curvature the complete three-state conditioning invariant. NB-103 proves fixed-multiplicity confluent normalized volume is path-independent and curvature-blind, governed instead by radial depth. The endpoint depends on whether the target consumes stable inversion, determinant volume, growing multiplicity or a repeated-zero derivative state.

Prime Circle has pushed classical reproducibility deeper into the finite-section branch. PC-282--PC-283 classicalize the selector and Green remainder; PC-284 shows the exact logarithmic normalized singular-spectrum law remains Li-grid reproducible for `log B=o(sqrt(log q))`. The surviving one-profile question begins only at the rational-microscopic transition or after enriching the observable.

Prime Flute has moved from packet survival to relative source-generated locality. PF-330 conserves packet mass; PF-331 removes polar instability; PF-332 cancels the baseline seam square-root defect and writes `F-I` as a resolvent-dressed pant insertion. The endpoint is uniform control of that relative transfer, not abstract square-root locality.

Prime Lattice has moved past the pure singular-limit derivative obstruction. PL-297 blocks uniform half-Sobolev control, but PL-299 proves `C_s->C_0` in `C^1` on the pure principal branch through uniform BV. The endpoint is now compactness of the actual completed-Weil eigenstate plus completion and sign, not fixed-order or pure-branch differentiability.

Robin Extremal has compressed the unmatched-fringe problem to one terminal event. RE-106 forces corrected Chebyshev debt across the whole fan; all intermediate fringes telescope, and a raw reset requires super-stretch ending on the deep base-two host ladder. The endpoint is that extreme lacunary reset branch, not matched-run frequency.

Visual Exploration has closed the one-cancellation long-start Schur frontier that was previously only a clue. VIS-211 proves `E_1=o(R_v^2)` throughout the diverging strictly subcritical regime. The endpoint is higher cancellation, the critical scale, or worst-start coherence—not further optimization of the one-cancellation anchor kernel there.

Weil Inertia has produced a first exact source-specific compatibility law beyond generic cut geometry. WI-281 shows that complete one-signed screening forces `log 2` support packing and strictly lowers the positive archimedean budget to `K_pack<K_+`. The endpoint is to add enough actual-source constraints to cross the spectral-area requirement or handle sign-changing modes, not another generic covariance inequality.

Weil Positivity now has an explicit signed source transformation rather than an abstract request for one. WP-290 kills the pole source-natively, but admissibility is already RH-equivalent. The endpoint is unconditional category descent plus exact Weil quadratic identification, not pole removal.

The durable rule is to re-identify the **type of the residual** after every exact reduction, closure or withdrawal. A source margin may need a target transfer; a local normal form may expose normalization complexity; a conditioning singularity may disappear from determinant volume; a finite section may remain classically reproducible beyond the complete-domain limit; conserved packet mass may expose relative locality; and a global sign problem may collapse to one extreme endpoint event.

**Boundary.** The residual currencies are not equivalent and need not be easier. This synthesis exists to prevent stale mathematical questions from surviving after the evidence has changed.
