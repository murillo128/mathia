# MI-013 — Closing a universal bulk often leaves a thinner source-specific endpoint

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current local minds and canonical findings, now including AF-369, FD-149, RE-154, VIS-246, WI-308 and WP-323. The reductions are exact only in their owning lines; no theorem is asserted that the mechanisms transfer between objects.

A recurring pattern is sharper than “the endpoint gets thinner.” After a broad analytic, geometric or operator obstruction is closed, the remaining problem often changes category: from bulk regularity to a source-specific sign, from essential spectrum to finitely many gapped modes, from local selector data to a canonical global average, from broad source freedom to one class-dependent scalar, or from weak-family calibration to an exact conditional law. Continuing to strengthen the solved bulk variable then attacks the wrong resource.

Prime Lattice remains the cleanest positive example. PL-322 uses conservative endpoint flux to force the critical coefficient and residual moment; PL-323 turns the source-coupled residual equation `2Qm+Hm->0` into `Qm->0` by coercive comparison; PL-321 completes the moving Green transfer. The analytic moving-diagonal gate is closed for the actual branch. The remaining problem is the **arithmetic orientation** of the rank-one endpoint datum: a rational-prime sign/first-crossing theorem, not more generic endpoint smoothness.

Weil Inertia now makes the operator reduction more concrete. WI-307 raises the admissible reserve so that the fixed-aperture essential spectrum becomes positive. WI-308 retains the clipped source tail and proves that it contributes an exact multiplier bounded below by `c_(chi,B)>0`. The residual question is no longer an unspecified finite-rank repair: positivity is reduced to the finite discrete inequality `lambda_min(D_(L,chi,B))>=-c_(chi,B)`, while an RH first crossing would force a negative eigenvalue below `-c`. The thin endpoint is an effective/uniform comparison between a finite spectral sector and a source-exact tail budget.

Robin Extremal quantifies how thin can still mean highly sensitive. RE-153 identifies the Pareto-weighted Chebyshev-deficit average consumed by a self-tangent counterexample. RE-154 shows that relocating only `sqrt(p)/log p` post-selector labels—an asymptotically vanishing fraction of a proportional prime shell—moves that exact destination average by order one while preserving exact source agreement below a fixed multiple of `p` and PNT-scale density. The endpoint is therefore not stronger local counting but arithmetic coupling of a sparse, coherently weighted future shell to the selected state.

Nyman--Beurling gives the signed version. NB-148 cancels the universal Hadamard logarithm with a zero-mass sampler, but exports equal negative Poisson mass. NB-149 shows that generic Jordan/absolute-value control of that negative lobe restores the same positive logarithmic budget. The remaining resource is location-sensitive or genuinely signed control after cancellation.

Arithmetic Fidelity now supplies both an obstruction and a positive class-specific closure. AF-366--AF-368 show that finite prefixes and even arbitrarily tight fixed logarithmic generalized-integer counting leave broad Beurling completion fibres target-mixed. AF-369 narrows the source to integer-supported Delone systems: the zero divisor is still universal across the class, but the discarded pole residue equals the generalized-integer density and `residue=1` exactly characterizes the ordinary prime system. A thin endpoint can therefore be **one scalar**, but only after an independently justified source category makes that scalar rigid.

Visual Exploration and Weil Positivity sharpen the calibration/category endpoints. VIS-246 proves that the concentration statistic `C` identifies the symmetric-Dirichlet nuisance but does not remove it for `m>=3`; the exact sufficient coordinate is instead `L=sum log X_i`, so the new gate is the tractability and signal retention of `Law(bar T|L)`. WP-323 shows that even arbitrary fixed finite boundary Pick jets remain higher-resolvent Grams; more exact scalar derivatives add source fingerprints without changing the universal positivity mechanism.

The common diagnostic is therefore: **after a closure theorem, rewrite the frontier in the new minimal variable before investing in stronger estimates of the old one**. Identify the exact discarded remainder, scalar, finite sector, signed lobe, conditional coordinate or source coupling that now carries the unresolved implication. In several lines the useful next theorem is not “stronger everywhere” but “correct on the thin channel the destination actually consumes.”

**Boundary.** “Thin endpoint” is an organizational pattern, not a claim that the remaining gates are easy or equivalent. In several lines the thinner endpoint may be the genuinely RH-hard part, and no coefficient, rate or theorem is transferred between the different mathematical objects.
