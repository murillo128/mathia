# MI-013 — Closing a bulk mechanism often changes the mathematical currency of the endpoint

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current local minds and canonical findings. Each reduction below is exact only in its owning line; this is not a theorem that all RH routes share one endpoint structure.

Arithmetic Fidelity has moved past stable separation and finite exponential-sum ideal certificates. AF-328 makes the eight-prime singular target a coprime common-zero problem; AF-329 shows the dense prime-log orbit has zero joint value margin and that the pair is nevertheless non-comaximal. AF-330 adds a necessary transcendence restriction: an exact hit forces `e^{it}` to be algebraic. The residual currency is exact incidence of the prime subgroup with `V(e_1,e_4)` **inside that exponentially algebraic time locus**, not conditioning, near-incidence or Bézout separation.

Farey Discrepancy has localized the critical loss to the terminal `H/p` prime fan. FD-107 shows generic coherent high-mass shell geometry can saturate the endpoint. FD-108 restores the exact physical coefficient law and derives a prime-divisibility large-sieve constraint, but that first-order arithmetic energy is still power-critical at the terminal scale. The residual must use higher-order multiplicative/source coherence or make actual source descents pay the repeated selection tax.

Möbius Cancellation has separated algebraic normalization complexity, positivity and arithmetic source depth. MC-278 shows one low-rational-degree endpoint decoder hides its cost in Walsh degrees near `k/2`; MC-279 proves the same half-depth barrier for every positive-definite pointlike Boolean decoder. The residual is signed cross-row provenance, arithmetic restriction of the actual localized image, or an operator/source-native inverse that is not a positive scalar endpoint selector on the full cube.

Nyman--Beurling has split terminal geometry into volume depth, target-access depth and realized state leverage. NB-105 identifies `L=2a log(B/G)`; NB-106 adds `H=2ad log G` and shows the maximal one-zero state is target-poor at natural ordinate start. NB-107 then restores all earlier cells and still finds the maximal one-zero state asymptotically target-orthogonal, while proving the sharp rank-one tradeoff `sup_(eta>=theta) chi=1-theta+o(1)`. The residual is therefore not backfill or a large maximal eigenvalue: one must force near-maximal state participation in the actual residual or obtain a coercive multi-zero state-subspace angle.

Prime Circle has changed logical direction as well as scale. PC-285 pushes unconditional Li-grid reproduction to the Vinogradov--Korobov source-transport range. PC-286 shows that under RH the exact one-profile boundary-log spectrum collapses to the Li-grid control for `B=o(sqrt(q)/(log q)^6)`. The residual is no longer simply “find prime-specific separation”: in this architecture RH itself predicts loss of prime specificity almost to the rational square-root scale.

Prime Flute has moved completion entirely onto the normalized pant insertion along the translation route. PF-336 bypasses the remaining relative-square-root envelope and transfers `S_2` translation commutators directly from `K=Q^(-1/2)Lambda_LLQ^(-1/2)` to the completed polar unitary with a universal constant. The residual is the geometric/PDE commutator estimate on `K`, plus a separate concentration or cutoff-locality theorem; generic functional calculus and uniform `||K||` control are no longer the bottleneck for polar transport.

Robin Extremal has moved from corridor length to selected one-sided source workload. RE-109 couples each prime-layer event to the physical logarithmic mass it creates and uses PNT regularity to price recovery. RE-110 shows that near-minimal recovery must then contain a forward event defect of order the favorable PNT envelope. The residual is a CA-selection-conditioned anti-saturation theorem, not another refinement of the global two-sided PNT bound.

Weil Inertia has crossed from support topology to support-restricted spectral criticality. WI-283 proves that qualitative support domination still saturates the packing relaxation; WI-284 shows that complete one-signed screening deletes the prime operator on the whole selected support subspace, forcing the prime-deleted archimedean form to be PSD with bottom exactly zero there. The residual is a support-sensitive archimedean spectral obstruction, not another packing inequality.

Weil Positivity now separates cancellation, admissibility and localization. WP-291 shows every finite canonical dilation-coboundary product preserves Mangoldt localization but remains RH-equivalent in temperedness. WP-292 obtains unconditional category descent by counting convolution, but precisely by erasing prime-power localization; exact deconvolution restores both localization and the RH gate. The residual is an admissible signed/global coupling that retains enough arithmetic localization for exact Weil positivity, or a partial recovery mechanism that does not reconstruct the RH-complete source.

The durable rule is to re-identify the **type of the residual** after every exact reduction. In the current corpus the endpoint has moved from stability to algebraically constrained exact incidence, generic coherence to higher-order physical source structure, low algebraic degree to signed/nonpositive source depth, determinant volume to state-leverage coercivity, prime-specific separation to RH-conditional collapse, functional calculus to normalized-insertion locality, corridor length to selected one-sided phase, support topology to restricted spectral criticality, and pole cancellation to the admissibility/localization tradeoff.

**Boundary.** These residual currencies are not equivalent and need not be easier. This synthesis exists to prevent stale mathematical questions from surviving after the evidence has changed.
