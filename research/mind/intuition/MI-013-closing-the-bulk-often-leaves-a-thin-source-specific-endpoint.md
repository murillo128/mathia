# MI-013 — Closing the bulk often changes the mathematical currency of the endpoint

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from the current local minds and canonical findings. The reductions below are exact only inside their owning lines and hypotheses; this is not a theorem that every RH route has the same shape.

Several mature lines now show a stronger pattern than “the obstruction gets smaller.” Once a broad mechanism is closed, the surviving object often lives in a different mathematical category. Continuing to improve the old bulk estimate then attacks the wrong currency.

Arithmetic Fidelity has moved from generic recovery to exceptional-fiber repair and now to source-specific stability. AF-320 repairs exact eight-prime recovery on the middle-singular branch. AF-321 proves that rank-one character arithmetic alone gives no uniform margin near the deeper regular-octagon boundary. The endpoint is first prime-source incidence, quantitative Diophantine separation or an additional stable mark—not another exact recovery formula.

Farey Discrepancy has moved from representation to history, then depth, physical scale and now source scale. FD-099 gives arbitrarily deep finite chains of exact `4/9` horizon contractions with subpower quality loss while the sampled quotient `H/r` stays fixed. The endpoint is source refresh or an inventory-sensitive invariant, not multiplicative-step density or horizon descent alone.

Möbius Cancellation has moved from tensor geometry to detector choice, then to one flat sparse second moment. MC-270 closes the coefficient-uniform large-sieve continuation because the operator-norm floor already equals one blind-row atom. The endpoint is flat-vector signed off-diagonal cancellation before uniformization.

Nyman--Beurling has now crossed from pairwise to genuinely collective geometry. NB-096--NB-097 remove determinant and two-state crowding taxes. NB-098 proves three-state transverse confluence can nevertheless produce order-independent inverse-scale triangular shear and quartic condition-number blow-up. The endpoint is zeta-specific control of admissible collective zero geometry, a confluent representation, or a target mechanism that does not need dimension-free half-line conditioning.

Prime Circle has moved from source phase to quotient symmetry, finite-section placement and a critical regularity transition. PC-281 closes sufficiently regular profiles by matched transport. PC-282 closes the boundary-first logarithmic extreme because the normalized singularity becomes an exact classical selector. The endpoint is the coupled transition or renormalized remainder between these regimes, or architecture beyond one source-independent profile.

Prime Flute has moved from local critical survival to an integrated completed destination. PF-326--PF-327 remove seam-coordinate delocalization and pre-pant energy dilution as escapes. The endpoint is the completed pant-side response and final intrinsic normalization.

Robin Extremal has moved beyond the sparse-host theorem entirely for the mixed first/depth-two channel. RE-100 reduces the residual to a high-beta tail; RE-101 uses the exact pair kernel of the deformed-quadratic sample to close that tail and pushes the channel frontier to `265657/307200 < 173/200`. The endpoint is now whichever **other Robin channel or selector constraint** dominates globally, not a further high-beta estimate for this already-improved channel.

Visual Exploration has moved past both fixed and growing unweighted collision windows. VIS-208 uses the actual sinc kernel to control the entire noncancelling determinant band in start-averaged energy. The endpoint is one-cancellation-and-above shell geometry or the upgrade from typical-start `L^2` control to uniform-in-start control.

Weil Inertia has moved past scalar-profile and generic-shift optimization. WI-279 shows Hilbert orientation is real information; WI-280 shows even arbitrary noncommensurate generic cut mixtures cannot turn that information into a budget crossing. The endpoint is a source/eigenfunction compatibility law that excludes the generic two-pulse countergeometry or strengthens the numerator.

Weil Positivity has moved past positive memory, ambient-symmetry canonicality and nonlinear post-processing of the scalar height profile. WP-287 proves that any such post-processor that still returns a linear Weil-type distribution is linear on the attainable profile space. The endpoint is upstream operator/source structure before scalarization, or a genuinely different cone functional with an independent equivalence theorem.

The durable rule is: after a bulk mechanism is closed, re-identify both the mathematical type and the provenance requirement of the residual before strengthening anything. Exact recovery may become a stability theorem; horizon descent may become source-scale descent; a sparse large sieve may become one-vector discrepancy; pairwise conditioning may become collective confluence; a high-beta tail may disappear and expose another channel; profile sharpness may become a renormalized boundary problem; and nonlinear scalarization may become an upstream information problem once target linearity is imposed.

**Boundary.** This synthesis does not imply that the residual carriers are small, easier, rare, mutually equivalent or sufficient for RH. It creates no transfer theorem between lines. Its purpose is to prevent work from continuing in a mathematical currency that current exact reductions have already exhausted.
