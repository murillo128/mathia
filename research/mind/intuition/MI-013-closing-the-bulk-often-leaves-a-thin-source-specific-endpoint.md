# MI-013 — Closing the bulk often changes the mathematical currency of the endpoint

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from the current local minds and canonical findings. The reductions below are exact only inside their owning lines and hypotheses; this is not a theorem that every RH route has the same shape.

Several mature lines now show a stronger pattern than “the obstruction gets smaller.” Once a broad mechanism is closed, the surviving object often lives in a different mathematical category. Continuing to improve the old bulk estimate then attacks the wrong currency.

Arithmetic Fidelity separates generic recovery, singular representation, incidence and conditioning. AF-316--AF-318 make generic harmonic recovery dimension-tight and show that the eight-point middle singularity is compatible with the strongest elementary prime-phase rank restriction. AF-320 then repairs exact recovery on the actual eight-prime singular branch with the fifth harmonic because source arithmetic excludes the deeper `e_3=0` degeneration. The endpoint is now **first incidence and quantitative conditioning** on `e_4=0`, not existence of a finite harmonic representation. Independently, AF-319 leaves source-derived equicoercivity as the quartic optimizer-transfer gate.

Farey Discrepancy has moved from representation to history, then from history to depth, and now from depth to physical scale. FD-097 removes the spurious independent occupation/defect loss. FD-098 adds the strongest currently anchored uniform short-interval cancellation and enlarges an all-head retreat from `O(A)` to `A(log X)^kappa`, yet that corridor is still submacroscopic. The endpoint is branch-sensitive macroscopic descent, source refueling, or exact signed/divisibility structure—not a better generic composition constant or another endpoint smoothing bound.

Möbius Cancellation has moved from tensor geometry to source arithmetic, from a monomial moment to an endpoint-optimal detector, and now from high moments to a flat sparse second moment. MC-269 writes the Christoffel endpoint energy as a quadratic-character large-sieve matrix whose target is its literal diagonal. The endpoint is diagonal-scale orthogonality for the exact fixed-weight shell-product/lower-prime-product family, preserving off-diagonal cancellation; it is not more universal Krawtchouk algebra, a higher monomial moment, or a generic all-moduli large sieve.

Nyman--Beurling has moved from one-state visibility to fixed-finite interaction, then from raw volume crowding to canonical conditioning. NB-096 cancels the raw Cauchy determinant penalty exactly. NB-097 shows every canonical pivot is one and every distinct two-zero subsystem is uniformly conditioned. The endpoint is therefore genuinely many-body: collective unit-triangular shear as the divisor grows, finite-window/discretization uniformity, confluence, or target conversion—not pairwise zero separation.

Prime Circle has moved from source phase to symmetry quotient, then to finite-section placement, and now to a regularity boundary. PC-280 classifies truncation as `P_(p,B) D_f P_(r,B)`. PC-281 shows the complete singular spectrum of every sufficiently regular profile in the PNT transport regime is Li-grid reproducible. The endpoint is microscopic/sharp or singular profile structure that defeats this transport, or a construction outside the single-profile finite-section category—not generic window placement.

Prime Flute has moved from local critical survival to an integrated destination test. PF-324 gives two arithmetic terminal geometries, PF-325 turns the Hilbert--Schmidt budget into an integral of packet leakage, PF-326 proves exact seam normalization preserves fixed-band translation/localization, and PF-327 shows the same normalized shape retains a positive compactification-critical transverse-energy floor. The endpoint is now strictly downstream: the one-cusp-pant crossing, physical `L/H` projection, common completion and final intrinsic normalization. Neither seam-coordinate delocalization nor pre-pant energy dilution is still an escape.

Prime Lattice separates source regularity, sign and operator realization. PL-295 shows subcritical topology cannot protect sign; PL-296 rejects an explicit Volterra torsion shortcut because its `ell(delta)` boundary order contradicts the audited `ell(delta)^(1/2)` zero-exterior law. The endpoint remains source-specific suppression/order information from the actual localized-Weil equation, not a generic regularity or inverse-formula bypass.

Robin Extremal has changed currency more dramatically than the fourth-moment frontier suggested. RE-098--RE-099 prove a genuine `3/8` obstruction inside the global fourth-moment architecture, but RE-100 shows the sparse-host destination can use a cheaper statistic: a second-moment large sieve plus current zero-density estimates already gives the required polynomial host saving through `beta` roughly `0.814--0.815`. The endpoint is now only the **high-beta tail**, where density is thinner and shifted-energy/kernel machinery should be re-optimized. The critical-line fourth-moment obstruction remains true but is no longer the sparse-host bottleneck that must be repaired globally.

Visual Exploration has moved beyond fixed collision windows. VIS-207 shows the positive autocorrelation shell energy remains dilute whenever `H A^(2+4alpha)(log y)^2/y^2 -> 0`; the endpoint is now the limiting/intermediate sinc-weighted shell range, not the fixed-scale or moderately growing close-pair regime.

Weil Inertia has moved past an overly strong scalarization. WI-278 shows current scalar aperture-profile data cannot force the source reserve past `K_+`. WI-279 restores Hilbert cut orientation and obtains a strictly stronger bottom-Toeplitz reserve, proving that scalarization lost real information; nevertheless the same scalar-profile ceiling still blocks every such reserve because its numerator has not gained source-specific strength. The endpoint is exact source/null equations, noncommensurate compatibility, nodal/shape information or archimedean sign geometry—not another universal coefficient optimization.

Weil Positivity has sharpened the canonicality boundary twice. WP-285 shows zero-independent growing memory can generically erase the favorable-RH zero screen. WP-286 shows even Haar/Følner averaging canonically forced by the ambient multiplicative scale group is arithmetic-blind. The endpoint is therefore **source specificity beyond ambient symmetry**: a finite-prime/archimedean coupling, boundary law, nonlinear operation or cohomological structure whose selection and sign are not shared by generic logarithmic dynamics.

The durable rule is: after a bulk mechanism is closed, re-identify both the mathematical type and the provenance requirement of the residual before strengthening anything. A singular representation can become an incidence/conditioning theorem; a composition problem can become branch-sensitive scale descent; a moment problem can become a sparse quadratic second moment; a finite-state interaction problem can become collective shear; a symmetry escape can become microscopic regularity; a local packet can become a completed response budget after both coordinate and energy survival are priced; a fourth-moment obstruction can collapse to a narrow high-beta tail once the destination accepts a second moment; and a successful canonical average can become a source-specificity problem.

**Boundary.** This synthesis does not imply that the residual carriers are small, easier, rare, mutually equivalent or sufficient for RH. It creates no transfer theorem between lines. Its purpose is to prevent work from continuing in a mathematical currency that the current exact reductions have already exhausted.
