# MI-006 — Classify the complete boundary/control model, including finite defects, stochastic rigidity, gauges, and collision limits, before calling an escape arithmetic

**Evidence level:** supported by independent exact boundary classifications across the active Mathia research lines

## Core intuition

Many plausible advances arise by leaving a category that has just failed: add randomness, nonlocality, a spectral parameter, a singular determinant, richer local statistics, a larger buffer, or a stronger matrix invariant. The recurring danger is that the enlarged carrier itself has a complete universal/classical control model, sometimes visible only after a finite defect, normalization gauge, or collision limit is included.

The correct order is: identify the carrier change, determine the strongest admissible control family including finite and singular boundaries, compute the exact information quotient and quantitative visibility budget, and only then credit a residual.

## Consequence for synthesis

A proposed escape should be credited only after four things are explicit: the enlarged carrier, its complete matched/null family including finite and singular limits, the target-scale conditioning/visibility budget, and the residual that survives the final assembly. Increasing randomness, nonlocality, spectral dimension, determinant sophistication, matrix rank, visual order, or buffer width is not evidence by itself.

## Status / novelty

The component diffraction, renewal, approximation-property, maximum-entropy, Feshbach, marked-length, inertia, Matrix-Tree, and localization mechanisms are classical or persisted exact results. The synthesis is a cross-line research discipline, not a universal theorem that every category change classicalizes.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a source-forced carrier whose complete admissible control family cannot reproduce the leading signal and whose residual remains quantitatively coercive in the final category, or show that one of the stated control classifications fails inside its exact hypotheses.
