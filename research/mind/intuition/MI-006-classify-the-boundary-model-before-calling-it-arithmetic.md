# MI-006 — Classify the complete boundary/control model, including stochastic closures, moving families, gauges, deterministic quotients, and collision limits, before calling an escape arithmetic

**Evidence level:** supported by independent exact boundary classifications across the active Mathia research lines

## Core intuition

Many plausible advances arise by leaving a category that has just failed: add randomness, a moving comparator, nonlocality, a spectral smoothing, a singular determinant, richer visuals, a different positive metric, or overlapping localization. The recurring danger is that the enlarged carrier itself has a complete universal/classical boundary model.

The correct order is: identify the carrier change, determine the strongest admissible control family including finite and singular limits, compute the exact information quotient and quantitative visibility budget, and only then credit a residual.

## Consequence for synthesis

A proposed escape should be credited only after four things are explicit: the enlarged carrier, its complete matched/null family including singular and moving limits, the target-scale conditioning/resolution budget, and the residual that survives final assembly. Increasing randomness, mobility, nonlocality, smoothing resolution, metric sophistication, visual multiplicity, or overlap is not evidence by itself.

## Status / novelty

The component diffraction, character-sum, spectral-continuity, SPD, Feshbach, and localization mechanisms are classical or persisted exact results. The synthesis is a cross-line research discipline, not a universal theorem that every category change classicalizes.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a source-forced carrier whose complete admissible boundary/control family cannot reproduce the leading signal and whose residual remains quantitatively coercive in the final category, or show that one of the stated control classifications fails inside its exact hypotheses.
