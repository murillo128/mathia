# MI-006 — Classify the complete boundary/control model before calling an escape arithmetic

**Evidence level:** supported by independent exact boundary classifications across the active Mathia research lines

## Core intuition

Many plausible advances arise by leaving a category that has just failed: add randomness, a moving comparator, a new positive derivative, richer scalar tests, visual higher-order statistics, or overlapping localization. The enlarged carrier can still have a complete universal/classical control model.

The correct order is to identify the carrier change, determine its strongest admissible controls including singular/moving limits, compute the exact information quotient and quantitative budget, and only then credit a residual.

## Strongest justified principle

Analytic Frontier provides an instructive reversal. Natural stochastic diffraction controls fail, but ANF-033 proves that even the complete finite-real diffraction closure does **not** enforce the Montgomery--Taylor ceiling. The next control must therefore be the actual complex/conjugation-invariant zero geometry, not ever richer real stochastic surrogates.

Möbius Cancellation shows that moving-character controls must include conductor and uniformity resources: MC-057--MC-061 force quadratic/higher-order conductor growth, pair repulsion, and twisted-Möbius costs. Visual Exploration classicalizes the first three-gap residual into Markov likelihood, Pearson interaction, and correspondence analysis, with singular-value compression losing orientation.

Weil Inertia exposes the opposite scalar boundary: finite scalar tests can be repaired by remote spectral mass, while broad source-free universality forces Fourier positivity or its Gaussian-smoothed finite-depth version. Weil Positivity shows that canonical positive differentiation can erase the prime-power support carried by the underlying potential. Xi Flow shows that changing from fixed stencils to symmetric overlapping discriminants genuinely repairs collision signs, but the resulting local algebra remains universal and must still pass source controls.

## Consequence for synthesis

A proposed escape should be credited only after the enlarged carrier, complete matched family, target-scale conditioning/resolution budget, and surviving source-specific residual are explicit. The strongest control can change direction: sometimes a larger control closes a route; sometimes, as in ANF-033, the supposed universal control itself is decisively separated and the frontier must move closer to the actual source.

## Status / novelty

The component mathematics is classical or persisted exact work. The synthesis is a cross-line discipline, not a theorem that every enlargement classicalizes.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a source-forced carrier whose complete admissible control family reproduces the claimed residual despite the proposed distinction, or derive a residual that remains quantitatively coercive after the strongest source-appropriate control is applied.
