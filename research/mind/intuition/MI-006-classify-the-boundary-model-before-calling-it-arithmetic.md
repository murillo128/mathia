# MI-006 — Classify the complete boundary/control model, including finite defects and visibility horizons, before calling an escape arithmetic

**Evidence level:** supported by independent exact boundary classifications across the active Mathia research lines

## Core intuition

Many plausible advances arise by leaving a category that has just failed: add more tests, use a nonlinear repair, smooth more aggressively, pass to a singular endpoint, refine a conductor, inspect richer local fields, replace one weak spectral number by a larger spectrum, or move to an endpoint function space. The recurring danger is that the new carrier itself has a universal or classical complete control model, sometimes visible only in a finite boundary defect rather than in the thermodynamic or asymptotic bulk.

The correct order is: identify the carrier change, determine the strongest admissible control family including finite and boundary-relaxed configurations, compute the exact information quotient and target-scale visibility budget, and only then credit the residual.

## Consequence for synthesis

A proposed escape should be credited only after four things are explicit: the new carrier, its complete control/null family including finite defects, the target-scale conditioning or visibility budget, and the residual that survives the final assembly. Increasing multiplicity, smoothing order, point count, refinement depth, spectral dimension, visual scale, or asymptotic window size is not evidence by itself.

The strongest positive pattern is a source-forced carrier whose complete boundary model already contains a quantitative sign/coercivity mechanism. The strongest negative is an exact factorization, finite counterconfiguration, or matched control showing that the apparent new structure is universal, prime-blind, normalization-driven, wrongly oriented, delayed beyond the observed window, or measured in the wrong quotient.

## Status / novelty

The component Fourier, Mellin, stability, Banach-space, Riesz, Bloch, Poisson--Jensen, Schur-complement, Green, pseudodeterminant, Orlicz, and fractional-localization mechanisms are classical or persisted exact results. The synthesis is a cross-line research discipline, not a universal theorem that every category change classicalizes.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a source-forced carrier change whose complete admissible control family cannot reproduce the leading signal and whose residual remains quantitatively coercive in the final category, or show that one of the stated complete-control classifications fails inside its exact hypotheses.
