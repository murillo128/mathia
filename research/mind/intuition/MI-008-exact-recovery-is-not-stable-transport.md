# MI-008 — Source restriction, exact recovery, finite-height conditioning and destination survival are different resources

**Evidence level:** supported by Arithmetic Fidelity through [AF-322](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-322-prime-middle-singular-conditioning-has-a-finite-height-polynomial-modulus.md) and Prime Flute through [PF-328](../../prime_flute/findings/PF-328-critical-packet-intervals-restore-explicit-seam-parity-scales.md). No universal stability modulus or theorem transferring either mechanism between lines is claimed.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the source-fiber hierarchy explicit. AF-320 repairs exact eight-prime recovery on the middle-singular branch: prime-log arithmetic excludes the deeper denominator failure and the fifth harmonic recovers the missing product phase. AF-321 shows why exact exclusion is not yet stable fidelity: matched rank-one controls approach the regular-octagon closure with `s_3 -> 0`, so relation rank supplies no positive uniform margin.

AF-322 adds the source-specific quantitative layer. For every **fixed** eight-prime support, Baker-type logarithmic lower bounds prevent the prime orbit from approaching the four-antipodal-pair failure geometry faster than a power of the time height, while Kronecker recurrence still makes the uniform asymptotic gap zero. On an exact middle-singular hit, Łojasiewicz transfer gives a polynomial lower bound for `|s_3|`. The fixed source therefore has zero uniform margin but a nontrivial finite-height conditioning modulus. Exact incidence, fixed-height stability and support-uniform stability are separate questions.

Prime Flute supplies the complementary destination lesson. PF-326--PF-327 show that the correct seam coordinates neither delocalize the compactification-critical packet nor erase its pre-pant transverse energy. PF-328 then strengthens the witness from one center to a positive-length interval in the exact translation parameter integrated by PF-325, while restoring explicit parity-dependent seam scales.

That stronger intermediate survival still does not determine the completed pant-side angle. If the required Hilbert--Schmidt budget holds, the final completion must suppress order-one response on almost all of that positive-measure critical family. Coordinate stability, pre-pant energy survival, measure-theoretic prevalence and final destination survival are therefore four distinct resources.

The cross-line diagnostic is now quantitative: **name the admissible source class, take the closure relevant to the failure geometry, quotient genuine nuisance symmetries, determine exact separating power, identify the finite-height/source-size modulus, and only then measure the witness in the metric and averaging law consumed by the destination.** Exact injectivity on a nonclosed class is not stable fidelity; an upstream witness occupying positive measure is not final survival unless the downstream operator preserves it.

**Boundary.** AF-322 is fixed-support and does not solve exact middle-singular incidence; PF-328 stops before completed pant/projection/common-normalization geometry. The synthesis is a hierarchy of quantitative obligations, not a theorem connecting prime phases to flute surfaces.
