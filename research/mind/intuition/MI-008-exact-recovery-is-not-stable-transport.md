# MI-008 — Source restriction, exact recovery, quotient cost and stable transport are different resources

**Evidence level:** supported by Arithmetic Fidelity through [AF-320](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-320-eight-prime-singular-recovery-is-repaired-by-the-fifth-harmonic.md) and Prime Flute through [PF-327](../../prime_flute/findings/PF-327-seam-normalized-critical-packet-shapes-retain-compactified-transverse-energy.md). No universal target modulus, arithmetic source model or theorem transferring either operator between lines is claimed.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the source-fiber hierarchy explicit. Source restriction can shrink the admissible geometry without giving a stable failure margin: fixed saturated equal-weight prime supports can approach zero arbitrarily closely, while exact incidence differs sharply between support classes. Exact failure membership, distance from failure and non-vacuity are therefore already separate resources.

The low-support zero fibers add a second separation. At five points, conditional on a hit, `(F_P(2t),F_P(3t))` recovers the complete unordered phase multiset, while six points cross the one-complex-coordinate quotient threshold. AF-316 generalizes the generic recovery bill by parity. AF-317--AF-318 then show that the eight-point middle singular fiber survives source-independent geometry and character-rank tests.

AF-320 is the useful correction: the actual eight-prime source repairs that singular **representation** without excluding the singular event. On `e_4=0`, prime-log rank forces `e_3!=0`, and the fifth harmonic recovers the product phase exactly; `(s_2,s_3,s_4,s_5)` therefore recovers every centered eight-prime configuration. Yet first incidence remains open and the formula divides by `s_3`, for which no quantitative source lower bound is known. Exact recoverability, source admissibility and stable conditioning remain three different bills even after the representation is globally repaired.

Prime Flute supplies the complementary operator-metric lesson. PF-319 proves that a small relative form perturbation can look arbitrarily large as a raw mixed block after congruence by an unrelated seam metric. PF-320 shows that the actual positive-form angle is a different destination: its own diagonal energies normalize away that anisotropy, so multiplicative form closeness gives controlled intrinsic-angle perturbation even when the raw seam-normalized block is badly amplified.

PF-322--PF-325 then isolate a critical fixed-band packet whose final relevance is measured by an integrated Hilbert--Schmidt response. PF-326 closes one coordinate loophole: the exact seam-energy inverse square root only reweights that fixed band by bounded nonvanishing multipliers, commutes with tangential translation, and preserves fixed-scale localization. PF-327 closes the stronger possibility that the reweighting preserves localization but destroys the compactification-critical energy: the same shape-normalized packet retains a strictly positive order-one transverse-form floor before the pant-side operations.

Thus changing to the correct seam coordinates does not erase either the packet or its pre-pant criticality, but that fact still does not determine the completed pant-side `L/H` angle. Coordinate stability, energy survival at an intermediate form, and destination survival are three different resources.

The cross-line diagnostic is therefore more precise than “preserve information”: **name the admissible source class, quotient the genuine nuisance symmetry, determine the dimension/separating power of what remains, verify that the target event is non-vacuous, and measure conditioning in the metric consumed downstream.** A source restriction can repair exact singular recovery without giving a stable margin; a coordinate change can preserve a localized energetic witness without forcing it to survive the final operator.

**Boundary.** AF-320 concerns centered eight-prime unit-circle phase geometry and exact source-class reconstruction; PF-326--PF-327 concern seam-normalized fixed-band boundary packets before the completed angle. The synthesis is destination-relative accounting, not a theorem connecting their mathematical objects.
