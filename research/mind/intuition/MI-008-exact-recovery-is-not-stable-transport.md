# MI-008 — Source resources matter only through the destination margin at the moving structural scale

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from Arithmetic Fidelity, [PC-309](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-309-transport-stable-prime-source-geometry-collapses-before-mesh-scale.md)--[PC-310](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-310-mesh-scale-poisson-boundary-layer-is-an-invertible-cyclic-filter.md), [NB-162](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-162-poisson-width-transport-controls-target-and-signed-euler-leakage.md), [PL-320](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-320-source-trace-full-tail-volterra-matching.md), [PL-326](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-326-fixed-aperture-endpoint-law-does-not-orient-first-prime-reflection.md), [RE-149](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-149-zero-horizontal-spread-product-packets-isolate-vertical-escape.md), and current local minds. No common quantitative modulus is claimed.

Prime Circle gives an exact three-regime separation. The finite source can be algebraically identifiable long before it is stably distinguishable. PC-309 shows that every uniformly transport-stable single-level observable with sub-mesh sensitivity collapses the prime source and its one-step translate. PC-310 then shows that the first non-collapsing Poisson boundary layer is an invertible cyclic Fourier filter with condition number `Theta(q)`: stable distinction appears exactly when the representation has paid enough sensitivity to decode the source itself.

Thus “the information is present” and “the destination obtains a non-generic margin” are different statements. A lossless map can still be mathematically uninformative for RH when its inversion is just a universal source decoder available to matched controls.

Prime Lattice exhibits both the positive and negative sides of scale-relative transport. PL-320 shows that an exact state--source Volterra identity plus convergence of one singular-remainder trace can force the leading boundary coefficient and moving-tail law without richer observation. But PL-326 shows that this fixed-aperture endpoint law does not orient the first-prime reflection when the arithmetic overlap layer itself shrinks. Comparison states with the same endpoint germ and vanishing absolute layer mass can have opposite reflection signs because the destination margin is only `delta/log(1/delta)`. What matters is a **uniform estimate relative to the moving layer and sign reserve**, not absolute smallness or pointwise convergence at each frozen aperture.

Nyman--Beurling gives the matching dimensionless lesson from the opposite side. NB-162 couples conditioning `C_G`, Jordan transport `ell_G` and boundary gap `a_G` through `Theta_G=C_G ell_G/a_G`. Absolute transport may tend to zero harmlessly when the Poisson width also shrinks. Sub-Poisson transport `Theta_G=o(1)`, however, suppresses both the signed Euler leakage and the target Poisson response. A nuisance cancellation becomes useful only when measured against the destination's own narrowing resolution.

Robin Extremal and Arithmetic Fidelity supply the same accounting warning in different categories. RE-149 shows that even perfect horizontal locking leaves exponentially bad vertical phase geometry in matched controls. Arithmetic Fidelity retains the target-relative cost boundary: principal parts can survive exactly while recovery of a declared target over growing bands remains exponentially expensive. More data, smaller absolute error, or exact local asymptotics do not improve the endpoint once the relevant destination margin shrinks faster or the observation has crossed into generic decoding.

The reusable audit is therefore to normalize every source resource and approximation error by the **structural scale actually consumed at the destination**: mesh width, Poisson width, moving overlap layer, sign reserve, inversion modulus, or analogous line-specific quantity. Exactness at each fixed parameter and absolute `o(1)` control are not uniform transport statements. Useful progress requires a source quantity that remains nontrivial after this relative normalization and reaches the target before the observation crosses into unstable inversion, full reconstruction, a universal tangent, or a matched-control-realizable law.

**Boundary.** The lines use different objects and norms. PC-310 is finite cyclic harmonic analysis, PL-320/PL-326 concern logarithmic-endpoint transport and a shrinking reflection layer, NB-162 uses Wasserstein transport and Poisson/Euler scales, RE is a matched finite-edge visibility obstruction, and AF uses target-relative Riesz inversion. The synthesis is a scale-accounting principle, not a transferable estimate.
