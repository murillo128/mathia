# MI-008 — Source resources matter only through the destination margin at the moving structural scale

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from Arithmetic Fidelity through [AF-380](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-380-prime-gap-bounds-give-finite-prime-log-negativity-witnesses.md), [PC-309](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-309-transport-stable-prime-source-geometry-collapses-before-mesh-scale.md)--[PC-310](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-310-mesh-scale-poisson-boundary-layer-is-an-invertible-cyclic-filter.md), [NB-162](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-162-poisson-width-transport-controls-target-and-signed-euler-leakage.md), [PL-320](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-320-source-trace-full-tail-volterra-matching.md), [PL-326](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-326-fixed-aperture-endpoint-law-does-not-orient-first-prime-reflection.md)--[PL-330](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-330-first-prime-norm-resolvent-graph-topology.md), [RE-164](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-164-quantitative-pnt-fidelity-collapses-terminal-generalized-source-ambiguity.md)--[RE-170](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-170-critical-mertens-matching-still-permits-compensated-local-robin-excursions.md), and [WP-333](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-333-three-point-mangoldt-martingales-evade-weak-scalar-rigidity.md)--[WP-337](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-337-approximate-barycentric-preservation-needs-bias-times-propagation-control.md). No common quantitative modulus is claimed.

Across these lines, “the information is present” and “the destination obtains a usable non-generic margin” are different statements. The relevant source resource is not its absolute size or exactness but its size **after normalization by the moving decision boundary, interaction scale, propagation radius, compensation capacity or inversion modulus actually consumed by the destination**.

Prime Circle gives an exact reconstruction version. PC-309 shows that every uniformly transport-stable single-level observable with sub-mesh sensitivity collapses the prime source and its one-step translate. PC-310 shows that the first non-collapsing Poisson boundary layer is an invertible cyclic Fourier filter with condition number `Theta(q)`: stable distinction appears exactly when enough sensitivity has been paid to decode the source itself. Losslessness can therefore arrive only after crossing into generic source reconstruction rather than an RH-selective statistic.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the same distinction inside sampling. AF-379 proves that vanishing prime-log mesh already determines continuous translation total positivity exactly. AF-380 then shows what finite visibility costs. A fixed negative determinant with margin `eta` survives row approximation radius `r` only when the determinant perturbation is smaller than `eta`; prime-gap bounds turn that into a finite witness scale. Exact determination and finite witness conditioning are therefore separate resources even for the same sample set.

Prime Lattice makes the moving-margin distinction especially sharp. PL-326--PL-327 show that fixed-aperture endpoint control and the canonical half-log regularity can leave a shrinking reflection of size only `delta/L_delta` vulnerable to a sign-reversing residual. PL-329 identifies the source-side scale that excludes that residual, while PL-330 proves norm-resolvent continuity and continuous simple eigenbranches across the first-prime activation. Nevertheless the sharp hostile residual can itself tend to zero in that graph norm while reversing the sign. A topology sufficient for spectral transport is not automatically calibrated to a finer moving destination observable.

Nyman--Beurling gives the corresponding dimensionless transport currency. NB-162 couples conditioning, Jordan transport and boundary gap through `Theta_G=C_G ell_G/a_G`. Absolute transport may vanish while remaining order one relative to the shrinking Poisson width. Nuisance removal helps only after being priced against the destination resolution it is supposed to improve.

Robin Extremal separates global conservation from local rigidity. RE-164--RE-168 identify the moving KV source-capacity boundary. RE-169 shows that critical-scale Mertens normalization turns a local Robin displacement into future compensation debt. RE-170 then proves that even `o((sqrt p log p)^(-1))` Mertens mismatch does not control the selected prefix while later KV capacity remains large: an order-one first-shell excursion can be repaid in a second shell. The destination margin is therefore not only the normalization precision but also the **remaining spatial capacity to compensate it**.

Weil Positivity gives the sharpest moving-observable ledger. WP-336 shows that under exact barycentric preservation the variance defect is controlled by channel error times bidirectional propagation,

`||Q_R|| <= (1/2)||Psi_R-I|| sup_x L_(-,R)L_(+,R)`.

WP-337 adds the independent price of approximate preservation. With coordinate bias `beta_x=Psi_R(X_R)(x)-X_R(x)`, the rowwise defect also contains `beta_x(L_+-L_-)`. Hence small channel norm and small absolute coordinate bias are jointly insufficient unless they are subcritical after multiplication by their **different** geometric amplifiers: bidirectional spread for escape mass and propagation asymmetry for barycentric bias. A dyadic Mangoldt control retains fixed defect with both raw errors tending to zero because the bias-times-asymmetry product stays critical.

The reusable audit is to identify the **decision margin and its intrinsic coordinate before measuring error**. Depending on the line this may be a row-pattern covering radius versus determinant margin, a mesh-scale inverse condition number, a Poisson width, a shrinking reflection reserve, a monotone source-capacity coordinate, remaining compensation capacity, or one of several channel-error × propagation products. Exactness at each fixed parameter, absolute `o(1)` control, norm-resolvent continuity, graph convergence, matched global normalization or agreement at a leading exponent are not uniform destination statements unless they dominate every moving scale the destination actually consumes.

**Boundary.** These mechanisms are not quantitatively interchangeable. AF-380 is a determinant-continuity witness theorem, PC-310 finite cyclic harmonic analysis, PL-329--PL-330 a source-amplitude/topology separation, NB-162 a Wasserstein/Poisson transport estimate, RE-169--RE-170 a conservation-plus-capacity boundary, and WP-336--WP-337 sharp martingale variance inequalities. The synthesis is a resource-accounting principle: every proposed bridge must expose its own target margin, structural scale, regularity/conditioning map and source class rather than importing a modulus from another line.
