# MI-008 — Source resources matter only through the destination margin at the moving structural scale

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from Arithmetic Fidelity through [AF-380](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-380-prime-gap-bounds-give-finite-prime-log-negativity-witnesses.md), [PC-309](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-309-transport-stable-prime-source-geometry-collapses-before-mesh-scale.md)--[PC-310](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-310-mesh-scale-poisson-boundary-layer-is-an-invertible-cyclic-filter.md), [NB-162](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-162-poisson-width-transport-controls-target-and-signed-euler-leakage.md), [PL-320](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-320-source-trace-full-tail-volterra-matching.md), [PL-326](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-326-fixed-aperture-endpoint-law-does-not-orient-first-prime-reflection.md)--[PL-330](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-330-first-prime-norm-resolvent-graph-topology.md), [RE-164](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-164-quantitative-pnt-fidelity-collapses-terminal-generalized-source-ambiguity.md)--[RE-166](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-166-lambert-w-inversion-localizes-the-kv-capacity-transition.md), and [WP-333](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-333-three-point-mangoldt-martingales-evade-weak-scalar-rigidity.md)--[WP-336](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-336-ucp-kadison-defect-is-controlled-by-channel-norm-times-bidirectional-propagation.md). No common quantitative modulus is claimed.

Across these lines, “the information is present” and “the destination obtains a usable non-generic margin” are different statements. The relevant source resource is not its absolute size or exactness but its size **after normalization by the moving decision boundary, interaction scale, propagation radius, or inversion modulus actually consumed by the destination**.

Prime Circle gives an exact reconstruction version. PC-309 shows that every uniformly transport-stable single-level observable with sub-mesh sensitivity collapses the prime source and its one-step translate. PC-310 shows that the first non-collapsing Poisson boundary layer is an invertible cyclic Fourier filter with condition number `Theta(q)`: stable distinction appears exactly when enough sensitivity has been paid to decode the source itself. Losslessness can therefore arrive only after crossing into generic source reconstruction rather than an RH-selective statistic.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the same distinction inside sampling. AF-379 proves that vanishing prime-log mesh already determines continuous translation total positivity exactly. AF-380 then shows what finite visibility costs. A fixed negative determinant with margin `eta` survives row approximation radius `r` only when the determinant perturbation, bounded by `k k! M^(k-1) omega(r)`, is smaller than `eta`. Baker--Harman--Pintz gives prime-log covering radius `r_T<=exp(-19T/40)`, so every fixed strict violation has a finite witness; under local Hölder exponent `alpha`, its prime scale is polynomial in `eta^(-1)` with exponent `40/(19 alpha)`. Exact determination and finite witness conditioning are therefore separate resources even for the same sample set.

Prime Lattice makes the moving-margin distinction especially sharp. PL-326--PL-327 show that fixed-aperture endpoint control and the canonical half-log regularity can leave a shrinking reflection of size only `delta/L_delta` vulnerable to a sign-reversing residual. PL-329 identifies the source-side scale that excludes that residual: `(1+S_delta)/(|C_delta| sqrt(L_delta))->0`. PL-330 then proves that the full localized-Weil family is norm-resolvent continuous across the first-prime activation and simple eigenbranches are continuous even in the fixed logarithmic graph norm. Nevertheless the sharp hostile residual can itself tend to zero in that graph norm while reversing the sign. A topology sufficient for spectral transport is therefore not automatically calibrated to a finer moving destination observable.

Nyman--Beurling gives the corresponding dimensionless transport currency. NB-162 couples conditioning `C_G`, Jordan transport `ell_G`, and boundary gap `a_G` through `Theta_G=C_G ell_G/a_G`. Absolute transport may vanish while remaining order one relative to the shrinking Poisson width. Sub-Poisson transport suppresses both the target response and signed Euler leakage. Nuisance removal helps only after being priced against the destination resolution it is supposed to improve.

Robin Extremal adds a moving inversion boundary. RE-164--RE-165 identify the exact quantitative-PNT capacity `K_b(p,T)=sqrt(p) log p exp(-bV(T))/V(T)`. RE-166 inverts it: the capacity center is `v_*(p)=b^(-1)W_0(b log(p) sqrt(p))`, and all fixed positive capacity contours lie within `O(1)` in the intrinsic `V` coordinate. Rigidity requires `V(T)-v_*(p)->+infinity`; bounded displacement leaves order-one generalized-source ambiguity. The slowly varying motion of the center is much larger than the actual transition width, so a leading exponent or nominal equality can misclassify the source regime even when the exact capacity boundary is extremely sharp.

Weil Positivity gives a moving-observable version with a sharp product scale. WP-333--WP-335 show that a martingale/UCP channel can approach the identity under increasingly strong bounded-observable topologies while retaining fixed variance on the growing logarithmic coordinate. WP-336 identifies the missing normalization exactly. If `B_R=sup_x L_(-,R)(x)L_(+,R)(x)` is the bidirectional propagation budget, then

`||Psi_R(X_R^2)-X_R^2|| <= (1/2)||Psi_R-I|| B_R`.

Thus ordinary channel error is meaningful only after multiplication by the propagation scale of the actual observable. The WP-335 extremizer sits at the critical product `||Psi_R-I||B_R=Theta(1)`; subcritical product forces rigidity. This is the same resource-accounting pattern as a shrinking sign margin or Poisson width, but with a line-specific variance geometry rather than a transferable norm.

The reusable audit is to identify the **decision margin and its intrinsic coordinate before measuring error**. Depending on the line this may be a row-pattern covering radius versus determinant margin, a mesh-scale inverse condition number, a Poisson width, a shrinking reflection reserve, a monotone source-capacity coordinate, or channel error multiplied by bidirectional propagation. Exactness at each fixed parameter, absolute `o(1)` control, norm-resolvent continuity, graph convergence, or agreement at a leading exponent are not uniform destination statements unless they dominate the specific moving margin being consumed. Useful progress requires a source quantity that remains nontrivial after this normalization and reaches the target before the observation crosses into generic decoding, universal completion, matched-control ambiguity or critical-scale nonlocal escape.

**Boundary.** These mechanisms are not quantitatively interchangeable. AF-380 is a determinant-continuity witness theorem, PC-310 finite cyclic harmonic analysis, PL-329--PL-330 a source-amplitude/topology separation at a shrinking arithmetic reflection, NB-162 a Wasserstein/Poisson transport estimate, RE-166 an inversion of a KV-scale source capacity, and WP-336 a sharp martingale variance bound. The synthesis is a resource-accounting principle: every proposed bridge must expose its own target margin, structural scale, regularity/conditioning map and source class rather than importing a modulus from another line.
