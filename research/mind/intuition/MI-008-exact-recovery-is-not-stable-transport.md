# MI-008 — Stable transport is jointly limited by source resolution, destination danger, interface topology, and observable bandwidth

**Evidence level:** supported by exact adaptive-fidelity controls through AF-188, physical near-extremizer reductions through ANF-103, and exact Prime-Flute/Xi interface reductions through PF-206 and XF-100

## Core intuition

Exact recovery, small forward error, and even an exact source representation do not determine whether information reaches the final theorem stably. The correct transport problem has four distinct gates: **source resolution**, **destination danger geometry**, the **analytic topology of the interface**, and the **observable bandwidth/structure actually retained after transport**. These gates can move independently, and closing one should not be confused with closing the next.

Arithmetic Fidelity now gives an exact example where changing bandwidth changes the mathematical nature of the same matched control: derivative-limited invisibility becomes an algebraic harmonic filter once reciprocal frequencies are resolved. Analytic Frontier shows that destination instability must be measured against the actual dangerous physical direction and that source-negligible geometric escape can be trimmed away. Prime Flute and Xi Flow show how interface topology and physical scale can be simplified until the residual burden is exposed in the category actually consumed downstream.

## Strongest justified principle

AF-169--AF-177 show that after the correct output quotient, finite-Blaschke local inversion is governed by a separation/interpolation modulus. AF-180--AF-186 reveal a different family-wide obstruction: positive parity-split clusters remain exactly `W_1=delta` apart while higher cancellation order suppresses every observation allowed by a derivative budget. For general weights `M_m`, the optimized matched-control envelope is `exp[-Omega_(M,n_delta)(2/(A_delta delta))]`.

AF-187--AF-188 show why that envelope cannot be promoted to a universal inverse law. For the Euler logarithm, fixed positive reciprocal bandwidth makes the same controls order-one visible, while the logarithmic Rayleigh regime `delta T log(1/delta) -> lambda` has an exact algebraic discrepancy exponent `pi sigma x/lambda`. Source complexity, observation regularity, bandwidth, and kernel algebra must therefore be declared together.

ANF-097--ANF-099 supply the destination-side correction. A raw source Gram condition number can be dominated by harmless separable near-null directions, and convexifying the safe set fills the physical cone. For one declared notch, the exact relevant scalar is the physical near-extremizer exposure `beta_eta`; improvement exists exactly when it remains below the target ratio. ANF-100--ANF-103 then remove fake noncompactness: source-negligible sparse tails can be trimmed without changing the limiting exposure, while surviving source-heavy sub-square-root tails must pay an explicit logarithmic-plus-loglog height cost.

PF-203--PF-206 give an interface example where the category and scale determine the apparent burden. Form-level gluing removes an artificial cuff surface term; the native two-Hilbert-space factorization keeps both resolvents smooth; natural-width seam smoothing has uniform first metric regularity and summable physical `L log L` mass; but fixed-unit normalization destroys exactly that small-support currency. The remaining theorem must either keep physical scale inside the critical operator estimate or recover it through a separately proved weighted resolvent/square-function mechanism.

Xi Flow supplies a case where the current source interface is closed **only at a declared bandwidth**. XF-095--XF-097 realize the visible prefix and prove guarded fixed-time shadowing by an exact real periodic carrier. XF-099 shows that even an extensive collision transition can be exactly invisible below a symmetry frequency, and XF-100 shows that the whole current guarded Xi trajectory is asymptotically shadowed by dynamics generated from a prime-free endpoint prefix. Prime arithmetic first appears at a parametrically larger bandwidth where the current interface theorem no longer applies.

## Program consequence

For each source-to-destination bridge, declare together the destination quotient/norm and dangerous direction, admissible source geometry and complexity growth, observation regularity and algebraic structure, exact observable bandwidth, continuum/discrete or rough/smooth interface topology, and the destination coercivity theorem required after transport.

A scale change that activates new source information is not a harmless parameter adjustment if it invalidates the existing transport theorem. Conversely, exact transport at one scale does not license conclusions about information that lies outside that observable band.

## Counterevidence / boundary

AF-188 calibrates one explicit source family and kernel rather than the full inverse problem. ANF-099 still lacks the decisive physical exposure inequality on all surviving near-extremizers. PF-206 does not prove the scale-aware seam weak-`S_1` theorem. XF-100 does not prove exact positive-time continuum prime-independence below `lambda_2`; it proves guarded indistinguishability at the current scales, and a redesigned prime-band interface remains open.

These examples therefore support a modular transport discipline; they do not assert that every problem admits a canonical quotient, stable renormalization, or coercive destination variable.

## Epistemic status

**Supported cross-line synthesis from exact component theorems; no RH implication is claimed.**

## Falsification criterion

Produce a uniform source-to-destination theorem in one of the declared regimes while violating its exact source-resolution, destination-exposure, interface-scale, or bandwidth constraints; or show that a supposedly closed interface contributes a nonvanishing error in the exact downstream norm. Conversely, a source-forced theorem that crosses all declared gates and supplies destination coercivity would narrow this synthesis.
