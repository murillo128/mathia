# MI-008 — Source restriction, reachable fibers, non-vacuity and stable transport are different resources

**Evidence level:** supported by Arithmetic Fidelity through AF-307 and Prime Flute through PF-316. No universal target modulus, arithmetic source model or theorem transferring either operator between lines is claimed.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the source-fiber hierarchy explicit. AF-300 lowers the scalar observation bill from ambient dimension to the intrinsic dimension of an independently justified complex-affine source, but every remaining complex direction stays target-visible. AF-301--AF-305 then replace nullspaces by positive cones/convex reachable bodies and identify exact cap/support saturation as the categorical boundary where continuous coefficient freedom disappears.

AF-306--AF-307 show why even that is not enough to speak of “fidelity” without naming the destination currency. Every fixed saturated equal-weight prime support with `k>=2` can approach zero arbitrarily closely, so stable failure margin is zero. Yet exact failure membership varies discontinuously with the discrete source class: two-prime supports have exact zeros and same-fiber zero/safe collisions, whereas three- and four-prime supports are exactly zero-free on the imaginary axis.

This exposes a third resource beyond exact versus stable recovery: **non-vacuity**. An exact discriminator may factor perfectly through a compression simply because the admissible source class never realizes the bad event. Such vacuous exact fidelity carries no conditioning information when the source still accumulates on the failure set.

Prime Flute supplies the complementary positive-form case. PF-313--PF-316 show that an inverse-singular residual direction is harmless only when its source amplitude is paid at the same Schur-defect scale, and that in the full physical angle the residual factor `V_H` cannot hide bad behavior for free: its attenuation is quadratically charged to the direct angle `Z_LH`. Compactness/weak-trace is therefore a property of the coupled destination channels, not of one convenient representation alone.

The cross-line diagnostic is precise: **state the admissible source class, compute its target-reachable fibers, verify that the target event remains nontrivial on that class, and quantify the distance/defect scale actually consumed downstream.** Shrinking the source or exactly excluding failure can be mathematically real while leaving the relevant stable transport problem untouched.

**Boundary.** AF-307 concerns equal-weight distinct-prime Dirichlet polynomials on the imaginary axis, while the PF results concern positive block operators and principal angles. The synthesis is destination-relative accounting, not a theorem connecting their objects.
