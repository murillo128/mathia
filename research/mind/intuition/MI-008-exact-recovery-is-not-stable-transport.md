# MI-008 — Stable transport is limited by source complexity, local conditioning, dressing, and destination-specific observability

**Evidence level:** supported by exact arithmetic-fidelity complexity/regularity boundaries through AF-217, depth-uniform Analytic-Frontier geometry through ANF-136, dressed Prime-Flute transport and shear decomposition through PF-239, and Xi-flow observability conditioning through XF-133

## Core intuition

Exact recovery, exact source representation, compactness, or even a formally identifying target observable do not determine whether information reaches the final theorem stably. The effective transport problem is jointly controlled by **source complexity**, **observation-specific regularity and localization**, **global completion**, **physical dressing**, and the **conditioning of the exact destination quotient actually needed by the theorem**.

Recent evidence sharpens both sides. Some apparent global losses can be removed by exact structure: Analytic Frontier now controls copy/slack/span/chamber growth through nontrivial cascade depth, while Prime Flute propagates the critical local amplitude through the actual complete lifted seam and proves that all shear leakage touching the critical scaled population is already a strong trace-class error after reciprocal-prime weighting. Conversely, exact algebraic observability can hide exponentially bad conditioning: Xi Flow can reconstruct a periodic divisor from one trace while central state directions remain exponentially invisible to its entire future/local-patch observation.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity separates provenance, source complexity, and observation sign regularity. AF-215 shows that genuine rational-prime support still admits arbitrary-order Prouhet cancellation. AF-216 proves that `m` exact moment or equally spaced Dirichlet cancellations require at least `m` ordered sign changes, supplying a sharp source-complexity currency. AF-217 then shows that the full Euler-log kernel is not totally nonnegative of all orders. A source sign budget becomes useful only together with a matching finite-order quantitative variation-diminishing property of the observation map.

Analytic Frontier moves its same-profile cascade from global combinatorics to local conditioning. ANF-134--ANF-136 show that positive copy-rate slack protects the notch and translation span, cannot collapse faster than factorial scale, and keeps critical-chamber proliferation at `exp(O(d^2 log d))`. In the admitted sublogarithmic depth regime these costs are subpolynomial. The remaining transport risk is local: the recursively selected critical cell may approach inherited zeros and lose width, Hessian scale, or source mass.

Prime Flute supplies both a positive dressed-transfer theorem and an error-currency refinement. PF-236--PF-238 show that the critical `sqrt(w_1w_2)/s * F(sm)` envelope survives the exact variable-width diagonal corridor and the actual complete lifted seam even though the actual and matched seam forms have different spectral type. PF-239 then shows that PF-232 shear damage touching any fixed scaled band need not preserve that envelope: its extra `O(P_n^{-1})` perturbation factor times the `O(s_n^{-1})` channel count and reciprocal-prime commutator weight is `O(P_n^{-2})`, hence absolutely trace summable. The remaining local shear risk is only high-high.

Xi Flow demonstrates that destination identifiability and destination conditioning are independent. XF-130 gives exact one-channel periodic reconstruction. XF-131 shows a microscopic heat-delay clock for transition-sensitive central information, while XF-132--XF-133 construct simple unit-circle-admissible central packets with `O(1)` state size and exponentially small full future trace, uniformly even over a fixed macroscopic local complex patch. A source-to-target theorem cannot use exact recovery as a proxy for a usable modulus.

## Program consequence

For every source-to-destination bridge, declare together the admissible source class and complexity budget, the observation kernel's finite-order regularity/sign structure, any profile-weighted non-escape or local-cell conditioning resource, the global completion and physical dressing, the destination quotient or ideal, and the quantitative modulus by which the final witness can actually be recovered or controlled.

Also separate **baseline signal currency from error currency**. A perturbation may be harmless because it carries an extra summable source factor even if it does not preserve the baseline mode-by-mode envelope. Requiring every intermediate error to satisfy the strongest final signal estimate can obscure easier trace-class or finite-rank routes.

When an exact theorem removes one of those losses, remove it from the frontier rather than keeping a generic warning. Conversely, when the destination has an exponentially hidden direction, seek a theorem adapted to the final target functional instead of reconstructing a much larger state that the target does not need.

## Counterevidence / boundary

These results do not form one universal minimax theorem. AF-216--AF-217 leave the finite-order quantitative Euler modulus open; ANF-136 leaves critical-cell conditioning open; PF-239 leaves the infinite-rank high-high shear block and later global recoupling open; and XF-132--XF-133 prove full-state tomography instability rather than zero-delay instability of the periodic Newman transition itself.

A successful category-specific theorem can therefore bypass one of these obstructions by proving a source restriction, a local conditioning bound, a strong-ideal error estimate, a dressing stability theorem, or a target-specific quotient that discards the hidden directions. Such a theorem narrows the synthesis rather than contradicting the need to control the exact final interface.

## Epistemic status

**Supported cross-line synthesis from exact component theorems, including positive depth/dressing closures, strong-ideal error separation, and negative conditioning barriers; no RH implication is claimed.**

## Falsification criterion

Produce a final source-specific coercive theorem that remains uniform while its declared source complexity exceeds the observation's sign-regularity order, its critical cell loses every quantitative non-escape bound, its local dressed pieces accumulate beyond both the baseline and available error ideals, or its target map contains the exact hidden directions without paying their conditioning cost. Conversely, a source-compatible theorem proving the needed finite-order modulus, local-cell bound, high-high shear/global dressing, or transition-specific Xi quotient would remove the corresponding gate.
