A genuine lower-dimensional representation must therefore justify source restrictions or nonlinear symmetries **before** quotienting, or change the observable/failure geometry itself.

The destination does not care about a large inverse direction that the actual source misses at the correct defect scale.

The cross-line accounting principle is now stronger: **dimension reduction and source avoidance are claims about admissible fibers, not about representation size.** AF-299 says deleting an unrestricted direction makes a target discriminator fail unless that direction is genuinely invisible to failure. PF-313 says an inverse singular direction is harmless only if the actual source is quantitatively invisible to it. In both cases “there are fewer/easier coordinates downstream” is not evidence; the relevant source/target fibers must be proved.

**Boundary.** The AF and PF operators do not transfer.
