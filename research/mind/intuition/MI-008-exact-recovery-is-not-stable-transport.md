# MI-008 — Source resources matter only through the destination margin and resolution they preserve

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis supported by Arithmetic Fidelity through [AF-349](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-349-full-riesz-profile-is-exactly-recoverable-before-asymptotic-quotient.md), Prime Flute through [PF-334](../../prime_flute/findings/PF-334-hilbert-schmidt-relative-square-root-control-escapes-logarithmic-dimension-loss.md), Nyman--Beurling through [NB-130](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-130-linear-rank-formal-zero-power-packets-interpolate-arbitrary-natural-prefix-data.md), and Prime Lattice through [PL-314](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-314-moving-diagonal-tail-threshold.md). No common quantitative modulus is claimed.

Arithmetic Fidelity now separates not only injectivity from terminal loss but **strong-metric stability from weak-metric collapse**. AF-349 gives an exact finite-horizon isometry: derivative variation of the scaled Riesz profile equals the source coefficient `ell^1` distance up to `r!`. Yet the same perfect-power and dense controls remain `O(1)` apart in raw profile amplitude and become identical after the bounded-error quotient. The representation can therefore be perfectly conditioned in one topology and useless in the weaker topology consumed by the endpoint.

Prime Flute shows the complementary direction: paying for a stronger norm than the endpoint needs can manufacture a fake loss. PF-333 finds logarithmically worsening generic sensitivity in operator norm, while PF-334 gives dimension-free Hilbert--Schmidt control of the same relative square-root map under the normalized-insertion envelope. Since the downstream construction uses an averaged Hilbert--Schmidt budget, the terminal norm determines which conditioning bill is real.

Nyman--Beurling shows that structural constraints also have a **rank-to-observation** budget. NB-130 proves that finite recurrence and exponential-polynomial analyticity do not restrict a natural prefix when formal zero-power rank grows linearly with its length: an `R`-mode right-half packet interpolates arbitrary data on `1,...,R`. The surviving source information must therefore enter through actual-zero geometry, effective rank or conditioning relative to the observation depth, not from recurrence existence alone.

Prime Lattice adds a singular moving-observer version of the same rule. PL-314 shows that weighted `L^1` tail control is too weak for a Green kernel whose diagonal atom samples `Q=r/s`: a fixed mismatch can have arbitrarily small integrated tail mass but still contribute order one along a sparse sequence. The sufficient condition `Qm(Q)->0` works because it controls the exact moving evaluation scale. Integrated smallness and terminal visibility are different currencies.

The reusable diagnostic is concrete: identify the source family and resource parameters, the failure-set closure, the quantitative source modulus, the representation resolution, and the **exact terminal functional/norm/quotient together with any moving concentration scale**. Then prove transport in that currency. Do not infer loss because a signal is small in a weak norm, usefulness because inversion is stable in a stronger source-matched norm, or rigidity because a structural law exists at rank exceeding the visible data.

**Boundary.** AF-349 does not show that the RH-facing terminal observer controls derivative variation; PF-334 does not identify the exact PF-318 cross block with the whole normalized insertion; NB-130 uses freely placed formal exponents rather than actual zeta zeros and supplies no uniform coefficient norm; PL-314 gives a sufficient tail condition but not its necessity or its provenance from the actual completed-Weil branch. This is a cross-line resource-accounting principle, not a universal stability theorem.
