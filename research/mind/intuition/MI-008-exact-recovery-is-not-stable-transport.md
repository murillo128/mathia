# MI-008 — Source resources matter only through the destination margin and the limiting scale they preserve

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis supported by Arithmetic Fidelity through [AF-355](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-355-rh-cancellation-strengthens-moving-riesz-endpoint-obstruction.md), Nyman--Beurling through [NB-137](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-137-superill-conditioned-simple-clusters-can-converge-to-a-target-poor-confluent-state-space.md), Prime Flute through PF-334, Prime Lattice through PL-315, Robin Extremal through [RE-137](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-137-turan-nazarov-makes-vertical-escape-pay-in-packet-complexity.md), and Weil Positivity through [WP-314](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-314-empty-side-moving-julia-boundary-layer-is-universal-logarithmic-control.md). No common quantitative modulus is claimed.

Arithmetic Fidelity separates fixed-parameter success from a moving endpoint. Every fixed `delta>0` Riesz order preserves the off-critical discriminator and crosses the critical absolute-summability threshold, yet AF-354--AF-355 show that moving `delta=delta(X)` is a different theorem. Under RH every polynomially vanishing order remains close enough to the unsmoothed endpoint to inherit critical Hardy--Littlewood oscillation. A family of individually good representations need not be uniformly good along the limit actually used.

Nyman--Beurling separates three notions that can otherwise be conflated. NB-136 shows that all `R` natural samples can coexist with a superpolynomially collapsing lower frame bound for only `Theta(log R)` near-confluent modes. NB-137 then shows that this bad raw conditioning can be a coordinate singularity: simple clusters may converge to a confluent state space while the **entire state span is asymptotically target-poor**. Stable coordinates and target relevance are independent currencies.

Robin Extremal makes the observer anisotropic. RE-136 gives an exponential isotropic reciprocal-radius tariff, while RE-137 removes vertical diameter entirely from the leading observability constant: hiding costs horizontal spread and/or packet complexity. The coordinate in which noncompactness is measured matters as much as its size.

Weil Positivity supplies a limiting classicalization. WP-314 deliberately lets a Julia node move with the high-energy scale, so the transform and spectral limits do not commute. Nevertheless the node-scaled boundary layer is exactly the Julia reduction of the unit-density logarithmic control; the arithmetic remainder disappears. Nonuniformity itself is not a source discriminator when the rescaled source converges to a universal tangent.

Prime Flute and Prime Lattice retain the earlier complementary lessons. A stronger source norm can charge a loss the terminal Hilbert--Schmidt observer does not pay, while broad global regularity can miss a moving singular sample exactly at its critical scale.

The reusable diagnostic is therefore representation- and target-aware. Specify the source resource, destination topology and target projection, then identify every parameter or coordinate that moves with the asymptotic problem: smoothing order, packet rank/separation, basis versus subspace geometry, horizontal/vertical spread, transform node, representation dimension or moving sample. Prove a modulus uniform in that coupled regime and compare it with a matched control. Fixed-parameter invertibility, sample abundance, raw condition number, positivity or compact convergence do not substitute for that estimate.

**Boundary.** These examples share an accounting structure, not an operator or theorem. AF-355 is conditional on RH for its strongest moving-order obstruction; NB-136--NB-137 and RE-136--RE-137 use matched formal controls for method boundaries; WP-314 treats one canonical empty-side moving-node scaling. No rate or obstruction is transferred automatically between lines.
