# MI-008 — Stable transport is limited by profile-weighted non-escape, global completion, dressing, and destination realization

**Evidence level:** supported by exact adaptive-fidelity calibration through AF-204, full-profile near-extremizer controls through ANF-120, canonical Prime-Flute multiplicity through PF-227, and full q-scale Xi coercivity/realization through XF-119

## Core intuition

Exact recovery, small forward error, compactness, and an exact source representation do not determine whether information reaches the final theorem stably. The effective transport problem is set jointly by **source resolution and admissible optimization**, **source non-escape weighted by the surviving profile**, **global completion or destination elimination**, the **analytic topology/physical scale of the interface**, and the **resolution and realization requirements of the final witness family**.

The recent evidence also gives an important positive refinement. A gap between simple tests and the full witness cone is not permanent when the source admits the right positive resolution. XF-118 lifts uniform smooth-carrier source flatness to the entire q-scale Toeplitz cone, and XF-119 pushes that positivity one step further to exact equal-weight realization at the native Xi node budget. The rule is therefore not “intermediate tests are always insufficient,” but “the claimed resource must be proved to resolve and realize the exact destination object.”

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity now calibrates non-escape exactly in a solved varying-profile model. AF-200 shows that fixed `W_1` does not prevent Peano-carrier escape. AF-201--AF-204 sharpen this to the intrinsic fixed-band currency: transfer is equivalent to `Q Lambda_sigma(beta) -> 0`, and in the quartic five-node minimal-support class to `Q D -> 0`. Stable transport depends on the product of surviving profile size and observation-specific localization defect, not on source distance alone.

Analytic Frontier makes source aggregation and global completion exact. ANF-118 controls the complete interior-saddle translation profile by a Gaussian Gram form rather than finite jets. ANF-119 shows that a source-heavy packet embedded in a global near-extremizer must be excisable, force an overpolarized same-band companion, or be profile-mirrored. ANF-120 adds a notch-efficiency mismatch corridor and forces bounded-mass mirroring near maximal notch efficiency. The relevant object is the globally admissible profile-plus-companion, not the isolated packet.

Prime Flute exposes the difference between raw geometry and dressed ideal membership. PF-222 proves the reciprocal-prime Schur commutator compact; PF-223 shows abstractly that compact fixed-edge fluxes can miss weak trace class through growing multiplicity. PF-226 proves that the actual thin-pant crossing has order `s_n^{-1}` saturated channels, and PF-227 shows that fixed or slowly growing physical high-pass projection leaves that multiplicity intact. Any positive endpoint theorem must therefore use the quantitative Poisson/resolvent damping of the **dressed** recoupling words, not qualitative smoothing or low-sector dimension.

Xi Flow supplies the positive destination-resolution theorem. XF-113 shows coarse q-scale constraints permit negative matched controls; XF-114--XF-118 progressively lift actual source flatness to `||T_hat_K-I||_op -> 0` on the full Toeplitz cone. XF-119 then applies controlled backward/forward Poisson smoothing: near-isometry survives deconvolution, forces local regularity of a representing measure, and yields a positive density with the original first `K` moments. Equal-weight quadrature realizes those moments exactly at the prescribed degree because `N/K -> infinity`. The remaining transport problem has therefore moved to the transition theorem or prime-band interface, not static source admissibility.

## Program consequence

For every source-to-destination bridge, declare together the destination quotient/norm/ideal, every optimization or completion implicit in that destination, the admissible source class and its geometric degrees of freedom, the profile-weighted compactness/tightness property preventing escape, observation regularity/algebra/bandwidth, interface topology and normalization, the exact witness family, and any realization/cardinality constraint the final theorem actually needs.

Perform source optimization, aggregation, global completion, projection, Schur complement, dressing, limiting transport, resolution-matched testing, and exact realization before calling an intermediate resource quantitative. Conversely, once a source-compatible positive representation proves control and realization of the entire destination object, remove that gate from the frontier rather than continuing to treat it as an analogy-level limitation.

## Counterevidence / boundary

These results do not yield a universal minimax theorem. AF-204's exact weighted defect is class- and kernel-specific. ANF-120 does not rule out every fatal global completion. PF-226--PF-227 concern the raw crossing and do not prove the dressed cut flux fails weak trace class. XF-118--XF-119 prove q-scale full-cone positivity and exact static realization but do not imply `Lambda<=0`, control high periodic moments, or cover the first prime-band atoms.

The synthesis is therefore category-specific: a proposed bridge is not validated until its quantitative resource survives the exact admissible optimization/completion and resolves the destination object used by the final theorem; but a proved full-cone/full-realization lift is a genuine closure of that intermediate gate.

## Epistemic status

**Supported cross-line synthesis from exact component theorems, including an exact positive full-witness and exact-realization lift; no RH implication is claimed.**

## Falsification criterion

Produce a final source-specific coercive theorem that remains uniform while its declared carrier escapes the observation-sensitive region, its globally completed companion defeats the measured source profile, its local compact pieces accumulate beyond the destination ideal after dressing, or its final witness/realization constraints contain unresolved directions. Conversely, prove a source-compatible resolution-and-realization theorem analogous to XF-118--XF-119 in one of those categories; that would narrow the corresponding transport obstruction rather than falsify the general need to reach the actual destination object.
