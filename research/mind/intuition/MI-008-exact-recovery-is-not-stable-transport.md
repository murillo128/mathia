# MI-008 — Source restriction, exact recovery, quotient cost and stable transport are different resources

**Evidence level:** supported by Arithmetic Fidelity through [AF-321](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-321-rank-one-eight-point-singular-recovery-has-no-uniform-harmonic-conditioning.md) and Prime Flute through [PF-327](../../prime_flute/findings/PF-327-seam-normalized-critical-packet-shapes-retain-compactified-transverse-energy.md). No universal target modulus, arithmetic source model or theorem transferring either operator between lines is claimed.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the source-fiber hierarchy explicit. Generic low-harmonic recovery is dimension-limited, exceptional fibers can change the information bill, and source restrictions can repair exact recovery without excluding the exceptional event. AF-320 proves this sharply for eight prime phases: on the middle-singular branch `e_4=0`, prime-log relation rank forces `e_3!=0`, so `(s_2,s_3,s_4,s_5)` recovers the full centered configuration exactly.

AF-321 now proves that this exact repair has **no uniform conditioning consequence from character rank alone**. Rank-one middle-singular controls approach a regular octagon with `s_3->0`; their retained low harmonics converge to the same boundary datum while common rotation leaves macroscopically different product phases. The exact inverse therefore fails uniform continuity on the rank-one class. At the closure fiber the first seven power sums are blind to the missing phase and `s_8` is the first harmonic separator. A discrete exclusion of exact denominator failure can coexist with zero quantitative distance from the forbidden stratum.

Prime Flute supplies the complementary operator-metric lesson. PF-319 proves that a small relative form perturbation can look arbitrarily large as a raw mixed block after congruence by an unrelated seam metric. PF-320 shows that the actual positive-form angle is a different destination: its own diagonal energies normalize away that anisotropy, so multiplicative form closeness gives controlled intrinsic-angle perturbation even when the raw seam-normalized block is badly amplified.

PF-322--PF-325 isolate a critical fixed-band packet whose final relevance is measured by an integrated Hilbert--Schmidt response. PF-326 closes one coordinate loophole: the exact seam-energy inverse square root only reweights that fixed band by bounded nonvanishing multipliers, commutes with tangential translation, and preserves fixed-scale localization. PF-327 closes the stronger possibility that the reweighting preserves localization but destroys the compactification-critical energy: the same shape-normalized packet retains a strictly positive order-one transverse-form floor before the pant-side operations.

Thus changing to the correct seam coordinates does not erase either the packet or its pre-pant criticality, but that fact still does not determine the completed pant-side `L/H` angle. Coordinate stability, energy survival at an intermediate form, and destination survival are three different resources.

The cross-line diagnostic is therefore more precise than “preserve information”: **name the admissible source class, take its closure in the perturbation geometry actually used, quotient the genuine nuisance symmetry, determine exact separating power, and then measure the stability modulus in the metric consumed downstream.** Exact injectivity on a nonclosed source class is not stable fidelity; an intermediate energetic witness is not final destination survival.

**Boundary.** AF-321 concerns an eight-point harmonic compression and a matched rank-one closure class; PF-326--PF-327 concern seam-normalized fixed-band boundary packets before the completed angle. The synthesis is destination-relative accounting, not a theorem connecting their mathematical objects.
