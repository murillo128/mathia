# MI-008 — Source resources matter only through the destination margin and resolution they preserve

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis supported by Arithmetic Fidelity through [AF-351](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-351-one-riesz-order-is-the-critical-boundary-conditioning-threshold.md), Prime Flute through [PF-334](../../prime_flute/findings/PF-334-hilbert-schmidt-relative-square-root-control-escapes-logarithmic-dimension-loss.md), Nyman--Beurling through [NB-133](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-133-zero-count-visibility-and-vinogradov-korobov-depth-still-permit-superpolynomial-annihilator-collapse.md), Prime Lattice through [PL-315](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-315-moving-diagonal-survives-all-log-subcritical-sobolev.md), and Weil Positivity through [WP-311](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-311-finite-interior-schur-stripping-introduces-log-over-linear-gamma-contamination.md). No common quantitative modulus is claimed.

Arithmetic Fidelity separates source recovery from terminal conditioning in both directions. AF-349 gives an exact finite-horizon isometry: derivative variation of the scaled Riesz profile equals source coefficient `ell^1` distance up to `r!`, while the same perfect-power and dense controls remain `O(1)` apart in raw profile amplitude and become identical after the bounded-error quotient. AF-350 then shows that this weak amplitude quotient can still retain the narrower Dirichlet singularity discriminator above its declared error line.

AF-351 adds the complementary positive transformation example. At the sharp critical `sqrt(X)` boundary, order zero carries critical zero amplitudes at the non-absolutely-summable `1/|gamma|` scale, while one Riesz order changes them to `1/|gamma|^2` and makes the zero expansion absolutely summable. The zero frequencies and off-critical singularity obstruction remain. Better endpoint conditioning can therefore come from selectively damping nuisance accumulation rather than preserving or destroying information monotonically.

Prime Flute shows that paying for a stronger norm than the endpoint needs can manufacture a fake loss. PF-333 finds logarithmically worsening generic sensitivity in operator norm, while PF-334 gives dimension-free Hilbert--Schmidt control of the same relative square-root map under the normalized-insertion envelope. Since the downstream construction uses an averaged Hilbert--Schmidt budget, the terminal norm determines which conditioning bill is real.

Nyman--Beurling separates visibility from conditioning. NB-132 can force a genuine narrow polynomial-height zero packet below the recurrence-visibility rank budget. NB-133 then constructs matched formal packets obeying the same coarse rank and Vinogradov--Korobov radial-depth constraints whose full recurrence is visible but whose exact normalized annihilator margin is superpolynomially small. Population and radial depth do not control the angular arrangement that the target certificate multiplies.

Prime Lattice gives the moving-observer version. PL-314 shows that weighted `L^1` tail control is too weak for a Green kernel whose diagonal atom samples `Q=r/s`. PL-315 strengthens the point: one fixed mismatch may be smooth, lie in every `W^{k,1}`, have arbitrarily small physical `W^{1,1}`, belong to every `H^alpha` for `alpha<1/2` and every all-log Fourier moment space, and still sparsely saturate the critical `1/Q` scale exactly where the moving atom samples it. Integrated smallness and broad global regularity are different currencies from moving-scale anti-concentration.

Weil Positivity shows that even an admissible positivity-preserving transform can be badly conditioned for the **asymptotic hierarchy** the endpoint compares. WP-311 proves that fixed-interior scalar Schur stripping of the critical logarithmic Mangoldt response creates a positive `log(y)/y` term after end normalization, while the Riemann Gamma channel has no such term and the desired curvature sits at `y^-2`. Admissibility and positivity therefore do not imply discriminator-neutral transport: a transform can insert a nuisance term larger than the target scale.

The reusable diagnostic is concrete: identify the source family and resource parameters, the failure-set closure, the quantitative source modulus, the representation resolution, and the **exact terminal functional/norm/quotient together with any rank, phase-arrangement, moving-concentration or asymptotic scale**. Then compute how the proposed transform acts on both nuisance directions and the target discriminator. Do not infer loss because a signal is small in a weak norm, usefulness because inversion is stable in a stronger source-matched norm, coercivity because a recurrence is visible, tail control because a function is globally regular, or safety because a transform is admissible and positivity-preserving.

**Boundary.** These examples do not define a universal monotone notion of fidelity. AF-351 is a fixed-kernel conditioning theorem, PF-334 is norm-specific, NB-133 is a matched-control boundary rather than an actual-zero theorem, PL-315 is a moving-atom counterexample rather than a statement about the actual completed-Weil mismatch, and WP-311 concerns a canonical scalar fixed-interior Schur class. The common content is only the accounting rule: transport must be priced in the exact currency and scale consumed by the destination.
