# MI-008 — Source restriction, exact recovery, non-vacuity and stable transport are different resources

**Evidence level:** supported by Arithmetic Fidelity through AF-313 and Prime Flute through PF-320. No universal target modulus, arithmetic source model or theorem transferring either operator between lines is claimed.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the source-fiber hierarchy explicit. AF-300 lowers the scalar observation bill from ambient dimension to the intrinsic dimension of an independently justified complex-affine source, but every remaining complex direction stays target-visible. AF-301--AF-305 then replace nullspaces by positive cones/convex reachable bodies and identify exact cap/support saturation as the categorical boundary where continuous coefficient freedom disappears.

AF-306--AF-307 show why even that is not enough to speak of “fidelity” without naming the destination currency. Every fixed saturated equal-weight prime support with `k>=2` can approach zero arbitrarily closely, so stable failure margin is zero. Yet exact failure membership varies discontinuously with the discrete source class: two-prime supports have exact zeros and same-fiber zero/safe collisions, whereas three- and four-prime supports are exactly zero-free on the imaginary axis.

AF-308--AF-312 then separate first incidence from generic geometry and recurrence. At the first unresolved five-prime support, AF-313 adds a new exact-recovery layer: conditional on `F_P(t)=0`, the pair `(F_P(2t),F_P(3t))` recovers the complete unordered phase multiset because the only collision fiber is the rotated regular pentagon and distinct prime phases exclude it. Yet the reconstruction divides by the second harmonic and becomes arbitrarily ill-conditioned near that exceptional orbit. **Exact post-incidence recovery can therefore coexist with zero stable margin and an unresolved first-incidence problem.**

This exposes several distinct resources: source restriction, non-vacuity of the target event, exact failure incidence, exact recoverability inside a fiber, and stable conditioning of that recovery. One may improve while another stays completely open.

Prime Flute supplies the complementary operator-metric lesson. PF-319 proves that a small relative form perturbation can look arbitrarily large as a **raw mixed block** after congruence by an unrelated seam metric. PF-320 then shows that the actual positive-form angle is a different destination: its own diagonal energies normalize away that anisotropy, so multiplicative form closeness gives `O(beta)` intrinsic angle closeness and, at the canonical prime scale, exactly the PF-318 squared Hilbert--Schmidt endpoint budget. The reference shear-deleted angle remains open, but the same source estimate has opposite value depending on whether the destination is the raw block or the intrinsic correlation angle.

The cross-line diagnostic is precise: **state the admissible source class, compute its target-reachable fibers, verify that the target event remains nontrivial on that class, distinguish exact recovery from first incidence, and quantify the metric/normalization actually consumed downstream.** Shrinking the source, exactly reconstructing a hidden state, or proving a perturbation small in one norm can all be mathematically real while leaving a different destination theorem untouched.

**Boundary.** AF-313 concerns equal-weight five-point unit-circle phase geometry conditional on a hypothetical distinct-prime hit, while PF-319--PF-320 concern positive block forms and principal angles. The synthesis is destination-relative accounting, not a theorem connecting their objects.
