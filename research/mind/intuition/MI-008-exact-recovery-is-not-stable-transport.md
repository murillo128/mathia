# MI-008 — Source resources matter only through the destination margin and the limiting scale they preserve

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis supported by Arithmetic Fidelity through [AF-363](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-363-source-native-sobolev-probes-have-half-order-riesz-recovery-transition.md), Nyman--Beurling through [NB-144](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-144-euler-product-poisson-mass-forces-logarithmic-confluence-away-from-the-one-line.md), Prime Circle through [PC-307](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-307-polylog-oriented-prime-packets-require-near-qlogq-conditioning.md), Prime Flute through PF-334, Prime Lattice through [PL-318](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-318-bounded-logarithmic-forcing-moving-diagonal-obstruction.md), Robin Extremal through [RE-146](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-146-scale-maximality-turns-zero-density-into-an-exponential-rightward-shell-budget.md), and Weil Positivity through [WP-314](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-314-empty-side-moving-julia-boundary-layer-is-universal-logarithmic-control.md). No common quantitative modulus is claimed.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes target dependence exact. AF-362 prices normalized Riesz recovery of a declared linear target by the exponential second moment of its scaled spectral-energy distribution, separately from common base attenuation. AF-363 then computes that currency for source-independent Sobolev/Bessel probes: on every fixed scaled band, `beta<1/2` pays the full normalized upper-edge cost `e^v`, while `beta>1/2` has normalized recovery modulus tending to `1`; `beta=1/2` remains a distinct logarithmic endpoint. Regularity can therefore change transport conditioning without moving the off-critical discriminator or creating arithmetic specificity.

Prime Circle gives the complementary exact-information warning. PC-306 already makes the prime support algebraically identifiable from a degree-one cyclotomic centroid. PC-307 shows that the actual low/polylogarithmic prime Fourier packet and its disjoint one-step translate are nevertheless separated by only `O((1+log(2+K))/(q log q))`. A fixed-margin readout must therefore pay conditioning at least `q log q/(1+log(2+K))`. Exact identifiability and stable destination separation can diverge by an asymptotically enormous factor.

Nyman--Beurling supplies a source-side success. Generic xi-type completion, the functional equation and coarse zero laws permit target-poor logarithmic packets, but the actual Euler-product half-plane yields a positive local Poisson budget. That budget forbids some matched-control packets and forces near-one confluence to become sublogarithmic. The remaining fixed-interior gap shows that source specificity can be real yet still too weak for the destination target.

Prime Lattice sharpens the moving-observer warning. Global smoothness and critical Sobolev control can miss a moving Green atom; PL-318 further shows that even bounded zero-order forcing under the natural logarithmic endpoint operator does not suppress the offending sample. What matters is not merely source norm size but a structural state--source relation that controls the exact scale where the observer moves.

Robin Extremal now prices both a local packet and one actual-zeta exterior component in the same reciprocal-radius variable. RE-145 gives a local visibility floor `exp(-O(R))`. RE-146 shows that when the anchor is chosen variationally as the maximal Robin atom at scale `U`, rightward displacement first becomes an exponential coefficient penalty and then, on an earlier subwindow, a fractional coefficient moment that Ingham zero density can sum to an `exp(-cR)` shell budget. The population theorem becomes effective only after source/anchor structure converts the destination geometry into its summable currency.

Weil Positivity supplies the limiting classicalization: a moving Julia boundary layer can remain universal after rescaling even when limits fail to commute. Nonuniformity itself is not a source discriminator. Prime Flute retains the complementary lesson that a stronger source norm can charge a loss the terminal Hilbert--Schmidt observer does not pay.

Across all of these examples the reusable diagnostic is representation- and target-aware: specify the source resource, destination topology and target projection, then identify every parameter or coordinate moving with the asymptotic problem and prove a modulus uniform in exactly that coupled regime. **Source regularity, exact source identification, stable transport, arithmetic specificity, variational anchoring and destination sensitivity are distinct resources.** Improving one does not silently upgrade the others.

**Boundary.** These examples share an accounting structure, not an operator or theorem. AF-363 is a conditional zero-shell Sobolev target-recovery classification; PC-307 is a prime-packet conditioning lower bound; NB-144 is a local zeta-source occupancy law; PL-318 is a matched forcing obstruction; RE-145/146 concern Robin visibility and a scale-maximal rightward shell; WP uses a different boundary geometry. No rate, positivity statement or obstruction transfers automatically between lines.
