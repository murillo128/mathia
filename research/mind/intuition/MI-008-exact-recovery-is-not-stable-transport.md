# MI-008 — Source restriction, exact recovery, finite-height conditioning, support-union topology and destination survival are different resources

**Evidence level:** supported by Arithmetic Fidelity through [AF-323](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-323-varying-prime-supports-are-tail-dense-in-fixed-time-phase-space.md) and Prime Flute through [PF-329](../../prime_flute/findings/PF-329-final-diagonal-normalization-turns-seam-pulled-packets-into-total-energy-unit-traces.md). No universal stability modulus or theorem transferring either mechanism between lines is claimed.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the source-fiber hierarchy explicit. AF-320 repairs exact eight-prime recovery on the middle-singular branch: prime-log arithmetic excludes the deeper denominator failure and the fifth harmonic recovers the missing product phase. AF-321 shows why exact exclusion is not yet stable fidelity: matched rank-one controls approach the regular-octagon closure with `s_3 -> 0`, so relation rank supplies no positive uniform margin.

AF-322 adds the fixed-source quantitative layer. For every **fixed** eight-prime support, Baker-type logarithmic lower bounds prevent the prime orbit from approaching the four-antipodal-pair failure geometry faster than a power of the time height, while Kronecker recurrence still makes the uniform asymptotic gap zero. On an exact middle-singular hit, Łojasiewicz transfer gives a polynomial lower bound for `|s_3|`. The fixed source therefore has zero asymptotic margin but a nontrivial finite-height conditioning modulus.

AF-323 shows why this cannot simply be made support-uniform. Fix any nonzero time. Even after imposing an arbitrarily large lower prime cutoff, the union over distinct `m`-prime supports is dense in the whole phase torus `(S^1)^m`. For eight points, actual rational-prime supports approach regular-octagon configurations with the first seven power sums tending to zero while the product phase can tend to any prescribed value. Thus finite continuous phase data have no support-independent open separation from the ambient collision geometry once the support itself is allowed to vary.

The quantifier order is therefore part of the fidelity claim. “For each fixed support there is a modulus” and “there is one modulus for all supports” are different resources, not weaker and stronger versions of the same estimate. Exact incidence of the middle-singular equations remains separate from both: density of the support union gives arbitrarily close configurations, not an exact hit.

Prime Flute supplies the complementary destination lesson. PF-326--PF-328 establish a positive-length family of compactification-critical packets with controlled seam-pulled pre-angle energy. PF-329 then identifies a missing normalization layer: a unit packet `psi_t` in the completed intrinsic-angle coordinate corresponds in raw pant coordinates to `Q_L^(-1/2) A^(-1/2) psi_t`, not merely to its seam pullback. The completed diagonal inverse square root is operator-valued, need not commute with translations, and can reshape the packet family.

Thus even a positive-measure upstream witness with explicit amplitude scales is not destination survival. The relevant witness must first be expressed in the **total-energy-normalized variable actually consumed by the final angle**. Seam congruence itself is only a block coordinate change at the intrinsic-angle level; the unresolved quantity is the completed total-energy correlation after `A^(-1/2)`.

The cross-line diagnostic is now quantitative and quantifier-aware: **name the admissible source class and which source parameters are fixed, take the closure relevant to the failure geometry, quotient genuine nuisance symmetries, determine exact separating power, identify the finite-height/source-size modulus under that quantifier order, and finally express the witness in the metric and normalization actually consumed by the destination.** Exact injectivity on a nonclosed class is not stable fidelity; a componentwise modulus need not survive a union over source components; a positive-measure upstream witness is not final survival if destination preconditioning changes the relevant geometry.

**Boundary.** AF-322 and AF-323 are compatible: the first fixes support and varies time, the second fixes time and varies support. AF-323 proves neither equidistribution nor exact middle-singular incidence. PF-329 is an exact representation correction, not an endpoint estimate. The synthesis is a hierarchy of quantitative obligations, not a theorem connecting prime phases to flute surfaces.
