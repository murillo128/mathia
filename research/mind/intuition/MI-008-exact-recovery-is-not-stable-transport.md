No common quantitative modulus is claimed.

Across these lines, “the information is present” and “the destination obtains a usable non-generic margin” are different statements. The relevant source resource is not its absolute size or exactness but its size **after normalization by the moving decision boundary, inverse norm, interaction scale, propagation radius, compensation capacity or transmission modulus actually consumed by the destination**.

Prime Circle gives an exact reconstruction version. PC-309 shows that every uniformly transport-stable single-level observable with sub-mesh sensitivity collapses the prime source and its one-step translate. PC-310 shows that the first non-collapsing Poisson boundary layer is an invertible cyclic Fourier filter with condition number `Theta(q)`: stable distinction appears exactly when enough sensitivity has been paid to decode the source itself. Losslessness can therefore arrive only after crossing into generic source reconstruction rather than an RH-selective statistic.

Arithmetic Fidelity now separates four scales inside source-recovery channels. AF-379 proves that vanishing prime-log mesh can determine continuous translation total positivity exactly, and AF-380 prices the finite witness through target margin and sampling regularity. AF-486 then shows that a moving positive-Dirichlet Hankel window retains its `J`th generator only when absolute noise reaches the sharp exponential scale `q_J^(-t)`. AF-487 sharpens the inverse side: at that very survival threshold, the best uniform raw localization of the generator is only `Theta(1/t)` in `log q_J`. Supplying a discrete candidate set with positive log-spacing can turn this approximate estimate into eventual exact identification, but that changes the admissible source class by adding a discriminator-bearing mark; it is not a stronger inverse modulus for the unmarked observation map.

AF-534 makes the injective-versus-stable separation exact in an infinite-dimensional Gaussian nuisance model. If the target carrier `u` is not in the nuisance subspace `N` but lies in its Cameron--Martin closure, distinct targets have no exactly equal nuisance representatives yet their laws can be made arbitrarily close. The robust margin is `dist_H(u,closure(N))`, not algebraic non-membership in `N`; for a moving family uniform nuisance-robust recovery on a fixed compact target interval occurs exactly when `delta_A^2 dist_H(u_A,closure(N_A))^2 -> infinity`. Completion is therefore part of the destination geometry. Exact determination, observation survival, parameter resolution, source-class side information and positive distance from the **closed** collision set are distinct resources even before leaving one research line.

Farey Discrepancy shows that the **destination norm itself** can consume an otherwise favorable inverse scale. FD-193--FD-194 give a uniformly stable raw signed-Fourier inverse above the geometric square-root threshold, with geometric quotient rows contracting like `n^(-1+o(1))`. FD-195 computes the same witness in the Franel/GCD Hilbert geometry and finds an order-one dual norm. No algebraic source information was lost, but the scalar quadratic compression erased the contraction required for a sharper pointwise conclusion.

Prime Lattice makes the moving-margin distinction especially sharp. PL-326--PL-327 show that fixed-aperture endpoint control and the canonical half-log regularity can leave a shrinking reflection of size only `delta/L_delta` vulnerable to a sign-reversing residual. PL-329 identifies the source-side scale that excludes that residual, while PL-330 proves norm-resolvent continuity and continuous simple eigenbranches across the first-prime activation. Nevertheless the sharp hostile residual can itself tend to zero in that graph norm while reversing the sign. A topology sufficient for spectral transport is not automatically calibrated to a finer moving destination observable.

Nyman--Beurling gives the corresponding dimensionless transport currency. NB-162 couples conditioning, Jordan transport and boundary gap through `Theta_G=C_G ell_G/a_G`. Absolute transport may vanish while remaining order one relative to the shrinking Poisson width. Nuisance removal helps only after being priced against the destination resolution it is supposed to improve.

Robin Extremal separates global conservation from local rigidity. RE-164--RE-168 identify the moving KV source-capacity boundary. RE-169 shows that critical-scale Mertens normalization turns a local Robin displacement into future compensation debt. RE-170 then proves that even `o((sqrt p log p)^(-1))` Mertens mismatch does not control the selected prefix while later KV capacity remains large: an order-one first-shell excursion can be repaid in a second shell. The destination margin is therefore not only the normalization precision but also the remaining spatial capacity to compensate it.

Weil Inertia adds a transmission-scale version. WI-343--WI-344 show that a finite height bank on source log-aperture `Delta` lies near a low-dimensional carrier space at a rate controlled by `H Delta`, and any bounded height-independent linear preprocessing inherits that compression. If normalized outputs are required to retain relative transmission `eta`, the residual Gram tail is bounded by the approximation error divided by `eta`. A linear map can reveal tiny residual directions only by paying for them in transmitted amplitude; nominal output rank is not a scale-preserving information gain.

Weil Positivity gives the sharpest moving-observable ledger. WP-336 shows that under exact barycentric preservation the variance defect is controlled by channel error times bidirectional propagation. WP-337 adds the independent price of approximate preservation through coordinate bias times propagation asymmetry. Small raw errors are therefore insufficient unless they are subcritical after multiplication by their different geometric amplifiers.

The reusable audit is to identify the **decision margin and its intrinsic coordinate before measuring error or rank**. Depending on the line this may be determinant margin, inverse dual norm, mesh-scale condition number, Cameron--Martin distance to a closed nuisance space, Poisson width, shrinking reflection reserve, monotone source capacity, remaining compensation capacity, transmitted carrier amplitude, channel-error × propagation products, or a nonlinear parameter modulus such as the `(-1/t)` root that turns bounded multiplicative sample uncertainty into `Theta(1/t)` log-coordinate uncertainty. Also state whether a proposed gain comes from the observation map itself or from shrinking the admissible source class with external side information. Exactness at each fixed parameter, absolute `o(1)` control, norm-resolvent continuity, algebraic injectivity, large nominal rank or agreement at a leading exponent are not uniform destination statements unless they dominate every moving scale the destination actually consumes.

**Boundary.** These mechanisms are not quantitatively interchangeable. The synthesis is a resource-accounting principle: every proposed bridge must expose its own target margin, structural scale, topology/conditioning map, source class and transmission requirement rather than importing a modulus from another line.
