# MI-008 — Positive local models and exact recovery do not determine stable target transport

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from exact target-fidelity, quotient-graph, Robin-transform, and all-future transition-conditioning boundaries

## Core intuition

Exact representation, exact state recovery, or a sharp positive local estimate does not determine whether the final target is stably transported. The missing quantity often lives in the map between stages: source depth, graph energy, off-diagonal locality, calibration precision, or inverse modulus.

The newest results sharpen this from both directions. A previously feared intermediate operator can turn out not to be the physical object that needs control, while a broad observation class can remain exactly below a target-specific visibility threshold.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity separates coarse target class from source access: the Li root-rate endpoint tolerates subexponential error, but sublinear zeta jets cannot affect it. Nyman--Beurling separates available quotient directions from useful enrichment: exact all-depth reciprocal blind modes force source-specific graph energy rather than generic frame coercivity.

Prime Flute now has both a positive and a corrective result. PF-253 supplies the Bessel-four square-root margin; PF-255--PF-256 show geometric/corridor transport preserves two derivatives. PF-257 then demonstrates that the physical Robin map is the combined resolvent transform `(I+XX*)^{-1}X`, not the naked polar factor isolated earlier. Stable transport should be proved for that combined map, because singular amplitude can regularize unstable orientation.

Xi Flow gives the complementary negative boundary. XF-152--XF-153 turn the shallow-collar obstruction into an exact one-sided all-future threshold for the maximal-contact packet: below outward depth `h_*(d)~(pi/2)d^2/L`, even optimization over all positive heat times remains exponentially blind.

## Program consequence

At every stage distinguish state sufficiency, local smoothing, orientation/locality of the actual physical map, source access, and conditioning of the final functional. Once the true transport map/modulus is isolated, estimate it directly rather than enlarging neighboring representations.

## Counterevidence / boundary

These controls are category-specific. Prime Flute's combined seam transform may be local enough; Xi may possess source restrictions or deeper observables; Nyman may admit nonlinear certificates; Arithmetic Fidelity may find another cheap sufficient source map. None is ruled out by the synthesis.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a final theorem in one cited setting while bypassing the identified transport modulus without adding new source structure, or invalidate the exact map/conditioning boundary on which the synthesis relies. A valid continuation should add the missing transport mechanism and thereby narrow this principle.
