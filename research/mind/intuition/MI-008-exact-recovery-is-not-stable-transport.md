# MI-008 — Source resources matter only through the destination margin and resolution they preserve

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis supported by Arithmetic Fidelity through [AF-349](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-349-full-riesz-profile-is-exactly-recoverable-before-asymptotic-quotient.md), Prime Flute through [PF-334](../../prime_flute/findings/PF-334-hilbert-schmidt-relative-square-root-control-escapes-logarithmic-dimension-loss.md), and Nyman--Beurling through [NB-129](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-129-target-bearing-natural-grid-phase-visibility-has-a-sharp-reciprocal-height-defect-threshold.md). No common quantitative modulus is claimed.

Arithmetic Fidelity separates representation fidelity from terminal observation. AF-324--AF-327 already show a source margin transported into a target-native Gram determinant/eigenvalue slack. AF-349 now gives the complementary exact boundary: every fixed finite Riesz cutoff profile retains all source coefficients, yet the AF-348 perfect-power control and dense unit mass collide after the later quotient that forgets bounded residual profiles. The intermediate transform is faithful; the terminal observer is not. Moreover the exact inverse differentiates, so injectivity supplies no stability margin by itself.

Prime Flute shows that even the **norm** used to measure the destination is part of the theorem. PF-333 finds logarithmically worsening generic sensitivity in operator norm, but PF-334 proves dimension-free control of the same relative square-root map in Hilbert--Schmidt norm and a nonlinear `S_2` transfer under a uniform normalized-insertion envelope. Since PF-318 already consumes an averaged Hilbert--Schmidt budget, paying an operator-norm penalty would manufacture a loss the endpoint does not require. What remains is localized `S_2` control of the actual pant/core insertion and its geometry.

Nyman--Beurling makes the resolution bill explicit on the target-bearing side. NB-128 gives the growing natural prefix a local phase inverse modulus, but NB-129 proves that the phase witness reaches the weighted target only when the target-angle defect is `O(1/|T|)` at height `|T|`. That reciprocal-height threshold is sharp for the NB-117 Dirichlet energy: a matched endpoint-preserving residual with `Theta(1/|T|)` excess can erase the entire phase-sensitive block. Fixed positive capture is therefore not enough; extra zero-power structure must prevent that amplitude escape.

The reusable diagnostic is concrete: identify the source family and resource parameters, the failure-set closure, the quantitative source modulus, the resolution scale of the representation, and the **exact terminal functional/norm/quotient** consumed by the final theorem. Then prove transport into that currency with the needed conditioning. Do not infer loss merely because an intermediate signal becomes small, and do not infer usefulness merely because an intermediate map is injective.

**Boundary.** AF-349 does not show that the RH-facing terminal observer retains the complete Riesz profile or that its inverse is stable; PF-334 does not prove locality or identify the exact PF-318 cross block with the whole normalized insertion; NB-129 is sharp only for the ambient endpoint-plus-Dirichlet-energy model and leaves genuine zero-power recurrence/analyticity unused. This is resource accounting across lines, not a universal stability theorem.
