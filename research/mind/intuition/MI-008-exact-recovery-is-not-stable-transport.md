# MI-008 — Source resources matter only through the destination margin and resolution they preserve

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis supported by Arithmetic Fidelity through [AF-327](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-327-prime-height-time-separates-lacunary-moment-gram-boundary.md), Prime Flute through [PF-334](../../prime_flute/findings/PF-334-hilbert-schmidt-relative-square-root-control-escapes-logarithmic-dimension-loss.md), and Nyman--Beurling through [NB-104](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-104-boundary-confluence-has-an-ordinate-delay-laguerre-law.md). No common quantitative modulus is claimed.

Arithmetic Fidelity now carries the source-to-target chain through an entire retained feature boundary. AF-324--AF-325 price support height and observation time; AF-326 transfers the arithmetic margin into one retained harmonic; AF-327 proves that the retained `{1,z,z^2,z^4}` moment Gram stays quantitatively inside its rank-deficient boundary. The source resource is useful because it becomes a target-native determinant/eigenvalue slack. Exact incidence and downstream sufficiency remain separate.

Prime Flute shows that even the **norm** used to measure the destination is part of the theorem. PF-333 finds logarithmically worsening generic sensitivity in operator norm, but PF-334 proves dimension-free control of the same relative square-root map in Hilbert--Schmidt norm and a nonlinear `S_2` transfer under a uniform normalized-insertion envelope. Since PF-318 already consumes an averaged Hilbert--Schmidt budget, paying an operator-norm penalty would manufacture a loss the endpoint does not require. What remains is localized `S_2` control of the actual pant/core insertion and its geometry.

Nyman--Beurling adds a resolution resource. NB-103 makes fixed-multiplicity confluent volume depend on radial depth `x=2a log q`, while NB-104 shows that near the boundary the finite cells spend an ordinate delay `y=2a log^+(|Gamma|/m)` before they resolve the Mellin phase. The surviving volume is `e^(-dy)Delta_d(x-y)`, not merely `Delta_d(x)`. Continuum height invariance is intact; the finite representation has a source-resolution coupling absent from the continuum model.

The reusable diagnostic is concrete: identify the source family and resource parameters, the failure-set closure, the quantitative source modulus, the resolution scale of the representation, and the **exact destination functional/norm** consumed by the final theorem. Then prove transport into that currency without silently replacing it by a stronger norm or a continuum quantity that the finite representation cannot resolve.

**Boundary.** AF-327 does not decide middle-singular incidence; PF-334 does not prove locality or identify the exact PF-318 cross block with the whole normalized insertion; NB-104 is fixed-multiplicity and its ordinate delay can be changed by moving the band start. This is resource accounting across lines, not a universal stability theorem.
