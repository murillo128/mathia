# MI-008 — Source resources matter only through the destination margin and the limiting scale they preserve

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis supported by Arithmetic Fidelity through [AF-362](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-362-riesz-target-recovery-is-exponential-picard-moment.md), Nyman--Beurling through [NB-144](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-144-euler-product-poisson-mass-forces-logarithmic-confluence-away-from-the-one-line.md), Prime Flute through PF-334, Prime Lattice through [PL-318](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-318-bounded-logarithmic-forcing-moving-diagonal-obstruction.md), Robin Extremal through [RE-145](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-145-reciprocal-box-visibility-has-an-exponential-scaled-radius-floor.md), and Weil Positivity through [WP-314](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-314-empty-side-moving-julia-boundary-layer-is-universal-logarithmic-control.md). No common quantitative modulus is claimed.

Arithmetic Fidelity now makes target dependence exact. Every fixed positive Riesz order crosses the critical regularity threshold without moving the off-critical discriminator, but the limiting transport has two separate costs. Base height produces common attenuation, while a declared target is priced by the exponential second moment of its scaled spectral-energy distribution. A diverging raw bandwidth can therefore be harmless for one localized target and fatal for another whose energy reaches the upper edge. Vector invertibility is not the same resource as target recoverability.

Nyman--Beurling supplies a complementary source-side success. Generic xi-type completion, the functional equation and coarse zero laws permit target-poor logarithmic packets, but the actual Euler-product half-plane yields a positive local Poisson budget. That budget forbids some matched-control packets and forces near-one confluence to become sublogarithmic. The remaining fixed-interior gap shows that source specificity can be real yet still too weak for the destination target.

Prime Lattice sharpens the moving-observer warning. Global smoothness and critical Sobolev control can miss a moving Green atom; PL-318 further shows that even bounded zero-order forcing under the natural logarithmic endpoint operator does not suppress the offending sample. What matters is not merely source norm size but a structural state--source relation that controls the exact scale where the observer moves.

Robin Extremal makes the observer cost quantitative. RE-145 proves that a positive reciprocal-box packet of scaled radius `R` retains normalized visibility at least `exp(-O(R))`, independent of cardinality, and RE-144 shows this exponential order is sharp in the matched class. Thus the local destination margin is a radius-dependent rate, not a binary bounded/unbounded property; it becomes useful only after comparison with the exterior cancellation budget.

Weil Positivity supplies the limiting classicalization: a moving Julia boundary layer can remain universal after rescaling even when limits fail to commute. Nonuniformity itself is not a source discriminator. Prime Flute retains the complementary lesson that a stronger source norm can charge a loss the terminal Hilbert--Schmidt observer does not pay.

Across all of these examples the reusable diagnostic is representation- and target-aware: specify the source resource, destination topology and target projection, then identify every parameter or coordinate moving with the asymptotic problem and prove a modulus uniform in exactly that coupled regime.

**Boundary.** These examples share an accounting structure, not an operator or theorem. AF-362 is a diagonal target-recovery result; NB-144 is a local zeta-source occupancy law; PL-318 is a matched forcing obstruction; RE-145 is a positive-measure visibility rate; WP uses a different boundary geometry. No rate, positivity statement or obstruction transfers automatically between lines.
