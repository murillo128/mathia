# MI-008 — Source resources matter only through the destination margin they preserve

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis supported by Arithmetic Fidelity through [AF-326](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-326-toeplitz-moment-positivity-couples-singular-second-and-third-harmonics.md), Prime Flute through [PF-332](../../prime_flute/findings/PF-332-completed-square-root-defect-is-a-resolvent-dressed-pant-insertion.md), and Nyman--Beurling through [NB-103](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-103-confluent-nyman-volume-is-curvature-blind-and-controlled-by-radial-depth.md). No common quantitative modulus is claimed.

Arithmetic Fidelity now gives an explicit source-to-target conditioning chain. AF-324--AF-325 price support height and observation time: bounded-height prime supports form increasingly fine phase nets, while a Baker--Wüstholz bound gives a finite-height exclusion from the deeper antipodal singularity. AF-326 shows how such a source margin can become a retained destination margin. On the exact middle-singular stratum,

`4-|s_2| >= |s_3|^2/64`,

so the arithmetic lower bound on the recovery denominator `|s_3|` forces a quantitative gap in the already-retained second harmonic. Exact incidence is still separate, but the route from source provenance to target geometry is no longer only qualitative.

Prime Flute shows a different failure mode: the relevant norm can conserve all packet mass while the destination meaning depends on spatial placement. PF-330 preserves the packet family as a unitary-rotated tight frame; PF-331 removes polar instability; PF-332 identifies the relative defect `F-I` as one resolvent-dressed pant/core insertion. The destination margin is therefore locality of that relative transfer, not total energy and not the separate size of the seam square roots.

Nyman--Beurling makes the destination dependence even more explicit. NB-102 says local three-state conditioning is governed by Menger curvature, while NB-103 proves that fixed-multiplicity normalized confluent volume is curvature-blind and governed by radial depth `2 Re(lambda_*) log q`. The same source packet can therefore be bad in one destination metric and harmless in another.

The reusable diagnostic is precise: specify the source family and size parameters, the observation/limit scale, the failure-set closure, the quantitative source modulus, and the **exact destination functional** into which that modulus must transfer. “The information survives” is not a theorem. A source restriction is useful only if it produces a margin in the currency actually consumed downstream.

**Boundary.** AF-326 is conditional on exact middle-singular incidence; PF-332 proves identities but no uniform locality estimate; NB-103 is fixed-multiplicity and does not decide whether the Nyman target consumes volume or stable inversion. This is resource accounting across lines, not a universal stability theorem.
