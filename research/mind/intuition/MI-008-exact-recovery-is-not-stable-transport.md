# MI-008 — Stable transport is limited by source optimization, non-escape, destination elimination, and witness resolution

**Evidence level:** supported by exact adaptive-fidelity controls through AF-200, physical near-extremizer controls through ANF-117, Prime-Flute cut-flux results through PF-223, and Xi q-scale source constraints through XF-115

## Core intuition

Exact recovery, small forward error, compactness, and an exact source representation do not determine whether information reaches the final theorem stably. The effective transport problem is set jointly by **source resolution and admissible optimization**, **source non-escape in the observation-sensitive region**, **destination danger geometry and variational elimination**, the **analytic topology/physical scale of the interface**, and the **resolution and shape of the final witness family**.

A resource visible on a convenient raw representative can disappear after source reshaping, aggregation, shorting, or source-to-grid averaging. Conversely, a source-specific theorem can remove one matched control without closing the whole destination cone. The useful quantity is whatever remains quantitative after *all* admissible transformations used by the final theorem.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity separates source metric from source localization. AF-180--AF-194 show that cancellation complexity, kernel algebra, bandwidth, and asymptotic accuracy produce distinct recovery regimes. AF-195--AF-199 then expose free source-shape optimization through Peano B-splines. AF-200 proves an additional non-escape requirement: at fixed positive `W_1` and fixed quartic cancellation order, the Peano carrier can drift to infinity and make a fixed Euler band asymptotically blind. Stable recovery therefore needs tightness in an observation-relevant carrier, not merely separation in the declared source metric.

Analytic Frontier makes aggregation and resolution exact. ANF-115 realizes exponentially source-unsaturated packets at an interior spectral saddle. ANF-116 shows that componentwise source-heavy packets can be phase-balanced into excisable aggregates inside genuine near-extremizers. ANF-117 proves that every fixed finite saddle jet can be cancelled while macroscopic source mass survives at the natural `A^(-1/2)` saddle width. The relevant object is the full mesoscopic profile after excisable aggregates are removed.

Prime Flute exposes the difference between qualitative topology and quantitative ideal membership. PF-222 proves the exact reciprocal-prime Schur commutator compact by expressing it as a norm-convergent sum of fixed-cut Schatten fluxes. PF-223 constructs a matched positive block-Jacobi control where all those local pieces are finite rank and the total commutator is compact, yet the required low/high transport is not weak trace class. Vanishing local norms and compactness do not control how effective multiplicity accumulates.

Xi Flow supplies the witness-family boundary. XF-113 shows that coarse support, roughness, bad-set measure, and Fourier-mass constraints do not rule out a negative Toeplitz direction. XF-114--XF-115 then use actual Xi source structure to make every degree-`K` Fejer/plane-wave average asymptotically flat on the whole q-scale circle. This decisively kills the fixed-well controls, but arbitrary polynomial Rayleigh vectors may still combine frequencies in ways not represented by one Fejer translate. Source-specific scalar testing is therefore stronger than coarse symbol control but weaker than full finite-section coercivity.

## Program consequence

For every source-to-destination bridge, declare together the destination quotient/norm/ideal, every optimization or shorting implicit in that destination, the admissible source class and its geometric degrees of freedom, the compactness/tightness property preventing source escape, observation regularity/algebra/bandwidth, interface topology and normalization, and the exact witness family whose coercivity is needed.

Perform source optimization, aggregation, projection, Schur complement, limiting transport, and resolution-matched testing before calling an intermediate resource quantitative. Raw mass, source distance, compactness, one chosen matched stencil, finitely many saddle derivatives, one plane wave, or one leading asymptotic profile is not enough when the admitted problem can rearrange or concentrate around it.

## Counterevidence / boundary

These results do not yield a universal minimax theorem. AF-200 identifies one exact escape but not the weakest possible localization hypothesis. ANF-117 does not construct a fatal non-excisable near-extremizer. PF-223 is an abstract block-Jacobi control and does not show the canonical flute fails weak trace class. XF-115 proves full-circle Fejer flatness but not full Toeplitz positivity.

The synthesis is therefore a methodological impossibility principle at the current evidence level: a proposed bridge is not validated until its quantitative resource survives the exact admissible optimization, non-escape gate, destination elimination, and witness resolution used by the final theorem.

## Epistemic status

**Supported cross-line synthesis from exact component theorems; no RH implication is claimed.**

## Falsification criterion

Produce a final source-specific coercive theorem that remains uniform while its declared source carrier escapes every observation-sensitive compact region, its local compact pieces accumulate beyond the destination ideal, or its witness cone contains directions invisible to the claimed test family without compensating information. Conversely, a theorem controlling the fully optimized/non-escaping object directly would narrow this principle for that route.
