# MI-008 — Stable transport is limited by source optimization, destination elimination, interface scale, and observable bandwidth

**Evidence level:** supported by exact adaptive-fidelity controls through AF-197, physical near-extremizer reductions through ANF-112, Prime-Flute interface/shorting results through PF-217, and Xi source-interface constraints through XF-109

## Core intuition

Exact recovery, small forward error, and an exact source representation do not determine whether information reaches the final theorem stably. The effective transport problem is set jointly by **source resolution and admissible optimization**, **destination danger geometry and variational elimination**, the **analytic topology/physical scale of the interface**, and the **observable bandwidth and algebra retained after transport**.

The crucial refinement is that a resource visible on a convenient raw representative may disappear after the optimization or shorting that defines the destination problem. Conversely, a weak-looking pointwise obstruction can become genuinely quantitative when one asks for mass in the correct dual variable rather than one extreme coefficient or derivative.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity shows that source complexity and source geometry are independent axes. AF-180--AF-188 produce exact positive matched controls whose visibility changes from super-algebraic to algebraic to order one as the Euler observation bandwidth crosses its natural scales. AF-189--AF-194 add a correction hierarchy: logarithmic matching of adjacent asymptotic descriptions can hold on a larger window than multiplicative matching. AF-195--AF-197 then show that even at fixed cancellation order and Wasserstein separation, equispaced parity stencils are not generally extremal once node geometry is optimized.

Analytic Frontier supplies the destination-side counterpart. ANF-097--ANF-103 replace raw Gram instability by physical fixed-notch exposure and remove source-negligible escape. ANF-111--ANF-112 show why the relevant decomposition must also survive aggregation: pairwise polarization can recycle through previously extracted sparse layers, while the aggregate packet has a robust dichotomy—source-negligible packets are excisable and source-heavy endpoint-localized packets force new disjoint height mass.

Prime Flute makes the elimination issue exact. PF-213 reduces smooth boundary reassembly to off-diagonal locality of the simultaneous Schur factor. PF-214 shows its precision is block Jacobi, but PF-215 rules out uniformly bounded adjacent hopping. PF-216 finds a compensating raw mass/conductance floor on constant modes, and PF-217 then shows that tangential boundary modes can screen any fixed fraction of that floor after the correct variational shorting. The needed resource must therefore be proved for the **effective shorted form**, not inherited from raw compression.

Xi Flow gives the dual lesson. The low-band source-to-periodic bridge is exact at its declared guarded scale but prime-free. At q-scale, XF-109 strengthens a mere maximum-derivative obstruction: every nonpositive source-Toeplitz witness must place a fixed positive fraction of its Fourier `L^2` mass outside a PNT-flat central window. A vanishing high-frequency contaminant can fake pointwise roughness but cannot fake this mass condition.

## Program consequence

For every source-to-destination bridge, declare together the destination quotient/norm, every optimization or shorting implicit in that destination, the admissible source class and its geometric degrees of freedom, observation regularity/algebra/bandwidth, interface topology and physical normalization, and the exact coercivity or inverse-locality theorem required after transport.

When an intermediate resource is supposed to survive a quotient, minimization, projection, Schur complement, source optimization, or asymptotic limit, perform that operation before calling the resource quantitative. Raw mass, raw conditioning, one chosen matched stencil, one large derivative, or one leading asymptotic profile is not enough if the admissible problem can rearrange it away.

## Counterevidence / boundary

These results do not provide a universal minimax theorem. AF-197 optimizes explicit low-order families rather than all positive source controls. ANF-112 still needs a restart condition to iterate its packet dichotomy indefinitely. PF-217 does not prove that the effective Schur factor lacks sufficient weighted locality; it only kills the raw fixed-fraction coercivity route. XF-109 constrains nonpositive q-scale witnesses but does not prove transition coercivity or a prime-band interface.

The synthesis is therefore a methodological impossibility principle at the current evidence level: a proposed bridge is not validated until its quantitative resource survives the exact admissible optimization and destination elimination used by the final theorem.

## Epistemic status

**Supported cross-line synthesis from exact component theorems; no RH implication is claimed.**

## Falsification criterion

Produce a source-to-destination theorem whose final coercivity remains uniform while an admitted optimization or shorting destroys the intermediate resource claimed to be necessary; or prove that one of the cited controls does not survive its stated quotient/scale. Conversely, a theorem controlling the optimized/shorted object directly would narrow the role of this principle for that route.
