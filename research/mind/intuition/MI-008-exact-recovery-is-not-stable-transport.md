# MI-008 — Source resources matter only through the destination margin at the moving structural scale

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from Arithmetic Fidelity through [AF-380](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-380-prime-gap-bounds-give-finite-prime-log-negativity-witnesses.md), [PC-309](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-309-transport-stable-prime-source-geometry-collapses-before-mesh-scale.md)--[PC-310](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-310-mesh-scale-poisson-boundary-layer-is-an-invertible-cyclic-filter.md), [NB-162](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-162-poisson-width-transport-controls-target-and-signed-euler-leakage.md), [PL-320](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-320-source-trace-full-tail-volterra-matching.md), [PL-326](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-326-fixed-aperture-endpoint-law-does-not-orient-first-prime-reflection.md)--[PL-327](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-327-critical-log-holder-first-prime-reflection-threshold.md), and [RE-164](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-164-quantitative-pnt-fidelity-collapses-terminal-generalized-source-ambiguity.md)--[RE-166](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-166-lambert-w-inversion-localizes-the-kv-capacity-transition.md). No common quantitative modulus is claimed.

Across these lines, “the information is present” and “the destination obtains a usable non-generic margin” are different statements. The relevant source resource is not its absolute size or exactness but its size **after normalization by the moving decision boundary, interaction scale, or inversion modulus actually consumed by the destination**.

Prime Circle gives an exact reconstruction version. PC-309 shows that every uniformly transport-stable single-level observable with sub-mesh sensitivity collapses the prime source and its one-step translate. PC-310 shows that the first non-collapsing Poisson boundary layer is an invertible cyclic Fourier filter with condition number `Theta(q)`: stable distinction appears exactly when enough sensitivity has been paid to decode the source itself. Losslessness can therefore arrive only after crossing into generic source reconstruction rather than an RH-selective statistic.

Arithmetic Fidelity now makes the same distinction inside sampling. AF-379 proves that vanishing prime-log mesh already determines continuous translation total positivity exactly. AF-380 then shows what finite visibility costs. A fixed negative determinant with margin `eta` survives row approximation radius `r` only when the determinant perturbation, bounded by `k k! M^(k-1) omega(r)`, is smaller than `eta`. Baker--Harman--Pintz gives prime-log covering radius `r_T<=exp(-19T/40)`, so every fixed strict violation has a finite witness; under local Hölder exponent `alpha`, its prime scale is polynomial in `eta^(-1)` with exponent `40/(19 alpha)`. Exact determination and finite witness conditioning are therefore separate resources even for the same sample set.

Prime Lattice exhibits the moving-sign version. PL-320 closes a fixed-aperture endpoint transport law, but PL-326 shows that this does not orient the first-prime reflection on a layer of width `2 delta`: absolute `o(1)` residual mass can dominate a sign reserve only of order `delta/log(1/delta)`. PL-327 identifies the exact generic threshold. The canonical logarithmic boundary profile has half-log regularity, and that `alpha=1/2` scale is still large enough for an antisymmetric residual to reverse the reflection. A residual modulus with any `alpha>1/2`, under the corresponding relative coefficient condition, is sufficient. The resource is therefore supercritical control of the residual **relative to the selected moving layer**, not sharper generic regularity of the whole state.

Nyman--Beurling gives the corresponding dimensionless transport currency. NB-162 couples conditioning `C_G`, Jordan transport `ell_G`, and boundary gap `a_G` through `Theta_G=C_G ell_G/a_G`. Absolute transport may vanish while remaining order one relative to the shrinking Poisson width. Sub-Poisson transport suppresses both the target response and signed Euler leakage. Nuisance removal helps only after being priced against the destination resolution it is supposed to improve.

Robin Extremal adds a moving inversion boundary. RE-164--RE-165 identify the exact quantitative-PNT capacity `K_b(p,T)=sqrt(p) log p exp(-bV(T))/V(T)`. RE-166 inverts it: the capacity center is `v_*(p)=b^(-1)W_0(b log(p) sqrt(p))`, and all fixed positive capacity contours lie within `O(1)` in the intrinsic `V` coordinate. Rigidity requires `V(T)-v_*(p)->+infinity`; bounded displacement leaves order-one generalized-source ambiguity. The slowly varying motion of the center is much larger than the actual transition width, so a leading exponent or nominal equality can misclassify the source regime even when the exact capacity boundary is extremely sharp.

The reusable audit is to identify the **decision margin and its intrinsic coordinate before measuring error**. Depending on the line this may be a row-pattern covering radius versus determinant margin, a mesh-scale inverse condition number, a Poisson width, a shrinking reflection reserve, or a monotone source-capacity coordinate. Exactness at each fixed parameter, absolute `o(1)` control, or agreement at a leading exponent are not uniform transport statements. Useful progress requires a source quantity that remains nontrivial after this normalization and reaches the target before the observation crosses into generic decoding, universal completion, or matched-control ambiguity.

**Boundary.** These mechanisms are not quantitatively interchangeable. AF-380 is a determinant-continuity witness theorem, PC-310 finite cyclic harmonic analysis, PL-327 a logarithmic moving-layer threshold, NB-162 a Wasserstein/Poisson transport estimate, and RE-166 an inversion of a KV-scale source capacity. The synthesis is a resource-accounting principle: every proposed bridge must expose its own target margin, structural scale, regularity/conditioning map, and source class rather than importing a modulus from another line.
