# MI-008 — Source resources matter only through the destination margin and the limiting scale they preserve

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis supported by Arithmetic Fidelity through [AF-364](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-364-critical-half-order-riesz-recovery-scales-as-e-v-over-sqrt-v.md), Nyman--Beurling through [NB-144](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-144-euler-product-poisson-mass-forces-logarithmic-confluence-away-from-the-one-line.md), Prime Circle through [PC-309](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-309-transport-stable-prime-source-geometry-collapses-before-mesh-scale.md), Prime Flute through PF-334, Prime Lattice through [PL-318](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-318-bounded-logarithmic-forcing-moving-diagonal-obstruction.md), Robin Extremal through [RE-146](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-146-scale-maximality-turns-zero-density-into-an-exponential-rightward-shell-budget.md), and Weil Positivity through [WP-314](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-314-empty-side-moving-julia-boundary-layer-is-universal-logarithmic-control.md). No common quantitative modulus is claimed.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes target dependence exact. AF-362 prices normalized Riesz recovery of a declared linear target by the exponential second moment of its scaled spectral-energy distribution, separately from common base attenuation. AF-363 identifies the source-native half-order Sobolev threshold. AF-364 resolves the critical endpoint: on growing scaled bands the `beta=1/2` target costs `Theta(e^v/sqrt(v))`, while the absolute bill additionally pays `e^u`. The `sqrt(v)` gain comes from logarithmically spread target energy and is reproduced by matched frequency systems with comparable counting density. Better transport geometry still does not create arithmetic specificity.

Prime Circle gives the complementary exact-information and resolution warning. PC-306 makes the prime support algebraically identifiable from a degree-one cyclotomic centroid. PC-307 shows that the true low/polylogarithmic prime packet and its disjoint one-step translate are separated by only `O((1+log(2+K))/(q log q))`; PC-308 extends vanishing separation to every ordinary `K=o(q)` packet, with linear stability first appearing through classical major arcs. PC-309 removes dependence on a Fourier coordinate choice: the same matched sources are only `O(1/q)` apart in every physical Wasserstein metric, so any fixed-margin uniformly transport-stable single-level observable must spend `Omega(q)` conditioning. Even the complete normalized logarithmic-potential field collapses throughout every boundary layer wider than the mesh scale `1/q`.

Together those two lines show why “more information” and “better conditioning” are insufficient descriptions. In Arithmetic Fidelity the declared target determines how much of an exponentially broad band must be inverted; in Prime Circle the single-level source geometry remains stable only until the observation acquires mesh-scale sensitivity, after which it is already resolving classical/discrete source structure. The relevant question is which destination margin is achieved **before** the representation crosses into a generic, classical or decoding regime that matched controls already realize.

Nyman--Beurling supplies a source-side success. Generic xi-type completion, the functional equation and coarse zero laws permit target-poor logarithmic packets, but the actual Euler-product half-plane yields a positive local Poisson budget. That budget forbids some matched-control packets and forces near-one confluence to become sublogarithmic. The remaining fixed-interior gap shows that source specificity can be real yet still too weak for the destination target.

Prime Lattice sharpens the moving-observer warning. Global smoothness and critical Sobolev control can miss a moving Green atom; PL-318 further shows that even bounded zero-order forcing under the natural logarithmic endpoint operator does not suppress the offending sample. What matters is not merely source norm size but a structural state--source relation that controls the exact scale where the observer moves.

Robin Extremal prices both a local packet and one actual-zeta exterior component in the same reciprocal-radius variable. RE-145 gives a local visibility floor `exp(-O(R))`. RE-146 shows that when the anchor is chosen variationally as the maximal Robin atom, rightward displacement first becomes an exponential coefficient penalty and then a fractional coefficient moment that zero density can sum to an `exp(-cR)` shell budget. The population theorem becomes effective only after source/anchor structure converts the destination geometry into its summable currency.

Weil Positivity supplies the limiting classicalization: a moving Julia boundary layer can remain universal after rescaling even when limits fail to commute. Nonuniformity itself is not a source discriminator. Prime Flute retains the complementary lesson that a stronger source norm can charge a loss the terminal Hilbert--Schmidt observer does not pay.

Across all of these examples the reusable diagnostic is representation- and target-aware: specify the source resource, destination topology and target projection, identify every parameter moving with the asymptotic problem, and prove a modulus uniform in exactly that coupled regime. **Source regularity, exact source identification, stable transport, geometric resolution, classical/generic decoding, arithmetic specificity, variational anchoring and destination sensitivity are distinct resources.** Improving one does not silently upgrade the others.

**Boundary.** These examples share an accounting structure, not an operator or theorem. AF-364 is a conditional zero-shell target-recovery classification; PC-309 concerns a translated prime control and transport-stable single-level geometry; NB-144 is a local zeta-source occupancy law; PL-318 is a matched forcing obstruction; RE-145/146 concern Robin visibility and one exterior shell; WP uses a different boundary geometry. No rate, positivity statement or obstruction transfers automatically between lines.
