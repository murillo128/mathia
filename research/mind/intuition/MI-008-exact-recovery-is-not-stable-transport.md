# MI-008 — Source resources matter only through the destination margin before observation becomes generic decoding

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from Arithmetic Fidelity, [PC-309](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-309-transport-stable-prime-source-geometry-collapses-before-mesh-scale.md)--[PC-310](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-310-mesh-scale-poisson-boundary-layer-is-an-invertible-cyclic-filter.md), [NB-144](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-144-euler-product-poisson-mass-forces-logarithmic-confluence-away-from-the-one-line.md), [PL-320](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-320-source-trace-full-tail-volterra-matching.md), [RE-149](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-149-zero-horizontal-spread-product-packets-isolate-vertical-escape.md), and current local minds. No common quantitative modulus is claimed.

Prime Circle now gives an exact three-regime separation. The finite source can be algebraically identifiable long before it is stably distinguishable. PC-309 shows that every uniformly transport-stable single-level observable with sub-mesh sensitivity collapses the prime source and its one-step translate. PC-310 then shows that the first non-collapsing Poisson boundary layer is an invertible cyclic Fourier filter with condition number `Theta(q)`: stable distinction appears exactly when the representation has paid enough sensitivity to decode the source itself.

Thus “the information is present” and “the destination obtains a non-generic margin” are different statements. A lossless map can still be mathematically uninformative for RH when its inversion is just a universal source decoder available to matched controls.

Prime Lattice exhibits the complementary possibility. PL-320 does not demand a richer observation. An exact state--source Volterra identity plus convergence of one singular-remainder trace forces the leading boundary coefficient and the moving-tail law. Structural coupling can therefore reduce the information the destination needs, rather than increasing source resolution.

Nyman--Beurling and Robin Extremal show why a source-specific local theorem must also beat the destination's exceptional geometry. NB-144 supplies an arithmetic Poisson occupancy budget, but the corrected NB-146 classification shows where that positive proof class is already optimal. RE-149 shows that even perfect horizontal locking leaves an exponentially bad vertical phase geometry in matched controls. More data of the same mathematical type do not improve the endpoint once the proof cone or visibility rate is saturated.

Arithmetic Fidelity retains the target-relative cost boundary: principal parts can survive exactly while recovery of a declared target over growing bands remains exponentially expensive. Across the examples, useful progress requires a source quantity that reaches the target **before** the observation crosses into unstable inversion, full reconstruction, a universal tangent, or a matched-control-realizable law.

**Boundary.** The lines use different objects and norms. PC-310 is finite cyclic harmonic analysis, PL-320 is a logarithmic-endpoint Volterra identity, NB is a zero-occupancy theorem, RE is a matched finite-edge visibility obstruction, and AF uses target-relative Riesz inversion. The synthesis is an accounting distinction, not a transferable estimate.
