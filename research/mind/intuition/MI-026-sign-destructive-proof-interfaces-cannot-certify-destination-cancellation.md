# MI-026 — Sign-destructive or scale-mismatched proof interfaces cannot certify destination cancellation

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [VIS-275](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-275-wang-laplace-null-source-bound-floor.md) through [VIS-283](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-283-wang-fixed-source-cross-frequency-separation.md), and [WI-334](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-334-fixed-order-gowers-uniformity-does-not-force-bow-variance-cancellation.md) through [WI-339](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-339-diagonal-deleted-gowers-still-misses-bow-variance.md). The results concern different analytic objects; the common statement is about whether an intermediate retains the signed information, physical scale and destination coupling actually required.

Two current frontiers fail for related structural reasons: the desired improvement is a **signed destination statement**, while an available intermediate either forgets the needed covariance, is evaluated in a larger parameter regime than the destination uses, or removes a generic self-term without adding the source-specific relation that the destination consumes.

In the fixed-source Wang channel, VIS-275 shows that once the residual has been replaced by a sign-blind absolute envelope, extra signed packet moments cannot improve the certified exponent. VIS-276--VIS-278 separate convention-dependent gamma normalization from an invariant combined residual whose standalone energy lives at `L^-2`. VIS-279--VIS-281 then show that theorem-uniform direct-cross and localization errors shrink to `L^-2` or become power-small after specialization to the physical source scale.

VIS-282 exposes the exact fixed-source Dirichlet diagonal profile

`S_D(x)=x^(-2) sum_(n<=x)n Lambda(n)^2 + x^2 sum_(n>x)Lambda(n)^2/n^3`,

whose prime-power derivative jumps survive at the coefficient scale. VIS-283 now resolves the signed recombination rather than leaving it as a possible cancellation mechanism. The arithmetic residual in `B_x+E_x` has the same temporal-frequency orientation as `D_x` after conjugation, so the product frequencies are `-log(mn)` and there is no zero-frequency diagonal. The complete source cross is power-small after packet normalization, while the fixed-source field energy through constant order is the positive diagonal sum

`Q_gamma(x)=S_D(x)+C_Lambda/x^2`

after retaining the exact gamma baseline. Thus preserving sign does not mean cancellation must occur: **frequency support can forbid the equal-scale cross term altogether**. The next interface question is what the actual packet projection does to this diagonal arithmetic profile, not how to manufacture a cancellation that the field decomposition does not contain.

In Weil Inertia, ordinary Gowers smallness loses the needed two-point sign in a different way. WI-338 squeezes the ordinary hierarchy against its deterministic zero-increment diagonal wall: below the wall generic matched-energy signs can be Gowers-small on every bow interval while preserving full sliding variance; at the wall source energy prevents smallness. WI-339 removes every zero-increment cube, but the matched-energy countermodel still has small diagonal-deleted amplitude while retaining essentially full bow variance. Subtracting generic self-energy does not manufacture the distinguished negative covariance.

The shared lesson is therefore sharper than “preserve sign” or “renormalize the diagonal.” An intermediate is useful only if it preserves the destination-sensitive relation, is evaluated at the same physical scale, and retains the exact source-frequency/covariance structure that decides whether a signed cross term even exists. A normalization-invariant quantity can still be too coarse; a diagonal-free statistic can still be source-blind; and a uniform theorem bound can hide both a smaller true error and a structured coefficient-scale term.

There are three honest ways across such a boundary. Recover and estimate the destination-sensitive structure before majorization or norm compression; define a renormalization whose subtraction is tied to the source/carrier relation rather than generic degeneracy; or specialize the upstream theorem early enough that exact frequency and diagonal structure survive through the physical coordinate map. More constraints inside an already sign-destructive, scale-mismatched or source-blind summary do not help unless they control the actual destination observable.

**Boundary.** VIS-283 does not prove the complete pair-correlation remainder is `O(L^-2)` or that every admissible packet preserves `Q_gamma`; it proves the direct prime/source cross cannot cancel the coefficient-scale diagonal profile before projection. WI-339 leaves a top growing-order strip and does not rule out source-dependent or carrier-dependent renormalizations. The synthesis does not identify the two problems mathematically; it states the common interface test.
