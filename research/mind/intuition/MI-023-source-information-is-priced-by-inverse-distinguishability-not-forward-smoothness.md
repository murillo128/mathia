# MI-023 — Source information is priced by the declared source class, physical map and inverse discrimination geometry

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [FD-167](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-167-full-field-l2-inversion-has-uniform-square-root-prefix-conditioning.md) through [FD-183](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-183-subcritical-prime-extension-energy-forces-semiprime-scale-support.md), [MC-342](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-342-forward-source-regularity-does-not-price-analog-capacity.md) through [MC-359](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-359-total-variation-source-edge-tariff.md), [NB-176](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-176-euler-weighted-fourier-fingerprints-certify-source-complexity.md) through [NB-193](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-193-weighted-euler-band-recurrence-still-has-double-exponential-inclusion-length.md), and [RE-184](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-184-growing-euler-jet-depth-below-kv-shell-scale-remains-nonrigid.md) through [RE-193](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-193-dyadic-laplace-compression-makes-euler-stable-dimension-logarithmic.md). No universal acquisition theorem or identification of these currencies is claimed.

Across these lines, raw coordinate count is repeatedly the wrong currency. What matters is the declared source class, the quotient or destination that must be distinguished, the physical source-coordinate map, the observation/acquisition topology, the tolerated precision and the inverse discrimination or repair cost after the representation has been conditioned as well as the source allows.

Farey Discrepancy now makes the **source-fibre dependence of a relation tariff** explicit. FD-181 proves that any squarefree-supported perturbation of Möbius with changed-coordinate support `o(x/log x)` pays prime-extension multiplicativity energy at scale `x/log x`, even when prime coordinates themselves may move. FD-182 composes that coercive theorem with the permanent FD-179 dyadic-null source: one fixed non-Möbius source matches the entire dyadic midpoint ladder exactly while retaining prime-extension defect `(C+o(1))x/log x`. Thus the relation itself is expensive, but those observations do not expose it below the critical scale.

FD-183 shows that the apparent cardinal price changes when the admissible fibre changes. If prime coordinates are frozen at the Möbius values and `n_0` is the first changed coordinate, subcritical prime-extension energy forces support at least `(1/n_0+o(1))x loglog x/log x`. The extra `loglog x` comes from a second multiplicative generation that cannot be screened by changing prime labels. The same relation therefore has a prime-fan repair cost on a moving-prime fibre and a semiprime-scale repair cost on the fixed-prime fibre. A source tariff is meaningful only together with the exact source freedoms under which it was proved and an observation theorem that sees the tariff-producing relation.

Möbius Cancellation identifies analogous representation dependence in likelihood geometry. MC-354--MC-356 price regular smooth channels through mean/covariance motion and integrated Fisher action. MC-357--MC-358 show that source discrimination can move between a latent selector and source-dependent components inside an actual common lifted architecture but cannot disappear: `b_e(Y) <= b_e(Theta)+c_e(K)`.

MC-359 adds a bounded source-edge currency that remains useful when KL/Jeffreys becomes infinite. If `t_e=TV(P_x,P_x')` and `T_1=(2/m)sum_e t_e`, then transcript information is bounded above by `(log 2)T_1` up to the punctured-cube correction, so bounded-energy nontrivial dephasing forces `T_1/R` away from zero. Under genuine shared randomness `Y=F(x,U_0)`, each edge TV is bounded by the probability that a one-source flip changes the transcript under the common seed. Thus source information may be priced by smooth divergence, bounded distinguishability, or support singularity depending on the architecture; changing the metric does not make the destination-imposed edge separation free.

Nyman--Beurling separates representation cost, acquisition variation and locality recurrence. NB-190 shows that a scalar Euler-translate mixture approximating a probe supported on source mass `Theta` needs total variation `Omega(1/Theta)`. NB-191 shows that high-block support adds no universal surcharge at fixed source width because finite active frequencies recur syndetically. NB-192 then prices coordinatewise recurrence: one fixed log-width Euler band already contains `m_a~a exp(r_0/a)` rationally independent prime-log phases, forcing `log L_a >> a exp(r_0/a)`. NB-193 shows that replacing max-phase recurrence by the natural positive true-source-weighted `ell^1` mismatch does not remove the scale. Its target Haar volume is exponentially small in the effective dimension `D_eff=1/sum w_j^2`, and for the true prime weights `D_eff asymp m_a`. Thus both positive syndetic transplant mechanisms retain the same double-exponential worst-gap tariff. A surviving shortcut must use block-specific early return, signed aggregation, a different topology, or a different synthesis architecture rather than another positive recurrence relaxation.

Robin Extremal gives a continuous stable-rank version of the same warning. RE-189--RE-192 show that high normalized Euler jets, shrinking spectral windows and finite-Laplace rectangles can have very small robust rank even when exact analytic data remain source-rigid. RE-193 sharpens the mixed-scale law substantially. For `R=U log(Q_+/Q_-)`, the exact scalar Euler channel admits, above the prime-power floor, a rank

`O((1+log(1+R))(1+log(1/epsilon)))`

approximation at tolerance `epsilon`. At fixed precision, polynomial source separation with constant spectral span therefore supplies only `O(loglog p)` robust scalar coordinates. Growing source support or even growing `R` is not by itself new information capacity; the transform rectangle and **source-native precision** determine what can be stably resolved.

The reusable accounting rule is therefore: before assigning an information or rigidity cost, fix the admissible source fibre, destination quotient, physical source map, dual observation window, relation graph, acquisition operator class, noise/likelihood geometry, allowed renormalization, localization/recurrence scale, matched-control class, requested precision and inverse discrimination/repair geometry. Then identify both the resource that cannot be made free **and the observation theorem that actually exposes it**. A tariff hidden in an observation nullspace, altered by changing the admissible fibre, relocated inside an unobserved latent/component channel, charged through a recurrence target whose volume collapses, expressed in a metric that breaks at the endpoint, or encoded in a transform rectangle with logarithmic stable rank does not become destination leverage merely because the upstream source relation is exact.

**Boundary.** The cited results concern different mathematical models. FD-181--FD-183 do not rule out richer Farey observations or identify sharp thresholds on every source fibre. MC-359 supplies no arithmetic upper bound for an actual Möbius channel and TV is not interchangeable with Jeffreys. NB-192--NB-193 bound positive syndetic recurrence mechanisms, not every possible high-block synthesizer or unusually early return. RE-193 is a finite-precision upper bound, not a matching lower bound or a Robin criterion. The synthesis is an audit rule about pricing and observing the actual source motion, not a theorem equating prime-fan energy, source-edge TV/divergence, recurrence entropy or finite-Laplace conditioning.
