# MI-023 — Source information is priced by the declared source class, physical map and inverse discrimination geometry

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [FD-167](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-167-full-field-l2-inversion-has-uniform-square-root-prefix-conditioning.md) through [FD-182](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-182-exact-dyadic-midpoint-null-data-do-not-control-prime-extension-multiplicativity.md), [MC-342](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-342-forward-source-regularity-does-not-price-analog-capacity.md) through [MC-358](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-358-source-dependent-mixture-component-tariff.md), [NB-176](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-176-euler-weighted-fourier-fingerprints-certify-source-complexity.md) through [NB-193](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-193-weighted-euler-band-recurrence-still-has-double-exponential-inclusion-length.md), and [RE-184](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-184-growing-euler-jet-depth-below-kv-shell-scale-remains-nonrigid.md) through [RE-192](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-192-spectral-span-times-log-source-width-controls-euler-stable-rank.md). No universal acquisition theorem or identification of these currencies is claimed.

Across these lines, raw coordinate count is repeatedly the wrong currency. What matters is the declared source class, the quotient or destination that must be distinguished, the physical source-coordinate map, the observation/acquisition topology, and the inverse discrimination or repair cost after the representation has been conditioned as well as the source allows.

Farey Discrepancy separates **relation coercivity from observability of that relation**. FD-181 proves that any squarefree-supported perturbation of Möbius with changed-coordinate support `o(x/log x)` pays prime-extension multiplicativity energy at scale `x/log x`, with weighted reciprocal perturbation mass as the coefficient. FD-182 composes that theorem with the permanent FD-179 dyadic-null source: one fixed non-Möbius source matches the entire dyadic midpoint ladder exactly while retaining prime-extension defect `(C+o(1))x/log x`. Thus the relation itself is expensive, but the dyadic midpoint measurements do not see enough of it to force the defect below the critical scale. A source tariff is useful only when the declared observation family controls the tariff-producing relation.

Möbius Cancellation identifies an analogous principle in likelihood geometry. MC-354 prices common additive log-smooth readouts; MC-355 adds source-dependent Gaussian covariance; MC-356 gives integrated Fisher action for regular exponential families. MC-357 moves source discrimination into the latent selector for fixed-component mixtures. MC-358 closes the immediate moving-component escape for an actual common latent architecture: with `Q_x(dtheta,dy)=nu_x(dtheta)K_x(dy|theta)`,

`b_e(Y) <= (1/2)J(Q_x,Q_x') = b_e(Theta)+c_e(K)`.

Combined with the endpoint lower tariff, source information may be paid by latent-law motion, conditional component motion, or another explicit source path, but it cannot disappear by changing mixture representation. The invariant resource is the lifted divergence of the real architecture; a convenient post-hoc mixture decomposition is not an acquisition theorem.

Nyman--Beurling separates representation cost, acquisition variation and locality recurrence. NB-190 shows that a scalar Euler-translate mixture approximating a probe supported on source mass `Theta` needs total variation `Omega(1/Theta)`. NB-191 shows that high-block support adds no universal surcharge at fixed source width because finite active frequencies recur syndetically. NB-192 then prices coordinatewise recurrence: one fixed log-width Euler band already contains `m_a~a exp(r_0/a)` rationally independent prime-log phases, forcing `log L_a >> a exp(r_0/a)`. NB-193 shows that replacing max-phase recurrence by the natural positive true-source-weighted `ell^1` mismatch does not remove the scale. Its target Haar volume is exponentially small in the effective dimension `D_eff=1/sum w_j^2`, and for the true prime weights `D_eff asymp m_a`. Thus both positive syndetic transplant mechanisms retain the same double-exponential worst-gap tariff. A surviving shortcut must use block-specific early return, signed aggregation, a different topology, or a different synthesis architecture rather than another positive recurrence relaxation.

Robin Extremal gives a continuous stable-rank version of the same warning. RE-189 shows that high factorial-normalized boundary jets converge toward a common net-multiplicity mode. RE-190 makes an entire `o(1)` spectral neighborhood asymptotically rank one on a fixed selector shell. RE-191 gives factorial compression on a fixed interval, and RE-192 identifies the dimensionless resource governing the mixed regime. For source support `[Q_-,Q_+]` and spectral span `U`, the normalized Euler channel has rank-`m` error bounded by

`[U log(Q_+/Q_-)]^m/m! + O(Q_-^-1)`.

Thus spectral width and logarithmic source diameter trade through `R=U log(Q_+/Q_-)`. Even polynomial source separation can have uniformly bounded stable rank when the spectral window shrinks like `1/log p`. Growing support is not by itself new information capacity; the transform rectangle and source-native precision determine what can be stably resolved.

The reusable accounting rule is therefore: before assigning an information or rigidity cost, fix the admissible source fibre, destination quotient, physical source map, dual observation window, relation graph, acquisition operator class, noise/likelihood geometry, allowed renormalization, localization/recurrence scale, matched-control class and inverse discrimination/repair geometry. Then identify both the resource that cannot be made free **and the observation theorem that actually exposes it**. A tariff hidden in an observation nullspace, relocated inside an unobserved latent/component channel, charged through a recurrence target whose volume collapses, or encoded in a transform rectangle with bounded stable rank does not become destination leverage merely because the upstream source relation is exact.

**Boundary.** The cited results concern different mathematical models. FD-181--FD-182 do not rule out richer Farey observations. MC-358 prices a specified lifted architecture but supplies no arithmetic upper bound and no canonical decomposition of an arbitrary marginal. NB-192--NB-193 bound positive syndetic recurrence mechanisms, not every possible high-block synthesizer or unusually early return. RE-189--RE-192 concern scalar Euler observables and give stable-rank upper bounds, not matching lower bounds or a Robin criterion. The synthesis is an audit rule about pricing and observing the actual source motion, not a theorem equating prime-fan energy, lifted Jeffreys divergence, recurrence entropy or finite-Laplace conditioning.
