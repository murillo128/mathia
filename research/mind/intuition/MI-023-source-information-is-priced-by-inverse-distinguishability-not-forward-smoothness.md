# MI-023 — Source information is priced by the declared source class, physical map and inverse discrimination geometry

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [FD-167](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-167-full-field-l2-inversion-has-uniform-square-root-prefix-conditioning.md) through [FD-187](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-187-prime-translated-divisor-cubes-remove-the-rank-penalty-from-physical-prime-rigidity.md), [MC-342](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-342-forward-source-regularity-does-not-price-analog-capacity.md) through [MC-364](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-364-product-affinity-log-depth-tariff.md), [NB-176](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-176-euler-weighted-fourier-fingerprints-certify-source-complexity.md) through [NB-198](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-198-brun-titchmarsh-phase-occupancy-excludes-accurate-positive-euler-returns-at-every-sublinear-mellin-power.md), and [RE-184](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-184-growing-euler-jet-depth-below-kv-shell-scale-remains-nonrigid.md) through [RE-197](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-197-subcritical-growing-jets-collapse-the-full-real-euler-ray-superlogarithmically.md). These lines use different mathematical objects; no universal identification of their currencies is claimed.

Across them, raw dimension, exact injectivity and forward smoothness repeatedly fail as complexity currencies. The useful quantity is the resource that the **declared source class and physical observation map force the destination to acquire**, measured in the inverse geometry native to that architecture.

Farey Discrepancy makes the source-fibre dependence of a relation tariff explicit. Sparse dyadic midpoint observations can have a permanent kernel even when the hidden source pays critical prime-extension multiplicativity energy. On the physical-prime fibre, however, one changed composite cannot hide at subcritical energy. FD-187 sharpens the mechanism from one anchored detour to the exact capacity of the entire translated divisor cube. If `n_0` has defect `Delta`, rank `k`, and `q>1`, then

`liminf (log x/x) M_(a,q)(x) >= Delta^q/[n_0 B_(k,q)^(q-1)]`,

with `B_(k,q)->1` for fixed `q` as `k->infinity`. The earlier path-length penalty was therefore not intrinsic: parallel relation routes recover almost the full local defect cost. Yet FD-182 still shows that the dyadic midpoint ladder does not observe this tariff. The unresolved resource is the **observation theorem exposing relation capacity**, not merely the existence of a coercive relation.

Möbius Cancellation shows the analogous dependence on statistical architecture. TV is the finite currency for genuine common-seed change probability; Hellinger is the bounded compositional currency for genuine latent/product channels. MC-363 forces the output Hellinger boundary to become almost maximal near deterministic dephasing. MC-364 then moves to the additive coordinate native to multiplicative affinity. For source-independent latent/product architectures, directional negative log-affinity depth `Lambda_i` satisfies

`(1/R) sum_i Lambda_i >= -log beta_R`,

and at matched-filter error `epsilon` the lower bound is asymptotically `(1/2)log(1/epsilon)`. The quantile bound on shallow directions prevents this cost from being hidden in a small exceptional set. Splitting a transcript into many weak components therefore does not merely preserve an extensive tariff; near the endpoint it requires **diverging Bhattacharyya depth** unless a genuinely singular component pays the cost.

Nyman--Beurling separates representation, recurrence volume and source occupancy. Exact high-block recurrence exists at fixed source width, but as the Euler band shrinks the prime-log phase family becomes enormous. NB-192--NB-193 price generic positive recurrence by effective dimension, while NB-194--NB-197 use increasingly sharp source-resolution theorems to exclude returns on the physical block. NB-198 reveals a different inverse resource: to exclude a sufficiently accurate positive return one needs only upper occupancy control of the favorable phase windows. Brun--Titchmarsh therefore reaches every fixed Mellin exponent below one, with a return gap that deteriorates as the endpoint is approached. The true remaining positive frontier is `t=x_a^(1-o(1))`, not the earlier `13/15` source-approximation corridor. Positivity is load-bearing; signed synthesis remains outside this occupancy tariff.

Robin Extremal gives the stable-inverse counterpart. Exact scalar Euler data identify the source at infinite precision, but matched ordinary-prime controls can make that scalar channel almost blind while preserving a macroscopic Robin transient. RE-196 established arbitrary fixed log-power suppression on the complete positive real Euler ray. RE-197 now lets the matched jet depth grow throughout `K loglog p=o(log p)` and obtains

`sup_(s>=1)|D_Q(s)| <= d_p exp(-(1-o(1))K loglog p)`

for `K->infinity`, up to a much smaller floor. A wider spectral domain and slowly growing local depth therefore still do not provide selector-scale stable discrimination. The relevant source resource must exclude the fixed-window moment repair or control a joint/nonlocal observable that it cannot annihilate.

These examples refine the same accounting rule. A source tariff is useful only when four pieces line up: the admissible source fibre, the physical acquisition map, the destination-relevant quotient, and an inverse modulus in the coordinate in which the architecture actually composes. The local geometry may be a relation-network capacity, negative log-affinity depth, positive phase occupancy, or a Laplace-moment inverse modulus. Replacing any of those by raw rank, support size, transform width, a single path, linearized product cost or exact injectivity can manufacture an apparent resource that the source can move, parallelize, balance or hide.

The newer results also sharpen how to attack such problems. Before importing a generic complexity measure, identify the exact finite/analytic network through which source information must travel and compute its native extremal quantity: full network capacity rather than one route; log-affinity rather than a linear union bound; occupancy rather than continuum approximation when positivity permits it; first omitted transport moment rather than spectral span. Only then ask whether the physical observation actually controls that quantity.

**Boundary.** The currencies above are not interchangeable. FD-187 does not supply a Farey observation-to-relation theorem or remove moved-prime escapes. MC-364 supplies no arithmetic upper bound for an actual Möbius transcript. NB-198 is positivity-specific and does not constrain signed/complex synthesis or endpoint-layer returns. RE-197 is a matched-control inverse-modulus theorem restricted to the subcritical fixed-window jet regime. The synthesis is an audit principle about where quantitative source information really resides, not a theorem equating multiplicative capacity, statistical divergence, recurrence occupancy and Euler-ray conditioning.
