# MI-023 — Source information is priced by the declared source class, physical map and inverse discrimination geometry

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [FD-167](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-167-full-field-l2-inversion-has-uniform-square-root-prefix-conditioning.md) through [FD-180](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-180-subpolynomial-deletion-sources-pay-an-exact-prime-extension-defect-floor.md), [MC-342](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-342-forward-source-regularity-does-not-price-analog-capacity.md) through [MC-355](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-355-gaussian-location-scale-covariance-side-channel-tariff.md), [NB-176](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-176-euler-weighted-fourier-fingerprints-certify-source-complexity.md) through [NB-190](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-190-normalized-euler-bands-have-a-sharp-inverse-source-mass-scalar-translate-synthesis-cost.md), and [RE-184](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-184-growing-euler-jet-depth-below-kv-shell-scale-remains-nonrigid.md) through [RE-189](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-189-high-euler-boundary-jets-collapse-to-net-multiplicity-past-log-squared-depth.md). No universal acquisition theorem is claimed.

Across these lines, raw coordinate count is repeatedly the wrong currency. What matters is the declared source class, the quotient or destination that must be distinguished, the physical source-coordinate map, the observation/acquisition topology, and the inverse discrimination or repair cost after the representation has been conditioned as well as the source allows.

Farey Discrepancy now prices a concrete relational boundary rather than merely naming multiplicativity. FD-179 leaves an exact dyadic-midpoint null source whose coordinate defects are confined to a subpolynomial extremal shell. FD-180 proves that within the deletion-only class with physical primes, that sparse repair nevertheless emits an exact prime-extension boundary: normalized finite-`L^q` multiplicativity defect is asymptotic to `((zeta(2)C_S)/(log x loglog x))^(1/q)`. Requiring a smaller rate forces the deletion set to vanish. The source relation is therefore a quantitative resource, but its coercive scale is source-class specific.

Möbius Cancellation identifies how source information can migrate inside a statistical channel without disappearing from inverse geometry. MC-354 prices common additive log-smooth readouts by mean displacement and noise curvature. MC-355 allows source-dependent Gaussian covariance and finds an additional exact positive Jeffreys term given by affine-invariant covariance deformation. Mean motion can be traded for covariance motion, but both remain charged. Exact dephasing leaves the finite nondegenerate Gaussian class altogether because neighboring transcript KL must become infinite.

Nyman--Beurling separates representation cost from acquisition cost. NB-189 shows that true Euler amplitudes, positive source mass, bounded fanout and the sharp square-root amplification tariff do not prevent a matched-control Fourier code. NB-190 then prices the normalization operation itself: a scalar Euler-translate mixture approximating a probe supported on source mass `Theta` needs total variation `Omega(1/Theta)`, sharply so at fixed accuracy. For `K` balanced bands this is linear `K` variation per coordinate, larger than the representation-level square-root tariff. The control can still pay it if unrestricted mixing is allowed, so the next invariant must bound the *admissible acquisition budget*, not merely exhibit a cost.

Robin Extremal adds a different warning: a resource can stop being informative rather than merely becoming expensive. RE-188 reduces the constructive Euler-jet repair width to linear in depth over a large sub-log-squared range. RE-189 shows that at `j~(log p)^2`, factorial-normalized boundary jets themselves collapse exponentially toward a common net-multiplicity coordinate. Beyond that crossover, greater scalar depth no longer means more robust prime-location information unless one pays the inverse conditioning needed to recover exponentially small modes.

The reusable accounting rule is therefore: before assigning an information or rigidity cost, fix the admissible source fibre, destination quotient, physical source map, relation graph, acquisition operator class, noise/likelihood geometry, allowed renormalization, matched-control class and inverse discrimination/repair geometry. Then identify the resource that cannot be moved into a different channel, diluted over a relation graph, purchased by a matched control, or lost through common-mode saturation.

**Boundary.** The cited results concern different mathematical models. FD-180 is deletion-class specific; MC-355 covers finite nondegenerate Gaussian location-scale channels; NB-190 prices unrestricted Fourier--Stieltjes scalar mixing rather than the actual Nyman operator budget; RE-189 concerns normalized scalar boundary jets rather than all Euler observables. The synthesis is an audit rule, not a theorem equating these currencies.
