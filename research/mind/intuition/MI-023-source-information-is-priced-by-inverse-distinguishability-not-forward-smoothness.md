# MI-023 — Source information is priced by the declared source class, physical map and inverse discrimination geometry

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [FD-167](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-167-full-field-l2-inversion-has-uniform-square-root-prefix-conditioning.md) through [FD-178](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-178-variable-dyadic-blocks-hide-second-order-near-maximal-multiplicative-rank-fidelity.md), [MC-342](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-342-forward-source-regularity-does-not-price-analog-capacity.md) through [MC-353](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-353-privacy-loss-tail-budget.md), [NB-176](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-176-euler-weighted-fourier-fingerprints-certify-source-complexity.md) through [NB-188](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-188-hadamard-scale-euler-aggregation-forces-square-root-source-amplification-and-bounded-fanout.md), and [RE-184](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-184-growing-euler-jet-depth-below-kv-shell-scale-remains-nonrigid.md) through [RE-187](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-187-expanding-chebyshev-windows-push-euler-jet-non-rigidity-past-the-scalar-crossover.md). No universal acquisition theorem is claimed.

Across these lines, raw coordinate count is repeatedly the wrong currency. What matters is the declared source class, the quotient or destination that must be distinguished, the physical source-coordinate map, the observation/acquisition topology, and the inverse discrimination or repair cost after the representation has been conditioned as well as the source allows.

Farey Discrepancy gives the strongest warning about **coordinate fidelity without relational fidelity**. Complete fields and consecutive histories invert unrestricted sources, while true multiplicativity makes sparse geometric samples target-rigid. Yet FD-178 constructs permanent deletion-only controls that match every dyadic midpoint while agreeing with Möbius throughout the second-order near-maximal rank window

`omega(n) <= (log n/loglog n)(1+c/loglog n)`

for every fixed `0<c<1`, with only `x^o(1)` defects. The missing information is a cross-coordinate product relation, not another individually correct coefficient.

Möbius Cancellation prices the opposite side: how much inverse statistical distinction an endpoint transcript must actually expose. Source information forces an extensive channel-law boundary and aggregate edge KL. MC-353 identifies the exact local currency as the privacy-loss distribution: edge half-Jeffreys divergence is `E psi(|L|)`. Thus a bounded-energy dephasing architecture cannot have privacy loss tend to zero in probability together with uniform integrability; the compulsory information must appear as typical reverse contrast or a quantitatively heavy tail. Forward smoothness says nothing comparable.

Nyman--Beurling shows that source mass and phase rank must be charged together. The first `O(1/a)` prime channels can carry Hadamard-scale raw phases but only a vanishing fraction of the Euler--Wiener source mass. NB-188 shows that compressing a positive source fraction into `O(1/a)` linear observables forces square-root source amplification and, at bounded row gain, bounded effective fanout. A large phase matrix obtained by normalizing away weak channels is therefore not a source-faithful information certificate.

Robin Extremal adds a complementary lesson about **where a resource cost can move after reconditioning**. Successive constructions remove monomial conditioning and coarse-generation transport losses. RE-187 then pushes exact scalar Euler-jet matching parametrically past the former `log p/loglog p` crossover by widening the prime repair reservoir, paying `W~K^2/log p` in spatial support while remaining invisible to every fixed KV envelope. A dimension threshold is not intrinsic if an admissible matched control can trade that dimension for another source coordinate.

The reusable accounting rule is therefore: before assigning an information or rigidity cost, fix the admissible source fibre, destination quotient, physical source map, acquisition norm, allowed renormalization, matched-control class and inverse discrimination/repair geometry. Then identify the resource that cannot be moved elsewhere by reparameterization, sparse high-rank repair, hidden precision, amplitude renormalization, broad aggregation or spatial relocation.

**Boundary.** The cited results concern different mathematical models. FD-178 does not settle full extremal-rank fidelity; MC-353 gives no analytic privacy-loss bound for a concrete Möbius architecture; NB-188 is a linear same-frequency Wiener aggregation theorem; RE-187 gives a sufficient non-rigid range rather than a sharp spatial-capacity lower bound. The synthesis is an audit rule, not a theorem equating these currencies.
