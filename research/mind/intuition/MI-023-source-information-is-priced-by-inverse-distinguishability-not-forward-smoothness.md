# MI-023 — Source information is priced by the declared source class, physical map and inverse discrimination geometry

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [FD-195](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-195-franel-duality-has-a-nonvanishing-cost-on-geometric-quotient-witnesses.md), [FD-196](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-196-weighted-fourier-duality-has-a-sharp-geometric-recovery-line.md), [FD-197](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-197-rademacher-multiplicative-second-moments-saturate-weighted-fourier-deficit.md), [MC-378](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-378-moving-smooth-frame-forces-iterated-log-source-cost-rate.md), [NB-212](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-212-smooth-euler-barycenter-saturates-the-log-squared-vinogradov-korobov-corridor.md), [NB-213](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-213-absolute-mellin-primitivization-preserves-a-positive-power-vk-tariff.md), [RE-213](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-213-adaptive-kaiser-tests-close-the-separated-vertical-endpoint-at-unit-rate.md), and [WI-347](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-347-extensive-bow-feature-rank-requires-projective-resolution-collapse.md). These lines use different mathematical objects; no universal identification of their currencies is claimed.

Raw dimension, exact injectivity and forward smoothness repeatedly fail as complexity currencies. The useful quantity is the resource that the **declared source class and physical observation map force the destination to acquire**, measured in the inverse geometry and norm native to that architecture.

Farey Discrepancy makes the destination-norm dependence explicit. Above the geometric square-root threshold the cumulative source has a stable raw inverse, but FD-195 shows that the same quotient witness has order-one dual cost after compression to the Franel/GCD quadratic energy. FD-196 quantifies the weighted family: a square-root band with energy exponent `gamma` gives the RH-facing Mertens exponent only when `gamma+sigma<=3/2`, while the classical Franel transfer remains on `gamma+sigma=2`. FD-197 shows that generic squarefree Rademacher multiplicativity already saturates this noncritical line. The useful tariff is therefore not Fourier count or generic random cancellation, but deterministic structure surviving the destination norm.

Möbius Cancellation strengthens the source-realization side. MC-377 proves that an actual exact endpoint cannot have **any fixed polynomial common source radical**. MC-378 makes the escape quantitative: if an exact package has source rank `R` and common lower-prime radical `P`, then the present moving smooth-character/frame argument gives

`log P/log y >= c min{sqrt(R/log(R+2)), log log log y}`

with a nonoptimized absolute constant. The old near-quadratic conductor/bias picture remains conditional anatomy of a ruled-out finite-power regime. The current source tariff is a moving common-radical exponent, and its proof-rate bottlenecks are the `O(A^2)` smooth-subspace codimension and exponentially deteriorating character-saving uniformity.

Nyman--Beurling shows both that a representation used to expose a source tariff can be nonessential and that a formally smoother representation can fail to reduce the **reconstructed** tariff. NB-210--NB-212 show that one fixed smooth full-von-Mangoldt barycenter already reaches the log-squared Vinogradov--Korobov corridor, so prime-harmonic multiplicity is not load-bearing. NB-213 then tests the natural primitive repair: replacing `-zeta'/zeta` by `log zeta` and estimating absolutely differentiates the Mellin carrier and costs `X=log x=Q^(2/3+o(1))` on the coupled diagonal. Even an optimistically subpolynomial primitive envelope is therefore converted back into a positive-power `Q` tariff. The price must be measured **after source reconstruction**, not at the apparently cheaper intermediate representation.

Robin Extremal gives a remote-support continuum tariff and now pins the best exponential rate of the positive compact-test representation. RE-209--RE-212 convert hiding a selector-scale packet over a full vertical interval into an exponentially large signed prime-prefix cost. RE-213 makes the Kaiser parameter adaptive and proves

`log R >= H T-O(sqrt(H T log(H T)))`.

For `T_Q=O(log Q)`, preserving every fixed KV envelope therefore forces `((1/2)log Q-H T_Q)/V(Q)->+infinity`. In particular, fixed `T_Q=c log Q` requires strict `c<1/(2H)`; for `H=log 8`, `c>=0.2404491734...` is excluded, while explicit hiding is known only below `0.0289079...`. The same finding proves `sup_w Gamma(w)=1` for nonnegative compact windows, so further coercive improvement cannot come from merely shaping a better positive test. The physical currency is signed-prefix/Chebyshev discrepancy, and the remaining constant gap belongs to source construction or to a genuinely different converse mechanism.

Weil Inertia gives a resolution tariff for representation complexity. WI-347 shows that any height-independent feature family with a height-uniform projective continuity radius `rho_epsilon` has effective rank bounded by `1+O(H Delta/rho_epsilon)`. At fixed aperture `H Delta<=1`, extensive rank therefore forces the useful projective source-resolution radius to collapse like `O(1/r)`. Channel count, nonlinearity and ambient dimension are not source information unless the physical map resolves progressively finer source differences at nonvanishing transmitted scale.

The reusable accounting rule is therefore: specify the admissible source fibre and topology, physical observation family, destination quotient and norm, requested precision, source support domain and moving parameter regime, then compute the **inverse** resource needed to distinguish the target in that same currency. Finally test whether the physical source can pay it. A contraction visible before destination compression may disappear afterward; a finite conductor floor can become a quantitative superpolynomial common-source tariff; an apparently cheaper primitive can repay its gain during shell reconstruction; continuum coverage becomes useful only when its signed-prefix cost exceeds the source budget; and feature richness matters only when the source-resolution modulus scales with the requested destination rank.

**Boundary.** The currencies are not interchangeable. Farey weighted energy, Möbius common-radical size, Nyman reconstructed contour-prefactor growth, Robin signed-prefix discrepancy and Weil-Inertia projective resolution live in different source/destination architectures. The synthesis is an audit principle about where quantitative information is actually priced, not a theorem transferring one line's numerical threshold to another.
