# MI-023 — Source information is priced by the declared source class, physical map and inverse discrimination geometry

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [FD-195](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-195-franel-duality-has-a-nonvanishing-cost-on-geometric-quotient-witnesses.md), [FD-196](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-196-weighted-fourier-duality-has-a-sharp-geometric-recovery-line.md), [FD-197](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-197-rademacher-multiplicative-second-moments-saturate-weighted-fourier-deficit.md), [MC-378](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-378-moving-smooth-frame-forces-iterated-log-source-cost-rate.md), [MC-379](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-379-rough-squarefree-terminal-block-lifts-moving-source-cost-ceiling.md), [NB-212](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-212-smooth-euler-barycenter-saturates-the-log-squared-vinogradov-korobov-corridor.md), [NB-214](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-214-positive-shell-preconditioning-cannot-remove-the-mellin-reconstruction-tariff.md), [RE-213](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-213-adaptive-kaiser-tests-close-the-separated-vertical-endpoint-at-unit-rate.md), and [WI-347](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-347-extensive-bow-feature-rank-requires-projective-resolution-collapse.md). These lines use different mathematical objects; no universal identification of their currencies is claimed.

Raw dimension, exact injectivity and forward smoothness repeatedly fail as complexity currencies. The useful quantity is the resource that the **declared source class and physical observation map force the destination to acquire**, measured in the inverse geometry and norm native to that architecture.

Farey Discrepancy makes the destination-norm dependence explicit. Above the geometric square-root threshold the cumulative source has a stable raw inverse, but FD-195 shows that the same quotient witness has order-one dual cost after compression to the Franel/GCD quadratic energy. FD-196 quantifies the weighted family: a square-root band with energy exponent `gamma` gives the RH-facing Mertens exponent only when `gamma+sigma<=3/2`, while the classical Franel transfer remains on `gamma+sigma=2`. FD-197 shows that generic squarefree Rademacher multiplicativity already saturates this noncritical line. The useful tariff is therefore not Fourier count or generic random cancellation, but deterministic structure surviving the destination norm.

Möbius Cancellation strengthens the source-realization side and shows why the source class can change the apparent analytic tariff. MC-377 proves that an actual exact endpoint cannot have **any fixed polynomial common source radical**. MC-378 first makes the escape quantitative, with a rank-sensitive lower bound and a generic smooth-modulus ceiling of order `log log log y`. MC-379 then returns to the actual endpoint conductors. After removing tiny and very large source primes, the surviving characters are primitive, squarefree and rough from below; the terminal complete-sum estimate can be multiplied prime by prime before the final differencing root, avoiding the generic `k2^k<<log log Q` absorption condition. This yields the complementary bound

`log P/log y >= c min{log(R+2), log log y}`.

The improvement is not a universal character-sum theorem: it is bought by the endpoint source-incidence geometry. The current tariff is therefore not “whatever the generic moving theorem charges,” but the strongest cost after all exact source-specific structure has been exposed. MC-378's stronger `sqrt(R/log R)` rank side remains complementary to MC-379's improved analytic ceiling.

Nyman--Beurling shows both that a representation used to expose a source tariff can be nonessential and that source normalization can restore a cost hidden by preconditioning. NB-210--NB-212 show that one fixed smooth full-von-Mangoldt barycenter already reaches the log-squared Vinogradov--Korobov corridor, so prime-harmonic multiplicity is not load-bearing. NB-213 shows that replacing `-zeta'/zeta` by `log zeta` and estimating absolutely differentiates the Mellin carrier and repays the primitive gain by a factor `X`. NB-214 makes the repayment profile-uniform: for every nonnegative `X`-dependent profile supported in a fixed logarithmic shell, the normalized primitive reconstruction kernel satisfies

`||K_X||_1/m_X >= c_Delta X`.

Thus shrinking, concentrating or reshaping the positive source profile cannot reduce the inverse tariff. The price must be measured **after source reconstruction and normalization**, not at the apparently cheaper intermediate transform. Only joint arithmetic/carrier cancellation before absolute values, or a genuinely different signed/complex or moving-support source class, can escape this particular bound.

Robin Extremal gives a remote-support continuum tariff and pins the best exponential rate of the positive compact-test representation. RE-209--RE-213 convert hiding a selector-scale packet over a full vertical interval into an exponentially large signed prime-prefix cost and prove

`log R >= H T-O(sqrt(H T log(H T)))`.

For `T_Q=O(log Q)`, preserving every fixed KV envelope forces `((1/2)log Q-H T_Q)/V(Q)->+infinity`. The same result gives `sup_w Gamma(w)=1` for nonnegative compact windows, so further coercive improvement cannot come from merely shaping a better positive test. The physical currency is signed-prefix/Chebyshev discrepancy, and the remaining constant gap belongs to source construction or to a genuinely different converse mechanism.

Weil Inertia gives a resolution tariff for representation complexity. WI-347 shows that any height-independent feature family with a height-uniform projective continuity radius `rho_epsilon` has effective rank bounded by `1+O(H Delta/rho_epsilon)`. At fixed aperture `H Delta<=1`, extensive rank therefore forces the useful projective source-resolution radius to collapse like `O(1/r)`. Channel count, nonlinearity and ambient dimension are not source information unless the physical map resolves progressively finer source differences at nonvanishing transmitted scale.

The reusable accounting rule is therefore: specify the admissible source fibre and topology, physical observation family, destination quotient and norm, requested precision, source support domain and moving parameter regime, then compute the **inverse** resource needed to distinguish the target in that same currency. Before accepting a generic analytic tariff, use every exact structural restriction of the declared source class; before accepting an apparent preconditioning gain, reconstruct and normalize the physical observable. Finally test whether the physical source can pay the resulting cost.

A contraction visible before destination compression may disappear afterward; a generic moving-character loss may vanish on a rough squarefree source family; an apparently cheaper primitive or adaptive positive profile may repay its gain during shell reconstruction; continuum coverage becomes useful only when its signed-prefix cost exceeds the source budget; and feature richness matters only when the source-resolution modulus scales with the requested destination rank.

**Boundary.** The currencies are not interchangeable. Farey weighted energy, Möbius common-radical size, Nyman reconstructed contour-prefactor growth, Robin signed-prefix discrepancy and Weil-Inertia projective resolution live in different source/destination architectures. The synthesis is an audit principle about where quantitative information is actually priced, not a theorem transferring one line's numerical threshold to another.
