# MI-023 — Source information is priced by the declared source class, physical map and inverse discrimination geometry

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [FD-167](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-167-full-field-l2-inversion-has-uniform-square-root-prefix-conditioning.md) through [FD-184](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-184-subcritical-prime-extension-energy-forces-every-fixed-almost-prime-layer.md), [MC-342](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-342-forward-source-regularity-does-not-price-analog-capacity.md) through [MC-360](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-360-hellinger-product-component-edge-tariff.md), [NB-176](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-176-euler-weighted-fourier-fingerprints-certify-source-complexity.md) through [NB-194](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-194-vinogradov-korobov-dephasing-excludes-positive-euler-band-returns-on-specific-high-blocks.md), and [RE-184](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-184-growing-euler-jet-depth-below-kv-shell-scale-remains-nonrigid.md) through [RE-193](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-193-dyadic-laplace-compression-makes-euler-stable-dimension-logarithmic.md). No universal acquisition theorem or identification of these currencies is claimed.

Across these lines, raw coordinate count is repeatedly the wrong currency. What matters is the declared source class, the quotient or destination that must be distinguished, the physical source-coordinate map, the observation/acquisition topology, the tolerated precision and the inverse discrimination or repair cost after the representation has been conditioned as well as the source allows.

Farey Discrepancy now makes the **source-fibre dependence and depth dependence of a relation tariff** explicit. FD-181 proves that any squarefree-supported perturbation of Möbius with changed-coordinate support `o(x/log x)` pays prime-extension multiplicativity energy at scale `x/log x`, even when prime coordinates themselves may move. FD-182 composes that coercive theorem with the permanent FD-179 dyadic-null source: one fixed non-Möbius source matches the entire dyadic midpoint ladder exactly while retaining prime-extension defect `(C+o(1))x/log x`. Thus the relation itself is expensive, but those observations do not expose it below the critical scale.

FD-183 showed that the apparent cardinal price changes when the admissible fibre changes: fixing the prime coordinates makes subcritical energy force a semiprime-scale support tariff. FD-184 iterates that mechanism through every fixed almost-prime rank. If `n_0` is the first changed coordinate and the prime-extension energy is `o(x/log x)`, then for every fixed `r>=1`,

`S(x) >= (1/(n_0(r-1)!)+o(1)) x (loglog x)^(r-1)/log x`.

So the fixed-prime support tariff beats every fixed power of `loglog x` over the prime-fan scale. The unresolved currency is no longer “one more multiplicative generation” but **uniformity as the allowed rank grows**. The same relation has a first-generation price on the moving-prime fibre and arbitrarily deep fixed-rank coercivity on the fixed-prime fibre; a source tariff is meaningful only together with those source freedoms and depth quantifiers.

Möbius Cancellation identifies analogous representation dependence in likelihood geometry. MC-354--MC-356 price regular smooth channels through mean/covariance motion and integrated Fisher action. MC-357--MC-358 show that source discrimination can move between a latent selector and source-dependent components inside an actual common lifted architecture but cannot disappear: `b_e(Y) <= b_e(Theta)+c_e(K)`.

MC-359 adds a bounded source-edge currency that remains useful when KL/Jeffreys becomes infinite. If `t_e=TV(P_x,P_x')` and `T_1=(2/m)sum_e t_e`, transcript information is bounded above by `(log 2)T_1` up to the punctured-cube correction, so bounded-energy nontrivial dephasing forces `T_1/R` away from zero. Under genuine shared randomness `Y=F(x,U_0)`, each edge TV is bounded by the probability that a one-source flip changes the transcript under the common seed.

MC-360 adds a second bounded currency with a different architectural advantage. Squared Hellinger distance remains finite at singularity, while affinity factorizes for conditionally independent products. For a genuine source-dependent latent law followed by product components, the output edge cost is bounded by latent-law Hellinger distance plus an affinity-weighted sum of conditional component Hellinger costs. With source-independent latent randomness, the endpoint information floor becomes an extensive weighted source-coordinate incidence tariff. Thus source information may be priced by smooth divergence, bounded common-seed distinguishability, or bounded product/latent affinity depending on the architecture; changing the metric or splitting the transcript into components does not make the destination-imposed edge separation free.

Nyman--Beurling separates representation cost, acquisition variation, recurrence entropy and source dephasing. NB-190 shows that a scalar Euler-translate mixture approximating a probe supported on source mass `Theta` needs total variation `Omega(1/Theta)`. NB-191 shows that high-block support adds no universal surcharge at fixed source width because finite active frequencies recur syndetically. NB-192 then prices coordinatewise recurrence: one fixed log-width Euler band already contains `m_a~a exp(r_0/a)` rationally independent prime-log phases, forcing `log L_a >> a exp(r_0/a)`. NB-193 shows that replacing max-phase recurrence by the natural positive true-source-weighted `ell^1` mismatch does not remove the scale; its target Haar volume is exponentially small in effective dimension `D_eff asymp m_a`.

NB-194 closes part of the specific-block loophole with actual arithmetic cancellation rather than entropy. A positive weighted return forces the normalized von-Mangoldt shell barycenter to stay near one, while Vinogradov--Korobov PNT remainder makes that barycenter uniformly small throughout a deterministic corridor reaching `exp(c V(e^(r_0/a)))`. Hence the physical block cannot be exceptionally lucky when `log T=o(V(e^(r_0/a_T)))`. The syndetic and specific-block results live on different scales and leave a large intermediate regime, but together they show that locality can be priced either by phase-space volume or by source-specific dephasing depending on the coupled source-height regime.

Robin Extremal gives a continuous stable-rank version of the same warning. RE-189--RE-192 show that high normalized Euler jets, shrinking spectral windows and finite-Laplace rectangles can have very small robust rank even when exact analytic data remain source-rigid. RE-193 sharpens the mixed-scale law substantially. For `R=U log(Q_+/Q_-)`, the exact scalar Euler channel admits, above the prime-power floor, a rank

`O((1+log(1+R))(1+log(1/epsilon)))`

approximation at tolerance `epsilon`. At fixed precision, polynomial source separation with constant spectral span therefore supplies only `O(loglog p)` robust scalar coordinates. Growing source support or even growing `R` is not by itself new information capacity; the transform rectangle and **source-native precision** determine what can be stably resolved.

The reusable accounting rule is therefore: before assigning an information or rigidity cost, fix the admissible source fibre, destination quotient, physical source map, dual observation window, relation graph, multiplicative depth, acquisition operator class, noise/likelihood geometry, allowed renormalization, localization/recurrence scale, matched-control class, requested precision and inverse discrimination/repair geometry. Then identify both the resource that cannot be made free **and the observation theorem that actually exposes it**.

A tariff hidden in an observation nullspace, altered by changing the admissible fibre, lost when fixed-depth estimates are extrapolated to growing depth, relocated inside an unobserved latent/component channel, split among product components without an aggregate ledger, charged through a recurrence target whose volume collapses, contradicted by source dephasing on the physical block, expressed in a metric that breaks at the endpoint, or encoded in a transform rectangle with logarithmic stable rank does not become destination leverage merely because the upstream source relation is exact.

**Boundary.** The cited results concern different mathematical models. FD-181--FD-184 do not rule out richer Farey observations, give growing-rank uniformity, or identify sharp thresholds on every source fibre. MC-360 supplies no arithmetic upper bound for an actual Möbius channel, and TV, Hellinger and Jeffreys are not interchangeable currencies. NB-192--NB-194 bound positive recurrence/dephasing mechanisms in different coupled regimes, not every possible high-block synthesizer, signed aggregate or intermediate source-width range. RE-193 is a finite-precision upper bound, not a matching lower bound or a Robin criterion. The synthesis is an audit rule about pricing and observing the actual source motion, not a theorem equating prime-extension energy, source-edge divergence, recurrence entropy, dephasing or finite-Laplace conditioning.
