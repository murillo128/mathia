# MI-023 — Source information is priced by the declared source class, physical map and destination inverse geometry

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [FD-199](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-199-half-sobolev-is-the-prime-shell-centering-threshold.md), [MC-381](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-381-fixed-power-smoothness-makes-source-cost-linear-in-rank.md), [NB-215](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-215-signed-fixed-shell-preconditioning-can-only-export-the-mellin-tariff.md), [RE-215](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-215-continuous-kaiser-window-sharpens-prime-count-capacity-bound.md), and [WI-349](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-349-physical-bow-carrier-saturates-the-quadratic-resolution-cost.md), together with the preceding line-local results they refine. These lines use different mathematical objects; no universal identification of their currencies is claimed.

Raw dimension, exact injectivity, forward smoothness and nominal representation complexity repeatedly fail as information currencies. The useful quantity is the resource that the **declared source class and physical observation map force the destination to acquire**, measured only after exact normalization, nuisance-mode removal, reconstruction and quotient operations native to the architecture.

Farey Discrepancy separates common-mode cost from genuine positive-norm coercion. FD-199 computes the exact measurement-space centering tariff on a reciprocal-prime shell: it is `1+sum p^(-2sigma)`, asymptotically bounded exactly for `sigma>=1/2`. Below one half, part of the loss is therefore an avoidable rank-one common-row artifact. Above it, however, the RH-facing positive energy still forces subpower RMS on `Theta(K/log K)` distinct Mertens samples. Center first, then audit what simultaneous source suppression remains.

Möbius Cancellation shows that a proof should spend only as much source rank as its terminal contradiction needs. MC-380 packs tiny source primes instead of deleting them. MC-381 then fixes the smoothness cutoff at a shell power and observes that an exponential frame does not need `R-o(R)` surviving dimensions: a fixed positive fraction suffices. The common-radical source cost becomes `c min{R,log log y}` rather than a square-root rank scale. A default near-full-rank requirement can therefore create a fictitious source tariff.

Nyman--Beurling shows that preconditioning can relocate rather than remove a tariff. Signed/complex fixed-shell profiles can suppress a central channel, but the global Mellin reconstruction cost reappears in source conditioning or frequency tails. Acquisition, normalization and reconstruction must be priced together.

Robin Extremal separates **continuum observability**, **window realization**, **carrier capacity** and **arithmetic fidelity**. The unit exponential spectral-gap rate is structural. RE-215 improves the positive-window subexponential cost enough that bounded ordinary-prime multiplicity gives a one-`log log` boundary layer above the half-log endpoint. KV remains a finer currency needed at the exact endpoint and below it. The strongest available arithmetic theorem should not be credited for an obstruction already caused by raw source capacity.

Weil Inertia gives the representation-resolution version and its sharpness. WI-348 shows that extensive ordinary feature rank at fixed aperture forces a square-root resolution/sensitivity tariff. WI-349 proves that this exponent is already attainable on the actual one-parameter physical carrier in the relevant coupled limit. Low parameter dimension is therefore not a cheaper information currency when the source geometry winds on a growing scale.

The reusable accounting rule is: specify the admissible source fibre and topology, physical observation family, destination quotient/norm, requested precision, support/multiplicity constraints and moving regime; remove exactly removable nuisance modes; then compute the **inverse resource** needed in that same currency. Before accepting a rank loss, ask how much rank the contradiction actually needs. Before accepting a preconditioning gain, reconstruct the destination. Before invoking fine fidelity, test crude capacity. Before crediting low-dimensional or rapidly varying features, measure their family-dependent entropy at the physical output resolution.

A contraction can disappear under destination compression; a positive norm can overcoerce many coordinates after its common mode is removed; a source coordinate can sometimes be packed instead of discarded; a frame may need only positive-density rank; a signed preconditioner can export cost rather than erase it; a source can fail first by finite carrier capacity and only later by arithmetic fidelity; and a one-dimensional path can still saturate a quadratic resolution law in a coupled high-frequency limit.

**Boundary.** Farey weighted energy, Möbius common-radical size, Nyman reconstructed contour cost, Robin prime-prefix capacity/fidelity and Weil-Inertia metric entropy live in different architectures. The synthesis is an audit principle about where quantitative information is actually priced, not a theorem transferring one line's numerical threshold to another.
