# MI-023 — Source information is priced by the declared source class, physical map and inverse discrimination geometry

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [FD-167](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-167-full-field-l2-inversion-has-uniform-square-root-prefix-conditioning.md) through [FD-179](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-179-dyadic-midpoint-null-defects-fit-inside-an-o-first-correction-rank-cap.md), [MC-342](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-342-forward-source-regularity-does-not-price-analog-capacity.md) through [MC-354](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-354-log-smooth-additive-readout-edge-tariff.md), [NB-176](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-176-euler-weighted-fourier-fingerprints-certify-source-complexity.md) through [NB-189](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-189-positive-mass-euler-band-routing-saturates-source-fidelity-tariffs-on-poisson-packed-controls.md), and [RE-184](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-184-growing-euler-jet-depth-below-kv-shell-scale-remains-nonrigid.md) through [RE-188](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-188-logarithmic-chebyshev-growth-cuts-the-euler-jet-spatial-tariff-to-linear.md). No universal acquisition theorem is claimed.

Across these lines, raw coordinate count is repeatedly the wrong currency. What matters is the declared source class, the quotient or destination that must be distinguished, the physical source-coordinate map, the observation/acquisition topology, and the inverse discrimination or repair cost after the representation has been conditioned as well as the source allows.

Farey Discrepancy now pushes **coordinate fidelity without relational fidelity** essentially to the extremal boundary. FD-179 constructs permanent deletion-only controls matching every dyadic midpoint while agreeing with Möbius on every sufficiently large squarefree coordinate whose rank lies below `R(n)-2g(n)`, where the remaining cap thickness `g(n)` is `o` of the first extremal correction scale. The defect population is still subpolynomial. The missing information is therefore a cross-coordinate compatibility, not another layer of individually correct coefficients.

Möbius Cancellation identifies when forward geometry really can price inverse statistical distinction. MC-353 expresses the compulsory edge information as a privacy-loss tariff. MC-354 shows that for an additive readout with `beta`-Lipschitz log-noise score, edge half-Jeffreys divergence is at most `(beta/2)||h_e||^2`. Combined with the endpoint lower bound, `beta G_2^2` must stay bounded below. This does not rehabilitate generic forward smoothness: the useful regularity is specifically the likelihood geometry that converts source displacement into reverse divergence.

Nyman--Beurling shows that even paying the known source-fidelity tariffs may remain nonselective. NB-188 forces positive source mass, square-root amplification and bounded fanout for a dense same-frequency code. NB-189 realizes those tariffs sharply with true positive Euler amplitudes and disjoint log-frequency bands, yet a Poisson-packed matched control still produces a DFT-scale `K^(3/2)` nuclear response. The next cost must therefore price **independent spectral-band access or the actual acquisition algebra**, not merely amplitudes, source mass and fanout.

Robin Extremal strengthens the cost-migration warning. RE-187 moved a scalar Euler-jet repair cost into spatial support; RE-188 then shows that its `K^2/log p` Chebyshev width was itself a proof artifact. Exact exterior growth reduces the sufficient width to `W=Theta(K)` and extends the non-rigid jet depth to `K=o((log p)^(5/3)(loglog p)^(1/3))`, while preserving KV fidelity and an order-one transient Robin ambiguity. A capacity threshold is not intrinsic while admissible controls can recondition the representation and move or shrink its resource bill.

The reusable accounting rule is therefore: before assigning an information or rigidity cost, fix the admissible source fibre, destination quotient, physical source map, acquisition operator class, noise/likelihood geometry, allowed renormalization, matched-control class and inverse discrimination/repair geometry. Then identify the resource that cannot be moved elsewhere by reparameterization, extremal-shell repair, spectral partitioning, amplitude normalization, broad aggregation or spatial relocation.

**Boundary.** The cited results concern different mathematical models. FD-179 does not settle full extremal-rank fidelity; MC-354 covers a specific additive log-smooth channel class; NB-189 uses a matched packed destination rather than the actual Nyman range; RE-188 gives a representation-specific linear width scale rather than a universal repair lower bound. The synthesis is an audit rule, not a theorem equating these currencies.
