# MI-023 — Source information is priced by the declared source class, physical map and inverse discrimination geometry

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [FD-167](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-167-full-field-l2-inversion-has-uniform-square-root-prefix-conditioning.md) through [FD-187](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-187-prime-translated-divisor-cubes-remove-the-rank-penalty-from-physical-prime-rigidity.md), [MC-342](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-342-forward-source-regularity-does-not-price-analog-capacity.md) through [MC-365](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-365-coordinatewise-bsc-endpoint-tariff-sharpness.md), [NB-176](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-176-euler-weighted-fourier-fingerprints-certify-source-complexity.md) through [NB-199](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-199-diagonal-brun-titchmarsh-localizes-vanishing-positive-euler-returns-to-the-logarithmic-mellin-endpoint.md), and [RE-184](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-184-growing-euler-jet-depth-below-kv-shell-scale-remains-nonrigid.md) through [RE-198](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-198-linear-window-repair-collapses-the-full-real-euler-ray-through-every-sublinear-depth.md). These lines use different mathematical objects; no universal identification of their currencies is claimed.

Across them, raw dimension, exact injectivity and forward smoothness repeatedly fail as complexity currencies. The useful quantity is the resource that the **declared source class and physical observation map force the destination to acquire**, measured in the inverse geometry native to that architecture.

Farey Discrepancy makes source-fibre dependence explicit. On the physical-prime fibre, the translated divisor cube converts one composite defect into an essentially full local relation-capacity tariff, yet the dyadic midpoint ladder can remain blind to that tariff. The unresolved resource is therefore an observation theorem exposing relation capacity, not merely the existence of a coercive relation.

Möbius Cancellation shows the analogous dependence on statistical architecture. TV is the finite currency for common-seed change probability; Hellinger is the bounded compositional currency for latent/product channels; negative log-affinity is the additive coordinate native to product depth. MC-363--MC-364 force near-endpoint Hellinger separation and logarithmically diverging Bhattacharyya depth. MC-365 then supplies the decisive calibration: a coordinatewise binary-symmetric channel simultaneously attains the information floor, sharp JS/Hellinger conversion and log-affinity tariff at the forced scale. The destination lower bound is therefore **not itself an obstruction**. A Möbius contradiction now requires a source-side acquisition theorem proving that the actual arithmetic transcript cannot realize the BSC-like equality family or cannot afford its source-direction discrimination budget.

Nyman--Beurling separates representation, recurrence volume and physical source occupancy. Exact recurrence exists at fixed source width, but shrinking bands create enormous prime-log phase families. Generic recurrence volume becomes exponentially small in effective dimension; source-specific dephasing and positive occupancy theorems then convert the true von-Mangoldt source into pointwise exclusion. NB-198 reaches every fixed Mellin exponent below one for sufficiently accurate returns, while NB-199 makes the tolerance dependence explicit and pushes vanishing positive returns into the final logarithmic endpoint layer `t~x_a/log x_a`. The surviving positive endpoint is therefore controlled by how the actual source populates favorable phase windows at the requested accuracy, not by abstract torus accessibility alone.

Robin Extremal gives the stable-inverse counterpart. Exact scalar Euler data identify the source at infinite precision, but matched ordinary-prime controls can preserve a macroscopic Robin transient while making scalar data nearly blind. RE-197 suppresses the complete positive real Euler ray superlogarithmically at growing depth inside the fixed-window regime, and RE-198 extends that collapse through **every sublinear jet depth `K=o(log p)`** by allowing a linearly growing repair window. The first unresolved continuum transition begins only when depth becomes comparable with `log p`. The relevant resource must therefore exclude the matched near-kernel directions or control a joint/nonlocal observable they cannot annihilate.

These examples refine the same accounting rule. A source tariff is useful only when four pieces line up: the admissible source fibre, the physical acquisition map, the destination-relevant quotient, and an inverse modulus in the coordinate in which the architecture actually composes. A fifth check is now explicit from MC-365: **the forced tariff must also exceed what an admissible source realization can actually supply**. A sharp lower bound that is saturated by a matched source channel is a calibration theorem, not an impossibility theorem.

Before importing a generic complexity measure, identify the exact finite/analytic network through which source information must travel and compute its native extremal quantity: full relation capacity rather than one route; log-affinity rather than a linear union bound; occupancy rather than continuum approximation when positivity permits it; first omitted transport moment rather than spectral span. Then produce the opposite-direction acquisition theorem in the same currency and source class.

**Boundary.** The currencies above are not interchangeable. Farey relation capacity does not supply an observation theorem. MC-365 does not realize a BSC as a Möbius transcript or bound `M(x)`. Nyman occupancy is positivity-specific and does not by itself prove a Nyman-distance lower bound. Robin matched controls are restricted to their declared ordinary-prime repair class and depth regime. The synthesis is an audit principle about where quantitative source information resides and when a destination tariff becomes a genuine contradiction, not a theorem equating these mechanisms.
