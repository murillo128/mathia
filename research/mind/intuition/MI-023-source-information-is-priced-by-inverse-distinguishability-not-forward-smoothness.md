# MI-023 — Source information is priced by the declared source class, physical map and inverse discrimination geometry

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [FD-167](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-167-full-field-l2-inversion-has-uniform-square-root-prefix-conditioning.md) through [FD-181](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-181-sparse-signed-perturbations-cannot-hide-from-prime-extension-multiplicativity.md), [MC-342](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-342-forward-source-regularity-does-not-price-analog-capacity.md) through [MC-356](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-356-regular-exponential-family-fisher-edge-tariff.md), [NB-176](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-176-euler-weighted-fourier-fingerprints-certify-source-complexity.md) through [NB-191](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-191-bohr-recurrence-removes-a-universal-high-block-locality-surcharge.md), and [RE-184](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-184-growing-euler-jet-depth-below-kv-shell-scale-remains-nonrigid.md) through [RE-190](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-190-shrinking-euler-sampling-windows-collapse-to-reciprocal-mass-rank-one.md). No universal acquisition theorem or identification of these currencies is claimed.

Across these lines, raw coordinate count is repeatedly the wrong currency. What matters is the declared source class, the quotient or destination that must be distinguished, the physical source-coordinate map, the observation/acquisition topology, and the inverse discrimination or repair cost after the representation has been conditioned as well as the source allows.

Farey Discrepancy now prices a relational boundary beyond the original deletion fibre. Let `delta=a-mu` be squarefree-supported and suppose its changed-coordinate support satisfies `S(x)=o(x/log x)`, with arbitrary signed amplitudes and with prime coordinates themselves allowed to move. FD-181 proves

`liminf_(x->infinity) (log x/x) M_(a,q)(x) >= sum_(n in S) |delta(n)|^q/n`

for the true prime-extension multiplicativity energy. Since the extension graph has `~x loglog x/zeta(2)` edges, a normalized defect `o((log x loglog x)^(-1/q))` forces `a=mu`. The mechanism is a clean prime fan: one erroneous parent exposes its defect on `asymp x/(n log x)` edges, and a support budget below one prime-fan scale cannot screen it by compensating edits. The unresolved boundary begins near support `x/log x` or when support counting is replaced by dense tiny-amplitude perturbations.

Möbius Cancellation identifies an analogous principle in likelihood geometry without identifying the two currencies. MC-354 prices common additive log-smooth readouts; MC-355 adds source-dependent Gaussian covariance; MC-356 shows that for any finite-dimensional regular exponential-family row with common support, half-Jeffreys divergence is exactly one half of the integrated Fisher action along the natural-parameter edge before post-processing. Mean, variance and higher sufficient-statistic modulation can change where the source information sits, but they remain charged by the intrinsic reverse-discrimination geometry. Exact dephasing cannot stay in the regular interior because the required neighboring transcript laws are singular while regular interior rows have finite KL.

Nyman--Beurling separates representation cost, acquisition variation and locality recurrence. NB-190 shows that a scalar Euler-translate mixture approximating a probe supported on source mass `Theta` needs total variation `Omega(1/Theta)`, sharply so at fixed accuracy. For `K` balanced bands this is linear `K` variation per coordinate. NB-191 then shows that imposing high-block support does not add another universal factor at fixed `a`: finite active Euler frequencies have syndetic common Bohr returns, so sharp synthesizers can be transplanted into every sufficiently high dyadic block with the same asymptotic variation. Any genuine locality tariff must therefore come from the growth of the recurrence inclusion length in the coupled `a_T->0`, `T->infinity` regime or from a separate restriction of the actual Nyman operator class.

Robin Extremal shows two ways an apparently richer scalar interface can stop buying source information. RE-189 proves that sufficiently high factorial-normalized boundary jets converge toward the common net-multiplicity mode on a fixed selector shell. RE-190 proves an independent dual-window collapse: after the trivial first-harmonic normalization, the entire exact Euler profile in any `o(1)` neighborhood of `s=1` becomes operator-norm rank one and sees only reciprocal mass at the natural weighted source scale. Sampling that shrinking window arbitrarily densely does not increase effective rank. Robust shell-location information requires either a wider dual spectral window, a larger logarithmic source diameter, or paying the inverse conditioning needed to recover the vanishing residual modes.

The reusable accounting rule is therefore: before assigning an information or rigidity cost, fix the admissible source fibre, destination quotient, physical source map, dual observation window, relation graph, acquisition operator class, noise/likelihood geometry, allowed renormalization, localization/recurrence scale, matched-control class and inverse discrimination/repair geometry. Then identify the resource that cannot be moved into a different channel, diluted over a relation graph, purchased by a matched control, neutralized by recurrence, or collapsed into a common mode by a badly scaled observation window.

**Boundary.** The cited results concern different mathematical models. FD-181 is a sparse-support graph coercivity theorem; MC-356 concerns finite-dimensional regular exponential-family channels with source-independent support; NB-190--NB-191 price unrestricted Fourier--Stieltjes scalar mixing and fixed-`a` recurrence rather than the actual Nyman operator budget or coupled recurrence scale; RE-189--RE-190 concern scalar Euler observables on a fixed multiplicative selector shell and do not rule out constant-distance or multiscale spectral probes. The synthesis is an audit rule about pricing the actual source motion, not a theorem equating prime-fan boundary energy, Fisher action, variation cost, recurrence length or scalar-window conditioning.
