# MI-023 — Source information is priced by the declared source class, physical map and inverse discrimination geometry

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [FD-195](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-195-franel-duality-has-a-nonvanishing-cost-on-geometric-quotient-witnesses.md), [FD-196](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-196-weighted-fourier-duality-has-a-sharp-geometric-recovery-line.md), [FD-197](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-197-rademacher-multiplicative-second-moments-saturate-weighted-fourier-deficit.md), [MC-377](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-377-smooth-subspace-prime-frame-forces-superpolynomial-source-cost.md), [NB-212](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-212-smooth-euler-barycenter-saturates-the-log-squared-vinogradov-korobov-corridor.md), and [RE-212](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-212-kaiser-bessel-powers-reach-every-subunit-separated-vertical-coercive-exponent.md). These lines use different mathematical objects; no universal identification of their currencies is claimed.

Raw dimension, exact injectivity and forward smoothness repeatedly fail as complexity currencies. The useful quantity is the resource that the **declared source class and physical observation map force the destination to acquire**, measured in the inverse geometry and norm native to that architecture.

Farey Discrepancy makes the destination-norm dependence explicit. Above the geometric square-root threshold the cumulative source has a stable raw inverse, but FD-195 shows that the same quotient witness has order-one dual cost after compression to the Franel/GCD quadratic energy. FD-196 quantifies the weighted family: a square-root band with energy exponent `gamma` gives the RH-facing Mertens exponent only when `gamma+sigma<=3/2`, while the classical Franel transfer remains on `gamma+sigma=2`. FD-197 shows that generic squarefree Rademacher multiplicativity already saturates this noncritical line. The useful tariff is therefore not Fourier count or generic random cancellation, but deterministic structure surviving the destination norm.

Möbius Cancellation strengthens the source-realization side. Earlier finite-power near-equality analyses priced primitive conductor and endpoint bias inside a hypothetical quartic-efficient package. MC-377 proves that an actual exact endpoint cannot have **any fixed polynomial common source radical**: `log P/log y -> infinity`. The source cost has moved out of the fixed-exponent hierarchy entirely. The old near-quadratic/bias picture remains conditional anatomy of a regime that exact endpoints cannot occupy; the current quantitative question is the rate of superpolynomial escape under moving smooth-character uniformity.

Nyman--Beurling shows that the representation used to expose a source tariff can itself be nonessential. NB-210--NB-211 obtain the log-squared Vinogradov--Korobov return-gap corridor through a prime-harmonic plateau and prime-supported Halász inequality. NB-212 shows that one fixed smooth **full-von-Mangoldt barycenter** already yields the same corridor. It then isolates the true method tariff in the contour-error prefactor: every positive polynomial `Q^A` forces the log-squared threshold, whereas a polylogarithmic prefactor would approach the Ford scale and a bounded prefactor would permit Ford-scale access in principle. The relevant currency is therefore the growth class of the source-faithful contour loss, not harmonic count or prime-only support by itself.

Robin Extremal gives a remote-support continuum tariff. Finite vertical frames can be aliased, but hiding a selector-scale packet from a whole interval across a fixed logarithmic repair gap forces an exponentially large signed prime-prefix. RE-212 uses positive Kaiser--Bessel convolution powers to obtain every coercive exponent below one. For the current `H=log 8` architecture, fixed KV fidelity excludes `T=c log Q` for every `c>0.2404491734...`, while explicit hiding remains constructed below `0.0289079...`. The physical currency is signed-prefix/Chebyshev discrepancy; sample count and local rank are not substitutes.

The reusable accounting rule is therefore: specify the admissible source fibre and topology, physical observation family, destination quotient and norm, requested precision, source support domain and moving parameter regime, then compute the **inverse** resource needed to distinguish the target in that same currency. Finally test whether the physical source can pay it. A contraction visible before destination compression may disappear afterward; a finite conductor floor can be superseded by a superpolynomial common-source obstruction; a prime-only reduction may be unnecessary once the full source exposes the same contour tariff; and continuum coverage becomes useful only when its signed-prefix cost exceeds the source budget.

**Boundary.** The currencies are not interchangeable. Farey weighted energy, Möbius common-radical size, Nyman contour-prefactor growth and Robin signed-prefix discrepancy live in different source/destination architectures. The synthesis is an audit principle about where quantitative information is actually priced, not a theorem transferring one line's numerical threshold to another.
