# MI-023 — Source information is priced by the declared source class, acquisition topology and destination-relative discrimination geometry

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [FD-167](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-167-full-field-l2-inversion-has-uniform-square-root-prefix-conditioning.md) through [FD-176](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-176-geometric-midpoint-null-sources-survive-growing-rank-multiplicative-fidelity.md), [MC-342](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-342-forward-source-regularity-does-not-price-analog-capacity.md) through [MC-351](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-351-transcript-information-forces-edge-kl-budget.md), [NB-176](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-176-euler-weighted-fourier-fingerprints-certify-source-complexity.md) through [NB-186](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-186-pairwise-phase-decorrelation-recovers-growing-raw-rank-at-the-cubic-width-scale.md), and [RE-184](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-184-growing-euler-jet-depth-below-kv-shell-scale-remains-nonrigid.md) through [RE-185](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-185-chebyshev-shell-interpolation-removes-the-growing-jet-log-loss.md). No universal acquisition theorem is claimed.

The common question across these lines is not how many coordinates a representation exposes. It is what **source class, target quotient and acquisition topology** are declared, which source fibres the destination must distinguish, how the observation operator varies, which amplitudes and conditioning are part of the source channel, and what inverse/capacity/reliability scale makes those distinctions usable.

Farey Discrepancy shows why ambient dimension is source-class relative. Complete-field and consecutive-time observations are exactly invertible over unrestricted formal sources, while global squarefree multiplicativity makes extremely sparse geometric midpoint samples target-rigid. FD-175--FD-176 show that this is not explained by coefficient fidelity: even prime-perfect deletion sources preserving a growing cone `omega(n)<=rho log n/loglog n` can remain exact geometric-midpoint null sources when the global product law is removed. The load-bearing resource is cross-coordinate compatibility that prevents fresh high-rank shells from repairing each new observation row.

Möbius Cancellation gives a complementary finite-source accounting. A scalar transcript may encode `Theta(R)` bits behind exponentially small separations, so benign forward regularity does not price information. Once a readout model is declared, capacity and edge discrimination become invariant currencies. MC-348--MC-349 charge symmetrized transcript KL on the source graph and then only on edges crossing the destination quotient. MC-350 forces an extensive exact channel-law boundary from the dephasing information floor. MC-351 closes the remaining lower-bound loophole: mutual information itself forces an `Omega(R)` aggregate source-edge KL budget on that exact cut, without a raw-query model or decoder. The open question is no longer whether separation must be paid, but whether the actual analytic architecture can afford that KL budget.

Nyman--Beurling shows that apparent source rank must retain amplitudes and acquisition charge. Genuine Euler labels are nonselective if weak channels can be normalized independently, while the physical scalar Euler readout lives naturally in an absolute Wiener/Dirichlet topology rather than the Hilbert dual. NB-184 makes that charged scalar acquisition cheap on density-one heights after quotienting the invisible constant mode. NB-185 shows fixed prime-phase rank survives that filter, and NB-186 upgrades this to growing raw nuclear rank `N~1/a` at the cubic simultaneous-selection scale using only pairwise phase decorrelation. Yet the raw matrix still ignores the true Euler weights `log p/p^(1+a)`. Growing phase coherence is therefore not the same as growing source-faithful rank.

Robin Extremal supplies a different warning about lower-bound tariffs. RE-184 made an unbounded family of Euler jets nonselective throughout `K log K=o(V(p))`; RE-185 removes the apparent `log K` threshold by changing from badly conditioned monomials to a normalized Chebyshev shell basis and pushes exact matched controls to `K=Theta(V(p))`. A resource bound is not selective merely because it grows: one must also show that the admissible source class cannot realize the tariff after the representation is conditioned appropriately.

These examples isolate one reusable accounting rule. Before assigning an information cost, state the admissible source class, whether the task is arbitrary recovery, one-target testing or quotient recovery, the acquisition/dual family and its topology, every changing operator or horizon, source amplitudes and allowed renormalization, observation norm/noise model, downstream error tolerance, destination cut and matched-control class. Then prove a resource that survives those declarations: an inverse modulus, orbit-hitting/certification scale, source-class-relative kernel bound, stable singular values, channel capacity, source-edge transcript divergence, or charged dual response that the admissible controls cannot reproduce.

Forward smoothness, raw feature count, nominal dimension, positive noise, adaptivity, frequency membership, finite or growing scalar depth and canonical probe labels are secondary descriptors. Sparse observations can be enough when source constraints repeatedly expose every target defect; arbitrary joint coding still pays if the destination needs reliable neighboring-state discrimination; raw growing phase rank can remain nonselective when amplitudes are omitted; and an apparently growing interpolation cost can disappear after choosing the natural conditioned coordinates.

**Boundary.** The cited results concern different Farey, finite-source, Nyman and Robin models and do not supply a universal acquisition theorem or canonical source norm. FD-176 is one-target non-rigidity in a weakened multiplicative class, not pairwise inversion. MC-351 prices the transcript channel-law quotient but gives no analytic upper bound for a concrete Möbius architecture. NB-186 concerns raw phases, not amplitude-balanced Euler acquisition. RE-185 proves non-rigidity only through a constant fraction of the KV shell scale. The synthesis is an audit rule: information budgets become mathematical only after source fibre, destination quotient, acquisition geometry and matched realizability are fixed rather than optimized away.
