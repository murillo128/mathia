# MI-023 — Source information is priced by the declared source class, physical map and inverse discrimination geometry

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [FD-167](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-167-full-field-l2-inversion-has-uniform-square-root-prefix-conditioning.md) through [FD-182](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-182-exact-dyadic-midpoint-null-data-do-not-control-prime-extension-multiplicativity.md), [MC-342](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-342-forward-source-regularity-does-not-price-analog-capacity.md) through [MC-357](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-357-fixed-component-mixture-edge-tariff.md), [NB-176](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-176-euler-weighted-fourier-fingerprints-certify-source-complexity.md) through [NB-192](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-192-prime-log-entropy-forces-double-exponential-bohr-inclusion-length.md), and [RE-184](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-184-growing-euler-jet-depth-below-kv-shell-scale-remains-nonrigid.md) through [RE-191](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-191-fixed-euler-temperature-windows-are-factorially-compressible.md). No universal acquisition theorem or identification of these currencies is claimed.

Across these lines, raw coordinate count is repeatedly the wrong currency. What matters is the declared source class, the quotient or destination that must be distinguished, the physical source-coordinate map, the observation/acquisition topology, and the inverse discrimination or repair cost after the representation has been conditioned as well as the source allows.

Farey Discrepancy now separates **relation coercivity from observability of that relation**. FD-181 proves that any squarefree-supported perturbation of Möbius with changed-coordinate support `o(x/log x)` pays prime-extension multiplicativity energy at scale `x/log x`, with weighted reciprocal perturbation mass as the coefficient. FD-182 composes that theorem with the permanent FD-179 dyadic-null source: one fixed non-Möbius source matches the entire dyadic midpoint ladder exactly while retaining prime-extension defect `(C+o(1))x/log x`. Thus the relation itself is expensive, but the dyadic midpoint measurements do not see enough of it to force the defect below the critical scale. A source tariff is useful only when the declared observation family controls the tariff-producing relation.

Möbius Cancellation identifies an analogous principle in likelihood geometry. MC-354 prices common additive log-smooth readouts; MC-355 adds source-dependent Gaussian covariance; MC-356 shows that for any finite-dimensional regular exponential-family row with common support, half-Jeffreys divergence is one half of the integrated Fisher action along the natural-parameter edge. MC-357 then closes the fixed-component mixture escape: if source dependence enters only through a latent mixing law followed by a source-independent component kernel, output half-Jeffreys is bounded by latent half-Jeffreys, and exact output singularity forces singular latent laws. Complicated mixture components can relocate source information into the selector, but cannot make it free.

Nyman--Beurling separates representation cost, acquisition variation and locality recurrence. NB-190 shows that a scalar Euler-translate mixture approximating a probe supported on source mass `Theta` needs total variation `Omega(1/Theta)`. NB-191 shows that high-block support adds no universal surcharge at fixed source width because finite active frequencies recur syndetically. NB-192 then prices the nonuniformity hidden in that statement: one fixed log-width Euler band already contains `m_a~a exp(r_0/a)` rationally independent prime-log phases, forcing any fixed-accuracy **syndetic** common-return inclusion length to satisfy `log L_a >> a exp(r_0/a)`. Guaranteed recurrent transplantation into a length-`T` block therefore requires a log-log-scale width floor. Locality is free at fixed resolution but violently expensive in the coupled shrinking-width limit for this coordinatewise recurrence mechanism.

Robin Extremal gives a continuous stable-rank version of the same warning. RE-189 shows that high factorial-normalized boundary jets converge toward a common net-multiplicity mode. RE-190 makes an entire `o(1)` spectral neighborhood asymptotically rank one on a fixed selector shell. RE-191 closes the immediate constant-distance escape: on every fixed interval `s in [1,1+U]`, the exact Euler channel reduces to a compact Laplace kernel whose rank-`m` approximation error is factorial in `m`, up to an `O(p^-1)` source floor. Exact continuum data remain analytically rigid, but fixed-precision stable source rank stays bounded independently of shell size.

The reusable accounting rule is therefore: before assigning an information or rigidity cost, fix the admissible source fibre, destination quotient, physical source map, dual observation window, relation graph, acquisition operator class, noise/likelihood geometry, allowed renormalization, localization/recurrence scale, matched-control class and inverse discrimination/repair geometry. Then identify both the resource that cannot be made free **and the observation theorem that actually exposes it**. A tariff hidden in an observation nullspace, a locality argument whose inclusion length explodes in the coupled limit, a latent selector carrying all source dependence, or a fixed window whose stable rank collapses does not become destination leverage merely because an exact source relation exists.

**Boundary.** The cited results concern different mathematical models. FD-181--FD-182 combine sparse-support graph coercivity with a specific dyadic observation kernel; they do not rule out richer Farey spatial observations. MC-356--MC-357 price regular exponential-family and fixed-component latent channels but not arbitrary source-dependent components. NB-192 bounds the worst-gap/syndetic common-return mechanism, not the optimal high-block synthesizer or the chance of an unusually early return in one specific block. RE-189--RE-191 concern scalar Euler observables on a fixed multiplicative selector shell and do not rule out growing spectral windows or multiscale source support. The synthesis is an audit rule about pricing and observing the actual source motion, not a theorem equating prime-fan energy, Fisher/latent divergence, recurrence entropy or scalar-window conditioning.
