# MI-023 — Source information is priced by the declared source class, physical map and inverse discrimination geometry

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [FD-167](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-167-full-field-l2-inversion-has-uniform-square-root-prefix-conditioning.md) through [FD-186](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-186-prime-detour-cycles-make-physical-prime-subcritical-energy-rigid.md), [MC-342](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-342-forward-source-regularity-does-not-price-analog-capacity.md) through [MC-362](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-362-tv-to-hellinger-near-endpoint-transfer.md), [NB-176](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-176-euler-weighted-fourier-fingerprints-certify-source-complexity.md) through [NB-196](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-196-short-interval-prime-density-forbids-positive-euler-returns-through-the-13-30-mellin-corridor.md), and [RE-184](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-184-growing-euler-jet-depth-below-kv-shell-scale-remains-nonrigid.md) through [RE-195](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-195-chebyshev-balanced-padding-makes-reciprocal-variation-noncoercive.md). No universal acquisition theorem or identification of these currencies is claimed.

Across these lines, raw coordinate count is repeatedly the wrong currency. What matters is the declared source class, the quotient or destination that must be distinguished, the physical source-coordinate map, the observation/acquisition topology, the tolerated precision and the inverse discrimination or repair cost after the representation has been conditioned as well as the source allows.

Farey Discrepancy makes the **source-fibre dependence of a relation tariff** explicit. FD-181 proves that any squarefree-supported perturbation of Möbius with changed-coordinate support `o(x/log x)` pays prime-extension multiplicativity energy at scale `x/log x`, even when prime coordinates themselves may move. FD-182 composes that coercive theorem with the permanent FD-179 dyadic-null source: one fixed non-Möbius source matches the entire dyadic midpoint ladder exactly while retaining prime-extension defect `(C+o(1))x/log x`. Thus the relation itself is expensive, but those observations do not expose it below the critical scale.

FD-183--FD-184 showed that fixing the prime coordinates turns one changed composite into coercivity through every fixed almost-prime rank. FD-186 identifies the stronger finite mechanism and removes the apparent growing-rank boundary. On the physical-prime fibre `a(p)=-1`, normalize `c=a/mu`. Every prime is an anchor `c(p)=1`; if a squarefree `n_0` has defect `Delta>0`, then each fresh prime `r` creates an edge-disjoint finite detour between the anchored value at `r` and the changed value at `n_0`. Hölder on that detour gives

`liminf_(x->infinity) (log x/x) M_(a,q)(x) >= Delta^q/(n_0(omega(n_0)+1)^(q-1)) > 0`.

Hence `M_(a,q)(x)=o(x/log x)` already forces `a=mu` on the entire physical-prime squarefree fibre, without any support or growing-rank hypothesis. FD-185 identifies the complementary escape: moving a prime coordinate can keep relation energy zero, but it forces positive-density changed support. Therefore `S(x)=o(x)` together with `M_(a,q)(x)=o(x/log x)` forces `a=mu` globally in the declared squarefree class. The unresolved source currency is no longer multiplicative depth; it is the **observation theorem that exposes relation energy**, plus the appropriate weighted sparsity notion when moved-prime or dense perturbation amplitudes can shrink.

Möbius Cancellation identifies analogous representation dependence in likelihood geometry. MC-354--MC-356 price regular smooth channels through mean/covariance motion and integrated Fisher action. MC-357--MC-358 show that source discrimination can move between a latent selector and source-dependent components inside an actual common lifted architecture but cannot disappear: `b_e(Y) <= b_e(Theta)+c_e(K)`.

MC-359 adds a bounded source-edge currency that remains useful when KL/Jeffreys becomes infinite. If `t_e=TV(P_x,P_x')` and `T_1=(2/m)sum_e t_e`, transcript information is bounded above by `(log 2)T_1` up to the punctured-cube correction, so bounded-energy nontrivial dephasing forces `T_1/R` away from zero. Under genuine shared randomness `Y=F(x,U_0)`, each edge TV is bounded by the probability that a one-source flip changes the transcript under the common seed.

MC-360 adds a second bounded currency with a different architectural advantage. Squared Hellinger distance remains finite at singularity, while affinity factorizes for conditionally independent products. For a genuine source-dependent latent law followed by product components, the output edge cost is bounded by latent-law Hellinger distance plus an affinity-weighted sum of conditional component Hellinger costs. With source-independent latent randomness, the endpoint information floor becomes an extensive weighted source-coordinate incidence tariff.

MC-361 sharpens the destination-side information floor, and MC-362 transfers that endpoint sharpness back into the compositional Hellinger currency. If `q_R=(m-1)/m`, the aggregate budgets obey

`T_1/(R q_R) <= sqrt((H_1/(R q_R))(2-H_1/(R q_R)))`.

Thus a normalized TV lower bound `s` forces `H_1/R >= q_R(1-sqrt(1-s^2))`. Combining the MC-361 near-full entropy requirement with the MC-359 endpoint-sharp TV tariff makes `H_1/R` tend to one as unit-energy dephasing becomes exact. The permanent `log 2` slack of the direct `JS<=H^2` route was a conversion artifact, not a product-architecture escape. Source information may therefore be priced by smooth divergence, bounded common-seed distinguishability, or bounded product/latent affinity depending on the architecture, but near the deterministic endpoint the Hellinger/product tariff itself becomes almost maximal.

Nyman--Beurling separates representation cost, acquisition variation, recurrence entropy and source-specific dephasing. NB-190 prices normalized scalar Euler-band access by inverse source mass. NB-191 shows that fixed-width high-block transplantation itself adds no universal asymptotic surcharge. NB-192--NB-193 show why this changes when the source width shrinks: prime-log effective dimension makes positive recurrence double-exponentially nonuniform even after replacing coordinatewise phase return by the natural true-source-weighted defect.

NB-194 first excludes positive returns pointwise on the actual von-Mangoldt shell through the Vinogradov--Korobov corridor. NB-195 extends the obstruction in measure far beyond it: under the mild condition `a_TT->infinity`, positive returns occupy vanishing relative measure on the physical block. NB-196 then restores pointwise exclusion through a much wider Mellin corridor by using current uniform short-interval prime density. With `x_a=exp(r_0/a)`, the true shell is uniformly approximated by its continuum response for `|t|<=x_a^(13/30-epsilon)`, so `[T,2T]` is eventually return-free whenever `limsup a_T log(2T)/r_0 < 13/30`. The `13/30` is the complement of the present `17/30` short-interval exponent, hence a theorem-uniformity boundary rather than an intrinsic Nyman constant. Beyond it the remaining positive route is an isolated-large-value problem, not ordinary recurrence.

Robin Extremal gives a continuous stable-rank version of the same warning. RE-189--RE-193 show that high normalized Euler jets and finite-Laplace rectangles can have very small robust rank even when exact analytic data remain source-rigid. For `R=U log(Q_+/Q_-)`, the exact scalar Euler channel admits, above the prime-power floor, a rank

`O((1+log(1+R))(1+log(1/epsilon)))`

approximation at tolerance `epsilon`. RE-194 identifies the selector-scale relative tolerance `epsilon_Rob~min(1,d_p/V)`, where `d_p~1/(sqrt(p)log p)` and `V=sum|c_q|/q`, so the corresponding worst-case capacity bound contains `log_+(V/d_p)`.

RE-195 shows why even that source norm is not automatically an inverse complexity resource. For every fixed Euler depth and any prescribed finite reciprocal variation, an ordinary-prime `{0,1,2}` matched control can add a remote Chebyshev-balanced signed packet with arbitrarily large `V` while its signed primitive stays logarithmic and its entire fixed Euler response is `O(p^-2)`. Exact jet matching, KV fidelity and the transient Robin excursion survive on a disjoint repair channel. Thus `V` controls a worst-case Lipschitz upper bound but does **not** lower-bound scalar information capacity. A useful source-complexity currency must quotient balanced near-kernel directions or otherwise be tied to actual Euler response.

The reusable accounting rule is therefore: before assigning an information or rigidity cost, fix the admissible source fibre, destination quotient, physical source map, dual observation window, relation graph, acquisition operator class, noise/likelihood geometry, allowed renormalization, localization/recurrence scale, matched-control class, requested precision and inverse discrimination/repair geometry. Then identify both the resource that cannot be made free **and the observation theorem that actually exposes it**.

A tariff hidden in an observation nullspace, altered by changing the admissible fibre, relocated inside an unobserved latent/component channel, split among product components without an aggregate ledger, charged through a recurrence target whose volume collapses, contradicted by source dephasing on the physical block, expressed in a metric that breaks at the endpoint, or encoded in an unsigned source norm with large balanced near-kernel directions does not become destination leverage merely because the upstream source relation is exact.

**Boundary.** The cited results concern different mathematical models. FD-181--FD-186 do not rule out richer Farey observations, determine a sharp critical-energy constant, or provide a weighted replacement for support sparsity on every source fibre. MC-362 supplies no arithmetic upper bound for an actual Möbius channel, and TV, Hellinger and Jeffreys are not interchangeable currencies. NB-192--NB-196 price positive recurrence/dephasing through current source-resolution technology, not every possible high-block synthesizer, signed aggregate or post-`13/30` isolated large value. RE-193--RE-195 are scalar capacity/noncoercivity results, not a matching lower bound or a Robin criterion. The synthesis is an audit rule about pricing and observing the actual source motion, not a theorem equating prime-extension energy, source-edge divergence, recurrence entropy, dephasing or finite-Laplace conditioning.
