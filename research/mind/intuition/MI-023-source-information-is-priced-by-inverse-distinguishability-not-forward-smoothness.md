# MI-023 — Source information is priced by the declared source class, physical map and inverse discrimination geometry

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [FD-167](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-167-full-field-l2-inversion-has-uniform-square-root-prefix-conditioning.md) through [FD-195](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-195-franel-duality-has-a-nonvanishing-cost-on-geometric-quotient-witnesses.md), [MC-342](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-342-forward-source-regularity-does-not-price-analog-capacity.md) through [MC-376](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-376-quadratic-boundary-carries-macroscopic-inverse-root-rank-bias.md), [NB-176](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-176-euler-weighted-fourier-fingerprints-certify-source-complexity.md) through [NB-211](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-211-vinogradov-korobov-prime-halasz-reaches-a-log-squared-diagonal-corridor.md), and [RE-184](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-184-growing-euler-jet-depth-below-kv-shell-scale-remains-nonrigid.md) through [RE-210](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-210-exact-sinc-tail-geometry-sharpens-the-separated-vertical-kv-ceiling.md). These lines use different mathematical objects; no universal identification of their currencies is claimed.

Across them, raw dimension, exact injectivity and forward smoothness repeatedly fail as complexity currencies. The useful quantity is the resource that the **declared source class and physical observation map force the destination to acquire**, measured in the inverse geometry native to that architecture. The current evidence sharpens that principle in several directions: scale coverage, destination norm, value precision, primitive conductor with amplitude, source-matched off-diagonal control, atomic capacity and remote-support prefix cost.

Farey Discrepancy has both source-side rigidity and a source-class-sensitive observation threshold. FD-187--FD-188 show that subcritical coefficient/relation error forces the Möbius source once the prime-label gauge and composite consistency are priced correctly. FD-189--FD-194 identify the geometric square-root coverage threshold and an explicit stable raw inverse. FD-195 then shows that this favorable inverse geometry is not preserved automatically by scalar compression: the same geometric quotient witness whose raw inverse row contracts has order-one dual cost in the Franel/GCD quadratic energy. The source tariff must therefore be quoted in the destination norm that will actually consume it.

Möbius Cancellation exposes two further hidden resources: **precision of a scalar side channel and amplitude at primitive conductor**. MC-366--MC-369 show that an exact-real endpoint scalar can encode the full sign vector through subset sums, while specified finite-resolution Hamming quotienting restores a small leakage tariff. MC-371--MC-375 then force quartic-efficient endpoint modes toward near-quadratic primitive conductors on almost the whole Fourier cube. MC-376 adds that this expensive population is not amplitude-negligible: a positive fraction of all modes simultaneously has minimum primitive conductor `y^(2+o(1))` and endpoint bias at least `1/sqrt(2R)`. A useful conductor lower bound must therefore be paired with the moving bias scale carried by the modes it prices.

Nyman--Beurling shows that the theorem used to price the source must itself be matched to the source geometry and coupled limit. NB-208 forces a phase-pinned prime-harmonic plateau. Generic large-value theory pays an unnecessary ambient-support logarithm, while NB-210 removes it with a prime-supported Halasz inequality. NB-211 then removes the fixed-epsilon presentation loss by retaining the full Vinogradov--Korobov contour, pushing the order-one gap to `X_a^(3/2)/(log X_a)^2`. The remaining tariff is the actual prime-correlation off-diagonal at the moving height, not raw prime sparsity or a fixed proof parameter.

Nyman's earlier atomic-capacity results make requested precision part of the same accounting. As favorable phase windows shrink below lattice spacing, the source currency changes from interval occupancy to one-atom-per-cell capacity, and the distance below the atomic edge gives a quadratic stability modulus. Exact threshold location without a modulus loses the quantitative information needed by the destination.

Robin Extremal gives the complementary remote-support warning and then converts it into a continuum cost. Finite vertical frames can be well conditioned on one shell while admitting remote aliases. For a whole interval `|t|<=T` separated from the repair source by log-gap `H`, RE-209--RE-210 show that hiding a selector-scale packet forces an exponentially large signed weighted prefix. With the exact sinc sidelobe rate `gamma_*=0.5979019108...`, fixed KV fidelity imposes `c<1/(2 gamma_* H)` on `T=c log Q`. Thus local linear rank and global source coercivity are different currencies: continuum Fourier coverage is paid in signed-prefix/Chebyshev discrepancy, not merely sample count.

These examples refine one accounting rule. Before assigning a complexity or information tariff, specify the admissible source fibre, physical observation family, destination-relevant quotient and norm, value precision/noise model, source support domain, requested accuracy and inverse modulus. Then compute the native obstruction: scale coverage plus cross-coordinate compatibility rather than raw Fourier count; subset-sum conditioning and resolution quotient for weighted scalars; primitive conductor together with the bias amplitude of the affected population; source-matched prime-correlation error in a moving diagonal; atomic carrying capacity according to source granularity; and signed-prefix growth under remote continuum hiding.

The opposite-direction test remains essential. A destination lower bound is useful only if the physical source mechanism cannot pay it in the same currency. Exact-real coding can make a nominally tiny scalar reveal the whole source, remote support can create a global near-kernel for a locally high-rank frame, destination compression can erase a useful inverse contraction, a high conductor lower bound matters more when a macroscopic set of modes still carries inverse-root-rank amplitude, and continuum coverage becomes coercive only after the corresponding prefix cost exceeds what the source class can supply. The declared architecture determines which resource is real.

**Boundary.** The currencies above are not interchangeable. Farey square-root bandwidth and Franel dual cost concern different destination norms; MC-376 concerns the endpoint coefficient law in the quartic-saturated regime; NB-211 is a positive-source prime-correlation theorem, not a Nyman destination theorem; RE-210 assumes a fixed separated ordinary-prime repair architecture. The synthesis is an audit principle about where quantitative source information resides and how inverse geometry must be priced, not a theorem transferring any one mechanism between lines.
