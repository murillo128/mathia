# MI-023 — Source information is priced by the declared source class, physical map and inverse discrimination geometry

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [FD-167](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-167-full-field-l2-inversion-has-uniform-square-root-prefix-conditioning.md) through [FD-190](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-190-sub-square-root-dyadic-fourier-bands-remain-null-on-prime-faithful-squarefree-sources.md), [MC-342](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-342-forward-source-regularity-does-not-price-analog-capacity.md) through [MC-369](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-369-hamming-shell-quantization-stabilizes-near-balance.md), [NB-176](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-176-euler-weighted-fourier-fingerprints-certify-source-complexity.md) through [NB-201](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-201-atomic-endpoint-capacity-has-a-quadratic-stability-modulus.md), and [RE-184](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-184-growing-euler-jet-depth-below-kv-shell-scale-remains-nonrigid.md) through [RE-201](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-201-commensurate-vertical-euler-frames-admit-remote-shell-aliases-at-selector-debt-scale.md). These lines use different mathematical objects; no universal identification of their currencies is claimed.

Across them, raw dimension, exact injectivity and forward smoothness repeatedly fail as complexity currencies. The useful quantity is the resource that the **declared source class and physical observation map force the destination to acquire**, measured in the inverse geometry native to that architecture. The newest results sharpen that principle in four different directions: scale coverage, value precision, atomic capacity and support aliasing.

Farey Discrepancy now has both source-side rigidity and a source-class-sensitive observation threshold. FD-187--FD-188 show that, once the prime-label gauge and composite consistency are priced in the right `L^q` currencies, subcritical coefficient/relation error forces the Möbius source. FD-189 shows that along dyadic horizons the complete signed Fourier band `k<=H^alpha` recovers the full unrestricted formal-source tail exactly when `alpha>1/2`; at and below the square-root endpoint infinitely many coordinates remain unseen. FD-190 then tests whether coordinatewise Möbius fidelity repairs the subcritical kernel. It does not: for every `alpha<1/2`, the sampled quotient set has zero density on `[N,2N]`, leaving unsampled corridors with polynomially many composite squarefree coordinates. Two-coordinate perturbations inside one corridor can preserve every prime value, every nonsquarefree zero, every squarefree Möbius sign and bounded coefficient size while producing exactly the same retained Fourier history. The missing resource is therefore relational, not merely coordinatewise: beating the strict sub-square-root barrier requires multiplicativity/divisor compatibility/prime-extension consistency or a different observation family. The endpoint `alpha=1/2` remains separate.

Möbius Cancellation exposes a different hidden resource: **precision of a scalar side channel**. MC-366--MC-367 price posterior predictability when the acquisition mechanism is restricted to adaptive coordinate or parity queries. MC-368 shows that the actual endpoint scalar `T=sum_i d_iX_i` need not behave like a one-dimensional low-information transcript. At exact balance it leaks only order `1/R` own-phase predictability, but arbitrarily near-balanced integer weights can make every subset sum distinct, so exact-real observation of the same scalar recovers the whole source. MC-369 supplies the complementary finite-resolution law: if `d_i=1/R+epsilon_i`, then the worst displacement from the balanced Hamming level is exactly controlled by `||epsilon||_1`; under the sharp strict margin `||d-R^(-1)1||_1<1/R`, nearest-shell quantization collapses the transcript back to Hamming weight and restores the order-`1/R` leakage tariff. Thus coefficient closeness becomes informative only after the physical resolution/coarsening is specified.

Nyman--Beurling makes requested precision part of the acquisition currency. NB-198--NB-200 move from interval occupancy to one-atom-per-cell capacity as favorable phase windows shrink below the integer lattice spacing. NB-201 retains the finite capacity ratio and obtains a stability law: if the endpoint ratio lies a relative distance `d` below the certified atomic edge, the positive chord defect is at least order `d^2/X_a`, up to the PNT resolution floor. Thus the observation tolerance itself determines how tightly a putative return must approach the source-capacity boundary. Exact threshold location without a modulus would lose the quantitative information needed by the destination.

Robin Extremal gives the complementary support-domain warning. RE-200 proves that vertical complex Euler samples have linear stable dimension on one fixed prime shell; the positive real ray is not intrinsically low-dimensional. RE-201 then shows that this conditioning is only local in logarithmic support. A commensurate vertical grid aliases shell index modulo its log period, and ordinary remote primes with integer multiplicities can realize the alias at selector reciprocal scale while preserving every fixed KV source envelope. A well-conditioned observation operator on one support window therefore need not have a useful global inverse once the admissible source can move across distant shells.

These examples refine one accounting rule. Before assigning a complexity or information tariff, specify the admissible source fibre, physical observation family, destination-relevant quotient, value precision/noise model, source support domain, requested accuracy and inverse modulus. Then compute the native obstruction: scale coverage plus cross-coordinate compatibility rather than raw Fourier count; own-coordinate/span provenance for restricted sketches; subset-sum conditioning and resolution quotient for weighted scalars; interval occupancy or atomic carrying capacity according to source granularity; and alias distance/support displacement for periodic vertical frames.

The opposite-direction test remains essential. A destination lower bound is useful only if the physical source mechanism cannot pay it in the same currency. MC-368 and RE-201 are strong warnings: a nominally tiny observation may secretly encode the whole discrete source, while a nominally high-rank frame may still admit a global near-kernel. FD-190 gives the converse warning that strong coordinatewise source fidelity may still leave an exact observational kernel when the observation geometry misses whole corridors. MC-369 and NB-201 show how a specified resolution/capacity modulus can turn a qualitative boundary into a quantitative tariff.

**Boundary.** The currencies above are not interchangeable. Farey square-root bandwidth is a theorem for the dyadic signed observation family, and FD-190 still does not settle the endpoint `alpha=1/2` or the fully multiplicative source cone. MC-369 assumes a specified nearest-Hamming-shell quotient and does not prove that the actual analytic architecture has that finite resolution. Nyman's quadratic modulus is positivity- and shell-specific and remains one-sided. Robin's alias uses a commensurate vertical grid and a remote shell and does not rule out incommensurate designs. The synthesis is an audit principle about where quantitative source information resides and how inverse geometry must be priced, not a theorem transferring any one mechanism between lines.
