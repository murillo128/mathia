# MI-023 — Source information is priced by the declared source class, physical map and inverse discrimination geometry

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [FD-167](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-167-full-field-l2-inversion-has-uniform-square-root-prefix-conditioning.md) through [FD-188](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-188-subcritical-prime-extension-energy-forces-linear-lq-mass-for-moved-primes.md), [MC-342](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-342-forward-source-regularity-does-not-price-analog-capacity.md) through [MC-366](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-366-adaptive-coordinate-query-own-phase-revealment-tariff.md), [NB-176](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-176-euler-weighted-fourier-fingerprints-certify-source-complexity.md) through [NB-200](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-200-atomic-phase-cell-packing-forces-a-2pi-exp-delta-endpoint-threshold.md), and [RE-184](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-184-growing-euler-jet-depth-below-kv-shell-scale-remains-nonrigid.md) through [RE-199](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-199-full-real-euler-collapse-persists-through-sub-quarter-linear-depth.md). These lines use different mathematical objects; no universal identification of their currencies is claimed.

Across them, raw dimension, exact injectivity and forward smoothness repeatedly fail as complexity currencies. The useful quantity is the resource that the **declared source class and physical observation map force the destination to acquire**, measured in the inverse geometry native to that architecture.

Farey Discrepancy makes the separation between source coercivity and source observation exact. On the physical-prime fibre, translated divisor-cube capacity prices any changed composite at the `x/log x` relation-energy scale with no support hypothesis. FD-188 then prices the complementary moving-prime gauge by coefficient amplitude rather than support cardinality: if

`D_(a,q)(x)=sum_(n<=x,mu^2(n)=1)|a(n)-mu(n)|^q`

and `M_(a,q)` is prime-extension relation energy, then

`D_(a,q)(x)=o(x)` and `M_(a,q)(x)=o(x/log x)  =>  a=mu`.

A fixed moved prime forces positive linear `D_q` mass whenever `M_q` is subcritical, while the exact one-prime multiplicative deformation shows that the linear scale is genuine. Yet the complete dyadic midpoint ladder can remain blind to critical `M_q`. The missing resource is therefore an observation theorem exposing the two source currencies, not a stronger source-identification theorem in isolation.

Möbius Cancellation shows the analogous dependence on acquisition architecture. MC-363--MC-364 force near-endpoint Hellinger separation and logarithmically diverging Bhattacharyya depth, but MC-365 supplies a coordinatewise BSC that attains those generic tariffs. The destination lower bound is therefore only an admission condition. MC-366 then gives the opposite-direction theorem once the acquisition mechanism is restricted to adaptive coordinate queries: for the even-rank punctured-cube source,

`rho_i^2=E|E[X_i|Y_i]|^2 <= p_i + 1/(2^R-1)`,

where `p_i` is the probability that the transcript ever reads its own source coordinate. Combined with the endpoint energy inequality, bounded-energy constant dephasing requires positive-density own-phase revealment, and exact unit-energy dephasing requires the sharp rate `1-1/(2^R-1)`. The BSC pays this cost with `p_i=1`. The source-side currency is therefore **provenance of destination-relevant source access**, not abstract transcript information alone.

Nyman--Beurling separates representation, recurrence volume and physical source occupancy. NB-198 replaces source approximation by positive phase-window occupancy; NB-199 follows the actual window scale and pushes `o(a)` returns into the logarithmic endpoint layer `t~x_a/log x_a`. NB-200 then changes the source currency exactly when those windows become physically sub-unit: each favorable phase cell can hold at most one lattice atom, so total capturable positive mass is bounded by number of cells times maximal atom mass. This yields the one-sided endpoint threshold

`liminf |t| log(x_a)/x_a >= 2 pi e^Delta`

for positive returns with error `o(1/log x_a)`. The constant is a shell-dependent capacity boundary rather than a return-existence theorem. The source acquisition mechanism therefore changes from continuum interval occupancy to atomic packing as the requested precision reaches the lattice scale.

Robin Extremal gives the stable-inverse counterpart. Exact scalar Euler data identify the source at infinite precision, but matched ordinary-prime controls can preserve a macroscopic Robin transient while making the complete positive real Euler ray nearly blind. RE-198 showed this through every sublinear jet depth. RE-199 now keeps the exact RE-188 repair radius `W=4K` and penetrates a genuine linear wedge: for every fixed `eta<1/4`, every growing `K<=eta log p` admits the same `{0,1,2}` ordinary-prime matched control with

`sup_(s>=1)|D_Q(s)| <= d_p e^(-c_eta K)`.

The quarter threshold is representation-specific, not universal; it is where the present absolute first-moment factor `(4K/log p)^(K+1)` ceases to decay. The relevant source resource is therefore not jet count or continuum span but **repair radius relative to selector damping at the requested precision**.

These examples refine the same accounting rule. A source tariff is useful only when the admissible source fibre, physical acquisition map, destination-relevant quotient and inverse modulus all line up. MC-365 adds the calibration requirement that the forced destination tariff must exceed what an admissible source realization can supply; MC-366 shows how this becomes a theorem once the architecture exposes a physical provenance coordinate. FD-188 gives the deterministic analogue of choosing the correct fidelity norm. NB-200 shows that the correct acquisition currency can change when the observation cells cross source granularity. RE-199 shows that an apparently natural parameter threshold can be only a representation constant unless repair geometry itself is controlled.

Before importing a generic complexity measure, identify the exact finite/analytic network through which source information must travel and compute its native extremal quantity: full relation capacity rather than one route; coefficient amplitude rather than support count when perturbations can be dense and small; own-phase revealment rather than transcript entropy when generic information is attainable; occupancy or atomic carrying capacity according to phase-cell scale; repair radius versus selector damping rather than raw jet depth. Then produce the opposite-direction acquisition theorem in the same currency and source class.

**Boundary.** The currencies above are not interchangeable. Farey `D_q`/relation capacity does not supply an observation theorem. MC-366 applies to noiseless adaptive coordinate queries without arbitrary joint side channels and gives no estimate for `M(x)`. Nyman atomic capacity is positivity- and shell-specific and does not by itself prove a Nyman-distance lower bound. Robin's `1/4` is a threshold of the current `W=4K` absolute-moment representation, not a universal scalar-rigidity constant. The synthesis is an audit principle about where quantitative source information resides and when a destination tariff becomes a genuine contradiction, not a theorem equating these mechanisms.
