# MI-023 — Source information is priced by the declared acquisition primitive and its inverse/capacity geometry

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [FD-167](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-167-full-field-l2-inversion-has-uniform-square-root-prefix-conditioning.md), [FD-168](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-168-prefix-mobius-rows-give-coordinate-minimal-stable-partial-farey-sampling.md), [FD-169](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-169-unrestricted-mixed-measurements-collapse-to-target-rank.md), [MC-342](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-342-forward-source-regularity-does-not-price-analog-capacity.md), [MC-343](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-343-gaussian-noise-transcript-capacity.md), [MC-344](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-344-bit-flip-source-readout-capacity.md), [MC-345](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-345-adaptive-source-query-feedback-capacity.md), [NB-176](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-176-euler-weighted-fourier-fingerprints-certify-source-complexity.md), [NB-177](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-177-square-summable-euler-spectrum-caps-canonical-complexity-certificate.md), and [NB-178](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-178-coordinatewise-phase-codes-recover-linear-source-complexity-certificates.md). No common transform theorem is claimed.

Farey Discrepancy, Möbius Cancellation and Nyman--Beurling expose complementary failure modes of raw information counting. The common question is not how many coordinates a representation has, but what **acquisition or dual-access primitive** is allowed, which source fibres it identifies, and at what inverse/capacity scale those distinctions remain usable.

FD-167 gives a positive inverse theorem. The complete Farey discrepancy field determines the consecutive source prefix through `floor(sqrt(H))` with an `H`-independent modulus in the natural unnormalized `L^2` field norm. FD-168 shows that the same conditioning survives severe coordinate sparsification: for a target prefix `r<=R`, the union of the nonzero Möbius inverse rows is the unique coordinate-minimal subset of raw Fourier coordinates that recovers it, and for `R<=H^(1/2-delta)` only `R H^(o(1))=o(sqrt H)` coordinates are needed.

FD-169 shows why that count is meaningful only because the acquisition family was fixed. If arbitrary target-aware linear measurements are allowed, exact recovery of the rank-`R` prefix map is possible exactly when `ker L subset ker T`; then `T=B L`, and the minimal number of measurements is simply `R`, attained by measuring the target functionals themselves. Unrestricted mixing does not discover a new compression law: it **moves the inverse into the sensor**. Any nontrivial measurement budget has to constrain acquisition before counting outputs.

MC-342 gives the opposite pathology. A scalar dyadic transcript can carry essentially `R` source bits while range and benign forward regularity remain bounded; the cost appears only in exponentially small inverse separation. MC-343 converts that hidden precision into an SNR bill under Gaussian readout. MC-344 shows that a fixed set of `k` BSC source accesses carries at most `k c(q)` information, yet an extensive fixed-noise readout still retains `Theta(R)` capacity. MC-345 makes the channel budget stable under feedback: even if the controller chooses the next source coordinate from the complete previous noisy transcript, revisits coordinates and stops adaptively,

`I(X;T) <= c(q) E[tau]`.

The query indices carry no extra source information after conditioning on the history that selected them. Thus once the acquisition primitive is genuinely restricted to memoryless noisy source queries, adaptivity can allocate capacity but cannot exceed the per-access budget. This is the precise complement to FD-169: **a free target-aware acquisition map trivializes measurement count, whereas a declared restricted channel has a compositional capacity law even under adaptive control.**

NB-176--NB-178 show that the same declaration problem occurs on the **dual witness** side. The canonical Euler feature map is source-native and jointly Hilbert-contracting, but NB-177 proves that its square-summed spectrum is compact enough to under-certify the unresolved source complexity. NB-178 weakens the architecture to coordinatewise scalar contractions. The enlarged witness class pays an explicit factor `K`; duplicated or merely separated probes do not beat that tariff, but a dense phase-code response with many `sqrt N` singular values can repay it and recover a linear complexity certificate. Hence “canonical” or “source-native” does not fix the information budget by itself: the joint norm imposed across probes is part of the observation primitive.

These examples isolate one reusable accounting rule. Before assigning an information cost, state the admissible source class, the acquisition/dual family, the target quotient, the observation norm or noise model and the downstream resolution. Then prove a resource that survives those declarations: lower separation/inverse modulus, stable singular values, capacity per admitted access, or a dual response whose spectral spread remains nondegenerate after charging its probe budget. Only after this geometry is fixed do measurement count, dimension or adaptivity become meaningful currencies.

Forward smoothness, raw feature count, positive noise and nominal dimension are secondary descriptors. Sparse coordinates can be enough when the inverse is uniformly conditioned; unrestricted mixtures can make count tautological by embedding the target inverse; many noisy accesses can still have enough aggregate capacity; feedback cannot create free capacity in a constrained memoryless channel; one square-summed source-native witness can become compact; and a coordinatewise phase architecture can escape that compactness only by paying for and actually realizing coherent spectral reuse.

**Boundary.** FD-167--FD-169 concern a finite linear/Fourier Farey model; MC-342--MC-345 concern specific finite-source analog/BSC channels; NB-176--NB-178 concern lower certificates for Nyman approximate complexity. This synthesis does not provide a universal acquisition theorem, declare a canonical noise/dual norm, construct the Nyman phase code, or prove an RH estimate. It supplies a cross-line audit: information budgets are meaningful only after the acquisition and dual-access geometry has been fixed rather than optimized away.
