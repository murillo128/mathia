# MI-023 — Source information is priced by the declared source class, physical map and inverse discrimination geometry

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [FD-195](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-195-franel-duality-has-a-nonvanishing-cost-on-geometric-quotient-witnesses.md), [FD-198](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-198-prime-square-root-fourier-shell-forces-subpower-mertens-rms.md), [MC-380](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-380-tiny-source-primes-can-be-packed-before-terminal-differencing.md), [NB-215](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-215-signed-fixed-shell-preconditioning-can-only-export-the-mellin-tariff.md), [RE-214](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-214-prime-count-capacity-caps-separated-vertical-hiding-without-kv-fidelity.md), and [WI-348](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-348-global-phase-sensitivity-still-needs-resolution-collapse.md), together with the preceding line-local source-cost results they refine. These lines use different mathematical objects; no universal identification of their currencies is claimed.

Raw dimension, exact injectivity, forward smoothness and nominal representation complexity repeatedly fail as information currencies. The useful quantity is the resource that the **declared source class and physical observation map force the destination to acquire**, measured after all exact normalization, reconstruction and quotient operations native to that architecture.

Farey Discrepancy now shows both sides of norm mismatch. Above the geometric threshold, raw signed Fourier data have a stable contractive inverse on the desired quotient witnesses, while scalar Franel/Sobolev energy loses that rowwise contraction. FD-198 adds the opposite warning: lowering the positive half-Sobolev energy far enough to recover one RH-scale Mertens value also forces subpower RMS on `Theta(K/log K)` distinct reciprocal-prime samples. The right tariff is therefore neither raw inversion cost nor the exponent of one positive energy in isolation; one must inspect the strongest simultaneous source consequence that the destination norm imposes.

Möbius Cancellation shows why even a correct analytic tariff may be an artifact of how source coordinates are handled. MC-379 improved the generic moving-character ceiling by using the primitive rough-squarefree structure of actual endpoint modes, but paid an exponential codimension cost for deleting tiny source primes. MC-380 instead packs those tiny coordinates into a preliminary exact factor and reserves roughness only for the terminal block where the complete-sum estimate needs it. The result keeps the strong rank-side geometry while reaching the `log log y` analytic ceiling. A nuisance coordinate should therefore be priced as lost dimension only when the proof genuinely has to delete it; sometimes exact factorization can carry it for free until the stage that matters.

Nyman--Beurling shows that preconditioning can relocate rather than remove a tariff. NB-215 proves a sign-blind global `Omega(X)` source-normalized reconstruction cost for every nonzero-mean signed/complex fixed shell, with repeated primitives costing `Omega(X^j)`. Suppressing the central kernel trades against the signed-source conditioning `kappa`; at least one channel remains polynomially expensive under bare operator-norm closure. The accounting must therefore include acquisition, normalization and reconstruction together. A local Fourier cancellation is not a gain if the inverse price reappears as source conditioning or frequency-tail mass.

Robin Extremal separates **carrier capacity** from **arithmetic fidelity**. RE-214 shows that bounded ordinary-prime multiplicity alone forces the leading half-logarithmic ceiling for separated continuum hiding; above the endpoint there are simply not enough distinct prime edits to support the required signed prefix. KV fidelity is a second, finer currency needed to exclude the exact endpoint and impose the thicker subendpoint deficit. Using the strongest available arithmetic bound without first checking raw source capacity can hide which mechanism actually causes the obstruction.

Weil Inertia gives the representation-resolution version. WI-348 phase-saturates the carrier rather than quotienting global phase. At fixed aperture the entire phase-height source set still has covering number `O(rho^(-2))`, so extensive output rank forces the ordinary source-resolution radius to collapse or the sensitivity/transmission ratio to diverge. Rapid winding of a compact nuisance coordinate is not extensive source information at fixed physical resolution.

The reusable accounting rule is therefore: specify the admissible source fibre and topology, physical observation family, destination quotient and norm, requested precision, support/multiplicity constraints and moving regime; then compute the **inverse resource** needed in that same currency. Before accepting a tariff, use every exact structural restriction of the source class and every exact factorization that can postpone or avoid deletion. Before accepting a preconditioning gain, reconstruct and normalize the physical destination. Before invoking fine fidelity, test crude carrier capacity. Before crediting a rapidly varying feature, measure its entropy at destination resolution.

A contraction can disappear under destination compression; a positive global norm can overcoerce many coordinates at once; a generic analytic loss can disappear on the actual source family; a source coordinate can sometimes be packed instead of discarded; a signed preconditioner can export cost rather than erase it; a source can fail first by finite carrier capacity and only later by arithmetic fidelity; and feature richness matters only when the physical source-resolution modulus scales with the requested destination rank.

**Boundary.** Farey weighted energy, Möbius common-radical size, Nyman reconstructed contour cost, Robin prime-prefix capacity/fidelity and Weil-Inertia metric entropy live in different architectures. The synthesis is an audit principle about where quantitative information is actually priced, not a theorem transferring one line's numerical threshold to another.
