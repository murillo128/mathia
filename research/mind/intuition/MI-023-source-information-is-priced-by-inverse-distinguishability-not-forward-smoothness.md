# MI-023 — Source information is priced by the declared source class, acquisition topology and destination-relative discrimination geometry

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [FD-167](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-167-full-field-l2-inversion-has-uniform-square-root-prefix-conditioning.md) through [FD-177](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-177-long-dyadic-block-repair-preserves-every-subextremal-rank-window.md), [MC-342](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-342-forward-source-regularity-does-not-price-analog-capacity.md) through [MC-352](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-352-edge-likelihood-ratio-stability-dephasing-barrier.md), [NB-176](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-176-euler-weighted-fourier-fingerprints-certify-source-complexity.md) through [NB-186](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-186-pairwise-phase-decorrelation-recovers-growing-raw-rank-at-the-cubic-width-scale.md), and [RE-184](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-184-growing-euler-jet-depth-below-kv-shell-scale-remains-nonrigid.md) through [RE-186](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-186-microstepped-chebyshev-repair-reaches-every-subcritical-scalar-jet-depth.md). No universal acquisition theorem is claimed.

The common question across these lines is not how many coordinates a representation exposes. It is what **source class, target quotient and acquisition topology** are declared, which source fibres the destination must distinguish, how the observation operator varies, which amplitudes and conditioning are part of the source channel, and what inverse/capacity/reliability scale makes those distinctions usable.

Farey Discrepancy shows why ambient dimension is source-class relative. Complete-field and consecutive-time observations are exactly invertible over unrestricted formal sources, while global squarefree multiplicativity makes extremely sparse geometric midpoint samples target-rigid. FD-175--FD-176 showed that this is not explained by coefficient fidelity. FD-177 closes the strongest remaining coordinatewise version: even on the dyadic ladder, a permanent deletion-only source can agree with Möbius on every fixed subextremal rank cone `omega(n)<=rho log n/loglog n`, for arbitrary fixed `rho<1`, while differing on only `O(x^eta)` coefficients up to `x` for any `eta>0` and still matching every sampled midpoint. The load-bearing resource is cross-coordinate compatibility that prevents rare near-extremal-rank shells from repairing each observation block; correctness of almost all individually named coordinates is not a substitute.

Möbius Cancellation gives a complementary finite-source accounting. A scalar transcript may encode `Theta(R)` bits behind exponentially small separations, so benign forward regularity does not price information. Once a readout model is declared, capacity and edge discrimination become invariant currencies. MC-348--MC-349 charge symmetrized transcript KL on the source graph and then only on edges crossing the destination quotient. MC-350 forces an extensive exact channel-law boundary from the dephasing information floor, and MC-351 forces an `Omega(R)` aggregate source-edge KL budget on that exact cut without a raw-query model or decoder.

MC-352 adds the matching reverse-sensitivity currency. If `epsilon_e` is the essential source-edge log-likelihood radius, then the symmetrized edge divergence obeys `b_e<=psi(epsilon_e)` with `psi(u)=u tanh(u/2)<=u^2/2`. Thus an architecture whose neighboring transcript laws become uniformly likelihood-close cannot pay the extensive KL tariff required by bounded-energy nontrivial dephasing. Exact matched-energy dephasing demands singular neighboring laws. The open analytic problem is correspondingly concrete: derive small source-edge likelihood ratios, or a sufficiently small averaged/exceptional-tail `psi` budget, from the actual coefficient, bandwidth, precision or regularity resources of the proposed arithmetic channel.

Nyman--Beurling shows that apparent source rank must retain amplitudes and acquisition charge. Genuine Euler labels are nonselective if weak channels can be normalized independently, while the physical scalar Euler readout lives naturally in an absolute Wiener/Dirichlet topology rather than the Hilbert dual. NB-184 makes that charged scalar acquisition cheap on density-one heights after quotienting the invisible constant mode. NB-185 shows fixed prime-phase rank survives that filter, and NB-186 upgrades this to growing raw nuclear rank `N~1/a` at the cubic simultaneous-selection scale using only pairwise phase decorrelation. Yet the raw matrix still ignores the true Euler weights `log p/p^(1+a)`. Growing phase coherence is therefore not the same as growing source-faithful rank.

Robin Extremal supplies a different warning about lower-bound tariffs. RE-184 showed growing Euler jets remain interpolable below the KV shell scale, RE-185 removed a `log K` conditioning artifact and reached `K=Theta(V(p))`, and RE-186 removes the remaining coarse-generation transport loss by microstepping the repair geometry. Honest ordinary-prime `{0,1,2}` controls can now match every boundary-jet depth `K=o(log p/loglog p)` while preserving KV fidelity and an order-one transient Robin excursion. The live scalar boundary is the critical capacity scale itself, not merely a growing or KV-sized family.

These examples isolate one reusable accounting rule. Before assigning an information cost, state the admissible source class, whether the task is arbitrary recovery, one-target testing or quotient recovery, the acquisition/dual family and its topology, every changing operator or horizon, source amplitudes and allowed renormalization, observation norm/noise model, downstream error tolerance, destination cut and matched-control class. Then prove a resource that survives those declarations: an inverse modulus, orbit-hitting/certification scale, source-class-relative kernel bound, stable singular values, channel capacity, source-edge transcript divergence, source-edge likelihood-ratio budget, or charged dual response that the admissible controls cannot reproduce.

Forward smoothness, raw feature count, nominal dimension, positive noise, adaptivity, frequency membership, finite or merely subcritical growing scalar depth and canonical probe labels are secondary descriptors. Sparse observations can be enough when source constraints globally couple every repair coordinate; arbitrary joint coding still pays if the destination needs reliable neighboring-state discrimination; raw growing phase rank can remain nonselective when amplitudes are omitted; and a scalar interpolation tariff may recede all the way to its critical capacity scale after the representation and repair geometry are conditioned appropriately.

**Boundary.** The cited results concern different Farey, finite-source, Nyman and Robin models and do not supply a universal acquisition theorem or canonical source norm. FD-177 is one-target non-rigidity in a weakened multiplicative class, not pairwise inversion, and does not settle the extremal-rank endpoint. MC-352 prices KL from reverse likelihood stability but gives no analytic likelihood-ratio bound for a concrete Möbius architecture. NB-186 concerns raw phases, not amplitude-balanced Euler acquisition. RE-186 reaches every asymptotically subcritical scalar Euler-jet depth but does not settle a fixed positive fraction of the critical `log p/loglog p` capacity, the full critical scale, or a genuinely joint/nonlocal source invariant. The synthesis is an audit rule: information budgets become mathematical only after source fibre, destination quotient, acquisition geometry, reverse distinguishability and matched realizability are fixed rather than optimized away.
