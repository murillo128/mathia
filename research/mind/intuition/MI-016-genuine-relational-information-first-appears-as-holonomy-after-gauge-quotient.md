# MI-016 — Gauge quotient separates genuine loop information from programmable pointwise support deletion

**Evidence level:** exact within two distinct current representations: WP-297--[WP-298](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-298-faithful-nonparallel-site-metrics-cannot-evade-mobius-support-rigidity.md) for multiplicative boundary transport/pointwise metrics, and VIS-214--[VIS-215](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-215-weighted-cycle-frustration-packing.md) for prime-phase quadratic optimization. No equivalence between the models, common arithmetic-curvature theorem, or RH implication is claimed.

Weil Positivity gives a two-stage rigidity test. WP-297 proves that a flat multiplicative cocycle is pure gauge:

`C(m,n)=G(mn)G(n)^(-1)`.

After quotienting this frame choice, linear equivariant transport collapses back to Dirichlet convolution. WP-298 then shows that arbitrary **faithful** pointwise positive metrics remain support-neutral even when they are completely nonparallel: exact mixed-composite zero energy forces the vector response itself to vanish and hence returns to the Möbius direction. Within that pointwise class, exact support can change only through a genuine nullspace. But an arbitrary moving nullspace can program the Mangoldt support, so degeneracy is meaningful only when its kernel is source-forced independently.

Visual Exploration exhibits the complementary loop side. Vertex phases are gauge on the edge gains of the prime-torus quadratic form; cycle holonomy survives that quotient. VIS-214 makes holonomy the exact obstruction to coefficient-envelope attainment. VIS-215 adds a quantitative global certificate: cycle defects weighted by harmonic resistance can be fractionally packed subject to edge capacities, and their optimal sum lower-bounds unavoidable synchronization energy.

The reusable rule is therefore more precise than “look for nonlocal structure.” **First quotient local gauge freedom. Then distinguish closed-loop relational invariants from pointwise degeneracy.** A nontrivial loop response cannot be removed by a frame change and can accumulate quantitatively, as in the visual cycle packing. A pointwise positive readout has no analogous support-selecting power while faithful; when it becomes degenerate, the burden shifts to proving that the nullspace is generated by source geometry rather than inserted as a selector.

This helps prevent two opposite mistakes. Rich site dependence can be pure coordinate choice or faithful reweighting and carry no new support information. Conversely, exact support deletion through a moving kernel can look powerful while merely encoding the desired answer. The first robust relational datum is a gauge-invariant loop response; the first robust pointwise support datum would have to be a **canonically forced** degeneracy pattern.

**Boundary.** VIS-215's holonomy lives in a finite Hermitian gain graph; WP's prime-diamond curvature would live in a multiplicative boundary bundle. A visual frustrated cycle does not supply Weil curvature, and a non-flat Weil connection would not imply positivity. The common content is only the diagnostic hierarchy: gauge quotient, then loop invariant or independently forced kernel, before scalar norms/determinants erase provenance.
