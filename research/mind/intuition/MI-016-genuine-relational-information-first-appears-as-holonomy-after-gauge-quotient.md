# MI-016 — After local gauge freedom is removed, the first durable relational variable is closed-loop holonomy

**Evidence level:** exact within two distinct current representations: [WP-297](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-297-flat-multiplicative-boundary-cocycles-are-gauge-trivial.md) for multiplicative boundary transport and [VIS-214](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-214-cycle-holonomy-frustration-envelope.md) for prime-phase quadratic optimization. No equivalence between the two models, common arithmetic curvature theorem, or RH implication is claimed.

Two independent lines now expose the same structural test in concrete form. In Weil Positivity, a site-dependent multiplicative transport that satisfies the flat cocycle law is pure gauge:

`C(m,n)=G(mn)G(n)^(-1)`.

After quotienting by `G`, every continuous linear intertwiner returns to ordinary multiplicative-shift equivariance and hence to Dirichlet convolution; exact finite Weil localization then forces the same visible Möbius direction. The first possible linear datum not removable this way is a nontrivial prime-diamond holonomy: the two paths from `n` to `pqn` must fail to agree.

In Visual Exploration, vertex phases act as a gauge on the preferred edge phases of the prime-torus quadratic form. Raw edge phases therefore have no invariant meaning. The coefficient envelope is attainable exactly when the edge gains are balanced or antibalanced, and balance is decided by products around cycles. Weighted cycle holonomy is the first gauge-invariant obstruction to coherent phase alignment.

The reusable rule is precise: **when local coordinates can absorb a candidate relation, test the shortest closed loops before attributing mathematical content to it.** Open-path data may only describe a choice of frame. A nontrivial loop response survives frame changes and can therefore carry genuine relational information.

This rule is useful both positively and negatively. Vanishing loop holonomy can collapse an apparently rich site-dependent architecture back to a simpler quotient, as in WP-297. Nonzero holonomy can certify a quantitative failure of global alignment, as in VIS-214. In either case the loop invariant should be computed before norms, determinants or positive scalarizations erase its origin.

The analogy stops at that methodological boundary. VIS-214's cycle phase is a frustration invariant of a finite Hermitian coefficient graph; WP-297's prime-diamond curvature would be a transport invariant of a multiplicative boundary bundle. A frustrated visual cycle does not supply Weil curvature, and non-flat Weil transport would not by itself imply positivity. The common content is only the **gauge-quotient test for whether a proposed relational coordinate is real or removable**.
