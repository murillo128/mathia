# MI-034 — Positivity is informative only when the induced constraint remains proper after the destination quotient

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of [PL-418](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-418-mod5-covariance-j-nonnegative-vacuity.md), [PL-420](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-420-local-factor-schur-inverse-positivity-obstruction.md), [VIS-369](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-369-source-free-degenerate-positive-metric-prescribes-singular-spectrum.md), [VIS-370](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-370-source-free-commuting-positive-metrics-are-spectral-filters.md), [WP-398](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-398-critical-centralizer-quotient-positivity-is-order-trivial.md), and [WP-401](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-401-hereditary-cstar-source-fibres-have-norm-dense-sign-symmetric-tangents.md), contrasted with the source-fixed Hankel reconstruction of [AF-486](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-486-moving-hankel-determinants-recover-finite-dirichlet-depth-at-sharp-exponential-precision.md). The operators and positivity notions are different; the common statement is an evidence-accounting criterion, not a transfer of theorems between lines.

Positive structure is useful only if it excludes something relevant **after** the exact representation, source repair and destination reduction. Several current lines now exhibit distinct ways in which positivity can become too cheap, be erased by a quotient, or fail to survive the arithmetic map that one actually needs.

Prime Lattice first gives an automatic-positivity failure. The canonical raw mod-5 covariance satisfies `C>=0`, and after factoring the signed metric as `D=RJR`, the lift `T=J R C R` obeys `JT=RCR>=0` for every channel covariance. Spectral reality then follows for arbitrary data, including matched off-line controls. The conclusion is true, but it cannot localize the zeta divisor because the positivity premise did not exclude the control.

PL-420 adds a different failure after source fidelity has been restored. The ordinary prime-pair local factor is a positive Schur symbol `B_p`, so `R -> B_p circ R` is completely positive. Exact deletion is the inverse Schur multiplier `R -> G_p circ R`, where the reciprocal symbol has inertia `(1,p-2,0)`. Thus the exact arithmetic inverse is not positive on the ambient PSD cone even when the full residue matrix is retained. Positivity before a source correction cannot be carried through that correction merely because the forward map was completely positive; preservation must be proved on the actual arithmetic image or replaced by a different representation.

Visual Exploration gives a programmable-metric failure. A free positive relative metric aligned with the right-singular basis can prescribe any desired active singular spectrum. Even a canonical source-free rule `C=f(G)` generated from the baseline Gram is only a spectral filter: whitening or ridge regularization can make the spectrum look dramatically better while transferring the inverse conditioning cost into the metric. Positive definiteness does not identify arithmetic leverage when the metric itself is free or generated entirely from the target's source-free geometry.

Weil Positivity gives both a quotient-order and a tangent-geometry failure. After forgetting the critical centralizer, which contains the order unit, the image of the bounded positive cone is the entire self-adjoint information quotient. Fixing the centralizer shadow still leaves two-sided positive perturbations in every sufficiently small centered direction. WP-401 strengthens the locality statement at the source C-star level: on the hereditary fixed-shadow fibre, continuous functional-calculus cutoffs produce norm-dense two-sided tangents. Every bounded real linear orientation forced only by positivity therefore vanishes on any cutoff-stable centered hereditary source space. The cone may be proper in the ambient algebra while carrying no local linear orientation in the exact source directions that survive the fixed shadow.

These negative controls should not be read as “positivity is never informative.” Arithmetic Fidelity supplies the useful contrast. In AF-486 the positive Hankel/Gram structure is not selected to improve a target spectrum or inherited from an arbitrary quotient; its entries are fixed by actual moving Dirichlet samples, and ordered exponential scales plus a Vandermonde determinant give a falsifiable reconstruction formula with a sharp noise scale. The positive matrix matters because the source fixes it and the downstream map uses its detailed scale structure to distinguish admissible sources.

The reusable audit is therefore concrete. For any positivity-based mechanism, ask: **who fixes the cone or metric, what classes does it exclude, does the exact source correction preserve that order on the admissible image, what remains after the destination quotient, is the surviving tangent geometry genuinely one-sided, and does the claimed consequence fail on a matched source control?** Automatic Gram positivity, a nonpositive inverse repair, target-aligned preconditioning, an improper projected cone and a norm-dense two-sided fixed-shadow tangent family fail in different ways, but all show that a positive representation is not evidence unless its remaining asymmetry is independently source-constrained.

**Boundary.** This is not a common theorem about covariance matrices, Schur multipliers, Krein spaces, source C-star algebras, positive metrics or Hankel determinants. PL-418 does not rule out divisor-faithful residual positivity; PL-420 does not rule out positivity on a smaller arithmetic covariance cone; VIS-369--VIS-370 do not rule out externally mandated or source-derived metric geometry; WP-398/WP-401 do not rule out nonlinear, singular, unbounded or cutoff-breaking source relations; AF-486 does not provide rational-prime provenance or unlimited-depth stability. The shared conclusion concerns when positivity is evidential after representation, repair, quotient and locality costs are fully accounted for.
