# MI-034 — Positivity is informative only when the induced constraint remains proper after the destination quotient

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of [PL-418](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-418-mod5-covariance-j-nonnegative-vacuity.md), [VIS-369](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-369-source-free-degenerate-positive-metric-prescribes-singular-spectrum.md), [VIS-370](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-370-source-free-commuting-positive-metrics-are-spectral-filters.md), and [WP-398](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-398-critical-centralizer-quotient-positivity-is-order-trivial.md), contrasted with the source-fixed Hankel reconstruction of [AF-486](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-486-moving-hankel-determinants-recover-finite-dirichlet-depth-at-sharp-exponential-precision.md). The operators and positivity notions are different; the common statement is an evidence-accounting criterion, not a transfer of theorems between lines.

Positive structure is useful only if it excludes something relevant **after** the exact representation and destination reduction. Several current lines now exhibit distinct ways in which positivity can become too cheap to carry arithmetic information.

Prime Lattice gives an automatic-positivity failure. The canonical raw mod-5 covariance satisfies `C>=0`, and after factoring the signed metric as `D=RJR`, the lift `T=J R C R` obeys `JT=RCR>=0` for every channel covariance. Spectral reality then follows for arbitrary data, including matched off-line controls. The conclusion is true, but it cannot localize the zeta divisor because the positivity premise did not exclude the control.

Visual Exploration gives a programmable-metric failure. A free positive relative metric aligned with the right-singular basis can prescribe any desired active singular spectrum. Even a canonical source-free rule `C=f(G)` generated from the baseline Gram is only a spectral filter: whitening or ridge regularization can make the spectrum look dramatically better while transferring the inverse conditioning cost into the metric. Positive definiteness does not identify arithmetic leverage when the metric itself is free or generated entirely from the target's source-free geometry.

Weil Positivity gives a quotient-order failure. After forgetting the critical centralizer, which contains the order unit, the image of the bounded positive cone is the entire self-adjoint information quotient. Fixing the centralizer shadow to `1` still leaves both signs of every sufficiently small centered direction. Positivity survives before the quotient, but the induced cone after the actual information reduction is not proper and therefore cannot orient the surviving correlations.

These negative controls should not be read as “positivity is never informative.” Arithmetic Fidelity supplies the useful contrast. In AF-486 the positive Hankel/Gram structure is not selected to improve a target spectrum or inherited from an arbitrary quotient; its entries are fixed by actual moving Dirichlet samples, and ordered exponential scales plus a Vandermonde determinant give a falsifiable reconstruction formula with a sharp noise scale. The positive matrix matters because the source fixes it and the downstream map uses its detailed scale structure to distinguish admissible sources.

The reusable audit is therefore concrete. For any positivity-based mechanism, ask: **who fixes the cone or metric, what classes does it exclude, what survives after the exact quotient/destination map, and does the claimed consequence fail on a matched source control?** Automatic Gram positivity, target-aligned preconditioning and an improper projected cone fail in different ways, but all show that a positive representation is not evidence unless its remaining asymmetry is independently source-constrained.

**Boundary.** This is not a common theorem about covariance matrices, Krein spaces, Wang maps, C-star order or Hankel determinants. PL-418 does not rule out divisor-faithful residual positivity; VIS-369--VIS-370 do not rule out externally mandated or source-derived metric geometry; WP-398 does not rule out joint nonlinear invariants retaining the centralizer; AF-486 does not provide rational-prime provenance or unlimited-depth stability. The shared conclusion concerns when positivity is evidential after representation costs and quotients are fully accounted for.
