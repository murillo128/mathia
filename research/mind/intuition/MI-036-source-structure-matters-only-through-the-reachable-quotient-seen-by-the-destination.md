# MI-036 — Source structure matters only through the reachable quotient seen by the destination

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis of the exact local frontiers in [arithmetic-fidelity MI-073](../../arithmetic_fidelity/mind/intuition/MI-073-structured-nuisance-fidelity-is-recession-quotient-plus-bounded-escape.md), [Farey MI-058](../../farey_discrepancy/mind/intuition/MI-058-one-sided-full-grid-cost-is-post-convolution-variation-plus-sign-polarization.md), [Möbius MI-093](../../mobius_cancellation/mind/intuition/MI-093-conductor-reduction-does-not-undo-a-product-quotient.md), [Nyman--Beurling MI-081](../../nyman_beurling/mind/intuition/MI-081-first-free-zero-jets-have-sparse-support-but-unpriced-hilbert-conditioning.md), [visual MI-065](../../visual_exploration/mind/intuition/MI-065-finite-external-operator-calculus-inherits-gram-orientation-from-its-generators.md), [Weil-inertia MI-071](../../weil_inertia/mind/intuition/MI-071-positive-carrier-portfolios-inherit-the-square-root-prime-exponent-barrier.md), and [Weil-positivity MI-076](../../weil_positivity/mind/intuition/MI-076-thin-fixed-shadow-slices-can-create-orientation-for-nonarithmetic-reasons.md), refining MI-035.

Nominal source richness is not itself a mathematical resource. What matters is the geometry that remains after every nuisance quotient, convolution, recombination, positive closure and destination projection has been applied. The new local results sharpen this in two complementary directions.

First, many apparent resources disappear because the destination identifies them. Arithmetic fidelity quotients permanent nuisance recession before depth can count. Farey repair sees the positive part of a divisor-smoothed field, not raw Mertens block population. Möbius factor tuples collapse to their product if the kernel depends only on that product, and ordinary conductor reduction does not recreate the lost variables. Nyman multiplicity jets collapse to the Burnol defect, while even the first free jet has sparse finite-section support; only its actual synthesis cost remains unpriced. Finite external operator calculus inherits Gram orientation from its generators, and positive Weil-inertia portfolios inherit the same square-root exponent barrier as one carrier.

Second, WP-404 shows the converse subtlety: a source restriction can matter if it genuinely changes the **reachable** geometry before the destination acts. A thin fixed-shadow slice can destroy two-sided tangency and create a one-sided linear orientation. But this can happen in a completely nonarithmetic control model. Reachability change is therefore a real resource, while arithmetic provenance is an additional gate.

The resulting audit rule is stronger than “preserve multiplicity.” A proposed mechanism must identify either a source variable that the exact destination still resolves, or a source-forced restriction that changes the reachable pre-destination set in a way that survives matched nonarithmetic controls. It must then price that surviving geometry in the norm or exponent actually consumed by the final theorem. Factor count, sample count, carrier count, jet count, operator complexity or codimension do not count before this reduction.

This also separates obstruction from opportunity. Quotient-collapse theorems show why a proposed currency is unavailable; thin-source examples show that changing the reachable set can evade such theorems. Neither direction supplies arithmetic cancellation by itself. The remaining work is always to connect the surviving reduced geometry to a source-specific quantitative estimate rather than infer leverage from representation richness alone.

**Boundary.** This is a synthesis principle, not a theorem asserting one common formal quotient for all seven lines. Each local result has its own hypotheses and destination map. In particular, thinness, noncommutation, bounded-nuisance escape and signed variation are not interchangeable. The cross-line claim is only the evidence-backed methodological one: count a source resource after the exact reachability and destination reductions that act on it, and demand an independent provenance test whenever the reduced geometry can be manufactured in matched controls.
