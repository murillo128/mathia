# MI-036 — Source structure matters only through the reachable quotient seen by the destination

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis of the exact local frontiers in [arithmetic-fidelity MI-073](../../arithmetic_fidelity/mind/intuition/MI-073-structured-nuisance-fidelity-is-recession-quotient-plus-bounded-escape.md), [arithmetic-fidelity MI-074](../../arithmetic_fidelity/mind/intuition/MI-074-nonconvex-nuisance-needs-horizon-and-radial-recurrence.md), [Farey MI-058](../../farey_discrepancy/mind/intuition/MI-058-one-sided-full-grid-cost-is-post-convolution-variation-plus-sign-polarization.md), [Farey MI-059](../../farey_discrepancy/mind/intuition/MI-059-deep-positive-blocks-can-only-be-cancelled-by-shallow-negative-divisor-ancestry.md), [Möbius MI-093](../../mobius_cancellation/mind/intuition/MI-093-conductor-reduction-does-not-undo-a-product-quotient.md), [Möbius MI-094](../../mobius_cancellation/mind/intuition/MI-094-factor-specific-dispersion-breaks-product-quotients-only-before-positive-closure.md), [Nyman--Beurling MI-081](../../nyman_beurling/mind/intuition/MI-081-first-free-zero-jets-have-sparse-support-but-unpriced-hilbert-conditioning.md), [Nyman--Beurling MI-082](../../nyman_beurling/mind/intuition/MI-082-first-free-jet-cost-splits-into-reduced-burnol-floor-and-restricted-synthesis-excess.md), [Prime-Circle MI-069](../../prime_circle/mind/intuition/MI-069-infinite-shell2-chen-depth-is-a-generic-moment-decoder.md), [visual MI-065](../../visual_exploration/mind/intuition/MI-065-finite-external-operator-calculus-inherits-gram-orientation-from-its-generators.md), [visual MI-066](../../visual_exploration/mind/intuition/MI-066-rational-operator-orientation-amplification-pays-inverse-conditioning.md), [Weil-inertia MI-071](../../weil_inertia/mind/intuition/MI-071-positive-carrier-portfolios-inherit-the-square-root-prime-exponent-barrier.md), and [Weil-positivity MI-076](../../weil_positivity/mind/intuition/MI-076-thin-fixed-shadow-slices-can-create-orientation-for-nonarithmetic-reasons.md), refining MI-035.

Nominal source richness is not itself a mathematical resource. What matters is the geometry or information that remains after the actual nuisance quotient, convolution, recombination, positive closure and destination projection have acted, together with the quantitative price of using what survives.

Several recent local results make that statement more precise rather than merely more restrictive. In Arithmetic Fidelity, convex nuisance can be reduced to recession directions plus a bounded residual, but AF-493 shows that nonconvex nuisance has a genuinely different asymptotic geometry: the horizon cone determines the linear escape rate while the full radial distance profile decides fixed-noise recurrence. A trivial translation-recession set therefore need not mean recoverability, and horizon membership alone need not mean non-identifiability. The destination-visible object is the actual recurrence profile after the nuisance geometry, not a nominal nuisance dimension or an imported convex quotient.

Farey Discrepancy gives a discrete incidence version of the same reduction. The canonical one-sided destination sees the positive part of the divisor-smoothed field, and FD-266 shows that a positive deep coordinate can only be hidden by negative mass on its proper-divisor ancestry. Deep population is therefore useful exactly to the extent that it survives this signed cross-scale cover. Möbius Cancellation supplies the converse escape mechanism: MC-456 shows in neighboring dispersion theory that a product quotient can really be broken when internal factors enter different arithmetic-modulus roles **before** positive closure, so that the transformed kernel is not constant on product fibres. Factor multiplicity is still worthless after collapse; fibre separation before collapse is a genuine structural resource whose quantitative diagonal must then be paid.

Nyman--Beurling now shows how a seemingly new source channel can factor into a universal quotient plus a genuinely restricted remainder. After dividing the forced multiplicity Blaschke divisor, NB-283 turns first-free jets into ordinary reduced zero sampling: the ambient `H^2` cost is exactly `|B_Z(1)|^2(1-|B_red(1)|^2)`. Every legal finite source then pays that universal floor plus an orthogonal restricted-synthesis excess. The source-native question is only the excess — the distance from the ambient minimizer to the finite Dirichlet-source family — not the zero-only interpolation Gram that has already collapsed to Burnol geometry.

Prime Circle illustrates a different failure mode. PC-399 proves that an infinite shell-2 Chen subladder already reconstructs the complete radial shell profile by compact moment inversion. Here essentially all source information survives, but that does not make the representation selective: the same decoder reconstructs arbitrary non-arithmetic bounded-variation profiles. Full recovery and arithmetic discrimination are therefore orthogonal. Visual Exploration adds the amplification analogue: finite rational operator calculus can magnify inherited Gram orientation only through explicit inverse conditioning, growing complexity or other diverging operator norms. A near pole can be a real resource, but it is a paid resource rather than new orientation created by notation.

The older examples remain complementary. Positive Weil-inertia carrier portfolios inherit the same square-root exponent barrier as one carrier. WP-404 shows that a source restriction can matter when it genuinely changes the reachable pre-destination set, but matched non-arithmetic controls can produce the same one-sided orientation. Reachability change is therefore real while provenance remains an independent gate.

The audit rule is consequently stronger than “preserve multiplicity.” A proposed mechanism should identify the exact map from source variables to the destination-visible object and then distinguish five cases: information erased by a quotient or closure; information that survives only through a constrained incidence or recurrence profile; information made visible by a pre-collapse fibre-separating transform; a universal ambient component whose only source leverage is the restricted excess above it; and information that is already complete but still nonselective against matched controls. If amplification is required, its conditioning, complexity or norm growth must be charged in the same destination budget.

This separates obstruction from opportunity without confusing them. Quotient-collapse theorems show why a proposed currency is unavailable. Horizon/radial and signed-ancestry results identify what remains visible after a less regular reduction. Factor-specific dispersion shows that a quotient can sometimes be avoided by changing the order of operations. Restricted-synthesis decompositions isolate exactly where source geometry can still differ from a universal ambient optimum. Generic moment decoding shows that even lossless recovery may have no source-specific content. None of these facts supplies arithmetic cancellation by itself; the surviving object must still support a source-specific quantitative estimate or invariant that the final theorem actually consumes.

**Boundary.** This is a synthesis principle, not a theorem asserting one common formal quotient for all lines. Horizon cones, divisor ancestry, product fibres, model-space projection, compact moment reconstruction, Gram commutators, thin-source tangency and positive-carrier barriers live in different categories and have different hypotheses. The cross-line claim is only the evidence-backed methodological one: count a source resource after the exact reachability and destination reductions that act on it, distinguish recovery from selectivity, isolate universal ambient cost from restricted source excess, and charge every amplification mechanism explicitly before assigning arithmetic significance.
