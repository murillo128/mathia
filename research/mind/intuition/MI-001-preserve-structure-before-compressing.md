# MI-001 — Preserve discriminating structure, source-fixed gauge, and composable assembly before canonical compression

**Evidence level:** supported by exact deterministic, analytic, statistical, interaction-kernel, quotient-selection, multiscale-recovery, factorization-gauge, operator-limit, incidence, signed-cancellation, and source-dynamics results across the current Mathia corpus

## Core intuition

Across Mathia, the recurring failure is applying a transformation whose induced indistinguishability relation already identifies states, interactions, signs, or models that the later arithmetic claim needs to distinguish. A representation may retain the target only through an arbitrary gauge, a witness-relative quotient that has not been saturated for composition, a noncanonical domain/exhaustion, a generic complement that reconstructs the target, or a positive/averaged scalarization that deletes exactly the signed relation carrying provenance.

A useful pipeline must therefore derive the **source-fixed category and gauge**, compute the complete retained relation and strongest matched control, saturate downstream witnesses as required for composition, preserve any source-selected signed/nonseparable interaction through the analytic carrier, and prove a canonical non-escaping assembly before optimization, spectralization, positivity, determinant formation, closure, or averaging.

## Consequence for synthesis

The robust order is:

`derive source-fixed category/gauge -> identify the complete quotient and matched controls -> saturate destination witnesses -> introduce and retain any missing signed/nonseparable interaction -> prove source-funded domain/topology/transport -> only then optimize, spectralize, complete, regularize, average, or impose positivity`.

A proposed bridge should fail fast if its metric is unfixed, its destination witness is omitted, its “partial” statistic reconstructs the target through a generic complement bound, its positive completion erases signed support, its averaging turns the new relation into generic pseudorandomness, or its local success disappears in the natural global operator topology.

## Status / novelty

The component notions — witness duality, convex saturation, generalized pencils, commutant gauges, Dirichlet recovery, Schatten ideals, signed flux, Gowers averaging, Markov/KL geometry, and heat-zero transport — are largely classical. The persisted synthesis is program-level: **arithmetic leverage survives only when the source fixes the relation, sign, gauge, witnesses, and assembly before canonical processing can erase, universalize, reconstruct, or arbitrarily redistribute it**.

## Falsification criterion

Find a covered pipeline in which the target varies inside the complete retained quotient/gauge orbit, no later stage receives new source information, yet a stable target theorem follows; or a pipeline whose decisive signed relation is destroyed/reconstructible under the stated operation but nevertheless yields source-specific leverage without additional information.
