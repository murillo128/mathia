# MI-001 — Preserve discriminating structure, source-fixed gauge, and composable assembly before canonical compression

**Evidence level:** supported by exact deterministic, analytic, statistical, interaction-kernel, quotient-selection, multiscale-recovery, factorization-gauge, operator-limit, incidence, and source-dynamics results across the current Mathia corpus

## Core intuition

Across Mathia, the recurring failure is applying a transformation whose induced indistinguishability relation already identifies states, interactions, or models that the later arithmetic claim needs to distinguish. The newest evidence sharpens this beyond ordinary information loss: a representation may retain the target only through an arbitrary gauge, a witness-relative quotient that has not been saturated for composition, a noncanonical domain/exhaustion, or a regularization/average that deletes exactly the signed relation one hoped to use.

A useful pipeline must therefore derive the **source-fixed category and gauge**, compute the complete retained relation and strongest matched control, saturate the downstream witness class as required for composition, and prove a canonical non-escaping assembly before optimization, spectralization, positivity, determinant formation, closure, or averaging. New arithmetic structure has to enter upstream of the stage that would otherwise quotient, universalize, or regularize it away.

The source/control distinction remains decisive. Analytic Frontier closes equal-height scalar danger and isolates a compact unequal-height base zero-freeness question; Visual Exploration separates residual magnitude from orientation and requires one predeclared common information geometry; Weil Inertia closes both continuous confluence detectors and naive singular repairs; Xi Flow exhibits a counting-compatible memory wave that is nevertheless excluded at the endpoint by a prime-free explicit-formula Fourier selector.

## Consequence for synthesis

The robust order is:

`derive source-fixed category/gauge -> identify the complete retained quotient and matched controls -> saturate destination witnesses -> introduce any missing interaction upstream -> prove canonical domain/topology/transport -> only then optimize, spectralize, complete, regularize, average, or impose positivity`.

A proposed bridge should fail fast if its decisive relation is absent before completion, if its factorization or metric has unfixed gauge freedom, if its witness class is not closed under downstream pullback, if its apparent amplification disappears in the natural operator topology, or if its regularization removes the very signed term that carries source specificity.

## Status / novelty

The component notions — witness duality, convex saturation, Dirichlet convolution, Toeplitz/GCD operators, norm-resolvent convergence, torsion incidence, regularized determinants, logarithmic correlation, and heat-zero transport — are largely classical. The persisted synthesis is program-level: **arithmetic leverage survives only when the source fixes the relation, gauge, witnesses, and assembly before canonical processing can erase, universalize, or arbitrarily redistribute it**.

## Falsification criterion

Find a covered pipeline in which the target varies inside the complete retained quotient/gauge orbit, no later stage receives new source information, yet a stable target theorem follows; or a multistage recovery whose destination witnesses are not included in the minimal backward saturation but whose error still composes uniformly under the stated hypotheses. A construction that introduces an independently source-forced new interaction before compression would evade rather than falsify the principle.

## Lean-formalizable core

- Quotient/post-processing monotonicity and witness-induced pseudometrics.
- Minimal backward witness saturation and composition obstruction.
- Dirichlet factorization/recovery identities and information-deficit controls.
- Fixed/refined operator-class vanishing, nonclosability, or classicalization lemmas.
- Norm-resolvent stability of bounded functional calculus.
- Primary splitting/flatness preservation under canonical completion.
