# MI-001 — Preserve discriminating structure, relational depth, and stability before compressing

**Evidence level:** supported by exact deterministic, analytic, statistical, interaction-kernel, fusion-frame, quotient-selection, and algebraic closure theorems

## Core intuition

Across Mathia, the recurring failure is applying a transformation whose induced indistinguishability relation already identifies states or models that the later arithmetic claim needs to distinguish. The newer evidence sharpens the audit in two directions. First, **more channels of the same marginal order are not the same as greater relational depth**: overlapping local relations can contain compatibility information that no collection of independently scalarized pair statistics retains. Second, even when a target survives algebraically, global reconstruction requires a compatible topology; compact admissible fibers can globalize local repairs exactly, while naturality and equivariance remain separate gates.

A useful pipeline must therefore audit the complete statistic, interaction/order support, admissible closure, gluing compactness, and stability modulus before optimization, spectralization, positivity, or completion.

## Strongest current principle

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the general information geometry. Scalar tests retain their closed span; complete marginals can forget joint coupling; Boolean and Hoeffding decompositions expose the exact missing interaction faces; dependent sources replace coordinate degree by source-dependent fusion spans. AF-039 separates exact from stable recovery through the Friedrichs angle.

AF-088--AF-089 add a local-to-global boundary: finite-subset Lipschitz repairs globalize with **no loss of the local constant** when their admissible fiber slices are compact and compatible. Reflexivity is only one compactness mechanism. This proves existence without proving canonicity, equivariance, or source-natural parameter dependence.

Analytic Frontier provides a concrete arithmetic witness that relational depth matters independently of channel count. ANF-006 applies an ordered local-gap constraint before pair compression and beats the Montgomery--Taylor baseline; ANF-007 proves that, for that bridge, two points cannot improve the baseline while three consecutive points can. The gain lives in the consistency of two adjacent gaps and their sum, not in adding another independently scalarized pair moment.

The earlier programmable and matched controls remain essential. Infinite test families can be complete or sharply aliased depending on parameter geometry; source-tunable finite classes can locally program recovery and therefore do not prove intrinsic specificity. Prime Circle supplies an algebraic analogue: increasing finite topology or fixed interaction order often remains inside endpoint/fixed-state closures, and its entire fixed polynomial moment hierarchy now classicalizes into bounded-support singular-series data.

## Consequence for synthesis

The correct order is:

`derive admissible category -> compute complete marginal/relational statistic -> compute source fiber and stability -> prove compatible global gluing -> test matched controls -> only then optimize, spectralize, complete, or prove positivity`.

A canonical lift is useful only if it reduces the actual indistinguishability fiber **and** remains quantitatively usable in the limit. Perfect two-point information can miss an ordered three-point constraint; multiplicative closure can recover information absent from a linear span; dependence can change interaction grading; compact fibers can eliminate local-to-global existence loss; and an exact decoder can still become unstable or non-natural.

Conversely, increasing graph rank, number of pair channels, derivative order, polynomial moment degree at fixed depth, or numerical precision does not help when the complete retained relation has not changed. The mathematical question is always what the final theorem actually receives, at what relational order, and with what condition number/topological compatibility.

## Status / novelty

The component closed-span, Hoeffding, conditional-expectation, fusion-frame, compact-selection, Friedrichs-angle, local-programming, and configuration inequalities use classical ingredients and persisted findings. Their synthesis as a complete-statistic-plus-relational-depth-plus-stability gate is supported rather than a universal theorem for every nonlinear observation category.

## Falsification criterion

Find a pipeline where the target varies inside the complete admissible measured/fusion fiber, no later stage receives new relational information, yet the final invariant recovers the target. Within the ANF-006/007 bridge, show that the three-point gain factors through admissible two-point scalar data. Or produce compatible compact fibers satisfying AF-089 for which exact local Lipschitz constants fail to globalize.
