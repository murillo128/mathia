# MI-001 — Preserve the discriminating structure before compressing

**Evidence level:** supported by exact deterministic, analytic, statistical, interaction-kernel, and algebraic closure theorems

## Core intuition

Across Mathia, the recurring failure is applying a transformation whose induced indistinguishability relation already identifies states or models that the later arithmetic claim needs to distinguish. The converse danger is equally real: apparent injectivity can be manufactured if the admissible observable class has enough target-tunable freedom. The audit must therefore identify the **complete statistic actually measured**, its fibers, its interaction support, and the freedom available inside those fibers before any optimization, spectralization, positivity, or completion.

## Strongest current principle

Arithmetic Fidelity now gives an exact linear form of this gate. AF-030 shows that scalar linear tests retain precisely the closed span `V_F` of the functions actually measured; the signed-measure kernel is `V_F^perp`, and restricted-source fidelity is `(S-S) intersect V_F^perp={0}`. An unmeasured function does not become observable merely because it lies in an algebra generated by the tests. AF-028--AF-029 show why raw cardinality is secondary: an analytically accumulating infinite double-positive family is complete on its visible interval, while a lattice-sampled infinite family has an exact cosine alias horizon.

AF-031--AF-033 add an independent interaction gate. Complete individual marginals can still forget the joint coupling. On a Boolean product, a marginal scenario retains exactly the Walsh interactions indexed by the faces of its downward-closed simplicial complex; missing faces are the exact kernel, and target-relative recovery depends on whether the target interaction support is contained in that complex. “More marginals” and “jointly faithful” are therefore different claims.

The earlier deterministic, stochastic, and analytic evidence remains category-sensitive. Maximal admissible quotients, likelihood-ratio sufficiency, normalized Euler/log-derivative categories, finite-test Beurling fibers, and local programming all show that neither a small error nor a singular source point establishes exact fidelity without the correct ambient category.

Prime Circle supplies a parallel algebraic closure theorem. PC-096--PC-097 reduce arbitrary finite canonical cotangent multigraphs, including cycles, repeated shells, and collision strata, to endpoint/cyclotomic algebra. PC-098--PC-099 then show that fixed-support infinite depth and canonical complete-preimage growth can remain finite-state or universal. Increasing nonlinear depth or graph complexity does not help when the complete retained algebra has not changed.

The order of operations is therefore

`derive admissible category -> compute measured span/joint interaction quotient and programmable fiber -> test source specificity -> only then optimize, spectralize, complete, or prove positivity`.

## Consequence for synthesis

A canonical mark, higher-order lift, or stronger constraint is useful only if it reduces the actual indistinguishability fiber at the layer consumed by the final theorem. Perfect low-order marginals can still miss a high-order target interaction; an infinite family can still alias; and a complicated finite network can remain inside a small endpoint algebra. Conversely, isolated recovery is not evidence of arithmetic specificity if the observable family was flexible enough to program it around arbitrary sources.

## Status / novelty

The component fidelity, closed-span, marginal-kernel, local-programming, and cotangent-reduction results are persisted findings with classical ingredients. Their synthesis as a measured-statistic/interaction-support gate is supported rather than a universal theorem for all nonlinear observations.

## Falsification criterion

Find a pipeline where the target varies inside the complete admissible measured fiber, no later stage receives new information, yet the final invariant recovers the target. Or show that an interaction known to lie outside the observed marginal complex is nevertheless determined by those marginals on the full declared source class.
