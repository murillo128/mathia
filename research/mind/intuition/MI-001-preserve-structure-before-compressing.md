# MI-001 — Preserve discriminating relation through the exact target extraction

**Evidence level:** supported by exact quotient, source-selection, principal-channel, operator, signed-cancellation, and calibration boundaries across the current Mathia corpus

## Core intuition

Across Mathia, failure repeatedly occurs when the operation that makes the target readable also identifies or erases the relation needed to prove something about it. The decisive question is not whether rich arithmetic structure exists upstream, but whether its sign, provenance, coupling or orientation survives **the exact residue, quotient, character block, trace, positive completion, normalization or observation map used by the theorem**.

## Strongest current principle

Prime Lattice gives the clearest new algebraic example. PL-249 starts from canonical adelic/class-field prime monodromy, yet the trivial character whose Artin L-function is Riemann zeta maps every Frobenius label to `1`. PL-250 shows that retaining the complete finite abelian cover does not help inside linear equivariant formalism: the regular representation canonically decomposes into character blocks, with Riemann zeta remaining an invariant trivial factor and the richer arithmetic stored in neighboring factors.

Weil Positivity gives the operator analogue. WP-231 preserves hollow indefiniteness under all bounded energy-bimodule processing; WP-232 shows canonical noncommuting Poisson whitening exposes an indefinite Jacobi form; WP-233 matches the adjacent Riemann-Gamma jump density exactly to critical Bost--Connes prime-ray amplitudes, but ordinary positive completion reinstates precisely the divergent diagonal repair and one real log coordinate misses mixed-prime geometry.

Prime Circle supplies a domain/readout version. PC-236 closes regular one-fiber observables into `C*(S)`, and PC-237 shows that the natural singular inverse-chord extension either lies outside the packet domain or, after canonical normalization, collapses to two boundary phases. Preserving more formal Fourier/singular structure before the final packet limit is not enough.

## Consequence for synthesis

For every proposed bridge, write the source relation, the exact target-extraction operation, its indistinguishability/block/domain class, and the independent theorem expected to act afterward. If the relation vanishes or finite-dimensionalizes there, move the interaction earlier or change the final carrier; do not ask later positive/scalar processing to recreate information already discarded.

## Status / novelty

The component mathematics is classical or persisted exact work. The synthesis is a program-level discipline: **source richness matters only when the target-facing map retains the discriminating relation in a quantitatively usable form**.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a covered pipeline where the target theorem uniformly distinguishes states already identified by its complete retained channel without receiving any new source information, or show that one of the cited principal/positive/domain collapses actually retains the claimed missing relation. A successful new bridge should narrow this intuition by exhibiting the explicit surviving carrier.
