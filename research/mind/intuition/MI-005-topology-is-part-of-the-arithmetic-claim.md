# MI-005 — Topology, metric, target transport, spectral type, and operator category are part of the arithmetic claim

**Evidence level:** supported by exact topology-, metric-, target-, operator-, quotient-, sampling-, spectral-type-, and stability-sensitive results across several branches

## Core intuition

The same arithmetic or geometric signal can be invisible, exactly recoverable but unstable, representation-dependent, compact, Hilbert--Schmidt, nuclear, weak-trace-class, recurrent, rank-limited, pure point, continuous, or infinitely rough depending on how it is realized. The category is not bookkeeping after the mathematics: it determines which distinctions survive, whether structural targets descend under re-encoding, which matched controls are identified, and which determinant/scattering/sign theorem is even available.

Recent evidence adds a particularly sharp warning: **matching the same scalar dispersion law does not identify two operator realizations if their spectral measures/types differ**. A dense sampling of the right eigenvalue formula can still admit no nonzero bounded exact intertwiner to the continuous carrier.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the source/target distinction exact. Distance-to-collision and safe-target-envelope results show that information-preserving refinements can still change structural target distances, while category-relative quotient repair can exist nonlinearly even when linear splitting fails. AF-088--AF-089 further show that compact compatible fibers can globalize local Lipschitz repairs with no loss, but do not supply equivariance or canonicity for free.

Prime Circle supplies ideal and limit-topology gates. Fixed-shell trace-class determinants have zero divisors too sparse for Riemann density; large-conductor Hardy limits classicalize into universal Hilbert/Carleman/Gaussian channels; and every fixed-degree full-chord polynomial moment now stratifies into classical finite-support singular-series data. A surviving defect must therefore escape both operator-ideal and fixed-polynomial classicalization controls.

Prime Flute shows how functional calculus and summation order change relative theory without creating new data. Relative resolvent/scattering constructions can transport one ordinary phase or universal cusp thresholds, while complete marked-tail geometry can approach matched composite controls. Crossing a convergence boundary or subtracting a residue is a categorical move whose residual must still be shown arithmetic.

Prime Lattice separates multiplicative scaffolding from analytic completion. Beurling controls preserve the exponent cone while changing zeros; PL-134 identifies completed functional equation with an added global reciprocal modular-inversion layer; PL-135 shows that a finite Boolean/Walsh cube can participate in genuine modular structure while contributing only finite Euler factors to an already classical Eisenstein zeta product.

Weil Inertia distinguishes algebraic leakage from extractable spectral leakage. Finite-period compensated motifs can hide in counting and moving-edge marginals yet must leak into a reciprocal harmonic; without localization against the complement that leakage is diagnostic rather than coercive.

Weil Positivity now makes spectral type an explicit gate. The Prime-Flute Schiffer compression and finite-Weil operator lie in incompatible Schatten classes; continued DtN loses physical positivity; and WP-131 proves that the canonical solenoid Gamma carrier (complete pure point spectrum) and radial Gamma carrier (continuous multiplication spectrum with no `L^2` eigenvectors) admit **no nonzero bounded exact heat-semigroup intertwiner in either direction**, despite using the same scalar Gamma dispersion.

Xi Flow supplies the dynamical-scale counterpart. Finite collision jets are reproducible by polynomial controls, and bounded local neighborhoods near arithmetic equilibrium lose fixed-time memory on a shrinking scale; order-one memory first becomes plausible only on a growing mesoscopic window.

## Consequence for synthesis

A useful realization category must satisfy at least these compatibility conditions:

1. the target discriminator is present in the retained support and relational order;
2. the structural target descends through every admitted equivalence or lies in the exact safe envelope;
3. the discriminator has positive quantitative stability margin;
4. local repairs glue in the required topology and the chosen repair is as natural/equivariant as the arithmetic source demands;
5. the strongest matched control is not automatically identified by the metric, normalization, or quotient;
6. the operator ideal, spectral type, and functional calculus support the intended determinant, scattering, continuation, or sign theorem;
7. any category-changing map transports the relevant spectral measure/sign structure rather than only a scalar symbol;
8. limiting, singular, finite-window, and regularity procedures are classified before their residual is called arithmetic.

Changing category can be a real advance. But every change must audit what becomes visible, which target states become admissible, which spectral type is carried, how far the signal lies from loss, and which new theorem actually becomes available.

## Status / novelty

All cited stability, target-envelope, trace-ideal, sampling, functional-calculus, compact-selection, spectral-type, and regularity separations are persisted findings with classical ingredients. Their interpretation as a joint realization-category gate is supported synthesis, not a universal operator theorem.

## Falsification criterion

Derive a canonical category in which a selector is present, target-compatible, stably recoverable, control-separating, spectrally compatible, and supported by the required global theorem. Conversely, produce a nonzero bounded exact intertwiner across the WP-131 pure-point/continuous carriers, or show a proposed category changes target/spectral type under an admitted equivalence while still preserving the claimed arithmetic theorem without new structure.
