# MI-005 — Category, assembly topology, conditioning, operator ideal, spectral type, and transport direction are part of the arithmetic claim

**Evidence level:** supported by exact category-sensitive findings across Arithmetic Fidelity, Prime Circle, Prime Flute, Prime Lattice, Weil Inertia, Weil Positivity, and Xi Flow

## Core intuition

The same source signal can be present yet unusable because it lives in the wrong range, requires unbounded finite conditioning, sits in an operator ideal with the wrong spectral density, has incompatible spectral type, can be transported only opposite to the target direction, or disappears when finite stages are assembled in the required topology. A category change is therefore a mathematical claim, not bookkeeping.

Arithmetic Fidelity now makes the assembly layer exact. Original-range recovery, WOT-closed admissibility, weak compactness, and compact-operator limits have different family-level non-escape gates. The fact that every finite approximant lies in a desired class is not enough when that class is not closed in the topology used for the limit.

## Strongest justified principle

AF-099--AF-104 calibrate finite visibility, bounded accessibility, and original-range provenance. AF-105 shows that pointwise relatively weakly compact repaired orbits can keep WOT cluster points in the original target. AF-106 separates this from admissibility: equivariance, positivity cones, and fixed affine identities are WOT closed, whereas weak compactness and compactness need not survive bounded WOT/SOT limits. AF-107 identifies collective norm compactness as the compact-operator assembly gate.

Prime Circle and Prime Flute continue to supply operator-ideal/post-processing limits: trace/Schatten categories constrain determinant/scattering behavior, and nonlocalization after a prime-blind quotient does not recover provenance.

Prime Lattice supplies the order-direction gate: canonical completed positivity transports outward to safer half-planes, while the inverse direction is not positive. Weil Inertia supplies the spectral-resolution gate: exact inertia is discontinuous at confluence while every preassigned continuous regularization can lose its charge. Xi Flow supplies the boundary-category analogue: far-tail control survives only after changing localization scale, while centered finite-range mean removal has collision-positive modes.

## Consequence for synthesis

A useful realization must specify source/target range, norm budget, assembly topology, admissible closure class, quotient, operator ideal, spectral type/resolution, boundary scaling, and direction of positivity/order transport. It must prove that the required discriminator survives in that exact category with a quantitative source-forced margin.

## Status / novelty

The underlying functional analysis, operator theory, semigroup order, spectral continuity, and localization theory are classical. The synthesis is the compatibility gate: **finite correctness does not imply a valid limit in the category needed by the final arithmetic theorem**.

## Falsification criterion

Construct a source-forced category that crosses the current range/assembly/ideal/order barriers while preserving target discrimination and matched-control separation, or show that one of the claimed category distinctions collapses under the exact hypotheses of a concrete line.
