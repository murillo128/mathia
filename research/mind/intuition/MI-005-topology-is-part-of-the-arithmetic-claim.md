# MI-005 — Category, conditioning, range, operator ideal, spectral type, and transport direction are part of the arithmetic claim

**Evidence level:** supported by exact category-sensitive findings across Arithmetic Fidelity, Prime Circle, Prime Flute, Prime Lattice, Weil Inertia, Weil Positivity, and Xi Flow

## Core intuition

The same source signal can be present yet unusable because it lives in the wrong range, requires unbounded finite conditioning, sits in an operator ideal with the wrong spectral density, has incompatible spectral type, or can be transported only in the direction opposite to the target. A category change is therefore a mathematical claim, not bookkeeping.

The newest evidence adds two precise gates. Arithmetic Fidelity makes original-range accessibility a tensor-norm condition after finite budgets are calibrated. Prime Lattice shows that even a canonical positive semigroup can have the wrong **order direction**: positivity moves outward toward easier half-planes, while the inward map needed for RH is not positive.

## Strongest justified principle

AF-099--AF-101 show that finite values become complete quantitative witnesses only after a norm budget is declared. Bidual accessibility is weaker than original-range assembly; the universal gap is exactly the distinction between the genuine-target pair norm `alpha` and the projective/bidual norm `pi`.

Prime Circle and Prime Flute show operator-category and post-processing limits. Trace-class or `S_2` boundaries constrain determinant/scattering density, and PC-162 proves that even an infinite-rank nonlocal Kron response can remain a prime-blind trace-class classicalization. Changing local to nonlocal does not guarantee new information.

PL-144--PL-145 separate native/completed positivity and transport direction. Completed screw positivity is already RH-equivalent, while the shifted Volterra semigroup preserves positivity only toward safer zero-free half-planes. The target direction needs an additional arithmetic theorem.

Weil Inertia shows that quotient choice changes quantitative meaning: multiplicity slack and simple-real screening must be charged before interpreting a low spectral tail. Xi Flow shows that localization category matters similarly: fixed-ratio `H^{1/2}` cutoffs are critical, diverging capacitary buffers are not, and uncentered cross-ratio localization trades conductance singularity for span control.

## Consequence for synthesis

A useful realization must specify the source/target range, norm budget, repair topology, quotient, operator ideal, spectral type, functional calculus, boundary scaling, and direction of any positivity/order transport. It must then prove that the required discriminator survives in that exact category with a positive quantitative margin.

## Status / novelty

The underlying functional analysis, operator theory, semigroup order, fractional localization, and spectral theory are classical. The synthesis is the compatibility gate: **a mathematically correct representation can still point in the wrong category or direction for the target theorem**.

## Falsification criterion

Construct a source-forced category that crosses the current range/ideal/order barriers while preserving the target discriminator and matched-control separation, or show that one of the claimed category distinctions collapses under the exact hypotheses of a concrete line.

## Lean-formalizable core

- Finite interpolation polar duality and tensor accessibility.
- Trace/Schatten closure under bounded operations.
- Order-preserving semigroup versus non-positive inverse.
- Quotient/slack correction before spectral-tail estimates.
