# MI-005 — The topology and norm of the realization are part of the arithmetic claim

**Evidence level:** supported by exact topology- and norm-sensitive obstructions in several branches

## Core intuition

The same arithmetic or geometric signal can be invisible, compact, nuclear, recurrent, discontinuous, or unaffordable depending on the completion and norm in which it is realized. A topology can be too coarse, too strong, or simply diagnostic rather than convergent. The surviving category has to be derived from the mathematics, and the arithmetic selector must remain continuous or otherwise operational there; it cannot be rescued by choosing a topology after seeing the desired answer.

## Strongest justified principle

Prime Flute now resolves both a coarse endpoint and most coordinate-level assembly loopholes. PF-125 puts the exact prime flute and the all-composite `p_n+1` clone in the same compact-resolvent class, so essential spectrum is too coarse, while PF-112 excludes first-resolvent `S_1` for a nonisometric smooth comparison. PF-131--PF-136 give summable split-ray data, polynomial scalar moments, and a boundary-coherent strong-`L^1` extension through the long pre-corner narrowing. PF-137 shows that the stronger inverse-unit-ball wave weight is automatically harmless on every fixed ambient thick part. The unresolved topology is therefore tied to actual Margulis-thin geometry and global assembly, not to small internal chart width.

Weil Inertia supplies a different norm separation on one fixed deterministic object. WI-058--WI-060 show that the `W`-local conditioning spectrum is concentrated at subpolynomial conductor in squared `L^2` mass, yet any fixed polylog cutoff is insufficient for lossless capture and the sufficiently retained spectrum has super-polylogarithmic Wiener `l^1` mass. A route that is feasible as a collective square function can be impossible by modewise absolute summation. The assembly norm is part of the theorem interface.

Weil Positivity sharpens the point from continuity rather than size. WP-067--WP-068 recover the Mangoldt functional exactly on the algebraic Hardy shell span, but construct a `Q_H`-null sequence on which the functional stays equal to one. The selector is therefore unbounded in the natural positive Hilbert norm, and no finite scalar renormalization repairs the completed square. Information can be algebraically present but topologically unavailable to a positive realization.

Prime Lattice contributes the convergence side: natural completed-Weil moving bands can collapse, while the fixed-smooth second scale produces a bounded almost-periodic zero signal rather than a limit. Prime Circle gives the operator-ideal side: finite mixed Hardy words are trace class and canonically finite-section convergent while the finite essential algebra is universal. These are different manifestations of the same category gate, not one universal preferred topology.

## Consequence for synthesis

A useful realization category must satisfy four properties simultaneously:

1. the claimed arithmetic discriminator is continuous or otherwise well defined there;
2. the strongest matched control is not automatically identified there;
3. the norm/topology is compatible with the available global assembly estimates;
4. the category is independently forced by the construction rather than chosen to expose a desired singularity.

Relative `S_p`, higher resolvents, wave/scattering spaces, weighted metric classes, distributional forms, twisted determinants, or alternative positive completions remain candidates only after all four properties are proved.

## Status / novelty

All topology and norm separations cited above are persisted findings. Their cross-branch interpretation as a mandatory **realization-continuity gate** is a supported synthesis rather than a universal operator theorem.

## Falsification criterion

Derive a canonical topology from one branch's intrinsic geometry in which the relevant selector is continuous, global assembly closes, and the strongest matched control differs for a structural reason. Conversely, show that a proposed category either identifies the controls, makes the selector discontinuous, falls into a universal local obstruction, or merely repackages explicit zero data.
