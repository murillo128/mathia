# MI-005 — Topology, metric, target transport, range, spectral type, domain, and operator category are part of the arithmetic claim

**Evidence level:** supported by exact topology-, metric-, target-, operator-, quotient-, sampling-, spectral-type-, domain-, and stability-sensitive results across several branches

## Core intuition

The same arithmetic or geometric signal can be invisible, exactly recoverable but unstable, representation-dependent, confined to a completion, compact, Hilbert--Schmidt, nuclear, weak-trace-class, recurrent, rank-limited, pure point, continuous, nonclosable, or infinitely rough depending on how it is realized. The category is not bookkeeping after the mathematics: it determines which distinctions survive, whether structural targets descend under re-encoding, where representatives actually live, which matched controls are identified, and which determinant/scattering/sign theorem is even available.

Recent evidence sharpens two boundaries. Matching the same scalar dispersion law does not identify operator realizations with incompatible spectral type, and passing to generalized eigenfunctions does not repair the mismatch if the resulting map is nonclosable in the natural Hilbert geometry. Likewise, a locally exact metric repair may globalize only after canonical bidual relaxation; range fidelity is an independent part of the claim.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the source/target distinction exact. Distance-to-collision and safe-target-envelope results show that information-preserving refinements can still change structural target distances, while category-relative quotient repair can exist nonlinearly even when original-space linear splitting fails. AF-088--AF-089 show that compact compatible fibers can globalize local Lipschitz repairs with no loss. AF-090 then proves that any local Lipschitz quotient patch already globalizes with the same constant in the canonical bidual, forces local complementation of the kernel, and makes the bidual quotient split linearly. The remaining escape is not absence of structure after completion but whether the source-forced repair remains in the original range with the needed naturality and quantitative fidelity.

Prime Circle supplies ideal and support-complexity gates. Fixed-shell trace-class determinants have zero divisors too sparse for Riemann density; large-conductor Hardy limits classicalize into universal Hilbert/Carleman/Gaussian channels; every fixed-degree full-chord polynomial moment stratifies into finite-tuple singular-series data; and PC-154 shows that even a nonpolynomial `log det` resummation classicalizes at every fixed connected support size.

Prime Flute shows how functional calculus and summation order change relative theory without creating new data. Relative resolvent/scattering constructions can transport one ordinary phase or universal cusp thresholds, while complete marked-tail geometry can approach matched composite controls. Crossing a convergence boundary or subtracting a residue is a categorical move whose residual must still be shown arithmetic.

Prime Lattice separates multiplicative scaffolding from analytic completion. Beurling controls preserve the exponent cone while changing zeros; completed functional equations require added global reciprocal/Fourier structure; finite Boolean/Walsh modular enrichments can be genuine while still contributing only finite Euler factors to an already classical global analytic object.

Weil Inertia distinguishes algebraic leakage from extractable spectral leakage. Finite-period compensated motifs can hide in counting and moving-edge marginals, yet exact zeta mirror symmetry forces some reciprocal leakage already in the lower half of the support band. Without an amplitude and localization theorem against the complement that leakage is diagnostic rather than coercive.

Weil Positivity makes spectral type and domain explicit gates. The Prime-Flute Schiffer compression and finite-Weil operator lie in incompatible Schatten classes; continued DtN loses physical positivity; WP-131 rules out every nonzero bounded exact bridge between the pure-point solenoid Gamma carrier and continuous radial Gamma carrier; and WP-132 classifies the distributional exact escape as point sampling/jets, which is nonclosable from natural radial `L^2`. At the critical prime-power weights the sampler fails even square summability. A regularizing subtraction is possible only by changing the operator/covariance itself.

Xi Flow supplies the dynamical-scale counterpart. Finite collision jets are reproducible by polynomial controls; bounded local neighborhoods lose fixed-time memory on a shrinking scale; and the first fixed-time perturbative carrier is a mesoscopic Cauchy/half-Laplacian field with algebraic tails and an equilibrium defect of only `O(log^-2 T)`.

## Consequence for synthesis

A useful realization category must satisfy at least these compatibility conditions:

1. the target discriminator is present in the retained support and relational order;
2. the structural target descends through every admitted equivalence or lies in the exact safe envelope;
3. the repair lands in the source range actually required by the arithmetic claim, not merely in a convenient completion;
4. the discriminator has positive quantitative stability margin and the local repairs glue in the required topology;
5. the strongest matched control is not automatically identified by the metric, normalization, or quotient;
6. the operator ideal, spectral type, domain/closability, and functional calculus support the intended determinant, scattering, continuation, or sign theorem;
7. any category-changing map transports the relevant spectral measure/sign structure rather than only a scalar symbol;
8. limiting, singular, finite-window, support-growth, and regularity procedures are classified before their residual is called arithmetic.

Changing category can be a real advance. But every change must audit what becomes visible, where representatives live, which spectral type/domain is carried, how far the signal lies from loss, and which new theorem actually becomes available.

## Status / novelty

All cited stability, target-envelope, bidual-splitting, trace-ideal, sampling, functional-calculus, spectral-type, distributional-domain, and regularity separations are persisted findings with classical ingredients. Their interpretation as a joint realization-category gate is supported synthesis, not a universal operator theorem.

## Falsification criterion

Derive a canonical category in which a selector is present, target-compatible, original-range faithful, stably recoverable, control-separating, spectrally/domain compatible, and supported by the required global theorem. Conversely, produce a nonzero closable exact bridge across the WP-132 canonical radial/solenoid cores or a local Lipschitz quotient repair contradicting AF-090's bidual/local-complementation conclusions.
