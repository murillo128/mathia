# MI-005 — The topology of the limit is part of the arithmetic claim

**Evidence level:** supported by exact topology-sensitive obstructions in several branches

## Core intuition

The same arithmetic/geometric perturbation can be invisible, universal, compact, nuclear, recurrent, or nonclosable depending on the completion and topology. A topology can be too coarse, too strong, or simply diagnostic rather than convergent. The surviving category has to be derived from the mathematics, not selected because it exposes a desired singularity.

## Strongest justified principle

Prime Flute resolves both a coarse endpoint and several assembly loopholes. PF-125 puts the exact prime flute and the all-composite `p_n+1` clone in the same compact-resolvent class, so essential spectrum is too coarse. PF-112 excludes first-resolvent `S_1` for a nonisometric smooth comparison. PF-131--PF-134 then show that the complete one-dimensional Lambert split-ray mismatch, including its deep-cusp scalar tail and `O(log p_n)` cusp-entry transport, is summably controlled. The remaining question is a genuinely two-dimensional global metric/operator comparison leading, if possible, to `S_r` for `r>1`, higher resolvents, wave/scattering, or relative determinants.

Prime Lattice narrows the topology gate in two complementary ways. Natural moving bands below `log N=o(exp L)` collapse under RH, while order-one noncollapse gives an inverse rightmost-zero certificate (PL-066). At fixed smooth second scale, the natural object is not a limit at all: under RH it is a bounded almost-periodic zero signal. Its hull topology exists, but PL-069 shows that it retains only the zero frequency module unless one adds the evaluation observable that simply restores the original explicit series.

Prime Circle provides a different separation: finite mixed Hardy words are trace class and canonically finite-section convergent, but the finite essential algebra is universal; the remaining arithmetic sits in relative cyclotomic periods or a genuinely infinite completion. Weil Positivity shows that positive determinant-line information can disappear under full cohomological torsion or be converted to an absolute value by the canonical Krein repair.

## Consequence for synthesis

A useful topology must be strong enough to keep the claimed arithmetic distinction continuous, weak enough to avoid universal local ideal obstructions, independently canonical, and paired with an observable that has not already imported the target. Relative determinants, spectral shift, twisted torsion, mesoscopic smoothing, distributional forms, or higher Schatten classes are candidates only after all four properties are proved.

## Status / novelty

All topology separations cited above are persisted findings. Their cross-branch interpretation as a mandatory topology/observable gate is a supported synthesis rather than a universal operator theorem.

## Falsification criterion

Derive a canonical topology from one branch's geometry and prove that a zero-sensitive invariant is stable there while the strongest matched controls differ for a structural reason. Conversely, show that a proposed topology either identifies those controls, falls into a universal obstruction, or merely repackages explicit zero data.
