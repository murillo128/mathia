# MI-005 — Topology, metric, target transport, finite conditioning, range, spectral type, domain, and operator category are part of the arithmetic claim

**Evidence level:** supported by exact topology-, metric-, target-, operator-, quotient-, interpolation-, sampling-, spectral-type-, domain-, and stability-sensitive results across several branches

## Core intuition

The same arithmetic or geometric signal can be invisible, exactly recoverable but unstable, representation-dependent, finitely interpolable but globally unbounded, confined to a completion, compact, Hilbert--Schmidt, nuclear, weak-trace-class, pure point, continuous, nonclosable, or infinitely rough depending on how it is realized. The category is not bookkeeping after the mathematics: it determines which distinctions survive, where representatives actually live, which controls are identified, and which determinant, scattering, interpolation, or sign theorem is available.

Recent evidence sharpens three boundaries. Matching finite observations need not imply norm-level recovery; matching the same scalar dispersion law need not identify operator realizations with incompatible spectral type; and a positive kernel property in the native Euler half-plane need not survive, or even be the relevant category, after global completion.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the source/target/range distinction exact. Distance-to-collision and safe-target-envelope results show that information-preserving refinements can still change structural target distances, while category-relative quotient repair can exist nonlinearly even when linear splitting fails. AF-088--AF-090 show that local repairs can globalize in compact or bidual settings, but original-range recovery is separate.

AF-097--AF-098 add the finite-conditioning gate. Every bounded fiber operator can be matched exactly on every finite-dimensional source subspace, so the extendable range is SOT/WOT dense even when a positive norm defect survives. Uniform finite interpolation cost is the missing quantitative resource: it bounds a global bidual extension, and for `1`-complemented targets the finite and global extension costs coincide. Finite exactness without a norm budget is therefore a weak topology, not robust fidelity.

Prime Circle supplies ideal, support-complexity, and matrix-pencil gates. Fixed-shell trace-class determinants have zero divisors too sparse for Riemann density; large-conductor Hardy limits classicalize into universal Hilbert/Carleman/Gaussian channels; fixed connected moment hierarchies reduce to bounded-support singular-series data; and PC-160 shows that the intrinsic full-chord Bloch pencil is strictly accretive in its formal critical strip rather than zero-bearing there.

Prime Flute shows how functional calculus and summation order change relative theory without creating new data. Relative resolvent/scattering constructions can transport one ordinary phase or universal cusp thresholds, while complete marked-tail geometry can approach matched composite controls. Crossing a convergence boundary or subtracting a residue is a categorical move whose residual must still be shown arithmetic.

Prime Lattice separates native analytic category from completed zero-sensitive category. The standard HLS zeta kernel fails complete Pick on a finite two-prime face but has unconditional positive powers and compound-Poisson logarithmic geometry in its evaluation half-plane. Global completion changes the object: screw/CND/Lévy positivity becomes RH-equivalent and reduces, for the actual zeta function, to Suzuki's scalar completed sign. Native kernel positivity and completed zero localization are not the same category.

Weil Inertia distinguishes algebraic leakage from extractable spectral leakage. A single normalized Schur minimum can collapse from endpoint tapering while the true horizontal remainder stays extensive; the correct density-scale carrier is the depth-weighted lower spectral distribution.

Weil Positivity makes spectral type and domain explicit. Bounded exact bridges between pure-point solenoid Gamma and continuous radial Gamma carriers vanish, while distributional exact escapes become nonclosable point samplers. Canonical refinement responses also exhibit wrong depth laws even when positive or singular.

Xi Flow supplies the dynamical-scale counterpart. Finite collision jets are reproducible, fixed-time information is mesoscopic and Cauchy-like, and XF-016 shows that fixed-shape localization is itself critical in the same `H^{1/2}` topology as the useful bulk term.

## Consequence for synthesis

A useful realization category must satisfy at least these compatibility conditions:

1. the target discriminator is present in the retained support and relational order;
2. the target descends through every admitted equivalence or lies in the exact safe envelope;
3. finite/local repairs remain uniformly conditioned and glue in the required topology;
4. the repair lands in the source range actually required, not merely a convenient completion;
5. the strongest matched control is not automatically identified by the metric, normalization, or quotient;
6. the operator ideal, spectral type, domain/closability, and functional calculus support the intended global theorem;
7. category-changing maps transport the relevant spectral measure/sign structure rather than only a scalar symbol;
8. limiting, singular, finite-window, support-growth, and regularity procedures are classified before their residual is called arithmetic.

Changing category can be a real advance. But every change must audit what becomes visible, what finite conditioning is required, where representatives live, which spectral type/domain is carried, and which new theorem actually becomes available.

## Status / novelty

All cited stability, target-envelope, finite-interpolation, bidual-splitting, trace-ideal, sampling, functional-calculus, spectral-type, distributional-domain, and regularity separations are persisted findings with classical ingredients. Their interpretation as a joint realization-category gate is supported synthesis, not a universal operator theorem.

## Falsification criterion

Derive a canonical category in which a selector is target-compatible, uniformly finitely/global recoverable, original-range faithful, control-separating, spectrally/domain compatible, and supported by the required global theorem. Conversely, produce a category covered by AF-098 with bounded finite costs but no bidual extension, a nonzero closable exact radial/solenoid bridge in the covered Gamma setting, or a zero of the PC-160 pencil in its strictly accretive strip.
