# MI-005 — Category, assembly topology, operator ideal, information profile, and domain data are part of the arithmetic claim

**Evidence level:** supported by exact category-sensitive findings across the active Mathia lines

## Core intuition

A source signal can be present yet unusable because it lives in the wrong range, requires unbounded conditioning, sits in the wrong operator ideal or spectral type, needs a multiscale information budget that diverges under coherent refinement, aliases under the chosen sampling geometry, or collapses when the global energy/domain is assembled. Finite correctness at every cutoff or tolerance does not determine the infinite object.

The current evidence adds a sharper regularity gate. Even when two representations encode the same measurable source, a uniformly controlled decoder orders the entire fine-quantization entropy profile, while a merely measurable encoding can evade that order. Thus topology and ideal are not enough: the **modulus and profile consumed by recovery** also belong to the claim.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity now separates coherent exact information from multiscale regular recovery. AF-117--AF-119 show that bounded-information exact generation is a finite-entropy atomic phenomenon. AF-120 identifies information dimension as only the Cesaro innovation slope, and AF-121--AF-122 prove that Lipschitz/Hölder/general uniform-modulus recovery orders conditional information dimension and, more strongly, the full conditional fine-quantization entropy profile after the forced scale change. Zero dimension can still hide unbounded sublinear information cost.

Prime Circle gives a domain/boundary analogue. Full refinement covariance fixes local homogeneous coefficients, leaves only universal inverse-square domain fixed points, kills bounded Robin corrections, and collapses strongly covariant unbounded fixed Robin operators to four universal first-order circle parameters. PC-172 closes the finite-support singular multiplication-form escape as well: exact first-order covariance forces `u=c delta'_1`; atomic root weights vanish, the survivor is reflection-odd and universal, and it is not a semibounded perturbation of the canonical bulk form. Domain or singular boundary data can therefore be additional structure rather than arithmetic provenance.

Prime Lattice shows that observation topology matters after completion. Dense/fine sets can inherit RH-complete information, while fixed-step sampling folds zero ordinates modulo the sampling frequency. PL-160--PL-161 sharpen the quotient without removing it: lower boundedness excludes attained frontiers and one-sided boundedness pins any surviving near-frontier modes to phase identity.

Weil Inertia gives the changing-test regularity analogue. Fixed and regular-changing support-one scalar families collapse to Montgomery--Taylor, but WI-158 shows that the published pointwise pair-correlation error is not uniform on singular near-extremizers at the natural dual norm threshold. Passing a changing test through a theorem is itself a category claim.

Weil Positivity adds a global-assembly obstruction beyond finite rank. The canonical resultant graph is positive at every finite cutoff but spectator-prime bypasses collapse its all-prime finite-energy space. WP-150 shows that vertex-local normalization can restore bounded fixed-shell energy only by erasing every fixed arithmetic edge; normalized adjacency mass may instead escape to infinity, which is a different topology rather than hidden finite-core structure.

Xi Flow adds the norm of near-nullness. Finite-range scale-invariant shape assemblies have affine log-gap null modes, while translated source counting converts the quantitative borderline resource `M V_M=O(1)` into uniform and total-variation lattice rigidity. The topology in which source-valid near-nullness is controlled is part of the dynamical theorem.

## Consequence for synthesis

A useful realization must specify source/target range, norm and ideal resource, assembly topology, sampling geometry, coherent information profile, decoder modulus, and operator/form domain. If the final theorem needs exact generation, dense finite-energy vectors, a singular changing test, a point-supported boundary relation, or fixed-step anti-aliasing, those are load-bearing requirements and cannot be inferred from per-scale approximation or finite-cutoff positivity.

Changing category can be a genuine advance, but the new category must be forced upstream and support a theorem unavailable in the old one. A new measure, normalization, codebook, boundary domain, sampling set, singular coefficient, or decoder chosen after the target appears is additional structure that must be justified.

## Status / novelty

The component coding theory, functional analysis, network theory, sampling theory, distribution theory, and boundary/operator theory are classical. The synthesis is the compatibility gate: **the arithmetic claim includes the resource, regularity, and domain needed to assemble and decode its finite pieces, not merely the pieces themselves**.

## Falsification criterion

Construct a source-forced category crossing the current entropy/profile/domain/sampling/ideal barriers while preserving target discrimination, or show that one of the stated category distinctions collapses under the exact hypotheses of a concrete line.
