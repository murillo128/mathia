# MI-005 — Topology, stability, functional calculus, and operator ideal are part of the arithmetic claim

**Evidence level:** supported by exact topology-, norm-, operator-, quotient-, sampling-, and stability-sensitive results across several branches

## Core intuition

The same arithmetic or geometric signal can be invisible, exactly recoverable but unstable, compact, Hilbert--Schmidt, nuclear, weak-trace-class, recurrent, rank-limited, or unaffordable depending on how it is realized. The category is not bookkeeping after the mathematics: it determines which distinctions survive, whether they survive robustly, which universal controls are identified, and which determinant/scattering/sign theorem is even available.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity now makes stability geometrically exact. AF-040 shows that multichannel stability is collective rather than pairwise. AF-041--AF-045 go further: in Hilbert, Euclidean-linear, compact smooth, and general Lipschitz settings, robust fidelity is controlled by transversality or closed secants, and the lower fidelity modulus is literally a distance to the collision/stable-loss boundary in the admitted norm. Exact injectivity without positive distance is a different mathematical claim.

Prime Circle supplies both an ideal gate and a limit-topology warning. Fixed-shell trace-class determinants have zero divisors too sparse for Riemann density. Letting the conductor grow crosses that ideal boundary only through universal Hilbert/Carleman channels in the canonical strong and microlocal scalings; the off-origin compact microlocal defect is again trace class. Affine scaling and Möbius birth extraction add only unitary dilation/radical repetition. A change of topology or scale can recover escaped mass without recovering arithmetic.

Prime Flute shows how functional calculus changes available scattering theory while regularization does not create new data. Global trace class of the squared relative resolvent would imply a first relative resolvent at the sharp `S_2` boundary and still transport one ordinary Krein/Birman--Krein phase to the original Laplacians. Higher even powers give the same phase, the natural `det_2` adds no zero locations, the critical finite part is weight-dependent, and the entire local heat tower is area-only. The surviving information is necessarily nonlocal and value-sensitive.

Prime Lattice now gives a clean Schatten sampling phase. In the first growing-depth regime the normalized coherent Gram is `S_2`-universal at every observation horizon. The remaining `S_1` discrepancy is controlled by classical prolate time-bandwidth and finite rank: above Nyquist a universal rank floor is unavoidable, while below it a deterministic midpoint grid attains the continuum in trace norm. Failure of convergence in a stronger topology can therefore be just as non-arithmetic as convergence in a weaker one.

Weil Inertia gives the norm/source analogue. Two-dimensional source restriction is expensive in every fixed finite `L^p`; scalar projection removes that sparsity but positive/unweighted scalar energies are maximally large. Signs can help only before the positive large-sieve projection, in a finite-window Ramanujan operator whose complete-period blocks are orthogonal and whose pairwise leakage ranks are often already maximal. The theorem category must preserve exactly the signed source structure it hopes to exploit.

Weil Positivity gives the sign/coherence analogue. Positive finite coefficients survive many families, but fixed positive mixtures, finite multireference projection calculus, and refinement-coherent finite global couplings cannot generate the required logarithmic response. A singular or moving-angle category can, in principle, but then its scale, domain, and sign become new mathematical inputs that must be forced independently.

## Consequence for synthesis

A useful realization category must satisfy six properties simultaneously:

1. the target discriminator is actually present in the retained support;
2. it lies a positive distance from the relevant collision/control boundary in the topology used downstream;
3. the strongest matched control is not automatically identified by that topology or normalization;
4. the operator ideal and functional calculus support the needed determinant, scattering, continuation, or sign theorem without imposing a universal density/residue/rank artifact;
5. limiting, singular, and finite-window procedures are classified before their residual is called arithmetic;
6. the topology, norm, module, action, normalization, and singular operations are independently forced by the construction rather than chosen because they expose the desired answer.

Changing category can be a real mathematical advance. But every change must carry an explicit audit of what becomes visible, how far it is from loss, which controls remain matched, and which new theorem is actually available.

## Status / novelty

All cited stability, trace-ideal, sampling, functional-calculus, norm-restriction, and positivity separations are persisted findings with classical ingredients. Their interpretation as a realization-category gate is supported synthesis, not a universal operator theorem.

## Falsification criterion

Derive a canonical category in which a selector is present and stably recoverable, the strongest control differs, and the required global theorem closes. Conversely, show that a proposed category identifies the controls, leaves zero stability margin, turns the residual into a universal boundary model, moves the obstruction into an equally hard source theorem, or merely repackages existing zero/spectral data.
