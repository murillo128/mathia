# MI-005 — Category, assembly topology, information profile, operator ideal, and domain data are part of the arithmetic claim

**Evidence level:** supported by exact category-sensitive findings across the active Mathia lines

## Core intuition

A source signal can be present yet unusable because it lives in the wrong range, requires unstable or nonuniform conditioning, sits in the wrong operator ideal, aliases under the chosen sampling geometry, or collapses when the global energy/domain is assembled. Finite correctness at every cutoff or tolerance does not determine the infinite object.

The current evidence sharpens both recovery and assembly. A worst-case decoder modulus may be unnecessarily strong: almost-sure fiberwise regularity can preserve first-order information while the full profile is governed by a stratified resolution/entropy budget. Conversely, a weak finite-core operator limit can hide incompatible strong mass behavior under different exhaustions. The category therefore includes **how recovery cost is distributed over source fibers and how finite pieces are identified across scale**.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity separates coherent exact information from multiscale regular recovery. AF-117--AF-122 distinguish bounded exact generation, information-dimension slope, and full conditional quantization profiles under uniform regularity. AF-123--AF-125 then show that nonuniformity must be treated intrinsically: almost-sure finite fiberwise Lipschitz constants preserve information dimension, random resolution depth gives high-probability scale transfer, and the strongest current bound couples each fiber's required input depth to its own conditional mark entropy before averaging.

Prime Circle gives a domain/boundary analogue. PC-165--PC-173 show that full refinement covariance fixes local homogeneous coefficients, canonical domains, strong boundary operators, and arbitrary fixed distributional multiplication coefficients to universal structures. PC-174 identifies the larger weak continuous first-order class as multiplicative Toeplitz after `|D|^(1/2)` normalization and proves that every compact/Schatten member is zero. The operator ideal and covariance notion decide which determinant or spectral theorem is even available.

Prime Lattice shows that observation topology matters after completion. Dense/fine sets can inherit RH-complete information, while fixed-step sampling folds zero ordinates modulo the sampling frequency. PL-162 proves that finite-prime vertical phase identity recurrence is generic for the complete zero sequence, and PL-163 shows that the canonical positive first-moment carrier loses resolution at an interior horizontal frontier. Sampling richness and source-conditioned resolution are separate resources.

Weil Inertia gives the changing-test regularity analogue. Fixed and regular-changing support-one scalar families collapse to Montgomery--Taylor; WI-158--WI-159 show that pointwise form-factor control plus generic analytic/square structure is still insufficient on singular near-extremizers. Passing a changing test through an asymptotic theorem is itself a norm/category claim.

Weil Positivity adds a global-assembly obstruction beyond finite rank. The canonical resultant graph is positive at every finite cutoff but spectator-prime bypasses collapse its all-prime finite-energy space. WP-150 shows local normalization erases every fixed arithmetic edge, and WP-151 shows the residual normalized mass has incompatible strong behavior under different canonical subgeometries/exhaustions despite the same weak finite-core disappearance. A limiting topology is load-bearing data, not bookkeeping.

Xi Flow adds the norm of near-nullness. Source counting and `D_M=o(1)` can force Cauchy-like long-range geometry without recovering the microscopic flux budget; XF-040 then shows one exact periodic microcorrugation is dynamically damped. Static geometry, flux-variation topology, and finite-window dynamics are distinct categories of control.

## Consequence for synthesis

A useful realization must specify source/target range, recovery regularity and fiberwise profile budget, norm and operator ideal, sampling geometry, assembly/exhaustion topology, and operator/form domain. If the final theorem needs exact generation, a dense energy domain, a singular changing test, a noncompact weak boundary form, a fixed-step anti-aliasing estimate, or a strong limiting operator, those are load-bearing requirements and cannot be inferred from per-scale approximation or finite-cutoff positivity.

Changing category can be a genuine advance, but the new category must be forced upstream and support a theorem unavailable in the old one. A new measure, normalization, cutoff, exhaustion, boundary domain, sampling set, singular coefficient, or decoder chosen after the target appears is additional structure that must be justified.

## Status / novelty

The component coding theory, functional analysis, network theory, sampling theory, distribution theory, and boundary/operator theory are classical. The synthesis is the compatibility gate: **the arithmetic claim includes the resource, nonuniformity, domain, and limiting identification needed to assemble and decode its finite pieces, not merely the pieces themselves**.

## Falsification criterion

Construct a source-forced category crossing the current profile/domain/sampling/ideal/exhaustion barriers while preserving target discrimination, or show that one of these category distinctions collapses under the exact hypotheses of a concrete line.
