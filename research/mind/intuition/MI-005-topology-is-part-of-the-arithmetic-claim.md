# MI-005 — Topology, metric, target transport, functional calculus, and operator ideal are part of the arithmetic claim

**Evidence level:** supported by exact topology-, metric-, target-, operator-, quotient-, sampling-, and stability-sensitive results across several branches

## Core intuition

The same arithmetic or geometric signal can be invisible, exactly recoverable but unstable, representation-dependent, compact, Hilbert--Schmidt, nuclear, weak-trace-class, recurrent, rank-limited, or infinitely rough depending on how it is realized. The category is not bookkeeping after the mathematics: it determines which distinctions survive, whether structural targets descend under harmless re-encodings, which matched controls are identified, and which determinant/scattering/sign theorem is even available.

The newer evidence sharpens this from a topology warning to a **source/target/metric compatibility gate**. Even an information-preserving isometric refinement can change distance to a structural target if the refined target contains states that do not descend. Conversely, algebraic rank loss can be extensive while metric overlap remains bounded, and a positive measure can be equivalent to Haar while still having infinite first-order Fisher energy. Qualitative category labels are therefore too coarse; the exact metric, target family, and functional domain matter.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the source/target distinction exact. AF-041--AF-045 identify lower fidelity moduli with distance to collision/stable-loss sets in several natural categories. AF-046--AF-051 show that discrepancies and zero-error targets induce different repair geometries. AF-052--AF-054 then prove that Blackwell-reversible, TV-isometric cloning can reduce an unrestricted target distance because new refined target points fail to descend; the maximal safe target envelope gives the exact largest enlargement preserving every source repair radius. AF-055--AF-057 show that important safe-envelope classes are classical in Hilbert space but that equivalent product norms can disagree even on existence of a finite safe lift. Topology alone does not determine robustness.

Prime Circle supplies both an ideal gate and a limit-topology warning. Fixed-shell trace-class determinants have zero divisors too sparse for Riemann density. Large-conductor Hardy limits cross that ideal boundary only through universal Hilbert/Carleman/Gaussian channels. PC-120 shows that divergent real scalar amplification does not create a finite determinant divisor; it destroys `det_2` normality on the imaginary axis. PC-121--PC-125 show that changing from endpoint data to standard finite Toeplitz/CMV/resultant spectralizations still remains inside finite-state/cyclotomic categories.

Prime Flute shows how functional calculus and summation order change available relative theory without creating new data. Relative resolvent powers transport one ordinary scattering phase rather than independent phases; `det_2` adds no new zeros; local heat towers are area-only. PF-159--PF-160 now decompose the canonical Selberg `1/4` and `0` boundaries into one-ended propagation and the bottom Ruelle layer. Crossing a convergence boundary or subtracting a residue is a categorical move whose residual must still be shown arithmetic.

Prime Lattice gives both sampling and readout gates. `S_2` universality versus `S_1` discrepancy is governed by prolate/Nyquist and Poisson controls; the `log n` spectrum itself can encode arbitrary Dirichlet polynomials through a diagonal probe, so operator realization is not source specificity unless the probe is forced.

Weil Inertia makes the distinction between algebraic and metric leakage explicit. The finite-window scalar quotient may exhibit a sharp one-third prime-pair rank defect, but WI-092--WI-093 show that extensive defect forces only `O(1)` total squared canonical overlap. Rank, singular-value mass, and weighted cancellation are different categories of information.

Weil Positivity gives the regularity analogue. Exact critical one-prime moments admit finite positive mixed-prime completions, and correlations can even restore Haar equivalence. Yet WP-102 proves that every such critical completion has infinite cylindrical Fisher energy in the standard product-coordinate geometry. Measure class, positivity, and first-order regularity are independent gates.

## Consequence for synthesis

A useful realization category must satisfy seven properties simultaneously:

1. the target discriminator is actually present in the retained support;
2. the structural target descends through every equivalence declared information-preserving, or lies inside the exact safe envelope of the refinement;
3. the discriminator lies a positive distance from the relevant collision/control boundary in the **quantitative metric** used downstream;
4. the strongest matched control is not automatically identified by that metric, normalization, or quotient;
5. the operator ideal and functional calculus support the needed determinant, scattering, continuation, or sign theorem without imposing a universal density/residue/rank artifact;
6. limiting, singular, finite-window, and regularity procedures are classified before their residual is called arithmetic;
7. the topology, metric, target transport, module/action, normalization, and singular operations are independently forced by the construction rather than selected because they expose the desired answer.

Changing category can be a real mathematical advance. But every change must carry an explicit audit of what becomes visible, which target states become admissible, how far the signal is from loss, which controls remain matched, and which new theorem becomes available.

## Status / novelty

All cited stability, target-envelope, trace-ideal, sampling, functional-calculus, metric-overlap, and Fisher-energy separations are persisted findings with classical ingredients. Their interpretation as a joint realization-category gate is supported synthesis, not a universal operator theorem.

## Falsification criterion

Derive a canonical category in which a selector is present, target-compatible, stably recoverable, control-separating, and supported by the required global theorem. Conversely, show that a proposed category changes the target under an admitted equivalence, leaves zero stability margin, turns rank into a weak-overlap artifact, moves the signal into an infinite-energy domain, or merely repackages existing zero/spectral data.
