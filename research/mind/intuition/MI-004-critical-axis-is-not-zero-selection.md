# MI-004 — A critical axis, complete event carrier, vertical phase recurrence, or positive auxiliary geometry is not zero selection

**Evidence level:** supported by exact/classical mechanisms across Prime Lattice, Prime Circle, Weil Positivity, and current zero-distribution controls

## Core intuition

Mathia now contains several structures that reproduce pieces of the target exactly: Tate and compatible dilation give the half-weight, rational-prime phase data can identify the source, Suzuki's prime-power event sequence is RH-complete, prime phases of actual zeta ordinates recur densely in finite tori, and local geometric constructions produce genuine positive logarithms or Hessians. These achievements are not interchangeable.

The missing step remains a **source-forced sign, polarization, or horizontally conditioned coercivity on the zero-sensitive completed object itself**. It must exclude the off-axis alternative without deriving its force from an already RH-equivalent carrier, a classical normalization, generic vertical recurrence, or a positive object formed only after the arithmetic selector has disappeared.

## Strongest justified principle

Prime Lattice makes event encoding exact. PL-153 shows that one-sided boundedness of Suzuki checkpoint values is already RH-equivalent, while PL-159--PL-161 isolate the fixed-step lower loophole as an unattained phase-pinned off-critical frontier. PL-162 then proves that phase identity is not itself exotic: the complete zeta zero ordinates are recurrent near identity in every fixed finite prime torus. Vertical prime-phase fidelity therefore does not select the horizontal zero location.

PL-163 reaches one layer deeper. The classical Landau--Gonek prime-power moments already couple horizontal displacement and prime phase, and a positive harmonic Fejer combination is available on the full critical strip. But positivity from the trivial strip boundary exponentially damps an interior frontier; narrowing the boundary to recover frontier resolution makes the published moment error blow up. The missing selector is therefore not the existence of a horizontal/phase moment but a quantitatively sharper source theorem in the regime where localization is needed.

Prime Circle supplies a complementary normalization control. PC-165 identifies the compatible radial half-density `-1/2+it` with ordinary unitary dilation, while PC-166--PC-174 show that broad fixed refinement-covariant boundary structures classicalize to universal or multiplicative-Toeplitz data. A canonical half factor or large covariant boundary algebra can therefore appear without a zero Hamiltonian.

Weil Positivity sharpens the selector/sign separation. The zero-order cyclotomic resultant has exact prime-power support, but its canonical positive completion either loses that selector, collapses its energy domain, erases fixed edges under normalization, or leaves exhaustion-dependent escaped mass. Genuine positivity exists, yet it does not act canonically on the same arithmetic discriminator.

## Evidence against overgeneralization

A critical normalization, exact event representation, phase statistic, or positive auxiliary geometry may be necessary. The restriction concerns explanatory force: the final localization theorem must be forced upstream and survive matched source/control changes in the same object on which the sign or coercivity theorem acts.

## Status / novelty

The component mechanisms are classical or persisted exact findings. Their separation into representation normalization, completed criterion encoding, observation recurrence, arithmetic selection, and zero localization is cross-line synthesis.

## Falsification criterion

Derive the completed zeta sign, a zero-side polarization, or a horizontally conditioned anti-aliasing theorem from upstream rational-prime/global-completion structure without assuming an RH-equivalent scalar criterion and without losing the arithmetic selector before positivity is applied.
