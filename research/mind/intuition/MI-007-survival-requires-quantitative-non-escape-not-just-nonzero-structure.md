# MI-007 — Survival requires source-calibrated non-escape in the exact destination resource

**Evidence level:** supported by current recovery, decision-theoretic, spectral-screening, signed-shell, passive-closure, geometric-rigidity, statistical, Fourier-observation, and Xi-localization evidence

## Core intuition

Detecting or storing richer structure is not enough. Across the current program, useful information repeatedly disappears because the final theorem consumes a narrower resource: a reference-dependent recovery loss, a bounded radial degree range, a fixed finite local source algebra, an affine phase window, a finite-rank spectral screen, a passive Hardy scalarization, a geometric norm, a high-dimensional uncertainty ball, or a source-to-periodic localization map.

The strongest common rule is now three-part. First, **match the source certificate to the exact resource consumed by the destination** and quotient directions that the destination provably ignores. Second, preserve enough effective complexity -- scale, rank, parity-sensitive coupling, topology, identity provenance, or observation coherence -- that the final consumption step cannot erase the source discriminator. Third, test whether the proposed survivor can actually **activate** under source constraints; a formally retained nonlinear term is useless if trace, confluence, seams, or another boundary force it to vanish.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity makes both calibration and provenance explicit. AF-157--AF-160 show that a source-side Pearson loss tracks optimal common recovery only after choosing a recoverable reference and controlling its likelihood complexity; the best barycentric cost is the Shtarkov complexity times an exact convex-hull penalty. AF-161--AF-162 then separate two composition laws. Full Cartesian recombination multiplies the cost exponentially, while shared-identity repeated evidence keeps it bounded and drives the extra hull penalty to zero. Source identity structure is therefore part of the information budget.

Möbius Cancellation supplies the scale version. The persisted shell cascade through the established sub-log-log regime is positive and increasingly dominant, so a finite or sub-log-log radial carrier cannot contain the hard endpoint cancellation. A surviving theorem must reach the unresolved growing signed shell scale or use a genuinely non-radial source relation. The newer proportional-shell claims remain under an owner-persistence adversarial review and are therefore not used to strengthen this synthesis yet.

Prime Circle supplies the source-algebra version. Fixed finite polynomial products of anchored cyclotomic jets Mellinize into classical colored conical/Tornheim families. More local nonlinear depth is not a new arithmetic carrier unless the construction leaves that fixed finite source algebra.

Prime Flute sharpens topology versus norm. Generic strain does not force `C^1` chart entry, but PF-188 shows that on one fixed larger marked germ, vanishing strain does force qualitative `W^{1,r}` convergence even without boundary normalization. The live theorem needs the quantitative Sobolev modulus and fixed-germ confinement that the global collar sum actually consumes, not an unavailable pointwise derivative topology.

Prime Lattice now closes both ordinary affine averaging extremes. Diverging effective phase windows erase every bounded target; fixed positive-width bounded-center windows can flatten only if compact-support Fourier uniqueness already forces the zero-frequency arithmetic mean to cancel. A surviving affine route must remain pointwise/shrinking/unbounded-center or become genuinely joint, while the source side still needs parity-sensitive large-tail coupling beyond local sieve/Kubilius information.

Weil Inertia exhibits a joint survivor and its activation barrier. Distinct exceptional elements bound the spectral rank available to screen supercritical Gram modes, but WI-181 shows that unit trace imposes a substantial threshold before the tail can activate. WI-182 then kills a tempting free source-proximal gain by bounding it under the confluence-small remainder, while WI-183 supplies Maynard--Pratt bows as a slow-drift source control. Retention, activation, and source coercivity are three separate gates.

Weil Positivity strengthens category closure. Weak boundary limits, relation-valued Nevanlinna families, and ordinary trace-class Fredholm determinants all remain in the Schur/passive closure and cannot realize the Gamma phase. Regularized determinants genuinely leave that category, but the inherited passive sign leaves with it; a replacement coercivity theorem is mandatory.

The correct uncertainty geometry depends on what is being certified.

XF-073 gives super-polynomial relative recovery of Xi on safe interior high-line segments after Gaussian/Appell periodization and reference division. Analytic Frontier supplies the support-complexity analogue. ANF-075 proves that every fixed real-support cap can be escaped by narrowing the central notch, while ANF-076 closes the unbounded uniform-occupancy class exactly. The surviving scalar obstruction, if any, must combine **growing support with heterogeneous occupancy**; neither fixed support cardinality nor uniform replication carries enough complexity.

## Program consequence

For every candidate, identify the source discriminator, the exact destination resource, the operation that can erase it, and the quantitative theorem preventing that escape. Also state the minimum effective complexity that must survive: degree scale, spectral rank, heterogeneous support growth, parity-sensitive factor coupling, identity provenance, analytic category, spatial regularity, phase-window coherence, statistical claim dimension, or localization geometry.

Then ask a separate activation question. Does the retained term remain order-one under trace/population constraints? Does a local quotient stay in a zero-free domain? Does a recovery constant stay bounded under the actual provenance law? Does the source geometry exclude confluence or slow-drift controls? A richer intermediate object is useful only when its distinguishing information survives the complete chain **and remains quantitatively available at the endpoint**.

Before strengthening source assumptions, test whether problematic directions are destination-null and should instead be quotiented. Before enriching an intermediate representation, test whether the final readout collapses it back to a classical or low-complexity class. Before celebrating a formally nonzero correction, test whether an exact source or boundary constraint forces its normalized contribution to vanish.

## Status / novelty

The component approximation, information-theoretic, statistical, spectral, operator, geometric, arithmetic, Fourier, and transport facts are classical or persisted exact findings. The synthesis is the non-escape/activation gate: **mathematical leverage requires destination-matched quantitative control, enough surviving effective complexity to resist the lossy operation, and a source theorem proving that the retained resource actually activates.**

## Falsification criterion

Produce a source-specific final theorem that retains the desired coercive/recovery/sign conclusion despite losing the claimed destination resource and without compensating information; show that one of the asserted destination-null sectors carries order-one theorem-level mass; or derive a missing bridge proving that a currently demanded scale/rank/topology/provenance/coherence/activation condition is unnecessary. Any such result should narrow this synthesis rather than be forced into it.
