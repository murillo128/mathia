# MI-007 — Survival requires source-calibrated non-escape in information mode, metric, domain, assembly, and dynamics

**Evidence level:** supported by current multiscale, signed-feedback, metric-gauge, operator-domain, assembly, sampling, and Xi-flow evidence

## Core intuition

Detecting the right defect is not the same as being able to use it. A carrier may separate finite controls while its witness cost grows under composition, its discarded piece becomes cheap but the retained statistic still carries the target top mode, its spectral scale depends on an unfixed metric, its unbounded form is nonclosable, its positive scalarization destroys a cancellation-supported selector, its interface estimate loses common-mode cancellation, or its source-specific dynamical exclusion disappears before the final coercive observable.

The common requirement is a **quantitative non-escape theorem in the exact resource consumed by the final assembly**. That resource can be saturated witness complexity, a genuinely reduced target mode, a source-fixed statistical metric and score, operator domain/ideal, a signed mixed interaction, a bounded gauge family, or a localized low-frequency transport margin.

## Program consequence

For every candidate, identify the source discriminator, the exact resource preventing it from escaping/classicalizing/reconstructing the target, the theorem supplying that resource uniformly on the conditioned family, and the margin surviving the **actual final assembly**. Audit retained and omitted modes separately, and take source/control differences before norms or positive completion when cancellation is the claimed resource.

A new direction should not be credited merely because no finite control defeats it. It should name the source theorem preventing witness blowup, target-mode persistence, metric drift, gauge tuning, domain collapse, selector loss, interface accumulation, or downstream transport loss at the exact scale used by the destination.

## Status / novelty

The component information-theoretic, spectral, operator-domain, arithmetic, statistical, and heat-flow estimates are classical or persisted exact findings. The synthesis is the strengthened non-escape gate: **per-object success becomes leverage only when the source supplies the destination-matched quantitative resource through the whole conditioned family and every lossy operation in the assembly**.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a construction lacking the claimed information-mode/metric/domain/gauge/assembly/dynamical resource but still yielding a stable source-specific theorem without new information, or derive one of the currently missing downstream source-calibrated margins in a live line.
