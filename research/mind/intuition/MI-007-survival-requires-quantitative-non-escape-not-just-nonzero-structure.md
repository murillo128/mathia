# MI-007 — Survival requires source-calibrated non-escape in the exact destination resource

**Evidence level:** supported by current recovery, decision-theoretic, signed-shell, scalar-spectral, passive-operator, geometric-rigidity, statistical, Fourier-observation, zero-density, and Xi-localization evidence

## Core intuition

Detecting or storing richer structure is not enough. Across the current program, useful information repeatedly disappears because the final theorem consumes a narrower resource: an endpoint quotient, a bounded-complexity scalar profile, an absolute shell norm, a fixed local source algebra, an affine observation window, a finite-rank spectral screen, a globally normalized quadratic form, a passive scalarization, a gluing topology, a high-dimensional uncertainty ball, or a source-to-periodic localization map.

The strongest common rule is now four-part. First, **declare the exact destination quotient/resource** and do not charge distinctions the endpoint ignores. Second, preserve enough effective complexity -- scale, signed coupling, code geometry, rank, topology, provenance, or source identity -- that the consumption step cannot erase the discriminator. Third, prove that source constraints let the retained resource **activate and be extracted** rather than cancel or collapse. Fourth, verify that its normalized size is actually **visible at the theorem's scale**.

## Program consequence

For every candidate, identify: the source discriminator; the exact endpoint equivalence relation or destination norm; the operation that can erase information; the minimum effective complexity that must survive; the theorem that prevents cancellation/escape; and the normalization at which the retained resource must remain visible. These are separate proof obligations.

Before strengthening source assumptions, test whether the problematic directions are destination-null and should be quotiented. Before enriching an intermediate representation, test whether the final readout collapses it to a classical scalar class, an invertible coordinate change, or an absoluteized norm. Before celebrating a nonzero nonlinear/spectral correction, test activation and extraction. Before treating an extracted substructure as coercive, compare its maximum possible contribution with the theorem's normalization and error scale.

The emerging common mistake is to prove that information exists somewhere upstream and then silently identify that with information available at the endpoint. The correct object is **source-calibrated information that survives the exact consumption map, remains activated/extractable under source constraints, and is quantitatively visible in the final resource**.

## Status / novelty

The component approximation, information-theoretic, statistical, spectral, operator, geometric, arithmetic, Fourier, zero-density, and transport facts are classical or persisted exact findings. The synthesis is the non-escape/visibility gate described above. It is a research heuristic supported by current Mathia evidence, not a theorem about RH.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a source-specific final theorem whose coercive/recovery/sign conclusion survives despite losing one of the claimed destination resources and without compensating information; show that a direction classified as destination-null contributes order-one theorem-level mass; derive an endpoint theorem whose hidden-source complexity rather than endpoint quotient is intrinsically necessary; or prove that a currently subscale retained signal forces a global contradiction without a new localization/aggregation mechanism. Any such result should narrow this synthesis rather than be forced into it.
