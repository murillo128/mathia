# MI-007 — Survival requires source-calibrated non-escape in the exact destination resource

**Evidence level:** supported by current recovery, decision-theoretic, signed-feedback, metric-gauge, operator-assembly, periodic-witness, perturbation, approximation, and Xi-flow evidence

## Core intuition

Detecting the right defect is not the same as being able to use it. A carrier may be nonzero, coordinate invariant, source-native, full rank, canonically centered, nonlinear, vector-valued, arithmetically labelled, or separated by pointwise probes and still lose the information needed by the final theorem. The common requirement is a **quantitative non-escape theorem in the exact resource consumed by the destination assembly**.

That resource can be worst-case family recovery rather than a source-natural aggregate, a weighted relative operator ideal rather than local near-isometry, a genuinely joint invariant rather than affine vector readout, signed cross-degree cancellation rather than low-bias amplitude, control-only finite-object uncertainty rather than an invented target process model, or a destination-weighted collision-safe Xi quotient rather than every raw Vieta coordinate. Intermediate structure earns leverage only when the source supplies the margin that survives the actual lossy operation downstream.

## Program consequence

For every candidate, identify four things explicitly: the source discriminator; the exact destination resource that must retain it; the operation that can erase, average, scalarize, ill-condition, or render it null; and the theorem preventing that escape with a quantitative margin. Before strengthening the source hypothesis, also identify the destination-null directions: if exact controls show that a sector cannot contribute at the theorem's scale, quotient or weight it away rather than demand unnecessary source control.

A new direction should not be credited merely because a carrier is exact, canonical, nonzero, nonlinear, vector-valued, noncommutative, arithmetic-looking, or invariant. It should name the source theorem that blocks the strongest known escape **in the same category, topology, scale, and decision resource as the final claim**.

## Status / novelty

The component information-theoretic, statistical, spectral, operator, geometric, approximation, arithmetic, transport, and time-frequency facts are classical or persisted exact findings. The synthesis is the stronger non-escape gate: **a carrier becomes mathematically useful only when the source supplies destination-matched quantitative control through the complete chain of lossy operations, after quotienting directions the destination provably does not consume.**

## Falsification criterion

Produce a stable source-specific theorem whose final coercive/recovery/sign conclusion survives despite failure of the claimed destination resource and without compensating information; show that one of the asserted destination-null sectors actually carries order-one theorem-level mass; or derive a currently missing source-calibrated margin in a way that proves the stated resource was unnecessarily strong.
