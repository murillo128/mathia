# MI-007 — Survival requires a source-calibrated quantitative margin through detection, family coherence, coercivity, and assembly

**Evidence level:** supported by the current finite-conditioning, assembly, moving-comparator, confluence, refinement, and mesoscopic-flow evidence

## Core intuition

Detecting the right defect is not the same as being able to use it. A carrier may see the source discrepancy while every coherent realization requires unbounded norm, a moving family loses uniform constants, an exact sign invariant has arbitrarily small magnitude below every chosen smoothing scale, or boundary/operator assembly consumes the local margin.

The common resource is a **source-calibrated quantitative margin that survives from the finite detector through the actual family and into the final aggregate**.

Arithmetic Fidelity now gives an explicit assembly hierarchy. AF-105--AF-107 distinguish pointwise weak original-range non-escape, WOT-closed admissibility, and collective norm compactness. Per-stage compactness or admissibility is not enough when the limiting topology does not preserve it.

The missing budget is uniform in both cancellation and movement of the comparator.

## Program consequence

For every candidate, distinguish what detects the source difference, what uniform family/conditioning category realizes it, what source theorem keeps it away from its degeneracy boundary, and what margin remains after complete assembly. All budgets must be forced independently of the desired conclusion and uniform in the relevant scale.

## Status / novelty

The component estimates are classical or persisted exact findings. The synthesis is the strengthened non-escape gate: **per-object success, exact sign, or local positivity is not leverage without a uniform family and assembly margin**.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a construction lacking one of these uniform budgets but still yielding a stable source-specific theorem without new information, or derive the missing family/coercivity/assembly margin from the source in a live line.
