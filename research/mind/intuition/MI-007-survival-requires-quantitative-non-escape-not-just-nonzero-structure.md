# MI-007 — Quantitative non-escape must be priced in the source-selected complexity actually consumed by the theorem

**Evidence level:** supported by current source-normal-form, coding, screening, residual-localization, and selection-sensitive evidence

## Core intuition

Detecting richer structure is not enough. The final theorem consumes a particular norm, count, entropy, sign, transition modulus, or source-selected subsequence, and information can escape before reaching that currency. The useful question is quantitative: **what complexity is forced by the source at the required resolution, and how much of the final theorem budget does that complexity actually buy?**

Recent evidence also warns against equating representation size with information. A sublinear mark alphabet can carry almost full source entropy; `M/log T` screen rank can still be zero-density relative to `M`; an infinite exact selector can be noncoercive at every generic finite cutoff; and a global prime-race bound can be too strong even though a selected-subsequence theorem might remain viable.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity separates two coding currencies exactly. AF-225--AF-227 show that prime-tail proximity marking has an alphabet phase law at resolution `r_Q log Q` but an entropy phase law at `log(1/r_Q)/log Q`. Cardinality compression can therefore coexist with asymptotically full source-information rate.

Weil Inertia prices screening against observation width. WI-215 rules out fixed continuum rank; WI-216 finds a logarithmic threshold on linearly widened slabs; WI-217 forces rank `Omega(M/log T)` on fixed multiplicative width and asymptotically one channel per available unit translate. This is strong collective rigidity but still does not reach positive-density zero count.

Nyman--Beurling shows why source selection can beat generic complexity barriers. Generic finite harmonic probes alias and periodicity locates a residual witness only at exponential lcm scale, yet the actual best residual's single `g_2` normal equation forces a witness at `O(d_N^{-2})`, hence polylogarithmic index under the known distance lower bound. The relevant complexity comes from source optimality, not from scanning the full representation.

Analytic Frontier supplies an orthogonal source-normal-form lesson. Once realness, prime support, exact twist, magnitudes, and diagonal scale are matched away, the bow reduces to the combined `vartheta-Lambda^sharp` correlation. The missing factor cannot be priced by support complexity; it must be paid by source arithmetic coherence.

Robin Extremal gives the selection analogue. The normalized counterexample obstruction is a known mixed prime-race coordinate whose global one-sided control is already RH-equivalent. The possible weaker theorem lives only on the CA-selected boundary-prime subsequence.

## Program consequence

For each candidate, declare the source discriminator, resolution scale, exact destination currency, and the conversion theorem between them. Separate alphabet count from entropy, channel rank from zero count, witness existence from aggregate energy, and global bounds from selected-subsequence bounds.

Do not demand a stronger intermediate theorem merely because it is easy to state globally; do not celebrate a large lower bound until it occupies the final budget. A useful complexity theorem should close the quantitative conversion itself.

## Counterevidence / boundary

The cited laws belong to different categories and do not form one universal information inequality. A future source theorem can legitimately exploit structure absent from the free coding/screening models or use a different sufficient statistic. It must still state the replacement modulus explicitly.

## Epistemic status

The component facts are persisted exact or literature-backed results; the common complexity-budget principle is supported synthesis.

## Falsification criterion

Exhibit a final source-specific theorem whose conclusion is uniform even though its declared discriminator carries vanishing mass/entropy/count in the exact destination budget with no compensating relation, or show that one of the current source-selected complexity conversions is quantitatively irrelevant to its stated target. Either result should narrow this synthesis.
