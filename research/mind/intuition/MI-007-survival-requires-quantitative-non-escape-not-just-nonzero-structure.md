# MI-007 — Weak disappearance can reduce to drift, norm-controlled escape, or one generalized-kernel problem

**Evidence level:** supported by the cited line-specific mechanisms through NB-060; no universal equivalence among them is claimed.

Prime Circle gives **ballistic drift**: a refined coordinate can leave a fixed packet window while preserving its norm, and following the moving frame returns the channel to an already-classified quotient algebra. Weak invisibility there is relocation, not decay.

Prime Flute gives **quantitative escape exclusion**: the fixed physical high-pass spaces have a diverging local frequency floor, and the canonical synthesis has uniform positive-Sobolev control whenever its weighted source moment is bounded. A response with that family bound cannot maintain a local high-frequency witness.

Nyman--Beurling supplies a third mechanism. NB-057 makes every sufficiently late individual shifted block invisible to the canonical residual, while NB-058 identifies the collective loophole with finite-time defect sectors in the arithmetic defect space `D=A^perp`.

NB-060 collapses the whole finite-time family to one dyadic backward shift

\[
B_2=U_{\log2}^*|_D.
\]

The exact identities are

\[
K_{2^k}=\ker B_2^k,
\qquad
K_{\rm fin}=\overline{\bigcup_{k\ge1}\ker B_2^k}.
\]

In particular `K_2=0` iff every finite-time sector is zero. Any compact-time defect at any scale has a nonzero descendant already in the first dyadic window. Existence is therefore not an escape-to-larger-cutoff issue.

The collective survival question is instead **generalized-root abundance**. For the canonical residual `h_*`, the limiting late-tail mass is its squared distance from `K_fin`. A nonzero `ker B_2` need not make that distance vanish; the whole root tower must reach `h_*`. Conversely, if `ker B_2=0`, the collective late span remains the full defect space at every finite cutoff despite individual-column disappearance.

The reusable rule is sharper than “weak limits can hide mass.” First identify the mechanism: a permitted moving frame, a quantitative family norm that forbids escape, or a collective problem reducible to a concrete generalized nullspace. Pointwise disappearance settles none of the latter geometry, while a cofinal one-operator reduction can turn a moving localization question into a fixed kernel plus a root-space density problem.
