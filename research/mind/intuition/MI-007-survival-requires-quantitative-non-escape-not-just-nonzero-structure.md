# MI-007 — Survival requires source-calibrated non-escape in the exact destination resource

**Evidence level:** supported by current recovery, extremizer, signed-excursion, endpoint, checkpoint, coefficient-weighted resonance, alias-localization, positivity-orientation, and Xi transport evidence

## Core intuition

Detecting or storing richer structure is not enough. Useful information repeatedly disappears because the final theorem consumes a narrower quotient, gauge class, norm, scalarization, localization, exceptional-set geometry, discretization, normalization, checkpoint family, or moving-support limit. The strongest current rule is quantitative: identify the exact destination resource, prove that the source discriminator survives all universal reductions, and show that it remains usable at theorem scale on the actual source class.

Recent evidence also shows that “nonzero,” “signed,” “higher-dimensional,” “dense in scale,” or “outside the old theorem” is much too weak as a generic criterion. A surviving configuration can still be hidden by projection conditioning, poor degree-uniform inversion, endpoint concentration, coefficient-weighted small-divisor decay, a structured exceptional alias locus, positive-semidefinite orientation loss, or a singular continuum-to-grid or local-to-global interface. Conversely, an apparently strong coverage requirement can disappear when the exact target has a source-specific converse theorem.

## Program consequence

For every candidate, declare the source discriminator; strongest quotient/gauge/algebraic control; exact destination theorem and whether it has a source-specific converse; destination norm and normalization; source regularity class; coefficient or concentration weight; operation that can erase sign/coupling; theorem preventing quantitative escape; and any structured exceptional set, growing-support limit, discretization error, or reassembly step the final argument must cross. Conversely, the existence of arbitrarily small frequencies is not a theorem-level resource unless their coefficient-weighted contribution survives the actual support/window limit, just as local weak-trace membership is not enough unless its constants survive global assembly.
