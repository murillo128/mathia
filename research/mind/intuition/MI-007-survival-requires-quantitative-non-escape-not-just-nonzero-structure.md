# MI-007 — Quantitative non-escape must be priced in the source-selected complexity actually consumed by the theorem

**Evidence level:** supported by current source-normal-form, information-budget, screening, residual-localization, variance-pricing, and selection-sensitive evidence

## Core intuition

Detecting richer structure is not enough. The final theorem consumes a particular norm, count, entropy, sign, transition modulus, coefficient budget, or selected subsequence, and information can escape before reaching that currency. The useful question is quantitative: **what complexity is forced by the source at the required resolution, and how much of the final theorem budget does that complexity actually buy?**

The newest evidence makes two failure modes explicit. First, a large intermediate complexity can still occupy the wrong currency: a fixed-band screen may require `Theta(M)` free channels without implying an admissible `Theta(M)` zeta-zero population. Second, a representation can be compressed without reducing its information bill: deterministic and stochastic prime-tail localization share the same first-order source-information phase.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity now supplies an exact information-accounting model. AF-228--AF-230 show that separation and localization split source entropy exactly in the zero-error proximity graph and that stochastic positive-error localization has the same first-order rate-distortion phase. Alphabet compression and randomization therefore do not automatically reduce the source-information burden.

Weil Inertia supplies the matching complexity/accounting lesson. WI-218 forces `Omega(M)` screen rank across a fixed power-scale band, but WI-219 gives a matching `Theta(M)` relaxed screen and WI-220 shows that remote support alone is noncoercive. Rank becomes useful only after a theorem converts it into coefficient, multiplicity, local-count, radial, or source cost for actual zeros.

Nyman--Beurling shows a different saturation. NB-011 extracts logarithmic amplification from prime normal equations; NB-012 proves that arbitrary linear combinations, including composites, cannot improve that order because the Ramanujan large-sieve geometry fixes the optimal target-load ratio. More linear complexity no longer buys more destination coercivity.

Analytic Frontier replaces support complexity by spectral placement: after strong local-statistics controls, the bow target is near-nullspace alignment of the post-sieve innovation. Möbius Cancellation prices signed local variance directly against global Mertens power. Robin Extremal confines the plausible weaker theorem to a source-selected event subsequence rather than a global race bound.

## Program consequence

For every candidate, declare the source discriminator, resolution/observation scale, exact destination currency, and the conversion theorem between them. Separate alphabet count from entropy, screen rank from admissible zero count, normal-equation count from coercive gain, local variance from global power, and witness existence from aggregate target energy.

Once a free or linear model has reached a matching upper construction or saturation theorem, stop asking that model for a stronger constant. The next theorem must consume a source constraint absent from the control.

## Counterevidence / boundary

These laws belong to different mathematical categories and do not form one universal information inequality. A future source theorem can exploit structure absent from the free coding, screen, or linear-normal-equation models. It must still state the replacement modulus and show how it enters the final budget.

## Epistemic status

The component facts are persisted exact or literature-backed results; the common complexity-budget principle is supported cross-line synthesis.

## Falsification criterion

Exhibit a source-specific final theorem whose conclusion is uniform while its declared discriminator has vanishing mass/entropy/count in the exact destination budget and no compensating relation, or show that one of the current source-selected conversions is quantitatively irrelevant to its stated target. Either result should narrow this synthesis.
