# MI-007 — Survival requires quantitative non-escape across finite conditioning, scale visibility, spectral tails, and global assembly

**Evidence level:** supported by exact compactness, closed-range, finite-interpolation, transfer, refinement, spectral-tail, operator-domain, and mesoscopic-flow results

## Core intuition

A signal can be nonzero, algebraically recoverable, exactly present at every finite stage, or visible in a bulk asymptotic and still become useless in the target theorem. It may require diverging finite interpolation norms, emerge only beyond a smoothing visibility horizon, fail on a finite boundary-relaxed cluster, collapse into a small spectral sector, lose exact arithmetic scale under normalization, or dissipate internally while boundary leakage remains order one.

The common resource is **quantitative non-escape in the exact topology, scale, spectral distribution, and assembly used by the final argument**.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity makes finite conditioning explicit. AF-097 shows that every finite-dimensional restriction can be repaired exactly even when a positive norm-level extension defect persists. AF-098 identifies what prevents this finite witness family from escaping: a uniform finite interpolation bound compactifies into a global bidual repair, and for `1`-complemented targets it equals the genuine global extension cost. Per-scale exact witnesses without a common norm budget are not stable recovery.

Möbius Cancellation gives scale-information non-escape. MC-046 computes how endpoint-vanishing Riesz kernels delay a fixed terminal-prime defect into future scales; a bank of positive orders can remain subcritical throughout a long source-dependent future window while the ordinary sum is already polynomially large. MC-047 shows that a strong power-aware convolution metric sees that defect, clarifying that the missing resource is not generic detectability but a source theorem and transfer mechanism at the target normalization.

Analytic Frontier gives finite-configuration non-escape. ANF-016 passes the long-lattice/Mellin floor, yet ANF-017 fails on a finite edge-detuned cluster. ANF-018 identifies the complete finite-real margin with a pair-potential stability constant. Thermodynamic success does not survive unless the finite binding budget is controlled too.

Prime Circle and Prime Flute supply assembly controls. A nonuniversal defect can survive locally while canonical refinement, strip-accretive complete-lift geometry, puncture budgets, or an all-composite marked-tail clone remove its source specificity from the final operator.

Weil Inertia quantifies spectral-distribution non-escape. WI-133 proves that one Schur eigenvalue can collapse for a harmless endpoint-taper reason while total horizontal charge stays extensive. WI-134 replaces the scalar `lambda_min` with the exact depth-weighted lower-tail inequality `H>=a(D_{2,A}-A^2r_a)_+`: density-scale screening requires density-scale spectral collapse, not one weak mode.

Weil Positivity supplies exact-scale non-escape. A response can remain positive while its magnitude is stationary, extensive, bounded, or generically logarithmic. WP-139 shows that even the minimal singular puncture finite part becomes bounded and prime-blind after the intrinsic normalization; the universal nullity pole cannot be counted as the missing Mangoldt birth.

Xi Flow separates bulk from localization escape. The mesoscopic endpoint carrier has the right `h^2` scale and accepted inverse-square bulk comparison. XF-016 shows that a fixed-shape cutoff has an `H^{1/2}` leakage budget of the same order as the useful bulk term, even after centering. Fixed-time survival therefore needs source-specific signed flux control or a genuinely multiscale localization law, not merely a larger smooth window.

## Program consequence

For every limiting mechanism, demand a positive margin in the intended topology, uniformly conditioned finite-to-global coherence, a controlled visibility horizon across smoothing/scale changes, survival against finite boundary-relaxed controls, a depth/support-aware spectral margin rather than one weakest mode, exact arithmetic normalization, and an assembly estimate in the actual quotient carrying the target information.

This is stronger than information preservation. The data may be present while continuously uncertifiable, delayed to future scales, thermodynamically stable but finitely bound, hidden in a macroscopic near-null sector, prime-blind after normalization, or lost through external flux.

## Status / novelty

The component closed-range, finite-interpolation, compactness, Tauberian, stability, Bloch, clone, Schur-complement, pseudodeterminant, Orlicz, and fractional-localization results are classical or persisted exact findings. The synthesis is the common quantitative resource principle.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a canonical construction whose signal has no uniform finite conditioning, lies before its source-dependent visibility horizon, fails a finite stability control, collapses in the relevant spectral tail, loses exact arithmetic scale, or leaks through final assembly, yet still yields a stable source-specific invariant without additional structure. Conversely, derive one of these missing uniform margins from the arithmetic source.
