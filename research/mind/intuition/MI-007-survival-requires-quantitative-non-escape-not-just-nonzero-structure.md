# MI-007 — Weak disappearance can reduce to drift, norm-controlled escape, or a conditioned causal target leakage

**Evidence level:** supported by the cited line-specific mechanisms through NB-062; no universal equivalence among them is claimed.

Prime Circle gives **ballistic drift**: a refined coordinate can leave a fixed packet window while preserving its norm, and following the moving frame returns the channel to an already-classified quotient algebra. Weak invisibility there is relocation, not decay.

Prime Flute gives **quantitative escape exclusion**: the fixed physical high-pass spaces have a diverging local frequency floor, and the canonical synthesis has uniform positive-Sobolev control whenever its weighted source moment is bounded. A response with that family bound cannot maintain a local high-frequency witness.

Nyman--Beurling supplies a third mechanism. NB-057 makes every sufficiently late individual shifted block invisible to the canonical residual, while NB-058--NB-061 identify the collective loophole with finite-time defect sectors and show that every compact-time defect sector is infinite-dimensional. Abundance of defects is therefore not the issue.

NB-062 closes exact finite-window rank loss as well. If `J_R` truncates to `[0,log R]` and `G_R=J_RA`, the adjacent weighted differences

\[
D_j=(j+1)F_{j+1}-jF_j
\]

form a causal triangular basis:

\[
G_R=\operatorname{span}\{J_RD_j:1\le j<R\},
\qquad \dim G_R=R-1.
\]

Each new logarithmic cell contributes exactly one nonzero innovation. The arithmetic deformation relative to the flat control is not a missing direction but the **forward tail** carried by each innovation.

For the canonical residual `h_*=Qe`, global orthogonality turns finite-window target pairings into tail pairings. If `H_R` is the Gram matrix of the causal basis and `b_R` the tail-induced pairing vector, then

\[
\|P_{G_R}J_Rh_*\|^2=b_R^*H_R^{-1}b_R.
\]

Thus a moving visible space can retain target mass even with maximal rank and tiny tail data if its triangular conditioning amplifies those data. Exact nondegeneracy is not quantitative non-escape.

The reusable rule is sharper than “weak limits can hide mass.” First identify the actual mechanism: a permitted moving frame, a family norm that forbids escape, or a target projection through an increasingly ill-conditioned finite causal flag. Pointwise disappearance, nonzero kernels, and full finite-window rank settle none of the latter geometry; the load-bearing quantity can be the target's occupation after inverse-Gram amplification.

**Boundary.** NB-062 proves neither a uniform frame bound nor decay of the target leakage. The comparison among lines is structural, not an identification of their operators.
