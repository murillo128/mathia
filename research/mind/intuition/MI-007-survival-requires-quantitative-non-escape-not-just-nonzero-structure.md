# MI-007 — Survival requires source-calibrated non-escape in resolution, signed coupling, domain, exhaustion, and dynamics

**Evidence level:** supported by current multiscale, signed-feedback, changing-test, operator-domain, assembly, sampling, and mesoscopic-flow evidence

## Core intuition

Detecting the right defect is not the same as being able to use it. A carrier may separate finite controls while its witness cost grows under composition, its signed gain becomes target-reconstructing at the required resolution, its unbounded form is nonclosable, its normalized mass escapes under noncanonical exhaustions, or a source-compatible dynamical mode survives universal smoothing.

The common requirement is a **quantitative non-escape theorem in the exact resource consumed by the final assembly**. That resource can be saturated witness complexity, genuinely partial signed information, a source-fixed coefficient metric, operator domain/ideal, conditioned phase resolution, canonical limiting topology, or a localized low-frequency transport budget.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity makes witness/metric non-escape explicit. AF-134 gives the minimal backward witness saturation required for composition; if that family explodes, local small defects are not a useful global guarantee. AF-135--AF-136 show that target projection geometry is stable under generator gauge while source spectral scales require an independently fixed metric.

Möbius Cancellation supplies a signed-resolution version. MC-082 proves sign is genuinely missing from unsigned divisor-density controls, but MC-083--MC-085 show that several natural signed annular carriers become Mertens-equivalent once resolved finely enough to control the generic remainder. Non-escape must preserve cancellation without retaining enough coordinates to reconstruct the target.

Prime Circle and Prime Flute supply domain/ideal versions. PC-178's raw prime-axis resultant form is nonclosable, while Abel regularization loses it in strong resolvent topology; formal coefficient survival is not operator survival. PF-169 shows the opposite possibility: infinitely many local central blocks can have a convergent trace-ideal budget, pushing the non-escape question to the omitted global transmission terms.

Weil Inertia supplies the singular-scale version. Continuous detectors lose mass at confluence; Schur normalization cancels the horizontal singular scale; and a quantized projection jump costs more than the vanishing source deficit. A singular observable survives only if the zeta source itself funds the relevant scale or interaction.

Weil Positivity supplies incidence/selector non-escape. Fixed algebraic torsion families reduce to primary-separable cosets, while a real radial deformation retains Mangoldt only at zero order and loses it in the first positive local jet. The selector must survive the operation producing the final sign, not merely exist beforehand.

Xi Flow gives the dynamical resource. XF-047 shows that a memory-scale mode can survive fixed-time universal Cauchy smoothing while passing the source counts used by the earlier argument. XF-048 adds an endpoint source-specific low-frequency exclusion, and XF-049 gives a finite one-sided transport law. The missing non-escape theorem is now to carry that prime-free coefficient through infinite renormalized/tapered flow with `o(1)` error.

## Program consequence

For every candidate, identify what detects the source difference, the exact resource preventing that information from escaping, classicalizing, reconstructing the target, or becoming ill-posed, the theorem supplying that resource uniformly on the relevant conditioned family, and the margin surviving complete assembly. The budget must be fixed independently of the desired conclusion and measured in the same topology, norm, gauge, regularity, or dynamical category as the destination.

A new direction should not be credited merely because no finite control defeats it. It should name the source theorem preventing witness blowup, cancellation fragmentation, domain collapse, selector loss, exhaustion dependence, or dynamical replenishment at the exact scale used downstream.

## Status / novelty

The component entropy, character, Fourier, operator-domain, network, incidence, and heat-flow estimates are classical or persisted exact findings. The synthesis is the strengthened non-escape gate: **per-object success becomes leverage only when the source supplies the destination-matched quantitative resource through the whole conditioned family and assembly**.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a construction lacking the claimed witness/coupling/domain/topology/dynamical resource but still yielding a stable source-specific theorem without new information, or derive one of the currently missing source-calibrated budgets in a live line.
