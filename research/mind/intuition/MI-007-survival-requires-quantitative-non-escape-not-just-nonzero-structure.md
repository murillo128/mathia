# MI-007 — Survival requires source-calibrated non-escape in the exact destination resource

**Evidence level:** supported by current multiscale, recovery, signed-feedback, metric-gauge, operator-domain, assembly, matrix-source, and Xi-flow evidence

## Core intuition

Detecting the right defect is not the same as being able to use it. A carrier may be nonzero, coordinate invariant, pairwise discriminating, full rank, source-native, or separated by every pointwise probe and still lose the information needed by the final theorem. The common requirement is a **quantitative non-escape theorem in the exact resource consumed by the destination assembly**.

That resource can be a family-wide reverse channel, a source-fixed metric and score, a weighted relative operator ideal, an ordered multi-carrier interaction, a signed exceptional-population budget, a non-gauge-trivial operator datum, or an aggregate time-frequency norm. Intermediate structure earns leverage only when the source supplies the uniform margin that survives every lossy operation downstream.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity now gives the sharpest recovery-category warning. AF-142 shows that Fisher near-isometry can coexist with global aliasing; AF-143 shows that even global identifiability plus uniform pairwise TV retention need not give one good reverse experiment. AF-144 identifies a positive finite certificate: a common-reference chi-square loss controls one Bayes/Petz reverse kernel. The non-escape object is family-wide recovery compatibility, not a collection of local or pairwise metrics.

Visual Exploration supplies the quotient/sensitivity analogue. VIS-045--VIS-047 control bounded Fisher gauges, but VIS-048--VIS-049 show that an exactly gauge-invariant angle can be degenerate and first-order flat on matched controls. Removing coordinate freedom does not imply that the quotient retains the signal.

Prime Circle and Weil Positivity supply ordered and operator-category failures. PC-181 shows that after selector dependence has scalarized into one skew carrier, functional calculus and positive class functions cannot restore it. WP-165--WP-166 show something stronger: even a genuinely nonzero off-diagonal arithmetic carrier produces source-blind positive bulk energy when used as a one-dimensional skew-Hermitian connection, because parallel transport gauges it to the free derivative.

Prime Flute supplies the relative-ideal version. PF-173 cancels the absolute zero-mode trace obstruction in matched source/control recoupling; PF-174 then turns a weighted geometric defect into a smoothed Schatten estimate. The remaining non-escape theorem is a source-level weighted defect rate preserved by the complete body/interface assembly.

Weil Inertia supplies source-budget separation. WI-170 decomposes pair-correlation slack into off-line mass, twice multiplicity excess, and nonnegative source slack, with sharp coefficients. WI-171 shows generic PSD/Toeplitz consistency does not remove the positive-cover saturation witness. The next usable resource is the actual Montgomery--Taylor kernel/placement/pressure coupling, not another generic matrix constraint; WI-172 isolates a bounded formal test of that coupling.

Xi Flow gives the decisive aggregation warning. XF-055--XF-056 exclude every coherent slow mode throughout a continuous frequency cone, but XF-057 constructs a chirped matched control whose every individual selector coefficient vanishes while critical transition-side flux remains nonzero. Pointwise spectral exclusion, even uniformly over a continuum, is weaker than aggregate time-frequency coercivity.

Möbius Cancellation gives the top-mode version. MC-091--MC-093 supply a source-forced polynomial deformation channel, but exact factorization does not itself reduce the target Mertens mode. A signed norm or jointly estimated recurrence must change the top coefficient.

Analytic Frontier provides the complementary success case: ANF-061 removes the last noncompact common-translation escape from the Montgomery--Taylor five-point geometry. Once every geometric escape is quantitatively bounded, the residual problem genuinely becomes a finite certificate rather than another search for asymptotic structure.

## Program consequence

For every candidate, identify four things explicitly: the source discriminator; the exact destination resource that must retain it; the theorem preventing escape, scalarization, gauge removal, family incompatibility, or frequency spreading; and the margin surviving the final assembly. Audit source/control differences before positive completion or norms whenever cancellation/order is the claimed resource.

A new direction should not be credited merely because no finite control defeats it or because an invariant survives one quotient. It should name the source theorem that blocks the strongest known escape **in the same category and scale as the final claim**.

## Status / novelty

The component information-theoretic, statistical, spectral, operator, differential-geometric, arithmetic, and time-frequency facts are classical or persisted exact findings. The synthesis is the stronger non-escape gate: **a carrier becomes mathematically useful only when the source supplies destination-matched quantitative control through the complete chain of lossy operations**.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a stable source-specific theorem whose final coercive/recovery/sign conclusion survives despite failure of the claimed destination resource and without introducing compensating information, or derive one of the currently missing source-calibrated margins in a live line.
