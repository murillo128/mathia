# MI-007 — Survival requires quantitative non-escape, not merely nonzero structure at every finite scale

**Evidence level:** supported by exact cross-line compactness, conditioning, mesoscopic-spectrum, and reference-instability results

## Core intuition

A structure can survive every finite cutoff in the weakest algebraic sense and still fail to define a stable arithmetic invariant in the limit. Finite injectivity can lose coercivity, finite witnesses can escape every compact set, a nonuniversal spectral sector can move out of every fixed window, and a selected relative scalar can vary continuously under harmless reference changes.

The common missing resource is **quantitative non-escape in the category where the final claim lives**. One must control not only whether information is present, but whether it remains uniformly accessible, compactly representable, reference-stable, and separated from matched controls as resolution grows.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the abstract compactness model. AF-071--AF-075 show that compact-target consequence in infinite resolution is not certified by excellent finite witnesses at every scale. The exact gate is a compact-transversal/coherent-approximation condition, equivalently precompactness of the pooled witness family and, for bounded families, decay of Kolmogorov widths.

Weil Inertia gives the operator-stability model. WI-104 restores full source row rank by combining distinct residual blocks, yet WI-105--WI-109 construct families where the defect kernels become asymptotically parallel. The system remains algebraically injective while its lower singular value collapses; scalar, target-local, and boundedly conditioned invertible reparametrizations cannot remove the loss.

Prime Circle gives the spectral-window model. PC-139 proves that a mesoscopic family of macroscopic arithmetic defect eigenvalues survives universal bulk convergence, but PC-141 shows every fixed-width protected Fourier edge window classicalizes. The information has not vanished; it has escaped the fixed observation scale. A growing or provenance-adapted window is required before that survival can become a useful invariant.

Prime Flute gives the reference-stability model. PF-162--PF-165 show that a selected connected Ruelle cusp coefficient can be changed by the comparison reference, including compactly supported changes, and its residuals densely fill an interval. A nonzero, singular, or asymptotically computable scalar is therefore not intrinsic until its reference orbit has been quotiented or stabilized.

Visual Exploration supplies the representation control: VIS-001 shows that conspicuous finite patterning can be exact rank-one coordinate structure, while VIS-004 shows that introducing visible sign changes need not alter the aggregate convergence threshold. Non-escape must be tested in the downstream invariant, not inferred from appearance.

## Program consequence

For any growing construction, ask four quantitative questions before interpreting the limit: does the witness family remain precompact or have decaying widths; does the operator retain a lower coercivity/frame bound; does the signal remain inside a mathematically forced observation window; and is it stable under the admissible reference/representation changes? Failure of any one can turn persistent finite structure into a limiting artifact.

This is stronger than ordinary "preserve information before compression." The information may remain algebraically present while becoming arbitrarily expensive, delocalized, or gauge-dependent to recover.

## Status / novelty

Compactness theory, Kolmogorov widths, principal angles, spectral windows, and reference equivalence are classical notions. The cross-line synthesis is the common resource principle: **durable arithmetic information requires quantitative non-escape in its final representation category**.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a canonical Mathia construction that lacks all of the stated non-escape controls—escaping witness widths, vanishing coercivity, signal outside every fixed/admissible window, or unstable reference value—yet still yields a stable source-specific limiting invariant without importing additional structure. Conversely, a theorem establishing one of these controls from the arithmetic construction would identify a genuine surviving resource.

## Lean-formalizable core

- Precompact witness/width equivalence.
- Principal-angle and lower-singular-value stability.
- Fixed-window versus growing-window projection statements.
- Equivalence-class invariance under reference changes.
