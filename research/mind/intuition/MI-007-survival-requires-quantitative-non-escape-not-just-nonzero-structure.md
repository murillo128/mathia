# MI-007 — Survival requires quantitative non-escape after target rescaling and global assembly

**Evidence level:** supported by exact cross-line compactness, conditioning, microscopic-scale, marked-tail, and spectral-localization results

## Core intuition

A structure can survive every finite cutoff in the weakest algebraic sense and still fail to define a stable arithmetic invariant in the limit. Finite injectivity can lose coercivity, witnesses can escape compact sets, a fixed-depth exponent gain can vanish at microscopic scale, a nonuniversal spectral sector can localize into a classical short-range model, and infinitely many individually small geometric defects can accumulate only through a collective global object.

The common missing resource is **quantitative non-escape in the category and normalization where the final claim lives**.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the abstract compactness model. AF-071--AF-075 show that excellent finite witnesses do not imply compact-target consequence; the exact gate is coherent/precompact witness realization, equivalently vanishing widths for bounded families.

Weil Inertia gives the conditioning model. Full row rank can coexist with asymptotically parallel kernels and vanishing lower singular value, so algebraic identifiability without a quantitative frame bound is not stable information.

Analytic Frontier and Xi Flow supply two scale-collapse models. ANF-001 shows that a fixed-horizontal-depth power saving can reduce to only constant-factor information when the depth is `O(1/log T)`. XF-004 shows that an absolute-gap continuation budget gives only `O(1/log^2 T)` backward time at high zeros. A theorem must be evaluated **after** the target's natural rescaling.

Prime Circle supplies the observation-topology model. Arithmetic defect modes survive universal bulk convergence, yet short/growing chord windows already recover the first top-band projector scale and the first connected moments reduce to pair/triple singular-series data. Nonzero mesoscopic spectrum is not automatically a new global invariant; its residual must survive the topology actually used.

Prime Flute supplies the assembly model. PF-166 shows uniform asymptotic equality of the complete marked tail length function between the prime flute and an all-composite clone, even for arbitrarily complicated tail words. Any separation must come from collective infinite-orbit/operator assembly, not from an individual defect that stays nonzero at each finite stage.

Reference stability remains another non-escape requirement: PF-162--PF-165 show that a selected relative cusp scalar can wander through an interval under admissible reference changes.

## Program consequence

For any growing or limiting construction, ask: after the natural target rescaling, is there a uniform coercive margin; do witnesses remain compactly representable; does the signal stay in an admissible observation topology; is it stable under reference changes; and do local errors remain controlled after summing the complete global family?

This is stronger than ordinary “preserve information before compression.” The information may remain present while becoming arbitrarily weak, delocalized, gauge-dependent, or visible only through an uncontrolled infinite accumulation.

## Status / novelty

The component compactness, conditioning, asymptotic, and bilipschitz facts are classical or persisted exact results. The synthesis is the common quantitative resource principle.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a canonical construction whose apparent gain vanishes under the target rescaling or whose witnesses/operator modes/reference values escape all stated controls, yet which still yields a stable source-specific limiting invariant without additional structure. Conversely, prove a uniform microscopic/coercive/assembly bound from the arithmetic source.

## Lean-formalizable core

- Precompact witness and width criteria.
- Lower singular-value stability.
- Rescaling of exponent/gap budgets.
- Uniform marked-length comparison versus summation over infinite families.
- Reference-equivalence invariance.
