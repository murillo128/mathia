# MI-007 — Survival requires source-calibrated non-escape in resolution, signed coupling, error duality, exhaustion, and dynamics

**Evidence level:** supported by current multiscale, signed-feedback, changing-test, graph-assembly, sampling, and mesoscopic-flow evidence

## Core intuition

Detecting the right defect is not the same as being able to use it. A carrier may separate finite controls while its information cost concentrates on rare fibers, its signed gain disappears when pieces are bounded separately, a singular changing family probes an uncontrolled error norm, normalized mass escapes under noncanonical exhaustions, or a static source-compatible pattern is rapidly erased by the actual dynamics.

The common requirement is a **quantitative non-escape theorem in the exact resource consumed by the final assembly**. That resource can be stratified entropy/resolution, cross-block signed cancellation, test-function dual norm, conditioned phase resolution, strong-limit topology, or microscopic flux transport.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity makes multiscale non-escape explicitly fiberwise. AF-123--AF-125 show that worst-case regularity is not the right invariant: almost-sure local Lipschitz control can preserve information dimension, random resolution depth gives a probabilistic scale-transfer budget, and the sharpest current profile keeps required resolution coupled to conditional entropy before averaging. Global dimension alone misses this distribution of cost.

Möbius Cancellation supplies a signed-coupling version. MC-070 eliminates the remaining low-conductor positive-feedback corridor, while MC-071--MC-072 show that extracting a standalone inverse-kernel or dyadic-annulus bound already asks for zero-free information. Any gain must survive only in the **joint weighted sum**, so non-escape means preserving cancellation across the decomposition rather than controlling every term separately.

Prime Lattice supplies a conditioned-resolution version. PL-162 shows that finite-prime phase recurrence is abundant in the full zero population, while PL-163 shows that positive full-strip first moments lose resolution at an interior frontier. A source theorem must retain horizontal conditioning at the scale where phase pinning matters.

Weil Inertia supplies the dual-norm version. WI-158--WI-159 prove that the published pointwise BGSTB error remains insufficient on singular near-extremizers even after imposing generic analytic and square structure. A scalar gain must come with new arithmetic control in the norm dual to the actual changing tests.

Weil Positivity gives the strongest assembly-topology warning. WP-150 erases every fixed resultant edge under local finite-energy normalization; WP-151 then shows that the remaining normalized mass can have unit-norm escape or strong decay depending on exhaustion. Weak finite-core disappearance does not select a canonical non-escaping strong object.

Xi Flow gives the dynamical resource. XF-039 shows that translated source counting and Cauchy-rigid geometry do not recover the inverse-buffer flux budget. XF-040 shows that one exact periodic microscopic corrugation is nevertheless damped on the vanishing heat clock. A viable theorem must control localized generation and transport of flux variation, not merely its static amplitude.

## Program consequence

For every candidate, identify what detects the source difference, the quantitative resource preventing that information from escaping or decorrelating, the theorem that supplies the resource uniformly on the relevant conditioned family, and the margin surviving complete assembly. The budget must be fixed independently of the desired conclusion and measured in the same topology, norm, regularity, or dynamical category as the destination.

A new direction should not be credited merely because no finite control defeats it. It should name the source theorem preventing resolution blowup, cancellation fragmentation, error concentration, phase delocalization, exhaustion dependence, or dynamical replenishment at the exact scale used downstream.

## Status / novelty

The component entropy, character, Fourier-error, sampling, network, and heat-flow estimates are classical or persisted exact findings. The synthesis is the strengthened non-escape gate: **per-object success becomes leverage only when the source supplies the destination-matched quantitative resource through the whole conditioned family and assembly**.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a construction lacking the claimed profile/coupling/error/topology/dynamical resource but still yielding a stable source-specific theorem without new information, or derive one of the currently missing source-calibrated budgets in a live line.
