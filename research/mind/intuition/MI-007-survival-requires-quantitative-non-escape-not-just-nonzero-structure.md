# MI-007 — Survival requires source-calibrated non-escape in scale, family turnover, signed mass, and mesoscopic assembly

**Evidence level:** supported by current assembly, multiscale, moving-comparator, scalar-inertia, and mesoscopic-flow evidence

## Core intuition

Detecting the right defect is not the same as being able to use it. A carrier may separate all finite controls while its margin disappears on the source, remain in an operator ideal at every stage while ideal mass escapes, imitate the source with moving objects that cannot cover scale coherently, or repair local collision signs while the unresolved aggregate bulk has no uniform orientation.

The common resource is a **source-calibrated quantitative non-escape theorem matched to the exact resource consumed by final assembly**. That resource can be compactness, scale-covering complexity, conductor turnover, signed-mass budget, or mesoscopic coercivity; there is no single scalar notion of “survival.”

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity now makes scale non-escape exact. AF-114--AF-116 distinguish one-scale pairwise log-ratio tightness, finite `k`-scale separation, tolerance covering, and weighted description cost. A family can fail every fixed scale count without having large average coding cost, so the appropriate non-escape metric must come from the destination observable.

Möbius Cancellation turns family coherence into a power-scale resource. MC-062--MC-063 show that distinct good subquadratic quadratic comparators are power-separated, while MC-064 shows that constant defect does not transfer a Mertens power saving at all: the defect must decay polynomially and the conductor must remain in a compatible window. Per-scale existence without coherent coverage is therefore not a usable family theorem.

Weil Inertia supplies a signed-mass analogue. WI-151--WI-152 force the negative spectrum of a universally admissible bounded-depth scalar profile into a central layer with total mass `O(phi(0)/B)`. A surviving scalar detector must keep the central value large enough relative to depth or obtain a source theorem that prevents the collapse.

Xi Flow supplies a spatial/assembly analogue. XF-029--XF-031 show that a wide taper can make the explicit localization coefficient small, and at full nonlinear triple level the defect is exactly a taper difference. But the fixed-time theorem still requires aggregate alignment between two conductance Laplacians on reciprocal gaps across `Theta(log^2 T)` cores, with collision coverage and source-valid buffers. Local algebraic success is not yet global coercivity.

## Program consequence

For every candidate, identify what detects the source difference, which quantitative resource keeps that information from escaping, how the resource scales across the family, and what margin survives after complete assembly. The budget must be fixed independently of the desired conclusion and must be measured in the same category as the final theorem.

## Status / novelty

The component tightness, coding, conductor, Fourier, and conductance estimates are classical or persisted exact findings. The synthesis is the strengthened non-escape gate: **per-object success, local positivity, or finite-control separation becomes leverage only when the source supplies the destination-matched resource through the entire assembly**.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a construction lacking the claimed scale/family/mass/assembly resource but still yielding a stable source-specific theorem without new information, or derive one of the missing source-calibrated budgets in a live line.
