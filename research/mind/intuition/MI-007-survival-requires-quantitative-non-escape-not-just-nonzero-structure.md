# MI-007 — Survival requires a source-calibrated quantitative margin through detection, transfer, and global assembly

**Evidence level:** supported by the current finite-conditioning, stability, transfer, spectral-tail, refinement, and mesoscopic-flow evidence

## Core intuition

Detecting a defect is not the same as being able to use it. A carrier may see the correct source discrepancy at the correct scale while the transfer theorem loses a half exponent, the required comparator already contains the target theorem, the global witnesses escape the original range, or boundary assembly consumes the entire local margin.

The common resource is a **source-calibrated quantitative margin that survives from representation through transfer to the exact destination aggregate**.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity identifies conditioning non-escape: unbudgeted finite exactness is universal, but a fixed norm budget makes finite polar witnesses complete, and original-range assembly is a further tensor-norm gate.

Analytic Frontier identifies control-body non-escape: finite binding persists under dilute replication, so a thermodynamic survivor must also survive the complete finite-configuration/diffraction body. Local full-band shape matters, not only small-frequency slope.

Möbius Cancellation separates detection from transfer explicitly. MC-047 sees the terminal defect at target scale; MC-048 shows prime-only Cauchy transfer overpays it; MC-049 shows a broad fixed Liouville-like comparator route already forces the intended zero-free region. The missing resource is cheaper signed/source-specific transfer, not a stronger metric.

Prime Circle and Prime Flute show assembly/provenance non-escape. Fixed-base defects and even their nonlocal harmonic responses can converge to matched prime-blind limits; prime-flute tail geometry can be uniformly matched by an all-composite clone. A source label must survive the full operator/orbit assembly, not merely individual local data.

Weil Inertia shows distributional non-escape: density-scale depth can hide only through a macroscopic low spectral sector after multiplicity and free-screen slack are removed. Xi Flow shows boundary non-escape: capacitary taper can make geometric leakage small, but the gain is useful only if source-level span and endpoint-flux terms remain controlled.

## Program consequence

For every candidate, distinguish three questions: what detects the source difference; what quantitative theorem transfers that difference toward the target; and what margin remains after the full infinite/boundary/operator assembly. All three budgets must be source-forced and uniform in the relevant scale.

## Status / novelty

The component estimates are classical or persisted exact findings. The synthesis is the three-stage non-escape gate: **detectability without cheap transfer, or transfer without surviving assembly, is not leverage**.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a construction that lacks one of these uniform budgets but still yields a stable source-specific target theorem without adding new information, or derive the missing transfer/assembly margin from the source in a live line.
