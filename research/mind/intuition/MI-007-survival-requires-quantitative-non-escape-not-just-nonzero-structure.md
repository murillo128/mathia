# MI-007 — Quantitative non-escape must preserve the final decision statistic

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from endpoint-charge, affine-fiber, projective-defect, source-decoder, conditioned-quotient, transport-functional-calculus, form-inertia, amplitude-capacity, selector-recurrence, moment-capacity, automorphic-scalarization, and Xi-source boundaries

## Core intuition

The mature Mathia routes increasingly turn on a distinction between a mathematically rich proxy and the **exact statistic the final RH-equivalent criterion consumes**. Information can survive in a representation yet disappear from the primitive, sign, index, target distance, or root-rate decision statistic after quotienting or averaging. Conversely, a drastic compression can be legitimate when that decision statistic and its quantitative conditioning are proved to survive.

The central non-escape question is therefore not whether some arithmetic structure remains visible, but whether the target-bearing state remains quantitatively observable after the full source-to-destination map.

## Strongest justified principle

Analytic Frontier makes proxy failure exact. A matched source can satisfy stronger-than-any-fixed-log long-interval discrepancy while a short initial charge leaves an absolute primitive plateau of size `asymp sqrt(X log X)` and target-sized endpoint square energy. Relative increment control is insufficient unless it crosses the absolute gate `rho_X H_0=o(sqrt(X log X))`.

Nyman--Beurling provides the positive counterpart. Its weighted moving-tail quotient discards `N-M` directions yet preserves the finite-section target distance up to `1/M`, retains all low Möbius coefficients plus one weighted tail scalar, and has Gram conditioning `O(M^2 (log M)^3)` uniformly in `N`. Compression is safe because the target statistic and source coordinates are certified, not because the quotient is small.

Prime Flute shows why local operator estimates must be propagated through functional calculus. The naked seam's nonzero lowest Green mass gives strict sublinear leakage, but taking the square root produces a complete-Bernstein continuum down to zero mass. PF-274 then identifies the fixed-axis scalar operator with the square of a symmetric continuous-Hahn Jacobi operator, turning the remaining matrix-coefficient problem into an exact spectral integral without solving the full normalized leakage gate.

Prime Lattice sharpens the hierarchy from moments to full sign information. First/second moments of a Weil compression force a positive critical-line proportion but can miss `o(N)` off-line defects; Bombieri's finite forms retain finite-total defects through negative index; and the Connes--Consani semilocal Laurent core finitely detects **every** RH failure because its nested spaces are a core for the closed Weil form. Thus the completeness problem can disappear while the sign problem remains fully open: knowing that a finite negative eigenvalue must appear under RH failure gives no source-side reason it cannot appear.

The same pattern appears elsewhere. Arithmetic Fidelity identifies the affine fiber-collapse exponent rather than density as sparse root-rate currency. Farey Discrepancy replaces absolute Schur distance by normalized projective defect and finds a forced `4`-adic average gap. Möbius Cancellation finds a well-conditioned quadratic decoder but proves that affine centering destroys its principal coordinate when it removes the principal term. Weil Inertia couples selected amplitude to a spacing-surplus budget. Robin Extremal shows that finite dominant spectral packets themselves do not exclude the threshold cone: every nonzero same-real-part packet has recurrent cone-compatible phases, so the decision statistic must include the CA selector or genuinely non-finite spectral information. Visual Exploration proves that moment-null rescaling cannot beat uncertainty controlled in the same total-variation norm. Weil Positivity shows that extra automorphic channels can leave global arithmetic scalarized. Xi Flow finally shows that even exact source identification can remain noncoercive for the heat transition.

## Program consequence

For each candidate, specify the source information, every quotient/normalization/transport step, the final decision statistic, and the quantitative modulus by which source perturbations affect that statistic. A claimed simplification is progress only if it proves one of two things: discarded information is genuinely target-null with controlled conditioning, or the surviving source theorem is strictly narrower/cheaper than the original RH-equivalent obligation.

Prefer exact currencies such as primitive height, negative index, projective angle, fiber-collapse exponent, target distance, leakage norm, selector-conditioned phase, or signal-to-error ratio over generic claims of richness, positivity, cancellation, or spectral structure. Once a representation is proved complete for the decision statistic, stop optimizing completeness and attack the sign/coercivity/source theorem itself.

## Counterevidence / boundary

There is no universal decision statistic across all lines. A new representation may change the correct target currency, and a global identity can couple information that appears lost when stages are analyzed separately. The principle therefore requires deriving the actual composed map, not imposing one preferred norm everywhere.

A certified compression can still be mathematically useless if the retained source theorem is as hard as RH. Conversely, source identification or exact recoverability can be valuable infrastructure without being coercive. The claim is about what counts as progress toward the final theorem, not about intrinsic mathematical interest.

**Supported cross-line principle that current progress is controlled by preservation and conditioning of the exact final decision statistic: several rich proxies are now proved insufficient, several aggressive reductions are certified because they preserve the target-bearing state quantitatively, and one Weil family shows that complete finite detectability can be settled while source-side sign coercivity remains untouched.**

## Falsification criterion

Produce a valid final theorem in one cited setting while the stated decision statistic is not preserved or controlled by the source-to-destination map and no compensating source relation exists, or invalidate one of the exact information-loss/preservation boundaries on which this synthesis rests.
