# MI-007 — Survival requires source-calibrated non-escape in resolution, signed coupling, metric, domain, assembly, and dynamics

**Evidence level:** supported by current multiscale, signed-feedback, metric-gauge, operator-domain, assembly, sampling, and mesoscopic-flow evidence

## Core intuition

Detecting the right defect is not the same as being able to use it. A carrier may separate finite controls while its witness cost grows under composition, its signed gain becomes target-reconstructing at the required resolution, its spectral scale depends on an unfixed metric, its unbounded form is nonclosable, its positive scalarization destroys a cancellation-supported selector, or a source-compatible dynamical mode is replenished from an unresolved endpoint band.

The common requirement is a **quantitative non-escape theorem in the exact resource consumed by the final assembly**. That resource can be saturated witness complexity, genuinely partial signed information, a source-fixed coefficient metric, operator domain/ideal, a non-diffuse additive family, canonical limiting topology, or a localized low-frequency transport budget.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity makes witness/metric non-escape explicit. AF-134 gives the minimal backward witness saturation required for composition. AF-135--AF-138 show that projection geometry survives full generator gauge, compact symmetry still leaves a commutant metric ambiguity, and only an independently supplied source metric makes generalized spectral scale intrinsic.

Möbius Cancellation supplies the signed-resolution version. MC-082 proves sign is missing from unsigned controls, but MC-083--MC-087 show that natural signed carriers remain Mertens-equivalent whenever their omitted information is generically restorable at target resolution. Non-escape must preserve cancellation while deliberately crossing that reconstruction boundary.

Prime Circle and Prime Flute supply domain/ideal versions. PC-178's raw prime-axis form is nonclosable while Abel regularization loses it. PF-171 shows the opposite local success: the entire short central first-resolvent family already has sharp `S_r`, `r>1`, summability, so any remaining singular-value mass must enter through uncut global transmission rather than local proliferation.

Prime Lattice supplies an averaging-resource version. Addition is genuinely new information, but PL-176 shows complete degree-four cube averaging is already Fourier/Gowers-flat. A surviving additive signal must be source-forced before diffuse averaging.

Weil Inertia supplies the source-budget version. Continuous detectors lose mass at confluence; Schur normalization cancels the horizontal scale; and WI-168 proves that same-deficit integer depth satisfies `rank J<=4 Delta`. A macroscopic layer survives only if new source information funds it.

Weil Positivity supplies signed-selector non-escape. WP-162 recovers `Lambda(n)` as the total cyclotomic radial flux and classifies prime powers by pointwise flux positivity, but mixed-prime zeros depend on sign cancellation. Positive shellwise energies erase exactly the support one needs to retain.

Xi Flow gives the dynamical resource. XF-050--XF-051 make the endpoint memory carrier exact for complex/colliding zeros and in the infinite Xi system. The missing non-escape theorem is now only the quantitative control of the shrinking lower-positive band near `xi=0` over the relevant heat interval.

## Program consequence

For every candidate, identify what detects the source difference, the exact resource preventing that information from escaping, classicalizing, reconstructing the target, becoming metric-dependent, or becoming ill-posed, the theorem supplying that resource uniformly on the relevant conditioned family, and the margin surviving complete assembly. The budget must be fixed independently of the desired conclusion and measured in the same topology, norm, gauge, regularity, or dynamical category as the destination.

A new direction should not be credited merely because no finite control defeats it. It should name the source theorem preventing witness blowup, complement reconstruction, metric drift, additive averaging, domain collapse, selector loss, interface accumulation, or low-frequency replenishment at the exact scale used downstream.

## Status / novelty

The component entropy, Fourier, generalized spectral, operator-domain, network, incidence, Gowers, and heat-flow estimates are classical or persisted exact findings. The synthesis is the strengthened non-escape gate: **per-object success becomes leverage only when the source supplies the destination-matched quantitative resource through the whole conditioned family and assembly**.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a construction lacking the claimed witness/coupling/metric/domain/topology/dynamical resource but still yielding a stable source-specific theorem without new information, or derive one of the currently missing source-calibrated budgets in a live line.
