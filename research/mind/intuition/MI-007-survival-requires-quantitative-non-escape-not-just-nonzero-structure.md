# MI-007 — Survival requires source-calibrated non-escape in scale, family turnover, error duality, and mesoscopic assembly

**Evidence level:** supported by current multiscale, moving-comparator, changing-test, graph-assembly, and mesoscopic-flow evidence

## Core intuition

Detecting the right defect is not the same as being able to use it. A carrier may separate finite controls while its margin disappears on the source, imitate the source with moving objects that cannot cover scale coherently, pass every fixed test while a singular changing family probes an uncontrolled error norm, or repair local signs while the aggregate bulk has no source-calibrated compactness budget.

The common requirement is a **quantitative non-escape theorem in the exact dual resource consumed by the final assembly**. That resource can be entropy profile, conductor turnover, exceptional-family cardinality, test-function norm, effective-resistance/domain budget, or mesoscopic flux variation.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity now makes multiscale non-escape finer than one dimension. AF-120--AF-122 show that information dimension is only the linear Cesaro slope, while uniformly regular recovery orders the full conditional quantization profile. A source can have zero dimension and still require an unbounded sublinear information budget.

Möbius Cancellation turns family coherence into a sharply filtered conductor resource. MC-062--MC-067 already require power-scale turnover and simultaneous transfer/feedback control. MC-068--MC-069 show that the positive-feedback condition itself leaves only `O(log X)` prime-conductor candidates below square-root scale and at most the unique Landau--Page exceptional character through a quasi-subpower range. Per-scale existence has nearly collapsed to an exceptional-object problem.

Weil Inertia supplies a dual-norm version. WI-156 eliminates finite adaptive scalar portfolios, and WI-157 closes regular changing support-one families when the integrated pair-correlation error is uniformly `o(1)`. WI-158 proves that the published pointwise BGSTB error is insufficient exactly at the singular near-extremizer scale `||r_L||_1=Theta(sqrt(log T))`. A scalar gain must therefore come with new arithmetic control in the norm dual to the changing tests.

Weil Positivity gives an assembly-domain version. Finite graph positivity is not enough when spectator-prime conductance makes every effective resistance zero. WP-150 shows that restoring finite fixed-shell energy by vertex-local normalization merely pushes the fixed arithmetic interaction to zero; any surviving mass must be followed in a different nonlocal topology and justified there.

Xi Flow now supplies a positive source-calibrated spatial example. XF-036--XF-037 prove that once `M V_M=O(1)` is available, translated Xi counting forces uniform gap flattening and vanishing total log-gap variation on the super-mesoscopic buffer. The missing non-escape resource is no longer qualitative proximity to a null mode but the dynamical derivation of the borderline flux budget itself, together with collision-safe sign control.

## Program consequence

For every candidate, identify what detects the source difference, the quantitative resource preventing that information from escaping, the theorem that supplies the resource uniformly across scale/family, and the margin surviving complete assembly. The budget must be fixed independently of the desired conclusion and measured in the same topology, norm, or regularity category as the destination.

A new direction should not be credited merely because no finite control defeats it. It should name the source theorem that prevents witness turnover, error concentration, mass escape, or mesoscopic folding at the exact scale used downstream.

## Status / novelty

The component entropy, conductor, Fourier-error, network, and variation estimates are classical or persisted exact findings. The synthesis is the strengthened non-escape gate: **per-object success becomes leverage only when the source supplies the destination-matched quantitative resource through the whole family and assembly**.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a construction lacking the claimed profile/family/error/domain/assembly resource but still yielding a stable source-specific theorem without new information, or derive one of the currently missing source-calibrated budgets in a live line.
