# MI-022 — Unbounded compatibility can turn finite-cutoff ambiguity into a stability problem

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [AF-387](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-387-connected-support-does-not-repair-nonstrict-solid-pf3-recognition.md), [AF-389](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-389-multiscale-solid-pf3-excludes-all-finite-order-boundary-contact.md), [FD-164](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-164-one-horizon-complete-farey-field-leaves-dense-orientation-kernel.md), [FD-166](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-166-unbounded-exact-farey-horizons-rigidify-a-permanent-source.md), [RE-173](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-173-infinite-prime-supported-exact-mertens-compensation-survives-kv-fidelity.md), and [RE-174](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-174-unbounded-euler-temperature-samples-restore-infinite-prime-source-rigidity.md). The three mechanisms are different; only the compatibility/escape pattern is shared.

Several Mathia lines now exhibit the same sharp distinction between **finite-cutoff freedom** and **one latent object constrained coherently across an unbounded hierarchy**.

Arithmetic Fidelity supplies the smooth geometric version. At one lattice, lower-order degeneracy can let every solid order-three Toeplitz minor be nonnegative while a gapped minor is negative. If the same smooth profile must satisfy the solid `PF_2/PF_3` inequalities on meshes tending to zero, AF-389 eliminates every finite-order contact with the lower-order boundary: any zero of `q=-(log f)''` must be infinitely flat. The ambiguity has not vanished completely, but it has been pushed from finite Taylor data into an infinitely-flat stratum.

Farey Discrepancy supplies the discrete source version. At every finite cutoff, and even across large finite horizon bundles, exact matched controls can move a dense set of high Möbius orientations. Yet one permanent source matching complete fields on any unbounded horizon set is rigid, because each horizon reveals a consecutive coefficient prefix of length `sqrt(H)` and those prefixes exhaust every fixed coordinate. The finite kernels survive only by moving their defects outward with the cutoff.

Robin Extremal supplies the analytic-source analogue. One exact Mertens boundary charge plus strong KV fidelity admits an infinite delete/duplicate prime tail that creates and later repays a Robin excursion. But an unbounded sequence of exact Euler temperatures determines every bounded prime multiplicity: high temperature exposes the smallest changed Euler factor. Again the noncompact finite/coarse ambiguity disappears once one fixed source must satisfy an unbounded compatible family.

The common lesson is not that “more scales solve the problem.” **Compatibility changes the compactness structure of the source fibre.** An unbounded hierarchy can force every fixed finite defect to reveal itself, while finite-cutoff controls evade detection by pushing the defect toward infinity, into an infinitely-flat boundary layer, or into another noncompact tail.

This shifts the mathematical frontier rather than closing it. Exact hierarchy rigidity may be useless at the destination if its inverse is badly conditioned. RE-174 detects a selector-scale prime defect through a signal `P^(-sigma)`; FD-166 leaves the conditioning of the exact Farey inversion open; AF-389 leaves the infinitely-flat smooth boundary open. After exact compatibility has removed fixed finite defects, the next question is the **stability modulus against the remaining escaping defect class**.

A reusable audit is therefore: identify the latent object shared across scales; classify how finite-cutoff counterexamples are allowed to move with the cutoff; prove whether unbounded compatibility fixes each finite coordinate/jet; and then quantify the inverse modulus at the destination's moving scale. Exact identification without the final stability step is source rigidity, not yet RH leverage.

**Boundary.** This is not a transfer theorem between Toeplitz total positivity, Farey inversion and Euler products. AF uses differential determinant asymptotics, FD uses quotient-set prefix recovery, and RE uses Dirichlet-series coefficient extraction. The synthesis claims only a common information geometry: coherent unbounded observations can kill finite-cutoff ambiguity by forbidding defect escape at fixed coordinates, while quantitative stability remains line-specific.
