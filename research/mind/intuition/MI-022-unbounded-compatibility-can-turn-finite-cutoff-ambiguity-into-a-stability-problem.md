# MI-022 — Unbounded compatibility can turn finite-cutoff ambiguity into a source-class and stability problem

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [AF-387](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-387-connected-support-does-not-repair-nonstrict-solid-pf3-recognition.md), [AF-389](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-389-multiscale-solid-pf3-excludes-all-finite-order-boundary-contact.md), [AF-390](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-390-quasianalytic-curvature-closes-multiscale-pf3-recognition.md), [FD-164](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-164-one-farey-horizon-leaves-dense-coefficient-orientations-undetermined.md), [FD-165](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-165-polylogarithmically-many-farey-horizons-still-leave-dense-orientations-undetermined.md), [FD-166](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-166-unbounded-exact-farey-horizons-rigidify-a-permanent-source.md), [FD-167](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-167-full-field-l2-inversion-has-uniform-square-root-prefix-conditioning.md), [RE-173](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-173-infinite-prime-supported-exact-mertens-compensation-survives-kv-fidelity.md), and [RE-174](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-174-unbounded-euler-temperature-samples-restore-infinite-prime-source-rigidity.md). The mechanisms differ; only the compatibility/escape pattern is shared.

Several Mathia lines exhibit the same distinction between **finite-cutoff freedom** and **one latent object constrained coherently across an unbounded hierarchy**, but the fate of the residual ambiguity is line-dependent.

Arithmetic Fidelity supplies the smooth geometric version. At one lattice, lower-order degeneracy can let every solid order-three Toeplitz minor be nonnegative while a gapped minor is negative. If the same smooth profile must satisfy the solid `PF_2/PF_3` inequalities on meshes tending to zero, AF-389 eliminates every finite-order contact with the lower-order boundary: any zero of `q=-(log f)''` must be infinitely flat. AF-390 then shows that a source class with flat-jet unique continuation collapses even that residual stratum and yields continuum `TN_3`. The hierarchy recovers the jet; the source category decides whether the jet uniquely determines the relevant global branch.

Farey Discrepancy supplies the discrete source version. At every finite cutoff, and even across large finite horizon bundles, exact matched controls can move a dense set of high Möbius orientations. One permanent source matching complete fields on any unbounded horizon set is rigid because each horizon reveals a consecutive coefficient prefix of length `sqrt(H)`. FD-167 further shows that the complete-field inverse on that prefix is uniformly conditioned in `L^2`; the finite kernels escape outward rather than becoming invisibly close in the native full-field norm.

Robin Extremal supplies the analytic-source contrast. One exact Mertens boundary charge plus strong KV fidelity admits an infinite delete/duplicate prime tail, but an unbounded sequence of exact Euler temperatures determines every bounded prime multiplicity. Here the inverse signal of the first defect at prime `P` is only of scale `P^(-sigma)`, so exact hierarchy rigidity can coexist with severe destination-relevant ill-conditioning.

The common lesson is not that “more scales solve the problem.” **Compatibility changes the compactness structure of the source fibre; the next obstruction is then determined by the admissible source class and the inverse modulus of the observation.** A finite-cutoff control may survive by pushing defects outward, into an infinitely-flat boundary layer or into another noncompact tail. An unbounded hierarchy may kill every fixed defect, but that can lead to a uniform inverse theorem, a quasianalytic uniqueness gate, or an exponentially weak signal.

A reusable audit is therefore: identify the latent object shared across scales; classify how finite-cutoff counterexamples move with the cutoff; determine exactly what finite coordinates or jets the compatible hierarchy recovers; state the source-class principle, if any, that turns those data into global rigidity; and quantify the inverse modulus in the destination's native observation norm. Exact identification alone is not RH leverage, but neither should bad conditioning be assumed once exact rigidity is known — FD-167 shows it can be uniformly good.

**Boundary.** This is not a transfer theorem between Toeplitz total positivity, Farey inversion and Euler products. AF uses differential determinant asymptotics plus source-class unique continuation, FD uses divisor/Fourier inversion and a discrete source lattice, and RE uses Dirichlet-series coefficient extraction. The synthesis claims only a common information geometry and the need to compute, rather than guess, the residual source-class and inverse-conditioning cost.
