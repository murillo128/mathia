# MI-037 — Only intrinsic destination budgets count as surviving source resources

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis of [Arithmetic Fidelity MI-075](../../arithmetic_fidelity/mind/intuition/MI-075-bounded-translation-fibres-not-horizon-membership-set-the-fixed-noise-alias-threshold.md), [Farey MI-060](../../farey_discrepancy/mind/intuition/MI-060-finite-mellin-packets-cannot-supply-the-missing-divisor-depth-exponent.md), [Möbius MI-095](../../mobius_cancellation/mind/intuition/MI-095-independent-factor-residues-disappear-under-complete-fixed-conductor-completion.md), [Prime-Lattice MI-062](../../prime_lattice/mind/intuition/MI-062-raw-and-exactly-centered-residue-grams-both-miss-the-dephasing-positivity-cone.md), [visual MI-067](../../visual_exploration/mind/intuition/MI-067-rational-orientation-is-priced-by-intrinsic-derivative-not-syntax-conditioning.md), and [Weil-inertia MI-072](../../weil_inertia/mind/intuition/MI-072-the-square-root-fourier-budget-becomes-horizontally-blind-in-zero-density.md), sharpening MI-030 and MI-036.

A recurring Mathia failure mode is to assign leverage to a quantity before applying the exact reduction and norm used by the destination theorem. The newest local results show that this is not only a quotient issue. Even information that survives formally can become useless because the destination sees it through a stricter asymptotic layer, image cone, completion, derivative norm, or weighted source budget.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the cleanest scale separation. Horizon membership records only normalized `O(t)` geometry; the fixed-noise theorem is controlled instead by the bounded translation fibre and its distance from the origin. Farey Discrepancy gives the analogous amplification separation: a fixed Mellin zero packet may contain genuine arithmetic frequencies, yet consecutive differencing followed by exact divisor replication leaves only linear depth growth, below the destination's `D^(2-beta)` threshold. Source information is present in both cases, but not in the scale consumed by the final obstruction.

Möbius Cancellation and Prime Lattice show two different ways formal retention can disappear. A sieve factor kept as an independent coprime residue label is annihilated on complete fixed-conductor blocks by CRT tensorization, so the factor matters only if a non-tensor transform uses it before completion. In Prime Lattice the full raw residue matrix is retained, but short-AP equidistribution drives its normalized Gram to rank one, outside the exact positive image cone required for local-factor deletion. Exact common-mode centering does not repair that mismatch: it creates an exact principal-channel zero mode, while the same image cone requires a strictly positive normalized floor. The viable direct covariance question is therefore deterministic model subtraction that preserves a nonzero principal fluctuation while meeting the image-cone bound, not “more matrix data” or “center it” in the abstract.

Visual Exploration isolates the correct notion of amplification. Large intermediate inverse norms in one rational expression are not intrinsic because rational identities can remove them. The invariant budget is the Fréchet derivative of the represented rational function along the Gram-generated similarity tangent. Weil Inertia supplies the source-side analogue: coherent scalar shaping is measured by the square-root weighted Fourier norm that also pays the absolute prime term, and after zero density is inserted the horizontal zero signal per unit of that norm tends to zero. A useful signed-prime escape must therefore improve the actual arithmetic evaluation relative to that intrinsic budget, not merely rearrange the same Fourier mass.

The common audit is consequently: first identify the exact destination-visible object, then identify the intrinsic scale or norm in which the final theorem charges it, and only then count source complexity, multiplicity, conditioning, coherence or amplitude. A proposed resource is not mathematical leverage if it lives only in a coarser horizon, an expression tree, a pre-completion label, an ambient positive cone, an unnormalized common mode, an exactly projected singular subspace, or an absolute envelope that the destination immediately quotients or overwhelms.

This sharpens rather than replaces MI-036. Reachability and quotient survival remain necessary, but they are not sufficient. **A source feature counts only when it survives in the intrinsic destination budget at the quantitative scale needed by the final theorem.** The surviving escape can therefore be positive — a pre-collapse fibre-separating transform, a model-subtracted residual that remains inside the admissible image, genuine function-level sensitivity, or signed arithmetic cancellation — but its gain must be measured after the same reduction that certifies the destination.

**Boundary.** The six local budgets above are mathematically different: translation fibres, divisor-depth exponents, CRT completion, dephasing image cones, rational derivatives and weighted Fourier norms are not instances of one universal Banach-space theorem. The global claim is only the evidence-backed proof discipline that representation-level currencies must be pushed through the exact destination interface before they are credited. None of the local results by itself supplies the missing arithmetic estimate or an RH consequence.
