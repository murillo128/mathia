A recurring Mathia failure mode is to assign leverage to a quantity before applying the exact reduction, topology and norm used by the destination theorem. The newest local results show that this is not only a quotient issue. Even information that survives formally can become useless because the destination sees it through a stricter asymptotic layer, image cone, completion, derivative norm, robustness metric, sign interface or weighted source budget.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives two complementary scale separations. Horizon membership records only normalized `O(t)` geometry, whereas the fixed-noise theorem is controlled by the bounded translation fibre and its distance from the origin. More sharply, AF-509--AF-511 show that the whole curvature profile can classify a simple source exactly, and primitive integer support can fix the dilation gauge, while remote prime-to-composite edits still approach the prime source arbitrarily closely in `sup_(s>=sigma)|Delta log kappa|` for every fixed `sigma>1`. Exact source information therefore survives, but prime provenance has no positive margin in that topology. A robust claim must price the inverse in the metric of the claimed decision, not infer robustness from injectivity.

Farey Discrepancy gives the analogous sign-and-amplification separation. A fixed Mellin zero packet may contain genuine arithmetic frequencies, yet consecutive differencing followed by exact divisor replication leaves only linear depth growth, below the destination's `D^(2-beta)` threshold. FD-281--FD-282 sharpen the same lesson for the complementary Perron remainder: moving the physical repair map inside the contour improves the critical-line kernel, but if the grouped horizontal collar is then replaced by pointwise absolute values, prime self-divisors force an `Nx`-scale certificate up to logarithms at low height. Formal regrouping is not the resource; the resource would be signed cancellation that survives until the destination norm is applied.

Möbius Cancellation and Prime Lattice show two different ways formal retention can disappear. A sieve factor kept as an independent coprime residue label is annihilated on complete fixed-conductor blocks by CRT tensorization, so the factor matters only if a non-tensor transform uses it before completion. In Prime Lattice the full raw residue matrix is retained, but short-AP equidistribution drives its normalized Gram to rank one, outside the exact positive image cone required for local-factor deletion. Exact common-mode centering does not repair that mismatch: it creates an exact principal-channel zero mode, while the same image cone requires a strictly positive normalized floor. The viable direct covariance question is therefore deterministic model subtraction that preserves a nonzero principal fluctuation while meeting the image-cone bound, not “more matrix data” or “center it” in the abstract.

Visual Exploration isolates the correct notion of amplification. Large intermediate inverse norms in one rational expression are not intrinsic because rational identities can remove them. The invariant budget is the Fréchet derivative of the represented rational function along the Gram-generated similarity tangent. Weil Inertia supplies two source-side analogues. Coherent scalar shaping is measured by the square-root weighted Fourier norm that also pays the absolute prime term, and after zero density is inserted the horizontal zero signal per unit of that norm tends to zero. WI-411 then shows that even perfect vertical retargeting has an invariant integrated cost: one translated Blaschke defect profile has total height area at most `2 pi d`, so a finite-TV mixture needs `Theta(T)` coefficient mass to maintain `Omega(d)` sensitivity across `[T,2T]`. Peak sensitivity and coverage budget are different currencies.

The common audit is consequently: first identify the exact destination-visible object, then identify the topology, sign structure and intrinsic scale or norm in which the final theorem charges it, and only then count source complexity, multiplicity, conditioning, coherence, localization or amplitude. A proposed resource is not mathematical leverage if it lives only in a coarser horizon, an expression tree, a pre-completion label, an ambient positive cone, an unnormalized common mode, an exactly projected singular subspace, a topology with zero decision margin, or an absolute envelope that the destination immediately quotients or overwhelms.

This sharpens rather than replaces MI-036. Reachability and quotient survival remain necessary, but they are not sufficient. **A source feature counts only when it survives in the intrinsic destination budget at the quantitative scale needed by the final theorem.** The newest three examples make the missing qualifier explicit in different ways: exact injectivity can coexist with zero robust margin; exact regrouping can coexist with a destructive absolute-value tariff; and exact local targeting can coexist with a conserved coverage budget. A surviving escape may therefore be positive — a stronger provenance topology, a sign-preserving contour estimate, a source norm that genuinely affords growing height coverage, a pre-collapse fibre-separating transform, a model-subtracted residual that remains inside the admissible image, or genuine function-level sensitivity — but its gain must be measured after the same reduction that certifies the destination.

**Boundary.** The local budgets above are mathematically different: translation fibres, curvature-profile topology, divisor-depth exponents, horizontal-contour sign, CRT completion, dephasing image cones, rational derivatives, weighted Fourier norms and Blaschke height area are not instances of one universal Banach-space theorem. The global claim is only the evidence-backed proof discipline that representation-level currencies must be pushed through the exact destination interface before they are credited. None of the local results by itself supplies the missing arithmetic estimate or an RH consequence.
