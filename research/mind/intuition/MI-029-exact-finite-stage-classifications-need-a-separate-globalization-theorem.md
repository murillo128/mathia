# MI-029 — Exact finite-stage classifications need a separate globalization theorem

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from [PC-362](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-362-subcritical-unbounded-raising-perturbations-remain-spectrally-blind.md), [PL-379](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-379-infinite-prime-wold-globalization-failure.md), the cofinal-control boundaries WI-358--WI-359, and the signed-Mellin globalization obstruction [WI-405](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-405-signed-widder-mellin-averages-collapse-to-superzeta-and-require-renormalization.md). The mechanisms are different; only the finite-to-global audit is shared.

Several Mathia lines now contain exact examples in which every finite or local stage is mathematically well behaved while the infinite/cofinal object has an additional degree of freedom that the finite classifications do not determine.

Prime Circle gives a domain/spectral example. In the weighted raising family behind PC-362, every finite grade truncation is triangular and keeps the same diagonal eigenvalues. Subcritical relatively bounded infinite-depth perturbations remain isospectral as well. Yet beyond the relative-growth threshold the full closed operator can lose point spectrum. No finite triangular truncation sees the domain phenomenon responsible for that category change.

Prime Lattice gives a measure-theoretic decomposition example. PL-379 specializes prior art on `N_0^infinity` to the prime-exponent semigroup: every finite set of doubly commuting isometric prime generators has the ordinary Wold decomposition, while the countable family may have no global atomic decomposition into pure/unitary coordinate types. Finite sectors are compatible cylinder sets; the missing information is whether their infinite intersection is carried by atoms or by a diffuse type measure.

Weil Inertia supplies related cofinal warnings in two different categories. Local threshold continuity on selected states can be correct while scalar energy, graph norm and an abstract zero-mode condition remain too weak to iterate through an unbounded sequence of prime-power thresholds. WI-405 adds a summability/renormalization version: a critical signed Widder calculation can assign an exact local defect charge to each finite configuration while the corresponding global zero sum lies outside absolute convergence. Moving into the absolutely convergent Mellin region classicalizes the family into Voros superzeta data; analytically continuing back defines a renormalized object but does not preserve the local positive/defect decomposition. A global coercive statement therefore needs a separate cancellation or renormalization theorem that retains the load-bearing defect term.

The reusable audit is therefore: **after proving every finite-stage statement, identify the extra compactness, uniformity, atomicity, domain control, summability/renormalization law or source-specific compatibility needed to pass to the infinite object, and prove that property separately.** Projective compatibility of finite answers, existence of analytic continuation, or exact local charges are not by themselves the missing theorem. The global object may contain diffuse measure, escaping domain behavior, nonuniform constants, divergent background terms or exceptional subsequences that vanish from every fixed-stage description.

This is not an argument against finite truncations. Finite models remain valuable when the desired theorem itself has a finite witness, when uniform estimates close the limit, when an absolutely convergent sum preserves the needed decomposition, or when a source law forces a unique global completion. The warning applies only when the conclusion consumes an infinite/cofinal object but the proof supplies no quantitative or structural globalization mechanism.

**Boundary.** PC-362, PL-379, WI-358--WI-359 and WI-405 prove different statements in different mathematical categories; no numerical threshold or operator theorem transfers between them. PL-379's diffuse Wold pathology is generic and not rational-prime specific, PC-362's supercritical shift is not a zeta operator, and WI-405 inherits the conditional recent Widder framework. The cross-line claim is only methodological but exact: finite-stage correctness does not license a global conclusion unless the passage to the limit has its own proved control.
