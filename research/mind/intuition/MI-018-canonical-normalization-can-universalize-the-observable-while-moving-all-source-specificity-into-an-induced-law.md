# MI-018 — Exact nuisance quotients and exact scalar jets still must be separated from source-selective destination theorems

**Evidence level:** exact cross-line synthesis of VIS-239--VIS-246 and WP-318--WP-323. The probabilistic and Herglotz constructions are different; no common transform theorem is claimed.

Visual Exploration gives the nuisance-quotient side. Fixed count, independently specified cumulative-intensity normalization and cut-phase averaging remove representation variables while preserving circular spacing. The symmetric-Dirichlet family then supplies an exact dependence null. VIS-244 identifies its nuisance parameter through the complete-gap concentration `C`; VIS-245 computes the exact covariance of `C` with the confirmation statistic `bar T`.

VIS-246 shows that exact nuisance **identification** is still not exact nuisance **elimination**. For `m>=3`, conditioning on `C` leaves a simplex-sphere law proportional to `(prod_i X_i)^(alpha-1)`, so the conditional distribution still depends on `alpha`. The exact sufficient statistic is instead

`L=sum_i log X_i`,

because the symmetric-Dirichlet likelihood is `K(alpha) exp((alpha-1)L)`. Therefore `Law(bar T|L)` is `alpha`-free. This removes the nuisance exactly inside the declared family, but it creates a new destination question: does the conditional tent statistic remain tractable and retain the prime-specific signal, or has the sufficient quotient removed useful geometry with the nuisance?

Weil Positivity gives the source-selective-sign side. WP-318 normalizes a positive scalar Herglotz source into a universal Pick response plus a source-specific derivative-biased law. WP-319--WP-321 show that weakly continuous scalar family statistics are reproducible after smoothing. WP-322 then makes finite exact multipoint Pick positivity universal without any smoothing. WP-323 goes further: every finite family of boundary Pick-kernel derivatives is a Gram matrix of higher resolvent vectors. Arbitrary fixed finite arithmetic jets can retain detailed source fingerprints while every positivity consequence inherited from their confluent Pick matrix remains universal across positive scalar Herglotz measures.

The shared diagnostic is now sharper than “use a more exact statistic.” First identify the nuisance or universal category exactly. Then quotient it with a mathematically sufficient operation rather than an estimator surrogate when possible. Finally ask whether the **destination theorem itself** survives the quotient in a source-selective form. Exactness of the coordinates is not enough: `L` can be an exact nuisance coordinate yet conditioning may lose power, while exact Herglotz jets can be source-specific yet their Pick positivity remains automatic.

On the Visual side, the next honest confirmation either freezes `alpha` independently or derives/validates the `alpha`-free conditional law given `L` and checks its signal retention. On the Weil side, the next scalar discriminator must be a support/multiplicative invariant whose sign is not a consequence of any finite scalar Pick-kernel jet Gram, or the construction must leave that finite scalar category for matrix/operator/infinite-domain/global structure.

**Boundary.** VIS-246 does not prove a prime anomaly or give a closed conditional tent law. WP-323 is finite-section and does not classify nonlinear support invariants, infinite jets/nodes, matrix-valued Herglotz functions or direct archimedean coupling. The shared content is the separation among exact source data, exact nuisance removal, observation category and source-selective destination theorem.
