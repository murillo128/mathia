# MI-018 — Canonical normalization can expose a source-specific induced law while leaving its calibrated theorem and sparse family coherence universal

**Evidence level:** exact cross-line synthesis of [VIS-239](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-239-cumulative-intensity-gauge-uniformizes-specified-iid-intensity.md)--[VIS-243](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-243-complement-symmetry-closes-cyclic-overlap-multiplicities.md) and [WP-318](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-318-scalar-julia-tangents-are-boolean-self-energies-of-derivative-biased-measures.md)--[WP-320](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-320-sparse-gap-family-coherence-is-still-one-source-matched-control.md). The probabilistic and Herglotz transforms are different; no common transform theorem is claimed.

Visual Exploration gives the nuisance-quotient side. Fixing the observed count, applying an independently specified cumulative-intensity coordinate and averaging a nuisance cut phase remove three representation variables without destroying the intrinsic circular spacing statistic. Symmetric Dirichlet gaps leave an induced dependence law parameterized by `alpha`.

VIS-242 reduces the cut-averaged tent variance to low-dimensional Dirichlet overlap expectations. VIS-243 finishes the deterministic quotient: complement symmetry restricts arc lengths to a half-circle and gives closed overlap multiplicities `m-r-s+1`, `b-a+1`, and `2`. Once `alpha` and endpoint semantics are fixed independently, the null calibration is explicit; a residual can no longer be attributed to uncomputed covariance or displacement bookkeeping.

Weil Positivity gives the source-law side. WP-318 sends a positive source measure, after Julia derivative normalization, to a probability law `rho` and the normalized scalar response `-K_rho`. WP-319 proves that gap scaling can make the actual Mangoldt laws tight and source-specific: subsequential limits have shifted-lattice support, inverse-square bias and quantized local prime-power coefficients. Yet one such arithmetic law has an exact non-arithmetic positive matched control.

WP-320 shows that the obvious family-level repair is still insufficient. After sparse thinning, a single finite non-atomic positive Herglotz measure can graft infinitely many blocks at the same arithmetic midpoint nodes and gap scales, match the boundary derivative normalizations relatively, and reproduce the derivative-biased laws in bounded-Lipschitz distance. That topology is strong enough for the normalized Julia fields to converge locally uniformly. Thus **even common-source provenance across an infinite sparse family can remain universal when the charts can be separated and independently grafted**.

The shared diagnostic is now three-stage. First fix the nuisance quotient and identify the induced law. Second calibrate the theorem/statistic acting on that law exactly. Third ask whether the source imposes a relation among laws that the matched-control class cannot reproduce by local realization or sparse grafting. A clean arithmetic fingerprint, exact null calibration or common source can all be genuine structure without yielding source-selective positivity.

On the Visual side the next question is confirmatory: does prime geometry leave a residual outside a predeclared calibrated dependence family, and can that residual be translated through the signed prime-phase kernel? On the Weil side it is structural: does a non-sparse/overlapping family of Mangoldt induced laws satisfy an exact cross-gap or finite--archimedean relation that one positive Herglotz source cannot assemble blockwise?

**Boundary.** VIS-243 does not prove a prime anomaly. WP-320 uses aggressive sparse thinning and an engineered control; it does not reproduce every gap, a positive-density family, multiplicative correlations or the global Weil form. The shared content is the separation between source fingerprint, calibrated post-normalization theorem, and genuinely non-graftable family coherence.
