# MI-018 — Exact nuisance quotients and exact scalar jets still must be separated from source-selective destination theorems

**Evidence level:** exact cross-line synthesis of VIS-239--VIS-249 and WP-318--WP-323. The probabilistic and Herglotz constructions are different; no common transform theorem is claimed.

Visual Exploration gives the nuisance-quotient side. Fixed count, independently specified cumulative-intensity normalization and cut-phase averaging remove representation variables while preserving circular spacing. The symmetric-Dirichlet family then supplies an exact dependence null. VIS-244 identifies its nuisance parameter through the complete-gap concentration `C`; VIS-245 computes the exact covariance of `C` with the confirmation statistic `bar T`.

VIS-246 shows that exact nuisance **identification** is still not exact nuisance **elimination**. For `m>=3`, conditioning on `C` leaves a simplex-sphere law proportional to `(prod_i X_i)^(alpha-1)`, so the conditional distribution still depends on `alpha`. The exact sufficient statistic is instead

`L=sum_i log X_i`,

because the symmetric-Dirichlet likelihood is `K(alpha) exp((alpha-1)L)`.

VIS-247 makes `Law(X|L=lambda)` explicit as the alpha-free coarea measure `Z(lambda)^(-1)J(x)^(-1)d sigma_lambda` and proves that the tent statistic need not be constant on a product level. VIS-248 gives an exact one-dimensional `m=3` conditional audit law. VIS-249 then shows that every three-coordinate block conditional of the high-dimensional quotient is exactly that same three-gap law, so a fixed random-scan heat-bath is reversible for the full target. Its three-coordinate tangent directions span the entire regular conditioned tangent space.

This closes a stronger nuisance-removal question than merely finding a sufficient coordinate: the quotient has an explicit exact local sampler and no infinitesimal rank defect. It still does **not** establish global irreducibility, useful mixing, numerical accuracy, a prime residual, or a destination theorem. Exact nuisance elimination, exact conditional simulation and source-selective significance are therefore three separate gates.

Weil Positivity gives the source-selective-sign side. WP-318 normalizes a positive scalar Herglotz source into a universal Pick response plus a source-specific derivative-biased law. WP-319--WP-321 show that weakly continuous scalar family statistics are reproducible after smoothing. WP-322 then makes finite exact multipoint Pick positivity universal without any smoothing. WP-323 goes further: every finite family of boundary Pick-kernel derivatives is a Gram matrix of higher resolvent vectors. Arbitrary fixed finite arithmetic jets can retain detailed source fingerprints while every positivity consequence inherited from their confluent Pick matrix remains universal across positive scalar Herglotz measures.

The shared diagnostic is sharper than “use a more exact statistic.” First identify the nuisance or universal category exactly. Then quotient it with a mathematically sufficient operation rather than an estimator surrogate when possible. Next establish that the quotient is operationally accessible in the actual topology/geometry being used. Finally ask whether the **destination theorem itself** survives in a source-selective form. Exactness of coordinates or local dynamics is not enough: the Dirichlet quotient can have an exact reversible full-rank local kernel while its prime residual remains unproved, and exact Herglotz jets can be source-specific while their Pick positivity remains automatic.

On the Visual side, the honest confirmation gate is now global calibration: validate the exact VIS-249 block kernel against VIS-248, establish adequate high-dimensional convergence/error control, then test the frozen prime statistic. On the Weil side, the next scalar discriminator must be a support/multiplicative invariant whose sign is not a consequence of any finite scalar Pick-kernel jet Gram, or the construction must leave that finite scalar category for matrix/operator/infinite-domain/global structure.

**Boundary.** VIS-247--VIS-249 do not prove global Markov-chain convergence, a prime anomaly or an RH consequence. WP-323 is finite-section and does not classify nonlinear support invariants, infinite jets/nodes, matrix-valued Herglotz functions or direct archimedean coupling. The shared content is the separation among exact source data, exact nuisance removal, operational access to the quotient, observation category and a source-selective destination theorem.
