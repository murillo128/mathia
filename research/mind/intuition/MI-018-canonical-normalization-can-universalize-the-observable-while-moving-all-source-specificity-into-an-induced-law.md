# MI-018 — Canonical normalization can universalize the observable while moving all source specificity into an induced law

**Evidence level:** exact cross-line synthesis of [VIS-239](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-239-cumulative-intensity-gauge-uniformizes-specified-iid-intensity.md) and [WP-318](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-318-scalar-julia-tangents-are-boolean-self-energies-of-derivative-biased-measures.md). The two transformations are mathematically different; no common transform theorem is claimed.

Visual Exploration gives an exact probabilistic instance. For a fixed-count iid point model with specified continuous CDF `F`, the cumulative-intensity coordinate `U=F(X)` is uniform. The one-point intensity disappears completely from the tent-collision calibration. Any source specificity must therefore live in dependence, higher-order gap geometry, or uncertainty in an independently estimated `F`. If `F` is replaced by the same-sample empirical CDF, the normalization goes too far and turns the tested spacing geometry into a deterministic rank statistic.

Weil Positivity gives an exact analytic instance. At a regular scalar Herglotz boundary node, Julia reduction followed by derivative normalization sends the source measure to the derivative-biased probability law `nu_t` and the entire normalized response to `tau-K_(nu_t)`. The Herglotz sign of this Boolean self-energy is universal for every probability law, and every gap-supported probability law is realizable by a matched positive measure. Arithmetic specificity survives only in restrictions on the induced law `nu_t` and in any additional finite--archimedean coupling.

The common mechanism is not that the two transforms are equivalent. It is that a **canonical normalization can exactly remove a nuisance/source marginal while leaving a universal observable class**. Once that happens, proving positivity, uniformity, or a clean limiting profile of the normalized observable does not by itself recover source specificity. The proof obligation migrates to the induced law: which distributions are actually reachable from the arithmetic source, what relational structure survives the normalization, and what matched controls realize the same normalized object.

This gives a concrete audit rule. Whenever a proposed coordinate change, tangent normalization, whitening, centering, or conditionalization produces a simpler universal law, write down the pushforward/biased measure or sigma-field explicitly and ask where the source information went. If arbitrary controls can realize the same induced law, the normalization has classified the background rather than created an arithmetic mechanism. If the normalization is estimated from the same data, also check whether it has conditioned away the destination statistic.

**Boundary.** VIS-239 concerns iid one-point intensity and a tent-pair statistic; WP-318 concerns pure-measure scalar Herglotz Julia responses. Neither result transfers estimates to the other. The shared content is the exact location of source information after normalization, not a shared stochastic or operator model.
