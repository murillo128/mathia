# MI-018 — Source-specific data can survive normalization while both calibrated second moments and exact scalar positivity remain insufficient

**Evidence level:** exact cross-line synthesis of VIS-239--VIS-245 and WP-318--WP-322. The probabilistic and Herglotz constructions are different; no common transform theorem is claimed.

Visual Exploration gives the nuisance-quotient side. Fixed count, independently specified cumulative-intensity normalization and cut-phase averaging remove representation variables while preserving circular spacing. The symmetric-Dirichlet family then supplies an exact dependence null. VIS-244 identifies its nuisance parameter through the complete-gap concentration `C`; VIS-245 computes the exact covariance of `C` with the confirmation statistic `bar T`, completing the fixed-`alpha` mean/covariance block.

This is enough to prove that the channels are coupled and to quantify that coupling, but not enough to calibrate a same-sample plug-in test. The nonlinear map `alpha_hat(C)` requires the conditional/full joint law, or a separately justified approximation with controlled error. Exact nuisance identifiability plus exact covariance is still weaker than nuisance-free confirmation.

Weil Positivity gives a stronger universality hierarchy. WP-318 normalizes a positive scalar Herglotz source into a universal Pick response plus a source-specific derivative-biased law. WP-319--WP-321 show that even full consecutive-gap coherence in the natural weak/Julia topology can be reproduced by one atomlessly smoothed positive source. WP-322 then removes the obvious exact finite-node escape: the boundary Loewner matrix at arbitrary regular nodes is the universal resolvent Gram of the representing measure, and finite Julia reductions are only Schur complements of that Gram. Exact arithmetic entries survive, but **positivity itself remains source-independent**.

The shared diagnostic is not simply “use a more exact statistic.” First quotient genuine nuisances without flattening the geometry under test. Then propagate fitted nuisance uncertainty through the actual confirmation law. Finally distinguish source-specific numerical data from a source-specific **theorem about the sign or relation consumed by the destination**. An observable can retain arithmetic fingerprints while its calibration or positivity principle remains universal.

On the Visual side, the next honest confirmation either freezes `alpha` independently or derives/validates `Law(bar T | C)` or the equivalent joint calibration. On the Weil side, the next scalar discriminator must be an exact support/multiplicative invariant whose sign is not a scalar Pick theorem, or the construction must leave the scalar Herglotz category for matrix/operator/global structure.

**Boundary.** VIS-245 does not prove a prime anomaly or the joint nuisance law. WP-322 is finite-section and does not classify genuinely matrix-valued Herglotz functions, infinite-domain operators or arbitrary support-sensitive invariants. The shared content is the separation among source fingerprint, nuisance calibration, observation topology and source-selective destination theorem.
