# MI-018 — Canonical normalization can universalize the observable while moving source specificity into an induced law

**Evidence level:** exact cross-line synthesis of [VIS-239](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-239-cumulative-intensity-gauge-uniformizes-specified-iid-intensity.md), [VIS-240](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-240-rotation-randomized-dirichlet-gaps-give-fixed-count-uniform-intensity-dependence-null.md) and [WP-318](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-318-scalar-julia-tangents-are-boolean-self-energies-of-derivative-biased-measures.md). The probabilistic and Herglotz transformations are mathematically different; no common transform theorem is claimed.

Visual Exploration gives an exact probabilistic instance. For a fixed-count iid point model with specified continuous CDF `F`, the cumulative-intensity coordinate `U=F(X)` is uniform. The one-point intensity disappears completely from the tent-collision calibration. If `F` is replaced by the same-sample empirical CDF, the normalization goes too far and turns the tested spacing geometry into a deterministic rank statistic.

VIS-240 sharpens what remains after the legitimate normalization. Symmetric Dirichlet cyclic gaps with random rotation produce a whole one-parameter family with the **same fixed count and exactly uniform one-point intensity** but different dependence. The iid-uniform law is only the `alpha=1` member. Thus removing the marginal does not leave one canonical null; source specificity, if any, must survive a family of admissible induced dependence laws. The next proof obligation moves to variance/full-law control under a frozen dependence parameter and then to a residual outside that envelope.

Weil Positivity gives an exact analytic instance. At a regular scalar Herglotz boundary node, Julia reduction followed by derivative normalization sends the source measure to the derivative-biased probability law `nu_t` and the entire normalized response to `tau-K_(nu_t)`. The Herglotz sign of this Boolean self-energy is universal for every probability law, and every gap-supported probability law is realizable by a matched positive measure. Arithmetic specificity survives only in restrictions on the induced law `nu_t` and in any additional finite--archimedean coupling.

The common mechanism is not that the transformations are equivalent. It is that a **canonical normalization can exactly remove one nuisance/source marginal while leaving a broad universal observable class**. Once that happens, proving positivity, uniformity or a clean limiting profile of the normalized observable does not recover source specificity. The proof obligation migrates to the induced law: which distributions/dependence structures are reachable from the arithmetic source, what relational structure survives the normalization, and which matched controls realize the same normalized object.

VIS-240 adds an important warning to this audit. Even when a nuisance is removed correctly and the representation is preserved, the residual law can still have free dependence parameters invisible to the normalized one-point data. Therefore “canonical coordinates” should be followed by an explicit **induced-law envelope**, not by assuming the simplest iid/background member. Conversely, estimating that envelope from the same confirmation statistic can condition away the effect one is trying to test.

Whenever a proposed coordinate change, tangent normalization, whitening, centering or conditionalization produces a simpler universal law, write down the pushforward/biased measure or sigma-field explicitly and ask where the source information went. If arbitrary controls can realize the same induced law or a parameterized family containing it, the normalization has classified the background rather than created an arithmetic mechanism.

**Boundary.** VIS-239--VIS-240 concern fixed-count ordered point geometry and a tent-pair statistic; WP-318 concerns pure-measure scalar Herglotz Julia responses. Neither result transfers estimates to the other. The shared content is the exact relocation of source information after normalization and the need to audit the resulting law against matched controls, not a shared stochastic or operator model.
