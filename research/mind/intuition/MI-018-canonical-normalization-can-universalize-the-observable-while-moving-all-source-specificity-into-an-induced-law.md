# MI-018 — Canonical normalization can expose a source-specific induced law while leaving the theorem acting on it universal

**Evidence level:** exact cross-line synthesis of [VIS-239](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-239-cumulative-intensity-gauge-uniformizes-specified-iid-intensity.md)--[VIS-242](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-242-dirichlet-gap-tent-variance-overlap-classes.md) and [WP-318](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-318-scalar-julia-tangents-are-boolean-self-energies-of-derivative-biased-measures.md)--[WP-319](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-319-gap-adapted-mangoldt-julia-laws-are-tight-but-matched-control.md). The probabilistic and Herglotz transforms are different; no common transform theorem is claimed.

Visual Exploration gives the nuisance-quotient side. Fixing the observed count, applying an independently specified cumulative-intensity coordinate and averaging a nuisance cut phase remove three distinct representation variables without destroying the intrinsic circular spacing statistic. Symmetric Dirichlet gaps then leave an induced dependence law parameterized by `alpha`.

VIS-242 shows that the remaining null fluctuation is not an unknown high-dimensional object. For the cut-averaged tent statistic, every covariance depends only on cyclic arc lengths and their overlap cardinality; Dirichlet aggregation reduces the exact variance to low-dimensional overlap-class expectations. At `alpha=1` the variance is an explicit polynomial. Once `alpha` and endpoint semantics are fixed independently, any residual is no longer attributable to an uncomputed null covariance.

Weil Positivity gives the source-law side. WP-318 shows that Julia derivative normalization sends a positive source measure to a probability law `nu` and the normalized scalar response to the universal Boolean self-energy `tau-K_nu`. WP-319 then proves that a **source-specific** normalization can make the actual Mangoldt laws tight and arithmetic: gap-scaled subsequential limits are inverse-square laws on shifted integer lattices with quantized prime-power coefficients.

Yet the sign theorem remains universal. For each such gap-supported arithmetic probability law, a non-arithmetic positive Herglotz measure has the same derivative-biased law and therefore the identical normalized Julia response. The source fingerprint survives in the induced law, but scalar Pick positivity does not use the fingerprint.

The common diagnostic is therefore two-stage. First ask whether the normalization/quotient is legitimate and what law remains. Then ask whether the **theorem applied after normalization depends on the source-specific features of that law or only on a universal property shared by matched controls**. Exact calibration, tightness, arithmetic support or a clean positive transform can all be real advances without providing source-selective evidence.

On the Visual side the next source question is empirical/mathematical: does prime geometry leave a residual outside a predeclared calibrated dependence family, and can that residual be translated through the signed prime-phase kernel? On the Weil side it is structural: is there a cross-gap, finite--archimedean or other relation among the Mangoldt induced laws that no positive matched source can reproduce?

**Boundary.** VIS-242 does not prove a prime anomaly, and WP-319 does not prove uniqueness or cross-gap rigidity of the arithmetic tangent laws. The shared content is relocation of information into an induced law plus the separate matched-control audit of the theorem acting on that law.
