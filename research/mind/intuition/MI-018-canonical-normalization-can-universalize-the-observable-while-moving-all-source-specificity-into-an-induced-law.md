# MI-018 — Canonical normalization can expose a source-specific law while its calibrated theorem and even all-gap weak family coherence remain universal

**Evidence level:** exact cross-line synthesis of [VIS-239](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-239-cumulative-intensity-gauge-uniformizes-specified-iid-intensity.md)--[VIS-244](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-244-empirical-gap-concentration-identifies-dirichlet-alpha.md) and [WP-318](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-318-scalar-julia-tangents-are-boolean-self-energies-of-derivative-biased-measures.md)--[WP-321](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-321-atomwise-smoothing-matches-every-gap-law-and-the-full-bounded-gap-julia-family.md). The probabilistic and Herglotz transforms are different; no common transform theorem is claimed.

Visual Exploration gives the nuisance-quotient side. Fixed count, independently specified cumulative-intensity normalization and cut-phase averaging remove representation variables while preserving circular spacing. VIS-242--VIS-243 make the symmetric-Dirichlet tent calibration explicit. VIS-244 then identifies the remaining parameter through a separate exact statistic

`C=m sum_i X_i^2-1`,  `E[C]=(m-1)/(m alpha+1)`,

with exact finite-sample variance. This prevents `alpha` from being an undefined nuisance, but it also exposes a second gate: when `C` and the confirmation statistic `bar T` come from the same gap vector, their dependence and fit uncertainty must be propagated. An identifiable nuisance parameter is not automatically a fixed nuisance parameter.

Weil Positivity gives the source-law side. WP-318 sends a positive source measure, after Julia derivative normalization, to a probability law and a universal scalar Pick response. WP-319 makes the actual Mangoldt gap laws tight and source-specific. WP-320 shows sparse common-source family coherence can be grafted. WP-321 removes the sparsity loophole in the natural weak topology: atomwise smoothing produces one positive non-atomic locally absolutely continuous source whose derivative-biased laws converge in bounded-Lipschitz distance at **every consecutive gap**, with local-uniform convergence of the normalized Julia fields on the full fixed-additive-gap family.

The shared diagnostic is now topology-sensitive as well as nuisance-sensitive. First fix the representation quotient. Second identify and calibrate nuisance parameters without reusing the confirmation statistic as if the fitted value were fixed. Third ask whether the source imposes a relation that is discontinuous enough to survive the matched-control operation actually available. A source fingerprint can be real while every statistic continuous in the chosen weak topology remains reproducible.

On the Visual side, the next honest confirmation either freezes `alpha` from held-out/theoretical information or derives the joint `(C,bar T)` calibration before testing the same configuration. On the Weil side, the next scalar discriminator must detect exact atomic/support arithmetic or another relation discontinuous under bounded-Lipschitz/Julia convergence, or else couple finite and archimedean information before scalarization.

**Boundary.** VIS-244 does not prove a prime anomaly or the joint nuisance law. WP-321 does not reproduce exact atomic support, multiplicative relations or the global Weil form. The shared content is the separation among source fingerprint, nuisance calibration, observation topology and a genuinely non-reproducible destination theorem.
