# MI-014 — Cross-component coherence is proof currency only when simultaneous constraints or switching costs are nonredundant

**Evidence level:** supported by Arithmetic Fidelity through [AF-327](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-327-prime-height-time-separates-lacunary-moment-gram-boundary.md), Farey Discrepancy through [FD-122](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-122-squarefree-dilations-force-fourier-skeleton-antichain-gaps.md), and Nyman--Beurling through [NB-121](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-121-finite-nested-cutoff-coherence-collapses-to-the-maximal-section.md) and [NB-169](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-169-piecewise-adaptive-templates-pay-a-refresh-penalty.md). The mechanisms are logically comparable but mathematically distinct.

Componentwise coercivity can fail under free switching unless the component freedom is priced, removed from the state variable, or coupled by a genuinely new compatibility equation. The current corpus contains both positive and negative examples of “same-source coherence,” plus an exact example in which switching itself acquires a quantitative cost.

Arithmetic Fidelity retains and prices the component family. Fixed prime support gives a time-height modulus, while unrestricted support variation destroys every time-only gap. Bounding maximum prime by `H` restores a uniform but degenerating source envelope, and AF-327 transfers that `H,t` control into the determinant/eigenvalue slack of a retained four-feature Gram. Support height is part of the quantitative destination margin rather than an auxiliary label.

Farey Discrepancy shows genuine nonredundant coherence. FD-117--FD-120 allow each horizon separately to approach its Möbius equality ray. FD-121 proves that one common source cannot do so at `N` and `2N`, and FD-122 extends the incompatibility to every fixed squarefree dilation. The decisive mechanism is a repeated-prime coordinate: the two horizons demand equality-ray values that cannot be realized by the same source because `mu(r) ne 0` while `mu(rp)=0`. On a finite prime cube, cheap horizons form an antichain. Sharing the source therefore adds a real constraint that is absent from either marginal problem.

Nyman--Beurling supplies both matched negative and quantitative switching examples. The natural-cell projections satisfy `Q_R Q_S=Q_R` for `R<=S`, and the Nyman spaces are nested; NB-121 proves that every fixed finite nested ladder has exactly the same simultaneous feasible set as its maximal member. There, “coherence” is only a restatement of nesting.

By contrast NB-169 lets the compact signed template change across `M_T` height cells and computes the price of that freedom. If `Xi_j` is the capped-transport budget on cell `j`, the mean-square estimate contains the refresh term

`R_T=(1/(T a_T^2)) sum_j Xi_j^2`.

Under a uniform cap `Xi_j<=X_T`, positive-density logarithmic rescue with `X_T=o(log T)` requires at least `M_T ≳ T a_T^2 log^2 T/X_T^2` template changes. The family can switch, but switching is no longer free: persistence forces a complexity bill at the moving Poisson scale.

The reusable test is therefore stronger than asking whether the same source appears at several components or scales. **Write the simultaneous feasible set, identify the freedom to switch among marginal optimizers, and ask whether joint use either shrinks that set or charges switching in a destination-relevant norm.** If the joint constraints project from one maximal member, coherence is fake currency. If components demand incompatible source coordinates, as in the Farey collision, the coupling is real. If adaptation is allowed, a theorem like NB-169 can make the switching rate itself the missing resource.

One may therefore retain a component parameter and propagate its quantitative cost into the destination; quotient out component identity by a coercive state; add a nonredundant compatibility relation; or prove a switching/variation penalty for an adaptive family. In every case a worst-case scalar envelope can still erase which component pays, so an averaged or pairwise gap must be tested against free switching before it is promoted to a pointwise endpoint theorem.

**Boundary.** No numerical potential transfers between these lines. FD-122 still has deteriorating constants and leaves nonsquarefree transitions/pointwise globalization open. NB-121 treats finite nested ladders; NB-169 treats a particular piecewise compact-sampler family and does not yet provide a universal variation/entropy theorem for arbitrary continuous adaptation. AF support height does not determine exact support provenance. The synthesis classifies when coherence or switching control can plausibly add information; it does not prove a common coercivity theorem.
