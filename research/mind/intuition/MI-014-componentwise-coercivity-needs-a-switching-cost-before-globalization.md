# MI-014 — Componentwise coercivity needs a switching cost before globalization

**Evidence level:** supported as a cross-line structural synthesis by [AF-322](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-322-prime-middle-singular-conditioning-has-a-finite-height-polynomial-modulus.md), [AF-323](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-323-varying-prime-supports-are-tail-dense-in-fixed-time-phase-space.md), [FD-100](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-100-source-neutral-predecessor-genealogies-drain-carrier-rows-to-the-four-adic-head.md), and [FD-101](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-101-one-shell-exactification-makes-tagged-nonhead-source-descent-impossible.md). No common theorem or shared numerical switching functional between the two lines is established.

Two apparently different failures have the same quantifier shape.

In Arithmetic Fidelity, fixing a prime support `P` gives a genuine coercive statement: the phase orbit stays at least `c_P(1+|t|)^(-kappa_P)` away from the four-antipodal-pair geometry. But changing the component index `P` destroys every modulus depending only on time. AF-323 proves that at one fixed `tau != 0`, the union over prime supports beyond any cutoff is already dense in the full phase torus. Componentwise Diophantine separation therefore does not globalize across a free union of components.

In Farey Discrepancy, fixing a tagged carrier genealogy gives a different coercive statement: non-head motion spends the strict row potential `log_2(r/4)`, and FD-101 shows that rounding changes the source quotient at most once before exactifying the carrier. Yet the adaptive predecessor-energy argument is not forced to remain in one component: it may retag onto another row/genealogy and thereby acquire a new local inventory budget. Componentwise termination therefore does not globalize while component switching is unpriced.

The reusable point is not “all unions are bad.” It is more specific: **a coercive estimate indexed by a source component is useful globally only if changing the component is itself restricted, recorded, or costly in a quantity that survives the destination argument.** Taking an unrestricted union can erase a separation modulus; dynamically reselection of components can reset a finite Lyapunov budget.

This suggests two line-specific proof obligations with the same logical role. Arithmetic Fidelity needs a retained support resource—support height, a restricted support family, provenance, or another exact condition—that blocks the AF-323 density mechanism while remaining visible to the downstream metric. Farey needs an energy-weighted carrier identity/variation quantity for which repeated retagging consumes projective quality, forces source movement, or otherwise prevents indefinite reset of the row potential.

The diagnostic can be phrased before attempting a global estimate. If a theorem is proved only after fixing an index `lambda`, inspect the union or adaptive process over `lambda`. Ask whether there is a bound uniform in `lambda`; if not, identify the parameter controlling its degeneration. If the process may switch `lambda`, ask what changes at the switch and whether that change is observable after the same quotient/normalization used by the final theorem.

**Boundary.** AF support variation and Farey carrier retagging are not mathematically identical operations, and no transfer theorem between them is claimed. The synthesis identifies a common globalization failure mode and the form of the missing resource; each line must derive its own switching cost from its own source structure.
