# MI-031 — Source dependence counts only when it changes the surviving quotient

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of [AF-471](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-471-bounded-bernstein-signs-classify-smooth-radial-tv-hilbert-repairs.md), [MC-434](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-434-canonical-prime-order-rigid-zero-source-type-diversity.md), [PC-386](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-386-supercritical-primitive-mesh-chords-are-coprimality-sieved.md), [PC-387](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-387-critical-and-subcritical-primitive-mesh-chords-lose-leading-sieve-data.md), and [VIS-355](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-355-source-adaptive-compact-bank-floor.md). Their quantitative mechanisms are different and are not transferred between lines; the common statement is an evidence-accounting principle about what survives the actual quotient/classification consumed downstream.

A representation can depend on the arithmetic source in a literal sense and still add no new usable source information after the relevant quotient. Möbius Cancellation gives the cleanest discrete example: the increasing-prime successor relation is generated canonically from each source and is rigid, yet at fixed `omega(n)` every such relation has the same isomorphism type. It provides internal coordinates without source-type diversity, and factorwise Möbius sign erases those rank coordinates anyway.

Visual Exploration gives the corresponding conditioning example. An arithmetic source may choose the coefficients and geometry of a finite Wang bank arbitrarily, but if every choice stays inside one compact constant-rank nondegenerate family, the same uniform singular-value lower bound holds pointwise. The selector is source-dependent, yet it does not create a new asymptotic resource unless it drives the representation out of the compact ledger or supplies an independent signed estimate of the source.

Prime Circle shows that even genuine source variation can classicalize or disappear after scalarization. Above the primitive-shell integrability threshold, exact-order restriction distinguishes degree sequences but the surviving dependence is exactly a Möbius/Ramanujan coprimality sieve, with the inverse-square endpoint reducing to Jordan/Dedekind-psi data. At the threshold the leading law is universal and the first explicit zero-cutoff source correction is only a finite-Euler-product derivative at `s=1`; below the threshold even the leading primitive average is universal. Literal prime/composite sensitivity in one regime therefore does not by itself produce a new spectral mechanism.

Arithmetic Fidelity supplies a complementary classification boundary. On the unrestricted TV mixture simplex the entire smooth scalar radial Hilbert repair class is explicit in overlap powers. Once that quotient has been classified, another scalar radial formula cannot be credited as new source geometry merely because its parameters were motivated arithmetically; a surviving route must change the physical source class, retain non-radial marked structure, or alter what the destination consumes.

The reusable audit is therefore stricter than “is the construction source-dependent?” Ask instead: after the exact symmetry, scalarization, conditioning quotient or kernel classification has been applied, **which invariant still varies with the source?** Then ask whether that varying invariant survives the destination map with the sign, phase, precision or stability actually required. Source provenance, adaptivity and prime/composite separation are useful diagnostics, but they are not substitutes for this surviving invariant.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem identifying a common numerical information measure across the four lines. Automorphism quotients, singular-value floors, coprimality/finite-Euler-product scalarizations and negative-type kernel classifications remain different mathematical objects. The shared conclusion is procedural and falsifiable: a claimed source resource should be exhibited after the line-specific quotient that removes universal scaffolds and representation artifacts, not before.
