# MI-031 — Source dependence counts only when it changes the surviving quotient

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of [AF-474](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-474-best-s-term-tail-mass-gives-sharp-additive-hilbert-fidelity.md), [PC-388](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-388-primorial-boundary-windows-are-rough-number-sieves.md), [VIS-358](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-358-dense-cluster-occupancy-width-floor.md), and [WP-392](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-392-full-bost-connes-positive-cone-cannot-isolate-prime-power-support.md), extending the earlier quotient/classicalization controls in MC-434, PC-386--PC-387, VIS-355 and WP-390--WP-391. Their mechanisms are different and are not transferred between lines; the common statement is an evidence-accounting principle about what survives the actual quotient/category consumed downstream.

A representation can depend on the arithmetic source in a literal sense and still add no new usable source information after the relevant reduction. Prime Circle gives a sharp support-level example. On a primorial shell, retaining a growing primitive boundary point cloud preserves actual prime and rough-number locations, but the selected support is exactly the classical primorial wheel. Before the first rough composite enters, the surviving nontrivial indices are literally the primes above the sieve threshold. Source dependence is real, yet support-only prime detection has already been supplied by the mask rather than by the subsequent geometry.

Visual Exploration gives the corresponding conditioning example. An arithmetic source may choose or enlarge a finite Wang bank, but the resource must be evaluated after the moment kernel and full rectangular Gram are taken. VIS-358 shows that even collapse of every fixed square anchor can be repaid by dense occupancy: in the canonical cluster the surviving quotient scale is `sqrt(N) epsilon^(q-1)`. Source-dependent collision geometry earns leverage only if it changes that full-map quantity rather than a bad minor.

Arithmetic Fidelity supplies the scale version of the same audit. Approximate sparsity does not become a usable source property merely because most mass lies on few atoms. AF-474 identifies what survives into Hilbert fidelity: the support budget, the actual tail mass, and the downstream separation scale. A source restriction matters only through the quantitative tail profile and resolution that reach the destination comparison.

Weil Positivity supplies a category-level negative control. The canonical Bost--Connes source algebra contains much richer off-diagonal structure than its divisibility diagonal, but WP-392 proves that no nonzero **positive bounded** element can have exact diagonal support only on prime powers. Passing further to the weak closure would trivially recover arbitrary selectors because the bicommutant is all bounded operators. Thus “larger source representation” is not itself a surviving resource: either the source law still constrains the enlarged category in a target-independent way, or the enlargement merely restores programmability.

The reusable audit is therefore stricter than “is the construction source-dependent?” Ask instead: after the exact symmetry, scalarization, conditioning quotient, support classicalization or operator-category closure has been applied, **which invariant still varies with the source?** Then ask whether that invariant survives the destination map with the sign, phase, precision or stability actually required. Source provenance, adaptivity and prime/composite separation are useful diagnostics, but they are not substitutes for this surviving invariant.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem identifying a common numerical information measure across the lines. Rough-number support, rectangular singular values, tail-resolution fidelity and C-star source selection are different mathematical objects. The shared conclusion is procedural and falsifiable: a claimed source resource should be exhibited after the line-specific reduction that removes universal scaffolds, classical preselection and category-programmable choices, not before.
