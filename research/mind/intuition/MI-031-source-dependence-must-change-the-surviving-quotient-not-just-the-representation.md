# MI-031 — Source dependence counts only when it changes the surviving quotient

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of [AF-474](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-474-best-s-term-tail-mass-gives-sharp-additive-hilbert-fidelity.md), [AF-483](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-483-prime-density-square-mass-free-multiplicativity-admit-nonprime-control.md), [PC-388](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-388-primorial-boundary-windows-are-rough-number-sieves.md), [VIS-366](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-366-fixed-depth-vandermonde-partition-sum-spectrum.md), and [WP-392](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-392-full-bost-connes-positive-cone-cannot-isolate-prime-power-support.md), extending the earlier quotient/classicalization controls in MC-434, PC-386--PC-387, VIS-355 and WP-390--WP-391. Their mechanisms are different and are not transferred between lines; the common statement is an evidence-accounting principle about what survives the actual quotient/category consumed downstream.

A representation can depend on the arithmetic source in a literal sense and still add no new usable source information after the relevant reduction. Prime Circle gives a sharp support-level example. On a primorial shell, retaining a growing primitive boundary point cloud preserves actual prime and rough-number locations, but the selected support is exactly the classical primorial wheel. Before the first rough composite enters, the surviving nontrivial indices are literally the primes above the sieve threshold. Source dependence is real, yet support-only prime detection has already been supplied by the mask rather than by the subsequent geometry.

Visual Exploration gives the corresponding conditioning example. An arithmetic source may choose or enlarge a Wang bank, but the resource must be evaluated after the moment kernel and full Gram are taken. VIS-366 shows that at fixed polynomial depth every positive compact coefficient measure—finite, growing-support or continuous—reduces to one fixed-dimensional moment Gram whose singular products are the Vandermonde partition sums `Z_k`. Source-dependent support proliferation earns leverage only if it changes a resource outside that quotient, not merely the number or arrangement of positive compact atoms.

Arithmetic Fidelity now gives a provenance-level matched control. Approximate sparsity matters only through the support/tail/resolution budget of AF-474. More sharply, AF-483 constructs a nonintegral Beurling-prime sequence arbitrarily close to the rational primes that matches prime-scale counting density, exact square mass `P(2)`, one-crossing Gibbs geometry and free multiplicativity through rational independence of the logarithms. The canonical Gibbs boundary margin is still exactly the rational-prime leading asymptotic. Literal multiplicative source structure therefore counts only when the destination retains a discriminator stronger than free factorization plus the lower-order metric data already matched by the control.

Weil Positivity supplies a category-level negative control. The canonical Bost--Connes source algebra contains much richer off-diagonal structure than its divisibility diagonal, but WP-392 proves that no nonzero **positive bounded** element can have exact diagonal support only on prime powers. Passing further to the weak closure would trivially recover arbitrary selectors because the bicommutant is all bounded operators. Thus “larger source representation” is not itself a surviving resource: either the source law still constrains the enlarged category in a target-independent way, or the enlargement merely restores programmability.

The reusable audit is therefore stricter than “is the construction source-dependent?” Ask instead: after the exact symmetry, scalarization, conditioning quotient, support classicalization, matched-source control or operator-category closure has been applied, **which invariant still varies with the source?** Then ask whether that invariant survives the destination map with the sign, phase, precision or stability actually required. Source provenance, adaptivity, unique factorization and prime/composite separation are useful diagnostics, but they are not substitutes for this surviving invariant.

**Boundary.** This is not a theorem identifying a common numerical information measure across the lines. Rough-number support, moment-Gram partition sums, tail-resolution fidelity, Beurling matched controls and C-star source selection are different mathematical objects. AF-483 does not match integral/additive/congruence structure or all prime Dirichlet data. The shared conclusion is procedural and falsifiable: a claimed source resource should be exhibited after the line-specific reduction that removes universal scaffolds, classical preselection, matched lower-layer structure and category-programmable choices, not before.
