# MI-012 — Information content and destination sensitivity are separate mathematical resources

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from the current canonical findings, including AF-316--AF-317, PC-278--PC-279, PF-320--PF-323, VIS-203--VIS-206, FD-094--FD-095, MC-264--MC-266, NB-092--NB-094, RE-095--RE-097, WI-274--WI-275 and the previously persisted line evidence. Each statement retains its owning line's hypotheses; no mechanism transfers automatically between the objects.

Across the program, a representation can remain mathematically rich while the exact destination discards the resource that looked promising. The relevant losses now occur at several distinct interfaces: source incidence, exceptional fibers, symmetry quotients, target normalization, historical composition, family-order cancellation, sparse-sample selection and relaxed variational constraints. “More information” is therefore not a usable currency until the downstream map and its equivalence relation are fixed.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives a clean example inside one representation. AF-316 closes generic low-harmonic recovery at every support size with the dimension-tight parity law. AF-317 then shows why that theorem does not settle the even singular fiber: the six-point implication from middle-coefficient vanishing to global antipodal/torsion geometry breaks at eight points. Generic exact recoverability and **source admissibility of the exceptional fiber** are separate problems; the next arithmetic datum is character-lattice rank on `s_1=0,e_4=0`, not another generic coordinate.

Prime Circle exposes two successive quotients. PC-278 shows that a fixed positive/Hermitian spectralization discards single-source phase modulation by diagonal-unitary gauge. PC-279 shows that even after an anchored nonlinear selector breaks that gauge entrywise, the complete coefficient torus still discards the source by row/column permutations; the singular spectrum is merely the Fourier magnitude of the scalar profile. The candidate source information appears only when the actual finite window is not invariant under those permutations. Retention before one quotient says nothing about retention after the next.

Prime Flute gives the metric version. PF-320 can make the intrinsic positive-form angle stable even when raw seam blocks amplify. PF-322--PF-323 prove the converse warning: the nominal factor `s_n^2` does not make the whole unprojected low sector small, and the critical packet can be centered strictly inside the cuff. The destination theorem must therefore be proved after the pant quotient, seam-energy normalization, `L/H` projections and common completion. Neither a small prefactor nor removal of the seam endpoint controls the target angle by itself.

Visual Exploration separates a target probability from a stronger surrogate. VIS-203--VIS-205 reduce fixed-scale positive shell energy to a two-sample close-pair mass `Q(A)`, not the one-sided nearest-neighbor mass used by the older `D_v` route. VIS-206 proves fixed-`A` `Q(A)->0` throughout the strictly subcritical regime even though no theorem for the stronger one-sided resource is needed. A method threshold can therefore be an artifact of paying for a statistic the destination never asks for.

Farey and Möbius show the same principle at composition and family order. FD-094--FD-095 reduce every current one-step branch to bounded backward normalized-energy record defect; the former source-head exception disappears once source coherence is used at the right physical scale. MC-264--MC-266 reduce the high-moment blind-support problem first past family entropy and then past tensor-pair geometry to a finite signed radial Krawtchouk hierarchy. In each case the expensive generic resource becomes irrelevant after an exact structural reduction exposes the true residual carrier.

Nyman--Beurling now separates coordinate visibility from collective inverse geometry. NB-093 writes the one-zero normalized determinant as global leverage divided by coordinatewise inflation. NB-094 proves that an actual zero makes every causal-state coordinate only `O(R^-1/2)` while the same state has leverage `asymp q^(2beta-1)` because it aligns with a collective near-kernel. The normalized signal survives with full exponent and a definite sign. Tiny coordinates therefore do not imply a tiny destination effect when the destination contains an ill-conditioned inverse Gram.

Robin Extremal separates sparse selection from the information class used to prove sparsity. RE-095 says a bad-host power saving would improve the depth-two frontier, and RE-096 identifies a phase-stable deformed quadratic host sequence. RE-097 proves that counting bad samples by packing them into the ambient PNT exceptional set cannot improve the existing Robin envelope—even though the same estimate is now evaluated on a sparse sequence. To matter downstream, the scarcity theorem must use direct sparse-sample phase information unavailable to the ambient one-variable exceptional measure.

Weil Inertia supplies a variational version. WI-274 gains information by integrating all cuts before estimating the source. WI-275 takes the exact minimax envelope of the two complementary mass bounds and proves that CDF monotonicity is already compatible with equality. Any further gain must use information outside that relaxation—paired-radius spectral shape, null-equation regularity, or cross-cut source coupling—not a different scalar average of the same masses.

The operational rule is concrete: before claiming progress, identify the admissible source set, exceptional strata, symmetry quotient, target norm/normalization, inverse geometry, composition history, sample-selection mechanism, family order and variational relaxation actually used downstream. Then state what survives **that** interface. Exact reconstruction can coexist with singular source inaccessibility; a nonlinear observable can still be permutation gauge; a strong crowding norm can be unnecessary; small coordinates can coexist with large inverse leverage; sparse-host counting can still carry only ambient information; and a positive reserve can be sharp only relative to the information retained in its relaxation.

**Boundary.** This is a cross-line accounting principle, not a theorem relating the mathematical objects. Each destination sensitivity, quotient and residual carrier must be proved in its own line.
