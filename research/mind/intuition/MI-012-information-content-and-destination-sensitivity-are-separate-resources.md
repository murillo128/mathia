# MI-012 — Information content and destination sensitivity are separate mathematical resources

**Evidence level:** supported by AF-290--AF-294, PC-272's unchallenged exact-lattice quotient result, RE-068--RE-073, WI-254--WI-257, VIS-184--VIS-187, and PL-284--PL-285; the operators and targets remain distinct. PC-272's source-domain `Delta_B` counterexample remains under open review and is not used as established support.

Several lines now show, with different exact mechanisms, that a rich intermediate representation can fail only after a quotient, kernel, topology, equation, observation window, or conditioning map is applied.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the target-conditioning case. AF-290 identifies the zero-count discriminant radius, AF-291 shows that it shrinks with eta breadth, and AF-292--AF-293 show that diagonal or dense Hilbert mixing cannot repair it for free. AF-294 extends the obstruction to uniformly quasisymmetric nonlinear representations with honest transport of the bad set: a vanishing relative discriminator margin remains vanishing under one uniformly controlled nonlinear geometry. Repair requires scale-degenerating relative distortion or a genuine change of target/information category.

Prime Circle gives a quotient-information case. PC-272 proves that for fixed upper prime `q`, every swap-invariant exact-lattice/full-orbit statistic depends only on `a=pr^-1 mod q`, and the prime ratio law has character transform `|A_q(chi)|^2/M_q^2`. The carrier therefore keeps multiplicative autocorrelation while discarding common multiplicative phase/placement. The challenged source-domain `Delta_B` witness is still under review, so no stronger source non-factorization statement is promoted here.

Visual Exploration gives a readout-kernel case. VIS-185--VIS-186 show that every fixed subpolynomial or polynomial mesoscopic gap shell is killed by the exact interval kernel and full-prefix normalization. VIS-187 then shows that the remaining logarithmic cross-scale accumulation can itself be generated by a flat deterministic even-gap null when a scaled rectangular-window endpoint is resonant. The destination readout can therefore create apparent multiscale coherence even when the source has none; an arithmetic residual must first survive a control preserving the same endpoint geometry.

Weil Inertia gives the equation/regularity/specificity case. The exact aperture profile determines autocorrelation, yet firstness provides only an envelope; the scalar null equation erases the first source-sensitive correction; WI-256 shows the next coefficient is inaccessible without stronger translation control; and WI-257 proves that generic bounded-perturbation zero-modehood cannot supply that control. Any gain must use the actual von-Mangoldt translation structure rather than the abstract operator class.

Prime Lattice gives the topology case. A simple isolated positive ground state exists conditionally at one aperture, but PL-285 shows that moving source-static translation channels are not small in ambient `L^2` operator norm. Only the logarithmic form topology restores continuity, with a generic modulus too weak to transport the tiny endpoint gap usefully.

Robin Extremal supplies a complementary positive survivor. Additive endpoint labels and direct timing are blind/compressed, yet selector geometry forces a joint Chebyshev--Mertens drift. RE-072 shows that the survivor accumulates with one orientation through matched runs, and RE-073 strengthens this to a **span-free joint capacity law**: physical stretch may trade Chebyshev and Mertens burden against each other, but cannot make their geometric mean simultaneously cheap.

The common diagnostic is concrete: **after locating source information, identify the exact quotient, equation, topology, normalization, observation kernel, bad set, and representation map that intervene before the theorem, then quantify what survives at the required breadth or scale**. More coordinates, more accurate recovery, denser mixing, more local shells, or a longer physical span do not help if the destination map erases, ill-conditions, or itself manufactures the relevant pattern. Conversely, source covariance can force a pre-compression quantity to accumulate even when simpler coordinates have been erased.

**Boundary.** No discriminant estimate, character-autocorrelation identity, window-resonance law, translation modulus, prime-gap sieve bound, or Robin error law transfers between these lines. The synthesis is the separation and accounting of resources, not a shared operator.
