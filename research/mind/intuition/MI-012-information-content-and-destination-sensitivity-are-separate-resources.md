# MI-012 — Information content and destination sensitivity are separate mathematical resources

**Evidence level:** supported by AF-290--AF-303, PC-272--PC-273, RE-068--RE-083, WI-254--WI-263, VIS-184--VIS-195, PL-284--PL-289, PF-303--PF-316, MC-244--MC-252, NB-074--NB-080, and WP-270--WP-275; conditional/computational gates retain their stated evidence boundaries and the operators/targets remain distinct.

Several lines now show that a rich intermediate representation can fail only after an admissible-source restriction, quotient, topology, observation family, continuation map, source/target interface, target-conditioned metric, horizon geometry, activation/certification gate, representation-selection rule, or spectral realization is applied.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the sharpest source-fiber geometry. AF-300 shows that a proved complex-affine source dimension `r` really changes the leverage bill from `N^(-1/2)` to `r^(-1/2)`. AF-301--AF-302 replace deleted linear directions by a positive cone and then a bounded normalized convex body. AF-303 shows that a fixed coordinate cap still does not give uniform fidelity: enough comparable discarded primes admit recurrent capped barycentric zero polygons. The right invariant is the target-relative feasible fiber, not labels such as positive, normalized or bounded-coordinate.

Prime Circle separates information retention from source discrimination. PC-272 proves that ordered finite-window `Delta_B` can retain common placement information lost by the ratio-only exact lattice. PC-273 then proves that the raw law of this extra information is asymptotically indistinguishable from the all-residue control whenever `B=o(log q)`. More information than a quotient is not useful if the destination observes it below the scale at which arithmetic fluctuations survive.

Visual Exploration makes the observation-scale bill arithmetic. VIS-194 replaces the worst prime-ratio gap by the coefficient-weighted spacing horizon `D_v`; VIS-195 proves that `D_v=o(y^2)` is equivalent to vanishing taper-energy mass on fixed top-scale bounded-determinant incidence families. A tiny gap is destination-relevant only in proportion to the coefficient mass it carries.

Prime Lattice separates ambient regularity from state-specific regularity and then from quantitative usefulness. PL-289 upgrades the actual one-dimensional Weil eigenstate to a squared-log Fourier moment and an `O(log^-2)` translation modulus. That is a genuine extra resource, yet an order-one constant still cannot transport the tiny endpoint margin. The destination now asks for the constant or source cancellation, not merely a better asymptotic exponent.

Prime Flute makes the source/target interface exact. PF-315 factorizes the full physical angle into `Z_LH` and `T_HV_H`; PF-316 proves that `V_H` cannot independently hide bad conditional singular values. At compactness/weak-trace scale the full angle is equivalent to the pair `(T_H,Z_LH)`. Source attenuation and direct-channel saturation are not separate free resources.

Möbius cancellation separates algebraic arithmetic structure from quantitative localization. MC-252 shows that arbitrary low-codimension binary codes can be realized by genuine separated-prime Legendre matrices. Thus quadratic reciprocity itself carries insufficient destination-sensitive rigidity; the active shell condition or another simultaneous coupling is the actual resource still missing.

Nyman--Beurling adds horizon geometry to the ledger. NB-079 cancels the common coarse-future prediction mode exactly, but NB-080 proves that the resulting fixed-ratio residual can still diverge linearly on a boundedly invertible outer source with zero stable tail. A positive-density shallow boundary layer is mathematically real yet target-benign once unlimited future screening is allowed. Residual information must be separated from finite-depth horizon cost before it is interpreted as persistent memory.

Robin Extremal sharpens the same destination test under weighting. RE-083 proves exact first-layer prime-set conservation on arbitrary mixed fan segments and shows that the macroscopically large raw delay area contributes only `O(1/Z_0)` through the exact Robin derivative kernel. Dominant support growth and large unweighted timing can therefore be destination-neutral; the remaining information is nonlinear event ordering and CA assembly.

Weil Inertia separates source activation, exact object identity, certificate validity and first-crossing rigidity. WI-263 shows that even full-span positive-definite autocorrelation can screen the first prime exactly; support information and Bochner positivity are insufficient until the actual zero-mode/firstness constraints are used.

Weil Positivity adds causal representation and spectral type. WP-274 shows that a nonlocal positive model can be reconstructed from the desired target carrier itself. WP-275 shows that the natural pure-point source carrier cannot even be connected to the continuum by a nonzero bounded exact modulation intertwiner. The destination requires an independently selected construction that changes the spectral realization before positivity, not merely a host with the right output.

The common diagnostic is concrete: **after locating source information, identify the exact admissible fibers, reachable body, quotient/bad set, metric/topology, observation resolution, coefficient mass, horizon depth, residual channel, assembly constraint, target kernel, certificate object, selection direction and spectral representation that intervene before the theorem; then quantify what survives at that exact scale.** More coordinates, positivity, normalization, caps, smaller nominal dimension, preserved ordered information, better asymptotic regularity, arithmetic residue symbols, a large residual charge, dominant prime support, full support, nonlocal positivity, or an exact source symmetry can all be irrelevant when the destination consumes a different resource.

**Boundary.** None of the discriminant/leverage, convex-reachability, near-resonance, determinant-incidence, character/code, residual-information, boundary-layer, selector-assembly, translation-tail, positivity-certificate, autocorrelation-screening, Schur-angle, inverse-model, or spectral-type theorems transfer between these lines. The synthesis is separation and accounting of destination resources, not a shared operator or a new mathematical implication across targets.
