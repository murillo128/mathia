# MI-012 — Information survival, admissible completion and destination sensitivity are separate resources

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current line-local exact results, including AF-365--AF-368, FD-145, MC-316, NB-145/149, PC-307/313, PL-321--PL-323, RE-148/153, VIS-241/245, WI-241/307 and WP-318/322. Each mechanism remains line-specific; no estimate is transferred between objects.

A representation can retain a decisive discriminator and still be unusable because the destination consumes it through an unstable topology, a stronger target, a moving quantifier or an observation whose admissible completions remain target-mixed. Conversely, a transform that discards most source detail can remain faithful to the exact quotient the endpoint needs. The right question is not “how much information survives?” but **which source distinction survives, over which source class, and with what modulus at the declared destination**.

Arithmetic Fidelity now separates four currencies. AF-365 proves that global eventual Riesz control preserves Dirichlet principal parts above the corresponding half-plane. AF-366 shows finite-prefix fibres contain invisible pole-carrying tails. AF-367 keeps zero geometry mixed even after positivity, multiplicativity and near-square-root Beurling counting are imposed. AF-368 goes further on source identity: for every fixed `c>0`, a nonordinary Beurling system can satisfy `|N(x)-floor(x)|<c log x`. Thus transform fidelity, finite completion control, scalar counting proximity and exact arithmetic provenance are distinct resources.

Prime Circle gives the compression analogue. Exact source data can be recoverable at mesh scale while the chosen destination has already become generic decoding. The first direct mixed-conductor bilinear repair then classicalizes to a periodic Dirichlet `L`-bundle. PC-313 shows that even adjoining the Fourier dual after this compression only produces the universal Hurwitz swap functional equation. Increasing state dimension after a lossy/classical scalarization is not automatically new source information when the extra components are generated by a universal transform.

Prime Lattice shows the converse: an exact source identity can lower the destination regularity bill. PL-322 makes the residual moment automatic by conservative endpoint-flux cancellation; PL-323 uses the source-coupled coercive equation to force `Qm->0`; PL-321 then transports the conditional moment through the moving Green kernel. The previous independent singular-trace/absolute-`L^1` requirements were sufficient-proof artifacts rather than true resources. What remains is arithmetic orientation of the transferred scalar, not more ambient endpoint regularity.

Nyman--Beurling demonstrates that changing sign can merely move the bill. NB-148 cancels the universal Hadamard term with a zero-mass signed sampler but exports an equal negative lobe. NB-149 shows that paying that lobe by Jordan or total-variation domination immediately restores the positive `Theta(log G)` budget. Cancellation is a resource only while the later argument retains sign/location information.

Robin Extremal identifies a target-specific source average rather than a stronger global norm. RE-153 rewrites the self-tangent counterexample condition as a `sqrt(2)` lower bound on a Pareto-weighted Chebyshev deficit whose mass is spread over multiplicative scales. Local selector information near the frontier prime is therefore the wrong source currency unless a theorem propagates it into that canonical average.

Visual Exploration and Weil Positivity expose two different calibration failures. VIS-245 makes the fixed-parameter covariance of nuisance statistic `C` and confirmation statistic `bar T` exact, but a same-sample plug-in test still needs their conditional/full joint law. WP-322 shows that exact finite cross-gap scalar Pick positivity is a universal resolvent Gram even without smoothing; arithmetic matrix entries are not the same resource as arithmetic provenance of the sign.

Weil Inertia adds an obstruction-class distinction. WI-304--WI-306 give an essential negative band for a frozen high-frequency reserve. WI-307 raises the admissible reserve and turns that fixed-aperture essential obstruction into a finite negative-index problem. The source bill has not vanished: the discrete modes still need source-valid domination, and the reserve/cutoff scaling must be paid as aperture grows.

The cross-line accounting rule is therefore: **track source supply, admissible completion, quotient fidelity, anchoring, topology, scalarization category, target conditioning, sign debt, nuisance propagation, obstruction type and final rate compatibility separately until the last implication**. A route is promising only when it identifies a resource a matched control cannot fake and proves that the destination consumes it without reconstructing or assuming the endpoint.

**Boundary.** This is an accounting principle, not a theorem transferring estimates between lines. The examples use different objects, norms and admissible categories; only the separation of proof obligations is shared.
