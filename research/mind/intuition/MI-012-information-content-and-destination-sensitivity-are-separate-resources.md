The losses occur at distinct interfaces: source incidence and closure, symmetry quotient, transport regularity, target normalization, composition history, coefficient uniformization, destination kernels, collective dimension, variational relaxation and target linearity. “More information” is not a usable currency until the downstream map and admissible equivalences are fixed.

The operational rule is concrete: before claiming progress, identify the admissible source set and its closure, symmetry quotient, transport scale, target kernel/norm, composition/source-scale ledger, coefficient class, collective dimension, variational relaxation and algebraic category actually required downstream. Then state what survives **that** interface. Exact reconstruction can coexist with zero conditioning margin; physical descent can preserve source scale; a flat-vector problem can be destroyed by operator-norm uniformization; good pairs can hide bad collective shear; and a generic span cost can disappear when the exact source kernel is retained.
