# MI-012 — Information content and destination sensitivity are separate mathematical resources

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current canonical findings, including AF-349--AF-360, PC-302--PC-306, FD-137--FD-143, MC-307--MC-313, NB-137--NB-142, PL-313--PL-317, RE-139--RE-143, VIS-232--VIS-236, WI-298--WI-301, WP-314--WP-317 and previously persisted line evidence. Each statement retains its owning line's hypotheses; no mechanism transfers automatically between the objects.

Across the program, a representation can remain mathematically rich while the exact destination discards the resource that looked promising. The losses occur at distinct interfaces: source incidence and closure, symmetry quotient, transport regularity, target normalization, composition history, coefficient uniformization, destination topology, collective dimension, numerical precision, conditional sigma-fields, representation support, local compactness and worst-case versus average quantifiers. “More information” is not a usable currency until the downstream observer, admissible sample space and resolution at which it consumes that information are fixed.

Arithmetic Fidelity now prices both moving bandwidth and normalized shell geometry. AF-359 shows that when `delta log T->u`, retained critical Riesz shell energy tends to `e^-2u` and the endpoint defect to `(1-e^-u)^2`; order-one absolute detection begins only at `T=exp(Theta(1/delta))`. AF-360 then proves that bounded-ratio shell phase/shape does not bypass that price: after one common complex scalar is removed, the smoothed shell converges projectively to the endpoint shell for every path `delta->0`, `T->infinity`. Even relative amplitudes across shells remain blind unless their separation is exponential in `1/delta`. Surviving information is useful only if it survives the **actual quotient and moving bandwidth** of the observer.

Prime Circle and Farey Discrepancy expose both extremes of source-state capacity. PC-306 proves that at prime conductor one exact degree-one centroid already determines every uniform vertex subset, so the canonical prime support is algebraically recoverable from one cyclotomic scalar. Yet a support and its one-step translate can be disjoint while their first `K=o(q)` Fourier coefficients differ by only `O(K/q)`; exact injectivity is therefore violently nonuniform in the natural analytic topology. FD-142 similarly shows that one fixed scalar `log rad(n)` with `Theta(log T)` bits reconstructs the squarefree prefix.

FD-143 then enters the genuinely sub-reconstructive regime. Arbitrary source-independent prime-cell partitions with total budget `K_T M_T=o(sqrt(r_T))` can all be fooled by one fixed infinite prime coloring along a subsequence while a fixed orientation remains macroscopically wrong; in the central regime this is `o(sqrt(log log T))`. Thus finite dimension can hide enormous exact capacity, but a growing noninjective observation can still be provably too weak. Capacity, topology, conditioning and provenance are separate.

Möbius Cancellation adds overlap and realizability. MC-312 forces a redundancy/source-scale tradeoff for a common lower-prime representation. MC-313 shows the coarse generalized-weight hierarchy itself is not yet arithmetic: generalized Griesmer collapses to Singleton at the live scale, and generic full-length `[2R,R]` binary codes can match the required near-half erasure-list profile up to `O(R/log R)`. The next resource is not generic code redundancy but **arithmetic realizability or a sharper joint source budget**.

Nyman--Beurling separates average rarity from worst-cell source control. NB-140 makes logarithmic-rank polynomial confluence cells target-poor and NB-141 shows zero density makes them rare without excluding a sparse sequence. NB-142 identifies the exact source currency of such a sequence: on polynomially short windows the smooth zero-count main term changes by `o(1)`, so a `c log G` right-half cell forces an order-`log G` positive jump of the Riemann--von Mangoldt remainder `R(T)`, equivalently of `S(T)`. The relevant missing theorem is a sublogarithmic **short positive increment** bound, not another average density exponent.

Prime Lattice gives an exact observer-relative regularity threshold. PL-316 shows zero-extension `H^{1/2}` can be arbitrarily small while a moving Green diagonal still extracts order-one mass. PL-317 shows every `H^{1/2+eta}` gain is sufficient because the boundary trace becomes Hölder and decays at the moving sample. The relevant resource is the exact trace property consumed by the observer, not a generic smoothness label.

Robin Extremal now shows that even the choice of anchoring changes which source relation is priced. RE-139--RE-142 use a rightmost-anchored Turán certificate and expose a potentially fatal `T^-2` amplitude transfer. RE-143 anchors instead at the strong target atom and applies Turán's First Main Theorem to its rightward packet, eliminating that transfer altogether. The unresolved cost moves into signed cancellation by zeros in an `O(log U/U)` slab immediately to the **left** of the target; farther-left mass is algebraically negligible. Endpoint difficulty can therefore be a target-relative one-sided relation invisible to global population bounds.

Visual Exploration supplies both conditional-observer and representation-support versions. VIS-232--VIS-235 show that a statistic can be conditioned away by the preserved sigma-field and that escaping to a higher interaction order must be checked on the realized type. VIS-236 adds a stricter gate: the generic type may itself be too large. For ordered prime-coordinate cells the labels are monotone, so the proposed lag-two statistic collapses back to occupancies and the exact first-order type has one realization. Statistical nondegeneracy manufactured by allowing impossible label revisits is not admissible information.

Weil Inertia separates structural compression, verification and source reserve. WI-300 independently verifies the compact positivity input, and WI-301 converts complete dyadic screening of a one-signed test into a uniform archimedean reserve that any nonpositive test must repay through unscreened prime-power interaction.

Weil Positivity adds local compactness, collectivity and finite-degree classicalization. WP-315 shows the intrinsic support-side atom scale can be noncompact under prime clustering. WP-316 closes the opposite isolated-pole regime. WP-317 then closes every fixed finite positive atomic multipole as a new scalar sign mechanism: Julia reduction merely performs classical interlacing degree reduction `N->N-1->...->0`. A nontrivial scalar frontier therefore needs a genuinely unbounded/infinite collective limit or a category change.

The operational rule is concrete: before claiming progress, identify the admissible source set and its closure, symmetry quotient, physical observation scale, numerical/analytic resolution, target kernel/norm, source-incidence overlap, preserved sigma-field, representation support, local compactness, collective degree, quantifier structure and algebraic category actually required downstream. Then state what survives **that** interface. Exact reconstruction can be topologically unstable; a slowly growing observation can remain blind; exponentially many expensive modes can share cost; a generic code can match a coarse hereditary hierarchy; average rarity can leave a fatal exceptional subsequence; a target-relative slab can carry the only dangerous cancellation; normalized phase can survive while its endpoint discriminator vanishes; a conditional null can create impossible configurations; and a source-specific finite cluster can remain trapped in universal function theory.

**Boundary.** This is a cross-line accounting principle, not a theorem relating the mathematical objects. Each quotient, closure, precision budget, incidence resource, representation support, local tangent, target-relative geometry, quantifier and destination sensitivity must be proved in its own line. The synthesis does not assert that one missing theorem solves any other line; it identifies why nominal information size is an unreliable proxy for endpoint usefulness.
