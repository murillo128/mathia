# MI-012 — Information content and destination sensitivity are separate mathematical resources

**Evidence level:** supported by AF-290--AF-299, the accepted PC-272 quotient/non-factorization result, RE-068--RE-079, WI-254--WI-260, VIS-184--VIS-192, PL-284--PL-287, PF-303--PF-313, MC-244--MC-248, NB-074--NB-076, and WP-270--WP-273; conditional/computational gates retain their stated evidence boundaries and the operators/targets remain distinct.

Several lines now show that a rich intermediate representation can fail only after a quotient, topology, observation family, continuation map, source/target interface, target-conditioned metric, source-activation gate, localizability restriction, or effective-dimension constraint is applied.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the sharpest quotient geometry. AF-298 shows that optimal full-rank boundary-observation weighting removes an evaluation bill but leaves a sharp `N^(-1/2)` effective-dimension ceiling. AF-299 then proves that a nontrivial additive linear quotient of the unrestricted coefficient space does not lower that bill: because the boundary-zero hyperplanes have trivial common kernel, the failure set fills every proper quotient and rank-deficient PSD metrics have zero failure distance. Lower dimension must come from a proved source restriction/nonlinear symmetry or from changing the observable itself.

Prime Circle gives an exact quotient-versus-ordering separation. PC-272 classicalizes swap-invariant fixed-upper lattice statistics through prime-ratio power-spectrum data, while its accepted witness proves that ordered mesoscopic information does not factor through that quotient even on the actual prime source. Surviving information still needs a theorem coupling it to the final target.

Visual Exploration separates absolute resolution from signed observation. VIS-191 proves that below `y/log y` unresolved consecutive-prime pairs force an order-one absolute envelope. VIS-192 nevertheless gives signed start-averaged variance `O(1/H)` for every diverging `H`, with matching `1/H` scale in the strictly subcritical regime. The remaining destination gate is whether infinite/long start averaging can be replaced by the deterministic or finite start control actually consumed downstream.

Prime Lattice separates ambient topology from actual-state transport. PL-285--PL-286 give the sharp inverse-log worst-case modulus on the full `H^log` ball, including the complete active arithmetic comb. PL-287 proves that compact `H^log` families have a strict little-o improvement and that, conditional on the simple endpoint certificate, the actual ground branch is locally such a compact family. The missing resource is quantitative branch-tail control, not qualitative continuity.

Prime Flute gives an exact interface test. PF-312 separates normalized angle from diagonal Green amplification. PF-313 says bounded source-weighted Green strength is equivalent to domination by the fully shorted energy form, so source overlap with a near-unit singular direction must vanish at `sqrt(1-sigma^2)` scale. Large inverse directions matter only when the physical source/reassembly actually occupies them at more than the defect scale.

Robin Extremal now separates coordinate multiplicity from independent source information. RE-076 and RE-078 produce opposite corrected monotone coordinates, but RE-079 proves that—up to the explicit first-layer-birth correction—they are the unweighted and `1/(s log s)`-weighted moments of one positive fan-transport measure. On bounded selector dilation they are equivalent after the natural `Z log Z` factor. The destination resource is therefore the scale distribution of that measure or source-specific restrictions on it, not the existence of two monotonicities that can be charged independently.

Möbius cancellation supplies an exact quotient-inversion case. MC-244--MC-246 collapse the complete blind family to one rough coset. MC-248 proves that no fixed generic witness scale `O(log^A q)` with `A>1` can force the needed shell generation through minimum support; matched codes show the information type remains too weak. The quotient itself, shell-specific simultaneous arithmetic, or the signed coset amplitude must be controlled.

Nyman--Beurling supplies a continuation-map case. NB-076 resolves the future cancellation budget into nonnegative interval-conditioned partial-correlation atoms. A finite band of contiguous Gram blocks can in principle cancel the raw continuation volume, so a large raw determinant or raw pair correlation is not the destination quantity; the residual cross-cut area deficit is.

Weil Inertia separates source activation from certificate trust. WI-259 shows that first-prime phase information is unavailable until positivity is certified beyond `(log 2)/2`. WI-260 then shows that numerical replay of the current public `FP-0.35` script is not that certificate: exact constants are not enclosed rigorously and the residual test is not a valid induced-norm proof. The gate is exact recertification, not reproducibility alone.

Weil Positivity adds localizability to the resource ledger. WP-273 shows that a positive compression of the pole-completed carrier is exactly a contraction from arithmetic prime-power samples to continuum mass. Localizable continuous test spaces fail this requirement, so a surviving compression must be globally nonlocal and source-native rather than merely lower-dimensional or non-scalar.

The common diagnostic is concrete: **after locating source information, identify the exact quotient, bad set, admissible source class, effective dimension, metric category, topology, observation averaging, activation/certification threshold, continuation map, inverse-strength factor, localizability rule, and source/target range that intervene before the theorem, then quantify what survives at the required breadth or scale.** More coordinates, a stronger upstream norm, a lower nominal dimension, or a positive numerical candidate do not help when the destination discards, duplicates, or amplifies a different resource.

**Boundary.** None of the discriminant, leverage, character/coset, partial-correlation, prime-gap, translation-modulus, positivity-certificate, Schur/Green, fan-transport, or Krein-sampling theorems transfer between these lines. The synthesis is the separation and accounting of destination resources, not a shared operator or a new mathematical implication across targets.
