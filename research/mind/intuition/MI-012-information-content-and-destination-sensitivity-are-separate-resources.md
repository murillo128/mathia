# MI-012 — Information content and destination sensitivity are separate mathematical resources

**Evidence level:** supported by AF-290--AF-298, the accepted PC-272 quotient/non-factorization result, RE-068--RE-077, WI-254--WI-259, VIS-184--VIS-191, PL-284--PL-286, PF-303--PF-312, and MC-244--MC-247; MC-247 is conditional on ERH and the operators/targets remain distinct.

Several lines now show that a rich intermediate representation can fail only after a quotient, topology, observation family, continuation map, source/target interface, target-conditioned metric, source-activation gate, or effective-dimension constraint is applied.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the target-geometry case. AF-295 identifies normalized zero-count margin as intrinsic projective distance after quotienting global complex amplitude/phase. AF-296--AF-297 classify radial metric repair: finite boundary length preserves collapse, while singular profiles send failure to an ideal boundary or explicitly pay reciprocal raw-margin scale. AF-298 then tests full-rank anisotropic geometry generated by the scalar boundary observations themselves. Optimal design can remove the extra evaluation-growth bill, but the exact leverage identity `int K dmu=N` leaves a sharp best possible margin `asymp N^(-1/2)`. **Changing coordinates, changing radial distance, redistributing sensitivity, and reducing target-relevant dimension are different operations.**

Prime Circle gives an exact quotient-versus-ordering separation. PC-272 classicalizes swap-invariant fixed-upper lattice statistics through prime-ratio power-spectrum data, while its accepted witness proves that ordered mesoscopic information does not factor through that quotient even on the actual prime source. Surviving information still needs a theorem coupling it to the final target.

Visual Exploration gives an observation-resolution closure. VIS-187--VIS-190 remove endpoint artifacts and prove absolute quadratic diagonalization whenever `H >> y/log y`. VIS-191 supplies the opposite side: for `H=o(y/log y)`, ordinary consecutive primes alone force an order-one absolute envelope. The threshold is therefore genuine for the absolute method. Below it, only signed/source-specific cancellation can rescue the same quadratic observable; extra fixed smoothness cannot.

Prime Lattice gives the topology/state case. PL-285 finds the sharp generic logarithmic form modulus, and PL-286 proves that even the complete active `2,3,4` arithmetic comb has the same worst-case inverse-logarithmic modulus. The remaining transport resource must come from the actual low eigenbranch, not generic regularity or generic channel cancellation.

Prime Flute now gives an exact strength-versus-angle interface factorization. PF-311 bounds the absolute first residual high conversion after low shorting. PF-312 shows that subsequent block inversion does **not** create a worse normalized angle: the diagonal-normalized mixed Green block has exactly the singular values of the original post-low angle `T_H`. All extra raw inverse growth sits in the two diagonal Green-strength factors. The destination question is therefore source-weighted diagonal Green strength plus the already-known angle/multiplicity, not a new inverse-normalized angle.

Robin Extremal gives a coupled-erasure-cost case. RE-075 makes substantial reciprocal recovery pay in new first-layer prime supports, RE-076 supplies a monotone corrected-Chebyshev coordinate, and RE-077 proves that buying the support/physical span needed for recovery necessarily creates additional corrected-Chebyshev growth. Erasing one surviving source datum can make another source ledger worse.

Möbius cancellation supplies an exact quotient-inversion case. MC-244--MC-246 collapse the complete blind family to one rough multiplicative coset and show that quadratic full rank may still miss an odd-order quotient. MC-247 adds the conditional diagnostic that even ERH can force every surviving blind character to have support `>>sqrt(y)/log y` while leaving generic rank pressure far below full shell dimension. Nonlocal support of each obstruction is not the same as small quotient capacity.

Weil Inertia supplies abstraction loss, source-specific recovery, and an activation gate. WI-257 rules out generic bounded-perturbation regularity as control of the local translation defect. WI-258 produces phase-coupled inequalities from the actual arithmetic source. WI-259 then shows that using the first prime atom at every hypothetical crossing first requires finite-scale positivity beyond `(log 2)/2`.

The common diagnostic is concrete: **after locating source information, identify the exact quotient, bad set, effective dimension, metric category, topology, normalization, observation resolution, activation threshold, continuation map, inverse-strength factors, and source/target range that intervene before the theorem, then quantify what survives at the required breadth or scale.** More coordinates, a stronger upstream norm, or a more anisotropic metric do not help if the destination quotients, filters, remains high-dimensional, has not activated, or later amplifies a different source-weighted factor.

**Boundary.** No leverage identity, discriminant estimate, character/coset theorem, mesoscopic witness, prime-gap floor, translation modulus, positivity gate, Schur/Green factorization, reset surcharge, or continuation theorem transfers between these lines. The synthesis is the separation and accounting of resources, not a shared operator.
