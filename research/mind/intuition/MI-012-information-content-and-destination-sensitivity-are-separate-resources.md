# MI-012 — Information survival and destination sensitivity are separate resources

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis of exact/current findings, including [AF-365](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-365-fractional-riesz-error-quotients-preserve-prime-pole-fidelity.md), [FD-145](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-145-rank-truncated-ancestry-defects-have-an-exact-component-orientation-gauge.md), [MC-316](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-316-systematic-submatrix-rank-wei-dual-reflection.md), [NB-145](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-145-positive-exterior-poisson-sampling-cannot-beat-the-NB-144-coefficient.md), [PC-307](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-307-polylog-oriented-prime-packets-require-near-qlogq-conditioning.md), [PL-319](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-319-endpoint-log-source-trace-tauberian.md), [RE-148](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-148-early-window-dilation-removes-location-synchronization-but-exposes-a-rate-gap.md), [VIS-241](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-241-random-cut-separates-endpoint-phase-variance.md), [WI-241](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-241-pole-cancels-pnt-main-density-leaving-prime-discrepancy.md), and [WP-318](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-318-scalar-julia-tangents-are-boolean-self-energies-of-derivative-biased-measures.md).

A representation can retain a decisive discriminator and still be unusable at the endpoint because the destination consumes it through an unstable topology, a stronger target, a moving quantifier or a badly ordered rate comparison. Conversely, a transformation that discards most source detail can remain perfectly faithful to the one quotient the endpoint actually needs. The correct question is therefore not “how much information survives?” but **which source distinction survives, in what quotient, and with what modulus at the exact destination**.

Arithmetic Fidelity now makes this distinction exact inside a single transform. AF-365 proves that for every fixed real Riesz order the beta Mellin multiplier is zero-free on `Re s>0`, so an `O(X^theta)` profile quotient preserves all Dirichlet principal parts above `theta`; for the centered prime source that includes the off-critical zeta-zero poles. Yet AF-361--AF-364 show that stronger finite-band coordinate recovery through the same channel can cost `e^u`, `e^(u+v)` or `e^(u+v)/sqrt(v)`. **Perfect pole fidelity and severe inverse conditioning coexist.** Injectivity or singularity survival is not a stability theorem.

Farey Discrepancy shows that even information count is secondary to observation connectivity. FD-145 proves that relative ancestry equations on a visible component disconnected from a Möbius-fixed anchor have an exact `Z_2` orientation gauge. More observations inside the same disconnected quotient do not buy the missing bit. Möbius Cancellation gives the ambient-realizability analogue: MC-316 identifies several apparently different coding constraints as reflected presentations of the same generic frontier, so counting them separately would double-count one resource.

Prime Circle gives another exact-versus-stable extreme. The finite source is algebraically identifiable at degree one, while PC-307 shows that disjoint translated supports can be separated only by a margin tending to zero in every low/polylogarithmic packet. Exact description can be complete while a fixed-margin destination is asymptotically blind.

Prime Lattice adds a moving-observer version. PL-319 shows that once the actual source equation supplies a convergent endpoint trace, the pointwise tail law needed by the moving Green atom becomes a Tauberian consequence — but only after the leading boundary profile is removed and the residual is integrable with a vanishing singular remainder. The source contains the needed information; the remaining problem is whether it reaches the destination in the right boundary class.

Nyman--Beurling and Robin Extremal show that even a source-native local budget may be quantitatively insufficient. NB-145 proves the positive Euler-product Poisson proof cone has already reached its fixed-interior minimax coefficient, so more positive samples cannot improve the destination rank bound. RE-148 puts the local Robin floor and rightward-shell estimate at the same observation point, only to reveal that their exponential constants have the wrong ordering. A local law matters only when its proof class is not saturated and its rate beats the destination loss.

Visual Exploration gives the normalization/sigma-field version. VIS-241 factors the variance of a rotation-randomized finite-window statistic into intrinsic circular-gap fluctuation and a separate endpoint-cut channel. Whether the latter is information or nuisance depends on the declared representation semantics. Weil Inertia similarly removes density-one source mass before exposing the signed discrepancy that the explicit formula actually sees; Weil Positivity normalizes scalar Julia response into a universal class whose remaining specificity lies only in the induced derivative-biased law.

The cross-line accounting rule is therefore: **track source supply, quotient fidelity, anchoring, ambient realizability, target-relative conditioning, absolute attenuation, local proof-class optimality, moving-observer regularity, normalization sigma-field and final rate compatibility separately until the last inequality**. A route is promising only when it identifies one resource a matched control cannot fake and proves that the endpoint can consume that resource with the required quantitative modulus.

**Boundary.** This is a synthesis principle, not a theorem transferring estimates between research lines. The objects, norms and target functionals differ. In particular, AF-365's Mellin principal-part fidelity does not automatically provide a stable finite-data pole extractor, PL-319's trace identity does not transfer to other operators, and the Nyman/Robin rate barriers are line-specific.
