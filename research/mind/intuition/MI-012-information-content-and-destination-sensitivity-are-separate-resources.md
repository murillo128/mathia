# MI-012 — Information content and destination sensitivity are separate mathematical resources

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current canonical findings, including AF-349--AF-358, PC-302--PC-305, FD-137--FD-141, MC-307--MC-311, NB-137--NB-140, RE-139--RE-141, VIS-232--VIS-235, WI-298--WI-301, WP-314--WP-315 and previously persisted line evidence. Each statement retains its owning line's hypotheses; no mechanism transfers automatically between the objects.

Across the program, a representation can remain mathematically rich while the exact destination discards the resource that looked promising. The losses occur at distinct interfaces: source incidence and closure, symmetry quotient, transport regularity, target normalization, composition history, coefficient uniformization, destination topology, collective dimension, numerical precision, conditional sigma-fields and local compactness. “More information” is not a usable currency until the downstream map and the resolution at which it observes that information are fixed.

Arithmetic Fidelity now gives an exact topology threshold. AF-356 shows the Riesz endpoint is Lipschitz-stable in `B^2` and in limiting-law distance, and AF-357 identifies exponentially thin physical lags `exp(-Theta(1/delta))`. AF-358 sharpens this spectrally: for fixed `delta>0`, the smoothed profile belongs to the zero-Sobolev scale exactly for `s<1/2+delta`, but `E_delta-E_0` belongs only for `s<1/2`. Every fixed positive smoothing order crosses the ordinary absolute-summability/uniform-convergence threshold, while removing that smoothing never does. A stronger quadratic norm is therefore not the missing source theorem; the endpoint needs phase/maximal/cross-scale information in the topology it actually consumes.

Prime Circle and Farey Discrepancy now expose the opposite sides of **resolution capacity**. PC-305 shows that the canonical prime source, with only `L_q=pi(q)-2` atoms, is exactly recoverable from `L_q` consecutive power moments; with a width-`B` frame, `dB>=L_q` already suffices. Thus sublinear tensor order can be lossless. FD-141 goes further in a different representation: one exact real strongly additive coordinate can encode the entire squarefree prime support, with minimum spacing of order `3^(-pi(T))`. Its dimension is one, but its precision budget grows like `Theta(pi(T))` bits. Coordinate dimension, tensor order and ambient modulus size are therefore not information capacities by themselves. The useful variable is how many source states remain distinguishable at the precision/frequency resolution actually available.

This corrects two tempting but opposite inferences. FD-140 remains an exact obstruction to every **fixed congruence-periodic alphabet**, but it cannot be extended to arbitrary finite-dimensional additive coordinates without a precision/entropy/regularity restriction. PC-303 remains an exact fixed-order Fourier classicalization, but `d(q)<<q` does not guarantee an intermediate regime because sparse-moment reconstruction can saturate far earlier. Under-resolution and over-resolution must both be proved, not inferred from nominal representation size.

Möbius Cancellation adds a closure and reuse layer. MC-310 turns Page-cheap linear closure into a 2-adic rank ceiling. MC-311 then applies Bose--Burton to force at least `2^(R-nu_2(R))-O(1)` Page-expensive reciprocal directions. But the finite-geometry extremizer shows why that is still not a source-cost theorem: exponentially many expensive functionals may share one large source package. The destination needs a nonshareable incidence resource, not just many individually costly directions. Cardinality, linear closure and arithmetic source overlap are distinct currencies.

Nyman--Beurling separates coordinate singularity from target-bearing subspace geometry even after arbitrary two-dimensional perturbation. NB-137--NB-139 show that arbitrary simple nodes inside one shrinking vertical confluence cell inherit a target-poor Grassmann limit without any minimum-gap cost. NB-140 complexifies the displacement: simultaneous horizontal-depth and ordinate motion inside the same polynomial complex cell still converges to the same target-poor confluent state space. A better chart, irregular spacing or small two-dimensional motion does not help if the represented subspace itself misses the target; the source resource is occupancy of the whole complex cell.

Robin Extremal separates where a source estimate is spent. RE-139 makes finite rightmost-anchored packets visible without a horizontal-spread tax. RE-140 exposes the full-packet gain/complexity/tail budget. RE-141 inserts zero density into the **coefficient-weighted exterior tail**, improving the polynomial cutoff exponent from `1` to `2-nu_I(Theta)`, while the same polynomial zero-density information remains useless if charged directly inside the exponential Turán mode-count penalty. A source theorem can be powerful or powerless depending on the destination term to which it is coupled.

Visual Exploration supplies the conditional-observer version. VIS-232 shows that preserving the realized occupancy histogram conditions away the collision statistic; VIS-233 extends this to every additive range-one observable under endpoint-matched transition counts; VIS-234 gives the exact realized-type law for the range-two statistic `T_2`. VIS-235 then shows that the exact conditional mean can be obtained cheaply by two-transition deletion and Whittle type counts, while support/nondegeneracy may still require the full recursion. Computational tractability of one moment and statistical admissibility of the observable are separate questions.

Weil Inertia separates structural compression, verification and source reserve. WI-300's `L=0.8` compact-window certificate has now been independently replayed, so the complete one-signed first-crossing screening branch is actually excluded rather than conditionally redirected. WI-301 is stronger: dyadic screening alone forces a uniform archimedean reserve on every one-signed compactly supported test, so any nonpositive test must pay that reserve through unscreened prime-power interaction. The remaining resource is not “more screening” but where the mandatory unscreened arithmetic mass lives.

Weil Positivity adds a local-compactness gate. WP-314 shows the canonical empty-side moving Julia scale is universal and arithmetic-blind. WP-315 tests the support-side atom-mass scale and finds the opposite failure: Maynard prime clusters collapse arbitrarily many comparable Mangoldt atoms into every fixed normalized neighborhood, so the local positive measures are not Radon-precompact. A source-specific normalization is not enough if the source geometry is not locally controlled on that scale.

The operational rule is concrete: before claiming progress, identify the admissible source set and its closure, symmetry quotient, physical observation scale, numerical/analytic resolution, target kernel/norm, source-incidence overlap, preserved sigma-field, local compactness and algebraic category actually required downstream. Then state what survives **that** interface. Exact reconstruction can coexist with no zero-sensitive mechanism; exact conditioning can miss a finer source direction; one exact scalar can encode an enormous source through precision; many expensive modes can share one source package; the same density theorem can improve a tail without controlling a retained core; and a source-specific local scale can fail because nearby atoms collapse faster than the normalization.

**Boundary.** This is a cross-line accounting principle, not a theorem relating the mathematical objects. Each quotient, closure, precision budget, incidence resource, local tangent and destination sensitivity must be proved in its own line. The synthesis does not assert that the same missing theorem solves AF, FD, MC, NB, PC, RE, VIS, WI or WP; it identifies why nominal information size is an unreliable proxy for endpoint usefulness.
