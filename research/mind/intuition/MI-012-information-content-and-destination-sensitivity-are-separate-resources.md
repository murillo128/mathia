# MI-012 — Information content and destination sensitivity are separate mathematical resources

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current canonical findings, including AF-349--AF-357, PC-302--PC-304, FD-137--FD-140, MC-307--MC-310, NB-137--NB-139, VIS-232--VIS-234, WI-298--WI-300 and previously persisted line evidence. Each statement retains its owning line's hypotheses; no mechanism transfers automatically between the objects.

Across the program, a representation can remain mathematically rich while the exact destination discards the resource that looked promising. The losses occur at distinct interfaces: source incidence and closure, symmetry quotient, transport regularity, target normalization, composition history, coefficient uniformization, destination kernels, collective dimension, variational relaxation, conditional sigma-fields and target linearity. “More information” is not a usable currency until the downstream map and admissible equivalences are fixed.

Arithmetic Fidelity now makes both the topology and the physical observation scale explicit. Fixed positive Riesz order preserves the RH discriminator, while AF-356 shows the endpoint is Lipschitz-stable in `B^2` and in limiting-law distance even though the critical supremum norm is singular. AF-357 identifies the missing resolution: the Riesz Beta kernel samples relative lags `exp(-Theta(1/delta))`, and the `min(1,delta log X)` supremum modulus is already sharp for a scalar partial-sum envelope plus pointwise jump control. Average stability and scalar source bounds can therefore coexist with failure in the exact endpoint topology because neither constrains the rare cross-scale excursions seen by the supremum observer.

Prime Circle supplies the opposite information extreme. PC-302 restores labelled orientation but its quadratic content is ordinary sine/cosine Fourier covariance; PC-303 shows every fixed finite oriented tensor is joint Fourier/polyspectral source data. PC-304 then proves that degrees through `q-1` recover the complete additive DFT and hence the source exactly, while the canonical nowhere-zero DFT makes the full bispectrum complete up to cyclic translation. Increasing retained information all the way to lossless source reconstruction still does not create an RH mechanism. The useful question is what operation consumes a partial packet before classicalization or reconstruction saturation.

Farey Discrepancy shows why exact localization can still be under-resolved. FD-137 exposes one hidden rank wall, but FD-138--FD-139 rotate it into a transverse additive prime color. FD-140 conditions on the entire exact residue-class count vector modulo any fixed `Q` and still constructs a finer quadratic color modulo a coprime prime `ell` whose sign wall has vanishing conditional boundary mass. Exact conditioning is coercive only relative to the directions its alphabet resolves; completeness of a **fixed** periodic coordinate vector is not completeness of the source.

Möbius Cancellation adds a closure requirement to source visibility. MC-309 shows moderate-rank low-product quotients are forced to at least half occupancy by all but one Page-exceptional frequency. MC-310 then uses the reciprocal endpoint's rational bias lattice to turn small individual biases into an exact middle-layer condition; only after requiring the cheap frequencies to form a linear subspace does Walsh inversion force the 2-adic ceiling `t<=nu_2(R)+1`. Many informative coordinates are not equivalent to an informative **closed family**. The destination must consume the algebraic organization the source theorem actually controls.

Nyman--Beurling separates coordinate singularity from target-bearing subspace geometry even after arbitrary local reparametrization. NB-137--NB-138 show that a polynomially near-confluent simple packet can be catastrophically ill-conditioned in a power basis while its entire state span has vanishing normalized projection of the Nyman target. NB-139 removes dependence on equal spacing or a minimum gap: arbitrary simple nodes inside the same shrinking polynomial cell inherit the target-poor Grassmann limit through divided differences. More detailed internal coordinates do not help if the whole represented subspace remains target-poor.

Visual Exploration supplies the conditional-observer version. VIS-232 shows that preserving the realized occupancy histogram conditions away the collision statistic; VIS-233 extends this to every additive range-one observable under endpoint-matched transition counts. VIS-234 then gives an exact generating recursion for the range-two statistic `T_2` inside a realized first-order type. Escaping the preserved sigma-field is necessary but not sufficient: the candidate must still have nontrivial support under the **specific conditioned type**. A more faithful null can remove the very signal one intended to test.

Weil Inertia separates structural reduction from verification input. WI-298 converts one-signed complete screening into a finite aperture obstruction, and WI-299 carries the analytic tail control to that boundary. WI-300 shows that any independently validated positive compact window above `r_2` would kill the branch by monotonicity; a recent reported `L=0.8` certificate is therefore potentially decisive, but its computer-assisted positivity is a separate evidence object from the exact rearrangement theorem. A strong destination consequence cannot upgrade an unverified numerical premise into established mathematics.

The operational rule is concrete: before claiming progress, identify the admissible source set and its closure, symmetry quotient, physical observation scale, transport scale, target kernel/norm, composition/source-scale ledger, coefficient class, collective dimension, variational relaxation, preserved sigma-field and algebraic category actually required downstream. Then state what survives **that** interface. Exact reconstruction can coexist with no zero-sensitive mechanism; exact conditioning can miss a finer source direction; many cheap coordinates can fail to close to a large cheap subspace; a bad chart can span a target-poor space; and a formally higher-order observable can still be conditionally degenerate in the realized type.

**Boundary.** This is a cross-line accounting principle, not a theorem relating the mathematical objects. Each quotient, closure, residual carrier, source scale and destination sensitivity must be proved in its own line. The synthesis does not assert that the same missing source theorem solves AF, FD, MC, NB, PC, VIS or WI; it identifies where “retained information” ceases to be a sufficient description of what the endpoint actually needs.
