# MI-012 — Information content and destination sensitivity are separate mathematical resources

**Evidence level:** supported by AF-290--AF-300, the accepted PC-272 quotient/non-factorization result, RE-068--RE-080, WI-254--WI-261, VIS-184--VIS-193, PL-284--PL-288, PF-303--PF-313, MC-244--MC-249, NB-074--NB-077, and WP-270--WP-273; conditional/computational gates retain their stated evidence boundaries and the operators/targets remain distinct.

Several lines now show that a rich intermediate representation can fail only after a quotient, topology, observation family, continuation map, source/target interface, target-conditioned metric, source-activation gate, localizability restriction, or effective-dimension constraint is applied.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the sharpest source/quotient geometry. AF-298 leaves the optimal ambient `N^(-1/2)` observation bill; AF-299 proves that simply deleting a nontrivial unrestricted complex direction destroys the exact zero-count discriminator. AF-300 then proves the positive counterpart: an independently justified complex-affine source family of intrinsic dimension `r` really changes the optimal bill to `r^(-1/2)`, but every remaining complex source direction is still essential and any rank-`<r` compression again collapses the margin. Lower dimension is useful only when it is a theorem about admissible source geometry.

Prime Circle gives an exact quotient-versus-ordering separation. PC-272 classicalizes swap-invariant fixed-upper lattice statistics through prime-ratio power-spectrum data, while its accepted witness proves that ordered mesoscopic information does not factor through that quotient even on the actual prime source. Surviving information still needs a theorem coupling it to the final target.

Visual Exploration now separates three observation scales. VIS-191 gives the `y/log y` threshold for absolute resolution; VIS-192 gives signed Cesaro variance `asymp 1/H` in the diverging subcritical regime; VIS-193 proves that every deterministic start interval of length `L>=C y^2` inherits uniform `O(1/H)` mean-square cancellation. The remaining destination resource is the weighted near-collision geometry needed to shorten that coarse worst-spacing horizon, or evidence that the target already supplies a sufficient start window.

Prime Lattice separates compactness, ordinary tail size and transport regularity. PL-287 proves that the actual simple ground branch beats the sharp ambient inverse-log modulus qualitatively. PL-288 shows that even the one-stroke certificate's exceptionally strong `L^2` localization of the exact ground state—tail below `6.65e-9`—does not transport a `10^-17` positivity margin through shifts: the uncertainty is linear in tail norm and the square-root eigenvalue scale is sharp from those inputs. The missing resource is weighted Fourier-tail regularity or source-specific cancellation, not smaller ordinary `L^2` error.

Prime Flute gives an exact interface test. PF-313 says bounded source-weighted Green strength is equivalent to domination by the fully shorted energy form, so source overlap with a near-unit singular direction must vanish at `sqrt(1-sigma^2)` scale. Large inverse directions matter only when the physical source/reassembly actually occupies them above the defect scale.

Robin Extremal now supplies a scale-location case. RE-079 reduces two monotone coordinates to moments of one positive fan-transport measure. RE-080 proves that on a long same-block fan the effective selector and the two moment tails are forced into complementary power windows. The destination resource is no longer merely “some transport mass” but whether ordinary-prime event geometry can realize the required moving front profile.

Möbius cancellation supplies distinct quotient invariants. MC-244--MC-246 collapse the complete blind family to one rough coset. MC-248 shows generic witness support cannot force full generation; MC-249 further squeezes every prime-power torsion order using shell progression occupancy. Yet bounded/small exponent can coexist with arbitrarily large primary rank, so support, torsion order, quotient rank/index and signed amplitude remain separate resources.

Nyman--Beurling supplies a continuation-map and localization case. NB-076 resolves future cancellation into nonnegative interval-conditioned partial-correlation atoms. NB-077 proves that the rectangular cross-cut sum is a log-determinant information charge preserved monotonically under exact integer branching, so signed refinement cannot erase coarse predictive information. But the descendant rectangle widens with scale and both raw continuation volume and total cross-cut information can grow. The destination quantity is therefore **mesoscopic localization of the preserved cross-cut charge**, not its mere existence or raw Gram mass.

Weil Inertia separates source activation from exact object identity. WI-259 shows first-prime phase information is unavailable until positivity is certified beyond `(log 2)/2`. WI-260 shows numerical replay is not enough. WI-261 additionally proves that the nominal exact-prime proofctl path assembles a midpoint/surrogate matrix whose advertised interval can exclude the true entry and whose positivity judge changes `c_L`. A valid certificate must enclose and prove positivity of the same exact operator consumed by the phase theorem.

Weil Positivity adds localizability to the ledger. WP-273 shows that a positive compression of the pole-completed carrier is exactly a contraction from arithmetic prime-power samples to continuum mass. Localizable continuous test spaces fail this requirement, so a surviving compression must be globally nonlocal and source-native rather than merely lower-dimensional or non-scalar.

The common diagnostic is concrete: **after locating source information, identify the exact admissible source class, quotient/bad set, effective dimension, metric/topology, observation window, continuation map, localization scale, activation threshold, certificate object, inverse-strength factor, localizability rule, and source/target range that intervene before the theorem; then quantify what survives at the required scale.** More coordinates, smaller nominal dimension, smaller ordinary tail norm, stronger support, narrower torsion, preserved total prediction information, or a positive numerical candidate do not help when the destination consumes a different resource.

**Boundary.** None of the discriminant/leverage, character/coset, partial-correlation/branching, prime-ratio spacing, translation-tail, positivity-certificate, Schur/Green, fan-transport, or Krein-sampling theorems transfer between these lines. The synthesis is separation and accounting of destination resources, not a shared operator or a new mathematical implication across targets.
