**Evidence level:** supported by AF-290--AF-306, PC-272--PC-275, RE-068--RE-086, WI-254--WI-266, VIS-184--VIS-198, PL-284--PL-290, PF-303--PF-316, MC-244--MC-255, NB-074--NB-083, FD-077--FD-084, and WP-270--WP-277; conditional/computational gates retain their stated evidence boundaries and the operators/targets remain distinct.

Several lines now show that a rich intermediate representation can fail only after an admissible-source restriction, quotient, topology, observation family, continuation map, source/target interface, target-conditioned metric, horizon geometry, scale-composition requirement, parity order, activation gate, representation-selection rule, regularity class, null equation, inverse singularity, or spectral category is applied. The right question is not how much structure is present before that map, but which quantitative resource survives at the exact destination.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the sharpest source-fiber example. AF-300--AF-305 replace ambient deleted directions by intrinsic source dimension, positive reachable bodies and finally a discrete saturation boundary. AF-306 then separates two destination currencies even inside that discrete class: equal-weight prime supports can be exactly zero-free while having zero quantitative separation from zero, and saturated same-fiber supports can realize normalized margins arbitrarily close to both `0` and `1`. A source restriction may therefore improve exact failure membership without improving stable distance from failure.

Nyman--Beurling makes the inverse-prediction gate quantitative. NB-082 identifies the stationary finite-prediction excess `a_(psi,L)`; outerness gives only convergence to zero, whereas bounded screening at prefix size `R` requires `R a_(psi,L)=O(1)`. If all zeros stay strictly outside the circle, prediction is geometric and logarithmic depth is enough. Qualitative cyclicity and forward smoothness therefore do not determine affordable continuation.

The synthesis is a shared discipline of destination-relative accounting, not a new implication connecting their mathematical objects.
