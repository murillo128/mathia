# MI-012 — Information content and destination sensitivity are separate resources

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis of exact/current findings, including [AF-363](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-363-source-native-sobolev-probes-have-half-order-riesz-recovery-transition.md), [FD-145](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-145-rank-truncated-ancestry-defects-have-an-exact-component-orientation-gauge.md), [MC-316](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-316-systematic-submatrix-rank-wei-dual-reflection.md), [NB-144](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-144-euler-product-poisson-mass-forces-logarithmic-confluence-away-from-the-one-line.md), [PC-307](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-307-polylog-oriented-prime-packets-require-near-qlogq-conditioning.md), [RE-145](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-145-reciprocal-box-visibility-has-an-exponential-scaled-radius-floor.md), [VIS-239](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-239-cumulative-intensity-gauge-uniformizes-specified-iid-intensity.md), [WI-241](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-241-pole-cancels-pnt-main-density-leaving-prime-discrepancy.md), and [WP-318](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-318-scalar-julia-tangents-are-boolean-self-energies-of-derivative-biased-measures.md).

A representation can retain substantial information and still fail the endpoint because the destination consumes a narrower resource. Conversely, an apparently lossy representation may preserve exactly the discriminator needed by the target. The relevant question is therefore never just “how much information survives?” but **which source distinction survives in the topology, quotient, scale, precision, target and quantifier used by the final theorem**.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes target sensitivity quantitative. AF-362 prices a declared linear target by an exponential Picard moment rather than raw bandwidth, and AF-363 shows that natural Sobolev probes undergo a sharp normalized conditioning transition at `beta=1/2`. Relative vector conditioning, target regularity, target recovery, absolute attenuation and arithmetic specificity are different resources.

Farey Discrepancy shows that even the information count is secondary to observation connectivity. FD-145 proves that relative ancestry equations on a visible component disconnected from a Möbius-fixed anchor have an exact `Z_2` orientation gauge: every visible equation can hold while the whole component has the wrong absolute sign. Only after anchoring does FD-144's sublinear-rank observation-budget obstruction become the relevant quantitative lower bound. More tests inside the wrong quotient do not buy the missing bit.

Möbius Cancellation gives the ambient-realizability version. MC-314 turns a small source-scale slack into a second-order generalized-weight defect; MC-315 identifies the constant-defect endpoint with a diagonal projective generalized-cap problem; MC-316 then proves that primal/dual generalized weights and systematic submatrix-rank tests are reflected presentations of the same constraint. Counting both sides as independent evidence would double-count one generic frontier.

Prime Circle gives an extreme exact-versus-stable example. The source is already algebraically identifiable at degree one, but PC-307 shows that a disjoint one-step translated prime support differs from it by only `O((1+log(2+K))/(q log q))` in a low/polylogarithmic Fourier packet. Any fixed-margin decoder must pay nearly `q log q` conditioning. Exact information content can therefore be essentially complete while the destination margin tends to zero.

Nyman--Beurling and Robin Extremal give two local-geometry versions. NB-144 shows that the actual Euler-product source imposes a positive local occupancy tax absent from an xi-type matched control, yet the tax remains only `Theta(log G)` at fixed interior real part. RE-145 shows that positive reciprocal-box geometry has a cardinality-free visibility floor `exp(-O(R))`, while RE-144 shows matched packets can achieve exponential suppression in the same scaled radius. In each line a useful local law exists, but endpoint coercivity requires comparing that law with the exact residual budget consumed by the target.

Visual Exploration gives the normalization/sigma-field version. VIS-239 proves that an independently specified one-point intensity can be removed exactly by cumulative-intensity coordinates, while the same-sample empirical CDF makes the spacing statistic deterministic. A normalization can therefore remove a genuine nuisance or erase the quantity being tested, depending on what information was used to construct it. Weil Inertia gives the source-quotient version: WI-241 cancels the density-one prime main term, leaving the signed discrepancy of `psi-x` as the relevant source variable.

Weil Positivity gives the induced-law version. WP-318 shows that normalized scalar Julia response has universal positive Boolean self-energy form for every derivative-biased probability law, and matched positive Herglotz controls realize every gap-supported law. Infinite support or positivity is not itself source information; the only possible scalar specificity lies in restrictions on the particular induced Mangoldt laws and in additional finite--archimedean coupling.

The cross-line principle is therefore an accounting rule: **track source supply, component anchoring, representation fidelity, quotient loss, ambient realizability, target-relative conditioning, absolute attenuation, local exceptional geometry, normalization sigma-field, induced-law freedom and destination sensitivity separately until the final inequality**. A proof route is promising only when it identifies one resource that the matched control cannot imitate and shows that the destination actually consumes that resource.

**Boundary.** This is a synthesis principle, not a theorem transferring estimates between research lines. The objects, norms and target functionals differ. Similar accounting failures do not establish mathematical equivalence between Riesz transport, Farey ancestry, coding profiles, prime packets, zero packets, Robin visibility, geometric nulls, Weil discrepancy or Julia self-energies.
