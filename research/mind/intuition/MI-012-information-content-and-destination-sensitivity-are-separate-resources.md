# MI-012 — Information content and destination sensitivity are separate mathematical resources

**Evidence level:** supported by AF-290--AF-291, RE-068--RE-070, WI-254--WI-255, and VIS-184; the operators and targets remain distinct.

Several current lines now isolate different ways in which a mathematically rich intermediate representation can fail—or unexpectedly survive—when passed to the final theorem.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the target-conditioning case. AF-290 identifies distance to the boundary-zero discriminant as the exact finite zero-count robustness radius. AF-291 shows that this normalized radius can shrink with coefficient breadth even while the analytic approximants converge uniformly on a fixed zero-free contour. More source coordinates can improve approximation while worsening robustness in the upstream coefficient metric.

Robin Extremal separates two destination coordinates of the same matched source. RE-068 makes every additive endpoint prime statistic exactly blind, and RE-069 shows direct prime/event timing transport is macroscopically large before readout but absolutely summable after the exact Robin kernel. RE-070 nevertheless finds a pre-compression geometric survivor: the tied selector interval expands while its exact Chebyshev prime mass is conserved, forcing a positive `Z-vartheta(Z)` increment. The lesson is not simply that the target compresses information; **which coordinate is evaluated, and at which stage of the pipeline, matters**.

Weil Inertia gives the equation-redundancy case. WI-254 shows that the exact sliding-aperture profile determines real autocorrelation, while firstness supplies only a lower envelope. WI-255 goes further: the first subleading coefficient initially contains prime autocorrelation, but the defining scalar equation `Q_W(v)=0` cancels those source-sensitive terms exactly. Information present in the representation can become algebraically redundant after imposing a relation already used to define the candidate.

Visual Exploration gives the normalization-rate case. VIS-184 proves that every predeclared sublogarithmic prime-gap band in the full-prime power-weight profile has vanishing normalized coefficient mass before oscillatory cancellation is even considered. A visually or combinatorially rich family of close frequencies can carry asymptotically no weight in the exact quadratic destination normalization.

These mechanisms impose one diagnostic: **after showing that a representation contains a desired source variable, identify the exact quotient, equation, normalization, and readout that intervene before the final target, then quantify the sensitivity of the surviving coordinate at the relevant breadth or scale.**

The missing resource may be a distance to an ill-posed locus, a pre-compression geometric density, an independent equation beyond an already-used null constraint, or enough normalized mass at the exact frequency scale. More source coordinates, more accurate reconstruction, more apertures, or more close pairs do not help if the downstream map erases them; conversely, failure of one readout does not prove every source-dependent coordinate has been erased.

**Boundary.** No discriminant estimate, Robin selector law, aperture expansion, or prime-gap sieve bound transfers between these lines. The synthesis is only the resource separation: representation content, independent constraints, pipeline location, and destination sensitivity must each be established in the mathematical category of the actual target.
