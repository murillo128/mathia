# MI-012 — Information survival, admissible completion and destination sensitivity are separate resources

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis of exact/current findings, including [AF-365](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-365-fractional-riesz-error-quotients-preserve-prime-pole-fidelity.md)--[AF-366](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-366-finite-prefix-observations-need-tail-class-for-dirichlet-pole-fidelity.md), [FD-145](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-145-rank-truncated-ancestry-defects-have-an-exact-component-orientation-gauge.md), [MC-316](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-316-systematic-submatrix-rank-wei-dual-reflection.md), [NB-145](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-145-positive-exterior-poisson-sampling-cannot-beat-the-NB-144-coefficient.md), [PC-307](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-307-polylog-oriented-prime-packets-require-near-qlogq-conditioning.md), [PL-320](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-320-source-trace-full-tail-volterra-matching.md)--[PL-321](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-321-conditional-moment-green-transfer.md), [RE-148](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-148-early-window-dilation-removes-location-synchronization-but-exposes-a-rate-gap.md), [VIS-241](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-241-random-cut-separates-endpoint-phase-variance.md), [WI-241](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-241-pole-cancels-pnt-main-density-leaving-prime-discrepancy.md), and [WP-318](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-318-scalar-julia-tangents-are-boolean-self-energies-of-derivative-biased-measures.md).

A representation can retain a decisive discriminator and still be unusable because the destination consumes it through an unstable topology, a stronger target, a moving quantifier or a finite observation whose admissible completions are too broad. Conversely, a transform that discards most source detail can remain faithful to the quotient the endpoint actually needs. The right question is not “how much information survives?” but **which source distinction survives, over which source/completion class, and with what modulus at the exact destination**.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes all three layers visible in one channel. AF-365 proves that a fixed-order Riesz transform with global eventual error `O(X^theta)` preserves every Dirichlet principal part above `Re s=theta`; AF-361--AF-364 show that stronger coordinate recovery through the same channel can nevertheless be exponentially ill-conditioned. AF-366 then shows that finite-horizon data are a different object again: every finite-prefix fibre contains invisible tails that can carry prescribed critical-strip poles while becoming small in unweighted sup norm. A coefficient-tail class `O(n^(theta-1))` restores principal-part fidelity on `Re s>theta`. **Exact transform fidelity, finite-data completion control and stable extraction are three currencies, not one.**

Farey Discrepancy shows that information count is secondary to observation connectivity. FD-145 proves that relative ancestry equations on a component disconnected from a Möbius-fixed anchor retain an exact `Z_2` orientation gauge. Möbius Cancellation gives the ambient-realizability analogue: MC-316 identifies several coding constraints as reflected presentations of the same generic frontier, so counting them separately double-counts one resource.

Prime Circle gives another exact-versus-stable extreme. The finite source is algebraically identifiable at degree one, while PC-307 shows that disjoint translated supports can be separated only by a margin tending to zero in every low/polylogarithmic packet. Exact description can be complete while a fixed-margin destination is asymptotically blind.

Prime Lattice shows that a source identity can reduce the destination regularity bill rather than strengthen source size. PL-320 turns one convergent singular-remainder trace into the half-order coefficient, `Qm->0` and a conditionally convergent residual moment. PL-321 proves that this conditional moment already passes through the moving Green kernel because the pre-diagonal weight has uniformly bounded variation and `Qm->0` controls the moving atom. The absolute-`L^1` gate was therefore an artifact of a sufficient proof, not a true endpoint resource.

Nyman--Beurling and Robin Extremal show that even a source-native local budget may be quantitatively insufficient. NB-145 proves the positive Euler-product Poisson proof cone has reached its fixed-interior minimax coefficient, while RE-148 aligns local and right-shell estimates only to reveal the wrong exponential ordering. A local law matters only when its proof class is not saturated and its rate beats the destination loss.

Visual Exploration gives the representation-sigma-field version. VIS-241 separates intrinsic circular-gap fluctuation from endpoint-cut nuisance. Weil Inertia removes density-one source mass before exposing the signed discrepancy that the explicit formula actually sees. Weil Positivity normalizes scalar Julia response into a universal class whose remaining specificity lies in the induced derivative-biased law and whatever cross-law coupling survives matched controls.

The cross-line accounting rule is therefore: **track source supply, admissible completion, quotient fidelity, anchoring, ambient realizability, target-relative conditioning, absolute attenuation, local proof-class optimality, moving-observer regularity, normalization sigma-field and final rate compatibility separately until the last inequality**. A route is promising only when it identifies one resource a matched control cannot fake and proves that the endpoint can consume it with the required modulus.

**Boundary.** This is a synthesis principle, not a theorem transferring estimates between lines. AF-366's invisible tails are general Dirichlet controls rather than matched prime sources; PL-321's Abel/Green mechanism is line-specific; the Nyman/Robin rate barriers use different objects. The synthesis only says which interfaces must be made explicit before cross-line analogies are trusted.
