# MI-012 — Information content and destination sensitivity are separate mathematical resources

**Evidence level:** supported by AF-290--AF-301, PC-272, RE-068--RE-081, WI-254--WI-262, VIS-184--VIS-194, PL-284--PL-288, PF-303--PF-314, MC-244--MC-250, NB-074--NB-078, and WP-270--WP-274; conditional/computational gates retain their stated evidence boundaries and the operators/targets remain distinct.

Several lines now show that a rich intermediate representation can fail only after an admissible-source restriction, quotient, topology, observation family, continuation map, source/target interface, target-conditioned metric, activation/certification gate, or representation-selection rule is applied.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the sharpest source-fiber geometry. AF-300 shows that a proved complex-affine source dimension `r` really changes the leverage bill from `N^(-1/2)` to `r^(-1/2)`, while every remaining complex direction stays essential. AF-301 shows why merely replacing signed fibers by positive ones is insufficient: coordinate deletion is governed by a reachable cone, and multiplicatively independent discarded phases can recurrently make that cone fill the complex plane. Source restrictions matter through the exact reachable correction set and its resource bounds, not through a label such as “positive.”

Prime Circle supplies an exact quotient-versus-ordering separation: fixed-upper swap-invariant statistics factor through prime-ratio power-spectrum data, while the accepted ordered mesoscopic witness does not. Surviving information still needs a theorem coupling it to the final target.

Visual Exploration now has four observation currencies. VIS-191 gives the absolute `y/log y` resolution threshold; VIS-192 gives signed Cesaro variance `asymp 1/H`; VIS-193 gives a uniform deterministic start horizon `O(y^2)` from the worst frequency gap; VIS-194 replaces that coarse resource by the coefficient-weighted local-spacing horizon `D_v`. Improvement now means controlling energy on near-colliding prime ratios, not finding another global minimum-gap estimate.

Prime Lattice separates compactness, ordinary tail size and transport regularity. PL-288 shows that even an exact ground-state `L^2` tail below `6.65e-9` cannot transport a `10^-17` positivity margin through bounded shifts from the existing inputs. Weighted frequency-tail regularity or source-specific cancellation is a distinct destination resource.

Prime Flute gives the complementary interface distinction. PF-313 identifies defect-scale source avoidance as the exact condition for suppressing diagonal Green amplification. PF-314 shows that this condition is automatic for an internal source sector normalized in the energy of the same positive form, but not for arbitrary external injections. The source **representation** decides whether the inverse-strength gate is free or a genuine estimate.

Robin Extremal turns scale location into the destination resource. RE-080 forces complementary power fronts for the one transport ledger on long fans; RE-081 shows those fronts survive after every discrete higher-layer atom is removed. The remaining target is the continuous adaptive selector-dilation carrier, not prime-power event count.

Möbius cancellation separates support, torsion order, multiplicity and signed amplitude. MC-250 improves the ledger: at each torsion level the exact selected-shell occupancy slack bounds primary multiplicity through Singleton. Yet fixed low-order rank can remain large, so even quantitative order--rank coupling is not quotient triviality.

Nyman--Beurling separates total information from refinement balance. NB-078 resolves both continuation and prediction growth into nonnegative `q`-adic edge/contrast information charges and makes the stable-tail obstruction the cumulative sum of their differences. The destination need not consume total cross-cut information; it consumes enough prediction gain relative to new continuation gain, possibly through mesoscopic localization.

Weil Inertia separates source activation, exact object identity and certificate validity. WI-260--WI-261 show that a numerically plausible or exact-looking checker can certify a surrogate. WI-262 adds that even a correctly assembled matrix would not be certified by the audited LDL judge because its Schur update admits exact rational false positives. The theorem consumes a valid positivity statement about the exact intended operator, not positive numbers printed by a pipeline.

Weil Positivity adds representation selection. WP-273 shows that localizable positive compressions fail; WP-274 shows that a nonlocal Clark/model-space compression can always be manufactured from the normalized target measure itself. Nonlocal realizability is therefore not destination evidence unless the analytic object was selected independently of the carrier whose sign it is meant to explain.

The common diagnostic is concrete: **after locating source information, identify the exact admissible source fibers, quotient/bad set, effective dimension, metric/topology, observation horizon, refinement/localization scale, activation threshold, certificate object and judge, source/target range, and representation-selection direction that intervene before the theorem; then quantify what survives at that exact scale.** More coordinates, positivity, smaller nominal dimension, smaller ordinary tail norm, stronger support, narrower torsion, preserved total information, a nonlocal representation, or a positive numerical candidate can all be irrelevant when the destination consumes a different resource.

**Boundary.** None of the discriminant/leverage, conic-reachability, character/coset, partial-correlation/refinement, prime-ratio spacing, translation-tail, positivity-certificate, Schur/Green, fan-transport, or Clark-model theorems transfer between these lines. The synthesis is separation and accounting of destination resources, not a shared operator or a new mathematical implication across targets.
