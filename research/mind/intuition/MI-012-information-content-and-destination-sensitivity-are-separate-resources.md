# MI-012 — Information survival, admissible completion and destination sensitivity are separate resources

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of current line-local exact results. Each mechanism remains line-specific; no estimate is transferred between objects.

A representation can retain a decisive discriminator and still be unusable because the destination consumes it through an unstable topology, a stronger target, a moving quantifier or an observation whose admissible completions remain target-mixed. Conversely, a compressed representation can remain faithful to exactly the quotient the endpoint needs. The useful question is not “how much information survives?” but **which source distinction survives, over which source class, after which natural closure/reconstruction, and with what modulus at the declared destination**.

Farey Discrepancy now makes the distinction between visibility and information exact. FD-163 forces a positive-density anchor under bounded ancestry resources, but FD-164 preserves the complete one-horizon discrepancy field while changing almost every Möbius sign on any positive-density squarefree family. Geometric exposure is therefore not orientation information. The source bridge has to come from cross-horizon coherence or another arithmetic law outside the same-horizon observation kernel.

Möbius Cancellation provides the reconstruction analogue. MC-338 expresses dephasing capacity through own-source predictability `rho` and normalized coefficient energy `E`, with `delta >= (1-rho sqrt(E))_+`. MC-339 identifies the invariant predictability excess with HGR maximal correlation and bounds it by mutual information. Low degree, low rank or syntactic variable exclusion are useful only when they actually make the target phase hard to infer after exact source relations are quotiented out and when energy cannot compensate.

Nyman--Beurling shows that adaptation has more than one information coordinate. NB-171 controls **ordered** source-resolution refresh complexity via finite `p`-variation; NB-172 controls the **unordered repertoire** by the capped-transport covering number of the template range with no path regularity at all. Infinite temporal roughness can therefore coexist with very little source information if the family merely revisits a small set of shapes. Path complexity and range entropy must be accounted for separately.

Prime Lattice gives the moving-topology analogue. PL-331 constructs the critical first-prime sign reverser so that it tends to zero in every fixed logarithmic Fourier seminorm while retaining a leading-order effect on the shrinking reflection. The source equation still sees it at `sqrt(L_delta)` scale. Membership or convergence in an arbitrarily rich fixed hierarchy is therefore not the same as stability at the moving destination margin; quantitative growth of the hierarchy or direct source amplitude is a distinct resource.

Robin Extremal shows why both source class and **completion by an infinite tail** belong in the theorem statement. RE-172 proves exact Mertens injectivity for finite ordinary-prime rational/integer multiplicity perturbations. RE-173 then passes to an infinite ordinary-prime `{0,1,2}` multiplicity tail and restores exact Mertens compensation while remaining smaller than every fixed KV envelope and preserving an order-one transient Robin displacement. A finite arithmetic rigidity can disappear under noncompact completion even when standard quantitative source norms barely change.

Visual Exploration supplies an exact conditioning completion. VIS-259 identifies every cusp-adapted moment-null packet problem with a quotient norm in uniform approximation: the sharp `2m`-th carrier capacity is `E_m b^(2m)` per unit total variation. Once that dual problem is solved, more packet design cannot improve the minimax ratio. Source-carrier existence and same-order transfer/covariance error become separate obligations.

Weil Positivity gives the moving-observable transport analogue. WP-339 classifies every scalar displacement cost: under channel convergence it controls the UCP defect uniformly exactly when its tail eventually dominates the quadratic displacement. WP-340 shows stationary Hilbertian geometry obeys the same rule; only an invariant Euclidean drift has quadratic growth. A topology that succeeds only by containing the desired second moment is not an independent sign mechanism.

Weil Inertia supplies a normalization/duality version. WI-322 fixes a source-compatible bow at or below the Riemann--Siegel reciprocal length, and WI-323 shows the direct logarithmic stationary transform is then noncontracting while preserving relative bandwidth and square-root normalization. Exact phase geometry removes a geometric escape but does not control the signed von-Mangoldt covariance; the missing resource is in the coefficients, not the reciprocal transform.

Across the corpus, **source supply, admissible source class, finite-versus-noncompact completion, natural closure, quotient fidelity, relational arity, reconstruction complexity, ordered adaptation complexity, repertoire entropy, topology, moving scale, target conditioning, coefficient energy, sign debt and final rate compatibility must remain separate until the last implication**. A route is promising only when it identifies a resource a matched control cannot fake and proves that the destination consumes it without reconstructing, completing away, rescaling, delaying, or simply renaming the target quantity.

**Boundary.** This is an accounting principle, not a theorem transferring estimates between lines. The examples use different objects, norms and admissible categories; only the separation of proof obligations is shared.
