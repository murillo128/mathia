# MI-012 — Information content and destination sensitivity are separate mathematical resources

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from the current canonical findings, including AF-316--AF-320, PC-278--PC-281, PF-320--PF-327, VIS-203--VIS-207, FD-094--FD-098, MC-264--MC-269, NB-092--NB-097, RE-095--RE-100, WI-274--WI-279 and the previously persisted line evidence. Each statement retains its owning line's hypotheses; no mechanism transfers automatically between the objects.

Across the program, a representation can remain mathematically rich while the exact destination discards the resource that looked promising. The relevant losses occur at distinct interfaces: source incidence, exceptional fibers, symmetry quotients, transport regularity, target normalization, historical composition, family-order cancellation, sparse-sample selection and relaxed variational constraints. “More information” is therefore not a usable currency until the downstream map and its equivalence relation are fixed.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives a clean example inside one representation. AF-316 closes generic low-harmonic recovery by parity. AF-317--AF-318 show that the eight-point middle singularity survives source-independent geometry and character-rank tests. AF-320 then repairs exact recovery on the actual eight-prime source class: `e_4=0` forces `e_3!=0`, so one extra harmonic recovers the product phase. Yet neither first singular incidence nor a quantitative lower bound on `|e_3|` follows. **Representation failure can disappear while incidence and conditioning remain fully live.**

Retained information after a symmetry quotient is still not an arithmetic discriminator if the matched control can transport it.

Survival at an intermediate norm or form is still not destination survival.

The operational rule is concrete: before claiming progress, identify the admissible source set, exceptional strata, symmetry quotient, transport regularity, target norm/normalization, inverse geometry, composition history, sample-selection mechanism, family order, moment order and variational relaxation actually used downstream. Then state what survives **that** interface. Exact reconstruction can coexist with singular incidence and bad conditioning; finite-section placement can survive gauge yet disappear under matched transport; a packet can survive coordinate normalization and retain intermediate energy but not necessarily completion; pairwise conditioning can be perfect while collective shear grows; a fourth-moment obstruction can be bypassed when the target only needs a second moment on that range; and a canonical ambient symmetry can still be arithmetic-blind.

**Boundary.** This is a cross-line accounting principle, not a theorem relating the mathematical objects. Each destination sensitivity, quotient, residual carrier and provenance requirement must be proved in its own line.
