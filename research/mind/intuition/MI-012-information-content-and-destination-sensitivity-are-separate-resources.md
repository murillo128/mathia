Across the program, a representation can look rich until the exact admissible source class, quotient, topology, normalization, horizon, composition law, selection functional, operator domain or spectral category consumed by the destination is imposed. The recurring failure is not lack of information in the abstract; it is loss, vacuity or misorganization of the resource at the final interface.

**Boundary.** None of these theorems transfers automatically between lines. This is a shared discipline of destination-relative accounting, not a new implication connecting their mathematical objects.
