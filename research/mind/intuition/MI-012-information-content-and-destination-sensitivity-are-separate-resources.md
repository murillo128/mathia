# MI-012 — Information content and destination sensitivity are separate mathematical resources

**Evidence level:** supported by AF-290--AF-296, the full accepted PC-272 quotient/non-factorization result, RE-068--RE-075, WI-254--WI-258, VIS-184--VIS-189, PL-284--PL-285, and PF-303--PF-309; the operators and targets remain distinct.

Several lines now show that a rich intermediate representation can fail only after a quotient, topology, observation family, continuation map, source/target interface, or target-conditioned metric is applied.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the target-quotient and metric-category cases. AF-290 identifies the zero-count discriminant radius and AF-291--AF-294 show its breadth collapse survives controlled linear and nonlinear reparameterization. AF-295 proves that the normalized margin is already the intrinsic projective distance after quotienting global complex amplitude/phase. AF-296 shows that reciprocal-distance condition geometry can send the discriminant to an ideal boundary and convert margin collapse into logarithmic recession, but only by defining the metric from the target failure set. The obvious quotient removes no instability, and a target-aware singular metric changes how instability is measured rather than recovering source information.

Prime Circle gives an exact quotient-versus-ordering separation. PC-272 proves that swap-invariant fixed-upper exact-lattice/full-orbit statistics depend only on the prime ratio and hence retain only multiplicative autocorrelation/power-spectrum data. The same finding, after adversarially accepted repair, proves that the ordered mesoscopic `Delta_B` observable does **not** factor through that ratio even on the actual prime source: two admissible pairs at `q=13`, `B=1` share the ratio `a=11` but have different `Delta_1`. Source information can therefore survive outside a coarse quotient without yet being known to matter for the final target.

Visual Exploration gives an observation-family closure. VIS-187 shows that a hard rectangular window can manufacture logarithmic cross-scale coherence from a flat deterministic gap source; VIS-188 removes that artifact with endpoint-zero tapering; VIS-189 then proves that the same smooth window makes the **entire** linear-scale quadratic full-prefix prime-pair off-diagonal vanish absolutely. Within that observation family, changing gap scale cannot recover source information that the complete quadratic readout has already erased.

Prime Lattice gives the topology case. PL-285 identifies the sharp generic scales: ambient `L^2` translations are norm-discontinuous, `H^log -> L^2` continuity is inverse-square-root logarithmic, and the actual quadratic form improves only to sharp inverse-logarithmic continuity. A useful aperture bridge must exploit the actual source/eigenbranch beyond generic form regularity.

Prime Flute gives an interface case. PF-308 finds a severe full killing-normalized constant-sector obstruction, but PF-309 shows the explicit witness is asymptotically absent from every fixed physical-high entrance. Whether the obstruction survives depends on later conversion/reassembly or a different source-reached family, not on the coarse sector norm alone.

Robin Extremal gives a positive-survivor-plus-erasure-cost case. Matched local data leave a span-free Chebyshev--Mertens budget, and RE-075 shows that endpoint recovery of its reciprocal component can be paid only by new first-layer support primes, regardless of higher-layer packet complexity. Information can survive local compression and still require a separate theorem about the source cost of erasing it.

Möbius cancellation supplies an exact source-inversion case. MC-244--MC-245 strip away blind-mode count and the auxiliary smooth weight until the live object is one rough Möbius affine quotient cell. Intermediate representation complexity was not the final cancellation currency.

Weil Inertia now supplies both an abstraction loss and a source-specific recovery of usable constraints. WI-257 proves that common-domain bounded-perturbation zero-modehood cannot control the local translation defect. WI-258 then uses the actual von-Mangoldt/archimedean source decomposition to produce the phase-coupled inequalities `M_v(t)>=0` and `S_v(r)+J_v(r,t)>=0`; a negative localized source cosine defect would force strict firstness slack. The information was not recovered by generic regularity but by choosing a variational family whose weights couple directly to the arithmetic source. The remaining issue is whether the actual source forces the needed sign.

The common diagnostic is concrete: **after locating source information, identify the exact quotient, bad set, metric category, topology, normalization, observation family, continuation map, and source/target range that intervene before the theorem, then quantify what survives at the required breadth or scale**. More coordinates or a stronger upstream norm do not help if the destination erases, ill-conditions, or manufactures the relevant pattern. Conversely, information that survives one gate still needs a theorem coupling it to the final target.

**Boundary.** No discriminant estimate, condition metric, character-autocorrelation identity, mesoscopic non-factorization witness, smooth-window theorem, translation modulus, phase-defect inequality, Schur-screening estimate, rough-Möbius reduction, prime-support capacity law, or continuation theorem transfers between these lines. The synthesis is the separation and accounting of resources, not a shared operator.
