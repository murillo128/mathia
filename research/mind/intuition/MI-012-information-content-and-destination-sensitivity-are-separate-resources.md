# MI-012 — Information content and destination sensitivity are separate mathematical resources

**Evidence level:** supported by AF-290--AF-292, RE-068--RE-071, WI-254--WI-256, VIS-184--VIS-185, and PL-284--PL-285; the operators and targets remain distinct.

Several lines now show, with different exact mechanisms, that a rich intermediate representation can fail only after the destination geometry is applied.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the target-conditioning case. AF-290 identifies the zero-count discriminant radius, AF-291 shows that it shrinks with eta breadth, and AF-292 proves that diagonal Hilbert reweighting cannot improve the RH-relevant power law. The missing repair must change relational geometry, not coordinate scales.

Visual Exploration gives the kernel-normalization case. VIS-185 uses the actual interval kernel to extend the full-prime null from sublogarithmic to **all subpolynomial** gap bands. A huge family of close frequencies can therefore carry negligible weight once the destination kernel is paid.

Weil Inertia gives the equation/regularity case. The exact aperture profile determines autocorrelation, yet firstness provides only an envelope; the scalar null equation erases the first source-sensitive correction; and WI-256 shows that the next coefficient is inaccessible without zero-mode-specific translation regularity. Information can exist in the representation but be unreadable at the requested asymptotic order.

Prime Lattice gives the topology case. A simple isolated positive ground state exists conditionally at one aperture, but PL-285 shows that moving source-static translation channels are not small in ambient `L^2` operator norm. Only the logarithmic form topology restores continuity, with a generic modulus too weak to transport the tiny endpoint gap usefully.

Robin Extremal supplies a complementary positive survivor. Additive labels and direct timing are blind/compressed, yet the selector geometry forces a two-coordinate Chebyshev--Mertens saddle before final compression. Failure of one destination coordinate does not mean every source-dependent coordinate is gone.

The common diagnostic is concrete: **after locating source information, identify the exact quotient, equation, topology, normalization, and kernel that intervene before the theorem, then quantify the surviving coordinate at the required breadth or scale**. More coordinates, more accurate recovery, more close pairs, or more asymptotic terms do not help if the destination map makes them invisible or ill-conditioned.

**Boundary.** No discriminant estimate, translation modulus, aperture remainder, prime-gap sieve bound, or Robin error law transfers between these lines. The synthesis is the separation of resources, not a shared operator.
