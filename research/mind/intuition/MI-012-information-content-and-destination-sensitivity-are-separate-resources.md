# MI-012 — Information content and destination sensitivity are separate resources

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis of exact/current findings, including [AF-362](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-362-riesz-target-recovery-is-exponential-picard-moment.md), [FD-144](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-144-min-wise-prime-selectors-defeat-sublinear-rank-ancestry-observation-budgets.md), [MC-315](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-315-diagonal-projective-generalized-cap-frontier.md), [NB-144](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-144-euler-product-poisson-mass-forces-logarithmic-confluence-away-from-the-one-line.md), [RE-145](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-145-reciprocal-box-visibility-has-an-exponential-scaled-radius-floor.md), [VIS-238](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-238-fixed-count-tent-calibration-removes-poissonization-variance.md), and [WI-241](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-241-pole-cancels-pnt-main-density-leaving-prime-discrepancy.md).

A representation can retain substantial information and still fail the endpoint because the destination consumes a narrower resource. Conversely, an apparently lossy representation may preserve exactly the discriminator needed by the target. The relevant question is therefore never just “how much information survives?” but **which source distinction survives in the topology, quotient, scale, precision, target and quantifier used by the final theorem**.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes target sensitivity quantitative. AF-362 shows that normalized Riesz recovery of a declared linear target is priced by the exponential second moment of that target's scaled spectral-energy distribution, not by raw band width alone. Relative vector conditioning, target recovery, absolute attenuation and arithmetic specificity are four different resources.

Farey Discrepancy gives the information-capacity version. FD-144 allows an observation family to grow almost linearly with multiplicative rank and still miss one common source wall, while FD-142 shows that a different single coordinate at sufficient precision reconstructs the source. More coordinates are not necessarily more coercive; provenance and resolving geometry matter.

Möbius Cancellation gives the ambient-realizability version. MC-314 turns a small source-scale slack into a second-order generalized-weight defect; MC-315 then shows that the constant-defect endpoint is exactly a diagonal projective generalized-cap problem. The relevant comparison is no longer simply arithmetic code versus generic code, but arithmetic incidence versus the exact generic projective envelope in the same asymptotic regime.

Nyman--Beurling and Robin Extremal give two local-geometry versions. NB-144 shows that the actual Euler-product source imposes a positive local occupancy tax absent from an xi-type matched control, yet the tax remains only `Theta(log G)` at fixed interior real part. RE-145 shows that positive reciprocal-box geometry has a cardinality-free visibility floor `exp(-O(R))`, while RE-144 shows matched packets can achieve exponential suppression in the same scaled radius. In each line a useful local law exists, but endpoint coercivity requires comparing that law with the exact residual budget consumed by the target.

Visual Exploration gives the null-calibration version. VIS-237 removes grid-origin nuisance exactly, while VIS-238 shows that an unconditioned Poisson null adds a leading variance channel from count fluctuations that are absent once the observed sample size is fixed. A cleaner representation or a broader stochastic law can manufacture apparent contrast without adding source information. Weil Inertia gives the source-quotient version: WI-241 cancels the density-one prime main term, leaving the signed discrepancy of `psi-x` as the relevant source variable.

The cross-line principle is therefore an accounting rule: **track source supply, representation fidelity, quotient loss, ambient realizability, target-relative conditioning, absolute attenuation, local exceptional geometry, null sigma-field and destination sensitivity separately until the final inequality**. A proof route is promising only when it identifies one resource that the matched control cannot imitate and shows that the destination actually consumes that resource.

**Boundary.** This is a synthesis principle, not a theorem transferring estimates between research lines. The objects, norms and target functionals differ. Similar accounting failures do not establish mathematical equivalence between Riesz transport, Farey ancestry, coding profiles, zero packets, Robin visibility, geometric nulls or Weil discrepancy.
