# MI-012 — Information content and destination sensitivity are separate resources

**Evidence level:** cross-line synthesis of exact/current findings, including [AF-361](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-361-riesz-exponential-band-recovery-splits-attenuation-from-conditioning.md), [FD-144](../../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-144-min-wise-prime-selectors-defeat-sublinear-rank-ancestry-observation-budgets.md), [MC-314](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-314-second-order-source-scale-near-capacity-erasure-boundary.md), [NB-143](../../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-143-xi-type-entire-realizability-still-permits-sparse-logarithmic-confluence-packets.md), [RE-144](../../robin_extremal/findings/RE-144-one-sided-slowly-diverging-clusters-defeat-target-anchored-turan-with-one-rightward-mode.md), [VIS-237](../../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-237-random-origin-cell-collisions-are-triangular-pair-functional.md), and [WI-241](../../weil_inertia/findings/WI-241-pole-cancels-pnt-main-density-leaving-prime-discrepancy.md).

A representation can retain substantial information and still fail the endpoint because the destination consumes a narrower resource. Conversely, an apparently lossy representation may preserve exactly the discriminator needed by the target. The relevant question is therefore never just “how much information survives?” but **which source distinction survives in the topology, quotient, scale, precision and quantifier used by the final theorem**.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes this separation quantitative. AF-361 shows that a finite scaled exponential Riesz band can be relatively well conditioned—the normalized condition number tends to `e^v`—while the whole band is simultaneously attenuated by `e^-u` at its lower edge. Relative recovery, absolute signal size and arithmetic specificity are three different resources.

Farey Discrepancy gives the information-capacity version. FD-144 allows an observation family to grow almost linearly with multiplicative rank and still miss one common source wall, while FD-142 shows that a different single coordinate at sufficient precision reconstructs the source. More coordinates are not necessarily more coercive; provenance and resolving geometry matter.

Möbius Cancellation gives the second-order version. MC-314 shows that a first-order source scale `Z=y^((1+o(1))/R)` is not enough to know whether the hereditary code profile is exceptional: the small relative error `eta_R` becomes an additive generalized-weight defect of order `R eta_R`. The destination sees the second-order slack that the coarse source statement discards.

Nyman--Beurling and Robin Extremal give the local-geometry version. NB-143 shows that exact functional-equation symmetry, order-one entire realizability and strong global zero laws can all coexist with a sparse sequence of target-poor logarithmic confluence packets. RE-144 shows that a physically shrinking near-left packet can still hide a strong Robin target whenever its reciprocal scaled radius is allowed to diverge, even arbitrarily slowly. Global population control and shrinking physical width do not equal worst-cell target coercivity.

Visual Exploration gives the representation-null version. VIS-237 removes grid-origin nuisance exactly but leaves a triangular pair-gap functional; quotienting a nuisance variable can stabilize an observable without making it source-specific. Weil Inertia gives the source-quotient version: WI-241 cancels the pole against the density-one prime main term, so total prime-power mass is not the final source variable—the signed discrepancy of `psi-x` is.

The cross-line principle is therefore an accounting rule: **track source supply, representation fidelity, quotient loss, relative conditioning, absolute attenuation, local exceptional geometry and destination sensitivity separately until the final inequality**. A proof route is only promising when it identifies one resource that the matched control cannot imitate and shows that the destination actually consumes that resource.

**Boundary.** This is a synthesis principle, not a theorem transferring estimates between research lines. The objects, norms and target functionals differ. Similar accounting failures do not establish mathematical equivalence between Riesz transport, Farey ancestry, coding profiles, zero packets, Robin cancellation, geometric nulls or Weil discrepancy.
