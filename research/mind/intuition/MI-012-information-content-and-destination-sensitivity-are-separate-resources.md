# MI-012 — Information content and destination sensitivity are separate mathematical resources

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from current canonical findings, including AF-349--AF-359, PC-302--PC-305, FD-137--FD-142, MC-307--MC-312, NB-137--NB-141, PL-313--PL-317, RE-139--RE-142, VIS-232--VIS-235, WI-298--WI-301, WP-314--WP-316 and previously persisted line evidence. Each statement retains its owning line's hypotheses; no mechanism transfers automatically between the objects.

Across the program, a representation can remain mathematically rich while the exact destination discards the resource that looked promising. The losses occur at distinct interfaces: source incidence and closure, symmetry quotient, transport regularity, target normalization, composition history, coefficient uniformization, destination topology, collective dimension, numerical precision, conditional sigma-fields, local compactness and worst-case versus average quantifiers. “More information” is not a usable currency until the downstream observer and the resolution at which it consumes that information are fixed.

Arithmetic Fidelity now prices the moving bandwidth exactly. AF-358 shows that fixed positive Riesz smoothing raises the zero-Sobolev threshold from `1/2` to `1/2+delta`, while the difference from the unsmoothed endpoint never crosses `1/2`. AF-359 adds the two-parameter law: when `delta log T->u`, the retained critical shell energy tends to `e^-2u` and the endpoint defect to `(1-e^-u)^2`. A subexponential zero cutoff in `1/delta` therefore sees asymptotically none of the endpoint tail; order-one detection begins only at `T=exp(Theta(1/delta))`. The physical Beta kernel sees the reciprocal scale `exp(-Theta(1/delta))`. The endpoint needs information in the topology **and moving bandwidth** it actually consumes.

Prime Circle and Farey Discrepancy expose the opposite sides of resolution capacity. PC-305 shows that the canonical prime source is recoverable from only `L_q=pi(q)-2` consecutive moments, so a strongly sublinear tensor hierarchy may already be lossless. FD-141 shows one exact strongly additive scalar can encode the whole squarefree source; FD-142 sharpens this dramatically by using `L(n)=log rad(n)`. On squarefree `n<=T`, inverse-linear absolute spacing suffices, and complete recovery costs only `Theta(log T)` bits, essentially the source-identity entropy itself. Finite dimension, finite precision and inverse-polynomial resolution therefore do not define compression. Capacity and provenance matter.

Möbius Cancellation adds the overlap layer. MC-311 forces exponentially many Page-expensive directions but does not make their individual source costs additive. MC-312 is the first nonshareability theorem for a common source package: if `n` distinct lower-prime coordinates with largest prime `Z` represent `R` endpoint generators, generalized Hamming supports force `(n-R+O(log R))log Z >= (1-o(1))log y`. Reuse is possible only by paying in support redundancy or source scale. Direction cardinality, linear closure, hereditary support and arithmetic coordinate size are distinct currencies.

Nyman--Beurling separates average source rarity from target-bearing worst-cell geometry. NB-140 shows that logarithmic-rank packets in sufficiently small complex confluence cells are target-poor independent of internal gaps. NB-141 proves Selberg density makes such cells power-law rare in height, but also constructs a sparse matched divisor satisfying the current global count, density and zero-free envelopes while retaining infinitely many bad cells. If the destination can be defeated by a sparse subsequence, population rarity is not coercivity.

Prime Lattice gives an exact observer-relative regularity threshold. PL-316 shows zero-extension `H^{1/2}` can be arbitrarily small while a moving Green diagonal at `Q=r/s` still extracts order-one mass. PL-317 shows every `H^{1/2+eta}` gain is sufficient: the zero boundary trace becomes Hölder and therefore exponentially decaying in logarithmic `Q`, killing the moving sample. The relevant resource is not a generic smoothness label but the exact trace/decay property consumed by the moving observer.

Robin Extremal separates where density information is useful from what it cannot encode. RE-141 spends zero density productively on the coefficient-weighted exterior tail. RE-142 constructs one formal spectrum obeying the current upper source envelopes with `o(log T)` retained complexity yet rightmost transfer `G(U,T)=T^-2+o(1)`. Upper population control can improve the tail while remaining silent about relational recurrence between a strong atom and a farther-right atom.

Visual Exploration supplies the conditional-observer version. VIS-232--VIS-235 show that a statistic can be conditioned away by the preserved sigma-field, while a cheap exact conditional mean need not imply a nondegenerate conditional law. Computational tractability, stochastic admissibility and source sensitivity are separate.

Weil Inertia separates structural compression, verification and source reserve. WI-300 independently verifies the compact positivity input, and WI-301 converts complete dyadic screening of a one-signed test into a uniform archimedean reserve that any nonpositive test must repay through unscreened prime-power interaction. The remaining resource is where that mandatory unscreened arithmetic mass lives.

Weil Positivity adds both local compactness and collectivity. WP-314 makes the canonical empty-side moving tangent universal. WP-315 shows the intrinsic support-side atom scale can be noncompact under prime clustering. WP-316 closes the opposite isolated-pole regime: if one pole dominates the boundary derivative, scalar Julia reduction turns its normalized local response into a removable constant. A nonconstant scalar support tangent must therefore be genuinely multipole. Source specificity requires a controlled collective geometry, not merely a source-dependent scale.

The operational rule is concrete: before claiming progress, identify the admissible source set and its closure, symmetry quotient, physical observation scale, numerical/analytic resolution, target kernel/norm, source-incidence overlap, preserved sigma-field, local compactness, quantifier structure and algebraic category actually required downstream. Then state what survives **that** interface. Exact reconstruction can coexist with no zero-sensitive mechanism; finite precision can still reconstruct the source; exponentially many expensive modes can share cost until hereditary support is priced; average rarity can leave a fatal exceptional subsequence; critical regularity can miss a moving sample; and a source-specific local pole can be either noncompact or Julia-trivial.

**Boundary.** This is a cross-line accounting principle, not a theorem relating the mathematical objects. Each quotient, closure, precision budget, incidence resource, local tangent, quantifier and destination sensitivity must be proved in its own line. The synthesis does not assert that one missing theorem solves AF, FD, MC, NB, PC, PL, RE, VIS, WI or WP; it identifies why nominal information size is an unreliable proxy for endpoint usefulness.
