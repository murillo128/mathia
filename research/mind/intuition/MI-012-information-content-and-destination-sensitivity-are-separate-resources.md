The common diagnostic is concrete: **after locating source information, identify the exact quotient, bad set, metric category, topology, normalization, observation resolution, activation threshold, continuation map, inverse/amplification stage, and source/target range that intervene before the theorem, then quantify what survives at the required breadth or scale.** More coordinates or a stronger upstream norm do not help if the destination erases, ill-conditions, filters, has not activated, or later amplifies the relevant component.
