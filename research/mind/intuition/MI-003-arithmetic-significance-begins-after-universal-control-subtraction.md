# MI-003 — Arithmetic significance begins only after a control matches the claimed information layer

**Evidence level:** supported

## Core intuition

A critical exponent, self-dual line, positive kernel, resonance threshold, elegant spectrum, nonlinear coupling, or exact finite selector is not arithmetic evidence merely because it arose from prime-labelled input. The control must match the **same quotient, operator class, topology, pairing, interaction law, or correlation layer** used by the candidate. Arithmetic Fidelity supplies the formal lower-level rule: if the discriminator varies inside the maximal admissible fiber, no downstream invariant built only from that quotient can recover it.

## Strongest justified principle

Prime Flute provides an operator-class control. PF-125 constructs a global marked identification between the exact prime flute and the exact all-composite shift clone with compact first relative resolvent, so essential spectrum and every compact-class invariant are background rather than prime evidence. The newer Lambert work pushes the next comparison to true ambient thin geometry rather than coordinate narrowing.

Prime Circle shows that controls must also match the **algebra of interaction**. The nonlocal Hardy construction escapes scalar reduction, yet its finite mixed essential algebra is a wedge of universal Hilbert bands. At lower nonlinear order, PC-087 makes the natural sector-product cocycle an explicit coboundary, PC-088 makes the cotangent Leibniz coupling Hochschild-exact, and PC-089 collapses one-intermediate-shell paths to endpoint cyclotomic potentials. A control matching only input shells but not the induced multiplication/coboundary structure would therefore be too weak.

Prime Lattice supplies a correlation-layer control. The `N~T` positive-cone transition is a universal sinc geometry; unpointed Möbius orientation is a torus gauge; after pointing, fixed-lag terms reduce to Mirsky, Chowla, or Hardy--Littlewood correlations. PL-075 shows that the apparent `sigma=1/2` fixed-lag balance is reproduced by any coefficient system with a nonzero lag density. The right control must therefore match not only the frequency spacing but also the retained coefficient-correlation class.

Weil Inertia and Weil Positivity give norm/pairing controls. The `W`-local conditioning spectrum is cheap in `L^2` but super-polylogarithmically expensive in Wiener `l^1`, so a bound proved in one norm cannot be interpreted as closing the other assembly. In positivity, exact finite selectors can survive algebraically while the natural positive completion makes their anchor functional unbounded, or a self-adjoint metric repair simply imports the operator's polar sign.

## A control ladder

Controls should be chosen by the information layer actually consumed: regular/density controls; local-statistics controls; geometric clones; representation-matched controls; admissible-quotient controls; interaction/cohomology controls; finite-prefix/localized controls; coefficient-correlation controls; operator-class controls; and topology/norm/pairing controls. The first layer at which a candidate changes should coincide with the mathematical source it claims to detect.

## Evidence against overgeneralization

Controls are falsifiers, not proofs. Surviving a strong control only removes one universal explanation. Canonical subtraction or gauge breaking can be useful, but neither creates evidence if the resulting object remains matched at the actual destination layer.

## Status / novelty

The individual controls are rigorous persisted findings or literature-backed comparisons. Their hierarchy is a methodological mathematical synthesis.

## Falsification criterion

For a concrete candidate, exhibit two systems matching every admissible input and the exact algebra/topology/pairing on which the invariant depends but producing different invariant values without new information. Conversely, a feature that first changes at a stronger, independently forced information layer would validate the control classification and deserve a fresh novelty audit.
