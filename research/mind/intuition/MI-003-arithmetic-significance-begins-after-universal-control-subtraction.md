# MI-003 — Arithmetic significance begins only after a control matches the claimed information layer

**Evidence level:** supported

## Core intuition

A critical exponent, positive kernel, resonance threshold, elegant spectrum, nonlinear coupling, or exact finite selector is not arithmetic evidence merely because it arose from prime-labelled input. The control must match the same **measured quotient, interaction algebra, correlation layer, population normalization, operator ideal, functional calculus, topology, and stability notion** used by the candidate. The first layer at which the control ceases to match should coincide with the mathematical source the mechanism claims to detect.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity now makes the control requirement source- and norm-sensitive. The complete linear statistic is a measured closed span; dependent channel families are classified by fusion spans of sigma-field joins; and exact recovery can still be unstable when the relevant Friedrichs/fusion angle collapses. A control matched only to algebraic support but not to the stability category may therefore be too weak—or may reveal that an apparent distinction is only conditioning.

Prime Circle supplies two strong classicalizing controls. Arbitrary finite canonical cotangent networks and fixed-support depth reduce to endpoint/fixed-state algebra, while the cumulative primitive-root cloud under any fixed translation-invariant kernel reduces to classical Ramanujan/Mertens Fourier data. The Hardy lift genuinely escapes that quotient, but PC-103--PC-107 then match its finite/fixed-shell layer much more strongly: finite traces are cyclotomic hyperlogarithmic, and a fixed-shell trace-class Fredholm determinant has a zero divisor too sparse for Riemann density. A candidate must therefore identify the exact global operator-ideal layer where the control finally stops matching.

Prime Flute shows why functional calculus must be included explicitly. The prime flute and all-composite shift clone share compact first-relative-resolvent class. If the squared relative resolvent is globally trace class, classical invariance theory would give complete wave operators and an ordinary Krein/Birman--Krein scattering phase even though the first resolvent is only `S_2\setminus S_1`. PF-150 shows that the `S_2` endpoint is abstractly sharp, PF-149 removes the natural volume-identification gauge, and PF-151 shows that the canonical critical Wodzicki residue is area-only. None of these category properties is a primality selector by itself.

Prime Lattice sharpens normalization controls. Fixed and growing prime-power depth can change population geometry, but natural von-Mangoldt weighting collapses ordinary bulk/determinant scales; after the first `K^2` repair, normalized trace-norm equivalence removes the depth grading from every ordinary Lipschitz spectral statistic through the first growing-depth transition. A control must therefore match whether the candidate reads weak bulk, hard edge, inverse statistics, or a singular target-relative observable.

Weil Inertia shows that the control must match **representation and weight**, not just support dimension. The two-dimensional Yang free-shift incidence union is power-sparse and every fixed-`L^p` ambient restriction pays that sparsity; Cartesian pruning cannot remove it. But scalar projection to the LCM does not preserve the same sparsity: WI-076 forces at least `X/(log X)^4` effective weighted scalar moduli on one fixed slope. Thus neither ambient incidence sparsity nor scalar support density alone is the right control for a scalar large-sieve route; the remaining discriminator must lie in the actual weights, additive energy, labels, or source-specific multivariate structure.

Weil Positivity gives the parallel sign control. Exact `log n` and prime-power `Lambda` carriers persist across a whole positive fixed-shift family, while the discriminating response becomes singular, indefinite, or reference-relative. “Positive and arithmetic” is therefore a much weaker matched layer than “completed positive selector with an intrinsic global sign.”

## A control ladder

Controls should match the actual destination: measured-span/source fibers; interaction and multiplicative closure; stability modulus; geometric marking; finite/cumulative algebra; coefficient-correlation support **with its weights**; population versus hard-edge normalization; operator ideal and operator function; topology/norm/module/action; and finally the sign or continuation theorem being claimed. The correct falsifier is the strongest independently defined control that preserves everything the candidate actually reads.

## Status / novelty

The individual controls are rigorous persisted findings or literature-backed comparisons. Their hierarchy is mathematical synthesis, not a proof that surviving a control establishes arithmetic significance.

## Falsification criterion

For a concrete candidate, exhibit two systems matching every admissible input and the exact algebra/topology/operator readout on which the invariant depends but producing different invariant values without new information. Conversely, a feature that first changes at a stronger independently forced layer validates the control classification and deserves a fresh novelty audit.
