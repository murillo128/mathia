# MI-003 — Arithmetic significance begins only after a control matches the claimed information layer

**Evidence level:** supported

## Core intuition

A critical exponent, positive kernel, resonance threshold, elegant spectrum, nonlinear coupling, exact finite selector, or strong cancellation statistic is not arithmetic evidence merely because it arose from prime-labelled input. The control must match the same **measured quotient, interaction algebra, correlation/bulk layer, population normalization, operator ideal, functional calculus, topology, stability notion, and information budget** used by the candidate. The first layer at which the control ceases to match should coincide with the mathematical source the mechanism claims to detect.

## A control ladder

Controls should match the actual destination: measured-span/source fibers; interaction and multiplicative closure; representation equivalences; stability modulus; geometric marking; finite/cumulative algebra; coefficient-correlation support **with its weights**; deterministic sampling and stochastic bulk law; population versus hard-edge normalization; operator ideal and operator function; topology/norm/module/action; quantitative transfer budget; and finally the sign or continuation theorem being claimed. The correct falsifier is the strongest independently defined control that preserves everything the candidate actually reads.

## Status / novelty

The individual controls are rigorous persisted findings or literature-backed comparisons. Their hierarchy is mathematical synthesis, not a proof that surviving a control establishes arithmetic significance.

## Falsification criterion

For a concrete candidate, exhibit two systems matching every admissible input and the exact algebra/topology/operator readout on which the invariant depends but producing different invariant values without new information. Conversely, a feature that first changes at a stronger independently forced layer validates the control classification and deserves a fresh novelty audit.
