# MI-003 — Arithmetic significance begins only after a control matches the claimed information layer

**Evidence level:** supported

## Core intuition

A critical exponent, positive kernel, resonance threshold, elegant spectrum, nonlinear coupling, exact selector, RH-equivalent criterion, or strong cancellation statistic is not arithmetic evidence merely because it arose from prime-labelled input. The control must match the same **measured quotient, interaction algebra, correlation/bulk layer, population normalization, operator ideal, functional calculus, topology, stability notion, and cancellation budget** used by the candidate. The first layer at which the control ceases to match should coincide with the mathematical source the mechanism claims to detect.

AF-059--AF-063 classify powered safe lifts by convex-hull position and weighted distance-excess decay; near-isometric renorming can move the critical exponent. A proposed proof may not replace an internally cancelling carrier by separate absolute budgets and still claim to match the information used by the target. Excursion-level coupling is the first surviving mean-absolute carrier, while the Pintz RH-equivalence endpoint remains under audit.

A control matched only to the resultant divisor but not its differential/collective response is now too weak.

## A control ladder

Controls should match the actual destination: admissible fibers; interaction closure; representation equivalences; first- and higher-order stability scale; marking/provenance; finite/cumulative algebra; coefficient-correlation support with weights and cancellation signs; deterministic/stochastic bulk; operator ideal/function; regularity/information geometry; quantitative transfer budget; and finally the sign, continuation, or zero theorem claimed. The strongest independently defined control preserving all layers actually read by the candidate is the relevant falsifier.

## Status / novelty

The individual controls are rigorous persisted findings or literature-backed comparisons. Their hierarchy is mathematical synthesis, not a proof that surviving a control establishes arithmetic significance.

## Falsification criterion

For a concrete candidate, exhibit two systems matching every admissible input and exact readout layer on which the invariant depends but producing different claimed arithmetic behavior without new information. Conversely, a feature that first changes at a stronger independently forced layer validates the control classification and deserves a fresh novelty audit.

## Lean-formalizable core

- Equality on maximal admissible fibers implies equality of downstream invariants.
- Weighted/exact cancellation versus absolute-budget distinction.
- Operator-functional-calculus/ideal comparison gates.
- Boundary-model and scalarization controls.
- Correlation-robust regularity lower bounds.
