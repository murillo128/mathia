# MI-003 — Arithmetic significance begins only after matched-control subtraction

**Evidence level:** supported

## Core intuition

A critical exponent, self-dual line, positive kernel, resonance threshold, elegant spectrum, or optimized defect is not arithmetic evidence merely because it appears in a prime-derived construction. The control must match the **same information layer** the candidate claims to use. The newest evidence makes the control ladder topology-sensitive as well: a system can match tail geometry, a realized Gram architecture, finite localized spectral reality, or a positive finite selector while differing only in the boundary/completion channel where the claimed arithmetic information is supposed to live.

## Strongest justified principle

A proposed arithmetic feature belongs to the background whenever a matched control reproduces the complete input on which that feature depends.

Prime Flute now supplies controls at coordinate, gauge, coarse, and operator levels. The `p_n+1` all-composite clone has increasingly strong pant/collar/separator/gluing agreement; raw generator right limits are escaping-center gauge data; prime and clone share the same non-Gromov coarse class; and first-resolvent non-trace-class behavior is a universal local pseudodifferential obstruction. Any tail claim must therefore survive a weaker operator-native comparison after these backgrounds are removed.

Prime Lattice adds a new localized control hierarchy. PL-044 shows that finite localized spectral reality can be completely prime-free. Under boundary rescaling, PL-051 gives a universal PNT rank-one strong limit, while PL-052--PL-054 show that norm/Calkin topology retains order-one prime-log recurrence and essential partial reflections. Fixed smooth probes instead return to the classical explicit formula. A claimed “boundary spectrum” must state which topology it uses before arithmetic meaning can be assigned.

Weil Inertia now has matched controls **inside the realized four-point architecture**. Span/packing geometry can improve the abstract scalar envelope, but WI-025--WI-026 provide exact ceilings and periodic witnesses once the same single-profile pressure and shifted assembly are fixed. Better optimization no longer counts as new information after that match.

Weil Positivity supplies pairing/completion controls. Finite positive rank or shell-logdet readouts can recover `Lambda`, yet direct critical Hilbert completion is nonclosable and natural positive radial contrasts/Schur reductions delete the Weil birth operator. Matching the finite selector is therefore not enough; the control must also match the global pairing in which positivity is claimed.

## A control ladder

Controls should be chosen according to the claimed mechanism rather than by one fixed recipe:

1. **regular/density controls** test dimensionality, ordering, and smooth background;
2. **local-statistics controls** preserve gap marginals or short blocks while destroying order depth;
3. **geometric clones** preserve projective or asymptotically exact marked geometry while removing primality of the labels;
4. **representation-matched controls** preserve the full compressed matrix/operator statistic while changing the hidden configuration;
5. **finite-prefix/localized controls** preserve every inspected prime direction or even prime-free spectral reality while changing the infinite completion;
6. **topology/pairing controls** compare strong, norm/Calkin, Hilbert-form, boundary, and positive-completion channels on the same underlying data.

A candidate earns arithmetic interpretation only when the first control level at which it changes matches the information it claims to detect.

## Evidence against overgeneralization

Controls are falsifiers, not proofs. Surviving a strong control does not establish novelty or RH relevance; it only rules out a class of universal explanations. Conversely, a feature shared with a control may still be an essential carrier on which a more discriminating residual is built.

Canonical subtraction is sometimes possible, but not mandatory. When subtraction is used, its reference and topology must be forced independently of the desired singularity rather than selected post hoc.

## Status / novelty

The individual controls are rigorous persisted findings or literature-backed counterexamples. The hierarchy is a methodological mathematical synthesis.

## Falsification criterion

For a concrete candidate, exhibit two systems that match all inputs and topology/pairing data the candidate mathematically uses but yield different values of the claimed invariant without any additional information. Conversely, a feature that changes only after moving to a stronger topology or adding a target is evidence that the earlier control was correctly classified as background.

## Lean-formalizable core

- Equality-of-input/equality-of-invariant implications for deterministic constructions.
- Exact clone and matched-representation identities.
- Strong-versus-essential topology separation for moving boundary operators.
- Common-mode cancellation under positive completion.
