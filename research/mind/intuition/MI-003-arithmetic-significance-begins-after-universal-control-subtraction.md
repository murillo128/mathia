# MI-003 — Arithmetic significance begins only after the complete destination control and source-conditioned regime are matched

**Evidence level:** supported across the current evidence-bearing lines

## Core intuition

A prime- or zeta-labelled construction is not arithmetic evidence until its matched control preserves the entire information layer consumed by the final theorem. The current evidence sharpens this in both directions: destination controls can eliminate apparent asymptotic escapes, while source restrictions may have to be conditioned on the exact exceptional population rather than imposed on the bulk.

The strongest adversary must therefore match representation, sampling, symmetry, boundary geometry, transfer budget, operator category, regularity, limiting topology, and source constraints **without itself subtracting the arithmetic target**.

## Program consequence

For each proposed mechanism, identify the strongest destination control, impose the source restrictions on the **same exceptional population and scale** as the claim, and audit whether the control or regularization itself removes target arithmetic. A positive residual must survive all three steps quantitatively and in a canonical limiting category.

A universal alias remains decisive against representation-level identification even if the actual source later excludes one extremizer. Conversely, a bulk source theorem does not exclude a conditioned frontier subsequence, and a source theorem excluding a control does not restore information already erased by the representation.

## Status / novelty

The component controls are persisted findings or literature-backed specializations. Their hierarchy is synthesis, not a theorem that survival of all current controls proves arithmetic specificity.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a candidate whose claimed source-specific invariant survives only because a destination-relevant control, source conditioning, or limiting-topology choice was omitted; or derive a source theorem that excludes every matched extremizer while leaving a quantitative residual in the exact final category without subtracting the target signal.
