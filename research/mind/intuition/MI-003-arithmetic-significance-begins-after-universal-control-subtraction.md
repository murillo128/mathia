# MI-003 — Arithmetic significance begins only after matched-control subtraction

**Evidence level:** supported

## Core intuition

A critical exponent, self-dual line, positive kernel, resonance threshold, elegant spectrum, or optimized defect is not arithmetic evidence merely because it appears in a prime-derived construction. The control must match the **same information layer** the candidate claims to use. Recent results strengthen this rule further: useful adversaries can preserve exact sampled tails, full single-profile spectral defects, arbitrary finite prime prefixes, or the complete positive bulk while changing the signed information channel.

## Strongest justified principle

A proposed arithmetic feature belongs to the background whenever a matched non-prime/control system reproduces the complete input on which that feature depends.

Prime Flute supplies a hierarchy of increasingly severe controls. Integer replacement and even one compact endpoint defect reproduce selected propagation thresholds; projective dilation clones preserve the tangent process; the exact `p_n+1` all-composite clone is summably close in several collar/spine coordinates and has uniformly vanishing all-span separator distortion. PF-107--PF-110 show that one must now test the clone in an operator-native geometry rather than infer significance from whichever Fenchel--Nielsen coordinate looks largest.

Prime Lattice supplies both arithmetic and operator controls. Beurling systems preserve the free exponent cone, strong counting regularity, and arbitrarily long exact rational-prime prefixes while changing zero theory. PL-023--PL-031 add symmetry controls: exact prime-shift covariance may be too rigid, while compact/Schatten resolvent covariance, GCD/Poisson boundaries, and magnetic Hasse phases can be automatic, universal, or gauge.

Weil Inertia now has matched controls **inside the exact collapsed representation**. WI-015--WI-019 construct periodic and off-lattice configurations on which the full single-profile Gram defect remains compatible with densities near `0.6736`; WI-020 proves the local trace--energy envelope itself is sharp. These controls show that a better optimizer cannot manufacture information absent from that representation.

Weil Positivity supplies completion and sign controls. WP-019--WP-021 keep positive Hodge-square data fixed while nonzero supertrace/eta information cancels or reverses sign, and WP-023 shows that the positive critical information-divergence limit can collapse to universal simple-pole geometry even though the pre-limit score carried exact prime-power coefficients.

## A control ladder

Controls should be chosen according to the claimed mechanism rather than by one fixed recipe:

1. **regular/density controls** test dimensionality, ordering, and smooth background;
2. **local-statistics controls** preserve gap marginals or short blocks while destroying order depth;
3. **geometric clones** preserve projective or asymptotically exact marked geometry while removing primality of the labels;
4. **representation-matched controls** preserve the full compressed matrix/operator statistic while changing the hidden configuration;
5. **finite-prefix arithmetic controls** preserve every inspected prime direction while changing the infinite norm map;
6. **completion/sign controls** preserve the positive bulk or finite local data while varying the archimedean, target, asymmetry, or signed channel.

A candidate earns arithmetic interpretation only when the first control level at which it changes matches the information it claims to detect.

## Evidence against overgeneralization

Controls are falsifiers, not proofs. Surviving a strong control does not establish novelty or RH relevance; it only rules out a class of universal explanations. Conversely, a feature shared with a control may still be an essential carrier on which a more discriminating residual is built.

Canonical subtraction is sometimes possible, but not mandatory. When subtraction is used, its reference must be forced independently of the desired singularity rather than selected post hoc.

## Status / novelty

The individual controls are rigorous persisted findings or literature-backed counterexamples. The hierarchy is a methodological mathematical synthesis.

## Falsification criterion

For a concrete candidate, exhibit two systems that match all inputs the candidate mathematically uses but yield different values of the claimed arithmetic invariant. Conversely, the narrow principle would fail if an invariant identical on such matched systems could nevertheless distinguish them without any additional information.

## Lean-formalizable core

- Equality-of-input/equality-of-invariant implications for deterministic constructions.
- Exact projective/shift-clone identities and summability estimates.
- Finite-prefix indistinguishability as an obstruction to finite-coordinate decision rules.
- Abstract matched-representation lemma: an optimizer over a fixed representation has the same value on inputs with equal represented data.
