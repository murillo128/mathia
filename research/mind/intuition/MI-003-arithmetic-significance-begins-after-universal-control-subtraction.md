# MI-003 — Arithmetic significance begins only after a control matches the claimed information layer

**Evidence level:** supported

## Core intuition

A critical exponent, positive kernel, resonance threshold, elegant spectrum, nonlinear coupling, or exact finite selector is not arithmetic evidence merely because it arose from prime-labelled input. The control must match the same **measured quotient, interaction algebra, operator category, correlation layer, functional calculus, or topology** used by the candidate. The first layer at which the control ceases to match should coincide with the mathematical source the mechanism claims to detect.

## Strongest justified principle

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the abstract rule: the complete linear statistic is the measured closed span and its annihilator; complete marginals retain only their interaction support. A control that matches those exact quotients cannot be separated later without new information.

Prime Circle now gives an unusually strong interaction-layer control. Arbitrary finite canonical cotangent multigraphs, including cycles, repeated shells, and collision strata, reduce to endpoint/cyclotomic algebra; fixed-support infinite depth is finite-state, and the canonical complete-preimage growth has universal band limits. Matching only input shells while changing the graph is therefore too weak a control because graph complexity itself does not leave the retained algebra.

Prime Flute shows that operator controls must specify the functional calculus. The prime flute and all-composite shift clone share compact first-relative-resolvent class. First-resolvent trace class fails locally, but PF-146 shows that the squared relative resolvent can be trace class on the same short collar and would imply wave equivalence if globalized. “Not `S_1`” is not an operator-class distinction until the actual spectral function required by the theorem is fixed.

Prime Lattice sharpens the correlation control. At `T~X/log X`, support-only prime Gram bulk is the Poisson-sinc law under the local Hardy--Littlewood hierarchy; bounded clusters separately dominate extremes; prime-only von-Mangoldt weighting is deterministic grading; and higher prime powers are vanishing-rank for the ordinary weak bulk. A candidate bulk statistic must therefore beat controls matched to the exact local-tuple, extreme, and population-normalization layer it consumes.

Weil Inertia and Weil Positivity show analogous norm/module controls. Inertia's hierarchical `L^2` route opens to a genuine four-prime rectangle, so a vector norm does not erase the source theorem required. In positivity, the diagonal cover coinvariant quotient classicalizes to ordinary trace while the full standard operator-ideal quotient collapses to zero. A quotient or topology that kills the obstruction may also kill the arithmetic information.

## A control ladder

Controls should be matched to the actual destination: measured-span/source fibers; joint interaction support; geometric clones; interaction/cohomology algebra; finite-prefix/localized data; coefficient-correlation class; population versus hard-edge statistics; operator ideal plus functional calculus; and topology/norm/module/action. The correct falsifier is the strongest control that preserves everything the candidate actually reads.

## Status / novelty

The individual controls are rigorous persisted findings or literature-backed comparisons. Their hierarchy is methodological mathematical synthesis, not a proof that surviving a control establishes arithmetic significance.

## Falsification criterion

For a concrete candidate, exhibit two systems matching every admissible input and the exact algebra/topology/operator readout on which the invariant depends but producing different invariant values without new information. Conversely, a feature that first changes at a stronger independently forced layer validates the control classification and deserves a fresh novelty audit.
