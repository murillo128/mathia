# MI-003 — Arithmetic significance begins only after the complete destination control and source regime are matched

**Evidence level:** supported across the current evidence-bearing lines

## Core intuition

A prime- or zeta-labelled construction is not arithmetic evidence until its matched control preserves the **entire information layer consumed by the final theorem**. Convenient bulk, local, low-cardinality, asymptotic, visually distinct, or source-free controls can be either too weak or too strong. The right adversary must match the actual representation, sampling category, symmetry, transfer budget, and source constraints at the scale of the claim.

Recent findings make this gate sharper in both directions. Some supposed universal controls have been decisively beaten, forcing the frontier closer to the true source geometry; other enlarged carriers collapse once their complete symmetry or family budget is imposed.

## Strongest justified principle

Analytic Frontier now has an explicit finite-real separator. ANF-034 constructs a support-one central-notch family that beats the Montgomery--Taylor finite-real stability ratio, while ANF-035--ANF-036 prove that common vertical fibers and all conjugation-invariant configurations through four points still collapse to real controls. The first positivity-alone complex obstruction is sharp at five points. Thus ever richer real stochastic controls are no longer the relevant adversary.

Visual Exploration supplies an independent exact-control hierarchy. VIS-026 holds the full gap multiset fixed and randomizes only order; VIS-027 additionally conditions on reflection symmetry, proving that every odd Dirichlet discrepancy mode vanishes for a purely geometric reason. Residual suppression after those controls is real ordering information, but not yet an arithmetic mechanism until stronger local/denominator structure is matched.

Möbius Cancellation shows that source matching must include the transfer resource itself. MC-062--MC-064 make fresh subquadratic quadratic comparators power-separated in scale and show that constant defect is insufficient for a Mertens power saving; fidelity must decay at the rate required by the Burgess/square-divisor transfer while conductors remain in a compatible window.

Prime Lattice gives the opposite sampling warning. PL-155--PL-157 show that once Suzuki's completed scalar/kernel already contains the arithmetic, a fixed two-prime face or even non-prime sub-`3/4` meshes can retain RH-equivalent information. An RH-complete observation set is therefore not automatically source-specific.

Weil Inertia and Weil Positivity close further artificial escapes. WI-150--WI-152 show that full bounded-depth scalar universality squeezes negative spectral mass into a quantitatively expensive central layer. WP-146--WP-147 show that keeping the faithful zero-order resultant does not gain a simple sign: mixed-prime chains create unbounded two-sided primitive inertia that no fixed finite-dimensional repair removes.

## Program consequence

For each proposal, identify the maximal admissible source fiber and strengthen controls until they match the final configuration class, deterministic quotient, symmetry, observation mesh, operator category, transfer theorem, and quantitative source regime. A positive requires a residual that survives this **complete source-appropriate family** with a usable margin; neither survival against an obsolete surrogate nor failure under an unrealistically broad source-free family is decisive.

## Status / novelty

The component controls are persisted findings or literature-backed specializations. Their hierarchy is synthesis, not a theorem that surviving all known controls proves arithmetic specificity.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a candidate whose claimed source-specific invariant survives only because a destination-relevant control was omitted, or conversely prove a source theorem that excludes every matched extremizer and leaves a quantitative residual in the exact final category.

## Lean-formalizable core

- Downstream invariance on maximal measured fibers.
- Exact symmetry/order matched controls.
- Uniform-family transfer budgets.
- Observation-set controls after scalar completion.
- Post-processing monotonicity of lost provenance.
