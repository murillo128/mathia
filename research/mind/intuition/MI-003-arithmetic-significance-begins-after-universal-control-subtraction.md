\[
\Lambda_{p,r;q}=\{(h,k):hp+kr\equiv0\pmod q\},

Thus matching the control must now include not only the **orbit closure** but also the relevant **classical dual-lattice resolution geometry**. What may still be source-sensitive is the distribution of shortest/near-shortest dual vectors along prime triples, their directions, or their coupling to a source-forced observable that matched nonprime rational rotations do not reproduce.

The remaining Prime-Circle candidates are therefore finer or genuinely source-conditioned: arithmetic statistics of `\lambda` or `\Delta_B`, microscopic/shrinking spectra, singular log-determinant/cocycle data, transfer-norm/Lyapunov growth, subleading projective fluctuations, or cross-level operators.
