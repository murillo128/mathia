# MI-020 — Incommensurability removes collisions, but the residual fibre may stay target-mixed, collapse to decoding, or disappear under closure

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from exact results [AF-375](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-375-sampling-difference-subgroup-classifies-separable-determinant-gauges.md), [AF-376](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-376-uniform-separation-defeats-e-polya-frequency-determination.md), [PC-318](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-318-irrational-radial-rays-are-tensor-decoders-and-barnes-zeta-packets.md), and [PL-383](../../prime_lattice/findings/PL-383-two-prime-adjoint-closure-erases-discrete-dilation-lattice.md).

Several current constructions use the same algebraic move—make sampling/frequency scales incommensurate so a known collision family disappears—but land at different bad endpoints for compression.

In Arithmetic Fidelity, `H_E=<E-E>` controls the continuous separable multiplicative gauge of sampled Toeplitz minors. An irrational second lattice phase makes `H_E` dense and kills every nonconstant periodic separable factor modulo exponential tilt. AF-376 then shows that this does **not** make the observation determining on Ganzburg's broad class `L`: the actual two-phase row set remains uniformly discrete, so compact-support kernels narrower than its minimum spacing satisfy every sampled determinant inequality while failing global Pólya-frequency structure. Collision/gauge removal leaves a non-separable target-mixed fibre.

In Prime Circle, irrational radial slopes remove residue-frequency collisions so completely that the trace becomes a decoder. A Vandermonde inversion recovers the full residue tensor, canonical normalization recovers the source packets, and the Mellin image is a Barnes double-zeta packet. Here collision removal does not leave a useful compressed quotient; it collapses toward source reconstruction.

Prime Lattice supplies a third endpoint in which the residual distinction disappears only **after a topology-changing closure**. In logarithmic coordinates the Nyman dilations are sampled right shifts `S_(log p)`. For two incommensurate times, such as `log 2` and `log 3`, adjoining adjoints generates their integer differences; those times are dense. Strong continuity then recovers the full continuous right-shift semigroup, and its von Neumann algebra is all of `B(L^2(R_+))`. Incommensurability plus self-adjoint/SOT closure has not exposed finer arithmetic—it has erased the discrete sampling completely.

The shared principle is therefore not “irrationality adds arithmetic richness.” It is:

**incommensurability destroys a specified alias class; the mathematical value is determined by the fibre and topology that remain.**

That residual object can stay target-mixed for a different geometric reason, become essentially a source decoder, or be universalized away by a closure that turns dense sampled differences into the full ambient continuous model. A useful intermediate representation requires the harder case: target-pure for the declared discriminator, not already a decoder, and still source-sensitive in the topology actually consumed downstream.

This is not a universal theorem about irrational sampling. It is a reusable audit forced by the exact constructions: after removing collisions, classify the observation algebra, the actual observation geometry/source class, **and the closure operations later applied**. A distinction visible for the distinguished generators may disappear after adjoining adjoints or taking strong closure, just as a nonidentifiable fibre may survive despite a dense difference subgroup.
