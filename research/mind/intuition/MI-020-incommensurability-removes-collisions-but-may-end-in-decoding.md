# MI-020 — Incommensurability removes collisions, but the resulting fibre may be source recovery rather than a useful quotient

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from exact results [AF-375](../../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-375-sampling-difference-subgroup-classifies-separable-determinant-gauges.md) and [PC-318](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-318-irrational-radial-rays-are-tensor-decoders-and-barnes-zeta-packets.md).

Two current constructions show the same algebraic move with different endpoints: make two sampling/frequency scales incommensurate so additive collisions disappear.

In Arithmetic Fidelity, the relevant object is the subgroup `H_E=<E-E>` generated by sampling differences. A single lattice, or finitely many commensurate phases, leaves a discrete subgroup and hence a periodic continuous multiplicative gauge. Adding an incommensurate phase makes `H_E` dense and collapses that separable gauge to the universal exponential tilt. This improves fidelity, but AF-375 explicitly stops short of a determining theorem because non-separable aliases may remain.

In Prime Circle, the relevant frequencies are `lambda i+mu j`. Rational `lambda/mu` creates collisions and finite Hurwitz/quasipolynomial recoding. Irrational `lambda/mu` removes every residue-frequency collision, but the result is not a subtle intermediate invariant: a Vandermonde inversion recovers the entire residue tensor, and canonical normalization recovers both source packets. Collision removal has gone all the way to decoding.

The shared principle is therefore not “irrationality adds arithmetic richness.” It is:

**incommensurability changes the observational fibre by destroying additive aliases; what matters is the fibre that remains.**

After an irrational/incommensurate repair, one must classify whether the new fibre is target-pure but nontrivial, still target-mixed through a different alias, or essentially a singleton/source decoder. Only the first case provides the kind of compressed faithful carrier sought by many Mathia routes.

This is not a universal theorem about every irrational sampling scheme. It is a reusable audit forced by the two exact constructions above: prove the residual fibre before interpreting collision removal as progress toward the destination.
