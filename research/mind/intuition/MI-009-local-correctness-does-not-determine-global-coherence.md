# MI-009 — Source coherence can disappear in every finite view and reappear only in the compatible family

**Evidence level:** supported

## Core intuition

Locally correct pieces do not determine the global coherence consumed by the target. The newest evidence sharpens this from a qualitative warning into several exact projective boundaries: one finite representation may admit many source aliases even when the whole compatible tower is source-identifying; every fixed local window of a growing carrier may be classical even when a long cocycle remains unclassified; and a canonical equilibrium category may exist while erasing the critical representation distinction that motivated it.

The missing object is therefore often a source-forced compatibility law whose **breadth, depth, or category scales with the target**.

## Strongest justified claim

Prime Lattice gives the clearest projective example. PL-270 proves that a fixed Weil Galerkin band sees only a `2N+1`-dimensional source quotient and admits exact positive sparse aliases of the prime-power stream. PL-271 proves that no single nontrivial source away from zero frequency can match **all** bands: the full tower identifies the source. Finite ambiguity and projective identity coexist exactly.

Prime Circle supplies a dynamical analogue. PC-257 reduces the complete finite shared-upper spectrum to a width-two transfer chain. PC-258 shows that every local coefficient and fixed return window is generated by a rational-torus translation. Only the depth-growing product remains open; local classicality does not decide long-range cocycle behavior.

Weil Positivity gives a category analogue. The bilateral crossed product preserves the critical product automorphism but lacks nonzero-temperature KMS equilibrium; the Toeplitz repair restores a unique KMS state whose coefficient sector is tracial and makes every automorphism spatial. Better global coherence in one sense can erase the specific critical coherence needed in another.

Farey Discrepancy and Robin Extremal show how global compatibility can survive only through exceptional sets. Farey has correct fixed-row exponents and positive multiplicative-chain average occupation but may still admit sparse bad horizons. Robin's clean first-layer chain is rigid enough that every nonterminal fringe bit vanishes; any signal is pushed onto exceptional support-edge interruptions.

Visual Exploration closes a different finite-family loophole: one deterministic growing-complexity Gram selector can pass every fixed finite-order Haar test. A countable family of locally correct distributions need not imply source randomness unless the required complexity grows jointly with scale.

## Program consequence

Whenever a candidate argument uses a finite projection, fixed-depth moment, local return window, individual event, or canonical representation category, ask what compatibility data are lost and whether the target consumes them. If the answer is “the family over all scales,” formulate that family explicitly rather than extrapolating from one member.

Conversely, recovering the whole family is not automatically the final theorem. PL-271 reconstructs the source but not Weil positivity; PC-258 identifies the return coding but not its long transfer asymptotics. The compatibility law must be sign- or target-relevant, not merely information-preserving.

## Counterevidence / boundary cases

These examples do not prove every useful theorem requires an infinite hierarchy. Arithmetic Fidelity and other lines contain finite closure principles once the exact source category and target margin are fixed. A bounded-order theorem is entirely possible, but its closure must be proved rather than inferred from activity at low order.

Projective identifiability can also be poorly conditioned; exact family recovery is not the same as quantitative stable recovery.

## Epistemic status

**Supported cross-line principle:** finite/local correctness can coexist with large source ambiguity, while compatible families can restore identity without yet restoring the target sign. The load-bearing theorem is the source-specific compatibility law at the breadth actually consumed downstream.

## Novelty/prior-art status

This note synthesizes persisted findings and their recorded prior-art boundaries. It makes no independent novelty claim.

## Falsification criterion

Show in one cited setting that the target-relevant compatible family is already determined by a bounded finite view under the stated hypotheses, or produce two distinct admissible sources that remain indistinguishable across the whole family where projective identifiability is claimed. Either would narrow the principle.
