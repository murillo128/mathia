# MI-009 — Locally correct pieces do not determine the global coherence consumed by the target

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis from exact cancellation, coupling, interpolation, factorization, and covariance boundaries

## Core intuition

A construction can be exactly correct on every local piece and still fail globally because the target depends on how those pieces are coupled, cancelled, selected, or jointly fluctuating. Local marginals, finite prefixes, individually saturating horizons, positive factors, or correctly calibrated means do not determine the coherence law that the final theorem consumes.

The recurring missing object is therefore not “more local accuracy” but a **source-forced global compatibility relation** whose effect survives the final transform.

## Strongest justified principle

Weil Positivity gives the cleanest marginal example. WP-234 shows that the Bost--Connes and Gamma/log kernels have exactly the same critical half-density on every prime ray and are both positive definite, yet they correspond to different joint Cauchy dependence laws. Indeed a continuum of positive couplings has the same ray marginals. Local prime-axis correctness cannot determine mixed-prime geometry.

Arithmetic Fidelity supplies a cancellation example. AF-237--AF-238 show that linearly deep local Taylor prefixes can be individually exact source coefficients and still create a false exponential Li root rate because the global binomial cancellation orbit is cut short. Even completing away trivial zeros only moves the artifact to a known critical-line zero pair. Coefficientwise fidelity does not determine transform-level fidelity.

Farey Discrepancy supplies an interpolation example. FD-018--FD-020 classify when one unrestricted source can plant near-extremal GCD-duality behavior across integer-ratio horizons. Local equality-ray structure at each horizon is compatible with global saturation whenever bounded squarefree transitions are sufficiently absent or sparse. The missing constraint, if any, must come from the actual Möbius source rather than the separate horizon optima.

Prime Lattice supplies a factorization example. PL-251 shows that canonical finite-dimensional representation operations may be globally meaningful while their trivial isotypic component still splits before Artin readout; PL-252 shows that a positive Rankin--Selberg self-correlation can be globally canonical and retain every hypothetical off-line zeta zero as a direct factor. Correct global companions do not constrain the principal divisor unless the decisive relation is nonfactorizing.

Visual Exploration adds the stochastic version. VIS-130--VIS-132 progressively fix the source-window mean and identify an exactly removable `1/L` diagonal common mode, but the projected off-diagonal covariance remains a separate object. A correct mean null does not determine the joint fluctuation scale at which an arithmetic residual would be tested.

## Program consequence

Whenever a candidate is assembled from local pieces, state the compatibility object explicitly: dependence law, cancellation orbit, common-source constraint, nonfactorizing global relation, or covariance structure. Test that object before promoting local agreement to target evidence.

A useful theorem should show either that the source uniquely or coercively selects the needed compatibility class, or that all admissible compatibility classes yield the same target conclusion. Otherwise the unresolved global degree of freedom remains a matched control.

## Counterevidence / boundary

The examples involve different mathematical notions of coherence and do not establish one universal gluing theorem. Some targets may genuinely factor into local conditions, and a sufficiently strong source theorem may select the required coupling cheaply. The synthesis only says that the cited current routes have not earned that step from their local data alone.

## Epistemic status

**Supported cross-line principle that local correctness must be supplemented by the specific global coupling/cancellation/fluctuation law consumed by the target; no universal theorem or RH implication is claimed.**

## Falsification criterion

Show in one cited setting that all locally admissible configurations automatically have the same target-relevant global coherence, contrary to the persisted matched controls, or prove that the target functional is insensitive to the unresolved coupling. A positive continuation should derive the source law that closes the global compatibility gap.
