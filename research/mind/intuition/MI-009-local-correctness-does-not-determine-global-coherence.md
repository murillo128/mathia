# MI-009 — Global coherence must be target-relevant, not merely projectively recoverable

**Evidence level:** supported

## Core intuition

Finite views can alias the source, while compatible families recover it; local windows can be classical while a long product remains open; and richer dynamical categories can remain thermodynamically equivalent. The newest evidence adds a stronger warning: even **perfect source recovery across both band and cutoff can coexist with universal atomwise sign failure, while the cutoff coherence itself is generic affine kinematics**.

The global law that matters is therefore not coherence for its own sake, but a compatibility relation whose breadth/depth/category scales with the target and constrains its sign or coercivity.

## Strongest justified claim

Prime Lattice is the exact projective example. PL-270 gives fixed-band aliases and PL-271 removes them in the full tower. PL-272--PL-273 show why recovery is not the destination: every active interior source atom is eventually indefinite. PL-274 then adds all cutoffs: the full `(L,N)` family identifies the underlying log-source exactly, but the inter-cutoff transport is the same affine law for any locally finite source measure. Source identity plus universal coherence still does not create order.

Prime Circle supplies the long-depth analogue. The complete finite spectrum is a fixed-width rational-torus cocycle; common dilation only repeats blocks and reflection halves the independent word. Only primitive parity-reduced depth-growing behavior can still distinguish the arithmetic sequence from its local torus model.

Weil Positivity supplies the category analogue. Stabilization, arbitrary spatial compact clocks, and bounded non-product module couplings can all look globally richer while staying KMS-Morita equivalent to the original gauge system and preserving its empty nonzero-temperature equilibrium cone.

Farey Discrepancy and Robin Extremal show coherence surviving only through exceptional geometry. Farey has correct exponents, positive terminal bulk, and logarithmic-density control yet may still have rare low-row bad horizons. Robin's fringe hosts are base-two, lacunary, summable, and threshold-dimension zero even while a sufficiently off-critical false-RH fan must expose polynomially many states.

Visual Exploration gives a quantitative fixed-family null: an omitted prime has a dimension-coded collision tail and polynomial discrepancy regime. A zeta observable must separate from that family in a jointly growing regime rather than merely fail a fixed decoder.

## Program consequence

Whenever a candidate uses one band, cutoff, fixed moment, local window, individual event, or canonical category, identify what compatible family is actually consumed by the target. Then ask whether recovering that family imposes the needed sign/coercivity, or merely reconstructs source identity through a generic transport law.

Projective recovery is infrastructure. The decisive theorem is the source-specific relation **among** recovered pieces that the target sign cannot ignore.

## Counterevidence / boundary cases

Not every theorem needs an infinite hierarchy; a finite closure can be sufficient when proved in the exact target category. Nor are all cross-cutoff laws generic: PL-274 classifies the affine normalization transport, leaving room for extra von-Mangoldt-specific relations. The principle only rejects extrapolating target force from local correctness or source identity without such a relation.

## Epistemic status

**Supported global-coherence principle strengthened by exact order, generic-transport, and Morita counterexamples:** compatible families can restore source identity while leaving target sign completely open.

## Falsification criterion

Show in one cited setting that the target-relevant global compatibility law is already determined by a bounded finite view under the same hypotheses, or produce two distinct sources that remain indistinguishable across a family claimed to be projectively identifying.
