# MI-009 — Nonlocal reconstruction matters only when the analytic object is independently selected and changes the source-to-continuum spectral comparison

**Evidence level:** supported by the Weil-positivity carrier/category/sign/compression results through WP-275; the spectral-type no-go is representation-theoretic and not a theorem about every arithmetic completion.

WP-267--WP-270 separate source coherence, hostability and category descent. The canonical pole-completed carrier is tempered if and only if RH, so ordinary analytic cleanup is already target-strength. WP-271--WP-272 show that finite-order conditional positivity and continuous scalar filtering cannot repair the sign while retaining the Mangoldt atoms.

WP-273 identifies the next gate. A positive compression of the canonical positive-minus-positive carrier is exactly a contraction from the arithmetic sample component to the pole continuum. For function-generated ranges this is a sampling/reconstruction inequality, and localizable continuous spaces fail. A survivor must therefore be globally nonlocal.

WP-274 shows that necessary nonlocality is not sufficient. The normalized arithmetic samples form the positive Herglotz measure `sigma_Lambda` with masses `Lambda(n)/(n+1)`. Clark and de Branges inverse theories can reconstruct positive analytic models from that target measure. Those constructions are valid but universal: the target carrier is supplied first and the model is reverse-engineered to return it.

WP-275 adds a different causal obstruction. Before any inverse model is chosen, the natural arithmetic log-energy representation is pure point while the pole continuum has nonatomic absolutely continuous multiplication spectrum. A bounded map on an invariant source domain that preserves the canonical modulation/time action exactly must vanish. The same applies to the source-native prime-power Fock energy representation.

The explanatory direction therefore has two independent requirements. The nonlocal object must arise from **independent source geometry before the desired carrier is installed as spectral data**, and that source construction must **change, twist, enlarge or break the canonical pure-point-to-continuum spectral realization** before positivity is demanded. Otherwise one gets either an inverse-representation tautology or a zero exact intertwiner.

The compatibility ledger is now: **hostability, category descent, local sign, arithmetic retention, nonlocal reconstruction, independent model selection and spectral-type compatibility are separate gates.** A noncommuting boundary operation, enlarged mixed carrier or source-forced change of representation can evade WP-275; mere nonlocality cannot.

**Boundary.** WP-275 does not rule out non-equivariant Clark/de Branges/canonical-system routes, unbounded or distributional couplings, or models whose independently derived boundary representation has continuous spectral type. The full Gamma/polar completion remains an additional obligation.
