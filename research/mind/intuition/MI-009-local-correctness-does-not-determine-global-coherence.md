# MI-009 — Source-native hostability does not determine positive spectral admissibility, global coherence, or Weil orientation

**Evidence level:** supported by the Weil-positivity carrier/category results through WP-270; this is not a statement about every arithmetic completion.

WP-267--WP-269 separate three layers that are easy to conflate. Standard relation closure can destroy critical common-vacuum coherence; a source-generated rapid-energy rigging can nevertheless host the singular critical amplitude; and the positive stationary spectrum generated by that amplitude can still lie outside the tempered and Fourier-hyperfunction categories.

WP-270 tests the canonical cancellation suggested by the explicit formula. Subtracting the zeta-pole density changes the growth category in exactly the right direction, but the resulting signed critical Mangoldt distribution is tempered **if and only if RH**. This is an exact prior-art identification, not a new criterion.

The consequence is structural: source-forced cancellation and category descent are not automatically harmless analytic preprocessing. A descent from exponential growth to ordinary temperedness may itself encode the target theorem. Any proposed completion must state which part is unconditional, which cancellation is source-forced, and whether the category assumption already excludes off-line zeros.

Even when a host category is admissible, the missing sign remains separate. The pole-completed object is signed, and a larger ultra-hyperfunctional category merely permits the unmodified positive spectrum to exist. Neither fact supplies the finite--archimedean coupling or Weil orientation.

The reusable warning is that **hostability, growth cancellation, positive-definite category, global coherence, and arithmetic orientation are independent compatibility gates**. A mathematically canonical singular host can be too small for the spectrum, while a canonical cancellation can be strong enough to be RH-equivalent.

**Boundary.** WP-270 does not rule out larger categories, different source quotients, or a genuinely new proof of the RH-equivalent descent. It rules out treating classical pole subtraction plus temperedness as a free preliminary step.
