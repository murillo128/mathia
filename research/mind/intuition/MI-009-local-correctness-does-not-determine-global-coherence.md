# MI-009 — A source-native continuum can emerge in an infinite-volume tangent, but global coherence still requires an independent operator and fluctuation law

**Evidence level:** supported by Weil-positivity results through WP-278. The high-energy tangent theorem is PNT-derived measure-level convergence; it is not a strong-resolvent limit, a positivity theorem or an RH criterion.

WP-267--WP-274 separate source coherence, hostability, category descent, sign and inverse reconstruction. A nonlocal positive analytic model can be reconstructed from an already supplied target measure, so host existence is explanatory only when the object is selected independently of the desired answer.

WP-275--WP-277 then isolate the spectral-category obstruction. The canonical arithmetic log-energy representation is pure point with compact resolvent. Exact bounded intertwiners into the absolutely continuous pole representation vanish, and bounded or relative-bound-`<1` mixing between compact-resolvent sectors preserves compact resolvent. Ordinary source surgery on one fixed Hilbert space cannot create the missing continuum.

WP-278 shows that the surviving infinite-volume category is not merely hypothetical. Recenter the weighted Mangoldt comb `Lambda(n)n^{-sigma}` at high logarithmic energy and normalize by its local mass growth. Its vague tangent is `cosh((1-sigma)x)dx`; at the source-selected critical weight `sigma=1/2`, this is exactly `cosh(x/2)dx`, the pole continuum already present in the completed carrier.

This supplies **continuum provenance** without supplying global coherence. The tangent uses only first-order prime density and survives generalized-prime or even full-integer matched controls. The RH-sensitive information remains in the centered fluctuations around that continuum, the missing Gamma/archimedean completion, and the sign-producing global operation.

The causal order is therefore now: derive the source-native infinite-volume background; retain the arithmetic fluctuations through a canonical operator/boundary/cohomological lift; supply the independent archimedean term; only then ask for Weil positivity. Reconstructing the pole continuum is no longer an arbitrary modeling step, but it is still far from a sign theorem.

**Boundary.** Vague tangent convergence does not give a common Hilbert space, strong-resolvent convergence, an isometry, exact covariance or positive compression. Global subtraction of the pole density remains RH-strength in the canonical centered carrier. The result narrows the viable architecture; it does not complete it.
