# MI-009 — Source-native continuum and logarithmic growth still do not determine global arithmetic coherence

**Evidence level:** supported by Weil-positivity results through [WP-304](../../weil_positivity/findings/WP-304-bounded-source-resolvents-are-too-small-pole-normalized-mangoldt-energy-yields-only-an-oriented-universal-logarithmic-herglotz-tail.md). The high-energy tangent theorem is PNT-derived measure-level convergence; the resolvent/Herglotz statements distinguish finite-mass decay from renormalized infinite-mass growth. None of these is a strong-resolvent limit, a Weil-positivity theorem or an RH criterion.

WP-267--WP-274 separate source coherence, hostability, category descent, sign and inverse reconstruction. A nonlocal positive analytic model can be reconstructed from an already supplied target measure, so host existence is explanatory only when the object is selected independently of the desired answer.

WP-275--WP-277 isolate a spectral-category obstruction. The canonical arithmetic log-energy representation is pure point with compact resolvent. Exact bounded intertwiners into the absolutely continuous pole representation vanish, and bounded or relative-bound-`<1` mixing between compact-resolvent sectors preserves compact resolvent. Ordinary source surgery on one fixed Hilbert space cannot create the missing continuum.

WP-278 shows that a source-native continuum nevertheless appears in an infinite-volume tangent. Recenter the weighted Mangoldt comb `Lambda(n)n^-sigma` at high logarithmic energy and normalize by its local mass growth. Its vague tangent is `cosh((1-sigma)x)dx`; at `sigma=1/2`, this is the same `cosh(x/2)dx` pole continuum appearing in the completed carrier. This supplies continuum provenance, but only from first-order prime density; matched generalized-prime or full-integer controls retain the same background.

WP-304 sharpens the distinction from continuum provenance to **response scale**. For a self-adjoint source operator and bounded incidence `V`, the compressed resolvent satisfies

`||V^*(H-iy)^(-1)V|| <= ||V||^2/y`.

Finite compressed spectral mass therefore cannot generate the Gamma/digamma logarithm. The first natural way across that barrier is the pole-normalized positive-energy Mangoldt measure, whose cumulative mass is `R+O(1)`. Its regularized Herglotz response has `Re m(iy)=-log y+O(1)` and `Im m(iy)=pi/2+O(1/y)`.

But this apparently stronger match is still background-level information. Every positive measure with cumulative mass `R+O(1)` has the same leading logarithm, including Lebesgue measure, so the log is a **unit-density effect** rather than a Riemann-specific response. Restoring the canonical even `+/- log n` carrier cancels the real logarithm identically. The source-specific information must therefore live in what the leading continuum/log asymptotic forgets: orientation, subleading fluctuations, exact pole/Gamma structure, finite--archimedean coupling and the global sign-producing operation.

The causal order is now more constrained. Derive a source-native infinite-volume background; identify which mass/renormalization class can carry the required response; retain arithmetic fluctuations through a canonical operator/boundary/scattering/cohomological lift; justify the one-sided orientation rather than choose it for target matching; couple the independent archimedean structure; only then ask for Weil positivity. Producing the continuum or even the correct logarithmic scale is not the same as producing the arithmetic completion.

**Boundary.** Vague tangent convergence does not give a common Hilbert space, strong-resolvent convergence, exact covariance or positive compression. WP-304 does not rule out unbounded boundary maps, scattering or determinant-type observables. Its universal-log control does not prove that every subleading renormalized response is universal. The durable distinction is that source-native background geometry may be real while the RH-sensitive coherence survives only in finer information that the background limit and leading Herglotz asymptotic erase.
