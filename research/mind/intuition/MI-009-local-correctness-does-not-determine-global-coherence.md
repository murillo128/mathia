# MI-009 — Locally correct pieces do not determine the global coherence consumed by the target

**Evidence level:** supported

## Core intuition

A construction can be exact on many local pieces and still fail globally because the target depends on how those pieces are coupled, cancelled, selected, transported, or jointly fluctuating. The latest evidence strengthens this beyond the earlier all-orders/marginal examples: local event geometry can be exact but template-universal, local attenuation can be exact but amplified by reservoirs, and even a correct representation change can be arithmetically empty unless the global coupling law is supplied.

The missing object is therefore a source-forced compatibility law whose **breadth, order, or category scales with the target**, unless a theorem proves a finite closure principle.

## Strongest justified claim

Weil Positivity provides an all-orders and category hierarchy. WP-234--WP-241 show that all fixed interaction orders or a scalar global normalizer can leave target-relevant coupling free. WP-248 then proves that the critical all-prime product state is genuinely disjoint from the spherical quasi-local representation, while WP-249 shows that making the product automorphism inner in a crossed product does not manufacture the missing arithmetic: the canonical shift/positive Fourier core is universal or finite-support local. Correct local rotations plus algebraic transport still do not determine a global Weil sign.

Prime Lattice supplies the eventwise version. PL-264 proves that every prime-power singular jet in the finite CvS path is universal apart from the inserted von Mangoldt scalar and edge location. Single-edge negativity, interlacing, and Weyl identities are all locally correct but cannot be the Riemann-specific sign selector. The missing law must couple multiple events and the archimedean/polar completion. The proposed localized-Weil clue asks whether Suzuki's finite-radius source operator can serve as an exact nonlocal destination for that coupling.

Prime Flute gives a geometric analogue. PF-279 derives the exact complete-cut Schur formula: local boundary impedance is insufficient because the cross-cut norm also contains the extension masses of the endpoint-normalized reservoir states. A favorable edge estimate can therefore fail after global embedding even in a positive scalar chain.

Robin Extremal supplies a useful negative control on selector coherence. RE-038 forces a same-block monotone selector fan across a continuum of threshold exponents, but RE-039 proves that the entire convex-envelope fan, its exact secant laws, the small-height regime, logarithmic spacing, and the inherited quantitative witness can be manufactured by positive synthetic heights with no arithmetic input. The global coherence that remains to be explained is not selector kinematics by itself but its **joint coupling to the translated standard/reciprocal prime-race law**.

Farey Discrepancy and Arithmetic Fidelity show two different closure boundaries. FD-040 proves that every fixed finite supercritical Mellin packet is coerced by nonsquarefree Jordan rows, while the remaining physical possibility lies in genuinely growing/infinite spectral complexity. AF-258--AF-259 show that an apparently global Liouville-avoidance problem can instead close under a strong output-coverage hypothesis: periodic completeness reduces the two-mode obstruction to profinite congruence leakage and bounded odd-multiplier classes at fixed margin. Finite closure is possible, but it must be proved in the exact source/sampler category.

Visual Exploration supplies the deterministic-delay hierarchy. VIS-151--VIS-153 classify every fixed-dimensional equal-step consecutive Gram-lag vector through Newton finite differences of the inverse Gram clock. Additional finite delay coordinates buy higher clock-cancellation order but no new source. Escaping the null requires a dimension, support, sampling law, or analytic coordinate that genuinely grows beyond that closure theorem.

## Program consequence

Whenever a candidate is assembled from locally correct pieces, state the compatibility object explicitly and ask how its required order, breadth, selector/source coupling, reservoir mass, or representation category grows with scale. Either derive that law from the source or prove a closure theorem that makes the unresolved degrees of freedom irrelevant.

In particular, do not infer a global sign from eventwise sign, complete-cut decay from an isolated edge, arithmetic selector rigidity from convex-envelope kinematics, arithmetic positivity from an algebraic crossed-product implementation, or source randomness from fixed-dimensional clock-generated Haar behavior.

## Counterevidence / boundary cases

The examples do not prove that every useful compatibility law is infinite-order or infinite-dimensional. Arithmetic Fidelity supplies an explicit counterexample to that overstatement: periodic completeness plus continued-fraction structure yields a finite profinite control at any fixed positive margin. Determinantal, automorphic, or other source structure may similarly close a hierarchy, but that closure must itself be established in the target regime.

Nor does local universality imply global universality. The full ordered von Mangoldt event stream, variable-reservoir geometry, a prime-race-coupled selector fan, or a native critical-sector state can carry information absent from each local piece separately.

## Epistemic status

**Supported cross-line principle:** local correctness, even at many fixed constraints or inside an exact local operator model, is insufficient when the target consumes a source-forced global coherence law; finite closure is a theorem to prove, not an assumption, and purely algebraic coherence must be separated from arithmetic coupling.

## Novelty/prior-art status

This note synthesizes current persisted findings and their recorded prior-art boundaries. It makes no independent novelty claim.

## Falsification criterion

Show in one cited setting that the persisted free configurations cannot vary the target-relevant global coherence despite satisfying the stated local constraints, or derive a source-specific bounded-order/breadth/category closure theorem that fully determines the target in the exact regime.
