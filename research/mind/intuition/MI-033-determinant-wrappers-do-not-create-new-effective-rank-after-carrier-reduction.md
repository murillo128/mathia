# MI-033 — Determinant wrappers do not create new effective rank after carrier reduction

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of [MC-439](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-439-principal-minor-determinant-full-interaction-fixed-rank-collapse.md), [MC-440](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-440-path-distance-determinant-buries-gap-order-two-degrees-down.md), and [PC-392](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-392-fixed-gap-log-determinants-cannot-amplify-the-thin-multi-deletion-contrast-sector.md). The determinant problems are different; the shared statement is a carrier-and-scale audit, not a transfer of formulas or constants.

A nonlinear observable can look much richer than the linear object from which it is built. Determinants are a particularly strong temptation: they mix all coordinates multiplicatively and can remain sensitive to low-rank perturbations that disappear from bulk empirical spectra. The current Möbius and prime-circle lines show that this nonlinear wrapping must still be evaluated **after** the surviving carrier has been reduced exactly and at the scale where source-specific variation first appears.

In Möbius Cancellation, principal-minor determinants pass the full Boolean-interaction gate: Möbius convolution gives `det(K-I_r)` rather than zero. But when the pair kernel has fixed separable feature rank `d`, Sylvester reduction turns the `r x r` determinant into a `d x d` determinant whose entries are additive prime-feature moments. The nonlinear statistic survives while its effective source carrier has already collapsed to fixed dimension.

MC-440 shows that clearing the rank gate is still not the whole audit. The growing-rank path-distance kernel genuinely retains gap order, yet under common scaling its determinant has two leading homogeneous layers that depend only on the gap multiset. Different orders first separate two degrees lower, with relative size `O(X^(-2))` on a fixed-shape dilation family. Effective rank can grow while the desired source relation remains quantitatively subleading.

In Prime Circle, the irreducible simultaneous-Kron correction lives on the deleted-vertex contrast sector. The regularized determinant ratio reduces exactly to a determinant on that sector, and positivity bounds its trace mass. On fixed polynomial primorial windows, every fixed-gap or moderately closing-gap normalized log-determinant correction therefore vanishes like `O(log p/(p lambda_p))`. Taking a determinant does not turn a vanishing-density contrast carrier into an extensive bulk one.

The reusable audit is narrower than “determinants are weak.” First identify the exact carrier that survives the source projection, quotient or elimination. Then compute its effective rank **and** the first scale at which the desired source relation changes the determinant. A genuine determinant escape must show an additional mechanism explicitly—for example growing effective rank with leading-order source sensitivity, a stable intrinsic subtraction exposing a subleading ordinal layer, a singular spectral scale, a set-dependent kernel that defeats fixed-feature reduction, or an unnormalized exceptional contribution.

**Boundary.** This is not a general theorem about determinants. MC-439 concerns divisor Möbius projection of principal-minor statistics with fixed separable rank; MC-440 concerns common-scale path-distance determinants; PC-392 concerns positive Laplacian Kron reduction and regularized log-determinants on fixed polynomial windows. Other growing-rank kernels, singular pseudodeterminants, signed operators and other nonlinear observables remain separate mathematical regimes.
