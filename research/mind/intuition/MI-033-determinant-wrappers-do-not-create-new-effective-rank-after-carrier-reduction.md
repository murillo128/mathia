# MI-033 — Determinant wrappers do not create new effective rank after carrier reduction

**Evidence level:** supported cross-line synthesis of [MC-439](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-439-principal-minor-determinant-full-interaction-fixed-rank-collapse.md), [MC-440](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-440-path-distance-determinant-buries-gap-order-two-degrees-down.md), [MC-441](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-441-first-order-sensitive-path-coefficient-is-adjacency-endpoint-scalar.md), [MC-442](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-442-distance-polynomial-coefficients-have-universal-sign-rigidity.md), [MC-443](../../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-443-fixed-rank-prime-gap-shapes-are-projectively-dense.md), [PC-392](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-392-fixed-gap-log-determinants-cannot-amplify-the-thin-multi-deletion-contrast-sector.md), and [PC-393](../../prime_circle/findings/PC-393-complete-survivor-connectivity-closes-the-singular-normalized-determinant-edge.md). The determinant problems are different; the shared statement is a carrier-and-scale audit, not a transfer of formulas or constants.

A determinant can mix all coordinates while still inheriting every information loss of the carrier from which it is built. In Möbius Cancellation, MC-439 first shows that a principal-minor determinant may survive the full Boolean interaction yet collapse through fixed separable rank to additive feature moments.

The path-distance kernel clears that rank obstruction but exposes two further reductions. MC-440 places gap order two homogeneous degrees below permutation-blind leading terms, and MC-441 factors the first order-sensitive coefficient through the single endpoint-plus-nearest-neighbor scalar `J(g)`. MC-442 then proves that every nonconstant coefficient of `det(XD-I)` has the same universal sign on the positive-gap cone and that `J(g)<0` identically. Descending determinant degree therefore cannot manufacture an arithmetic sign-cancellation layer.

MC-443 adds a provenance/topology reduction to the remaining magnitudes. At every fixed rank, normalized selected-prime gap shapes are dense in the full positive projective simplex. Hence every continuous scale-invariant finite vector of normalized determinant coefficients has the same image closure on prime-derived and generic positive-gap shapes. The determinant may retain rich magnitude variation without creating a robust rational-prime range discriminator.

Prime Circle supplies the spectral analogue. Its simultaneous-Kron correction lives on a thin contrast carrier. PC-392 reduces the determinant ratio to that carrier, and PC-393 shows that the ambient constant zero mode cannot amplify it because the contrast is orthogonal to that mode while the mean-zero survivor sector remains uniformly conditioned. The normalized determinant correction stays small through the pseudodeterminant endpoint.

The reusable audit now has four gates: identify the exact source-sensitive carrier; compute its effective rank and internal conditioning; locate the first scale/feature where the desired source relation changes the determinant; then test whether that feature is actually source-separating rather than universal in sign or dense in the matched control space. Determinant nonlinearity creates none of these resources by itself.

**Boundary.** This is not a general theorem about determinants. The Möbius statements concern fixed-rank separable kernels and finite-rank positive path-distance geometry; the density obstruction is fixed-rank and continuous/projective. The Prime Circle statements concern positive complete Laplacian Kron reduction. Growing-rank shape distributions, absolute-scale arithmetic, discontinuous invariants, signed operators, other kernels and exceptional unnormalized determinants remain separate regimes.
