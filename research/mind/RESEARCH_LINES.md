## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than merely transport, refactor or renorm the source?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) and [MI-028](intuition/MI-028-complete-measurement-families-can-close-an-escape-by-reconstructing-the-classical-object.md) remain the common interface audit.

Prime Circle now separates three automorphism outcomes. Full noncommutative depth can recover the centered source while triangular spectralization erases its oriented coordinate. PC-352 shows that even a non-solvable `SL_2` development is spectrally control-blind when reciprocal parity is inner. PC-353 proves the boundary is sharp: making parity outer in `SL_3` can make source and control spectra unequal, but only through classical contragredient duality `D -> D^-T`. The surviving odd spectral orientation is shared by every admissible reciprocal control, so escaping inner conjugacy is still not source selectivity.

Prime Lattice gives a four-regime summation/filter control. Finite signed filters cross the positive convergence wall only through classical periodic continuation; unrestricted infinite filters are programmable by Möbius inversion; `ell^1` removes programmability but collapses to limit-periodic `0/1` convergence geometry; and PL-366 shows that pole-neutral `ell^2` filters automatically satisfy square-root summatory cancellation and converge on `Re(s)>1/2` for a generic Hilbert-space reason. Even a canonical critical exponent can therefore be manufactured by the ambient norm without localizing zeta zeros.

Farey Discrepancy supplies a constructive counterpoint. FD-202 shows that a signed pre-norm projection can reduce the random source tariff from `H^(3/2)` to `H`. FD-203 shows that cheap scalar anchoring still leaves path resistance `Theta(m)`. FD-204 then changes the graph geometry without abandoning source locality: a lowbit tree reduces anchored resistance to `Theta(log m)` while each physical source slab is reused only logarithmically, giving random cost `H^(1+o(1))`. Representation enrichment can buy leverage when its new edges have exact source preimages and improve inverse geometry faster than they increase source reuse.

Nyman--Beurling adds a proof-interface refinement. NB-218 shows that exact zeta-primitive reconstruction contains the source cancellation before absolute-value decoupling; NB-219 shows that the full vertical domain can be smoothly localized on the `1+` side with superalgebraically small **source** error, preserving the signed carrier-frequency structure. The live loss is no longer the global tail but the contour transport across `Re(s)=1` where the localized source profile develops noncompact tails.

Weil Inertia separates formal support size from destination-visible charge tails. Weil Positivity strengthens the globalization warning: WP-367 shows that even an arbitrary common positive radial operator/form loses Whittaker `nY` incidence if exact dilation homogeneity is imposed componentwise; the finite cutoff factors back into Rankin--Selberg data before any limiting issue appears.

The shared question is: **what new source-specific coordinate is acquired, what closure/summation/globalization or pre-norm projection is unavoidable, what quotient and inverse geometry remain after it, which ambient norm or symmetry already explains the apparent critical scale, and which matched control reproduces the survivor?** Richer data deserve credit only after those gates survive.

## Where does the obstruction move after a nominal critical boundary is resolved?

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the clearest collision hierarchy. AF-417--AF-424 move from `s_n/n` to the joint coordinate `b_n(a)=a n^(s_n/n-2)`, then to square-transition fibers `b=m^2`, rectangle balance, complete-packet drift and the derivative-offset correction `m alpha_n`. AF-425 pushes one order deeper and exposes a new kind of obstruction: the formal cancellation variable is an integer source coordinate. Adjacent admissible values of `d_n=s_n-2n` are separated by `~log n` in the corrected cancellation coordinate, so a continuously solvable saddle equation may not be realizable by the physical source. The frontier is now a shrinking-target problem on the source lattice, not merely another asymptotic coefficient.

Visual Exploration supplies the mean-value analogue. Exact gamma recentering removes the moving lower-source wall; zero-free localization removes the first upper coherence wall; exact coefficients save a logarithm; parity support deletes a sector; and support-sensitive frequency spacing moves the generic estimate to `x log log x=o(H)`. VIS-305 then shows that a parity- and density-matched Bernoulli support already has the same `x log log x` absolute reciprocal-spacing cost. The obstruction has moved again: the live object is the signed endpoint Hilbert form or arithmetic spacing correlation not reproduced by that control.

Farey Discrepancy has the same procedural motion in finite-dimensional form. The apparent constant-mode defect gives way to path resistance, and FD-204 then removes most of that resistance with a source-localized multiscale graph. The live gap is now analytic control of the lowbit energy and outer recovery from interval increments to absolute Mertens information.

Nyman--Beurling similarly moves the loss twice. NB-218 moves it from exact reconstruction to absolute-value decoupling; NB-219 then removes the full-line localization problem on `Re(s)>1`. The live obstruction is now transporting the localized signed coefficient through the pole/branch and zero-free contour without recreating the absolute Mellin tariff. Prime Circle moves from inner-conjugacy blindness to outer contragredient orientation and then asks for a source-specific destination beyond that generic duality. Weil Positivity moves from scalar averaging to a broader classification of common homogeneous radial positive forms.

Across these lines, a resolved threshold should be followed by identifying the **new source lattice, graph metric, support sector, proof inequality, symmetry class or theorem hypothesis** that becomes load-bearing, rather than promoting the old scale as structural.

## Which physical cost survives source specialization and analytic transport?

The shared audit is to price the route **after exact source specialization and nuisance removal**, keeping theorem admission, transport loss, family breadth, conductor geometry, exceptional incidence, source-lattice spacing, graph resistance, source-overlap, localization duality, support spacing and proof-decoupling loss distinct until a theorem proves a dependency between them.

## Can sign-indefinite structure preserve critical information before closure or norm compression scalarizes it?

Weil Positivity remains the strongest positive-closure warning. WP-367 shows that sophistication of the positive radial form is not enough: if the same form is applied componentwise and exact dilation homogeneity is imposed, the finite--archimedean incidence factorizes at finite cutoff and the critical positive diagonal is either divergent or null. A surviving route must break that common homogeneous scalarization through cross-index coupling, source-fixed nonhomogeneity, an independently forced remainder/counterterm or a different sign theorem.

Prime Lattice brackets the signed-filter problem even more sharply. Leaving the positive cone is necessary to cross one convergence wall, but finite signs are periodic, unrestricted infinite signs are programmable, `ell^1` signs are limit-periodic and pole-neutral `ell^2` signs already manufacture the square-root exponent by Hilbert duality. The missing structure is a **source-forced law whose zero-sensitive consequence exceeds what the ambient norm supplies generically**.

Farey Discrepancy gives a finite-dimensional constructive example of useful sign placement. Adjacent or multiscale differences must be taken before the positive norm so shared source bulk cancels rather than being squared independently. FD-204 shows that this can be combined with favorable inverse geometry when the nonlocal edges remain exact source observables with controlled overlap.

Nyman--Beurling gives the analytic analogue and now a positive localization result. Keeping primitive and reconstruction kernel coupled avoids the absolute reconstruction tariff, and smooth vertical truncation can be transferred back to a negligible source perturbation rather than paid through an `L^1 x L^infinity` estimate. The unresolved question is whether that coupling survives the contour shift itself.

These examples do not transfer estimates across lines, but they sharpen the interface: useful nonpositivity must occur before the scalarizing operation, be forced by the source, survive required transport, avoid being reproduced by an ambient function-space threshold, and remain distinct after the destination's symmetry quotient and inverse geometry are applied.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the admissible source class and topology, observation family and nullspace, destination quotient and norm, source-coordinate map, acquisition budget, requested precision, normalization gauge, analytic domain, approximation/summation protocol, moving-complexity coordinates, discrete source lattice when present, support constraints, theorem-admission range, exceptional-set incidence on the source image, localization/transport duality, reconstruction loss, proof-decoupling loss, sign/covariance structure, source-overlap cost, inverse graph geometry, target automorphism group, contragredient/duality quotient and matched control.

Then ask in sequence: **what is genuinely new at acquisition; what unavoidable closure/summation/globalization or pre-norm projection follows; what survives the destination quotient; what ambient geometry already explains the apparent critical scale; what is the first inequality or theorem hypothesis that becomes load-bearing; does the required continuous tuning intersect the admissible source class at the needed precision; which inverse resource must the source pay; and does a matched non-arithmetic control reproduce the same survivor?**

The current synthesis narrows rather than proliferates directions. Complete fidelity is not enough if invariantization erases it. Outer automorphism is not enough if it leaves only generic contragredient orientation. A canonical `1/2` is not enough if Hilbert geometry produces it generically. A one-dimensional kernel is not enough to characterize recovery cost, but multiscale enrichment can help if source reuse stays controlled. Exponential family breadth and strong nonexceptional estimates are not enough if the conductor, admission range or source-family exceptional incidence absorbs them. A global convolution domain is not a tariff if it can be localized as a negligible source perturbation before estimation. A theorem-level big-O is not structural until exact coefficients, exact support geometry, admissible source discretization and matched controls have been applied to the signed object underneath it.
