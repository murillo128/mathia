# Global mathematical questions

## Can a source-native transformation reach the exact Weil target without positive scalarization erasing the source?

[MI-009](intuition/MI-009-local-correctness-does-not-determine-global-coherence.md), [MI-012](intuition/MI-012-information-content-and-destination-sensitivity-are-separate-resources.md) and [MI-013](intuition/MI-013-closing-the-bulk-often-leaves-a-thin-source-specific-endpoint.md) separate continuum provenance, target sensitivity and source forcing.

Weil Positivity has now closed both the easy target-side and source-side nonlinear/positive escapes. [WP-288](../weil_positivity/findings/WP-288-exact-cone-only-weil-quadratic-forms-linearize-to-positive-definite-distributions.md) shows that an exact continuous Weil-square functional satisfying the identities forced by convolution squares automatically polarizes to a translation-invariant distribution; if it matches all Weil squares, factorization forces that distribution to be the Weil distribution itself. [WP-289](../weil_positivity/findings/WP-289-critical-mangoldt-covariances-admit-no-continuous-positive-weil-majorant.md) shows that the non-tempered critical Mangoldt covariance cannot be preserved monotonically as a positive summand inside any continuous positive completion.

The open theorem is therefore upstream and signed: derive a source-native finite--archimedean interference, quotient/compression, operator coupling, cohomological/intersection pairing or other transformation that changes the critical arithmetic block before final positivity is read, while preserving enough arithmetic provenance to identify the result. Then prove the exact quadratic and translation identities required by the Weil target. Arbitrary nonlinear scalarization and positive additive completion are no longer separate escape categories.

## Which source-local geometry can exclude collective zero-state failures that survive global controls?

Nyman--Beurling, Prime Lattice and Weil Inertia increasingly agree that good low-dimensional or generic geometry is not enough.

Nyman--Beurling has exact determinant cancellation and uniform two-state conditioning after canonical Blaschke deflation. NB-098 nevertheless gives a transverse three-state family with unit pivots and good pairs but quartic full conditioning blow-up. [NB-099](../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-099-zeta-symmetry-and-arbitrarily-sparse-zero-counts-cannot-regularize-collective-shear.md) now proves that common height is gauge for this geometry and that standard zeta symmetries, simplicity and arbitrarily sparse global zero counts can coexist with the synthetic bad clusters. The next theorem must use genuinely **local** zeta geometry, a different symmetry/confluence representation, or a target mechanism that avoids dimension-free canonical conditioning.

Prime Lattice still has all-log transport for actual eigenstates but no source-specific positive-order regularity or sign theorem; its surviving boundary improvement must come from the localized-Weil source/eigen-equation rather than generic transport.

Weil Inertia separates scalar-profile exhaustion, retained Hilbert orientation and actual source geometry. WI-279 recovers orientation lost by scalarization, while WI-280 shows generic noncommensurate cut mixtures still cannot break the scalar-profile ceiling. The next gain must prove a compatibility law for the actual Suzuki null mode, strengthen the numerator through the source equation, or use a genuinely nonlinear source invariant.
