# Global mathematical questions

## Which source relation cannot be repaired by moving the cost elsewhere?

[MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md) now has a sharper resource ledger. Farey Discrepancy has moved from the qualitative statement “multiplicativity matters” to an exact relation-boundary scale: within deletion-only sources, prime-extension defect below `((log x loglog x)^(-1))^(1/q)` forces the sparse dyadic-null repair to disappear. Nyman--Beurling shows that independently normalized Euler bands hide a different cost: scalar translates can synthesize them only with total variation proportional to inverse source mass, linear in the number of balanced bands. Robin Extremal supplies the opposite failure mode—past log-squared derivative depth, normalized scalar Euler jets themselves collapse toward net multiplicity, so more coordinates cease to carry robust prime-location information.

Möbius Cancellation shows that even changing the channel does not automatically evade a correctly chosen inverse currency. Source-dependent Gaussian covariance is a real side channel, but its deformation contributes an exact positive Jeffreys cost alongside mean displacement. A useful route must therefore identify a resource that cannot be diluted over a relation graph, purchased by a matched control, moved into covariance or another side channel, or lost by common-mode saturation.

The common discriminator is not dimension, coefficient accuracy, nominal source fidelity or the existence of a nonzero tariff. It is a **destination-relevant quantitative resource together with a justified admissible budget**. A proposed route should state why its control class cannot simply pay that tariff, relocate it, or enter a regime where the observable stops resolving the source.

The general rule is now: state the physical source scale first, verify density/uniformity on the actual sampling set, carry structured main terms through the induced coordinate rescaling, and then inspect whether the residual theorem interface still retains the sign/phase information required by the proposed cancellation. Ambient almost-everywhere statements, fixed-test corollaries, generic seminorm bounds and absolute envelopes can all misprice a moving source window when applied before that same-scale audit.

Across these lines the useful resource must survive exactly where the destination becomes thin: trace noncollapse, a physically bounded acquisition budget, same-scale theorem uniformity, or globally discoverable support.

Möbius Cancellation now prices two regular channel classes. MC-354 bounds reverse discrimination for common additive log-smooth noise through mean displacement and curvature. MC-355 extends the ledger to source-dependent nondegenerate Gaussian covariance: mean motion and affine-invariant covariance motion contribute separate positive Jeffreys costs, and exact dephasing cannot remain inside finite nondegenerate Gaussian rows at all. The remaining question is whether the arithmetic endpoint factors through one of these priced classes or needs a genuinely non-Gaussian, singular or side-channel-specific reverse-divergence theorem.

Weil Inertia strengthens a different norm mismatch. Below `theta<3/16`, all-start control removes the source-location obstruction. WI-332--WI-334 now show that `U^2`, square-root phase cancellation and even an arbitrary fixed finite hierarchy of power-small local Gowers norms can all coexist with full diagonal-scale sliding variance. Above `3/16`, the same covariance problem remains together with the exceptional-start quantifier.

[MI-026](intuition/MI-026-sign-destructive-proof-interfaces-cannot-certify-destination-cancellation.md) isolates the common failure shared by this bow obstruction and the Wang Laplace-null residual. Once an intermediate inequality or norm has quotient out the sign/phase datum the destination needs, stacking more constraints inside that summary cannot recreate it. A successful continuation must recover the missing signed structure before the lossy step or replace the interface by a theorem that controls the destination-sensitive signed quantity directly.

Weil Positivity closes another direct geometric escape. WP-352 shows that projective Berry/Chern geometry is blind to the common scalar zeta factor, the full scalar connection contributes only an exact central term, and singular scalar curvature is just the meromorphic divisor. The zero-free reciprocal factor `exp(a(s-1/2))` preserves reciprocal symmetry, critical-line unitarity, divisor data and projective geometry while shifting the phase generator. A surviving sign route therefore needs an independently forced noncentral/global coupling or phase anchor before scalar-gauge quotienting.

Prime Circle and Prime Flute continue to reinforce the same discipline from other representations: scalar magnitude packages and local hard-cut correlations can look rich while matched controls preserve the visible structure or compactness fails before global counting matters. Positivity, pseudorandomness, self-adjointness or source fidelity counts only when it is selective for the exact destination quotient and defeats an internal control for an arithmetic reason.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, state the admissible source class, exact observation/target quotient, physical source-coordinate map, relation graph, acquisition operator class and budget, conditioning, sampling regime, likelihood/covariance/sign norm and matched-control class. Then ask what the destination actually distinguishes; whether changing horizons, operators or coordinates adds source information or only relocates a cost; whether the source theorem is valid at the induced moving scale; whether structured main terms have been rescaled before generic error bounds are applied; whether the proof has discarded sign or covariance needed later; whether true amplitudes and source mass survive normalization; whether reverse likelihood contrast or signed off-diagonal covariance is affordable; whether a relational source law blocks sparse repair at a quantitative rate; whether deeper scalar observables retain source resolution; and whether the final survivor connects to a zero/sign theorem rather than only reconstructing arithmetic data.

The program advances when one resource becomes **destination-selective under the same source fibre, physical scale, acquisition class, conditioning and control class used by the claimed RH mechanism**. Raw dimension, deep scalar jets, high coordinate fidelity, exact coordinate dictionaries, paid-but-generic source tariffs, fixed-order pseudorandomness, sign-blind residual bounds and scalar-gauge-invariant positivity remain intermediate resources until that matched-control test is passed.
