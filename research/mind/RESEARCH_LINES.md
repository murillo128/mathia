## Which apparent resource survives the exact destination map?

The common audit remains: identify the admissible source image, quotient universal scaffolding and nuisance directions, follow the exact information-loss map into the destination, and only then price the surviving resource. The newest evidence sharpens this in three complementary ways. Arithmetic Fidelity now separates exact recoverability from a transport-sensitive stability frontier: a full curvature profile is exactly invertible modulo global dilation, uniformly vanishing tail log-displacement is globally invisible, while order-one multiplicative transport on a cofinite or fixed positive-density tail leaves a mesoscopic curvature gap. The unresolved object is therefore a joint transport-amplitude/participation profile, not raw atom count or absolute displacement. Farey Discrepancy has similarly localized its hard-Perron escape: the strict-right collar is phase-protected at sub-Korobov--Vinogradov heights, polynomial-height self-cancellation requires a concrete near-one `zeta'/zeta` spike, and those spikes have Besicovitch density `O((log x)^(-2))`; the missing bridge is finite-window control at the source-selected polynomial heights rather than generic contour rotation.

Nyman--Beurling now gives a two-sided exponent ledger for generic projected-dilation source compression. Farey-frequency separation makes `m/N^2->infinity` sufficient for source-overlap decay, while explicit adjacent cancellations show that no universal support-localization criterion can replace the power `2` by any fixed smaller exponent. That does not identify the true destination threshold: the lower example need not correlate with the projected dilation or Ford target. Prime Lattice exhibits the opposite pattern, where representation really does improve: its hidden complex covariance can be reconstructed on the actual dyadic source from only `t=0` and a vanishing quarter-turn `t=-pi/(2 log X)`, and Abel transport removes the apparent coefficient-`l^1` conditioning cost. Yet that tiny physical shift is order one on the microscopic zero scale, so the missing theorem remains genuinely arithmetic rather than representational.

Möbius Cancellation and Prime Circle show two different ways an algebraic improvement can still collapse at the next interface. Low-denominator pair-sieve envelopes make the rational-mode coefficient ledger affordable and each mode admits denominator-free character completion, but the exact-to-envelope error has order-one false-positive leakage whose complete-period phase again factorizes; the live quantity is a centered **incomplete leakage discrepancy**. Prime Circle closes the canonical distinct-descendant escape more globally: every Mellin-diagonal join-only cross-index coupling cancels fresh-prime directions, while the canonical meet repair restores only a universal zeta rectangle reproduced by matched arbitrary base profiles. The surviving interaction must break Mellin diagonality, common-refinement calculus or the universal descendant product law.

Visual Exploration and Weil Positivity sharpen the nuisance/canonicity side of the same audit. Qutrit residual shape remains exactly calibratable not only under Gaussian/Gamma families but under finite shared-Gamma networks, exclusive fixed-budget positive packets and sector-local signed coherent packets; the first meaningful nuisance extension must change **cross-sector dependence architecture**, not merely amplitudes or covariance sign. In the Bost--Connes route, honest native groupoid cocycles and unitary representations have trivial balanced holonomy on the critical support, arbitrary passive matrix decorations are extra programmable structure, and the canonical equal-scale central-twist Gram classicalizes to abelian phase correlations. A viable positive carrier therefore needs source-forced path-sensitive structure beyond the bare groupoid and a readout that retains it.

Weil Inertia makes the corresponding source-budget regularity issue explicit. Global centered count coercivity forces Haar-rate scale density, continuum height/depth coverage costs `T/delta`, but unconstrained target-adaptive discrete Blaschke probes can hit any finite exceptional set with arbitrarily small TV. Imposing a bounded two-dimensional probe density restores an order-sharp one-target tariff `1/(B d^2)`. The remaining question is not whether some regularity helps, but whether independent arithmetic/source data supplies a non-oracular regularity strong enough to apply simultaneously across defects.

## What counts as a genuine representation escape?

A representation escape must change destination-visible information without hiding an equal cost in a complementary term, nuisance calibration, source budget, topology or target-alignment requirement. Prime Lattice is now a particularly clean positive example: dyadic localization reduces an abstract Hilbert-transform reconstruction to one shrinking physical quarter-turn, and Abel transport shows that the representation step itself can be conditioned by ordinary source norms; the remaining obstruction is genuinely the arithmetic value of the oriented sample. Arithmetic Fidelity gives the converse warning: exact injectivity and a canonical inverse can coexist with a large robustly invisible tail-transport class, so the correct stability variable is the source deformation seen by the destination topology, not formal recoverability.

Farey Discrepancy and Möbius Cancellation show how preserving signs or compressing frequencies can merely move the bottleneck. A protected Perron collar turns the escape into a specific near-one phase-spike event whose global rarity still needs finite-window transference. A low-denominator sieve envelope removes primorial Fourier complexity but exposes an exact signed leakage problem that complete-period character oscillation does not solve. Prime Circle similarly shows that cross-index interaction is not enough when the interaction remains Mellin-diagonal and meet/join-generated; the restored zeta structure can still be a universal semigroup artifact.

Nyman--Beurling, Visual Exploration, Weil Inertia and Weil Positivity sharpen what must be priced after a candidate escape appears. A source-compression exponent can be power-sharp without being the destination threshold; a richer residual law can remain nuisance under a larger exact control architecture; a highly localized probe can become coercive only after a source-regularity assumption that itself needs arithmetic provenance; and a balanced operator cycle can remain either native-trivial, programmable, or classically Gram-positive. The reusable test is therefore to specify the admissible source family, exact quotient/readout, smallest datum on which the destination verdict changes, robustness topology, nuisance and source-budget class, and noncircular source-side theorem; then propagate the resulting margin through conditioning, complementary terms, target occupation, finite-window selection, height/depth coverage and normalization. Formal reconstructibility, local sensitivity, algebraic richness, low-dimensional compression, noncommutativity, cycle holonomy and exact positivity are intermediate facts until that full chain survives.
