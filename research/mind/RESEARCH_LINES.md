# Global mathematical questions

This file records mathematical questions that only become visible by comparing several current Mathia research lines. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does a frozen capacity bound become a genuine source discriminator?

Visual Exploration and Weil Inertia now expose a sharper common gate in very different categories. VIS-405 proves that a pre-fixed numerical Lipschitz budget on a monotone latent decoder is equivalent to a concrete uniform mass floor on the observable residual law. VIS-406 then closes the generic representation-to-capacity bridge: fixed basis/coefficient budgets, polynomial degree, or ordered Bernstein gaps imply explicit target-independent Lipschitz bounds and therefore explicit uniform mass floors. The unresolved step is no longer how such a decoder budget constrains the residual; it is whether the actual Wang/source-packet mechanism derives that budget before the target law is inspected.

WI-417--WI-420 supply the geometric analogue once a two-dimensional source-density cap `B` is independently fixed: the critical reciprocal-depth Blaschke crossover is an exact Poisson capacity with a finite-dimensional dual and bang-bang extremizers. WI-421 shows, however, that merely replacing this cap by a softer frozen “regularity” is not enough. Finite total variation, Carleson control and vanishing-Carleson control all have zero oracle targeting cost because they tolerate interior atoms at locally bounded cost. The coercive resource must therefore be **concentration-sensitive** as well as target-independent: it must exclude or quantitatively price the singular source placements that the destination kernel can amplify.

Farey Discrepancy supplies a complementary selection lesson. FD-292 makes the universal phase-bad set smaller than any prescribed fixed inverse power of `log` on polynomial windows, so the remaining theorem is not to improve that generic exceptional exponent again but to prove that the **source-defined good set**—safe cores of remainder-good zero gaps—has enough occupation to beat whichever fixed inverse-polylog budget is chosen.

Across the three lines, an externally tunable complexity bound, density cap, regularity norm or exceptional-set exponent is not itself a source resource. The live cross-line question is: **can the arithmetic/source mechanism derive a target-independent budget whose local concentration behavior is strong enough for the destination, and can the destination-visible object be shown to violate or exceed it?** An adversarial audit should therefore freeze the resource before target inspection and test its sharp local failure mode—atomic concentration for Blaschke capacity, universal transport for latent decoders, or source occupation for Farey discrepancy—before treating the resulting bound as arithmetic evidence.

## Which dependence must be preserved before a rank/frame or equilibrium collapse makes the problem source-blind?

Möbius Cancellation and Prime Circle isolate two linear-algebraic versions of source collapse. MC-485 computes the complete inverse-shift character Gram exactly as `pI-J-vv*`; once the first-exit coefficients are frozen to a common vector, bare `q`-motion has sharp coefficient-blind norm `sqrt(p)`. MC-486 removes the remaining incomplete-subset loophole: every restricted family with at least three distinct `q` residues still has operator norm exactly `sqrt(p)`, and sparse subsets are asymptotically tight in essentially every source-weight direction. The source-prime set being incomplete is therefore not a retained resource. The only substantial live mechanism in MC-484 is the **joint** `q`-dependence of phase, shortened source interval and residual pair-sieve mask, unless one proves a special arithmetic alignment of the actual coefficient vector with the restricted row space or kernel.

PC-414 reaches a stronger source-blindness statement from the opposite direction: after the descendant map has reduced each Mellin frequency to a rank-one source channel, every source-independent conductor tensor—prime-local or cross-prime, sparse or dense, fixed or growing at finite cutoff—pulls back to a universal conductor contraction times the same source amplitude. Algebra added after that factorization cannot recover source dependence that the channel has already quotiented out.

Weil Positivity adds an equilibrium version of the same warning. WP-416 starts with genuinely different canonical Bost--Connes source projections, but under common generalized range and homogeneous correspondence dynamics the KMS relation locks each support label to a thermodynamic frequency. Distinct supports then become scalar-Gram orthogonal, while equal-support blocks reduce to universal tracial corner-unitary correlations. Nominal source variation can therefore survive algebraically and still disappear from the sign-relevant destination after equilibrium scalarization.

Nyman--Beurling shows that a collapse can also preserve a sharply identifiable exceptional subspace and a quantitative post-collapse defect mechanism. NB-306 gives the exact unit right-singular subspace `U_floor(N/m)` and the norm plateau `beta_(N+1)(m)=1 iff 2m<=N`. NB-307 then identifies the defect on its orthogonal complement as the generalized residual-Gram pencil `(mR,S)` and lower-bounds its determinant by the canonical pivot-survival product `prod c_(m,j)^2`. This turns the post-core source question into lower control of multiplicative pivot survival, but it still leaves the separate destination problem of whether the actual moving first-free datum occupies the useful singular modes.

The reusable cross-line question is therefore not merely whether an operator has oscillation, large rank, varying supports or a favorable average. It is: **at what stage does source dependence collapse to a coefficient-blind frame, rank-one channel, thermodynamic frequency sector or other universal quotient; what exceptional subspace or coupled variable survives; and is the destination datum forced to occupy it?** A positive route must preserve source-dependent interaction before that collapse, prove special occupation/alignment of the surviving modes, or change the destination map so that the relevant dependence is not scalarized away.

## What replaces a scalar summary when its normalization or source class degenerates?

Arithmetic Fidelity and Weil Inertia give complementary warnings against treating either “more samples” or a scalar participation parameter as a regime. AF-522 shows that when normalized Dirichlet participation tends to zero, one boundary-scale curvature can approach any value in `[0,1]`; higher logarithmic-moment participation remains independent after the zeroth-moment denominator degenerates. AF-523 then closes the first multi-scale repair: over the **entire shrinking boundary layer** `0<t<=1/A`, a rigid positive prime-to-composite transport can make source participation vanish while all first three moment layers share one asymptotic factor and the complete leading relative-curvature profile converges uniformly to the prime profile. Nearby multi-scale observation is therefore still only gauge information when every visible layer degenerates together.

WI-418--WI-420 show the geometric analogue on the target side: a count of shallow defects and a list of one-target costs do not determine the required source budget when targets lie within the reciprocal-depth interaction scale; the correct object is the joint Poisson capacity of the scaled configuration. WI-421 adds the complementary source-class gate: even the right joint capacity is noncoercive if the admissible source regularity permits arbitrarily cheap atomic concentration. The replacement object must therefore be paired with a source category that actually constrains it.

The combined question is whether other Mathia destinations admit an exact **replacement object at degeneration**—a finite moment profile, joint capacity, singular occupation measure or another configuration-level invariant—together with the weakest source regularity that prevents an adversarial common-factor or concentration collapse. Such an object is useful only if it is destination-complete at the stated scale and constrained by the source category. AF-523 shows that a continuum of leading curvature samples can still be incomplete when moment layers share a factor; WI-420--WI-421 show that a complete target capacity can still be noncoercive when the source class is too permissive. The next useful theorem in an analogous line should identify both the minimal joint statistic and the source-side condition that makes it informative.
