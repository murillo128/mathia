# Global mathematical questions

## Which source relation cannot be repaired by moving the cost elsewhere?

[MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md) separates several different failure modes of nominally rich source interfaces.

Farey Discrepancy distinguishes **relation coercivity from observability of that relation**, and the current tariff is sharply source-fibre dependent. FD-181 proves that every squarefree-supported perturbation of Möbius with changed-coordinate support `o(x/log x)` pays prime-extension multiplicativity energy at scale `x/log x`, even when prime coordinates may move. FD-182 composes that theorem with the permanent FD-179 dyadic-null source: one fixed non-Möbius source matches the complete dyadic midpoint ladder exactly while retaining `M_(a,q)(x)=(C+o(1))x/log x`. On the fixed-prime fibre, FD-183 forces a second multiplicative generation and FD-184 pushes the same mechanism through every fixed rank:

`S(x) >= (1/(n_0(r-1)!)+o(1)) x (loglog x)^(r-1)/log x`

for every fixed `r>=1` under subcritical prime-extension energy. Hence the fixed-prime support tariff beats every fixed power of `loglog x` above `x/log x`. The unresolved source-side currency is now **growing-rank uniformity**, not another fixed generation. A source-native bridge must therefore specify both an observation family that exposes the relation and the admissible source fibre/depth regime on which the relation price was proved.

Nyman--Beurling shows how a locality cost can be absent at fixed resolution yet become source-selective in a coupled limit. NB-190 prices a normalized Euler-band probe by inverse source mass in scalar-translate variation. NB-191 shows that at fixed band width this cost can eventually be transplanted into every high dyadic block by common Bohr recurrence without an additional asymptotic surcharge. NB-192 reveals the hidden nonuniformity: one fixed log-width band contains `m_a~a exp(r_0/a)` rationally independent prime-log phases, forcing coordinatewise syndetic common returns to satisfy `log L_a >> a exp(r_0/a)`. NB-193 closes the direct positive weighted relaxation: the true-source-weighted `ell^1` return set has exponentially small Haar volume in effective dimension `D_eff asymp m_a`, so the same double-exponential worst-gap scale survives aggregate positive recurrence.

NB-194 adds a different obstruction for the **specific physical block**. The normalized full von-Mangoldt shell has a barycenter whose high-frequency response is uniformly small by the Vinogradov--Korobov PNT remainder. Since a positive weighted phase return would force that barycenter near one, the actual shell has a deterministic return-free corridor up to `exp(c V(e^(r_0/a)))`. Thus the particular block `[T,2T]` cannot be exceptionally lucky when `log T=o(V(e^(r_0/a_T)))`. The positive-syndetic and source-dephasing regimes do not yet meet; signed aggregation, stronger blockwise arithmetic cancellation or a different synthesis topology remain live in the intermediate region.

Robin Extremal supplies a precision-aware stable-rank analogue. RE-189--RE-192 show that high normalized Euler jets and finite-Laplace observation windows can be extremely compressible despite exact analytic source rigidity. RE-193 improves the large-rectangle bound: for `R=U log(Q_+/Q_-)`, the scalar Euler channel has rank

`O((1+log(1+R))(1+log(1/epsilon)))`

at tolerance `epsilon` above the prime-power floor. Fixed spectral span across polynomial source scales therefore carries only `O_epsilon(loglog p)` robust scalar directions at fixed precision. Exact continuum data may remain source-rigid while usable capacity grows only logarithmically with the transform rectangle; the relevant resource is now the pair `(R,epsilon)`, not sample count or source cardinality separately.

Möbius Cancellation shows that source information can move inside a statistical architecture without becoming free. MC-356 prices finite-dimensional regular exponential-family channels by integrated Fisher action. MC-357--MC-358 move the tariff into latent/component structure while preserving the exact lifted divergence ledger. MC-359 adds total variation as a bounded edge currency when KL/Jeffreys becomes singular: bounded-energy nontrivial dephasing forces a positive average TV separation across source directions, and in a genuine shared-randomness architecture a one-source flip must change the complete transcript on a positive fraction of common seeds.

MC-360 adds a bounded **product-compositional** currency. Hellinger edge distance still upper-bounds the source information needed by the reciprocal endpoint, while affinity factorizes through conditionally independent components. For a genuine latent/product architecture, output Hellinger cost is bounded by latent-law Hellinger distance plus an affinity-weighted sum of component costs; with source-independent latent randomness this becomes an extensive weighted source-coordinate incidence tariff. Splitting a transcript into many weak components therefore does not erase the destination-imposed separation. The remaining problem is to expose the actual Möbius-derived architecture and prove an arithmetic upper bound in the finite currency—TV/common-seed change, Hellinger product incidence, lifted divergence or another architecture-native quantity—that remains meaningful there.

The common discriminator is not raw dimension, exactness or the mere existence of a source tariff. It is a **destination-relevant quantitative resource together with an observation/acquisition theorem that actually exposes it under the declared source class, precision, depth and coupled scaling**.

## Which physical source scale, analytic domain and representation gauge does the theorem actually control?

Arithmetic Fidelity has two independent scale ledgers. On the sampling side, uniform short-interval PNT gives all-prime-start conversion for `h_N=p_N^(-beta)` throughout `beta<13/30`, while exceptional-set inertia plus Brun--Titchmarsh packing transfers a stronger almost-all ambient theorem to relative density one of prime starts through every `beta<13/15`. Those thresholds measure theorem uniformity and sampling geometry, not an intrinsic detector constant.

On the finite-order analytic side, AF-403--AF-405 turn strict translation positivity into a recursive confluent/Toda hierarchy. The full Euler-log profile has `H_1,H_2,H_3,H_4>0` globally, hence strict total positivity through order four. AF-217 still forces eventual failure, so the first possible bad order is at least five and the next local gate is `(log R_4)''<4q`.

AF-406 adds a representation-specific fidelity boundary. For `xi_1(z)=xi(sqrt(z)+1/2)`, every fixed finite **coefficient-sequence** Pólya-frequency order can be forced by a sufficiently large zero-free multiplier `e^{nz}` while the complete zero divisor is unchanged. But an affine exponential **profile** tilt `f(t)->e^{at+b}f(t)` preserves every translation-determinant sign, scales `H_r` only by a positive factor and leaves normalized `R_r` and `q` invariant. Coefficient `PF_m` gauge sensitivity therefore cannot be transferred wholesale to the translation-kernel Toda hierarchy.

AF-407 adds an independent analytic-domain audit. For the affine-tilted Euler-log profile, the bilateral Laplace transform is zero-free throughout its actual convergence half-plane. The nontrivial zeta zeros appear only in meromorphic continuation beyond that wall, with a gauge-invariant offset from the wall. All-order Pólya-frequency theory can exploit them because Schoenberg supplies reciprocal-entire continuation; finite-order translation positivity does not inherit that continuation theorem automatically. The order-five Toda gate therefore remains a genuine local kernel question unless a separate continuation/localization bridge is proved.

Visual Exploration adds both normalization and parameter-regime audits. VIS-272 gives the exact Green-potential dictionary and VIS-274 carries the concentrating main term through the fixed-source `1/log T` pullback. VIS-275 shows why a sign-blind absolute remainder cannot benefit from extra signed packet moments. VIS-276--VIS-277 then establish that the apparent signed remainder mean is partly a classical gamma-factor convention, not an invariant source residual.

VIS-278 closes the first normalization-invariant pure source-energy question. With

`R_x(t)=x(B_x(t)+E_x(t))-log(t/(2*pi))`,

one has `R_x(t)=zeta'/zeta(3/2-it)+O_K(t^-1)`, asymptotically zero mean, and a positive limiting mean-square `C_Lambda=sum_p (log p)^2/(p^3-1)`. Its standalone fixed-source packet contribution is only `L^-2`.

VIS-279 shows that the `D_x`--`E_x` cross is also only `L^-2` after Wang's exact mean-square theorem is specialized to compact fixed source before rescaling. VIS-280 closes the remaining direct prime/source cross: Wang's explicit `D_x`--`B_x` bound becomes `O_K(1/(HL))` after packet normalization and is power-small in `T`. The entire direct interaction `D_x` against `B_x+E_x` is therefore excluded as a compulsory `L^-3/2` mechanism. The correct next theorem must inspect the remaining proof-loss/localization sectors and first demonstrate that any intrinsic fixed-source `L^-3/2` term exists at all.

Robin's precision-aware `R=U log(Q_+/Q_-)` law gives the dual observation-window version of the same discipline. The theorem must control the actual physical source window, the observation window and tolerance must resolve the claimed degrees of freedom, nuisance/gauge normalization must be fixed before a residual scale is interpreted, the analytic domain/continuation carrying the target divisor must actually be controlled, and a uniform theorem bound must be re-specialized to the destination parameter regime before its exponent is treated as a mathematical frontier.

## Which quantitative resource survives a thin or moving destination?

Prime Lattice has moved beyond both the local activation-birth gate and the positive-tail dilution gate. PL-338 shows that every finite prime-power activation is first-order flat and is born with favorable reflected sign whenever the transported boundary trace is nonzero. PL-339 computes the exact path-chain gap of each compressed shift and shows that the outer logarithmic half-window carries asymptotically all weighted von-Mangoldt mass with unit gap. Consequently

`J_a >= (1-o(1)) W_a I`.

Accumulating active channels therefore does not soften the favorable positive jump bulk relative to total source mass. But the exact prime block remains `P_a=J_a-2W_aI`; the negative scalar compensation survives at the same scale. The frontier is now global branch persistence/noncollapse and a rational-prime-specific control of compensation/completion or another mesoscopic source law beyond the PNT-universal bulk.

Nyman--Beurling sharpens what “locality” means in a moving destination. Fixed-`a` recurrence eventually reaches arbitrary height, but NB-192--NB-193 show that both coordinatewise and natural positive weighted recurrence have double-exponential worst-gap inclusion length in `1/a`. NB-194 adds that the actual von-Mangoldt shell is not merely hard to guarantee: in the narrower Vinogradov--Korobov corridor it is deterministically dephased throughout the physical block, so positive recurrence is impossible there. A more efficient signed, block-specific or non-recurrence mechanism remains possible in the large intermediate regime; generic positive recurrence cannot simply be extrapolated into the shrinking-width limit.

Xi Flow remains a useful control in the opposite direction: once a consecutive packet is localized, its local holomorphic inversion is well conditioned. Its unresolved cost is global support discovery and assembly rather than local packet inversion. Across these lines, the moving destination is governed by the resource that scales with localization, recurrence, source dephasing, support discovery, stable transform width, precision or branch transport—not by the mere existence of a local inverse or positive bulk.

## Can reverse discrimination, signed structure or positivity be made destination-selective?

Möbius Cancellation now prices both regular and singular source channels. MC-354--MC-358 cover smooth noise, Gaussian deformation, regular exponential families and source-dependent latent/components through finite divergence ledgers. MC-359 supplies the bounded TV edge tariff and common-seed change bound at the singular endpoint. MC-360 supplies a bounded Hellinger ledger that tensorizes through latent/product architectures. A useful contradiction still needs the other half: an arithmetic upper theorem showing that the **actual** Möbius endpoint architecture cannot afford the compulsory adjacent separation in a metric and decomposition that stay meaningful for that architecture.

Weil Inertia exposes a different information loss. Below `theta<3/16`, all-start control removes the source-location obstruction. WI-332--WI-335 show that low and slowly growing ordinary Gowers orders can be small while full diagonal-scale sliding variance survives. WI-336 closes the high-order side: diagonal zero-increment cubes force `U^s` to become non-small once `2^s loglog N/log K` reaches the complementary threshold, leaving at most one binary-order transition window for an all-start theorem.

WI-337 shows that the corresponding **density-one/almost-all** interface has no such open window. Matched-energy Rademacher models are simultaneously `U^s=o(1)` on a density-one set of intervals for every fixed margin below the diagonal threshold `R_s=2`, while those same good intervals retain the full sliding variance. Above `R_s=2+eta`, comparable-energy ordinary smallness is impossible. Thus an almost-all higher-uniformity theorem cannot be rescued merely by letting the ordinary Gowers order grow. A surviving route must use source-specific signed two-point covariance, genuinely all-start input where available, or a diagonal-free/renormalized critical statistic.

[MI-026](intuition/MI-026-sign-destructive-proof-interfaces-cannot-certify-destination-cancellation.md) links that covariance boundary to the Visual result. In Wang's channel, signed information is useless if decomposition-dependent, already absolute-valued, or attached to a theorem-uniform scale that disappears after fixed-source specialization; VIS-280 now shows that even the entire direct prime/source cross is below the alleged `L^-3/2` frontier. In the bow, a growing list of ordinary smallness constraints is useless if it does not determine the required off-diagonal sign, and for density-one input the generic countermodel reaches the same diagonal threshold at which ordinary smallness becomes impossible. A useful intermediate must preserve destination-sensitive sign/covariance, survive harmless normalization freedom, live at the exact physical scale and carry the source-start quantifier consumed by the terminal theorem.

Weil Positivity closes another scalar escape. WP-352 isolates the reciprocal exponential phase gauge and shows that projective Berry/Chern geometry is blind to the common scalar zeta factor. WP-353 proves that, under order-at-most-one growth, this exponential family is the complete zero-free reciprocal/unitary scalar gauge. Standard arithmetic cusp normalization can remove its one real parameter—but after that normalization the scalar meromorphic normalizer is determined by its divisor plus normalization. Scalar phase recovery has therefore collapsed to divisor reconstruction rather than an independently generated positivity mechanism. A surviving Weil route must introduce a noncentral/global coupling or a larger independently positive object **before scalar reconstruction**.

Prime Circle closes a parallel scalar-selector escape. PC-338 extends the finite point-supported Mellin classification to every compactly supported distribution in Mellin frequency. PC-339 now shows that noncompact support alone does not escape: every finite global Mellin measure with the natural critical Gamma-strip boundary admissibility still collapses under semiprime nulls to zero off `s=1` and to `c Lambda` on the endpoint. The decisive resource is the analytic boundary class, not support compactness. A surviving radial route must leave this Gamma-strip-admissible measure class—through faster-growth/non-measure analytic functionals, shell-dependent/nonlinear/matrix structure or cross-shell coupling—and still supply an independent zero-sensitive destination theorem.

Prime Flute reinforces the complementary warning. The current sharp physical low/high completed angle is already noncompact because adjacent Fourier modes can straddle the cutoff while becoming almost identical on a collapsing corridor. More endpoint counting cannot repair a representation whose local operator already has infinitely many order-one canonical correlations; the split or invariant itself must change before the global trace-ideal question becomes meaningful.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, state the admissible source class, observation family and its nullspace, exact target quotient, physical source-coordinate map, dual observation window, relation graph and depth quantifier, acquisition operator class and budget, conditioning, requested precision, sampling/start regime, likelihood/covariance/sign norm, normalization gauge, positivity representation, native analytic domain and continuation class, analytic boundary class and matched-control class. Then ask what the destination actually distinguishes; whether the source relation being priced is visible to the observations; whether changing the admissible source fibre or multiplicative depth changes the repair tariff; whether a cost has merely moved into a latent selector, source-dependent component, product-incidence budget, shared-seed change event, recurrence target or transform rectangle; whether localization introduces a quantitatively new scale or is neutralized only at fixed resolution; whether source-specific dephasing forbids the nominal recurrence on the physical block; whether dense exact sampling adds stable rank or only logarithmically compressed finite-precision observations; whether the source theorem is valid at the induced moving scale; whether a finite positivity certificate is actually gauge-sensitive in the representation being used and controls the continuation where zeros are visible; whether structured main terms have been normalized and source-uniform bounds re-specialized before generic error exponents are interpreted; whether the proof has discarded sign/covariance needed later; whether ordinary Gowers smallness is below its countermodel threshold, above its diagonal-energy threshold, or weakened to a density-one quantifier whose countermodel already reaches the wall; whether a scalar phase is independently forced or merely reconstructed from a divisor; whether a global Mellin selector genuinely leaves the Gamma-strip-admissible measure class; and whether the final survivor connects to a zero/sign theorem rather than only reconstructing arithmetic data.

The program advances when one resource becomes **destination-selective under the same source fibre, observation family, physical scale, acquisition class, depth regime, conditioning, precision, normalization, analytic domain/class, start quantifier and matched-control class used by the claimed RH mechanism**. Raw dimension, exact-null observations, deep scalar jets, dense exact samples, generic mixture complexity, positive syndetic recurrence, ordinary Gowers smallness at a density-one interface, universal PNT bulk coercivity, normalization-dependent residuals, coefficient finite positivity on a movable gauge orbit, continued zeros unavailable to the finite certificate, source-uniform exponents unused by the destination, Gamma-strip-admissible scalar Mellin measures and divisor-reconstructed scalar positivity remain intermediate resources until that matched-control test is passed.
