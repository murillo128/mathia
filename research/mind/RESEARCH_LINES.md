## Which cancellation theorem remains after fixed-depth source laws stop adding endpoint force?

[MI-010](intuition/MI-010-source-cancellation-budgets-are-family-specific.md), [MI-013](intuition/MI-013-closing-the-bulk-often-leaves-a-thin-source-specific-endpoint.md), [MI-014](intuition/MI-014-componentwise-coercivity-needs-a-switching-cost-before-globalization.md) and [MI-015](intuition/MI-015-relaxations-that-retain-an-endpoint-extremizer-cannot-prove-the-endpoint.md) track relocated endpoint costs.

These results sharpen the cross-line discriminator for “more source structure.” Farey shows that finitely many exact multiplicative directions may remain cheap; Arithmetic Fidelity shows that even a sharp concentration law can be saturated by a simple recoding; Möbius shows that endpoint geometry may force an entire subset spectrum expensive once overlap is controlled. The useful question is which **growing joint source law is actually consumed by the destination**, not how many fixed syntactic constraints are present.

## Which exact source incidence survives the rare-host localization in Weil Inertia?

WI-288--WI-292 reduce complete one-signed symmetric two-island screening to a rare-host problem. The global composite-safe reservoir has many components, but only `O(X^(1/10+epsilon))` of them can meet eligible ordinary-prime hosts after localization. WI-293 audits a tempting external shortcut. The claim cannot be used as evidence in its present form. The actual frontier remains a **cross-scale incidence theorem inside the rare host family**, or a separate amplitude/sign/support argument. A future valid Cramér-scale theorem would of course change that frontier immediately.

## What should Prime Circle retain after both diffuse one-profile spectra classicalize?

PC-295 closes the complete absolute ordered singular spectrum under diffuse averaging: it converges to the deterministic Green sequence. PC-296 closes the direct raw-eigenvalue escape under the same hypothesis: the normalized spectral radius tends to zero even while the operator norm tends to `1/2`.

The mechanism distinguishes two resources. Same-side Gram matrices preserve strong singular channels; the left/right cross-Gram vanishes, so those channels do not close into eigenvalue loops. Prime and matched diffuse controls share both limits.

Any surviving prime signal must therefore lie **before or outside both scalar spectral compressions**: source-specific pseudospectral/transient behavior, joint noncommuting profiles, cross-level/source coupling, retained left/right geometry, or an exceptional non-diffuse regime. PC-296 does not prove the pseudospectrum small, so that route remains logically open, but raw eigenvalue statistics are closed at order-one scale.

`I_abs(B) <= M_abs(A) <= U_abs^R(B) <= K_gamma^R I_abs(B)`, `K_gamma^R<2.188`,

where `I_abs` is the binary sign optimum.

The live static theorem is to estimate `I_abs(B_(y,H))/sqrt(R_v)` in a growing prime regime with representation-matched controls. ## Global discriminator: after a closure, identify the new currency before strengthening the old one

The newest evidence reinforces [MI-013](intuition/MI-013-closing-the-bulk-often-leaves-a-thin-source-specific-endpoint.md). AF-346 saturates the sharp concentration trade; FD-127 absorbs every fixed finite prime breadth; MC-297 isolates overlap as the only finite-harmonic rescue; PC-296 separates singular strength from eigenvalue-loop closure; RE-128 separates narrow magnitude localization from signed cancellation; VIS-224 collapses coarse static scale to a binary Hamiltonian; WP-306 closes support-preserving affine repair.

These are not one theorem, but they impose the same research discipline: once evidence shows that a representation or resource can be matched or saturated, a stronger estimate in that same variable is unlikely to be the missing bridge. The next claim must identify the **surviving source coordinate and the exact destination mechanism that charges it**. If that coordinate is only plausible rather than established, it belongs in a clue rather than being promoted to a global intuition.
