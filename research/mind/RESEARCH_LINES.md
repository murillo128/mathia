## What should Prime Circle do with an observable that RH itself makes non-prime?

PC-285 strengthens unconditional Li-grid reproduction of the exact boundary-log finite-section spectrum to the Vinogradov--Korobov source-transport range. PC-286 goes much farther conditionally: under RH the same spectral law is Li-grid reproducible whenever `B=o(sqrt(q)/(log q)^6)`.

Otherwise the architecture should move to cross-level, multi-source, directional, noncommuting or richer observables.

In the quantitative false-RH regime higher-layer mass is too small to carry it; near the minimal recovery scale this forces `gg_J Y^(1-b)/E(Y)` distinct **ordinary first-layer primes** with the recovery-favorable sign and `p-vartheta(p) asymp Y E(Y)`.

An isolated-large-value theorem or a better unconditioned two-sided PNT sup norm does not address this multiplicity-and-selection gate.
