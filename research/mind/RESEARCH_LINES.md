## Where has the exact obstruction moved?

Arithmetic Fidelity: to **uniform growing-depth conditioning, restricted or structured nuisance classes, other noise/correlation laws, and a rational-prime discriminator that matched generalized-prime controls cannot preserve**. The resource is the target discriminator after nuisance quotient, not raw depth before it.

Farey Discrepancy: to **a replication-weighted population of consecutive Mertens blocks whose joint amplitude and density cross the current capacity/first-row phase boundary, a switched-only repair with materially different intermediate motion, or a structural monotone-response upgrade**. Canonical full-grid inversion and isolated deep spikes are no longer generic sources of the missing exponent.

Möbius Cancellation: in the gap branch to **growing rank, a genuinely different source measure/coordinate system, absolute scale or discontinuous arithmetic data**; in the q-vdC branch to **an auxiliary retained-variable representation whose factorable structure is real for the transformed coefficients and whose signed estimate transfers back to Möbius before recombination or majorization erases it**.

Nyman--Beurling: simultaneously to **the established NB-272 section-defect/anchor-mismatch fork** and, on the source-native route, to **the inverse conditioning of the Hardy zero-evaluation Gram `G_(jk)=1/(rho_j+conj(rho_k)-1)` at Ford scale, or a new boundary/nonlinear mechanism not equivalent to the same finite point-evaluation channel**. The finite contour itself is no longer the missing bridge.

Prime Circle: to **the residual beyond the universal subcritical Buchstab--Riesz macroscopic mode**—centered fluctuations, mesoscopic/arithmetic scales or cross-level information—not merely to `alpha<1/2`.

Prime Lattice: to **the PL-407-scale signed mod-5 supertrace with off-diagonal coherences retained, a non-circular `lambda_min(C_arith)>=1/4` lower-frame theorem for raw exact local-factor deletion or a different divisor-faithful positive representation, and a separate residual-order bridge after model subtraction**.

Visual Exploration: to **gap-resolved noncommuting orientation, non-negligible cluster width or paid metric degeneration whose cost survives the complete relative-metric accounting and whose geometry has independent source/destination provenance**.

Weil Inertia: to **a defect-anchored mechanism that crosses the square-root-prime crossover through short-prime-polynomial cancellation, stronger per-defect response or collective sparse-defect aggregation, rather than only collective on-line density**.

Weil Positivity: to **a source-forced linear relation that is nonlocal under continuous hereditary cutoff or genuinely nonclosable, or a different nonlinear/quadratic/one-sided/global completion category**.
