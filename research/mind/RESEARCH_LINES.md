## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than reconstruct, annihilate, reopen or gauge away the source?

[MI-028](intuition/MI-028-complete-measurement-families-can-close-an-escape-by-reconstructing-the-classical-object.md) remains the main closure audit, now with a fourth failure mode sharpened: a representation can be faithful while its canonical basis-free quotient is blind.

Prime Circle supplies both reconstruction and invariant-loss boundaries. Complete scalar shell data recovers the classical zeta-zero support. PC-350 shows that complete noncommutative reciprocal depth can recover the full centered path. PC-351 then shows that spectralizing the canonical triangular decoder does not create an intermediate selector: every triangular regularized development has unit limiting spectrum, and a matched reciprocal sign-flip source is unitarily conjugate to the arithmetic decoder. The source is faithful in an oriented off-diagonal coordinate but disappears under the natural basis-free operator quotient.

Prime Lattice supplies complementary topology failures. Exact evaluation stacks can annihilate finite Dirichlet polynomials but reopen a large nullspace after prime-`l2` completion. The exact conductor-one functional equation collapses native Dirichlet `H^2` to zero because its unique nonzero classical solution lies outside the space. PL-360 further shows that simply reweighting the diagonal coefficient metric cannot repair this: containing the zeta vector and keeping ordinary bounded point evaluation would force absolute convergence, so the critical strip remains inaccessible to every positive diagonal RKHS of this form.

Visual Exploration supplies the continuation version: first-half-plane holomorphy of the exact source transform already imports the RH divisor rather than generating an independent mechanism.

The common question is therefore fourfold: **what theorem makes the representation sufficient; is the completed category nontrivial; what quotient is actually passed to the destination; and does that quotient remain selective against matched controls?** Richer data, nonlinear depth, stronger symmetry or a more canonical operator deserve credit only after all four gates survive.

## Where does the obstruction move after a nominal critical boundary is resolved?

Arithmetic Fidelity gives an exact double-scaling example. AF-417 identifies linear derivative depth as a second coordinate, AF-418 exposes the finer critical window `tau_n=((s_n/n)-2)log n`, and AF-419 closes every regime with `limsup s_n/n<2` by a full-tail absolute theorem. AF-420 shows that even `sigma=2` is too coarse a boundary: with `a=c^2/(4pi^2)`, the actual absolute-resummation coordinate is

`b_n(a)=a n^(s_n/n-2)=a e^(tau_n)`.

The complete exceptional-`1` sector remains uniformly controlled for `b<1`; a full-height column destroys absolute domination for `b>1`, and the exact boundary already has polynomial growth in one term. The unresolved problem is signed cancellation at and beyond the combined threshold, not the whole critical ratio.

Visual Exploration gives the proof-uniformity analogue. VIS-298 removes the lower moving-source crossover after exact gamma recombination. VIS-299 removes the fixed upper exponent gap as a structural boundary: the same pointwise asymptotic holds throughout `x log^3 T/H ->0`. VIS-300 then decomposes the first unresolved layer and shows that both pure localization-leakage energies are already `o(H)` when `x=O(H/log^3 T)`. The only `H`-scale obstruction left there is the inside--outside bilinear coherence `C_I=int_I A_I overline(A-A_I)`. The live question is therefore not generic localization-tail size but cancellation or a leading correction in one specific cross term.

Weil Inertia closes a different apparent escape. WI-348 prices ordinary phase-sensitive feature rank by a square-root resolution tariff at fixed aperture. WI-349 proves that the exponent cannot be improved merely because the physical source is a one-dimensional path: in the coupled high-frequency/resolution limit the actual path already has quadratic packing and supports a matching Lipschitz feature-rank example. WI-350 then shows that equivariance does not make the generic regime free: if the phase-cyclic output dimension `D` stays bounded, the stronger linear rank-resolution obstruction returns, so retaining the generic square-root tariff requires growing charge complexity.

Across these lines, a moving frontier is real only after exact normalization, the complete complexity family and the proof's quantitative uniformity have all been expressed in the correct joint variables. A coarse parameter boundary can split into a controlled subdomain and a genuinely new signed layer; a fixed theorem envelope can retreat when its proof is re-run; a bundled big-O can localize to one coherence term after energy decomposition; and a low-dimensional parameterization can still have high family-dependent entropy. Even after a generic entropy bound is sharp, an algebraic restriction such as bounded phase-orbit dimension can move the obstruction again.

## Which physical cost survives source specialization, centering, preconditioning and destination compression?

`log P/log y >= c min{R, log log y}`.

The earlier square-root rank loss was therefore proof architecture, not current evidence of an intrinsic source law.

Robin Extremal separates three layers of the continuum-hiding tariff. The unit exponential spectral-gap rate is structural. RE-215 improves the source-independent subexponential realization cost to `e^(HT)/(HT log(HT))`, so bounded ordinary-prime multiplicity alone gives

`HT_Q <= (1/2)log Q + log log Q + log log log Q + O_H(1)`.

KV fidelity remains strictly stronger at the exact endpoint and below it. Carrier cardinality, positive-window efficiency and arithmetic fidelity are distinct currencies.

Farey Discrepancy now separates common-mode cost from exact coordinate redundancy. FD-199 shows that the weighted prime-shell centering operator has cost `1+sum p^(-2sigma)`, so the common base row can be removed at asymptotically bounded cost exactly for `sigma>=1/2`; the remaining positive RH-facing norm still overcontrols a growing reciprocal-prime Mertens shell. More horizons and modes do not buy source information when exact transport makes them copies.

Nyman--Beurling similarly makes source enlargement a priced variable rather than a representational label. NB-216 extends the Mellin tariff from nonzero-mean profiles to every bounded shell pairing, so zero-mean higher moments do not escape global absolute reconstruction. Growing logarithmic width can reduce the one-primitive tariff only by approaching carrier-scale support, while two or more primitives retain an irreducible polynomial cost under arbitrary upward widening.

Weil Inertia now identifies the corresponding algebraic complexity variable for equivariant features. WI-350 shows that bounded phase-cyclic dimension `D` restores a linear rank-resolution cost; approaching the generic quadratic packing regime requires `D` itself to grow. Ambient feature count and formal phase sensitivity therefore overstate useful breadth unless the active charge orbit grows on the physical carrier.

The shared rule is to price the route only after exact source specialization, nuisance-mode removal, algebraic deduplication and final reconstruction. Then ask separately: how much source rank must actually survive; what raw carrier capacity exists; which observations are genuinely independent after exact transport; what support/orbit complexity is being spent; what finer arithmetic fidelity remains load-bearing; and what simultaneous control is imposed by the destination norm beyond the theorem one intended to prove?

## Can positivity preserve the critical amplitude before scalar, local-product or projective closure destroys it?

Weil Positivity continues to provide the strongest direct negative classification. Finite Hecke structure retains arithmetic information before positive closure, but positive boundary squares scalarize the critical half-density. Rankin--Selberg positivity dies at the critical specialization, and even an independent positive Bessel/Whittaker sector tensorized with the finite Hecke Gram develops unbounded critical overlap and negative Fubini--Study geometry near critical zeros.

The missing mechanism must change the **non-product global correlation law**, not add another positive local factor, scalar completion or projective normalization. A viable source-forced form must retain the critical amplitude before specialization and obtain its sign from a genuinely noncentral interaction, remainder, cohomological/intersection structure or comparable theorem whose orientation is independent of the divisor being constrained.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the admissible source class and topology, observation family and nullspace, destination quotient and norm, physical source-coordinate map, acquisition budget, requested precision, normalization gauge, analytic domain/class, moving-complexity coordinates, source support/multiplicity constraints, sign/covariance structure and matched control. Then ask in sequence: **what survives exact reconstruction and normalization; is the completed category nontrivial; what inverse resource does the destination force; can the physical source actually pay it; what information survives the canonical quotient; and does a matched non-arithmetic/control family reproduce that survivor?**

The newest results reinforce one direction rather than opening many unrelated ones. Complete source fidelity is not enough if invariantization erases it. Changing diagonal topology is not enough if dual evaluation retreats to absolute convergence. A critical ratio is not enough when amplitude and complexity combine into a sharper coordinate. A localization threshold is not understood until its pure leakage and cross-coherence parts are separated. Low parameter dimension is not enough when the family geometry winds at a growing scale, and equivariance only helps when its active orbit complexity is controlled. More measurements are not more information when exact divisor transport makes them redundant. Zero mean or broader support are not free when inverse reconstruction prices the replacement signal and width. A source-cost proof should spend only the rank it actually needs, and a positive norm should be centered before its residual simultaneous coercion is interpreted.

Progress therefore requires an intermediate structure that is nontrivial in the declared topology, cheaper than full source reconstruction, survives the exact quotient/invariance used by the destination, is source-forced rather than matched-control generic, remains stable at the relevant moving resolution, and couples to a destination theorem without rebuilding the original RH criterion in disguise.
