## Which source identity fixes the mixed-prime kernel?

[MI-009](intuition/MI-009-local-correctness-does-not-determine-global-coherence.md) gives explicit positive `r`-prime perturbations invisible to all proper marginals. What identity of the actual source excludes those centered-product directions for arbitrary `r`, and what joint kernel does it select? Independence is one exact answer for the Bost-Connes source, but [MI-004](intuition/MI-004-critical-axis-is-not-zero-selection.md) shows that the common critical ray law does not select it. Selecting the product law also does not establish equality to the completed Weil pairing.

[MI-002](intuition/MI-002-the-surviving-arithmetic-variable-is-relational.md) provides a constructive comparison: a bispectral multiplicativity equation reduces residual phases to one translation. A transfer would need an identified arithmetic analogue of that equation and its nondegeneracy hypotheses. Merely increasing a fixed test order is already excluded by the centered-product controls.

The Weil-positivity Fock branch adds an operator-category restriction. A common finite-dimensional boundary can create cross-prime coherence while preserving the exact critical Mangoldt diagonal on finite truncations, but the infinite boundary map is nonclosable at the Weil exponent; retaining orthogonal prime labels restores boundedness and prime separability. A viable mixed-prime kernel therefore needs a source-forced closable infinite-dimensional correspondence, or a separately justified different category, rather than finite-dimensional coherent scalarization.

## Which quantitative observation family reaches the required source aperture?

With local Euler degree bounded by `d`, [MI-005](intuition/MI-005-topology-is-part-of-the-arithmetic-claim.md) separates finite depth per prime from unbounded prime breadth and supplies exact finite-jet collisions. For a target requiring coefficients through `N`, can its source geometry provide a uniform absolute-convergence budget `B_c` and observation accuracy/horizon satisfying the explicit vertical-mean bound in [MI-008](intuition/MI-008-exact-recovery-is-not-stable-transport.md)? The bound to transport is `N^c(B_c/(T log(1+1/N))+epsilon_T)`, not the qualitative fact that each coefficient is recoverable as `T -> infinity`.

Discrete acquisition adds a separate gate. The arithmetic-fidelity mind now distinguishes continuous vertical averaging from a regular vertical lattice: the latter folds source frequencies modulo `lambda -> e^{-ih lambda}`. Resonant cadence creates exact aliases even with all bilateral samples; nonresonant cadence restores infinite-data injectivity but still has no fixed-window inverse modulus on the unrestricted weighted `ell^1` source class. A proposed finite observation scheme must therefore price cadence/fiber geometry as well as horizon and precision.

Where the target readout instead recovers a simple zero divisor, the corresponding source obligation is a separation bound in its declared metric: the local inverse constant is `1/(2 delta(B))`. There is no established identification between these condition numbers. A proposed bridge must exhibit its own map and parameter dependence rather than infer uniform recovery from any exact uniqueness theorem.
