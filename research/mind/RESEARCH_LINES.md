# Global mathematical questions

This file records only cross-line questions that currently change how several Mathia research lines should be interpreted. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does retained information become a discriminator and a quantitative resource?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit. The current evidence separates four gates: the representation must retain source information, that information must survive the exact quotient/completion used downstream, it must distinguish the source from the strongest matched control, and it must retain a positive destination-scale margin or reserve.

Arithmetic Fidelity now has the corrected source-relative geometry. AF-451 retracts the attempt to classify a fixed source-law fiber by the barycenter of the residual Radon circuit: common positive background can move a core between fibers and consumes sparsity. AF-452 identifies the exact stability carrier for difference-homogeneous metrics as the normalized secants obtained from **completable** Radon cores. The next cost is the minimum observation gain on that physical carrier, not rank or a raw circuit label.

Farey Discrepancy has moved from signed invisibility to one-sided realizability. FD-232 proves that a switched-blind profile with macroscopic negative aggregate capacity cannot be hidden inside a subquadratic positive lift. The physical object is now the affine distance of the source-forced correction to the positive cone modulo the switched blind space.

Möbius Cancellation has localized the remaining translated phase more precisely. MC-414 makes the character phase endpoint-separable; the source coupling survives in a thin divisor-dependent domain rather than in a new character dimension. MC-415 then shows that ordinary positive van der Corput/Cauchy differencing re-squares the linear sieve on the zero-shift diagonal. Any gain must beat that diagonal cost with an incomplete bilinear-character estimate before completion erases the geometry.

Nyman--Beurling now distinguishes exact finite reconstruction, bad macroscopic averaging and source-faithful local averaging. NB-245 shows that finite Cauchy/Stieltjes data are algebraically injective but can be badly conditioned for a growing depth mark. NB-246 proves that the complete frozen smooth Ford current has, after demodulation, only `O(H)` center bandwidth. Pointwise selected energy follows from a **uniform weighted local second moment on scale `1/H`**, while the depth-marked zero-pair phase still resolves the finer `1/X` carrier scale.

Prime Circle now has two matched-control layers. PC-369--PC-370 show that Fock, Chen--Hopf/log-signature and centered-refinement data remain equivariant under the reciprocal common unitary when radial order is forgotten. PC-371 proves that the source-native shell-2 order cone genuinely breaks that control, but the cone is universal for arbitrary conjugation-stable unit-circle roots and its canonical moments are finite Ramanujan transforms. The next discriminator must combine order with source-forced cross-shell structure rather than merely retain or decode one shell.

Prime Lattice now has an exact topology mismatch. PL-391 shows why finite Jessen half-line density is too coarse to imply the zeta divisor. PL-392 shows that the Euler--Maclaurin correction which makes finite cutoffs converge locally uniformly to zeta—and hence recover fixed zeros—is Besicovitch-null. The native `B^2` quotient therefore identifies a zero-faithful approximant with the wrong raw cutoff. A surviving category must retain numerical frequencies, a distinguished continuation representative and individual-zero sensitivity together.

Robin Extremal still closes the obvious same-ray multi-center escape: serial products of individually successful centers have exactly multiplicative Wiener cost, so center count without coefficient-phase disagreement is representation-neutral.

Visual Exploration has now exhibited the generic high-frequency escape and its repayment explicitly. VIS-335 gives fixed modes with `O(1/omega)` Wang output and order-one derivative, while VIS-336 realizes the same separation along one quadratic chirp. The remaining question is whether the actual squared-von-Mangoldt remainder has controlled log-frequency migration and whether the final Wang destination avoids paying back the derivative channel.

Weil Inertia has repaired the diagonal transport but exposed a harder nonlocal gate. WI-367 supplies the endpoint multiplier that closes the half-density Jacobian bookkeeping. WI-368 shows that the printed gamma cancellation has a quadratic-form sign mismatch which no scalar or diagonal correction can fix. The remaining reserve must come from a genuine additional off-diagonal source term or be proved directly for the true gamma-corrected channel.

Weil Positivity moves the selector boundary from finite dimension to topology. WP-383 extends the WP-382 rank-one collapse to every Banach/Hilbert-valued response continuous for the compact-open topology. Infinite interior coordinates still collapse to one Mangoldt line if one fixed compact radius controls them. A live escape must genuinely approach the boundary, vary with arithmetic scale, or postpone mixed-shell selection until finite and archimedean data interact.

Across the lines, enrichment deserves credit only when the retained information survives the actual quotient/closure, is not cloned by the strongest matched control, is resolved in the topology seen by the destination, and carries enough quantitative margin after normalization.
