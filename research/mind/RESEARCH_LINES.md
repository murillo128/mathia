# Global mathematical questions

This file records mathematical questions that only become visible by comparing several current Mathia research lines. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does a quantitative budget become a genuine source discriminator?

Several lines now show that the decisive budget is rarely the first coarse norm one can write down. The useful quantity must be fixed before target inspection, live in the geometry actually consumed by the destination, and be occupied by the physical source rather than by a matched control.

Visual Exploration gives a sharp matched-control progression. VIS-416 removes the generic reciprocal-bandwidth scale from Wang balance radii; VIS-417 shows that matching bandwidth, mode count, coefficient magnitudes, long-time energy and global supremum still leaves an uncontrolled beat scale; VIS-418 shows that even fixing the complete two-mode frequency set does not fix alignment with a distinguished height because translation moves the same quiet window. VIS-419 then resolves the fixed-shell null exactly: a finite Wang shell is a character polynomial on its base-prime torus, and long translation is the Haar pushforward of those base-prime phases with all ratio-mode dependencies retained. The remaining discriminator is therefore a source-specific anomaly at pre-specified arithmetic heights relative to that exact self-null, not a different random-phase surrogate. Quantitative finite-window convergence and jointly growing shell/height limits remain outside the fixed-shell theorem.

Weil Inertia supplies the source-category analogue. WI-417--WI-423 show that a frozen density or function-space budget is meaningful only when it belongs to an independently generated source and controls the Poisson-capacity geometry of the actual defect; assigning regularity directly to the canonical atomic zero measure would already build the target conclusion into the hypothesis. WI-424--WI-428 then show that bounded processing on a fixed safe strip remains cubically blind to shallow defects. WI-429 identifies the exact category change: on a line inside a defect, a norm-one Hardy projection separates deeper Kunik zeros and recovers order-`1/d` energy without an operator blow-up. The new source budget is therefore not singular operator norm but the ability to control a **predetermined boundary-approaching family of probes** for the full zeta source, independently of the unknown defect depths.

Farey Discrepancy turns a destination capacity target into an increasingly rigid source anomaly. FD-298--FD-300 reduce the low-height hard complement to the block-Mertens field and show that near-capacity `l^1` mass forces a dyadic shell with mean-square block increment at least `N x^(2c-o(1))` and a positive aligned same-block Möbius correlation. FD-301 removes the same-block mask at logarithmic Fourier cost, forcing a large rational additive-twist two-point sum; FD-302 shows that in the genuinely twisted branch the required frequency lies in a low block-Fourier arc, shrinking for polynomial depth `lambda>1/(4c)`, while the zero mode remains the alternative. A uniform cancellation theorem for this exact source family would therefore kill the near-capacity route. These are still necessary conditions: even a large low-frequency correlation need not survive divisor replication into the destination `l^1` field.

Prime Lattice gives a complementary lesson about using the exact destination geometry rather than a convenient ambient norm. PL-437 compresses the unresolved mod-5 orientation to two coordinates `(s,f)` and gives the Euclidean Weyl disk as a sufficient condition. PL-438 derives the exact strict-branch Schur ellipse `(g-mu)^T E^(-1)(g-mu)<=alpha`, with its center and anisotropy determined by visible real-product data plus the residue diagonal, and exhibits an admissible covariance accepted by the ellipse but rejected by the Weyl disk. The remaining theorem is arithmetic: place the actual prime source inside that source-adapted ellipse at variance scale by an unconditional input.

Across these examples, a bound becomes a source discriminator only after four checks: its source object is independently generated; its geometry matches the destination failure mode; matched controls preserve every nuisance scale that can manufacture the effect; and the physical source is forced to satisfy or violate the bound with quantitative margin. VIS-419, WI-429, FD-301--FD-302 and PL-438 sharpen different parts of that test: an exact self-null can replace a vague control ensemble, a geometric approach to a boundary can replace a singular operator, a masked energy condition can collapse to a low-frequency arithmetic anomaly, and an anisotropic destination region can replace a coarse Euclidean norm.

## Which source dependence survives quotienting, population ceilings and equilibrium scalarization?

Möbius Cancellation now prices the short-endpoint branch in the actual source geometry in three stages. MC-497 makes large quadratic short-overlap bias exceptional in the physical start coordinate, and MC-498 turns that into an effective-support condition on the induced start weights with explicit quotient-fibre costs. MC-499 adds a per-block label ceiling `1+Q/p`. MC-500 then shows that optimizing the dyadic scale cannot evade scarcity: reciprocal quotient compression gives the Q-free ceiling

`R_start <= 4+sqrt(2Z_*/p)`

with `Z_*=max(X,X+h)`. The current exceptional-start second-moment certificate can work at some dyadic scale only when the source height is large enough relative to `p T_(p,H)^2`. Failure before that gate is **method-limited source scarcity across all Q**, not coefficient concentration; only after the Q-free and per-block gates pass does a failed MC-498 participation test diagnose quotient folding, weight concentration or winning-side imbalance.

Farey Discrepancy provides a complementary exact-carrier example after a mask quotient. The FD-300 aligned same-block correlation initially depends on a growing periodic mask, but FD-301 expresses that mask in additive characters with only logarithmic `l^1` cost, and FD-302 proves that high block frequencies cannot alone carry a near-capacity anomaly in the live depth range. What survives the masking representation is therefore an untwisted or quantitatively low-frequency two-point Möbius mode. The carrier is not the raw number of aligned pairs and not an arbitrary Fourier coefficient; it is the low-mode anomaly forced after the exact mask has been priced.

Prime Circle supplies the stronger scalarization warning. PC-414--PC-419 show that finite/infinite conductor descendants, finite Mellin jets, bounded Mellin translations, unrestricted Weyl closure and even the norm-closed single-shell diagonal algebra do not recover a source interaction already lost before shell-2 scalarization. The surviving object must therefore be cross-shell, non-diagonal or otherwise source-coupled before the scalar channel discards it.

Visual Exploration gives the corresponding phase quotient. VIS-409--VIS-411 show that one-shell Wigner data are repackaging, cross-shell fields retain only already-present labelled coherence, and projective shell phases reduce to Bargmann cycle holonomies. VIS-418--VIS-419 sharpen the distinction between gauge and source anchoring: absolute phase relative to a distinguished arithmetic height is meaningful only when the source fixes that height, and its correct fixed-shell null is translation/Haar on the base-prime torus rather than independent ratio-mode phases. The quotient category and its matched null must be fixed before interpreting residual phase as signal.

Weil Positivity closes two large equilibrium escapes. WP-423 proves separate invariance for the covered atomic/block product-dynamics KMS fibres. WP-424 shows that moving to a commutative non-atomic fibre does not help: every invariant central commutative fibre is time-trivial in the KMS GNS quotient, so nonzero opposite-frequency fibre modes vanish before they can pair with the source. Any surviving equilibrium mechanism must therefore use genuinely noncentral noncommutative fibre degrees or source-derived interacting/non-product dynamics, not merely more spectral support.

Nyman--Beurling supplies a quantitative scalarization boundary. NB-319 shows that every mesoscopic reciprocal annulus up to the quadratic frontier contains a direction whose primitive envelope is within a square-root logarithm of linear growth, so primitive `L^infinity` control cannot yield a fixed-power gain. NB-320 then computes the actual weighted pairing on the adjacent half-period resonance: the `1/t^2` weight suppresses the primitive obstruction by a quadratic factor, yet the Hilbert-normalized rank-one error is still `Theta(1/sqrt(log n))` along `m=n(n-1)/2`. The scalarized bilinear channel therefore retains much less than the primitive envelope but still enough resonance to forbid polynomial convergence on that sequence. The correct carrier is the weighted pairing itself, with its frequency arithmetic, not the pre-scalarization supremum used to bound it.

The reusable cross-line question is therefore: **after the lawful quotient and the actual destination scalarization, what source-dependent quantity remains both invariant and quantitatively load-bearing?** Effective support is not raw pair multiplicity and may be capped before weights enter; a masked correlation may reduce to a low Fourier mode; a phase coordinate is not intrinsic unless the source fixes its gauge and null; an auxiliary Bohr mode is not active if the KMS quotient freezes its algebra; and a large primitive envelope is not the actual bilinear error. These are different manifestations of the same discipline: price the exact carrier that remains after the representation has finished discarding information.

## What replaces a scalar summary when nuisance geometry or leading normalization degenerates?

Arithmetic Fidelity now gives both an obstruction and a precise replacement geometry. AF-523--AF-528 show that scalar boundary-curvature summaries can be matched to all algebraic orders while an exact relational derivative still recovers hidden source shape; that recovery can nevertheless be too small on the physical observation window to be stable. AF-533--AF-534 then identify the nuisance-relative carrier: effective Gaussian information is the squared precision/Cameron--Martin distance of the target direction from the **closed** nuisance geometry, so algebraic identifiability need not imply a positive robust margin.

AF-535 adds observation loss. With upstream efficient score `e`, observation contraction `C`, and downstream nuisance closure `M`, the efficient carrier is `(I-P_M)Ce`; information can disappear by channel erasure or by nuisance recontamination. AF-536 gives the sharper deterministic quotient: for `P=E[.\mid Y]` and `K=ker P`,

`I_Y^eff = dist(e, closure(Lambda+K))^2`.

Thus deterministic compression identifies the target only modulo the closed sum of nuisance and invisible directions, and nested deterministic statistics produce an increasing orthogonal loss budget. AF-537 extends this to sequential Markov compression with evolving nuisance semantics: every stage loses information through direct erasure, pushed-nuisance recontamination, or **new-nuisance reclassification**. Even lossless data cannot repair the third term while the downstream equivalence relation itself declares part of the target carrier to be nuisance.

This sharpens the replacement-object requirement. A successful non-scalar observable must not merely break an algebraic collision; it must carry the efficient target direction outside the closed nuisance-plus-invisible geometry after the actual observation chain, and any downstream enlargement of the nuisance class must itself be source-justified. A target-dependent score lift can be first-order sufficient without being an admissible arithmetic representation. Prime Lattice's Schur ellipse is an exact finite-dimensional destination geometry of this type, while Weil Inertia's moving-line Hardy separation shows that changing the observation geometry can preserve a target signal without singular operator norm only if the source can support the required boundary approach.

Prime Circle remains the negative control: a finite derivative or richer operator readout can be perfectly informative about a chosen representation while still adding no arithmetic mechanism if matched source classes reproduce it. The remaining cross-line challenge is to construct replacement objects that are simultaneously **source-natural, destination-complete and stable after the real quotient/compression**. Exact infinitesimal recovery, a convenient nuisance-dependent lift, an ambient norm bound, or a richer representation is not enough; the carrier must be forced by the source before target inspection and retain quantitative separation after every nuisance, gauge, observation and semantic map that the destination actually applies.
