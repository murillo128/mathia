The exponents price available source-density technology, not intrinsic locality.

Farey Discrepancy sharpens the source-class side nearly to the extremal coordinatewise boundary. FD-177 closes the dyadic gap and reaches every fixed `rho<1`: one can preserve all sufficiently large squarefree coefficients with `omega(n)<=rho log n/loglog n`, match every dyadic midpoint sample, and confine deviations to `O(x^eta)` coefficients for arbitrarily small `eta>0`. The load-bearing resource is therefore not coefficient fidelity, even almost up to extremal multiplicative rank, but cross-coordinate compatibility that prevents rare high-rank shells from repairing each observation block.

Robin Extremal now pushes the conditioning warning to the scalar capacity boundary. RE-184 showed that a growing Euler-jet family remains interpolable below the KV shell scale, RE-185 removed a coordinate-conditioning loss and reached `K=Theta(V(p))`, and RE-186 removes the remaining coarse-generation transport loss with microstepped Chebyshev shell tracks. Honest ordinary-prime `{0,1,2}` controls can match every depth `K=o(log p/loglog p)` while retaining KV fidelity and an order-one transient Robin excursion. The unresolved scalar boundary is the critical capacity crossover itself, not finite versus growing dimension.

Together these results say that observation count, scalar depth and coefficient accuracy become mathematical resources only after the admissible source fibre, physical source map, conditioning and same-scale source theorem are fixed. Even extraordinarily high coordinate fidelity may leave repair freedom if the missing constraint is relational, and even a large scalar family may remain nonselective until it reaches its true conditioned capacity scale. A useful destination theorem must identify a relation that matched controls cannot keep repairing, not merely accumulate more coordinates.

The common question is therefore not “how many measurements?” but **which source-native quantitative resource survives exactly where the destination becomes thin or moves**: state boundedness, amplitude-balanced rank, scale-matched source density, theorem uniformity under an exact coordinate pullback, critical-scale scalar capacity, or globally discoverable holomorphic support.

## Global discriminator: identify the exact resource a matched control cannot fake

For each proposed route, first state the source class, exact observation/target quotient, physical source-coordinate map, acquisition norm and destination. Then ask which source directions the destination actually distinguishes; whether changing horizons, heights or operators supplies new source coordinates or only reparameterizes one cell; whether the theorem is uniform at the moving scale obtained after the exact coordinate map; whether amplitudes and conditioning are retained rather than normalized away; whether a source constraint couples coordinates strongly enough to block fresh-shell repair; whether a lower-bound tariff remains unaffordable after the representation is optimally conditioned all the way to its true capacity scale; whether reverse likelihood sensitivity of the actual transcript can be bounded rather than inferred from forward smoothness; whether positive or self-adjoint structure still permits an internal divisor-changing matched control; and whether the final survivor is tied to a zero/sign theorem rather than merely reconstructing the arithmetic source.

A useful bridge should fail on a matched control for an independently arithmetic reason. Exact reconstruction, high nominal rank, dense phase families, smooth source laws, positive operator geometry and merely subcritical growing scalar depth are intermediate resources. The program advances only when one of them becomes **destination-selective under the same source fibre, coordinate map, conditioning, theorem scale and control class that the claimed RH mechanism actually uses**.
