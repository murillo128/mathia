# Global mathematical questions

This file records only cross-line questions that currently change how several Mathia research lines should be interpreted. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than another absorbed channel?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit, but the recent boundaries make the alternatives sharper.

Farey Discrepancy now shows that squarefree ternary structure and **every fixed finite collection of exact Möbius Euler sign relations** can still coexist with an all-integer switched blind mode. Prime Lattice shows that finite-dimensional completely multiplicative matrix structure is filtered through scalar characters, while the first `2x2` nonnormal self-extension is only a completely additive tangent jet. Prime Circle shows a different failure mode: an infinite completion can genuinely destroy bounded reciprocal similarity yet compactify positive-degree transport so strongly that ordinary spectrum becomes trivial anyway.

These are not one theorem, but they enforce the same audit: **what new source relation reaches the destination observable after all exact quotients, filtrations and regularization are applied?** Finite structural enrichment may be absorbed by a Green operator, scalarized by a common flag, or killed by compactness. More representation is useful only when it changes the destination-visible equivalence class.

Weil Inertia supplies an important counterexample to the idea that all enrichment is cosmetic. WI-355 changes the information channel itself: the full localized Weil quadratic form has a monotone ground energy, and any RH failure forces a finite-radius zero crossing. The normalized short-interval pair-correlation statistic can remain blind to sparse defects, while the uncompressed variational form cannot hide every failure in the same way. A richer representation is therefore meaningful when it changes defect sensitivity, not merely when it adds coordinates.

## Where has the next exact obstruction moved after the latest boundary results?

**Arithmetic Fidelity.** AF-433 corrects the provenance picture: the square amplitudes `a=m^2` coincide exactly with source singularity shells `c=2pi m`, so they have genuine source-side realization. But the probe family still does not select one shell. AF-434 then shows that the present determinant sums shell labels into zeta factors before partition packets form, so the square saddle threshold is not transport of source shell index into packet index. The live gates are now **selection plus transport**, before shrinking-target membership.

**Farey Discrepancy.** FD-214 preserves all-integer switched blindness under squarefree ternary rounding, and FD-215 preserves it after imposing any fixed finite set of exact Möbius Euler sign relations. The next discriminator must therefore use scale-growing/global multiplicative coherence or an observation that genuinely mixes the dyadic mantissa fibres. The proposed growing-Euler-coherence clue asks for the quantitative threshold rather than assuming one.

**Nyman--Beurling.** NB-225--NB-227 close population-only, fixed-power phase and every separable `M^vartheta/Q(D)` repair at the exact-Ford boundary. NB-228 identifies the nonseparable object consumed by the residue: Ford damping is the Laplace dual of the nested cumulative depth-phase carrier `C(D)`. Critical matched controls have type `e^(YD)`; a zeta-specific subcritical bound `e^((1-delta)YD)` would force the deep residue tail small. The missing theorem is now a joint cumulative depth-phase estimate, not another per-depth penalty.

**Möbius Cancellation.** MC-394--MC-395 sharpen the source-cost obstruction below the logarithmic horizon. With `A=log P/log y`, rank `R`, and `L=log log y`, `R=o(L)` forces

`A >= R - O(R^2/L + log R)`.

Thus the current endpoint package pays asymptotically full source cost per rank until the transition `R~log log y`; moving the cutoff closer to the shell is itself paid through analytic differencing depth.

**Prime Circle.** PC-359 shows that unbounded reciprocal parity is necessary but not sufficient. In ratio-decaying scalar grade completions the parity intertwiner becomes unbounded, yet positive-degree creation compactifies and every norm-convergent strictly grade-raising multiplier has trivial spectrum. The live completion must both break bounded similarity and retain noncompact high-grade transport.

**Prime Lattice.** PL-374--PL-375 close the first finite-dimensional matrix escape from the scalar Venturini class. Complete multiplicativity gives scalar composition factors; distinct `2x2` extensions split and nonvanishing self-extensions are additive first jets. The next mechanism must genuinely use higher/infinite extension structure, break exact complete multiplicativity, or use a destination observable that does not factor through the scalar filtration.

**Robin Extremal.** RE-226 closes the vanishing-arc boundary by an anti-aligned Wiener evaluation, and RE-227 upgrades the family-wise statement by diagonal closure: for each fixed corridor ratio `R` there is a genuine class constant `eta_R>0` above rate two. The positive-real single-center fixed-corridor class is therefore closed; the constructive frontier has moved to complex centers, multi-center/minimax geometry, another basis, or quantitative estimation of `eta_R`.

**Visual Exploration.** VIS-315--VIS-316 reduce the natural Wang mean-square window to a taper-weighted prime-ratio occupancy problem. A Fourier cell is a thin determinant strip around a represented prime ratio, and the destination needs only the weighted shell profile `B=o((log log x)^(3/2))`, not uniform near-average density in every shell. The live input is a weighted determinant-strip/sparse-energy estimate or a coherent central-shell exception.

**Weil Inertia.** WI-354 remains a barrier for compressed zero-count-normalized pair correlation, but WI-355 changes the full-form frontier. The localized Weil ground energy is monotone in radius, so any RH failure has a finite first zero crossing; at radius `a` its arithmetic kernel uses only prime powers up to `e^(2a)`. The missing theorem is source-side coercivity for every finite radius, not merely stronger bulk zero statistics.

**Weil Positivity.** The mixed-prime completion frontier remains after the universal product control removes the divergent local completion tax. The question is whether the arithmetic/archimedean source forces a non-universal mixed-prime coefficient pattern whose positivity and orientation survive that control.

**Analytic Frontier, Prime Flute and Xi Flow.** Their current local frontiers are unchanged by the present evidence delta: respectively the mesoscopic residual-slack/tail regime, genuine geometric weak-trace reassembly beyond multiplier locality, and Xi-specific tail/stability control beyond finite log-jet reconstruction.

Across these lines, continuing to optimize a variable already proved nondecisive attacks the wrong layer. The useful next step is to identify the newly exposed source relation, transport map, growth threshold, noncompact channel, cumulative carrier or coercive observable exactly.

## Which physical cost survives once representation artifacts are removed?

The recent results refine the cost ledger rather than collapsing it. Arithmetic Fidelity now separates source realization from source selection and transport. Farey asks how the cost of exact Euler coherence grows when the constrained prime set itself grows. Nyman--Beurling replaces separable phase/depth budgets by the exact Laplace-weighted cumulative carrier. Möbius Cancellation turns relation codimension into physical conductor cost and then exposes the analytic-depth tariff of a moving cutoff. Visual Exploration weights local frequency crowding by the actual Wang taper instead of paying a uniform shell maximum. Prime Circle shows that regularization can itself destroy the spectral resource one hoped to use.

The reusable question is: **after the exact source specialization and the strongest representation cleanup, what quantity still has to be paid by the physical source rather than by coordinates, metric, cutoff, completion or normalization?** A source-side cost is credible only when it is measured in variables consumed by the final theorem and survives the matched control.

## Can sparse or deep defects be made visible without first making the whole statistic stronger?

Several lines distinguish bulk control from destination-sensitive defect detection. Normalized pair-correlation in Weil Inertia can tolerate sparse off-line defects even with strong bulk information; WI-355 shows that full localized Weil energy instead turns any failure into a finite-radius variational event. Nyman--Beurling shows an analogous need to preserve the destination weighting: only the nested cumulative phase process paired with Ford damping controls the actual residue; per-depth cancellation can look strong while a slowly moving matched packet survives. Robin Extremal closes a local approximation escape by evaluating the same global coefficient vector in an anti-aligned direction outside the observed arc. Visual Exploration asks whether a central thin determinant cluster actually carries enough weighted coherent mass to affect the destination, not merely whether such a cluster exists.

The common design principle is to identify a **destination-sensitive functional before strengthening averages**. If normalization, scalarization or separable bookkeeping has already erased the relevant defect, a better estimate of the same compressed statistic may not recover it. A successful change of observable must come with an exact argument showing why the defect now has persistent cost.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the source class, parameter provenance, source-to-representation transport, observation family and nullspace, destination quotient, exact inverse or ordered transfer, preserved fibre coordinates, metric covariance/similarity law, analytic divisor/gauge freedom, source mesh, population/depth coupling, relation codimension and conductor cost, finite-window spectral aggregation, cumulative attenuation duality, completion class, defect normalization and destination quantifier.

Then ask: **what genuinely new source information is acquired; by what exact map does it reach the destination; what equivalence or matched control does it break; and what physical cost remains after the most faithful representation is used?** A route that survives this audit has isolated a mathematical bottleneck. A route that fails it has usually rediscovered a provenance, transport, fibrewise-inverse, similarity, gauge, separability, conductor, spectral-density, normalization or completion artifact in different notation.
