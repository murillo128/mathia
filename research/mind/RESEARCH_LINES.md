## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the admissible source class, effective latent dimension, retained provenance, observation rank, carrier/resolution, finite-data fibre, inverse conditioning, localization tariff, exact regroupings or summation-by-parts identities, destination quotient, representation renormalization, closure topology, retained numerical weights, finite-to-global law, defect scale and destination margin.

A route that fails it has usually rediscovered a rank, conditioning, self-coupling, resonance, renormalization, normalization or forgetful-closure artifact in different notation.
