## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than merely transport, tune or reconstruct the same source?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) and [MI-028](intuition/MI-028-complete-measurement-families-can-close-an-escape-by-reconstructing-the-classical-object.md) remain the common interface audit.

Arithmetic Fidelity adds a provenance gate before the usual inverse audit. AF-433 shows that the left-tail amplitude `a=c^2/(4pi^2)` is a bijective reparameterization of the freely chosen diagonal probe `x_n(c)=c/n`. The residual/null shrinking-target structure of AF-432 therefore classifies exceptional probe paths, not intrinsic source amplitudes. Before asking whether a distinguished point hits a critical target, one must first prove that the source actually selects that point independently of the desired target geometry.

Prime Circle now distinguishes covariance from similarity. Full-signature/free-Fock data remain algebraically transported by reciprocal parity. A source-native Gram metric recomputed on the control is carried to `J^*GJ` and restores an isometry. Freezing one common parity-breaking metric can destroy unitarity, but PC-357 shows that after whitening the source/control multipliers remain exactly similar through `S_N=direct_sum S^(tensor m)`, with singular/resolvent separation priced by `kappa_H(J)^N`. A richer Hilbert geometry is not discriminating when its entire depth dependence can be explained by one static nonunitary intertwiner.

Farey Discrepancy now separates fixed from nonautonomous enrichment. For a fixed finite polynomial family applied to the physical root increment, a common characteristic root in `sqrt(2)<|rho|<=2` gives a bounded-source super-square-root blind mode; if the gcd has no root in that annulus, square-root pointwise control already reconstructs the destination. FD-212 then proves that bounded-depth **scale switching** is a genuinely different regime: the periodic schedule `q=(0,3,3)` has a supercritical Floquet multiplier even though each instantaneous filter is annulus-free and the fixed family has gcd `1`. The sparse dyadic mode is realizable by a bounded source at critical random cost. The missing resource is now cross-ray/all-integer source coherence.

Prime Lattice separates source selection from global analytic power. Complete multiplicativity plus continuation gives a genuine zero-sensitive theorem interface, and local pole neutrality forces witnesses into a finite prime-harmonic neighbourhood of Liouville. PL-371 shows that local rigidity alone gives no continuation gain. PL-372 then proves that actual continuation forces Liouville closeness at every crossed prime exponent; for a finite prime alphabet, the relative Euler correction is holomorphic and zero-free and the witness is divisor-equivalent to `zeta(2s)/zeta(s)`. Finite-alphabet freedom therefore cannot alter the zero obstruction. A distinct route must use conditional infinite-alphabet first-order structure or another global mechanism.

Nyman--Beurling has a sharp population/phase resolution boundary. A proportional logarithmic source shell narrows the carrier enough to reach the exact Ford denominator. NB-225 shows that every sublinear shell admits a phase-aligned matched zero packet compatible with all population bounds currently used. NB-226 strengthens the no-go: for every fixed `0<vartheta<1`, the packet can satisfy `O(m^vartheta)` phase cancellation on every consecutive vertical subpacket and still contribute order one after horizontal damping is tuned to compensate that bias. Below linear width, the missing resource is not generic random-phase cancellation but a **joint depth-phase or weighted-residue estimate**.

Visual Exploration has now crossed the dense-spectrum representation wall constructively. VIS-312 showed that pointwise nearest-neighbour spacing has no cutoff-free meaning on the full dense ratio spectrum. VIS-313 replaces it by a fixed near/far ratio split and dyadic arithmetic-height shells. Within each near shell the reduced ratios remain separated, and Wang's taper makes the shell energies summable; this gives a cutoff-free sufficient horizon `V >> x log^3 x/(log log x)^2` at `H~x log log x`. The previous polynomial finite-window loss was a representation artifact. The remaining gap to the natural local scale is logarithmic and quantifier-level.

Möbius Cancellation sharpens the source-image warning quantitatively. MC-391 shows that an `O(H/R)` exceptional theorem at `o(R^(-1/2))` scale requires linearly growing normalized weighted genuine four-character collision mass. MC-392 then prices the entire fourth-moment resource by source codimension `c=dim W^perp`: `E|g|^4 <= (2c+3)(E|g|^2)^2`, so a fixed `K H/R` exception budget forces `c=Omega(R)`. Additive collisions are not a free combinatorial resource; enough independent source relations must exist first.

Robin Extremal has localized the real single-center representation boundary. The global separated-prediction floor is one, while compact interior positive-real single-center families pay strictly more than Wiener rate two. RE-223 rules out collapsing finite corridors, and RE-224 shows from the exact normalized polynomial that a bounded-corridor predictor cannot send its real center to infinity or its arc to `pi/2`. Any putative rate-two escape in that architecture is now forced into `alpha->0` or the contraction boundary `r->1`.

Weil Positivity adds an exact positive-completion obstruction. The critical prime-power coefficients are the nonzero Fourier coefficients of a centered Poisson kernel. The centered carrier is indefinite; the smallest scalar Toeplitz completion adds `2/(sqrt(p)+1)` on the diagonal at prime `p`, and the required `log p`-weighted diagonal tax diverges globally. Local positive completion is therefore not a harmless normalization. A viable operator route must absorb or quotient that direction inside one global finite--archimedean/cross-prime sign structure before positivity is asserted.

The shared question is: **what genuinely new source coordinate is acquired, who selects it, and what happens when its exact inverse, ordered scale evolution, covariance/similarity law, spectral nullspace or minimal positive completion is computed before crediting it with leverage?** A coordinate that is freely tuned rather than source-selected, faithful but control-similar, signed but fixed-family destination-complete, locally positive only after a divergent diagonal repair, or dense-spectrum only through a cutoff-dependent gap functional has changed representation without yet changing the theorem interface.

## Where does the obstruction move after a nominal critical boundary is resolved?

Arithmetic Fidelity now places provenance before the shrinking-target hierarchy. The critical displaced branch can miss an integer target at second order, and above criticality the first `R_n/n` saddle window is automatically hit on rational-density source progressions. AF-432 classifies the next `R_n/n^2` packet-corrected target as dense `G_delta` but Lebesgue-null. AF-433 shows that the amplitude being classified is still a free probe coordinate. The next exact obstruction is therefore not pointwise membership yet; it is constructing or excluding a source-intrinsic amplitude selector.

Farey Discrepancy has moved past fixed filter design. Common sublogarithmic depth remains destination-complete and fixed finite polynomial families are either annularly blind or destination-complete. FD-212 shows that periodic scale switching creates a third sparse-ray regime with a physical supercritical Floquet multiplier at bounded depth. The next boundary is whether all-integer cross-ray coherence kills this nonautonomous mode.

Visual Exploration has moved from localization to spacing, from spacing to dense-spectrum aggregation, and now from polynomial to logarithmic finite-window cost. VIS-313 shows that dyadic shell aggregation removes the cutoff and lowers the sufficient horizon to near-linear scale. The next wall is the residual `log^3 x/(log log x)^3` factor relative to `V~x log log x`, together with prescribed-height/joint-limit control.

Nyman--Beurling has crossed the exact-Ford population wall only by paying linear logarithmic source width. NB-225 shows that population information cannot reach exact Ford with any sublinear width; NB-226 shows that even square-root or any other fixed-power raw phase cancellation can be neutralized by moving the packet horizontally. The next boundary is a depth-sensitive phase theorem or direct weighted-residue cancellation, not another unweighted phase exponent.

Robin Extremal has collapsed the positive-real fixed-corridor single-center escape to two singular geometries. Interior drift pays a strict `2+eta_K` Wiener exponent, narrow corridors have an independent superoscillatory surcharge, and exact local flatness excludes unbounded centers. A putative rate-two route must now approach `alpha->0` or `r->1`, or leave the representation entirely.

Möbius Cancellation moves beyond quartic collision counting to a relation-budget obstruction. MC-392 says that sparse source-frame exceptions at the desired scale require linear quotient codimension. The next object is whether the actual arithmetic source architecture can supply `Omega(R)` independent relations without violating conductor/source-cost constraints; if it cannot, the endpoint route closes before finer additive-energy optimization.

Weil Positivity moves the non-diagonal escape from “couple translates” to “assemble the compensating diagonal globally before the sign theorem.” Independent scalar prime-ray completion is closed by a divergent zero-mode tax; genuinely cross-prime, matrix-valued or finite--archimedean assembly remains open.

Across these lines, a resolved threshold should be followed by identifying the **next exact provenance gate, source mesh, inverse-coherence class, ordered scale monodromy, joint depth-phase resource, aggregate spectral density, independent relation budget, singular approximation regime or positive-completion counterterm** that becomes load-bearing. Continuing to optimize the previous coarse boundary usually attacks a variable already proved nondecisive.

## Which physical cost survives source specialization and analytic transport?

[MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md), [MI-025](intuition/MI-025-combinatorial-breadth-must-be-priced-through-the-source-coordinate-map.md) and [MI-017](intuition/MI-017-sparse-exceptional-geometry-survives-upper-population-control.md) remain the main source-cost syntheses.

Only after the source selects a parameter does it make sense to compare its mesh to `R_n/n` or `R_n/n^2` target apertures. Category, measure and pointwise membership are downstream currencies, not substitutes for source selection.

Nyman--Beurling prices localization by source width and then prices phase by depth. A proportional logarithmic shell buys a `1/X` vertical carrier and exact-Ford population control. Under sublinear width, fixed-power raw phase cancellation is not an independent saving because the matched packet can move to depth `D` where `e^(-YD)` exactly pays the remaining phase bias. The new currency is a joint weighted depth-phase estimate.

Robin Extremal prices spectral support width and parameter degeneration separately. For support in `[H,RH]`, the exact superoscillatory lower exponent `B(R)` diverges as the corridor collapses. Within a bounded corridor, exact single-center prediction also forces the real center below the truncation scale, leaving only vanishing-arc or contraction-boundary degeneration at the rate-two frontier.

The remaining cost is logarithmic rather than polynomial; infinite-tail transport must be charged using aggregate scale-local density, not a fictitious global isolation radius.

Weil Positivity gives a completion cost rather than an inverse cost. The desired off-diagonal prime-power coefficients can sit inside a positive Poisson kernel only after adding a diagonal direction whose optimal total prime-weighted mass diverges. Any global positivity claim must explain where that diagonal goes before closure.

The shared audit is to price a route **after exact source specialization and before the final closure**, keeping source provenance, source mesh, pointwise membership, fixed versus switched inverse coherence, source width, horizontal depth, raw versus weighted phase cancellation, support corridor, singular parameter geometry, relation codimension, additive collision energy, dense-spectrum shell density, diagonal completion mass and destination normalization distinct until a theorem proves a dependency.

## Can sign-indefinite structure preserve critical information before closure or norm compression scalarizes it?

Farey Discrepancy now gives three regimes. A fixed common annular root preserves a genuine blind mode; removing all common annular roots makes a fixed family square-root complete; periodic diagonal switching can nevertheless produce a supercritical Floquet mode from individually harmless filters on a sparse ray. Sign and scale dependence are useful only insofar as the ordered source inverse fails, and the next test is whether full cross-ray coherence restores completeness.

Visual Exploration shows that exact support and complete same-base multiplicativity still cancel under cross-prime Haar phases, while the actual deterministic orbit has the same long-time quadratic mean. The dense infinite ratio spectrum does not itself destroy finite-height cancellation: aggregate dyadic-shell separation recovers a near-linear mean-square horizon. The unresolved signed object is now the remaining logarithmic finite-height cost and exceptional-height coherence.

Nyman--Beurling isolates why “phase cancellation” must be weighted correctly. A fixed-power saving in every raw vertical phase block can coexist with an order-one residue once horizontal depth is tuned. Useful sign information must therefore survive the carrier weight, not merely shrink an unweighted phase sum.

Weil Positivity makes the closure loss literal. The Weil-shaped prime-power carrier is the centered, sign-indefinite part of the positive Poisson kernel. Restoring local positivity adds a divergent diagonal. A useful sign theorem must therefore couple the compensating sector before positivity, not square/complete locally and subtract afterwards.

Möbius Cancellation gives the finite-group counterpart. Four-wise-independent/Sidon-like source signs force critical-scale anti-concentration. Useful cancellation needs many exact quartic relations, but MC-392 shows that such relations can only concentrate enough mass if the source quotient has linearly many independent constraints. Random-looking sign structure is not itself the source of the needed sign.

These examples sharpen one interface: useful sign or positivity must act while the source-specific relation is present, survive the exact physical inverse or completion, and depend on information whose pointwise/phase/additive/ordered-scale structure is not already equivalent to the desired conclusion.
