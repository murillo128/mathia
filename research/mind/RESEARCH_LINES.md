## What counts as a genuine representation escape?

The adversarial pattern is now stricter than “changing representation may preserve information.” Prime Circle shows that even ordered higher-dimensional signature packets can be deterministic functionals of already decoded profiles. Nyman--Beurling now goes further than exact sample preservation: the exact Dirichlet sample table, its semigroup algebra and the complete canonical scalar pivot profile can all coexist with arbitrary finite-section lethargy, leaving only the vector/operator dilation geometry as a possible source-specific carrier. Visual Exploration shows how successive presentation gauges must be quotiented exactly and then exposes both an isotropic source-free baseline and a universal self-normalized spectral floor. Arithmetic Fidelity shows the complementary lift failure: restoring an entire infinite-dimensional quotient fibre can itself destroy raw compactness even when the quotient observable is well behaved. Weil Positivity shows that an invariant can survive the representation quotient yet still fail because the canonical source has no nontrivial carrier for it. Prime Lattice continues to show the separate failure in which the desired information is obtainable only from hypotheses already as strong as the target.

The durable proof order is therefore: determine the actual source image and exact nuisance/gauge quotient; audit which states or fibres become admissible after that quotient; avoid reintroducing discarded infinite-dimensional nuisance through an uncontrolled lift; locate the carrier that survives continuation, aggregation, de-aliasing, neutralization or completion; quotient any residual presentation scale and remove universal baselines; fix any metric or interaction law that changes the destination statistic; test whether the surviving datum is genuinely present in the canonical source and absent from matched controls; distinguish information loss from theorem-hypothesis strength; and only then price amplitude, density, conditioning, phase coherence, matrix tomography, spectral margin, commutator occupation or tangent asymmetry. A substantive escape needs a source-specific coupling or invariant that survives every destination reduction **and** is established from premises weaker than the conclusion it is meant to prove.
