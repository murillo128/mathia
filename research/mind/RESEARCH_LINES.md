## Which physical cost survives after representation artifacts are removed?

The ledger is source-relative. Arithmetic Fidelity prices target geometry against the actual mixture secants, not nominal embedding dimension. Farey prices observability at a topology fine enough not to quotient out the source-forced correction. Möbius prices independent translation bandwidth rather than channel count. Nyman prices the carrier interval the source can legally generate, not an artificially enlarged Fourier window. Prime Circle prices mechanism changes that escape the canonical half-density weighted-isometry control, not branch phase by itself. Weil Positivity prices genuinely nonlocal or delayed coupling after local positive selectors have collapsed to the Mangoldt ray. Weil Inertia prices coercive operator mass on a positive-density negative subspace rather than scalar reserve or nominal source dimension.

The reusable question is: after the source class, coupled limits, support, quotient/topology, representation choice, locality/operator category and matched controls are made exact, what quantitative object remains that the physical arithmetic source must pay for rather than coordinates, generic capacity, a canonical measure change or a repackaging of a classical carrier?
