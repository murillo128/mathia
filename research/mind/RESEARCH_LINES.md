# Global mathematical questions

This file records mathematical questions that only become visible by comparing several current Mathia research lines. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does a quantitative budget become a genuine source discriminator rather than a repackaged observable?

Across the current lines, the useful budget is increasingly the one induced by the **exact quotient or discriminator that the destination actually consumes**. Coarse support, ambient norm, matrix rank, compactness or selector sparsity can all be large while the destination-visible source channel is unchanged or fully reproducible by a matched control.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the topology issue explicit. AF-542 shows that a value-level accurate carrier-to-curvature approximation can fail catastrophically after the shrinking transverse contrast is applied. AF-543 then constructs an exact curvature-native inverse using the additional reference coordinate `r(t)`, and AF-544 proves that the calibration precision is itself part of the theorem: the prime-zeta finite-part constant is negligible in the leading asymptotic of `r(t)` but load-bearing in the downstream seminorm. Common calibration error and differential calibration error also live on different scales. The reusable lesson is that representation fidelity must be measured **after** the final discriminator, with every auxiliary reference coordinate charged as source information.

Möbius Cancellation reaches the analogous minimal quotient in a different geometry. MC-505 sees the varying start/profile table only through a matched Fourier diagonal; MC-506 identifies that diagonal as the Fourier transform of the one-dimensional physical pushforward `t=x+j`. The exact quadratic bias is therefore controlled by the excess-collision law of that conductor argument. MC-507 shows why broad start/profile marginals, support, singular values and even centered matrix norms are not source discriminators: tables with those summaries fixed can have either uniform or maximally concentrated anti-diagonal law. The next theorem must constrain the physical **coupling relative to the observed sum map**, not the coordinates separately.

Farey Discrepancy shows both the value and the danger of downstream spectral compression. FD-305--FD-307 reduce a near-capacity block-Mertens anomaly first to additive spectrum and then, using the original sliding/Fejer structure, to a sharply low-frequency fixed-block witness. FD-308 performs the necessary threshold-dominance audit: the later FD-307 principal-major-arc and principal-arc-kill thresholds lie entirely above the earlier unconditional source-energy ceiling `lambda<=1/(2c)=1.204710...`, so those deep exclusions are mathematically correct but vacuous for the live capacity region. The nonvacuous spectral output is the low-pass witness on `3/(10c)<lambda<=1/(2c)`, especially near `lambda=1`, where the forced coefficient is still only about `X^(0.65--0.67)` and current general `X^(4/5)` minor-arc control is too weak by a fixed power. A useful source compression must therefore be audited against all earlier feasibility ceilings before a later threshold is treated as progress.

Visual Exploration supplies the selector analogue. VIS-419--VIS-424 identify the exact base-prime Haar self-null, finite-window character tariff and fixed-moment normalized-amplitude tariff for Wang shells at Gram points. VIS-425 shows that fixed-radius persistence is not an independent nonlinear escape in the uniformly band-limited regime: Bernstein control reduces the path supremum to a fixed mesh, so the same mixed-character tariffs apply. VIS-426 separately shows that an integrated Fisher budget controls average radial representative motion and an exceptional set, not a uniform modulus. The remaining source resource is therefore a quantified tariff failure, non-Haar selector, growing path complexity, boundary concentration, or a destination theorem that can consume average/exceptional-set control; visual persistence by itself is not evidence.

Prime Lattice provides the same discipline through symmetry rather than compression. PL-437--PL-440 reduce the unresolved mod-5 defect to the exact Schur coordinates and then separate a symmetry-forbidden stationary mode from one quadratic-character lag channel plus an anisotropic remainder. The useful budget is not “more covariance” but the variance-scale control of those exact destination-visible modes.

The global question is: **for each proposed representation, what is the smallest source-derived object that the destination actually sees, and what quantitative norm/seminorm on that object is stable under the real compression?** A budget becomes evidence only when it controls that object independently of matched controls, survives the downstream discriminator at the required scale, and remains nonvacuous after all upstream feasibility gates are imposed.

## Which source dependence survives scalarization, equilibrium and canonicality gates?

Prime Circle, Weil Inertia and Weil Positivity now expose a common danger from opposite directions: adding sophisticated operator structure can either be too universal, too rigid, or merely repackage information already present in a scalar source symbol.

Prime Circle PC-419--PC-420 show that the norm-closed bounded-radial/discrete-shift descendants contain no nonzero compacts, while the opposite strong/weak closure becomes all of `B(H)`. PC-421 tests the natural continuous-scale escape: the Hilbert commutator of the exact cyclotomic log-radius symbol is genuinely compact and Hilbert--Schmidt, but it is a standard Hankel repackaging of the one-shell Mellin transform for **every** vertical line `Re(s)=sigma>0`. Dilation changes the Fourier-zero ordinate scale while leaving the compact spectrum unchanged. Thus compactness alone is not arithmetic spectralization; a useful operator must introduce source-forced interaction before or across Mellin scalarization.

Weil Inertia has the parallel “exact representation is not independent control” obstruction. WI-433--WI-435 show that perfect horizontal transport and vanishing edge/safe-line errors can leave the interior Hardy defect field untouched, because the field simply becomes the transported residue signal. WI-436 then closes the most canonical mixed-frequency repair: the opposite-Hardy bilinear pairing of the interior logarithmic derivative is exactly the positive boundary Blaschke defect energy. It is highly coercive, but the finite Kunik block itself saturates it. A surviving mixed-frequency theorem must therefore add a non-translation-invariant, nonlinear or explicit-formula source constraint that the finite inner factor cannot manufacture from its own divisor.

Weil Positivity sharpens canonicality into a rigidity-versus-programmability boundary. WP-428 says Bohr-diagonal positive sandwiches are too rigid and annihilate the nonzero source modes. WP-429 says unrestricted coherent sandwiches are too flexible: on every finite faithful Gibbs block they realize arbitrary positive functionals and can orient a zero-mode-free defect with either sign while the dynamics and KMS state stay fixed. WP-430 says thermodynamic canonicity alone swings back to the rigid side: invariance under the stabilizer of the Hamiltonian forces a selector into `C*(H)`, hence zero Bohr degree. The only surviving category is **extra arithmetic source structure that canonically selects cross-frequency coherence before the Weil sign is inspected**.

These three lines suggest one shared gate. Non-diagonal or mixed-frequency structure is relevant only when its coupling law is fixed by the arithmetic source **before scalarization or target inspection**, and when matched generic/finite controls cannot reproduce it. “Compact”, “coherent”, “noncommuting” and “mixed-frequency” are categories, not evidence. The unresolved mathematics is a source-canonicity theorem for a specific coupling, together with a destination estimate that uses that coupling without re-importing the target divisor or desired sign.

## Where do apparent arithmetic thresholds disappear once the exact source geometry is retained?

Nyman--Beurling provides the clearest current example. NB-322--NB-325 showed that the `1/sqrt(log n)` rank-one-error resonance persists across the entire exact-divisor skeleton, independent of denominator size and parity. NB-327 removes divisibility as the sustaining mechanism: every integer-multiple ray `m=d(n-1)` with `d=o(n)` retains the same asymptotic adjacent matrix coefficient. NB-328 then moves a full selector cell to the neighboring ray `m=dn` and finds the **same magnitude with opposite sign**. The true unresolved geometry is the intermediate phase `m=d(n-1)+r`, `0<r<d`: if the adjacent channel crosses zero there, one must still determine whether that is robust operator mixing or only sign cancellation in one witness. Denominator size, parity, divisibility and even a complete cell of off-lattice drift are not intrinsic thresholds.

Farey Discrepancy gives the complementary warning. FD-307 appeared to create meaningful principal-major-arc depth thresholds, but FD-308 shows that the upstream finite-support energy ceiling already excludes every depth where those thresholds activate. What survives is not the impressive-looking deep contradiction but the lower-depth Fejer certificate that overlaps the feasible source range. A threshold becomes programmatically meaningful only when its hypotheses intersect the parameter region left open by earlier source constraints.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives a third version. AF-544 finds a precision threshold rather than a scale threshold: the leading reference asymptotic is insufficient, while adding the finite-part constant crosses the downstream calibration barrier. Here the threshold is genuine because it is measured in the destination-induced seminorm, not because the omitted term looks small or large in the source expansion itself.

The reusable question is therefore: **is a proposed transition intrinsic to the source/destination coupling, or only to the proof coordinate currently being used?** Stress-test every threshold by enlarging the exact arithmetic skeleton, checking neighboring source regimes, comparing against upstream feasibility ceilings, changing representation without losing the source relation, and measuring error only after the final discriminator. If the transition disappears under those controls, it should not be promoted to a research principle; if it survives them, identify the exact source quantity that enforces it.
