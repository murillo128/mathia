# Global mathematical questions

## Which source relation cannot be repaired by moving the cost elsewhere?

[MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md) now separates four different failure modes of nominally rich source interfaces.

Farey Discrepancy distinguishes **relation coercivity from observability of that relation**. FD-181 proves that every squarefree-supported perturbation of Möbius with changed-coordinate support `o(x/log x)` pays prime-extension multiplicativity energy at scale `x/log x`. FD-182 then composes that coercive theorem with the permanent FD-179 dyadic-null source: one fixed non-Möbius source matches the complete dyadic midpoint ladder exactly while retaining

`M_(a,q)(x)=(C+o(1)) x/log x`

with `C>0`. The sparse relation is expensive, but those observations do not expose it below the critical scale. A source-native bridge must therefore enrich the Farey observation family or restrict the source class; sharpening an inequality on the same exact-null midpoint data cannot work.

Nyman--Beurling shows how a locality cost can be absent at fixed resolution yet explode in a coupled limit. NB-190 prices a normalized Euler-band probe by inverse source mass in scalar-translate variation. NB-191 shows that at fixed band width this cost can eventually be transplanted into every high dyadic block by common Bohr recurrence without an additional asymptotic surcharge. NB-192 reveals the hidden nonuniformity: one fixed log-width band contains `m_a~a exp(r_0/a)` rationally independent prime-log phases, so any fixed-accuracy **syndetic** common-return inclusion length satisfies `log L_a >> a exp(r_0/a)`. Guaranteed recurrent transport into a length-`T` block therefore requires a log-log-scale lower bound on `a`. A specific block may still contain an unusually early return, and aggregate weighted recurrence may avoid coordinatewise phase recurrence; those are now the live acquisition questions.

Robin Extremal supplies a stable-rank analogue. High factorial-normalized Euler jets approach the common net-multiplicity mode; an `o(1)` spectral neighborhood of `s=1` becomes rank one on a fixed selector shell; and RE-191 now shows that even a fixed constant-distance interval reduces to a compact Laplace kernel with factorial rank approximation. Exact continuum data can remain analytically source-rigid while the number of robust source coordinates at fixed tolerance stays bounded independently of shell size. A scalar escape needs growing spectral span, growing logarithmic source diameter or source-native precision fine enough to resolve the factorial tail, not merely denser exact sampling.

Möbius Cancellation shows that source information can move inside a statistical architecture without becoming free. MC-356 prices finite-dimensional regular exponential-family channels by integrated Fisher action. MC-357 closes the immediate non-exponential mixture loophole: if source dependence enters only through a latent mixing law followed by a source-independent component kernel, half-Jeffreys divergence contracts from the latent law to the transcript, and exact neighboring transcript singularity forces singular latent laws. Complex fixed components therefore only relocate the tariff into the selector. A genuine escape must make the components themselves source-dependent, change support/singularity structure, adapt the architecture or expose another source-dependent transcript path.

The common discriminator is not raw dimension, exactness or the mere existence of a source tariff. It is a **destination-relevant quantitative resource together with an observation/acquisition theorem that actually exposes it under the declared source class and coupled scaling**.

## Which physical source scale does the theorem actually control?

Arithmetic Fidelity has two independent scale ledgers. On the sampling side, uniform short-interval PNT gives all-prime-start conversion for `h_N=p_N^(-beta)` throughout `beta<13/30`, while exceptional-set inertia plus Brun--Titchmarsh packing transfers a stronger almost-all ambient theorem to relative density one of prime starts through every `beta<13/15`. Those thresholds measure theorem uniformity and sampling geometry, not an intrinsic detector constant.

On the finite-order analytic side, AF-403--AF-405 turn strict translation positivity into a recursive confluent/Toda hierarchy. The full Euler-log profile now has `H_1,H_2,H_3,H_4>0` globally, hence strict total positivity through order four. AF-217 still forces eventual failure, so the first possible bad order is at least five; the next local gate is the single curvature inequality `(log R_4)''<4q`. This closes another finite-order certificate without supplying a uniform determinant margin or rational-prime selectivity.

Visual Exploration adds a normalization audit to physical-scale bookkeeping. VIS-272 gives the exact Green-potential dictionary, VIS-274 carries the concentrating main term through the fixed-source `1/log T` pullback, and VIS-275 shows why a sign-blind absolute remainder cannot benefit from extra signed packet moments. VIS-276 isolated the signed remainder mean `M_T(x)`, but VIS-277 evaluates it as

`M_T(x)=-log(2*pi)+O_K(H^-1+T^-1)`.

The apparent residual is therefore partly a classical gamma-factor convention. Recentring can make that particular remainder mean vanish only by moving the same constant into the explicit term. The next useful object is the first **normalization-invariant signed correction of the combined source** after the `log(t/(2*pi))` baseline, not a decomposition-dependent remainder coordinate.

RE-190--RE-191 give the dual observation-window version of the same discipline: shrinking windows collapse to rank one and fixed windows remain factorially compressible on a fixed multiplicative source shell. The theorem must therefore control the actual physical source window, the observation window must resolve the claimed source degrees of freedom at the available precision, and nuisance normalization must be fixed before a residual scale is interpreted.

## Which quantitative resource survives a thin or moving destination?

Prime Lattice has moved beyond both the local activation-birth gate and the positive-tail dilution gate. PL-338 shows that every finite prime-power activation is first-order flat and is born with favorable reflected sign whenever the transported boundary trace is nonzero. PL-339 computes the exact path-chain gap of each compressed shift and shows that the outer logarithmic half-window carries asymptotically all weighted von-Mangoldt mass with unit gap. Consequently

`J_a >= (1-o(1)) W_a I`.

Accumulating active channels therefore does not soften the favorable positive jump bulk relative to total source mass. But the exact prime block remains `P_a=J_a-2W_aI`; the negative scalar compensation survives at the same scale. The frontier is now global branch persistence/noncollapse and a rational-prime-specific control of compensation/completion or another mesoscopic source law beyond the PNT-universal bulk.

Nyman--Beurling sharpens what “locality” must mean in a moving destination. The fixed-`a` recurrence theorem is qualitatively enough to reach arbitrary height, but NB-192 proves that its worst-gap inclusion length is double-exponential in the natural resolution parameter `1/a`. Thus locality becomes a real resource only when the recurrence/acquisition scale is quantitatively coupled to the physical destination. A more efficient weighted or block-specific recurrence mechanism remains possible; the generic syndetic route cannot simply be extrapolated into the shrinking-width regime.

Xi Flow remains a useful control in the opposite direction: once a consecutive packet is localized, its local holomorphic inversion is well conditioned. Its unresolved cost is global support discovery and assembly rather than local packet inversion. Across these lines, the moving destination is governed by the resource that scales with localization, recurrence, support discovery or branch transport—not by the mere existence of a local inverse or positive bulk.

## Can reverse discrimination, signed structure or positivity be made destination-selective?

Möbius Cancellation now prices a hierarchy of increasingly broad channel classes. MC-354 controls common additive log-smooth noise; MC-355 adds source-dependent Gaussian mean/covariance motion; MC-356 identifies integrated Fisher action for regular exponential families; MC-357 transfers the tariff through every fixed-component latent mixture. The remaining question is whether the arithmetic endpoint actually factors through one of these priced categories or requires a theorem for genuinely source-dependent components, singular/support-changing selectors, adaptive constructions or side channels.

Weil Inertia exposes a different information loss. Below `theta<3/16`, all-start control removes the source-location obstruction, yet WI-332--WI-334 show that `U^2`, square-root phase cancellation and even any fixed finite hierarchy of power-small local Gowers norms can coexist with full diagonal-scale sliding variance. The missing theorem must retain genuinely two-point signed covariance or perform an equivalent source-fixed subtraction before the proof destroys the required off-diagonal sign.

[MI-026](intuition/MI-026-sign-destructive-proof-interfaces-cannot-certify-destination-cancellation.md) now adds a normalization requirement to that rule. VIS-277 shows that even a signed pre-majorization coordinate can be misleading if it changes under harmless redistribution between explicit and remainder terms. A useful intermediate variable must preserve the destination-sensitive sign **and** survive the natural gauge/normalization freedom of the representation.

Weil Positivity closes another scalar escape. WP-352 isolates the reciprocal exponential phase gauge and shows that projective Berry/Chern geometry is blind to the common scalar zeta factor. WP-353 proves that, under order-at-most-one growth, this exponential family is the complete zero-free reciprocal/unitary scalar gauge. Standard arithmetic cusp normalization can remove its one real parameter—but after that normalization the scalar meromorphic normalizer is determined by its divisor plus normalization. Scalar phase recovery has therefore collapsed to divisor reconstruction rather than an independently generated positivity mechanism. A surviving Weil route must introduce a noncentral/global coupling or a larger independently positive object **before scalar reconstruction**.

Prime Circle now closes a parallel compact-support escape. PC-338 extends the finite point-supported Mellin classification to every compactly supported distribution in Mellin frequency: semiprime-null scalar shell response is invisible off `s=1` and reduces to `c Lambda` on the endpoint line. Widening a finite selector to a compact interval, compact singular measure or arbitrary finite-order compact distribution therefore adds no new exact prime-power discriminator. A surviving radial route must use genuinely noncompact/global frequency structure, shell-dependent or nonlinear/matrix operations, or cross-shell coupling—and still supply an independent zero-sensitive destination theorem. Prime Flute continues to reinforce the complementary warning that local geometric/spectral richness is not enough when compactness or the final zero/sign bridge is missing.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, state the admissible source class, observation family and its nullspace, exact target quotient, physical source-coordinate map, dual observation window, relation graph, acquisition operator class and budget, conditioning, sampling regime, likelihood/covariance/sign norm, normalization gauge and matched-control class. Then ask what the destination actually distinguishes; whether the source relation being priced is visible to the observations; whether a cost has merely moved into a latent selector, source-dependent component or recurrence length; whether localization introduces a quantitatively new scale or is neutralized only at fixed resolution; whether dense exact sampling adds stable rank or only parallel/factorially compressed observations; whether the source theorem is valid at the induced moving scale; whether structured main terms have been normalized and rescaled before generic error bounds are applied; whether the proof has discarded sign/covariance needed later; whether a scalar phase is independently forced or merely reconstructed from a divisor; whether a compact-support enlargement genuinely changes the representation class; and whether the final survivor connects to a zero/sign theorem rather than only reconstructing arithmetic data.

The program advances when one resource becomes **destination-selective under the same source fibre, observation family, physical scale, acquisition class, conditioning, normalization and matched-control class used by the claimed RH mechanism**. Raw dimension, exact-null observations, deep scalar jets, dense samples, fixed-component mixture complexity, qualitative recurrence, fixed-order pseudorandomness, universal PNT bulk coercivity, normalization-dependent residuals, compact Mellin selectors and divisor-reconstructed scalar positivity remain intermediate resources until that matched-control test is passed.
