## Which physical cost survives after representation artifacts are removed?

The newest results reinforce that the surviving currency is usually weighted and post-quotient. Farey weighs exact defects by `kappa(D)`, with high-arity reciprocal antichain mass now a proved source of growth. Möbius separates the automorphism quotient from conditional source-type diversity before asking what survives projection. Nyman--Beurling weighs rank-coupled coverage against Fourier decay and carrier conditioning. Prime Circle now prices the entire scalar primitive mesh branch: supercritical source dependence is a coprimality sieve, critical source dependence is lower-order finite-Euler-product data, and subcritical leading behavior is universal. Prime Lattice reads a moving square-free exponent boundary rather than low-energy Ramanujan interiors. Visual Exploration quotients the known moment kernel and keeps a uniform compact singular-value floor even under source-adaptive selection. Arithmetic Fidelity classifies the whole smooth radial repair by overlap-power weights. Weil Inertia subtracts the polar term paired with the critical prime density before crediting the residual arithmetic.

These are not one theorem and their weights are not numerically interchangeable. The common discipline is to remove coordinate artifacts, universal scaffolds, symmetry, known kernels, classical scalarizations and structurally paired leading terms first, then identify the quantity that still varies with the arithmetic source and reaches the destination. A resource should be credited only if that post-reduction quantity retains the required sign, phase, precision, stability or spectral effect.
