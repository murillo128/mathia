## Which source relation cannot be repaired by moving the cost elsewhere?

[MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md) is the current synthesis. Across Farey Discrepancy, Möbius Cancellation, Nyman--Beurling and Robin Extremal, the recurring issue is not raw source dimension but whether the declared physical observation exposes a destination-relevant resource with a stable inverse modulus.

Farey Discrepancy now has a support-free two-currency source theorem. FD-187 prices composite inconsistency on the physical-prime fibre by translated divisor-cube capacity; FD-188 prices movement of the prime labels themselves by coefficient amplitude mass `D_q`. Together, `D_(a,q)(x)=o(x)` and `M_(a,q)(x)=o(x/log x)` force `a=mu`. Yet FD-182 still gives an exact dyadic-midpoint null source with critical relation energy. Source rigidity is therefore no longer the missing theorem: the open bridge is a Farey observation that quantitatively exposes enough of the coefficient-fidelity and relation-energy currencies.

Möbius Cancellation now separates generic destination tariffs from source-specific acquisition costs at two sketch levels. MC-365 constructs a coordinatewise BSC that attains the information, JS/Hellinger and Bhattacharyya-depth endpoint tariffs, so those lower bounds alone are admission conditions. MC-366 prices adaptive coordinate queries by direct own-phase revealment. MC-367 extends the same provenance theorem to adaptive parity queries: apart from the exact puncture correction, predictability of `X_i` is acquired only when the query span contains the target direction `e_i`. For a common rank-`q` parity transcript, average squared predictability is at most `q/R` plus the exponentially small puncture term, so unit-energy exact all-source dephasing requires full rank. The next arithmetic question is whether an actual Möbius transcript has subcritical coordinate/span revealment, or what analogous provenance tariff governs genuinely nonlinear joint measurements.

Nyman--Beurling reaches the endpoint in two source currencies. NB-198--NB-199 use positive interval occupancy to show that `o(a)` returns cannot occur below the logarithmic layer `x_a/log x_a`; NB-200 takes over when favorable phase windows become sub-unit and prices the source by atomic carrying capacity. The one-sided threshold `liminf |t| log(x_a)/x_a >= 2 pi e^Delta` is a capacity obstruction, not a return-existence theorem.

Robin Extremal now supplies both a near-kernel and a capacity counterpoint. RE-199 keeps the exact linear repair radius and proves exponential near-nullity on the full positive real Euler ray throughout every fixed wedge `K/log p<1/4`. RE-200 shows that this compression is not intrinsic to the Euler source: on one fixed prime shell, vertical complex sampling realizes a weighted DFT with `K` uniformly conditioned directions using height `O(K)`, and `K~c log Q` is admissible. The source information currency therefore depends on the analytic sampling geometry. The live question is whether honest matched controls can simultaneously defeat that vertical frame at Robin-scale precision; stable Fourier dimension by itself is not destination leverage.

## Which physical source scale, analytic domain and representation gauge does the theorem actually control?

Arithmetic Fidelity still requires a strict separation between theorem uniformity and intrinsic detector geometry. AF-408 proved eventual order-five positivity in both Euler-log tails. AF-409 now makes the right side explicit: `A_4(t)>4e^t-3>0` for `e^t>=50`, so the right semi-infinite interval is no longer part of the effective gap. The remaining quantitative tail bridge is left-sided, while the finite compact interaction problem remains separate. Coefficient-sequence gauge sensitivity and continuation-visible zeros still cannot be transferred automatically to finite-order translation positivity.

Prime Circle makes the analytic-domain part of the audit exact in two complementary ways. PC-341 shows that the prime-log zero set is a uniqueness set for bounded-characteristic functions exactly at strip half-width `a>=pi/2`; narrower strips admit bounded Blaschke survivors. PC-342 shows that full-plane analyticity alone is not stronger in the needed sense: nonzero entire survivors with those prime-log zeros exist, but every such survivor must have infinite order. Thus domain width and growth class are separate currencies. “Analytic continuation exists” is too coarse; one must state both where the theorem controls the function and in what growth class.

Visual Exploration gives the parallel representation warning. VIS-278--VIS-283 remove the apparent generic `L^-3/2` frontier from the fixed-source direct/localization sectors and show that the expected equal-scale signed source cross is absent after exact specialization. Through constant field-energy order the surviving arithmetic profile is a positive diagonal `L^-2` object after retaining the gamma baseline. The next question is what the admissible packet/test projection does to that profile, not whether a discarded signed cross can be recovered by rhetoric.

Robin Extremal now sharpens the representation warning from the opposite side. RE-199 shows that a formally complete positive-real continuum can be exponentially ill-conditioned even at linear jet depth, while RE-200 shows that a vertical complex segment on the same prime shell can have linear stable dimension. Analytic “amount of data” is therefore not a source invariant; the direction and normalization of sampling are part of the claim.

## Which quantitative resource survives a thin or moving destination?

Prime Lattice has separated recurrence, centering, explicit-formula identity and completion. PL-341--PL-344 identify the sharp raw prime edges, their essential-spectrum recurrence, their exponential rarity in long-time density, and the bounded-modulation PNT profile that canonical pole completion cancels.

PL-345 now identifies the centered atomic-prime-minus-PNT residual itself as the classical first logarithmic Riesz mean. Its zero expansion weights a zero `rho` by `e^((2 Re rho-1)a)`, so the critical line is exactly the neutral square-root scale; boundedness at zero modulation is already equivalent to RH. This is a precise arithmetic dictionary but also a prior-art redirect. The prime-centered residual is not the full completed same-state observable: the exact pole term differs from the PNT continuum by `3a-1+o(1)`, larger than the bounded critical-line zero signal. The live problem is therefore the exact completed competition among the Riesz residual, pole/archimedean corrections and the unbounded principal energy.

Nyman--Beurling supplies the contrasting source-specific pointwise mechanism. Its shrinking-band program separates qualitative recurrence, target-set volume, block density and return-free scale, then changes source currency from interval occupancy to atomic capacity when the favorable cells become shorter than one lattice unit. [MI-027](intuition/MI-027-recurrence-existence-prevalence-first-hit-and-canonical-centering-are-different-currencies.md) remains the audit rule: match a recurrence theorem to the destination time quantifier, source granularity and the observable surviving canonical centering.

Xi Flow remains the opposite control: once a consecutive packet is localized, local holomorphic inversion is well conditioned, while support discovery/assembly is global. Across these moving-destination problems, the relevant currency is the quantity that scales with localization, source occupancy or atomic capacity, recurrence time, support discovery, centered residual size, branch transport or unbounded frequency cost—not merely existence of a local inverse or a bounded spectral edge.

## Can reverse discrimination, signed structure or positivity be made destination-selective?

Möbius Cancellation now gives a useful hierarchy. Generic discrimination tariffs can be paid by an abstract matched channel; direct-coordinate acquisition is priced by own-phase revealment; parity sketches are priced by target-direction span revealment. A useful arithmetic contradiction must therefore constrain the actual source provenance at the architecture the construction really uses, not merely sharpen another architecture-free divergence lower bound.

Weil Inertia reaches the analogous boundary for signed covariance. Ordinary and diagonal-deleted Gowers smallness can coexist with full bow variance in matched-energy controls, so more generic uniformity constraints do not force the required negative covariance. A surviving statistic must retain source-specific signed two-point structure rather than only remove universal diagonal energy.

Weil Positivity tests arithmetic insertion before positivity directly. WP-356 couples the normalized prime Hecke correspondence to the cusp cutoff before taking a norm, but on the constant cusp mode the commutator square is exactly a log-height annulus projection. The prime label survives as unsigned annulus size; the divisor/scattering data remain only in sign-indefinite interference, and the positive square loses the desired half-density. Source coupling must survive the positive closure, not merely occur before it.

Prime Circle adds a complementary analytic warning: full-plane continuation may retain the prime-log zero set while allowing infinite-order survivors, and even exact source distinguishability still needs a destination theorem. Robin Extremal adds the conditioning warning: high stable vertical source dimension does not itself imply Robin discrimination. In each case the next mechanism must be destination-selective under the same source and representation class, not merely richer.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the admissible source class, observation family and nullspace, destination quotient, physical source-coordinate map, acquisition budget, requested precision, normalization gauge, analytic domain/class, source granularity, recurrence/time quantifier, canonical centering, sign/covariance structure and matched control. Then ask two opposite-direction questions in the same currency: **what does the destination force, and what can the physical source mechanism actually supply?**

The newest findings sharpen five versions of that test. AF-409 shows that an asymptotic certificate becomes actionable only when its tail scale is made effective. MC-367 shows that direct-access provenance generalizes from coordinate incidence to linear-span incidence for parity sketches. PC-342 shows that enlarging an analytic domain can lose uniqueness unless growth control is retained. PL-345 shows that a beautifully explicit critical-line residual can already be a classical RH-equivalent criterion and still fail to equal the completed destination at the required scale. RE-200 shows that source capacity can jump from logarithmic compression to linear stable dimension when the analytic sampling direction changes. Progress requires the missing observation, acquisition, inverse-modulus, analytic-growth or completed-destination theorem in the exact representation where the target lives.
