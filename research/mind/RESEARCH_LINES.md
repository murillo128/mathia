## Which physical cost survives after representation artifacts are removed?

The newest results reinforce one common proof order without producing a universal numerical invariant. AF-480--AF-482 show that a sharp global conditioning law can be exact yet still provenance-generic. FD-255--FD-258 show that signed reachability, global extension, deep-band positive capacity and first-row arithmetic slot capacity are distinct layers: the latter can inherit the zeta zero frontier even before the later positivity mechanism becomes active. NB-269--NB-271 price concentration by the anchor-aligned inverse Gram rather than by pointwise residue. VIS-365 prices finite branching by exterior-volume subset energies rather than by tree complexity. WI-387 adds spectral tightness as the relevant compactness resource for adaptive fixed-width trials. WP-396 shows that even a genuinely noncentral source degree of freedom can disappear under the operation that creates positivity.

PL-414 supplies a complementary representation lesson. Once the source-realized `p=5` correction is pushed to actual prime pairs, finite local arithmetic does not create a new infinite-dimensional carrier: it becomes five rational-frequency variance sectors, and the quantitative power-saving requirement survives unchanged. This is the same audit principle behind MI-021 and MI-031 in a concrete form: changing representation matters only if it changes the surviving source-specific quotient, whereas a finite exact coordinate transform merely relocates the estimate that still has to be proved.

MI-033 records the nonlinear version in determinant routes. MC-439 reduces fixed-rank principal-minor wrappers to additive feature moments; MC-440--MC-441 push gap order into a subleading endpoint-adjacency scalar; MC-442 removes coefficient sign as an arithmetic carrier; MC-443 removes fixed-rank continuous normalized magnitude as a robust range discriminator. Prime Circle independently reduces determinant corrections to a thin contrast carrier whose internal mean-zero conditioning remains controlled through the singular endpoint.

The currencies remain line-specific. The durable audit is to quotient universal scaffolds first, restrict to the actually admissible image, identify the exact carrier that survives the destination operation, and then price its geometry, conditioning, scale, spectral compactness, measure class and provenance. A resource counts only after all later reductions preserve it.
