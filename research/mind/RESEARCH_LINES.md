# Global mathematical questions

## When does repeated observation actually shrink the admissible source fibre?

[MI-007](intuition/MI-007-survival-requires-quantitative-non-escape-not-just-nonzero-structure.md), [MI-008](intuition/MI-008-exact-recovery-is-not-stable-transport.md), [MI-012](intuition/MI-012-information-content-and-destination-sensitivity-are-separate-resources.md), [MI-019](intuition/MI-019-source-identification-and-zero-selection-are-orthogonal-gates.md), [MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md), [MI-022](intuition/MI-022-unbounded-compatibility-can-turn-finite-cutoff-ambiguity-into-a-stability-problem.md), and [MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md) separate retained information, common-source compatibility, conditioning and final destination selection.

Arithmetic Fidelity now closes one multiscale recognition boundary conditionally on the source class. AF-387 shows that one connected `PF_2` lattice can satisfy every solid order-three Toeplitz inequality while failing a gapped order-three minor because an interior lower-order zero blocks propagation. AF-388--AF-389 show that one smooth profile sampled on arbitrarily fine compatible lattices cannot sustain any finite-order version of that boundary defect: for `q=-(log f)''`, every zero must be infinitely flat. AF-390 then proves that if the admissible class has flat-jet unique continuation for `q` — in particular suitable quasianalytic Denjoy--Carleman classes — the residual fibre collapses and the shrinking solid certificate becomes exact for continuum `TN_3`. The unrestricted smooth question is now whether a genuinely non-quasianalytic infinitely-flat alias can survive.

Farey Discrepancy supplies the discrete analogue with an unexpectedly strong stability conclusion. FD-164--FD-165 leave dense orientation kernels at every finite cutoff and even across large finite horizon bundles. FD-166 shows that one permanent source matching complete fields on any unbounded horizon set is exactly Möbius, because each horizon reveals a consecutive coefficient prefix through `sqrt(H)`. FD-167 goes further: the complete-field inverse on that prefix has an `H`-independent `L^2` modulus `sqrt(30)`, and integer-valued sources snap exactly once the full-field error is below `1/sqrt(30)`. The finite kernels survive by escaping outward, not by becoming arbitrarily close in the complete-field norm. The live problem is partial observation and destination coupling.

Robin Extremal brackets source fidelity with two very different observation geometries. RE-173 shows that one exact Mertens boundary charge, ordinary-prime support, bounded integer multiplicity and arbitrarily strong fixed KV fidelity allow an infinite delete/duplicate tail with an order-one transient Robin excursion. RE-174 shows that any unbounded sequence of exact high-temperature Euler samples determines every bounded prime multiplicity, but the first defect at `P` appears only at scale `P^{-sigma}`. RE-175 inserts a useful intermediate regime: the Euler boundary jets at `s=1` are log-prime moment coordinates; matching the first `K+1` jets forces at least `K+1` source sign changes, and the first slope already detects the specific RE-173 delayed compensator at the Robin normalization `1/(sqrt(p) log p)`. This is destination-aligned detection rather than full source recovery, and those jets are not yet known to be controlled by the original arithmetic problem.

Together AF-390, FD-167 and RE-174--RE-175 sharpen the multiscale lesson: scale count is not the resource. **One latent object constrained coherently across an unbounded hierarchy can eliminate every fixed finite defect, but what happens next depends on the admissible source class and the inverse geometry of the chosen observation.** The residual may be an infinitely-flat noncompact stratum, a uniformly conditioned growing prefix, exponentially weak coefficient recovery, or a finite moment hierarchy that detects dangerous placement without identifying the whole source. Exact compatibility must therefore be followed by an explicit source-class, observation and conditioning theorem, not by a generic appeal to “more scales”.

The next problem is no longer whether a quadratic source carrier can exist, but whether one explicit frozen application weight has nonzero `C_2` and a faithful dictionary to the intended support-edge observable; moving-edge uniformity and four-level covariance remain separate.

Nyman--Beurling measures adaptive source complexity at the resolution actually visible to `zeta'/zeta`. NB-170--NB-173 separately price ordered motion, range entropy and exact stationary source rank/energy. NB-174 replaces brittle exact rank by `mathfrak L_T(epsilon)`, the cheapest stationary linear representation after allowing a capped-Poisson residual below source resolution, and proves that positive-density rescue requires large complexity after sub-resolution motion is quotiented out. NB-175 now supplies an invariant lower certificate for that currency: finitely many realized templates probed through one jointly contractive capped-transport `ell^2` budget produce a response matrix `A`, and its nuclear norm lower-bounds `mathfrak L_T(epsilon)` after the exact residual penalty. Near-orthogonal source-visible responses therefore force linear approximate complexity independently of the sampler's syntactic parametrization. The live task is to instantiate such witnesses for natural nonlinear adaptive families, or quantify the gap between this one-sided certificate and the true approximate complexity.

The bottleneck is global support discovery, multiscale assembly, source-valid complex access and the bridge to a zero-sensitive Xi quantity, not local inversion itself.

## How much source information can an analytic architecture exploit at bounded conditioning?

Möbius Cancellation now has a four-stage invariant budget. MC-338 turns own-phase blindness into a projection/energy inequality; MC-339--MC-340 replace coordinate restrictions by HGR/mutual information and show that bounded-energy nontrivial dephasing requires `Omega(R)` **joint** source information. MC-341 prices the analog-coordinate loophole once a source-natural Euclidean resolution is supplied: encoding that information in `d` dimensions forces spread at least `exp(Theta(R/d))`. MC-342 then proves that bounded range, bounded forward Hamming-Lipschitz constant and every fixed positive-moment source-gradient energy still allow a scalar transcript to carry the entire source state; the price is exponentially small minimum separation. The missing invariant is therefore inverse distinguishability/noise/precision, not forward smoothness.

FD-167 provides the complementary positive example. The full Farey field has an explicit observation norm in which a growing square-root source prefix is recovered with a uniform inverse modulus. Taken together with MC-342, this yields a sharper audit: **source information becomes analytically useful only when the actual observation supplies a lower distinguishability or inverse-recovery bound in its native norm**. Benign forward geometry says almost nothing about analog capacity, while a good inverse modulus can make a large discrete source prefix robustly coercive.

NB-175 adds a dual version of the same principle. Once the source resolution itself has been fixed by the capped-Poisson norm, many simultaneously distinguishable responses can certify that no low-complexity stationary representation exists at that resolution. Farey, Möbius, Nyman--Beurling and Prime Circle therefore expose complementary versions of one accounting rule: count observations, coordinates, features, adaptive choices or spectral data only after identifying the exact source quotient, reconstruction closure, native source resolution and **inverse or dual distinguishability** that survives downstream.

## Which source fingerprint is selective rather than merely reconstructive?

Robin Extremal and Farey Discrepancy make source identification itself a controlled negative example. An unbounded exact observation hierarchy may identify the source completely while leaving the RH-facing estimate untouched; even when the full Farey inverse is uniformly stable, coefficient coercivity does not prove the Franel--Landau destination. Exact prime-multiplicity recovery through high-temperature Euler data is much worse conditioned and still does not control a Robin excursion at usable precision. RE-175 shows that full identification is not the only useful alternative: a finite boundary moment can detect a known dangerous compensation at the destination scale, while a hierarchy of such moments forces increasing source oscillation. The open question is whether that cheaper source fingerprint can be derived from genuine arithmetic input and made quantitatively selector-coercive, rather than merely imposed as an extra constraint.

Arithmetic Fidelity says the same from the analytic side: source-class rigidity can turn a recovered flat jet into exact `TN_3`, and Hamburger-type rigidity can identify `zeta`, but neither mechanism selects the nontrivial zeta zero divisor. Prime Circle similarly shows that source-sensitive autocorrelation, relative bound states, a universal singular conductor limit and even large canonical escaping outliers are not automatically zero selectors when smooth source backgrounds or matched controls reproduce them. The global program must therefore separate **source fingerprint**, **stable or destination-aligned transport of that fingerprint**, **background subtraction against matched controls**, and **destination-selective sign/zero theorem** at every stage.

## Global discriminator: identify the exact resource a matched control cannot fake

The current frontier is a classification of source-specific coupling that remains after every free closure, reconstruction, compatibility, quotient and matched-control operation. For each proposed route ask: what exact source distinction reaches the destination; what admissible source class is being compared; what information is regenerated for free; whether a finite-cutoff ambiguity survives only by escaping to infinity or into a flat boundary stratum; what native observation norm gives a useful inverse modulus; whether a cheaper destination-aligned moment detects the dangerous control without reconstructing the whole source; what sub-resolution motion the source cannot see; what joint source information the complete transcript contains and what inverse precision or dual witness certifies it; whether an operator preserves oriented phase or only an autocorrelation quotient; what universal singular or smooth low-frequency background must be subtracted before source dependence is credited; what spectral-density law its determinant is forced to obey; whether a transport metric derives the desired second moment or merely inserts it; and what theorem finally converts the survivor into a Riemann-zero or positivity restriction.

A useful bridge should fail on a matched control for an independently arithmetic reason. Exact reconstruction, unbounded observation count, source-specific labels, a critical boundary, a rich spectrum, a positive metric, a small formal error, benign forward regularity, a singular limit or an escaping eigenvalue are not enough unless the surviving quantity is both stably distinguishable and selectively coupled to the RH destination.
