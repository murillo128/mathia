## Characterize the maximal admissible quotient before choosing the repair

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-preserve-structure-before-compressing`.

Arithmetic Fidelity now gives an exact transversal formulation for the first gate. Given the observables naturally available to a construction, form their joint quotient and ask whether the desired discriminator is constant on its fibers. Only after that should one optimize, diagonalize, take determinants, add marks, or prove positivity. An arbitrary faithful mark is not a repair if it simply leaks the target; admissibility must be fixed by symmetry, locality, naturality, geometry, or operator structure first.

The cross-branch goal is to identify the coarsest admissible information layer through which each proposed RH distinction actually factors. A decisive negative result is a conflicting pair inside one maximal admissible fiber. A decisive positive result is an exact factorization or a canonically forced relational lift that removes precisely the intended ambiguity.

## Distinguish exact relational fidelity from global arithmetic specificity

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-the-surviving-arithmetic-variable-is-relational`.

Higher-order or marked relational data can be exactly faithful modulo an intended gauge: Arithmetic Fidelity supplies bispectral, annihilator-lattice, and matrix-valued spectral examples, while Prime Flute supplies directed multi-neck memory. But the all-composite flute clone and generalized-prime lattice controls show that a locally faithful relation can still be shared by non-Riemann arithmetic sources.

A decisive mechanism must pass both gates: prove the local relational observable is complete modulo the intended gauge, then show that an independently forced global norm/target/completion distinguishes the strongest system matching that local package. Local fidelity without source specificity is a coordinate theorem, not an RH selector.

## Make matched controls as strong as the claimed quotient, operator class, and pairing

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-arithmetic-significance-begins-after-universal-control-subtraction`.

Controls should match the exact information layer consumed by the proposed invariant. The current frontier includes an all-composite prime-flute clone in the **same compact-resolvent class**, a finite Hardy shell algebra whose Calkin image is only a wedge of universal Hilbert bands, localized Weil bands that collapse through large zero-free sampling ranges, realized Yang four-form controls after local-main centering, and positive-completion controls that erase finite selectors only at the destination pairing.

A canonical relative object is valuable only when its reference and topology are forced independently of the desired signal. The mathematical goal is to identify the first control depth at which an invariant changes and verify that this depth matches its claimed source of arithmetic information.

## Separate critical-axis generation from zero selection

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-critical-axis-is-not-zero-selection`.

Several branches independently generate distinguished analytic boundaries or spectrally real carriers, including Tate's self-dual `Re(s)=1/2`, Hilbert/Schatten thresholds, prime-torus measure boundaries, localized Weil spectral reality, PNT-controlled boundary limits, and universal cusp/propagation exponents. Some structures even recover exact finite Mangoldt/Weil coefficients.

The missing theorem is a **selector** whose sign, totality, rigidity, or defect elimination is independent of the already-known zero divisor. Re-deriving a self-dual axis, a convergence wall, finite-stage real spectrum, or correct Euler coefficients without such an independent mechanism does not advance the RH implication.

## Identify a canonical topology stronger than matched compact background but weaker than universal obstruction

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-005-topology-is-part-of-the-arithmetic-claim`.

The topology gate is now concrete. Prime Flute shows that compact-resolvent class is too coarse — the exact prime flute and an all-composite clone share it — while first-resolvent trace class is too strong locally. Prime Lattice shows that completion cancels the PNT bulk and that fixed smoothing, spatial repairs, and huge moving bands collapse before ultra-high essential recurrence appears. Prime Circle shows that scalar compact-resolvent covariance is structurally blocked and projective properization becomes classical lattice zeta. Weil Positivity shows parallel failures when positive completion passes to the wrong class or kills a torsion carrier.

The live program-level question is whether any branch forces a relative `S_p` (`p>1`), higher-resolvent, heat/wave/scattering, mesoscopic, distributional, or other topology in which a stable arithmetic residual survives the strongest matched controls and comes with an independent selector/sign theorem. A general no-go showing that every intrinsic middle category either identifies the matched controls or collapses to classical/essential behavior would be equally decisive.
