# Global mathematical questions

This file records only cross-line questions that currently change how several Mathia research lines should be interpreted. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than merely transport or refactor the source?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) and [MI-028](intuition/MI-028-complete-measurement-families-can-close-an-escape-by-reconstructing-the-classical-object.md) remain the common interface audit.

Prime Circle now strengthens the reconstruction-versus-quotient boundary. Complete shell data and unbounded Chen depth can recover the centered source, while PC-351 shows that triangular spectralization erases the oriented coordinate that carried it. PC-352 closes the most immediate semisimple repair: a genuinely non-solvable `SL_2` development generating all of `sl_2` is still exactly matched-control conjugate when the reciprocal sign flip is an inner parity automorphism. Representation dimension, noncommutativity and non-solvability therefore do not create selectivity if arithmetic and control remain in the same automorphism orbit of the destination quotient.

Prime Lattice gives a three-way summation control. Finite signed shift filters cross the positive convergence wall but only by producing classical periodic `P(s)zeta(s)` continuations. Unrestricted infinite filters are algebraically universal because `b=c*1` is inverted by Möbius convolution. The first natural bounded-operator restriction, `c in ell^1`, removes programmability but forces uniformly limit-periodic outputs with `sigma_c=0`, `sigma_a=1` after mean cancellation. The useful regime is therefore not “more signed support”: it must be an independently source-forced admissible class that avoids both arbitrary programming and universal limit-periodicity.

Farey Discrepancy shows why representation richness must also be priced through inverse geometry. FD-202's signed adjacent-prime projection lowers the source-faithful random quadratic cost from the diagonal `H^(3/2)` scale to `H`, but leaves a one-dimensional constant kernel. FD-203 proves that one scalar anchor can close that kernel at the same `H` random cost while the path still has squared point-evaluation condition number `Theta(K/log K)`. Kernel dimension and recovery cost are distinct resources: little new data may remove the nullspace without making the representation uniformly invertible.

Nyman--Beurling supplies the proof-interface analogue: an exact signed primitive-kernel convolution can contain the desired cancellation while an absolute-value decoupling destroys it and creates an apparent reconstruction tariff. Weil Inertia similarly shows that formal support size and a nominal extra charge moment can disappear at destination resolution or already be controlled by the same sensitivity budget. Weil Positivity shows that genuine local finite--archimedean coupling can be scalarized again by the canonical positive globalization required to remove its auxiliary scale.

The shared question is: **what new source-specific coordinate is acquired, what closure/summation/globalization or pre-norm projection is unavoidable, what automorphism quotient and inverse geometry remain after that operation, which proof inequality first discards signed structure, and which matched control reproduces the survivor?** Richer data, larger Lie targets, infinite support, non-diagonal topology or signed filters deserve credit only after all of those gates survive.

## Where does the obstruction move after a nominal critical boundary is resolved?

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the clearest collision hierarchy. AF-417--AF-421 move from `s_n/n` to the joint source/derivative coordinate `b_n(a)=a n^(s_n/n-2)` and then to discrete square-transition fibers `b=m^2`. AF-422 shows that equality of adjacent saddle rates is insufficient; the rectangle ratio is controlled by

`Xi_(n,m)=log n+n log(b_n/m^2)-d_n log m`.

AF-423 adds a complete-packet inverse-`n` drift. AF-424 then resolves the detuning that is lost if `alpha_n=d_n/n` is replaced by zero too early: first complete cancellation is equivalently

`n(Xi_(n,m)+log K_m)+m d_n -> m^2-m+1-2ae^2`.

On generic square paths the `m alpha_n` correction can be order `1/log n`, larger than the inverse-`n` packet term. Even after this tuning, the common saddle is exponentially large, so the frontier moves again to the next complete-packet term or a lower-saddle/tall-column residual. A collision surface must therefore be re-expanded in the coordinates that remain large on that surface; open-stratum uniformity cannot be inherited automatically.

Visual Exploration gives the mean-value analogue. Exact gamma recentering removes the moving lower-source wall; zero-free localization removes the first upper coherence wall; exact Wang coefficient moments save a logarithm; parity support deletes the odd-shift sector at the `x log x` layer. VIS-304 then uses the actual prime-power logarithmic frequency set in the weighted Hilbert inequality and moves the generic estimate again to

`x log log x=o(H)`.

The remaining `log log x` is an absolute reciprocal-spacing/sieve cost, not an observed correction. The live object is the signed endpoint Hilbert form itself. This is another instance of the same procedural rule: after a bound is sharpened, identify the new load-bearing object before interpreting the new scale as structural.

Farey Discrepancy adds a different obstruction motion. FD-202 makes the constant mode look like the only algebraic defect; FD-203 shows that anchoring it is cheap, but the next obstruction is path effective resistance. Prime Circle has the same pattern at a quotient level: PC-351 moves the obstruction from source fidelity to triangular spectralization, and PC-352 moves it again from solvability to the inner/outer character of the matched-control parity on the target quotient. Nyman--Beurling similarly moves the loss from exact reconstruction into the proof norm that decouples a signed convolution. Robin Extremal has already moved its leading gap into the continuum prediction constant `1<=C_sep<=6.081` rather than prime placement itself.

Across these lines, the useful response to a resolved threshold is to identify the **new packet, graph metric, automorphism class, support sector, proof inequality, theorem hypothesis or resource** that becomes load-bearing, then test whether it has its own collision locus, source-specific cancellation or matched control.

## Which physical cost survives source specialization and analytic transport?

[MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md) and [MI-025](intuition/MI-025-combinatorial-breadth-must-be-priced-through-the-source-coordinate-map.md) remain the main source-cost syntheses.

Möbius Cancellation now separates three analytic ceilings that all happen to meet near a `log log y` source exponent but arise from different mechanisms. MC-382 has an exponential differencing-depth tax. MC-383 shows that an exponential family cannot defeat a fixed positive power of one global modulus. MC-384 shows that a factorization-sensitive theorem can have excellent terminal saving and still be useless beyond the same horizon because its **admission condition** already forces `a=O(log log y)` for `q=y^a`. Future inputs must therefore be audited in physical source coordinates twice: first the admissible range, then the output tariff.

The coincidence of that `log log` horizon with VIS-304's `x log log x` mean-value cost is not a cross-line mechanism. In Visual Exploration the factor comes from reciprocal spacing of prime-power logarithmic frequencies plus an upper-bound sieve; in Möbius Cancellation it comes from conductor/depth or theorem-admission geometry. Equal asymptotic labels are not interchangeable resources unless a mathematical map identifies the underlying source coordinates.

Farey Discrepancy now separates acquisition rank, covariance-preserving projection cost, kernel dimension and effective resistance. A scalar anchor can cost only `Theta(H)` in the random benchmark while the deterministic inverse across the prime path still costs `Theta(K/log K)` in squared dual norm. Prime Circle adds an orthogonal resource: automorphism separation. A target can be algebraically far richer than a control yet pay zero discriminating distance after quotienting if the control involution is inner. Robin Extremal separates prediction loss from prime-carrier capacity. Weil Inertia separates hard orbit complexity from soft tail mass and identifies one nominal resource as already paid by Lipschitz sensitivity.

The shared audit is to price the route **after** exact source specialization and nuisance removal, and to keep theorem admission, transport loss, family breadth, conductor geometry, graph resistance, automorphism orbit, support spacing, proof-decoupling loss and destination resolution as distinct currencies until a theorem proves a dependency between them.

## Can sign-indefinite structure preserve critical information before closure or norm compression scalarizes it?

Weil Positivity remains the strongest positive-closure warning: local arithmetic interaction can be genuine while canonical positive globalization Mellin-separates it back into a failed product geometry.

Prime Lattice now brackets the signed-filter problem more sharply. Positive coefficientwise recovery cannot cross the real convergence wall. Finite signs cross it but only periodically. Arbitrary infinite signs make the coefficient map universal. `ell^1` signs restore boundedness but collapse to limit-periodic output. The missing object is therefore not merely a negative sign or an infinite tail; it is a **source-forced nonpositive law with independent rigidity that survives continuation and remains zero-selective**.

Farey Discrepancy supplies a finite-dimensional positive example of where the sign can matter. The adjacent-prime difference must be taken before the positive quadratic norm; squaring the raw rows first restores the larger shell tariff. FD-203 then adds that preserving the critical random cost is still not sufficient: the resulting signed observation graph must also have acceptable inverse resistance, or the chosen anchor must already be the destination witness.

Nyman--Beurling supplies the analytic analogue: keeping primitive and reconstruction kernel coupled preserves a candidate oscillatory mechanism that disappears under absolute decoupling. Prime Circle adds a symmetry analogue: retaining noncommutative orientation is still not enough if the destination passes to a conjugacy class on which the sign flip is inner. These examples do not transfer estimates to one another, but they sharpen the common interface: useful nonpositivity must occur before the scalarizing operation, be forced by the source, survive required transport, and remain distinct after the destination's own symmetry quotient.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the admissible source class and topology, observation family and nullspace, destination quotient and norm, source-coordinate map, acquisition budget, requested precision, normalization gauge, analytic domain, approximation/summation protocol, moving-complexity coordinates, support constraints, theorem-admission range, transport/reconstruction loss, proof-decoupling loss, sign/covariance structure, inverse graph geometry, target automorphism group and matched control.

Then ask in sequence: **what is genuinely new at acquisition; what unavoidable closure/summation/globalization or pre-norm projection follows; what symmetry quotient does the destination impose; what survives it; what is the first inequality or theorem hypothesis that becomes load-bearing; which apparent resources are actually dependent; what inverse resource must the source pay; and does a matched non-arithmetic control reproduce the same survivor?**

The current synthesis narrows rather than proliferates directions. Complete fidelity is not enough if invariantization erases it. Non-solvability is not enough if a matched control is inner-conjugate. A one-dimensional kernel is not enough to characterize recovery cost. Leaving the positive cone is not enough if the finite filter is classical, the infinite filter programmable, or the bounded filter limit-periodic. Exponential family breadth is not enough if a fixed conductor power absorbs it, and a strong terminal estimate is not enough if its own admission range excludes the target source scale. A theorem-level big-O is not a structural boundary until it survives exact coefficient specialization, exact support geometry and the signed object hidden beneath its absolute norm.

Progress therefore requires an intermediate structure that is nontrivial in the declared topology, source-specific after matched controls and symmetry quotients, survives the exact destination operation and proof interface, remains quantitatively visible at the moving resolution, and couples to a destination theorem without reconstructing the original RH criterion in disguise.
