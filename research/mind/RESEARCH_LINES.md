## Require operator-category and spectral-type compatibility, not merely matching scalar symbols

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-005-topology-is-part-of-the-arithmetic-claim`.

Several branches now show that apparently close scalar laws can live in incompatible operator realizations. Prime-Flute/Weil comparisons cross Schatten boundaries; positive DtN response loses Hermitian positivity under critical continuation; and Weil Positivity proves that the solenoid and radial Gamma carriers, despite sharing the same scalar Gamma dispersion at rational frequencies, admit no nonzero bounded exact intertwiner because one is pure point and the other continuous.

A decisive bridge must therefore specify how spectral measure/type, domains, operator ideal, and sign structure are transported. Dense agreement of eigenvalue formulas, a bounded nonunitary coordinate change, or a category-changing operation without an independent positivity theorem does not establish a common arithmetic operator.

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-005-topology-is-part-of-the-arithmetic-claim` and `MI-007-survival-requires-quantitative-non-escape-not-just-nonzero-structure`.

Nonzero finite-stage structure, pointwise vanishing error, or increasing local derivative order are insufficient without a quantitative non-escape theorem.
