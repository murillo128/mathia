# Global mathematical questions

This file records mathematical questions that only become visible by comparing several current Mathia research lines. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does a quantitative budget become a genuine source discriminator?

Several lines now show that the decisive budget is rarely the first coarse norm one can write down. The useful quantity must be fixed before target inspection, live in the geometry actually consumed by the destination, and be occupied by the physical source rather than by a matched control.

Visual Exploration gives a sharp selector-level progression. VIS-416 removes generic reciprocal-bandwidth scaling; VIS-417--VIS-418 show that beat geometry and time-origin alignment can manufacture long quiet windows even after coarse spectral controls are fixed; VIS-419 identifies the exact fixed-shell base-prime Haar self-null; VIS-420 prices finite-window replacement by the actual prime-log small divisors; and VIS-421 shows that Wang's source does not itself select distinguished heights. VIS-422 eliminates ordinary Gram points as a fixed-shell anomaly source because their finite prime-phase vectors already converge to Haar. VIS-423 then reaches changing polynomial observables: on dyadic Gram blocks, deviation from Haar is controlled by total Fourier mass, frequency-weighted mass and reciprocal-frequency mass. VIS-424 removes normalized pointwise amplitude as a generic nonlinear escape: fixed moments pay an explicit tariff through normalized Wiener mass, outer character scale and fixed-order prime-log small divisors, and fixed-threshold tails additionally require only uniform Haar anti-concentration. A growing shell therefore escapes the null only through a quantitatively visible tariff failure, non-Haar selector, boundary concentration, or a genuinely path-valued observable such as persistence; increasing dimension or taking an absolute value is not a source discriminator by itself.

Weil Inertia supplies the continuation analogue. WI-429--WI-430 give a bounded Hardy detector with a positive first-defect quantum, while WI-431--WI-432 show that the absolutely convergent safe-line source has no persistent positive-frequency carrier, even after adiabatic localization. WI-433 supplies the exact horizontal transport identity, and WI-434 shows that the raw principal-pole divergence in the edge term disappears after the exact semigroup transport. WI-435 then closes the proposed edge-only bootstrap itself: even if both safe-line leakage and the **entire** transported edge term are `o(1)`, a finite Kunik Blaschke block retains a nonzero interior Hardy defect quantum, with the interior field simply converging to the negative residue signal. The live discriminator must therefore be independent information that upper-bounds or otherwise constrains the interior Hardy component in a way the finite-Blaschke model cannot saturate; regular-edge control alone is not such information.

Farey Discrepancy gives a complementary positive example of a source anomaly becoming progressively less representational. FD-298--FD-305 reduce near-capacity low-height behavior from a block-Mertens field through aligned correlations to one common interval and finally, in the deepest RH-conditional regime, to an additive Fourier fourth-moment/pointwise spike at a source-selected scale. FD-306 further shows that above `lambda_maj=41/(90c-6)=1.3076742164...`, current Vinogradov-quality Möbius minor-arc control forces the entire FD-305-required fourth-moment mass onto rational major arcs of total measure `O(X^(-1/5))`. The compression remains one-way: major-arc concentration does not reconstruct the population or divisor-replication geometry that forced it, and no specific rational denominator is yet selected.

Prime Lattice sharpens what “matching the destination geometry” means. PL-437 compresses the unresolved mod-5 orientation to two coordinates, PL-438 identifies the exact Schur ellipse, and PL-439 realizes the missing coordinates as two paired differences of real signed-residue energies. PL-440 now resolves their symmetry types: every translation-stationary lag kernel cancels the `s`-difference exactly, while the stationary part of the `f`-difference is one explicit quadratic-character lag projection. The remaining source theorem is therefore not generic covariance control but variance-scale control of a reflection/start-residue anisotropy channel plus one `chi_5` lag channel and its anisotropic remainder, measured in the exact Schur metric.

Across these examples, a budget becomes a source discriminator only after its selector, quotient, continuation and symmetry semantics are fixed. VIS-423--VIS-424 show that a growing or nonlinear observable may already realize the Haar null once its moment and anti-concentration tariffs are paid; WI-433--WI-435 show that exact continuation can move the defect signal into the interior field without producing an independent upper bound on it; FD-305--FD-306 show that a complicated source anomaly can compress first to a standard Fourier statistic and then to a thin rational region only after its alignment freedoms are paid; and PL-440 shows that an apparently two-dimensional covariance defect may split into a symmetry-forbidden mode and one character mode. The reusable test is to identify the exact surviving source channel in the destination-native geometry and then ask whether the proposed budget constrains that channel independently rather than merely re-expressing it.

## Which source dependence survives quotienting, population ceilings and equilibrium scalarization?

Möbius Cancellation now prices both the endpoint and incomplete-hard-mask branches in the physical source geometry. MC-497--MC-501 turn the quadratic endpoint problem into source-location, source-width, population and effective-participation gates. MC-502 removes conductor-power gain from complete-period plus/minus hard-mask coherence. MC-503--MC-504 show that fixed-profile or profile-class bias is controlled by start/profile participation. MC-505 then removes the arbitrariness of that decomposition: for the actual varying start/profile matrix `B`, the centered character kernel sees only the matched Fourier diagonal of `PBP`, and its bias is bounded by `C sqrt(p)||PBP||_*`. Equivalently, the canonical interaction participation `R_int=W^2/||PBP||_*^2` must be only conductor-scale for order-one bias to remain compatible with the square-root multiplier. The remaining arithmetic resource is therefore concentration in this **centered matched start-profile interaction channel**, not profile count, marginal support or mask irregularity by themselves.

Farey Discrepancy and Prime Circle mark opposite sides of lawful compression. FD-301--FD-306 progressively discard masks, moving-window freedoms and unrestricted frequency only after quantitative payment, ending in the deepest regime with a rational-major-arc additive-spectral certificate. PC-414--PC-420 show the converse danger: once a source interaction is lost before shell scalarization, finite/infinite conductor descendants, Mellin jets, real translations, operator-norm Weyl closure and even arbitrary bounded radial diagonal enrichment with the discrete source-forced shifts cannot recover a compact/discrete spectral sector. The strong/weak closure is all of `B(H)` while the relevant norm-closed classes contain no nonzero compacts. A simpler representation is useful only when one has proved that it retains the exact source dependence needed by the destination.

Visual Exploration gives the analogous phase quotient. VIS-419--VIS-423 separate the valid base-prime torus, finite-window calibration, selector provenance, fixed-shell selector equidistribution and a quantitative growing-family character tariff. VIS-424 pushes the same audit through nonlinear pointwise amplitude by reducing continuous amplitude tests to scalar moments and fixed-threshold tails to those moments plus uniform Haar anti-concentration. Ordinary Gram points survive the provenance gate but fail the fixed-shell discrimination gate, and many growing normalized-amplitude observables also fail once their moment tariffs are small. A future phase statistic must therefore preserve not only the shell's internal character dependencies but also the specific high-frequency, small-divisor, threshold-concentration or path information claimed to survive the Gram/Haar pushforward.

Weil Positivity now closes several increasingly permissive KMS escapes. WP-425 kills auxiliary-frequency compensation under product KMS dynamics; WP-426 shows that state-selected modular/interacting dynamics is programmable; WP-427 shows that even a general source-preserving KMS extension annihilates nonzero source Bohr modes when the compensator lies in the source relative commutant. WP-428 then allows genuine noncommutativity but proves that every **Bohr-diagonal positive sandwich** is still source-time invariant and kills the critical nonzero-frequency defect. The matched control is exact: coherent cross terms between distinct auxiliary Bohr sectors can move source frequency to zero. The surviving category is therefore source-forced cross-frequency coherence, not noncommutativity or interaction by themselves, and its relative phases/amplitudes must be derived before the target sign is inspected.

Nyman--Beurling supplies a different warning about apparent thresholds. NB-322 found logarithmic rank-one-error resonances for growing rational denominators below `sqrt n`, and NB-323 found the same scale again at the linear endpoint. NB-324 now proves that on the exact even-divisor skeleton the resonance persists for **every growing denominator**, with `E_(n,q)=(H_q-H_(q/2))/(4n)+O(1/(nq))`; power-of-two subsequences realize every fixed scale `m=n^(alpha+o(1))`, `1<=alpha<2`. The old intermediate-denominator gap was therefore an estimate artifact on this arithmetic family. Any upper mixing theorem must now exploit odd/nondivisor/near-rational arithmetic, cancellation unavailable to the adjacent witness, or target occupation rather than denominator size alone.

The reusable cross-line question is: **after the lawful quotient and scalarization, what conditional dependence or coherence still carries the source?** MC-505 says the current quadratic hard-mask kernel sees only a centered matched Fourier diagonal, not the full start/profile table; WP-427--WP-428 say noncommutativity is harmless unless distinct Bohr sectors retain coherent relative data; NB-324 shows that a putative scale transition can disappear when an exact arithmetic relation is retained; VIS-424 says pointwise nonlinearity is harmless when all fixed moment tariffs and boundary anti-concentration are paid; and FD-306 says a surviving large additive spectrum can be forced into a thin rational locus without yet identifying the source-selected denominator. Source dependence often survives in a jointly constrained relation, and the right complexity measure is the one induced by the exact kernel or quotient that actually observes that relation.

## What replaces a scalar summary when nuisance geometry or leading normalization degenerates?

Arithmetic Fidelity gives both an obstruction and a hierarchy of replacement coordinates. AF-523--AF-528 show that scalar boundary-curvature summaries can be matched while an exact relational derivative still recovers hidden source shape. AF-533--AF-538 then identify the nuisance-relative geometry: target information is distance from the **closed** nuisance-plus-invisible space, and in finite-dimensional targets the surviving object is an efficient-information spectrum rather than one scalar coefficient.

AF-539 located a sharp cancellation scale for the first projective log-slope under scalar tail-multiplier nuisance, AF-540 showed that every fixed higher log-jet `k>=2` annihilates the unbounded affine multiplier direction, and AF-541 converted the same idea into a unique minimal two-node finite-resolution contrast for the exact carrier. AF-542 now supplies the missing physical stress test and it is negative: at `h=A^(-1)`, the first nonlinear correction from exact carrier to raw normalized curvature is only `O(C_A/log A)` at value level but is magnified by the AF-541 transverse normalization until it dominates the target signal. The exact-carrier discriminator therefore does **not** survive the raw curvature representation merely because that representation is relatively accurate pointwise. The live transfer problem is to control representation error in the seminorm induced by the downstream discriminator, or to use a curvature-native correction/renormalization or retained pre-compression information that removes the nonlinear defect.

Prime Lattice and Weil Inertia show two complementary forms of the same discipline. PL-440 does not ask for “more covariance”; it decomposes the exact two-coordinate Schur defect into a symmetry anisotropy and a character-lag mode before any estimate is attempted. WI-433--WI-435 do not ask for “more Hardy localization”: exact horizontal transport first removes a false principal-pole barrier, and the finite-Blaschke countermodel then shows that even perfect edge control supplies no independent upper bound for the interior defect field. In both cases the replacement object is useful only if the unresolved component is explicit enough to falsify a proposed source estimate.

Prime Circle remains the operator-topology negative control. PC-419 shows that the norm-closed source-forced Weyl-descendant algebra has no compact sector, PC-420 shows that arbitrary bounded radial diagonal enrichment does not change that fact, and PC-418 shows that the opposite strong/weak closure is all of `B(H)`. The meaningful replacement cannot therefore be “more closure” of the same scalarized generators. It must add a source-derived non-diagonal/integrated/resolvent/cross-shell structure whose matched controls do not already reproduce it.

The cross-line challenge is to construct replacement objects that are simultaneously **source-natural, destination-complete and stable in the topology actually consumed after the real quotient/compression**. AF-542 shows that value-level representation fidelity can fail after a sensitive transverse discriminator; MC-505 shows that a large ambient two-dimensional source table can quotient to one matched Fourier diagonal; WI-435 shows that making every transport error small can still leave the target defect untouched because the destination field itself carries it; and PC-420 shows that enlarging bounded radial data within the wrong operator topology still cannot manufacture the desired compact sector. Promotion from a useful representation to a mathematical research line therefore requires an explicit stability/coercivity statement in the destination-native geometry, not merely exact recovery, more coordinates or a smaller ambient error norm.
