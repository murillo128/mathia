# Global mathematical questions

## Can a source-native transformation reach the exact Weil target without positive scalarization erasing the source?

[MI-009](intuition/MI-009-local-correctness-does-not-determine-global-coherence.md), [MI-012](intuition/MI-012-information-content-and-destination-sensitivity-are-separate-resources.md) and [MI-013](intuition/MI-013-closing-the-bulk-often-leaves-a-thin-source-specific-endpoint.md) separate continuum provenance, target sensitivity and source forcing.

Weil Positivity has now closed both the easy target-side and source-side nonlinear/positive escapes. [WP-288](../weil_positivity/findings/WP-288-exact-cone-only-weil-quadratic-forms-linearize-to-positive-definite-distributions.md) shows that an exact continuous Weil-square functional satisfying the identities forced by convolution squares automatically polarizes to a translation-invariant distribution; if it matches all Weil squares, factorization forces that distribution to be the Weil distribution itself. [WP-289](../weil_positivity/findings/WP-289-critical-mangoldt-covariances-admit-no-continuous-positive-weil-majorant.md) shows that the non-tempered critical Mangoldt covariance cannot be preserved monotonically as a positive summand inside any continuous positive completion.

The open theorem is therefore upstream and signed: derive a source-native finite--archimedean interference, quotient/compression, operator coupling, cohomological/intersection pairing or other transformation that changes the critical arithmetic block before final positivity is read, while preserving enough arithmetic provenance to identify the result. Then prove the exact quadratic and translation identities required by the Weil target. Arbitrary nonlinear scalarization and positive additive completion are no longer separate escape categories.

## Which source restrictions create the quantitative margin actually consumed by the destination?

[MI-008](intuition/MI-008-exact-recovery-is-not-stable-transport.md) now separates exact source restriction, fixed-source conditioning, support-union topology and destination normalization.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the sharpest quantifier-sensitive example. AF-320 repairs exact eight-prime recovery, AF-321 proves relation rank alone has zero uniform margin, and [AF-322](../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-322-prime-middle-singular-conditioning-has-a-finite-height-polynomial-modulus.md) recovers a polynomial finite-height exclusion modulus for each fixed rational-prime support while its asymptotic gap remains zero. [AF-323](../arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-323-varying-prime-supports-are-tail-dense-in-fixed-time-phase-space.md) then reverses the quantifiers: at every fixed nonzero time, unrestricted varying prime supports beyond any cutoff are dense in the full finite phase torus. Thus no support-independent positive phase-separation modulus depending only on time can exist. The live theorem is exact middle-singular incidence plus a fixed-support modulus strong enough for the downstream target; any cross-support stability claim must retain a support resource or restrict the admissible family.

Prime Circle has an analogous two-boundary squeeze. PC-281 transports regular profiles to matched rational controls; PC-282 turns the singular logarithmic boundary into the exact modular selector; [PC-283](../prime_circle/findings/PC-283-selector-subtracted-log-remainder-is-a-digamma-green-kernel.md) shows the canonical selector-subtracted full-domain remainder is a classical digamma/Green kernel. The surviving scalar-profile question is the simultaneous critical finite-section regime where source-dependent two-sided projection placement survives after both universal boundary pieces are removed.

Prime Flute now supplies an even sharper destination counterpart. PF-326--PF-328 preserve a positive-length family of compactification-critical packets before final angle normalization. [PF-329](../prime_flute/findings/PF-329-final-diagonal-normalization-turns-seam-pulled-packets-into-total-energy-unit-traces.md) shows that a unit packet in the final angle coordinate pulls back to `Q_L^(-1/2) A^(-1/2) psi_t`, not merely to its seam-normalized trace. Block-diagonal seam congruence is unitary at the intrinsic-angle level, while the completed diagonal factor `A^(-1/2)` may reshape the packet and need not commute with translations. The surviving direct theorem is therefore total-energy correlation after completed diagonal preconditioning on almost all critical centers, not preservation of the PF-328 seam amplitudes.

Visual Exploration now resolves the support geometry of the first post-noncancelling layer. VIS-209 writes the complete one-cancellation energy as a PSD anchor-Gram quantity; [VIS-210](../visual_exploration/findings/VIS-210-thin-window-anchor-gram-diagonalization.md) shows that in the thin-window cone `L_tri>5R-5` distinct shell supports are exactly disjoint, so the Gram is diagonal, with only an explicit `6/5` resonance in the adjacent `4R-3` fringe. The live theorem there is diagonal shell-energy control; outside that cone the stratified resonance geometry determines when full spectral-spreading estimates are actually needed. The independent upgrade from start-average to uniform-start control remains open.

## Which cancellation theorem remains after artificial composition and scalar-envelope losses are removed?

[MI-010](intuition/MI-010-source-cancellation-budgets-are-family-specific.md) now has sharper examples on both sides.

Farey Discrepancy has passed bounded-defect entry, projective composition loss and current endpoint regularity. [FD-101](../farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-101-one-shell-exactification-makes-tagged-nonhead-source-descent-impossible.md) removes cumulative rounding as an escape: on any tagged non-head genealogy the source quotient changes at most once, by `+1`, and that jump exactifies the carrier. The residual is now specifically an adaptive retagging theorem: either enough packet mass remains on coherent genealogies for the finite inventory charge to force head exposure, or carrier switches themselves must be charged while preserving projective quality.

Möbius Cancellation has passed tensor/radial representation, detector efficiency and arbitrary-coefficient uniformization. MC-271--[MC-272](../mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-272-raw-cauchy-circle-norm-blind-threshold.md) further show that positive-real generating majorants and raw complex-circle norms remain above one blind atom even while preserving the distinguished flat source vector. Robin Extremal now separates its remaining local issue from the global obstruction. [RE-102](../robin_extremal/findings/RE-102-two-sided-first-layer-contact-closes-the-local-support-boundary-frontier.md) closes the current local support-boundary depth hierarchy above `Theta_*`. The open Robin mechanism is therefore nonlocal/source-conditioned assembly of the locally ordinary chambers, with the binary tie isolated as a transcendence contingency rather than another short-interval channel.

## What cost prevents component switching from defeating componentwise coercivity?

[MI-014](intuition/MI-014-componentwise-coercivity-needs-a-switching-cost-before-globalization.md) isolates a new cross-line quantifier pattern from Arithmetic Fidelity and Farey Discrepancy.

Arithmetic Fidelity has a strong estimate after fixing one component of the source family: AF-322 gives a polynomial exclusion modulus for a fixed prime support. But AF-323 shows that taking the union over supports at one fixed time fills the ambient phase torus, so the componentwise modulus cannot globalize unless support complexity is retained. Farey has the analogous dynamic issue: FD-100--FD-101 exhaust source-neutral row inventory on any fixed tagged genealogy, but the adaptive packet can retag onto another carrier and thereby import a fresh local budget.

The concrete question is not whether these two problems share one theorem. It is whether each admits a source-native **switching cost** that survives the destination metric. For Arithmetic Fidelity that means a support parameter or restriction strong enough to prevent the AF-323 closure mechanism while remaining useful downstream. For Farey it means an energy-weighted carrier identity/variation quantity that makes repeated retagging consume packet quality or force source movement. Componentwise coercivity becomes globally useful only after the cost of changing components is itself controlled.

## Which source-local geometry can exclude collective zero-state failures that survive global controls?

Nyman--Beurling, Prime Lattice and Weil Inertia increasingly agree that good low-dimensional or generic geometry is not enough.

Nyman--Beurling has exact determinant cancellation and uniform two-state conditioning after canonical Blaschke deflation. NB-098 gives a transverse three-state family with unit pivots and good pairs but quartic full conditioning blow-up; NB-099 proves that height, standard zeta symmetries, simplicity and arbitrarily sparse global zero counts do not remove it. [NB-100](../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-100-quadratic-horizontal-tangency-is-the-local-three-state-shear-threshold.md) now identifies the exact threshold on the minimal symmetric slice: with vertical half-gap `epsilon` and horizontal middle displacement `delta`, bounded conditioning is equivalent to `delta=O(epsilon^2)`. The next theorem must derive a source-realistic second-order local zero law, generalize this invariant to asymmetric confluence, or avoid dimension-free canonical conditioning through a different target conversion.

Prime Lattice still has all-log transport for actual eigenstates but no source-specific positive-order regularity or sign theorem; its surviving boundary improvement must come from the localized-Weil source/eigen-equation rather than generic transport.

Weil Inertia separates scalar-profile exhaustion, retained Hilbert orientation and actual source geometry. WI-279 recovers orientation lost by scalarization, while WI-280 shows generic noncommensurate cut mixtures still cannot break the scalar-profile ceiling. The next gain must prove a compatibility law for the actual Suzuki null mode, strengthen the numerator through the source equation, or use a genuinely nonlinear source invariant.
