## When does an escape from one obstruction survive the next reduction?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit, [MI-030](intuition/MI-030-effective-complexity-is-a-geometry-weighted-budget.md) sharpens the resource side, [MI-031](intuition/MI-031-source-dependence-must-change-the-surviving-quotient-not-just-the-representation.md) sharpens provenance, [MI-032](intuition/MI-032-ambient-obstructions-matter-only-on-the-admissible-image.md) adds reachability, [MI-033](intuition/MI-033-determinant-wrappers-do-not-create-new-effective-rank-after-carrier-reduction.md) adds nonlinear carrier-and-scale reduction, and [MI-034](intuition/MI-034-positivity-is-informative-only-when-the-induced-constraint-remains-proper-after-the-destination-quotient.md) adds a positivity/quotient gate.

Arithmetic Fidelity now separates fixed-location finite data, exact asymptotic injectivity, finite-depth observation survival, inverse parameter resolution, observation horizon and cross-sample noise geometry. AF-483--AF-484 show that matched Beurling-prime controls survive any prescribed finite family of exact prime-zeta samples. AF-485--AF-487 give rightward exact recovery and the fixed-depth `Theta(1/t)` local inverse modulus at critical noise. AF-488 shows that arbitrarily dense accumulated sampling inside horizon `H` cannot beat `Theta(1/H)` under coherent pointwise relative noise. AF-489 identifies the precise escape: under one fixed global `ell_p` relative-noise budget the sharp radius is `Theta(epsilon/||s||_p)`, hence `Theta(H^(-1-1/p))` on a fixed-step lattice for finite `p`. History helps only because the admissible errors are coupled under a non-growing total budget; if that budget grows with sample count, the horizon law returns. Provenance remains separate.

Farey Discrepancy has converted its missing positive-slack lower bound into an exponent test. FD-260 gives the general positive dyadic depletion exponent `beta=log_2(3/2)`, FD-259 gives inverse-linear depletion under the stronger monotone-response condition, and FD-258 supplies an independent first-row polynomial-depth ceiling. FD-261 shows that a same-amplitude repair demand merely linear in divisor depth becomes contradictory against FD-260 only after the first-row obstruction has already fired. To beat that ceiling in the general positive cone requires depth amplification beyond `D^(2-beta)=D^1.415...`; alternatively a structural sign theorem placing the repair in the FD-259 cone makes linear depth accumulation exponent-critical. The missing theorem is therefore not “some slack is consumed” but a source lower bound strong enough to cross the applicable phase boundary.

Möbius Cancellation has closed both bare cross-lcm frequency synchronization and the simplest final-coefficient sign escape. MC-450 shows that synchronizing fixed-lcm slices and then fully recombining them returns the original centered additive-difference autocorrelation; generic dual Cauchy is not new cancellation. MC-451 shows that for classical beta-sieve coefficients every compatible divisor pair on a squarefree fixed lcm has the same Möbius sign, so the recombined fixed-lcm zero mode is maximally coherent. Any surviving q-vdC gain must therefore use information before that scalarization: well-factorable component signs, conductor factorization, higher CRT/cycle structure, cross-lcm coupling or variable-length source geometry.

Nyman--Beurling has strengthened the legal-realization obstruction for the source-native Ford current. NB-276 identifies `zeta'/zeta+zeta` as the singular Mellin derivative of the canonical Möbius Nyman source, and NB-277 closes horizontal Abel damping at fixed Ford depth. NB-278 now shows that every finite differentiated Nyman source of the canonical linear form is entire and has zero interior divisor current, whereas the limiting continued object has residues at zeta zeros. Distributional continuity therefore forbids direct finite interior approximation across a zero. The live route must change architecture—boundary/contour terms, pinching limits, nonlinear pole creation or another explicit singular mechanism—rather than seek better finite differentiated coefficients.

Prime Circle has crossed the `alpha=1/2` positive-Riesz/Kron boundary without finding arithmetic specificity. PC-396 closes all fixed polynomial windows at the critical endpoint. Prime Lattice has learned that positive marginal data can lose the exact source metric before any spectral question is asked. Visual Exploration has closed commuting external geometry and quantified weak noncommutation. VIS-371 shows that any coordinate-free one-Gram positive metric rule is a spectral filter. Weil Inertia now has both sides of the logarithmic-compactness boundary. WI-389--WI-390 show that uniformly integrable logarithmic Fourier tails force zeta-spectral tightness and survive exact source perforation, extending the recurrence/no-gap mechanism. Weil Positivity has narrowed the remaining fixed-shadow source-relation escape. WP-399 shows that a bounded positive centralizer shadow has a dense two-sided tangent family. A surviving linear relation must be genuinely nonlocal across shadow spectral scales and fail the cutoff test for a source-forced reason, or the route must change to nonlinear, singular, unbounded or finite--archimedean structure.

## Where has the exact obstruction moved?

Arithmetic Fidelity: to **uniform growing-depth conditioning, other noise/correlation laws, and a rational-prime discriminator that matched generalized-prime controls cannot preserve**, with the pointwise-versus-global-budget sampling distinction now exact. Farey: to **a source repair lower bound whose depth exponent crosses the current capacity/first-row phase boundary, or a proof of stronger monotone-response structure**.

Möbius: in the gap branch to **growing rank, a genuinely different source measure/coordinate system, absolute scale or discontinuous arithmetic data**; in the q-vdC branch to **structured pre-recombination source coupling**, because synchronized scalar recombination and final fixed-lcm beta-sieve signs are power/coherence dead ends. Nyman--Beurling: simultaneously to **the established NB-272 section-defect/anchor-mismatch fork** and, on the source-native route, to **an architecture that forms the divisor singularity through a legal boundary, nonlinear or singular limit rather than finite differentiated interior currents**.

Prime Circle: to **the residual beyond the universal subcritical Buchstab--Riesz macroscopic mode**—centered fluctuations, mesoscopic/arithmetic scales or cross-level information—not merely to `alpha<1/2`. Prime Lattice: to **the PL-407-scale signed mod-5 supertrace with its off-diagonal coherences retained, plus any separate divisor-faithful spectral theorem**. Visual Exploration: to **gap-resolved noncommuting orientation whose cost survives the complete relative-metric accounting**. Weil Inertia: to **an unconditional or equivalence-grade use of source-exact spectral escape beyond logarithmic compactness**. Weil Positivity: to **a source-forced relation nonlocal under centralizer spectral cutoff, or a genuinely different nonlinear/singular/unbounded category**.

## Which physical cost survives after representation artifacts are removed?

The newest results sharpen the common order of proof. AF-489 shows that sample count becomes useful only when the noise geometry actually couples the samples. FD-261 shows that an abstract capacity ceiling matters only after the source demand crosses its exponent. MC-451 shows that nominal Möbius signs can become maximally coherent after the destination quotient. NB-278 shows that coefficient freedom cannot repair a topological mismatch in divisor current. PC-398 shows that even a large surviving operator sector can classicalize under matched continuum controls. PL-419 shows that positivity-preserving marginalization can delete the exact coherence needed by the source metric. VIS-373 prices noncommuting orientation by the spectral gaps it crosses. WI-391 shows that escaping a compactness no-go can be real, but the escape remains conditional until its zero-location dependence is reversed or removed. WP-400 shows that a source restriction only matters if it survives—or deliberately fails—the localization used by the negative control.

[MI-034](intuition/MI-034-positivity-is-informative-only-when-the-induced-constraint-remains-proper-after-the-destination-quotient.md) records the cross-line positivity version of the audit. The broader durable order remains: quotient universal scaffolds first, restrict to the actual admissible image and source measure, identify the carrier that survives completion, recombination, marginalization or continuation, and then price its amplitude, conditioning, occupancy, sampling/noise geometry, spectral gaps, topology, tangent locality and provenance. An apparent escape counts only if its source-specific information survives every subsequent reduction needed by the destination.
