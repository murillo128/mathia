## When does an escape from one obstruction survive the next reduction?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit, [MI-030](intuition/MI-030-effective-complexity-is-a-geometry-weighted-budget.md) sharpens the resource side, [MI-031](intuition/MI-031-source-dependence-must-change-the-surviving-quotient-not-just-the-representation.md) sharpens the provenance side, [MI-032](intuition/MI-032-ambient-obstructions-matter-only-on-the-admissible-image.md) adds the reachability side, and [MI-033](intuition/MI-033-determinant-wrappers-do-not-create-new-effective-rank-after-carrier-reduction.md) adds a nonlinear carrier-and-scale audit: a determinant is only as informative as the reduced rank, trace, feature geometry and first source-sensitive scale that reaches it.

Arithmetic Fidelity has separated metric deformation, hard source restriction, finite-resolution soft restriction and realizable secant geometry. AF-475 converts a source compressibility profile `tau_s<=phi(s)` into the achieved coordinate condition curve `K_phi(delta)`, while AF-476 gives the complementary nonlinear lower bound on the full profile envelope through realizable head-plus-private-tail cubes. The arithmetic subclass therefore needs both its approximation/realizable-breadth profile and its actual secant sign geometry. Any favorable restriction must still retain a rational-prime discriminator, because the one-crossing Gibbs mechanism also holds for matched nonprime sources.

Farey Discrepancy has separated support conditioning from the actual omission coefficient map. ## Which physical cost survives after representation artifacts are removed?

The newest results sharpen the distinction between an ambient, nonlinear or asymptotic resource and the carrier that actually survives. Arithmetic Fidelity shows both sides: finite affine moments can leave large exact source-point cubes in some fibers, while a one-crossing secant law can restore scale-free Hilbert fidelity by restricting the realizable Jordan geometry; neither ambient support size nor parameter count decides the metric problem alone. Farey shows that huge ambient support conditioning can be irrelevant when the paired omission image misses its bad directions, and that even companion conditioning is reduced by congruence-restricted chain entropy. Visual Exploration reduces both inner-scale rank loss and polynomially vanishing masses to one finite weighted-Hermite cost spectrum. Prime Lattice turns the apparent moving endpoint into a fixed heavy-tailed probability law and shows that the failure of finite moment renormalization is itself an exact feature of that law. Weil Inertia shows that a positive-looking stationary arithmetic reserve is not physical surplus when the exact explicit-formula ledger forces the matching archimedean cancellation.

MI-033 records the parallel nonlinear lesson from Möbius Cancellation and Prime Circle. A determinant can survive a linear projection or react to a low-rank correction without creating new effective rank or leading-order source sensitivity. In MC-439 the determinant factors through fixed additive feature moments; MC-440 clears that rank obstruction but places gap order two homogeneous degrees below permutation-blind leading terms; in PC-392 the regularized log-determinant remains limited by the thin contrast sector's trace and density. The common proof order is to identify the surviving carrier first and only then ask whether a nonlinear observable introduces a genuinely new rank or scale.

The other current currencies remain equally post-quotient. Möbius adds full interaction, effective interaction rank and separation scale to source entropy. Nyman--Beurling separates aggregate linear mesh mass, diagonal quadratic energy and the full projected Gram norm. Prime Lattice prices and removes every fixed finite regulator jet before crediting the endpoint zero-scale signal, then represents the endpoint by its exact source law. Weil Positivity says even a genuinely unbounded positive operator is not a new selector resource if its canonical resolvent maps it back into the already-classified bounded source algebra.

These are not one theorem and their currencies are not numerically interchangeable. The adversarial commonality recorded in MI-030--MI-033 is an order of proof: remove universal scaffolds, symmetry, known kernels, classical preselection and category-programmable choices; restrict to the actual admissible image; identify the exact surviving carrier; then price every linear or nonlinear loss and compensating resource in the source-varying quantity and asymptotic scale that reaches the destination. The latest evidence sharpens several line-specific instances of that audit but does not justify a stronger universal mathematical bridge across the lines.
