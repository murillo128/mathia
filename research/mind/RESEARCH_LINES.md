## Which growing source law is informative rather than sparse, diluted, under-resolved, over-resolved, capacity-saturating or circular?

The remaining problem is no longer to make the expensive complement large; it is to prove that these directions cannot reuse one small common source package. Direction cardinality, linear closure and nonshareable arithmetic incidence are different currencies.

The cross-line lesson is now sharper than “use more source information.” A source condition is informative only relative to the **number of states it resolves at the available precision, the closure operations the destination consumes, and the overlap structure of the source resources that realize those states**. Exact conditioning can be too coarse, one exact scalar can be maximally fine, a sublinear moment hierarchy can already reconstruct the source, and exponentially many expensive modes can still share one source package.
