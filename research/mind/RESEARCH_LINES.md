## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than merely transport, refactor or renorm the source?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) and [MI-028](intuition/MI-028-complete-measurement-families-can-close-an-escape-by-reconstructing-the-classical-object.md) remain the common interface audit.

Prime Circle now closes both obvious representation lifts and the direct source-native metric repair. PC-355 shows that the full-signature/free-Fock lift is still related to reciprocal control by unitary second quantization, while every finite truncation has ordinary spectrum `{1}`. PC-356 goes further: the actual radial-energy Gram metric can be non-diagonal and fail to commute with parity, yet recomputing the same metric on the reciprocal control gives `G~ = J^*GJ`, so the source and control Hilbert spaces remain isometric. New leverage must break this **covariant transport**, not merely make the source metric richer.

Prime Lattice separates ambient critical walls from two genuinely source-rigid layers. PL-367 shows that pole-neutral `ell^p` filters manufacture the exponent ladder `1-1/p`; PL-368 shows that scalar Dirichlet Hardy spaces manufacture a generic `1/2` absolute-convergence wall. PL-369 supplies the nonlinear theorem interface: bounded complete multiplicativity plus pole neutrality and holomorphic continuation can force zeta zero-freeness. PL-370 then shows that local pole neutrality is itself rigid: a holomorphic zero at `s=1` is simple and forces finite prime-harmonic distance from Liouville. But at `sigma_0=1/2` the required continuation remains RH-equivalent through the Liouville control. Source rigidity is real, yet proof advantage still depends on an independent continuation theorem.

Robin Extremal sharpens the parameter-enrichment counterexample. RE-217 gives the global unit Wiener floor. RE-220 shows that a complex single center does not improve the factor-two lower exponent inside the absolute-tail certificate, because radialization preserves the exact coefficient Wiener norm while improving the symmetric-arc contraction. RE-221 then shows that the factor-two floor for fixed positive-real parameters also survives **exact cancellation-sensitive treatment of the finite truncated polynomial**. The obstruction is therefore not merely the triangle inequality used in RE-219, although the theorem remains representation-local and does not raise the universal floor above one.

Farey Discrepancy remains the strongest constructive example of useful pre-norm structure, but its growing-depth escape has narrowed. FD-204--FD-205 give low-resistance, source-localized recovery geometry. FD-206 shows that the positive root anchor is RH-complete, and FD-207 does the same for every fixed-depth signed dyadic root difference. FD-208 leaves `q=o(log n)` as the only power-critical growing-depth window. FD-209 now proves that a **common** sublogarithmic depth controlled on each square-root scale window is still RH-equivalent: its binary-prefix inverse costs only `(1+sqrt(2))^q` times subpower errors. The only remaining binomial-depth escape is scale-dependent depth incoherence or a collective signed relation that never yields common-depth pointwise control.

Nyman--Beurling now demonstrates the positive counterpart of the representation audit. NB-221 isolates the same local `-zeta'/zeta` carrier after fixed-depth primitivization and faithful transport. NB-222 proves that the contour scale is sharp over the generic bounded-analytic strip class, so formal oscillation, holomorphy and Cauchy derivatives cannot improve it. NB-223 then retains the actual zeta singularity spectrum: a global shift writes the smooth carrier as a weighted zero sum, and Bellotti's near-one zero-density theorem makes that sum `o(1)` whenever `X/R` dominates any fixed positive power of `log log |t|`. The positive Euler branch consequently has an order-one gap throughout every fixed `log^epsilon` margin below Ford height. Here source-specific spectral information really does defeat the generic matched extremizer; the exact Ford layer remains open.

Visual Exploration has matched not only support and multiplicative phase structure but also the actual deterministic time orbit in long-time quadratic mean. VIS-309 proves that, for fixed support, the Bohr mean of the true phase orbit `p^{-it}` equals the completely multiplicative Haar quadratic mean. VIS-310 then shows why a straightforward finite-time transfer fails: hard uniform tail truncation plus the global minimum ratio-frequency gap preserves the small Bohr coefficient energy only at the price of a sufficient window beyond `x^5` up to logarithms when `H~x log log x`. This is a proof-method loss, not a true mixing lower bound. The remaining source-specific candidate is coefficient-weighted finite-height coherence, exceptional heights or a joint moving-frequency regime.

Weil Positivity now has a scale classification sharpened by naturality. WP-367 shows that exact homogeneous globalization factorizes away `nY` incidence; WP-368 shows that bounded fixed-scale positivity preserves incidence only while exponentially suppressing high indices. WP-369 finds one critical power law, `Y_n=c/n`, precisely where `nY_n` freezes and ordinary positive `L^2` squares the half-density into a divergent Rankin--Selberg norm. WP-370 proves that exact **diagonal Hecke equivariance** forces that same ray: `Y_(pm)=Y_m/p`, hence `Y_n=c/n`. A naturality condition can therefore select the critical exponent while simultaneously collapsing the mixed coordinate.

The shared question is: **what new source-specific coordinate is actually acquired; which quotient, norm, approximation certificate, continuation, scale choice, finite inverse, covariance/naturality law or averaging limit can remove its leverage; is the critical boundary ambient or source-rigid; and does the strongest matched control reproduce the structure at the same finite-height/pointwise quantifier consumed by the destination?**

## Where does the obstruction move after a nominal critical boundary is resolved?

Arithmetic Fidelity gives a nested lattice hierarchy rather than one critical surface. AF-425--AF-428 show that critical square-amplitude cancellation descends to an integer derivative-depth shrinking target whose exceptional amplitude set is simultaneously residual and Lebesgue-null. AF-429 then shows that the genuinely supercritical branch develops a smooth moving rectangle saddle at `R_n=a^(1/q_n)n^(1-2/q_n)`, but neighboring integer widths are comparable only when the real saddle center hits `Z` within `O(R_n/n)=o(1)`. AF-430 closes the first complete-packet escape: residual partitions preserve the same shrinking window and move the refined real balance center only by `O(R_n/n^2)`. Continuous optimization, Baire genericity, metric prevalence and packet resummation all stop before the pointwise source-lattice gate.

Farey Discrepancy moves from path resistance to a positive anchor, from that anchor to fixed-depth signed differences, and from transform-cost pricing to an explicit growing-depth inverse. FD-209 shows that throughout the full `q=o(log X)` power-critical window, a common-depth square-root estimate on a scale window still has only subpower inversion cost and remains RH-complete. The surviving escape therefore requires depth to vary along the relevant binary ancestors or a genuinely collective estimate that avoids common-depth pointwise recovery.

Visual Exploration moves the obstruction from localization to absolute spacing, then to signed phase, past same-base multiplicative coherence, past generic common-time cross-prime alignment in Bohr mean, and now past the most naive finite-window transfer. VIS-310 localizes the loss to **uniform tail truncation plus global-gap compression**. The surviving problem is coefficient-sensitive finite-height/moving-frequency coherence: how far can the actual prime-log torus orbit deviate from Haar on the observation window when the weighted ratio spectrum and infinite tail are retained faithfully?

Nyman--Beurling has now moved through the generic strip ceiling into an explicit zeta-zero population problem. NB-222 proves that bounded strip analyticity itself is exhausted. NB-223 then crosses the zeros and uses their near-one density to recover an order-one Euler gap up to every fixed logarithmic margin below Ford height. At the exact Ford scale `X/R=Theta(1)`, the unresolved cost is the final layer of zeros at depth comparable with `log log T`, or cancellation among their weighted residue phases. The obstruction is no longer generic analytic transport; it is the endpoint zero spectrum.

Prime Lattice moves from ambient topology to a valid nonlinear source law and then to a narrow source class. PL-370 says any pole-neutral witness continuing through `s=1` is Liouville-like in prime-harmonic distance. The question is no longer whether source rigidity can force zero-freeness, but whether that Liouville-neighbourhood rigidity supplies continuation beyond `1` by information independent of the zero-free region it would imply.

Robin Extremal moves the constructive prediction obstruction from tail certification to fixed single-center geometry. RE-220 removes complex phase inside the absolute-tail class; RE-221 proves that exact remainder cancellation still cannot beat exponent two for fixed positive-real center/arc/truncation ratios. The next route must change the approximation geometry or asymptotic parameter regime: multiple centers, minimax/different bases, drifting/degenerate single-center parameters, or genuinely complex cancellation outside the radialized absolute certificate.

Möbius Cancellation moves its source-frame obstruction from a qualitative extremizer mismatch to an explicit threshold gap. MC-386 gives the abstract sharp `H/R` exceptional scale; MC-387 rigidifies near-extremizers; MC-388 excludes them from the exact high-dimensional endpoint source image; MC-389 uses the affine rank of the projected simplex to obtain `E(S)<=3|S|^3/4` and, conditional on `epsilon_R=o(R^(-1/2))`, the explicit bound `|B|>=(1+1/4096)H/R`. The frontier is now the analytic good-mode estimate and, secondarily, the optimal endpoint constant—not effectivizing the source gap.

Weil Positivity moves the scale obstruction to a naturality equation. Fixed scale kills the tail, homogeneous globalization kills incidence, the unique critical pure-power scale freezes `nY`, and exact diagonal Hecke intertwining forces that same frozen ray. A surviving route must keep `nY` nonconstant by breaking diagonal Hecke equivariance for a source-justified reason or by moving to genuinely cross-index structure.

Across these lines, a resolved threshold should be followed by identifying the **new discrete lattice, inverse-cost coherence window, approximation class, finite-height weighted spectrum, endpoint zero layer, source-image extremizer gap, continuation class, analytic carrier or covariance-constrained scale coordinate** that becomes load-bearing, rather than promoting the old threshold as structural.

## Which physical cost survives source specialization and analytic transport?

An ambient exceptional theorem must first be pulled back to the source-difference frame; the generic frame scale must be identified; near-sharp ambient extremizers must be classified; those extremizers must be pulled through the exact arithmetic source map; and finally the source mismatch must be quantified. Source admissibility must be priced in the discrete coordinate actually consumed by cancellation.

Farey Discrepancy adds both transform-cost and inverse-cost audits. Robin Extremal separates universal physical cost from representation cost. RE-221 shows that this extra cost survives exact finite-remainder cancellation for fixed real parameters; it is not just an absolute-tail bookkeeping artifact. Visual Exploration adds the matched-time audit. Exact support, magnitudes and same-base multiplicativity do not count as decisive source cost when the signed destination channel cancels under multiplicative Haar phases; VIS-309 further identifies the actual deterministic long-time mean with that model. VIS-310 shows that finite-time transfer must be priced by weighted spectral geometry: replacing the tail by a uniform norm and the spectrum by its closest pair can manufacture a huge horizon unrelated to the true dephasing scale.

Nyman--Beurling now shows how a generic analytic cost can be genuinely lowered by source specialization. NB-222 says the carrier functional costs `e^(-X eta)` on the full bounded-analytic strip class. NB-223 retains the actual zero spectrum and replaces the pointwise polynomial tariff by a finite near-one layer count, allowing any positive power of `log log |t|` in `X/R` to dominate the residues. The shared audit is to price the route **after exact source specialization and nuisance removal**, keeping theorem admission, family breadth, conductor geometry, source-frame energy, source-image compatibility, admissible lattice spacing, category versus measure, approximation/parameter class, transform depth, finite inverse coherence, positive-anchor hardness, coefficient topology, analytic continuation, zero-population/phase information, carrier-frequency cancellation, finite-time weighted phase geometry, scale/index naturality and matched controls distinct until a theorem proves a dependency.

## Can sign-indefinite structure preserve critical information before closure or norm compression scalarizes it?

Farey Discrepancy gives both a constructive and a negative answer. Differences before a positive norm can remove shared bulk and improve geometry, but FD-209 now shows that even **growing common sublogarithmic depth** remains destination-complete on scale windows. Sign helps only if scale-varying depth defeats coherent physical inversion or if a collective relation never first produces a common-depth critical pointwise bound.

Visual Exploration supplies a complementary control. Exact support plus complete multiplicative same-base phase structure cancels under Haar random cross-prime phases, and VIS-309 shows that the actual deterministic time orbit reproduces the same quadratic Haar mean over long time. VIS-310 says the next issue is not simply “make time finite,” but retain enough coefficient-weighted ratio geometry to avoid a worst-gap proof artifact.

Nyman--Beurling shows that preserving sign through faithful transport is necessary but not sufficient, but also that the retained arithmetic spectrum can matter once generic analyticity is exhausted. NB-223 obtains a positive source theorem from absolute zero-residue population bounds; at the exact Ford endpoint the natural unresolved object is the **signed/complex weighted zero sum itself**, where phase cancellation may outperform counting.

Weil Positivity shows the same issue through Hilbert closure. The critical `n^-1/2` Whittaker amplitude is visible before positivity at `Y_n=c/n`, but ordinary positive `L^2` immediately squares it and diverges. WP-370 adds that exact diagonal Hecke naturality itself forces this factorized ray. A viable sign mechanism must therefore live in a richer cross-index object or in a justified breaking of that diagonal covariance before scalarization.

Prime Lattice adds a different positive-logarithm example. Venturini's conjugate symmetrization can convert a nonlinear completely multiplicative source law into a zero-free theorem, and PL-370 shows that local pole neutrality already drives the prime axes into a Liouville neighbourhood. Yet the critical continuation remains the hard input. A valid sign/positivity interface still needs an independently obtainable continuation theorem.

These examples sharpen the same interface: useful nonpositivity or positivity must occur at the stage where the source-specific relation is still present, survive required transport, avoid a coherent finite inverse or destination-complete positive anchor, respect the physical source lattice and covariance laws, and depend on an input whose quantifier and analytic domain are not already equivalent to the desired conclusion.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the admissible source class and topology, observation family and nullspace, destination quotient and norm, source-coordinate map, requested precision, approximation/parameter class, analytic domain, moving-complexity coordinates, discrete source lattice, relevant notion of exceptional-set size, invariant recovery coordinate, theorem-admission range, source-frame extremizer class, localization/transport duality, transported analytic carrier, singularity spectrum, finite inverse, positive anchors, parity/automorphism structure, covariance/naturality constraints, sign/phase data, finite-time averaging window, weighted frequency geometry, scale/index coupling, target automorphism group and matched control.

Then ask in sequence: **what is genuinely new at acquisition; what unavoidable closure or projection follows; what survives the destination quotient; what ambient theorem already explains the critical scale; does the richer parameterization change the consumed norm/certificate or only re-coordinate it; does a nonnegative coordinate or coherently invertible signed transform already encode the destination; if transform complexity grows, what forward and inverse costs does it pay; does continuous tuning hit the actual source lattice; are sharp ambient extremizers admissible and quantitatively sharp after source pullback; if source-adapted structure obeys a naturality equation, does that equation itself force a trivial/factorized coordinate; if continuation is required, what source class does local pole neutrality actually allow and is further continuation independently proved; if a signed analytic carrier survives transport, what source-specific spectral theorem excludes generic extremizers; and does a matched control reproduce the survivor at the same pointwise or finite-window quantifier rather than only asymptotically?**

The current synthesis narrows rather than proliferates directions. Richer representation is not enough when a quotient, parity covariance, Fock functor or covariantly transported metric removes the new parameter. Favorable signed preprocessing is not enough when both its forward cost and physical inverse remain subpower. A smooth or residual saddle is not enough when complete packet resummation still leaves a shrinking integer hit. A sharp ambient source-frame obstruction is not decisive when the exact source image forces an explicit stronger threshold. Exact prime-power support and multiplicative phase structure are not enough when the actual deterministic orbit is Haar-typical in long-time quadratic mean, while a worst-gap finite-time reduction can manufacture irrelevant polynomial losses. A complex or exact-remainder single-center predictor is not richer when the fixed architecture still pays exponent two. Generic strip analyticity is not the end of an arithmetic carrier when the actual zero spectrum excludes its matched extremizer, as NB-223 now demonstrates. And a critical Whittaker half-density is not enough when the intrinsic Hecke covariance of diagonal sampling forces the unique scale that freezes the mixed coordinate.
