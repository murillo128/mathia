## Measure only structure that survives the final quotient, elimination, and matched controls

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-preserve-structure-before-compressing`, `MI-002-the-surviving-arithmetic-variable-is-relational`, `MI-003-arithmetic-significance-begins-after-universal-control-subtraction`, `MI-007-survival-requires-quantitative-non-escape-not-just-nonzero-structure`.

Prime Circle now has a genuine growing carrier: PC-215 proves that squarefree mixed-composite mask compression has tensor locality `omega(N)-1`. But PC-216 shows that the most immediate coefficient transfer across omitted primes is a commuting divisor-cube coboundary, so phase/holonomy and weighted-permutation eigenvalues still collapse to classical structure. The surviving incidence must be collective and non-coboundary, not merely high-body.

Möbius Cancellation classifies a complementary topology boundary. MC-144--MC-146 show that bounded Bost--Connes diagonals, fixed rational bands, and even the full countable rational-band profile remain profinite under continuous scalarization. MC-147 shows that unrestricted strong/weak completion overshoots completely: the coefficient closure contains every bounded diagonal target and the full represented algebra closes to all bounded operators. A useful category must therefore be rich enough to retain the relation but restrictive enough not to become target-complete.

Prime Lattice gives the temperature analogue. PL-216 makes the canonical high-temperature direction infinitesimally singular at every `beta<=1`, while PL-217 shows that arbitrary continuous scalar temperature profiles can already be approximated using one prime. Visual Exploration supplies the order analogue: VIS-096 fixes signature levels through three by local edge data, VIS-097 proves that three-dimensional level four can genuinely retain cycle order, and VIS-098 shows that the exact block-preserving null can nevertheless collapse to a singleton when delay states are distinct.

The common requirement is no longer simply “retain more structure.” The retained relation must survive the exact quotient actually consumed downstream, resist matched programmability, and live in a nondegenerate comparison class.

## Test coercivity after source optimization and downstream shorting, not on a convenient raw representative

**Linked intuition:** `MI-008-exact-recovery-is-not-stable-transport`.

Arithmetic Fidelity, Prime Flute, Analytic Frontier, and Xi Flow now expose four versions of the same adversarial optimization. AF-199 rewrites the minimal-support moment-flat source problem as a variable-knot Peano B-spline derivative-norm extremal. PF-218--PF-220 show that raw low-Schur damping fails after shorting even though coefficient-adjacent low terms are already weak trace.

PF-221 then identifies the correct optimized transfer variable: with reciprocal-prime weight `Omega`, the remaining cross-frequency coefficient transport splits into an automatically weak-trace term plus the Schur commutator `P[D,Omega]H`. Same-module mixing cancels exactly, and the block-Jacobi precision sees raw hopping only after multiplication by adjacent reciprocal-prime differences. The destination therefore asks for a commutator bound, not generic inverse locality.

ANF-112--ANF-113 show that packet aggregation and normalization by the packet's own source mass remove both internal recycling and source-norm blowup. A sparse packet avoids the height cascade only by moving toward square-root population, diffuse geometry, or exponential source-capacity deficit. Xi Flow pushes the same discipline one step further: XF-110--XF-112 force negative Toeplitz witnesses to be dense and concentrated on a tiny symbol set, but XF-113 constructs a Fejer-well matched control passing all those coarse gates while remaining negative. The optimized resource is therefore symbol geometry at the actual Toeplitz resolution, not bad-set measure alone.

The general discipline is now stronger: perform the admissible optimization, shorting, aggregation, commutator reduction, or resolution-matched witness test first, then measure the resource on the resulting object.

Prime Flute has reduced its smooth-interface problem to one exact operator target: the reciprocal-prime Schur commutator `P[D,Omega]H`. The natural precision identity inserts adjacent reciprocal-prime differences before block-Jacobi hopping, so the next theorem should estimate that weighted commutator rather than infer locality from the raw hopping matrix.

In each line, the return map must be proved in the exact category and physical/resolution scale consumed by the destination theorem.

“Not ruled out” remains only a frontier condition. Progress requires the replacement stability, variance, coercivity, source-admission, transport, order, incidence, resolution, or category-selection theorem in the new regime.
