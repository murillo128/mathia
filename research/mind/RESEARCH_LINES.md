# Global Mathia research lines

This file holds genuinely cross-branch mathematical questions suggested by the durable Mathia intuitions. It is not a summary of the local minds, a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they discriminate among program-level mechanisms.

## Characterize the minimal information-preserving quotient and the correct order of operations

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-preserve-structure-before-compressing`.

For a candidate chain from arithmetic data through geometry and operators to spectral, positivity, or zeta data, determine the coarsest equivalence relation through which the desired arithmetic distinction actually factors **before** optimizing, diagonalizing, taking a determinant, or proving positivity. Full injectivity is unnecessary, and high rank is not sufficient: the surviving directions must also escape classical character/refinement data that already determine them.

The decisive object is the fiber and determination structure of each canonical transformation. Current examples include pointed circulant classicalization, path-independent Schur refinement, maximal-rank old/new carriers that still decompose into fixed character data, inner factors invisible to Gram geometry, realized single-profile countermodels after Fenchel/packing improvements, and positive completions that erase a finite birth selector. A candidate fails this line when the target distinction is already constant on one of those fibers or fixed by the classical package; better optimization downstream cannot repair that loss.

## Distinguish local relational memory from exact global arithmetic placement

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-the-surviving-arithmetic-variable-is-relational`.

Resolved multi-gap eigenvalues show that directed relation-between-relation memory can survive local geometric spectralization. But all-composite prime-flute clones and Beurling prime-lattice controls show that relational richness alone does not imply arithmetic specificity. Determine what global normalization, target, norm assignment, or completion couples such local memory to the exact rational-prime system rather than to a large equivalence class of matched controls.

A decisive mechanism should pass a two-stage test: it must first retain the ordered local variable after natural gauge reduction, and then distinguish a control that preserves that local structure while changing the global arithmetic source. A feature that passes only the first test is a useful coordinate, not yet an RH selector.

## Make matched controls as strong as the claimed information layer

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-arithmetic-significance-begins-after-universal-control-subtraction`.

Do not stop at regular lattices when a proposal uses deeper structure. Use geometric all-composite clones for tail invariants, representation-matched periodic/off-lattice controls for collapsed Gram mechanisms, finite-prefix and prime-free localized controls for exponent-lattice mechanisms, and topology/pairing-matched controls when the proposed theorem is an operator or positivity statement. The mathematical goal is to identify the first control depth at which an invariant changes and verify that this depth matches the mechanism's claimed source of arithmetic information.

A canonical relative/subtracted object is especially valuable when the reference is independently forced by the geometry. But subtraction is not itself evidence: if two inputs share all data consumed by the proposed invariant in the stated topology, equality on them is a no-go, while survival under a strong control is only a prerequisite for further novelty and RH audits.

## Separate critical-axis generation from zero selection

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-critical-axis-is-not-zero-selection`.

Several branches independently generate distinguished analytic boundaries or spectrally real carriers, including Tate's unitary self-dual `Re(s)=1/2`, Hilbert/Schatten membership thresholds, prime-torus measure-class and Fisher boundaries, prime-free localized Weil spectral reality, PNT-controlled boundary limits, and universal cusp/propagation exponents. Some structures even recover the correct finite Mangoldt/Weil coefficients. Determine what additional canonical mechanism could turn such a carrier into a **selector** that eliminates off-axis zero configurations rather than merely reflecting or re-encoding them.

The decisive test is a theorem whose sign, totality, defect elimination, or rigidity is independent of the already-known zero divisor and genuinely uses the distinguished carrier. Re-deriving functional-equation symmetry, a convergence wall, real finite-stage characteristic functions, a PNT boundary model, or correct first-order Euler coefficients without such a selector does not advance the RH implication.

## Identify a canonical operator topology in which arithmetic survives without becoming an essential artifact

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-005-topology-is-part-of-the-arithmetic-claim`.

Prime Lattice now gives an exact topology split: fixed-window strong limits erase moving arithmetic states or universalize to a PNT rank-one model, while norm/Calkin topology retains essential prime-log recurrence and infinite-multiplicity threshold reflections. Prime Flute blocks first-resolvent trace class locally but leaves compact/`S_p`, `p>1`, and heat/wave/scattering comparison open. Prime Circle and Weil Positivity show parallel failures of regular compact-resolvent and ordinary Hilbert-form completions; in particular PC-069--PC-070 rule out ordinary compact-resolvent solenoid Hamiltonians with exact scalar affine dilation covariance.

The live program-level question is whether Mathia forces a **relative, mesoscopic, Schatten, distributional, or other topology** in which the universal background can be subtracted and a stable arithmetic residual remains, without choosing the category post hoc to expose a preferred singularity. A decisive result should derive the topology from the geometry, prove convergence/compactness or a spectral-shift/sign theorem there, and pass the strongest matched controls. A general no-go showing that every intrinsic candidate falls into universal weak limits or essential non-Fredholm recurrence would be equally decisive.
