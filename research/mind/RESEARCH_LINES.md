# Global mathematical questions

This file records mathematical questions that only become visible by comparing several current Mathia research lines. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does a frozen capacity bound become a genuine source discriminator?

Visual Exploration and Weil Inertia expose a common gate in very different categories. VIS-405 proves that a pre-fixed numerical Lipschitz budget on a monotone latent decoder is equivalent to a concrete uniform mass floor on the observable residual law, and VIS-406 turns ordinary fixed representation budgets into explicit examples. VIS-407 adds the invariance test: a numerical regularity bound in a singular source chart can be a coordinate artifact even when the intrinsic source path is regular.

VIS-408 now resolves the direct Wang handoff negatively. The actual final Wang packet class is infinite-dimensional and has no packet-uniform derivative, finite-basis, degree or Lipschitz budget, while its variational cosine optimizer is a scalar zero-pair kernel with no proved source-to-qutrit response dictionary. Thus the missing object is not a better decoder estimate inside the same packet interface. It is a **source-derived response map with a target-independent capacity bound**. Until that map exists, qutrit decoder capacity is an externally imposed representation choice rather than arithmetic evidence.

WI-417--WI-420 supply the geometric analogue once a two-dimensional source-density cap `B` is independently fixed: the critical reciprocal-depth Blaschke crossover is an exact Poisson capacity with a finite-dimensional dual and bang-bang extremizers. WI-421 shows that generic regularity is not enough because TV, Carleson and vanishing-Carleson control tolerate target-adaptive atoms at bounded local cost. WI-422 sharpens the source-space boundary again. For the translated-Blaschke kernel, the bare global response is bounded on `L^p` exactly for `1<p<2`; `p=2` is only weak-`L^2`, `p>2` is defeated by diffuse far-field mass, and `p=1` by local concentration. The endpoint `L^(2,1)` works, while a second finite-TV budget can restore coercivity for every `p>1`. A frozen norm is therefore useful only when it controls the actual local and far-tail singularities of the destination kernel.

Farey Discrepancy supplies the population analogue. FD-292--FD-293 show that the phase side is already aggregate and that any successful reciprocal-log occupation certificate requires a near-linear population of remainder-good zero gaps. FD-294 then converts suitable safe occupation in the complete-packet regime into near-linear many short complete zero-packet cancellations. FD-295 shows that below square-root height those complete packets are automatically capacity-subcritical. The remaining scarcity is therefore not generic packet capacity: it is **source occupation plus completeness** in the regime where the cancellation mechanism is valid.

Across the three lines, a numerical capacity, norm, packet budget or exceptional-set estimate becomes a source discriminator only when three things are proved in the same model: the source fixes the admissible resource before target inspection, that resource controls the destination's actual singular or population failure mode, and the physical source is forced to occupy or violate the resulting constraint. VIS-408, WI-422 and FD-294--FD-295 are useful precisely because they separate these stages rather than treating “finite capacity” as a source theorem.

## Which dependence survives normalization, rank/frame collapse and equilibrium scalarization?

Möbius Cancellation now gives an exact progression of increasingly intrinsic first-exit variables. MC-487 shows that the visible inverse shifts `h q^(-1)` are affine gauge. MC-488 proves that the exact progression-plus-cutoff first-exit cells form a disjoint least-prime partition, so a common transform cannot manufacture cross-`q` incidence. MC-489 then shows that even a genuinely `q`-dependent row dilation retains a `p^(1/2-o(1))` complete-row diagonal under coefficient-blind positive `L^2` closure. The surviving Möbius resource must therefore be load-bearing arithmetic asymmetry—kernel, coefficient or modulus structure in which different first-exit labels occupy different roles **before** positive closure—not another coordinate version of the same rows.

Prime Circle reaches a stronger source-blindness result after descendant scalarization. PC-414 shows that every source-independent finite-cutoff conductor tensor pulls back through the rank-one descendant channel to a universal conductor contraction times source amplitudes. PC-415 closes the natural infinite-frame continuation: in the trace-class regime the full descendant frame is still governed by the universal degree-two conductor symbol, and arbitrary Mellin phase changes of the base source act only by unitary conjugacy. PC-416 then breaks that phase gauge with the canonical log-scale position operator but shows that every finite-order extension still closes on a finite Mellin jet of the same one-shell source plus derivatives of universal conductor symbols. Richer finite operator algebra therefore does not restore interaction discarded before the shell-2 scalar reduction.

Weil Positivity adds the equilibrium version. WP-416 shows that under common generalized range, KMS dynamics locks distinct canonical source supports to distinct time degrees and orthogonalizes them. WP-417 removes the most canonical varying-range escape: for `mu_m mu_n^*`, KMS scalarization blocks by the ratio `m/n`, and every fixed-ratio block normalizes to the same GCD/Poisson Gram `(gcd(k,l)/sqrt(k l))^beta`, recovering the already-audited critical Gram at `beta=1`. Genuine source/range variation before scalarization can therefore still collapse to universal incidence afterwards.

Nyman--Beurling shows the complementary positive possibility: a collapse can leave an intrinsic defect that remains quantitatively nonzero. NB-306 identifies the exact unit singular core and its sharp end at `2m=N`; NB-307 identifies the post-core residual-Gram pencil; NB-308 supplies a canonical reciprocal pivot floor; and NB-309 replaces the exponentially tiny product certificate by a direct polynomial post-core singular-gap lower bound. Even there, the destination theorem still requires the actual first-free datum to occupy the useful singular directions.

The reusable cross-line question is therefore: **after every lawful normalization and the actual destination scalarization, what source-dependent quantity is still invariant and load-bearing?** Positive evidence must distinguish a surviving canonical defect from coordinate gauge, finite one-shell jets or universal Gram structure, and then prove destination occupation/alignment. MC-488--MC-489, PC-415--PC-416 and WP-417 are adversarial controls against nominally richer representations; NB-309 is the counterexample showing that a genuine quantitative defect can survive when the representation isolates it canonically.

## What replaces a scalar summary when its leading normalization degenerates?

Arithmetic Fidelity now gives a three-stage answer in one rigid positive transport family. AF-523 shows that throughout the shrinking layer `0<t<=1/A`, source participation can vanish while the first three normalized moment layers share one factor and the complete **leading** relative-curvature profile becomes prime-like. AF-524 identifies the leading-order re-entry: when the retained zeroth moment de-locks from the tail-dominated first and second moments, the curvature ratio becomes informative on the scale `log(1/t)/(C_A log log A)`.

AF-525 then shows that “leading-order blindness” is not the same as absence of all local information. Inside the AF-523 blind layer, the first nonzero curvature defect satisfies a uniform law of size `C_A log log A/log(1/t)` and asymptotically recovers the transport parameter `C_A`. This is a real provenance signal for the chosen control family, but not yet a prime-source discriminator: a parameter describing how one matched control was built may itself be matchable by a broader source class. The next stable object is therefore not simply “more scales” but the **smallest asymptotic jet that survives the control family**, together with a proof that the source class constrains that jet uniquely enough to matter.

Prime Circle provides an instructive negative analogue of the word “jet.” PC-416 shows that a finite Mellin jet can be perfectly source-sensitive in the narrow sense of detecting the phase erased by PC-415 while still adding no new arithmetic mechanism: for the prime-circle shell it is just classical Gamma/zeta/local-factor differential data, and an arbitrary matched base profile supplies its own jet in the same operator architecture. A recovered derivative is informative only if its source class makes that derivative non-reproducible by controls.

Weil Inertia supplies the dual source-category warning. WI-420 gives the correct joint target object—the Poisson capacity of a scaled defect configuration—but WI-421--WI-422 show that its coercivity depends sharply on the source space. Excluding atoms is not sufficient; the source norm must also price the diffuse far tail in the dual geometry, or be paired with another budget such as total variation.

The combined question is whether other Mathia destinations admit an exact **replacement object at degeneration**—a lower-order asymptotic jet, joint capacity, singular occupation measure or another configuration-level invariant—whose informative component survives matched controls and whose admissible source class is independently forced. AF-525 warns against upgrading a recovered control parameter to source identity; PC-416 warns against upgrading a recovered finite derivative to a new mechanism; WI-422 warns against upgrading absolute continuity or a generic norm to coercivity. A useful replacement object needs both destination completeness at the stated scale and a source theorem that prevents the same object from being reproduced by an admissible control.
