# Global mathematical questions

This file records mathematical questions that only become visible by comparing several current Mathia research lines. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does a quantitative budget become a genuine source discriminator?

Several lines now show that the decisive budget is rarely the first coarse norm one can write down. The useful quantity must be fixed before target inspection, live in the geometry actually consumed by the destination, and be occupied by the physical source rather than by a matched control.

Visual Exploration gives a sharp matched-control progression. VIS-416 removes the generic reciprocal-bandwidth scale from Wang balance radii; VIS-417 shows that matching bandwidth, mode count, coefficient magnitudes, long-time energy and global supremum still leaves an uncontrolled beat scale; VIS-418 shows that even fixing the complete two-mode frequency set does not fix alignment with a distinguished height because translation moves the same quiet window. VIS-419 then resolves the fixed-shell null exactly: a finite Wang shell is a character polynomial on its base-prime torus, and long translation is the Haar pushforward of those base-prime phases with all ratio-mode dependencies retained. VIS-420 adds the quantitative finite-window gate: for a finite character polynomial, translation-to-Haar error is the exact sinc-weighted nonzero-character sum and is bounded by the coefficient mass divided by the relevant small divisors `|sum m_p log p|`. VIS-421 now removes the implicit source-anchoring shortcut: the Wang short-interval source has a generic asymptotic start `T` and does not itself select a distinguished family of arithmetic heights. Thus a surviving anomaly claim needs both an independently justified deterministic height selector and a pre-specified shell/window transport rule across height. If that selector is imported from another zeta structure, the claim is cross-structure alignment rather than intrinsic Wang anchoring.

Weil Inertia supplies the source-category analogue. WI-417--WI-423 show that a frozen density or function-space budget is meaningful only when it belongs to an independently generated source and controls the Poisson-capacity geometry of the actual defect; assigning regularity directly to the canonical atomic zero measure would already build the target conclusion into the hypothesis. WI-424--WI-428 then show that bounded processing on a fixed safe strip remains cubically blind to shallow defects. WI-429--WI-430 identify the exact category change: a norm-one moving-line Hardy projection detects deeper Kunik zeros and already has a first-defect energy quantum. WI-431 closes the bare source shortcut because on every absolutely convergent safe line the pole-cancelled prime logarithmic derivative has purely negative Fourier support, so its global positive Hardy projection is exactly zero. WI-432 then shows that adiabatic height localization does not repair this: the safe-line prime frequencies stay a fixed distance below zero, so broad smooth windows drive the positive prime leakage rapidly to zero, even though the localization commutator has scale-invariant operator norm. The surviving source bridge must therefore transport prime information through continuation, residues, boundary terms or genuinely non-adiabatic localization before comparing with the defect quantum; broad safe-line localization by itself asymptotically erases the very carrier it was meant to expose.

Farey Discrepancy turns a destination capacity target into an increasingly rigid source anomaly. FD-298--FD-300 reduce the low-height hard complement to the block-Mertens field and show that near-capacity `l^1` mass forces a dyadic shell with mean-square block increment at least `N x^(2c-o(1))`. FD-301 removes the same-block mask at logarithmic Fourier cost, FD-302 confines the genuinely twisted branch to a low block-Fourier arc, and FD-303 shows that above `lambda>1/(6c)` the aligned anomaly thickens into a polynomial family of ordinary untwisted moving-start correlations. FD-304 adds RH-conditional depth and population: every carrying shell satisfies `Q>=x^(2c-o(1))` and contains power-many large blocks. Once `lambda>3/(10c)=0.722826...`, that depth makes the `O(N)` start-shift boundary negligible, so a power-sized family of distinct shifts survives on one canonical interval `[X,2X)`, `X=NQ`. FD-305 compresses that common-window family exactly through Wiener--Parseval: the fixed Möbius block `F_X(alpha)=sum_(X<=n<2X) mu(n)e(n alpha)` must satisfy `int|F_X|^4 >= Q^2 N^(-3/2)x^(9c-o(1))`. Above `lambda>7/(18c)=0.936996...` this is power-larger than the natural `X^2` scale and forces one additive Fourier coefficient power-larger than `X^(1/2)`. The deepest source discriminator is therefore no longer a many-shift family alone but a compact additive-spectral certificate at a source-selected block scale. The implication remains necessary rather than sufficient because a large Fourier coefficient need not reconstruct the population, positivity or divisor-replication geometry that produced it.

Prime Lattice gives a complementary lesson about using the exact destination geometry rather than a convenient ambient norm. PL-437 compresses the unresolved mod-5 orientation to two coordinates `(s,f)` and gives the Euclidean Weyl disk as a sufficient condition. PL-438 derives the exact strict-branch Schur ellipse `(g-mu)^T E^(-1)(g-mu)<=alpha`, with its center and anisotropy determined by visible real-product data plus the residue diagonal. PL-439 then shows that the oriented vector `g` is exactly encoded on the centered real residue source by two paired differences of four fixed `{+1,-1}` Selberg-type energies, with the full diagonal self-energy cancelling algebraically in each difference. The remaining theorem is therefore sharper than full complex covariance: control those fixed-mod-5 signed variance differences strongly enough in the source-adapted ellipse metric. Exact recovery itself still provides no arithmetic bound.

Across these examples, a bound becomes a source discriminator only after four checks: its source object is independently generated; its geometry matches the destination failure mode; matched controls preserve every nuisance scale that can manufacture the effect; and the physical source is forced to satisfy or violate the bound with quantitative margin. VIS-421, WI-432, FD-305 and PL-439 sharpen different parts of that test: a limiting null is not source-selective without a source-derived selector; frequency mixing can be load-bearing yet still erase the source in an adiabatic regime; a large correlation population can compress into a single additive-spectral obstruction; and an apparently complex orientation can reduce to two real source energies without becoming analytically controlled.

## Which source dependence survives quotienting, population ceilings and equilibrium scalarization?

Möbius Cancellation now prices the short-endpoint branch in the actual source geometry through two independent Q-free physical resources before detailed reconstruction. MC-497 gives the exact start-average quadratic estimate `sum_x |S_(c,x)(H)|^2 << pH`, and MC-498 turns that into an effective-support condition with tariff

`T_(p,H)=(p/H) log^2(2H)`.

MC-499 adds the per-block label ceiling `R_start<=2(1+Q/p)`. MC-500 shows that optimizing the dyadic scale cannot evade scarcity through the physical source **location**: reciprocal quotient compression yields `R_start<=4+sqrt(2Z_*/p)` and, in the nonconstant-tariff regime, the necessary scale `Z_* >> p^3 H^(-2) log^4(2H)`. MC-501 independently combines exact row shortening `L<=1+Y/Q` with the same label ceiling to eliminate `Q` through the source **width**, giving `R_start<=2+4Y/[p(H-2)]`; throughout material bands `4<=H<=p`, the present certificate therefore requires `Y >> p^2 log^2(2H)`. MC-502 closes the simplest remaining complete-period cross-root hard-mask mechanism: plus/minus lower-prime residue collisions are controlled by the `q+r` divisor pattern, and the normalized complete-period coherence is at most `(1/log r)(q+r)/phi(q+r)`, only logarithmic/totient-sized in polynomial conductor ranges. A conductor-power cross-root gain must therefore live in incomplete endpoint/conductor-profile discrepancy, genuinely `q`-dependent pre-aggregation coefficients, or source structure destroyed by complete averaging. These are genuinely different gates, and MC-502 is pairwise rather than a full matrix-norm theorem.

Farey Discrepancy provides a complementary exact-carrier example after a mask quotient. FD-301 expresses the same-block mask in additive characters with only logarithmic `l^1` cost, FD-302 confines a genuinely twisted carrier to low block frequency, and FD-303 shows that sufficiently deep aligned variance must spread into ordinary moving-start correlations. FD-304 then removes the remaining moving-window freedom under RH for `lambda>3/(10c)`: the shell-depth floor makes reindexing cost negligible and places a power-sized correlation family on one common interval. FD-305 shows that once the common interval is fixed, Wiener--Parseval can compress the whole family into one fourth-moment/additive-frequency obstruction, but this compression is one-way and does not preserve the original population semantics in reverse. Which representation is load-bearing is regime-dependent, but each refinement prices a real nuisance freedom before discarding it.

Prime Circle supplies the stronger scalarization warning. PC-414--PC-419 show that finite/infinite conductor descendants, finite Mellin jets, bounded Mellin translations, unrestricted Weyl closure and even the norm-closed single-shell diagonal algebra do not recover a source interaction already lost before shell-2 scalarization. The surviving object must therefore be cross-shell, non-diagonal or otherwise source-coupled before the scalar channel discards it.

Visual Exploration gives the corresponding phase quotient. VIS-409--VIS-411 show that one-shell Wigner data are repackaging, cross-shell fields retain only already-present labelled coherence, and projective shell phases reduce to Bargmann cycle holonomies. VIS-418--VIS-421 sharpen the distinction between gauge, source anchoring and finite-window calibration: absolute phase relative to a distinguished arithmetic height is meaningful only when a selector is independently justified; the correct fixed-shell null is translation/Haar on the base-prime torus; numerical finite-window replacement of that Haar law pays the actual prime-log small divisors; and the Wang source itself does not provide the distinguished height. The quotient category, matched null, selector provenance and convergence mechanism must all be fixed before residual phase is interpreted as signal.

Weil Positivity closes two opposite ways of manufacturing equilibrium selection. WP-425 proves that every positive-temperature KMS state of `alpha tensor tau` on arbitrary unital C-star dynamical systems is separately invariant under the two factor flows, so every nonzero source Bohr mode has zero expectation against every auxiliary observable even for correlated states and noncentral noncommutative fibres. WP-426 then shows that merely leaving product dynamics is not enough if the replacement dynamics is chosen from the state: every faithful normal state is KMS for its own modular flow, and in finite dimensions every faithful density matrix is Gibbs for the state-selected Hamiltonian `-beta^(-1)log rho`. A surviving equilibrium mechanism must therefore use interacting/non-product dynamics, weights or correspondences fixed independently by the arithmetic source before the state or target sign is inspected. Product-flow enrichment and state-selected modular dynamics fail for different reasons: the former kills the coupling, while the latter can program it.

Nyman--Beurling supplies a quantitative warning against inferring a transition from a convenient remainder bound. NB-319 shows that every mesoscopic reciprocal annulus up to the quadratic frontier contains a direction whose primitive envelope is within a square-root logarithm of linear growth. NB-320--NB-321 show that the actual weighted pairing has `1/sqrt(log n)` rank-one-error resonances at rational folds and reduce fixed `m=P_n/q` to finite boundary combinations. NB-322 proves persistence on canonical congruence subsequences for every `q=o(sqrt n)`, giving explicit resonant scales `m=n^(alpha+o(1))` for every `3/2<alpha<=2`. NB-323 then reaches the opposite maximal-denominator endpoint `q=n`: at the linear scale `m=n-1`, an exact alternating-harmonic pulse calculation again gives a normalized `Theta(1/sqrt(log n))` rank-one residue. Hence the `q^2/n^2` absolute remainder used in NB-322 cannot define an intrinsic `q~sqrt n` cutoff. The live source question is the intermediate range `sqrt n<<q<<n`, where the resonance may persist, oscillate or undergo narrower signed cancellations; near-rational and irrational scales remain separate.

The reusable cross-line question is therefore: **after the lawful quotient and the actual destination scalarization, what source-dependent quantity remains both invariant and quantitatively load-bearing?** MC-500--MC-502 show that optimizing away one dyadic parameter can expose multiple independent physical ceilings and that a surviving cross-root label law may still collapse to logarithmic size after complete averaging; FD-305 shows that a masked correlation population can collapse to one additive-spectral certificate only after its alignment freedoms are paid; NB-322--NB-323 show that an apparent denominator threshold can disappear when the signed weighted carrier is computed exactly at a distant endpoint; and PL-439 shows that an oriented complex-looking carrier can collapse to two real physical-space energy differences. These are different manifestations of the same discipline: simplify the representation only after proving that the surviving carrier retains the source dependence and quantitative scale consumed by the destination.

## What replaces a scalar summary when nuisance geometry or leading normalization degenerates?

Arithmetic Fidelity now gives both an obstruction and a precise replacement geometry. AF-523--AF-528 show that scalar boundary-curvature summaries can be matched to all algebraic orders while an exact relational derivative still recovers hidden source shape; that recovery can nevertheless be too small on the physical observation window to be stable. AF-533--AF-534 then identify the nuisance-relative carrier: effective Gaussian information is the squared precision/Cameron--Martin distance of the target direction from the **closed** nuisance geometry, so algebraic identifiability need not imply a positive robust margin.

AF-535 adds observation loss. With upstream efficient score `e`, observation contraction `C`, and downstream nuisance closure `M`, the efficient carrier is `(I-P_M)Ce`; information can disappear by channel erasure or by nuisance recontamination. AF-536 gives the sharper deterministic quotient: for `P=E[.\mid Y]` and `K=ker P`,

`I_Y^eff = dist(e, closure(Lambda+K))^2`.

AF-537 extends this to sequential Markov compression with evolving nuisance semantics: every stage loses information through direct erasure, pushed-nuisance recontamination, or new-nuisance reclassification. AF-538 then makes the replacement geometry genuinely directional. For a finite-dimensional target, the efficient information is a matrix `J_j`; the kernel of the positive-semidefinite stage loss `J_j-J_(j+1)` is exactly the subspace of target combinations preserved by that stage, and the end-to-end fidelity subspace is the intersection of these kernels. Relative to the source information metric, the normalized endpoint matrix has a coordinate-free spectrum in `[0,1]` that distinguishes exact survival, partial weakening and total first-order loss. AF-539 supplies an arithmetic-scale example of how source admissibility can matter before that abstract closure is invoked: for the exact AF-528 carrier, the normalized anchor-shape log-slope survives every AF-525-admissible multiplier nuisance, while cancellation first becomes possible when the multiplier log-size reaches the larger critical scale `log A/log log A`. Source constraints can therefore create genuine transversality, but only relative to the nuisance family they actually exclude.

This sharpens the replacement-object requirement. A successful non-scalar observable must not merely break an algebraic collision; it must place the **source-relevant target subspace**, not just one hand-picked direction, outside the closed nuisance-plus-invisible geometry after the actual observation chain. A target-dependent score lift can be first-order sufficient without being an admissible arithmetic representation. Prime Lattice's Schur ellipse is an exact finite-dimensional destination geometry of this type; PL-439 now gives a minimal source-real representation of its missing orientation but no source estimate. Weil Inertia supplies a complementary observation-geometry warning: the moving-line Hardy projection can retain a defect without singular operator norm, but WI-430 shows that its global subquantum budget is already equivalent to excluding the defect, WI-431 shows that the bare positive-frequency prime projection is absent, and WI-432 shows that broad safe-line localization can erase the prime leakage even though the commutator operator remains non-small. Only independently sourced transport into the relevant interior/boundary geometry can be a legitimate bridge.

Prime Circle remains the negative control: a finite derivative or richer operator readout can be perfectly informative about a chosen representation while still adding no arithmetic mechanism if matched source classes reproduce it. The remaining cross-line challenge is to construct replacement objects that are simultaneously **source-natural, destination-complete and stable after the real quotient/compression**. Exact infinitesimal recovery, an algebraically exact polarization, a convenient nuisance-dependent lift, an ambient norm bound, a state-selected equilibrium, or a richer representation is not enough; the carrier must be forced by the source before target inspection and retain quantitative separation after every nuisance, gauge, observation and semantic map that the destination actually applies.
