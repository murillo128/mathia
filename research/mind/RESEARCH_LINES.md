Exterior, symmetric, tensor and highest-weight lifts therefore cannot recover source information lost after the ordinary finite spectral quotient. Farey Discrepancy remains the strongest constructive example of useful pre-norm structure, but FD-206 adds a circularity audit. The shared question is: **what new source-specific coordinate is acquired; at what stage can it be lost by quotient, representation functor, norm or continuation; does one positive coordinate already encode the destination-complete problem; which ambient Banach/Hardy geometry explains the apparent critical scale; what signed carrier remains after faithful transport; and which matched control preserves everything except the proposed arithmetic coherence?** Richer data deserve credit only after those gates survive.

## Can sign-indefinite structure preserve critical information before closure or norm compression scalarizes it?

Weil Positivity remains the strongest positive-closure warning from both directions. Exact common homogeneous scale treatment factorizes the finite--archimedean incidence; bounded fixed-scale positivity preserves the incidence but exponentially kills its high-index carrier. A surviving positive route must use cross-index or index-dependent scale coupling, controlled unboundedness, a source-forced boundary/remainder sector or another sign theorem outside those componentwise classes.

Prime Lattice brackets signed filtering even more sharply. Leaving the positive cone is necessary to cross one convergence wall, but finite signs are periodic, unrestricted infinite signs are programmable and `ell^1` signs are limit-periodic. Pole-neutral `ell^p` signs manufacture arbitrary sharp walls, and canonical scalar Hardy classes manufacture a generic half-wall. Useful nonpositivity must therefore be source-forced beyond both ambient geometries.

Farey Discrepancy gives a constructive and a cautionary example. Differences before the positive norm remove shared bulk and improve source cost, but FD-206 shows that the current final norm still pays one RH-equivalent root anchor positively. Sign placement helps only if it acts **before** the hard coordinate becomes a standalone nonnegative cost.

Visual Exploration supplies the complementary matched control. Even with exact prime-power support and coefficient magnitudes, independent phase randomization makes the signed endpoint form `o_p(H)`. Useful nonpositivity must therefore retain a source-specific deterministic phase relation rather than merely keep the signed quadratic form syntactically intact.

Nyman--Beurling gives the analytic analogue. Keeping primitive and reconstruction kernel coupled avoids the absolute reconstruction tariff, and NB-221 shows this coupling can survive local transport back to `-zeta'/zeta`. The unresolved question is now whether cancellation can be exploited inside that transported signed carrier rather than replacing it by a pointwise absolute envelope.

These examples do not transfer estimates across lines, but they sharpen the interface: useful nonpositivity must occur before the scalarizing operation, be forced by the source, survive required transport, avoid hiding a destination-complete positive anchor, avoid being reproduced or canceled by a matched control, and remain distinct after the destination's symmetry and representation quotients are applied.

Positive finite--archimedean incidence is not enough when globalization erases it or fixed bounded scale extinguishes the arithmetic tail.
