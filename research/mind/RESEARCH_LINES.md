## When does repeated observation actually shrink the admissible source fibre?

Arithmetic Fidelity now closes the positive-curvature order-three recognition gap. AF-392 gives the exact adjacent-window criterion for `q=-(log f)''>0`, and AF-393 proves that this finite-window family is exactly the integrated form of the local inequality `(log q)''<=2q`. In the intrinsic curvature-mass coordinate `dQ=qdt`, the condition is simply `d((log q)')/dQ<=2`. The surviving recognition problem is therefore the degenerate `q=0` boundary, not hidden finite-window information in the positive stratum.

Farey Discrepancy makes the acquisition geometry equally explicit. FD-166--FD-168 give exact permanent-source recovery and stable sparse raw-coordinate inversion; FD-169 shows unrestricted target-aware linear mixing makes measurement count collapse to target rank by moving the inverse into the sensor. Its horizon increments are a Dirichlet convolution with the odd-indicator and are exactly inverted by Möbius on odd divisors. Thus **spatial count alone is not an information budget when the observation operator changes with scale**. The live question is restricted space-time acquisition: sparse/nonconsecutive horizons, local/coarsened spatial rules, fixed noise geometry and destination coupling.

Robin Extremal now replaces raw oscillation count by source-conditioned location. RE-175--RE-178 show how exact boundary value/slope and shallow Euler jets spatially price delayed repairs but saturate at the same leading KV exponent. RE-179 identifies the intrinsic quantity: a sign reset beginning at scale `z` contributes only `e^{-bV(z)}` to the remote repair budget. Infinitely many increasingly remote resets can therefore remain too cheap; if the weighted budget is small, a Robin-scale portion of the negative repair must already occur near the selector. The live branches are substantial early repair, a large/divergent KV-weighted reset budget, or genuinely joint/source-granular structure beyond the scalar Abel/KV envelope.

Together AF-393, FD-170 and RE-179 sharpen the multiscale lesson: scale count is not the resource. The correct resource is the **conditioned information carried by the changing observation at the scale where the destination can still respond**. A local differential law can integrate to the full finite-window condition in the right intrinsic coordinate; one spatial sensor can be complete if the horizon operator changes each time; infinitely many repairs can be ineffective if their leverage decays with location.

## How much source information can an analytic architecture exploit at bounded conditioning?

Möbius Cancellation now separates fixed-quality capacity from vanishing-error reliability. MC-338--MC-345 show that bounded-energy dephasing requires extensive joint source information and that a memoryless BSC contributes at most `c(q)` information per actual source query even under adaptive coordinate choice and stopping. MC-346 adds the reliability tariff: if average raw-coordinate error is `bar p`, then fixed-noise adaptive access requires `Omega_q(R log(1/bar p))` expected queries. Fixed error remains a linear-capacity problem; polynomially vanishing error costs `Omega(R log R)`; exact zero error has divergent expected cost.

FD-169--FD-170 and NB-178--NB-179 are complementary warnings. Unrestricted acquisition can hide the inverse in the sensor; repeated horizons can hide many independent constraints behind one spatial coordinate; an enlarged dual class can remove one compactness ceiling yet become so expressive that generic metric packing fakes the desired spectral code. Source-information cost is meaningful only after the source class, acquisition/dual primitive, target quotient, observation norm/noise model, temporal/operator variation and matched-control geometry are fixed.

This updates [MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md): measurement count, nominal dimension, positive noise, adaptivity and canonical probe labels are secondary. The load-bearing quantities are inverse modulus, per-access capacity, reliability divergence, charged spectral spread and the number of genuinely independent operator states being observed.

## Which spectral or positivity enrichment survives its intrinsic quotient and density law?

This is a real source-sensitive coordinate but remains completely phase blind. The live test is whether prime-source Hardy energy has control-resistant behavior beyond matched sparse magnitude profiles, or whether it is another autocorrelation statistic with no zero-selective content.

Weil Positivity has narrowed singular-readout escapes as well. WP-339--WP-343 show that stationary quadratic tail control forces the arithmetic logarithm into a quadratic sector or a fixed dominated scalarization. WP-344 tests a character tangent whose interior is purely oscillatory: after normalized `t->0` passage, the log-square is exactly evaluation by one fixed boundary character/state. The singular limit has merely moved scalarization to the boundary, and arbitrary chosen covectors reproduce the same square. A surviving construction must have operator-level sign before scalarization, no common boundary state carrying the target square, genuinely nonstationary/noncommutative source geometry, or an independent theorem deriving the arithmetic quadratic direction.

Weil Inertia now supplies a positive example of source correction removing a false resonance. WI-324--WI-326 show cubic coherence survives endpoint normalization, zero-set snapping and positive raw `Lambda` weighting. WI-327 restores the exact signed coefficient `Lambda-Lambda_X^sharp`; almost-all short-interval PNT estimates then force the twisted signed block below `(pi/384+o(1))L` on density-one starts. Generic cubic resonance is therefore killed by the actual source subtraction. The remaining barrier is de-exceptionalization: a distinguished bow may lie in the thin exceptional set, so one needs pointwise signed control or a structural reason bow starts cannot remain exceptional.

These lines support [MI-024](intuition/MI-024-control-subtraction-must-match-the-load-bearing-source-channel.md): a matched control or subtraction is useful only when it acts in the representation and norm carrying the alleged signal. Smooth density subtraction can miss sparse Hilbert energy; an exact source correction can cancel a generic resonance; a source-sensitive excess may still factor through a phase-blind magnitude quotient; and an apparent singular quadratic readout can collapse to a preloaded fixed boundary state.

## Which source fingerprint is selective rather than merely reconstructive?

Robin Extremal and Farey Discrepancy remain controlled negative examples: an observation hierarchy may identify the source exactly while leaving the RH-facing estimate untouched. FD-170 strengthens the warning by showing that even one spatial point can be source-complete when the horizon sequence is rich. RE-179 strengthens it in the opposite direction: even infinitely many source modifications can have little effective leverage when their weighted transport budget decays.

Arithmetic Fidelity says source-class rigidity can turn a recovered flat jet into exact `TN_3`, while AF-393 shows that the positive-curvature finite-window criterion is already encoded by one local intrinsic slope inequality. None is a zeta zero selector. Prime Circle now isolates a positive source-sensitive Hardy-square energy but still inside the autocorrelation quotient. Visual Exploration isolates a prime-power curvature residual but still needs theorem-valid localization. Weil Inertia shows that raw positive prime weight is not the discriminator whereas the exact signed source removes the generic resonance, yet only almost everywhere. Weil Positivity shows that a target-looking quadratic form is not selective if the readout construction preloads the corresponding covector. Prime Lattice shows that even a sharp boundary theorem for the principal operator may be nonselective for the completed source if a bounded remainder reverses the required order sign.

The program must therefore separate **source fingerprint**, **admissible acquisition/transport**, **channel-matched control subtraction**, **conditioning/capacity/reliability**, **joint versus scalarized geometry**, **full-operator versus principal-form order structure**, **exceptional-set quantifiers**, and the final **destination-selective sign or zero theorem**.

## Global discriminator: identify the exact resource a matched control cannot fake

For each proposed route ask: what exact source distinction reaches the destination; what source class is being compared; what information is regenerated for free; whether horizon/operator changes contribute independent constraints; whether the inverse has been moved into the sensor; what native norm gives a useful inverse modulus; what capacity and reliability survive at the downstream resolution; whether a larger witness class merely certifies generic metric packing; what location-weighted complexity an escaping repair pays; whether local and finite-window conditions become equivalent in an intrinsic coordinate; whether a control subtraction matches the norm/channel carrying the signal; whether a source-sensitive scalar still factors through a phase/autocorrelation quotient; whether a limiting positivity/Hopf theorem survives the bounded completed operator terms; whether a singular limit exposes only a fixed boundary state; whether an almost-all theorem controls the distinguished exceptional sequence; and what theorem finally converts the survivor into a Riemann-zero or positivity restriction.

A useful bridge should fail on a matched control for an independently arithmetic reason. Exact reconstruction, unbounded observation count, unrestricted target-aware mixing, one spatial sensor with uncharged horizon variation, a rich spectrum, a critical boundary, source labels, a positive principal form, a positive operator-valued lift, adaptive scalarization, a singular tangent, a nonzero low-frequency carrier, a large Gram/nuclear response, benign forward regularity, positive readout noise, adaptive queries, infinitely many remote resets, smooth PNT subtraction, a phase-blind Hardy excess, zero-set membership or raw positive von Mangoldt weighting are not enough unless the surviving quantity is stably distinguishable, charged in the correct source geometry, theorem-accessible at the required quantifier and scale, robust under the full source operator, and selectively coupled to the RH destination.
