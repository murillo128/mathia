## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than another exact quotient or matched control?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit.

Several recent exact results sharpen it in different ways. FD-213 shows that even **all-integer** observation can remain blind when dilation preserves an unobserved mantissa fibre: a supercritical scale-index mode can be profile-lifted and stitched into one bounded source. PC-358 shows that arbitrary finite Hilbert geometry cannot break reciprocal control when the underlying algebraic parity still gives an exact similarity. PL-373 shows that infinite-alphabet freedom inside the Venturini class changes only a zero-free holomorphic gauge around the Liouville divisor. WP-372 shows the opposite phenomenon: allowing mixed-prime coordinates really does remove the divergent local positivity tax, but the resulting finite global completion is universal and therefore not source-selective.

The shared question is now precise: **which added coordinate changes the actual source/control equivalence class consumed by the destination?** More samples do not help if the operator remains fibrewise; more metric structure does not help if a bounded similarity survives; more coefficient freedom does not help if it is only a zero-free gauge; and more positive-completion freedom does not help if a universal product control reproduces it.

A proposed enriched representation should therefore identify the new source-selected coordinate, the exact equivalence relation it breaks, and a matched control that preserves all cheaper structure. If no such coordinate exists, the enrichment is a reparameterization, completion or conditioning change rather than a new theorem interface.

## Where has the next exact obstruction moved after the latest boundary results?

**Farey Discrepancy.** The frontier has moved past cross-ray additive coherence. FD-213 annihilates the selected switched filter at every integer with bounded increments. The next discriminator must be an arithmetic source law absent from that countermodel—multiplicativity, squarefree support or another relation that genuinely couples dyadic mantissas—or a sensor whose dilation dynamics mixes those fibres.

**Nyman--Beurling.** The frontier has moved past both fixed-power raw phase cancellation and every separable depth-only strengthening `M^vartheta/Q(D)`. NB-227 shows that a slowly diverging depth can diagonalize against any predetermined `Q`. The next input must couple admissible population, depth and phase nonseparably, constrain nested depth slabs jointly, or control the actual weighted residue.

**Möbius Cancellation.** The frontier has moved past asking only for linear source codimension. MC-393 combines the codimension/exception inequality with physical source incidence and forces logarithmic source cost `min{(1-eta)R,a_eta log log y}`. A surviving route must change the relation-cost dictionary, relax the exceptional budget without losing the destination theorem, or escape the fixed-power endpoint package.

**Prime Circle.** The frontier has moved past nonfactorizing finite metrics. PC-358 shows that every fixed finite Hilbertization preserves reciprocal similarity. A metric-only infinite-depth route must make parity unbounded, or the construction must change the domain/operator/control law before the algebraic conjugacy becomes a bounded similarity.

**Prime Lattice.** The frontier has moved past infinite-alphabet coefficient freedom inside the Venturini class. PL-373 fixes the full divisor to Liouville times a zero-free holomorphic gauge. A distinct mechanism must leave that scalar completely multiplicative witness class or supply a genuinely different global theorem interface.

**Robin Extremal.** The two apparent rate-two singular regimes have collapsed to one. RE-225 proves that contraction degeneration at fixed positive arc width is impossible in a fixed corridor. The only remaining positive-real single-center boundary is `alpha->0`, with the contraction corner additionally forcing `a->1/2`.

**Visual Exploration.** The cutoff-free Wang horizon has improved from a cubic/log-cubed shell tariff to `V >> x log^2 x/(log log x)^2` after VIS-314 retains prime-pair sparsity. The remaining gap to `V~x log log x` is only logarithmic. The next question is whether that residual factor is another aggregate-energy loss or a real exceptional-height packet.

**Weil Positivity.** The WP-371 divergent diagonal tax is no longer the global boundary. WP-372 proves that arbitrary mixed-prime completion has a finite sharp mass threshold. The live question is whether the arithmetic/archimedean source forces a non-universal mixed-prime coefficient pattern whose positivity and orientation survive the WP-372 product control.

Across these lines, continuing to optimize the previous coarse obstruction would attack a variable already proved nondecisive. The useful next step is to identify the newly exposed source relation, joint variable or quotient boundary exactly.

## Which physical cost survives once representation artifacts are removed?

Arithmetic Fidelity starts with provenance: a parameter must be source-selected before its mesh or shrinking-target membership matters. Nyman--Beurling prices localization by source width and then shows that separable depth/phase improvements can be diagonalized away. Möbius Cancellation prices relation codimension back through conductor. Visual Exploration replaces fictitious global frequency gaps by aggregate shell density and then prices the remaining sieve logarithms. Weil Positivity replaces a divergent sum of local completion costs by a finite global maximum once mixed coordinates are allowed.

These are different currencies and must not be collapsed. The reusable question is: **after the exact source specialization and the strongest representation cleanup, what quantity still has to be paid by the physical source rather than by a choice of coordinates, metric, cutoff, completion or normalization?**

For a proposed route, write that cost explicitly in the same variables consumed by the final theorem. If the apparent obstruction disappears after a more faithful global representation, it was a method artifact. If it survives source pullback and matched controls, it becomes a genuine mathematical bottleneck.

## Can sign-indefinite structure preserve critical information before closure scalarizes it?

Farey Discrepancy shows that ordered signed dynamics can create a genuine blind Floquet mode even when every instantaneous filter is harmless; FD-213 further shows that all-integer sampling does not kill it without fibre coupling. Nyman--Beurling shows that raw phase cancellation is irrelevant unless it survives horizontal damping in the weighted residue. Möbius Cancellation shows that random-looking source signs do not provide the needed fourth moment unless enough exact relations exist. Weil Positivity shows that a centered sign-indefinite prime-power carrier can be globally completed at finite mass only by introducing mixed-prime correlations; the open issue is whether arithmetic forces useful correlations rather than a universal control.

The shared interface is therefore not “use signs” or “use positivity.” It is to preserve a source-specific relation through the exact inverse/weight/completion step and show that its orientation cannot be replicated by the corresponding matched control.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the source class, parameter provenance, observation family and nullspace, destination quotient, exact inverse or ordered transfer, preserved fibre coordinates, metric covariance/similarity law, analytic divisor/gauge freedom, source mesh, population/depth coupling, relation codimension and conductor cost, finite-window spectral aggregation, global positive-completion class and destination quantifier.

Then ask: **what genuinely new source information is acquired; what exact equivalence or control does it break; and what physical cost remains after the most faithful representation is used?** A route that survives this audit has isolated a mathematical bottleneck. A route that fails it has usually rediscovered a provenance, fibrewise-inverse, similarity, gauge, separability, conductor, spectral-density or completion artifact in different notation.
