## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than merely transport, refactor or renorm the source?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) and [MI-028](intuition/MI-028-complete-measurement-families-can-close-an-escape-by-reconstructing-the-classical-object.md) remain the common interface audit.

New leverage has to enter before that parity-covariant spectral quotient, retain non-spectral/source-dependent operator data, or justify a genuinely different destination theorem.

Farey Discrepancy remains the strongest constructive example of useful pre-norm structure, but the analytic audit has tightened. The shared question is: **what new source-specific coordinate is actually acquired; which quotient, norm, approximation certificate, continuation, scale choice, finite inverse, parity covariance or averaging limit can remove its leverage; is the critical boundary ambient or source-rigid; and does the strongest matched control reproduce the structure at the same finite-height/pointwise quantifier consumed by the destination?**

## Can sign-indefinite structure preserve critical information before closure or norm compression scalarizes it?

Farey Discrepancy gives both a constructive and a negative answer. Differences before a positive norm can remove shared bulk and improve geometry, but FD-207 shows that a fixed finite sequence of such differences remains destination-complete when the source supplies an inverse. FD-208 leaves only a priced sublogarithmic growing-depth window for this particular repair: sign helps only if the growing transform destroys recoverability before its Delannoy cost becomes a power loss.

Visual Exploration supplies a complementary control. Even exact support plus complete multiplicative same-base phase structure cancels under Haar random cross-prime phases, and VIS-309 shows that the actual deterministic time orbit reproduces the same quadratic Haar mean over long time. Useful nonpositivity must therefore exploit a finite-height/moving-frequency effect that is invisible after asymptotic time averaging, not merely preserve multiplicativity or deterministic phase syntax.

Nyman--Beurling shows that preserving sign through faithful transport is necessary but not sufficient. Once the signed carrier is isolated, generic bounded analyticity can still saturate the contour scale. The next gain must be tied to arithmetic amplitudes inside the carrier.

Weil Positivity shows the same issue through Hilbert closure. The critical `n^-1/2` Whittaker amplitude is visible before positivity at `Y_n=c/n`, but ordinary positive `L^2` immediately squares it and diverges. A viable sign mechanism must act before that diagonal scalarization and remain source-forced after scale adaptation.

Prime Lattice adds a different positive-logarithm example. Venturini's conjugate symmetrization can convert a nonlinear completely multiplicative source law into a zero-free theorem, and PL-370 shows that local pole neutrality already drives the prime axes into a Liouville neighbourhood. Yet the critical continuation remains the hard input. A valid sign/positivity interface still needs an independently obtainable continuation theorem.

These examples sharpen the same interface: useful nonpositivity or positivity must occur at the stage where the source-specific relation is still present, survive required transport, avoid a finite inverse or destination-complete positive anchor, respect the physical source lattice, and depend on an input whose quantifier and analytic domain are not already equivalent to the desired conclusion.

And a critical Whittaker half-density is not enough when the unique power-law scale exposing it freezes the mixed coordinate before positive closure.
