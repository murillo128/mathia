Together AF-390, FD-167--FD-168 and RE-174--RE-176 sharpen the multiscale lesson: scale count is not the resource. **One latent object constrained coherently across an unbounded hierarchy can eliminate every fixed finite defect, but what happens next depends on the admissible source class and the inverse geometry or weighted capacity of the chosen observation.** The residual may be an infinitely-flat noncompact stratum, a uniformly conditioned growing prefix visible from a coordinate-minimal sparse Fourier skeleton, exponentially weak coefficient recovery, or a finite moment hierarchy that detects dangerous placement and forces a moving tail-capacity horizon without identifying the whole source. Exact compatibility must therefore be followed by an explicit source-class, observation, conditioning and placement theorem, not by a generic appeal to “more scales”.

## How much source information can an analytic architecture exploit at bounded conditioning?

Möbius Cancellation now has a five-stage invariant budget. MC-338 turns own-phase blindness into a projection/energy inequality; MC-339--MC-340 replace coordinate restrictions by HGR/mutual information and show that bounded-energy nontrivial dephasing requires `Omega(R)` **joint** source information. MC-341 prices the analog-coordinate loophole once a source-natural Euclidean resolution is supplied: encoding that information in `d` dimensions forces spread at least `exp(Theta(R/d))`. MC-342 then proves that bounded range, bounded forward Hamming-Lipschitz constant and every fixed positive-moment source-gradient energy still allow a scalar transcript to carry the entire source state by hiding it in exponentially small separations. MC-343 closes that loophole once a positive Gaussian readout scale is declared: `I(X;T+N) <= (d/2)log(1+V_T/(d sigma^2))`, so the linear information requirement forces explicit variance/SNR and diameter/noise costs; in fixed dimension the required SNR is exponential, and exact dephasing is impossible for every positive Gaussian noise level. Inverse precision is therefore no longer only a qualitative warning: under a concrete readout model it becomes a channel-capacity budget.

FD-168 provides the complementary positive example in sharper form than complete-field inversion alone. The Möbius-row Fourier skeleton for a sub-square-root prefix has an explicit observation norm in which a growing discrete source prefix is recovered with a uniform inverse modulus, while unused raw Fourier coordinates are irrelevant. Taken together with MC-343, this yields a stronger audit: **source information becomes analytically useful only when the actual observation supplies a lower distinguishability/inverse-recovery theorem at the resolution consumed downstream**. Benign forward geometry says almost nothing about analog capacity; a declared noise model can price hidden precision, while a good inverse modulus can make a sparse but growing source observation robustly coercive.

NB-176 adds a dual source-native version of the same principle. Once source resolution has been fixed by capped-Poisson transport, the actual Euler expansion supplies a jointly contractive feature map whose Gram singular values can certify that many template distinctions survive above that resolution. Farey, Möbius, Nyman--Beurling and Prime Circle therefore expose complementary versions of one accounting rule: count observations, coordinates, features, adaptive choices or spectral data only after identifying the exact source quotient, reconstruction closure, native source resolution and **inverse or dual distinguishability** that survives downstream.

## Which source fingerprint is selective rather than merely reconstructive?

Robin Extremal and Farey Discrepancy make source identification itself a controlled negative example. An unbounded exact observation hierarchy may identify the source completely while leaving the RH-facing estimate untouched; even when a sub-square-root Möbius prefix is uniformly recoverable from its coordinate-minimal raw Fourier skeleton, coefficient coercivity does not prove the Franel--Landau destination. Exact prime-multiplicity recovery through high-temperature Euler data is much worse conditioned and still does not control a Robin excursion at usable precision. RE-175--RE-176 show that full identification is not the only useful alternative: the first boundary moment detects the known dangerous compensation at the destination scale and, for the minimal two-sign-change KV-faithful repair, forces a quantitative placement horizon on the terminal tail. The open question is whether this cheaper source fingerprint can be derived from genuine arithmetic input and extended through further sign alternations strongly enough to become selector-coercive, rather than merely imposed as an extra constraint.

Arithmetic Fidelity says the same from the analytic side: source-class rigidity can turn a recovered flat jet into exact `TN_3`, and Hamburger-type rigidity can identify `zeta`, but neither mechanism selects the nontrivial zeta zero divisor. Prime Circle similarly shows that source-sensitive autocorrelation, relative bound states, a universal singular conductor limit and even large canonical escaping outliers are not automatically zero selectors when smooth source backgrounds or matched controls reproduce them. Visual Exploration now gives a particularly sharp local version: a rigorously nonzero quadratic pair-correlation carrier can be entirely zero-frequency pole compensation while resolving no prime frequency at all. The global program must therefore separate **source fingerprint**, **stable or destination-aligned transport of that fingerprint**, **background subtraction against matched controls**, and **destination-selective sign/zero theorem** at every stage.

## Global discriminator: identify the exact resource a matched control cannot fake

The current frontier is a classification of source-specific coupling that remains after every free closure, reconstruction, compatibility, quotient and matched-control operation. For each proposed route ask: what exact source distinction reaches the destination; what admissible source class is being compared; what information is regenerated for free; whether a finite-cutoff ambiguity survives only by escaping to infinity or into a flat boundary stratum; what subset of the declared observation is actually used by the inverse; what native observation norm gives a useful inverse modulus; whether a cheaper destination-aligned moment detects and spatially prices the dangerous control without reconstructing the whole source; what sub-resolution motion the source cannot see; what joint source information the complete transcript contains and what declared noise/precision or source-native dual witness certifies it; whether an operator preserves oriented phase or only an autocorrelation quotient; what universal singular, pole-compensating or smooth low-frequency background must be subtracted before source dependence is credited; what spectral-density law its determinant is forced to obey; whether a transport metric or positive scalarization derives the desired second moment or merely inserts/exposes it; and what theorem finally converts the survivor into a Riemann-zero or positivity restriction.

A useful bridge should fail on a matched control for an independently arithmetic reason. Exact reconstruction, unbounded observation count, source-specific labels, a critical boundary, a rich spectrum, a positive metric, a positive operator-valued lift, a nonzero low-frequency carrier, a large source-native Gram certificate, a small formal error, benign forward regularity, a singular limit or an escaping eigenvalue are not enough unless the surviving quantity is both stably distinguishable and selectively coupled to the RH destination.
