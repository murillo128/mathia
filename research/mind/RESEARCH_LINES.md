## Can the required Robin cancelling geometry occur for actual zeros?

RE-129--RE-137 reduce attained-edge hiding to a selected strong packet and show that leading observability costs scaled horizontal spread plus packet cardinality, not vertical diameter. RE-138 then inserts the actual-zeta source law: algebraic strength places the distinguished atom at polynomial ordinate height, and Riemann--von Mangoldt gives

`N_U << (1+W_U)(log U + log(2+W_U))`

for a packet of absolute vertical half-width `W_U`. Consequently bounded `W_U` and `H_U=O(log U)` force a fixed polynomial visibility floor for the selected leading packet. Superalgebraic hiding must send either `H_U/log U` or the **absolute vertical span** to infinity along a subsequence.

The live theorem is no longer an independent packet-cardinality bound. For actual zeros, complexity has been converted into geometry. What remains is to control cancellation from zeros outside the selected packet, or prove that the required horizontal/absolute-vertical escape is incompatible with the source. Fixed finite-order transfers must still satisfy their explicit logarithmic stability budget.

## Global discriminator: price what the endpoint actually sees

The newest evidence strengthens one common distinction: **retained information is meaningful only relative to the observer topology, physical resolution, coordinate quotient and conditional sigma-field that consume it**. AF-357 shows that the Riesz observer samples exponentially thin endpoint shells and that scalar envelope+jump resources are already sharp there; FD-140 shows a complete fixed congruence alphabet still misses a coprime refinement; MC-310 shows low-cost frequencies become useful only when their linear closure defeats a 2-adic rank ceiling; NB-139 makes total confluence-cell occupancy rather than internal gaps the target-bearing geometry; PC-304 places exact source reconstruction at the far end of growing tensor order; VIS-234 makes conditional nondegeneracy an exact type-level calculation; WI-300 separates a structural branch reduction from the independent validity of a finite computer-assisted positivity certificate.

For every proposed route ask: **what is the source discriminator; which coordinates or quotient frequencies see it; what physical scale does the observer actually sample; where can information be quotiented or averaged away; what topology and normalization retain it; which constants remain uniform in the parameter limit actually used; is the relevant object a coordinate matrix or a target-bearing subspace; which sigma-field does the null preserve and is the observable nondegenerate in the realized type; do individually cheap modes close to a useful linear family; does stronger resolution merely reconstruct the source; how does source counting constrain formal packet complexity; is a moving limit source-specific or only a universal tangent; and do the hypotheses already encode the desired endpoint?**

A route can fail by under-resolution, rank excess, near-confluence, target-poor local occupancy, topology mismatch, moving-parameter singularity, coordinate rotation, fixed-alphabet refinement, over-rigidity, hereditary restriction failure, exceptional Fourier modes, cheap-subspace rank collapse, source-count escape, carrier gauge, finite Fourier classicalization, reconstruction saturation, anisotropic noncompactness, conditional-null degeneracy, transform inadmissibility, universal moving-limit classicalization or terminal quotienting.
