# Global mathematical questions

## Can a source-native completion survive scalarization and still produce the Weil target?

[MI-009](intuition/MI-009-local-correctness-does-not-determine-global-coherence.md), [MI-012](intuition/MI-012-information-content-and-destination-sensitivity-are-separate-resources.md) and [MI-013](intuition/MI-013-closing-the-bulk-often-leaves-a-thin-source-specific-endpoint.md) separate continuum provenance, target sensitivity and source forcing.

Weil Positivity has ruled out fixed finite-state, positive Stieltjes and fixed compact-memory scalar completions. WP-284--WP-286 show that target-coded memory, ordinary Cesaro averaging and even canonical Haar/Følner scale averaging can suppress the regularized oscillatory zero field for generic harmonic reasons. [WP-287](../weil_positivity/findings/WP-287-nonlinear-arthur-height-scalarizations-that-remain-distributions-collapse-to-linear-filters.md) closes post-hoc nonlinear repair when the output is still a Weil-type distribution: any arbitrary function of the scalar Arthur-height profile that is linear in the test function is automatically linear on the attainable profile space.

The open theorem is upstream. Derive an operator/source coupling, boundary law, finite--archimedean interaction or other structure **before scalar profile collapse**, and prove both its arithmetic provenance and the required sign/linearity. A nonlinear cone statistic remains possible only with an independent polarization/equivalence theorem identifying it with the canonical Weil criterion.

## Can source-specific collective geometry control zero-state conditioning and Weil crossings?

Nyman--Beurling, Prime Lattice and Weil Inertia increasingly agree that good low-dimensional or generic geometry is not enough.

Nyman--Beurling has exact determinant cancellation and uniform two-state conditioning after canonical Blaschke deflation. [NB-098](../nyman_beurling/findings/NB-098-transverse-three-state-confluence-forces-order-independent-collective-shear.md) nevertheless gives a synthetic transverse three-state family with unit pivots and good pairs but `epsilon^-4` full conditioning blow-up for every deflation order. The next theorem must use zeta-specific restrictions on collective zero configurations, a proper confluent/Jordan representation, or a target mechanism that avoids dimension-free half-line conditioning.

Prime Lattice has all-log transport for actual eigenstates but no source-specific positive-order regularity or sign theorem; its surviving boundary improvement must come from the localized-Weil source/eigen-equation.

Weil Inertia separates scalar-profile exhaustion, retained Hilbert orientation and actual source geometry. WI-279 recovers useful orientation lost by scalarization. [WI-280](../weil_inertia/findings/WI-280-noncommensurate-cut-mixtures-cannot-break-the-scalar-profile-wall-generically.md) shows arbitrary finite noncommensurate positive mixtures of generic cut covariances still admit a two-pulse counterpath and cannot break the same scalar-profile ceiling. The next gain must prove a compatibility law for the actual Suzuki null mode that excludes this countergeometry, strengthens the numerator through the source equation, or uses a genuinely nonlinear source invariant.
