## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than another absorbed channel?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit, and the newest results sharpen it in several independent categories.

Arithmetic Fidelity now shows both sides of the transport problem: exact coefficients retain the full shell ladder at exponentially separated residual scales, but the current determinant aggregates that provenance before packet geometry. Source information can survive exactly while becoming unusable after representation compression.

Farey Discrepancy quantifies persistence by overlap rather than by source identity alone. A same source can serve horizons `N` and `2N` and remain blind below the half-scale Euler cutoff; rigidity begins when the later window covers the earlier prefix. Cross-horizon compatibility therefore needs a coverage geometry, not just a shared source label.

Möbius Cancellation shows that a source ensemble is not automatically an extra analytic dimension: source-mode averaging can collapse exactly to a signature projector. Prime Circle shows that a proper unbounded grade Hamiltonian remains eigenvalue-blind to bounded one-sided raising signatures. Prime Lattice shows that bounded normal infinite-dimensional complete multiplicativity remains a measure mixture of scalar semicharacters. In all three cases formal enlargement is consumed by exact algebra before the destination can use it.

Visual Exploration gives the constructive converse. The Bezout coordinates preserve both prime exclusions exactly and retain a long transverse direction, so the representation has now passed the information audit. The unresolved object is a genuine thin-domain prime-distribution estimate rather than another coordinate repair.

Weil Positivity makes the topology/radical gate stronger: stationarity is not needed for exact mixed-shell nullity to collapse prime-power shells in a closable positive radial form. Weil Inertia makes the complementary distinction: local threshold continuity holds on null-equation-selected states, but the energy budget alone cannot be iterated cofinally. The missing resource is persistent source-side structure or reserve, not generic regularity.

Robin Extremal similarly turns a quantifier escape into a resource trade. Complex center drift inside a fixed single-center corridor is now blocked in both bounded- and unbounded-center regimes; keeping an unbounded center alive forces corridor width to grow with the center modulus.

Across these examples, enrichment deserves credit only when a new source relation survives the exact downstream quotient **in the topology, normalization and quantifier required by the destination theorem** and cannot be reproduced by a matched control.

## Where has the exact obstruction moved after the latest boundary results?

**Selection versus transport.** Arithmetic Fidelity has both a natural source selector and an exact shell-resolved source carrier; the open problem is a stable representation that transports that exponentially fine provenance into the target packet mechanism. Prime Lattice has left bounded normal exact complete multiplicativity. Prime Circle has left bounded one-sided graded eigenvalue spectralization.

**Local fidelity versus overlap.** Farey Discrepancy has moved from “one persistent source” to a quantitative first-crossing question: how much moving-horizon coverage is required to close the repair reservoir without assuming Möbius tautologically? Nyman--Beurling retains its width-dependent depth frontier: after Ford damping, the active horizontal depth is tied to the vertical capacity rather than ambient height.

**Formal dimension versus surviving arithmetic variable.** Möbius Cancellation removes both an artificial inverse-gap stage count and an apparent source-mode averaging direction; the remaining analytic gain must use a variable that survives source Fourier collapse. Visual Exploration has found faithful joint coordinates, leaving uniform arithmetic distribution on a long-thin skew region as the real theorem.

**Parameter motion versus physical budget.** Robin Extremal shows that bounded complex drift remains Wiener-expensive and unbounded drift forces corridor blowup. A moving parameter is useful only if it changes geometry without repaying the gain in another physical resource.

**Regularity versus sign/topology.** Weil Inertia has local null-state regularity but not budget-only cofinal continuity; the open gate is source-side reserve/coherence. Weil Positivity shows that exact positive nullity is even more rigid than previously known: closability in the canonical radial topology alone can propagate mixed-shell radicality to every prime-power shell.

Analytic Frontier, Prime Flute and Xi Flow retain their existing local boundaries; no new persisted evidence in the inspected delta changes their current Mind state.

## Which physical cost survives once representation artifacts are removed?

The cost ledger is increasingly source-relative. Arithmetic Fidelity now prices shell resolution itself. Farey prices overlap of persistent source constraints. Möbius prices relation codimension and the analytic variables left after Fourier collapse. Nyman--Beurling prices only the Ford-active depth range. Prime Circle prices nontriangular/domain information rather than operator size. Prime Lattice prices departures from bounded normal abelian source algebra. Robin prices corridor width against center drift. Visual Exploration prices thin-domain discrepancy after exact coordinate fidelity. Weil Positivity prices what survives the positive radical in the chosen topology. Weil Inertia prices reserve/coherence beyond local form continuity.

The reusable question is: **after the strongest exact source specialization and faithful representation cleanup, what quantity must still be paid by the physical source rather than by coordinates, metric, cutoff, completion, normalization, projection or quantifier choice?** A claimed obstruction is credible only when it survives the matched control in variables consumed by the final theorem.

## Can sparse or deep defects be made visible without first strengthening the whole statistic?

Several lines say yes, but only after changing the destination-sensitive object rather than merely improving an average. Full localized Weil energy sees failures normalized pair correlation can hide. Nyman--Beurling keeps the cumulative depth-phase carrier paired with Ford damping. Visual Exploration retains both prime coordinates and the Wang taper instead of projecting to a determinant. Robin Extremal probes the normalized predictor in microscopic directions that expose raw-delay behavior even when center motion looks flexible.

The common design principle is to identify the **destination-sensitive functional and its active source range first**. If exact algebra, normalization, projection, scalarization or positive nullity has already erased the defect, a sharper estimate of the same compressed statistic will not restore it. If the right object is chosen, the remaining theorem should target the smallest source range and topology on which the defect still has persistent cost.
