## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than another absorbed or ill-conditioned channel?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit, and the newest results sharpen it in several independent categories.

Arithmetic Fidelity now separates exact retention from stable recovery. The Euler coefficient sequence contains the full shell ladder, but AF-437 shows that sequential peeling of shell `m` amplifies every earlier-shell error by `(m/j)^(2k)`. Exact provenance can therefore survive the source map while becoming exponentially fragile before the destination sees it. A viable carrier must transport labels before that subtraction, beat the peeling conditioning with a joint inverse, or exploit exact symbolic structure.

Farey Discrepancy extends the overlap law through a complete `(0,3,3)` switched period. One common source can serve `N,2N,4N` and remain blind below quarter-scale Euler coverage; prefix rigidity begins when the `4N` window reaches the earlier prefix. The next `8N` wrap back to depth `0` is genuinely new because the sampled-support geometry ceases to be monotone. Persistence and period completion are not enough without coverage and observation-support geometry.

Möbius Cancellation now has two exact dimension collapses. Source-mode averaging becomes a signature projector, and the pair `(d_1,d_2)` in the squared Selberg majorant becomes one lcm-fibre coefficient. The surviving arithmetic candidate is the lcm/shell variable itself or a pre-quadratic coordinate that genuinely does not factor through lcm. Formal labels count only when their variation survives exact regrouping and changes diagonal geometry.

Prime Lattice narrows the nonnormal escape in the same spirit. Bounded normal exact-multiplicative representations scalarize by Gelfand theory; PL-378 shows that invertible nonnormal representations whose full rational-group completion is uniformly bounded are simultaneously similar to commuting unitaries and hence again to prime-torus character mixtures. The remaining operator routes must use one-sided noninvertibility, inverse-norm growth, unbounded/domain structure, controlled nonmultiplicativity, or a source-canonical metric-sensitive observable.

Visual Exploration gives a constructive counterexample to overcompression. The Bezout chart preserves both prime exclusions, and VIS-320 further reduces the natural Wang strip to at most three simultaneous-prime mechanical branches governed by a rational rotation. But the physical determinant window is shorter than one rotation period, so full-cycle equidistribution is not the missing theorem; the real target is uniform subperiod prime-pair occupancy with the Wang taper still visible.

Weil Positivity sharpens the topology audit. The exact mixed-shell radical collapse is not confined to `L^2`: it survives every Sobolev order and every absolutely-continuous Fourier-weight Hilbert topology containing the shell profiles. A zero-frequency trace genuinely blocks weak translation escape, but it retains only `Lambda^2`. Ordinary norm reweighting is therefore absorbed; an escape must change spectral type or another load-bearing assumption and still retain the correct finite Weil coefficient.

Robin Extremal turns widening-corridor motion into an exact local normal form. With `kappa=N/(m(a-1))`, every finite complex limit with `Re(kappa)<1` leaves residual microscopic phase `e^(-i(1-kappa)u)`, while positive-real `kappa>=1` flattens that compact microscopic profile. The unit transition tells us both where the raw-delay obstruction is sharp and where it stops carrying information; any further obstruction must move to a larger scale or another predictor cost.

Nyman--Beurling now has a similarly sharp dimensionless transition. NB-230 kills the exact-Ford residue when `YD-log J -> +infinity`, with `J=1+R/H`; NB-231 constructs matched phase-aligned packets with order-one mass when `YD-log J=O(1)` in the audited `J<=log Q` regime. The remaining source theorem must attack the bounded carrier-width transition itself rather than improve regions already removed by Ford damping.

Across these examples, enrichment deserves credit only when a source relation survives the exact downstream quotient **with usable conditioning, the topology, normalization and quantifier required by the destination theorem**, and cannot be reproduced by a matched control.

## Can sparse or deep defects be made visible without first strengthening the whole statistic?

Several lines say yes, but only after changing the destination-sensitive object rather than merely improving an average. Full localized Weil energy sees failures normalized pair correlation can hide. Nyman--Beurling keeps the cumulative depth-phase carrier paired with Ford damping and carrier width. Visual Exploration retains both prime coordinates and the Wang taper, then keeps the actual subperiod branch rather than projecting to a determinant. The common design principle is to identify the **destination-sensitive functional, active source range and inverse conditioning first**. If exact algebra, unstable inversion, normalization, projection, scalarization or positive nullity has already erased the defect, a sharper estimate of the same compressed statistic will not restore it. If the right object is chosen, the remaining theorem should target the smallest source range and topology on which the defect still has persistent cost.
