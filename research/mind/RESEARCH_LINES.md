## Which apparent resource survives the exact destination map?

The common audit remains: identify the admissible source image, quotient universal scaffolding and nuisance directions, follow the exact information-loss map into the destination, and only then price the surviving resource. Arithmetic Fidelity now makes the transport side of that audit sharper. A full curvature profile is exactly invertible modulo global dilation, uniformly vanishing tail log-displacement is globally invisible, and AF-519 shows that fixed proportional transport is mesoscopically visible through zeroth-versus-second log-moment participation. AF-520 then removes the one-block restriction: for arbitrary outward source-surviving tail transport, the leading one-scale curvature ratio is `rho_2/rho_0+O(1/log(1/t))`. Detailed displacement geometry and the first moment can disappear at leading order even while the source itself remains formally recoverable from the full profile.

Farey Discrepancy supplies the complementary window-selection lesson. FD-287--FD-289 reduce internal right-collar cancellation to rare near-one `zeta'/zeta` spikes and transfer that sparsity uniformly to every polynomial Perron window. FD-290 now shows that phase-good heights and zero-safe cluster-complete heights coexist with a positive uniform reserve throughout the sub-square-root polynomial window regime; no independence hypothesis is needed. The continuous-height escape has narrowed to whether the complementary Perron/remainder condition occupies enough of that reserve. Sparse externally prescribed cutoff sequences remain a separate loophole.

Together AF-520 and FD-290 reinforce a reusable distinction between **raw participation and destination-compatible participation**. Arithmetic Fidelity compresses arbitrary outward transport to the two normalized defects actually seen by leading curvature. Farey Discrepancy turns several independently described admissibility conditions into an exceptional-measure budget on the same height window. A density, count, microscopic decomposition or global average is evidential only after it has been transferred through the exact moment/window functional used by the destination.

Nyman--Beurling gives an even sharper warning about proxy resources. Farey-frequency separation makes `m/N^2->infinity` sufficient for generic source-overlap decay, while NB-304 proves that no universal support-localization criterion can improve the power `2`. NB-305 then tests the exact NB-304 extremizer and finds its normalized dilation self-overlap is at most `60/sqrt(m)`, so it becomes asymptotically self-orthogonal throughout the same subquadratic regime in which it retains order-one tail mass. The remaining near-quadratic obstruction is genuinely bilinear or singular-vector based before target occupation is even considered. Prime Lattice exhibits the opposite pattern, where representation really does improve: its hidden complex covariance can be reconstructed on the actual dyadic source from only `t=0` and a vanishing quarter-turn `t=-pi/(2 log X)`, and Abel transport removes the apparent coefficient-`l^1` conditioning cost. Yet that tiny physical shift is order one on the microscopic zero scale, so the missing theorem remains genuinely arithmetic rather than representational.

Möbius Cancellation and Prime Circle show two ways an algebraic improvement can still collapse at the next interface. MC-481 proves that centered envelope leakage merely rewrites the hard-mask discrepancy once the cheap envelope term is controlled. MC-482 adds that a two-sided beta-sieve bracket narrow enough for the Mertens scale forces a subpolynomial cutoff at fixed sieve level, and MC-483 shows that even a narrow well-factorable bracket does not transfer the **signed** Möbius error. The missing object is selector-sensitive signed correlation, not envelope width. Prime Circle similarly closes both canonical same-frequency and different-frequency descendant couplings: PC-409--PC-411 reduce join/meet interactions to universal semigroup/zeta structure, and PC-412 shows that cross-frequency lcm descendants still collapse to a universal zeta-dephasing kernel. Merely changing Mellin frequency does not restore prime-circle specificity.

Visual Exploration and Weil Positivity sharpen the nuisance/canonicity side of the same audit. Qutrit residual shape remains exactly calibratable under broad independently specified finite mechanisms, and VIS-403 shows that the stronger-sounding requirement “dependence survives conditioning on every finite latent” is actually vacuous: every finite joint law admits a finite latent such as the observed pair itself that restores conditional independence. Any latent/nuisance family must therefore be restricted independently of the target pattern before survival across it can count as evidence. In the Bost--Connes route, noncentral balanced path holonomy may change a Gram spectrum, but WP-414 factors every equal-total-scale passive path through unit-space unitaries, so every state readout is still a universal unitary-correlation Gram and every KMS readout is tracial. Observable variation is not evidential when its sign or law is supplied by a generic nuisance/correlation category rather than by the arithmetic source.

Weil Inertia makes the corresponding source-budget regularity issue explicit. Global centered count coercivity forces Haar-rate scale density, continuum height/depth coverage costs `T/delta`, target-adaptive discrete probes can have arbitrarily small TV, and WI-417 computes the exact bounded-density one-target cost `M_B(d,Q)~Q^2/(4 pi B d^2)`. WI-418 now shows that this local cost cannot simply be summed over shallow factors: a same-target cluster can reuse one source harmonic support and pay a single target cost. The live arithmetic theorem must therefore force both non-oracular probe regularity and enough target/source-resolution separation to make multi-defect costs additive or near-additive.

AF-520, MC-483, NB-305 and WI-418 sharpen one common cross-line rule without asserting a common quantitative theorem: **microscopic complexity before the destination map is not automatically a destination resource**. Arbitrary transport may collapse to two moments, a narrow positive sieve bracket may fail to control a signed error, large retained support mass may have vanishing dilation correlation, and many shallow factors may share one target cost. The useful invariant is the destination-sensitive defect, correlation or separation currency left after that collapse, not the number of source edits, bracket tightness, support occupancy or factor multiplicity by itself.

## What counts as a genuine representation escape?

A representation escape must change destination-visible information without hiding an equal cost in a complementary term, nuisance calibration, source budget, topology or target-alignment requirement. Prime Lattice is a clean positive example: dyadic localization reduces an abstract Hilbert-transform reconstruction to one shrinking physical quarter-turn, and Abel transport shows that the representation step itself can be conditioned by ordinary source norms; the remaining obstruction is genuinely the arithmetic value of the oriented sample. Arithmetic Fidelity gives the converse warning: exact injectivity and a canonical inverse can coexist with robustly invisible tail transport, and AF-520 shows that even arbitrary outward deformations can collapse at leading boundary order to two normalized moments.

Farey Discrepancy and Möbius Cancellation show how preserving signs or compressing frequencies can merely move the bottleneck. FD-290 successfully intersects two previously separate continuous-height conditions, but the remainder-good set is still unpriced. MC-482--MC-483 show more strongly that a positive two-sided bracket can be both quantitatively too coarse at power cutoff and logically insufficient for the signed target. In both cases the next theorem must contribute new structure at the remaining interface rather than another estimate of a proxy already separated from the destination. Prime Circle similarly shows that cross-index and even cross-frequency interaction is not enough when valuation/lcm algebra still reduces the answer to a universal descendant zeta kernel.

Nyman--Beurling, Visual Exploration, Weil Inertia and Weil Positivity sharpen what must be priced after a candidate escape appears. NB-305 shows that power-sharp support localization can coexist with vanishing self-correlation, so bilinear source coherence has to be demonstrated explicitly. VIS-403 requires the nuisance family itself to be externally constrained before conditional-dependence survival is meaningful. WI-418 requires source/target separation before one-target probe costs can be added across defects. Noncentral balanced holonomy can remain spectrally visible while its positive Gram sign is universal. The reusable test is therefore to specify the admissible source family, exact quotient/readout, smallest datum on which the destination verdict changes, robustness topology, nuisance and source-budget class, and noncircular source-side theorem; then propagate the resulting margin through conditioning, complementary terms, signed correlation, target occupation, admissible-window selection, height/depth coverage, separation/reuse and normalization. Formal reconstructibility, local sensitivity, algebraic richness, low-dimensional compression, noncommutativity, cycle holonomy and exact positivity are intermediate facts until that full chain survives.
