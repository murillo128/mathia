## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than merely transport, invert or positively complete the same source?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) and [MI-028](intuition/MI-028-complete-measurement-families-can-close-an-escape-by-reconstructing-the-classical-object.md) remain the common interface audit.

Prime Circle closes both the obvious representation lifts and the direct source-native metric repair: full-signature/free-Fock data are transported by reciprocal second quantization, while the radial-energy Gram metric on the matched control is `J^*GJ`. A richer source metric is not discriminating when the same construction remains covariantly isometric.

Farey Discrepancy now gives an exact fixed-filter dichotomy. For a fixed finite polynomial family applied to the physical root increment, a common characteristic root in `sqrt(2)<|rho|<=2` gives a bounded-source super-square-root blind mode; if the gcd has no root in that annulus, square-root pointwise control of the family already reconstructs the destination for every bounded source and is RH-complete for Möbius. Finitely enriching the signed filter family therefore has no intermediate theorem surface: it is either spectrally blind or destination-complete.

Prime Lattice separates source selection from global analytic power. Complete multiplicativity plus continuation gives a genuine zero-sensitive theorem interface, and local pole neutrality forces witnesses into a finite prime-harmonic neighbourhood of Liouville. Sparse reciprocal-prime perturbations can nevertheless block continuation at any prescribed real exponent in `(1/2,1)`. Local rigidity is real; continuation remains an independent global resource.

Nyman--Beurling now has a sharp population-only resolution boundary. Generic strip analyticity is exhausted. Zeta-specific near-one population reaches every fixed margin below Ford at fixed source width, while a proportional log shell narrows the carrier enough to reach the exact Ford denominator. NB-225 proves the complementary method no-go: every sublinear shell admits a phase-aligned matched zero packet compatible with all population bounds currently used. Below linear width, the missing resource is joint depth-window or phase information, not another absolute count.

Visual Exploration has preserved exact support, coefficient magnitudes, multiplicative phase relations, the actual deterministic time orbit in long-time quadratic mean and coefficient-weighted local spacing in finite cutoffs. VIS-312 shows that the full supported ratio spectrum is dense, so the nearest-neighbor spacing functional that improved the finite-cutoff estimate has no infinite-spectrum limit. The next representation must aggregate local spectral density or finite-window kernel energy rather than assign one isolation radius to each frequency.

Möbius Cancellation sharpens the source-image warning quantitatively. High dual distance forces macroscopic frame-scale correlations, but merely inserting one low-weight relation does not help. After duplicate coordinate characters are merged exactly, an `O(H/R)` exceptional theorem at `o(R^(-1/2))` scale requires normalized weighted genuine four-character collision mass of order `R`; in a balanced rank-linear regime this means `Omega(R^3)` four-relations. The useful source coordinate is additive collision mass, not generic code quality.

Weil Positivity adds an exact positive-completion obstruction. The critical prime-power coefficients are the nonzero Fourier coefficients of a centered Poisson kernel. The centered carrier is indefinite; the smallest scalar Toeplitz completion adds `2/(sqrt(p)+1)` on the diagonal at prime `p`, and the required `log p`-weighted diagonal tax diverges globally. Local positive completion is therefore not a harmless normalization. A viable operator route must absorb or quotient that direction inside one global finite--archimedean/cross-prime sign structure before positivity is asserted.

The shared question is: **what genuinely new source coordinate is acquired, and what happens when its exact inverse, covariance law, spectral nullspace or minimal positive completion is computed before crediting it with leverage?** A representation that is faithful but control-conjugate, signed but invertibly destination-complete, locally positive only after a divergent diagonal repair, or dense-spectrum only through a cutoff-dependent gap functional has changed coordinates without yet changing the theorem interface.

## Where does the obstruction move after a nominal critical boundary is resolved?

Arithmetic Fidelity now gives the cleanest source-mesh hierarchy. The critical displaced branch can miss an integer target at second order. Above criticality, a fixed rational density automatically hits the first `R_n/n` saddle window because the source mesh has exactly that scale. AF-432 classifies the next `R_n/n^2` packet-corrected target in amplitude: the hit set is dense `G_delta` but Lebesgue-null with Hausdorff dimension at most `1-1/q`. Genericity has now been exhausted; a distinguished amplitude requires pointwise arithmetic membership information.

Farey Discrepancy has moved past fixed filter design. Common sublogarithmic dyadic depth remains destination-complete, and FD-210--FD-211 show that every fixed finite polynomial family is either annularly blind or destination-complete. The surviving escape must vary with scale, grow in degree, weaken the observation norm, or otherwise destroy the coherent fixed inverse.

Visual Exploration moves from localization to spacing and then from spacing to density. Coefficient-weighted finite-cutoff gaps recover two powers of the crude horizon, but the full ratio support is dense and destroys pointwise isolation. The next wall is an aggregate-density/energy estimate for the infinite spectrum versus a genuine sparse exceptional-height resonance.

Nyman--Beurling has crossed the exact-Ford population wall only by paying linear logarithmic source width. NB-225 shows that the same population inputs cannot reach exact Ford with any sublinear width. The next boundary is not another width optimization inside those estimates; it is a new phase-sensitive or joint depth-window zero theorem.

Robin Extremal has localized the single-center continuum escape. Interior real parameter drift pays a strict `2+eta_K` Wiener exponent, and narrow repair corridors have an independent superoscillatory surcharge. In a fixed finite corridor, a putative rate-two real single-center escape cannot use `lambda->0`; it must approach a genuine arc/center boundary while exploiting exact remainder cancellation, or leave the single-center representation entirely.

Möbius Cancellation moves from dual distance to weighted quartic incidence. MC-391 replaces the coarse target “find a word of weight at most four” by the quantitative requirement `Q_4/sigma_A^4=Omega(R)` under the desired exceptional-set scale. The next object is the actual arithmetic quartic collision mass of the source-incidence system, not another generic code bound.

Weil Positivity moves the non-diagonal escape from “couple translates” to “assemble the compensating diagonal globally before the sign theorem.” Independent scalar prime-ray completion is closed by a divergent zero-mode tax; genuinely cross-prime, matrix-valued or finite--archimedean assembly remains open.

Across these lines, a resolved threshold should be followed by identifying the **next exact source mesh, inverse-coherence class, aggregate spectral density, width/phase resource, quartic incidence mass, boundary approximation regime or positive-completion counterterm** that becomes load-bearing. Continuing to optimize the previous coarse boundary usually attacks a variable already proved nondecisive.

## Which physical cost survives source specialization and analytic transport?

[MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md), [MI-025](intuition/MI-025-combinatorial-breadth-must-be-priced-through-the-source-coordinate-map.md) and [MI-017](intuition/MI-017-sparse-exceptional-geometry-survives-upper-population-control.md) remain the main source-cost syntheses.

Arithmetic Fidelity shows that source cost is scale-specific and genericity-specific. One source mesh can be exactly matched to an `R_n/n` aperture while the next `R_n/n^2` condition becomes a pointwise shrinking-target problem. Residuality and metric thinness can coexist, so neither category nor measure substitutes for membership of the distinguished source.

Nyman--Beurling prices localization by source width. A proportional logarithmic shell buys a `1/X` vertical carrier and exact-Ford population control, while every sublinear shell leaves enough unresolved vertical capacity for a coherent matched packet under the same zero-count theorems. The new required currency is joint horizontal-depth/vertical-window information or phase cancellation.

Robin Extremal prices spectral support width directly. For support in `[H,RH]`, the exact superoscillatory lower exponent `B(R)` diverges as the corridor collapses. This cost is representation-independent inside that support class and therefore separates genuine boundary degeneration from harmless parameter retuning.

Möbius Cancellation prices source geometry after pullback. Ambient Welch geometry, dual distance and quartic additive collision mass are different resources. The physical source must carry enough weighted four-character collisions; generic pseudorandomness is actively hostile.

Visual Exploration gives the finite-time spectral analogue. Local gaps are useful at finite cutoff but vanish pointwise in the full dense ratio spectrum. Infinite-tail transport must be charged using aggregate density or the actual finite-window kernel, not a fictitious cutoff-free nearest-neighbor distance.

Weil Positivity gives a completion cost rather than an inverse cost. The desired off-diagonal prime-power coefficients can sit inside a positive Poisson kernel only after adding a diagonal direction whose optimal total prime-weighted mass diverges. Any global positivity claim must explain where that diagonal goes before closure.

The shared audit is to price a route **after exact source specialization and before the final closure**, keeping source mesh, pointwise membership, inverse coherence, support corridor, carrier width, zero population, phase coherence, additive collision energy, dense-spectrum local density, diagonal completion mass and destination normalization distinct until a theorem proves a dependency.

## Can sign-indefinite structure preserve critical information before closure or norm compression scalarizes it?

Farey Discrepancy shows both possibilities for fixed signed filters. A common annular root preserves a genuine blind mode; removing all common annular roots makes the fixed family square-root complete. Sign is useful only if the source inverse ceases to be one fixed polynomial problem or the observation norm changes.

Visual Exploration shows that exact support and complete same-base multiplicativity still cancel under cross-prime Haar phases, while the actual deterministic orbit has the same long-time quadratic mean. The unresolved signed object is finite-height coherence of a dense infinite ratio spectrum, where aggregate density replaces pointwise isolation.

Nyman--Beurling now isolates phase information exactly. Population bounds suffice at linear shell width, but below that width they permit an order-one phase-aligned zero packet. Any sublinear-width advance must use information that can distinguish the true residue phases from that matched control.

Weil Positivity makes the closure loss literal. The Weil-shaped prime-power carrier is the centered, sign-indefinite part of the positive Poisson kernel. Restoring local positivity adds a divergent diagonal. A useful sign theorem must therefore couple the compensating sector before positivity, not square/complete locally and subtract afterwards.

Möbius Cancellation gives the finite-group counterpart. Four-wise-independent/Sidon-like source signs force critical-scale anti-concentration; useful cancellation requires a large weighted family of exact quartic relations. Random-looking sign structure is not itself the source of the needed sign.

These examples sharpen one interface: useful sign or positivity must act while the source-specific relation is present, survive the exact physical inverse or completion, and depend on information whose pointwise/phase/additive structure is not already equivalent to the desired conclusion.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the admissible source class and topology, observation family and nullspace, destination quotient and norm, source-coordinate map, requested precision, approximation class, analytic domain, discrete source mesh, pointwise shrinking target, source width and dual carrier width, exceptional-set notion, source-frame collision structure, finite inverse, covariance/naturality constraints, sign/phase data, finite-time averaging window, aggregate frequency density, tail norm, minimal positive completion and destination normalization.

Then ask in sequence: **what is genuinely new at acquisition; what exact inverse or completion does the representation admit; what survives the destination quotient; what physical source mesh realizes the tuning; do measure/category/population statements decide the distinguished point; if localization is improved, what source resolution is lost; if time averaging is used, does the spectral quantity survive the infinite dense support; if positivity is restored, what diagonal or counterterm was added and is it globally finite; and does the final statement see the individual defect at the quantifier actually required?**

A route that survives this audit has identified a mathematical bottleneck. A route that fails it has usually rediscovered a source-resolution, inverse, approximation, population, spectral-density or positive-completion cost in different notation.
