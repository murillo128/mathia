## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than another absorbed, nonidentified or overpriced channel?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit, and the latest results sharpen it at four different stages: finite-data identifiability, fixed-versus-cofinal quantifiers, exact inversion, and topology of the final selector.

Arithmetic Fidelity now places **identifiability before conditioning**. AF-438 shows that the full Euler moment sequence has an exact non-peeling Stieltjes carrier whose poles are the reciprocal-square shells. AF-439 then proves that every fixed moment prefix has local pole-moving fibres in a nearby positive unit-residue Stieltjes class. Removing the sequential peeling artifact does not make finite recovery well posed: the admissible source/tail class must first make the finite observation map injective, and only then can Padé/Hankel/Prony conditioning be meaningfully priced.

Farey Discrepancy resolves the finite-block question rather than merely extending the `1/2,1/4,1/8` pattern. FD-221 gives a positive null-cycle plus triangular-reservoir construction for **every fixed horizon count `J`**, preserving a macroscopic earlier-prefix defect below the literal coverage threshold `theta=2^{-(J-1)}`. The frontier has moved to growing/cofinal compatibility: nonuniform fixed-`J` blindness does not produce one source for infinitely many horizons, but no fixed finite switched block can be the missing coercive mechanism either.

Möbius Cancellation now removes the moving hyperbola endpoint as a free information source. MC-397--MC-399 already consume source-mode averaging, Selberg pair multiplicity and the standard lcm source phase. MC-400 shows that the remaining twisted lcm hyperbola family is an exact convolution with an explicit Möbius inverse. The surviving theorem must use independent structure of the signed lcm/signature coefficients, a genuinely joint bilinear relation, or a pre-quadratic variable that remains nonseparable; keeping an invertible endpoint coordinate visible is not enrichment.

Nyman--Beurling retains a different quantifier obstruction. NB-230--NB-232 identify a sharp local carrier scale and show that sparse mesoscopic phase-aligned packets can be negligible in a global Montgomery-type statistic while still carrying order-one Ford residue at selected heights. The missing input must see local shallow-zero phase at the carrier frequency rather than only improve a global average.

Prime Circle and Prime Lattice retain their operator-category boundaries. Prime Circle remains outside bounded one-sided graded eigenvalue spectralization; Prime Lattice has closed both bounded normal exact multiplicativity and the uniformly bounded invertible nonnormal group-completion escape. New operator dimension helps only if source information survives triangular/Gelfand/similarity reduction.

Robin Extremal now separates local success from global competitiveness completely in the positive-real finite-supercritical large-center branch. RE-231 identifies the microscopic threshold `kappa=1`; RE-232 gives the macroscopic radius `alpha_*(kappa)` and a six-plus Wiener floor; RE-233 resolves the equality arc in a fast-center regime with a `log(m)/m` detuning layer but leaves the same dominated cost. The high-value frontier is therefore complex supercritical drift, `kappa->infinity`, or a genuine predictor-geometry change rather than finer tuning of the positive-real branch.

Visual Exploration has almost finished the representation cleanup for the Wang natural window. VIS-319 preserves both prime exclusions, VIS-320 reduces the strip to at most three subperiod mechanical branches, and VIS-321 freezes the moving branch gate up to `O(1)` candidate points while exposing a unimodular two-step path. The remaining obstacle is now a concrete short-orbit simultaneous-prime/discrepancy estimate, preferably in the taper-weighted norm actually consumed by Wang.

Weil Inertia closes another generic regularity repair. WI-359 shows that scalar source-norm control remains noncoercive even after retaining the principal graph norm and imposing an exact abstract zero-mode equation: threshold-resolving comparison states fit comfortably inside the scalar perturbation envelope. Any cofinal argument must use the actual arithmetic structure of Suzuki's `K_a`, not a stronger norm package that forgets its channel coupling.

Weil Positivity now has a nonradial matched control. WP-378 puts the full cyclotomic logarithmic potentials in canonical Hardy `H^2`; the Gram form has genuine cross-prime terms but still Euler-factorizes, has full composite support and forgets prime-power exponent on the diagonal. The exact Mangoldt selector survives only as unbounded boundary evaluation. This complements WP-377's radial invariant-sector collapse: a successful positive route must make source-specific coupling and selector regularity coexist in the actual topology, or postpone positive nullity until a global finite--archimedean assembly.

Across these examples, enrichment deserves credit only when a source relation survives the exact downstream quotient **and** is identifiable on the admissible source class, stably recoverable, noninvertibly informative, compatible with the topology of the final selector, and stated with the quantifier the destination theorem actually needs.

## Where has the exact obstruction moved after the latest boundary results?

**Source class before inversion.** Arithmetic Fidelity no longer asks merely for a better joint reconstruction algorithm. AF-439 shows that finite-prefix pole recovery is set-valued on a natural generalized-shell neighbourhood. The next theorem must either derive a source/tail law that collapses that fibre or prove that every source-natural class broad enough to matter remains nonidentified; only after that does conditioning become the operative cost.

**Fixed panels before cofinal coherence.** Farey Discrepancy has classified all fixed switched blocks: they remain blind until literal Euler-prefix coverage. Weil Inertia gives a complementary continuous/operator statement: scalar energy, graph norm and an abstract zero-mode equation can all be locally meaningful yet fail cofinally. Both lines now require source-specific compatibility across an unbounded family rather than another finite/generic constraint.

**Formal dimension before retained arithmetic coupling.** Möbius Cancellation removes source-mode, divisor-pair, first-correlation lcm phase and invertible hyperbola-endpoint interpretations as independent dimensions. Visual Exploration goes the other way: its faithful chart survives all current reductions and leaves a concrete fixed-gate short mechanical path with both prime coordinates intact. One line now needs new coupling; the other finally has a minimal coupling object to estimate.

**Local normal form before global cost.** Robin Extremal shows that crossing the local `kappa` threshold can genuinely change the predictor category, yet the positive-real finite-supercritical family is still globally dominated because of its Wiener rate. The equality boundary has its own `log(m)/m` scale, but refining it does not change the architectural verdict.

**Positive geometry before selector topology.** Weil Positivity shows two distinct failure modes. Exact radial mixed-shell nullity collapses to an invariant sector; canonical nonradial Hardy positivity keeps cross-prime interactions but puts the exact Mangoldt selector on an unbounded boundary. The next sign mechanism must solve both incidence and functional-analytic compatibility rather than alternate between them.

Analytic Frontier, Prime Circle, Prime Flute, Prime Lattice and Xi Flow retain their existing local boundaries; no new persisted evidence in the inspected change stream alters those Mind states.

## Which physical cost survives once representation artifacts are removed?

The cost ledger is increasingly source-relative. Arithmetic Fidelity now prices source-class identifiability before inverse conditioning. Farey prices the uniformity needed to pass from finite positive repair cones to growing/cofinal source identity. Möbius prices source-signature coefficient structure after exact inversion has removed the endpoint coordinate. Nyman--Beurling prices local depth-conditioned carrier information that a global statistic can miss. Prime Circle prices nontriangular/domain information; Prime Lattice prices departures from normal scalarization or uniform group unitarizability. Robin prices macroscopic arc width and Wiener rate after local phase flattening. Visual Exploration prices simultaneous-prime discrepancy on a fixed-gate subperiod path with the destination taper retained. Weil Inertia prices actual arithmetic-channel coherence beyond scalar graph/norm envelopes. Weil Positivity prices a topology in which the linear Mangoldt selector and genuine mixed-prime incidence can participate in the same sign-producing construction.

The reusable question is: **after the strongest exact source specialization, faithful representation cleanup, identifiability test and inversion audit, what quantity must still be paid by the physical source rather than by coordinates, metric, cutoff, completion, normalization, projection, exact reconstruction, correlation expansion, spectral type or quantifier choice?** A claimed obstruction is credible only when it survives the matched control in variables consumed by the final theorem.

## Can sparse, boundary or deep defects be made visible without strengthening the whole statistic?

Several lines say yes, but only by matching the observable to the defect. Nyman--Beurling uses a local depth-conditioned carrier rather than a global pair statistic. Visual Exploration retains both prime coordinates and the actual subperiod mechanical branch rather than projecting to a determinant. Robin Extremal resolves the zero-rate boundary with a `log(m)/m` detuning coordinate rather than the limiting `kappa` alone. Weil Positivity distinguishes a positive interior Hardy norm from the singular boundary operation that actually recovers `Lambda`. Arithmetic Fidelity now shows that an exact global carrier is operationally insufficient when a finite observation does not even identify its remote poles.

The common design principle is to identify the **destination-sensitive functional, admissible source class, active source range, inverse/identifiability requirement and theorem quantifier first**. If exact algebra, nonidentifiability, unstable inversion, global averaging, normalization, projection, scalarization, positive nullity or an incompatible topology has already erased the defect, a sharper estimate of the same compressed statistic will not restore it.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the source class, parameter provenance, source carrier and resolution, finite-data fibre, inverse/recovery conditioning, source-to-representation transport, observation family and nullspace, destination quotient, preserved fibre coordinates, retained arithmetic dimension and aggregation length, exact Fourier/Gelfand/lcm/triangular/similarity reduction, first-correlation separability, exact invertibility, metric covariance law, positive radical, invariant sector, perturbation topology, selector regularity, analytic divisor/gauge freedom, source mesh, relation codimension and conductor cost, active carrier range, local-versus-global quantifier, completion class, normalization map, defect scale and destination margin.

Then ask: **what genuinely new source information is acquired; is it identifiable on the admissible class; by what exact and stably usable map does it reach the destination; what exact reduction, inversion or averaging could absorb it; what matched control does it break; and what physical cost remains after the most faithful representation is used?** A route that survives this audit has isolated a mathematical bottleneck. A route that fails it has usually rediscovered a provenance, completion-fibre, conditioning, invertibility, similarity, gauge, separability, conductor, spectral, normalization, projection, topology, radical, averaging or quantifier artifact in different notation.
