# Global mathematical questions

## When does repeated observation actually shrink the admissible source fibre?

[MI-007](intuition/MI-007-survival-requires-quantitative-non-escape-not-just-nonzero-structure.md), [MI-008](intuition/MI-008-exact-recovery-is-not-stable-transport.md), [MI-012](intuition/MI-012-information-content-and-destination-sensitivity-are-separate-resources.md), [MI-019](intuition/MI-019-source-identification-and-zero-selection-are-orthogonal-gates.md), [MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md), [MI-022](intuition/MI-022-unbounded-compatibility-can-turn-finite-cutoff-ambiguity-into-a-stability-problem.md), and [MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md) separate retained information, common-source compatibility, conditioning, acquisition geometry, aggregate readout capacity and final destination selection.

Under the shrinking solid hierarchy the first two analytic conditions are already forced, so component topology is the exact residual global resource. Infinitely-flat boundary zeros are allowed; a zero separating two positive-curvature regions is not.

Farey Discrepancy makes the acquisition geometry equally explicit. FD-166--FD-168 give exact permanent-source recovery and stable sparse raw-coordinate inversion; FD-169 shows unrestricted target-aware linear mixing makes measurement count collapse to target rank by moving the inverse into the sensor. Omitting any one horizon leaves an explicit permanent null direction, and on a finite prefix a selected horizon set `S` has rank exactly `|S|`. The live question is no longer unrestricted sparse midpoint sampling; it is whether genuinely source-faithful restrictions destroy the pulse-pair kernel, or how many additional spatial rows are needed in a mixed space-time acquisition family.

Robin Extremal now replaces raw oscillation count by two complementary source-conditioned costs. RE-175--RE-179 show how exact boundary value/slope and shallow Euler jets spatially price delayed repairs, with the intrinsic KV-weighted reset budget measuring the remote branch. The live escape is therefore a hybrid discrete/continuous dichotomy: selector-scale many early deletions or a sufficiently large/divergent KV-weighted reset budget.

Together AF-394, FD-171 and RE-180 sharpen the multiscale lesson: raw scale count is not the resource. Arbitrarily fine local certificates can recover the full analytic cone and still omit a global component relation; a single spatial sensor can contain many independent constraints because the operator changes with horizon; and a continuous repair budget can miss an atomic one-sided source tariff until honest multiplicities are restored. The correct resource is the **conditioned information retained in the representation at the scale, topology and source granularity where the destination can still respond**.

The live question is therefore not merely “arithmetic frequency support”, but a **source-natural frequency-amplitude acquisition class** whose spectral spread survives a matched packing control at the same conditioning cost.

## How much source information can an analytic architecture exploit at bounded conditioning?

Möbius Cancellation now separates fixed-quality capacity from variable-fidelity reliability. MC-347 identifies the invariant currency when query quality can vary: the accumulated directional discrimination divergence `mathfrak D` must satisfy `mathfrak D=Omega(R log(1/bar p))`. Many weak measurements can be traded for fewer strong ones, but the total pairwise evidence cannot be optimized away.

FD-169--FD-171 and NB-178--NB-180 are complementary warnings. Unrestricted acquisition can hide the inverse in the sensor; repeated horizons can hide many independent rows behind one spatial coordinate, yet those rows may be exactly irredundant; an enlarged dual class can remove one compactness ceiling and still be so expressive that generic metric packing fakes the desired spectral code; even genuine Euler frequency labels remain nonselective when their source amplitudes are freely renormalized away. Source-information cost is meaningful only after the source class, acquisition/dual primitive, target quotient, observation norm/noise model, temporal/operator variation, source amplitudes and matched-control geometry are fixed.

This updates [MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md): measurement count, nominal dimension, positive noise, adaptivity and canonical probe labels are secondary. The load-bearing quantities are inverse modulus, independent operator-row rank, per-access capacity, accumulated discrimination divergence, source-amplitude conditioning, charged spectral spread and the number of genuinely independent operator states being observed.

The program must therefore separate **source fingerprint**, **admissible acquisition/transport**, **source amplitude and renormalization cost**, **channel-matched control subtraction**, **conditioning/capacity/reliability**, **local analytic recovery versus global component topology**, **joint versus scalarized geometry**, **full-operator versus principal-form order structure**, **exceptional-set quantifiers**, and the final **destination-selective sign or zero theorem**.

## Global discriminator: identify the exact resource a matched control cannot fake

For each proposed route ask: what exact source distinction reaches the destination; what source class is being compared; what information is regenerated for free; whether horizon/operator changes contribute independent constraints and whether those rows are redundant; whether the inverse has been moved into the sensor; whether weak source channels were independently renormalized and at what cost; what native norm gives a useful inverse modulus; what capacity and discrimination divergence survive at the downstream resolution; whether a larger witness class merely certifies generic metric packing; what location-weighted and discrete source complexity an escaping repair pays; whether local certificates recover only the differential cone or also the required component topology; whether a control subtraction matches the norm/channel carrying the signal; whether a source-sensitive scalar still factors through a phase/autocorrelation quotient; whether a limiting positivity/Hopf theorem survives the bounded completed operator terms; whether a singular limit exposes only a fixed boundary state; whether a positive boundary square survives divisor-changing matched controls; whether an almost-all theorem controls the distinguished exceptional sequence; and what theorem finally converts the survivor into a Riemann-zero or positivity restriction.

A useful bridge should fail on a matched control for an independently arithmetic reason. Exact reconstruction, unbounded observation count, unrestricted target-aware mixing, one spatial sensor with uncharged horizon variation, genuine frequency labels with independently erased amplitudes, a rich spectrum, a critical boundary, source labels, a positive principal form, a positive operator-valued lift, adaptive scalarization, a singular tangent, a positive divisor-blind boundary square, a nonzero low-frequency carrier, a large Gram/nuclear response, benign forward regularity, positive readout noise, adaptive or variable-fidelity queries, infinitely many remote resets, smooth PNT subtraction, a phase-blind Hardy excess, zero-set membership or raw positive von Mangoldt weighting are not enough unless the surviving quantity is stably distinguishable, charged in the correct source geometry, theorem-accessible at the required quantifier and scale, robust under the full source operator, and selectively coupled to the RH destination.
