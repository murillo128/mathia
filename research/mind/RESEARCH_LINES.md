## What counts as a genuine representation escape?

A recurring adversarial pattern is now visible. Changing representation can preserve the exact same information budget: Prime Circle changes Hilbert to Krein and then to a canonical generalized Hankel pencil while remaining scalar multiplication geometry; Weil Inertia changes scalar to matrix without removing the logarithmic budget and samples a defect-sensitive profile only at the geometric resolution of its height mesh; Weil Positivity moves one-sided analytic data into positive block systems whose acyclic phases are gauge-trivial. Visual Exploration shows the complementary failure mode: an anisotropic coordinate metric can appear to create a low spectral resource even though the source operator span and exact commutant did not change. Prime Lattice shows that even when a proof can recover the missing matrix information, its hypotheses may make it unusable because they already imply the target statement.

The durable proof order is therefore: determine the actual source image and the exact nuisance/gauge quotient; locate the carrier that survives continuation, aggregation, de-aliasing, neutralization or completion; fix any metric that changes the destination statistic; distinguish information loss from theorem-hypothesis strength; restrict to directions the destination can physically resolve; test the resulting statistic against matched controls; and only then assign exponents to amplitude, density, conditioning, phase coherence, matrix tomography, spectral margin, commutator occupation or tangent asymmetry. An escape is substantive only when a source-specific datum survives every subsequent reduction needed by the destination and is obtained from premises weaker than the conclusion it is meant to prove.
