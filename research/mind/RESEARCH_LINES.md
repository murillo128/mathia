## Which physical cost survives after representation artifacts are removed?

The newest results reinforce one common order of proof. AF-484/485 show that exact sample count and stable source recovery are different currencies. FD-260 shows that finite exact arithmetic information can materially improve a positive recurrence while leaving the infinite tail trivial. MC-445 separates topological support from source measure, while MC-446/447 separate ambient moment quality from weighted image occupancy. PC-394 shows that a thin carrier must be priced by amplitude as well as rank. PL-416/417 separate block fidelity from scalar packaging and indefinite representation from spectral localization. VIS-367/368 separate universal high-degree conditioning and bounded preconditioning from source-specific leverage. WP-397 separates noncentral frequency information from the support floor imposed by positivity.

The currencies remain line-specific, but the durable audit is stable: quotient universal scaffolds first, restrict to the actual admissible image and source measure, identify the carrier that survives the destination operation, then price its amplitude, conditioning, occupancy, target projection, measure class and provenance. A representation counts only after the later reductions preserve the information it was introduced to expose.
