# Global mathematical questions

This file records mathematical questions that only become visible by comparing several current Mathia research lines. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does a frozen capacity bound become a genuine source discriminator?

Visual Exploration and Weil Inertia now give two exact examples of the same structural gate in very different categories. VIS-405 proves that a pre-fixed numerical Lipschitz budget on a monotone latent decoder is equivalent to a concrete uniform mass floor on the observable residual law. The restriction is real only because the constant is fixed independently of the target; choosing it from the observed law restores the universal quantile representation. WI-420 similarly shows that, once an external two-dimensional source-density cap `B` is fixed, the entire critical reciprocal-depth Blaschke crossover becomes an exact Poisson capacity with a finite-dimensional dual and bang-bang extremizers. Again, the capacity becomes coercive only if the regularity budget is justified before the defect configuration is known.

Farey Discrepancy supplies a complementary selection lesson. FD-292 makes the universal phase-bad set smaller than any prescribed fixed inverse power of `log` on polynomial windows, so the remaining theorem is not to improve that generic exceptional exponent again but to prove that the **source-defined good set**—safe cores of remainder-good zero gaps—has enough occupation to beat whichever fixed inverse-polylog budget is chosen. Across the three lines, an externally tunable complexity bound, density cap or exceptional-set exponent is not the resource. The live cross-line question is: **can the arithmetic/source mechanism itself derive a target-independent capacity or occupation budget, and can the destination-visible object be shown to violate or exceed it?**

This comparison also gives an adversarial test. A claimed anomaly should survive after the nuisance/resource constant is frozen before target inspection, and the proof should identify the exact observable consequence of that frozen constant. VIS-405 supplies the mass-floor consequence, WI-420 the Poisson-capacity consequence, and FD-292 shows how a generic obstruction can be made negligible relative to any fixed occupation scale without manufacturing the needed occupation itself.

## Which dependence must be preserved before a rank/frame collapse makes the problem coefficient-blind?

Möbius Cancellation and Prime Circle now isolate two different linear-algebraic collapses. MC-485 computes the complete inverse-shift character Gram exactly as `pI-J-vv*`; once the first-exit coefficients are frozen to a common vector, bare `q`-motion has sharp coefficient-blind norm `sqrt(p)` and supplies no uniform subconductor saving. The only substantial live resource in MC-484 is therefore the **joint** `q`-dependence of phase, source interval and residual pair-sieve mask. PC-414 reaches a stronger source-blindness statement from the opposite direction: after the descendant map has reduced each Mellin frequency to a rank-one source channel, every source-independent conductor tensor—prime-local or cross-prime, sparse or dense, fixed or growing at finite cutoff—pulls back to a universal conductor contraction times the same source amplitude.

Nyman--Beurling shows that such a collapse can also preserve a sharply identifiable exceptional subspace rather than only destroy information. The sharpened NB-306 gives the exact unit right-singular subspace `U_floor(N/m)` and the exact norm plateau `beta_(N+1)(m)=1 iff 2m<=N`; beyond that threshold the unit core disappears and the unresolved quantity is the singular spectrum together with occupation by the actual first-free datum.

The reusable cross-line question is therefore not merely whether an operator has oscillation, large rank or a favorable average. It is: **at what stage does the source enter only through a coefficient-blind frame/rank-one channel, what exceptional subspace survives that collapse, and is the destination datum forced to occupy it?** A positive route must either keep a source-dependent interaction before the collapse, as MC-484 now requires and PC-414 explicitly leaves open, or prove nontrivial occupation of the surviving singular modes, as NB-306 makes necessary. Algebra added after the collapse cannot restore information that the exact factorization has already removed.

## What replaces a scalar summary when its normalization degenerates or its targets cluster?

Arithmetic Fidelity and Weil Inertia give complementary warnings against treating one scalar participation parameter as a regime. AF-522 constructs anchored, simple, unit-weight outward prime-to-composite transports for which the normalized Dirichlet value tends to zero while the relative curvature tends to any prescribed value in `[0,1]`. Even a common extinction order does not determine the curvature limit: higher logarithmic-moment participation remains independent once the zeroth-moment denominator degenerates. WI-418--WI-420 show the geometric analogue: a count of shallow defects and a list of one-target costs do not determine the required source budget when targets lie within the reciprocal-depth interaction scale; the correct object is the joint Poisson capacity of the scaled configuration.

The combined question is whether other Mathia destinations admit an exact **replacement object at degeneration**—a finite moment profile, joint capacity, singular occupation measure or another configuration-level invariant—rather than an ever larger list of microscopic details. Such an object is useful only if it is both destination-complete at the stated scale and constrained by the source category. AF-522 shows that `rho_0->0` alone is not complete; WI-420 shows that raw multiplicity is not complete at critical clustering. The next useful theorem in any analogous line should identify the minimal joint statistic before trying to bound it.
