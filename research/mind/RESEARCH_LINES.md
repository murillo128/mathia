## When does an escape from one obstruction survive the next reduction?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit, [MI-030](intuition/MI-030-effective-complexity-is-a-geometry-weighted-budget.md) sharpens the resource side, [MI-031](intuition/MI-031-source-dependence-must-change-the-surviving-quotient-not-just-the-representation.md) sharpens provenance, [MI-032](intuition/MI-032-ambient-obstructions-matter-only-on-the-admissible-image.md) adds reachability, and [MI-033](intuition/MI-033-determinant-wrappers-do-not-create-new-effective-rank-after-carrier-reduction.md) adds nonlinear carrier-and-scale reduction.

Arithmetic Fidelity now rules out a stronger provenance package at the canonical Gibbs interface. AF-480--AF-482 show that the sharp local and global `1/log` boundary-conditioning law is already determined by prime-scale counting density, one-crossing geometry and the scalar `P_Q(2)`. AF-483 adds a nonintegral Beurling-prime control arbitrarily close termwise to the rational primes with the same counting law, exact `P_Q(2)=P(2)`, and rationally independent logarithms, hence free numerical factorization. The canonical Gibbs margin is still identical at leading order. A rational-prime discriminator must therefore survive controls matched not only in density and conditioning but also in exact square mass and free multiplicativity.

Farey Discrepancy has pinned the macroscopic signed exact-omission transition at the density scale and now separates two positive-capacity regimes. FD-255 makes every `K=o(H)` signed family coercive at `H^2` scale; FD-256 shows that `K=Theta(H)` can hide essentially the full signed top-band capacity. FD-257 proves fixed-power relative-capacity depletion for general nonnegative cushions with bounded switched response. FD-259 shows that if the Möbius increments `mu*b` are also nonnegative, so the divisor response is monotone, the aggregate depletion strengthens sharply to `O(1/x)` on divisor scale `x`. Thus positivity of the coefficients and positivity of the response increments are different source resources. FD-258 separately caps polynomial reservoir depth through the first switched row and the Mertens zero frontier.

Möbius Cancellation has closed the simplest fixed-rank continuous projective gap route both topologically and quantitatively. MC-442 makes every path-distance coefficient sign, and the sign of the first order-sensitive scalar `J`, source-generic throughout the positive-gap cone. MC-443 proves that normalized gap shapes of any fixed number of selected primes are dense in the full positive simplex. MC-444 then shows that on every compact interior shape class an `eta`-approximation can be realized by selected primes with squarefree source height `n<=C eta^(-40r/19)`. Fixed-rank continuous normalized gap statistics therefore cannot regain robust prime specificity by appealing to an unspecified superpolynomial realization cost. Surviving information must be distributional/natural-source, growing-rank, scale-sensitive, discontinuous/arithmetic, or governed by a different quantitative constraint.

Nyman--Beurling now separates source-Gram repair from ordinary target sensitivity. NB-269 shows that an order-one center residue can occupy a `1/K` spike while the actual projected Hardy energy is `Theta(1/K)`. NB-270--NB-271 show that a rank-uniform Euler-anchor floor requires reciprocal-summable diagonal strength or, in full Gram form, `Theta(K)` operator growth relative to the canonical resolved channel. NB-272 writes the target-aware anchored residual exactly as a section defect plus an anchor-mismatch penalty. The target pairing changes the unconstrained projection but not the source Hessian, so it cannot itself supply the missing K-scale Gram repair. The live fork is to estimate the actual section defect/anchor mismatch or identify a genuinely new source-source component with rank-growing anchor-aligned strength.

Prime Circle remains beyond the normalized positive complete-Kron determinant branch after PC-391--PC-393. The thin contrast sector, not ambient size or the constant zero mode, controls the determinant correction. A useful continuation needs exceptional/cross-level information or a different state/operator category.

Prime Lattice has repaired the endpoint Mellin zero detector at model, source and actual-prime representation levels without obtaining the required quantitative transfer. Visual Exploration has now closed the whole fixed-depth positive compact moment-geometry branch as a standalone escape. VIS-365 prices fixed finite power-valued branching by minimum subset energies. Growing discrete support, continuous support and non-power compact scales therefore remain source-free conditioning at fixed depth. Weil Inertia and Weil Positivity have both tightened their category boundaries. WI-386 makes the fixed-width Weil zero floor profile-universal, and WI-387 removes the fixed-profile hypothesis for every uniformly spectrally tight adaptive family. WP-396 gives an analogous category warning under positivity formation: one nonzero modular/gauge frequency can be noncentral, but its modulus and positive square return to the diffuse centralizer already classified by WP-395, and the nonzero spectral subspace itself has trivial positive cone.

## Where has the exact obstruction moved?

Arithmetic Fidelity: to **a rational-prime discriminator stricter than prime-scale density, one-crossing geometry, exact `P(2)`, sharp global Gibbs conditioning and free numerical factorization**. Farey: at subpolynomial depth to **the genuinely signed `mu*b` regime and the amount of slack required by the actual Mertens repair**, because monotone response is now limited to inverse-linear aggregate capacity; at polynomial depth the same reservoir separately faces **the FD-258 first-row ceiling `alpha<=1-Theta`**. Möbius: to **distribution/natural-source frequency, growing rank, absolute scale, discontinuous arithmetic information or another quantitative restriction beyond fixed-rank continuous projective shape with polynomial realization cost**. Nyman--Beurling: to **the actual target section defect and Euler-anchor mismatch, or an exact source-source component with K-scale anchor-aligned Gram strength**.

Prime Circle: to **exceptional/cross-level or different-category information outside normalized positive complete Kron transport**. Prime Lattice: to **a PL-407-scale power saving for the single signed mod-5 Dirichlet-character variance supertrace, without importing auxiliary GRH, or a source-forced cancellation on its explicit-formula side**. Visual Exploration: to **growing effective depth, different coefficient topology, source-dependent signed arithmetic information, noncompact/global geometry or nonlinear/infinite-dimensional structure outside fixed-depth positive compact moment Grams**. Weil Inertia: to **either a stronger source-admissible control of zeta-sampled tails, or an explicit use of escaped high-frequency mass whose endpoint phases prevent recurrence; merely retaining a negative gamma margin is insufficient**. Weil Positivity: to **multi-frequency or another source-forced category in which positivity does not erase the useful arithmetic frequency or quotient the datum to null support**.

## Which physical cost survives after representation artifacts are removed?

The newest results sharpen the common proof order without producing a universal numerical invariant. AF-483 shows that even exact square mass and free multiplicativity can remain provenance-generic after the Gibbs quotient. FD-259 shows that one extra sign condition on the Möbius increments changes the positive-capacity currency from an unspecified fixed power to the sharp inverse-linear aggregate rate. MC-444 converts a topological density statement into an explicit precision-to-source-height tariff and closes a superpolynomial-cost excuse at fixed rank. NB-272 shows that target pairing and quadratic source strength must not be conflated: the former changes projection mismatch but leaves the source Hessian unchanged. PL-415 shows that five convenient coordinates can reduce to one signed destination statistic, so component count is not the intrinsic estimate. VIS-366 removes support cardinality and valuation language by replacing them with exact Vandermonde partition sums. WI-388 shows the opposite phenomenon: a vanishing raw sideband mass can remain order-one at the destination when logarithmic carrier cost is exactly compensated by logarithmic spectral sampling density.

WP-396 shows that even a genuinely noncentral source degree of freedom can disappear under the operation that creates positivity. MI-030 records the resource-side synthesis: complexity counts only in the weighted geometry actually seen by the destination. MI-031 records the provenance-side synthesis: literal source dependence counts only if it changes the surviving quotient after matched controls and classicalization. MI-033 records the nonlinear version in determinant routes. These are audit principles, not interchangeable numerical theorems.

The currencies remain line-specific. The durable order is to quotient universal scaffolds first, restrict to the actually admissible image, identify the exact carrier that survives the destination operation, and then price its geometry, conditioning, target projection, scale, spectral sampling, measure class and provenance. A resource counts only after all later reductions preserve it.
