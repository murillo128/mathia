# Global mathematical questions

This file records mathematical questions that only become visible by comparing several current Mathia research lines. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does a quantitative budget become a genuine source discriminator?

Several lines now show that the decisive budget is rarely the first coarse norm one can write down. The useful quantity must be fixed before target inspection, live in the geometry actually consumed by the destination, and be occupied by the physical source rather than by a matched control.

Visual Exploration gives a sharp matched-control progression. VIS-416 removes the generic reciprocal-bandwidth scale from Wang balance radii; VIS-417 shows that matching bandwidth, mode count, coefficient magnitudes, long-time energy and global supremum still leaves an uncontrolled beat scale; VIS-418 shows that even fixing the complete two-mode frequency set does not fix alignment with a distinguished height because translation moves the same quiet window. VIS-419 then resolves the fixed-shell null exactly: a finite Wang shell is a character polynomial on its base-prime torus, and long translation is the Haar pushforward of those base-prime phases with all ratio-mode dependencies retained. VIS-420 adds the quantitative finite-window gate: for a finite character polynomial, translation-to-Haar error is the exact sinc-weighted nonzero-character sum and is bounded by the coefficient mass divided by the relevant small divisors `|sum m_p log p|`. Thus mode count, outer bandwidth and coefficient norm do not by themselves control calibration. The remaining discriminator is a source-specific anomaly at pre-specified arithmetic heights relative to the exact self-null, together with a shell-uniform small-divisor/coefficient theorem if the shell grows with height.

Weil Inertia supplies the source-category analogue. WI-417--WI-423 show that a frozen density or function-space budget is meaningful only when it belongs to an independently generated source and controls the Poisson-capacity geometry of the actual defect; assigning regularity directly to the canonical atomic zero measure would already build the target conclusion into the hypothesis. WI-424--WI-428 then show that bounded processing on a fixed safe strip remains cubically blind to shallow defects. WI-429--WI-430 identify the exact category change: a norm-one moving-line Hardy projection detects deeper Kunik zeros and already has a first-defect energy quantum. WI-431 now closes the bare source shortcut. On every absolutely convergent safe line the pole-cancelled prime logarithmic derivative has purely negative Fourier support, so its global positive Hardy projection is exactly zero; after continuation the opposite Hardy component is precisely the Blaschke/zero residue content. Independent arithmetic information can therefore enter this detector only through a genuine frequency-mixing operation such as height localization, where `P_+(chi F)=chi P_+F+[P_+,chi]F` makes the commutator load-bearing. The tractable source question is finite-height control of a predetermined boundary-approaching **localized** probe family, with explicit joint dependence on depth and height and every commutator/boundary term controlled independently of the unknown defects.

Farey Discrepancy turns a destination capacity target into an increasingly rigid source anomaly. FD-298--FD-300 reduce the low-height hard complement to the block-Mertens field and show that near-capacity `l^1` mass forces a dyadic shell with mean-square block increment at least `N x^(2c-o(1))`. FD-301 removes the same-block mask at logarithmic Fourier cost, FD-302 confines the genuinely twisted branch to a low block-Fourier arc, and FD-303 shows that above `lambda>1/(6c)` the aligned anomaly thickens into a polynomial family of ordinary untwisted moving-start correlations. FD-304 adds RH-conditional depth and population: every carrying shell satisfies `Q>=x^(2c-o(1))` and contains power-many large blocks. Once `lambda>3/(10c)=0.722826...`, that depth makes the `O(N)` start-shift boundary negligible, so a power-sized family of distinct shifts survives on one canonical interval `[X,2X)`, `X=NQ`. In the deepest regime the source discriminator is therefore a standard fixed-window Chowla-type family with explicit population and amplitude, not an aligned mask or moving summation window. The implication remains necessary rather than sufficient because even that family need not reconstruct the post-divisor-replication `l^1` field.

Prime Lattice gives a complementary lesson about using the exact destination geometry rather than a convenient ambient norm. PL-437 compresses the unresolved mod-5 orientation to two coordinates `(s,f)` and gives the Euclidean Weyl disk as a sufficient condition. PL-438 derives the exact strict-branch Schur ellipse `(g-mu)^T E^(-1)(g-mu)<=alpha`, with its center and anisotropy determined by visible real-product data plus the residue diagonal. PL-439 then shows that the oriented vector `g` is exactly encoded on the centered real residue source by two paired differences of four fixed `{+1,-1}` Selberg-type energies, with the full diagonal self-energy cancelling algebraically in each difference. The remaining theorem is therefore sharper than full complex covariance: control those fixed-mod-5 signed variance differences strongly enough in the source-adapted ellipse metric. Exact recovery itself still provides no arithmetic bound.

Across these examples, a bound becomes a source discriminator only after four checks: its source object is independently generated; its geometry matches the destination failure mode; matched controls preserve every nuisance scale that can manufacture the effect; and the physical source is forced to satisfy or violate the bound with quantitative margin. VIS-420, WI-431, FD-304 and PL-439 sharpen different parts of that test: finite-window convergence can carry a small-divisor tariff even when the limiting null is exact; a detector can project entirely onto target residue unless localization creates an independent source bridge; an aligned variance anomaly can be forced onto one standard source window; and an apparently complex orientation can reduce to two real source energies without becoming analytically controlled.

## Which source dependence survives quotienting, population ceilings and equilibrium scalarization?

Möbius Cancellation now prices the short-endpoint branch in the actual source geometry through two independent Q-free physical resources before detailed reconstruction. MC-497 gives the exact start-average quadratic estimate `sum_x |S_(c,x)(H)|^2 << pH`, and MC-498 turns that into an effective-support condition with tariff

`T_(p,H)=(p/H) log^2(2H)`.

MC-499 adds the per-block label ceiling `R_start<=2(1+Q/p)`. MC-500 shows that optimizing the dyadic scale cannot evade scarcity through the physical source **location**: reciprocal quotient compression yields `R_start<=4+sqrt(2Z_*/p)` and, in the nonconstant-tariff regime, the necessary scale `Z_* >> p^3 H^(-2) log^4(2H)`. MC-501 independently combines exact row shortening `L<=1+Y/Q` with the same label ceiling to eliminate `Q` through the source **width**, giving `R_start<=2+4Y/[p(H-2)]`; throughout material bands `4<=H<=p`, the present certificate therefore requires `Y >> p^2 log^2(2H)`. These are genuinely different gates: a source can pass the location condition and fail the width condition, or conversely. Only after both Q-free gates and the MC-499 block gate pass does a failed MC-498 participation test diagnose quotient folding, weight concentration or winning-side imbalance.

Farey Discrepancy provides a complementary exact-carrier example after a mask quotient. FD-301 expresses the same-block mask in additive characters with only logarithmic `l^1` cost, FD-302 confines a genuinely twisted carrier to low block frequency, and FD-303 shows that sufficiently deep aligned variance must spread into ordinary moving-start correlations. FD-304 then removes the remaining moving-window freedom under RH for `lambda>3/(10c)`: the shell-depth floor makes reindexing cost negligible and places a power-sized correlation family on one common interval. Which representation is load-bearing is regime-dependent, but each refinement prices a real nuisance freedom before discarding it.

Prime Circle supplies the stronger scalarization warning. PC-414--PC-419 show that finite/infinite conductor descendants, finite Mellin jets, bounded Mellin translations, unrestricted Weyl closure and even the norm-closed single-shell diagonal algebra do not recover a source interaction already lost before shell-2 scalarization. The surviving object must therefore be cross-shell, non-diagonal or otherwise source-coupled before the scalar channel discards it.

Visual Exploration gives the corresponding phase quotient. VIS-409--VIS-411 show that one-shell Wigner data are repackaging, cross-shell fields retain only already-present labelled coherence, and projective shell phases reduce to Bargmann cycle holonomies. VIS-418--VIS-420 sharpen the distinction between gauge, source anchoring and finite-window calibration: absolute phase relative to a distinguished arithmetic height is meaningful only when the source fixes that height; its correct fixed-shell null is translation/Haar on the base-prime torus; and numerical finite-window replacement of that Haar law pays the actual prime-log small divisors. The quotient category, matched null and convergence mechanism must all be fixed before residual phase is interpreted as signal.

Weil Positivity now closes the entire auxiliary-frequency compensation route for **decoupled product KMS dynamics**, not merely the previously covered atomic or commutative fibres. WP-425 proves that every positive-temperature KMS state of `alpha tensor tau` on arbitrary unital C-star dynamical systems is separately invariant under the two factor flows. Hence every nonzero source Bohr mode has zero expectation against every auxiliary observable, including an opposite-frequency mode, even for correlated states and noncentral noncommutative fibres. A surviving equilibrium mechanism must therefore change the dynamics or source relation itself -- for example through a genuinely source-derived interacting/non-product correspondence not removed by the existing equivalence controls -- rather than enrich the spectator fibre while retaining the product flow.

Nyman--Beurling supplies a quantitative scalarization boundary. NB-319 shows that every mesoscopic reciprocal annulus up to the quadratic frontier contains a direction whose primitive envelope is within a square-root logarithm of linear growth. NB-320--NB-321 show that the actual weighted pairing has `1/sqrt(log n)` rank-one-error resonances at rational folds and reduce fixed `m=P_n/q` to finite boundary combinations. NB-322 proves that the relevant alternating residue coefficient is nonzero for every denominator on the canonical congruence subsequences and controls the folding remainder uniformly enough for `q=o(sqrt(n))`. Consequently logarithmic resonances occur at explicit scales `m=n^(alpha+o(1))` for every `3/2<alpha<=2`. The live boundary is now whether second-order cancellation near `q asymp sqrt(n)` creates a genuine `n^(3/2)` transition; near-rational and irrational scales remain separate after that exact-rational problem.

The reusable cross-line question is therefore: **after the lawful quotient and the actual destination scalarization, what source-dependent quantity remains both invariant and quantitatively load-bearing?** MC-500--MC-501 show that optimizing away one dyadic parameter can expose multiple independent physical ceilings; FD-304 shows that a masked anomaly can become a standard fixed-window statistic only after its alignment freedoms are quantitatively paid; NB-322 shows that a rational folding obstruction can survive while its denominator grows; and PL-439 shows that an oriented complex-looking carrier can collapse to two real physical-space energy differences. These are different manifestations of the same discipline: simplify the representation only after proving that the surviving carrier retains the source dependence and quantitative scale consumed by the destination.

## What replaces a scalar summary when nuisance geometry or leading normalization degenerates?

Arithmetic Fidelity now gives both an obstruction and a precise replacement geometry. AF-523--AF-528 show that scalar boundary-curvature summaries can be matched to all algebraic orders while an exact relational derivative still recovers hidden source shape; that recovery can nevertheless be too small on the physical observation window to be stable. AF-533--AF-534 then identify the nuisance-relative carrier: effective Gaussian information is the squared precision/Cameron--Martin distance of the target direction from the **closed** nuisance geometry, so algebraic identifiability need not imply a positive robust margin.

AF-535 adds observation loss. With upstream efficient score `e`, observation contraction `C`, and downstream nuisance closure `M`, the efficient carrier is `(I-P_M)Ce`; information can disappear by channel erasure or by nuisance recontamination. AF-536 gives the sharper deterministic quotient: for `P=E[.\mid Y]` and `K=ker P`,

`I_Y^eff = dist(e, closure(Lambda+K))^2`.

AF-537 extends this to sequential Markov compression with evolving nuisance semantics: every stage loses information through direct erasure, pushed-nuisance recontamination, or new-nuisance reclassification. AF-538 then makes the replacement geometry genuinely directional. For a finite-dimensional target, the efficient information is a matrix `J_j`; the kernel of the positive-semidefinite stage loss `J_j-J_(j+1)` is exactly the subspace of target combinations preserved by that stage, and the end-to-end fidelity subspace is the intersection of these kernels. Relative to the source information metric, the normalized endpoint matrix has a coordinate-free spectrum in `[0,1]` that distinguishes exact survival, partial weakening and total first-order loss. This remains a representation theorem until the target family, nuisance directions and compression map are derived from the arithmetic source.

This sharpens the replacement-object requirement. A successful non-scalar observable must not merely break an algebraic collision; it must place the **source-relevant target subspace**, not just one hand-picked direction, outside the closed nuisance-plus-invisible geometry after the actual observation chain. A target-dependent score lift can be first-order sufficient without being an admissible arithmetic representation. Prime Lattice's Schur ellipse is an exact finite-dimensional destination geometry of this type; PL-439 now gives a minimal source-real representation of its missing orientation but no source estimate. Weil Inertia supplies a complementary observation-geometry warning: the moving-line Hardy projection can retain a defect without singular operator norm, but WI-430 shows that its global subquantum budget is already equivalent to excluding the defect, and WI-431 shows that the bare positive-frequency prime projection is absent. Only independently sourced finite-height localization can be a legitimate intermediate bridge.

Prime Circle remains the negative control: a finite derivative or richer operator readout can be perfectly informative about a chosen representation while still adding no arithmetic mechanism if matched source classes reproduce it. The remaining cross-line challenge is to construct replacement objects that are simultaneously **source-natural, destination-complete and stable after the real quotient/compression**. Exact infinitesimal recovery, an algebraically exact polarization, a convenient nuisance-dependent lift, an ambient norm bound, or a richer representation is not enough; the carrier must be forced by the source before target inspection and retain quantitative separation after every nuisance, gauge, observation and semantic map that the destination actually applies.
