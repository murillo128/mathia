## Which apparent resource survives the exact destination map?

The common audit remains: identify the admissible source image, quotient universal scaffolding and nuisance directions, follow the exact information-loss map into the destination, and only then price the surviving resource. The newest evidence shows that failure can occur even when exact information survives and even after an apparently sensible regrouping or localization. Arithmetic Fidelity now has an explicit canonical inverse for a whole curvature profile after quotienting only global dilation, yet AF-511 shows that exact prime provenance has zero positive margin in a natural relative whole-tail metric under remote prime-to-composite edits. Farey Discrepancy moves the exact physical repair map inside a Perron contour, but FD-282 shows that taking absolute values on the resulting horizontal collar still exposes a self-divisor depth plateau. Weil Inertia can move a Blaschke probe to any target height, but WI-411 shows that this local sensitivity carries a conserved height-area budget and uniform coverage of a length-`L` interval costs `Theta(L)` coefficient mass.

Nyman--Beurling now gives an exact intermediate example at the level of ordinary finite-section coercivity. The canonical dilation can eject a large-rank source subspace, but NB-298 shows that its baseline-free sample Gram `Xi=L P_R L^*` is only a positive subblock of the pre-existing full future representer tail `E`: `0<=Xi<=E`. The nonzero principal-angle magnitudes used to describe source escape cancel after normalization in `YQ^+Y^*=LP_RL^*`, and for a fixed packet the gain vanishes uniformly in the dilation once the whole tail vanishes. The only surviving possibility is a moving-packet occupation theorem in which a nonvanishing target-relevant tail is actually occupied by the normalized escape subspace. Möbius Cancellation gives a complementary compression phenomenon on a complete CRT period: the translated character phase and pair sieve factor exactly, leaving only the ordinary local density; expanding the remaining incomplete sieve exactly and estimating every branch independently then pays exponential branch multiplicity. Weil Positivity is sharper still: passive full-unitary matrix coefficients may be genuinely noncommuting before readout but cancel identically from the source/range projections that form the critical Gram.

Visual Exploration shows the dual nuisance problem: after all obvious qutrit quotients, source-stabilizer symmetry leaves the residual weight law completely arbitrary outside a specified probabilistic mechanism; an explicit replicated-Gaussian packet model restores a falsifiable normalized-Gamma null only by fixing its replica architecture independently. Across these cases, nominal information, symmetry, dimension, exact injectivity, local sensitivity or an algebraically improved representation counts only after the destination topology, sign interface and source budget are fixed.

## What counts as a genuine representation escape?

A representation escape must change destination-visible information without hiding an equal cost in a complementary term, nuisance calibration, source budget, topology or target-alignment requirement. Prime Lattice remains a positive example: vertical shift changes the scalar observation family so that a formerly missing complex quadrature becomes recoverable, with finite-aperture conditioning explicitly priced. Arithmetic Fidelity now supplies a sharper contrasting endpoint: the full curvature map can be exactly and constructively invertible, with its scale gauge fixed, while a natural whole-tail topology still gives zero positive prime-provenance margin. Farey Discrepancy shows that moving an exact physical map inside a contour is not enough if the next interface destroys the needed sign: the grouped critical-line kernel improves, yet the absolute horizontal collar still pays a self-divisor depth tariff. Weil Inertia shows the complementary localization cost: moving the probe can restore local linear-depth sensitivity, but conserved height area forces a linear coefficient budget for uniform coverage.

Möbius Cancellation shows both extremes of representation loss: complete-period completion factors the hoped-for interaction down to density, while complete inclusion-exclusion of the remaining short problem explodes the branch count unless sieve structure is retained. Nyman--Beurling now shows that even a canonical baseline-free source escape is not a new coercivity reservoir: after normalization, principal-angle magnitudes disappear from the sample Gram and the projected dilation occupies only a subblock of the already-existing future tail; fixed-packet gain therefore vanishes uniformly in dilation with that tail. Visual Exploration shows that symmetry can leave so much nuisance freedom that no residual law is identified, while a stronger null becomes meaningful only when a concrete replica mechanism fixes its shape. Weil Positivity shows that even genuinely noncommuting fibre data are irrelevant if the chosen projection readout annihilates them exactly.

The reusable test is therefore: specify the admissible source family; state the exact quotient/readout and any packet/remainder split; identify the smallest datum on which the destination verdict changes; expose which signs, phases, membership bits, accessibility profiles, nuisance parameters or spectral directions survive that map; choose the topology or norm in which robustness is actually claimed; exhibit source-side control under assumptions weaker than the target theorem; and propagate the resulting margin through conditioning, truncation, completion, complementary terms, target occupation, height coverage, budget and normalization. Carrier capacity, formal reconstructibility, nicer component coordinates, local positivity, nominal coefficient signs, complete-period formulas, branchwise easy estimates, aggregate determinant growth, source escape rank, principal-angle size, symmetry invariance, matrix noncommutativity and finite moment budgets are intermediate facts, not substitutes for that chain.
