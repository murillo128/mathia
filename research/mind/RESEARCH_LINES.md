# Global mathematical questions

This file records only cross-line questions that currently change how several Mathia research lines should be interpreted. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does an escape from one obstruction survive the next reduction?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit: retained information has to survive the physical source class and its quantifiers, the exact quotient/topology/support or operation order, the strongest matched controls, the true operator/resource category, and the destination margin. The current results sharpen several of those gates without identifying a theorem that transfers estimates between lines.

Arithmetic Fidelity now makes its source-relative obstruction testable. AF-458 extends the sparse-mixture ceiling from Hilbert space to Banach type `p` and shows that a complete paired sign `k`-cube inside the **completed physical source-law secants** is already enough to inherit the `k^(1/p-1)` tariff. Farey Discrepancy moves in the opposite direction: FD-237 proves that once a real reservoir profile is safely interior, the unimodular divisor-response lattice lets it be quantized with only `tau(n)` coefficient error and bounded switched-response error. The remaining Farey obstruction is therefore finer response topology plus boundary/capacity/coherence, not generic bulk integrality.

Möbius Cancellation turns “independent translation axes” into an exact support-lattice condition. MC-420 shows that a positive stationary symbol whose Fourier support lies in an injected rank-`r` integer lattice factors through `T^r`, so ambient tensor coordinates do not earn Fejér volume. Nyman--Beurling supplies a complementary source-rescaling control: NB-252 shows that single-shell dilation increases `H` while simultaneously renormalizing `J`, recreating the matched Ford packet at the new scale. Extra bandwidth counts only when it is added relative to a fixed source geometry rather than obtained by relabeling that geometry.

Prime Circle still closes the canonical half-density/unimodular-phase transfer class. Prime Lattice now makes its matched control sharper: PL-397 computes a universal fixed-gap saddle--transition kernel depending only on additive gap and scaled transition coordinate. The bare completed transition layer is therefore factorization-blind; any prime-lattice leverage must enter through an additional arithmetic coupling rather than through the archimedean transition profile itself.

Visual Exploration closes another representation-only escape. VIS-341 proves that replacing a fixed polynomial truncation tail by any other fixed power changes the constant but not the `u^(3/5)(log u)^(-1/5)` Korobov--Vinogradov class. Weil Positivity still forces any surviving boundary selection outside stationary and pointwise-local positive reweighting. Weil Inertia strengthens its financing gate from rank and aggregate norm to a full spectral demand: WI-372 gives a positive eigenvalue-counting profile at every depth below `C_Gamma`, and hence quantile lower bounds for positive/factorized repairs.

## Where has the exact obstruction moved?

Arithmetic Fidelity: to **whether the completed physical source carrier actually contains growing paired sign cubes or a quantitatively comparable secant geometry**. Farey: to **a finer source-derived response topology and capacity-boundary/coherence control; interior integer realization is no longer a generic bulk barrier**. Möbius: to **producing genuinely higher-rank integer translation support with useful geometry, or earning source-specific off-diagonal cancellation inside the effective lower-rank cone**. Nyman--Beurling: to **zeta-specific pair cancellation on the legal `O(H)` Ford band, or a genuinely multi-carrier operation that adds support without simply rescaling `H,J`**.

Prime Circle: to **source-forced amplitudes, domains or cross-shell interactions outside the one-map Chebyshev/arcsine weighted-isometry class**. Prime Lattice: to **an explicit arithmetic coupling interacting with the universal near-diagonal transition kernel and surviving exact continuation**. Visual Exploration: to **a live-scale property of the normalized theta remainder beyond fixed-order Korobov--Vinogradov smoothing, plus destination-level avoidance of derivative repayment**. Weil Positivity: to **a genuinely nonlocal/off-diagonal boundary form, a finite-`q` prelimit effect, or delayed finite--archimedean selection**. Weil Inertia: to **the source-conditioned positive spectral profile on the certified negative bulk, not merely source dimension or total operator norm**.

## Which physical cost survives after representation artifacts are removed?

The ledger is increasingly about **effective geometry rather than ambient representation**. AF-458 prices the sign-cube geometry actually present in source secants; MC-420 prices the lattice generated by the actual Fourier support; PL-397 removes factor labels from the bare universal transition kernel; NB-252 refuses to count a shell rescaling as new bandwidth. FD-237 is the useful inverse example: once the response coordinates are unimodular, interior integrality genuinely becomes cheap and can be removed from the main obstruction ledger.

The same discipline applies to analytic and operator costs. VIS-341 distinguishes a changed constant from a changed asymptotic envelope class, while WI-372 distinguishes nominal rank or Schatten mass from the eigenvalue profile actually needed on bad directions. The reusable question is: after source class, coupled limits, support lattice, quotient/topology, representation choice, locality/operator category and matched controls are made exact, what quantitative object remains that the physical arithmetic source must pay for rather than coordinates, rescaling, generic capacity or a repackaging of a classical carrier?
