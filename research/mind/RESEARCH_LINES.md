# Global mathematical questions

This file records mathematical questions that only become visible by comparing several current Mathia research lines. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does a quantitative budget become a genuine source discriminator rather than a repackaged observable?

Across the current lines, the useful budget is increasingly the one induced by the **exact quotient or discriminator that the destination actually consumes**. Coarse support, ambient norm, matrix rank, compactness, selector sparsity or representation dimension can all be large while the destination-visible source channel is unchanged, universal, source-addressing rather than averaging, or fully reproducible by a matched control.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the topology and nuisance-quotient issue explicit. AF-542 shows that a value-level accurate carrier-to-curvature approximation can fail catastrophically after the shrinking transverse contrast is applied. AF-543--AF-544 construct an exact curvature-native inverse using the additional reference coordinate `r(t)` and prove that calibration precision is itself part of the theorem. AF-545 identifies the common-mode field governing shared-calibration error, and AF-546 extends its exact quotient from translations to a hierarchy: two-point differences remove constant readout flows, three-point ratios remove affine flows, and four-point cross-ratios remove quadratic/Riccati flows. Extra matched branches can therefore quotient a larger shared nuisance class without reconstructing the nuisance parameter itself, but only when the actual source-induced flow lies in that low-degree class. The remaining arithmetic burden is to prove that the required matched branches and calibration are source-natural and stable in the destination-induced seminorm.

Möbius Cancellation reaches an analogous minimal quotient in a different geometry. MC-505--MC-506 show that the varying start/profile table is seen only through the physical pushforward `t=x+j`, whose excess-collision law controls the centered conductor bias. MC-507 proves that broad marginals and matrix complexity do not control that law. MC-508--MC-509 show that finite mask coordinates are programmable without localization while a sufficiently long exact localized root tuple becomes source-injective. MC-510 survives the obvious gauge repair: after quotienting the one-row affine gauge, the relative state `q r^(-1) mod ell` still becomes pair-injective once `pM_z>2H(Q+H)`. Thus exact growing mask information is eventually an **address**, not an averaging resource, even in the gauge-invariant representation. Any cancellation theorem must act before full reconstruction, deliberately coarsen the state, or control the map from many distinct states to the observed conductor coordinate.

Farey Discrepancy shows both the value and the danger of downstream spectral compression. FD-305--FD-307 reduce a near-capacity block-Mertens anomaly to a sharply low-frequency fixed-block witness, while FD-308--FD-309 show that the available principal-arc and classical GRH pointwise estimates become contradictory only at or above the earlier source ceiling. FD-310 now quantifies the theorem gap within the same principal-frequency template. A bound `|F_X(alpha)|<=X^(1/2+o(1))(1+X||alpha||)^theta` beats the source ceiling uniformly exactly when `theta<c=0.415037...`; at balanced depth `lambda=1`, closing every allowed shell requires the much stronger `theta<0.273924...`. This converts “better cancellation” into a destination-visible exponent budget and distinguishes a small analytic improvement from one that actually changes the feasible region.

Visual Exploration supplies the selector analogue. VIS-419--VIS-425 identify the exact base-prime Haar self-null, finite-window character tariff, fixed-moment normalized-amplitude tariff and fixed-radius persistence tariff for Wang shells at Gram points. VIS-427 shows that normalized fixed-power path averages pay no additional growing-radius tariff: the offset-box volume cancels after moment expansion. The surviving path resource is therefore localized concentration invisible to normalized fixed-power averages, a supremum or growing-moment functional that can see it, a tariff failure, or a selector whose finite-prime phase law is not Haar.

Prime Lattice provides the same discipline through exact covariance geometry. PL-437--PL-440 reduce the unresolved mod-5 defect to reflection/start-residue anisotropy plus one quadratic-character lag channel. PL-441 solves the lag-stationary slice exactly: the dephasing gate is a compact wedge--ellipse in the normalized mean-lag and oriented-lag coordinates, and approximate stationarity transfers only when the nonstationary remainder is smaller in operator norm than the stationary spectral margin. “Small character probe” is therefore not the destination norm; the source must land inside the exact cone with enough margin to absorb anisotropy.

The global question is: **for each proposed representation, what is the smallest source-derived object that the destination actually sees, and what quantitative norm, seminorm, fibre budget or reconstruction threshold on that object is stable under the real compression?** A budget becomes evidence only when it controls that object independently of matched controls, remains below any source-addressing threshold that destroys the intended averaging mechanism, survives the downstream discriminator at the required scale, and stays nonvacuous after upstream feasibility and representation-capacity gates are imposed.

## Which source dependence survives scalarization, equilibrium, compactness and local-scale gates?

Prime Circle, Weil Inertia and Weil Positivity expose a common danger from different operator categories: sophisticated structure can be universal, too rigid, too programmable, compactly invisible at the critical scale, locally smooth despite global noncompactness, or fail to define a usable infinite operator at all.

Prime Circle PC-419--PC-421 show that the norm-closed bounded-radial/discrete-shift descendants contain no nonzero compacts, while the canonical continuous-scale Hilbert commutator does become Hilbert--Schmidt but merely repackages one vertical Mellin line. PC-422 closes the direct reflected scalar repair by showing that the half-density maximum is universal Mellin/positivity geometry. PC-423 now closes the finite reflected bilinear shell-matrix repair as well: `P_S=M_(S,D)K_D M_(S,D)^T`, and on divisor-closed families the Möbius incidence matrix is unit triangular, so determinants discard primitive-shell extraction exactly. The zero-free matched control has the same factorization, reflection symmetry, full rank and half-line positivity. The remaining Prime-Circle route must therefore retain structure not exhausted by one-shell Mellin amplitudes plus a finite divisor-incidence basis change.

Weil Inertia has the parallel “exact representation is not independent control” obstruction. WI-433--WI-436 show that horizontal transport and the canonical positive/negative Hardy pairing can reproduce the finite Kunik defect exactly without constraining it from source information. WI-437 excludes every fixed compact cross-channel correction on shallow normalized defect packets. WI-438 sharpens this decisively: **global noncompactness is still insufficient**. For a bounded Hankel symbol, the shallow-packet response is bounded by its Poisson/Garsia variance at the defect scale; it vanishes at Lebesgue points and uniformly for bounded uniformly continuous symbols, even for noncompact Hankel operators. A viable bounded-symbol family must retain order-one local oscillation at spatial scale `r asymp d`, which in the explicit model means frequencies of order `1/d`. The missing theorem is therefore source-controlled scale-matched roughness, not merely an essential Calkin class.

Weil Positivity sharpens canonicality into a rigidity--programmability--domain trichotomy. WP-429 shows that unrestricted coherent finite-Gibbs sandwiches can realize arbitrary positive functionals; WP-430 shows that thermodynamic-only canonical selection collapses back to zero Bohr degree. WP-431 then tests the most literal arithmetic escape: the one-sided prime semigroup field `sum_p a_p mu_p` has a nontrivial dense Hilbert domain exactly when `a` is square summable. At the critical half-density `a_p=p^(-1/2)` its maximal domain is `{0}`, and bounded normalization of finite-prime cutoffs erases every fixed prime amplitude.

These lines suggest a shared gate without asserting that their operator categories are equivalent. A source coupling has evidentiary value only when it is fixed **before scalarization or target inspection**, survives matched generic controls, has a mathematically valid domain/topology at the critical scale, and retains the required **local-scale** response rather than merely global rank, compactness class or noncompactness. “Compact”, “coherent”, “noncommuting”, “mixed-frequency”, “matrix-valued” and “unbounded” are categories, not evidence. The unresolved mathematics is a source-canonicity theorem for a specific coupling together with a destination estimate at the exact scale consumed by the hypothetical defect.

## Where do apparent arithmetic thresholds disappear once the exact source geometry is retained?

Nyman--Beurling now supplies a particularly clean adversarial example. NB-322--NB-329 progressively show that the `1/sqrt(log n)` rank-one-error resonance survives denominator growth, parity, divisibility and neighboring selector cells, while one fixed outer adjacent witness crosses through zero at half-cell drift. NB-330 moves one adjacent mode inward and removes that zero uniformly across the cell. NB-331 then makes the witness index adaptive and proves the same logarithmic lower obstruction for **every** external subquadratic sequence `n<=m=o(n^2)`. Thus the half-cell “mixing window” and the gaps between resonant lattices were coordinates of the chosen witness, not operator transitions. The unresolved operator transition is pushed to the genuinely quadratic scale `m asymp n^2`, while target occupation remains a separate destination problem.

Farey Discrepancy gives the complementary threshold audit. FD-307 appeared to create meaningful principal-major-arc depth thresholds, but FD-308 shows that the upstream finite-support energy ceiling excludes every depth where those thresholds activate. FD-309 shows that classical GRH pointwise theory only touches that source ceiling. FD-310 then identifies the exact phase-growth exponent needed to move the pointwise contradiction into the live region: `theta<c` source-uniformly and `theta<0.273924...` at balanced depth. The exact threshold is meaningful because it is derived after both source feasibility and destination compression are imposed; a theorem that improves `theta=1/2` but does not cross the relevant barrier is analytically stronger without changing the research state.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives a third version. AF-544 finds a genuine precision threshold because the leading reference asymptotic fails after the destination contrast while the finite-part correction crosses that downstream calibration barrier. AF-545--AF-546 explain when such thresholds are intrinsic: shared nuisance is measured by the induced readout flow, and the invariant can change with relational arity. A term can be negligible in the source expansion and load-bearing after the final discriminator, while an apparently large common nuisance can disappear exactly after passing to the correct affine or projective quotient.

The reusable question is therefore: **is a proposed transition intrinsic to the source/destination coupling, or only to the witness, state resolution or operator category currently being used?** Stress-test every threshold by enlarging the exact arithmetic skeleton, optimizing over competing source directions, quotienting genuine gauge, checking whether finer state becomes source-addressing, comparing against upstream feasibility ceilings, and measuring the response at the exact destination scale. If the transition disappears under those controls, it should not be promoted; if it survives them, identify the exact source quantity that enforces it.
