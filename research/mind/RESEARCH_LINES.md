## How much source information can an analytic architecture exploit at bounded conditioning?

Möbius Cancellation now has a six-stage invariant budget. Positive noise alone is not the obstruction; **aggregate surviving channel capacity** is.

FD-168 provides the complementary positive example. A sub-square-root Möbius prefix is recovered from a coordinate-minimal sparse Fourier skeleton with a uniform inverse modulus. Nyman--Beurling supplies the dual caution: NB-176 gives a canonical source-native Gram witness above the capped-Poisson resolution, but NB-177 shows that square-summed Euler amplitudes make this witness spectrally compact, so the certificate can saturate below the complexity actually required downstream. These three lines now support one sharper audit: source information becomes analytically usable only after the exact source quotient, observation map, resolution and **inverse or dual distinguishability** are stated, and after the total surviving capacity is shown large enough for the destination.

Forward smoothness, nominal dimension, source-native coordinates, positive noise and raw feature count are therefore secondary descriptors. A sparse observation may be enough when it has a stable inverse; an extensive noisy readout may still have enough capacity; and an infinite canonical feature map may under-certify complexity if its singular spectrum collapses below the declared resolution.

## Which source fingerprint is selective rather than merely reconstructive?

Robin Extremal and Farey Discrepancy make source identification itself a controlled negative example. An unbounded exact observation hierarchy may identify the source completely while leaving the RH-facing estimate untouched; even a uniformly recoverable sparse Möbius prefix does not prove the Franel--Landau destination. RE-175--RE-177 show that full identification is not the only useful alternative: one boundary moment detects the known dangerous compensation at the destination scale, and finite sign complexity forces a quantitative placement horizon. The open question is whether genuine arithmetic input supplies enough boundary-jet information to make high-complexity oscillatory repairs expensive, rather than merely imposing those moments as extra hypotheses.

Arithmetic Fidelity says the same from the analytic side: source-class rigidity can turn a recovered flat jet into exact `TN_3`, while AF-391 shows a direct curvature shape theorem can do the same even without quasianalyticity. Neither mechanism is a zeta zero selector. Prime Circle similarly shows that source-sensitive autocorrelation, relative bound states, universal conductor singularity and even canonical escaping outliers remain nonselective when smooth PNT/Li structure or matched phase controls reproduce them; PC-329 adds that the smooth background itself cannot be removed at fixed complexity. Visual Exploration now identifies its source-sensitive coordinate exactly as the prime-power curvature residual after pole subtraction. In all cases the program must separate **source fingerprint**, **stable or destination-aligned transport**, **background subtraction/matched controls**, and **destination-selective sign or zero theorem**.

## Global discriminator: identify the exact resource a matched control cannot fake

The current frontier is a classification of source-specific coupling that remains after every free closure, reconstruction, compatibility, quotient, background subtraction and matched-control operation. For each proposed route ask: what exact source distinction reaches the destination; what admissible source class is being compared; what information is regenerated for free; whether a finite-cutoff ambiguity survives only by escaping to infinity or into a flat boundary stratum; what subset of the declared observation is actually used by the inverse; what native observation norm gives a useful inverse modulus; what aggregate channel capacity survives at the downstream resolution; whether a source-native dual witness itself becomes spectrally compact; what cheaper destination-aligned moment detects and spatially prices a dangerous control; what complexity an escaping compensation or renormalization must pay; whether an operator preserves oriented phase or only an autocorrelation quotient; what universal singular, pole-compensating or smooth low-frequency background must be subtracted and at what rank/profile complexity; whether a transport metric or moving positive readout derives the desired second moment or merely inserts/exposes it; whether a claimed arithmetic height property distinguishes the actual source from nearby matched zero ordinates; and what theorem finally converts the survivor into a Riemann-zero or positivity restriction.

A useful bridge should fail on a matched control for an independently arithmetic reason. Exact reconstruction, unbounded observation count, source-specific labels, a critical boundary, a rich spectrum, a positive metric, a positive operator-valued lift, state-dependent scalarization, a nonzero low-frequency carrier, a large source-native Gram certificate, a small formal error, benign forward regularity, positive readout noise, membership in the zeta zero set, a singular limit or an escaping eigenvalue are not enough unless the surviving quantity is stably distinguishable, survives the relevant background/complexity audit and is selectively coupled to the RH destination.
