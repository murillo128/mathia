# Global mathematical questions

This file records mathematical questions that only become visible by comparing several current Mathia research lines. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does a frozen capacity bound become a genuine source discriminator?

Visual Exploration and Weil Inertia expose a common gate in very different categories. VIS-405 proves that a pre-fixed numerical Lipschitz budget on a monotone latent decoder is equivalent to a concrete uniform mass floor on the observable residual law, and VIS-406 turns ordinary fixed representation budgets into explicit examples. VIS-407 adds the invariance test: a numerical regularity bound in a singular source chart can be a coordinate artifact even when the intrinsic source path is regular.

VIS-408 resolves the direct Wang handoff negatively. The actual final Wang packet class is infinite-dimensional and has no packet-uniform derivative, finite-basis, degree or Lipschitz budget, while its variational cosine optimizer is a scalar zero-pair kernel with no proved source-to-qutrit response dictionary. Thus the missing object is not a better decoder estimate inside the same packet interface. It is a **source-derived response map with a target-independent capacity bound**. Until that map exists, qutrit decoder capacity is an externally imposed representation choice rather than arithmetic evidence.

WI-417--WI-420 supply the geometric analogue once a two-dimensional source-density cap `B` is independently fixed: the critical reciprocal-depth Blaschke crossover is an exact Poisson capacity with a finite-dimensional dual and bang-bang extremizers. WI-421 shows that generic regularity is not enough because TV, Carleson and vanishing-Carleson control tolerate target-adaptive atoms at bounded local cost. WI-422 sharpens the source-space boundary again. For the translated-Blaschke kernel, the bare global response is bounded on `L^p` exactly for `1<p<2`; `p=2` is only weak-`L^2`, `p>2` is defeated by diffuse far-field mass, and `p=1` by local concentration. The endpoint `L^(2,1)` works, while a second finite-TV budget can restore coercivity for every `p>1`. A frozen norm is therefore useful only when it controls the actual local and far-tail singularities of the destination kernel.

Farey Discrepancy now separates two occupation regimes. FD-292--FD-295 show that, once complete packets are available below square-root height, the phase and packet-capacity sides are not the main scarcity: the physical remainder must occupy enough reciprocal-log-safe gaps. FD-296 proves that at lower height, under RH, simple zeros and the stated background bound, even that population gate becomes a path-rigidity problem. When the full safe hard-remainder path has capacity-subcritical diameter, **one buffered physical return** fills essentially the whole safe part of the window. Below the rigidity threshold the missing source fact is therefore first return; above it, the near-linear occupation problem remains.

Prime Lattice supplies a norm-scale version of the same distinction. PL-429--PL-433 solve the relevant representation and deterministic transport problems for the dyadic quarter-turn probe. PL-434 then shows that the directly matched 2026 MRSTT theorem already controls that twist and fixed mod-5 progression channels with arbitrarily strong logarithmic savings, but only at density/first-moment scale. The PL-421 destination is a covariance cone, so source replacement must be small at the natural `L^2` variance scale. On polynomial intervals the published estimate misses that transfer by a polynomial `sqrt(H/L_X)` factor modulo every fixed log saving. A theorem can therefore be extremely strong in its native norm and still fail to constrain the destination geometry at the scale where positivity is decided.

Across these lines, a numerical capacity, norm, packet budget or exceptional-set estimate becomes a source discriminator only when four stages line up in the same model: the source fixes the admissible resource before target inspection; the resource controls the destination's actual singular, population or covariance failure mode; the theorem is quantitative in the **norm and scale actually consumed by the destination**; and the physical source is forced to occupy or violate the resulting constraint. VIS-408, WI-422, FD-296 and PL-434 are useful precisely because they identify different places where an apparently strong bound fails one of those gates.

## Which dependence survives normalization, rank/frame collapse and equilibrium scalarization?

Möbius Cancellation now gives an exact progression of increasingly intrinsic first-exit variables. MC-487 shows that the visible inverse shifts `h q^(-1)` are affine gauge. MC-488 proves that the exact progression-plus-cutoff first-exit cells form a disjoint least-prime partition, so a common transform cannot manufacture cross-`q` incidence. MC-489 shows that even a genuinely `q`-dependent row dilation retains a `p^(1/2-o(1))` complete-row diagonal under coefficient-blind positive `L^2` closure, and MC-490 rules out hidden scalar favorable directions on sublinear distinct-residue families. MC-491 extends small-family Riesz stability across repeated conductor residues by pricing their exact sieve-mask overlap.

MC-492 sharpens the remaining dense repeated-residue escape in the direction that matters for the actual source coefficient. Within one root branch, repeated-residue rows have identical character phase and nonnegative exact amplitudes, so their native common-sign Buchstab coefficients make every cross term constructive even on incomplete intervals. A dense Gram may possess a sign-changing small eigenvector, but the source does not get to choose it for free. Cross-root interaction drops to Jacobi-sum scale pairwise. The surviving Möbius resource is therefore not “more row multiplicity”; it is a source-derived inner sign/phase or kernel asymmetry, a genuinely collective cross-root mechanism with all tariffs restored, or a non-complete signed transform.

Prime Circle reaches a stronger source-blindness result after descendant scalarization. PC-414 shows that every source-independent finite-cutoff conductor tensor pulls back through the rank-one descendant channel to a universal conductor contraction times source amplitudes. PC-415 closes the natural infinite-frame continuation: in the trace-class regime the full descendant frame is still governed by the universal degree-two conductor symbol, and arbitrary Mellin phase changes of the base source act only by unitary conjugacy. PC-416 then breaks that phase gauge with the canonical log-scale position operator but shows that every finite-order extension still closes on a finite Mellin jet of the same one-shell source plus derivatives of universal conductor symbols. Richer finite operator algebra therefore does not restore interaction discarded before the shell-2 scalar reduction.

Visual Exploration now gives the multi-shell counterpart. VIS-409 shows that a full one-shell Wigner portrait is only a faithful repackaging of the shell state. VIS-410 proves that a full matrix of cross-Wigner fields for genuinely separate shells does retain their existing labelled coherence, while diagonal portraits forget independent phases and scalar trace aggregation forgets the entire shell frame up to `U(K)` mixing. VIS-411 then quotients the remaining representative freedom correctly: if shells are only projective rays, raw pair phases are gauge and the intrinsic phase-sensitive data are Bargmann cycle holonomies. Richer representation is useful only where the source itself supplies a selective relational law on those gauge-invariant cycles.

Weil Positivity adds the equilibrium version. WP-416 shows that under common generalized range, KMS dynamics locks distinct canonical source supports to distinct time degrees and orthogonalizes them. WP-417 removes the canonical varying-range escape: fixed-ratio blocks normalize to the old GCD/Poisson Gram. WP-418 then inserts the native phase algebra and finds exactly what survives: a rational-character compatibility mask can delete overlaps, but every surviving amplitude is still the same GCD carrier and the whole block is a profinite-Haar Gram. Unless the source independently forces the phase labels or a non-character structure, the new incidence is programmable rather than Weil-selective.

Nyman--Beurling shows the complementary quantitative boundary. NB-306 identifies the exact unit singular core and its sharp end at `2m=N`; NB-309 gives a polynomial lower bound on the first post-core gap. NB-310 showed explicit near-isometries immediately beyond the plateau, and NB-311 reveals that the apparent square-root-width restriction was mainly an endpoint triangle-inequality loss: exact remainder cancellation keeps the first post-core strip near-isometric for every sublinear overrun `h=o(N)`, with a broader general criterion `(h/m)(1+H_(floor(N/h)))->0`. A canonical defect can therefore be nonzero, quantitatively lower-bounded, and yet remain asymptotically tiny across a much wider structured boundary layer.

The reusable cross-line question is therefore: **after every lawful quotient, normalization and actual destination scalarization, what source-dependent quantity is still both invariant and load-bearing at the required quantitative scale?** Positive evidence must distinguish source-forced coefficient orientation from a free Gram eigenvector, cycle holonomy from phase gauge, forced incidence from programmable labels, and a macroscopic defect from one that survives only formally while shrinking on boundary layers. MC-488--MC-492, PC-415--PC-416, VIS-409--VIS-411 and WP-417--WP-418 are adversarial controls against nominally richer representations; NB-309--NB-311 show that even a genuine intrinsic defect still needs a scale and occupation theorem before it can count as coercivity.

## What replaces a scalar summary when its leading normalization degenerates?

Arithmetic Fidelity gives an increasingly sharp warning in one positive transport family. AF-523 shows that throughout the shrinking layer `0<t<=1/A`, source participation can vanish while the first three normalized moment layers share one factor and the complete **leading** relative-curvature profile becomes prime-like. AF-524 identifies the leading-order re-entry: when the retained zeroth moment de-locks from the tail-dominated first and second moments, the curvature ratio becomes informative on the scale `log(1/t)/(C_A log log A)`.

AF-525 then shows that leading-order blindness is not absence of all local information. Inside the AF-523 blind layer, the first nonzero curvature defect is of size `C_A log log A/log(1/t)` and recovers the transport multiplier when the anchor normalization is known. AF-526 shows that varying the cutoff already makes fixed-power anchor changes invisible at first relative order. AF-527 closes the obvious perturbative repair much more strongly: once cutoff and multiplier may co-vary, provenance-distinct controls can match the **same first curvature defect** beyond every finite algebraic order in `1/log log A` on the common shrinking layer.

The qualification is essential. AF-527 does not prove equality of higher curvature jets or of the complete curvature profile; exact full-profile inversion remains intact. What it does prove is that repeatedly extracting one more algebraic coefficient from the same renormalized first defect is not a robust route in the broader two-parameter control class. A successful replacement object must change what information is measured, use a genuinely nonperturbative/beyond-all-orders distinction, or derive an independent source normalization that prevents the matched controls from co-tuning their provenance parameters.

Prime Circle provides an instructive negative analogue of the word “jet.” PC-416 shows that a finite Mellin jet can be perfectly source-sensitive in the narrow sense of detecting the phase erased by PC-415 while still adding no new arithmetic mechanism: for the prime-circle shell it is just classical Gamma/zeta/local-factor differential data, and an arbitrary matched base profile supplies its own jet in the same operator architecture. A recovered derivative is informative only if its source class makes that derivative non-reproducible by controls.

Weil Inertia supplies the dual source-category warning. WI-420 gives the correct joint target object—the Poisson capacity of a scaled defect configuration—but WI-421--WI-422 show that its coercivity depends sharply on the source space. Excluding atoms is not sufficient; the source norm must also price the diffuse far tail in the dual geometry, or be paired with another budget such as total variation.

The combined question is whether other Mathia destinations admit an exact **replacement object at degeneration**—a genuinely new jet, joint capacity, singular occupation measure, nonperturbative invariant or another configuration-level object—whose informative component survives matched controls and whose admissible source class is independently forced. AF-527 sharpens the warning: even an all-orders perturbative signature can fail to identify provenance if the control class has enough coupled nuisance freedom. PC-416 warns against upgrading a recovered finite derivative to a new mechanism; WI-422 warns against upgrading absolute continuity or a generic norm to coercivity. A useful replacement object needs both destination completeness at the stated scale and a source theorem that prevents the same object from being reproduced by an admissible control.
