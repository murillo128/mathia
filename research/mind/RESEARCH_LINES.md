# Global mathematical questions

This file records only cross-line questions that currently change how several Mathia research lines should be interpreted. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does an escape from one obstruction survive the next reduction?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit, [MI-030](intuition/MI-030-effective-complexity-is-a-geometry-weighted-budget.md) sharpens the resource side, and [MI-031](intuition/MI-031-source-dependence-must-change-the-surviving-quotient-not-just-the-representation.md) sharpens the provenance side: after the representation is fixed, nominal complexity and nominal source dependence must both be reduced to the quantity that actually survives the line-specific quotient and destination map.

Arithmetic Fidelity has now separated metric deformation from genuine source restriction. AF-471 completes the smooth scalar-radial Hilbertization class on the full TV diameter. AF-472 keeps the original TV metric and proves that the full `s`-sparse simplex has exact Hilbert distortion `sqrt(s)`. AF-473 then shows the all-scales boundary is singular: allowing any fixed positive diffuse tail on an infinite alphabet restores arbitrarily high-dimensional Hamming cubes and infinite distortion. A surviving approximate-sparsity route must therefore control tail structure, weaken the fidelity notion to a scale-aware one, or exploit a narrower arithmetic subclass; small tail mass alone is not a scale-free resource.

Farey Discrepancy has pushed exact-omission conditioning far beyond the single-star regime. FD-248 identifies the weighted incidence-Möbius inverse norm `kappa(D)`; FD-249 shows one high-arity merge makes it unbounded. FD-250 constructs exact adjacent-pair omissions with `K=N^(o(1))` for which `log kappa(D)` is at least `(1-o(1))(log log log N)(log log log log N)`, so the norm beats every fixed power of `log log N`. The scale still remains far below the `N/K` growth required for macroscopic hidden coefficient mass.

Möbius Cancellation has now priced both sides of the relational-capacity boundary. MC-435 proves that bounded or `r^(o(1))` local decorations on the canonical prime-order scaffold have only subpolynomial primorial-scale source diversity unless their aggregate information budget reaches `Theta(r log r)`. MC-436 shows that the global ordinal pattern of adjacent selected-prime gaps realizes all `(r-1)!` types and hence reaches `N_r^(1-o(1))` source diversity, but only by paying that identity-scale information budget. The open issue is no longer raw capacity; it is source-height efficiency and survival of a useful residue through a Möbius-facing analytic operation.

Nyman--Beurling has narrowed the rank/coverage boundary to the logarithmic endpoint. NB-263 still shows that a strip fixed before eventual rank can be flattened when conditioning and later rank are free. NB-264--NB-265 force bounded-cost Euler-anchor response on fixed-power and almost-logarithmic radii. NB-266 pushes the same exact reconstruction through every fixed finite iterated-log enlargement of `log K`, while preserving aggregate `Omega(m)` response. Pure `O(log K)` coverage, its possible necessity, and the final Hardy/Nyman projection remain separate.

Prime Circle now has a completed scalar primitive-shell mesh phase diagram. PC-384--PC-385 classify full-root fixed positive inverse-power mesh/submesh behavior as universal. PC-386 shows that primitive restriction restores only a coprimality-sieved lattice kernel for `alpha>1/2`, with the inverse-square endpoint reducing to Jordan/Dedekind-psi data. PC-387 closes `alpha<=1/2`: the threshold has universal leading growth `phi(m) log(m)/pi` with a finite-Euler-product correction at zero cutoff, and the subcritical primitive average is universal. A surviving route must therefore retain nonseparable primitive information before scalar summation.

Prime Lattice now has both a source-precision curve and a model-side spectral localization. PL-407 says an LPZ-shaped variance saving exponent `mu` preserves the cubic `H^(-7/4)` layer at `H=X^theta` when `mu>3theta/(4(1-theta))`. PL-408 shows the same cubic filter suppresses every fixed interior of the square-free Ramanujan exponent-energy ball. The live bridge is therefore a moving-boundary signed spectral functional, or a variance theorem strong enough to dominate it.

Visual Exploration has classified the remaining compact fixed-depth scale geometry more sharply. VIS-356 proves that raw bank-size growth cannot lower the quotient floor while one uniformly conditioned `q`-scale anchor survives. VIS-357 shows that loss of every such anchor inside a fixed compact scale interval is exactly `q`-point packing collapse, equivalently concentration into at most `q-1` shrinking clusters. This still does not imply degeneration of the full rectangular map because growing cluster multiplicity adds Gram mass; the live compact resource is the width/occupancy/weight tradeoff, not scale count or collision alone.

Weil Inertia and Weil Positivity have both moved from broad structural hopes to sharply localized missing terms. WI-382 proves that eventual one-sided boundedness of the fixed-width renormalized Chebyshev remainder is RH-equivalent, including on the actual integer lattice. WI-383 then proves that this limiting remainder has negative liminf unconditionally: under RH it recurrently approaches a fixed negative almost-periodic floor, and off RH it is unbounded below. The only direct-repair carrier left in this trial family is the exact order-one source-perforation/rooted correction, or domain exclusion. On the positivity side, WP-391 shows that the canonical Bost--Connes divisibility diagonal cannot contain a nonzero exact prime-power-supported observable, complementing WP-390 where stationary positive interface selection is merely an explicit spectral mask. The surviving positivity routes must leave both categories through source-forced off-diagonal, modular, prelimit or globally coupled structure.

## Where has the exact obstruction moved?

Arithmetic Fidelity: to **source restrictions that control metric complexity at the relevant scale, non-radial source-forced structure, or a downstream theorem explicitly compatible with additive/resolution-aware loss**. Farey: to **the maximal weighted incidence-Möbius growth allowed by exact adjacent-pair supports after alternating rank merges already beat every fixed `log log` power**. Möbius: to **source-height-efficient, identity-scale relational data whose useful residue survives Möbius-facing projection rather than merely attaining factorial type capacity**. Nyman--Beurling: to **the pure logarithmic rank-coupled coverage endpoint, its conditioning/variation cost, and the actual Hardy projection**.

Prime Circle: to **phase-sensitive, nonnormal, tensorial, cross-level or otherwise nonseparable primitive information after the scalar full/primitive inverse-power branch has been classified**. Prime Lattice: to **the moving square-free Ramanujan boundary in the signed cubic source functional, reached either directly or through sufficient variance precision**. Visual Exploration: to **the clustered rectangular width-versus-multiplicity problem, growing order/scale/phase complexity, or a new signed source estimate**. Weil Inertia: to **the exact order-one source-perforation/rooted correction after the limiting fixed-width remainder is known not to provide a positive reserve, or exact domain exclusion**. Weil Positivity: to **source-forced structure beyond both stationary spectral masks and the norm-closed divisibility diagonal**.

## Which physical cost survives after representation artifacts are removed?

The newest results reinforce that the surviving currency is weighted, scale-aware and post-quotient. Arithmetic Fidelity prices hard support exactly by `sqrt(s)` and shows that arbitrarily small tail amplitude can still carry unbounded metric dimension. Farey prices exact defects by `kappa(D)`, with factorial rank-selected Möbius amplification now a proved source of growth. Möbius prices conditional source variation by an aggregate `r log r` information budget before asking whether any of it survives projection. Nyman--Beurling trades reconstruction radius against the Fourier decay permitted by compact nonquasianalytic cutoffs. Visual Exploration replaces raw bank size by anchor packing and then by the full clustered rectangular Gram. Weil Inertia removes both the polar main term and the stationary zero remainder before crediting the residual source-dependent correction. Weil Positivity distinguishes a source-forced divisibility Gram from both programmable masks and the too-small canonical divisibility diagonal.

These are not one theorem and their currencies are not numerically interchangeable. The adversarial commonality remains the one already recorded in MI-030 and MI-031: remove universal scaffolds, symmetry, known kernels, paired leading terms and category-programmable choices first, then price the source-varying quantity that actually reaches the destination. The latest evidence does not justify a stronger universal synthesis beyond that principle; each surviving frontier still depends on its own scale, operator category and notion of fidelity.
