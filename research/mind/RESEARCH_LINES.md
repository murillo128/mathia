## When does retained information become a discriminator and a quantitative resource?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit. The latest results sharpen it into three separate gates: a representation may retain source information, that information may still be cloned by a matched control, and even a genuine discriminator may be too poorly conditioned or too weakly normalized to finance the destination.

Arithmetic Fidelity now has an exact source-law collision geometry. Known mass, positivity and prescribed affine statistics filter the admissible signed circuits; AF-450 localizes Radon collisions by the source-law barycenter instead of using the worst global augmented dictionary. The next cost is quantitative separation from near-collisions **inside the fixed source-law fiber**, not another nominal rank count.

Farey Discrepancy has reached a positivity boundary. Nonnegative linear-capacity cushions cannot retain macroscopic late-band mass while remaining switched-subquadratic, but FD-231 constructs macroscopic signed blind directions with all-integer response only first order. The remaining physical cost is the one-sided reservoir slack needed to realize the signed Mertens correction as one admissible nonnegative occupancy.

Möbius Cancellation has found the first real translated phase in the well-factorable frame and an exact place where it disappears. Additive differencing creates divisor-coupled character translations, but every complete conductor block collapses to the Ramanujan scalar `c_q(h)`. Any new gain must control the incomplete translated family before completion, rather than count a variable that exact averaging later erases.

Nyman--Beurling has corrected its information target. Absolute carrier phase is needed for signed reconstruction but not for decay: the critical Ford residue squared is exactly a depth-marked local pair energy whose diagonal already vanishes. The missing theorem is a selected-height off-diagonal carrier-energy estimate at the Ford window/period. Published short-interval pair correlation and near-one clustering remain too coarse at that scale.

Prime Circle now distinguishes information retention from discrimination explicitly. Heterogeneous Fock degrees genuinely preserve cross-grade resonant contractions beyond homogeneous Gram data, yet the complete canonical word-Gram package is invariant under the same one-particle unitary that matches the reciprocal control. The next operator invariant must break that control rather than merely retain more tensors.

Prime Lattice shows a complementary topology conflict. Numerical frequency weights can be recovered when the energy structure is retained, but the native bounded almost-periodic zeta symbol exits its algebra at `Re(s)=1`. Besicovitch `B^2` carries zeta throughout `Re(s)>1/2` but erases isolated zero values and lacks the multiplicative algebra of the previous index. A useful continuation-sensitive invariant must retain numerical weights, a distinguished analytic representative and the RH-relevant target simultaneously.

Robin Extremal still closes the obvious same-ray multi-center escape: serial products of individually successful centers have exactly multiplicative Wiener cost, so center count without coefficient-phase disagreement is representation-neutral.

Visual Exploration has closed branchwise Wang occupancy and now has an exact inverse cost on the fixed-source transform. Coherent or fixed-frequency source structure is not selectively erased, and a smooth improvement of both the Wang remainder and its first log derivative transfers back to the source. Any transform-specific gain must be genuinely nonstationary/high-frequency and survive the derivative cost in the final destination.

Weil Inertia now separates sign from reserve. The odd-fold gamma image is positive and can be dropped for a lower bound, but its boundary-layer scaling prevents it from uniformly supplying the fixed bare-Carleman reserve. The unresolved bridge is the complete diagonal transport plus an exact or source-aware provenance for the quantitative rebound.

Weil Positivity closes the fixed finite interior-selector escape. A bounded Mangoldt Riesz anchor exists, but exact mixed-shell nullity forces every fixed finite interior jet packet back onto the same one-dimensional Mangoldt line. More selector coordinates do not create a richer source response unless mixed shells, boundary accumulation or genuinely new finite--archimedean structure remain active.

Across all lines, enrichment deserves credit only when the retained information survives the exact downstream quotient/closure, is resolved at the destination scale, is not cloned by the strongest matched control, and carries enough quantitative margin or reserve to be useful after normalization.

## Where has the exact obstruction moved?

**Source-law fiber -> oriented circuit -> near-collision margin.** Arithmetic Fidelity has moved beyond parameter counting. Exact sparse fidelity is fiberwise affine/Radon geometry; stability is the next unresolved invariant.

**Signed blind space -> positive realization cost.** Farey has explicit large signed null directions, so the remaining obstruction must be one-sided and source-realizable rather than a norm coercivity statement.

**Translated phase -> incomplete conductor boundary.** Möbius now knows that completion itself can erase the first genuinely nonseparable variable. The analytic work has to happen before that boundary.

**Anchored current -> marked pair energy -> detector scale.** Nyman no longer needs an absolute phase reference for decay, but still needs Ford-local marked correlation at a scale finer than available clustering and pair theorems.

**Mixed-grade retention -> common-unitary control.** Prime Circle has escaped homogeneous information loss without escaping the matched-control equivalence.

**Frequency weights -> symbol algebra -> analytic representative.** Prime Lattice can preserve the prime energies or carry zeta through the strip, but not yet in one category that also remembers isolated zeros and supports the needed index.

**Transform attenuation -> inverse derivative tax.** Visual Exploration must show that the destination consumes the attenuated Wang observable without repaying the hidden derivative scale.

**Positive omitted channel -> quantitative reserve provenance.** Weil Inertia must derive the actual reserve after full form transport rather than infer it from sign.

**Bounded selector -> finite-packet collapse.** Weil Positivity must postpone exact mixed-shell selection or use a genuinely non-finite/interior structure if it wants more than the Mangoldt line.
