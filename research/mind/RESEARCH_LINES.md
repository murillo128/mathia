## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the admissible source class, retained provenance/marks, observation rank versus latent dimension, carrier and resolution, finite-data fibre, inverse conditioning, destination quotient, arithmetic dimension, aggregation length, exact reductions/inversions, representation renormalizations, finite-to-global law, selector topology, active source range, local-versus-global quantifier, defect scale and destination margin.

Then ask: **what genuinely new source information is acquired; is it identifiable on that class; at what growing resolution; by what exact and stably usable map does it reach the destination; what reduction, inversion or renormalization could merely relocate the difficulty; what theorem globalizes it; and what physical cost remains after the most faithful representation is used?** A route that survives this audit has isolated a mathematical bottleneck. A route that fails it has usually rediscovered a provenance, rank, completion-fibre, conditioning, invertibility, gauge, separability, spectral, normalization, topology or quantifier artifact in different notation.
