## Which source relation cannot be repaired by moving the cost elsewhere?

[MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md) is the current synthesis. Across Farey Discrepancy, Möbius Cancellation, Nyman--Beurling and Robin Extremal, the recurring issue is not raw source dimension but whether the declared physical observation exposes a destination-relevant resource with a stable inverse modulus over the actual source class, precision and support domain.

Farey Discrepancy now separates source rigidity from observation coverage and from coordinatewise fidelity. FD-187--FD-188 price composite consistency and moved-prime amplitude strongly enough to force the Möbius source when both currencies are subcritical. FD-189 identifies a sharp observation threshold over unrestricted formal sources: dyadic signed Fourier bands `k<=H^alpha` recover the source tail exactly for `alpha>1/2`, while at and below `1/2` infinitely many formal coordinates remain invisible. FD-190 shows that every strict sub-square-root exponent remains noninjective even after fixing all prime values, all nonsquarefree zeros, every squarefree Möbius sign, bounded coefficients and an arbitrary finite prefix: unsampled quotient corridors contain composite squarefree coordinates supporting exact two-coordinate null perturbations with `D_q=O(1)`. The missing resource is therefore genuinely relational. Any strict sub-square-root improvement must exploit multiplicativity, divisor compatibility or prime-extension consistency, or change the observation family; the endpoint `alpha=1/2` remains open for this stronger source class.

Möbius Cancellation now has four sharply separated acquisition regimes. MC-366 prices adaptive coordinate queries by own-phase revealment; MC-367 prices parity sketches by target-direction span revealment. MC-368 shows that the actual scalar shell bias can sit outside both simple classes: `T=sum_i d_iX_i` is a subset-sum code. Exact balance leaks only order `1/R` own-phase predictability, but arbitrarily near-balanced integer weights can make the scalar exactly decode the whole sign vector. MC-369 then identifies the exact finite-resolution repair. If `d_i=1/R+epsilon_i`, the worst perturbation of a balanced Hamming level is `||epsilon||_1`; therefore the nearest-Hamming-shell quotient is statewise stable under the sharp strict condition `||d-R^(-1)1||_1<1/R`, and the leakage returns to order `1/R`. The next arithmetic question is twofold: force that aggregate imbalance bound at the endpoint scale and justify why the actual coefficient architecture cannot resolve below the Hamming half-gap. Near-balance without the second statement remains vulnerable to MC-368.

Nyman--Beurling has reached a quantitative endpoint capacity law. NB-200 forces every `o(1/X_a)` positive return with bounded endpoint ratio to lie at or beyond the certified atomic edge `2 pi e^Delta`. NB-201 sharpens this to a modulus: a relative deficit `d` below the finite edge costs at least order `d^2/X_a`, up to the inherited PNT resolution floor. The positive-source frontier is now at and above that edge, where enough cells exist in principle and a further obstruction must use actual von-Mangoldt occupancy rather than only cell count or maximal atom mass.

Robin Extremal now shows both sides of representation dependence. RE-200 proves linear stable dimension for vertical Euler sampling inside one fixed prime shell. RE-201 shows that the commensurate DFT panel is nevertheless globally noncoercive: logarithmic shell index aliases modulo the sampling period, and ordinary remote primes with integer multiplicities can realize the alias at selector reciprocal scale while preserving the KV source envelope. The live question is whether noncommensurate or jittered height designs can force every approximate alias far enough away that source fidelity or repair cost becomes prohibitive.

## Which physical source scale, analytic domain and representation gauge does the theorem actually control?

Arithmetic Fidelity now has an entirely compact order-five frontier. Prime Circle now separates analytic-class escape from observation-family completeness. PC-341 shows that the prime-log zero set is a uniqueness set for bounded-characteristic functions exactly at strip half-width `a>=pi/2`; narrower strips admit bounded Blaschke survivors. PC-342 shows that full-plane analyticity alone is not stronger in the needed sense: nonzero entire survivors with those prime-log zeros exist, but every such survivor must have infinite order. PC-343 then changes the observation family from semiprime constraints to **all shell responses**. Divisor inversion upgrades the zero set to every `log m`; finite-measure Gamma decay allows only polynomial boundary blow-up, while that dense integer-log set would require `exp(c/delta)` growth. The interpolation transform therefore vanishes, leaving exactly the common zeta-zero support for `sigma!=1` and a trivial kernel at `sigma=1`. This closes the arbitrary finite-measure all-shell escape but does not create a new zero mechanism: the surviving kernel is the universal zeta factor already explicit in the response.

Visual Exploration supplies the parallel representation warning at a moving-test interface. Shrinking support with bounded total variation therefore kills the coefficient-scale response; preserving a nonzero response costs at least `||h||_1=Omega(1/b)` and amplifies unresolved remainders by the same factor. Any genuine gain must cross a source boundary, leave the fixed compact regime or control a singular normalization quantitatively.

## Which quantitative resource survives a thin or moving destination?

Prime Lattice now classifies both natural extremes of its endpoint state family. PL-345 identifies the PNT-centered outer response with the classical first logarithmic Riesz residual, whose zero expansion weights `rho` by `e^((2 Re rho-1)a)` and whose boundedness at zero modulation is already equivalent to RH. PL-346 closes the full same-state completion and finds

`Q_W^a(u_a)=2+gamma_E-log(4 pi)-R_a(0)+o(1)`.

The apparent `O(a)` pole mismatch was caused by omitting the same-end/inner prime sector, not by a new archimedean mechanism. PL-347 then tests the opposite extreme: keep the full Gram matrix of all Fourier modulations of the endpoint envelope. Those cross terms retain genuine prime phases, but the envelope is an invertible multiplier and the modulation span is a form core for the localized completed Weil form. Hence finite-matrix positivity of the full Gram family is exactly localized Weil positivity; over all apertures it is exactly the classical Weil RH criterion. One endpoint channel is therefore classical Riesz data, while the complete modulation family reconstructs the entire classical localized Weil problem.

[MI-028](intuition/MI-028-complete-measurement-families-can-close-an-escape-by-reconstructing-the-classical-object.md) records the cross-line analogue with PC-343: enlarging an observation family until a classical inversion or density theorem makes it complete can close a loophole by reconstructing the old ambient object rather than by creating a new source-selective law. The useful frontier is then intermediate: a source-forced family that is not automatically complete but has a nontrivial arithmetic sufficiency theorem.

Nyman--Beurling gives the contrasting source-specific moving-scale mechanism. Its shrinking-band program separates qualitative recurrence, target-set volume, return-free scale, interval occupancy and finally atomic capacity. NB-201 adds a stability modulus at the atomic edge. [MI-027](intuition/MI-027-recurrence-existence-prevalence-first-hit-and-canonical-centering-are-different-currencies.md) remains the audit rule: match a recurrence theorem to the destination time quantifier, source granularity, requested precision and the observable surviving canonical centering.

Xi Flow remains the opposite control: once a consecutive packet is localized, local holomorphic inversion is well conditioned, while support discovery and assembly are global. Across these moving-destination problems, the relevant currency is the quantity that scales with localization, source occupancy/capacity, recurrence time, support discovery, centered residual size, branch transport, packet conditioning or unbounded frequency cost—not merely existence of a local inverse or a bounded spectral edge.

## Can reverse discrimination, signed structure or positivity be made destination-selective?

Möbius Cancellation now gives a sharper hierarchy. Generic discrimination tariffs can be paid by an abstract matched channel; coordinate/parity architectures are priced by provenance; exact-real weighted scalars can defeat naive dimensional counting through subset-sum coding; and MC-369 shows that a **specified** finite-resolution quotient can remove that code under an exact aggregate `L^1` margin. A useful arithmetic contradiction must therefore constrain both the actual source map and the physical resolution at which the destination observes it.

Weil Inertia reaches the analogous boundary for signed covariance. Ordinary and diagonal-deleted Gowers smallness can coexist with full bow variance in matched-energy controls, so generic uniformity constraints do not force the required negative covariance. A surviving statistic must retain source-specific signed two-point structure rather than only remove universal diagonal energy.

Weil Positivity now closes the natural finite constant-mode Hecke escape. WP-356 shows that one prime Hecke correspondence inserted before a cusp-boundary norm collapses to unsigned annulus energy. WP-357 extends this to every finite Hecke polynomial: the boundary-defect Gram kernel is `1_(ab>0)min(|a|,|b|)`, two copies of Brownian covariance. Arbitrary positive replacement lengths preserve the normalized Hecke multiplication law and all finite Gram positivity, while arbitrary reciprocal unimodular standing-wave phase preserves divisor-changing freedom. Finite arithmetic operator algebra before positivity therefore still does not create a selective Weil sign.

The surviving positivity routes must leave that universal finite constant-mode closure: nonconstant Fourier modes, infinite/all-prime completion with a genuine convergence constraint, nonlocal order not reducible to finite interval Grams, higher-rank/adelic truncation, or a source theorem that installs the critical weight/orientation in a noncentral positive carrier before scalarization.

Progress requires the missing theorem in the exact representation where the destination lives, while distinguishing genuinely source-forced intermediate structure from a complete reconstruction of prior art.
