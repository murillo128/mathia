# Global mathematical questions

## Which source relation cannot be repaired by moving the cost elsewhere?

[MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md) now has stronger stress tests on every side. Farey Discrepancy pushes coordinatewise fidelity to the exact maximal squarefree-rank boundary minus an `o(first-correction)` cap while sparse dyadic observations remain exactly repairable; the missing resource is cross-coordinate compatibility. Robin Extremal removes another apparent capacity tariff by reducing the deep Euler-jet repair width from quadratic to linear in jet depth. Nyman--Beurling shows that even true Euler amplitudes, positive source mass, bounded fanout and the sharp square-root amplification tariff can coexist with a DFT-scale matched-control code. Möbius Cancellation identifies a channel class in which inverse discrimination can finally be priced: additive log-smooth readouts convert source-edge displacement directly into Jeffreys/privacy-loss cost.

The common discriminator is therefore not dimension, coefficient accuracy, nominal source fidelity or a resource tariff that a matched control can pay sharply. A proposed route should identify the source relation or quantitative resource that an admissible control cannot reproduce by extremal-shell repair, reconditioning, spatial relocation, independently normalized spectral partitioning, hidden precision or likelihood-preserving side channels.

## Which physical source scale does the theorem actually control?

Arithmetic Fidelity has a two-regime prime-locality ledger. Uniform short-interval PNT gives all-prime-start conversion for `h_N=p_N^(-beta)` throughout `beta<13/30`. AF-402 uses persistence of ambient failures plus Brun--Titchmarsh packing to transfer a stronger almost-all theorem to relative density one of prime starts, reaching every `beta<13/15`. The difference records theorem uniformity and sparse-sampling geometry, not an intrinsic detector threshold.

Visual Exploration now supplies a complementary correction to the theorem-coordinate ledger. VIS-272 gives an exact Green-potential dictionary between Wang's diagonal field and the Rodgers weighted prime-power measure. A fixed physical `q` window pulls back to Wang width `Theta(1/log T)` and therefore has derivative norm `Theta(log T)`, but VIS-274 shows that this is not an obstruction when the concentrating exponential main term is retained and rescaled at the same time. The normalized statistic has a finite fixed-`q` Laplace limit with `O_h(1/log T)` remainder. The genuine next boundary appears only after cancelling that leading Laplace functional, where the first residual coefficient is still unresolved.

The general rule is now: state the physical source scale first, verify density/uniformity on the actual sampling set, carry structured main terms through the induced coordinate rescaling, and only then compare the residual theorem error with the desired arithmetic signal. Ambient almost-everywhere statements, fixed-test corollaries and generic seminorm bounds can all misprice a moving source window if used before this same-scale reduction.

## Which quantitative resource survives a thin or moving destination?

Prime Lattice has moved beyond the first-prime concentration gate. PL-338 shows that every finite prime-power activation is first-order flat, and its newborn reflected correlation is favorable whenever the transported boundary trace is nonzero. The local activation problem is therefore a trace-noncollapse gate; the remaining difficulty is global branch persistence, ordering and scalable multi-channel tail control across many activations.

Nyman--Beurling now gives the opposite warning about source-side richness. NB-188 proves that positive-mass Hadamard-scale Euler aggregation must pay square-root source-density amplification and bounded fanout. NB-189 pays those tariffs exactly with disjoint true-amplitude log-frequency bands and still obtains a DFT-scale nuclear code on a Poisson-packed matched control. The next resource is therefore not merely positive source mass or bounded fanout but **restriction of the acquisition algebra**: why should the actual Nyman architecture have access to a source decomposition that the matched control can already exploit?

Xi Flow remains a useful control: once a consecutive packet is localized, its local holomorphic inversion is well conditioned. The unresolved cost is global support discovery and assembly, not local packet inversion. Across these lines the useful resource must survive exactly where the destination becomes thin: trace noncollapse, physically admissible acquisition coupling, same-scale theorem uniformity, or globally discoverable support.

## Can reverse discrimination, covariance or positivity be made destination-selective?

Möbius Cancellation now has both sides of a concrete channel test for additive log-smooth readouts. The endpoint forces an extensive aggregate Jeffreys/privacy-loss tariff, while MC-354 bounds each edge cost by `(beta/2)||h_e||^2`. Such an architecture is impossible if source-edge displacement times inverse-noise curvature is too small. The remaining question is whether the real analytic endpoint channel belongs to this class or admits an analogous reverse-divergence upper theorem when it does not.

Weil Inertia sharpens a different norm mismatch. Below `theta<3/16`, all-start cancellation removes the source-location obstruction, but WI-332--WI-333 show that even very strong one-point phase pseudorandomness cannot beat the positive diagonal sliding variance. The missing theorem must be genuinely two-point and signed: a source covariance mechanism whose off-diagonal term cancels a macroscopic part of the diagonal, or an equivalent source-fixed diagonal subtraction. Above `3/16`, that covariance problem remains together with the exceptional-start quantifier.

Weil Positivity closes another direct geometric escape. WP-352 shows that projective Berry/Chern geometry is blind to the common scalar zeta factor, the full scalar connection contributes only an exact central term, and singular scalar curvature is just the meromorphic divisor. The zero-free reciprocal factor `exp(a(s-1/2))` preserves reciprocal symmetry, critical-line unitarity, divisor data and projective geometry while shifting the phase generator. A surviving sign route therefore needs an independently forced noncentral/global coupling or phase anchor before scalar-gauge quotienting.

Prime Circle and Prime Flute continue to reinforce the same discipline from other representations: scalar magnitude packages and local hard-cut correlations can look rich while matched controls preserve the visible structure or compactness fails before global counting matters. Positivity, pseudorandomness, self-adjointness or source fidelity counts only when it is selective for the exact destination quotient and defeats an internal control for an arithmetic reason.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, state the admissible source class, exact observation/target quotient, physical source-coordinate map, acquisition operator class, conditioning, sampling regime, likelihood/covariance/sign norm and matched-control class. Then ask what the destination actually distinguishes; whether changing horizons, operators or coordinates adds source information or only relocates a cost; whether the source theorem is valid at the induced moving scale; whether structured main terms have been rescaled before generic error bounds are applied; whether true amplitudes and source mass survive normalization; whether reverse likelihood contrast or signed off-diagonal covariance is affordable; whether a relational source law blocks sparse repair; whether positive geometry retains the relevant scalar phase; and whether the final survivor connects to a zero/sign theorem rather than only reconstructing arithmetic data.

The program advances when one resource becomes **destination-selective under the same source fibre, physical scale, acquisition class, conditioning and control class used by the claimed RH mechanism**. Raw dimension, deep scalar jets, high coordinate fidelity, exact coordinate dictionaries, paid-but-generic source tariffs, one-point pseudorandomness and scalar-gauge-invariant positivity remain intermediate resources until that matched-control test is passed.
