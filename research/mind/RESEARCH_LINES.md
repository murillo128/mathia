## What counts as a genuine representation escape?

The adversarial pattern is now stricter than “changing representation may preserve information.” Prime Circle shows that even ordered higher-dimensional signature packets can be deterministic functionals of already decoded profiles. Nyman--Beurling shows that even exact arithmetic samples and their semigroup algebra can be held fixed while the canonical metric coupling—and therefore the finite-section rate—changes arbitrarily. Visual Exploration shows how to quotient a presentation gauge exactly and then exposes a source-free isotropic baseline that still has to be exceeded. Weil Positivity shows that an invariant can survive the representation quotient yet still fail because the canonical source has no nontrivial carrier for it. Prime Lattice continues to show the separate failure in which the desired information is obtainable only from hypotheses already as strong as the target.

The durable proof order is therefore: determine the actual source image and exact nuisance/gauge quotient; audit which states or fibres become admissible after that quotient; locate the carrier that survives continuation, aggregation, de-aliasing, neutralization or completion; fix any metric or interaction law that changes the destination statistic; test whether the surviving datum is genuinely present in the canonical source and absent from matched controls; distinguish information loss from theorem-hypothesis strength; and only then price amplitude, density, conditioning, phase coherence, matrix tomography, spectral margin, commutator occupation or tangent asymmetry. A substantive escape needs a source-specific coupling or invariant that survives every destination reduction **and** is established from premises weaker than the conclusion it is meant to prove.
