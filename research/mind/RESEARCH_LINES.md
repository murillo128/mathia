## Which apparent resource survives the exact destination map?

The common audit remains: identify the admissible source image, quotient universal scaffolding and nuisance directions, follow the exact information-loss map into the destination, and only then price the surviving resource. The newest evidence shows that failure can occur even when exact information survives, when a singular boundary is added, and after an apparently sensible regrouping or localization. Arithmetic Fidelity has an explicit canonical inverse for a whole curvature profile after quotienting only global dilation, yet AF-511--AF-512 show that exact prime provenance has zero positive margin in the natural uniform relative-curvature topology even when the observation covers the full convergence domain `(1,infinity)`. Farey Discrepancy moves the exact physical repair map inside a Perron contour, but FD-282--FD-283 show that making the grouped right collar absolute exposes a self-divisor depth plateau, while delaying absolute values through strict-right `sigma` integration merely converts the problem into a Möbius Mellin sine transform with positive radial weights. Weil Inertia can localize a Blaschke probe in height and then mix it over horizontal scales, but WI-411--WI-412 show complementary conservation laws: uniform height coverage costs linear coefficient mass, while a depth-independent centered quantum over arbitrarily shallow defects requires infinite logarithmic-scale TV and creates a nonintegrable height tail.

Nyman--Beurling now supplies both boundary failures of its canonical baseline-free projected-dilation block. NB-298 places `Xi=L P_R L^*` inside the pre-existing full future representer tail `E`, so for a fixed zero packet `Xi` vanishes uniformly in the dilation when the ordinary section is taken deep. NB-299 closes the opposite large parameter: for fixed section and fixed off-critical packet, the dilated source subspace becomes asymptotically orthogonal to the old section while off-critical sampling contracts like `m^(-delta_Z)`, forcing `Xi=O(m^(-2delta_Z))` and making its ordinary finite-section gain vanish. Large source escape is therefore not merely insufficient; at fixed packet it becomes sample-null in both separate limits. Möbius Cancellation gives a complementary compression phenomenon: complete-period factorization leaves only ordinary pair-sieve density; exact divisor expansion of the short problem pays exponential branch multiplicity; and exact additive Fourier compression still pays exponential `l^1` spectral norm if twisted modes are controlled independently, despite small `l^2` energy. Weil Positivity is sharper still: passive full-unitary matrix coefficients cancel from the source/range projection Gram, and WP-412 shows that KMS scalarization also annihilates every unbalanced off-diagonal scale mode, leaving only scale-balanced cycles as a possible passive holonomy carrier.

Visual Exploration shows the dual nuisance problem: after all obvious qutrit quotients, source-stabilizer symmetry leaves the residual weight law completely arbitrary outside a specified probabilistic mechanism. Replicated-Gaussian controls restore falsifiability only when both sector rates and the full integer replica-count vector are fixed independently of the candidate. Prime Circle shows an analogous representation closure on the analytic side: the complete descendant semigroup remains universal not only for polynomial moments but under the full complex Mellin-character diagonalization, where the descendant factor is just a classical zeta ratio multiplying the already-known one-shell radial-flux Mellin carrier. Across these cases, nominal information, symmetry, dimension, exact injectivity, local sensitivity or an algebraically improved representation counts only after the destination topology, sign interface and source budget are fixed.

## What counts as a genuine representation escape?

A representation escape must change destination-visible information without hiding an equal cost in a complementary term, nuisance calibration, source budget, topology or target-alignment requirement. Prime Lattice remains a positive example: vertical shift changes the scalar observation family so that a formerly missing complex quadrature becomes recoverable, with finite-aperture conditioning explicitly priced. Arithmetic Fidelity supplies a sharper contrasting endpoint: the full curvature map can be exactly and constructively invertible, with its scale gauge fixed, while even observing the entire convergence domain in the natural uniform relative topology gives zero positive prime-provenance margin. Farey Discrepancy shows that moving an exact physical map inside a contour is not enough if the next interface destroys the needed sign, and FD-283 further shows that postponing that interface through strict-right `sigma` integration does not manufacture a new phase; the remaining saving must come from actual Möbius Mellin cancellation or cross-collar interaction. Weil Inertia shows the complementary budget trade: height localization and scale mixing can each restore local sensitivity, but uniform coverage in the conjugate variable costs coefficient mass, with the scale-invariant count-like limit leaving the finite-budget category altogether.

Möbius Cancellation shows three representations of the same warning: complete-period completion factors the hoped-for interaction down to density; complete inclusion-exclusion of the remaining short problem explodes branch count; and exact Fourier compression retains small energy but still fails if one converts the mode family to `l^1` by independent estimates. Nyman--Beurling now adds a two-parameter warning: the canonical projected source escape can be a subblock of a vanishing future tail when `n` grows, and can become almost perfectly source-orthogonal yet simultaneously sample-null when `m` grows at fixed off-critical packet. The surviving object is not source escape rank or principal-angle size but joint-scale occupation of a moving target-relevant tail. Visual Exploration shows that symmetry can leave so much nuisance freedom that no residual law is identified, while a stronger null becomes meaningful only when a concrete replica mechanism fixes all of its shape parameters. Prime Circle shows that increasing analytic resolution from polynomial moments to all complex dilation characters can still reveal only a universal zeta ratio and a previously known source transform. Weil Positivity shows that noncommuting fibre data can be annihilated both algebraically by projection readout and dynamically by KMS spectral selection; the first passive survivor is not a generic off-diagonal but a scale-balanced cycle.

The reusable test is therefore: specify the admissible source family; state the exact quotient/readout and any packet/remainder split; identify the smallest datum on which the destination verdict changes; expose which signs, phases, membership bits, accessibility profiles, nuisance parameters or spectral directions survive that map; choose the topology or norm in which robustness is actually claimed; exhibit source-side control under assumptions weaker than the target theorem; and propagate the resulting margin through conditioning, truncation, completion, complementary terms, target occupation, height/depth coverage, budget and normalization. Carrier capacity, formal reconstructibility, nicer component coordinates, local positivity, nominal coefficient signs, complete-period formulas, branchwise easy estimates, aggregate determinant growth, source escape rank, principal-angle size, symmetry invariance, matrix noncommutativity, unbalanced off-diagonal moments and finite moment budgets are intermediate facts, not substitutes for that chain.
