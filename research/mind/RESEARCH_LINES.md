# Global mathematical questions

This file records only cross-line questions that currently change how several Mathia research lines should be interpreted. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than merely transport, reconstruct, compress or refactor the source?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) and [MI-028](intuition/MI-028-complete-measurement-families-can-close-an-escape-by-reconstructing-the-classical-object.md) give the common interface audit.

Prime Circle supplies the reconstruction-versus-quotient boundary. Complete shell information can recover the underlying source, but PC-351 shows that the canonical triangular decoder has trivial ordinary spectrum and admits a matched reciprocal sign-flip control that is unitarily conjugate to the arithmetic decoder. Faithful acquisition is therefore not selective if the destination quotient erases the oriented source coordinate.

Prime Lattice sharpens the topology-and-summation gate. PL-360--PL-362 show that diagonal reweighting, non-diagonal completion with canonical coefficient cutoffs, and coefficientwise-positive smoothing all reconstruct the original convergence wall. PL-363 then supplies the first signed control: a fixed finite signed lattice-shift filter with zero period mean crosses to ordinary convergence on `Re s>0`, but the result is just a periodic Dirichlet series `P(s)zeta(s)` with classical Hurwitz continuation. Escaping the positive cone is a genuine mechanism change, yet it still does not create critical-line selectivity.

Farey Discrepancy separates acquisition from compression. FD-201 proves that fixed-mode cross-horizon differences really contain new Möbius information, namely multiplicative Mertens increments, but their immediate positive half-Sobolev compression still forces shellwide subpower RMS. A representation can gain source information and then overcoerce or destroy the useful correlation at the next layer.

Weil Inertia shows that formal output size and formal complexity budgets can both be illusory. WI-351 extends the finite charge-support obstruction to controlled infinite tails, while WI-352 proves that the first charge-Sobolev moment `B_1` is already bounded by the same Lipschitz constant `L`. Infinite support may be invisible at destination resolution, and an apparently separate complexity currency may simply be a repackaging of a cost already paid elsewhere.

Weil Positivity adds a globalization failure. WP-366 starts from a genuinely non-product fixed-height Whittaker feature in which finite and archimedean variables interact through `nY`; positive scalar dilation-covariant globalization forces power-Haar averaging and Mellin-separates that interaction back into the failed Rankin--Selberg product. Local coupling counts only if it survives the canonical operation that removes its auxiliary scale.

The shared question is therefore: **what source-specific coordinate is newly acquired, what exact closure/summation/globalization the destination must apply, what survives that operation in the declared topology and resolution, which apparent resource is genuinely independent, and which matched control fails to reproduce the survivor?** Richer data, nonlinear depth, non-diagonal topology, sign-indefinite filtering, infinite formal support or local non-product structure deserve credit only after all of those gates survive.

## Where does the obstruction move after a nominal critical boundary is resolved?

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the clearest stratified double-scaling example. AF-417--AF-420 refine derivative depth from the coarse ratio `s_n/n` to the joint critical coordinate

`b_n(a)=a n^(s_n/n-2)`.

AF-421 then resolves the signed finite-window regime into full-column saddle packets with transition fibers at `b=m^2`. AF-422 shows that even the equality of the two adjacent exponential rates is still too coarse: on a square fiber the next coordinate

`Xi_(n,m)=log n+n log(b_n/m^2)-d_n log m`

controls their amplitude ratio. Even parity cannot cancel; odd parity can cancel at leading rectangular order only when `Xi_(n,m)->-log K_m`. The remaining finite-window frontier is therefore the complete residual packet ratio on that tuned codimension-one interface, not the whole square fiber.

Visual Exploration gives the proof-boundary analogue. Exact gamma recentering removes the lower moving-source wall; VIS-299--VIS-301 then move the apparent upper boundary from `H/log^3 T` localization to a generic `H/log^2 T` Montgomery--Vaughan coefficient estimate. VIS-302 evaluates the exact Wang coefficient weight and saves another logarithm:

`sum_n n a_n^2=(4/3)x log x+(8/9)x+o(x)`.

The pointwise asymptotic therefore persists whenever `x log x=o(H)`. The first unresolved generic layer is now the actual off-diagonal time integral near `x log x asymp H`, not localization and not the coarse coefficient majorant.

Robin Extremal shows a third kind of boundary motion. RE-216 changes the analytic predictor without changing the arithmetic carrier and lowers the constructive coefficient-variation exponent from `12 log 2` to at most `6.08073274`. This materially expands the proven hiding range while leaving the converse carrier-capacity ceiling untouched. A proof constant can therefore move because the representation became cheaper even when no new source information was added.

Across these lines, the right response to a resolved threshold is to identify the **new load-bearing packet, error term or resource**, then ask whether that new object has its own collision locus, coefficient-specific cancellation or dependency relation. Critical ratios, theorem envelopes and representation constants are provisional until the full complexity family and exact destination scale expose what actually dominates next.

## Which physical cost survives source specialization, centering, reconstruction and analytic transport?

[MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md) and [MI-025](intuition/MI-025-combinatorial-breadth-must-be-priced-through-the-source-coordinate-map.md) remain the main source-cost syntheses, but the recent results further separate currencies that had looked interchangeable.

Möbius Cancellation distinguishes combinatorial source rank, differencing depth, family breadth and global conductor cost. MC-381 raises the exact-endpoint common-source obstruction to linear-in-rank scale until the analytic ceiling intervenes. MC-382 shows that the current q-van der Corput architecture has an intrinsic `a=O(log log y)` source-exponent horizon because required depth grows with `a` while terminal saving decays exponentially. MC-383 then stress-tests a different collective theorem shape: even an exponential family `A<=exp(CR)` cannot buy a rank-linear source exponent when the analytic estimate pays a fixed positive power of one global modulus. Its gain is only additive `O(R)` in `log P`; reaching `Omega(R log y)` needs factor-local/rank-sensitive conductor dependence or a different invariant.

Nyman--Beurling identifies the corresponding reconstruction currency. NB-217 replaces shell-width heuristics by the exact reciprocal log-support mass

`J_j(E)=int_E u^(-j)du`.

For fixed `j>=2`, signs, disconnected support and arbitrary upward widening do not remove the polynomial normalized reconstruction tariff while the source remains in a relative logarithmic annulus. A cheaper route must exploit correlation before absolute values, change the normalized source functional, or genuinely globalize toward much smaller logarithmic scales.

Farey Discrepancy shows that source-sensitive acquisition can become too expensive after positive compression. Robin Extremal separates predictor/Wiener norm from raw prime-carrier capacity and finer KV fidelity. Visual Exploration shows that a generic coefficient bound may overprice the exact source by a logarithm. Weil Inertia separates hard phase-orbit cardinality from soft high-charge tail mass, and WI-352 shows that `B_1` is not independent of the Lipschitz sensitivity budget at all. These costs cannot be merged into a generic notion of “complexity”: each lives at a different stage of the source-to-destination map, and some are algebraically dependent.

The shared audit is to price the route **after** exact source specialization and nuisance removal, then separately record acquisition rank, algebraic redundancy, predictor or transport loss, support/reconstruction cost, family breadth, conductor geometry, carrier capacity, destination-resolution complexity and finer arithmetic fidelity. Improving one currency is progress only if another stage does not repay the gain, and a new currency counts only after proving it is not already controlled by an existing one.

## Can sign-indefinite structure preserve critical information before closure or globalization scalarizes it?

Weil Positivity remains the strongest direct positive-closure classification. Positive finite Hecke structure retains arithmetic information before closure, but boundary squares scalarize the critical half-density. Rankin--Selberg positivity dies at critical specialization; projectivization does not repair it; and adding an independent positive Bessel/Whittaker Hilbert sector still leaves unbounded critical overlap and negative Fubini--Study geometry near critical zeros.

WP-366 rules out a more local escape under canonical scalar globalization. The fixed-height coordinates `lambda_r(n)K_(ir)(2 pi nY)` genuinely couple the finite and archimedean variables, but dilation-covariant positive averaging in `Y` is forced to be power-Haar and Mellin-separates the interaction back to Rankin--Selberg geometry. The critical positive diagonal then already diverges.

Prime Lattice now gives both sides of a coefficient-space control. PL-362 says coefficientwise-positive recovery cannot cross the real convergence wall. PL-363 shows that finite sign-indefinite cancellation can cross it, but the resulting periodic Dirichlet series is only `P(s)zeta(s)` and gains classical continuation without a new critical selector. The missing ingredient is therefore not merely “some negative sign”: it is a **source-forced nonpositive relation that remains selective after continuation or globalization**.

The missing mechanisms in both settings must explain where that nonpositive information comes from independently of the divisor being constrained. In Weil Positivity this may require a source-fixed scale with an independent theorem, intrinsically non-Haar/operator-valued scale mixing, a Maaß--Selberg-type remainder, or a cohomological/intersection sector whose orientation is established before scalar separation. In Prime Lattice it requires more than a chosen finite pole-cancelling filter: an infinite or scale-dependent source-forced summation law, nonlocal mixing, a genuine renormalized remainder or another global arithmetic correlation with an independently zero-sensitive destination theorem.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the admissible source class and topology, observation family and nullspace, destination quotient and norm, physical source-coordinate map, acquisition budget, requested precision, normalization gauge, analytic domain, approximation/summation protocol, moving-complexity coordinates, support/multiplicity constraints, transport or reconstruction loss, sign/covariance structure, dependency among apparent resource budgets and matched control. Then ask in sequence: **what is genuinely new at acquisition; what exact closure/summation/globalization is unavoidable; what survives it; where does the necessary signed or renormalized information enter; which resources are genuinely independent; what inverse resource must the source pay; and does a matched non-arithmetic control reproduce the same survivor?**

The current synthesis narrows rather than proliferates directions. Complete fidelity is not enough if invariantization erases it. New measurements are not enough if destination compression overcoerces them. Non-diagonal topology is not enough if canonical cutoffs rebuild the convergence wall; leaving the positive cone is not enough if a finite filter merely manufactures classical periodic continuation. Infinite formal support is not enough if the destination sees only a bounded effective tail, and a charge moment is not a new cost if Lipschitz regularity already pays it. Local finite--archimedean interaction is not enough if canonical scale averaging factorizes it. Exponential family breadth is not enough if a fixed global-modulus power absorbs the gain, and a theorem-level big-O is not a structural boundary until it survives specialization to the exact coefficients.

Progress therefore requires an intermediate structure that is nontrivial in the declared topology, source-specific after matched controls, survives the exact destination operation, remains quantitatively visible at the moving resolution, uses genuinely independent resources, and couples to a destination theorem without reconstructing the original RH criterion in disguise.
