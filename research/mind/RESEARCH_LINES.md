## Characterize the complete admissible statistic before choosing the repair

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-preserve-structure-before-compressing`.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives exact deterministic and stochastic formulations of the first gate. Given the observables naturally available to a construction, form their joint quotient; for a finite statistical experiment, form the full reference-relative likelihood-ratio statistic. The newer Weil-test hierarchy adds a resolution warning: a complete test functional may be faithful while every finite-dimensional projection has exact generalized-prime collision fibers, even arbitrarily locally at regular source points.

A decisive negative result is a conflicting pair inside one complete admissible fiber/statistic. A decisive positive result is exact recovery or a canonically forced lift, including a proof that any finite observable family is actually identifying on the declared source class. Downstream optimization, diagonalization, determinants, marks, or infinite numerical precision cannot repair a discriminator absent from the retained statistic.

## Distinguish exact relational fidelity from global rational-prime specificity and realizability

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-the-surviving-arithmetic-variable-is-relational`.

Higher-order or marked relational data can be exactly faithful modulo an intended gauge, but local fidelity can still be shared by non-Riemann sources. Prime Lattice now makes the intermediate case explicit: pointing breaks the Möbius torus gauge, yet the first recovered fixed-lag variables are Mirsky, Chowla, or Hardy--Littlewood correlations carried by a universal sinc kernel. Recovering a previously quotiented relation is not yet a new arithmetic mechanism.

A decisive mechanism must therefore pass three gates: prove local relational completeness modulo the intended gauge; prove that the retained structure distinguishes the exact rational-prime norm/completion from matched controls; and prove that the discriminator is usable in the topology/operator category required by the final argument. Provenance, gauge breaking, and algebraic recoverability are each weaker than specificity plus realization.

## Make matched controls as strong as the claimed quotient, interaction law, operator class, correlation layer, and analytic category

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-arithmetic-significance-begins-after-universal-control-subtraction`.

The mathematical goal is to identify the first matched-control depth at which an invariant changes and verify that this depth matches its claimed source of arithmetic information.

## Separate critical-axis generation and RH-equivalent diagnostics from zero selection

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-critical-axis-is-not-zero-selection`.

A distinguished axis, a real spectral carrier, correct finite Euler coefficients, or even an RH-equivalent boundedness criterion is weaker than a new zero-selection mechanism. Prime Lattice supplies two complementary controls: the second-scale completed Weil coefficient is an explicit zero-frequency sum whose boundedness on generic fixed smooth probes is RH-equivalent but generically nonconvergent under RH, while the positive-cone fixed-lag balance selects `sigma=1/2` universally whenever a coefficient system has nonzero correlation density.

The missing theorem is an independent selector or estimate derived upstream from rational-prime structure. Repackaging the continued zero divisor into a bounded signal, hull, Koopman system, joining, or universal critical finite-horizon balance is diagnostic unless the decisive bound/sign can be proved without importing the zero locations or classical correlation statement it is meant to explain.

## Derive a realization topology in which the arithmetic selector is continuous and the matched controls remain separated

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-005-topology-is-part-of-the-arithmetic-claim`.

Prime Flute shows that compact-resolvent class is too coarse and first-resolvent trace class too strong; strong unweighted metric control now extends through the long pre-corner region, and the remaining wave-weight issue localizes to genuine ambient thin geometry. Prime Lattice and Prime Circle add nonconvergent/hull and essential-versus-trace-class versions of the same category dependence.

The live program-level question is whether any branch forces a relative `S_p` (`p>1`), higher-resolvent, heat/wave/scattering, mesoscopic, distributional, twisted-determinant, alternative positive-completion, or other middle category in which the claimed arithmetic residual is continuous, global assembly closes, and the strongest matched control genuinely differs. A no-go showing that every intrinsic middle category either identifies the controls, makes the selector discontinuous, or only repackages classical/explicit zero data would be equally decisive.
