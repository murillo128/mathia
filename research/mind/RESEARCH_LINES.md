## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than merely transport, refactor or renorm the source?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) and [MI-028](intuition/MI-028-complete-measurement-families-can-close-an-escape-by-reconstructing-the-classical-object.md) remain the common interface audit.

Prime Circle closes finite spectral enrichment strongly: non-solvable development, outer reciprocal parity and fixed finite-dimensional algebraic representation lifts still reduce to reciprocal functions of the same finite spectrum. New leverage has to enter before that quotient, retain non-spectral covariant data, or justify a genuinely different destination.

Prime Lattice now separates ambient critical walls from a genuine source-rigid theorem interface. PL-367 shows that pole-neutral `ell^p` filters manufacture the entire exponent ladder `1-1/p`; PL-368 shows that scalar Dirichlet Hardy spaces manufacture a generic `1/2` absolute-convergence wall. PL-369 then supplies the missing nonlinear control from classical prior art: bounded complete multiplicativity plus pole neutrality and holomorphic continuation can force zeta zero-freeness. But at `sigma_0=1/2` witness existence is RH-equivalent through Liouville. A source-forced class can therefore be genuinely zero-sensitive while moving the hard problem unchanged into its continuation theorem.

Farey Discrepancy remains the strongest constructive example of useful pre-norm structure, but the analytic audit has tightened. FD-204--FD-205 give low-resistance, source-localized recovery geometry. FD-206 shows that the positive root anchor is already the RH-equivalent increment `M(2n)-M(n)`. FD-207 then shows that every **fixed-depth signed dyadic difference** of that root remains RH-equivalent pointwise while retaining the favorable random benchmark. Signed preprocessing buys leverage only if the resulting transform is not finitely invertible back to the hard scalar problem on the physical source class.

Nyman--Beurling removes representation and transport artifacts down to one local `-zeta'/zeta` carrier. NB-221 shows that fixed primitive depth, soft source continuation and finite-height contour transport all return to the same carrier. NB-222 proves that the contour gain is sharp over the entire bounded-analytic strip class: a carrier-matched analytic extremizer also satisfies the generic Cauchy derivative bounds. Any further saving must therefore use arithmetic spectral information of the actual logarithmic derivative, not generic oscillation, holomorphy or integration by parts.

Visual Exploration has pushed its matched control from support to phase structure. VIS-307 preserves exact prime-power support and magnitudes while randomizing support-point phases and still obtains an `o_p(H)` signed remainder. VIS-308 preserves complete multiplicativity as well, tying every `p,p^2,p^3,...` family to one base-prime phase, and cancellation still survives. The first remaining source-specific candidate is now collective deterministic alignment **across distinct base primes**, not support geometry or same-base harmonic coherence.

Weil Positivity now has a three-sided Whittaker scale classification. WP-367 shows that exact homogeneous globalization factorizes away `nY` incidence; WP-368 shows that bounded fixed-scale positivity preserves incidence only while exponentially suppressing high indices. WP-369 lets the scale move and finds one critical power law, `Y_n=c/n`; precisely there `nY_n` freezes, finite and archimedean factors separate, and ordinary positive `L^2` squares the half-density into a divergent Rankin--Selberg norm. The remaining route needs source-forced nonconstant transition coordinates, cross-index structure or another sign theorem.

The shared question is: **what new source-specific coordinate is actually acquired; which quotient, norm, continuation, scale choice or finite inverse can remove its leverage; is the critical boundary ambient or source-rigid; does a positive coordinate or finitely invertible signed transform already encode the destination-complete problem; and does the strongest matched control preserve everything except the proposed arithmetic relation?**

## Where does the obstruction move after a nominal critical boundary is resolved?

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the clearest nested collision hierarchy. AF-425 turns continuous cancellation into an integer source-lattice problem; AF-426 excludes every exact square-amplitude fiber; AF-427 makes displaced second-order recurrence Lebesgue-null with Hausdorff dimension at most `2/3`; AF-428 proves the same exceptional set is nevertheless a dense `G_delta`. The branch is therefore residual but metrically thin. Generic perturbation arguments and almost-everywhere arguments are both insufficient for a distinguished source amplitude; the remaining question is exact pointwise arithmetic membership.

Farey Discrepancy moves from path resistance to a positive anchor and then beyond the simplest signed repair. FD-207 closes every fixed-depth dyadic root difference as a pointwise escape. The frontier is growing-depth structure, aggregate signed relations that do not first bound each coefficient critically, or a different Farey-native observable whose inverse is not a fixed dilation polynomial.

Visual Exploration moves the obstruction from localization to absolute spacing, then to signed phase, then past same-base multiplicative coherence. The surviving candidate is cross-prime deterministic phase alignment at the actual endpoint torus point.

Nyman--Beurling moves the obstruction from reconstruction, localization and contour transport to arithmetic frequency concentration of the transported logarithmic derivative. NB-222 shows that strip analyticity itself is already exhausted at the current contour scale.

Prime Lattice moves from ambient topology to a valid nonlinear source law. The question is no longer whether source rigidity can force zero-freeness, but whether the continuation hypothesis of the source-rigid witness can be proved independently of the zero-free region it implies.

Möbius Cancellation moves its source-frame obstruction one level deeper. MC-386 gives the abstract sharp `H/R` exceptional scale; MC-387 forces every minimally concentrated near-extremizer toward a coset/annihilator model; MC-388 proves the exact high-dimensional endpoint source cannot approach that model because its Bochner support is a projected affine simplex of rank at least `d-1`. The frontier is now a quantitative energy/spread gap, not qualitative compatibility with the abstract extremizer.

Weil Positivity moves the scale obstruction to a transition coordinate. Fixed scale kills the tail, homogeneous globalization kills incidence, and the unique critical pure-power moving scale freezes `nY`. A surviving route must keep `nY` nonconstant for a source-forced reason and obtain sign before diagonal squaring.

Across these lines, a resolved threshold should be followed by identifying the **new source lattice, category/measure distinction, positive anchor, finite inverse, phase channel, source-image extremizer constraint, coefficient topology, continuation hypothesis, analytic carrier or scale coordinate** that becomes load-bearing, rather than promoting the old threshold as structural.

Farey Discrepancy adds the inverse audit: a signed transform with favorable source reuse and random cost is not cheaper if a bounded physical inverse restores the destination-complete coordinate at the same exponent. Prime Lattice adds a parallel continuation cost: a source-rigid witness is useful only when its continuation is obtained independently rather than encoded by the target zero-free region.

The shared audit is to price the route **after exact source specialization and nuisance removal**, keeping theorem admission, family breadth, conductor geometry, source-frame energy, near-extremizer shape, source-image compatibility, admissible lattice spacing, category versus measure, finite inverse cost, positive-anchor hardness, coefficient topology, analytic continuation, carrier-frequency cancellation, phase coherence, graph geometry, scale/index coupling and matched signed controls distinct until a theorem proves a dependency.

## Can sign-indefinite structure preserve critical information before closure or norm compression scalarizes it?

Farey Discrepancy gives both a constructive and a negative answer. Differences before a positive norm can remove shared bulk and improve geometry, but FD-207 shows that a fixed finite sequence of such differences remains destination-complete when the source supplies a bounded inverse. Sign helps only when it destroys or bypasses that recoverability before the hard coordinate is isolated as a pointwise cost.

Visual Exploration supplies a complementary control: even exact support plus complete multiplicative same-base phase structure cancels under random cross-prime phases. Useful nonpositivity must retain a concrete cross-prime deterministic relation rather than merely preserve multiplicativity or the signed quadratic form syntactically.

Nyman--Beurling shows that preserving sign through faithful transport is necessary but not sufficient. Once the signed carrier is isolated, generic bounded analyticity can still saturate the contour scale. The next gain must be tied to arithmetic amplitudes inside the carrier.

Weil Positivity shows the same issue through Hilbert closure. The critical `n^-1/2` Whittaker amplitude is visible before positivity at `Y_n=c/n`, but ordinary positive `L^2` immediately squares it and diverges. A viable sign mechanism must act before that diagonal scalarization and remain source-forced after scale adaptation.

Prime Lattice adds a different positive-logarithm example: Venturini's conjugate symmetrization really can convert a nonlinear completely multiplicative source law into a zero-free theorem, but the witness continuation is already the hard problem. A valid sign/positivity interface still needs an independently obtainable input.

These examples sharpen the same interface: useful nonpositivity or positivity must occur at the stage where the source-specific relation is still present, survive required transport, avoid a finite inverse or destination-complete positive anchor, and depend on an input that is not already equivalent to the desired conclusion.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the admissible source class and topology, observation family and nullspace, destination quotient and norm, source-coordinate map, requested precision, analytic domain, moving-complexity coordinates, discrete source lattice, relevant notion of exceptional-set size, invariant recovery coordinate, theorem-admission range, source-frame extremizer class, localization/transport duality, transported analytic carrier, finite inverse, positive anchors, sign/covariance/phase structure, scale/index coupling, target automorphism group and matched control.

Then ask in sequence: **what is genuinely new at acquisition; what unavoidable closure or projection follows; what survives the destination quotient; what ambient theorem already explains the critical scale; does a nonnegative coordinate or finitely invertible signed transform already encode the destination; does continuous tuning hit the actual source rather than only a residual or full-measure ambient set; are sharp ambient extremizers admissible after source pullback; if continuation is required, is it independently proved or equivalent to the target; if a signed carrier survives transport, what source-specific theorem excludes generic phase-matched extremizers; and does a matched control preserving support, magnitudes and internal source laws reproduce or cancel the survivor?**

The current synthesis narrows rather than proliferates directions. Richer representation is not enough when a quotient or scale choice removes the new coupling. A canonical `1/2` is not enough when Banach/Hardy geometry explains it, but a source-rigid zero-free criterion is also not enough when its witness continuation is RH-equivalent. Favorable graph geometry and signed preprocessing are not enough when a finite physical inverse recovers the hard scalar coordinate. A continuous critical tuning is not settled by genericity when its successful set is residual but null. A sharp ambient source-frame obstruction is not decisive when the exact source image excludes its near-extremizers. Exact prime-power support and same-base multiplicativity are not enough when only cross-prime phase alignment distinguishes the true point. Faithful contour transport is not enough when the generic strip class saturates the carrier bound. And a critical Whittaker half-density is not enough when the unique power-law scale exposing it freezes the mixed coordinate before positive closure.
