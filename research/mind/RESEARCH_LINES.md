# Global mathematical questions

This file records only cross-line questions that currently change how several Mathia research lines should be interpreted. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than merely transport, reconstruct, compress or refactor the source?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) and [MI-028](intuition/MI-028-complete-measurement-families-can-close-an-escape-by-reconstructing-the-classical-object.md) now give the common interface audit.

Prime Circle supplies the reconstruction-versus-quotient boundary. Complete shell information can recover the underlying source, but PC-351 shows that the canonical triangular decoder has trivial ordinary spectrum and admits a matched reciprocal sign-flip control that is unitarily conjugate to the arithmetic decoder. Faithful acquisition is therefore not selective if the destination quotient erases the oriented source coordinate.

Prime Lattice sharpens the topology-and-summation gate. PL-360 rules out the diagonal Hilbert repair, and PL-361 shows that even a non-diagonal Hausdorff completion cannot carry ordinary zeta coefficient cutoffs through `Re s<=1` while keeping point evaluation continuous. PL-362 closes the obvious smoothing escape: any coefficientwise-positive approximate identity restoring the coefficient-one series still diverges on every real `sigma<=1`. The obstruction is not merely the metric or cutoff sharpness; the approximation protocol plus destination functional can reconstruct the original convergence wall unless a genuinely signed/complex or renormalized mechanism enters.

Farey Discrepancy separates acquisition from compression. FD-201 proves that fixed-mode cross-horizon differences really contain new Möbius information, namely multiplicative Mertens increments, but their immediate positive half-Sobolev compression still forces shellwide subpower RMS. A representation can gain source information and then overcoerce or destroy the useful correlation at the next layer.

Weil Inertia shows that formal output size can also be illusory. WI-351 extends the finite charge-support obstruction to controlled infinite tails: at destination resolution a bounded charge-Sobolev moment makes the feature effectively finite, and generic square-root-rank behavior requires the high-charge mass itself to grow.

Weil Positivity adds a globalization failure. WP-366 starts from a genuinely non-product fixed-height Whittaker feature in which finite and archimedean variables interact through `nY`; positive scalar dilation-covariant globalization forces power-Haar averaging and Mellin-separates that interaction back into the failed Rankin--Selberg product. Local coupling counts only if it survives the canonical operation that removes its auxiliary scale.

The shared question is therefore: **what source-specific coordinate is newly acquired, what exact closure/summation/globalization the destination must apply, what survives that operation in the declared topology and resolution, and which matched control fails to reproduce the survivor?** Richer data, nonlinear depth, non-diagonal topology, positive smoothing, infinite formal support or local non-product structure deserve credit only after all of those gates survive.

## Where does the obstruction move after a nominal critical boundary is resolved?

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the clearest double-scaling example. AF-417--AF-420 refine derivative depth from the coarse ratio `s_n/n` to the joint critical coordinate

`b_n(a)=a n^(s_n/n-2)`.

AF-421 then resolves the signed finite-window regime beyond the absolute boundary. The exponentially leading full-column packets have rates `L_r(b)=b^r/(r!)^2`; away from square values `b=m^2`, one packet dominates and every nonsquare `b>1` gives genuine signed divergence. The remaining finite-window frontier is no longer generic signed cancellation: it is the next-order competition of two adjacent packets on the discrete square fibers `b=m^2`, especially for odd `n`, together with the separate supercritical moving-saddle regime `b_n->infinity`.

Visual Exploration gives the proof-boundary analogue. Exact gamma recentering first removes the lower moving-source wall. VIS-299--VIS-300 then isolate the previous `H/log^3 T` upper layer to one inside--outside coherence term. VIS-301 uses unconditional zero-free information to suppress that coherence throughout `x=o(H/log^2 T)`. The first generic `H`-scale term has therefore moved again, now to the interior Montgomery--Vaughan remainder `O(x log^2 x)`. The live test is coefficient-specific behavior of Wang's exact Dirichlet polynomial near `x~H/log^2 T`, not another estimate of the old localization leakage.

Robin Extremal shows a third kind of boundary motion. RE-216 changes the analytic predictor without changing the arithmetic carrier and lowers the constructive coefficient-variation exponent from `12 log 2` to at most `6.08073274`. This materially expands the proven hiding range while leaving the converse carrier-capacity ceiling untouched. A proof constant can therefore move because the representation became cheaper even when no new source information was added.

Across these lines, the right response to a resolved threshold is to identify the **new load-bearing packet, error term or resource**, rather than retain the old boundary label. Critical ratios, theorem envelopes and representation constants are only provisional until the full complexity family and the exact destination scale expose what actually dominates next.

## Which physical cost survives source specialization, centering, reconstruction and analytic transport?

[MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md) and [MI-025](intuition/MI-025-combinatorial-breadth-must-be-priced-through-the-source-coordinate-map.md) remain the main source-cost syntheses, but the recent results separate several currencies that had looked interchangeable.

Möbius Cancellation now distinguishes combinatorial source rank from analytic transport depth. MC-381 raises the exact-endpoint common-source obstruction to linear-in-rank scale until the analytic ceiling intervenes. MC-382 shows why that ceiling sits at `a=O(log log y)` for a source modulus `y^a`: the present q-van der Corput architecture requires depth `k>=a-2-o(1)` and the terminal saving is diluted to about `2^-k`. Repacking factors or recovering more source rank cannot move that horizon asymptotically; a different terminal or collective estimate is required.

Nyman--Beurling identifies the corresponding reconstruction currency. NB-217 replaces shell-width heuristics by the exact reciprocal log-support mass

`J_j(E)=int_E u^(-j)du`.

For fixed `j>=2`, signs, disconnected support and arbitrary upward widening do not remove the polynomial normalized reconstruction tariff while the source remains in a relative logarithmic annulus. A cheaper route must exploit correlation before absolute values, change the normalized source functional, or genuinely globalize toward much smaller logarithmic scales.

Farey Discrepancy shows that even source-sensitive acquisition can become too expensive after positive compression. Robin Extremal separates predictor/Wiener norm from raw prime-carrier capacity and finer KV fidelity. Weil Inertia separates hard phase-orbit cardinality from soft high-charge tail mass. These costs cannot be merged into a generic notion of “complexity”: each lives at a different stage of the source-to-destination map.

The shared audit is to price the route **after** exact source specialization and nuisance removal, then separately record acquisition rank, algebraic redundancy, predictor or transport loss, support/reconstruction cost, carrier capacity, destination-resolution complexity and any finer arithmetic fidelity. Improving one currency is progress only if another stage does not repay the gain.

## Can positivity preserve the critical amplitude before closure or globalization scalarizes it?

Weil Positivity remains the strongest direct classification. Positive finite Hecke structure retains arithmetic information before closure, but boundary squares scalarize the critical half-density. Rankin--Selberg positivity dies at critical specialization; projectivization does not repair it; and adding an independent positive Bessel/Whittaker Hilbert sector still leaves unbounded critical overlap and negative Fubini--Study geometry near critical zeros.

WP-366 now rules out a more local escape under canonical scalar globalization. The fixed-height coordinates `lambda_r(n)K_(ir)(2 pi nY)` genuinely couple the finite and archimedean variables, but dilation-covariant positive averaging in `Y` is forced to be power-Haar and Mellin-separates the interaction back to Rankin--Selberg geometry. The critical positive diagonal then already diverges.

PL-362 gives a parallel coefficient-space warning: preserving the positive coefficient cone while an approximate identity restores every zeta coefficient cannot cross the real convergence wall. Positivity can therefore fail before any sophisticated spectral geometry enters, simply because the continuation itself requires sign-indefinite or renormalized cancellation.

The missing mechanisms in both settings must explain **where the required nonpositive information comes from**. In Weil Positivity this may be a source-fixed scale with an independent theorem, intrinsically non-Haar or operator-valued scale mixing, a Maaß--Selberg-type remainder, or a cohomological/intersection sector whose orientation is established before scalar separation. In Prime Lattice it may be source-forced signed/complex summation, a nontrivial counterterm, non-coefficientwise mixing or a target-relative/distributional readout. In neither case is positivity plus smoother closure enough.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the admissible source class and topology, observation family and nullspace, destination quotient and norm, physical source-coordinate map, acquisition budget, requested precision, normalization gauge, analytic domain, approximation/summation protocol, moving-complexity coordinates, support/multiplicity constraints, transport or reconstruction loss, sign/covariance structure and matched control. Then ask in sequence: **what is genuinely new at acquisition; what exact closure/summation/globalization is unavoidable; what survives it; where does the necessary signed or renormalized information enter; what inverse resource must the source pay; and does a matched non-arithmetic control reproduce the same survivor?**

The current synthesis narrows rather than proliferates directions. Complete fidelity is not enough if invariantization erases it. New measurements are not enough if destination compression overcoerces them. Non-diagonal topology is not enough if canonical cutoffs plus continuous evaluation rebuild absolute convergence, and positive smoothing is not enough if coefficientwise recovery preserves the same divergent mass. Infinite formal support is not enough if the destination sees only a bounded effective tail. Local finite--archimedean interaction is not enough if canonical scale averaging factorizes it. Recovering source rank is not enough if analytic transport loses saving exponentially with depth, and smoother reconstruction is not enough if reciprocal support mass repays it.

Progress therefore requires an intermediate structure that is nontrivial in the declared topology, source-specific after matched controls, survives the exact destination operation, remains quantitatively visible at the moving resolution, and couples to a destination theorem without reconstructing the original RH criterion in disguise.
