# Global mathematical questions

## Which source resources reach the destination before stable observation turns into generic decoding or unrestricted completion?

[MI-007](intuition/MI-007-survival-requires-quantitative-non-escape-not-just-nonzero-structure.md), [MI-008](intuition/MI-008-exact-recovery-is-not-stable-transport.md), [MI-012](intuition/MI-012-information-content-and-destination-sensitivity-are-separate-resources.md) and [MI-013](intuition/MI-013-closing-the-bulk-often-leaves-a-thin-source-specific-endpoint.md) separate information survival from the modulus, source class and category actually consumed by the target.

Prime Circle closes the first unresolved mesh-scale linear harmonic escape. PC-309 gives collapse throughout every transport-stable pre-mesh regime; PC-310 shows that at `1-r=Theta(1/q)` the sampled Poisson/radial-flux field becomes an invertible cyclic multiplier with condition number `Theta(q)`. The representation becomes lossless exactly when it pays enough sensitivity to decode the finite source.

Arithmetic Fidelity now adds the completion-side analogue. AF-365 proves that global eventual Riesz error control preserves Dirichlet principal parts because the Mellin multiplier is zero-free. AF-366 shows that a finite Riesz horizon does not inherit that theorem: every finite-prefix observation has invisible tails that can insert prescribed critical-strip poles while becoming small in unweighted sup norm. Pole fidelity from finite data therefore requires an independently justified tail/continuation class; `O(n^(theta-1))` coefficient difference is one sufficient certificate for preserving principal parts on `Re s>theta`.

Prime Lattice moves in the opposite direction by reducing what must be controlled. PL-320 shows that convergence of the full source trace plus the local singular remainder forces the `Q^(-1/2)` coefficient, `Qm(Q)->0`, and a conditionally convergent residual moment. PL-321 then proves that those weaker data already suffice for the moving Green transfer. The former absolute-`L^1` alternative disappears: the sole analytic endpoint-transfer gate in this chain is convergence of the singular translation remainder.

Across these lines, the key quantity is the **destination margin obtained before observation crosses into unstable inversion, generic decoding, or an unrestricted completion fibre**.

## Which local source laws beat the exceptional geometry allowed by global population control?

[MI-017](intuition/MI-017-sparse-exceptional-geometry-survives-upper-population-control.md) has corrected boundaries in Nyman--Beurling and a stronger matched obstruction in Robin Extremal.

NB-147 completes the positive exterior-Poisson classification for arbitrary tight interior packets. The exact currency is the weighted reflection closure, not the packet center: near the critical line, if `d_G` is packet multiplicity and `b_G` counts occurrences whose reflected partner is absent, then `d_G+b_G <= (1/4+o(1))log G`. The separated `1/8` and self-dual `1/4` coefficients are the endpoints of the same orbit-exposure law. Positive sampling is minimax at this closure budget; nearly maximal packets must be nearly reflection-saturated, but `Theta(log G)` packets remain possible. The fixed-interior route therefore needs a source resource beyond positive Poisson mass plus the absolute Euler-product majorant.

RE-149 shows that reciprocal-box visibility loss is already exponentially sharp for pure vertical escape with zero horizontal spread. A matched finite-edge spectrum can hide a unique scale-maximal target by phase geometry alone while maintaining sparse one-level counts. Better horizontal localization or global density therefore cannot close the branch without an actual-zeta theorem on vertical occupancy/phase structure.

PL-320--PL-321 give a third model: rather than strengthen a population law, an exact source identity plus an Abel-compatible kernel changes the endpoint gate itself. This is the useful alternative when source and destination rates cannot be aligned by stronger counting.

## Which growing source law is informative rather than merely connected, high-dimensional, probabilistic or reconstructive?

Farey Discrepancy has an exact binary answer before the observation-count gate. FD-147 identifies finite-`L^q` ancestry recovery with an anchored Cheeger ratio. Connectivity is only the zero-boundary endpoint; under uniform full-ancestry weights, fixed seeds still have cuts whose normalized mass forces a diverging coercivity constant.

Möbius Cancellation gives two complementary warnings. MC-317 shows that the relevant constant-defect binary-code endpoint is generically realizable, so the next theorem must constrain arithmetic realizability rather than repackage dimension/rank. MC-318 shows that a probabilistic comparator can fail even earlier: deterministic conditioning at `Omega=1` contradicts the stated squarefree independence, and the proposed auxiliary sum collapses exactly to `lambda(n)M(x)`. A source law is informative only after exact arithmetic simplification shows that it is not merely the target in disguise.

Prime Circle adds the stable-decoding warning above. “Number of observations” is not one scalar resource; anchor exposure, topology, exact algebra, precision, invertibility and matched-control realizability have to be priced separately.

## Which representation-faithful null remains after nuisance variables are quotiented out?

[MI-018](intuition/MI-018-canonical-normalization-can-universalize-the-observable-while-moving-all-source-specificity-into-an-induced-law.md) now has fully explicit calibration on the Visual side and a stronger same-source matched-control obstruction on the Weil-positivity side.

VIS-242 reduces the symmetric-Dirichlet tent variance to low-dimensional overlap classes. VIS-243 uses complement symmetry to eliminate the remaining cyclic displacement bookkeeping: only half-circle arc lengths remain, with closed overlap multiplicities `m-r-s+1`, `b-a+1`, and `2`. Once the dependence parameter and endpoint semantics are fixed independently, the null variance is an explicit deterministic combination of low-dimensional Dirichlet moments.

WP-319 shows that gap-adapted Mangoldt derivative-biased laws are tight and carry source-specific shifted-lattice/quantized structure, while one such law is still exactly matched by a non-arithmetic positive Herglotz source. WP-320 strengthens the obstruction: after sparse thinning, **one finite non-atomic positive source** can graft infinitely many arithmetic gap tangents at the same physical nodes and scales, matching derivative normalizations relatively and normalized Julia fields asymptotically. Common provenance across a sparse family is therefore not itself source-selective.

The common discriminator is whether the induced law or family carries a **non-graftable relation** that the matched-control class cannot reproduce after the nuisance quotient. On the Visual side this means a frozen residual surviving the exact null; on the Weil side it means non-sparse/overlapping cross-gap rigidity or genuine finite--archimedean coupling.

## Which noncompact source channels must survive a positivity reduction?

Weil Inertia sharpens its tail-retention question. WI-304 gives the channel-wise essential numerical-range criterion `w_q>beta` for compact-irrepairable omitted atoms. WI-305 shows that, for Liu's localizer class and reserve `1/16`, the obstruction already occurs at `q=2`: the compressed translation at `log 2` is nilpotent of order three, so the numerical-radius bound forces enough autocorrelation loss that `w_2>1/16`. Thus compact repair fails as soon as the first arithmetic channel becomes active, at `L>log(2)/2`.

This is still a statement about the tail-dropped comparison, not the full Weil form. The later gate remains the signed density-subtracted discrepancy of WI-241, not merely retained positive arithmetic mass.

## Global discriminator: identify the exact resource that the matched control cannot fake

The newest results sharpen several boundaries without supplying a final RH bridge. AF-366 identifies admissible completion as part of finite-data pole fidelity. NB-147 classifies positive Poisson occupancy by reflection closure. PL-321 removes absolute integrability from the moving Green gate. MC-318 requires probabilistic proxies to survive exact arithmetic simplification. VIS-243 closes deterministic null bookkeeping. WI-305 pushes compact-irrepairability down to the first prime threshold. WP-320 shows that even sparse infinite same-source coherence can be assembled by a matched positive control.

For every proposed route ask: **what exact object reaches the destination; under which norm, scale, quantifier and admissible source class; what gauge/nuisance/universal component has been removed; what source-specific variable remains; can the matched-control class realize that same variable, law or family by decoding, completion or grafting; and does the source gain dominate the destination loss without reconstructing the endpoint?** A new representation is useful only when one of those answers changes mathematically.
