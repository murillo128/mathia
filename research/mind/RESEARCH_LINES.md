# Global mathematical questions

## Which source relation cannot be repaired by moving the cost elsewhere?

[MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md) separates several different failure modes of nominally rich source interfaces.

Farey Discrepancy distinguishes **relation coercivity from observability of that relation**, and FD-183 now makes the coercivity itself source-fibre dependent. FD-181 proves that every squarefree-supported perturbation of Möbius with changed-coordinate support `o(x/log x)` pays prime-extension multiplicativity energy at scale `x/log x`, even when prime coordinates may move. FD-182 composes that theorem with the permanent FD-179 dyadic-null source: one fixed non-Möbius source matches the complete dyadic midpoint ladder exactly while retaining `M_(a,q)(x)=(C+o(1))x/log x`. FD-183 shows that if prime coordinates are frozen, subcritical energy forces support at least `(1/n_0+o(1))x loglog x/log x`. The tariff grows by a `loglog x` factor because repair propagates to a second multiplicative generation. A source-native bridge must therefore specify both an observation family that exposes the relation and the admissible source fibre on which the relation price was proved.

Nyman--Beurling shows how a locality cost can be absent at fixed resolution yet explode in a coupled limit. NB-190 prices a normalized Euler-band probe by inverse source mass in scalar-translate variation. NB-191 shows that at fixed band width this cost can eventually be transplanted into every high dyadic block by common Bohr recurrence without an additional asymptotic surcharge. NB-192 reveals the hidden nonuniformity: one fixed log-width band contains `m_a~a exp(r_0/a)` rationally independent prime-log phases, forcing coordinatewise syndetic common returns to satisfy `log L_a >> a exp(r_0/a)`. NB-193 closes the direct positive weighted relaxation: the true-source-weighted `ell^1` return set has exponentially small Haar volume in effective dimension `D_eff=1/sum w_j^2`, and the true prime weights satisfy `D_eff asymp m_a`. The same double-exponential worst-gap scale therefore survives aggregate positive recurrence. A specific block may still contain an unusually early return, and signed aggregation or a different synthesis topology may avoid this recurrence architecture; those are now the live acquisition questions.

Robin Extremal supplies a precision-aware stable-rank analogue. RE-189--RE-192 show that high normalized Euler jets and finite-Laplace observation windows can be extremely compressible despite exact analytic source rigidity. RE-193 improves the large-rectangle bound: for `R=U log(Q_+/Q_-)`, the scalar Euler channel has rank

`O((1+log(1+R))(1+log(1/epsilon)))`

at tolerance `epsilon` above the prime-power floor. Fixed spectral span across polynomial source scales therefore carries only `O_epsilon(loglog p)` robust scalar directions at fixed precision. Exact continuum data may remain source-rigid while usable capacity grows only logarithmically with the transform rectangle; the relevant resource is now the pair `(R,epsilon)`, not sample count or source cardinality separately.

Möbius Cancellation shows that source information can move inside a statistical architecture without becoming free. MC-356 prices finite-dimensional regular exponential-family channels by integrated Fisher action. MC-357--MC-358 move the tariff into latent/component structure while preserving the exact lifted ledger. MC-359 adds total variation as a bounded edge currency when KL/Jeffreys becomes singular: bounded-energy nontrivial dephasing forces a positive average TV separation across source directions, and in a genuine shared-randomness architecture a one-source flip must change the complete transcript on a positive fraction of common seeds. The remaining problem is no longer to name a richer generic family, but to expose the actual Möbius-derived source graph and prove an arithmetic upper bound in whichever finite currency—TV/common-seed change, lifted divergence, or another architecture-native quantity—remains meaningful at the endpoint.

The common discriminator is not raw dimension, exactness or the mere existence of a source tariff. It is a **destination-relevant quantitative resource together with an observation/acquisition theorem that actually exposes it under the declared source class, precision and coupled scaling**.

## Which physical source scale and representation gauge does the theorem actually control?

Arithmetic Fidelity has two independent scale ledgers. On the sampling side, uniform short-interval PNT gives all-prime-start conversion for `h_N=p_N^(-beta)` throughout `beta<13/30`, while exceptional-set inertia plus Brun--Titchmarsh packing transfers a stronger almost-all ambient theorem to relative density one of prime starts through every `beta<13/15`. Those thresholds measure theorem uniformity and sampling geometry, not an intrinsic detector constant.

On the finite-order analytic side, AF-403--AF-405 turn strict translation positivity into a recursive confluent/Toda hierarchy. The full Euler-log profile has `H_1,H_2,H_3,H_4>0` globally, hence strict total positivity through order four. AF-217 still forces eventual failure, so the first possible bad order is at least five and the next local gate is `(log R_4)''<4q`.

AF-406 adds a representation-specific fidelity boundary. For `xi_1(z)=xi(sqrt(z)+1/2)`, every fixed finite **coefficient-sequence** Pólya-frequency order can be forced by a sufficiently large zero-free multiplier `e^{nz}` while the complete zero divisor is unchanged. But the same finding proves that an affine exponential **profile** tilt `f(t)->e^{at+b}f(t)` preserves every translation-determinant sign, scales `H_r` only by a positive factor and leaves normalized `R_r` and `q` invariant. Coefficient `PF_m` gauge sensitivity therefore cannot be transferred wholesale to the translation-kernel Toda hierarchy. The next order-five gate remains a genuine affine-gauge-invariant kernel question, though finite translation positivity still needs an independent zero-selection theorem.

Visual Exploration adds both normalization and parameter-regime audits. VIS-272 gives the exact Green-potential dictionary and VIS-274 carries the concentrating main term through the fixed-source `1/log T` pullback. VIS-275 shows why a sign-blind absolute remainder cannot benefit from extra signed packet moments. VIS-276--VIS-277 then establish that the apparent signed remainder mean is partly a classical gamma-factor convention, not an invariant source residual.

VIS-278 closes the first normalization-invariant pure source-energy question. With

`R_x(t)=x(B_x(t)+E_x(t))-log(t/(2*pi))`,

one has `R_x(t)=zeta'/zeta(3/2-it)+O_K(t^-1)`, asymptotically zero mean, and a positive limiting mean-square `C_Lambda=sum_p (log p)^2/(p^3-1)`. Its standalone fixed-source packet contribution is only `L^-2`.

VIS-279 shows that the first obvious non-source cross term is also only `L^-2` after Wang's exact mean-square theorem is specialized to compact fixed source before rescaling. The earlier `L^-3/2` scale came from retaining a uniform-in-growing-`x` bound in a destination that uses fixed physical `q`. The correct next theorem must therefore determine whether any intrinsic fixed-source `L^-3/2` sector exists at all, rather than assuming such a term and designing cancellation around it.

Robin's precision-aware `R=U log(Q_+/Q_-)` law gives the dual observation-window version of the same discipline. The theorem must control the actual physical source window, the observation window and tolerance must resolve the claimed degrees of freedom, nuisance/gauge normalization must be fixed before a residual scale is interpreted, and a uniform theorem bound must be re-specialized to the destination parameter regime before its exponent is treated as a mathematical frontier.

## Which quantitative resource survives a thin or moving destination?

Prime Lattice has moved beyond both the local activation-birth gate and the positive-tail dilution gate. PL-338 shows that every finite prime-power activation is first-order flat and is born with favorable reflected sign whenever the transported boundary trace is nonzero. PL-339 computes the exact path-chain gap of each compressed shift and shows that the outer logarithmic half-window carries asymptotically all weighted von-Mangoldt mass with unit gap. Consequently

`J_a >= (1-o(1)) W_a I`.

Accumulating active channels therefore does not soften the favorable positive jump bulk relative to total source mass. But the exact prime block remains `P_a=J_a-2W_aI`; the negative scalar compensation survives at the same scale. The frontier is now global branch persistence/noncollapse and a rational-prime-specific control of compensation/completion or another mesoscopic source law beyond the PNT-universal bulk.

Nyman--Beurling sharpens what “locality” means in a moving destination. Fixed-`a` recurrence eventually reaches arbitrary height, but NB-192--NB-193 show that both coordinatewise and natural positive weighted recurrence have double-exponential worst-gap inclusion length in `1/a`. Thus locality becomes a real resource only when the recurrence/acquisition scale is quantitatively coupled to the destination. A more efficient signed, block-specific or non-recurrence mechanism remains possible; the generic positive syndetic route cannot simply be extrapolated into the shrinking-width regime.

Xi Flow remains a useful control in the opposite direction: once a consecutive packet is localized, its local holomorphic inversion is well conditioned. Its unresolved cost is global support discovery and assembly rather than local packet inversion. Across these lines, the moving destination is governed by the resource that scales with localization, recurrence, support discovery, stable transform width, precision or branch transport—not by the mere existence of a local inverse or positive bulk.

## Can reverse discrimination, signed structure or positivity be made destination-selective?

Möbius Cancellation now prices both regular and singular source channels. MC-354--MC-358 cover smooth noise, Gaussian deformation, regular exponential families and source-dependent latent/components through finite divergence ledgers. MC-359 supplies the bounded TV edge tariff and common-seed change bound at the singular endpoint. A useful contradiction still needs the other half: an arithmetic upper theorem showing that the **actual** Möbius endpoint architecture cannot afford the compulsory adjacent separation in a metric that stays meaningful for that architecture.

Weil Inertia exposes a different information loss. Below `theta<3/16`, all-start control removes the source-location obstruction. WI-332--WI-335 show that low and slowly growing ordinary Gowers orders can be small while full diagonal-scale sliding variance survives. WI-336 closes the opposite side: diagonal zero-increment cubes force high-order `U^s` to become non-small once `2^s loglog N/log K` crosses the complementary threshold. Because one increment in `s` doubles this parameter, ordinary normalized Gowers smallness is squeezed to at most one critical order window. Below it the statistic is too weak; above it the source energy itself prevents smallness. A surviving route must use the critical window with genuinely new quantitative content, renormalize/remove the diagonal cubes, or directly control the source-specific signed two-point covariance.

[MI-026](intuition/MI-026-sign-destructive-proof-interfaces-cannot-certify-destination-cancellation.md) links that covariance boundary to the Visual result. In Wang's channel, signed information is useless if decomposition-dependent, already absolute-valued, or attached to a theorem-uniform scale that disappears after fixed-source specialization. In the bow, a growing list of ordinary smallness constraints is useless if it does not determine the required off-diagonal sign and eventually becomes diagonal-dominated. A useful intermediate must preserve destination-sensitive sign/covariance, survive harmless normalization freedom and live at the exact physical scale consumed by the terminal theorem.

Weil Positivity closes another scalar escape. WP-352 isolates the reciprocal exponential phase gauge and shows that projective Berry/Chern geometry is blind to the common scalar zeta factor. WP-353 proves that, under order-at-most-one growth, this exponential family is the complete zero-free reciprocal/unitary scalar gauge. Standard arithmetic cusp normalization can remove its one real parameter—but after that normalization the scalar meromorphic normalizer is determined by its divisor plus normalization. Scalar phase recovery has therefore collapsed to divisor reconstruction rather than an independently generated positivity mechanism. A surviving Weil route must introduce a noncentral/global coupling or a larger independently positive object **before scalar reconstruction**.

Prime Circle closes a parallel scalar-selector escape. PC-338 extends the finite point-supported Mellin classification to every compactly supported distribution in Mellin frequency. PC-339 now shows that noncompact support alone does not escape: every finite global Mellin measure with the natural critical Gamma-strip boundary admissibility still collapses under semiprime nulls to zero off `s=1` and to `c Lambda` on the endpoint. The decisive resource is the analytic boundary class, not support compactness. A surviving radial route must leave this Gamma-strip-admissible measure class—through faster-growth/non-measure analytic functionals, shell-dependent/nonlinear/matrix structure or cross-shell coupling—and still supply an independent zero-sensitive destination theorem.

Prime Flute reinforces the complementary warning. The current sharp physical low/high completed angle is already noncompact because adjacent Fourier modes can straddle the cutoff while becoming almost identical on a collapsing corridor. More endpoint counting cannot repair a representation whose local operator already has infinitely many order-one canonical correlations; the split or invariant itself must change before the global trace-ideal question becomes meaningful.
