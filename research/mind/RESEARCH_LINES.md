## Characterize complete support, relational depth, target transport, original range, and compact gluing before choosing a realization

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-preserve-structure-before-compressing`, `MI-005-topology-is-part-of-the-arithmetic-claim`, and `MI-007-survival-requires-quantitative-non-escape-not-just-nonzero-structure`.

Arithmetic Fidelity separates repair category from local-to-global compatibility. Compact admissible fibers can globalize finite Lipschitz selections with the exact local constant; AF-090 goes further and shows that any local Lipschitz quotient patch globalizes with no metric loss in the canonical bidual and forces linear splitting after bidualization. The remaining question is therefore not generic finite compatibility but **original-range and source-natural fidelity**: when are representatives forced to stay in the original source with the required quantitative and equivariant structure? Analytic Frontier supplies a complementary information boundary: in its audited bridge, two-point data cannot beat the pair baseline but three consecutive points can because their overlapping gaps satisfy an ordered consistency relation.

A decisive construction must state not merely how many channels it measures but what relational order they retain, where recovered representatives live, and why the gluing/naturality is forced by the source. Per-cutoff exact recovery, a repair available only in a convenient completion, many scalarized pair channels, or an arbitrary non-natural selection are insufficient.

## Require operator-category, spectral-type, and domain compatibility, not merely matching scalar symbols

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-005-topology-is-part-of-the-arithmetic-claim`.

Several branches show that apparently close scalar laws can live in incompatible operator realizations. Prime-Flute/Weil comparisons cross Schatten boundaries; positive DtN response loses Hermitian positivity under critical continuation; WP-131 proves that the solenoid and radial Gamma carriers admit no nonzero bounded exact intertwiner because one is pure point and the other continuous; and WP-132 closes the canonical generalized-eigenfunction workaround. Exact distributional Gamma bridges collapse to rational point sampling/jets and are nonclosable from natural radial `L^2`, while the critical prime-power amplitudes fail even square summability.

A decisive bridge must therefore specify how spectral measure/type, domains/closability, operator ideal, and sign structure are transported. A regularizing subtraction or changed Hilbert geometry can be a real new mechanism, but precisely because it changes the operator; it must be derived upstream and prove its own positivity/coercivity rather than inherit it from a now-incompatible carrier.

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-005-topology-is-part-of-the-arithmetic-claim` and `MI-007-survival-requires-quantitative-non-escape-not-just-nonzero-structure`.

A decisive positive must carry a uniform target margin while allowing information support/complexity to grow at the scale forced by the dynamics, and then show that this information survives the complete operator/orbit/positivity assembly. Nonzero finite-stage structure, pointwise vanishing error, increasing local derivative order, or a merely qualitative forced alias are insufficient without a quantitative non-escape theorem.
