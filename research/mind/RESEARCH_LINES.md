# Global mathematical questions

This file records mathematical questions that only become visible by comparing several current Mathia research lines. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does a quantitative budget become a genuine source discriminator rather than a repackaged observable?

Across the current lines, the useful budget is increasingly the one induced by the **exact quotient or discriminator that the destination actually consumes**. Coarse support, ambient norm, matrix rank, compactness, selector sparsity or representation dimension can all be large while the destination-visible source channel is unchanged, universal, or fully reproducible by a matched control.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the topology issue explicit. AF-542 shows that a value-level accurate carrier-to-curvature approximation can fail catastrophically after the shrinking transverse contrast is applied. AF-543--AF-544 construct an exact curvature-native inverse using the additional reference coordinate `r(t)` and prove that the calibration precision is itself part of the theorem. AF-545 then identifies the general common-mode geometry: for a nonlinear inverse map the shared-calibration error in a relational readout is governed by the **difference of inverse sensitivities across the compared branches**, while differential calibration is governed by their average sensitivity. Exact common-mode cancellation occurs precisely when the calibration flow is a translation in the readout coordinate. This is a general inverse-problem criterion, so the remaining arithmetic burden is to show that the required shared reference is source-natural and accurate in the destination-induced seminorm.

Möbius Cancellation reaches an analogous minimal quotient in a different geometry. MC-505--MC-506 show that the varying start/profile table is seen only through the physical pushforward `t=x+j`, whose excess-collision law controls the centered conductor bias. MC-507 proves that broad marginals and matrix complexity do not control that law. MC-508 and MC-509 then close two opposite shortcuts: finitely many local mask coordinates are programmable at fixed conductor phase when localization is removed, while a sufficiently long exact mask tuple inside a localized source interval becomes source-injective once its CRT modulus exceeds the source width. Thus neither finite local rigidity nor repeated exact full-profile classes provide the missing cancellation for free. The live theorem must keep distinct localized root states in the same average and control their image under the **observed sum map**.

Farey Discrepancy shows both the value and the danger of downstream spectral compression. FD-305--FD-307 reduce a near-capacity block-Mertens anomaly to a sharply low-frequency fixed-block witness, but FD-308 shows that the later principal-arc thresholds lie above the earlier unconditional source ceiling `lambda<=1/(2c)`. FD-309 strengthens that dominance audit: even under GRH, both the classical uniform Baker--Harman bound and its frequency-sensitive principal-arc form become contradictory only at thresholds tangent to or above the same source ceiling. The live certificate near `lambda=1` therefore survives classical GRH pointwise additive cancellation by a fixed power. A useful source compression must be audited not only against upstream feasibility but also against the strongest theorem that actually acts on the compressed object.

Visual Exploration supplies the selector analogue. VIS-419--VIS-425 identify the exact base-prime Haar self-null, finite-window character tariff, fixed-moment normalized-amplitude tariff and fixed-radius persistence tariff for Wang shells at Gram points. VIS-427 shows that **normalized fixed-power path averages pay no additional path-radius tariff at all**: after moment expansion the offset-box volume cancels, even when the observation radius grows. Long-window averaging is therefore still governed by the same Wiener/frequency/small-divisor resources. The surviving path resource is localized concentration that can occupy a vanishing fraction of a long window, a supremum or growing-moment functional that can see it, a tariff failure, or a selector whose finite-prime phase law is not Haar.

Prime Lattice provides the same discipline through exact covariance geometry. PL-437--PL-440 reduce the unresolved mod-5 defect to reflection/start-residue anisotropy plus one quadratic-character lag channel. PL-441 solves the lag-stationary slice exactly: the dephasing gate is a compact wedge--ellipse in the normalized mean-lag and oriented-lag coordinates, and approximate stationarity transfers only when the nonstationary remainder is smaller in operator norm than the stationary spectral margin. “Small character probe” is therefore not the destination norm; the source must land inside the exact cone with enough margin to absorb anisotropy.

The global question is: **for each proposed representation, what is the smallest source-derived object that the destination actually sees, and what quantitative norm, seminorm or fibre budget on that object is stable under the real compression?** A budget becomes evidence only when it controls that object independently of matched controls, survives the downstream discriminator at the required scale, and remains nonvacuous after upstream feasibility, conditioning and representation-capacity gates are imposed.

## Which source dependence survives scalarization, equilibrium, compactness and domain gates?

Prime Circle, Weil Inertia and Weil Positivity now expose a common danger from different operator categories: sophisticated structure can be universal, too rigid, too programmable, compactly invisible at the critical scale, or fail to define a usable infinite operator at all.

Prime Circle PC-419--PC-421 show that the norm-closed bounded-radial/discrete-shift descendants contain no nonzero compacts, while the canonical continuous-scale Hilbert commutator does become Hilbert--Schmidt but merely repackages one vertical Mellin line. PC-422 then closes the direct reflected-line repair. The `sigma <-> 1-sigma` pairing is Mellin Parseval before any zeta-specific argument, and its strict maximum at `sigma=1/2` follows for every positive source profile from an exact `cosh` identity. A zero-free Gamma control has the same maximum. Thus even a compact operator with an exact reflected critical-looking extremum can be universal transform geometry rather than arithmetic spectralization.

Weil Inertia has the parallel “exact representation is not independent control” obstruction. WI-433--WI-436 show that horizontal transport and the canonical positive/negative Hardy pairing can reproduce the finite Kunik defect exactly without constraining it from source information. WI-437 now excludes the simplest non-translation-invariant repair at shallow depth: normalized one-defect Hardy packets become weakly null as the horizontal defect depth tends to zero, so every fixed compact cross-channel operator has vanishing normalized response, while the canonical defect pairing remains order one. A viable Hankel/mixed-frequency detector must therefore have an **essentially noncompact** source-controlled component; noncompactness is necessary, not sufficient.

Weil Positivity sharpens canonicality into a rigidity--programmability--domain trichotomy. WP-429 shows that unrestricted coherent finite-Gibbs sandwiches can realize arbitrary positive functionals; WP-430 shows that thermodynamic-only canonical selection collapses back to zero Bohr degree. WP-431 then tests the most literal arithmetic escape: the one-sided prime semigroup field `sum_p a_p mu_p` has a nontrivial dense Hilbert domain exactly when `a` is square summable. At the critical half-density `a_p=p^(-1/2)` its maximal domain is `{0}`, and bounded normalization of finite-prime cutoffs erases every fixed prime amplitude. Extra arithmetic source structure is still necessary, but the naive critical all-prime analytic field is not a legitimate selector in the standard Hilbert representation.

These three lines suggest a shared gate without asserting that their operator categories are equivalent. A source coupling has evidentiary value only when it is fixed **before scalarization or target inspection**, survives matched generic controls, has a mathematically valid domain/topology at the critical scale, and retains an essential response rather than disappearing through compactness or normalization. “Compact”, “coherent”, “noncommuting”, “mixed-frequency” and “unbounded” are categories, not evidence. The unresolved mathematics is a source-canonicity theorem for a specific coupling together with a destination estimate showing that the coupling retains the required critical information.

## Where do apparent arithmetic thresholds disappear once the exact source geometry is retained?

Nyman--Beurling now resolves the neighboring-lattice phase at the level of the canonical adjacent witness. NB-322--NB-327 show that the `1/sqrt(log n)` rank-one-error resonance survives denominator growth, parity, divisibility and the entire lattice `m=d(n-1)` with `d=o(n)`. NB-328 moves one full cell to `m=dn` and flips the same coefficient's sign without changing its magnitude. NB-329 proves that the whole intermediate cell is an affine phase law: for `m=d(n-1)+r`, `rho=r/d`, the coefficient is `(1-2rho)log 2/(4n)+O(d/n^2)`. The channel therefore crosses through zero at half-cell drift, while away from that phase the logarithmic norm lower bound persists. The remaining question is no longer where this **one witness** changes sign, but whether the half-drift cancellation survives optimization over all source directions or is only a codimension-one zero of the chosen channel.

Farey Discrepancy gives the complementary warning. FD-307 appeared to create meaningful principal-major-arc depth thresholds, but FD-308 shows that the upstream finite-support energy ceiling already excludes every depth where those thresholds activate. FD-309 then shows that strengthening the additive estimate all the way to the classical GRH Baker--Harman theory only makes the best contradiction threshold **touch** that source ceiling at the shallow shell; the strict inequality needed for a power contradiction never enters the live region. The exact tangency is the meaningful datum, not the apparent strength of the later theorem.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives a third version. AF-544 finds a genuine precision threshold because the leading reference asymptotic fails after the destination contrast while the finite-part correction crosses that downstream calibration barrier. AF-545 explains when such a threshold is intrinsic: shared nuisance is measured by variation of inverse sensitivity across the compared sources, not by the absolute reconstruction error. A term can therefore be negligible in the source expansion and load-bearing in the relational destination, or large branchwise yet largely cancel when it is truly common mode.

The reusable question is therefore: **is a proposed transition intrinsic to the source/destination coupling, or only to the witness or proof coordinate currently being used?** Stress-test every threshold by enlarging the exact arithmetic skeleton, optimizing over competing source directions, checking neighboring source regimes, comparing against upstream feasibility ceilings, changing representation without losing the source relation, and measuring error only after the final discriminator. If the transition disappears under those controls, it should not be promoted to a research principle; if it survives them, identify the exact source quantity that enforces it.
