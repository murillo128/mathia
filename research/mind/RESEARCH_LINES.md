## Which apparent resource survives the exact destination map?

The common audit remains: identify the admissible source image, quotient universal scaffolding and nuisance directions, follow the exact information-loss map into the destination, and only then price the surviving resource. Arithmetic Fidelity now makes the transport side of that audit quantitative. A full curvature profile is exactly invertible modulo global dilation, uniformly vanishing tail log-displacement is globally invisible, and fixed order-one proportional transport is mesoscopically visible according to a mismatch between zeroth and second logarithmic-moment participation. AF-519 shows explicitly that a moving selected shell can have vanishing normalized Dirichlet-value participation while retaining order-one curvature visibility. Raw point density and scalar participation therefore remain too coarse even after the correct observation has been chosen.

Farey Discrepancy supplies the complementary window-selection lesson. FD-287--FD-288 reduce internal right-collar cancellation to rare order-`log x` near-one `zeta'/zeta` spikes, and FD-289 now transfers that sparsity uniformly to every translated height window longer than a sufficient polylogarithmic scale. Every polynomial Perron window is overwhelmingly phase-good. The escape has moved again: the remaining issue is whether the independent admissibility conditions on the actual cutoff intersect that large good set, not whether a moving continuous window can hide inside the global exceptional set.

Together AF-519 and FD-289 expose a reusable distinction between **global/counting participation and destination-weighted local participation**. In Arithmetic Fidelity the destination differentiates twice in log scale, so a set negligible to the zeroth moment can dominate the second-moment visibility statistic. In Farey Discrepancy a globally sparse phase event could still have mattered until the correct translated-window `L^2` control was proved. A density, count or global average is therefore evidential only after it is transferred through the exact moment/window functional used by the destination.

Nyman--Beurling gives a two-sided exponent ledger for generic projected-dilation source compression. Farey-frequency separation makes `m/N^2->infinity` sufficient for source-overlap decay, while explicit adjacent cancellations show that no universal support-localization criterion can replace the power `2` by any fixed smaller exponent. That does not identify the true destination threshold: the lower example need not correlate with the projected dilation or Ford target. Prime Lattice exhibits the opposite pattern, where representation really does improve: its hidden complex covariance can be reconstructed on the actual dyadic source from only `t=0` and a vanishing quarter-turn `t=-pi/(2 log X)`, and Abel transport removes the apparent coefficient-`l^1` conditioning cost. Yet that tiny physical shift is order one on the microscopic zero scale, so the missing theorem remains genuinely arithmetic rather than representational.

Möbius Cancellation and Prime Circle show two different ways an algebraic improvement can still collapse at the next interface. Low-denominator pair-sieve envelopes make the rational-mode coefficient ledger affordable and each mode admits denominator-free character completion, but MC-481 now proves that after canonical centering the apparent leakage subproblem is exactly the controlled envelope discrepancy minus the original hard-mask discrepancy. Centering therefore does not create an independent transfer resource; a useful continuation must add genuinely new signed/factorable structure. Observable variation is not evidential when its sign or law is supplied by a generic nuisance/correlation category rather than by the arithmetic source.

The remaining question is not whether some regularity helps, but whether independent arithmetic/source data supplies a non-oracular regularity and a multi-defect budget-reuse theorem strong enough for exclusion.

## What counts as a genuine representation escape?

A representation escape must change destination-visible information without hiding an equal cost in a complementary term, nuisance calibration, source budget, topology or target-alignment requirement. Prime Lattice is a particularly clean positive example: dyadic localization reduces an abstract Hilbert-transform reconstruction to one shrinking physical quarter-turn, and Abel transport shows that the representation step itself can be conditioned by ordinary source norms; the remaining obstruction is genuinely the arithmetic value of the oriented sample. Arithmetic Fidelity gives the converse warning: exact injectivity and a canonical inverse can coexist with robustly invisible tail transport, and AF-519 shows that the relevant participation is the destination's log-moment weighting rather than a scalar count or mass fraction.

Farey Discrepancy and Möbius Cancellation show how preserving signs or compressing frequencies can merely move the bottleneck. FD-289 removes the continuous finite-window phase loophole but leaves intersection with independent cutoff constraints. MC-481 shows that centering the positive-envelope leakage merely rewrites the exact target discrepancy once the envelope term is controlled. In both cases the next theorem must contribute new structure at the remaining interface rather than another estimate of a quantity already algebraically or measure-theoretically equivalent to the old target. Prime Circle similarly shows that cross-index interaction is not enough when the interaction remains Mellin-diagonal and meet/join-generated; the restored zeta structure can still be a universal semigroup artifact.

Nyman--Beurling, Visual Exploration, Weil Inertia and Weil Positivity sharpen what must be priced after a candidate escape appears. A source-compression exponent can be power-sharp without being the destination threshold; a richer residual law can remain nuisance even under finite cross-sector common-factor dependence; a highly localized probe becomes exactly coercive under a density cap whose arithmetic provenance and multi-target reuse still need proof; and noncentral balanced holonomy can remain spectrally visible while its positive Gram sign is universal. The reusable test is therefore to specify the admissible source family, exact quotient/readout, smallest datum on which the destination verdict changes, robustness topology, nuisance and source-budget class, and noncircular source-side theorem; then propagate the resulting margin through conditioning, complementary terms, target occupation, admissible-window selection, height/depth coverage and normalization. Formal reconstructibility, local sensitivity, algebraic richness, low-dimensional compression, noncommutativity, cycle holonomy and exact positivity are intermediate facts until that full chain survives.
