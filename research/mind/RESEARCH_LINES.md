## Where has the exact obstruction moved?

Arithmetic Fidelity: to **uniform growing-depth conditioning, restricted or structured nuisance classes, other noise/correlation laws, and a rational-prime discriminator that matched generalized-prime controls cannot preserve**. The resource is the target discriminator after nuisance quotient, not raw depth before it.

Farey Discrepancy: to **one-sided or `l^1` within-band repair variation whose replication-weighted amplitude-density crosses the capacity/first-row phase boundary, a switched-only repair with materially different intermediate motion, or a structural monotone-response upgrade**. Canonical full-grid inversion, isolated deep spikes and signed deep-band population averages are no longer generic sources of the missing exponent.

Möbius Cancellation: in the gap branch to **growing rank, a genuinely different source measure/coordinate system, absolute scale or discontinuous arithmetic data**; in the q-vdC branch to **an auxiliary retained-variable representation whose internal factor variables or coherent component interference are used before positive closure and whose signed estimate transfers back to Möbius before recombination or majorization erases it**.

Nyman--Beurling: simultaneously to **the established NB-272 section-defect/anchor-mismatch fork** and, on the source-native route, to **a finite arithmetic-section interaction, higher-jet channel, or genuinely different nonlinear/boundary mechanism not equivalent to the canonical finite point-evaluation Burnol defect**. Better conditioning of the same zero-evaluation Gram is no longer a live resource.

Prime Circle: to **the residual beyond the universal subcritical Buchstab--Riesz macroscopic mode**—centered fluctuations, mesoscopic/arithmetic scales or cross-level information—not merely to `alpha<1/2`.

Prime Lattice: to **the PL-407-scale signed mod-5 supertrace with off-diagonal coherences retained, a non-circular `lambda_min(C_arith)>=1/4` lower-frame theorem for raw exact local-factor deletion or a different divisor-faithful positive representation, and a separate residual-order bridge after model subtraction**.

Visual Exploration: to **gap-resolved noncommuting orientation, non-negligible cluster width or paid metric degeneration whose cost survives the complete relative-metric accounting and whose geometry has independent source/destination provenance**.

Weil Inertia: to **a defect-anchored mechanism that crosses the square-root-prime crossover through short-prime-polynomial cancellation, stronger per-defect response or collective sparse-defect aggregation, rather than only collective on-line density**.

Weil Positivity: to **a source-forced relation that genuinely destroys dense order-bounded two-sided tangency—rather than merely violating cutoffs through finitely many global constraints—or to a singular/nonclosable, nonlinear/quadratic, one-sided or globally coupled completion category**.
