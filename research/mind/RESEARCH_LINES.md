## Move beyond finite-dimensional sampling geometries already classified as deterministic controls

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-006-classify-the-boundary-model-before-calling-it-arithmetic`, `MI-009-local-correctness-does-not-determine-global-coherence`.

Visual Exploration now classifies fixed-dimensional consecutive Gram-lag geometry through the inverse Gram clock. Single-lag Haar behavior extends across the well-sampled supercritical range; an `r`-lag vector has an order-`r` finite-difference threshold; and at critical scaling an exact Newton transform yields Haar lower differences times one deterministic top-difference curve. Increasing a fixed delay dimension therefore buys higher deterministic clock cancellation, not a new arithmetic source.

This reinforces the broader Mathia criterion: a new observable should identify the dimension, support, sampling law, source relation, or analytic coordinate that grows beyond the relevant closure theorem. Random-looking fixed-dimensional phase clouds, growing collision rank with classical modular organization, or larger algebraic representation spaces do not count as new arithmetic information unless the residual survives their exact deterministic controls.
