# Global mathematical questions

This file records mathematical questions that only become visible by comparing several current Mathia research lines. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does a quantitative budget become a genuine source discriminator?

Several lines now show that the decisive budget is rarely the first coarse norm one can write down. The useful quantity must be fixed before target inspection, live in the geometry actually consumed by the destination, and be occupied by the physical source rather than by a matched control.

Visual Exploration gives a sharp matched-control progression. VIS-416 removes the generic reciprocal-bandwidth scale from Wang balance radii; VIS-417 shows that matching bandwidth, mode count, coefficient magnitudes, long-time energy and global supremum still leaves an uncontrolled beat scale; VIS-418 shows that even fixing the complete two-mode frequency set does not fix alignment with a distinguished height because translation moves the same quiet window. VIS-419 then resolves the fixed-shell null exactly: a finite Wang shell is a character polynomial on its base-prime torus, and long translation is the Haar pushforward of those base-prime phases with all ratio-mode dependencies retained. VIS-420 adds the quantitative finite-window gate: for a finite character polynomial, translation-to-Haar error is the exact sinc-weighted nonzero-character sum and is bounded by the coefficient mass divided by the relevant small divisors `|sum m_p log p|`. Thus mode count, outer bandwidth and coefficient norm do not by themselves control calibration. The remaining discriminator is a source-specific anomaly at pre-specified arithmetic heights relative to the exact self-null, together with a shell-uniform small-divisor/coefficient theorem if the shell grows with height.

Weil Inertia supplies the source-category analogue. WI-417--WI-423 show that a frozen density or function-space budget is meaningful only when it belongs to an independently generated source and controls the Poisson-capacity geometry of the actual defect; assigning regularity directly to the canonical atomic zero measure would already build the target conclusion into the hypothesis. WI-424--WI-428 then show that bounded processing on a fixed safe strip remains cubically blind to shallow defects. WI-429 identifies the exact category change: on a line inside a defect, a norm-one Hardy projection separates deeper Kunik zeros and recovers order-`1/d` energy without an operator blow-up. WI-430 shows that this projected energy is already coercive at the first-defect level: each zero deeper than `a` costs more than `q(a)=pi/(1/2-a)`, and the global subquantum threshold would itself exclude every such zero. The tractable source question is therefore not a vague global regularity bound but finite-height control of `E_a(T)` for a **predetermined boundary-approaching family**, with explicit joint dependence on `a` and `T` and no target-adaptive probe choice.

Farey Discrepancy turns a destination capacity target into an increasingly rigid source anomaly. FD-298--FD-300 reduce the low-height hard complement to the block-Mertens field and show that near-capacity `l^1` mass forces a dyadic shell with mean-square block increment at least `N x^(2c-o(1))`. FD-301 removes the same-block mask at logarithmic Fourier cost, forcing a large rational additive-twist two-point sum, and FD-302 confines the genuinely twisted branch to a low block-Fourier arc. FD-303 then shows that above the polynomial-depth threshold `lambda>1/(6c)` this aligned anomaly cannot remain localized: the Lipschitz dependence of length-`N` Möbius sums on their start forces a polynomially superdiagonal complete sliding second moment and hence a polynomially thick family of ordinary untwisted positive two-point correlations at many moving shifts. A cancellation theorem for this exact family would kill the near-capacity route in that regime. The implication remains necessary rather than sufficient because large ordinary correlations need not reconstruct the post-divisor-replication `l^1` field.

Prime Lattice gives a complementary lesson about using the exact destination geometry rather than a convenient ambient norm. PL-437 compresses the unresolved mod-5 orientation to two coordinates `(s,f)` and gives the Euclidean Weyl disk as a sufficient condition. PL-438 derives the exact strict-branch Schur ellipse `(g-mu)^T E^(-1)(g-mu)<=alpha`, with its center and anisotropy determined by visible real-product data plus the residue diagonal, and exhibits an admissible covariance accepted by the ellipse but rejected by the Weyl disk. The remaining theorem is arithmetic: place the actual prime source inside that source-adapted ellipse at variance scale by an unconditional input.

Across these examples, a bound becomes a source discriminator only after four checks: its source object is independently generated; its geometry matches the destination failure mode; matched controls preserve every nuisance scale that can manufacture the effect; and the physical source is forced to satisfy or violate the bound with quantitative margin. VIS-420, WI-430, FD-303 and PL-438 sharpen different parts of that test: finite-window convergence can carry a small-divisor tariff even when the limiting null is exact; a boundary detector can already contain a fixed first-defect quantum; an aligned variance anomaly can force a much thicker ordinary-correlation population; and an anisotropic destination region can replace a coarse Euclidean norm.

## Which source dependence survives quotienting, population ceilings and equilibrium scalarization?

Möbius Cancellation now prices the short-endpoint branch in the actual source geometry in three stages. MC-497 gives the exact start-average quadratic estimate `sum_x |S_(c,x)(H)|^2 << pH`, so fixed normalized short-overlap bias occupies only `O(1/H)` of the conductor classes; there is no residual `p^(-1/2)` exceptional-density floor in this averaged statement. MC-498 turns that into an effective-support condition on the induced start weights with explicit quotient-fibre costs, and MC-499 adds the per-block label ceiling `1+Q/p`. The corrected second-moment tariff is

`T_(p,H)=(p/H) log^2(2H)`.

MC-500 then shows that optimizing the dyadic scale cannot evade scarcity: reciprocal quotient compression gives the Q-free ceiling

`R_start <= 4+sqrt(2Z_*/p)`

with `Z_*=max(X,X+h)`. In the nonconstant-tariff regime the current certificate therefore needs the single source-height scale `Z_* >> p^3 H^(-2) log^4(2H)` up to the target-saving constants. The older independent `p^2 log^2(2H)` gate belonged to a superseded square-root term and should not be carried forward. Failure before the Q-free gate is method-limited source scarcity across all `Q`; only after the Q-free and per-block gates pass does a failed participation test diagnose quotient folding, weight concentration or winning-side imbalance.

Farey Discrepancy provides a complementary exact-carrier example after a mask quotient. FD-301 expresses the same-block mask in additive characters with only logarithmic `l^1` cost, and FD-302 proves that high block frequencies cannot alone carry a near-capacity anomaly in the live depth range. FD-303 then shows that, once the aligned variance is large enough and `lambda>1/(6c)`, even that low-frequency description is not the terminal carrier: the anomaly necessarily thickens into ordinary untwisted moving-shift correlations on a complete start interval. Which representation is load-bearing is therefore regime-dependent, but in each case it is obtained by pricing and then removing the exact source mask rather than by choosing a convenient ambient Fourier statistic.

Prime Circle supplies the stronger scalarization warning. PC-414--PC-419 show that finite/infinite conductor descendants, finite Mellin jets, bounded Mellin translations, unrestricted Weyl closure and even the norm-closed single-shell diagonal algebra do not recover a source interaction already lost before shell-2 scalarization. The surviving object must therefore be cross-shell, non-diagonal or otherwise source-coupled before the scalar channel discards it.

Visual Exploration gives the corresponding phase quotient. VIS-409--VIS-411 show that one-shell Wigner data are repackaging, cross-shell fields retain only already-present labelled coherence, and projective shell phases reduce to Bargmann cycle holonomies. VIS-418--VIS-420 sharpen the distinction between gauge, source anchoring and finite-window calibration: absolute phase relative to a distinguished arithmetic height is meaningful only when the source fixes that height; its correct fixed-shell null is translation/Haar on the base-prime torus; and numerical finite-window replacement of that Haar law pays the actual prime-log small divisors. The quotient category, matched null and convergence mechanism must all be fixed before residual phase is interpreted as signal.

Weil Positivity now closes the entire auxiliary-frequency compensation route for **decoupled product KMS dynamics**, not merely the previously covered atomic or commutative fibres. WP-425 proves that every positive-temperature KMS state of `alpha tensor tau` on arbitrary unital C-star dynamical systems is separately invariant under the two factor flows. Hence every nonzero source Bohr mode has zero expectation against every auxiliary observable, including an opposite-frequency mode, even for correlated states and noncentral noncommutative fibres. A surviving equilibrium mechanism must therefore change the dynamics or source relation itself -- for example through a genuinely source-derived interacting/non-product correspondence not removed by the existing equivalence controls -- rather than enrich the spectator fibre while retaining the product flow.

Nyman--Beurling supplies a quantitative scalarization boundary. NB-319 shows that every mesoscopic reciprocal annulus up to the quadratic frontier contains a direction whose primitive envelope is within a square-root logarithm of linear growth, so primitive `L^infinity` control cannot yield a fixed-power gain. NB-320 computes the actual weighted pairing at the half-period ratio `m/n^2->1/2` and finds a Hilbert-normalized rank-one error of order `1/sqrt(log n)`. NB-321 shows that this is a rational-period folding mechanism rather than an isolated point: at the distinct ratio `m/n^2->1/4` an explicit boundary term again gives a `1/sqrt(log n)` obstruction, while every fixed rational denominator reduces to finitely many primitive samples even though those samples may cancel for some denominators. The correct carrier is therefore the weighted rational-folding boundary combination itself, and the live problem is to classify rational, near-rational and genuinely irrational quadratic scales at the correct subpower rate.

The reusable cross-line question is therefore: **after the lawful quotient and the actual destination scalarization, what source-dependent quantity remains both invariant and quantitatively load-bearing?** Effective support is not raw pair multiplicity and may be capped before weights enter; a masked correlation may reduce first to a low Fourier mode and then to an ordinary correlation population; a phase coordinate is not intrinsic unless the source fixes its gauge, null and finite-window calibration; an auxiliary Bohr mode is inert under every decoupled product KMS state; and a large primitive envelope is not the actual bilinear error. These are different manifestations of the same discipline: price the exact carrier that remains after the representation has finished discarding information.

## What replaces a scalar summary when nuisance geometry or leading normalization degenerates?

Arithmetic Fidelity now gives both an obstruction and a precise replacement geometry. AF-523--AF-528 show that scalar boundary-curvature summaries can be matched to all algebraic orders while an exact relational derivative still recovers hidden source shape; that recovery can nevertheless be too small on the physical observation window to be stable. AF-533--AF-534 then identify the nuisance-relative carrier: effective Gaussian information is the squared precision/Cameron--Martin distance of the target direction from the **closed** nuisance geometry, so algebraic identifiability need not imply a positive robust margin.

AF-535 adds observation loss. With upstream efficient score `e`, observation contraction `C`, and downstream nuisance closure `M`, the efficient carrier is `(I-P_M)Ce`; information can disappear by channel erasure or by nuisance recontamination. AF-536 gives the sharper deterministic quotient: for `P=E[.\mid Y]` and `K=ker P`,

`I_Y^eff = dist(e, closure(Lambda+K))^2`.

AF-537 extends this to sequential Markov compression with evolving nuisance semantics: every stage loses information through direct erasure, pushed-nuisance recontamination, or new-nuisance reclassification. AF-538 then makes the replacement geometry genuinely directional. For a finite-dimensional target, the efficient information is a matrix `J_j`; the kernel of the positive-semidefinite stage loss `J_j-J_(j+1)` is exactly the subspace of target combinations preserved by that stage, and the end-to-end fidelity subspace is the intersection of these kernels. Relative to the source information metric, the normalized endpoint matrix has a coordinate-free spectrum in `[0,1]` that distinguishes exact survival, partial weakening and total first-order loss. This remains a representation theorem until the target family, nuisance directions and compression map are derived from the arithmetic source.

This sharpens the replacement-object requirement. A successful non-scalar observable must not merely break an algebraic collision; it must place the **source-relevant target subspace**, not just one hand-picked direction, outside the closed nuisance-plus-invisible geometry after the actual observation chain. A target-dependent score lift can be first-order sufficient without being an admissible arithmetic representation. Prime Lattice's Schur ellipse is an exact finite-dimensional destination geometry of this type. Weil Inertia supplies a complementary observation-geometry warning: the moving-line Hardy projection can retain a defect without singular operator norm, but WI-430 shows that its global subquantum budget is already equivalent to excluding the defect, so only independently sourced finite-height control is a legitimate intermediate target.

Prime Circle remains the negative control: a finite derivative or richer operator readout can be perfectly informative about a chosen representation while still adding no arithmetic mechanism if matched source classes reproduce it. The remaining cross-line challenge is to construct replacement objects that are simultaneously **source-natural, destination-complete and stable after the real quotient/compression**. Exact infinitesimal recovery, a convenient nuisance-dependent lift, an ambient norm bound, or a richer representation is not enough; the carrier must be forced by the source before target inspection and retain quantitative separation after every nuisance, gauge, observation and semantic map that the destination actually applies.
