## Can a source-native signed transformation become admissible without erasing the localization needed by Weil positivity?

[MI-009](intuition/MI-009-local-correctness-does-not-determine-global-coherence.md), [MI-012](intuition/MI-012-information-content-and-destination-sensitivity-are-separate-resources.md) and [MI-013](intuition/MI-013-closing-the-bulk-often-leaves-a-thin-source-specific-endpoint.md) separate source provenance, admissibility and target sensitivity.

Weil Positivity has closed positive additive preservation, scalar nonlinear post-processing and the entire finite canonical dilation-coboundary hierarchy as easy escapes. WP-290--WP-291 show that finite source-native pole killing preserves Mangoldt localization but leaves temperedness exactly RH-equivalent.

WP-292 supplies the complementary failure mode: counting convolution removes the nontrivial-zero poles and permits unconditional temperedness after finite differencing, but only by replacing the prime-power carrier with an all-integer one. Exact Möbius deconvolution restores Mangoldt localization and simultaneously restores the RH-equivalent zero screen.

The open theorem is therefore not merely “find another pole killer.” Derive a genuinely different signed/global coupling that becomes admissible unconditionally **while preserving enough arithmetic localization for the exact Weil form**, or prove a non-exact recovery of localized information that does not reconstruct the RH-complete source. Matched counting/generalized-prime controls must distinguish genuine arithmetic content from category descent obtained by smoothing away the source.

## What should Prime Circle do with an observable that RH itself makes non-prime?

PC-285 strengthens unconditional Li-grid reproduction of the exact boundary-log finite-section spectrum to the Vinogradov--Korobov source-transport range. PC-286 goes much farther conditionally: under RH the same spectral law is Li-grid reproducible whenever `B=o(sqrt(q)/(log q)^6)`.

The one-profile source-placement branch therefore has a reversed diagnostic near the rational scale: persistent order-one prime-versus-Li separation in that range would contradict RH, while collapse does not imply RH. The remaining one-profile frontier is the polylogarithmic neighborhood of the square-root denominator scale, or a different RH signature than separation. Otherwise the architecture should move to cross-level, multi-source, directional, noncommuting or richer observables.

## Can CA selection forbid the one-sided PNT-envelope saturation required by fast Robin recovery?

RE-107 closes whole-fan positive Chebyshev transport. RE-108 prices normalization-only recovery, and RE-109 restores the PNT-regular event source to add a stretched-subpower recovery factor.

RE-110 turns the residual “source phase” into a precise workload statement. Any recovery corridor of length `L` must carry positive one-sided event workload `W_+ >= (1+o(1))h(C)Y^2 log Y`; near the RE-109 lower corridor scale, some selected event must have forward defect of order `Y E(Y)`. Thus fast recovery requires near-saturation of the favorable side of the global PNT discrepancy envelope on a CA-selected interval.

The open theorem is now a selected anti-saturation result: prove that those intervals have `M_+=o(YE(Y))`, or couple another arithmetic coordinate strongly enough that one-sided saturation destroys the next quantitative positive witness. A better two-sided global PNT error alone does not address this sign-conditioned gate.
