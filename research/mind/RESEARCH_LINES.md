## When does an escape from one obstruction survive the next reduction?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit, [MI-030](intuition/MI-030-effective-complexity-is-a-geometry-weighted-budget.md) sharpens the resource side, and [MI-031](intuition/MI-031-source-dependence-must-change-the-surviving-quotient-not-just-the-representation.md) sharpens the provenance side: after the representation is fixed, nominal complexity and nominal source dependence must both be reduced to the quantity that actually survives the line-specific quotient and destination map.

Arithmetic Fidelity has now separated metric deformation, hard source restriction and finite-resolution soft restriction. AF-472 keeps the original TV metric and proves that the full `s`-sparse simplex has exact Hilbert distortion `sqrt(s)`. AF-473 shows the all-scales boundary is singular: allowing any fixed positive diffuse tail on an infinite alphabet restores arbitrarily high-dimensional Hamming cubes and infinite distortion. AF-474 then supplies the positive scale-aware counterpart: best-`s`-term tail mass enters additively, and on a tail budget `epsilon` finite multiplicative conditioning returns above every declared separation `delta>2epsilon`, with cost `sqrt(s)/(1-2epsilon/delta)`. The surviving question is the actual arithmetic source tradeoff among support, tail and destination resolution.

Farey Discrepancy has now bounded the response-side exact-omission gap from both directions. FD-248 identifies the weighted incidence-Möbius inverse norm `kappa(D)`; FD-249 shows one high-arity merge makes it unbounded; FD-250 constructs exact adjacent-pair omissions with `K=N^(o(1))` for which `kappa(D)` beats every fixed power of `log log N`. FD-251 proves the universal ceiling `kappa(D)<=N^(rho_*-1+o(1))`, where `zeta(rho_*)=2` and `rho_*=1.728647...`, by injecting divisibility chains into ordered factorizations. Hence all subpolynomial exact-omission families are coefficient-capacity coercive, and the live response gap has moved to the polynomial omission window and the special adjacent-pair geometry.

Möbius Cancellation has now priced both sides of the relational-capacity boundary. MC-435 proves that bounded or `r^(o(1))` local decorations on the canonical prime-order scaffold have only subpolynomial primorial-scale source diversity unless their aggregate information budget reaches `Theta(r log r)`. MC-436 shows that the global ordinal pattern of adjacent selected-prime gaps realizes all `(r-1)!` types and hence reaches `N_r^(1-o(1))` source diversity, but only by paying that identity-scale information budget. The open issue is no longer raw capacity; it is source-height efficiency and survival of a useful residue through a Möbius-facing analytic operation.

Nyman--Beurling has reached the logarithmic rank-coupled coverage endpoint for the aggregate Ford quantity. NB-263 still shows that a strip fixed before eventual rank can be flattened when conditioning and later rank are free. NB-264--NB-266 force bounded-cost Euler-anchor response on fixed-power, almost-logarithmic and iterated-log-enlarged radii. The open issues are now sublogarithmic minimality, the cost of rank-adaptive dual complexity, and survival through the actual Hardy/Nyman projection.

Prime Circle now has two distinct classicalization boundaries. PC-384--PC-387 complete the scalar full/primitive inverse-power phase diagram: primitive arithmetic is either a coprimality-sieved lattice kernel, a universal leading law with finite-Euler-product correction, or universal after normalization. Visual Exploration has closed the canonical compact fixed-depth cluster loophole more sharply. VIS-356 proves that raw bank-size growth cannot lower the quotient floor while one uniformly conditioned `q`-scale anchor survives. Weil Inertia and Weil Positivity have both moved from broad structural hopes to sharply localized missing terms. WI-382 proves that eventual one-sided boundedness of the fixed-width renormalized Chebyshev remainder is RH-equivalent, including on the actual integer lattice. WI-383 proves that this limiting remainder has negative liminf unconditionally, leaving the exact order-one source-perforation/rooted correction or domain exclusion as the direct-repair carrier in this trial family. On the positivity side, WP-391 rules out an exact prime-power selector in the canonical Bost--Connes divisibility diagonal, and WP-392 extends the obstruction to the full bounded positive cone: exact mixed-prime diagonal nullity forces every bounded source-algebra diagonal to vanish, and positivity then forces the operator itself to vanish. A surviving Bost--Connes route must therefore make a canonical category change or delay support selection rather than merely add bounded off-diagonal structure.

## Which physical cost survives after representation artifacts are removed?

The newest results reinforce that the surviving currency is weighted, scale-aware and post-quotient. Arithmetic Fidelity prices soft concentration by support cost `sqrt(s)`, tail floor `2epsilon`, and destination resolution `delta`. Farey prices exact defects by `K kappa(D)` and now bounds `kappa(D)` itself by ordered-factorization chain entropy. Möbius prices conditional source variation by an aggregate `r log r` information budget before asking whether any of it survives projection. Nyman--Beurling now reaches logarithmic coverage by spending rank-dependent dual-certificate order while losing the previous pointwise lower bound, so cutoff complexity, aggregate response and destination projection must remain distinct currencies. Prime Circle distinguishes arithmetic interaction from support already supplied by the primorial sieve. Visual Exploration replaces raw bank size first by anchor packing and then by the full occupancy-width Gram scale `N epsilon^(2(q-1))`. Weil Inertia removes both the polar main term and the stationary zero remainder before crediting the residual source-dependent correction. Weil Positivity distinguishes a source-forced critical Gram from programmable masks, the too-small divisibility diagonal, and the still-too-restrictive full bounded positive source algebra.

These are not one theorem and their currencies are not numerically interchangeable. The adversarial commonality remains the one recorded in MI-030 and MI-031: remove universal scaffolds, symmetry, known kernels, classical preselection and category-programmable choices first; then price every loss and compensating resource in the source-varying quantity that actually reaches the destination. The latest evidence strengthens that audit principle but does not justify a stronger universal mathematical bridge across the lines.
