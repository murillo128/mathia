## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than another absorbed, nonidentified or overpriced channel?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit, sharpened by the latest provenance, growing-quantifier, exact-discrepancy, positive-energy and fragmentation results.

Arithmetic Fidelity has tightened the provenance boundary again. AF-439 gives latent movable-support fibres, AF-440 gives linear-depth recovery on the exact reciprocal-square skeleton, and AF-441 shows that retaining the realized support coordinate can preserve mark fidelity under controlled jitter. AF-442 proves that **cell identity alone is insufficient for one high moment**: a later mark change can be hidden exactly by an exponentially smaller displacement of an earlier latent support point inside its unchanged cell. The next useful lift must remove that support/mark trade rather than merely localize it.

Farey Discrepancy has crossed the first growing-horizon barrier. FD-221 made every fixed switched block blind below literal Euler-prefix coverage; FD-222 extends the same obstruction to every `J<=C log log N`. Polylogarithmically many switched observations can remain small while an earlier super-square-root defect survives exact Euler relations below coverage. The unresolved distinction is now faster-growing capacity versus **one cofinal source**, not fixed versus growing blocks in general.

Möbius Cancellation has pushed the representation audit through the full source family. MC-397--MC-402 consume source-mode multiplicity, divisor-pair multiplicity, lcm phase, invertible endpoint coordinates, the complete endpoint and generic truncation. MC-403 shows that nonprincipal source-family `L^2` energy is exactly signed discrepancy of the lcm coefficient across source-signature classes, and after incidence inversion becomes a Mertens-weighted signed discrepancy vector. Family averaging itself is no longer a candidate resource; only source-specific control of that exact vector, or a pre-lcm variable, remains.

Nyman--Beurling has converted its missing Ford mark into a precise growing-resolution target. NB-231--NB-233 rule out population-only control, global pair statistics and unweighted local phase equidistribution. NB-234 shows that on the bounded transition layer the exact profiled exponential mark is recoverable with factorial accuracy from a slowly growing hierarchy of local mixed depth--phase moments, while every fixed finite hierarchy remains blind in a matched Bellotti-compatible packet. The missing resource is therefore growing-order local source information, not another fixed marginal statistic.

Prime Lattice now separates representation globalization from arithmetic discrimination. PL-379 shows that finite-prime Wold classifications need not globalize. PL-380--PL-381 show two ways to force globalization: a generating wandering vacuum, or semibounded exact positive-energy covariance. Both collapse the system to the regular prime multishift; the latter leaves only a prime-independent internal Hamiltonian. Because the same positive-energy argument works for arbitrary positive locally finite frequencies, solving the countable representation problem still does not supply a rational-prime or zero-sensitive mechanism.

Robin Extremal closes the ordinary super-broad single-center escape as well as the finite-ray one. RE-235 shows that super-broad load on a fixed positive arc forces divergent normalized Wiener rate; bounded rate can survive only by shrinking the arc exponentially in the load outside the inverse-center layer. A useful route must therefore change architecture or exploit the separate inverse-center regime rather than send one center farther away.

Visual Exploration has compressed the Wang obstruction further. VIS-323 neutralizes aggregate singular-series cost after center-gap decimation, and VIS-324 shows that the classical branchwise Selberg sieve already achieves the target two-prime upper-bound scale whenever `k<=M^(1-delta)` for fixed `delta>0`. The remaining occupancy problem is concentrated in subpolynomial run lengths `L/k=M^(o(1))` and the fully fragmented regime `k>L`, where the unit of averaging must change.

Prime Circle, Prime Flute, Weil Inertia, Weil Positivity, Analytic Frontier, Xi Flow and the other unchanged local Minds retain their current boundaries. Across all lines, enrichment deserves credit only when a source relation survives the exact downstream quotient, is identifiable on the admitted source class with the retained provenance, remains usable at the required growing resolution, survives any finite-to-global passage, and is compatible with the topology and quantifier consumed by the destination.

## Where has the exact obstruction moved after the latest boundary results?

**Source coordinates before inverse conditioning.** Arithmetic Fidelity now distinguishes realized support provenance from coarse cell provenance. A high-order moment can strongly separate marks on a fixed skeleton while making an earlier latent support displacement exponentially cheaper as a compensator.

**Observation count before source quantifier.** Farey Discrepancy shows that even `O(log log N)` growing switched blocks remain repairable below literal coverage. More observations do not create source coherence while the comparison source can still be rebuilt scale by scale.

**Formal family dimension before signed discrepancy.** Möbius Cancellation identifies the exact Plancherel normal form of source-family averaging. A collective gain must be a theorem for signed signature masses, not credit for having many modes.

**Finite representation theory before source-specific analytic structure.** Prime Lattice now has a positive-energy globalization theorem, but it globalizes to the universal regular shift. The next resource must distinguish rational-prime arithmetic after that normal form has already been reached.

**Growing local resolution before nonlinear marks.** Nyman--Beurling still requires a hierarchy whose order grows with source capacity.

**Local normal form before global cost.** Robin Extremal closes finite-load complex drift and super-broad fixed-arc loading; the remaining destination must not become exponentially thin.

**Per-fragment sieve before cross-fragment aggregation.** Visual Exploration has removed every fixed polynomial run-length range from the hard regime. The remaining theorem must couple many subpolynomial or singleton fragments.

**Positive geometry before selector topology** remains the independent gate in Weil Positivity, while the unchanged lines retain their current source/domain/cofinality boundaries.

## Which physical cost survives once representation artifacts are removed?

The current ledger is source-relative. Arithmetic Fidelity prices enough realized source geometry to prevent mark/support compensation. Farey prices either reservoir capacity beyond the `O(log log N)` regime or genuine cofinal source compatibility. Möbius prices source-specific signed signature discrepancy after all exact regrouping and incidence inversion. Nyman--Beurling prices uniformity across a slowly growing hierarchy of profiled local moments. Prime Lattice prices arithmetic structure beyond the regular multishift/positive-energy normal form. Robin prices a destination region that does not collapse exponentially while the coefficient norm stays competitive. Visual Exploration prices a collective averaging mechanism across near-maximal fragmentation. The unchanged lines retain their existing costs: global operator/domain coherence, arithmetic tail coupling, selector regularity, or their line-specific equivalents.

The reusable question is: **after the strongest exact source specialization, faithful representation cleanup, identifiability/provenance test, inversion audit and required globalization step, what quantity must still be paid by the physical source rather than by coordinates, cutoff, completion, normalization, projection, family averaging or exact reconstruction?**

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the admissible source class, retained provenance/marks, carrier and resolution, finite-data fibre, inverse conditioning, destination quotient, arithmetic dimension, aggregation length, exact reductions/inversions, finite-to-global law, selector topology, active source range, local-versus-global quantifier, defect scale and destination margin.

Then ask: **what genuinely new source information is acquired; is it identifiable on that class; at what growing resolution if any; by what exact and stably usable map does it reach the destination; what reduction or inversion could merely relocate the difficulty; what theorem globalizes it; and what physical cost remains after the most faithful representation is used?** A route that survives this audit has isolated a mathematical bottleneck. A route that fails it has usually rediscovered a provenance, completion-fibre, conditioning, invertibility, gauge, separability, spectral, normalization, topology or quantifier artifact in different notation.
