## Which apparent resource survives the exact destination map?

The common audit remains: identify the admissible source image, quotient universal scaffolding and nuisance directions, follow the exact information-loss map into the destination, and only then price the surviving resource. The newest evidence shows that failure can occur on either side of that map. Arithmetic Fidelity now has an explicit canonical inverse for a whole curvature profile after quotienting only global dilation, so exact information can survive almost completely while finite-window/noise stability remains unresolved. Möbius Cancellation gives the opposite phenomenon on a complete CRT period: the translated character phase and pair sieve factor exactly, leaving only the ordinary local density; expanding the remaining incomplete sieve exactly and estimating every branch independently then pays exponential branch multiplicity. Weil Positivity is sharper still: passive full-unitary matrix coefficients may be genuinely noncommuting before readout but cancel identically from the source/range projections that form the critical Gram.

Nyman--Beurling provides an exact intermediate factorization. The canonical dilation ejects a large-rank source subspace from the old section, but ordinary first-free enrichment sees it only through `Xi=YQ^+Y^*`, where `Q` measures source escape and `Y` is the sample intertwining defect. Large source rank can therefore coexist with zero destination gain. Visual Exploration shows the dual nuisance problem: after all obvious qutrit quotients, source-stabilizer symmetry leaves the residual weight law completely arbitrary outside a specified probabilistic mechanism; an explicit replicated-Gaussian packet model restores a falsifiable normalized-Gamma null only by fixing its replica architecture independently. Weil Inertia adds a budget version of the same ledger: optimizing the whole real Poisson-scale family improves individual high-zero sensitivity only to `O(d/gamma)` under finite total variation, while the classical height-unweighted Carleman charge is obtained without the independently finite source budget needed for exclusion. Across these cases, nominal information, symmetry, dimension or local sensitivity counts only after the exact readout and its budget are fixed.

## What counts as a genuine representation escape?

A representation escape must change destination-visible information without hiding an equal cost in a complementary term, nuisance calibration, source budget or target-alignment requirement. Prime Lattice remains a positive example: vertical shift changes the scalar observation family so that a formerly missing complex quadrature becomes recoverable, with finite-aperture conditioning explicitly priced. Arithmetic Fidelity now supplies a useful contrasting endpoint: a quotient can be exactly and constructively invertible while still leaving a hard stability problem once only finite/noisy observations are allowed. Möbius Cancellation shows both extremes of representation loss: complete-period completion factors the hoped-for interaction down to density, while complete inclusion-exclusion of the remaining short problem explodes the branch count unless sieve structure is retained. Nyman--Beurling shows that abundant canonical source escape becomes ordinary gain only through the sample-visible intertwining defect. Visual Exploration shows that symmetry can leave so much nuisance freedom that no residual law is identified, while a stronger null becomes meaningful only when a concrete replica mechanism fixes its shape. Weil Inertia shows that stronger defect tariffs may require abandoning the finite budget that made the original identity useful. Weil Positivity shows that even genuinely noncommuting fibre data are irrelevant if the chosen projection readout annihilates them exactly.

The reusable test is therefore: specify the admissible source family; state the exact quotient/readout and any packet/remainder split; identify the smallest datum on which the destination verdict changes; expose which signs, phases, membership bits, accessibility profiles, nuisance parameters or spectral directions survive that map; exhibit source-side control under assumptions weaker than the target theorem; and propagate the resulting margin through conditioning, truncation, completion, complementary terms, target occupation, budget and normalization. Carrier capacity, formal reconstructibility, nicer component coordinates, local positivity, nominal coefficient signs, complete-period formulas, branchwise easy estimates, aggregate determinant growth, symmetry invariance, matrix noncommutativity and finite moment budgets are intermediate facts, not substitutes for that chain.
