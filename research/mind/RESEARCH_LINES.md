## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than another absorbed, nonidentified or overpriced channel?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit, sharpened by the latest source-provenance, exact-inversion, marked-statistic and fragmentation results.

Fragmentation is arithmetically neutral at that level; the remaining question is whether runs of length about `L/k` are long enough for a uniform two-prime sieve/correlation estimate before maximal fragmentation.

Farey Discrepancy still resolves every fixed switched block rather than the cofinal problem. Prime Circle and Prime Lattice retain their distinct finite-to-global operator/domain boundaries. Across these examples, enrichment deserves credit only when a source relation survives the exact downstream quotient, is identifiable on the admissible source class with the retained provenance, remains stably recoverable at the required resolution, survives any finite-to-global passage, and is compatible with the topology and quantifier consumed by the destination.

## Where has the exact obstruction moved after the latest boundary results?

**Source class and provenance before inversion.** Arithmetic Fidelity now asks not merely whether support can move, but which source identity remains attached while it moves. Marked jitter can preserve fidelity; latent jitter can restore fibres.

**Formal dimension before retained arithmetic coupling.** Möbius Cancellation now audits the incomplete lcm mask itself: exact incidence inversion pays with Mertens weights. A new theorem must exploit source-specific structure rather than relabel transferred difficulty.

**Growing local resolution before nonlinear marks.** Nyman--Beurling shows that the Ford exponential mark is reconstructible from polynomial moments only when moment order grows with source capacity. Fixed-order cancellation and growing-order reconstruction are qualitatively different information channels.

**Local normal form before global cost.** Robin Extremal closes finite-load complex drift and now super-broad load on fixed arcs; the latter can keep bounded rate only by making the destination exponentially thin.

**Fragmentation before arithmetic loss.** Visual Exploration shows that more pieces need not mean more singular-series cost. After exact averaging neutralizes that factor, the load-bearing quantity is the number of usable samples per piece.

**Finite stages before global coherence** and **positive geometry before selector topology** remain independent global gates in Farey/Prime Circle/Prime Lattice and Weil Positivity respectively.

## Which physical cost survives once representation artifacts are removed?

The current ledger is source-relative. Arithmetic Fidelity prices retained provenance before conditioning. Möbius prices genuinely source-specific cancellation after regrouping and Möbius incidence inversion. Nyman--Beurling prices uniformity across a slowly growing hierarchy of profiled local moments. Robin prices a destination region that does not collapse exponentially while the coefficient norm stays competitive. Visual Exploration prices run length and sieve uniformity after aggregate local factors have been neutralized. The unchanged lines retain their existing costs: cofinal compatibility, global operator/domain coherence, arithmetic tail coupling, selector regularity, or their line-specific equivalents.

The reusable question is: **after the strongest exact source specialization, faithful representation cleanup, identifiability/provenance test, inversion audit and required globalization step, what quantity must still be paid by the physical source rather than by coordinates, cutoff, completion, normalization, projection or exact reconstruction?**

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the admissible source class, retained provenance/marks, carrier and resolution, finite-data fibre, inverse conditioning, destination quotient, arithmetic dimension, aggregation length, exact reductions/inversions, finite-to-global law, selector topology, active source range, local-versus-global quantifier, defect scale and destination margin.

Then ask: **what genuinely new source information is acquired; is it identifiable on that class; at what growing resolution if any; by what exact and stably usable map does it reach the destination; what reduction or inversion could merely relocate the difficulty; what theorem globalizes it; and what physical cost remains after the most faithful representation is used?** A route that survives this audit has isolated a mathematical bottleneck. A route that fails it has usually rediscovered a provenance, completion-fibre, conditioning, invertibility, gauge, separability, spectral, normalization, topology or quantifier artifact in different notation.
