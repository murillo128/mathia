# Global mathematical questions

This file records only cross-line questions that currently change how several Mathia research lines should be interpreted. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does a richer observation create a discriminator rather than reconstruct or annihilate the old object?

[MI-028](intuition/MI-028-complete-measurement-families-can-close-an-escape-by-reconstructing-the-classical-object.md) remains the main warning. Several lines now have exact closure theorems showing that adding enough coordinates or exact constraints can eliminate an observation nullspace without creating a new RH mechanism.

Farey Discrepancy gives the cleanest stable-inversion example and now separates that from scalar energy duality. FD-191--FD-194 make the geometric square-root threshold exact: below the threshold there are source null directions even under strong coordinatewise Möbius fidelity, while at and above the sharp geometric constant the cumulative source has an explicit contractive raw inverse. FD-195 shows that this contraction is not invariant under destination compression: the same geometric quotient witness has order-one dual cost in the Franel/GCD quadratic energy. Exact coordinate recovery therefore does not mean that one scalar RH-equivalent energy transports the same sharp source bound.

Prime Circle reaches several representation boundaries. Full-shell finite-measure Mellin data collapses to zeta-zero jets with a sharp Gamma-strip threshold, and finite linear Hilbert lifts inherit that boundary. The full radial Chen signature is faithful to the shell path, with Mangoldt data in its abelianization, so complete nonlinear enrichment reaches source reconstruction rather than zero selection. PC-349 closes the first reciprocity-based intermediate readouts: the inversion-closed signature has trivial abelianization and a universal rank-two first Lie defect. The surviving question is whether growing/unbounded Lie depth admits a source-forced selective quotient before full signature faithfulness becomes mere decoding.

Prime Lattice quantifies the same intermediate problem by Beurling--Malliavin density and destination topology. Prime-log modulations already form a form core, while finite prime-axis families have only a finite completeness budget and finite coefficient constraints survive only as genuine continuous dual traces. PL-354 closes a further literal escape: finite exact positive-real Dirichlet evaluation stacks remain dense after completion, whereas an infinite exact stack annihilates the finite Dirichlet-polynomial algebraic family. The naive exact stack therefore jumps from invisible to zero rather than producing a nonzero proper intermediate family.

Visual Exploration supplies a continuous analogue with an analytic warning. Wang's symmetric source smoothing is an invertible log-scale Green resolvent, so exact source recoverability survives smoothing. VIS-293 shows that the weighted source transform satisfies `D(s)=(log zeta)''(s)+A(s)` with `A` holomorphic on `Re(s)>1/2`; after the elementary pole is removed, first-half-plane holomorphy is exactly RH. Recovering the source and analytically continuing it across the critical region are therefore very different achievements: the latter can simply restate the target divisor.

## Where does the obstruction move after every fixed-complexity regime closes?

Arithmetic Fidelity now has a sharper diagonal-complexity frontier. AF-411--AF-412 prove eventual positivity in both tails for every fixed confluent order. AF-413 identifies the first left-tail balance `m e^t=Theta(1)`, and AF-414 proves that every fixed excess partition sector survives there with alternating Plancherel weight. AF-415 then closes the complete exceptional-`1` double-rank-two partition ensemble: its normalized sum converges locally uniformly to the positive Cauchy exponential `exp(-c^2/(4pi^2))`. Thus fixed-degree negative corrections were genuine nonuniform competitors but not a sign obstruction after exact same-scale resummation. The remaining diagonal risk lies in exponent families omitting `1`, sectors with fewer singular-block entries, and their interaction with the positive resummed sector.

Nyman--Beurling now supplies a complementary example in which a fixed-parameter loss was not intrinsic. NB-208 forces a phase-pinned prime-harmonic plateau, NB-210 uses the prime-supported Halasz inequality to convert it into an order-one return gap throughout every fixed `X_a^(3/2-delta)` range, and NB-211 reopens the proof at the full Vinogradov--Korobov contour width. The order-one gap then survives uniformly through `log|t|<=kappa X_a^(3/2)/(log X_a)^2`, strictly beyond every fixed subcritical exponent. The remaining gap to Ford's `X_a^(3/2)/sqrt(log X_a)` corridor is a factor `(log X_a)^(3/2)` and is now localized to the true prime-correlation off-diagonal when the zero-free contour supplies only constant exponential saving. The next useful structure is the arithmetic progression and phase pinning of the large values, not another fixed-epsilon theorem or prime-density correction.

Weil Inertia gives two independent negative controls on nominal hierarchy breadth. Ordinary Gowers norms, sparse cube deletion, deterministic scalar reweighting and linear mixing across orders remain inside a Walsh-character quotient that matched-energy Rademacher sources can reproduce. WI-343--WI-344 add a physical carrier obstruction: any finite height family living on source log-aperture `Delta` is approximated by `d+1` linear dimensions with error `(H Delta/4)^(d+1)/(d+1)!`, and bounded height-independent linear preprocessing cannot create extensive normalized diversity without paying the same residual in transmitted amplitude. On the natural Gallagher window `H Delta=1`, even power-growing height portfolios remain effectively bounded-dimensional.

Across these lines, “take the complexity parameter or sample count larger” is not a research mechanism. The load-bearing questions are whether an estimate is uniform in the moving parameter, whether a published loss survives inspection of its proof, whether the whole same-scale ensemble has been summed, whether the theorem is matched to the actual source geometry, and whether the destination topology converts nominal breadth into genuinely new retained information. A low-order negative term can disappear after exact resummation, a fixed epsilon can disappear after restoring the natural contour, while a nominally large linear bank can expose residual directions only by sacrificing transmitted scale.

## Which physical cost survives exact-real coding, remote repair and conductor minimization?

[MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md) remains the broad synthesis, but the new evidence makes the relevant physical currencies more specific.

Möbius Cancellation now distinguishes finite-resolution shell stability from minimum primitive source conductor and from the amplitude carried at that boundary. Exact-real subset-sum access can encode an entire endpoint sign vector even near balance; `l1` shell quotienting and `l2` average stability remove different parts of that leakage. MC-371--MC-375 force a quartic-efficient endpoint toward quadratic primitive conductors on almost the whole Fourier cube while its source mass is Hamming-central and incidence-delocalized. MC-376 then proves that a positive fraction of those modes has endpoint bias at least inverse-root-rank. The quadratic boundary is therefore both expensive and macroscopically populated; a useful source theorem must address a moving `R^(-1/2)` bias scale, not only one exceptional mode or a fixed-positive threshold.

Robin Extremal now has a quantitatively sharper two-sided continuum source-cost theorem. Finite vertical panels remain aliasable, and ordinary-prime constructions can hide selector-scale data through `T=c log Q` for `c<0.0289079...` while preserving every fixed KV envelope. RE-209 converts continuum hiding with a fixed log-support gap into an exponentially large signed prime-prefix requirement. RE-210 keeps the exact sinc sidelobe geometry and raises the coercive exponent to `gamma_*=0.5979019108...`, lowering the universal ceiling for the current `H=log 8` architecture to `c<0.4021549...`. A substantial gap remains, but it is now explicitly between constructive signed-prefix control and compact-support spectral-gap coercivity rather than between injectivity and noninjectivity.

Farey Discrepancy contributes the complementary observation-side lesson: the square-root band threshold is an exact coverage threshold with a stable raw inverse, while critical prime-extension energy can still hide in the nullspace below it. FD-195 shows that even when the raw inverse is contractive, compressing to the Franel quadratic norm can erase that contraction. Source relation cost matters only when the observation exposes the relation **and the destination norm preserves the useful inverse scale**.

## Can positivity preserve the critical amplitude before scalar closure squares or imports it?

Weil Positivity gives a detailed negative classification of the direct level-one Hecke/Eisenstein route. Nonconstant Fourier modes retain genuine divisibility projections, but positive cusp-boundary squares scalarize them at `1/p` rather than the critical `p^(-1/2)` amplitude. Smooth height cutoffs add only `p^2`-orbit coherence and retain the same scalar density. An all-prime strong completion exists only for `l2` coefficients, excluding the critical amplitudes.

Passing to the globally correlated Eisenstein line does not fix the issue: finite Hecke Grams are rank-one test pullbacks, while the unrenormalized critical prime multiplier diverges. The canonical Euler completion is exactly `zeta(s+ir)zeta(s-ir)`; at the center it gives a modulus square containing finite and archimedean data but no zero-selective orientation. Its logarithmic derivative recovers the desired Mangoldt half-density only by importing the zeta divisor as poles and abandoning inherited positivity.

This sharpens a global design rule also visible in Möbius Cancellation, Prime Lattice and Visual Exploration: **the critical carrier must survive before the final quotient, positive square or analytic completion**. If the source-specific amplitude appears only after taking a square root of a scalarized projection, differentiating a completed scalar `L`-function, reconstructing the full classical form, or demanding analytic continuation whose poles are already the target zeros, the mechanism has not generated an independent sign theorem.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the admissible source class, observation family and nullspace, destination quotient and norm, physical source-coordinate map, acquisition budget, requested precision, normalization gauge, analytic domain/class, moving-complexity regime, source support domain and granularity, sign/covariance structure and matched control. Then ask two opposite-direction questions in the same currency: **what does the destination force, and what can the physical source mechanism actually supply?**

The newest results sharpen recurring failure modes. A complete family may merely reconstruct the classical object; an exact constraint stack may jump from topologically invisible to algebraically zero; a useful inverse contraction may disappear after changing destination norm; a fixed-degree sign competition may resum to a positive full sector; a published fixed-parameter loss may disappear when the underlying proof is diagonalized correctly; nominally many linear probes may collapse to bounded effective dimension unless one pays in transmitted amplitude; exact-real or remote-source freedom may move cost into resolution, conductor or signed-prefix discrepancy; a near-quadratic source boundary may still carry a macroscopic inverse-root-rank population; and analytic continuation of a source transform may simply reinsert the target divisor. Progress requires a source-forced intermediate structure that survives these audits and has an independent destination theorem.
