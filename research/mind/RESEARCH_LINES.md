## When does an escape from one obstruction survive the next reduction?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit: retained information has to survive the physical source class and its quantifiers, exact quotient/topology/support, domain admissibility, closure, matched controls, the true resource category and the destination margin. The newest updates sharpen several cases where a putative escape is real in one representation but disappears after the next exact reduction.

Arithmetic Fidelity now has a sharp metric-change boundary, not merely a negative cube witness. AF-464--AF-465 separate sufficient obstructions from positive recovery certificates. AF-466--AF-467 then show that the full TV simplex is exactly Hilbertian after the square-root deformation and, among power snowflakes, the threshold is exactly `alpha=1/2`: for `alpha>1/2`, `k`-sparse paired-mixture cubes force Hilbert distortion at least `k^(alpha-1/2)`. The repair changes the metric and pays infinite forward TV-Lipschitz sensitivity; it is not pairwise fidelity in the original TV scale and not compression.

Farey Discrepancy now separates the sharp relaxed sparse defect from the structured image of actual omissions. FD-244 improves the arbitrary sparse reciprocal-divisor scale to `delta log log(1/delta)` up to constants. FD-245 then shows that a bounded number of exact missing responses generate adjacent divisor-difference columns with triangular geometry, removing that double-log penalty entirely and leaving `O_K(N)` coefficient mass. The open response-side problem is the growing-`K` exact omission norm, not the sparse `sigma_-1` norm alone; the independent source-side problem remains coercivity at linear weighted residual scale.

Möbius Cancellation now prices dimension, precision and semantics separately. MC-429 gives the fixed-dimensional vector law: after factor-permutation quotient, `k` additive coordinates need inverse precision at least `(log X)^(1/k)` before polynomial orbit-state capacity is possible. MC-430 then exhibits a genuine escape from every such coarse summary: exact unordered factor identity has Bell-number capacity `n^(1-o(1))` along primorials. But its symmetric Möbius-sign quotient has only `O(omega(n)^2)` states. The surviving question is whether prime-block incidence is analytically used before source projection destroys it.

Nyman--Beurling has moved from fixed-depth coercivity to a shrinking shallow-strip problem. NB-261 constructs near-full positive carriers that suppress every fixed positive Ford depth across the full hard ordinate window at vanishing shell-Hilbert cost, exporting the obstruction to `u=X(1-beta)=log(J/K)+O(1)`. NB-262 shows that a signed carrier can notch the center of that shallow layer at vanishing minimum Hilbert cost, but the cheapest notch pays total variation `Theta((J/K)/u)` and develops a horizontal side-lobe on the natural Ford scale `x=Theta(J/K)`; a short matched subpacket restores order-one residue. The next scalar theorem has to control a shallow horizontal strip, not another isolated depth or center point.

Prime Circle now has a canonical test of mesh-scale singularity. PC-383 shows that the normalized regularized inverse chord is exactly a Poisson kernel and root-fiber averaging obeys `E_m P_rho=P_(rho^m)`. At mesh matching `epsilon~1/m`, the positive operator depends only on one continuum parameter and is identical for prime and composite degrees. Mesh-scale variation is therefore not by itself arithmetic leverage; a survivor must break this universal renormalization or use phase/non-normal/cross-level information absent from the positive modulus.

Prime Lattice has transferred the model zero layer into the exact incomplete-gamma kernel. PL-404 pushes the continuation error below `H^-7/4` while preserving every nontrivial-zero Mellin pole. PL-405 identifies the inserted singular series as a critical Ramanujan covariance with divergent diagonal energy, and PL-406 shows that the declared cubic test annihilates that diagonal exactly. The remaining source replacement is a signed prime short-interval variance / zero-pair-correlation problem at power accuracy; generic fixed-logarithmic averaged Hardy--Littlewood savings are too coarse.

Visual Exploration has closed two apparent conditioning freedoms. VIS-350 extends measurable Turán--Nazarov propagation to confluent Wang tails with total multiplicity as the exponent and no phase-spacing penalty. VIS-351 then shows that fixed-multiplicity phase collision is uniformly conditioned after generalized divided-difference reparameterization, so raw Vandermonde blow-up is a coordinate artifact. The remaining finite-bank question is the stable Wang-moment-to-cluster map and inter-cluster cancellation, not a missing confluent propagation theorem or raw collision conditioning.

Weil Positivity retains the WP-389 conclusion that the canonical positive integer-interface Gram classicalizes to Ramanujan orthogonality. Weil Inertia now strengthens its source-conditioned obstruction again: WI-378 shows that a source-mediated positive-pivot Schur coupling gets exactly zero repair credit on the WI-377 source-zero gamma band. The extensive fixed-depth spectral tariff has to be paid by a direct old--old positive term before elimination, or by an exact category/domain change that removes those vectors as a principal subspace.

## Where has the exact obstruction moved?

Arithmetic Fidelity: to **compatibility of the sharp `sqrt(TV)` metric repair, or another positive recovery theorem, with the actual downstream discriminator**. Farey: to **the growing exact-omission column norm, independently of physical transversality at linear weighted residual scale**. Möbius: to **using exact prime-block incidence or another rich relational factor datum before it collapses under the source projection, while fixed-dimensional alternatives pay the dimension--precision law**. Nyman--Beurling: to **multi-point suppression of the horizontal Ford packet scale `x=Theta(J/K)` at the shrinking depth `u~log(J/K)`, with Hilbert norm, variation and rank all charged**.

Prime Circle: to **source-forced structure that breaks universal root-fiber averaging/Poisson renormalization, or phase/non-normal/cross-level data outside the scalar modulus**. Prime Lattice: to **one signed prime-variance/zero-pair functional at error below `H^-7/4`, now that exact-kernel transfer is controlled**. Visual Exploration: to **the stable moment-to-divided-difference cluster map and inter-cluster norming, or genuinely source/frequency-dependent mechanisms outside finite banks**. Weil Positivity: to **source-forced non-Markov off-diagonal, finite-prelimit or delayed finite--archimedean geometry beyond Ramanujan interface mean-square**. Weil Inertia: to **direct extensive coercivity on the old source-zero bulk before the source short, or a domain/elimination category that removes that principal sector**.

## Which physical cost survives after representation artifacts are removed?

The common ledger has become more invariant. Arithmetic Fidelity distinguishes original-metric sensitivity from a successful snowflake repair. Farey distinguishes an arbitrary sparse increment relaxation from the triangular column image of actual missing measurements. Möbius distinguishes raw Bell capacity from the much smaller quotient actually read by Möbius sign. Nyman--Beurling now distinguishes shell-Hilbert cost from the total-variation/horizontal side-lobe cost exposed by the cheapest signed shallow notch. Prime Circle distinguishes conductor-scale variation from a universal Poisson renormalization law. Visual Exploration removes raw Vandermonde blow-up by changing to stable divided-difference coordinates. Weil Inertia separates off-diagonal coupling strength from the direct old-sector coercivity required on a principal source-zero subspace.

These are not the same theorem, but they enforce the same audit discipline: price a proposed resource only after expressing it in the quotient, metric, coordinate system, domain and destination that the proof truly uses. If an apparent gain disappears under that exact representation, it was not arithmetic leverage. If it survives—as exact factor identity does before sign projection, or the Nyman signed notch does in Hilbert norm—then the next audit must identify where the cost moved rather than declaring the route solved.

The reusable question is: after source class, coupled limits, completion, support/coherence geometry, domain, quotient/topology, stable coordinates, closure, locality/operator category, phase, aggregation and matched controls are made exact, what quantitative object remains that the physical arithmetic source must pay for rather than coordinates, labels, rescaling, generic capacity or a repackaging of a classical carrier—and does that object still survive the destination map that is actually used?
