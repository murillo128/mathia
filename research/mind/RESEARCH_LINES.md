## Which apparent resource survives the exact destination map?

The common audit remains: identify the admissible source image, quotient universal scaffolding and nuisance directions, follow the exact information-loss map into the destination, and only then price the surviving resource. Arithmetic Fidelity now makes the transport side of that audit sharper. A full curvature profile is exactly invertible modulo global dilation, uniformly vanishing tail log-displacement is globally invisible, and AF-519 shows that fixed proportional transport is mesoscopically visible through zeroth-versus-second log-moment participation. AF-520 extends that two-moment compression to arbitrary outward source-surviving transport. AF-521 then identifies its exact boundary: for a general positive Dirichlet source the one-point curvature ratio depends exactly on normalized zeroth, first and second logarithmic moments, and the first moment becomes leading when it reaches the `sqrt(log(1/t))` scale. The destination carrier expands from two moments to three rather than back to the microscopic transport geometry.

Farey Discrepancy supplies the complementary window-selection lesson. FD-287--FD-289 reduce internal right-collar cancellation to rare near-one `zeta'/zeta` spikes and transfer that sparsity uniformly to every polynomial Perron window. FD-290 shows that phase-good heights and zero-safe cluster-complete heights coexist. FD-291 then uses a stronger structural fact: the **complete hard remainder is constant on each zero gap**. Remainder-goodness can therefore be imposed gapwise and zero-safety obtained by trimming each good gap internally, so the final intersection only needs safe good-gap occupation exceeding the `O(H/L^2)` phase-exceptional budget rather than a fixed positive fraction of the height window.

Together AF-521 and FD-291 reinforce a reusable distinction between **raw participation and destination-compatible participation**. Arithmetic Fidelity reduces a large transport family to the few logarithmic moments actually entering scalar curvature, with the first moment promoted only at a precise scale. Farey Discrepancy converts continuous admissibility into weighted occupation of exact invariance plateaus of the hard decomposition. A density, count, microscopic decomposition or global average is evidential only after it has been transferred through the exact moment/window functional used by the destination.

Nyman--Beurling now separates three source notions that had been easy to conflate. NB-304 proves quadratic power sharpness for universal support localization; NB-305 shows its explicit high-index extremizer can nevertheless have vanishing dilation self-overlap; NB-306 then proves that the **operator norm** is carried by a different low-index structure. For integer dilation, `A_m U_floor(N/m) subseteq U_N`, so projected dilation has singular value `1` on that whole core and `beta_(N+1)(m)=1` whenever `m<=N/2`. Combined with NB-303's superquadratic decay, the unresolved source transition is bracketed between linear and quadratic scales. The surviving resource is the evolving singular spectrum, not support mass and not one-vector Rayleigh overlap. Prime Lattice exhibits the opposite pattern, where representation really does improve: its hidden complex covariance can be reconstructed on the actual dyadic source from only `t=0` and a vanishing quarter-turn `t=-pi/(2 log X)`, and Abel transport removes the apparent coefficient-`l^1` conditioning cost. Yet that tiny physical shift is order one on the microscopic zero scale, so the missing theorem remains genuinely arithmetic rather than representational.

Möbius Cancellation and Prime Circle show two ways an algebraic improvement can still preserve the decisive obstruction. MC-482--MC-483 separate positive bracket quality from signed Möbius transfer, but MC-484 proves that the selector loss is not intrinsic to the hard mask: exact Buchstab first exit exposes the first-exit prime before absolute values. Visual Exploration and Weil Positivity sharpen the nuisance/canonicity side of the same audit. VIS-403 shows that unrestricted finite-latent conditional independence is vacuous; VIS-404 goes further and fixes the latent law itself. A single pre-fixed uniform scalar latent plus an unrestricted monotone quantile decoder can still realize every Borel residual law, and qualitative smoothness does not help when its constants remain target-dependent. AF-521, MC-484, NB-306 and WI-419 sharpen one common cross-line rule without asserting a common quantitative theorem: **microscopic complexity before the destination map is not automatically a destination resource, but removing one bottleneck can reveal a smaller exact survivor rather than no survivor at all**. ## What counts as a genuine representation escape?

A representation escape must change destination-visible information without hiding an equal cost in a complementary term, nuisance calibration, source budget, topology or target-alignment requirement. Prime Lattice is a clean positive example: dyadic localization reduces an abstract Hilbert-transform reconstruction to one shrinking physical quarter-turn, and Abel transport shows that the representation step itself can be conditioned by ordinary source norms; the remaining obstruction is genuinely the arithmetic value of the oriented sample. Arithmetic Fidelity gives the converse warning: exact injectivity and a canonical inverse can coexist with robustly invisible tail transport, while AF-521 shows that leaving the outward regime does not restore microscopic provenance—it promotes a specific first logarithmic moment into the scalar destination at a sharp scale.

Farey Discrepancy and Möbius Cancellation show how preserving exact structure can move rather than erase the bottleneck. FD-291 exploits exact gapwise constancy of the full hard complement to reduce a three-way height intersection to a vanishing `H/L^2` occupation condition, but it still needs a theorem that produces good gaps at the physical remainder budget. MC-484 removes the positive-bracket selector exactly through Buchstab first exit, but the remaining family is still dimension two and only useful if the first-exit prime stays coupled through the analytic estimate. In both cases the representation improvement is real because it removes a proved loss; neither is complete until the new survivor is priced. Prime Circle supplies the corresponding negative test: even arbitrary two-label conductor dependence is not a representation escape if it factorizes prime by prime and matched controls reproduce the same incidence-difference Euler product.

Nyman--Beurling, Visual Exploration, Weil Inertia and Weil Positivity sharpen what must be priced after a candidate escape appears. NB-306 shows that abandoning a failed high-index witness does not imply source contraction: an exact low-index isometric core pins the top singular value through linear scale, so the relevant object is the whole singular spectrum and its destination occupation. VIS-404 requires decoder/interaction capacity, not merely the latent prior, to be externally constrained. WI-419 shows that a one-target tariff becomes summable only after a quantified separation/reuse theorem. WP-415 shows that changing from full fibres to non-full correspondences still leaves ordinary positive-functional Gram sign universal unless source-forced support/incidence changes the coefficient problem itself. The reusable test is therefore to specify the admissible source family, exact quotient/readout, smallest datum on which the destination verdict changes, robustness topology, nuisance and source-budget class, and noncircular source-side theorem; then propagate the resulting margin through conditioning, complementary terms, signed correlation, target occupation, admissible-window selection, height/depth coverage, separation/reuse and normalization. Formal reconstructibility, local sensitivity, algebraic richness, low-dimensional compression, noncommutativity, non-fullness, cycle holonomy and exact positivity are intermediate facts until that full chain survives.
