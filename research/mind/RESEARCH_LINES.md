# Global mathematical questions

This file records only cross-line questions that currently change how several Mathia research lines should be interpreted. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than merely transport, reconstruct, compress or refactor the source?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) and [MI-028](intuition/MI-028-complete-measurement-families-can-close-an-escape-by-reconstructing-the-classical-object.md) give the common interface audit.

Prime Circle supplies the reconstruction-versus-quotient boundary. Complete shell information can recover the underlying source, but PC-351 shows that the canonical triangular decoder has trivial ordinary spectrum and admits a matched reciprocal sign-flip control that is unitarily conjugate to the arithmetic decoder. Faithful acquisition is therefore not selective if the destination quotient erases the oriented source coordinate.

Prime Lattice sharpens the topology-and-summation gate. PL-360--PL-362 show that diagonal reweighting, non-diagonal completion with canonical coefficient cutoffs, and coefficientwise-positive smoothing all reconstruct the original convergence wall. PL-363 then supplies the first signed control: a fixed finite signed lattice-shift filter with zero period mean crosses to ordinary convergence on `Re s>0`, but the result is just a periodic Dirichlet series `P(s)zeta(s)` with classical Hurwitz continuation. Escaping the positive cone is a genuine mechanism change, yet it still does not create critical-line selectivity.

Farey Discrepancy now separates acquisition, covariance-preserving projection and compression. FD-201 proves that fixed-mode cross-horizon differences really contain new Möbius information, namely multiplicative Mertens increments, but their immediate positive half-Sobolev compression still forces a random-multiplicative shell tariff of `H^(3/2+o(1))`. FD-202 then takes an exact adjacent-prime signed derivative before squaring. The overlapping long Mertens intervals collapse to disjoint endpoint slabs and the shell-normalized path-Laplacian energy has random-multiplicative mean `~(6/pi^2)(1+r)H`. A representation can therefore gain source information and lose it under diagonal compression, while an exact non-diagonal projection can recover the critical scale by retaining covariance. The remaining obstruction is only the one-dimensional constant-mode kernel, which must be anchored without restoring the shellwide tariff.

Nyman--Beurling gives the analogous proof-interface version. NB-217 proves that absolute Mellin reconstruction after fixed primitivization pays reciprocal log-support mass. NB-218 then shows that the exact signed primitive-kernel convolution has no intrinsic `X^j` multiplier loss: the primitive divisor and reconstruction multiplier cancel coefficientwise, and a genuine shell kernel annihilates every finite polynomial jet of the primitive. The tariff is introduced when the coupled oscillatory pairing is decoupled by absolute values. Thus a representation can already contain the desired cancellation while the proof norm used to certify it destroys the mechanism.

Weil Inertia shows that formal output size and formal complexity budgets can both be illusory. WI-351 extends the finite charge-support obstruction to controlled infinite tails, while WI-352 proves that the first charge-Sobolev moment `B_1` is already bounded by the same Lipschitz constant `L`. Infinite support may be invisible at destination resolution, and an apparently separate complexity currency may simply be a repackaging of a cost already paid elsewhere.

Weil Positivity adds a globalization failure. WP-366 starts from a genuinely non-product fixed-height Whittaker feature in which finite and archimedean variables interact through `nY`; positive scalar dilation-covariant globalization forces power-Haar averaging and Mellin-separates that interaction back into the failed Rankin--Selberg product. Local coupling counts only if it survives the canonical operation that removes its auxiliary scale.

The shared question is therefore: **what source-specific coordinate is newly acquired, what exact closure/summation/globalization or covariance-preserving projection the destination must apply, what proof interface is used to estimate the survivor, what remains visible in the declared topology and resolution, which apparent resource is genuinely independent, and which matched control fails to reproduce it?** Richer data, nonlinear depth, non-diagonal topology, sign-indefinite filtering, infinite formal support or local non-product structure deserve credit only after all of those gates survive.

## Where does the obstruction move after a nominal critical boundary is resolved?

Arithmetic Fidelity gives the clearest stratified double-scaling example. AF-417--AF-420 refine derivative depth from the coarse ratio `s_n/n` to the joint critical coordinate

`b_n(a)=a n^(s_n/n-2)`.

AF-421 then resolves the signed finite-window regime into full-column saddle packets with transition fibers at `b=m^2`. AF-422 shows that equality of the two adjacent exponential rates is still too coarse: on a square fiber the next coordinate

`Xi_(n,m)=log n+n log(b_n/m^2)-d_n log m`

controls the rectangle-amplitude ratio. Even parity cannot cancel; odd parity can cancel at leading rectangular order only when `Xi_(n,m)->-log K_m`.

AF-423 moves the frontier one scale deeper again. If `G_(n,r)` denotes the complete residual Schur--Cauchy packet around the `r`-column rectangle, then

`log(G_(n,m)/G_(n,m-1))=2ae^2/n+o(1/n)`.

Thus rectangle balance `F_(n,m)->1` still loses a deterministic packet asymmetry. First complete-packet cancellation requires

`n log F_(n,m)->-2ae^2`.

For `m>=2`, any surviving order-`1/n` mismatch remains exponentially divergent after multiplication by the tied saddle. The live finite-window question is now the next packet-ratio/F-ratio asymptotic under this stronger tuning and its comparison with lower saddle packets. The lesson is not merely that critical windows exist: each tuned collision can expose a finer retained coordinate that was invisible at the previous scale.

Visual Exploration gives the proof-boundary analogue. Exact gamma recentering removes the lower moving-source wall; VIS-299--VIS-301 then move the apparent upper boundary from `H/log^3 T` localization to a generic `H/log^2 T` Montgomery--Vaughan coefficient estimate. VIS-302 evaluates the exact Wang coefficient weight and saves another logarithm:

`sum_n n a_n^2=(4/3)x log x+(8/9)x+o(x)`.

The pointwise asymptotic therefore persists whenever `x log x=o(H)`. VIS-303 then decomposes the newly exposed actual remainder using the exact prime-power support: powers of two have vanishing energy and bounded weighted mass, so every odd-additive-shift contribution is `o(H)` throughout `x log x=O(H)`. The unresolved upper layer is no longer the unrestricted off-diagonal integral near `x log x asymp H`, but specifically its odd-prime-power/even-shift sector.

Nyman--Beurling shows a different kind of obstruction motion. NB-217 appears to close fixed-order primitivization by an `L1` reconstruction tariff. NB-218 relocates that obstruction: exact reconstruction itself is tariff-free inside the signed primitive-kernel convolution, while the loss appears only when the proof decouples the two factors. The frontier therefore moves from redesigning the shell or improving pointwise primitive size to estimating the native carrier-frequency Fourier coefficient while keeping contour transport, tails, branches and poles coupled.

Robin Extremal now isolates its remaining leading-order gap as a pure spectral approximation constant. RE-216 lowers the constructive Wiener exponent to at most `6.081` without changing the arithmetic carrier. RE-217 proves the universal lower floor `1`, so the separated prediction constant satisfies

`1 <= C_sep <= 6.081`.

The existing prime realization already accepts every strict coefficient exponent below `1/2`; if `C_sep=1`, it reaches every fixed `c<1/(2H)`, exactly the leading bounded-prime capacity threshold. The live obstruction is therefore no longer an undifferentiated “predictor/source mismatch” but the numerical value of `C_sep` under support geometry compatible with the arithmetic realization.

Across these lines, the right response to a resolved threshold is to identify the **new load-bearing packet, error term, support sector, proof inequality or resource**, then ask whether that new object has its own collision locus, coefficient-specific cancellation, source-support sparsity or dependency relation. Critical ratios, theorem envelopes and representation constants are provisional until the full complexity family and exact destination scale expose what actually dominates next.

## Which physical cost survives source specialization, centering, reconstruction and analytic transport?

[MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md) and [MI-025](intuition/MI-025-combinatorial-breadth-must-be-priced-through-the-source-coordinate-map.md) remain the main source-cost syntheses, but the recent results further separate currencies that had looked interchangeable.

Möbius Cancellation distinguishes combinatorial source rank, differencing depth, family breadth and global conductor cost. MC-381 raises the exact-endpoint common-source obstruction to linear-in-rank scale until the analytic ceiling intervenes. MC-382 shows that the current q-van der Corput architecture has an intrinsic `a=O(log log y)` source-exponent horizon because required depth grows with `a` while terminal saving decays exponentially. MC-383 then stress-tests a different collective theorem shape: even an exponential family `A<=exp(CR)` cannot buy a rank-linear source exponent when the analytic estimate pays a fixed positive power of one global modulus. Its gain is only additive `O(R)` in `log P`; reaching `Omega(R log y)` needs factor-local/rank-sensitive conductor dependence or a different invariant.

Nyman--Beurling now splits reconstruction cost into an exact algebraic layer and an analytic decoupling layer. NB-217 says that once kernel and primitive are priced separately, the reciprocal support mass `J_j(E)=int_E u^(-j)du` forces the familiar positive-power tariff on relative logarithmic shells. NB-218 says the exact signed convolution does not pay that factor at all and is blind to finite polynomial jets of the primitive. The next physical cost is therefore whatever cancellation-sensitive norm or local Fourier estimate is strong enough to control the coupled carrier-frequency coefficient through the zero-free contour, not the primitive's pointwise magnitude by itself.

Farey Discrepancy similarly distinguishes acquisition cost, diagonal compression cost and kernel cost. FD-201 shows that source-sensitive cross-horizon rows can still be overcoerced when independently squared. FD-202 proves that the diagonal `H^(3/2+o(1))` random tariff is not intrinsic to positivity or to the observation family: the exact path incidence projection removes repeated bulk and lowers the random benchmark to `H`, leaving only a one-dimensional constant mode. The next price to audit is therefore the cost of anchoring that kernel, not the cost of all prime-shell rows independently.

Robin Extremal separates three currencies exactly enough to expose a sharp sandwich. RE-217 forces separated continuum prediction to spend at least unit Wiener exponent; RE-216 supplies a current construction at exponent at most `6.081`; RE-208's ordinary-prime realization accepts every strict coefficient exponent below `1/2`; and RE-214--RE-215 place the leading carrier-capacity ceiling at `Hc=1/2`. The remaining constant-factor uncertainty belongs to prediction theory rather than prime placement, while KV discrepancy remains a finer source-fidelity input at and below the endpoint.

Visual Exploration shows two source-specialization savings: VIS-302 removes a logarithm from a generic coefficient bound, and VIS-303 removes an entire odd-shift support sector from the actual boundary remainder. Weil Inertia separates hard phase-orbit cardinality from soft high-charge tail mass, and WI-352 shows that `B_1` is not independent of the Lipschitz sensitivity budget at all. These costs cannot be merged into a generic notion of “complexity”: each lives at a different stage of the source-to-destination map, and some are algebraically dependent or disappear after the correct source projection.

The shared audit is to price the route **after** exact source specialization and nuisance removal, then separately record acquisition rank, algebraic redundancy, predictor or transport loss, support/reconstruction cost, proof-decoupling loss, family breadth, conductor geometry, carrier capacity, covariance-preserving projection cost, residual kernel dimension, destination-resolution complexity and finer arithmetic fidelity. Improving one currency is progress only if another stage does not repay the gain, and a new currency counts only after proving it is not already controlled by an existing one.

## Can sign-indefinite structure preserve critical information before closure, norm compression or globalization scalarizes it?

Weil Positivity remains the strongest direct positive-closure classification. Positive finite Hecke structure retains arithmetic information before closure, but boundary squares scalarize the critical half-density. Rankin--Selberg positivity dies at critical specialization; projectivization does not repair it; and adding an independent positive Bessel/Whittaker Hilbert sector still leaves unbounded critical overlap and negative Fubini--Study geometry near critical zeros.

WP-366 rules out a more local escape under canonical scalar globalization. The fixed-height coordinates `lambda_r(n)K_(ir)(2 pi nY)` genuinely couple the finite and archimedean variables, but dilation-covariant positive averaging in `Y` is forced to be power-Haar and Mellin-separates the interaction back to Rankin--Selberg geometry. The critical positive diagonal then already diverges.

Prime Lattice gives both sides of a coefficient-space control. PL-362 says coefficientwise-positive recovery cannot cross the real convergence wall. PL-363 shows that finite sign-indefinite cancellation can cross it, but the resulting periodic Dirichlet series is only `P(s)zeta(s)` and gains classical continuation without a new critical selector. The missing ingredient is therefore not merely “some negative sign”: it is a **source-forced nonpositive relation that remains selective after continuation or globalization**.

Farey Discrepancy supplies a complementary finite-dimensional example of where the sign has to occur. FD-202 uses a signed adjacent-prime projection before applying a positive quadratic norm. That projection cancels the common bulk of neighboring source intervals and preserves a critical-scale covariance structure; squaring first would lose it and restore the `H^(3/2)` shell tariff.

Nyman--Beurling supplies the analytic analogue. NB-218 keeps the primitive and reconstruction kernel coupled until their oscillations interact; only after replacing that signed convolution by an absolute product does the NB-217 tariff appear. The line has not yet proved a saving on the Vinogradov--Korobov contour, but it identifies exactly which proof step destroys the candidate cancellation.

These examples do not transfer their estimates to the Weil or lattice settings, but they sharpen the interface question: a useful nonpositive relation can live **before** an otherwise positive destination functional or norm, provided it is source-native, survives the required transport, and any residual kernel or tail is explicitly controlled. In Weil Positivity this may require a source-fixed scale with an independent theorem, intrinsically non-Haar/operator-valued scale mixing, a Maaß--Selberg-type remainder, or a cohomological/intersection sector whose orientation is established before scalar separation. In Prime Lattice it requires more than a chosen finite pole-cancelling filter: an infinite or scale-dependent source-forced summation law, nonlocal mixing, a genuine renormalized remainder or another global arithmetic correlation with an independently zero-sensitive destination theorem.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the admissible source class and topology, observation family and nullspace, destination quotient and norm, physical source-coordinate map, acquisition budget, requested precision, normalization gauge, analytic domain, approximation/summation protocol, moving-complexity coordinates, support/multiplicity constraints, transport or reconstruction loss, proof-decoupling loss, sign/covariance structure, dependency among apparent resource budgets and matched control. Then ask in sequence: **what is genuinely new at acquisition; what exact closure/summation/globalization or pre-norm projection is unavoidable; what survives it; which proof inequality is the first place signed structure is discarded; which resources are genuinely independent; what inverse resource must the source pay; and does a matched non-arithmetic control reproduce the same survivor?**

The current synthesis narrows rather than proliferates directions. Complete fidelity is not enough if invariantization erases it. New measurements are not enough if destination compression overcoerces them. Non-diagonal topology is not enough if canonical cutoffs rebuild the convergence wall; leaving the positive cone is not enough if a finite filter merely manufactures classical periodic continuation. Infinite formal support is not enough if the destination sees only a bounded effective tail, and a charge moment is not a new cost if Lipschitz regularity already pays it. Local finite--archimedean interaction is not enough if canonical scale averaging factorizes it. Exponential family breadth is not enough if a fixed global-modulus power absorbs the gain, and a theorem-level big-O or absolute reconstruction bound is not a structural boundary until it survives specialization to the exact coefficients, exact source support and exact signed coupling.

Progress therefore requires an intermediate structure that is nontrivial in the declared topology, source-specific after matched controls, survives the exact destination operation and proof interface, remains quantitatively visible at the moving resolution, uses genuinely independent resources, and couples to a destination theorem without reconstructing the original RH criterion in disguise.
