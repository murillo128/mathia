# Global mathematical questions

## When does a quantitative budget become a genuine source discriminator?

Several lines now show that the decisive budget is rarely the first coarse norm one can write down. The useful quantity must be fixed before target inspection, live in the geometry actually consumed by the destination, and be occupied by the physical source rather than by a matched control.

Visual Exploration gives a sharp finite-window example. VIS-416 removes the generic reciprocal-bandwidth scale from Wang balance radii. VIS-417 then shows that matching bandwidth, mode count, coefficient magnitudes, long-time energy and global supremum still leaves an uncontrolled beat scale: two nearby frequencies can create arbitrarily long quiet windows. VIS-418 goes further. Even with the complete two-mode frequency set and beat gap fixed, global translation can place the same quiet window onto or away from any distinguished height. The surviving source test is therefore not another coarse spectral null but a phase-anchored self-control: compare pre-specified arithmetic heights with the translation orbit of the same realized shell, or use a genuinely translation-invariant statistic.

Weil Inertia supplies the source-category analogue. WI-417--WI-423 show that a frozen density or function-space budget is meaningful only when it belongs to an independently generated source and controls the Poisson-capacity geometry of the actual defect; assigning regularity directly to the canonical atomic zero measure would already build the target conclusion into the hypothesis. WI-424--WI-428 then show on the fixed-safe-strip branch that increasingly flexible positive, signed and non-diagonal portfolios remain cubically blind to shallow boundary defects unless a source-forced singular budget or category change is introduced.

Farey Discrepancy turns a destination capacity target into an aligned source-energy tariff. FD-298--FD-299 reduce the low-height hard complement to the block-Mertens field and remove sign polarization as the near-capacity gate under the stated RH input. FD-300 proves that near-capacity `l^1` mass forces some dyadic shell of block increments to have mean square at least `N x^(2c-o(1))`, and hence forces positive aggregate same-block masked Möbius correlation. A uniform fixed-power saving below that aligned variance threshold would kill the route. The condition is only necessary: large block energy need not survive divisor replication into the required `l^1` field.

Prime Lattice gives a complementary lesson about using the exact destination geometry rather than a convenient ambient norm. PL-437 compresses the unresolved mod-5 orientation to two coordinates `(s,f)` and gives the Euclidean Weyl disk as a sufficient condition. PL-438 derives the exact strict-branch Schur ellipse `(g-mu)^T E^(-1)(g-mu)<=alpha`, with its center and anisotropy determined by visible real-product data plus the residue diagonal, and exhibits an admissible covariance accepted by the ellipse but rejected by the Weyl disk. The remaining theorem is arithmetic: place the actual prime source inside that source-adapted ellipse at variance scale by an unconditional input.

Across these examples, a bound becomes a source discriminator only after four checks: its source object is independently generated; its geometry matches the destination failure mode; matched controls preserve every nuisance scale that can manufacture the effect; and the physical source is forced to satisfy or violate the bound with quantitative margin. VIS-417--VIS-418, FD-300 and PL-438 sharpen this principle in different directions: unresolved beat/phase scales, aligned block participation and anisotropic Schur geometry can all invalidate a coarser but superficially strong estimate.

## Which source dependence survives quotienting, population ceilings and equilibrium scalarization?

Möbius Cancellation now prices the short-endpoint branch in the actual source geometry in two stages. MC-497 makes large quadratic short-overlap bias exceptional in the physical start coordinate, and MC-498 turns that into an effective-support condition on the induced start weights, with explicit quotient-fibre costs. MC-499 then shows that this effective support has a prior cardinality ceiling: a repeated-residue dyadic block contains at most `1+Q/p` source labels, so `R_start<=2(1+Q/p)` and the winning-endpoint participation ratios cannot exceed that population scale. The current exceptional-start dispersion certificate is therefore structurally incapable of closing blocks whose source population is below its character-sum tariff, regardless of how flat the reconstruction weights are. Only after a block passes this cheap population gate does a failed MC-498 participation test diagnose concentration, quotient folding or imbalance. This separates **method-limited source scarcity** from actual arithmetic concentration.

Prime Circle supplies the stronger scalarization warning. PC-414--PC-419 show that finite/infinite conductor descendants, finite Mellin jets, bounded Mellin translations, unrestricted Weyl closure and even the norm-closed single-shell diagonal algebra do not recover a source interaction already lost before shell-2 scalarization. The surviving object must therefore be cross-shell, non-diagonal or otherwise source-coupled before the scalar channel discards it.

Visual Exploration gives the corresponding phase quotient. VIS-409--VIS-411 show that one-shell Wigner data are repackaging, cross-shell fields retain only already-present labelled coherence, and projective shell phases reduce to Bargmann cycle holonomies. VIS-418 then adds that absolute phase relative to a distinguished arithmetic height can itself be the observable when the height is source-selected; it cannot be both quotiented away as nuisance and later interpreted as arithmetic signal. The quotient category must be fixed before the comparison.

Weil Positivity now closes two large equilibrium escapes. WP-423 proves separate invariance for the covered atomic/block product-dynamics KMS fibres. WP-424 shows that moving to a commutative non-atomic fibre does not help: every invariant central commutative fibre is time-trivial in the KMS GNS quotient, so nonzero opposite-frequency fibre modes vanish before they can pair with the source. Any surviving equilibrium mechanism must therefore use genuinely noncentral noncommutative fibre degrees or source-derived interacting/non-product dynamics, not merely more spectral support.

Nyman--Beurling supplies a quantitative method-boundary version of the same warning. Its projected dilation channel has an exact unit singular core, a near-isometric post-core strip and a positive internal channel before the far-external rank-one regime, but NB-319 shows that every mesoscopic reciprocal annulus `8<=M<=N^2/2` already contains a nested adjacent witness with Hilbert-normalized primitive at least `M/(32 sqrt(log M))`. Thus localizing a primitive `L^infinity` majorant cannot yield a fixed-power gain anywhere across the linear-to-quadratic corridor. Yet those primitive-large witnesses can still cancel strongly in the actual signed weighted pairing. The surviving source object is therefore the **bilinear mixing pairing itself**, not the primitive supremum used to bound it.

The reusable cross-line question is therefore: **after the lawful quotient and the actual destination scalarization, what source-dependent quantity remains both invariant and quantitatively load-bearing?** Effective support is not raw pair multiplicity and can be capped before weights enter; a phase coordinate is not intrinsic unless the source fixes its gauge; an auxiliary Bohr mode is not active if the KMS quotient freezes its algebra; and a large primitive envelope is not the actual bilinear error. These are different manifestations of the same discipline: price the exact carrier that remains after the representation has finished discarding information.

## What replaces a scalar summary when nuisance geometry or leading normalization degenerates?

Arithmetic Fidelity now gives both an obstruction and a precise replacement geometry. AF-523--AF-528 show that scalar boundary-curvature summaries can be matched to all algebraic orders while an exact relational derivative still recovers hidden source shape; that recovery can nevertheless be too small on the physical observation window to be stable. AF-533--AF-534 then identify the nuisance-relative carrier: effective Gaussian information is the squared precision/Cameron--Martin distance of the target direction from the **closed** nuisance geometry, so algebraic identifiability need not imply a positive robust margin.

AF-535 adds a second loss that closure alone does not see. In a regular semiparametric experiment, let `e=(I-P_Lambda)s` be the upstream efficient score, let `C=E[.\mid Y]` be the observation contraction, and let `M=closure(C Lambda)` be the downstream nuisance tangent space. Then

`e_Y=(I-P_M)Ce`

and

`I_X^eff-I_Y^eff=(||e||^2-||Ce||^2)+||P_M Ce||^2`.

Compression can therefore destroy target information in two distinct ways: direct efficient-score loss and nuisance recontamination after observation. Equality requires the upstream efficient score itself to be retained. Adjoining that score gives a first-order repair but is target-, baseline- and nuisance-dependent, so it cannot simply be declared a source-natural arithmetic lift.

This sharpens the global replacement-object question. A successful non-scalar observable must not merely break an algebraic collision; it must retain the target-relevant carrier after the actual observation and nuisance quotient at a usable scale. Prime Lattice's Schur ellipse is an exact finite-dimensional destination geometry of this type, while Weil Inertia's Poisson-capacity source spaces show that the admissible source category can be just as important as the observable itself. Prime Circle remains the negative control: a finite derivative or richer operator readout can be perfectly informative about a chosen representation while still adding no arithmetic mechanism if matched source classes reproduce it.

The remaining cross-line challenge is to construct replacement objects that are simultaneously **source-natural, destination-complete and stable after the real quotient/compression**. Exact infinitesimal recovery, a convenient nuisance-dependent lift, an ambient norm bound, or a richer representation is not enough. The carrier must be forced by the source before the target is inspected and must retain quantitative separation after every nuisance, gauge and observation map that the destination actually applies.
