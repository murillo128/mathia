## Which apparent resource survives the exact destination map?

The common audit remains: identify the admissible source image, quotient universal scaffolding and nuisance directions, follow the exact information-loss map into the destination, and only then price the surviving resource. The newest evidence makes the last step more explicit. Arithmetic Fidelity shows that after quotient closure has fixed a scalar threshold, boundary membership has decided exact contact and admission height has decided plateau onset, the no-contact approach rate is itself carried by a nested accessibility profile `rho`; the scalar distance sees that profile only through a universal one-sided quadratic envelope. Möbius Cancellation gives a discrete representation-loss analogue: alternating upper-sieve coefficients can recombine under the actual divisor map into a nonnegative boundary count, so nominal coefficient signs are not a cancellation resource after that map. Visual Exploration shows that a genuine qutrit residual coordinate can survive the algebraic quotients and still admit an exact multi-parameter nonarithmetic anisotropy calibration. Weil Inertia shows that increasingly strong local defect weights can survive only as logarithmic moments without determining whether the underlying positive defect measure vanishes.

Nyman--Beurling now gives a particularly clean hierarchy inside one positive matrix carrier. Above the minimum-displacement/conditioning gate, off-critical dilation forces a uniform spectral floor and target occupation is automatic. Below that gate, aggregate off-critical displacement still forces condition-number-free log-volume and at least one strong generalized mode through an exact determinant identity, but this existential capacity does not say that the actual Ford datum occupies the forced subspace. Farey Discrepancy supplies the complementary decomposition warning: completing a hard-cut close-zero packet restores divided-difference conditioning, while the exact complementary remainder jumps by the equal-and-opposite packet increment. Across these cases, the operative resource is neither nominal dimension, local conditioning nor total capacity by itself; it is information that survives the whole source-to-destination decomposition with complementary terms, nuisance laws, conditioning and target occupation charged.

## What counts as a genuine representation escape?

A representation escape must change destination-visible information without hiding an equal cost in a complementary term, nuisance calibration or target-alignment requirement. Prime Lattice is a positive example: vertical shift changes the scalar observation family so that a formerly missing complex quadrature becomes recoverable, and PL-430 quantifies the conditioning needed for the exact target functional. Arithmetic Fidelity is the complementary warning: closure and quotienting can preserve the scalar limit while deleting a boundary-membership bit or admission height, and even after those are classified the no-contact scalar rate is a smoothed image of a finer accessibility profile. Farey Discrepancy adds a decomposition warning: completing an ill-conditioned packet can improve its coordinates while the exact remainder absorbs the opposite change. Möbius Cancellation shows that a signed source representation can become a positive observable after the destination map. Visual Exploration shows that a truly independent coordinate can still be explained by a calibrated nonarithmetic law family. Weil Inertia shows that an infinite hierarchy of finite scalar moments can still fail to determine the defect configuration. Nyman--Beurling shows the intermediate possibility: exact source geometry can force aggregate spectral volume and even a large mode, while the final datum can remain uncontrolled until occupation is proved; only a uniform spectral floor removes that final alignment gate.

The reusable test is therefore: specify the admissible source family; state the exact quotient/readout and any packet/remainder split; identify the smallest datum on which the destination verdict changes; expose which signs, phases, membership bits, accessibility profiles, nuisance parameters or spectral directions survive that map; exhibit source-side control under assumptions weaker than the target theorem; and propagate the resulting margin through conditioning, truncation, completion, complementary terms, target occupation and normalization. Carrier capacity, formal reconstructibility, nicer component coordinates, local positivity, nominal coefficient signs, aggregate determinant growth and finite moment budgets are intermediate facts, not substitutes for that chain.
