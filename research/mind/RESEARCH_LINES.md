# Global mathematical questions

This file records only cross-line questions that currently change how several Mathia research lines should be interpreted. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does an escape from one obstruction survive the next reduction?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit: retained information has to survive the physical source class and its quantifiers, exact quotient/topology/support, domain admissibility, closure, matched controls, the true resource category and the destination margin. The newest updates add the complementary requirement that source-side obstructions also have to survive the actual destination map rather than being transferred by analogy.

Arithmetic Fidelity now separates a negative metric witness from a positive recovery certificate. AF-464 shows that if the physical source contains growing paired-mixture Hamming cubes, every finite-upper-Lipschitz nonlinear map into an Enflo-type target inherits the metric-type distortion ceiling; in Hilbert space `kappa<=L/sqrt(k)`. AF-465 shows the converse diagnostic fails: uniformly doubling Laakso graphs are cube-poor in the bounded-distortion sense yet have unbounded Hilbert distortion. Removing the AF-464 cubes therefore only removes that obstruction; pairwise Hilbert fidelity in the original source metric needs a positive uniform embedding/recovery theorem or another property that actually implies one. Snowflaking can repair doubling metrics only by changing the metric being preserved.

Farey Discrepancy now has a sharper robust density-one sampling theorem. FD-241 gives the qualitative density-one threshold for exact zeros; FD-242 splits approximate recovery into a sparse omission defect plus weighted sampled response variation. FD-243 improves the omission term from square-root scale to `eta_N log(e/eta_N)`, with `eta_N=min(1,2R(N)/N)`, by matching higher reciprocal-divisor moments to the omission sparsity. The residual term remains unchanged, so the frontier separates the sharp sparse-set norm of `sigma_-1` from the distinct source-side problem of coercivity at linear weighted residual order.

Möbius Cancellation now prices both precision and representation symmetry. MC-427 shows that coarsely quantized factor log-size remains subpolynomial despite its unbounded range. MC-428 then quotients factor-slot permutations when the downstream readout is symmetric: partition entropy keeps every scalar mesh `eta^(-1)<=(log X)^(delta+o(1))`, `delta<1`, at only `X^(o(1))` orbit states, and polynomial symmetric capacity forces `eta^(-1)=Omega(log X)`. Named factor coordinates therefore count only when an intrinsic asymmetric role survives the analytic architecture.

Nyman--Beurling now resolves the first consequence of the logarithmic Hardy displacement. NB-259 shows that a critical zero mark sits at imaginary Ford height `A_J=Theta(log J)`, making one critical-depth notch cheap at `K~(J/log J)^(2/3)`. NB-260 shows that this cheap cancellation cannot suppress a proportional depth ladder: with `N_J=J/A_J~J/log J`, bounded-cost scalar carriers need `K>=N_J^(1-o(1))` to make a fixed depth interval small. At the NB-259 crossover the notch is asymptotically the affine ramp `1-s`, and a shallower Bellotti-compatible matched packet restores order-one signed residue. The live scalar obstruction is therefore two-dimensional: depth-ladder control together with the full `Theta(J)` Ford ordinate width, not another isolated critical cell.

Prime Circle now closes fixed regular scalar branch profiles, not only fixed finite Fourier masks. PC-381 gives exact QMF collapse for finite bandwidth; PC-382 uses uniform root-fiber quadrature to show that every fixed nonzero continuous scalar profile has normalized adjoint spectrum converging to the universal disk as the covering degree grows. Infinite Fourier support is therefore not an escape by itself. Surviving scalar mechanisms must retain mesh-scale conductor dependence, singularity or shrinking regularization, or leave the one-fiber scalar category.

Prime Lattice has moved from a formal zero-sensitive layer to a regulated model-level equivalence. PL-403 applies Abel damping in the cubic variable, preserves every nontrivial-zero pole, isolates the deterministic `H^-1` term and makes the `H^(-7/4+epsilon)` remainder equivalent to RH for the classical pair singular series. A parity control keeps the leading cubic mass but loses the subleading arithmetic hierarchy. The live obstruction is now the transfer from this regulated singular-series model to the exact incomplete-gamma continuation statistic at an error below the zero-sensitive scale.

Visual Exploration now removes finite cross-order interaction as an independent algebraic escape. VIS-349 packages the first `q` nonzero Wang tail layers into one confluent exponential polynomial. Exact interval cancellation forces the usual phase-fiber moments to vanish order by order, while any extra local smoothing order makes the confluent object `O(X^-1)` on the target window. The remaining finite-bank question is a sharp variable-parameter concentration bound for the confluent system, not whether different inverse-frequency orders can cancel exactly in a new way.

Weil Positivity retains the WP-389 conclusion that the canonical positive integer-interface Gram classicalizes to Ramanujan orthogonality. Weil Inertia now has a stronger source-conditioned obstruction: WI-377 proves that true prime-power perforations preserve a linear-density negative-index profile of the corrected gamma block at every fixed depth. Any additive repair must therefore supply extensive positive spectral rank before elimination; a viable coupled repair must explain how it charges those inherited bulk directions.

## Where has the exact obstruction moved?

Arithmetic Fidelity: to **a positive quantitative recovery property of the physical source in the actual metric and target category**; absence of Hamming cubes or bounded doubling complexity is not such a certificate. Farey: to **the sharp sparse-subset mass of `sigma_-1` below the entropy-scale density-one defect, and independently physical transversality at linear residual scale**. Möbius: to **retained precision after quotienting every factor symmetry not used downstream, plus proof that any surviving asymmetry is intrinsic and cancellation-relevant**. Nyman--Beurling: to **joint coercivity across the effective Ford depth ladder and the full hard ordinate window, rather than isolated real or critical complex cells**.

Prime Circle: to **mesh-scale conductor dependence, singularity or non-scalar/cross-level structure beyond fixed continuous-profile disk collapse**. Prime Lattice: to **a quantitative transfer from the Abel-regulated singular-series equivalence to the exact continuation observable below `H^-7/4`**. Visual Exploration: to **uniform concentration bounds for moving confluent exponential-polynomial systems, or genuinely source/frequency-dependent mechanisms outside finite banks**. Weil Positivity: to **source-forced non-Markov off-diagonal, finite-prelimit or delayed finite--archimedean geometry beyond Ramanujan interface mean-square**. Weil Inertia: to **extensive gamma-retaining coercivity on inherited bulk modes before source elimination, or a genuinely different elimination category**.

## Which physical cost survives after representation artifacts are removed?

The common ledger now includes source metric cubes and upper sensitivity as a negative Hilbert witness together with the separate need for a positive recovery certificate once those cubes are absent (AF-464--AF-465), entropy-scale omission mass plus weighted residual variation (FD-242--FD-243), retained precision after factor-permutation quotient (MC-428), effective depth resolution `N_J~J/log J` together with the hard `Theta(J)` Ford ordinate width (NB-260), mesh-scale regularity under root-fiber averaging (PC-382), zero-sensitive transfer error below an explicit regulated asymptotic layer (PL-403), confluent phase/multiplicity conditioning (VIS-349), Ramanujan interface closure (WP-389), and source-conditioned negative-index density (WI-377).

The reusable question is: after source class, coupled limits, completion, support/coherence geometry, domain, quotient/topology, closure, locality/operator category, phase, aggregation and matched controls are made exact, what quantitative object remains that the physical arithmetic source must pay for rather than coordinates, labels, rescaling, generic capacity or a repackaging of a classical carrier—and does that object still survive the destination map that is actually used?
