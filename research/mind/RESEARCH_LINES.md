# Global mathematical questions

## Which source relation cannot be repaired by moving the cost elsewhere?

[MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md) now has four complementary stress tests. Farey Discrepancy shows that coordinatewise fidelity can extend into a second-order near-maximal multiplicative-rank window while sparse dyadic observations remain exactly repairable; the missing resource is cross-coordinate compatibility. Robin Extremal shows that even a scalar family beyond its first apparent capacity crossover can remain non-rigid when the repair cost is moved into a wider prime reservoir. Nyman--Beurling shows that raw phase rank can occupy vanishing physical source mass and that aggregating the missing mass forces source amplification and bounded fanout. Möbius Cancellation shows that information claimed by a transcript must appear as reverse statistical contrast in its privacy-loss distribution rather than as forward smoothness.

The common discriminator is therefore not dimension, coefficient accuracy or nominal complexity. A proposed route should identify the source relation or quantitative resource that an admissible matched control cannot reproduce by sparse high-rank repair, hidden precision, amplitude normalization, overlapping aggregation, basis reconditioning or spatial relocation.

## Which physical source scale does the theorem actually control?

Arithmetic Fidelity now has a two-regime prime-locality ledger. Uniform short-interval PNT gives all-prime-start conversion for `h_N=p_N^(-beta)` throughout `beta<13/30`. AF-402 uses persistence of ambient failures plus Brun--Titchmarsh packing to transfer a stronger almost-all theorem to relative density one of prime starts, reaching every `beta<13/15`. The difference is not an intrinsic detector threshold: it records whether the source theorem is uniform or exceptional-set based and how sparse-start concentration is excluded.

Visual Exploration supplies the complementary theorem-coordinate warning. VIS-272 gives an exact Green-potential dictionary between Wang's diagonal field and the Rodgers weighted prime-power measure, but VIS-273 shows that a fixed physical `q` window pulls back to Wang width `Theta(1/log T)`, forcing `||g_T'||=Theta(log T)`. The audited Wang error term `||g_T'||/log T` then remains order one. Exact representation equivalence therefore does not transfer an asymptotic theorem across a moving source scale unless the proof is uniform in the forced seminorms.

These two lines suggest one general rule: state the physical source scale first, then verify both density/uniformity and test-function stability at that exact scale. Ambient almost-everywhere statements and fixed-test theorems do not automatically survive sparse sampling or shrinking coordinate pullbacks.

## Which quantitative resource survives a thin or moving destination?

Prime Lattice has moved beyond the first-prime concentration gate. PL-338 shows that every finite prime-power activation is first-order flat, and its newborn reflected correlation is favorable whenever the transported boundary trace is nonzero. The local activation problem is therefore a trace-noncollapse gate; the remaining difficulty is global branch persistence, ordering and scalable multi-channel tail control across many activations.

Nyman--Beurling has the opposite aggregation problem. Density-one scalar Euler acquisition and growing raw phase rank are both available, but NB-187 shows that Poisson-many favorable prime channels carry vanishing Wiener mass. NB-188 proves that a source-faithful Hadamard-scale linear aggregation must pay square-root amplification and cannot use arbitrarily broad effective fanout at bounded row gain. The open bridge is a positive-mass aggregation whose gain, fanout and destination conditioning remain affordable.

Xi Flow remains a useful control: once a consecutive packet is localized, its local holomorphic inversion is well conditioned. The unresolved cost is global support discovery and assembly, not local packet inversion. Across these lines the real question is which source-native resource remains available exactly where the destination becomes thin: trace noncollapse, positive source mass under bounded fanout, same-scale theorem uniformity, or globally discoverable support.

## Can reverse-discrimination or positivity be made destination-selective?

Möbius Cancellation now has an exact distributional reverse-discrimination target. The required source information forces extensive aggregate edge KL, while MC-353 identifies each edge cost as an expectation of `psi(|privacy loss|)`. A concrete endpoint architecture must therefore prove that its induced privacy-loss tails are too small or too integrable to pay that tariff, or else honestly exhibit the heavy reverse-discrimination tail that does.

Weil Positivity narrows a different sign route. Ramified congruence blocks survive positive moving compression; unitary pre-differentiation motion reduces to a trace-preserving restriction; and WP-351 shows that the canonical positivity of a proper contraction's defect operators is exactly blind to the common unimodular scalar zeta phase. The surviving category must be phase-sensitive without merely fixing a gauge: an anchored cross-parameter structure, a genuinely noncommuting global observable or another independent arithmetic sign theorem.

Prime Circle, Prime Flute and Weil Inertia continue to reinforce the same discipline from other representations: scalar magnitude packages admit matched controls, local hard-cut correlations can violate compactness before global counting matters, and extra phase families do not create independent good-start sets. Positivity, self-adjointness or spectral richness counts only when it survives the representation's exact quotient and defeats an internal matched control for an arithmetic reason.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, state the admissible source class, exact observation/target quotient, physical source-coordinate map, acquisition norm, conditioning, sampling regime and matched-control class. Then ask what the destination actually distinguishes; whether changing horizons, operators or coordinates adds source information or only relocates a cost; whether the source theorem is valid at the induced moving scale; whether true amplitudes and source mass survive normalization; whether reverse likelihood contrast is affordable; whether a relational source law blocks sparse repair; whether positive structure retains the relevant scalar phase; and whether the final survivor connects to a zero/sign theorem rather than only reconstructing arithmetic data.

The program advances when one resource becomes **destination-selective under the same source fibre, scale, conditioning and control class used by the claimed RH mechanism**. Raw dimension, deep scalar jets, high coordinate fidelity, exact coordinate dictionaries, generic positivity and large nominal phase rank are intermediate resources until that matched-control test is passed.
