## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than another absorbed channel?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit, and the newest results sharpen it in several independent categories.

Arithmetic Fidelity now has a genuine source-only selector in one natural class: coefficient-root neutrality fixes the reciprocal-order constant at the Taylor radius, hence `c=2pi` and `a=1`. But the existing determinant still does not transport shell identity, and the selected square fibre fails the current adjacent-packet cancellation. **Source selection, transport and target success are independent gates.**

Farey Discrepancy pushes local source fidelity almost to saturation without removing blindness. At one target horizon, every prime below any fixed fraction `theta N` may obey the exact Möbius Euler law while the switched observation remains bounded and the summatory source is `asymp N/log N`; only the endpoint `theta=1` forces the target prefix to be Möbius. The missing resource is therefore same-source cross-horizon compatibility, not local prime count.

Prime Circle and Prime Lattice now close two different “make the representation bigger” routes. Scalar grade completions either compactify positive-degree transport or, at asymptotically geometric weights, reduce modulo compacts to radial standard Fock transport; even the resulting source/control radius gap can be manufactured by a nonarithmetic metric. Every finite-dimensional completely multiplicative matrix source, meanwhile, is a finite Artinian thickening of scalar multiplicative channels for linear observables. Dimension alone does not create a new arithmetic coupling.

Visual Exploration locates a representation between two failures. The scalar determinant `h=qv-ur` keeps ratio-cell geometry but erases the two prime coordinates needed for the sieve; freezing `h` retains those coordinates but leaves only `O(1)` same-shell lattice points, so there is no long affine parameter to sieve. The viable object is the **family of short slices together**, retaining pair coordinates while aggregating enough transverse range.

Weil Positivity adds two global positivity warnings. Mixed-prime coefficients can remove a divergent local completion tax, but a continuous scalar selector cannot delete the resulting dense mixed spectrum. Separately, exact zero energy for composite cyclotomic shells in a stationary positive radial form creates radical relations; the source coboundary identities then force surviving prime-power shells into the zero Mellin-frequency sector. Positive completion and positive nullity are different operations, and both can destroy the intended arithmetic profile.

Weil Inertia supplies the constructive topology lesson. The full localized Weil form makes every RH failure a finite first crossing. A newly activated prime channel is discontinuous in ambient `L^2` operator norm but form-small in Suzuki's logarithmic topology; the null equation itself bounds the logarithmic energy of actual ground states and makes the complete source ledger quantitatively continuous there. The remaining problem is **positive reserve/arithmetic sign**, not regularity.

Across these examples, enrichment deserves credit only when a new source relation survives the exact downstream quotient **in the topology, normalization and quantifier required by the destination theorem** and cannot be reproduced by a matched control.

## Where has the exact obstruction moved after the latest boundary results?

Several fronts have become narrower rather than merely more complicated.

**Local fidelity versus persistent coherence.** Farey Discrepancy shows that even `Theta(N/log N)` exact prime relations at one horizon do not force the target statistic; persistence of one source across horizons is the unresolved currency. Nyman--Beurling provides the width analogue: after the exact Ford carrier is retained, Bellotti's local count `M(D)<<D+1+R/H` and Ford damping compress the dangerous horizontal depth to roughly `(1/Y)log(1+R/H)` inside the Bellotti range. **Apparent proof cost versus physical cost.** Möbius Cancellation removes the artificial inverse-gap differencing depth and leaves the genuine `2^(O(A))` tariff, exceptional family, source-incidence ledger and destination geometry. Visual Exploration shows that one can preserve either sieve dimension or averaging length with a naive reduction, but the physical theorem needs both simultaneously. Nyman--Beurling likewise turns the linear carrier capacity `R/H` into only a logarithmic depth budget after the actual Ford attenuation is applied.

**Regularity versus sign.** Weil Inertia has now closed the threshold-regularity subproblem on actual null states; the open gate is a coercive reserve that does not collapse along the prime-threshold sequence. ## Can sparse or deep defects be made visible without first strengthening the whole statistic?

Several lines now say “yes, but only after changing the destination-sensitive object rather than merely improving an average.” Normalized pair correlation can tolerate sparse off-line defects, whereas full localized Weil energy turns any failure into a finite variational event. Nyman--Beurling keeps the cumulative depth-phase carrier paired with its Ford damping, removes depths already negligible under the smooth source profile, and then prices the residual shallow range by the carrier width itself. Visual Exploration keeps the Wang taper while refusing a scalar projection that removes the prime-pair exclusions. Robin Extremal tests the normalized coefficient object in directions that expose cost even when the observed arc exhibits exact cancellation.

The common design principle is to identify the **destination-sensitive functional and its active source range first**. If normalization, projection, scalarization or a positivity quotient has already erased the defect, a sharper estimate of the same compressed statistic will not restore it. If the right object is chosen, the remaining theorem should target the smallest source range and topology on which the defect still has persistent cost.
