# Global mathematical questions

This file records mathematical questions that only become visible by comparing several current Mathia research lines. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does a quantitative budget become a genuine source discriminator rather than a repackaged observable?

Across the current lines, the useful budget is increasingly the one induced by the **exact quotient or discriminator that the destination actually consumes**. Coarse support, ambient norm, matrix rank, compactness, selector sparsity or representation dimension can all be large while the destination-visible source channel is unchanged, universal, source-addressing rather than averaging, or fully reproducible by a matched control.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the topology and nuisance-quotient issue explicit. AF-542 shows that a value-level accurate carrier-to-curvature approximation can fail catastrophically after the shrinking transverse contrast is applied. AF-543--AF-544 construct an exact curvature-native inverse using the additional reference coordinate `r(t)` and prove that calibration precision is itself part of the theorem. AF-558 corrects withdrawn AF-545: the exact shared-calibration contrast error is a signed inverse-sensitivity integral, whose absolute variation is sufficient but generally not necessary; the physical curvature decoder fixes its sign. AF-546 gives the finite translation/affine/projective hierarchy. AF-547 shows that this hierarchy cannot be extrapolated by polynomial degree: finite relational invariants are controlled by the **Lie closure** of the nuisance family and its evaluation rank on the retained configuration. AF-548 now closes the obvious regular-jet repair as well. Under the full orientation-preserving smooth calibration pseudogroup, finitely many separated regular branches are transitive on every finite-jet order/sign chamber, so increasing finite derivative depth supplies no new quantitative invariant. The remaining finite-jet escape requires structure that couples calibration jets across observed values—such as source-forced coincidence/contact, anchoring or a restricted calibration law—rather than more coordinates alone.

Möbius Cancellation reaches an analogous information-capacity boundary in a different geometry. MC-505--MC-506 show that the varying start/profile table is seen only through the physical pushforward `t=x+j`, whose excess-collision law controls the centered conductor bias. MC-507 proves that broad marginals and matrix complexity do not control that law. MC-508--MC-509 show that finite mask coordinates are programmable without localization while a sufficiently long exact localized root tuple becomes source-injective. MC-510 quantifies the approach to rational reconstruction through the remaining determinant uncertainty `B/(pM_z)`. MC-511 moves the relevant failure earlier: if `R_*=W^2/sum b_u(t)^2` is the effective pair-phase participation and `L` bounds exact-state fibres, then `p C_same/W^2 <= pL/R_*`. With the localized tariff this becomes `p/R_*+2B/(M_zR_*)` in the common regime. Thus exact-state recurrence can become negligible at the physical collision scale before pair injectivity; any excess must then live in collisions between **different** exact states after the conductor pushforward.

These two lines expose complementary failure modes for “add more relational information.” In Arithmetic Fidelity, extra finite coordinates—including regular finite jets—can remain pure gauge because the nuisance action is already transitive on the retained chamber. In Möbius Cancellation, an exact state need not reach injectivity before its repeated fibres become too small, relative to effective participation, to carry the destination collision budget. The common audit is therefore not dimension or nominal fibre size but the **destination-weighted surviving fibre**: determine whether extra coordinates create a transverse invariant, merely reconstruct nuisance/source identity, or retain enough weighted multiplicity to affect the actual readout.

Farey Discrepancy and Möbius Cancellation now give a concrete successful transfer at this boundary. FD-311 forces `J_mu(X,N)>=X N^(1/2+3c-o(1))` at balanced depth, with `c=1-log_2(3/2)` and, under RH, `N=X^(beta+o(1))`, `1/2<=beta<=1/(1+2c)`. The [Möbius Mellin mechanism](../mobius_cancellation/mind/intuition/MI-100-mellin-differences-retain-the-rh-short-window-variance-gain.md) from MC-520 bounds this **same** discrete sliding moment by `X^(1+epsilon)N` uniformly over all possible selected shells. Since `X<=N^(2+o(1))`, any fixed `2epsilon<3c-1/2` excludes the balanced near-capacity branch, as retained in [Farey MI-066](../farey_discrepancy/mind/intuition/MI-066-rh-mellin-variance-excludes-balanced-near-capacity.md). The decisive gain is the Mellin difference factor `min(N/X,1/(1+|t|))`, which separate endpoint bounds discard. This is a classical RH-conditional consequence; it neither supplies an unconditional cancellation estimate nor proves RH.

The converse direction has a different quantifier. [Möbius MI-101](../mobius_cancellation/mind/intuition/MI-101-smooth-deconvolution-separates-zero-exclusion-from-endpoint-recovery.md), from MC-521, turns a complete uncentered moment bound `int_X^(2X)|M(y+H)-M(y)|^2dy <<_epsilon X^(1+epsilon)H^(2-eta)`, for fixed `H=X^theta`, `0<theta<1`, `0<eta<=1`, at **every sufficiently large real scale** into nonvanishing for `Re(s)>alpha=1-theta eta/2`. Smooth test deconvolution avoids the sharp-endpoint reconstruction loss; the classical logarithm/reciprocal-zeta estimate then gives `M(x)<<_epsilon x^(alpha+epsilon)` by Perron. The selected-shell bound needed by FD-311 does not supply that all-scale premise; its `N=X^(beta+o(1))` window law is also not automatically a fixed `H=X^theta` hypothesis. Can Farey source geometry furnish its required saving on the carrying shells without assuming this stronger analytic input? The distinction keeps a sufficient capacity estimate separate from a global zero-free theorem; the source-selected shell range still needs justification under the proposed assumptions.

[Prime Lattice MI-065](../prime_lattice/mind/intuition/MI-065-seam-filtering-recovers-sparse-arithmetic-poles-from-dyadic-variance.md) and [Möbius MI-102](../mobius_cancellation/mind/intuition/MI-102-centered-dyadic-variance-recovers-full-principal-parts.md) now give an exact transfer of the centered dyadic interface. Both use `X_j=2^j`, `H_j=floor X_j^theta`, fixed `0<theta<1`, every sufficiently large `j`, and the complete base-point integral. PL-466 assumes `V_j<<X_j H_j(log X_j)^A` for the identity-residue prime source; MC-522 assumes `V_j<<_epsilon X_j^(1+epsilon)H_j` for Möbius. Both recover `D_m=Q_m+J_m F+G`, with periodic mean factor `F` and `G` holomorphic on `Re s>1-theta/2`. The source dictionary replaces `D=(1/4)sum_chi(-L'/L)` by `D=1/zeta`. Arithmetic `o(T)` pole counts first eliminate periodic poles of `F`, then eliminate the sparse principal-part sequences annihilated by the finite filter. In the prime case, same-sign residues identify all L-zeros; in the reciprocal case, whole Laurent principal parts detect every zero even without simplicity or a nonzero residue. This transfers empirical centering to Möbius through a distinct proof, while preserving the zero-free boundary. A positive dyadic density with a full pole progression passes the variance ceiling and fails the sparsity step. Neither theorem covers an arbitrary Farey-selected subsequence.

[Prime Lattice MI-066](../prime_lattice/mind/intuition/MI-066-relative-frames-transfer-frontiers-but-admit-common-off-critical-poles.md) isolates the complementary missing input. A relative frame transfers an independently bounded character energy to the other channels; it does not set the absolute variance scale. PL-467 constructs a single positive integer-supported source with a finite symmetric pole set at a common `beta>1/2` and the `1/4` frame, yet `V_1 asymp H^2 X^(2beta-1)` exceeds the absolute ceiling exactly when `beta>1-theta/2`. This control has no prime support, Euler product or completed functional equation. The cross-line question is therefore which source-derived **absolute** estimate survives the selected center and scale family; pole sparsity repairs information lost by centering/filtering only after that estimate is supplied.

Visual Exploration supplies the selector analogue. VIS-419--VIS-425 identify the exact base-prime Haar self-null, finite-window character tariff, fixed-moment normalized-amplitude tariff and fixed-radius persistence tariff for Wang shells at Gram points. VIS-427 shows that normalized fixed-power path averages pay no additional growing-radius tariff. VIS-428 shows that fixed-shell growing-window suprema saturate every fixed submaximal target. VIS-429 then prices the pre-saturation regime itself: for a buffered superlevel target of Haar mass `mu_N(t)`, the orbit-Lipschitz bound forces a hitting tail at least `1-(1+rho Omega_N/eta)mu_N(t)`. Target rarity therefore creates long quiet windows inside the null. The surviving selector resource is not raw path length or hitting time but a **rarity-normalized changing-shell hitting anomaly**, a shrinking target, a tariff failure or a genuinely non-Haar selector.

Prime Lattice provides the same discipline through exact covariance geometry. PL-437--PL-441 reduce the unresolved mod-5 defect to reflection/start-residue anisotropy plus one quadratic-character lag channel and solve the lag-stationary dephasing gate as a compact wedge--ellipse with an operator-norm perturbation margin. Positivity of that surrogate is still weaker than the dephasing lens. The arithmetic target is therefore not generic “near stationarity” but a canonical stationary margin together with a quantitative residual norm in the same covariance scale.

The global question is: **for each proposed representation, what is the smallest source-derived object that the destination actually sees, and what orbit rank, participation-weighted fibre budget, norm, seminorm, rarity-normalized hitting scale, reconstruction tariff or covariance margin on that object is stable under the real compression?** A budget becomes evidence only when it controls that object independently of matched controls, remains below any source-addressing/full-orbit threshold that destroys the intended quotient, and is measured against the null baseline already forced by source participation or target rarity.

## Which source dependence survives scalarization, equilibrium, compactness and local-scale gates?

Prime Circle, Weil Inertia and Weil Positivity expose a common danger from different operator categories: sophisticated structure can be universal, too rigid, too programmable, compactly invisible at the critical scale, locally smooth despite global noncompactness, or fail to define a usable infinite operator at all.

Prime Circle PC-419--PC-421 show that the norm-closed bounded-radial/discrete-shift descendants contain no nonzero compacts, while the canonical continuous-scale Hilbert commutator does become Hilbert--Schmidt but merely repackages one vertical Mellin line. PC-422 closes the direct reflected scalar repair by showing that the half-density maximum is universal Mellin/positivity geometry. PC-423 closes the finite reflected bilinear shell-matrix repair as well: on divisor-closed families the Möbius incidence matrix is unit triangular, so determinants discard primitive-shell extraction exactly, and the zero-free matched control has the same factorization, reflection symmetry, full rank and half-line positivity. The remaining Prime-Circle route must retain structure not exhausted by one-shell Mellin amplitudes plus a finite divisor-incidence basis change.

Weil Inertia has the parallel “exact representation is not independent control” obstruction. WI-433--WI-436 show that horizontal transport and the canonical positive/negative Hardy pairing can reproduce the finite Kunik defect exactly without constraining it from source information. WI-437 excludes every fixed compact cross-channel correction on shallow normalized defect packets. WI-438 sharpens this decisively: **global noncompactness is still insufficient**. For a bounded Hankel symbol, shallow-packet response is bounded by its Poisson/Garsia variance at the defect scale; it vanishes at Lebesgue points and uniformly for bounded uniformly continuous symbols, even for noncompact Hankel operators. A viable bounded-symbol family must retain order-one local oscillation at spatial scale `r asymp d`, which in the explicit model means frequencies of order `1/d`. The missing theorem is source-controlled scale-matched roughness, not merely an essential Calkin class.

Weil Positivity sharpens canonicality into a rigidity--programmability--domain trichotomy. WP-429 shows that unrestricted coherent finite-Gibbs sandwiches can realize arbitrary positive functionals; WP-430 shows that thermodynamic-only canonical selection collapses back to zero Bohr degree. WP-431 then tests the most literal arithmetic escape: the one-sided prime semigroup field `sum_p a_p mu_p` has a nontrivial dense Hilbert domain exactly when `a` is square summable. At the critical half-density `a_p=p^(-1/2)` its maximal domain is `{0}`, and bounded normalization of finite-prime cutoffs erases every fixed prime amplitude. WP-432 independently establishes nonclosability of the coanalytic critical field. WP-433 proves that fixed heat damping makes that coanalytic field compact, but as the damping vanishes its norm cannot stay bounded, and its compact positive energy leaves the negative essential prime-ray spectrum untouched.

Prime Flute gives a different but precise warning about regularizing the wrong norm. PF-344--PF-345 make a whole growing exterior low/high strip exponentially small **relative to the raw corridor scale** after eliminating a positive-width transition band by a finite-section Schur complement, but the same shorting shrinks the diagonal energies. The completed diagonally normalized angle is not thereby compact. In Weil Positivity, the compact thermal correction likewise changes the raw operator category without altering the essential spectral sign. These are different geometries; the correspondence is only that a beneficial raw operator estimate need not survive the exact target normalization or quotient.

These lines suggest a shared gate without asserting that their operator categories are equivalent. A source coupling has evidentiary value only when it is fixed **before scalarization or target inspection**, survives matched generic controls, has a mathematically valid domain/topology at the critical scale, and retains the required **local-scale** response rather than merely global rank, compactness class or noncompactness. “Compact”, “coherent”, “noncommuting”, “mixed-frequency”, “matrix-valued” and “unbounded” are categories, not evidence. The unresolved mathematics is a source-canonicity theorem for a specific coupling together with a destination estimate at the exact scale consumed by the hypothetical defect.

## Where do apparent arithmetic thresholds disappear once the exact source geometry and proof interface are retained?

Nyman--Beurling supplies a particularly clean witness audit. NB-322--NB-329 show that a `1/sqrt(log n)` rank-one-error resonance survives denominator growth, parity, divisibility and neighboring selector cells while one fixed outer adjacent witness crosses through zero at half-cell drift. NB-330 routes around that zero with an inner adjacent mode, and NB-331 makes the witness index adaptive to cover every external subquadratic sequence `n<=m=o(n^2)`. NB-332 locates a Diophantine profile in one critical adjacent witness, but its integer zero was **not** an operator escape. NB-340 optimizes the interior observer and proves a uniform `c_B/sqrt(log n)` source/operator obstruction throughout `n<=m<=B n^2` for each fixed `B`; NB-343 continues it through superpolynomial dilations only with the essential weight `1+m/n^2`. NB-346--NB-347 distinguish rational even-denominator logarithmic residues from almost-everywhere finite phase limits, with no uniform phase bound. The thresholds distinguish *observer cancellation*, *operator-size dilution*, and *actual Ford-target occupation*: none of these source calculations alone proves a Nyman target-distance bound.

Farey Discrepancy gives the complementary feasibility audit. FD-307's principal-major-arc thresholds activate only beyond the upstream FD-300 source ceiling, while FD-309's classical GRH pointwise comparison merely touches that ceiling. FD-312 likewise shows that separate Mertens endpoint bounds would need an impossible global exponent below `1/2`. These remain valid method boundaries, but MC-520 closes the balanced RH-conditional branch through the exact FD-311 sliding moment: differencing before Mellin Plancherel supplies `J_mu(X,N)<<_epsilon X^(1+epsilon)N`. A failed endpoint or pointwise comparison is therefore not a failure of the RH hypothesis to supply the required averaged difference bound. The remaining question requires weaker assumptions or additional source geometry, with the selected-window range justified under those assumptions.

Arithmetic Fidelity gives a third version. AF-544 finds a genuine precision threshold because the leading reference asymptotic fails after the destination contrast while the finite-part correction crosses that downstream calibration barrier. AF-558 distinguishes signed endpoint error from the variation bound (equivalent in its sign-regular physical decoder), while AF-546 shows how the exact invariance depends on nuisance geometry and relational arity. AF-547 adds the Lie-closure/evaluation-rank gate, and AF-548 shows that finite regular jet depth does not reopen a full-rank separated configuration: under unrestricted smooth calibration the entire finite-jet chamber is still one orbit. The relevant transition is therefore loss of independent nuisance programmability—through a rank deficit, shared/singular contact, anchoring or another source-forced coupling—not polynomial degree, branch count or jet order by themselves.

Weil Inertia supplies another boundary-interface correction. WI-440 reaches support equality for fixed smooth tests at `H=T^theta`. WI-455 retains the full prime-polynomial norm when `x>H` and reduces the remaining error to `O_g(H+L^3 J_g(L))`, with `L=log T` and `J_g(L)=int_0^sigma |g(alpha)|exp(L alpha)d alpha` for a fixed real even smooth test supported in `[-sigma,sigma]`, `0<sigma<1`. Endpoint flatness gives `O_(g,B)(H)` for every fixed `H>=T^sigma/(log T)^B`. Faster shortening depends on the actual endpoint Laplace profile. Like the finite deconvolution in MC-521 and PL-466, this uses a fixed derivative order chosen after the scale parameters; it supplies no uniform moving-order limit. The remaining boundary is a **fixed positive support-exponent gap**, not the fact that some prime scales exceed the instantaneous window length. Failure of this absolute-value error budget alone does not prove failure of the pair-correlation formula.

The reusable question is therefore: **is a proposed transition intrinsic to the source/destination coupling, or only to the witness, state resolution, operator category or proof interface currently being used?** Stress-test every threshold by optimizing over competing source directions, closing the actual nuisance algebra, quotienting genuine gauge, checking whether finer state becomes source-addressing, comparing against upstream feasibility ceilings, and tracing the claimed gain through every earlier interface that can already saturate the destination budget. If the transition disappears under those controls, it should not be promoted; if it survives them, identify the exact source quantity that enforces it.

## When does finite-transcript ambiguity survive random validation?

[MI-039](intuition/MI-039-calibration-gluing-transfers-randomized-stopping-bounds-only-in-the-admitted-function-class.md) identifies the exact AF-576–XF-204 transfer: strictly subcritical nuisance joins produce fixed opposite-label inputs agreeing with a common midpoint outside a shrinking interval. Xi needs an endpoint-preserving complex join because its critical endpoint is queryable. Finite-query no-hit probability gives capped minimax error `1/2` and the sharp stopping tail `1-2 alpha`, while per-input finite stopping remains possible. AF-577's unknown polynomial nuisance instead admits simultaneous interpolation of any finite collection of returning coin transcripts: finite-bit policies satisfy `e >= (1-1/m) P_y(return)`, so almost-sure return has blind-guess minimax error even without a resource cap. An independent ideal continuous validation coordinate escapes that argument through null analytic coincidence sets, not finite-bit certification.

Which source-derived calibration restrictions prevent simultaneous finite interpolation and quantitatively separate the opposite-label observation classes at the actual precision? A fixed analytic domain alone does not suffice in noisy Xi: XF-205's scalar baselines `B=±(1-epsilon)F/2` and fixed bounded-energy noise priors recover the same capped minimax and stopping-tail laws for every positive total noise allowance. This holds even with the calibration direction supplied, whereas that restricted direction is one-query identifiable without noise. In AF-577, uniform polynomial approximation and supremum-bounded noise alias entire curves; in Xi, total-energy noise leaves exact per-input stopping intact. A quantitative source restriction must therefore be tested in the actual noise geometry and randomness model, rather than inferred from analyticity or from intersection of uniform closures alone.

## When is an absolute source-error bound necessary rather than merely sufficient?

AF-558 and RE-247 exhibit **different quantifier orders** for signed error integrals. On a fixed shared-calibration path, Arithmetic Fidelity has `E_AF=int Delta K(v)dv` and `|E_AF|<=int|Delta K|`; cancellations can make a positive variation bound non-necessary. In the AF-543/544 physical curvature decoder, the inverse-sensitivity difference preserves sign and the inequality is equality.

RE-247 instead computes the *supremum* of a positive-kernel terminal Robin discrepancy over all pairs of discrete generalized-prime sources obeying a uniform Chebyshev-error envelope. The sharp modulus is `(2+o(1)) sqrt(p)log(p) exp(-bV(U))/log(U)` for agreement below `e^(-2)U`; discrete envelope-saturating sources realize the absolute-kernel bound. This is not saturation by any *particular* ordinary-prime source.

Consequently the variation bound can be unnecessary for one source but **minimax sharp** across a flexible admissible source class. Which sign-coherent or envelope-extremizing profiles remain possible after imposing the arithmetic source and physical destination restrictions is a distinct, unresolved question; neither integral identifies the other operator nor implies RH.
