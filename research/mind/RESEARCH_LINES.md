## Characterize the complete admissible statistic and its programmable freedom before choosing the repair

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-preserve-structure-before-compressing`.

Arithmetic Fidelity now gives both sides of the audit. A complete test functional can be faithful while finite projections have exact generalized-prime fibers; but singular point isolation is not automatically better, because flexible smooth tests can manufacture an isolated source around any finite nonresonant block. Finite linear constraints may be invisible on the measurement quotient, and even normalized compact double-positive test cones retain full-dimensional finite response jets.

A decisive negative result is a conflicting pair inside one complete admissible fiber/statistic. A decisive positive result must show exact recovery in the declared source/category **and** that the observable class was not tuned to program that recovery around the target. Downstream optimization, diagonalization, positivity, marks, determinants, or infinite numerical precision cannot repair lost information; conversely, a source-tuned singularity is not evidence of intrinsic arithmetic fidelity.

## Distinguish exact relational fidelity from global rational-prime specificity and realizability

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-the-surviving-arithmetic-variable-is-relational`.

Higher-order or marked relational data can be exactly faithful modulo an intended gauge, but local fidelity can still be shared by non-Riemann sources. Prime Lattice makes the intermediate case explicit: pointing breaks the Möbius torus gauge, yet finite-horizon arithmetic is routed into classical additive correlations, short-interval variance, prime-gap density, prolate/Nyquist sampling, or Montgomery pair correlation depending on scale.

A decisive mechanism must pass three gates: prove local relational completeness modulo the intended gauge; prove that the retained structure distinguishes the exact rational-prime norm/completion from matched controls; and prove that the discriminator is usable in the topology/operator category required by the final argument. Provenance, gauge breaking, algebraic recoverability, and a canonical scale are each weaker than specificity plus realization.

## Separate critical-axis generation and RH-equivalent diagnostics from zero selection

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-critical-axis-is-not-zero-selection`.

A distinguished axis, exact finite Euler coefficients, or even an RH-equivalent boundedness criterion is weaker than a new zero-selection mechanism. The half-weight `1/2` is now canonically generated in several unrelated ways: Tate self-duality, universal finite-horizon correlation balance, and the unique isometric normalization of pointed degree-`n` root covers. Prime Lattice globalizations of the finite-horizon branch lead to classical correlation/sampling theories, while Weil Positivity can force the half-weight and `log p` positively yet still lacks the global Weil sign.

The missing theorem is an independent selector or sign estimate derived upstream from rational-prime structure. Repackaging the continued zero divisor into a bounded signal, hull, Koopman system, pair-correlation statistic, or canonically normalized positive carrier is diagnostic/kinematic unless the decisive support or sign theorem is proved without importing the zero locations or classical equivalent it is meant to explain.

## Derive a realization topology in which the selector is continuous and controlled local pieces assemble globally

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-005-topology-is-part-of-the-arithmetic-claim`.

Prime Flute no longer has a known divergent local wave family: all tail short cores are classified and locally summable, cusp/body handoffs are controlled, and constant collar phase is gauge. Its remaining gate is coherent global interface/shape assembly. Weil Inertia has genuine cross-conductor martingale orthogonality but needs a hierarchical/vector-valued estimate that avoids both Wiener `l^1` cost and one giant refinement modulus. Weil Positivity shows a positive topology can genuinely repair selector continuity: the pointed local Dirichlet norm succeeds where every rotation-invariant Hilbert completion fails, but global finite--archimedean sign compatibility remains open.

The program-level question is therefore whether an intrinsic middle category can satisfy **continuity, matched-control separation, and global assembly/sign compatibility simultaneously**. Relative `S_p` (`p>1`), wave/scattering, hierarchical square-function, singular boundary, twisted-determinant, or pointed positive forms remain candidates only after all three gates are proved. A no-go showing that every intrinsic category either identifies the controls, loses continuity, pays an unavoidable assembly cost, or only repackages classical/explicit zero data would be equally decisive.
