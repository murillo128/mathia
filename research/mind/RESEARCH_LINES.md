# Global Mathia research lines

This file holds genuinely cross-line mathematical questions suggested by the durable Mathia intuitions. It is not a summary of the local minds, a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Measure the complexity that survives the exact endpoint quotient, not the complexity visible upstream

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-preserve-structure-before-compressing`, `MI-002-the-surviving-arithmetic-variable-is-relational`, and `MI-007-survival-requires-quantitative-non-escape-not-just-nonzero-structure`.

Several lines now identify the wrong complexity measure by exact counterexample. Arithmetic Fidelity shows that hidden provenance should be treated as a codebook and then quotiented by the actual downstream discriminator: whole-source cardinality can be exponentially larger than endpoint complexity. Analytic Frontier shows the converse phenomenon inside one scalar carrier: support size and maximum multiplicity are not enough; bounded multiplicative occupancy-scale complexity is still controllable, so a surviving scalar obstruction must proliferate across genuinely different scales.

Möbius Cancellation supplies a signed version. The relevant endpoint resource is not shell mass but coherent parity across many degrees: direct truncation, positive diagonal shell norms, and first-order local filters all leave almost-square bulk once signs are discarded. Prime Lattice similarly locates shifted parity outside the subpower local-factor sigma-algebra, while ordinary affine averaging either erases the target, restates the pointwise problem, or already contains the hard zero-frequency cancellation.

The cross-line question is therefore to identify an intrinsic **effective complexity after the declared quotient**: provenance-code rate/overlap, multiplicity-scale complexity, signed degree interaction, parity-sensitive tail coupling, or another source-specific relational resource. Counting coordinates before the consumption map is not enough.

## Separate retention, extractability, activation, and theorem-scale visibility

A resource can survive algebraically and still fail to matter at the final theorem. Weil Inertia now provides the cleanest four-stage example: exceptional geometry can create a coherent reciprocal signal; the rest of the zeta amplitude may cancel it; unit trace can prevent a nonlinear spectral tail from activating; and even a perfectly extracted Maynard--Pratt bow of length `T^epsilon`, `epsilon<1/2`, has vanishing principal support-one form at the global Montgomery normalization.

The same separation appears elsewhere. Prime Flute's entire decoupled short-collar sector reaches weak trace class, so any worse uncut endpoint must enter through reassembly rather than local multiplicity. Xi Flow recovers Xi super-polynomially well on a safe center strip, yet the global Gaussian seam forces a larger derivative bandwidth than the natural zero-count carrier. Visual Exploration can estimate a frozen scalar witness dimension-freely while full-law certification retains a genuine support-dimension cost.

A candidate should therefore state four different obligations when relevant: what is retained, how it is isolated from the complement, whether source constraints let it activate, and whether its normalized size is visible to the theorem actually being used. Passing one gate is not evidence for the others.

## Treat scalar spectral features as controls until matched source classes are classified

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-arithmetic-significance-begins-after-universal-control-subtraction`, `MI-004-critical-axis-is-not-zero-selection`, and `MI-006-classify-the-boundary-model-before-calling-it-arithmetic`.

Prime Circle now exhibits both extremes for canonical reciprocal cyclotomic scalarizations: the intrinsic inversion-even raw completion has strictly positive Fourier spectrum, while minimal renormalization forces real Fourier zeros on every non-prime-power control shell. Zero existence is therefore not a prime-selective signal. Weil Positivity shows an analogous category warning: `det_2` escapes the scalar Schur modulus, yet a regular lossless passive Hilbert--Schmidt boundary still forces one-sided regularized phase motion incompatible with the Gamma phase.

Visual Exploration adds an information-coordinate version. In a hybrid Euler--Hadamard factorization, the complete prime/zero increment field is an invertible shear of one factor plus the explicit residual; nonlinear post-processing of those same fields cannot manufacture an additional interaction channel.

Across these lines, a spectral zero, critical axis, determinant, contrast, or nonlinear visualization becomes arithmetically interesting only after matched controls show that the feature is not forced by endpoint normalization, positivity/passivity, an invertible coordinate change, or another universal boundary mechanism.

## Match local-to-global transport to the exact topology and norm consumed downstream

Prime Flute and Xi Flow now sharpen the same structural warning from different categories. In Prime Flute, local collars are controlled through every `S_r`, `r>1`, and the whole decoupled central family even has weak-`S_1` control; the unresolved difficulty is the topology/cost of gluing them into the uncut operator. In Xi Flow, center-local relative recovery is excellent, but global entire exact repair is rigid and ordinary full-period Sobolev approximation faces a seam bandwidth gap; the unresolved target is the weighted log-Vieta resource, not generic Sobolev closeness.

Prime Lattice gives the observation-side analogue: shrinking phase windows collapse to pointwise evaluation, positive-width bounded-center flattening invokes analytic uniqueness, and broad windows erase bounded targets. The topology/observation regime is part of the claim rather than a technical wrapper.

The next cross-line bridge theorem should therefore be formulated directly in the destination topology or quotient. Proving a stronger but mismatched norm can be impossible for irrelevant reasons; proving a weaker norm can leave the decisive information uncontrolled.

## Demand new coercivity whenever a no-go category is exited

Escaping a closed representation class does not inherit the theorem that motivated the escape. Weil Positivity makes this explicit: regularized determinants may leave Schur boundedness, but passivity no longer supplies the desired Gamma sign and `det_2` still carries a one-sided phase obstruction. Möbius Cancellation has the signed analogue: a higher-order/nonlocal shell filter may leave the diagonal no-go, but it must still prove endpoint-scale cancellation rather than merely avoid a previous lower bound. Xi Flow's approximate finite surrogate likewise must pay transport residual, seam/bandwidth, conditioning, and normalization costs once exact quotient transport is abandoned.

The general research line is to pair every category change with a replacement coercivity/reconstruction theorem stated after the change. “Not covered by the obstruction” is a frontier condition, not positive evidence.
