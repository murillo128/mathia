## Where has the exact obstruction moved?

Arithmetic Fidelity: to **uniform growing-depth conditioning, restricted or structured nuisance classes, other noise/correlation laws, and a rational-prime discriminator that matched generalized-prime controls cannot preserve**. The resource is the target discriminator after nuisance quotient, not raw depth before it.

Farey Discrepancy: to **a source repair whose demand crosses the current capacity/first-row phase boundary by stronger deep arithmetic amplitude, switched-only intermediate motion, or a structural monotone-response upgrade**. The canonical full-grid divisor inverse is now excluded as a source of superlinear depth amplification.

Möbius Cancellation: in the gap branch to **growing rank, a genuinely different source measure/coordinate system, absolute scale or discontinuous arithmetic data**; in the q-vdC branch to **structured pre-recombination source coupling**, because synchronized scalar recombination and final fixed-lcm beta-sieve signs are power/coherence dead ends.

Nyman--Beurling: simultaneously to **the established NB-272 section-defect/anchor-mismatch fork** and, on the source-native route, to **the inverse conditioning of the Hardy zero-evaluation Gram `G_(jk)=1/(rho_j+conj(rho_k)-1)` at Ford scale, or a new boundary/nonlinear mechanism not equivalent to the same finite point-evaluation channel**. The finite contour itself is no longer the missing bridge.

Prime Circle: to **the residual beyond the universal subcritical Buchstab--Riesz macroscopic mode**—centered fluctuations, mesoscopic/arithmetic scales or cross-level information—not merely to `alpha<1/2`.

Prime Lattice: to **the PL-407-scale signed mod-5 supertrace with its off-diagonal coherences retained, plus either an arithmetic restricted-cone theorem for exact local-factor deletion or a genuinely different divisor-faithful spectral attachment**.

Visual Exploration: to **gap-resolved noncommuting orientation whose cost survives the complete relative-metric accounting**.

Weil Inertia: to **an unconditional/equivalence-grade mechanism beyond logarithmic compactness that is also sensitive to sparse off-line defects, rather than only to collective on-line sampling density**.

Weil Positivity: to **a source-forced relation nonlocal under continuous hereditary source cutoff, or a genuinely different nonlinear/singular/unbounded category**.

## Which physical cost survives after representation artifacts are removed?

The newest results sharpen the common order of proof. AF-490 shows that a nominally large sensitivity can disappear exactly when a nuisance symmetry is quotiented out; only transverse spread is a resource. FD-262 shows that repeated divisor inversion carries only a divisor-average `D log D` depth cost after arithmetic forcing is separated, so apparent triangular depth is not automatically amplification. MC-451 shows that nominal Möbius signs can become maximally coherent after the destination quotient. NB-279 shows that an exact boundary residue can classicalize completely into the inverse conditioning of a target-side reproducing-kernel Gram once the singular dual is priced. PC-398 shows that even a large surviving operator sector can classicalize under matched continuum controls. PL-420 shows that restoring lost coherence is not enough when the exact arithmetic inverse destroys ambient matrix positivity. VIS-373 prices noncommuting orientation by the spectral gaps it crosses. WI-392 shows that an order-one collective floor can coexist with vanishing sensitivity to each sparse defect. WP-401 shows that moving from von Neumann to C-star source topology does not create linear orientation when continuous source cutoffs still generate norm-dense two-sided tangents.

[MI-034](intuition/MI-034-positivity-is-informative-only-when-the-induced-constraint-remains-proper-after-the-destination-quotient.md) records the strengthened cross-line positivity audit.
