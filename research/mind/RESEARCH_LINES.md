# Global mathematical questions

## Which source relation cannot be repaired by moving the cost elsewhere?

[MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md) separates several different failure modes of nominally rich source interfaces.

Farey Discrepancy distinguishes **relation coercivity from observability of that relation**. FD-181 proves that every squarefree-supported perturbation of Möbius with changed-coordinate support `o(x/log x)` pays prime-extension multiplicativity energy at scale `x/log x`. FD-182 then composes that coercive theorem with the permanent FD-179 dyadic-null source: one fixed non-Möbius source matches the complete dyadic midpoint ladder exactly while retaining

`M_(a,q)(x)=(C+o(1)) x/log x`

with `C>0`. The sparse relation is expensive, but those observations do not expose it below the critical scale. A source-native bridge must therefore enrich the Farey observation family or restrict the source class; sharpening an inequality on the same exact-null midpoint data cannot work.

Nyman--Beurling shows how a locality cost can be absent at fixed resolution yet explode in a coupled limit. NB-190 prices a normalized Euler-band probe by inverse source mass in scalar-translate variation. NB-191 shows that at fixed band width this cost can eventually be transplanted into every high dyadic block by common Bohr recurrence without an additional asymptotic surcharge. NB-192 reveals the hidden nonuniformity: one fixed log-width band contains `m_a~a exp(r_0/a)` rationally independent prime-log phases, forcing coordinatewise syndetic common returns to satisfy `log L_a >> a exp(r_0/a)`. NB-193 closes the direct positive weighted relaxation: the true-source-weighted `ell^1` return set has exponentially small Haar volume in effective dimension `D_eff=1/sum w_j^2`, and the true prime weights satisfy `D_eff asymp m_a`. The same double-exponential worst-gap scale therefore survives aggregate positive recurrence. A specific block may still contain an unusually early return, and signed aggregation or a different synthesis topology may avoid this recurrence architecture; those are now the live acquisition questions.

Robin Extremal supplies a stable-rank analogue. High factorial-normalized Euler jets approach the common net-multiplicity mode; an `o(1)` spectral neighborhood of `s=1` becomes rank one on a fixed selector shell; and a fixed constant-distance interval is factorially compressible. RE-192 identifies the dimensionless mixed-scale variable: for spectral span `U` and source interval `[Q_-,Q_+]`, the normalized Euler channel has rank-`m` error at most

`[U log(Q_+/Q_-)]^m/m! + O(Q_-^-1)`.

Thus even polynomial source separation can have bounded stable rank when the spectral window shrinks inversely with logarithmic source width. Exact continuum data may remain analytically source-rigid while the number of robust coordinates at fixed precision is controlled by the transform rectangle `R=U log(Q_+/Q_-)`, not by sample or source cardinality separately.

Möbius Cancellation shows that source information can move inside a statistical architecture without becoming free. MC-356 prices finite-dimensional regular exponential-family channels by integrated Fisher action. MC-357 moves the tariff into the selector for fixed-component mixtures. MC-358 now gives the exact generic latent/component split for a declared architecture:

`b_e(Y) <= (1/2)J(Q_x,Q_x') = b_e(Theta)+c_e(K)`.

The endpoint lower tariff therefore survives whether source dependence is carried by the latent law or by the conditional components. Exact neighboring transcript singularity forces singularity somewhere in the lifted architecture; in a finite categorical mixture every shared positive-weight label must carry singular paired components. The remaining problem is no longer to name a more complicated generic mixture family, but to expose the actual Möbius-derived lifted source graph and prove an arithmetic upper bound on its adjacent divergence, or identify the singular/high-divergence path that genuinely pays the resource.

The common discriminator is not raw dimension, exactness or the mere existence of a source tariff. It is a **destination-relevant quantitative resource together with an observation/acquisition theorem that actually exposes it under the declared source class and coupled scaling**.

## Which physical source scale and representation gauge does the theorem actually control?

Arithmetic Fidelity has two independent scale ledgers. On the sampling side, uniform short-interval PNT gives all-prime-start conversion for `h_N=p_N^(-beta)` throughout `beta<13/30`, while exceptional-set inertia plus Brun--Titchmarsh packing transfers a stronger almost-all ambient theorem to relative density one of prime starts through every `beta<13/15`. Those thresholds measure theorem uniformity and sampling geometry, not an intrinsic detector constant.

On the finite-order analytic side, AF-403--AF-405 turn strict translation positivity into a recursive confluent/Toda hierarchy. The full Euler-log profile has `H_1,H_2,H_3,H_4>0` globally, hence strict total positivity through order four. AF-217 still forces eventual failure, so the first possible bad order is at least five and the next local gate is `(log R_4)''<4q`.

AF-406 adds a separate fidelity boundary. For `xi_1(z)=xi(sqrt(z)+1/2)`, every fixed finite Pólya-frequency order can be forced by a sufficiently large zero-free exponential multiplier `e^{nz}`, while the complete zero divisor is unchanged. Finite positivity is therefore not an intrinsic observable of the zero-divisor quotient. Continuing the Toda hierarchy remains meaningful kernel analysis, but a finite positivity certificate becomes zero-set evidence only after the zero-preserving gauge is fixed canonically or the certificate is replaced by a gauge-invariant datum. This is the same source-identification/zero-selection separation captured by [MI-019](intuition/MI-019-source-identification-and-zero-selection-are-orthogonal-gates.md), now with an explicit positivity-gauge example.

Visual Exploration adds a normalization audit to physical-scale bookkeeping. VIS-272 gives the exact Green-potential dictionary and VIS-274 carries the concentrating main term through the fixed-source `1/log T` pullback. VIS-275 shows why a sign-blind absolute remainder cannot benefit from extra signed packet moments. VIS-276--VIS-277 then establish that the apparent signed remainder mean is partly a classical gamma-factor convention, not an invariant source residual.

VIS-278 closes the first normalization-invariant pure source-energy question. With

`R_x(t)=x(B_x(t)+E_x(t))-log(t/(2*pi))`,

one has `R_x(t)=zeta'/zeta(3/2-it)+O_K(t^-1)`, asymptotically zero mean, and

`(1/H) int |R_x(t)|^2 dt = C_Lambda+o_K(1)`,

`C_Lambda=sum_p (log p)^2/(p^3-1)>0`.

The gamma-normalized source residual therefore has a classical nonzero variance floor, but its standalone fixed-source packet contribution is only `L^(-2)`. The still larger `L^(-3/2)` theorem-level frontier must arise in another interaction sector or in a coupling to the source. The next theorem must locate that sector before the current proof destroys its sign.

Robin's `R=U log(Q_+/Q_-)` law gives the dual observation-window version of the same discipline. The theorem must control the actual physical source window, the observation window must resolve the claimed degrees of freedom at the available precision, and nuisance/gauge normalization must be fixed before a residual scale or positivity certificate is interpreted.

## Which quantitative resource survives a thin or moving destination?

Prime Lattice has moved beyond both the local activation-birth gate and the positive-tail dilution gate. PL-338 shows that every finite prime-power activation is first-order flat and is born with favorable reflected sign whenever the transported boundary trace is nonzero. PL-339 computes the exact path-chain gap of each compressed shift and shows that the outer logarithmic half-window carries asymptotically all weighted von-Mangoldt mass with unit gap. Consequently

`J_a >= (1-o(1)) W_a I`.

Accumulating active channels therefore does not soften the favorable positive jump bulk relative to total source mass. But the exact prime block remains `P_a=J_a-2W_aI`; the negative scalar compensation survives at the same scale. The frontier is now global branch persistence/noncollapse and a rational-prime-specific control of compensation/completion or another mesoscopic source law beyond the PNT-universal bulk.

Nyman--Beurling sharpens what “locality” means in a moving destination. Fixed-`a` recurrence eventually reaches arbitrary height, but NB-192--NB-193 show that both coordinatewise and natural positive weighted recurrence have double-exponential worst-gap inclusion length in `1/a`. Thus locality becomes a real resource only when the recurrence/acquisition scale is quantitatively coupled to the destination. A more efficient signed, block-specific or non-recurrence mechanism remains possible; the generic positive syndetic route cannot simply be extrapolated into the shrinking-width regime.

Xi Flow remains a useful control in the opposite direction: once a consecutive packet is localized, its local holomorphic inversion is well conditioned. Its unresolved cost is global support discovery and assembly rather than local packet inversion. Across these lines, the moving destination is governed by the resource that scales with localization, recurrence, support discovery, stable transform width or branch transport—not by the mere existence of a local inverse or positive bulk.

Prime Flute reinforces the complementary warning. More endpoint counting cannot repair a representation whose local operator already has infinitely many order-one canonical correlations; the split or invariant itself must change before the global trace-ideal question becomes meaningful.
