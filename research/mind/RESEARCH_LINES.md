## What counts as a genuine representation escape?

The adversarial pattern is now stricter than “changing representation may preserve information.” Prime Circle shows that not only ordered higher-dimensional signature packets but also their canonical cyclotomic descendant refinements can be deterministic functionals of already decoded one-shell data. Nyman--Beurling now supplies a sharper positive/negative split: the scalar sample/pivot data are reproducible by controls, a genuine matrix-valued dilation-band cocycle survives, and NB-293 gives its exact target readout; what remains unproved is arithmetic occupation by the moving transformed target, so matrix existence or size alone still does not buy coercivity. Visual Exploration separates low-dimensional collapse from higher-dimensional capacity: qubits have no independent normalized spectral-shape channel, while rank-one qutrits do, but that algebraic room is generic and is not evidence of source-specific Wang coupling. Arithmetic Fidelity shows that restoring a discarded nuisance fibre is harmless exactly at one critical Hilbert truncation yet necessarily reintroduces noncompactness immediately above it, while AF-505 shows that even the exact critical compactness data do not decide whether the asymptotic threshold is ever attained. Weil Positivity separates source provenance from readout visibility twice: the native critical source lacks the desired scalar holonomy, and even externally supplied scalar mixed-prime projective curvature is useless to the canonical divisibility Gram if the readout quotients it away. Prime Lattice shows both a coefficient-field information barrier—real scalar probes miss imaginary coherences—and a separate theorem-strength barrier when complex matrix recovery is available only under assumptions as strong as the target. Weil Inertia adds a globalization barrier: a locally exact critical signed defect charge does not become a global coercive statistic until summability or a renormalization law preserving the defect term is proved.

The durable proof order is therefore: determine the actual source image and exact nuisance/gauge quotient; audit which states or fibres become admissible after that quotient and at which truncation radius; prove that the temporal/contact witness required by an equality statement actually exists; locate the carrier that survives continuation, aggregation, de-aliasing, neutralization or completion; quotient residual presentation scale and universal baselines; determine whether the carrier has genuine algebraic capacity beyond those baselines; determine the coefficient field, metric, interaction law or positive observable actually visible to the destination; test whether the surviving datum is genuinely present in the canonical source and absent from matched controls; distinguish existence of a carrier from source provenance, readout visibility, target occupation/coercivity, summability/globalization and theorem-hypothesis strength; and only then price amplitude, density, conditioning, phase coherence, matrix tomography, spectral margin, commutator occupation or tangent asymmetry. A substantive escape needs a source-specific coupling or invariant that survives every destination reduction **and** is established from premises weaker than the conclusion it is meant to prove.
