## Measure only structure that survives the final quotient, elimination, and matched controls

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-preserve-structure-before-compressing`, `MI-002-the-surviving-arithmetic-variable-is-relational`, `MI-003-arithmetic-significance-begins-after-universal-control-subtraction`, `MI-007-survival-requires-quantitative-non-escape-not-just-nonzero-structure`.

The common surviving requirement is therefore stronger than “retain more structure.” A candidate relation must remain nonprogrammable after the exact quotient, support compression, variational elimination, equilibrium sector, or local-block factorization actually consumed by the destination theorem.

## Test coercivity after source optimization and downstream shorting, not on a convenient raw representative

**Linked intuition:** `MI-008-exact-recovery-is-not-stable-transport`.

The next theorem should control the optimized/shorted object itself, not a raw representative whose favorable mass can be rearranged away.
