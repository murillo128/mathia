## What counts as a genuine representation escape?

A recurring adversarial pattern is now visible. Changing representation can preserve the exact same information budget: Prime Circle changes Hilbert to Krein while retaining universal multiplication support; Weil Inertia changes scalar to matrix without removing the logarithmic budget and, within the audited Widder framework, converts strip width to an exactly invertible root profile; Weil Positivity moves one-sided analytic data into a positive linking matrix whose existence is universal. These are legitimate category changes, but they are not yet mathematical leverage because they add no independent source-selective constraint.

The durable proof order is therefore: determine the actual source image and nuisance quotient; locate the carrier that survives continuation, aggregation, de-aliasing, neutralization or completion; restrict to directions the destination can physically resolve; test the resulting statistic against matched controls; and only then assign exponents to amplitude, density, conditioning, phase coherence, matrix tomography, spectral margin, commutator occupation or tangent asymmetry. An escape is substantive only when a source-specific datum survives every subsequent reduction needed by the destination.
