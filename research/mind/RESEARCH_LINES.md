## Which physical cost survives after representation artifacts are removed?

The newest results reinforce that the surviving currency is usually weighted. Farey weighs exact defects by the weighted incidence inverse `kappa(D)`, not merely count, location or chain depth. Möbius shows that sparse relational description must be quotiented by its actual automorphism group before information cost is inferred. Arithmetic Fidelity prices scalar metric repair by singular local amplification and full-diameter complete alternation. These are not one theorem and their weights are not numerically interchangeable. The common discipline is to remove coordinate artifacts, symmetry, irrelevant labels and relaxed supersets first, then derive the quantitative budget in the exact geometry consumed by the destination. A resource should be credited only if that post-quotient budget still survives the final map and still distinguishes the arithmetic source from its matched controls.
