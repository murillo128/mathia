## When does an escape from one obstruction survive the next reduction?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit, while [MI-030](intuition/MI-030-effective-complexity-is-a-geometry-weighted-budget.md) sharpens the resource side: after the representation is fixed, nominal counts must be replaced by the scale-, symmetry- and geometry-weighted budget actually visible to the destination.

Arithmetic Fidelity now has two scalar-repair gates. AF-468 shows that every bounded-distortion scalar Hilbert repair of unrestricted TV mixtures must amplify small distances at least at square-root scale; AF-469 shows that exact radial repair additionally forces local Bernstein alternating finite differences for the squared transform. The surviving exits are proved source restrictions, non-radial source-forced structure, or a downstream theorem genuinely compatible with the singularly changed metric.

Farey Discrepancy has moved beyond both defect cardinality and absolute location. FD-246 prices exact omissions by harmonic divisor footprint, while FD-247 shows that even low-scale omissions have only sharp linear `NK` cost when the active increments form a divisibility antichain; every single multiplicative octave is such a regime. The exact frontier is now cross-scale divisibility incidence in `supp(Delta Y)`, with physical source transversality still separate.

Möbius Cancellation now has an entropy law for the exact-factor escape. MC-431--MC-432 show that quotient factor capacity is governed by `D=log(r!/prod r_a!)`; any subpolynomial effective prime alphabet remains subpolynomial on the primorial scale, while retaining a polynomial Bell exponent requires polynomial prime distinguishability. The remaining question is whether such high-entropy relational information is canonical and analytically useful before Möbius projection.

Nyman--Beurling now has both sides of a rank/coverage quantifier boundary. NB-263 shows any positive-depth strip chosen before rank can be flattened at vanishing resolved Hilbert cost if conditioning is free. NB-264 shows bounded-cost suppression cannot cover any fixed-power carrier-locked radius `K^delta`: exact mesh reconstruction recovers the Euler anchor and forces aggregate response `Omega(m)`. The unresolved transition is subpolynomial in rank, with variation and the exact Hardy projection still unpriced.

Prime Circle has closed the fixed positive inverse-power common-anchor modulus across exact-mesh and submesh scaling. PC-384 gives QMF or wrapped Student-t limits at mesh scale; PC-385 shows submesh behavior is only universal uniformization, anchor spike/divergence or concentration. Prime and composite degrees remain matched throughout. Remaining routes must retain arithmetic information before this positive scalar pushforward.

Prime Lattice now has an explicit source-precision curve. PL-407 shows that an LPZ-shaped variance saving exponent `mu` preserves the cubic `H^-7/4` layer at `H=X^theta` when `mu>3theta/(4(1-theta))`; square-root relative saving reaches `theta<2/5`. The alternative is to exploit the sign of the cubic kernel directly rather than pay the absolute-value tariff.

Visual Exploration has priced both bounded intra-cluster and inter-cluster cancellation. VIS-352 charges a fixed confluent cluster by `delta^(N-1)`; VIS-353 gives a uniform Riesz floor for finitely many already-separated bounded clusters. The remaining finite-bank question is the actual source-to-Wang-coefficient map or a genuinely growing/hierarchical geometric resource.

Weil Inertia and Weil Positivity both sharpen “more structure” into a restricted operator question. WI-379 shows inherited arithmetic translations have extensive rank even on source-zero cells, so source-rank elimination cannot kill that channel; what is missing is a sign/coercivity estimate for their signed sum on the gamma-negative band. WP-390 shows every stationary positive interface form is merely a nonnegative rational-frequency mask, so exact mixed-shell deletion explicitly programs prime-power support; the surviving route must be nonstationary, prelimit or globally coupled.

## Which physical cost survives after representation artifacts are removed?

The newest results reinforce that the surviving currency is usually weighted. Farey weighs omissions by divisor reach and then by actual divisibility incidence. Möbius weighs prime types by multinomial entropy. Visual Exploration weighs collisions by physical diameter and confluent multiplicity. Arithmetic Fidelity weighs metric repair by its local amplification and exact finite-difference laws. Prime Lattice weighs source precision by coupled source/shift exponents. Nyman--Beurling shows that the order of quantifiers between coverage and rank changes the answer: preassigned strips are cheap in resolved Hilbert norm, whereas fixed-power rank-coupled carrier-locked coverage is coercive.

These are not one theorem and their weights are not numerically interchangeable. The common discipline is to remove coordinate artifacts, symmetry, irrelevant labels and relaxed supersets first, then derive the quantitative budget in the exact geometry consumed by the destination. A resource should be credited only if that post-quotient budget still survives the final map and still distinguishes the arithmetic source from its matched controls.
