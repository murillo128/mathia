## Characterize complete support, admissible interaction closure, target transport, and intrinsic robust distance before choosing a repair

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-preserve-structure-before-compressing` and `MI-005-topology-is-part-of-the-arithmetic-claim`.

Arithmetic Fidelity now supplies exact models for support loss, interaction closure, collective instability, perturbative fragility, and repair geometry. AF-046--AF-051 show that law separation can have positive TV margin while zero-error support fidelity has zero TV robustness, different divergences induce different exact repair problems, and a reversible re-encoding can change an exact repair cost.

AF-052--AF-057 make the gate sharper. Blackwell-reversible, TV-isometric refinement can reduce distance to an unrestricted structural target because the refined target contains non-descending states; AF-054 gives the exact maximal safe target envelope that preserves every source repair radius. Important Hilbert cases reduce to coapproximation/convex-roof geometry, while equivalent product norms can disagree on whether finite safe lifts exist. Thus harmless source equivalence, quantitative metric, and target transport must all be declared before a repair radius is interpreted.

A decisive negative is a conflicting pair inside the **complete** statistic, a vanishing distance-to-loss margin in the intended completion, or an allowed refinement that changes the target outside its safe envelope. A decisive positive must prove target recovery and stable realization in a metric/category whose equivalences and target transport are specified independently of the desired conclusion.

A decisive mechanism must pass four gates: prove joint relational completeness modulo the intended gauge; prove that the retained structure distinguishes the exact rational-prime source from matched controls; realize that discriminator stably in the required metric/operator category; and show that the global completion preserves it while supplying the needed sign, continuation, or spectral theorem.

## Separate critical-axis generation, qualitative pseudorandomness, and RH-equivalent readouts from zero selection

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-critical-axis-is-not-zero-selection`.

A distinguished axis, exact finite Euler coefficient, RH-like determinant theorem, physical zero readout, or strong qualitative pseudorandomness is weaker than a new zero-selection mechanism. Prime Lattice gives both sides of the warning: homogeneous local geometry produces `Re(s)=1/2` with exact graph RH theorems in generic Ramanujan families, while PL-106 shows that a `log n` Hamiltonian plus a chosen diagonal probe can encode the eta/zeta divisor or any other unimodular Dirichlet polynomial.

Möbius Cancellation gives the quantitative analogue. Prime-power enrichment naturally reaches the square-root boundary but does not create cancellation there; qualitative Chowla, multiplicativity, exact support, and currently available averaged correlations do not carry a polynomial anchored-sum budget. MC-008 even gives an RH-equivalent comparator criterion with a nonmasking auxiliary factor despite failure of absolute inversion, emphasizing that equivalence/readout is not an upstream estimate.

The missing theorem is an independent selector, sign, or quantitative transfer estimate derived from rational-prime structure before the target divisor is used. Repackaging the continued zero set into a bounded signal, universal half-axis, graph RH, programmable DQPT, Poisson bulk statistic, or comparator criterion remains diagnostic/kinematic unless its decisive source theorem is proved independently.

A no-go showing that every intrinsic category fails at least one of these gates would be equally decisive.

## Classify the universal boundary model and its information budget before using a singular, limiting, finite-window, or refinement escape

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-006-classify-the-boundary-model-before-calling-it-arithmetic` and `MI-005-topology-is-part-of-the-arithmetic-claim`.

Several current escapes live exactly where a simpler category breaks: harmless representation refinement, prime-square transfer, large-conductor Hardy limits, `S_2` versus `S_1` Gram topology, finite-window Ramanujan truncation, mixed-prime positive completion, relative Selberg subtraction, or local-to-global correlation transfer. The newer evidence shows that these boundaries often carry their own exact model — safe target envelopes, square-convolution thresholds, Hilbert/Carleman/Gaussian limits, finite cyclotomic spectralization, prolate sampling plus Poisson bulk, `N`-coordinate alias quotients with bounded metric overlap, one-ended plus bottom-Ruelle Selberg layers, or correlation-robust Fisher divergence.

A decisive positive must identify a source-specific residual **after** that boundary model and its information budget are removed or matched, then prove that the residual survives in the same category. A decisive negative is a theorem that the entire boundary escape factors through the universal model. Merely observing noncompactness, singularity, stronger-norm failure, a new regularized object, physical zero readout, or strong local cancellation is no longer enough.
