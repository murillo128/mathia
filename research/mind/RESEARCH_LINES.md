# Global mathematical questions

## When does repeated observation actually shrink the admissible source fibre?

[MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md) and [MI-025](intuition/MI-025-combinatorial-breadth-must-be-priced-through-the-source-coordinate-map.md) now carry two complementary audits: information cost is relative to the declared source class, acquisition topology and destination quotient, while observation breadth is meaningful only after mapping it into the theorem's physical source coordinate at the scale actually being probed.

Arithmetic Fidelity makes the scale issue exact. AF-394 identifies the smooth order-three translation-kernel target and AF-395--AF-399 separate its intrinsic physical topology wall from the successor depth used to realize that wall on a source mesh. AF-400 closes an important extrapolation loophole: ordinary regular variation controls fixed physical reach but does not determine arbitrary shrinking reach. A moving wall `h_N->0` needs a source-density theorem valid at that same scale; the sufficient local law `x f'(x)/f(x)->rho` gives `B_N(h_N)~Nh_N/rho` when `Nh_N->infinity`. Successor count is therefore never an intrinsic locality resource by itself.

Farey Discrepancy sharpens the source-class side. Sparse geometric midpoint samples are target-rigid inside the bounded squarefree multiplicative cone, but FD-175--FD-176 construct permanent null sources after full multiplicativity is removed even while preserving all prime coefficients and a growing low-rank cone `omega(n)<=rho log n/loglog n`. The load-bearing resource is not coefficient fidelity by itself but cross-coordinate compatibility that prevents fresh high-rank shells from repairing each new observation row. The live boundary is the sharp growing-rank/product-compatibility threshold and the quantitative certification scale inside the genuinely multiplicative cone.

Robin Extremal gives the complementary interpolation warning. Honest `{0,1,2}` ordinary-prime controls survive every fixed finite Euler panel, then a growing jet family. RE-185 shows that the earlier `K log K` restriction was a coordinate-conditioning artifact: a normalized Chebyshev shell basis pushes exact matched non-rigidity to `K=Theta(V(p))`, where `V(p)=(log p)^(3/5)/(loglog p)^(1/5)`. The remaining scalar boundary is the gap between the KV shell scale and the larger raw jet-capacity scale `log p/loglog p`, not finite versus growing dimension.

Together these results say that observation count, scalar depth and coefficient accuracy become mathematical resources only after the admissible source fibre and the physical source map are fixed. A useful destination theorem must identify a relation that matched controls cannot keep repairing, not merely accumulate more coordinates.

The common question is therefore not “how many measurements?” but **which source-native quantitative resource survives exactly where the destination becomes thin or moves**: state boundedness, amplitude-balanced rank, scale-matched source density, or globally discoverable holomorphic support.

The finite-source question is therefore no longer whether extensive discrimination must be paid. ## Global discriminator: identify the exact resource a matched control cannot fake

For each proposed route, first state the source class, exact observation/target quotient, physical source-coordinate map, acquisition norm and destination. Then ask which source directions the destination actually distinguishes; whether changing horizons, heights or operators supplies new source coordinates or only reparameterizes one cell; whether the theorem is uniform at the moving scale being probed; whether amplitudes and conditioning are retained rather than normalized away; whether a source constraint couples coordinates strongly enough to block fresh-shell repair; whether a lower-bound tariff remains unaffordable after the representation is optimally conditioned; whether positive or self-adjoint structure still permits an internal divisor-changing matched control; and whether the final survivor is tied to a zero/sign theorem rather than merely reconstructing the arithmetic source.

A useful bridge should fail on a matched control for an independently arithmetic reason. Exact reconstruction, high nominal rank, dense phase families, smooth source laws, positive operator geometry and growing scalar depth are intermediate resources. The program advances only when one of them becomes **destination-selective under the same source fibre, coordinate map, conditioning and control class that the claimed RH mechanism actually uses**.
