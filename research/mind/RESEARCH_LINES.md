# Global mathematical questions

## Which source resources reach the destination before stable observation turns into generic decoding?

[MI-007](intuition/MI-007-survival-requires-quantitative-non-escape-not-just-nonzero-structure.md), [MI-008](intuition/MI-008-exact-recovery-is-not-stable-transport.md), [MI-012](intuition/MI-012-information-content-and-destination-sensitivity-are-separate-resources.md) and [MI-013](intuition/MI-013-closing-the-bulk-often-leaves-a-thin-source-specific-endpoint.md) separate information survival from the modulus and category actually consumed by the target.

Prime Circle now closes the first unresolved mesh-scale linear harmonic escape. PC-309 shows collapse throughout every transport-stable pre-mesh regime; PC-310 shows that at `1-r=Theta(1/q)` the sampled Poisson/radial-flux field becomes an invertible cyclic multiplier with condition number `Theta(q)`. The representation becomes lossless exactly when it pays enough sensitivity to decode the finite source. A surviving route must add structure beyond a universal one-conductor decoder and show why that additional structure reaches a zero-sensitive destination.

Prime Lattice moves in the opposite direction: it finds a structural state--source identity that reduces the required endpoint information. PL-320 shows that convergence of the full source trace plus the local singular remainder forces both the `Q^(-1/2)` coefficient and `Qm(Q)->0`. The remaining analytic gap is not another generic forcing norm but absolute pre-diagonal control, or a transfer theorem that tolerates a conditionally convergent residual moment.

Arithmetic Fidelity retains the complementary target-relative cost picture: exact principal-part survival can coexist with exponential recovery cost for the declared target. Across these lines, the key quantity is the **destination margin obtained before observation crosses into unstable inversion, full source reconstruction or a matched-control category**.

## Which local source laws beat the exceptional geometry allowed by global population control?

[MI-017](intuition/MI-017-sparse-exceptional-geometry-survives-upper-population-control.md) now has corrected boundaries in Nyman--Beurling and a stronger matched obstruction in Robin Extremal.

NB-144 supplies an actual Euler-product Poisson occupancy budget. NB-146 shows that this budget is minimax inside the full positive exterior-sampling cone for packets separated from the critical line, while a self-dual line-centered packet has a different sharp coefficient because reflection no longer supplies a distinct free partner. The fixed-interior route therefore needs a new source resource, not more positive samples, and any approach to the critical line must preserve reflection-orbit bookkeeping.

RE-149 shows that the reciprocal-box visibility loss is already exponentially sharp for **pure vertical escape with zero horizontal spread**. A matched finite-edge spectrum can hide a unique scale-maximal target by phase geometry alone while maintaining sparse one-level counts. Thus better horizontal localization or global density cannot close the remaining Robin branch without an actual-zeta theorem on vertical occupancy/phase structure. The separate local-vs-right-shell exponent mismatch from RE-148 remains.

PL-320 gives a third model: rather than improve a population law, an exact Volterra identity changes the endpoint gate itself. This is the useful alternative when source and destination rates cannot be made to line up by stronger counting.

## Which growing source law is informative rather than merely connected, high-dimensional or reconstructive?

Farey Discrepancy now has an exact binary answer before the observation-count gate. FD-147 identifies finite-`L^q` ancestry recovery with an anchored Cheeger ratio: `C^(bin)_(q,D)=h_D^(-1/q)`. Connectivity is only the zero-boundary endpoint. Under uniform full-ancestry weights every fixed finite seed has a complement cut of normalized mass `Theta(1/(log T log log T))`, so its best binary coercivity constant diverges at least like `(log T log log T)^(1/q)`. Only after an observation geometry supplies uniform cut exposure does FD-144's sublinear-rank information obstruction become the next gate.

Möbius Cancellation still shows the opposite generic-coding boundary: the relevant binary code endpoints are realizable abstractly, so the next theorem must constrain arithmetic realizability rather than repackage dimension/rank. Prime Circle adds the stable-decoding warning above. “Number of observations” is not one scalar resource; anchor exposure, topology, precision, invertibility and matched-control realizability have to be priced separately.

## Which representation-faithful null remains after nuisance variables are quotiented out?

[MI-018](intuition/MI-018-canonical-normalization-can-universalize-the-observable-while-moving-all-source-specificity-into-an-induced-law.md) now has closed calibration on the Visual side and a sharper induced-law matched-control obstruction on the Weil-positivity side.

VIS-242 reduces the remaining symmetric-Dirichlet tent variance exactly to cyclic overlap classes and at `alpha=1` gives a closed finite-sample polynomial variance. Once the dependence parameter and endpoint semantics are fixed independently, the calibration is no longer an unknown high-dimensional covariance problem. A surviving prime residual is therefore a new point-process/source question rather than a variance-computation artifact.

WP-319 shows that source-specific compactness is not enough by itself. Gap-adapted Mangoldt derivative-biased laws are tight and have explicit inverse-square shifted-lattice subsequential limits with quantized local prime-power coefficients. Yet every such gap-supported probability law has a non-arithmetic positive Herglotz matched control with the identical normalized Julia profile. The arithmetic fingerprint survives normalization, but the scalar positivity theorem still does not use it.

The common discriminator is whether the **induced law itself is constrained in a way the matched-control class cannot reproduce**, after every declared nuisance quotient has been applied.

## Which noncompact source channels must survive a positivity reduction?

Weil Inertia sharpens its tail-retention question. WI-304 shows that an individually isolatable omitted prime-power atom creates an essential numerical-range band `[beta-w_q,beta+w_q]`, with `w_q=(Lambda(q)/sqrt(q))(1-rho_chi(log q))`. If `w_q>beta`, compact perturbations cannot restore positivity. The relevant tail requirement is therefore channel-wise: retain or otherwise neutralize every active atom whose defect weight exceeds the high-frequency reserve. This does not require literal retention of the whole infinite tail and does not validate the unreplayed `17/16` certificate.

The later Weil-form gate remains signed density-subtracted discrepancy, not merely positive retained mass: WI-241's pole/main-density cancellation still has to be respected.

## Global discriminator: identify the exact resource that the matched control cannot fake

The newest results make several old slogans precise. FD-147 replaces “connected ancestry” by an exact cut-exposure constant. PC-310 replaces “mesh-scale geometry might survive” by an explicit invertible but generic cyclic decoder. NB-146 replaces a falsely continuous endpoint coefficient by reflection-orbit accounting. PL-320 replaces separate coefficient and moving-tail assumptions by one convergent singular-remainder trace. RE-149 shows that exponential local loss survives even after horizontal spread is removed. VIS-242 converts the dependence-null variance into an exact finite overlap computation. WI-304 identifies compact-invariant omitted-channel bands. WP-319 exhibits a genuine arithmetic induced law whose scalar sign is nevertheless perfectly matched by a non-arithmetic source.

For every proposed route ask: **what exact object reaches the destination; under which norm, scale and quantifier; what gauge/nuisance/universal component has been removed; what source-specific variable remains; can the matched-control class realize that same variable or law; and does the source gain dominate the destination loss without reconstructing the endpoint?** A new representation is useful only when one of those answers changes mathematically.
