The live global question is therefore where a genuinely source-forced interaction can be formed **before** these quotients, direct sums, or scalar normalizers lose the mixed arithmetic needed by the final sign.

The common task is to prove that every compression, transport, or functional calculus preserves exactly the target-bearing state and its quantitative margin -- no more and no less.

## Turn classified source boundaries into cofinal quantitative defects

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-006-classify-the-boundary-model-before-calling-it-arithmetic`, `MI-007-survival-requires-quantitative-non-escape-not-just-nonzero-structure`.

Farey Discrepancy has moved past the linear-prefix angle conjecture: absolute tail mismatch can be diluted by unconditionally superlinear Schur energy, but square-divisor modes force a positive normalized defect on average along every `4`-adic chain. The next step is a pointwise/cofinal projective gap or a multiscale mechanism that prevents sparse saturation between those forced defects.

Arithmetic Fidelity now classifies finite affine phase constraints by a fiber-collapse exponent. Homogeneous relations and nondegenerate affine motion leave destructive phases thin; positive-measure failure requires exponential collapse of the whole restricted fiber along the quotient orbit. Robin Extremal gives a complementary warning: not only one dominant off-critical pair but every finite nonzero same-real-part dominant packet admits infinitely many phases realizing the threshold race cone. The obstruction must therefore come from the CA selector, quantitative selector/spectral resolution, or genuinely non-finite spectral structure rather than finite coefficient signs. Weil Inertia prices its spacing escape: once selected amplitude exceeds the near-edge reservoir, local spacing surplus must beat every fixed Korobov--Vinogradov correction at the natural power threshold.

Across these lines the useful theorem is no longer generic cancellation or rarity. It is a source-specific **cofinal non-escape statement** on the exact normalized defect selected by the construction.

## Preserve full sign/form information when aggregate moments are provably blind

**Linked intuition:** `MI-007-survival-requires-quantitative-non-escape-not-just-nonzero-structure`.

Prime Lattice now separates three levels of Weil observability. First/second moments of a source-forced Hermitian compression localize a positive proportion of zeros but are insensitive to `o(N)` off-line populations. Bombieri's exact finite-form inertia detects every finite-total defect. Connes--Consani's semilocal Laurent core goes further: because the nested finite spaces are a form core, **every** RH failure forces finite negativity at some semilocal scale, even for an infinite sparse divisor. The existential finite-detectability problem is therefore solved for that family; the missing theorem is a source-forced reason those finite forms are nonnegative.

This distinction matters beyond that line. Analytic Frontier loses absolute primitive memory when only increments are retained, and Visual Exploration loses a source/null distinction when a common Taylor moment is projected out. A compression should be justified by preservation of the **decision statistic** -- sign, index, primitive, target distance, or final rate -- rather than by richness of the compressed representation or accuracy of a few aggregate moments. Once preservation/completeness is certified, effort should move to the source-side coercivity of that statistic rather than build another complete representation.

## Convert exact source identification into transition/coercivity rather than another identifier

**Linked intuition:** `MI-009-local-correctness-does-not-determine-global-coherence`.

Xi Flow now closes a major source-identification gap. Positive Jacobi-reflecting complete-monotone prekernels can still have a positive de Bruijn--Newman threshold when their Laplace support mixes scaled square lattices, but if the support is fixed to the single Xi lattice `pi n^2`, Hamburger rigidity forces every coefficient to equal the theta weight `1`.

This is exact arithmetic source identification, not RH. The global lesson is that uniqueness of the intended source can itself become another noncoercive endpoint. The next theorem must connect the identified global source law to the mesoscopic mass, collision geometry, sign index, or other dynamic quantity that controls the RH-equivalent transition. A new characterization that only recognizes Xi more elegantly does not cross that final bridge.
