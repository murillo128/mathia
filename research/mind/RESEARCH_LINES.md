# Global mathematical questions

## Which source identity fixes the mixed-prime kernel?

[MI-009](intuition/MI-009-local-correctness-does-not-determine-global-coherence.md) gives explicit positive `r`-prime perturbations invisible to all proper marginals. What identity of the actual source excludes those centered-product directions for arbitrary `r`, and what joint kernel does it select? Independence is one exact answer for the Bost-Connes source, but [MI-004](intuition/MI-004-critical-axis-is-not-zero-selection.md) shows that the common critical ray law does not select it. Selecting the product law also does not establish equality to the completed Weil pairing.

[MI-002](intuition/MI-002-the-surviving-arithmetic-variable-is-relational.md) provides a constructive comparison: a bispectral multiplicativity equation reduces residual phases to one translation. A transfer would need an identified arithmetic analogue of that equation and its nondegeneracy hypotheses. Merely increasing a fixed test order is already excluded by the centered-product controls.

## What readout retains a source-dependent quantity after the matched control?

For the flute hierarchy, can an observable retain the resolved `w_j^2/w_(j-1)` correction through its final aggregation and distinguish a matched nonprime gap geometry? Its low-mode product cannot: [MI-001](intuition/MI-001-preserve-structure-before-compressing.md) identifies the exact determinant cancellation. Realization of the resolvable prime hierarchy is separately conditional.

For the fixed-base circle construction, which proposed observable lies outside the exact CRT coupled-chord spectrum and the classified fixed-sector singular/shift limits while remaining forced by the source? [MI-003](intuition/MI-003-arithmetic-significance-begins-after-universal-control-subtraction.md) supplies the coprime control, [MI-006](intuition/MI-006-classify-the-boundary-model-before-calling-it-arithmetic.md) supplies the rank-two singular limit, and [MI-007](intuition/MI-007-survival-requires-quantitative-non-escape-not-just-nonzero-structure.md) supplies the ballistic recentering. Changing the representation without leaving those exact readout classes does not define a new discriminator.

## Which quantitative observation family reaches the required source aperture?

With local Euler degree bounded by `d`, [MI-005](intuition/MI-005-topology-is-part-of-the-arithmetic-claim.md) separates finite depth per prime from unbounded prime breadth and supplies exact finite-jet collisions. For a target requiring coefficients through `N`, can its source geometry provide a uniform absolute-convergence budget `B_c` and observation accuracy/horizon satisfying the explicit vertical-mean bound in [MI-008](intuition/MI-008-exact-recovery-is-not-stable-transport.md)? The bound to transport is `N^c(B_c/(T log(1+1/N))+epsilon_T)`, not the qualitative fact that each coefficient is recoverable as `T -> infinity`.

Where the target readout instead recovers a simple zero divisor, the corresponding source obligation is a separation bound in its declared metric: the local inverse constant is `1/(2 delta(B))`. There is no established identification between those two condition numbers. A proposed bridge must exhibit its own map and parameter dependence rather than infer uniform recovery from either exact uniqueness theorem.

## Does the complete flute response preserve the weighted source budget?

Can the physical mixed response be factored as `G_n=T_nY_n(a_n)+R_n`, with uniformly positive-Sobolev-bounded transport, `sup_n sum_i q_i|a_(n,i)|<infinity`, and vanishing physical-high projection of `R_n`? The known canonical synthesis estimate and high-pass inequality in [MI-007](intuition/MI-007-survival-requires-quantitative-non-escape-not-just-nonzero-structure.md) would then exclude persistent local shorting-deficit witnesses. The unresolved content is the actual coefficient budget and residual operator, not smoothness of an arbitrarily chosen fixed profile.

Even successful local annihilation would leave the global heavy-range projection product and its singular-value counts to estimate; these are retained explicitly in the [prime-flute questions](../prime_flute/mind/RESEARCH_LINES.md). The supercritical fixed-axis leakage estimate is excluded, not an outstanding positive target.
