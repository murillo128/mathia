Every convergence wall in `(0,1)` can therefore be manufactured by coefficient topology. The Hilbert `1/2` boundary is sharp but is one point on a non-arithmetic Holder continuum.

The physical prime and horizon can change at every scale because the coherent coordinate is the quotient index. Representation enrichment buys leverage when new relations have exact source preimages, controlled inverse/source cost and an exact map back to the destination.

The shared question is: **what new source-specific coordinate is acquired, what closure/summation/globalization or pre-norm projection is unavoidable, which internal coordinate stays coherent if physical sensors move, what quotient and inverse geometry remain, which ambient topology or symmetry already explains the critical scale, what source-side continuation remains analytically identified with the intended destination object, what tail survives the scale treatment, and which matched control reproduces the actual signed destination channel?** Richer data deserve credit only after those gates survive.

## Can sign-indefinite structure preserve critical information before closure or norm compression scalarizes it?

Weil Positivity remains the strongest positive-closure warning, now from both directions. Exact common homogeneous scale treatment factorizes the finite--archimedean incidence; bounded fixed-scale positivity preserves the incidence but exponentially kills its high-index carrier. A surviving positive route must use cross-index or index-dependent scale coupling, controlled unboundedness, a source-forced boundary/remainder sector or another sign theorem outside those componentwise classes.

Prime Lattice brackets the signed-filter problem even more sharply. Leaving the positive cone is necessary to cross one convergence wall, but finite signs are periodic, unrestricted infinite signs are programmable and `ell^1` signs are limit-periodic. Pole-neutral `ell^p` signs then manufacture the sharp generic wall `Re(s)>1-1/p`; the Hilbert half-line is only the `p=2` instance. Useful nonpositivity must therefore be source-forced in a way that distinguishes the destination from the full ambient norm ladder.

Farey Discrepancy gives a finite-dimensional constructive example of useful sign placement. Differences must be taken before the positive norm so shared source bulk cancels rather than being squared independently; FD-204 retains favorable inverse geometry and FD-205 shows that these signed local increments can still telescope exactly to the global destination through quotient coordinates.

Visual Exploration supplies the complementary control: VIS-306 shows that merely retaining the signed endpoint form is not enough to produce a critical obstruction, because a matched Bernoulli source already cancels it to `o_p(H)`. Useful nonpositivity must therefore be source-specific, not just present syntactically.

Nyman--Beurling gives the analytic analogue. Keeping primitive and reconstruction kernel coupled avoids the absolute reconstruction tariff. NB-220 additionally shows that one can choose a soft window whose dual source is entire, so neither full vertical support nor left-of-one source summability forces sign collapse. The unresolved question is whether the signed coupling survives the analytic identification/contour shift back to the zeta primitive itself.

These examples do not transfer estimates across lines, but they sharpen the interface: useful nonpositivity must occur before the scalarizing operation, be forced by the source, survive required transport and analytic identification, avoid being reproduced or canceled by a matched control, and remain distinct after the destination's symmetry quotient and inverse geometry are applied.
