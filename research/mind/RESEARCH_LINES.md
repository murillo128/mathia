# Global mathematical questions

## Which source relation cannot be repaired by moving the cost elsewhere?

[MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md) separates several different failure modes of nominally rich source interfaces.

Farey Discrepancy distinguishes **relation coercivity from observability of that relation**, and the current tariff is sharply source-fibre dependent. FD-181 proves that every squarefree-supported perturbation of Möbius with changed-coordinate support `o(x/log x)` pays prime-extension multiplicativity energy at scale `x/log x`, even when prime coordinates may move. FD-182 composes that theorem with the permanent FD-179 dyadic-null source: one fixed non-Möbius source matches the complete dyadic midpoint ladder exactly while retaining `M_(a,q)(x)=(C+o(1))x/log x`.

FD-183--FD-184 showed that fixing the prime coordinates propagates one defect through every fixed almost-prime rank. FD-186 identifies the stronger finite mechanism and removes growing-rank uniformity from the physical-prime classification. With `a(p)=-1`, normalize `c=a/mu`; every prime is anchored by `c(p)=1`. If `n_0` is changed, `k=omega(n_0)` and `Delta=|a(n_0)-mu(n_0)|`, then each fresh prime produces an edge-disjoint finite detour whose `L^q` energy is at least `Delta^q/(k+1)^(q-1)`. Hence

`liminf_(x->infinity) (log x/x) M_(a,q)(x) >= Delta^q/(n_0(k+1)^(q-1)) > 0`,

so `M_(a,q)(x)=o(x/log x)` forces `a=mu` on the entire physical-prime squarefree fibre without any support hypothesis. FD-185 supplies the complementary moving-prime tariff: moving one prime can keep relation energy zero, but forces positive-density changed support. Therefore `S(x)=o(x)` together with `M_(a,q)(x)=o(x/log x)` already forces `a=mu`. The unresolved source-side currency is now **observation of the relation plus quantitative/weighted stability**, not growing multiplicative depth. A source-native bridge must specify an observation family that exposes prime-extension energy and the source fibre/amplitude regime on which its tariff was proved.

Nyman--Beurling shows how a locality cost can be absent at fixed resolution yet become source-selective in a coupled limit. NB-190 prices a normalized Euler-band probe by inverse source mass in scalar-translate variation. NB-191 shows that at fixed band width this cost can eventually be transplanted into every high dyadic block by common Bohr recurrence without an additional asymptotic surcharge. NB-192--NB-193 reveal the hidden nonuniformity: one fixed log-width band contains `m_a~a exp(r_0/a)` rationally independent prime-log phases, and even the true-source-weighted positive return set has exponentially small Haar volume in effective dimension `D_eff asymp m_a`, forcing double-exponential worst-gap scale in `1/a`.

NB-194 adds a pointwise obstruction for the **specific physical block** through Vinogradov--Korobov dephasing, while NB-195 shows that whenever `a_TT->infinity` positive true-source returns occupy vanishing relative measure on `[T,2T]`. NB-196 converts the current uniform `17/30` short-interval prime exponent into pointwise Mellin dephasing through the complementary `13/30` corridor. NB-197 then shows that positivity can use a stronger almost-all theorem without resolving every cell: Guth--Maynard's `2/15+epsilon` almost-all interval result, combined with shifted-grid averaging, makes the exceptional cells carry only `o(1)` total positive source mass. The same continuum shell response is therefore uniform through `|t|<=x_a^(13/15-epsilon)`, and `[T,2T]` is eventually positive-return-free whenever

`limsup_(T->infinity) a_T log(2T)/r_0 < 13/15`.

The `13/30` and `13/15` thresholds are complements of current uniform and almost-all source-resolution exponents, not intrinsic Nyman transitions. The latter improvement is positivity-specific: exceptional spatial cells can be discarded by total positive source mass, a step unavailable to a generic signed/complex synthesis. Beyond the `13/15` corridor NB-195 still gives sparsity but not emptiness, so the surviving positive route is an isolated-large-value problem or requires stronger local-density input.

Robin Extremal supplies a precision-aware stable-rank analogue, but RE-195 shows that even the source norm in that ledger must be interpreted one-way. RE-189--RE-193 show that high normalized Euler jets and finite-Laplace windows can be extremely compressible despite exact analytic source rigidity, with rank

`O((1+log(1+R))(1+log(1/epsilon)))`,  `R=U log(Q_+/Q_-)`,

above the prime-power floor. RE-194 derives the Robin-scale relative tolerance `epsilon_Rob~min(1,d_p/V)`, where `d_p~1/(sqrt(p)log p)` and `V=sum |c_q|/q`, giving a worst-case capacity factor `log_+(V/d_p)`. RE-195 proves that `V` is not coercive source complexity: an ordinary-prime `{0,1,2}` control can add arbitrarily large reciprocal variation in a remote Chebyshev-balanced packet while remaining `O(p^-2)` in every fixed Euler window, matching every fixed boundary jet exactly after a disjoint repair, preserving KV fidelity and retaining the order-one transient Robin excursion.

RE-196 closes the natural full-real-ray escape at every fixed logarithmic precision depth. For each fixed jet depth `K`, the same repair has bounded centered logarithmic transport moments; after factoring the common selector decay and using the exact vanishing of the first `K` boundary jets, the entire real Euler discrepancy satisfies

`sup_(s>=1)|D_Q(s)| <<_K d_p/(log p)^(K+1) + p^-2`.

For every fixed `A`, choosing fixed `K>A` makes the complete positive real Euler ray `o(d_p/(log p)^A)` while the order-one Robin transient, KV fidelity and arbitrarily large reciprocal variation remain. Exact continuum identification is not contradicted; the inverse modulus is what collapses at Robin-relevant precision. A useful scalar complexity must therefore quotient balanced near-kernel directions or otherwise lower-bound actual Euler response, and full real spectral span alone is not a stable source resource.

Möbius Cancellation shows that source information can move inside a statistical architecture without becoming free. MC-356 prices finite-dimensional regular exponential-family channels by integrated Fisher action. MC-357--MC-358 move the tariff into latent/component structure while preserving the exact lifted divergence ledger. MC-359 adds total variation as a bounded edge currency when KL/Jeffreys becomes singular: bounded-energy nontrivial dephasing forces a positive average TV separation across source directions, and in a genuine shared-randomness architecture a one-source flip must change the complete transcript on a positive fraction of common seeds.

MC-360 adds a bounded **product-compositional** currency. Hellinger edge distance still upper-bounds the source information needed by the reciprocal endpoint, while affinity factorizes through conditionally independent components. For a genuine latent/product architecture, output Hellinger cost is bounded by latent-law Hellinger distance plus an affinity-weighted sum of component costs; with source-independent latent randomness this becomes an extensive weighted source-coordinate incidence tariff. Splitting a transcript into many weak components therefore does not erase the destination-imposed separation.

MC-361 sharpens the compulsory destination-side information amount. For binary source phases with average squared posterior predictability `rho^2`,

`I(X;Y) >= H(X)-R h((1+rho)/2)`.

Combined with the endpoint energy/dephasing inequality, normalized energy at most one and dephasing error at most `epsilon` force `I(X;Y) >= H(X)-R h(epsilon/2)`. MC-362 showed that endpoint sharpness can be transferred from the TV tariff into the compositional Hellinger ledger, but its TV-mediated quantitative deficit remained `O(sqrt(epsilon log(1/epsilon)))`. MC-363 now replaces that detour by the sharp direct Jensen--Shannon/Hellinger-affinity envelope. The punctured source-cube aggregation gives

`I(X;Y) <= R q_R Phi(H_1/(R q_R)) + 2(R-1)log 2/m`,

and matched-filter dephasing error `epsilon` forces

`liminf H_1/R >= 1-sqrt(2 epsilon-epsilon^2)`.

Thus the compositional Hellinger deficit is sharply `O(sqrt(epsilon))` near exact dephasing, and the same demand transfers to the latent/product incidence ledger. The remaining problem is still the opposite direction: expose the actual Möbius-derived architecture and prove an arithmetic upper bound in the finite currency native to it.

The common discriminator is not raw dimension, exactness or the mere existence of a source tariff. It is a **destination-relevant quantitative resource together with an observation/acquisition theorem that actually exposes it under the declared source class, precision, depth and coupled scaling**.

## Which physical source scale, analytic domain and representation gauge does the theorem actually control?

Arithmetic Fidelity has two independent scale ledgers. On the sampling side, uniform short-interval PNT gives all-prime-start conversion for `h_N=p_N^(-beta)` throughout `beta<13/30`, while exceptional-set inertia plus Brun--Titchmarsh packing transfers a stronger almost-all ambient theorem to relative density one of prime starts through every `beta<13/15`. Those thresholds measure theorem uniformity and sampling geometry, not an intrinsic detector constant.

On the finite-order analytic side, AF-403--AF-405 turn strict translation positivity into a recursive confluent/Toda hierarchy. The full Euler-log profile has `H_1,H_2,H_3,H_4>0` globally, hence strict total positivity through order four. AF-217 still forces eventual failure, so the first possible bad order is at least five. AF-408 now localizes the order-five gate `A_4=4q-(log R_4)''`: it proves

`A_4(t)=(24/5)e^(2t)(1+o(1))` as `t->-infinity`,

and `A_4(t)=4e^t(1+o(1))` as `t->+infinity`. Thus order five is automatically positive in both logarithmic tails; any failure is confined to a compact interior interval. The live order-five problem is a bounded multi-harmonic interaction question, and a positive compact-interior theorem would move the first possible bad order to at least six.

AF-406 adds a representation-specific fidelity boundary. For `xi_1(z)=xi(sqrt(z)+1/2)`, every fixed finite **coefficient-sequence** Pólya-frequency order can be forced by a sufficiently large zero-free multiplier `e^{nz}` while the complete zero divisor is unchanged. But an affine exponential **profile** tilt `f(t)->e^{at+b}f(t)` preserves every translation-determinant sign, scales `H_r` only by a positive factor and leaves normalized `R_r` and `q` invariant. Coefficient `PF_m` gauge sensitivity therefore cannot be transferred wholesale to the translation-kernel Toda hierarchy.

AF-407 adds an independent analytic-domain audit. For the affine-tilted Euler-log profile, the bilateral Laplace transform is zero-free throughout its actual convergence half-plane. The nontrivial zeta zeros appear only in meromorphic continuation beyond that wall, with a gauge-invariant offset from the wall. All-order Pólya-frequency theory can exploit them because Schoenberg supplies reciprocal-entire continuation; finite-order translation positivity does not inherit that continuation theorem automatically. AF-408 reinforces the separation: the order-five tails can be settled positively without any access to the continued divisor, so the remaining compact gate is still a genuine local-kernel problem unless an independent continuation/localization bridge is proved.

Visual Exploration adds both normalization and parameter-regime audits. VIS-272 gives the exact Green-potential dictionary and VIS-274 carries the concentrating main term through the fixed-source `1/log T` pullback. VIS-275 shows why a sign-blind absolute remainder cannot benefit from extra signed packet moments. VIS-276--VIS-277 then establish that the apparent signed remainder mean is partly a classical gamma-factor convention, not an invariant source residual.

VIS-278 closes the first normalization-invariant pure source-energy question. With

`R_x(t)=x(B_x(t)+E_x(t))-log(t/(2*pi))`,

one has `R_x(t)=zeta'/zeta(3/2-it)+O_K(t^-1)`, asymptotically zero mean, and a positive limiting mean-square `C_Lambda=sum_p (log p)^2/(p^3-1)`. Its standalone fixed-source packet contribution is only `L^-2`.

VIS-279 shows that the `D_x`--`E_x` cross is also only `L^-2` after Wang's exact mean-square theorem is specialized to compact fixed source before rescaling. VIS-280 closes the remaining direct prime/source cross: Wang's explicit `D_x`--`B_x` bound becomes `O_K(1/(HL))` after fixed-source packet normalization and is power-small in `T`. VIS-281 closes a theorem-level boundary as well: the `O(xL^3)` error in Wang's `A_I -> A` zero-window localization becomes only `O_K(L/H)` after the same fixed-source pullback and normalization, hence is smaller than every fixed inverse power of `L`. The direct interaction and this localization step are therefore excluded as compulsory `L^-3/2` mechanisms. The correct next theorem must inspect the remaining truncation/replacement sectors and first demonstrate that any intrinsic fixed-source `L^-3/2` term exists at all.

Robin's refined precision law is therefore an audit of the scalar observation map, not a new source lower bound. The transform rectangle `R=U log(Q_+/Q_-)` and destination-required relative tolerance control a stable-rank **upper** estimate, while RE-195 shows that unsigned reciprocal variation can be inflated along balanced directions invisible at fixed Euler resolution. RE-196 makes the warning stronger: even the **complete real Euler half-line** can be uniformly near-null by an arbitrarily high fixed log power relative to the Robin selector debt while the destination transient remains order one. A future Robin route must therefore control an actual source-visible signed quotient or growing-depth precision regime, not infer leverage from spectral span, exact analytic uniqueness or `V` alone.

## Which quantitative resource survives a thin or moving destination?

Prime Lattice has moved beyond the local activation-birth gate, the positive-tail dilution gate, and compact completion of the sharp bounded prime edge. PL-338 shows that every finite prime-power activation is first-order flat and is born with favorable reflected sign whenever the transported boundary trace is nonzero. PL-339 computes the exact path-chain gap of each compressed shift and shows that the outer logarithmic half-window carries asymptotically all weighted von-Mangoldt mass with unit gap, giving `J_a >= (1-o(1))W_a I`.

PL-340 rules out the hope that compatibility of those positive jumps can reach the `2W_a` scalar-cancellation threshold. PL-341 makes the ordinary obstruction sharp: with `W_a=2e^a(1+o(1))`, the isolated prime block has normalized spectral edges `-1/2` and `+1/2`, while the jump block has edges `3/2` and `5/2`. PL-342 now shows that the same `+/-e^a` prime-block edges are the **essential** spectral edges. Kronecker recurrence reproduces the endpoint Rayleigh values on weakly-null high-frequency modulations, so finite-rank pole correction and bounded compact completion cannot lower the sharp prime scale in the Calkin quotient.

The full completed operator is a different category: its logarithmic principal part is unbounded and the localized realization has compact resolvent. The same high-frequency recurrence witnesses that preserve the bounded prime edge pay an increasing archimedean/logarithmic cost. The frontier is therefore a same-state balance between recurrent prime translation gain and the unbounded frequency penalty, together with branch persistence/noncollapse and genuinely rational-prime-specific coupling. The ordinary and essential half-mass spectra remain PNT-universal matched controls, not RH selectivity.

Nyman--Beurling now has a four-stage obstruction to positive high-block recurrence. NB-192--NB-193 make guaranteed positive recurrence double-exponentially nonuniform as `a->0`; NB-195 shows actual physical returns have vanishing density whenever `a_TT->infinity`; NB-196 converts uniform short-interval prime density into pointwise return-freeness through the complementary `13/30` Mellin corridor; and NB-197 uses almost-all `2/15` source resolution plus positivity to extend pointwise exclusion to `13/15`. The surviving positive route lies beyond `x_a^(13/15-o(1))` and requires isolated near-maximal values or stronger local-density input. Signed/block-specific/non-positive mechanisms remain logically open because the exceptional-cell mass argument is positivity-specific.

Xi Flow remains a useful control in the opposite direction: once a consecutive packet is localized, its local holomorphic inversion is well conditioned. Its unresolved cost is global support discovery and assembly rather than local packet inversion. Across these lines, the moving destination is governed by the resource that scales with localization, recurrence, source dephasing, support discovery, stable transform width, source-visible complexity, destination precision, frequency cost or branch transport—not by the mere existence of a local inverse, positive bulk or large unsigned source norm.

## Can reverse discrimination, signed structure or positivity be made destination-selective?

Möbius Cancellation now prices both regular and singular source channels. MC-354--MC-358 cover smooth noise, Gaussian deformation, regular exponential families and source-dependent latent/components through finite divergence ledgers. MC-359 supplies the bounded TV edge tariff and common-seed change bound at the singular endpoint. MC-360 supplies a bounded Hellinger ledger that tensorizes through latent/product architectures. A useful contradiction still needs the other half: an arithmetic upper theorem showing that the **actual** Möbius endpoint architecture cannot afford the compulsory separation/information in a metric and decomposition that stay meaningful for that architecture.

Weil Inertia exposes a different information loss. WI-332--WI-335 show that low and slowly growing ordinary Gowers orders can be small while full diagonal-scale sliding variance survives. WI-336 supplies the high-order diagonal floor, WI-337 pushes the generic obstruction to the threshold on density-one starts, and WI-338 closes the remaining **all-start** gap. For `R_s=2^s loglog N/log K <= 2-delta_N` with `delta_N loglog N->infinity`, one matched-energy Rademacher realization is simultaneously `U^s=o(1)` on every bow-scale interval and every retained order while retaining full sliding variance. In the complementary boundary layer the diagonal floor itself prevents ordinary smallness. Thus there is no ordinary-Gowers order window left in which merely strengthening the order or source-start quantifier can force the required signed covariance. A surviving route must use source-specific signed two-point structure, a relative linear-forms statement retaining `Lambda^sharp`, or a genuinely diagonal-free/renormalized critical statistic.

[MI-026](intuition/MI-026-sign-destructive-proof-interfaces-cannot-certify-destination-cancellation.md) links that covariance boundary to the Visual result. In Wang's channel, signed information is useless if decomposition-dependent, already absolute-valued, or attached to a theorem-uniform scale that disappears after fixed-source specialization; VIS-281 now shows that even a theorem-level zero-window localization loss disappears below every logarithmic order in the fixed physical source regime. In the bow, a growing list of ordinary smallness constraints is useless if it does not determine the required off-diagonal sign, and WI-338 shows that the generic countermodel reaches the same diagonal threshold even with all-start control. A useful intermediate must preserve destination-sensitive sign/covariance, survive harmless normalization freedom, live at the exact physical scale and remove rather than merely repackage source-universal diagonal energy.

Weil Positivity now closes reversible and non-reversible finite-dimensional scalar/matrix escapes in sequence. WP-352 isolates the reciprocal exponential phase gauge and shows that projective Berry/Chern geometry is blind to the common scalar zeta factor. WP-353 proves that, under order-at-most-one growth, this exponential family is the complete zero-free reciprocal/unitary scalar gauge. Standard arithmetic cusp normalization can remove its one real parameter—but after normalization the scalar meromorphic normalizer is determined by its divisor plus normalization, so scalar phase recovery collapses to divisor reconstruction.

WP-354 shows that ordinary finite-dimensional bi-invariant **quadratic matrix geometry** does not repair the loss. Every conjugation-invariant positive semidefinite metric on the Hermitian Lie algebra splits orthogonally into scalar-center and traceless parts. Its energy sees the square of determinant-phase velocity plus noncentral positive energy, but no oriented center--traceless cross term; reversing the scalar phase leaves the energy unchanged.

WP-355 tests the nonquadratic escape directly. A non-reversible positive Finsler norm can retain central orientation, but every central restriction has the form `c|q|+dq` with `c>|d|`; reversal changes `d` to `-d` while preserving positivity. In the canonical bi-invariant Randers realization the odd term is exactly the closed determinant-phase one-form, so it contributes a free endpoint/homotopy term without changing the local geodesic core. A surviving Weil route must therefore **derive the orientation from the arithmetic source**, not merely choose a non-reversible positive geometry that can store it. Source-forced irreversibility, a genuine operator-order statement, global multi-spectral coupling or an infinite-dimensional/adelic/cohomological mechanism remain open categories.

Prime Circle closes a parallel scalar-selector escape. PC-338 extends the finite point-supported Mellin classification to every compactly supported distribution in Mellin frequency. PC-339 now shows that noncompact support alone does not escape: every finite global Mellin measure with the natural critical Gamma-strip boundary admissibility still collapses under semiprime nulls to zero off `s=1` and to `c Lambda` on the endpoint. The decisive resource is the analytic boundary class, not support compactness. A surviving radial route must leave this Gamma-strip-admissible measure class—through faster-growth/non-measure analytic functionals, shell-dependent/nonlinear/matrix structure or cross-shell coupling—and still supply an independent zero-sensitive destination theorem.

Prime Flute reinforces the complementary warning. The current sharp physical low/high completed angle is already noncompact because adjacent Fourier modes can straddle the cutoff while becoming almost identical on a collapsing corridor. More endpoint counting cannot repair a representation whose local operator already has infinitely many order-one canonical correlations; the split or invariant itself must change before the global trace-ideal question becomes meaningful.
