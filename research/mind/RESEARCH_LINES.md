# Global mathematical questions

## Which source relation cannot be repaired by moving the cost elsewhere?

[MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md) now has a sharper edge-resource ledger. Farey Discrepancy no longer needs deletion-only errors or fixed prime coordinates for its coercive prime-extension boundary: FD-181 shows that any squarefree-supported perturbation of Möbius with changed-coordinate support `o(x/log x)` pays

`liminf (log x/x) M_(a,q)(x) >= sum |a(n)-mu(n)|^q/n`.

Thus signed compensations and sparse movement of prime data cannot hide one erroneous parent from its clean prime fan. The natural normalized separator remains `(log x loglog x)^(-1/q)`; the unresolved cardinal frontier begins when the perturbation budget reaches the size of one prime fan, while dense tiny-amplitude errors require a different currency.

Nyman--Beurling shows a different acquisition cost: independently normalized Euler bands can be synthesized from scalar translates only with total variation proportional to inverse source mass. NB-191 now proves that high-block locality alone does not raise this tariff at fixed source width, because finite-frequency Bohr recurrence can transplant the sharp synthesizers into every sufficiently high dyadic block. Any additional locality cost must therefore come from the coupled growth of recurrence length as source resolution increases.

Robin Extremal supplies two opposite failures of scalar enrichment. Past log-squared derivative depth, normalized Euler jets collapse toward net multiplicity; RE-190 now shows that replacing jets by arbitrarily dense exact sampling in any `o(1)` neighborhood of `s=1` also collapses, this time to reciprocal mass on a fixed multiplicative source shell. More local scalar coordinates can therefore cease to add robust source information either through high-order saturation or through a dual window that is too narrow.

Möbius Cancellation shows that source information can also move inside a statistical law without becoming free. MC-356 upgrades the Gaussian ledger to every finite-dimensional regular exponential-family readout with common support: row half-Jeffreys divergence is exactly one half of the integrated Fisher action along the natural-parameter edge. Combined with the endpoint lower bound, bounded-energy nontrivial dephasing forces a nonvanishing average Fisher action; exact dephasing must leave the regular interior entirely.

The common discriminator is not dimension, coefficient accuracy, nominal source fidelity or the existence of a nonzero tariff. It is a **destination-relevant quantitative resource together with a justified admissible budget**. A proposed route should state why its control class cannot pay that tariff, relocate it to another edge/channel, exploit recurrence, or enter a regime where added coordinates become a common mode.

## Which physical source scale does the theorem actually control?

Arithmetic Fidelity has a two-regime prime-locality ledger. Uniform short-interval PNT gives all-prime-start conversion for `h_N=p_N^(-beta)` throughout `beta<13/30`. AF-402 uses persistence of ambient failures plus Brun--Titchmarsh packing to transfer a stronger almost-all theorem to relative density one of prime starts, reaching every `beta<13/15`. The difference records theorem uniformity and sparse-sampling geometry, not an intrinsic detector threshold.

Visual Exploration supplies a complementary theorem-coordinate audit. VIS-272 gives an exact Green-potential dictionary between Wang's diagonal field and the Rodgers weighted prime-power measure. VIS-274 shows that a fixed physical `q` window remains accessible after its `Theta(1/log T)` pullback because the concentrating main term must be retained and rescaled on the same scale. VIS-275 then shows that signed packet moments cannot beat the available absolute `1/log T` remainder majorant.

VIS-276 now localizes that floor before the sign is discarded. The elementary `B_x^2` variation is power-smaller on the short interval; the surviving first-order channel is the signed explicit-formula remainder mean `M_T(x)=(x/H) Re int E_x(t)dt`. The current pointwise bound yields only `M_T=O(1)`. Proving `M_T(e^(2pi q))=o(1)` on fixed source windows would cross the existing `1/log T` barrier without any additional packet moments.

RE-190 adds the dual warning. On a fixed multiplicative prime shell, exact Euler samples in an `o(1)` spectral neighborhood of `s=1` become operator-norm rank one even if sampled arbitrarily densely. The relevant currency is spectral-window width times logarithmic source diameter, not sample count. Together with Arithmetic Fidelity and Visual Exploration, this makes the scale rule two-sided: the theorem must control the source on the physical window used, and the observation window must itself be wide enough to resolve the source degrees of freedom being claimed.

The general rule is now: state the physical source and dual windows first, verify density/uniformity on the actual sampling set, carry structured main terms through the induced coordinate rescaling, and identify the signed residual before applying a generic majorant. Ambient almost-everywhere statements, fixed-test corollaries, dense-but-nearly-parallel samples, generic seminorm bounds and absolute envelopes can all misprice the same-scale information actually available.

## Which quantitative resource survives a thin or moving destination?

Prime Lattice has moved beyond the first-prime concentration gate. PL-338 shows that every finite prime-power activation is first-order flat, and its newborn reflected correlation is favorable whenever the transported boundary trace is nonzero. The local activation problem is therefore a trace-noncollapse gate; the remaining difficulty is global branch persistence, ordering and scalable multi-channel tail control across many activations.

Nyman--Beurling now separates three quantities that had been conflated. NB-188 forces square-root source-density amplification and bounded fanout for positive-mass aggregation. NB-190 proves that scalar Euler translates pay inverse source mass in Fourier--Stieltjes variation. NB-191 then shows that forcing those synthesizers into high dyadic blocks adds no universal asymptotic surcharge at fixed `a`: finite active frequency sets recur syndetically, and the high-block infimum remains `Theta(H_T)` beyond an `a`-dependent threshold. The unresolved resource is therefore the recurrence inclusion length in the simultaneous `a_T->0`, `T->infinity` regime, or some independent operator budget of the actual Nyman architecture.

Xi Flow remains a useful control: once a consecutive packet is localized, its local holomorphic inversion is well conditioned. The unresolved cost is global support discovery and assembly, not local packet inversion. Across these lines, locality becomes a real resource only when the relevant support/recurrence/discovery scale is quantitatively coupled to the physical destination rather than merely required to be “large.”

## Can reverse discrimination, signed structure or positivity be made destination-selective?

Möbius Cancellation now prices a hierarchy of regular channel classes. MC-354 bounds reverse discrimination for common additive log-smooth noise; MC-355 prices source-dependent Gaussian mean/covariance motion; MC-356 identifies the invariant extension to regular exponential families, where the exact row cost is integrated Fisher action. The remaining question is whether the arithmetic endpoint actually factors through a priced regular class or instead requires a singular/support-changing, mixture/nonparametric, adaptive or side-channel-specific reverse-divergence theorem.

Weil Inertia strengthens a different norm mismatch. Below `theta<3/16`, all-start control removes the source-location obstruction. WI-332--WI-334 show that `U^2`, square-root phase cancellation and even an arbitrary fixed finite hierarchy of power-small local Gowers norms can coexist with full diagonal-scale sliding variance. The missing theorem must be genuinely two-point and signed: a source covariance mechanism whose off-diagonal term cancels a macroscopic part of the diagonal, or an equivalent source-fixed subtraction before the proof destroys that sign. Above `3/16`, the same covariance problem remains together with the exceptional-start quantifier.

[MI-026](intuition/MI-026-sign-destructive-proof-interfaces-cannot-certify-destination-cancellation.md) isolates the common failure shared by this bow obstruction and the Wang residual. VIS-276 makes the Wang side sharper: the missing sign is not merely hypothetical but sits in the explicit signed mean `M_T(x)` immediately before the current pointwise majorant. Once an intermediate inequality or norm has quotient out the sign/phase datum the destination needs, stacking more constraints inside that summary cannot recreate it. A successful continuation must recover the missing signed structure before the lossy step or replace the interface by a theorem that controls the destination-sensitive signed quantity directly.

Weil Positivity closes another direct geometric escape. WP-352 shows that projective Berry/Chern geometry is blind to the common scalar zeta factor, the full scalar connection contributes only an exact central term, and singular scalar curvature is just the meromorphic divisor. The zero-free reciprocal factor `exp(a(s-1/2))` preserves reciprocal symmetry, critical-line unitarity, divisor data and projective geometry while shifting the phase generator. A surviving sign route therefore needs an independently forced noncentral/global coupling or phase anchor before scalar-gauge quotienting.

Prime Circle and Prime Flute continue to reinforce the same discipline from other representations. PC-337 now classifies every finite point-supported semiprime-null Mellin selector on a fixed vertical line: away from `s=1` the shell response is zero, and on the endpoint line the only nonzero response is `c Lambda`. Finite singular enrichment therefore adds no new zero-sensitive radial mechanism. More generally, scalar magnitude packages and local hard-cut correlations can look rich while matched controls preserve the visible structure or compactness fails before global counting matters.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, state the admissible source class, exact observation/target quotient, physical source-coordinate map, dual observation window, relation graph, acquisition operator class and budget, conditioning, sampling regime, likelihood/covariance/sign norm and matched-control class. Then ask what the destination actually distinguishes; whether changing horizons, operators or coordinates adds source information or only relocates a cost; whether localization introduces a quantitatively new scale or is neutralized by recurrence; whether dense sampling adds rank or only parallel observations; whether the source theorem is valid at the induced moving scale; whether structured main terms have been rescaled before generic error bounds are applied; whether the proof has discarded sign or covariance needed later; whether true amplitudes and source mass survive normalization; whether reverse likelihood contrast or signed off-diagonal covariance is affordable; whether a relational source law blocks sparse repair at a quantitative rate; whether deeper scalar observables retain source resolution; and whether the final survivor connects to a zero/sign theorem rather than only reconstructing arithmetic data.

The program advances when one resource becomes **destination-selective under the same source fibre, physical scale, acquisition class, conditioning and control class used by the claimed RH mechanism**. Raw dimension, deep scalar jets, dense local samples, high coordinate fidelity, exact coordinate dictionaries, paid-but-generic source tariffs, fixed-order pseudorandomness, sign-blind residual bounds and scalar-gauge-invariant positivity remain intermediate resources until that matched-control test is passed.
