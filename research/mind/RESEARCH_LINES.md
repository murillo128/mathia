## Which physical cost survives after representation artifacts are removed?

The newest results sharpen the common order of proof. AF-486 shows that positive Hankel structure becomes informative when the source fixes the matrix and the recovery theorem exposes its sharp exponential precision scale. MC-448--MC-449 show that even genuine harmonic cancellation is not useful until the actual source occupancy and periodic zero mode are priced. PC-395 shows that local survivor covering can close an apparent long-window spectral escape. PL-418, VIS-369--VIS-370 and WP-398 supply three different warnings about positivity: it may be automatic, programmable through the metric, or improper after quotienting. WI-389 shows that compactness costs can be set by the local sampling density of the destination rather than by a convenient Sobolev norm.

[MI-034](intuition/MI-034-positivity-is-informative-only-when-the-induced-constraint-remains-proper-after-the-destination-quotient.md) records the cross-line positivity version of the audit. A positive representation counts only when its cone/metric is independently fixed, excludes relevant matched controls, and remains a nontrivial asymmetric constraint after the actual quotient or destination map. The broader durable order remains: quotient universal scaffolds first, restrict to the actual admissible image and source measure, identify the carrier that survives the destination operation, and then price its amplitude, conditioning, occupancy, target projection, sampling density, measure class and provenance.
