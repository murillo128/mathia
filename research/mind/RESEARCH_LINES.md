# Global mathematical questions

This file records mathematical questions that only become visible by comparing several current Mathia research lines. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does a quantitative budget become a genuine source discriminator?

Several lines now show that the decisive budget is rarely the first coarse norm one can write down. The useful quantity must be fixed before target inspection, live in the geometry actually consumed by the destination, and be occupied by the physical source rather than by a matched control.

Visual Exploration gives a sharp selector-level progression. VIS-416 removes generic reciprocal-bandwidth scaling; VIS-417--VIS-418 show that beat geometry and time-origin alignment can manufacture long quiet windows even after coarse spectral controls are fixed; VIS-419 identifies the exact fixed-shell base-prime Haar self-null; VIS-420 prices finite-window replacement by the actual prime-log small divisors; and VIS-421 shows that Wang's source does not itself select distinguished heights. VIS-422 eliminates ordinary Gram points as a fixed-shell anomaly source because their finite prime-phase vectors already converge to Haar. VIS-423 then reaches changing polynomial observables: on dyadic Gram blocks, deviation from Haar is controlled by total Fourier mass, frequency-weighted mass and reciprocal-frequency mass. A growing shell therefore escapes the null only by a quantitatively visible high-frequency, small-divisor, coefficient-mass or nonlinear-approximation obstruction; increasing dimension by itself is not a source discriminator.

Weil Inertia supplies the continuation analogue. WI-429--WI-430 give a bounded Hardy detector with a positive first-defect quantum, while WI-431--WI-432 show that the absolutely convergent safe-line source has no persistent positive-frequency carrier, even after adiabatic localization. WI-433 now supplies the exact horizontal transport identity: after continuation, the localized positive-frequency field is the sum of damped safe-line leakage, explicit crossed-zero residues and one window-edge forcing integral. Once the safe-line term is small, every non-residue compensation is forced into that edge term. The live source discriminator is therefore not a generic localized norm but an **independently controlled edge estimate** strong enough to beat the defect quantum without importing the same right-half divisor through zero-distance or zero-density information.

Farey Discrepancy gives a complementary positive example of a source anomaly becoming progressively less representational. FD-298--FD-305 reduce near-capacity low-height behavior from a block-Mertens field through aligned correlations to one common interval and finally, in the deepest RH-conditional regime, to an additive Fourier fourth-moment/pointwise spike at a source-selected scale. The compression is only one-way: a large Fourier coefficient does not reconstruct the population, positivity or divisor-replication geometry that forced it.

Prime Lattice sharpens what “matching the destination geometry” means. PL-437 compresses the unresolved mod-5 orientation to two coordinates, PL-438 identifies the exact Schur ellipse, and PL-439 realizes the missing coordinates as two paired differences of real signed-residue energies. PL-440 now resolves their symmetry types: every translation-stationary lag kernel cancels the `s`-difference exactly, while the stationary part of the `f`-difference is one explicit quadratic-character lag projection. The remaining source theorem is therefore not generic covariance control but variance-scale control of a reflection/start-residue anisotropy channel plus one `chi_5` lag channel and its anisotropic remainder, measured in the exact Schur metric.

Across these examples, a budget becomes a source discriminator only after its selector, quotient, continuation and symmetry semantics are fixed. VIS-422--VIS-423 show that a seemingly meaningful arithmetic selector or growing family may already realize the null once its character tariff is paid; WI-433 shows that analytic continuation can move the entire unresolved burden into an edge term; FD-305 shows that a complicated source anomaly can compress to a standard statistic only after its alignment freedoms are paid; and PL-440 shows that an apparently two-dimensional covariance defect may split into a symmetry-forbidden mode and one character mode. The reusable test is to identify the exact surviving source channel before asking for a stronger bound on an ambient norm.

## Which source dependence survives quotienting, population ceilings and equilibrium scalarization?

Möbius Cancellation now prices both the endpoint and incomplete-hard-mask branches in the physical source geometry. MC-497--MC-501 turn the quadratic endpoint problem into source-location, source-width, population and effective-participation gates. MC-502 removes conductor-power gain from complete-period plus/minus hard-mask coherence. MC-503 then shows that an arbitrary **fixed incomplete profile** can be strongly biased only on a conductor-start set priced by its effective support, and MC-504 inserts the actual conditional source measure: for a fixed profile class, normalized bias is at most `C sqrt(p/(R_start|profile R_profile))`. Thus order-one bias requires product participation only of conductor size. The remaining arithmetic resource is no longer “mask irregularity” but material source mass in low-support/low-start-participation profile classes, noncompressible profile diversity, or a genuinely signed/complex pre-aggregation coefficient. No recurrence or compressibility theorem for the physical profiles has yet been proved.

Farey Discrepancy and Prime Circle mark opposite sides of lawful compression. FD-301--FD-305 progressively discard masks and moving-window freedoms only after quantitative payment, ending with a compact additive-spectral certificate. PC-414--PC-419 show the converse danger: once a source interaction is lost before shell scalarization, finite/infinite conductor descendants, Mellin jets, translations and even closed diagonal algebras cannot recover it. A simpler representation is useful only when one has proved that it retains the exact source dependence needed by the destination.

Visual Exploration gives the analogous phase quotient. VIS-419--VIS-423 separate the valid base-prime torus, finite-window calibration, selector provenance, fixed-shell selector equidistribution and a first quantitative growing-family character tariff. Ordinary Gram points survive the provenance gate but fail the fixed-shell discrimination gate, and many changing Laurent-polynomial observables also fail once `A_0`, `A_1` and `A_(-1)` are small enough. A future phase statistic must therefore preserve not only the shell's internal character dependencies but also the specific high-frequency, small-divisor or nonlinear information claimed to survive the Gram/Haar pushforward.

Weil Positivity now closes several increasingly permissive KMS escapes. WP-425 kills auxiliary-frequency compensation under product KMS dynamics; WP-426 shows that state-selected modular/interacting dynamics is programmable; WP-427 shows that even a general source-preserving KMS extension annihilates nonzero source Bohr modes when the compensator lies in the source relative commutant. WP-428 then allows genuine noncommutativity but proves that every **Bohr-diagonal positive sandwich** is still source-time invariant and kills the critical nonzero-frequency defect. The matched control is exact: coherent cross terms between distinct auxiliary Bohr sectors can move source frequency to zero. The surviving category is therefore source-forced cross-frequency coherence, not noncommutativity or interaction by themselves, and its relative phases/amplitudes must be derived before the target sign is inspected.

Nyman--Beurling supplies a different warning about apparent thresholds. NB-322 found logarithmic rank-one-error resonances for growing rational denominators below `sqrt n`, and NB-323 found the same scale again at the linear endpoint. NB-324 now proves that on the exact even-divisor skeleton the resonance persists for **every growing denominator**, with `E_(n,q)=(H_q-H_(q/2))/(4n)+O(1/(nq))`; power-of-two subsequences realize every fixed scale `m=n^(alpha+o(1))`, `1<=alpha<2`. The old intermediate-denominator gap was therefore an estimate artifact on this arithmetic family. Any upper mixing theorem must now exploit odd/nondivisor/near-rational arithmetic, cancellation unavailable to the adjacent witness, or target occupation rather than denominator size alone.

The reusable cross-line question is: **after the lawful quotient and scalarization, what conditional dependence or coherence still carries the source?** MC-503--MC-504 show that marginal profile irregularity is harmless unless the source couples it to exceptional starts; WP-427--WP-428 show that noncommutativity is harmless unless distinct Bohr sectors retain coherent relative data; NB-324 shows that a putative scale transition can disappear when an exact arithmetic relation is retained; and VIS-422--VIS-423 show that a sparse selector or growing character family can become statistically invisible after its phase pushforward pays the Haar tariff. Source dependence often survives not in an individual marginal object but in a jointly constrained relation that must itself be derived and quantitatively occupied.

## What replaces a scalar summary when nuisance geometry or leading normalization degenerates?

Arithmetic Fidelity gives both an obstruction and a hierarchy of replacement coordinates. AF-523--AF-528 show that scalar boundary-curvature summaries can be matched while an exact relational derivative still recovers hidden source shape. AF-533--AF-538 then identify the nuisance-relative geometry: target information is distance from the **closed** nuisance-plus-invisible space, and in finite-dimensional targets the surviving object is an efficient-information spectrum rather than one scalar coefficient.

AF-539 located a sharp cancellation scale for the first projective log-slope under scalar tail-multiplier nuisance. AF-540 now shows that this obstruction is representation-order specific: every fixed higher log-jet `k>=2` recovers the power-separation exponent uniformly over arbitrarily large scalar multipliers because the unbounded multiplier contribution is exactly affine in the local parameter and disappears after two derivatives. This is a real strengthening of the exact carrier, but not yet of the physical experiment. Higher jets remain infinitesimal, can be cancelled by a broader `t`-dependent nuisance with matching curvature, and can be reproduced by generalized sources with the same reciprocal-prime moment hierarchy. The live transfer problem is therefore to place these higher-jet directions in the actual observation/efficient-information geometry and prove a finite-resolution margin under a source-justified nuisance class.

Prime Lattice and Weil Inertia show two complementary forms of the same discipline. PL-440 does not ask for “more covariance”; it decomposes the exact two-coordinate Schur defect into a symmetry anisotropy and a character-lag mode before any estimate is attempted. WI-433 does not ask for “more Hardy localization”; it writes the localized continuation exactly and isolates the edge forcing that must be controlled independently of the residue signal. In both cases the replacement object is useful only because its unresolved component is explicit enough to falsify a proposed source estimate.

Prime Circle remains the negative control: a richer derivative or operator readout can be perfectly informative about a chosen representation while adding no arithmetic mechanism if matched source classes reproduce it. The cross-line challenge is therefore to construct replacement objects that are simultaneously **source-natural, destination-complete and stable after the real quotient/compression**. AF-540, PL-440 and WI-433 sharpen this requirement in different ways: annihilating one nuisance family is not the same as robust discrimination; resolving a covariance direction is not the same as controlling it arithmetically; and an exact continuation identity is not a source-side bound on its edge term. Those distinctions should remain explicit in any promotion from a useful representation to a mathematical research line.
