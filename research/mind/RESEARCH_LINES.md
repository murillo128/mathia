# Global mathematical questions

## When does repeated observation actually shrink the admissible source fibre?

[MI-007](intuition/MI-007-survival-requires-quantitative-non-escape-not-just-nonzero-structure.md), [MI-008](intuition/MI-008-exact-recovery-is-not-stable-transport.md), [MI-012](intuition/MI-012-information-content-and-destination-sensitivity-are-separate-resources.md), [MI-019](intuition/MI-019-source-identification-and-zero-selection-are-orthogonal-gates.md), [MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md), [MI-022](intuition/MI-022-unbounded-compatibility-can-turn-finite-cutoff-ambiguity-into-a-stability-problem.md), and [MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md) separate retained information, common-source compatibility, conditioning, acquisition geometry, aggregate readout capacity and final destination selection.

Arithmetic Fidelity now separates three resources at smooth order three. AF-394 identifies the exact continuum target: `q=-(log f)''` must satisfy `q>=0`, `(log q)''<=2q` on `{q>0}`, and `{q>0}` must be connected. The shrinking solid hierarchy recovers the first two analytic conditions but not component topology. AF-395 makes that failure quantitative: on any vanishing mesh, every fixed-index-radius `3x3` stencil can eventually be positive while a negative nonlocal witness persists only beyond the inverse-mesh scale. **Resolution is not relational reach**; refining the prime-log mesh while keeping successor radius bounded makes the observation more local, not more topologically complete.

Farey Discrepancy shows that redundancy is also source-class relative. FD-171 proves consecutive midpoint horizons have zero exact redundancy over the unrestricted formal source class: omitting any horizon leaves a permanent pulse-pair null direction. FD-172 destroys that conclusion after imposing a squarefree multiplicative source cone with `-1<=a(p)<=1`. The transformed odd coefficients become nonnegative, dyadic annuli cannot cancel, and exponentially sparse horizons `H=2^m` already force the Möbius target exactly; small dyadic errors bound every prime defect. This is target-rigidity, not pairwise injectivity, and it shows that row rank over an ambient linear space need not price recovery inside a nonlinear arithmetic source class.

Robin Extremal gives the converse warning about source tariffs. RE-180 proves that honest nonnegative prime multiplicity forces any early two-jet repair to pay `Omega(sqrt(p)/log p)` actual deletions unless it pays a remote KV reset budget. RE-181 then constructs a `{0,1,2}` ordinary-prime source that pays exactly that order of tariff, matches the boundary value and first slope, stays under every fixed KV envelope, and still preserves a transient Robin excursion. A necessary resource bill is not a selector if the declared source class can afford it. The live distinction is now placement/joint geometry of the compensators, not deletion count itself.

Together AF-395, FD-172 and RE-181 sharpen the multiscale lesson. Raw scale count, ambient row rank and lower-bound tariffs are not intrinsic information currencies. One must declare the admissible source fibre and ask which relations remain accessible at the destination's physical scale: fixed-radius local tests can miss topology despite infinite resolution; nonlinear source positivity can collapse an ambient sparse-sensing kernel; and a large discrete source tariff can still be nonselective when a matched source construction realizes it.

## Which quantitative resource reaches a thin or moving destination?

Prime Lattice retains the cleaner first-prime dependency graph. PL-329 gives the sharp source-amplitude condition for the shrinking reflected sign; PL-330 transports isolated eigenbranches in norm-resolvent/`L^2` topology; PL-331 proves every fixed logarithmic Fourier seminorm still misses the hostile moving layer; and PL-332 shows that, once the endpoint remainder is family-uniform, `L^2` continuity transports the selected asymptotic coefficient to the fixed threshold. The remaining gates are therefore the genuine uniform source/remainder estimate and a proof of `C_0!=0` for the **full threshold operator**, not the principal Dirichlet form.

Visual Exploration now has a scale-sharp moving-test transfer law. VIS-259--VIS-264 separate packet conditioning, universal pole background and the prime-power curvature residual. VIS-265 imports Wang's short-interval pair-correlation support restriction `lambda<theta`, and VIS-266 shows `T`-dependent tests are allowed when their support and seminorms fit the proof budget. VIS-267 then proves that a width-`b` smooth packet carrying unit `2m`-th moment necessarily has `||g||_inf \gtrsim b^(-(2m+1))` and `||g'||_inf \gtrsim b^(-(2m+2))`, with matching constructions. The current Wang interface therefore requires `1/(L b^(2m+2))->0`; for `b=L^(-q)` this gives the explicit gate `q<1/(2m+2)`. The barrier is not “fixed tests only” but an unavoidable moment-order/seminorm budget inside a fixed support margin.

Nyman--Beurling now identifies the topology of the acquisition space as part of the source cost. NB-176--NB-180 show that genuine Euler frequencies are nonselective if weak channels may be normalized independently, while retaining the true Euler amplitudes kills the synthetic Fourier code. NB-181 proves the stronger bounded-Hilbert statement: an `N x N` Hadamard-scale response requires RMS Hilbert acquisition norm growing like `sqrt N`. The actual scalar Euler readout escapes because its phase vector is not `ell^2`; its convergence uses stronger weighted Dirichlet/`ell^1` structure. The live question is therefore a source-natural **non-Hilbert or explicitly charged weighted acquisition geometry**, not another bounded Hilbert witness.

Xi Flow gives the inverse lesson: once the correct consecutive packet is known, local holomorphic recovery can be uniformly conditioned with linear measurement complexity even far out. The bottleneck is global support discovery, multiscale assembly, source-valid complex access and the bridge to a zero-sensitive Xi quantity, not local inversion itself.

## How much source information can an analytic architecture exploit at bounded conditioning?

Möbius Cancellation separates fixed-quality capacity from variable-fidelity reliability. MC-338--MC-345 show that bounded-energy dephasing requires extensive joint source information and that a memoryless BSC contributes at most `c(q)` information per actual source query even under adaptive coordinate choice and stopping. MC-346 adds the fixed-channel reliability tariff: vanishing average raw-coordinate error costs `Omega_q(R log(1/bar p))` expected accesses. MC-347 identifies the invariant currency when query quality can vary: the accumulated directional discrimination divergence `mathfrak D` must satisfy `mathfrak D=Omega(R log(1/bar p))`. Many weak measurements can be traded for fewer strong ones, but the total pairwise evidence cannot be optimized away.

FD-169--FD-172 and NB-178--NB-181 are complementary warnings. Unrestricted acquisition can hide the inverse in the sensor; changing horizons can hide many independent rows behind one spatial coordinate; ambiently irredundant rows can become redundant for testing one target inside a positive multiplicative source cone; an enlarged dual class can remove one compactness ceiling and still be so expressive that generic metric packing fakes the desired spectral code; genuine Euler frequency labels remain nonselective when their amplitudes are renormalized; and restoring those amplitudes inside a bounded Hilbert dual can exclude the actual unbounded phase functional used by the Euler scalar readout. Source-information cost is meaningful only after the source class, acquisition topology, target notion, observation norm/noise model, operator variation, source amplitudes and matched-control geometry are fixed.

This updates [MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md): measurement count, nominal dimension, positive noise, adaptivity and canonical probe labels are secondary. The load-bearing quantities are inverse modulus, source-class-relative kernel, independent operator-row rank, per-access capacity, accumulated discrimination divergence, source-amplitude conditioning, acquisition-space norm and the number of genuinely independent operator states being observed.

## Which spectral or positivity enrichment survives its intrinsic quotient and density law?

Prime Circle now has an exact dyadic decomposition of its growing-conductor Green defect. PC-325--PC-330 establish the phase/autocorrelation quotient, universal Hilbert background and failure of smooth Li subtraction. PC-331 writes the source-sensitive excess as a positive Hankel/Hardy-square energy `E` of Fourier magnitude squares. Uniform dyadic concentration gives only `O(log q)` excess; any superlogarithmic excess forces a specific dyadic scale with anomalous magnitude concentration. This is a much sharper diagnostic, but a magnitude-matched control still reproduces it exactly and phase information remains absent.

Weil Positivity has narrowed the Maaß--Selberg escape further. Generic self-adjointness, unitary-axis symmetry and positivity of the complete norm therefore still do not constrain the moved divisor. The surviving route must use specifically automorphic/local-global structure that couples the finite part to positive geometry in a way generic self-adjoint scattering cannot imitate.

Weil Inertia supplies a positive example of source correction removing a false resonance. WI-324--WI-326 show cubic coherence survives endpoint normalization, zero-set snapping and positive raw `Lambda` weighting. WI-327 restores the exact signed coefficient `Lambda-Lambda_X^sharp`; almost-all short-interval PNT estimates then force the twisted signed block below `(pi/384+o(1))L` on density-one starts. Generic cubic resonance is therefore killed by the actual source subtraction. The remaining barrier is de-exceptionalization: a distinguished bow may lie in the thin exceptional set, so one needs pointwise signed control or a structural reason bow starts cannot remain exceptional.

These lines support [MI-024](intuition/MI-024-control-subtraction-must-match-the-load-bearing-source-channel.md): a matched control or subtraction is useful only when it acts in the representation and norm carrying the alleged signal. Smooth density subtraction can miss sparse Hilbert energy; an exact source correction can cancel a generic resonance; a source-sensitive excess may still factor through a phase-blind magnitude quotient; and even a positive self-adjoint scattering realization may be too universal to constrain the divisor it contains.

Source restrictions matter only when they remove the *destination-relevant* matched fibre, not merely when they make reconstruction more expensive.
