## When does a richer observation create a discriminator rather than reconstruct, annihilate or reopen the old object?

[MI-028](intuition/MI-028-complete-measurement-families-can-close-an-escape-by-reconstructing-the-classical-object.md) remains the main closure audit, but Prime Lattice adds an important topology and nonlinear-law correction.

Farey Discrepancy gives a clean stable-inversion example. FD-191--FD-194 make the geometric square-root coverage threshold exact: below it, source null directions survive even under strong coordinatewise Möbius fidelity; at and above the sharp geometric constant, the cumulative source has an explicit contractive raw inverse. FD-195--FD-197 show why that does not make a scalar Farey energy equally informative. Weighted Fourier duality reaches the RH-facing Mertens exponent only on `gamma+sigma<=3/2`, while the classical Franel transfer remains on `gamma+sigma=2`; a squarefree Rademacher multiplicative source already saturates that noncritical second-moment line. Exact coordinate recovery, scalar quadratic energy and deterministic Möbius-specific coherence are therefore distinct resources.

Prime Circle still supplies the reconstruction boundary: sufficiently complete shell/signature families can recover the classical zero/source object rather than select RH. Prime Lattice shows that the opposite exact-stack boundary is topology-dependent. PL-354 annihilates the finite-support Dirichlet-polynomial family with an infinite exact real evaluation stack, but PL-355 moves to the natural prime `l2` completion and classical Seip theory produces nonzero functions vanishing on boundary Blaschke stacks accumulating at `1/2`. PL-356 makes the common nullspace infinite-dimensional and shows that every finite family of bounded homogeneous coefficient repairs still leaves infinitely many solutions.

PL-357--PL-358 now show that the first canonical nonlinear repair has no useful middle behavior either. Complete multiplicativity turns a square-summable Dirichlet series into a product Szegő reproducing kernel and makes it zero-free throughout the native `H^2` half-plane `Re s>1/2`; ordinary multiplicativity tensorizes into independent one-prime factors and allows an arbitrary point of `1/2<Re s<1` to be programmed as a zero with bounded coefficients. The missing family must constrain prime-power strings globally enough to defeat one-coordinate interpolation without collapsing to geometric kernel factors. “Add multiplicativity” is therefore not a sufficient specification of an intermediate source law.

Visual Exploration supplies the analytic-continuation version. Wang's log-source Green filter is invertible, but VIS-293 shows that first-half-plane holomorphy of the exact weighted source transform is already RH-equivalent. Recovering the source and demanding the target continuation are different operations; the latter can simply reinsert the zero divisor.

The common question is not whether more data or stronger algebra help. It is: **in the declared topology, what theorem makes the enlarged family sufficient, and what remains after that theorem is applied?** A useful intermediate structure must avoid automatic reconstruction of the classical object, algebraic annihilation, interpolation freedom reopened by completion, local zero programmability, over-rigid kernel collapse, or analytic continuation whose singularities are already the target zeros.

## Where does the obstruction move after every fixed-complexity regime closes?

Arithmetic Fidelity gives a sharp hierarchy of moving scales. AF-411--AF-412 close both tails for every fixed confluent order. AF-413--AF-416 locate and then close the full fixed-shift reciprocal-order diagonal `x=c/n`. AF-417 shows that linear derivative depth `s_n/n->sigma` renormalizes every fixed partition sector by `e^(sigma d)`.

AF-418 shows why fixed-sector limits are still not the full asymptotic state. For full-height rectangles `lambda=(r^n)`, absolute size changes at the new threshold `sigma=2`: the rectangles are superexponentially negligible for `sigma<2` and diverge in absolute value for `sigma>2`. At `sigma=2`, the finer variable `tau_n=((s_n/n)-2)log n` survives, with `(C_(n,r))^(1/n)->e^(r tau)a^r/(r!)^2`. Thus the pair `(c,sigma)` is sufficient for every fixed partition but not for the complete growing family. Below `sigma=2` the open problem is shape-uniform absolute resummation; at and above it, any persistence of the fixed-degree candidate requires genuinely signed global cancellation among growing sectors.

Nyman--Beurling separates source representation from final reconstruction cost. NB-210--NB-212 show that one fixed smooth full-von-Mangoldt barycenter already reaches the log-squared Vinogradov--Korobov corridor. NB-213 closes ordinary Mellin primitivization as a growth-class repair: replacing `-zeta'/zeta` by `log zeta` and then taking absolute values restores a factor `X` through the shell carrier. NB-214 strengthens that into a profile-uniform positive-shell obstruction. For every nonnegative `X`-dependent profile supported in a fixed logarithmic shell, the normalized primitive reconstruction kernel has `L1` norm at least `c X`. Positive shell preconditioning therefore cannot remove the positive-power tariff; the surviving route must exploit arithmetic/carrier cancellation before absolute values or leave the nonnegative fixed-shell class.

Weil Inertia closes the generic nonlinear-feature escape more broadly. WI-343--WI-347 show that every height-independent feature family with a height-uniform projective continuity radius `rho_epsilon` has effective rank bounded by `1+O(H Delta/rho_epsilon)`. At `H Delta<=1`, extensive rank forces the usable projective resolution radius itself to collapse like `O(1/r)`. “Use more nonlinear features” is therefore not a mechanism unless the map becomes increasingly source-resolving, ill-conditioned, height-dependent, wider-aperture, or the route bypasses generic rank through a direct arithmetic covariance identity.

Visual Exploration turns an apparent moving-scale error wall into a deterministic normalization plus one genuinely separate frontier. VIS-294 controls the lower moving-source family to absolute `H` accuracy for `x>>sqrt(log T)`, and VIS-295 extends relative gamma-subtracted linearization below that. VIS-296 reduces the finite relative crossover to the mean `Q_E` of the explicit-formula remainder. VIS-297 computes `Re Q_E=-log(2pi)+o(1)`: the entire order-one relative correction is the gamma normalization hidden by Wang's coarse baseline, not a new arithmetic source term. The relative crossover is therefore closed as a baseline effect; the unresolved lower question is the absolute-`H` regime near `x=O(sqrt(log T))` after deterministic normalization is removed.

Across these lines, increasing order, feature count, harmonic count or source motion matters only when the theorem is uniform in the moving parameter **and** the new parameter survives after reconstruction at destination scale. Fixed-complexity closure can be real, but the next candidate must identify the exact joint scaling, resolution modulus, tail variable or prefactor class that escapes it rather than merely increasing nominal breadth.

## Which physical cost survives exact-real coding, source minimization and destination compression?

[MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md) remains the main source-cost synthesis, now with sharper source-specific moving-rate and profile-uniform reconstruction refinements.

Möbius Cancellation has moved beyond every fixed polynomial common-source regime. MC-377 proves `log P/log y -> infinity` for the common lower-prime radical of an exact endpoint. MC-378 gives the first quantitative moving bound, including a rank-sensitive term of order `sqrt(R/log R)` and a generic analytic ceiling `log log log y`. MC-379 exploits the actual primitive squarefree endpoint source family: after pruning tiny source coordinates, a rough squarefree terminal block admits a fixed-power complete-sum saving without the generic `k2^k<<log log Q` absorption gate. This yields the complementary bound

`log P/log y >= c min{log(R+2), log log y}`.

The important point is not only the stronger iterated logarithm. The physical source class changed which generic analytic loss was actually payable. The remaining costs are source-specific `2^(O(A))` pruning/differencing losses and the still-uncombined rank regimes, not the old generic smooth-modulus ceiling by itself.

Robin Extremal has a sharp positive-window converse inside the separated ordinary-prime architecture. RE-209--RE-213 convert continuum hiding across a fixed logarithmic gap into a signed prime-prefix cost and prove `log R >= H T-O(sqrt(H T log(H T)))`. For `T_Q=O(log Q)`, preserving every fixed KV envelope forces `((1/2)log Q-H T_Q)/V(Q) -> +infinity`; the positive compact-window representation itself has reached its unit exponential rate ceiling.

Farey Discrepancy gives the destination-norm counterpoint. Stable raw inversion above the square-root coverage threshold does not survive automatically when compressed to the Franel/GCD quadratic energy, and generic multiplicative random signs already sit on the same noncritical weighted-energy line.

Nyman--Beurling adds a reconstruction counterpoint stronger than a single integration-by-parts example. NB-214 proves that any nonnegative source-normalized fixed-shell profile retains a central Fourier lobe of order `X`; changing the positive profile cannot prepay the inverse carrier cost. A claimed source saving must therefore be measured after reconstruction and source normalization, not at a smoother intermediate transform.

These results reinforce one rule: a source tariff must be stated in the currency forced by the declared physical map and then compared with what that source class can actually supply. Fixed conductor exponent, sample count, forward smoothness, raw injectivity, the size of an intermediate transform, or a generic theorem's parameter loss can all be the wrong currency.

## Can positivity preserve the critical amplitude before scalar, Rankin--Selberg or projective closure destroys it?

Weil Positivity now gives a three-stage negative classification of the direct Hecke/Eisenstein route. Nonconstant modes retain divisibility projections, but positive cusp-boundary squares scalarize them at `1/p` rather than the critical `p^(-1/2)` amplitude. The globally correlated scalar Eisenstein completion is a classical zeta product; its logarithmic derivative recovers Mangoldt half-density only by importing the zeta divisor as poles and abandoning inherited positivity.

WP-363 shows that retaining a genuinely non-scalar two-parameter Gram does not solve this. The Eisenstein Rankin--Selberg kernel is positive semidefinite for `s>1`, but its exact factor `1/zeta(2s)` makes the entire kernel vanish at `s=1/2`. The first nonzero critical jet is entrywise nonnegative yet indefinite.

WP-364 then removes the strongest scalar-normalization objection. Projectivizing the coefficient vectors cancels `1/zeta(2s)` intrinsically before continuation, but the continued normalized overlap becomes unbounded near every unconditional critical-line zero and violates `|C|<=1`; the continued Fubini--Study metric tends to `-infinity` on the same punctured neighborhoods. Scalar Gamma/completion factors are invisible to this geometry. The missing mechanism must therefore change the **nonseparable finite--archimedean correlations themselves** before critical specialization, not merely renormalize, complete or projectivize the bare coefficient Gram.

This remains aligned with the Prime-Lattice, Visual and Weil-Inertia controls. A critical boundary, complete form core, analytic continuation, enormous feature bank or canonical projective quotient can all be mathematically natural without selecting the Riemann zero divisor. The critical carrier must survive before the final square, local-factor decoupling, Rankin--Selberg diagonal normalization, topological closure or analytic completion, and an independent destination theorem must use it without reconstructing the whole classical criterion.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the admissible source class, source topology, observation family and nullspace, destination quotient and norm, physical source-coordinate map, acquisition budget, requested precision, normalization gauge, analytic domain/class, moving-complexity parameters, source support domain and granularity, sign/covariance structure and matched control. Then ask two opposite-direction questions in the same currency: **what does the destination force, and what can the physical source mechanism actually supply?**

The newest results sharpen several failure modes. A complete exact family may reconstruct the classical object, annihilate only a finite-support class, or reopen a large nullspace after completion. Multiplicativity can be too local to restrict zeros or so rigid that it collapses to zero-free kernels. A fixed-partition asymptotic can miss a growing-tail variable such as `((s/n)-2)log n`. A smoother source transform or positive shell profile can repay its apparent gain through source reconstruction. A generic moving-parameter theorem can charge a loss that disappears once the actual squarefree rough source geometry is used. A relative error boundary can reduce to deterministic gamma normalization. A non-scalar positive Gram can die at the critical exponent, while canonical projectivization removes its scalar zero only to produce unbounded overlaps and negative metric. Progress therefore requires a source-forced intermediate structure whose topology, moving-scale uniformity, reconstruction tariff, critical survival and destination leverage all survive these audits.
