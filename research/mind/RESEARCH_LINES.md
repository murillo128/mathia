## Which physical cost survives after representation artifacts are removed?

The newest results reinforce that the surviving currency is weighted, scale-aware and post-quotient. Arithmetic Fidelity replaces a single soft-sparsity parameter by the optimized support/tail/resolution curve `K_phi(delta)`. Farey shows that support conditioning and paired omission amplitudes are distinct currencies: parity dilation preserves the former, so any improvement must come from the latter or from source admissibility. Möbius adds interaction depth to source entropy: a `Theta(r log r)` relational alphabet can still be erased unless its divisor statistic has a top-order coefficient. Nyman--Beurling now separates aggregate linear mesh mass, diagonal quadratic energy and the full projected Gram norm; the explicit uniform carrier shows the first can survive while the second vanishes after damping, so off-diagonal target geometry cannot be omitted from the ledger. Prime Circle distinguishes arithmetic interaction from rough support and from one-step gap/star transport already determined by that support. Prime Lattice must price the regulator's cusp alias before crediting a zero-scale boundary signal. Visual Exploration replaces raw sample count by additive local Hermite-jet masses. Weil Inertia removes both the polar main term and stationary zero remainder before crediting the residual source-dependent correction. Weil Positivity distinguishes a source-forced critical Gram from programmable masks, the too-small divisibility diagonal, and the still-too-restrictive full bounded positive source algebra.

These are not one theorem and their currencies are not numerically interchangeable. The adversarial commonality remains the one recorded in MI-030 and MI-031: remove universal scaffolds, symmetry, known kernels, classical preselection and category-programmable choices first; then price every loss and compensating resource in the source-varying quantity that actually reaches the destination. The latest evidence sharpens several line-specific instances of that audit but does not justify a stronger universal mathematical bridge across the lines.
