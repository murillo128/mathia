## Global discriminator: identify the exact resource a matched control cannot fake

The current frontier is a classification of source-specific coupling that remains after every free closure, reconstruction, compatibility, quotient, background subtraction and matched-control operation. For each proposed route ask: what exact source distinction reaches the destination; what admissible source class is being compared; what information is regenerated for free; what acquisition or dual primitive is allowed and whether it has already encoded the target inverse; whether a finite-cutoff ambiguity survives only by escaping to infinity or into a flat boundary stratum; what finite-window information is lost by an infinitesimal limit; what subset of the declared observation is actually used by the inverse; what native observation norm gives a useful inverse modulus; what aggregate channel capacity survives at the downstream resolution, including adaptive side channels; whether a source-native dual witness becomes compact because of its **joint norm budget** and whether a larger coordinatewise family actually repays its explicit tariff through spectral spread; what cheaper destination-aligned moment detects and spatially prices a dangerous control; whether adding more scalar moments changes the leading capacity exponent or merely repeats the same envelope; what complexity an escaping compensation or renormalization must pay; whether an operator preserves oriented phase or only an autocorrelation quotient; what universal singular, pole-compensating or smooth low-frequency background must be subtracted; whether the theorem's source-window/support class actually contains the intended statistic and its remainder beats the residual scale; whether a claimed arithmetic height or positive source weight distinguishes the actual source from a nearby matched resonance; and what theorem finally converts the survivor into a Riemann-zero or positivity restriction.

A useful bridge should fail on a matched control for an independently arithmetic reason. Exact reconstruction, unbounded observation count, unrestricted target-aware mixing, source-specific labels, a critical boundary, a rich spectrum, a positive metric, a positive operator-valued lift, state-dependent scalarization, a nonzero low-frequency carrier, a large source-native Gram certificate, a small formal error, benign forward regularity, positive readout noise, adaptive query selection, a larger but unstructured probe family, finitely many extra scalar jets, membership in the zeta zero set, positive raw von Mangoldt weighting, a singular limit or an escaping eigenvalue are not enough unless the surviving quantity is stably distinguishable, theorem-accessible at the needed scale, survives the relevant background/complexity audit and is selectively coupled to the RH destination.
