This file records only cross-line questions that currently change how several Mathia research lines should be interpreted. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does a richer observation create a discriminator rather than reconstruct, annihilate or reopen the old object?

[MI-028](intuition/MI-028-complete-measurement-families-can-close-an-escape-by-reconstructing-the-classical-object.md) remains the main closure audit, now with both nonlinear-faithfulness and global-symmetry extremes sharpened.

Prime Circle supplies two reconstruction boundaries. Complete scalar shell data closes interpolation by recovering the classical zeta-zero support. PC-350 now shows that going all the way to a complete noncommutative reciprocity defect does not solve the selectivity problem either: a `2x2` all-depth decoder reconstructs the full centered reciprocal path, and the same decoder works for broad non-arithmetic reciprocal controls. Infinite depth is therefore source fidelity, not zero selection, unless a strict quotient/invariant is identified.

Prime Lattice shows that the opposite failure can occur after completion. Infinite exact evaluation stacks annihilate finite Dirichlet polynomials but reopen an infinite-dimensional nullspace in the natural prime `l2` completion. PL-359 adds a second topology warning: imposing the exact conductor-one Riemann functional equation with standard Hamburger regularity on native Dirichlet `H^2` leaves only zero, because the unique nonzero classical solution is zeta and zeta lies outside that Hilbert space. A canonical global relation can therefore overshoot the desired intermediate category instead of selecting it.

Visual Exploration supplies the continuation version. Exact source access plus first-half-plane holomorphy merely reinstates the RH divisor, while VIS-298 shows that an apparent new moving-source regime can disappear entirely once deterministic gamma structure is recombined before norm estimates.

The common question is now threefold: **what theorem makes the enriched family sufficient, is the resulting family nontrivial in the declared topology, and what selective quotient remains after reconstruction/closure?** More data, stronger global relations and richer nonlinear representations deserve credit only when all three gates survive matched controls.

## Where does the obstruction move after every fixed-complexity regime closes?

Arithmetic Fidelity now gives an exact two-phase example. AF-417 identifies linear derivative depth `sigma=s_n/n` as a second left-tail coordinate, and AF-418 shows that growing full-height partitions create an absolute-tail transition at `sigma=2`, with the finer critical coordinate `tau_n=((s_n/n)-2)log n`. AF-419 proves that this boundary is sharp for the absolute-uniform-integrability mechanism: throughout `limsup sigma<2`, the **full** exceptional-`1` partition tail is uniformly resummable and converges to the fixed-degree exponential. The unresolved regime is only critical/supercritical, where any continuation of that exponential must come from genuinely signed global cancellation rather than another absolute majorant. This is a useful control against both errors: a fixed-complexity asymptotic may miss a growing-tail coordinate, but one growing-family obstruction does not define a phase boundary until the opposite regime is uniformly closed.

Nyman--Beurling has moved the fixed-shell branch further. NB-214 ruled out positive shell preconditioning; NB-215 shows that signed/complex nonzero-mean profiles cannot remove the global absolute Mellin tariff either. They can only export it to source conditioning or frequency tails. The remaining branch must exploit actual arithmetic/carrier correlation before absolute values, use a genuinely different normalized signal such as a source-justified zero-mean/higher moment, or change the fixed-shell geometry.

Weil Inertia similarly closes another nominal escape. WI-347 priced projective resolution, while WI-348 restores absolute carrier phase and phase-saturates the source. At fixed bow aperture the resulting phase-height cylinder still has bounded fixed-resolution entropy; extensive output rank forces ordinary source resolution to collapse like `r^(-1/2)` or the sensitivity/transmission ratio to diverge. Global phase sensitivity is therefore not a cheap smooth source of extensive height information.

Visual Exploration removes rather than shifts the former lower moving-source boundary. VIS-298 proves that under one fixed upper exponent every `x(T)->infinity` is already resolved to absolute `o(H)` after exact gamma recentering. The earlier `sqrt(log T)` wall was an error-packaging artifact. The live visual questions are now fixed-power arithmetic improvement and theorem uniformity near the upper source ceiling, not a slower lower moving scale.

Across these lines, a moving frontier is real only when the new parameter survives exact normalization, reconstruction and source-class simplification **and** the neighboring regime is controlled uniformly enough to distinguish a true phase boundary from one bad family. If an apparent boundary disappears after recombination, merely choosing a different moving rate does not reopen it.

## Which physical cost survives source specialization, preconditioning and destination compression?

[MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md) remains the main source-cost synthesis, with several new separations between raw capacity, source-specific geometry and reconstruction tariffs.

Möbius Cancellation now combines its strongest rank and analytic regimes. MC-380 packs tiny source primes into a preliminary exact factor rather than deleting them, preserving the large smooth mode subspace while retaining roughness only where terminal complete-sum cancellation needs it. The current exact-endpoint source-cost lower bound reaches

`log P/log y >= c min{sqrt(R/log(R+2)), log log y}`.

This is a general warning against charging codimension merely because a nuisance coordinate is inconvenient at one proof stage: if it can be carried through an earlier exact factor, the physical source may pay less than the generic argument suggests.

Robin Extremal cleanly separates source capacity from source fidelity. RE-214 shows that bounded ordinary-prime multiplicity alone forces

`H T_Q <= (1/2)log Q+2 log log Q+O_H(1)`

for separated continuum hiding, excluding every fixed `c>1/(2H)` without any KV requirement on the modified source. KV fidelity is needed only to exclude the exact endpoint and enforce the stronger `omega(V(Q))` deficit. The leading obstruction above the endpoint is therefore carrier cardinality, not fine prime-distribution fidelity.

Farey Discrepancy adds a destination-norm counterpoint. FD-198 shows that the positive half-Sobolev budget sufficient for one RH-scale recovery would simultaneously force subpower RMS on a growing reciprocal-prime Mertens shell, while the generic random-multiplicative baseline sits at square-root RMS. The same positive norm is therefore both less efficient than raw rowwise inversion and much more coercive globally than the pointwise target suggests.

Nyman--Beurling adds the reconstruction version: signed preconditioning can move the Mellin cost but not remove its global absolute source-normalized size. Weil Inertia adds the resolution version: rapid phase winding does not create new fixed-resolution source states.

The shared rule is to price the route in the currency forced by the physical source and final destination **after** every exact source specialization, normalization and reconstruction. Then ask separately whether the source has enough raw capacity, whether finer arithmetic fidelity supplies the remaining margin, and whether the destination norm is demanding more simultaneous control than the target theorem actually needs.

## Can positivity preserve the critical amplitude before scalar, local-product or projective closure destroys it?

Weil Positivity now gives a four-stage negative classification of the direct Hecke/Eisenstein route. Nonconstant modes retain divisibility projections, but positive boundary squares scalarize the critical amplitude. Scalar Eisenstein completion recovers the desired logarithmic derivative only after importing the divisor. The finite Rankin--Selberg Gram is positive before continuation but dies at the Weil exponent; intrinsic projectivization removes the common scalar yet produces unbounded overlaps and negative Fubini--Study geometry near critical zeros.

WP-365 removes the strongest remaining local-completion objection. The independent Bessel/Whittaker sector is an honest positive archimedean Hilbert Gram and couples nonseparably to the spectral parameters. Tensoring it with the finite Hecke Gram gives a canonical positive finite-times-archimedean feature space and produces completed zeta factors automatically. Nevertheless the critical projective overlap still blows up and the metric still tends to `-infinity` near critical-line zeros.

The missing mechanism must therefore change the **non-product global correlation law**, not merely add another positive local factor, Gamma completion or scalar/projective normalization. A viable source-forced form must retain the critical half-density before specialization and obtain its sign from a genuinely noncentral interaction, remainder, cohomological/intersection structure or comparable mechanism whose positivity theorem is independent of the divisor being constrained.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the admissible source class, source topology, observation family and nullspace, destination quotient and norm, physical source-coordinate map, acquisition budget, requested precision, normalization gauge, analytic domain/class, moving-complexity parameters, source support/multiplicity constraints, sign/covariance structure and matched control. Then ask in sequence: **what survives exact reconstruction and normalization; is the completed category nontrivial; what inverse resource does the destination force; can the physical source actually pay it; and does a matched non-arithmetic/control family reproduce the survivor?**

The newest results sharpen several ways a seemingly stronger route can fail. Complete nonlinear depth can be a faithful generic source decoder. A canonical functional equation can annihilate the chosen Hilbert class. Signed preconditioning can export rather than erase a tariff. A nuisance source factor can sometimes be packed rather than paid as lost rank. Raw carrier capacity can impose a barrier before arithmetic fidelity becomes relevant. A positive critical energy can overcoerce many source coordinates simultaneously. Rapid phase winding can have bounded metric entropy. An apparent moving error wall can vanish after exact deterministic recombination. A growing-tail obstruction can coexist with a fully controlled subcritical phase and only become a genuine transition at the uniform boundary. And even a canonical product of independently positive finite and archimedean Grams can lose Hilbert positivity at the target divisor.

Progress therefore requires an intermediate structure that is nontrivial in the declared topology, cheaper than full source reconstruction, source-forced rather than matched-control generic, stable at the relevant moving resolution, and coupled to a destination theorem without rebuilding the original RH criterion in disguise.
