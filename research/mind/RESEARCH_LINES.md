## When does an escape from one obstruction survive the next reduction?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit, [MI-030](intuition/MI-030-effective-complexity-is-a-geometry-weighted-budget.md) sharpens the resource side, [MI-031](intuition/MI-031-source-dependence-must-change-the-surviving-quotient-not-just-the-representation.md) sharpens the provenance side, and [MI-032](intuition/MI-032-ambient-obstructions-matter-only-on-the-admissible-image.md) adds the reachability side: an ambient bad direction or apparent category enlargement matters only when the actual admissible image reaches it after the exact quotient/category map.

Arithmetic Fidelity has separated metric deformation, hard source restriction and finite-resolution soft restriction. AF-475 converts a source compressibility profile `tau_s<=phi(s)` into the achieved coordinate condition curve `K_phi(delta)`, while AF-476 gives the complementary nonlinear lower bound on the full profile envelope through realizable head-plus-private-tail cubes. AF-477 now closes a distinct finite-side-information escape for full positive moment fibers: affine rank `d` still leaves some `r`-sparse fiber with an exact Hamming cube of dimension `floor(r/(d+1))`, hence Hilbert distortion at least its square root. The arithmetic subclass inherits these lower bounds only when the corresponding source switches are actually admissible. Its live problem is therefore to determine both the prime-derived approximation profile and the realizable-complexity profile, and to identify any additional arithmetic restriction beyond finite affine moments that excludes the generic Radon switches while preserving a rational-prime discriminator.

Farey Discrepancy has separated support conditioning from the actual omission coefficient map. FD-252 shows that exact-omission support geometry can retain arbitrarily bad induced-divisor conditioning. FD-253 then proves that the bad support direction can be unreachable: isolated omissions impose adjacent opposite pairs, and when companion rows close the inverse is controlled by `kappa(E+1)`. Large dilations make the companions an antichain and keep the paired inverse linear in the omission count even while the full support norm is super-polylogarithmically bad. The live response problem is now companion-side conditioning or start-to-companion coupling inside the paired image, not a large support norm by itself.

Möbius Cancellation has moved beyond raw relational capacity. MC-436 shows that global prime-gap order can attain `(r-1)!=N_r^(1-o(1))` source types, while MC-437 says divisor Möbius convolution keeps only the top Boolean interaction of the induced divisor statistic. A surviving gap-order route must therefore use a genuinely global/nonadditive or growing-depth statistic whose full interaction is computed before source capacity is credited.

Nyman--Beurling has now separated logarithmic aggregate Ford coercivity from the target quadratic norm. NB-264--NB-267 force `Omega(m)` aggregate mesh response down to `O(log K)` rank-coupled coverage under bounded resolved cost. NB-268 gives a legal uniform positive carrier with the same aggregate linear obstruction but only `sum |D_n|^2 asymp m`; after the live `1/m` damping its diagonal quadratic diagnostic is `asymp 1/m -> 0`. Thus neither generic `ell^1`-to-`ell^2` conversion nor diagonal sample energy bridges NB-267 to the Hardy/Nyman distance. The decisive object is now the actual projected Gram form on that carrier: a lower bound would expose the missing off-diagonal coercivity, while decay would exhibit a genuine escape candidate. Sublogarithmic aggregate minimality remains a separate source-side question.

Prime Circle now has a support-and-transport classicalization boundary that extends through simultaneous linear Kron deletion. PC-384--PC-387 complete the scalar full/primitive inverse-power phase diagram, PC-388 identifies the primorial primitive boundary with the classical rough-number wheel, and PC-389--PC-390 reduce one-step pairwise/Laplacian/Kron transport to Buchstab deletion plus the deleted incident star. Visual Exploration has closed another nominal compact resource. VIS-358--VIS-360 show that regular dense clusters, separated multiclusters and colliding centers all reduce to the same fixed-depth occupancy-width law once the correctly rescaled polynomial-sampling Gram is nondegenerate. ## Which physical cost survives after representation artifacts are removed?

The newest results sharpen the distinction between an ambient resource and a reachable one. Arithmetic Fidelity now shows that even positivity plus a hard support budget and finitely many affine moments still permit large exact source-point cubes in some fibers; a thinner arithmetic image needs additional restrictions that actually remove those switches. Farey shows the converse phenomenon: a huge ambient support inverse can be irrelevant when the paired omission map misses its bad directions. Visual Exploration says positive weight imbalance is irrelevant once the weighted sampling image is nondegenerate. Weil Positivity says even a genuinely unbounded positive operator is not a new selector resource if its canonical resolvent maps it back into the already-classified bounded source algebra. These are line-specific instances of MI-032, not a common numerical theorem.

The other current currencies remain equally post-quotient. Möbius adds full interaction survival to source entropy: factorial relational capacity can collapse to endpoint-only data. Nyman--Beurling separates aggregate linear mesh mass, diagonal quadratic energy and the full projected Gram norm. Prime Circle now separates the full simultaneous Kron response from its genuinely new deleted-vertex contrast sector, and then shows that sector has vanishing rank fraction for bulk spectral purposes. Prime Lattice prices and removes every fixed finite regulator jet before crediting an endpoint zero-scale signal. Weil Inertia removes both the polar main term and stationary zero remainder before crediting the residual source-dependent correction.

These are not one theorem and their currencies are not numerically interchangeable. The adversarial commonality recorded in MI-030--MI-032 is an order of proof: remove universal scaffolds, symmetry, known kernels, classical preselection and category-programmable choices; restrict to the actual admissible image; then price every loss and compensating resource in the source-varying quantity that reaches the destination. The latest evidence sharpens several line-specific instances of that audit but does not justify a stronger universal mathematical bridge across the lines.
