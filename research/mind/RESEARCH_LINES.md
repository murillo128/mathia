## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than another absorbed, nonidentified or overpriced channel?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit. The latest results sharpen it at finite-data source-class fidelity, exact endpoint collapse, operator-domain/globalization boundaries, faithful arithmetic decomposition and topology of the final selector.

## Where has the exact obstruction moved after the latest boundary results?

**Source class before inversion.** Arithmetic Fidelity now brackets finite-moment recovery rather than giving only a no-go. AF-439 shows that movable positive Stieltjes support is nonidentified at every fixed depth; AF-440 shows that exact reciprocal-square support with separated discrete marks gives linear-depth prefix recovery. The next theorem should interpolate between those source classes and then price the surviving margin, instead of treating either broad nonidentifiability or rigid exact recovery as universal.

**Finite stages before global coherence.** Farey Discrepancy classifies all fixed switched blocks but not one cofinal source. PC-362 shows that every finite triangular truncation can miss an infinite-depth domain transition. PL-379 shows that all finite-coordinate Wold decompositions can coexist with a diffuse global type field having no atomic Wold decomposition. Weil Inertia shows that local threshold control need not iterate cofinally. These are distinct mechanisms, but together they force a separate globalization theorem whenever the destination consumes an infinite family.

**Formal dimension before retained arithmetic coupling.** Möbius Cancellation removes source-mode, divisor-pair, first-correlation lcm phase, invertible hyperbola endpoint and now the complete lcm endpoint as independent dimensions. Visual Exploration goes the other way: its faithful chart survives current reductions and VIS-322 exposes a smaller exact object, fixed-gap diagonal runs whose fragmentation is explicitly priced. One line now needs new coupling; the other has reduced its coupling object enough to ask a recognizable arithmetic-distribution question.

**Marked local statistics before marginal equidistribution.** Nyman--Beurling now shows that even the right carrier frequency and right short window are insufficient if horizontal depth is projected away. The Ford damping is a mark attached to each zero; phase marginal equidistribution can coexist with an order-one weighted residue. The live source theorem must control the joint depth--phase law or an equivalent weighted coefficient.

**Local normal form before global cost.** Robin Extremal now closes both positive-real and finite-ray complex large-center tuning at finite load. Complex phase changes the effective radial predictor but cannot discount the Wiener budget, so a new route must leave that finite-ray single-center geometry rather than refine it.

**Positive geometry before selector topology.** Weil Positivity shows two distinct failure modes. Exact radial mixed-shell nullity collapses to an invariant sector; canonical nonradial Hardy positivity keeps cross-prime interactions but puts the exact Mangoldt selector on an unbounded boundary. The next sign mechanism must solve both incidence and functional-analytic compatibility rather than alternate between them.

Analytic Frontier, Prime Flute and Xi Flow retain their existing local boundaries; no new persisted evidence in the inspected change stream alters those Mind states.

## Which physical cost survives once representation artifacts are removed?

The cost ledger is increasingly source-relative. Arithmetic Fidelity prices the structural assumptions needed to make finite data target-pure and then the quantitative separation margin. Farey prices uniformity needed to pass from finite repair cones to growing/cofinal source identity. Möbius prices source-signature coefficient structure after exact regrouping, inversion and full-endpoint factorization. Nyman--Beurling prices a local **marked** depth--phase carrier statistic that neither global averaging nor unweighted local phase balance controls. Prime Circle prices critical/domain or nontriangular information beyond subcritical raising perturbations. Prime Lattice prices a source-forced global condition that rules out generic diffuse infinite-prime type fields without collapsing to a classical scalar model. Robin prices a geometry outside the finite-ray single-center family after complex drift fails to reduce the six-plus Wiener floor. Visual Exploration prices fixed-gap prime-pair occupancy together with the center-gap fragmentation count and Wang taper. Weil Inertia prices actual arithmetic-channel coherence and noncompact tail retention beyond scalar graph/norm envelopes and compact repair. Weil Positivity prices a topology in which the linear Mangoldt selector and genuine mixed-prime incidence can participate in the same sign-producing construction.

The reusable question is: **after the strongest exact source specialization, faithful representation cleanup, identifiability test, inversion audit and required globalization step, what quantity must still be paid by the physical source rather than by coordinates, metric, cutoff, completion, normalization, projection, exact reconstruction, correlation expansion, spectral type or quantifier choice?** A claimed obstruction is credible only when it survives the matched control in variables consumed by the final theorem.

## Can sparse, boundary or deep defects be made visible without strengthening the whole statistic?

Several lines say yes, but only by matching the observable to the defect. Nyman--Beurling now requires the joint depth-marked carrier harmonic rather than either a global pair statistic or an unweighted local phase marginal. Visual Exploration retains both prime coordinates and the center-gap run decomposition rather than projecting to a determinant. Robin Extremal resolves the zero-rate boundary with a `log(m)/m` detuning coordinate but then shows complex finite-ray drift still pays the same global cost. Weil Positivity distinguishes a positive interior Hardy norm from the singular boundary operation that actually recovers `Lambda`. Arithmetic Fidelity shows that exact global carrier information can be operationally insufficient on a movable finite-data class yet surprisingly strong on a rigid discrete support class. Prime Circle and Prime Lattice add a different warning: a defect may live only in the infinite-depth/domain or diffuse global completion and therefore be invisible to every fixed finite stage.

The common design principle is to identify the **destination-sensitive functional, admissible source class, active source range, inverse/identifiability requirement, finite-to-global requirement, retained marks and theorem quantifier first**. If exact algebra, nonidentifiability, unstable inversion, nonuniform globalization, marginalization, global averaging, normalization, projection, scalarization, positive nullity or an incompatible topology has already erased the defect, a sharper estimate of the same compressed statistic will not restore it.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the source class, parameter provenance, source carrier and resolution, finite-data fibre, inverse/recovery conditioning, source-to-representation transport, observation family and nullspace, destination quotient, preserved fibre coordinates, retained marks, retained arithmetic dimension and aggregation length, exact Fourier/Gelfand/lcm/triangular/similarity reduction, first-correlation separability, exact invertibility, finite-to-global completion law, metric covariance, positive radical, invariant sector, perturbation topology, selector regularity, analytic divisor/gauge freedom, source mesh, relation codimension and conductor cost, active carrier range, local-versus-global quantifier, normalization map, defect scale and destination margin.

Then ask: **what genuinely new source information is acquired; is it identifiable on the admissible class; by what exact and stably usable map does it reach the destination; what exact reduction, inversion, marginalization or averaging could absorb it; what theorem globalizes the finite/local statements; what matched control does it break; and what physical cost remains after the most faithful representation is used?** A route that survives this audit has isolated a mathematical bottleneck. A route that fails it has usually rediscovered a provenance, completion-fibre, conditioning, invertibility, similarity, gauge, separability, conductor, spectral, normalization, projection, topology, radical, averaging or quantifier artifact in different notation.
