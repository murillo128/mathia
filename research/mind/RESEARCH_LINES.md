## Where has the exact obstruction moved?

Arithmetic Fidelity: to **uniform growing-depth conditioning, genuinely larger observation horizons or stronger cross-sample noise structure, and a rational-prime discriminator that the matched Beurling controls cannot preserve**, rather than to more samples inside the same critical horizon. Farey: to **a lower bound for the positive slack actually required by the Mertens repair**, compared against the current general and monotone ceilings.

Möbius: in the gap branch to **growing rank, a genuinely different source measure/coordinate system, absolute scale or discontinuous arithmetic data**; in the q-vdC branch to **structured pre-recombination source coupling, factorization or collective variable-length estimates**, because both one-lcm generic envelopes and bare synchronized-lcm recombination are power-closed. Nyman--Beurling: simultaneously to **the established NB-272 section-defect/anchor-mismatch fork** and, on the independent source-native route, to **a finite legal regularization of the exact differentiated Möbius current that controls the logarithmic derivative and Hardy topology without moving the Ford scale**. The proposed NB-273--NB-275 claims remain outside this synthesis.

Prime Circle: to **the nonsummable `alpha<1/2` regime, justified renormalized observables, cross-level contrast-subspace information or a different operator category**, not longer fixed polynomial windows even at the critical endpoint. Prime Lattice: to **the PL-407-scale signed mod-5 block supertrace and a post-subtraction divisor-faithful sign/reality theorem that fails under off-line controls**. Visual Exploration: to **a net destination gain after capacity, coefficient topology and the complete relative-metric geometry are charged, with any noncommuting orientation forced by structure external to a single source-free Gram**. Weil Inertia: to **a phase-stable use of spectral mass that genuinely escapes uniform logarithmic tail compactness**. Weil Positivity: to **a source-forced joint relation that makes the fixed-shadow tangent family genuinely asymmetric, or a different nonlinear/singular/unbounded category**.

## Which physical cost survives after representation artifacts are removed?

The newest results sharpen the common order of proof. AF-488 shows that accumulated observations do not buy resolution when they remain inside the same coherent-noise horizon. MC-450 shows that exact frequency synchronization does not buy cancellation when generic recombination returns the original autocorrelation. NB-276--NB-277 show that an exact source identity can still be unusable until the derivative, continuation and regularization costs are paid. PC-396 shows that a critical endpoint can close only after its borderline degree/overlap scale is priced. VIS-371 shows that coordinate-free one-Gram equivariance itself forces spectral filtering. WI-390 shows that source perforation must be priced in the same logarithmic compactness currency as the destination sampling density. WP-399 shows that retaining a non-scalar positive shadow still does not help if its admissible normal tangent geometry remains densely sign-symmetric.

[MI-034](intuition/MI-034-positivity-is-informative-only-when-the-induced-constraint-remains-proper-after-the-destination-quotient.md) records the cross-line positivity version of the audit. A positive representation counts only when its cone/metric is independently fixed, excludes relevant matched controls, and remains a nontrivial asymmetric constraint after the actual quotient or destination map. The broader durable order remains: quotient universal scaffolds first, restrict to the actual admissible image and source measure, identify the carrier that survives completion, recombination or continuation, and then price its amplitude, conditioning, occupancy, target projection, sampling density, measure class, tangent geometry, topology and provenance.
