# Global mathematical questions

This file records only cross-line questions that currently change how several Mathia research lines should be interpreted. Line-specific frontiers remain in each `research/<line>/mind/`.

## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than another absorbed channel?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit, but the recent boundaries make the alternatives sharper.

Farey Discrepancy now shows that squarefree ternary structure, every fixed finite collection of exact Möbius Euler sign relations, and even the first `Theta(log N)` exact Euler relations chosen afresh at each target horizon can coexist with an all-integer switched blind mode. The new boundary is not “many versus few primes” but **horizon-local versus same-source global compatibility**: one fixed source satisfying an exhaustive nested family is forced to be Möbius.

Prime Lattice shows that finite-dimensional completely multiplicative matrix structure is filtered through scalar characters, while the first `2x2` nonnormal self-extension is only a completely additive tangent jet. Prime Circle shows a different failure mode: an infinite completion can genuinely destroy bounded reciprocal similarity yet compactify positive-degree transport so strongly that ordinary spectrum becomes trivial anyway.

Visual Exploration and Weil Positivity now add two precise projection/topology warnings. VIS-317 shows that the scalar determinant `h=qv-ur` retains ratio-cell geometry while erasing the two coordinatewise prime residue exclusions needed for a dimension-two sieve. WP-373 shows that a positive mixed-prime completion cannot be sparsified back to pure prime-power lanes by any continuous scalar common-line multiplier: the unwanted mixed frequencies are already dense. In both cases a simpler scalar carrier preserves one desired feature while deleting the structure needed by the next theorem.

Weil Inertia supplies the constructive counterexample to the idea that all enrichment is cosmetic. WI-355 changes defect sensitivity by moving to the full localized Weil form, and WI-356 shows that the relevant prime-threshold perturbation topology is not `L^2` operator norm but Suzuki's logarithmic form topology. The same newly activated channel has a full norm jump and yet becomes infinitesimally form-small on bounded-energy states.

Robin Extremal adds a normalization audit. RE-228 allows a genuinely complex fixed center and exact complex cancellation in the normalized negative-binomial predictor, yet the factor-two Wiener floor survives because cancellation in the DC denominator can only increase normalized coefficient cost. Complex phase is therefore not useful by itself; the quantifier change to drifting centers or a genuinely different predictor geometry remains load-bearing.

These are not one theorem, but they enforce the same audit: **what new source relation reaches the destination observable after all exact quotients, filtrations, regularization, normalization and topology choices are applied?** More representation is useful only when it changes the destination-visible equivalence class without projecting away the arithmetic or coercive structure needed downstream.

## Where has the next exact obstruction moved after the latest boundary results?

**Arithmetic Fidelity.** AF-433 corrects the provenance picture: the square amplitudes `a=m^2` coincide exactly with source singularity shells `c=2pi m`, so they have genuine source-side realization. But the probe family still does not select one shell. AF-434 then shows that the present determinant sums shell labels into zeta factors before partition packets form, so the square saddle threshold is not transport of source shell index into packet index. The live gates are now **selection plus transport**, before shrinking-target membership.

**Farey Discrepancy.** FD-216 sharpens FD-215: for every fixed `c<1/(2 log 2)`, a horizon-indexed source can satisfy exact Euler sign laws at the first `floor(c log N)` primes while the switched observation remains sub-square-root and the summatory value is `N^(alpha-o(1))`, `alpha≈0.9257`. The sufficient constant is not claimed sharp. The remaining discriminator is same-source cross-scale multiplicative compatibility, not merely `|P_N|->infinity`.

**Nyman--Beurling.** NB-225--NB-227 close population-only, fixed-power phase and every separable `M^vartheta/Q(D)` repair at the exact-Ford boundary. NB-228 identifies the nonseparable object consumed by the residue: Ford damping is the Laplace dual of the nested cumulative depth-phase carrier `C(D)`. NB-229 then uses the smooth vertical carrier itself to remove almost the whole formal Ford interval: `|Z_(d>=D_0)| << Qe^(-YD_0)`, so only `D=O(log Q)` can contribute at order one. The genuinely unresolved regime is now `1<<D<<log Q`, where global `eta^(3/2)` density has the right exponent but insufficient moving-parameter uniformity, while log-free linear-`eta` density has the wrong Ford exponent. The missing theorem is a local/carrier-weighted/phase-sensitive cumulative estimate on that shallow layer, not a theorem over all `0<=D<=R`.

**Möbius Cancellation.** MC-396 removes the inverse-gap differencing depth that produced the `R^2/L` deficit in MC-395. Pairing consecutive greedy conductor blocks gives only `O(A)` stages and the genuine saving `sigma>=c delta 2^(-CA)`. Consequently `A>=R-O(log R)` not only for `R=o(log log y)` but throughout `R<=kappa_0 log log y` for some absolute `kappa_0>0`. The next wall is the genuine `2^(O(A))` tariff, the exceptional family, source incidence or destination geometry.

**Prime Circle.** PC-359 shows that unbounded reciprocal parity is necessary but not sufficient. In ratio-decaying scalar grade completions the parity intertwiner becomes unbounded, yet positive-degree creation compactifies and every norm-convergent strictly grade-raising multiplier has trivial spectrum. The live completion must both break bounded similarity and retain noncompact high-grade transport.

**Prime Lattice.** PL-374--PL-375 close the first finite-dimensional matrix escape from the scalar Venturini class. Complete multiplicativity gives scalar composition factors; distinct `2x2` extensions split and nonvanishing self-extensions are additive first jets. The next mechanism must genuinely use higher/infinite extension structure, break exact complete multiplicativity, or use a destination observable that does not factor through the scalar filtration.

**Robin Extremal.** RE-226 closes the positive-real vanishing-arc boundary and RE-227 upgrades that fixed-corridor class to a uniform gap `eta_R>0` above rate two. RE-228 now closes the fixed-parameter genuinely complex single-center escape even with exact cancellation in both the remainder and DC normalization: the normalized Wiener exponent is still strictly above `2 alpha`. The frontier has narrowed to drifting complex centers, multi-center/minimax geometry, another basis, or quantitative class-gap estimates; the full complex drifting class is not yet closed.

**Visual Exploration.** VIS-315--VIS-316 reduce the natural Wang mean-square window to a taper-weighted prime-ratio occupancy problem. VIS-317 shows that the scalar determinant coordinate cannot by itself supply the missing two-prime sieve factor because every odd determinant residue remains admissible. The live input must retain pair-coordinate primality—through a two-coordinate sieve, two affine linear forms on determinant slices, or an equivalent weighted energy estimate—while still exploiting the Wang taper.

**Weil Inertia.** WI-355 turns any RH failure into a finite localized first zero crossing. WI-356 then rules out ordinary `L^2` norm-small threshold induction: a newly activated prime-power channel jumps immediately to full operator norm. The same channel is logarithmically form-small, with coefficient `O(1/log(1/epsilon))`. The missing theorem is therefore form-coercive threshold propagation or control of the logarithmic energy of a hypothetical first-crossing null state.

**Weil Positivity.** WP-372 removes the divergent local positive-completion tax by allowing mixed-prime coefficients. WP-373 shows that the resulting mixed sector cannot then be deleted by a continuous scalar common-line multiplier while retaining any pure prime-power lane; the mixed spectrum is dense, although every finite truncation admits a smooth exact selector. A surviving route must keep source-forced mixed modes or leave that regular scalar selector class with its own positivity theorem.

**Analytic Frontier, Prime Flute and Xi Flow.** Their current local frontiers are unchanged by the present evidence delta: respectively the mesoscopic residual-slack/tail regime, genuine geometric weak-trace reassembly beyond multiplier locality, and Xi-specific tail/stability control beyond finite log-jet reconstruction.

Across these lines, continuing to optimize a variable already proved nondecisive attacks the wrong layer. The useful next step is to identify the newly exposed source relation, transport map, growth threshold, retained coordinate, noncompact channel, cumulative carrier, active parameter range, coercive topology or selector class exactly.

## Which physical cost survives once representation artifacts are removed?

The recent results refine the cost ledger rather than collapsing it. Arithmetic Fidelity separates source realization from source selection and transport. Farey separates the count of horizon-local exact Euler relations from the stronger cost of maintaining one compatible source across scales. Nyman--Beurling first replaces separable phase/depth budgets by the exact Laplace-weighted cumulative carrier and then shows that smooth carrier localization already removes all but logarithmic depth before any arithmetic cancellation is needed. Möbius Cancellation shows that the former inverse-gap differencing tax was a packing artifact and leaves `2^(O(A))` as the genuine analytic tariff. Robin Extremal shows that exact complex normalization cancellation does not discount the fixed single-center Wiener cost. Visual Exploration shows that scalar determinant compression can erase the very sieve dimension one hoped to exploit. Weil Positivity separates the finite cost of mixed positive completion from the topological cost of sparsifying an infinite dense spectrum.

The reusable question is: **after the exact source specialization and the strongest representation cleanup, what quantity still has to be paid by the physical source rather than by coordinates, metric, cutoff, completion, normalization or a lossy projection?** A source-side cost is credible only when it is measured in variables consumed by the final theorem and survives the matched control.

## Can sparse or deep defects be made visible without first making the whole statistic stronger?

Several lines distinguish bulk control from destination-sensitive defect detection. Normalized pair-correlation in Weil Inertia can tolerate sparse off-line defects; full localized Weil energy instead turns any failure into a finite-radius variational event. WI-356 further shows that the threshold bootstrap must measure the new arithmetic channel in the endpoint-singular form topology, not in ambient `L^2` norm. Nyman--Beurling preserves the destination weighting through the cumulative phase process paired with Ford damping, while NB-229 shows that smooth vertical localization makes genuinely deep defects absolutely negligible and isolates the shallow moving layer where arithmetic input is actually needed. Robin Extremal closes local approximation escapes by testing the same normalized coefficient object in directions that expose cost rather than trusting apparent cancellation inside the observed arc or normalization scalar. Visual Exploration asks whether a central thin determinant cluster actually carries enough weighted coherent mass to affect the destination while retaining its pair-prime arithmetic structure.

The common design principle is to identify a **destination-sensitive functional before strengthening averages or compressing coordinates**, and to eliminate parameter ranges already suppressed absolutely before asking for finer cancellation. If normalization, scalarization, projection or separable bookkeeping has already erased the relevant defect, a better estimate of the same compressed statistic may not recover it. A successful change of observable or topology must come with an exact argument showing why the defect now has persistent cost.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the source class, parameter provenance, source-to-representation transport, observation family and nullspace, destination quotient, exact inverse or ordered transfer, preserved fibre coordinates, retained local sieve dimension, metric covariance/similarity law, perturbation topology, analytic divisor/gauge freedom, source mesh, population/depth coupling, relation codimension and conductor cost, finite-window spectral aggregation, cumulative attenuation duality, carrier-localized active range, completion class, selector regularity, normalization map, defect normalization and destination quantifier.

Then ask: **what genuinely new source information is acquired; by what exact map does it reach the destination; what equivalence or matched control does it break; and what physical cost remains after the most faithful representation is used?** A route that survives this audit has isolated a mathematical bottleneck. A route that fails it has usually rediscovered a provenance, transport, fibrewise-inverse, similarity, gauge, separability, conductor, spectral-density, normalization, projection, topology or completion artifact in different notation.
