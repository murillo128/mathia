## Preserve the target/source relation through the exact quotient, not merely the ambient geometry

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-preserve-structure-before-compressing`, `MI-002-the-surviving-arithmetic-variable-is-relational`, `MI-003-arithmetic-significance-begins-after-universal-control-subtraction`, `MI-006-classify-the-boundary-model-before-calling-it-arithmetic`.

Several new boundaries now make this rule concrete. Farey Discrepancy shows that cumulative harmonic, Franel, and GCD geometry is exactly a Mertens transform/energy, so generic quadratic richness adds no independent channel. Nyman--Beurling shows the complementary loss: the complete generator Gram matrix can stay fixed while target distance changes, and canonical semigroup copies preserve local Gram geometry while global target mass decays like `1/m`. Prime Circle keeps the full fresh-prime mode vector and still gets only finite fixed-base cyclotomic Cauchy packets; repeated-prime depth then aliases through the squarefree radical.

The cross-line question is therefore: **what relation survives after the natural algebra/module/energy/target quotient, and is that relation source-selected?** A larger state space, exact recovery, positive quadratic form, or self-similar local block is not enough when the decisive target pairing, sign, chronology, or mixed-prime relation has already been compressed away.

## Require quantitative transport only after the remaining loss mechanism is identified

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-007-survival-requires-quantitative-non-escape-not-just-nonzero-structure`, `MI-008-exact-recovery-is-not-stable-transport`.

Arithmetic Fidelity now has global Euler sign regularity through order three and arbitrary prescribed finite order on separated-curvature windows, while faithful translation reparameterizations are affinely rigid even in vector-valued/stable approximate form. The right transport theorem may therefore be a target-specific quotient, not increasingly elaborate tomography.

## Distinguish source relation from symmetry, state freedom, and sign-programmable restriction

Prime Lattice and Weil Positivity now sharpen a common selection discipline from opposite sides. Prime Lattice shows that full prime exchangeability gives only vacuum normal equilibrium on the integer representation, ordinary quasi-local completion gives de Finetti product mixtures, and fixed finite arithmetic colors still leave fatal infinite symmetry blocks. A useful equilibrium construction must introduce sufficiently fine rational-prime relations before state evaluation.

Weil Positivity already has a source-forced critical Poisson/GCD Gram but the Weil defect has wrong essential orientation. Invariant source submodules cannot remove both signs; arbitrary coinvariant model spaces can instead program either sign. Thus a positive quotient is meaningful only when the source canonically selects it before sign inspection. The open operation must change the essential orientation or derive the quotient/shorting from global arithmetic structure, including the archimedean/polar sectors.

## Demand collective complexity when bounded probes are algebraically screenable

Weil Inertia extends the positive-observation warning. Single-window and PSD multiwindow lifts cannot amplify an isolated residue, and every fixed finite reciprocal-harmonic packet can be screened exactly by only finitely many deeper off-line pairs at negligible count cost. A viable defect bootstrap must therefore charge radial depth, use frequency complexity growing with the bow, impose source-specific restrictions on screens, or attack the full signed von-Mangoldt covariance.

Möbius Cancellation shows a parallel trichotomy: finite coefficient/twist operations yield phase-blind size or local phase selection, while the natural signed heat orbit becomes RH-equivalent and long positive windows wash phase out. In both lines, adding bounded channels is weaker than deriving the collective signed source relation.

## Treat information admission, amplitude, matched-null significance, and target conditioning as separate gates

Visual Exploration has isolated a collision-free third-factorial channel that escapes fixed-block/two-point information, but its null mean depends strongly on the exact source and window: independent phases, full Haar CUE, and a finite wrapped CUE block are not interchangeable. Source significance requires the transformed statistic's matched finite-window covariance after the selection rule is frozen.

Robin Extremal supplies a different kind of source-selected local gate. RH failure would force infinitely many regular self-tangent CA peaks, and genuine CA transitions make their secant condition asymptotically a half-boundary-mass event-gap requirement. The remaining theorem is about the actual event process, not continuous rounding heuristics.

A no-go theorem only defines the next admissible category. Farey Discrepancy needs staircase constraints or pre-cumulative order; Nyman--Beurling needs target-aware multiscale projection control; Analytic Frontier needs fatal non-excisability; Arithmetic Fidelity needs a tail-uniform finite-order modulus; Prime Circle needs squarefree-radical growth or an operation on packet state; Prime Flute needs actual-seam locality then physical/global reassembly; Prime Lattice needs a fine source relation before equilibrium; Möbius Cancellation needs an intermediate signed cancellation currency; Visual Exploration needs the exact finite-window third-factorial null; Weil Inertia needs collective/growing-complexity screening control; Weil Positivity needs a source-derived sign-changing operation or quotient; Xi Flow needs a transition-specific stable quotient; Robin Extremal needs exclusion of the self-tangent half-mass event gaps.

“Not ruled out,” “can carry information,” “is positive after a chosen compression,” and “is exactly reconstructive” are frontier conditions, not conclusions.
