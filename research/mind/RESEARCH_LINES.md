## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than another absorbed or ill-conditioned channel?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) remains the common interface audit, and the newest results sharpen it in several independent categories.

Arithmetic Fidelity now separates exact retention, representation and stable recovery even more sharply. AF-437 shows that sequential peeling of shell `m` amplifies every earlier-shell error by `(m/j)^(2k)`, but AF-438 exhibits an exact non-peeling Stieltjes carrier: `F(z)=sum_m 1/(m^2-z)` carries every shell simultaneously as a pole at `m^2`. The sequential cascade is therefore representation-specific. The remaining cost is the conditioning of recovering those poles from finite/noisy moments and then transporting them through the destination without re-aggregation.

Farey Discrepancy extends the overlap law through the first scheduler wrap. FD-220 gives one source for `N,2N,4N,8N` with depths `(0,3,3,0)`, bounded switched observations and a macroscopic initial defect for every `theta<1/8`; a positive reservoir-response cone repairs even the wrapped observation. Literal prefix rigidity begins at `theta>=1/8`. The next issue is no longer whether wraparound is special, but whether finite-block response cones remain repairable for every fixed block until coverage, or eventually fail earlier.

Möbius Cancellation now has three exact dimension audits. Source-mode averaging becomes a signature projector; the pair `(d_1,d_2)` in the squared Selberg majorant becomes one lcm-fibre coefficient; and MC-399 shows that the remaining standard lcm source phase separates by complete multiplicativity and disappears at the first additive correlation. The live arithmetic content is the twisted lcm/signature hyperbola transform and its moving endpoint, or a genuinely non-lcm-factorable pre-quadratic variable that survives the relevant Cauchy/differencing geometry.

Nyman--Beurling has sharpened both the carrier scale and the quantifier of the missing zero theorem. NB-230--NB-231 identify the sharp bounded transition `YD-log(1+R/H)=O(1)`. NB-232 shows that the corresponding coherent packets are mesoscopic, separated by `Q/X -> infinity` mean zero spacings, and can be inserted sparsely enough to leave a global Montgomery-type pair statistic asymptotically unchanged while still producing order-one Ford residue at selected heights. The missing input must therefore be local, depth-conditioned and carrier-frequency sensitive; density-one/global pair information can average away exactly the exceptional windows that matter to a uniform endpoint.

Prime Lattice narrows the nonnormal escape in the same spirit. Bounded normal exact-multiplicative representations scalarize by Gelfand theory; PL-378 shows that invertible nonnormal representations whose full rational-group completion is uniformly bounded are simultaneously similar to commuting unitaries and hence again to prime-torus character mixtures. The remaining operator routes must use one-sided noninvertibility, inverse-norm growth, unbounded/domain structure, controlled nonmultiplicativity, or a source-canonical metric-sensitive observable.

Visual Exploration gives a constructive counterexample to overcompression. The Bezout chart preserves both prime exclusions, and VIS-320 further reduces the natural Wang strip to at most three simultaneous-prime mechanical branches governed by a rational rotation. But the physical determinant window is shorter than one rotation period, so full-cycle equidistribution is not the missing theorem; the real target is uniform subperiod prime-pair occupancy with the Wang taper still visible.

Weil Positivity now closes the generic singular-spectral continuation of exact radial nullity. WP-377 uses only a strongly continuous unitary radial action, the exact cyclotomic difference relation and the shrinking mesh of prime logarithms to show that spectator-prime mixed shells generate every non-invariant component of each prime-power shell. Any closable positive form with exact mixed-shell nullity therefore factors through the translation-invariant sector. In scalar spectral realizations that sector is only the zero Mellin atom and again gives `Lambda^2`; changing from absolutely-continuous to singular/non-Rajchman spectrum does not create a new selector.

Robin Extremal turns widening-corridor motion into an exact local normal form. With `kappa=N/(m(a-1))`, every finite complex limit with `Re(kappa)<1` leaves residual microscopic phase `e^(-i(1-kappa)u)`, while positive-real `kappa>=1` flattens that compact microscopic profile. The unit transition tells us both where the raw-delay obstruction is sharp and where it stops carrying information; any further obstruction must move to a larger scale or another predictor cost.

Across these examples, enrichment deserves credit only when a source relation survives the exact downstream quotient **with usable conditioning, the topology, normalization, correlation geometry and quantifier required by the destination theorem**, and cannot be reproduced by a matched control.

## Where has the exact obstruction moved after the latest boundary results?

**Selection, recovery and transport.** Arithmetic Fidelity has a natural source selector and now an exact joint shell-resolved Stieltjes carrier. The unresolved issue is finite/noisy joint inversion and downstream transport, not existence of a non-peeling exact representation. Prime Lattice has left both bounded normal scalarization and the uniformly bounded group-like nonnormal subclass. Prime Circle remains outside bounded one-sided graded eigenvalue spectralization.

**Local fidelity, overlap and reachable cones.** Farey Discrepancy remains blind through the first `(0,3,3,0)` wrap below eighth-scale coverage. The next discriminator is a structural theorem for finite-block reservoir response cones, not another isolated horizon. Nyman--Beurling has a different carrier-relative threshold: after Ford damping, `YD-log(1+R/H)` is sharp for the audited information, and global pair correlation is too averaged to exclude sparse transition packets. Its next source theorem must see local shallow-zero phase at the carrier frequency.

**Formal dimension versus surviving arithmetic coupling.** Möbius Cancellation removes an artificial differencing-stage count, the source-mode average, the independent Selberg-pair interpretation and now the standard lcm-as-mixed-source-phase interpretation. Visual Exploration has gone the other direction: faithful coordinates are explicit and the remaining arithmetic variable is a concrete subperiod rational-rotation branch, not a coordinate artifact.

**Parameter motion versus physical budget.** Robin Extremal identifies the exact dimensionless transition controlling the microscopic normal form. Below it, widening leaves residual delay; at the positive-real unit threshold that local obstruction disappears. A moving parameter is useful only if it changes the destination-visible normal form without repaying the gain elsewhere.

**Invariant quotient versus spectral type/sign.** Weil Positivity shows that arbitrary singular spectral type does not escape exact positive radial nullity when translations remain strongly continuous and unitary: prime-log shrinking mesh collapses the quotient to the invariant sector. Weil Inertia retains its complementary boundary: local threshold continuity holds on null-equation-selected states, but the energy budget alone cannot be iterated cofinally.

Analytic Frontier, Prime Circle, Prime Flute, Weil Inertia and Xi Flow retain their existing local boundaries; no new persisted evidence in the inspected delta changes their current Mind state.

## Which physical cost survives once representation artifacts are removed?

The cost ledger is increasingly source-relative. Arithmetic Fidelity prices the condition number of finite/noisy joint shell recovery after exact transport has been separated from sequential peeling. Farey prices overlap plus positive reachable-cone geometry. Möbius prices relation codimension and the coefficient/endpoint structure left after Fourier, lcm and correlation separability. The reusable question is: **after the strongest exact source specialization, faithful representation cleanup and conditioning audit, what quantity must still be paid by the physical source rather than by coordinates, metric, cutoff, completion, normalization, projection, inverse reconstruction, correlation expansion, spectral type or quantifier choice?** A claimed obstruction is credible only when it survives the matched control in variables consumed by the final theorem.

## Can sparse or deep defects be made visible without first strengthening the whole statistic?

Several lines say yes, but only after changing the destination-sensitive object rather than merely improving an average. Full localized Weil energy sees failures normalized pair correlation can hide. Nyman--Beurling now gives an explicit example: a globally negligible set of mesoscopic phase-aligned packets can still carry order-one Ford residue at selected heights, so the relevant statistic must be conditioned on depth and local carrier phase. Visual Exploration retains both prime coordinates and the Wang taper, then keeps the actual subperiod branch rather than projecting to a determinant. Robin Extremal probes the normalized predictor in the dimensionless microscopic coordinate that exposes exactly when raw-delay behavior survives. Arithmetic Fidelity now shows that an exact joint source carrier is still useless operationally unless its deep components can be recovered at the destination's precision.

The common design principle is to identify the **destination-sensitive functional, active source range, inverse conditioning and theorem quantifier first**. If exact algebra, unstable inversion, global averaging, normalization, projection, scalarization or positive nullity has already erased the defect, a sharper estimate of the same compressed statistic will not restore it. If the right object is chosen, the remaining theorem should target the smallest source range and topology on which the defect still has persistent cost.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the source class, parameter provenance, source carrier and resolution, inverse/recovery conditioning, source-to-representation transport, observation family and nullspace, destination quotient, inverse/ordered transfer, preserved fibre coordinates, retained arithmetic dimension and aggregation length, Fourier/Gelfand/lcm/triangular/similarity scalarization, first-correlation separability, metric covariance law, positive radical generated by zero-energy relations, translation invariant sector, perturbation topology, analytic divisor/gauge freedom, source mesh, relation codimension and conductor cost, carrier-localized active range and capacity variable, local-versus-global quantifier, completion class, selector regularity, normalization map, defect normalization and destination quantifier.

Then ask: **what genuinely new source information is acquired; by what exact and stably usable map does it reach the destination; what exact reduction or averaging could absorb it; what equivalence or matched control does it break; and what physical cost remains after the most faithful representation is used?** A route that survives this audit has isolated a mathematical bottleneck. A route that fails it has usually rediscovered a provenance, conditioning, transport, fibrewise-inverse, similarity, gauge, separability, conductor, spectral, normalization, projection, topology, radical, averaging, quantifier or completion artifact in different notation.
