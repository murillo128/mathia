## When does a richer representation create a discriminator rather than merely transport, refactor or renorm the source?

[MI-021](intuition/MI-021-representation-enrichment-only-buys-leverage-where-source-specific-coupling-actually-lives.md) and [MI-028](intuition/MI-028-complete-measurement-families-can-close-an-escape-by-reconstructing-the-classical-object.md) remain the common interface audit.

Prime Circle closes both the obvious representation lifts and the direct source-native metric repair: full-signature/free-Fock data are transported by reciprocal second quantization, while the radial-energy Gram metric on the matched control is `J^*GJ`. A richer source metric is not discriminating when the same construction remains covariantly isometric.

Farey Discrepancy remains the strongest constructive pre-norm example, but FD-209 shows that a common sublogarithmic signed depth on a square-root scale window still has only subpower inverse cost and is RH-complete. The surviving escape is not depth by itself but loss of coherent depth across the physical inverse or a collective relation that never yields common-depth pointwise control.

Prime Lattice separates source selection from global analytic power. Complete multiplicativity plus continuation gives a genuine zero-sensitive theorem interface, and PL-370 forces every locally pole-neutral witness into a finite prime-harmonic neighbourhood of Liouville. PL-371 proves that this neighbourhood still contains `{+1,-1}` witnesses with a simple zero at `1` and a prescribed real continuation obstruction arbitrarily close to `1`. Local rigidity is real; continuation remains an independent global resource.

Nyman--Beurling gives the positive counterpart and now prices its resolution. Generic bounded strip analyticity is sharp for the transported logarithmic-derivative carrier. NB-223 retains the actual zero spectrum and reaches every fixed logarithmic margin below Ford height. NB-224 widens the logarithmic source shell to `Theta(X)`, narrows the carrier to vertical width `Theta(1/X)`, and combines that with local zero counts to reach the exact Ford denominator for a sufficiently small fixed Ford constant. The gain is source-specific but purchases height by sacrificing multiplicative localization.

Visual Exploration has now preserved exact support, coefficient magnitudes, same-base multiplicative phases, the actual deterministic time orbit in long-time quadratic mean, and finally coefficient-weighted local ratio spacing in finite windows. VIS-311 lowers the hard-cutoff sufficient dephasing horizon from quintic to cubic; the remaining explicit loss is the uniform hard-tail approximation. Coefficient sensitivity itself is no longer the missing ingredient.

Möbius Cancellation adds a stronger source-image warning. MC-389 shows that source pullback raises the generic Welch exceptional threshold by a fixed factor. MC-390 then proves that high dual distance of the physical source subspace is actually hostile: four-wise independent coordinate signs force `Omega(H)` correlations at the `R^(-1/2)` frame scale. A successful route must retain explicit weight-`<=4` source-incidence parity relations instead of making the source look more generically pseudorandom.

Weil Inertia adds a localization-normalization control. WI-354 shows that shrinking Wang's pair-correlation interval simultaneously shrinks the admissible fixed Fourier aperture through the `T^lambda L^2` error, while the optimized certified proportion also weakens and remains normalized by a macroscopically growing local zero population. More local data are not automatically more individual-defect-sensitive.

Weil Positivity keeps the scale/naturality warning: fixed scale kills the tail, homogeneous globalization kills incidence, and diagonal Hecke equivariance forces the same `Y_n=c/n` ray on which `nY_n` freezes. A naturality condition can select the critical exponent while erasing the mixed coordinate that made the representation interesting.

The shared question is: **what new source-specific coordinate is actually acquired; which quotient, norm, inverse, continuation, source-width choice, covariance/naturality law, exceptional-set geometry or averaging limit can remove its leverage; and does the strongest matched control reproduce the structure at the same pointwise/finite-height/individual-defect quantifier consumed by the destination?**

## Where does the obstruction move after a nominal critical boundary is resolved?

Arithmetic Fidelity now gives the cleanest multi-scale source-mesh example. AF-425--AF-428 reduce critical cancellation to a shrinking integer depth target whose exceptional amplitude set is residual but metrically thin. AF-429--AF-430 produce a supercritical saddle with first integer aperture `R_n/n` and a finer complete-packet correction `R_n/n^2`. AF-431 shows that the first aperture is not intrinsically obstructive: every fixed rational supercritical density samples the saddle with mesh `Theta(R_n/n)`, so monotone crossing forces infinitely many hits. The obstruction has moved one level deeper, to source classes with coarser motion and to the factor-`n` finer packet target.

Farey Discrepancy moves from path resistance to a positive anchor, from fixed-depth signed differences to a growing-depth inverse, and then to depth coherence. Throughout `q=o(log X)`, any one common depth on the relevant scale window remains destination-complete. The surviving question is whether scale-dependent depth or a truly collective relation avoids that inverse.

Visual Exploration moves from localization to absolute spacing, through signed matched controls and Bohr/Haar equivalence, into the finite-window representation itself. VIS-311 proves that weighted local spacing recovers two powers of `x`; the next wall is the uniform hard tail. The live question is energy-sensitive control of the infinite ratio spectrum versus a genuine sparse exceptional-height resonance.

Nyman--Beurling has now crossed the former exact-Ford population wall by changing source resolution. NB-224 turns a `Theta(X)` log shell into a `Theta(1/X)` vertical carrier and reaches exact Ford with local disk counts. The obstruction is no longer the Ford denominator alone; it is the **width-versus-height tradeoff**. Fixed-width and sublinear-width endpoint behavior remain open.

Prime Lattice moves from ambient topology to a nonlinear source law, then to local Liouville rigidity, and now to a sparse-tail counterexample. PL-371 closes any attempt to bootstrap continuation from finite prime-harmonic distance alone. A surviving mechanism must control the exceptional-prime tail across the relevant Dirichlet exponents or supply an independent global continuation principle.

Möbius Cancellation moves past both the abstract Welch threshold and the source-image extremizer gap. MC-390 splits the frontier by dual distance: high dual distance forces a positive-density bad set and is closed negatively; low dual distance is not solved but supplies a finite structural target. The next object is the arithmetic classification and use of weight-2/3/4 words in the large-source incidence span.

Weil Inertia closes a different cheap bootstrap. WI-354 shows that the same normalized Montgomery/Lamzouri mechanism cannot be localized down from bulk proportions to sparse-defect exclusion while retaining its current prime-side support error. The surviving paths require stronger short-interval arithmetic, a non-normalized cofinal inertia mechanism, or source-specific signed covariance.

Weil Positivity moves the scale obstruction to a naturality equation. A surviving route must keep `nY` nonconstant by justifiably breaking diagonal Hecke equivariance or by moving to genuinely cross-index structure.

Across these lines, a resolved threshold should be followed by identifying the **next source mesh, inverse-coherence window, tail norm, width/resolution trade, low-weight incidence relation, continuation class, support-localization budget or covariance-constrained scale coordinate** that becomes load-bearing, rather than continuing to optimize the old boundary.

## Which physical cost survives source specialization and analytic transport?

[MI-023](intuition/MI-023-source-information-is-priced-by-inverse-distinguishability-not-forward-smoothness.md), [MI-025](intuition/MI-025-combinatorial-breadth-must-be-priced-through-the-source-coordinate-map.md) and [MI-017](intuition/MI-017-sparse-exceptional-geometry-survives-upper-population-control.md) remain the main source-cost syntheses.

Arithmetic Fidelity shows that source cost is scale-specific. A discrete coordinate can be too coarse at one shrinking target and exactly matched at another. AF-431 therefore replaces “discreteness is an obstruction” by the sharper test: compare the physical source mesh with the aperture consumed at that exact asymptotic order.

Möbius Cancellation shows that source geometry must be priced twice: first through the admissibility of ambient extremizers and then through the code structure of the physical source subspace. The projected-simplex affine rank improves the abstract threshold, while high dual distance independently forces anti-concentration. The viable source is neither an arbitrary frame nor a generic good code.

Nyman--Beurling makes localization cost explicit. A wider logarithmic source shell buys a narrower vertical carrier and hence stronger use of local zero counts, but the integer source range expands by a power. Exact-Ford height and fixed multiplicative resolution are distinct resources.

Visual Exploration gives the finite-time analogue. Global-gap compression manufactured two powers of `x`; coefficient-weighted spacing recovers them. The remaining price is the norm used on the infinite tail. A finite-window theorem should be charged in the same energy norm as the destination before a large horizon is interpreted as arithmetic.

Prime Lattice shows that a local source metric can miss global analytic cost. Reciprocal-prime mass controls pole neutrality near `1`, while sparse defects can have a different Dirichlet abscissa and stop continuation anywhere in `(1/2,1)`. The continuation cost lives across exponent scales, not in one pretentious distance.

Weil Inertia shows a support/normalization cost. Shortening the vertical interval is useful only if Fourier support and arithmetic error survive the same move and if the resulting statistic is sensitive to an individual defect rather than merely a fraction of an expanding population.

Farey Discrepancy and Robin Extremal retain the inverse/approximation controls: a formal transform is useful only after its physical inverse is priced, and a strict approximation floor matters only while the approximation class producing it remains explicit.

The shared audit is to price a route **after exact source specialization and nuisance removal**, keeping source mesh, exceptional geometry, inverse coherence, approximation class, continuation, carrier width, local zero population, finite-time weighted spectrum, tail norm, naturality and destination normalization distinct until a theorem proves a dependency.

## Can sign-indefinite structure preserve critical information before closure or norm compression scalarizes it?

Farey Discrepancy shows that signed differences improve pre-norm geometry but remain destination-complete whenever a coherent sublogarithmic depth can be inverted. Sign helps only if the physical inverse loses that coherence or if a collective relation never produces a pointwise critical coordinate.

Visual Exploration shows that exact support and complete same-base multiplicativity still cancel under cross-prime Haar phases, while the actual deterministic orbit has the same long-time quadratic mean. The unresolved signed object is now finite-height infinite-spectrum coherence, not generic phase complexity.

Nyman--Beurling shows both an absolute and a phase-sensitive route. Population bounds plus a deliberately narrowed carrier already reach exact Ford for wide shells. At fixed width, the unresolved endpoint may still require cancellation among weighted zero residues rather than another absolute count.

Weil Positivity shows why sign may need to appear before Hilbert closure: the critical half-density is visible before positive `L^2` squares it into a divergent norm, and exact diagonal Hecke naturality forces the factorized critical ray.

Prime Lattice provides a different positive-logarithm example. Complete multiplicativity and conjugate symmetrization yield a valid zero-free theorem interface, but PL-371 shows that the required continuation cannot be obtained from local Liouville rigidity alone. A useful sign/positivity mechanism still needs genuinely global analytic input.

These examples sharpen one interface: useful sign or positivity must act while the source-specific relation is present, survive the physical inverse and source lattice, preserve the required localization/resolution, and depend on an input whose quantifier and analytic domain are not already equivalent to the desired conclusion.

## Global discriminator

For every proposed route, identify the admissible source class and topology, observation family and nullspace, destination quotient and norm, source-coordinate map, requested precision, approximation class, analytic domain, discrete source mesh, source width and dual carrier width, exceptional-set notion, theorem-admission range, source-frame extremizer/code class, transported analytic carrier, singularity spectrum, finite inverse, positive anchors, covariance/naturality constraints, sign/phase data, finite-time averaging window, weighted frequency geometry, tail norm, destination normalization and matched control.

Then ask in sequence: **what is genuinely new at acquisition; what closure or projection is unavoidable; what survives the destination quotient; what ambient theorem already explains the critical scale; what physical inverse or source mesh must realize the tuning; are ambient extremizers or generic-code heuristics admissible after source pullback; if continuation is required, what sparse perturbation preserves local diagnostics while breaking it; if localization is improved, what source resolution or Fourier support is lost; if time averaging is used, which spectral geometry and tail norm are preserved; and does the final statement see an individual defect at the quantifier actually required?**

A route that survives this audit has identified a mathematical bottleneck. A route that fails it has usually rediscovered a representation, source-resolution, inverse, continuation, support, tail or normalization cost in different notation.
