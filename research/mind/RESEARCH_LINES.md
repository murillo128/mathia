In the analytic category the boundary collapses to the exponential/strict dichotomy. The remaining smooth question is whether an infinitely-flat zero can still hide a negative non-solid continuum minor.

Farey Discrepancy supplies the discrete analogue with a different mechanism. FD-164--FD-165 leave dense orientation kernels at every finite cutoff and even across large finite horizon bundles. Robin Extremal now brackets source fidelity from the opposite side. RE-173 shows that one exact Mertens boundary charge, ordinary-prime support, bounded integer multiplicity and arbitrarily strong fixed KV fidelity still allow an infinite delete/duplicate tail with an order-one transient Robin excursion. RE-174 shows that any unbounded sequence of exact high-temperature Euler samples determines every bounded prime multiplicity; the first defect at `P` appears only at scale `P^{-sigma}`. Exact source identification is complete at that extreme but exponentially ill-conditioned for the Robin destination.

Together AF-389, FD-166 and RE-174 sharpen the multiscale lesson: scale count is not the resource. **One latent object constrained coherently across an unbounded hierarchy can eliminate every fixed finite defect, while finite-cutoff controls survive by pushing defects into an escaping tail, an infinitely-flat stratum or another noncompact region.** Once that exact rigidity is known, the real question becomes the inverse/stability modulus at the destination scale.

## How much source information can an analytic architecture exploit at bounded conditioning?

MC-338 turns own-phase blindness into a projection/energy inequality; MC-339--MC-340 replace coordinate restrictions by HGR/mutual information and show that bounded-energy nontrivial dephasing requires `Omega(R)` **joint** source information. Low syntactic dimension is therefore meaningful only together with an intrinsic precision/conditioning bound.

The live arithmetic question is to identify a genuine Möbius transcript and its canonical metric. Either its joint source information must be sublinear at bounded coefficient energy, or acquiring linear information must force a conditioning/energy cost too large for the endpoint. An arbitrary coordinate chart cannot supply this theorem because mutual information is invariant while Euclidean spread is not.

Many complete horizons can remain orientation-ambiguous at finite cutoff; many weak channels can jointly reconstruct a source; an apparently rich adaptive continuum can collapse after quotienting sub-resolution motion and approximate source span; and a complete operator spectrum can quotient independent source phase down to autocorrelation. Count observations, coordinates, features or choices only after identifying the exact source quotient, reconstruction closure, source resolution and conditioning budget.

## Which source fingerprint is selective rather than merely reconstructive?

Robin Extremal and Farey Discrepancy now make source identification itself a controlled negative example. An unbounded exact observation hierarchy may identify the source completely while leaving the RH-facing estimate untouched or badly conditioned. Exact Möbius-prefix recovery does not prove the Franel--Landau norm, and exact prime-multiplicity recovery through high-temperature Euler data does not control a Robin excursion at usable precision.

Arithmetic Fidelity says the same from the analytic side: Hamburger-type source rigidity can identify `zeta` without selecting its nontrivial zero divisor. Prime Circle similarly shows that source-sensitive autocorrelation and relative bound states are not automatically zero selectors. The global program must therefore separate **source fingerprint**, **stable transport of that fingerprint**, and **destination-selective sign/zero theorem** at every stage.

## Global discriminator: identify the exact resource a matched control cannot fake

The current frontier is a classification of source-specific coupling that remains after every free closure, reconstruction, compatibility, quotient and matched-control operation. For each proposed route ask: what exact source distinction reaches the destination; what admissible source class is being compared; what information is regenerated for free; whether a finite-cutoff ambiguity survives only by escaping to infinity; what metric/topology makes the surviving information stable; what sub-resolution motion the source cannot see; what joint source information the complete transcript contains and what energy/precision it costs; whether an operator preserves oriented phase or only an autocorrelation quotient; what spectral-density law its determinant is forced to obey; whether a transport metric derives the desired second moment or merely inserts it; and what theorem finally converts the survivor into a Riemann-zero or positivity restriction.

A useful bridge should fail on a matched control for an independently arithmetic reason. Exact reconstruction, unbounded observation count, source-specific labels, a critical boundary, a rich spectrum, a positive metric, or a small formal error are not enough unless the surviving quantity is both stably accessible and selectively coupled to the RH destination.
