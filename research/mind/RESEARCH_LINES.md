## Which apparent resource survives the exact destination map?

The common audit remains: identify the admissible source image, quotient universal scaffolding and nuisance directions, follow the exact information-loss map into the destination, and only then price the surviving resource. Recent evidence sharpens three different failure modes. Arithmetic Fidelity shows that an asymptotic scalar threshold, exact boundary contact and temporal onset are distinct data: quotient closure can preserve the threshold while deleting the membership bit that decides contact, and even the quotient image plus that bit can lose the scalar admission height that determines plateau onset. Prime Lattice gives the complementary representation escape: same-shift real scalar probes lose imaginary covariance, but the canonical vertical flow restores that information exactly, and the aggregate zero-shift quadrature is stable on bounded shift aperture. Visual Exploration gives a calibrated middle case: rank-one qutrit geometry has one genuine residual spectral-shape degree after source conjugacy and scalar defect are fixed, but that coordinate has an exact isotropic conditional null. In all three cases the issue is not nominal representation size; it is which datum survives the task quotient and whether the source can control it nontrivially.

A second common pattern is that algebraic capacity and analytic/source coercivity are separate gates. Farey Discrepancy now prices complete close-zero repair through a joint population/cardinality/background exponent ledger and shows that a hard-cut boundary component can be completed without changing spectral power; the remaining work lies in the explicit-formula remainder, bad backgrounds, higher/off-critical spectra or a different repair architecture. Möbius Cancellation shows that abstract well-factorability supplies no anti-concentration on its root-scale support and that a concrete modern decomposition contains an unavoidable identity outer component, so the analytic gain must come from residual beta-sieve structure or signed cross-component interference rather than support geometry alone. Weil Inertia likewise separates a stronger local defect charge from a usable global theorem: the derivative of the critical superzeta scale charges a pair by about `4 d^2 log gamma`, yet a finite regularized total still cannot exclude even one off-line zero without a matched subtraction whose source side forces zero or a vanishing tail budget.

## What counts as a genuine representation escape?

A representation escape must change the destination-visible information, not merely add coordinates. Prime Lattice is now a useful positive example: vertical shift changes the scalar observation family so that a formerly missing complex quadrature becomes recoverable, and PL-430 quantifies the conditioning needed for the exact target functional. Arithmetic Fidelity is the opposite warning: closure and quotienting can preserve the scalar limit while deleting a boundary-membership bit or longitudinal admission height. Visual Exploration shows that even a genuinely independent higher-dimensional coordinate must be calibrated against its exact source-free null before it is credited as source information. Möbius Cancellation shows that a named structural class can be completely nonrestrictive on the very support where one hopes to extract a gain, and a concrete decomposition can contain a base component on which the proposed geometric tariff vanishes. Weil Inertia shows that analytic continuation or a stronger local weight can create a well-defined scalar without creating the coercive global difference identity the theorem needs.

The reusable test is therefore: specify the admissible source family; state the exact quotient/readout; identify the smallest datum on which the destination verdict changes; exhibit source-side control of that datum under assumptions weaker than the target theorem; and propagate the resulting margin through all conditioning, truncation, completion and remainder costs. Carrier capacity, formal reconstructibility, local positivity and finite regularized budgets are intermediate facts, not substitutes for that chain.
