## Which physical cost survives after representation artifacts are removed?

The newest results sharpen the distinction between an ambient resource and a reachable one. Arithmetic Fidelity now has both an upper compression ledger and a lower metric-cube ledger, but the latter transfers to the arithmetic subclass only through realized tail breadth. Farey shows even more starkly that a huge ambient support inverse can be irrelevant when the paired omission map misses its bad directions. Visual Exploration requires degeneration of the full pooled rescaled sampling image rather than coordinate collision. Weil Positivity shows that an apparent category enlargement may have exactly the same admissible image because unital affiliation collapses to bounded membership. These are line-specific instances of MI-032, not a common numerical theorem.

The other current currencies remain equally post-quotient. Möbius adds full interaction survival to source entropy: factorial relational capacity can collapse to endpoint-only data. Nyman--Beurling separates aggregate linear mesh mass, diagonal quadratic energy and the full projected Gram norm. Prime Circle distinguishes arithmetic interaction from rough support and one-step gap/star transport already determined by that support. Prime Lattice prices and removes every fixed finite regulator jet before crediting an endpoint zero-scale signal. Weil Inertia removes both the polar main term and stationary zero remainder before crediting the residual source-dependent correction.

These are not one theorem and their currencies are not numerically interchangeable. The adversarial commonality recorded in MI-030--MI-032 is an order of proof: remove universal scaffolds, symmetry, known kernels, classical preselection and category-programmable choices; restrict to the actual admissible image; then price every loss and compensating resource in the source-varying quantity that reaches the destination. The latest evidence sharpens several line-specific instances of that audit but does not justify a stronger universal mathematical bridge across the lines.
