## Research question

Can the surviving Bost--Connes work be factored into a source-selection obligation and a genuinely distinct Weil-sign obligation, or do the two lines currently require the same unproved relation on the same source class? More broadly, do the proposed Prime-Circle collective survivor, Möbius stable decoder, and Robin extremal exchange each add a distinct, source-justified inequality or information gain after the complete destination losses are restored?

A useful answer should distinguish four situations: a new representation of the same target with no demonstrated analytic advantage; a genuinely useful transformation with independent estimates; a proved obstruction that closes a meaningful family; and an unfinished test that remains unresolved. None of these can be identified reliably by counting findings, shared vocabulary, or failed proof searches.

## Why it may matter

The missing comparison is between actual theorem obligations, not between research-line names. A separation witness can show that apparently overlapping work is complementary; an exact reduction can show that two proposals are duplicating the same missing step. Robin can broaden the mathematical objects being tested, but diversity alone does not establish fertility, and a hidden RH-strength prime-distribution assumption would defeat the proposed advantage.

The request is to reconsider the reasoning on both sides. The existence of an unrefuted possibility is not itself a quantitative measure of research value; conversely, repeated negative results can be valuable and are not automatically circular. Any later allocation conclusion remains subject to the Master skill's evidence and review gates.
