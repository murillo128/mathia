# Can source fidelity and destination realization be separated by one exact cross-line criterion?

## Observation
Several current lines now fail in ways that look structurally related but are not yet known to be mathematically equivalent. Arithmetic Fidelity exhibits exact collision fibers after finite compression. Prime Circle and Prime Lattice repeatedly reduce natural retained data to endpoint/gauge or classical Fourier-resolution objects. Weil Positivity can retain an exact arithmetic selector while losing bounded positive realization in the destination topology. By contrast, Prime Flute and Weil Inertia retain source-specific information but are blocked at global operator or cross-conductor assembly.

## Research question
Is there a precise common framework in which a candidate RH mechanism factors into a source-side retained map and a destination-side realization, with two genuinely distinct failure modes:

1. **fidelity failure** — the retained map is non-injective on the relevant admissible source class; and
2. **realization failure** — the retained information survives, but no map into the required operator/positive/topological category preserves it with the continuity, coercivity, sign, or collective norm demanded by the final claim?

Within such a framework, is local-to-global assembly a third mathematically distinct obstruction, or can it be reduced to one of the two failures above under exact hypotheses?

## Why it may matter
If the distinction can be made theorem-level rather than analogical, the program could tell when different-looking negative results are instances of one reusable obstruction and when they are genuinely complementary. That would sharpen cross-line transfer without treating repeated vocabulary or telemetry patterns as mathematical evidence.

## Decisive test
Choose at least two contrasting pairs among the cited lines and write the exact source space, retained statistic/operator, admissible controls, destination category, and required topology/norm. Prove a proposition that places both pairs under one fidelity/realization/assembly trichotomy, or construct an explicit counterexample showing that no such common criterion survives the line-specific hypotheses. The test must reproduce the cited line-specific obstruction as a consequence, not merely rename it.

## Evidence boundary
The cited findings establish only their own line-specific collision, classicalization, topology, or assembly statements. No current finding proves a cross-line equivalence or trichotomy. The Graph Curator's frontier telemetry is derived strategic instrumentation and is not evidence for this clue; it only makes the repeated pattern easier to notice.
