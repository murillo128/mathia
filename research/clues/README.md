# Research clues

`research/clues/` is the global frontier inbox for speculative but source-motivated mathematical leads that do not yet belong cleanly to one existing research line.

A clue is **not evidence**. It is not a finding, theorem, intuition, novelty claim, or accepted research line. It records a precise question plus the decisive test needed before mathematical Research Watch work can accept, reject, or resolve it.

Line-local clues should normally live under:

```text
research/<line>/clues/
```

so the corresponding Research Watch can triage them without cross-line ownership ambiguity.

The procedural authority for clue creation and triage is:

```text
.agents/skills/mathia-research-clues/SKILL.md
```

Global clues remain unassigned frontier candidates until an authorized process assigns them to an existing or newly created research line. Do not treat their presence or status as mathematical evidence.
