# Keep distinct source gates; do not open another umbrella line

## Observation

This extraordinary Master/Visionary audit covered the portfolio map and decisive current findings across analytic_frontier, arithmetic_fidelity, farey_discrepancy, mobius_cancellation, nyman_beurling, prime_circle, prime_flute, prime_lattice, robin_extremal, visual_exploration, weil_inertia, weil_positivity, and xi_flow. It is not a certification of every proof, a scheduled Visionary campaign run, or a change to the active campaign.

The portfolio does not have one universal obstruction. Several branches share a representational temptation: recovering or re-encoding data is mistaken for obtaining an estimate on the final observable. The exact losses are different and must remain visible. In particular, prime_lattice lacks a source-fixed state selection, whereas weil_positivity has an essential-sign obstruction after a state/kernel is selected. These are not interchangeable gates.

Farey, Nyman-Beurling and Robin now have first findings, FD-001, NB-001 and RE-001; synthesis predating those files must not keep describing them as pre-evidence pilots. Their existence still does not establish a new RH estimate.

## Research question

Can the Master narrow each line to a source-specific, falsifiable next test, rather than increase the number of equivalent representations, generic conditioning statements, or harmonic packets? The proposed portfolio allocation is three focused continuations, seven narrowly gated lines, and three evidence-bearing pilots, without opening another standalone research line.

## Why it may matter

A shared adversarial testing method is useful even when a shared theorem is not. NB-001 exhibits exact non-identifiability of target distance from generator Gram data under a common inner factor. Prime Circle has a fiber/left-unitary invariance obstruction. Xi Flow instead has an unstable inverse, which may remain injective. These require different tests: defeat the invariant transformation, recover the missing target observable, or prove a source-specific stability modulus. Combining their terminology into a single universal reconstruction theorem would lose that distinction.

The existing accepted Nyman-Beurling target-aware certificate already addresses the missing target overlap. Preserve it and do not issue another Gram-plus-target clue. Likewise, respect the existing Prime Flute weak-trace reassembly clue; the derivative test below is a proposed refinement for its owner, not an automatic duplicate finding.

## Decisive test

### Three focused continuations

**Prime Flute.** PF-240 makes the target weighted regularity r1+r2>1, with a uniform O(beta) bound for the genuine Q-dressed crossing block. PF-242 proves exponential locality of a multiplier, not of the full geometric DtN operator, its derivative term, or the complete seam normalization. Before attempting the nonlinear bound, test the actual first shape derivative at beta=0 in the same weighted spaces and uniformly in the geometric scale. Appropriate differentiability plus a uniform weighted O(beta) estimate would force the corresponding weighted first derivative to be bounded; failure on an admissible packet therefore rejects that estimate. Passing this necessary test is not sufficient: the owner still needs a uniform remainder and the actual Q/projector/end-point/reassembly estimates. Do not replace the genuine Q with a toy diagonal proxy.

**Analytic Frontier.** ANF-138 absorbs arbitrary finite-depth constants by sufficiently slow diagonalization. Stop reopening generic finite-prefactor conditioning as the main obstruction. It does not produce a fixed gain when residual slack tends to zero. ANF-141 isolates the live mesoscopic tail regime 1 << A*T << A, between an identity tail and bounded-factor tails. Require a source-level positive residual-slack or mesoscopic tail-excision argument, with its uniformity stated. A new finite-depth estimate without that implication is not a reopened gate.

**Weil Inertia.** WI-211 supplies a synthetic two-offline-pair configuration that screens a full two-harmonic packet. Do not treat a synthetic configuration as actual zeta zeros, but also do not assume counts or second-harmonic positivity exclude it. The next deliverable must identify and test a covariance constraint imposed by the actual arithmetic source. More harmonics count as progress only after proving why the source restriction blocks collective screening, not merely after improving a plot.

### Seven narrowly gated lines

Arithmetic Fidelity should retain positive/fixed-sign finite-order progress but require an actual-source restriction on signed complexity before claiming stable inversion. Mobius Cancellation must exhibit a quantitative cancellation estimate absent from the original Mobius sequence, not just an odd/even or operator recoding. Prime Circle must break the relevant fiber/left-unitary invariance or account for the cost of growing the base. Prime Lattice needs a source-fixed state-selection mechanism, not another interchangeable KMS or Gibbs completion.

Weil Positivity should test the noncompact counterterm in the companion Farey/GCD clue, not try a compact positivity repair. Xi Flow needs a Xi-specific tail exclusion or stability modulus rather than more finite log jets. Visual Exploration should calibrate an observable against its matching null and re-export a discrimination test; third-order structure by itself is not evidence for RH.

### Three evidence-bearing pilots

Farey must distinguish its exact Ramanujan-Mertens recoding from genuinely additional information, such as an explicitly used order/mediant constraint. Nyman-Beurling should execute its already accepted target-aware certificate rather than accumulate generator-only Gram invariants. Robin should first consume the companion interval-checked CA kill test: actual regular peak values can increase below the Robin barrier, so an unconditional nonpositive-workload or nonnegative-cumulative-workload conjecture is too strong. A viable barrier-conditioned estimate must state what additional arithmetic hypothesis avoids that counterexample.

The two companion clues are proposed tests, not accepted findings. The Farey/Weil bridge has an exact elementary operator identity but no verified full source compensation. The Robin clue has two genuine interval-checked regression integers but no proof of the desired global inequality.
