# PC-184 — finite scale-covariant radial jets are Mellin rank one

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED` + `LITERATURE+DERIVED` + `CLASSICAL-MELLIN-STRUCTURE` + `DECISIVE-NEGATIVE` for obtaining a second positive Prime-Circle radial carrier by adjoining finitely many scale-normalized local derivatives of the cyclotomic log-potential and then applying a fixed refinement-covariant matrix-valued kernel.

PC-183 left matrix-valued/radially varying operators and a second source-forced carrier outside its scalar positive-kernel theorem. The most intrinsic finite local candidate is generated by the dilation operator itself. Put

\[
F_n(x):=\log\Phi_n(e^{-x}),
\qquad
D:=x\frac{d}{dx},
\]

and form any fixed finite vector of Euler derivatives

\[
J_n(x)
=
\bigl(P_1(D)F_n(x),\ldots,P_r(D)F_n(x)\bigr)^{\mathsf T},
\]

where the `P_j` are fixed polynomials. This class already contains the obvious potential/flux pair because

\[
DF_n(x)=-x\rho_n(x).
\]

The key obstruction is exact: under Mellin transform every component of `J_n` is proportional to the **same scalar shell transform**. At every Mellin frequency the entire finite jet therefore spans only one shell-dependent direction. A positive matrix-valued homogeneous kernel cannot manufacture a second arithmetic carrier from that internal finite-dimensional space.

More strongly, the common scalar factor for the potential has no zeros anywhere in the right Mellin half-plane. Hence any nonzero positive refinement-covariant finite-jet energy that is finite on the shells is positive on **every** shell `n>1`. It cannot preserve even one non-prime-power zero while retaining a positive prime-power response.

## 1. The cyclotomic potential has an exact shifted Mellin factorization

PC-179 gives

\[
\rho_n(x)=-F_n'(x)
\]

and, for `Re(s)>0`,

\[
\mathcal R_n(s)
=
\int_0^\infty \rho_n(x)x^{s-1}\,dx
=
-\Gamma(s)\zeta(s)n^{1-s}
\prod_{p\mid n}(1-p^{s-1}).
\tag{1}
\]

For every `n>1`, `F_n` is bounded at `0+` because `Phi_n(1)` is nonzero, and it decays exponentially at infinity. Integration by parts therefore gives, for `Re(s)>0`,

\[
\mathcal F_n(s)
:=
\int_0^\infty F_n(x)x^{s-1}\,dx
=
\frac{\mathcal R_n(s+1)}{s}.
\tag{2}
\]

Using `Gamma(s+1)=s Gamma(s)` in (1),

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal F_n(s)
=
-\Gamma(s)\zeta(s+1)n^{-s}
\prod_{p\mid n}(1-p^s).
}
\tag{3}
\]

This is again classical cyclotomic/Mellin data, but the shift by one has a useful consequence. If `Re(s)>0`, then

\[
\operatorname{Re}(s+1)>1,
\]

so the Euler product implies `zeta(s+1) != 0`. Also

\[
|p^s|=p^{\operatorname{Re}(s)}>1,
\]

hence no factor `1-p^s` can vanish. Since `Gamma` has no zeros,

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal F_n(s)\neq0
\qquad
(n>1,\ \operatorname{Re}(s)>0).
}
\tag{4}
\]

Thus the interior log-potential has no shell-selective Mellin null channel in its natural convergence half-plane. The exact Mangoldt selector sits instead at the boundary value

\[
F_n(0)=\log\Phi_n(1)=\Lambda(n),
\]

which is not an interior Mellin frequency of (3).

## 2. Every finite Euler jet is rank one in Mellin space

For any sufficiently regular function bounded at `0+` and decaying at infinity,

\[
\mathcal M[D f](s)
=
\int_0^\infty x f'(x)x^{s-1}\,dx
=
-s\,\mathcal M[f](s),
\qquad \operatorname{Re}(s)>0.
\tag{5}
\]

The boundary term vanishes because `x^s f(x) -> 0` at both endpoints. Iterating (5) gives

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal M[P(D)F_n](s)
=
P(-s)\mathcal F_n(s)
}
\tag{6}
\]

for every polynomial `P`.

Therefore, if

\[
p(s)
:=
\bigl(P_1(-s),\ldots,P_r(-s)\bigr)^{\mathsf T},
\]

then the full vector jet satisfies

\[
\boxed{
\widehat J_n(s)
=
\mathcal F_n(s)\,p(s).
}
\tag{7}
\]

All shell dependence is carried by the one scalar `mathcal F_n(s)`; `p(s)` is universal and independent of `n`. The fiber rank in the internal jet index is exactly one whenever `p(s) != 0`.

The first nontrivial example is already the candidate suggested by the frontier after PC-183:

\[
J_n=(F_n,DF_n)^{\mathsf T}
=
(F_n,-x\rho_n)^{\mathsf T},
\]

for which

\[
\widehat J_n(s)
=
\mathcal F_n(s)
\binom{1}{-s}.
\tag{8}
\]

So the potential and the scale-normalized flux are not two independent Mellin carriers. They are one carrier written in two local coordinates.

More generally, the usual scale-normalized derivative jets `x^j d^jF_n/dx^j` are polynomials in `D`, so they are also covered by (7). Increasing the finite differential order does not increase the shell-dependent Mellin rank.

## 3. Positive matrix-valued homogeneous kernels scalarize on the jet

Now let `K(u,v)` be a continuous Hermitian positive-semidefinite `r x r` kernel and suppose it obeys the same scalar refinement covariance considered in PC-183. Full integer refinement plus continuity reduces this to real homogeneity

\[
K(\lambda u,\lambda v)
=
\lambda^\gamma K(u,v),
\qquad \lambda>0.
\tag{9}
\]

Assume `gamma>-2`, the natural integrability threshold for bounded jets at `0+`, and assume the quadratic energies below are finite on the shell family under consideration.

Removing the homogeneous envelope gives a translation-invariant positive matrix kernel on the log-depth line:

\[
K(u,v)
=
(uv)^{\gamma/2}
\kappa(\log u-\log v).
\tag{10}
\]

The finite-dimensional operator-valued Bochner theorem supplies a positive-semidefinite matrix-valued measure `M` such that, with a harmless choice of Fourier sign,

\[
\kappa(t)
=
\int_{\mathbb R}e^{-it\xi}\,dM(\xi).
\tag{11}
\]

Put

\[
c=1+\frac{\gamma}{2}>0.
\]

For the shell energy

\[
Q_K(n)
:=
\int_0^\infty\!\!\int_0^\infty
J_n(u)^*K(u,v)J_n(v)\,du\,dv,
\tag{12}
\]

equations (7), (10), and (11) give

\[
Q_K(n)
=
\int_{\mathbb R}
|\mathcal F_n(c+i\xi)|^2\,
p(c+i\xi)^*\,dM(\xi)\,p(c+i\xi).
\tag{13}
\]

Thus the matrix-valued kernel has collapsed on the source-derived jet to a scalar positive spectral measure. Define the effective positive measure

\[
d\nu(\xi)
:=
p(c+i\xi)^*\,dM(\xi)\,p(c+i\xi).
\tag{14}
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
Q_K(n)
=
\int_{\mathbb R}
|\mathcal F_n(c+i\xi)|^2\,d\nu(\xi).
}
\tag{15}
\]

Any matrix directions orthogonal to `p(c+i xi)` are simply invisible to every cyclotomic shell. They may make the ambient matrix kernel nontrivial, but they do not create a second observable arithmetic carrier.

## 4. A nonzero positive jet energy is positive on every shell

Equation (4) applies at every point of the line `Re(s)=c>0`. Hence

\[
|\mathcal F_n(c+i\xi)|^2>0
\qquad
\text{for every }n>1\text{ and every }\xi\in\mathbb R.
\tag{16}
\]

If `Q_K(n_0)>0` for even one shell `n_0`, then the effective measure `nu` in (15) is nonzero. Equation (16) immediately forces

\[
\boxed{
Q_K(n)>0
\qquad
\text{for every }n>1.
}
\tag{17}
\]

Under the standing admissibility assumption the values are finite; without it the conclusion is still that no other shell can have zero response.

This is stronger than the selector obstruction in PC-183. For the signed flux itself, the special Mellin point `s=1` survives as the constant-kernel boundary form and gives `Lambda(n)^2`. For the finite Euler jet of the potential, the natural Mellin line lies strictly in `Re(s)>0` and its scalar factor is zero-free there. Positive refinement-covariant finite-jet scalarization therefore has only two possibilities:

\[
\boxed{
Q_K(n)=0\ \text{for all shells}
\quad\text{or}\quad
Q_K(n)>0\ \text{for all shells}.
}
\tag{18}
\]

In particular it is impossible to have

\[
Q_K(p^a)>0,
\qquad
Q_K(pq)=0
\]

for any prime power `p^a` and any product of distinct primes `pq`.

The smallest matched control is explicit. For `n=2`,

\[
\mathcal F_2(s)
=
\Gamma(s)\zeta(s+1)(1-2^{-s}),
\]

while for `n=6`,

\[
\mathcal F_6(s)
=
-\Gamma(s)\zeta(s+1)6^{-s}(1-2^s)(1-3^s).
\]

Both are nonzero for every `Re(s)>0`, even though the boundary values distinguish the shells maximally:

\[
F_2(0)=\log2,
\qquad
F_6(0)=0.
\]

A positive finite-jet interior form therefore destroys the selector before any later spectral functional calculus is applied.

## 5. What this says about the proposed second carrier

The result closes the most canonical finite local matrix repair left after PC-181--PC-183. The dilation generator `D` is not chosen externally: it is the infinitesimal operator of the same radial scale action `x -> qx` induced by the intrinsic power refinements `z -> z^q`. The pair `(F_n,-x rho_n)` and all higher scale-normalized radial derivatives are therefore source-forced candidates.

Nevertheless, equation (7) shows that adding any finite number of them is only a change of fiber coordinates around one scalar Mellin channel. A matrix-valued positive kernel can have internal eigenvectors and noncommuting matrices before restriction to the shell data, but after restriction every Mellin fiber is probed only along the single universal vector `p(s)`. Such internal matrix structure is not arithmetic information supplied by Prime Circle.

This also clarifies why the unweighted flux `rho_n` behaves differently. Multiplication by `x^{-1}` moves one outside the dimensionless Euler-jet bundle and exposes the singular endpoint channel whose total integral is `Lambda(n)`. PC-179 and PC-183 already classify that boundary-sensitive positive route. PC-184 shows that adjoining the regular finite local interior jet does not provide the missing independent carrier.

## 6. Prior-art and novelty audit

No novelty is claimed for the analytic ingredients. The identity

\[
\mathcal M[D f](s)=-s\mathcal M[f](s)
\]

is standard Mellin-transform calculus: the Mellin transform is Fourier analysis on the multiplicative group, and the Euler/dilation generator becomes multiplication by the spectral parameter. PC-165 already identifies the same dilation generator and Mellin half-density as the classical continuous dilation/Berry--Keating structure.

Likewise, factorization of homogeneous kernels into a power envelope times a log-ratio kernel and Mellin diagonalization are classical scale-invariant harmonic analysis; PC-183 anchored a direct modern formulation in Jacobs--Frank, arXiv:2606.07644 (2026). The matrix-valued step is the standard operator-valued extension of Bochner's theorem; a direct reference is Ha Quang Minh, *Operator-Valued Bochner Theorem, Fourier Feature Maps for Operator-Valued Kernels, and Vector-Valued Learning*, arXiv:1608.05639 (2016).

The cyclotomic shell factor in (3) is only equation (1) from PC-179 shifted by integration by parts. Directed searches across cyclotomic Mellin transforms, scale-invariant kernels, Euler/dilation operators, and matrix-valued Bochner theory found these classical ingredients, not an independent Prime-Circle/RH mechanism generated by finite radial jets. Absence of a wording match is not used as evidence of historical novelty.

The durable contribution is therefore a line-specific **negative architecture theorem**: the most natural finite local way to manufacture a second refinement-covariant radial carrier has Mellin fiber rank one and, after positive matrix scalarization, loses all shellwise zeros.

## 7. Scope, falsifiers, and surviving frontier

The theorem assumes:

1. the additional radial data are a fixed finite collection of scale-normalized local derivatives, equivalently fixed polynomials in `D` applied to `F_n`;
2. the matrix-valued positive kernel is shell-independent and transforms by one scalar homogeneity under the full refinement semigroup, as in the positive refinement class of PC-183;
3. the resulting quadratic form is an honest interior form with `gamma>-2`, not a singular boundary distribution.

It does **not** classify a matrix kernel with a genuinely non-scalar refinement cocycle, shell-dependent matrices, indefinite/sign-changing matrix kernels, infinite-order or nonlocal functions of radial depth that are not finite Euler jets, singular boundary/domain data, angular-radial coupled carriers, or cross-shell operators that mix shell labels before taking a positive form. Those remain separate candidates and require their own matched controls.

The decisive falsifier for the exact part is simple: one would need an `n>1` and an `s` with `Re(s)>0` for which either (3) vanishes or a polynomial Euler jet has a Mellin component not proportional to `mathcal F_n(s)`. Equation (3), the Euler product on `Re(s+1)>1`, and (5)--(7) exclude both possibilities.

For the accepted signed-radial-flux clue, the frontier narrows accordingly. A genuine next carrier must be created **before** finite local scale-covariant jet compression, or must use structure outside scalar-homogeneous positive matrix kernels: a source-forced indefinite two-depth law, a non-scalar refinement cocycle, an intrinsically angular/radial coupling, singular boundary/domain data, or a cross-level/all-shell interaction with a matched non-prime-power cancellation that is not inherited from the classical Mellin factors.
