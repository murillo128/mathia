## 2. Exact gcd-decimated coefficient correlation

Let

\[
g=(m,n),\qquad a=\frac m g,\qquad b=\frac n g,
\qquad (a,b)=1.
\tag{4}
\]

Define the finite Laurent correlation coefficients

\[
\kappa_r^{m,n}
:=\sum_{\substack{0\le i\le d_m\\0\le j\le d_n\\j-i=r}}
\overline{a_{m,i}}a_{n,j}
=[z^r]\,\overline{\Phi_m(z^{-1})}\Phi_n(z).
\tag{5}
\]

(The bar is harmless for cyclotomic polynomials, whose coefficients are real integers, but makes the transfer formula valid for general complex masks.)

For each positive integer `r`, introduce the canonical residue decomposition

\[
J_r:\ell^2(\mathbb N_0)\otimes\mathbb C^r\longrightarrow H^2,
\qquad
J_r(e_\ell\otimes f_t)=e_{r\ell+t},
\quad 0\le t<r.
\tag{6}
\]

This is unitary. Define the `b x a` finite matrix

\[
\boxed{
K_{m,n}(u,t)
:=\kappa_{\,g(au-bt)}^{m,n},
\qquad
0\le u<b,\quad 0\le t<a.
}
\tag{7}
\]

Then the full infinite-dimensional transfer satisfies the exact identity

\[
\boxed{
J_b^*W_m^*W_nJ_a
=I_{\ell^2(\mathbb N_0)}\otimes K_{m,n}.
}
\tag{8}
\]

To prove it, apply the transfer to one residue basis vector `e_{a\ell+t}`. A contribution from coefficients `a_{m,i}` and `a_{n,j}` lands at `e_{b\ell+u}` precisely when

\[
m(b\ell+u)+i=n(a\ell+t)+j.
\]

The coarse terms cancel because `ma=nb=gab`, leaving

\[
j-i=g(au-bt).
\tag{9}
\]

Summing the coefficient products satisfying (9) gives exactly (7), independently of `\ell`. No limit, averaging, or asymptotic approximation is involved.

Equation (8) gives a sharper description than merely placing `T_{m,n}` inside a known semigroup algebra. The transfer sees the masks only through the coefficient correlation (5), **decimated by the gcd and reshaped into finitely many residue channels**. The infinite coarse coordinate contributes an identity factor.

## 3. Positive spectral data are finite packets with infinite multiplicity

Taking adjoints in (8) gives

\[
\boxed{
J_a^*(W_m^*W_n)^*(W_m^*W_n)J_a
=I\otimes K_{m,n}^*K_{m,n}.
}
\tag{10}
\]

Therefore every nonzero singular value of `W_m^*W_n` is a singular value of the finite matrix `K_{m,n}`, repeated with countably infinite multiplicity. In particular, if the transfer is nonzero then it is not compact and belongs to no finite Schatten class. Any positive spectral invariant of one finite transfer is already exhausted by the finite matrix `K_{m,n}^*K_{m,n}`; there is no hidden discrete tail on which a Fredholm determinant could place the nontrivial zeros of zeta.

The finite packet can nevertheless be nontrivial, so the result is not a claim that adjoint transfer vanishes. For example

\[
\Phi_4(z)=1+z^2,
\qquad
\Phi_6(z)=1-z+z^2.
\]

Here `g=2`, `a=2`, `b=3`, and (7) gives

\[
K_{4,6}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
2&0\\
0&1\\
0&1
\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{11}
\]

Thus `W_4^*W_6` has singular values `2` and `sqrt(2)`, each with infinite multiplicity. The relational information is real, but it is exactly a two-channel finite mask packet repeated over the coarse index, not a new infinite-dimensional spectral species.

## 4. The prime-power/doubled-prime control collapses exactly

The natural `n <-> 2n` control becomes especially sharp at the transfer level. Let `p` be an odd prime and `r>=1`. The classical identities

\[
\Phi_{p^r}(z)=\sum_{j=0}^{p-1}z^{j p^{r-1}},
\qquad
\Phi_{2p^r}(z)=\Phi_{p^r}(-z)
\tag{12}
\]

imply

\[
\kappa_0^{p^r,2p^r}
=\sum_{j=0}^{p-1}(-1)^j=1.
\tag{13}
\]

All possible lag `p^r` terms vanish because both cyclotomic degrees are strictly smaller than `p^r`. In (4) one has `g=p^r`, `a=1`, `b=2`, so

\[
K_{p^r,2p^r}
=\begin{pmatrix}1\\0\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{14}
\]

Consequently

\[
\boxed{
W_{p^r}^*W_{2p^r}=C_2,
\qquad
W_{2p^r}^*W_{p^r}=C_2^*.
}
\tag{15}
\]

The entire native adjoint transfer between a prime-power shell and its doubled shell is therefore the same bare dyadic refinement/transfer for every odd `p` and every `r`. In particular the relation does not distinguish the prime case `r=1` from the composite prime-power cases `r>1`, and it does not recover new information from the prime-versus-`2p` pairing that defeated several earlier single-level observables.

## 5. Every finite native star word has an affine partial-bijection normal form

The two-factor result is part of a broader finite-depth closure. For positive integers `a,b` and residues `0<=t<a`, `0<=u<b`, define

\[
R_{a,b}^{t,u}e_{a\ell+t}=e_{b\ell+u}
\quad(\ell\ge0),
\qquad
R_{a,b}^{t,u}e_k=0
\quad(k\not\equiv t\pmod a).
\tag{16}
\]

These are partial isometries implementing the order-preserving affine bijection between the arithmetic progressions `a N_0+t` and `b N_0+u`. If `S=M_z` is the unilateral shift, then

\[
\boxed{
R_{a,b}^{t,u}=S^u C_b C_a^*S^{*t}.
}
\tag{17}
\]

In particular, because `d_n<n`, equation (2) is the finite expansion

\[
\boxed{
W_n=\sum_{j=0}^{d_n}a_{n,j}R_{1,n}^{0,j}.
}
\tag{18}
\]

The family of `R` operators is closed under adjoint and composition. Adjoint simply swaps the source and target data:

\[
(R_{a,b}^{t,u})^*=R_{b,a}^{u,t}.
\tag{19}
\]

For composition, let `delta=(b,c)`. If `delta` does not divide `v-u`, then

\[
R_{c,d}^{v,w}R_{a,b}^{t,u}=0.
\tag{20}
\]

Otherwise there is a unique

\[
\ell_0\in\{0,\ldots,c/\delta-1\}
\]

such that

\[
b\ell_0+u\equiv v\pmod c.
\]

Put

\[
s_0=\frac{b\ell_0+u-v}{c}.
\]

The canonical residue bounds imply `0<=s_0<b/delta`, and direct substitution gives

\[
\boxed{
R_{c,d}^{v,w}R_{a,b}^{t,u}
=R_{ac/\delta,\,db/\delta}^{\,a\ell_0+t,\,ds_0+w}.
}
\tag{21}
\]

Equations (18)--(21) prove by induction that **every finite star word in arbitrary native cyclotomic weighted covers `W_n` is a finite linear combination of the same partial affine maps**. All source dependence occurs in the scalar coefficients obtained from finite products and sums of cyclotomic coefficients. The operator arrows themselves are the prime-blind arithmetic-progression maps of the affine semigroup.

A finite collection of explicit path labels does not change this conclusion at the algebraic level: retaining `r` labels produces only a finite matrix amplification `M_r` of this affine partial-map algebra. Such a label space can preserve more finite mask correlations than a scalar path sum, but generic finite matrix amplification is not a new geometric mechanism. To escape, a path/tensor construction must derive a nontrivial **growing or infinite** relational law, topology, or normalization from the Prime-Circle source rather than merely keep finitely many labels.
