# PC-134 — finite resultant-Hessian tensor networks collapse to confluent Cauchy algebra

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED` + `LITERATURE+DERIVED` + `STRUCTURAL-COLLAPSE` + `DECISIVE-BOUNDARY` for fixed finite polynomial/tensor contractions generated only from the PC-128 vertexwise resultant-Hessian conductance. PC-133 left non-symmetric block/tensor data open after the full finite characteristic polynomial collapsed to a Galois-stable integral/rational packet. The most direct tensor repair does not create a new operator algebra: on roots of unity the PC-128 inverse-square chord edge is itself exactly a **second-order Cauchy edge**, so every fixed finite Hessian conductance network lies inside the confluent endpoint algebra already exposed for cotangent networks by PC-096 and PC-097.

There is also a stronger arithmetic consequence for fully closed Hessian networks. If each edge joining shell orders `m` and `n` is normalized by `|Res(Phi_m,Phi_n)|^2`, then the complete closed contraction is an integer. Thus the finite tensor branch carries more combinatorics than a single characteristic polynomial, but it still does not manufacture a new analytic/spectral species relevant to RH. Infinite-depth or growing-level renormalizations, intrinsically non-Cauchy operators, and cross-level organizations not representable by one fixed finite network remain outside this result.

## 1. The Hessian edge is already a confluent Cauchy edge

For distinct unit roots `x,y`, PC-128 uses the conductance

\[
c(x,y)=\frac1{|x-y|^2}.
\]

Because `x^{-1}=\bar x` and `y^{-1}=\bar y`, one has

\[
|x-y|^2
=(x-y)(x^{-1}-y^{-1})
=-\frac{(x-y)^2}{xy}.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
c(x,y)=-\frac{xy}{(x-y)^2}.
}
\]

This identity is the key boundary. The nonlinear-looking chord Hessian does not introduce a new local kernel after algebraic rationalization: it is a Cauchy pole of order two with a one-body monomial on each endpoint.

Let `G=(V,E)` be any finite multigraph whose vertex `v` is assigned an exact-order shell `P_{n_v}^*`, and suppose every edge joins two distinct shell orders, as in the PC-128 pair-shell Hessian. For one assignment `x_v in P_{n_v}^*`, the edge product is exactly

\[
\boxed{
\prod_{\{v,w\}\in E}c(x_v,x_w)
=
(-1)^{|E|}
\left(\prod_{v\in V}x_v^{\deg(v)}\right)
\prod_{\{v,w\}\in E}(x_v-x_w)^{-2}.
}
\]

Parallel Hessian edges merely increase the Cauchy pole order. Thus the entire fixed finite network is a confluent Cauchy multigraph term before any shell summation is performed.

## 2. Hidden-shell contraction closes on cyclotomic Cauchy jets

Choose some vertices as exposed and sum the remaining vertices over their complete primitive shells. To eliminate one hidden variable `u in P_n^*`, collect equal neighboring endpoints after the ordinary collision stratification of PC-097. Its dependence has the form

\[
u^d\prod_{j=1}^q(u-z_j)^{-r_j},
\qquad r_j\ge 1,
\]

with coefficients rational in the remaining endpoints. Repeated-pole partial fractions in `u` reduce this to one-body shell transforms

\[
H_{n,r}^{(d)}(z)
:=
\sum_{u\in P_n^*}\frac{u^d}{(u-z)^r}.
\]

Write

\[
\ell_n(z)=\frac{\Phi_n'(z)}{\Phi_n(z)}.
\]

For `d=0`, differentiation of the logarithmic derivative gives

\[
\boxed{
H_{n,r}^{(0)}(z)
=-\frac1{(r-1)!}\,\ell_n^{(r-1)}(z),
\qquad z\notin P_n^*.
}
\]

For general `d`, expand `u^d=(z+(u-z))^d`. Terms whose remaining power of `(u-z)` is negative are the same logarithmic-derivative jets; the polynomial remainder reduces to primitive-root power sums

\[
\sum_{u\in P_n^*}u^j=c_n(j),
\]

the classical Ramanujan sums (with `c_n(0)=varphi(n)`). Therefore every hidden-vertex elimination stays inside a finite algebra generated by endpoint Cauchy factors, cyclotomic logarithmic-derivative jets, and Ramanujan sums.

Inducting over the hidden vertices proves the fixed finite closure. If the same shell label occurs at nonadjacent vertices, equality strata can make endpoints collide after earlier eliminations; PC-097's collision stratification and punctured/confluent limits apply verbatim. The mechanism is algebraic and works for arbitrary finite disjoint point sets before the cyclotomic specialization, so no prime-specific associator or hidden tensor is created by the elimination itself.

## 3. Exact two-step witness

The simplest nontrivial Hessian composition makes the collapse visible without graph notation. Let `a,c` be distinct unit roots outside `P_n^*`. Then

\[
\sum_{u\in P_n^*}c(a,u)c(u,c)
=
ac\sum_{u\in P_n^*}
\frac{u^2}{(u-a)^2(u-c)^2}.
\]

The repeated-pole decomposition

\[
\frac{u^2}{(u-a)^2(u-c)^2}
=
\frac{a^2}{(a-c)^2(u-a)^2}
+
\frac{c^2}{(a-c)^2(u-c)^2}
-
\frac{2ac}{(a-c)^3(u-a)}
+
\frac{2ac}{(a-c)^3(u-c)}
\]

gives the exact endpoint formula

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{u\in P_n^*}c(a,u)c(u,c)
=
ac\left[
-\frac{a^2\ell_n'(a)+c^2\ell_n'(c)}{(a-c)^2}
+
\frac{2ac\bigl(\ell_n(a)-\ell_n(c)\bigr)}{(a-c)^3}
\right].
}
\]

The confluent diagonal is equally explicit:

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{u\in P_n^*}c(a,u)^2
=
-a^2\left[
\ell_n'(a)+a\ell_n''(a)+\frac{a^2}{6}\ell_n'''(a)
\right].
}
\]

For comparison, the one-step degree already has

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{u\in P_n^*}c(a,u)
=a^2\ell_n'(a)+a\ell_n(a).
}
\]

Thus even the first genuinely quadratic composition of Hessian edges produces no new interior object: it is a finite endpoint jet of the original cyclotomic polynomial.

## 4. Fixed finite polynomial contractions of Hessian blocks are covered

A PC-128 Hessian block is a weighted graph Laplacian. Its off-diagonal entries are signed conductances `-c(x,y)`, while every diagonal entry is a finite degree sum of the same conductances. Consequently any fixed finite polynomial expression in Hessian entries, followed by any finite index contraction over complete exact-order shells, expands into a finite signed sum of the conductance multigraphs treated above. Diagonal factors simply introduce summed incident edges before contraction.

Therefore replacing a single Hessian spectrum by a finite product, mixed moment, finite tensor network, polynomial block word, or other finite polynomial contraction does **not** evade the PC-096/097 endpoint-collapse mechanism. This statement does not include inverses, genuinely non-polynomial functional calculus, or a limit whose network depth grows with the level; those require separate analysis rather than being inferred from finite expansion.

## 5. Closed Hessian networks have a natural integer normalization

There is an additional arithmetic constraint that is specific to complete cyclotomic shells. For a closed network `G`, sum every vertex over its assigned primitive shell:

\[
Z_G
=
\sum_{(x_v)}
\prod_{e=\{v,w\}\in E}c(x_v,x_w).
\]

For each edge `e={v,w}`, put

\[
R_e
=
\left|Res\bigl(\Phi_{n_v},\Phi_{n_w}\bigr)\right|.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
\left(\prod_{e\in E}R_e^2\right)Z_G\in\mathbb Z.
}
\]

The proof is edgewise. For fixed endpoints `x in P_m^*`, `y in P_n^*`, the cyclotomic resultant is, up to sign,

\[
Res(\Phi_m,\Phi_n)
=
\prod_{\alpha\in P_m^*}\prod_{\beta\in P_n^*}(\alpha-\beta).
\]

Hence `R_{m,n}/(x-y)` is, up to sign, a product of the remaining cyclotomic differences and is an algebraic integer. Using the Cauchy form of the conductance,

\[
\boxed{
R_{m,n}^2c(x,y)
=-xy\left(\frac{R_{m,n}}{x-y}\right)^2
}
\]

is therefore an algebraic integer. After multiplying the whole graph by the edgewise normalization, every assignment summand is an algebraic integer, so their finite sum is one as well.

Now work in `Q(zeta_M)` with `M=lcm_v(n_v)`. Every Galois automorphism `zeta_M -> zeta_M^a` with `a in (Z/MZ)^times` preserves each exact-order shell and merely permutes the complete assignment set. The normalized scalar is therefore Galois-fixed, hence rational. A rational algebraic integer is an integer, proving the claim.

Equivalently,

\[
\boxed{
Z_G\in
\left(\prod_{e\in E}R_e^2\right)^{-1}\mathbb Z.
}
\]

If every edge pair has resultant one, the raw closed contraction is already integral. This strengthens the broad rationality warning of PC-091 for this Hessian subclass; it is not a claim that the resulting integers are arithmetically trivial.

An exact exceptional control is the two-parallel-edge network between `P_3^*` and `P_6^*`. PC-133 gives the conductances `1,1,1/4,1/4`, so

\[
Z_G
=1^2+1^2+(1/4)^2+(1/4)^2
=\frac{17}{8}.
\]

Here `R_{3,6}=4` and there are two edges, hence

\[
\boxed{
4^4 Z_G
=256\cdot\frac{17}{8}
=544\in\mathbb Z.
}
\]

This control also prevents accidentally strengthening the theorem to unnormalized integrality in the exceptional resultant regime.

## 6. Novelty audit and RH boundary

The algebraic engine is not new mathematics. Cauchy and confluent Cauchy partial fractions are classical; PC-096 already used the repeated-pole version to collapse finite disjoint cotangent multigraphs, and PC-097 extended that mechanism through repeated-shell collision strata. PC-091 already records the general Galois fixed-field reason that complete finite rational-function shell contractions are rational. The new durable point for this research line is the transfer to the later PC-128 Hessian branch through the exact identity `c(x,y)=-xy/(x-y)^2`, together with the edgewise resultant normalization above. A targeted search did not locate this exact Prime-Circle Hessian-network specialization, but no historical novelty is claimed from that absence.

For RH, the result is a negative structural boundary rather than a new bridge. A fixed finite Hessian tensor network reduces to finite cyclotomic jets/Ramanujan data at exposed legs, and a fully closed one is rational with the explicit resultant denominator bound above. The construction introduces no complex analytic parameter `s`, no gamma factor, no `s <-> 1-s` functional equation, no distinguished critical line, and no zero divisor related intrinsically to the nontrivial zeros of `zeta`.

This does **not** imply that sequences of these rational/integer quantities are useless. The surviving boundary is precisely where a fixed finite Cauchy network description stops being the whole object: infinite-depth or growing-level renormalization, cross-level organizations of the finite data, genuinely non-polynomial limits, intrinsically non-Cauchy tensor/operator couplings, and the global uniformization/monodromy branch. Those possibilities need independent derivations and novelty audits.
