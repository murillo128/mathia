# PC-122 — coherent cross-shell Toeplitz determinant collapses to resultant and zero

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED` + `CLASSICAL-IDENTITY` + `DECISIVE-NEGATIVE` + `PRIOR-ART-REDIRECTION`. The finite-section rank and threshold determinant are derived exactly below. The resultant identification is classical Sylvester-matrix algebra, and the cyclotomic arithmetic at the threshold is exactly the pairwise-shell resultant already isolated in PC-002. No theorem-level novelty is claimed for general block Toeplitz or matrix spectral-factorization theory.

PC-121 classified the canonical single-shell Toeplitz determinant for the scalar symbol `|Phi_n|^2` and explicitly left matrix-valued symbols that couple several shells before compression outside its obstruction. The most direct coherent lift is to keep the shell amplitudes as a vector and form their positive matrix Gram symbol. That candidate does couple the shells before taking a finite-section determinant, but its coherence is too strong: the symbol has one amplitude channel, so the block Toeplitz section becomes an overcomplete polynomial Gram matrix. For two shells the last nonsingular section is exactly a cyclotomic resultant; immediately after that threshold the determinant is identically zero.

## 1. Canonical coherent cross-shell symbol

Fix distinct shell indices

\[
S=\{n_1,\ldots,n_r\},\qquad r\ge2,
\]

and set

\[
p(z)=
\begin{pmatrix}
\Phi_{n_1}(z)\\
\vdots\\
\Phi_{n_r}(z)
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
W_S(z)=p(z)p(z)^*.
\]

Thus

\[
(W_S)_{ij}(z)=\Phi_{n_i}(z)\overline{\Phi_{n_j}(z)},
\qquad |z|=1.
\]

This is the matrix-valued analogue of the scalar weight in PC-121: on the diagonal it is exactly `|Phi_{n_i}|^2`, while the off-diagonal entries retain the relative phase between different cyclotomic shells before compression.

With normalized Haar measure `dmu` on the unit circle, define the `rN x rN` finite block Toeplitz section by

\[
\mathbb T_N(W_S)_{(i,k),(j,\ell)}
=
\int_{\mathbb T}
 z^k\Phi_{n_i}(z)
 \overline{z^\ell\Phi_{n_j}(z)}\,d\mu(z),
\qquad 0\le k,\ell<N.
\]

This differs at most by the harmless transpose convention from the standard Fourier-index definition of a block Toeplitz section. In this convention its Gram structure is explicit.

## 2. Exact Gram factorization and dimension obstruction

Let

\[
D=\max_{1\le j\le r}\deg\Phi_{n_j}
 =\max_{1\le j\le r}\varphi(n_j).
\]

The section `mathbb T_N(W_S)` is the Gram matrix of the `rN` polynomials

\[
\mathcal G_{S,N}
=
\{z^k\Phi_{n_j}(z):0\le k<N,\ 1\le j\le r\}.
\]

Every member has degree at most `N-1+D`, so all of them lie in the `(N+D)`-dimensional coefficient Hilbert space

\[
\mathcal P_{<N+D}=\operatorname{span}\{1,z,\ldots,z^{N+D-1}\}.
\]

Consequently

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{rank}\mathbb T_N(W_S)\le N+D.
}
\]

In particular,

\[
(r-1)N>D
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\boxed{\det\mathbb T_N(W_S)=0}.
\]

Thus a fixed finite collection of shells with a single coherent amplitude channel cannot support a nonzero large-section Toeplitz determinant. The zero is not an asymptotic approximation: it is exact finite-dimensional linear dependence.

## 3. The eventual rank is exactly `N+D`

The upper bound is sharp once `N>D`. Choose a shell polynomial

\[
f=\Phi_{n_a},\qquad \deg f=D,
\]

and any second distinct shell polynomial

\[
g=\Phi_{n_b},\qquad \deg g=d\le D.
\]

Distinct cyclotomic polynomials are coprime. Consider the coefficient map generated by these two shell channels,

\[
M_N:\mathcal P_{<N}\oplus\mathcal P_{<N}
\longrightarrow
\mathcal P_{<N+D},
\qquad
(q,r)\longmapsto qf+rg.
\]

If `(q,r)` is in its kernel, coprimality gives

\[
q=gh,\qquad r=-fh
\]

for a polynomial `h`. Since `deg r<N` and `deg f=D`, one must have

\[
\deg h<N-D.
\]

Conversely every such `h` produces a kernel vector, and the `q` degree constraint is automatic because `d\le D`. Therefore, for `N>D`,

\[
\dim\ker M_N=N-D,
\]

so

\[
\operatorname{rank}M_N=2N-(N-D)=N+D.
\]

The two-shell columns are already a subset of the columns defining the full `r`-shell Gram matrix. Hence, for every `r\ge2` and every `N>D`,

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{rank}\mathbb T_N(W_S)=N+D
}
\]

and therefore

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{nullity}\mathbb T_N(W_S)=(r-1)N-D.
}
\]

The zero-eigenspace is extensive: for fixed `r>1` its multiplicity grows linearly with section size.

## 4. Two shells have a sharp resultant threshold

For two shells there is an exact all-`N` rank formula. Keep `f` monic of degree `D` and `g` monic of degree `d\le D`. If `N\le D`, a nonzero kernel relation

\[
qf+rg=0,
\qquad \deg q,\deg r<N,
\]

would again require `r=-fh`, impossible because a nonzero multiple of `f` has degree at least `D\ge N`. Thus the two-shell family is linearly independent for every `N\le D`. Combining this with the previous section gives

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{rank}\mathbb T_N(pp^*)
=
\min(2N,N+D),
\qquad p=(f,g)^T.
}
\]

So `N=D` is the last nonsingular two-shell section. At that threshold the coefficient map

\[
M_D:\mathcal P_{<D}\oplus\mathcal P_{<D}
\to\mathcal P_{<2D}
\]

is square. Its determinant is, up to sign, the resultant `Res(f,g)`.

For equal degrees this is the ordinary Sylvester determinant. If `d<D`, order the `q` coefficients so that the `D-d` highest shifts of the monic degree-`D` polynomial `f` are eliminated first. On output degrees `D+d,\ldots,2D-1` they form a unit triangular block, because neither `rg` nor the lower `q` shifts reach those degrees. What remains is exactly the ordinary `(D+d) x (D+d)` Sylvester map

\[
\mathcal P_{<d}\oplus\mathcal P_{<D}
\to\mathcal P_{<D+d}.
\]

Hence

\[
|\det M_D|=|\operatorname{Res}(f,g)|.
\]

Because the Toeplitz section is the Gram matrix `M_D^*M_D`,

\[
\boxed{
\det\mathbb T_D(pp^*)
=|\operatorname{Res}(f,g)|^2.
}
\]

Specializing back to two cyclotomic shells gives the exact transition

\[
\boxed{
\det\mathbb T_D(W_{\{m,n\}})
=|\operatorname{Res}(\Phi_m,\Phi_n)|^2,
\qquad
\det\mathbb T_N(W_{\{m,n\}})=0\quad(N>D).
}
\]

By Apostol's cyclotomic resultant theorem already used in PC-002, the nonzero threshold value is therefore only the classical prime-power-jump invariant: for `n>m>1`, it is `p^{2 phi(m)}` when `n/m` is a positive power of a prime `p`, and `1` otherwise.

## 5. Exact checks

The coefficient-Gram construction gives simple audit cases.

For `Phi_3` and `Phi_6`, both degrees are `2` and

\[
|\operatorname{Res}(\Phi_3,\Phi_6)|=4.
\]

Thus

\[
\det\mathbb T_2=16,
\qquad
\det\mathbb T_N=0\quad(N\ge3).
\]

For `Phi_3` and `Phi_9`, the degrees are `2` and `6`, so `D=6`, while

\[
|\operatorname{Res}(\Phi_3,\Phi_9)|=9.
\]

Hence

\[
\det\mathbb T_6=81,
\qquad
\det\mathbb T_N=0\quad(N\ge7).
\]

Exact coefficient-matrix calculations also reproduce the predicted rank `N+D` beyond the threshold. These checks require no floating-point root evaluation.

## 6. Matched non-arithmetic control

The collapse is not specifically cyclotomic. The same rank proof works for **any** two distinct coprime fixed polynomials `f,g`, and the `N=D` determinant for monic polynomials is the square of their ordinary resultant.

This is an important falsification control. It shows that the extensive nullspace is forced by the one-channel polynomial factorization `W=pp^*`, not by prime arithmetic. Cyclotomy enters only when the generic polynomial resultant is evaluated, and then PC-002 already classifies it completely.

Therefore neither the singularity nor the threshold determinant supplies a new arithmetic mechanism. The prime-circle geometry merely chooses a particularly rigid collection of coprime monic polynomials inside a generic algebraic phenomenon.

## 7. Prior-art and novelty audit

Three different layers of the result have different novelty status.

1. **Block Toeplitz / matrix spectral factorization is classical.** Matrix-valued Fejer--Riesz theory and finite sections of block Toeplitz operators already provide the surrounding operator-theoretic framework, including rank-deficient polynomial symbols. A directed literature search across matrix Fejer--Riesz factorization, rank-deficient polynomial matrix functions, and finite block Toeplitz sections found that general framework. The present claim does not rely on asserting novelty for it.
2. **The threshold determinant is classical resultant algebra.** The coefficient matrix at `N=D` is the equal-padded-degree Sylvester variant; for monic `f` its determinant is exactly the usual resultant. In the cyclotomic case the resultant itself is the Apostol invariant already stored in PC-002 and anchored in `SOURCES.md`.
3. **The project-specific contribution is a no-go classification.** The useful new information for this research line is that the most canonical matrix-valued continuation explicitly left open by PC-121 does not escape the scalar collapse: before an asymptotic block-Toeplitz regime can arise, its determinant hits a classical pairwise resultant and then becomes identically zero.

A search did not reveal this exact Prime-Circle formulation under cyclotomic cross-shell Toeplitz terminology, but that absence is not treated as theorem-level novelty. The decisive value is the exact obstruction and its reduction to already-known cyclotomic data.

## 8. Why this rules out the canonical coherent repair of PC-121

The intended repair was

\[
\text{several primitive shells}
\to
\text{matrix symbol retaining cross-shell phase}
\to
\text{block Toeplitz determinant}
\to
\text{new spectral divisor}.
\]

For the direct geometry-forced coherent lift, the actual chain is

\[
\boxed{
\{\Phi_{n_j}\}
\to p p^*
\to \text{polynomial Gram section}
\to
\begin{cases}
|\operatorname{Res}(\Phi_m,\Phi_n)|^2,& r=2,\ N=D,\\
0,& N>D\text{ for }r=2,
\end{cases}
}
\]

with eventual exact singularity for every fixed `r\ge2`.

There is therefore no nonzero large-`N` Toeplitz determinant whose zeros or asymptotics could encode a Riemann-zero divisor. Regularizing by deleting the extensive zero eigenspace and taking a pseudodeterminant would define a different observable; it is not generated by the ordinary finite-section determinant itself.

## 9. Boundary of the obstruction

This finding is deliberately narrower than a no-go for all matrix-valued Toeplitz ideas.

It rules out the **single coherent amplitude channel**

\[
W_S(z)=p(z)p(z)^*
\]

built directly from finitely many cyclotomic shell amplitudes. It also rules out interpreting its ordinary finite-section determinant as a new RH mechanism.

It does **not** rule out:

- a full-rank matrix symbol with several independently geometry-forced amplitude channels;
- a pseudodeterminant or quotient after removing the forced polynomial syzygy space, provided that regularization itself is derived rather than chosen ad hoc;
- cross-level limits in which the number of shells or conductors vary with `N`;
- matrix symbols built from genuinely different prime-circle fields rather than copies of one polynomial amplitude channel;
- the extensive old/new cotangent coupling surviving after PC-047;
- the nonlinear uniformization/monodromy branch rooted in PC-017.

Those constructions would require new structure beyond the direct matrix lift of `|Phi_n|^2`.

## 10. Falsification surface

The claim has five exact audit points.

1. **Gram convention:** the stated block Toeplitz entry must equal the inner product of `z^k Phi_{n_i}` and `z^ell Phi_{n_j}`.
2. **Ambient dimension:** all `rN` Gram vectors must lie in the `N+D` dimensional polynomial space `P_{<N+D}`.
3. **Syzygy calculation:** for two coprime shell polynomials every relation must have the form `(q,r)=(gh,-fh)`; this must give kernel dimension `N-D` when `N>D` and no kernel when `N\le D`.
4. **Threshold determinant:** at `N=D`, triangular elimination of the padded high-degree shifts must reduce the coefficient matrix to the ordinary Sylvester matrix, so the Gram determinant is `|Res(f,g)|^2`.
5. **Non-arithmetic control:** replacing cyclotomic polynomials by arbitrary coprime monic polynomials of the same degrees must preserve the rank/singularity theorem. If it does not, the derivation is wrong; if it does, the only cyclotomic arithmetic left at the threshold is the already-classical resultant.

Failure of any item invalidates the no-go. If they all hold, the ordinary coherent cross-shell determinant cannot supply an asymptotic spectral divisor.

## Research consequence

The matrix-valued boundary left open by PC-121 has now been partially but sharply narrowed. Merely retaining several shell amplitudes coherently as one vector before Toeplitz compression is insufficient:

\[
\boxed{
W_S=p p^*
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\operatorname{nullity}\mathbb T_N(W_S)=(r-1)N-D
\text{ for }N>D.
}
\]

For two shells the exact last nonsingular section returns PC-002's pairwise resultant and the next section is already singular. Further Toeplitz work should therefore require multiple independent geometry-forced channels, a justified quotient/pseudodeterminant, or a genuinely varying cross-level construction rather than another one-channel coherent packaging of the same cyclotomic amplitudes.
