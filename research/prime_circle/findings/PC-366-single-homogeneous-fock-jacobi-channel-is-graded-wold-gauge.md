# PC-366 — single homogeneous Fock Jacobi channel is graded-Wold gauge

- **Finding ID:** `PC-366`
- **Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED` + `LITERATURE+DERIVED` + `NUMERICAL-SANITY` + `DECISIVE-NEGATIVE`
- **Status detail:** decisive negative for the simplest non-scalar/tensor-valued two-sided escape left by PC-365: a fixed homogeneous Prime-Circle tensor used as one full-Fock creation channel together with any source-independent scalar grade Hamiltonian
- **Workflow:** `DERIVED + PRIOR-ART AUDIT + ADVERSARIAL CONTROL`
- **Research line:** `prime_circle`
- **Result type:** exact operator-theoretic no-go / graded unitary classification
- **RH relevance:** high as a boundary result. A homogeneous tensor can retain detailed oriented/cyclotomic information before spectralization, but a single creation/annihilation channel coupled to tensor grade forgets its direction, entanglement and arithmetic content completely; only its degree and norm survive.

## Claim

PC-365 closes the scalar two-sided repair `aN+cA+conj(c)A*`, but leaves open grade-dependent, matrix-valued or multichannel recurrences. The first source-native non-scalar candidate is already present in PC-349: the degree-two inversion Lie tensor, which is an actual element of the two-letter tensor sector rather than a scalar amplitude. One can therefore ask whether putting such a tensor directly into a self-adjoint full-Fock creation/annihilation operator creates a spectrum that remembers its Prime-Circle geometry.

For a **single homogeneous channel**, it does not.

Let `K` be a finite-dimensional complex Hilbert space of dimension `d>=1`, let

\[
\mathcal F(K)=\bigoplus_{n\ge0}K^{\otimes n}
\tag{1}
\]

be the full Fock space, and let the number operator be

\[
N|_{K^{\otimes n}}=nI.
\tag{2}
\]

Fix a nonzero homogeneous tensor `g in K^{otimes q}` of degree `q>=1`, put `r=||g||`, and let

\[
L_g\xi=g\otimes\xi,
\qquad
S_g:=r^{-1}L_g.
\tag{3}
\]

Then `S_g` is a pure isometry satisfying

\[
NS_g=S_g(N+q).
\tag{4}
\]

Its wandering space `W_g=ker S_g^*` is graded, and its grade-`k` multiplicity is

\[
\boxed{
\dim W_{g,k}=
\begin{cases}
d^k,&0\le k<q,\\
d^k-d^{k-q},&k\ge q.
\end{cases}}
\tag{5}
\]

These dimensions depend only on `(d,q)`, not on the direction of `g` inside `K^{otimes q}`.

Consequently, if `g,h in K^{otimes q}` are nonzero and have the same norm, there is a unitary `U` on `F(K)` such that

\[
\boxed{
UN=NU,
\qquad
UL_gU^*=L_h.
}
\tag{6}
\]

Thus the **joint unitary type of `(N,L_g)` depends only on `(d,q,||g||)`**. Every scalar grade-local operator built functorially from this pair has the same blindness. In particular, for `a>0` and `c in C`,

\[
H_{a,c,g}:=aN+cL_g+\overline c\,L_g^*
\tag{7}
\]

is self-adjoint on `D(N)`, has compact resolvent, and is unitarily equivalent to `H_{a,c,h}` whenever `||g||=||h||`. The phase of `c` is itself removable by the grade gauge generated by `N`, so the spectrum of (7) depends only on

\[
\boxed{d,\ q,\ a,\ |c|\,||g||.}
\tag{8}
\]

For the actual Prime-Circle degree-two inversion tensor from PC-349 this means that the entire tensor direction -- including its oriented shell coefficients -- disappears under the most literal self-adjoint creation/annihilation spectralization. At this single-channel level, tensor-valued input is not yet a genuinely matrix-valued spectral mechanism.

## 1. A homogeneous left creator is a pure `q`-step isometry

For `xi in F(K)`,

\[
\|L_g\xi\|=\|g\|\,\|\xi\|,
\tag{9}
\]

because the Fock norm is the Hilbert tensor norm. Hence `S_g=L_g/r` is an isometry. Since it raises tensor degree by exactly `q`,

\[
N S_g\xi=S_g(N+q)\xi
\tag{10}
\]

on the finite-word core and therefore on the natural operator domain.

Moreover,

\[
\operatorname{Ran}S_g^m
=\widehat g^{\otimes m}\otimes\mathcal F(K),
\qquad
\widehat g=g/r.
\tag{11}
\]

Every vector in `Ran S_g^m` has tensor degree at least `mq`. Therefore

\[
\bigcap_{m\ge0}\operatorname{Ran}S_g^m=\{0\},
\tag{12}
\]

so the isometry has no unitary part: it is pure.

The grade-`k` part of `Ran S_g` is zero for `k<q` and is

\[
\widehat g\otimes K^{\otimes(k-q)}
\subset K^{\otimes k}
\tag{13}
\]

for `k>=q`. Since tensoring by the unit vector `hat g` is isometric, this subspace has dimension `d^{k-q}`. Its orthogonal complement inside `K^{otimes k}` is exactly `W_{g,k}=W_g cap K^{otimes k}`. This proves (5).

The important point is not merely that `S_g` is a shift. The number operator remembers the starting grade of every wandering vector, so a useful classification must preserve **both** the shift and `N`. Formula (5) shows that even this graded multiplicity data is independent of the tensor direction.

## 2. Graded Wold decomposition removes the tensor direction exactly

The ordinary Wold decomposition for a pure isometry gives

\[
\mathcal F(K)
=
\bigoplus_{m\ge0}S_g^mW_g.
\tag{14}
\]

Because `W_g` is graded, choose an orthonormal basis `B_{g,k}` for each `W_{g,k}`. Then (14) refines to the orthogonal chain decomposition

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal F(K)
=
\bigoplus_{k\ge0}
\bigoplus_{w\in B_{g,k}}
\overline{\operatorname{span}}
\{S_g^m w:m\ge0\}.
}
\tag{15}
\]

On every such chain,

\[
S_g(S_g^mw)=S_g^{m+1}w,
\qquad
N(S_g^mw)=(k+qm)S_g^mw.
\tag{16}
\]

Now take another homogeneous tensor `h in K^{otimes q}` with `||h||=||g||`. Formula (5) gives

\[
\dim W_{g,k}=\dim W_{h,k}
\qquad\text{for every }k.
\tag{17}
\]

Choose arbitrary grade-preserving unitaries

\[
U_k:W_{g,k}\to W_{h,k}
\tag{18}
\]

and extend them along the Wold chains by

\[
U(S_g^m w):=S_h^mU_kw.
\tag{19}
\]

Orthogonality of (15) makes this extension unitary. Equation (16) then gives simultaneously

\[
US_g=S_hU,
\qquad
UN=NU.
\tag{20}
\]

Multiplying the first identity by the common norm proves (6).

This equivalence is much stronger than a reciprocal-parity control. It does not require `g` and `h` to be related by a one-particle unitary, by second quantization, by a cyclotomic symmetry, or by any simple tensor automorphism. **Any two same-degree tensors of the same norm, including tensors with completely different Schmidt/entanglement structure, are indistinguishable by the joint pair `(N,L_g)`.** The freedom lies in the graded wandering spaces of the induced isometry.

## 3. The self-adjoint repair is a direct sum of classical scalar Jacobi chains

Because `L_g` is bounded, (7) is a bounded symmetric perturbation of `aN`; hence it is self-adjoint on `D(N)`. Since each tensor grade is finite-dimensional and the eigenvalues of `N` tend to infinity, `(N+i)^{-1}` is compact. Bounded perturbation preserves compact resolvent, so `H_{a,c,g}` has discrete spectrum of finite multiplicity with no finite accumulation point.

The decomposition (15) makes its remaining structure completely explicit. After removing the phase of `c` by a diagonal chain gauge, the restriction to the chain based at a grade-`k` wandering vector is the scalar half-line Jacobi operator

\[
(J_k u)_m
=
a(k+qm)u_m
+|c|r\,(u_{m+1}+u_{m-1}),
\qquad u_{-1}=0.
\tag{21}
\]

The chain occurs with multiplicity exactly `dim W_{g,k}` from (5). Therefore

\[
\boxed{
H_{a,c,g}
\simeq
\bigoplus_{k\ge0}
J_k^{\oplus\dim W_{g,k}},
}
\tag{22}
\]

and every datum on the right-hand side is determined by `(d,q,a,|c|r)`.

So the apparently tensor-valued operator has not produced a matrix Jacobi problem at all. Wold reduction turns it into scalar discrete-Stark/Jacobi chains. Linear diagonal and constant off-diagonal Jacobi matrices are classical; in particular Štampach and Šťovíček study precisely this class and its characteristic functions/special-function solutions. Any zeros introduced by the scalar chain characteristic function are therefore zeros of a classical Jacobi special-function problem, not a source-derived zeta divisor.

A phase in `c` contains no hidden orientation. From (4),

\[
e^{itN}L_ge^{-itN}=e^{itq}L_g,
\tag{23}
\]

so choosing `t=-arg(c)/q` replaces `c` by `|c|`. This leaves (8) as the complete parameter list.

## 4. Application to the first nonzero Prime-Circle inversion tensor

PC-349 constructs the reciprocity-closed radial signature and proves that its abelianization is trivial. Its first nonzero Lie datum is the degree-two bivector

\[
A_S\in K_S^{\otimes2},
\tag{24}
\]

whose coefficients are the exact radial areas

\[
A_{mn}=\varphi(n)I_m-\varphi(m)I_n.
\tag{25}
\]

Thus `A_S` is source-native, oriented, and genuinely noncommutative before spectralization. It is an especially natural candidate for the first non-scalar repair left open after PC-365.

If `A_S != 0`, set `g=A_S` in (7). Equations (6)--(8) show that the resulting spectral problem forgets every coefficient in (25) except their aggregate Hilbert norm `||A_S||`. A non-arithmetic tensor `h in K_S^{otimes2}` with

\[
\|h\|=\|A_S\|
\tag{26}
\]

produces an operator unitarily equivalent by a unitary that also fixes the grade observable `N`. The control need not imitate the cyclotomic coefficient pattern, prime-power support, totients, radicals, Ramanujan moments, or reciprocal geometry.

For the standard Fock metric, the reciprocal parity already used in PC-355 is a still simpler matched control: `J^{otimes2}` is unitary and therefore preserves `||A_S||`. PC-366 strengthens that obstruction from one particular parity twin to the entire norm sphere of the homogeneous tensor sector.

If `A_S=0` for a chosen shell set, the proposed channel is of course trivial and supplies no spectrum at all.

## 5. Prior-art and novelty audit

No novelty is claimed for Wold decomposition, Fock left creation, or scalar Jacobi theory. The decomposition of a pure isometry into unilateral-shift copies is classical operator theory. In the Fock/tensor setting, Paul S. Muhly and Baruch Solel, **Tensor Algebras, Induced Representations, and the Wold Decomposition**, *Canadian Journal of Mathematics* 51:4 (1999), 850--880, DOI `10.4153/CJM-1999-037-8`, develops the Wold/induced-representation framework on full Fock modules. PC-355 and PC-360 already anchor the neighboring free-Fock/left-creation theory through the standard Popescu and Davidson--Pitts frameworks.

For the scalar chains in (21), František Štampach and Pavel Šťovíček, **The characteristic function for Jacobi matrices with applications**, *Linear Algebra and its Applications* 438:11 (2013), 4130--4155, DOI `10.1016/j.laa.2013.01.021`, treats Jacobi operators with discrete spectrum and explicitly includes the case of a linear diagonal and constant neighboring diagonals, expressing characteristic functions through classical special functions.

The line-local mathematical delta is narrower: combine the exact Prime-Circle homogeneous tensor candidate with the **grade-preserving** Wold multiplicity calculation (5). That calculation proves that adjoining the physically/naturally available grade operator does not rescue the tensor direction. The result is therefore a negative boundary theorem for this research line, not a new Wold or Jacobi theorem and not an RH criterion.

A directed novelty search found no basis for interpreting the unitary class (22) as a new zeta spectral construction. The only source scalar left after the reduction is `||g||`; unless an independent theorem ties that scalar sequence to zeta zeros, wrapping it in the classical Jacobi spectrum would be exactly the arbitrary-spectral-wrapper failure excluded by the Prime-Circle mandate.

## 6. Falsification and adversarial controls

The result has several direct failure tests.

- **Isometry test:** `L_g^*L_g=||g||^2I` must hold for the full-Fock Hilbert tensor norm.
- **Purity test:** the intersection of the ranges of `S_g^m` must vanish. Exact grade raising by `mq` proves this.
- **Wandering multiplicity test:** on grade `k>=q`, the range is one copy of `K^{otimes(k-q)}` and therefore has dimension `d^{k-q}`; any tensor-direction dependence in (5) would refute the classification.
- **Joint-grade test:** the intertwiner must commute with `N`, not merely conjugate the shift. Equation (19) preserves every grade `k+qm`, so this stronger requirement is met.
- **Control test:** replacing the Prime-Circle tensor by an arbitrary same-norm tensor must leave the joint `(N,L_g)` unitary class unchanged. This is exact, not asymptotic.
- **Spectral test:** finite Fock truncations for `d=2,q=2` and two visibly different same-norm tensors, for example `e_0 tensor e_0` and `(e_0 tensor e_0+e_1 tensor e_1)/sqrt(2)`, give identical eigenvalues for the corresponding truncated `N+alpha(L_g+L_g^*)`; this numerical check was used only as a sanity test for the exact proof.
- **Wrapper test:** any RH claim based only on the scalar Jacobi spectrum must explain why the same construction on an arbitrary same-norm non-arithmetic tensor is excluded. Without such an explanation, the candidate fails the mandate's matched-control criterion.

These tests distinguish the present theorem from PC-355. PC-355 obtains source/control equivalence from a known tensor-algebra parity. PC-366 shows that for one homogeneous channel the **operator representation itself has enough Wold gauge freedom to erase all tensor direction**, even when no parity relation exists between source and control.

## Boundary assessment

PC-366 closes a real but limited part of the post-PC-365 frontier. Replacing the scalar raising amplitude by a single fixed homogeneous Prime-Circle tensor does not create a matrix-valued spectral problem; after graded Wold decomposition it is a direct sum of universal scalar Jacobi chains. The first nonzero inversion tensor from PC-349 therefore cannot become RH-facing merely by being inserted as `L_g+L_g^*` next to the number operator.

The result deliberately does **not** close genuinely multichannel constructions. Two or more independent homogeneous tensors `g_1,g_2,...` can retain relative Gram data, overlaps of ranges, noncommutative relations and source-dependent channel geometry that a single isometry cannot see. It also does not cover source-forced grade-dependent operator-valued coefficients, same-grade blocks, nonlocal mixed-degree couplings, or source-dependent domains/boundary conditions. Those remain mathematically distinct live directions and must be audited against matched non-arithmetic controls before any spectral feature counts as progress.
