# PC-240 — old projection and original coordinate generate only fresh matrix defects

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED` + `PRIOR-ART-CLASSICALIZATION` + `DECISIVE-BOUNDARY` for the most direct genuinely noncommuting fixed-base escape left open by PC-225, PC-238 and PC-239.

At a coprime refinement `N -> Nq`, retain simultaneously the canonical old-space projection `E_{N,q}` and the intrinsic original-coordinate multiplier `Z=M_z`, before any singular-value or multiplication-functional-calculus compression. They genuinely do not commute. In fact their commutator has operator norm one for every fresh degree. Nevertheless their complete finite-level C*-algebra is exactly

\[
\boxed{
C^*(Z,E_{N,q})\cong C(\mu_N)\otimes M_q(\mathbb C)
\cong\bigoplus_{x\in\mu_N}M_q(\mathbb C).
}
\]

Thus the pair is maximally noncommutative **inside each fresh fiber** but remains completely decomposed over the coarse CRT coordinate. No cross-coarse arithmetic coupling is created. The proof uses only `gcd(N,q)=1`; replacing the prime `q` by any coprime refinement degree gives the same matrix-fiber architecture.

There is also a precise growing-fiber boundary. The pair itself canonically marks one fresh Fourier vector and one cyclic generator, hence supplies an intrinsic **orbit coordinate** on that fresh fiber. In this pointed orbit topology the generator pair tends to the bilateral shift `S` plus one rank-one projection `P_0`, and the algebra generated by that limiting pair is

\[
\boxed{
C^*(S,P_0)=C^*(S)+\mathcal K(\ell^2(\mathbb Z)),
\qquad C^*(S)\simeq C(\mathbb T).
}
\]

This is deliberately **not** a claim that the whole sequence of finite matrix algebras converges in norm, nor that the same local limit holds in the original Fourier-label packet topology of PC-239. In that original labeling the fresh component of `M_z` is a ballistic Bezout shift. The pointed orbit coordinate follows that source-forced shift instead. Its limiting compact extension splits through the actual bilateral unitary `S`, so every Fredholm element in this limiting algebra has index zero. The noncommutativity therefore survives only as a compact defect over the already-classified bilateral-shift symbol, not as a new Toeplitz/index mechanism.

This finding does **not** classify simultaneous `N,q -> infinity`, source-forced operators that mix distinct coarse fibers, exact-order projectors for a growing composite fresh conductor, unbounded/domain-sensitive limits, or global uniformization/accessory data.

## 1. Exact source-forced pair

Let

\[
H_m=L^2(\mu_m)
\]

with normalized counting measure. Fix `N>1` and an integer `q>1` with `(N,q)=1`. The canonical refinement pullback is

\[
J_{N,q}:H_N\to H_{Nq},
\qquad
(J_{N,q}f)(z)=f(z^q),
\tag{1}
\]

and the old-space projection is

\[
E_{N,q}=J_{N,q}J_{N,q}^*.
\tag{2}
\]

The second operator is the original coordinate of the refined polygon,

\[
Z_{N,q}=M_z.
\tag{3}
\]

It is intrinsic to the common-anchor geometry because `|1-z|` is the anchored chord. PC-239 classified regular functional calculus of this coordinate after its Bezout winding was resolved but explicitly left genuinely noncommuting old/new insertions outside its theorem.

Use the CRT isomorphism

\[
\mu_{Nq}\stackrel{\sim}{\longrightarrow}\mu_N\times\mu_q,
\qquad
z\longmapsto(x,y)=(z^q,z^N).
\tag{4}
\]

Choose integers `a,b` with

\[
aq+bN=1.
\tag{5}
\]

Then `z=x^a y^b`, so under `H_{Nq}\simeq H_N\otimes H_q`,

\[
\boxed{
Z_{N,q}=A\otimes B,
\qquad
A=M_{x^a},
\qquad
B=M_{y^b},
}
\tag{6}
\]

while

\[
\boxed{
E_{N,q}=I\otimes P,
\qquad
Ph=\langle\mathbf1,h\rangle\mathbf1.
}
\tag{7}
\]

Because `aq == 1 (mod N)` and `bN == 1 (mod q)`, `A` and `B` are full cyclic generators of their respective factors.

In the fresh Fourier basis `e_j(y)=y^j`, `B` translates by the unit `b modulo q` and `P=|e_0><e_0|`. Reindex the fresh basis by the **orbit of the marked vector under `B`**,

\[
f_j:=B^j e_0,
\qquad j\in\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z.
\tag{8}
\]

This is not an arbitrary spectral relabeling: it is determined by the ordered pair `(B,P)` itself, up to reversing the chosen generator. In this orbit basis,

\[
Bf_j=f_{j+1},
\qquad
P=|f_0\rangle\langle f_0|.
\tag{9}
\]

Write this cyclic shift as `S_q` below.

## 2. Noncommutativity survives in norm but has fixed fresh rank

From (6)--(9),

\[
[E_{N,q},Z_{N,q}]
=A\otimes[P,S_q].
\tag{10}
\]

On the fresh factor,

\[
[P,S_q]
=|f_0\rangle\langle f_{-1}|
-|f_1\rangle\langle f_0|.
\tag{11}
\]

The two partial matrix units have orthogonal initial and final spaces. Hence, for every `q>1`,

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{rank}[E_{N,q},Z_{N,q}]=2N,
\qquad
\|[E_{N,q},Z_{N,q}]\|=1,
}
\tag{12}
\]

and the nonzero singular values are exactly `1`, with multiplicity `2N`. Consequently

\[
\boxed{
\|[E_{N,q},Z_{N,q}]\|_{HS}^2=2N,
\qquad
\frac1{Nq}\operatorname{Tr}([E,Z]^*[E,Z])=\frac2q.
}
\tag{13}
\]

Thus norm noncommutativity does not disappear, but its normalized Hilbert-space density does. At fixed coarse conductor it occupies only two fresh orbit links per coarse block.

## 3. Exact finite algebra is a full fresh matrix fiber over each coarse point

The complete finite algebra can be recovered directly from the two generators. From (6),

\[
Z_{N,q}^q=A^q\otimes I.
\tag{14}
\]

Since `q` is a unit modulo `N`, choose `c` with `cq == 1 (mod N)`. Because `A^N=I`,

\[
\boxed{
A\otimes I=(Z_{N,q}^q)^c.
}
\tag{15}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
I\otimes B=(A^*\otimes I)Z_{N,q}
\in C^*(Z_{N,q},E_{N,q}).
}
\tag{16}
\]

The orbit basis then gives every fresh matrix unit,

\[
\boxed{
S_q^iPS_q^{-j}=|f_i\rangle\langle f_j|,
\qquad i,j\in\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z.
}
\tag{17}
\]

Hence

\[
C^*(B,P)=M_q(\mathbb C).
\tag{18}
\]

Since `A` has all `N` distinct `N`-th roots as its spectrum,

\[
C^*(A)\simeq C(\mu_N)\simeq\mathbb C^N.
\tag{19}
\]

Equations (15)--(19) prove

\[
\boxed{
C^*(Z_{N,q},E_{N,q})
=C^*(A)\otimes M_q(\mathbb C)
\simeq C(\mu_N)\otimes M_q(\mathbb C).
}
\tag{20}
\]

The center is exactly `C(mu_N) tensor I`. The pair therefore reconstructs every linear operator **within** each fresh `q`-fiber but cannot move between distinct coarse CRT points. This sharpens PC-225: retaining the terminal fiber before the left-unitary quotient really does recover maximal fiber noncommutativity, but that noncommutativity is the universal matrix algebra of one cyclic fiber rather than a mixed-conductor relation among coarse points.

Equation (20) is also a strong spectral-wrapper control. By allowing arbitrary `q`-dependent words and coefficients one can manufacture essentially arbitrary matrices, hence arbitrary finite spectra, inside each block. Such freedom is not evidence: a Prime-Circle spectral proposal must identify a particular element and normalization forced independently by the source geometry.

## 4. Coprime composite control

No step above uses primality. For every `m>1` with `(N,m)=1`, the same argument gives

\[
\boxed{
C^*(Z_{N,m},E_{N,m})
\simeq C(\mu_N)\otimes M_m(\mathbb C),
}
\tag{21}
\]

and

\[
\operatorname{rank}[E_{N,m},Z_{N,m}]=2N,
\qquad
\|[E_{N,m},Z_{N,m}]\|=1,
\qquad
\frac1{Nm}\|[E,Z]\|_{HS}^2=\frac2m.
\tag{22}
\]

Thus this algebraic mechanism sees the fresh degree as the size of one cyclic fiber and does not use its factorization. At a prime `q`, all nonzero fresh Fourier characters have exact order `q`; for composite `m` they split by proper divisors, but `(E,Z)` does not distinguish that exact-order decomposition. Reading primality from the externally supplied matrix size would merely read the refinement label already present in the construction.

This is a matched **architecture** control, not a claim that different-dimensional prime and composite fibers are unitarily identical. Adding an exact-order projector that distinguishes the divisor decomposition is outside (21) and requires a separate source/novelty test.

## 5. Pointed orbit limit is a split Laurent-plus-compact algebra

There are two different large-`q` topologies, and conflating them would give a false statement.

In the original fresh Fourier labeling, `B=M_{y^b}` is translation by the Bezout exponent `b=N^{-1} (mod q)`, which is typically of order `q`. This is precisely the ballistic phenomenon of PC-239: on a fixed local Fourier window the unitary has no strong limit and its fixed-window matrix coefficients vanish. PC-240 does **not** replace that conclusion.

However the noncommuting pair `(B,P)` carries its own pointed coordinate, namely the orbit basis (8). Following that source-forced orbit is the natural topology for asking what algebra the pair itself approaches as the cyclic fiber grows. Let `m -> infinity` through integers coprime to `N`, and identify bounded orbit indices `f_j^(m)` with the standard basis `e_j` of `ell^2(Z)`. Then on every finitely supported vector,

\[
S_m\to S,
\qquad
Se_j=e_{j+1},
\tag{23}
\]

while

\[
P_m\to P_0=|e_0\rangle\langle e_0|.
\tag{24}
\]

The C*-algebra generated by this **limiting pair** is

\[
\mathcal B:=C^*(S,P_0).
\tag{25}
\]

This statement concerns the generator-pair limit. It does not assert norm convergence of the full finite algebras `M_m`: arbitrary `m`-dependent elements of those algebras can encode arbitrary data and need not possess any canonical limit.

The limiting algebra is elementary to classify. First,

\[
S^iP_0S^{-j}=|e_i\rangle\langle e_j|,
\tag{26}
\]

so all compact operators `K=K(ell^2(Z))` lie in `B`. Conversely every algebraic word containing `P_0` has finite rank, while words with no `P_0` are Laurent polynomials in `S`. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal B=C^*(S)+\mathcal K.
}
\tag{27}
\]

The sum is norm closed: the quotient map to the Calkin algebra is injective on `C^*(S)` because

\[
C^*(S)\cap\mathcal K=\{0\}.
\tag{28}
\]

Indeed, if `f(S)` were compact, the basis vectors `e_j` converge weakly to zero while translation invariance gives

\[
\|f(S)e_j\|=\|f(S)e_0\|;
\tag{29}
\]

compactness therefore forces `f(S)=0`.

Since `S` is bilateral and unitary,

\[
C^*(S)\simeq C(\mathbb T).
\tag{30}
\]

Hence the fresh pointed limit fits into

\[
\boxed{
0\longrightarrow\mathcal K
\longrightarrow\mathcal B
\longrightarrow C(\mathbb T)
\longrightarrow0,
}
\tag{31}
\]

and this extension splits by the actual *-homomorphism

\[
f\longmapsto f(S).
\tag{32}
\]

Including the fixed coarse center gives `C(mu_N) tensor B`. The noncommutativity is therefore a compact pointed defect over the ordinary bilateral Laurent symbol, not a second bulk spectral coordinate.

## 6. No Toeplitz/Fredholm index is forced

Because (31) resembles the classical Toeplitz extension, the index question must be checked rather than inferred by analogy. Let

\[
T=f(S)+K\in\mathcal B,
\qquad f\in C(\mathbb T),
\qquad K\in\mathcal K.
\tag{33}
\]

By Atkinson's theorem, `T` is Fredholm exactly when its Calkin image is invertible, equivalently when `f` is nowhere zero on `T`. Then `f(S)` itself is invertible, so `T` is a compact perturbation of an invertible operator. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
T\text{ Fredholm}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\operatorname{ind}T=0.
}
\tag{34}
\]

Thus the source-forced pointed limit does not secretly generate the nontrivial winding-index mechanism of a unilateral Toeplitz operator. A nonzero index would require an additional one-sided boundary/compression or another non-split extension, and that extra structure is not forced by `(E_{N,q},M_z)`.

For prior-art orientation, the unilateral comparison belongs to classical Toeplitz/C*-algebra theory: L. A. Coburn, **The C*-algebra generated by an isometry**, *Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society* 73 (1967), 722--726, DOI `10.1090/S0002-9904-1967-11845-7`. The bilateral-shift, matrix-unit, compact-ideal and Atkinson ingredients are standard operator theory. No novelty is claimed for those abstract facts. The project-specific content is their exact realization by this source-forced Prime-Circle pair and the resulting boundary on the post-PC-239 escape.

## 7. Relation to the current Prime-Circle frontier

PC-224 and PC-225 showed that refinement/mask networks built from pullbacks, diagonal masks and conditional expectations remain modules over the coarse diagonal algebra, so final `T^*T` data scalarize fiberwise. They left open retaining an intrinsic terminal-fiber operator before quotienting by left unitaries. Equation (20) answers the simplest canonical version: the internal fiber becomes fully noncommutative, but only as an independent `M_q` over each coarse point.

PC-238 showed that the two quotient coordinates generate a commutative CRT cylinder. PC-239 then showed that the original coordinate carries a ballistic Bezout winding which, when its moving channels are tracked, returns to bilateral-shift calculus. PC-240 inserts the old/new projector **before** reducing to multiplication functional calculus. The commutator is genuinely nonzero, so this is not another commutative-cylinder statement; nevertheless its exact finite algebra is universal and its pointed orbit limit is only the split compact extension (31).

Accordingly this closes the fixed-base route

\[
\text{canonical old/new projection}
+\text{ original coordinate}
\longrightarrow
\text{noncommuting algebra alone}
\longrightarrow
\text{new RH spectral/index mechanism}
\tag{35}
\]

when the proposed novelty comes solely from the algebra generated by those two operators. No zeta divisor, gamma factor, functional-equation involution, or distinguished real part `1/2` is produced.

## Research consequence

Noncommutativity alone is no longer a sufficient frontier criterion at fixed coarse conductor. `(E_{N,q},M_z)` is strongly noncommuting and generates every fresh matrix fiber, yet all coarse fibers remain central and independent. Following the pair's own marked orbit as the fresh degree grows produces only a split Laurent-plus-compact algebra with zero Fredholm index, while the original Fourier packet topology remains subject to the ballistic collapse already established in PC-239.

A viable next operator test must therefore add structure not generated by this pair: a source-forced map that couples distinct coarse CRT fibers, a simultaneous `N,q -> infinity` regime in which the coarse center itself grows and a nonclassical coupling survives quantitative controls, an exact-order operation whose composite-conductor behavior is essential rather than merely the old/new complement, or a genuinely unbounded/domain-sensitive/global construction. Any proposed spectral invariant should also be tested against the full-matrix freedom in (20): if the desired spectrum appears only after choosing level-dependent coefficients inside `M_q`, it is engineered rather than extracted from Prime-Circle geometry.
