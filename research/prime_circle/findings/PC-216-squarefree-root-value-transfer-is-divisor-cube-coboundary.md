# PC-216 — squarefree root-value transfer is an exact divisor-cube coboundary

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED` + `LITERATURE+DERIVED` + `PRIOR-ART-CLASSICALIZATION` + `DECISIVE-BOUNDARY` for the source-coefficient transfer/holonomy continuation left open by PC-215: retain the actual nonzero cyclotomic root values in every proper-divisor interaction sector, rather than only the growing support locality, and ask whether their coherent change under omitted-prime refinement carries a nontrivial phase, cocycle, or eigenvalue invariant.

It does not at the first canonical transfer level. Let

\[
N=dM
\]

be squarefree with `(d,M)=1`, let `a` be a unit modulo `d`, and put

\[
\zeta_a=e^{-2\pi i a/d},
\qquad
\eta_d(a):=\zeta_a\Phi_d'(\zeta_a)
\qquad(d>1).
\tag{1}
\]

For a prime `q` coprime to `d`, define the multiplicative finite-difference operator

\[
(\Delta_q f)(a):=\frac{f(qa)}{f(a)}.
\tag{2}
\]

The operators `Delta_q` commute. If `M` is prime, then exactly

\[
\boxed{
\Phi_{dM}(\zeta_a)=M\,(\Delta_M\eta_d)(a).
}
\tag{3}
\]

If `M` has at least two prime factors, then exactly

\[
\boxed{
\Phi_{dM}(\zeta_a)
=
\left(\prod_{q\mid M}\Delta_q\right)\eta_d(a).
}
\tag{4}
\]

For `d=1`, equations (3)--(4) extend with the convention `eta_1=1`, giving the classical values `Phi_p(1)=p` and `Phi_M(1)=1` for squarefree `omega(M)>=2`.

Thus every proper-divisor Fourier coefficient that drives the squarefree mixed-mask compression of PC-215 is an **exact multiplicative finite difference of one lower-conductor derivative potential**. In particular, all prime-ordering curvature vanishes: the coefficient cube is generated by commuting coboundaries. The corresponding canonical weighted permutation along any omitted-prime action is diagonally similar to the unweighted residue permutation, so its eigenvalues and multiplicative holonomy contain no cyclotomic information beyond the elementary prime scale in the one-prime-complement case and the modular order of the permutation.

This does **not** collapse the full PC-215 operator `T_N`: different change conductors `d` have different potentials `eta_d`, and diagonal similarity is not unitary in the canonical Haar metric. Singular values, norms, pseudospectra, and the collective assembly of several conductor sectors remain outside this result. What is closed is the tempting route from the source-forced high-body coefficient phases themselves to a new refinement cocycle/holonomy or eigenvalue mechanism.

## 1. The one-prime complement is a first coboundary

The elementary cyclotomic recursion for a prime `q` not dividing `m` is

\[
\boxed{
\Phi_{mq}(x)=\frac{\Phi_m(x^q)}{\Phi_m(x)}.
}
\tag{5}
\]

Take `m=d` and let `x` tend to the primitive `d`-th root `zeta_a`. Both numerator and denominator in (5) vanish simply. L'Hospital's rule gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
\Phi_{dq}(\zeta_a)
&=q\zeta_a^{q-1}
\frac{\Phi_d'(\zeta_a^q)}{\Phi_d'(\zeta_a)}\\
&=q
\frac{\zeta_a^q\Phi_d'(\zeta_a^q)}
{\zeta_a\Phi_d'(\zeta_a)}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{6}
\]

Since `zeta_a^q=zeta_{qa}`, equation (1) turns (6) into

\[
\boxed{
\Phi_{dq}(\zeta_a)
=q\frac{\eta_d(qa)}{\eta_d(a)}
=q(\Delta_q\eta_d)(a).
}
\tag{7}
\]

No branch of a logarithm has been chosen. The statement is an identity in the cyclotomic field, and `eta_d(a)` is nonzero because all roots of `Phi_d` are simple.

Equation (7) already sharpens the scalar resultant statement relevant to PC-215. Multiplication by `q` permutes the units modulo `d`, hence

\[
\prod_{a\in(\mathbb Z/d\mathbb Z)^\times}
\frac{\eta_d(qa)}{\eta_d(a)}=1.
\tag{8}
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\prod_{(a,d)=1}\Phi_{dq}(\zeta_a)
=q^{\varphi(d)}.
}
\tag{9}
\]

This is precisely the prime-ratio case of the classical cyclotomic resultant law, but (7) is stronger for the present operator question: it exhibits the entire pointwise coefficient packet as a normalized coboundary before the product is taken.

## 2. Every further omitted prime is another commuting finite difference

Now let

\[
M=q_1q_2\cdots q_t,
\qquad t\ge2,
\tag{10}
\]

with distinct primes, all coprime to `d`. Suppose first that `M'=q_1\cdots q_{t-1}>1` and put `q=q_t`. The primitive `d`-th root `zeta_a` is **not** a zero of `Phi_{dM'}`, because its exact order is `d<dM'`. Applying (5) with `m=dM'` therefore needs no limiting argument:

\[
\Phi_{dM'q}(\zeta_a)
=
\frac{\Phi_{dM'}(\zeta_a^q)}
{\Phi_{dM'}(\zeta_a)}
=(\Delta_q F_{M'})(a),
\tag{11}
\]

where

\[
F_{M'}(a):=\Phi_{dM'}(\zeta_a).
\tag{12}
\]

Starting from (7), the first additional prime kills the scalar factor `q_1`, and induction yields

\[
\boxed{
F_M
=\Delta_{q_t}\cdots\Delta_{q_1}\eta_d
\qquad(t\ge2).
}
\tag{13}
\]

Because multiplication by distinct primes commutes modulo `d`, the finite-difference operators commute exactly:

\[
\boxed{
\Delta_p\Delta_q f
=\Delta_q\Delta_p f.
}
\tag{14}
\]

Thus equation (13) is independent of the order in which the complementary primes are introduced. Explicitly,

\[
\boxed{
\Phi_{dM}(\zeta_a)
=
\prod_{S\subseteq\{q:q\mid M\}}
\eta_d\!\left(a\prod_{q\in S}q\right)^{(-1)^{t-|S|}}
\qquad(t\ge2).
}
\tag{15}
\]

Equation (15) is the exact multiplicative analogue of a Boolean-cube mixed finite difference. There is no residual two-prime or higher curvature hidden in the ordering of the source coefficients: every face is generated from one potential `eta_d`.

As an immediate check, for `M=pq`,

\[
\boxed{
\Phi_{dpq}(\zeta_a)
=
\frac{\eta_d(pqa)\eta_d(a)}
{\eta_d(pa)\eta_d(qa)}.
}
\tag{16}
\]

This is symmetric in `p,q` before any trace, determinant, or modulus is taken.

## 3. The induced omitted-prime transport has pure-gauge spectrum

The coefficient packet has a natural finite transport associated with the same residue action that generated it. Fix a prime `q|M` and let

\[
R_q e_a=e_{qa}
\tag{17}
\]

on `C^{U(d)}`, where `U(d)=(Z/dZ)^times`.

If `M=q`, normalize the packet by its forced scalar `q` and set

\[
w_q(a):=\frac{\Phi_{dq}(\zeta_a)}q
=\frac{\eta_d(qa)}{\eta_d(a)}.
\tag{18}
\]

If `omega(M)>=2`, put

\[
F_{M/q}:=\prod_{r\mid M/q}\Delta_r\eta_d,
\tag{19}
\]

so that (13) gives

\[
\Phi_{dM}(\zeta_a)
=
\frac{F_{M/q}(qa)}{F_{M/q}(a)}.
\tag{20}
\]

In either case the relevant normalized weight has the form

\[
w(a)=\frac{F(qa)}{F(a)}
\tag{21}
\]

for an explicit nowhere-zero `F`. Let `D_F` be the diagonal matrix with entries `F(a)`. The weighted shift

\[
W_{q,F}e_a=w(a)e_{qa}
\tag{22}
\]

then satisfies the exact similarity

\[
\boxed{
W_{q,F}=D_FR_qD_F^{-1}.
}
\tag{23}
\]

Hence the source-derived coefficient phases produce no new eigenvalue spectrum. If

\[
L=\operatorname{ord}_d(q),
\tag{24}
\]

then every orbit of multiplication by `q` on `U(d)` has length `L`, and

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Spec}(W_{q,F})
=\{e^{2\pi i j/L}:0\le j<L\},
}
\tag{25}
\]

with every eigenvalue repeated `varphi(d)/L` times. In the one-prime-complement case, the unnormalized weights `Phi_{dq}(zeta_a)` simply multiply this spectrum by `q`.

Equivalently, the multiplicative holonomy around every `q`-orbit is trivial after that forced normalization:

\[
\boxed{
\prod_{j=0}^{L-1}w(q^ja)=1.
}
\tag{26}
\]

For `omega(M)>=2`, taking products of (20) over all units also gives

\[
\boxed{
\prod_{(a,d)=1}\Phi_{dM}(\zeta_a)=1.
}
\tag{27}
\]

Together, (9) and (27) recover the squarefree cases of Apostol's classical cyclotomic resultant theorem: a prime quotient contributes the corresponding prime power, while a quotient with at least two distinct primes contributes unit resultant. The operator-level lesson is that the same classicalization already occurs pointwise as a coboundary, not only after determinant/product contraction.

## 4. Relation to the PC-212--PC-215 mixed-mask branch

PC-212 identifies the Fourier coefficient of the normalized coefficient field `g_N` at an exact-order `d` character as

\[
\widehat g_N(a/d)
=
\frac{\Phi_N(e^{-2\pi ia/d})}{\sqrt{Nh_N}},
\qquad d\mid N,
\tag{28}
\]

and shows that the own exact-order `N` packet vanishes. PC-214 then proves that for `N=pq` the direct compression to `E_N` is a binary rook/Kronecker-sum operator. PC-215 shows that this bounded binary locality does not persist: for squarefree `N` with `omega(N)=k`, the minimal CRT interaction locality of

\[
T_N=P_NM_{g_N}P_N\big|_{E_N}
\tag{29}
\]

is exactly `k-1`.

The nonzero matrix entry associated with a CRT change set `S` has reduced conductor

\[
d=\prod_{p\in S}p,
\qquad
M=N/d,
\tag{30}
\]

and its source coefficient is exactly the root value on the left of (3) or (4), divided by `sqrt(Nh_N)`. Therefore PC-215's entire squarefree coefficient table admits the divisor-cube representation (3)--(4).

This materially narrows the interpretation of the growing locality theorem. The support complexity is genuine: no bounded-valence tensor decomposition can reproduce all nonzero change patterns. But the **coherent source weights on each fixed proper-divisor packet do not carry independent refinement curvature**. They are finite differences of the lower-conductor derivative potential `eta_d`.

In particular, the first natural attempt suggested by PC-215's surviving frontier,

\[
\text{growing mixed-composite sectors}
\longrightarrow
\text{source coefficient phase/transfer cocycle}
\longrightarrow
\text{new spectral holonomy},
\tag{31}

is closed by (23)--(26). The eigenvalue data of that transfer are only the ordinary multiplicative order of `q mod d`, with the forced scalar `q` when the complement consists of one prime.

## 5. Prior art and novelty audit

The arithmetic ingredients are classical. The recursion (5) is a standard cyclotomic identity. R. P. Kurshan and A. M. Odlyzko, **Values of cyclotomic polynomials at roots of unity**, *Mathematica Scandinavica* 49 (1981), 15--35, DOI `10.7146/math.scand.a-11919`, systematically study cyclotomic values at roots of unity. B. Bzdęga, A. Herrera-Poyatos and P. Moree, **Cyclotomic polynomials at roots of unity**, *Acta Arithmetica* 184 (2018), 215--230, DOI `10.4064/aa170112-20-12`, give finite-Fourier formulas for these values and explicitly connect them with cyclotomic resultants. These are already the direct prior-art boundary recorded in PC-215.

T. M. Apostol, **Resultants of cyclotomic polynomials**, *Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society* 24 (1970), 457--462, is the classical resultant source already anchored in `SOURCES.md`; equations (9) and (27) are exactly its squarefree quotient cases.

The language of a multiplicative coboundary and diagonal similarity is standard algebra rather than a novelty claim. A directed search for the combination of cyclotomic root values with `coboundary`, `cocycle`, and weighted-permutation language did not locate this exact PC-215 operator organization, but absence of that wording is not evidence of novelty. The pointwise identities follow elementarily from the classical recursion, and the scalar products reproduce classical resultants.

The durable contribution is therefore project-local: **the apparently new high-body coefficient carrier of PC-215 has zero source-forced refinement holonomy at the level of its natural omitted-prime transfer**. This is a structural no-go inside the Prime-Circle operator program, not a claim of new cyclotomic number theory.

## 6. Adversarial controls and exact boundary

Several limits on the conclusion are essential.

First, equation (23) is a diagonal **similarity**, not a unitary conjugacy in the canonical counting/Haar inner product. The modulus profile `|F(qa)/F(a)|` can therefore survive in singular values, condition numbers, pseudospectra, or metric quantities. Replacing similarity by unitary equivalence would be false in general.

Second, the theorem is packetwise in the reduced conductor `d`. The complete matrix `T_N` sums interaction sectors with different `d`, hence different derivative potentials `eta_d`. There is no single diagonal gauge exhibited here that simultaneously turns all of `T_N` into a universal operator. PC-215's exact locality `omega(N)-1` remains valid.

Third, the coboundary statement uses squarefree complement primes. Repeated prime powers require confluent/valuation versions of the cyclotomic recursion and are not silently covered by (4).

Fourth, modular-order spectra are themselves classical finite-group data. Their appearance does not create an RH bridge. No spectral parameter `s`, gamma factor, `s<->1-s` symmetry, critical line, or explicit-formula zero term is produced by (25).

Finally, the matched-control test is severe: for any nowhere-zero function `F` on `U(d)`, the weights `F(qa)/F(a)` produce exactly the same eigenvalue spectrum (25). Thus the pure-gauge spectral response cannot certify that `F` arose from cyclotomic geometry. Arithmetic can survive only in metric data of the specific `F`, in the simultaneous assembly of several conductor packets, or in a different source-forced operation not reducible to one omitted-prime weighted shift.

## 7. Consequence for the research frontier

PC-215 remains a genuine incidence theorem: squarefree mixed-composite masks force interaction order growing as `omega(N)-1`. PC-216 shows that **growing incidence alone does not generate growing holonomy**. The actual cyclotomic coefficient on every proper-divisor packet is already an exact divisor-cube finite difference, and its canonical prime-action transfer is pure gauge.

A useful continuation must therefore avoid scalarizing the coefficient system into orbit products or eigenvalues of one weighted residue permutation. The live part of this branch is narrower: test unitary/metric invariants that survive diagonal similarity, or retain several conductor sectors simultaneously and ask whether their incompatible gauges produce a source-specific collective invariant. Any such invariant must still beat a conductor-matched non-arithmetic control and connect to the zeta target without inserting a Mellin/Dirichlet weight or spectral parameter by hand.
