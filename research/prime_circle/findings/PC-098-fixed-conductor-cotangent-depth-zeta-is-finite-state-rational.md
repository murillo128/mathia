# PC-098 — fixed-conductor cotangent depth zeta is finite-state rational

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED` + `CLASSICAL-IDENTITY` + `STRUCTURAL-COLLAPSE` + `DECISIVE-BOUNDARY` for analytically controlled infinite-depth constructions obtained by repeatedly composing the canonical zero-diagonal Prime-Circle cotangent transfer on a **fixed finite union of exact-order shells** (the common anchor may be included as `P_1^*={1}`), together with fixed shell projectors and shell-local scalar multipliers. At fixed conductor support the generated operator algebra is finite-dimensional and already closed; Cayley–Hamilton makes every repeated-depth matrix element linearly recurrent, every depth generating function rational, and the natural all-loop exponential exactly the reciprocal of a finite characteristic determinant.

This closes the most direct fixed-support version of the `infinite-depth/limit` escape left after PC-097. It does **not** cover limits in which the set of shell orders or total state dimension grows without bound, singular renormalizations that do not converge in a fixed finite matrix space, genuinely different intrinsic operators/tensors, global uniformization/monodromy, or cross-level organizations whose state space itself grows with refinement.

## 1. Fixed shell support is a finite state space

Let `Nset` be a finite set of exact orders and put

\[
X=\bigsqcup_{n\in Nset}P_n^*,
\qquad
M=|X|<\infty.
\]

If the common anchor is to remain active, include `1` in `Nset`, since `P_1^*={1}`. Different exact-order shells are disjoint.

On distinct points of `X` use the intrinsic cotangent kernel

\[
K(z,u)=i\cot\!\left(\frac{\theta_z-\theta_u}{2}\right)
       =\frac{z+u}{u-z},
\]

with the canonical zero diagonal `K(z,z)=0`. Let `P_n` denote the exact-shell coordinate projector. Allow also any fixed collection of shell-local diagonal multiplication operators required by the construction; allowing the whole diagonal algebra on `X` only strengthens the conclusion.

Let

\[
\mathcal A_X
=\operatorname{alg}_{\mathbb C}
\bigl(I,\,P_m K P_n,\,D_f\bigr)
\subseteq M_M(\mathbb C)
\]

be the unital algebra generated by these intrinsic finite-support operations. Then

\[
\boxed{\dim_{\mathbb C}\mathcal A_X\le M^2.}
\]

In particular `\mathcal A_X` is a finite-dimensional linear subspace of `M_M(C)`, hence is closed in every matrix norm. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\text{every norm-convergent infinite linear combination or limit of finite operator words remains in }\mathcal A_X.
}
\]

Infinite depth does not create a new operator space while the conductor support is fixed. A divergent word sequence is not an operator limit; any renormalized sequence that actually converges in the same finite matrix space again lands in `\mathcal A_X`.

## 2. Repeated depth satisfies an exact finite recurrence

Take any transfer operator `T in A_X`. Let its minimal polynomial be

\[
\mu_T(\lambda)
=\lambda^d+a_{d-1}\lambda^{d-1}+\cdots+a_0,
\qquad d\le M.
\]

Cayley–Hamilton gives `\mu_T(T)=0`, so for every `r>=0`,

\[
\boxed{
T^{r+d}
=-\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}a_jT^{r+j}.
}
\]

Consequently every endpoint-to-endpoint or anchored depth sequence

\[
a_r=\langle u,T^r v\rangle
\]

obeys the same constant-coefficient recurrence of order at most `M`. Its depth generating function is therefore rational:

\[
\boxed{
G_{u,v}(q)
=\sum_{r\ge0}q^r\langle u,T^r v\rangle
=\langle u,(I-qT)^{-1}v\rangle.
}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
G_{u,v}(q)=\frac{P_{u,v}(q)}{\det(I-qT)},
\]

with cancellation possible; the reduced denominator divides the reversed minimal polynomial. The same conclusion holds for a periodic finite sequence of shell transfers after replacing `T` by the finite monodromy product for one period.

More generally, if `f` is holomorphic on a neighborhood of `spec(T)`, finite-dimensional holomorphic functional calculus gives

\[
\boxed{f(T)=p_f(T)}
\]

for a polynomial `p_f` of degree `<d`, obtained by Hermite interpolation on the eigenvalues with the Jordan multiplicities prescribed by `\mu_T`. Thus exponentials, resolvents, logarithms on an admissible branch, and other analytic resummations of one fixed transfer do not add an independent operator direction.

## 3. The natural all-loop “zeta” is exactly a finite determinant

The strongest fixed-support repair is to sum closed cotangent paths of **all** depths rather than inspect one finite word. For `|q|` sufficiently small,

\[
-\log\det(I-qT)
=\sum_{r\ge1}\frac{q^r}{r}\operatorname{tr}(T^r).
\]

Hence the natural loop exponential is

\[
\boxed{
Z_T(q)
:=\exp\!\left(
\sum_{r\ge1}\frac{q^r}{r}\operatorname{tr}(T^r)
\right)
=\frac1{\det(I-qT)}.
}
\]

The identity is also formal around `q=0`; analytically, continuation is just the same rational function. Therefore an infinite sum over every closed depth is not an infinite-dimensional spectral object. It is the reciprocal of one polynomial of degree at most `M`.

This directly blocks an apparent zeta-like escape. If one writes `q=e^{-s}`, the singular set of `Z_T(e^{-s})` is, up to logarithm branches,

\[
\boxed{
s=\Log\lambda_j+2\pi i k,
\qquad
\lambda_j\in\operatorname{spec}(T)\setminus\{0\},\ k\in\mathbb Z,
}
\]

so it is a finite union of vertical arithmetic progressions determined by the finite spectrum. No gamma factor, intrinsic `s<->1-s` symmetry, or Riemann-zero divisor is produced by depth resummation itself. Choosing a different map from `s` to `q` merely wraps the same finite determinant unless that map brings additional arithmetic structure from outside the fixed Prime-Circle transfer.

## 4. PC-097 prevents the determinant from restoring hidden shell memory

The finite-dimensional argument alone says that fixed support cannot create an infinite operator algebra. PC-097 gives the complementary Prime-Circle statement about the **coefficients**.

Every matrix element of a finite word in shell-projected cotangent blocks is a finite canonical cotangent network with exposed endpoints. PC-097 reduces arbitrary finite networks — including repeated shells, collisions, cycles, and parallel edges — to collision-stratified endpoint algebra built from one-body ordinary or diagonal-punctured Cauchy jets. Thus every coefficient `tr(T^r)` already belongs to the finite-network endpoint class.

The determinant does not evade that theorem. Expanding `det(I-qT)` is a finite sum over permutations/cycle covers of the finite state set `X`, hence a finite combination of the same cotangent-network data. The infinite-looking loop exponential merely reorganizes this finite polynomial:

\[
\boxed{
\text{all finite-depth loop traces}
\longrightarrow
\det(I-qT)^{-1}
\longrightarrow
\text{finite endpoint/cyclotomic data at fixed support}.
}
\]

So an irreducible hidden-shell tensor that was absent from every finite canonical network cannot reappear solely by summing the depth to infinity while the state space remains fixed.

## 5. Small exact check: the primitive third shell

Let `X=P_3^*={zeta_3,zeta_3^2}`. In that ordered basis,

\[
K=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&-i/\sqrt3\\
i/\sqrt3&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\operatorname{spec}(K)=\left\{-\frac1{\sqrt3},\frac1{\sqrt3}\right\}.
\]

Hence

\[
\operatorname{tr}(K^{2m+1})=0,
\qquad
\operatorname{tr}(K^{2m})=2\,3^{-m},
\]

and the infinite loop series collapses exactly to

\[
\boxed{
Z_K(q)
=\exp\!\left(\sum_{r\ge1}\frac{q^r}{r}\operatorname{tr}(K^r)\right)
=\frac1{1-q^2/3}.
}
\]

The example is deliberately minimal: infinitely many loop lengths produce only the two finite transfer eigenvalues.

## 6. Matched controls and novelty audit

Nothing in Sections 1–3 uses primality, roots of unity, or even the cotangent formula. The same finite-state closure and trace-determinant identity hold for an arbitrary finite point set with any fixed finite transfer matrix. Prime-Circle arithmetic can affect the entries and finite spectrum of `T`; it does not alter the resummation mechanism.

No historical novelty is claimed for the algebraic ingredients. Cayley–Hamilton, finite-dimensional closure, rational transfer-matrix generating functions, and the trace/log-determinant identity are classical linear algebra. The Prime-Circle-specific content is the **research boundary** obtained by combining those facts with PC-097: a fixed finite set of canonical cotangent shells cannot escape finite endpoint compression merely by passing from finite words to norm-convergent infinite depth or to an all-loop determinant/zeta wrapper.

A directed prior-art check across finite-state transfer matrices, rational/recognizable series, and finite graph walk-zeta determinant formulas found the same classical finite-state mechanism rather than a Prime-Circle-specific analytic structure. This is therefore a decisive narrowing result, not a novelty claim.

## 7. Stress tests and falsification controls

1. For any proposed fixed-support transfer `T`, compute its minimal polynomial. Every scalar depth sequence `u^*T^r v` must satisfy the resulting finite recurrence. Failure would refute the finite-state claim.
2. Compare the direct partial sums `sum_{r<=R} q^r T^r` with `(I-qT)^{-1}` inside the convergence disk. Their difference must tend to zero in matrix norm.
3. Compare the loop coefficients obtained from `tr(T^r)` with the Taylor expansion of `-log det(I-qT)`. Any mismatch refutes Section 3.
4. Expand `det(I-qT)` by permutations. Every term is a finite cycle-cover network and must fall under the PC-097 finite-network reduction when `T` is built from the canonical cotangent blocks and shell-local scalar weights.
5. Replace every primitive shell by arbitrary matched finite point sets and repeat the same transfer construction. The finite-state/rational resummation mechanism must remain unchanged.
6. A construction whose shell set or total state dimension grows with depth is **not** a counterexample; it lies outside the fixed-conductor hypothesis. Likewise a singular scaling that has no limit in the fixed matrix space must be analyzed as a separate infinite-dimensional or renormalized problem rather than called a fixed-support operator limit.

## 8. Remaining frontier

PC-097 closes every **fixed finite canonical-cotangent network**. The present result closes the immediate analytic repair in which one keeps a fixed finite shell support and tries to regain information by summing arbitrarily long serial/closed cotangent paths, taking resolvents, or forming the corresponding loop determinant.

A genuine infinite-depth escape must therefore cross at least one precise boundary:

- the conductor/shell support and hence the state dimension grows without bound under refinement;
- the relevant limit is singular and requires a geometry-forced renormalization not equivalent to a fixed finite matrix functional calculus;
- a genuinely different intrinsic operator or higher tensor replaces the canonical cotangent transfer;
- or cross-level endpoint jets/profiles are organized in an infinite state space that cannot be represented by one fixed finite collection of shell coordinates.

That is the remaining `infinite-depth/limit` content of the accepted preimage-tube clue. Merely resumming depth at fixed conductor is no longer an open escape hatch.
