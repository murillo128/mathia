# PC-099 — growing complete cotangent-tube spectra converge to finite uniform bands

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED` + `LITERATURE+DERIVED` + `DECISIVE-NEGATIVE` for the canonical **growing-state complete-preimage-tube limit** of the oriented Prime-Circle cotangent operator classified at finite refinement depth by PC-053. Fix a base primitive shell `P_d^*`, let the power-map refinement multiplier `m -> infinity`, keep the entire complete preimage tube `Y_{d,m}`, and use the geometry-forced linear normalization by `1/m`. Then the empirical spectrum does not develop a new infinite arithmetic divisor: it converges explicitly to a finite mixture of uniform real bands, one band for each eigenvalue of the fixed base matrix `A_d=H_d+J_d`. Its moments and Stieltjes transform are determined by finite base spectral data.

This closes the most direct growing-conductor/state-dimension escape left by PC-098 for the canonical complete cotangent preimage tube. It does **not** cover incomplete conductor selections, a different intrinsic operator or higher tensor, singular renormalizations not equivalent to the natural linear cotangent scale, nonlinear mixing after sector decomposition, cross-level endpoint-profile constructions with genuinely growing state, or global uniformization/monodromy.

## 1. Exact finite-refinement spectrum after the natural linear scaling

Fix `d>1` and write

\[
r=\varphi(d).
\]

Let `H_d` be the primitive-shell restriction of the intrinsic oriented cotangent matrix and put

\[
A_d=H_d+J_d.
\]

Because `H_d` is Hermitian and `J_d` is Hermitian, `A_d` has real eigenvalues

\[
\lambda_1,\ldots,\lambda_r
\]

counted with multiplicity.

For every integer `m>=1`, PC-053 considers the complete power-map preimage tube

\[
Y_{d,m}=\{z\in S^1:z^m\in P_d^*\}
\]

and the augmented cotangent operator

\[
\mathcal A_{d,m}=C_{d,m}+J_{Y_{d,m}}.
\]

Its exact fiber-Fourier decomposition is

\[
\mathcal A_{d,m}
\cong
\bigoplus_{j=0}^{m-1}
\left(mD_jA_dD_j^{-1}-2jI\right).
\]

Hence, after the intrinsic linear normalization

\[
\widehat{\mathcal A}_{d,m}=\frac1m\mathcal A_{d,m},
\]

one has the **exact** spectrum

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Spec}(\widehat{\mathcal A}_{d,m})
=
\left\{
\lambda_\alpha-\frac{2j}{m}:
1\le\alpha\le r,\ 0\le j<m
\right\}.
}
\]

Thus increasing `m` does not create new sector eigenvalues. It fills, with mesh `2/m`, the same finite collection of affine ladders generated by the fixed base spectrum.

The normalization by `1/m` is not inserted to manufacture a limit. The unscaled PC-053 blocks are exactly `mD_jA_dD_j^{-1}-2jI`, so both the base term and the full fiber excursion are of order `m`. Dividing by `m` is the unique scalar normalization that keeps both contributions on a finite nonzero scale without introducing additional arithmetic weights.

## 2. The empirical spectrum converges to a finite mixture of uniform bands

Define the normalized empirical spectral measure

\[
\mu^+_{d,m}
=
\frac1{rm}
\sum_{\alpha=1}^{r}
\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}
\delta_{\lambda_\alpha-2j/m}.
\]

For every bounded continuous test function `f`,

\[
\int f\,d\mu^+_{d,m}
=
\frac1r\sum_{\alpha=1}^{r}
\frac1m\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}
f\!\left(\lambda_\alpha-\frac{2j}{m}\right).
\]

For each fixed `alpha`, the inner expression is the left Riemann sum for `t -> f(lambda_alpha-2t)` on `[0,1]`. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\mu^+_{d,m}\Longrightarrow\mu_d
\qquad(m\to\infty),
}
\]

where

\[
\boxed{
\mu_d(dx)
=
\frac1{2r}
\sum_{\alpha=1}^{r}
\mathbf 1_{[\lambda_\alpha-2,\lambda_\alpha]}(x)\,dx.
}
\]

Overlapping intervals simply add their densities. The limiting measure is compactly supported, absolutely continuous, piecewise constant, and determined entirely by the finite matrix `A_d`.

The convergence is quantitative for Lipschitz probes. If `Lip(f)` denotes the Lipschitz constant of `f`, then on each Riemann cell the function `t -> f(lambda_alpha-2t)` has Lipschitz constant at most `2 Lip(f)`. Summing the elementary cell error gives

\[
\boxed{
\left|
\int f\,d\mu^+_{d,m}
-
\int f\,d\mu_d
\right|
\le
\frac{\operatorname{Lip}(f)}{m}.
}
\]

So there is no slow or irregular spectral emergence hidden in the refinement variable: the complete-tube empirical law approaches its finite-band limit at the ordinary Riemann-sum rate.

## 3. Every limiting moment is finite base trace data

For every nonnegative integer `k`, direct integration of the limiting bands gives

\[
\boxed{
M_k(d)
:=
\int x^k\,d\mu_d(x)
=
\frac1{2r(k+1)}
\sum_{\alpha=1}^{r}
\left[
\lambda_\alpha^{k+1}
-(\lambda_\alpha-2)^{k+1}
\right].
}
\]

Expanding the second power shows that `M_k(d)` is a finite polynomial combination of

\[
\operatorname{tr}(A_d^0),
\operatorname{tr}(A_d^1),
\ldots,
\operatorname{tr}(A_d^k).
\]

Thus passing to infinitely many lift sectors does not create an independent sequence of spectral invariants. The entire limiting moment hierarchy is generated by the finite base characteristic polynomial.

This is a stronger obstruction than merely saying that each finite `m` is algebraic. A priori a sequence of increasing finite matrices can converge to a genuinely new spectral law. Here the increasing dimension is explicit enough that the limiting law itself remains a finite convolutional thickening of fixed base data.

## 4. The normalized determinant has only the finite-band Stieltjes limit

Let

\[
D_{d,m}(z)
=
\det\!\left(zI-\widehat{\mathcal A}_{d,m}\right).
\]

Away from the spectrum,

\[
\frac1{rm}\frac{D_{d,m}'(z)}{D_{d,m}(z)}
=
\int\frac{d\mu^+_{d,m}(x)}{z-x}.
\]

The same Riemann-sum argument gives locally uniform convergence on compact subsets of `C\setminus supp(mu_d)` to

\[
\boxed{
G_d(z)
=
\int\frac{d\mu_d(x)}{z-x}
=
\frac1{2r}
\sum_{\alpha=1}^{r}
\log\!\left(
\frac{z-\lambda_\alpha+2}{z-\lambda_\alpha}
\right),
}
\]

with compatible logarithm branches on each simply connected component of the complement of the bands.

Equivalently, the branch-independent statement is the Stieltjes integral itself. The limiting singular set is the finite union

\[
\boxed{
\bigcup_{\alpha=1}^{r}
[\lambda_\alpha-2,\lambda_\alpha]
\subset\mathbb R.
}
\]

At finite `m`, the zeros are exactly equally spaced meshes in these intervals after scaling. In the limit they condense onto real bands; they do not approach an isolated nontrivial-zeta zero set, and no distinguished vertical line can arise from this spectral condensation.

## 5. The unaugmented complete tube has the same empirical limit

The canonical unaugmented cotangent tube `C_{d,m}` differs from the augmented operator only in the constant fiber sector. PC-053 gives

\[
\operatorname{Spec}(C_{d,m})
=
m\operatorname{Spec}(H_d)
\sqcup
\bigsqcup_{j=1}^{m-1}
\left(m\operatorname{Spec}(A_d)-2j\right).
\]

After division by `m`, only `r` eigenvalues out of the total `rm` come from `H_d`; the remaining `(m-1)r` points lie on the same affine ladders as above. Hence the normalized empirical measure of `C_{d,m}/m` has the same weak limit `mu_d`. For bounded `f`, replacing the `j=0` augmented sector by the unaugmented one changes the normalized integral by at most

\[
\boxed{
\frac{2\|f\|_\infty}{m}.
}
\]

Therefore the finite-band collapse is not an artifact of the rank-one augmentation used to simplify the PC-053 block formula.

Without scaling, the same statement has an even more rigid form: every base eigenvalue generates the exact arithmetic progression

\[
\boxed{
\{m\lambda_\alpha-2j:0\le j<m\}.
}
\]

The spacing is always `2`, and PC-053 already proves that composite refinement is independent of how `m` is factored into prime steps. The large-state limit therefore adds density, not new prime-order holonomy or new sector arithmetic.

## 6. Prior-art and novelty audit

No historical novelty is claimed for the limiting-analysis ingredients. Weak convergence of equally spaced empirical measures by Riemann sums, Stieltjes transforms of uniform intervals, and logarithmic derivatives of characteristic polynomials are elementary spectral/complex analysis.

The relevant cotangent prior-art boundary is already recorded in `research/prime_circle/SOURCES.md`:

- Wiktor Ejsmont and Franz Lehner, **The Trace Method for Cotangent Sums**, *Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series A* 177 (2021), 105324, provides neighboring finite self-adjoint cotangent-matrix spectral theory and trace formulas.
- Matthias Beck, **Dedekind cotangent sums**, *Acta Arithmetica* 109 (2003), 109–130, is a classical anchor for cotangent distribution and Petersson–Knopp-type scale identities.
- John Lewis and Don Zagier, **Cotangent sums, quantum modular forms, and the generalized Riemann hypothesis**, *Research in the Mathematical Sciences* 6 (2019), Article 4, is the crucial positive control: cotangent constructions can participate in genuine GRH criteria, but their mechanism uses a different asymptotic dilation/Gram and Beurling-type functional-analytic structure rather than the complete preimage-tube spectrum studied here.

The Prime-Circle-specific contribution of the present finding is only the **research boundary** obtained by combining PC-053's exact finite affine spectrum with the `m -> infinity` limit left open by PC-098. A directed comparison with the neighboring cotangent literature gives no basis for treating the resulting finite-band law as a new analytic structure.

## 7. Stress tests and falsification controls

The result is exact and has direct finite checks.

1. For fixed `d,m`, diagonalize `A_d`; the spectrum of `mathcal A_{d,m}/m` must be exactly the `m`-point meshes `lambda_alpha-2j/m`. Any additional eigenvalue refutes the PC-053 specialization used here.
2. For a Lipschitz test function, compare the empirical average with the stated band integral. Violation of the `Lip(f)/m` bound refutes the quantitative limit.
3. Compare normalized moments of the finite spectra with the Riemann sums of `(lambda_alpha-2t)^k`; they must converge to the displayed polynomial in base traces.
4. Compute the normalized logarithmic derivative of `D_{d,m}` away from the real bands. It must converge to the displayed Stieltjes transform locally uniformly.
5. Repeat with the unaugmented `C_{d,m}/m`. The difference of bounded test-function averages from the augmented law must be `O(1/m)` and both must have the same limit.
6. Refactor `m` into different prime-step orders. PC-053's exact affine semigroup law requires the same finite spectrum and therefore the same limiting measure.

Small direct matrix checks at several `(d,m)` values agree with the exact PC-053 affine spectrum to numerical precision; they are controls only, not evidence replacing the derivation.

PC-098 showed that infinite depth on a **fixed** finite conductor support is only finite-state rational functional calculus, but deliberately left growing support/state dimension open. The present result closes the most canonical instance of that escape: complete power-map preimage tubes with the intrinsic oriented cotangent kernel and their natural linear spectral scaling.

\[

\to
m\to\infty
\to
