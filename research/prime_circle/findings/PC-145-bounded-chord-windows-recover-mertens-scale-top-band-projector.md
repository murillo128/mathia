# PC-145 — bounded chord windows recover the Mertens-scale top-band projector

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED` + `LITERATURE+DERIVED` + `DECISIVE-BOUNDARY` + `CLUE-RESOLUTION`. PC-143 proves that, along primorials, the isolated gap-two top band of the primitive-shell inverse-square chord Laplacian converges to the exact gap-two matching space in normalized Frobenius average but not in operator norm. PC-144 sharpens the average matching-space remainder energy to the exact scale `1/log x`. `CLUE-short-chord-mertens-projector-locality` asks whether the remaining projector rotation at that natural scale is genuinely nonlocal or can be recovered from bounded chord windows.

The answer is **finite-local at Mertens/Frobenius scale**. For every fixed chord cutoff `H`, retain only nonmatching primitive edges of cyclic distance at most `H`, while keeping the exact gap-two matching block. Let `Q_x` be the full isolated top-band projector and `Q_x^(H)` the corresponding projector of the truncated operator. Then

\[
\boxed{
\lim_{H\to\infty}\limsup_{x\to\infty}
\frac{\log x}{E_x}
\left\|Q_x-Q_x^{(H)}\right\|_F^2=0.
}
\tag{1}
\]

Thus the long-chord tail cannot carry a coherent contribution to the **average** top-band rotation at the `1/log x` scale. This does not restore operator-norm locking: the PC-143 obstruction is already generated by the fixed offsets `4` and `6`, so sparse macroscopic defect directions survive inside bounded windows.

## 1. Setup and the long-chord tail

Let

\[
N_x=\prod_{p\le x}p,
\qquad
U_x=(\mathbb Z/N_x\mathbb Z)^\times,
\]

and write the primitive-shell inverse-square chord Laplacian as in PC-142/PC-143,

\[
L_x=A_x+R_x,
\qquad
A_x=\beta_{N_x}P_x,
\qquad
\beta_N=\frac1{2\sin^2(2\pi/N)}.
\tag{2}
\]

Here `P_x` is the orthogonal projector onto the exact gap-two matching space, of rank

\[
E_x=\prod_{3\le p\le x}(p-2).
\tag{3}
\]

For a cyclic distance `h`, put

\[
w_h(N)=\frac1{4\sin^2(\pi h/N)}.
\tag{4}
\]

Fix an integer `H>=3`. Let `R_x^(H)` be the Laplacian formed from those edges of the remainder `R_x` whose symmetric cyclic distance is at most `H`, and put

\[
L_x^{(H)}=A_x+R_x^{(H)},
\qquad
T_x^{(H)}=R_x-R_x^{(H)}\succeq0.
\tag{5}
\]

Thus `T_x^(H)` is precisely the discarded long-chord tail. The PC-143 chord estimate

\[
\frac{w_h(N)}{N^2}\le\frac1{16h^2}
\qquad
(1\le h\le N/2)
\tag{6}
\]

gives a uniform operator bound. At a fixed vertex there are at most two possible neighbors at each cyclic distance, so if

\[
\sigma_H:=\sum_{h>H}\frac1{h^2},
\qquad
a_H:=\frac{\sigma_H}{4},
\tag{7}
\]

then the weighted tail degree satisfies

\[
d_{\max}(T_x^{(H)})
\le \frac{N_x^2}{8}\sigma_H.
\tag{8}
\]

For a positive weighted graph Laplacian, `||L||<=2d_max`. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\|T_x^{(H)}\|\le a_HN_x^2,
\qquad
a_H\longrightarrow0.
}
\tag{9}
\]

This estimate is arithmetic-free: it uses only summability of the intrinsic inverse-square chord kernel.

## 2. The matching-compressed long tail has a vanishing Mertens constant

Define the normalized matching-compressed tail energy

\[
t_{x,H}
:=
\frac{\operatorname{tr}(P_xT_x^{(H)}P_x)}{E_xN_x^2}.
\tag{10}
\]

PC-144 gives an exact oriented-CRT formula for the full compressed remainder and proves dominated convergence after multiplication by `log x`. Restricting the same exact sum to signed offsets with `|h|>H` gives, for every fixed `H`,

\[
\boxed{
(\log x)t_{x,H}\longrightarrow C_{\rm gap}^{>H},
}
\tag{11}
\]

where

\[
C_{\rm gap}^{>H}
=
\frac{\kappa}{4\pi^2}
\sum_{\substack{|h|>H\\h\equiv0,2\ (6)\\h\ne0,2}}
\frac1{h^2}
\prod_{\substack{p\ge5\\p\mid h(h-2)}}
\frac{p-2}{p-3},
\tag{12}
\]

and `kappa` is the positive Euler-product constant of PC-144. That finding already proves absolute convergence of the complete series. Hence

\[
\boxed{C_{\rm gap}^{>H}\longrightarrow0.}
\tag{13}
\]

So long chords become small in two different senses: their full operator norm is `o_H(N_x^2)` by (9), while the amount of their energy seen on the canonical matching space is `C_gap^{>H}/log x` at the exact arithmetic scale.

## 3. Every fixed truncation retains the same isolated rank-`E_x` band

PC-142 supplies a universal remainder bound

\[
\|R_x\|\le\rho_{N_x}<\beta_{N_x},
\qquad
\delta_{N_x}:=\beta_{N_x}-\rho_{N_x}>c_6N_x^2,
\tag{14}
\]

with

\[
c_6=\frac1{4\pi^2}-\frac5{216}>0.
\tag{15}
\]

Since `0<=R_x^(H)<=R_x`,

\[
\|R_x^{(H)}\|\le\rho_{N_x}.
\tag{16}
\]

The same min-max/Weyl argument used in PC-142 therefore applies to every fixed-window operator `L_x^(H)`: its `E_x`th eigenvalue is at least `beta_N`, its `(E_x+1)`st is at most `rho_N`, and hence it has exactly `E_x` eigenvalues at or above `beta_N`. Let `Q_x^(H)` denote their spectral projector. The full projector `Q_x` and every truncated projector `Q_x^(H)` are thus separated from their complementary spectra by the **same** lower gap `delta_N`.

The truncated band is also uniformly close to the matching space in the PC-143 average sense. Repeating its Sylvester argument with `R_x^(H)` gives

\[
\left\|(I-Q_x^{(H)})P_x\right\|_F^2
\le
\frac{\rho_{N_x}}{\delta_{N_x}^2}
\operatorname{tr}(P_xR_x^{(H)}P_x)
\le
\frac{\rho_{N_x}}{\delta_{N_x}^2}
\operatorname{tr}(P_xR_xP_x).
\tag{17}
\]

Because `P_x` and `Q_x^(H)` have equal rank,

\[
\|(I-P_x)Q_x^{(H)}\|_F
=
\|(I-Q_x^{(H)})P_x\|_F.
\tag{18}
\]

Moreover PC-142 gives `rho_N/N^2<5/216`, and PC-144 gives

\[
\frac{\log x}{E_xN_x^2}
\operatorname{tr}(P_xR_xP_x)
\longrightarrow C_{\rm gap}.
\tag{19}
\]

Consequently

\[
\boxed{
\sup_H\limsup_{x\to\infty}
\frac{\log x}{E_x}
\|(I-P_x)Q_x^{(H)}\|_F^2
\le
K_{\rm gap},
}
\tag{20}
\]

where one may take

\[
K_{\rm gap}
:=\frac{5}{216c_6^2}C_{\rm gap}<\infty.
\tag{21}
\]

The important point is that the scaled mismatch is bounded **uniformly in the cutoff**.

## 4. Sylvester comparison of the full and truncated projectors

Put

\[
Q=Q_x,
\qquad Q_H=Q_x^{(H)},
\qquad T=T_x^{(H)},
\qquad
X=(I-Q)Q_H.
\tag{22}
\]

Let

\[
L_-=(I-Q)L_x(I-Q),
\qquad
L_{H,+}=Q_HL_x^{(H)}Q_H.
\tag{23}
\]

Since `Q` commutes with `L_x`, `Q_H` commutes with `L_x^(H)`, and `L_x=L_x^(H)+T`, direct multiplication gives the exact Sylvester equation

\[
\boxed{
L_-X-XL_{H,+}=(I-Q)TQ_H.
}
\tag{24}
\]

The spectrum of `L_-` is at most `rho_N`, while the spectrum of `L_{H,+}` is at least `beta_N`, so the standard self-adjoint Sylvester estimate yields

\[
\|X\|_F
\le
\frac{\|TQ_H\|_F}{\delta_N}.
\tag{25}
\]

Equal-rank orthogonal projections satisfy

\[
\|Q-Q_H\|_F^2=2\|(I-Q)Q_H\|_F^2=2\|X\|_F^2.
\tag{26}
\]

To retain the arithmetic information in the tail rather than use only its operator norm, decompose through the matching projector:

\[
TQ_H=TP_xQ_H+T(I-P_x)Q_H.
\tag{27}
\]

Then

\[
\|TQ_H\|_F^2
\le
2\|TP_x\|_F^2
+2\|T\|^2\|(I-P_x)Q_H\|_F^2.
\tag{28}
\]

Because `T` is positive semidefinite,

\[
\|TP_x\|_F^2
=
\operatorname{tr}(P_xT^2P_x)
\le
\|T\|\operatorname{tr}(P_xTP_x).
\tag{29}
\]

Combining (25)--(29),

\[
\boxed{
\|Q_x-Q_x^{(H)}\|_F^2
\le
\frac4{\delta_{N_x}^2}
\left[
\|T_x^{(H)}\|\operatorname{tr}(P_xT_x^{(H)}P_x)
+
\|T_x^{(H)}\|^2
\|(I-P_x)Q_x^{(H)}\|_F^2
\right].
}
\tag{30}
\]

This is the decisive estimate: the first term couples the small long-range operator norm to the **exact Mertens tail mass**, while the second couples its squared norm to a uniformly bounded scaled mismatch.

## 5. The double limit

Insert (9), (11), (14), and (20) into (30). For each fixed `H`,

\[
\limsup_{x\to\infty}
\frac{\log x}{E_x}
\|Q_x-Q_x^{(H)}\|_F^2
\le
\frac4{c_6^2}
\left[
 a_H C_{\rm gap}^{>H}
+a_H^2K_{\rm gap}
\right].
\tag{31}
\]

Both terms on the right vanish as `H->infinity`: `a_H->0` by the summable inverse-square chord tail, and `C_gap^{>H}->0` by the absolutely convergent conditioned singular-series sum of PC-144. This proves (1).

The proof is intentionally one-sided. It does **not** assert that the full projector deficit itself has an exact `Theta(1/log x)` asymptotic, only that whatever Mertens-scale average rotation exists can be recovered by increasing finite chord windows. In particular no matching lower bound is being smuggled in from PC-144, whose exact asymptotic concerns compressed remainder energy rather than projector distance.

## 7. Boundary and falsification surface

1. For every fixed `H`, direct construction must satisfy `0<=R_x^(H)<=R_x` and the operator bound (9). Failure of the weighted-degree estimate would invalidate the truncation control.
2. Restricting the exact PC-144 offset formula to `|h|>H` must give (11)--(12). The constants `C_gap^{>H}` must decrease to zero because the full series is absolutely convergent.
3. Every truncated operator must have exactly `E_x` eigenvalues at or above `beta_N`; this follows from (16) but can also be checked directly at finite primorials.
4. Numerical diagonalization can test (30) and the scaled projector distances for increasing `H`. Such computations are controls only; the proof does not rely on extrapolating finite conductors.
5. Equation (1) is a normalized-Frobenius/Mertens-scale statement. It makes no claim that `||Q_x-Q_x^(H)||` tends to zero uniformly in `x` for fixed `H`, nor that `||P_x-Q_x||` tends to zero. PC-143 already proves the latter is false.
6. The result does not classify internal spacings within the isolated band, the arithmetic organization of its sparse exceptional directions, or cross-level transport. Those remain outside this finite-local average statement.

The clue is therefore resolved positively but as a **classicalization boundary**: long chords do not supply the missing average nonlocal information at the natural `1/log x` scale.
