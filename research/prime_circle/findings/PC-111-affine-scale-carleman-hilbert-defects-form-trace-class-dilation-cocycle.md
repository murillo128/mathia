# PC-111 — affine-scale Carleman–Hilbert defects form a trace-class dilation cocycle

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED` + `LITERATURE+DERIVED` + `DECISIVE-NEGATIVE` + `DECISIVE-BOUNDARY`. PC-109 and PC-110 leave open whether changing the affine conductor scale itself can rescue new spectral information from the universal Carleman–Hilbert defect. For every fixed mesh scale `h>0`, let

\[
(Cf)(x)=\int_0^\infty \frac{f(y)}{x+y}\,dy,
\]

let `H=(1/(a+b+1))_{a,b>=0}` be the Hilbert matrix, and embed its basis at cell size `h` by

\[
V_he_a=h^{-1/2}\mathbf 1_{[ah,(a+1)h)}.
\]

Put

\[
S_h=V_hHV_h^*,
\qquad
D_h=C-S_h.
\]

Then the entire affine family is generated by dilation from one universal defect,

\[
\boxed{D_h=U_hD_1U_h^*},
\qquad
(U_hf)(x)=h^{-1/2}f(x/h).
\]

More strongly, every fixed relative-scale defect

\[
E_h:=D_h-D_1=S_1-S_h
\]

is trace class and satisfies

\[
\boxed{E_h\in\mathcal S_1,\qquad \operatorname{Tr}E_h=\frac12\log h.}
\]

The family is an exact dilation cocycle,

\[
\boxed{E_{hk}=E_h+U_hE_kU_h^*.}
\]

Thus canonical fixed affine rescaling does not create a new prime-sensitive continuum operator after PC-109/110: all scales are unitarily equivalent, and their pairwise differences are nuclear, depending on a conductor ratio only through an ordinary positive real dilation parameter. No historical novelty is claimed for the Carleman operator, Hilbert matrix, dilation invariance, trace ideals, or Fredholm determinants. The durable Prime-Circle result is the exact classification of this specific residual scale freedom left by PC-110.

## 1. Exact dilation covariance of the continuum defect

The step kernel of `S_h` is

\[
\boxed{
s_h(x,y)=
\frac1{h\bigl(\lfloor x/h\rfloor+\lfloor y/h\rfloor+1\bigr)}.
}
\]

Define the unitary dilation

\[
(U_hf)(x)=h^{-1/2}f(x/h).
\]

The Carleman kernel is homogeneous of degree `-1`, so a direct change of variables gives

\[
\boxed{U_hCU_h^*=C.}
\]

Also `U_hV_1=V_h`. Therefore

\[
U_hS_1U_h^*=S_h
\]

and hence

\[
\boxed{D_h=U_hD_1U_h^*.}
\]

This already rules out obtaining conductor arithmetic from the spectrum of any single fixed affine scale: every `D_h` has exactly the same spectral type and multiplicities. In particular, choosing `h=1/p`, `p`, `q/p`, or any other conductor-derived positive scale changes only the coordinate dilation, not the operator up to unitary equivalence.

The remaining question is whether *relative* scale comparison can carry a nontrivial compact spectral correction. Define

\[
\boxed{E_h=D_h-D_1=S_1-S_h.}
\]

The individual operators `D_h` retain the universal noncompact origin singularity from PC-109. The difference `E_h`, however, cancels that common singularity exactly.

## 2. PC-110 reduces the relative-scale problem to a local Carleman annulus

Let `P_1` denote multiplication by `1_(0,1)` and write the PC-110 decomposition

\[
D_1=A+K,
\qquad
A=P_1D_1P_1,
\qquad
K=D_1-P_1D_1P_1\in\mathcal S_1.
\]

Inside the first cell, the step-Hilbert kernel is identically `1`, so

\[
\boxed{
A=P_1CP_1-
|\mathbf1_{(0,1)}\rangle\langle\mathbf1_{(0,1)}|.
}
\]

Conjugating by `U_h`, put `P_h=U_hP_1U_h^*`, the projection onto `(0,h)`. Then

\[
D_h=U_hAU_h^*+U_hKU_h^*.
\]

Since `K` is trace class, the term

\[
U_hKU_h^*-K
\]

is trace class. It remains only to compare the two origin-cell compressions of `C` and the two normalized rank-one cell terms.

Assume first `h>1` and let `R=P_{(1,h)}`. Since `P_h=P_1+R` with orthogonal ranges,

\[
\boxed{
P_hCP_h-P_1CP_1
=RCR+RCP_1+P_1CR.
}
\]

The normalized rank-one term also changes by a finite-rank operator. Thus `E_h` is trace class once the three displayed Carleman pieces are trace class.

## 3. The annular and cross terms are trace class

Use the classical Laplace rank-one representation of the Carleman kernel,

\[
\boxed{
C=\int_0^\infty |e_t\rangle\langle e_t|\,dt,
\qquad
e_t(x)=e^{-tx},
}
\]

initially in the weak sense. For the diagonal annular compression,

\[
RCR=\int_0^\infty |Re_t\rangle\langle Re_t|\,dt
\]

is positive and

\[
\int_0^\infty\|Re_t\|^2dt
=\int_1^h\int_0^\infty e^{-2tx}\,dt\,dx
=\int_1^h\frac{dx}{2x}
=\frac12\log h<\infty.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{RCR\in\mathcal S_1,\qquad \operatorname{Tr}(RCR)=\frac12\log h.}
\]

For the off-diagonal block,

\[
RCP_1=\int_0^\infty |Re_t\rangle\langle P_1e_t|\,dt.
\]

Its nuclear norm is bounded by

\[
\int_0^\infty \|Re_t\|\,\|P_1e_t\|\,dt.
\]

Near `t=0` both factors are bounded. As `t->infinity`,

\[
\|Re_t\|\,\|P_1e_t\|
=O\!\left(\frac{e^{-t}}{t}\right),
\]

so the integral converges. Hence

\[
RCP_1,\;P_1CR\in\mathcal S_1.
\]

Together with the finite-rank cell correction and `U_hKU_h^*-K`, this proves

\[
\boxed{E_h\in\mathcal S_1\qquad(h>1).}
\]

For `0<h<1`, dilation covariance gives

\[
E_h=-U_hE_{1/h}U_h^*,
\]

so the same conclusion holds for every `h>0`; `E_1=0` trivially.

## 4. The trace is exactly the logarithmic dilation character

The trace of `U_hKU_h^*-K` is zero by unitary invariance. The two normalized origin-cell rank-one operators each have trace `1`, so their difference has trace zero. The off-diagonal trace-class blocks `RCP_1` and `P_1CR` have trace zero because they map between orthogonal subspaces. Therefore for `h>1` the only trace contribution is the positive annular compression:

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Tr}E_h
=\operatorname{Tr}(RCR)
=\frac12\log h.
}
\]

The inversion relation gives the same formula for `0<h<1`. Thus

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Tr}E_h=\frac12\log h
\qquad(h>0).
}
\]

There is also an exact multiplicative cocycle relation. From

\[
D_{hk}=U_hD_kU_h^*
\]

we obtain

\[
\boxed{
E_{hk}=E_h+U_hE_kU_h^*.
}
\]

Taking traces recovers

\[
\operatorname{Tr}E_{hk}
=\operatorname{Tr}E_h+\operatorname{Tr}E_k
=\frac12\log(hk).
\]

At the scalar trace level, the entire affine-scale freedom is therefore exactly the ordinary logarithmic character of the positive dilation group.

## 5. Direct falsification checks

A useful independent check comes from the diagonal of the step kernels. Formally,

\[
s_1(x,x)=\frac1{2\lfloor x\rfloor+1},
\qquad
s_h(x,x)=
\frac1{h(2\lfloor x/h\rfloor+1)}.
\]

Because `E_h=S_1-S_h` is trace class by the proof above, the diagonal difference may be integrated by truncation. Exact piecewise integration to cutoff `X=10^4` gives

| `h` | truncated diagonal trace | `(1/2) log h` |
|---:|---:|---:|
| `0.7` | `-0.1783374716` | `-0.1783374720` |
| `sqrt(2)` | `0.1732867952` | `0.1732867951` |
| `1.5` | `0.2027325550` | `0.2027325541` |
| `2` | `0.3465735897` | `0.3465735903` |
| `3.3` | `0.5969612379` | `0.5969612342` |

The residual decreases with cutoff and agrees with the derived trace. These checks are not evidence for the theorem; they are falsification tests against indexing, normalization, and dilation-sign mistakes.

Two algebraic edge checks are also exact:

- `h=1` gives `E_1=0` and trace `0`.
- replacing `h` by `1/h` gives `E_h=-U_hE_{1/h}U_h^*`, forcing the trace to change sign, exactly as `log h` does.

## 6. Fredholm zero-density obstruction at every fixed affine relative scale

For every fixed `h>0`, `E_h` is self-adjoint and trace class. If its nonzero eigenvalues are `mu_j(h)`, then

\[
\sum_j|\mu_j(h)|<\infty.
\]

Hence the ordinary Fredholm determinant

\[
F_h(z)=\det(I-zE_h)
\]

has nonzero zeros `z_j(h)=1/mu_j(h)` satisfying

\[
\boxed{
\sum_j\frac1{|z_j(h)|}<\infty,
\qquad
N_h(R)=o(R).
}
\]

This is the same nuclear zero-density obstruction used in PC-107 and PC-110. Under any asymptotically linear identification of `z` with Riemann-zero ordinate, a fixed affine relative-scale determinant cannot reproduce the Riemann--von Mangoldt `R log R` zero count.

If the scale is chosen from two conductors, for example `h=q/p`, then

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Tr}E_{q/p}=\frac12\log(q/p).
}
\]

But the formula holds unchanged for every positive real ratio, including composite and non-arithmetic values. The relative scale itself therefore supplies no prime discriminator.

## 7. Prior-art and novelty audit

The analytic ingredients are classical. The Carleman operator and its dilation/Mellin structure are standard; the Hilbert matrix and its continuous Hankel representations go back through the classical Hilbert-matrix literature represented in this line by Rosenblum. Yafaev's continuous/discrete Hankel correspondence gives a modern general framework for unitary relations between Hankel operators on `l^2(Z_+)` and `L^2(R_+)`; Pushnitski--Yafaev likewise provide the surrounding spectral/scattering Hankel framework already anchored for PC-109/110. Trace-class perturbations and Fredholm determinants are standard trace-ideal theory already used in PC-107 and PC-110.

Directed searches for Carleman/Hilbert step discretizations, dilation covariance, and trace-class relative defects found these established general frameworks and recent Hilbert-matrix/Carleman asymptotic work, but no basis for claiming that the particular Mathia family

\[
D_h=C-V_hHV_h^*
\]

or the exact cocycle/trace formulas above are historically new. In particular, the literature already makes it unsurprising that dilation is the natural symmetry. The claim retained here is deliberately line-specific: **the full fixed affine scale freedom left open after PC-110 consists of unitary dilation conjugates, and every pairwise relative defect is trace class with trace `(1/2) log h`.**

No theorem-level novelty beyond this research-program obstruction is claimed.

## 8. Consequence for the surviving Prime-Circle boundary

PC-109--PC-111 now separate three effects cleanly:

\[
\text{prime-conductor affine blowup}
\longrightarrow
\text{universal Carleman--Hilbert defect}
\longrightarrow
