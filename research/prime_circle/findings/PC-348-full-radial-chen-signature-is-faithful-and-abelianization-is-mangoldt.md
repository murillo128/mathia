# PC-348 — full radial Chen signature is faithful while its abelianization is pure Mangoldt

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED` + `LITERATURE+DERIVED` + `DECISIVE-NEGATIVE` for commutative/determinant readouts of the full infinite-depth radial signature, and `DECISIVE-BOUNDARY` for genuinely noncommutative scalarizations.

PC-347 classifies every fixed finite level of the source-native Chen hierarchy built from the Prime-Circle radial one-forms `d log Phi_n`: complete symmetrization collapses to products of `Lambda(n)`, while each ordered coefficient lies in the classical cyclotomic hyperlogarithm algebra. It deliberately leaves open whether the **entire nonnilpotent/infinite-depth path-ordered exponential** can escape that finite-level boundary.

There is an exact answer for the representation-free object and for every commutative scalarization. The full Chen series is the ordinary path signature of the finite-dimensional radial cyclotomic path. Its abelianization is exactly the exponential of the common-vertex Mangoldt endpoint vector, so no infinite resummation that factors through a commutative algebra can recover radial interior information. Conversely, as soon as the shell set contains a prime-power shell, the full noncommutative signature is faithful: it determines the whole radial path, not merely its endpoints. Thus infinite depth does not create a new selector by itself; it upgrades the bounded-depth hierarchy into a lossless encoding of the original cyclotomic profiles. A genuinely new mechanism still requires a source-forced noncommutative representation or invariant that does not factor through abelianization.

A useful corollary is exact for arbitrary finite-dimensional matrix substitutions. If `U(1)` is the noncommutative radial holonomy generated by fixed matrices `M_n`, then

\[
\boxed{\det U(1)=\exp\!\left(\sum_n \Lambda(n)\operatorname{tr}M_n\right).}
\]

Hence the determinant, product of eigenvalues, and every one-dimensional multiplicative character of this holonomy sees only the classical Mangoldt endpoint data, even though traces or other genuinely noncommutative spectral invariants may retain ordered interior information.

## 1. The complete radial hierarchy is a path signature

Fix a finite shell set

\[
S\subset\{2,3,\ldots\}
\]

and define

\[
X_n(z):=\log\Phi_n(z),
\qquad
X_S(z):=(X_n(z))_{n\in S},
\qquad 0\le z\le1.
\tag{1}
\]

For `n>1`, `Phi_n` has no zero on `[0,1]`, `Phi_n(0)=1`, and `Phi_n(z)>0` there. Thus `X_S` is a real-analytic bounded-variation path with

\[
X_n(0)=0,
\qquad
X_n(1)=\log\Phi_n(1)=\Lambda(n).
\tag{2}
\]

Write

\[
\omega_n=dX_n=\frac{\Phi_n'(z)}{\Phi_n(z)}\,dz.
\tag{3}
\]

With noncommuting formal letters `e_n`, the complete Chen series is

\[
\mathsf S_S
:=1+\sum_{r\ge1}\sum_{n_1,\ldots,n_r\in S}
J_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}
 e_{n_1}\cdots e_{n_r},
\tag{4}
\]

where

\[
J_{n_1,\ldots,n_r}
=
\int_{0<z_1<\cdots<z_r<1}
\omega_{n_1}(z_1)\cdots\omega_{n_r}(z_r).
\tag{5}
\]

This is exactly the tensor signature of `X_S`, equivalently the universal path-ordered exponential

\[
\mathsf S_S
=\mathcal P\exp\!\left(\int_0^1\sum_{n\in S}e_n\,\omega_n\right).
\tag{6}
\]

Because `X_S` has finite variation `V`, the degree-`r` signature satisfies the standard factorial estimate in any admissible tensor norm,

\[
\|\mathsf S_S^{(r)}\|\le \frac{V^r}{r!}.
\tag{7}
\]

Consequently the infinite series is not merely formal when evaluated in any fixed finite-dimensional bounded matrix representation: the Dyson series converges absolutely.

## 2. Infinite-depth abelianization is exactly the Mangoldt endpoint exponential

Let

\[
\operatorname{ab}:\widehat T(\mathbb C^S)
\longrightarrow
\widehat{\operatorname{Sym}}(\mathbb C^S)
\tag{8}
\]

be the map making the letters `e_n` commute. After abelianization, path ordering disappears. Therefore (6) becomes the ordinary exponential of the integrated connection:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{ab}(\mathsf S_S)
&=
\exp\!\left(\int_0^1\sum_{n\in S}e_n\,\omega_n\right)\\
&=
\exp\!\left(\sum_{n\in S}e_n[X_n(1)-X_n(0)]\right).
\end{aligned}
\tag{9}
\]

Using (2),

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{ab}(\mathsf S_S)
=
\exp\!\left(\sum_{n\in S}\Lambda(n)e_n\right).
}
\tag{10}
\]

Equation (10) is the all-depth version of the finite shuffle collapse in PC-347. For multiplicities `r_n>=0`, the coefficient of the commutative monomial `prod_n e_n^{r_n}` is

\[
\boxed{
\prod_{n\in S}\frac{\Lambda(n)^{r_n}}{r_n!}.
}
\tag{11}
\]

Thus every infinite resummation that first forgets word order, every algebra homomorphism from the signature into a commutative algebra, and every scalar observable factoring through such an abelianization is determined entirely by the common-vertex endpoint vector `Lambda|_S`.

This has an immediate matched control. Replace the cyclotomic path by **any** bounded-variation path `Y:[0,1]->R^S` with

\[
Y(0)=0,
\qquad
Y_n(1)=\Lambda(n).
\tag{12}
\]

Its abelianized signature is identical to (10), regardless of its interior geometry. Therefore no commutative full-signature observable can distinguish the cyclotomic radial structure from a non-arithmetic control having the same endpoint displacement.

This closes a genuine infinite-depth loophole rather than merely repeating the finite-degree statement: summing all symmetric degrees does not create a new analytic variable or a hidden functional equation. It produces exactly the exponential generating function of the classical Mangoldt endpoint data.

## 3. Determinants of arbitrary matrix holonomies also collapse exactly

Let `M_n` be arbitrary fixed complex `d x d` matrices and let

\[
A(z)=\sum_{n\in S}M_n\frac{\Phi_n'(z)}{\Phi_n(z)}.
\tag{13}
\]

Consider the right-ordered evolution

\[
U'(z)=U(z)A(z),
\qquad
U(0)=I_d.
\tag{14}
\]

By (7), `U(1)` is the convergent evaluation of the universal signature under `e_n -> M_n`. Noncommutativity can be completely active inside `U(1)`.

Nevertheless Jacobi's determinant identity gives

\[
\frac{d}{dz}\log\det U(z)
=
\operatorname{tr}(U(z)^{-1}U'(z))
=
\operatorname{tr}A(z).
\tag{15}
\]

Integrating and using (2),

\[
\begin{aligned}
\log\det U(1)
&=
\sum_{n\in S}\operatorname{tr}(M_n)
\int_0^1\omega_n\\
&=
\sum_{n\in S}\Lambda(n)\operatorname{tr}(M_n).
\end{aligned}
\tag{16}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\det U(1)
=
\exp\!\left(\sum_{n\in S}\Lambda(n)\operatorname{tr}M_n\right).
}
\tag{17}
\]

In particular, if all `M_n` are traceless then

\[
\boxed{\det U(1)=1.}
\tag{18}
\]

No commutator, Magnus term, or infinite Dyson resummation can alter (17). The determinant is therefore a decisive negative control for proposals in which the hoped-for spectral arithmetic is supposed to appear in the product of the holonomy eigenvalues.

This statement does **not** extend to `tr U(1)`, `det(lambda I-U(1))`, individual eigenvalues, or other genuinely noncommutative class functions. Those can depend on ordered iterated integrals. The point is precisely to separate the closed abelian channel from the surviving nonabelian one.

## 4. With one prime-power coordinate, the full noncommutative signature is faithful

Suppose `S` contains a prime-power shell

\[
q=p^a.
\tag{19}
\]

Its cyclotomic polynomial has the elementary positive-coefficient form

\[
\Phi_{p^a}(z)
=
1+z^{p^{a-1}}+z^{2p^{a-1}}+\cdots+z^{(p-1)p^{a-1}}.
\tag{20}
\]

Therefore

\[
X_q(z)=\log\Phi_q(z)
\tag{21}
\]

is strictly increasing on `[0,1]`. It follows immediately that `X_S` is injective: if `z_1<z_2`, then `X_q(z_1)<X_q(z_2)`, so `X_S(z_1) != X_S(z_2)`.

Hambly and Lyons proved that the signature of a bounded-variation path determines its unique tree-reduced representative up to reparametrization. A path with a strictly monotone coordinate has no nontrivial retracing/tree-like segment, so `X_S` is already tree-reduced. Consequently

\[
\boxed{
\mathsf S_S
\text{ determines the geometric path }
X_S([0,1])
\text{ with orientation.}
}
\tag{22}
\]

For Prime Circle even the residual reparametrization ambiguity is removable from the source data. Since `X_q` is explicitly known and strictly increasing, use

\[
t=X_q(z)
\tag{23}
\]

as path parameter and recover

\[
z=X_q^{-1}(t).
\tag{24}
\]

The reconstructed curve then gives every coordinate `X_n(z)` on the original radial interval. Because `X_S(0)=0`, no translation datum is missing either.

Thus, for the mixed prime-power/non-prime-power shell sets demanded by the signed-flux clue, the complete noncommutative Chen signature is **lossless source encoding**. This explains why infinite depth can retain all of the compensating signed radial structure that finite scalarizations lose. But it also changes the evidential burden: retaining that information is not itself a new arithmetic theorem or RH mechanism. One still needs a source-forced operation on the signature whose output has an independently established zeta-relevant property.

## 5. The noncommutative completion remains in established signature/cyclotomic territory

PC-347 proves coefficientwise that if

\[
N=\operatorname{lcm}(S),
\tag{25}
\]

then every word coefficient of `mathsf S_S` is a convergent iterated integral with letters in `mu_N`, hence a cyclotomic hyperlogarithmic/multiple-polylogarithm value of level `N`. Equation (7) shows that the full signature is the degree-completed, group-like generating series of those coefficients.

The ambient structures are classical. Chen's iterated-integral theory supplies the shuffle/group-like signature formalism. A. B. Goncharov, *Multiple polylogarithms, cyclotomy and modular complexes*, Mathematical Research Letters 5 (1998), 497--516, DOI `10.4310/MRL.1998.v5.n4.a7`, supplies the root-of-unity multiple-polylogarithm period framework already used in PC-100/PC-102/PC-347. Ben Hambly and Terry Lyons, *Uniqueness for the signature of a path of bounded variation and the reduced path group*, Annals of Mathematics 171 (2010), 109--167, DOI `10.4007/annals.2010.171.109`, supplies the exact uniqueness theorem used in Section 4.

There is also extensive neighboring prior art on noncommutative root-of-unity holonomy. Benjamin Enriquez, *Quasi-reflection algebras and cyclotomic associators*, Selecta Mathematica 13 (2007), 391--463, DOI `10.1007/s00029-007-0048-2`, develops cyclotomic associators and a root-of-unity KZ pseudotwist. The Prime-Circle connection (13) is **not claimed to equal Enriquez's cyclotomic KZ system**; the relevance is the novelty boundary: completed noncommutative holonomies generated by logarithmic root-of-unity forms already belong to a well-developed classical/cyclotomic framework.

The line-local mathematical delta is therefore narrower. For the exact Prime-Circle shell grouping, (10) gives the complete infinite-depth abelianization, (17) gives the determinant of every finite-dimensional matrix evaluation, and the prime-power monotone coordinate upgrades the universal series from an information-rich formal object to a faithful encoding of the entire radial cyclotomic path.

No historical novelty is claimed for path signatures, Chen shuffle theory, signature uniqueness, Jacobi/Liouville determinant evolution, cyclotomic multiple polylogarithms, or cyclotomic associators.

## 6. Consequence for the surviving Prime-Circle branch

The infinite-depth radial-ordering branch now has a sharp dichotomy.

If the final observable factors through commutativity, the whole hierarchy collapses exactly to

\[
\exp\!\left(\sum_n\Lambda(n)e_n\right),
\tag{26}
\]

so it is no stronger than the common-anchor prime-power selector. The determinant of every fixed matrix holonomy belongs to this closed channel.

If the final observable genuinely uses noncommutativity, the universal infinite signature can retain the entire original radial path whenever a prime-power shell is present. That is real information, but it is **reconstruction**, not yet a zero-sensitive destination theorem. A surviving proposal must therefore provide at least one additional source-forced ingredient: a canonical noncommuting representation/multiplication law derived from the root/refinement geometry, a canonical nonabelian scalar invariant such as a trace/characteristic quantity whose choice is not arbitrary, or a genuinely cross-level evolution in which the state space changes with refinement. It must then establish a zeta/functional-equation/critical-line consequence that fails an appropriate non-arithmetic or uncoupled control.

Arbitrary choices of matrices `M_n` remain arbitrary spectral wrappers and are excluded by the research mandate. Merely observing that a chosen noncommutative holonomy contains more information than `Lambda` is also no longer evidence of progress: the full signature is expected to contain all radial information by (22).

## Audit / falsification tests

This finding should be revised or withdrawn if any of the following fails:

1. for some `n>1`, `Phi_n` vanishes or changes sign on `[0,1]`, invalidating the real analytic path (1);
2. the endpoint identity `log Phi_n(1)=Lambda(n)` fails for the shell range used here;
3. abelianizing the path-ordered exponential does not give the ordinary exponential of the integrated commuting connection, contradicting (10);
4. a finite-dimensional matrix evaluation of the signature violates the determinant evolution (15)--(17);
5. for a prime power `q=p^a`, `log Phi_q(z)` is not strictly increasing on `[0,1]`;
6. the Hambly--Lyons uniqueness theorem does not apply to the bounded-variation path `X_S`, or a strictly monotone coordinate still permits a nontrivial tree-like reduction;
7. the claimed boundary is interpreted as closing genuinely noncommutative traces/eigenvalue invariants or cross-level changing-state constructions, which are explicitly outside the theorem.

## Bottom line

PC-347's finite-depth boundary extends cleanly to the complete source-native radial signature. **All commutative infinite-depth information is exactly `exp(sum Lambda(n)e_n)`, and every matrix determinant collapses to the same endpoint data. The genuinely noncommutative infinite signature does not collapse; with one prime-power shell it is instead a faithful encoding of the entire radial cyclotomic path.** The remaining RH question is therefore not whether infinite path ordering preserves information, but whether Prime Circle forces a specific nonabelian representation and invariant whose analytic consequences go beyond faithful cyclotomic reconstruction and beyond established root-of-unity holonomy theory.
