# PC-416 — canonical log-scale phase breaking is only finite one-shell Mellin-jet data

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED` + `CLASSICAL-MELLIN-STRUCTURE` + `DECISIVE-NEGATIVE` for the finite-order source-side escape left open by PC-415. On the shell-2 Haar space, adjoining the natural log-scale position operator

\[
Qf(x):=(\log x)f(x)
\]

to the intrinsic descendant dilation algebra genuinely breaks the arbitrary Mellin-phase gauge of PC-415. However, every finite word in `Q` and the descendant operators, and every finite `Q`-jet descendant frame/Gram construction, factors through a finite Mellin jet of the single-shell source together with derivatives of the already universal conductor symbols. For the prime-circle source those jets are only the classical Gamma/zeta/local-prime-factor derivatives. Thus the first canonical phase-sensitive extension of the shell-2 descendant operator route does not produce a new RH mechanism.

This finding is deliberately finite-order. It does **not** rule out infinite-order/nonlocal functional calculus in `Q`, source-dependent operators, angular/chord/old-new incidence geometry, nonlinear state changes before Mellin diagonalization, or other structures not generated by finite words in the radial log-position and the source-independent descendant algebra.

## 1. The intrinsic log-scale position operator really breaks the PC-415 gauge

Use the PC-405--PC-415 Hilbert representation

\[
\mathcal H=L^2(\mathbb R_+,dx/x),
\qquad
\widehat f(t)=\int_0^\infty f(x)x^{-it}\frac{dx}{x}.
\tag{1}
\]

The descendant operators are finite combinations of dilations. For

\[
(V_af)(x):=f(ax),
\tag{2}
\]

one has

\[
\widehat{V_af}(t)=a^{it}\widehat f(t).
\tag{3}
\]

The coordinate `u=log x` is the translation coordinate for this dilation representation, so the multiplication operator `Q=log x` is the canonical position operator associated with the already intrinsic scale action. On its natural domain,

\[
\boxed{\mathcal M Q\mathcal M^{-1}=i\partial_t.}
\tag{4}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
QV_a=V_aQ-(\log a)V_a,
\qquad
[Q,V_a]=-(\log a)V_a.
\tag{5}
\]

This extension is not covered by the commuting-multiplier no-go of PC-415. If `U_theta` is the Mellin-phase unitary

\[
\widehat{U_\theta f}(t)=e^{i\theta(t)}\widehat f(t),
\tag{6}
\]

then, for locally absolutely continuous `theta` on the domain under consideration,

\[
\boxed{
U_\theta^*QU_\theta=Q-\theta'(t).
}
\tag{7}
\]

Thus nonconstant Mellin phase becomes observable as soon as `Q` is admitted. The point of the result below is not that the phase remains gauge, but that this most natural way of measuring it yields only derivatives of data already present in the one-shell Mellin transform.

For the actual prime-circle shell-2 profiles the unboundedness of `Q` causes no domain obstruction at any finite order: PC-407 gives `h_n(x)=O(x)` as `x->0+` and exponential decay as `x->infinity`, hence

\[
(\log x)^j h_n(x)\in\mathcal H
\quad\text{for every fixed }j\ge0,
\tag{8}
\]

and the same holds for every finite descendant because descendants are finite dilation combinations.

## 2. Every finite affine word normal-orders to one-shell Mellin jets

Let `C_m` denote any source-independent descendant operator at conductor label `m`, with Mellin symbol `c_m(t)`:

\[
\widehat{C_mf}(t)=c_m(t)\widehat f(t).
\tag{9}
\]

Equation (4) gives the exact commutator

\[
\boxed{
[Q,C_m]\ \widehat{}\ (t)=i c_m'(t).
}
\tag{10}
\]

More generally, in Mellin space,

\[
Q M_c=M_cQ+iM_{c'},
\tag{11}
\]

where `M_c` denotes multiplication by `c(t)`. Repeatedly applying (11) puts every finite word generated by `Q` and finitely many descendant multipliers into the normal form

\[
\boxed{
W=\sum_{j=0}^{d}M_{a_j(t)}Q^j,
}
\tag{12}
\]

with each `a_j(t)` belonging to the finite differential algebra generated by the relevant universal descendant symbols.

Let

\[
H_n(t):=\widehat h_n(t).
\tag{13}
\]

Applying (12) to the base shell gives

\[
\boxed{
\widehat{Wh_n}(t)
=\sum_{j=0}^{d}a_j(t)i^jH_n^{(j)}(t).
}
\tag{14}
\]

Therefore finite-order phase breaking does not create an independent arithmetic carrier. It exposes a finite jet of the same one-shell source, combined with universal derivatives of conductor symbols.

This differs materially from PC-184 and PC-185. There the radial Euler generator and fixed refinement-equivariant operators remain Mellin multipliers and hence do not escape the one-carrier multiplier description at all. Here `Q` is noncommuting and genuinely differentiates Mellin phase, but after normal ordering the additional information is still only the finite jet (14).

## 3. The complete finite-Q-jet descendant frame is a derivative of the old kernel

The same collapse survives when all descendants are retained simultaneously rather than evaluated one by one. Fix real `sigma>1`, and for nonnegative integers `j` define weighted synthesis columns

\[
T_j e_m:=m^{-\sigma/2}Q^j h_{nm}.
\tag{15}
\]

Using the PC-415 notation

\[
\widehat{h_{nm}}(t)=H_n(t)c_m(t)
\tag{16}
\]

and

\[
Q_{n,\sigma}(t,u)
:=\sum_{m\ge1}m^{-\sigma}c_m(t)\overline{c_m(u)},
\tag{17}
\]

the Mellin transform of the `j`th column is

\[
\widehat{Q^jh_{nm}}(t)
=i^j\partial_t^j\bigl(H_n(t)c_m(t)\bigr).
\tag{18}
\]

Hence the `(j,k)` block of the finite-jet frame kernel is exactly

\[
\boxed{
K_{j,k}(t,u)
=i^j(-i)^k
\partial_t^j\partial_u^k
\left[
H_n(t)\overline{H_n(u)}Q_{n,\sigma}(t,u)
\right].
}
\tag{19}
\]

For each fixed finite `j,k`, differentiating the series in (17) term by term is legitimate in the native regime `sigma>1`. Derivatives of the finite conductor symbols add only finite powers of `log m` to the divisor-type growth already controlled in PC-407--PC-415; consequently the resulting majorants are of the form

\[
\sum_{m\ge1}m^{-\sigma}4^{\omega(m)}(1+\log m)^A<\infty
\tag{20}
\]

for fixed `A` and `sigma>1`.

Thus retaining any finite collection of phase-sensitive log-scale moments does not reveal a hidden cross-descendant interaction. The full block operator is obtained by finite differentiation of the same source-times-universal-conductor kernel already classified in PC-415.

## 4. For the prime-circle source the Mellin jet is classical zeta data

PC-407 gives the exact one-shell transform

\[
\boxed{
H_n(t)
=-\Gamma(1-it)\zeta(1-it)n^{it}
\prod_{p\mid n}(1-p^{-it}),
}
\tag{21}
\]

with the usual removable/cancelled zero-frequency presentation understood where appropriate. Consequently every finite derivative `H_n^{(j)}(t)` is a finite Leibniz combination of derivatives of the Gamma factor, zeta, and the elementary local prime factors.

Where the factors in (21) are nonzero, the first logarithmic jet is particularly explicit:

\[
\boxed{
i\frac{H_n'(t)}{H_n(t)}
=
\psi(1-it)
+
\frac{\zeta'(1-it)}{\zeta(1-it)}
-
\log n
-
\sum_{p\mid n}
\frac{(\log p)p^{-it}}{1-p^{-it}}.
}
\tag{22}
\]

At zeros of a local factor one uses the undivided derivative of (21), so (14) remains the correct global statement. Higher `Q` powers similarly generate higher polygamma/zeta/local-factor derivatives.

Equation (22) is a decisive novelty check rather than a proposed bridge: the first canonical observable that detects the phase erased by PC-415 immediately returns standard one-shell Mellin differential data, including the classical logarithmic derivative of zeta. Recovery of that object is explicitly not progress under the line mandate.

## 5. Matched control and scope of the no-go

Let `g` be an arbitrary base profile in the same Mellin-domain class and propagate it by exactly the same descendant semigroup. Writing `G=\widehat g`, equations (10)--(20) remain true verbatim with `H_n` replaced by `G`. In particular all finite affine words depend only on a finite jet of `G`, while the full finite-jet descendant kernel is

\[
i^j(-i)^k\partial_t^j\partial_u^k
\left[G(t)\overline{G(u)}Q_{n,\sigma}(t,u)\right].
\tag{23}
\]

Thus the conductor/refinement geometry forces the universal differential-conductor calculus, but it does not force the prime-circle-specific zeta content in (21). That content remains entirely in the chosen one-shell source and its derivatives. An arbitrary matched source reproduces the same operator architecture with its own Mellin jet.

The negative result is intentionally bounded. It rules out the natural route

\[
\text{shell-2 source}
+\text{finite descendant dilations}
+\text{finite powers of }\log x
\longrightarrow
\text{new RH spectral mechanism},
\tag{24}
\]

because this route is exactly a finite Weyl/affine differential calculus over one Mellin source. It does not justify extending the conclusion to infinite-order functions of `Q` such as genuinely nonlocal Mellin-frequency kernels, to source-dependent operators, to nonlinear state constructions, or to angular/chord/old-new incidence data that were discarded before the shell-2 scalar reduction.

In particular multiplication by a general function of `x`, or an operator corresponding to an infinite-order function of `Q`, is not automatically covered merely because `Q` itself is. Such a construction would need its own source-forcing and topology audit.

## 6. Prior-art and novelty assessment

The identities `M[(log x)^j f]=i^j partial_t^j M[f]`, the dilation/translation correspondence under logarithmic coordinates, and the resulting affine commutator calculus are standard Mellin/Fourier analysis. No novelty is claimed for that operator algebra. The line-local content is the exact consequence of applying this classical calculus to the specific PC-405 descendant system after PC-415: the most canonical operator that breaks the newly identified Mellin-phase gauge still closes on finite one-shell source jets and derivatives of the already universal conductor kernel.

This also prevents an artificial spectral wrapper from being mistaken for progress. One may package the finite jet blocks into larger matrices, Gram operators, determinants, or finite systems, but equation (19) shows that these are differential repackagings of the same kernel, while equation (21) identifies all source-specific arithmetic as classical Gamma/zeta/local-factor data.

## 7. Research consequence

PC-415 left open the possibility that simply measuring Mellin phase with a natural source-side operator could expose the missing arithmetic coupling. PC-416 closes that **finite-order log-scale** version of the escape. The next viable radial/operator route must therefore add structure not generated by finite descendant dilations and finite powers of the canonical log-scale position operator—for example a genuinely nonlocal Mellin-frequency coupling with independently forced kernel/topology, or a nonlinear source-side state law.

Alternatively, the search should move back before the scalar shell-2 reduction to angular, chordal, primitive/old-new, or other incidence geometry capable of carrying information that a one-shell Mellin jet cannot.
