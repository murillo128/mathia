# Does the first non-scalar Prime-Circle interaction create genuine mixed-prime off-diagonal coupling?

## Observation

Prime Circle has now classified the most canonical scalar interaction extracted from its common-anchor and shell-resultant geometry. `PC-177` proves that the exact ratio symbol is supported on prime-power jumps, but on the normalized weak-refinement space it is unbounded; after the simplest valuation-length damping it becomes a sum of **independent one-prime Toeplitz chains**, bounded only for `sigma>1`, with a classical interval spectrum governed by `-zeta'(sigma)/zeta(sigma)`. Thus literal prime-power provenance is not enough once the geometry has been scalarized into separate valuation axes. `PC-177` explicitly leaves the full vertexwise resultant Hessian, chord blocks, nonlinear tensors, and other pre-shell-scalarization objects outside that negative result.

A separate source-side result identifies what the first classical arithmetic correction looks like when a multiplicative representation really does escape its diagonal sector. In Prime Lattice, `PL-169` audits the first beyond-diagonal ratios term and finds that the surviving arithmetic variable is not a new intrinsic exponent-lattice circuit: it is the additive shifted correlation between multiplicative coefficients at `n` and `n+h`. In other words, arithmetic specificity returns only after multiplication is coupled to an additional relation that cannot be written as independent prime-coordinate motion.

Weil Positivity now supplies a stronger algebraic control. WP-158 proves that any finite system of fixed translated torus-character equations among distinct prime-primary torsion coordinates factors into independent primary solution sets. The multiplication graph on coprime shells is likewise a CRT identification, not by itself an interaction. Thus replacing a shell scalar by a monomial relation or group-law diagram does not automatically leave the closed family. This source-side statement concerns character equations; it does not classify full embedded chord or vertexwise Hessian responses.

The transfer to Prime Circle is not established. The useful question is whether one of the exact Prime-Circle objects discarded by shell scalarization already contains an analogous source-forced coupling between distinct valuation directions, or whether every such candidate still factorizes into one-prime pieces.

## Research question

Take the **simplest canonical Prime-Circle interaction before the PC-177 shell/rational-ratio scalarization**—preferably the first full vertexwise resultant Hessian or chord block already forced by persisted Prime-Circle geometry. Does its two-prime compression for distinct primes `p` and `q` contain a mixed term that cannot be written as a sum/product of independent `p`- and `q`-valuation contributions after the natural normalization?

If a genuine mixed term exists, determine what arithmetic relation indexes it. In particular, test whether the first nonseparable coefficient is equivalent to an additive/semiring coupling of the underlying integer labels, analogous in structural role to the `n` versus `n+h` off-diagonal isolated in `PL-169`, rather than a new prime-axis symbol chosen by hand.

## Decisive test

Work at the smallest level where two distinct prime coordinates coexist and compute the exact normalized two-prime block before any determinant, trace, shell average, or Bohr/Toeplitz diagonalization. Extract a mixed invariant such as a cross block, mixed finite difference, commutator, or rank-two Schur complement that vanishes identically for a sum/product of independent one-prime operators.

The direction survives only if all of the following hold: the mixed invariant is nonzero for the exact Prime-Circle object; its coefficient is forced by the persisted geometry rather than by an externally selected weight; and a matched relabelled/composite control preserving the one-prime marginals does not reproduce it automatically. If the normalized two-prime object factorizes for arbitrary distinct `p,q`, or if every nonzero mixed term is only population/rank weighting that disappears under the natural control, this proposed escape is killed before any spectral interpretation.

Apply the WP-158 character/primary-decomposition test before computing a spectrum. Fix one candidate block and one mixed statistic for the calculation; account for rank and logarithmic normalization artifacts explicitly. For any survivor, determine a norm bound or domain that persists as the two-prime box grows. Nonzero finite mixing with divergent normalization does not evade PC-177 at the operator level.

If a nonzero source-forced term survives, rewrite it in ordinary integer labels and determine whether it is an additive shifted relation, a finite--archimedean coupling, or a genuinely different interaction. That classification is more informative than immediately forming another scalar spectrum.

## Why it may matter

The current Prime-Circle frontier already says that a successful fixed weak-form route must create genuine coupling between valuation directions or leave the one-symbol setting. `PL-169` supplies a concrete control from another line: in a mature zeta asymptotic, arithmetic-specific information beyond the multiplicative diagonal reappears through an extra relation between different integers. Testing the first exact Prime-Circle pre-scalarization block against that pattern can either expose a real new carrier or close a broad class of vaguely defined “full Hessian/tensor” escapes quickly.

A positive result would not prove RH, but it would identify a source-derived interaction that survives the strongest separability control and therefore deserves theorem-level analysis. A negative result would materially strengthen the PC-177 boundary by showing that going back one layer before shell scalarization still does not restore mixed-prime structure.

## Evidence boundary

Established source-side facts are limited to the following. `PC-177` proves the separability/classicalization statement only for its exact scalar prime-power ratio symbol and the stated damped family; it does not classify the full vertexwise Hessian or nonlinear blocks. `PL-169` identifies additive shifted correlations as the first standard beyond-diagonal ratios mechanism under the hypotheses and prior-art interfaces stated there; it does not imply that Prime Circle contains such a coupling.

WP-158 closes character-based couplings only under its fixed torus-character hypotheses. A non-character response, its bounded realization, and its arithmetic consequence remain separate unproved steps. A control that transports the same arithmetic target can diagnose invariance without falsifying that target implication; specify the target whenever a matched response is used as a no-go.

Therefore the Prime-Circle transfer above is **only a proposed falsifiable test**. Do not treat additive structure, a nonzero mixed block, a new spectrum, or any RH consequence as established unless the destination-line calculation proves it from Prime-Circle geometry itself.
