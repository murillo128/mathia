# Does the complete preimage tube force a prime-specific nonlinear fiber-sector cocycle?

## Observation

The linear Prime-Circle refinement route is flat at exactly the point where a two-prime square might have supplied curvature. PC-052 and PC-053 identify the complete power-map preimage tube with a cyclic lift fiber and show that its canonical cotangent operator Fourier-diagonalizes into affine copies of base data; the corresponding embedded refinement correspondences therefore do not acquire a nonzero square defect merely by composing distinct prime refinements. This is the exact obstruction that killed the parent Visionary candidate rather than evidence for a surviving attack family.

Two persisted boundaries leave a narrower question. PC-024 classicalizes fixed finite nonlinearities of Fourier modes on one primitive shell, while the current Prime-Circle Mind still leaves genuinely cross-level, nonlinear anchored interactions open. The resolved transverse-solenoid clue likewise ends by requiring any successor to derive a coupling from embedded old/new geometry before exact-order diagonalization rather than assign an abstract transverse weight by hand.

## Research question

Let `p,q` be distinct refinement primes coprime to the base level `d`, and view the complete divisor-square preimage tube over `P_d^*` through its two lift coordinates, equivalently through the finite abelian fiber-translation group

\[
G_{p,q}\cong \mathbb Z/p\mathbb Z\times\mathbb Z/q\mathbb Z.
\]

After Fourier decomposition, write `E_chi` for the sector of character `chi` of `G_{p,q}`. Does the **embedded Prime-Circle chord/cotangent old-new geometry itself** canonically determine a bilinear or quadratic interaction `B` between distinct sectors, with no freely chosen coefficients or cocycle, such that

\[
B(E_\chi,E_\psi)\subseteq E_{\chi\psi}
\]

and the induced associativity defect

\[
A_B(x,y,z)=B(B(x,y),z)-B(x,B(y,z))
\]

is nonzero for the prime-derived tube while vanishing, becoming a coboundary/trivial reparameterization, or otherwise changing under a matched composite/conductor control that preserves the same fiber-translation representation?

If the interaction is naturally defined only up to phase, the corresponding coherent phase defect may be expressed as a group-cohomology class, but a nontrivial `H^2` or `H^3` class must be **derived from the embedded geometry** rather than selected from the abstract cohomology of `G_{p,q}`.

## Why it may matter

This asks for information in a different place from the killed linear-refinement commutator. PC-052/PC-053 show that the complete tube is linearly functorial after fiber Fourier decomposition; a geometry-forced nonlinear cross-sector tensor could retain relational information that is absent from each affine Fourier block separately. Conversely, proving that every natural equivariant coupling is functorial, cohomologically trivial, or identical on a conductor-matched control would close a concrete nonlinear escape hatch without reopening the dead linear route.

The question is local to `prime_circle`: it uses the exact power-map preimage tube, its embedded old/new roots, and the already-audited cotangent/chord geometry. It does not propose a new research line or an abstract group-cohomology model of RH.

## Decisive test

On the smallest admissible coprime two-refinement squares, first define and classify the bilinear/quadratic couplings that are **functorially generated by the persisted embedded Prime-Circle data** and equivariant under the full fiber translations. In the character basis, determine their allowed sector coefficients and compute `A_B` (or the coherent phase class when the product is only projectively associative).

Then run the same construction on a matched composite/conductor control preserving the fiber group, character multiplicities, and ambient cotangent/chord rules while removing the rational-prime birth distinction. Kill the direction if every geometry-forced coupling is associative/functorial, differs only by a coboundary or basis gauge, factors through the affine block data of PC-052/PC-053 or the fixed-mode arithmetic of PC-024, or is unchanged by the control. Continue only if one explicit canonical coupling has a nontrivial defect that survives these reductions and separates the prime tube from the matched control.

## Evidence boundary

No such coupling, associator, or prime-specific cohomology class has been established. Standard finite-group representation theory supplies the character-sector selection rule but does not select the coupling coefficients. Likewise, the existence of abstract cocycle twists is not evidence that Prime-Circle geometry carries one: A. O. Morris, *Projective Representations of Abelian Groups*, J. London Math. Soc. (2) 7 (1973), 235–238, DOI `10.1112/jlms/s2-7.2.235`, places projective twists in the standard multiplier/cohomology setting; I. Angiono and C. Galindo, *Pointed finite tensor categories over abelian groups*, Int. J. Math. 28 (2017), DOI `10.1142/S0129167X17500872`, makes the cohomology class of the associator explicit in the pointed setting; and O. Braunling, *Quinn's Formula and Abelian 3-Cocycles for Quadratic Forms*, Algebr. Represent. Theory 24 (2021), 1523–1555, DOI `10.1007/s10468-020-10001-1`, treats explicit abelian 3-cocycles/associators.

Directed searches across roots-of-unity power-map fibers, cotangent/Fourier decompositions, projective representations, and pointed-category associators did not locate the exact geometry-forced Prime-Circle construction above. That is only a bounded literature boundary, not a novelty claim. The decisive burden remains to derive `B` canonically from the embedded tube and to show a difference after the matched control; choosing a nontrivial cocycle by hand would fail the clue immediately.

## Research disposition

Initial triage found and killed the most canonical nonlinear coupling. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-087-preimage-tube-sector-product-cocycle-is-pure-gauge]] proves that intrinsic pointwise multiplication, when transported through the exact PC-053 sector gauges, acquires only the carry factor `D_jD_kD_[j+k]^{-1}`; this is an explicit 2-coboundary, its associator is zero, and the matched total-fiber control is identical.

[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-088-cotangent-leibniz-defect-associator-is-hochschild-gauge]] kills the next canonical operator-derived repair. The Leibniz/Nijenhuis defect of the complete-tube cotangent operator is exactly a Hochschild coboundary; its raw associator can be nonzero, but that associator is itself the next coboundary in the trivial deformation obtained by conjugating pointwise multiplication. In the PC-053 sector gauges its exact coefficient depends only on `d`, the total multiplier `m`, `A_d`, and the ordinary carry modulo `m`, so the matched total-fiber control is again identical.

[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-089-two-step-cotangent-shell-paths-collapse-to-endpoint-cyclotomic-potentials]] kills the simplest repair that explicitly restores exact-order shell projectors before composition. For any one intermediate shell, the two-step cotangent block factors exactly as the direct endpoint cotangent block multiplied on the left and right by scalar endpoint potentials, plus a rank-one cardinality term; for a primitive shell those potentials are `varphi(r)-2z Phi_r'(z)/Phi_r(z)`. Hence the natural two-path defect around a `p,q` divisor square can be nonzero but is entirely endpoint cyclotomic data, not a new shell curvature. The same identity holds for an arbitrary finite intermediate set, so primality does not enter the composition law itself.

The clue remains worth active investigation only in the narrower form: derive from embedded chord/cotangent old-new geometry a bilinear or quadratic tensor that is **not** gauge-equivalent to pointwise multiplication, is not the Leibniz/Nijenhuis-derived product of a single complete-tube linear operator, does not factor through the affine PC-052/053 block data, and does not reduce to a single exact-order shell inserted between two cotangent propagations as in PC-089. Any surviving candidate must use genuinely higher shell-aware relational memory (for example, at least two weighted intermediate shells or a distinct multi-operator tensor) and distinguish the prime-derived tube from a matched control without coefficients or cocycles chosen by hand.

[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-090-odd-shell-projected-cotangent-loops-vanish-by-reflection-chirality]] closes the first genuinely higher-memory scalar repair left by PC-089. Reflection on any conjugation-stable union of exact shells anticommutes with cotangent propagation and commutes with exact-shell projectors, so every cyclic trace or common-anchor matrix element of odd total reflection parity vanishes. In particular every unweighted odd-`K` loop, including the three-propagator/two-intermediate-shell candidate, is identically zero on prime shells and matched non-prime controls. The clue remains accepted only for even-parity multi-shell tensors or words, reflection-parity-cancelling geometry-forced weights, non-fixed-endpoint block observables, or genuinely different multioperator couplings; this is a stricter target, not evidence of novelty.

[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-092-finite-weighted-cotangent-shell-paths-collapse-to-endpoint-displacement-form]] closes the finite **serial weighted** cotangent branch that remained explicit after PC-089. The weighted three-point identity holds for an arbitrary diagonal profile on every intermediate shell, and the resulting endpoint-displacement class is closed under finite composition: any fixed serial word reduces to endpoint-weighted copies of the direct cotangent block plus separable rank-one terms and endpoint diagonals on returns, with representation size depending on path depth rather than shell cardinality. Therefore adding a second, third, or any fixed finite number of shell-local diagonal memories does not create an irreducible interior tensor. The clue remains accepted only beyond that class: non-serial/branching tensors, genuinely different intrinsic multioperator couplings, infinite-depth/limit constructions, or a cross-level use of the surviving endpoint profiles that escapes the PC-092 normal form.

[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-095-two-leg-cotangent-trees-with-disjoint-path-shells-collapse-to-endpoint-displacement-form]] closes the acyclic two-leg part of the branching escape on the domain actually justified by PC-092: the exact-order shell sets on the exposed path are pairwise disjoint, or form the separately covered direct-return configuration. Exact tree contraction turns every off-path subtree into a scalar message on its attachment vertex, after which PC-092 reduces the remaining weighted path to endpoint displacement. Repeated nonadjacent shells on the exposed path are explicitly outside this theorem. The clue therefore remains accepted for that repeated-shell/coincidence boundary, genuinely multi-leg branching outside the PC-094 disjoint class, residual cyclic/loopy networks, distinct intrinsic multioperator couplings, infinite-depth/limit constructions, or cross-level use of surviving endpoint profiles.

[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-094-finite-multileg-cotangent-trees-collapse-to-endpoint-forest-algebra]] closes the globally pairwise-disjoint finite acyclic **multi-leg** part. Hermite's classical arbitrary-valence cotangent product identity reduces a weighted hidden-shell star to one-body endpoint transforms with endpoint-only cotangent coefficients (plus the even-valence scalar term), and recursive hidden-vertex elimination sends every finite cotangent tree to a finite weighted-forest expression on the exposed legs only. The mechanism is prime-blind and leaves no irreducible hidden-shell tensor. The clue therefore remains accepted only for residual cyclic/loopy or repeated-shell/repeated-edge networks, genuinely distinct intrinsic multioperator couplings, infinite-depth/limit constructions, or cross-level use of the surviving endpoint profiles outside the finite-tree reductions.

[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-096-finite-disjoint-cotangent-multigraphs-collapse-to-confluent-endpoint-algebra]] closes the residual finite cyclic/loopy and parallel-edge topology when the participating vertex shells are globally pairwise disjoint. Repeated-pole partial fractions reduce every hidden cotangent star to finitely many one-body Cauchy jets on its neighbors, and the resulting Cauchy-multigraph algebra is closed under recursive hidden-vertex elimination; for an unweighted primitive shell those jets are derivatives of `-Phi_n'/Phi_n`. The mechanism is again prime-blind and has arbitrary disjoint finite point-set controls. The clue therefore remains accepted only for repeated-shell/coincidence networks, genuinely distinct intrinsic multioperator couplings, infinite-depth/limit constructions, or cross-level uses of the surviving endpoint jets/profiles that lie outside the finite disjoint-shell cotangent multigraph algebra.
