# Do irrational one-parameter radial rays avoid Hurwitz collapse only by becoming two-index decoders?

## Observation

PC-317 proves that every rational one-parameter radial ray of the nonlinear PC-316 field collapses to eventually quasipolynomial coefficient data and hence to a finite packet of rational-shift Hurwitz zeta functions. Its proof uses commensurability of the two effective radial rates. The finding therefore leaves a precise complementary case unclassified: a one-parameter ray whose effective rate ratio is irrational.

The unresolved issue is not merely whether the rational generating-function proof stops working. The original nonlinear field carries two independent indices, so removing commensurability may preserve much more of that pair data and thereby cross the opposite failure boundary: exact or unstable source decoding rather than useful compression.

## Research question

For the PC-316 nonlinear cross-level Poisson product restricted to a ray with irrational effective rate ratio, determine the exact information quotient of the one-parameter exponential signal. Does the irrational frequency geometry retain the full `(k,l)` coefficient array, making the scalar trace essentially a two-index decoder, or is there a genuine nonreconstructive invariant that avoids both PC-317's finite-Hurwitz recoding and the mesh-scale decoding failures already known in Prime Circle?

If a nonreconstructive quotient survives, identify what source-specific structure it retains and whether any independent analytic theorem could make that quotient zero-sensitive.

## Why it may matter

PC-317 closes the geometrically canonical rational scalarizations but does not show that one-dimensional radial reduction is intrinsically useless. Classifying the irrational case would separate two very different possibilities: rational commensurability may be the whole reason for the classical Hurwitz collapse, or every one-parameter escape may simply trade classical recoding for reconstruction of the richer source data. Either outcome would materially sharpen the remaining Prime-Circle frontier.

## Decisive test

Write the irrational-ray restriction explicitly as a one-variable exponential sum with frequencies generated by the two effective radial rates. Determine whether distinct index pairs can collide and, more importantly, whether the resulting spectral data determine the original two-index coefficient array in the exact and destination-relevant topology.

A decisive negative outcome is an injectivity/reconstruction theorem showing that avoiding rational commensurability merely turns the ray into source decoding, or an exact reduction to another already-classical family. A positive outcome requires an explicit nontrivial quotient: construct matched sources that agree on the retained scalar data while differing in the full pair array, then identify a source-forced statistic of that quotient not already covered by the finite-Fourier/Hurwitz/Mordell--Tornheim closures.

## Evidence boundary

PC-317 establishes only the rational-ray classicalization. It does not establish injectivity, stable inversion, nonreconstructive compression, or any zero-selection property for irrational rays. The possible decoding interpretation is therefore a research question, not a finding or intuition, until the exact frequency/inversion analysis is persisted by the Prime-Circle Research Watch.

## Research disposition

Outcome: supported

Resolved by:
- [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-318-irrational-radial-rays-are-tensor-decoders-and-barnes-zeta-packets.md]]

The irrational effective-rate case is exactly injective on the full periodic residue tensor: after removing the two known geometric denominators, the ray becomes a finite exponential polynomial with pairwise distinct frequencies and an invertible Vandermonde reconstruction. For the canonical normalized sources, row and column sums recover both source Fourier packets separately. Its Mellin transform is a finite classical Barnes double-zeta packet. Together with PC-317, this exhausts positive one-parameter radial rays of the PC-316 construction: rational rays classicalize by collisions, while irrational rays avoid collisions only by becoming lossless source decoders.
