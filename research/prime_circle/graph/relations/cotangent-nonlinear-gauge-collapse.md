# Canonical cotangent nonlinear repairs collapse to gauge or endpoint data

[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-087-preimage-tube-sector-product-cocycle-is-pure-gauge|PC-087]] tests the intrinsic pointwise product after the complete preimage-tube Fourier gauges of PC-053. Its apparent sector carry is an explicit `2`-coboundary: the product is strictly associative and does not distinguish a prime-factor refinement from a matched realization with the same total fiber multiplier.

[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-088-cotangent-leibniz-defect-associator-is-hochschild-gauge|PC-088]] then derives the most canonical operator-generated bilinear coupling, the cotangent Leibniz defect. The coupling is exactly a Hochschild coboundary and its raw associator belongs to the same trivial deformation obtained by conjugating pointwise multiplication; in the PC-053 coordinates it still depends only on total refinement and the ordinary carry, not on a prime factorization.

[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-089-two-step-cotangent-shell-paths-collapse-to-endpoint-cyclotomic-potentials|PC-089]] tests a shell-aware alternative before Fourier diagonalization. A single intermediate-shell cotangent contraction collapses exactly to the direct endpoint block, two endpoint scalar potentials, and a rank-one cardinality term; for a primitive shell the potentials are cyclotomic logarithmic derivatives.

Together these findings close three specific canonical nonlinear/two-step escapes without asserting a general no-go for every bilinear kernel or longer weighted path. The surviving source-backed directions must introduce genuinely different multi-shell interactions, longer weighted interior structure, Hardy higher-trace data, or global uniformization/monodromy rather than a transported pointwise product, its Leibniz defect, or one intermediate cotangent contraction.
