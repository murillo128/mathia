# Finite cotangent nonlinear networks collapse to gauge, endpoint, or classical scalar data

[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-087-preimage-tube-sector-product-cocycle-is-pure-gauge|PC-087]] and [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-088-cotangent-leibniz-defect-associator-is-hochschild-gauge|PC-088]] show that the first transported nonlinear products are explicit coboundaries rather than new refinement curvature. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-089-two-step-cotangent-shell-paths-collapse-to-endpoint-cyclotomic-potentials|PC-089]] then reduces one intermediate cotangent shell to direct endpoint blocks, endpoint cyclotomic potentials, and a rank-one term.

The newer shell-aware audit closes the obvious finite-memory enlargements. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-090-odd-shell-projected-cotangent-loops-vanish-by-reflection-chirality|PC-090]] kills odd-reflection-parity scalar words. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-091-fully-contracted-cotangent-shell-scalars-are-galois-rational|PC-091]] confines every finite fully contracted even exact-shell scalar to `Q`, so increasing shell count alone cannot hide a new cyclotomic field/phase. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-092-finite-weighted-cotangent-shell-paths-collapse-to-endpoint-displacement-form|PC-092]] closes finite serial propagation even with arbitrary shell-local diagonal weights: only endpoint-weighted direct blocks, separable terms, and return diagonals survive.

Branching does not reopen the same mechanism in the disjoint-shell regime. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-095-two-leg-cotangent-trees-with-disjoint-path-shells-collapse-to-endpoint-displacement-form|PC-095]] contracts off-path branches to one-body messages and reduces the exposed path to PC-092. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-094-finite-multileg-cotangent-trees-collapse-to-endpoint-forest-algebra|PC-094]] uses the classical Hermite cotangent product identity to eliminate arbitrary hidden vertices for pairwise-disjoint shell trees.

This is not a general cotangent no-go. The surviving source-backed boundary is narrower: repeated-shell/coincidence effects on exposed paths, cycles or repeated-edge powers, genuinely different intrinsic operators/tensors, infinite-depth/all-level limits, the separate Hardy/Hankel trace sector, and global uniformization/monodromy. No edge from those open classes is asserted until a canonical finding establishes it.
