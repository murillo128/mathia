# Canonical cotangent networks collapse to gauge, endpoint, finite-state, or uniform-band data

[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-087-preimage-tube-sector-product-cocycle-is-pure-gauge|PC-087]] and [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-088-cotangent-leibniz-defect-associator-is-hochschild-gauge|PC-088]] show that the first transported nonlinear products are explicit coboundaries rather than new refinement curvature. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-089-two-step-cotangent-shell-paths-collapse-to-endpoint-cyclotomic-potentials|PC-089]] then reduces one intermediate cotangent shell to direct endpoint blocks, endpoint cyclotomic potentials, and a rank-one term.

The finite shell-aware audit progressively closes the obvious memory enlargements. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-090-odd-shell-projected-cotangent-loops-vanish-by-reflection-chirality|PC-090]] kills odd-reflection-parity scalar words, [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-091-fully-contracted-cotangent-shell-scalars-are-galois-rational|PC-091]] confines finite fully contracted even exact-shell scalars to `Q`, and [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-092-finite-weighted-cotangent-shell-paths-collapse-to-endpoint-displacement-form|PC-092]] closes serial propagation with arbitrary shell-local diagonal weights. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-094-finite-multileg-cotangent-trees-collapse-to-endpoint-forest-algebra|PC-094]] and [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-095-two-leg-cotangent-trees-with-disjoint-path-shells-collapse-to-endpoint-displacement-form|PC-095]] extend the endpoint/forest collapse through finite branching in the audited disjoint-shell regimes.

The former repeated-shell exception is now closed for the canonical finite kernel. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-097-repeated-shell-cotangent-networks-collapse-to-collision-stratified-endpoint-algebra|PC-097]] decomposes coincidence patterns into equality strata and reduces arbitrary finite repeated-shell networks to endpoint data plus one-body diagonal-punctured Cauchy jets. Reusing a primitive shell finitely many times therefore does not preserve a hidden interior tensor.

Nor does the most direct infinite-depth repair survive on fixed support. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-098-fixed-conductor-cotangent-depth-zeta-is-finite-state-rational|PC-098]] shows that repeated canonical cotangent transfer on any fixed finite union of shells stays inside a finite-dimensional matrix algebra; Cayley--Hamilton makes every depth sequence recurrent and every depth generating function rational. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-099-growing-complete-cotangent-tube-spectrum-converges-to-uniform-bands|PC-099]] then closes the canonical complete-preimage growing-state limit under its natural linear scaling: the empirical spectrum converges to finitely many uniform bands determined by the fixed base spectrum.

This is still not a universal cotangent no-go. Surviving source-backed classes include incomplete or selectively growing conductor families, genuinely different intrinsic operators or higher tensors, singular renormalizations outside the natural finite-state/linear scale, cross-level organizations of the surviving endpoint jets, the separate Hardy/Hankel trace sector, and global uniformization/monodromy. No edge from those classes is asserted until a canonical finding establishes it.
