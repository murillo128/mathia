# Compatible-circle solenoid and the proper-scale obstruction

[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-064-compatible-circle-refinement-is-the-adelic-solenoid|PC-064]] identifies the compatible-circle inverse limit with the classical arithmetic solenoid, and [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-065-solenoid-leafwise-laplacian-has-dense-rational-square-spectrum|PC-065]] shows that its canonical leafwise spectrum is dense, noncompact and non-trace-class.

The subsequent source-backed obstruction stack narrows progressively broader scalar properizations:

- [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-066-transverse-profinite-symmetry-fixes-exact-order-projectors-not-rh-hamiltonian|PC-066]] and [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-067-compatible-inverse-square-chord-energy-resolves-order-but-not-rh-scale|PC-067]] show that intrinsic transverse symmetry and compatible chord energy recover exact order without fixing an RH scale.
- [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-068-regular-commuting-leaf-fiber-calculus-cannot-be-compact|PC-068]] rules out regular commuting scalar leaf/fiber calculus as a compact-resolvent repair.
- [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-069-exact-solenoid-dilation-covariance-forbids-compact-resolvent|PC-069]] and [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-070-additive-solenoid-dilation-covariance-also-forbids-compact-resolvent|PC-070]] close the exact homogeneous and additive scalar dilation-covariance routes.
- [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-071-homeomorphic-dilation-covariance-cannot-have-compact-resolvent|PC-071]] extends the no-go to every real homeomorphic scalar spectral law, while [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-073-bounded-drift-scalar-dilation-covariance-still-forbids-compact-resolvent|PC-073]] rules out the regular non-bijective bounded-drift escape.
- [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-074-projective-height-properization-is-primitive-lattice-zeta|PC-074]] shows that projective/global height really can restore compact resolvent, but its spectral zeta is the classical primitive-lattice/projective-height zeta rather than a new zero selector.

The supported boundary is therefore regular scalar covariance/properization, not every solenoidal construction. Singular, operator-valued, one-sided, representation-changing or otherwise intrinsically forced non-scalar mechanisms remain outside this relation.
