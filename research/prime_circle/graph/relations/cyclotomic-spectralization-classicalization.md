# Natural finite cyclotomic spectralizations remain finite-state or classical

The boundary-potential route does not escape single-shell classicalization by ordinary finite-section spectralization. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-121-cyclotomic-toeplitz-sections-are-finite-period-quasipolynomials|PC-121]] shows that the scalar Toeplitz determinants of `|Phi_n|^2` are finite-period quasipolynomials in section size; their leading data are the classical cyclotomic discriminant and their generating functions have only root-of-unity poles.

Coupling shells coherently before compression also closes in the canonical one-amplitude model. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-122-coherent-cross-shell-toeplitz-determinant-collapses-to-resultant-and-zero|PC-122]] identifies an exact finite-dimensional rank obstruction: after a fixed threshold the block Toeplitz determinant is identically zero, and the last nonsingular two-shell section is a cyclotomic resultant. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-123-coherent-toeplitz-pseudodeterminant-incoherentizes-to-banded-scalar-recurrence|PC-123]] then shows that deleting the forced nullspace by pseudodeterminant removes the off-diagonal coherence and leaves a fixed-band scalar recurrence, with the two-shell case factoring through the same resultant data.

Two other canonical spectral parameters land directly on classical cyclotomic objects. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-124-primitive-shell-opuc-cmv-spectralization-is-classical-ramanujan-cyclotomic-data|PC-124]] makes the primitive-root shell an OPUC/CMV spectral measure, but the CMV eigenvalues are exactly the input primitive roots, the terminal polynomial is `Phi_n`, and the moments/discriminant are Ramanujan/cyclotomic data. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-125-relative-scale-resultant-spectralization-is-purely-cyclotomic|PC-125]] promotes relative shell scale to a complex parameter, yet its entire divisor is finite cyclotomic torsion with multiplicities given by finite Ramanujan correlations.

These findings strengthen G1 soft pruning and narrow G2's finite coherent escapes without closing genuinely growing/infinite cross-level organization. A surviving Prime-Circle spectralization must retain information not exhausted by a fixed finite shell polynomial carrier, one coherent amplitude channel, ordinary finite-section quotienting, or a relative scale whose collisions stay inside finite cyclotomic torsion.
