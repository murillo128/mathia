# Hardy/Hilbert shell collapse and relative trace sector

The canonical interior/exterior Hardy lift is genuinely nonlocal, but its finite-shell operator algebra is now sharply classified.

- [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-075-cyclotomic-log-hankel-core-is-universal-hilbert-channels|PC-075]] decomposes each primitive-shell Hankel operator into universal Hilbert channels plus a trace-class arithmetic remainder.
- [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-076-hardy-hilbert-relative-trace-is-parity-twisted-von-mangoldt|PC-076]] and [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-077-hardy-hilbert-second-relative-trace-is-radical-divisor-data|PC-077]] reduce the first two relative readouts to endpoint/Mangoldt and radical/divisor data.
- [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-078-repeated-prime-hardy-refinement-is-signed-radical-tensor-inflation|PC-078]] and [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-079-hardy-refinement-is-commuting-moebius-hilbert-dilation|PC-079]] show repeated-prime inflation and new-prime refinement are universal tensor/commuting Möbius mechanisms; insertion order is lost.
- [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-080-distinct-hardy-shell-products-are-trace-class-resultants|PC-080]] and [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-081-finite-hardy-shell-algebra-is-a-wedge-of-hilbert-bands|PC-081]] show distinct-shell products are trace class, their first mixed trace is the classical cyclotomic resultant, and every finite algebraic cross-shell essential-spectrum mechanism collapses in the Calkin algebra to universal one-shell Hilbert bands.
- [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-082-higher-hardy-cyclic-traces-are-critical-cyclotomic-cone-periods|PC-082]] proves that higher cyclic traces contain information beyond pairwise resultants, but identifies them with critical cyclotomic cone-period data rather than a new spectral parameter.
- [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-086-cyclically-separated-hardy-root-words-length-at-least-two-converge-without-abel-regularization|PC-086]] is the corrected replacement for the withdrawn PC-083: cyclically separated root words have ordinary trace-class finite-section convergence only for word length at least two; the separated one-letter scalar cutoff may converge without making the operator trace class.
- [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-084-all-finite-mixed-hardy-shell-words-converge-in-trace-norm|PC-084]] strengthens the finite completed-shell statement to every nonconstant finite word, which converges in trace norm under the natural finite sections.
- [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-085-common-repeated-prime-depth-is-universal-mixed-hardy-tensor-inflation|PC-085]] shows common repeated-prime depth across a mixed word is only universal tensor inflation and cannot generate new finite mixed-shell spectral shape.

Thus the finite essential-spectrum route is closed to universal Hilbert bands, while the finite trace-class sector is nontrivial but increasingly classicalized and canonically finite-section defined. The source-backed frontier is an all-level/infinite relative construction or a new cross-level invariant not reducible to endpoint/divisor/resultant/common-depth data.
