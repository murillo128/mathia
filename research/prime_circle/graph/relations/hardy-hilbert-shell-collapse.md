# Hardy/Hilbert shell collapse and conductor-universal boundary

[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-075-cyclotomic-log-hankel-core-is-universal-hilbert-channels|PC-075]]--[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-114-new-prime-hardy-two-scale-corrector-is-ramanujan-reflection-tensor|PC-114]] reduce the canonical finite-shell, fixed-shell and first conductor/two-scale sectors to cyclotomic periods, Hilbert/Carleman defects and Ramanujan reflection data; a fixed trace-class Fredholm determinant is also too sparse in zero density to realize the Riemann divisor.

The conductor limit left by PC-114 is now closed for the exact corrector at every ordinary scalar normalization of interest. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-115-growing-prime-two-scale-corrector-has-zero-free-gaussian-determinant|PC-115]]--[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-117-arbitrary-conductor-joint-hardy-corrector-has-zero-free-gaussian-determinant|PC-117]] show that prime, joint-prime and arbitrary composite old-conductor limits all produce the same zero-free Gaussian regularized determinant after the Hilbert--Schmidt normalization, while the unnormalized strong boundary becomes noncompact reflection-type data rather than a discrete divisor.

Representation changes do not rescue that divisor. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-118-bounded-similarity-and-isometric-unfolding-preserve-hardy-corrector-divisor|PC-118]] proves that arbitrary bounded similarities and isometric unfoldings preserve the determinant divisor exactly. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-119-bounded-two-sided-hardy-preconditioning-cannot-retain-a-finite-fredholm-divisor|PC-119]] allows genuinely operator-changing two-sided maps but shows that any uniformly bounded effective preconditioning still leaves the operator norm infinitesimal and all finite Fredholm zeros escaping compact sets.

The surviving Prime-Circle boundary is therefore narrower than "non-scalar conductor dependence": it must cross a genuinely singular/unbounded operator scale, alter the construction before the PC-113 corrector split, use nonlinear joint all-shell organization, or move to another intrinsic operator/global uniformization mechanism. Ordinary bounded coordinate changes, compressions and residue weights are no longer distinct escapes.
