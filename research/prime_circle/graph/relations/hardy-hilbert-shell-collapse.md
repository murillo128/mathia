# Hardy/Hilbert shell collapse and resultant remainder

The canonical interior/exterior Hardy lift is genuinely nonlocal, but the current findings classify much of its surviving arithmetic.

- [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-075-cyclotomic-log-hankel-core-is-universal-hilbert-channels|PC-075]] decomposes each primitive-shell Hankel operator into universal Hilbert channels plus a trace-class arithmetic remainder.
- [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-076-hardy-hilbert-relative-trace-is-parity-twisted-von-mangoldt|PC-076]] reduces the first relative trace to the two scalar cyclotomic endpoints, while [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-077-hardy-hilbert-second-relative-trace-is-radical-divisor-data|PC-077]] reduces the normalized second moment to radical/divisor data.
- [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-078-repeated-prime-hardy-refinement-is-signed-radical-tensor-inflation|PC-078]] shows repeated-prime refinement is only signed finite tensor inflation.
- [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-079-hardy-refinement-is-commuting-moebius-hilbert-dilation|PC-079]] shows new-prime refinement is a commuting Möbius calculus, so prime insertion order and refinement holonomy disappear in this linear branch.
- [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-080-distinct-hardy-shell-products-are-trace-class-resultants|PC-080]] proves distinct shell products are trace class and identifies their first mixed trace with the classical cyclotomic resultant.
- [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-081-finite-hardy-shell-algebra-is-a-wedge-of-hilbert-bands|PC-081]] then closes every finite algebraic cross-shell essential-spectrum mechanism: mixed monomials vanish in the Calkin algebra and the finite shell algebra is a wedge of universal Hilbert bands.

The remaining source-backed Hardy frontier lies below essential spectrum, in trace-class higher cyclic data/determinants, or in a genuinely new cross-level operator not algebraically generated by the completed single-shell operators.
