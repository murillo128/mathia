# Hardy/Hilbert shell collapse and higher-cycle period boundary

The canonical interior/exterior Hardy lift is genuinely nonlocal, but its finite-shell operator algebra is sharply classified.

[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-075-cyclotomic-log-hankel-core-is-universal-hilbert-channels|PC-075]] decomposes each primitive-shell Hankel operator into universal Hilbert channels plus a trace-class arithmetic remainder. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-076-hardy-hilbert-relative-trace-is-parity-twisted-von-mangoldt|PC-076]] and [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-077-hardy-hilbert-second-relative-trace-is-radical-divisor-data|PC-077]] reduce the first two relative readouts to endpoint/Mangoldt and radical/divisor data. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-078-repeated-prime-hardy-refinement-is-signed-radical-tensor-inflation|PC-078]]--[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-081-finite-hardy-shell-algebra-is-a-wedge-of-hilbert-bands|PC-081]] show that repeated-prime inflation and new-prime refinement are universal tensor/commuting Möbius mechanisms, distinct-shell products are trace class, and finite cross-shell essential-spectrum information collapses in the Calkin algebra to universal one-shell Hilbert bands.

[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-082-higher-hardy-cyclic-traces-are-critical-cyclotomic-cone-periods|PC-082]] proves that higher cyclic traces contain information beyond pairwise resultants. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-086-cyclically-separated-hardy-root-words-length-at-least-two-converge-without-abel-regularization|PC-086]] supplies the corrected trace-class boundary for cyclically separated root words, while [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-084-all-finite-mixed-hardy-shell-words-converge-in-trace-norm|PC-084]] and [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-085-common-repeated-prime-depth-is-universal-mixed-hardy-tensor-inflation|PC-085]] fix the finite-section and common-depth behavior.

The first genuinely higher period is now classicalized. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-100-cubic-hardy-cycle-periods-are-cyclotomic-multiple-polylogarithms|PC-100]] reduces every separated cubic root-channel trace to weight-three cyclotomic multiple-polylogarithm data at the corresponding cyclotomic level. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-101-hardy-cycle-index-map-has-odd-even-incidence-fibers|PC-101]] then classifies the denominator-index map for every cycle length: odd cycles have an index-two lattice image with unique fibers after parity, while even cycles acquire one alternating kernel and an explicit degree-one fiber multiplicity. The even rank drop therefore preserves critical homogeneity rather than generating a subcritical new invariant.

Thus finite essential-spectrum mechanisms are universal, the cubic higher-trace novelty boundary lands in classical cyclotomic period theory, and arbitrary cycle lengths have an exact incidence-fiber model. The source-backed frontier is narrower: determine whether the `k>=4` critical cyclotomic conical boundary values themselves reduce to known multiple-polylogarithm/`L`-value packages, or whether a genuinely infinite all-shell construction organizes them into an arithmetic invariant not already fixed by the finite cyclotomic period algebra.
