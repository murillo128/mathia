# Hardy/Hilbert shell collapse and conductor-universal boundary

The canonical interior/exterior Hardy lift is genuinely nonlocal, but the source-backed finite-shell and canonical conductor-limit sectors are now sharply constrained.

[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-075-cyclotomic-log-hankel-core-is-universal-hilbert-channels|PC-075]]--[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-086-cyclically-separated-hardy-root-words-length-at-least-two-converge-without-abel-regularization|PC-086]] reduce finite essential structure to universal Hilbert bands and make the nonconstant mixed finite sector trace class under the natural finite sections. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-100-cubic-hardy-cycle-periods-are-cyclotomic-multiple-polylogarithms|PC-100]]--[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-106-all-single-shell-hardy-relative-moments-are-cyclotomic-hyperlogarithmic|PC-106]] now close the remaining ordinary finite trace loopholes much further: quartic and separated cycles are cyclotomic hyperlogarithmic, every finite nonconstant mixed shell trace is a cyclotomic multiple-polylogarithmic period, and the fixed-shell moment tower stays in that classical arithmetic sector.

The most direct fixed-shell Fredholm escape also closes at the scale needed for a Riemann divisor. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-107-trace-class-hardy-determinant-cannot-have-riemann-zero-density|PC-107]] shows that a fixed trace-class Hardy remainder has Fredholm zero count `o(R)`, incompatible with Riemann--von Mangoldt density under an asymptotically linear normalization.

The large-conductor escape is likewise increasingly universal. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-108-prime-conductor-hardy-remainder-converges-to-universal-hilbert-corner|PC-108]]--[[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-112-moebius-birth-dilation-cocycle-is-radical-unitary|PC-112]] identify the canonical fixed-coordinate, microlocal and affine-scale limits with Hilbert/Carleman discretization defects, trace-class dilation cocycles and radical-unitary birth data. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-113-one-new-prime-hardy-microlocal-limit-is-reflection-tensor-inflation|PC-113]] shows that the canonical one-new-prime strong limit only reflection-inflates the old fixed-conductor residual. [[research/prime_circle/findings/PC-114-new-prime-hardy-two-scale-corrector-is-ramanujan-reflection-tensor|PC-114]] then recovers the strongly invisible two-scale mass and identifies it as a classical Ramanujan reflection tensor times the same universal Carleman--Hilbert defect, again with a nuclear zero divisor.

The source-backed frontier has therefore moved beyond finite traces, fixed-shell determinants and the canonical affine/conductor microlocalizations. A survivor must be formed jointly across levels before these reductions, use a genuinely non-affine or otherwise intrinsic all-shell organization, or extract source-specific norm/Schatten information that remains after the strongest Hilbert/Carleman/Ramanujan matched controls. Global uniformization/monodromy remains outside this relation.
