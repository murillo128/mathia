This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-circle intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Leave the cyclotomic Cauchy-packet algebra through a source-forced cross-prime relation

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-011-scalar-fourier-zero-existence-is-deformation-dependent`, `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra`.

PC-224--PC-232 show that canonical refinement networks, fixed-base packets, repeated-prime depth, normalized residue branching, chord compression, pointwise multiplication, Fourier-dual convolution, and arbitrary finite operation depth remain inside explicit squarefree cyclotomic/Cauchy packet algebras. Growing squarefree conductor enlarges classical torsion support but does not manufacture a new source relation.

PC-233--PC-234 close two further normalization escapes. Fixed-base Cauchy packets under bounded macroscopic multiplier scaling freeze to regular-polygon/chord multipliers, and prime-base packet normalization itself reduces to binary cyclotomic chord data. Passing from a fixed base to a prime base or taking bounded continuum-style packet limits therefore does not leave the same classical scalar closure.

The surviving construction must use information **outside the canonical packet shift/scalar algebra**: source-forced nontranslation-invariant operators, mode mixing, a genuinely non-frozen scaling law, coupled amplitudes/normalizations, interacting multi-prime state, or another relation whose content is not reducible to cyclotomic torsion and chord data.

## Treat fixed-base refinement, finite scalar fusion, and bounded packet normalization as controls

Pole-residue branching, chord compression, pointwise packet products, bilateral packet convolution, finite cross-shell fusion, bounded macroscopic multipliers, and prime-base scalar normalization all remain classicalized in the current evidence. A genuine continuation must prove that its final observable survives this complete packet-algebra control and is not merely denser cyclotomic data in another coordinate.
