## Add a destination theorem that survives the autocorrelation quotient, fixed-conductor edge-gap law and growing-conductor backgrounds

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-037-fixed-complexity-deflation-cannot-remove-canonical-green-background`.

PC-295--PC-315 close scalar one-level and finite-Fourier packet routes: mesh-scale recovery is already a generic cyclic decoder, direct bilinear fusion stays in classical periodic Dirichlet packets, and canonical scalar eigensectors can have abundant zeros in `Re s>1`.

PC-316--PC-321 move before scalar compression and classify the main multivariable/radial alternatives. Rational radial restrictions collapse to finite Hurwitz packets, irrational restrictions decode the full source tensor into Barnes double-zeta data, weak genuine coupling inherits an off-critical divisor, and sufficiently strong additive coupling becomes zero-free on the right polyhalf-plane. More variables do not supply a zero selector by themselves.

PC-322--PC-325 classify the canonical Green lift. The scalar Green slice lands on universal Hilbert criticality; the full operator is one universal Hilbert channel plus a trace-class defect; one-residue controls already produce classical off-band relative zeros; and independent Fourier phases are removable by simultaneous left/right unitary gauge. For the finite Fourier sources the complete separable Green/relative spectral package therefore factors through source magnitudes, equivalently cyclic autocorrelations.

PC-326 adds a second, independent obstruction at fixed conductors. The zeros of the perturbation determinant are exactly the discrete eigenvalues outside the universal Hilbert band, and trace class forces `sum dist(lambda_j,[-E,E]) <= ||V||_1`. The relative zero divisor therefore has an `ell^1` band-edge gap budget and is too sparse for direct identification with the Riemann ordinates, including natural reciprocal/Cayley edge compactifications.

PC-327 audits the first growing-conductor escape. After normalizing by the intrinsic band scale `c=sqrt(mu_a mu_b)`, generalized-Hilbert offsets force logarithmic loss of uniform `S_2` and `S_1` control for every nonzero centered source pair. That divergence is source-independent and therefore not an arithmetic discriminator.

PC-328 shows that the canonical prime-support source has a much larger second background. Its fixed Fourier modes satisfy `a_q(h)=C_h/log q+O_h(log(q)^(-2))`, while the intrinsic Hilbert-band scale is only `mu_q^(1/2)~sqrt(log q/q)`. The `(1,1)` Green coefficient alone then forces band-normalized discrete outlier pairs growing at least like `sqrt(qr)/(log q log r)^(3/2)`; for comparable conductors this is `Q/(log Q)^3`. The leading amplitude is classical PNT/Li data, and PC-325 supplies exact non-arithmetic phase controls with the same full Green spectrum. The raw outliers are spectral facts, not RH signals.

PC-329 closes the proposed **fixed low-mode deflation** repair. Removing any fixed set of periodic source residues leaves another fixed Fourier mode with the same `1/log q` PNT scale. More strongly, even an arbitrary conductor-dependent correction of fixed rank `R` cannot remove the background: the first `(R+1)x(R+1)` normalized Cauchy/Hilbert block has full rank and retains an outlier lower bound of the same `sqrt(qr)/(log q log r)^(3/2)` scale, with an `R`-dependent constant. The problem is not that the wrong finite packet was frozen; **no fixed-complexity subtraction can make the canonical Green family tight.**

The surviving Green question is therefore a genuinely growing-complexity renormalization problem. A viable conductor limit must remove or incorporate the universal shifted-Hilbert singularity and subtract a PNT/Li background whose effective rank grows with conductor, or subtract an entire source-derived smooth profile. Only after that operation is proved tight may a residual be tested against exact phase-gauge and smooth matched controls. It still needs an independent destination theorem connecting the surviving sign, divisor or spectrum to a zeta-zero restriction.

## Demand a zero-sensitive destination theorem before interpreting source distinguishability

Prime Circle now exhibits many forms of retained structure without zero selection: lossless cyclic decoding, multivariable source tensors, irrational radial reconstruction, analytic continuation, a universal Hilbert `1/2` boundary, trace-class relative data, source autocorrelation, discrete Green bound states, a universal generalized-Hilbert conductor singularity and PNT-driven escaping canonical outliers. PC-329 adds a methodological warning: failure of every fixed-rank renormalization does not make the divergence more arithmetic; it only shows that the smooth source background itself has growing effective complexity.

The next proposal should state the exact source-sensitive zero/sign statement first, identify the matched controls and every universal or smooth source background it must beat, specify the complexity of the subtraction allowed to remove those backgrounds, and only then build the representation or operator that is supposed to force it. Source distinguishability and spectral richness count only after the surviving invariant is both control-resistant and connected to the Riemann zero restriction.
