## Turn ordered signed radial interior into a source-forced self-adjoint structure before endpoint or Mellin collapse

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-anchor-required-but-local-anchor-is-classical` and `MI-009-signed-radial-interior-survives-only-in-ordered-structure-before-mellin-or-self-adjoint-boundary-collapse`.

PC-179 confirms that the cyclotomic inward flux is a real prime-power selector, but shellwise Mellinization is exactly the classical eta/zeta transform and every shell carries the same nontrivial zero set. PC-180 then closes the simplest cross-shell repair: for the ordered flux--potential matrix, the entire symmetric part is `Lambda Lambda^T/2`, so every constant self-adjoint shell mixer is already a von-Mangoldt endpoint form. The only radial interior left at this level is the antisymmetric ordered sector, which has zero real quadratic form by itself.

A survivor must derive from Prime-Circle geometry an intrinsic radial-depth-dependent/nonlocal coupling, a second noncommuting/skew structure, or another operation that uses the ordered sector before symmetrization. The decisive control is whether the resulting self-adjoint/sign margin survives a mixed-prime shell and is not inherited from the `n=2` eta representation or the endpoint `Lambda` vector.

## Couple prime directions before closure, or leave the fixed refinement-covariant boundary category

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-compatible-completion-lacks-coercive-rh-scale`.

PC-165--PC-178 squeeze fixed refinement-covariant coefficients, distributions, weak forms, and multiplicative-Toeplitz symbols into a singular unbounded corner. Otherwise leave the category through shell/level dependence, cross-level relations, renormalized boundary data, or nonlinear/noncommuting radial--arithmetic coupling.
