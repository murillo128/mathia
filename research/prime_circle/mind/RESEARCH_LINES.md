## Add structure beyond single-level decoding and scalar classical mixed-conductor packets

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-028-canonical-mixed-packet-fails-finite-fourier-self-duality`.

PC-295--PC-309 progressively separate phase retention, classical Fourier/polyspectral data and stable source separation. PC-310 classifies the mesh boundary layer `1-r=Theta(1/q)`: the sampled Poisson/radial-flux field is an invertible cyclic multiplier on centered source data, with condition number `Theta(q)`. The first non-collapsing single-level radial layer is therefore exactly where the observable has paid enough sensitivity to decode the finite cyclic source.

PC-311 closes the most direct cross-level escape. Bilinearly pairing two centered Poisson fields at conductors `q,r` passes to the common refinement `Z/(qr)Z`; the periodic unit-supported coefficient has a finite character expansion, and the double Mellin transform is a finite bundle of ordinary Dirichlet functions `L(s+t,chi)`. CRT factorization splits the coefficients into one-level transforms. The apparent two-conductor coupling therefore collapses to classical periodic arithmetic in the single combined variable `s+t`.

PC-312 closes the obvious scalar functional-equation repair of that bundle. For the canonical centered prime-support sources, the exact-period coefficient `d` vanishes at the nonunit residues `q,r`, while its normalized finite Fourier dual takes the nonzero value `2/sqrt(qr)` at both residues. Hence `d` is not a finite-Fourier eigenvector, and the classical degree-one periodic-Dirichlet criterion excludes a source-native scalar Riemann-type functional equation for the canonical packet.

The live construction must now keep structure that is destroyed by this scalar periodic reduction: a source-forced coupling of `d` with its Fourier dual, a genuinely vector/matrix-valued mixed object, nonlinear coupling before cyclic diagonalization, or a non-circulant pointed/shell-dependent interaction. Any such carrier still needs an independently justified zero-sensitive destination theorem; manually Fourier-symmetrizing the scalar packet is not source-native progress.

## Demand a destination theorem before interpreting source distinguishability

Sublinear/pre-mesh observations are stable but collapse matched sources; mesh-scale single-level observations separate by decoding the source itself; direct bilinear mixed-conductor pairing classicalizes to finite character data; and its canonical scalar coefficient then fails the finite-Fourier self-duality needed for a degree-one functional equation. A viable observable must state what additional quantity survives **before** these reductions and how it reaches a zero-sensitive destination without an uncontrolled inverse.

Future mixed-conductor proposals should be reduced on the common refinement first, then audited for the exact Fourier-duality category they retain. Nonlinear proposals should be inspected before any Gram/scalarization step that might erase the claimed nonseparable structure.
