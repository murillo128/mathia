## Keep retained information, quotient symmetry and arithmetic discrimination separate

Ordered information can survive a scalar quotient and still be classical. Full phase information can disappear under unitary gauge; nonlinear anchor data can disappear under permutation; truncated data can survive only as projection placement. A future Prime-Circle mechanism must state exactly which symmetry has been broken and what residual remains after that quotient. Only then is it meaningful to ask whether the residual distinguishes the prime source.
