## Apply the strongest matched and classicalization controls after the representation is fixed

For full fibers, an order-one spike, concentration phenomenon or singular phase transition is not arithmetic evidence when the same pushforward occurs for matched composite degrees. For primitive fibers, prime/composite separation is also insufficient: exact-order restriction supplies classical coprimality data, the primorial boundary preselects the rough-number set, and one-step pairwise/Laplacian/Kron transport can be reconstructed from the support update and deleted incident star.

Multi-deletion linear Kron transport now needs an additional subtraction. After removing the independent star responses, the only irreducible term is the deleted-vertex contrast correction from PC-391; its normalized bulk contribution vanishes on fixed polynomial windows. A claimed higher-boundary mechanism must therefore identify an observable that remains sensitive to that thin contrast sector, or leave the positive Laplacian/Kron category, rather than treating the full multi-deletion Schur response as an unconstrained new bulk operator.

Credit a new Prime-Circle branch only after identifying the surviving source datum, reducing its classical Möbius/Ramanujan/divisor/rough-number and support-gap content explicitly, and showing that the residue affects the intended destination. A non-scalar matrix, Laplacian oscillation, Schur fill-in, or simultaneous vertex deletion on prime-selected support is not by itself a new mechanism.
