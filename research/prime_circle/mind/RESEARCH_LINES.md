## Add a destination theorem that survives the autocorrelation quotient, classical short-interval variance boundary, exact binary complement control and source-native Hilbert background

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-039-green-hardy-band-control-is-short-interval-variance-in-fourier-coordinates`.

PC-295--PC-315 close scalar one-level and finite-Fourier packet routes: mesh-scale recovery is already a generic cyclic decoder, direct bilinear fusion stays in classical periodic Dirichlet packets, and canonical scalar eigensectors can have abundant zeros in `Re s>1`.

PC-316--PC-321 classify the main multivariable/radial alternatives. Rational radial restrictions collapse to finite Hurwitz packets, irrational restrictions decode the full source tensor into Barnes double-zeta data, weak genuine coupling inherits an off-critical divisor, and sufficiently strong additive coupling becomes zero-free on the right polyhalf-plane. More variables do not supply a zero selector by themselves.

PC-322--PC-326 classify the canonical Green lift: one universal Hilbert channel plus trace-class relative data, phase-gauge/autocorrelation quotienting, and an `ell^1` band-edge gap budget for discrete relative zeros. These are strong structural statements but do not produce the density or zero selector required for the Riemann divisor.

PC-327--PC-329 expose the growing-conductor background. Band normalization has a universal shifted-Hilbert singularity; the prime source adds much larger PNT-scale low Fourier amplitudes; and no fixed set of modes or fixed-rank conductor-dependent correction can remove the resulting escaping Green outliers. Fixed-complexity deflation is therefore impossible.

PC-330 closes the most natural smooth growing-complexity repair. Subtracting the full Li/PNT profile removes fixed low-frequency mean structure but leaves the canonical Green Hilbert channel asymptotically unchanged because the sparse prime source has RMS `log q/q`, while the diffuse Li-grid control has only `1/q`. The residual retains asymptotically all of the sparse `L^2` mass; energy removed from fixed low modes is relocated to growing frequencies rather than canceled.

PC-331 isolates the exact source-sensitive excess above the universal logarithmic floor. For comparable conductors `q<=r<=2q`, the band-normalized Hilbert--Schmidt Green defect is `log q` plus a positive Hankel/Hardy-square energy of the Fourier magnitude-square profiles. PC-332 gives its exact multiscale form: if `Gamma_a(H)=[1/(H mu(a))] sum_(1<=i<=H)|a_hat(i)|^2`, then the excess is equivalent up to absolute constants to `sum_m Gamma_a(2^m) Gamma_b(2^m)`. A superlogarithmic excess therefore forces an explicit dyadic band with anomalous magnitude concentration, but it still contains no phase information.

PC-333 identifies the remaining band estimate itself. Cyclic Parseval writes moving-window discrepancy variance as the same Fourier magnitude energy with a Dirichlet-kernel weight. At reciprocal scales `H asymp q/L`, uniform control `E_q(H) lesssim H mu_q^res` and the natural short-window variance bound imply each other up to absolute constants, with Fejer/hard cutoffs interchangeable at neighboring scales. After undoing the source normalization this is the finite-cyclic analogue of the classical short-interval prime-variance/Selberg-integral problem.

PC-334 closes the obvious discrete-source escape from the magnitude quotient. For a binary support and its literal complement, subtracting the correspondingly complemented Li baseline makes the centered normalized residuals exact scalar negatives. Hence the prime support and nonprime complement have identical normalized Fourier-power profiles, dyadic Green--Hardy energies, cyclic window variances and source-normalized two-level Green/Hilbert spectral data. The normalized magnitude-only branch therefore has an exact matched control **inside the literal binary source category**.

The scalar Green--Hardy route has consequently reached both a **classical autocorrelation boundary** and an exact binary matched-control boundary. A substantive prime-circle advance must retain information lost by the magnitude quotient — for example through genuinely nonseparable phase coupling — or use a source amplitude/normalization that the complemented control cannot match, and then prove an independent zero-sensitive destination theorem. Merely proving the surviving scalar band estimate by ordinary analytic-number-theory means would establish a classical short-interval discrepancy theorem in Fourier coordinates, not a new geometric RH mechanism.

## Treat Gamma-strip-admissible scalar Mellin measures as a classified classical channel

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-009-signed-radial-interior-survives-only-in-ordered-structure-before-mellin-or-self-adjoint-boundary-collapse`, `MI-024-exact-cyclotomic-recovery-can-be-algebraically-lossless-and-analytically-useless`, `MI-040-endpoint-mellin-singularities-form-a-classical-von-mangoldt-jet`, `MI-041-compact-mellin-frequency-support-still-collapses-to-the-von-mangoldt-endpoint`, `MI-042-gamma-strip-admissibility-collapses-many-global-mellin-measures-to-the-von-mangoldt-endpoint`.

PC-179 gives the exact signed cyclotomic radial flux with Mellin transform `R_n(s)=-Gamma(s)zeta(s)n^(1-s) prod_(p|n)(1-p^(s-1))`, and the singular endpoint identity `R_n(1)=Lambda(n)`. PC-183--PC-187 show that bounded refinement-equivariant filtering, positive scalarization, exact child-shell differences and the first ordered weighted refinement all collapse back to one universal Mellin carrier or a cyclotomic coboundary.

PC-335 closes bounded sign-changing/indefinite quadratic selectors. On every line `Re(s)=sigma>0`, a bounded real Mellin symbol that annihilates the mixed-prime controls cannot retain the desired prime-power response. PC-336 then classifies every finite-order distribution supported at the endpoint `s=1`: its shell response is a finite generalized-von-Mangoldt jet `Lambda_1,...,Lambda_(K+1)`, with higher derivatives exposing almost-prime strata but no new zero-sensitive mechanism.

PC-337 closes finite point-supported singular selectors on an entire fixed Mellin line: semiprime nulls force zero response away from `s=1`, while the endpoint response reduces to `c Lambda(n)`. PC-338 shows that discreteness was not the essential hypothesis. The same classification holds for every compactly supported distribution in Mellin frequency.

PC-339 now closes a broad noncompact/global escape as well. Let `T` be a finite complex Borel measure on the real Mellin-frequency line satisfying the natural critical Gamma-strip boundary integrability. The Gamma decay produces a bounded holomorphic strip function. Exact semiprime-null response forces zeros at every prime logarithm, and after conformal transport those zeros violate the Blaschke condition because `sum_p 1/p` diverges. The strip function therefore vanishes identically. Off `s=1` the scalar response is shell-invisible; on the endpoint line the only surviving visible response is again `c Lambda(n)`.

Thus compact intervals, compact singular measures, compact distributions of arbitrary finite order, finite unions of compact Mellin bands, and many genuinely global measures with Gamma-compatible boundary growth add no new exact prime-power selector. Noncompact support alone is not a new resource; the analytic boundary class is what matters.

A surviving radial route must genuinely leave this enlarged class: a global Mellin object outside the critical strip-admissibility hypothesis, a non-measure analytic functional, shell-dependent symbols/order, nonlinear radial operations, matrix-valued/non-scalar refinement operators, or genuinely cross-shell coupling before scalar evaluation. Any such route still needs an independent zero-sensitive destination theorem; escaping the measure/boundary class is not itself progress toward RH.

## Demand a zero-sensitive destination theorem before interpreting source distinguishability

Prime Circle now exhibits many forms of retained structure without zero selection: lossless cyclic decoding, multivariable source tensors, analytic continuation, a universal Hilbert `1/2` boundary, trace-class relative data, source autocorrelation, discrete Green bound states, universal conductor singularity, PNT-driven escaping outliers and a positive Hardy-square excess functional. PC-331--PC-334 make the normalized magnitude branch multiscale, identify its scalar theorem target with classical short-interval variance and provide an exact complemented binary control. Separately, PC-335--PC-339 classify bounded indefinite, compact-frequency and Gamma-strip-admissible global scalar Mellin selectors: the only nontrivial semiprime-null visible response in this broad class is the classical von Mangoldt endpoint.

The next proposal should therefore state the source-sensitive zero/sign statement first and identify what extra information survives the relevant matched-control/no-go boundary. Source distinguishability, spectral richness, sign-indefinite weighting, compact Mellin singularity, global Mellin support or a hard band estimate count only after the surviving invariant is control-resistant and independently coupled to the Riemann zero restriction.
