# Prime-circle research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-circle intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they discriminate among mathematically different mechanisms.

## Preserve genuinely cross-level information before classicalization

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-anchor-required-but-local-anchor-is-classical`.

One-shell anchored, rotation-invariant, or regular circulant constructions repeatedly collapse to classical polygon, Ramanujan, Dirichlet, divisor, or endpoint data. High rank does not prevent that collapse. A surviving mechanism must couple distinct refinement levels before reduction and produce an invariant not determined by the same classical character package.

A decisive test is a cross-level observable whose value changes under a control that preserves every one-shell classical invariant consumed by the construction. Merely increasing rank or adding a fixed character decomposition does not qualify.

## Locate the first mechanism beyond canonical finite cotangent closure

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-anchor-required-but-local-anchor-is-classical`.

The audited finite canonical cotangent sector is now closed far beyond forests: arbitrary finite multigraphs, cycles, parallel edges, repeated shells, coincidence strata, and scalar shell-local weights reduce to collision-stratified endpoint Cauchy/cyclotomic algebra. Fixed finite support with arbitrary depth is finite-state and rational, while the canonical normalized complete-preimage growth has universal affine-band limits determined by its base spectrum.

The live question is therefore not a more complicated finite cotangent graph. It is to derive a genuinely different information layer: a new intrinsic operator/tensor, an independently forced incomplete or selective growing family, a singular renormalized limit, or a global completion whose invariant provably does not factor through the endpoint/fixed-state controls. Any candidate must establish that nonfactorization before assigning arithmetic significance.

## Use compatible completion as a carrier, not as an automatic Hamiltonian

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-compatible-completion-lacks-coercive-rh-scale`.

The adelic solenoid preserves exact-order labels, but regular commuting calculi and a broad class of scalar dilation covariances cannot have the desired compact-resolvent scale. Projective-height properization does produce compactness, but its zeta reduces to classical primitive-lattice zeta data.

The live question is whether the compatible geometry forces a non-scalar, one-sided, singular, or relative operator scale that is neither a scalar reparametrization of the dilation action nor a decoded projective height. A decisive result must derive that scale intrinsically and show that it carries an RH-relevant invariant beyond functional-equation or lattice-zeta structure.

## Classify higher Hardy cycle periods before invoking an all-level completion

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-005-hardy-shell-core-is-universal`.

The canonical Hardy/Hankel lift escapes cotangent endpoint closure, while its finite-shell essential algebra remains a wedge of universal Hilbert bands. Mixed finite words are trace class; cubic cycle periods are weight-three cyclotomic multiple polylogarithms; and the general separated-cycle denominator map is an odd/even incidence problem with explicit parity or alternating fibers.

Determine whether the `k>=4` incidence-constrained traces remain inside known cyclotomic multiple-polylogarithmic/conical period spaces or contain a genuinely new canonical period component. Only after that finite algebra is classified is an infinite all-shell organization meaningful. A decisive positive result must eventually supply an RH-relevant selector or analytic parameter not already imported from classical period theory; a full finite reduction would narrow the route to truly infinite coupling.
