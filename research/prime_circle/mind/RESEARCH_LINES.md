## Add a destination theorem that selects something stricter than the canonical multivariable zero divisor

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-030-canonical-mordell-tornheim-coupling-inherits-off-critical-zeros-near-decoupling`.

PC-295--PC-310 separate phase retention, classical Fourier/polyspectral data and stable source separation. At mesh scale the one-level Poisson field is already an invertible cyclic decoder, so source recovery there is not a new RH-sensitive observable.

PC-311--PC-315 close direct bilinear scalar fusion and the complete finite-Fourier orbit: the resulting periodic Dirichlet packets are classical, and canonical scalar eigensectors can have abundant zeros in `Re s>1`.

PC-316 moves before scalar cyclic compression. The pointwise product of two oriented Hardy fields retains two Fourier indices and triple Mellinization lands in a finite bundle of character-weighted Mordell--Tornheim double `L`-functions. PC-317--PC-318 classify every positive one-parameter radial restriction: rational effective slopes collapse to finite Hurwitz packets, while irrational slopes decode the complete source tensor into a Barnes double-zeta packet. There is no useful intermediate radial quotient.

PC-319 tests the source-specific zero hope directly. For the smallest canonical pair `(5,7)`, the exact one-level factor has zeros in `Re s>1`, and Rouché stability shows that those zeros persist for every sufficiently small positive Mordell--Tornheim coupling.

PC-320 closes the obvious full-divisor escape near that face. Joint holomorphy across `u=0` and Weierstrass preparation turn every PC-319 decoupled zero into a genuine local complex-analytic zero hypersurface, finite of positive degree over an open bidisk of `(t,u)` parameters and still contained in `Re s>1`. A nonvanishing holomorphic completion factor leaves this divisor unchanged, and a local biholomorphic mixing of Mellin variables only relabels it.

The live construction must therefore specify a destination object **different from the raw local divisor of the canonical PC-316 packet**: a distinguished source-forced submanifold separated from the decoupled neighborhood, a nontrivial relation among several divisor components, a determinant/quotient with genuinely new zeros, or a different nonlinear coupling. Ordinary completion, coordinate mixing, another radial recoding, and an unqualified claim that multivariable nonseparability should select critical geometry are closed near `u=0`.

## Demand a zero-sensitive destination theorem before interpreting source distinguishability

Prime Circle now exhibits four versions of “more information” without zero selection: rational radialization compresses into classical packets, irrational radialization becomes lossless decoding, genuine canonical Mordell--Tornheim coupling inherits off-critical zeros, and the full nearby multivariable divisor carries those zeros over an open parameter set. A viable observable must state what nontrivial quotient or selector survives and which independent theorem converts it into control of the relevant divisor.

Source distinguishability, multivariable notation, irrationality, nonseparability, analytic continuation, standard completion and coordinate changes are not destination criteria by themselves. The next proposal should identify the exact zero/sign statement first and show why its hypotheses exclude the PC-320 local off-critical hypersurface rather than merely repackage it.
