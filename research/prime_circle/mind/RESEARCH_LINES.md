# Prime-circle research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable prime-circle intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Add a destination theorem that selects something stricter than the canonical multivariable zero divisor

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-032-canonical-green-slice-is-universal-hilbert-criticality`.

PC-295--PC-310 separate phase retention, classical Fourier/polyspectral data and stable source separation. At mesh scale the one-level Poisson field is already an invertible cyclic decoder, so source recovery there is not a new RH-sensitive observable.

PC-311--PC-315 close direct bilinear scalar fusion and the complete finite-Fourier orbit: the resulting periodic Dirichlet packets are classical, and canonical scalar eigensectors can have abundant zeros in `Re s>1`.

PC-316 moves before scalar cyclic compression. The pointwise product of two oriented Hardy fields retains two Fourier indices and triple Mellinization lands in a finite bundle of character-weighted Mordell--Tornheim double `L`-functions. PC-317--PC-318 classify every positive one-parameter radial restriction: rational effective slopes collapse to finite Hurwitz packets, while irrational slopes decode the complete source tensor into a Barnes double-zeta packet. There is no useful intermediate radial quotient.

PC-319 tests the source-specific zero hope directly. For the smallest canonical pair `(5,7)`, the exact one-level factor has zeros in `Re s>1`, and Rouché stability shows that those zeros persist for every sufficiently small positive Mordell--Tornheim coupling. PC-320 strengthens this to a local complex-analytic zero hypersurface over an open `(t,u)` neighborhood of the decoupled face; ordinary completion and biholomorphic coordinate mixing cannot remove it.

PC-321 closes the opposite endpoint. For the same canonical packet, `Z_(5,7)(s,t,u) != 0` whenever `Re s>=0`, `Re t>=0`, and `Re u>=5`. Strong additive coupling isolates the unique lowest output mode and destroys the zero geometry on the right polyhalf-plane.

PC-322 tests the most natural coefficient-free intermediate slice. Ordinary downstream Green inversion selects exactly `u=1`, where `(k+l)^(-1)` is the classical Hilbert matrix. The induced Dirichlet vectors enter `ell^2` exactly for `Re s>1/2`, but the same boundary occurs for every nonzero periodic source. The canonical Green slice therefore selects universal Hilbert criticality, not a Prime-Circle zero selector.

PC-323 then retains the **full Green operator** instead of scalarizing it. For periodic sources `a,b`, residue grouping gives

`X_(a,b)=A_(a,b) tensor H_1 + S`, with `S` trace class,

where the finite channel `A_(a,b)=L^(-1)a_L b_L^T` has rank one and singular value `c_(a,b)=sqrt(mu_a mu_b)`. The canonical self-adjoint dilation consequently has one universal absolutely-continuous Hilbert band `[-pi c_(a,b),pi c_(a,b)]`; detailed periodic arithmetic survives only in the trace-class relative defect. For `(5,7)`, `c=sqrt(2/7)`.

The absolute determinant route is therefore blocked by noncompactness. The first determinant-like object that can still be source-sensitive is a perturbation determinant, scattering invariant or spectral-shift quantity **relative to the universal Hilbert core**. Such a relative object is only a candidate destination until its divisor or sign content is independently analyzed and shown to fail on matched periodic controls.

The surviving raw-packet/operator possibility is narrower than “finite intermediate coupling” or “retain the operator.” Small `u` carries off-critical zeros, large `u` becomes zero-free, the canonical `u=1` scalar boundary is universal, and even the full `u=1` continuous spectrum remembers only source RMS. A viable proposal must now produce a source-sensitive **relative** spectral invariant, discrete/singular datum, or a different nonseparable operator/nonlinear construction whose leading channel is not already universal.

## Demand a zero-sensitive destination theorem before interpreting source distinguishability

Prime Circle now exhibits several versions of “more structure” without zero selection: rational radialization compresses into classical packets, irrational radialization becomes lossless decoding, weak genuine coupling inherits an off-critical divisor, the canonical Green slice lands on universal Hilbert criticality, the full Green lift has only one universal Hilbert absolutely-continuous band, and strong additive exponent collapses to a unique lowest output mode. A useful destination must state what nontrivial quotient, relative invariant, selector or relation survives **between** those failures and which independent theorem converts it into control of the relevant zeros.

Source distinguishability, multivariable notation, irrationality, nonseparability, analytic continuation, standard completion, coordinate changes, canonical Green inversion, a `1/2` Hilbert boundary, operator lifting, relative determinants as formal objects and large coupling are not destination criteria by themselves. The next proposal should identify the exact zero/sign statement first and show why its hypotheses exclude the PC-320 off-critical hypersurface, the PC-322/PC-323 universal Hilbert geometry and the PC-321 first-mode-dominance regime rather than merely repackage one of them.
