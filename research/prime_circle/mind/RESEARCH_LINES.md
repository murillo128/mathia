# Prime-circle research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable prime-circle intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Preserve a genuinely nonclassical carrier before source reconstruction or finite-character reduction

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-023-growing-oriented-tensor-order-terminates-in-source-reconstruction`.

PC-295--PC-301 identify the diffuse fixed-scale boundary: the normalized boundary-log operator approaches a universal square-zero class, positive quadratic compression loses phase, independently averaged loop hierarchies collapse to one real symmetric average, and centered same-source Gram formation erases coordinatewise orientation before averaging.

PC-302 tests the simplest pre-Gram repair. Reflection acts by latent mode reversal, so unlabelled frame/subspace geometry remains gauge. Keeping labels restores a reflection-odd channel, but its first quadratic component is exactly sine/cosine Fourier covariance.

PC-303 closes the entire **fixed finite tensor** extension of that repair. Every labelled frame monomial is one additive character of the source coordinates; averaging gives a joint Fourier coefficient, and fixed polynomial invariants generate the classical polyspectral algebra. For the canonical distinct-prime source, fixed-order joint transforms reduce by partition-lattice inclusion--exclusion to finite polynomials in the ordinary prime exponential sums already present at quadratic order.

PC-304 now identifies the opposite endpoint of the growing-order escape. For prime modulus `q`, powers of a single native oriented coordinate satisfy

`E[y_s^d]=N^(-d/2) \hat mu(d mod q)`.

Degrees `1,...,q-1` therefore recover the complete additive DFT and hence the source law exactly by Fourier inversion. For the canonical nonempty proper prime support, every Fourier coefficient is nonzero; consequently the full cyclic bispectrum already determines the entire source up to translation. Higher translation-invariant polyspectra cannot add further orbit information once that packet is present.

The live route is therefore an **intermediate mechanism**, not “more tensor order” by itself. A successful carrier must identify a scaling `d(q)<<q` with a source-sensitive asymptotic effect that is neither finite-order classicalization nor approach to exact source reconstruction, apply a geometry-forced nonlinear/operator evolution before Fourier reduction, or use genuinely cross-level/mixed-conductor structure that cannot be reproduced by reconstructing each modulus separately and then post-processing it.

## Keep defect resolution, carrier gauge, Fourier classicalization and reconstruction saturation separate

Reaching the `r_q` defect scale is necessary to escape the universal square-zero bulk but not sufficient. PC-299 shows that positive quadratic compression forgets phase; PC-300--PC-301 show that independence and centered Gram formation can erase still more structure before any higher statistic is formed.

PC-302--PC-303 sharpen the finite-order classicalization boundary. Moving to labelled oriented frames restores orientation, and higher fixed tensor order can recover phase-sensitive polyspectra, but that information is still exactly source Fourier data. PC-304 adds the saturation boundary: sufficiently high order does not create a new carrier either; it becomes a lossless encoding of the original source, and translation-invariant orbit recovery already saturates at the bispectrum because the canonical DFT is nowhere zero.

Any growing-order proposal must therefore state **where it sits between those two boundaries**: which frequencies become available, how tensor complexity scales with `q`, which destination operation consumes the partial packet before full reconstruction, and what theorem distinguishes the intermediate regime from ordinary source recovery. Recovering more source coordinates is not itself RH progress unless the geometry supplies a new coercive or zero-sensitive operation on them.
