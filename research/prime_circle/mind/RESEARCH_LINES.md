## Add source-native zero-selecting structure beyond finite-Fourier closure and all one-parameter radial recodings

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-029-irrational-radial-rays-decode-the-full-source-tensor`.

PC-295--PC-310 separate phase retention, classical Fourier/polyspectral data and stable source separation. At mesh scale the one-level Poisson field is already an invertible cyclic decoder, so source recovery there is not a new RH-sensitive observable.

PC-311--PC-315 close direct bilinear scalar fusion and the complete finite-Fourier orbit: the resulting periodic Dirichlet packets are classical, and the canonical scalar Fourier eigensectors can have abundant zeros in `Re s>1`.

PC-316 moves before that scalar cyclic compression. The pointwise product of two oriented Hardy fields retains two independent Fourier indices and triple Mellinization lands in a finite bundle of classical character-weighted Mordell--Tornheim double `L`-functions.

PC-317--PC-318 now classify **every positive one-parameter radial restriction** of that two-index field. Rational effective rate ratio creates additive collisions, eventually quasipolynomial coefficients and a finite Hurwitz packet. Irrational ratio removes all residue-frequency collisions; after stripping the geometric denominators a Vandermonde inversion recovers the complete residue tensor, and for the canonical source it recovers both input cyclic packets. Its Mellin transform is a finite Barnes double-zeta packet. There is no intermediate radial regime that is simultaneously nonreconstructive and nonclassical.

The live construction must therefore retain genuinely multivariable source geometry or introduce a different source-forced coupling, and it must come with a zero-sensitive destination theorem. A theorem about the exact Mordell--Tornheim/Barnes combination would be legitimate; another one-parameter radial slope is not a new direction unless its observation map is mathematically different from the classified additive-frequency trace.

## Demand a zero-sensitive destination theorem before interpreting source distinguishability

Prime Circle now exhibits both failure modes of “more information”: rational radialization compresses the source into classical packets, while irrational radialization becomes lossless decoding. Neither supplies zero selection. A viable observable must state what nontrivial quotient survives between those extremes and which theorem turns that quotient into control of the relevant divisor. Source distinguishability, multivariable notation, irrationality and analytic continuation are not destination criteria by themselves.
