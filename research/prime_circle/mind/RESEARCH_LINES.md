## Add a multivariable destination theorem that survives the canonical off-critical-zero test

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-030-canonical-mordell-tornheim-coupling-inherits-off-critical-zeros-near-decoupling`.

PC-295--PC-310 separate phase retention, classical Fourier/polyspectral data and stable source separation. At mesh scale the one-level Poisson field is already an invertible cyclic decoder, so source recovery there is not a new RH-sensitive observable.

PC-311--PC-315 close direct bilinear scalar fusion and the complete finite-Fourier orbit: the resulting periodic Dirichlet packets are classical, and canonical scalar eigensectors can have abundant zeros in `Re s>1`.

PC-316 moves before scalar cyclic compression. The pointwise product of two oriented Hardy fields retains two Fourier indices and triple Mellinization lands in a finite bundle of character-weighted Mordell--Tornheim double `L`-functions. PC-317--PC-318 then classify every positive one-parameter radial restriction: rational effective slopes collapse to finite Hurwitz packets, while irrational slopes decode the complete source tensor into a Barnes double-zeta packet. There is no useful intermediate radial quotient.

PC-319 now tests the source-specific zero hope directly. For the smallest canonical pair `(5,7)`, the exact one-level factor has zeros in `Re s>1`, and Rouché stability shows that those zeros persist for every sufficiently small **positive** Mordell--Tornheim coupling. Thus genuine two-index coupling plus the canonical coefficient vector does not yield coordinatewise Riemann-line confinement near the natural decoupling face.

The live construction must therefore keep genuinely multivariable source geometry **and** specify a destination theorem that escapes this obstruction: a distinguished positive-coupling locus separated from `u=0`, a completion/diagonal mixing variables, a theorem about the full multivariable divisor, or a different source-forced coupling. Another radial recoding or an unqualified claim that the exact Mordell--Tornheim coefficients should select critical-line zeros is closed.

## Demand a zero-sensitive destination theorem before interpreting source distinguishability

Prime Circle now exhibits three versions of “more information” without zero selection: rational radialization compresses into classical packets, irrational radialization becomes lossless decoding, and the genuinely coupled canonical Mordell--Tornheim packet still inherits off-critical zeros near decoupling. A viable observable must state what nontrivial quotient survives and which independent theorem converts it into control of the relevant divisor.

Source distinguishability, multivariable notation, irrationality, nonseparability and analytic continuation are not destination criteria by themselves. The next proposal should identify the exact zero/sign statement first and then show why its hypotheses exclude the PC-319 coupled off-critical regime rather than merely repackage it.
