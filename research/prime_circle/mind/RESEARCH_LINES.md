# Prime-circle research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-circle intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should be merged, redirected, or removed when the mathematics changes.

## Retain fine/coarse old-new structure before character or fiber averaging

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-anchor-required-but-local-anchor-is-classical`.

Pointing a translation-invariant shell is not a live escape by itself: local spectral measures and Schur self-energies reduce to full-shell derivative data (PC-038), canonical divisor-level Kron refinement is path independent (PC-039), and fixed primitive metric/chiral blocks classicalize to Dirichlet special values (PC-044--PC-045). PC-047 corrects the former low-rank interpretation of the natural old/new cotangent carrier: it can instead have maximal or full rank. PC-048--PC-049 then show why rank is not the discriminator: natural birth-order/character decompositions and fiber pushforwards still reduce to fixed `L(0,eta)` data and commuting invertible refinement maps.

The surviving question is whether a **simultaneous multilevel construction that keeps fine/coarse old-new structure active before character/fiber averaging** carries information absent from all of those classical packages. A decisive positive result would exhibit a canonical mixed invariant that changes while one-shell spectra, fixed character data, and canonical refinement multipliers are held fixed. Reduction to those data would close the candidate regardless of matrix rank.

## Break the universal full-cover reduction in nonlinear uniformization

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-first-order-transport-forgets-the-interior`.

First-order accessory/projective transport is already too flat, and PC-040--PC-043 show that the canonical Weil--Petersson second-order response on the full roots-of-unity cover is still too universal: normalized divisor refinement is metric-flat, while the nonzero curvature couples character sectors only through a fixed twisted resolvent `2(L_theta+2)^(-1)` on the thrice-punctured base. The corresponding automorphic parameter is `s=2`, not the critical line.

The live nonlinear question is therefore whether the primitive-only composite birth surface, an off-symmetric deformation, or another genuinely global uniformization problem breaks this fixed-base reduction. A decisive invariant must retain level-specific old/new geometry that cannot be reconstructed from covering degree, rational holonomy characters, and the universal base resolvent. Higher derivatives that remain inside the same universal package do not qualify.

## Long-range propagation after factoring the exact conformal background

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-exact-circle-defect-is-relational-but-trace-class`.

Treat the Grunsky--Schiffer kernel of `V(z)=pi cot(pi/z)` as a canonical conformal background rather than the spectral generator. Investigate whether a geometrically forced long-block propagation or branching mechanism composed with this background leaves a residual that carries ordered prime-gap information.

The decisive test is to factor out the endpoint-only/trace-class contribution and compare the residual on primes with integers, density-matched sequences, and order-destroying controls. A surviving singularity or coefficient is arithmetically interesting only if it depends on ordered gap structure and is not reproduced by the same propagation on the regular controls.

## Derive a proper all-level scale without decoding conductor by hand

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-compatible-completion-lacks-coercive-rh-scale`.

The compatible inverse limit is now identified with the adelic solenoid and retains exact-order arithmetic labels, but its natural regular spectra are noncoercive or noncompact: the leafwise Laplacian has dense rational-square spectrum, transverse symmetry leaves the exact-order scale arbitrary, and regular commuting leaf/fiber calculus cannot have compact resolvent. PC-069--PC-070 further rule out ordinary compact-resolvent Hamiltonians obeying an exact scalar affine dilation law `V_m^* H V_m=cH+dI` on the natural solenoid representation.

The live question is whether Prime-Circle geometry forces a **singular, noncommuting, or one-sided leaf/fiber scale** that is proper on arithmetic directions without merely applying a chosen height to already-decoded conductor. A decisive construction must derive that scale intrinsically, evade the regular commuting/affine-dilation no-go results, and remain nontrivial under matched all-level controls. Otherwise the solenoid should be treated as a canonical carrier rather than an RH Hamiltonian.
