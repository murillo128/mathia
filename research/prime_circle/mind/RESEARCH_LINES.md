## Apply the strongest matched and classicalization controls after the representation is fixed

For full fibers, an order-one spike, concentration phenomenon or singular phase transition is not arithmetic evidence when the same pushforward occurs for matched composite degrees. For primitive fibers, prime/composite separation is also insufficient: exact-order restriction already supplies classical coprimality data, and a primorial boundary can literally preselect the prime/rough-number set before any operator is applied.

Credit a new Prime-Circle branch only after identifying the surviving source datum, reducing its classical Möbius/Ramanujan/divisor/rough-number content explicitly, and showing that the residue affects the intended spectral or cancellation quantity. A non-scalar matrix on prime-selected vertices is not by itself a new mechanism; the mathematical delta must lie in a source-forced interaction or cross-scale law that is not determined by that support.
