## Leave the cyclotomic Cauchy-packet algebra through a source-forced cross-prime relation

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-011-scalar-fourier-zero-existence-is-deformation-dependent`, `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra`.

PC-224--PC-230 show that canonical refinement networks remain coarse modules, fixed-base fresh-prime states reduce to cyclotomic Cauchy packets, repeated-prime depth factors through the squarefree radical, residue branching becomes path-independent/isometric after normalization, and the canonical chord operator renormalizes to its parent.

PC-231 closes the first nonlinear repair before scalar compression. Pointwise multiplication and Fourier-dual convolution of fixed-base packets create only confluent poles at existing rational shifts, additive shift composition, or resonant lattice deltas. PC-232 extends the closure to arbitrary finite operation depth and simultaneous squarefree conductors: the fractional shifts remain in the subgroup generated by the input shifts, whose direct limit is the classical squarefree torsion subgroup of `Q/Z`. Growing nonlinear depth cannot manufacture a new shift carrier such as `p^2`-torsion.

The surviving construction must therefore use information **outside the canonical packet shift algebra**: source-forced nontranslation-invariant operators, coupled amplitudes/normalizations, genuinely interacting multi-prime state, or another relation whose content grows with the squarefree radical and is not merely cyclotomic torsion in denser form.

## Treat fixed-base refinement, canonical bilinearity, and squarefree shift growth as controls

Fixed-base depth refines a fixed cyclotomic object. Pole-residue branching, chord compression, pointwise packet products, and bilateral packet convolution all close inside explicit classical algebras. Letting the squarefree conductor grow enlarges the torsion support but does not by itself create a new operation or zeta-specific invariant.

A genuine continuation must identify which source relation survives the complete packet-algebra closure and prove that its final observable is not reducible to finite/confluent Cauchy data, cyclotomic torsion, or a parent fixed point.
