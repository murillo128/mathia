# Prime-circle research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-circle intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Break the coarse-fiber module before interpreting cross-level operator richness

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-011-scalar-fourier-zero-existence-is-deformation-dependent`, `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra`.

PC-197--PC-223 classify fixed-level scalar/spectral wrappers, lower-conductor leakage, common-anchor Mangoldt selection, and arbitrarily deep repeated prime-axis words. Those constructions either reduce to classical coefficient/Ramanujan data or factor through a thin conductor state that is exactly blind to large parts of the mixed-conductor lattice.

PC-224 tests the first direct cross-level escape without collapsing immediately to prime axes. Under a coprime prime refinement, the full old-to-new leakage after one diagonal source mask decomposes independently over coarse CRT fibers. Its positive square is multiplication by the fiberwise conditional variance, so singular values, rank, Schatten norms, and every invariant depending only on `L^*L` retain only one scalar profile per coarse point. Equal-profile leakage maps differ only by a fiber unitary.

PC-225 extends that boundary to arbitrary finite refinement networks built from canonical pullbacks, diagonal masks, and conditional expectations/complements. Every such network is a module map over the original coarse diagonal algebra, hence has the exact form `(Tf)(a,y)=v_y(a)f(y)` and `T^*T=M_{w_T}`. Branching, path labels, arbitrarily many fresh-prime coordinates, and even bounded strong/weak infinite-depth limits remain coarse-fiber preserving; their positive/singular data are still one scalar coarse profile.

The live cross-level route must therefore **break the coarse-module architecture or retain a non-left-unitary invariant of the internal fiber vector before compression**. Source-forced exact-order/Fourier projectors, a canonical operator that genuinely mixes distinct coarse residues, or a controlled simultaneous limit in which the coarse base itself grows remain admissible. Merely adding refinement depth, masks, branches, or codomain dimension is closed.

## Leave one-shell reciprocal Fourier zeros unless the source selects the deformation

PC-193--PC-196 show that scalar reciprocal-shell Fourier sign, zero existence, and first-zero scale depend on the chosen exponent. A useful scalar law needs a source-forced deformation/operator rather than a parameter chosen because it creates favorable zeros.

## Keep topology and realization separate from cyclotomic/operator identities

Potential-theoretic, geometric, old/new-fiber, nonabelian, non-Haar infinite-refinement, joint-algebra, or monodromy enlargements remain live only when the required object exists canonically and retains information beyond the classified coefficient, Ramanujan, conductor-resolution, prime-axis, and coarse-fiber module data. A larger state space is not a mechanism if natural positive/spectral compression sends it back to those controls.
