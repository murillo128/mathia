# Prime-circle research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-circle intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they discriminate among mathematically different mechanisms.

## Preserve genuinely cross-level information before finite or Fourier classicalization

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-anchor-required-but-local-anchor-is-classical`.

Finite cotangent networks, fixed-support depth, and canonical complete-preimage growth reduce to endpoint/cyclotomic or finite-state data. The cumulative primitive-root cloud has exact Ramanujan/Mertens Fourier coefficients, and every fixed translation-invariant positive kernel is only a weighted package of those classical modes.

A surviving mechanism must therefore retain cross-level incidence, provenance, or another nonconvolutional/non-endpoint structure before cumulative or scalar compression. A decisive observable must change under a control preserving the complete endpoint algebra and the relevant cumulative Fourier package; merely increasing graph rank or replacing discrepancy by a fixed positive kernel is insufficient.

## Locate the first mechanism beyond canonical finite cotangent closure

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-anchor-required-but-local-anchor-is-classical`.

The audited finite canonical cotangent sector is closed far beyond forests: arbitrary finite multigraphs, cycles, parallel edges, repeated shells, coincidence strata, and scalar shell-local weights reduce to collision-stratified endpoint Cauchy/cyclotomic algebra. Fixed finite support with arbitrary depth is finite-state and rational, while the canonical normalized complete-preimage growth has universal affine-band limits determined by its base spectrum.

The live question is therefore not a more complicated finite cotangent graph. It is to derive a genuinely different information layer: a new intrinsic operator/tensor, an independently forced incomplete or selective growing family, a singular renormalized limit, or a global completion whose invariant provably does not factor through the endpoint/fixed-state controls.

## Use compatible completion as a carrier, not as an automatic Hamiltonian

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-compatible-completion-lacks-coercive-rh-scale`.

The adelic solenoid preserves exact-order labels, but regular commuting calculi and a broad class of scalar dilation covariances cannot have the desired compact-resolvent scale. Projective-height properization does produce compactness, but its zeta reduces to classical primitive-lattice zeta data.

The live question is whether the compatible geometry forces a non-scalar, one-sided, singular, or relative operator scale that is neither a scalar reparametrization of the dilation action nor a decoded projective height. A decisive result must derive that scale intrinsically and show that it carries an RH-relevant invariant beyond functional-equation or lattice-zeta structure.

## Cross the Hardy boundary before the one-new-prime corrector becomes infinitesimal

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-005-hardy-shell-core-is-universal`.

The canonical Hardy escape has now been closed through the natural two-scale and conductor limits. PC-114 recovers the strong-limit-escaped Schatten mass as a finite Ramanujan reflection tensor times the universal Carleman--Hilbert defect. PC-115--PC-117 show that growing prime conductors, arbitrary simultaneous paths, and unrestricted composite old conductors all have the same canonical Hilbert--Schmidt normalization: operator norm tends to zero and the regularized determinant tends to a zero-free Gaussian. PC-118 shows that bounded similarity or isometric unfolding cannot change the finite divisor at all, while PC-119 shows that arbitrary uniformly bounded two-sided preconditioning, projections, and compressions still cannot retain a finite Fredholm divisor.

The surviving boundary is therefore genuinely singular. A viable Hardy mechanism must act before the PC-113 one-new-prime split, use a jointly organized all-shell object not reducible to those correctors, or derive an unbounded/domain-changing/nonlinear operation from the geometry itself. A mere change of coordinates, conductor-dependent unfolding, scalar normalization, or bounded preconditioner is no longer a distinct route.

A decisive positive result must identify the source-specific invariant before universal Ramanujan/Carleman and Gaussian limits take over and prove that its singular scale is independently forced rather than chosen to stop the operator norm from vanishing.
