## Add a destination theorem that survives both the autocorrelation quotient and the relative Green spectral-density gate

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-034-trace-class-relative-green-spectrum-has-an-l1-edge-gap-budget`.

PC-295--PC-315 close scalar one-level and finite-Fourier packet routes: mesh-scale recovery is already a generic cyclic decoder, direct bilinear fusion stays in classical periodic Dirichlet packets, and canonical scalar eigensectors can have abundant zeros in `Re s>1`.

PC-316--PC-321 move before scalar compression and classify the main multivariable/radial alternatives. Rational radial restrictions collapse to finite Hurwitz packets, irrational restrictions decode the full source tensor into Barnes double-zeta data, weak genuine coupling inherits an off-critical divisor, and sufficiently strong additive coupling becomes zero-free on the right polyhalf-plane. More variables do not supply a zero selector by themselves.

PC-322--PC-325 classify the canonical Green lift. The scalar Green slice lands on universal Hilbert criticality; the full operator is one universal Hilbert channel plus a trace-class defect; one-residue controls already produce classical off-band relative zeros; and independent Fourier phases are removable by simultaneous left/right unitary gauge. For the finite Fourier sources the complete separable Green/relative spectral package therefore factors through source magnitudes, equivalently cyclic autocorrelations.

PC-326 adds a second, independent obstruction to the remaining relative determinant. At fixed source conductors the zeros of the perturbation determinant are exactly the discrete eigenvalues outside the universal Hilbert band, and trace class forces

`sum dist(lambda_j,[-E,E]) <= ||V||_1`.

The relative zero divisor therefore has an `ell^1` band-edge gap budget. It cannot be identified directly with the unbounded Riemann ordinates, and even a reciprocal/Cayley-type compactification with edge distance comparable to `1/gamma` is too dense because `sum 1/gamma` diverges. This is distinct from the older PC-107 absolute trace-class determinant obstruction: the surviving Green operator is noncompact, but its **relative bound states** are still spectrally too sparse in the intrinsic edge coordinate.

The fixed-conductor Green branch now has two simultaneous gates. Any useful invariant must first survive the exact autocorrelation quotient of PC-325, and then avoid treating the trace-class off-band divisor of PC-326 as a direct Hilbert--Polya zero sequence. A continuation therefore needs a source-forced autocorrelation statistic with an independent zeta-zero theorem, a genuinely nonseparable phase-sensitive operator, or a derived singular conductor/cross-level limit in which the comparison defect ceases to be trace class. An arbitrary nonlinear map from ordinates to a band edge is not an escape.

## Demand a zero-sensitive destination theorem before interpreting source distinguishability

Prime Circle now exhibits many forms of retained structure without zero selection: lossless cyclic decoding, multivariable source tensors, irrational radial reconstruction, analytic continuation, a universal Hilbert `1/2` boundary, trace-class relative data, source autocorrelation, and discrete Green bound states. None is a destination criterion by itself.

The next proposal should state the exact source-sensitive zero/sign statement first, identify the matched controls and spectral-density law it must beat, and only then build the representation or operator that is supposed to force it. Source distinguishability and spectral richness count only after a theorem connects the surviving invariant to the Riemann zero restriction.
