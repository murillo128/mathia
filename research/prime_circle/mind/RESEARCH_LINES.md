The live route must therefore change the information law, not merely increase signature depth or matrix dimension. A surviving construction needs a cross-level/refinement operation that changes the state space before moment reduction, a source-derived kernel or interaction measure that does not factor into the individual shell profiles and universal order relation, or a nonlinear datum not determined by those separately decoded profiles. More target insertions, more shell-2 letters, interval-to-simplex moment lifting, antisymmetrization, or the canonical multivariate generalized spectrum are now matched-control structure rather than evidence of a new arithmetic carrier.
