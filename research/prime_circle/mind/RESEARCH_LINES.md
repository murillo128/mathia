# Prime-circle research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-circle intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Leave one-shell reciprocal Fourier zeros unless a source principle selects the deformation

**Linked intuition:** `MI-011-scalar-fourier-zero-existence-is-deformation-dependent`.

PC-193--PC-196 show that scalar reciprocal-shell Fourier sign, zero existence, and first-zero scale depend on the chosen exponent. A useful scalar law therefore needs a source-forced operator/deformation rather than a representation selected because it creates favorable zeros.

## Leave fixed finite scalar packet algebra and the one-coordinate regular mismatch lift

**Linked intuition:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra`.

PC-197--PC-202 close arbitrary fixed finite same-angle scalar Haar fusion inside classical Dirichlet, Mordell--Tornheim, and colored rational-cone data. PC-203--PC-205 then close several natural repairs: anchored non-Haar jets and scale-self-similar scalar filters reduce to one extra Haar/Mellin leg, while fixed finite-dimensional homogeneous matrix filters still collapse to conical data after scalar observation.

PC-206 closes the canonical infinite-dimensional lift of the *total* signed mismatch. Retaining every mismatch coefficient on `ell^2(Z)` gives the regular representation of one abelian coordinate, which Fourier-diagonalizes to multiplication by the original scalar packet (or its Poisson smoothing). Its fixed finite trace moments return to the already-classified scalar fusion algebra.

A viable cross-shell mechanism must therefore preserve structure richer than the total mismatch coordinate before scalarization: individual Fourier legs, source-forced nonabelian state, shell/refinement-dependent transfer, genuinely infinite-depth structure, or a global construction whose final observable does not collapse to one Laurent symbol.

## Require quantitative deformation and algebraic-closure controls of any proposed spectral relation

A candidate should survive source-compatible scalar deformations and then be reduced against the complete packet algebra generated by intrinsic character fields, power-map correspondences, anchored scalar filters, fixed finite matrix filters, and the regular representation of the total mismatch. Stability requires that the claimed arithmetic discriminator survive after boundary/refinement asymptotics and classical scalar closures are removed.

## Keep topology and realization separate from scalar cyclotomic identities

Potential-theoretic, geometric, operator-valued, infinite-refinement, nonabelian, or monodromy enlargements remain live only when the required object exists canonically and retains information beyond the classical scalar packet/Laurent data. A larger state space is not a new mechanism if Fourier transform makes it one scalar symbol again.
