## Break matched-control equivalence, not merely triangularity

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-045-reciprocity-closed-signature-is-abelian-trivial-and-rank-two-at-first-lie-level` through `MI-047-inner-parity-can-make-semisimple-spectralization-control-blind`.

PC-347--PC-350 move beyond the finite linear class. The full radial Chen signature is faithful to the shell path; after reciprocity closure its abelianization vanishes and its first Lie term is a rank-two decomposable area, while an all-depth `2x2` solvable decoder recovers the complete centered radial profile. Raw noncommutative depth is therefore source fidelity, not zero selection.

PC-351 closes the triangular spectralization route. Every simultaneously upper-triangular regularized development has limiting diagonal one, so ordinary spectrum is trivial. For the canonical decoder the source lives in one oriented off-diagonal coefficient, and the matched reciprocal control `W -> -W` is implemented by a fixed unitary conjugation. Every basis-free unitary-conjugacy invariant therefore agrees between arithmetic and control.

PC-352 shows that **non-solvability is not enough to escape that obstruction**. Put the calibration direction in the even part of a `Z/2`-graded target and centered shell directions in the odd part. If the parity automorphism is inner, implemented by one fixed group element `J`, then the arithmetic and reciprocal-control connections, finite-cutoff developments and regularized defects are all conjugate by `J`.

The obstruction already occurs in a genuinely non-solvable `SL_2(C)` target. With `A=alpha H_0` and `B=beta E+gamma F`, `alpha beta gamma!=0`, the generated Lie algebra is all of `sl_2`, yet `J=diag(i,-i)` fixes `A` and negates `B`. Hence the arithmetic and control developments have identical characteristic polynomials, eigenvalue multisets, traces, determinants and, after the natural unitary normalization, singular values and related metric spectral invariants. Passing to higher finite-dimensional `SL_2` representations preserves the same conjugacy.

The surviving question is therefore stricter than “find a non-triangular/non-solvable representation.” A viable representation must explain **before spectralization** why the source/control involution is not inner-equivalent on the chosen observable quotient, or retain an additional source-forced structure that does not descend to that conjugacy class. Oriented matrix coefficients can break the equivalence only by keeping gauge-dependent source coordinates, which returns toward reconstruction unless an independent zero-sensitive destination theorem uses them.

Candidate repairs should therefore audit the target automorphism class explicitly. Larger matrices, higher-spin representations, semisimplicity and full Lie generation are not new mechanisms when the matched reciprocal control remains in the same conjugacy orbit.

## Keep source recovery, nonlinear completeness, automorphism quotient and zero selection separate

Prime Circle now exhibits several distinct endpoints. Full scalar measurement reconstructs classical zeta structure. Full noncommutative depth reconstructs the centered source path. Low-order reciprocity quotients can erase most information. Triangular spectralization can discard the only oriented source coordinate. And PC-352 shows that even a full semisimple Lie algebra can remain spectrally control-blind when the reciprocal sign flip is inner.

The relevant audit is therefore not whether a representation is rich, faithful, noncommutative or non-solvable. State the destination discriminator first; identify the strict quotient it actually uses; determine the automorphism group of that quotient; and test whether a matched source-respecting control occupies the same orbit. Progress requires a quotient that is simultaneously source-specific after this automorphism audit and independently tied to zeta-zero geometry.
