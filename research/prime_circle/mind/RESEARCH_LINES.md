## Find source-forced interaction before the descendant state collapses to a rank-one conductor channel

PC-391--PC-405 progressively remove universal compact/Kron geometry, recovered shell-profile repackaging, finite multishell ordering and canonical cyclotomic refinement as independent source carriers. After the shell-2 change of coordinates, multiplicative descendants are generated by a universal dilation/difference semigroup from one base measure.

PC-414 removes the remaining cross-prime-conductor loophole at finite cutoff. At each Mellin frequency the descendant vector is the image of the scalar source amplitude under the rank-one map `z -> z c_n(t)`. Therefore an **arbitrary source-independent conductor matrix**, even dense and coupling valuations at different rational primes, pulls back to

`A_h(t,u)=hhat_n(t) conjugate(hhat_n(u)) K_W(t,u)`.

The same rank-one factorization holds for fixed-arity conductor tensors, finite spectral determinants and growing finite-cutoff conductor operators. A matched arbitrary base profile reproduces the entire conductor factor. Cross-prime dependence, large matrix rank or growing support do not restore source specificity when they act only after this scalarization.

The live route must therefore violate an actual hypothesis of the rank-one pullback. Candidate mechanisms must retain **source-derived geometry before conductor scalarization**—for example angular/chord/old-new incidence not determined by descendant conductor labels—or alter the descendant state through a genuinely nonlinear source-coupled interaction before the tensor is evaluated. Another possibility is an operator whose definition is itself coupled to the source before the PC-405 Mellin reduction. Merely choosing a more complicated external conductor kernel, diagonalizing it, taking a resolvent, increasing its rank with cutoff, or coupling rational primes is closed by PC-414.

The decisive test for any proposed escape is a matched-control factorization audit before analytic continuation. If the candidate can still be written as a universal conductor functional multiplied or congruenced by source amplitudes, its zeta-sensitive poles, determinants or spectra belong to the readout rather than to Prime-Circle geometry. A successful route must exhibit a source-dependent interaction term that cannot be reproduced after replacing the cyclotomic base profile by an arbitrary Mellin-tame one propagated through the same descendant law.
