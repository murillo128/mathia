## Add a destination theorem that survives the autocorrelation quotient, edge-gap law and source-native Hilbert background

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-038-green-excess-is-hardysquare-energy-of-fourier-magnitudes`.

PC-295--PC-315 close scalar one-level and finite-Fourier packet routes: mesh-scale recovery is already a generic cyclic decoder, direct bilinear fusion stays in classical periodic Dirichlet packets, and canonical scalar eigensectors can have abundant zeros in `Re s>1`.

PC-316--PC-321 classify the main multivariable/radial alternatives. Rational radial restrictions collapse to finite Hurwitz packets, irrational restrictions decode the full source tensor into Barnes double-zeta data, weak genuine coupling inherits an off-critical divisor, and sufficiently strong additive coupling becomes zero-free on the right polyhalf-plane. More variables do not supply a zero selector by themselves.

PC-322--PC-326 classify the canonical Green lift: one universal Hilbert channel plus trace-class relative data, phase-gauge/autocorrelation quotienting, and an `ell^1` band-edge gap budget for discrete relative zeros. These are strong structural statements but do not produce the density or zero selector required for the Riemann divisor.

PC-327--PC-329 expose the growing-conductor background. Band normalization has a universal shifted-Hilbert singularity; the prime source adds much larger PNT-scale low Fourier amplitudes; and no fixed set of modes or fixed-rank conductor-dependent correction can remove the resulting escaping Green outliers. Fixed-complexity deflation is therefore impossible.

PC-330 closes the most natural smooth growing-complexity repair. Subtracting the full Li/PNT profile removes fixed low-frequency mean structure but leaves the canonical Green Hilbert channel asymptotically unchanged because the sparse prime source has RMS `log q/q`, while the diffuse Li-grid control has only `1/q`. The residual retains asymptotically all of the sparse `L^2` mass; energy removed from fixed low modes is relocated to growing frequencies rather than canceled.

PC-331 isolates the exact source-sensitive excess above the universal logarithmic floor. For comparable conductors `q<=r<=2q`, the band-normalized Hilbert--Schmidt Green defect satisfies, up to absolute constants,

`D_(q,r)(a,b) asymp log q + E_(q,r)(a,b)`,

where

`E_(q,r)(a,b) = [1/(mu(a)mu(b))] sum_(i,j) |a_hat(i)|^2 |b_hat(j)|^2/(i+j)^2`.

The second term is a positive Hankel/Hardy-square energy of the Fourier **magnitude-square profiles**. It measures simultaneous low-frequency concentration and contains no phase information, consistently with PC-325.

PC-332 now gives the exact multiscale form of that excess. If

`Gamma_a(H)=[1/(H mu(a))] sum_(1<=i<=H)|a_hat(i)|^2`,

then `E_(q,r)` is equivalent up to absolute constants to the dyadic square function

`sum_m Gamma_a(2^m) Gamma_b(2^m)`.

Thus a superlogarithmic Green excess cannot remain diffuse across all scales: it forces an explicit dyadic band where normalized Fourier magnitude energy concentrates. Conversely, a uniform bound on the `Gamma` profiles yields only `O(log q)` excess. This converts the source-sensitive question from a global Hankel sum into a concrete multiscale concentration test, but it still uses only Fourier magnitudes.

The surviving Green problem is therefore precise. Determine whether prime sources force a dyadic magnitude-concentration law that matched sparse controls with the same one-point density and autocorrelation/magnitude constraints cannot reproduce, or construct a relative/channel subtraction that removes both the universal `log q` floor and generic dyadic low-frequency concentration. Only after such a residual survives PC-325 phase-gauge controls and PC-332 scale-matched magnitude controls is it eligible for an independent theorem connecting its sign, divisor or spectrum to a zeta-zero restriction.
