## Resolve the intermediate rational-microscopic window or force genuinely sharp finite-section sensitivity

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra`, `MI-013-growing-collision-geometry-can-still-be-classical`, `MI-014-single-source-positive-spectral-lifts-are-pure-gauge`.

PC-224--PC-277 close fixed-conductor packets, finite trace words, complete finite singular data, common-dilation growth and broad normalized moving-band regimes, leaving the intermediate rational-microscopic scalar window between current PNT transport and universal denominator resonance.

PC-278--PC-280 separately classify the positive spectral escape. Complete-domain phase wrappers are unitary gauge, complete-torus pointwise nonlinearities are source-blind Fourier data, and a truncated coefficient window is exactly a two-sided finite section of one source-independent cyclic convolution operator:

`M_(p,r;B)^f ~= E_(pH_B) C_f E_(rH_B)^* ~= P_(p,B) D_f P_(r,B)`.

PC-281 now shows that finite-section placement is still insufficient for every uniformly regular profile in the classical transport regime. The full normalized ordered singular spectrum is Lipschitz in the two source phases with constant `B L_q`; combining this with the denominator-`q` Li-grid coupling makes the prime and matched-control spectral laws asymptotically identical whenever the explicit transport error vanishes, in particular for bounded profiles with `B L_q=exp(o(sqrt(log q)))`.

Thus the live spectral carrier is narrower than generic window placement. A viable order-one discriminator must exploit **microscopic or singular profile variation** that defeats the PC-281 source transport, or leave the single-profile finite-section architecture through source-dependent/cross-level coupling. Exact anchor selectors, the unsmoothed logarithmic singularity and the transition regime where `B L_q` is too large for the current bound are concrete boundaries to test.

A useful theorem should therefore attack one of two precise carriers: close the surviving scalar rational-microscopic window, or determine whether a genuinely sharp/singular finite-section profile retains prime-specific concentration after the strongest matched control. Adding smoother pointwise nonlinearities or merely enlarging a regular finite window is no longer a distinct route.

## Keep retained information, quotient symmetry, transport regularity and arithmetic discrimination separate

Ordered information can survive a scalar quotient and still be classical. Full phase information can disappear under unitary gauge; nonlinear anchor data can disappear under permutation; truncation can retain projection placement; PC-281 shows that even this retained placement can be transported away when the profile is regular enough. A future Prime-Circle mechanism must identify both the symmetry-breaking residual and the quantitative regularity scale at which that residual escapes the matched source transport before calling it arithmetic.
