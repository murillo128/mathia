# Prime-circle research lines

This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Prime-Circle evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Find source-forced interaction before the descendant state collapses to one-shell Mellin data

PC-391--PC-415 progressively show that multiplicative descendants, finite and infinite conductor tensors, positive frame constructions and trace-class spectral invariants collapse after shell-2 scalarization to one-shell source amplitudes times universal conductor data. Matched nonarithmetic base profiles reproduce that conductor structure, so large support, matrix rank, Euler products or Fredholm data are not source specificity by themselves.

PC-416--PC-418 test increasingly strong operator extensions. Finite powers of the intrinsic log-scale position operator expose only finite Mellin jets of the same one-shell transform; bounded Fourier-Stieltjes functional calculus gives real Mellin translations of that same data; and unrestricted bounded-Borel/von-Neumann closure of the prime dilation/Weyl system becomes all of `B(L^2(R_+,dx/x))`, losing selectivity rather than recovering arithmetic structure.

PC-419 closes the operator-norm Weyl-descendant continuation from the opposite side. In logarithmic coordinates the source-forced rational dilations and log-position modulations generate a norm-closed Weyl algebra whose elements are norm recurrent under translation conjugacy. That recurrence is incompatible with nonzero compact operators, so the algebra has trivial compact, finite-rank and trace-class sectors and every element has spectrum equal to essential spectrum. It cannot supply a Hilbert--Pólya-style discrete finite-multiplicity spectrum or a nontrivial Fredholm-determinant channel from those generators alone.

PC-420 strengthens this negative boundary beyond the specific descendant algebra. For any unital translation-invariant `C*`-subalgebra `C` of bounded radial log-position multipliers, adjoining the source-forced rational-log shifts still gives an algebra with zero intersection with the compact operators; the conclusion remains true even for `C=L^infinity(R)`. Thus adding arbitrary bounded radial source profiles, their rational-log translates, or all bounded Borel radial multipliers does not repair the norm-closed compactness obstruction. The surviving classical boundary is qualitatively different: integrated continuous-scale crossed-product or resolvent/smoothing constructions can contain compact operators, but such kernels are new structure and must be derived from the Prime-Circle source rather than inserted after the desired spectral behavior is known.

The surviving route must therefore leave both failed closure extremes: the unrestricted strong/weak closure of PC-418 is too large, while the norm-closed bounded-radial/discrete-shift classes of PC-419--PC-420 have no compact sector. A viable source-forced mechanism must retain cross-shell coherence, a genuinely non-diagonal or integrated kernel, an angular/chord/old-new relation, a source-derived resolvent/smoothing operator, or another pre-scalarization interaction that an arbitrary matched base profile cannot reproduce. Its formula, topology and coupling must be fixed by the Prime-Circle source before the desired zeta readout is inspected.

## Apply matched controls after every representation change

A representation should be credited only after identifying the source-selected carrier, quotienting universal geometry and testing the same construction on a matched nonarithmetic profile. PC-414--PC-420 now rule out source-independent conductor tensors, the full trace-class descendant frame, finite Mellin jets, bounded real Mellin translations, unrestricted Weyl closure, the norm-closed Weyl-descendant algebra, and even arbitrary bounded radial diagonal enrichment joined with the discrete source-forced shifts as independent arithmetic mechanisms.

The live discriminator is therefore concrete: exhibit a source relation that is lost by the shell-2 scalar channel but survives in the proposed destination as cross-shell, non-diagonal, integrated or otherwise source-coupled data. If the candidate can still be reconstructed from one-shell Mellin amplitudes plus bounded radial multipliers and the source-independent discrete shift calculus, PC-420 says that it cannot create the missing compact/discrete spectral sector. If compactness appears only after adjoining a continuous-scale integral, resolvent or smoothing ingredient, that ingredient itself is the object that must pass the source and matched-control gates.
