# Prime-circle research lines

This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Prime-Circle evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Find source-forced interaction before the descendant state collapses to one-shell Mellin data

PC-391--PC-415 progressively show that multiplicative descendants, finite and infinite conductor tensors, positive frame constructions and trace-class spectral invariants collapse after shell-2 scalarization to one-shell source amplitudes times universal conductor data. Matched nonarithmetic base profiles reproduce that conductor structure, so large support, matrix rank, Euler products or Fredholm data are not source specificity by themselves.

PC-416--PC-418 test increasingly strong operator extensions. Finite powers of the intrinsic log-scale position operator expose only finite Mellin jets of the same one-shell transform; bounded Fourier-Stieltjes functional calculus gives real Mellin translations of that same data; and unrestricted bounded-Borel/von-Neumann closure of the prime dilation/Weyl system becomes all of `B(L^2(R_+,dx/x))`, losing selectivity rather than recovering arithmetic structure.

PC-419--PC-420 close the opposite norm-closed direction. The source-forced rational-log shifts, even after adjoining arbitrary bounded radial multipliers, generate no nonzero compact sector. Every element of the relevant translation-covariant norm-closed classes has spectrum equal to essential spectrum, so bounded radial enrichment plus discrete source shifts cannot supply a Hilbert--Polya-type discrete finite-multiplicity spectrum or a trace-class determinant channel. The surviving category had to leave that algebra through a genuinely integrated continuous-scale, resolvent, smoothing or other non-diagonal construction.

PC-421 tests the most canonical such escape. From the exact cyclotomic radial flux `rho_n`, the log-radius half-density

`h_(n,sigma)(u)=exp(sigma u) rho_n(exp u)`

has Fourier transform equal to the existing Mellin transform on the vertical line `Re(s)=sigma`. The Hilbert-transform commutator

`C_(n,sigma)=[H,M_(h_(n,sigma))]`

is nonzero Hilbert--Schmidt and therefore genuinely escapes the PC-420 no-compact theorem. However, the same compact architecture exists for **every** `sigma>0`; in the critical strip its symbol zeros are exactly the already-known zeta zeros on the chosen vertical line. Relative to Hardy projections the commutator is just the standard off-diagonal Hankel block, so compactness, spectral symmetry and Schatten behavior are generic operator theory rather than a critical-line selector.

PC-422 closes the most direct reflected-line repair of PC-421. Pairing `C_(n,sigma)` with `C_(n,1-sigma)` does produce the exact Mellin product on complementary vertical lines, but the unweighted `s <-> 1-s` product is classical Mellin Parseval. More decisively, for every strictly positive real source profile the reflected Hilbert--Schmidt pairing is uniquely maximized at `sigma=1/2` by an exact real-space `cosh` identity. The zero-free matched control `rho_0(x)=exp(-x)`, whose Mellin transform is just `Gamma(s)`, has the same strict half-density maximum. The apparent critical selector is therefore universal positivity/half-density geometry, not evidence about zeta-zero location.

PC-423 closes the finite reflected bilinear cross-shell matrix repair left open by PC-422. For any finite shell set `S`, the reflected pairing matrix factors exactly as

`P_S(sigma)=M_(S,D) K_D(sigma) M_(S,D)^T`,

where `M_(S,D)` is the ordinary divisor Möbius incidence matrix and `K_D` is a universal reflected Mellin kernel on divisor labels. On a nontrivial-divisor-closed family, `M_D` is unit lower triangular, so `det P_D(sigma)=det K_D(sigma)` exactly: primitive-shell extraction is only an invertible incidence-basis change. At `sigma=1/2`, `K_D` is a strictly positive Gram matrix, and a zero-free matched control obtained by deleting the common zeta factor has the same incidence factorization, reflection symmetry, full rank and half-line positivity.

Thus neither one-shell compactness, reflected scalar pairing nor **finite reflected bilinear shell matrices and their determinants** create an arithmetic spectral law. The zeta factor remains a common scalar spectral weight while the shell dependence is finite incidence geometry. The surviving route must introduce source structure before this scalarization/congruence collapse: genuinely noncommutative operator words before trace, shell-dependent kernels or domains, angular--radial or old--new interactions, sign-sensitive couplings not reducible to the divisor incidence matrix, or an infinite-dimensional limit whose information is not exhausted by a finite scalar moment matrix. Any such candidate must still fail a zero-free/matched-profile control for a source-specific reason.

## Apply matched controls after every representation change

A representation should be credited only after identifying the source-selected carrier, quotienting universal geometry and testing the same construction on a matched nonarithmetic profile. PC-414--PC-423 now rule out source-independent conductor tensors, the full trace-class descendant frame, finite Mellin jets, bounded real Mellin translations, unrestricted Weyl closure, norm-closed bounded-radial/discrete-shift algebras, the canonical continuous-scale Hilbert commutator, its direct reflected Hilbert--Schmidt polarization, and finite reflected bilinear cross-shell determinants as independent arithmetic mechanisms.

The current discriminator is concrete: exhibit a relation that is lost by shell-2 scalarization and by the finite divisor-incidence congruence, but survives in the proposed destination as genuinely cross-shell, non-diagonal, sign-sensitive or otherwise source-coupled data. If the candidate is reconstructed from one-shell Mellin amplitudes plus universal operator calculus or a finite incidence basis change, or if its `1/2` positivity survives on a zero-free control, it has only repackaged classical information. If it introduces a new continuous-scale, infinite-dimensional or compact kernel, the provenance and matched-control theorem for that kernel are themselves the mathematical burden.
