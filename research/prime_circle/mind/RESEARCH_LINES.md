# Prime-circle research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable prime-circle intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Preserve a genuinely nonclassical orientation carrier before finite character reduction

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-022-every-fixed-finite-oriented-frame-tensor-is-joint-fourier-polyspectral-source-data`.

PC-295--PC-301 identify the diffuse fixed-scale boundary: the normalized boundary-log operator approaches a universal square-zero class, positive quadratic compression loses phase, independently averaged loop hierarchies collapse to one real symmetric average, and centered same-source Gram formation erases coordinatewise orientation before averaging.

PC-302 tests the simplest pre-Gram repair. Reflection acts by latent mode reversal, so unlabelled frame/subspace geometry remains gauge. Keeping labels restores a reflection-odd channel, but its first quadratic component is exactly sine/cosine Fourier covariance.

PC-303 closes the entire **fixed finite tensor** extension of that repair. Every labelled frame monomial is one additive character of the source coordinates; averaging gives a joint Fourier coefficient, and fixed polynomial invariants generate the classical polyspectral algebra. For the canonical distinct-prime source, fixed-order joint transforms reduce by partition-lattice inclusion--exclusion to finite polynomials in the ordinary prime exponential sums already present at quadratic order.

The live route is therefore narrower than “retain higher joint information.” A successful carrier must escape finite additive-character algebra itself: growing tensor order with a nontrivial controlled limit, a cross-level/multi-modulus operation performed before Fourier reduction, a source-forced asymmetric/order-sensitive operation, or another representation whose invariant is not determined by finitely many joint additive Fourier coefficients.

## Keep defect resolution, carrier gauge, Fourier classicalization and growing-complexity limits separate

Reaching the `r_q` defect scale is necessary to escape the universal square-zero bulk but not sufficient. PC-299 shows that positive quadratic compression forgets phase; PC-300--PC-301 show that independence and centered Gram formation can erase still more structure before any higher statistic is formed.

PC-302--PC-303 sharpen the classicalization boundary. Moving to labelled oriented frames restores orientation, and higher finite tensor order can recover phase-sensitive polyspectra, but that information is still exactly source Fourier data. A higher-order statistic is not automatically a new carrier merely because it is nonlinear or phase-sensitive.

Any growing-order proposal must therefore state what changes as the order grows: which frequencies become available, how the required tensor complexity scales with `q`, whether the resulting limit remains stable and source-sensitive, and why it is not just asymptotically complete reconstruction of the classical prime Fourier packet in another basis.
