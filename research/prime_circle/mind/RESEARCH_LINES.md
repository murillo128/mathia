## Break reciprocal-control similarity with structure that is neither compactified nor a radial Fock reweighting

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-045-reciprocity-closed-signature-is-abelian-trivial-and-rank-two-at-first-lie-level` through `MI-051-unbounded-reciprocal-parity-is-not-enough-if-completion-compactifies-transport`.

PC-347--PC-358 show that increasingly rich finite signature/spectral constructions remain reciprocal-control blind. Full signature data can reconstruct the source path, but spectralization can discard orientation; finite algebraic lifts retain the same similarity; source-native Gram metrics restore exact isometry; and every fixed positive Hermitian tensor metric preserves reciprocal similarity after whitening.

PC-359 identifies a genuine infinite-dimensional escape from that proof: scalar grade weights can make reciprocal parity unbounded. But if fixed-lag weight ratios tend to zero, every positive-degree creation block compactifies and any norm-convergent strictly grade-raising multiplier has trivial spectrum. Breaking bounded similarity is therefore only one gate.

PC-360 classifies the natural scalar borderline `w_(m+1)/w_m -> r in (0,infinity)`. For each fixed lag, weighted creation is a compact perturbation of ordinary free-Fock creation after the radial degree dilation `d_k -> r^(k/2)d_k`; finite positive-degree polynomials have the same Calkin-class geometry. This preserves noncompact transport, but the first obvious source/control spectral-radius gap is still not arithmetic: a two-dimensional matched nonarithmetic one-particle metric can make that gap arbitrarily large.

The scalar-weight frontier is therefore substantially closed. A surviving completion needs a category change that cannot be reproduced by radial Fock rescaling or by choosing the one-particle metric: non-scalar grade coupling, genuinely uncontrolled infinite mixed-degree tails, unbounded/closed operators whose domains carry information, same-grade/backward/two-sided interactions, or an observable whose source/control distinction survives matched nonarithmetic metric controls. PC-360 does not classify arbitrary infinite series without the norm-convergence/summability conditions stated there.

## Keep source recovery, bounded similarity, high-grade transport and matched metric controls separate

Prime Circle now has four distinct gates. A representation may recover the path yet lose orientation after spectral quotienting. A completion may break the bounded reciprocal intertwiner yet compactify the source operator. A borderline scalar completion may retain noncompact transport yet reduce modulo compacts to standard radial free-Fock geometry. Even then, a spectral gap may be reproducible by nonarithmetic metric choice.

A future RH-facing discriminator must therefore survive all four audits and be independently tied to zeta-zero geometry. Merely making the parity unbounded, keeping a nonzero essential spectrum, or observing different source/control radii is not enough.
