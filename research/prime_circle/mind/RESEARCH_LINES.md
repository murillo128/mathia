## Add source-native structure before periodic scalarization

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-028-canonical-mixed-packet-fails-finite-fourier-self-duality`.

PC-295--PC-310 progressively separate phase retention, classical Fourier/polyspectral data and stable source separation. At the mesh boundary `1-r=Theta(1/q)`, the sampled Poisson field is already an invertible cyclic decoder with condition number `Theta(q)`; single-level recovery there is source reconstruction rather than a new RH-sensitive observable.

PC-311 closes the most direct cross-level escape. Bilinearly pairing two centered Poisson fields at conductors `q,r` passes to `Z/(qr)Z`; the resulting periodic coefficient has a finite character expansion, and the double Mellin transform is a finite bundle of ordinary `L(s+t,chi)` functions. The apparent two-conductor coupling therefore classicalizes in the single combined variable `s+t`.

PC-312 excludes accidental scalar closure for the canonical weights: the exact-period coefficient is not a finite-Fourier eigenvector. PC-313 now closes the minimal post-compression vector repair as well. For every even periodic coefficient, the pair `(d,F_N d)` satisfies the universal Hurwitz/finite-Fourier swap functional equation, and diagonalizing the swap gives the manually symmetrized eigensectors `d±F_N d`. Nothing in that closure is Prime-Circle-specific.

The live construction must therefore retain structure **before** the reduction to one periodic coefficient and its universal Fourier dual. Candidates include a source-forced coupling of the separate prime-level fields, a larger state retaining independent Fourier indices or pointed/shell data, nonlinear coupling before cyclic diagonalization, or a matrix/operator whose transform law depends genuinely on the source rather than on the universal finite-Fourier involution. Any such carrier still needs an independently justified zero-sensitive destination theorem.

## Demand a destination theorem before interpreting source distinguishability

Stable sub-mesh observations collapse matched sources; mesh-scale one-level observations decode the source; the direct bilinear mixed-conductor pairing classicalizes; the canonical scalar packet fails self-duality; and adjoining its Fourier dual yields only universal periodic-series closure. A viable observable must state what additional quantity survives **before** these reductions and how that quantity reaches a zero-sensitive destination without an uncontrolled inverse or a universal recoding.

Future mixed-conductor proposals should be reduced on the common refinement and audited for the exact state retained before scalar periodicization. Merely increasing the number of components after scalarization does not help when those components are generated by a universal transform of the same compressed state.
