## Add a destination theorem that selects something stricter than the canonical multivariable zero divisor

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-032-canonical-green-slice-is-universal-hilbert-criticality`.

PC-295--PC-310 separate phase retention, classical Fourier/polyspectral data and stable source separation. At mesh scale the one-level Poisson field is already an invertible cyclic decoder, so source recovery there is not a new RH-sensitive observable.

PC-311--PC-315 close direct bilinear scalar fusion and the complete finite-Fourier orbit: the resulting periodic Dirichlet packets are classical, and canonical scalar eigensectors can have abundant zeros in `Re s>1`.

PC-316 moves before scalar cyclic compression. The pointwise product of two oriented Hardy fields retains two Fourier indices and triple Mellinization lands in a finite bundle of character-weighted Mordell--Tornheim double `L`-functions. PC-317--PC-318 classify every positive one-parameter radial restriction: rational effective slopes collapse to finite Hurwitz packets, while irrational slopes decode the complete source tensor into a Barnes double-zeta packet. There is no useful intermediate radial quotient.

PC-319 tests the source-specific zero hope directly. For the smallest canonical pair `(5,7)`, the exact one-level factor has zeros in `Re s>1`, and Rouché stability shows that those zeros persist for every sufficiently small positive Mordell--Tornheim coupling. PC-320 strengthens this to a local complex-analytic zero hypersurface over an open `(t,u)` neighborhood of the decoupled face; ordinary completion and biholomorphic coordinate mixing cannot remove it.

PC-321 closes the opposite endpoint. For the same canonical packet,

`Z_(5,7)(s,t,u) != 0` whenever `Re s>=0`, `Re t>=0`, and `Re u>=5`.

The mechanism is exact first-output-mode domination: after scaling by `2^u`, the unique `(k,l)=(1,1)` contribution survives while every higher additive output mode is uniformly suppressed. Thus the strong-coupling limit is not a hidden Riemann-like selector; it destroys the zero geometry altogether on the right polyhalf-plane.

PC-322 tests the most natural coefficient-free intermediate slice rather than choosing `u` after inspecting zeros. Ordinary downstream Green inversion of the PC-316 field selects exactly `u=1`, where `(k+l)^(-1)` is the classical Hilbert matrix. This is the precise real `ell^2` operator transition: `0<u<1` is unbounded, `u=1` bounded noncompact, and `u>1` Hilbert--Schmidt. The induced Dirichlet coefficient vectors enter `ell^2` exactly for `Re s>1/2`, but the same half-plane boundary occurs for **every nonzero periodic source**. The canonical Green slice therefore selects classical Hilbert criticality, not a Prime-Circle zero selector.

The surviving raw-packet possibility is narrower than merely “finite intermediate coupling.” The most canonical geometric intermediate value `u=1` already has a universal matched-control explanation for its `1/2` boundary, while small `u` carries off-critical zeros and large `u` becomes zero-free. A viable proposal must now produce an actual zero-sensitive invariant at an intermediate locus, or replace the raw packet by a determinant/quotient or different nonlinear object whose divisor is genuinely new. A canonical parameter choice and a critical half-plane boundary are not enough.

## Demand a zero-sensitive destination theorem before interpreting source distinguishability

Prime Circle now exhibits several versions of “more structure” without zero selection: rational radialization compresses into classical packets, irrational radialization becomes lossless decoding, weak genuine coupling inherits an off-critical divisor, the canonical Green slice lands on universal Hilbert criticality, and strong additive exponent collapses to a unique lowest output mode and becomes zero-free. A useful destination must state what nontrivial quotient, selector or relation survives **between** those failures and which independent theorem converts it into control of the relevant zeros.

Source distinguishability, multivariable notation, irrationality, nonseparability, analytic continuation, standard completion, coordinate changes, canonical Green inversion, a `1/2` Hilbert boundary and large coupling are not destination criteria by themselves. The next proposal should identify the exact zero/sign statement first and show why its hypotheses exclude the PC-320 off-critical hypersurface, the PC-322 universal Hilbert threshold, and the PC-321 first-mode-dominance regime rather than merely repackage one of them.
