## Add a destination theorem that selects something stricter than the canonical multivariable zero divisor or autocorrelation quotient

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-033-relative-green-spectrum-factors-through-autocorrelation`.

PC-295--PC-310 separate phase retention, classical Fourier/polyspectral data and stable source separation. At mesh scale the one-level Poisson field is already an invertible cyclic decoder, so source recovery there is not a new RH-sensitive observable.

PC-311--PC-315 close direct bilinear scalar fusion and the complete finite-Fourier orbit: the resulting periodic Dirichlet packets are classical, and canonical scalar eigensectors can have abundant zeros in `Re s>1`.

PC-316 moves before scalar cyclic compression. The pointwise product of two oriented Hardy fields retains two Fourier indices and triple Mellinization lands in a finite bundle of character-weighted Mordell--Tornheim double `L`-functions. PC-317--PC-318 classify every positive one-parameter radial restriction: rational effective slopes collapse to finite Hurwitz packets, while irrational slopes decode the complete source tensor into a Barnes double-zeta packet. There is no useful intermediate radial quotient.

PC-319 tests the source-specific zero hope directly. For the smallest canonical pair `(5,7)`, the exact one-level factor has zeros in `Re s>1`, and Rouché stability shows that those zeros persist for every sufficiently small positive Mordell--Tornheim coupling. PC-320 strengthens this to a local complex-analytic zero hypersurface over an open `(t,u)` neighborhood of the decoupled face; ordinary completion and biholomorphic coordinate mixing cannot remove it.

PC-321 closes the opposite endpoint. For the same canonical packet, `Z_(5,7)(s,t,u) != 0` whenever `Re s>=0`, `Re t>=0`, and `Re u>=5`. Strong additive coupling isolates the unique lowest output mode and destroys the zero geometry on the right polyhalf-plane.

PC-322 tests the most natural coefficient-free intermediate slice. Ordinary downstream Green inversion selects exactly `u=1`, where `(k+l)^(-1)` is the classical Hilbert matrix. The induced Dirichlet vectors enter `ell^2` exactly for `Re s>1/2`, but the same boundary occurs for every nonzero periodic source. The canonical Green slice therefore selects universal Hilbert criticality, not a Prime-Circle zero selector.

PC-323 retains the **full Green operator** instead of scalarizing it. For periodic sources `a,b`, residue grouping gives

`X_(a,b)=A_(a,b) tensor H_1 + S`, with `S` trace class,

where the finite channel `A_(a,b)=L^(-1)a_L b_L^T` has rank one and singular value `c_(a,b)=sqrt(mu_a mu_b)`. The canonical self-adjoint dilation consequently has one universal absolutely-continuous Hilbert band `[-pi c_(a,b),pi c_(a,b)]`; detailed periodic arithmetic survives only in the trace-class relative defect.

PC-324 audits the first relative invariant against the simplest matched controls. A one-residue periodic source pair reduces exactly to one generalized Hilbert matrix and produces classical off-band eigenvalues/relative-determinant zeros depending only on the residue offset. **Off-band relative zeros therefore fail the matched-control test before any Prime-Circle arithmetic enters.**

PC-325 now closes the phase-sensitive continuation of that relative branch much more broadly. For the full separable Green kernel `X_(a,b)=D_a K_1 D_b`, independent Fourier phases of `a` and `b` are removable by left/right diagonal unitaries, and the induced block unitary simultaneously conjugates both the full self-adjoint Green operator and its comparison Hilbert core. The complete spectrum and simultaneously-unitary invariants of the relative pair — including the perturbation determinant where defined — depend only on `|a|` and `|b|`.

For the PC-316 finite Fourier sources, `|a|^2` and `|b|^2` are exactly the cyclic autocorrelations by finite Wiener--Khinchin. Distinct phase-twisted real centered sources can therefore have identical autocorrelations and **identical complete Green/relative spectral data**. The previously open hope that ordinary multi-residue phase interference might survive inside the PC-323 separable operator is closed.

The surviving operator route is now narrower than “retain multi-residue relative data.” Inside the PC-323 Green architecture it must extract a source-specific invariant already visible in the magnitude/autocorrelation pair and then prove that matched autocorrelation controls cannot reproduce the RH-facing effect. Alternatively the operator itself must change so that source phases enter nonseparably — for example through a higher-order polyspectral coupling or a kernel in which the relevant phases cannot be removed by independent left/right gauges. Either route still needs an independent theorem connecting the survivor to zeta zeros or the critical line.

## Demand a zero-sensitive destination theorem before interpreting source distinguishability

Prime Circle now exhibits several versions of “more structure” without zero selection: rational radialization compresses into classical packets, irrational radialization becomes lossless decoding, weak genuine coupling inherits an off-critical divisor, strong coupling becomes zero-free, the canonical Green slice lands on universal Hilbert criticality, the full Green lift has only one universal Hilbert absolutely-continuous band, one-channel relative zeros are classical, and the **entire separable Green relative spectrum quotients independent source phase down to autocorrelation**.

Source distinguishability, multivariable notation, irrationality, nonseparability in the scalar Dirichlet expression, analytic continuation, standard completion, coordinate changes, canonical Green inversion, a `1/2` Hilbert boundary, operator lifting, the existence of a relative determinant, off-band zeros, relative scattering data or multi-residue indexing are not destination criteria by themselves. The next proposal should state the exact autocorrelation-sensitive or genuinely nonseparable phase-sensitive zero/sign statement first, show why matched periodic controls cannot reproduce it, and then provide the theorem turning that residual structure into an RH-relevant zero restriction.
