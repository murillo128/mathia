## Leave subcritical one-sided graded eigenvalue spectralization, not merely bounded similarity

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-045-reciprocity-closed-signature-is-abelian-trivial-and-rank-two-at-first-lie-level` through `MI-052-proper-grade-hamiltonians-do-not-spectralize-bounded-raising-data`.

PC-347--PC-360 show that increasingly rich finite signature/spectral constructions remain reciprocal-control blind. Scalar grade weights either compactify positive-degree creation or, at the geometric borderline, reduce modulo compacts to ordinary radially rescaled free-Fock transport. Even the first obvious source/control spectral-radius gap can be reproduced by a matched nonarithmetic one-particle metric.

PC-361 closes the most direct bounded-Hamiltonian repair. A proper diagonal grade Hamiltonian `H_0` with compact resolvent plus any bounded strictly grade-raising source term `Q` has exactly the grade spectrum of `H_0`, with the same algebraic multiplicities. The source may change eigenvectors, Riesz projections, Jordan structure or resolvent norms while leaving eigenvalues blind.

PC-362 shows that simply making the raising term unbounded is still insufficient. If `Q` remains strictly grade-raising and `H_0`-relatively bounded with relative bound below one, the closed operator `H_0+Q` still has exactly the bare grade eigenvalues and algebraic multiplicities. In the explicit weighted-shift family, however, crossing the relative-growth threshold can change the infinite-depth domain problem qualitatively: finite truncations remain triangular while the full operator may lose point spectrum. The meaningful category boundary is therefore critical/supercritical domain geometry, not the adjective “unbounded.”

The live escape routes are now sharper: source-dependent domain/boundary data, critical or supercritical one-sided growth justified by the geometry, same-grade/backward/two-sided coupling, uncontrolled mixed-degree structure, or a non-eigenvalue observable whose arithmetic relevance survives matched controls.

## Keep source recovery, triangular position, domain growth, metric control and spectral observable separate

A representation may recover the source path and still lose orientation after spectral quotienting. Breaking bounded reciprocal similarity can compactify the operator. Preserving essential transport can still reduce to radial Fock geometry. Placing bounded or subcritical relatively bounded source data strictly above a proper grading can leave the entire eigenvalue spectrum unchanged, while a supercritical infinite-depth limit can change spectral category even though every finite truncation looks identical.

A future RH-facing discriminator must therefore state not only the operator category but **where the source term sits relative to the grading, how its graph/domain growth scales, and which spectral datum reaches the zeta-zero destination**. Unboundedness, nonzero essential spectrum or source/control radius differences do not by themselves supply that map.
