## Preserve relational source information beyond independent cyclic averaging

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-019-independent-cyclic-noncommutativity-collapses-to-one-hankel-moment-matrix`.

PC-295--PC-298 identify the diffuse fixed-scale boundary: the normalized boundary-log operator approaches a universal square-zero class, with source information surviving only at or below the explicit defect scale `r_q`. PC-299 then identifies the first positive quadratic defect there as a Fejer-smoothed source autocorrelation, so squaring and averaging loses Fourier phase and lands in classical additive prime exponential-sum data.

PC-300 closes the most direct multi-profile repair. Source-coupled pair profiles can form genuinely noncommuting matrices before averaging, but if the source residues around the cycle are sampled independently then every averaged `k`-loop factors exactly as

`E L_k = tr((D Gbar_mu)^k)`.

The single matrix `Gbar_mu` is real symmetric Hankel-type and depends only on the reflection-even part of the additive source Fourier transform. Hence the full independently averaged cyclic hierarchy is just the moment sequence of one self-adjoint matrix. Replacing the source law by its reflection leaves **every** loop unchanged.

The surviving route must therefore preserve relational information **before independent averaging collapses it**. Concrete possibilities are reused/correlated source variables, source-dependent ordering, non-cyclic tensor contractions, growing-depth regimes where collision corrections are load-bearing, cross-level compositions, or constructions that avoid the centered-window reflection symmetrization. Merely increasing the loop length of independently sourced profiles adds powers, not a new information carrier.

## Keep defect resolution, source phase and dependence structure separate

Reaching `O(r_q)` is necessary to escape the universal square-zero bulk but not sufficient. PC-299 shows that positive quadratic compression at that scale forgets phase. PC-300 shows that noncommutativity before expectation is likewise insufficient when independence factorizes the product into powers of one averaged matrix.

Any next observable must state which non-factorizing source relation survives its averaging procedure. A higher-order statistic is not automatically higher-information: if its source variables separate before the expectation, the hierarchy may still be generated by one low-order averaged object.
