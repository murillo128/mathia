## Add source-native zero-selecting structure before periodic scalarization

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-028-canonical-mixed-packet-fails-finite-fourier-self-duality`.

PC-295--PC-310 progressively separate phase retention, classical Fourier/polyspectral data and stable source separation. At the mesh boundary `1-r=Theta(1/q)`, the sampled Poisson field is already an invertible cyclic decoder; single-level recovery there is source reconstruction rather than a new RH-sensitive observable.

PC-311 closes the direct cross-level escape: bilinearly pairing two centered prime-level fields passes to one periodic coefficient on `Z/(qr)Z`, and the double Mellin transform becomes a finite bundle of ordinary `L(s+t,chi)` functions. PC-312 proves that the canonical coefficient is not a finite-Fourier eigenvector. PC-313 then shows that adjoining its Fourier dual gives only the universal Hurwitz swap relation; diagonalizing produces the manually symmetrized scalar sectors `d±F_N d` for every even periodic sequence.

PC-314 now makes that post-compression closure decisively unsuitable as an RH-like repair. For the smallest admissible canonical mixed pair `(q,r)=(5,7)`, both source-generated Fourier eigensectors fail the exceptional `P(s)L(s,chi)` classification. Saias--Weingartner then forces linearly many zeros in strips strictly inside `Re s>1`. Thus the exact scalar functional equations are not merely universal: **the canonical source-native eigensectors themselves have abundant zeros far to the right of the critical line**.

The live construction must therefore retain source-forced structure before the reduction to one periodic coefficient and its universal Fourier closure, and it must arrive with an independently justified zero-selection theorem. Candidates may retain separate prime-level indices, pointed/shell state, nonlinear coupling before cyclic diagonalization, or a source-dependent matrix/operator not conjugate to the universal finite-Fourier involution. Merely symmetrizing the compressed packet or adding transform-generated components is closed.

## Demand a zero-sensitive destination theorem before interpreting source distinguishability

Stable sub-mesh observations collapse matched sources; mesh-scale one-level observations decode the source; direct bilinear fusion classicalizes; the canonical scalar packet fails self-duality; the universal Fourier repair is source-independent; and PC-314 shows that its canonical scalar eigensectors can have infinitely many zeros in the half-plane of absolute convergence.

A viable observable must therefore state what additional source-native quantity survives **before** these reductions and which theorem converts that quantity into zero confinement. Functional-equation symmetry by itself is no longer a plausible destination criterion for this branch. Future mixed-conductor proposals should be reduced on the common refinement and audited for the exact state retained before scalar periodicization and for the source-specific mechanism that controls its divisor.
