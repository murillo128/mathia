## Preserve a stable nonclassical carrier before mesh-scale source decoding becomes available

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-025-uniformly-transport-stable-single-level-geometry-collapses-before-the-polygon-mesh-scale`.

PC-295--PC-301 identify the diffuse fixed-scale boundary: the normalized boundary-log operator approaches a universal square-zero class, positive quadratic compression loses phase, independently averaged loop hierarchies collapse to one real symmetric average, and centered same-source Gram formation erases coordinatewise orientation before averaging.

PC-302--PC-303 show that keeping oriented labels restores only classical Fourier/polyspectral source data at fixed order. PC-304--PC-305 then show that growing ordinary moments reconstruct the complete source, while PC-306 makes the exact-information boundary even more extreme: at prime conductor, the normalized centroid of any nonempty vertex subset is already injective.

PC-307 prices the true prime low/polylogarithmic packet. For the disjoint one-step translated prime support, PNT/Li asymptotics make the packet distance only

`O((1+log(2+K))/(q log q))`,

so a fixed-margin decoder needs almost `q log q` conditioning throughout the polylogarithmic range.

PC-308 closes the ordinary unweighted larger-bandwidth escape. The exact translation phase gives `Delta_q(K)<=2 pi K/q`, hence every sublinear bandwidth `K=o(q)` still has vanishing separation. Linear bandwidth changes the behavior, but for a classical reason: parity and fixed-denominator rational resonances governed by PNT in arithmetic progressions/Ramanujan major arcs already give order-one separation.

PC-309 now removes the idea that replacing finite packets by a smooth full geometric field avoids this boundary. The source and its disjoint one-step rotate satisfy

`W_p(nu_q,nu_q^+) <= 2 sin(pi/q) = O(1/q)`

for every physical Wasserstein metric. Any uniformly transport-Lipschitz single-level observable with fixed output margin must therefore have condition number `Omega(q)`. For the complete normalized logarithmic potential the whole interior field collapses uniformly whenever `q(1-r_q)->infinity`; the first unresolved boundary layer is exactly the polygon mesh scale `1-r=O(1/q)`.

The live route must therefore become genuinely singular or genuinely non-single-level. A viable mechanism must use a mesh-scale boundary operator with a canonically justified diverging norm, a Li-centered/source-sensitive residual that survives the matched rotation, a mixed-conductor/cross-level coupling, or another topology whose order-one output cannot be reduced to ordinary mesh decoding or classical major-arc congruence information.

## Explain what is gained by the singular mesh-scale regime

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-024-exact-cyclotomic-recovery-can-be-algebraically-lossless-and-analytically-useless`, `MI-025-uniformly-transport-stable-single-level-geometry-collapses-before-the-polygon-mesh-scale`.

The ordinary and transport-stable interpolation is now classified sharply. Every sublinear ordinary packet is unstable. Every uniformly transport-stable single-level observable with `o(q)` modulus is unstable. Linear Fourier modes and mesh-scale geometric resolution can separate the matched pair, but they spend exactly the `Theta(q)` angular sensitivity needed to resolve individual polygon steps, and their first stable arithmetic content is classical congruence structure.

A surviving proposal must therefore state its singularity rather than hiding it. If its transport modulus is `Omega(q)` or its boundary layer is `O(1/q)`, the proof must identify what nonclassical, zero-sensitive quantity becomes accessible there before full source decoding. Singular weights or boundary evaluation are admissible only with an explicit destination norm and controlled amplification; mixed-conductor structure must show that its separation is not just one-level mesh information in another basis.

## Keep defect resolution, carrier gauge, Fourier classicalization, algebraic injectivity, transport stability and arithmetic usefulness separate

PC-299--PC-303 classify several ways phase/orientation either disappears or reduces to classical Fourier data. PC-304--PC-306 show that exact source identification can occur long before stable analytic separation. PC-307 quantifies the severe low/polylogarithmic instability for the actual prime packet. PC-308 completes the ordinary-bandwidth picture, and PC-309 lifts the same matched-source obstruction to every uniformly transport-stable single-level physical geometry plus the full pre-mesh logarithmic-potential field.

The relevant resource is therefore not how many exact moments determine the source or how geometric the representation looks. It is how the chosen observation separates admissible sources at the precision and conditioning consumed by the destination, and what arithmetic content becomes available at the first scale where that separation is stable. Recovering mesh-scale source structure is not RH progress unless an additional theorem couples that structure to a zero-sensitive destination.
