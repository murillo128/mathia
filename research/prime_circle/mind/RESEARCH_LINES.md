PC-369--PC-370 show that order-forgetting constructions remain matched by the reciprocal common-unitary control. PC-371 supplies a genuine source-native escape: shell-2 pointwise radial order has a strict cone that the sign-flipped reciprocal control violates, but its canonical moment data are universal and Ramanujan-classical. PC-372 identifies the complete shell-2 order polynomial with the maximal-real cyclotomic polynomial and natural power maps with Chebyshev/cyclotomic refinement. PC-373--PC-374 then show that the derivative weight is an arcsine coboundary and the critical half-density completion is unitarily equivalent to the adjoint of Chebyshev composition.

PC-375 closes arbitrary unimodular branch-phase decoration of one map, and PC-376 closes the obvious finite multidegree repair. For pairwise-coprime degrees, the canonical refinements decompose into countably many standard polydisk-shift copies, so prime-degree tuples and matched composite-degree tuples have the same joint model. Scalar combinations have disk radius `sum |a_j|`; the half-density phases only rotate coefficients.

PC-377 classifies the literal infinite scalar assembly of those same refinement channels on the canonical finite-mode core. For `A_a=sum_p a_p P_p`, closability holds exactly for `(a_p) in ell^2`, while boundedness holds exactly in `ell^1`, with norm `sum_p |a_p|`. The source-derived coefficients `a_p(s)=p^(1-s)` are nonclosable for `Re(s)<=3/2`, including the half-density line, and bounded only for `Re(s)>2`. Matched prime-power degrees move both thresholds, so these boundaries are sequence-space/degree-growth effects rather than arithmetic critical geometry.

PC-378 closes the most direct multiplicative repair. The algebraic commuting Euler product `prod_p (I-p^(1-s)P_p)^(-1)` is already, on each finite mode, the weighted divisor transform `e_n -> sum_(d|n)d^(1-s)e_(n/d)`. Under the Bohr lift its adjoint multiplier is exactly `zeta(w+conj(s)-1)`. It inherits the same sharp closability threshold `Re(s)>3/2` and boundedness threshold `Re(s)>2`, and prime-power controls move them again. The explicit appearance of zeta is therefore a collapse to the classical divisor/Euler-product multiplier already present in the line, not a new spectral mechanism.

The live question is consequently beyond finite canonical families, direct scalar all-prime sums and their commuting Euler-product completion. A surviving route must retain information destroyed by the divisor/Bohr quotient: source-forced matrix or noncommuting cross-shell coupling, nonlinear interaction before Chebyshev reduction, or a genuinely source-forced domain/topology not given by closure of the canonical finite-mode graph. Repackaging the same commuting shifts by addition or multiplication no longer counts as a new infinite-family escape.
