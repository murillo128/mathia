## Apply the strongest matched and classicalization controls after the representation is fixed

For full fibers, an order-one spike, concentration phenomenon or singular phase transition is not arithmetic evidence when the same pushforward occurs for matched composite degrees. For primitive fibers, prime/composite separation is also insufficient: exact-order restriction supplies classical coprimality data, the primorial boundary preselects the rough-number set, and one-step pairwise/Laplacian/Kron transport can be reconstructed from the support update and deleted incident star.

Multi-deletion linear Kron transport now needs the full carrier restriction before any nonlinear spectral observable is credited. After removing independent star responses, the irreducible correction has rank at most `k-1`; fixed-gap determinants cannot amplify it, and PC-393 shows that the common zero mode does not reopen the normalized determinant branch at `lambda=0`. A claimed higher-boundary mechanism must therefore exhibit a source-sensitive exceptional/cross-level observable or leave the positive complete Laplacian/Kron category rather than treating small regularization or determinant nonlinearity itself as a new carrier.

Credit a new Prime-Circle branch only after identifying the surviving source datum, reducing its classical Möbius/Ramanujan/divisor/rough-number and support-gap content explicitly, and showing that the residue affects the intended destination. A non-scalar matrix, Laplacian oscillation, Schur fill-in, simultaneous vertex deletion, small spectral regulator, or normalized determinant on prime-selected support is not by itself a new mechanism.
