# Prime-circle research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-circle intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they discriminate among mathematically different mechanisms.

## Target the residual after fixed-support, canonical refinement, strip-accretive Bloch control, and one-hole controls

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-anchor-required-but-local-anchor-is-classical`, `MI-006-mesoscopic-defect-survives-bulk-but-not-fixed-window-classicalization`, and `MI-007-bloch-half-reflection-is-universal-time-reversal`.

Every fixed connected scalar support is controlled by finite Hardy--Littlewood tuple data, including all-orders `log det` resummation. The canonical full-chord refinement carrier is constrained too: repeated-prime full fibers reduce to one fixed quadratic pencil, new-prime normalized bulk converges to a base-level Bloch law, and the one-missing-residue puncture has only a bounded positive spectral-shift budget reproduced by composite fiber sizes.

PC-159--PC-160 close the most tempting analytic interpretation of that pencil. Its exact `t <-> 1-t` reflection holds for arbitrary root subsets, while the complete-lift positivity makes the analytically continued pencil strictly accretive and zero-free throughout `0<Re(t)<1`; every polynomial eigenvalue lies on the real exterior intervals. The live target is therefore a conductor-growing fine-fiber/provenance residual, nonlinear or noncommuting cross-level coupling, or another operation that genuinely breaks this universal complete-lift positivity.

## Distinguish average top-band capture from uniform subspace fidelity

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-006-mesoscopic-defect-survives-bulk-but-not-fixed-window-classicalization`.

Short-chord models recover the first top-band projector strongly in average/Mertens senses while operator-norm leakage can persist. A positive spectral mechanism must state which topology its arithmetic conclusion needs and show that the residual worst-direction subspace remains source-specific after the refinement and prime-blind puncture controls.

## Locate the first carrier beyond canonical finite cyclotomic/collision/Bloch spectralization

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-anchor-required-but-local-anchor-is-classical` and `MI-007-bloch-half-reflection-is-universal-time-reversal`.

Finite networks, repeated shells, coincidence strata, Toeplitz/CMV/resultant constructions, vertexwise Hessian collision response, finite Bloch pencils, their exact half-reflection, and their strip hyperbolicity all classicalize or become universal. A genuinely different carrier must be all-shell, provenance-sensitive, singular/domain-changing, nonlinear across levels, or otherwise outside finite confluent/cyclic complete-lift algebra.

## Use compatible completion as a carrier, not as an automatic Hamiltonian

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-compatible-completion-lacks-coercive-rh-scale`.

The adelic solenoid preserves exact-order labels, while regular commuting calculi and broad scalar dilation covariances have the wrong compact-resolvent scale. A useful completion must force a non-scalar or relative operator that cannot be decoded as ordinary projective height or scalar dilation.

## Cross the Hardy boundary before the one-new-prime corrector becomes infinitesimal

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-005-hardy-shell-core-is-universal`.

Canonical conductor limits, bounded preconditioning, and scalar zooms converge to universal Hilbert/Carleman/Gaussian behavior or destroy determinant normality. Any surviving Hardy mechanism must act before universalization, through a jointly organized all-shell object or a geometry-forced non-scalar singular operation.
