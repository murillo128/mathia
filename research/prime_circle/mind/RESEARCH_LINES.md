## Combine the genuine radial order cone with a source-forced cross-shell operation that generic unit-circle controls cannot reproduce

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-052-proper-grade-hamiltonians-do-not-spectralize-bounded-raising-data`, `MI-053-shell2-order-escapes-parity-but-classicalizes-to-ramanujan-data`.

PC-369 and PC-370 extend the common-unitary obstruction beyond Fock word data: Chen--Hopf/log signatures and centered cyclotomic refinement remain equivariant under the reciprocal matched control whenever the construction forgets pointwise radial order.

PC-371 corrects the frontier rather than extending that no-go. The shell-2 centered profile has an intrinsic strict radial order cone: it is negative away from the radial ends, inversion-symmetric and uniquely minimized at the common vertex. The sign-flipped reciprocal control is outside this cone, so order really does break the PC-355 common-unitary parity control.

The first canonical use of that escape is nevertheless too universal. After the coordinate `y=4r/(1+r)^2`, the positive potential is a finite Hausdorff-moment potential with atoms `lambda_j=cos^2(theta_j/2)`. Its moments are finite binomial transforms of Ramanujan sums, and the same order cone is reproduced by arbitrary conjugation-stable non-arithmetic unit-circle root configurations. Full moment recovery merely decodes the original angular shell.

The live problem is therefore narrower: couple the genuine source-native order cone to an additional cross-shell or nonlocal arithmetic operation that generic unit-circle configurations fail, while avoiding a construction whose complete invariants simply reconstruct the cyclotomic root set or return to classical Ramanujan/Dirichlet data.

## Apply both matched controls at the representation where they are strongest

For order-forgetting constructions, the reciprocal common-unitary control remains decisive. For order-sensitive constructions, the stronger adversary is the family of arbitrary conjugation-stable unit-circle root configurations, which reproduces the entire shell-2 cone. A candidate earns arithmetic discrimination only if it survives the first control by retaining order and then survives the second by using structure not present in a generic root configuration.
