# Prime-circle research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-circle intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Leave one-shell reciprocal Fourier zeros unless a source principle selects the deformation

**Linked intuition:** `MI-011-scalar-fourier-zero-existence-is-deformation-dependent`.

PC-193--PC-196 show that scalar reciprocal-shell Fourier sign, zero existence, and first-zero scale depend on the chosen exponent. The attractive `p^(3/4)` quadratic scale is a singular even-exponent resonance and does not survive fixed `1<alpha<2` deformations. A useful scalar law therefore needs a source-forced operator/deformation rather than a representation selected because it creates favorable zeros.

## Leave the entire fixed finite scalar character/cone packet algebra before calling cross-shell fusion new

**Linked intuition:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra`.

PC-197 identifies the global Cauchy--Poisson character packet with ordinary Dirichlet `L`-data. PC-198 shows that finite holomorphic products can mix coefficients without creating genuinely mixed-root poles. PC-199 shows that the canonical sesquilinear same-angle fusion collapses to the product character, while PC-200 shows that source-native power-map pullbacks merely replace the diagonal by a rational Fourier ray that again reduces to one Dirichlet packet.

PC-201 crosses that one-ray boundary without escaping classical packet algebra: fixed finite one-output Haar fusion produces Mordell--Tornheim multiple `L`-data. PC-202 closes the several-output loophole as well. Arbitrary fixed finite numbers of holomorphic and antiholomorphic packet legs leave the balanced lattice `sum k_i=sum ell_j`; independent radial Mellin transform gives a finite colored rational-cone Dirichlet series, reducible by rational-cone subdivision to established conical/cyclotomic packet data. The first `2+2` case already decomposes into two explicit three-dimensional chambers, and primitive composite conductors obey the same species.

A viable cross-shell mechanism must therefore preserve usable multi-index information **before fixed finite scalar Haar/Mellin contraction**, or leave that scalar conical algebra through a source-forced matrix/operator-valued, non-Haar, infinite-refinement, non-scalar positivity, or genuinely global construction. Higher finite valence, balanced Fourier support, or a higher-dimensional cone alone is no longer a discriminator.

## Require quantitative deformation and algebraic-closure controls of any proposed spectral relation

A candidate should survive source-compatible scalar deformations and then be reduced against the complete finite packet algebra generated by the intrinsic character fields, power-map correspondences, and arbitrary fixed finite same-angle scalar Haar fusions. Stability does not require fixed zero locations; it requires that the claimed arithmetic discriminator survive after boundary/refinement asymptotics, rational-ray and rational-cone classicalization, and arbitrary finite wrapper factors are removed.

## Keep topology and realization separate from scalar cyclotomic identities

Potential-theoretic, geometric, operator-valued, infinite-refinement, or monodromy enlargements remain live only when the required object exists canonically and retains information beyond the classical cyclotomic/Euler/Dirichlet/conical packet data. A new coordinate system, a finite scalar wrapper, or a higher-dimensional balanced scalar cone around the same packet is not a new mechanism.
