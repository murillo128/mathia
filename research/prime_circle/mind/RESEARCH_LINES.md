# Prime-circle research lines

This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Prime-Circle evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Find source-forced structure beyond universal scalar shell multiplication

PC-391--PC-398 isolate simultaneous-deletion positive Kron transport, price its thin contrast carrier, close every fixed polynomial window at `alpha>=1/2`, and show that below the endpoint the surviving smooth macroscopic sector converges after normalization to a universal Buchstab--Riesz continuum form reproduced by matched point clouds. The live positive-Kron resource is therefore not raw operator amplitude below `alpha=1/2`, but the residual beyond that continuum mode: centered fluctuations, mesoscopic/arithmetic-scale vectors, cross-level orientation, or another source datum that matched controls cannot reproduce.

PC-400--PC-401 close the single-measure Jacobi/Krein spectral repackaging escape on the shell-2 moment side. The positive Hankel moment sequence defines multiplication by the shell coordinate on `[0,log 2]`, and its Jacobi spectrum is the whole interval independently of the arithmetic source. Passing to the natural indefinite/Krein version does not change that spectral set. The source can alter the cyclic spectral measure, recurrence data and sign partition, but not the support spectrum itself.

PC-402 closes the most canonical two-form extension. Pairing a target shell Hankel form with the intrinsic positive shell-2 form gives the relative operator `M_(w_n)` on `L^2([0,log 2],dt)`, where `w_n` is exactly the previously known radial-flux density. Its generalized spectrum is only the scalar range `[min w_n,max w_n]`, and finite cross-Hankel pencils are Ritz approximations whose extremal generalized eigenvalues converge to those endpoints. The prime-power sign transition is therefore the old radial-flux criterion, not a new spectral localization mechanism.

The immediate audit is stronger than asking for a non-scalar, indefinite or generalized-eigenvalue operator. A new spectral route must use information not reducible to multiplication by one scalar shell density relative to a recovered reference measure: for example a source-forced off-diagonal cross-shell kernel, cross-level/refinement coupling before moment reduction, a genuinely independent second form, mesoscopic correlations, or nonlinear interaction structure. Merely changing Hilbert to Krein, forming a Jacobi matrix, or pairing shell moments against the canonical shell-2 Hankel matrix does not create new arithmetic spectral information.

## Apply matched controls after every representation change

A candidate Prime-Circle mechanism should be reduced in this order: identify the actual source-selected carrier, quotient universal compact/Riesz geometry, determine whether a proposed second form is genuinely independent or only a scalar density relative to the first, price the amplitude on the surviving carrier, and finally test it on a matched nonarithmetic point-cloud or measure control. Schur fill-in, simultaneous deletion, long polynomial windows, positive subcritical contrast, Hankel positivity, indefinite multiplication and the canonical shell-2 cross-Hankel pencil can all produce rich operators while leaving the decisive destination invariant universal or already known.
