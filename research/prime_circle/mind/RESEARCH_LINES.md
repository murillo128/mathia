# Prime-circle research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable prime-circle intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Preserve a stable nonclassical carrier before exact source decoding becomes available

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-024-exact-cyclotomic-recovery-can-be-algebraically-lossless-and-analytically-useless`.

PC-295--PC-301 identify the diffuse fixed-scale boundary: the normalized boundary-log operator approaches a universal square-zero class, positive quadratic compression loses phase, independently averaged loop hierarchies collapse to one real symmetric average, and centered same-source Gram formation erases coordinatewise orientation before averaging.

PC-302 tests the simplest pre-Gram repair. Reflection acts by latent mode reversal, so unlabelled frame/subspace geometry remains gauge. Keeping labels restores a reflection-odd channel, but its first quadratic component is exactly sine/cosine Fourier covariance.

PC-303 closes the entire fixed finite tensor extension: every labelled frame monomial is one additive character of the source coordinates, averaging gives a joint Fourier coefficient, and fixed polynomial invariants generate the classical polyspectral algebra. PC-304 shows degrees through `q-1` recover the complete additive DFT, and PC-305 lowers a constructive sparse-moment recovery scale to `L_q=pi(q)-2` consecutive moments or `dB>=L_q` in a windowed tensor hierarchy.

PC-306 shows that even those constructive thresholds are not intrinsic information thresholds. At prime conductor the normalized centroid of any nonempty vertex subset is already injective; for the canonical source the exact degree-one oriented moment determines the full prime support as a cyclotomic-field element. Exact finite-level “contains the source” therefore occurs essentially immediately.

The same finding separates that algebraic fact from stable analytic information. The prime support and its one-step translate are disjoint, but their first `K` Fourier coefficients differ by at most `2 pi K/q`. Every `K=o(q)` packet is therefore topologically unable to keep a fixed margin without amplification of order at least `q/K`; even the PC-305 scale `K=L_q=O(q/log q)` requires logarithmically diverging conditioning.

The live route is consequently not to locate a new exact reconstruction threshold. It must identify a geometry-forced operation acting in a declared topology/precision **before or instead of unstable exact decoding**, and prove that its output retains an order-one zero-sensitive discriminator with controlled conditioning. Genuinely cross-level or mixed-conductor structure remains an honest escape only when it cannot be reproduced by exact finite-level source recovery followed by post-processing.

## Keep defect resolution, carrier gauge, Fourier classicalization, algebraic injectivity and analytic conditioning separate

Reaching the `r_q` defect scale is necessary to escape the universal square-zero bulk but not sufficient. PC-299--PC-301 show that positive quadratic compression, independence and centered Gram formation can erase phase/orientation before any higher statistic is formed. PC-302--PC-303 show that labelled oriented frames restore only classical Fourier/polyspectral source data at fixed order.

PC-304--PC-305 add constructive source-reconstruction saturation. PC-306 then shows that exact cyclotomic injectivity can precede both of those scales while remaining violently nonuniform in the ordinary growing-`q` Fourier topology. The relevant resource is therefore not how many exact moments determine the source, but how the chosen observation separates admissible sources at the precision and conditioning consumed by the destination.

Any growing-order proposal must state which frequencies become available, what topology measures their difference, how the required inverse/amplification constant scales, which destination operation acts before source reconstruction, and what theorem distinguishes that intermediate regime from ordinary exact decoding. Recovering the source algebraically is not RH progress; a useful carrier needs stable, source-specific and zero-sensitive transport.
