## Require incidence beyond the scalar Haar/profinite prime-power quotient

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-011-scalar-fourier-zero-existence-is-deformation-dependent`, `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra`.

That repair still collapses on the prime-power sector. No irreducible mixed-prime coefficient survives.

A viable old/new mechanism must therefore retain structure outside both the scalar Haar-refinement algebra and this compact-open/profinite mask incidence: genuinely mixed-composite masks, vertexwise old/new incidence not reducible to support projections, non-Haar matrix fibers, growing nonlinear tensors, a distinguished source algebra coupled before the profinite quotient, or global uniformization/monodromy. Each candidate must exhibit a prime-specific relation that fails on the corresponding integer/refinement control before arithmetic meaning is assigned.
