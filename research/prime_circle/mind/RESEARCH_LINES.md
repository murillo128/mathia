## Demand a destination theorem after scalar Mellin closure

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-040-endpoint-mellin-singularities-form-a-classical-von-mangoldt-jet` through `MI-044-full-shell-finite-measure-nullspace-is-only-the-universal-zeta-factor`.

Enlarging the scalar channel by a finite linear package therefore repackages the same divisor data rather than selecting its zeros.

## Break reciprocal-control similarity without compactifying away the spectrum

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-045-reciprocity-closed-signature-is-abelian-trivial-and-rank-two-at-first-lie-level` through `MI-051-unbounded-reciprocal-parity-is-not-enough-if-completion-compactifies-transport`.

PC-347--PC-355 move beyond the finite linear class. Full noncommutative signature data can reconstruct the centered source path, but triangular spectralization discards the oriented coordinate; inner parity makes semisimple spectra exactly control-blind; outer parity gives only generic contragredient orientation; fixed algebraic representation lifts add weight functions of the same spectrum; and the canonical full-Fock lift remains reciprocal-control conjugate by second-quantized parity.

PC-356 shows that a source-native Gram metric recomputed covariantly on the control restores an exact isometry. PC-357 shows that freezing one parity-breaking tensor metric can destroy unitarity but not similarity. PC-358 removes the factorization loophole completely at every finite cutoff: for any fixed positive Hermitian metric, whitening preserves exact reciprocal similarity. At infinite depth the same blindness persists whenever algebraic parity extends boundedly to the completion.

PC-359 tests the first genuine escape. Scalar grade weights can make reciprocal parity unbounded whenever the one-particle parity norm exceeds one, so the bounded-similarity argument really fails. But if every fixed-lag ratio `w_(m+k)/w_m` tends to zero, the same completion compactifies every fixed positive-degree creation block. Any norm-convergent strictly grade-raising multiplier `Q` is then compact and has `sigma(Q)={0}`, hence `sigma(I+Q)={1}`. The source and control need not be boundedly similar for their ordinary spectra to become separately trivial.

The surviving completion problem therefore has **two independent gates**. Reciprocal parity must fail to extend as a bounded intertwiner, and the natural source multiplier must retain enough high-grade transport to remain spectrally nontrivial. The next useful test is the borderline noncompact regime—for example weights with a nonzero asymptotic fixed-lag ratio—or an operator/domain construction whose source/control relation is not merely the old algebraic involution on a more strongly regularized space. Unbounded or closed multipliers, non-scalar grade couplings, same-grade/backward interactions and genuinely different observables remain outside PC-359.

## Keep source recovery, automorphism quotient, similarity and transport compactness separate

Prime Circle now has several exact endpoints. Scalar measurement reconstructs classical zeta structure; noncommutative depth reconstructs the path; spectral quotients can erase orientation; reciprocal automorphisms can make source and control conjugate; arbitrary fixed finite Hilbert metrics still leave a nonunitary similarity; and an infinite completion can break bounded similarity while simultaneously collapsing positive-degree spectral transport to a compact quasinilpotent.

The audit should therefore identify the destination discriminator first, write the reciprocal algebraic intertwiner before choosing a Hilbert norm, and then ask two questions separately: does the completion change the boundedness/domain status of that intertwiner, and does it preserve noncompact source transport rather than regularizing the operator to a trivial spectrum? Progress requires a source-forced invariant that survives both audits and is independently tied to zeta-zero geometry.
