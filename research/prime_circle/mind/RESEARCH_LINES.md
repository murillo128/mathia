## Find source-forced interaction before the descendant state collapses to one-shell Mellin data

PC-414 removes the remaining cross-prime-conductor loophole at finite cutoff. At each Mellin frequency the descendant vector is the image of the scalar source amplitude under the rank-one map `z -> z c_n(t)`. Therefore an **arbitrary source-independent conductor matrix**, even dense and coupling valuations at different rational primes, pulls back to

`A_h(t,u)=hhat_n(t) conjugate(hhat_n(u)) K_W(t,u)`.

The same rank-one factorization holds for fixed-arity conductor tensors, finite spectral determinants and growing finite-cutoff conductor operators. A matched arbitrary base profile reproduces the entire conductor factor. Cross-prime dependence, large matrix rank or growing support do not restore source specificity when they act only after this scalarization.

PC-415 closes the natural infinite-frame continuation in the native trace-class regime. The complete Dirichlet-weighted descendant orbit defines a positive trace-class frame operator, but its two-frequency Mellin kernel is still the PC-408 universal degree-two conductor symbol multiplied by the outer one-shell source amplitudes. Arbitrary Mellin phase changes of the base source act by unitary conjugacy on the frame operator and leave the conductor Gram exactly unchanged. Hence the complete nonzero spectrum, all Schatten traces and the Fredholm determinant depend on the source only through the one-shell Mellin power spectrum; the remaining conductor interaction is universal.

PC-416 then tests the first canonical way to break that phase gauge rather than merely spectralize it. The log-scale position operator `Qf=(log x)f` is intrinsic to the dilation representation and genuinely sees Mellin phase because it becomes `i partial_t`. Nevertheless every finite word in `Q` and the descendant multipliers normal-orders to a finite differential expression in the **same one-shell Mellin transform**, with coefficients from derivatives of the universal conductor symbols. The full finite-`Q`-jet descendant frame is correspondingly just a finite derivative of the PC-415 source-times-universal-conductor kernel. For the actual prime-circle source, those jets are the classical Gamma/zeta/local-prime-factor derivatives; the first logarithmic jet already returns `zeta'/zeta` rather than a new source mechanism.

PC-417 extends this obstruction beyond finite jets. The intrinsic unitary group `exp(iaQ)` produces real Mellin translations, and its bounded Fourier-Stieltjes calculus normal-orders against descendant multipliers into finite-measure superpositions of the same one-shell Mellin function sampled along `Re(s)=1`. Horizontal contour motion requires the unbounded nonunitary weight `exp(beta Q)=x^beta`, which merely chooses another Mellin line and can insert `Re(s)=1/2` by hand.

PC-418 identifies the opposite boundary of that continuation. The exact prime-child operators recover every prime dilation, so in logarithmic radius they generate the dense translation subgroup `log Q_{>0}`. Together with the already intrinsic group `exp(iaQ)`, these are a Weyl system. Their unrestricted bounded-Borel/von-Neumann completion is the full algebra `B(L^2(R_+,dx/x))`. Thus simply enlarging PC-417 from Fourier-Stieltjes functions to arbitrary bounded Borel functional calculus does not recover missing source structure; it destroys selectivity because every bounded operator becomes available. A surviving nonlocal route must therefore specify a **proper operator class, kernel and topology independently forced by the Prime-Circle source**, not merely take a stronger closure of the existing radial generators.

The live route must violate an actual hypothesis **before** the shell-2 one-carrier collapse or introduce a proper source-forced structure that remains selective after it. Candidate mechanisms may retain source-derived angular/chord/old-new incidence not determined by descendant conductor labels, alter the descendant state through a genuinely nonlinear source-coupled interaction before Mellin diagonalization, or identify a specific nonlocal Mellin-frequency kernel/proper operator algebra whose formula and topology are themselves forced by the roots-of-unity geometry. Merely choosing a more complicated external conductor kernel, keeping the whole descendant frame, adjoining finite powers of `log x`, taking bounded Fourier-Stieltjes functions of `Q`, or passing to unrestricted Borel/strong/weak closure is closed by PC-414--PC-418.

The decisive test for any proposed escape is a matched-control factorization and novelty audit before analytic continuation. If the candidate can still be written as a universal conductor functional multiplied or congruenced by one-shell source amplitudes, differs only by Mellin-phase conjugacy, closes on one-shell jets/translates for an arbitrary matched base profile, or becomes available only because the generated operator algebra is already all of `B(H)`, its zeta-sensitive poles, derivatives, determinants or spectra belong to the readout rather than to Prime-Circle geometry. A successful route must exhibit source-dependent interaction that cannot be reproduced after replacing the cyclotomic base profile by an arbitrary Mellin-tame one propagated through the same descendant law.
