## Require source-forced collective conductor geometry beyond Ramanujan-compressed prime-axis dynamics

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-011-scalar-fourier-zero-existence-is-deformation-dependent`, `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra`.

PC-197--PC-220 classify the fixed-level scalar and spectral wrappers around the cyclotomic masks. Mixed squarefree masks retain genuine growing tensor locality, but phase transport is a coboundary, packet metrics classicalize to discriminant/Dirichlet data, and the full nonzero transverse spectrum is a signed compression of the standard Ramanujan/exact-order Fourier projector to cyclotomic coefficient support.

PC-221 closes the most direct cross-sector repair at one conductor. The complete unweighted Feshbach/leakage response from the exact-order sector to all proper conductor sectors again factors through the coefficient data and Ramanujan projections; the total conductor-path resolution is complete but not a new arithmetic carrier. PC-222 then closes the canonical scalar source-weighting repair: common-anchor Mangoldt weighting selects prime-primary axes, and scalar functional calculus of that anchor cannot create a genuinely collective mixed-conductor interaction.

PC-223 closes the next noncommutative escape. Repeated mask-mediated words through the Mangoldt prime-axis selector are all moments `J_N^*B_N^rJ_N` of one operator on the thin state space `K_N=oplus_{p|N}E_p`. At every depth they see only conductor orders `1`, `p`, `pq`, `N/p`, and `N`; for `omega(N)>=5` they are exactly blind to the middle mixed-conductor sectors. Their rank is at most `sum_{p|N}(p-1)`, an asymptotically vanishing fraction of the primitive sector along primorials. Noncommutativity and arbitrary word depth therefore do not help when every intermediate step collapses back to prime-primary axes.

The live route is now more specific than “repeat masks” or “go cross-level.” It must **retain mixed exact-order sectors as intermediate state**, transport them through refinement before prime-axis compression, or introduce another intrinsic operation whose state space grows with the mixed conductor lattice. A successful construction must pass an exact information-gain test against controls preserving all one-axis, Ramanujan, and repeated-prime-axis data; merely increasing word depth inside the same thin compression is closed.
