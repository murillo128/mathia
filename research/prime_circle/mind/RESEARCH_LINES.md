# Prime-circle research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable prime-circle intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Add a destination theorem that selects something stricter than the canonical multivariable zero divisor

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-031-strong-additive-coupling-collapses-to-the-lowest-output-mode`.

PC-295--PC-310 separate phase retention, classical Fourier/polyspectral data and stable source separation. At mesh scale the one-level Poisson field is already an invertible cyclic decoder, so source recovery there is not a new RH-sensitive observable.

PC-311--PC-315 close direct bilinear scalar fusion and the complete finite-Fourier orbit: the resulting periodic Dirichlet packets are classical, and canonical scalar eigensectors can have abundant zeros in `Re s>1`.

PC-316 moves before scalar cyclic compression. The pointwise product of two oriented Hardy fields retains two Fourier indices and triple Mellinization lands in a finite bundle of character-weighted Mordell--Tornheim double `L`-functions. PC-317--PC-318 classify every positive one-parameter radial restriction: rational effective slopes collapse to finite Hurwitz packets, while irrational slopes decode the complete source tensor into a Barnes double-zeta packet. There is no useful intermediate radial quotient.

PC-319 tests the source-specific zero hope directly. For the smallest canonical pair `(5,7)`, the exact one-level factor has zeros in `Re s>1`, and Rouché stability shows that those zeros persist for every sufficiently small positive Mordell--Tornheim coupling. PC-320 strengthens this to a local complex-analytic zero hypersurface over an open `(t,u)` neighborhood of the decoupled face; ordinary completion and biholomorphic coordinate mixing cannot remove it.

PC-321 closes the opposite endpoint. For the same canonical packet,

`Z_(5,7)(s,t,u) != 0` whenever `Re s>=0`, `Re t>=0`, and `Re u>=5`.

The mechanism is exact first-output-mode domination: after scaling by `2^u`, the unique `(k,l)=(1,1)` contribution survives while every higher additive output mode is uniformly suppressed. Thus the strong-coupling limit is not a hidden Riemann-like selector; it destroys the zero geometry altogether on the right polyhalf-plane.

The surviving raw-packet possibility is therefore confined to a **finite intermediate coupling region** separated from both endpoints: small `u` provably carries off-critical zeros, while `Re u>=5` is zero-free. A viable proposal must explain why a particular intermediate locus is source-forced and prove a zero-selection theorem there, or replace the raw Mordell--Tornheim divisor by a determinant/quotient or different nonlinear object with genuinely new zeros. Neither “turn on coupling” nor “make coupling strong” supplies the missing selector.

## Demand a zero-sensitive destination theorem before interpreting source distinguishability

Prime Circle now exhibits several versions of “more information” without zero selection: rational radialization compresses into classical packets, irrational radialization becomes lossless decoding, weak genuine coupling inherits an off-critical divisor, and strong additive exponent collapses to a unique lowest output mode and becomes zero-free. A useful destination must state what nontrivial quotient, selector or relation survives **between** those failures and which independent theorem converts it into control of the relevant zeros.

Source distinguishability, multivariable notation, irrationality, nonseparability, analytic continuation, standard completion, coordinate changes and large coupling are not destination criteria by themselves. The next proposal should identify the exact zero/sign statement first and show why its hypotheses exclude both the PC-320 off-critical hypersurface and the PC-321 first-mode-dominance regime rather than merely repackage one of them.
