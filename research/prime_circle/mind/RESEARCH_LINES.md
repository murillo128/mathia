## Add a destination theorem that survives the autocorrelation quotient, fixed-conductor edge-gap law and both growing-conductor backgrounds

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-036-canonical-green-outliers-are-pnt-background-until-low-modes-are-deflated`.

PC-295--PC-315 close scalar one-level and finite-Fourier packet routes: mesh-scale recovery is already a generic cyclic decoder, direct bilinear fusion stays in classical periodic Dirichlet packets, and canonical scalar eigensectors can have abundant zeros in `Re s>1`.

PC-316--PC-321 move before scalar compression and classify the main multivariable/radial alternatives. Rational radial restrictions collapse to finite Hurwitz packets, irrational restrictions decode the full source tensor into Barnes double-zeta data, weak genuine coupling inherits an off-critical divisor, and sufficiently strong additive coupling becomes zero-free on the right polyhalf-plane. More variables do not supply a zero selector by themselves.

PC-322--PC-325 classify the canonical Green lift. The scalar Green slice lands on universal Hilbert criticality; the full operator is one universal Hilbert channel plus a trace-class defect; one-residue controls already produce classical off-band relative zeros; and independent Fourier phases are removable by simultaneous left/right unitary gauge. For the finite Fourier sources the complete separable Green/relative spectral package therefore factors through source magnitudes, equivalently cyclic autocorrelations.

PC-326 adds a second, independent obstruction at fixed conductors. The zeros of the perturbation determinant are exactly the discrete eigenvalues outside the universal Hilbert band, and trace class forces `sum dist(lambda_j,[-E,E]) <= ||V||_1`. The relative zero divisor therefore has an `ell^1` band-edge gap budget and is too sparse for direct identification with the Riemann ordinates, including natural reciprocal/Cayley edge compactifications.

PC-327 audits the first growing-conductor escape. After normalizing by the intrinsic band scale `c=sqrt(mu_a mu_b)`, generalized-Hilbert offsets force logarithmic loss of uniform `S_2` and `S_1` control for every nonzero centered source pair. That divergence is source-independent and therefore not an arithmetic discriminator.

PC-328 shows that the canonical prime-support source has a much larger second background. Its fixed Fourier modes satisfy `a_q(h)=C_h/log q+O_h(log(q)^(-2))`, while the intrinsic Hilbert-band scale is only `mu_q^(1/2)~sqrt(log q/q)`. The `(1,1)` Green coefficient alone then forces band-normalized discrete outlier pairs growing at least like `sqrt(qr)/(log q log r)^(3/2)`; for comparable conductors this is `Q/(log Q)^3`. The band-normalized defect therefore diverges even in operator norm. But the leading amplitude is classical PNT/Li data, and PC-325 supplies exact non-arithmetic phase controls with the same full Green spectrum. The raw outliers are spectral facts, not RH signals.

The surviving Green question is now **double-renormalized residual selectivity**. A viable conductor limit must remove or incorporate both the universal shifted-Hilbert singularity of PC-327 and the canonical low-frequency PNT/Li packet of PC-328, then show that the residual remains tight enough to define a useful invariant and separates the canonical source from matched controls. The next decisive test is low-mode deflation against a frozen smooth PNT/Li reference, followed by a matched-control audit of the residual. It must still survive the autocorrelation quotient and be connected by an independent theorem to a zeta-zero restriction.

## Demand a zero-sensitive destination theorem before interpreting source distinguishability

Prime Circle now exhibits many forms of retained structure without zero selection: lossless cyclic decoding, multivariable source tensors, irrational radial reconstruction, analytic continuation, a universal Hilbert `1/2` boundary, trace-class relative data, source autocorrelation, discrete Green bound states, a universal generalized-Hilbert conductor singularity and PNT-driven escaping canonical outliers. None is a destination criterion by itself.

The next proposal should state the exact source-sensitive zero/sign statement first, identify the matched controls and every universal or smooth source background it must beat, and only then build the representation or operator that is supposed to force it. Source distinguishability and spectral richness count only after the surviving invariant is both control-resistant and connected to the Riemann zero restriction.
