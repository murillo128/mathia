The line `Re(s)=1/2` is exactly the Radon--Nikodym half-density condition transporting Lebesgue measure to the invariant arcsine measure, and the normalized transfer is unitarily equivalent to the adjoint of Chebyshev composition, with universal closed-disk spectrum for prime and composite degrees.

PC-375 closes the most direct phase/orientation repair inside that completion. Its normalized spectrum is still exactly the closed unit disk for every integer degree. Thus retaining branch phase pointwise is not enough if the complete operator remains in the one-map Chebyshev/arcsine weighted-isometry class.

The live question is now mechanism-level. Can a source-forced amplitude, domain/boundary condition, cross-shell matrix coupling, nonlinear interaction or genuinely multi-coordinate operator retain radial/order information without reducing to one weighted Chebyshev isometry or to classical cyclotomic data? A candidate must also survive matched composite-degree controls. Merely choosing a singular function space, preserving branch orientation, or adding unit-modulus phases does not earn arithmetic spectral leverage.
