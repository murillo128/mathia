## Leave bounded one-sided graded spectralization, not merely bounded similarity

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-045-reciprocity-closed-signature-is-abelian-trivial-and-rank-two-at-first-lie-level` through `MI-052-proper-grade-hamiltonians-do-not-spectralize-bounded-raising-data`.

PC-347--PC-360 show that increasingly rich finite signature/spectral constructions remain reciprocal-control blind. Scalar grade weights either compactify positive-degree creation or, at the geometric borderline, reduce modulo compacts to ordinary radially rescaled free-Fock transport. Even the first obvious source/control spectral-radius gap can be reproduced by a matched nonarithmetic one-particle metric.

PC-361 closes the most direct unbounded-Hamiltonian repair of that picture. A proper diagonal grade Hamiltonian `H_0` with compact resolvent plus any bounded strictly grade-raising source term `Q` has exactly the grade spectrum of `H_0`, with the same algebraic multiplicities. The source may change eigenvectors, Riesz projections, Jordan structure or resolvent norms while leaving eigenvalues blind.

The live category change must therefore be stronger than “make the operator unbounded.” It needs source information in nontriangular domain/boundary data, an unbounded signature perturbation, same-grade/backward/two-sided coupling, uncontrolled mixed-degree structure, or a non-eigenvalue observable whose arithmetic relevance survives matched controls.

## Keep source recovery, triangular position, metric control and spectral observable separate

A representation may recover the source path and still lose orientation after spectral quotienting. Breaking bounded reciprocal similarity can compactify the operator. Preserving essential transport can still reduce to radial Fock geometry. Finally, placing bounded source data strictly above a proper grading can leave the entire eigenvalue spectrum unchanged.

A future RH-facing discriminator must state not only the operator category but **where the source term sits relative to the grading and which spectral datum reaches the zeta-zero destination**. Unboundedness, nonzero essential spectrum or source/control radius differences do not by themselves supply that map.
