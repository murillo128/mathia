## Add structure beyond single-level cyclic source decoding

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-026-mesh-scale-poisson-recovery-is-only-an-invertible-cyclic-decoder`.

PC-295--PC-306 progressively separate phase retention, classical Fourier/polyspectral data and exact algebraic source identification. PC-307--PC-309 then price stable separation: the canonical prime source and its one-step translate remain arbitrarily close throughout every sublinear ordinary Fourier bandwidth and every uniformly transport-stable single-level geometry. The complete logarithmic-potential field also collapses whenever `q(1-r)->infinity`, leaving the mesh boundary layer `1-r=Theta(1/q)` as the first unresolved radial scale.

PC-310 now classifies the most direct escape at that scale. Sampling the Poisson field, equivalently the radial derivative of the centered logarithmic potential, on the intrinsic `q`-gon diagonalizes the matched source/control difference by the exact multiplier

`M_(q,a)(h)=(e^(-2 pi i h/q)-1)(e^(-a h/q)+e^(-a(1-h/q)))/(1-e^(-a))`

for `r=e^(-a/q)`. Every nonzero cyclic frequency survives, so the map is invertible on the centered source space; its condition number is `Theta(q)`. The first non-collapsing boundary layer is therefore exactly where the observable has paid enough sensitivity to decode the original finite cyclic source through a universal circulant filter.

This closes the direct linear single-level route from mesh-scale Poisson/log-potential flux to a new arithmetic carrier. Lossless recovery at that boundary is not a zero-sensitive invariant: it is ordinary cyclic Fourier reconstruction at the conditioning threshold already predicted by PC-309.

The live construction must add a mechanism not equivalent to a universal one-conductor multiplier: mixed-conductor or cross-level coupling, a nonlinear operation before cyclic diagonalization, a non-circulant pointed interaction, a shell-dependent operator, or an independently justified map from recovered source data to a zero-sensitive destination.

## Demand a destination theorem before interpreting mesh-scale distinguishability

Linear bandwidth, transport modulus and boundary depth now give the same warning in different coordinates. Sublinear/pre-mesh observations are stable but collapse the matched sources; mesh/linear-scale observations separate them by resolving the source itself. A viable singular observable must therefore state what additional quantity becomes available **before mere source decoding is mistaken for RH information**, and how its amplification is controlled in the destination norm.

Mixed-conductor proposals should be tested for whether they genuinely couple levels or simply concatenate independently invertible one-level decoders. Nonlinear proposals should be tested before any Gram/scalarization step that would again erase the claimed extra structure.
