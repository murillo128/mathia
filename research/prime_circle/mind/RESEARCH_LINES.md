## Break reciprocal-control similarity algebraically, not merely metrically

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-045-reciprocity-closed-signature-is-abelian-trivial-and-rank-two-at-first-lie-level` through `MI-050-fixed-common-hilbert-metrics-only-create-similarity-distortion`.

PC-347--PC-355 move beyond the finite linear class. Full noncommutative signature data can reconstruct the centered source path, but triangular spectralization discards the oriented coordinate; inner parity makes semisimple spectra exactly control-blind; outer parity gives only generic contragredient orientation; fixed algebraic representation lifts add weight functions of the same spectrum; and the canonical full-Fock lift remains reciprocal-control conjugate by second-quantized parity.

PC-356 shows that a source-native Gram metric recomputed covariantly on the control restores an exact isometry. PC-357 shows that freezing one parity-breaking tensor metric can destroy unitarity but not similarity: after whitening, the source and control remain intertwined and metric-sensitive separation is bounded by a static condition-number budget.

PC-358 removes the factorization loophole completely at every finite cutoff. Let `P_N` be the algebraic reciprocal parity on the full truncated word space and let `G_N>0` be **any** fixed Hermitian metric, with arbitrary cross-word, cross-shell and cross-degree couplings. Since the algebraic multipliers satisfy `L~_N=P_N L_N P_N`, whitening by `R_N=G_N^(1/2)` gives

`A~_N=S_N A_N S_N^(-1)`, `S_N=R_N P_N R_N^(-1)`.

Thus every fixed finite Hilbertization preserves exact reciprocal similarity, ordinary spectrum/Jordan data stay identical, and singular/resolvent/pseudospectral differences are bounded by the single global distortion `kappa_2(S_N)`. At infinite depth the same blindness persists whenever algebraic parity extends boundedly to the chosen Hilbert completion.

The surviving question is therefore stricter than “use a nonfactorizing metric.” A metric-only route must make reciprocal parity genuinely **unbounded** in the completion, or alter the operator/domain/control law so that the algebraic identity `L~=PLP` itself no longer induces a bounded similarity. Viable candidates include a source-forced domain or boundary condition not transported by parity, an asymmetric coupling inserted before the reciprocal quotient, or another joint invariant whose source/control relation is not generated by the fixed algebraic involution. Any metric-sensitive separation must first be divided by the exact similarity budget.

## Keep source recovery, automorphism quotient, metric similarity and zero selection separate

Prime Circle now has several exact endpoints. Scalar measurement reconstructs classical zeta structure; noncommutative depth reconstructs the path; spectral quotients can erase orientation; reciprocal automorphisms can make source and control conjugate; covariant metrics restore isometry; and arbitrary fixed finite Hilbert metrics still leave a nonunitary similarity.

The audit should therefore identify the destination discriminator first, write the reciprocal algebraic intertwiner before choosing a Hilbert norm, and ask whether the proposed completion changes the boundedness/domain status of that intertwiner rather than merely its condition number. Progress requires a source-forced invariant that survives this recovery/automorphism/similarity audit and is independently tied to zeta-zero geometry.
