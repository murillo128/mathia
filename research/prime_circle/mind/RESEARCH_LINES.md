## Break reciprocal-control equivalence before spectralization, not by wrapping the same signature in a richer representation

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-045-reciprocity-closed-signature-is-abelian-trivial-and-rank-two-at-first-lie-level` through `MI-049-free-fock-completion-preserves-reciprocal-parity-and-makes-the-canonical-spectrum-control-blind`.

PC-347--PC-350 move beyond the finite linear class. The full radial Chen signature is faithful to the shell path; after reciprocity closure its abelianization vanishes and its first Lie term is a rank-two decomposable area, while an all-depth `2x2` solvable decoder recovers the complete centered radial profile. Raw noncommutative depth is therefore source fidelity, not zero selection.

PC-351 closes triangular spectralization. The faithful source coordinate lives in an oriented off-diagonal entry while ordinary spectrum is trivial, and the matched reciprocal control is related by fixed unitary conjugation. PC-352 shows that non-solvability does not repair this when the reciprocal parity is inner: even a full `sl_2` development remains exactly control-conjugate on every class-function spectral quotient.

PC-353 proves that the inner-parity boundary is sharp but also closes the most immediate outer-parity escape. In `SL_d`, `d>=3`, the standard outer involution `Theta(g)=g^(-T)` makes the source and reciprocal-control spectra contragredient. In `SL_3` it exchanges the two nontrivial characteristic coefficients, so a duality-odd coordinate can distinguish a path from its reciprocal twin, but the same orientation occurs for every admissible non-arithmetic matched profile.

PC-354 closes fixed finite-dimensional algebraic representation lifts. For every such representation, the lifted spectrum is a fixed multiset of weight monomials in the original eigenvalues, and the reciprocal control merely inverts that multiset. A representation wrapper applied after the finite spectral quotient cannot recover source information already discarded there.

PC-355 closes the canonical all-depth representation escape as well. On truncated full Fock space the left-regular signature operator for the reciprocal control is unitarily conjugate to the source operator by the second quantization of the fixed parity involution. Moreover the operator is `I` plus a strictly degree-raising nilpotent, so its ordinary finite-truncation spectrum is always `{1}`. Singular values, pseudospectra, numerical ranges and other unitary invariants remain matched-control blind, and parity-compatible degree weights or growing shell alphabets preserve the obstruction.

The surviving question is therefore stricter than “make the involution outer,” “choose a richer finite representation,” or “keep every tensor depth in Fock space.” A viable construction must introduce **source-forced parity-breaking structure before the spectral quotient**: for example a canonical domain, metric, boundary condition, coupling or non-spectral covariant not transported by the same reciprocal-control unitary. Alternatively it must justify an infinite-dimensional destination whose zero-sensitive theorem uses genuinely new source structure rather than the functorial signature tower itself.

## Keep source recovery, nonlinear completeness, automorphism quotient, parity covariance and zero selection separate

Prime Circle now exhibits several distinct endpoints. Full scalar measurement reconstructs classical zeta structure. Full noncommutative depth reconstructs the centered source path. Triangular spectralization discards the oriented source coordinate. Inner parity makes semisimple spectra exactly control-blind. Outer parity can restore only generic contragredient orientation. Finite algebraic representation lifts add only weight functions of that same spectrum, while the canonical free-Fock lift preserves the reciprocal parity by unitary second quantization and has trivial finite-truncation spectrum.

The audit should therefore state the destination discriminator first; identify the quotient it uses; determine the automorphism or unitary covariance induced by reciprocal control; and test whether any surviving coordinate is source-specific rather than a functorial consequence shared by matched controls. Progress requires a source-forced invariant that survives this full recovery/automorphism/parity/representation audit and is independently tied to zeta-zero geometry.
