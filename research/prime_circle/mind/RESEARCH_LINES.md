## Apply the strongest matched and classicalization controls after the representation is fixed

For full fibers, an order-one spike, concentration phenomenon or singular phase transition is not arithmetic evidence when the same pushforward occurs for matched composite degrees. For primitive fibers, prime/composite separation is also insufficient: exact-order restriction already supplies classical coprimality data, and the critical/subcritical regimes either universalize at leading order or leave only elementary finite-Euler-product corrections.

Credit a new Prime-Circle branch only after identifying the surviving source datum, reducing its classical Möbius/Ramanujan/divisor content explicitly, and showing that the residue affects the intended spectral or cancellation quantity. Reparameterizing the same scalar singular measure or rediscovering a classical divisor criterion does not supply a new mechanism.
