## Leave one-sided grade raising, including its canonical critical closure, rather than tuning a universal `1/2` edge

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-045-reciprocity-closed-signature-is-abelian-trivial-and-rank-two-at-first-lie-level` through `MI-052-proper-grade-hamiltonians-do-not-spectralize-bounded-raising-data`.

PC-347--PC-360 show that increasingly rich finite signature/spectral constructions remain reciprocal-control blind. Scalar grade weights either compactify positive-degree creation or, at the geometric borderline, reduce modulo compacts to ordinary radially rescaled free-Fock transport. Even the first obvious source/control spectral-radius gap can be reproduced by a matched nonarithmetic one-particle metric.

PC-361 closes the bounded-Hamiltonian repair: a proper diagonal grade Hamiltonian `H_0` with compact resolvent plus any bounded strictly grade-raising source term has exactly the bare grade eigenvalues with the same algebraic multiplicities. PC-362 extends the blindness to strictly grade-raising perturbations that are `H_0`-relatively bounded with relative bound below one.

PC-363 now resolves the canonical critical weighted-shift boundary rather than leaving it as an escape. At relative coefficient `c=1`, the natural operator on `D(N)` is closable but not closed; its closure is a maximal first-difference operator with exact spectrum

`{lambda : Re(lambda)>=-1/2}`.

After the harmless sign flip, the spectral boundary is literally `Re(s)=1/2`, but the **whole half-plane** is spectral, there are no arithmetic eigenvalues to select, and the edge moves under rescaling or translation of the grade Hamiltonian. The `1/2` comes from the ambient `ell^2`/Hardy summability threshold, not from Prime-Circle data. Criticality therefore changes spectral category, but the canonical one-sided critical completion is an explicit false positive for RH.

The live operator routes are narrower: source-dependent domain/boundary conditions that themselves retain cyclotomic data, same-grade/backward/two-sided or otherwise nontriangular coupling, genuinely source-sensitive critical/supercritical geometry, or a non-eigenvalue observable whose arithmetic destination survives matched controls. Merely increasing one-sided raising amplitude until a critical line appears is closed.

## Keep source recovery, triangular position, domain growth, completion topology and spectral observable separate

A representation may recover the source path and still lose orientation after spectral quotienting. Breaking bounded reciprocal similarity can compactify the operator. Preserving essential transport can still reduce to radial Fock geometry. Strictly upper-triangular source data can leave every eigenvalue unchanged below the relative-bound threshold, while the first critical domain transition can replace the discrete spectrum by a universal half-plane.

A future RH-facing discriminator must therefore state not only the operator category but **where the source term sits relative to the grading, how its graph/domain growth scales, what closed extension is forced, and which spectral datum reaches the zeta-zero destination**. Unboundedness, spectral-category change, nonzero essential spectrum, a source/control radius gap, or even an ambient boundary exactly at `1/2` do not by themselves supply that map.
