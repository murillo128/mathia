# Prime-circle research lines

This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Prime-Circle evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Find source-forced structure beyond decoded shell profiles and universal ordering

PC-391--PC-398 isolate simultaneous-deletion positive Kron transport, price its thin contrast carrier, close every fixed polynomial window at `alpha>=1/2`, and show that below the endpoint the surviving smooth macroscopic sector converges after normalization to a universal Buchstab--Riesz continuum form reproduced by matched point clouds. The live positive-Kron resource is therefore not raw operator amplitude below `alpha=1/2`, but the residual beyond that continuum mode: centered fluctuations, mesoscopic/arithmetic-scale vectors, cross-level orientation, or another source datum that matched controls cannot reproduce.

PC-400--PC-402 close the single-measure Jacobi/Krein and canonical two-form spectral repackaging routes. The shell-2 moment ladder is multiplication geometry on `[0,log 2]`; pairing a target shell with the shell-2 Hankel form gives multiplication by the already-known radial-flux density, so neither the support spectrum nor the canonical generalized spectrum creates new arithmetic information.

PC-403 shows that putting one target shell between arbitrary left/right shell-2 blocks still adds no carrier. The whole two-sided placement packet is the Bernstein-basis presentation of the same recovered one-shell measure, and its square generalized pencil is a fixed right-basis transform of the old Hankel pencil. PC-404 closes the natural finite multishell continuation as well: for any fixed ordered list of target shells, arbitrary shell-2 interleavings are exactly Bernstein moments of the **product** of the separately recovered one-shell measures restricted to the universal ordered simplex. The two-target antisymmetric kernel is a universal skew Volterra operator, and the canonical multivariate moment pencil is again multiplication by the separable product density.

PC-405 closes the most direct canonical cross-level refinement escape from that no-go. In shell-2 flux coordinates the cyclotomic prime-child law is a universal pushforward semigroup: `mu_(pn)=(T_p-I)mu_n` for a fresh prime and `mu_(pn)=T_p mu_n` for a repeated prime. Under logarithmic radius this semigroup is just ordinary dilation, and every multiplicative descendant is a finite integral-linear combination of dilated copies of one base shell measure. Substituting those descendants into the PC-404 ordered Chen packet therefore produces a universal ordered kernel against tensor powers of the same base measure; canonical child refinement creates no independent cross-level interaction measure.

PC-406 closes the canonical infinite-level completion more strongly than the polynomial-moment statement alone. After the shell-2 coordinate change, the descendant measure is exactly the signed radial-flux measure already used in PC-179. Summing the entire multiplicative descendant tree by conductor gives, in operator norm for `Re(s)>1`, a universal dilation Euler product with scalar Möbius factor. Polynomial Mellin moments reduce to finite Euler corrections times `zeta(s+j)/zeta(s)`, and the full complex dilation-character readout satisfies

`Z_n(s,z)=M_z(nu_n) zeta(s+z)/zeta(s) prod_(p|n)(1-p^(-s))^(-1)`

in its native absolute-convergence domain. For the genuine cyclotomic source, `M_z(nu_n)` is exactly the already-classical PC-179 radial-flux Mellin factor. A matched arbitrary base measure generated by the same descendant operators has the same universal descendant ratio. Thus neither integer moments nor the full Mellin-character diagonalization exposes a hidden descendant interaction.

PC-407 closes the first canonical positive quadratic completion of the same descendant law. Writing the shell-2 flux in `L^2(R_+,dx/x)`, dilation is unitary and a fresh prime contributes the Mellin-frequency factor `a_p(t)=|p^(it)-1|^2`. The conductor-weighted descendant energy therefore has exact multiplier

`D_*(s,t)=sum_(m>=1)|J_(it)(m)|^2/m^s = zeta(s)^2/[zeta(s-it)zeta(s+it)] H(s,t)`,

where `H` is already absolutely convergent for `Re(s)>1/2`. For real `s in (1/2,1)`, the finite parent corrections and `H` are positive, so off-critical zeta zeros appear as real poles of this multiplier; this yields an exact RH-equivalent pole criterion. But the entire multiplier, including that criterion, is reproduced by **every arbitrary nonarithmetic base profile** propagated by the same dilation/difference descendant semigroup. The actual cyclotomic source enters only through its already-known one-shell Mellin factor. Polarization likewise changes only the base spectral density and leaves the universal multiplier untouched.

PC-408 extends that no-go from the quadratic energy to **every fixed-degree ordinary pointwise product of the same-conductor descendants**. For Mellin frequencies `t_1,...,t_r`, the conductor symbol factors as a finite Boolean-lattice hypercube

`Z_r(s;t)=prod_(S subseteq {1,...,r}) zeta(s-i t_S)^((-1)^(r-|S|))`

times an Euler product already absolutely convergent for `Re(s)>1/2` and finite corrections at parent primes. The cases `r=1` and the quadratic diagonal recover PC-406 and PC-407 exactly. The same hypercube and Euler correction occur for matched arbitrary Mellin-tame base profiles propagated by the universal descendant semigroup. Thus increasing any **fixed** pointwise polynomial degree does not restore prime-circle specificity; the shifted-zeta factors are generated by finite inclusion-exclusion of universal fresh-prime differences, not by retained cyclotomic geometry.

PC-409--PC-410 close the most canonical “distinct descendant indices” escape left after PC-408. Weighting cross-index Hilbert pairings by a function of the common refinement `[m,k]` does not combine fresh-prime directions into a richer source carrier. For the reciprocal-lcm kernel they telescope away exactly, and PC-410 shows this is structural: **every Mellin-diagonal join-only coupling** deletes every positive fresh-prime max shell. Changing the lcm weight cannot help while both join-only dependence and Mellin diagonality are retained.

PC-411 tests the canonical meet repair. Multiplying the join weight by a power of `(m,k)` restores fresh-prime factors, but they classicalize immediately into a universal finite-difference zeta rectangle

`zeta(s-it)zeta(s+it)zeta(s+u)^2/[zeta(s)^2 zeta(s+u-it)zeta(s+u+it)]`

plus an absolutely more convergent Euler correction and finite parent factors. The same factorization holds for an arbitrary matched base profile propagated by the descendant semigroup. The meet term therefore escapes the algebraic cancellation of PC-410 without escaping source blindness.

The live route must now break a more precise pair of information constraints. A distinct-index mechanism must either mix **different Mellin frequencies**, use a two-label kernel not reducible to common refinement/meet-join calculus, couple source-derived angular/chord/old-new information to the refinement, alter the descendant state/product law, or let interaction complexity grow with conductor. A genuinely renormalized infinite-degree object is also outside the present closure. Merely increasing signature depth, adding shell-2 letters, summing the universal descendant tree, multiplying fixed-degree same-index descendants, pairing distinct descendants through a Mellin-diagonal join kernel, or adding the canonical power meet regularization does not create a source-specific carrier.
