## Find a strict source-forced quotient outside faithful triangular decoding

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-045-reciprocity-closed-signature-is-abelian-trivial-and-rank-two-at-first-lie-level`, `MI-046-faithful-triangular-decoding-can-be-basis-free-spectrally-trivial`.

PC-347--PC-350 move beyond the finite linear class. The full radial Chen signature is faithful to the shell path; after reciprocity closure its abelianization vanishes and its first Lie term is a rank-two decomposable area, but an all-depth `2x2` solvable decoder recovers the complete centered radial profile. Raw unbounded depth is therefore source fidelity, not zero selection.

PC-351 now closes the most immediate attempt to spectralize that faithful decoder. Every simultaneously upper-triangular regularized development has limiting diagonal equal to one, hence trivial ordinary spectrum. For the canonical `2x2` decoder, the source sits entirely in the oriented off-diagonal coefficient. More strongly, the matched reciprocal sign-flip control `W->-W` is implemented by one fixed unitary conjugation of the entire decoder family. Eigenvalues, singular values, pseudospectra, numerical ranges and every simultaneous unitary-conjugacy invariant therefore agree between the arithmetic decoder and an exact matched reciprocal control.

The surviving question is consequently stricter than “find a quotient of the full signature.” Identify a **source-forced non-triangular/non-solvable representation or another strict control-selective quotient** whose value is not merely a gauge-oriented source coordinate and whose destination theorem is independently zero-sensitive. Keeping all off-diagonal information simply reconstructs the source; quotienting it through ordinary basis-free spectral geometry destroys the distinction in the triangular sector.

## Keep source recovery, nonlinear completeness, spectralization and zero selection separate

Prime Circle now exhibits four different endpoints. Full scalar measurement reconstructs classical zeta structure. Full noncommutative depth reconstructs the centered source path. Low-order reciprocity quotients can erase almost all information. And PC-351 shows that a faithful oriented decoder can become spectrally trivial or matched-control equivalent after passing to basis-free operator invariants.

The relevant audit is therefore not whether a representation is rich, faithful or noncommutative. State the destination discriminator first, identify the strict quotient of source information it actually needs, and test whether that quotient survives the proposed basis/gauge invariance while failing on matched reciprocal controls. Larger matrices, higher fixed Chen degree, unbounded raw depth, exact source reconstruction or triangular spectralization count only when an independently zero-sensitive invariant survives.
