This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable prime-circle intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Preserve a genuinely nonclassical orientation carrier before positive compression

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-021-oriented-frame-repair-is-gauge-or-classical-sine-fourier-data`.

PC-295--PC-298 identify the diffuse fixed-scale boundary: the normalized boundary-log operator approaches a universal square-zero class, with source information surviving only at or below the explicit defect scale `r_q`. PC-299 identifies the first positive quadratic defect there as a Fejer-smoothed source autocorrelation, so squaring and averaging loses Fourier phase and lands in classical additive prime exponential-sum data.

PC-300 closes the most direct independently sampled multi-profile repair. Source-coupled pair profiles can form genuinely noncommuting matrices before averaging, but independent source residues around the cycle factor every averaged `k`-loop exactly through powers of one real symmetric Hankel-type average. The full independently averaged hierarchy therefore keeps only reflection-even source Fourier data.

PC-301 closes the correlated/reused-source escape **while the centered same-source Gram carrier is retained**. The centered window gives `G_(-s)=G_s` pointwise, before averaging. Hence every cyclic word formed from the `G_s` is invariant under independent reflection of every source coordinate for arbitrary joint source laws. Correlation can retain relations among the unoriented classes `{s,-s}`, but it cannot recover coordinatewise reflection-odd orientation discarded by the map `s -> G_s` itself.

PC-302 now tests the simplest pre-Gram oriented-frame repair. Reflection acts by exact latent mode reversal, `X_-s=X_s J`, so unlabelled frame/subspace geometry, principal angles, singular values and other positive basis-invariant cross-frame data remain reflection gauge. Keeping the labels does recover an odd channel, but its first quadratic component is exactly a sine cross-Gram; under same-law averaging it becomes a positive Gram of the imaginary additive Fourier coefficients of the source. For prime sources this is ordinary additive exponential-sum data over primes.

The live route is therefore narrower than “retain orientation before Gram formation.” A successful carrier must retain orientation in a way that is neither removable by mode relabelling nor reduced by the first positive quadratic compression to classical sine/cosine Fourier covariance. Candidates must justify a canonical chirality or labelled-mode structure, higher joint information beyond quadratic Fourier covariance, an order-sensitive observable with independent geometric meaning, an asymmetric window forced by the Prime-Circle construction, or a cross-level carrier not determined by the same finite Fourier packet.

## Keep defect resolution, carrier gauge, Fourier classicalization and dependence structure separate

Reaching `O(r_q)` is necessary to escape the universal square-zero bulk but not sufficient. PC-299 shows that positive quadratic compression at that scale forgets phase. PC-300 shows that noncommutativity before expectation is insufficient when independence factorizes the product into powers of one averaged matrix. PC-301 shows that even without independence, coordinatewise source orientation is already lost if every observable factors through centered same-source `G_s`.

PC-302 adds a finer distinction. Moving to the frame level can restore a labelled reflection-odd channel, but basis-invariant positive geometry still quotients it by mode reversal, while the first labelled quadratic odd sector classicalizes to additive Fourier covariance. A higher-order or correlated statistic is not automatically higher-information: it must state which orientation data survives the carrier gauge and why the resulting arithmetic object is not merely another representation of the classical prime Fourier packet.
