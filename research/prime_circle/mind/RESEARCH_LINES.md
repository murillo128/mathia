## Leave every fixed finite residue-packet category through genuinely growing relational coupling

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-011-simultaneous-scale-coupling-must-survive-coprime-controls`, `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra`.

PC-224--PC-248 show that scalar fusion, noncommuting refinements, drift/path coordinates, native weighted packets, and every fixed finite nonlinear readout of complete centered prime-transfer Gram packets repeatedly close into classical finite packet, CRT, or Dirichlet-character algebras.

PC-249 closes one of the main pre-Gram escapes left open by that classification. If two rectangular transfer packets share the same varying upper-prime row space but have fixed lower primes `p<r`, then beyond the Frobenius threshold `q>pr-p-r` their mixed cross-Gram freezes exactly as `K_{p,q}^* K_{r,q}=q K_{p,r}^T`. For any fixed finite lower-prime family, every off-diagonal shared-upper block therefore becomes a scalar multiple of lower-level packet data; the remaining `q` dependence is confined to the already-classified diagonal signed-residue blocks.

The live construction must now preserve more than pairwise shared-row Gram geometry at fixed lower scales. Viable escapes include genuinely growing lower moduli, several independent upper-prime variables, higher-order rectangular relations before pairwise Gram contraction, native composite/source masks, or an infinite-dimensional limit whose state growth cannot be reduced to a finite CRT quotient. The candidate must identify the surviving variable explicitly and show why it is not only a larger package of classical character data.
