## Break reciprocal-control similarity before spectralization, not merely reciprocal-control isometry

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-045-reciprocity-closed-signature-is-abelian-trivial-and-rank-two-at-first-lie-level` through `MI-050-fixed-common-hilbert-metrics-only-create-similarity-distortion`.

PC-347--PC-350 move beyond the finite linear class. The full radial Chen signature is faithful to the shell path; after reciprocity closure its abelianization vanishes and its first Lie term is a rank-two decomposable area, while an all-depth `2x2` solvable decoder recovers the complete centered radial profile. Raw noncommutative depth is therefore source fidelity, not zero selection.

PC-351 closes triangular spectralization. The faithful source coordinate lives in an oriented off-diagonal entry while ordinary spectrum is trivial, and the matched reciprocal control is related by fixed unitary conjugation. PC-352 shows that non-solvability does not repair this when reciprocal parity is inner: even a full `sl_2` development remains exactly control-conjugate on every class-function spectral quotient.

PC-353 proves that the inner-parity boundary is sharp but also closes the most immediate outer-parity escape. In `SL_d`, `d>=3`, the standard outer involution `Theta(g)=g^(-T)` makes the source and reciprocal-control spectra contragredient. In `SL_3` it exchanges the two nontrivial characteristic coefficients, so a duality-odd coordinate can distinguish a path from its reciprocal twin, but the same orientation occurs for every admissible non-arithmetic matched profile.

PC-354 closes fixed finite-dimensional algebraic representation lifts. For every such representation, the lifted spectrum is a fixed multiset of weight monomials in the original eigenvalues, and the reciprocal control merely inverts that multiset. A representation wrapper applied after the finite spectral quotient cannot recover source information already discarded there.

PC-355 closes the canonical all-depth representation escape. On truncated full Fock space the left-regular signature operator for the reciprocal control is unitarily conjugate to the source operator by the second quantization of the fixed parity involution. Moreover the operator is `I` plus a strictly degree-raising nilpotent, so its ordinary finite-truncation spectrum is always `{1}`. Singular values, pseudospectra, numerical ranges and other unitary invariants remain matched-control blind when the metric is parity-compatible.

PC-356 closes the most direct source-dependent metric repair. The centered cyclotomic radial derivatives define a genuinely non-diagonal positive Gram form `G_S=T_S^*T_S`, but the matched reciprocal control has realization `T_S J`, hence its independently recomputed native metric is `G~_S=J^*G_SJ`. The source and control Hilbert spaces remain exactly isometric. A metric built covariantly from data transported by the same reciprocal involution cannot break the control.

PC-357 treats the complementary fixed-metric escape. Use one independently chosen positive metric `H` on both source and control, even when `J^*HJ!=H`. This can break unitary equivalence, but after whitening the operators remain exactly similar through

`S_N=direct_sum_(m<=N) (H^(1/2) J H^(-1/2))^(tensor m)`.

Ordinary spectrum is unchanged, and singular/resolvent/pseudospectral separation is bounded by the static similarity budget `kappa_H(J)^N`. Tensor depth can therefore amplify one fixed metric/parity mismatch exponentially without creating new shell arithmetic.

The surviving question is stricter than “make the involution outer,” “keep every tensor depth,” “use a source-native metric,” or “freeze a parity-breaking ambient metric.” A viable construction must break the **algebraic reciprocal similarity itself**, or produce a joint source-specific invariant that is not explained by the fixed intertwiner: a canonical domain, boundary condition, asymmetric coupling, anchored frame, nonfactorizing Hilbert structure, or another pre-spectral datum whose matched-control reconstruction cannot be transported by `J` or its whitened similarity. Any depth-dependent singular separation must first be normalized against the generic `kappa_H(J)^N` budget.

## Keep source recovery, nonlinear completeness, automorphism quotient, metric covariance, metric similarity and zero selection separate

Prime Circle now exhibits several distinct endpoints. Full scalar measurement reconstructs classical zeta structure. Full noncommutative depth reconstructs the centered source path. Triangular spectralization discards the oriented source coordinate. Inner parity makes semisimple spectra exactly control-blind. Outer parity can restore only generic contragredient orientation. Finite algebraic representation lifts add only weight functions of that same spectrum. The canonical free-Fock lift preserves reciprocal parity by unitary second quantization. A source-native Gram metric remains control-isometric when recomputed covariantly, while a fixed common parity-breaking metric produces only nonunitary similarity distortion with an explicit tensor-depth condition-number budget.

The audit should therefore state the destination discriminator first; identify the quotient it uses; determine the automorphism, unitary, metric-covariance or similarity relation induced by reciprocal control; and test whether any surviving coordinate is source-specific rather than a functorial or conditioning consequence shared by matched controls. Progress requires a source-forced invariant that survives this full recovery/automorphism/parity/representation/metric/similarity audit and is independently tied to zeta-zero geometry.
