## Resolve the intermediate rational-microscopic window or change observable class

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra`, `MI-013-growing-collision-geometry-can-still-be-classical`, `MI-014-single-source-positive-spectral-lifts-are-pure-gauge`.

PC-224--PC-271 close fixed-conductor packets, finite trace words, complete finite singular data, common-dilation growth and the main bounded-mode orbit regimes, then isolate the classical dual-lattice near-resonance carriers. PC-272 proves that finite-window timing retains ordered source placement lost by the exact ratio quotient, but PC-273--PC-277 show that raw scalar law, fixed logarithmic recentering and a broad normalized moving-band regime remain source-only/classical, while the far rational endpoint becomes exactly resonant for every source.

The surviving scalar branch is the intermediate rational-microscopic window between the current PNT-transport regime and universal denominator resonance. A useful theorem must close it with sharper source-only control or exhibit a residual that survives such a control; a gap in the present transport bound is not evidence of a new prime scale.

[PC-278](../findings/PC-278-single-source-dual-window-phase-spectra-are-diagonal-unitary-pure-gauge.md) shows that a fixed positive/Gram/Hermitian wrapper on the single-source phase field is diagonal-unitary pure gauge. [PC-279](../findings/PC-279-full-dual-torus-anchor-nonlinearities-are-source-blind-fourier-data.md) closes the most symmetric nonlinear escape as well: on the **complete coefficient torus**, every anchored pointwise profile

`M_(p,r)^f(h,k)=f(hp+kr)`

is row/column-permutation equivalent to `M_(1,1)^f`, and its singular values are exactly the one-dimensional Fourier magnitudes `|f_hat(j)|`. Source-selected threshold masks and full translation-invariant nonlocal kernels therefore remain source-blind after complete-torus spectralization.

What survives is not “add a nonlinearity before the spectrum.” The candidate carrier is the extra structure that prevents the source multiplication from being quotiented out: a **truncated/non-invariant coefficient window**, genuine coefficient-graph flux, or relative/cross-source/cross-level coupling with no common relabeling. In the present near-resonance branch, future work should treat the window/source permutation mismatch itself as the object and continue to test it against the Li-weighted rational controls of PC-277.

## Keep retained information, permutation/gauge quotient and arithmetic discrimination separate

Ordered information can survive a scalar quotient and still be classical. Full phase information can survive entrywise and disappear under diagonal-unitary equivalence; even an anchor-dependent nonlinear mask can survive entrywise and disappear under complete-torus permutation equivalence. A future Prime-Circle mechanism must state both what source information is retained and exactly which symmetry the downstream operation still quotients out. Only the residual after that quotient and the strongest source-only matched control is a candidate arithmetic discriminator.
