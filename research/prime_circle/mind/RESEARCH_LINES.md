# Prime-circle research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-circle intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they discriminate among mathematically different mechanisms.

## Preserve genuinely cross-level information before classicalization

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-anchor-required-but-local-anchor-is-classical`.

One-shell anchored, rotation-invariant, or regular circulant constructions repeatedly collapse to classical polygon, Ramanujan, Dirichlet, or divisor data. A surviving mechanism must couple distinct refinement levels before that reduction, and the resulting invariant must not be determined by the same classical character package.

The decisive test is a cross-level observable whose value changes under a control that preserves every one-shell classical invariant consumed by the construction. Merely increasing rank or adding a fixed character decomposition does not qualify.

## Determine whether nonlinear anchored interactions create a new arithmetic variable

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-anchor-required-but-local-anchor-is-classical`.

The common anchor is necessary to prevent translation-gauge loss, but many linear or quadratic anchored quantities still classicalize. Study nonlinear mixed interactions whose arithmetic content cannot be reconstructed from endpoint potentials, Ramanujan sums, resultants, or ordinary divisor data.

A decisive candidate must pass the maximal-classical-determination audit before any spectral or positivity interpretation is attached.

## Use compatible completion as a carrier, not as an automatic Hamiltonian

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-compatible-completion-lacks-coercive-rh-scale`.

The adelic solenoid preserves exact-order labels, but regular commuting calculi and a broad class of scalar dilation covariances cannot have the desired compact-resolvent scale. Projective-height properization does produce compactness, but its zeta reduces to classical primitive-lattice zeta data.

The live question is whether the compatible geometry forces a non-scalar, one-sided, singular, or relative operator scale that is neither a scalar reparametrization of the dilation action nor a decoded projective height. A decisive result must derive that scale intrinsically and show that it carries an RH-relevant invariant beyond functional-equation or lattice-zeta structure.

## Determine whether all-level Hardy relative data contain more than endpoint/resultant arithmetic

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-005-hardy-shell-core-is-universal`.

The canonical Hardy/Hankel lift escapes modewise diagonalization, but each shell has a universal Hilbert-band essential core and every finite mixed algebraic word is trace class. The remaining arithmetic is therefore in nuclear relative data or in a genuinely infinite cross-shell construction.

A decisive positive result would derive an all-level Hardy operator or relative determinant whose invariant cannot be reduced to the known endpoint, radical/divisor, resultant, or finite-wedge data and which survives matched controls. A theorem that every natural infinite completion remains a sum/product of those classical relative invariants would close the route.
