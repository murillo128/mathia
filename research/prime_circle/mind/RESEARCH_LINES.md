# Prime-circle research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-circle intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they discriminate among mathematically different mechanisms.

## Preserve genuinely cross-level information before classicalization

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-anchor-required-but-local-anchor-is-classical`.

One-shell anchored, rotation-invariant, or regular circulant constructions repeatedly collapse to classical polygon, Ramanujan, Dirichlet, or divisor data. High rank does not prevent that collapse. A surviving mechanism must couple distinct refinement levels before reduction and produce an invariant not determined by the same classical character package.

A decisive test is a cross-level observable whose value changes under a control that preserves every one-shell classical invariant consumed by the construction. Merely increasing rank or adding a fixed character decomposition does not qualify.

## Locate the first nonlinear shell interaction that is not gauge or endpoint data

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-anchor-required-but-local-anchor-is-classical`.

The first canonical nonlinear repairs are now constrained. Pointwise sector multiplication produces only a coboundary carry phase; the cotangent Leibniz defect is Hochschild-exact; and one intermediate cotangent-shell contraction collapses to endpoint cyclotomic potentials plus cardinality. The mathematical question is whether a genuinely higher interaction — for example multiple independently weighted intermediate shells or another intrinsically forced tensor — survives these reductions.

A decisive candidate must remain nontrivial after the gauge/coboundary and endpoint-potential quotients and must distinguish a matched refinement/composite control at the same interaction layer.

## Use compatible completion as a carrier, not as an automatic Hamiltonian

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-compatible-completion-lacks-coercive-rh-scale`.

The adelic solenoid preserves exact-order labels, but regular commuting calculi and a broad class of scalar dilation covariances cannot have the desired compact-resolvent scale. Projective-height properization does produce compactness, but its zeta reduces to classical primitive-lattice zeta data.

The live question is whether the compatible geometry forces a non-scalar, one-sided, singular, or relative operator scale that is neither a scalar reparametrization of the dilation action nor a decoded projective height. A decisive result must derive that scale intrinsically and show that it carries an RH-relevant invariant beyond functional-equation or lattice-zeta structure.

## Classify the finite Hardy period algebra before invoking an all-level completion

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-005-hardy-shell-core-is-universal`.

The canonical Hardy/Hankel lift escapes modewise diagonalization, while its finite-shell essential algebra remains a wedge of universal Hilbert bands. The relative finite-word sector is richer than pairwise resultants: higher cyclic traces are cyclotomic cone periods, and cyclically separated root words of length at least two have ordinary trace-class limits under the natural finite sections. The one-letter scalar/operator distinction is now explicit, and common repeated-prime depth factors as universal finite tensor inflation.

Determine which primitive/radical higher-trace periods are genuinely independent of known endpoint, resultant, divisor, and cyclotomic-period packages, and whether any intrinsically infinite all-shell object organizes them into a new arithmetic invariant. A decisive positive result must produce an RH-relevant selector or analytic parameter not already imported from classical period theory; a complete reduction of the finite trace algebra to known cyclotomic data would narrow the remaining route to truly infinite coupling.
