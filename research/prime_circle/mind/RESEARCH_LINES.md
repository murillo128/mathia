# Prime-circle research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-circle intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should be merged, redirected, or removed when the mathematics changes.

## Anchored nonlocal observables beyond the cyclotomic jet

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-anchor-required-but-local-anchor-is-classical`.

Determine whether the anchor can support a genuinely nonclassical invariant only when it is coupled to finite-distance/global relations among several labeled shells, separated regions, or the cyclotomic uniformized surface. The key question is which anchored nonlocal functionals preserve inversion/interior-exterior structure without collapsing to known cyclotomic derivative, Jordan-totient, resultant, Farey, or related classical data.

A decisive test is an exact or controlled family of such functionals whose fibers distinguish prime-derived configurations after the known `n ~ 2n` unmarked-shell and finite-jet collapses, together with matched non-prime controls that show the distinction is not carried solely by the ambient cyclotomic map.

## Non-flat structure beyond one-dimensional concatenation

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-first-order-transport-forgets-the-interior`.

Look for canonical observables whose composition is not reducible to a single functorial path product: global uniformization Hessians, branched or multi-path holonomy, multi-shell interactions, or spatially separated boundary response are examples of mechanisms that could retain interior refinement.

The discriminating calculation is subdivision/cocycle exactness. A candidate becomes interesting only if, after fixing the same external endpoints and removing gauge choices, an interior refinement changes a canonical mixed curvature, second variation, holonomy, or other non-concatenative quantity. Exact telescoping demotes the construction immediately.

## Long-range propagation after factoring the exact conformal background

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-exact-circle-defect-is-relational-but-trace-class`.

Treat the Grunsky--Schiffer kernel of `V(z)=pi cot(pi/z)` as a canonical conformal background rather than the spectral generator. Investigate whether a geometrically forced long-block propagation or branching mechanism composed with this background leaves a residual that carries ordered prime-gap information.

The decisive test is to factor out the endpoint-only/trace-class contribution and compare the residual on primes with integers, density-matched sequences, and order-destroying controls. A surviving singularity or coefficient is arithmetically interesting only if it depends on ordered gap structure and is not reproduced by the same propagation on the regular controls.
