# Prime-circle research lines

This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Prime-Circle evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Find source-forced interaction before the descendant state collapses to one-shell Mellin data

PC-391--PC-415 progressively show that multiplicative descendants, finite and infinite conductor tensors, positive frame constructions and trace-class spectral invariants collapse after shell-2 scalarization to one-shell source amplitudes times universal conductor data. Matched nonarithmetic base profiles reproduce that conductor structure, so large support, matrix rank, Euler products or Fredholm data are not source specificity by themselves.

PC-416--PC-418 test increasingly strong operator extensions. Finite powers of the intrinsic log-scale position operator expose only finite Mellin jets of the same one-shell transform; bounded Fourier-Stieltjes functional calculus gives real Mellin translations of that same data; and unrestricted bounded-Borel/von-Neumann closure of the prime dilation/Weyl system becomes all of `B(L^2(R_+,dx/x))`, losing selectivity rather than recovering arithmetic structure.

PC-419 closes the natural norm-closed **single-shell diagonal** continuation from the other side. On shell 2, log-Weyl shifts of the defect generators can interpolate finite diagonal data with controlled tails, so the generated `C*`-algebra contains `c0(Z)`: every diagonal compact on that shell is already available. Enlarging the on-shell norm closure, or appealing to periodic/almost-periodic completion of the same diagonal sequence data, therefore cannot expose a missing spectral direction.

The surviving route must violate the one-shell diagonal hypothesis itself. It must retain source-forced cross-shell coherence, a genuinely non-diagonal kernel, or another pre-scalarization relation that an arbitrary matched base profile cannot reproduce. A proposed proper operator class is useful only if its formula, topology and coupling are fixed by the Prime-Circle source before the desired zeta readout is inspected; neither unrestricted closure nor a larger single-shell diagonal algebra meets that gate.

## Apply matched controls after every representation change

A representation should be credited only after identifying the source-selected carrier, quotienting universal geometry and testing the same construction on a matched nonarithmetic profile. PC-414--PC-419 now rule out source-independent conductor tensors, the full trace-class descendant frame, finite Mellin jets, bounded real Mellin translations, unrestricted Weyl closure and norm-closed single-shell diagonal completion as independent arithmetic mechanisms.

The live discriminator is therefore concrete: exhibit a source relation that is lost by the shell-2 scalar channel but survives in the proposed destination as cross-shell, non-diagonal or otherwise source-coupled data. If the candidate can still be reconstructed from one-shell Mellin amplitudes plus a source-independent operator calculus, its zeta-sensitive poles, derivatives, determinants or spectra are readout rather than new Prime-Circle geometry.
