# Prime-circle research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-circle intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Leave one-shell reciprocal Fourier zeros unless a source principle selects the deformation

**Linked intuition:** `MI-011-scalar-fourier-zero-existence-is-deformation-dependent`.

PC-193--PC-196 show that scalar reciprocal-shell Fourier sign, zero existence, and first-zero scale depend on the chosen exponent. The attractive `p^(3/4)` quadratic scale is a singular even-exponent resonance and does not survive fixed `1<alpha<2` deformations. A useful scalar law therefore needs a source-forced operator/deformation rather than a representation selected because it creates favorable zeros.

## Leave the classical character and rational-ray packet algebra before calling cross-shell fusion new

**Linked intuition:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra`.

PC-197 identifies the global Cauchy--Poisson character packet with an ordinary Dirichlet `L`-function. PC-198 shows that finite holomorphic products can mix coefficients but create no genuinely mixed-root poles. PC-199 shows that the canonical sesquilinear same-angle fusion collapses to the product character.

PC-200 closes the first source-native non-diagonal angular repair. Pullback by two different anchor-fixing power maps imposes a rational Fourier ray `ak=bl`; gcd reduction and complete character multiplicativity collapse that ray to one ordinary Dirichlet packet, and even the two-variable Mellin transform sees only `L(s+t,eta)`. Finite shell-independent linear combinations merely multiply the same classical carrier by an externally chosen finite factor.

A viable cross-shell mechanism must therefore preserve at least two independent Fourier directions, or introduce another source-forced relation that does not reduce to the finite character/rational-ray algebra. More shells or a non-diagonal formula are not sufficient by themselves.

## Require quantitative deformation and algebraic-closure controls of any proposed spectral relation

A candidate should survive source-compatible scalar deformations and then be reduced against the complete finite packet algebra generated by the intrinsic character fields and power-map correspondences. Stability does not require fixed zero locations; it requires that the claimed arithmetic discriminator survive after boundary/refinement asymptotics, rational-ray collapse, and arbitrary finite wrapper factors are removed.

## Keep topology and realization separate from scalar cyclotomic identities

Potential-theoretic, geometric, operator-valued, infinite-refinement, or monodromy enlargements remain live only when the required object exists canonically and retains information beyond the classical cyclotomic/Euler/Dirichlet packet data. A new coordinate system or finite scalar wrapper around the same packet is not a new mechanism.
