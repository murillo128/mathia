# Prime-circle research lines

This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Prime-Circle evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Find source-forced interaction before the descendant state collapses to one-shell Mellin data

PC-391--PC-415 progressively show that multiplicative descendants, finite and infinite conductor tensors, positive frame constructions and trace-class spectral invariants collapse after shell-2 scalarization to one-shell source amplitudes times universal conductor data. Matched nonarithmetic base profiles reproduce that conductor structure, so large support, matrix rank, Euler products or Fredholm data are not source specificity by themselves.

PC-416--PC-418 test increasingly strong operator extensions. Finite powers of the intrinsic log-scale position operator expose only finite Mellin jets of the same one-shell transform; bounded Fourier-Stieltjes functional calculus gives real Mellin translations of that same data; and unrestricted bounded-Borel/von-Neumann closure of the prime dilation/Weyl system becomes all of `B(L^2(R_+,dx/x))`, losing selectivity rather than recovering arithmetic structure.

PC-419--PC-420 close the opposite norm-closed direction. The source-forced rational-log shifts, even after adjoining arbitrary bounded radial multipliers, generate no nonzero compact sector. Every element of the relevant translation-covariant norm-closed classes has spectrum equal to essential spectrum, so bounded radial enrichment plus discrete source shifts cannot supply a Hilbert--Polya-type discrete finite-multiplicity spectrum or a trace-class determinant channel. The surviving category had to leave that algebra through a genuinely integrated continuous-scale, resolvent, smoothing or other non-diagonal construction.

PC-421 tests the most canonical such escape. From the exact cyclotomic radial flux `rho_n`, the log-radius half-density

`h_(n,sigma)(u)=exp(sigma u) rho_n(exp u)`

has Fourier transform equal to the existing Mellin transform on the vertical line `Re(s)=sigma`. The Hilbert-transform commutator

`C_(n,sigma)=[H,M_(h_(n,sigma))]`

is nonzero Hilbert--Schmidt and therefore genuinely escapes the PC-420 no-compact theorem. However, the same compact architecture exists for **every** `sigma>0`; in the critical strip its symbol zeros are exactly the already-known zeta zeros on the chosen vertical line. Relative to Hardy projections the commutator is just the standard off-diagonal Hankel block, so compactness, spectral symmetry and Schatten behavior are generic operator theory rather than a critical-line selector.

The decisive matched control is dilation covariance. Replacing `h(u)` by `h(cu)` gives a unitarily equivalent commutator with exactly the same compact spectrum while rescaling every nonzero Fourier-zero ordinate. Thus the compact spectrum cannot determine the ordinate scale carried by the Mellin symbol. The Prime-Circle source fixes a preferred log-radius coordinate, but then the zeta information lives in that source symbol itself, not in a new spectral law generated by the commutator.

The live route must therefore add source structure **before or across the Mellin scalarization**, not merely package one vertical Mellin line into a compact operator. Surviving categories include a source-forced coupling between different `sigma` values, an intrinsic `sigma <-> 1-sigma` relation, angular/chord or old-new data mixed with scale before Mellinization, a genuinely new nonlocal kernel whose spectral parameter is not just log-radius Fourier frequency, or another cross-shell interaction that matched nonarithmetic profiles cannot reproduce. Any compactness-producing integral or resolvent is evidence only after its kernel and coupling are derived from the Prime-Circle source independently of the desired zeta readout.

## Apply matched controls after every representation change

A representation should be credited only after identifying the source-selected carrier, quotienting universal geometry and testing the same construction on a matched nonarithmetic profile. PC-414--PC-421 now rule out source-independent conductor tensors, the full trace-class descendant frame, finite Mellin jets, bounded real Mellin translations, unrestricted Weyl closure, norm-closed bounded-radial/discrete-shift algebras, and the canonical continuous-scale Hilbert commutator as independent arithmetic mechanisms.

The current discriminator is concrete: exhibit a relation that is lost by shell-2 scalarization but survives in the proposed destination as cross-shell, non-diagonal, integrated or otherwise source-coupled data. If the candidate is reconstructed from one-shell Mellin amplitudes plus universal operator calculus, it has only repackaged classical information. If it introduces a new continuous-scale or compact kernel, the provenance and matched-control theorem for that kernel are themselves the mathematical burden.
