# Prime-circle research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable prime-circle intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Exploit growing squarefree mask locality only through a collective non-coboundary invariant

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-011-scalar-fourier-zero-existence-is-deformation-dependent`, `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra`.

PC-197--PC-213 close the fixed finite scalar packet algebra, separated finite-valence Hardy lifts, the canonical weighted-cover shift, the scalar Haar-refinement star algebra, and prime-power/profinite mask incidence. Direct prime-power coupling before scalar contraction still leaves only classical anchored arithmetic or prime-local support geometry.

PC-214--PC-215 show that mixed-composite masks change that conclusion in a precise but limited way. For `N=pq`, the exact-order transverse compression is a bounded-complexity Kronecker sum. For squarefree `N` with `k=omega(N)` distinct primes, however, the same canonical compression has exact tensor locality `k-1`: genuine growing multi-coordinate incidence survives and cannot be represented by uniformly bounded-valence tensor terms.

PC-216 then kills the most tempting interpretation of the surviving coefficient values. Across omitted-prime refinement, every proper-divisor cyclotomic root value is an exact commuting multiplicative finite difference of a lower-conductor derivative potential. The first canonical transfer cube is therefore a coboundary: prime-ordering curvature and coefficient holonomy vanish, and the associated weighted permutation spectra carry no new cyclotomic information beyond elementary scales and modular permutation order.

The live mixed-mask route is consequently **collective rather than phase-holonomic**. A viable invariant must use the assembly of several conductor sectors in a way not removed by the PC-216 diagonal similarities—for example singular-value/norm/pseudospectral geometry, a genuinely non-Haar matrix fibre, or another source-forced mixed-composite incidence—and must fail on masks with the same divisor-Fourier support but no prime-specific coefficient relation. Growing locality is now established structure; arithmetic selection is not.

## Leave one-shell reciprocal Fourier zeros unless the source selects the deformation

PC-193--PC-196 show that scalar reciprocal-shell Fourier sign, zero existence, and first-zero scale depend on the chosen exponent. A useful scalar law needs a source-forced deformation/operator rather than a parameter chosen because it creates favorable zeros.

## Keep topology and realization separate from cyclotomic/operator identities

Potential-theoretic, geometric, old/new-fiber, nonabelian, non-Haar infinite-refinement, joint-algebra, or monodromy enlargements remain live only when the required object exists canonically and retains information beyond the classified packet/Toeplitz/Smirnov/Haar/profinite data. A larger state space is not a mechanism if a natural transform, support compression, coboundary, or refinement identity sends it back to universal operator structure.
