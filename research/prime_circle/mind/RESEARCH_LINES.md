# Prime-circle research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable prime-circle intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Preserve a genuinely nonclassical carrier before source-sparsity reconstruction

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-012-finite-scalar-cross-shell-fusion-is-closed-under-classical-packet-algebra` through `MI-023-growing-oriented-tensor-order-terminates-in-source-reconstruction`.

PC-295--PC-301 identify the diffuse fixed-scale boundary: the normalized boundary-log operator approaches a universal square-zero class, positive quadratic compression loses phase, independently averaged loop hierarchies collapse to one real symmetric average, and centered same-source Gram formation erases coordinatewise orientation before averaging.

PC-302 tests the simplest pre-Gram repair. Reflection acts by latent mode reversal, so unlabelled frame/subspace geometry remains gauge. Keeping labels restores a reflection-odd channel, but its first quadratic component is exactly sine/cosine Fourier covariance.

PC-303 closes the entire fixed finite tensor extension: every labelled frame monomial is one additive character of the source coordinates, averaging gives a joint Fourier coefficient, and fixed polynomial invariants generate the classical polyspectral algebra. For the canonical distinct-prime source, fixed-order joint transforms reduce to finite polynomials in the ordinary prime exponential sums.

PC-304 identifies the generic high-order endpoint: degrees through `q-1` recover the complete additive DFT, and the canonical nowhere-zero DFT makes the full cyclic bispectrum complete up to translation.

PC-305 sharply lowers the actual saturation scale. The canonical prime source has only `L_q=pi(q)-2` equal-weight atoms, so its first `L_q` power sums already recover the complete support by Newton--Girard. With the full spatial window `H_B={-B,...,B}`, degree `d` exposes every consecutive frequency through `dB`; therefore

`dB >= L_q`

is sufficient for exact source reconstruction. Since `L_q=O(q/log q)`, even a strongly sublinear tensor hierarchy may already be lossless.

The live route is therefore not “choose `d(q)<<q`.” It must keep the actually exposed frequency packet below a source-reconstruction threshold **and** identify a geometry-forced nonlinear/operator operation that consumes that partial packet before classical sparse-moment recovery becomes available. Genuinely cross-level or mixed-conductor structure remains an honest escape only when it cannot be reproduced by reconstructing each modulus separately and post-processing the sources.

## Keep defect resolution, carrier gauge, Fourier classicalization and reconstruction capacity separate

Reaching the `r_q` defect scale is necessary to escape the universal square-zero bulk but not sufficient. PC-299 shows that positive quadratic compression forgets phase; PC-300--PC-301 show that independence and centered Gram formation can erase still more structure before any higher statistic is formed.

PC-302--PC-303 sharpen the fixed-order classicalization boundary. Moving to labelled oriented frames restores orientation and higher tensor order can recover phase-sensitive polyspectra, but that information is still exactly source Fourier data. PC-304--PC-305 add the saturation boundary: sufficiently many source moments do not create a new carrier either; they become a lossless encoding of the prime support. The relevant scale is source sparsity and the accessible additive frequency alphabet, not ambient modulus size or tensor degree alone.

Any growing-order proposal must therefore state which frequencies become available, how `d(q)B(q)` or the actual character alphabet compares with `L_q`, which destination operation acts before source reconstruction, and what theorem distinguishes that intermediate regime from ordinary source recovery. Recovering more source coordinates is not itself RH progress unless the geometry supplies a new coercive or zero-sensitive operation on them.
