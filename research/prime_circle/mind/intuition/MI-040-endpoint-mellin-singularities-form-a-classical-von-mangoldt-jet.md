# MI-040 — Endpoint Mellin singularities form a classical von Mangoldt jet

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from PC-179, PC-335 and [PC-336](../../findings/PC-336-endpoint-mellin-distributions-are-generalized-von-mangoldt-jets.md), together with the classical structure theorem for distributions supported at one point.

The singular endpoint `s=1` is not an unlimited escape from the bounded Mellin no-go. For the exact prime-circle radial flux, every finite Taylor jet at `s=1` is related by an invertible universal lower-triangular transformation to the generalized von Mangoldt hierarchy `Lambda_1,...,Lambda_{K+1}`. Equivalently, every finite-order distribution supported at the endpoint sees only a finite generalized-von-Mangoldt jet.

The vanishing order makes the stratification explicit: `ord_(s=1) R_n(s)=omega(n)-1`. Higher endpoint derivatives successively expose almost-prime layers, but they do so through classical arithmetic functions already present before the geometric packaging. Increasing the order of a point-supported singularity therefore adds arithmetic resolution without creating an independent zeta-zero spectrum, functional-equation symmetry or critical-line selector.

This sharpens the distinction between **singular access and new mechanism**. Delta evaluation recovers ordinary `Lambda`; delta derivatives recover higher `Lambda_k`; approximate identities approach that support only by allowing the multiplier norm to diverge. The singular limit matches the bounded-multiplier obstruction of PC-335 rather than bypassing it.

**Boundary.** The classification covers finite-order distributions supported at `s=1`. It does not rule out unbounded or distributional Mellin symbols with non-endpoint support, shell-dependent singular order, genuinely cross-shell operations before scalarization, nonlinear radial constructions or a second carrier not generated by the same refinement algebra.
