# MI-013 — Prime-Circle information survives only beyond classical finite-component and total-rotation data

**Evidence level:** proved structural boundary through PC-268; only finer spectral/transfer channels remain open.

Growing collision geometry is not enough. Common dilation is exact repetition, nonresonant bulk spectrum and normalized positive log-volume converge to classical torus-return limits, fixed off-spectrum characteristic potentials are transforms of the same IDS, and PC-266--PC-267 show that connected-component simplicity, nodal order, and reflection parity ordering are classical consequences of the collision matrix structure.

PC-268 now closes the most canonical long-transfer escape as well. For the native symmetric Schur/Riccati sweep of `H_n-EI`, the cumulative focal/projective rotation is exactly the negative inertia,

\[
\operatorname{Rot}_n(E)=n_-(H_n-EI)=\#\{\lambda\in\operatorname{Spec}(H_n):\lambda<E\}.
\]

So the total Pruefer/Maslov/Schur rotation is not additional information beyond the finite eigenvalue-counting function. In the nonresonant simultaneous-growth regime, its normalized limit is exactly the distribution function of the deterministic torus IDS already classicalized in PC-262--PC-265, and matched pairwise-coprime rational-partition controls have the same limit. At the chiral center the count is even simpler: it is exactly the collision rank.

Together with PC-266--PC-267, this removes internal degeneracy, ordinary nodal count, reflection parity order, and **leading normalized total projective rotation** as arithmetic carriers. Recasting the same finite spectrum as an oscillation or winding index does not create a new source variable.

The surviving information, if any, must be finer than the IDS/inertia count: microscopic or mesoscopic spacing, subleading `O(1)` rotation fluctuations, individual matrix-valued Pruefer phases before totalization, shrinking energy windows, near-resonant scaling, transfer-norm/Lyapunov growth, or resonance itineraries whose complexity grows with the primitive source. Controls must match the local collision pattern and the relevant resonant approach itinerary.

**Boundary.** PC-268 classicalizes only the canonical total focal/rotation index and its leading nonresonant normalization. It does not identify every projective coordinate, subleading winding fluctuation, transfer norm, or near-resonant cocycle invariant with the IDS.
