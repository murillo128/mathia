# MI-013 — Growing collision geometry can be classical all the way to a fixed-width torus cocycle

**Evidence level:** proved structural boundary

## Core intuition

Escaping a fixed finite residue packet by letting the collision carrier grow is necessary but not sufficient. In the shared-upper Prime-Circle construction, growing rank, orientation, determinant volume, the complete finite singular spectrum, and every fixed local collision window all admit classical reductions. The remaining object is not a higher-dimensional operator but a **long fixed-width transfer cocycle driven by rational-torus returns**.

The useful discriminator is therefore no longer “does the dimension or spectral object grow?” It is whether a depth-growing transfer statistic retains information not determined by the classical return coding that generates every local coefficient.

## Strongest justified claim

PC-253--PC-254 show that shared-upper rank is an oriented congruence-lattice count in a rational triangle and that the pseudodeterminant factors into collision weights and elementary path-run factors. Growing support and orientation are genuine, but their first global compression remains classical.

PC-255--PC-256 extend the closure to several upper variables and every fixed-depth trace word. Fixed spectral moments reduce to finitely many shifted scalar collision correlations.

PC-257 removes the apparent full-spectrum loophole. The symmetric dilation of the complete shared-upper defect has support pathwidth/treewidth two. The characteristic polynomial and all singular values are therefore generated by a fixed-width Schur/transfer recursion along the ordered collision chain. Growing rank increases length, not interaction width.

PC-258 identifies that chain as an induced rational-torus cocycle. Collision locations are returns of `(0,0)` under the translation `(x,y)->(x+p/q,y+r/q)` to a fixed rectangle, and the exact normalized Green-kernel weights are bounded observables of the return point. Fixed local windows consequently have classical torus-translation limits.

## Synthesis of evidence

Prime Circle has reached a one-dimensional long-range boundary. All finite/local views of the carrier are now explained by classical modular or torus dynamics, while the complete operator is a product of fixed-size transfer updates. If new arithmetic information survives, it must live in an asymptotic property of that **long product**: a Lyapunov exponent, rare-return resonance, nonlocal cancellation, or another invariant whose observation depth grows with `q` or collision rank.

This is a stronger control than fixed-finite CRT closure. It says that even the complete finite spectrum can be organized by a classical fixed-width dynamical coding; only long-range cocycle behavior remains unclassified.

## Counterevidence / boundary cases

PC-258 does not compute the asymptotic transfer product or prove that all depth-growing statistics are universal. Rational torus returns at denominators tied to primes may retain long-range arithmetic correlations not visible in fixed windows. A fixed-width cocycle can have nontrivial Lyapunov or resonance behavior.

Nor does classical local coding make the whole construction irrelevant: the unresolved long-range statistic could still be highly arithmetic. The claim is only that another fixed-depth or local-window observable will not escape the established control.

## Epistemic status

**Exact local/full-finite classicalization boundary:** the growing collision carrier has classical oriented support, elementary determinant volume, fixed-width complete spectral recursion, and rational-torus local coding. The sole surviving branch is genuinely depth-growing cocycle dynamics.

## Novelty/prior-art status

The surrounding lattice, pathwidth, transfer, and torus-rotation ingredients retain their finding-level prior-art classifications. This intuition synthesizes their programmatic consequence.

## Falsification criterion

Exhibit a finite-window or fixed-depth statistic of the PC-257/PC-258 collision chain that is not determined by the stated weighted torus-return coding, or prove that a depth-growing transfer invariant reduces to classical torus data in a way that closes the remaining branch. Either would move the present boundary.

## Lean-formalizable core

The finite pathwidth-two decomposition, weighted collision-word factorization, and fixed-width characteristic-polynomial recursion are natural finite combinatorial linear-algebra targets.
