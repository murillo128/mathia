# MI-013 — Prime-Circle information survives only beyond classical finite-component spectral structure

**Evidence level:** proved structural boundary; the surviving long-transfer/fine-spectrum branch remains open.

Growing collision geometry is not enough. Common dilation is exact repetition, reversal halves the independent word, nonresonant bulk spectrum and normalized positive log-volume converge to classical torus-return limits, and fixed off-spectrum characteristic potentials are transforms of the same limiting IDS. PC-266 now removes another tempting finite-dimensional carrier: **internal eigenvalue multiplicity and ordinary nodal ordering inside a connected collision component are classical oscillatory-matrix structure**.

PC-253--PC-260 reduce the shared-upper defect to a fixed-width rational-torus transfer cocycle, show exact repetition under common dilation, and establish reversal parity. PC-261 shows that resonant path dependence is real but can be a bounded congruence itinerary rather than new arithmetic. PC-262--PC-265 classicalize the nonresonant bulk, positive log-volume, leading finite-patch edge, and fixed off-spectrum determinant/resolvent observables under matched controls.

PC-266 gives an exact finite-component refinement. After one checkerboard gauge, the two collision Gram factors are totally nonnegative; on every component separated only by double-gap seams, the weighted product is nonsingular oscillatory in the Gantmacher--Krein sense. Its squared singular spectrum is therefore positive and simple, with the classical sign-variation ordering. Repeated eigenvalues in the full problem can only come from coincidences between disconnected components; reversal supplies one forced source by pairing reflected components.

Thus an exact degeneracy, exceptional-point mechanism based on an internal collision, or ordinary Sturm/Pruefer nodal count cannot be the missing arithmetic observable for the finite connected shared-upper problem. Simplicity itself is prime-blind: any matched control with the same positive weights and binary adjacency pattern has it.

The surviving information, if any, must live in **quantitative placement and evolution of the simple spectrum** or in genuinely long transfer data: fine spacing, subleading edge fluctuations, near-spectrum/mesoscopic characteristic statistics, projective/rotation quantities, Lyapunov/subadditive behavior, or explicit resonance itineraries whose complexity grows with the primitive source. These candidates must be matched against controls with the same local collision pattern and resonance path, not merely the same limiting ratios.

**Boundary.** Oscillatory simplicity is proved only for the PC-257 shared-upper collision matrix with its common checkerboard gauge and double-gap decomposition. It does not automatically transfer to another operator. Nor does it classicalize fine spacing or asymptotic projective dynamics.
