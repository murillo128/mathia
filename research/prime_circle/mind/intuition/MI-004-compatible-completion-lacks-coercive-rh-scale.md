# MI-004 — Compatible all-level completion preserves labels, but full refinement covariance rigidifies local geometry to universal power laws

**Evidence level:** supported by exact/classical completion, covariance, metric, and finite-order operator rigidity through PC-168

## Core intuition

Passing from Prime-Circle shells to the compatible inverse limit preserves exact-order labels, divisor filtrations, and rational characters. But canonical local geometries on that completion do not turn those labels into an RH-selecting spectrum. Compatible power refinement is ordinary dilation in logarithmic radius, its unitary normalization forces the classical half-density exponent, and full refinement covariance now rigidifies not only regular metrics but every finite-order local differential coefficient into scale-homogeneous power laws.

Thus label preservation, the critical half-density, singular-looking local coefficients, and arithmetic zero selection remain distinct gates. A local singularity is not evidence of prime provenance when the refinement semigroup itself forces the same power law on a naked continuum.

## Strongest justified principle

PC-055--PC-074 establish the earlier boundary: the adelic solenoid preserves the rational label system, while broad commuting calculi and scalar dilation covariance fail compact resolvent; imposing a projective proper height recovers only a classical primitive-lattice zeta problem.

PC-165 makes the compatible radial action explicit. The completed punctured-plane power system splits as a logarithmic radial line times the solenoid. Haar `L^2` pullback is the classical unitary dilation representation with exponent `-1/2+it`; a formal integer pullback sum can display `zeta(1/2-it)`, but it is unbounded and the radial generator has continuous spectrum.

PC-166 shows that a regular local metric compatible with power homotheties becomes a flat constant-coefficient log-cylinder metric. PC-167 removes the special role of the squaring map: covariance under the full refinement semigroup forces any continuous positive local metric on either radial side to be `t^kappa` times a constant tensor. The only member regular through the logarithmic origin is the flat case `kappa=0`; singular members carry only a free scalar exponent and remain prime-blind.

PC-168 extends the rigidity from metrics to arbitrary finite-order local differential operators. Exact covariance under all refinements forces every coefficient to be a monomial `C_ab t^(a+b-sigma)`, eliminating angular dependence and log-periodic freedom. At second order the only scalar potential escape is inverse-square type. Any nontrivial self-adjoint extension/domain anomaly is therefore **additional domain data**, not arithmetic information generated by the local covariant coefficients themselves.

## What remains possible

A surviving completion operator must leave the classified local finite-order coefficient category. Live possibilities include nonlocal or cross-level operators, shell/level-dependent structure, genuinely radial--arithmetic coupling, nonlinear/noncommuting constructions, or a singular domain/boundary condition independently forced by the root system. Merely choosing a power-law singular metric, inverse-square potential, or extension parameter after the fact does not meet the source-selection requirement.

## Status / novelty

Solenoids, unitary dilation, homogeneous metrics, Euler-homogeneous differential operators, and inverse-square extension theory are classical. The synthesis is the separation: **full refinement covariance can canonically generate half-density and singular local power laws while remaining completely blind to primitive-shell arithmetic**.

## Falsification criterion

Derive from the compatible Prime-Circle data a local finite-order covariant coefficient outside the PC-167--PC-168 power-law classification, or produce a source-forced domain/nonlocal coupling whose discrete or relative invariant retains primitive-shell information and cannot be matched by the naked refinement semigroup.

- Full-semigroup homogeneity classification of local metrics.
- Monomial coefficient classification for refinement-covariant finite-order operators.
