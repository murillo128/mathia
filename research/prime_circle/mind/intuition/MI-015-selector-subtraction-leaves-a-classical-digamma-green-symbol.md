# MI-015 — Selector subtraction and subcritical finite sections are classically reproducible

**Evidence level:** proved by PC-282--[PC-284](../../findings/PC-284-boundary-log-finite-section-spectra-are-li-grid-reproducible.md) for the radial logarithmic one-profile boundary branch. No theorem is claimed for the rational-microscopic regime `log B ~ sqrt(log q)` or for observables outside the normalized singular-spectrum law.

PC-282 separates the divergent boundary anchor from the finite-section operator: after normalization, the boundary-first regime collapses to the exact modular collision selector. PC-283 classifies the selector-subtracted complete-domain remainder. Its nonconstant Fourier multipliers are strictly negative, its finite-`q` spectrum is explicit through the digamma function, and its low-mode large-`q` limit is the classical logarithmic Green kernel.

PC-284 closes the most immediate attempt to recover arithmetic information from source-dependent finite-section placement of that exact logarithmic remainder. The singularity can be clipped only on a microscopic residue set while keeping the anchor exact. The clipped profile is regular enough for the classical prime-to-Li transport, and the exact/clipped spectral discrepancy is small in mean. Consequently the entire normalized singular-spectrum law on prime pairs is reproduced by the denominator-`q` Li-grid control whenever

`log B=o(sqrt(log q))`.

The important separation is now exact. The complete-domain symbol is classical, and **finite-section placement remains classically reproducible throughout the same sub-`exp(sqrt(log q))` transport window**. A source-dependent matrix for an individual prime pair is not enough; the statistic consumed downstream must separate the prime law from the strongest non-prime control.

The surviving one-profile window is therefore genuinely critical: bandwidth large enough to leave the PC-284 transport regime, or a simultaneous `(q,B,rho)` scaling in which selector, remainder and placement interact before any one limit dominates. Otherwise the architecture must enrich the observable through cross-level/cross-source coupling or information not compressed to one ordered singular-value vector.

**Boundary.** PC-284 does not cover `log B` comparable to or larger than `sqrt(log q)`, additional magnification of a small prime-minus-Li residual, the pre-boundary three-parameter scaling, or enriched observables. It is a no-go only for the exact boundary-renormalized one-profile singular-spectrum law in the stated classical transport regime.
