# MI-028 — Post-compression Fourier closure is universal and its canonical eigensectors need not have RH-like zeros

**Evidence level:** exact-derived source-specific obstruction from [PC-312](../../findings/PC-312-mixed-conductor-periodic-packet-fails-finite-fourier-self-duality.md), strengthened by the universal closure theorem [PC-313](../../findings/PC-313-finite-fourier-pair-functional-equation-is-universal-hurwitz-duality.md) and the canonical off-critical-zero theorem [PC-314](../../findings/PC-314-fourier-symmetrized-canonical-packet-has-off-critical-zeros.md).

PC-311 reduces the direct bilinear mixed-conductor Poisson pairing to one even periodic coefficient `d` of period `N=qr`, whose Mellin transform is a finite bundle of ordinary Dirichlet `L(s+t,chi)` functions. PC-312 proves that the particular coefficient forced by the canonical Prime-Circle sources is not a finite-Fourier eigenvector.

PC-313 classifies the minimal vector repair. For every even periodic coefficient, classical Hurwitz duality gives a universal swap between `d` and `F_N d`; diagonalizing the swap produces `d+F_N d` and `d-F_N d`, exact scalar `+` and `-` finite-Fourier eigensectors. This closure is available for every even periodic sequence and therefore carries no Prime-Circle-specific mechanism by itself.

PC-314 closes the weaker hope that the **canonical source-generated** eigensectors might nevertheless inherit unusually good zero geometry. For the smallest admissible mixed pair `(5,7)`, an exact coefficient test shows that neither eigensector is a Dirichlet polynomial times a single Dirichlet `L`-function. Saias--Weingartner's classification then gives linearly many zeros in suitable fixed strips strictly inside `Re s>1` for both sectors. These zeros lie in the ordinary half-plane of absolute convergence, so the failure is not a continuation or gamma-factor artifact.

The surviving design boundary is therefore stronger than “couple before compression.” A viable Prime-Circle carrier must retain source-native information not determined by `(d,F_N d)` **and** provide a zero-selecting theorem stronger than scalar functional-equation symmetry. Source-dependent vector/operator structure, pointed shell data or nonlinear pre-scalarization coupling remain logically open only if their divisor control is independently justified.

The reusable lesson is that an exact Riemann-type functional equation is a symmetry statement, not a zero-confinement mechanism. Universal transform closure can manufacture the symmetry after information has already been compressed, and PC-314 shows that even the canonical source instance can then have abundant off-critical zeros.

**Boundary.** PC-314 is a decisive counterexample to the general post-compression Fourier repair, not a classification of every canonical pair. It does not rule out all vector/operator Prime-Circle structures, and it says nothing negative about the individual classical Dirichlet `L`-components before they are combined into the periodic eigensectors.
