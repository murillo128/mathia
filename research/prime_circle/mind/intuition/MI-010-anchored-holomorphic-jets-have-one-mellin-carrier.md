# MI-010 — Anchored holomorphic local jets do not create a second arithmetic carrier

**Evidence level:** exact through PC-188

## Core intuition

Making the cyclotomic field locally two-dimensional does not by itself escape the radial Mellin collapse. On the anchored ray, the holomorphic logarithm already ties angular and radial derivatives together by Cauchy--Riemann. After the scale normalization imposed by power refinement, every fixed finite angular/radial jet is just a fixed Euler jet of the same radial profile.

## Strongest justified principle

PC-184 proves that finite Euler jets have only one shell-dependent Mellin carrier. PC-188 supplies the missing local complex-analytic bridge: every mixed derivative of the holomorphic cyclotomic log field at the anchored ray equals a universal phase times the radial derivative of the same total order. The first transverse derivative of the harmonic conjugate is exactly the radial flux already classified upstream.

Consequently adjoining finitely many angular derivatives, harmonic-conjugate coordinates, or fixed linear combinations of normalized local jets changes the coordinate presentation but not the shell-dependent rank of the Mellin fiber. Positive shell-independent refinement-covariant quadratic readouts still cannot retain the Mangoldt null selector.

## What remains possible

The theorem is local and finite-jet. Global angular information, nonlocal couplings between distinct angles/radii, nonlinear products of jets, shell-dependent operators, genuinely independent source fields, and mixed-prime relations are outside it. Any such proposal must show explicitly which second carrier survives the eventual scalarization.

## Status / novelty

Holomorphy, Cauchy--Riemann, Euler derivatives, and Mellin calculus are classical. The durable line-specific synthesis is: **finite local complexification of one cyclotomic field remains one-carrier after the refinement-normalized Mellin quotient**.

## Falsification criterion

Produce a fixed finite normalized local angular/radial jet on the anchored ray whose shell-dependent Mellin data are linearly independent of the PC-184 carrier, invalidate the Cauchy--Riemann reduction in the stated holomorphic domain, or exhibit a covered positive quadratic readout retaining the prime-power null selector.
