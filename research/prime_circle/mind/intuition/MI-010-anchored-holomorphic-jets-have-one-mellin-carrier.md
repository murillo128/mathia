# MI-010 — Finite local cyclotomic nonlinearity adds classical depth, not a new arithmetic carrier

**Evidence level:** exact reduction plus classical cyclotomic cone/Tornheim prior art through PC-189

## Core intuition

The local cyclotomic loophole is now closed beyond linear jets. Finite polynomial products of anchored angular/radial jets do create additive mode coupling, so the output is no longer rank one. But after Mellinization that extra depth is exactly a finite root-of-unity-colored Mordell--Tornheim/conical Dirichlet family. Nonlinearity manufactures more indices from copies of the same cyclotomic source; it does not supply an independent arithmetic field.

Positive polynomial self-energies fail even more directly: every nontrivial even local jet energy is positive on every shell `n>1`, so it cannot preserve the exact prime-power versus mixed-shell selector.

## Strongest justified principle

PC-184 and PC-188 show that every fixed finite anchored local angular/radial jet is a fixed Euler jet of one cyclotomic logarithmic field and therefore carries one shell-dependent Mellin source at the linear level. PC-189 expands an arbitrary finite local jet monomial exactly, applies the root-of-unity divisibility filter, and shows that its Mellin transform lies in a finite span of colored Tornheim/conical functions with colors determined by the shell divisors.

For potential products this is directly the classical cyclotomic conical-zeta setting; at depth two it is the colored Tornheim class. Finite derivative order only shifts parameters inside the same architecture. Thus **fixed finite polynomial local enrichment changes classical cyclotomic depth, not the source category**. A prime-power selector can only survive through specially signed cancellations inside that classical family, while positive even-power readouts lose it on every composite shell.

## What remains possible

Growing or infinite nonlinear depth, genuinely nonlocal kernels coupling distinct radii or angles, shell-dependent old/new operators, infinite all-shell limits, singular boundary data, global uniformization/monodromy, or a genuinely second source field remain outside the theorem. These are not minor local refinements: each changes the information source or the limiting category before scalarization.

A proposed survivor should identify explicitly which carrier is unavailable to every fixed finite colored-cone representation and why the final readout preserves it.

## Status / novelty

Cyclotomic Möbius inversion, Mellin transforms, root-of-unity filters, Mordell--Tornheim series, and conical cyclotomic periods are classical. The durable synthesis is the closure statement: **finite local polynomial nonlinearity does not escape the cyclotomic source algebra already exposed by the linear Mellin reductions.**

## Falsification criterion

Produce a fixed finite local polynomial jet whose Mellin transform cannot be represented by the PC-189 colored-cone formula, or a positive even local jet energy that vanishes on a matched mixed-prime shell while remaining nontrivial on prime powers. Otherwise a new local route must leave fixed finite polynomial depth or introduce genuinely nonlocal/source-independent structure.
