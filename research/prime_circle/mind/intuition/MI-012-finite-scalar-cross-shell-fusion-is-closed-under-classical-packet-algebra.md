# MI-012 — Cross-shell richness stays classical when it closes inside coarse modules or cyclotomic packet algebra

**Evidence level:** exact coarse-module and Cauchy-packet algebra closure through PC-232

## Core intuition

Cross-shell coupling, noncommutativity, refinement depth, nonlinear operations, and a large retained codomain are not automatically new relational information. The decisive object is the **state category before the final invariant**. A construction can retain every new-prime sector, path label, and packet mode yet remain a module over a coarse base or an algebra generated by classical rational shifts.

The latest boundary is stronger than positive-square scalarization. Even before taking singular values, the two canonical Fourier-dual bilinear operations on the full fixed-base Cauchy packets close in an explicit rational-shift algebra. Growing the squarefree conductor makes that torsion support dense but does not create a new kind of shift state.

## Strongest justified principle

PC-224--PC-225 show that canonical pullbacks, diagonal masks, and conditional expectations/complements intertwine the coarse diagonal algebra. Any finite network is a coarse-module map, bounded limits preserve that structure, and the complete positive square is one scalar multiplier on the coarse level.

PC-227 identifies the full fresh-prime limit before residue/chord compression as finitely many shifted Cauchy packets. PC-229--PC-230 then show that residue branching and the source-native chord Laplacian fall back to normalized fixed-point behavior.

PC-231 tests canonical nonlinearity directly. Pointwise multiplication produces confluent poles at the same shifts; bilateral convolution adds shifts modulo one, with complementary resonances producing only punctures/deltas. PC-232 proves closure at arbitrary finite depth. At squarefree conductor `N` all nonintegral shifts stay in `(1/N)Z/Z`, and over all squarefree conductors the direct-limit shift group is exactly squarefree cyclotomic torsion. Operation depth cannot manufacture prime-power torsion absent from the source shifts.

## Program consequence

A successful Prime-Circle continuation must leave both the coarse-module quotient and the canonical cyclotomic packet algebra. Candidate exits need a source-forced relation among amplitudes, modes, or prime directions that is not generated by translation-invariant pointwise/convolution algebra and remains nontrivial after the final spectral/positive quotient.

Increasing conductor, pole order, packet count, or nonlinear expression depth is not enough unless the new state can be shown not to factor through the existing cyclotomic closure.

## Counterevidence / boundary

PC-232 does not classify nontranslation-invariant operators, several noncommuting source-forced operators, coupled numerator/normalization state, global uniformization/accessory data, or arbitrary dynamics whose operation class changes with the conductor. Simultaneous squarefree growth remains open in those categories.

## Epistemic status

**Exact closure of coarse-fiber refinement and canonical Cauchy-packet bilinear algebra; genuinely coupled cross-prime state remains open.**

## Falsification criterion

Within the declared operation classes, produce a network that leaves coarse-module equivariance or a finite packet expression whose fractional shift lies outside the subgroup generated by its inputs. A positive escape should define an intrinsic operation outside those classes and prove information gain against controls preserving the complete current packet state.
