# MI-012 — Cross-shell richness remains classical when it factors through thin or coarse-fiber state

**Evidence level:** proved for fixed finite scalar packet fusion, repeated prime-axis recoupling, one-step full new-prime leakage, and coarse-fiber-preserving refinement networks through PC-225

## Core intuition

Cross-shell coupling, noncommutativity, refinement depth, and a large retained codomain are not automatically new relational information. The decisive question is the **state category before the final invariant**. A construction can carry every new-prime sector and arbitrarily many path labels yet remain fiberwise rank one over the original coarse coordinate, after which singular-value compression sees only one scalar energy profile.

The missing carrier must therefore preserve a relation that cannot be removed by independent left unitaries inside each coarse refinement fiber, or it must genuinely mix different coarse residues before positive/spectral scalarization. Merely enriching the terminal fiber while preserving the coarse module does not cross the information boundary.

## Strongest justified principle

PC-197--PC-223 classify fixed-level packet fusion, Hardy/lower-conductor lifts, common-anchor Mangoldt selection, and arbitrary-depth prime-axis mask words. The repeated noncommuting words still factor through a thin prime-primary state and are exactly blind to middle mixed-conductor sectors.

PC-224 keeps the entire new-prime complement after one canonical coprime refinement. Nevertheless the old-to-new leakage decomposes independently over coarse CRT fibers, with `L^*L` equal to multiplication by the conditional-variance profile. Equal-profile masks are related by a direct-sum fiber unitary, so singular values and positive-square invariants forget the internal exact-order orientation.

PC-225 identifies the structural reason and closes the repeated-refinement repair. Canonical pullbacks, diagonal masks, and conditional expectations/complements all intertwine the coarse diagonal algebra. Any finite network they generate is therefore a coarse-module map `(Tf)(a,y)=v_y(a)f(y)`, and every bounded operator limit preserves that property. The complete positive square is always `T^*T=M_{w_T}`.

Thus richness must be audited **before** interpreting operator depth, refinement depth, or codomain dimension. A genuine escape must break coarse-module equivariance, interrogate the internal fiber vector through a distinguished source-forced operator/projector before the left-unitary quotient, or use a cross-base limit where the coarse coordinate itself changes essentially.

## Counterevidence / boundary

PC-225 does not prove that its scalar profile `w_T` is arithmetically trivial, nor does it classify source-forced operators that mix coarse residues, exact-order projectors inserted before compression, or simultaneous growing-base limits. Those are genuine category exits rather than positive evidence.

The theorem also concerns the positive/singular data that quotient terminal fibers by left unitaries. A joint observable retaining internal fiber orientation can distinguish maps with the same `T^*T`; it must be justified intrinsically and tested against matched controls.

## Epistemic status

**Exact closure of separated/thin-state and coarse-fiber-preserving refinement architectures; source-forced coarse-mixing or internal-fiber relational state remains open.**

## Falsification criterion

Within the PC-225 operation class, exhibit a network that fails to intertwine the coarse diagonal algebra or whose `T^*T` is not a scalar multiplier on the coarse level. A successful escape should identify a canonical operation outside that class and prove exact information gain against controls preserving the complete coarse profile and existing coefficient/Ramanujan data.
