# MI-012 — Canonical fixed-conductor enrichment closes inside classical packet and refinement algebras

**Evidence level:** exact finite/fresh-packet reductions and prior-art identifications through PC-243

## Core intuition

Escaping scalarity by adding coordinates or noncommuting refinement operators does not automatically preserve new arithmetic information. At fixed conductor, the natural enrichments repeatedly close into classical matrix, shift, Cuntz `ax+b`, and Bost--Connes structures whose behavior is already explained without a new prime-specific coupling.

## Strongest justified principle

PC-224--PC-239 show that finite scalar fusion, regular joint quotient coordinates, singular fibers, and original-coordinate drift either factor through commutative packet data or collapse after recentering to shift calculus. PC-240 confirms that the first genuinely noncommuting original-coordinate/projection pair still generates only matrix defects over that classical packet picture.

PC-241 identifies the full original-coordinate/refinement semigroup with the classical Cuntz `ax+b` system. PC-242 identifies the natural Hardy refinement Hamiltonian with Bost--Connes, and PC-243 reduces the cyclotomic Hardy covariance defects to shift/quasinilpotent compact models. The repeated failure mode is categorical: canonical fixed-conductor enrichment changes presentation without creating the missing relational invariant.

## Program consequence

Require the next candidate to name a relation that cannot be expressed inside the fixed-conductor packet/refinement category. Prefer simultaneous-scale or genuinely multi-conductor structure, and test it immediately on matched coprime controls before building further operator machinery.

## Counterevidence / boundary

Classicalizing the canonical operators does not rule out all operator-algebraic approaches. A simultaneous limit, global relation, off-circle analytic structure, or source-dependent coupling may leave the covered category. The no-go is against enrichment that remains functorially generated by the same fixed-conductor coordinates and refinements.

## Epistemic status

**Exact classicalization of the main fixed-conductor scalar and noncommutative refinement escapes; the open variable is a relation not generated by that category.**

## Falsification criterion

Exhibit a fixed-conductor invariant of the covered canonical operators that distinguishes prime refinement from matched coprime controls and survives the identified packet/Cuntz/Bost--Connes reductions.
