# MI-012 — Finite scalar cross-shell fusion is closed under classical packet algebra

**Evidence level:** proved for the declared Cauchy--Poisson character fields, finite holomorphic products, sesquilinear pairing, and source-native finite power-map correspondences through PC-200

## Core intuition

Cross-shell coupling is not automatically new relational information. In the current Prime-Circle scalar packet, several genuinely different-looking operations remain inside one classical algebra: a character field Mellin-transforms to a Dirichlet `L`-function; finite holomorphic products mix coefficients without mixing singular loci; same-angle sesquilinear fusion produces a product character; and different intrinsic power-map pullbacks merely rotate the Fourier diagonal to a rational ray that collapses back to one character packet.

The relevant boundary is therefore not “one shell versus several shells” or “diagonal versus non-diagonal.” A new scalar mechanism must leave the **finite character/rational-ray closure** after all source-forced multiplicative reductions have been applied.

## Strongest justified principle

PC-197 identifies the Cauchy--Poisson packet with classical Dirichlet `L` data. PC-198 shows that finite holomorphic cross-shell multiplication does not create genuinely mixed-root poles. PC-199 proves that the natural sesquilinear angular pairing is exactly a composite-character packet.

PC-200 then inserts different anchor-fixing power maps `z -> z^a` and `z -> z^b`, so the angular pairing is genuinely off-diagonal: only indices with `ak=bl` survive. That constraint is nevertheless a one-dimensional rational lattice ray. Writing `k=b_0r`, `l=a_0r` and using complete multiplicativity turns the ray into the single product-character sequence `eta(r)`. The two-depth Mellin transform is a nonvanishing elementary factor times `L(s+t,eta)`. Finite shell-independent combinations of these correspondences can add only an externally chosen finite prefactor.

## Counterevidence / boundary

The classification is finite and scalar. It does not cover kernels with genuinely two-dimensional Fourier support, shell-dependent operators forced by additional geometry, nonlinear or matrix-valued correspondences retaining several rays simultaneously, infinite refinement/solenoidal limits, or global uniformization/monodromy. Those categories remain open but receive no positive evidence from this closure result.

## Epistemic status

**Proved for the declared packet algebra.** The underlying character, Mellin, and composition-operator identities are classical; the durable contribution is the internal boundary they impose on Prime-Circle synthesis.

## Falsification criterion

Within the declared finite scalar class, exhibit a source-native cross-shell operation whose singular carrier cannot be reduced to shellwise factors or one classical Dirichlet-character packet after the exact Fourier constraints are solved. Outside that class, a successful mechanism must identify which additional source-forced degree of freedom prevents the rational-ray/character collapse.
