# MI-012 — Cross-shell richness stays classical under fixed packet shifts, scalar fusion, and finite tangent calculus

**Evidence level:** exact coarse-module, Cauchy-packet, normalization, fresh-fiber shift, and tangent/commutator closure through PC-235

## Core intuition

Cross-shell coupling, noncommutativity, mode mixing, refinement depth, and a large retained codomain are not automatically new relational information. The decisive object is the **state category before the final invariant**. The current fixed-base prime-circle constructions repeatedly reduce to classical rational-shift packet structure even when the operator is no longer diagonal in Fourier space.

The latest boundary is stronger than finite scalar packet closure. The canonical fresh-fiber mode mixer is exactly a bilateral shift, and the first subleading smooth-multiplier coordinate is ordinary lattice position. Their bounded commutator calculus is universal and does not remember a new cross-prime relation.

## Strongest justified principle

PC-224--PC-232 show that canonical refinement networks and the two natural packet bilinear operations remain inside explicit coarse-module/cyclotomic Cauchy-packet algebras at arbitrary finite depth. PC-233--PC-234 show that bounded macroscopic normalization and prime-base normalization do not leave that closure.

PC-235 tests the first geometry-forced nontranslation-invariant operator. Multiplication by the fresh-fiber coordinate becomes the bilateral packet shift `S`; the anchored chord Laplacian is `2I-S-S^{-1}` and maps a Cauchy packet to finitely many integer-translated poles. Thus fixed Laurent mode mixing remains in the same rational-shift state category.

The same finding identifies the first fresh-scale Taylor tangent as the lattice position operator `J_sigma`. The source Cauchy packet is outside every nontrivial `J_sigma` power domain, so the naive renormalized tangent does not define a new bounded packet observable. The commutators that remain bounded satisfy the standard Laurent-shift relation and lose the cyclotomic fractional center except as a scalar offset.

## Program consequence

A successful continuation must change the operation class in a source-forced way: conductor-dependent or growing-range mixing, simultaneous prime/coarse limits, several coupled packet sectors or new-prime directions, a controlled singular operator with the correct source domain, or genuinely global uniformization data. It must then show that the final target state cannot be generated by the fixed rational-shift/tangent algebra.

Merely adding Fourier non-diagonality, another fixed Laurent operator, higher finite commutator depth, or a formal subleading Taylor coefficient is now a control.

## Counterevidence / boundary

PC-235 does not classify operators whose shift support/degree grows with the fresh prime, nonsmooth/singular multipliers on a justified domain, simultaneous `N,q` limits, coupled sectors before the fixed-base limit, or global accessory/uniformization data. Those categories can evade the fixed Laurent-shift closure but still need a source-derived cross-prime relation.

## Epistemic status

**Exact closure of fixed-base refinement, packet algebra, Laurent mode mixing, and finite tangent calculus; genuinely conductor-dependent coupled cross-prime state remains open.**

## Falsification criterion

Within the declared fixed operation classes, produce a state outside the generated rational-shift algebra or a bounded source tangent contradicting PC-235's domain calculation. A positive escape should define an intrinsic conductor-dependent/coupled operation and prove information gain against controls preserving the complete current packet state.
