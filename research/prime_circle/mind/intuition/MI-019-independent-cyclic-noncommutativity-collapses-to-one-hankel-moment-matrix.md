# MI-019 — Independent cyclic noncommutativity collapses to one reflection-even Hankel moment matrix

**Evidence level:** exact cyclic factorization and matched reflection control from [PC-300](../../findings/PC-300-independent-cyclic-boundary-log-profiles-collapse-to-one-hankel-average.md)

Noncommuting factors do not by themselves preserve higher relational information. In the shared-source cyclic profile construction of PC-300, each adjacent pair contributes a same-source cross-Gram matrix `G_p`, but independent sampling around the cycle gives the exact factorization

`E L_k = tr((D Gbar_mu)^k)`.

All loop lengths are therefore generated by one averaged matrix. The centered source window makes `Gbar_mu` real symmetric Hankel-type and keeps only the reflection-even part of the additive source Fourier transform. In particular, `mu` and its reflected law `mu^vee` produce identical `Gbar_mu` and identical averaged cyclic moments for every `k`.

The concrete obstruction is **factorization before the statistic is read**. Higher loop order adds powers of the same compressed carrier instead of retaining new source relations. This is stronger than the quadratic Fejer collapse of PC-299: even an explicitly noncommutative multi-profile product can become ordinary self-adjoint moment data after independent averaging.

A viable multi-profile escape must prevent that factorization by keeping reused/correlated source variables, source-dependent ordering, a non-cyclic contraction, cross-level dependence, or a growing regime where collision terms contribute at the signal scale. PC-300 does not rule out those mechanisms, and it does not claim exact factorization for without-replacement sampling; it closes the order-one fixed-loop independent-sampling route.
