# MI-006 — Nonuniversal spectrum survives, but fixed-support scalar layers and canonical refinement bulk/section defects classicalize

**Evidence level:** supported by PC-135--PC-158 and the exact projector, cluster, refinement, and puncture classifications

## Core intuition

Prime-Circle spectra retain a genuine nonuniversal defect sector, but increasingly broad ways of reading that sector collapse to classical or base-level models. Every fixed connected scalar support classicalizes to finite-tuple singular-series data, and the most canonical cross-level full-chord refinements now classicalize as well: new-prime coarse compression is a commuting conjugacy polynomial, repeated-prime full fibers sample one fixed quadratic pencil, the normalized bulk of a genuinely new-prime fine fiber converges to a fixed Bloch density, and the one-missing-residue puncture contributes only a positive **prime-blind** spectral-shift budget.

The surviving object is therefore more specific than “arithmetic eigenvalues remain.” It must live in **growing support, microscopic/fine-fiber structure beyond the puncture, provenance-sensitive cross-level coupling, or a genuinely noncommuting/nonlinear assembly** not determined by those canonical limits.

## Strongest justified principle

PC-135--PC-141 establish that universal bulk convergence does not erase every arithmetic defect: a mesoscopic family of macroscopic defect eigenvalues survives. PC-142--PC-147 then show that the first top-band projector scale is nevertheless controlled by short chords in average/Mertens regimes, while operator-norm leakage distinguishes average capture from full subspace fidelity.

PC-149--PC-154 classify scalar support complexity. Fixed chord words are translated-coprimality counts; fixed-degree full-chord moments stratify into Hardy--Littlewood tuple singular-series data; and even all-orders `log det` resummation on a fixed connected vertex support stays in that finite-tuple class.

PC-155 closes the canonical coarse new-prime transport. Fiber-constant compression from `Nq` to `N` is an explicit affine combination of `A_N` and one multiplicative conjugate; prime-step superoperators commute and are invertible. Ordered-prime holonomy cannot be manufactured by this compression.

PC-156 classifies repeated-prime/full-fiber depth. Fiber Fourier transform turns the entire normalized operator into rational samples of one fixed finite-dimensional quadratic metric--cotangent pencil. Extra exponent depth refines the sampling grid but introduces no new operator coefficients.

PC-157 classifies the new-prime bulk. The primitive shell is a one-point-per-fiber puncture of an exact cyclic ambient model, and its normalized empirical spectrum converges in `W_1` at `O(1/q)` to a fixed base-level integrated Bloch density independent of the new prime.

PC-158 closes the most immediate sparse-outlier interpretation of that puncture. After padding the punctured operator, the ambient-minus-punctured difference is exactly a positive weighted-graph Laplacian with explicit trace norm. Only `O(phi(d))` ordered spectral positions can move by an order-one amount, uniformly in `q`, and the same formulas hold for any coprime composite fiber size `m`. The positive one-hole spectral-shift budget is therefore prime-blind and cannot itself be the sought prime-specific edge mechanism.

## Evidence synthesis and boundaries

None of these results proves that the entire defect sector is classical. Fixed-support results are not uniform in support size, empirical/budget controls do not classify microscopic spacings of all fine modes, and the canonical fiber geometry does not exhaust provenance-marked nonlinear couplings across several levels.

A viable continuation must state exactly where it leaves the classified carrier: support size growing with conductor, a fine-fiber statistic not determined by the fixed pencil plus one-hole positive shift, or a cross-level operation whose order/noncommutativity is geometrically forced.

## Status / novelty

Singular-series expansions, Bloch/Floquet decomposition, cosecant multiplication, graph Laplacians, interlacing/trace-norm bounds, and determinant cumulants are classical ingredients. The persisted synthesis is the current information boundary: **arithmetic defect survives, but fixed-support scalarization and the canonical macroscopic/refinement defects are increasingly universal or prime-blind**.

## Falsification criterion

Exhibit a fixed-support scalar full-chord observable outside the tuple hierarchy, a repeated-prime full-fiber coefficient outside the PC-156 pencil, an order-one new-prime bulk law beyond PC-157, or a prime-specific puncture shift contradicting the composite matched control of PC-158. A growing-support/nonlinear fine residual would evade rather than falsify these controls.

## Lean-formalizable core

- Fixed-support trace and linked-cluster stratification.
- New-prime coarse compression as commuting conjugacy superoperator.
- Repeated-prime fiber Fourier decomposition into a fixed quadratic pencil.
- New-prime bulk `W_1` convergence to a fixed Bloch density.
- Positive prime-blind puncture spectral-shift budget.
