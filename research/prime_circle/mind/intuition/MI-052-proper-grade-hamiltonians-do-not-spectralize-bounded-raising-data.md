# MI-052 — Common-unitary matched controls survive Fock, signature and centered cyclotomic enrichment when radial order is forgotten

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from PC-361--[PC-370](../../findings/PC-370-centered-cyclotomic-refinement-is-matched-control-equivariant.md), including the Chen--Hopf equivariance of [PC-369](../../findings/PC-369-chen-hopf-lie-structure-is-matched-control-equivariant.md). PC-371 supplies the explicit order-sensitive boundary.

Prime Circle has several ways to retain structure that homogeneous scalar summaries discard. Mixed Fock grades preserve resonant word contractions, path signatures retain ordered iterated-integral data, and centered cyclotomic refinement retains a richer finite Fourier geometry. None of those facts by itself supplies arithmetic discrimination.

The reciprocal matched control is related to the source by a common one-particle unitary. On Fock space that unitary acts tensorially and preserves canonical word-Gram data. PC-369 shows the same for Chen/Hopf signatures: tensor signatures are carried by tensor powers of the unitary and log signatures by the induced free-Lie automorphism. PC-370 shows that centering and canonical cyclotomic refinement remain matched by the corresponding unitary/orthogonal transport.

The recurring obstruction is equivariance before scalarization. Once the entire enriched object is functorially transported from source to control, later norms, spectra, Gram matrices, determinants or canonical contractions cannot distinguish them. Adding a richer algebra merely moves the same matched control into a richer category.

**Boundary.** The statement is intentionally restricted to constructions that forget the pointwise radial order cone. PC-371 proves that the shell-2 centered order is not preserved by the sign-flipped reciprocal control, so order-sensitive representations genuinely escape this particular no-go. They must then face the separate generic-unit-circle control and the classicalization of their moment data.
