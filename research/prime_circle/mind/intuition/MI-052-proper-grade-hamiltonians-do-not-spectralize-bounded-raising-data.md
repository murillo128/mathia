# MI-052 — Graded operator enrichment is useful only beyond scalar universality and common-unitary Fock controls

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [PC-361](../../findings/PC-361-proper-grade-hamiltonians-are-spectrally-blind-to-bounded-raising-signatures.md)--[PC-368](../../findings/PC-368-heterogeneous-fock-channels-retain-resonant-word-contractions.md), extending the completion and matched-control audits of PC-347--PC-360.

PC-361--PC-364 classify the canonical scalar one-sided route across its amplitude range. Bounded or relatively subcritical strictly raising source terms leave the proper grade spectrum unchanged; the critical closure produces a universal half-plane whose edge is the ambient summability threshold; and the supercritical family fills the complex plane through residual/compression spectrum. The apparent `1/2` edge is therefore not an arithmetic discriminator.

PC-365 shows that adding the scalar lowering partner does not automatically fix this: the two-sided repair enters classical `su(1,1)` Jacobi spectral theory. PC-366 and PC-367 give the analogous Fock-space collapse for homogeneous creator channels. One homogeneous channel is Wold/gauge data, and finitely many same-degree channels are classified by their Gram matrix together with the grading.

PC-368 locates the first exact failure of that homogeneous collapse. If channel degrees differ, words of different lengths can reach the same total grade and retain resonant contractions `<g_alpha,g_beta>`. These are genuine cross-grade source invariants and can appear in canonical vacuum moments of selfadjoint Fock combinations. Thus “mixed degree retains more information” is mathematically true.

The matched-control gate remains decisive. A common one-particle unitary sends every word of a fixed total grade through the same tensor power and therefore preserves every resonant word-Gram invariant. The Prime-Circle reciprocal control already has exactly that unitary relation. Heterogeneous Fock enrichment repairs information loss but not arithmetic discrimination.

The reusable audit is consequently two-stage. First classify what the infinite-dimensional closure actually retains beyond triangular or homogeneous universality. Then quotient that retained data by the strongest source/control equivalence. Only structure outside that equivalence can plausibly carry a Prime-Circle arithmetic selector.

**Boundary.** PC-368 does not classify every heterogeneous graded operator tuple and does not say that all mixed-degree observables are common-unitary invariants. It says the canonical word-contraction package is. Source-dependent boundary conditions, domains, non-word observables or a downstream theorem using additional arithmetic structure remain open. None of these results supplies a zeta-zero theorem.
