# MI-053 — Shell-2 radial order escapes the reciprocal parity control but classicalizes to Ramanujan moment data

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [PC-371](../../findings/PC-371-shell2-centered-order-cone-is-universal-ramanujan-moment-data.md), interpreted against PC-355 and PC-369--PC-370. No RH selector is claimed.

For `n>2`, the shell-2 centered profile admits the exact factorization `exp W_n(r)=prod_j(1-lambda_j y(r))`, where `y(r)=4r/(1+r)^2` and `lambda_j=cos^2(theta_j/2)` for the primitive-root conjugate pairs. Hence `W_n(r)<0`, it is inversion-symmetric, has a unique minimum at `r=1`, and `-W_n` is an absolutely monotone finite Hausdorff-moment potential in `y`.

This is the first canonical source structure in the recent Prime Circle chain that genuinely defeats the sign-flipped reciprocal control: `W_n -> -W_n` leaves the common-unitary tensor data matched but violates the source-native order cone. The PC-355 parity control therefore must not be promoted to a no-go for representations that retain pointwise order.

The escape is real but not yet arithmetic. Every conjugation-stable finite multiset of non-real unit-circle points has the same normalized cone with its own positive atoms `lambda_j`. Moreover the cyclotomic moment sequence `M_m(n)=sum_j lambda_j^m` is an explicit finite binomial transform of `phi(n)` and the Ramanujan sums `c_n(h)`. The canonical moment/resolvent spectralization therefore returns to classical primitive-root data; retaining all moments simply reconstructs the angular shell.

The durable boundary is **order plus arithmetic coupling**, not order alone. A useful next invariant must retain this cone while introducing a cross-shell or nonlocal source operation that generic unit-circle root configurations cannot reproduce and that does not become a lossless decoder of the input roots.

**Boundary.** PC-371 does not say that every order-sensitive construction is classical. It classifies the intrinsic shell-2 cone and its canonical moment/resolvent data. A further source-forced coupling may still discriminate, but its arithmetic content has to be demonstrated beyond these universal cone axioms.
