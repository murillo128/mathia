# MI-009 — Signed radial interior survives only before Mellin/refinement-coboundary collapse

**Evidence level:** exact through PC-187

## Core intuition

The signed cyclotomic radial flux is a genuine prime-power selector, but an increasingly broad source-native class of radial operations erases its mixed-shell discrimination. The boundary now includes not only fixed refinement-equivariant linear filters but also the exact child-shell refinement calculus and the first primitive-shell weighted ordered transport.

A useful continuation must therefore introduce genuinely new relational information before positive scalarization; formal noncommutativity or shell dependence is not enough when it is generated by a cyclotomic coboundary.

## Strongest justified principle

PC-179--PC-185 show that shellwise Mellinization, constant symmetric coupling, positive radial weighting, finite Euler jets, and every fixed bounded shell-independent refinement-equivariant operator reduce to one classical Mellin carrier. Positive covariant readouts then fill the Mangoldt nullspace rather than preserve prime-power selectivity.

PC-186 closes the canonical linear cross-level escape. New-prime birth is exactly a finite difference of the dilation action, repeated-prime refinement is pure dilation, and distinct-prime finite-difference directions commute. The mixed-prime radial refinement curvature is therefore identically zero; the Mangoldt boundary value is a first-difference anomaly of the singular seed, not curvature among prime directions.

PC-187 closes the first source-native nonlinear/ordered repair. Weighting dilation by the primitive-shell multiplier produces a nonzero order defect at composite depth, but the resulting holonomy is an explicit finite cyclotomic monomial and its cocycle is a coboundary. Its logarithm is a finite integer combination of existing shell fields, its flux has zero total mass, and its Mellin transform carries the same universal `Gamma(s) zeta(s)` factor. Nonzero order dependence inside this algebra does not create an independent arithmetic carrier.

## What remains possible

A second source field not generated by the same refinement algebra, a non-coboundary mixed-prime interaction, a genuinely relational cross-shell construction, or another source-forced sign-indefinite coupling remains outside the theorem. Any continuation must demonstrate mixed-prime discrimination after its final operation, not merely noncommutativity before a cyclotomic reduction.

## Status / novelty

Cyclotomic recursion, dilation/Mellin harmonic analysis, crossed products, and coboundaries are classical. The durable synthesis is the operation-order boundary: **the canonical radial refinement algebra is too flat/cohomologically trivial to manufacture a second arithmetic carrier from the existing shell field**.

## Falsification criterion

Find nonzero distinct-prime curvature in the exact PC-186 refinement law, show that the PC-187 weighted holonomy has a nontrivial cocycle class or a genuinely new Mellin carrier, or construct a covered positive/refinement readout that preserves mixed-shell discrimination.

## Lean-formalizable core

- Cyclotomic child-shell finite-difference factorization.
- Commutation of distinct-prime refinement differences.
- Finite cyclotomic factorization and coboundary identity for the weighted holonomy.
