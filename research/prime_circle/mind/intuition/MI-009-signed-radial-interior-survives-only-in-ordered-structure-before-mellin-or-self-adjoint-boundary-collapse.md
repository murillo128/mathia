# MI-009 — Signed radial interior survives only before Mellin/refinement-coboundary collapse

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from PC-179--PC-187, strengthened by [PC-335](../../findings/PC-335-bounded-indefinite-radial-forms-cannot-preserve-semiprime-nulls.md).

## Core intuition

The signed cyclotomic radial flux is a genuine prime-power selector, but an increasingly broad source-native class of radial operations erases its mixed-shell discrimination. The boundary includes not only fixed refinement-equivariant linear filters, exact child-shell refinement calculus and the first primitive-shell weighted ordered transport, but now every bounded sign-changing/indefinite quadratic Mellin readout of the resulting rank-one carrier.

A useful continuation must therefore introduce genuinely new relational information before bounded scalarization. Formal noncommutativity, shell dependence generated by a cyclotomic coboundary, or simply replacing positivity by an indefinite bounded multiplier is not enough.

## Strongest justified principle

PC-179--PC-185 show that shellwise Mellinization, constant symmetric coupling, positive radial weighting, finite Euler jets, and every fixed bounded shell-independent refinement-equivariant operator reduce to one classical Mellin carrier. Positive covariant readouts then fill the Mangoldt nullspace rather than preserve prime-power selectivity.

PC-186 closes the canonical linear cross-level escape. New-prime birth is exactly a finite difference of the dilation action, repeated-prime refinement is pure dilation, and distinct-prime finite-difference directions commute. The mixed-prime radial refinement curvature is therefore identically zero; the Mangoldt boundary value is a first-difference anomaly of the singular seed, not curvature among prime directions.

PC-187 closes the first source-native nonlinear/ordered repair. Weighting dilation by the primitive-shell multiplier produces a nonzero order defect at composite depth, but the resulting holonomy is an explicit finite cyclotomic monomial and its cocycle is a coboundary. Its logarithm is a finite integer combination of existing shell fields, its flux has zero total mass, and its Mellin transform carries the same universal `Gamma(s) zeta(s)` factor. Nonzero order dependence inside this algebra does not create an independent arithmetic carrier.

PC-335 removes the positivity hypothesis from the natural bounded one-carrier selector escape. On any Mellin line `Re(s)=sigma>0`, every bounded real multiplier `h(t)` gives a quadratic shell response `Q_(sigma,h)(n)=int h(t)|R_n(sigma+it)|^2dt`. If this response vanishes on `pq` for a fixed prime `p` and an unbounded sequence of auxiliary primes `q`, then it also vanishes on every `p^a`. The large-`q` radical factor forces the one-prime moment to zero; at `sigma=1` the same conclusion follows by Riemann--Lebesgue. Thus arbitrary bounded indefinite Hermitian readouts of the rank-one Mellin carrier cannot preserve the semiprime nulls while retaining prime-power response.

The exact selector `R_n(1)=Lambda(n)` survives only as the singular endpoint/frequency evaluation already known from PC-179. Point evaluation is distributional in Mellin frequency, not a bounded multiplier readout.

## What remains possible

A second source field not generated by the same refinement algebra, a non-coboundary mixed-prime interaction, a genuinely cross-shell construction that mixes shell labels before scalarization, or a singular/unbounded/nonlinear Mellin operation remains outside the theorem. Any continuation must demonstrate mixed-prime discrimination after its final operation, not merely noncommutativity or sign-indefiniteness before reduction to one bounded carrier.

## Status / novelty

Cyclotomic recursion, dilation/Mellin harmonic analysis, crossed products, coboundaries and Riemann--Lebesgue are classical. The durable synthesis is the architecture boundary: **the canonical radial refinement algebra is too flat/cohomologically trivial to manufacture a second arithmetic carrier, and bounded indefinite scalarization of its remaining one-carrier Mellin channel still cannot retain the Mangoldt mixed-shell nulls**.

## Falsification criterion

Find nonzero distinct-prime curvature in the exact PC-186 refinement law, show that the PC-187 weighted holonomy has a nontrivial cocycle class or a genuinely new Mellin carrier, or construct a bounded real Mellin symbol and one prime `p` whose quadratic response vanishes on `pq` for unbounded auxiliary primes `q` while remaining nonzero on some `p^a`.

## Lean-formalizable core

- Cyclotomic child-shell finite-difference factorization.
- Commutation of distinct-prime refinement differences.
- Finite cyclotomic factorization and coboundary identity for the weighted holonomy.
- The PC-335 auxiliary-prime moment implication once the analytic integrability/Riemann--Lebesgue hypotheses are isolated.
