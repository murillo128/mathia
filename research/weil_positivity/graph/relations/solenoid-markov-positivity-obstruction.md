# Solenoid positivity and the translation-invariant Markov obstruction

[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-038-compatible-adelic-solenoid-dirichlet-energy-is-rational-square-and-noncoercive|WP-038]] tests the direct positive-energy route opened by the compatible arithmetic solenoid and derives the unique refinement-compatible leafwise Dirichlet form. Its rational-square symbol is noncoercive and does not provide the Mangoldt selector required by Weil positivity.

[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-039-translation-invariant-markov-energies-cannot-carry-mangoldt-support-as-their-fourier-symbol|WP-039]] explicitly takes the nonlocal transverse escape left by WP-038 and proves a broader obstruction: a conservative translation-invariant symmetric Dirichlet form on a compact abelian group cannot have the required prime-power-only Mangoldt support as its scalar Fourier energy symbol.

The supported refinement is:

```text
canonical compatible solenoid energy
    -> positive but noncoercive rational-square symbol
    -> allow nonlocal translation-invariant Markov energy
    -> subgroup-zero-set obstruction forbids Mangoldt support
```

The obstruction is scoped to scalar translation-invariant Markov/Dirichlet forms. The source explicitly leaves non-translation-invariant, matrix-valued/internal-state, compressed/quotient, nonlinear, and genuinely coupled finite-archimedean constructions open.
