# Solenoid positivity and the regular transverse-energy obstruction

[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-038-compatible-adelic-solenoid-dirichlet-energy-is-rational-square-and-noncoercive|WP-038]] derives the unique refinement-compatible leafwise Dirichlet form on the arithmetic solenoid and shows that its rational-square symbol is noncoercive and does not provide the Mangoldt selector.

The subsequent source-backed tests progressively enlarge that regular positive class:

- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-039-translation-invariant-markov-energies-cannot-carry-mangoldt-support-as-their-fourier-symbol|WP-039]] rules out scalar translation-invariant conservative Markov/Dirichlet forms whose Fourier energy would have prime-power-only Mangoldt support.
- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-040-canonical-solenoid-sobolev-trace-is-only-l2-and-cannot-host-weil-haar-tangent|WP-040]] shows that Sobolev scales generated by the canonical leafwise Laplacian induce only `L^2` regularity on the profinite fiber and cannot host the singular Weil/Haar tangent.
- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-041-compatible-profinite-cycle-energy-is-bounded-and-leaf-time-derived|WP-041]] derives the canonical compatible polygon-edge energy on the profinite fiber, but it is bounded, has arbitrarily soft high-conductor modes, and is exactly an integer leaf-time translation rather than an independent finite-adic derivative.

The supported refinement is:

```text
canonical solenoid leaf energy
    -> positive but noncoercive
nonlocal scalar translation-invariant Markov energy
    -> Mangoldt-support obstruction
leaf-generated Sobolev trace
    -> no conductor-growing regularity / cannot host the singular tangent
embedded compatible cycle energy
    -> bounded and leaf-time derived
```

This relation closes regular scalar translation-invariant/Sobolev/cycle-energy repairs of the canonical solenoid carrier. It does not cover non-translation-invariant, matrix/internal-state, shell-dependent, singular, nonseparable finite-archimedean, or other category-changing constructions explicitly left open by the source findings.
