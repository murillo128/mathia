# Positive finite-place geometry still does not supply the global Weil pairing

[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-043-cycle-laplacian-shell-logdet-recovers-mangoldt-but-spectral-positivity-is-the-wrong-pairing|WP-043]]--[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-068-full-root-hardy-differences-make-mangoldt-anchor-functional-unbounded|WP-068]] establish the older boundary: intrinsic positive carriers can recover arithmetic selectors and the `q=2` channel, yet natural positive/Krein/metric reductions either change the pairing, force the polar sign, or leave the exact Mangoldt anchor unbounded in the canonical Hardy energy.

The regular-extension escape is now sharply closed. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-069-positive-hardy-extensions-cannot-carry-unbounded-mangoldt-anchor-at-finite-energy|WP-069]] rules out every regular positive extension that keeps the Hardy finite-shell energy and tries to represent the anchor by a finite-energy vector. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-070-q2-antipodal-hardy-correction-is-blind-to-odd-full-root-controls|WP-070]] shows the canonical positive antipodal correction remains blind to the required null controls, and [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-071-rotation-invariant-hilbert-completions-cannot-bound-mangoldt-boundary-anchor|WP-071]] rules out the entire rotation-invariant scalar Hilbert/RKHS repair class if cyclotomic shells and bounded boundary evaluation are both required.

A genuinely positive survivor nevertheless exists once the topology itself is pointed. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-072-base-point-local-dirichlet-energy-regularizes-mangoldt-anchor-but-is-not-global-weil-form|WP-072]] gives a base-point local Dirichlet form in which all cyclotomic shells have finite energy and the Mangoldt boundary anchor is continuous. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-073-pointed-dirichlet-root-cover-isometry-forces-critical-half-weight|WP-073]] then derives the `n^{-1/2}` normalization from intrinsic root-cover isometry rather than inserting the critical half-weight by hand.

[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-074-pointed-cover-inverse-scale-defect-has-positive-log-degree-trace-but-poisson-weil-lift-is-indefinite|WP-074]] strengthens the finite-place synthesis: a positive trace-class inverse-scale defect has trace `log n`, and primitive prime-ray Gram coefficients give `(log p)p^{-k/2}`. But the corresponding positive Poisson kernel reaches the Weil finite summand only through an indefinite subtraction. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-075-positive-shifted-resolvent-cover-defects-mix-log-degree-with-digamma-but-exact-finite-weights-force-zero-shift|WP-075]] adds the compatibility obstruction: turning on the shifted positive-resolvent parameter that exposes the digamma profile necessarily changes the exact finite `log p` coefficient; exact finite weights force the zero shift where that archimedean correction disappears.

The territory is therefore not “finite arithmetic recovery is missing.” The finite selector, half-weight and log-degree now coexist in one pointed positive geometry. The unresolved source-backed problem is a **global finite-plus-archimedean/polar coupling with an independent sign theorem** that preserves those exact finite weights rather than reaching them through an indefinite Poisson subtraction or contaminating them when the digamma/Gamma sector is activated.
