# Positive finite-place geometry still does not supply the global Weil pairing

[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-043-cycle-laplacian-shell-logdet-recovers-mangoldt-but-spectral-positivity-is-the-wrong-pairing|WP-043]]--[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-071-rotation-invariant-hilbert-completions-cannot-bound-mangoldt-boundary-anchor|WP-071]] establish the older boundary: intrinsic positive carriers can recover arithmetic selectors and the `q=2` channel, yet natural positive/Krein/metric reductions either change the pairing, force the polar sign, or leave the exact Mangoldt anchor unbounded in the canonical Hardy energy. Regular positive extensions and rotation-invariant Hilbert repairs are correspondingly closed under their stated hypotheses.

A genuinely positive survivor exists once the topology itself is pointed. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-072-base-point-local-dirichlet-energy-regularizes-mangoldt-anchor-but-is-not-global-weil-form|WP-072]]--[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-075-positive-shifted-resolvent-cover-defects-mix-log-degree-with-digamma-but-exact-finite-weights-force-zero-shift|WP-075]] put the finite Mangoldt selector, critical half-weight and positive `log n` cover-scale defect into one pointed Dirichlet geometry, while showing that the obvious Poisson/shifted-resolvent completions either become indefinite at the Weil subtraction or contaminate the exact finite coefficients when the archimedean profile is activated.

The most canonical positive globalization by boundary averaging is now classified negatively. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-077-semigroup-invariant-basepoint-averaging-is-pointed-plus-haar-and-haar-kills-cover-defects|WP-077]] proves that a positive basepoint average compatible with every root-cover degree is only a convex combination of the pointed form and Haar Dirichlet energy; the Haar branch kills both the bounded anchor and the positive cover defect. Thus averaging the existing local fibers does not create a third semigroup-compatible sign mechanism.

Möbius support extraction almost isolates exactly the desired arithmetic support, but positivity fails at the operator level. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-078-mobius-primitive-of-positive-cover-cocycle-is-indefinite-off-prime-powers|WP-078]] shows that the divisor-Möbius primitive of the positive cover cocycle has trace `Lambda(n)` and remains positive on prime powers, but is nonzero trace-zero and indefinite on mixed-prime integers. Standard sign repairs restore unwanted composite mass.

The natural quotient repairs also collapse. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-079-cover-coinvariant-quotient-collapses-to-trace|WP-079]] identifies the Hausdorff cover-coinvariant quotient of the diagonal trace ideal with the ordinary trace, so mixed composites disappear only because all trace-zero operator geometry has been discarded. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-080-full-trace-ideal-cover-coinvariants-collapse-to-zero|WP-080]] shows that enlarging to the full trace class or standard Schatten/compact ideals is even more destructive: the corresponding cover coinvariants vanish entirely.

The territory is therefore not missing finite arithmetic recovery. The surviving source-backed problem is a genuinely richer **finite-plus-archimedean/polar sign mechanism** that is neither positive averaging of the pointed fibers, Möbius cancellation disguised as positivity, nor zeroth cover coinvariants on standard operator ideals. Higher/cohomological coefficients, selective geometry-forced quotients, nonlinear rank/volume mechanisms, or another nonseparable coupling remain outside these no-go classes and require their own exact sign and fidelity tests.
