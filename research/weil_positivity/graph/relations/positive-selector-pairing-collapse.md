# Exact cover-positive geometry survives, but the global Weil pairing is still missing

[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-043-cycle-laplacian-shell-logdet-recovers-mangoldt-but-spectral-positivity-is-the-wrong-pairing|WP-043]]--[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-092-refinement-coherent-fixed-infinite-auxiliary-sectors-still-freeze-to-one-collective-block|WP-092]] establish the earlier boundary: pointed cover geometry can recover the finite Mangoldt/half-weight data, while regular positive completions, standard coinvariants, fixed positive mixtures and fixed refinement-coherent auxiliary sectors either change the pairing, collapse or lose the required global scale/sign.

A non-diagonal exact-cover-positive supplier genuinely exists. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-093-finite-band-cover-covariance-forces-a-weighted-dirichlet-cone-with-continuous-dual-hahn-critical-ray|WP-093]] classifies every fixed finite-band solution and finds a weighted Dirichlet cone with an independently positive critical ray, but that ray is classical Cesàro/continuous-dual-Hahn geometry, works for every integer cover degree and does not itself recover the archimedean Weil form.

The most canonical stationary infinite-range repairs close negatively. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-094-exact-cover-covariance-collapses-positive-block-toeplitz-forms-to-singular-endpoint-anchor|WP-094]] forces every positive block-Toeplitz solution to the singular endpoint anchor and shows every nonzero such form is nonclosable. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-095-finite-band-cover-positive-energy-cannot-regularize-the-endpoint-anchor|WP-095]] proves that adding the full finite-band positive cone cannot regularize that endpoint while preserving positivity and closability.

The arbitrary infinite-range cone is nevertheless larger. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-096-exact-cover-positive-forms-are-prime-torus-grams-but-sparse-weil-support-needs-infinite-diagonal|WP-096]] conjugates every exact cover-positive form to a multiplicative Toeplitz/prime-torus Gram measure. Sparse one-prime Weil Fourier support would require infinite diagonal mass, but [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-097-mixed-prime-product-completion-gives-sharp-finite-diagonal-threshold|WP-097]] shows that mixed-prime interactions restore positivity with an explicit finite sharp diagonal threshold. Those mixed coefficients are essential: deleting them recreates the divergent sparse obstruction.

The live frontier is therefore more precise than "find another positive metric": use the nonseparable mixed-prime positive cone in a structural operation that preserves its sign while producing the exact finite Weil readout and the missing archimedean/polar contribution. The current findings do not supply that operation, and no global Weil positivity theorem follows from the positive prime-torus completion alone.
