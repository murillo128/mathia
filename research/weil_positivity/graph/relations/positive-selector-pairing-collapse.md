# Positive selector readouts do not supply the Weil pairing

[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-043-cycle-laplacian-shell-logdet-recovers-mangoldt-but-spectral-positivity-is-the-wrong-pairing|WP-043]] proves that intrinsic positive cycle geometry can recover `Lambda(n)` while still using the wrong pairing for the finite Weil birth matrix. The subsequent findings test increasingly structured sign-preserving repairs.

- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-044-radial-gram-contrasts-cancel-boundary-weil-birth-term|WP-044]]--[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-047-noncomparable-radial-schur-scales-trade-arithmetic-for-divergent-self-energy|WP-047]] show that shell-blind, shell-dependent and even noncomparable positive radial Schur/Feshbach schemes either cancel the common arithmetic birth term, attenuate it to zero, or retain it only after subtracting the positive divergent self-energy and thereby lose the inherited sign theorem.
- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-048-anchored-reflection-and-cycle-extremum-select-q2-riemann-gamma-channel|WP-048]] gives a genuine local bridge: anchored reflection and cycle extremality intrinsically select the order-two Riemann Gamma channel.
- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-049-anchored-reflection-grading-cancels-primitive-shell-mangoldt-contrast|WP-049]] shows that the same reflection cannot grade the finite Mangoldt term: primitive shells above order two have paired even/odd spectra.
- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-050-reflection-odd-cycle-current-adds-dyadic-mangoldt-shadow|WP-050]] shows the canonical reflection-odd current adds an unavoidable dyadic Mangoldt shadow rather than a Weil grading.
- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-051-positive-schatten-energies-of-hardy-remainder-have-full-composite-support|WP-051]] rules out the direct positive Schatten-energy escape of the Hardy trace-class remainder because it has full composite support.

The finite selector and the archimedean channel are therefore both present in intrinsic positive constructions, but no current source-backed positive pairing combines them with the required Weil sign. The territory remains open only for a genuinely new coupled/category-changing operation with its own independent sign theorem.
