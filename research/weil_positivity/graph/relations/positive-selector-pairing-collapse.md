# Exact cover-positive geometry survives, but the global Weil pairing is still missing

[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-043-cycle-laplacian-shell-logdet-recovers-mangoldt-but-spectral-positivity-is-the-wrong-pairing|WP-043]]--[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-092-refinement-coherent-fixed-infinite-auxiliary-sectors-still-freeze-to-one-collective-block|WP-092]] establish the earlier boundary: pointed cover geometry can recover the finite Mangoldt/half-weight data, while regular positive completions, standard coinvariants, fixed positive mixtures and fixed refinement-coherent auxiliary sectors either change the pairing, collapse or lose the required global scale/sign.

The full algebraic cover-positive cone is larger. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-096-exact-cover-positive-forms-are-prime-torus-grams-but-sparse-weil-support-needs-infinite-diagonal|WP-096]] conjugates every exact cover-positive form to a multiplicative Toeplitz/prime-torus Gram measure. Sparse one-prime Weil Fourier support requires infinite diagonal mass, but [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-097-mixed-prime-product-completion-gives-sharp-finite-diagonal-threshold|WP-097]] shows that mixed-prime interactions restore positivity with an explicit finite sharp threshold.

Those mixed modes cannot simply be removed by the most canonical positive linear operations. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-098-positive-prime-torus-quotients-preserving-coordinate-observables-cannot-erase-mixed-prime-modes|WP-098]] uses the multiplicative domain to show that a positive unital quotient preserving every prime-coordinate unitary must preserve all mixed characters as well; the linear Fourier projection that deletes them is not positive. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-099-passive-positive-auxiliary-elimination-cannot-sparsify-the-mixed-prime-completion|WP-099]] extends the obstruction to arbitrary positive auxiliary sectors eliminated by shorting, Schur complement or energy minimization: passive reduction is order-dominated and cannot turn the finite-mass completion into the sparse Weil carrier, and exact diagonal preservation freezes all coefficients.

The measure-class boundary is subtler. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-100-mixed-prime-product-completion-is-haar-singular-at-the-weil-boundary|WP-100]] proves that the independent product completion is Kakutani-singular to product Haar at the critical exponent. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-101-correlations-restore-haar-equivalence-at-critical-diagonal-but-only-below-zygmund-endpoint|WP-101]] then gives a genuine positive survivor: correlated mixed-prime completions can restore Haar equivalence at the same sharp diagonal mass while retaining every exact one-prime Weil ray. The price is unavoidable roughness below the Zygmund `L(log L)^{1/2}` endpoint. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-102-exact-critical-prime-torus-completions-have-infinite-cylindrical-fisher-energy|WP-102]] proves that every exact critical completion nevertheless has infinite cylindrical Fisher/square-root Dirichlet energy.

[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-103-gibbs-exponentiation-sparsifies-weil-score-but-critical-relative-entropy-diverges|WP-103]] separates mixed-mode sparsification from the sign problem. Exponentiating the exact one-prime score produces a strictly positive Gibbs carrier whose **log density** has exactly the sparse Weil prime-power rays and no mixed-prime Fourier modes, so nonlinear logarithmic readout really can bypass the positive-linear obstructions of WP-098--WP-099. But positivity of an exponential density is tautological for arbitrary bounded signed potentials, while at the critical exponent both the all-prime relative entropy and the canonical multiplicative/Kronecker log-density energy diverge. The matched free-generator control shows that neither the positivity nor the regularity threshold is specifically Riemannian.

The live frontier is therefore no longer "find a way to sparsify mixed modes". A nonlinear map can do that exactly. What is missing is a genuinely transforming finite--archimedean operation that ties the selected signed finite-prime observable to a nontrivial independent positive form, retains the exact coefficients, stays meaningful at the critical all-prime scale, and derives the polar/archimedean contribution plus global sign rather than importing them. No current finding supplies that operation, so C3 remains open.
