# Positive finite-place geometry still does not supply the global Weil pairing

[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-043-cycle-laplacian-shell-logdet-recovers-mangoldt-but-spectral-positivity-is-the-wrong-pairing|WP-043]]--[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-080-full-trace-ideal-cover-coinvariants-collapse-to-zero|WP-080]] establish the older boundary: pointed positive cover geometry can recover the exact finite Mangoldt/half-weight data, but regular positive completions, basepoint averaging, Möbius support extraction and standard cover coinvariants either change the pairing, become indefinite, collapse to ordinary trace, or erase the operator geometry.

The regular scale-covariant escape is now tighter. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-081-fixed-shift-cover-coboundaries-form-a-positive-half-line-with-the-same-log-degree-class|WP-081]]--[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-084-fixed-shift-positive-cover-family-has-singular-fisher-boundary-at-covariant-origin|WP-084]] show that fixed-shift positive cover families all carry the same logarithmic-degree class, while regular Jensen/Fisher attempts either reduce to bounded/dyadic readouts or place the exact Mangoldt behavior at a singular boundary.

Finite positive reference dictionaries do not repair this. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-085-cover-range-principal-angle-defect-is-positive-but-log-degree-response-is-reference-relative|WP-085]]--[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-088-finite-multireference-cover-projection-spectral-readouts-are-affine-on-common-multiple-towers|WP-088]] show that principal-angle and bounded two-projection/multireference readouts are reference-relative or affine on common-multiple towers. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-089-cover-coherent-finite-global-couplings-freeze-to-one-collective-block|WP-089]] confines every fixed refinement-coherent finite auxiliary coupling to one collective block. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-092-refinement-coherent-fixed-infinite-auxiliary-sectors-still-freeze-to-one-collective-block|WP-092]] removes the finite-dimensional loophole: a fixed arbitrary Hilbert auxiliary sector, even with unbounded global dynamics, still freezes to the same single collective channel and produces tower-independent standard relative spectral responses.

The shifted-resolvent repair is also rigid. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-090-fixed-positive-shift-mixtures-cannot-cancel-digamma-contamination|WP-090]] proves that a fixed positive mixture matching exact `log n` data on an unbounded degree set must be the zero-shift carrier itself, so positive averaging cannot cancel the digamma contamination while retaining the finite weights. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-091-schiffer-finite-certificate-is-generic-compact-positivity-not-pole-structure|WP-091]] separately classicalizes the finite Schiffer certificate: its existence reflects generic compact positivity after finite-order contact, not a hidden finite avatar of the global pole expansion.

The territory remains open, but the survivor must leave these fixed separated families: a genuinely singular/domain-changing or refinement-dependent operation, an infinite/nonperiodic construction whose auxiliary sector itself transforms with the arithmetic tower, a higher-cohomological/twisted coefficient object, or a nonseparable finite--archimedean coupling introduced before scalar positivity. Any survivor must retain the exact finite selector and produce the global Weil sign independently.
