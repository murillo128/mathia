# Positive selector readouts do not supply the Weil pairing

[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-043-cycle-laplacian-shell-logdet-recovers-mangoldt-but-spectral-positivity-is-the-wrong-pairing|WP-043]]--[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-051-positive-schatten-energies-of-hardy-remainder-have-full-composite-support|WP-051]] show that intrinsic positive geometry can recover `Lambda`, anchored reflection can select the `q=2` Riemann Gamma channel, and yet the natural positive reductions either erase the finite birth term, cancel its grading, add a dyadic shadow, or have the wrong composite support.

The full-root Hardy audit closes an increasingly broad family of direct sign repairs:

- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-061-q2-hardy-gram-is-positive-but-full-root-channel-is-indefinite|WP-061]] shows the primitive order-two Hardy shell gives a genuine positive Gram sandwich, but the independently selected full-root `q=2` channel is indefinite and the primitive positive readout does not preserve Weil finite-place locality.
- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-062-q2-half-turn-equivariant-compression-cannot-be-positive|WP-062]] proves the full-root channel anticommutes with the canonical half-turn, so every nonzero compression preserving that symmetry remains indefinite.
- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-063-q2-hardy-krein-repair-is-uniquely-the-polar-absolute-value|WP-063]], [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-064-bounded-self-adjoint-metric-repairs-force-the-polar-sign|WP-064]] and [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-065-unbounded-self-adjoint-metric-products-still-force-polar-sign|WP-065]] progressively remove parity-exchanging, arbitrary bounded self-adjoint, and operator-level unbounded self-adjoint metric escapes: positivity forces the same polar sign, with only a positive commuting weight left.
- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-067-base-shell-hardy-canonical-zero-finite-part-is-indefinite|WP-067]] identifies an exact positive Hardy Gram geometry whose base-shell polarization is `Lambda`, but canonical zero-finite-part subtraction is indefinite and reduces any finite scalar repair to boundedness of the anchor functional.
- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-068-full-root-hardy-differences-make-mangoldt-anchor-functional-unbounded|WP-068]] resolves that branch negatively: cumulative full-root shell differences give a `Q_H`-null sequence on which the Mangoldt anchor stays nonzero, so no finite scalar counterterm can restore positivity.

The finite selector and the selected archimedean channel are both present, but these symmetric Hardy/Krein/metric and finite-anchor renormalizations do not combine them with the required Weil pairing. The territory remains open only for a larger or genuinely nonseparable finite--archimedean construction, or a more singular form-level category outside the operator repairs classified above, whose positivity/sign is forced independently rather than imported through polar absolute value or a finite scalar shift.
