# Arithmetic-intersection and Hodge/Prym boundary

The category-changing branch asks whether Prime-Circle shell data can enter a nontrivial cohomological/non-torsion carrier before positivity is formed.

[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-052-prime-circle-resultant-intersection-kernel-is-arakelov-principal|WP-052]]--[[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-055-minimal-cyclotomic-double-cover-hodge-transfer-is-degree-flat|WP-055]] show the `P^1` arithmetic-intersection/correspondence routes are principal or class-trivial, while the minimal higher-genus cover has genuine positive Hodge theory but normalized transfer is degree-flat. [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-056-prym-polarization-stores-log-p-in-integral-discriminant|WP-056]] and [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-057-prym-discriminant-is-torsion-invisible-to-real-quadratic-positivity|WP-057]] then locate `log p` in a Prym integral discriminant but show the finite torsion carrier cannot itself support the required ordered real quadratic pairing.

The newest findings test the strongest canonical non-torsion exports:

- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-058-prym-hodge-determinant-norm-exports-log-p-but-polarization-spectrum-is-flat|WP-058]] exports `log p` to a canonical positive determinant-line/covolume norm, while the real polarization operator itself remains spectrally flat.
- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-059-prym-heisenberg-adjoint-defect-is-cycle-laplacian-logdet|WP-059]] lets the torsion act first on the non-torsion theta/Heisenberg endomorphism space; a phase-free positive defect recovers `log p`, but exactly as the log-determinant of copies of the ordinary `p`-cycle Laplacian.
- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-060-prym-untwisted-analytic-torsion-completion-erases-determinant-line-prime-scale|WP-060]] shows the canonical untwisted de Rham/Dolbeault analytic-torsion completion cancels the determinant-line prime scale and contributes no replacement correction.

Thus a canonical non-torsion export exists, but the direct action classicalizes to cycle-Laplacian logdet and the standard untwisted completion erases it. The remaining source-backed route needs genuinely additional twisting/family/boundary or non-cover data before positivity, together with an independent sign theorem.
