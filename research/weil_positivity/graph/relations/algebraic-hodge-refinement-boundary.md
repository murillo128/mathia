# Arithmetic-intersection and Hodge/Prym boundary

The current category-changing branch tests whether Prime-Circle shell data can be moved into arithmetic intersection or genuine cohomology before positivity is applied.

- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-052-prime-circle-resultant-intersection-kernel-is-arakelov-principal|WP-052]] realizes the cyclotomic resultant as literal finite arithmetic intersection on `P^1_Z`, but canonical degree-zero/Arakelov completion is principal and cancels by the product formula.
- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-053-prime-circle-rotation-correspondences-have-no-primitive-hodge-class|WP-053]] upgrades shells to rotation correspondences on `P^1 x P^1`; prime-power collision survives vertically, but the normalized correspondence has no primitive divisor/Hodge class and the residual is principal.
- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-054-reflection-fixed-two-section-coupling-is-principal|WP-054]] shows the intrinsically selected reflection-fixed pair `{+1,-1}` reproduces the endpoint arithmetic exactly yet is again principal before Hodge positivity.
- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-055-minimal-cyclotomic-double-cover-hodge-transfer-is-degree-flat|WP-055]] grants the minimal hyperelliptic higher-genus lift and obtains genuine positive Hodge theory, but normalized transfer is degree-flat and carries only universal cover calculus.
- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-056-prym-polarization-stores-log-p-in-integral-discriminant|WP-056]] finds a stronger carrier: the Prym polarization kernel stores `log p` intrinsically in its integral discriminant while the real Hodge metric remains degree-flat.
- [[research/weil_positivity/findings/WP-057-prym-discriminant-is-torsion-invisible-to-real-quadratic-positivity|WP-057]] closes the direct positivity extraction from that carrier: the finite kernel is torsion and every compatible real biadditive/quadratic or Neron--Tate pairing kills it; the surviving theta pairing is alternating and root-of-unity-valued.

The source-backed Hodge frontier is therefore not ordinary class-level positivity or a real quadratic form on the Prym discriminant itself. A surviving route would have to make the torsion/integral carrier act on a larger non-torsion space before the positive pairing is formed, or introduce genuinely new non-cover correspondence data with an independent sign theorem.
