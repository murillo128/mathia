## Current source frontier

WP-117 supplies a genuine positive ingredient: the independently selected Riemann Gamma/digamma vertical variation is an exact conditionally negative-definite Lévy/Markov symbol. But WP-116 and WP-118--126 show that direct critical prime coupling, parity or matrix mixing, local jump measures and finite-order local positive renormalizations retain coherent divergence or collapse to finite jets. The surviving target is therefore a nonseparable finite/archimedean, sign-producing global operation rather than another scalar positive completion.
