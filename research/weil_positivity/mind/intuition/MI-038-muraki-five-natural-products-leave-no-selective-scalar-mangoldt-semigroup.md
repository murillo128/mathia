# MI-038 — Muraki's five natural products leave no selective scalar Mangoldt semigroup

**Evidence level:** exact source-specific synthesis from [WP-325](../../findings/WP-325-prime-power-support-blocks-classical-two-divisibility.md), [WP-326](../../findings/WP-326-prime-power-gaps-make-mangoldt-tilts-freely-indecomposable-while-boolean-divisibility-is-universal.md), and [WP-327](../../findings/WP-327-monotone-isolated-atom-obstruction-closes-the-five-natural-product-source-semigroup-escape.md), using classical divisibility/indecomposability theorems and Muraki's classification of natural products.

For every positive tilt, the pole-normalized Mangoldt probability law is supported on log prime powers and has isolated atoms at `log 2`, `log 3`, and beyond. That exact support geometry now classifies the direct scalar additive-convolution-semigroup route across all five natural products.

Classical/tensor convolution already fails two-divisibility. Free additive convolution is blocked by the first atom gap. Boolean convolution always admits roots, but precisely because every probability law is Boolean infinitely divisible, so its positivity carries no arithmetic-selective provenance.

WP-327 closes the two remaining natural products. Hasebe's isolated-atom theorem says a monotone-infinitely-divisible law with an isolated atom can have only an absolutely continuous remainder separated from that atom. The further atom at `log 3` contradicts this immediately. Anti-monotone self-powers coincide with monotone self-powers because order reversal disappears when the same law is convolved repeatedly, so anti-monotone infinite divisibility fails as well.

Thus changing only the natural notion of additive independence has no selective surviving category: four products reject the exact source, while Boolean accepts it universally. **The direct source-preserving scalar additive-semigroup architecture is exhausted inside Muraki's five natural products.**

This is not a no-go for noncommutative probability in general. Operator-valued, conditionally free, bi-free, deformed, multiplicative, non-semigroup, indefinite-to-positive, or direct finite--archimedean constructions lie outside the classification. A surviving route must introduce additional canonical structure whose sign theorem is both nonautomatic and compatible with the exact arithmetic source; merely changing the scalar convolution law is no longer a live mechanism.
