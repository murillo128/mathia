# MI-019 — Source-visible curvature must survive the readout, not merely be reachable or non-scalar

**Evidence level:** exact linear/projective classification from WP-295--WP-301, sharpened by the canonical-source and scalar-projective obstructions WP-408--WP-409. These close the direct frustration-free non-scalar zero-mode escape, bare native Bost--Connes holonomy, and the base-independent scalar scale-twist repair of the critical divisibility Gram; they do not rule out base-dependent groupoid twists, noncentral/operator-valued cocycles, non-frustration-free finite--archimedean couplings or other readouts that retain mixed-prime curvature, and they do not prove Weil positivity.

WP-295--WP-298 show that exact finite Weil localization is rigid under fixed positive channels, multiplicative-equivariant linear depth, flat transport and faithful site metrics. WP-299 then proves that making only the **output** connection non-flat is insufficient: an exactly equivariant linear response of the ordinary commuting multiplicative source lives in the common holonomy-fixed sector.

WP-300 changes the upstream source itself and asks for a prime-symmetric scalar projective action. Full finite relabeling symmetry leaves only flat holonomy or the universal fermionic/Koszul sign. The nonflat branch is genuine source-visible curvature and positively selects prime-power support, but generalized-prime free monoids reproduce it exactly and the global Weil sign remains absent.

WP-301 shows that replacing the scalar sign by arbitrary matrix holonomies does not help while the positivity theorem is a frustration-free sum of local squares and the readout is the zero-energy projection. For

`H_S=sum_j c_j B_j^* B_j`, `c_j>0`,

one has, without any commutativity assumption,

`ker H_S = intersection_j ker B_j`.

For plaquette holonomies this is the intersection of their fixed spaces. Exact rejection of every mixed-prime support is therefore already equivalent to the two-prime condition `Fix(K_pq)={0}` for every pair. Higher noncommutative coherence, matrix size and excited-state spectrum disappear from the Boolean support selector; positive reweighting cannot change the kernel. The same common-fixed-space rule transports to generalized-prime controls.

The archimedean consequence is equally restrictive. If a positive archimedean operator `Q_infty` is another frustration-free summand and `P` is the zero-mode projector, then

`P Q_infty P=0` and `P f(Q_infty) P=f(0)P`.

Thus the same harmonic readout that extracts the finite support cannot retain nonconstant Gamma/polar information stored in the positive archimedean spectrum. Varying only positive scalar weights leaves the kernel fixed.

WP-408--WP-409 expose the same distinction in the Bost--Connes projective route. WP-408 shows that the native critical source does not provide useful scalar isotropy holonomy. Even after a scalar projective extension is granted externally, WP-409 shows that a base-independent multiplier on the scale group leaves the divisibility range projections and the full critical GCD/Poisson Gram unchanged; every one-prime restriction is gauge-flat, and the only genuine scalar curvature lives on mixed-prime pairs that this Gram never reads. Curvature can therefore be mathematically real and still be **destination-invisible**. Under full prime relabeling symmetry the remaining pair phase collapses back to the universal flat/fermionic alternatives of WP-300.

The surviving requirement is therefore stronger than “introduce non-scalar curvature before scalarization.” A useful mechanism must make arithmetic information survive the **readout**: for example through canonically moving kernels, a base-dependent or noncentral twisted carrier whose positive observable sees the mixed-prime curvature, a boundary/cohomological observable that retains nonzero spectrum, or a genuinely non-frustration-free finite--archimedean coupling whose sign has an independent theorem. Rich internal geometry is irrelevant if the final projection, Gram or scalarization keeps only a universal quotient.

**Boundary.** WP-301 classifies the zero-mode selector of positive frustration-free sums, WP-408 classifies the relevant native Bost--Connes isotropy support, and WP-409 classifies base-independent scalar scale multipliers only as seen by the canonical divisibility/KMS carrier. They do not exclude arbitrary groupoid twists that alter source/range geometry, operator-valued cocycles, noncommuting global Hamiltonians with cancellation between local terms, parameter-dependent operators whose kernels genuinely move, or readouts that use excited spectral/boundary information. Exact positive Mangoldt extraction remains distinct from global Weil positivity.
