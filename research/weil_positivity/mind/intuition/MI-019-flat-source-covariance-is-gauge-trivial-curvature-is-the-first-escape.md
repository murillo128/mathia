# MI-019 — Source-visible curvature must be arithmetic, not merely reachable or fermionic

**Evidence level:** exact linear classification from WP-295--WP-299, sharpened by [WP-300](../../findings/WP-300-prime-symmetric-projective-source-curvature-is-fermionic-and-boolean.md). This closes the prime-symmetric scalar projective source escape; it does not construct a surviving non-scalar arithmetic geometry or prove Weil positivity.

WP-295--WP-298 show that exact finite Weil localization is rigid under fixed positive channels, multiplicative-equivariant linear depth, flat transport and faithful site metrics. WP-299 then proves that making only the **output** connection non-flat is insufficient: an exactly equivariant linear response of the ordinary commuting multiplicative source lives in the common holonomy-fixed sector.

WP-300 changes the upstream source itself and asks for a prime-symmetric scalar projective action. Full finite relabeling symmetry leaves only two commutator laws on distinct prime generators:

`beta(p,q)=+1` or `beta(p,q)=-1`.

The nonflat `-1` branch is not a new arithmetic phase. It is exactly the universal fermionic/Koszul sign already realized by the Clifford generators. Its positive plaquette-defect operator has zero space precisely when the occupied support has size at most one, and multiplying that projection by the Clifford radius recovers `Lambda(n)` on prime powers.

This is a genuine source-visible curvature mechanism, but it still fails the arithmetic-specificity test. The identical construction works for arbitrary generalized-prime free monoids; it sees Boolean support cardinality, not the geometry of rational primes. Adding one always-present archimedean Clifford direction on equal footing kills the selector, while keeping the infinite place separate supplies no common positivity theorem. After coefficient extraction, the global Weil autocorrelation lift remains the same indefinite operator.

The surviving requirement is therefore stronger than “introduce curvature before scalarization.” A useful mechanism needs **source-visible curvature whose holonomy is forced by arithmetic rather than universal grading**, together with a nonseparable finite--archimedean completion and an independent reason for the final Weil sign. This points beyond prime-symmetric scalar projective laws toward genuinely non-scalar/cohomological source structure or another category change.

Source-forced moving degeneracy remains a separate possible escape, but its kernel must be independently derived rather than fitted to the Mangoldt zero set.

**Boundary.** WP-300 classifies scalar commutator holonomy only under full prime-relabeling symmetry. Pair-dependent phases become possible if that symmetry is relaxed, but without a source theorem forcing them they are programmable data. Exact positive local coefficient extraction is not global Weil positivity.
