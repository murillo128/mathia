# MI-008 — Critical half-density exposes the archimedean phase only before Gram scalarization

**Evidence level:** exact through WP-169

## Core intuition

The forced critical half-density is structurally meaningful, but positivity obtained by taking a scalar Gram is too late: it keeps only a modulus square. In the pointed-shell scaling limit that modulus square is exactly classical Nyman--Müntz geometry. The discarded relative phase contains the exact real-place Gamma/scattering factor, yet that phase is not selected by Gram positivity itself.

## Strongest justified principle

WP-168 proves that critically normalized pointed shells converge to a stationary positive kernel with spectral density `|zeta(1/2+it)|^2/(1/4+t^2)`. The kernel is an unconditional autocorrelation and is exactly the classical fractional-part/Nyman Mellin Gram; it is blind to off-critical inner factors and supplies neither the explicit-formula spectral type nor the completed Gamma/polar terms.

WP-169 compares the Mathia scaling factor with the canonical Nyman factor and obtains the unitary relative phase `R_infty(t)=pi^(it) Gamma(1/4-it/2)/Gamma(1/4+it/2)`. Its logarithmic phase derivative is exactly the nonconstant archimedean Weil digamma symbol. But arbitrary phase gauges preserve the same scalar Gram, the two-channel Gram is rank-one positive for tautological reasons, and the same phase bridge arises from all degrees before prime-power support is used.

## What remains possible

A source-native boundary/operator construction may canonically select an equivalent real-place phase before scalarization and couple it to the signed finite-prime selector. Such a construction needs an independent sign theorem and must distinguish prime from all-degree controls. Merely recognizing the Gamma factor after choosing the Nyman comparator is not enough.

## Status / novelty

Nyman--Beurling/Müntz analysis and the functional-equation scattering factor are classical. The durable synthesis is the operation-order boundary: **the critical geometry reaches the correct real-place phase, but Gram positivity erases exactly the phase choice that would have to be coupled to finite arithmetic**.

## Falsification criterion

Show that the WP-168 scalar Gram intrinsically determines `R_infty`, that its positivity fails under an off-critical zero, or derive the finite Mangoldt and polar terms from the same all-degree Gram without adding source-specific structure.
