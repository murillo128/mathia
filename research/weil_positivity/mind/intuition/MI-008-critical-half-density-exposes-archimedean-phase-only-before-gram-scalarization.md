# MI-008 — Critical half-density exposes the archimedean phase before Gram scalarization, but scalar passivity cannot supply its sign

**Evidence level:** exact through WP-170

## Core intuition

The forced critical half-density is structurally meaningful, but positivity obtained by taking a scalar Gram is too late: it keeps only a modulus square. The discarded relative phase contains the exact real-place Gamma/scattering factor, yet that phase is neither selected by the Gram nor itself an ordinary passive scalar boundary response. Recovering the real-place contribution therefore requires a source-native coupling and a positivity mechanism in a genuinely different category.

## Strongest justified principle

WP-168 proves that critically normalized pointed shells converge to a stationary positive kernel with spectral density `|zeta(1/2+it)|^2/(1/4+t^2)`. This is classical Nyman--Müntz modulus-square geometry and is blind to the phase needed for the completed Weil term.

WP-169 compares the Mathia scaling factor with the canonical Nyman factor and obtains the exact unitary relative phase

`R_infty(t)=pi^(it) Gamma(1/4-it/2)/Gamma(1/4+it/2)`.

Its logarithmic phase derivative is exactly the nonconstant archimedean Weil digamma symbol. But arbitrary phase gauges preserve the same scalar Gram, and the same phase bridge already appears in all-degree controls before prime-power support is used.

WP-170 closes the direct scalar-passive repair. Although `|R_infty(t)|=1` on the real axis, its half-plane zeros violate the Blaschke condition, so it is not a Schur/inner characteristic function in either causal orientation. Its boundary phase velocity also changes sign. Thus the exact Gamma phase is a signed response, not the positive delay/Clark density of an ordinary scalar Herglotz/Schur system.

## What remains possible

A source-native construction may couple finite-prime and real-place data before scalarization in a matrix/operator-valued, graded, compressed, domain-changing, indefinite-to-positive, or other nonseparable category. Such a route must prove a new coercivity theorem and explain why the same geometry selects both the finite arithmetic and the archimedean compensation. Merely embedding the raw Gamma phase into a passive scalar boundary model is closed.

## Status / novelty

Nyman--Beurling/Müntz analysis, Gamma scattering, Blaschke theory, and Herglotz/Schur passivity are classical. The durable synthesis is the operation-order boundary: **the critical geometry reaches the correct real-place phase, but scalar Gram positivity erases its selection and scalar passivity cannot recover its signed Weil contribution**.

## Falsification criterion

Show that the WP-168 scalar Gram intrinsically determines `R_infty`, construct an ordinary scalar Schur/inner realization of the exact `R_infty` despite its persisted zero geometry, or derive the finite Mangoldt/polar contribution from the same scalar positive channel without new source-specific structure.
