# MI-042 — Faithful exact-law UCP readouts are rigid, but the rigidity has a vanishing faithfulness modulus

**Evidence level:** exact/classical-mechanism synthesis from [WP-329](../../findings/WP-329-deterministic-amalgamated-lifts-make-operator-valued-free-divisibility-universal-before-scalarization.md) through [WP-333](../../findings/WP-333-approximate-faithful-ucp-rigidity-is-nonuniform-at-mangoldt-cutoffs.md). Kadison--Schwarz, Stinespring dilation, Choi's multiplicative-domain theorem and standard continuity theory for completely positive maps are classical; the Mathia content is the exact finite-cutoff obstruction and its cutoff-uniform stability boundary in the Mangoldt readout architecture.

The operator-valued route has a precise readout trichotomy. WP-329 shows that a deterministic amalgamation-algebra coordinate can be operator-valued freely divisible for every scalar source while all arithmetic specificity sits only in the external scalar state. WP-330 shows that genuine centered covariance can be added without improving selectivity when scalarization annihilates the fluctuation ideal. WP-331 gives the opposite failure for conditional expectations: if the scalar readout is faithful and the exact scalar law of the same source observable is preserved, positive conditional variance must vanish.

WP-332 removes conditional expectation as the load-bearing hypothesis. Let `Phi:A->B` be unital completely positive, let `tau` be a faithful state on `B`, let `phi=tau o Phi`, and let `X=X*` with `b=Phi(X)`. If `X` under `phi` and `b` under `tau` have the same scalar law, equality of only their second moments gives

`tau(Phi(X^2)-Phi(X)^2)=0`.

Kadison--Schwarz makes the defect positive, and faithfulness forces it to vanish. Choi's equality criterion then puts `X` in the multiplicative domain of `Phi`, so

`Phi(XZ)=b Phi(Z)` and `Phi(ZX)=Phi(Z)b`

for every `Z`, and `Phi(f(X))=f(b)` on the full continuous functional calculus generated by `X`.

Stinespring geometry exposes exactly what disappears. If `Phi(a)=V* pi(a) V` and `P=VV*`, then the Kadison defect factors as

`((I-P) pi(X) V)* ((I-P) pi(X) V)`.

Exact faithful second-moment preservation therefore forces `(I-P)pi(X)P=0`: the compressed space reduces the source observable. The off-diagonal dilation block that could have carried a hidden boundary coupling of that same observable is identically zero.

WP-333 shows that the exact theorem is **not uniformly stable** merely because every finite cutoff state is faithful. On a finite output algebra `B_R=C(Sigma_R)` with positive weights and least atom `m_R`, any positive Kadison defect satisfies the exact modulus

`||D_R|| <= tau_R(D_R)/m_R`,

and the squared Stinespring coupling has the same norm. Approximate scalar second-moment preservation therefore forces approximate multiplicative-domain rigidity only when `tau_R(D_R)=o(m_R)`. Absolute scalar error `o(1)` is insufficient if `m_R->0`.

For the pole-normalized finite Mangoldt source, `m_R` tends to zero at least exponentially along the prime-power ray `2^k`. WP-333 gives an explicit sharp construction: split a least-mass atom symmetrically through a UCP channel. The induced scalar law changes by exactly `m_R` in total variation, every fixed polynomial moment converges to the same limit, yet `||D_R||` and the off-diagonal Stinespring coupling stay order one. The construction is non-arithmetic and works for any expanding finite probability laws whose minimum mass vanishes.

The correct readout principle is therefore two-scale. **Exact faithful same-observable recovery is rigid at each fixed cutoff; asymptotic scalar-law recovery is rigid only relative to the smallest visible mass (or under a stronger map-level topology).** A sequence of faithful states can become arbitrarily ill-conditioned for turning scalar expectation control into operator-norm control.

This prevents two opposite mistakes. A positive route cannot claim arithmetic leverage merely because order-one coupling hides in a vanishing-mass tail: matched controls can do the same. A negative route cannot extrapolate WP-332 to the infinite source from total-variation or fixed-moment convergence alone: it must prove a uniform estimate such as `tau_R(D_R)=o(m_R)`, control the CP maps in a stronger norm/topology, or otherwise exclude moving-tail concentration.

A surviving operator-valued route may therefore change the interface before rigidity applies, or deliberately use a moving source region—but then the location and scale of the coupling must be **source-forced**, must survive the finite-plus-archimedean destination, and must acquire a sign theorem that fails on matched controls. Changed observables, transfer functions, boundary Weyl functions, multivariable couplings and signed/nonlinear responses remain logically open only after this uniformity audit.

**Boundary.** The exact theorem kills only the off-diagonal Stinespring coupling of the same self-adjoint observable whose compressed image preserves the exact scalar second moment under a faithful output state. The quantitative modulus in WP-333 is exact for finite atomic output states and shows how this implication degenerates as the least mass vanishes. It does not trivialize the whole dilation algebra, rule out couplings through other operators, establish Weil positivity, or turn the moving-tail loophole into a source-selective mechanism.
