# MI-042 — Faithful exact-law UCP readouts put the source observable in the multiplicative domain

**Evidence level:** exact/classical-mechanism synthesis from [WP-329](../../findings/WP-329-deterministic-amalgamated-lifts-make-operator-valued-free-divisibility-universal-before-scalarization.md) through [WP-332](../../findings/WP-332-faithful-exact-law-ucp-readouts-force-multiplicative-domain-rigidity.md). Kadison--Schwarz, Stinespring dilation, and Choi's multiplicative-domain theorem are classical; the Mathia content is the obstruction they impose on the exact Mangoldt readout architecture.

The operator-valued route has a precise readout trichotomy. WP-329 shows that a deterministic amalgamation-algebra coordinate can be operator-valued freely divisible for every scalar source while all arithmetic specificity sits only in the external scalar state. WP-330 shows that genuine centered covariance can be added without improving selectivity when scalarization annihilates the fluctuation ideal. WP-331 gives the opposite failure for conditional expectations: if the scalar readout is faithful and the exact scalar law of the same source observable is preserved, positive conditional variance must vanish.

WP-332 removes conditional expectation as the load-bearing hypothesis. Let `Phi:A->B` be unital completely positive, let `tau` be a faithful state on `B`, let `phi=tau o Phi`, and let `X=X*` with `b=Phi(X)`. If `X` under `phi` and `b` under `tau` have the same scalar law, equality of only their second moments gives

`tau(Phi(X^2)-Phi(X)^2)=0`.

Kadison--Schwarz makes the defect positive, and faithfulness forces it to vanish. Choi's equality criterion then puts `X` in the multiplicative domain of `Phi`, so

`Phi(XZ)=b Phi(Z)` and `Phi(ZX)=Phi(Z)b`

for every `Z`, and `Phi(f(X))=f(b)` on the full continuous functional calculus generated by `X`.

Stinespring geometry exposes exactly what disappears. If `Phi(a)=V* pi(a) V` and `P=VV*`, then the Kadison defect factors as

`((I-P) pi(X) V)* ((I-P) pi(X) V)`.

Exact faithful second-moment preservation therefore forces `(I-P)pi(X)P=0`: the compressed space reduces the source observable. The off-diagonal dilation block that could have carried a hidden boundary coupling of that same observable is identically zero.

At every finite Mangoldt cutoff the canonical scalar state has strictly positive mass on every support point and is faithful. Thus replacing a conditional expectation by an arbitrary UCP compression does not rescue the same-observable exact-law architecture. The obstruction is also non-arithmetic: any strictly positive matched-control weights on the same finite support force the same multiplicative-domain collapse.

The reusable principle is that **positive enrichment and faithful exact-law recovery are not independent resources**. For a self-adjoint source coordinate read through a UCP map, preserving its exact scalar second moment under a faithful output state already removes the positive Schwarz defect that measures coupling to the dilation complement. More ambient Hilbert space or a more general positive compression does not create source-visible geometry if the readout is required to remain noiseless on the same observable.

A surviving operator-valued route must therefore change the interface before this rigidity applies: use a different source-derived observable or response (resolvent, transfer function, boundary Weyl function, derivative, joint observable), allow the scalar law to change in a controlled source-specific way, exploit a nonfaithful/null sector while proving that the final destination still sees it, or leave the UCP/positive-compression category through a signed or nonlinear response. Couplings through other operators are not ruled out, but they must be source-forced, survive matched controls, and generate the finite plus archimedean/global Weil structure before positivity is read off.

**Boundary.** The theorem kills only the off-diagonal Stinespring coupling of the same self-adjoint observable whose compressed image is used to preserve the exact scalar law. It does not trivialize the whole dilation algebra, rule out multivariable couplings through other operators, or establish Weil positivity. Nonfaithful states, changed observables, changed scalar laws, signed pairings, nonlinear Schur/Feshbach responses, and genuinely joint finite--archimedean constructions remain outside the obstruction and require their own sign and selectivity theorems.
