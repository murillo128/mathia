# MI-034 — All-gap weak Julia coherence is still atomwise-smoothing universal

**Evidence level:** exact all-gap matched-control obstruction from [WP-321](../../findings/WP-321-atomwise-smoothing-matches-every-gap-law-and-the-full-bounded-gap-julia-family.md), strengthening the sparse block-grafting result WP-320.

WP-320 left one obvious scalar escape: perhaps common-source coherence becomes arithmetic-selective once sparse thinning is forbidden and the construction must reproduce the full consecutive-gap family. WP-321 closes that escape in the natural bounded-Lipschitz/Julia topology.

Write the reflected Mangoldt Herglotz source as an atomic positive measure on `-log q_k`. Smooth every atom once inside a sufficiently tiny interval, choosing the radii diagonally so that the derivative-biased cores relevant to later gap midpoints are approximated arbitrarily accurately. Because each fixed atom belongs to only finitely many stringent cores, every smoothing radius can remain positive.

The resulting **single positive non-atomic, locally absolutely continuous measure** leaves every original consecutive-gap midpoint inside a support-free neighborhood and, for the same node and gap scale, satisfies

`Dhat_j/D_j -> 1`,

`d_BL(rhohat_j,rho_j) -> 0`

for the entire sequence of consecutive prime-power support gaps. No thinning is used.

On the fixed-additive-gap family isolated in WP-319, the arithmetic laws are uniformly tight. Bounded-Lipschitz convergence then upgrades to local uniform convergence of the reciprocal Cauchy/Boolean self-energy kernels and hence of the normalized scalar Julia profiles. The full unthinned bounded-gap scalar family is therefore asymptotically reproduced by one atomless positive source.

The surviving scalar discriminator must consequently live outside this weak continuity class. It must detect exact atomic/support arithmetic or another relation discontinuous under atomwise smoothing, or it must couple the finite source to genuinely global/archimedean information before scalar Julia reduction. Merely requiring one source, every gap, tightness, derivative normalization and local-uniform Julia coherence is not enough.

**Boundary.** WP-321 does not show that every exact cross-gap identity survives smoothing, and it does not construct or match the global Weil form. It closes scalar invariants continuous in the bounded-Lipschitz/Julia topology. Exact support arithmetic, discontinuous invariants, matrix/operator-valued structure and finite--archimedean coupling remain outside the theorem.
