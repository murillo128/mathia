# MI-006 — Exact finite--archimedean ray matching is still too late unless mixed-prime sign is formed first

**Evidence level:** exact singular-readout, energy-bimodule, canonical-whitening, and adjacent-Gamma/Bost--Connes bridge boundaries through WP-233

## Core intuition

The finite and archimedean pieces can agree far more strongly than a heuristic coefficient match and still fail to produce Weil positivity. The decisive issue is the **operation that combines places before positivity or scalarization**. Post-processing an already hollow/indefinite finite defect, or positively completing each matched ray independently, cannot create the missing global orientation.

## Strongest justified principle

WP-226--WP-230 show that scalar positive/singular readouts either erase repeated prime powers, diverge, or preserve the wrong inertia under faithful metric congruence. WP-231 enlarges this to every bounded map that remains a bimodule over the source energy algebra: zero diagonal corners persist, so a nonzero self-adjoint processed defect is indefinite.

WP-232 tests the source Gram's own noncommuting innovation geometry. Exact Poisson whitening is canonical but transforms the Weil ray into a shifted half-line Jacobi operator whose spectrum crosses zero. Other exact whiteners differ by an isometric range choice; positivity from a special non-shift-covariant range is therefore selectable unless the source forces it.

WP-233 supplies the strongest bridge so far. An adjacent Riemann-Gamma difference has positive jump density `e^{-|x|/2}`, and sampling at energies `k log p` gives exactly `p^{-k/2}`. Yet the induced positive jump form equals the finite Weil ray plus precisely its missing positive diagonal, and those diagonal repairs diverge over primes. The one-dimensional archimedean metric also agrees with the Bost--Connes Gram only on divisibility chains, not incomparable mixed-prime directions.

## Program consequence

Construct the finite--archimedean incidence/relation before taking independent ray energies, traces, positive completions or faithful congruences. The candidate must explain mixed-prime coupling, repeated prime powers, the real-place term and polar normalization in one source-forced form whose sign theorem is independent of RH.

## Counterevidence / boundary

WP-231 does not cover genuinely noncommuting changes of representation/domain; WP-232 leaves source-forced non-shift-covariant correspondences open; WP-233 does not rule out a global interaction whose cross terms cancel the divergent diagonal and restore the correct archimedean geometry. Those exits remain hypotheses, not evidence.

## Epistemic status

**Exact finite--archimedean ray compatibility together with strong no-go results for forming positivity after local reduction; no global Weil-positive form is established.**

## Falsification criterion

Produce a nonzero positive image of the formed defect inside the closed bimodule/whitening classes without source-selected range compression, or make the independent Gamma jump completion globally finite and exactly Weil-oriented without new cross-place structure. A positive continuation should derive that cross-place relation before positivity.
