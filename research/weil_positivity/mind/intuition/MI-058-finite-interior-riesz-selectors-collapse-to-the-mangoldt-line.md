# MI-058 — Exact mixed-shell nullity collapses every compact-open-continuous interior response to the Mangoldt line

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from WP-381--[WP-383](../../findings/WP-383-compact-open-continuous-interior-responses-with-exact-mixed-shell-nullity-collapse-to-the-mangoldt-line.md), interpreted against the pointed-bulk controls of WP-378--WP-380.

WP-381 establishes an important positive control: the pointed local-Dirichlet geometry admits a bounded interior Riesz coordinate that recovers `Lambda(n)` exactly. The obstruction is therefore not the mere existence of a bounded Mangoldt selector.

WP-382 showed that every fixed finite packet of interior evaluations and jets satisfying exact mixed-shell nullity maps the entire cyclotomic shell family into one Mangoldt line. WP-383 proves that finite dimensionality was not the essential assumption. Let `T:H(D)->K` be any Banach-valued linear response continuous for uniform convergence on compact subsets. If `T` annihilates every mixed-prime shell, then `T(B_m)=Lambda(m)v` for one fixed vector `v`.

The mechanism is exact and source-native. Adjoining a fresh prime gives a dilation `z -> z^q`; on every fixed compact subdisk that spectator term decays exponentially. Compact-open continuity forces the entire response packet, even an infinite-dimensional one, to ignore it in the limit. Exact mixed-shell nullity then determines the primitive-shell response. In a Hilbert target the resulting Gram is again rank one.

The relevant escape boundary is consequently topological, not dimensional. A genuinely new infinite sector must accumulate at the unit circle, vary its observation scale with the arithmetic source, or otherwise fail compact-open continuity; alternatively, mixed shells must remain active until after a later finite--archimedean coupling. Merely adding infinitely many coordinates controlled at one fixed interior radius does not create new source-response dimension.

**Boundary.** WP-383 does not rule out boundary distributions, scale-varying probes, unbounded/closable responses in another topology, or constructions that delay exact selection. Those possibilities remain open only if their singularity or scale dependence is source-derived and their positivity/coupling is established independently.
