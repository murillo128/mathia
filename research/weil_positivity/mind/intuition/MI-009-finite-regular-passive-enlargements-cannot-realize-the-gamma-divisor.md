# MI-009 — The Gamma phase cannot survive finite regular positive completion without changing projective and orientation category

**Evidence level:** exact/literature-backed passive, analytic-category, and Hardy-compression controls through WP-187

## Core intuition

The passive Gamma boundary is no longer just a scalar rigidity/flexibility problem. WP-182 shows that vector completion can preserve a finite positive form after scalar dissipation, but WP-183--WP-185 show that the **exact Gamma relative phase itself cannot be carried by finite regular bounded/projective analytic completion**. WP-186 then shows that pointwise positive-semidefinite encoding retains the phase only as an unoriented metric object.

WP-187 closes the most canonical nonlocal repair of that orientation loss. Hardy compression produces two genuine PSD Hankel leakage defects for the Gamma phase, but the reversal-odd quantity is their signed difference. Smooth finite-window Gamma phases make that Toeplitz self-commutator nonzero, trace zero, and therefore indefinite, while the exact untruncated phase lies outside `H^{1/2}` so at least one canonical leakage trace diverges.

A viable completion must therefore change the analytic/projective category enough to carry the exact source phase **and** preserve its orientation in the coercive global object. Positivity of separate universal leakage energies is not enough; the finite-prime, polar, and archimedean sectors must be coupled before the final operation that would otherwise subtract or scalarize away the sign.

## Strongest justified principle

WP-179--WP-181 classify scalar passive routes. Ordinary determinant and `det_2` inherit one-sided phase order, while higher regularization or dissipative Schur responses can change sign only after losing the corresponding scalar coercivity. WP-182 gives the canonical two-channel escape: a Pythagorean mate absorbs the positive boundary defect and leaves a finite positive completed kernel, but the ordered quantity is the weighted phase balance of both channels and the construction is universal.

WP-183 proves that exact Gamma relative phase is not preserved by any nonzero bounded analytic linear mixing of the natural channels. WP-184 identifies the deeper reason: the Gamma ratio lies outside bounded characteristic, so bounded-type and scalar Möbius repairs cannot realize it nondegenerately. WP-185 converts that into a finite-projective no-go: no finite packet of bounded-characteristic channels with a finite rational decoder can have the Gamma phase as a projective coordinate, and a common scalar normalizer cannot change the obstructing ratio.

WP-186 tests the direct pointwise positive lift. The rank-one projector onto `(1,e^{i theta})` is PSD for every unimodular phase, so positivity itself is universal; differentiation recovers `theta'` only in an indefinite traceless direction, while positive metric quantities retain its magnitude or square.

WP-187 then tests the canonical nonlocal Hardy polarization. For every unimodular symbol, `D_in=H_phi^*H_phi` and `D_out=H_barphi^*H_barphi` are positive, but orientation is `[T_phi^*,T_phi]=D_out-D_in`. An inner function is the positive matched control because one leakage channel vanishes. The Gamma phase lacks that one-sided analytic regime: every ordinary smooth finite-window closure has zero winding and a nonzero indefinite self-commutator, while the exact Cayley boundary is critically rough and does not admit two finite canonical Hankel trace energies.

## Counterevidence / boundary

These results do not rule out a source-forced singular relative index, genuinely infinite or domain-changing completion, matrix/noncommutative Hardy-type coupling, or a construction in which **one** positive Hankel defect is identified with the completed Weil form by an independent finite--archimedean theorem. They also do not prove that every renormalized self-commutator is indefinite.

Those categories remain live only if the polarization/renormalization is canonical from Mathia source structure, finite-prime and polar incidence enter the same object, and an independent coercivity theorem supplies the sign. A boundary-only positive identity or a hand-chosen winding/regularization is not enough.

## Epistemic status

**Proved finite regular projective boundary, pointwise orientation-loss control, and canonical Hardy-compression narrowing; open source-forced singular/global category exit.**

## Falsification criterion

Construct a finite bounded-characteristic packet and finite rational/projective decoder realizing the exact Gamma phase despite WP-183--WP-185; derive signed phase velocity from a universal pointwise PSD lift using only positive metric data despite WP-186; or produce an ordinary smooth finite-window Gamma symbol whose Toeplitz self-commutator is nonzero and semidefinite despite WP-187's zero-winding trace argument. A genuinely source-forced global Hardy/singular construction carrying both arithmetic incidence and coercive orientation would instead be the intended escape.
