# MI-009 — The Gamma phase cannot survive finite regular positive completion without changing projective category

**Evidence level:** exact/literature-backed passive and analytic-category controls through WP-186

## Core intuition

The passive Gamma boundary is no longer just a scalar rigidity/flexibility problem. WP-182 shows that vector completion can preserve a finite positive form after scalar dissipation, but WP-183--WP-185 show that the **exact Gamma relative phase itself cannot be carried by finite regular bounded/projective analytic completion**. WP-186 adds a separate sign warning: even when a positive-semidefinite boundary lift encodes the phase pointwise, positivity sees only an unoriented metric while the signed Weil density lives in phase velocity.

A viable completion must therefore do two things simultaneously: change the analytic/projective category enough to carry the exact source phase, and preserve its signed orientation in the coercive object actually used by the theorem.

## Strongest justified principle

WP-179--WP-181 classify scalar passive routes. Ordinary determinant and `det_2` inherit one-sided phase order, while higher regularization or dissipative Schur responses can change sign only after losing the corresponding scalar coercivity. WP-182 gives the canonical two-channel escape: a Pythagorean mate absorbs the positive boundary defect and leaves a finite positive completed kernel, but the ordered quantity is the weighted phase balance of both channels and the construction is universal.

WP-183 proves that exact Gamma relative phase is not preserved by any nonzero bounded analytic linear mixing of the natural channels. WP-184 identifies the deeper reason: the Gamma ratio lies outside bounded characteristic, so bounded-type and scalar Möbius repairs cannot realize it nondegenerately. WP-185 converts that into a finite-projective no-go: no finite packet of bounded-characteristic channels with a finite rational decoder can have the Gamma phase as a projective coordinate, and a common scalar normalizer cannot change the obstructing ratio.

WP-186 then tests the most direct positive geometric encoding. The rank-one projector onto `(1,e^{i theta})` is positive for every unimodular phase and therefore carries no arithmetic specificity by itself. Differentiation recovers `theta'` only in an indefinite traceless direction; positive quadratic/metric quantities retain `|theta'|` or its square and forget the sign needed by the Weil density.

## Counterevidence / boundary

The finite bounded-characteristic/rational-decoder no-go does not classify genuinely infinite, singular, distributional, domain-changing, noncommutative, or source-derived matrix completions. Nor does WP-186 rule out nonlocal oriented positivity, a connection or holonomy with a sign theorem, or an indefinite intermediate form that becomes coercive only after a global source operation.

Those categories remain live only if the replacement order and source forcing are stated explicitly; extra representational freedom is not itself evidence.

## Epistemic status

**Proved finite regular category boundary and orientation-loss control; open source-forced category exit.**

## Falsification criterion

Construct a finite bounded-characteristic channel packet and finite rational/projective decoder that realizes the exact Gamma relative phase despite WP-183--WP-185, or derive the signed phase velocity linearly from a universal pointwise PSD projector using only positive metric data despite WP-186. A genuinely source-forced category change carrying both phase and sign would instead be the intended escape.
