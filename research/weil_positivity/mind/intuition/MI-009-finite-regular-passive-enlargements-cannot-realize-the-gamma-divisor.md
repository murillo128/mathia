# MI-009 — Finite or regular passive enlargements cannot realize the exact Gamma divisor

**Evidence level:** exact/literature-backed no-go chain through accepted WP-171 and WP-173

## Core intuition

The obstruction to treating the archimedean Gamma phase as a passive response is not merely scalarity. Finite channel mixing, finite Pontryagin defect, and ordinary indefinite external ports followed by regular passive Hilbert termination all preserve too much of the Schur/generalized-Schur analytic structure. The exact Gamma factor carries an infinite divisor that those finite or regular repairs cannot absorb.

## Strongest justified principle

WP-171 shows that at a regular lossless boundary point a matrix Schur transfer has a positive-semidefinite delay matrix, so every positive channel readout has one sign. A global matrix-inner realization therefore cannot reproduce the globally sign-changing Gamma velocity by positive scalarization.

WP-172 then tests the first genuinely indefinite Hilbert-channel extension. Krein--Langer factorization allows only a finite Blaschke/Blaschke--Potapov denominator at finite negative index. The analytic Gamma orientation has infinitely many upper-half-plane zeros violating Blaschke, while the inverse has infinitely many poles. No finite defect or finite matrix coefficient/determinant can repair that divisor.

WP-173 goes beyond finite channel count: a regular `J`-contractive block with an ordinary Hilbert-Schur terminating load maps the contractive graph domain back into the Schur class even in infinite dimension. Indefinite ports therefore do not help if the final closure is still regular passive Hilbert feedback.

## What remains possible

Infinite negative index, singular/relation-valued or domain-changing closure, indefinite/nonpassive final loads with an independent positivity theorem, and nonseparable finite--archimedean constructions remain outside the chain. Their burden is stronger: escaping Schur structure is not enough; the source must explain both the required infinite/singular structure and the final coercive sign.

## Status / novelty

Matrix Schur kernels, Krein--Langer factorization, Pontryagin-space realization, and `J`-contractive linear-fractional theory are classical. The durable synthesis is the category boundary: **the exact Gamma phase cannot be obtained by adding only finitely many indefinite defects or by wrapping indefinite ports around an ultimately regular passive Hilbert response**.

## Falsification criterion

Construct a regular matrix-Schur positive readout reproducing the forbidden Gamma sign region, realize either Gamma orientation in a finite-index generalized-Schur class despite its divisor, or give a regular `J`-contractive/passive-Hilbert LFT whose closed response is not Schur under WP-173's hypotheses.
