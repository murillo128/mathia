# MI-037 — Prime-power gaps split free and Boolean source divisibility in opposite useless directions

**Evidence level:** supported by the literature-backed exact application in [WP-326](../../findings/WP-326-prime-power-gaps-make-mangoldt-tilts-freely-indecomposable-while-boolean-divisibility-is-universal.md); the free gap theorems and universal Boolean divisibility are classical prior art.

For every positive Laplace tilt of the pole-normalized Mangoldt source, the probability law on log prime powers has positive atoms at `log 2` and `log 3` with an empty interval between them. Bercovici--Wang's atom-gap theorem therefore makes the law freely indecomposable under additive free convolution. Exponentiating to the native positive prime-power coordinate gives the same obstruction under multiplicative free convolution because the support has atoms at `2` and `3` and no mass in `(2,3)`.

Thus the exact scalar arithmetic source cannot be the nontrivial time-one state of a source-preserving free additive or positive multiplicative convolution semigroup. The obstruction is structural and survives every positive tilt because the support gap does.

Boolean convolution points in the opposite direction. Its `K`-transform linearizes convolution and every probability law is Boolean infinitely divisible. The exact Mangoldt tilt therefore has Boolean roots and a Boolean semigroup, but so does every matched probability source. The sign/divisibility theorem is maximally nonselective.

The useful classification is not “noncommutative probability helps” or “fails.” For this scalar source, the nearest two categories fail for opposite reasons: **free factorization is too rigid to exist; Boolean divisibility is too universal to carry arithmetic sign provenance**. Changing the independence notion alone therefore does not produce Weil positivity.

This does not rule out operator-valued, matrix-valued, indefinite-to-positive, or otherwise globally coupled noncommutative constructions. A surviving route must introduce additional canonical structure whose positivity is neither prohibited merely by the prime-power support gap nor automatic for arbitrary probability laws, and must still account for the finite-prime and archimedean/global pieces of the Weil form.
