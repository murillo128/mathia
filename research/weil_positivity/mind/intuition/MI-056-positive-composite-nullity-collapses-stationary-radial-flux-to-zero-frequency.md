# MI-056 — Positive composite nullity collapses stationary radial flux to zero frequency

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [WP-374](../../findings/WP-374-log-radial-positive-forms-with-exact-composite-shell-nullity-collapse-to-the-zero-mellin-mode.md) and the cyclotomic radial-flux identities established in the earlier WP-161/WP-162 line.

The cyclotomic inward flux carries the exact signed Mangoldt mass before positivity. After passing to logarithmic radius, its shell profile `h_n(u)` satisfies a stronger identity than net cancellation: for a spectator prime `q` not dividing `n`,

`h_(nq)=tau_(log q) h_n-h_n`.

Thus adjoining a new prime is an exact translation coboundary on the common radial line.

WP-374 shows what positivity does to that identity. In any translation-invariant positive-semidefinite form, imposing zero energy on every shell with at least two distinct prime factors makes those mixed-shell coboundaries radical vectors. For a prime-power shell `p^a`, every spectator prime then gives

`[tau_(log q) h_(p^a)]=[h_(p^a)]`.

Two multiplicatively independent spectator primes generate a dense subgroup of real log-radius translations. Strong continuity therefore forces the surviving prime-power class to be fixed by **all** translations. Exact composite-shell sparsity has collapsed the radial dynamics to the zero Mellin-frequency sector before the sign theorem can use the shape of the signed profile.

The scalar Bochner case makes the collapse completely explicit. The only surviving scale-stationary positive form on the cyclotomic span is

`Q(h_m,h_n)=c Lambda(m)Lambda(n)`.

It remembers only the conserved net flux. With critical amplitude `n^(-1/4)` the resulting energy contains `Lambda(n)^2/sqrt(n)`, not the Weil coefficient `Lambda(n)/sqrt(n)`. Correcting that extra logarithm by an amplitude factor would already require inserting the Mangoldt selector on the prime-power shells.

The reusable principle is broader than this one radial model: **in a positive form, exact nullity is a quotient relation, not merely absence of diagonal mass. When source identities express excluded objects as symmetry coboundaries of retained objects, nullity can force the retained sector into the symmetry-fixed subspace.** A proposed positive sparsification should therefore compute the radical generated by the zero-energy source relations before crediting any surviving local profile.

For the Weil-positivity program, the surviving mechanisms must evade at least one load-bearing hypothesis: break log-radial stationarity in a source-forced way, keep mixed shells non-null through an indefinite intermediate/global assembly, or add nonradial multiplicity data whose sign mechanism is not generated by the one-dimensional translation action.

**Boundary.** The abstract result assumes a translation-invariant positive form with strongly continuous quotient action and exact nullity of every mixed shell. The rank-one conclusion is specific to scalar Bochner forms; an abstract fixed subspace may have higher multiplicity supplied by extra channel data. Nonstationary radial operators, indefinite intermediate pairings and nonradial/global geometries are not ruled out. No RH conclusion follows.
