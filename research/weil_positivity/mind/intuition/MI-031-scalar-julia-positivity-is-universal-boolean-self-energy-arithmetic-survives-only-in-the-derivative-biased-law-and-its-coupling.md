# MI-031 — Scalar Julia positivity is universal Boolean self-energy; arithmetic survives only in the derivative-biased law and its coupling

**Evidence level:** exact pure-measure scalar Herglotz classification and matched-control surjectivity from [WP-318](../../findings/WP-318-scalar-julia-tangents-are-boolean-self-energies-of-derivative-biased-measures.md), unifying the one-pole and finite-multipole reductions [WP-316](../../findings/WP-316-scalar-julia-reduction-erases-every-asymptotically-isolated-mangoldt-pole.md) and [WP-317](../../findings/WP-317-finite-multipole-julia-reduction-is-interlacing-degree-reduction.md).

For a pure-measure scalar Herglotz function `f` at a regular real node `t`, let `d=f'(t)>0` and define the derivative-biased probability law

`d nu_t(x) = d^-1 (t-x)^-2 d mu(x)`.

WP-318 proves the exact normal form

`J_t[f](z)-beta = d (t-K_(nu_t)(z))`,

where `K_nu(z)=z-1/G_nu(z)` is the Boolean `K`-transform/self-energy. After positive affine rescaling, the normalized tangent is exactly `tau-K_(tilde nu)`. No finiteness of the support is used, so the same formula contains the isolated one-pole constant, finite interlacing degree reduction, and the infinite collective case.

The sign is universal. For every probability measure `nu`, `t-K_nu` is Herglotz. More strongly, every probability law supported away from the node is realized by a positive Herglotz matched control after weighting it by `(t-x)^2`. Thus **scalar Julia positivity, finite or infinite support, cannot itself be the arithmetic discriminator**. Passing from finitely many atoms to an infinite cluster changes the probability law but does not create a new sign mechanism.

What remains source-specific is now sharply identified: the family of derivative-biased laws generated by the Mangoldt measure, together with any independent finite--archimedean coupling. A surviving scalar route must prove a rigidity, compactness, exceptional limit, or decomposition theorem for those particular probability laws that generic matched controls do not satisfy; positivity of their Boolean self-energy is already free.

This also changes the compactness question. Normalization by the total boundary derivative always produces a probability law when the derivative is finite, so the relevant infinite-collective issue is tightness and the weak-limit geometry of these derivative-biased laws, not merely local boundedness of the original atom-mass chart. Even a source-specific law would still need a canonical mechanism producing the Gamma/global counterterms with the correct normalization and sign.

**Boundary.** WP-318 assumes the pure-measure scalar Herglotz class and a regular node with finite nonzero derivative. An affine `alpha z` term, matrix/operator-valued reduction, generalized resolvent, nonseparable finite--archimedean coupling, or a separate Gamma channel is not classified. The result closes only the hope that infinite collective **scalar Julia positivity itself** supplies the missing Weil mechanism.
