**Evidence level:** supported by the exact carrier/category/sign/compression and spectral-type results through WP-277. WP-275 adds the spectral-type gate. The canonical arithmetic log-energy representation is pure point whereas the normalized pole sector is an absolutely continuous real-line multiplication representation, so a bounded exact modulation intertwiner between them must vanish.

WP-276 closes trace-class source surgery as the obvious repair for a general wholly singular source. Thus leaving trace class, Hilbert--Schmidt, or compact ideals is not enough; even an arbitrary bounded noncompact coupling cannot manufacture the missing continuum from two compact-resolvent ingredients.

The causal order is now stricter. A viable source-first architecture must first supply an intrinsic non-compact-resolvent/absolutely-continuous archimedean or boundary sector, or cross a genuinely supercritical/domain-changing/infinite-volume category boundary that destroys compact resolvent for an independently justified reason. Only after the continuum exists does finite--archimedean coupling and the Weil sign become meaningful.

Strong/direct limits of compact-resolvent systems may also create continuum, but then the continuum comes from the limit architecture rather than one bounded perturbation on a fixed Hilbert space.

**Boundary.** WP-277 does not rule out a zeroth-order a.c. sector, relative bound `>=1`, singular form/domain-changing couplings, noncompact-resolvent extensions, infinite-volume limits, or independently selected non-equivariant inverse constructions. Each surviving category still faces the independent-selection, exact finite-source reproduction and Weil-orientation gates established earlier.
