# MI-013 — Critical coherence needs an independently selected nonlocal law whose source continuum is nonperturbative or already present

**Evidence level:** supported by the exact carrier/category/sign/compression and spectral-type results through WP-276. A completed Weil-positive correspondence remains open; the trace-class spectral-type obstruction is classical representation theory applied to the Mathia source architecture, not a prime-specific theorem.

WP-267--WP-273 separate source coherence, critical-amplitude hostability, category descent, scalar sign and localizable compression. WP-273 shows that any positive compression of the pole-completed carrier must be genuinely nonlocal. WP-274 then shows that nonlocal hostability is not explanatory when the desired positive measure is supplied first: Clark and de Branges inverse theories can reconstruct positive models from the target carrier by universal inverse realization.

WP-275 adds the first spectral-type gate. The canonical arithmetic log-energy representation is pure point, whereas the normalized pole continuum `L^2(R,dx)` has nonatomic absolutely continuous multiplication spectrum. A bounded exact intertwiner of the canonical modulation groups from the natural source carrier to that continuum must therefore vanish.

WP-276 closes the most immediate attempt to evade that obstruction by changing the source dynamics with a small self-adjoint completion first. If a source operator and any appended auxiliary sector have no absolutely continuous spectrum, then a self-adjoint trace-class perturbation cannot create one: Kato--Rosenblum preserves the unitary equivalence class of the absolutely continuous part. The same conclusion holds for domain-changing boundary constructions whose resolvents differ by trace class. Finite-rank boundary variables and finite-rank couplings are included automatically.

Thus a viable source-first architecture now has four simultaneous obligations. It must be **globally nonlocal**, **selected independently of the desired Weil carrier**, avoid an exact bounded pure-point-to-continuum modulation intertwiner, and obtain the required absolutely continuous sector either **at zeroth order** from an independently derived infinite-dimensional archimedean object or through a genuinely non-trace-class/nonperturbative change of spectral realization.

The matched control is important. If an a.c. archimedean sector is already present, trace-class coupling to the arithmetic source may produce nontrivial scattering while preserving that continuum. WP-276 does not forbid this; it says the continuum was supplied before the coupling rather than manufactured by a finite boundary correction. Conversely, Hilbert--Schmidt but non-trace-class, unbounded/non-resolvent-comparable, nonlinear, or nonunitary constructions lie outside the theorem and remain possible only if they independently explain their geometry and sign.

The causal order is therefore stricter than “derive a positive model before matching the target.” The source geometry must first explain **where the continuum comes from and in which analytic category it couples to the pure-point arithmetic sector**. Only after that architecture exists independently can reproducing the Mangoldt, Gamma and polar terms carry explanatory positivity content.

**Boundary.** WP-276 is sharp at trace class and trace-class resolvent comparability; it is not a generic compactness no-go. It does not rule out pre-existing a.c. archimedean sectors, non-trace-class spectral surgery, unbounded couplings with non-trace-class resolvent difference, singular/nonlinear transforms, or independently selected non-equivariant Clark/de Branges/canonical-system constructions. Each surviving category still faces the independent-selection and Weil-orientation gates established earlier.
