# MI-013 — Canonical thermodynamic repair can erase criticality without creating arithmetic positivity

**Evidence level:** proved category/thermodynamic boundary

## Core intuition

Crossing the critical all-prime tensor boundary is a genuine representation change, but neither algebraic transport nor the existence of a canonical equilibrium state supplies the missing Weil sign. The bilateral crossed product keeps the critical sector visible but its gauge dynamics has no proper nonzero-temperature KMS object. The one-sided Toeplitz repair restores KMS equilibrium, yet forces the coefficient algebra into its tracial representation, where every product automorphism is spatial and the critical disjointness threshold disappears.

A viable thermodynamic route must therefore make the equilibrium law itself **arithmetic and source-forced**, not merely repair the operator-algebraic category.

## Strongest justified claim

WP-247--WP-248 identify the critical `p^(-1/2)` and `(log p)/sqrt(p)` deformations as outside the spherical incomplete tensor sector and prove disjointness of the corresponding quasi-local product states. No nonzero bounded identity-observable intertwiner transports spherical positivity into the critical sector.

WP-249 makes the product automorphism inner in a crossed product but finds only universal shift positivity and finite-prime conditional expectations. The completed von Mangoldt distribution and archimedean/polar terms are not generated by the algebraic implementation.

WP-250 tests the canonical bilateral gauge dynamics. Its implementing unitary is an analytic eigenunitary of nonzero gauge frequency, so no `beta!=0` KMS state exists; a proper KMS C*-weight on the unital algebra would normalize to such a forbidden state.

WP-251 applies the natural one-sided escape. Replacing the unitary by an isometry yields a unique `beta`-KMS state with moments

`phi_beta(v^n a v*^m)=delta_(n,m) e^(-n beta) tau(a)`.

The coefficient restriction is the unique trace `tau`, and every automorphism is spatial in its GNS representation. Critical square-summability/disjointness therefore vanishes as a representation invariant. The thermal degree law is universal and unchanged under subcritical, random, or finite-support local rotations. Arithmetic survives only in quasi-local trace correlations such as `tau(a alpha^k(b))`.

## Synthesis of evidence

The line now separates four gates: **critical representation access**, **algebraic implementation**, **equilibrium existence**, and **arithmetic sign production**. The first three can change independently, and moving to a category with better thermodynamics can actually remove the critical structure that motivated the construction.

A credible KMS/modular proposal must explain why its Hamiltonian, range-projection masses, modular spectrum, or weight carries prime/multiplicative energies and how that same source creates the completed Weil orientation. Bost--Connes-type systems evade the single-isometry control precisely by using multiplicative generators with arithmetic dynamics, but the existing sign obstructions for their positive Gram geometry remain.

## Counterevidence / boundary cases

WP-251 does not rule out source-dependent Toeplitz-Pimsner correspondences, nonunital systems, von Neumann weights, multiplicative semigroup dynamics, Hilbert modules, spectral triples, or other category changes. It rules out treating the elementary one-isometry gauge equilibrium itself as the missing arithmetic.

The tracial sector does retain finite/quasi-local correlations of the product automorphism. A new global operation could in principle assemble those correlations into arithmetic, but that assembly is an additional theorem rather than a consequence of KMS positivity.

## Epistemic status

**Exact representation/thermodynamic boundary:** bilateral critical transport has no canonical nonzero-temperature equilibrium; the natural one-sided repair restores equilibrium by thermalizing into a universal trace sector and thereby erases the critical representation boundary. Arithmetic positivity remains unproduced.

## Novelty/prior-art status

Product-state criteria, crossed products, Toeplitz-Pimsner KMS classification, and tracial spatiality are classical as recorded in the findings. This intuition states their combined consequence for the current architecture.

## Falsification criterion

Within the same single-isometry Toeplitz system and gauge dynamics, exhibit a KMS equilibrium whose coefficient representation retains the critical disjointness threshold or whose thermal masses depend intrinsically on the prime amplitudes rather than Fock degree. Alternatively, derive the completed Weil arithmetic from the universal tracial KMS core without adding source-dependent data.
