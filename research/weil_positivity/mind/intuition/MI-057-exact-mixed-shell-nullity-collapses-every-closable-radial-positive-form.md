# MI-057 — Exact mixed-shell nullity collapses every closable radial positive form in the canonical topology

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [WP-374](../../findings/WP-374-positive-composite-nullity-collapses-stationary-radial-flux-to-zero-frequency.md) and [WP-375](../../findings/WP-375-exact-mixed-shell-nullity-collapses-every-closable-log-radial-positive-form.md).

WP-374 first showed that translation-invariant positive radial forms are too rigid: mixed-prime shell nullity plus source coboundary identities force prime-power shell profiles into the translation-fixed zero-Mellin sector. The natural escape was to drop stationarity.

WP-375 removes that escape in the canonical logarithmic-radial Hilbert topology. Let the positive Hermitian form be closable on `L^2(R,du)` and contain the shell profiles in its domain. If every mixed-prime shell has zero energy, positivity puts every such shell in the radical. For a prime-power shell `h_(p^a)`, adjoining a distant spectator prime gives mixed shells of the form

`h_(p^a q)=tau_(log q) h_(p^a)-h_(p^a)`.

Far translates converge weakly to zero. Mazur convexification turns convex combinations of those radical mixed shells into strong `L^2` convergence to `-h_(p^a)`. Closability then propagates radicality to the prime-power shell itself.

The collapse therefore does **not** require translation invariance, locality, boundedness or dilation commutation of the positive form. What matters is the combination of exact mixed-shell nullity, positivity, closability and the canonical radial topology in which remote translates weakly disappear.

The surviving category changes are correspondingly sharper: keep the mixed sector active rather than null, abandon positivity at the intermediate stage, change the topology/domain so the closability argument no longer applies, or leave the canonical radial shell representation through genuinely nonradial/global geometry.

**Boundary.** WP-375 assumes exact mixed-shell nullity and a closable nonnegative form in the canonical logarithmic-radial `L^2` topology. It does not rule out approximate nullity, indefinite intermediate pairings, nonclosable/noncanonical completions or nonradial global constructions.
