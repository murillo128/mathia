# MI-001 — Weil positivity needs a sign-producing global operation, not a positive-looking ingredient

**Evidence level:** supported by several decisive obstructions

## Core intuition

The Weil-positivity search repeatedly finds mathematically natural objects that are positive, self-dual, Hodge-theoretic, or information-geometric in some local sense. The newer evidence makes the failure mode sharper: when an object actually recovers the finite Mangoldt data, the selector often sits in a **signed first variation, supertrace, noncommuting insertion, or boundary asymmetry** rather than in the positive bulk itself. The missing mechanism is therefore a canonical global operation whose assembled structure proves the sign after the arithmetic selector and the archimedean/polar data have both been retained.

## Strongest justified principle

Several independent routes fail for distinct reasons.

- WP-008: Tate's completed self-duality gives the correct critical axis but the Fourier involution has both signs. Self-duality is not positivity.
- WP-010: projection/Gram positivity is universal while the RH content is target totality. Turning the projection into a signed reflection only repackages the hard statement.
- WP-013--WP-014: a positive finite Mangoldt measure leaves the positive Hankel cone after canonical completion, and the exact Prime-Flute Schiffer kernel is pointwise positive but matrix-indefinite.
- WP-015: Prime-Flute zero-energy DtN is positive, but positive spectral energy creates a negative direction and the outgoing critical-line response is non-Hermitian. Universal Herglotz positivity survives only in the imaginary part; relative subtraction has no automatic sign.
- WP-016--WP-020: ordinary Hodge positivity pairs and cancels every positive nonzero mode in the arithmetic supertrace. The full exponent lattice does recover `Lambda(n)` exactly through a backward-Boolean supertrace (WP-018), but the successful residual-energy insertion is intrinsically non-`Q`-invariant. Decoupled or `Q`-invariant supersymmetric completions collapse to an index rather than a gamma factor.
- WP-021: APS/eta boundary terms escape that cancellation by retaining spectral asymmetry, but their sign is not determined by the positive square.
- WP-022--WP-023: the radial score of the canonical prime-torus Poisson family recovers the exact critical finite Weil cosine coefficients, yet Fisher positivity diverges exactly at `sigma=1/2`; standard positive divergences either return to the same Fisher metric or approach a universal pole geometry that loses the place-by-place Weil data.

The repeated fork is therefore not “positive versus negative object.” It is **information versus inherited sign**: the operation that preserves the arithmetic discriminator is typically the one for which positivity is no longer automatic.

## What remains possible

A viable construction can still use a signed selector, boundary response, spectral asymmetry, or noncommuting insertion, but the sign theorem must then be proved for the **completed coupled object** by an independent order/intersection/compression principle. Likewise, a positive bulk object remains useful if a canonical coupling prevents its arithmetic information from being paired away or pushed to infinite information distance.

The finite positive structures should not be discarded. WP-004, WP-018, and WP-022 show that the exact finite arithmetic scale can be realized or differentiated canonically. The unresolved step is to couple that scale to the infinite place and polar sector without either erasing the selector or inserting the desired Weil functional by hand.

## Status / novelty

The individual obstructions and exact finite selectors are persisted findings. The synthesis is a supported program-level constraint: **positivity must be generated after the discriminating arithmetic data have been assembled, not inferred from a component that forgets them.**

## Falsification criterion

Derive the full required Weil-type positivity from one of the audited positive/self-dual/Hodge/information-geometric ingredients by a canonical operation already forced by that ingredient, while retaining the exact finite selector and archimedean/polar terms and without inserting an RH-equivalent totality, zero divisor, or hand-picked sign correction.

## Lean-formalizable core

- Hodge/equivariant cancellation of positive nonzero modes.
- Noncommutation of the residual-energy insertion with the Boolean differential.
- `eta(-B)=-eta(B)` while `(-B)^2=B^2`.
- Divergence of the critical Fisher norm and exact finite-product score coefficients.
