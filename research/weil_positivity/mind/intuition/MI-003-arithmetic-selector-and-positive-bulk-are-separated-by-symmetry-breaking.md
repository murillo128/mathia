# MI-003 — Arithmetic selection can survive at zero order while positive completion, normalization, and exhaustion erase its intrinsic global use

**Evidence level:** supported through WP-151 by exact resultant, inertia, graph-Dirichlet, effective-resistance, finite-core normalization, and exhaustion controls

## Core intuition

The zero-order cyclotomic resultant is unusually faithful to finite Weil arithmetic: it retains prime-power support and the critical half-density amplitudes. Yet the simplest positive completions exhibit a four-way conflict. Keeping the original interaction makes the all-prime energy domain collapse; normalizing locally enough to restore finite fixed-shell energy erases every fixed arithmetic edge; weaker normalization leaves the divergence; and following the residual normalized mass depends on the chosen exhaustion of the same infinite graph.

Thus positivity, a nontrivial global domain, retention of the sparse prime selector, and a canonical limiting topology cannot be assembled independently by post-processing the same resultant graph. The interaction or assembly must change before spectator-prime proliferation has already destroyed one of those resources.

## Strongest justified principle

WP-140--WP-145 separate generic positive scale responses from the exact zero-order cyclotomic resultant. The resultant carries the desired sparse prime-power amplitudes, while its sign-flipped Hessian fills in missing support and loses the selector.

WP-146--WP-147 keep the zero-order kernel and test finite repair. Mixed-prime chains already have centered negative directions, and spectator-prime replication makes both primitive inertia indices unbounded. Fixed rank, fixed codimension, and fixed finite-dimensional auxiliary/archimedean Schur sectors cannot repair the sign.

WP-148 gives the canonical positive finite-cutoff completion: conservation plus off-diagonal interaction `-J` forces the weighted graph Laplacian. Every shell nevertheless has infinite fresh-prime degree in the full alphabet. WP-149 strengthens that domain obstruction: fresh spectator primes provide infinitely many exact parallel bypasses around every resultant edge, driving effective resistance to zero and forcing every finite-energy function to be constant, independently of faithful changes of vertex measure.

WP-150 closes the natural local renormalization escape. For any positive vertex-local diagonal congruence of the cutoff Laplacians, bounded energy on every fixed shell forces the local scale at that shell to zero and hence sends every fixed off-diagonal resultant coefficient to zero. Every finite-core cluster limit is diagonal; the symmetric normalized Laplacian tends to the identity on fixed finite cores.

WP-151 closes the residual interpretation left by that finite-core statement. Two increasing connected exhaustions of the same all-prime graph, using the same canonical weights and degree normalization, give incompatible strong behavior already on the root state: fresh-prime star blocks can make normalized adjacency mass escape with asymptotic unit norm, while Boolean-cube-dominant blocks drive the same vector strongly to zero. Both have identical weak finite-core disappearance. The moving normalized mass is therefore **regularization/exhaustion dependent**, not an intrinsic positive invariant of the infinite resultant geometry.

## What remains possible

A surviving positive completion must alter the interaction or identification before the all-prime spectator network forms, or independently derive a canonical exhaustion/topology from the arithmetic source. Live categories include pair-dependent/nonlocal renormalization, source-forced shell-size cutoffs, provenance-sensitive quotients that remove spectator replication, genuinely mixed-prime interactions, and nonseparable finite--archimedean/cohomological assembly.

Any such route must derive the selector, dense/nontrivial domain, normalization, limiting identification, and sign together. Choosing an exhaustion, subtracting spectator conductance, or following a moving basis merely because it preserves the desired coefficients is additional structure rather than inherited positivity.

## Status / novelty

Cyclotomic resultants, graph Laplacians, normalized Laplacians, electrical networks, Boolean cubes, and finite-rank inertia are classical. The persisted synthesis is the selector/sign/domain/topology boundary: **the faithful sparse resultant is indefinite; its canonical positive all-prime energy is short-circuited; local finite-energy normalization erases fixed interactions; and the residual mass has no exhaustion-independent strong meaning**.

## Falsification criterion

Construct a nonconstant finite-energy function for the unchanged WP-149 network, a vertex-local congruence with bounded fixed-shell energies and a nonvanishing fixed resultant edge, or an exhaustion-independent strong normalized-adjacency limit contradicting the star/cube alternatives of WP-151. A source-forced nonlocal or interaction-changing construction would evade rather than falsify the boundary.
