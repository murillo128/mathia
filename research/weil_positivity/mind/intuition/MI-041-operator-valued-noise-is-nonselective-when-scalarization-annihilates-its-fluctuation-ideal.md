# MI-041 — Operator-valued noise is nonselective when scalarization annihilates its fluctuation ideal

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [WP-329](../../findings/WP-329-deterministic-amalgamated-lifts-make-operator-valued-free-divisibility-universal-before-scalarization.md) and [WP-330](../../findings/WP-330-nondeterministic-amalgamated-noise-remains-universal-when-scalarization-annihilates-the-fluctuation-ideal.md).

WP-329 showed that a deterministic amalgamated lift can be freely infinitely divisible for a universal reason while all arithmetic specificity lives only in the scalar readout state. WP-330 closes the immediate repair: making the enriched variable genuinely non-deterministic and giving it nonzero operator-valued covariance still does not force scalar source selectivity.

The explicit direct-sum construction takes an arithmetic base coordinate together with an independent centered semicircular sector carrying nonzero completely positive covariance. The scalarization state ignores the fluctuation summand. Consequently every scalar moment is exactly the prescribed Mangoldt law while the operator-valued law contains genuine noise, and the same enriched law can be read with arbitrary scalar weights by changing only the scalar state.

The reusable object is therefore not merely the covariance or higher cumulant tensor but its **visibility ideal under the destination readout**. Let the centered fluctuations generate a positive/operator ideal `J`. If the scalarization annihilates `J`, nonzero mixed data can be mathematically real yet completely absent from the scalar arithmetic channel. Nondeterminism in a sector killed by the destination quotient is no more selective than deterministic enrichment.

A minimal anti-vacuity check is that the scalar readout not annihilate the positive ideal generated by the source-dependent fluctuations. Faithfulness on that ideal is a plausible necessary visibility condition, but WP-330 does not show it is sufficient for a source-selective sign theorem. The surviving route must additionally prove that arithmetic constrains the visible mixed data and that this constraint participates in the finite, archimedean and global Weil coupling rather than being another universal positive decoration.

**Program consequence.** Operator-valued proposals should state explicitly: which centered/mixed cumulants depend on the source; which ideal they generate; which quotient or scalar state defines the destination observable; whether that readout sees the source-dependent ideal; and why the resulting positivity is nonautomatic on matched controls. "Nonzero covariance" and "non-deterministic lift" are not source resources until they survive scalarization.

**Falsification criterion.** Freeze a proposed enriched law and identify a nonzero source-dependent fluctuation sector that the scalarization state annihilates. If the scalar arithmetic law and positive/divisible theorem are unchanged while that hidden sector or its weights vary, the proposed mechanism is nonselective. A surviving construction must prove an unavoidable source-dependent mixed invariant whose image under the actual scalar/Weil readout is nonzero and whose positivity cannot be reproduced by the matched class.
