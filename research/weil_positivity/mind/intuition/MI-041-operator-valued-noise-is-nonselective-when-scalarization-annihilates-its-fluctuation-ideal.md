# MI-041 — Exact-law-preserving operator-valued noise is trapped between readout invisibility and faithful collapse

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [WP-329](../../findings/WP-329-deterministic-amalgamated-lifts-make-operator-valued-free-divisibility-universal-before-scalarization.md), [WP-330](../../findings/WP-330-nondeterministic-amalgamated-noise-remains-universal-when-scalarization-annihilates-the-fluctuation-ideal.md), and [WP-331](../../findings/WP-331-faithful-scalarization-collapses-source-preserving-centered-enrichment.md).

WP-329 showed that a deterministic amalgamated lift can be freely infinitely divisible for a universal reason while all arithmetic specificity lives only in the scalar readout state. WP-330 closed the immediate nondeterministic repair: making the enriched variable genuinely non-deterministic and giving it nonzero operator-valued covariance still does not force scalar source selectivity when scalarization annihilates the fluctuation ideal.

WP-331 closes the opposite anti-vacuity repair for the same observable. Let `E:A->B` be a conditional expectation, `tau` a state on `B`, `phi=tau circ E`, and `X=X^*` with `b=E(X)` and `Y=X-b`. Then

`E(X^2)=b^2+E(Y^2)`

and hence equality between the scalar law of `X` under `phi` and the scalar law of `b` under `tau` forces `tau(E(Y^2))=0`. If `tau` is faithful on the positive sector containing `E(Y^2)`, then `E(Y^2)=0`; complete positivity gives `E(YZ)=0` for every `Z`, so every centered `B`-valued moment vanishes and `E(X^n)=b^n`. If `E` is faithful as well, `Y=0` algebraically.

The two results produce a sharp visibility dichotomy. If the scalar readout is nonfaithful, genuine operator-valued noise may survive upstream but hide in a null ideal and remain source-nonselective. If the same readout faithfully sees centered positive variance while the scalar source law is required to remain exactly unchanged, positivity forces that variance to disappear from the entire conditional-expectation law. **Faithfulness is therefore not a free repair of readout-null noise when exact same-observable source-law preservation is also imposed.**

For the finite Mangoldt lift this applies without an extra assumption: the scalar state on `B_R=C(Sigma_R)` has strictly positive finite weights and is faithful. Any self-adjoint enrichment with `E_R(X_R)=b_R` and exactly the same truncated Mangoldt scalar law must have zero centered `B_R`-valued moments. The direct route “exact finite source law plus visible centered positive covariance” is therefore closed.

The reusable object is no longer merely the fluctuation ideal but the **source/readout interface**. A viable enriched construction has to let arithmetic affect mixed data before the final scalar response is formed and use a destination observable that is not constrained to be the unchanged scalar distribution of its conditional mean. Possible interfaces include a compression, pairing, boundary response, transfer function, joint finite--archimedean object, or another source-dependent response whose positivity is nonautomatic on matched controls.

This does not rule out operator-valued free geometry. It says that one must choose which requirement changes. Keeping a nonfaithful readout permits hidden covariance but gives the WP-330 universality problem. Making the readout faithful while preserving the exact scalar law gives the WP-331 collapse. A surviving route must therefore change the scalar law, change the observable/readout, change the conditional-mean identification, or otherwise couple finite and archimedean/global information before scalarization.

**Program consequence.** Operator-valued proposals should state explicitly: which centered/mixed data depend on the source; which positive ideal they generate; which quotient or scalar state defines the destination observable; whether that readout is faithful on the relevant positive sector; whether the enriched variable is required to preserve the exact scalar law of `E(X)`; and why the final sign is nonautomatic on matched controls. “Nonzero covariance” is not a source resource when it is readout-null, and “faithful covariance visibility” is not a source resource when exact-law preservation forces the covariance to vanish.

**Falsification criterion.** Freeze a proposed exact-law-preserving enrichment and compute the conditional variance of the centered part. If the destination scalarization annihilates its positive ideal, the enrichment is readout-invisible as in WP-330. If the scalarization is faithful there, equality of scalar second moments forces the conditional variance to zero as in WP-331. To escape this dichotomy the proposal must identify a different response interface and show explicitly where the source-dependent mixed data enter that response before the final positivity theorem.
