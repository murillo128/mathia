# MI-048 — Ramified reciprocal inner factors have one-sign phase velocity under positive post-differentiation compression

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [WP-242](../../findings/WP-242-squarefree-gamma0-scattering-is-one-common-zeta-scalar-times-finite-local-factors.md) and [WP-345](../../findings/WP-345-maass-selberg-boundary-square-is-divisor-blind.md) through [WP-349](../../findings/WP-349-ramified-congruence-phase-velocities-have-one-sign-so-positive-moving-spectral-compressions-cannot-strip-them.md).

Squarefree congruence scattering already factors each arithmetic eigenchannel as a common completed-zeta scalar times finitely many bad-prime factors `nu_(p,+/-)`. Those local factors are reciprocal under `s -> 1-s`, unitary on the critical line and carry explicit off-critical zero/pole lattices. Arithmetic ramification therefore supplies genuine source-defined inner/CDD-type structure; generic automorphic or congruence arithmetic is not by itself divisor-selective.

WP-348 gives the fixed-channel no-go: any constant linear combination of logarithmic derivatives that removes a prime's two ramified channel factors also removes the common zeta logarithmic derivative. WP-349 strengthens this to arbitrary **positive moving compression after logarithmic differentiation**. On `s=1/2+it`, both local phase velocities have the same strict sign, with a uniform prime-dependent gap. The local logarithmic-derivative operator satisfies

`F_p(t) <= -c_p I`, `c_p=2 log(p) sqrt(p)/(sqrt(p)+1)>0`.

Hence every positive state or positive compression of `F_p(t)` remains negative; allowing the readout to depend on `t` cannot average the two ramified channels to zero. Summing over bad primes only reinforces the sign. Positivity preserves, rather than strips, the ramified phase velocity once differentiation has exposed it.

The surviving distinction is therefore more precise: a Maaß--Selberg route must separate the **unramified global normalizer from ramified local dressing before the positive scalar readout has collapsed them into same-sign phase velocities**. Algebraic division after scalarization is normalization, not a sign theorem; positive post-differentiation channel selection is now excluded as well.

A genuine escape could still use a moving projector/compression chosen **before** differentiation, because differentiating that moving subspace introduces connection/Berry terms not present in WP-349. A noncommuting adelic/global operator mechanism could also change the category. Such an escape must derive its separation from source geometry rather than insert the known factorization by hand.

**Boundary.** WP-349 is a no-go for positive post-differentiation compressions of the explicit ramified logarithmic-derivative block. It does not exclude pre-differentiation moving subspaces with connection terms, noncommutative global/adelic constructions, or another positive form that isolates the unramified coordinate before scalarization. It does not prove Weil positivity or RH.
