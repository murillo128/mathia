# MI-048 — Arithmetic ramification itself supplies off-critical reciprocal inner factors

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [WP-242](../../findings/WP-242-squarefree-gamma0-scattering-is-one-common-zeta-scalar-times-finite-local-factors.md) and [WP-345](../../findings/WP-345-maass-selberg-boundary-square-is-divisor-blind.md) through [WP-348](../../findings/WP-348-ramified-congruence-factors-are-arithmetic-inner-controls-and-fixed-channel-stripping-kills-the-common-zeta-block.md).

The arithmetic escape from the Maaß--Selberg matched controls cannot be stated as generic automorphic, Hecke or Euler realizability. In squarefree congruence scattering the unperturbed arithmetic eigenchannels already factor as a common completed-zeta scalar times finitely many bad-prime factors `nu_(p,+/-)`.

Those local factors are reciprocal under `s -> 1-s` and have modulus one on the critical line, yet carry explicit zero/pole lattices on `Re s=1` and `Re s=0`. The nonreal zeros survive in genuine congruence scattering eigenchannels. Thus finite ramification supplies source-defined arithmetic inner/CDD-type factors without any non-arithmetic perturbation.

There is also a fixed-channel no-go. In the constant cusp eigenbasis, a fixed linear combination of logarithmic derivatives that cancels a `p`-local `+/-` contribution identically must have zero total channel coefficient, and therefore cancels the common zeta logarithmic derivative as well. Constant positive channel geometry cannot simply project away the ramified factors while retaining the desired unramified block.

The surviving distinction is therefore not arithmetic versus non-arithmetic scattering, but **unramified global normalizer versus ramified local dressing inside the positive mechanism itself**. Algebraically dividing out known bad-prime factors after scalarization is normalization, not a sign theorem.

**Boundary.** The fixed-channel argument does not exclude source-forced `s`-dependent readouts, a genuinely adelic operator-level separation, or another positive form that distinguishes ramified from unramified coordinates before scalarization. Any such escape must prove that distinction geometrically rather than read the known meromorphic factorization back into the construction.
