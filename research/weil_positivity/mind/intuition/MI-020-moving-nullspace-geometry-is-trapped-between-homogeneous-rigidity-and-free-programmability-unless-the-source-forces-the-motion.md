# MI-020 — Moving-nullspace geometry is trapped between homogeneous rigidity, singular spectral cuts and free programmability

**Evidence level:** exact for the finite-dimensional homogeneous/projector models in [WP-302](../../findings/WP-302-homogeneous-moving-nullspaces-are-archimedean-rigid-while-free-projector-paths-are-programmable.md) and for fixed-source spectral-cut families in [WP-303](../../findings/WP-303-fixed-source-spectral-cut-kernels-have-no-regular-grassmann-motion-and-compressions-are-povm-universal.md), building on the fixed-kernel obstruction in WP-301. These results do not rule out genuinely varying noncommuting, singular nonlocal, resolvent/scattering, nonunitary or finite--archimedean coupled mechanisms and do not prove Weil positivity.

WP-301 shows that a fixed positive frustration-free completion cannot recover nonzero archimedean spectrum through its zero-mode projector. Allowing the kernel to move is therefore a genuine escape, but WP-302 puts two opposite exact controls around ordinary projector motion.

For a finite-dimensional homogeneous orbit `P(t)=e^(itA)P_0e^(-itA)`, intrinsic finite-jet scalars are constant and regular readouts relative to fixed bounded source operators remain bounded or finite exponential-polynomial. They cannot reproduce the logarithmically growing archimedean digamma profile. At the opposite extreme, a freely chosen rank-one projector path can realize any prescribed smooth positive Grassmann metric density by choosing the path speed. Agreement with the target is then programmed rather than forced.

WP-303 tests the most canonical source-forced middle ground: derive the moving kernel from the spectral calculus of one fixed self-adjoint source operator. For `P_t=1_(S_t)(H)`, all projectors commute. Distinct commuting projections satisfy `||P_s-P_t||=1`; consequently every norm-continuous spectral-cut path is constant. More strongly, whenever a strong derivative exists it is zero. A moving threshold of one fixed source operator therefore supplies only **singular/step-like motion**, not a regular nonzero Grassmann tangent.

On the source-native pure-point log-energy carrier, the canonical positive threshold gives an atomic nested filtration. Fixed positive compressions remain atomic on that support and cannot turn the threshold motion into a smooth nonconstant Gamma response. But freeing the spectral realization does not help conceptually: Naimark dilation says every normalized POVM is a compression of a PVM, so arbitrary positive response measures become representable without arithmetic provenance.

The reusable gate is now three-sided. A useful moving-nullspace mechanism must escape **homogeneous rigidity**, **fixed-source spectral-cut singularity**, and **free POVM programmability**. The source must force a genuinely varying noncommuting or nonlocal operator law rich enough to carry archimedean behavior, while constraining it tightly enough that the desired positive profile cannot be encoded by choosing the representation.

This sharpens the curvature/readout ledger: reachability, source specificity, readout fidelity and a nontrivial moving kernel are still insufficient. One must also specify the topology and operator category in which source-forced motion exists. A fixed abelian spectral calculus has no regular tangent, while the unrestricted positive-measure category has too much representational freedom.

**Boundary.** WP-303 does not rule out spectral subspaces of genuinely varying noncommuting operators, singular resolvent/scattering observables, source-forced boundary transforms, unbounded/infinite-dimensional couplings or nonlocal finite--archimedean operators. Any such escape must identify the independently forced law that both creates the needed motion and prevents the same programmability objection.
