# MI-068 — Single modular-frequency positivity falls back to the centralizer

**Evidence level:** exact operator-algebraic synthesis from [WP-396](../../findings/WP-396-single-modular-frequency-positivity-collapses-to-critical-centralizer.md), extending the centralizer boundary of WP-395 into one nonzero modular/gauge spectral sector.

A noncentral homogeneous mode can carry arithmetic energy frequency, but its canonical positive amplitude cannot. If `x` lies in the spectral subspace `M_r` of the critical Bost--Connes gauge action, then

`x^*x, xx^* in M_1`,

so `|x|`, its source/range support projections and the graph norm-square all belong to the zero-frequency modular centralizer. For `r!=1`, the positive cone of `M_r` itself is trivial: a nonzero positive operator cannot transform by a nontrivial unit-modulus character.

The same loss survives the natural unbounded extension. A homogeneous closed affiliated operator may be genuinely noncentral, but `T^*T` and `|T|` are affiliated with the centralizer. Thus the phase/partial-isometry can retain the modular frequency while the positive amplitude drops exactly into the diffuse category that WP-395 already showed to forget standard countable orbit support.

The reusable obstruction is a **positivity projection**: enlarging the operator category or exposing a nonzero frequency is not useful if the operation that creates positivity quotients that frequency away. One must audit the category after adjoint multiplication, modulus or other positivity construction, not only before it.

This leaves multi-frequency positivity as a genuinely changed hypothesis. Cross terms `x_r^*x_s` land in frequency `r^(-1)s` and need not be central, so a positive square built from several source-selected frequencies can retain noncentral structure. Whether that structure is canonical, arithmetic and Weil-useful remains completely open.

**Boundary.** WP-396 does not classify all positive elements of the critical factor, continuous-core constructions, correspondences or multi-frequency sums. It rules out only a single homogeneous spectral mode and its canonical bounded or affiliated positive modulus/square. No Weil positivity theorem follows from the surviving categories.
