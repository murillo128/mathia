# MI-050 — Arithmetic cusp normalization exhausts the order-one scalar gauge but leaves divisor reconstruction

**Evidence level:** exact/classical synthesis from [WP-352](../../findings/WP-352-scalar-phase-curvature-is-gauge-or-divisor.md) and [WP-353](../../findings/WP-353-finite-order-reciprocal-phase-gauge-is-only-an-exponential-normalization.md). Hadamard factorization, cusp scaling normalization and conformal scattering covariance are classical; the Mathia content is the resulting boundary for scalar Weil-positivity routes.

WP-352 isolates the reciprocal zero-free rephasing

`c_a(s)=exp(a(s-1/2))`, `a in R`,

which preserves critical-line unitarity and the complete zero/pole divisor while shifting the scalar phase generator by a constant. WP-353 shows that under the natural order-at-most-one hypothesis this is not one example among many: every entire nowhere-zero reciprocal, unitary, centrally normalized control is exactly of this form.

The scalar gauge is therefore only one real parameter in the finite-order category. Standard width-one normalization of an arithmetic cusp can remove that parameter, because admissible scaling matrices then differ only by right translation, which does not change the constant scattering coefficient. This corrects the stronger idea that arithmetic cusp scattering necessarily retains an arbitrary exponential phase ambiguity.

But fixing the gauge does not create an independent sign mechanism. Two order-one scalar meromorphic normalizers with the same divisor and the same reciprocal/unitary/source normalization have quotient one. After the arithmetic normalization is imposed, the scalar object is reconstructed from its divisor plus normalization. Recovering its phase is therefore canonical-product reconstruction of spectral data, not geometric production of Weil positivity.

The matched category control explains why both statements matter. Conformally compact scattering under boundary rescaling realizes exactly the same exponential gauge, so abstract scattering geometry does not remove it. Arithmetic cusp normalization is genuinely extra source structure; its limitation is that, once used, nothing scalar remains beyond divisor reconstruction.

The surviving route must act **before scalarization**: a noncentral finite--archimedean/global coupling whose sign is independently forced, or a larger positive geometric object whose projection yields the scalar Weil form without reading the zeta divisor back in as curvature or phase.

**Boundary.** The classification is only for zero-free order-at-most-one controls. Higher-order odd exponential gauges, controls with zeros/poles, matrix-valued/noncentral scattering and other global couplings remain outside it. Eliminating a normalization ambiguity is not itself positivity, and WP-353 derives no Weil sign theorem.
