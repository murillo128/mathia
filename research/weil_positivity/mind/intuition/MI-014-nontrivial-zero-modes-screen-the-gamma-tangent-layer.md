# MI-014 — Zero modes screen Gamma, and positive relaxation cannot repair the scalar channel

**Evidence level:** exact for the smoothed high-energy Mangoldt tangent residual through WP-279, the exact central Gamma identity and literature-backed RH equivalence in WP-280, the finite-state scale-local linear no-go of WP-281, and the completely-monotone positive-relaxation no-go of WP-282 under the same favorable-RH control. These results constrain scalar, fixed finite-dimensional and positive Stieltjes-type infinite-dimensional extraction; they do not rule out every completed operator architecture.

WP-278 shows that the critical Mangoldt half-density has the pole continuum `cosh(x/2) dx` as its source-native high-energy tangent. WP-279 inserts the exact differentiated von-Mangoldt formula one order deeper. After the pole term is removed, nontrivial-zero modes decay like `e^((rho-1)E)`, while the first nonconstant trivial-zero/Gamma correction appears only at `e^(-3E)`. Every nontrivial zero therefore lies parametrically above the Gamma layer, even under RH.

Any scalar normalization strong enough to retain the first Gamma correction amplifies at least one nontrivial-zero mode beyond bounded distributional convergence on the full smooth test space. Reaching Gamma through the differentiated positive measure would require subtracting the zero divisor or choosing zero-annihilating tests, which is target-adapted preprocessing rather than an independent source-native completion.

WP-280 tests the structurally different escape left open by that argument: keep the critical Chebyshev primitive that differentiation discarded. This really does recover the missing central scalar. The completed functional equation gives exactly `-zeta'/zeta(1/2)=1/2(psi(1/4)-log pi)`, and one logarithmic primitive produces this Gamma-center slope after division by `log x` under RH. But Suzuki's theorem identifies that normalized primitive limit itself, and even eventual sign of the same primitive, as equivalent to RH.

WP-281 then tests whether finite-dimensional memory can sit between local tangent extraction and the global primitive. After one stable regularizer, the RH residual is `C_Gamma` plus a nonconstant uniformly Bohr almost-periodic series with one nonzero coefficient at every nontrivial-zero ordinate. A fixed finite-dimensional stable linear input/output system has a rational transfer function `H`. Keeping the Gamma center requires `H(0)!=0`; convergence would require `H(i gamma)=0` for every distinct zeta ordinate. A nonzero rational function cannot have infinitely many such zeros.

WP-282 shows that **infinite dimension alone is not the missing resource**. Let a positive relaxation continuum have transfer

`H_nu(z)=int b/(b+z) dnu(b)`

for a probability measure on `b>0`. This class includes genuinely infinite-dimensional, nonrational positive self-adjoint relaxation baths. Yet on every finite real frequency,

`Re H_nu(i gamma)=int b^2/(b^2+gamma^2) dnu(b)>0`.

So the transfer cannot notch even one critical zero mode while preserving the DC Gamma mode. Every Fourier--Bohr coefficient of the zero screen survives, and the filtered output still cannot converge even under RH. The obstruction has moved from **finite-state cardinality** to **positive-real/Stieltjes geometry**.

The category boundary is therefore now precise. Scalarization is too aggressive; finite-dimensional local state cannot repair it; and replacing finite state by an arbitrary positive completely-monotone relaxation continuum still cannot create the required spectral cancellation. A surviving completion mechanism must use structure outside that positive relaxation category: an independently forced oscillatory/non-Stieltjes global response, genuinely growing or scale-nonlocal memory, nonlinear structure with a separately audited sign, or finite--archimedean coupling before the zero modes become the scalar residual.

**Boundary.** WP-281--WP-282 use RH only as a favorable falsification control; they do not infer RH. An arbitrary signed/complex infinite-dimensional transfer can be built with infinitely many zeros, but selecting those zeros from the zeta divisor simply inserts the target and remains disallowed. WP-280's global growing-window primitive lies outside the fixed-kernel theorems but reaches Gamma only at an RH-equivalent asymptotic. None of WP-279--WP-282 proves Weil positivity.
