# MI-014 — Nontrivial-zero modes screen the Gamma tangent, and scalar primitive recovery reaches it only at an RH-equivalent boundary

**Evidence level:** exact for the smoothed high-energy Mangoldt tangent residual through WP-279, plus the exact central Gamma identity and literature-backed RH equivalence in WP-280. These are no-go results for scalar tangent/primitive extraction as an independent completion mechanism, not for every possible completed operator architecture.

WP-278 shows that the critical Mangoldt half-density has the pole continuum `cosh(x/2) dx` as its source-native high-energy tangent. WP-279 inserts the exact differentiated von-Mangoldt formula one order deeper. After the pole term is removed, nontrivial-zero modes decay like `e^((rho-1)E)`, while the first nonconstant trivial-zero/Gamma correction appears only at `e^(-3E)`. Every nontrivial zero therefore lies parametrically above the Gamma layer, even under RH.

Any scalar normalization strong enough to retain the first Gamma correction amplifies at least one nontrivial-zero mode beyond bounded distributional convergence on the full smooth test space. Reaching Gamma through the differentiated positive measure would require subtracting the zero divisor or choosing zero-annihilating tests, which is target-adapted preprocessing rather than an independent source-native completion.

WP-280 tests the structurally different escape left open by that argument: keep the critical Chebyshev primitive that differentiation discarded. This really does recover the missing central scalar. The completed functional equation gives exactly `-zeta'/zeta(1/2)=1/2(psi(1/4)-log pi)`, and one logarithmic primitive produces this Gamma-center slope after division by `log x` under RH.

But the apparent bridge closes at the proof boundary. Suzuki's theorem identifies that normalized primitive limit itself, and even eventual sign of the same primitive, as equivalent to RH. Thus retaining global primitive data restores the right Gamma scalar only through an asymptotic statement already carrying RH-level content. It cannot serve as an independent geometric origin of the completion term.

The surviving completion category is therefore narrower: a global operator/boundary/cohomological construction must couple finite and archimedean sectors before scalarization, or use a non-scalar transformation forced independently of the zeros and independently of an RH-equivalent primitive limit/sign theorem.

**Boundary.** WP-280 does not rule out every use of primitive data inside a larger architecture, only treating its scalar normalized limit or eventual sign as an independently derived completion mechanism. Neither WP-279 nor WP-280 proves Weil positivity.
