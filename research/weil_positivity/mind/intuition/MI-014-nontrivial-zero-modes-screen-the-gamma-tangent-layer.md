# MI-014 — Nontrivial-zero modes screen the Gamma tangent layer

**Evidence level:** exact for the smoothed high-energy Mangoldt tangent residual through WP-279. This is a no-go for successive scalar renormalization of that residual, not a no-go for every possible completed operator architecture.

WP-278 shows that the critical Mangoldt half-density has the pole continuum `cosh(x/2) dx` as its source-native high-energy tangent. WP-279 inserts the exact differentiated von-Mangoldt formula one order deeper. After the pole term is removed, the residual decomposes into nontrivial-zero modes with factors `e^((rho-1)E)` and trivial-zero/Gamma modes with factors `e^(-(2k+1)E)`.

The scale order is decisive. For every nontrivial zero, `0<Re rho<1`, so its decay exponent lies strictly between `0` and `-1`; the first nonconstant Gamma/trivial-zero correction appears only at `e^(-3E)`. This remains true under RH, where the zero modes are `e^(-E/2)` up to oscillation. The zero divisor therefore sits spectrally between the pole tangent and the Gamma tail.

Any scalar normalization strong enough to retain the first Gamma term would amplify at least one nontrivial-zero mode beyond bounded distributional convergence on the full smooth test space. Reaching the Gamma layer by this route requires first subtracting the whole zero contribution or choosing a zero-annihilating test class. Either move uses the target divisor and cannot be counted as an independent source-native completion.

There is a second information loss: differentiating the Chebyshev primitive to the positive Mangoldt measure removes the archimedean normalization constant. Thus the leading positive measure tangent cannot contain all completion data even in principle.

**Boundary.** This does not rule out a global operator/cohomological construction that couples finite and archimedean sectors before the high-energy limit, a non-scalar transformation forced independently of the zeros, or a construction retaining primitive/global data. It also does not prove Weil positivity.
