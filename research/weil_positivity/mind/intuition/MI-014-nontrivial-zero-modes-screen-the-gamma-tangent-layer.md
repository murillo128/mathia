# MI-014 — Zero modes screen Gamma unless the completion is independently source-forced

**Evidence level:** exact for the smoothed critical Mangoldt residual through WP-279, the central Gamma identity/RH-equivalent primitive boundary in WP-280, the fixed finite-state no-go of WP-281, the positive Stieltjes-relaxation no-go of WP-282, the fixed compact-memory no-go of [WP-283](../../findings/WP-283-fixed-compact-memory-filters-cannot-notch-the-zeta-zero-screen.md), and the target-coded positive growing-memory matched control of [WP-284](../../findings/WP-284-zero-coded-positive-growing-windows-can-erase-the-critical-zero-screen.md) under the same favorable-RH control. These results do not rule out an independently source-forced nonlocal, nonlinear or pre-scalarized completed architecture.

WP-278 gives the critical Mangoldt half-density the correct pole-continuum tangent. WP-279 shows why the next scalar layer is not Gamma: after pole subtraction, every nontrivial-zero mode sits parametrically above the trivial-zero/Gamma correction. Scalar renormalization cannot expose a nonzero Gamma limit on the full smooth test space without first eliminating the zero field.

WP-280 keeps the critical Chebyshev primitive instead of differentiating it away and really recovers the central Gamma scalar. But the normalized primitive limit, and even eventual sign of the same primitive, is already RH-equivalent. That is a characterization, not an independent completion mechanism.

WP-281--WP-283 progressively close fixed local linear responses. Finite stable state has a rational transfer and cannot carry infinitely many required notches; positive exponential-relaxation mixtures have Stieltjes transfers with strictly positive real part at every finite imaginary frequency; and a bounded functional of one fixed compact history has an entire transfer of fixed exponential type, giving only `O(T)` zeros against `gg T log T` distinct critical zero ordinates. A normalized positive moving window that notches all simple zeros through `T` therefore needs memory length at least `c log T`.

WP-284 shows why **growing memory and positivity are not themselves evidence of a canonical completion**. For each target ordinate `gamma`, the positive two-tap probability measure

`nu_gamma = (delta_0 + delta_(pi/gamma))/2`

has transfer zero at `+-gamma`. Convolving these measures over all `0<gamma<=T` gives a positive normalized window `mu_T` with `H_T(0)=1`, `|H_T(omega)|<=1`, and exact notches at every selected zeta ordinate. Its support length is

`L_T = pi sum_(0<gamma<=T) 1/gamma = (1/4)(log T)^2+O(log T)`.

Under the same favorable-RH source model, this zero-coded family suppresses the entire zero screen asymptotically and recovers the central Gamma scalar. The construction is invalid as a Mathia mechanism for exactly one reason: the delays are reverse-engineered from the target zero divisor. The same positive construction works for any prescribed finite frequency set.

This turns source-forcing into the decisive category test. Positivity, bounded real-frequency gain, DC preservation, growing support and even successful Gamma extraction can all be manufactured after seeing the target. A viable growing-memory completion must derive its support/weights/evolution independently from the prime/archimedean geometry and only then prove that zero suppression follows. Otherwise it is a matched control rather than an explanation.

The surviving categories are therefore narrower than “anything with nonlocal memory”: a source-fixed growing-memory law, an independently forced oscillatory noncompact response, nonlinear/pre-scalarized finite--archimedean coupling with a separate sign audit, or another mechanism whose zero cancellation is a consequence rather than inserted data.

**Boundary.** WP-284 does not close growing-memory linear response and does not prove the `Omega(log T)` lower bound of WP-283 sharp; its explicit control costs `O((log T)^2)`. The result is adversarial: it proves that target performance plus positivity is insufficient without a theorem of independent source provenance. None of WP-279--WP-284 proves Weil positivity.
