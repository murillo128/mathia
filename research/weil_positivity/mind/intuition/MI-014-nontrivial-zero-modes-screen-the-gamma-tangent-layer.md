# MI-014 — Zero suppression is too generic; the completion must be source-forced

**Evidence level:** exact for the smoothed critical Mangoldt residual through WP-279--WP-283, the target-coded growing-memory control [WP-284](../../findings/WP-284-zero-coded-positive-growing-windows-can-erase-the-critical-zero-screen.md), and the stronger zero-independent Cesàro matched control [WP-285](../../findings/WP-285-zero-independent-growing-cesaro-windows-already-erase-rh-zero-screen.md), all where stated under the same favorable-RH control. These results do not rule out an independently source-forced nonlocal, nonlinear or pre-scalarized completed architecture.

WP-278 gives the critical Mangoldt half-density the correct pole-continuum tangent, while WP-279 shows that nontrivial-zero modes screen the scalar Gamma layer. WP-280 can recover the central Gamma scalar through a primitive, but the required asymptotic/sign is already RH-equivalent. WP-281--WP-283 then close fixed local linear responses: finite stable state, positive Stieltjes relaxation and fixed compact-memory transfer cannot remove the full zero screen in the required way.

WP-284 showed that positive growing memory can fake success when the target zero ordinates are explicitly inserted. WP-285 makes the adversarial control substantially stronger: **even zero data are unnecessary**. For the one-state regularized residual under favorable RH, the trailing Cesàro average

`C_L Y(E) = L^(-1) integral_0^L Y(E-t) dt`

has sinc transfer `H_L(omega)` and suppresses the entire zero field uniformly by `O(1/L)`. Any deterministic zero-independent law with `L(E)->infinity` and `E-L(E)->infinity` recovers the Gamma-center scalar. The memory may diverge arbitrarily slowly.

The mechanism is the generic Bohr-mean projection of an absolutely summable oscillatory field onto its zero-frequency component. It works for arbitrary frequency sets satisfying the same weighted summability condition. Therefore **target independence is still not source provenance**. A positive normalized window, bounded gain, growing memory, zero-independent support and successful Gamma extraction can all occur without any Riemann-specific selecting principle.

This also reclassifies the WP-283 logarithmic memory lower bound. `Omega(log T)` is a cost of exact notch interpolation by one compact window, not a lower bound for asymptotic removal of the regularized zero field. Once approximate averaging is allowed, no distinguished memory scale is forced.

The surviving criterion is stricter: derive the operation—its support, weights, evolution, coupling or nonlinear law—from the prime/archimedean source **before** examining the zero decomposition, then prove that the required zero suppression/sign/completion follows. Otherwise the construction is a matched control even if it is positive and target-independent.

**Boundary.** WP-285 assumes favorable RH for the oscillatory decomposition being averaged and proves no Weil positivity. Off-critical exponentially growing modes are not made harmless by slow Cesàro memory. The result is a canonicality/source-forcing obstruction, not an RH proof.
