# MI-014 — Zero suppression is too generic; even canonical scale averaging is not source provenance

**Evidence level:** exact for the smoothed critical Mangoldt residual through WP-279--WP-283, the target-coded growing-memory control [WP-284](../../findings/WP-284-zero-coded-positive-growing-windows-can-erase-the-critical-zero-screen.md), the zero-independent Cesàro matched control [WP-285](../../findings/WP-285-zero-independent-growing-cesaro-windows-already-erase-rh-zero-screen.md), and the scale-Haar/Følner extension [WP-286](../../findings/WP-286-scale-haar-folner-averaging-is-arithmetic-blind.md), all where stated under the same favorable-RH control. These results do not rule out an independently source-forced nonlocal, nonlinear or pre-scalarized completed architecture.

WP-278 gives the critical Mangoldt half-density the correct pole-continuum tangent, while WP-279 shows that nontrivial-zero modes screen the scalar Gamma layer. WP-280 can recover the central Gamma scalar through a primitive, but the required asymptotic/sign is already RH-equivalent. WP-281--WP-283 then close fixed local linear responses: finite stable state, positive Stieltjes relaxation and fixed compact-memory transfer cannot remove the full zero screen in the required way.

WP-284 showed that positive growing memory can fake success when the target zero ordinates are explicitly inserted. WP-285 makes the adversarial control substantially stronger: **even zero data are unnecessary**. For the one-state regularized residual under favorable RH, ordinary trailing Cesàro averages suppress the complete absolutely summable oscillatory zero field uniformly; any deterministic zero-independent memory law diverging slowly enough into the past recovers the Gamma-center scalar.

WP-286 closes the next provenance loophole. In logarithmic scale, let `F_j` be any Følner sequence and average with normalized Haar measure. Every nonzero character has vanishing Følner mean, so every absolutely summable discrete oscillatory field is killed uniformly, independent of the arithmetic frequency set. Under favorable RH this includes the regularized zeta zero screen. In the original positive scale variable this is exactly multiplicative Haar averaging `dx/x`.

Therefore even **canonicality from the ambient multiplicative scale symmetry** is too weak. A procedure can be positive, zero-independent, nonlocal, selected by a natural source coordinate, and justified by the Haar/Følner structure of scale while still being a generic invariant-mean projection that works for any summable almost-periodic spectrum. Source forcing must distinguish the arithmetic carrier from matched logarithmic dynamics, not merely inherit a symmetry that both share.

This also reclassifies the WP-283 logarithmic memory lower bound. `Omega(log T)` is a cost of exact notch interpolation by one compact window, not a lower bound for asymptotic removal of the regularized zero field. Once approximate invariant averaging is allowed, neither a distinguished memory length nor a distinguished Følner shape is forced by the zero-suppression task.

The surviving criterion is stricter: derive the operation—its support, weights, evolution, coupling, boundary condition or nonlinear law—from finite-prime/archimedean structure **before** examining the zero decomposition, and prove that the required zero suppression/sign/completion follows. “Canonical for the scale group” is not enough unless the same construction also uses source-specific data that a generic almost-periodic matched control does not possess.

**Boundary.** WP-285--WP-286 assume favorable RH for the oscillatory decomposition being averaged and prove no Weil positivity. Off-critical exponentially growing modes are not made harmless by character orthogonality. The results are source-provenance obstructions, not RH proofs, and they do not touch genuinely coupled pre-scalarized, cohomological, intersection-form or operator-boundary constructions whose positivity has an independent theorem.
