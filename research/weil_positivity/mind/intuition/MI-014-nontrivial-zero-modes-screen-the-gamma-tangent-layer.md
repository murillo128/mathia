# MI-014 — Zero modes screen Gamma across finite-state, positive-relaxation and fixed compact-memory responses

**Evidence level:** exact for the smoothed critical Mangoldt residual through WP-279, the central Gamma identity/RH-equivalent primitive boundary in WP-280, the fixed finite-state no-go of WP-281, the positive Stieltjes-relaxation no-go of WP-282, and the fixed compact-memory no-go of [WP-283](../../findings/WP-283-fixed-compact-memory-filters-cannot-notch-the-zeta-zero-screen.md) under the same favorable-RH control. These results do not rule out every nonlocal, nonlinear or pre-scalarized completed architecture.

WP-278 gives the critical Mangoldt half-density the correct pole continuum `cosh(x/2)dx`. WP-279 shows why the next scalar layer is not Gamma: after pole subtraction, every nontrivial-zero mode sits parametrically above the trivial-zero/Gamma correction. Scalar renormalization cannot expose a nonzero Gamma limit on the full smooth test space without first annihilating the zero divisor.

WP-280 keeps the critical Chebyshev primitive instead of differentiating it away and really recovers the central Gamma scalar. But the normalized primitive limit, and even eventual sign of the same primitive, is already RH-equivalent. That is a valid characterization, not an independent completion mechanism.

WP-281 shows that fixed finite-dimensional memory cannot interpolate between those extremes. After one stable regularizer, the favorable-RH source is `C_Gamma` plus a nonconstant uniformly almost-periodic field with one nonzero coefficient at every zero ordinate. A finite-state stable transfer is rational; preserving DC while making the output converge would require infinitely many transmission zeros.

WP-282 shows that infinite dimension alone is not enough. Any positive mixture of exponential relaxations has Stieltjes transfer with strictly positive real part at every finite imaginary frequency, so it cannot notch even one zero mode while preserving the Gamma mode.

WP-283 closes the remaining **fixed finite-memory oscillatory linear** gap. A bounded scalar functional of a fixed history window is convolution with a finite complex measure on `[0,L]`; its transfer is an entire function of exponential type at most `L`. Jensen gives only `O(LT)` zeros up to frequency `T`, while the current simple critical-line zero count supplies `gg T log T` distinct ordinates. Hence no fixed signed/complex compact-memory filter with nonzero DC response can annihilate the zero screen, even though it may have infinitely many notches.

The quantitative escape price is equally useful. A normalized positive moving window that notches every simple zero up to `T` must have memory length `L_T >= c log T` asymptotically. Thus successful linear notch density already forces **growing scale-nonlocal memory**; it cannot remain one fixed local completion.

The category boundary is now sharper. Scalarization is too aggressive; finite state fails by finite zero count; positive infinite relaxation fails by positive-real geometry; and arbitrary fixed compact memory fails by exponential-type zero density. A surviving mechanism must use genuinely growing/noncompact memory, an independently forced oscillatory infinite-memory structure, nonlinear frequency mixing with its own sign audit, or finite--archimedean coupling before the zero field is reduced to a scalar residual.

**Boundary.** A target-designed transfer built from the zeta zero divisor is still disallowed even if mathematically possible. WP-283 does not cover unbounded memory, growing total variation, nonlinear processing or pre-scalarized operator coupling, and none of WP-279--WP-283 proves Weil positivity.
