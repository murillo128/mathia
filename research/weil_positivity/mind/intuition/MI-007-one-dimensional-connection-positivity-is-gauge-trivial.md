# MI-007 — One-dimensional connection positivity is gauge-trivial even for a nonzero arithmetic carrier

**Evidence level:** exact scalar and finite-dimensional matrix connection reductions through WP-165--WP-166

## Core intuition

Keeping a nonzero signed arithmetic carrier is not enough if the operator category makes that carrier pure gauge. On a one-dimensional radial base, scalar and finite-dimensional skew-Hermitian connection coefficients can always be removed by parallel transport. The resulting covariant-energy positivity is free positivity in a moving frame, not an arithmetic sign theorem.

## Strongest justified principle

WP-165 closes the scalar radial-connection interpretation of cyclotomic flux. WP-166 uses the genuinely nonzero off-diagonal skew incidence inherited from Prime Circle, so the obstruction is not caused by a vanished carrier. For `nabla_K=d/ds+K(s)` with `K` skew-Hermitian on the radial interval, a unitary parallel-transport frame `Q` gives

`||(d/ds+K)f||_2 = ||(Qf)'||_2`.

Noncommutativity changes `Q` to a path-ordered exponential but cannot create a curvature two-form on a one-dimensional base. What can remain source-dependent is boundary transport/holonomy, not bulk energy positivity.

## What remains possible

A source-forced zero-order matrix potential, an independently signed boundary pairing, or a genuinely higher-dimensional/cohomological geometry can escape the one-dimensional gauge theorem. An extra geometric direction is useful only if Mathia forces it and it also participates in the finite--archimedean sign mechanism; adding dimension solely to manufacture curvature is not evidence.

## Status / novelty

Triviality of connections on an interval and parallel transport are classical differential geometry. The Mathia-specific synthesis is categorical: **before treating a signed carrier as a positivity mechanism, classify whether the proposed operator can gauge it away exactly**.

## Falsification criterion

Construct a skew-Hermitian one-dimensional connection in the stated finite-dimensional category whose covariant Dirichlet energy is not unitarily equivalent to the free one, or derive a source-specific positive bulk term from WP-166 without adding zero-order, boundary, topological, or higher-dimensional structure.
