# MI-012 — Automorphic multiplicity does not prevent global arithmetic scalarization

**Evidence level:** literature-backed exact squarefree/nonsquarefree congruence decompositions and higher-rank spherical Gindikin--Karpelevich reduction through WP-244

## Core intuition

Making an automorphic scattering problem matrix-valued is not enough to create the missing Weil sign. In the most canonical multi-cusp and higher-rank tests, the global zeta/Dirichlet arithmetic remains attached to scalar analytic normalizers, while the extra matrix structure is local dressing, orthogonal multiplicity, or Weyl combinatorics. The positive/unitary normalization often removes exactly the arithmetic scalar one hoped to exploit.

The useful distinction is therefore irreducible **arithmetic coupling** versus merely many channels.

## Strongest justified principle

WP-242 treats squarefree `Gamma_0(N)`. Its scattering matrix factors as the level-one completed-zeta scalar times a tensor of ramified local matrices, and an `s`-independent Walsh--Hadamard basis diagonalizes the local tensor. Every cusp channel carries the same global zeta package; direct Gram positivity on the unitary axis collapses further to identity.

WP-243 closes the ordinary nonsquarefree extension. New primitive character sectors appear, but the continuous spectrum has a canonical orthogonal newform/oldclass decomposition. Each primitive pair carries one scalar Dirichlet-`L` completion, and for trivial nebentypus the Riemann-bearing nontrivial sectors arise as independent quadratic-character channels. Returning to the cusp basis can hide this reducibility but does not create source-forced interaction between the scalar global divisors.

WP-244 tests split higher rank in the spherical minimal-parabolic channel. Gindikin--Karpelevich gives a scalar product of completed-zeta ratios on each spherical Weyl edge. Bundling chambers produces weighted permutation matrices; canonical normalization divides out the arithmetic weight and leaves the Weyl action. Higher-rank noncommutativity is therefore combinatorial on this sector, not irreducibly arithmetic.

## Program consequence

Demand an operator-level source relation that survives the natural decomposition. Promising categories are a canonical coupling across primitive oldclasses, non-spherical inducing data with genuinely nontrivial normalized intertwiners, or a multidirectional differential/tensor construction whose arithmetic constituents interact before scalar normalization and whose sign follows from independent Hilbert-space structure.

For every proposed matrix escape, first diagonalize/decompose it in the canonical spectral basis and identify whether the Riemann divisor is still carried by separable scalar factors.

## Counterevidence / boundary

These findings are not a no-go for Maaß--Selberg theory, arbitrary vector-valued automorphic forms, non-spherical representations, or derivatives of multidimensional normalizers. Oldform mixing inside one nonsquarefree primitive sector need not be constant-diagonalizable, and normalized higher-rank intertwiners can be genuinely operator-valued away from the spherical line.

The negative result applies to the tested canonical channels; a new coupling is viable if it is geometrically/source-forced rather than inserted to mix scalar factors by hand.

## Epistemic status

**Supported automorphic scalarization boundary: squarefree cusp multiplicity, nonsquarefree congruence sectors, and spherical higher rank all leave global Riemann/Dirichlet arithmetic in scalar normalizers or orthogonal scalar channels; a Weil-positive escape must retain genuinely coupled arithmetic before that reduction.**

## Falsification criterion

Exhibit within one of the classified canonical sectors a natural positive/unitary operator whose irreducible block contains nonseparable interaction between distinct global zeta/Dirichlet divisors contrary to WP-242--WP-244, or show that the stated canonical decomposition/normalization does not remove the scalar arithmetic as claimed.
