# MI-039 — Enriched semigroups inherit obstructed scalar shadows or universal lifts unless arithmetic is genuinely mixed

**Evidence level:** exact source-specific synthesis from [WP-326](../../findings/WP-326-prime-power-gaps-make-mangoldt-tilts-freely-indecomposable-while-boolean-divisibility-is-universal.md) and [WP-328](../../findings/WP-328-free-scalar-shadows-block-bi-free-and-c-free-source-lifts.md), with the bi-free/c-free shadow maps and Boolean specialization supplied by classical probability theory.

A category can be richer than a scalar convolution and still inherit a scalar obstruction. If `F` is a semigroup homomorphism and an enriched object has an `m`-th root, its shadow `F(X)` must have the corresponding scalar root. Therefore a fixed arithmetic law that is freely indecomposable cannot be retained as a homomorphic free shadow of an infinitely divisible enriched object.

For the pole-normalized Mangoldt law this immediately blocks two natural placements. Under additive bi-free convolution each coordinate marginal free-convolves, so no bi-freely infinitely divisible planar law can have the exact source as either marginal. Under c-free convolution the reference coordinate free-convolves, so the exact source cannot be the reference law of a c-freely infinitely divisible pair.

The opposite c-free placement exposes the dual failure mode. With trivial reference `delta_0`, c-free convolution of the outer laws reduces to Boolean convolution. Since every scalar law is Boolean infinitely divisible, `(mu,delta_0)` is c-freely infinitely divisible for every `mu`. The exact arithmetic source therefore admits this lift for a completely universal reason.

The reusable gate is: **extra coordinates matter only if the source-specific information lives in genuinely mixed data that do not descend to an already obstructed or universally positive scalar shadow.** A second face/state is not itself new arithmetic structure.

This leaves real space for operator-valued or genuinely mixed constructions. A bi-free model with freely divisible auxiliary marginals could encode arithmetic only in mixed cumulants; a nondegenerate c-free model could put the source in the outer coordinate against a canonically forced reference; operator-valued freeness need not scalarize through an arbitrary state. But each such proposal must show that the mixed data recover the exact finite-prime contribution canonically and that their positivity is nonautomatic on matched controls.

**Falsification criterion.** For any proposed enriched semigroup, identify every canonical scalar projection that is a convolution homomorphism. If one such shadow is the exact freely indecomposable Mangoldt law, infinite divisibility is impossible. If a specialization makes the lift available for every scalar source, it is nonselective. Only mixed structure escaping both tests remains a live positivity mechanism.
