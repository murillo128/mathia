## Treat source provenance as part of the mathematical completion

The pole tangent, Gamma completion and critical zero field are distinct spectral layers. Fixed state, positive relaxation and fixed compact memory fail for different analytic reasons, while WP-284 shows that growing-memory positivity is flexible enough to fake success once the target frequencies are supplied.

A future candidate must therefore expose two logically separate theorems: first, a source-native derivation of its support, weights, semigroup/evolution or coupling law; second, a consequence showing that this same object has the required sign/zero suppression/completion behavior. Reverse-engineering the transfer from the zero divisor, even with positive weights and modest memory growth, belongs to the control class and does not advance the Weil mechanism.

The open `Omega(log T)` versus `O((log T)^2)` memory gap may be interesting as an approximation problem, but closing it alone would not establish canonicality or Weil positivity. The decisive missing information is why the zeta source itself selects the completion.
