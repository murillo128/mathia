# Weil-positivity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable Weil-positivity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Derive the continuum source architecture before asking it to reproduce Weil positivity

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation`, `MI-011-prime-ray-marginals-do-not-determine-mixed-prime-coupling`, `MI-012-automorphic-multiplicity-does-not-prevent-global-scalarization`, `MI-013-critical-sector-transport-needs-new-global-arithmetic-structure`.

WP-267--WP-273 show that hostability, category descent, scalar sign and localizable compression all fail as free routes. A surviving positive compression must be globally nonlocal and reconstruct continuum mass from arithmetic samples.

WP-274 shows that nonlocal inverse representation is still not enough: Clark and de Branges machinery can manufacture positive analytic models when the desired normalized carrier is supplied first. WP-275 adds spectral-type incompatibility: the natural arithmetic dynamics is pure point while the pole continuum is absolutely continuous, so a bounded exact time-covariant intertwiner between those canonical representations is zero.

WP-276 now closes finite-dimensional/trace-class source surgery as the obvious repair. Starting from a wholly singular arithmetic source plus any wholly singular auxiliary sector, neither a self-adjoint trace-class perturbation nor a trace-class-resolvent boundary modification can manufacture a nonzero absolutely continuous channel. Finite-rank boundary sectors and couplings are included. The trace-class threshold is genuine rather than a generic compactness statement.

The surviving route must therefore explain the continuum **before** positivity is invoked. Either derive an intrinsic infinite-dimensional archimedean a.c. sector at zeroth order and then couple the arithmetic source to it, or justify a genuinely non-trace-class/nonperturbative spectral change. In either case the object must still be independently selected rather than reverse-engineered from the target and must reproduce the finite-prime, Gamma and polar sectors in the correct Weil orientation.

## Treat hostability, inverse realizability, spectral compatibility and continuum provenance separately

WP-273 makes nonlocality necessary. WP-274 shows that a positive nonlocal host can be reverse-engineered from the target. WP-275 prevents a direct bounded exact pure-point-to-a.c. modulation bridge. WP-276 says a small self-adjoint boundary correction cannot first change the source spectral type and then evade that bridge obstruction.

A pre-existing a.c. archimedean sector remains compatible with trace-class scattering, while Hilbert--Schmidt/non-trace-class, unbounded, nonlinear or otherwise nonperturbative constructions remain outside the no-go. Those are genuine surviving categories, not evidence for positivity. The next theorem must identify which such category is forced by source geometry and why its finite--archimedean coupling has the Weil sign without importing the target carrier by hand.
