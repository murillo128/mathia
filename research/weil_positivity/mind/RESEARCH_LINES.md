## Lift the source-native pole tangent into a completed architecture beyond scalar, finite-state and positive-relaxation extraction

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation`, `MI-011-prime-ray-marginals-do-not-determine-mixed-prime-coupling`, `MI-012-automorphic-multiplicity-does-not-prevent-global-scalarization`, `MI-013-critical-sector-transport-needs-new-global-arithmetic-structure`, `MI-014-nontrivial-zero-modes-screen-the-gamma-tangent-layer`.

WP-267--WP-277 rule out a sequence of free constructions: local positive hosts, inverse reconstruction from an already supplied target, exact bounded pure-point-to-a.c. intertwining, and bounded/subcritical mixing of compact-resolvent sectors. WP-278 then gives the surviving infinite-volume category a source-native measure-level realization: the critical Mangoldt half-density has high-energy tangent `cosh(x/2)dx`, exactly the polar continuum of the completed carrier.

WP-279 closes successive scalar extraction from that tangent. After subtracting the pole continuum, nontrivial-zero modes sit parametrically above the trivial-zero/Gamma layer, so no scalar renormalization can expose a nonzero Gamma limit on the full smooth test space without first subtracting or annihilating the zero divisor.

WP-280 closes the most canonical primitive alternative as an **independent** completion mechanism. Keeping the critical Chebyshev primitive really recovers the central Gamma slope `-zeta'/zeta(1/2)=1/2(psi(1/4)-log pi)`, but the normalized logarithmic-primitive limit that isolates this scalar is equivalent to RH; eventual sign of the same primitive is RH-equivalent as well. The primitive route therefore reaches the correct archimedean scalar only at an already RH-level asymptotic boundary.

WP-281 closes the natural finite-state compromise between those extremes. Even granting RH as a favorable control, one stable local smoother leaves a nonconstant uniformly almost-periodic field carrying every nontrivial-zero frequency. Any further fixed finite-dimensional stable linear system has a rational transfer function. Preserving the zero-frequency Gamma mode while making the output converge would require zeros at **every** zeta ordinate, impossible for a nonzero rational transfer function. Internal finite-dimensional state is therefore not enough merely because it is non-scalar.

WP-282 closes the most canonical positive infinite-dimensional enlargement as well. An arbitrary normalized positive mixture of exponential relaxations has Stieltjes transfer `H_nu(z)=int b/(b+z)dnu(b)`, possibly nonrational and genuinely infinite-dimensional. But `Re H_nu(i gamma)>0` for every finite frequency, so it cannot annihilate even one nontrivial-zero mode while retaining the Gamma DC component. The zero screen therefore survives every completely-monotone positive self-adjoint relaxation continuum. The obstruction is now positivity of the response class, not finite dimensionality.

The live burden is consequently a genuinely different global category. A completed operator/boundary/cohomological construction must retain source fluctuations and supply the archimedean sector **before** scalar high-energy asymptotics discard it, without explicit zero subtraction and without taking as input a primitive limit/sign theorem equivalent to RH. Surviving possibilities include an independently forced oscillatory/non-Stieltjes infinite-dimensional response, a genuinely scale-nonlocal operation, a nonlinear mechanism with its own sign audit, or finite--archimedean coupling formed before the critical zero field becomes the scalar residual.

## Treat pole provenance, zero fluctuations, primitive normalization, Gamma completion, memory class, response positivity and sign as different spectral layers

WP-278 explains the leading pole continuum at PNT/Weyl scale. WP-279 shows that the next scalar residual layer is not Gamma because the nontrivial zero divisor screens it. WP-281 shows that replacing the scalar readout by any fixed finite-dimensional stable local linear memory still cannot separate the Gamma center from the infinite critical zero spectrum. WP-282 shows that allowing infinitely many positive relaxation modes does not fix this: Stieltjes positivity forbids the required transmission zeros altogether.

A successful completion mechanism must therefore preserve more than the local measure tangent, more than a scalar global primitive, more than finitely many local boundary states, and more than an arbitrary positive relaxation continuum. The remaining categories are global finite/archimedean coupling before scalarization, independently forced oscillatory/non-Stieltjes infinite-dimensional structure, genuinely scale-nonlocal processing, or another architecture whose sign mechanism can be audited without importing the target divisor or an equivalent RH criterion.
