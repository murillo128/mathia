## Find a source-derived sign theorem outside universal scalar positivity and scalar convolution closures

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation` through `MI-037-prime-power-gaps-split-free-and-boolean-divisibility`.

WP-319--WP-321 then show that source-specific gap laws, sparse common-source coherence and even the full unthinned bounded-gap Julia family remain matched-control realizable in the natural weak topology by one atomlessly smoothed positive source.

WP-322 closes the canonical exact finite-node escape. For any positive scalar Herglotz source, the boundary Loewner matrix at arbitrary regular real nodes is a resolvent Gram, so every finite multipoint Pick matrix and finite Julia reduction is positive for universal reasons. WP-323 closes the immediate finite-jet escape: normalized confluent Pick matrices are again resolvent Grams.

WP-324 tests Schoenberg translation-total-positivity geometry. The positive-energy Mangoldt source has Laplace transform `L(s)`, but its ordered translation kernel has the universal alternating sign law for every nondegenerate positive measure; reversing orientation gives universal Hankel total positivity. WP-325 then closes the natural classical convolution-semigroup escape: a positive tilt of the prime-power law is not even two-divisible, while the nearby Bernstein construction `1-R_s` is universal compound-Poisson structure and loses prime-power localization.

WP-326 closes the two nearest noncommutative scalar semigroup variants as well. The first prime-power gap makes every positive Mangoldt tilt freely indecomposable under additive free convolution, and after exponentiation the same atom gap makes the native positive law freely indecomposable under multiplicative free convolution. Boolean convolution gives the opposite failure: every probability measure is Boolean infinitely divisible, so the exact source has a Boolean semigroup only for a matched-control universal reason.

The next sign theorem therefore cannot be another finite scalar Pick/jet determinant, the direct positive-measure Laplace translation/Hankel construction, or a source-preserving scalar convolution semigroup obtained merely by changing the notion of independence. A surviving route must use exact support/mass/multiplicative information through a source-forced nonlinear relation whose positivity is both nonautomatic and actually true for the Mangoldt source, or change category to genuinely matrix/operator-valued, indefinite-to-positive, infinite-domain, or direct finite--archimedean structure with its own sign theorem.

## Separate source fingerprint from source-selective sign

Shifted-lattice support, quantized Mangoldt coefficients, exact multipoint entries, finite jets and prime-power support gaps are all source-specific. The sign provenance can still be universal, or the desired positive structure can fail precisely because the source is selective.

WP-322--WP-324 show the first failure mode: arithmetic entries sit inside universal resolvent-Gram/Hankel sign mechanisms. WP-325--WP-326 show the second: exact prime-power support is selective enough to destroy classical and free divisibility, while the nearby Boolean/compound-Poisson positive structures that always exist are universal. Source specificity is useful only when the required positive theorem is itself source-selective and points in the Weil direction.

Future scalar arguments should identify the precise feature that fails all these controls: it must survive as exact arithmetic information while its sign theorem fails for a broad matched class **and** remains actually true for the Mangoldt source. A source-specific obstruction to positivity is not a substitute for a positive source theorem.

## Preserve mass class, curvature, topology, determinant orientation, domain, divisibility category and global coupling as distinct gates

WP-314--WP-326 expose successive independent gates: universal moving tangents, mass scaling, normalized positivity, source-specific compact limits with matched controls, exact finite multipoint/jet Gram universality, translation orientation and domain, classical infinite divisibility, free additive/multiplicative indecomposability, and universal Boolean divisibility. Passing or failing one category does not infer the next.

A viable route must change the topology, algebraic category, nonlinear source operation, infinite-domain behavior, or direct finite--archimedean coupling in a way that creates a **new nonautomatic sign obligation**, not merely rename the convolution or completion. Infinite jets/nodes and operator-valued constructions remain genuine category changes because domain, closability, completion and global coupling become part of the theorem; each still needs its own matched-control and sign audit.
