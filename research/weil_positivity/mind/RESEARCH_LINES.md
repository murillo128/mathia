WP-310 now closes the most canonical function-level escape left by that sequence. The critical Mangoldt positive source has density-one infinite mass and a logarithmic Herglotz end, whereas the classical infinity/moment Schur transform requires finite-mass `-s_0/z` asymptotics. Formal application of the first Schur step with any fixed finite moment parameter eventually leaves the Herglotz half-plane. Finite source cutoffs admit the transform, but their zeroth moment diverges in the critical limit; subtracting the density-one background creates a signed remainder and loses the positivity theorem one hoped to inherit.

The remaining route must therefore add genuine structure rather than a more flexible coordinate description or the standard finite-moment Schur recursion: a finite-reference interpolation transform with source-derived data, a cutoff coupled to an independently justified renormalization law, a noninvertible Herglotz operation with independent meaning, compression/generalized resolvent not equivalent to the classical infinity step, operator/matrix-valued boundary sector, added archimedean Herglotz channel, determinant/scattering object, or nonseparable finite--archimedean coupling.

## Treat mass class, curvature orientation, gauge rigidity and transform admissibility as separate gates

Matching `log y` establishes only the infinite-mass class. Producing a nonuniversal `y^-2` coefficient establishes arithmetic sensitivity but not the correct orientation. WP-307 closes positive affine input recentering; WP-308 closes constant projective output gauge; WP-309 collapses holomorphic scalar automorphism families to the same constant class. WP-310 adds a different gate: a positive Herglotz response may still lie outside the domain where a desired positivity-preserving transform is defined.

Dropping a WP-310 hypothesis is a real category change, not a technical escape. A finite-reference transform uses new interpolation data; a renormalized cutoff requires a theorem controlling the divergent source mass; an operator-valued compression can carry structure not present in the scalar moment sequence. Those remain open precisely because they introduce or justify new data rather than recode the existing scalar logarithmic response.

## Use matched controls against universal leading scale and post-hoc recoding

Unit-density positive measures control the leading logarithm, while the Mangoldt curvature sign is source-specific. WP-307--WP-309 show that affine or projective scalar gauge cannot flip that sign while preserving the required logarithmic end. WP-310 shows that passing directly to the standard moment-Schur algorithm cannot even start on that logarithmic source without changing its normalization or positivity class.

Future constructions must therefore alter the geometry or realization before final scalar comparison and must prove both **admissibility** and the desired sign/positivity theorem from the source rather than importing either from a transform whose hypotheses the critical source does not satisfy.
