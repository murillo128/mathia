## Derive a non-compact-resolvent continuum source architecture before asking it to reproduce Weil positivity

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation`, `MI-011-prime-ray-marginals-do-not-determine-mixed-prime-coupling`, `MI-012-automorphic-multiplicity-does-not-prevent-global-scalarization`, `MI-013-critical-sector-transport-needs-new-global-arithmetic-structure`.

WP-267--WP-274 show that hostability, category descent, scalar sign, localizable compression and inverse construction all fail as free routes. Clark/de Branges machinery can manufacture positive analytic models when the desired normalized carrier is supplied first, so existence alone is a representation tautology when the target measure is inserted by hand.

WP-275 adds spectral-type incompatibility: the natural arithmetic dynamics is pure point while the pole continuum is absolutely continuous, so a bounded exact time-covariant intertwiner between those canonical representations is zero. WP-276 closes trace-class and trace-class-resolvent surgery as a way to manufacture the missing a.c. channel from wholly singular ingredients.

WP-277 makes the current source restriction stronger. The logarithmic arithmetic Hamiltonian has compact resolvent. If the auxiliary zeroth-order sector also has compact resolvent, **every bounded self-adjoint coupling, including bounded noncompact couplings, preserves compact resolvent**, and the same holds for every symmetric coupling of relative bound `<1`. Moving from trace class to Hilbert--Schmidt or another bounded perturbation class therefore does not open the continuum.

The surviving route must explain the continuum before positivity is invoked. Either derive an intrinsic infinite-dimensional archimedean/boundary sector that already has noncompact resolvent and the required a.c. spectral type, or justify a genuinely supercritical domain-changing/infinite-volume mechanism that destroys compact resolvent. In either case the object must be independently selected, reproduce the finite Mangoldt/Gamma/polar source correctly, and only then prove the Weil orientation.

## Treat hostability, inverse realizability, spectral compatibility, compact-resolvent provenance and sign separately

WP-273 makes nonlocality necessary. WP-274 shows that a positive nonlocal host can be reverse-engineered from the target. WP-275 blocks a direct bounded exact pure-point-to-a.c. modulation bridge. WP-276 blocks trace-class manufacture of the missing continuum. WP-277 shows that for the actual compact-resolvent arithmetic source the no-go extends far beyond trace class to all bounded and subcritical relatively bounded coupling between compact-resolvent sectors.

A pre-existing a.c. archimedean sector remains compatible with ordinary bounded/scattering couplings, and a genuinely supercritical/domain-changing/direct-limit construction remains outside the theorem. Those are surviving categories, not evidence for positivity. The next theorem must identify which category is forced by source geometry and why its finite--archimedean coupling has the correct Weil sign without importing the target carrier by hand.
