## Find a source-derived sign theorem outside scalar shadows and universal enriched lifts

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation` through `MI-039-enriched-semigroups-inherit-obstructed-scalar-shadows-or-universal-lifts`.

WP-325--WP-327 close the direct scalar additive-semigroup escape across Muraki's five natural products. Classical, free, monotone and anti-monotone divisibility reject the exact pole-normalized Mangoldt law, while Boolean divisibility accepts every probability law and is therefore nonselective.

WP-328 tests the two closest enriched enlargements. Divisibility descends through every semigroup homomorphism. Since the exact Mangoldt law is freely indecomposable, no bi-freely infinitely divisible planar law can retain it as either scalar marginal, and no c-freely infinitely divisible pair can retain it as the free reference coordinate. Moving the source to the outer c-free coordinate with trivial reference gives the opposite failure: `(mu,delta_0)` is c-freely infinitely divisible for every scalar law because the convolution reduces to universal Boolean convolution.

Thus merely adding a second face or state does not rescue the source-semigroup program when the arithmetic law remains a homomorphic free shadow, and the obvious degenerate lift is again matched-control universal. A surviving enriched construction must place essential arithmetic information in genuinely mixed coupling data not recoverable as an obstructed scalar shadow, and that mixed data must itself generate a nonautomatic positive theorem compatible with the archimedean and global terms.

Operator-valued free convolution remains genuinely open because a generic scalar state composed with amalgamated expectation need not be a homomorphic free shadow. Nondegenerate c-free first-coordinate constructions also remain open. Each proposal must identify exactly which scalar shadow survives, which mixed cumulant/coupling carries arithmetic, and why positivity is neither impossible by shadow descent nor automatic on controls.
