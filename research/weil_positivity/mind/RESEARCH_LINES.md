## Use noncentral arithmetic information without asking positivity to erase or orient its centralizer shadow

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm`, `MI-039-enriched-semigroups-inherit-obstructed-scalar-shadows-or-universal-lifts` through `MI-059-pointwise-local-boundary-nullity-collapses-to-the-mangoldt-ray`, `MI-060-strongly-local-markov-forms-either-reject-mixed-shells-or-become-unit-cell-invisible`, `MI-061-logarithmic-interface-current-classicalizes-to-ramanujan-orthogonality`, `MI-062-stationary-positive-interface-selection-is-an-explicit-spectral-mask`, `MI-063-the-canonical-divisibility-diagonal-cannot-hide-a-prime-power-selector`, `MI-064-the-full-bounded-bost-connes-positive-cone-cannot-isolate-prime-power-support`, `MI-065-woronowicz-affiliation-does-not-enlarge-the-unital-bost-connes-source-algebra`, `MI-066-a-source-valued-resolvent-pulls-positive-unbounded-selectors-back-into-the-bounded-bost-connes-no-go`, `MI-067-a-diffuse-modular-centralizer-can-erase-exact-arithmetic-point-support`, `MI-068-single-modular-frequency-positivity-falls-back-to-the-centralizer`, `MI-069-positive-multi-frequency-data-cannot-outrun-its-centralizer-support-shadow`, `MI-070-quotienting-out-a-centralizer-that-contains-the-order-unit-destroys-inherited-positivity`, `MI-071-fixed-centralizer-shadows-have-dense-sign-symmetric-tangents`, `MI-072-cutoff-stable-linear-source-restrictions-inherit-fixed-shadow-sign-symmetry`, `MI-073-continuous-source-cutoffs-make-hereditary-fixed-shadow-sign-symmetry-norm-dense`.

WP-391--WP-394 rule out exact positive prime-power selection in the canonical divisibility diagonal, the full bounded Bost--Connes positive cone, Woronowicz affiliation with the same unital source algebra, and positive unbounded forms whose canonical resolvent returns to that represented algebra.

WP-395 changes category to the critical factor but finds a diffuse centralizer `L^infinity(hat Z,m_Haar)` in which the standard countable arithmetic orbit is Haar-null. Exact point support is then either zero as an operator or representative-dependent rather than invariant.

WP-396 shows that one nonzero modular/gauge frequency does not fix the problem: the positive square, modulus and support projections of a homogeneous mode return to the zero-frequency centralizer, and a nonzero spectral subspace contains no positive operator of its own.

WP-397 closes the immediate multi-frequency interference escape for bounded positive elements. If `E` is the critical gauge expectation, `a>=0`, `d=E(a)` and `p=s(d)`, every Fourier coefficient satisfies `a_r=p a_r p`; finite-frequency squares satisfy `E(y^*y)=sum_r x_r^*x_r`. Cross-frequency information can survive, but not outside the measurable support of the positive centralizer floor.

WP-398 closes the next interpretation: keep the diffuse floor but quotient it away and read positivity from the noncentral correlations. On the self-adjoint information quotient `M_sa/N_sa`, the image of the positive cone is the entire quotient because the discarded centralizer contains the order unit. Even with the shadow fixed by `E(a)=1`, both `1+epsilon k` and `1-epsilon k` are positive for every sufficiently small centered self-adjoint direction `k`. Ambient positivity therefore supplies no nontrivial linear order or first-order sign orientation on the surviving bounded noncentral data.

WP-399 removes the possibility that a non-scalar source-forced fixed shadow repairs that local sign problem by itself. For any bounded positive `d` in the centralizer, the self-adjoint centered directions supported on `p=s(d)` that are order-bounded by `d` are genuine two-sided fixed-shadow tangents. Spectral cutoffs of `d` make such tangents strongly and ultraweakly dense in the entire centered supported space. The tangent geometry is therefore sign-symmetric at normal first order even when the shadow is nonconstant.

WP-400 narrows the remaining source-relation escape. Let `V` be a linear source-selected subspace of centered supported directions. If it is stable under the canonical shadow cutoffs `k -> e_n k e_n`, then those cutoffs remain inside `V`, are order-bounded by `d`, and converge strongly/ultraweakly to every direction in `V`. The source-restricted tangent family is again densely two-sided, so every normal linear sign functional forced only by positivity must vanish on `V`. Centralizer bimodules and fixed gauge-frequency selections satisfy this locality condition automatically.

WP-401 closes the source-C-star loophole in that cutoff argument on hereditary corners. If `H_d=Her_A(d)` and `V_d={k in (H_d)_sa:E(k)=0}`, the continuous source-internal cutoffs `c_n=min(1,nd)` satisfy `c_nkc_n->k` in norm while remaining centered and order-bounded by a multiple of `d`. Hence two-sided positive fixed-shadow tangents are norm dense, and every bounded real linear sign functional forced only by positivity vanishes. The same holds for any linear source subspace stable under these continuous compressions. Missing discontinuous von Neumann spectral projections therefore does not by itself create an orientation mechanism.

The surviving bounded linear route must now use a source-forced relation that is **nonlocal even under continuous `C*(d)` localization**: the relation must lose a specific cross-scale quantity under `k -> f_n(d) k f_n(d)`, and that quantity must be shown to carry the destination sign. Otherwise the route must change category to a nonlinear/global readout, a singular or genuinely unbounded functional/domain, a one-sided or nonunital boundary construction, or a finite--archimedean assembly that does not reduce to the classified fixed-shadow geometry.

## Keep selector existence, support, frequency, quotient order, tangent topology and destination sign distinct

Category enlargement can fail by being too programmable, returning through bounded transforms, erasing the arithmetic orbit as null, projecting a single frequency under positivity, forcing every multi-frequency coefficient to live over the same diffuse support, making the projected positive cone improper after the fixed component is forgotten, leaving a strongly dense two-sided tangent family after the shadow is fixed, or leaving a **norm-dense** two-sided tangent family already inside the hereditary source C-star algebra. These are different obstructions.

A new proposal must state which arithmetic information remains invariant, how positivity is formed, what its fixed-point component is, where every useful frequency is supported, whether the post-quotient cone is proper, what topology controls the fixed-shadow tangent set, and whether the defining source relation survives continuous source localization. A successful bounded linear relation must fail that cutoff test for a source-forced reason and carry the final Weil sign through the resulting cross-scale coupling; noncentrality, ambient positivity, gauge-band selection and absence of source spectral projections are no longer enough.
