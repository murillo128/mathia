## Find a source-forced sign mechanism that survives scalar reconstruction after normalization

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-039-enriched-semigroups-inherit-obstructed-scalar-shadows-or-universal-lifts` through `MI-051-nonreversible-positivity-can-carry-orientation-without-forcing-it`.

The stationary-geometry results show that the Weil log-square cannot be manufactured for free by oscillatory negative-type structure: a scalar or fixed-positive-functional readout obtains the required quadratic arithmetic direction only when that direction is already present in the quadratic sector. Singular-character tangents merely relocate the same target square to a boundary state.

The Maaß--Selberg/congruence branch gives complementary matched-control obstructions. Positive truncated norms and boundary squares can be divisor-blind; arithmetic congruence scattering can carry independent reciprocal inner factors; and bad-prime logarithmic-derivative blocks have one strict sign. WP-350 closes unitary moving compression, while WP-351 shows that canonical proper-contraction defect positivity is exactly blind to the common unimodular scalar zeta phase.

WP-352--WP-355 classify the main finite-dimensional phase-geometry repairs. Projective Berry/Chern geometry cannot see a common scalar factor; the full scalar Maurer--Cartan term is exact and central; normalized order-one scalar gauges reduce to `exp(a(s-1/2))`; every conjugation-invariant positive quadratic metric orthogonally splits scalar center from traceless directions and therefore sees only the square of determinant-phase velocity; and non-reversible Finsler positivity can store central orientation only through a freely chosen closed odd term whose sign may be reversed without losing positivity.

WP-356 tests the most literal arithmetic escape left by the earlier Maaß--Selberg matched controls: couple a finite-prime Hecke correspondence to the cusp boundary before taking the positive square. On the constant Fourier mode, the normalized prime Hecke operator becomes `T_p=S_(log p)+S_(-log p)` in log height and

`[P_tau,T_p]^*[P_tau,T_p]=1_(tau-log p,tau+log p]`.

The source-native positive form recovers the prime logarithmic scale, but only as unsigned annulus energy. The scattering/divisor information remains in a sign-indefinite interference term, averaging erases it, and the positive square does not preserve the desired critical `p^(-1/2)` attenuation.

WP-357 now closes the natural finite-Hecke extension of that route. For every `n`, the constant-mode normalized Hecke operator is the finite symmetric translation polynomial

`T_n = sum_(d|n) S_(log(n/d^2))`.

The sharp-boundary defect Gram kernel is

`K(a,b)=1_(ab>0) min(|a|,|b|)`,

namely two copies of Brownian covariance. Hence every finite Hecke polynomial and every finite positive Gram/correlation construction built from these boundary defects factors through universal nested-interval overlap geometry.

This universality is robust enough to preserve the normalized Hecke multiplication law after replacing the prime logarithms by arbitrary positive lengths. Simultaneously, a standing wave `e^(itx)+eta(t)e^(-itx)` remains an eigenwave for arbitrary reciprocal unimodular phase `eta`, so divisor-changing CDD freedom survives. Finite cross-prime interference is therefore present but not selective: its positive closure still depends only on universal overlap data.

The constant-mode finite-Hecke frontier is now classified more sharply. A viable automorphic route must use structure absent from finite Brownian-overlap boundary Grams: nonconstant Fourier modes, an infinite/all-prime completion where convergence itself adds a constraint, genuinely nonlocal operator order not reducible to finite interval overlaps, higher-rank/adelic truncation, or a source theorem that forces the critical half-density and orientation into a noncentral positive object before this universal quotient appears.

## Keep source dependence distinct from sign coercivity

Arithmetic labels, self-adjoint realization, unitary scattering, moving channel choice, contraction defects, projective curvature, a canonically normalized scalar phase, a bi-invariant positive matrix metric, a non-reversible positive Finsler metric and even the **finite constant-mode Hecke algebra before positivity** can all be source-dependent or canonical-looking. Yet the resulting positive closure may admit matched controls, discard scalar orientation, reduce to divisor reconstruction, or collapse to a universal kernel. None is Weil positivity merely because it is arithmetic, canonical, positive or geometric.

WP-356--WP-357 sharpen the audit: inserting arithmetic before positivity is necessary but still not sufficient, and even preserving a finite arithmetic operator algebra is not enough. One must compute the exact post-closure carrier and then test whether its sign, normalization and source selectivity survive arbitrary-length, continuous-translation and unitary-phase controls.

The next proposal should state exactly which scalar phase or divisor coordinate survives its quotient, which noncentral/infinite-dimensional controls remain admissible, what source normalization removes, whether finite-prime and archimedean data couple before positivity, whether the positive closure preserves or squares the desired arithmetic amplitude, and what fixes any surviving orientation independently of the target Weil sign. A source-defined positive object that collapses to universal overlap geometry is still a representation mechanism rather than an independently generated sign theorem.
