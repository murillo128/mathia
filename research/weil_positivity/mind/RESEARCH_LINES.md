# Weil-positivity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable Weil-positivity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they discriminate among mathematically different mechanisms.

## Couple the exact pointed finite package to the archimedean/polar sector before the selector becomes singular or indefinite

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`, and `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm`.

Finite arithmetic recovery and continuity are no longer the main mysteries. Intrinsic covers force the `n^{-1/2}` normalization, positive cover coboundaries have trace `log n`, and their prime-power Möbius primitives recover the exact finite coefficient. Fixed-shift positive controls preserve the same coefficient, while WP-090 shows that fixed positive averaging of shifted resolvents cannot create a different exact representative: matching the logarithmic degree data on an unbounded set forces the zero-shift carrier itself.

The boundary is now sharper. Regular scale-covariant Jensen responses either collapse to bounded/dyadic traces or lose exact Mangoldt support except at a singular endpoint. Fisher variation diverges at the covariant origin, additive shift contrasts are indefinite, and positive mixtures cannot cancel the contamination. A decisive mechanism must therefore form the finite--archimedean coupling before these separated positive families are differentiated or scalarized and prove the final sign independently.

## Derive a genuinely new singular/nonperiodic sign mechanism rather than finite positive reference assembly

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-operator-category-is-a-hard-compatibility-gate` and `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`.

The bounded two-projection route and its obvious multireference repairs are now closed much further. Positive cover-angle pseudologs are reference-relative; fixed positive mixtures violate dyadic logarithmic scaling; every fixed bounded construction from a finite reference dictionary has additive spectral readouts affine on common-multiple towers; and a fixed finite auxiliary sector with refinement-coherent coupling changes only one frozen collective block. Local-strength repetition instead conflicts with positivity.

A surviving operation must therefore be genuinely singular/unbounded with a geometrically forced domain and sign, infinite/nonperiodic, higher-cohomological, twisted/family/boundary, or nonseparable finite--archimedean. A moving principal angle can produce `log n` in principle, but a decisive result must derive that moving scale intrinsically rather than insert degree dependence to obtain the answer.

## Classify nonseparable or higher-cohomological completions beyond cover averages and zeroth coinvariants

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm`, `MI-005-cover-coinvariants-classicalize-or-collapse`, and `MI-002-operator-category-is-a-hard-compatibility-gate`.

The most canonical separated globalizations remain sharply constrained. Positive basepoint averaging compatible with all covers is only pointed-plus-Haar. Zeroth Hausdorff coinvariants classicalize to ordinary trace on the diagonal and collapse to zero on full standard operator ideals. The new multireference/coherent-coupling results show that simply adding finitely many noncommuting cover sectors or one fixed coherent auxiliary block does not evade that repetition structure.

The live question is whether a mathematically forced **higher** cohomological object, nontrivial coefficient module, infinite auxiliary sector, selective geometric quotient, twisted family/boundary form, or nonseparable finite--archimedean coupling retains the exact pointed selector and supplies a new sign. A decisive result must identify the surviving information explicitly and show that it is neither ordinary trace/Möbius scalarization, finite common-multiple repetition, nor a repackaged polar/singular logarithmic sign.
