# Weil-positivity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable Weil-positivity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they discriminate among mathematically different mechanisms.

## Couple the exact pointed finite package to the archimedean/polar sector before positivity completes it with mixed-prime data

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`, and `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm`.

Finite arithmetic recovery and continuity are no longer the main mysteries. Intrinsic covers force the `n^{-1/2}` normalization, positive cover coboundaries have trace `log n`, and their prime-power Möbius primitives recover the exact finite coefficient. Fixed-shift positive controls preserve the same coefficient, while regular scale-covariant Jensen responses either collapse to bounded/dyadic traces or lose exact Mangoldt support except at a singular endpoint.

WP-096--WP-097 now expose the full algebraic positive-cover boundary. After the critical first-order conjugacy, every finite-valued exact-cover-positive quadratic form is a positive Gram measure on the infinite prime torus. If its Fourier support is forced to contain only the desired one-prime Weil rays, positivity requires infinite diagonal self-energy. Allowing mixed-prime coefficients repairs positivity with a sharp finite threshold and an explicit product completion, but those mixed terms are essential rather than cosmetic.

The live question is therefore a finite--archimedean coupling **before** sparse selector and positive completion are separated. A decisive mechanism must explain how the required mixed-prime positive completion is used or canonically removed by the global structure, while retaining the exact one-prime coefficients and deriving the final sign independently. Deleting the mixed modes by hand simply returns the WP-096 obstruction.

## Derive a genuinely transforming global/singular sign mechanism rather than a fixed coherent auxiliary sector

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-operator-category-is-a-hard-compatibility-gate` and `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`.

The obvious positive reference and auxiliary repairs are now closed much further. WP-092 shows that even an infinite-dimensional fixed auxiliary Hilbert space with unbounded self-adjoint dynamics freezes to one collective block under refinement-coherent bounded coupling; standard relative resolvent, determinant, and spectral-shift responses are then tower-independent. WP-093 classifies every fixed finite-band exact-cover-positive form as a weighted Dirichlet cone whose critical ray is classical Cesaro/continuous-dual-Hahn geometry. WP-094 collapses positive block-Toeplitz infinite-range covariance to a singular nonclosable endpoint anchor, and WP-095 proves that adding the entire finite-band positive cone cannot regularize that anchor.

A surviving global operation must therefore transform with refinement, change domain, use a genuinely noncoherent but canonical boundary coupling, be higher-cohomological/twisted, or couple finite and archimedean sectors nonseparably before scalarization. Merely making a fixed auxiliary sector infinite, unbounded, or nonlocal is not enough.

## Classify canonical quotients of the prime-torus positive completion and higher-cohomological alternatives

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm`, `MI-005-cover-coinvariants-classicalize-or-collapse`, and `MI-002-operator-category-is-a-hard-compatibility-gate`.

The exact-cover-positive cone is now simultaneously broader and more constrained than the earlier finite-reference picture. WP-096 proves that arbitrary infinite-range positive forms correspond to multiplicative Toeplitz/prime-torus Grams, so genuine closable nonlocal positive geometry exists. WP-097 proves that the critical one-prime Weil rays admit finite positive completion only by introducing a forced hierarchy of mixed-prime Fourier coefficients.

The live question is whether an independently forced quotient, conditional expectation, boundary map, cohomology, coefficient module, or finite--archimedean coupling can turn that completed positive object into the required sparse Weil selector **without destroying the sign or reintroducing infinite diagonal energy**. The proposed clue `CLUE-mixed-prime-positive-completion-selector-quotient` isolates the first finite-torus test of this question. A negative theorem showing that every positivity-preserving canonical quotient keeps enough mixed modes to obstruct the selector would be as valuable as a positive construction.

Any successful completion must identify the surviving information explicitly and show that it is neither ordinary trace/Möbius scalarization, fixed coherent repetition, a hand-programmed Fourier deletion, nor a repackaged polar/singular logarithmic sign.
