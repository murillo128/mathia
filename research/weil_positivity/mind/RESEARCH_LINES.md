## Cross the boundary with genuinely nonlocal structure, or delay mixed-shell selection

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm`, `MI-039-enriched-semigroups-inherit-obstructed-scalar-shadows-or-universal-lifts` through `MI-059-pointwise-local-boundary-nullity-collapses-to-the-mangoldt-ray`.

WP-374--WP-383 show that exact mixed-shell nullity collapses every fixed-interior response: finite packets and then every compact-open-continuous Banach/Hilbert-valued linear response reduce the primitive-shell family to one Mangoldt line. This makes a genuine boundary/scale-varying response, or delayed selection after finite--archimedean interaction, necessary.

WP-384 supplies a canonical boundary escape but classicalizes it. The fresh-prime boundary profiles have positive limiting energy and Mellin multipliers `c_m(tau)=m^(-i tau) prod_{p|m}(1-p^(-1/2+i tau))`; their Gram is the Nyman modulus-square carrier with finite Euler factors, and mixed shells remain positive. WP-385 closes every closed dilation-invariant positive reweighting because those finite Euler factors are boundedly invertible on the real Mellin axis.

WP-386 closes the complementary zeroth-order nonstationary local class. For an arbitrary positive Borel measure in the native boundary coordinate, exact nullity of every mixed-prime profile forces the measure to be supported on `(0,1)`. On that interval every profile is exactly the constant `Lambda(m)/sqrt(m)`, so the complete arithmetic Gram and every local-anchor incidence have rank at most one in the shell variable. Dropping scale stationarity therefore does not help if positivity remains pointwise local.

The surviving boundary route must now be structurally nonlocal/off-diagonal in the native coordinate, retain a finite-`q` term lost in the continuum limit, or postpone exact mixed-shell selection until after a source-derived finite--archimedean interaction. What source-forced kernel, derivative/interface energy or prelimit coupling can retain more than the Mangoldt ray while remaining positive for a reason independent of the desired Weil conclusion? Any candidate should first be tested against the WP-386 fresh-large-prime control, which makes a single mixed squarefree shell strictly positive on every prescribed compact interval `[1,X]`.

## Keep selector existence, locality, stationarity and destination sign distinct

WP-381 proves that a bounded exact Mangoldt selector exists in the pointed geometry; WP-382--WP-383 show that exact selection collapses a large interior response space to rank one. WP-384 shows that moving to a genuine boundary layer preserves nonzero energy but lands in the wrong positive resource class. WP-385 excludes all stationary positive Mellin reweightings, while WP-386 excludes arbitrary nonstationary **pointwise-local** positive measures with exact mixed-shell nullity. Neither theorem classifies nonlocal positive kernels, derivative/interface forms, finite-prelimit constructions or indefinite finite--archimedean coupling before the final sign theorem. Those are now the categories in which a new mechanism would have to live.
