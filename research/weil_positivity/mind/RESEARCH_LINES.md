## Find a source-derived sign theorem outside scalar shadows and universal enriched lifts

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation` through `MI-040-deterministic-amalgamated-divisibility-can-hide-all-source-specificity-in-the-readout-state`.

WP-293--WP-318 close the natural pure-measure scalar Julia orbit at one node: derivative normalization reduces the response to a universal Pick/Boolean self-energy form. WP-319--WP-323 extend the matched-control closure through bounded-gap Julia families, exact finite multipoint Pick matrices and finite jets, all reducing to universal positive-source Gram structure. WP-324 shows the analogous translation/Laplace sign mechanisms are universal or have the wrong orientation.

WP-325--WP-327 close the direct scalar additive-semigroup escape across Muraki's five natural products. Classical, free, monotone and anti-monotone divisibility reject the exact pole-normalized Mangoldt law, while Boolean divisibility accepts every probability law and is therefore nonselective.

WP-328 tests the two closest enriched enlargements. Divisibility descends through every semigroup homomorphism. Since the exact Mangoldt law is freely indecomposable, no bi-freely infinitely divisible planar law can retain it as either scalar marginal, and no c-freely infinitely divisible pair can retain it as the free reference coordinate. Moving the source to the outer c-free coordinate with trivial reference gives the opposite failure: `(mu,delta_0)` is c-freely infinitely divisible for every scalar law because the convolution reduces to universal Boolean convolution.

WP-329 closes the weakest operator-valued escape without incorrectly forcing scalar free convolution through scalarization. At every finite source cutoff, the exact law is the scalarization of a deterministic element of the amalgamation algebra `B_R=C(Sigma_R)` with `E=id`; the `B_R`-valued point law is freely infinitely divisible simply because the deterministic coordinate divides inside the base algebra. The same operator-valued law survives arbitrary changes of the scalar weights. Algebraically, the full source has the universal lift `B=C[t]`, `X=t`, with every higher `B`-valued cumulant zero; all arithmetic sits only in the scalar state `tau_s` used to read out the law.

Thus merely observing that an operator-valued freely infinitely divisible object scalarizes to the exact Mangoldt source is also matched-control universal. A surviving enriched construction must place essential arithmetic **before scalarization** in non-deterministic mixed/operator data—higher `B`-valued cumulants, a nontrivial completely positive covariance, boundary coupling, or another invariant that cannot be changed independently by replacing the readout state—and derive a nonautomatic positive theorem there. The same architecture must still reproduce the archimedean and global Weil terms.

Genuinely non-deterministic operator-valued free geometry, nondegenerate c-free first-coordinate constructions, deformed/multiplicative structures and direct finite--archimedean couplings remain open. Each proposal must identify exactly which scalar shadows survive, which mixed data carry arithmetic, whether source specificity has merely been moved into a state/readout, and why positivity is neither impossible by shadow descent nor automatic on matched controls.

## Separate source fingerprint from source-selective sign

Shifted-lattice support, Mangoldt coefficients, exact multipoint entries, finite jets and prime-power atom gaps are source-specific. The sign provenance can still be universal, the desired positive architecture can fail because source specificity descends into an incompatible scalar shadow, or an enriched positive object can be universal while the source is stored only in its scalarization state.

WP-322--WP-324 exhibit universal resolvent-Gram/Hankel signs. WP-325--WP-327 exhibit scalar divisibility rejection versus Boolean universality. WP-328 adds homomorphic-shadow obstructions in enriched categories. WP-329 adds the complementary operator-valued failure: a freely divisible object can exist for every scalar source when the enriched law is deterministic and all specificity is deferred to the readout state.

Future arguments should state the precise arithmetic feature that a broad matched class cannot fake while preserving a genuinely nonautomatic sign theorem. A source-specific obstruction to positivity is not a substitute for a positive source theorem, and an enriched lift is not source-selective when its positive structure is unchanged under arbitrary replacement of the scalarization state.
