WP-317 now closes **fixed finite multipole** geometry as a separate escape. Repeated reduction gives `N->N-1->...->0`, while reverse augmentation is classical rational Herglotz continued-fraction data. The sign theorem is generic for arbitrary positive atoms and does not preserve a finite set of prime poles as an arithmetic carrier.

The scalar support route therefore has a sharper structural fork:

`one-pole dominated -> Julia tangent is constant`,

`fixed finite multipole -> classical interlacing degree reduction -> constant after finitely many steps`,

`nontrivial scalar frontier -> genuinely unbounded/infinite collective geometry or a non-rational component`.

Combined with WP-315, this makes the next scalar question precise: can the source force a canonical **infinite collective positive tangent** that is locally controlled despite clustering, survives generic positive-measure matched controls, and couples to the Gamma/global discriminator with the correct orientation? If not, the construction must leave the audited scalar category through operator/matrix-valued boundary structure, generalized resolvents, noninvertible maps, an added archimedean channel or nonseparable finite--archimedean coupling.
