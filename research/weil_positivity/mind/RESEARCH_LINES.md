# Weil-positivity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable Weil-positivity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they discriminate among mathematically different mechanisms.

## Couple the exact finite selector to the archimedean/polar sector before class or positive reduction

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation` and `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`.

Finite arithmetic recovery is no longer the main mystery. Canonical positive Gram/cycle determinants recover `Λ`, the anchored reflection intrinsically selects the `q=2` Riemann Gamma channel, and Prym polarization data can store `log p` integrally. But the direct completions repeatedly change the pairing: radial Schur reductions erase the birth operator, genus-zero arithmetic divisors/correspondences become principal, normalized Hodge transfer is degree-flat, and the Prym discriminant lives in torsion invisible to real quadratic positivity.

The live question is to form the finite-plus-archimedean/polar object **before** those quotients. A decisive mechanism must retain the exact finite coefficient and the canonically selected archimedean channel in one nontrivial class/operator and derive its sign independently. Appending the Gamma term to a finite selector or taking a nonlinear cardinality/determinant and then wrapping it in a positive scalar does not qualify.

## Derive a category-changing operation with its own sign theorem

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-operator-category-is-a-hard-compatibility-gate` and `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`.

Natural finite positive reductions are now tightly closed: comparable and noncomparable shell-dependent radial Schur clocks either cancel arithmetic, diverge, or return the indefinite birth form. Direct positive pairing on the strongest Prym discriminant carrier is also impossible because the carrier is finite torsion. The surviving operation must therefore act before torsion/class quotient, use a larger non-torsion representation, or be genuinely infinite-dimensional/nonseparable across finite and archimedean sectors.

A decisive result must cross that category boundary while preserving the exact selector and provide an independent ordering/intersection/sign theorem at the destination. A phase-valued theta pairing, a representation whose only `p`-dependence is its dimension, or a positive elimination that sends arithmetic to zero is not enough.
