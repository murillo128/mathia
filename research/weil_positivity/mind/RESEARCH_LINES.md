## Find a source-derived sign theorem outside scalar shadows and universal enriched lifts

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation` through `MI-042-faithful-exact-law-ucp-readouts-force-multiplicative-domain-rigidity`.

WP-293--WP-328 close broad scalar and enriched semigroup routes: many natural positivity/divisibility structures are either incompatible with the exact source shadow or universal on matched controls. WP-329--WP-330 show that operator-valued enrichment is likewise nonselective when all arithmetic remains in the scalarization state or the readout annihilates the fluctuation ideal.

WP-331--WP-332 give the opposite rigidity. For a faithful finite-cutoff UCP readout preserving the exact second moment of the same self-adjoint source observable, the positive Kadison defect vanishes; the observable lies in the multiplicative domain and its off-diagonal Stinespring coupling disappears.

WP-333 identifies the sharp nonuniformity of this finite-cutoff theorem. If `m_R` is the least output-state atom, then

`||D_R|| <= tau_R(D_R)/m_R`.

Approximate multiplicative-domain rigidity therefore requires the scalar defect to be `o(m_R)`, not merely `o(1)`. The finite Mangoldt states have exponentially vanishing least mass, so scalar convergence can hide an order-one operator defect in a moving thin tail.

The strengthened WP-333 control shows that this escape does **not** depend on adding external support. Three consecutive points on the intrinsic dyadic prime-power log ray support a Markov/UCP self-map that replaces the middle value by the average of its neighbors. It fixes the source coordinate pointwise, has Kadison defect `(log 2)^2` at the middle atom and Stinespring coupling `log 2`, yet the induced scalar law returns to the original one in total variation, every fixed polynomial moment and every fixed Wasserstein metric as the modified atom moves into the exponentially thin tail. Meanwhile the maps stay maximally far from the identity in operator norm.

Thus exact support preservation, prime-power adjacency and very strong scalar-law convergence are still not source-selective resources. A surviving operator-valued route must either obtain **relative faithfulness control** strong enough to beat the smallest visible source mass, control the maps in a genuinely stronger topology, change the observable/interface before same-observable rigidity applies, or make the moving coupling source-forced and destination-visible through a sign theorem that matched martingale controls cannot reproduce.

## Separate source fingerprint from source-selective sign

Shifted-lattice support, Mangoldt coefficients, prime-power gaps and exact scalar laws are source-specific fingerprints. WP-333 shows that even preserving the exact finite support and using nearest-neighbor moves along a canonical arithmetic ray does not make a positive operator coupling arithmetic: the same martingale mechanism is generic. The sign provenance must fail on a matched control, not merely use arithmetic labels.

## Preserve readout conditioning and map topology as distinct gates

Normalization, algebraic category, scalar shadows, fluctuation visibility, faithfulness modulus and map-level control are independent. Pointwise faithful cutoffs can become arbitrarily ill-conditioned; convergence in total variation, fixed moments or fixed Wasserstein distance is a statement about scalar laws, not about the UCP map or Stinespring coupling. Future constructions must state which topology controls the actual mixed data consumed by the Weil destination and why that control is uniform at the moving cutoff.
