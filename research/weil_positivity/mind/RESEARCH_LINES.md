WP-374--WP-390 reduce the canonical continuum/local/interface positive categories. The boundary current becomes the Ramanujan basis `j_m=c_m/sqrt(m)`, and every shift-invariant positive interface form is exactly a nonnegative rational-frequency mask. Exact mixed-prime nullity therefore programs prime-power support explicitly; boundedness or positivity does not make that selector source-native.

WP-216 supplies a different source-forced object in the Bost--Connes branch: the canonical divisibility range projections at critical conditioning produce the Poisson/GCD Gram and the correct half-density/log-prime scale. WP-391 closes the most literal diagonal selector escape: the canonical norm-closed divisibility diagonal contains no nonzero observable whose diagonal is supported only on prime powers.

WP-392 closes the corresponding full bounded positive escape. For every bounded Bost--Connes element, exact mixed-prime nullity of its basis diagonal already forces that diagonal to vanish everywhere; if the element is positive, it is therefore zero. Off-diagonal crossed-product terms consequently cannot produce a nonzero exact positive prime-power selector inside the bounded source C-star algebra.

WP-393 removes one apparent category change as well. In Woronowicz's C-star-algebraic sense, affiliation with a unital algebra does not enlarge it: `A_BC^eta=A_BC`. Thus an “unbounded Woronowicz-affiliated element of the Bost--Connes source algebra” is actually bounded and falls straight back under WP-392.

WP-394 closes a broader unbounded positive-form escape whenever one canonical resolvent remains visible in the represented bounded source algebra. For a closed nonnegative form with operator `T>=0`, if `(T+lambda I)^(-1)` lies in `pi(A_BC)` and the form vanishes on every mixed-prime basis vector, then the positive bounded transform `T(T+lambda I)^(-1)` lies in `pi(A_BC)` with the same mixed-prime nullity. WP-392 forces the bounded transform, hence `T` and the form, to vanish. The same applies to a closable form after closure under the stated domain and resolvent hypotheses. This is a genuine extension: unbounded operators with source-valued resolvent exist, but they cannot also realize the exact positive selector.

The remaining possibilities are therefore narrower and category-explicit. A positive form route must have its relevant canonical resolvent/bounded transform leave the represented norm-closed Bost--Connes algebra, or change the exact domain/nullity mechanism. Other exits remain a representation von Neumann algebra with a canonical modular or weight constraint, a one-sided Hilbert-module/correspondence defect, a canonically produced boundary category whose represented functional calculus does not fall back into `pi(A_BC)`, a finite-`q` prelimit mechanism, active finite--archimedean coupling, or a global construction in which prime-power support and the final sign emerge together.

## Keep selector existence, source selection, operator category and destination sign distinct

The recent sequence separates several different facts. Arbitrary Hamiltonian spectral calculus can encode prime powers; stationary positive interface geometry can realize the selector only as an explicitly chosen mask; the canonical divisibility diagonal cannot realize it; the full bounded Bost--Connes positive cone cannot realize it with exact mixed-prime nullity; Woronowicz affiliation with the same unital source algebra adds no new unbounded objects; and even a genuinely unbounded closed positive form collapses if one canonical resolvent re-enters the represented bounded source algebra.

Passing to a genuinely larger category is therefore not automatically progress. The standard bicommutant is already all of `B(ell^2(N^x))`, where arbitrary diagonal selectors are available, while a nonunital or boundary construction can still fall back under WP-394 if its canonical resolvent lands in `pi(A_BC)`. A von Neumann/modular, form, module or boundary-algebra route earns source-selection credit only if an additional weight, domain, covariance law, expectation, correspondence, or functional-calculus boundary is fixed canonically before the target support is inspected.

A new proposal must state exactly which operator category supplies the missing selector/orientation, why that category and its domain are fixed by the source, whether its canonical bounded transforms remain inside the already-classified source algebra, and how the construction survives the archimedean/polar/global sign theorem. Merely passing from bounded membership to affiliation, choosing a favorable mask, enlarging to weak closure, or exhibiting an unbounded form whose resolvent returns to the bounded source algebra does not answer the source-selection problem.
