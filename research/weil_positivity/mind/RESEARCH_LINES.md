WP-175 removes weak boundary convergence as an ordinary scalar-passive escape: uniformly contractive Schur traces cannot converge to the Gamma phase even distributionally on any open interval. ## Derive mixed-prime incidence before positivity erases it

Pairwise resultants, scalar radial filling, fixed finite arity, refinement-positive kernels, and separated completions do not manufacture irreducible mixed-prime geometry absent upstream. A survivor must carry a source-forced mixed relation before the sign theorem.

## Change the operator category only with an explicit new coercivity theorem

Indefiniteness, singularity, weak convergence, relation-valuedness, domain change, or unbounded renormalization is not itself positivity. Any surviving matrix/operator construction must identify the signed arithmetic datum that persists and prove the new positive/coercive theorem after the category change, rather than infer usefulness merely from escaping one representation.
