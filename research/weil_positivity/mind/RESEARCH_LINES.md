# Weil-positivity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable Weil-positivity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they discriminate among mathematically different mechanisms.

## Couple the exact pointed finite package to the archimedean/polar sector before the selector becomes singular or indefinite

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`, and `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm`.

Finite arithmetic recovery and continuity are no longer the main mysteries. Intrinsic covers force the `n^{-1/2}` normalization, positive cover coboundaries have trace `log n`, and their prime-power Möbius primitives recover the exact finite coefficient. WP-081 strengthens the matched control: the same positive `log n`/prime-power mechanism persists for every fixed shift `c>=0`, so positivity plus the finite coefficient does not single out the covariant representative.

The new boundary is sharper. Regular scale-covariant Jensen responses either collapse to bounded/dyadic traces or, throughout the homogeneous positive family, lose exact Mangoldt support except at the singular inverse-scale endpoint. The natural Fisher/Hellinger variation of the fixed-shift positive family diverges exactly at the covariant origin, while additive shift contrasts are indefinite. A decisive mechanism must therefore form the finite--archimedean coupling before these separated positive families are differentiated/scalarized and prove the final sign independently.

## Derive a genuinely new noncommuting/singular sign mechanism rather than bounded cover calculus or polar repair

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-operator-category-is-a-hard-compatibility-gate` and `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`.

The full-root Hardy channel already showed that bounded and natural unbounded self-adjoint metric repairs yielding positivity contain the channel's own polar sign. WP-085--WP-086 now close the simplest pointed-cover noncommuting escape as well. Cover-range principal angles produce a positive local defect and a reduced log determinant with `log n` on odd degrees, but the result is reference-relative, parity-twisted, and not a positive kernel on degree variables. Moreover every fixed bounded norm-continuous positive two-projection calculus has trace only linear in the degree or bounded; a logarithm appears only through a singular endpoint operation as the principal angle closes.

A surviving operation must therefore be genuinely singular/unbounded with a geometrically forced domain and sign, nonperiodic, higher-cohomological, twisted/family/boundary, or nonseparable finite--archimedean. A decisive result must preserve the exact selector and provide an independent sign theorem rather than merely extract `log n` with `-log` or replace an indefinite channel by its absolute value.

## Classify nonseparable or higher-cohomological completions beyond cover averages and zeroth coinvariants

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm`, `MI-005-cover-coinvariants-classicalize-or-collapse`, and `MI-002-operator-category-is-a-hard-compatibility-gate`.

The most canonical separated globalizations remain sharply constrained. Positive basepoint averaging compatible with all covers is only pointed-plus-Haar. Quotienting the diagonal trace ideal by cover transports remembers only ordinary trace, while the same zeroth Hausdorff coinvariant construction on full standard operator ideals collapses to zero. The new Jensen, Fisher, and principal-angle results reinforce the same lesson: regular positive separated functoriality either classicalizes, loses the selector, or reaches the desired logarithm only at a singular/reference-relative boundary.

The live question is whether a mathematically forced **higher** cohomological object, nontrivial coefficient module, selective geometric quotient, twisted family/boundary form, or nonseparable finite--archimedean coupling retains the exact pointed selector and supplies a new sign. A decisive result must identify the surviving information explicitly and show that it is neither ordinary trace/Möbius scalarization, a bounded two-projection background, nor a repackaged polar/singular logarithmic sign.
