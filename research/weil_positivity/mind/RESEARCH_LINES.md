# Weil-positivity research lines

This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Weil-Positivity evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Force a genuinely one-sided source relation, then prove its arithmetic provenance

WP-391--WP-404 show that selector enlargement, bounded Bost--Connes positivity, affiliation, modular-frequency decomposition, quotient order, cutoff breaking and broad linear readouts do not by themselves produce a Weil-oriented functional. Dense two-sided tangent geometry survives these changes. A sufficiently thin same-shadow slice can be one-sided, but matched nonarithmetic constructions can manufacture the same orientation. Any linear route therefore has two separate gates: the source relation must genuinely change the reachable tangent closure, and that change must be forced by arithmetic structure rather than generic thinness.

## Make any enlarged positive block route survive genuine KMS joining, not programmable fibre data

WP-405--WP-418 progressively close native analytic half-spectrum positivity, central and noncentral cycle holonomy, projective repairs, passive full-fibre decorations, balanced KMS blocks, Hilbert-module path Grams, varying canonical supports/ranges and phase-character refinements as automatic sources of Weil orientation. After the actual equilibrium readout these constructions either gauge away, become spectrally orthogonal, reduce to universal positive Grams, or leave a compatibility mask whose labels are not themselves forced by the source.

WP-421 isolates the first apparent escape from separate invariance: a merely jointly invariant state can pair a source Bohr mode with an opposite active-fibre mode. WP-422 then shows that a countable atomic Gibbs fibre can be programmed with all prime-logarithmic gaps, so finite spectral capacity is not the decisive obstruction.

WP-423 closes that mechanism at the genuine KMS boundary for decoupled product dynamics. For a finite matrix fibre, and more generally the unitization of a countable `c_0` sum of finite matrix blocks with block-preserving inner dynamics, every positive-temperature product-dynamics KMS state is separately invariant in source and fibre. On each nonzero block it factors as a source KMS state times the canonical Gibbs state. Consequently every nonzero source time degree has zero expectation against **every** fibre observable, even one carrying the opposite Bohr frequency. Programming all prime-log frequencies into an atomic auxiliary Hamiltonian therefore does not create a KMS joining.

WP-424 removes commutative non-atomic active fibres as the next product-dynamics escape. In the GNS representation of a positive-temperature KMS state, every invariant central commutative subalgebra is pointwise time-trivial. For a decoupled product `A tensor B` with commutative active fibre `B`, one has

`pi(1 tensor tau_t(b)) = pi(1 tensor b)`

for every fibre observable `b`. Nonzero fibre Bohr modes are therefore GNS-null, so an opposite-frequency fibre observable cannot revive a nonzero source Bohr expectation. This is a central/modular obstruction, not a finite-dimensionality obstruction. It does not freeze the noncentral part of a genuinely noncommutative fibre merely because that fibre has a large center.

The live equilibrium question is therefore narrower. A surviving KMS route must use a **genuinely noncentral noncommutative fibre degree of freedom** not covered by WP-423, or change the architecture through source-derived interacting/non-product dynamics; alternatively it must justify why a merely invariant non-KMS state has an independently source-forced positive Weil interpretation. Any such route must still derive, rather than program, the prime-power/Mangoldt coefficients, archimedean Gamma term, polar normalization, admissible domain and global sign. Larger atomic Bohr support, or replacing the atomic fibre by a commutative non-atomic one under a product flow, is no longer a viable escape.

## Keep source canonicity, order and destination sign separate

A candidate can be canonical yet order-trivial, positive yet universal, gauge-invariant yet absent from the source, or infinite-dimensional yet fully programmable. Future arguments must identify where the asymmetry lives, prove that the source algebra actually carries it, and show that the positive readout retains it after equilibrium normalization. Representation novelty, larger active support or a richer Gram matrix is not evidence if the final scalarization returns to a universal or freely programmable kernel. WP-424 adds a sharper category warning: enlarging cardinality or topological type does not help when the active degrees still lie in the KMS center, because the equilibrium quotient freezes them before frequency matching can occur.
