# Weil-positivity research lines

This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Weil-Positivity evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Force a genuinely one-sided source relation, then prove its arithmetic provenance

WP-391--WP-403 show that selector enlargement, bounded Bost--Connes positivity, ordinary affiliation, modular-frequency decomposition, quotient order, cutoff breaking, closable unbounded linear readouts and finite-codimensional global constraints do not by themselves produce a Weil-oriented linear functional. Dense two-sided order-bounded tangent geometry survives these changes. WP-404 proves the obstruction is geometrically sharp: a sufficiently thin same-shadow slice can be one-sided, but a matched nonarithmetic construction can manufacture the same orientation.

The surviving linear route therefore has two independent gates. The admissible source relation must genuinely change the closure of the reachable two-sided tangent core, and that change must be forced by arithmetic/source structure rather than by generic thinness. A one-sided slice without provenance is not evidence.

## Make any enlarged positive block route carry source-forced non-native holonomy

WP-405 closes the most canonical Bost--Connes one-sided candidate: self-adjoint positivity inside a pointed analytic half-spectrum collapses to the fixed-point algebra. WP-406 shows that a generic positive `2x2` dilation escapes that literal collapse only universally and phase-blindly. WP-407 extends the phase obstruction to every acyclic central-scalar block architecture: forest phases are vertex coboundaries and gauge away, while cycle holonomy is the first scalar phase invariant that positivity can see.

WP-408 tests that surviving invariant against the canonical Bost--Connes source itself and now closes the honest operator-valued version as well. The native commensurability groupoid has nontrivial isotropy only at `rho=0`, which is null for the critical `KMS_1` Haar measure; the scale-retaining dual groupoid is free everywhere. Consequently every normalized group-valued 1-cocycle and every honest unitary groupoid representation, including noncommutative varying-fibre transport, has identity holonomy on the relevant native cycles. Noncentrality cannot manufacture balanced source holonomy when the coefficient assignment genuinely descends to the bare groupoid arrow.

WP-409 then closes the simplest scalar projective repair of that failure. A base-independent `U(1)` multiplier on `Q_+^x` changes projective scale multiplication but leaves every divisibility range projection, its lcm intersection law and the critical KMS-normalized GCD/Poisson Gram exactly unchanged. Its restriction to each one-prime subgroup is gauge-trivial because `H^2(Z,T)=0`, so genuine scalar projective curvature begins only on mixed-prime pairs. Those mixed-prime phases are invisible to the canonical projection Gram; if full prime-relabeling symmetry is imposed, WP-300 already reduces them to the flat or universal fermionic branch. Thus the existence of scalar projective curvature is not enough: the positive readout must actually retain it, and the source must force more than a programmable/generalized-prime phase law.

WP-410 extends the same readout obstruction from base-independent multipliers to central groupoid twists: changing scalar phase data over the same Bost--Connes source/range geometry still leaves the canonical critical range-projection/KMS Gram unchanged. WP-411 then closes the most direct noncentral finite-dimensional repair. If the canonical integer bisections are decorated by arbitrary full-fibre unitary matrices and the fibre dynamics remain passive, the matrix coefficients cancel **before** any state is applied: `V_n^*V_n=1 tensor I`, `V_nV_n^*=E_n tensor I`. Their intersections and normalized `KMS_beta` masses therefore reproduce exactly the scalar GCD/Poisson Gram, even when coefficients at different scales do not commute. Noncommutativity that is discarded by the range-projection map is not a positive resource.

WP-412 shows that merely retaining an isolated off-diagonal bisection observable does not escape either. Every positive-temperature KMS state annihilates a homogeneous analytic observable of nonzero scale degree. In particular, for passive full-unitary sections,

`Phi_beta(V_m^*V_n)=0` for `m!=n`,

and the GNS Gram of the vectors `pi_beta(V_n)Omega_beta` is exactly diagonal. A passive word can have nonzero thermal expectation only when its multiplicative scale degree balances to one, `prod_j n_j^(epsilon_j)=1`. Arbitrary matrix decorations can therefore survive algebraically on balanced cycles, but WP-408 now separates those decorations from honest native groupoid transport: if they satisfy the native functorial composition law, the balanced holonomy is again forced to the identity off the Haar-null exceptional unit.

WP-413 closes the canonical equal-scale central-twist readout left between those two boundaries. Alternative positive factorization paths with one total scale share the same Bost--Connes bisection; their relative products lie in the abelian diagonal `C(Z-hat)`. Every state therefore sees only an ordinary phase-correlation Gram. In the scale-only projective-multiplier subclass the relative phases are constants, the Gram is rank one, and mixed-prime commutator phase changes matrix entries but not positive-semidefiniteness, determinant, rank or spectrum. Central twisting can retain nontrivial balanced phase data, but this canonical positive readout classicalizes it before any Weil orientation appears.

WP-414 closes the immediate noncentral continuation of that path-Gram escape. For any finite family of passive full-fibre factorization paths with the same total scale, all path operators are isometries on the same Bost--Connes bisection with the same range. Relative to one reference path they therefore factor as

`X_a=X_(a_0)D_a`,

where each `D_a` is a unitary in the unit-space matrix algebra `C(Z-hat,M_d)`. Every state path Gram is consequently the universal unitary-correlation Gram `Phi(D_a^*D_b)`, and on a positive-temperature KMS state the `D_a` lie in the fixed algebra so the readout is specifically tracial. Noncentral holonomy can change the Gram spectrum, but its **positivity remains generic state-Gram positivity**. If the strict Bost--Connes semigroup law is retained, all equal-product paths coincide and the Gram collapses further to rank one. Any nontrivial noncentral path holonomy therefore already imports extra fibre multiplication/cocycle data beyond the strict source semigroup.

WP-415 closes the corresponding ordinary non-full Hilbert-correspondence escape. For any right Hilbert `A`-module and positive functional `phi`, every scalarized module path Gram `G_ab=phi(<xi_a,xi_b>_A)` is positive semidefinite before any arithmetic input. Non-fullness can constrain the available coefficients but does not make the Gram sign selective. Under common generalized range support, path vectors factor through coefficient-algebra partial isometries; if their source projections also agree, those coefficients are corner unitaries and the readout is again a corner-unitary correlation Gram. Equal-degree KMS scalarization is tracial on the fixed coefficient algebra, and a genuinely non-full nonarithmetic proper-corner control already reproduces a continuous family of such positive Grams.

WP-416 closes the most direct native varying-source-support continuation left by WP-415. For homogeneous correspondence paths with canonical Bost--Connes source projections `E_(n_a)` and one common generalized range, the coefficient partial isometries carry definite thermodynamic frequencies. The KMS relation together with `Phi_beta(E_n)=n^(-beta)` forces

`d_a-d_0 = log(n_0/n_a)`.

Hence distinct divisibility supports necessarily lie in distinct time degrees, and KMS invariance annihilates every scalar Gram entry between them. After normalization the path Gram is block diagonal by exact support; within a fixed support it falls back to the universal tracial corner-unitary correlation geometry of WP-415. The native family `xi_n=mu_n^*` makes the collapse explicit: common range and varying canonical supports produce an orthogonal KMS family rather than a new arithmetic positive coupling. Support-frequency locking itself is thermodynamic, so even this exact source law is an obstruction, not a Weil-oriented sign mechanism.

The live enlarged-category route is consequently narrower than “retain off-diagonal fibre data,” “use balanced cycles,” “make holonomy noncommutative,” “pass to a non-full correspondence,” or simply “vary the canonical source support.” Honest native groupoid representations have trivial holonomy; arbitrary passive full-rank decorations are extra programmable structure; central equal-scale paths classicalize to abelian correlations; noncentral full-fibre paths reduce to universal tracial unitary correlations; ordinary non-full correspondence Grams retain the same generic sign; and common-range homogeneous paths with canonical varying divisibility support are KMS-orthogonal. A surviving route must provide **source-forced varying generalized range/incidence, source-forced mixing of time degrees, twisted/projective multiplication, active modular fibre dynamics, a changed scalarization, or an orientation-sensitive non-Gram readout** whose sign is not inherited from Hilbert-module positivity. In every case the extra structure must fail matched nonarithmetic controls and derive the completed finite-plus-archimedean Weil coefficients rather than merely encode programmable phases, supports or frequencies.

## Keep source canonicity, order and destination sign separate

A candidate can be canonical yet order-trivial, one-sided yet nonpositive, positive yet universal, gauge-invariant yet absent from the source, genuinely curved yet invisible to the chosen positive carrier, operator-valued yet scalar again after projection readout, off-diagonal yet annihilated by thermal spectral selection, noncentral/non-full and spectrally variable while its positive sign remains a universal correlation identity, or source-varying yet frequency-orthogonalized by the equilibrium state. Future arguments must therefore identify where the asymmetry lives, which phase, support or fibre data survive self-adjointness and corner gauge, whether the source groupoid/correspondence actually has the twisted or incidence structure needed to carry them, whether source-support variation survives common-range factorization and KMS scalarization, whether the final positive observable retains rather than quotients out that structure, whether its total scale degree survives the chosen equilibrium state, and what additional source relation makes the surviving balanced cycle, support pattern or range incidence selective. Noncentrality, analytic support, thinness, unboundedness, block positivity, graph cyclicity, scalar projective twisting, matrix amplification, generic off-diagonal coherence, bare-groupoid balanced transport, non-fullness, canonical support variation and tracial unitary/corner-unitary correlation Grams remain representation features until a source-specific exclusion theorem survives the destination map.
