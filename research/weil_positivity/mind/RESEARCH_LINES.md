WP-302 now audits the most obvious moving-kernel escape. If the projector moves by a finite-dimensional fixed-generator unitary orbit, every intrinsic finite-jet scalar is constant; relative to fixed bounded source operators, regular local readouts remain bounded and trace-polynomial ones are finite exponential polynomials. They therefore cannot reproduce the logarithmically growing archimedean digamma symbol. But if the projector path is freed from a source law, even the canonical positive Grassmann metric is exactly programmable: a rank-one path with speed `theta'(t)` realizes any prescribed smooth positive density through `g(t)=theta'(t)^2`.

The live route must therefore supply an **independent source theorem** that forces the required motion/readout before comparison with the explicit formula. Plausible exits are source-forced nonhomogeneous or infinite-dimensional/unbounded motion, a singular/nonlocal boundary or cohomological observable with forced singular structure, or a genuinely non-frustration-free finite--archimedean coupling whose global sign follows from an independent theorem. Merely allowing the zero space to move is no longer evidence: homogeneous motion is too rigid and unconstrained motion is too programmable.

## Treat localization, reachability, curvature, source law, completion, readout and sign as distinct gates

WP-300 shows that source-visible local curvature plus exact positive Mangoldt extraction does not supply global Weil positivity. WP-301 adds that rich noncommuting local curvature can still collapse under the final readout: a frustration-free harmonic projector remembers only common kernels and erases nonzero archimedean spectrum. WP-302 adds a separate **motion-law gate**: after making the kernel move, one must prove that the source forces a motion rich enough to cross the bounded homogeneous barrier but rigid enough not to encode the desired Gamma profile by hand.

Every candidate must therefore state which source structure creates the mixed-prime relation, why generalized-prime controls do not reproduce it, how finite and archimedean places couple before positivity is taken, which law determines any parameter-dependent kernel or readout, **which part of the coupled spectrum survives the final observable**, and why the resulting global quadratic form has the Weil sign.

## Use matched controls against universal curvature, common-kernel selectors and programmable motion

The fermionic sign is genuine curvature but not rational-prime-specific curvature. WP-299--WP-302 now give four distinct controls: curvature may be unreachable from the ordinary source, reachable only after enriching the source by a universal Clifford law, non-scalar yet reduced by a frustration-free zero-mode readout to a universal fixed-space predicate, or moving but either bounded by homogeneous finite-dimensional dynamics or freely programmable when no source law fixes the path.
