# Weil-positivity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable Weil-positivity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Derive a source-forced prime-layer correlation law before critical normalization erases it

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation`, `MI-011-prime-ray-marginals-do-not-determine-mixed-prime-coupling`, `MI-012-automorphic-multiplicity-does-not-prevent-global-scalarization`, `MI-013-critical-sector-transport-needs-new-global-arithmetic-structure`.

WP-216--WP-255 show that canonical gauge/Toeplitz/Morita/KMS routes either miss the critical sector, scalarize the needed relation, or cannot pack independent prime channels into a normalized `KMS_1` state.

WP-256--WP-259 give the positive intermediate carrier. Free-prime multiplicity canonically produces the exact diagonal `Lambda(n)/sqrt(n)`, and the permutation-fixed sector restores the Gibbs boundary to `beta=1` without losing that diagonal. WP-260 shows that the canonical bosonic Gibbs/Fisher Hessian inserts the wrong extra occupation factor.

WP-261 shows that coherent collapse into a fixed finite-dimensional boundary is nonclosable at the Weil exponent. WP-262 shows that infinite-dimensional closability is too weak a replacement: any locally finite prime graph gives a bounded positive mixed-composite incidence Gram with the exact critical diagonal, while graph support and edge phases remain programmable.

WP-263 tests whether the source Hamiltonian itself removes that programmability by using complete conditional-Gibbs incidence. It does select a canonical weighted complete graph, but the critical `beta=1` normalization fails exactly on the prime layer: `sum_p 1/p` diverges, and every finite-cutoff unit-diagonal normalization drives each fixed prime-prime correlation to zero. Higher prime-power layers `k>=2` remain nontrivial, so the obstruction is specifically where the Weil prime term is most exposed.

The live theorem must therefore obtain a nonprogrammable prime-layer interaction **before** the divergent conditional normalization, or from a genuinely global quotient, boundary, cohomological, or other source-derived mechanism that keeps the critical half-density without erasing cross-prime coherence. After source selection, the completed Weil orientation and Gamma/polar sectors remain separate gates.

## Treat coefficient sourcing, criticality, interaction, closability, source selection, prime-layer normalization, and Weil orientation separately

The Fock hierarchy solves coefficient sourcing and symmetric-sector criticality. Finite-dimensional mixing fails closability; arbitrary infinite-dimensional incidence is closable but programmable; and the most direct Hamiltonian-selected complete incidence becomes diagonal on the critical prime layer. A successful construction must explain where its nontrivial prime-prime law comes from and why the critical normalization preserves rather than washes it out.
