## Cross beyond one-mode critical Bost--Connes positivity without losing the arithmetic datum

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm`, `MI-039-enriched-semigroups-inherit-obstructed-scalar-shadows-or-universal-lifts` through `MI-059-pointwise-local-boundary-nullity-collapses-to-the-mangoldt-ray`, `MI-060-strongly-local-markov-forms-either-reject-mixed-shells-or-become-unit-cell-invisible`, `MI-061-logarithmic-interface-current-classicalizes-to-ramanujan-orthogonality`, `MI-062-stationary-positive-interface-selection-is-an-explicit-spectral-mask`, `MI-063-the-canonical-divisibility-diagonal-cannot-hide-a-prime-power-selector`, `MI-064-the-full-bounded-bost-connes-positive-cone-cannot-isolate-prime-power-support`, `MI-065-woronowicz-affiliation-does-not-enlarge-the-unital-bost-connes-source-algebra`, `MI-066-a-source-valued-resolvent-pulls-positive-unbounded-selectors-back-into-the-bounded-bost-connes-no-go`, `MI-067-a-diffuse-modular-centralizer-can-erase-exact-arithmetic-point-support`, `MI-068-single-modular-frequency-positivity-falls-back-to-the-centralizer`.

WP-391--WP-394 rule out exact positive prime-power selection in the canonical divisibility diagonal, the full bounded Bost--Connes positive cone, Woronowicz affiliation with the same unital algebra, and positive unbounded forms whose canonical resolvent returns to that represented source algebra.

WP-395 tests a genuine category change. The critical `KMS_1` modular centralizer is diffuse `L^infinity(hat Z,m_Haar)`, so the standard countable arithmetic orbit is Haar-null. Exact prime-power support there is either the zero operator or, if encoded only through point values on the orbit, not an operator invariant.

WP-396 closes the most immediate noncentral escape from that result. For a homogeneous modular/gauge mode `x in M_r`, its positive squares `x^*x` and `xx^*`, its modulus and its support projections all lie in the zero-frequency centralizer. If `r!=1`, the spectral subspace itself contains no nonzero positive operator. The same reduction holds for suitably homogeneous affiliated operators: `T^*T` and `|T|` are centralizer-affiliated. Thus moving one frequency away from the centralizer and then applying the canonical positivity operation loses that frequency before it can rescue exact arithmetic point support.

The remaining critical-factor route must therefore be genuinely multi-frequency or use another category in which positivity is not merely the modulus of one homogeneous mode. Cross-frequency terms in `(sum x_r)^*(sum x_r)` can remain noncentral; continuous-core/crossed-product constructions, one-sided correspondences, nonunital source boundaries or finite--archimedean assemblies also remain logically open. These are surviving categories only, not evidence that they work.

The live test is sharper: identify a source-forced construction whose positive form retains arithmetic information after the relevant quotient/measure class **and** whose canonical positivity operation does not project the useful source frequency back into a previously classified category. Merely entering one noncentral modular spectral subspace is closed.

## Keep selector existence, source selection, operator category, measure class and destination sign distinct

Category enlargement can fail by being too programmable, by returning through bounded transforms, by erasing the arithmetic orbit as null, or by losing the noncentral datum under positivity itself. A new proposal must state which category fixes the selector/orientation, which arithmetic data remain invariant there, how positivity is formed, where each frequency lands under adjoint multiplication, and how the final archimedean/global sign theorem is obtained.
