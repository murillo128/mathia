# Weil-positivity research lines

This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Weil-Positivity evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Force a genuinely one-sided source relation, then prove its arithmetic provenance

WP-391--WP-404 show that selector enlargement, bounded Bost--Connes positivity, affiliation, modular-frequency decomposition, quotient order, cutoff breaking and broad linear readouts do not by themselves produce a Weil-oriented functional. Dense two-sided tangent geometry survives these changes. A sufficiently thin same-shadow slice can be one-sided, but matched nonarithmetic constructions can manufacture the same orientation. Any linear route therefore has two separate gates: the source relation must genuinely change the reachable tangent closure, and that change must be forced by arithmetic structure rather than generic thinness.

## Derive cross-frequency coherence from arithmetic structure rather than from thermodynamic freedom

WP-405--WP-425 progressively close native analytic half-spectrum positivity, central and noncentral cycle holonomy, passive fibre decorations, balanced KMS blocks, path Grams, phase-character refinements and auxiliary-frequency compensation under genuine product KMS dynamics. Positive-temperature KMS states of decoupled product systems are separately invariant, so auxiliary richness cannot orient a nonzero source Bohr mode by itself.

WP-426 gives the opposite canonicity warning: leaving product dynamics is vacuous if the replacement flow is reconstructed from the state one wants to certify. WP-427 shows that even in a general source-preserving KMS extension, insertions from the source relative commutant still annihilate nonzero source Bohr modes. WP-428 then allows genuine noncommutativity but proves that every **Bohr-diagonal positive sandwich** remains source-time invariant; positive sums of homogeneous sandwiches cannot move the critical defect into frequency zero. The exact surviving mechanism is coherent interference between distinct auxiliary Bohr sectors with frequency differences matching the source mode.

WP-429 tests that survivor at the finite faithful Gibbs level and finds the opposite obstruction: unrestricted coherence is **too flexible**. For a fixed faithful Gibbs state `rho`, every positive functional `Tr(sigma .)` is realized by one coherent sandwich with

`b = sigma^(1/2) rho^(-1/2)`

through `omega_rho(b^* a b)=Tr(sigma a)`. Consequently every nonzero self-adjoint zero-mode-free defect on a finite Gibbs block can be made positive or negative by choosing the coherent sandwich, while the Hamiltonian and KMS state remain fixed. Analyticity and finite Bohr decomposition do not remove this programmability in finite dimension. The matched finite Bost--Connes truncations inherit the same sign programmability while retaining the exact critical prime-power coefficients present below the cutoff.

WP-430 closes the thermodynamic-only attempt to select a privileged mixer. If a finite-Gibbs selector `B` is canonical from the thermodynamic pair alone in the minimal sense

`UHU*=H  =>  UBU*=B`,

then `B` belongs to the bicommutant of `H`, hence to `C*(H)`: it is scalar on every energy eigenspace and has only zero Bohr degree. In finite-prime Bost--Connes truncations the occupation energies have simple spectrum by unique factorization, so such a selector is literally diagonal in the arithmetic occupation basis. Sandwiching by it preserves source frequency and the Gibbs state still kills every nonzero Bohr mode.

The finite control therefore leaves a narrow and explicit category. **Thermodynamic naturality alone is too rigid, while unrestricted coherent choice is too programmable.** A viable positive route must derive from additional arithmetic source structure a distinguished nonzero-Bohr coherence rule whose amplitudes and phases are fixed before the Weil sign is inspected. That extra datum must break the stabilizer of the bare Gibbs pair without becoming a target-programmed basis choice. In Bost--Connes terms, a proposed shift, endomorphism, correspondence or other noncommuting source operator must be identified as genuine source data and shown to select the coherent mixer compatibly across finite truncations and the infinite critical limit.

Any successful mechanism must still recover the exact Mangoldt/prime-power coefficients, the archimedean Gamma term, polar normalization, admissible domain and global sign. WP-429--WP-430 do not prove that a source-forced coherent correspondence is impossible; they define its canonicity gate. Coherence has evidentiary value only after the allowed class is narrow enough to defeat the finite Gibbs state-universality construction and broad enough to escape the thermodynamic spectral-functional-calculus collapse.

## Keep source canonicity, order and destination sign separate

A candidate can be canonical yet order-trivial, positive yet universal, gauge-invariant yet absent from the source, or infinite-dimensional yet fully programmable. Future arguments must identify where the asymmetry lives, prove that the source algebra actually carries it, and show that the positive readout retains it after equilibrium normalization.

The current matched-control ladder is now sharp: WP-425 removes auxiliary-fibre richness under product KMS dynamics; WP-426 removes state-selected dynamics; WP-427 removes commuting compensation in source-preserving extensions; WP-428 removes Bohr-diagonal noncommuting positive sandwiches; WP-429 removes arbitrary coherent sandwiches as evidence because they are sign-programmable on finite faithful Gibbs blocks; and WP-430 removes thermodynamic-only canonical selection because naturality forces the selector back into zero Bohr degree. The remaining theorem is specifically a **source-canonicity theorem for arithmetic cross-frequency coherence** together with a positive readout that survives those controls.
