# Weil-positivity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable Weil-positivity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they discriminate among genuinely different positivity mechanisms.

## Find a canonical sign-producing completion of the finite arithmetic data

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`.

The finite Prime-Lattice Mangoldt measure admits exact positive realizations, and Tate supplies the correct global self-duality, but the obvious completions fail: Fourier self-duality is indefinite, Nyman projection positivity is tautological, and the canonical pole/gamma completion leaves the positive Hankel cone. The missing object must therefore couple finite-prime and archimedean/polar data in a form whose sign follows from an independent geometric theorem.

A decisive candidate must specify the completed space and pairing before invoking RH, derive the prime and archimedean terms from the same construction, and prove nonnegativity without inserting the Weil functional or the zero divisor as an unexplained kernel. A proof that every natural completion in a clearly defined class necessarily acquires an indefinite direction would be equally substantive.

## Derive, rather than choose, a singular bridge from exact Prime-Flute geometry to Weil scale

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-operator-category-is-a-hard-compatibility-gate`.

The canonical exact Prime-Flute Schiffer coupling is trace class and indefinite as a kernel, whereas the exact finite-Weil operator is not Hilbert--Schmidt. Standard bounded positive repairs therefore cannot connect the two. Any surviving Prime-Flute contribution must pass through a category-changing operation such as a singular boundary map, dynamical propagation, quotient/cohomological construction, or unbounded weighting.

The decisive test is not merely to manufacture the correct tail. The operation must be canonically forced by the geometry, its domain and positivity theorem must be independent of the desired zeta conclusion, and its output must reproduce the finite arithmetic scale while surviving the all-composite and other matched controls already established in the source branches.
