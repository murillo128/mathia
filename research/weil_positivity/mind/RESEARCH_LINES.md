# Weil-positivity research lines

This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Weil-Positivity evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Force a genuinely one-sided source relation, then prove its arithmetic provenance

WP-391--WP-404 show that selector enlargement, bounded Bost--Connes positivity, affiliation, modular-frequency decomposition, quotient order, cutoff breaking and broad linear readouts do not by themselves produce a Weil-oriented functional. Dense two-sided tangent geometry survives these changes. A sufficiently thin same-shadow slice can be one-sided, but matched nonarithmetic constructions can manufacture the same orientation. Any linear route therefore has two separate gates: the source relation must genuinely change the reachable tangent closure, and that change must be forced by arithmetic structure rather than generic thinness.

## Make any enlarged positive block route carry source-forced compatibility, not programmable fibre data

WP-405--WP-418 progressively close native analytic half-spectrum positivity, central and noncentral cycle holonomy, projective repairs, passive full-fibre decorations, balanced KMS blocks, Hilbert-module path Grams, varying canonical supports/ranges and phase-character refinements as automatic sources of Weil orientation. After the actual equilibrium readout these constructions either gauge away, become spectrally orthogonal, reduce to universal positive Grams, or leave a compatibility mask whose labels are not themselves forced by the source.

WP-422 strengthens the programmability obstruction beyond finite active support. Even with a countably infinite active fibre, the Bohr support remains countable and its admissible ratios can be programmed; enlarging the active support to an infinite countable family therefore does not by itself create rigidity. A selective positive block must impose **common-anchor compatibility, incidence geometry, sector restrictions, or another cross-fibre relation that the source forces simultaneously across the active family**. Cardinality alone is not such a relation.

The live question is to identify a source-derived compatibility law that survives KMS scalarization and excludes matched programmable fibres. A candidate may use genuinely non-character incidence, source-forced mixing of time degrees, retained twisted/projective multiplication, active modular fibre dynamics, an orientation-sensitive non-Gram observable, or a canonical finite--archimedean coupling, but the labels and anchors must be derived rather than chosen to fit the desired Weil sign.

## Keep source canonicity, order and destination sign separate

A candidate can be canonical yet order-trivial, positive yet universal, gauge-invariant yet absent from the source, or infinite-dimensional yet fully programmable. Future arguments must identify where the asymmetry lives, prove that the source algebra actually carries it, and show that the positive readout retains it after equilibrium normalization. Representation novelty, larger active support or a richer Gram matrix is not evidence if the final scalarization returns to a universal or freely programmable kernel.
