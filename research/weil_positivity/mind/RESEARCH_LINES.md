## Couple the exact finite package to the archimedean/polar sector before positive completion and singular endpoint selection separate

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`, `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm`, and `MI-006-finite-mass-positive-completion-forces-mixed-prime-coupling-and-critical-roughness`.

Finite arithmetic recovery is no longer the main mystery. Exact covers force critical normalization and positive finite selectors, while WP-104--WP-106 show that the strongest regular cover-Jensen route has a complete boundary model: the positive Jensen defect has an extensive universal trace density, its bulk symbol is alias relative entropy, exact `log n` forces the singular zero-frequency state, and the cover Markov semigroup itself drives every regular state to that endpoint. The endpoint is canonical for cover dynamics but still universal, nonclosable, and missing the archimedean/polar sector.

Meanwhile WP-096--WP-108 show that exact all-prime positivity requires mixed-prime completion and critical roughness. Correlations can repair positivity and even Haar measure class, but not standard Fisher, KL, or intrinsic Kronecker-flow density energy. The live question is therefore a finite--archimedean coupling formed **before** sparse selector, positive completion, and endpoint scalarization become separate operations.

A mere regularization of the divergent critical energy needs an independently derived sign and normalization.

## Classify non-passive, renormalized, or higher-cohomological alternatives beyond the prime-torus completion

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-005-cover-coinvariants-classicalize-or-collapse`, `MI-002-operator-category-is-a-hard-compatibility-gate`, and `MI-006-finite-mass-positive-completion-forces-mixed-prime-coupling-and-critical-roughness`.

The remaining categories must genuinely change the architecture: non-passive relative/off-diagonal maps, higher semigroup cohomology or coefficient modules, domain-changing boundary constructions, renormalized relative information geometries with independently forced reference, or finite--archimedean objects in which the prime torus is only one sector.

A decisive negative would show that one of these enlarged canonical categories still factors through the trace/endpoint/completion/regularity controls already known.
