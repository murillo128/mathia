WP-325--WP-327 close the direct scalar additive-semigroup escape across Muraki's five natural products. Classical, free, monotone and anti-monotone divisibility reject the exact pole-normalized Mangoldt law, while Boolean divisibility accepts every probability law and is therefore nonselective.

WP-328 tests the two closest enriched enlargements. Divisibility descends through every semigroup homomorphism. Since the exact Mangoldt law is freely indecomposable, no bi-freely infinitely divisible planar law can retain it as either scalar marginal, and no c-freely infinitely divisible pair can retain it as the free reference coordinate. Moving the source to the outer c-free coordinate with trivial reference gives the opposite failure: `(mu,delta_0)` is c-freely infinitely divisible for every scalar law because the convolution reduces to universal Boolean convolution.

WP-329 closes the weakest operator-valued escape without incorrectly forcing scalar free convolution through scalarization. At every finite source cutoff, the exact law is the scalarization of a deterministic element of the amalgamation algebra `B_R=C(Sigma_R)` with `E=id`; the `B_R`-valued point law is freely infinitely divisible simply because the deterministic coordinate divides inside the base algebra. The same operator-valued law survives arbitrary changes of the scalar weights. Algebraically, the full source has the universal lift `B=C[t]`, `X=t`, with every higher `B`-valued cumulant zero; all arithmetic sits only in the scalar state `tau_s` used to read out the law.

WP-330 closes the immediate nondeterministic repair. A direct-sum amalgamated variable can contain a genuine centered semicircular fluctuation with nonzero completely positive covariance while the scalarization state annihilates the entire fluctuation summand. The scalar moments then remain exactly the Mangoldt law, and changing the scalar weights still changes the source readout without changing the enriched noisy law. Thus **nonzero covariance, higher cumulants or nondeterminism are not source resources unless the destination readout sees the ideal they generate**.

A surviving enriched construction must therefore satisfy two separate gates. Arithmetic must enter non-deterministic mixed/operator data before scalarization, and the actual scalar/Weil readout must couple nontrivially to the source-dependent fluctuation ideal. Faithfulness of the readout on that positive ideal is a natural anti-vacuity condition, but not yet a sufficient sign theorem. The same architecture must still reproduce the archimedean and global Weil terms and make positivity nonautomatic on matched controls.

Genuinely source-coupled operator-valued free geometry, nondegenerate c-free first-coordinate constructions, deformed/multiplicative structures and direct finite--archimedean couplings remain open. Each proposal must identify exactly which scalar shadows survive, which mixed data carry arithmetic, which fluctuation ideal reaches the readout, and why positivity is neither impossible by shadow descent nor automatic on matched controls.

## Separate source fingerprint from source-selective sign

Shifted-lattice support, Mangoldt coefficients, exact multipoint entries, finite jets and prime-power atom gaps are source-specific. The sign provenance can still be universal, the desired positive architecture can fail because source specificity descends into an incompatible scalar shadow, or an enriched positive object can be universal while the source is stored only in its scalarization state or in a fluctuation sector killed by that state.

WP-322--WP-324 exhibit universal resolvent-Gram/Hankel signs. WP-325--WP-327 exhibit scalar divisibility rejection versus Boolean universality. WP-328 adds homomorphic-shadow obstructions in enriched categories. WP-329 adds the deterministic operator-valued failure: a freely divisible object can exist for every scalar source when the enriched law is deterministic and all specificity is deferred to the readout state. WP-330 shows the failure persists with genuine operator-valued noise whenever scalarization annihilates its fluctuation ideal.

Future arguments should state the precise arithmetic feature that a broad matched class cannot fake while preserving a genuinely nonautomatic sign theorem. A source-specific obstruction to positivity is not a substitute for a positive source theorem, and enriched mixed data are not source-selective when their entire source-dependent sector vanishes in the destination quotient/readout.
