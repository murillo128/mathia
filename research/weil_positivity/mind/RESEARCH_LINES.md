## Force source-specific finite--archimedean structure beyond universal empty-side tangents and noncompact single-atom support scaling

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation` through `MI-029-the-mangoldt-atom-mass-scale-is-not-a-uniform-isolated-pole-scale`.

WP-293--WP-313 close the natural fixed scalar gauge/Schur/Julia orbit. The critical Mangoldt source has the correct logarithmic mass class but the wrong-sign source-specific curvature. Fixed-interior Schur stripping inserts a larger positive `log(y)/y` term; finite fixed-boundary Julia reduction either does the same or worsens the positive curvature; an infinite fixed-node chain cannot repair this whenever the relevant high-energy invariant is stable under the chain limit.

WP-314 tests the canonical nonuniform empty-side escape. A regular Julia node moving to `X->infinity` and observed on scale `z=X zeta` converges, after unit-log normalization, to the same boundary layer as the unit-density logarithmic control. The arithmetic remainder disappears, while fixed spectral scales degenerate to a constant. Noncommutation of limits is therefore insufficient when the moving tangent is universal.

WP-315 tests the most obvious support-side replacement. Around a prime pole, the intrinsic Mangoldt mass is `a_p~log p/p`. But bounded prime clusters occur on a much smaller logarithmic spacing scale. After translating by `log p` and scaling by `a_p`, arbitrarily many comparable prime atoms collapse into every fixed neighborhood; the normalized positive measures are not uniformly locally bounded and the Herglotz field itself has unbounded limsup response at mass-scale height. Along bounded triples, both immediately adjacent support gaps are `o(a_p)`.

The scalar moving-node route is therefore bracketed by two opposite failures:

`empty-side bulk scale -> universal arithmetic-blind tangent`,

`support-side single-atom mass scale -> nonprecompact multi-pole collapse`.

A viable scalar continuation must use **additional source geometry**: the actual neighboring gap scale, a canonical cluster/multipole normalization, or another source-dependent scale on which arithmetic survives without losing Pick positivity or the Gamma discriminator. Otherwise the construction must leave the audited scalar class through operator/matrix-valued boundary structure, generalized resolvents, noninvertible maps, an added archimedean channel or nonseparable finite--archimedean coupling.

## Treat mass class, curvature orientation, transform admissibility, limit stability, tangent universality and local compactness as separate gates

Matching `log y` establishes only the infinite-mass class. Producing a nonuniversal `y^-2` coefficient establishes arithmetic sensitivity but not the correct orientation. WP-307--WP-313 show that admissible positive scalar changes can preserve the wrong invariant, contaminate the Gamma scale or fail under unstable infinite limits.

WP-314 adds the universality gate: a nonuniform rescaling can erase the arithmetic remainder. WP-315 adds a different local-compactness gate: even a source-specific pole scale can fail because nearby atoms collapse faster than the chosen normalization. A proposed moving operation must therefore identify not just a source-dependent scale but a locally controlled **collective geometry** at that scale, and show that the resulting positive object remains nonuniversal for the right reason.

## Use matched controls against universal leading scale, post-hoc recoding and moving-limit classicalization

Unit-density positive measures control the leading logarithm and the canonical empty-side moving Julia layer. Prime-cluster subsequences now provide the complementary support-side stress test: a one-pole approximation must survive arbitrarily dense normalized clusters or explicitly replace the one-pole model by a collective construction.

Future constructions must alter the geometry or realization before final scalar comparison and prove both **positivity/admissibility** and **source-specific survival with a controlled local normalization**. A universal tangent, an unstable single-atom chart or a post-hoc source recoding is not a finite--archimedean completion.
