## Cross the boundary without classicalizing to a positive Nyman Gram, or delay mixed-shell selection

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm`, `MI-039-enriched-semigroups-inherit-obstructed-scalar-shadows-or-universal-lifts` through `MI-057-exact-mixed-shell-nullity-collapses-every-closable-radial-positive-form`, and `MI-058-finite-interior-riesz-selectors-collapse-to-the-mangoldt-line`.

WP-374--WP-383 show that exact mixed-shell nullity collapses every fixed-interior response: finite packets and then every compact-open-continuous Banach/Hilbert-valued linear response reduce the primitive-shell family to one Mangoldt line. This makes a genuine boundary or scale-varying response, or delayed selection after a finite--archimedean interaction, necessary.

WP-384 tests the canonical boundary escape rather than assuming it is useful. For a fixed base `m>1` and fresh prime `q`, the critical shell `V_{m,q}=F_{mq}/sqrt(mq)` tends to zero on every compact subdisk while its pointed `D_1` energy has a strictly positive limit. The escape is real, but its Gram limit is the classical Nyman modulus-square density multiplied by the finite Euler/Jordan factor
`prod_{p|m}|1-p^(-1/2+i tau)|^2`.
Mixed shells with `Lambda(mq)=0` retain positive energy, and no Gamma or polar completion appears.

The surviving route therefore needs more than boundary sensitivity. A source-forced boundary/scale-varying channel must avoid reduction to this finite-Euler Nyman carrier and must retain the finite data in a form that can couple with the archimedean/polar terms with the correct Weil sign. Alternatively, exact mixed-shell selection must be postponed until after such a coupling. The first test for any boundary candidate is now two-stage: it must genuinely escape compact-open continuity **and** survive the WP-384 classicalization/mixed-shell-positive control.

## Keep selector existence, topology, classicalization and destination sign distinct

WP-381 proves that a bounded exact Mangoldt selector exists in the pointed geometry; WP-382--WP-383 show that exact selection can still collapse a large interior response space to rank one; WP-384 shows that simply moving to a genuine boundary layer can preserve nonzero energy while still producing the wrong positive resource class. A viable Weil-positive construction must explain where mixed-prime information is retained, why its boundary energy is not merely a Nyman modulus square with finite Euler factors, and how the surviving finite coefficient couples to the Gamma/polar completion at the destination scale.
