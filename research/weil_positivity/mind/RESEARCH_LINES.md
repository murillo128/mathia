WP-319--WP-323 extend the matched-control closure through bounded-gap Julia families, exact finite multipoint Pick matrices and finite jets, all reducing to universal positive-source Gram structure. WP-324 shows the analogous translation/Laplace sign mechanisms are universal or have the wrong orientation.

WP-325--WP-327 close the direct scalar additive-semigroup escape across Muraki's five natural products. Classical, free, monotone and anti-monotone divisibility reject the exact pole-normalized Mangoldt law, while Boolean divisibility accepts every probability law and is therefore nonselective.

WP-328 tests the two closest enriched enlargements. Divisibility descends through every semigroup homomorphism. Since the exact Mangoldt law is freely indecomposable, no bi-freely infinitely divisible planar law can retain it as either scalar marginal, and no c-freely infinitely divisible pair can retain it as the free reference coordinate. Moving the source to the outer c-free coordinate with trivial reference gives the opposite failure: `(mu,delta_0)` is c-freely infinitely divisible for every scalar law because the convolution reduces to universal Boolean convolution.

WP-329 closes the weakest operator-valued escape without incorrectly forcing scalar free convolution through scalarization. At every finite source cutoff, the exact law is the scalarization of a deterministic element of the amalgamation algebra `B_R=C(Sigma_R)` with `E=id`; the `B_R`-valued point law is freely infinitely divisible simply because the deterministic coordinate divides inside the base algebra. The same operator-valued law survives arbitrary changes of the scalar weights. Algebraically, the full source has the universal lift `B=C[t]`, `X=t`, with every higher `B`-valued cumulant zero; all arithmetic sits only in the scalar state `tau_s` used to read out the law.

WP-330 closes the immediate nondeterministic repair. A direct-sum amalgamated variable can contain a genuine centered semicircular fluctuation with nonzero completely positive covariance while the scalarization state annihilates the entire fluctuation summand. The scalar moments then remain exactly the Mangoldt law, and changing the scalar weights still changes the source readout without changing the enriched noisy law. Thus **nonzero covariance, higher cumulants or nondeterminism are not source resources when the destination readout kills the ideal they generate**.

WP-331 closes the complementary faithful-readout repair for the same-observable architecture. If `b=E(X)` is the arithmetic base coordinate and the scalar law of self-adjoint `X` under `tau circ E` is required to remain exactly the scalar law of `b`, then conditional variance gives `tau(E((X-b)^2))=0`. When `tau` is faithful on that positive sector—as it is for the finite Mangoldt state with strictly positive weights—this forces `E((X-b)^2)=0`; complete positivity then kills every centered `B`-valued moment. If `E` is faithful, `X=b` outright. Exact source-law preservation plus faithful visibility therefore collapses the enriched operator-valued law back to the deterministic one.

Together WP-330--WP-331 produce a sharp readout dichotomy for this route. If the readout is nonfaithful, a genuine fluctuation can hide in a null ideal and remain nonselective. If the same readout faithfully sees centered positive variance while the scalar source law is held fixed, that variance must vanish in the conditional-expectation geometry. The surviving operator-valued direction must therefore change the interface before scalarization: arithmetic must live in mixed data whose final response is not merely the unchanged scalar law of `E(X)`, or the finite and archimedean sectors must interact through a compression, pairing, boundary response, transfer function, joint finite--archimedean object, or another source-dependent observable.

Genuinely source-coupled operator-valued free geometry, nondegenerate c-free first-coordinate constructions, deformed/multiplicative structures and direct finite--archimedean couplings remain open only when they evade that same-observable dichotomy. Each proposal must identify exactly which scalar shadows survive, which mixed data carry arithmetic, how the readout couples to them, whether preserving the old scalar law is actually required, and why positivity is neither impossible by shadow descent nor automatic on matched controls.

## Separate source fingerprint from source-selective sign

Shifted-lattice support, Mangoldt coefficients, exact multipoint entries, finite jets and prime-power atom gaps are source-specific. The sign provenance can still be universal, the desired positive architecture can fail because source specificity descends into an incompatible scalar shadow, or an enriched positive object can be universal while the source is stored only in its scalarization state or in a fluctuation sector killed by that state.

WP-322--WP-324 exhibit universal resolvent-Gram/Hankel signs. WP-325--WP-327 exhibit scalar divisibility rejection versus Boolean universality. WP-328 adds homomorphic-shadow obstructions in enriched categories. WP-329 adds the deterministic operator-valued failure: a freely divisible object can exist for every scalar source when the enriched law is deterministic and all specificity is deferred to the readout state. WP-330 shows that the failure persists with genuine operator-valued noise whenever scalarization annihilates its fluctuation ideal. WP-331 shows that making the same scalarization faithful while insisting on the unchanged source law does not repair the mechanism: positivity of conditional variance collapses the centered operator-valued law instead.

Future arguments should state the precise arithmetic feature that a broad matched class cannot fake while preserving a genuinely nonautomatic sign theorem. A source-specific obstruction to positivity is not a substitute for a positive source theorem, and enriched mixed data are not source-selective when they are either annihilated by the destination quotient/readout or forced to vanish by exact-law preservation plus faithful positive visibility.
