## Use noncentral arithmetic information without asking positivity to erase its centralizer support

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm`, `MI-039-enriched-semigroups-inherit-obstructed-scalar-shadows-or-universal-lifts` through `MI-059-pointwise-local-boundary-nullity-collapses-to-the-mangoldt-ray`, `MI-060-strongly-local-markov-forms-either-reject-mixed-shells-or-become-unit-cell-invisible`, `MI-061-logarithmic-interface-current-classicalizes-to-ramanujan-orthogonality`, `MI-062-stationary-positive-interface-selection-is-an-explicit-spectral-mask`, `MI-063-the-canonical-divisibility-diagonal-cannot-hide-a-prime-power-selector`, `MI-064-the-full-bounded-bost-connes-positive-cone-cannot-isolate-prime-power-support`, `MI-065-woronowicz-affiliation-does-not-enlarge-the-unital-bost-connes-source-algebra`, `MI-066-a-source-valued-resolvent-pulls-positive-unbounded-selectors-back-into-the-bounded-bost-connes-no-go`, `MI-067-a-diffuse-modular-centralizer-can-erase-exact-arithmetic-point-support`, `MI-068-single-modular-frequency-positivity-falls-back-to-the-centralizer`, `MI-069-positive-multi-frequency-data-cannot-outrun-its-centralizer-support-shadow`.

WP-391--WP-394 rule out exact positive prime-power selection in the canonical divisibility diagonal, the full bounded Bost--Connes positive cone, Woronowicz affiliation with the same unital source algebra, and positive unbounded forms whose canonical resolvent returns to that represented algebra.

WP-395 changes category to the critical factor but finds a diffuse centralizer `L^infinity(hat Z,m_Haar)` in which the standard countable arithmetic orbit is Haar-null. Exact point support is then either zero as an operator or representative-dependent rather than invariant.

WP-396 shows that one nonzero modular/gauge frequency does not fix the problem: the positive square, modulus and support projections of a homogeneous mode return to the zero-frequency centralizer, and a nonzero spectral subspace contains no positive operator of its own.

WP-397 closes the most immediate multi-frequency interference escape for bounded positive elements. If `E` is the critical gauge expectation, `a>=0`, `d=E(a)` and `p=s(d)`, then every Fourier coefficient satisfies `a_r=p a_r p`. For finite sums `y=sum_r x_r`,

`E(y^*y)=sum_r x_r^*x_r`.

Cross-frequency terms can remain noncentral, but they cannot cancel the positive centralizer floor or support nonzero arithmetic data outside its measurable support. In particular, a bounded positive element whose canonical diagonal support is confined to the Haar-null prime-power orbit must vanish.

The surviving bounded critical-factor route must therefore accept a nonzero diffuse centralizer shadow and use genuinely nonzero-frequency correlations inside that support as the arithmetic datum. A successful construction must show that those correlations are source-forced, survive the relevant quotient, and produce the required global/archimedean sign. Exact atomic selection cannot be recovered by destructive multi-frequency interference.

Other categories remain logically open: continuous-core or crossed-product constructions, one-sided correspondences, nonunital source boundaries, genuinely unbounded nonhomogeneous forms with controlled domains, or finite--archimedean assemblies. They count as new hypotheses only if the positivity/readout operation does not reduce them to a previously classified bounded centralizer shadow.

## Keep selector existence, support, frequency, measure class and destination sign distinct

Category enlargement can fail by being too programmable, returning through bounded transforms, erasing the arithmetic orbit as null, projecting a single frequency under positivity, or forcing every multi-frequency coefficient to live over the same diffuse centralizer support. These are different obstructions.

A new proposal must state which arithmetic information remains invariant, how positivity is formed, what its fixed-point component is, where every useful frequency is supported, and how the final Weil sign theorem uses information not already quotiented away. Noncentrality by itself is no longer enough; the surviving datum must matter after the centralizer-support audit.
