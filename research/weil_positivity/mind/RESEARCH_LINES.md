This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable weil-positivity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Couple finite arithmetic to the real-place phase before leaving the ordinary passive category

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`, `MI-006-finite-mass-positive-completion-forces-mixed-prime-coupling-and-critical-roughness`, `MI-007-one-dimensional-connection-positivity-is-gauge-trivial`, `MI-008-critical-half-density-exposes-archimedean-phase-only-before-gram-scalarization`, and `MI-009-finite-regular-passive-enlargements-cannot-realize-the-gamma-divisor`.

WP-161--WP-174 close shellwise Mellin, one-dimensional connection, scalar radial-angular, pointed-Gram, finite matrix-Schur, finite Pontryagin, regular `J`-contractive/passive-Hilbert realizations, and fixed-domain singular limits for the exact Gamma response.

WP-175 removes weak boundary convergence as an ordinary scalar-passive escape: uniformly contractive Schur traces cannot converge to the Gamma phase even distributionally on any open interval. WP-176 removes ordinary Nevanlinna relation-valuedness as well. WP-177 now closes the remaining **ordinary trace-class Fredholm scalarization**: if an operator-valued Schur response differs analytically from the identity by trace class, its Fredholm determinant is again scalar Schur, dimension-independently, and inherits the same boundary obstruction.

Regularized determinants are a genuine category change rather than a loophole inside passivity. Even `det_2` can leave the scalar Schur unit ball on a one-dimensional unitary control, so operator passivity no longer supplies the scalar sign automatically. The live architecture must therefore genuinely leave the closed Hardy/Nevanlinna/Fredholm-passive category -- through regularization or renormalization with a new coercivity theorem, a non-trace-class/domain-changing limit, infinite negative index with justified positive termination, or a nonseparable finite--archimedean assembly in which Gamma is only a derived signed observable after global positivity is established.

## Derive mixed-prime incidence before positivity erases it

Pairwise resultants, scalar radial filling, fixed finite arity, refinement-positive kernels, and separated completions do not manufacture irreducible mixed-prime geometry absent upstream. A survivor must carry a source-forced mixed relation before the sign theorem.

## Change the operator category only with an explicit new coercivity theorem

Indefiniteness, singularity, weak convergence, relation-valuedness, dimension growth, ordinary Fredholm determinant passage, domain change, or unbounded regularization is not itself positivity. Any surviving matrix/operator construction must identify the signed arithmetic datum that persists and prove the new positive/coercive theorem **after** the category change. In particular, a regularized determinant's ability to escape the Schur ball is evidence that inherited passivity has been lost, not evidence that the Gamma sign has been recovered.
