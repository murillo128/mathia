# Weil-positivity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable Weil-positivity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they discriminate among mathematically different mechanisms.

## Couple the exact pointed finite package to the archimedean/polar sector before positivity completes it with mixed-prime data

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`, `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm`, and `MI-006-finite-mass-positive-completion-forces-mixed-prime-coupling-and-critical-roughness`.

Finite arithmetic recovery and continuity are no longer the main mysteries. Intrinsic covers force the `n^{-1/2}` normalization, positive cover coboundaries have trace `log n`, and their prime-power Möbius primitives recover the exact finite coefficient. Fixed-shift positive controls preserve the same coefficient, while regular scale-covariant Jensen responses either collapse to bounded/dyadic traces or lose exact Mangoldt support except at a singular endpoint.

WP-096--WP-097 expose the full algebraic positive-cover boundary. After the critical first-order conjugacy, every finite-valued exact-cover-positive quadratic form is a positive Gram measure on the infinite prime torus. Sparse exact one-prime Weil rays require infinite diagonal self-energy, whereas allowing mixed-prime coefficients restores positivity with a sharp finite threshold.

WP-098--WP-102 show that the mixed completion cannot be treated as disposable. Same-algebra positive/unital quotients preserve mixed products, passive positive auxiliary elimination moves in the wrong order direction, and the independent product completion is singular at the critical exponent. Correlations can restore Haar equivalence, so measure class is not the robust obstruction; the robust boundary is regularity: every exact critical completion has infinite standard cylindrical Fisher energy, and any absolutely continuous one lies below the Zygmund `L(log L)^(1/2)` endpoint.

The live question is therefore a finite--archimedean coupling **before** sparse selector and positive completion are separated. A decisive mechanism must derive its mixed-prime correlations, archimedean/polar interaction, and final sign in one geometry, while accepting or replacing the critical roughness rather than deleting the mixed modes after the fact.

## Derive a genuinely transforming global/singular sign mechanism rather than a fixed coherent auxiliary sector

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-operator-category-is-a-hard-compatibility-gate`, `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`, and `MI-006-finite-mass-positive-completion-forces-mixed-prime-coupling-and-critical-roughness`.

The obvious positive reference and auxiliary repairs are closed much further. WP-092 shows that even an infinite-dimensional fixed auxiliary Hilbert space with unbounded self-adjoint dynamics freezes to one collective block under refinement-coherent bounded coupling; standard relative resolvent, determinant, and spectral-shift responses are then tower-independent. WP-093 classifies every fixed finite-band exact-cover-positive form as a weighted Dirichlet cone whose critical ray is classical Cesaro/continuous-dual-Hahn geometry. WP-094 collapses positive block-Toeplitz infinite-range covariance to a singular nonclosable endpoint anchor, and WP-095 proves that adding the entire finite-band positive cone cannot regularize that anchor.

WP-098--WP-099 now also rule out the two most canonical post-completion auxiliary reductions: a positive conditional expectation/Markov quotient that keeps the prime coordinates cannot delete their products, and a passive Schur/Dirichlet reduction cannot turn a finite-mass mixed completion into the sparse Weil carrier. WP-102 shows that remaining inside the standard product-coordinate first-order geometry at the critical exponent carries infinite Fisher energy regardless of how correlations are chosen.

A surviving global operation must therefore transform with refinement, change domain or regularity category, use a genuinely noncoherent but canonical boundary coupling, be higher-cohomological/twisted, or couple finite and archimedean sectors nonseparably before scalarization. Merely making a fixed auxiliary sector infinite, unbounded, nonlocal, or passively eliminable is not enough.

## Classify non-passive quotients or higher-cohomological alternatives beyond the prime-torus completion

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-005-cover-coinvariants-classicalize-or-collapse`, `MI-002-operator-category-is-a-hard-compatibility-gate`, and `MI-006-finite-mass-positive-completion-forces-mixed-prime-coupling-and-critical-roughness`.

The exact-cover-positive cone is simultaneously broader and more constrained than the earlier finite-reference picture. WP-096--WP-097 prove that genuine closable infinite-range positive geometry exists and that the critical one-prime rays admit finite positive completion only through mixed-prime Fourier structure. WP-098 closes same-algebra positive deletion, WP-099 closes passive positive auxiliary elimination, and WP-100--WP-102 classify the regularity price of keeping the completed state.

The accepted local clue `CLUE-mixed-prime-positive-completion-selector-quotient` is therefore narrowed to operations that genuinely change the architecture before the closed reductions apply: a non-passive relative/off-diagonal map, a higher semigroup cohomology or coefficient module, a domain-changing boundary construction, or a finite--archimedean object in which the prime-torus carrier is only one sector rather than the final positive state.

A decisive positive result must identify what information survives, prove why the mixed correlations and critical roughness are intrinsic, and derive the final sign without hand-programmed Fourier deletion. A decisive negative would show that even one of these enlarged but canonical categories still factors through the trace/coinvariant, passive-order, or Fisher-energy obstructions already known.
