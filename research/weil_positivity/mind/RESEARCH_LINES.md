## Couple the pointed finite positive package to the archimedean/polar sector before sign-changing reduction

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`, and `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm`.

Finite arithmetic recovery and continuity are no longer the main mysteries. The base-point local Dirichlet geometry makes the Mangoldt boundary anchor continuous; intrinsic root covers force the `n^{-1/2}` normalization; and a positive trace-class inverse-scale defect supplies `log p` on primitive prime rays. These are independently derived positive structures.

The live obstruction is global compatibility. The resulting positive prime-ray kernel is Poisson-like, whereas the finite Weil multiplier is obtained by a sign-changing subtraction. Positive shifted-resolvent defects can expose a digamma profile, but exact finite `log n` coefficients force the shift back to zero. A decisive mechanism must therefore form the finite-plus-archimedean/polar object **before** Poisson subtraction or separated shifted-resolvent reduction and prove its sign independently.

## Derive a genuinely new symmetry-breaking metric or form rather than the canonical polar repair

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-operator-category-is-a-hard-compatibility-gate` and `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`.

A decisive result must preserve the exact finite selector rather than merely convert an indefinite operator into `|A|`.

## Classify positive pointed completions beyond the separated Poisson/resolvent family

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm` and `MI-002-operator-category-is-a-hard-compatibility-gate`.

Rotation-invariant positive Hilbert repairs are closed: bounded boundary evaluation excludes the cyclotomic shell functions. The next question is whether an independently forced extension of this pointed topology can couple finite and archimedean data nonseparably while retaining continuity and exact coefficients.

A decisive positive result would derive such a global form and its sign theorem from intrinsic cover/boundary geometry. A decisive negative result would show that every natural pointed extension factors through the same positive Poisson/resolvent data and therefore re-enters the finite-sign or shifted-coefficient obstruction.
