# Weil-positivity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable Weil-positivity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they discriminate among mathematically different mechanisms.

## Couple the exact finite selector to the archimedean/polar sector before determinant, class, or absolute-value reduction

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`, and `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm`.

Finite arithmetic recovery is no longer the main mystery. Canonical positive objects can store `Lambda`, `log p`, and higher Hardy cross-shell information. But the direct completions repeatedly externalize the desired coefficient into a determinant/index, erase it in a cohomological completion, replace the signed full-root channel by an absolute-value-type object, or make the exact anchor functional discontinuous in the natural positive norm.

The live question is to form the finite-plus-archimedean/polar object **before** those reductions. A decisive mechanism must retain the exact finite coefficient and the canonically selected archimedean channel in one nontrivial class/operator/form, make the selector continuous in the chosen completion, and derive its sign independently. A positive determinant whose logarithm is read afterwards, a universal cycle Laplacian, a finite scalar renormalization of the unbounded Hardy anchor, or a polar absolute value does not qualify.

## Derive a genuinely new symmetry-breaking metric or form rather than the canonical polar repair

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-operator-category-is-a-hard-compatibility-gate` and `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`.

The strongest current carriers make the obstruction explicit. On the independently selected full-root `q=2` Hardy channel, every bounded self-adjoint metric repair that yields positivity contains the spectral polar sign; WP-065 shows the same for natural unbounded self-adjoint operator-level metric products. Unboundedness therefore does not by itself create a new sign mechanism.

A surviving operation must use additional geometry that forces a non-polar metric/sign, a genuinely singular form-level construction outside the classified operator-product category, a twisted/family/boundary completion, or a nonseparable finite--archimedean construction. A decisive result must provide its own sign theorem and preserve the exact arithmetic selector rather than merely converting an indefinite operator into `|A|`.

## Find a canonical positive completion in which the exact arithmetic anchor is continuous

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm` and `MI-002-operator-category-is-a-hard-compatibility-gate`.

The positive Hardy Gram form is exact on the algebraic shell span, but the Mangoldt functional is unbounded in that norm and no finite scalar counterterm repairs the canonical completed square. Determine whether another independently forced topology/form can retain the same exact shell selector continuously without simply encoding `L` into the norm by hand.

A decisive positive result would derive such a completion from intrinsic finite--archimedean or boundary geometry and prove a sign theorem there. A decisive negative result would show that every natural completion compatible with the shell operations either keeps the anchor unbounded, collapses the selector, or re-enters the polar-sign obstruction.
