## Find a sign mechanism that survives scalar gauge, divisor matching and arithmetic dressing

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-039-stationary-negative-type-transport-needs-quadratic-drift-for-weil-tail-control` through `MI-049-unitary-moving-compressions-have-no-berry-trace-escape`.

The stationary-geometry results show that the Weil log-square cannot be manufactured for free by oscillatory negative-type structure: a scalar or fixed-positive-functional readout obtains the required quadratic arithmetic direction only when that direction is already present in the quadratic sector. Singular-character tangents merely relocate the same target square to a boundary state.

The Maaß--Selberg/congruence branch gives complementary matched-control obstructions. Positive truncated norms and boundary squares can be divisor-blind; arithmetic congruence scattering can carry independent reciprocal inner factors; and bad-prime logarithmic-derivative blocks have one strict sign. WP-350 closes unitary moving compression, while WP-351 shows that canonical proper-contraction defect positivity is exactly blind to the common unimodular scalar zeta phase.

WP-352 now closes the most direct cross-parameter Berry/Chern repair. Projective eigenspace geometry cannot see a common scalar factor at all; the full scalar Maurer--Cartan term is exact and central; and complex scalar curvature is flat away from the divisor while its singular Poincare--Lelong curvature is exactly the divisor itself. The zero-free reciprocal control `exp(a(s-1/2))g(s)` preserves reciprocal symmetry, critical-line unitarity, zeros/poles, divisor current and all projective geometry while shifting the critical-line phase generator by `a`.

The surviving category is therefore narrower than “use a connection” or “allow nonunitarity.” A successful route must contain a **noncentral/global coupling or independently forced phase anchor before scalar-gauge quotienting**. It must explain why that extra structure is canonical for the arithmetic source, why a reciprocal zero-free rephasing is excluded, and where the required sign comes from without reading the zeta divisor back in as curvature.

## Keep source dependence distinct from sign coercivity

Arithmetic labels, self-adjoint realization, unitary scattering, moving channel choice, contraction defects and projective curvature are all source-dependent structures, yet each admits a matched control that preserves the corresponding positivity/geometry while changing or erasing the divisor-sensitive scalar information. They are not Weil positivity merely because they are canonical, positive or geometric.

The next proposal should state exactly which scalar phase or divisor coordinate survives its quotient, what ramified/rephased control is excluded, and which independent theorem forces the required sign. Algebraic division by known local factors, a reducing subspace, scalar-gauge-blind defects, projective curvature or Poincare--Lelong recovery of the divisor are normalization/representation mechanisms rather than independently generated positivity.
