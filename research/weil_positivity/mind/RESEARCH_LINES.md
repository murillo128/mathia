## Cross the boundary outside pointwise and Markov-jump positive geometry, or delay mixed-shell selection

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm`, `MI-039-enriched-semigroups-inherit-obstructed-scalar-shadows-or-universal-lifts` through `MI-059-pointwise-local-boundary-nullity-collapses-to-the-mangoldt-ray`.

WP-374--WP-383 show that exact mixed-shell nullity collapses every fixed-interior response to one Mangoldt line. WP-384 supplies a canonical boundary escape but classicalizes it into the Nyman modulus-square carrier, and WP-385 closes every closed dilation-invariant positive reweighting because the finite Euler factors are boundedly invertible on the real Mellin axis.

WP-386 closes arbitrary nonstationary pointwise-local positive measures: mixed-shell nullity forces support into `(0,1)`, where every profile equals `Lambda(m)/sqrt(m)`. WP-387 closes the canonical positive pure-jump continuation as well. Fresh mixed shells separate the unit cells of the floor coordinate, so a Beurling--Deny jump measure that annihilates all mixed shells must stay within individual cells; every arithmetic profile is constant there, and the jump sector becomes invisible to every shell. Adding the killing sector restores only the same rank-one Mangoldt Gram.

The surviving boundary route must therefore obtain its extra arithmetic degree of freedom from a genuinely different component: a source-forced strongly local/interface form whose domain contains the shell steps, a non-Markov positive off-diagonal geometry with an independent canonical kernel, a finite-`q` prelimit term lost in WP-384, or a finite--archimedean coupling in which mixed-shell selection and final positivity occur only after assembly. Another pointwise weight or positive Dirichlet jump completion of the same continuum carrier cannot provide the missing channel.

## Keep selector existence, operator category and destination sign distinct

WP-381 proves that a bounded exact Mangoldt selector exists in the pointed geometry; WP-382--WP-383 show that exact selection collapses a large interior response space to rank one. WP-384--WP-387 now separate four boundary categories: the raw Nyman carrier remains too broad, stationary positive reweighting cannot kill mixed shells, pointwise-local positive selection collapses to the Mangoldt ray, and Markov jump geometry becomes completely arithmetic-blind under exact mixed-shell nullity.

None of these results classifies strongly local/interface forms, arbitrary non-Markov positive kernels, finite-prelimit constructions or indefinite finite--archimedean coupling before the final sign theorem. A new mechanism must state which category it inhabits and why its positivity/kernel is source-forced rather than chosen to encode the desired Weil conclusion.
