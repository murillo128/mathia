# Weil-positivity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable Weil-positivity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Build a source-native critical boundary correspondence, not merely a space that can host the singular row

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation`, `MI-011-prime-ray-marginals-do-not-determine-mixed-prime-coupling`, `MI-012-automorphic-multiplicity-does-not-prevent-global-scalarization`, `MI-013-critical-sector-transport-needs-new-global-arithmetic-structure`.

WP-216--WP-266 separate coefficient sourcing, interaction, regulator covariance, critical normalization, and finite-part sign. Hamiltonian diagonal centering gives a regulator-independent trace-class index-one operator below critical scale, but the source-canonical transport restoring the Weil half-density produces a non-square-summable critical row.

WP-267 shows that ordinary linear-relation closure is too destructive: it makes the critical boundary coordinate maximally multivalued and its regular operator part zero, deleting exactly the common-vacuum coherence that carried the critical prime amplitudes and mixed-prime Gram entries.

WP-268 classifies the next category question. The critical row is not continuous on **any finite polynomial Hilbert scale** generated by the logarithmic prime Hamiltonian, nor on the ordinary smooth-vector Fréchet space. No finite resolvent power regularizes it. However the same source Hamiltonian canonically generates the rapid-energy nuclear space

\[
\Phi_{\exp}
=
\bigcap_{a>0}\operatorname{Dom}e^{aH},
\]

on which the row is a continuous distribution. A single exponential Hilbert level becomes sufficient only beyond the sharp threshold `a>1/4`, which shifts normalized prime-event amplitudes back into the ordinary Euler half-plane; the projective-limit rigging avoids choosing one such level.

This is a genuine analytic-category escape from WP-267 but **not** a Weil-positivity bridge. The surviving boundary form is still rank one, has no demonstrated source-forced embedding of the full Weil test class, supplies no Gamma/polar sector, and does not create the required translation/autocorrelation orientation.

The live theorem is therefore stricter than finding a topology in which the critical row exists. It must derive, from the source, a finite--archimedean boundary correspondence on `Phi_exp` or another equally canonical category that preserves mixed-prime coherence, remains regulator-covariant, and produces the Weil orientation before the destination kernel is inserted.

## Treat hostability, critical scaling, mixed-prime coherence, and Weil orientation separately

WP-268 removes a false dichotomy. The critical row is neither an ordinary finite-order singular vector nor mathematically unhostable: it lives naturally in an exponential nuclear rigging. But continuity of a distribution is much weaker than a completed arithmetic positivity theorem.

Future proposals must therefore pass two independent gates: the source must select the analytic category, and the resulting boundary law must still produce the correct global finite--archimedean coupling. Strengthening the topology merely so that an algebraic functional becomes continuous is not evidence unless the downstream Weil structure is also forced.
