## Find a source-forced mixed/nonstationary sign mechanism beyond scalar completion, regular sparsification and stationary radial nullity

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-039-enriched-semigroups-inherit-obstructed-scalar-shadows-or-universal-lifts` through `MI-056-positive-composite-nullity-collapses-stationary-radial-flux-to-zero-frequency`.

WP-356--WP-370 classify finite Hecke, finite--archimedean and componentwise Whittaker scale routes. Genuine arithmetic incidence survives in nonconstant finite modes, but canonical positive squares scalarize critical half-density; common homogeneous globalization separates variables; the unique critical Whittaker scale freezes `nY`; and ordinary positive boundary `L^2` squares the desired amplitude.

WP-371 isolates the prime-power Toeplitz carrier. Independent positive completion of each prime ray pays a divergent scalar diagonal tax. WP-372 shows that this divergence is not a global scalar positivity obstruction once mixed-prime coefficients are allowed: a finite-mass positive completion exists. WP-373 then shows that one cannot recover pure prime-power lanes afterward with a continuous scalar common-line multiplier, because the mixed two-prime frequencies are dense; every finite truncation remains smoothly selectable, so the obstruction is genuinely infinite/topological.

WP-374 closes a complementary radial escape left by the earlier signed cyclotomic-flux program. In logarithmic radius, adjoining a spectator prime turns the shell profile into an exact translation coboundary. If a translation-invariant positive form assigns zero energy to every shell with at least two distinct prime factors, positivity makes those mixed shells radical vectors. Two multiplicatively independent spectator primes then force every surviving prime-power shell to be fixed by all real translations. The signed radial profile has collapsed to zero Mellin frequency before it can supply a sign theorem.

For scalar Bochner forms the collapse is sharp: the entire form on the cyclotomic shell span is the rank-one net-mass square `c Lambda(m)Lambda(n)`. Critical half-density gives the wrong squared logarithmic coefficient, and repairing it by `Lambda^(-1/2)` would insert the desired selector by hand. Thus a common scale-stationary positive radial geometry cannot both keep signed composite flux dynamically available and impose exact composite-shell nullity.

The surviving Weil route must therefore change category in a source-forced way. Viable directions include a nonstationary radial/boundary operator, an indefinite intermediate coupling in which mixed shells remain active until finite and archimedean pieces are assembled, genuinely nonradial multiplicity/global geometry, or a mixed-prime sector whose final positivity theorem uses rather than deletes those coefficients. Returning to a stationary scalar positive norm plus exact composite nullity or a regular scalar post-selector is already classified.

## Keep positivity, mixed-prime freedom, nullspace radicality and selector topology distinct

WP-372 teaches that a divergent sum of local completion costs may disappear when cross-coordinate coefficients are admitted. WP-373 then shows that the resulting mixed sector can be topologically impossible to erase with a continuous scalar selector. WP-374 adds a different rigidity: in a positive form, exact zero energy is not a harmless diagonal condition—it creates radical relations, and source dilation identities can propagate those relations until only an invariant sector remains.

Any new construction should therefore specify which mixed-prime coefficients are source-forced, which mixed/composite channels remain non-null through global assembly, what covariance or stationarity the positive form obeys, and what operator class performs any final quotient. Positivity can buy a finite completion while simultaneously making exact nullity too rigid; regularity can make an infinite selector impossible even when every finite truncation works.
