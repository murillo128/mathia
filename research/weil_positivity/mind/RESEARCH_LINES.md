# Weil-positivity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Weil-positivity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Keep mixed shells active or leave the absolutely-continuous radial positive category

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-039-enriched-semigroups-inherit-obstructed-scalar-shadows-or-universal-lifts` through `MI-057-exact-mixed-shell-nullity-collapses-every-closable-radial-positive-form`.

WP-356--WP-373 classify finite Hecke, finite--archimedean, Whittaker and mixed-prime positive-completion routes. Genuine arithmetic incidence can survive in nonconstant finite modes, and mixed-prime coefficients can remove divergent local completion taxes, but continuous scalar post-selection cannot then delete the dense mixed spectrum.

WP-374 shows that stationary positive radial forms with exact mixed-shell nullity collapse surviving prime-power profiles to zero Mellin frequency. WP-375 removes stationarity from the essential hypothesis: in the canonical logarithmic-radial `L^2` topology, any closable nonnegative Hermitian form that gives zero energy to every mixed-prime shell must also annihilate every prime-power shell.

WP-376 shows that ordinary topology changes do not escape this closure mechanism. The canonical cyclotomic shell profiles are Schwartz, and remote translations still converge weakly to zero in every finite-order Sobolev `H^r` and, more generally, in every translation-invariant Fourier-weight Hilbert space with absolutely-continuous positive weight that contains the profiles. Mazur convexification and closability therefore propagate exact mixed-shell radicality to the prime-power sector in all of these spaces.

The matched escape is spectral rather than merely regularity-based. Adjoining the zero-frequency trace prevents remote translates from vanishing weakly and gives a bounded positive form that kills every mixed shell while retaining prime powers, but its value is exactly `c Lambda(n)^2`: the zero-Mellin survivor already isolated in WP-374, not the linear Weil coefficient. A single nonzero Fourier atom cannot perform the same exact selection.

The surviving route therefore cannot be “change the Sobolev norm but keep exact mixed-shell nullity.” It must change a load-bearing assumption: keep mixed shells active through the global assembly, use an indefinite intermediate coupling before final positivity, weaken exact nullity, introduce genuinely singular/non-absolutely-continuous spectral data with new arithmetic content, or leave radial translation geometry altogether.

## Keep positive completion, exact nullity, spectral type and final selector distinct

WP-372 shows that positivity can make a mixed completion cheap; WP-373 shows that regular scalar projection may be unable to remove that mixed sector; WP-374--WP-376 show that exact positive nullity can be even more destructive, and that reweighting ordinary Fourier modes does not change the obstruction.

A new construction should therefore state which mixed-prime coefficients remain source-active, at what stage positivity is imposed, what spectral type/topology controls remote translations, and whether any quotient is exact or approximate. A singular trace that merely recovers the zero Mellin mode is a matched control, not a new Weil mechanism.
