# Weil-positivity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable weil-positivity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Seek a source-forced completion that changes the analytic category before scalarization

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`, `MI-006-finite-mass-positive-completion-forces-mixed-prime-coupling-and-critical-roughness`, `MI-008-critical-half-density-exposes-archimedean-phase-only-before-gram-scalarization`, and `MI-009-finite-regular-passive-enlargements-cannot-realize-the-gamma-divisor`.

WP-179--WP-182 show that scalar lossless order is too rigid, scalar dissipation is too flexible, and a canonical Pythagorean completion can restore a finite positive two-channel kernel only by moving the ordered object away from the scalar target phase. That completion is universal function theory unless its compensating channel is source-forced.

WP-183--WP-185 close the obvious finite regular projective repairs for the exact Gamma relative phase. WP-186 shows that pointwise PSD lifting can encode the phase while losing the sign of its velocity, and WP-187 closes the direct Hardy repair: the canonical compression yields two positive leakage defects whose reversal-odd difference is indefinite on smooth Gamma windows and singular at the exact critical boundary.

WP-188 now closes the most classical self-adjoint scattering escape. Any ordinary trace-class Birman--Krein determinant has an `L^1` spectral-shift phase and is therefore `L^1`-close to a constant unitary at high energy, whereas the exact Gamma phase is not `L^1`-close to **any** constant phase. Even a positive trace-class perturbation, which would have supplied a genuine nonnegative spectral-shift theorem, cannot realize the carrier.

The live completion must therefore cross a substantive analytic-category boundary: a source-forced generalized/renormalized or domain-changing scattering theory, singular/infinite relative index, matrix/noncommutative global coupling, or a nonseparable finite--archimedean construction in which the Gamma factor is not an ordinary separated determinant. The replacement renormalization and coercivity theorem must be derived, not inherited rhetorically from the excluded trace-class setting.

## Derive mixed-prime incidence inside the same positive completion

Pairwise resultants, scalar radial filling, fixed finite arity, refinement-positive kernels, and separated completions do not manufacture irreducible mixed-prime geometry absent upstream. A survivor must carry a source-forced mixed relation before the sign theorem and show that finite-prime and real-place/defect sectors enter one common coercive object.

## Do not infer signed Weil density from PSD lifts, unordered leakage energies, or an ordinary scattering phase

WP-186 supplies the pointwise orientation control: the canonical rank-one positive projector attached to a unimodular boundary phase exists for every phase, and its positive metric data lose the sign of phase velocity. WP-187 shows that nonlocal Hardy compression still leaves signed orientation in an indefinite difference of two PSD leakage energies.

WP-188 adds a complementary category control. Ordinary trace-class spectral-shift scattering really can carry an independent sign when the perturbation is positive, but its determinant is too close to `1` in `L^1` to match the exact Gamma tail. A useful boundary-positive mechanism must therefore make both the **ordered channel and its renormalization** source-forced, or couple finite-prime and archimedean data inside a larger coercive object before scalar determinant reduction.

## Treat every category exit as successful only when the replacement order survives the full completion

A vector completion can restore positivity after scalar dissipation, but WP-183--WP-188 show that finite projective analytic completion, pointwise PSD lifting, canonical Hardy leakage splitting, and ordinary trace-class scattering can each erase the exact phase, its orientation, finite trace control, or admissible high-energy tail. Every category escape should state exactly which completed object is positive, what counterterm/regularization defines it, which component carries the signed target term, how finite-prime incidence enters that same object, and why the sign survives the final quotient.
