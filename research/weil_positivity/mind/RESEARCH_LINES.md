## Lift the source-native high-energy continuum into an independently selected global operator architecture

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation`, `MI-011-prime-ray-marginals-do-not-determine-mixed-prime-coupling`, `MI-012-automorphic-multiplicity-does-not-prevent-global-scalarization`, `MI-013-critical-sector-transport-needs-new-global-arithmetic-structure`.

WP-267--WP-274 show that hostability, category descent, scalar sign, localizable compression and inverse construction all fail as free routes. A positive nonlocal analytic host can be reconstructed when the desired target measure is supplied first, so existence alone is not explanatory.

WP-275--WP-277 isolate the spectral-category gate. The natural arithmetic log-energy carrier is pure point with compact resolvent; bounded exact pure-point-to-a.c. intertwining vanishes, and bounded or relative-bound-`<1` coupling between compact-resolvent sectors cannot manufacture an absolutely continuous channel. A continuum must therefore be present at zeroth order or arise through a genuine infinite-volume/domain-changing category transition.

WP-278 supplies the first source-native version of that escape. The critical Mangoldt half-density, shifted to high logarithmic energy and normalized by its local mass growth, converges vaguely to `cosh(x/2) dx`, exactly the polar continuum in the completed carrier. More generally the weight `Lambda(n)n^{-sigma}` has tangent density `cosh((1-sigma)x) dx`; the critical exponent `sigma=1/2` is therefore selected simultaneously by the discrete source amplitude and the continuum tangent.

This is provenance, not positivity. The tangent theorem is PNT-level and survives generalized-prime/full-integer matched controls; it does not construct a fixed-Hilbert-space a.c. sector, preserve prime-power fluctuations, supply the Gamma/digamma term, or prove the Weil sign. The live burden is to **lift this measure-level infinite-volume correspondence into a canonical boundary/operator/cohomological architecture** that retains the fluctuations and archimedean completion without inserting the target carrier by hand.

## Treat continuum provenance, fluctuation retention, Gamma completion and sign separately

WP-278 explains why the polar density is naturally attached to the same critical source that carries `Lambda(n)/sqrt n`, and it fits exactly the direct-limit category left open by WP-277. But the leading high-energy density is universal PNT/Weyl information. The RH-sensitive content remains in the global fluctuations around that density and in the sign of the completed Weil form.

A useful next theorem must therefore preserve the source-minus-tangent residual through the limit, identify the missing archimedean Gamma contribution, and derive a positive/nonlocal law independently. Reconstructing the known pole continuum alone is now source-grounded but still insufficient.
