# Weil-positivity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable weil-positivity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Seek a source-forced completion whose order acts on the Weil test cone itself

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`, `MI-006-finite-mass-positive-completion-forces-mixed-prime-coupling-and-critical-roughness`, `MI-008-critical-half-density-exposes-archimedean-phase-only-before-gram-scalarization`, and `MI-009-finite-regular-passive-enlargements-cannot-realize-the-gamma-divisor`.

WP-179--WP-188 show that scalar lossless order is too rigid, scalar dissipation too flexible, finite regular projective completions fail on the Gamma divisor, PSD/Hardy lifts lose signed orientation, and ordinary trace-class Birman--Krein scattering has the wrong high-energy tail.

WP-189--WP-193 sharpen the surviving category boundary. Burnol supplies genuine independent positive scattering carriers at both local types, but the positivity does not order the Weil observable: at a finite prime the nonnegative p-adic time-delay operator is exactly the Prime-Circle Poisson ray while the local Weil block is its conductor defect; at the real place positive supersymmetric Hamiltonians coexist with a Gamma response whose relevant signed phase velocity is not positive. Burnol's genuinely global adelic Lax--Phillips assembly couples the places, but its missing causality condition is RH-equivalent. Moving to higher regularized spectral-shift order gives genuine positive densities at even orders, yet the Weil autocorrelation cone imposes a stricter order requirement.

WP-192 excludes the second-order Koplienko remainder on wide autocorrelations. WP-193 generalizes the gate: even orders `2 mod 4` have the wrong broad-scale sign for every nontrivial pair, while orders `0 mod 4` survive that asymptotic test only if the even spectral-shift density is **positive definite**, equivalently has nonnegative Fourier transform. Pointwise nonnegativity of the density is not enough, and an explicit scalar fourth-order control already violates that stronger condition.

The live completion must therefore provide an **independent order theorem in the actual Weil autocorrelation category**. In the higher-order spectral-shift branch this means source-forcing positive definiteness of the even `4k`-order density, not merely its pointwise positivity, while the same construction still generates the finite-prime, Gamma, and polar/global terms.

## Derive mixed-prime incidence inside the same positive completion

Pairwise resultants, scalar radial filling, fixed finite arity, refinement-positive kernels, separated local scattering carriers, and adelic assembly with RH-equivalent causality do not by themselves manufacture an independently ordered mixed-prime Weil object. A survivor must carry a source-forced mixed relation before the sign theorem and show that finite-prime and real-place/defect sectors enter one common coercive object.

## Use Burnol's scattering dictionary and higher-order spectral shift as matched controls

WP-190 identifies the exact local relation `A_p=(log p)I-K_p`, with `K_p` the nonnegative p-adic time-delay Toeplitz operator and `A_p` the signed conductor/Weil block. WP-191 supplies the global control: assembling the local ingredients adelically is not enough when the desired causal order is equivalent to RH.

WP-192--WP-193 supply the test-cone control. A positive spectral-shift density can induce a functional with the wrong sign on autocorrelations; at order `4k`, the exact remaining requirement is positive definiteness of the even density. A proposed generalized scattering/regularization mechanism should be tested against this criterion before its positivity is interpreted as Weil positivity.

## Treat every category exit as successful only when the replacement order survives the full completion

Every category escape should state exactly which completed object is positive, what counterterm/regularization defines it, which component carries the signed target term, how finite-prime incidence enters that same object, and why the order survives the final Weil test cone rather than only convex functions, pointwise positive densities, or an RH-equivalent global condition.
