## Couple the exact finite package to the archimedean/polar sector before positive completion and scalar energy separate

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`, `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm`, and `MI-006-finite-mass-positive-completion-forces-mixed-prime-coupling-and-critical-roughness`.

Finite arithmetic recovery is no longer the main mystery. Exact covers force critical normalization and positive finite selectors, while the regular cover-Jensen route classicalizes to a universal singular endpoint. The prime-torus route has a stronger obstruction: every exact critical positive completion carries unavoidable mixed-prime low-frequency mass, and the compulsory prime axes obstruct standard positive scalar energies.

The Riemann Gamma digamma variation supplies an independently positive archimedean ingredient, but local ways of gluing it to the finite-prime carrier remain divergent even after endpoint damping and singular/local regularization. The live question is a finite--archimedean construction formed **before** positivity, completion, and local Gamma energy become separate operations.

## Change the operator before the natural Gamma carriers become spectrally incompatible

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-operator-category-is-a-hard-compatibility-gate`, `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`, and `MI-006-finite-mass-positive-completion-forces-mixed-prime-coupling-and-critical-roughness`.

The direct radial--solenoid Gamma identification is now exhausted in the natural Hilbert category. WP-131 rules out every nonzero bounded exact intertwiner because the carriers have incompatible spectral type. WP-132 classifies the distributional exact escape: it collapses to rational point sampling/jets, is nonclosable from natural radial `L^2`, and at the critical prime-power amplitudes fails even square summability at `sigma=1/2`.

A surviving finite--archimedean bridge must therefore alter the architecture **before** asking it to inherit positivity: for example a source-forced zero-mode/triangular coupling, a different Hilbert/domain geometry, a non-exact boundary response, a graded/cohomological construction, or another nonlocal operator formed jointly from the finite and archimedean data. WP-132's anchored subtraction is a useful boundary control: it can regularize sampling, but precisely because it changes the semigroup covariance. The new operator must then prove its own sign/coercivity theorem and recover the required Mangoldt plus archimedean/polar terms.

## Classify non-passive, nonlocal, renormalized, or higher-cohomological alternatives beyond prime-torus completion
