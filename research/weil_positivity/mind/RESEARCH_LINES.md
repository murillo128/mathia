WP-327 completes Muraki's five natural products. The direct source-preserving scalar additive-semigroup route is therefore exhausted within the five natural products. ## Separate source fingerprint from source-selective sign

Shifted-lattice support, Mangoldt coefficients, exact multipoint entries, finite jets and prime-power atom gaps are source-specific. The sign provenance can still be universal, or the desired positive architecture can fail exactly because the source is selective.

WP-322--WP-324 exhibit the first failure mode: arithmetic entries live inside universal resolvent-Gram/Hankel mechanisms. WP-325--WP-327 exhibit the second across the full natural-product scalar semigroup class: selective prime-power support destroys four divisibility notions, while the one surviving notion is universal. Source specificity matters only when the **positive theorem itself** is source-selective and points in the Weil direction.

Future scalar arguments should identify the precise arithmetic feature that a broad matched class cannot fake while preserving a genuinely nonautomatic sign theorem. A source-specific obstruction to positivity is not a substitute for a positive source theorem.
