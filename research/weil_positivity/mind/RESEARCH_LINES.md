# Weil-positivity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable Weil-positivity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they discriminate among mathematically different mechanisms.

## Couple the exact pointed finite package to the archimedean/polar sector before support selection breaks positivity

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`, and `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm`.

Finite arithmetic recovery and continuity are no longer the main mysteries. The pointed local Dirichlet geometry makes the Mangoldt anchor continuous; intrinsic covers force the `n^{-1/2}` normalization; the positive cocycle has trace `log n`; and its Möbius primitive has exact trace `Lambda(n)` and is genuinely positive on every prime-power ray. The full scalar coefficient `Lambda(n)/sqrt(n)` is therefore already present in the same intrinsic package.

The obstruction occurs at completion. Mixed-prime Möbius primitives are nonzero indefinite operators; shifted positive defects add only a telescoping digamma coboundary; and semigroup-compatible positive basepoint averaging is exhausted by the pointed and Haar branches, with Haar killing the scale defect. A decisive mechanism must couple finite support selection and the archimedean/polar term **before** these separated reductions and prove its sign independently.

## Derive a genuinely new symmetry-breaking metric or form rather than the canonical polar repair

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-operator-category-is-a-hard-compatibility-gate` and `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`.

On the independently selected full-root `q=2` Hardy channel, bounded and natural unbounded self-adjoint metric repairs that yield positivity contain the channel's own polar sign. Unboundedness therefore does not by itself create a new sign mechanism.

A surviving operation must use additional geometry that forces a non-polar sign, a genuinely singular form-level construction outside the classified operator-product category, a twisted/family/boundary completion, or a nonseparable finite--archimedean construction. A decisive result must preserve the exact finite selector rather than merely convert an indefinite operator into `|A|`.

## Classify nonseparable or higher-cohomological completions beyond cover averages and zeroth coinvariants

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm`, `MI-005-cover-coinvariants-classicalize-or-collapse`, and `MI-002-operator-category-is-a-hard-compatibility-gate`.

The most canonical separated globalizations are now sharply closed. Positive basepoint averaging compatible with all covers is only pointed-plus-Haar. Quotienting the diagonal trace ideal by cover transports remembers only ordinary trace, while the same zeroth Hausdorff coinvariant construction on full standard operator ideals collapses to zero.

The live question is whether a mathematically forced **higher** cohomological object, nontrivial coefficient module, selective geometric quotient, twisted family/boundary form, or nonseparable finite--archimedean coupling retains the exact pointed selector and supplies a new sign. A decisive result must identify the surviving information explicitly and show that it is neither ordinary trace/Möbius scalarization nor a repackaged polar sign.
