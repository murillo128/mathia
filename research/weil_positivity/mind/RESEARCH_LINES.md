## Change the operator category only with an explicit new coercivity theorem

Indefiniteness, singularity, weak convergence, relation-valuedness, dimension growth, ordinary or modified determinant passage, domain change, or unbounded regularization is not itself positivity. WP-178 makes this especially sharp for `det_2`: losing the Schur modulus does not remove the passive phase orientation.

Any surviving matrix/operator construction must identify the signed arithmetic datum that persists and prove the new positive/coercive theorem **after** the category change. An analytic counterterm or parameter-dependent reference that repairs the Gamma phase is new mechanism and must be derived intrinsically rather than chosen to fit the target.
