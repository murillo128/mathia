# Weil-positivity research lines

This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Weil-Positivity evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Force a genuinely one-sided source relation, then prove its arithmetic provenance

WP-391--WP-404 show that selector enlargement, bounded Bost--Connes positivity, affiliation, modular-frequency decomposition, quotient order, cutoff breaking and broad linear readouts do not by themselves produce a Weil-oriented functional. Dense two-sided tangent geometry survives these changes. A sufficiently thin same-shadow slice can be one-sided, but matched nonarithmetic constructions can manufacture the same orientation. Any linear route therefore has two separate gates: the source relation must genuinely change the reachable tangent closure, and that change must be forced by arithmetic structure rather than generic thinness.

## Make any enlarged positive block route survive genuine KMS joining and source-forced cross-frequency coherence

WP-405--WP-418 progressively close native analytic half-spectrum positivity, central and noncentral cycle holonomy, projective repairs, passive full-fibre decorations, balanced KMS blocks, Hilbert-module path Grams, varying canonical supports/ranges and phase-character refinements as automatic sources of Weil orientation. After the actual equilibrium readout these constructions either gauge away, become spectrally orthogonal, reduce to universal positive Grams, or leave a compatibility mask whose labels are not themselves forced by the source.

WP-421 isolates the first apparent escape from separate invariance: a merely jointly invariant state can pair a source Bohr mode with an opposite active-fibre mode. WP-422 then shows that a countable atomic Gibbs fibre can be programmed with all prime-logarithmic gaps, so finite spectral capacity is not the decisive obstruction.

WP-423 closes that mechanism at the genuine KMS boundary for decoupled product dynamics in finite-matrix and countable atomic type-I fibres, with stronger finite-corner factorization. WP-424 removes commutative non-atomic active fibres even more strongly: in the KMS GNS quotient every invariant central commutative fibre is pointwise time-trivial.

WP-425 closes the remaining decoupled product-dynamics escape without any fibre-category hypothesis: every positive-temperature KMS state of `alpha tensor tau` is separately invariant under the two factor flows, so every nonzero source Bohr mode has zero expectation against every auxiliary observable. WP-426 gives the opposite canonicity warning: merely leaving product dynamics is vacuous if the replacement flow is reconstructed from the state one wants to certify, since every faithful normal state is KMS for its own modular flow and every faithful finite-dimensional density matrix is Gibbs for a state-selected Hamiltonian.

WP-427 sharpens the source-preserving interacting case. If the ambient KMS dynamics restricts to the original source flow, then every insertion from the source relative commutant still annihilates nonzero source Bohr modes. Thus abandoning product structure is not enough while the compensator continues to commute with the source algebra. A viable coupled extension must use a genuinely noncommuting correspondence or change the source dynamics itself; the latter remains subject to the WP-426 programmability gate.

WP-428 closes the first natural noncommuting positive repair. For any homogeneous auxiliary operator `b_nu`, the positive sandwich `a -> Omega(b_nu^* a b_nu)` is still source-time invariant: the auxiliary frequency cancels between the two sides, so every nonzero source Bohr mode vanishes. Positive sums of such Bohr-diagonal sandwiches remain closed, and an equivariantly expected operator-valued version falls back into the source-time-covariant positive-map obstruction already classified by WP-225. Noncommutativity by itself therefore does not move the critical defect into frequency zero.

The matched control in WP-428 is load-bearing: a coherent nonhomogeneous auxiliary `b=sum_nu b_nu` creates cross terms `b_nu^* a_omega b_mu` with total frequency `omega+mu-nu`, and these can survive exactly when `nu-mu=omega`. The live KMS route is consequently narrower than “find an interacting auxiliary.” It must derive, **from arithmetic source data and before inspecting the Weil sign**, coherent amplitudes and relative phases between distinct auxiliary Bohr sectors whose cross terms generate the required zero-frequency orientation, or else derive a genuinely new source flow that is neither state-programmed nor exterior-equivalent/Morita-trivial.

Any such mechanism must still recover the exact prime-power/Mangoldt coefficients, archimedean Gamma term, polar normalization, admissible domain and global sign from the same source structure. A coherent multi-frequency auxiliary is only an admissible category, not evidence: arbitrary mixed-sector phases can be programmed just as easily as a state-selected Hamiltonian. The unresolved theorem is therefore a source-canonicity statement for cross-frequency coherence together with a positive readout that survives the existing equivalence controls.

## Keep source canonicity, order and destination sign separate

A candidate can be canonical yet order-trivial, positive yet universal, gauge-invariant yet absent from the source, or infinite-dimensional yet fully programmable. Future arguments must identify where the asymmetry lives, prove that the source algebra actually carries it, and show that the positive readout retains it after equilibrium normalization. WP-425 removes auxiliary-fibre richness under product KMS dynamics; WP-426 removes state-selected dynamics; WP-427 removes commuting compensation in source-preserving KMS extensions; and WP-428 removes Bohr-diagonal noncommuting positive sandwiches. A genuine escape must now explain source-forced **cross-frequency coherence** or a source-forced deformation of the flow, rather than merely enlarge the ambient algebra or choose a more flexible equilibrium representation.
