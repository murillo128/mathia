## Find a source restriction on the derivative-biased Mangoldt law that matched positive controls cannot realize

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation` through `MI-032-gap-adapted-mangoldt-julia-laws-are-tight-but-still-matched-control`.

WP-293--WP-318 close the natural pure-measure scalar Julia orbit. Fixed gauges and Schur reductions either preserve the wrong curvature, contaminate the Gamma scale or universalize under moving normalization. Finite multipoles reduce by classical interlacing. For arbitrary positive measures, derivative normalization at a regular node reduces the scalar Julia response to `tau-K_nu`, whose Pick positivity is universal for every probability law; every gap-supported probability law has a positive matched-control realization.

WP-319 tests the first obvious source-specific refinement of that induced-law frontier. The atom-mass scale of WP-315 is noncompact under bounded prime clustering, but normalizing by an **actual adjacent prime-power support gap** repairs compactness along an unavoidable bounded-gap subsequence. The derivative-biased Mangoldt laws become tight and every subsequential limit is an inverse-square law on a shifted integer lattice,

`rho proportional to sum_h theta_h/(h-d/2)^2 delta_(1/2-h/d)`,

with quantized local prime-power coefficients `theta_h in {0} union {1/k}` and a support hole around the Julia node.

This is genuine local arithmetic structure, but it still does not make scalar Julia positivity arithmetic. WP-318 constructs a non-arithmetic positive Herglotz source with **the same** gap-supported probability law, hence exactly the same normalized Boolean self-energy profile. Gap scaling therefore recovers an arithmetic fingerprint while leaving the sign theorem inside the matched-control class.

The live scalar question is narrower than “prove compactness of the Mangoldt tangent.” One must prove a restriction on the family of induced laws or on their coupling to Gamma/global terms that generic positive Herglotz controls cannot reproduce. If no such restriction exists, the construction must leave the pure-measure scalar category through matrix/operator-valued structure, a mass-at-infinity channel, generalized resolvents, noninvertible maps, or genuinely nonseparable finite--archimedean coupling.

## Separate local arithmetic fingerprint from source-selective positivity

Tightness, shifted-lattice support and quantized coefficients are source information. The positivity of `-K_rho` is not: it uses only that `rho` is a probability law. Future arguments must identify where the actual values or relations among the `theta_h` enter a theorem unavailable to the matched law with the same `rho`; otherwise the arithmetic content is present in the representation but unused by the sign mechanism.

## Preserve mass class, curvature, compactness and coupling as distinct gates

WP-314--WP-319 show four different failure modes: universal moving tangents, noncompact atom-mass scaling, universal normalized positivity after derivative biasing, and source-specific compact limits whose complete induced law is still matched-control realizable. Passing one gate does not pass the next. In particular, repairing local compactness is not equivalent to obtaining a finite--archimedean completion.
