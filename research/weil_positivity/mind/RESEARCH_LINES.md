WP-194--WP-196 show that this `4k` gate is both nonempty and dangerously nonselective. Thus neither zero centering, off-center positive-definite multiplicity interference, nor positivity of both commuting energies supplies arithmetic selection. The live completion must source-force the **actual finite-prime, Gamma, polar/global incidence and orientation inside the same ordered object**, and its order must survive the Weil autocorrelation cone without being a universal spectral-multiplicity effect.
