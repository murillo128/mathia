WP-281 closes the natural finite-state compromise between those extremes. Internal finite-dimensional state is therefore not enough merely because it is non-scalar.

The live burden is consequently a genuinely new global category. A completed operator/boundary/cohomological construction must retain source fluctuations and supply the archimedean sector **before** scalar high-energy asymptotics discard it, without explicit zero subtraction and without taking as input a primitive limit/sign theorem equivalent to RH. Surviving possibilities include an independently forced infinite-dimensional/nonrational response, a genuinely scale-nonlocal operation, a nonlinear mechanism with its own sign audit, or finite--archimedean coupling formed before the critical zero field becomes the scalar residual.
