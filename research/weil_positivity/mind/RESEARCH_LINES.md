## Find a sign mechanism that survives scalar reconstruction after normalization

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-039-stationary-negative-type-transport-needs-quadratic-drift-for-weil-tail-control` through `MI-050-arithmetic-cusp-normalization-exhausts-the-order-one-scalar-gauge-but-leaves-divisor-reconstruction`.

The stationary-geometry results show that the Weil log-square cannot be manufactured for free by oscillatory negative-type structure: a scalar or fixed-positive-functional readout obtains the required quadratic arithmetic direction only when that direction is already present in the quadratic sector. Singular-character tangents merely relocate the same target square to a boundary state.

The Maaß--Selberg/congruence branch gives complementary matched-control obstructions. Positive truncated norms and boundary squares can be divisor-blind; arithmetic congruence scattering can carry independent reciprocal inner factors; and bad-prime logarithmic-derivative blocks have one strict sign. WP-350 closes unitary moving compression, while WP-351 shows that canonical proper-contraction defect positivity is exactly blind to the common unimodular scalar zeta phase.

WP-352 closes the most direct cross-parameter Berry/Chern repair. Projective eigenspace geometry cannot see a common scalar factor; the full scalar Maurer--Cartan term is exact and central; complex scalar curvature is flat away from the divisor; and singular Poincare--Lelong curvature is exactly the divisor itself. Its zero-free reciprocal control `exp(a(s-1/2))` preserves reciprocal symmetry, critical-line unitarity, zeros/poles, divisor current and all projective geometry while shifting the critical-line phase generator by `a`.

WP-353 classifies that residual control in the natural finite-order category. Every entire nowhere-zero order-at-most-one reciprocal/unitary control with central normalization is exactly `exp(a(s-1/2))`. Standard width-one arithmetic cusp normalization can remove this one real dilation parameter, so the scalar gauge is not unavoidable in the normalized arithmetic problem.

WP-354 now closes the most direct finite-dimensional matrix-metric repair. Any conjugation-invariant positive semidefinite quadratic form on the Hermitian Lie algebra of `U(m)` splits orthogonally into the scalar center and traceless directions:

`B(H,K)=a tr(H_0 K_0)+b tr(H)tr(K)`, `a,b>=0`.

For a unitary path whose common scalar phase carries the zeta phase, the corresponding Wigner--Smith energy therefore sees a noncentral positive square plus the **square** of the total determinant-phase velocity, with no center--traceless cross term. Reversing the scalar phase leaves the positive metric energy unchanged while reversing the oriented phase velocity needed by the explicit-formula sign. Thus ordinary finite-dimensional bi-invariant quadratic Riemannian geometry cannot manufacture Weil positivity by coupling a scalar zeta phase to noncentral matrix motion.

These closures narrow rather than solve the sign problem. Once arithmetic normalization fixes the order-one scalar gauge, scalar recovery collapses to divisor reconstruction; once finite-dimensional bi-invariant quadratic geometry is imposed, central phase orientation is orthogonal to noncentral positive energy. A successful route must force positivity **before scalar reconstruction and outside that decoupled metric class**: through source-defined/non-bi-invariant coupling, nonquadratic or order-sensitive geometry, genuinely global multi-parameter structure, infinite-dimensional/adelic/cohomological structure, or another independently positive object whose projection yields the Weil form without encoding the zeta divisor as the source of its sign.

## Keep source dependence distinct from sign coercivity

Arithmetic labels, self-adjoint realization, unitary scattering, moving channel choice, contraction defects, projective curvature, a canonically normalized scalar phase and even a bi-invariant positive matrix metric are all source-dependent or canonical structures. Yet each can either admit matched controls, discard scalar orientation, or reduce to reconstruction of already-present divisor data. They are not Weil positivity merely because they are canonical, positive or geometric.

The next proposal should state exactly which scalar phase or divisor coordinate survives its quotient, which higher-order/CDD/noncentral controls remain admissible, what source normalization removes, whether the geometry couples the scalar center to noncentral directions **before** positivity is taken, and which independent theorem forces the required sign. Algebraic division by known local factors, a reducing subspace, scalar-gauge-blind defects, projective curvature, Poincare--Lelong recovery of the divisor, divisor-plus-normalization reconstruction, or a bi-invariant quadratic energy that orthogonally splits the center are representation mechanisms rather than independently generated positivity.
