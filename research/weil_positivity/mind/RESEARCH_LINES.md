## Force source-specific finite--archimedean structure beyond universal empty-side tangents and isolated-pole scalar Julia geometry

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation` through `MI-030-isolated-pole-julia-reduction-forces-collective-support-geometry`.

WP-293--WP-313 close the natural fixed scalar gauge/Schur/Julia orbit. The critical Mangoldt source has the correct logarithmic mass class but the wrong-sign source-specific curvature. Fixed-interior Schur stripping inserts a larger positive `log(y)/y` term; finite fixed-boundary Julia reduction either does the same or worsens the positive curvature; an infinite fixed-node chain cannot repair this whenever the relevant high-energy invariant is stable under the chain limit.

WP-314 tests the canonical nonuniform empty-side escape. A regular Julia node moving to `X->infinity` and observed on scale `z=X zeta` converges, after unit-log normalization, to the same boundary layer as the unit-density logarithmic control. The arithmetic remainder disappears, while fixed spectral scales degenerate to a constant. Noncommutation of limits is therefore insufficient when the moving tangent is universal.

WP-315 tests the obvious support-side replacement. Around a prime pole, the intrinsic Mangoldt mass is `a_p~log p/p`, but bounded prime clusters occur on a much smaller logarithmic spacing scale. After translating by `log p` and scaling by `a_p`, arbitrarily many comparable prime atoms collapse into every fixed neighborhood; the normalized positive measures are not uniformly locally bounded and the Herglotz field has unbounded limsup response at mass-scale height.

WP-316 closes the opposite support-side escape. Suppose instead that a moving node is chosen on a source gap where one focal Herglotz pole `-a/(z-x)` asymptotically dominates the positive boundary derivative. If

`kappa=(r^2/a) h'(t) -> 0`,

then the canonically normalized scalar Julia response converges locally to the removable real constant `sigma`; for a pure pole the collapse is exact at every scale. For the Mangoldt source,

`kappa_n = sum_(m!=n) (a_m/a_n) (r_n/(x_m-t_n))^2`,

so any nonconstant scalar support-side Julia tangent requires an order-one positive contribution from other prime-power atoms.

The scalar support route therefore has a structural fork:

`one-pole dominated -> Julia tangent is constant`,

`nonconstant Julia tangent -> genuinely multipole background`.

Combined with WP-315, this rules out interpreting the missing completion as a canonically isolated prime pole: the intrinsic atom scale can be noncompact because clusters collapse, while a gap-adapted regime that really isolates the pole is trivialized by the Julia operation itself.

A viable scalar continuation must consequently derive a canonical **collective multipole normalization/coupling** whose local positive geometry is controlled, nonuniversal and compatible with the Gamma discriminator. Otherwise the construction must leave the audited scalar class through operator/matrix-valued boundary structure, generalized resolvents, noninvertible maps, an added archimedean channel or nonseparable finite--archimedean coupling.

## Treat mass class, curvature orientation, transform admissibility, limit stability, tangent universality, local compactness and collectivity as separate gates

Matching `log y` establishes only the infinite-mass class. Producing a nonuniversal `y^-2` coefficient establishes arithmetic sensitivity but not the correct orientation. WP-307--WP-313 show that admissible positive scalar changes can preserve the wrong invariant, contaminate the Gamma scale or fail under unstable infinite limits.

WP-314 adds the universality gate: a nonuniform rescaling can erase the arithmetic remainder. WP-315 adds local compactness: a source-specific pole scale can fail because nearby atoms collapse faster than the normalization. WP-316 adds the complementary collectivity gate: if the background becomes negligible enough that the pole really is isolated, classical scalar Julia reduction removes the pole as nonconstant geometry. A useful support-side tangent must therefore control a **collective** positive source configuration rather than merely choose a better one-pole chart.

## Use matched controls against universal leading scale, post-hoc recoding and moving-limit classicalization

Unit-density positive measures control the leading logarithm and the canonical empty-side moving Julia layer. Prime-cluster subsequences stress the support-side single-atom chart, while the pure-pole model is the exact matched control for the isolated regime: its Julia response is only a removable constant.

Future constructions must alter the collective geometry or realization before final scalar comparison and prove both **positivity/admissibility** and **source-specific survival with a controlled multipole normalization**. A universal tangent, an unstable atom-mass chart, an isolated-pole Julia constant or a post-hoc source recoding is not a finite--archimedean completion.
