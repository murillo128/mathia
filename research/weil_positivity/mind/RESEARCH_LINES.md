WP-311 now closes the canonical fixed-interior scalar repair that WP-310 left open. The one-point interior Schur reduction is admissible and positivity-preserving, but after the unique end normalization restoring unit logarithmic growth it adds a strictly positive `log(y)/y` coefficient. For the reflected critical Mangoldt source one step contributes `(pi v/eta) log(y)/y`, and every finite end-normalized chain adds further positive increments. The Riemann Gamma channel has no term at that order; its relevant curvature first appears at `y^-2`.

The remaining route must therefore add genuine structure rather than another scalar coordinate description. It must either avoid/cancel the `log(y)/y` contamination by an independently justified mechanism or leave the audited scalar class: boundary- or moving-reference geometry, an independently justified cutoff/renormalization law, a noninvertible Herglotz operation with new meaning, operator/matrix-valued boundary sector, generalized resolvent, determinant/scattering object, added archimedean channel, or nonseparable finite--archimedean coupling.

## Treat mass class, curvature orientation, gauge rigidity, transform admissibility and asymptotic neutrality as separate gates

Matching `log y` establishes only the infinite-mass class. Producing a nonuniversal `y^-2` coefficient establishes arithmetic sensitivity but not the correct orientation. WP-307 closes positive affine input recentering; WP-308 closes constant projective output gauge; WP-309 collapses holomorphic scalar automorphism families to the same constant class. WP-310 adds the admissibility gate: a positive Herglotz source can lie outside the domain of the desired transform.

WP-311 adds another independent gate: **an admissible positivity-preserving transform can still damage the target asymptotic hierarchy.** Fixed-interior Schur stripping is legal on the source class, yet it inserts a larger `log(y)/y` term before the desired `y^-2` comparison. A future transform must therefore prove both admissibility and discriminator-scale neutrality; satisfying one does not imply the other.

## Use matched controls against universal leading scale and post-hoc recoding

Unit-density positive measures control the leading logarithm, while the Mangoldt curvature sign is source-specific. WP-307--WP-309 show that affine or projective scalar gauge cannot flip that sign while preserving the required logarithmic end. WP-310 shows that the standard moment-Schur algorithm cannot start on the critical source without changing its normalization or positivity class. WP-311 shows that the natural fixed-interior scalar alternative starts successfully but generates a source-phase-dependent asymptotic defect larger than the Gamma curvature.

Future constructions must therefore alter the geometry or realization before final scalar comparison and must prove both **positivity/admissibility** and **asymptotic-scale compatibility** from the source rather than importing either by recoding or by target-dependent cancellation.
