## Derive a source-native completion whose zero suppression is a consequence

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation`, `MI-011-prime-ray-marginals-do-not-determine-mixed-prime-coupling`, `MI-012-automorphic-multiplicity-does-not-prevent-global-scalarization`, `MI-013-critical-sector-transport-needs-new-global-arithmetic-structure`, `MI-014-nontrivial-zero-modes-screen-the-gamma-tangent-layer`.

WP-267--WP-280 leave a source-native pole continuum but no independent Gamma completion. WP-281--WP-283 close fixed finite-state, positive Stieltjes and fixed compact-memory linear responses. WP-284 shows that positive growing memory can erase the favorable-RH zero screen if it is programmed from the target ordinates; WP-285 shows that zero programming is unnecessary because ordinary Cesàro averaging already removes the absolutely summable oscillatory screen.

WP-286 closes the next easy repair. Replacing an externally chosen box by **any normalized Haar/Følner average on logarithmic scale** still kills every nonzero character and therefore every absolutely summable oscillatory field, independent of its arithmetic frequencies. In positive scale this is the canonical multiplicative Haar action itself. Thus an operation can be natural for the ambient scale symmetry and still be completely arithmetic-blind.

The live completion problem is therefore stricter than “find a positive, zero-independent, canonical growing-memory filter.” Derive the operation itself from finite-prime/archimedean geometry in a way that distinguishes the arithmetic carrier from generic logarithmic dynamics, before using the zero decomposition, and then prove that the same operation has the required sign/zero-suppression/completion behavior. Viable categories include source-forced oscillatory/noncompact responses, nonlinear mechanisms with an independent sign theorem, cohomological/intersection pairings, or finite--archimedean coupling formed before scalarization.

## Treat arithmetic source provenance, not ambient scale symmetry or memory length, as the missing theorem

WP-283's `Omega(log T)` lower bound concerns exact notch interpolation by fixed compact-memory transfers. WP-285 shows that asymptotic removal of the regularized favorable-RH zero field can be achieved with arbitrarily slowly diverging memory. WP-286 shows that even choosing the averaging from the multiplicative scale group's Haar/Følner structure does not repair provenance: invariant means suppress generic nonzero characters for representation-theoretic reasons.

A future candidate must expose two logically separate theorems. First, why the **arithmetic source** selects its support/weights/evolution/coupling or boundary law, using information not shared by generic almost-periodic controls. Second, why zero suppression and the desired sign follow from that source law. Target-independent post hoc averaging and ambient-symmetry canonicality are both below this threshold.
