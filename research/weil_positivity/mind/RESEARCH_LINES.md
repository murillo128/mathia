# Weil-positivity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Weil-positivity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Use noncentral arithmetic information without asking positivity to erase or orient its centralizer shadow

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm`, `MI-039-enriched-semigroups-inherit-obstructed-scalar-shadows-or-universal-lifts` through `MI-059-pointwise-local-boundary-nullity-collapses-to-the-mangoldt-ray`, `MI-060-strongly-local-markov-forms-either-reject-mixed-shells-or-become-unit-cell-invisible`, `MI-061-logarithmic-interface-current-classicalizes-to-ramanujan-orthogonality`, `MI-062-stationary-positive-interface-selection-is-an-explicit-spectral-mask`, `MI-063-the-canonical-divisibility-diagonal-cannot-hide-a-prime-power-selector`, `MI-064-the-full-bounded-bost-connes-positive-cone-cannot-isolate-prime-power-support`, `MI-065-woronowicz-affiliation-does-not-enlarge-the-unital-bost-connes-source-algebra`, `MI-066-a-source-valued-resolvent-pulls-positive-unbounded-selectors-back-into-the-bounded-bost-connes-no-go`, `MI-067-a-diffuse-modular-centralizer-can-erase-exact-arithmetic-point-support`, `MI-068-single-modular-frequency-positivity-falls-back-to-the-centralizer`, `MI-069-positive-multi-frequency-data-cannot-outrun-its-centralizer-support-shadow`, `MI-070-quotienting-out-a-centralizer-that-contains-the-order-unit-destroys-inherited-positivity`, `MI-071-fixed-centralizer-shadows-have-dense-sign-symmetric-tangents`.

WP-391--WP-394 rule out exact positive prime-power selection in the canonical divisibility diagonal, the full bounded Bost--Connes positive cone, Woronowicz affiliation with the same unital source algebra, and positive unbounded forms whose canonical resolvent returns to that represented algebra.

WP-395 changes category to the critical factor but finds a diffuse centralizer `L^infinity(hat Z,m_Haar)` in which the standard countable arithmetic orbit is Haar-null. Exact point support is then either zero as an operator or representative-dependent rather than invariant.

WP-396 shows that one nonzero modular/gauge frequency does not fix the problem: the positive square, modulus and support projections of a homogeneous mode return to the zero-frequency centralizer, and a nonzero spectral subspace contains no positive operator of its own.

WP-397 closes the immediate multi-frequency interference escape for bounded positive elements. If `E` is the critical gauge expectation, `a>=0`, `d=E(a)` and `p=s(d)`, every Fourier coefficient satisfies `a_r=p a_r p`; finite-frequency squares satisfy `E(y^*y)=sum_r x_r^*x_r`. Cross-frequency information can survive, but not outside the measurable support of the positive centralizer floor.

WP-398 closes the next interpretation: keep the diffuse floor but quotient it away and read positivity from the noncentral correlations. On the self-adjoint information quotient `M_sa/N_sa`, the image of the positive cone is the entire quotient because the discarded centralizer contains the order unit. Even with the shadow fixed by `E(a)=1`, both `1+epsilon k` and `1-epsilon k` are positive for every sufficiently small centered self-adjoint direction `k`. Ambient positivity therefore supplies no nontrivial linear order or first-order sign orientation on the surviving bounded noncentral data.

WP-399 removes the possibility that a non-scalar source-forced fixed shadow repairs that local sign problem by itself. For any bounded positive `d` in the centralizer, the self-adjoint centered directions supported on `p=s(d)` that are order-bounded by `d` are genuine two-sided fixed-shadow tangents. Spectral cutoffs of `d` make such tangents strongly and ultraweakly dense in the entire centered supported space. The tangent geometry is therefore sign-symmetric at normal first order even when the shadow is nonconstant: any normal real linear functional that is nonnegative on every positive fixed-shadow displacement must vanish.

The surviving bounded critical-factor route must therefore keep a **joint source-forced relation** that restricts the admissible noncentral tangent directions beyond the fixed-shadow support condition, or use a nonlinear/global readout whose sign theorem genuinely depends on that joint structure. A useful relation must survive the spectral-cutoff test behind WP-399; it is no longer enough that correlations are noncentral, support-dominated by a positive shadow, or attached to a non-scalar fixed shadow.

Other categories remain logically open: continuous-core or crossed-product constructions, one-sided correspondences, nonunital source boundaries, genuinely unbounded nonhomogeneous forms with controlled domains, singular/non-normal readouts, or finite--archimedean assemblies. They count as new hypotheses only if the positivity/readout operation does not reduce them to a previously classified bounded centralizer shadow or to the same sign-symmetric tangent geometry.

## Keep selector existence, support, frequency, quotient order, tangent geometry and destination sign distinct

Category enlargement can fail by being too programmable, returning through bounded transforms, erasing the arithmetic orbit as null, projecting a single frequency under positivity, forcing every multi-frequency coefficient to live over the same diffuse support, making the projected positive cone improper after the fixed component is forgotten, or leaving a strongly dense two-sided tangent family even after the shadow is fixed. These are different obstructions.

A new proposal must state which arithmetic information remains invariant, how positivity is formed, what its fixed-point component is, where every useful frequency is supported, whether the post-quotient cone is actually proper, whether the fixed-shadow admissible tangent set is genuinely asymmetric, and how the final Weil sign theorem uses a source-specific relation not present in generic unital positive systems. Noncentrality and ambient positivity by themselves are no longer enough.
