**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation` through `MI-031-scalar-julia-positivity-is-universal-boolean-self-energy-arithmetic-survives-only-in-the-derivative-biased-law-and-its-coupling`.

WP-293--WP-313 close the natural fixed scalar gauge/Schur/Julia orbit. The critical Mangoldt source has the correct logarithmic mass class but the wrong-sign source-specific curvature. Fixed-interior Schur stripping inserts a larger positive `log(y)/y` term; finite fixed-boundary Julia reduction either does the same or worsens the positive curvature; an infinite fixed-node chain cannot repair this whenever the relevant high-energy invariant is stable under the chain limit.

WP-314 tests the canonical nonuniform empty-side escape. A regular Julia node moving to `X->infinity` and observed on scale `z=X zeta` converges, after unit-log normalization, to the same boundary layer as the unit-density logarithmic control. The arithmetic remainder disappears, while fixed spectral scales degenerate to a constant. Noncommutation of limits is therefore insufficient when the moving tangent is universal.

WP-315 tests the obvious support-side replacement. Around a prime pole, the intrinsic Mangoldt mass is `a_p~log p/p`, but bounded prime clusters occur on a much smaller logarithmic spacing scale. After translating by `log p` and scaling by `a_p`, arbitrarily many comparable prime atoms collapse into every fixed neighborhood; the normalized positive measures are not uniformly locally bounded and the Herglotz field has unbounded limsup response at mass-scale height.

WP-316 closes the opposite one-pole regime. If a moving node is chosen on a source gap where one focal Herglotz pole asymptotically dominates the positive boundary derivative, the canonically normalized scalar Julia response converges locally to a removable real constant. For the Mangoldt source, every nonconstant scalar support tangent must therefore retain order-one positive multipole background.

WP-317 closes **fixed finite multipole** geometry as a separate escape. For any finite positive atomic scalar Herglotz tangent with `N` poles, one regular normalized Julia reduction removes the original poles and produces exactly `N-1` positive interlacing poles. Repeated reduction gives `N->N-1->...->0`, while reverse augmentation is classical rational Herglotz continued-fraction data. The sign theorem is generic for arbitrary positive atoms and does not preserve a finite set of prime poles as an arithmetic carrier.

WP-318 closes the remaining hope that simply taking an **infinite** scalar positive measure makes Julia positivity arithmetic. At every regular node of a pure-measure scalar Herglotz function, derivative biasing produces a probability law

`d nu_t(x)=d^-1 (t-x)^-2 d mu(x)`

and the Julia response is exactly

`J_t[f](z)-beta = d (t-K_(nu_t)(z))`.

After affine normalization the tangent is `tau-K_(tilde nu)`. The Herglotz sign is universal for every probability law, and every gap-supported probability law is realizable by a positive matched control. Finite versus infinite support changes only which derivative-biased law appears; it does not create a new scalar positivity mechanism.

The scalar frontier is therefore no longer “find an infinite collective Julia tangent.” It is: prove a **source-specific theorem on the Mangoldt derivative-biased probability laws**—tightness, rigidity, a canonical exceptional limit, or another restriction unavailable to generic controls—and then couple that law to the Gamma/global counterterms through an independent canonical mechanism. If no such law exists, the construction must leave the audited pure-measure scalar category through an affine mass-at-infinity channel, matrix/operator-valued boundary structure, generalized resolvents, noninvertible maps, or nonseparable finite--archimedean coupling.
