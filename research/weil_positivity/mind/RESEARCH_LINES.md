## Lift the source-native pole tangent into a completed architecture beyond fixed local linear memory

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation`, `MI-011-prime-ray-marginals-do-not-determine-mixed-prime-coupling`, `MI-012-automorphic-multiplicity-does-not-prevent-global-scalarization`, `MI-013-critical-sector-transport-needs-new-global-arithmetic-structure`, `MI-014-nontrivial-zero-modes-screen-the-gamma-tangent-layer`.

WP-267--WP-278 leave a source-native infinite-volume pole continuum but no independent Gamma completion. WP-279 shows that nontrivial-zero modes screen the scalar Gamma layer; WP-280 shows that the canonical primitive can recover the central Gamma scalar only through an RH-equivalent asymptotic/sign condition.

WP-281 closes every fixed finite-dimensional stable linear response: a rational transfer cannot preserve DC while vanishing at all zero ordinates. WP-282 closes arbitrary positive completely-monotone relaxation continua: their Stieltjes transfers cannot vanish at even one finite nonzero frequency.

[WP-283](../findings/WP-283-fixed-compact-memory-filters-cannot-notch-the-zeta-zero-screen.md) now closes the broad fixed-window gap between those classes. A bounded signed or complex scalar readout of any fixed compact history has an entire transfer of finite exponential type and therefore only `O(T)` transmission zeros below `T`, while distinct simple critical-line zeta ordinates have `gg T log T` density. Fixed compact memory cannot notch the zero screen even when infinitely many oscillatory zeros are allowed.

For normalized positive moving windows, suppressing all simple zero modes up to `T` already requires memory length `L_T >= c log T`. The live linear escape is therefore genuinely **scale-nonlocal/growing-memory**, not merely “infinite-dimensional.” Other surviving categories are independently forced oscillatory noncompact responses, nonlinear mechanisms with a separate sign audit, or finite--archimedean coupling formed before scalarization.

## Treat pole provenance, zero fluctuations, memory growth and sign as different spectral layers

The pole tangent, Gamma completion and critical zero field are distinct. Finite state, positive relaxation and fixed compact memory fail for different reasons—rational zero count, positive-real geometry and exponential-type zero density—but all leave the same screening layer. A candidate that uses growing support or variation must price that nonlocality explicitly; a candidate that couples sectors before scalarization must explain its sign mechanism without importing the target divisor or an RH-equivalent primitive limit.
