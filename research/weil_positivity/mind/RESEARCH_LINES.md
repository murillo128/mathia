WP-391--WP-394 rule out exact positive prime-power selection in the canonical divisibility diagonal, the full bounded Bost--Connes positive cone, Woronowicz affiliation with the same unital source algebra, and positive unbounded forms whose canonical resolvent returns to that represented algebra.

WP-395 changes category to the critical factor but finds a diffuse centralizer `L^infinity(hat Z,m_Haar)` in which the standard countable arithmetic orbit is Haar-null. WP-396 shows that one nonzero modular/gauge frequency does not fix the problem, and WP-397 closes the immediate multi-frequency interference escape: every useful Fourier coefficient of a bounded positive element remains supported over the same positive centralizer floor.

WP-398 shows that quotienting away a centralizer containing the order unit destroys inherited order: the image of the positive cone fills the entire self-adjoint information quotient. WP-399 then shows that fixing a nonconstant centralizer shadow still leaves strongly and ultraweakly dense two-sided centered tangents on its support.

WP-400 narrows the source-relation escape. Any linear source-selected subspace stable under canonical shadow cutoffs inherits the same dense two-sided tangent geometry, so every normal linear sign functional forced only by positivity vanishes. WP-401 replaces discontinuous von Neumann projections by continuous `C*(d)` cutoffs inside the hereditary source C-star algebra and obtains **norm-dense** two-sided tangents. Missing source spectral projections therefore do not create an orientation mechanism by themselves.

WP-402 now closes the apparent “make the readout genuinely unbounded” continuation whenever the map remains closable. Let `D` be a real linear source domain stable under the canonical continuous cutoffs and `T:D->Y` a possibly unbounded linear map into a Banach space with pointed cone. If `d+h>=0` is required to place `T(h)` in that cone, every localized cutoff response is exactly zero. The tails `r_n=k-c_nkc_n` then satisfy `r_n->0` while `T(r_n)=T(k)`, so closability forces `T(k)=0`. Every such closable cutoff-stable orientation is trivial.

The surviving linear route is therefore narrower than “use an unbounded functional.” It must be source-nonlocal in a precise sense: the domain must fail stability under `k -> f_n(d) k f_n(d)`, the response must be deliberately nonclosable/singular, or its sign must not be an ordinary pointed-cone consequence of fixed-shadow positivity. Alternatively the route must change category to nonlinear/quadratic, one-sided, representation-changing, or a genuinely global finite--archimedean assembly.

## Keep selector existence, support, frequency, quotient order, tangent topology and destination sign distinct

Category enlargement can fail by being too programmable, returning through bounded transforms, erasing the arithmetic orbit as null, projecting a single frequency under positivity, forcing every multi-frequency coefficient to live over the same diffuse support, making the projected positive cone improper after the fixed component is forgotten, leaving dense two-sided fixed-shadow tangents, or leaving an explicit graph-defect sequence for every nonzero closable linear orientation. These are different obstructions.

A new proposal must state which arithmetic information remains invariant, how positivity is formed, what its fixed-point component is, where every useful frequency is supported, whether the post-quotient cone is proper, what topology controls the fixed-shadow tangent set, whether the defining source relation survives continuous source localization, and—if the readout is unbounded—whether its graph is closable. A successful linear relation must fail one of those gates for a source-forced reason and carry the final Weil sign through the resulting cross-scale coupling; noncentrality, ambient positivity, gauge-band selection, absence of source spectral projections and unboundedness alone are no longer enough.
