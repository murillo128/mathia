## Cross the boundary without collapsing to a stationary positive Nyman carrier, or delay mixed-shell selection

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm`, `MI-039-enriched-semigroups-inherit-obstructed-scalar-shadows-or-universal-lifts` through `MI-058-finite-interior-riesz-selectors-collapse-to-the-mangoldt-line`.

WP-374--WP-383 show that exact mixed-shell nullity collapses every fixed-interior response: finite packets and then every compact-open-continuous Banach/Hilbert-valued linear response reduce the primitive-shell family to one Mangoldt line. This makes a genuine boundary/scale-varying response, or delayed selection after finite--archimedean interaction, necessary.

WP-384 supplies a canonical boundary escape but classicalizes it. The fixed-base fresh-prime boundary profiles have positive limiting energy and Mellin multipliers `c_m(tau)=m^(-i tau) prod_{p|m}(1-p^(-1/2+i tau))`; their Gram is the Nyman modulus-square carrier with finite Euler factors, and mixed shells remain positive. WP-385 closes every attempt to repair that support failure by merely changing to another closed dilation-invariant positive geometry: each `c_m` is boundedly invertible on the real Mellin axis, so any such form obeys `alpha_m^2 Q(h_1) <= Q(h_m) <= beta_m^2 Q(h_1)`. Nulling one mixed base already nulls the prime packet.

The surviving route therefore has to leave the stationary positive Mellin-multiplier class, not merely choose a better weight inside it. What source-forced nonstationary or cross-frequency boundary coupling retains the finite arithmetic distinction and can couple it to the archimedean/polar terms with the correct Weil sign? Alternatively, can a finite-`q` prelimit or an indefinite finite--archimedean interaction preserve the needed information until after global assembly, so that exact mixed-shell selection is imposed only at the destination?

## Keep selector existence, topology, stationary positivity and destination sign distinct

WP-381 proves that a bounded exact Mangoldt selector exists in the pointed geometry; WP-382--WP-383 show that exact selection can collapse a large interior response space to rank one; WP-384 shows that moving to a genuine boundary layer can preserve nonzero energy while still producing the wrong positive resource class; WP-385 shows that arbitrary fixed nonnegative Mellin reweighting of that boundary class cannot repair the arithmetic support. A viable Weil-positive construction must therefore identify which hypothesis is genuinely left—compact-open interior continuity, boundary dilation stationarity, positivity before finite--archimedean coupling, or the fixed-base boundary limit—and then derive the correct finite coefficient and destination sign in that new category.
