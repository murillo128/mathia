WP-312 closes every **finite** fixed-real-node Julia--Nevanlinna chain. A regular reduction adds `+2q/d` to the `log(y)/y` coefficient, while a Mangoldt-pole reduction leaves that order zero but adds a positive amount to the already-positive arithmetic `y^-2` curvature.

WP-313 now isolates the only infinite-chain loophole. For a unit-log Pick function define the translation-invariant first defect `L` at the `log(y)/y` scale and, when `L=0`, the second curvature `C` at the `y^-2` scale. Along every finite fixed-node reduction chain from the reflected Mangoldt source,

`L(f_N)=pi sum_regular 1/d_j >= 0`,

and the first regular step makes this permanently positive. A pure pole chain keeps `L=0` but has

`C(f_N)=A_M + sum_{n in S_N} Lambda(n)log n/(n+1) > 0`.

If all Mangoldt poles are stripped, that positive curvature diverges already on primes like `(1/2)(log X)^2`. Therefore an infinite fixed-node chain whose convergence preserves the relevant high-energy invariant still cannot reach the Gamma end `L=0`, `C=-1/24`.

The surviving scalar-looking escape is no longer “take infinitely many reductions and prove convergence.” It must be **nonuniform at infinity** at exactly the discriminator scale: a moving boundary layer, scale-dependent reference/node choice, or another source-native mechanism for which the chain limit and high-energy limit fail to commute, while positivity remains independently justified. Otherwise the route must leave the audited scalar class through operator/matrix-valued boundary structure, generalized resolvents, noninvertible maps, an added archimedean channel or nonseparable finite--archimedean coupling.

## Treat mass class, curvature orientation, gauge rigidity, transform admissibility, asymptotic neutrality and limit stability as separate gates

Matching `log y` establishes only the infinite-mass class. Producing a nonuniversal `y^-2` coefficient establishes arithmetic sensitivity but not the correct orientation. WP-307 closes positive affine input recentering; WP-308 closes constant projective output gauge; WP-309 collapses holomorphic scalar automorphism families to the same constant class. WP-310 adds the admissibility gate: a positive Herglotz source can lie outside the domain of a desired transform.

WP-311--WP-312 add a distinct gate: **an admissible positivity-preserving transform can damage the target asymptotic hierarchy even when it is canonical.** Fixed-interior Schur stripping and regular fixed-boundary Julia reduction insert a larger positive `log(y)/y` term; pure pole boundary stripping preserves that order but worsens the wrong-sign curvature.

WP-313 adds the infinite-stage gate. Local uniform convergence of Pick functions is not enough: the limiting operation must also transport whichever high-energy coefficient is being compared with Gamma. If it does, the finite-stage monotonic defects survive and the route is closed. If it does not, the noncommutation is not a technical nuisance but the mathematical mechanism that must be derived and controlled.

A future transform must therefore prove admissibility, positivity, discriminator-scale neutrality and the required limit interchange — or explicitly derive why that interchange fails in a source-native way.

## Use matched controls against universal leading scale, post-hoc recoding and unstable limits

Unit-density positive measures control the leading logarithm, while the Mangoldt curvature sign is source-specific. WP-307--WP-309 show that affine or projective scalar gauge cannot flip that sign while preserving the required logarithmic end. WP-310 shows that the standard moment-Schur algorithm cannot start on the critical source without changing its normalization or positivity class. WP-311--WP-312 show that the natural fixed interior and finite fixed boundary scalar alternatives are defined and positivity-preserving but move larger subleading terms or curvature in the wrong direction.

WP-313 prevents an infinite fixed-node chain from being accepted merely because it converges on compact subsets. Any such proposal must test whether `L` or `C` passes to the limit. If it does, regular steps produce a monotone positive first defect and pole stripping produces monotone positive curvature; exhaustive pole stripping even diverges. A genuine scalar escape must therefore exhibit and justify a moving high-energy boundary layer rather than hide the known defect beyond every fixed compact set.

Future constructions must alter the geometry or realization before final scalar comparison and must prove both **positivity/admissibility** and **asymptotic compatibility of the limiting operation** rather than importing either by recoding, target-dependent cancellation or an unjustified moving normalization.
