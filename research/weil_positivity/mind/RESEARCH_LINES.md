This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable Weil-positivity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Find arithmetic coupling that survives automorphic scalarization

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation`, `MI-011-prime-ray-marginals-do-not-determine-mixed-prime-coupling`, `MI-012-automorphic-multiplicity-does-not-prevent-global-scalarization`.

WP-216--WP-241 show that correct critical prime amplitudes, positivity, bounded-order marginals, geometric channel manipulations, and ordinary scalar postprocessing still do not produce the Weil orientation once the arithmetic has collapsed into a central scalar normalizer.

WP-242--WP-244 now test three natural matrix-valued escapes. Squarefree `Gamma_0(N)` multi-cusp scattering is one common completed-zeta scalar times a constant-diagonalizable tensor of local level factors. Nonsquarefree congruence level introduces more Eisenstein sectors, but the canonical newform decomposition separates them into orthogonal oldclasses, each carrying its own scalar Dirichlet-`L` completion; the Riemann-bearing trivial-nebentypus sectors remain parallel scalar channels rather than irreducibly coupled arithmetic. Passing to split higher rank does not fix the spherical sector either: Gindikin--Karpelevich intertwiners carry completed-zeta products only as scalar weights on Weyl edges, and standard normalization removes those arithmetic scalars, leaving arithmetic-free Weyl permutations.

The live escape must therefore change category more substantially. Candidate mechanisms include a canonical source-defined coupling between distinct Eisenstein/newform sectors, non-spherical inducing data whose normalized intertwiners retain genuinely operator-valued arithmetic, or a multidirectional derivative/tensor of scalar normalizers that produces the required sign before positive reduction. The construction must show where mixed-prime dependence and Weil orientation survive **before** orthogonal/direct-sum or spherical scalarization.

## Treat cusp multiplicity, parallel L-channels, and spherical higher rank as controls

More channels do not imply more irreducible arithmetic information. If a fixed or canonical basis exposes a direct sum of scalar global normalizers, or normalization divides out the zeta factor before positivity is used, matrix notation has not escaped the scalar obstruction. A survivor must exhibit a source-forced coupling not removable by the natural spectral decomposition.
