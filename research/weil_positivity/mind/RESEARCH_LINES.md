WP-302 audits moving kernels. Finite-dimensional fixed-generator unitary motion has constant intrinsic geometry and bounded regular relative readouts, incompatible with logarithmically growing archimedean behavior. At the opposite extreme, a freely chosen rank-one projector path can program any smooth positive metric density. WP-303 closes the canonical fixed-source spectral interpolation: spectral cuts of one self-adjoint source operator commute, are norm-discrete, and have zero strong derivative wherever differentiable. Free positive compression is again too broad because Naimark dilation makes the category POVM-universal.

WP-304 now closes the simplest nonlocal resolvent escape. For self-adjoint `H` and bounded source incidence `V`,

`M_V(z)=V^*(H-z)^(-1)V`

has finite compressed spectral mass and satisfies `||M_V(iy)||<=||V||^2/y`. A bounded source resolvent therefore cannot generate the logarithmically growing Gamma/digamma scale.

The pole-normalized positive-energy Mangoldt measure gives the narrow infinite-mass escape. Its cumulative mass is `R+O(1)`, so its regularized Herglotz transform has

`Re m_+(iy)=-log y+O(1)`, `Im m_+(iy)=pi/2+O(1/y)`.

But the leading logarithm fails arithmetic specificity: every positive measure with cumulative mass `R+O(1)` has the same asymptotic, including Lebesgue measure. Worse, the canonical even `+/- log n` carrier cancels the real logarithm exactly on the imaginary axis. The surviving information must therefore lie in a source-forced orientation, renormalization/subtraction law, subleading spectral remainder, pole structure or a genuinely coupled finite--archimedean observable.

The live route is no longer “find a transform that makes a logarithm.” That is cheap. A viable construction must force, **before target comparison**, an unbounded/generalized boundary channel, scattering/determinant-type object, varying noncommuting operator family, or finite--archimedean coupling whose orientation and subtraction are source-determined and whose subleading response distinguishes the Riemann Gamma sector from arbitrary unit-density Herglotz controls.

## Treat localization, source law, completion, topology, mass class, orientation, readout and sign as distinct gates

WP-300 shows that source-visible local curvature plus exact positive Mangoldt extraction does not supply global Weil positivity. WP-301 adds readout loss. WP-302 adds the motion-law gate. WP-303 adds a topology/category gate: fixed spectral calculus is singular and commuting, while unrestricted positive representation is programmable.

WP-304 adds two more separations. **Finite spectral mass versus infinite renormalized mass** determines whether logarithmic growth is even possible, and **orientation versus evenization** determines whether the real logarithm survives. Even after both choices, unit-density matched controls show that the leading log is not Riemann-specific. Any candidate must therefore state what source structure creates mixed-prime relations, how finite and archimedean places couple before positivity, which law fixes the parameter-dependent operator/boundary object, which mass/renormalization class it lives in, why the one-sided orientation is canonical, which subleading spectrum survives the readout, and why the resulting quadratic form has the Weil sign.

## Use matched controls against universal curvature, common-kernel selectors, singular spectral cuts, programmable motion and unit-density Herglotz tails

The fermionic sign is genuine curvature but not rational-prime-specific curvature. WP-299--WP-304 now give a layered control family: curvature may be unreachable from the ordinary source; reachable only through a universal Clifford law; non-scalar yet reduced by a frustration-free readout to a universal fixed-space predicate; moving but homogeneous and bounded; source-generated by a fixed spectral cut but singular/commuting; freely representable through POVM dilation; bounded-resolvent positive but too small; or logarithmically growing only because an infinite positive measure has unit asymptotic density.

A future construction must survive these controls before geometric or Gamma-like agreement is counted as arithmetic progress. In particular, reproducing `log y` after a standard Nevanlinna subtraction is not evidence of a prime-derived archimedean factor unless the source also forces the orientation and nonuniversal remainder that the matched unit-density controls do not reproduce.
