## Keep selector existence, source selection, operator category and destination sign distinct

The recent sequence separates three different facts. Arbitrary diagonal spectral calculus can encode prime powers; stationary positive interface geometry can realize the selector only as an explicitly chosen mask; and the canonical divisibility diagonal cannot realize any nonzero exact prime-power-supported observable at all. Meanwhile the Bost--Connes critical Gram is genuinely source-forced but its local Weil orientation remains an indefinite defect rather than a positive completion.

A new proposal must therefore state exactly which operator category supplies the missing selector/orientation, why that category and its domain are fixed by the source before the target support is known, and how the construction survives the archimedean/polar/global sign theorem. Merely enlarging the diagonal algebra, choosing a favorable mask, or exhibiting positivity after target-dependent restriction does not answer the source-selection problem.
