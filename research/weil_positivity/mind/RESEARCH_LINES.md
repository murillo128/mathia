## Find a source-derived sign theorem outside scalar shadows and universal enriched lifts

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation` through `MI-042-faithful-exact-law-ucp-readouts-force-multiplicative-domain-rigidity`.

WP-319--WP-323 extend the matched-control closure through bounded-gap Julia families, exact finite multipoint Pick matrices and finite jets, all reducing to universal positive-source Gram structure. WP-324 shows the analogous translation/Laplace sign mechanisms are universal or have the wrong orientation.

WP-325--WP-327 close the direct scalar additive-semigroup escape across Muraki's five natural products. Classical, free, monotone and anti-monotone divisibility reject the exact pole-normalized Mangoldt law, while Boolean divisibility accepts every probability law and is therefore nonselective.

WP-328 tests the two closest enriched enlargements. Divisibility descends through every semigroup homomorphism. Since the exact Mangoldt law is freely indecomposable, no bi-freely infinitely divisible planar law can retain it as either scalar marginal, and no c-freely infinitely divisible pair can retain it as the free reference coordinate. Moving the source to the outer c-free coordinate with trivial reference gives the opposite failure: `(mu,delta_0)` is c-freely infinitely divisible for every scalar law because the convolution reduces to universal Boolean convolution.

WP-329 closes the weakest operator-valued escape without incorrectly forcing scalar free convolution through scalarization. At every finite source cutoff, the exact law is the scalarization of a deterministic element of the amalgamation algebra `B_R=C(Sigma_R)` with `E=id`; the `B_R`-valued point law is freely infinitely divisible simply because the deterministic coordinate divides inside the base algebra. The same operator-valued law survives arbitrary changes of the scalar weights. Algebraically, the full source has the universal lift `B=C[t]`, `X=t`, with every higher `B`-valued cumulant zero; all arithmetic sits only in the scalar state `tau_s` used to read out the law.

WP-330 closes the immediate nondeterministic repair. A direct-sum amalgamated variable can contain a genuine centered semicircular fluctuation with nonzero completely positive covariance while the scalarization state annihilates the entire fluctuation summand. The scalar moments then remain exactly the Mangoldt law, and changing the scalar weights still changes the source readout without changing the enriched noisy law. Thus **nonzero covariance, higher cumulants or nondeterminism are not source resources when the destination readout kills the ideal they generate**.

WP-331 closes the complementary faithful-readout repair for conditional expectations. If `b=E(X)` is the arithmetic base coordinate and the scalar law of self-adjoint `X` under `tau circ E` is required to remain exactly the scalar law of `b`, then conditional variance gives `tau(E((X-b)^2))=0`. When `tau` is faithful on that positive sector—as it is for the finite Mangoldt state with strictly positive weights—this forces `E((X-b)^2)=0`; complete positivity then kills every centered `B`-valued moment. If `E` is faithful, `X=b` outright.

WP-332 removes conditional expectation as the load-bearing hypothesis. For any UCP readout `Phi:A->B` with faithful state `tau`, equality of the scalar second moment of a self-adjoint source observable `X` and its compressed image `b=Phi(X)` forces the Kadison defect `Phi(X^2)-Phi(X)^2` to vanish. Hence `X` lies in the multiplicative domain of `Phi`, and in Stinespring form the compressed subspace reduces the source observable. Exact same-observable law preservation plus faithful positive visibility therefore kills the off-diagonal source coupling even for general UCP compression.

WP-333 identifies the sharp finite-to-infinite stability boundary of that exact rigidity. At cutoff `R`, if `m_R` is the least output-state atom, then the positive Kadison defect satisfies

`||D_R|| <= tau_R(D_R)/m_R`.

Thus approximate multiplicative-domain rigidity requires the scalar second-moment defect to be `o(m_R)`, not merely `o(1)`. For the finite Mangoldt source `m_R` tends to zero at least exponentially, and an explicit atom-splitting UCP channel has scalar laws converging in total variation and all fixed polynomial moments while its Stinespring source coupling remains order one. Pointwise faithful finite cutoffs therefore do **not** yield a cutoff-uniform no-go from scalar-law convergence alone.

Together WP-330--WP-333 refine the readout boundary. Exact finite-cutoff same-observable preservation under faithful readout collapses the coupling, but asymptotically vanishing scalar error can hide order-one coupling in a moving thin tail when the faithfulness modulus degenerates. This escape is universal under matched controls, so it is not itself a Weil sign mechanism. A viable operator-valued route must either prove uniform relative control strong enough to close the escape, or exploit a **source-forced moving coupling** whose placement and final finite-plus-archimedean response are genuinely arithmetic before scalarization.

Genuinely source-coupled operator-valued free geometry, nondegenerate c-free first-coordinate constructions, deformed/multiplicative structures and direct finite--archimedean couplings remain open only when they evade both the exact same-observable rigidity and the nonselective thin-tail matched control. Each proposal must identify exactly which scalar shadows survive, which mixed data carry arithmetic, how the readout couples to them, what cutoff-uniform norm or relative-faithfulness scale is controlled, and why positivity is neither impossible by shadow descent nor automatic on matched controls.

## Separate source fingerprint from source-selective sign

Shifted-lattice support, Mangoldt coefficients, exact multipoint entries, finite jets and prime-power atom gaps are source-specific. The sign provenance can still be universal, the desired positive architecture can fail because source specificity descends into an incompatible scalar shadow, or an enriched positive object can be universal while the source is stored only in its scalarization state or in a fluctuation sector killed by that state.

WP-322--WP-324 exhibit universal resolvent-Gram/Hankel signs. WP-325--WP-327 exhibit scalar divisibility rejection versus Boolean universality. WP-328 adds homomorphic-shadow obstructions in enriched categories. WP-329 adds the deterministic operator-valued failure: a freely divisible object can exist for every scalar source when the enriched law is deterministic and all specificity is deferred to the readout state. WP-330 shows that the failure persists with genuine operator-valued noise whenever scalarization annihilates its fluctuation ideal. WP-331--WP-332 show that making the same scalarization faithful while insisting on exact same-observable source-law preservation does not repair the mechanism: positivity of the Schwarz/variance defect collapses the source coupling instead. WP-333 then shows that this exact finite-cutoff rigidity is badly conditioned when the least state weight vanishes, so asymptotic scalar-law fidelity alone is not a uniform substitute.

Future arguments should state the precise arithmetic feature that a broad matched class cannot fake while preserving a genuinely nonautomatic sign theorem. A source-specific obstruction to positivity is not a substitute for a positive source theorem, and enriched mixed data are not source-selective when they are annihilated by the destination quotient/readout, forced to vanish by exact-law preservation plus faithful positive visibility, or hidden in a moving low-mass sector that the same matched controls can reproduce.
