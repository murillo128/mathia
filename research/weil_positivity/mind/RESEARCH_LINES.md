# Weil-positivity research lines

This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Weil-Positivity evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Force a genuinely one-sided source relation, then prove its arithmetic provenance

WP-391--WP-403 show that selector enlargement, bounded Bost--Connes positivity, ordinary affiliation, modular-frequency decomposition, quotient order, cutoff breaking, closable unbounded linear readouts and finite-codimensional global constraints do not by themselves produce a Weil-oriented linear functional. Dense two-sided order-bounded tangent geometry survives these changes. WP-404 proves the obstruction is geometrically sharp: a sufficiently thin same-shadow slice can be one-sided, but a matched nonarithmetic construction can manufacture the same orientation.

The surviving linear route therefore has two independent gates. The admissible source relation must genuinely change the closure of the reachable two-sided tangent core, and that change must be forced by arithmetic/source structure rather than by generic thinness. A one-sided slice without provenance is not evidence.

## Make any enlarged positive block route carry source-forced cycle holonomy

WP-405 closes the most canonical Bost--Connes one-sided candidate. For a pointed analytic half-spectrum of a compact abelian gauge action, self-adjointness pairs every nontrivial frequency with its inverse, so the self-adjoint part—and hence the ambient positive cone—collapses to the fixed-point algebra. In the Bost--Connes specialization the positive semigroup `N^times` is genuinely source-canonical and one-sided, but ambient positivity deletes exactly the nonzero gauge frequencies that carried the orientation.

WP-406 shows that moving positivity to a larger `2x2` linking matrix escapes this literal collapse but becomes universal: every bounded element has a positive scalar-diagonal completion once the diagonal exceeds its norm, and scalar phase is invisible. WP-407 extends the phase obstruction to arbitrary acyclic block architectures. For central scalar edge phases `omega_ij`, every forest phase field is a vertex coboundary `omega_ij=u_i overline{u_j}`, so diagonal corner unitaries remove all phases while leaving even arbitrary source-forced diagonal blocks fixed. Positivity on a tree, chain or star can constrain amplitudes but cannot select the scalar arithmetic phase.

Cycles are the exact first place where scalar phase survives corner gauge. After switching a spanning forest to phase one, the remaining invariants are cycle holonomies, with `|E|-|V|+c` independent `U(1)` degrees of freedom; a scalar triangle shows that changing only its holonomy can flip positivity. This is a sharp structural boundary, not yet an arithmetic mechanism.

The live enlarged-category question is therefore no longer merely to add non-scalar diagonal data or more blocks. It is to derive a **canonical cyclic coupling whose holonomy is source-forced**, survives matched generalized/nonarithmetic controls and the required quotient/tangent reductions, and whose positive sign recovers the finite plus archimedean Weil destination without choosing the holonomy to fit the target sign. Acyclic enlargement, universal scalar-diagonal completion, or a cycle whose phase is programmable by hand does not qualify.

## Keep source canonicity, order and destination sign separate

A candidate can be canonical yet order-trivial, one-sided yet nonpositive, positive yet universal, or genuinely asymmetric yet nonarithmetic. Future arguments must therefore identify where the asymmetry lives, which phase data survive self-adjointness and corner gauge, what additional relation makes any cyclic dilation selective, and whether matched nonarithmetic controls satisfy the same constraints. Noncentrality, analytic support, thinness, unboundedness, block positivity and graph cyclicity are representation features until a source-specific exclusion theorem survives the destination map.
