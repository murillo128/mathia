# Weil-positivity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable Weil-positivity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Force genuinely new finite--archimedean structure beyond scalar gauge freedom

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation` through `MI-023-holomorphic-scalar-automorphism-gauges-cannot-vary-spectrally`.

WP-293--WP-303 close scalar/fixed-positive recovery, finite multiplicative-equivariant depth, flat transport and freely programmable moving representations. WP-304 identifies the mass-class barrier, while WP-305 finds the first source-specific remainder and its wrong sign: the reflected Mangoldt curvature is positive whereas the Riemann-Gamma coefficient is negative.

WP-307 closes orientation-preserving affine changes of the **spectral input** through the positive pole-normalized curvature invariant. WP-308 closes constant scalar Herglotz **output** recodings: non-affine real Möbius maps compactify the logarithmic end, while end-preserving maps reduce to positive affine recodings and retain the wrong curvature sign.

WP-309 now removes the apparent loophole of allowing that scalar automorphism gauge to depend holomorphically on the spectral variable. If `Phi(z,w)` is jointly holomorphic and every slice `w -> Phi(z,w)` is an automorphism of the Herglotz half-plane, Schwarz--Pick equality forces the family to be constant in `z`. Thus a spectrally varying **invertible scalar coordinate gauge** adds no freedom beyond WP-308.

The remaining route must therefore add genuine structure rather than a more flexible coordinate description: a noninvertible Herglotz self-map with independent meaning, a source-moment Schur/Nevanlinna transform, compression/generalized resolvent, operator/matrix-valued boundary sector, added archimedean Herglotz channel, determinant/scattering object, or nonseparable finite--archimedean coupling. Each must derive its extra data and positivity/sign theorem from the source rather than label it gauge freedom.

## Treat mass class, curvature orientation and gauge rigidity as separate gates

Matching `log y` establishes only the infinite-mass class. Producing a nonuniversal `y^-2` coefficient establishes arithmetic sensitivity but not the correct orientation. WP-307 closes positive affine input recentering; WP-308 closes constant projective output gauge; WP-309 shows that joint holomorphy plus pointwise scalar invertibility collapses the spectral-variable automorphism family back to the same constant class.

Dropping one WP-309 hypothesis is a real category change, not a technical escape. A noninvertible map can add a positive channel, and a source-dependent function-level Schur transform can use moments/operator realizations. Those remain open precisely because they introduce new data rather than reparameterize the same scalar Herglotz response.

## Use matched controls against universal leading scale and post-hoc recoding

Unit-density positive measures control the leading logarithm, while the Mangoldt curvature sign is source-specific. WP-307--WP-309 show that neither affine spectral recentering nor constant or holomorphically varying scalar automorphism gauge can flip that sign while preserving the required logarithmic end. Future constructions must alter the geometry or realization **before** the scalar response is finally compared with the Gamma target.
