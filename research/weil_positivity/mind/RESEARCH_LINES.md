## Find a source-derived sign theorem outside scalar shadows and universal enriched lifts

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation` through `MI-042-faithful-exact-law-ucp-readouts-force-multiplicative-domain-rigidity`.

WP-293--WP-328 close broad scalar and enriched semigroup routes: many natural positivity/divisibility structures are either incompatible with the exact source shadow or universal on matched controls. WP-329--WP-330 show that operator-valued enrichment is likewise nonselective when all arithmetic remains in the scalarization state or the readout annihilates the fluctuation ideal.

WP-331--WP-332 give the opposite rigidity. For a faithful finite-cutoff UCP readout preserving the exact second moment of the same self-adjoint source observable, the positive Kadison defect vanishes; the observable lies in the multiplicative domain and its off-diagonal Stinespring coupling disappears.

WP-333 identifies the sharp nonuniformity of this finite-cutoff theorem. If `m_R` is the least output-state atom, then `||D_R|| <= tau_R(D_R)/m_R`; approximate rigidity therefore requires scalar defect `o(m_R)`, not merely `o(1)`. On three consecutive points of the intrinsic dyadic prime-power ray, a support-preserving martingale UCP map fixes the source coordinate pointwise while retaining an order-one Kadison/Stinespring defect on a moving atom of vanishing mass. Total variation, fixed moments and every fixed Wasserstein metric all miss it.

WP-334 shows that replacing those weak metrics by ordinary information divergence still does not globalize the rigidity. A lazy version of the same intrinsic three-point map keeps source and transformed laws mutually absolutely continuous with likelihood ratio bounded above and below by fixed positive constants. Nevertheless **every fixed finite Csiszar `f`-divergence in both directions and every fixed finite-order Renyi divergence tend to zero**, while the operator defect stays order one. The threshold occurs at max-divergence: `D_infinity` remains bounded away from zero because it controls the worst likelihood ratio on the rare atom.

Thus the needed scalar topology is not merely “stronger than total variation” or “entropy-like.” It must control rare moving source states uniformly: an `L^infinity`/max-likelihood-ratio type condition, a defect estimate relative to the vanishing faithfulness modulus, or direct map/operator control. Even bounded density ratios plus two-sided vanishing KL are insufficient.

A surviving operator-valued route must then do more than control topology: the remaining coupling must be source-forced and destination-visible through a sign theorem that matched martingale controls cannot reproduce, or the observable/interface must change before the same-observable multiplicative-domain rigidity applies.

## Separate source fingerprint from source-selective sign

Shifted-lattice support, Mangoldt coefficients, prime-power gaps and exact scalar laws are source-specific fingerprints. WP-333--WP-334 show that even exact support preservation, nearest-neighbor motion on a canonical arithmetic ray, mutual absolute continuity and bounded likelihood ratios do not make a positive operator coupling arithmetic: the same martingale mechanism is generic. Sign provenance must fail on a matched control, not merely carry arithmetic labels.

## Preserve readout conditioning and map topology as distinct gates

Normalization, algebraic category, scalar shadows, fluctuation visibility, faithfulness modulus, scalar-law topology and map-level control are independent. Pointwise faithful cutoffs can become arbitrarily ill-conditioned; averaged scalar topologies—including KL and every fixed finite Renyi order—can ignore an order-one coupling concentrated on vanishing source mass. Future constructions must state which topology controls the actual mixed data consumed by the Weil destination and whether that control is uniform on rare moving atoms. The WP-334 calibration says that finite averaged divergences are not enough; the first scalar topology in this family that sees the constructed defect is the essential-supremum/max-divergence endpoint.
