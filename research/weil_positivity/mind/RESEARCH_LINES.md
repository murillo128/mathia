This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable weil-positivity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Seek a source-forced completion that changes the analytic category before scalarization

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`, `MI-006-finite-mass-positive-completion-forces-mixed-prime-coupling-and-critical-roughness`, `MI-008-critical-half-density-exposes-archimedean-phase-only-before-gram-scalarization`, and `MI-009-finite-regular-passive-enlargements-cannot-realize-the-gamma-divisor`.

WP-179--WP-182 show that scalar lossless order is too rigid, scalar dissipation is too flexible, and a canonical Pythagorean completion can restore a finite positive two-channel kernel only by moving the ordered object away from the scalar target phase. That completion is universal function theory unless its compensating channel is source-forced.

WP-183--WP-185 close the obvious finite regular projective repairs for the exact Gamma relative phase. WP-186 then shows that pointwise PSD lifting can encode the phase while losing the sign of its velocity. WP-187 closes the most direct nonlocal Hardy repair: the canonical compression yields two positive Hankel leakage defects, but their reversal-odd difference is an indefinite Toeplitz self-commutator for every ordinary smooth finite-window Gamma phase, while the exact phase is too rough at the Cayley endpoint for both canonical leakage traces to remain finite.

The live completion must therefore change the analytic category in a mathematically substantive way -- for example through a source-forced singular/domain-changing, infinite, matrix/noncommutative, relative-index, or other non-rational construction -- and must state the replacement coercivity theorem. Merely adding finite bounded channels, pointwise PSD geometry, or a universal Hardy compression around an already fitted Gamma response is classified.

## Derive mixed-prime incidence inside the same positive completion

Pairwise resultants, scalar radial filling, fixed finite arity, refinement-positive kernels, and separated completions do not manufacture irreducible mixed-prime geometry absent upstream. A survivor must carry a source-forced mixed relation before the sign theorem and show that finite-prime and real-place/defect sectors enter one common coercive object.

## Do not infer signed Weil density from PSD boundary lifts or unordered leakage energies

WP-186 supplies the pointwise orientation control: the canonical rank-one positive projector attached to a unimodular boundary phase exists for every phase, and its positive metric data lose the sign of phase velocity.

WP-187 shows that nonlocality alone does not repair this. Hardy compression gives two canonical PSD defect forms `D_in` and `D_out`, but phase reversal exchanges them and the signed orientation is their difference. For source-preserving smooth finite-window Gamma phases that difference is nonzero, trace zero, and indefinite. For the exact Gamma phase the two-positive-energy decomposition is singular at the `H^{1/2}` threshold.

A useful boundary-positive mechanism must therefore make the **ordered** leakage channel source-forced, or couple finite-prime and archimedean data inside a larger coercive operator before defect subtraction. One-sided inner/passive behavior is a valid positive control only when the source actually forces it; hand-inserted winding or regularization is not.

## Treat every category exit as successful only when the replacement order survives the full completion

A vector completion can restore positivity after scalar dissipation, but WP-183--WP-187 show that finite projective analytic completion, pointwise PSD lifting, and the canonical Hardy leakage splitting can still erase the exact phase, its orientation, or finite trace control. Every category escape should state exactly which completed object is positive, which component carries the signed target term, what source theorem fixes the compensating channel or singular renormalization, how finite-prime incidence enters that same object, and how the sign survives the final quotient.
