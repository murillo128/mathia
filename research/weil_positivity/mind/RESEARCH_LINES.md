# Weil-positivity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable Weil-positivity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Derive a nonlocal source object that changes spectral realization before asking it to reproduce Weil positivity

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation`, `MI-011-prime-ray-marginals-do-not-determine-mixed-prime-coupling`, `MI-012-automorphic-multiplicity-does-not-prevent-global-scalarization`, `MI-013-critical-sector-transport-needs-new-global-arithmetic-structure`.

WP-267--WP-273 show that hostability, category descent, scalar sign and localizable compression all fail as free routes. A surviving positive compression must be globally nonlocal and reconstruct continuum mass from arithmetic samples.

WP-274 shows that **nonlocal inverse representation is still not enough**. Pole normalization turns the critical Mangoldt carrier into the positive Herglotz measure with masses `Lambda(n)/(n+1)`. Classical Clark inversion can manufacture a model space with that sampling law, and de Branges inverse spectral theory can manufacture a positive trace-normalized canonical-system Hamiltonian from the same target measure. Both are universal inverse realizations when the desired carrier is supplied first.

WP-275 adds a third gate. The canonical arithmetic log-energy representation is pure point, while the pole continuum carries nonatomic absolutely continuous multiplication spectrum. Any bounded map on an invariant source domain that exactly intertwines the two modulation groups must vanish. Consequently a nonnegative graph compression invariant under the diagonal canonical time flow is trivial on the continuum side. The same obstruction applies to the source-native Fock carrier.

The surviving route must therefore reverse and alter the construction before positivity: derive an inner function, canonical system, de Branges Hamiltonian, mixed/noncommutative boundary object, or other globally nonlocal structure from independent Mathia/source geometry **and** let that construction break, twist, enlarge, or change the canonical pure-point spectral realization before comparison with the pole continuum. Only then ask whether its pre-existing sampling/Weyl/positivity law reproduces the Mangoldt, Gamma and polar sectors in the correct Weil orientation.

## Treat hostability, scalar sign, localizability, inverse realizability, independent selection and spectral-type compatibility separately

WP-273 makes nonlocality necessary. WP-274 shows that a positive nonlocal host can be reverse-engineered from the target. WP-275 shows that an independently motivated host still cannot simply be a bounded exact time-covariant bridge from the natural pure-point source carrier to the continuum: spectral type kills that interface before arithmetic weights matter.

The remaining discriminator is therefore stronger than “find a positive nonlocal representation.” The representation must be independently selected and its finite--archimedean coupling must arise through a mechanism that is not merely inverse realization and not an exact pure-point-to-continuum modulation intertwiner.
