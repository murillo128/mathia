# Weil-positivity research lines

This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Weil-Positivity evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Force a genuinely one-sided source relation, then prove its arithmetic provenance

WP-391--WP-403 show that selector enlargement, bounded Bost--Connes positivity, ordinary affiliation, modular-frequency decomposition, quotient order, cutoff breaking, closable unbounded linear readouts and finite-codimensional global constraints do not by themselves produce a Weil-oriented linear functional. Dense two-sided order-bounded tangent geometry survives these changes. WP-404 proves the obstruction is geometrically sharp: a sufficiently thin same-shadow slice can be one-sided, but a matched nonarithmetic construction can manufacture the same orientation.

The surviving linear route therefore has two independent gates. The admissible source relation must genuinely change the closure of the reachable two-sided tangent core, and that change must be forced by arithmetic/source structure rather than by generic thinness. A one-sided slice without provenance is not evidence.

## Make any enlarged positive block route carry source-forced non-native holonomy or incidence

WP-405 closes the most canonical Bost--Connes one-sided candidate: self-adjoint positivity inside a pointed analytic half-spectrum collapses to the fixed-point algebra. WP-406 shows that a generic positive `2x2` dilation escapes that literal collapse only universally and phase-blindly. WP-407 extends the phase obstruction to every acyclic central-scalar block architecture: forest phases are vertex coboundaries and gauge away, while cycle holonomy is the first scalar phase invariant that positivity can see.

WP-408 tests that surviving invariant against the canonical Bost--Connes source itself and closes the honest operator-valued version as well. The native commensurability groupoid has nontrivial isotropy only at `rho=0`, which is null for the critical `KMS_1` Haar measure; the scale-retaining dual groupoid is free everywhere. Consequently every normalized group-valued 1-cocycle and every honest unitary groupoid representation, including noncommutative varying-fibre transport, has identity holonomy on the relevant native cycles. Noncentrality cannot manufacture balanced source holonomy when the coefficient assignment genuinely descends to the bare groupoid arrow.

WP-409--WP-411 then close scalar projective and passive full-fibre repairs at the canonical projection readout. Projective scale curvature can exist, but the divisibility range projections and normalized KMS GCD/Poisson Gram do not retain it; arbitrary full-fibre unitary decorations cancel before the state is applied. WP-412--WP-414 show that isolated off-diagonal observables are killed by KMS spectral selection unless total scale degree balances, while equal-scale central or noncentral path families reduce after scalarization to ordinary phase or tracial unitary-correlation Grams. Their positivity is generic state-Gram positivity rather than a Weil-oriented sign.

WP-415 extends that obstruction to ordinary non-full Hilbert correspondences. Scalarized module path Grams are positive by Hilbert-module geometry before arithmetic input, and common generalized range support reduces surviving coefficients to corner-unitary correlations. WP-416 then shows that allowing the canonical source projections to vary while keeping one common generalized range does not help: KMS dynamics locks distinct divisibility supports to distinct time degrees, making them scalar-Gram orthogonal; equal-support blocks again reduce to universal tracial corner geometry.

WP-417 closes the most canonical varying-source **and varying-range** continuation left by WP-416. For the native Bost--Connes partial isometries `v_(m,n)=mu_m mu_n^*`, KMS invariance first blocks the family by the rational ratio `m/n`. On a fixed reduced ray `m/n=a/b`, writing `(m,n)=(ak,bk)`, one has the exact overlap

`v_(ak,bk)^* v_(aell,bell)=E_(b lcm(k,ell))`.

After KMS normalization the ratio labels disappear and every nonzero block is exactly

`(gcd(k,ell)/sqrt(k ell))^beta`.

At the critical source-selected temperature `beta=1`, this is the same critical GCD/Poisson Gram already isolated in WP-216. Thus canonical varying range is genuine before scalarization but collapses after the actual equilibrium readout to a previously audited divisibility kernel. It does not supply a new Weil orientation, Gamma term or polar sector.

The live enlarged-category route is consequently narrower than “retain off-diagonal fibre data,” “use balanced cycles,” “make holonomy noncommutative,” “pass to a non-full correspondence,” “vary canonical source support,” or “vary both source and range along the native semigroup.” A surviving route must provide **source-forced structure not reduced by the KMS ratio/support decomposition to a universal Gram**: for example non-native but source-derived incidence, source-forced mixing of time degrees, twisted/projective multiplication retained by the positive readout, active modular fibre dynamics, a changed scalarization, or an orientation-sensitive non-Gram observable. In every case the extra structure must fail matched nonarithmetic controls and derive the completed finite-plus-archimedean Weil coefficients rather than merely encode programmable phases, supports, frequencies or ratio labels.

## Keep source canonicity, order and destination sign separate

A candidate can be canonical yet order-trivial, one-sided yet nonpositive, positive yet universal, gauge-invariant yet absent from the source, genuinely curved yet invisible to the chosen positive carrier, operator-valued yet scalar again after projection readout, off-diagonal yet annihilated by thermal spectral selection, noncentral/non-full and spectrally variable while its positive sign remains a universal correlation identity, source-varying yet frequency-orthogonalized by equilibrium, or genuinely varying in both source and range while fixed-ratio KMS blocks reproduce the old GCD Gram. Future arguments must identify where the asymmetry lives, whether the source algebra actually carries it, whether the positive observable retains it, and what source relation makes the surviving block selective. Representation novelty is not evidence if the final scalarization returns to a universal positive kernel.
