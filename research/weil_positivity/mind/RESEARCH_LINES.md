## Find a non-sparse cross-gap restriction that one positive Herglotz source cannot graft independently

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation` through `MI-033-sparse-same-source-julia-coherence-is-still-matched-control`.

WP-293--WP-318 close the natural pure-measure scalar Julia orbit. Fixed gauges and Schur reductions either preserve the wrong curvature, contaminate the Gamma scale or universalize under moving normalization. For arbitrary positive measures, derivative normalization at a regular node reduces the scalar Julia response to `tau-K_nu`, whose Pick positivity is universal for every probability law; every gap-supported law has a positive matched-control realization.

WP-319 repairs the obvious local compactness failure by scaling with an actual adjacent prime-power support gap. Along an unavoidable bounded-gap subsequence the derivative-biased Mangoldt laws are tight, with shifted-lattice inverse-square subsequential limits and quantized local prime-power coefficients. This is genuine source structure, but one tangent remains exactly matched-control realizable.

WP-320 now closes the weakest family-level escape. After thinning the fixed-gap family sufficiently sparsely, a **single finite non-atomic positive Herglotz measure** can graft blocks at the same physical nodes and gap scales so that its boundary derivatives match relatively and its derivative-biased laws converge in bounded-Lipschitz distance to the arithmetic laws. The normalized Julia fields therefore converge locally uniformly as well. Merely requiring infinitely many scalar tangents to have one common source is not arithmetic-selective.

The remaining scalar question must prevent this separated grafting. A candidate restriction must couple a non-sparse/all-gap family, overlapping or nearby charts, or an exact cross-gap invariant in a topology strong enough for Julia reduction. Common provenance alone is not enough. Even a successful local-family rigidity theorem would still have to couple the finite arithmetic side to the Gamma/pole/trivial-zero terms of the global Weil form.

## Separate local arithmetic fingerprint from source-selective positivity

Tightness, shifted-lattice support and quantized coefficients are source information. The positivity of `-K_rho` is not. WP-320 strengthens the matched-control audit: even a sparse infinite family of those fingerprints can be reproduced asymptotically by one non-atomic source when the charts are physically separated.

Future scalar arguments must identify a relation among the induced laws that the grafted control cannot realize **without thinning**. A property that survives only at isolated nodes or can be imposed block by block is not yet a source-selective sign mechanism.

## Preserve mass class, curvature, compactness, family coherence and global coupling as distinct gates

WP-314--WP-320 expose successive independent gates: universal moving tangents, noncompact atom-mass scaling, universal normalized positivity after derivative biasing, source-specific compact limits whose individual laws are matched, and now sparse same-source family coherence that is also matched by block grafting. Passing local compactness or common-source provenance does not produce finite--archimedean completion.

A viable route must change category or supply a genuinely nonseparable constraint: positive-density/non-sparse family rigidity, matrix/operator-valued response, a mass-at-infinity channel, generalized resolvents, or direct finite--archimedean coupling. Each candidate still needs its own matched-control and sign audit.
