WP-384 supplies a canonical boundary escape but classicalizes it into the Nyman modulus-square carrier, and WP-385 closes every closed dilation-invariant positive reweighting because the finite Euler factors are boundedly invertible on the real Mellin axis.

WP-386 closes arbitrary nonstationary pointwise-local positive measures: mixed-shell nullity forces support into `(0,1)`, where every profile equals `Lambda(m)/sqrt(m)`. WP-387 closes the canonical positive pure-jump continuation: fresh mixed shells force jump conductance inside individual unit cells, where every arithmetic profile is constant, so the jump sector is invisible.

WP-388 now closes the regular strongly local diffusion sector under the natural domain requirement. A squarefree mixed shell jumps at every integer, while strongly local domain functions are continuous on effective intervals. If an effective interval crosses an integer, the mixed shell is not in the domain; if all mixed shells are admitted, every effective interval must lie inside a unit cell, where all shell profiles are constant and the strongly local energy vanishes. Thus the canonical killing, jump and diffusion sectors of continuum Markov geometry provide no new mixed-shell arithmetic channel.

The surviving boundary route must leave this category: a genuinely non-Markov positive off-diagonal form with an independent source-forced kernel, an interface form carrying extra boundary degrees of freedom beyond regular strongly local diffusion, a finite-`q` prelimit term lost in WP-384, or a finite--archimedean coupling in which mixed-shell selection and final positivity occur only after assembly. Another pointwise weight or continuum Markov completion cannot provide the missing channel.

## Keep selector existence, domain admissibility, operator category and destination sign distinct

WP-381 proves that a bounded exact Mangoldt selector exists in the pointed geometry; WP-382--WP-383 show that exact selection collapses a large interior response space to rank one. WP-384--WP-388 now separate the main continuum positive categories: the raw Nyman carrier remains too broad, stationary positive reweighting cannot kill mixed shells, pointwise-local selection collapses to the Mangoldt ray, jump geometry becomes unit-cell blind, and strongly local diffusion either rejects mixed shells from its domain or is equally invisible when they are admitted.

None of these results classifies non-Markov positive kernels, nonstandard interface forms, finite-prelimit constructions or indefinite finite--archimedean coupling before the final sign theorem. A new mechanism must state its domain and operator category explicitly and explain why its kernel is source-forced rather than chosen to encode the desired Weil conclusion.
