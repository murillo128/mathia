## Cross the boundary beyond continuum Markov sectors and Ramanujan interface energy, or delay mixed-shell selection

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm`, `MI-039-enriched-semigroups-inherit-obstructed-scalar-shadows-or-universal-lifts` through `MI-059-pointwise-local-boundary-nullity-collapses-to-the-mangoldt-ray`, `MI-060-strongly-local-markov-forms-either-reject-mixed-shells-or-become-unit-cell-invisible`, `MI-061-logarithmic-interface-current-classicalizes-to-ramanujan-orthogonality`.

WP-374--WP-383 show that exact mixed-shell nullity collapses every fixed-interior response to one Mangoldt line. WP-384 supplies a canonical boundary escape but classicalizes it into the Nyman modulus-square carrier, and WP-385 closes every closed dilation-invariant positive reweighting because the finite Euler factors are boundedly invertible on the real Mellin axis.

WP-386--WP-388 then exhaust the canonical continuum Markov sectors on the boundary carrier. Pointwise-local positive selection collapses to `(0,1)` and the Mangoldt ray; pure-jump mixed-shell nullity forces conductance into unit cells where every shell profile is constant; regular strongly local diffusion either rejects the discontinuous mixed-shell profiles from its domain or becomes unit-cell invisible when they are admitted.

WP-389 reads the integer interfaces that the strongly local sector cannot see and closes the most immediate positive interface completion. For the WP-384 shell profile `h_m`, the logarithmically normalized jump current is exactly

`j_m(k)=k Delta_k h_m = c_m(k)/sqrt(m)`,

with `c_m` the classical Ramanujan sum. Its mean-square Gram and the scale-covariant nearest-neighbor interface energy both diagonalize as

`<j_m,j_n> = delta_(m,n) phi(m)/m`.

Every mixed-prime shell therefore has positive interface norm, while the diagonal depends only on the radical and forgets prime-power exponent data. The interface degrees of freedom are real, but their canonical first-order positive geometry classicalizes to Ramanujan orthogonality rather than forcing the Weil finite-place selector. A projector that kills mixed shells after this diagonalization would be an additional arithmetic multiplier, not a consequence of the interface geometry.

The surviving boundary route must now add a source-forced structure beyond both the Beurling--Deny sectors and the canonical Ramanujan mean-square interface form: a genuinely non-Markov off-diagonal kernel whose form is fixed before the desired selector is known, a finite-`q` prelimit effect lost in WP-384, or a finite--archimedean coupling in which mixed-shell selection and final positivity occur only after assembly. Another pointwise weight, continuum Markov completion or diagonal shell multiplier chosen in the Ramanujan basis does not provide the missing mechanism.

## Keep selector existence, domain admissibility, operator category and destination sign distinct

WP-381 proves that a bounded exact Mangoldt selector exists in the pointed geometry; WP-382--WP-383 show that exact selection collapses a large interior response space to rank one. WP-384--WP-389 now separate the main canonical positive categories: the raw Nyman carrier is too broad, stationary reweighting cannot kill mixed shells, pointwise-local selection collapses to the Mangoldt ray, jump and strongly local sectors become unit-cell blind, and the first natural interface current instead yields a full positive Ramanujan basis.

None of these results classifies arbitrary source-forced non-Markov kernels, finite-prelimit constructions or indefinite finite--archimedean coupling before the final sign theorem. A new mechanism must state its domain and operator category explicitly and explain why its off-diagonal structure is forced by the source rather than programmed after the Ramanujan shell decomposition is visible.
