## Couple arithmetic boundary data to positive bulk before critical normalization universalizes it

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-mangoldt-anchor-is-unbounded-in-positive-hardy-norm` and `MI-039-enriched-semigroups-inherit-obstructed-scalar-shadows-or-universal-lifts` through `MI-057-exact-mixed-shell-nullity-collapses-every-closable-radial-positive-form`.

WP-356--WP-377 classify a large family of finite, finite--archimedean and radial positive-completion routes. The strongest radial conclusion is representation-independent inside the strongly-continuous unitary category: spectator-prime mixed shells generate every non-invariant component of a prime-power shell, so exact mixed-shell nullity forces any closable positive form through the invariant sector. In scalar spectral realizations that sector is only the zero Mellin atom and yields the wrong `Lambda^2`-type readout.

WP-378 tests a natural nonradial analytic escape using the full cyclotomic logarithmic potentials `F_n=log Phi_n` in Hardy `H^2(D)`. This creates a genuine positive Gram geometry with nonzero cross-prime terms, but the kernel Euler-factorizes primewise. Its diagonal depends only on `rad(n)`, has full composite support, and forgets prime-power exponent. The exact Mangoldt selector remains only as the boundary value `F_n(1)=Lambda(n)`, and evaluation at `1` is unbounded in `H^2`.

WP-379 now tests the smallest pointed escape left by that topology warning. For the odd full-root shell `A_N` and its half-turn `B_N=A_N(-z)`, the critical pointed Dirichlet Gram satisfies

`N^(-1) Gram(A_N,B_N) -> [[C,(C-log 2)/2],[(C-log 2)/2,log 2]]`.

The limit is positive definite, so pointing really does recover nontrivial mixed geometry. But the same matrix occurs for **every odd degree**, prime or composite. Meanwhile the exact boundary contrast is `A_N(1)=log N`, `B_N(1)=0`, and on prime shells the critical half-density normalization sends the Mangoldt component to `log p/sqrt(p) -> 0`.

The obstruction is therefore sharper than unbounded Hardy evaluation. A positive topology may regularize the finite selector and still separate it asymptotically from the nondegenerate positive bulk: the critical geometry can universalize while the arithmetic boundary signal vanishes. A surviving pointed/analytic construction must couple boundary or multi-place arithmetic incidence to the positive form **before** the critical limit erases it, or derive a source-forced singular/archimedean topology in which the selector and bulk remain at compatible scale. It must also supply the Gamma/polar completion and avoid the mixed-shell-nullity collapse.

The other surviving option remains to keep mixed shells active through an indefinite or genuinely global finite--archimedean assembly until the final sign theorem, rather than demand that a positive finite-place Hilbert norm itself perform the prime-power selection.

## Keep positive bulk, matched controls, selector scale and archimedean completion distinct

WP-378 and WP-379 are complementary matched controls. The unpointed Hardy Gram has real cross-prime incidence but places Mangoldt on a singular boundary. Pointing removes that rotational blindness and gives a genuine positive mixed Gram, yet after the source-forced critical normalization the entire two-channel bulk is cloned by odd composite controls while the arithmetic boundary contrast decays to zero.

A new construction should therefore state which mixed-prime coefficients remain active, when positivity is imposed, what topology makes the selector legitimate, **at what normalization scale the selector survives**, and where the Gamma/polar terms enter. Replacing one canonical positive norm by another without proving that the arithmetic functional stays coupled to the positive bulk at the destination is unlikely to create the Weil sign.
