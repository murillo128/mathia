## Find a sign mechanism that survives both scalar gauge and matched arithmetic dressing

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-039-stationary-negative-type-transport-needs-quadratic-drift-for-weil-tail-control` through `MI-049-unitary-moving-compressions-have-no-berry-trace-escape`.

The stationary-geometry results show that the Weil log-square cannot be manufactured for free by oscillatory negative-type structure: a scalar or fixed-positive-functional readout obtains the required quadratic arithmetic direction only when that direction is already present in the quadratic sector. Singular-character tangents merely relocate the same target square to a boundary state.

The Maaß--Selberg/congruence branch gives a complementary matched-control obstruction. Positive truncated norms and boundary squares can be divisor-blind; self-adjoint and even arithmetic congruence scattering can carry independent reciprocal inner factors. In squarefree congruence channels the bad-prime logarithmic-derivative blocks have one strict sign, so positive moving post-differentiation compression cannot remove them.

WP-350 closes unitary pre-differentiation motion: a unitary compression is a reducing restriction, and Berry/connection terms cancel in the phase-generator trace. WP-351 closes the most immediate nonunitary repair. If a proper contraction is obtained by compressing `U=gR` with a common unimodular scalar phase `g`, its canonical defect operators and singular values are exactly independent of `g`. Standard contraction-defect positivity therefore throws away the scalar zeta phase rather than selecting it.

The live question is now narrower than “allow nonunitarity.” A successful route must exhibit a **phase-sensitive nonunitary or noncommutative structure** whose positivity survives ramified matched controls but does not quotient out the common scalar phase under unimodular gauge. An anchored cross-parameter connection, a genuinely global noncommuting construction or another arithmetic sign theorem could qualify, but the anchor/sign mechanism must be proved rather than inserted by normalization.

## Keep source dependence distinct from sign coercivity

Arithmetic labels, self-adjoint realization, unitary scattering, moving channel choice and contraction defects are all source-dependent structures, yet each admits a matched control that preserves the corresponding positivity while changing or erasing the divisor-sensitive scalar information. They are not Weil positivity merely because they are canonical or positive.

The next proposal should state exactly which scalar phase or divisor coordinate survives its quotient, what matched ramified/rephased control is excluded, and which independent theorem forces the required sign. Algebraic division by known local factors, a reducing subspace, or a scalar-gauge-blind defect operator is normalization rather than a generated positivity mechanism.
