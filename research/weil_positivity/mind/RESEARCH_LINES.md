WP-374--WP-390 reduce the canonical continuum/local/interface positive categories. The boundary current becomes the Ramanujan basis `j_m=c_m/sqrt(m)`, and every shift-invariant positive interface form is exactly a nonnegative rational-frequency mask. Exact mixed-prime nullity therefore programs prime-power support explicitly; boundedness or positivity does not make that selector source-native.

WP-216 supplies a different source-forced object in the Bost--Connes branch: the canonical divisibility range projections at critical conditioning produce the Poisson/GCD Gram and the correct half-density/log-prime scale. WP-391 closes the most literal diagonal selector escape: the canonical norm-closed divisibility diagonal contains no nonzero observable whose diagonal is supported only on prime powers.

WP-392 closes the corresponding **full bounded positive** escape. For every bounded Bost--Connes element, exact mixed-prime nullity of its basis diagonal already forces that diagonal to vanish everywhere; if the element is positive, it is therefore zero. Finite crossed-product observables have only finite divisibility/periodic horizons, and multiplication by one fresh mixed composite reproduces their entire diagonal. Off-diagonal crossed-product terms consequently cannot produce a nonzero exact positive prime-power selector inside the bounded source C-star algebra.

Thus neither an arbitrary stationary mask nor any positive bounded element of the standard Bost--Connes source algebra explains the finite Weil selector. The surviving route must genuinely change category or postpone support selection: a source-forced unbounded/closable form, modular or GNS constraint, one-sided correspondence/range defect, different boundary algebra, finite-`q` prelimit mechanism, active finite--archimedean coupling, or a global construction in which prime-power support and the final sign emerge together.

## Keep selector existence, source selection, operator category and destination sign distinct

The recent sequence separates four different facts. Arbitrary Hamiltonian spectral calculus can encode prime powers; stationary positive interface geometry can realize the selector only as an explicitly chosen mask; the canonical divisibility diagonal cannot realize the selector; and even the full bounded Bost--Connes crossed product cannot realize it **positively** with exact mixed-prime nullity. Meanwhile the critical Bost--Connes Gram is genuinely source-forced but its local Weil orientation remains an indefinite defect rather than a positive completion.

Passing to a larger category is therefore not automatically progress. The standard bicommutant is already all of `B(ell^2(N^x))`, where arbitrary diagonal selectors are available, so a von Neumann/modular route earns source-selection credit only if an additional weight, domain, covariance law, expectation or correspondence is fixed canonically before the target support is read.

A new proposal must state exactly which operator category supplies the missing selector/orientation, why that category and its domain are fixed by the source, and how the construction survives the archimedean/polar/global sign theorem. Merely enlarging the bounded algebra, passing to weak closure, choosing a favorable mask, or exhibiting positivity after target-dependent restriction does not answer the source-selection problem.
