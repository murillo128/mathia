# Weil-positivity research lines

This file holds the current mathematical lines of investigation suggested by the durable Weil-positivity intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they separate genuinely different mathematical mechanisms.

## Couple the exact finite birth/selector to the completion before positive reduction

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation` and `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`.

The finite arithmetic side is now less mysterious than the global pairing. Boolean supertraces and Poisson scores recover the exact finite selector (WP-018, WP-022), while WP-030 and WP-043 show that even canonical **positive** finite operators can recover `Lambda(n)` through incidence Gram volume or primitive-shell log-determinants. But those positive readouts are rank/cyclotomic tests, not the completed Weil quadratic form. Direct critical Gram completion is nonclosable (WP-032--WP-033), and the intrinsic Prime-Circle boundary birth operator has the correct interior Weil coefficients only in an unbounded-below or singular first-variation channel (WP-034, WP-037).

The live question is to construct a canonical finite-plus-archimedean/polar object **before** that selector is moved into the wrong pairing or averaged away. The decisive mechanism must generate the gamma/polar sector and a sign theorem together. Appending those terms to a finite selector, or using a positive functional whose global completion forgets the birth matrix, does not qualify.

## Derive a category-changing boundary or relative operation with its own sign theorem

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-operator-category-is-a-hard-compatibility-gate` and `MI-003-arithmetic-selector-and-positive-bulk-are-separated-by-symmetry-breaking`.

Several natural sign-preserving escapes are now closed. The exact Prime-Flute Schiffer coupling is too soft in operator-ideal terms, ordinary DtN/Feshbach positivity does not survive the critical scattering continuation, and WP-044--WP-045 show that even positive Prime-Circle radial contrasts and ordinary Schur elimination remove the boundary Weil birth operator together with the universal collision mode. The resolved Schwarzian clue (WP-042) also supplies an early local test: a projectively natural Schiffer kernel can fail positive definiteness already at the infinitesimal two-point level.

The remaining boundary route must therefore be genuinely shell-dependent, singular, noncommuting, distributional, cohomological, asymmetry-sensitive, or nonseparable across finite and archimedean sectors **and** come with a new ordering/intersection theorem at the destination. A decisive operation must cross the category/pairing mismatch while retaining the exact finite selector; a boundary invariant that merely carries information with uncontrolled sign, or a positive elimination that sends the selector to a vanishing correction, is not enough.
