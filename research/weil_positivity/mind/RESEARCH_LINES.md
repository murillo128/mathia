## Force source-specific finite--archimedean structure beyond fixed scalar reductions and the universal moving Julia layer

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation` through `MI-028-empty-side-moving-julia-reduction-has-a-universal-logarithmic-boundary-layer`.

WP-293--WP-313 close the natural fixed scalar gauge/Schur/Julia orbit. The critical Mangoldt source has the correct logarithmic mass class but the wrong-sign source-specific curvature. Fixed-interior Schur stripping inserts a larger positive `log(y)/y` term; finite fixed-boundary Julia reduction either does the same or worsens the positive curvature; an infinite fixed-node chain cannot repair this whenever the relevant high-energy invariant is stable under the chain limit.

WP-314 tests the canonical nonuniform escape rather than merely demanding it. Let a regular Julia node move to `X->infinity` on the empty positive side and observe on its natural scale `z=X zeta`. The unit-log-normalized reduction converges to

`J_*(zeta)=((zeta-1)Log zeta)/((zeta-1)-Log zeta)`,

exactly the same boundary layer obtained from the unit-density Lebesgue/logarithmic control. The arithmetic remainder disappears under the scaling, while on fixed spectral compacta the family degenerates to a constant.

So “make the limits fail to commute” is no longer sufficient. A surviving scalar-moving construction must choose a **source-specific boundary scale** on which the nonuniversal Mangoldt fluctuation survives the density-one tangent, while independently preserving positivity and the Gamma discriminator. Otherwise the route must leave the audited scalar class through operator/matrix-valued boundary structure, generalized resolvents, noninvertible maps, an added archimedean channel or nonseparable finite--archimedean coupling.

## Treat mass class, curvature orientation, transform admissibility, limit stability and tangent universality as separate gates

Matching `log y` establishes only the infinite-mass class. Producing a nonuniversal `y^-2` coefficient establishes arithmetic sensitivity but not the correct orientation. WP-307--WP-312 show that admissible positive scalar changes can preserve the wrong invariant or insert a larger contaminating term. WP-313 shows that compact convergence of an infinite chain is insufficient unless the endpoint discriminator also passes to the limit.

WP-314 adds another gate: even when the high-energy and transform limits deliberately fail to commute, the rescaled tangent may be **universal**. Density-one source scaling can erase the arithmetic remainder before the transform sees it. A proposed moving operation must therefore identify the exact fluctuation scale being magnified and show that the limiting object differs from the matched unit-density control for a source-specific reason.

## Use matched controls against universal leading scale, post-hoc recoding and moving-limit classicalization

Unit-density positive measures control the leading logarithm. WP-314 shows that they also control the canonical empty-side moving Julia boundary layer, not just the first fixed high-energy term. This strengthens the matched-control requirement: compare a proposed moving reduction with the same operation on the Lebesgue/logarithmic control before interpreting any limiting geometry as arithmetic.

Future constructions must alter the geometry or realization before final scalar comparison and prove both **positivity/admissibility** and **source-specific survival at the moving scale**. A nonuniform limit that merely exposes the universal tangent is another classicalization, not a finite--archimedean completion.
