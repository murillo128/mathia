# Weil-positivity research lines

This file records the current mathematical questions that survive the Weil-Positivity evidence. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Force a genuinely one-sided source relation, then prove its arithmetic provenance

WP-391--WP-404 show that selector enlargement, bounded Bost--Connes positivity, affiliation, modular-frequency decomposition, quotient order, cutoff breaking and broad linear readouts do not by themselves produce a Weil-oriented functional. Dense two-sided tangent geometry survives these changes. A sufficiently thin same-shadow slice can be one-sided, but matched nonarithmetic constructions can manufacture the same orientation. Any linear route therefore has two separate gates: the source relation must genuinely change the reachable tangent closure, and that change must be forced by arithmetic structure rather than generic thinness.

## Make any enlarged positive block route survive genuine KMS joining, not programmable fibre data

WP-405--WP-418 progressively close native analytic half-spectrum positivity, central and noncentral cycle holonomy, projective repairs, passive full-fibre decorations, balanced KMS blocks, Hilbert-module path Grams, varying canonical supports/ranges and phase-character refinements as automatic sources of Weil orientation. After the actual equilibrium readout these constructions either gauge away, become spectrally orthogonal, reduce to universal positive Grams, or leave a compatibility mask whose labels are not themselves forced by the source.

WP-421 isolates the first apparent escape from separate invariance: a merely jointly invariant state can pair a source Bohr mode with an opposite active-fibre mode. WP-422 then shows that a countable atomic Gibbs fibre can be programmed with all prime-logarithmic gaps, so finite spectral capacity is not the decisive obstruction.

WP-423 closes that mechanism at the genuine KMS boundary for decoupled product dynamics in finite-matrix and countable atomic type-I fibres, with stronger finite-corner factorization. WP-424 removes commutative non-atomic active fibres even more strongly: in the KMS GNS quotient every invariant central commutative fibre is pointwise time-trivial.

WP-425 closes the remaining decoupled product-dynamics escape at state level without any fibre-category hypothesis. For arbitrary C-star dynamical systems and every positive inverse temperature, **every** KMS state of `alpha tensor tau` is separately invariant under the two factor flows. Commutation of the factor copies plus the KMS boundary identity makes each cross-factor analytic matrix coefficient `i beta`-periodic; bounded-strip control and Liouville force it to be constant. Hence every nonzero source Bohr mode has zero expectation against every auxiliary observable, including an opposite-frequency mode, even for correlated KMS states and genuinely noncentral noncommutative fibres. WP-423 remains stronger through its atomic factorization, and WP-424 remains stronger through commutative GNS freezing.

WP-426 adds the converse canonicity warning for interacting or modular dynamics. In the Tomita--Takesaki setting every faithful normal state is KMS for its own modular automorphism group, and in finite dimensions every faithful density matrix can be written as a Gibbs state for the state-selected Hamiltonian `H_rho=-beta^(-1) log rho`, up to an additive scalar. Thus if the state is chosen first and the dynamics or interaction is then defined from it, the KMS condition is programmable and supplies no independent source selection. Even a bounded interaction relative to a reference Hamiltonian does not cure that circularity when the interaction is itself state-selected.

The live equilibrium question has therefore moved to **independently source-fixed interacting dynamics**. A surviving KMS route must derive the dynamics, interaction, correspondence or modular weight from the arithmetic source before the candidate state or target sign is inspected, and it must not reduce to the decoupled product setting already closed by WP-425. Alternatively it must justify why a merely invariant non-KMS state has an independently source-forced positive Weil interpretation. Any such route must still derive, rather than program, the prime-power/Mangoldt coefficients, archimedean Gamma term, polar normalization, admissible domain and global sign. Enlarging the auxiliary spectrum, changing the fibre category, entangling the KMS state under a product flow, or declaring the modular flow of a chosen state no longer supplies the missing arithmetic selection.

## Keep source canonicity, order and destination sign separate

A candidate can be canonical yet order-trivial, positive yet universal, gauge-invariant yet absent from the source, or infinite-dimensional yet fully programmable. Future arguments must identify where the asymmetry lives, prove that the source algebra actually carries it, and show that the positive readout retains it after equilibrium normalization. Representation novelty, larger active support or a richer Gram matrix is not evidence if the final scalarization returns to a universal or freely programmable kernel. WP-425 sharpens the category warning: under finite-temperature decoupled product KMS equilibrium, auxiliary-fibre cardinality, topology and noncommutativity cannot restore a nonzero source frequency because separate invariance is automatic. WP-426 sharpens the canonicity warning in the opposite direction: leaving product dynamics is not enough if the replacement flow is reconstructed from the state one wants to certify. A genuine escape must change the source relation or dynamics in a way fixed independently by the arithmetic source.
