## Build a native critical-sector global coupling rather than transporting spherical positivity

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-positivity-needs-a-sign-producing-global-operation`, `MI-010-source-forced-critical-gram-can-have-the-wrong-weil-orientation`, `MI-011-prime-ray-marginals-do-not-determine-mixed-prime-coupling`, `MI-012-automorphic-multiplicity-does-not-prevent-global-scalarization`, `MI-013-critical-sector-transport-needs-new-global-arithmetic-structure`.

WP-216--WP-247 show that correct critical prime amplitudes, positivity, cusp multiplicity, congruence sectors, spherical higher rank, and fixed-level nonspherical data repeatedly leave Riemann/Dirichlet arithmetic inside scalar normalizers or orthogonal channels. The direct all-prime nonspherical deformation crosses the square-summability boundary exactly at the critical half-density, so it leaves the canonical spherical-reference restricted tensor sector.

WP-248 makes that category change precise. At the critical scale the deformed product state is disjoint from the spherical product-state representation of the quasi-local finite-prime algebra; there is no nonzero bounded intertwiner implementing the identity observable algebra between the sectors. An abstract product automorphism still exists, but it is nonspatial in the spherical representation. Canonical spherical Maaß--Selberg/scattering positivity therefore cannot simply be inherited through a bounded sector comparison.

WP-249 tests the most immediate repair. Crossing the quasi-local algebra by the critical product automorphism makes the transport generator algebraically inner, but the bare crossed-product positive core is arithmetic-blind: the shift Laplacian is universal, commutator squares preserve finite prime support, and the canonical conditional expectation sends every finite Fourier positive square back to the same finite subsystem. It manufactures neither the all-prime von Mangoldt distribution nor the archimedean/polar completion.

The live escape must therefore build the sign **natively in the critical/global category**. A source-forced KMS or modular weight, non-quasi-local boundary state, Hilbert-module correspondence, unbounded cycle, or infinite-rank finite--archimedean coupling is relevant only if it intrinsically generates both the finite-prime coefficients and the Gamma/polar terms and comes with an independent domain/sign theorem. Changing sector or adjoining an implementer is transport, not yet arithmetic positivity.

## Treat matrix size, sector change, and algebraic implementation as separate controls

A nontrivial Hessian need not be positive, operator-valued local intertwiners can leave the cofinite Euler tail scalar, critical all-prime vectors can live in a disjoint representation, and a crossed product can make the corresponding automorphism inner without adding any global arithmetic content. Future proposals should identify the exact additional state, weight, correspondence, or finite--archimedean coupling that creates the Weil orientation rather than infer it from the existence of a larger algebraic category.
