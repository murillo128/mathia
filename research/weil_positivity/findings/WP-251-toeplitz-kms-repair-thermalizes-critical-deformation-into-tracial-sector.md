# WP-251 — Toeplitz KMS repair restores equilibrium but thermalizes the critical deformation into the tracial sector

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + TOEPLITZ-PIMSNER + KMS-CLASSIFICATION + UNIQUE-TRACE + CRITICALITY-ERASURE + QUASI-LOCAL-CONTROL + MATCHED-CONTROLS + DECISIVE-NARROWING + PRIOR-ART-CLASSICALIZATION + NOT-A-WEIL-BRIDGE`.

`WP-250` proves that the canonical dual gauge flow on the bilateral crossed product `A ⋊_α Z` has no nonzero-temperature KMS state or proper C*-KMS weight. Its most natural escape is to stop before group completion: replace the implementing unitary by a one-sided isometry, so the range defect can absorb the KMS factor exactly as it does in Bost--Connes-type systems.

That repair does restore nonzero-temperature equilibrium, but for the specific quasi-local algebra of `WP-248` it also changes the representation-theoretic content in a decisive way. The coefficient algebra

\[
\mathcal A=\bigotimes_{p\in\mathbb P}M_2
\tag{1}
\]

has a unique normalized trace `τ`. For the Toeplitz-Pimsner algebra generated by `A` and an isometry `v` satisfying

\[
v^*v=1,
\qquad
va=\alpha(a)v,
\tag{2}
\]

with gauge dynamics

\[
\sigma_t(a)=a,
\qquad
\sigma_t(v)=e^{it}v,
\tag{3}
\]

there is, for every `β>0`, a unique `β`-KMS state `φ_β`. Its normal-form moments are

\[
\boxed{
\phi_\beta(v^n a v^{*m})
=\delta_{n,m}e^{-n\beta}\tau(a).
}
\tag{4}
\]

Thus the one-sided range defect fixes the bilateral obstruction, but the thermal mass is the universal geometric distribution in **Toeplitz/Fock degree**, not a prime-dependent arithmetic distribution. More importantly, every such equilibrium restricts to the tracial state on `A`, and in the tracial GNS representation **every automorphism `α` is spatial**, including the critical non-square-summable product rotation of `WP-248`. The critical product-state disjointness threshold therefore disappears entirely in this equilibrium sector.

This is not the false statement that `α` becomes invisible. Mixed coefficient correlations such as `τ(a α^k(b))` still detect the local rotations. The exact narrowing is subtler: canonical Toeplitz KMS positivity carries no critical all-prime singularity of its own. Its thermal degree law, coefficient representation class, and range-projection masses are unchanged if the critical prime amplitudes are replaced by subcritical, random, or nonarithmetic local rotations. Any surviving arithmetic information remains in ordinary quasi-local trace correlations and must be separately converted into the finite Weil distribution and the archimedean/polar completion.

## 1. KMS equilibrium forces the unique trace on the coefficient algebra

Let `φ` be a `β`-KMS state for (3), with `β>0`. Since the dynamics fixes `A` pointwise, the KMS identity gives for `a,b∈A`

\[
\phi(ab)=\phi(ba).
\tag{5}
\]

Hence `φ|_A` is a tracial state. The UHF algebra (1) has a unique normalized trace, so

\[
\boxed{\phi|_\mathcal A=\tau.}
\tag{6}
\]

KMS states are invariant under their dynamics. Therefore every gauge-homogeneous element of nonzero degree has zero expectation. In particular,

\[
\phi(v^n a v^{*m})=0,
\qquad n\ne m.
\tag{7}
\]

For `n=m`, apply KMS with `x=v^n` and `y=a v^{*n}`. Since

\[
\sigma_{i\beta}(v^n)=e^{-n\beta}v^n
\]

and `v^{*n}v^n=1`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
\phi(v^n a v^{*n})
&=\phi\!\left(a v^{*n}\sigma_{i\beta}(v^n)\right)\\
&=e^{-n\beta}\phi(a)\\
&=e^{-n\beta}\tau(a).
\end{aligned}
\tag{8}
\]

Equations (7)--(8) prove the forced moment formula (4). The linear span of these Toeplitz monomials is dense, so at most one `β`-KMS state can exist.

Existence is the standard finite-type Toeplitz-Pimsner equilibrium associated to `τ`. Writing `q=e^{-β}`, its Fock realization places mass

\[
(1-q)q^k
\tag{9}
\]

on the `k`th Fock level. Equivalently, if

\[
P_k=v^k(1-vv^*)v^{*k},
\tag{10}
\]

then

\[
\boxed{
\phi_\beta(P_k)=(1-e^{-\beta})e^{-k\beta}.
}
\tag{11}
\]

The general Toeplitz-Pimsner KMS classification is classical; the point here is its consequence for the exact Mathia critical product automorphism.

## 2. The critical product rotation becomes spatial in the KMS coefficient sector

Let `(π_τ,L^2(A,τ),Λ_τ(1))` be the tracial GNS representation. Since `τ` is the unique trace, every automorphism `α` preserves it. Therefore

\[
W_\alpha\Lambda_\tau(a)
:=\Lambda_\tau(\alpha(a))
\tag{12}
\]

defines a unitary on `L^2(A,τ)` and

\[
W_\alpha\pi_\tau(a)W_\alpha^*
=\pi_\tau(\alpha(a)).
\tag{13}
\]

So `α` is spatial in the tracial representation for **every** sequence of local rotations.

This sharply contrasts with `WP-248`. There the spherical product state `ω_Ω` and the critical deformed product state are disjoint exactly when

\[
\sum_p |a_p|^2=\infty,
\tag{14}
\]

which occurs at the Weil half-density `a_p=p^{-1/2}` and for the Mangoldt amplitude `(\log p)/\sqrt p`. In the tracial GNS sector, no analogue of (14) appears: the cyclic trace vector is fixed by the induced action on `L^2(A,τ)`, and (12) exists whether the local rotation angles are square-summable or not.

Hence the one-sided KMS repair does not transport the critical-sector obstruction into a better positive category. It **forgets that criticality as a representation boundary**.

## 3. What arithmetic information survives

The previous conclusion must not be overstated. The KMS state is not completely independent of `α` on arbitrary words, because multiplication uses the covariance relation (2). For example,

\[
\phi_\beta(a v b v^*)
=e^{-\beta}\tau(a\alpha(b)).
\tag{15}
\]

Thus finite-prime trace correlations can detect the local rotations.

But this surviving dependence is ordinary quasi-local data. If `a_F→a` and `b_F→b` in norm with finite-prime-supported approximants, then for every fixed integer `k`,

\[
\begin{aligned}
|\tau(a\alpha^k(b))-\tau(a_F\alpha^k(b_F))|
&\le \|a-a_F\|\,\|b\|\\
&\quad+\|a_F\|\,\|b-b_F\|,
\end{aligned}
\tag{16}
\]

because `τ` is contractive and `α` is isometric. Every such coefficient correlation is therefore a norm-continuous limit of finite-prime correlations. There is no singular all-prime KMS term created at `Σ_p|a_p|^2=∞`.

This is exactly the local/global boundary relevant to the mandate. The one-isometry thermal sector provides a positive state and a spatial implementation, while the arithmetic dependence remains inside the coefficient algebra and must still be assembled into the sparse Mangoldt support, the Weil orientation, and the archimedean/polar counterterms by some additional source-defined operation.

## 4. Matched controls

**Subcritical control.** Replace `a_p=p^{-1/2}` by `a_p=p^{-σ}` with `σ>1/2`. Equations (4), (11), and the tracial spatial implementation (12) are unchanged. The KMS construction therefore does not distinguish the exact critical boundary that separated the spherical product representations in `WP-248`.

**Random/nonarithmetic control.** Replace the prime rotations by arbitrary local unitaries or by a product automorphism with no arithmetic meaning. The same unique trace, the same geometric Fock law (11), and the same spatiality theorem hold. Only coefficient correlations such as (15) change.

**Finite-support control.** If `α` acts nontrivially at finitely many primes, the same KMS formulas hold. No new thermodynamic signature appears when infinite critical support is switched on.

**Automorphism control.** More generally, for any unital coefficient algebra with a unique trace preserved by `α`, the argument through (8) and (12) is unchanged. This is a category-level Toeplitz/KMS mechanism rather than a Riemann-specific sign theorem.

These controls are appropriate for a negative classification result: the canonical equilibrium repaired by the isometry is too universal to be the missing source of the Weil half-density or global sign.

## 5. Why Bost--Connes escapes this exact no-go

The rational Bost--Connes system does not use one isometry implementing one global automorphism with a constant gauge energy. It has multiplicative isometries `μ_n` with source dynamics

\[
\sigma_t(\mu_n)=n^{it}\mu_n,
\tag{17}
\]

so KMS directly forces

\[
\phi_\beta(\mu_n\mu_n^*)=n^{-\beta}.
\tag{18}
\]

As `WP-216` proves, at `β=1` this source-defined range-projection geometry yields the exact critical GCD/Poisson Gram and simultaneously carries the logarithmic scale `\log p`. That is precisely what the single-isometry gauge law (3) lacks: its KMS factor is `e^{-nβ}` in iteration degree, not `p^{-β}` in arithmetic energy.

But `WP-216` also supplies the decisive control: the finite Weil ray is the indefinite defect `\log p(I-G_{1,p})`, not the positive Gram itself. Therefore replacing the bilateral crossed product by the elementary Toeplitz extension does not discover a new route around the Bost--Connes sign obstruction. To obtain prime-dependent KMS masses one must introduce prime/multiplicative generators or an arithmetic Hamiltonian, returning to the already-audited semigroup/endomotive class rather than extracting the coefficients from the single critical product automorphism.

## 6. Prior-art and novelty audit

The general KMS mechanism is classical. Marcelo Laca and Sergey Neshveyev, *KMS states of quasi-free dynamics on Pimsner algebras*, Journal of Functional Analysis **211** (2004), 457--482; arXiv:`math/0304435`, characterize KMS states on Toeplitz--Cuntz--Pimsner algebras in terms of traces on the coefficient algebra. Their theorem contains the abstract existence/classification used here; no novelty is claimed for Toeplitz-Pimsner equilibrium itself.

The broader one-sided-versus-bilateral contrast is likewise standard in crossed products by endomorphisms and in Bost--Connes-type systems: proper isometries retain range defects on which the KMS factor can live, whereas a gauge eigenunitary produces the contradiction isolated in `WP-250`.

The Mathia-specific derived content is the conjunction of three exact facts for the `WP-248` critical product automorphism: (i) unique-trace forcing makes the Toeplitz KMS coefficient state `τ`; (ii) every product automorphism, critical or not, is spatial in that tracial GNS representation; and (iii) the KMS range masses are universal Fock-degree weights while the remaining `α` dependence stays in norm-continuous quasi-local trace correlations. A bounded prior-art search found the general Pimsner/KMS classification but no source presenting this exact critical-sector erasure as a proposed Riemann/Weil-positivity mechanism. The novelty claim is only this application and narrowing, not the operator-algebra theorem.
