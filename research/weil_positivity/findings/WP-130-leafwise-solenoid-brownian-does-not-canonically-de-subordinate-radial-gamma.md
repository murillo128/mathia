# WP-130 — Leafwise solenoid Brownian does not canonically de-subordinate the radial Gamma form

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + CROSS-LINE-BRIDGE + MATCHED-CONTROL + SPECTRAL-TYPE-OBSTRUCTION + DECISIVE-CANONICALITY-NEGATIVE + PRIOR-ART-AUDITED`.

`WP-129` leaves one precise escape from its scalar-warp no-go: the inverse Gamma subordination would acquire genuine geometric content if Mathia independently supplied the underlying Brownian/Laplacian generator before the desired Weil symbol or cutoff was consulted. Prime Circle does contain such a generator. `PC-064` identifies the compatible circle inverse limit with the adelic solenoid, and `PC-065` gives its canonical leafwise Laplacian

\[
\Delta_{\rm leaf}\chi_q=(2\pi q)^2\chi_q,
\qquad q\in\mathbb Q.
\tag{1}
\]

Let `F` be the complete Bernstein function derived in `WP-129`, normalized by

\[
H_\infty(t)
=\operatorname{Re}\psi\!\left(\frac14+\frac{it}{2}\right)-\psi\!\left(\frac14\right)
=F(t^2).
\tag{2}
\]

Then the canonical Bochner subordination of the Prime-Circle leaf process is

\[
\boxed{G_\Sigma:=F(\Delta_{\rm leaf})},
\qquad
G_\Sigma\chi_q=H_\infty(2\pi q)\chi_q.
\tag{3}
\]

So the escape in `WP-129` is **not vacuous**: Mathia independently supplies a positive Brownian generator, and applying the already-derived Gamma Bernstein function to it gives an exact positive subordinate process with the Riemann digamma symbol sampled on rational leaf momenta.

Aggressive falsification nevertheless kills this as a direct route to Weil positivity. The Brownian generator supplied by Prime Circle lives in the **wrong intrinsic direction and the wrong spectral representation**. `WP-036` obtains the Riemann digamma variable from radial Mellin/log-radius geometry, whereas `PC-065` differentiates along the solenoid leaf translation coordinate. Identifying the radial Mellin frequency `t` with the leaf momentum `2\pi q` is not forced by any current Mathia intertwiner. More decisively, the canonical radial Gamma operator has continuous spectral type while (3) has a complete pure-point character basis. They therefore cannot be unitarily identified even though they use the same scalar dispersion `H_\infty`.

Subordination also leaves the solenoid's low-denominator noncoercivity intact. Along `q=1/n`, the nonzero eigenvalues of (3) converge quadratically to zero, so the subordinate heat operator remains non-trace-class and the resolvent remains noncompact. Finally, scalar functional calculus changes eigenvalues but not the character decomposition; it does not create the transverse valuation coupling, Mangoldt prime-power support, or global counterterms missing in `WP-038`.

The surviving burden is therefore narrower than after `WP-129`: a useful de-subordination must arise from a Brownian/stable generator intrinsic to the **radial Mellin variable itself**, or from an independently forced radial--leaf/finite--archimedean coupling or measure-changing transform that changes this spectral representation and simultaneously supplies the finite arithmetic structure. The present finding does **not** rule out such new nonseparable structure.

## 1. Prime Circle really does supply an independent Brownian generator

The compatible inverse-limit geometry in `PC-064` has Haar character group `\mathbb Q`. In the canonical Haar representation, `PC-065` derives the leaf generator and positive leafwise Laplacian

\[
A\chi_q=2\pi i q\chi_q,
\qquad
\Delta_{\rm leaf}=-A^2,
\qquad
\Delta_{\rm leaf}\chi_q=(2\pi q)^2\chi_q.
\tag{4}
\]

This construction predates the present Gamma target and does not insert zeros or explicit-formula coefficients. It therefore satisfies the strongest version of the supplier test suggested by `WP-129`: the underlying Brownian generator is genuinely Mathia-native rather than reconstructed from `H_\infty` by applying `F^{-1}`.

Because `F` in `WP-129` is complete Bernstein, standard spectral/Bochner subordination applies to every nonnegative Markov generator. Hence

\[
e^{-sG_\Sigma}=e^{-sF(\Delta_{\rm leaf})}
\tag{5}
\]

is a positive contraction Markov semigroup, and (3) follows exactly by the character diagonalization. No RH input, zero data, sign insertion, or hand-picked positive kernel is needed for this local sign theorem.

This is a useful matched control on `WP-129`: the statement “Mathia might independently produce the Brownian generator” is actually realized by an existing Prime-Circle construction.

## 2. The direct bridge identifies two different intrinsic directions by hand

The exact Riemann digamma channel in `WP-036` is generated by a radial Prime-Circle construction. Its geometric variable is the disk radius `x\in(0,1)`, and Mellin transformation in that radial variable produces the complex parameter `s`; the spectral variable entering `H_\infty(t)` is the imaginary Mellin/log-radius frequency.

By contrast, `PC-065` obtains (4) by differentiating along the one-parameter **leaf translation** on the adelic solenoid. Its label `q\in\mathbb Q` is a Pontryagin character frequency of that leaf action. Nothing in `PC-064`, `PC-065`, `WP-036`, or the current `weil_positivity` contract identifies these two tangent directions.

Equation (3) is therefore a legitimate functional-calculus construction but not yet a geometric identification of the Gamma place. It simply evaluates the same scalar function at

\[
t=2\pi q.
\tag{6}
\]

The weakness survives a matched non-arithmetic control: if any independent nonnegative Markov generator `L` is supplied, the same complete Bernstein function gives `F(L)`. Thus “the spectrum is obtained by feeding an existing Laplacian through the Gamma dispersion” is not selective for the arithmetic solenoid. A canonical bridge needs additional geometry that forces why the Mellin radial frequency and the leaf momentum should be the same observable, or otherwise derives an intertwiner between them before the target Gamma formula is used.

## 3. Spectral type gives an exact obstruction to canonical unitary identification

There is a stronger obstruction than the coordinate mismatch. Let

\[
G_{\mathbb R}:=F(-\partial_x^2)
\tag{7}
\]

on the canonical radial/log-scale model `L^2(\mathbb R,dx)`. Fourier transform diagonalizes it as multiplication by

\[
H_\infty(\xi)=F(\xi^2).
\tag{8}
\]

`WP-129` proves that `F` is continuous and strictly increasing on `[0,\infty)`. Therefore every level set of `H_\infty(\xi)` is finite (at most the pair `\{\pm\xi\}` away from zero), hence has Lebesgue measure zero. It follows that `G_{\mathbb R}` has **no `L^2` eigenfunctions**. In its canonical Fourier representation its spectral measures are absolutely continuous with respect to the pushforward of Lebesgue measure.

For the solenoid operator, however,

\[
\boxed{G_\Sigma\chi_q=H_\infty(2\pi q)\chi_q,
\qquad q\in\mathbb Q,}
\tag{9}
\]

and the characters `\{\chi_q\}_{q\in\mathbb Q}` form a complete orthonormal basis of `L^2(\Sigma_\mathbb Q)`. Thus `G_\Sigma` is **pure point**, with a dense set of eigenvalue labels inherited from `\mathbb Q`.

Point spectrum is preserved by unitary equivalence. Consequently

\[
\boxed{G_{\mathbb R}\not\simeq_{\rm unitary}G_\Sigma}
\tag{10}
\]

under their canonical Lebesgue/Haar representations. The closure of the numerical spectra can agree because `\mathbb Q` is dense and `H_\infty` is continuous, but equality of spectral sets is far weaker than equality of spectral representations or quadratic forms.

This rules out a tempting shortcut: dense rational sampling of the exact Gamma dispersion does not turn the solenoid leaf energy into the archimedean Weil Gamma energy.

## 4. Gamma subordination preserves the low-denominator soft modes

`PC-065` and `WP-038` already show that the bare leaf Laplacian is noncoercive because

\[
\Delta_{\rm leaf}\chi_{1/n}=\left(\frac{2\pi}{n}\right)^2\chi_{1/n}
\longrightarrow0.
\tag{11}
\]

The exact small-frequency expansion from `WP-129` is

\[
H_\infty(t)=\frac{\zeta(3,1/4)}4t^2+O(t^4).
\tag{12}
\]

Substitution into (9) gives

\[
\boxed{
H_\infty\!\left(\frac{2\pi}{n}\right)
=\frac{\pi^2\zeta(3,1/4)}{n^2}+O(n^{-4})
\longrightarrow0.
}
\tag{13}
\]

Hence an infinite orthonormal family of nonconstant characters still has energy tending to zero. The subordinate form has no positive spectral gap on the orthogonal complement of constants and its resolvent is noncompact for the same structural reason as the bare leaf form.

The heat trace obstruction is immediate. For every fixed `s>0`, the terms from the characters `q=1/n` satisfy

\[
\exp\!\left[-sH_\infty(2\pi/n)\right]\longrightarrow1,
\tag{14}
\]

so

\[
\operatorname{Tr}e^{-sG_\Sigma}=\infty.
\tag{15}
\]

Thus exact Gamma subordination changes the dispersion but does not cure the solenoid's accumulation of arbitrarily soft rational modes. Applying `F^{-1}` through spectral calculus simply returns

\[
F^{-1}(G_\Sigma)=\Delta_{\rm leaf},
\tag{16}
\]

so de-subordination does not remove the obstruction either.

## 5. Scalar functional calculus still cannot manufacture the finite Weil sector

The character basis in (9) is unchanged by replacing `\Delta_{\rm leaf}` with `F(\Delta_{\rm leaf})`, `F^{-1}(F(\Delta_{\rm leaf}))`, or any other scalar spectral function. Therefore every such construction remains diagonal in the same rational leaf characters.

`WP-038` already falsifies the idea that the bare canonical solenoid Dirichlet form contains enough transverse arithmetic. The leaf derivative sees the rational frequency but not, by itself, the finite-adic valuation decomposition needed for Mangoldt prime-power support. The product formula can recover a total `\log n` only after a separate valuation readout; that is not the sparse `\Lambda(n)` structure of the finite explicit-formula term.

Gamma subordination does not alter this verdict. It supplies no mechanism for

\[
\frac{\Lambda(p^k)}{p^{k/2}},
\tag{17}
\]

no mixed-prime selector/quotient, and no polar/global counterterm. In particular, the exact local positivity of (5) is still only positivity of an archimedean-looking scalar dispersion on an arithmetically insufficient carrier. No global Weil quadratic form follows.

A new transverse valuation coupling, non-Haar weighting, quotient, boundary response, or nonseparable radial--leaf construction could change the eigenvectors or the underlying Hilbert space and is therefore outside this no-go. Such a mechanism would be genuine additional structure and must be tested separately rather than silently folded into (3).

## 6. What is actually closed, and what remains open

This finding closes the most direct realization of the escape explicitly left by `WP-129`:

\[
\text{existing Prime-Circle Brownian generator}
\;\xrightarrow{\;F\;}\;
\text{Gamma subordinate generator}
\tag{18}
\]

is mathematically valid and independently positive, but it is **not** a canonical realization of the radial Weil Gamma form. The failure is stronger than a mismatch of eigenvalue scale: the intrinsic directions differ, the canonical spectral types differ, the noncoercive rational soft modes survive, and the finite arithmetic sector is unchanged.

The viable burden is now one of the following genuinely stronger constructions:

1. a Brownian/stable generator derived intrinsically in the Prime-Circle **radial log-scale/Mellin direction**, before using the desired Gamma symbol;
2. an independently forced transform that changes measure or spectral type and rigorously intertwines radial Mellin and solenoid leaf geometry;
3. a nonseparable finite--archimedean or radial--leaf coupling whose positivity theorem simultaneously produces the missing finite-prime selector and global counterterms.

These possibilities are not ruled out here. In particular, (10) is only a no-go for unitary identification under the current canonical Haar/Lebesgue representations; a new quotient, compression, nonunitary boundary map, or altered Hilbert structure requires its own analysis.

## 7. Novelty and prior-art audit

No theorem-level historical novelty is claimed for the ingredients. Bernstein functional calculus/subordinate Brownian motion and spectral diagonalization of compact abelian solenoidal groups are classical. `WP-129` records the relevant complete-Bernstein/subordination references, while `PC-064`--`PC-065` preserve the Prime-Circle solenoid provenance.

A bounded search was made for combinations of universal/adelic solenoids, leafwise Laplacians, subordinate Brownian motion, Bernstein functions, and digamma/Gamma generators. No source located in that search established the specific Mathia cross-line identification or the present spectral-type comparison. That absence is **not** evidence of historical novelty; the contribution here is the branch-local falsification that tests an explicit escape left by `WP-129` against an independently existing Mathia supplier.

The decisive distinction from prior branch findings is also narrow. `WP-038` rules out the **bare** solenoid leaf Dirichlet energy as a global Weil form. `WP-129` later proves that the Gamma symbol itself is subordinate Brownian and explicitly leaves an independently supplied Brownian generator as a possible way to make inverse subordination non-tautological. The present calculation joins those two facts and shows that the actual Prime-Circle Brownian supplier does not provide the required canonical bridge.
