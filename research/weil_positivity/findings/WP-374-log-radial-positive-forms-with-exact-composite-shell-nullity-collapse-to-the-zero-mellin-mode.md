# WP-374 — Log-radial positive forms with exact composite-shell nullity collapse to the zero Mellin mode

## Claim

`WP-162` leaves one especially natural escape open. Its canonical cyclotomic inward flux

\[
\rho_n(s)=-\frac{d}{ds}\log\Phi_n(e^{-s}),
\qquad s>0,
\tag{1}
\]

has the exact net mass

\[
\int_0^\infty \rho_n(s)\,ds=\Lambda(n),
\tag{2}
\]

but every non-prime-power shell achieves the zero in (2) by signed cancellation. One might therefore keep the full signed radial profile, couple different shells through a common positive nonlocal radial form, and impose positivity only after that coupling.

For the canonical scale-stationary class, that route collapses exactly.

Pass to logarithmic radius

\[
u=\log s,
\qquad
h_n(u):=e^u\rho_n(e^u).
\tag{3}
\]

Then `h_n in L^1(R) cap L^2(R)` and

\[
\int_{\mathbb R}h_n(u)\,du=\Lambda(n).
\tag{4}
\]

If `q` is prime and `q` does not divide `n`, the standard cyclotomic recurrence gives the exact **translation coboundary** identity

\[
\boxed{
h_{nq}(u)=h_n(u+\log q)-h_n(u).
}
\tag{5}
\]

Now let `Q` be a positive-semidefinite Hermitian form on a translation-invariant space containing all `h_n`, and assume translations are isometries for `Q` and act strongly continuously after quotienting the nullspace and completing. If the desired exact Mangoldt support is imposed on the positive diagonal,

\[
Q(h_n)=0
\qquad
\text{whenever }n\text{ has at least two distinct prime factors},
\tag{6}
\]

then for every prime power `p^a` its class in the `Q`-Hilbert space is fixed by **every real log-radial translation**.

Indeed, for each spectator prime `q ne p`, (5) and (6) imply

\[
[\tau_{\log q}h_{p^a}]=[h_{p^a}],
\tag{7}
\]

where `tau_t h(u)=h(u+t)`. Choosing two spectator primes `q,r ne p`, the ratio `log q/log r` is irrational, so `Z log q+Z log r` is dense in `R`; strong continuity extends (7) to all real `t`.

Thus the signed radial profile cannot remain dynamically visible in any such positive completion. **Every surviving prime-power shell is forced into the zero Mellin-frequency/fixed-vector sector before the sign theorem can use it.** If the translation representation has no fixed vectors, the whole prime-power sector vanishes. If it has fixed vectors, any surviving arithmetic distinction lives in a zero-frequency multiplicity space rather than in the radial geometry.

For the scalar Bochner class the collapse is sharper. Let

\[
Q_\sigma(f,g)
=\int_{\mathbb R}
\widehat f(\xi)\overline{\widehat g(\xi)}\,d\sigma(\xi),
\qquad \sigma\ge0,
\tag{8}
\]

with every `h_n` of finite `Q_sigma`-energy. Under (6), the entire form on the span of the cyclotomic fluxes is

\[
\boxed{
Q_\sigma(h_m,h_n)
=\sigma(\{0\})\,\Lambda(m)\Lambda(n).
}
\tag{9}
\]

So the unique nontrivial scalar scale-stationary escape is the rank-one net-flux square

\[
Q_0(f)=c\left|\int_{\mathbb R}f(u)\,du\right|^2,
\qquad c\ge0.
\tag{10}
\]

It remembers only the already-known boundary mass `Lambda(n)`. With the critical amplitude normalization `n^{-1/4}`, its shell energy is proportional to

\[
\frac{\Lambda(n)^2}{\sqrt n},
\tag{11}
\]

not the finite Weil weight `Lambda(n)/sqrt(n)`. Repairing the extra `Lambda(n)` by an amplitude factor `Lambda(n)^{-1/2}` on prime powers would insert the Mangoldt selector before positivity and is undefined on the composite shells whose cancellation was supposed to be retained.

This is therefore a decisive negative for a broad version of the surviving `WP-162` route: **a common scale-stationary positive radial geometry cannot both keep the signed composite flux available for cross-shell interaction and make the positive diagonal vanish exactly on those composite shells.** Positivity turns every zero-energy composite into a radical vector, and cyclotomic dilation identities then force all surviving prime-power information to zero Mellin frequency.

The theorem does not rule out a radial operator that is genuinely non-stationary in log radius, a finite--archimedean coupling that breaks radial translation covariance before positivity, an indefinite intermediate pairing whose composite channels are not null until after global assembly, or a source geometry with extra non-radial multiplicity data. Those are now the actual remaining escapes.

## 1. The canonical radial flux becomes a translation cocycle in logarithmic radius

From `WP-162`, for `n>1`,

\[
G_n(s)=\log\Phi_n(e^{-s}),
\qquad
\rho_n=-G_n'.
\tag{12}
\]

The flux is bounded at `s=0` and decays exponentially as `s to infinity`; hence (3) lies in both `L^1(R)` and `L^2(R)`. The change of variables `s=e^u` gives

\[
\int_{\mathbb R}h_n(u)\,du
=\int_0^\infty\rho_n(s)\,ds
=\Lambda(n),
\tag{13}
\]

proving (4).

Let `q` be prime with `q not | n`. The classical cyclotomic identity

\[
\Phi_{nq}(x)=\frac{\Phi_n(x^q)}{\Phi_n(x)}
\tag{14}
\]

gives

\[
G_{nq}(s)=G_n(qs)-G_n(s).
\tag{15}
\]

Differentiating and using `rho_n=-G_n'`,

\[
\rho_{nq}(s)=q\rho_n(qs)-\rho_n(s).
\tag{16}
\]

Multiplying by `s=e^u` yields

\[
\begin{aligned}
h_{nq}(u)
&=e^u\bigl(q\rho_n(qe^u)-\rho_n(e^u)\bigr)\\
&=h_n(u+\log q)-h_n(u),
\end{aligned}
\tag{17}
\]

which is (5).

This identity is stronger than the mere net cancellation in (2). A mixed shell obtained by adjoining a new prime is literally a coboundary for translation by that prime's logarithm on the common radial line.

## 2. Positive nullity turns every composite coboundary into an exact quotient relation

Let `B` be the Hermitian sesquilinear form underlying `Q`, so `Q(f)=B(f,f)>=0`. In any positive-semidefinite form, a zero-norm vector lies in the radical. Indeed Cauchy--Schwarz gives

\[
|B(v,w)|^2\le Q(v)Q(w),
\tag{18}
\]

so `Q(v)=0` implies `B(v,w)=0` for every `w`.

Quotient the domain by this radical and complete to a Hilbert space `H_Q`. Translation invariance of `Q` gives a unitary representation

\[
U(t)[f]=[\tau_t f].
\tag{19}
\]

By hypothesis it is strongly continuous.

Fix a prime power `p^a` and a prime `q ne p`. The shell `p^a q` has two distinct prime factors, so (6) gives

\[
[h_{p^a q}]=0.
\tag{20}
\]

Using (5),

\[
\bigl(U(\log q)-I\bigr)[h_{p^a}]=0.
\tag{21}
\]

Now choose two distinct spectator primes `q,r ne p`. Unique factorization implies

\[
\frac{\log q}{\log r}\notin\mathbb Q,
\tag{22}
\]

because `q^A=r^B` has no nonzero integer solution. Therefore

\[
\mathbb Z\log q+\mathbb Z\log r
\tag{23}
\]

is dense in `R`. Equations (21) for `q` and `r` imply that `[h_{p^a}]` is fixed by `U(t)` on this dense subgroup. Strong continuity then gives

\[
\boxed{
U(t)[h_{p^a}]=[h_{p^a}]
\qquad(t\in\mathbb R).
}
\tag{24}
\]

This proves the abstract zero-frequency collapse.

The argument uses only one prime-power shell plus two mixed spectator shells. It does not use RH, zeta zeros, an asymptotic prime theorem, or the archimedean Gamma factor.

## 3. Scalar Bochner forms collapse all the way to the net-mass square

Consider the scalar spectral form (8), with Fourier convention

\[
\widehat f(\xi)=\int_{\mathbb R}f(u)e^{-i\xi u}\,du.
\tag{25}
\]

Translation obeys

\[
\widehat{\tau_a f}(\xi)
=e^{i\xi a}\widehat f(\xi).
\tag{26}
\]

Fix `p^a` and choose a spectator prime `q ne p`. From (5), (6), and positivity,

\[
0
=Q_\sigma(h_{p^a q})
=\int
|e^{i\xi\log q}-1|^2
|\widehat h_{p^a}(\xi)|^2\,d\sigma(\xi).
\tag{27}
\]

Hence the positive measure

\[
|\widehat h_{p^a}(\xi)|^2d\sigma(\xi)
\tag{28}
\]

is supported on

\[
Z_q
=\left\{\frac{2\pi k}{\log q}:k\in\mathbb Z\right\}.
\tag{29}
\]

Repeating with a second spectator prime `r`, it is supported on `Z_q cap Z_r`. Irrationality (22) gives

\[
Z_q\cap Z_r=\{0\}.
\tag{30}
\]

Therefore `widehat h_{p^a}=0` `sigma`-almost everywhere away from zero. At zero, (13) gives

\[
\widehat h_{p^a}(0)=\log p.
\tag{31}
\]

Every composite shell has zero `Q_sigma`-norm by assumption and is therefore also zero in `L^2(sigma)` after Fourier transform. It follows by polarization that for arbitrary shell indices `m,n`,

\[
Q_\sigma(h_m,h_n)
=\sigma(\{0\})\widehat h_m(0)\overline{\widehat h_n(0)}
=\sigma(\{0\})\Lambda(m)\Lambda(n),
\tag{32}
\]

which proves (9). Finiteness of any prime-power energy forces `sigma({0})<infinity`.

The conclusion is sharp. Taking

\[
\sigma=c\delta_0
\tag{33}
\]

gives exactly (10), satisfies (6), and keeps every prime-power shell nonzero when `c>0`. So the theorem is not saying that exact positive sparsity is algebraically impossible. It classifies the only scalar scale-stationary way to obtain it: project onto the conserved net flux already equal to `Lambda`.

## 4. Critical half-density does not repair the arithmetic coefficient

A natural attempt is to insert the Riemann critical amplitude before taking the positive radial norm. More generally, let

\[
f_n=a_n h_n,
\qquad a_n\ne0.
\tag{34}
\]

For `q not | n`, (5) becomes

\[
f_{nq}
=\frac{a_{nq}}{a_n}
\bigl(\tau_{\log q}f_n-f_n\bigr).
\tag{35}
\]

The scalar factor is nonzero, so exact composite nullity still forces the same translation invariance and zero-frequency collapse. In the scalar Bochner class,

\[
Q_\sigma(f_n)
=c|a_n|^2\Lambda(n)^2.
\tag{36}
\]

For the critical amplitude `a_n=n^{-1/4}` this is

\[
Q_\sigma(f_{p^k})
=c\frac{(\log p)^2}{p^{k/2}},
\tag{37}
\]

whereas the finite Weil coefficient is proportional to

\[
\frac{\log p}{p^{k/2}}.
\tag{38}
\]

The prime-power support and the critical decay are present, but the logarithmic weight is squared by positivity. To turn (37) into (38) through amplitude normalization one must insert a factor `(log p)^(-1/2)`, equivalently `Lambda(p^k)^(-1/2)`, on exactly the surviving shells. That normalization has already used the arithmetic selector which the geometric form was meant to force; it has no canonical extension through the mixed shells where `Lambda(n)=0` but `h_n` is nonzero.

This is the radial analogue of a recurring Mathia boundary: a linear signed source can carry the exact arithmetic weight, while an ordinary positive norm naturally squares its amplitude.

## 5. Matched controls and exact scope

The obstruction has several clean controls.

First, one spectator prime is not enough. Requiring only `Q(h_{p^a q})=0` forces spectral support into the nonzero lattice `Z_q` as well as zero; a scale-stationary positive form supported on that lattice can survive. The collapse to zero frequency requires two multiplicatively independent spectator primes. The all-composite support condition supplies them automatically.

Second, if translation covariance in log radius is dropped, an arbitrary positive form can distinguish the functions `h_n` by hand. The theorem does not call that impossible; it says the common radial scale no longer explains the distinction.

Third, if composite shells are allowed nonzero final energy, they need not enter the radical and can participate in cross-shell coupling. That may be required in a genuinely global Weil form. The theorem applies specifically to constructions that try to obtain exact prime-power support as the positive diagonal of the radial shell geometry.

Fourth, an abstract translation-invariant Hilbert completion may have a multidimensional fixed subspace. Then the theorem forces all surviving radial information into that fixed subspace but does not force different primes to be collinear. Any such multiplicity is extra channel data not generated by the one-dimensional radial coordinate. The rank-one conclusion (9) is specific to scalar Bochner forms on the common log-radial line.

Finally, the theorem does not manufacture the missing archimedean term. Quite the opposite: after the collapse, the radial translation generator acts trivially on every surviving shell. A Gamma/digamma boundary response would therefore have to enter through an additional non-stationary or non-radial structure rather than emerge from the sign theorem classified here.

## 6. Prior-art and novelty audit

The ingredients are classical separately. Identity (14) is a standard cyclotomic recurrence; the logarithmic derivative/Ramanujan-sum description of `rho_n` is classical and was already isolated in `WP-162`; positive translation-invariant scalar forms admit the standard Bochner spectral description; and irrational prime-log ratios give a dense two-generator translation subgroup.

A focused literature search around cyclotomic logarithmic derivatives/Ramanujan sums, radial/Mellin transforms, and translation-invariant positive quadratic forms did not identify a prior theorem asserting the Mathia-specific implication proved here: exact nullity of all mixed cyclotomic radial shells forces the prime-power sector of a common scale-stationary positive form into zero Mellin frequency, with scalar forms reducing to the net-Mangoldt rank-one square. No grand novelty is claimed for the harmonic-analysis or cyclotomic ingredients.

The closest internal prior art is complementary rather than duplicative. `WP-162` proves that pointwise-positive radial bulk energies lose Mangoldt support but explicitly leaves global/nonlocal radial coupling open. `WP-272` rules out continuous scalar spectral orientation of a different pole-completed carrier. `WP-275` uses pure-point versus absolutely-continuous spectral type to kill source-to-continuum intertwiners. Here the obstruction comes from the exact cyclotomic dilation coboundary (5) plus positivity of the composite-shell nullspace.

## 7. Consequence for the active research line

The accepted noncharacter finite--archimedean incidence clue asks whether the signed `WP-162` flux can be retained until after a cross-shell or finite--archimedean positive coupling. The present result removes the most canonical stationary version of that escape.

A surviving mechanism cannot simply place all radial profiles in one common scale-homogeneous positive kernel, demand zero positive energy on every mixed shell, and hope cross-shell correlations preserve additional arithmetic information. Exact composite nullity first turns the mixed shells into radical relations; those relations make every prime-power survivor translation-fixed; and scalar common-line geometry then remembers only `Lambda(n)` through the conserved net mass.

The remaining search should therefore prioritize one of three genuine category changes: a source-forced **non-stationary** radial/boundary operator, an **indefinite intermediate** coupling in which mixed shells remain active until finite and archimedean pieces are assembled, or an additional global geometry whose non-radial channels create the sign theorem before the Mangoldt selector is scalarized. Any proposal that returns to a scale-stationary positive radial norm with exact composite-shell nullity has already reduced to (10).
