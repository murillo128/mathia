# WP-405 — Pointed analytic gauge positivity collapses to fixed points

## Claim

`WP-404` shows that a sufficiently thin one-sided source slice can evade the dense two-sided tangent obstruction and manufacture a linear orientation. A natural way to demand that such one-sidedness come from the arithmetic source rather than from a hand-picked slice is to use the canonical positive semigroup of the Bost--Connes gauge grading: retain the fixed algebra together with spectral degrees generated by `N^times`, but omit their inverse degrees.

For **ambient operator positivity**, that canonical analytic escape is empty.

Let `M` be a C*-algebra, or a von Neumann algebra, carrying a continuous respectively normal action `theta` of a compact abelian group `K`. Write

\[
\Gamma=\widehat K
\]

for its discrete dual and `M_gamma` for the spectral subspace of degree `gamma`. Let `P subset Gamma` be a unital pointed semigroup,

\[
P\cap P^{-1}=\{1\}.
\tag{1}
\]

Let `M_P` denote the norm-closed analytic algebra generated by `M_gamma`, `gamma in P`, in the C* case, or its weak-star closed analogue in the von Neumann case. Then

\[
\boxed{(M_P)_{sa}=M_1{}_{sa}}
\qquad\text{and hence}\qquad
\boxed{M_P\cap M_+=M_1\cap M_+.}
\tag{2}
\]

Thus a pointed one-sided Fourier support may retain non-self-adjoint arithmetic orientation, but the moment one asks for an element that is positive in the ambient operator algebra all nonzero one-sided frequencies disappear.

For the critical Bost--Connes gauge grading of `WP-395`--`WP-397`,

\[
\Gamma=\mathbf Q_+^\times,
\qquad
P=\mathbf N^\times,
\tag{3}
\]

and `P` is pointed inside `Gamma`. Therefore the canonical bounded analytic algebra generated by the fixed-point algebra and the positive semigroup degrees has exactly the same ambient positive cone as its fixed-point algebra. This closes the most direct **source-canonical one-sided positivity** repair suggested by the sharpness example `WP-404`.

It does not close non-self-adjoint correspondences, genuinely unbounded analytic domains, nonlinear positive forms, or constructions whose positivity lives in a larger dilation/crossed-product object rather than as ambient positivity of an element of `M_P`.

**Classification:** `EXACT-DERIVED + RH-INDEPENDENT + COMPACT-ABELIAN-GAUGE + POINTED-SEMIGROUP + ANALYTIC-SPECTRAL-ALGEBRA + SELF-ADJOINT-PART-FIXED + POSITIVE-CONE-FIXED + BOST-CONNES-N-TIMES-SPECIALIZATION + SOURCE-CANONICAL-ONE-SIDED-NO-GO + WP-404-FOLLOWUP + NOT-A-WEIL-BRIDGE`.

## 1. Fourier projections preserve the one-sided support

For `gamma in Gamma`, let

\[
E_\gamma(x)
=\int_K \overline{\gamma(k)}\,\theta_k(x)\,dk
\tag{4}
\]

be the spectral projection. In the von Neumann case the same formula is interpreted normally. The standard spectral identities give

\[
E_\gamma(x^*)=E_{\gamma^{-1}}(x)^*.
\tag{5}
\]

By construction, every algebraic polynomial in spectral terms whose degrees lie in `P` has zero `delta`-coefficient for `delta notin P`. Because each `E_delta` is continuous in the C* norm, and normal in the von Neumann setting, closure does not create forbidden Fourier coefficients. Hence

\[
x\in M_P,\ \delta\notin P
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
E_\delta(x)=0.
\tag{6}
\]

This is the only support property needed below. No positivity theorem or arithmetic interpolation is hidden in the definition of `M_P`.

## 2. Self-adjointness forces every nontrivial analytic coefficient to vanish

Take `x in M_P` with `x=x^*`. Let `gamma in P` be nontrivial. Pointedness (1) implies

\[
\gamma^{-1}\notin P.
\tag{7}
\]

Equation (6) therefore gives `E_{gamma^{-1}}(x)=0`, while (5) and self-adjointness give

\[
E_\gamma(x)
=E_\gamma(x^*)
=E_{\gamma^{-1}}(x)^*
=0.
\tag{8}
\]

All nontrivial coefficients in `P` vanish, and coefficients outside `P` already vanish by (6). Thus only the identity coefficient survives. Fourier uniqueness for compact group actions, equivalently the usual Fejer/Peter--Weyl approximation by spectral projections, yields

\[
x=E_1(x)\in M_1.
\tag{9}
\]

The reverse inclusion `M_1{}_{sa} subset (M_P)_{sa}` is immediate because `1 in P`. This proves the first equality in (2). Since a positive element is self-adjoint, the positive-cone equality follows immediately.

The mechanism is exact: **positivity requires adjoint pairing of a frequency with its inverse, while a pointed analytic spectrum deliberately keeps at most one member of every nontrivial inverse pair.**

## 3. Bost--Connes specialization

In the critical Bost--Connes representation used in `WP-395`--`WP-397`, the compact gauge action is indexed by

\[
K=\widehat{\mathbf Q_+^\times},
\]

with spectral degrees in `Gamma=Q_+^times`. The standard semigroup isometries `mu_n` have positive arithmetic degree `n`, and multiplication generates the semigroup

\[
P=\mathbf N^\times.
\tag{10}
\]

Inside `Q_+^times`,

\[
\mathbf N^\times\cap(\mathbf N^\times)^{-1}=\{1\},
\tag{11}
\]

so this is a pointed semigroup. Let `A_+` be the norm-closed algebra generated by the gauge-fixed part together with positive-degree spectral subspaces, or let `M_+^{an}` be the corresponding weak-star closed analytic algebra in the critical GNS von Neumann algebra. Then (2) gives

\[
A_+\cap A_{BC,+}=A_+\cap (A_{BC})^{\theta}_+,
\tag{12}
\]

and, in the von Neumann completion,

\[
M_+^{an}\cap M_+=M_1\cap M_+.
\tag{13}
\]

Thus choosing the canonical arithmetic half of the gauge spectrum does provide genuine source-forced one-sided **non-self-adjoint** geometry, but it cannot by itself provide a new ambient positive cone. Any bounded positive element visible inside that analytic algebra is already fixed by the gauge action.

This is the multi-frequency analytic counterpart of `WP-396`. There, one nonzero homogeneous frequency cannot itself be positive. Here, even an arbitrary or infinite superposition of frequencies from a pointed half-spectrum cannot evade the same adjoint-pair obstruction as long as the whole element remains inside the analytic algebra.

## 4. Relation to the `WP-404` sharpness escape

`WP-404` matters because it prevents an overstrong conclusion from `WP-401`--`WP-403`: one-sided positive geometry can exist on a thin source-selected slice. The remaining question is whether Bost--Connes already supplies such a slice canonically.

The positive semigroup `N^times` is an unusually natural candidate. Unlike the hand-picked rank-one line in `WP-404`, its orientation is source-defined and arithmetic. The present result shows, however, that **source canonicity alone is not enough**. In a pointed analytic operator algebra, the same feature that makes the Fourier support one-sided also prevents a nonzero-frequency element from being self-adjoint. Ambient positivity therefore projects the candidate back to frequency zero before any Weil-sign mechanism appears.

This is a stronger diagnostic than saying that the analytic algebra is non-self-adjoint. It identifies the exact positive cone and shows that no cancellation among infinitely many positive semigroup degrees can produce an extra positive element: every nonzero Fourier coefficient is killed coefficientwise by self-adjointness and pointedness.

## 5. Stress tests and boundaries

### Pointedness is essential

If `P` contains both `gamma` and `gamma^{-1}` for some nontrivial degree, the conclusion fails in general. A spectral element `y in M_gamma` can be paired with `y^* in M_{gamma^{-1}}`, and expressions such as

\[
(1+y)^*(1+y)
\tag{14}
\]

may be positive and non-fixed. Therefore the theorem is specifically about a genuine analytic half-spectrum, not about arbitrary multi-frequency subalgebras.

### Infinite analytic sums do not evade the argument

The proof never assumes finite Fourier support. Equation (6) survives norm or weak-star closure, and (8) kills every allowed nontrivial coefficient separately. Fourier uniqueness then removes the possibility of an invisible residual term. The obstruction is therefore not a finite-cutoff artefact.

### The statement concerns ambient operator positivity

A non-self-adjoint analytic algebra may carry other notions of order, accretivity, complete contractivity, Toeplitz positivity after dilation, graph-form positivity, or positive kernels on a module. Those are changed hypotheses. `WP-405` says only that an element of the pointed analytic algebra that is positive in the surrounding C* or von Neumann algebra already belongs to the fixed-point algebra.

### Compact gauge action matters

The proof uses discrete Fourier coefficients for a compact abelian gauge action. A noncompact modular `R`-action can have one-sided Arveson spectrum and requires the corresponding spectral theory; the compact argument should not be transferred to that setting by notation alone.

## 6. Consequence for the canonical Weil-positivity search

The accepted Bost--Connes completion clue remains broader than this finding, so it is not retired. But one concrete route is now closed: **take the canonical positive semigroup/analytic half of the Bost--Connes gauge spectrum and hope that its ambient positive cone supplies the missing one-sided arithmetic sign.** It does not; that cone is exactly the fixed-point positive cone.

A viable one-sided source construction must therefore change at least one structural ingredient. It could use a genuinely non-self-adjoint object whose orientation is read only after coupling to another sector; a singular or unbounded domain not represented by a positive element of the bounded analytic algebra; a correspondence/dilation whose positive form lives outside the analytic algebra; or a nonlinear/global construction in which inverse frequencies re-enter in a source-forced way without reducing to the already classified fixed-shadow selector mechanisms.

Any such replacement still has to meet the canonical mandate: recover the finite-prime Weil coefficients together with the archimedean/global normalization and survive matched non-arithmetic controls. Escaping (2) is only permission to continue, not evidence of the desired sign theorem.

## 7. Prior-art, novelty, and adversarial audit

The underlying operator-algebra theorem is classical in spirit. Arveson's analytic-subalgebra framework and the standard spectral-subspace theory for compact group actions already encode the relation between one-sided spectrum, adjoints, and fixed points. No theorem-level originality is claimed here. Bost--Connes supplies the arithmetic grading and its canonical semigroup orientation.

The repository novelty is the program-specific closure of the one-sided route exposed by `WP-404`: after thinness was shown capable of manufacturing orientation, the most canonical arithmetic half-spectrum was the obvious control to test. The exact positive-cone collapse above was not present in the existing `weil_positivity` findings, and it extends `WP-396` from one homogeneous mode to arbitrary bounded analytic superpositions from the pointed semigroup.

The adversarial checks isolate the assumptions sharply. Dropping pointedness allows inverse-paired positive elements; abandoning ambient operator positivity changes the object; abandoning compact spectral support requires different analysis. A matched non-arithmetic compact action with the same pointed semigroup obeys the identical theorem, so the collapse itself is not arithmetic evidence. That is appropriate for a no-go: it eliminates a structural escape rather than manufacturing a target-specific obstruction.

The decisive next test is therefore not to search for a more elaborate positive element inside the same bounded analytic algebra. It is to identify a source-canonical construction whose positivity is genuinely outside this ambient analytic positive cone and then test whether that added structure produces the actual Weil coefficients rather than another generic orientation.
