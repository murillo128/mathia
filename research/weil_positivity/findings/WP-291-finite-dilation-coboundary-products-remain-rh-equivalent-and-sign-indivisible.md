# WP-291 — Finite products of source-native dilation coboundaries remain RH-equivalent and sign-indivisible

## Claim

`WP-290` shows that one intrinsic integer-dilation coboundary removes the pole tangent of the critical Mangoldt half-density, but the resulting signed measure is tempered if and only if RH and its positive and negative Jordan channels are separately non-tempered. A natural remaining escape is to iterate the same source-native operation: higher finite differences might conceivably cancel more of the dangerous growth before any positivity theorem is read.

They do not.

Let

\[
\mu:=\sum_{n\ge2}\frac{\Lambda(n)}{\sqrt n}\,\delta_{\log n},
\qquad
\Delta_q:=I-\sqrt q\,S_{\log q},
\qquad q\ge2,
\tag{1}
\]

with `S_a delta_x=delta_(x+a)` as in `WP-290`. For any finite nonempty list of integers

\[
\mathbf q=(q_1,\ldots,q_r),\qquad q_j\ge2,
\tag{2}
\]

define the iterated source-dilation coboundary

\[
\boxed{
D_{\mathbf q}\mu
:=\Delta_{q_1}\cdots\Delta_{q_r}\mu.
}
\tag{3}
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
D_{\mathbf q}\mu\in\mathcal S'(\mathbb R)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\mathrm{RH}.
}
\tag{4}
\]

Moreover, writing the Jordan decomposition

\[
D_{\mathbf q}\mu=(D_{\mathbf q}\mu)_+-(D_{\mathbf q}\mu)_-,
\tag{5}
\]

**both positive measures are individually non-tempered for every finite nonempty `mathbf q`, unconditionally.** Thus no finite order of the intrinsic dilation-difference hierarchy converts the critical source into separately admissible positive channels.

The reason is rigid in transform space. For `Re s>1/2`,

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal L(D_{\mathbf q}\mu)(s)
=
\prod_{j=1}^r\bigl(1-q_j^{1/2-s}\bigr)
\left(-\frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}(s+1/2)\right).
}
\tag{6}
\]

Every factor vanishes at the zeta-pole location `s=1/2`, so the pole tangent is not merely canceled: for `r>1` the product has excess vanishing there. But at a pole coming from any nontrivial zeta zero `rho`, namely `s=rho-1/2`, every multiplier factor is

\[
1-q_j^{1-\rho}\neq0,
\tag{7}
\]

because every nontrivial zero satisfies `0<Re rho<1`, hence

\[
|q_j^{1-\rho}|=q_j^{1-\operatorname{Re}\rho}>1.
\tag{8}
\]

So **finite products of the canonical pole-killing factors cancel no nontrivial zeta zero at all**. The full zero obstruction survives every finite iteration.

A matched nonarithmetic control again separates generic homogeneous pole removal from arithmetic content. For

\[
\mu_{\mathbb N}:=\sum_{n\ge1}n^{-1/2}\delta_{\log n},
\tag{9}
\]

the same `D_mathbf q mu_N` is tempered unconditionally for every finite `mathbf q`. The finite-difference hierarchy is therefore a generic source-homogeneity mechanism; only the Mangoldt logarithmic derivative turns post-cancellation category descent into an RH-equivalent statement.

**Classification:** `EXACT-DERIVED-CLOSURE + SOURCE-NATIVE-FINITE-DILATION-HIERARCHY + POLE-ANNIHILATION + NO-ZERO-CANCELLATION + RH-EQUIVALENT-TEMPEREDNESS + BOTH-JORDAN-PARTS-NONTEMPERED + MATCHED-CONTROL + FINITE-ITERATION-ESCAPE-CLOSED + NOT-A-WEIL-POSITIVITY-PROOF`.

## 1. The finite hierarchy is canonically generated by the multiplicative source

The operators `Delta_q` commute because the translations do. Expanding (3),

\[
D_{\mathbf q}
=
\sum_{J\subseteq\{1,\ldots,r\}}
(-1)^{|J|}\sqrt{Q_J}\,S_{\log Q_J},
\qquad
Q_J:=\prod_{j\in J}q_j,
\tag{10}
\]

with `Q_emptyset=1`.

This is still entirely source-native. Each translation is multiplication by an integer `Q_J` in the original arithmetic coordinate, and each amplitude `sqrt(Q_J)` is forced by the same critical half-density scaling used in `WP-290`. No zero ordinate, test-dependent coefficient, or external regularization appears in (10).

On the pole tangent

\[
\lambda=e^{x/2}\mathbf 1_{x\ge0}\,dx,
\tag{11}
\]

the first coboundary gives the compact boundary layer from `WP-290`,

\[
\Delta_q\lambda
=e^{x/2}\mathbf 1_{[0,\log q)}(x)\,dx.
\tag{12}
\]

Every later `Delta_q` is a finite linear combination of a compactly supported distribution and its right translate. Hence

\[
\boxed{
D_{\mathbf q}\lambda\text{ has compact support for every }r\ge1.
}
\tag{13}
\]

So higher finite differences do genuinely remove the entire exponentially growing pole tangent in real space. Their failure below is not failure to cancel enough of the pole background; it is the persistence of the arithmetic zero modes.

## 2. Exact transform multiplier and zero rigidity

For a right translation,

\[
\mathcal L(S_{\log q}T)(s)=q^{-s}\mathcal L(T)(s),
\tag{14}
\]

therefore

\[
\mathcal L(\Delta_qT)(s)
=(1-q^{1/2-s})\mathcal L(T)(s).
\tag{15}
\]

Iterating (15) and using

\[
\mathcal L\mu(s)=-\frac{\zeta'}{\zeta}(s+1/2)
\qquad(\operatorname{Re}s>1/2)
\tag{16}
\]

gives (6).

At `s=1/2`, every factor in the finite product vanishes. Since `-zeta'/zeta(s+1/2)` has a simple pole there, one factor suffices to remove it and every additional factor only increases the order of vanishing of the completed expression.

Now take any nontrivial zero `rho` of zeta. Its logarithmic derivative pole is at

\[
s_\rho=\rho-1/2.
\tag{17}
\]

A factor in (6) could cancel that pole only if

\[
q_j^{1-\rho}=1.
\tag{18}
\]

Taking absolute values forces `Re rho=1`, whereas every nontrivial zero lies strictly inside the critical strip. Therefore (18) is impossible. This proves a stronger statement than merely preserving off-critical zeros:

\[
\boxed{
\prod_{j=1}^r(1-q_j^{1-\rho})\neq0
\quad\text{for every nontrivial zeta zero }\rho.
}
\tag{19}
\]

Thus finite products of the intrinsic dilation coboundaries distinguish the pole from the complete nontrivial zero divisor exactly: they annihilate the former and none of the latter.

## 3. Temperedness remains equivalent to RH at every finite order

Assume RH. By `WP-290`, `Delta_(q_1) mu` is tempered. Translation is a continuous automorphism of `S'(R)`, and each further operator

\[
\Delta_{q_j}=I-\sqrt{q_j}S_{\log q_j}
\tag{20}
\]

is therefore continuous on tempered distributions. Hence

\[
\mathrm{RH}
\Longrightarrow
D_{\mathbf q}\mu\in\mathcal S'(\mathbb R).
\tag{21}
\]

Conversely, suppose `D_mathbf q mu` is tempered. Its support is bounded below because all shifts in (10) are to the right. The Fourier--Laplace transform of a half-line-supported tempered distribution is holomorphic in `Re s>0`.

If RH fails, functional-equation symmetry gives a nontrivial zero `rho` with `Re rho>1/2`. Then `s_rho=rho-1/2` lies in that open half-plane. By (19), the finite multiplier in (6) is nonzero at `s_rho`, so the logarithmic-derivative pole survives. That contradicts the holomorphy forced by temperedness. Hence

\[
D_{\mathbf q}\mu\in\mathcal S'(\mathbb R)
\Longrightarrow
\mathrm{RH},
\tag{22}
\]

which proves (4).

This closes the obvious higher-finite-difference escape from `WP-290`: killing the pole to arbitrarily high **finite** order does not move the arithmetic admissibility gate by even one step. The gate remains exactly RH.

## 4. The positive Jordan channel always retains the full prime half-density

The coefficient of `D_mathbf q mu` at the atom `log N` follows directly from (10):

\[
c_{\mathbf q}(N)
=
\frac1{\sqrt N}
\sum_{\substack{J\subseteq\{1,\ldots,r\}\\Q_J\mid N}}
(-1)^{|J|}Q_J\Lambda(N/Q_J).
\tag{23}
\]

Let `p` be a prime. Any nonempty term in (23) requires `Q_J|p`. If this occurs then necessarily `Q_J=p`, and the Mangoldt factor is `Lambda(1)=0`. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
c_{\mathbf q}(p)=\frac{\log p}{\sqrt p}>0}
\tag{24}
\]

for **every prime**, independently of the chosen finite list `mathbf q`.

By the prime number theorem and partial summation,

\[
\sum_{p\le X}\frac{\log p}{\sqrt p}
\sim2\sqrt X.
\tag{25}
\]

Consequently

\[
(D_{\mathbf q}\mu)_+([0,x])
\ge(2+o(1))e^{x/2},
\tag{26}
\]

so the positive Jordan measure is never tempered.

This is already enough to show that finite source differences do not turn the arithmetic carrier into a positive admissible measure. But the negative channel can also be forced directly, without assuming RH.

## 5. A universal negative prime ray survives every finite product

Let

\[
d:=\min_j q_j,
\tag{27}
\]

and let `m>=1` be the multiplicity of this minimum among the `q_j`. Put `Q=prod_j q_j` and choose a prime `p` with `p not| Q`.

Evaluate (23) at `N=dp`. The empty-subset term is zero because `dp` has at least two distinct prime divisors, so

\[
\Lambda(dp)=0.
\tag{28}
\]

For a nonempty subset `J`, if `Q_J|dp`, then `p not|Q` implies `Q_J|d`. But every factor `q_j` is at least `d`, so `Q_J>=d`; equality can occur only for a singleton with `q_j=d`. Any subset of size at least two has `Q_J>=d^2>d`.

Thus exactly the `m` singleton minimum factors contribute, and

\[
\boxed{
c_{\mathbf q}(dp)
=-m\sqrt d\,\frac{\log p}{\sqrt p}<0.}
\tag{29}
\]

As `p` ranges over primes avoiding the fixed finite set of prime divisors of `Q`, partial summation again yields square-root mass growth. Hence

\[
(D_{\mathbf q}\mu)_-([0,x])
\ge(2m+o(1))e^{x/2}
\tag{30}
\]

up to the harmless fixed shift and scale from `d`. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
(D_{\mathbf q}\mu)_+\notin\mathcal S',
\qquad
(D_{\mathbf q}\mu)_-\notin\mathcal S'
}
\tag{31}
\]

for every finite nonempty hierarchy, with no RH assumption.

This is the finite-order sign-indivisibility statement. The only possible tempered object in the hierarchy is the **signed cancellation itself**, and even that exists in the ordinary tempered category exactly when RH holds. Taking absolute values, total variation, positive direct sums, or separately completed positive and negative sectors destroys the category descent at every finite order.

## 6. Matched control: the hierarchy is generic pole cancellation

For the counting half-density

\[
\mu_{\mathbb N}
=\sum_{n\ge1}n^{-1/2}\delta_{\log n},
\tag{32}
\]

`WP-290` proves that `Delta_q mu_N` is tempered unconditionally for every fixed `q>=2`. Applying any additional finite sequence of translations and finite linear combinations preserves temperedness. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
D_{\mathbf q}\mu_{\mathbb N}\in\mathcal S'(\mathbb R)
\quad\text{unconditionally for every finite }\mathbf q.
}
\tag{33}
\]

Both the arithmetic and nonarithmetic sources have the same leading `e^(x/2)` homogeneity, and the same finite coboundary hierarchy removes that homogeneity. The difference is what remains: `zeta(s+1/2)` has no zero-generated poles, while `-zeta'/zeta(s+1/2)` does. Hence the RH-equivalent obstruction in (4) is not a generic feature of finite differences; it is precisely the residual arithmetic zero divisor.

## 7. Aggressive falsification and scope

**Repeated differences do not help.** Taking `q_1=...=q_r=q` gives multiplier

\[
(1-q^{1/2-s})^r.
\tag{34}
\]

This kills the pole to order `r-1` after multiplication by the simple logarithmic-derivative pole, yet (19) shows that no nontrivial zero pole is canceled. More pole annihilation is therefore irrelevant to the remaining category obstruction.

**Using several different integers does not help.** Each elementary factor has the same zero-rigidity property in the critical strip. Their finite product cannot acquire a nontrivial-zero zero that none of its factors possesses.

**This finding does not classify arbitrary finite signed translation filters.** A general finite exponential polynomial

\[
\sum_k a_k d_k^{1/2-s}
\tag{35}
\]

can have zeros away from the pole line, and coefficients chosen after inspecting a target zero could cancel selected zero poles. Such a construction is outside the present theorem and, if zero-selected, would violate the research mandate anyway. The result here closes the canonical multiplicative ideal generated by the intrinsic elementary pole-killing coboundaries; it does not claim that every conceivable finite signed source coupling factors through them.

**No positive theorem has appeared.** Equations (24) and (29) show that the higher-order hierarchy remains intrinsically sign-indefinite at source level, with exponentially large positive and negative channels. Temperedness, when it occurs, is a cancellation property rather than a geometric nonnegativity theorem.

**No archimedean Gamma term is generated.** Just as in `WP-290`, the finite semigroup differences remove the pole tangent but do not produce the Riemann Gamma/digamma contribution or the full Weil explicit-formula pairing.

**Infinite products are genuinely different and are not covered.** An infinite product or infinite signed semigroup coupling would require a source-defined topology, domain and convergence theorem. If its convergence or admissibility is equivalent to controlling the zeta zero divisor, it merely relocates the RH gate. If its coefficients are selected from zero locations, it is circular. This remains a possible category change only if the source itself canonically forces the infinite coupling and an independent sign theorem survives it.

## 8. Prior-art and novelty audit

No historical novelty is claimed for the analytic ingredients. Finite differences as products of translation operators, the Dirichlet-series identity for `-zeta'/zeta`, pole-killing Euler-type factors, prime-number-theorem asymptotics, and Fourier--Laplace theory are classical. Arias de Reyna's exact pole-completed critical measure remains the closest prior-art anchor for the RH-equivalent temperedness boundary identified in `WP-270`.

A bounded literature search was made for higher finite multiplicative/dilation differences of the critical weighted Mangoldt measure, finite products of factors `1-q^(1-s)` applied to the logarithmic derivative, and RH criteria phrased through such iterated differences. The search did not locate a direct named result matching the exact finite-hierarchy statement (4) together with the unconditional two-Jordan-channel obstruction (31). That absence is not evidence of historical novelty.

The retained contribution is therefore a **Mathia-internal structural closure** of `WP-290`: the first source-native signed cancellation was not an accident of order one. Every finite composition of the same intrinsic pole-killing operation has exactly the same target-strength admissibility boundary and the same failure of positive-channel separation.

## Consequence for `weil_positivity`

The finite-difference branch can now be narrowed sharply. The route

\[
\text{critical Mangoldt source}
\to
\text{finite product of intrinsic dilation coboundaries}
\to
\text{ordinary admissible signed carrier}
\to
\text{positive geometry}
\tag{36}
\]

fails at the second arrow unconditionally: ordinary tempered admissibility is still equivalent to RH, while the two positive Jordan channels remain exponentially non-tempered regardless of RH.

A surviving source-native mechanism must therefore leave this finite factorized hierarchy. It would need a genuinely non-factorized global signed coupling, a canonically forced infinite/category-changing construction with independent convergence, or a different geometric object in which cancellation, admissibility and positivity are controlled by separate source-derived theorems. Merely iterating the pole-killing dilation difference, even to arbitrarily high finite order and with arbitrary fixed integer scales, cannot supply the missing Weil positivity mechanism.
