# WP-300 — Prime-symmetric projective source curvature has only flat or fermionic holonomy, and the fermionic branch is only a Boolean support selector

## Claim

`WP-299` closes scalar projective curvature placed only in the **output** of an exactly equivariant linear response from the ordinary commuting multiplicative source. It leaves one structurally different escape: change the source geometry itself so that distinct prime directions already carry a compatible projective action.

Under the strongest pre-arithmetic canonicality condition — invariance under finite relabelings of the prime generators — the scalar commutator holonomy of such a projective source has only two possibilities:

\[
\boxed{\beta(p,q)=+1\quad\text{or}\quad\beta(p,q)=-1\qquad(p\ne q).}
\tag{1}
\]

The `+1` branch is flat. The sole nonflat scalar commutator law is the universal fermionic/Koszul sign. Mathia already contains exactly this branch in the Clifford generators of `WP-029`.

On the occupied-prime Boolean fiber of `n`, the corresponding ordinary plaquette holonomy is `-I` for every pair of distinct occupied primes. Its positive plaquette-defect energy has zero kernel exactly when `n` has at most one distinct prime divisor. Multiplying the harmonic projection by the existing Clifford radius gives

\[
\boxed{
M_n=A(n)\mathbf 1_{\{0\}}(H_n)=\Lambda(n)I.
}
\tag{2}
\]

So the source-curvature escape is mathematically real at the local finite-place level. But the nonflat sign is standard CAR/Koszul structure, survives unchanged on arbitrary generalized-prime free monoids, and carries no Gamma or polar data. A naive equal-footing addition of one always-present archimedean Clifford direction destroys the selector even on prime powers. After coefficient extraction, `WP-005` still forces the same indefinite Weil autocorrelation lift.

Thus fully prime-symmetric **scalar** projective source curvature is not a rich new arithmetic degree of freedom: its nonflat branch collapses to the Boolean prime-power support already isolated by `WP-030`.

**Classification:** `EXACT-DERIVED + PROJECTIVE-SOURCE-ESCAPE-TESTED + PRIME-RELABELING-INVARIANT-SCALAR-HOLONOMY-CLASSIFIED + UNIQUE-NONFLAT-FERMIONIC-BRANCH + MATHIA-CLIFFORD-REALIZATION + POSITIVE-CURVATURE-NULLSPACE + EXACT-MANGOLDT-SUPPORT + GENERALIZED-PRIME-GENERIC + KOSZUL-PRIOR-ART-REDIRECT + ARCHIMEDEAN-EQUAL-FOOTING-NO-GO + GLOBAL-WEIL-POSITIVITY-STILL-MISSING`.

## 1. Prime symmetry leaves only flat and fermionic scalar commutator laws

Let

\[
G=\bigoplus_{p\in\mathcal P}\mathbb Z e_p
\tag{3}
\]

be the free abelian group generated by prime labels, and let a projective unitary representation satisfy

\[
V_pV_q=\beta(p,q)V_qV_p,
\qquad \beta(p,q)\in U(1).
\tag{4}
\]

For a multiplier on an abelian group, the commutator ratio is alternating:

\[
\beta(p,p)=1,
\qquad
\beta(q,p)=\beta(p,q)^{-1}.
\tag{5}
\]

Assume the commutator law is invariant under every finite permutation `pi` of the prime labels,

\[
\beta(\pi p,\pi q)=\beta(p,q).
\tag{6}
\]

All ordered pairs of distinct labels lie in one orbit, so `beta(p,q)=c` for one constant `c`. A permutation can swap `p` and `q`, hence

\[
c=\beta(p,q)=\beta(q,p)=c^{-1}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{c\in\{+1,-1\}.}
\tag{7}
\]

This classifies the scalar **commutator/holonomy law**, not literal cocycle representatives: symmetric coboundary freedom remains. The flat sign is `+1`; the unique nonflat prime-symmetric sign is `-1`.

## 2. The nonflat branch is already the WP-029 Clifford sign

For

\[
S(n)=\{p:p\mid n\},
\qquad r=|S(n)|=\omega(n),
\tag{8}
\]

`WP-029` identifies the backward Boolean cube with `Lambda^* C^r` and defines

\[
\gamma_p=i(\iota_p-\epsilon_p),
\qquad
\gamma_p^2=I,
\qquad
\gamma_p\gamma_q=-\gamma_q\gamma_p
\quad(p\ne q).
\tag{9}
\]

The ordinary holonomy comparing the two oriented paths around an occupied-prime diamond is therefore

\[
K_{p,q}
:=(\gamma_q\gamma_p)^{-1}\gamma_p\gamma_q
=-I.
\tag{10}
\]

This realizes exactly the nonflat sign in (7).

The interpretation matters. The `gamma_p` are odd Clifford operators, and (9) says that they **supercommute**. Thus the minus sign is the standard Koszul/CAR sign after forgetting the natural grading, not a newly discovered arithmetic curvature invariant. Standard multiplier theory and group-theoretic treatments of CAR are direct prior art for this projective structure.

## 3. The positive curvature-nullspace recovers `Lambda` exactly

On the occupied-prime Boolean fiber define

\[
H_n
:=\frac14\sum_{\{p,q\}\subset S(n)}
(I-K_{p,q})^*(I-K_{p,q})
\succeq0.
\tag{11}
\]

Since every `K_{p,q}=-I`, each normalized summand equals `I`, so

\[
\boxed{H_n=\binom{r}{2}I.}
\tag{12}
\]

Its harmonic projection is

\[
P_n:=\mathbf 1_{\{0\}}(H_n)
=
\begin{cases}
I,&r\le1,\\
0,&r\ge2.
\end{cases}
\tag{13}
\]

For `n>1`, `r=1` is exactly the condition `n=p^k`. Now use the intrinsic Clifford radius from `WP-029`,

\[
A(n)=\left(\sum_{p\mid n}(\log p)^2\right)^{1/2}.
\tag{14}
\]

Then `M_n:=A(n)P_n` is positive and

\[
M_n=
\begin{cases}
(\log p)I,&n=p^k,\\
0,&\omega(n)\ge2,
\end{cases}
\]

with `A(1)=0`. Hence

\[
\boxed{M_n=\Lambda(n)I\qquad(n\ge1).}
\tag{15}
\]

Critical attenuation gives `n^{-1/2}M_n=(Lambda(n)/sqrt(n))I`, exactly the finite positive-location Weil coefficient.

This differs from `WP-030` in packaging: there the support zero is a top-volume/rank degeneration of a positive Gram operator; here it is the harmonic subspace of a positive plaquette-defect operator. The arithmetic content is nevertheless the same Boolean statement `omega(n)<=1`.

## 4. Why this does not contradict WP-299

The ordinary scalar source has commuting shifts. `WP-299` proves that a linear intertwiner from that source cannot populate a scalar projective output sector with `K=-I`: a reachable vector would have to be fixed by `-I` and hence vanish.

The present construction changes the upstream object. It retains the odd/oriented Boolean incidence directions themselves, with the same `-1` multiplier. There is no nonzero ordinary-equivariant linear embedding of the scalar source into this fermionic sector that would evade `WP-299`.

So the escape is genuine only because the source has been enriched before positivity is formed. Mathia's local Clifford fiber supplies such enrichment canonically, but it does not turn the original scalar arithmetic sequence into a curved source representation for free.

## 5. Generalized-prime control collapses the arithmetic interpretation

Replace rational primes by arbitrary generators `q_j` of a free commutative monoid and assign arbitrary positive lengths `a_j`. Give every finite occupied set the same Clifford law. Then

\[
H_S=\binom{|S|}{2}I,
\qquad
P_S=I\iff |S|\le1,
\tag{16}
\]

and with `A_S=(sum_{j in S} a_j^2)^(1/2)`,

\[
A_SP_S=
\begin{cases}
a_jI,&S=\{j\},\\0,&|S|\ge2.
\end{cases}
\tag{17}
\]

Choosing `a_j=log|q_j|` gives the same generalized prime-power weight rule. The fermionic curvature therefore survives matched free-monoid controls unchanged. It distinguishes support cardinality, not the arithmetic geometry of the rational primes.

If prime-relabeling symmetry is relaxed, pair-dependent phases `beta(p,q)` can encode arbitrary pair data. Without a separate Mathia theorem forcing those phases, that is programmable input rather than evidence. Equation (7) isolates the canonical scalar boundary before such arithmetic information is inserted.

## 6. An equal-footing infinite-place Clifford direction kills the selector

Suppose the infinite place is added as one additional always-present Clifford direction and given the same fermionic sign against every finite direction. Then for every `n>1`,

\[
S_{\rm tot}(n)=S(n)\cup\{\infty\}
\]

has at least two elements. The same defect gives

\[
H_n^{\rm tot}=\binom{r+1}{2}I>0,
\]

hence

\[
\boxed{\mathbf 1_{\{0\}}(H_n^{\rm tot})=0\qquad(n>1).}
\tag{18}
\]

The exact selector is therefore destroyed even on prime powers. Keeping the infinite place outside the harmonic selector only appends a decoupled contribution and supplies no common positivity theorem. A surviving completion must instead introduce a genuinely different, nonseparable finite--archimedean coupling before the positive projection is taken.

There is also a direct information mismatch: (12) depends only on `omega(n)`, and (14) only on finite generator lengths. Neither produces the Gamma-factor kernel or the polar/global counterterms of the completed explicit formula.

## 7. The global Weil sign is still missing

Equation (15) solves coefficient extraction, not Weil positivity. Once the scalar coefficient `Lambda(n)/sqrt(n)` is extracted, the actual finite-prime Weil contribution uses the translation/autocorrelation lift analyzed in `WP-005`. That operator is unchanged by how the coefficient was obtained and remains indefinite.

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\text{positive local curvature defect}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{positive global Weil quadratic form}.
}
\tag{19}
\]

No zero data or RH assumption enters this conclusion.

## 8. Prior-art and novelty audit

No theorem-level novelty is claimed for the representation-theoretic ingredients. Multipliers on abelian groups and their commutator bicharacters are classical; Clifford/CAR anticommutation and the Koszul sign are classical; positive defect operators and zero spectral projections are elementary operator theory. The literature audit around Kleppner's multiplier theory, twisted group algebras, and group-theoretic CAR confirms that this projective/fermionic structure is established prior art.

The Mathia-specific result is the frontier composition: `WP-299` requires genuinely curved upstream geometry to escape the output-only no-go; full prime-relabeling symmetry then leaves only the flat or fermionic scalar commutator law; the nonflat law is exactly the existing `WP-029` Clifford structure; and its positive harmonic selector is shown to be generalized-prime Boolean support with an exact equal-footing archimedean failure.

So the durable conclusion is not a new projective-representation theorem. It is a narrowing of the research space: **prime-symmetric scalar projective source curvature is not a rich new arithmetic degree of freedom in Mathia.**

## 9. Adversarial falsification gates

This finding must be narrowed or withdrawn if any of the following fails:

1. the commutator ratio of a scalar projective representation of a free abelian source satisfies `beta(q,p)=beta(p,q)^(-1)`;
2. full finite prime-relabeling invariance makes `beta(p,q)` constant on distinct ordered pairs and therefore forces that constant to `+1` or `-1`;
3. the `WP-029` operators satisfy the Clifford relations in (9);
4. their ordinary plaquette holonomy is exactly `-I`;
5. the positive defect (11) equals `binom(omega(n),2)I`;
6. its zero spectral projection is nonzero exactly for support size at most one;
7. multiplication by the Clifford radius gives exactly `Lambda(n)`;
8. the same construction survives arbitrary free-monoid/generalized-prime controls;
9. adjoining one always-present fermionic infinite-place direction makes the harmonic projection vanish for every `n>1`;
10. using the extracted coefficients in the Weil autocorrelation lift leaves the `WP-005` sign obstruction unchanged.

Items 1-9 are algebraic finite-support statements. Item 10 is an internal dependency already established independently. None uses zeta zeros, analytic continuation, an RH-equivalent positivity criterion as input, or a hand-picked prime-pair kernel.

## 10. Consequence for the research frontier

The source-curvature escape left by `WP-299` is now substantially narrower. A scalar projective source law that is canonical under prime relabeling is either flat or the universal fermionic sign. The fermionic branch is intrinsic to Prime Lattice's Boolean/Clifford geometry and really does yield an exact positive finite Mangoldt selector, but only by detecting mixed-prime support. It is generalized-prime generic, graded/Koszul in origin, incompatible with the naive equal-footing infinite-place completion, and does not make the Weil autocorrelation form positive.

A genuinely new global route must therefore use a **richer non-scalar/projective/cohomological source structure or a forced nonseparable finite--archimedean geometry** whose curvature is arithmetic rather than a universal sign and whose final nonnegativity follows from an independent geometric theorem.
