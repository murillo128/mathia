# WP-409 — Scalar Bost–Connes scale twists are invisible to the critical divisibility Gram and flat on every prime axis

## Claim

`WP-408` rules out bare isotropy holonomy in the Bost–Connes commensurability groupoid and leaves a genuine projective/twisted extension as one possible escape. The first scalar version of that escape can now be classified sharply.

Let

\[
G=\mathbb Q_+^\times\cong\bigoplus_p\mathbb Z e_p
\]

be the multiplicative scale group and let

\[
\sigma:G\times G\to\mathbb T
\]

be a normalized, base-independent scalar multiplier. Restrict it to the positive semigroup `N^×` and form the twisted positive-energy shifts on `ell^2(N^×)`

\[
V_n^\sigma\varepsilon_k
=\sigma(n,k)\varepsilon_{nk}.
\tag{1}
\]

Then three facts are exact.

First, the twist changes projective multiplication but **not the range geometry**:

\[
V_m^\sigma V_n^\sigma
=\sigma(m,n)V_{mn}^\sigma,
\qquad
E_n^\sigma:=V_n^\sigma V_n^{\sigma *}
=\mathbf 1_{n\mid k}.
\tag{2}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{E_m^\sigma E_n^\sigma=E_{\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)}^\sigma}
\tag{3}
\]

exactly as in the untwisted Bost–Connes carrier. If the usual scale dynamics is retained,

\[
\alpha_t(V_n^\sigma)=n^{it}V_n^\sigma,
\]

the KMS calculation of `WP-216` is also unchanged:

\[
\phi_\beta(E_n^\sigma)=n^{-\beta}.
\tag{4}
\]

Therefore the normalized conditional Gram is still

\[
\boxed{
G_\beta^\sigma(m,n)
=\left(\frac{\gcd(m,n)}{\sqrt{mn}}\right)^\beta,
}
\tag{5}
\]

and at the actual critical point `beta=1`,

\[
\boxed{G_1^\sigma=G_1.}
\tag{6}
\]

In particular, the one-prime Poisson block remains

\[
G_{1,p}^\sigma(a,b)=p^{-|a-b|/2},
\]

so the local finite-Weil defect found in `WP-216`,

\[
W_p=\log p\,(I-G_{1,p}),
\tag{7}
\]

is not altered by a scalar scale multiplier.

Second, every scalar multiplier is **gauge-trivial on a one-prime axis**. The subgroup generated by one prime is

\[
\langle p\rangle\cong\mathbb Z,
\]

and

\[
H^2(\mathbb Z,\mathbb T)=0.
\tag{8}
\]

Thus after rephasing the projective lifts `V_{p^k}` one has ordinary multiplication on that entire axis. No gauge-invariant scalar projective curvature can live on repeated powers of a single prime.

Third, for the free abelian group `G`, the gauge-invariant part of a scalar multiplier is its alternating commutator bicharacter

\[
\beta_\sigma(g,h)
:=\sigma(g,h)\sigma(h,g)^{-1}.
\tag{9}
\]

Consequently all genuine scalar projective information begins at **mixed-prime pairs** `beta_sigma(p,q)`, `p!=q`. This is exactly the sector in which `WP-300` already shows that full prime-relabeling symmetry collapses the commutator law to the flat sign or the universal fermionic sign.

So a base-independent scalar projective twist does not repair the closest Bost–Connes positivity carrier. It is invisible to the source-forced critical GCD/Poisson Gram, flat on every prime-power axis, and can add only mixed-prime phase data before a different off-diagonal readout is chosen.

**Classification:** `CLASSICAL-MULTIPLIER-THEORY + EXACT-DERIVED-BC-OBSTRUCTION + SCALAR-SCALE-TWIST + RANGE-PROJECTION-INVISIBILITY + CRITICAL-KMS-GRAM-RIGIDITY + PRIME-AXIS-H2-TRIVIALITY + MIXED-PRIME-CURVATURE-ONLY + GENERALIZED-PRIME-GENERIC + PROJECTIVE-ESCAPE-NARROWING + NOT-A-WEIL-POSITIVITY-PROOF`.

## 1. The twisted regular scale action has exactly the same range projections

The multiplier identity is

\[
\sigma(m,n)\sigma(mn,k)
=\sigma(n,k)\sigma(m,nk).
\tag{10}
\]

Applying (1) twice gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
V_m^\sigma V_n^\sigma\varepsilon_k
&=\sigma(n,k)\sigma(m,nk)\varepsilon_{mnk}\\
&=\sigma(m,n)\sigma(mn,k)\varepsilon_{mnk}\\
&=\sigma(m,n)V_{mn}^\sigma\varepsilon_k.
\end{aligned}
\tag{11}
\]

The phase in (1) has modulus one, so the range of `V_n^sigma` is exactly the closed span of the basis vectors labelled by multiples of `n`. Therefore

\[
E_n^\sigma\varepsilon_k
=\begin{cases}
\varepsilon_k,&n\mid k,\\
0,&n\nmid k.
\end{cases}
\tag{12}
\]

There is no `sigma` left in (12). Intersecting two such divisibility ranges proves (3).

This is the precise sense in which the scalar twist is invisible to the positive carrier isolated by `WP-216`: that carrier uses the range projections, their intersections, and their KMS masses, not the projective multiplication phase itself.

The statement applies directly to the canonical twisted left-regular positive-energy carrier and, more generally, to scalar multiplier deformations that preserve the Bost–Connes phase action and divisibility range-projection relations. It does **not** classify arbitrary groupoid twists that alter those relations.

## 2. The KMS normalization erases the scalar twist completely

Retain the ordinary scale dynamics

\[
\alpha_t(V_n^\sigma)=n^{it}V_n^\sigma.
\tag{13}
\]

For any `KMS_beta` state for which these analytic isometries are present,

\[
\begin{aligned}
\phi_\beta(E_n^\sigma)
&=\phi_\beta(V_n^\sigma V_n^{\sigma *})\\
&=\phi_\beta\!\left(
V_n^{\sigma *}\alpha_{i\beta}(V_n^\sigma)
\right)\\
&=n^{-\beta}\phi_\beta(V_n^{\sigma *}V_n^\sigma)\\
&=n^{-\beta}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{14}
\]

Now define

\[
\xi_n^{(\beta,\sigma)}
:=n^{\beta/2}\pi_\beta(E_n^\sigma)\Omega_\beta.
\tag{15}
\]

Using (3) and (14),

\[
\begin{aligned}
\langle\xi_m^{(\beta,\sigma)},\xi_n^{(\beta,\sigma)}\rangle
&=(mn)^{\beta/2}
\phi_\beta(E_{\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)}^\sigma)\\
&=(mn)^{\beta/2}\operatorname{lcm}(m,n)^{-\beta}\\
&=\left(\frac{\gcd(m,n)}{\sqrt{mn}}\right)^\beta.
\end{aligned}
\tag{16}
\]

Thus the exact source-forced half-density found in `WP-216` survives, but the scalar twist contributes nothing to its Gram. At `beta=1`, the same positive Poisson kernel and the same indefinite zero-diagonal defect (7) remain.

This closes a tempting but false shortcut: inserting a scalar magnetic/projective phase into scale multiplication does not rotate `I-G_{1,p}` into a positive operator, because the relevant `G_{1,p}` never sees that phase.

## 3. Every one-prime projective sector is gauge-flat

Fix a prime `p`. Restrict the projective action to powers `p^k`, `k in Z`. Classical multiplier theory gives (8). There is also an elementary representation-level proof.

Let `U_k` denote projective lifts with

\[
U_aU_b=\sigma_p(a,b)U_{a+b}.
\tag{17}
\]

Choose `U_1` once. Repeated multiplication shows that for each integer `k`, `U_1^k` differs from `U_k` by one scalar `c_k in T`. Rephase by

\[
\widetilde U_k:=c_kU_k.
\tag{18}
\]

Then

\[
\widetilde U_a\widetilde U_b
=\widetilde U_{a+b}.
\tag{19}
\]

So any apparent phase on repeated powers of one prime is a choice of section, not curvature. A scalar projective extension cannot create a new gauge-invariant one-prime sign mechanism beyond the untwisted Poisson block.

This is stronger than merely observing that a particular cocycle can be removed on one example: it is a cohomological classification of every scalar multiplier restricted to a rank-one prime subgroup.

## 4. Genuine scalar twist data begin on mixed-prime plaquettes

For an abelian group, the commutator ratio (9) of a multiplier is an alternating bicharacter. For the free abelian scale group it is determined by its values on pairs of prime generators. In particular,

\[
\beta_\sigma(p,p)=1,
\qquad
\beta_\sigma(q,p)=\beta_\sigma(p,q)^{-1}.
\tag{20}
\]

An explicit two-prime control shows the boundary is real rather than a proof that all scalar twists are trivial. On `Z^2`, let

\[
\sigma_\theta((a,b),(c,d))
:=e^{i\theta a d}.
\tag{21}
\]

The exponent is bilinear, so (21) is a normalized multiplier. It is trivial on either coordinate axis, while

\[
\beta_\theta((a,b),(c,d))
=e^{i\theta(ad-cb)}
\tag{22}
\]

can be nontrivial on mixed directions. Yet the twisted shifts still have exactly the same coordinate-range projections as the untwisted shifts.

Therefore the surviving scalar projective route is very specific: one must use off-diagonal mixed-prime observables that actually retain (22). The canonical conditional projection Gram does not.

`WP-300` then supplies the next control. If those pair phases are required to be invariant under arbitrary finite relabeling of prime generators, the scalar commutator law is only flat or fermionic. The fermionic branch is standard Koszul/CAR structure and is generalized-prime generic. If prime symmetry is relaxed, arbitrary pair phases become available, but without a separate source theorem forcing them they are programmable arithmetic input rather than evidence.

## 5. Relation to WP-297 and WP-408

There is no conflict with `WP-297`. That finding treats a **flat site-dependent transport cocycle** over the rooted multiplicative action and proves it is globally gauge-trivial. Here `sigma` is a projective multiplier on the scale group itself and may have nonzero mixed-prime commutator curvature. Equation (22) is an explicit nonflat example, so `WP-297` does not remove it.

There is also no contradiction with `WP-408`. `WP-408` says the bare Bost–Connes commensurability groupoid has no useful critical isotropy character from which to obtain a scalar holonomy. The present result instead grants a new scalar multiplier as an external projective extension and asks what that extension can change. The answer is: not the canonical range-projection/KMS Gram, and not any one-prime projective sector.

Thus the projective escape left by `WP-408` is narrowed rather than closed. A potentially relevant twist must be at least one of the following: a genuinely **base-dependent groupoid twist** whose source/range geometry is not reduced to a multiplier on `Q_+^x`; a noncentral/operator-valued cocycle; a source-forced mixed-prime phase law with arithmetic content beyond generalized-prime controls; or a larger finite–archimedean correspondence in which the scalar phase participates before the positive readout is formed.

## 6. Generalized-prime and archimedean controls

Nothing in Sections 1–4 uses the numerical distribution of the rational primes. Replace the prime generators by arbitrary free generalized-prime generators. The twisted regular shifts retain the same divisibility range projections, every rank-one subgroup is still `Z` and hence projectively flat, and all scalar curvature again lives in pairwise generator phases.

So the obstruction is clone-stable. A base-independent scalar twist is not by itself a Riemann-specific source of positivity.

Nor does such a multiplier contain the archimedean Gamma/polar structure. Adding an independent infinite-place phase after the fact would be an extra input. A successful continuation of this route must exhibit a canonical finite–archimedean coupling and prove that the same global positive form produces the completed Weil functional rather than merely a finite-place projective deformation.

## 7. Prior-art and novelty audit

The representation-theoretic ingredients are classical. Kleppner's multiplier theory for abelian groups covers the commutator bicharacter and equivalence of multiplier representatives; `H^2(Z,T)=0` is standard; twisted left-regular representations and scalar cocycle deformations are standard operator-algebra constructions. Bost–Connes supplies the untwisted arithmetic dynamical system and `WP-216` already derives the critical divisibility Gram from its range projections and KMS state.

No novelty is claimed for those ingredients. The Mathia-specific delta is their composition at the current frontier: `WP-408` leaves projective extension as an escape, while `WP-216` identifies the closest intrinsic positive carrier. Equations (2)–(6) show that the most direct scalar scale twist is **exactly invisible** to that carrier, and (8)–(9) show that it has no gauge-invariant one-prime curvature with which to repair the local Weil defect. That is a new route-closing statement relative to the persisted line.

## 8. Adversarial falsification gates

This finding must be narrowed or withdrawn if any of the following fails:

1. the twisted shifts (1) satisfy the projective multiplication law (11);
2. their ranges are exactly the divisibility subspaces, independently of `sigma`;
3. those ranges obey the lcm intersection law (3);
4. retaining the standard scale dynamics makes the KMS computation (14) independent of `sigma`;
5. (3) and (14) imply the exact unchanged Gram (16);
6. a scalar multiplier restricted to `<p> ~= Z` is cohomologically trivial;
7. the commutator ratio of a multiplier on an abelian group is an alternating bicharacter;
8. the explicit control (21) is a valid nonflat multiplier that is axis-trivial but mixed-prime nontrivial;
9. `WP-300` really applies only after imposing full prime-relabeling invariance, so pair-dependent arithmetic twists are not silently ruled out here.

The theorem does **not** claim that all twists of the Bost–Connes groupoid are scalar scale multipliers, that every twisted BC system has the same full representation theory, or that off-diagonal projective observables are phase-blind. Any of those stronger claims would exceed the derivation.

## 9. Consequence for the research frontier

The simplest projective repair of the Bost–Connes route is now closed. Multiplying the scale composition law by a base-independent `U(1)` 2-cocycle leaves the critical GCD/Poisson Gram untouched and cannot create a one-prime curvature invariant. The only new scalar information lives on mixed-prime plaquettes; under full prime symmetry that information collapses to the already-known universal fermionic branch.

Accordingly, the next useful projective test should not search over arbitrary scalar phases on `Q_+^x`. It should ask whether the arithmetic source itself forces a **base-dependent or noncentral twist, or a finite–archimedean correspondence**, whose mixed-prime curvature survives the generalized-prime control and enters the final positive form before scalarization.
