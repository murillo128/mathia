# WP-330 — Non-deterministic amalgamated noise remains universal when scalarization annihilates the fluctuation ideal

## Claim

`WP-329` showed that an operator-valued freely infinitely divisible lift of the Mangoldt source is nonselective when the lifted variable is deterministic over the amalgamation algebra and all arithmetic specificity is inserted only through the final scalar state. It left genuinely non-deterministic operator-valued geometry open, suggesting that a nonzero completely positive covariance or higher `B`-valued cumulants might carry the missing arithmetic information.

Non-determinism by itself is not enough. At every finite Mangoldt cutoff there is an exact `B`-valued freely infinitely divisible variable with **nonzero centered fluctuation and nonzero completely positive covariance** whose scalarization is still exactly the Mangoldt law, while the same operator-valued law scalarizes to arbitrary positive weights on the same support merely by changing the scalar state. The mechanism is a scalarization-null ideal: all non-deterministic operator-valued data can live in a positive direct summand that the scalar state annihilates.

Thus the escape condition after `WP-329` must be strengthened:

\[
\boxed{
\text{nonzero operator-valued covariance/higher cumulants are not selective unless the scalar readout sees them.}
}
\tag{1}
\]

A viable operator-valued route must force source-dependent mixed data into a sector that survives scalarization — for example by a scalar state faithful on the positive ideal generated by the centered covariance, or by another intrinsic coupling preventing the fluctuation sector from being quotiented away. Faithfulness is proposed here only as an anti-vacuity condition, not as a sufficient route to Weil positivity.

**Classification:** `DERIVED + EXACT-MATCHED-CONTROL + OPERATOR-VALUED-FREE + NONDETERMINISTIC + NONZERO-COVARIANCE + NULL-IDEAL + SCALARIZATION + UNIVERSALITY-OBSTRUCTION + NOT-WEIL-POSITIVITY`.

## Evidence class

The result is an exact finite-dimensional/C*-algebraic matched-control construction. It uses no zeta zeros, RH assumption, analytic continuation, prime-number asymptotic, numerical fit, or arbitrary regularization. The only arithmetic input is the positive probability weighting chosen at scalarization.

The free-probability framework is standard: operator-valued free cumulants and free infinite divisibility are used in their usual amalgamated sense. Popa--Vinnikov supplies the surrounding operator-valued free infinite-divisibility/Lévy--Hinčin context; the direct-sum null-ideal construction below is elementary and is not claimed as a new general theorem of free probability.

## 1. Exact finite-cutoff construction

Use the same finite support as `WP-329`. For `R>\log 2`, let

\[
\Sigma_R=\{\log n:n=p^k,\ \log n\le R\},
\tag{2}
\]

and let `nu_{s,R}` be the pole-normalized Mangoldt source restricted to `Sigma_R` and renormalized to total mass one. Write

\[
B_0=C(\Sigma_R),
\qquad
b_R(x)=x.
\tag{3}
\]

Let `(A_0,\varphi_0)` be any scalar `C^*`-probability space containing a centered semicircular variable `S=S^*` with variance

\[
\varphi_0(S)=0,
\qquad
\varphi_0(S^2)=\sigma^2>0.
\tag{4}
\]

Form the direct sums

\[
B=B_0\oplus\mathbb C,
\qquad
A=B_0\oplus A_0,
\tag{5}
\]

with the natural unital embedding `(f,c)\mapsto(f,c1_{A_0})`, and define

\[
E:A\to B,
\qquad
E(f,a)=(f,\varphi_0(a)).
\tag{6}
\]

`E` is a unital positive `B`-bimodule projection, hence a conditional expectation. Now take

\[
X=b_R\oplus S.
\tag{7}
\]

Its amalgamated mean and centered part are

\[
E(X)=(b_R,0),
\qquad
Y:=X-E(X)=(0,S).
\tag{8}
\]

Unlike the deterministic lift in `WP-329`, `Y\ne0`.

## 2. The operator-valued covariance is genuinely nonzero and positive

For `(f,c)\in B`, the centered second-moment map is

\[
\eta(f,c)
:=E\bigl(Y(f,c)Y\bigr)
=(0,c\sigma^2).
\tag{9}
\]

This is a nonzero completely positive map on the direct-sum `C^*`-algebra `B`: it is zero on the `B_0` summand and multiplication by the positive scalar `sigma^2` on the second summand. In particular,

\[
\eta(0,1)=(0,\sigma^2)>0.
\tag{10}
\]

Because the second component of `Y` is scalar semicircular and the first is zero, its `B`-valued cumulants satisfy

\[
\kappa_1^B(Y)=0,
\qquad
\kappa_2^B\ \text{is encoded by }\eta,
\qquad
\kappa_n^B(Y,\ldots,Y)=0\quad(n\ge3).
\tag{11}
\]

Thus the lift is genuinely non-deterministic in the operator-valued sense. The obstruction below is not the vanishing-higher-cumulant deterministic point law of `WP-329`.

## 3. The lift is freely infinitely divisible over `B`

For every integer `m\ge2`, choose scalar freely independent centered semicirculars `S_1,\ldots,S_m` of variance `\sigma^2/m` in a scalar free product realization and define

\[
X_j=\frac{b_R}{m}\oplus S_j.
\tag{12}
\]

The algebras generated by `B` and the `X_j` are free with amalgamation over `B`. Indeed, after applying `E`, every centered alternating word has zero first component because the `B_0` coordinate is deterministic, while its second component is a centered alternating scalar word in free semicircular families and therefore has scalar expectation zero.

Their sum is

\[
\sum_{j=1}^m X_j
=b_R\oplus\sum_{j=1}^m S_j.
\tag{13}
\]

The scalar sum is again centered semicircular of variance `sigma^2`, so (13) has the same `B`-valued distribution as `X`. Equivalently, its first operator-valued cumulant and covariance are split by `1/m` and the higher cumulants remain zero. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Law}_B(X)
=
\operatorname{Law}_B(X_1)^{\boxplus_B m}
}
\tag{14}
\]

for every `m`, with the evident interpretation that all `m` roots have the same `B`-valued law. Hence `X` is non-deterministic and freely infinitely divisible over `B`.

## 4. Scalarization erases every fluctuation

Define a scalar state on `B` by

\[
\tau_{s,R}(f,c)
=
\int_{\Sigma_R} f(x)\,d\nu_{s,R}(x).
\tag{15}
\]

This is positive and unital, but deliberately nonfaithful: it annihilates the nonzero central ideal

\[
J=\{0\}\oplus\mathbb C.
\tag{16}
\]

Let

\[
\phi_{s,R}=\tau_{s,R}\circ E.
\tag{17}
\]

For every scalar polynomial `P`, direct-sum functional calculus gives

\[
P(X)=P(b_R)\oplus P(S),
\tag{18}
\]

and therefore

\[
\phi_{s,R}(P(X))
=
\tau_{s,R}\bigl(P(b_R),\varphi_0(P(S))\bigr)
=
\int_{\Sigma_R}P(x)\,d\nu_{s,R}(x).
\tag{19}
\]

So the scalar law of `X` is **exactly** the truncated Mangoldt law even though `X` carries a nonzero semicircular fluctuation and nonzero operator-valued covariance.

But the covariance range lies entirely in the null ideal:

\[
\operatorname{Ran}\eta\subset J,
\qquad
\tau_{s,R}(J)=0.
\tag{20}
\]

The non-deterministic geometry is therefore invisible to every scalar moment.

## 5. Matched control: the same non-deterministic law realizes arbitrary source weights

Keep `B`, `A`, `E`, `X`, `S`, `sigma`, and hence the entire `B`-valued law fixed. Let `mu` be **any** probability measure on the same finite set `Sigma_R`, and replace only the scalar state by

\[
\tau_\mu(f,c)=\int_{\Sigma_R}f(x)\,d\mu(x).
\tag{21}
\]

Then the identical calculation yields

\[
(\tau_\mu\circ E)(P(X))
=
\int_{\Sigma_R}P(x)\,d\mu(x).
\tag{22}
\]

Thus the same non-deterministic, nonzero-covariance, `B`-valued freely infinitely divisible object scalarizes to Mangoldt weights, random positive weights, uniform weights, or any other weighting of the same support. Nothing in its positive/divisible structure selects the arithmetic source.

This is a stricter matched-control failure than `WP-329`: adding a bona fide positive covariance does not help if that covariance lives in a sector killed by the readout.

## 6. Consequence for the operator-valued frontier

`WP-329` correctly left open constructions in which arithmetic enters non-deterministic amalgamated coupling data. The present result narrows what that phrase must mean. It is insufficient to verify only

\[
\eta\ne0,
\tag{23}
\]

or even the existence of nonzero higher `B`-valued cumulants. Such data can be appended in a scalarization-null summand without changing one scalar arithmetic moment.

A candidate operator-valued positivity mechanism must therefore pass at least one anti-vacuity condition of the following kind: the scalar state must be faithful on the positive ideal generated by the centered covariance/mixed cumulants, or the construction must intrinsically couple those mixed data to the source coordinate so that quotienting out the fluctuation sector changes the arithmetic scalar law. The second formulation is more invariant: there must be no positive source-independent ideal carrying all the purported non-determinism that the scalar readout can annihilate.

This is a **necessary-control lesson, not a sufficiency theorem**. A faithful scalar state can still produce universal or irrelevant positive structures. No claim is made that faithfulness, nonzero covariance, or nontrivial higher cumulants imply a Weil pairing.

## 7. Prior-art and novelty audit

Operator-valued free probability, amalgamated cumulants, completely positive covariance maps, and operator-valued free infinite divisibility are standard structures. Popa--Vinnikov (2013), already registered in `SOURCES.md`, provides the relevant operator-valued free Lévy--Hinčin framework. The direct-sum construction above uses only elementary `C^*`-algebra and scalar semicircular facts.

The claim of interest is narrower and Mathia-specific: **the obvious strengthening suggested by `WP-329`, from deterministic lifts to merely non-deterministic/nonzero-covariance lifts, still fails the research line's matched-control gate whenever scalarization has a null fluctuation ideal**. A bounded literature audit found no reason to treat that observation as a new free-probability theorem, and it is not presented as one. Its value is to sharpen the falsification criterion for the remaining operator-valued route.

This also distinguishes the result from classical Weil positivity, Hilbert--Pólya, Connes-style trace formulas, Frobenius/cohomological positivity, and intersection-form programs: no global Weil form has been obtained here, and no arithmetic sign theorem is being repackaged. The construction instead explains why one enriched positivity certificate is still too cheap.

## 8. Boundaries and decisive next test

The construction exploits a nonfaithful scalar state and an explicit direct-sum ideal. It does **not** show that every non-deterministic operator-valued freely infinitely divisible lift is universal, nor that a faithful scalarization can be reduced to this example. It also does not address a model where the covariance itself varies canonically with Mangoldt masses and is forced to affect the scalar law.

The next surviving test is therefore precise. Construct, from Mathia-native data rather than a hand-picked kernel, a non-deterministic amalgamated variable whose source-dependent covariance or higher mixed cumulants cannot be removed by a scalarization-null ideal or source-independent quotient. Then hold the operator-valued object fixed while varying matched source weights. If the same positive/divisible certificate survives, the route remains universal; if it necessarily changes and the changed mixed data produce both the finite-prime and archimedean/global Weil terms, there is genuine new structure to audit.

Until such a coupling is exhibited, neither operator-valued free infinite divisibility nor the mere presence of positive covariance supplies the independent global positivity theorem required by the `weil_positivity` mandate.
