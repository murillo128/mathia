# WP-445: The real extension cancels the drift but does not supply its sign

## Result and evidence boundary

**LITERATURE+DERIVED; CLASSICAL MAHARAM RIGIDITY; NEGATIVE/OBSTRUCTION.**
WP-444's valuation energy has dilation drift \(\log n\), with no finite
measurable valuation-diagonal primitive. Its canonical Maharam extension
really does remove that measurability obstruction: adding a real coordinate
\(s\), shifted by \(\log n\), supplies the primitive \(s\).

There is, however, no hidden measurable choice within this extension.
Every finite real measurable multiplication counterterm satisfying the
exact drift equation for all integer dilations is
\[
 b(J,s)=s+C\quad\text{almost everywhere},\qquad C\in\mathbb R.
 \tag{1}
\]
This classification allows arbitrary joint dependence on the valuations
and the real coordinate before imposing the equation. It follows from the
classical type-\(\mathrm{III}_1\) theorem for the critical valuation relation.

Subtracting (1) from the lifted energy gives a dilation-invariant
quadratic expression, but its self-adjoint realization
\[
 B_C=A\otimes I-I\otimes M_s-CI
 \tag{2}
\]
has spectrum \(\mathbb R\), where \(A\) is WP-442's nonnegative generator.
In particular, it is unbounded below. Nor does its positive spectral
subspace preserve the lifted dilations: for each \(n>1\), an explicit
positive-sector vacuum test loses at least \(1/n\) of its squared norm
under projection of its dilation image back onto that sector.
Replacing \(B_C\) by its positive part likewise breaks the form invariance.

This settles the literal Maharam/multiplication repair, including its
most immediate positive spectral truncation. It does not rule out a
new nonlocal energy, another action or comparison space, or a genuinely
interacting finite--archimedean construction. The Maharam real coordinate
is not thereby identified with the Gamma or polar terms of the Weil
formula. No RH implication, historical novelty, formal verification, or
independent adversarial approval is claimed.

## The fixed source and its canonical real extension

Retain the critical product and closed energy of WP-442:
\[
 \Omega=\prod_p\mathbb N_0,\quad
 \nu=\prod_p\nu_p,\quad \nu_p(j)=(1-p^{-1})p^{-j},
\]
\[
 \mathcal E[g]=\sum_p\sum_{j<k}(\log p)\nu_p(k)
       \|g_{p,j}-g_{p,k}\|_{L^2(\nu_{\ne p})}^2,
 \qquad D(\mathcal E)=\{g\in L^2(\nu):\mathcal E[g]<\infty\}.
 \tag{3}
\]
Its generator satisfies \(A1=0\). Write \(a=v(n)\) and \(c=\log n\).
WP-444 proves, on the full form domain,
\[
 (V_ng)(J)=\sqrt n\,1_{J\ge a}g(J-a),\qquad
 \mathcal E[V_ng]=\mathcal E[g]+c\|g\|^2.
 \tag{4}
\]

Put
\[
 X=\Omega\times\mathbb R,\qquad dm(J,s)=d\nu(J)e^sds,
 \qquad \theta_n(J,s)=(J+a,s+c).
 \tag{5}
\]
The map \(\theta_n\) is a measure-preserving bijection from \(X\) onto
\(\{J\ge a\}\times\mathbb R\): the valuation mass factor \(n^{-1}\)
and real mass factor \(e^c=n\) cancel. It is a partial isomorphism,
not an invertible transformation of all of \(X\).
The associated isometry is
\[
 (W_nF)(J,s)=1_{J\ge a}F(J-a,s-c)
          =(V_n\otimes U_c)F(J,s),
 \quad (U_ch)(s)=e^{-c/2}h(s-c).
 \tag{6}
\]
Here \(U_c\) is unitary on \(L^2(\mathbb R,e^sds)\). These formulas
fix the normalization; adding a further \(\sqrt n\) to \(W_n\)
would be incorrect.

The relation generated by the \(\theta_n\) and their partial inverses
is the Maharam extension of the finite-coordinate-change relation on
\(\Omega\). Indeed, changing finitely many valuations by an integer
vector \(d\) changes the base mass by
\(\exp(-\sum_p d_p\log p)\), and (5) shifts \(s\) by the opposite
logarithm. Every such finite change is generated by coordinate
increments and their partial inverses. This identifies the relation
and its cocycle, not merely an analogy with a real auxiliary variable.

The lifted nonnegative closed form is
\[
 \widetilde{\mathcal E}[F]
   =\int_{\mathbb R}\mathcal E[F(\cdot,s)]e^sds.
 \tag{7}
\]
Its domain is the set of \(F\in L^2(m)\) for which the integral is
finite. Equivalently it is the form of \(A\otimes I\). Tonelli and
(4), or the direct-integral form theorem, give
\[
 \widetilde{\mathcal E}[W_nF]
     =\widetilde{\mathcal E}[F]+c\|F\|^2.
 \tag{8}
\]
The identity includes infinite energy, so \(W_n\) preserves this domain.

## Classification of every measurable multiplication compensator

The precise pointwise equation under consideration is
\[
 b(J+a,s+c)-b(J,s)=c
 \quad m\text{-almost everywhere for every }n\ge1.
 \tag{9}
\]
The quantifier over all primes is essential. Subtract \(s\) from
\(b\). The finite measurable function \(d(J,s)=b(J,s)-s\) is invariant
under every \(\theta_n\), hence also its partial inverse and the
countable generated relation. A common conull invariant set can be
chosen because these maps are countable and nonsingular.

In the proof of Theorem 2.1, Neshveyev explicitly identifies the product
of geometric valuation laws and proves that its finite-change relation
has type \(\mathrm{III}_1\) for \(0<\beta\le1\); here \(\beta=1\).
For an ergodic countable relation, type \(\mathrm{III}_1\) is equivalent
to ergodicity of its Maharam extension. The classical equivalence is
recorded in Houdayer--Marrakchi--Verraedt, introduction and Section 2.5.
Thus the relation in (5) is ergodic. Applying ergodicity to all rational
sublevel sets of \(d\) makes \(d\) constant almost everywhere, proving
(1). No moment assumption on \(b\), and no separability assumption
\(b=b_1(J)+b_2(s)\), was used.

To relate (9) to forms, define
\(\mathcal K_b[F]=\int b|F|^2dm\) on its absolute-integrability domain.
Change of variables in (6) gives
\[
 \mathcal K_b[W_nF]-\mathcal K_b[F]
   =\int (b\circ\theta_n-b)|F|^2dm.
 \tag{10}
\]
If \(b\) is integrable on every slab \(\Omega\times[-R,R]\), and
invariance of \(\widetilde{\mathcal E}-\mathcal K_b\) is required on
all bounded cylinder functions times compactly supported bounded real
functions, testing indicators of cylinder rectangles in (10) forces
(9). The relevant differences are locally integrable: the partial
measure-preserving change of variables bounds their integrals by those
on a larger slab. Rectangle indicators determine the signed measure.

Equation (1) classifies (9) even without slab integrability. We do not
infer (9) from an identity tested only on an unspecified proper subspace.
Restricting or changing the comparison class is a different problem.

## Exact cancellation and unavoidable negative directions

For \(b=s+C\), let
\[
 \mathcal D=\left\{F:\widetilde{\mathcal E}[F]
                   +\int |s||F|^2dm<\infty\right\},
 \quad
 q_C[F]=\widetilde{\mathcal E}[F]-\int(s+C)|F|^2dm.
 \tag{11}
\]
This is a dense domain; it contains finite linear combinations of
WP-442's divisibility vectors tensored with compactly supported real
functions. Equations (8)--(10) imply
\[
 W_n\mathcal D\subset\mathcal D,\qquad q_C[W_nF]=q_C[F].
 \tag{12}
\]
We do not call this an ordinary closed semibounded form. The strongly
commuting operators \(A\otimes I\) and \(I\otimes M_s\) define the
self-adjoint operator (2) by joint spectral calculus. Its spectral
quadratic expression agrees with (11) on \(\mathcal D\), since
\(|\eta-s-C|\le\eta+|s|+|C|\) for \(\eta\ge0\).

The reducing subspace \(\mathbb C1\otimes L^2(e^sds)\) carries exactly
\(-M_s-C\). That multiplication operator has spectrum \(\mathbb R\).
Hence so does \(B_C\). More concretely, fix a unit vector \(h\) of
bounded compact support and let \(F_R=1\otimes U_Rh\). Then
\[
 \|F_R\|=1,\qquad
 q_C[F_R]=-R-C-\int s|h(s)|^2e^sds\longrightarrow-\infty.
 \tag{13}
\]
Every vector in this test is in \(\mathcal D\). A scalar shift cannot
repair the lower bound. The same sequence defeats any additional
quadratic term that is invariant under vertical translations, provided
it is finite on this vacuum tensor and all its vertical translates.
No such invariance is assumed for a general interacting repair.

## A positive spectral cutoff does not preserve the source action

Let \(P_C=1_{[0,\infty)}(B_C)\). Fix \(n>1\), \(c=\log n\), and a
nonzero \(h\) supported in a compact subinterval of \((-C-c,-C)\).
The vector \(F=1\otimes h\) lies in the positive spectral subspace,
since \(-s-C>0\) on its support. On the other hand,
\[
 W_nF=\xi_n\otimes U_ch,\qquad
 \xi_n=\sqrt n\,1_{J\ge v(n)},\qquad
 \langle1,\xi_n\rangle=\frac1{\sqrt n}.
 \tag{14}
\]
The shifted real function is supported in \((-C,-C+c)\). Thus the
vacuum component \(n^{-1/2}1\otimes U_ch\) is strictly in the
negative spectral subspace of \(B_C\). Its orthogonal complement in
the radial variable is reducing for \(B_C\), so it cannot cancel
that component. Consequently
\[
 \boxed{\|(I-P_C)W_nF\|^2\ge\frac1n\|F\|^2>0.}
 \tag{15}
\]
In particular \(P_CW_n\) is not an isometry on this positive sector,
and the sector is not \(W_n\)-invariant.

There is an equally direct obstruction to replacing \(B_C\) by its
positive part \((B_C)_+\) on the whole space. The above vectors are
in the required operator domains: \(\xi_n\) has a finite expansion
in WP-443's excitation basis and the real supports are bounded.
The negative-part expectation on \(W_nF\) is at least
\[
 \frac1n\int (t+c+C)|h(t)|^2e^t dt>0.
 \tag{16}
\]
Since (12) holds for \(B_C\), while \(F\) has no negative component,
\(\langle W_nF,(B_C)_+W_nF\rangle>
\langle F,(B_C)_+F\rangle\). Positivity achieved by this spectral
operation therefore changes the exact covariance needed for the
proposed Weil comparison.

## Controls, prior art, and consequence for the line

The real extension is canonical from the Radon--Nikodym cocycle; it
is not selected from zero data. Canonicity alone does not force the
corrected energy's sign. The sign failure in (13) needs only a
zero-energy radial vacuum and the unbounded real coordinate. The
stronger uniqueness statement (1) needs the actual all-prime
critical type-\(\mathrm{III}_1\) input.

At a finite prime set \(F\), put \(S_F(J)=\sum_{p\in F}J_p\log p\).
Then \(s-S_F(J)\) is invariant under the lifted shifts from \(F\),
and \(b=s+f(s-S_F)\), for arbitrary finite measurable \(f\), solves
those shift equations. Thus finite-place tests cannot establish (1).
This is a matched control for the role of the all-prime ergodicity
hypothesis; no positive finite-cutoff Weil realization is inferred.

Maharam extension, its ergodicity criterion, and the critical valuation
type are classical. No new theorem about type-III relations is claimed.
The line-local result identifies the unique multiplication cancellation
for the actual WP-442 energy on that canonical extension and checks both
its full-domain sign and the failure of the immediate spectral repair.
WP-444 alone excludes a primitive on \(\Omega\); here a primitive
exists on \(X\), but is forced into an indefinite energy.

A surviving completion must change more than the probability space by
this real cocycle extension followed by multiplication subtraction or
positive spectral truncation. A nonlocal correction, a different
source-selected test subspace, or a genuinely interacting real/finite
form remains outside this result. Each still needs its own domain,
source-action comparison, Gamma/polar normalization and sign theorem.
The accepted Bost--Connes completion clue remains unresolved.

## Validation

An exact rational normalization audit used geometric parameters
\(1/2,1/3,1/5,2/5,3/4\), local scale one, and the partition
\(\{0\},\ldots,\{8\},\{9,10,\ldots\}\) with exact tail mass
\(r^9\). Shift lengths one through six and twenty rational real-coordinate
means in \((-a,0)\) per case gave 600 exact checks of the energy
cancellation and strictly negative vacuum contribution. The normalized
vacuum leakage was exactly \(r^a\). All 45 nonconstant local wavelets
also satisfied their eigenvalue equation at the quadratic-form level;
their eigenvalues exceed one, so at shift one the negative spectral
mass is exactly \(r\), not only bounded below by it.

The calculation uses infinite geometric tail masses, not a zero boundary
truncation. It checks local normalization and the spectral witness;
measurable uniqueness relies on the cited ergodicity theorem, and the
infinite-dimensional/domain conclusions rely on the proofs above.
