# WP-224 — Energy-nondemolition positive channels cannot orient the hollow Bost–Connes Weil defect

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + BOST-CONNES-CRITICAL-GRAM + STATE-ENERGY-MASA + UNITAL-COMPLETELY-POSITIVE + MULTIPLICATIVE-DOMAIN + SCHUR-CHANNEL + HOLLOW-DEFECT + DEPHASING-NO-GO + MODULAR-TIME-AVERAGE-NO-GO + DECISIVE-NARROWING + PRIOR-ART-CLASSICAL-TOOLS + NOT-A-WEIL-BRIDGE`.

`WP-222` shows that non-diagonal coinvariant quotients can change the sign of the critical one-prime Bost--Connes Weil defect, but only with a quotient-selection law that must itself come from the source. `WP-223` then closes the most immediate source selector: projections and positive congruences from the state Hamiltonian's diagonal spectral algebra either annihilate the finite defect or leave it indefinite.

There is a broader canonical operation not covered by that result. Instead of selecting vectors with the Hamiltonian, one can let the source dynamics act on **operators** through a positive channel: modular/time averaging, dephasing, phase damping, or any other unital completely positive map that leaves every state-energy spectral projection unchanged.

For the source-forced finite Bost--Connes defect this whole class has an exact obstruction. Let

\[
\mathcal D_F=W^*(H_F)
\]

be the diagonal masa generated by the finite-prime state Hamiltonian, and let

\[
\Phi:B(\mathcal H_F)\to B(\mathcal H_F)
\]

be unital and completely positive with

\[
\Phi(d)=d
\qquad(d\in\mathcal D_F).
\]

Then `D_F` lies in the multiplicative domain of `Phi`, so `Phi` is a diagonal-bimodule Schur channel. In the exponent basis,

\[
\Phi(E_{\alpha\beta})=c_{\alpha\beta}E_{\alpha\beta},
\]

with `C=(c_{alpha beta})` a correlation matrix. Since the critical Weil defect `W_F` from `WP-216`/`WP-223` has identically zero diagonal, `Phi(W_F)` also has identically zero diagonal. Positivity then forces the entire operator to vanish:

\[
\boxed{
\Phi(W_F)\succeq0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Phi(W_F)=0.
}
\]

Equivalently, every nonzero energy-nondemolition UCP image of the finite Weil defect is indefinite. This includes arbitrary source-time random-unitary averages and the canonical Gaussian dephasing semigroup. Thus scalar source dynamics can suppress or erase finite Weil coherences, but it cannot rotate their orientation into a nonzero positive form.

The result is distinct from `WP-214`. That finding concerns whether continuous scalar calculus of the Liouville generator can **create prime-power support** from a source occupying the full dense rational grading. Here the input is already the exact source-forced finite Weil defect, so support selection is no longer the issue. Even granting arbitrary energy-preserving completely positive Schur damping, including discontinuous finite-dimensional multipliers, the missing sign cannot be obtained.

This does **not** rule out a channel that actively changes state-energy populations, a source-forced coinvariant quotient, a nonlinear square/Gram construction, a nonunital shorting, an operator-valued mixed-prime kernel, or a finite--archimedean interaction formed before reduction. Those categories can create diagonal mass or change operator category and therefore lie outside the theorem. The surviving route must now use such active structure rather than a nondemolition averaging of the same finite source defect.

## 1. Exact finite-prime source object

Fix a nonempty finite prime set `F`. In the exponent basis

\[
e_\alpha,
\qquad
\alpha\in\mathbb N_0^F,
\]

the state Hamiltonian is

\[
H_F e_\alpha
=
E_\alpha e_\alpha,
\qquad
E_\alpha=\sum_{p\in F}\alpha_p\log p.
\tag{1}
\]

Unique factorization makes the energies `E_alpha` distinct, so

\[
\mathcal D_F=W^*(H_F)
\tag{2}
\]

is exactly the bounded diagonal algebra in this basis.

The source-forced critical conditional Gram from `WP-216` gives the finite-place Weil defect

\[
W_F
=
\sum_{p\in F}(\log p)(I-G_p),
\tag{3}
\]

where

\[
G_p(a,b)=p^{-|a-b|/2}
\tag{4}
\]

on the `p` coordinate. As recorded in `WP-223`,

\[
\langle e_\alpha,W_Fe_\alpha\rangle=0
\qquad\text{for every }\alpha,
\tag{5}
\]

and, for `alpha != beta`,

\[
\langle e_\alpha,W_Fe_\beta\rangle
=
-(\log p)p^{-|\alpha_p-\beta_p|/2}
\tag{6}
\]

when `alpha` and `beta` differ only in coordinate `p`, while the matrix element is zero if they differ in at least two coordinates.

Thus `W_F` is a self-adjoint hollow weighted graph operator on the exponent lattice.

## 2. Every UCP map fixing the state-energy algebra is a Schur channel

Let `Phi` be a unital completely positive map satisfying

\[
\Phi(d)=d
\qquad(d\in\mathcal D_F).
\tag{7}
\]

Write

\[
P_\alpha=|e_\alpha\rangle\langle e_\alpha|.
\]

Since `P_alpha` is a projection fixed by `Phi`,

\[
\Phi(P_\alpha^*P_\alpha)
=
P_\alpha
=
\Phi(P_\alpha)^*\Phi(P_\alpha),
\tag{8}
\]

and similarly on the other side. The equality case of the Schwarz inequality for UCP maps therefore puts every `P_alpha` in the multiplicative domain of `Phi`. Hence

\[
\Phi(P_\alpha X P_\beta)
=
P_\alpha\Phi(X)P_\beta
\tag{9}
\]

for every bounded `X`.

Apply (9) to the matrix unit

\[
E_{\alpha\beta}
=|e_\alpha\rangle\langle e_\beta|.
\]

The corner

\[
P_\alpha B(\mathcal H_F)P_\beta
\]

is one-dimensional, so there is a scalar `c_{alpha beta}` with

\[
\boxed{
\Phi(E_{\alpha\beta})
=c_{\alpha\beta}E_{\alpha\beta}.
}
\tag{10}
\]

Unitality gives

\[
c_{\alpha\alpha}=1,
\tag{11}
\]

and complete positivity is exactly the usual positivity condition on finite principal submatrices of

\[
C=(c_{\alpha\beta}).
\tag{12}
\]

Thus `Phi` is a correlation-matrix Schur multiplier. No assumption of scalar Liouville functional calculus has been made; the class includes arbitrary energy-nondemolition UCP phase channels.

The multiplicative-domain and Schur-multiplier statements are classical operator algebra. They are proved here only to make the source restriction explicit and to avoid attributing novelty to standard completely positive-map theory.

## 3. Hollowness makes nonzero positivity impossible

From (5) and (10),

\[
\langle e_\alpha,\Phi(W_F)e_\alpha\rangle
=c_{\alpha\alpha}
\langle e_\alpha,W_Fe_\alpha\rangle
=0
\tag{13}
\]

for every `alpha`. Since a positive linear map is star-preserving on self-adjoint inputs,

\[
\Phi(W_F)=\Phi(W_F)^*.
\tag{14}
\]

Suppose one off-diagonal entry survives:

\[
\langle e_\alpha,\Phi(W_F)e_\beta\rangle=z\ne0.
\]

The compression to

\[
\operatorname{span}\{e_\alpha,e_\beta\}
\]

is

\[
\begin{pmatrix}
0&z\\
\overline z&0
\end{pmatrix},
\tag{15}
\]

whose eigenvalues are `+|z|` and `-|z|`. Therefore

\[
\Phi(W_F)\ne0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Phi(W_F)\text{ is indefinite}.
\tag{16}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\boxed{
\Phi(W_F)\succeq0
\iff
\Phi(W_F)=0.
}
\tag{17}
\]

This is stronger than the diagonal-congruence statement of `WP-223` in a different direction. There the source operation acts on vectors through the Hamiltonian spectral algebra. Here the operation may act nontrivially on every off-diagonal coherence, but as long as it is an energy-nondemolition positive channel it cannot create the diagonal mass required for a nonzero positive operator.

## 4. Canonical source-time averaging is inside the no-go class

The Bost--Connes state dynamics is implemented in the standard positive-energy representation by

\[
U_t=e^{itH_F}.
\tag{18}
\]

Let `mu` be any probability measure on `R` and define the random-unitary channel

\[
\Phi_\mu(X)
=
\int_{\mathbb R}U_t X U_t^*\,d\mu(t).
\tag{19}
\]

Every element of `D_F` commutes with `U_t`, so `Phi_mu` fixes `D_F` pointwise. On matrix units,

\[
\Phi_\mu(E_{\alpha\beta})
=
\widehat\mu(E_\alpha-E_\beta)
E_{\alpha\beta}.
\tag{20}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\Phi_\mu(W_F)\ne0
\Longrightarrow
\Phi_\mu(W_F)\text{ is indefinite}.
}
\tag{21}
\]

No choice of a positive time-averaging measure can turn the finite defect into a nonzero positive operator. The average may only damp some coherences or erase all of them.

A particularly canonical example is Gaussian dephasing,

\[
\Phi_s
=
e^{-s\,\operatorname{ad}_{H_F}^2},
\qquad s>0,
\tag{22}
\]

for which

\[
\Phi_s(E_{\alpha\beta})
=
e^{-s(E_\alpha-E_\beta)^2}E_{\alpha\beta}.
\tag{23}
\]

Every multiplier in (23) is strictly positive at finite `s`, so every nonzero edge of `W_F` survives. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\Phi_s(W_F)\text{ is indefinite for every finite }s>0.
}
\tag{24}
\]

Only the singular infinite-dephasing limit collapses the off-diagonal defect to zero.

This also covers any scalar Liouville multiplier that defines a unital completely positive Schur channel. The theorem is not restricted to continuous multipliers: complete positivity plus energy nondemolition is enough.

## 5. Why this is not the support theorem of `WP-214`

`WP-214` starts from the full rational Liouville grading and asks whether a continuous scalar function of the Liouville generator can annihilate every non-prime-power grade while retaining the prime-power grades. Density of the unwanted grades proves that it cannot. Arbitrary Borel spectral calculus can perform the deletion, but only by target coding.

The present input is later and more structured. `WP-216` has already produced the exact critical Poisson/GCD Gram, and the finite Weil defect already has its source-forced prime-axis coefficients. We grant the source a much broader class than regular scalar grade selection: every UCP map fixing the full state-energy diagonal algebra.

Even a discontinuous Schur channel may delete any collection of coherences compatible with complete positivity. Equation (17) says that this freedom still cannot solve the **orientation** problem. If any finite Weil interaction survives, its hollow two-point compression retains both signs.

Thus two logically separate routes are closed:

\[
\text{regular Liouville selector}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{prime-power support}
\qquad\text{(`WP-214`),}
\tag{25}
\]

whereas

\[
\text{energy-nondemolition UCP channel on }W_F
\not\Rightarrow
\text{nonzero positivity}
\qquad\text{(this finding).}
\tag{26}
\]

## 6. Aggressive falsification and exact boundary

The theorem is intentionally conditional on **fixing the state-energy diagonal algebra pointwise**. Several escapes are real and therefore must not be folded into the claim.

A channel that exchanges energy populations can create nonzero diagonal entries and is outside (7). Merely calling such a map KMS-covariant or global is not enough; a surviving proposal must derive its population transfer from the source and audit the resulting diagonal cost and archimedean terms.

A compression to a different Hilbert space need not be a unital endomorphism fixing `D_F`. `WP-222` already shows that coinvariant compression can change sign, which is precisely why source selection of the quotient remains load-bearing.

A nonlinear construction such as `X -> X^*X`, an absolute value, or a Gram square can create positivity, but it changes the target operator and generally loses the signed Weil orientation. Such a construction must independently recover the completed explicit-formula coefficients rather than infer them from positivity of the square.

A positive diagonal counterterm added after `Phi(W_F)` is also outside the theorem. `WP-218` already shows that compact repair cannot remove the negative essential interval of the one-prime defect, while the obvious noncompact scalar compensation carries the divergent all-prime cost. Any new counterterm must therefore be source-derived and globally audited rather than introduced to satisfy (17).

Finally, a finite--archimedean or mixed-prime interaction formed **before** the reduction may change the finite principal block itself. The theorem does not exclude that category. It says that once the source object entering the channel is the hollow finite defect and the channel is nondemolition with respect to the same state-energy decomposition, the sign cannot emerge.

## 7. Matched controls

Nothing in the proof depends on primality or on the numerical values `log p` and `p^{-1/2}`. Let `A` be any self-adjoint hollow matrix or bounded hollow operator in a fixed orthonormal basis, and let `D` be the diagonal masa in that basis. For every UCP map `Phi` fixing `D` pointwise,

\[
\Phi(A)\succeq0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Phi(A)=0.
\tag{27}
\]

Thus the theorem survives arbitrary-generator, generalized-product, and randomized-weight controls. That universality is appropriate for a negative classification theorem: the obstruction belongs to the operator category, not to a hidden RH-equivalent arithmetic identity.

The Mathia-specific content is the placement of the source-forced critical Bost--Connes Weil defect inside this category and the consequent removal of a natural source-dynamics escape left open after `WP-222`--`WP-223`.

The durable Mathia delta is the exact consequence for the current frontier: **after the critical Bost--Connes Gram has source-forced the correct half-density and `WP-223` has excluded state-energy spectral selection, passing to the entire energy-nondemolition UCP channel class still cannot orient the hollow finite Weil defect into nonzero positivity**.
