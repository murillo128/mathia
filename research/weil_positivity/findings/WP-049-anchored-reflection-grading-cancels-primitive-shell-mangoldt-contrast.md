# WP-049 — anchored reflection grading cancels the primitive-shell Mangoldt contrast beyond order two

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + CLASSICAL-CYCLOTOMIC-SYMMETRY + DECISIVE-NEGATIVE` for the most direct attempt to turn the canonical reflection discovered in `WP-048` into the missing Hodge/Lefschetz-style sign mechanism. The same anchored reflection that intrinsically selects the `q=2` Riemann Gamma channel acts on every exact-order Prime-Circle shell by inversion `gamma -> -gamma`. For every order `n>2`, primitive characters occur in reflection pairs with no fixed point. Consequently every scalar spectral multiplier of the compatible cycle Laplacian has identical even and odd spectra on that shell: its reflection-graded trace is zero and its graded determinant ratio is one. In particular, the ordinary shell determinant from `WP-043` still contains `Lambda(n)`, but reflection splits that arithmetic determinant **equally** between the two parities instead of converting it into a signed index. The actual normalized Ramanujan features carrying the Prime-Circle birth form are themselves reflection-even, so the odd compression annihilates them while the even compression adds no sign mechanism.

Thus `WP-048` genuinely solves the narrow **channel-selection** problem for the real archimedean factor, but the selecting involution cannot simultaneously serve as the finite-prime grading that would explain Weil positivity. This does not rule out a larger noncommutative or cohomological construction in which reflection is only one symmetry and the relevant finite--archimedean coupling is formed before taking parity or scalar spectral calculus.

## 1. The reflection selected in WP-048 acts canonically on primitive shells

Let

\[
K=\widehat{\mathbb Z},
\qquad
\widehat K=\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z,
\]

as in `WP-037`--`WP-043`. For `n>=1`, let

\[
\Omega_n
:=\{\gamma\in\mathbb Q/\mathbb Z:\operatorname{ord}(\gamma)=n\}
\]

and let

\[
H_n
:=\operatorname{span}\{\chi_\gamma:\gamma\in\Omega_n\}
\]

be the exact-order primitive shell. Then `dim H_n=phi(n)`.

The anchored reflection of `WP-048` is complex conjugation on the boundary circle. On the profinite character model its corresponding unitary involution is

\[
\boxed{
(Rf)(x)=f(-x),
\qquad
R\chi_\gamma=\chi_{-\gamma},
\qquad
R^2=I.
}
\tag{1}
\]

Every exact-order set `Omega_n` is invariant under `gamma -> -gamma`, so every `H_n` is an `R`-invariant finite-dimensional space. Write

\[
H_n=H_n^+\oplus H_n^-,
\qquad
H_n^\pm=\ker(R\mp I).
\tag{2}
\]

The question is whether this very involution can do more than select the archimedean two-channel: can its parity decomposition convert the positive cycle geometry of `WP-043` into a signed finite-prime index or superdeterminant carrying the Mangoldt tower?

## 2. Primitive reflection has fixed points only in orders one and two

A primitive character is fixed by reflection exactly when

\[
\gamma=-\gamma\pmod 1,
\]

or equivalently

\[
2\gamma=0.
\tag{3}
\]

The only elements of `Q/Z` satisfying (3) are

\[
\gamma=0,
\qquad
\gamma=\frac12,
\]

of exact orders `1` and `2`, respectively. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Fix}(R|_{\Omega_n})
=\varnothing
\qquad(n>2).
}
\tag{4}
\]

For every `n>2`, inversion partitions `Omega_n` into disjoint two-element orbits

\[
\{\gamma,-\gamma\}.
\tag{5}
\]

On each pair define

\[
e_\gamma^+
=\frac{\chi_\gamma+\chi_{-\gamma}}{\sqrt2},
\qquad
 e_\gamma^-
=\frac{\chi_\gamma-\chi_{-\gamma}}{\sqrt2}.
\tag{6}
\]

Then `e_gamma^+` is reflection-even and `e_gamma^-` is reflection-odd. Hence, without invoking Euler's totient parity as a separate theorem,

\[
\boxed{
\dim H_n^+=\dim H_n^-=\frac{\varphi(n)}2,
\qquad n>2.
}
\tag{7}
\]

The exceptional shell `n=2` is exactly the one singled out in `WP-048`: `H_2` is one-dimensional and entirely even, generated by the fixed character `chi_{1/2}`.

This is already a warning for a Lefschetz-style interpretation. The involution has precisely the fixed shell required for the real Gamma selector, but no primitive fixed points at any higher order.

## 3. Scalar cycle spectral calculus is exactly parity-isospectral for every n>2

The compatible positive cycle Laplacian of `WP-043` is

\[
L_{\rm cyc}=(U-I)^*(U-I)
\tag{8}
\]

with

\[
L_{\rm cyc}\chi_\gamma
=\lambda(\gamma)\chi_\gamma,
\qquad
\lambda(\gamma)=4\sin^2(\pi\gamma).
\tag{9}
\]

Because

\[
\lambda(-\gamma)=\lambda(\gamma),
\tag{10}
\]

`L_cyc` commutes with `R`. More strongly, on each two-dimensional orbit span from (5),

\[
L_{\rm cyc}
=\lambda(\gamma)I_2.
\tag{11}
\]

Therefore every scalar spectral multiplier `f(L_cyc)` for which the finite-shell restriction is defined acts by the **same** scalar on the even and odd vectors in (6):

\[
f(L_{\rm cyc})e_\gamma^\pm
=f(\lambda(\gamma))e_\gamma^\pm.
\tag{12}
\]

It follows immediately that for every `n>2`,

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Tr}\!\left(
R\,f(L_{\rm cyc})\big|_{H_n}
\right)=0.
}
\tag{13}
\]

Equivalently, if `R` is used as the `Z_2` grading,

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{Tr}\!\left(f(L_{\rm cyc})|_{H_n^+}\right)
=
\operatorname{Tr}\!\left(f(L_{\rm cyc})|_{H_n^-}\right),
\qquad n>2.
}
\tag{14}
\]

This is an elementary finite-dimensional fixed-point cancellation, not an appeal to RH or analytic continuation. It is the exact shell analogue of the classical Lefschetz principle that a graded trace of a symmetry localizes on its fixed data. Here the permutation action is explicit enough that no index theorem is needed.

The scope of (13)--(14) is important. They hold for **scalar spectral calculus of the compatible cycle operator**, which is precisely the positive spectral route available in `WP-043`. They do not assert that every conceivable reflection-equivariant operator has parity-isospectral restrictions; a larger operator carrying genuinely new non-scalar data could distinguish the two sectors.

## 4. The Mangoldt shell determinant is duplicated across parity, not extracted by it

`WP-043` proves, for `n>1`,

\[
\boxed{
\frac12\log\det\!\left(L_{\rm cyc}|_{H_n}\right)
=\log\Phi_n(1)
=\Lambda(n).
}
\tag{15}
\]

The reflection splitting lets us sharpen the structure of this identity. For `n>2`, choose one representative from each pair `\{\gamma,-\gamma\}`. Equations (6), (9), and (11) give

\[
\det\!\left(L_{\rm cyc}|_{H_n^+}\right)
=
\det\!\left(L_{\rm cyc}|_{H_n^-}\right)
=
\prod_{\{\gamma,-\gamma\}}
|1-e^{2\pi i\gamma}|^2.
\tag{16}
\]

But pairing conjugate primitive roots in the cyclotomic product gives

\[
\prod_{\{\gamma,-\gamma\}}
|1-e^{2\pi i\gamma}|^2
=
\prod_{\zeta\ \mathrm{primitive}\ n}(1-\zeta)
=\Phi_n(1).
\tag{17}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\det\!\left(L_{\rm cyc}|_{H_n^+}\right)
=
\det\!\left(L_{\rm cyc}|_{H_n^-}\right)
=\Phi_n(1),
\qquad n>2.
}
\tag{18}
\]

For a prime power `n=p^k>2`, each parity sector therefore satisfies

\[
\log\det\!\left(L_{\rm cyc}|_{H_n^\pm}\right)
=\log p
=\Lambda(n).
\tag{19}
\]

For an integer with at least two distinct prime divisors, both determinants are `1`.

This is more informative than merely saying that a reflection supertrace vanishes. **The arithmetic survives perfectly in either parity sector taken alone, but it survives identically in both.** Reflection has duplicated the cyclotomic norm rather than separated an arithmetic positive part from a compensating negative part.

Consequently the graded determinant ratio is trivial:

\[
\boxed{
\frac{
\det(L_{\rm cyc}|_{H_n^+})
}{
\det(L_{\rm cyc}|_{H_n^-})
}=1,
\qquad n>2,
}
\tag{20}
\]

and therefore

\[
\boxed{
\log\det(L_{\rm cyc}|_{H_n^+})
-
\log\det(L_{\rm cyc}|_{H_n^-})
=0.
}
\tag{21}
\]

So the natural reflection-superdeterminant kills exactly the higher-order Mangoldt information that the ordinary positive shell determinant in `WP-043` had recovered.

The order-two shell is exceptional rather than the first member of a uniform signed tower. Its sole character is reflection fixed, so there is no odd partner. That exception is precisely why reflection can select the `q=2` archimedean channel in `WP-048`; it does not extend to a finite-prime parity mechanism for `p^k>2`.

## 5. The Weil-relevant Ramanujan birth features are all reflection-even

The obstruction is not confined to determinants. The normalized primitive-shell feature used in `WP-036`--`WP-043` is

\[
u_n(x)
=\frac{c_n(x)}{\sqrt{\varphi(n)}}
=\frac1{\sqrt{\varphi(n)}}
\sum_{\operatorname{ord}(\gamma)=n}\chi_\gamma(x).
\tag{22}
\]

Applying (1) merely permutes the summands `gamma <-> -gamma`, so

\[
\boxed{Ru_n=u_n.}
\tag{23}
\]

Thus every primitive birth feature lies entirely in the reflection-even subspace:

\[
P_-u_n=0,
\qquad
P_+=\frac{I+R}{2},
\quad
P_-=\frac{I-R}{2}.
\tag{24}
\]

The same is true of the pointwise products that carry the actual finite birth matrix,

\[
R(u_mu_n)=u_mu_n.
\tag{25}
\]

Recall from `WP-043` that

\[
C_{mn}=\eta_R(u_mu_n)
\tag{26}
\]

and that its interior prime-power rays contain

\[
C_{dp^k,d}
=-\frac{\log p}{p^{k/2}}.
\tag{27}
\]

Therefore a direct odd reflection compression annihilates the existing finite arithmetic carrier, while the even compression leaves it unchanged and supplies no parity contrast. The canonical reflection does not repair the category mismatch already isolated in `WP-043`: scalar spectral positivity lives on Fourier/convolution structure, whereas the Weil-signed birth operator uses pointwise products of even Ramanujan features.

This statement is deliberately narrower than claiming that reflection can never participate in a successful construction. An odd differential or non-scalar coupling could map even birth data into a larger odd sector before the final pairing. Such an operation would be genuinely new structure not present in the current reflection grading itself.

## 6. Why the tempting q=2-to-Hodge route fails

After `WP-048`, the most economical proposed chain was

```text
anchored reflection
    -> canonically selects the order-two / real Gamma channel
    -> use the same involution as a Z2 grading
    -> combine with positive cycle geometry
    -> obtain a Lefschetz/Hodge-style signed finite-prime index
    -> assemble global Weil positivity.
```

Sections 2--5 break the chain at the grading step.

For `n>2`, the reflection has no primitive fixed character, every scalar cycle spectral value occurs once in each parity, and the shell determinant carrying `Lambda(n)` is copied identically into both sectors. The corresponding graded trace and graded log-determinant are therefore zero. At the level of the actual birth features, all `u_n` are even, so the odd sector contains none of the existing finite-prime carrier.

The reflection consequently has two sharply different roles:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{order }2:&\text{a genuine fixed mode, hence an intrinsic selector;}\\[1mm]
\text{orders }>2:&\text{free inversion pairs, hence no scalar spectral parity contrast.}
\end{array}
}
\tag{28}
\]

That dichotomy explains why the same symmetry can remove the arbitrariness of the Riemann Gamma scale without also producing a Mangoldt sign theorem.

## 7. Relation to WP-020 and the classical fixed-point picture

This result is adjacent to but not a restatement of `WP-020`.

`WP-020` gives a general supersymmetric/equivariant cancellation theorem: if an even insertion commutes with an odd self-adjoint supercharge `Q`, positive `Q^2` eigenvalues cancel in the graded trace and only `ker Q` survives. That theorem does not identify what the canonical grading or fixed sector should be in Prime Circle.

Here no supercharge is assumed. The involution is the **specific anchored reflection forced by the original Prime-Circle geometry and used by `WP-048` to select `q=2`**. The new calculation determines its exact action on every primitive conductor shell and shows that the cycle spectrum is parity-isospectral beyond order two. It therefore closes a concrete escape created only after `WP-048`: reusing the newly canonical real-structure selector itself as the missing finite-prime grading.

The ambient mechanism is classical. Complex conjugation pairs non-real primitive roots of unity, and the general Lefschetz/Atiyah--Bott philosophy relates equivariant graded traces to fixed-point data. No historical novelty is claimed for either fact. The Mathia-specific content is the exact collision between three already-established intrinsic structures:

1. the anchored reflection selecting `q=2` from `WP-048`;
2. the positive cycle-Laplacian shell determinant recovering `Lambda(n)` from `WP-043`;
3. the reflection-even Ramanujan pointwise features carrying the finite birth form from `WP-036`--`WP-043`.

A targeted prior-art audit of equivariant Lefschetz fixed-point formulas, cyclotomic complex conjugation, and real cyclotomic norm decompositions found these ingredients as standard structure, not a pre-existing theorem asserting a new Weil-positive mechanism from this Prime-Circle combination. Accordingly the result is classified as a project-specific exact obstruction, not a novelty claim about fixed-point theory or cyclotomic fields.

## 8. Matched controls and boundary of the no-go

The strongest matched control is again unfavorable to overinterpretation. Any ordinary cyclic root tower on an anchored Euclidean circle has the same conjugation involution and the same pairing

\[
\zeta\leftrightarrow\bar\zeta.
\]

Thus equations (4), (7), and the parity duplication of conjugate-pair norms are generic real-structure facts. They do not distinguish rational primes from generalized or non-arithmetic cyclic shell data.

Several possible escapes remain outside this finding.

- A **non-scalar** operator on each `\{gamma,-gamma\}` pair can distinguish even and odd sectors; scalar functional calculus of `L_cyc` cannot.
- A coupling that **does not commute with reflection** may create a nontrivial parity transfer, but its sign would require a new theorem and could not be attributed to the bare reflection symmetry.
- A larger cohomological object may have fixed correspondences or intersection data that are not the primitive characters themselves. This is the type of additional global structure already indicated by the function-field and Connes-style prior art.
- A construction can use only the even parity determinant, which indeed retains `Lambda(n)` by (18). But that is simply another expression of the ordinary positive shell determinant from `WP-043`; it supplies no graded sign and remains a nonlinear shell readout without the critical `n^{-1/2}` weighting or the Weil quadratic pairing.
- Reflection may remain useful as the archimedean real-structure selector from `WP-048`. The claim here is only that **reusing it directly as the finite shell grading does not solve the sign problem**.

## 9. Exact falsification tests

The obstruction has finite, explicit tests.

1. For each `n>2`, enumerate primitive residues `a mod n` and verify that `a -> -a` has no fixed point among units modulo `n`.
2. On each pair `a,-a`, verify that the cycle eigenvalue `4 sin^2(pi a/n)` is identical and that the even/odd combinations in (6) are eigenvectors with the same eigenvalue.
3. Verify (13) for arbitrary scalar values assigned to those cycle eigenvalues; no analytic property of `f` is needed on the finite shell.
4. Compute the even and odd determinants and verify (18) directly. For `n=p^k>2`, each equals `p`; for `n` with at least two distinct prime divisors, each equals `1`.
5. Check the exceptional shell `n=2`: `chi_{1/2}` is reflection fixed and has no odd partner, matching the selector mechanism of `WP-048`.
6. Verify `u_n(-x)=u_n(x)` directly from the Ramanujan-sum definition and hence `P_-u_n=0`.

Failure of any of items 1--6 would invalidate the stated no-go. Passing them does not prove that every possible reflection-bearing cohomology fails; it proves only that the **canonical primitive-shell reflection grading and scalar positive cycle spectral calculus currently present in Prime Circle cannot provide the missing finite Mangoldt parity/sign mechanism**.

## 10. Consequence for the Weil-positivity frontier

`WP-048` remains a positive structural advance: the Prime-Circle geometry itself explains why the Riemann archimedean Mellin readout uses the two-level channel rather than an arbitrarily target-fitted `q`.

The present result shows that this success should not be overextended. The real-structure involution selects the archimedean channel **because order two is its only nontrivial primitive fixed shell**. The same fixed-point fact makes it useless as a uniform graded carrier for the higher prime-power tower.

The surviving design requirement is therefore sharper:

\[
\boxed{
\text{a successful global pairing needs new finite--archimedean coupling data beyond the anchored reflection itself.}
}
\]

Such data must act before the existing scalar spectral/parity split, preserve or intrinsically generate the exact finite coefficients `Lambda(n)/sqrt(n)`, internalize the Gamma/polar counterterms selected by the same geometry, and come with an independent sign theorem. A non-scalar correspondence, infinite-dimensional boundary response, or genuine cohomological/intersection construction could still satisfy those requirements; simply promoting the `WP-048` reflection to a `Z_2` grading cannot.
