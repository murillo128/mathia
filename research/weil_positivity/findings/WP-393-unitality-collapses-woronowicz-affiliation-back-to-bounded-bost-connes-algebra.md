# WP-393 — Unitality collapses Woronowicz affiliation back to the bounded Bost--Connes algebra

## Claim

`WP-392` leaves several genuinely different unbounded/operator-domain categories open, but one tempting reading of that boundary is actually empty: **an unbounded operator affiliated with the Bost--Connes C-star algebra itself in Woronowicz's C-star-algebraic sense cannot exist**.

Let `A_BC` denote the usual Bost--Connes C-star algebra and let `A_BC^eta` denote its affiliated elements in the sense used by Woronowicz. Then

\[
\boxed{A_{BC}^{\eta}=A_{BC}.}
\tag{1}
\]

Thus every Woronowicz-affiliated element of `A_BC` is bounded and already belongs to the same source C-star algebra. Combining (1) with `WP-392`, for every standard positive-energy representation `pi_alpha`, a positive `T eta A_BC` satisfying

\[
\langle \pi_\alpha(T)\varepsilon_k,\varepsilon_k\rangle=0
\qquad
\text{for every }k\text{ divisible by at least two distinct primes}
\tag{2}
\]

has

\[
\boxed{\pi_\alpha(T)=0.}
\tag{3}
\]

So **Woronowicz affiliation with the unital source algebra does not evade the bounded positive-cone obstruction**. A surviving "unbounded Bost--Connes" route must change the operator category in a mathematically substantive way rather than merely replace membership by Woronowicz affiliation.

**Classification:** `EXACT + CLASSICAL-AFFILIATION-THEOREM + RH-INDEPENDENT + BOST-CONNES-SPECIALIZATION + UNITALITY-COLLAPSE + WP-392-CONSEQUENCE + CATEGORY-BOUNDARY + NO-LITERAL-UNBOUNDED-AFFILIATED-SOURCE-ELEMENT + NOT-A-WEIL-BRIDGE`.

## 1. Woronowicz affiliation is genuinely larger only for nonunital C-star algebras

For a closed densely defined operator `T`, Woronowicz uses the bounded `z`-transform

\[
z_T=T(1+T^*T)^{-1/2}.
\tag{4}
\]

Affiliation with a C-star algebra `A` requires `z_T` to lie in the multiplier algebra together with the standard strict-density condition that lets (4) recover a densely defined operator. In Section 1 of *C*-algebras generated by unbounded elements*, Woronowicz records the exact boundary

\[
A\text{ unital}\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad A^{\eta}=A.
\tag{5}
\]

The surrounding statements also identify `M(A)` as precisely the bounded affiliated elements. This is not a new Mathia theorem: it is part of the classical affiliation framework, whose purpose is especially to handle nonunital C-star algebras arising from noncompact quantum spaces.

For intuition, the collapse can also be seen directly from the `z`-transform. In a unital algebra `M(A)=A`. Writing

\[
h=(1-z_T^*z_T)^{1/2},
\tag{6}
\]

the affiliation condition makes the right ideal `hA` dense in `A`. Because `1 in A`, some `ha` lies within norm distance `<1` of `1` and is therefore invertible. Hence `h` has a right inverse; since `h=h^*>=0`, taking adjoints gives a left inverse as well, so `h` is invertible. Equation (4) then reconstructs

\[
T=z_T h^{-1}\in A,
\tag{7}
\]

and `T` is bounded. This is exactly the mechanism behind (5).

## 2. The Bost--Connes source algebra lies on the unital side of the boundary

The ordinary Bost--Connes algebra is a separable **unital** C-star algebra. In its standard generator presentation the diagonal cyclotomic algebra already contains the identity (`e(0)=1`), and adjoining the multiplicative isometries does not remove it. Equivalently, the familiar semigroup crossed-product presentation starts from the unital algebra `C(hat Z)`.

Therefore the classical implication (5) applies without any extra analytic hypothesis:

\[
T\eta A_{BC}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
T\in A_{BC},\qquad \|T\|<\infty.
\tag{8}
\]

This sharply separates two phrases that can otherwise sound interchangeable:

- "unbounded operator affiliated with `A_BC`" in Woronowicz's C-star sense is impossible;
- "unbounded operator/form associated with a Bost--Connes representation or completion" can be meaningful, but only after specifying a different ambient category.

That distinction matters because simply saying "allow affiliated operators" would otherwise look like an escape from `WP-392` while in fact remaining inside its bounded source algebra.

## 3. `WP-392` immediately closes the positive prime-power selector in this category

Take `T eta A_BC` positive and a standard positive-energy representation `pi_alpha`. By (8), `T` is an ordinary bounded element of `A_BC`; hence

\[
A:=\pi_\alpha(T)\in\pi_\alpha(A_{BC})
\tag{9}
\]

is a bounded positive operator. If its basis quadratic form vanishes on every mixed-prime label as in (2), `WP-392` applies verbatim and gives `A=0`. No new recurrence argument is needed: the entire point is that Woronowicz affiliation has not enlarged the source category to which the recurrence theorem applies.

This consequence is narrower than saying that every unbounded completion fails. It only removes the **literal affiliation escape inside the same unital C-star algebra**.

## 4. What an actual unbounded escape must now specify

Several categories remain genuinely outside (1) and `WP-392`:

- affiliation with a **von Neumann algebra** in a KMS/GNS representation, together with a canonical modular or semifinite-weight constraint;
- a closed or closable quadratic form whose **domain is source-derived** and cannot be freely programmed after inspecting prime-power support;
- regular unbounded operators on a larger Hilbert C-star module or correspondence rather than on the source algebra viewed as its own module;
- Woronowicz-affiliated elements of a **nonunital ideal or boundary C-star algebra** canonically produced by the Bost--Connes geometry;
- weights, modular generators, one-sided range defects, or other objects that are not elements affiliated with the original unital `A_BC`.

These are not equivalent loopholes. Each must identify its ambient algebra/module, domain, representation or weight before the Weil target is inspected. In particular, passing to the standard irreducible representation's bicommutant without an extra source law is still too large: as `WP-392` notes, that bicommutant is `B(ell^2(N^x))`, where arbitrary diagonal support can be programmed.

## 5. Prior-art and novelty audit

The affiliation theorem itself is classical. Woronowicz's 1995 paper defines the `z`-transform/affiliation framework and explicitly records the equivalence (5); its stated motivation is precisely the nonunital setting of noncompact quantum groups. The Bost--Connes algebra's unitality is likewise standard. A bounded literature search around Woronowicz affiliation, unital C-star algebras, multiplier algebras, and unbounded generators found no basis for treating affiliation with a unital C-star algebra as a new unbounded source category; the primary source states the opposite directly.

Accordingly, **no novelty is claimed for (5) or (8)**. The durable Mathia contribution is the line-specific classification consequence: one of the explicit category escapes left after `WP-392` is classicalized and removed when "affiliated" means Woronowicz-affiliated with `A_BC` itself. The remaining research question is thereby forced to name a genuinely different unbounded ambient structure.

Primary literature anchor: S. L. Woronowicz, *C*-algebras generated by unbounded elements*, Reviews in Mathematical Physics **7** (1995), no. 3, 481--521, DOI `10.1142/S0129055X95000207`, especially Section 1.

## Limits

- This finding does **not** rule out unbounded operators affiliated with a von Neumann algebra generated by a Bost--Connes representation. That is a different notion and ambient algebra.
- It does **not** rule out closable forms, modular/KMS weights, unbounded generators on larger Hilbert modules, or Woronowicz affiliation with a canonically chosen nonunital ideal/boundary algebra.
- It does not show that any surviving unbounded category is source-selective enough to encode the Weil coefficients; that remains to be proved rather than assumed.
- The support conclusion (3) inherits the exact mixed-prime-nullity and standard-positive-energy-representation hypotheses of `WP-392`.
- No archimedean Gamma term, polar normalization, global Weil form, positivity theorem, or RH consequence is produced.

## Consequence for the research line

The Bost--Connes frontier should no longer list "unbounded affiliated operators of the source C-star algebra" as an escape from the bounded no-go. For the unital `A_BC`, that phrase collapses exactly to the bounded algebra already covered by `WP-392`.

The meaningful unbounded frontier is therefore more specific: derive a nonprogrammable domain/weight/module/boundary category from the source, or derive a global finite--archimedean completion in which the desired arithmetic support and sign emerge after assembly. Merely replacing `A in A_BC` by `T eta A_BC` changes notation, not mathematics.
