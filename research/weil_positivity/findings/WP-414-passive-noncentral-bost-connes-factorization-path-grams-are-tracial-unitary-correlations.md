# WP-414 — Passive noncentral Bost--Connes factorization-path Grams are tracial unitary correlations

## Claim

`WP-411` shows that passive full-unitary matrix decoration disappears from the canonical range-projection Gram. `WP-412` then forces any thermodynamically visible passive fibre observable into the scale-balanced sector, while `WP-413` proves that the most direct equal-scale path Gram is only an abelian phase Gram for central twists. The immediate remaining possibility is to retain genuinely noncommuting fibre holonomy among several ordered factorization paths with the same total scale.

That possibility can change correlations, but it still cannot make the positive sign arithmetic.

Work in the passive full-fibre matrix amplification of the Bost--Connes groupoid from `WP-411`. For every integer scale `n`, let `V_n` be a unitary-coefficient section supported on the canonical bisection `B_n`, with

\[
V_n^*V_n=1,
\qquad
V_nV_n^*=E_n\otimes I_d,
\qquad
\alpha_t(V_n)=n^{it}V_n.
\tag{1}
\]

For an ordered factorization path

\[
a=(n_1,\ldots,n_r),
\qquad N(a)=n_1\cdots n_r,
\]

put

\[
X_a:=V_{n_1}\cdots V_{n_r}.
\tag{2}
\]

Fix finitely many paths with the same total scale `N(a)=N`. Since the underlying Bost--Connes bisections obey `B_mB_n=B_{mn}`, every `X_a` is supported on the same bisection `B_N`, is an isometry, and has common range projection `E_N\otimes I_d`. For one reference path `a_0`, define

\[
D_a:=X_{a_0}^*X_a.
\tag{3}
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
D_a\in C(\widehat{\mathbf Z},M_d),\qquad
D_a^*D_a=D_aD_a^*=1,
}
\tag{4}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
X_a=X_{a_0}D_a,
\qquad
X_a^*X_b=D_a^*D_b.
}
\tag{5}
\]

Thus for **every state** `Phi` on the amplified algebra,

\[
\boxed{
G_{ab}:=\Phi(X_a^*X_b)=\Phi(D_a^*D_b)
}
\tag{6}
\]

is an ordinary unitary-correlation Gram in the GNS Hilbert space of the restriction of `Phi` to the unit-space matrix algebra. Its positivity is therefore universal:

\[
\sum_{a,b}\overline{c_a}G_{ab}c_b
=
\Phi\!\left(
\left(\sum_a c_aD_a\right)^*
\left(\sum_b c_bD_b\right)
\right)
\ge0.
\tag{7}
\]

If `Phi=Phi_beta` is a positive-temperature KMS state, every `D_a` is fixed by the scale dynamics because the two paths in (3) have the same total degree. The KMS condition therefore makes `Phi_beta` tracial on the C-star algebra generated by the `D_a`. Hence the thermal readout is more specifically a **tracial unitary-correlation Gram**.

Noncentral holonomy is not erased: unlike the central scalar subclass of `WP-413`, the spectrum of (6) can vary. But that variation is extra fibre information, while the positive sign remains the generic state-Gram inequality (7). Moreover, if the decorated sections are required to retain the strict commutative Bost--Connes semigroup law `V_mV_n=V_{mn}`, then all ordered paths with the same factorization product coincide and every `D_a=1`; the path Gram collapses to rank one. Consequently, a nontrivial noncentral path holonomy already requires additional cocycle/connection data beyond the strict source semigroup, and Gram positivity does not select which such data are arithmetic.

**Classification:** `CLASSICAL-GROUPOID-BISECTION-CALCULUS + CLASSICAL-CSTAR-STATE-GRAM-POSITIVITY + EXACT-DERIVED-BC-OBSTRUCTION + PASSIVE-MATRIX-FIBRES + SCALE-BALANCED-PATHS + COMMON-BISECTION-FACTORING + UNIT-SPACE-MATRIX-HOLONOMY + TRACIAL-UNITARY-CORRELATION-GRAM + NONCENTRAL-HOLONOMY-MAY-SURVIVE + STRICT-SEMIGROUP-RANK-ONE + MATCHED-CONTROL-PROGRAMMABLE + NOT-A-WEIL-POSITIVITY-PROOF`.

## 1. Equal-scale paths have common source and range

The matrix coefficient changes the fibre over an arrow but not the underlying groupoid arrow. Therefore convolution of sections supported on `B_{n_1},...,B_{n_r}` is supported on

\[
B_{n_1}\cdots B_{n_r}=B_{N(a)}.
\tag{8}
\]

Because each `V_n` is an isometry, every product `X_a` is an isometry. Full-unitary fibre coefficients also leave the range support unchanged, so

\[
X_a^*X_a=1,
\qquad
X_aX_a^*=E_{N(a)}\otimes I_d.
\tag{9}
\]

Hence equal-total-scale paths are not merely in the same KMS spectral subspace: they are full-unitary lifts of the **same** underlying bisection and have exactly the same initial and final projections.

This is the noncentral matrix analogue of the common-bisection step in `WP-413`. The difference is that relative path data need no longer land in a commutative algebra.

## 2. Relative holonomy is a unit-space unitary

Fix `a_0` and form (3). Since both factors are supported on `B_N`,

\[
\operatorname{supp}(D_a)
\subseteq B_N^{-1}B_N
=s(B_N)
=\widehat{\mathbf Z}.
\tag{10}
\]

For the trivial rank-`d` matrix amplification this is exactly the unit-space algebra `C(\widehat{\mathbf Z},M_d)`. Common range now gives unitarity algebraically:

\[
\begin{aligned}
D_a^*D_a
&=X_a^*X_{a_0}X_{a_0}^*X_a
 =X_a^*(E_N\otimes I_d)X_a
 =1,\\
D_aD_a^*
&=X_{a_0}^*X_aX_a^*X_{a_0}
 =X_{a_0}^*(E_N\otimes I_d)X_{a_0}
 =1.
\end{aligned}
\tag{11}
\]

Likewise

\[
X_{a_0}D_a
=X_{a_0}X_{a_0}^*X_a
=(E_N\otimes I_d)X_a
=X_a,
\tag{12}
\]

which proves (5).

No commutativity has been used. The `D_a` may be genuinely matrix-valued, base-dependent, and mutually noncommuting. What is rigid is their location: all equal-scale path ambiguity has been pushed into unit-space unitaries.

## 3. KMS scalarization makes the surviving correlation tracial

Equation (5) proves (6) for an arbitrary state and immediately gives the universal Gram inequality (7). At this level positivity contains no arithmetic information: the same proof works for any family of unitaries in any unital C-star algebra and any state.

The passive dynamics adds one further exact restriction. Since

\[
\alpha_t(X_a)=N^{it}X_a
\]

for every path in the family,

\[
\alpha_t(D_a)
=\alpha_t(X_{a_0}^*)\alpha_t(X_a)
=N^{-it}N^{it}D_a
=D_a.
\tag{13}
\]

For fixed elements `A,B` the KMS boundary condition gives

\[
\Phi_\beta(AB)=\Phi_\beta(BA),
\tag{14}
\]

because `alpha_{i beta}(A)=A`. Thus `Phi_beta` restricts to a trace on the fixed algebra generated by the `D_a`. The only passive thermal information in the path Gram is therefore a family of tracial moments `Phi_beta(D_a^*D_b)`.

This does **not** force those moments to be constant or scalar. It only identifies their category sharply: equal-scale noncentral path data survive as tracial unitary correlations, not as a new sign mechanism.

## 4. A matched matrix control changes the spectrum without changing the arithmetic source

The distinction between surviving holonomy and explanatory positivity is visible already at total scale `6`. Let `s_n` denote the undeformed scalar Bost--Connes isometry and take constant fibre matrices

\[
V_2=s_2\otimes A,
\qquad
V_3=s_3\otimes B,
\tag{15}
\]

with

\[
A=\begin{pmatrix}e^{i\theta}&0\\0&e^{-i\theta}\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
B=\begin{pmatrix}0&1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\tag{16}
\]

The two ordered paths have the same scalar support because `s_2s_3=s_6=s_3s_2`, but their matrix coefficients are `AB` and `BA`. Their relative holonomy is

\[
D=(AB)^*(BA)=B A^* B A=A^2.
\tag{17}
\]

For the product KMS state whose matrix factor is the normalized trace,

\[
z:=\Phi_\beta(D)=\tfrac12\operatorname{Tr}(A^2)=\cos(2\theta).
\tag{18}
\]

The two-path Gram is

\[
G_\theta=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&z\\
\overline z&1
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\operatorname{spec}(G_\theta)=\{1+|z|,1-|z|\}.
\tag{19}
\]

As `theta` varies, the same underlying Bost--Connes scales and bisections give a continuous family ranging from rank one (`|z|=1`) to the identity Gram (`z=0`). Every member is positive. The arithmetic source has not selected the matrix holonomy; it was inserted through the passive fibre decoration.

This control deliberately does **not** satisfy the strict semigroup relation when `A` and `B` fail to commute. That is the point. If strict multiplicativity is imposed, then

\[
V_2V_3=V_6=V_3V_2,
\tag{20}
\]

so the two paths coincide and (17) becomes `D=1`. Therefore nontrivial path holonomy is additional fibre multiplication/cocycle data. A source-native proposal must derive that data canonically; it cannot count the existence of a positive path Gram as evidence that the inserted holonomy has the Weil orientation.

## 5. Relation to the current frontier

This finding closes a specific gap rather than subsuming the preceding obstructions. `WP-411` concerns the projection readout, where full-unitary matrix coefficients cancel before any state is applied. `WP-412` is the KMS spectral gate, which kills scale-unbalanced moments but explicitly leaves balanced cycles open. `WP-413` analyzes equal-scale central twists and forces relative holonomy into the commutative diagonal algebra. The present result treats the immediate **noncentral** continuation: relative holonomy can remain matrix-valued and observable, but equal-scale path positivity still reduces to a universal unitary-correlation Gram.

It is also compatible with `WP-407`. Generic cycle holonomy can affect positive block spectra once a cycle exists. Here the stronger common-bisection/common-range structure does not gauge away the noncentral holonomy; instead it shows that the path vectors are all one reference isometry times unit-space unitaries. The spectrum can therefore feel holonomy, as (19) demonstrates, while the positive sign remains automatic.

The accepted Bost--Connes conditioning clue is not resolved. A viable continuation must now do more than choose a noncommuting balanced cycle and take its state Gram. It needs a **source-forced** noncentral cocycle/connection together with a readout whose arithmetic orientation is not merely generic C-star Gram positivity, or it must leave this passive full-fibre/common-bisection setting through non-full source/range geometry, active dynamics, a genuinely non-Gram functional, or finite--archimedean coupling.

## 6. Prior-art and novelty audit

The ingredients are classical. Bost--Connes supplies the multiplicative integer-scale bisections and time evolution. Standard groupoid/Fell-bundle section calculus supplies support under convolution and the fact that the product of a section on `B_N^{-1}` with one on `B_N` lies over the unit space; Muhly--Williams and Bice are representative general references. Positivity of `[Phi(D_a^*D_b)]` is the elementary GNS/state Gram identity, and the fact that a KMS state is tracial on fixed elements is standard C-star dynamical-systems theory.

No novelty is claimed for any of those theorems. The Mathia-specific contribution is their combination at the exact survivor left by `WP-411`--`WP-413`: **passive noncentral equal-scale Bost--Connes factorization paths reduce to unit-space unitary correlations, and KMS scalarization makes those correlations tracial.** This shows simultaneously that noncentral holonomy may remain numerically visible and that its Gram positivity cannot by itself certify arithmetic orientation.

The internal novelty audit found no existing `WP` finding making this noncentral common-bisection reduction. The closest results have distinct scopes: `WP-411` removes matrix coefficients from range projections, `WP-412` leaves balanced matrix cycles unresolved, and `WP-413` assumes central line-bundle twists and obtains an abelian phase Gram. Older universal Gram findings explain why positivity of correlations is generic, but they do not identify the surviving balanced Bost--Connes fibre datum as a unit-space matrix unitary or separate strict-semigroup collapse from extra noncentral path holonomy.

## 7. Adversarial boundary and falsification

The exact algebraic part can be falsified only by violating one of its declared structural premises. If equal-total-scale full-fibre paths in this model had different underlying bisection support or different range projections, (8)--(12) would fail; that would mean the proposed system is not the passive full-fibre Bost--Connes amplification used here. If common source and range hold, a nonunitary `D_a=X_{a_0}^*X_a` would contradict the direct identities (11).

A KMS counterexample would require fixed analytic elements `A,B` with `Phi_beta(AB) != Phi_beta(BA)`, contradicting the KMS boundary condition on the fixed-point algebra. By contrast, a nonconstant or noncommuting `D_a`, a path Gram with nontrivial spectrum, or a nonzero balanced holonomy is **not** a counterexample; those are explicitly allowed and are the reason the conclusion concerns explanatory positivity rather than invisibility.

The relevance conclusion would be overturned by a stronger source theorem: derive a unique or rigid noncentral fibre cocycle from the Bost--Connes/arithmetic source, show that an associated non-Gram or otherwise non-generic positive functional reproduces the finite Mangoldt sector together with the required mixed-prime controls, and then supply the archimedean and polar completion without target programming. Nothing here rules out that stronger route.

## Consequence

**Supported exact negative with a precise survivor.** Moving from central twists to passive noncommuting matrix fibres does not rescue equal-scale factorization-path Gram positivity as an explanation of the Weil sign. The matrix holonomy can survive and can change correlation spectra, but the sign is still the universal positivity of a tracial unitary-correlation Gram; if strict Bost--Connes semigroup multiplication is retained, even that holonomy collapses and the Gram is rank one.

The surviving research target is therefore narrower: derive genuinely arithmetic noncentral fibre/cocycle data from the source and use it in a mechanism stronger than direct state-Gram positivity, or change the passive full-fibre/common-bisection hypotheses. The finding does not prove Weil positivity, does not approach RH by itself, and does not close the accepted conditioning clue.
