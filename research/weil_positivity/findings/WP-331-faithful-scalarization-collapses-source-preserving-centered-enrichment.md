# WP-331 — Faithful scalarization collapses any exact source-preserving centered enrichment to the deterministic operator-valued law

## Claim

`WP-329` showed that a deterministic amalgamated lift can carry the exact Mangoldt source only through the scalarization state, so operator-valued free divisibility upstairs is universal. `WP-330` then showed that merely adding nonzero operator-valued noise does not help when scalarization annihilates the fluctuation ideal. It left a natural repair: require the scalar readout to be faithful on the positive sector generated by the centered fluctuation, so that the operator-valued covariance cannot hide in a null ideal.

That repair is incompatible with preserving the exact source law in the same scalar observable.

Let `B subset A` be unital `C*`-algebras, let

\[
E:A\to B
\tag{1}
\]

be a conditional expectation, let `tau` be a state on `B`, and put

\[
\phi=\tau\circ E.
\tag{2}
\]

For any self-adjoint `X in A`, define

\[
b=E(X)\in B_{\rm sa},
\qquad
Y=X-b,
\qquad
E(Y)=0.
\tag{3}
\]

Then the exact conditional-variance identity is

\[
E(X^2)=b^2+E(Y^2),
\tag{4}
\]

hence

\[
\phi(X^2)-\tau(b^2)=\tau(E(Y^2))\ge0.
\tag{5}
\]

If the scalar law of `X` under `phi` is required to equal the scalar law of the deterministic base coordinate `b` under `tau`, then equality of second moments forces

\[
\tau(E(Y^2))=0.
\tag{6}
\]

Therefore, whenever `tau` is faithful on the positive ideal containing `E(Y^2)` — in particular when `tau` is faithful on `B` — one has

\[
E(Y^2)=0.
\tag{7}
\]

Complete positivity of `E` then forces

\[
E(YZ)=0
\qquad
\text{for every }Z\in A.
\tag{8}
\]

Consequently every `B`-valued moment of `X` is exactly the deterministic moment of `b`:

\[
E(X^n)=b^n
\qquad(n\ge0),
\tag{9}
\]

and all centered operator-valued cumulant data vanish in the `E`-law. If `E` itself is faithful, (7) further implies `Y=0`, so `X=b` inside `A`.

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\text{exact source law}
+\text{ faithful visibility of centered positive variance}
\Longrightarrow
\text{deterministic operator-valued law}.
}
\tag{10}
\]

The operator-valued escape after `WP-330` therefore cannot simultaneously keep the exact Mangoldt source as the scalar law of `X`, take the canonical base coordinate to be `E(X)`, and add source-coupled centered covariance that is genuinely visible to the same faithful scalar readout. A viable enriched route must change the architecture before scalarization: the arithmetic source must appear through a different response observable, a compression/pairing, a joint finite--archimedean object, or another non-deterministic coupling whose scalar output is not required to remain the unchanged source law.

**Classification:** `DERIVED + EXACT + C*-CONDITIONAL-EXPECTATION + FAITHFUL-READOUT + VARIANCE-DECOMPOSITION + OPERATOR-VALUED-NULLSPACE + UNIVERSALITY-OBSTRUCTION + NOT-WEIL-POSITIVITY`.

## Evidence class

The result is an exact algebraic/`C*`-positive argument. It uses no zeta zeros, RH assumption, prime asymptotic, analytic continuation, numerical fit, or regularization. The Mangoldt source enters only when the universal statement is applied to the surviving operator-valued route from `WP-329`--`WP-330`.

The conditional-expectation and completely-positive Cauchy--Schwarz facts are standard operator-algebra machinery. The claim here is not a new general theorem about conditional expectations or free probability. The substantive research delta is the Mathia-specific obstruction (10): the most obvious anti-vacuity repair suggested by `WP-330` destroys the possibility of retaining the exact arithmetic scalar law while making the centered operator-valued fluctuation visible.

## 1. Exact conditional-variance decomposition

Because `b=E(X)` belongs to `B` and `E` is a `B`-bimodule map,

\[
E(bY)=bE(Y)=0,
\qquad
E(Yb)=E(Y)b=0.
\tag{11}
\]

Expanding `X=b+Y` gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
E(X^2)
&=E(b^2+bY+Yb+Y^2)\\
&=b^2+E(Y^2),
\end{aligned}
\tag{12}
\]

which is (4). Since `Y=Y^*`,

\[
Y^2=Y^*Y\ge0,
\qquad
E(Y^2)\ge0.
\tag{13}
\]

After scalarization,

\[
\phi(X)=\tau(b),
\tag{14}
\]

and

\[
\operatorname{Var}_{\phi}(X)
=
\tau(E(Y^2))
+
\bigl(\tau(b^2)-\tau(b)^2\bigr).
\tag{15}
\]

So the scalar variance splits into the positive scalarized conditional variance plus the variance already present in the base coordinate. This is the noncommutative conditional-variance decomposition needed here; no freeness assumption enters.

If `X` and `b` have the same scalar law under `phi` and `tau`, respectively, then their second moments agree. Equation (5) gives (6). If `tau` is faithful on the hereditary positive sector generated by `E(Y^2)`, then (7) follows.

Global faithfulness of `tau` is stronger than necessary. The exact requirement is only that the positive covariance element `E(Y^2)` cannot lie in a `tau`-null positive ideal. This is precisely the anti-vacuity condition left open by `WP-330`.

## 2. Zero conditional variance erases the whole operator-valued fluctuation law

Equation (7) is stronger than saying that one scalar second moment vanished. Conditional expectations are completely positive, so the `2 x 2` operator matrix

\[
\begin{pmatrix}
E(Y^*Y) & E(Y^*Z)\\
E(Z^*Y) & E(Z^*Z)
\end{pmatrix}
\tag{16}
\]

is positive for every `Z in A`. If its upper-left entry is zero, positivity forces the off-diagonal entries to vanish. Therefore

\[
E(Y^*Z)=0
\qquad(Z\in A).
\tag{17}
\]

Since `Y=Y^*`, this is (8).

Now expand any power `(b+Y)^n`. Every term other than `b^n` contains at least one `Y`. Write such a word as

\[
b^kYZ
\tag{18}
\]

for the initial string of `b`'s preceding its first occurrence of `Y`. Bimodularity and (8) give

\[
E(b^kYZ)=b^kE(YZ)=0.
\tag{19}
\]

Summing the expansion proves (9). More generally every mixed `B`-valued moment containing the centered variable `Y` vanishes in the Hilbert-`B`-module seminorm induced by `E`. Thus the `B`-valued distribution seen by `E` is exactly the deterministic point law at `b`.

This closes a loophole not covered by the explicit direct-sum example in `WP-330`. The centered variable may still be algebraically nonzero when `E` is nonfaithful, but if exact source preservation and scalar faithfulness force `E(Y^2)=0`, then that variable is already **operator-valued invisible**, not merely scalarization-invisible. If `E` is faithful as well, even that algebraic null direction disappears and `Y=0`.

## 3. Application to the Mangoldt-source operator-valued frontier

At a finite cutoff, `WP-329` represents the exact source on

\[
B_R=C(\Sigma_R)
\tag{20}
\]

by the coordinate `b_R(x)=x` and a scalar state carrying the Mangoldt weights. `WP-330` adds a non-deterministic direct-sum fluctuation but hides its covariance in a scalarization-null ideal.

Suppose one tries to repair that construction without changing the scalar observable. Let `X_R` be a self-adjoint enriched variable with

\[
E_R(X_R)=b_R,
\tag{21}
\]

and demand that the scalar law under `tau_{s,R} circ E_R` remain **exactly** the same truncated Mangoldt law as the scalar law of `b_R` under `tau_{s,R}`. The finite Mangoldt weights on `Sigma_R` are strictly positive, so the integration state `tau_{s,R}` on `C(Sigma_R)` is faithful. The theorem therefore gives

\[
E_R\bigl((X_R-b_R)^2\bigr)=0
\tag{22}
\]

and hence all centered `B_R`-valued moments vanish.

So the most direct faithful repair collapses exactly to the deterministic `B_R`-valued law already classified as universal in `WP-329`. There is no room to encode arithmetic-selective covariance or higher centered cumulants while keeping both (21) and the exact scalar source law.

This is stronger than the matched-control statement in `WP-330`: no specially designed direct-sum null ideal is needed. The incompatibility follows from positivity plus exact second-moment preservation.

## 4. Consequence for finite--archimedean completion

A tempting next move after `WP-330` is to keep the finite Mangoldt scalar law unchanged while adjoining a visible positive fluctuation intended to generate the Gamma/polar counterterms. Equations (5)--(9) show that this cannot work if the same faithful readout is supposed to see the centered positive variance.

If the archimedean/global sector contributes a genuinely positive conditional variance to the same self-adjoint observable, then its scalar second moment necessarily changes. If the scalar moments do not change, that sector is null in the conditional-expectation geometry and cannot carry nonzero `B`-valued centered data visible to the readout.

Therefore the finite and archimedean pieces cannot be assembled by the simple recipe

\[
\text{exact finite source as scalar law}
+
\text{visible centered positive noise}
\tag{23}
\]

while preserving the same scalar output. A surviving global construction must let the finite source and archimedean contribution interact **before** the final scalar law is formed, or use a response functional different from the scalar distribution of one enriched variable.

This does not yet produce the desired Weil sign. It identifies a structural incompatibility that any source-coupled operator-valued positivity proposal must evade explicitly.

## 5. Matched controls and falsification boundary

The obstruction is deliberately source-independent. Replace the Mangoldt weights by any strictly positive probability weights on the same finite support. The integration state on `C(Sigma_R)` remains faithful, and the same argument forces every exact-law-preserving centered enrichment to have zero operator-valued conditional variance. Hence the theorem is not an arithmetic sign mechanism; it is a matched-control obstruction to a proposed mechanism.

The hypotheses also expose exact escape routes.

1. **Nonfaithful scalarization.** Then a positive covariance element may lie in a scalarization-null ideal. This is exactly the universal failure already constructed in `WP-330`, not a source-selective escape.

2. **Change the scalar law.** A visible centered covariance may contribute nontrivially if the enriched variable is not required to have the unchanged source distribution. Any such construction must derive the finite-prime and archimedean/global terms from the new response rather than claim exact source preservation.

3. **Change the observable/readout.** The arithmetic source may be recovered from a compression, boundary response, derivative, pairing, transfer function, or another observable while the ambient enriched variable has a different scalar law. This lies outside the theorem and is the cleanest surviving operator-valued direction.

4. **Drop the conditional-mean identification.** If the canonical arithmetic coordinate is not `E(X)`, the decomposition above does not by itself compare the intended source with `X`. The replacement map must then be justified intrinsically and checked against matched controls.

5. **Leave the self-adjoint positive setting.** Non-self-adjoint constructions require a separate `X^*X`/matrix-linearization audit. Merely changing category does not evade the positivity issue automatically.

No weaker conclusion is being smuggled into a global no-go. The result does **not** rule out genuinely source-coupled operator-valued free geometry, noncommutative boundary pairings, or a joint finite--archimedean positive form. It rules out the specific but natural architecture in which an exact source scalar law is preserved while a faithful readout simultaneously sees a new centered positive fluctuation around its conditional mean.

## 6. Prior-art and novelty audit

Conditional expectations, complete positivity, the associated Hilbert-`B`-module seminorm, operator-valued covariance maps, and operator-valued free infinite divisibility are classical. Popa--Vinnikov, already registered in `SOURCES.md`, supplies the surrounding operator-valued free Lévy--Hinčin context. The variance decomposition and null-vector argument above are elementary consequences of standard conditional-expectation positivity and are not claimed as new operator-algebra theorems.

A bounded literature audit of conditional-expectation/Kadison--Schwarz theory and operator-valued semicircular covariance found no basis for claiming theorem-level novelty. The durable contribution is instead the exact application to the live Mathia route: combining `WP-329` and `WP-330` with a faithful scalar readout does not create the hoped-for source-selective fluctuation geometry; if the scalar source law is held fixed, faithfulness collapses the enriched `B`-valued law back to the deterministic one.

The mechanism is also distinct from classical Weil positivity, Hilbert--Pólya, Connes-style trace formulas, Frobenius/cohomological positivity, and intersection-form arguments. None of those is reproduced here, and no RH-equivalent positive functional is imported. The result is an obstruction on how a future enriched positive object may encode the arithmetic source before it can even be compared with the Weil form.

## Consequence for the research line

The operator-valued frontier is now narrower than the end of `WP-330` suggested. **Faithful visibility and exact source-law preservation cannot coexist with nontrivial centered operator-valued fluctuations around the conditional mean.** Thus the next serious candidate should not ask whether a faithful amalgamated lift of the exact Mangoldt law can carry a positive covariance. Under the natural same-observable formulation, it cannot.

The surviving question is architectural: can Mathia force a source-dependent operator-valued response in which arithmetic lives in mixed data *before* scalarization, the final readout is not merely the scalar law of the source coordinate, and the same nonautomatic geometry produces the finite-prime plus archimedean/global Weil terms? Such a construction would evade the present theorem because it changes the readout interface, not because it hides the fluctuation in another null ideal.
