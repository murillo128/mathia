# WP-421 — Finite active fibres can mix only finitely many Bost--Connes prime directions

## Claim

`WP-420` shows that an active auxiliary fibre does not restore coherence between distinct Bost--Connes time degrees when the readout is invariant under the source and fibre dynamics separately. Its Bell-state control also shows that this obstruction disappears for a state invariant only under the **joint** dynamics. The finite-dimensional version of that surviving escape nevertheless has an exact arithmetic boundary.

Let `(A_BC,sigma)` be the Bost--Connes system and let `B=M_d(C)` carry an autonomous dynamics

\[
\tau_t(b)=e^{itK} b e^{-itK},
\]

with `K=K^*`. Let `Omega` be **any** state on `A_BC tensor B` invariant under the joint flow `sigma tensor tau`; no product-state or separate-invariance hypothesis is imposed. For homogeneous source elements

\[
\sigma_t(a_i)=r_i^{it}a_i,
\qquad r_i\in \mathbf Q_{>0},
\]

and arbitrary `b_i in B`, form the positive Gram matrix

\[
G_{ij}=\Omega((a_i\otimes b_i)^*(a_j\otimes b_j)).
\]

Write `Spec(K)={kappa_1,...,kappa_s}` and define the finite rational Bohr-ratio set

\[
R_K:=\mathbf Q_{>0}\cap\exp(\operatorname{Spec}(K)-\operatorname{Spec}(K)).
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{G_{ij}\ne0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad r_j/r_i\in R_K.}
\]

Consequently there is a **finite set of ordinary primes** `S_K`, namely the primes occurring in the reduced numerators or denominators of elements of `R_K`, such that

\[
G_{ij}\ne0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad v_p(r_i)=v_p(r_j)
\quad\text{for every }p\notin S_K.
\]

Thus even an entangled/coupled state that evades the separate-invariance theorem of `WP-420` cannot use a fixed finite-dimensional autonomous active fibre to create all-prime Bost--Connes incidence. Its Gram matrix remains block diagonal with respect to every prime valuation outside one finite set. A KMS state for the joint dynamics is a special case because KMS states are invariant under that dynamics.

**Classification:** `CLASSICAL-FINITE-BOHR-SPECTRUM + EXACT-DERIVED-BC-SELECTION + JOINT-INVARIANCE-ONLY + ENTANGLED-STATES-ALLOWED + FINITE-PRIME-SUPPORT + MATCHED-NONARITHMETIC-CONTROL + INFINITE/INTERACTING-FIBRE-ESCAPE-REMAINS + NOT-A-WEIL-POSITIVITY-PROOF`.

## Derivation

Let `P_alpha` be the spectral projections of `K`. Every auxiliary operator has the finite Bohr decomposition

\[
b=\sum_{\alpha,\beta}P_\alpha bP_\beta,
\qquad
\tau_t(P_\alpha bP_\beta)
=e^{it(\kappa_\alpha-\kappa_\beta)}P_\alpha bP_\beta.
\]

The source factor in the `(i,j)` Gram entry is homogeneous of frequency

\[
\log(r_j/r_i),
\]

because `a_i^*a_j` transforms by `(r_j/r_i)^{it}`. Decompose `b_i^*b_j` into its Bohr blocks. Each summand of the tensor product then has total frequency

\[
\log(r_j/r_i)+\kappa_\alpha-\kappa_\beta.
\]

If a state is invariant under the joint flow, its expectation on a nonzero-frequency homogeneous element is zero: invariance would otherwise require a nonzero scalar to equal `e^{it omega}` times itself for every `t`. Hence a nonzero Gram entry requires at least one auxiliary Bohr block satisfying

\[
\log(r_j/r_i)+\kappa_\alpha-\kappa_\beta=0,
\]

or equivalently

\[
r_j/r_i=e^{\kappa_\beta-\kappa_\alpha}.
\]

Since `r_j/r_i` is rational, it lies in `R_K`. The set `R_K` is finite because `K` has finitely many eigenvalues. Taking the union of prime divisors of the finitely many reduced rational numbers in `R_K` gives finite `S_K`, and the valuation statement follows immediately.

There is a slightly stronger graph formulation. Put an edge between source ratios whenever the corresponding Gram coefficient can be nonzero. Every connected component lies inside a coset of the subgroup of `Q_{>0}^times` generated by `R_K`. That subgroup is supported on `S_K`. Therefore no chain of allowed finite-fibre coherences can ever change the valuation at a prime outside `S_K`; the obstruction is not merely a bound on a single matrix entry.

## Why this matters for the live completion route

The Bell-state control in `WP-420` proves that total-frequency compensation is a real escape from separate invariance, but it uses one compensating frequency. The present calculation shows what happens when that idea is promoted to a fixed finite-dimensional fibre: only finitely many rational source ratios can be compensated, so only finitely many prime directions can participate in the coupled block structure.

That is incompatible with interpreting a fixed finite active fibre by itself as the missing **global all-prime** completion of the critical Bost--Connes carrier. The Weil finite term ranges over every prime, whereas the finite fibre leaves infinitely many prime valuations as exact superselection labels. Enlarging `d` at each finite cutoff can hide this obstruction numerically, but then the auxiliary object changes with the cutoff and does not define a fixed global source geometry. To make such a growing construction relevant, one would have to derive the limit, its domain/topology, and its prime-energy spectrum canonically from the source rather than choose new gaps as more primes enter.

The result does not say that a finite fibre cannot alter any block. If `K` contains a gap `log q` with rational `q`, a jointly invariant entangled state may retain coherence across that source ratio. The point is that the set of such rational gaps is finite and therefore carries finite prime support. Choosing those gaps to be `log p` for selected primes is also visibly programmable unless another source theorem forces the Hamiltonian.

## Adversarial controls and falsification

The conclusion uses only the multiplicative rational grading of the Bost--Connes dynamics, finite-dimensional Bohr decomposition, and joint invariance. It does **not** use RH, the desired Weil sign, separate invariance, factorization of the state, or positivity beyond the automatic Gram positivity. The matched nonarithmetic control is therefore immediate: replace the Bost--Connes source by any system graded by a free abelian multiplicative group with infinitely many generators. A fixed finite Bohr spectrum can compensate only finitely many generator directions there as well. This finite-support selection is generic kinematics, not an arithmetic sign theorem.

The theorem is sharp at the categorical boundary. A finite-dimensional entangled state can preserve a chosen compensated frequency, exactly as in `WP-420`; the new result only proves that finitely many auxiliary energy gaps cannot cover infinitely many independent prime valuations. An infinite-dimensional auxiliary system with infinitely many source-forced Bohr frequencies, an unbounded generator, or a genuinely interacting joint dynamics not of the product form `sigma tensor tau` is outside the claim. So is a scalarization that is not an invariant state on the stated tensor-product system.

A direct falsification test is simple: keep `B=M_d`, autonomous `tau=Ad(e^{itK})`, and joint invariance, then exhibit a nonzero `G_ij` for which `r_j/r_i` is not `e^{kappa_beta-kappa_alpha}` for any pair of eigenvalues of `K`. Such an entry would contradict the homogeneous selection calculation above.

## Prior-art and novelty audit

The Bost--Connes time evolution and its rational logarithmic grading are classical. Finite-dimensional Hamiltonian dynamics decomposing operators into energy-difference/Bohr-frequency blocks is also standard, as is invariance of KMS states. Targeted searches across Bost--Connes/KMS tensor constructions and general automorphism-spectrum literature found those general ingredients, but no claim that a finite active auxiliary fibre forces the surviving Bost--Connes cross-ratio graph into a subgroup supported on finitely many ordinary primes.

No novelty is claimed for the spectral facts themselves. The durable contribution is the line-specific consequence and closure boundary: `WP-420` left arbitrary jointly invariant coupled states open; this finding closes the entire **fixed finite-dimensional autonomous-fibre** subcase, including entangled states, by converting finite Bohr spectrum into finite prime support.

## Consequence

**Supported exact negative with a narrower survivor.** A fixed finite-dimensional autonomous active fibre cannot be the global finite--archimedean incidence mechanism sought by the canonical Weil-positivity mandate, even when the readout state is entangled and only jointly invariant. It can couple at most a finite set of Bost--Connes prime directions. The active-fibre route must therefore obtain an infinite/source-forced compensating spectrum, alter the joint dynamics through a genuine interaction, or leave this tensor-product invariant-state category. Any such survivor still has to explain the archimedean and polar sectors and provide an independent sign theorem rather than merely a positive Gram.
