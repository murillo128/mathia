# WP-158 — Torus-character correspondences split over prime-primary shells

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + DECISIVE-NARROWING + PRIME-CIRCLE + ALGEBRAIC-TORUS + TORSION-CHARACTERS + PRIMARY-DECOMPOSITION + NO-MIXED-PRIME-CHARACTER-COUPLING + MULTIPLICATION-CRT-CLASSICALIZATION + MATCHED-COPRIME-CYCLIC-CONTROL + PRIOR-ART-CLASSICALIZATION` for the most canonical group-law correspondence escape left open by `WP-157`.

`WP-157` shows that merely tensoring independent cyclotomic factors cannot create the mixed finite-prime geometry required before a determinant or positivity theorem is applied. A natural next attempt is to use the structure that Prime Circle has before any determinant: roots of unity are a torsion subgroup of the circle, so one can impose source-forced algebraic-torus correspondences such as diagonals, multiplication graphs, or more general monomial character equations among distinct prime-primary shell coordinates.

For fixed algebraic-torus characters this also fails, for an elementary but rigid reason. Distinct prime-primary torsion groups are the primary components of a finite abelian torsion group. A monomial equation equal to `1` therefore splits prime by prime: on primitive shells of exact orders `p_i^{a_i}`, it is either tautological in a coordinate or impossible there. Allowing a fixed torsion translate does not help; the right-hand side has its own unique primary decomposition and the solution set is a Cartesian product of independent primary solution sets. Any finite system of translated character equations has the same factorization.

The multiplication map looks more collective because it sends several prime-primary roots to a root of composite order, but for pairwise coprime orders it is exactly the Chinese-remainder/product decomposition of the cyclic torsion group. On primitive shells it is a bijective reparameterization, not a new interaction. Thus the most canonical **group-law-defined subtori and torsion cosets cannot supply the nonseparable cross-prime correspondence demanded by `WP-157`**. A surviving Prime-Circle route must use geometry not generated solely by fixed torus characters: for example genuinely additive Laurent-polynomial incidence, analytic/metric data, a non-character correspondence, or finite--archimedean structure introduced before positivity.

## 1. Prime-primary shell product

Fix distinct primes

\[
P=\{p_1,\ldots,p_r\}
\]

and positive exponents `a_i`. Write

\[
X_i=\mu_{p_i^{a_i}}^{\mathrm{prim}}
\]

for the primitive roots of exact order `p_i^{a_i}`, and

\[
X_P=\prod_{i=1}^r X_i\subset (\mathbf G_m)^r.
\tag{1}
\]

An algebraic-torus character is determined by an integer vector

\[
u=(u_1,\ldots,u_r)\in\mathbf Z^r
\]

and has the monomial form

\[
\chi_u(x_1,\ldots,x_r)=\prod_{i=1}^r x_i^{u_i}.
\tag{2}
\]

The first candidate source-forced correspondence is a character kernel

\[
\chi_u(x)=1.
\tag{3}
\]

## 2. A character kernel cannot couple distinct primary shells

Suppose `x_i in X_i` and (3) holds. Put

\[
y_i=x_i^{u_i}.
\]

Then `y_i` has `p_i`-power order. Equation (3) says

\[
\prod_i y_i=1.
\tag{4}
\]

The subgroup generated by the `y_i` is finite abelian, and its primary decomposition is a direct product. Since the orders of different `y_i` are powers of distinct primes, equality (4) forces every primary component to be trivial:

\[
\boxed{y_i=1\quad\text{for every }i.}
\tag{5}
\]

Because `x_i` has exact order `p_i^{a_i}`,

\[
x_i^{u_i}=1
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
p_i^{a_i}\mid u_i.
\tag{6}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
X_P\cap\ker\chi_u=
\begin{cases}
X_P,&p_i^{a_i}\mid u_i\text{ for all }i,\\
\varnothing,&\text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}}
\tag{7}
\]

So a fixed character does not cut out a nontrivial mixed locus inside a product of distinct prime-primary primitive shells. It either imposes no condition on that shell product or excludes it entirely.

The diagonal is the simplest special case. For distinct primes `p != q`, primitive `p^a`- and `q^b`-roots cannot satisfy `x=y`, because an element having both a `p`-power and a `q`-power order must have order one, while the primitive shells have nontrivial exact order.

## 3. Fixed torsion translates still factor prime by prime

A more general torsion coset uses

\[
\chi_u(x)=\zeta
\tag{8}
\]

for a fixed root of unity `zeta`. Let

\[
\zeta=\prod_\ell \zeta_\ell
\tag{9}
\]

be its unique primary decomposition, where `zeta_ell` has `ell`-power order and only finitely many factors are nontrivial.

The left-hand side of (8) has primary components `x_i^{u_i}` at the primes `p_i`. Therefore (8) is equivalent to the independent conditions

\[
\boxed{x_i^{u_i}=\zeta_{p_i}\quad\text{for every }i}
\tag{10}
\]

plus the requirement that `zeta_ell=1` for every prime `ell` not in `P`.

Consequently the solution set on `X_P` factorizes exactly:

\[
\boxed{
\{x\in X_P:\chi_u(x)=\zeta\}
=
\prod_i
\{x_i\in X_i:x_i^{u_i}=\zeta_{p_i}\}.
}
\tag{11}
\]

There is again no cross-prime incidence. The translate can change which points survive in each primary shell, but it cannot correlate the choice in one prime coordinate with the choice in another.

## 4. Finite systems of monomial equations also split

Now impose any finite family

\[
\chi_{u^{(j)}}(x)=\zeta_j,
\qquad 1\le j\le s.
\tag{12}
\]

Apply the primary decomposition argument to each equation. For every prime `p_i`, collect the resulting one-coordinate constraints into

\[
Y_i
=
\left\{
x_i\in X_i:
 x_i^{u_i^{(j)}}=(\zeta_j)_{p_i}
 \text{ for all }j
\right\}.
\tag{13}
\]

Then the complete solution set is

\[
\boxed{Y=\prod_iY_i.}
\tag{14}
\]

Thus passing from one character kernel to an arbitrary finite intersection of translated subtori does not create an irreducible mixed-prime correspondence on the primary shell product. Any determinant, Gram metric, or positive form applied afterward starts from a correspondence that has already decomposed prime by prime.

This is the exact category relevant to algebraic-torus **character** geometry. It does not cover a general Laurent-polynomial equation containing sums of monomials, such as `x+y=1`; additive equations are genuinely different and remain outside the result.

## 5. The multiplication graph is CRT reparameterization, not entanglement

Prime Circle also supplies the group multiplication map. For coprime positive integers `m,n`,

\[
\mu_m\times\mu_n
\longrightarrow
\mu_{mn},
\qquad
(x,y)\longmapsto xy.
\tag{15}
\]

This map is an isomorphism of finite groups. Injectivity follows because if `xy=1`, then `x=y^{-1}` has order dividing both `m` and `n`, hence is `1`. The domain and codomain both have `mn` elements, so the map is bijective.

Restricting to exact-order elements gives

\[
\boxed{
\mu_m^{\mathrm{prim}}\times\mu_n^{\mathrm{prim}}
\xrightarrow{\sim}
\mu_{mn}^{\mathrm{prim}}
\qquad((m,n)=1).
}
\tag{16}
\]

Indeed the product of elements of coprime exact orders `m` and `n` has exact order `mn`, and the inverse decomposition is unique by the primary decomposition of the cyclic group.

For distinct prime powers this says

\[
\mu_{p^a}^{\mathrm{prim}}\times
\mu_{q^b}^{\mathrm{prim}}
\cong
\mu_{p^aq^b}^{\mathrm{prim}}.
\tag{17}
\]

So introducing the composite shell through multiplication does not evade the product obstruction of `WP-157`; it repackages the same independent primary coordinates as one cyclic shell. This agrees with the cyclotomic compositum/CRT classicalization already visible there.

## 6. Shell-dependent characters would insert the arithmetic by hand

Equation (7) also exposes a tempting but invalid repair. One can choose the exponent `u_i` after seeing the shell depth `a_i`, for example making `u_i` divisible by `p_i^{a_i}` on selected shell tuples and not on others. Then a character equation can of course implement an arbitrary support pattern across shell labels.

But the support is now encoded in the **choice of character**, not forced by a fixed geometric operation on Prime Circle. The same trick works for arbitrary labelled cyclic groups. It therefore fails the branch's source-forcing and matched-control requirements unless an independent Mathia construction canonically produces those shell-dependent exponents before the desired arithmetic selector is specified.

Likewise a torsion translate `zeta` whose primary components are chosen from the target shell data merely moves the encoding into the right-hand side. Fixed torus data split; varying torus data can encode what one wants but require a new forcing theorem.

## 7. Matched control: pairwise-coprime cyclic labels behave identically

Nothing in the proof uses primality beyond pairwise coprimality of the primary orders. Replace the Prime-Circle shells by arbitrary cyclic sets of exact orders

\[
M_1,\ldots,M_r,
\qquad (M_i,M_j)=1\ (i\ne j).
\tag{18}
\]

Every monomial relation with fixed integer exponents and fixed torsion translate decomposes across the coprime cyclic factors by the same argument, and multiplication gives the same CRT isomorphism.

Therefore the no-coupling theorem is structural primary decomposition, not a special arithmetic signature capable of distinguishing primes or producing the Weil finite-place coefficients. It is useful here precisely as a falsifier of a natural Prime-Circle escape.

## 8. Consequence for Weil positivity

`WP-157` leaves open a source-forced equation, correspondence, differential, metric, or boundary datum that entangles distinct prime coordinates before positivity. The present result closes the most canonical algebraic-group version of that escape:

\[
\boxed{
\text{fixed torus characters / torsion cosets}
\Longrightarrow
\text{primary-product correspondence}
\Longrightarrow
\text{no irreducible mixed-prime coupling}.
}
\tag{19}
\]

This fails before the archimedean problem is even addressed. Character geometry on finite torsion shells contains no intrinsic real-place variable and therefore cannot by itself generate the Gamma/polar counterterms required by the global Weil explicit formula.

The remaining escape boundary is narrower but real. A viable Prime-Circle mechanism would have to introduce at least one of:

- an additive Laurent-polynomial or other non-character incidence that genuinely couples primary coordinates;
- analytic, metric, differential, or boundary data not determined solely by the compact-group characters;
- a non-product cohomological correspondence whose differential mixes prime coordinates before its sign theorem;
- a finite--archimedean interaction introduced upstream rather than appended as an independent factor.

Any such candidate must still pass the matched-label control and independently produce the exact Weil normalization and PSD sign.

## 9. Prior art and novelty audit

No new theorem about finite abelian groups, algebraic tori, or roots of unity is claimed. The decomposition used in (5), (9), and (14) is the elementary primary decomposition of finite abelian torsion groups; the coprime multiplication isomorphism (15)--(17) is the Chinese remainder phenomenon for cyclic groups.

The broader external category is the theory of torsion points and torsion cosets on algebraic tori. Michel Laurent's work on exponential Diophantine equations established the toric Manin--Mumford/Lang picture in which torsion solutions are organized by torsion cosets; a contemporary bibliographic entry is M. Laurent, *Equations diophantiennes exponentielles*, Seminaire de Theorie des Nombres de Bordeaux 13 (1983--1984), 1--8, `http://eudml.org/doc/182179`. Iskander Aliev and Chris Smyth, *Solving algebraic equations in roots of unity*, Forum Mathematicum 24 (2012), 641--665, DOI `10.1515/FORM.2011.087`, gives a constructive modern treatment of maximal torsion cosets in `G_m^n` (arXiv `0704.1747`). That literature is substantially more general than the elementary character calculation here.

Targeted searches over the current Weil-positivity frontier and the adjacent `WP-154`--`WP-157` constructions found no persisted result applying primary decomposition specifically to close the **fixed torus-character correspondence** escape after separated tensor determinant lines. Repository code-search endpoints were transiently unavailable during this pass, so the internal audit was completed against the current canonical frontier and its explicit escape boundaries rather than claimed as an exhaustive lexical search of every historical finding.

The Mathia-specific contribution is therefore deliberately narrow: **once Prime Circle is resolved into distinct prime-primary torsion shells, the canonical algebraic-group correspondences generated only by fixed characters cannot provide the missing source-forced mixed-prime geometry.** This is a decisive branch-local restriction, not a claim of new algebraic-torus theory.
