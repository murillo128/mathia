## 2. Why the log-density score removes the repetition factor

`WP-216` correctly notes that direct differentiation of a prime-axis Gram coefficient gives

\[

The spectral log density is different. The classical Fourier expansion

\[

\[

Applying (16) to (15) cancels `k` exactly and yields

\[
2\partial_\beta\log P_{p^{-\beta/2}}(\theta)

2(\log p)\frac{p^{-\beta}}{1-p^{-\beta}}

Thus there is no contradiction with the direct-derivative boundary in `WP-216`. The distinction is exact:
