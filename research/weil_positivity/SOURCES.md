# Weil-positivity literature anchors

This file records only durable literature dependencies for canonical findings in `research/weil_positivity/findings/`. It is not a search log.

## Classical explicit formula and positivity target

- **André Weil**, *Sur les “formules explicites” de la théorie des nombres premiers*, Comm. Sém. Math. Univ. Lund (1952), 252–265. Original explicit-formula positivity framework underlying Weil's criterion. Mathia's retained summary is [`research/prior_art/weil-positivity-criterion.md`](../prior_art/weil-positivity-criterion.md).
- **Enrico Bombieri**, *Remarks on Weil's quadratic functional in the theory of prime numbers. I*, Atti Accad. Naz. Lincei Cl. Sci. Fis. Mat. Natur. Rend. Lincei (9) Mat. Appl. 11 (2000), no. 3, 183–233. Modern treatment of Weil's quadratic functional and its positivity formulation.

## Uniformization and geometric positivity

- **Peter G. Zograf and Leon A. Takhtadzhyan**, *On Liouville's equation, accessory parameters, and the geometry of Teichmüller space for Riemann surfaces of genus 0*, Mathematics of the USSR-Sbornik 60 (1988), no. 1, 143–161. DOI: `10.1070/SM1988v060n01ABEH003160`. Constructs a Weil–Petersson potential from the hyperbolic/Liouville metric and identifies it as a generating function for the Fuchsian accessory parameters. This is the classical geometric-positivity anchor for auditing whether the PC-017 projective-connection defect can yield a Weil-type positive energy.

## Local and semilocal trace structure

- **Jean-François Burnol**, *Scattering on the p-adic field and a trace formula*, International Mathematics Research Notices 2000, no. 2, 57–70. DOI: `10.1155/S1073792800000040`; arXiv: `math/9901051`. Gives a nonarchimedean scattering model in which a nonnegative time-delay spectral function coexists with the local Weil explicit formula only after an odd/even grading, as a **supertrace**. This is a key prior-art warning against identifying each finite-place Weil contribution with an ordinary positive local energy.
- **Alain Connes and Caterina Consani**, *Weil positivity and trace formula, the archimedean place*, Selecta Mathematica (N.S.) 27 (2021), Paper 77. DOI: `10.1007/s00029-021-00689-4`; arXiv: `2006.13771`. Locates an archimedean source of positivity in a compressed scaling action on the orthogonal complement of cutoff projections; the mechanism is operator/compression based rather than a bare positive kernel equal termwise to the explicit formula.
- **Alain Connes**, *The Riemann Hypothesis: Past, Present and a Letter Through Time*, arXiv: `2602.04022` (2026). Current survey and research perspective on restricted Weil quadratic forms and semilocal adele-class spaces. In the semilocal geometry, prime contributions are encoded by periodic orbits while the relevant positivity is a property of the assembled Weil quadratic form.
- **Maisara Shoeib and Malak Torky**, *A Fourier-Multiplier Obstruction for the Zeta Weil Form*, preprint (July 2026), Zenodo DOI: `10.5281/zenodo.21455507`. Proves that a fixed positive same-space assembly of translations and Fourier multipliers cannot equal the full zeta Weil form: the multiplier Gram form has absolutely continuous spectral measure, while Weil positivity would force an atomic zero-sampling measure. WP-005 uses this as direct prior art ruling out a commuting multiplier completion of the Prime-Lattice finite translation comb; the paper's explicit escape routes include compression, boundary conditions, different spaces/base measures, noncommutativity, and singular limits.
- **Marcus Chuk**, *Weil positivity in compact windows: certified two-sided bounds and a Landau--Widom decay law*, arXiv: `2608.24827` (2026 preprint; version dated 27 August 2026). Writes the restricted Weil form with the finite Mangoldt cosine comb, archimedean digamma symbol, and polar term, and gives an unconditional certified positivity result for tests supported in `[-0.8,0.8]`. WP-005 uses it only as a contemporary matched control: at that scale the finite prime comb is already indefinite, so the certified positivity is genuinely a property of the assembled global form, not of positive finite-place summands.

## Prime-lattice and generalized-prime controls

- **Håkan Hedenmalm, Peter Lindqvist, and Kristian Seip**, *A Hilbert space of Dirichlet series and systems of dilated functions in L²(0,1)*, Duke Mathematical Journal 86 (1997), no. 1, 1–37. DOI: `10.1215/S0012-7094-97-08601-4`; arXiv: `math/9512211`. Classical Hardy-Hilbert/Bohr realization in which square-summable Dirichlet coefficients become the `H²` geometry of the infinite prime polydisc/character space. This anchors the exponent-basis operator setup used in WP-004.
- **Harold G. Diamond, Hugh L. Montgomery, and Ulrike M. A. Vorhauer**, *Beurling primes with large oscillation*, Mathematische Annalen 334 (2006), no. 1, 1–36. DOI: `10.1007/s00208-005-0638-2`. Constructs a Beurling generalized-prime system with generalized-integer counting `N_B(x)=κx+O(x^θ)`, `1/2<θ<1`, while its zeta function has infinitely many zeros on a curve `σ=1-a/log t`. WP-004 uses this as a matched control showing that prime-exponent positivity plus a `1/2` Hilbert boundary does not force global RH/Weil positivity.
- **Szilárd Gy. Révész**, *The Carlson-type zero-density theorem for the Beurling ζ function*, Journal of the London Mathematical Society 111 (2025), no. 3, e70110. DOI: `10.1112/jlms.70110`. Records the standard Beurling generalized von Mangoldt function — `log|p|` on generalized prime powers and zero otherwise — and its logarithmic-derivative role. This is the literature anchor for the generalized-prime version of the WP-004 axis calculation.
- **Titus W. Hilberdink and Michel L. Lapidus**, *Beurling zeta functions, generalised primes, and fractal membranes*, Acta Applicandae Mathematicae 94 (2006), no. 1, 21–48. DOI: `10.1007/s10440-006-9063-0`; arXiv: `math/0410270`. Studies arbitrary generalized-prime systems and characterizes additional hypotheses under which analytic continuation and suitable generalized functional equations exist, underscoring that Euler/prime-exponent structure alone does not supply the Riemann archimedean completion.

## Jump-energy and infinitely-divisible controls

- **René L. Schilling**, *An Introduction to Lévy and Feller Processes*, Advanced Courses in Mathematics - CRM Barcelona, Birkhäuser (2017); arXiv: `1603.00251`. Standard audit anchor for Lévy--Khintchine theory, including the Lévy-measure condition `int (1 wedge x^2) nu(dx)<infinity`, compound-Poisson processes, and jump generators. WP-009 uses this to distinguish a genuine passive jump measure from a formally positive atomic coefficient measure.
- **René L. Schilling and Toshihiro Uemura**, *On the Structure of the Domain of a Symmetric Jump-type Dirichlet Form*, Publications of the Research Institute for Mathematical Sciences 48 (2012), no. 1, 1–20. DOI: `10.2977/PRIMS/58`. Studies pure jump Dirichlet forms in the Beurling--Deny framework and their domains. WP-009 uses it as the standard geometric/Markov comparison class for the proposed Prime-Lattice conductance energy.
- **Gwo Dong Lin and Chin-Yuan Hu**, *The Riemann zeta distribution*, Bernoulli 7 (2001), no. 5, 817–828. Classical probability anchor for the fact that `zeta(sigma+it)/zeta(sigma)`, `sigma>1`, is an infinitely divisible characteristic function / compound-Poisson law generated by the Euler product. WP-009 differentiates the corresponding prime-power Lévy intensities in `sigma` and identifies the resulting coefficient measure at `sigma=1/2` with the exact WP-004 Mangoldt measure.
- **Takashi Nakamura and Masatoshi Suzuki**, *On infinitely divisible distributions related to the Riemann hypothesis*, Statistics & Probability Letters 201 (2023), Article 109889. DOI: `10.1016/j.spl.2023.109889`; arXiv: `2306.08317`. Constructs the completed function `g_zeta` and proves that `exp(g_zeta)` is an infinitely divisible characteristic function **if and only if RH holds**; under RH its Lévy measure is the atomic zero-ordinate measure `sum_gamma m_gamma/gamma^2 delta_{-gamma}`. WP-009 uses this as the decisive prior-art redirect showing that the known global Lévy completion is an RH-equivalent zero-supported criterion, not an independent geometric positivity theorem.

## Arakelov and arithmetic-divisor completion controls

- **Gérard Freixas i Montplet**, *The Riemann–Roch theorem in Arakelov geometry*, lecture notes (CNRS / Institut de Mathématiques de Jussieu). Section 1.2 gives the arithmetic Chow/Picard description for `Spec O_K`, with principal relation `(div α,(-log|σα|²)_σ)`. For `Spec Z` it gives `widehat{deg}(sum_p n_p[p],λ)=sum_p n_p log p+λ/2` and identifies the Arakelov class group of `Q` with `R` by degree. WP-006 uses these formulas to show that the canonical product-formula completion of every Prime-Lattice integer vector is a principal zero class.
- **José Ignacio Burgos Gil, Jürg Kramer, and Ulf Kühn**, *Cohomological arithmetic Chow rings*, Journal of the Institute of Mathematics of Jussieu 6 (2007), no. 1, 1–172. DOI: `10.1017/S1474748007000011`; arXiv: `math/0404122`. General arithmetic-Chow framework recovering Gillet–Soulé intersection theory and incorporating the archimedean fibre cohomologically. This anchors the novelty boundary for attempts to relabel ordinary arithmetic Chow/intersection machinery as a Mathia-native global positivity mechanism.
- **Alain Connes and Caterina Consani**, *On the Jacobian of the completed arithmetic curve `Spec Z`*, arXiv: `2602.15941` (17 February 2026 preprint). Interprets the Riemann sector of the rational adele class space as a natural monoidal extension of the Picard geometry of the completed arithmetic curve, with torsion-free rank-one groups and archimedean rigidifying data; the larger monoid incorporates singular strata relevant to spectral realization. WP-006 uses it as close current prior art for the principal escape from the class-triviality obstruction: enlarge the ordinary Picard object rather than expect its principal classes to carry the Weil mechanism.
- **Qing Liu**, *Algebraic Geometry and Arithmetic Curves*, Oxford Graduate Texts in Mathematics 6, Oxford University Press (2002), DOI: `10.1093/oso/9780198502845.001.0001`, especially Chapter 9, §1. Theorem 1.23 gives the negative-semidefinite intersection matrix of a closed fiber on a regular arithmetic surface; the whole fiber is the one-dimensional defect direction. WP-011 uses this to audit the canonical lift of a Prime-Lattice axis to the whole vertical fiber and the positive quotient on component differences.

## Function-field intersection comparison

- **Kiran S. Kedlaya**, *Weil cohomology in practice*, Chapter 5, §5.2, “RH via surfaces”, online lecture notes/book. Gives an audit-friendly presentation of Weil's second proof for curves over finite fields: the Hodge index theorem is applied on `C x C` to the diagonal and graphs of Frobenius, whose intersections encode point counts over finite extensions. WP-011 uses this as the decisive comparison showing that the successful function-field positivity lives on global correspondences, not on base-fiber classes.

## Screw-function / Green-primitive completion

- **Masatoshi Suzuki**, *Aspects of the screw function corresponding to the Riemann zeta-function*, Journal of the London Mathematical Society 108 (2023), no. 4, 1448–1487. DOI: `10.1112/jlms.12785`. Defines the explicit continuous `Psi(t)` whose finite term is `-sum_{n<=e^t} Lambda(n)/sqrt(n) (t-log n)`, proves that for `g=-Psi` its distributional second derivative recovers the Weil distribution, and proves that `g` is a Krein screw function exactly when RH holds. WP-007 uses this as the exact prior-art identification of the canonical second primitive of the WP-004 axis measure and as the decisive circularity test for increment-kernel positivity.
- **Masatoshi Suzuki**, *Weil's quadratic form via the screw function*, arXiv: `2606.09096` [math.NT], v2 (17 August 2026). Gives an operator-theoretic realization of the Weil quadratic form through the screw function: on a finite interval the symmetric nonlocal differential operator `B_a=D^*G_aD` has the localized Weil operator as its Friedrichs extension, and the paper studies related self-adjoint realizations. WP-007 uses this to rule out presenting the direct `D^*GD`/boundary packaging as a new independent Mathia positivity mechanism.

## Global geometric comparison

- **Alain Connes, Caterina Consani, and Matilde Marcolli**, *The Weil proof and the geometry of the adeles class space*, in *Algebra, Arithmetic, and Geometry*, Vol. I, Progress in Mathematics 269, Birkhäuser (2009/2010), 339–405; arXiv: `math/0703392`. ## Semigroup correspondences and endomotive prior art

- **Alain Connes, Caterina Consani, and Matilde Marcolli**, *Noncommutative geometry and motives: the thermodynamics of endomotives*, Advances in Mathematics 214 (2007), no. 2, 761–831. The characteristic-zero Frobenius analogue is the scaling action on cyclic homology of a cokernel, giving a cohomological spectral realization of zeta/L-function zeros; the paper also gives an archimedean Lefschetz formula. WP-012 uses this as the decisive prior-art redirect for enriching the raw Prime-Lattice shift into a nontrivial Frobenius/cohomological object.
- **Alain Connes, Caterina Consani, and Matilde Marcolli**, *Fun with F1*, Journal of Number Theory 129 (2009), no. 6, 1532–1561. DOI: `10.1016/j.jnt.2008.08.007`; arXiv: `0806.2401`.
