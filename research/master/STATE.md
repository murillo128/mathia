# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia has six source-backed research lines: `arithmetic_fidelity`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `weil_inertia`, and `weil_positivity`.

The portfolio-level picture is now more two-sided than a simple information-loss story. Arithmetic Fidelity shows both failure and recovery of fidelity inside explicit test categories. `AF-020`--`AF-023` establish complete-functional fidelity together with exact finite-test collision fibers, while `AF-028` proves that a countable structured family of compact double-positive modulation tests can determine the visible generalized-prime measure on a finite log-scale horizon. `AF-029` sharpens the discrete case: a lattice modulation family retains exactly the cosine quotient, with a sharp alias threshold `hB=pi`. Therefore neither positivity, countability, nor exact scalar knowledge is itself the decisive issue. The decisive object is the observable algebra induced by the actual admissible test family, together with its support/resolution scale and the source class on which injectivity is claimed.

The application lines now divide into two different kinds of narrowing. Prime Circle and the unweighted finite-horizon part of Prime Lattice are increasingly classicalized: canonical finite cotangent networks collapse to endpoint/confluent finite-state algebra, and fixed-support infinite depth remains rational by Cayley--Hamilton (`PC-096`--`PC-098`); unweighted prime-support sharp Grams inherit extreme instability from bounded prime clusters and, at the mean-gap scale, their second spectral moment is the classical Poisson/Hardy--Littlewood prediction (`PL-082`--`PL-083`). These are strong reasons not to interpret algebraic depth, low Gram moments, or extreme eigenvalues as new RH selectors without additional structure.

Prime Flute, Weil Inertia, and Weil Positivity instead retain source-specific structure but encounter genuinely different realization or assembly gates. `PF-143` shows that the remaining nonconstant collar trace-shape mode is not automatically made cheap by pinching, so the last wave-operator question is a coherent global welding problem rather than another local collar estimate. `WI-062`--`WI-066` show that diagonal conductor norms, arbitrary spectral pruning, one common refinement, and redundant common refinements all retain a super-polylogarithmic cost; at the same time `WI-064` identifies an exact divisor-martingale structure, and `WI-066` shows that the desired residue-summed vector control opens into a source-faithful four-prime rectangle problem. Weil Positivity has moved past the earlier selector-continuity obstruction: `WP-072`--`WP-074` give a positive pointed topology with the critical half-weight and positive `log n` defect, while `WP-076`--`WP-078` show that the most canonical semigroup, averaging, and Möbius completions either telescope/classicalize or recover exact Mangoldt support only by reintroducing indefiniteness. Its live problem is therefore completed sign compatibility, not merely finding a positive finite carrier.

The reusable portfolio rule is: **separate source fidelity, rational-prime specificity, destination realization, and local-to-global assembly, and test each in the exact category where the final claim lives**. These gates can interact, but current findings do not prove that they reduce to one theorem. The proposed global clue `CLUE-cross-line-fidelity-realization-separation` remains the appropriate place to test that possible unification. The current graph and Atlas are navigation only; their source revision lags the current canonical findings and they are not used as mathematical evidence here.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Fidelity has an exact resolution/alias geometry, not a finite-versus-infinite dichotomy.** `AF-028` shows that an infinite structured double-positive family can be finite-scale faithful, while `AF-029` identifies the precise quotient and Nyquist-like alias threshold for a discrete family. Prime Lattice independently shows that finite observation time turns logarithmic frequency spacing into a resolution problem. The productive transfer is to compute the exact observable quotient and support horizon before interpreting retained scalars as arithmetic information; the AF theorems do not automatically transfer as proofs to the Prime-Lattice source class.

2. **Finite algebraic enrichment and low-order spectral enrichment repeatedly classicalize.** Prime Circle now closes the canonical finite cotangent network even with loops, repeated shells, and fixed-support infinite depth (`PC-096`--`PC-098`). Prime Lattice routes sharp-Gram extremes to bounded prime clusters and the critical second moment to Hardy--Littlewood/Poisson spacing (`PL-082`--`PL-083`). These are different mechanisms, but together they argue against treating more finite algebraic depth or another low spectral moment as evidence of a new zero selector without a demonstrably new information channel.

3. **Realization/assembly has split into at least three concrete mathematical forms.** Prime Flute has a geometric interface problem: the remaining nonconstant boundary shape has no automatic collapse gain. Weil Inertia has a collective arithmetic-norm problem: useful cross-conductor martingale structure exists, but the needed vector estimate raises the correlation order. Weil Positivity has a sign/completion problem after finite selector continuity has already been repaired. The analogy is strategically useful, but no current finding proves these are instances of one abstract obstruction; that theorem-level question remains appropriately quarantined in the proposed global fidelity/realization clue.

4. **Matched controls remain strongest when placed at the exact claimed layer.** Prime Circle must beat controls after endpoint/confluent reduction; Prime Flute compares against the all-composite shift clone at global operator level; Prime Lattice must separate a candidate from universal resolution and classical prime-gap statistics; Weil Inertia must preserve the exact conditioned four-prime source geometry rather than substitute marginal pair estimates; Weil Positivity must retain the exact Mangoldt selector through the operation that is supposed to generate sign. Moving the control upstream or downstream weakens the conclusion.

5. **Prior-art saturation favors interface theorems over another named framework.** The current prior-art projection already covers the major Nyman/Beurling, explicit-formula/Weil, Connes/QSM, trace/scattering, Hardy/Toeplitz, and related classical families. The newest findings reinforce the same stopping rule: Prime Circle and Prime Lattice often land in classical finite-state or correlation theory, while Weil Inertia and Weil Positivity now require narrowly specified interfaces not supplied by the existing black boxes. A new line is not justified while those questions are honestly owned by current lines.

## Highest-leverage unresolved questions

- **Arithmetic Fidelity:** which intrinsic global/source constraints prevent arbitrary local test programming while leaving an observable family that is provably faithful on the rational-prime class, and what is its exact support/sampling alias boundary?
- **Prime Circle:** can a growing-state cross-level limit or genuinely different intrinsic operator produce a prime-specific invariant outside the finite cotangent/confluent endpoint algebra and outside matched finite-point controls?
- **Prime Flute:** are the canonical reflection-odd centered collar traces summable in one boundary-coherent global comparison, or can a transverse/radial welding meet the wave-operator criterion despite the `PF-143` angular lower bound?
- **Prime Lattice:** at `T~X/log X` or a finer target-relative/completed-Weil scale, is there an invariant not determined by the classical Hardy--Littlewood/gap hierarchy, prolate resolution, or the zero divisor supplied in advance?
- **Weil Inertia:** can one obtain a source-faithful hierarchical conditioned four-prime rectangle estimate that exploits the exact divisor martingale and closes the Yang welding splice without diagonal or common-refinement cost?
- **Weil Positivity:** can a nonseparable finite--archimedean/polar construction retain the canonical `Lambda(n)/sqrt(n)` selector while proving the completed sign before the known Möbius/Poisson/resolvent operations introduce indefiniteness?
