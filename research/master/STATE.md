# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia has six source-backed research lines: `arithmetic_fidelity`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `weil_inertia`, and `weil_positivity`.

The strongest current cross-line signal is more specific than “preserve information before compression.” The Arithmetic Fidelity line now exhibits several distinct fidelity layers: fiberwise recoverability and finite discernibility (`AF-001`, `AF-002`), invariant orbit-closure loss (`AF-003`), recovery of phase by higher-order coupling (`AF-004`), annihilator-lattice control of monomial phase lifts (`AF-005`), marked spectral recovery through eigenspace Gram data (`AF-006`), and a smooth vertical-rank obstruction (`AF-007`). Across the application lines, scalar, positive, absolute, local, or essential-spectrum compressions repeatedly classicalize or lose the claimed arithmetic discriminator, while signed/relative, higher-order, marked, atomic, nonseparable, or cross-level information remains the plausible place to look. This is a portfolio heuristic grounded in the listed findings, not a theorem that such richer data must succeed.

Canonical subtraction and completion are also proving useful as **falsifiers rather than solutions**. Prime Lattice canonically removes the PNT rank-one mode without removing essential recurrence (`PL-059`); Prime Circle isolates a universal Hilbert-channel essential core (`PC-075`); Prime Flute's repaired all-composite shift clone has compact relative resolvent and the same essential spectrum (`PF-125`); Weil Inertia removes the genuine four-prime local main and then the entire fixed-polylog coefficient regime (`WI-049`, `WI-050`). The live questions are therefore increasingly about what survives after the correct universal or local background is removed.

Prior-art saturation remains interface-specific, not literature-wide. The graph/Atlas is derived navigation/telemetry and currently lags the source frontier; it is not evidence for novelty, closure, or line priority.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Signed/relative/higher-order data are informative but not automatically sufficient.** `AF-004` proves that third-order coupling can recover phase lost at the quadratic layer. `PC-076` retains a von-Mangoldt-type relative trace, while `PC-077` already classicalizes the next trace to radical/divisor data. `WP-051` gives the complementary warning: even positive Schatten energies can erase the prime-power birth selector. The reusable lesson is to delay absolute-value/positive compression until the relevant cancellation or coupling has been audited.

2. **Correct background removal sharpens the residual problem.** `PC-075`, `PF-125`, `PL-059`, and `WI-049` each identify a canonical universal/local layer whose removal does not itself create the desired discriminator. The productive transfer is procedural: identify the forced background, subtract or match it canonically, and state the residual in the exact topology or normalization where arithmetic specificity is claimed.

3. **Repeated or local structure often factorizes; the remaining information is pushed toward nonseparable assembly.** `PC-078` makes repeated-prime Hardy refinement a tensor inflation of squarefree data; `WI-050` gains decisive averaging only after the locked variables are unsliced into a genuine three-variable finite-complexity prime system; `AF-004` shows that correlation order can change recoverability. These findings justify testing mixed/cross-level coupling before further scalar refinements, but do not imply that such coupling will succeed.

4. **Matched controls have reached operator/topology level.** `PF-125` shows an all-composite clone can match essential spectral behavior, while the Prime Lattice frontier requires any claimed atomic prime residual to survive Beurling/generalized-prime controls at the same resolution. `WP-048` similarly shows that selecting the correct real-place `q=2` channel from generic anchored geometry is not yet an arithmetic discriminator. Controls must therefore be matched to the exact information layer, not merely to a weaker precursor.

5. **Prior-art closure is now sharply localized.** Prime Circle's scalar projective-height, universal Hardy core, first local relative traces, and repeated-prime refinement are largely classicalized (`PC-074`--`PC-078`). Prime Flute has closed essential spectrum as a shift-clone discriminator and pushed the problem to finer relative ideals (`PF-125`--`PF-127`). Prime Lattice has closed spatial recentering, ad hoc PNT centering, sub-PNT moving smoothing, and an archimedean balance at PNT resolution (`PL-058`--`PL-061`). Weil Positivity has closed several natural local grading/positive/genus-zero completions (`WP-049`--`WP-053`). Weil Inertia's fixed-polylog unsliced four-prime regime falls under existing finite-complexity prime-pattern theory (`WI-050`). None of these scoped closures justifies a literature-wide exhaustion claim.
