# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The program has converged on one strong design constraint: **arithmetic information must survive the chosen quotient/compression long enough to enter a marked, relational, or otherwise information-preserving observable, and any claimed arithmetic singularity or positivity must then survive canonical control subtraction.** The global Mind supports this through repeated quotient, transport, local-jet, spectral-mark, determinant, and universal-background failures across Prime Circle and Prime Flute.

There are currently two distinct high-value routes rather than one unified mechanism:

- **Quantitative Weil inertia** is the most direct RH-facing line: it already yields an unconditional certified improvement for simple critical-line zeros and has exposed additional slack in the global trace/energy assembly.
- **Geometric information retention** (especially Prime Flute, with Prime Circle upstream) is the strongest Mathia-native structural route: resolved finite-surface spectral data can retain directed multiscale gap memory, but recurrent realization on actual prime blocks and a canonical global spectral container remain unresolved.

Prime Lattice and Weil Positivity have been valuable mainly by locating boundaries. Prime Lattice repeatedly shows that ambient Bohr/Hardy, multiplicative-flow, generator-Gram, and several geometric/spectral encodings either reproduce known RH-equivalent structures or lose the off-line-zero information. Weil Positivity shows that many natural positive local/geometric constructions have the wrong support, sign, operator ideal, or global completion. Their remaining value is therefore in **narrow residual questions**, not continued horizontal enumeration of positive-looking or spectral-looking reformulations.

The main shared bottleneck is now precise: derive a **canonical global operation that preserves the arithmetic distinction exposed locally while producing the analytic/sign structure needed by RH, without inserting that structure by hand or recovering only a classical equivalent criterion.**

## Cross-line structure

### Preserve information before scalar/global compression

Prime Circle and Prime Flute provide the clearest repeated mechanism. Unmarked shells, path products, local jets, unmarked spectra and scalar determinants can all erase distinctions still present in the underlying prime-derived geometry. Conversely, cross-ratios, endpoint spectral measures and resolved individual modes can remain injective or retain directed multiscale memory. This supports the global rule that the fibers of every proposed compression must be understood before its analytic features are interpreted.

Prime Lattice reaches the same principle in a different category: the Nyman generator Gram matrix is unchanged when the common off-line-zero Blaschke factor is removed, so generator-only spectral conditioning cannot detect the RH obstruction. The missing information sits in the distinguished target/model-space component. Thus the common issue is not “geometry versus analysis”; it is **which marked relation survives the representation being used**.

### Positivity is not inherited from positive-looking local data

Weil Positivity repeatedly finds that local/nonnegative ingredients do not assemble into the required global Weil form. WP-014 is especially sharp: the exact Schiffer kernel has positive entries yet every nontrivial two-point Gram matrix is indefinite, while its standard positive repairs lie in the wrong Schatten class. Prime Lattice supplies a parallel warning: finite prime-axis weights can match the finite Weil coefficient without their positivity surviving the autocorrelation/global lift.

This materially narrows the global positivity program. A successful route must derive the sign at the **correct global level**; it cannot be obtained by attaching a standard positive completion to a local prime encoding.

### Weil Inertia is complementary, not a weaker copy of Weil Positivity

The inertia line does not require construction of a globally positive form. It extracts quantitative information from rank, trace, Gram defect and spectral convexity even when the full positivity problem remains unavailable. WI-011's unconditional improvement demonstrates that this weaker requirement is currently more productive numerically. The strategic consequence is to keep `weil_inertia` separate and let it probe how much RH-relevant information can be certified from controlled indefiniteness/defect while `weil_positivity` searches for a genuinely geometric positivity theorem.

### Universal carriers must be subtracted before arithmetic interpretation

Prime Circle/Flute controls show that propagation thresholds, conformal kernels and other striking features can exist before prime sampling or survive integer controls. Weil Positivity shows the analogous danger for generic positive constructions. Across the program, arithmetic significance begins only at the residual that changes under a sufficiently matched non-prime/control construction.

## Highest-leverage unresolved questions

1. **Weil inertia global optimization.** Before investing in larger local certificates, determine the sharp or materially sharper global variational/dual assembly for the already proved four-point Gram constraint. WI-011 gives an explicit baseline that any improved assembly must beat.
2. **Prime-flute recurrence/resolution bridge.** Close the gap between the finite graded-chain upstream-memory theorem and actual recurrent prime-derived surface families, either through quantitative prime-block recurrence or a stronger surface-to-graph resolution theorem.
3. **Weil-positivity escape from passive positivity.** Any surviving construction must identify a specific canonical operation outside bounded/trace-class Schiffer repair and passive local-energy completion, and prove both why it is geometrically forced and why its positivity is independent of RH.
4. **Prime-lattice target/model-space coupling.** The discrete Nyman frontier should focus on information not invariant under common inner multiplication: target cross-correlations, model-space projections, or an equivalent distinguished observable capable of detecting the Blaschke obstruction.
5. **Canonical control depth.** For any new geometric/spectral signal, identify the first matched control (regular, density-matched, gap-marginal, short-block-preserving) that removes it; this determines what relational depth the observable actually detects.

## Portfolio changes proposed

### Narrow `prime_circle`

Stop treating further scalar/projective/cyclotomic reformulations as independent routes unless they pass the known fiber and matched-control tests. Keep the line centered on exact nonprojective, nonlocal or multi-path observables that survive the established collapses. Reverse this recommendation if a currently excluded simple observable is proved injective on the relevant prime-derived family and survives matched controls.

### Narrow `prime_lattice`

The line has already mapped a large prior-art/obstruction region. Future work should concentrate on observables that retain the distinguished RH-sensitive component rather than ambient Bohr boundaries, bare Kronecker spectra, generator-only Gram data, or positive coefficients that are lost under global completion. Reverse this recommendation if one of those broad ambient structures acquires a new canonical ingredient that demonstrably distinguishes the off-line-zero factor.

### Narrow `weil_positivity`

The accumulated exact negatives make another passive positive-energy ansatz low-value. Restrict the line to mechanisms that genuinely change category: singular/dynamical propagation, cohomological/quotient constructions, boundary response, or a global coupling whose sign theorem is intrinsic and whose arithmetic sector has the right operator scale. Reverse this recommendation if a simpler currently known Mathia object is shown to evade the explicit support/sign/Schatten obstructions.

There is currently **no supported `merge-candidate`, `split-candidate`, `pause-candidate`, or `new-line-candidate`**. The present five lines still correspond to distinct mathematical information channels, and each proposed narrowing has a concrete surviving discriminator.
