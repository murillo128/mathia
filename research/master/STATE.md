# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia has six source-backed research lines: `arithmetic_fidelity`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `weil_inertia`, and `weil_positivity`.

The strongest portfolio-level pattern is now a three-gate distinction: **fidelity, specificity, and realization must all be checked in the actual destination category**. Arithmetic Fidelity makes the first gate explicit. `AF-020` proves that the complete Weil test functional is faithful to the generalized-prime norm multiset, with compact log-support giving only a finite arithmetic horizon. `AF-021`--`AF-023` then show that replacing a complete test family by finitely many scalar tests creates exact collision fibers; at every regular generalized-prime point with more movable generators than tests, the same-test fiber has positive dimension. Exact relational or analytic information therefore does not survive an arbitrary finite compression merely because the retained numbers are known exactly.

The application lines repeatedly meet the same failure in different forms. Prime Circle's canonical nonlinear cotangent constructions collapse to gauge, endpoint, rational, or endpoint-displacement data (`PC-087`--`PC-092`). Prime Lattice's pointed critical Gram exposes classical additive correlations and its local sharp-window spectrum is the classical prolate operator (`PL-074`--`PL-078`). Weil Positivity has an exact Mangoldt anchor, but that anchor is unbounded in the natural positive Hardy topology and cannot be made finite-energy by positive extensions that leave the finite Hardy sector unchanged (`WP-067`--`WP-071`). Prime Flute and Weil Inertia instead retain credible source-specific information but are blocked at global assembly: a boundary-coherent operator comparison in the former and a collective conditioned covariance splice in the latter.

The reusable portfolio rule is therefore stronger than "preserve structure before compressing": **after every quotient, scalarization, positivity operation, local model, or Fourier truncation, exhibit the exact retained information and the topology/norm in which the final claim must live, then compare against a matched control in that same layer**. The current graph and Atlas remain navigation only; their telemetry lags the current canonical findings and is not used as mathematical evidence.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Finite compression has become an explicit mathematical gate.** `AF-020`--`AF-023` separate complete-function fidelity, scale horizon, and finite-dimensional resolution. This transfers as a falsification discipline to Prime Circle, Prime Lattice, and Weil Positivity: before interpreting a finite scalar, trace, Gram block, or positive pairing as arithmetic structure, compute its collision/gauge fibers in the actual admissible source class. The transfer is methodological; the AF fiber theorems are not automatically proofs of collision in another category.

2. **Natural local enrichment repeatedly moves arithmetic into classical or endpoint data instead of creating a new selector.** `PC-087`--`PC-092` successively reduce canonical nonlinear shell constructions to gauge, endpoints, rational contractions, or endpoint-displacement form. `PL-074`--`PL-078` route pointed/fixed-lag/global-smoothed/local-sharp Gram behavior to Mirsky--Chowla, Hardy--Littlewood, pair correlation, Gallagher variance, and prolate bandlimiting. This is negative convergence across different objects, not evidence that the lines should merge.

3. **Topology and norm are part of the arithmetic claim.** Prime Flute has finite local thin-collar cost but still needs one global operator category (`PF-138`). Weil Inertia has an `L^2`-compressible spectrum whose `l^1` cost is prohibitive (`WI-060`). Weil Positivity has an algebraically exact selector that is discontinuous in the natural positive topology (`WP-068`--`WP-071`). Prime Circle likewise distinguishes finite scalar convergence from trace/operator significance. A candidate that changes topology must prove that the arithmetic selector survives the change rather than assuming continuity.

4. **Matched controls must live at the same layer as the claimed mechanism.** Prime Circle requires a control after the same gauge/endpoint reductions; Prime Flute compares against the all-composite shift clone at the operator-assembly level; Prime Lattice must distinguish arithmetic correlations from universal Fourier-resolution geometry; Weil Inertia needs residue-conditioned pair information rather than a marginal prime estimate; Weil Positivity must preserve the exact Mangoldt readout through the positivity operation. This is the strongest productive cross-line transfer currently available.

5. **Prior-art saturation favors interface-specific work over another named framework.** The repository already covers the standard Nyman/Beurling, Weil/explicit-formula, Connes, trace/scattering, Toeplitz/Hardy, circulant, and related classical routes. The live questions above are not missing-framework questions: they are precise source-fidelity, cross-conductor, topology, or global-assembly tests. A new line is not justified unless such a question cannot honestly be owned by an existing line.
