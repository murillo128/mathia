# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia currently has six source-backed research lines: `arithmetic_fidelity`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `weil_inertia`, and `weil_positivity`.

The strongest current program-level refinement is that **exact information survival and stable usable survival are separate gates**. Arithmetic Fidelity now extends its quotient/interaction picture through arbitrary dependent channel families (`AF-038`), shows that even uniformly good pairwise channel geometry need not control multichannel stability (`AF-040`), and identifies stable composition with a range--kernel transversality condition (`AF-041`). This sharpens the global audit to

`retained support -> collective/compositional stability -> rational-prime specificity -> destination realization -> global/source-faithful assembly`.

The distinction matters because the application lines now fail at different stages rather than through one generic information-loss mechanism. Prime Circle has pushed finite/fixed-shell Hardy structure through cyclotomic-period and trace-class determinant closures: `PC-107` rules out Riemann-compatible zero density for any fixed-shell trace-class Fredholm determinant, and `PC-110` shows that the canonical off-origin Carleman--Hilbert microlocal defect is itself trace class, so the natural `det_2` recentering does not escape that density obstruction. Prime Lattice similarly classicalizes ordinary growing-depth prime-power Gram spectra through the first `sqrt(log X)` population transition: `PL-093` makes the first `K^2` repair trace-norm equivalent to an unweighted envelope Gram, and `PL-095` pushes that envelope to deterministic PNT universality below the scale that resolves prime-counting error.

Prime Flute and Weil Inertia remain live for different reasons. In Prime Flute, local geometry/operator budgets are increasingly controlled but the global clone comparison is not closed. `PF-148` corrects an earlier overstatement: a global squared-resolvent `S_1` theorem would already yield an ordinary Krein/Birman--Krein scattering phase by invariance, while `PF-151` shows that the most canonical critical first-resolvent residue is only hyperbolic area. Thus the decisive question is global assembly and then the **value** of a source-specific relative invariant, not the mere existence of a scattering formalism. In Weil Inertia, the obstruction is source-faithful correlation structure: after the withdrawal of `WI-067` and replacement by corrected `WI-068`, `WI-076` proves that the weighted scalar LCM projection is not power-sparse, and `WI-078` shows that positive-weight additive energy remains maximal even under mass-preserving pruning. The surviving scalar route must therefore exploit signed centered weights, labels, or another genuinely source-specific theorem rather than cardinality or positive-energy sparsity.

Weil Positivity has also narrowed materially. `WP-086` shows that every fixed bounded norm-continuous positive two-projection calculus has only linear-or-bounded trace scale on the canonical cover-angle family; logarithmic degree appears only at a singular endpoint. `WP-089` then closes the canonical finite--global coherent coupling escape: refinement-coherent finite auxiliary coupling freezes to one fixed collective block, while fixed local-strength repetition is incompatible with positivity at large degree. The residual question must therefore leave the bounded/coherent finite calculus through a geometrically forced singular, infinite, noncoherent, higher-cohomological, or genuinely nonseparable finite--archimedean construction with its own sign theorem.

The existing proposed global clue `research/clues/CLUE-cross-line-fidelity-realization-separation.md` still owns the theorem-level question of whether fidelity, realization, and assembly admit one exact cross-line framework. The new stability results sharpen the evidence for that question but do not yet prove a cross-line theorem, so no duplicate clue is created. The current Atlas/graph remains useful only for structural navigation: its source revision and some local graph hubs lag the newest canonical findings/mind synthesis, so its telemetry is not used as evidence for the recommendations below.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Stability is now a reusable gate distinct from exact fidelity.** `AF-040`--`AF-041` show that exact support survival, pairwise conditioning, and stable multistage reconstruction are not interchangeable. This is a productive audit for Prime Flute global assembly and Weil Inertia multi-source restriction, but no theorem transfer is asserted until those lines instantiate the relevant spaces/operators.

2. **Prime Circle and Prime Lattice now exhibit parallel classicalization at different categories.** Prime Circle is blocked by operator-ideal/nuclear zero-density constraints; Prime Lattice by normalized trace-norm and PNT-envelope universality. The shared strategic lesson is to stop enlarging ordinary bounded/fixed-scale spectral summaries, but the mathematical mechanisms are different and do not justify merging the lines.

3. **Prime Flute and Weil Inertia are complementary global-assembly problems.** Prime Flute asks whether locally controlled geometric/operator differences assemble on an infinite surface; Weil Inertia asks whether a locked weighted prime source admits a theorem strong enough to survive restriction/conditioning. Both warn that good local or pairwise estimates need not imply a usable global object, but one is operator-geometric and the other arithmetic-correlation theoretic.

4. **Prime Circle and Weil Positivity both push surviving ideas toward singular/global categories for different reasons.** Prime Circle's bounded compact residuals are spectrally too sparse; Weil Positivity's bounded positive finite calculus has the wrong degree scale or freezes under coherence. A category change is therefore credible only when its singular/global structure is independently forced and its destination theorem remains available.

5. **Prior-art saturation favors exact interface lemmas over new broad frameworks.** Current closures repeatedly reduce to classical fusion/Friedrichs geometry, trace ideals and Fredholm theory, Carleman--Hilbert models, PNT envelopes, prime-pattern theorem boundaries, additive-energy inequalities, and Halmos two-projection calculus. The highest-value remaining work is to prove or falsify the exact source/category interface already exposed by each line rather than open another representation family on analogy alone.

## Highest-leverage unresolved questions

- **Arithmetic Fidelity:** when several forced channels are individually exact/stable, what source-dependent collective lower bound or range--kernel angle controls the full asymptotic reconstruction, and can it be computed before completion?
- **Prime Circle:** can a genuinely all-shell operator forced by cross-level incidence/provenance leave the trace-class/nuclear and cumulative-Fourier quotients while remaining rational-prime specific under matched controls?
- **Prime Flute:** can one globalize the interface or squared-resolvent estimates for the prime/shift clone, and if so does the resulting relative scattering/spectral-shift value distinguish them intrinsically?
- **Prime Lattice:** is there a canonical non-Lipschitz/target-relative/completed observable that survives both the growing-depth trace-norm reduction and the PNT-envelope regime before simply becoming a known zero diagnostic?
- **Weil Inertia:** can the centered signed Yang weights or retained slope labels produce cancellation unavailable to the positive-support/positive-energy controls, with a theorem at the exact locked source normalization?
- **Weil Positivity:** what geometrically forced operation can create the required moving logarithmic/global scale without bounded finite-calculus freezing or loss of positivity, and simultaneously couple the archimedean/polar sector?
