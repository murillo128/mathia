## Global picture

Mathia has six source-backed research lines: `arithmetic_fidelity`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `weil_inertia`, and `weil_positivity`.

The strongest current portfolio-level pattern is that **the retained category determines what arithmetic information is still available**. Arithmetic Fidelity sharpens this directly: `AF-018` shows that divisor data can become faithful only after independently fixing a rigid order/reflection/normalization class, while `AF-019` separates the exact logarithmic derivative from its principal parts — the former retains the zero-free factor modulo scale, the latter is only divisor data. The application lines exhibit the same distinction in their own categories: scalar convergence versus trace class in Prime Circle, compact-relative-resolvent versus stronger wave/Schatten information in Prime Flute, zero replay versus a genuinely upstream selector in Prime Lattice, marginal distribution versus source-conditioned covariance in Weil Inertia, and signed arithmetic selection versus polar/determinant positivity in Weil Positivity.

A second repeated obstruction is **gauge or universal symmetry at unpointed level**. `AF-014` identifies the residual orientation gauge of full-rank Gram compression, while `PL-073` proves that Möbius signs are exactly a torus translation/diagonal unitary gauge for unpointed square-free statistics. The reusable lesson is not generic symmetry breaking: a proposed discriminator must make explicit which target, basepoint, non-unimodular reference, conditioning, or relational datum survives the quotient and fails for a matched control.

The current bottlenecks are increasingly global and source-faithful rather than local. Prime Flute has controlled the canonical one-dimensional/pre-corner pieces down to the true ambient thin part (`PF-137`); Weil Inertia has positive-power fiber control but still lacks the exact conditioned covariance at the necessary effective conductor scale (`WI-054`--`WI-059`); Weil Positivity has now closed the entire natural self-adjoint left-metric repair class by showing that even unbounded repairs collapse to polar sign times positive commuting weight (`WP-063`--`WP-065`). Prior-art saturation is therefore interface-specific. The graph/Atlas remains navigation only and is not current evidence for novelty, closure, or priority.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Full information must be audited in the actual destination category.** `AF-018`--`AF-019` sharpen the distinction between divisor, rigid analytic-class, and exact logarithmic-derivative information. Prime Circle's corrected `PC-086` makes an analogous scalar/operator distinction: conditional scalar convergence at word length one does not turn the Hilbert channel into trace class. Prime Flute and Weil Positivity likewise show that adjacent operator categories can preserve radically different information.

2. **Gauge is a reusable obstruction only after the observable is specified.** `AF-014` and `PL-073` show exact loss under orientation/diagonal gauge for unpointed outputs, but neither rules out pointed or relational observables. This transfers as a design gate: expose the target/basepoint/conditioning explicitly and then test invariance, rather than claiming that symmetry breaking alone creates arithmetic specificity.

3. **Finite, unpointed, or symmetric enrichment repeatedly classicalizes rather than selects zeros.** Prime Circle's finite Hardy sector now reduces to cyclotomic cone periods, trace-class finite words, universal common-depth inflation, and no Abel spectral parameter (`PC-082`, `PC-084`--`PC-086`). Prime Lattice's second-scale/hull/resonance/positive-cone/sign enrichments become RH diagnostics, classical harmonic/Dirichlet objects, or gauge (`PL-068`--`PL-073`). Weil Positivity's natural metric repairs collapse to the polar absolute-value mechanism (`WP-063`--`WP-065`). These are reasons to narrow toward genuinely relational structure, not evidence that the lines should merge.

4. **Local control is no longer the main missing ingredient in several lines.** `PF-137` leaves a true-thin, globally assembled 2D operator problem after canonical local pieces are controlled. `WI-054`--`WI-059` leave a source-conditioned covariance problem after substantial fiber and conductor localization. `WP-065` says local repair flexibility inside the self-adjoint metric category is exhausted. The next decisive progress must control the exact global/source-faithful assembly, or leave the exhausted category.

5. **Prior-art saturation is concentrated on the classicalized interfaces.** Prime Circle is saturated along finite Hilbert/Hankel/cyclotomic reductions, Prime Lattice along Kronecker/Dirichlet/unpointed harmonic reductions, and Weil Positivity along polar/Krein metric repairs. Weil Inertia instead has strong nearby distribution technology whose hypotheses do not match the locked conditioned interface; Prime Flute's remaining obstacle is a global 2D thin-assembly theorem rather than another standard one-dimensional estimate.
