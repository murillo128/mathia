# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia currently has seven source-backed research lines: `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `weil_inertia`, and `weil_positivity`.

The strongest current program-level audit is now

`retained source information -> equivalence-compatible target -> quantitative information budget -> matched-control/source specificity -> stable realization -> global/sign assembly`.

Arithmetic Fidelity sharpens the first two gates. `AF-052` shows that a Blackwell-equivalent, total-variation-isometric output refinement can still change distance to an unrestricted structural target when the target gains repairs that do not descend through the equivalence. `AF-053` closes the permissive finite version: if null output symbols are presentation gauge, the Blackwell-invariant TV repair radius to unrestricted zero-error fidelity is exactly Bayes risk. Thus an intrinsic robustness claim requires not only an intrinsic metric/topology but an admissible target family that transforms compatibly with the declared representation equivalence.

Möbius Cancellation adds a distinct quantitative gate. `MC-001`--`MC-005` show that current almost-all local cancellation, one standard pretentious scalar, the natural Möbius--Liouville prime-power transfer, qualitative fixed-shift pseudorandomness without multiplicativity, and multiplicativity plus exact square-free support plus qualitative mean cancellation all fail to force a fixed polynomial global saving. The surviving question is not whether these structures are relevant, but which jointly retained signed, prime-local, correlation, or multiscale datum has enough quantitative strength to survive the final summation norm.

Across the RH-facing geometric/operator lines, several natural escapes have now been reduced to classical or generic boundary models. Prime Circle's canonical Hardy corrector gives only zero-free Gaussian `det_2` limits under bounded scalar scaling, while divergent real scalar amplification destroys local finiteness (`PC-117`--`PC-120`); the direct coherent cross-shell Toeplitz lift has an extensive polynomial syzygy, reaches only the classical cyclotomic resultant at its last nonsingular section, and is then exactly singular (`PC-121`--`PC-122`). Prime Flute's canonical relative Selberg separator sector is zero-free through the critical line, and `PF-159` localizes its sharp quarter-plane convergence wall entirely to one-ended propagation rather than collective prime-tail interaction. Prime Lattice now has deterministic prolate/Nyquist and Poisson bulk controls, universal Bruhat--Tits/Ihara half-axis controls, and `PL-106` shows that the recent zeta-DQPT architecture is a programmable Dirichlet-series readout rather than an independent zero-selection law. Weil Inertia's scalar finite-window quotient is exact and `WI-088`--`WI-089` confine the worst pairwise rank defects to sparse boundary layers rather than a dense sign-producing mechanism. Weil Positivity's finite-mass positive completion necessarily uses mixed-prime modes; `WP-098`--`WP-099` rule out both same-algebra positive deletion and passive positive auxiliary elimination as ways to recover the sparse selector.

These results reinforce one common strategy without identifying one common theorem: **classify and match the universal boundary model before calling the residual arithmetic**, then demand a source-forced invariant whose quantitative strength, target semantics, topology/operator ideal, and global sign all survive in the same category. The prior-art projection remains navigation/provenance rather than mathematical evidence; the current boundaries repeatedly land in classical comparison-of-experiments, Selberg--Delange, trace-ideal, resultant/Toeplitz, Selberg, Dirichlet-series, Loewner/Bezout, and positive-shortening machinery, so new broad analogical wrappers have lower value than exact interface theorems.

The adversarial handoff on power-cancellation-aware pretentiousness is reconciled by `MC-003`; its clue is resolved rather than evidence of an open bootstrap. The previously withdrawn `WI-067` remains excluded in favor of corrected `WI-068`. No portfolio conclusion below relies on graph topology or frontier telemetry as evidence.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Target semantics are part of fidelity.** `AF-052`--`AF-053` show that representation-equivalent objects can have different distances to a structural target unless the target descends under the equivalence. This is distinct from ordinary norm stability and must be audited whenever a line unfolds, stabilizes, quotients, adds auxiliaries, or changes presentation.

2. **Quantitative information budget is a separate gate from qualitative fidelity.** `MC-001`--`MC-005` supply the clearest exact controls: substantial local, multiplicative, support, or correlation structure may still carry only logarithmic or exceptional-set strength after global aggregation. RH relevance requires the transfer inequality itself to retain polynomial-scale information.

3. **Canonical escapes repeatedly classicalize after the missing scale is resolved.** Gaussian trace-ideal limits, polynomial/resultant Toeplitz collapse, one-ended Selberg propagation, prolate/Poisson sampling, programmable Dirichlet readout, finite-window alias quotients, sparse pairwise boundary layers, and positive shorting are different mechanisms, but each removes a tempting source-agnostic explanation before arithmetic significance can be assigned.

4. **Source specificity and global sign remain scarce and separate.** Prime Flute retains local geometric information without a decisive global relative value; Weil Inertia retains source structure without the needed fourth-moment theorem; Weil Positivity retains exact finite coefficients only inside a mixed positive completion whose final sign coupling is unresolved. Exact local recovery does not imply realizability or assembly.

5. **Prior-art saturation favors interface theorems.** New work should target the first source-specific residual after the strongest matched control, with its target, topology, normalization, quantitative budget, and global assembly fixed independently of the desired zero set.
