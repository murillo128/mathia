# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia currently has six source-backed research lines: `arithmetic_fidelity`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `weil_inertia`, and `weil_positivity`.

The strongest current program-level refinement is that **robust information survival is meaningful only after both the failure criterion and the destination topology are fixed**. Arithmetic Fidelity now has exact distance-to-loss formulas in several categories. `AF-043`--`AF-045` identify closed secants / Lipschitz molecules as the exact stable-fidelity carriers and show that the lower fidelity modulus is the sharp perturbation radius to collision. `AF-046` then gives the complementary boundary: in the same total-variation geometry, equality of conflicting output laws has a positive sharp margin while zero-error support fidelity has radius zero. `AF-047` shows that repair to exact zero-error fidelity is Bayes error plus a Hall-type coverage penalty. The global audit is therefore better written as

`retained support -> exact loss criterion/topology -> collective or perturbative stability -> rational-prime specificity -> destination realization -> global/source-faithful assembly`.

This sharper audit aligns with a second cross-line convergence: several natural attempts to escape a failed category now land on **universal boundary models** rather than arithmetic-specific structure. Prime Circle's canonical Hardy conductor escape is now closed much further. `PC-114` recovers the Hilbert--Schmidt mass hidden by strong convergence as an exact-order Ramanujan reflection tensor times the already-universal trace-class Carleman--Hilbert defect. `PC-115` shows that growing prime multiplicity has only a noncompact reflection limit, while the canonical Hilbert--Schmidt normalization has a zero-free Gaussian determinant. `PC-116` removes the iterated-limit loophole: along every joint two-prime path, the scalar-normalized finite corrector again tends in determinant to the same zero-free Gaussian. The surviving Hardy question is no longer another scalar conductor limit or determinant of the canonical corrector; it must reorganize conductors non-scalarly, act before the `PC-113` split, or introduce another intrinsic cross-level family.

Prime Lattice has reached an analogous but different sampling boundary. `PL-096` gives all-horizon `S_2` universality, `PL-097`--`PL-099` identify the raw `S_1` phase with finite-rank/prolate Nyquist geometry, and `PL-100`--`PL-101` now supply deterministic midpoint controls at exact Nyquist and throughout every shrinking near-critical regime `T_N Delta/(2 pi N) -> 1`. Thus bare trace-norm convergence, failure, ordered-spectrum convergence, or positive Fredholm-determinant convergence around the coarse Nyquist transition is not rational-prime evidence. Any surviving Gram route must measure an excess beyond the sharp sampling control or use target-relative, inverse/hard-edge, singular, or completed-indefinite data.

Weil Inertia has also crossed a decisive scalar boundary. `WI-082` proves that before the combined primitive-frequency dictionary becomes overcomplete, finite-window Ramanujan mixing cannot remove even one signed inertia direction: the full sign ledger is fixed by Vandermonde congruence. `WI-083` shows that after saturation the opposite extreme is possible: for prime moduli larger than the window every scalar Ramanujan block collapses to `p I-J`, and three such blocks admit exact nontrivial signed cancellation. Hence generic scalar finite-window geometry supplies neither a universal sign obstruction nor a universal sign gain. A viable scalar continuation must exploit restrictions imposed by the **actual Yang coefficients/moduli**, not merely Ramanujan-block geometry; otherwise the source must remain labelled, two-dimensional, or multivariate.

Weil Positivity has narrowed on both the global and nonlocal positive sides. `WP-092` extends the coherent-coupling freeze from finite auxiliaries to arbitrary fixed Hilbert sectors with unbounded self-adjoint dynamics: the relative coupled/decoupled response still lives in one fixed collective block and is degree-flat. `WP-093` classifies the fixed-finite-band non-diagonal exact-cover-covariant positive cone; its critical ray is a genuine positive supplier, but it classicalizes to weighted Dirichlet/Cesaro and continuous-dual-Hahn structure and is prime-blind. `WP-094` closes the canonical stationary infinite-range escape: positive block-Toeplitz exact cover covariance collapses to singular endpoint evaluation, and every nonzero survivor is nonclosable in the ambient `ell^2` topology. The remaining route must therefore be refinement-dependent/nonstationary, genuinely domain-changing or singular with a forced sign, higher-cohomological/twisted, or nonseparable finite--archimedean before scalarization.

Prime Flute remains at the previously isolated global boundary: local metric/interface and squared-resolvent estimates are strong, and `PF-153`--`PF-155` show that higher resolvent powers, natural `det_2`, and the complete local heat tower do not create independent arithmetic data. The decisive work is still to close or obstruct the global clone assembly and, if it closes, compute a genuinely nonlocal relative value that separates the prime flute from the exact composite control.

The existing proposed global clue `research/clues/CLUE-cross-line-fidelity-realization-separation.md` still owns the theorem-level question of whether fidelity, realization, and assembly admit one exact reusable framework. The new Arithmetic Fidelity results sharpen what such a framework must parameterize — in particular the loss set and topology — but do not yet prove a cross-line theorem, so no duplicate clue is warranted. Current review state supplies no unresolved `*.review.md` sidecar, and the previously withdrawn `WI-067` remains excluded in favor of corrected `WI-068`. ## Cross-line structure

1. **Stability is criterion-relative, not merely norm-relative.** `AF-043`--`AF-046` show that a stable-fidelity modulus can be an exact distance to a closed loss set, but a different structural loss event in the same ambient topology can have zero radius. Cross-line claims about robustness must therefore name both the topology/operator ideal and the exact failure set; small norm error or good pairwise separation is not a substitute for structural fidelity.

2. **Canonical boundary escapes repeatedly classicalize after the missing scale is resolved.** Prime Circle resolves strongly invisible conductor mass into Ramanujan reflection and a universal Carleman--Hilbert channel; Prime Lattice resolves the trace-class boundary into deterministic prolate/Nyquist sampling; Weil Inertia resolves signed scalar finite windows into Vandermonde/overcompleteness and, in a large-prime sector, the two-dimensional span `I,J`; Weil Positivity resolves exact cover covariance into Cesaro/dual-Hahn or singular endpoint models. These are distinct theorems, not one common operator, but strategically they demand the same next test: subtract or match the canonical boundary model before assigning arithmetic meaning to the residual.

3. **Source specificity is increasingly the scarce variable.** Prime Circle's two-scale spectrum changes only through exact-order multiplicity, Prime Lattice's coarse Nyquist behavior is reproduced by midpoint grids, Weil Inertia's unrestricted saturated scalar blocks permit exact cancellation, and Weil Positivity's positive cover forms exist for every integer degree. The surviving questions must use restrictions that are forced by the rational primes or by the exact source theorem, not merely by a natural representation of them.

4. **Global assembly and sign remain different terminal gates.** Prime Flute retains source-specific local geometry but still lacks a global clone comparison/value theorem. Weil Positivity retains exact finite selectors and independently positive suppliers but loses the required global logarithmic/sign response under fixed coherent or stationary assembly. Weil Inertia similarly has source-faithful locked structure but lacks a theorem strong enough in the required overcomplete/correlation regime. Exact local information does not imply that the destination category can assemble it with the needed sign or norm.

5. **Prior-art saturation now favors interface theorems over new analogical frameworks.** The active boundaries repeatedly land on classical secant/Lipschitz-free geometry, Bayes/Hall assignment, Ramanujan and Vandermonde structure, Carleman--Hilbert trace ideals, prolate sampling, Herglotz/Toeplitz positivity, Cesaro operators, and continuous dual Hahn polynomials. The highest-value work is to prove or falsify the exact source-specific interface exposed by each line rather than introduce another broad representation before the existing control has been beaten.

## Highest-leverage unresolved questions

- **Arithmetic Fidelity:** in the operator/quotient/asymptotic categories used by the RH-facing lines, what is the exact closed loss set and ambient perturbation topology, and does the corresponding distance-to-loss remain positive under global composition or limiting assembly?
- **Prime Circle:** after `PC-114`--`PC-116`, is there a canonical non-scalar or pre-decomposition all-shell Hardy observable whose nonzero spectral data changes with rational-prime cross-level structure after Ramanujan/Carleman controls are matched?
- **Prime Flute:** can the local interface/squared-resolvent estimates be globalized for the prime/shift clone, and if so does the resulting nonlocal relative value or continuation distinguish them intrinsically?
- **Prime Lattice:** what canonical microscopic, inverse, target-relative, singular, or completed statistic has nonzero excess over the deterministic prolate/Nyquist control and also separates rational primes from generalized-prime or Poisson controls?
- **Weil Inertia:** do the exact Yang coefficients and admissible modulus geometry forbid the saturated cancellations exhibited by `WI-083` strongly enough to yield a quantitative signed finite-window theorem, or must the locked slope information remain explicitly labelled/multivariate?
- **Weil Positivity:** can a refinement-dependent/nonstationary or higher-cohomological finite--archimedean coupling evade both the collective-block freezing of `WP-092` and the universal/singular cover-positive classifications of `WP-093`--`WP-094` while retaining an independently proved sign?
