## Global picture

Mathia has six source-backed research lines: `arithmetic_fidelity`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `weil_inertia`, and `weil_positivity`.

The strongest current portfolio-level conclusion is that **arithmetic fidelity is relative to the destination category and to the matched controls that survive the same map**. Arithmetic Fidelity now makes that precise for finite statistical experiments: `AF-012` identifies zero loss for any strictly convex binary `f`-divergence with likelihood-ratio sufficiency, and `AF-013` upgrades this to finite experiment sufficiency through the vector likelihood-ratio partition. This is a useful audit principle for the other lines: before interpreting a quotient, positive form, spectral invariant, or topology as arithmetic evidence, ask whether the whole relevant control family remains distinguishable after that exact transformation.

The application lines increasingly support the same negative discipline in different categories. Prime Circle has pushed every finite Hardy-shell essential-spectrum construction into a universal Calkin algebra (`PC-081`). Weil Inertia has narrowed the fourth-moment obstruction to genuinely coefficient-sensitive nonzero fibers: generic AP-maximal higher uniformity remains ambient-normalized and cannot by itself close power-modulus Yang cells (`WI-053`). Prior-art saturation remains interface-specific, not literature-wide. The graph/Atlas is useful only as derived navigation and may lag the canonical frontier; it is not evidence for novelty, closure, or line priority.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Sufficiency should be tested against the whole control family, not against one target statistic.** `AF-013` is the cleanest exact model of this principle: preserving one divergence or one marginal is weaker than preserving the vector likelihood-ratio data of the full experiment. The corresponding application-level audit is to compare prime and matched-control objects *after the exact quotient/operator/topology being proposed*, not at an upstream representation that may contain more information.

2. **Classicalization and control matching are converging on the same bottleneck.** `PC-081` makes finite Hardy essential data universal; `PF-125` matches an exact all-composite surface at compact-resolvent level; `PL-066`--`PL-067` show natural boundary observables can reduce directly to classical zero information; `WP-059` turns a nontrivial Prym action into the ordinary cycle-Laplacian determinant. Retaining a literal prime label or `log p` somewhere is therefore not enough: the destination invariant must fail for an appropriate control for a reason forced by the arithmetic construction.

3. **The topology or category is part of the mathematical claim.** Prime Flute shows that compactness, Schatten membership, and wave equivalence are genuinely different information layers. Prime Lattice shows that changing amplitude or moving-band topology can move from collapse to explicit zero replay without producing a stable new object. Weil Positivity shows that moving from integral/torsion data to a positive real or determinant-of-cohomology category can either classicalize or erase the arithmetic scale. A successful route has to justify why its destination category is both analytically usable and still discriminating.

4. **Local benignity is pushing several lines toward global assembly rather than new local formulas.** `PF-128`--`PF-132` leave interfaces/noncanonical thin regions/infinite gluing as the unresolved operator contribution. `WI-053` similarly says that strong ambient uniformity does not resolve source-faithful sparse fibers; the missing estimate must respect the localized coefficient geometry. In both cases, another local asymptotic is lower leverage than a theorem that controls the exact assembly mechanism.

5. **The common obstruction does not justify merging lines.** Prime Circle concerns finite-vs-infinite Hardy operator algebra, Prime Flute relative spectral/scattering ideals, Prime Lattice completed-Weil boundary topology, Weil Inertia anisotropic four-prime covariance, and Weil Positivity category-changing sign production. Arithmetic Fidelity is transferable methodology across them, not a replacement owner for their mathematical questions.

## Highest-leverage unresolved questions

- **Arithmetic Fidelity:** extend the exact likelihood-ratio/sufficiency viewpoint only where an application line supplies a concrete admissible category and matched control family; avoid unconstrained universal lifts that can encode the target by construction.
- **Prime Circle:** stay below the finite Calkin closure or become genuinely infinite-level. The open `PC-082` review must settle before its cyclic-trace claim can influence portfolio conclusions.
- **Prime Flute:** resolve the accepted sharp-Schatten and wave-equivalence clues at the complete-surface level, concentrating on boundary coherence, interfaces, noncanonical thin geometry, and infinite summation left after `PF-128`--`PF-132`.
- **Prime Lattice:** resolve the accepted mesoscopic-boundary question beyond `PL-066`--`PL-067`: either exhibit a stable rational-prime-specific transition observable that beats Beurling controls without importing zeros, or prove a broader no-go for the remaining natural topology/amplitude class.
- **Weil Inertia:** resolve the accepted locked-covariance clue on nonzero pair-correlation fibers for power-sized coefficients; `WI-053` rules out treating AP-maximal higher uniformity as a coefficient-uniform black box and redirects attention toward bilinear/fiber square-function consequences of prime-distribution technology.
- **Weil Positivity:** couple the finite selector to the archimedean/polar sector before the reductions classicalized by `WP-059` or erased by `WP-060`, and require the resulting category-changing operation to carry its own sign theorem.
