# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia has six source-backed research lines: `arithmetic_fidelity`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `weil_inertia`, and `weil_positivity`.

The strongest current program-level lesson is more specific than generic “information loss.” Arithmetic Fidelity now separates three exact questions that recur elsewhere: **what linear span is actually measured, which joint interactions survive marginalization, and whether the projection calculus assumed by the model is valid in the source category**. `AF-030` identifies linear-test fidelity with closed-span density on the declared source class; `AF-031`--`AF-034` show that complete marginals can forget coupling and classify the missing interaction support in Boolean/product settings; `AF-035` proves that the clean Boolean meet law for conditional-expectation projections is itself equivalent to the relevant conditional-independence structure. Downstream spectralization, positivity, or optimization cannot restore a direction or interaction that the admitted statistic has annihilated.

Three application lines have undergone substantial further classicalization. Prime Circle now closes not only finite cotangent networks and fixed-support depth but also the canonical complete growing tube and every fixed finite **cyclically separated Hardy cycle**: `PC-099` gives universal affine-band limits for the growing complete tube, while `PC-100`--`PC-103` place all finite separated Hardy cycle periods in cyclotomic hyperlogarithmic/multiple-polylogarithmic classes. Prime Lattice now closes the ordinary critical prime-support bulk under the stated Hardy--Littlewood hierarchy and shows that natural von-Mangoldt weighting and every fixed prime-power depth tail add only deterministic grading, time dilation, or vanishing-rank bulk corrections (`PL-084`--`PL-089`). Weil Positivity closes another large canonical family: cover coinvariants collapse to ordinary trace or zero (`WP-079`--`WP-080`), fixed-shift/log-Jensen responses are flat/coboundary-type (`WP-081`--`WP-082`), and `WP-083` proves that throughout the homogeneous operator-concave power/log Jensen family exact Mangoldt support occurs only at the singular inverse-scale endpoint.

Prime Flute and Weil Inertia instead remain dominated by global/source-faithful assembly. `PF-144`--`PF-145` identify sharp nonvanishing `L^1` currencies for the remaining angular and radial collar traces; `PF-146` opens a second sufficient wave route because the squared relative resolvent is trace class on each fixed matched short collar; and `PF-147` shows that a still-unproved **global** squared-resolvent `S_1` estimate would force the first relative resolvent into `S_2\setminus S_1` and the natural `det_2`/Koplienko regime. In Weil Inertia, the adversarial challenge to `WI-067` was substantive: the fixed-shift use of Shao--Teräväinen was withdrawn, and corrected successor `WI-068` leaves the prescribed-shift rectangle outside that finite-complexity bridge. `WI-069`--`WI-072` then show that the obvious escapes merely move the burden to a power-sparse slope slice, an anisotropic multivariate polynomial-prime interface, or an lcm-sparse selector whose restriction costs a power for every fixed ambient `L^p` argument.

The reusable portfolio rule is therefore: **audit source fidelity and interaction support first; then rational-prime specificity; then destination realization; then the exact local-to-global/source-restriction interface**. These are related gates but current findings do not prove that they collapse to one theorem. The proposed global clue `CLUE-cross-line-fidelity-realization-separation` already owns that theorem-level question, so no duplicate clue is warranted. The current Atlas remains useful for navigation, but its `source_revision` predates several material findings above; its telemetry is therefore treated as stale for this pass and is not used as evidence or as the basis for recommendations.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Interaction support is now an exact reusable audit, but not an automatic theorem transfer.** `AF-031`--`AF-035` show precisely how perfect marginals or an assumed projection lattice can lose relational information. This is directly useful as an audit question for any line that replaces a joint arithmetic object by diagonal, averaged, shellwise, or separately conditioned summaries. The transfer is methodological until the destination line proves its own source space and projection identities.

2. **Prime Circle and Weil Positivity show two different forms of canonical-algebra saturation.** Prime Circle repeatedly enlarges finite internal algebra and lands in endpoint, finite-state, or cyclotomic-period classes; Weil Positivity keeps an exact finite arithmetic selector but canonical cover/Jensen/coinvariant operations flatten, scalarize, or lose inherited sign. They should not be merged: one frontier is irreducible information beyond finite Hardy/cotangent closures, while the other is completion/sign compatibility after finite selector recovery.

3. **Prime Lattice and Weil Inertia both touch higher prime correlations, but in opposite strategic roles.** In Prime Lattice the ordinary bulk is *explained by* the local Hardy--Littlewood hierarchy and therefore classicalized. In Weil Inertia the desired Yang splice needs a very specific locked/source-weighted four-prime restriction that existing free-shift finite-complexity technology does not supply. The useful transfer is a warning: an ambient correlation theorem is not source evidence until the conditioning/slope selector is preserved with the required normalization.

4. **Prime Flute isolates a genuinely geometric/operator assembly problem.** Its current gate is neither missing marginal interaction nor prime-correlation asymptotics: local pieces are controlled but the uncut infinite surface and interface synchronization must produce a global operator statement. This is the clearest current counterexample to collapsing every obstruction into “information loss.”

5. **Prior-art saturation increasingly favors narrow interface theorems over additional named frameworks.** Recent lines repeatedly land in classical hyperlogarithms, Hardy--Littlewood/Poisson laws, standard Schatten/fractional-power theory, trace/coinvariant algebra, or known prime-pattern theorem boundaries. The canonical prior-art projection already covers the major RH programs relevant to these reductions. New effort has highest value where a present line can state the missing interface exactly, not by opening another broad representation family.

## Highest-leverage unresolved questions

- **Arithmetic Fidelity:** what is the exact joint measured statistic and annihilator for the globally forced Weil/explicit-formula test families on the rational-prime source class, especially when the source law is dependent and product projection identities fail?
- **Prime Circle:** do nonseparated/repeated-shell Hardy words or an intrinsically defined infinite-shell Fredholm/global object escape the cyclotomic/finite-state controls and distinguish a matched non-prime source?
- **Prime Flute:** can the local collar/body estimates be assembled into either a global Güneysu--Thalmaier welding or global squared-resolvent `S_1`; if so, what stronger scattering/resonant invariant still separates the all-composite clone?
- **Prime Lattice:** is there a hard-edge, determinant, growing-depth/moment, target-relative, or completed observable that survives the `PL-084`--`PL-089` bulk/rank reductions and is not another Hardy--Littlewood or sampling-density statistic?
- **Weil Inertia:** can one prove a source-normalized locked-shift/lcm-aware four-prime estimate with the exact Yang weights and centering, rather than infer it from a free-shift fixed-`L^p` theorem?
- **Weil Positivity:** can a nonseparable finite--archimedean or genuinely higher-cohomological construction preserve the exact Mangoldt selector while generating the completed sign before the now-classified flat/separated reductions?
