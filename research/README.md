The Atlas entrypoint narrative and several Mind frontiers lag canonical findings; selected episode rates are not a current whole-portfolio fertility measurement. ## Cross-line structure

**Coverage changes observation requirements, not arithmetic strength.** For lengths `L_1=H`, `L_2=floor(sqrt(2)H)`, `1<=H<=X/20`, both MC-533 and PL-478 bound complete smooth pairings through nearest-sample cell weights and the distance sum `D_i=sum_j dist(j,R_i)`. Writing `W_i(c_i)=sum_r w_(i,r)|S_(L_i)(r)-c_i|^2`, the recovery cost is

`sum_i [sqrt(X W_i(c_i)) + J_X D_i]/L_i`,

with jump price `J_X=2` for Möbius and `log(3X)` for primes. Their separate pole formulas retain respectively `X^beta (log X)^(m-1)` for a reciprocal pole of multiplicity `m`, and `X^beta` with noncancelling multiplicity weights for the prime logarithmic derivative. The test is complete and mean zero; a sample mask is not inserted into its Mellin transform.

At `H=floor(X/20)`, the grid of spacing `floor(sqrt X)` has `D_i=O(X^(3/2))` and largest cell size `O(sqrt X)`. Both ordinary sample-centered averages `Q_i/q_X <<_epsilon X^(1+epsilon)` on one unbounded integer scale set fixed before epsilon are equivalent to the source's own zero hypothesis. Each converse uses its own pointwise arithmetic estimate, not an inference from a complete mean square to a deterministic sparse sample. The [prime sparse-start clue](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-geometric-coverage-sparse-prime-variance.md) is resolved by PL-478. This does not identify the two zero sets or supply either ceiling.

[MC-536](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-536-stratified-random-starts-reduce-the-mobius-variance-sample-exponent.md) changes the observation guarantee. In each channel take one independent uniform integer start from each of `q=ceil(X^(1/3))` balanced cells. Deterministic cell-integrated mean-zero weights cancel every fitted constant exactly. For macroscopic lengths and uniformly bounded mean-zero `C^1` tests, a fixed source with jump bound `A` has pairing covariance bias `O(A X/q^2)` and fluctuation proxy `O(A^2 X^2/q^3)`. For Möbius, `A=2`; summable concentration bounds give error `O(sqrt(X log X))` on all sufficiently large integer scales almost surely. Its RH-equivalent energy condition may use a realized unbounded scale set, common to both channels and fixed before epsilon. The [stratified prime transfer clue](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-stratified-random-starts-prime-variance.md) asks whether the destination’s logarithmic jump bound and independent prime pole/converse formulas preserve this smaller observation count. The prime transfer is not established here; the method is classical stratified quadrature, not a new cancellation estimate.
