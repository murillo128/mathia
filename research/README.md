# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia currently has thirteen research lines. Ten carry canonical evidence: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain pre-evidence scaffolds.

The evidence still does **not** establish one common positive mechanism or one mathematically meaningful global bottleneck shared by the program. Progress is audited line by line at the first genuinely unsupported implication. This snapshot contains two kinds of useful movement that should not be conflated: Prime Flute and Analytic Frontier have moved live proof interfaces by proving estimates that remove previously open cases; Xi, Möbius, Prime Lattice, Visual Exploration, Arithmetic Fidelity, and Weil Positivity mostly sharpen controls or classify mechanisms negatively. Weil Inertia remains a direct source-specific test, but `WI-197` now proves that one apparently natural analytic upgrade cannot reach the norm it requires.

**Prime Flute remains the clearest continuous proof-interface progression.** `PF-211` removes exterior amplification from the low-mode gate by energy-normalized Dirichlet-to-Neumann gluing, and `PF-212` closes the local high-frequency/aspect-ratio estimates on the actual thin Fermi seams. `PF-213` then proves the abstract global reassembly implication under a quantitative module-locality hypothesis and supplies a positive-contraction counterexample showing that positivity/contractivity alone may fail even compactness. This is a real contraction of the first unsupported implication, but not closure of the interface: the actual simultaneous normalized Schur complement still has to satisfy summable off-diagonal module decay, a finite-range/fixed-color substitute, or another weighted ideal estimate with the same consequence. The unsmoothed `PF-204` route remains a competing interface proof, and the true-short-collar splice is still independent.

**Analytic Frontier has now moved enough of its own first unsupported implication to return to `continue`.** The formerly proposed gap-free bounded-strip localization handoff is no longer speculative: `ANF-106` proves that every bounded-height near-extremizer with fatal fixed-notch exposure has a finite physical witness with bounded multiplicity and horizontal span at fixed resolution. `ANF-107` then separates the scale at which a sparse tail can be source-heavy from the stronger scale at which it is visible to a fixed central notch. `ANF-108` proves that a matched-critical source-heavy compensator cannot cancel against a bounded or sub-`log log` core, and `ANF-109` strengthens this from a maximum-height statement to a source-energy statement: above every fixed subcritical fraction of the secondary `log log` height there is a source-heavy layer, with an explicit cardinality/height tradeoff. The unresolved work is now concrete: certify a strict finite-box exposure gap in the bounded-height chamber, and in the unbounded-height chamber either control the extracted layer's cardinality strongly enough to continue the cascade or recover the negative-polarization structure needed to iterate it. Promotion records movement of that proof interface, not evidence that RH itself is close.

**Weil Inertia keeps one exact source-specific target, while `WI-197` rules out a tempting shortcut.** `WI-195` identifies the bow's required multiplicative short-interval quadratic mean, `ANF-102` rules out a uniform twist-window saving, and `WI-196` shows that positivity, exact bandwidth, the diagonal coefficient, and asymptotically diagonal long averages still permit complete cancellation at a prescribed center. `WI-197` now proves that improving only the one-variable prime Dirichlet-polynomial input and feeding it through the published MRSTT Type-II module cannot repair the deficit: even granting maximal polynomial input allowed by that lemma, the `W^{-3/10}` variance transfer remains polynomially above the bow's `K log X` diagonal scale. A viable continuation therefore has to change the norm interface itself—e.g. a genuinely quadratic-mean Type-II/dispersion theorem at the distinguished twist, a source decomposition preserving cancellation before absolute-value losses, or a second source-fixed observable—not merely improve the existing pointwise black box.

**Xi Flow is more strongly classified negatively, not rescued by wider prime bands.** `XF-103` extends transition nonidentifiability through a prime-power band of size roughly `q/(log q)^4`; `XF-104` pushes the same construction to a fixed positive fraction of the arithmetic scale using Chebyshev mass control. `XF-105` then identifies a limitation of the present counterexample machinery itself: the archimedean background exhausts the raw-`l1` realization margin at about the quarter-scale, so the existing Gershgorin/raw-`l1` construction does not decide what happens at the natural full scale. This does **not** restore the current Vieta-prefix bridge. It leaves a replacement test: use a stronger feasibility criterion, such as Toeplitz/Caratheodory positivity, at `L=log a+O(1)`, or change the downstream state rather than seeking coercivity from the same autonomous prefix.

**The Bost--Connes handoff has been narrowed rather than opened into a new mechanism.** `MC-138` proves exact prime independence for the diagonal divisibility algebra and separates the phase-blind `beta=1/2` square-free-support threshold from Möbius orientation, whose diagonal `L^2` threshold is `beta>1`. `MC-139` shows that KMS invariance kills every nonzero time-evolution degree, so raw off-diagonal semigroup energy does not recover cross-prime orientation. `MC-140` further shows that in the unique high-temperature KMS regime every nontrivial cyclotomic character first moment vanishes. On the Prime-Lattice side, `PL-212` decomposes the canonical cyclotomic observable into a zeta-free invariant channel and `L(s,chi)/zeta(s)` nontrivial harmonics, while `PL-213` rules out a modular-factor-type transition at `beta=1/2` because the high-temperature KMS factor remains type `III_1` throughout `0<beta<=1`. The accepted Master handoff is therefore resolved as `narrowed`: any survivor must use genuinely non-diagonal, symmetry-neutral nonlinear information and still exhibit a cheaper-than-Mertens transfer.

**Arithmetic Fidelity, Visual Exploration, and Weil Positivity mainly improve calibration or matched controls in this snapshot.** `AF-191`--`AF-193` derive the moving-saddle law for growing Euler parity clusters and identify where leading theta matching is multiplicatively valid; this is a sharper control phase diagram, not a source-class inverse theorem. `VIS-090` proves fixed-finite-prime Haar population on superlogarithmic consecutive Gram blocks, while `VIS-091` and `VIS-092` collapse logarithmic-lag clock geometry and one-dimensional Rips persistence to classical sampling/gap data; these classify visual carriers rather than supply a new RH signal. `WP-200` sharpens the infinite-dimensional matched-control failure into an exact power-law threshold: throughout the natural rough strip `S^n\setminus S^{n-1}`, a source-blind centered bath can stabilize full Weil-cone positivity around arbitrary finite positive payloads, whereas that universal manufacture disappears once the extra source-variation moment becomes summable. This is a useful boundary theorem, but it makes generic higher-order positivity less, not more, arithmetically selective.

The Riemann Atlas remains historical/structural navigation only. Its declared `source_revision` remains far behind the evidence reconciled here; its percentages are stale derived telemetry and do not influence portfolio decisions.

## Cross-line structure

The earlier **Prime Lattice -> Möbius Cancellation** transfer is now resolved rather than proliferated. `PL-211` supplied a canonical arithmetic critical-half statistic; `MC-138`--`MC-140` show that its diagonal threshold is support rather than Möbius orientation, that nonzero time-evolution degree is killed by KMS invariance, and that nontrivial cyclotomic first moments vanish in the unique high-temperature phase. This is useful negative transfer: it prevents the program from treating the numerical appearance of `1/2` in Bost--Connes as evidence for an RH mechanism.

The **Weil Inertia -> Analytic Frontier** interface is also sharper, but it does not warrant a new generic clue. The previous multiplicative-Gallagher variance clue was correctly resolved by `ANF-102` because its uniform twist target is false. `WI-196` made the replacement pointwise, and `WI-197` now rules out the specific plan of improving only the one-variable Dirichlet-polynomial input while reusing the published Type-II transfer. Any new Analytic-Frontier contribution must therefore attack the quadratic-mean/dispersion interface at the source-selected twist itself; merely obtaining a stronger pointwise prime-polynomial estimate would not move Weil Inertia's first unsupported implication.

Prime Flute and Analytic Frontier now provide the clearest examples of genuine proof-interface movement for different reasons. Prime Flute repeatedly removes analytic losses from one fixed operator comparison. Analytic Frontier has converted a noncompact near-extremizer problem first to finite bounded-height witnesses and then to a source-heavy secondary height layer in the unbounded chamber. Neither result is a common mechanism for RH, and neither licenses importing its language into unrelated lines.

Weil Positivity supplies a program-level matched-control warning rather than a transferable theorem request. `WP-200` shows that even a sharp positive cone theorem can be generated throughout an entire rough Schatten strip by a detachable source-blind reservoir. Future positivity arguments should therefore identify where the Riemann source fixes the reservoir and its coupling before treating sign as arithmetic evidence.

## Highest-leverage unresolved questions

1. **Prime-Flute actual Schur locality and short-collar splice.** Does the simultaneous normalized exterior Schur complement for the canonical PF seams satisfy PF-213's summable module-decay condition, a finite-range/fixed-color specialization, or another weighted ideal estimate giving the same endpoint bounds? Independently, can the true-short-collar normalized slabs be spliced at weak `S_1` without importing the generic endpoint failures already classified?

2. **Bow distinguished-twist quadratic mean.** Can one prove a source-specific second-moment/dispersion theorem at the exact bow twist whose scale is `O(K log^{O(1)}X)` or better, with `Lambda^sharp` kept in the same signed norm, or produce a source-fixed second observable that forbids the exceptional near-null direction? `WI-197` means a better pointwise input fed unchanged through the published MRSTT Type-II module is not an answer.

3. **Montgomery--Taylor finite-box gap versus recursive height cascade.** After `ANF-106`, can the finite bounded-height witness supremum be certified strictly below the fatal fixed-notch threshold? If the obstruction escapes in height, can `ANF-109`'s source-heavy upper layer be shown either sparse enough and sufficiently negatively polarized to iterate the cascade, or populous enough to force a separate contradiction?

4. **Xi natural-scale feasibility.** Does the transition-nonidentifying construction survive when the visible band reaches natural scale and feasibility is tested by the full Toeplitz/Caratheodory positive-definiteness constraints rather than the raw-`l1` cone? A positive construction would extend the information barrier; a failure with a quantitative reason could identify genuinely new source information that the current counterexamples cannot fake.
