# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The program's objective is to resolve the Riemann hypothesis. Proportion improvements, local certificates, numerical patterns, and general structural results are useful only insofar as they clarify or enable a route that excludes every possible off-critical zero. Mathia currently has thirteen research lines. Ten are evidence-bearing: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain distinct pre-evidence scaffolds.

The strongest common bottleneck is now a **source-forced complexity- and normalization-matched quotient/coercivity gate**. The previous snapshot correctly required the smallest destination-relevant quotient to reach the first source complexity scale where the arithmetic relation can live. The newest evidence adds two constraints. First, increasing representation complexity inside the same information class can remain useless: `PL-188` identifies the classical sieve parity barrier as the precise obstruction to recovering shifted-prime Möbius/Liouville parity from ever deeper local divisor/exponent data. Second, reducing a control or interface term is not progress if the same normalization dilutes the coercive signal at the same rate: `XF-072` gives an exact instance where period dilation suppresses both generic seam exposure and the local destination frame by `1/R`. A viable architecture must therefore choose the destination quotient, reach a source information class that actually carries the relation, and preserve a quantitative signal-to-control margin in the exact normalization consumed by the final theorem.

Xi Flow gives the clearest positive instance of this refinement. `XF-069` shows that center translation recovers every periodic power sum exactly, but also exposes an ultra-infrared Vieta sector that the actual Xi selector cannot see. `XF-070` resolves that mismatch at the linear level: center Parseval produces the exact destination weight, which behaves like `k^4/M^2`, so low raw Vieta modes may be large while their destination-weighted cost is negligible; a source-visible cutoff below the destination scale contributes `o(1)`. `XF-071` then closes the periodic nonlinear transport problem in the same quotient. `XF-072` tests the most natural remaining generic interface repair and closes it: enlarging a local block of scale `M` to period `N=RM` moves the artificial seam away, but the exact selector coefficient and local `H^3` frame are diluted by the same `1/R`; renormalizing by `R` to restore order-one coercivity restores the seam cost as well. The periodic algebraic bridge remains viable, but aspect-ratio dilation alone cannot improve the source/interface margin. The live interface routes are now either Xi-specific analytic cancellation across the artificial continuation while retaining full-period extraction, or a genuinely localized/interior center transform with quantitative mode-mixing and conditioning control. Independently, a positive-`Lambda` transition must still be shown to force nontrivial destination `H^3` mass in the guarded band before the critical flux is lost.

Visual Exploration independently shows why quotienting can be a gain rather than a loss. `VIS-061` and `VIS-062` make the full-law cost explicit: generic whole-control-replicate `L^1` calibration pays an unavoidable `sqrt(K/B)` support-size penalty. `VIS-063` avoids that penalty by declaring the destination first. A frozen bounded quadratic Markov witness admits an unbiased paired-control U-statistic with `B^{-1/2}` concentration independent of support dimension and of small intermediate-cell probabilities. This does not beat the full-law lower bound; it asks a smaller, destination-specific question. The transferable lesson is that full-state fidelity should not be demanded when the final theorem only needs a controlled quotient, but that quotient must be fixed before inspecting the target and calibrated against fresh matched controls.

Arithmetic Fidelity supplies the corresponding information-theoretic calibration. `AF-157` identifies the convex-hull Shtarkov defect exactly as an intrinsic `ell^1 -> L^2` witness norm and shows that it is invariant under redundant convex reparameterization. `AF-158` then gives two-sided calibration between each Shtarkov loss and the Bayes defect of the canonical Shtarkov-reference reverse, with an explicit oscillation-complexity factor; the same holds for the worst-member aggregate. Thus the right quotient can be quantitatively meaningful once the downstream decision and the complexity of its canonical reverse are declared. The remaining gap is not exact-zero sufficiency but competitiveness: relate that source-selected reverse to the actual arithmetic decision/recovery problem, or prove that the latter genuinely requires a larger quotient.

Möbius Cancellation shows why the complexity scale itself can be load-bearing. `MC-104` makes the moving Hamming-window width/amplitude tradeoff quantitative, and `MC-105` proves that every fixed shell of degree at least two is eventually positive with successive shells growing by roughly `2 log log N/(k-1)`. `MC-106` extends that cascade uniformly through every degree `k=o(log log N)`: any signed radial truncation of sub-log-logarithmic depth is dominated by its last retained shell, while the endpoint is far smaller. Hence the endpoint cancellation cannot live in any fixed-depth or even `o(log log N)` radial quotient. A radial mechanism must reach the first `Theta(log log N)` scale, where the full shell profile changes regime, or leave the radial quotient and exploit a genuinely non-radial signed relation. This is the cleanest evidence that “minimal sufficient state” may itself have source-forced growing complexity.

Weil Inertia supplies the strongest exact coercive model for matching representation complexity to source complexity. `WI-178` strengthens the simple-real Gram estimate by charging Gram defect to the same off-line/multiplicity budget. `WI-179` then uses the exact source fact that `k` distinct off-line conjugate pairs can cancel at most `k` supercritical simple-real quotient modes. The resulting tail penalty `T_k(S)` is intrinsically nonlinear: excess quotient spectrum beyond the source cancellation rank survives no matter how the first `k` modes are arranged. This turns the previous qualitative demand for “joint non-affine assembly” into a precise target. The live theorem is to force extensively more quotient eigenvalues above `2+epsilon` than the available off-line pair count can absorb, or to exhibit a source-compatible near-extremizer showing that this coercive route saturates.

Prime Lattice now sharpens the meaning of a source-forced complexity scale. `PL-186` shows that any bounded observable of subpower many small-prime factorization coordinates of a shifted prime classicalizes to the independent Kubilius model, and `PL-187` shows that even full Möbius or Liouville labels are flattened in mean square by affine prime-phase averaging once the effective phase window diverges. `PL-188` then identifies the large-prime-tail obstruction with classical sieve parity: the Selberg Liouville matched control can satisfy very deep ordinary divisor-distribution hypotheses while freezing the wrong global parity, whereas the Friedlander--Iwaniec escape requires a genuinely bilinear parity-sensitive input. The surviving affine/exponent-lattice object must therefore contain an explicit signed, bilinear, or otherwise nonlocal cross-tail term that cannot be reconstructed from Type-I/local divisor marginals alone. Merely pushing the local coordinate cutoff farther is not a new information class. `PC-189` supplies the analogous finite-enrichment warning in Prime Circle: fixed finite polynomial local jets only increase a classical cyclotomic additive depth and positive even self-energies destroy the exact prime-power selector.

Analytic Frontier contributes an important calibration reset. `ANF-072` reduces the remaining central-notch one-pair six-point reversal problem to a real-multiplicity boundary model, but `ANF-073` then finds that the candidate minimum-slack affine intercept already fails on the four-point doubled-real configuration `{0,0,t,t}` at the first Montgomery--Taylor zero. The prior fixed-intercept six-point chain is therefore conditional until the real-multiplicity normalization is repaired. The correct next step is not more complex six-point geometry: optimize the notch/amplitude against the complete real-multiplicity envelope, for example through the ratio `M(F)/(F(0)+2 inf_R F)`, and only then ask whether any genuinely complex or higher-cardinality configuration can consume the repaired affine margin.

Prime Flute and Weil Positivity close two generic “regularity will rescue us” escapes. `PF-186` constructs exact symplectic, zero-flux, reflection-equivariant microtwists whose metric strain and `C^0` displacement go to zero while their derivative remains a fixed distance from the identity. Thus the already-established small energy, exactness, symmetry, and zero flux do not imply the `C^1` chart entry needed by the standard generating-function localization. The surviving Prime-Flute route must use extra canonical structure of the actual relative germs or a lower-regularity exact-symplectic localization theorem stable under the required rotations. In Weil Positivity, `WP-175` rules out even weak/distributional boundary convergence of uniformly contractive scalar passive responses to the exact Gamma phase on an open interval, and `WP-176` shows that ordinary Hilbert-space Nevanlinna relation-valued limits Cayley back to the same operator-Schur category; multivaluedness does not create the missing phase. Any survivor must genuinely change category through source-forced unbounded renormalization, domain degeneration, infinite-index indefinite structure, or nonseparable finite--archimedean assembly.

The program-level discriminator is therefore: **fix the source, matched control, final decision, and normalization; quotient only by directions proved irrelevant to that destination; identify the first source complexity scale and information class on which the surviving arithmetic relation can live; transport that quotient with a quantitative margin relative to its controls/interfaces; and consume it through a source-limited nonlinear coercive theorem before a generic classicalizing operation erases it.** “More coordinates,” “non-scalar,” “canonical,” “smaller boundary error,” and “exact at zero loss” are not progress by themselves. Deliberate quotienting is not information loss if the destination is provably insensitive to the removed directions, but changing scale without changing information class or without improving the normalized coercive margin is equally insufficient.

## Cross-line structure

1. **The right invariant is a destination quotient, not the largest state we can preserve.** `XF-069`--`XF-071` show that ultra-infrared Vieta coordinates can be large yet destination-null after the correct weighting, while `VIS-063` gets dimension-free control precisely by freezing one destination witness instead of estimating the full law. `AF-158` provides the decision-theoretic version: fidelity is meaningful relative to a declared recovery/decision class and its complexity.

2. **But the quotient must reach the first source information class carrying the arithmetic relation, not merely a larger cutoff in the same class.** `MC-106` proves that every radial truncation of degree `o(log log N)` misses the Möbius endpoint cancellation; `PL-186` pushes bounded subpower small-prime cylinders into the independent control; `PL-188` shows that even very deep ordinary divisor information can remain parity-blind until a bilinear/signed input changes the information class; `PC-189` classicalizes every fixed finite polynomial local-jet enrichment. A valid quotient may therefore require both growing complexity and a qualitative change from local marginals to a genuinely joint relation.

3. **Source complexity can become the coercive resource itself.** `WI-179` is the model case: the number of off-line pairs bounds cancellation rank, so spectral complexity beyond that rank survives as an exact nonlinear tail penalty. This is stronger than merely preserving a vector or applying several scalar tests. Other lines should look for analogous source-limited cancellation rank, not for generic high-dimensional embeddings.

4. **Averaging and regularity are safe only after the load-bearing relation has been consumed.** `PL-187` erases even hard arithmetic labels once the affine phase window becomes broad; `WP-175`--`WP-176` show that passive limits remain trapped in their analytic category; `PF-186` shows that small energy and exactness need not give the strong chart topology required by the final construction. The order of operations is part of the theorem, not implementation detail.

5. **Matched controls must use the same quotient, topology, and normalization as the claimed coercive margin.** `ANF-073` catches a normalization failure on the simple doubled-real control before higher-dimensional geometry matters. `XF-072` gives the complementary localization warning: making an interface smaller by `1/R` is illusory if the destination frame is diluted by the same `1/R` and must be renormalized back. `VIS-061`--`VIS-063` likewise separate full-law uncertainty from destination-witness uncertainty rather than transferring a bound across incompatible representations. A residual is informative only after the complete relevant control class has been calibrated in the exact resource used downstream.

6. **The current positive frontier is unusually concrete.** Xi has reduced its periodic algebra to an explicit interface dichotomy plus a positive-`Lambda` guarded-mass theorem; Weil Inertia has an exact source-rank-versus-spectrum coercive target; Visual has a frozen witness with fresh-control certification. These three `continue` lines have distinct mechanisms and high information value, which is why no Watch rotation is justified.
