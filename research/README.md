## Cross-line structure

The strongest cross-line synthesis is now an **information-budget hierarchy**. Arithmetic Fidelity proves that bounded local Euler degree turns infinite prime-power depth into a finite exact certificate, but leaves infinite prime breadth. Analytic Frontier proves almost the opposite kind of bound: a dangerous endpoint packet can absorb any local observation budget below the geometric scale `K/a_X`. Nyman shows that exact continuous information can be present while the natural logarithmic sampling misses the relevant off-grid direction. These are not versions of the same theorem, but they agree on the methodological point: the complexity budget of the proposed discriminator must scale with the complexity of the source defect it claims to exclude.

A second convergence concerns positivity and order. Prime Lattice now proves not only that individual interior atoms are indefinite but that **every finite positive interior aggregate is eventually indefinite**. Weil Positivity finds an exact positive Mangoldt half-density only in a semifinite free-Fock regime whose normalized equilibrium parameter is wrong. Weil Inertia proves that the positive archimedean reserve can be optimized exactly but still leaves the decisive signed von-Mangoldt source inequality untouched. Together these results make a stronger case that a successful Weil route must fix completion, mixed-prime coupling, source weights, and sign **before** the final quotient/thermal/Galerkin operation; positivity of a source component or exact source identification is not enough.

Prime Flute is the geometric analogue. A fixed smooth response profile cannot survive the dense physical high-pass, so noncompactness cannot be inferred from generic nonconstant geometry; it must be carried by an escaping relative response geometry. Möbius Cancellation gives the arithmetic analogue in parity form: the unsigned source population is enormous and well understood, yet the target depends on an `X^{-1/2+o(1)}` signed parity imbalance. In both cases the missing object is relational and normalized, not additional bulk mass.
