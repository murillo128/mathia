## Cross-line structure

Arithmetic Fidelity, Farey Discrepancy, and Nyman--Beurling now give three clean outcomes for strengthening a source representation. AF-348 shows **downstream erasure**: a source distinction may be real and still disappear under the exact destination operation. FD-129 shows **circular overconstraint**: enough exact source recovery plus a uniform critical envelope can simply restate the target. NB-128 shows **useful growing resolution**: increasing observation complexity can create a genuine inverse modulus, provided a separate amplitude theorem makes that modulus visible to the destination. The correct comparison is therefore not stronger versus weaker source data, but whether the added information remains independent, stable and target-visible after composition.

Möbius Cancellation and Weil Positivity add a complementary route-class audit. MC-300 says the current Deuring--Heilbronn mechanism has already been optimized to its natural asymptotic source-band scale under the stated zero input; a larger gain needs new arithmetic information or a collective capacity theorem. WP-308 says the scalar Herglotz coordinate class has likewise been exhausted; a repair must change the geometric category before scalarization. In both lines, another parameter tuning inside the closed class should be treated as mathematically inert unless it introduces a genuinely new source input.

Robin and Prime Lattice provide two different forms of endpoint dimensional reduction. `RE-130` turns an apparently diffuse algebraic packet into the existence of a strong atom and therefore one explicit pair-cancellation geometry. `PL-313` turns a compressed physical corner into one rank-one outer compatibility scalar. Neither reduction proves the endpoint, but each removes broad families of vague escape mechanisms and leaves a destination-native theorem with much less state freedom.

Prime Circle and Visual remain the cleanest contrast in representation fidelity. `PC-298` pushes one-profile diffuse universality down to an explicit shrinking defect scale, so information above that scale is classicalized. `VIS-226` instead matches a simple binary lower certificate to the exact static objective up to constants, and therefore settles the subcritical scale rather than losing it. The new Visual occupancy clue correctly starts only where that settled argument reaches logarithmic prime spacing; it does not reopen the already-closed subcritical optimization hierarchy.

The existing global fidelity/realization clue and `MI-008`, `MI-009`, `MI-013`, `MI-014`, `MI-015`, and `MI-016` already cover the reusable program-level lessons about stable transport, source realization, destination sensitivity, nonredundant coherence, endpoint-faithful relaxations and source-forced geometry.
