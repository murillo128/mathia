## Cross-line structure

Farey, Nyman, and Visual now expose three versions of the same methodological danger without sharing an operator. Farey shows that a matched source can **rotate the hidden defect into a new source coordinate** after the previous coordinate is exposed. Nyman shows that an alarming coordinate condition number can become irrelevant after passing to the represented subspace, because the whole subspace may be target-poor. Visual shows that a seemingly stronger control can become circular when its conditioning sigma-field already determines the tested statistic, while a statistic one local order higher can escape. The shared audit is therefore target-relative: identify what quotient or sigma-field is actually retained, what target functional is consumed, and whether the proposed source law still has discriminatory power after admissible coordinate changes and matched controls.

Möbius and Robin sharpen that audit into explicit quantitative thresholds. In Möbius, one uncontrolled Landau--Page exceptional frequency is enough to make exact half-occupancy compatible with perfect cancellation of every other quotient frequency; that half threshold is sharp for the available Fourier information and is precisely where the current Brun--Titchmarsh gain vanishes. In Robin, arbitrary vertical spread can be removed from the leading observability constant, but every extra cooperating mode incurs an exponential complexity cost. In both cases the next theorem must buy a resource the matched control has proved genuinely load-bearing, rather than strengthen a coordinate already known to be free.

Arithmetic Fidelity and Prime Lattice give the corresponding moving-scale warning. `AF-355` proves that fixed positive-order Riesz success does not survive any polynomial approach to the unsmoothed endpoint, even assuming RH; `PL-315` proves that broad smoothness and every current subcritical Sobolev gain do not control a singular observation whose sample location itself moves. A valid source theorem needs a modulus uniform in the exact coupled scale used by the endpoint, not merely good regularity at each fixed parameter.

Weil Inertia remains the clean counterexample to the idea that every noncompact escape requires a tail theorem. In its one-signed completely screened branch, rearrangement monotonically compresses the entire problem to the first-prime aperture, leaving one finite positivity gate. Robin lacks that monotone compression because its surviving difficulty is cooperative cancellation among actual zero modes. This distinction remains useful before investing in any new global tightness argument.

No new Master clue is justified. `FD-138`, `NB-137`, `MC-309`, and `RE-137` sharpen questions already owned by their lines, while `VIS-233` has already been folded into the accepted critical-occupancy clue. The accepted Robin zero-counting clue remains consumed by `RE-135`; its earlier cardinality question should not be reopened now that `RE-137` identifies the sharper horizontal-spread/complexity resource.
