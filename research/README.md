## Cross-line structure

Arithmetic Fidelity and Prime Lattice now distinguish **global recoverability from endpoint localization**. `AF-349` says the full Riesz object can retain all source information, yet the theorem may later quotient away the bounded residual carrying the distinction. `PL-314` says even a norm that sees the tail globally can miss the value sampled by a moving observer. The program-level lesson is not merely to preserve information somewhere: the surviving margin must be measured in the exact topology and at the exact moving scale charged by the endpoint.

Farey, Nyman, and Möbius expose three different notions of source complexity. `FD-131` defeats any fixed finite collection of prime/rank anchors by moving the mismatch to growing rank. `NB-130` defeats recurrence/analyticity as qualitative labels because linear model rank can interpolate arbitrary finite data. `MC-302` reaches all the way to linear conductor but still leaves too much total family capacity by a factor `R`. Directional depth, effective rank, conductor reach, and family dimension therefore cannot be substituted for one another.

Prime Circle and Analytic Frontier make dependence structure equally explicit. `PC-300` shows that nominally different cyclic profiles become powers of one averaged operator when source variables are independent, so additional loop depth adds no new source asymmetry. `ANF-186` already shows that correct marginal statistics remain insufficient when placement may adapt to the carrier. The next useful observables must preserve the **joint dependence** actually supplied by the arithmetic source rather than add formal complexity after independence or averaging has erased it.

Visual and Robin remain useful controls on what a successful reduction looks like. Visual's Ising/torus/SDP chain preserves the destination scale and can therefore falsify or confirm the static endpoint instead of merely producing another proxy; the critical cell test now asks for genuinely local prime information exactly where coarse density stops deciding the answer. Robin uses real zero-density/source information to eliminate a large formal cloud of controls and leaves a sharply localized companion configuration. Both routes reduce freedom while retaining the feature consumed by the endpoint.

The existing global fidelity/realization clue and `MI-008`, `MI-009`, `MI-013`, `MI-014`, `MI-015`, and `MI-016` already cover stable transport, source realization, destination sensitivity, nonredundant coherence, endpoint-faithful relaxations, and source-forced geometry. The present synthesis makes the separate **information budgets** more explicit but does not expose a distinct cross-line test requiring another Master clue.
