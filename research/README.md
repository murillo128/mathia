# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia has thirteen current research lines. Ten are evidence-bearing: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain distinct pre-evidence scaffolds.

The strongest common bottleneck is now a **source-forced coupled transport/assembly profile above the complete matched-control floor**. The previous gauge, topology, non-escape, resolution, and residual-margin gates remain necessary, but the newest evidence shows that a scalar margin is not enough: the decisive arithmetic information can disappear when an exact reconstruction is transported through an ill-conditioned map, when cancellation is split into regular factors, when prime coordinates are tensor-separated, or when a nonlinear statistic quotients away the leading source correction. The object that must survive is therefore the source-fixed interaction together with the quantitative regularity needed to carry it through the complete theorem-level assembly.

`AF-131`--`AF-132` give the clean abstract version. Wasserstein recovery errors do not compose by a scalar triangle law across changing metrics; they are amplified by the transport regularity of the intermediate recovery. The correct multistage state is the recovery profile `Phi(t)`, an exact concave error/regularity envelope that composes by profile composition. Exact recoverability can therefore coexist with arbitrarily bad downstream fidelity.

The arithmetic lines now show the same phenomenon as an interaction obstruction. `MC-079`--`MC-081` classify broad regular multiplicative Möbius factorizations and show that their Selberg--Delange resonance budget cannot be distributed harmlessly: unless the entire Möbius exponent is concentrated in one factor, at least one separate factor keeps a nonzero near-linear main term. `WP-156`--`WP-157` show that separated determinant/tensor constructions become rank-additive and have no irreducible mixed-prime curvature. `PC-177` reaches the same boundary from exact Prime-Circle geometry: the canonical prime-power ratio symbol is unbounded on the normalized space, while its simplest bounded damping exists only for `sigma>1` and decomposes into independent one-prime Toeplitz chains with a classical interval spectrum. These are independent reasons to preserve coupling before scalarization or positivity.

Several positive reductions sharpen where such coupling would have to live. `ANF-049`--`ANF-051` confine the remaining five-point scalar danger to an explicit compact cross-frequency coherence region and give exact fixed-height danger radii. `PL-169` shows that the first standard beyond-diagonal ratios correction reintroduces arithmetic through additive shifted correlations rather than through the bare multiplicative exponent lattice, while `PL-170` shows that relative-trace subtraction can remove the universal completion baseline without yet supplying the required sign or comparator. `XF-045` gains a full power of remote-buffer suppression by centering before taking absolute control. `VIS-039` proves the complementary warning that an even `N^-2` correction in the underlying finite-size cell law generically induces the same `N^-2` correction in CMI and can cancel to `N^-4`; an apparent power law in a derived statistic is therefore itself part of the matched-control quotient.

The program-level discriminator is consequently: **let the source fix the representation, interaction, gauge, scale, and admissible transport; quotient the strongest matched control; propagate the entire error/regularity profile rather than a scalar residual; and require the surviving coupled arithmetic component to remain quantitatively separated through the exact assembly consumed by the target theorem.** A nonzero invariant, exact inverse, natural local factor, positive completion, or attractive finite-size scaling is not sufficient if the decisive interaction is destroyed by the next map.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Coupling must survive before scalarization.** `MC-081`, `WP-157`, and `PC-177` independently show that regular separated factors, tensor-product determinant lines, and independent prime-axis operators lose exactly the mixed structure a global sign mechanism would need. `PL-169` identifies one classical place where arithmetic specificity returns: an additive off-diagonal relation coupling different integers.

2. **The theorem-level state is a profile, not a scalar margin.** `AF-131`--`AF-132` make this exact for recovery maps: downstream error depends jointly on reconstruction error and transport regularity. The same discipline applies whenever a Mathia construction passes through normalization, inversion, conditioning, or another representation before the final theorem.

3. **Derived statistics require their own matched controls.** `VIS-039` shows that a nonlinear information statistic can inherit or cancel the leading finite-size correction of the underlying law. Agreement with an expected power law is therefore not independent evidence unless the matched source-level baseline and estimator transfer are already controlled.

4. **The best positive reductions localize the missing source theorem.** `ANF-049`--`ANF-051` reduce the scalar branch to a compact cross-frequency coherence problem; `XF-045` pushes the truly remote Xi field to the critical buffer floor; `WI-163` removes the entire sublinear scalar support-edge class. These are productive because they shrink the unresolved category instead of introducing another representation.

5. **Several remaining routes now point to interaction that is external to their easiest coordinate systems.** Prime Lattice needs multiplication/addition coupling, Prime Circle needs nonseparable valuation-direction coupling, Weil Positivity needs pre-positivity mixed-prime/archimedean geometry, and Analytic Frontier needs coherence across frequencies rather than another pointwise separator. A successful route must derive that coupling from the source, not append it as a repair.
