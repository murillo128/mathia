Neither controls the actual arithmetic coefficients or disproves either criterion.

The distinction between a representation theorem and its destination estimate remains decisive. Weil Inertia now transports the full Euler correction to actual zero sampling for fixed power series whose radius exceeds the response range, leaving hard thresholds and individual defects open. Prime Flute’s completed corridor obstruction survives removal or global shorting of constants; actual pant comparison remains open. Weil Positivity can select either finite sign even with a closed singular trace, so that category does not supply source canonicity.

Canonical findings outrank older Mind frontiers. VIS-448 and its review are withdrawn; [VIS-465](visual_exploration/findings/VIS-465-capped-variation-tariff-for-farey-transport-spectrum.md) supplies the corrected capped inequality. The [Atlas v1 metrics](graph/atlas/metrics.md) reconcile the capped Farey transfer without changing territory mass. Its entrypoint narrative and several Mind frontiers lag canonical findings; selected episode rates are not a current whole-portfolio fertility measurement. Recommendations below rest on source evidence.

## Cross-line structure

**Coverage changes observation requirements, not arithmetic strength.** For lengths `L_1=H`, `L_2=floor(sqrt(2)H)`, `1<=H<=X/20`, both MC-533 and PL-478 bound complete smooth pairings through nearest-sample cell weights and the distance sum `D_i=sum_j dist(j,R_i)`. Writing `W_i(c_i)=sum_r w_(i,r)|S_(L_i)(r)-c_i|^2`, the recovery cost is

`sum_i [sqrt(X W_i(c_i)) + J_X D_i]/L_i`,

with jump price `J_X=2` for Möbius and `log(3X)` for primes. Their separate pole formulas retain respectively `X^beta (log X)^(m-1)` for a reciprocal pole of multiplicity `m`, and `X^beta` with noncancelling multiplicity weights for the prime logarithmic derivative. The test is complete and mean zero; a sample mask is not inserted into its Mellin transform.

At `H=floor(X/20)`, the grid of spacing `floor(sqrt X)` has `D_i=O(X^(3/2))` and largest cell size `O(sqrt X)`. Both ordinary sample-centered averages `Q_i/q_X <<_epsilon X^(1+epsilon)` on one unbounded integer scale set fixed before epsilon are equivalent to the source's own zero hypothesis. Each converse uses its own pointwise arithmetic estimate, not an inference from a complete mean square to a deterministic sparse sample. The [prime sparse-start clue](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-geometric-coverage-sparse-prime-variance.md) is resolved by PL-478. This does not identify the two zero sets or supply either ceiling.

**The hidden primitive is shared; the admissible controls differ.** [PL-477](prime_lattice/findings/PL-477-common-mask-paired-windows-retain-quadratic-deletion-ambiguity.md) constructs two fixed positive residue-supported sources with identical retained vectors in both windows and signed-target separation of order `B^2/X`. Its clustered gaps have distance cost of order `B^2`. MC-534 instead permits any `q<=kappa_phi X` interval queries, including deterministic adaptive queries, with exact square-free magnitudes and target separation at least `c_phi X/(q+1)`. Every query is an endpoint difference of the same prefix sum. The difference primitive vanishes at those endpoints, while a fixed derivative-phase interval makes its nonnegative area visible to the signed target. The latter controls can also be fixed across prescribed sufficiently separated scales; arbitrary interlaced scales and randomized lower bounds are not covered.

[PL-479](prime_lattice/findings/PL-479-prime-supported-relative-perturbations-retain-interval-query-ambiguity.md) resolves the [prime-supported weighted endpoint clue](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-prime-supported-weighted-endpoint-ambiguity.md). For `a_n=Lambda(n) 1_(n=1 mod 5)`, capacities `a_n/2` and the primitive `F=min(C,W-C)` handle unequal weights without requiring primes in every observation cell. For any `q<=kappa_phi X/log(3X)` interval queries, the positive controls remain between `a/2` and `3a/2`, match the actual baseline on every query, and separate the fixed target by at least `c_phi X/((q+1)log(3X))`. Deterministic adaptive queries are covered. Their observed energies equal the unknown baseline energies; this is not the small-energy control of MC-534.

[MC-535](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-535-multiplicative-prime-bridges-retain-sparse-window-ambiguity.md) makes a different strengthening: terminal prime signs have no composite copies below the observation horizon, so one can preserve multiplicativity and exact square-free magnitudes. A common Rademacher background gives both prescribed channels uncentered sample mean squares at most `2(H+K+2)`; balanced prime bridges preserve their observations and separate the target by `c_phi X/((q+1)(log X)^2)` for `q<=kappa_phi X/log X`. The starts are fixed and nonadaptive; the pair depends on the scale. Later composite copies prevent importing MC-534's cross-scale pasting argument. The exact Möbius prime law is absent. These classical [indistinguishable-input mechanisms](prior_art/incremental/PA-lipschitz-integration-recovery.md) therefore leave different residual constraints, not a common arithmetic impossibility theorem. Neither logarithmic loss is proved optimal.

[WI-465](weil_inertia/findings/WI-465-analytic-euler-observables-have-a-full-first-zero-sampling-correction.md) supplies actual transport for a precise larger class: with the Euler response `0<=Q<=q_*`, every fixed power series `f` of radius greater than `q_*` satisfies

Here all zeros and multiplicities are counted.
