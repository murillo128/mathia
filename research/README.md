## Cross-line synthesis

The strongest common boundary is now **information lost by scalarization or quotienting must be supplied before that loss**. `PC-244` shows that even coherent interference among several source-forced refinement histories becomes a universal shift once the paths are reduced to one scalar fixed-endpoint combination. `WP-237`--`WP-241` show an analogous but stronger failure after the automorphic scattering data have formed a central scalar normalizer: every ordinary meromorphic scalar postprocessor either preserves the whole completed package, corrupts divisor fidelity, or loses the standard divisor bridge. `PL-256` gives the complementary warning that merely reintroducing freedom by changing an operator domain does not help, because abstract Weyl data are programmable and the zeta-specific positivity condition is already RH-equivalent. The live spectral routes therefore have to derive coupling, sign, and completeness from arithmetic **before** the flexible/scalar stage.

A second boundary is **order of operations in transport**. Prime Flute is the cleanest example: the thin tangent really has the desired supercritical smoothing and the massive Green family is well controlled, yet `PF-268` proves that the naked finite seam loses the margin at arbitrarily high modes. The only surviving statement is about the combined Robin source transform followed by positive spatial propagation, which must be estimated before the endpoint weight is applied. Xi Flow reaches the information-theoretic analogue from the opposite side: even continuum spacetime data and positive analytic sources remain blind when all observations stay inside the maximal-contact region. In both cases an individually rich representation is irrelevant unless the exact source-to-destination composition preserves the discriminating currency.

A third convergence is that free geometry has become sufficiently sharp that the remaining work is increasingly **source-specific and scale-specific**. `ANF-162` localizes the bow endpoint threat to a square-root-`X` twisted residual event; `FD-027` shows that even finitely much exact Möbius ancestry cannot distinguish sparse GCD near-extremizers; `MC-196` makes the first genuinely nonlinear transverse-to-principal escape pay polynomial-shift pair/triple correlations; `NB-028` collapses the largest obvious coefficient quotient back to Mertens; and `WI-232` exhausts the local zero-count reservoir for a density-matched screen. These are different mathematical settings, but they agree on the procedural lesson: another generic norm, symmetry, or richer coordinate system is unlikely to move the endpoint. The next theorem must use a property of the actual arithmetic source at the exact scale consumed by the endgame.
