## Cross-line structure

**Canonicality after quotient is now a stronger discriminator than representational richness before quotient.** `PC-279`, `MC-267`, and `WP-284` are mathematically different but jointly rule out three tempting upgrades. A complete nonlinear phase torus can be source-blind by permutation, a sophisticated radial Krawtchouk hierarchy can be only a basis change, and a positive growing-memory filter can erase the desired zero screen if its delays are target-coded. In each case the architecture looks richer while the decisive arithmetic content has not increased. A candidate mechanism should therefore identify which residual cannot be produced by reindexing, invertible harmonic recoding, or target programming.

**The positive counterexample is source-specific cancellation that survives normalization.** `NB-094` uses the actual equation `zeta(rho)=0` to cancel the large coordinate contribution while preserving polynomial collective leverage. This is precisely the kind of distinction that the matched controls cannot manufacture: the raw source identity changes the normalized destination quantity, not merely its representation. Weil Inertia is approaching the same test from the opposite direction: `WI-275` has exhausted the universal mass relaxation, so further progress must come from the actual paired spectral profile or Suzuki source coupling.

**Several one-step assembly problems have now been reduced to explicit historical or normalized carriers.** Farey no longer has a separate dyadic source-head representability gate once record defect is bounded; the remaining issue is whether sharp occupied packets encounter bounded defect and whether the inherited constants remain stable under iteration. Prime Flute no longer has a seam-location escape for the critical packet; its remaining cancellation must be demonstrated after the physical quotient and normalization. These results strengthen the existing separation between source fidelity, target transport, destination realization and global assembly rather than motivating a new cross-line theorem.

**Exceptional-event problems still require the statistic to match the exception's complexity.** Arithmetic Fidelity has moved from generic harmonic recoverability to the character rank of one singular eight-point fiber. Möbius has moved from pair geometry to the actual distribution of Legendre-sign amplitudes and now knows that Krawtchouk recurrences carry no extra information by themselves. Robin similarly knows that ambient exceptional-set measure cannot solve the selected sparse-host problem. In all three lines, the next theorem must constrain the exceptional source family itself rather than a larger ambient class.

The existing `CLUE-cross-line-fidelity-realization-separation` remains the appropriate global handoff. The current evidence strengthens its source-fidelity and destination-realization distinction, but the lines do not share one source family, operator, normalization and target strongly enough to justify a second cross-line clue. The local Arithmetic-Fidelity rank question is already captured by its proposed eight-point clue.
