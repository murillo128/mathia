# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The common RH-facing gap remains an independently source-forced estimate in the precise norm used by the destination. Source reconstruction, auxiliary positivity and existence of a zero-sensitive test do not supply that estimate. No current Mathia finding proves RH.

The selected-scale recovery boundary is now closed for both prime and Möbius sources at fixed power windows. [MC-528](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-528-multiplicity-aware-annihilation-forces-mobius-variance-on-every-block.md) supplies the missing multiplicity-aware reciprocal-zeta division estimate and forces a variance floor on every sufficiently large block from any hypothetical off-critical zero. [PL-472](prime_lattice/findings/PL-472-gevrey-box-inversion-reaches-near-linear-prime-windows.md) separately reaches prime windows `H=floor(X/(log X)^B)`, for any fixed `B>1`: a diagonal ceiling on any unbounded scale set would then imply joint mod-5 GRH. These tests depend on the whole hypothetical zero set; neither finding supplies the independent variance ceiling. The concrete remaining transfer is [near-linear Möbius recovery](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-near-linear-mobius-variance-gevrey-inversion.md), not the already resolved fixed-power annihilation question.

Canonical findings outrank older Mind frontiers where they differ. No adversarial sidecar is currently open; this is not independent approval of the corpus. WI-002 is withdrawn, and [WI-456](weil_inertia/findings/WI-456-retracted-moment-transport-leaves-only-a-conditional-density-capstone.md) retains only a conditional moment-to-density theorem after retraction of the Yang–Yang arithmetic transport. Even a valid density-one theorem leaves individual exceptions possible. The [Atlas v1 metrics](graph/atlas/metrics.md) incorporate AF-581 selectively; their unchanged coverage model and older entrypoint narrative do not represent the full current source frontier. They are derived context, not evidence for the recommendations below.

## Cross-line structure

**Prime and reciprocal selected-scale recovery share a test mechanism, with different analytic inputs.** For `a_n=Lambda(n) 1_(n=1 mod 5)`, [PL-471](prime_lattice/findings/PL-471-sparse-zero-annihilators-force-prime-variance-on-every-large-block.md) isolates any zero `rho_0` with real part `beta>1/2` by a fixed compact mean-zero Mellin test. A power-saving off-critical zero count permits a genus-zero product and Gevrey multiplier that kill all unwanted zeros; the arithmetic logarithmic derivative leaves one noncancelling complex mode. No rightmost zero or finite exceptional family is needed.

For Möbius, [MC-528](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-528-multiplicity-aware-annihilation-forces-mobius-variance-on-every-block.md) repeats the unwanted factors with their full multiplicities. Titchmarsh's local product estimate lets these factors cancel before division is bounded: the residual product grows at most `T^C exp(C T^a)`, and the Gevrey multiplier decays as `exp(-c T^b)`, with `a<b<1`. This supplies the missing contour control, without zero separation or inverse-derivative bounds. A selected pole of order `m` retains a degree-`m-1` logarithmic polynomial even if its ordinary residue vanishes. Finite box inversion gives

`V_mu(X,floor X^theta) >= c floor(X^theta)^2 X^(2beta-1) (log X)^(2m-2)`

for every sufficiently large real `X`, at each fixed `0<theta<1`. Thus a ceiling `V_mu <<_epsilon X^(1+epsilon) H`, for every fixed `epsilon>0` uniformly on **any unbounded set**, excludes `beta>1-theta/2`. Both the [annihilator](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-multiplicity-aware-sparse-zero-annihilator.md) and [packet](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-reciprocal-zeta-selected-scale-pole-tail-certificate.md) clues are resolved. This does not prove an absolute reciprocal residue sum or a packet/residual inequality for every preassigned bank; MC-527's grouped contour remains a distinct valid representation.

[PL-472](prime_lattice/findings/PL-472-gevrey-box-inversion-reaches-near-linear-prime-windows.md) pays the moving-order cost explicitly. The sparse-zero test retains Gevrey order `q`; a growing finite Bernoulli inverse has bounded combined test norm and error `exp(-c (X/H)^(1/q))`. Choosing `1<q<B` gives the same prime floor uniformly for `1<=H<=X/(log X)^B`, each fixed `B>1`. One ceiling `V_1 << XH(log X)^A`, with fixed finite `A>=0`, at `H=floor(X/(log X)^B)` on any unbounded set then implies joint mod-5 GRH. The [Möbius handoff](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-near-linear-mobius-variance-gevrey-inversion.md) asks for this uniform extension with the reciprocal logarithmic polynomial retained. It must reconstruct the moving-order estimates; substituting a moving exponent into MC-528 is not licensed. No fixed finite derivative bank, effective acquisition from finitely many zeros, or result at `B=1` is supplied by PL-472.

The sparse matched controls remain relevant. [PL-470](prime_lattice/findings/PL-470-positive-source-strip-growth-and-the-infinite-pole-tail-gate.md) shows why its positive meromorphic control cannot replace the arithmetic source's growth estimates. Likewise [PL-465](prime_lattice/findings/PL-465-relative-frame-transfers-dirichlet-zero-bounds.md) and [PL-467's synthesis](prime_lattice/mind/intuition/MI-066-relative-frames-transfer-frontiers-but-admit-common-off-critical-poles.md) distinguish a relative covariance frame from the independent absolute ceiling needed for zero exclusion. The successful prime/reciprocal transfer removes a recovery obstacle, not that arithmetic obligation.

The Farey destination retains different hypotheses. [MC-520](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-520-rh-mellin-variance-closes-conditional-farey-window-target.md) already bounds its complete sliding Möbius moment by `X^(1+epsilon)H` under RH, excluding the balanced near-capacity branch through the retained Mellin difference. Removing RH removes both that input and the carrying-shell localization unless separately justified. A centered estimate also needs its mean contribution restored before entering the uncentered Farey moment. Neither selected-scale recovery theorem supplies a Möbius upper bound or Farey shell law.

**Finite stopping depends on the admitted calibration class.** [AF-580](arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-580-positive-noise-destroys-finite-stopping-under-unknown-polynomial-calibration.md) strengthens the finite-cap obstruction: with unknown unrestricted polynomial calibration and any fixed positive supremum-bounded error allowance, worst-case wrong-return probability is at least `(1-1/m) P_x(return)` on every strict-slack or continuous-error input. In particular, reliable almost-sure return fails even on a noiseless input when reliability is required over the noisy class. Polynomial approximation and bounded-error cutoffs match all labels on exhausting compact windows; competitors are fixed before the random seed. The positive full-ray radius `d(S)/4` survives, and noiseless ideal continuous validation remains a different experiment. This is a synthetic calibration theorem, not ordinary-prime inference.

[AF-581](arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-581-known-polynomial-degree-restores-sharp-full-ray-adaptive-recovery.md) supplies a concrete repair when degree `r` is known: `r+1` finite anchors, a locator and a pair-specific follow-up attain every strict allowance below the same full-ray radius for sufficiently remote finite prime-edit sets. Distant Lagrange anchors reproduce the polynomial exactly while their absolute weight sum tends to one. The required coordinate range and output magnitudes are not uniformly bounded; the equality is of supremal radii, not recovery at the endpoint. The sharp worst-family deficit with only `r+2` values remains. This changes an explicit nuisance hypothesis, not ordinary-prime inference.

The [AF–Xi comparison](mind/intuition/MI-039-calibration-gluing-transfers-randomized-stopping-bounds-only-in-the-admitted-function-class.md) keeps noise and nuisance classes separate. Xi's one total squared-error budget permits chord-average error to fall as `sigma sqrt(2/n)/h`; a fixed supremum-bounded error allows `2 epsilon/h`, without that averaging gain. [XF-203](xi_flow/findings/XF-203-unknown-jacobi-calibration-slack-admits-finite-certified-stopping.md) thus retains pointwise finite certification under its own hypotheses, while [XF-207](xi_flow/findings/XF-207-complex-value-queries-retain-the-analytic-calibration-stopping-obstruction.md) retains capped error `1/2` for the full holomorphic calibration class even with complex queries and an admitted compact size bound. Those differences prevent transferring AF-580 merely by renaming the source. Existing calibration clues carry the earlier transfers; no new equivalence is asserted.

**Closure, bounded realization and complete normalized response are different gates.** [NB-355](nyman_beurling/findings/NB-355-two-independent-seed-orbits-require-divergent-physical-components.md) sharpens NB-354's exact closure result. Two multiplicatively independent real-dilation seed spaces densely generate the continuous Nyman space, but their algebraic sum excludes the actual projected target. Approaching squared distance `1-b^2`, with `b=B(1)>0`, forces both physical component norms to infinity, with squared norms at least a seed-dependent constant times `log(1/epsilon)` for projection error `epsilon`. This persists after internal coefficient cancellations and is unconditional; under RH the projection is the target itself. The resulting explicit norm-budget error floor is necessary; no matching upper construction, finite-section rate, three-seed obstruction or zero-exclusion result follows.

[PC-454](prime_circle/findings/PC-454-self-adjoint-similarity-of-cotangent-towers-forces-haar-norm-equivalence.md) closes the bounded-metric repair within PC-453's bounded unchanged fixed-base cotangent actions. Similarity to any self-adjoint operator is equivalent to densities bounded above and below relative to Haar, not just orbit A2 boundedness or an unchanged real spectral band. For the augmented singleton, the optimal similarity condition number is exactly `sqrt(ess sup w / ess inf w)`, detected by its integer-translation exponential subgroup. The result allows arbitrary bounded invertible intertwiners. Changed kernels, unbounded intertwiners/realizations and growing bases remain outside it; the same classification holds for nonarithmetic base angles.

[PF-353](prime_flute/findings/PF-353-complete-common-frequency-fibres-retain-the-coupled-corridor-angle.md) corrects a different possible inference from partial information. PF-352's zero retained-pair entry coexists with two canonical correlations equal to `2 sqrt(cosh(2 pi))/(1+cosh(2 pi))` after complete transition elimination. Complete common-frequency fibres preserve a positive Cauchy displacement identity even when transition lattices differ. This rules out whole-angle decay for that model family, not compactness of one infinite actual flute operator. [PF-354](prime_flute/findings/PF-354-prime-cuff-lengths-are-rationally-independent-and-have-no-common-nonzero-frequency.md) now proves they share no nonzero frequency at all: exact cuff lengths are rationally independent. Pairwise coprime odd composite labels have the same property. This excludes the exact transplant without providing a quantitative near-frequency gap; approximate matching and pant/seam errors still require control in the same shorted diagonal norm. The [transition clue](prime_flute/clues/CLUE-positive-width-transition-schur-complement.md) still owns that actual-surface question.

[WP-449](weil_positivity/findings/WP-449-nonperiodic-maharam-positive-forms-from-coprime-counting.md) answers nonperiodic existence beyond WP-448's periodic classification. On the fixed critical Maharam space, weights `q_n(s)=e^s C_n(n e^(-s))/phi(n)` give a dense closed positive covariant form, where `C_n` counts integers coprime to `n`. Smoothing and adding a positive Hilbert norm yield bounded smooth strictly positive nonconstant vacuum profiles, but the full form stays unbounded through the excitation cost `n/phi(n)`. Its radial pairing is a classical floor-GCD incidence Gram; subtracting the unit baseline has both signs. Covariance selects neither the cutoff nor smoothing, and no signed finite-Weil/Gamma/polar comparison follows. Those are the remaining gates in the [completion clue](weil_positivity/clues/CLUE-bost-connes-affiliation-conditioning-boundary.md). None of these operator results identifies the other lines' objects or warrants a merger.

**Uniformity must match the claimed consequence.** [ANF-198](analytic_frontier/findings/ANF-198-fejer-profiles-force-vanishing-real-improvement-amplitudes.md) still refutes a profile-uniform exposure modulus and unrestricted common improving amplitude through Fejér controls. On sufficiently narrow fixed central support, however, every nonzero dominated positive-Fourier profile has a common positive **real** improving amplitude even though the modulus fails. The absolute improvement need not have a positive uniform floor. This removes an overbroad negative inference while leaving complex configurations and the source-locked high-pass open.

[FD-341](farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-341-linear-band-relative-transfer-is-equivalent-to-diverging-critical-energy.md) sharpens the Farey boundary: with `E_Q=sum D_k^2`, divergence `Q E_Q -> infinity` is equivalent to `Q^2 sum D_k^4/E_Q -> 0` and to vanishing relative rank/position coefficient error at every fixed linear cutoff. FD-339's absolute coefficient floor is compatible with that relative transfer. An unconditional Mertens–sawtooth truncation supplies sufficiency; neither the divergence condition nor its negation is proved. The same implication transfers normalized band energies, but the converse for energy shares is not asserted.

[XF-032](xi_flow/findings/XF-032-geometric-gap-ramps-are-null-modes-of-triple-bulk.md) likewise distinguishes local representation from the complete target. Its periodic heat-flow control has an adverse regrouped pointwise conductance, yet every fixed nonnegative tapered triple sum has nonnegative derivative at the tested instant. Taper-difference terms cannot be dropped after summation by parts. This control neither refutes aggregate positivity nor proves it for other configurations or later times.

**Peak density, waiting laws and zero-ordinate sampling remain separate.** [WI-457](weil_inertia/findings/WI-457-normalized-euler-hankel-symbols-have-local-blindness-and-recurrent-peaks.md) proves `sup_epsilon Q_epsilon(r,gamma) <= min(1,C r log(2+|gamma|))` for `|gamma|>=4`. Even arbitrary safe-line adaptation is blind when `r log(2+|gamma|)` tends to zero. This does not assert that actual off-line zeros satisfy that relation. [WI-459](weil_inertia/findings/WI-459-safe-line-optimization-preserves-double-exponential-euler-peak-rarity.md) extends double-exponential rarity and long-window bounds to the supremum over all safe-line offsets at each fixed small packet width. Two-sided scale localization and a finite parameter net justify the optimization. The global maximum nevertheless tends to `1/4`; no uniform decay over all heights follows. Neither this optimized height fraction nor recurrence bounds alignment with actual zero ordinates or gives a maximum-gap law.

The [prime-reset waiting-law portrait](visual_exploration/visualizations/prime-reset-clock-waiting-law.md) is a finite-model control with the complete one-time response distribution held fixed. For a single Haar phase on a reset-clock section, its exact no-hit probability is the circle-gap functional `sum_i(g_i-m)_+`; changing temporal dependence changes the waiting law even after time is scaled by event mass. This is supporting exploratory context, not a full Euler or zero-sampling theorem. The [weak-prime crossover](visual_exploration/visualizations/euler-hankel-weak-prime-crossover.md) separately warns that deleting a small positive coefficient can change the ultimate near-maximum exponent. The [return-length-bias portrait](visual_exploration/visualizations/prime-reset-return-length-bias.md) further distinguishes hit-start returns `tau` from zero-inclusive stationary forward waits `T`: `P(T>=N)=m E(tau-N)_+`, so `E(tau)=1/m` alone leaves the forward mean uncontrolled. It is a finite-model recurrence diagnostic, not an arithmetic sampling mechanism. These controls require the correct phase law, sampling ensemble, temporal dependence and accuracy relative to the deficit threshold.

**Robin source restrictions do not bound its remote tail.** [RE-263](robin_extremal/findings/RE-263-two-prime-balanced-mertens-repairs-have-one-candidate-and-a-square-root-gap-barrier.md) completes the two-deletion case beyond RE-262's support floor. For subdyadic odd primes `r<s`, at most one nontrivial count-preserving integer replacement exists, decided by one divisibility test; `(s-r)^2<=r+s` excludes it at every insertion height. Source realization additionally requires the smaller inserted label to be composite and beyond the retained prefix. Larger inseparable packets and extra-count repairs are not excluded. These synthetic Euler atoms are not ordinary divisor-sum factors, and the classification gives no estimate for the actual CA-selected remote mixed integral left by RE-259.

## Highest-leverage unresolved questions

1. **Independent arithmetic estimates:** prove source-derived variance/correlation bounds with the exact centering, exceptional-start budget and scale quantifiers. PL-472 and MC-528 resolve their stated selected-scale recovery implications, not their premises; relative frames still need an absolute bounded channel. Farey needs a weaker-hypothesis estimate and justified carrying shells.
2. **Near-linear reciprocal recovery:** test the [bounded-norm Gevrey box inverse](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-near-linear-mobius-variance-gevrey-inversion.md) with MC-528's multiplicity-aware test. A single near-linear variance law may replace the family of fixed-power premises; it remains an unvalidated transfer and supplies no upper estimate.
3. **Physical operator transport and selected tails:** find achievable NB cross-orbit norm budgets, compare PF's actual globally shorted form, and select a WP signed comparison beyond its classical counting-positive forms. Robin's CA-selected remote tail and WI's actual-zero alignment remain distinct arithmetic obligations.
4. **Observation robustness:** supply physical coordinate and precision bounds for AF's known-degree recovery, or source-derived restrictions excluding its unknown-degree aliases; establish Xi separation/access under its own noise geometry. A global margin, complex access or calibrated one-time peak law alone does not provide the required finite certificate or waiting law.
