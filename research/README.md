# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia has thirteen valid research lines. Ten are evidence-bearing and active: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain distinct pre-evidence scaffolds.

The strongest common bottleneck has sharpened from **carrier change plus quantitative transfer** into a **carrier-change plus source-valid margin gate**. A new representation is not useful merely because it retains a nonzero signal or admits an exact reformulation: after all universal controls and quotient symmetries are removed, it must retain a positive quantitative margin in the topology, scale, summability method, and operator category consumed by the final theorem. `AF-094`--`AF-095` give the cleanest abstract model: stable quotient-repair information is distance from a closed recoverable range, and that distance is exactly witnessed by a bounded dual functional with positive margin. This is now a useful program-level standard for deciding whether a surviving signal is robust or only algebraically nonzero.

`analytic_frontier` nearly closes the remaining universal affine scalar branch. `ANF-012` shows that conjugation-invariant high-multiplicity tests force every continuous compact-band spectral profile in that branch to be nonnegative, eliminating signed spectral density as an escape. `ANF-013` then reduces any scalar improvement over Montgomery--Taylor to a scale-free periodization problem: a viable profile must beat the ratio barrier `C(J)/p(J)`. Thus the remaining scalar question is a concrete extremal inequality; failure there would force an actual carrier change rather than more scalar enrichment.

`weil_inertia` clarifies what a useful pre-scalar margin must measure. `WI-128` shows that extensive horizontal screening would force a macroscopic collapsing tail in a raw reciprocal-node Vandermonde, but `WI-130` proves the converse diagnostic is false: a macroscopic raw tail can collapse through harmless confluent geometry while Lamzouri's anti-invariant `g/h` quotient remains extensively transverse. The task-aligned object is therefore the horizontal Schur-complement/transversality quotient, not raw conditioning. `WI-129` is under active adversarial review because conjugate-pair duplication does not by itself destroy scalar Riesz-basis stability; its bounded-strip equivalence must not be used until repaired.

The same pattern appears in the newer negative controls. `MC-042`--`MC-044` show that fixed Riesz smoothing remains RH-equivalent, while growing order faces both a normalization-vacuity threshold and an independent endpoint-visibility delay even after kernel-mass renormalization. `PC-158` makes the one-hole new-prime puncture a positive but prime-blind spectral shift. `WP-136` makes every fixed continuous nonnegative repeated-prime full-spectrum trace either zero or extensive, while `WP-137` shows the canonical singular Green escape produces the generic logarithm of cover degree rather than exact finite Mangoldt weight. `VIS-014` identifies circular zero-entry log means exactly with the radial zero-distance multiset, and `PL-140` shows that even all positive-power pretentious distances remain prime-wise reflection-blind. These are different manifestations of the same rule: complexity can grow while the decisive quotient stays unchanged.

There are also genuine refinements rather than only closures. `XF-013` finds an endpoint `L log L` adjacent-gap Lyapunov for the linearized Xi lattice: it retains the desired `h^2` raw scale and controls the `L^1` Cauchy driver without the logarithmic operator loss of the near-`L^1` `L^p` route. The remaining gap is source-specific and nonlinear: the estimate still belongs to the ordered real-gap linearization and does not itself control the full Xi heat flow or the de Bruijn--Newman constant. Across the portfolio, the next advance should look like this: identify a carrier that survives the exact quotient controls, prove a positive source-valid margin on that carrier, and prove the inverse/transfer theorem that preserves that margin all the way to the RH-adjacent target.

## Research-line portfolio

### `analytic_frontier` — `continue`, sharply narrowed

Ordered local processing remains the demonstrated way to beat the global pair-scalar baseline, but increasing point count inside the same pressure architecture saturates. `ANF-012` now forces nonnegative compact-band spectrum for every universal affine scalar certificate, and `ANF-013` reduces the residual support-one scalar route to the periodization ratio `C(J)/p(J)`. The highest-value scalar task is to prove or refute the Montgomery--Taylor floor for that ratio. If the floor is universal, the branch should stop trying richer affine scalar witnesses and move to a genuinely non-scalar, non-affine, source-specific, or higher-correlation carrier.

### `arithmetic_fidelity` — `continue`, narrowed to stable defect

`AF-092`--`AF-093` identify Lipschitz-versus-linear quotient repair with an operator-extension defect on the barycentric kernel. `AF-094` distinguishes algebraic defect from stable defect: the quotient is norm-separated exactly modulo the closure of extendable operators, with closed range equivalent to a uniform extension-cost bound. `AF-095` dualizes the result, making a bounded annihilating functional with positive margin exactly the certificate of robust nonlinear repair. The live arithmetic question is to produce such a defect canonically from a source representation, with the required equivariance, provenance, range fidelity, and downstream relevance rather than merely exhibiting a nonextendable operator in an enlarged category.

### `farey_discrepancy` — pre-evidence

Keep distinct but pre-evidence until a theorem-grade local or multiscale Farey discriminator survives the classical Franel--Landau/Möbius reduction and matched reduced-rational controls. A complete scalar reduction belongs in `mobius_cancellation`, not as a duplicate line.

### `mobius_cancellation` — `continue`, sharply narrowed

Qualitative logarithmic decorrelation and fixed-order smoothing are no longer credible transfer mechanisms by themselves. `MC-042` makes every fixed Riesz order carry the same RH zero-free burden. `MC-043` shows that a growing normalized order becomes vacuous near the square-root exponent once Gamma normalization alone shrinks total kernel mass, and `MC-044` identifies a second obstruction after renormalization: growing order makes the current endpoint exponentially hard to see, with constant-strength visibility displaced to future scales of order `kN`. A surviving smoothing route therefore needs an explicit inversion carrier—same-order multiscale control, localization near the current arithmetic scale, derivative/variation information from independently weaker arithmetic, or another nonlinear/multiplicative mechanism—with a proved polynomial transfer back to ordinary Mertens scale.

### `nyman_beurling` — pre-evidence

### `prime_circle` — `continue`, narrowed to residual fine organization

Fixed-support scalar data already classicalize to Hardy--Littlewood tuple information, and the canonical full-chord refinement channels are strongly constrained. `PC-155`--`PC-157` reduce new-prime coarse compression, repeated-prime fine fibers, and new-prime bulk to low-complexity commuting/Bloch structures. `PC-158` then closes the obvious puncture escape: removing the one old residue per fiber is an exact positive spectral shift with only a bounded macroscopic budget and the same geometry is reproduced for composite fiber sizes. ### `prime_flute` — `continue`, narrowed by reference stability

A surviving mechanism must therefore be a genuinely collective full-surface operator/scattering or infinite-orbit invariant, prove reference stability first, and then establish the exact trace ideal or analytic class needed to amplify a source-specific residual.

The line still requires mesoscopic rather than fixed-jet memory, but the norm boundary has improved. `XF-012` shows that near-endpoint `L^p` gap moments can recover strong fractional-driver control only with a logarithmic Hilbert-transform loss as `p` approaches one. `XF-013` replaces that family by an endpoint `L log L` adjacent-gap Orlicz seminorm that is an exact Lyapunov for the periodic linearized lattice, has raw scale `Theta(h^2)`, and controls the continuum `L^1` Cauchy driver with an `h`-independent constant. ## Cross-line structure

1. **Same-carrier complexity is repeatedly saturating.** Analytic Frontier reduces the residual affine scalar problem to one periodization extremal; Möbius Cancellation shows both fixed-order equivalence and growing-order visibility loss; Prime Circle and Weil Positivity classicalize increasingly complete refinement spectra; Visual Exploration and Prime Lattice identify exact radial/reflection quotients. A proposal should specify which quotient it escapes, not merely add order, depth, resolution, or more coordinates.

2. **The useful invariant is task-aligned quantitative separation, not raw nondegeneracy.** Arithmetic Fidelity makes this exact as distance from a closed recoverable range with a dual witness margin. Weil Inertia supplies the concrete warning: a raw Vandermonde tail can collapse while the anti-invariant horizontal quotient stays coercive. Prime Flute similarly requires a reference-stable full operator invariant rather than a nonzero coordinate residual, and Xi Flow requires control in the fractional norm actually driving the dynamics.

3. **Source specificity must survive matched controls before positivity or amplification matters.** `PC-158` is reproduced by composite fiber sizes, `WP-137` sees generic cover degree rather than primality, `PL-140` survives arbitrary prime-wise reflections, `VIS-014` survives angular rearrangement at fixed radii, and Prime Flute has an all-composite marked-tail clone. These controls do not prove the corresponding programs impossible; they identify the exact information that a surviving carrier must add.

4. **Every carrier change now owes an inverse theorem.** Möbius smoothing needs endpoint/current-scale recovery; Weil Inertia needs growing-period/source transfer for the grouped horizontal quotient; Arithmetic Fidelity needs a source-natural positive dual margin; Weil Positivity needs exact Mangoldt-scale extraction before regular positive assembly; Xi Flow needs nonlinear/global propagation of its endpoint Orlicz control. Exact representation without quantitative return to the target category is diagnostic progress, not a final mechanism.
