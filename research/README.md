## Cross-line structure

Arithmetic Fidelity, Möbius Cancellation, and Nyman now separate three meanings of “source cost.” `AF-345` identifies an exact quotient resource: sign count is merely prime-gap occupancy, while retained mass forces concentration moments when occupancy falls. `MC-296` proves that genuine arithmetic can make nearly an entire endpoint spectrum individually expensive, but expensive modes may still coexist in a large usable quotient. `NB-125` goes one step further by putting a source-native recurrence defect directly in the destination geometry and converting its possible horizontal escape into rank-weighted height entropy. The reusable test is therefore not whether a representation looks complex or expensive, but whether a **joint source budget can be shown to constrain the actual endpoint extremizer**.

Prime Circle and Visual give the complementary readout test. `PC-295--PC-296` show that both positive and nonnormal one-profile spectral readouts can erase source specificity in different ways: singular values retain a universal Green profile while eigenvalues become asymptotically quasinilpotent. `VIS-224`, by contrast, simplifies the live optimization without changing its coarse asymptotic scale: binary signs, the continuous torus, and the real SDP differ only by a universal factor. A useful reduction must therefore be audited for whether it **preserves endpoint scale**, not for whether it retains more formal degrees of freedom.

Farey and Analytic Frontier remain the clearest matched-control warnings about source marginals. `FD-126` preserves a remarkably physical squarefree/unit alphabet and still defeats generic multiscale coherence on sparse horizons; `ANF-186` preserves the prime/rough alphabet, densities, mean, diagonal scale and local mass while defeating the critical detector by carrier-aware placement. Both now require an actual arithmetic correlation law, not another marginal constraint. Nyman is the positive comparator because its zero-power annihilator is a relation that the generic inverse-section extremizer cannot satisfy for free.

The thin-endpoint lines continue to compress their residuals into specific exceptional configurations. Prime Lattice has one scalar finite-`s` matching condition plus amplitude selection; Robin has either a near-edge pair satisfying a cancelling phase arc or a diffuse cloud; Weil Inertia has rare-host incidence; Weil Positivity has to leave the support-preserving affine Herglotz family entirely to alter the wrong Gamma curvature. These are useful reductions because they identify the exact operation or configuration on which new arithmetic input must act.

The existing global fidelity/realization clue and `MI-009`, `MI-012`, `MI-013`, and `MI-015` already cover the reusable program-level lessons about source realization, destination sensitivity, relocated endpoint currencies, and endpoint-faithful relaxations. The current delta sharpens those principles but does not expose a new destination-specific cross-line transfer with a distinct cheapest decisive test, so no new Master clue is justified.
