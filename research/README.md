## Cross-line structure


**Local integrality, directed-edge inventories and even reflection do not bound global transport.** FD-323 gives an unreflected three-near-one-defect control, whereas FD-324 preserves reflection with four near-one defects. Both reach the same explicit-family 3/pi² energy supremum, and FD-319's smaller reflected constant was not a ceiling. These are artificial labeled-edge grids, not genuine Farey orders. RE-249/250 give the parallel warning for generalized-prime gap fidelity, and RE-251 shows that even fixed congruence-class PNT panels leave the *synthetic* ambiguity intact. In contrast, AF-563 uses discrete prime-label spacing to guarantee a high **relative** one-sample identification margin, not to identify actual Robin extremizers or prove source rigidity.


**Spectral comparison requires relative transport, variation, parity, and model-specific normalization.** VIS-447 controls every normalized full sine subset by min(1,sqrt(E_p/T_p)), VIS-448 bounds high-band mass by R_p/(2q), and VIS-449 identifies the vanishing even-sector m~k boundary layer of FD-323. VIS-450 strengthens the latter's transfer by projecting before normalization: with finite T_even>=c/k, k E_p->0 suffices, subject to separate finite-transport convergence. FD-324 has an equally energetic **even-only** reflected fixed-mode limit, opposite FD-323's odd-only limit. Each null needs its own expected total and band energy, an explicit absolute-mode or parity-index convention, and a fixed-parameter prime limit before the family limit. The accepted Farey–Visual clues remain open; [[research/visual_exploration/clues/CLUE-farey-reflection-parity-equal-energy-control]] proposes the distinct energy-matched parity test.

**Higher-order or changing-window information can survive second-order equality without arithmetic specificity.** MC-515's rectangular dependence yields a non-Gaussian limit despite exactly matching binomial mean, variance and pairwise statistics. VIS-441–VIS-444 show that matching full triple correlations can still leave different hitting laws, while the complete occupation-moment hierarchy recovers the Haar avoidance law. VIS-445/446 refine fixed-arc occupation near a moving gap boundary, but growing Wang shells require new uniformity. These are distinct models, not a proved Möbius–Wang identification; the outstanding test is an arithmetic, source-selected higher-order or hitting residual.


**Operator category, centering and normalization are part of the theorem.** PC-436 extends zero-free Hilbert-shell completeness through finite Fourier masks without a genuine zeta-weighted sparse converse or frame bounds. PL-450/451 exclude both raw Fejér and raw rectangular polynomial-resolution image gates below the input-limited 5/6 exponent, even when TH/X grows. PL-452 further shows that small centered *absolute* energy is compatible with either rank-one or identity **diagonal-normalized** centered frames under first-order matched controls. This is not a counterexample to a genuine second-order arithmetic covariance estimate. PF-345's raw Schur smallness does not prove a small normalized angle; WP-437's noncompact prime-ray limit retains a negative Weil block; XF-188/190/191 distinguish normalized-frame loss from raw physical known-endpoint recovery and show that one scale-adapted complex acquisition, not the row count itself, is the decisive known-atom resource. AF-561's corrected right-tail conditioning likewise coexists with AF-552/554's boundary/critical-window carriers.


**Near-optimal models and population statements cannot exclude individual zeros.** WI-445's no-real-triples theorem holds at Wang's exact optimizer and under bounded-span near-minimizer perturbations; WI-446 constructs admissible smooth near-minimizers with **infinite** orthogonal lattices whose spacings escape every fixed span. WI-447 now prices the optimizer's best triple floor by `3/(2 pi^4 R^4)` and the source-admissible smooth test needed for an exactly orthogonal triple by `Theta_lambda(R^-4)`. Every asymptotically sharp triple is balanced, full-span and phase-locked; none of these frequencies are known to be zeta ordinates. A viable application still needs independent zero-ordinate packing, fixed tests before the zeta-height limit and control of off-line conjugate blocks. ANF-191's external half-plane claim remains unverified, and NB-345's signed operator residue does not establish physical Ford-target occupation.

**A single measurement is not a source-independent identifiability theorem.** AF-563 constructs one prechosen real parameter for every *known* finite prime-edit candidate set and retains `rho≈0.979705` of a vanishing full-curve noise margin. XF-191 obtains a nonvanishing physical-displacement margin with one complex probe only after the moving atom radius is known, by letting the probe approach the imaginary heat boundary at the correct scale. Their different source families and norms do not imply a shared one-sample theorem; [[research/xi_flow/clues/CLUE-known-candidate-holomorphic-scale-grid]] asks how many prechosen holomorphic scales are needed when the Xi-model radius is unknown but belongs to a known finite set.
