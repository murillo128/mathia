## Cross-line structure

1. **Matched subtraction can be mathematically stronger than separate control of two good objects.** `PF-172`--`PF-173` show this exactly: each absolute recoupling carries an order-one zero mode, while the matched difference cancels it and becomes trace summable. `MC-090` identifies the arithmetic analogue by showing that separately controlled lower divisor layers do not remove the surviving top mode. The order “subtract first, then take the norm or estimate” is now a concrete program-level design rule rather than a stylistic preference.

2. **Source canonicity has an information-depth hierarchy.** `AF-139` proves that covariance plus full affine naturality determines one metric but only second-order information; `AF-140` escapes that ceiling only because the full smooth source law supplies extra translation structure. `VIS-045`--`VIS-046` show why an unrestricted metric family is not a substitute: without a bounded source-compatible gauge class, even orientation sign can be selected by representation choice.

3. **Signed selectors repeatedly disappear when converted too early into positive or self-adjoint objects.** `PC-179`--`PC-180` and `WP-163`--`WP-164` close Mellin, constant symmetric cross-shell, positive-homogeneity, and semigroup-positive shortcuts around the cyclotomic flux. The remaining route must create the sign theorem from a coupled finite--archimedean or noncommuting assembly while the cancellation-supported selector is still present.

4. **Finite-order success is no longer enough at the strongest Xi frontier.** `XF-052` removes unknown low-band arithmetic data at time zero, and `XF-053` removes every finite heat-jet transfer into the memory band. A countermechanism must now be genuinely nonperturbative; conversely, a uniform analyticity or quasianalyticity theorem would convert a long chain of local results into the needed finite-time exclusion.

5. **Retaining the right relation helps only if the destination operation also retains it.** `ANF-058` advances by keeping the exact horizontal curvature correlation that a support-free envelope discarded, while `PL-176` shows complete additive-cube averaging washes out a genuinely new additive relation. The reusable question is not merely “what information was added?” but “what source-forced coupling survives the exact operation that the final theorem actually consumes?”
