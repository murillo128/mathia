# Cross-line structure

The dominant new distinction is **per-resolution admissibility versus cross-resolution provenance**. Farey and Nyman now have sharp matched controls showing that surprisingly strong fixed-horizon or fixed-finite source equations can coexist with the bad endpoint behavior. This is not evidence that the true sources fail; it says the next theorem must couple different resolutions so that one physical source, rather than a freshly rebuilt control at every scale, has to satisfy all constraints simultaneously. Arithmetic Fidelity points in the same direction from the complexity side: the centered source is faithful but its complexity grows with scale, so a fixed-order view necessarily throws away the coherence one is trying to exploit.

A second pattern is **activation threshold versus mass threshold**. Prime Circle reaches the geometric onset of exact resonance at `B asymp sqrt(q)` but the microscopic band carries too little normalized operator mass. Visual has nontrivial cycle frustration but its packing certificate is useful only when it almost saturates the entire absolute envelope. Prime Lattice has a rigorous finite-`s` virial identity but the relevant trace migrates into an exponentially thin layer as the endpoint is approached. These lines now need mesoscopic accumulation, envelope saturation, or singular-layer control — not merely proof that the new structure exists.

A third pattern is **source-forced structure versus programmable representation**. Weil Positivity shows that fixed-source spectral cuts are too rigid while arbitrary compressions are too expressive to explain anything; Robin shows that even a full finite algebraic hierarchy can remain a deterministic re-expression of one edge state unless near-edge spectral mass enters; Analytic Frontier is stronger precisely because its critical detector is fixed and nonadaptive, leaving a clean arithmetic residual theorem rather than another representational degree of freedom.

The existing global fidelity/realization clue together with `MI-008`, `MI-012`, `MI-013`, `MI-015`, and `MI-016` already cover these distinctions. The local Visual watch has also produced an envelope-saturation clue matching `VIS-219`, superseding the older density-only criterion as a decision surface. The new cross-line synthesis is material, but it does not yet establish a reusable theorem strong enough to justify a new Master clue.
