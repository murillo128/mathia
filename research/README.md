## Cross-line structure

1. **Provenance now has a decoding-rate gate.** `AF-163`/`AF-164` separate the size of the admissible source family from the ability of the retained channel to distinguish it. Shared identity can prevent forbidden Cartesian explosion, but a large coherent codebook is still useless if its rate exceeds local discrimination capacity or its overlap geometry prevents reliable recovery. Source provenance is valuable only when it remains quantitatively decodable in the destination decision class.

2. **The adversarial family must grow in the same variables as the proposed resolution.** `ANF-075` defeats every fixed support cap and `ANF-078` defeats every fixed occupancy cap, so the remaining real control must let both grow. Möbius moves its first relevant shell scale to `Theta(log log N)`, while Prime Lattice reduces shrinking windows back to pointwise data. A fixed finite audit cannot certify a mechanism whose decisive relation migrates to a growing source scale.

3. **Signed extraction must happen before positive or separate summaries.** `MC-109`/`MC-110` show that huge positive Hamming shells cancel only after cross-degree recombination; `WI-184` leaves a coherent selected bow witness whose significance depends on extraction from the complementary zeta amplitude; `PC-192` shows that scalar Fourier zero creation after renormalization is anti-selective. A candidate proof should identify the signed source relation and the operation that isolates it before norms, positivity, or complementary mixing discard its phase.

4. **Excellent local transport can coexist with a genuine global-carrier obstruction.** `XF-073` gives source-relative accuracy at overwhelming margin, but `XF-075`/`XF-076` rule out the nearest exact reference-only and finite-band global repairs. Prime Flute has the complementary gap: qualitative marked rigidity exists, but the destination needs an energy-linear quantitative modulus. In both cases the interface theorem must be proved in the resource actually consumed downstream rather than inferred from a nearby exact or qualitative representation.

5. **Generic coordinate changes and averaging do not manufacture arithmetic information.** `VIS-065` collapses the simplest hybrid contrast to one factor plus residual, `PL-187`/`PL-190` collapse diffuse or shrinking phase observations, and `WP-177` keeps ordinary infinite-dimensional passive scalarization inside Schur.
