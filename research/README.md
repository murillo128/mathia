## Cross-line structure

**Screening must be optimized before complexity is counted.** `WI-264` can erase every finite prime-power lag at once; `NB-081` can remove the exact benign future-prediction contribution by increasing the screening depth; `FD-082` shows that even recurrent good scales may be too sparse in the logarithmic coordinate that a power-saving argument consumes. These are not the same theorem, but they impose the same procedural test: first quotient out the best admissible screening/completion, then ask whether the residual grows in the destination's currency. Counting source atoms, refinement steps, or good episodes before this elimination can badly overstate progress.

**The relevant rank is the rank of the target-visible block, not a nearby global object.** `MC-254` gives an explicit structural warning: full Rédei rank does not control the shell cross-block blind kernel. `AF-304` gives the geometric analogue: restricting the number of forgotten positive coordinates still leaves enough phase geometry to span the full complex correction plane. `PC-274`--`PC-275` give the observable analogue: retaining ordered information or centering a fixed-bandwidth statistic does not by itself make the residual nonclassical. The next theorem in each line must constrain the actually reachable/visible quotient, not a stronger-looking ambient proxy.

**Exact factorization prevents double counting.** `PF-316` identifies only two independent physical endpoint channels; `V_H` cannot be credited as a third regularizer because any attenuation is paid by `Z_LH`. `RE-084` similarly removes the first-layer event ledger as an independent source of assembly information once endpoints are fixed. This is a useful anti-circularity pattern: when two diagnostics are deterministic transforms or complementary defects of the same underlying datum, they cannot be accumulated as independent evidence.

**Destination realization remains an independent resource after source information survives.** `WP-275`--`WP-276` show that the missing absolutely continuous continuum cannot be obtained by an exact bounded intertwiner or trace-class self-adjoint surgery from the singular arithmetic carrier. `PL-289` gives a milder quantitative version: the correct eigenstate has much better regularity than the ambient space, yet the modulus constant still does not reach the destination margin. Source fidelity, destination spectral/topological type, and the final quantitative margin therefore remain theorem-level separate gates.

**Visual and Farey now expose aggregate rather than local obstructions.** `VIS-197` removes uncontrolled local singular-series growth from each dangerous affine gap fiber, while `FD-082` removes the inference that power-syndetic recurrence automatically supplies enough accumulated gain. Both lines now need an aggregate theorem—weighted fiber incidence for Visual, distinct-horizon anti-concentration or gap-sensitive accumulation for Farey—rather than another local witness.

The existing proposed `CLUE-global-cross-line-fidelity-realization-separation` already carries the program-level distinction between source fidelity, target transport, destination realization, and global assembly. The new screening/rank examples sharpen that clue's motivation but still do not share one source family, operator, normalization, and target on which a new cross-line theorem can be stated exactly.
