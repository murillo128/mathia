# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The common RH-facing gap remains an independently source-forced estimate in the precise norm used by the destination. Source reconstruction, auxiliary positivity and existence of a zero-sensitive test do not supply that estimate. No current Mathia finding proves RH.

The fixed-target recovery boundary is now closed for prime and Möbius sources within the iterated-log family. [PL-474](prime_lattice/findings/PL-474-nonquasianalytic-tests-close-the-iterated-log-window-boundary.md) and [MC-530](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-530-nonquasianalytic-tests-reach-logarithmic-mobius-windows.md) extend their zero-forced centered variance floors to every integer `1<=H<=X/[log X (loglog X)^r]`, for each fixed `r>1`, on every sufficiently large real block. [PL-473](prime_lattice/findings/PL-473-box-fourier-zeros-obstruct-logarithmic-window-test-inversion.md) excludes the same fixed-target power-saving inverse certificate at `0<=r<=1`. This is a boundary of the approximation method, not of variance itself. The independent arithmetic ceiling remains missing; [MC-529](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-529-single-near-linear-centered-variance-law-is-equivalent-to-rh.md) already identifies its precise epsilon-loss near-linear premise on any unbounded scale set as RH-equivalent.

Canonical findings outrank older Mind frontiers where they differ. No adversarial sidecar is currently open; this is not independent approval of the corpus. WI-002 is withdrawn, and [WI-456](weil_inertia/findings/WI-456-retracted-moment-transport-leaves-only-a-conditional-density-capstone.md) retains only a conditional moment-to-density theorem after retraction of the Yang–Yang arithmetic transport. Even a valid density-one theorem leaves individual exceptions possible. The [Atlas v1 metrics](graph/atlas/metrics.md) incorporate the shared logarithmic-inversion extension selectively; their unchanged coverage model and older entrypoint narrative do not represent the full current source frontier. They are derived context, not evidence for the recommendations below.

## Cross-line structure

**Prime and reciprocal selected-scale recovery share a test mechanism, with different analytic inputs.** For `a_n=Lambda(n) 1_(n=1 mod 5)`, [PL-471](prime_lattice/findings/PL-471-sparse-zero-annihilators-force-prime-variance-on-every-large-block.md) isolates any zero `rho_0` with real part `beta>1/2` by a fixed compact mean-zero Mellin test. A power-saving off-critical zero count permits a genus-zero product and Gevrey multiplier that kill all unwanted zeros; the arithmetic logarithmic derivative leaves one noncancelling complex mode. No rightmost zero or finite exceptional family is needed.

For Möbius, [MC-528](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-528-multiplicity-aware-annihilation-forces-mobius-variance-on-every-block.md) repeats the unwanted factors with their full multiplicities. Titchmarsh's local product estimate lets these factors cancel before division is bounded: the residual product grows at most `T^C exp(C T^a)`, and the Gevrey multiplier decays as `exp(-c T^b)`, with `a<b<1`. This supplies the missing contour control, without zero separation or inverse-derivative bounds. A selected pole of order `m` retains a degree-`m-1` logarithmic polynomial even if its ordinary residue vanishes. Finite box inversion gives

`V_mu(X,floor X^theta) >= c floor(X^theta)^2 X^(2beta-1) (log X)^(2m-2)`

for every sufficiently large real `X`, at each fixed `0<theta<1`. Thus a ceiling `V_mu <<_epsilon X^(1+epsilon) H`, for every fixed `epsilon>0` uniformly on **any unbounded set**, excludes `beta>1-theta/2`. Both the [annihilator](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-multiplicity-aware-sparse-zero-annihilator.md) and [packet](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-reciprocal-zeta-selected-scale-pole-tail-certificate.md) clues are resolved. This does not prove an absolute reciprocal residue sum or a packet/residual inequality for every preassigned bank; MC-527's grouped contour remains a distinct valid representation.

[MC-529](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-529-single-near-linear-centered-variance-law-is-equivalent-to-rh.md) completes the [near-linear handoff](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-near-linear-mobius-variance-gevrey-inversion.md). It preserves Gevrey order through the multiplicity-aware annihilator and logarithmic composition, reconstructs the finite Bernoulli inverse with error `exp(-c (X/H)^(1/q))`, and retains the full degree-`m-1` polynomial. For each fixed `B>1`, choosing `1<q<min(B,1/a)` gives the same floor uniformly for all integer `1<=H<=X/(log X)^B`. At the upper window the floor contradicts an epsilon-loss ceiling on every unbounded scale set if any `beta>1/2` exists. MC-520 supplies the RH converse, including real blocks and empirical centering. This equivalence does not assert an RH converse for a stronger fixed-logarithmic-loss ceiling. Constants depend on the whole hypothetical zero set; the derivative-bank size grows, and no effective onset is supplied.

[PL-474](prime_lattice/findings/PL-474-nonquasianalytic-tests-close-the-iterated-log-window-boundary.md) and [MC-530](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-530-nonquasianalytic-tests-reach-logarithmic-mobius-windows.md) settle the stronger compact regularity test. Classical infinite convolution supplies derivative envelope `C A^n n! log(e+n)^(q n)`, `1<q<r`. Each finding proves its preservation through the respective annihilator, logarithmic composition and finite Bernoulli inverse, obtaining bounded combined norm and error `C exp(-c/[h log(e+1/h)^q])`. At `H<=X/[log X (loglog X)^r]`, `r>1`, this absorbs the prime coefficient mass `O(X log X)` or Möbius mass `O(X)`. The reciprocal contour additionally retains the degree-`m-1` logarithmic polynomial. The [logarithmic-window clue](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-logarithmic-window-inversion-after-box-fourier-obstruction.md) is resolved. PL-473's Fourier-zero/logarithmic-integral obstruction remains for `0<=r<=1` with one fixed nonzero compact target and power-saving global norm error on all large real scales, including rounded lengths. Moving targets, multiple kernels, signed arithmetic error cancellation and sparse-scale inversion remain outside that obstruction. The closure supplies neither an arithmetic ceiling nor effective acquisition of the zero-dependent test.

The sparse matched controls remain relevant. **Finite stopping depends on the admitted calibration class.** [AF-580](arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-580-positive-noise-destroys-finite-stopping-under-unknown-polynomial-calibration.md) strengthens the finite-cap obstruction: with unknown unrestricted polynomial calibration and any fixed positive supremum-bounded error allowance, worst-case wrong-return probability is at least `(1-1/m) P_x(return)` on every strict-slack or continuous-error input. In particular, reliable almost-sure return fails even on a noiseless input when reliability is required over the noisy class. Polynomial approximation and bounded-error cutoffs match all labels on exhausting compact windows; competitors are fixed before the random seed. The positive full-ray radius `d(S)/4` survives, and noiseless ideal continuous validation remains a different experiment. This is a synthetic calibration theorem, not ordinary-prime inference.

[AF-581](arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-581-known-polynomial-degree-restores-sharp-full-ray-adaptive-recovery.md) supplies a concrete repair when degree `r` is known: `r+1` finite anchors, a locator and a pair-specific follow-up attain every strict allowance below the same full-ray radius for sufficiently remote finite prime-edit sets. [AF-582](arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-582-quadratic-calibration-changes-sharp-window-cost-from-logarithmic-to-algebraic.md) and [AF-583](arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-583-fixed-polynomial-degrees-share-algebraic-range-order.md) price coordinate range separately. **Closure, bounded realization and complete normalized response are different gates.** [NB-356](nyman_beurling/findings/NB-356-finite-orbit-target-assembly-is-equivalent-to-a-reciprocal-phase-series.md) supplies the reverse implication missing from NB-355's cost obstruction. For a fixed finite seed set `J` of at least two integers greater than one and `q in J`, the actual projected target belongs to the algebraic sum of the closed real-dilation orbit spaces exactly when

`sum_(ell>=1) [ell^2 sum_(j in J minus {q}) ||ell log j/log q||_(R/Z)^2]^(-1) < infinity`.

An onto Hardy lattice sampler constructs finite-norm components when this series converges. Every fixed family whose logarithms have rational rank two still has divergent total component energy, at least logarithmic in inverse approximation error, despite full closed-span recovery. Almost every pair of independent real seed exponents instead gives convergence; this is not a theorem for a specified integer triple such as `{2,3,5}`. Finite-energy orbit vectors may need infinitely many dilations, and under RH only does the projected target equal the target itself. No finite-section rate or new RH criterion follows.

Badly approximable ratios give the sharp order `log(1/epsilon)`; every fixed multiplicatively independent integer pair has a coarse polynomial upper bound from linear forms in logarithms. The two cutoff scales differ. The constructed vectors remain in complete real-dilation orbit spaces, retain the Burnol projection floor and may require infinitely many generators; no finite-section acquisition rate follows.

**Uniformity must match the claimed consequence.** [ANF-200](analytic_frontier/findings/ANF-200-small-real-notch-amplitudes-have-an-exact-two-site-floor.md) strengthens ANF-199's real stationary-contact criterion. **Peak density, waiting laws and zero-ordinate sampling remain separate.** [WI-460](weil_inertia/findings/WI-460-adaptive-euler-peaks-remain-rare-at-zeta-zero-ordinates.md) resolves a sampling gap left by WI-457--WI-459. Let `Z_R(T;tau)` count, with multiplicity, zeta zeros in `(T,2T]` whose response reaches a fixed `0<tau<1` after optimizing safe-line offset and every packet width `0<r<=R`. For sufficiently small `R` and sufficiently large `T`, it proves

`Z_R(T;tau)/(N(2T)-N(T)) <= C_tau (R^2 + log T/T)`.

Packet regularity makes each peak persist over width comparable to `r`; the unit-interval zero count pays a `1/r` sampling cost. The finite-window mean and deterministic `r log T` bound then control the infinite width bank. Thus every `R(T)->0` gives a vanishing responsive fraction. At fixed small `R`, the limiting fraction is double-exponentially small in `1/R`; that stronger rate is not uniform over arbitrary shrinking diagonals. This is actual discrete sampling, without random-phase or spacing assumptions. It still supplies **no lower response bound at an off-line zero** and cannot eliminate finite or zero-density exceptions. Recurrent height peaks and the absence of an RH conclusion remain consistent with it.

[WI-461](weil_inertia/findings/WI-461-fixed-scale-euler-peaks-have-positive-density-at-zeta-zero-ordinates.md) supplies the complementary fixed-scale lower result. Uniform Hilbert-valued truncation transfers it to each fixed Euler response. For each fixed sufficiently small `R` and `0<tau<1/4`, the responsive fraction has positive double-exponential-scale lower and upper bounds; every fixed response also has its full height supremum along zeros beyond any finite cutoff. The limit is taken in height **before** shrinking `R`. Neither the lower density nor the sampled supremum identifies off-line zeros or controls every individual defect. This sampling input is routed to Visual Exploration through the [actual-zero character question](visual_exploration/clues/CLUE-zero-ordinate-growing-shell-character-tariff.md); the quantitative changing-shell transfer is proposed, not established.
