# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The common RH-facing gap remains an independently source-forced estimate in the precise norm used by the destination. Source reconstruction, auxiliary positivity and existence of a zero-sensitive test do not supply that estimate. No current Mathia finding proves RH.

The selected-scale recovery boundary is closed for both prime and Möbius sources through near-linear windows. [MC-529](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-529-single-near-linear-centered-variance-law-is-equivalent-to-rh.md) proves that, for any one fixed `B>1`, the complete centered Möbius variance ceiling `V_mu(X,H) <<_epsilon X^(1+epsilon)H` for every fixed `epsilon>0`, at `H=floor(X/(log X)^B)` on any unbounded scale set is **equivalent to RH**. Centering and sparse scale selection do not weaken that precise premise. [PL-472](prime_lattice/findings/PL-472-gevrey-box-inversion-reaches-near-linear-prime-windows.md) gives the corresponding prime zero-exclusion implication with a logarithmic-loss ceiling. Neither supplies an independent arithmetic upper bound. [PL-473](prime_lattice/findings/PL-473-box-fourier-zeros-obstruct-logarithmic-window-test-inversion.md) separately blocks uniform fixed-target box inversion at certain larger windows; this is a method obstruction, not a variance counterexample.

Canonical findings outrank older Mind frontiers where they differ. No adversarial sidecar is currently open; this is not independent approval of the corpus. WI-002 is withdrawn, and [WI-456](weil_inertia/findings/WI-456-retracted-moment-transport-leaves-only-a-conditional-density-capstone.md) retains only a conditional moment-to-density theorem after retraction of the Yang–Yang arithmetic transport. Even a valid density-one theorem leaves individual exceptions possible. The [Atlas v1 metrics](graph/atlas/metrics.md) incorporate AF-582 selectively; their unchanged coverage model and older entrypoint narrative do not represent the full current source frontier. They are derived context, not evidence for the recommendations below.

## Cross-line structure

**Prime and reciprocal selected-scale recovery share a test mechanism, with different analytic inputs.** For `a_n=Lambda(n) 1_(n=1 mod 5)`, [PL-471](prime_lattice/findings/PL-471-sparse-zero-annihilators-force-prime-variance-on-every-large-block.md) isolates any zero `rho_0` with real part `beta>1/2` by a fixed compact mean-zero Mellin test. A power-saving off-critical zero count permits a genus-zero product and Gevrey multiplier that kill all unwanted zeros; the arithmetic logarithmic derivative leaves one noncancelling complex mode. No rightmost zero or finite exceptional family is needed.

For Möbius, [MC-528](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-528-multiplicity-aware-annihilation-forces-mobius-variance-on-every-block.md) repeats the unwanted factors with their full multiplicities. Titchmarsh's local product estimate lets these factors cancel before division is bounded: the residual product grows at most `T^C exp(C T^a)`, and the Gevrey multiplier decays as `exp(-c T^b)`, with `a<b<1`. This supplies the missing contour control, without zero separation or inverse-derivative bounds. A selected pole of order `m` retains a degree-`m-1` logarithmic polynomial even if its ordinary residue vanishes. Finite box inversion gives

`V_mu(X,floor X^theta) >= c floor(X^theta)^2 X^(2beta-1) (log X)^(2m-2)`

for every sufficiently large real `X`, at each fixed `0<theta<1`. Thus a ceiling `V_mu <<_epsilon X^(1+epsilon) H`, for every fixed `epsilon>0` uniformly on **any unbounded set**, excludes `beta>1-theta/2`. Both the [annihilator](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-multiplicity-aware-sparse-zero-annihilator.md) and [packet](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-reciprocal-zeta-selected-scale-pole-tail-certificate.md) clues are resolved. This does not prove an absolute reciprocal residue sum or a packet/residual inequality for every preassigned bank; MC-527's grouped contour remains a distinct valid representation.

[MC-529](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-529-single-near-linear-centered-variance-law-is-equivalent-to-rh.md) completes the [near-linear handoff](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-near-linear-mobius-variance-gevrey-inversion.md). It preserves Gevrey order through the multiplicity-aware annihilator and logarithmic composition, reconstructs the finite Bernoulli inverse with error `exp(-c (X/H)^(1/q))`, and retains the full degree-`m-1` polynomial. For each fixed `B>1`, choosing `1<q<min(B,1/a)` gives the same floor uniformly for all integer `1<=H<=X/(log X)^B`. At the upper window the floor contradicts an epsilon-loss ceiling on every unbounded scale set if any `beta>1/2` exists. MC-520 supplies the RH converse, including real blocks and empirical centering. This equivalence does not assert an RH converse for a stronger fixed-logarithmic-loss ceiling. Constants depend on the whole hypothetical zero set; the derivative-bank size grows, and no effective onset is supplied.

[PL-473](prime_lattice/findings/PL-473-box-fourier-zeros-obstruct-logarithmic-window-test-inversion.md) identifies a shared operator boundary. For the backward box `A_h`, every integrable inverse test satisfies `||A_h f-phi||_1 >= |phi_hat(2 pi k/h)|` at each nonzero integer `k`. For one fixed nonzero compact smooth target, a uniform `O(X^(-delta))` inverse error is impossible at `H=floor(X/(log X (loglog X)^r))`, `0<=r<=1`, on all sufficiently large real scales. Integer rounding does not evade the Fourier logarithmic-integral obstruction. This applies independently of the coefficient source, but does not exclude signed arithmetic error cancellation, moving targets, several kernels or sparse selected scales. The [logarithmic-window clue](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-logarithmic-window-inversion-after-box-fourier-obstruction.md) delivers this boundary to Möbius and asks whether a stronger compact derivative class reaches the still-undecided `r>1` range. That is a conditional recovery refinement, not an independent variance estimate.

The sparse matched controls remain relevant. [PL-470](prime_lattice/findings/PL-470-positive-source-strip-growth-and-the-infinite-pole-tail-gate.md) shows why its positive meromorphic control cannot replace the arithmetic source's growth estimates. Likewise [PL-465](prime_lattice/findings/PL-465-relative-frame-transfers-dirichlet-zero-bounds.md) and [PL-467's synthesis](prime_lattice/mind/intuition/MI-066-relative-frames-transfer-frontiers-but-admit-common-off-critical-poles.md) distinguish a relative covariance frame from the independent absolute ceiling needed for zero exclusion. The successful prime/reciprocal transfer removes a recovery obstacle, not that arithmetic obligation.

The Farey destination retains different hypotheses. [MC-520](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-520-rh-mellin-variance-closes-conditional-farey-window-target.md) already bounds its complete sliding Möbius moment by `X^(1+epsilon)H` under RH, excluding the balanced near-capacity branch through the retained Mellin difference. Removing RH removes both that input and the carrying-shell localization unless separately justified. A centered estimate also needs its mean contribution restored before entering the uncentered Farey moment. Neither selected-scale recovery theorem supplies an independent Möbius upper bound or Farey shell law.

**Finite stopping depends on the admitted calibration class.** [AF-580](arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-580-positive-noise-destroys-finite-stopping-under-unknown-polynomial-calibration.md) strengthens the finite-cap obstruction: with unknown unrestricted polynomial calibration and any fixed positive supremum-bounded error allowance, worst-case wrong-return probability is at least `(1-1/m) P_x(return)` on every strict-slack or continuous-error input. In particular, reliable almost-sure return fails even on a noiseless input when reliability is required over the noisy class. Polynomial approximation and bounded-error cutoffs match all labels on exhausting compact windows; competitors are fixed before the random seed. The positive full-ray radius `d(S)/4` survives, and noiseless ideal continuous validation remains a different experiment. This is a synthetic calibration theorem, not ordinary-prime inference.

[AF-581](arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-581-known-polynomial-degree-restores-sharp-full-ray-adaptive-recovery.md) supplies a concrete repair when degree `r` is known: `r+1` finite anchors, a locator and a pair-specific follow-up attain every strict allowance below the same full-ray radius for sufficiently remote finite prime-edit sets. Distant Lagrange anchors reproduce the polynomial exactly while their absolute weight sum tends to one. The required coordinate range and output magnitudes are not uniformly bounded; the equality is of supremal radii, not recovery at the endpoint. The sharp worst-family deficit with only `r+2` values remains. This changes an explicit nuisance hypothesis, not ordinary-prime inference.

[AF-582](arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-582-quadratic-calibration-changes-sharp-window-cost-from-logarithmic-to-algebraic.md) shows that coordinate range is independently costly. For the binary normalized profile `x exp(1-x)`, retaining fraction `1-delta` of the full-ray margin needs range asymptotic to `sqrt(log(1/delta)/delta)` under quadratic calibration, versus `1+log(1/delta)+o(1)` under affine calibration. Classical Chebyshev alternation certifies the entire-window optimum, so additional readings within the window cannot remove the loss. The proved prime-edit transfer is sufficient when profile error is below the intended quotient loss; unquantified convergence supplies no unrestricted joint range/prime/precision rate. These are generalized-prime controls, not ordinary-prime provenance.

The [AF–Xi comparison](mind/intuition/MI-039-calibration-gluing-transfers-randomized-stopping-bounds-only-in-the-admitted-function-class.md) keeps noise and nuisance classes separate. Xi's one total squared-error budget permits chord-average error to fall as `sigma sqrt(2/n)/h`; a fixed supremum-bounded error allows `2 epsilon/h`, without that averaging gain. [XF-203](xi_flow/findings/XF-203-unknown-jacobi-calibration-slack-admits-finite-certified-stopping.md) thus retains pointwise finite certification under its own hypotheses, while [XF-207](xi_flow/findings/XF-207-complex-value-queries-retain-the-analytic-calibration-stopping-obstruction.md) retains capped error `1/2` for the full holomorphic calibration class even with complex queries and an admitted compact size bound. Those differences prevent transferring AF-580 merely by renaming the source. Existing calibration clues carry the earlier transfers; no new equivalence is asserted.

**Closure, bounded realization and complete normalized response are different gates.** [NB-356](nyman_beurling/findings/NB-356-finite-orbit-target-assembly-is-equivalent-to-a-reciprocal-phase-series.md) supplies the reverse implication missing from NB-355's cost obstruction. For a fixed finite seed set `J` of at least two integers greater than one and `q in J`, the actual projected target belongs to the algebraic sum of the closed real-dilation orbit spaces exactly when

`sum_(ell>=1) [ell^2 sum_(j in J minus {q}) ||ell log j/log q||_(R/Z)^2]^(-1) < infinity`.

An onto Hardy lattice sampler constructs finite-norm components when this series converges. Every fixed family whose logarithms have rational rank two still has divergent total component energy, at least logarithmic in inverse approximation error, despite full closed-span recovery. Almost every pair of independent real seed exponents instead gives convergence; this is not a theorem for a specified integer triple such as `{2,3,5}`. Finite-energy orbit vectors may need infinitely many dilations, and under RH only does the projected target equal the target itself. No finite-section rate or new RH criterion follows.

[PC-455](prime_circle/findings/PC-455-one-resolvent-point-forces-singleton-cotangent-tower-boundedness.md) closes the unbounded useful-resolvent escape for the unchanged complete singleton cotangent action on any Borel probability. One resolvent point forces Haar equivalence with an orbit-A2 density, hence the unique bounded realization and spectrum `[-1,1]`. Outside that class, every closed extension, if one exists, has spectrum all of `C`. [PC-454](prime_circle/findings/PC-454-self-adjoint-similarity-of-cotangent-towers-forces-haar-norm-equivalence.md)'s stronger upper-and-lower Haar-density condition remains necessary for bounded self-adjoint similarity. PC-455 does not classify closability outside the bounded class, or multiple/growing bases, changed kernels or domains omitting cylinders. The same theorem holds for nonarithmetic singleton angles.

[PF-354](prime_flute/findings/PF-354-prime-cuff-lengths-are-rationally-independent-and-have-no-common-nonzero-frequency.md) still excludes PF-353's exact common-nonzero-frequency transplant to prime cuffs. [PF-355](prime_flute/findings/PF-355-one-complete-constant-fibre-retains-corridor-correlation-on-unequal-lattices.md) shows that this does not rescue the whole model angle: every module has a constant mode. For its positive Cauchy network, eliminating all transitions and then the complete constant fibre gives `B-C^T A^(-1)C=F B F`, where `B` is already shorted. Correlation with one actual high lattice mode is `2 sqrt(cosh(pi xi))/(1+cosh(pi xi))`, irrespective of unequal lattices. Growing bounded-frequency high-mode counts per module force the model angle norm toward one. The maximizing constant vector can spread across the whole section, so neither localized witnesses nor singular-value multiplicity nor infinite-operator noncompactness follows. The [existing transition clue](prime_flute/clues/CLUE-positive-width-transition-schur-complement.md) already carries this result and the unresolved actual-pant comparison in the same shorted norm.

[WP-449](weil_positivity/findings/WP-449-nonperiodic-maharam-positive-forms-from-coprime-counting.md) constructs nonperiodic closed positive Maharam forms with bounded vacuum profile but unbounded full excitation cost, using classical floor-GCD positivity. [WP-450](weil_positivity/findings/WP-450-bounded-maharam-diagonal-covariance-forces-a-scalar.md) proves that this unboundedness cannot be removed within the nonscalar excitation-diagonal covariant category: every bounded real operator there is `cI`, with no periodicity or positivity assumption. A nonscalar bounded spectral cap of WP-449 must therefore lose covariance. Off-diagonal or nonlocal operators remain outside this classification. The [completion clue](weil_positivity/clues/CLUE-bost-connes-affiliation-conditioning-boundary.md) continues to own source selection, domain and signed Weil/Gamma/polar comparison. These operator restrictions concern different objects and do not justify a merger.

**Uniformity must match the claimed consequence.** [ANF-199](analytic_frontier/findings/ANF-199-stationary-source-zero-contacts-characterize-linear-real-notch-stability.md) gives a precise local-to-global condition within the real configuration class. For a fixed real even dominated positive-Fourier profile with inverse-square Fourier tail and positive maximum on source zeros, linear exposure stability is equivalent to vanishing derivative at **every** maximizing source zero. This is also equivalent to an initial affine segment of the real certificate. Nonstationary active contacts force a square-root loss; nonscalar stationary profiles can strictly improve the real objective. The proof uses source-paid matching and paired tails, not a cardinality-losing sum over all pairs. Its constants depend on the profile; complex configurations and source-locked high-pass control remain open.

[FD-341](farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-341-linear-band-relative-transfer-is-equivalent-to-diverging-critical-energy.md) now closes its previously stated critical-energy question using existing source evidence. FD-033's superlinear physical Jordan-tail energy and FD-003's exact normalization imply `Q E_Q -> infinity` along every integer Farey order. FD-341's equivalence then gives vanishing relative rank/position coefficient error, and consequently normalized band-energy error, at each fixed linear cutoff. The absolute coefficient-error floor remains compatible with this conclusion. No quantitative rate, growing cutoff multiplier, limiting band share or new Franel upper bound is proved; necessity for energy-share transfer alone is still not asserted.

[XF-032](xi_flow/findings/XF-032-geometric-gap-ramps-are-null-modes-of-triple-bulk.md) likewise distinguishes local representation from the complete target. Its periodic heat-flow control has an adverse regrouped pointwise conductance, yet every fixed nonnegative tapered triple sum has nonnegative derivative at the tested instant. Taper-difference terms cannot be dropped after summation by parts. This control neither refutes aggregate positivity nor proves it for other configurations or later times.

**Peak density, waiting laws and zero-ordinate sampling remain separate.** [WI-460](weil_inertia/findings/WI-460-adaptive-euler-peaks-remain-rare-at-zeta-zero-ordinates.md) resolves a sampling gap left by WI-457--WI-459. Let `Z_R(T;tau)` count, with multiplicity, zeta zeros in `(T,2T]` whose response reaches a fixed `0<tau<1` after optimizing safe-line offset and every packet width `0<r<=R`. For sufficiently small `R` and sufficiently large `T`, it proves

`Z_R(T;tau)/(N(2T)-N(T)) <= C_tau (R^2 + log T/T)`.

Packet regularity makes each peak persist over width comparable to `r`; the unit-interval zero count pays a `1/r` sampling cost. The finite-window mean and deterministic `r log T` bound then control the infinite width bank. Thus every `R(T)->0` gives a vanishing responsive fraction. At fixed small `R`, the limiting fraction is double-exponentially small in `1/R`; that stronger rate is not uniform over arbitrary shrinking diagonals. This is actual discrete sampling, without random-phase or spacing assumptions. It still supplies **no lower response bound at an off-line zero** and cannot eliminate finite or zero-density exceptions. Recurrent height peaks and the absence of an RH conclusion remain consistent with it.

The [prime-reset waiting-law portrait](visual_exploration/visualizations/prime-reset-clock-waiting-law.md) is a finite-model control with the complete one-time response distribution held fixed. For a single Haar phase on a reset-clock section, its exact no-hit probability is the circle-gap functional `sum_i(g_i-m)_+`; changing temporal dependence changes the waiting law even after time is scaled by event mass. This is supporting exploratory context, not a full Euler or zero-sampling theorem. The [weak-prime crossover](visual_exploration/visualizations/euler-hankel-weak-prime-crossover.md) separately warns that deleting a small positive coefficient can change the ultimate near-maximum exponent. The [return-length-bias portrait](visual_exploration/visualizations/prime-reset-return-length-bias.md) further distinguishes hit-start returns `tau` from zero-inclusive stationary forward waits `T`: `P(T>=N)=m E(tau-N)_+`, so `E(tau)=1/m` alone leaves the forward mean uncontrolled. It is a finite-model recurrence diagnostic, not an arithmetic sampling mechanism. The [matched renewal portrait](visual_exploration/visualizations/prime-reset-renewal-order.md) goes further: full hit-start and stationary forward-wait distributions can agree exactly while consecutive long gaps are forbidden by the rotation and occur with positive probability under renewal. Marginal calibration therefore does not justify independence of successive peaks. These are finite-model controls, not zero-sampling or full Euler-source theorems.

**Robin source restrictions do not bound its remote tail.** [RE-263](robin_extremal/findings/RE-263-two-prime-balanced-mertens-repairs-have-one-candidate-and-a-square-root-gap-barrier.md) completes the two-deletion case beyond RE-262's support floor. For subdyadic odd primes `r<s`, at most one nontrivial count-preserving integer replacement exists, decided by one divisibility test; `(s-r)^2<=r+s` excludes it at every insertion height. Source realization additionally requires the smaller inserted label to be composite and beyond the retained prefix. Larger inseparable packets and extra-count repairs are not excluded. These synthetic Euler atoms are not ordinary divisor-sum factors, and the classification gives no estimate for the actual CA-selected remote mixed integral left by RE-259.

## Highest-leverage unresolved questions

1. **Independent arithmetic estimates:** prove source-derived variance/correlation bounds with the exact centering, exceptional-start budget and scale quantifiers. MC-529's single near-linear premise is already RH-equivalent. PL-472's prime implication and relative frames still need an absolute bounded channel. Farey needs a weaker-hypothesis estimate on justified carrying shells.
2. **Physical assembly and transport:** decide NB-356's reciprocal-phase series for specified higher-rank integer seeds; compare PF's actual shorted pant form with its retained-constant model and control witness localization; select a WP signed comparison outside bounded diagonal rigidity. Robin's CA-selected remote tail remains a distinct source obligation.
3. **Individual defects and calibrated sampling:** obtain independent source coupling that charges an individual off-line block. WI-460 supplies only an upper responsive-zero population bound; visual waiting-law controls cannot supply that missing lower bound or a renewal law.
4. **Observation and method budgets:** quantify AF's source-profile error together with accessible range and precision; retain Xi's distinct noise geometry. The [logarithmic box-inversion question](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-logarithmic-window-inversion-after-box-fourier-obstruction.md) isolates an undecided regularity range beyond MC-529 without reopening the resolved Gevrey transfer or claiming arithmetic cancellation.

None. Continue the source-estimate questions and narrow the classified control families as indicated above. The evidence closes specified recovery, relative-transfer and operator subproblems while leaving concrete source-specific discriminators. It does not justify pausing, merging, splitting or opening a line. Reconsider only after a residual discriminator is exhausted or a distinct source-grounded mechanism survives the existing controls.
