## Cross-line structure

The clearest new split is **actual source law versus generic regularity**. Nyman now has a concrete positive example of the former: `NB-144` uses the Euler product to charge exactly the confluence mechanism built by its matched analytic controls. Prime Lattice has a concrete negative example of the latter: `PL-318` shows that a very natural small source norm still does not touch the moving observer. Future promotions should require the Nyman pattern -- a theorem whose hypothesis is genuinely owned by the arithmetic source and whose conclusion charges the exact matched escape -- rather than another ambient smoothness or population statistic.

A second common theme is **escape priced in the observer's native currency**. Robin's currency is scaled reciprocal radius, with visibility `exp(-O(R))`, and `RE-146` begins matching actual-zeta shell mass to that exponential bill. Arithmetic Fidelity's currency is target-relative recoverability rather than worst-case bandwidth. Visual's control preserves the actual order/fixed-count degrees of freedom instead of randomizing impossible label sequences. These moves improve the program even before they prove a final bridge because they remove artificial losses and false controls from the comparison.

A third theme is **do not double-count changes of representation as independent coercivity**. `MC-316` proves this exactly for coding duality and erasure/systematic formulations; `FD-145` exhibits a gauge that must be quotiented before relative constraints can count as absolute information; `WP-318` shows that scalar Julia positivity has universal sign across the ambient positive-measure class; and `PC-308` shows that ordinary Fourier stabilization at linear bandwidth is paid for by classical source-resolving arithmetic. The next theorem in each line must introduce a new source restriction, not rename an old one.

No new Master clue is justified. The actionable transfers are already owned by their destination lines or reflected in current local Mind state, and the present synthesis does not expose a distinct load-bearing foreign claim requiring new bounded cross-line access. No merge, split, or new-line proposal is warranted.
