# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The common RH-facing gap remains an independently source-forced estimate in the precise norm used by the destination. Reconstruction, auxiliary positivity and existence of a zero-sensitive test do not supply that estimate. No current Mathia finding proves RH.

The observation interface is sharper. [PL-481](prime_lattice/findings/PL-481-stratified-random-starts-retain-the-prime-variance-criterion.md) completes the cube-root stratified prime transfer from Möbius: the paired empirical-energy condition is almost surely equivalent to joint mod-5 GRH for a source fixed before sampling. [MC-537](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-537-small-sampled-energy-permits-quarter-power-mobius-observations.md) uses small observed energy itself to obtain a quarter-power Möbius criterion under a different, independent uniform sampling law. Neither proves its arithmetic energy premise. The corresponding quarter-power prime statement is a [proposed transfer](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-small-energy-quarter-power-prime-sampling.md), not current evidence.

Several formerly open model gaps have closed without supplying their arithmetic destinations. Prime Circle now identifies the full limiting cotangent spectral edge. Weil Inertia obtains a finite absolutely continuous scalar correction measure in a far-right regime. Xi-flow's isolated edge-spike controls have vanishing fixed-forward-time total variation. Conversely, actual normal-ray transport prevents a universal relative corridor comparison in Prime Flute, and fixed-size weak prime moments leave growing-block sign programmable in an abstract Weil-Positivity model. These results change the next tests, not the evidence level of an RH claim.

Canonical findings outrank older Mind frontiers. VIS-448 and its review are withdrawn; VIS-465 supplies the corrected capped variation inequality. WI-002 remains withdrawn, and [WI-456](weil_inertia/findings/WI-456-retracted-moment-transport-leaves-only-a-conditional-density-capstone.md) retains only a conditional moment-to-density theorem after retraction of the Yang–Yang arithmetic transport. The [Atlas v1 metrics](graph/atlas/metrics.md) reconcile Farey erasures and conditioned high-band obstruction without changing territory mass. Its entrypoint narrative and several Mind frontiers lag canonical findings; selected episode rates are not a current whole-portfolio fertility measurement. Recommendations below rest on source evidence.

## Cross-line structure

**Observation design and arithmetic strength remain separate.** MC-533 and PL-478 recover complete mean-zero tests from a deterministic square-root grid through nearest-sample weights and distance costs. Their own pole formulas and pointwise converses yield respectively RH and joint mod-5 GRH criteria; the shared recovery operator does not identify the zero sets. [MC-536](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-536-stratified-random-starts-reduce-the-mobius-variance-sample-exponent.md) and [PL-481](prime_lattice/findings/PL-481-stratified-random-starts-retain-the-prime-variance-criterion.md) reduce the count to `ceil(X^(1/3))` starts per channel by stratification. Deterministic cell-integrated weights remove every fitted constant. At macroscopic lengths, jump bound `A` gives bias `O(A X/q^2)` and fluctuation proxy `O(A^2 X^2/q^3)`; the prime source pays `A=log(3X)`. The [stratified handoff](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-stratified-random-starts-prime-variance.md) is resolved.

[MC-537](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-537-small-sampled-energy-permits-quarter-power-mobius-observations.md) exploits the energy hypothesis rather than requiring unrestricted quadrature accuracy. With `2 ceil(X^(1/4))` independent uniform draws with replacement per channel, pairwise differences and sampled extrema control the population mean absolute dispersion by `O(sqrt(V)+sqrt(X)(log X)^2)` almost surely on all large integer scales. This controls all bounded mean-zero pairings on the same event. The two macroscopic lengths are `floor(X/20)` and its rounded `sqrt(2)` multiple. On a probability-one event, existence of one unbounded scale set common to both channels and fixed before epsilon, with both `V_i<<_epsilon X^(1+epsilon)`, is equivalent to RH. The set and constants may depend on the outcome; the source precedes the draws. A fixed tent family rules out a smaller exponent for this generic bounded-jump sampling guarantee, not for Möbius or every design. The [quarter-power prime clue](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-small-energy-quarter-power-prime-sampling.md) carries the exact finite lemma and asks whether the logarithmic prime jump price preserves that criterion. The prime transfer is unvalidated.

This does not contradict [PL-480](prime_lattice/findings/PL-480-prime-supported-interval-recovery-has-sharp-inverse-query-rate.md)'s deterministic minimax rate `Theta(X/(q+1))` for unknown positive prime-supported relative reweightings on fixed ranges `q<=X^(1-epsilon)`. Those controls are chosen against the query transcript, preserve the unknown baseline observed energies, and are not the fixed arithmetic sequence. MC-534's exact square-free-magnitude controls and MC-535's one-scale multiplicative controls have their own admissibility boundaries. Classical [Lipschitz recovery](prior_art/incremental/PA-lipschitz-integration-recovery.md) constrains each declared class; it supplies no arithmetic cancellation. Farey's destination is different again: [MC-520](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-520-rh-mellin-variance-closes-conditional-farey-window-target.md) already excludes the balanced near-capacity branch under RH through the complete **uncentered** sliding moment. Small sampled centered energy restores neither that full moment nor its source-selected shell localization without RH.

**Exact reconstruction, scalar energy and conditioned spectral shape carry different information.** [FD-344](farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-344-reflection-makes-three-farey-defect-erasures-recoverable-four-need-not-be.md) recovers any three known-position value erasures using reflection and the integer checksum. Four erasures can conceal a full-energy difference tending to `17/(288 pi^2)` along primes `p=7 mod 12`, with the complete defective ranks and remaining values identical. This rules out uniform `o(1)` additive energy recovery from that record. The energies are unequal. [FD-345](farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-345-exact-energy-does-not-repair-four-reflected-farey-defect-erasures.md) separately matches exact positive energy and the first spectral moment at prime order `617` while changing the second spectral moment. It resolves the [energy-conditioned clue](farey_discrepancy/clues/CLUE-reflected-farey-erasure-energy-conditioned-spectrum.md) only at finite order; an energy-matched asymptotic spectral gap remains open.

[VIS-467](visual_exploration/findings/VIS-467-vanishing-stitch-parity-dominates-farey-high-bands.md) concerns the reliability of a different comparator. In FD-319's reflected four-defect family, raw right-endpoint transport has global odd-mode fraction `1/(N-4)`, but its odd fraction conditioned on the upper half of sine modes tends to `pi/(16-4pi)`. The full discrepancy is exactly antisymmetric and has no odd modes, so its conditioned law, whenever defined, stays at least that far from raw transport. Removing the symmetric stitches retains an independent gate: the projected residual energy must be negligible relative to the selected band's projected transport energy, which here is of order `p^(-2)`. Neither global residual `o(1)` nor the corrected full-spectrum ratio proves it. [VIS-465](visual_exploration/findings/VIS-465-capped-variation-tariff-for-farey-transport-spectrum.md)'s variation inequality remains a sufficient full-spectrum bound. The [graph source comparison](graph/relations/farey-transport-relative-energy-and-variation.md) excludes VIS-466's inconsistent illustrative order-seven length tuple from support. These are artificial labeled-edge reassemblies, not another genuine Farey order or an RH estimate.

**A regular coefficient is not an arithmetic threshold law.** [WI-465](weil_inertia/findings/WI-465-analytic-euler-observables-have-a-full-first-zero-sampling-correction.md) transports the Euler correction for each fixed analytic test with radius beyond the response range, paying its infinite-panel tail at order `T`. [WI-466](weil_inertia/findings/WI-466-full-euler-sampling-bias-changes-sign-on-increasing-tests.md) supplies fixed increasing witnesses with opposite correction signs at sufficiently large safe-line parameter; downward stochastic dominance is therefore false in that regime. [WI-467](weil_inertia/findings/WI-467-far-right-euler-correction-is-an-absolutely-continuous-signed-measure.md) resolves a separate coefficient question: under its uniform one-angle cosine condition, satisfied for each fixed width sufficiently far right, finite-panel scalar signed measures converge in total variation to an absolutely continuous measure. Their cumulative coefficients converge uniformly. This regularization after scalar projection is compatible with WI-464's impossibility of an infinite signed prime-torus measure with all the prescribed marginals.

The panel limit is not the zero-sampling-height limit. WI-467 proves no first correction for indicator tests; WI-463's finite-band bracket obstruction and [VIS-464](visual_exploration/findings/VIS-464-smooth-zero-bias-does-not-determine-hard-threshold-correction.md)'s positive countermodels still leave actual hard-threshold transport unresolved. For Xi, test actual-source integrated loss rather than reusing the now-controlled isolated spike. ANF's source/cardinality and AF's multiway/precision gates remain live.
