## Cross-line structure

### 1. Calibrated deterministic geometry should now be treated as a boundary condition, not an invitation to sharpen the same proxy

Farey is the cleanest case: `FD-102` supplies a carrier-independent logarithmic budget and `FD-103` shows actual ladders saturate its coefficient, so another deterministic floor or carrier improvement cannot create the missing source descent. Visual reaches the same point differently: `VIS-211` closes the entire one-cancellation weighted tail, so work must move to higher-cancellation geometry rather than improve a proxy whose contribution is already controlled. Robin's `RE-104`--`RE-105` similarly push the residual from local event classification into global switching/stretch. These lines now have explicit transition points where further work on the old local surrogate is low value.

### 2. Positive or componentwise normalization repeatedly loses exactly the exceptional information

Möbius now has a theorem-level barrier: atomwise witness conditioning consumes too much Fourier degree, while any one-sided positive Boolean majorant with constant blind/nonblind gap requires `Omega(sqrt(t))` degree. Prime Lattice supplies a complementary control: the positive principal fractional/logarithmic branch remains uniformly regular through zero order, so a useful singular response cannot be attributed to generic principal-state shape. Weil Positivity's previous positive scalar completions failed for related reasons; `WP-290` is interesting precisely because it acts as a signed source-native coboundary before the final target is read. The common lesson is not that positivity is useless, but that the exceptional source must remain distinguishable until after the decisive transform.

### 3. Multi-component problems are increasingly controlled by compatibility geometry rather than marginal estimates

Nyman's residual is a dimensionless three-zero curvature, not a pairwise gap. Farey needs energy to remain usable across a logarithmically long sequence of source-neutral steps, not another good individual predecessor. Robin needs a switching/progress law across locally understood endpoint states. Weil Inertia needs constraints tying the entire cut path to the Suzuki null/source equation rather than sharper bounds on individual cut covariances. `MI-014` remains the right global warning: componentwise coercivity only globalizes after a quantitative continuation, compatibility, or switching cost is proved.

### 4. The most promising live routes expose a destination observable that survives normalization and matched controls

Prime Flute is currently the strongest example. `PF-330` proves that final low normalization cannot simply wash away packet mass, and `PF-331` identifies a concrete uniformly gapped transport factor whose locality can be tested directly; this is why the line remains `continue`. Arithmetic Fidelity now has a quantitative support-height/time-height window rather than a vague conditioning claim, but still needs exact incidence in the shared prime-log flow. `WP-290` similarly separates a source-native category change from generic pole cancellation. These are useful because a positive result or a counterexample can be stated at the actual interface consumed by the RH-facing endpoint.
