The completed-Weil side has acquired a similarly target-specific first-crossing condition. `WI-253` bypasses the odd-sector positivity obstruction of `WI-252` by starting from the **first unrestricted global crossing**. For every smaller radius `0<r<a_*`, one and the same nonnegative spectral density `|vhat|^2` must satisfy an explicit sliding autocorrelation inequality

`(1/(2*pi)) integral Sigma_r(z) |vhat(z)|^2 dz >= r lambda_r > 0`,

where `Sigma_r` is a finite-prime-plus-archimedean cosine symbol whose formula changes at prime-power thresholds `r=log n/2`. This does not yet contradict a zero mode, but it turns the next source-side question into a joint-`r` compatibility problem rather than another generic positivity or localization problem. Together with `WI-251`, Weil Inertia now has two complementary first-crossing reductions: a signed radial flux for the first odd mode and a continuum of sliding-symbol constraints for the first global mode.

Several other new findings close whole families of incremental optimizations. `FD-065` shows that a growing Schur head improves tail occupation only by paying the same power in scalar leverage, leaving the `Theta=2/3` crossover unchanged. `MC-237` shows that the applicable smooth-modulus Möbius variance theorem gives only subpower relative precision where the first-shell target needs a fixed power. `VIS-181` shows that for the natural full-prime critical weights `p^-alpha`, `alpha>=1/2`, even the linear-support regime `y~H` still has matched variance: the bounded-gap slow-beat obstruction of `VIS-180` is an arbitrary-subset phenomenon, not a discriminator for the natural weighted source. `RE-066` shows that positive bounded fan charges are lacunary and force a negative successor debt, moving the remaining Robin problem to the global interruption/debt ledger.

The common obstruction is therefore sharper than “preserve arithmetic information.” Exact finite recovery, abundant signs, local witnesses, or a mathematically well-defined carrier can all exist while the decisive relation is lost at the next transformation. The live problems are increasingly **source-selected and target-conditioned**: stable infinite transport, signed amplitude in the exact selected family, canonical critical topology, global assembly, and incompatibility of one distinguished target with the matched controls.

## Cross-line structure

**Finite acquisition is separating from target transport.** `AF-287` shows that a known finite Dirichlet prefix can be observed with essentially optimal row count and sharp timing resolution, while `NB-064` shows that a canonical target can nevertheless evade capture only by tracking a moving source-generated near-kernel. `XF-185`--`XF-186` make the same distinction for a known nonlinear Xi packet. The next useful theorem is therefore rarely another injectivity statement; it is a uniform statement about the distinguished target under the actual infinite transport.

**First-crossing problems are becoming source-family constraints rather than localization problems.** Prime Lattice already rules out hiding a finite negative index at infinity and makes fixed-aperture positivity finitely certifiable. Weil Inertia now has both the signed radial budget of `WI-251` and the continuum sliding-symbol constraint of `WI-253`. These constructions are not yet identified strongly enough to transfer certificates between lines, but together they narrow the missing theorem to source control at the first crossing rather than improved local detection.

**Signed cancellation budgets remain family-specific.** `MC-237` shows that smooth-modulus average variance is quantitatively too weak for the exact first-shell near-balance target. `WI-253` also consumes a signed prime-power symbol, but against a completely different first-zero-mode spectral density. The common lesson is not that one cancellation theorem transfers to the other; it is that the sign must be controlled in the exact source-selected family before absolute values, thresholding, or averaging erase it.

**Matched controls must grow with the readout complexity.** Prime Circle has pushed bulk resonances into classical dual-lattice geometry, Visual now shows that even linear-support variance for the natural critical prime weights remains matched, and Analytic Frontier has an explicit sharp aperture at which its adversarial packet finally becomes visible. A useful residual must survive the matched representation geometry at the same complexity scale as the proposed source discriminator.

**Local witnesses do not solve global assembly.** `FD-065` closes a large Schur-head optimization family without moving the terminal exponent; `PF-302` kills scalar round-trip survival without yet controlling the full Schur return; `RE-066` makes positive bounded fan charges sparse and debt-producing without yet summing the global ledger; `WP-267` shows that an apparently legitimate closure can delete the critical arithmetic channel altogether. In each case the next theorem has to change the global transport/normalization/topology, not add another local witness of the same type.
