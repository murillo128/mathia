# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia has thirteen valid research lines. Ten are evidence-bearing and active: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain distinct pre-evidence scaffolds.

The strongest common bottleneck has sharpened from a **source-calibrated complete-control margin gate** into a **source-provenance and target-calibrated budget gate**. A candidate carrier must retain something that is forced by the arithmetic source rather than created by a universal completion, quotient, scalarization, or representation choice; the norm/category/scale budget needed to recognize that provenance must be declared before the witness is selected; and the surviving quantity must then obey the exact asymptotic strength required by the destination theorem. The last clause matters: `PL-146` shows that even under RH the Suzuki checkpoint reserves have infimum zero, so the portfolio must not confuse theorem-grade quantitative control with a uniform positive margin when the target itself only requires a vanishing-margin no-crossing law.

`arithmetic_fidelity` makes the provenance/budget distinction exact. `AF-101` identifies original-target accessibility with equivalence of the relevant pairing norm, while `AF-102` shows that the formally similar second-leg functoriality is instead bidual accessibility. Most importantly, `AF-103` proves that preadjoint provenance is invisible to strong-operator finite-dimensional inspection without a uniform recovery budget: every finite restriction of an arbitrary dual-side operator can be matched exactly by a genuine preadjoint. Thus finite agreement can be complete for a declared interpolation cost and still fail to identify where the operator came from. The missing discriminator is a source-natural global budget/category condition, not another finite probe.

`analytic_frontier` supplies a complementary realization obstruction. `ANF-021`--`ANF-023` test three increasingly flexible classical support-one carriers—symplectic/Pfaffian scale mixtures, scale-mixed lattice diffraction, and iid perturbed lattices—and all still fail the Montgomery--Taylor target budget. The failures are different but converge on the same lesson: adding convex mixing, Bragg structure, or diffuse jitter inside a classical carrier class does not evade the complete finite-frequency/source constraints. A serious scalar improvement now needs a genuinely different realizability class or a separation theorem, not another mixture inside the already-controlled families.

The strongest positive carrier change now comes from `weil_inertia`. `WI-137` identifies Lamzouri slack with an exact quantized operator distance, `WI-138` shows that the tensor inertia counts off-line conjugate pairs exactly, and `WI-139` shows that the negative eigenspace aligns with horizontal defect rather than merely recording a scalar count. This is materially stronger than the earlier near-nullity diagnostics: the non-scalar spectral geometry preserves signed horizontal provenance before scalar collapse. It does not prove RH or yet supply the needed global coercive estimate, but it is enough to move `weil_inertia` from `narrow` to `continue`.

`xi_flow` and `visual_exploration` also make positive, source-aware progress. `XF-018` localizes uncentered gap-square energy with only bounded cross-ratio leakage, `XF-019` reduces the remaining block tail to a super-mesoscopic buffer, and `XF-020` shows that the actual zero-counting error controls such buffers. The unresolved step is now to turn this source-specific exterior control into fixed backward-time amplitude/flux control. `VIS-020`, meanwhile, turns the proposed three-gap residual into a falsifiable MaxEnt/Markov test whose null preserves the lower-order gap information; a surviving residual would therefore be relational information not already explained by adjacent-pair statistics.

Several negative results show where provenance is erased. `PC-163` closes the full zero-energy one-hole Kron determinant even after the prime-shell background is included, and `PC-164` closes the canonical energy-dependent Feshbach extension as well: its determinant is a prime-blind real Bloch quotient and its fixed-base self-energy converges to cyclotomic logarithmic-derivative data along both prime and matched composite refinements. `WP-140` identifies the corresponding minimal Kron pseudodeterminant logarithm as a normalization anomaly, while `WP-141` shows that the natural Fisher/logdet positivity repair removes even that anomaly without creating an arithmetic selector. `PF-167` supplies the geometric analogue: positive primitive-length accumulation is inherited by the all-composite shift clone, so more local clone geometry cannot replace a source-native global orbit selector.

Möbius and Prime Lattice clarify the transfer side. `MC-050` and `MC-051` show that a globally close real squarefree comparator with sufficiently strong cancellation self-upgrades to the desired zeta/Mertens region, so that route is not a cheaper intermediate theorem. `MC-052` leaves a genuinely different complex-amplitude frontier, but only at the price of an `L2` phase budget whose conversion to power cancellation remains the missing theorem. `PL-146` reduces Suzuki positivity exactly to a two-state prime-power event process with one convex checkpoint per interval and simultaneously proves that a uniform positive checkpoint reserve cannot be the missing theorem. The portfolio-level target is therefore no longer “find a positive margin”; it is **preserve source provenance, calibrate the admissible budget before inspection, and prove exactly the scale-dependent non-escape/no-crossing/transfer law that the destination theorem consumes**.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Provenance is invisible after some universalizations unless a budget is fixed first.** `AF-103` proves this exactly for preadjoints under finite strong-operator inspection. `PC-163`--`PC-164` and `WP-140`--`WP-141` show line-specific versions: once one-hole or positivity data is reduced to a fixed-base Bloch/cyclotomic determinant or normalization law, prime/composite provenance disappears. A successful route should therefore couple source-specific structure before the classicalizing map and carry a declared quantitative budget through it.

2. **Complete controls still beat carrier enrichment inside the same realization class.** `ANF-021`--`ANF-023` add Pfaffian mixing, lattice diffraction mixing, and iid jitter without escaping the Montgomery--Taylor budget. `PF-167` similarly shows that a stronger local accumulation invariant survives the shift clone. Complexity only counts as progress when it changes the information class seen by the matched controls.

3. **The target budget need not be a uniform positive margin.** `PL-146` is the clean counterexample to over-strong portfolio language: under RH the checkpoint reserves have infimum zero, yet the theorem only asks that they never cross below zero. Xi Flow has the same qualitative shape—bulk control must dominate a scale-dependent exterior budget—while Arithmetic Fidelity needs uniformity for a different reason, namely identification/assembly. Quantitative demands must therefore be attached to the destination theorem, not promoted to one universal margin axiom.

4. **Operator-valued sign/eigenspace information can preserve provenance that scalar summaries erase.** `WI-137`--`WI-139` turn horizontal off-line defect into exact operator distance, inertia, and negative-eigenspace alignment. This is the strongest current evidence that a carrier change before scalarization can matter. The remaining test is whether the structure survives source-valid truncation and yields a global coercive estimate rather than only an exact finite-model identity.

5. **Relational depth and source-specific exterior control are the two clearest positive experimental frontiers.** `VIS-020` removes adjacent-pair information before asking whether three-gap structure remains; `XF-018`--`XF-020` isolate a mesoscopic core while bounding the exterior using actual zero-counting input. Both now have matched/source-calibrated falsification surfaces, which is why they remain `continue` rather than merely exploratory.
