## Cross-line structure

### 1. Exact destination constraints can force the source inverse back in

`WP-294/295` make this principle unusually sharp. Smoothing the Mangoldt source to the all-integer logarithmic carrier is easy, but exact recovery of the finite Weil quadratic form forces Möbius inversion even if coefficientwise equality is never assumed; adding finitely many positive channels does not help because prime-power support itself collapses them to one common direction. `FD-111` is the finite-local analogue: odd polynomial source channels are invertibly equivalent to the original source and even channels lose the false-RH power scale. In both cases, adding representation freedom without changing the destination currency does not create new information.

The accepted global clue `CLUE-cross-line-fidelity-realization-separation` already covers the distinction between source-faithful encoding, transport, destination realization, and matched controls. These new findings sharpen that clue rather than justify a new Master clue.

### 2. Arithmetic-image adaptation now has a quantitative complexity threshold

`MC-281` showed why ambient Boolean-cube impossibility was too coarse; `MC-282` then showed that bare Legendre provenance and absence of a blind relation still do not force useful leverage; `MC-283` finally locates where tight-shell arithmetic could begin to matter. Any successful Christoffel separator must couple lower-prime coordinates beyond every fixed polylogarithmic product conductor. This is closely analogous to Arithmetic Fidelity: `AF-333` says, conditionally, that the decisive resource is not coordinatewise transcendence but a **joint algebraic-dependence budget** inherited from the common prime-log source and unitary provenance.

The common lesson is not that high complexity is automatically sufficient. It is that the source-specific theorem must operate beyond the entire regime where classical equidistribution or separated-prime realization can reproduce the desired local image.

### 3. Terminal observables are replacing generic regularity norms

Prime Flute now has the cleanest pass/fail observable: one raw two-mode pant-energy contrast at seam-width scale can kill the translation route. Prime Lattice supplies the negative counterpart: a spectral gap and ordinary `L^2` forcing cannot control the BV currency consumed by the aperture derivative, because the logarithmic principal operator is intrinsically too weak at high frequency. Nyman likewise shows that merely increasing the number of confluent states does not help below logarithmic rank; the target projection itself remains small after all early cells are restored.

These lines should therefore resist returning to stronger ambient norm estimates. The next theorem has to act in the exact metric consumed at the endpoint: raw pant DtN contrast and completed angle for Prime Flute, source-structured derivative measure/BV coercivity for Prime Lattice, and simultaneous target access plus conditioning at or beyond logarithmic rank for Nyman.

### 4. Finite local structure is repeatedly exhausted before global source assembly

Farey has now closed finite divisibility, ordering, and finite polynomial local nonlinearities. Weil Inertia has closed every fixed finite family of prime-power screens. Robin has turned finite zero-edge information into almost-linear recovery corridors and a large favorable-sign saturation packet but still needs the CA selector to make that local burden globally inconsistent. Prime Circle remains matched-control reproducible below the near-square-root transition, and Visual has exhausted the nontrivial shell hierarchy of its current exact observable.

Across these lines, the plausible escape is increasingly **growing or global coupling**: a prime family whose aperture grows, cross-horizon/nonlocal source laws, switching or recurrence costs, a critical transition, or an enriched observable. Fixed finite local refinements are no longer good default bets.
