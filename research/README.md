## Cross-line structure

### Acquisition complexity is separating from stable global transport

Arithmetic Fidelity now gives a particularly clean positive finite-prefix benchmark: inverse-breadth node spacing and linear mixed degree suffice for stable coefficient recovery, but the proved design still spends quadratic sample count. Xi supplies another local positive case in which known packet complexity costs only linearly many measurements. Nyman supplies the complementary warning: even after the entire late quotient is represented by one operator, the target question becomes generalized-nullspace reachability, not representation fidelity. These lines therefore share an information-budget theme but not a common theorem. Exact or stable finite recovery does not by itself pay global support discovery, infinite breadth, or target-oriented transport.

### Localization has stopped being the main escape route in the completed-Weil pair

`PL-281` and `WI-251` attack different operators but remove complementary geometric evasions. Prime Lattice proves that compact-support negative index is exhausted monotonically by finite apertures, so a finite defect cannot remain invisible by support drift. Weil Inertia uses the forced first odd zero mode and layer-cake localization to remove arbitrary smooth-collar cost while preserving the signed prime source. The combined strategic restriction is legitimate: a useful completed-Weil route now has to prevent or contradict a finite first crossing using source-specific sign/coercivity, rather than appeal to hidden behavior at infinity or generic localization loss. No cross-line clue is created because an exact normalization-preserving map between the two localized operators is not established.

### Source-selected sign and source-selected normalization are separate requirements

`MC-233`, `WI-251`, and `WP-265` expose three distinct states of the problem. Möbius has now stripped away incidence masks and roughness but still lacks the signed progression cancellation itself. Weil Inertia already preserves the orientation of the actual von-Mangoldt source and needs quantitative incompatibility on one forced mode. Weil Positivity can produce a strong Hodge-like inertia statement, but two natural source regulators yield different finite parts and even different inertia classes. A successful positivity architecture therefore needs both a sign-producing mechanism and a canonical normalization fixed before the target is consulted.

### Local amplification/composition is increasingly exact, but global survival remains the bill

Farey, Prime Flute, and Robin now quantify this boundary rather than merely naming it. `FD-061` proves that a local CRT packet survives global normalization strongly enough to improve a polynomial-loss exponent, yet not enough for positive occupation. `PF-300` converts prime-seam accumulation to an exact logarithmic integral test, yet the scalar channel is still only a compression of the physical mixed response. `RE-062` completely classifies bounded packet cancellation, yet the actual fan may still organize itself through the exceptional architectures. Further local refinement is useful only if it changes the denominator, full return operator, or global abundance law consumed by the final implication.

### Matched controls now extend into rational limits and positive-power finite-order windows

Analytic Frontier, Prime Circle, and Visual Exploration keep closing representation-level escape hatches. `ANF-183` fixes the matched endpoint packet's critical physical aperture. `PC-269` shows that even rational limiting torus geometry remains bulk-classical unless an exact finite-level resonance persists. The Visual clue extracted from `VIS-177` extends finite-order moment agreement into polynomial support whenever the order stays below the support-dependent boundary. The useful frontier is therefore increasingly in growing complexity, exact source-selected resonance, discrete/hybrid sampling, or a source theorem that crosses an explicit quantitative aperture—not in another fixed-order summary of the same representation.
