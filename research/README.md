# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The common RH-facing gap remains an independently source-forced estimate in the precise destination norm that can exclude each off-critical zero. Source reconstruction, auxiliary positivity, available operator witnesses and artificial spectral separation do not provide that estimate. No Mathia finding proves RH.

[MC-522](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-522-centered-dyadic-variance-recovers-all-reciprocal-zeta-poles.md) completes the centered-dyadic Möbius transfer, including arbitrary multiple reciprocal-zeta poles. [MC-523](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-523-density-one-dyadic-window-control-permits-near-linear-prefixes.md) shows why density-one block coverage cannot replace its every-block premise by generic interpolation: a nonmultiplicative square-free-supported control passes complete window bounds on the retained blocks yet has near-linear prefix excursions. Whether arithmetic continuation and sparse poles repair this gap is a distinct question. The balanced Farey variance target remains **classically resolved under RH** by [MC-520](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-520-rh-mellin-variance-closes-conditional-farey-window-target.md); independent continuation needs weaker hypotheses and justified carrying windows. [RE-259](robin_extremal/findings/RE-259-zero-density-removes-critical-robin-moment-boundary.md) removes Robin's first annulus but leaves the CA-selected remote mixed tail open.

The current canonical findings outrank older Mind frontiers where they differ. No adversarial sidecar is currently open; this is not independent approval of the corpus. WI-002 has been withdrawn. [WI-456](weil_inertia/findings/WI-456-retracted-moment-transport-leaves-only-a-conditional-density-capstone.md) replaces its obsolete live-candidate assessment with a precise conditional moment-to-density theorem. The Yang–Yang arithmetic transport is retracted; neither model certificates nor the surviving conditional theorem restore it. Even a valid density-one theorem leaves individual exceptional zeros possible. The [Atlas v1 metrics](graph/atlas/metrics.md) include the AF-577/XF-204 calibration boundaries in their selective telemetry. Their unchanged coverage model and older entrypoint narrative do not cover the whole current source frontier; portfolio judgments below rest on canonical evidence.

## Cross-line structure

**A proof-operation barrier need not survive a different classical input.** MC-520 bounds `J_mu(X,N)` by `X^(1+epsilon)N` under RH. FD-311's balanced premise forces `J_mu>=XN^(1/2+3c-o(1))`, where `c=1-log_2(3/2)`, on shells with `X<=N^(2+o(1))`. Any fixed `2epsilon<3c-1/2` gives a strict contradiction on the same complete moment. FD-312's separate-endpoint barrier survives because it discards the difference multiplier that MC-520 retains. The [resolved Möbius handoff](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-farey-selected-sliding-mertens-variance.md) and Farey MI-066 record this classical conditional outcome, not a new RH mechanism. Removing RH also removes its shell-localization guarantee unless separately recovered. RE-259 is another successful change of input, but uses a different prime statistic and supplies no Möbius transfer.

**Smooth recovery changes the analytic consequence, not the arithmetic premise.** MC-521 assumes the complete uncentered moment `J(X,X^theta)<<_epsilon X^(1+epsilon)H^(2-eta)` at every large real scale, for fixed `0<theta<1`, `0<eta<=1`. Finite correction of smooth weights excludes zeros in `Re s>alpha=1-theta eta/2`. Its classical logarithm/reciprocal-zeta estimate followed by Perron now also gives `M(x)<<_epsilon x^(alpha+epsilon)`; the generic bounded-coefficient smooth argument alone does not give that sharp cutoff.

PL-466 resolves the [dyadic seam clue](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-dyadic-variance-smooth-endpoint-recovery.md) for empirical identity-residue prime variance `V_j<<X_j H_j(log X_j)^A`, with `X_j=2^j`, `H_j=floor X_j^theta` and fixed finite `A>=0`. Seam-vanishing finite Fourier weights preserve the periodic unknown-mean factor. Sublinear arithmetic zero density excludes its pole progressions and then the polynomial residue sequences hidden by the filter, reaching `Re s>1-theta/2`. The positive dyadic density control still passes the variance bound and fails the pole-sparsity step. This is an arithmetic recovery theorem, not generic interpolation from sparse scales. The [Möbius handoff](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-dyadic-centered-mobius-pole-recovery.md) is resolved by MC-522: under `V_j<<_epsilon X_j^(1+epsilon)H_j`, the finite recurrence acts on whole Laurent principal parts, so it detects multiple poles even when their residues vanish. It gives the same zero-free boundary and, using the classical reciprocal-zeta growth step, `M(x)<<_epsilon x^(1-theta/2+epsilon)`. Every sufficiently large dyadic block is required. Arbitrary selected subsequences and independent bounds for either source variance remain open. A centered bound on a Farey carrying shell would also need its mean contribution restored before the uncentered FD-311 destination can use it.

[MC-523](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-523-density-one-dyadic-window-control-permits-near-linear-prefixes.md) rules out replacing that coverage by mesh density alone. Its nonmultiplicative control has exact square-free support, all qualitative fixed-shift Chowla correlations and `J_a(X,H)<=16XH log X` for every integer `1<=H<=X` on retained dyadic blocks, with no exceptional starts. Only `O(log J)` indices up to `J` are omitted, with successive retained scales at most a factor eight apart, yet some prefixes have size `>>T/log T`. Arithmetic continuation and sparse poles are not supplied for this control. The [density-one recovery clue](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-density-one-dyadic-variance-arithmetic-recovery.md) asks whether those additional Prime-Lattice inputs control the omitted-block Mellin remainder. This is a proposed conditional recovery test, not an actual variance estimate or a theorem about Farey-selected windows.

**A relative frame can transmit absolute information without supplying it.** Under PL-465's all-large-real-scale hypothesis `S_X>=c_theta Diag(S_X)`, a unit-modulus character probe has energy at least `c_theta tr(S_X)`. An independent upper bound for any one channel therefore controls identity-residue variance and, through its noncancelling logarithmic-derivative poles, all four zero sets. Writing `Theta_chi` for the supremum of zero real parts gives `Theta_max-Theta_min<=1-theta`. A frame family at fixed exponents approaching one equalizes these suprema; they may still exceed `1/2`. Separately, zeta RH plus the frame at a fixed exponent excludes all mod-5 zeros in `Re s>1-theta/2`. No absolute variance ceiling follows from the frame alone, no GRH-to-frame converse is proved, and the near-one family is not supplied by the existing short-window regime. PL-467 makes that distinction concrete: one bounded positive integer-supported control has the `1/4` relative frame at all sufficiently large real scales for each fixed `1-beta<theta<1`, while every character has a finite symmetric pole set with common right frontier `beta>1/2`. Its centered variance is `asymp H^2 X^(2beta-1)`, exceeding the absolute ceiling when `beta>1-theta/2`. It lacks prime support, an Euler product and a completed functional equation. Pole sparsity repairs lost information after an absolute estimate; it does not turn a relative floor into that estimate.

**Calibration knowledge, phase and noise norm determine the sampling problem.** [XF-202](xi_flow/findings/XF-202-rotating-jacobi-certificates-locate-the-calibration-crossover.md) extends the [resolved known-slack handoff](xi_flow/clues/CLUE-vanishing-calibration-slack-jacobi-row-cost.md): a rotating-tangent certificate overcomes the earlier one-chord restriction, while oscillatory clipping gives the matching adaptive adversary. With fixed positive total noise, the sharp small-slack cost is `1+sigma^2(1+Q epsilon)^2/(Q epsilon^4)`. Its mesh overhead is not known optimal in every simultaneous zero-noise limit.

[AF-575](arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-575-unknown-calibration-slack-requires-unbounded-sampling-depth.md) changes another quantifier: a strict but unknown derivative-budget slack permits input-dependent finite noiseless termination while defeating every uniform finite query cap. The full-curve uniform positive supremum-noise radius is zero. The [unknown-slack Jacobi handoff](xi_flow/clues/CLUE-unknown-slack-jacobi-stopping-certificate.md) is resolved by XF-203. Endpoint chord contrasts and increasingly populated distinct-row clusters certify finite stopping for each strict-slack input under one known total squared-noise budget. At fixed source and positive noise, the vanishing-slack quartic cost order survives; arbitrary coordinate precision and certified constants remain necessary. AF's uniform supremum-noise obstruction does not transfer to that norm.

[XF-204](xi_flow/findings/XF-204-randomized-jacobi-detection-has-a-sharp-error-stopping-tail.md) resolves the [randomized Jacobi clue](xi_flow/clues/CLUE-randomized-jacobi-unknown-slack-stopping-tail.md). Its strict complex-velocity join preserves the queryable endpoint and the tail, producing two fixed opposite-label inputs before the seed. AF-576's classical coupling/truncation method then gives capped minimax error `1/2` and sharp stopping tail `1-2alpha`, also with known total squared noise; per-input finite stopping survives. This is a validated destination-specific transfer, not a seed-dependent interpolation argument.

[AF-577](arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-577-unknown-polynomial-calibration-separates-continuous-randomness-from-finite-certification.md) prevents extending that sampling law by vocabulary alone. For a finite known analytic source family with nonpolynomial pairwise gaps and unknown finite-degree polynomial nuisance, every finite deterministic transcript aliases another label. Yet one continuously random exact validation coordinate, independent of all fitted interpolants, identifies each fixed input almost surely within its degree plus two queries. Finite-bit zero-error termination remains impossible and the uniform supremum-noise margin is zero. Polynomial nuisances exclude the smooth interval-gluing witnesses; this supplies neither an analytic-class minimax classification nor a finite-precision Jacobi algorithm. Both testing and interpolation mechanisms have registered classical prior art. Neither line supplies physical Xi access or a stable ordinary-prime decoder.

**A spectral representation can be informative without creating a new arithmetic input.** [FD-337](farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-337-sublinear-rank-sine-bands-transfer-to-the-mertens-spectrum.md) identifies genuine Farey rank-band energy at `q=o(Q)` with the normalized truncated spectrum of `B_Q(j)=sum_(d|j,d<=Q) d M(floor(Q/d))`, with additive error `O(q/Q+(q/Q)^2+1/Q)`. For the calibrated FD-335/336 nulls and diverging cutoffs, the null-normalized statistic has the same limiting arithmetic ratio; no rate for the null normalization or relative error at a vanishing share is supplied. The [owning Farey clue](farey_discrepancy/clues/CLUE-farey-gap-order-bridge-suppression.md) already carries this narrowed outcome. A bound on the ratio could still matter, but changing from fraction position to rank does not provide it. [FD-338](farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-338-linear-rank-band-transfer-fails-for-critical-energy-rational-grids.md) shows that reflection, comparable positive gaps, displacement `O(1/Q)` and even critical energy `Theta(1/Q)` do not extend this transfer generically to `q=Theta(Q)`: its rational grid has a positive limiting share discrepancy at `q=floor(Q/16)`. The grid lacks genuine Farey denominator constraints and the initial empty interval, so arithmetic-specific linear-band transfer remains open.

The [shifted-notch visualization](visual_exploration/visualizations/notch-shift-normalization.md) gives a separate measurement control: averaging normalized origin ratios tends to a universal value and loses the denominator–phase covariance that contains the signal. Averaging the energies before division preserves it for each fixed configuration. This classical estimator distinction supplies neither a uniform sampling rate nor an improved ANF-196 stability envelope.

**Source recovery, target occupation and quantitative stability are different claims.** NB-350's unrestricted late dilations recover the early-shell kernel, but [NB-351](nyman_beurling/findings/NB-351-geometric-source-seeds-retain-a-positive-target-distance-under-all-dilations.md) rules out geometric source compression: for each fixed integer `q>=2`, seeds `g_(q^k)` collapse to `g_q`, and even all real dilations leave squared target distance at least `1-q(log q)^2/(q-1)^2>0`. This is the exact cost of a common-Mellin-zero relaxation, not an exact distance for the original source space. [NB-352](nyman_beurling/findings/NB-352-finite-nyman-patches-cannot-remove-the-geometric-target-obstruction.md) also excludes every fixed finite patch of Nyman columns: simultaneous returns on the geometric Mellin lattice and slow zeta growth give a finite normed dual certificate. Its constant depends on the patch; a growing patch or all dilations of a multiplicatively independent seed is not covered. Finite-rate moving-packet occupation remains separate. WI-454's coherent isometry likewise recovers precisely the old Hardy energy; it supplies no independent upper bound.

[RE-260](robin_extremal/findings/RE-260-infinite-unit-prime-shifts-preserve-exact-mertens-matching-but-remain-robin-stable.md) separates source injectivity from destination stability: infinite unit shifts beyond a frozen prime prefix can preserve the Mertens constant exactly, yet every such admitted source has only `O(p^(-1/2))` normalized Robin discrepancy uniformly over all future cutoffs. This does not control the actual CA-selected mixed tail or restore uniqueness of infinite sources. [RE-261](robin_extremal/findings/RE-261-exact-mertens-matching-has-a-sharp-counting-discrepancy-robin-threshold.md) identifies the larger real-label class's exact scale: counting discrepancy `|Pi_Q-pi|<=K`, exact Mertens matching and `K=o(p/log p)` give worst-case normalized transient size `(1/2+o(1))K log(p)/sqrt(p)`. Finite real-label transports attain it. Distinct integer labels attain the same sharp constant through a finite packet plus infinitely many unit prime shifts that exactly pay its rounding debt; finite integer-label attainment is not proved. Thus exact normalization and a frozen prefix `p+o(p)` do not prevent a transient excursion at `K asymp sqrt(p)/log p`. These composite labels remain synthetic Euler atoms, not ordinary divisor-sum factors. This is a synthetic minimax statement, not a lower bound on the actual CA-selected tail.

**Domain repair must retain the correct representation and relaxation.** [PC-448](prime_circle/findings/PC-448-haar-admixture-does-not-close-rigid-cotangent-towers.md) excludes closability when a nonzero character-rigid singular component survives Haar admixture. [PC-449](prime_circle/findings/PC-449-equivalent-power-weights-give-cotangent-towers-empty-resolvent.md) shows a different failure after closability is restored: explicit equivalent power weights, bounded below but unbounded above, force every closed unchanged-action extension to have spectrum equal to the whole complex plane, even arbitrarily close to Haar in total variation. The canonical closure retains a dense span of real-eigenvalue eigenvectors. Two-sided bounded norm equivalence and half-density compensation remain different hypotheses; the control does not classify every unbounded weight.

[WP-442](weil_positivity/findings/WP-442-critical-valuation-product-supports-a-closed-log-gcd-energy.md) instead constructs a closed positive critical energy in a different occupation sector, and [WP-443](weil_positivity/findings/WP-443-critical-valuation-energy-has-an-explicit-hierarchical-spectrum.md) supplies simple spectrum, compact resolvent and heat trace finite exactly at `t>1`. Those properties do not identify the source divisibility observables or global Weil form. [WP-444](weil_positivity/findings/WP-444-critical-valuation-energy-drift-has-no-measurable-diagonal-counterterm.md) tests the missing comparison: normalized radial composition satisfies `E[V_n g]=E[g]+log(n)||g||^2` on the full form domain, whereas the Weil quadratic form is translation invariant. There is no finite measurable valuation-diagonal potential with increments `log(n)` to cancel this drift. The no-primitive mechanism is classical type-III theory, not a new general theorem. A nonlocal correction, different action, restricted comparison space or full GNS completion is not excluded. This is distinct from PC's weighted resolvent obstruction; a shared domain concern does not make the lines redundant.

[PF-349](prime_flute/findings/PF-349-local-full-parity-transition-shorting-retains-the-narrow-band-angle.md) now includes both face parities and all local sine/cosine transition modes. Relative form comparison with PF-348 preserves normalized correlation at least `1/4` for sufficiently narrow fixed bands. The [transition clue](prime_flute/clues/CLUE-positive-width-transition-schur-complement.md) retains neighboring-module transmission as the unresolved freedom: other cuffs are still held at zero, and normalized correlation is not monotone under further Schur relaxation. No global noncompactness follows from the local theorem.

**Fixed-test endpoint recovery does not cross a fixed support gap.** [WI-455](weil_inertia/findings/WI-455-wang-endpoint-flatness-controls-subpower-window-shortening.md) retains the complete prime norm when its scale exceeds the height-window length. For a fixed smooth test supported in `[-sigma,sigma]`, its derived error is `O_g(H+(log T)^3 J_g(log T))`, where `J_g(L)=int_0^sigma |g(alpha)|exp(L alpha)d alpha`. This admits every fixed logarithmic shortening `H>=T^sigma/(log T)^B`. Faster shortening depends on the endpoint profile; a fixed positive support-exponent gap and individual zero exclusion remain unresolved. As with MC-521/PL-466, a derivative order fixed after the parameters does not establish a uniform moving-order limit.

**Coherent peaks and averaged response answer different questions.** [WI-457](weil_inertia/findings/WI-457-normalized-euler-hankel-symbols-have-local-blindness-and-recurrent-peaks.md) tests the normalized safe-line Euler symbol itself. Its Hankel response tends to zero at shallow depth uniformly on each nonzero compact height set, even after choosing the safe-line parameter. At depth `r=q epsilon`, the pole-centered response tends to `q/(1+q)^2`, and almost periodicity reproduces its maximum beyond every finite height cutoff. No recurrence-time bound or intersection with zero ordinates follows. The [Euler-Hankel portrait](visual_exploration/visualizations/euler-hankel-mean-peak-cutoff.md) adds a truncation control: the long-height mean is asymptotic to `2q C epsilon^3`, with summable diagonal constant `C`, while every fixed Euler cutoff loses the persistent maximum as `epsilon` vanishes. Its supporting derivation supplies no joint height/depth estimate or zero-sampling theorem. The live WI question already requires an independent source law at actual zero ordinates; these are controls for that question, not a new route or an additional detector.

**Normalized density control cannot exclude each exceptional block.** WI-456 needs every fixed arithmetic matrix moment to converge to a Hamburger-determinate law with no nonpositive mass, plus the actual positive-index counting inequality and its tail error. Those premises would yield density one without uniformity in moment order. The control `diag(-1,1,...,1)` still retains one negative direction while meeting them. The retracted arithmetic transport and the individual-block detector are therefore separate missing inputs; the [higher-correlation clue](weil_inertia/clues/CLUE-higher-zero-correlations-horizontal-rigidity.md) pursues a signed-reservoir or horizontally coupled statistic, but its density-scale target does not itself provide an individual-block detector.

1. **Independent arithmetic estimates at the destination scale:** ANF's oscillatory high-pass, MC's signed physical conductor correlation and PL's direct relative lower frame with its channel-transfer consequences. Keep unrestricted joint zero-noise design open. Notch sampling needs energy-level normalization; Euler-Hankel portraits need coherent-tail control and cannot substitute long-height averages for observations at zero ordinates.
