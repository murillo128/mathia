# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The thirteen research lines remain **5 `continue` and 8 `narrow`**, with no evidence-based pause, merge, split, or new-line candidate. Their shared RH-facing deficit is a **source-enforced, correctly normalized, destination-visible estimate strong enough to exclude each individual off-critical zero**. A precise operator reconstruction, favorable averaged bound, generic positive form, or strong artificial control is not by itself that missing implication. No live Mathia finding proves RH.

The current source/observable boundary is sharper. FD-323 extends three positive-near-one Farey-index defects to an explicit two-cut, nonreflected control family with energy approaching the *family-specific* supremum `3/pi²`; VIS-447 transfers all normalized sine-mode shares from exact transport whenever `E_p/T_p -> 0`, with a further denominator condition for null-normalized shares. PC-435 classifies completeness under arbitrary prime thinning in the zero-free Hilbert-shell control, without proving the same converse for the genuine zeta-weighted seed. PL-447–PL-449 quantify the necessary critical and sufficient Fejér-smoothed finite horizons for the **uncentered** mod-5 Gram; their generic self-pair diagonalization is not the centered arithmetic covariance gate.

Other distinctions remain essential. NB-344/NB-345 exhibit signed finite-normalization residues at and near highly divisible dilations without physical Ford-target occupation. RE-249/RE-250 separate vanishing real gap tolerance from the one-unit discontinuity for *integer-labeled generalized* primes, not genuine primes. WP-437 shows that a noncompact prime-ray heat limit survives restriction yet retains an exact indefinite two-state Weil defect. XF-188 and XF-190 distinguish failure of a normalized multi-column nonlinear frame from stability of **raw** Jacobi measurements for one known endpoint at arbitrary packet width. AF-551's broad quotient-metric claim has an **open adversarial challenge** to its domain assumptions; its common-range Dirichlet-tail specialization is not directly refuted. AF-545 was withdrawn and replaced by AF-558; XF-186's earlier review is closed.

ANF-191 examines an *unverified external analytic claim* of a zero-free half-plane `Re(s)>0.874957...` and checks a conditional algebraic certificate; it neither independently verifies the external theorem nor closes the physical bow-carrier estimate. Riemann Atlas figures are **derived, source-lagged telemetry**, not mathematical evidence, portfolio probabilities, or a percentage of RH solved.

## Cross-line structure

**Local integrality and source-label fidelity do not control global transport.** FD-323's two-cut block swap keeps the entire directed edge inventory and makes precisely three index defects tend to the *positive* integer one, while the transport energy approaches `3/pi²` along a nested fixed-parameter/prime limit followed by growing coprime parameters. That is a sharp supremum only for the explicit unreflected family. FD-319 retains reflection but requires four defects. RE-249/RE-250 similarly show how matched generalized-prime sources retain a full terminal discrepancy under vanishing real gap perturbations or a single integer unit. Neither construction supplies a deformation of actual rational primes; **arithmetic source selection** must be proved independently.

**Spectral allocation needs a relative error and a separately calibrated null.** FD-320's exact discrepancy decomposition and VIS-447 imply total variation at most `min(1,sqrt(E_p/T_p))` between the normalized full sine spectra of an artificial Farey permutation and its displacement vector. This holds for every frequency band at once when `E_p=o(T_p)`, including the odd modes of FD-323; null-normalized allocation also divides by its own band fraction and may be unstable if that fraction vanishes. The accepted [[research/visual_exploration/clues/CLUE-farey-six-stitch-fixed-mode-null]] and the focused [[research/visual_exploration/clues/CLUE-farey-near-maximal-odd-mode-null]] require independent reflected/unreflected calibration. Fixed-mode saturation in FD-319/FD-322 and the fixed-inverse FD-323 family is an **energy/shape artifact**, not an established low-band-depletion false positive or a genuine-Farey theorem.

**Higher-order or changing-window information can survive second-order equality without arithmetic specificity.** MC-515's rectangular dependence yields a non-Gaussian limit despite exactly matching binomial mean, variance and pairwise statistics. VIS-441–VIS-444 show that matching full triple correlations can still leave different hitting laws, while the complete occupation-moment hierarchy recovers the Haar avoidance law. VIS-445/446 refine fixed-arc occupation near a moving gap boundary, but growing Wang shells require new uniformity. These are distinct models, not a proved Möbius–Wang identification; the outstanding test is an arithmetic, source-selected higher-order or hitting residual.

**Operator category and normalization are part of the theorem.** PC-435 gives a complete/incomplete dichotomy for zero-free thinned Hilbert shells, but no converse for genuine zeta weights and no stable frame. PL-449 can make the *uncentered* raw Gram nearly diagonal by Fejér averaging even in matched generic sources, whereas the oriented **centered** covariance gate remains open. PF-345's raw Schur trace does not control the normalized angle. WP-437 makes the analogous point more sharply: even a noncompact prime-ray limit does not repair a fixed negative Weil block. XF-188/190 show that a normalized-coordinate obstruction and stable raw-noise endpoint recovery can coexist. Improvements must be carried through the actual destination norm, domain, and source calibration.

**Population statements and unconditional model geometry do not exclude individual zeros.** ANF-191's external half-plane statement is explicitly conditional on an unverified proof and its untwisted consequence is too coarse for the distinguished carrier. NB-345's signed operator residue still decays and leaves the Ford target unmeasured. WI-445's real-frequency triple restriction cannot control off-critical conjugate blocks. The missing step in each is an independently justified, correctly scaled implication from its source observable to **every** hypothetical off-line zero.

## Highest-leverage unresolved questions

1. **Source-dependent signed second-order estimates.** Establish finite-resolution centered mod-5 covariance and its Reynolds-anisotropy margin, the source-selected sliding-Möbius variance saving, or the actual first-exit conductor's signed cross-state/rectangle cancellation. Fejér-smoothed self-pairs, binomial matching and infinite-time diagonalization do not substitute for the arithmetic source.
2. **Physical target and individual-zero detection.** Bound Analytic Frontier's distinguished prime/rough carrier at its critical high-pass, prove first-free Ford occupation beyond NB-345, or obtain Weil-scale defect coercivity uniformly down to arbitrarily shallow off-line zeros, with genuine support crossing.
3. **Normalized completion and physical observability.** Resolve PF-345's completed shorted canonical angle, the global finite–archimedean Weil sign/domains left by WP-437, and Xi Flow's unknown-support source access beyond XF-190's single known endpoint. Repair the AF-551 general quotient-metric domain claim before treating it as a global inverse.
4. **Source-natural spectral and hitting selection.** Compare genuinely ordered Farey data with **separately calibrated** reflected FD-319, unreflected FD-322, and FD-323's near-maximal fixed-inverse family; test odd/even full spectra, `E_p/T_p`, and each null's own expected band fraction without exchanging fixed-parameter and growing-prime limits. For Wang shells, supply quantitative growing-shell moment-order or hitting-tail bounds not implied by VIS-446.
