# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia has thirteen research lines in the current portfolio. Ten are evidence-bearing: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain distinct pre-evidence scaffolds.

The strongest common bottleneck is now a **source-forced residualization and amplification gate after complete matched-control quotient**. The earlier requirements on scale transfer, topology, non-escape, and coupled assembly remain necessary, but the latest evidence adds a sharper distinction: even a mathematically exact coupled object can still be only a universal gauge, a deterministic re-encoding, or a matched-control response. Progress begins only after the source fixes the admissible representation and quotient, the universal part is removed, and the surviving residual is shown to remain quantitatively visible in the category required by the destination theorem.

`MC-073`--`MC-075` make this explicit algebraically. Every normalized Dirichlet comparator participates in an exact factorization of `mu`, the factorization space is a torsor under normalized Dirichlet units, and even the symmetric fractional gauge has near-linear summatory size. The complete recovery identity is therefore not itself arithmetic cancellation; a viable mechanism must first obtain a source-natural gauge fixing and then control a proper non-universal residual. `AF-126`--`AF-128` provide a complementary information-theoretic formulation: finite recovery deficiency is exactly the maximal loss of bounded decision value after compression, and an optimal witness can be calibrated so that identity reconstruction is Bayes-optimal before compression. This gives a concrete test for whether a proposed quotient has actually discarded usable information.

The same gate now appears in geometric and analytic form. `ANF-043`--`ANF-045` remove vertical and horizontal escape for the remaining two-pair cardinality-five obstruction and reduce it to a compact three-dimensional shape problem with a strict Hilbert-space coherence gap. `PL-164`--`PL-166` show that extra prime dimensions do not amplify an RH-sensitive residual unless the source controls those dimensions: fixed marginals cap the response, and the direct unconditional FMZ extension hits a primorial frequency horizon while its available first-order bias is still subcritical. `PC-175`, `PF-168`, and `WP-152`--`WP-154` give three different matched-control warnings: smooth refinement symbols collapse to arithmetic dust, tail spectral filters become norm-resolvent composite-blind, and natural resultant cutoffs/local curvature reduce to universal or place-local laws.

`XF-040`--`XF-042` supply the most constructive recent counterpoint. Short finite-contrast periodic gap microstructure is nonlinearly damped on the microscopic Cauchy heat clock, and periodicity is no longer needed for the internal finite-window contraction. For an arbitrary finite block, the only adverse boundary input is the **centered exterior gap-mismatch field**; the rest of the exterior coupling is dissipative. Thus the Xi route now has an explicit source interface rather than a vague boundary-flux problem. `WI-159` supplies the opposite warning: generic positivity, analyticity, and Fejer-Riesz square structure still do not control the singular support-one remainder, so that missing estimate must be genuinely zeta-specific.

The program-level discriminator is therefore: **let the arithmetic source choose the representation, gauge, scale, topology, and admissible conditioning before compression; quotient every exact matched control; then prove that the remaining residual has a source-forced quantitative margin that survives the amplification and global assembly needed by the target theorem.** A residual that exists only after arbitrary gauge choice, uncontrolled coordinates, noncanonical regularization, or a destination-strength standalone estimate does not pass this gate.

## Cross-line structure

1. **A coupled identity is not enough; the source must fix the gauge before a residual can count as evidence.** `MC-073`--`MC-075` give the exact algebraic example, while `AF-126`--`AF-128` supply a quantitative way to ask what a compression has actually lost. `VIS-033` gives the deterministic analogue: several exact full-field representations can all be the same information channel. Future cross-line transfers should identify the quotient/gauge explicitly before attributing significance to a surviving formula.

2. **After escape directions are closed, the useful problem often becomes finite or conditioned rather than more global.** `ANF-043`--`ANF-045` compactify the full remaining two-pair search into a finite shape certification problem. `PL-164`--`PL-166` show the opposite failure mode: adding dimensions without source-controlled mixed-frequency information does not amplify the desired signal. The relevant complexity is therefore the source-conditioned residual dimension, not raw ambient dimension.

3. **Canonical-looking local or asymptotic processing repeatedly classicalizes unless provenance survives before the operation.** `PC-175` kills regular refinement-ratio symbols, `PF-168` makes bounded moving-tail spectral filters clone-blind, and `WP-152`--`WP-154` turn a natural cutoff and local curvature into universal or place-local laws. These are different categories but the strategic lesson is common: selection must precede universalization.

4. **Matched controls are becoming dynamical and decision-theoretic, not just static lookalikes.** `XF-040`--`XF-042` show that a static counterexample may disappear under the actual heat dynamics and identify the precise input required to sustain it. `AF-127`--`AF-128` similarly turn information loss into an optimal decision witness. A serious obstruction should now be tested for persistence under the source dynamics or destination decision, not merely for pointwise resemblance.

5. **The surviving analytic burdens are increasingly source-specific uniformity theorems.** `WI-159` rules out generic analytic-square regularity as the missing singular estimate; `PL-166` shows that the available unconditional off-RH phase comparison has the wrong growing-support horizon; and `XF-042` isolates centered exterior replenishment as the missing finite-window input. These are concrete places where stronger source arithmetic could produce a positive theorem rather than another representation.
