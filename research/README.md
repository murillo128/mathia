## Cross-line structure

The most reusable lesson is **perform every exact quotient before crediting a new observable**. Möbius gives the sharpest examples: an invertible scalar can still be RH-hard, and a genuinely translated character correlation can become a character-independent Ramanujan scalar after one complete conductor block. Weil Positivity similarly shows that a seemingly multidimensional finite interior selector packet becomes rank one once exact mixed-shell nullity is imposed. Prime Circle and Prime Lattice show the operator-theoretic analogue: closure can create a striking spectral boundary while forgetting the numerical source geometry. Future claims should therefore state which source variable remains after all completion, summation, symmetry, closure and normalization steps actually consumed downstream.

A second common theme is **detector resolution must match the source obstruction rather than merely be source-specific**. Nyman's exact pair-energy reduction removes the unnecessary absolute phase requirement, but it retains the microscopic Ford height window, moving carrier and depth marks; a detector that merges the entire resonant packet into one clump still loses the relevant information. Visual's inverse formula supplies a dual warning: attenuation in the transformed variable is not evidence of a stronger source law when the missing size simply moves into one derivative. The useful currency is therefore target-bearing signal at the detector's native scale, not generic smoothness, pair statistics or transform decay.

A third theme is **source laws can change the admissible collision geometry, but stability remains a separate bill**. Arithmetic Fidelity makes this exact: fixed mass changes the positive recovery problem from conic to affine/Radon circuits, and other support/weight laws change the sharp observation threshold. Analytic Frontier, Robin and the live Möbius branch then show the next step that such classification does not buy automatically: the actual arithmetic source must prevent the carrier-aligned, phase-aligned or incomplete-block matched configuration with a quantitative margin at the destination.

The Desogus audit is intentionally quarantined as external evidence. A fresh public-source check confirms the September 2026 preprint and its claimed RH conclusion, but Mathia credits only the subclaims independently reconstructed in the repository. The current upstream normalization defects and the `WI-366` reserve no-go are therefore reasons to continue the audit, not evidence either for or against the final RH theorem.

No new Master clue is justified. The current cross-line lessons are already represented by existing global intuition and by line-owned questions; there is no missing load-bearing foreign claim that requires new bounded cross-line ownership. No merge, split or new-line proposal is warranted.
