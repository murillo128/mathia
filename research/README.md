The strongest common bottleneck is now a **source-critical tightness and non-escape gate**. It is no longer enough for a carrier to retain information at each finite stage, or even to use a scale chosen canonically from the represented object. The arithmetic source must fix the destination category, the critical homogeneity or resolution at which the signal has nonvanishing mass, and the admissible complexity/resource law; the resulting family must then be tight relative to those source-fixed scales rather than exporting the missing information to ever smaller spectral scales, larger conductors, more remote repair mass, or farther physical distances. `AF-107`--`AF-113` make this hierarchy explicit. Three other lines independently realize the same escape mechanism. `ANF-030`--`ANF-032` show that sharp or near-sharp Montgomery--Taylor positive Palm witnesses become locally two-point sparse; any remaining abstract finite-configuration witness must therefore be a genuine **scale-escape sequence**, pushing its three-point structure beyond every fixed physical radius while somehow retaining nonwhite diffraction. `MC-056`--`MC-061` show that moving quadratic-character fits can imitate Möbius on an observed prefix only by paying rapidly growing conductor or cancellation constants, and `MC-061` makes the uniformity failure intrinsic: a single weighted quadratic fit already forces polynomial blow-up in every sublinear twisted-Möbius bound for that same moving character. Across these categories, the surviving question is not merely whether information exists, but whether the source prevents it from escaping the scale at which the destination theorem can use it.

There is also a constructive counterpoint in `xi_flow`. `XF-022`--`XF-025` show that broad fixed compact or summable quadratic localizations develop unavoidable adjacent-collision singularities. `XF-026`--`XF-027` isolate collision-smooth shape information and a scale-free normalized block discriminant, and `XF-028` removes the most immediate hard-boundary objection: overlapping normalized discriminants give every positively covered adjacent collision a leading `+8W_k/epsilon^2` production term, one order stronger than the remaining boundary contribution. This is not yet a Lyapunov theorem, but it is a real carrier change rather than another smoothing of a known obstruction. The open step is global finite-gap flux and support-edge control.
