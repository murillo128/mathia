# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia has thirteen valid research lines. Ten are evidence-bearing and active: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain distinct pre-evidence scaffolds.

The strongest common bottleneck has sharpened from a **source-calibrated complete-control margin gate** into a **source-provenance and target-calibrated budget gate**. A candidate carrier must retain something that is forced by the arithmetic source rather than created by a universal completion, quotient, scalarization, or representation choice; the norm/category/scale budget needed to recognize that provenance must be declared before the witness is selected; and the surviving quantity must then obey the exact asymptotic strength required by the destination theorem. The last clause matters: `PL-146` shows that even under RH the Suzuki checkpoint reserves have infimum zero, while `WI-140` shows that an exact off-line inertia count can coexist with arbitrarily small finite slack. The portfolio must therefore not confuse theorem-grade quantitative control with a universal positive charge per defect when the target/source geometry permits vanishing amplitudes.

`arithmetic_fidelity` makes the provenance/budget distinction exact. `AF-101` identifies original-target accessibility with equivalence of the relevant pairing norm, while `AF-102` shows that the formally similar second-leg functoriality is instead bidual accessibility. Most importantly, `AF-103` proves that preadjoint provenance is invisible to strong-operator finite-dimensional inspection without a uniform recovery budget: every finite restriction of an arbitrary dual-side operator can be matched exactly by a genuine preadjoint. Thus finite agreement can be complete for a declared interpolation cost and still fail to identify where the operator came from. The missing discriminator is a source-natural global budget/category condition, not another finite probe.

`analytic_frontier` supplies a complementary realization obstruction. `ANF-021`--`ANF-023` test three increasingly flexible classical support-one carriers—symplectic/Pfaffian scale mixtures, scale-mixed lattice diffraction, and iid perturbed lattices—and all still fail the Montgomery--Taylor target budget. The failures are different but converge on the same lesson: adding convex mixing, Bragg structure, or diffuse jitter inside a classical carrier class does not evade the complete finite-frequency/source constraints. A serious scalar improvement now needs a genuinely different realizability class or a separation theorem, not another mixture inside the already-controlled families.

`weil_inertia` now has the strongest non-scalar defect carrier in the portfolio, but its boundary is correspondingly sharper. `WI-137` identifies Lamzouri slack with an exact quantized operator distance, `WI-138` shows that tensor inertia counts off-line conjugate pairs exactly, and `WI-139` aligns the negative eigenspace with horizontal defect rather than merely recording a scalar count. `WI-140` then proves that these facts cannot be bootstrapped by a universal count-only stability charge: simple off-line pairs can confluence toward real doubles while the negative index stays nonzero and the complete slack collapses quadratically. The line therefore remains worth `continue`, but its live theorem is now explicitly source-specific: actual zeta density/correlation or another arithmetic interaction must forbid simultaneous cheap confluence or show that it remains possible.

`xi_flow` and `visual_exploration` also make source-aware progress while closing a tempting shortcut. `XF-018` localizes uncentered gap-square energy with only bounded cross-ratio leakage, `XF-019` reduces the remaining block tail to a super-mesoscopic buffer, and `XF-020` shows that the actual zero-counting error controls such buffers. `XF-021` proves why returning to a compact centered convex single-gap entropy does not solve the boundary problem: every compression-sensitive centered entropy admits an arbitrarily large positive adjacent-collision boundary spike unless it becomes blind to compression. The surviving route therefore needs nonlocal mean removal, signed boundary organization, or Xi-specific exterior information. `VIS-020`, meanwhile, turns the proposed three-gap residual into a falsifiable MaxEnt/Markov test whose null preserves the lower-order gap information.

Several negative results show where provenance is erased. `PC-163` closes the full zero-energy one-hole Kron determinant even after the prime-shell background is included, and `PC-164` closes the canonical energy-dependent Feshbach extension as well: its determinant is a prime-blind real Bloch quotient and its fixed-base self-energy converges to cyclotomic logarithmic-derivative data along both prime and matched composite refinements. `WP-140` identifies the corresponding minimal Kron pseudodeterminant logarithm as a normalization anomaly, while `WP-141` shows that the natural Fisher/logdet positivity repair removes even that anomaly without creating an arithmetic selector. `PF-167` supplies the geometric analogue: positive primitive-length accumulation is inherited by the all-composite shift clone, so more local clone geometry cannot replace a source-native global orbit selector.

Möbius and Prime Lattice clarify the transfer side. `MC-050` and `MC-051` show that a globally close real squarefree comparator with sufficiently strong cancellation self-upgrades to the desired zeta/Mertens region, so that route is not a cheaper intermediate theorem. `MC-052` leaves a genuinely different complex-amplitude frontier, but only at the price of an `L2` phase budget whose conversion to power cancellation remains the missing theorem. `PL-146` reduces Suzuki positivity exactly to a two-state prime-power event process with one convex checkpoint per interval and simultaneously proves that a uniform positive checkpoint reserve cannot be the missing theorem. The portfolio-level target is therefore no longer “find a positive margin”; it is **preserve source provenance, calibrate the admissible budget before inspection, and prove exactly the scale-dependent interaction, no-escape, no-crossing, or transfer law that the destination theorem consumes**.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Provenance is invisible after some universalizations unless a budget is fixed first.** `AF-103` proves this exactly for preadjoints under finite strong-operator inspection. `PC-163`--`PC-164` and `WP-140`--`WP-141` show line-specific versions: once one-hole or positivity data is reduced to a fixed-base Bloch/cyclotomic determinant or normalization law, prime/composite provenance disappears. A successful route should therefore couple source-specific structure before the classicalizing map and carry a declared quantitative budget through it.

2. **Complete controls still beat carrier enrichment inside the same realization class.** `ANF-021`--`ANF-023` add Pfaffian mixing, lattice diffraction mixing, and iid jitter without escaping the Montgomery--Taylor budget. `PF-167` similarly shows that a stronger local accumulation invariant survives the shift clone. Complexity only counts as progress when it changes the information class seen by the matched controls.

3. **The target budget need not be a uniform positive margin or charge.** `PL-146` shows that under RH Suzuki checkpoint reserves may tend to zero while only no-crossing is required. `WI-140` gives the operator-side analogue: exact negative index and perfect eigenspace orientation can survive while the associated eigenvalue/slack amplitude tends to zero. Arithmetic Fidelity needs uniformity for a different reason—provenance/assembly identification. Quantitative demands must therefore be attached to the exact source/destination interface rather than promoted to one universal margin axiom.

4. **Operator-valued sign/eigenspace information preserves structure, but amplitude still needs source interaction.** `WI-137`--`WI-139` show a genuine carrier gain before scalarization; `WI-140` proves that this gain alone does not furnish a per-defect coercive gap. The missing ingredient is now unusually precise: a zeta-compatible density/correlation constraint must make simultaneous confluence expensive, or a matched source-compatible countermodel must demonstrate that it does not.

5. **Nonlocal organization is increasingly forced rather than stylistic.** `PC-164` shows that one canonical nonlocal Feshbach extension can still classicalize completely, while `XF-021` shows that compact centered single-site convexity has a structural collision boundary failure. `XF-018`--`XF-020` and `VIS-020` point to the two healthier forms of nonlocality currently visible: source-controlled exterior aggregation and relational statistics defined only after lower-order controls are removed.

## Portfolio changes proposed

Upgrade `weil_inertia` from `narrow` to `continue`. `WI-137`--`WI-139` preserve the horizontal defect in a materially stronger operator carrier, and `WI-140` does not erase that gain; it removes the naive count-only coercivity bootstrap and leaves a sharply falsifiable source-specific interaction theorem as the decisive next test. This is a productive frontier rather than an open-ended search.

No pause, merge, split, new-line action, or Research Watch rotation is justified. The other nine enabled evidence-bearing lines retain their current status, and the three scaffolds `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` still have mandates but no theorem-grade local evidence warranting activation. No new Master clue is needed: `WI-140` and `XF-021` already state their next falsification surfaces canonically, the existing cross-line fidelity/realization clue covers the provenance-versus-destination distinction, and the updated Visual Exploration three-gap clue states the MaxEnt/Markov matched-control experiment.

The important portfolio correction is conceptual rather than organizational. The previous phrase “positive quantitative margin” was too strong as a universal requirement: `PL-146` and `WI-140` independently show target-relevant situations where the correct sign/defect classification can coexist with amplitudes tending to zero. The next portfolio-level discriminator is therefore: **preserve source provenance through the chosen carrier, declare the norm/category/scale budget before inspection, survive the complete source-valid control family, and prove the exact scale-dependent interaction, transfer, or no-escape law required by the destination theorem—without reintroducing the target hypothesis.**
