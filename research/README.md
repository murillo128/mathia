# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia has thirteen valid research lines. Ten are evidence-bearing and active: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain distinct pre-evidence scaffolds.

The strongest common bottleneck is now best stated as a **source-provenance, source-natural accessibility-cost, and target-calibrated destination gate**. A candidate carrier must retain something forced by the arithmetic source rather than created by a universal completion, quotient, scalarization, or representation choice; the norm/category/scale cost needed to recover or recognize that provenance must be fixed from the source before the witness is selected; and the surviving quantity must then obey exactly the asymptotic strength required by the destination theorem. The last clause matters: `PL-146` shows that even under RH the Suzuki checkpoint reserves have infimum zero, while `WI-140` shows that an exact off-line inertia count can coexist with arbitrarily small finite slack. Uniform bounded recovery can be essential for provenance even when a uniform positive defect charge is impossible at the destination.

`arithmetic_fidelity` now makes the cost side of this gate concrete rather than merely adversarial. `AF-101` identifies original-target accessibility with equivalence of the relevant pairing norm, `AF-102` separates this from bidual accessibility, and `AF-103` proves that preadjoint provenance is invisible to strong-operator finite-dimensional inspection without a uniform recovery budget. `AF-104` then realizes the obstruction in classical Banach-space theory: Reinov's AP-without-BAP example can match every bounded finite observation at every finite stage while the least norm needed for exact finite-dimensional recovery escapes every fixed constant. Thus finite observational completeness plus stagewise repair does not identify a globally bounded source-faithful assembly. A viable theorem must derive its admissible recovery/rank-growth budget from the arithmetic source itself and then prove that every fake realization violates that budget.

`analytic_frontier` supplies a complementary realizability obstruction. `ANF-021`--`ANF-023` exclude symplectic/Pfaffian scale mixtures, scale-mixed lattice diffraction, and iid perturbed lattices at the Montgomery--Taylor target budget. `ANF-024` now closes the whole stationary renewal/iid-gap family covered by the classical diffraction theorem: any `O(|h|)` diffuse floor forces the gap law to be deterministic, convex mixtures therefore reduce to lattice-scale mixtures, and the existing finite dilation certificate still blocks the Montgomery--Taylor constant. The stochastic scalar frontier has consequently moved past independent randomness; a survivor must use genuine inter-gap correlations, a different realizability class, direct finite-cluster convexification, or a separation theorem for the full admissible cone.

`weil_inertia` remains the strongest non-scalar defect carrier in the portfolio, but its boundary is correspondingly sharp. `WI-137` identifies Lamzouri slack with an exact quantized operator distance, `WI-138` shows that tensor inertia counts off-line conjugate pairs exactly, and `WI-139` aligns the negative eigenspace with horizontal defect rather than merely recording a scalar count. `WI-140` then proves that these facts cannot be bootstrapped by a universal count-only stability charge: simple off-line pairs can confluence toward real doubles while the negative index stays nonzero and the complete slack collapses quadratically. The line remains worth `continue`, but its live theorem is explicitly source-specific: actual zeta density/correlation or another arithmetic interaction must forbid simultaneous cheap confluence or show that it remains possible.

`xi_flow` and `visual_exploration` also make source-aware progress while closing a tempting shortcut. `XF-018` localizes uncentered gap-square energy with only bounded cross-ratio leakage, `XF-019` reduces the remaining block tail to a super-mesoscopic buffer, and `XF-020` shows that the actual zero-counting error controls such buffers. `XF-021` proves why returning to a compact centered convex single-gap entropy does not solve the boundary problem: every compression-sensitive centered entropy admits an arbitrarily large positive adjacent-collision boundary spike unless it becomes blind to compression. The surviving route therefore needs nonlocal mean removal, signed boundary organization, or Xi-specific exterior information. `VIS-020`, meanwhile, turns the proposed three-gap residual into a falsifiable MaxEnt/Markov test whose null preserves the lower-order gap information.

Several negative results show where provenance is erased. `PC-163` closes the full zero-energy one-hole Kron determinant even after the prime-shell background is included, and `PC-164` closes the canonical energy-dependent Feshbach extension as well: its determinant is a prime-blind real Bloch quotient and its fixed-base self-energy converges to cyclotomic logarithmic-derivative data along both prime and matched composite refinements. `WP-140` identifies the corresponding minimal Kron pseudodeterminant logarithm as a normalization anomaly, while `WP-141` shows that the natural Fisher/logdet positivity repair removes even that anomaly without creating an arithmetic selector. `PF-167` supplies the geometric analogue: positive primitive-length accumulation is inherited by the all-composite shift clone, so more local clone geometry cannot replace a source-native global orbit selector.

Möbius and Prime Lattice clarify the transfer side. `MC-050` and `MC-051` show that a globally close real squarefree comparator with sufficiently strong cancellation self-upgrades to the desired zeta/Mertens region, so that route is not a cheaper intermediate theorem. `MC-052` leaves a genuinely different complex-amplitude frontier, but only at the price of an `L2` phase budget whose conversion to power cancellation remains the missing theorem. `PL-146` reduces Suzuki positivity exactly to a two-state prime-power event process with one convex checkpoint per interval and simultaneously proves that a uniform positive checkpoint reserve cannot be the missing theorem. The portfolio-level target is therefore: **preserve source provenance, fix the source-natural recovery cost before inspection, and prove exactly the scale-dependent interaction, no-escape, no-crossing, or transfer law consumed by the destination theorem**.

## Research-line portfolio

### `analytic_frontier` — `narrow`

The support-one scalar frontier has survived another exact stress test without finding a new realizable carrier. `ANF-021` rules out symplectic/Pfaffian scale mixtures, `ANF-022` rules out scale-mixed lattice diffraction, `ANF-023` shows that iid lattice perturbations pay a unit cloaking tax, and `ANF-024` proves that stationary renewal processes with genuinely random iid gaps have a positive infrared diffuse floor and therefore cannot meet the required `O(|h|)` domination. Convexifying renewal laws only rigidifies the survivors to lattice mixtures, which are already excluded at the Montgomery--Taylor constant. Continue only with correlated-gap/interacting processes, a genuinely different realizability class, direct finite-cluster constructions, an extremal/separation theorem for the full finite-configuration cone, or signed/PSD/configuration-level carriers outside these closed classical families.

### `arithmetic_fidelity` — `narrow`

The live issue is now **provenance under a source-natural recovery-cost law**. `AF-101`--`AF-103` show why finite inspection cannot distinguish the desired original-range/preadjoint assembly without an a priori budget. `AF-104` strengthens this from a constructed loophole to a realized infinite-dimensional obstruction: AP can hold while BAP fails, so every finite stage admits exact finite-information repair even though every fixed norm budget eventually breaks. The next useful result must derive a canonical arithmetic budget or rank-growth schedule from the source and prove a genuine obstruction inside that budgeted category. More finite probes, post-selected norms, or unconstrained stagewise repairs cannot close the gap.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Finite observational completeness is not bounded source accessibility.** `AF-103` proves that preadjoint provenance can be invisible to every finite strong-operator inspection, and `AF-104` shows that exact finite-stage repair can persist in a classical AP space while every fixed norm budget eventually fails because BAP is absent. `PC-163`--`PC-164` and `WP-140`--`WP-141` show line-specific versions of the same warning after classicalization: once source data has been reduced to a fixed-base Bloch/cyclotomic determinant or normalization law, adding more finite inspection cannot recover lost provenance. A successful route should therefore couple source-specific structure before the classicalizing map and carry a source-derived cost law through it.

2. **Complete controls still beat carrier enrichment inside the same realization class.** `ANF-021`--`ANF-024` now eliminate Pfaffian mixing, lattice diffraction mixing, iid site jitter, and iid gap disorder. `ANF-024` is especially informative because the `O(|h|)` target itself forces renewal randomness to disappear before the Montgomery--Taylor band test; independent stochastic enrichment collapses back to a previously closed lattice family. `PF-167` similarly shows that a stronger local accumulation invariant survives the shift clone. Complexity counts as progress only when it changes the information class seen by the matched controls.

3. **The target budget need not be a uniform positive margin or charge.** `PL-146` shows that under RH Suzuki checkpoint reserves may tend to zero while only no-crossing is required. `WI-140` gives the operator-side analogue: exact negative index and perfect eigenspace orientation can survive while the associated eigenvalue/slack amplitude tends to zero. Arithmetic Fidelity needs uniformity for a different reason—source provenance and assembly cost. Quantitative demands must therefore be attached to the exact source/destination interface rather than promoted to one universal margin axiom.

4. **Operator-valued sign/eigenspace information preserves structure, but amplitude still needs source interaction.** `WI-137`--`WI-139` show a genuine carrier gain before scalarization; `WI-140` proves that this gain alone does not furnish a per-defect coercive gap. The missing ingredient is now unusually precise: a zeta-compatible density/correlation constraint must make simultaneous confluence expensive, or a matched source-compatible countermodel must demonstrate that it does not.

5. **Nonlocal organization is increasingly forced rather than stylistic.** `PC-164` shows that one canonical nonlocal Feshbach extension can still classicalize completely, while `XF-021` shows that compact centered single-site convexity has a structural collision boundary failure. `XF-018`--`XF-020` and `VIS-020` point to the two healthier forms of nonlocality currently visible: source-controlled exterior aggregation and relational statistics defined only after lower-order controls are removed. `ANF-024` adds an independent reason to seek interaction: iid gap randomness is too rigid at low frequency, so any stochastic scalar survivor must encode correlations between spacings.

## Portfolio changes proposed

The important portfolio update is conceptual. `AF-104` shows that unbounded repair cost is a realized invariant rather than a hypothetical loophole, while `ANF-024` shows that independent stochastic enrichment can be forced back into a previously classified carrier before the decisive band test. The current discriminator is therefore: **preserve source provenance through the chosen carrier, derive the admissible recovery/complexity cost from the source before inspection, survive the complete source-valid control family, and prove the exact scale-dependent interaction, transfer, or no-escape law required by the destination theorem—without reintroducing the target hypothesis.**
