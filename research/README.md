# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The common RH-facing gap remains an independently source-forced estimate in the precise norm used by the destination. Reconstruction, auxiliary positivity and existence of a zero-sensitive test do not supply that estimate. No current Mathia finding proves RH.

Two distinct recovery losses have a canonical combined repair. [MC-532](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-532-paired-boxes-recover-trimmed-mobius-variance-at-macroscopic-lengths.md) composes [PL-475's two-box inverse](prime_lattice/findings/PL-475-two-box-lengths-force-paired-macroscopic-prime-variance.md) with bounded-jump recovery of deleted Möbius starts. The paired macroscopic retained-energy and deletion-count criterion is now proved equivalent to RH, with separate masks and centers on one unbounded scale set fixed before epsilon varies. The [transfer clue is resolved](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-paired-box-trimmed-mobius-recovery.md); the independent arithmetic estimates remain missing. The reverse transfer of missing-start recovery to the prime-weighted source is a [proposed test](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-paired-prime-variance-missing-start-recovery.md), not an established prime theorem.

Canonical findings outrank older Mind frontiers where they differ. No adversarial sidecar is currently open; this is not independent approval of the corpus. WI-002 is withdrawn, and [WI-456](weil_inertia/findings/WI-456-retracted-moment-transport-leaves-only-a-conditional-density-capstone.md) retains only a conditional moment-to-density theorem after retraction of the Yang–Yang arithmetic transport. Even a valid density-one theorem leaves individual exceptions possible. The [Atlas v1 metrics](graph/atlas/metrics.md) incorporate MC-532 as a refinement of the existing missing-start episode, while their entrypoint narrative and other frontier descriptions lag current sources. Their selective episode rates are not a current whole-portfolio fertility measurement; recommendations below rest on canonical evidence.

## Cross-line structure

**Prime and reciprocal recovery share a smooth-test interface, with different analytic inputs.** [PL-471](prime_lattice/findings/PL-471-sparse-zero-annihilators-force-prime-variance-on-every-large-block.md) isolates a selected mod-5 zero using sparse zero counts and noncancelling logarithmic-derivative residues. [MC-528](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-528-multiplicity-aware-annihilation-forces-mobius-variance-on-every-block.md) reconstructs the test for reciprocal zeta, cancelling every unwanted pole with full multiplicity and proving the needed local division bound. Its retained order-`m` pole contributes `X^rho Q_(m-1)(log X)` with nonzero leading coefficient even when the ordinary residue vanishes. The complex test avoids selected-scale cosine zeros. Neither result needs a rightmost zero or finite exceptional family; both retain full-zero-set dependence and non-effective onset.

For one box, [PL-474](prime_lattice/findings/PL-474-nonquasianalytic-tests-close-the-iterated-log-window-boundary.md) and [MC-530](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-530-nonquasianalytic-tests-reach-logarithmic-mobius-windows.md) reach every integer `1<=H<=X/[log X (loglog X)^r]`, for each fixed `r>1`, on every sufficiently large real block. [PL-473](prime_lattice/findings/PL-473-box-fourier-zeros-obstruct-logarithmic-window-test-inversion.md) excludes the same fixed-target, all-scale power-saving inverse certificate for `0<=r<=1`. This is a boundary of that approximation method, not of variance itself. [MC-529](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-529-single-near-linear-centered-variance-law-is-equivalent-to-rh.md) already makes its epsilon-loss near-linear ceiling on any unbounded scale set RH-equivalent.

PL-475 changes the operator rather than the target's regularity. Its classical [compact-distribution two-channel inverse](prior_art/incremental/PA-multichannel-compact-distribution-deconvolution.md) retains bounded localized mean-zero tests after dilation. With `K=floor(sqrt(2)H)`, integer rounding costs `O(1/X)` in target sup norm and `O(log X)` against the prime coefficients. A hypothetical mod-5 zero of real part `beta>1/2` therefore forces

`V_1(X,H)/H^2 + V_1(X,K)/K^2 >= c X^(2beta-1)`

for all integer `1<=H<=X/20` on every sufficiently large real block. Both variances have their own empirical means on the same base block. Both ceilings are required for the resulting joint-GRH implication at macroscopic lengths; one favorable channel is insufficient. Rational common zeros and Liouville small divisors exclude arbitrary choices of the second kernel. This is finite-derivative continuity on smooth tests, not a bounded inverse on arbitrary L2 data.

MC-531 addresses a separate loss. For deleted integer starts `E` of cardinality `B<X/2`, retained energy `V_E(c)` around any single constant `c`, and its existing single-box window range, it proves

`sqrt(X V_E(c)) + B^2 >= C H X^beta (log X)^(m-1)`.

The exact jump bound of two recovers the complete linear pairing without cutting its mean-zero analytic test. At the near-linear window, simultaneous bounds `B <<_epsilon X^(3/4+epsilon)` and `V_E(c) <<_epsilon X^(1+epsilon)H` on any unbounded integer scale set are equivalent to RH, with the families fixed before the epsilon quantifier. Qualitative density one is insufficient. Generic bounded-coefficient tent controls make quadratic absolute-mass recovery sharp; they do not prove the optimal deletion threshold for signed Möbius tests.

[MC-532](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-532-paired-boxes-recover-trimmed-mobius-variance-at-macroscopic-lengths.md) proves the composition retained in [MI-040](mind/intuition/MI-040-paired-box-inversion-transports-trimmed-mobius-pole-tests.md). For `L_1=H`, `L_2=K=floor(sqrt(2)H)`, integer `1<=H<=X/20`, separate deleted-start counts `B_i<X/2` and retained energies `V_i` around arbitrary constants, it gives

`(sqrt(X V_1)+B_1^2)/H + (sqrt(X V_2)+B_2^2)/K >= c X^beta (log X)^(m-1)`.

At `H=floor(X/20)`, simultaneous `V_i <<_epsilon X^(1+epsilon)L_i` and `B_i <<_epsilon X^(3/4+epsilon)` on one unbounded integer scale set are equivalent to RH. The scale, mask and center families are fixed before epsilon varies; MC-520 gives the converse with empty masks. Rounding costs `O(1)` against Möbius, and complete mean-zero tests are never cut by the masks. The [resolved handoff](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-paired-box-trimmed-mobius-recovery.md) has therefore changed the admissible observations, not supplied their upper estimates. The [reverse prime-source clue](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-paired-prime-variance-missing-start-recovery.md) asks whether the bounded-jump repair survives the logarithmic coefficient envelope. Separately, the existing [single-box range clue](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-single-box-range-sparse-mellin-interpolation.md) asks whether a target chosen inside one fixed box range can isolate the sparse zero set; it neither reopens arbitrary-target inversion nor assumes the answer.

**Calibration restrictions, range and precision are separate resources.** [AF-580](arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-580-positive-noise-destroys-finite-stopping-under-unknown-polynomial-calibration.md) rules out a uniformly reliable almost-sure finite return even at a noiseless input when reliability is required over unknown unrestricted polynomial calibration and fixed positive supremum-bounded error, despite a positive full-ray margin. [AF-584](arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-584-growing-calibration-degree-has-sharp-quadratic-range-cost.md) closes the growing-degree range order. For the binary profile `x exp(1-x)` on `(0,B]`, writing `nu=r^2/B`, the lost margin is `Theta(nu^2 log(1/nu))` when `nu` is small, uniformly in `r>=2`; it tends to zero exactly when `r^2/B -> 0`. Retaining fraction `1-delta` needs range `Theta(r^2 sqrt(log(1/delta)/delta))`. [AF-585](arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-585-cubic-calibration-has-sharp-range-constant-from-endpoint-curvature.md) fixes the cubic deficit constant at `27/2`, giving range constant `3 sqrt(3)/2`; the limiting endpoint-constrained approximant is an affine Chebyshev transform. The uniform prime-edit profile error has no degree amplification, and `delta log p -> infinity` suffices for quantitative transfer. Constants for degree four and above, a common multiway design and absolute precision remain open. These are generalized-prime controls, not ordinary-prime discrimination.

The [AF–Xi comparison](mind/intuition/MI-039-calibration-gluing-transfers-randomized-stopping-bounds-only-in-the-admitted-function-class.md) prevents a false transfer: Xi's total squared-error budget permits chord-average error `sigma sqrt(2/n)/h`, whereas fixed supremum error permits `2 epsilon/h` without averaging gain. **Physical assembly, normalization and destination sign need different theorems.** [NB-356](nyman_beurling/findings/NB-356-finite-orbit-target-assembly-is-equivalent-to-a-reciprocal-phase-series.md) gives the finite-energy reciprocal-phase criterion for algebraic assembly from complete orbit spaces; specified higher-rank integer families remain undecided. For irrational `alpha=log p/log q` and squared component energy `E(epsilon)`, each `kappa>0` satisfies

`E(epsilon)=O(epsilon^(-kappa)) iff inf_(n>=1) n^(1+kappa/2)||n alpha|| > 0`.

[NB-359](nyman_beurling/findings/NB-359-quadratic-mean-digits-characterize-logarithmic-two-orbit-energy.md) closes the logarithmic endpoint: `E(epsilon)=Theta(log(1/epsilon))` exactly when the continued-fraction digits satisfy `sup_m m^(-1) sum_(j<=m) a_j^2<infinity`. This includes some unbounded-digit real seed ratios. The limsup power remains `2(mu(alpha)-2)`, without asserting an endpoint power bound; subpolynomial energy need not be logarithmic. No such digit property is established for a specified integer log ratio. The target remains projected, the Burnol floor remains, and complete real-dilation orbit vectors may need infinitely many generators; no discrete acquisition rate follows.

**All-zero sampling is now quantitative in specified regimes, but does not control individual defects.** [WI-460](weil_inertia/findings/WI-460-adaptive-euler-peaks-remain-rare-at-zeta-zero-ordinates.md) gives vanishing responsive fraction for every shrinking width bank `R(T)->0`. [VIS-462](visual_exploration/findings/VIS-462-zero-ordinate-shell-moments-pay-resonance-and-small-frequency-costs.md) resolves the [actual-zero character handoff](visual_exploration/clues/CLUE-zero-ordinate-growing-shell-character-tariff.md). For a bounded-bandwidth Wang shell at height `Y_T`, normalized Wiener mass `W_T`, and each fixed mixed moment degree `R`, it gives sufficient cost

`W_T^(2R) [1/log T + log T/T + (2Y_T)^(2R)/T] -> 0`.

The actual coefficient bound `W_T<=2Y_T` covers every nonzero shell with `log Y_T=o(loglog T)` at every fixed degree. Fixed-radius continuous path tests then follow; threshold indicators still need Haar boundary-mass control. A separate larger aligned `H=1` example is not the active Wang source scaling. Near-zero frequencies of order `1/T` can retain order-one box-transform response, while prime-power resonances have classical order-`1/log T` bias. Failure of the sufficient cost is not evidence of an anomaly. Growing radii, growing moments, shrinking thresholds and consecutive-zero gaps remain separate questions.

[VIS-463](visual_exploration/findings/VIS-463-prime-only-shell-symmetry-erases-zero-resonance-bias.md) resolves that leading bias coefficient by coefficient. For a fixed mixed amplitude polynomial `P=sum c_m z^m`, it is `-B(P)/log T`, with `B(P)=sum_(p,k!=0)c_(k e_p) log(p)/p^(|k|/2)`. Prime-only ratio shells are common-phase invariant and have `B(P)=0`; the full prime-power shell can have nonzero bias even after exact supremum normalization. The remainder cost is `W_T^(2R)[1/(log T)^2+log T/T+(2Y_T)^(2R)/T]`. Deleting higher powers therefore changes the calibrated observable at inverse-log resolution; their coefficient mass, not just their count, must be controlled. This is classical prime-resonant correction, not a selector anomaly.

The [prime missing-start transfer](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-paired-prime-variance-missing-start-recovery.md) and [single-box range test](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-single-box-range-sparse-mellin-interpolation.md) concern observation requirements, not estimates. The finite model and weak bulk questions already settled should not substitute for these remaining steps.
3. **Individual defects and physical sampling:** establish independent coupling at the actual depth of an off-line block. WI's controlled shrinking-scale overlap and VIS's growing-shell calibration are all-zero statements. The physical Wang coefficient/separation regime and any selector residual must be checked against the resolved null before it can motivate a new mechanism.
4. **Source constraints beyond matched controls:** quantify AF multiway range and absolute precision, preserve Xi's distinct noise geometry, and bound Robin's actual CA-selected signed remote tail. Another calibration profile or balanced synthetic construction alone does not supply those inputs.
