# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia has thirteen current research lines. Ten are evidence-bearing: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain distinct pre-evidence scaffolds.

The strongest common bottleneck is now a **source-forced pre-classicalization selector and transport gate**. Earlier work established that the source must fix the representation, gauge, topology, scale, and quantitative transport profile. The newest evidence adds a sharper warning: several natural operations that look like harmless completion, averaging, closure, or positive assembly are actually irreversible classicalization steps. Once the source-dependent interaction has been collapsed into a target-equivalent scalar, a vanishing average, a separable prime-axis operator, a character variety, or an already-scalarized positive bound, later analysis cannot reconstruct the missing arithmetic selector.

`MC-083` and `MC-084` now bracket this obstruction from both sides. The coarsest constant-weight annular Möbius contrast is exactly Mertens-square up to a controlled diagonal, while the fully source-completed exact sawtooth annulus, after its forced coarse terms are restored, is again equivalent to the Mertens target at doubled scale. Thus neither maximal compression nor maximal exact completion gives an independently weaker mechanism; the only live Möbius window is a **strict partial, nonconstant, source-fixed statistic** whose estimate and recombination ledger are genuinely weaker than the target.

The same irreversibility appears in other lines. `PL-173` shows that uniform affine Liouville averaging either vanishes strongly or, when all shifts are recombined, collapses to a Liouville summatory square. `PC-178` shows that the canonical prime-axis resultant form is semibounded but nonclosable, while Abel removal turns the singular boundary mode into a scalar operator. `WI-165` proves that any fixed positive mixture of already-scalarized block bounds cannot beat its best constituent. `WP-159`--`WP-160` close the simplest additive torsion geometry and then the whole fixed finite-dimensional algebraic torsion-incidence family by reducing scalable torsion sets to translated-character geometry already known to split prime-primary information. These results independently say: **preserve the source coupling before the operation that makes the object easy to average, close, diagonalize, or optimize.**

`XF-048`--`XF-049` provide the strongest positive counterpart. At the Xi endpoint, the explicit formula gives a genuine source-specific selector: the critical memory frequency tends to zero while the first nonzero prime-power Fourier line stays at `log 2/2`, and a Gaussian probe separates the actual Xi endpoint statistic from the coherent memory-wave control. `XF-049` then proves in every finite real-simple polynomial heat-flow model that positive-frequency zero dynamics is Volterra-triangular: frequency `xi` is driven only by frequencies in `[0,xi]`, and its flat-density linearization reproduces the previously derived Cauchy slow-mode clock exactly. Generic high-frequency nonlinear mixing therefore cannot manufacture the missing low-frequency compensation. The theorem-level gap is no longer to invent another observable, but to transport this endpoint selector through the renormalized, localized infinite Xi flow with an `o(1)` error.

`VIS-041` and `AF-131`--`AF-132` specify how such a transport claim must be audited. Three-gap residual directions are meaningful only after zeta and matched-null residuals are placed in one common Fisher metric and one common orientation; separate whitening can manufacture a directional difference. Likewise, exact recovery values do not compose by themselves: the downstream state is an error/regularity profile whose transport modulus can amplify a small residual. The program-level discriminator is therefore: **fix the source category and common gauge; take the strongest complete matched control; identify a pre-classicalization selector that survives it; and prove that the entire selector/error/regularity profile remains quantitatively separated through the exact transport and assembly consumed by the target theorem.**

## Cross-line structure

1. **Both over-compression and over-completion can be target-equivalent.** `MC-083`--`MC-084` make this exact in one line: the coarsest annular projection yields Mertens square, while the fully completed exact source statistic yields Mertens cancellation at doubled scale. The live object, if one exists, must sit strictly between those extremes and retain source structure without already encoding the target.

2. **Classicalization is effectively irreversible.** `PL-173`, `PC-178`, `WI-165`, and `WP-160` reach the same boundary through averaging, operator closure, convex positive mixing, and algebraic torsion classification. Once the surviving object has become zero, a one-point square, a scalar operator, the best constituent scalar bound, or a separable translated-character family, no post hoc determinant, mixture, or spectrum can restore the lost interaction.

3. **Xi now has a genuine source selector before that collapse.** `XF-048` uses the endpoint explicit formula to create a frequency gap that a coherent critical memory wave violates, and `XF-049` shows that finite heat-zero dynamics has no generic high-to-low Fourier convolution channel capable of repairing it. This is qualitatively stronger than another no-go: it isolates a source-specific discriminator and a precise transport theorem still missing.

4. **Transport and gauge are part of the arithmetic statement.** `AF-131`--`AF-132` require the entire error/regularity profile to survive composition, while `VIS-041` shows that even a residual direction can be a gauge artifact if source and control are whitened or oriented separately. A selector is useful only if it remains separated in the exact common metric and topology used downstream.

5. **The remaining promising constructions are pre-scalarization interactions, not richer post-processing.** Analytic Frontier needs cross-frequency coherence, Weil Inertia needs geometry-aware pre-collapse assembly, Weil Positivity needs a non-character or finite--archimedean/global coupling, Prime Lattice needs a nonuniform/additive relation, and Prime Circle must leave its fixed finite Cauchy/cyclotomic closure class. The common task is to derive the interaction from the source before a canonical simplification erases it.
