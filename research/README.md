## Cross-line structure

### 1. Ambient progress is increasingly separable from source progress

`FD-099`, `PC-282`, and `WP-288` make the distinction exact in three different currencies. Farey can descend through many predecessor horizons without changing the source quotient; Prime Circle can make the profile singular only to recover the already-classical modular selector; Weil Positivity can enrich the scalar cone only to polarize back to the same Weil distribution. These are not failures of representation size. They show that an operation can look stronger while adding no new source-forced constraint. The existing global fidelity/transport/destination clue remains the right accounting rule: a gain should be credited only when the arithmetic source forces it and the target cannot reproduce it through a matched control.

### 2. Exact local compatibility does not supply quantitative or collective margin

`AF-321`, `NB-098`, and `WI-280` close three tempting extrapolations. Exact finite recovery may become arbitrarily ill-conditioned; every pair may be benign while the full causal shear diverges; every individual or finitely mixed cut statistic may be strengthened while a generic counterpath still satisfies them all. The next theorems must therefore use quantitative prime-log separation, genuine zeta multi-state geometry, or the exact Suzuki source/eigenfunction equation respectively. More local tests of the same generic type are unlikely to change those endpoints.

### 3. Endpoint success is appearing when the source-specific geometry is kept through the observable

`RE-101` is the strongest current positive example: the deformed CA sample and its paired kernel are retained together long enough to cross an actual exponent frontier, rather than being replaced by a generic fourth moment. Prime Flute follows the same discipline by carrying an explicit critical packet through the exact seam normalization before asking whether the destination angle kills it. Prime Lattice's accepted boundary-stress clue has the same falsifiability standard: the universal logarithmic principal part is explicitly treated as a matched control, so only the non-universal completed remainder can count as new arithmetic information.

### 4. The remaining Möbius and Visual problems are structured global energies, not collections of bad local events

`MC-271` rules out positive generating-function majorization precisely because it discards signed fixed-shell cancellation. `VIS-209` moves in the opposite direction and packages all one-cancellation shell multiplicities into one PSD Gram kernel whose trace is already the correct normalization. In both lines the useful next object is global and source-structured: a signed fixed-shell energy for Möbius, and spectral spreading/effective rank of the anchor kernel for Visual. Pointwise control of every support or shell would be a stronger and less natural target than the endpoint actually requires.
