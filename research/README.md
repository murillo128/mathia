## Cross-line structure


**Local integrality, directed-edge inventories and even reflection do not bound global transport.** FD-323 gives an unreflected three-near-one-defect control, whereas FD-324 preserves reflection with four near-one defects. Both reach the same explicit-family 3/pi² energy supremum, and FD-319's smaller reflected constant was not a ceiling. These are artificial labeled-edge grids, not genuine Farey orders. RE-249/250 give the parallel warning for generalized-prime gap fidelity.


**Spectral comparison requires relative transport, variation, parity, and model-specific normalization.** VIS-447 controls every normalized full sine subset by min(1,sqrt(E_p/T_p)), VIS-448 bounds high-band mass by R_p/(2q), and VIS-449 identifies the vanishing even-sector m~k boundary layer of FD-323. VIS-450 strengthens the latter's transfer by projecting before normalization: with finite T_even>=c/k, k E_p->0 suffices, subject to separate finite-transport convergence. FD-324 has an equally energetic **even-only** reflected fixed-mode limit, opposite FD-323's odd-only limit. Each null needs its own expected total and band energy, an explicit absolute-mode or parity-index convention, and a fixed-parameter prime limit before the family limit. The accepted Farey–Visual clues remain open; [[research/visual_exploration/clues/CLUE-farey-reflection-parity-equal-energy-control]] proposes the distinct energy-matched parity test.

**Higher-order or changing-window information can survive second-order equality without arithmetic specificity.** MC-515's rectangular dependence yields a non-Gaussian limit despite exactly matching binomial mean, variance and pairwise statistics. VIS-441–VIS-444 show that matching full triple correlations can still leave different hitting laws, while the complete occupation-moment hierarchy recovers the Haar avoidance law. VIS-445/446 refine fixed-arc occupation near a moving gap boundary, but growing Wang shells require new uniformity. These are distinct models, not a proved Möbius–Wang identification; the outstanding test is an arithmetic, source-selected higher-order or hitting residual.


**Operator category, centering and normalization are part of the theorem.** PC-436 extends zero-free Hilbert-shell completeness through finite Fourier masks without a genuine zeta-weighted sparse converse or frame bounds. PL-450 excludes the raw uncentered Fejér image gate throughout every fixed polynomial-resolution exponent below 5/6, even when TH/X grows, but not the centered Reynolds/character covariance. PF-345's raw Schur smallness does not prove a small normalized angle; WP-437's noncompact prime-ray limit retains a negative Weil block; XF-188/190 distinguish normalized-frame loss from raw physical known-endpoint recovery. AF-561's corrected right-tail conditioning likewise coexists with AF-552/554's boundary/critical-window carriers.


**Near-optimal models and population statements cannot exclude individual zeros.** WI-445's no-real-triples theorem holds at Wang's exact optimizer and under bounded-span near-minimizer perturbations; WI-446 constructs admissible smooth near-minimizers with **infinite** orthogonal lattices whose spacings escape every fixed span. A viable application needs independent zero-ordinate packing, a fixed test before the zeta-height limit and control of off-line conjugate blocks. ANF-191's external half-plane claim remains unverified, and NB-345's signed operator residue does not establish physical Ford-target occupation.
