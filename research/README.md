# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The common RH-facing gap remains an independently source-forced estimate in the precise norm used by the destination. Reconstruction, auxiliary positivity and existence of a zero-sensitive test do not supply that estimate. No current Mathia finding proves RH.

Sparse observations now retain both arithmetic criteria. [MC-533](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-533-geometric-start-coverage-recovers-sparsely-sampled-mobius-variance.md) and [PL-478](prime_lattice/findings/PL-478-geometric-coverage-retains-sparsely-sampled-prime-variance.md) use a fixed grid of order `sqrt(X)` starts and two macroscopic window lengths. The respective sample-energy statements are equivalent to RH and joint mod-5 GRH; neither ceiling is proved independently. [MC-534](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-534-common-squarefree-coefficients-retain-signed-sampling-ambiguity.md) strengthens the opposing control: exact square-free magnitudes and one common source for all interval observations still allow signed-target ambiguity. Its arbitrary signs do not establish an information lower bound for actual Möbius data.

Several other apparent escapes now have sharper boundaries. Prime Flute has an infinite, globally shorted **corridor-model** obstruction, leaving actual pant comparison open. Nyman–Beurling separates ordinary Brjuno sufficiency from 2-Brjuno necessity without settling specified integer triples. Weil Inertia retains a full Euler correction in an integrated threshold norm despite failure of a finite signed torus measure; actual first-order threshold transport remains unproved. These changes identify more precise remaining hypotheses rather than resolving the source-estimate gap.

Canonical findings outrank older Mind frontiers. [VIS-448 has an owner-actionable open review](visual_exploration/findings/VIS-448-variation-tariff-for-farey-transport-spectrum.review.md) concerning a capped intermediate majorant. The review distinguishes that disputed comparison from the useful spectral-tail estimate; its disposition remains unsettled. WI-002 is withdrawn, and [WI-456](weil_inertia/findings/WI-456-retracted-moment-transport-leaves-only-a-conditional-density-capstone.md) retains only a conditional moment-to-density theorem after retraction of the Yang–Yang transport. The [Atlas v1 metrics](graph/atlas/metrics.md) incorporate the sparse prime transfer and square-free ambiguity, but the entrypoint narrative and other frontier descriptions lag canonical evidence. Selected episode rates are derived telemetry, not a current whole-portfolio fertility measurement. Recommendations below rest on source evidence.

## Cross-line structure

**Coverage changes observation requirements, not arithmetic strength.** For lengths `L_1=H`, `L_2=floor(sqrt(2)H)`, `1<=H<=X/20`, both MC-533 and PL-478 bound complete smooth pairings through nearest-sample cell weights and the distance sum `D_i=sum_j dist(j,R_i)`. Writing `W_i(c_i)=sum_r w_(i,r)|S_(L_i)(r)-c_i|^2`, the recovery cost is

`sum_i [sqrt(X W_i(c_i)) + J_X D_i]/L_i`,

with jump price `J_X=2` for Möbius and `log(3X)` for primes. Their separate pole formulas retain respectively `X^beta (log X)^(m-1)` for a reciprocal pole of multiplicity `m`, and `X^beta` with noncancelling multiplicity weights for the prime logarithmic derivative. The test is complete and mean zero; a sample mask is not inserted into its Mellin transform.

At `H=floor(X/20)`, the grid of spacing `floor(sqrt X)` has `D_i=O(X^(3/2))` and largest cell size `O(sqrt X)`. Both ordinary sample-centered averages `Q_i/q_X <<_epsilon X^(1+epsilon)` on one unbounded integer scale set fixed before epsilon are equivalent to the source's own zero hypothesis. Each converse uses its own pointwise arithmetic estimate, not an inference from a complete mean square to a deterministic sparse sample. The [prime sparse-start clue](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-geometric-coverage-sparse-prime-variance.md) is resolved by PL-478. This does not identify the two zero sets or supply either ceiling.

**The hidden primitive is shared; the admissible controls differ.** [PL-477](prime_lattice/findings/PL-477-common-mask-paired-windows-retain-quadratic-deletion-ambiguity.md) constructs two fixed positive residue-supported sources with identical retained vectors in both windows and signed-target separation of order `B^2/X`. Its clustered gaps have distance cost of order `B^2`. MC-534 instead permits any `q<=kappa_phi X` interval queries, including deterministic adaptive queries, with exact square-free magnitudes and target separation at least `c_phi X/(q+1)`. Every query is an endpoint difference of the same prefix sum. The difference primitive vanishes at those endpoints, while a fixed derivative-phase interval makes its nonnegative area visible to the signed target. The latter controls can also be fixed across prescribed sufficiently separated scales; arbitrary interlaced scales and randomized lower bounds are not covered.

These findings refine classical [Lipschitz recovery and indistinguishable-input arguments](prior_art/incremental/PA-lipschitz-integration-recovery.md) in different source classes. They do not prove optimal arithmetic sample counts, necessity of the prime logarithm or impossibility of arithmetic recovery gains. The new [prime-supported weighted endpoint clue](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-prime-supported-weighted-endpoint-ambiguity.md) asks whether MC-534's construction survives with exact prime-power support and bounded positive relative reweighting. It distinguishes support from fixed arithmetic amplitudes and includes an exact source-reading grant. That transfer remains unvalidated.

Farey's destination still requires different information. [MC-520](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-520-rh-mellin-variance-closes-conditional-farey-window-target.md) already excludes the balanced near-capacity branch under RH using the complete **uncentered** sliding moment. Removing RH also removes its stated carrying-shell localization unless separately recovered. Centered sparse observations do not restore the missing mean and mass. Independently, [FD-343](farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-343-linear-position-fourier-tails-have-a-positive-limiting-profile.md) sharpens the position tail to `(P(c)+o(1))/Q`, `P(c)>0`, above each fixed linear cutoff. FD-342's squared rank/position transfer error is only `o(E_Q)` and `Q E_Q` diverges, so it does not transfer that absolute `1/Q` asymptotic. Neither finding settles the `sqrt(Q)` or `Q^(2/3)` bands.

**Integrated threshold correction and pointwise arithmetic transport are distinct.** [WI-463](weil_inertia/findings/WI-463-first-euler-threshold-correction-exceeds-logarithmic-bracket-bandwidth.md) and [VIS-464](visual_exploration/findings/VIS-464-smooth-zero-bias-does-not-determine-hard-threshold-correction.md) close finite-band pointwise bracket certificates for their tested observables. WI-463's first nonconstant two-prime cutoff has zero signed threshold coefficient, but the actual first-order law still requires an `o(T)` dyadic interval discrepancy at frequency `log(3/2)`. Available logarithmic bands cannot provide the required `o(1/log T)` Haar bracket gap. Positive countermodels in VIS-464 match the controlled characters while changing the scaled hard-tail correction.

[WI-464](weil_inertia/findings/WI-464-full-euler-correction-has-an-integrable-threshold-primitive.md) now shows that finite Euler-panel cumulative coefficients converge in `L^1([0,1])`, independently of exhaustion, with an explicit omitted-coefficient error. Their integral is the positive coefficient of WI-462's downward mean bias. No finite signed measure on the infinite prime torus has all the prescribed panel marginals, yet an integrable scalar primitive survives. This neither proves pointwise convergence at a selected threshold nor identifies the full coefficient with the actual zero-sampled first correction. A distributional derivative acting on Lipschitz tests need not be a finite signed response measure. Direct interval information and stronger residual control remain live; all-zero calibration remains separate from detecting an individual off-line zero.

**Algebraic assembly, infinite completion and source sign remain separate.** [NB-362](nyman_beurling/findings/NB-362-brjuno-pairs-complete-generically-but-two-brjuno-alone-does-not.md) weakens finite Diophantine type to ordinary Brjuno convergence `sum_j log(Q_(j+1))/Q_j<infinity` for almost-everywhere real third-seed completion of the projected Nyman target. NB-361's 2-Brjuno condition is necessary for any third seed; NB-362 constructs a 2-Brjuno Liouville ratio with almost-everywhere failure, without excluding exceptional completing seeds. The target lies in an algebraic sum of closed orbit spaces when completion holds; finite-generator acquisition, specified integer triples and the Burnol floor are not supplied.

[PF-359](prime_flute/findings/PF-359-infinite-corridor-transition-shorting-has-essential-angle-norm-one.md) removes a different completion gap. This is an infinite-model result, not an identification with the actual surface. The [accepted transition clue](prime_flute/clues/CLUE-positive-width-transition-schur-complement.md) already records the remaining residual-scale pant/seam comparison and completion problem; raw small corridor errors do not provide it.

[WP-455](weil_positivity/findings/WP-455-closed-diagonal-trace-energies-lock-prime-phases-but-retain-both-weil-signs.md) explicitly leaves WP-454's bounded category: a densely defined closed unbounded covariant energy can have singular joint orbit measure and lock a finite panel's prime phases. Nevertheless every nonempty unchanged finite Weil block in this full-circle diagonal completion retains positive and negative quadratic values. Thus closedness does not imply bounded measure domination, and phase locking does not imply the desired sign. [PC-461](prime_circle/findings/PC-461-admissible-triplets-force-cotangent-populations-above-the-pair-spectrum.md) likewise distinguishes a population law from a spectral edge. The admissible residue patterns `{0,2,6}` and `{0,4,6}` force order `N/(log y)^3` eigenvalues of each sign above the largest pair-law atom, at fixed thresholds between `1/(2 pi)` and `7/(12 pi)`, uniformly in refinement. This is a lower bound using classical CRT/singular-series counts, not a second-order asymptotic, a prime-tuple conjecture or source-specific zeta identification.

**Uniformity and source realization remain load-bearing.** [ANF-202](analytic_frontier/findings/ANF-202-bounded-cardinality-small-notch-minima-are-exactly-real.md) upgrades limiting exposure to exact real reduction for small positive notch amplitudes, uniformly over dominated profiles at each fixed total-cardinality cap. The amplitude interval need not be uniform in that cap, so growing-cardinality configurations remain outside the conclusion. [AF-588](arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-588-fixed-prime-tail-changes-the-sharp-range-constant-and-limit-order.md) gives a separate explicit limit-order warning: the normalized fixed-pair far-tail range coefficient tends to `exp(B_1) log 2` when range is sent to infinity first, whereas the reverse iterated limit is one. Whole-ray absolute profile convergence does not preserve the relative far tail used by the polynomial quotient; the crossover is unproved.

[RE-269](robin_extremal/findings/RE-269-total-prime-spread-controls-positive-robin-excursions-for-arbitrary-packet-size.md) extends the Robin counting-primitive control beyond pairs and triples. A deletion-only Euler-logarithm spread condition forces a nonnegative primitive for individually exact subdyadic repairs of arbitrary size; a summed positive-part deficit prices failure of that sufficient condition. This does not classify all larger integer repairs, and a real-label counterexample is not an integer escape. Composite insertions carry synthetic Euler factors, and the actual CA-selected tail remains uncontrolled. [XF-208](xi_flow/findings/XF-208-smooth-tapers-do-not-uniformize-vanishing-collision-coverage.md) retains its separate relative-coverage obstruction; the [overlap clue](xi_flow/clues/CLUE-overlap-discriminant-taper-summation-by-parts.md) still owns gap floors and time-integrated alternatives.
