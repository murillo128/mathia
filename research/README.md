## Cross-line structure

### 1. Closing a bulk mechanism is increasingly changing the mathematical currency of the endpoint

The new findings are not merely smaller error terms. `AF-322` turns singular recovery into finite-height conditioning; `FD-100` turns horizon contraction into finite source-neutral inventory plus source refresh; `RE-102` turns local boundary-depth counting into nonlocal CA-state assembly; and `PC-283` turns a candidate singular scalar symbol into a classified classical Green kernel plus a finite-section placement problem. Once those reductions are exact, strengthening the old bulk estimate attacks the wrong quantity. The surviving target has to be reidentified before more work is credited as progress.

### 2. Finite/local structure and global assembly are now visibly different resources

`AF-322` supplies a quantitative theorem for every fixed prime support but not a support-uniform or height-uniform margin. `FD-100` proves a fixed source-neutral genealogy cannot continue forever, but adaptive packet selection can switch carriers and postpone source movement. `NB-099` shows that global symmetry and zero counts do not regularize a bad local several-state configuration. `RE-102` closes every current local support-boundary depth channel while leaving the global selected-state race untouched. These are four versions of the same warning: exact local control becomes useful only after one proves the assembly law consumed by the final theorem.

### 3. Positive or absolute-value completion is increasingly identifiable as information loss, not robustness

`MC-272` shows that complexification does not help if the final estimate is still an absolute circle norm; the blind coefficient needs phase-sensitive extraction. `WP-289` is stronger at the operator level: even keeping every finite arithmetic covariance positive and adding arbitrary positive completion cannot yield a continuous positive limit while monotonically preserving the source block. `PC-283` supplies a complementary control: after the forced singular selector is subtracted, the full-domain scalar remainder is universal classical harmonic data. Across these lines, the live arithmetic information sits in signed coupling, placement, or interference before the universal positive/scalar readout.

### 4. Endpoint observables are becoming family-level rather than witness-level

Prime Flute now has a positive-measure critical packet family, and the endpoint requires almost-everywhere suppression after the full physical destination map. Visual has the opposite simplification: exact anchor support geometry diagonalizes the whole one-cancellation Gram family in a substantial cone, reducing a global spectral-spreading problem to diagonal shell energies there. Both examples argue for posing the endpoint in the exact aggregate variable consumed by the theorem—measure of surviving packet responses or total shell energy—rather than proving stronger pointwise statements that the final criterion does not require.
