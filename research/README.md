# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The common RH-facing gap remains an independently source-forced estimate in the precise norm used by the destination. Reconstruction, auxiliary positivity and existence of a zero-sensitive test do not supply that estimate. No current Mathia finding proves RH.

Two distinct recovery losses have a canonical combined repair in both arithmetic sources. [MC-532](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-532-paired-boxes-recover-trimmed-mobius-variance-at-macroscopic-lengths.md) composes two-box inversion with bounded-jump recovery of deleted Möbius starts; [PL-476](prime_lattice/findings/PL-476-trimmed-paired-prime-variance-retains-the-grh-obstruction.md) reconstructs missing-start recovery for the prime source with its logarithmic coefficient cost. Their paired macroscopic retained-energy/deletion-count criteria are equivalent to RH and joint mod-5 GRH respectively, with one unbounded integer scale set and mask/center families fixed before epsilon varies. The [reverse transfer is resolved](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-paired-prime-variance-missing-start-recovery.md). The arithmetic upper estimates remain missing; the shared inverse does not identify the two zero sets or their distinct converse estimates.

Canonical findings outrank older Mind frontiers where they differ. No adversarial sidecar is currently open; this is not independent approval of the corpus. WI-002 is withdrawn, and [WI-456](weil_inertia/findings/WI-456-retracted-moment-transport-leaves-only-a-conditional-density-capstone.md) retains only a conditional moment-to-density theorem after retraction of the Yang–Yang arithmetic transport. Even a valid density-one theorem leaves individual exceptions possible. The [Atlas v1 metrics](graph/atlas/metrics.md) incorporate MC-532 and PL-476 as refinements of the existing missing-start episode, while their entrypoint narrative and other frontier descriptions lag current sources. Their selective episode rates are not a current whole-portfolio fertility measurement; recommendations below rest on canonical evidence.

For one box, [PL-474](prime_lattice/findings/PL-474-nonquasianalytic-tests-close-the-iterated-log-window-boundary.md) and [MC-530](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-530-nonquasianalytic-tests-reach-logarithmic-mobius-windows.md) reach every integer `1<=H<=X/[log X (loglog X)^r]`, for each fixed `r>1`, on every sufficiently large real block. [MC-529](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-529-single-near-linear-centered-variance-law-is-equivalent-to-rh.md) already makes its epsilon-loss near-linear ceiling on any unbounded scale set RH-equivalent.

PL-475 changes the operator rather than the target's regularity. Its classical [compact-distribution two-channel inverse](prior_art/incremental/PA-multichannel-compact-distribution-deconvolution.md) retains bounded localized mean-zero tests after dilation. With `K=floor(sqrt(2)H)`, integer rounding costs `O(1/X)` in target sup norm and `O(log X)` against the prime coefficients. A hypothetical mod-5 zero of real part `beta>1/2` therefore forces

`V_1(X,H)/H^2 + V_1(X,K)/K^2 >= c X^(2beta-1)`

for all integer `1<=H<=X/20` on every sufficiently large real block. Both variances have their own empirical means on the same base block. Both ceilings are required for the resulting joint-GRH implication at macroscopic lengths; one favorable channel is insufficient. Rational common zeros and Liouville small divisors exclude arbitrary choices of the second kernel. This is finite-derivative continuity on smooth tests, not a bounded inverse on arbitrary L2 data.

MC-531 addresses a separate loss. For deleted integer starts `E` of cardinality `B<X/2`, retained energy `V_E(c)` around any single constant `c`, and its existing single-box window range, it proves

`sqrt(X V_E(c)) + B^2 >= C H X^beta (log X)^(m-1)`.

The exact jump bound of two recovers the complete linear pairing without cutting its mean-zero analytic test. At the near-linear window, simultaneous bounds `B <<_epsilon X^(3/4+epsilon)` and `V_E(c) <<_epsilon X^(1+epsilon)H` on any unbounded integer scale set are equivalent to RH, with the families fixed before the epsilon quantifier. Qualitative density one is insufficient. Generic bounded-coefficient tent controls make quadratic absolute-mass recovery sharp; they do not prove the optimal deletion threshold for signed Möbius tests.

[MC-532](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-532-paired-boxes-recover-trimmed-mobius-variance-at-macroscopic-lengths.md) proves the composition retained in [MI-040](mind/intuition/MI-040-paired-box-inversion-transports-trimmed-mobius-pole-tests.md). For `L_1=H`, `L_2=K=floor(sqrt(2)H)`, integer `1<=H<=X/20`, separate deleted-start counts `B_i<X/2` and retained energies `V_i` around arbitrary constants, it gives

`(sqrt(X V_1)+B_1^2)/H + (sqrt(X V_2)+B_2^2)/K >= c X^beta (log X)^(m-1)`.

At `H=floor(X/20)`, simultaneous `V_i <<_epsilon X^(1+epsilon)L_i` and `B_i <<_epsilon X^(3/4+epsilon)` on one unbounded integer scale set are equivalent to RH. The scale, mask and center families are fixed before epsilon varies; MC-520 gives the converse with empty masks. Rounding costs `O(1)` against Möbius, and complete mean-zero tests are never cut by masks. [PL-476](prime_lattice/findings/PL-476-trimmed-paired-prime-variance-retains-the-grh-obstruction.md) proves the reverse prime-source transfer: replace each `B_i^2` by `B_i^2 log(3X)` and the reciprocal signal by `c X^beta`. Its converse uses their classical prime-counting error, with empty masks and centers `L_i/4`; it does not import the Möbius converse or assert a short-window ceiling.
