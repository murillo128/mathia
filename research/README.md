# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The common RH-facing gap remains an independently source-forced estimate in the precise norm used by the destination. Reconstruction, auxiliary positivity and existence of a zero-sensitive test do not supply that estimate. No current Mathia finding proves RH.

[MC-537](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-537-small-sampled-energy-permits-quarter-power-mobius-observations.md) and [PL-482](prime_lattice/findings/PL-482-small-empirical-energy-permits-quarter-power-prime-observations.md) give quarter-power sampled-energy criteria for RH and joint mod-5 GRH respectively. Neither proves its arithmetic energy premise. [MC-538](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-538-one-fixed-multiplicative-source-defeats-subquarter-random-observations.md) removes sample adaptation from the smaller-exponent obstruction: one fixed square-free multiplicative control defeats it on predetermined lacunary scales. That signed control is not a positive prime source or Möbius itself.

Several model questions are settled more sharply without supplying their arithmetic destinations. NB-366 classifies generic third-orbit completion by the Brjuno condition; ANF-206 makes the distant-copy cusp threshold sharp, including equality; WP-458 rules out the natural conditioned-block orbit form and every nonzero closable minorant on that domain. VIS-468 identifies genuine Farey coefficients that survive the artificial reassembly but are absent from projected transport. WI-468 separates finite scalar variation at each parameter from uniform variation at the first perturbation scale.

WI-002 remains withdrawn, and [WI-456](weil_inertia/findings/WI-456-retracted-moment-transport-leaves-only-a-conditional-density-capstone.md) retains only a conditional moment-to-density theorem after retraction of the Yang–Yang arithmetic transport. No current review sidecar is open; its absence is not independent approval. The [Atlas v1 metrics](graph/atlas/metrics.md) reconcile selected Farey results without changing territory mass. Their selected episode rates and older Atlas/Mind frontiers are not a current whole-portfolio fertility measurement. Recommendations below rest on canonical evidence.

## Cross-line structure

**Observation design and arithmetic strength remain separate.** The common recovery operator does not identify the Möbius and mod-5 zero sets. MC-533/PL-478 use deterministic square-root grids; MC-536/PL-481 use cube-root stratification. [MC-537](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-537-small-sampled-energy-permits-quarter-power-mobius-observations.md) and [PL-482](prime_lattice/findings/PL-482-small-empirical-energy-permits-quarter-power-prime-observations.md) instead exploit small observed energy with `2 ceil(X^(1/4))` independent uniform draws with replacement per channel. With `V` the average squared deviation from each channel's sample mean, retaining repeated draws, pairwise differences and sampled extrema bound population mean absolute dispersion by `O(sqrt(V)+sqrt(X)(log X)^2)` for Möbius and `O(sqrt(V)+sqrt(X)(log X)^3)` for primes. The prime logarithm is the jump price `log(3X)`.

Both probability-one events control every bounded mean-zero pairing simultaneously at all sufficiently large integer scales. For lengths `floor(X/20)` and its rounded `sqrt(2)` multiple, existence of one common unbounded scale set, fixed before epsilon, with `V_i<<_epsilon X^(1+epsilon)` is equivalent to the respective zero hypothesis. The set and constants may depend on the outcome; the source precedes the draws. Prime logarithmic-derivative pole weights stay positive and carry no reciprocal-zeta multiplicity logarithm; the prime proof also retains its `O(log X)` rounding error and separate pointwise GRH converse. The [quarter-power handoff](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-small-energy-quarter-power-prime-sampling.md) is resolved.

[MC-538](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-538-one-fixed-multiplicative-source-defeats-subquarter-random-observations.md) fixes a deterministic multiplicative `f`, `|f|=mu^2`, and lacunary scales before sampling. For `0<=alpha<1/4`, `2 ceil(X^alpha)` draws per channel and `3/4<kappa<1-alpha`, both uncentered sampled energies satisfy `X^(1+epsilon)` ceilings almost surely, but a fixed smooth target is at least a constant times `X^(2kappa-1)/log^2 X`. Balanced prime-sign blocks hide from sampled endpoints; lacunarity pays the old prescribed primes' finite second-moment cost. The result permits positive prime signs, has no dense-scale conclusion and lacks Möbius's reciprocal-zeta identity. It proves no lower bound for actual Möbius, positive prime data or other designs.

[PL-480](prime_lattice/findings/PL-480-prime-supported-interval-recovery-has-sharp-inverse-query-rate.md)'s deterministic minimax rate `Theta(X/(q+1))` for positive prime-supported relative reweightings on fixed ranges `q<=X^(1-epsilon)` has different quantifiers: its controls depend on the query transcript and preserve the baseline's unknown energies.
