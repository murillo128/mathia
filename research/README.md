# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia has thirteen valid research lines. Ten are evidence-bearing and active: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain distinct pre-evidence scaffolds.

The strongest common bottleneck has sharpened from a **source-rigid global margin gate** into a **source-calibrated complete-control margin gate**. A candidate carrier is useful only if its norm budget, category, scale, and normalization are justified by the source; its residual then has to survive the complete admissible family of finite, boundary, matched-control, multiplicity, category, and realizability tests; finally, a positive quantitative margin must remain in the topology and aggregation law consumed by the destination theorem. The new evidence repeatedly shows that improving the representation without closing one of these interfaces merely moves the obstruction.

`analytic_frontier` now makes the control-completeness issue exact. `ANF-018` identifies the finite-real floor `q_real` with a classical finite-particle stability constant, so strict scalar improvement below Montgomery--Taylor is exactly a global stability problem over all finite configurations rather than a thermodynamic lattice calculation. `ANF-019` shows why the free-density limit is insufficient: dilute copies of a finite bound cluster can preserve the binding gain. `ANF-020` then dualizes the same ceiling to diffraction/autocorrelation realizability. The sine/free-fermion projection determinantal process reaches the natural support-one cone but cannot supply the additional contraction needed for a strict scalar Montgomery--Taylor improvement. Any scalar survivor must therefore come from a genuinely different realizability class or separation argument, while ordered/configuration-level carriers remain a distinct route.

`arithmetic_fidelity` supplies the abstract calibration theorem. `AF-098` places uniformly bounded finite interpolation between bidual and original-target recovery; `AF-099` shows that finite matrix coefficients become complete polar witnesses once the interpolation budget is declared; and `AF-100` proves that bidual-optimal finite lifts are weak-star accessible from the original range at essentially the same norm. The missing information is therefore not finite exactness or another finite observable. It is **uniformly conditioned global assembly in the original category**, together with naturality strong enough to prevent the chosen coefficient space or norm budget from trivializing the defect.

Two further developments sharpen rather than weaken this gate. In `weil_inertia`, `WI-134`--`WI-136` replace `lambda_min` and raw rank by depth-weighted spectral mass after multiplicity slack: a macroscopic off-line population must force a macroscopic near-null/low-energy sector, and simple-real directions cannot freely screen it once the Lamzouri decomposition is recombined. In `xi_flow`, the adversarial challenge to `XF-014` has been resolved: the surviving finding now proves absolute convergence of the differenced gap equation by telescoping rather than by an unavailable uniform remote-gap bound. `XF-017` then finds a genuine escape from the fixed-ratio cutoff obstruction of `XF-016`: logarithmic capacitary tapers over a diverging outer/inner buffer make the pure Cauchy leakage `o(1)`. What remains is not fractional criticality itself but Xi-specific control of amplitudes, conductances, or signed exterior flux across that growing buffer.

The negative controls also continue to become more complete. `PC-161` shows that the one-hole puncture defect has a prime-blind trace-class star limit, and `PC-162` closes its most canonical nonlocal repair: harmonic/Kron elimination produces an infinite-rank star-mesh correction whose Fredholm determinant is still fixed-base cyclotomic data and has the same limit along prime and composite coprime refinements. `VIS-017`--`VIS-019` similarly close overlap-phase freedom, contour winding, and raw adjacent-two-gap geometry as independent arithmetic channels; the proposed three-gap conditional-residual clue is now the correct visual test because its null model preserves the adjacent-pair marginals that explain the lower-order geometry.

The portfolio-level target is therefore stricter than “find a richer representation.” A serious advance should produce a **source-forced residual with a calibrated quantitative margin that survives the complete admissible control class and already transfers at the strength required by the target theorem**.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Completeness of the control family matters as much as richness of the carrier.** `ANF-018`--`ANF-020` require all finite configurations or the full realizability body rather than a periodic thermodynamic slice; `AF-098`--`AF-100` require a uniform budget and global assembly rather than finite exact samples; `VIS-019` requires a null preserving adjacent-pair marginals before a three-gap residual counts. A survivor against a convenient subfamily is not yet arithmetic information.

2. **Calibration must come from the source.** Arithmetic Fidelity shows that interpolation cost is meaningful only after a norm budget and category are fixed; Möbius shows that a stronger metric can detect the hidden slab while its transfer theorem still overpays or relocates the zero-free-region hypothesis; Weil Positivity shows that an externally tuned singular cutoff can manufacture the missing logarithm without explaining it. The scale, norm, and topology cannot be chosen retrospectively to make a witness work.

3. **The correct quantitative object is often a mass or distribution, not an extremal mode.** `WI-133`--`WI-136` replace `lambda_min` by depth-weighted low-energy spectral mass after slack removal. `ANF-019` shows finite bound clusters can survive dilution and therefore must be controlled globally. Xi Flow similarly needs bulk dissipation minus the whole exterior budget, not positivity of the local generator alone.

4. **Nonlocality by itself is not a carrier change.** `PC-162` is an infinite-rank all-to-all Kron correction, yet its complete limiting determinant is still prime-blind fixed-base cyclotomic data. `VIS-017` glues overlapping local phases globally without creating new arithmetic information. A nonlocal construction only earns weight when the new relation preserves source provenance that its matched controls cannot reproduce.

5. **Boundary obstructions should be classified before being treated as fundamental.** Analytic Frontier learned that finite boundary detuning can destroy a thermodynamic survivor; Xi Flow now shows the converse phenomenon, where a fixed-ratio boundary obstruction disappears under a diverging logarithmic-capacity buffer. The reusable lesson is to identify which scale ratio and exterior information the destination theorem actually controls, then demand a quantitative margin in that exact boundary model.
