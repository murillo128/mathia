## Global picture

The common RH-facing gap remains an independently source-forced estimate in the precise norm used by the destination. Reconstruction, auxiliary positivity and existence of a zero-sensitive test do not supply that estimate. No current Mathia finding proves RH.

Existence, access cost and source admissibility remain separate gates. NB-371 and NB-372 require both divergent coefficients and divergent effective dilation range to approximate the projected Nyman target; neither supplies a matching construction or synthesis rate. PL-487 removes the quotient-decay obligation once a sufficiently regular fixed-box interpolant exists, leaving that existence question open. WI-473 rules out the actual first-correction formula for all continuous tests, while preserving WI-472's specified Gevrey class and the unresolved threshold problem. These are different norm and quantifier boundaries, not one shared impossibility theorem.

WI-002 remains withdrawn, and [WI-456](weil_inertia/findings/WI-456-retracted-moment-transport-leaves-only-a-conditional-density-capstone.md) retains only a conditional moment-to-density theorem after retraction of the Yang–Yang arithmetic transport. The [Atlas v1 metrics](graph/atlas/metrics.md) and [frontier telemetry](graph/atlas/telemetry/frontier-summary.md) now distinguish NB-372's effective-range barrier from NB-371's coefficient barrier and PL-487's conditional division result from interpolant existence. Their retained episodes are selective and the Atlas entrypoint has an older source window; this is not a current whole-portfolio saturation assessment. No allocation conclusion here relies on those rates. Recommendations here use canonical findings, which also outrank older local Mind frontiers.
