[RE-259](robin_extremal/findings/RE-259-zero-density-removes-critical-robin-moment-boundary.md) separately removes Robin's first-annulus gate by classical zero-density input; its remote CA-selected mixed tail remains open. None of these different source estimates may be imported without its quantifiers and hypotheses.

The current canonical findings outrank older Mind frontiers where they differ. No adversarial sidecar is currently open; this is not evidence that every finding has completed independent review. RE-259 and NB-349 explicitly retain that limitation. WI-006 now records the retraction of the Yang–Yang higher-even-moment and density-one claims: those claims supply no arithmetic input, while WI-006's independent replacement identity survives. The [Atlas v1 metrics](graph/atlas/metrics.md) incorporate the conditional variance resolution as selected derived telemetry; their unchanged coverage model and older entrypoint narrative are not a complete current frontier audit or evidence of RH progress.

## Cross-line structure

**A proof-operation barrier need not survive a different classical input.** MC-520 bounds `J_mu(X,N)` by `X^(1+epsilon)N` under RH. FD-311's balanced premise forces `J_mu>=XN^(1/2+3c-o(1))`, where `c=1-log_2(3/2)`, on shells with `X<=N^(2+o(1))`. Any fixed `2epsilon<3c-1/2` gives a strict contradiction on the same complete moment. FD-312's separate-endpoint barrier survives because it discards the difference multiplier that MC-520 retains. The [resolved Möbius handoff](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-farey-selected-sliding-mertens-variance.md) and Farey MI-066 record this classical conditional outcome, not a new RH mechanism. Removing RH also removes its shell-localization guarantee unless separately recovered. RE-259 is another successful change of input, but uses a different prime statistic and supplies no Möbius transfer.

**A convenient source estimate can already contain zero exclusion.** PL-461's raw identity-residue ceiling at `theta=1/2` entails joint GRH modulo 5; PL-463's all-large-real-scale empirical variance at the same exponent excludes only `Re s>3/4`. PL-464 now obtains the latter conclusion from every sufficiently large dyadic scale alone. Its general boundary is `max(1-theta/2,theta)` for fixed `0<theta<1`, with best value `2/3`; its endpoint cost prevents recovering GRH by taking `theta` to one. Full within-block variance and real arithmetic pole residues remain essential, and an arbitrary subsequence is not covered. These necessity results are different from MC-520's RH-sufficient bound and do not equate the prime-residue and Möbius norms.

**Continuum distinguishability and affordable sampling remain separate.** XF-200 resolves the [smooth-baseline Jacobi clue](xi_flow/clues/CLUE-bounded-smooth-baseline-jacobi-support-margin.md): a fixed positive derivative budget eventually aliases remote supports on the whole allowed interval, whereas a sufficiently small `Q^(-1/2)` budget retains linear row cost. AF-573 identifies a different near-threshold effect: its informative interval shrinks with derivative slack and its continuum margin is quadratic, so a fixed finite design can alias strictly below the full-curve threshold. The [vanishing-slack Jacobi clue](xi_flow/clues/CLUE-vanishing-calibration-slack-jacobi-row-cost.md) tests the corresponding unresolved sampling cost. AF's real supremum-norm formula does not transfer to complex Jacobi curves under total Euclidean noise.

**Exact signatures and normalized spectra have different stability.** FD-331's exact joint signature determines an excursion permutation, but VIS-461 now proves that FD-333's six-rank one-quantum pair retains distinct normalized spectral laws: mode 6 has limiting probability difference `2716/(36081 pi^2)>0`. The [spectral handoff](visual_exploration/clues/CLUE-one-quantum-farey-normalized-spectral-separation.md) is resolved for its specified `v=3` test, including full-discrepancy transfer, without a finite-prime rate. This is an artificial-control distinction, not a genuine Farey estimate. Independently, FD-336 shows that block shuffles with `b->infinity`, `b=o(sqrt(log Q))`, preserve density-one labeled adjacent occurrences yet retain the normalized Brownian null. Exact complete local inventories are not preserved. Its [owning Farey clue](farey_discrepancy/clues/CLUE-farey-gap-order-bridge-suppression.md) retains the stronger-order-control question; visual separation alone does not answer it.

**Changing the operator can evade a scoped obstruction without supplying arithmetic selection.** WP-440's unbounded additive diagonal leaves the ray-compact class of WP-439 and gives closed semibounded forms with compact resolvent, but its sufficient positivity is insensitive to arbitrary edge phases. PC-446 instead retains the exact cylinder action, so changing the measure cannot remove its eigenvalue bands. These statements concern different operators and allowed changes. PF-346 still requires comparison at the shorted diagonal-energy scale, and NB-349 now controls whole late spans rather than individual small columns; its vanishing result is for a fixed off-critical packet, with no uniform critical-line limit. Domain, normalization, target occupation and arithmetic provenance remain separate gates, not reasons to merge the lines.
