# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The program's objective is to resolve the Riemann hypothesis. Proportion improvements, local certificates, numerical patterns, and general structural results are welcome as tools for learning and testing possible mechanisms. They are not substitute objectives: the strategic question is what they enable toward settling RH, including how a proposed route handles every possible off-critical zero rather than only its asymptotic density.

Mathia has thirteen current research lines. Ten are evidence-bearing: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain distinct pre-evidence scaffolds.

The strongest common bottleneck is now a **source-canonical selector-preserving aggregation and assembly gate**. The previous synthesis correctly required a selector before classicalization, but the newest evidence shows that this is still not enough: the metric, pooling rule, positive scalarization, omission pattern, local-to-global gluing, and singular-boundary transport that carry the selector can themselves manufacture a residual, erase exact arithmetic support, or leave the construction target-equivalent. A viable route must therefore derive not only the selector but also the carrying geometry and assembly operation from the source, and must prove that the selector's quantitative margin survives them.

`AF-137`--`AF-138` make the metric part exact. Compact symmetry alone leaves a positive commutant cone and cannot canonically rank a nontrivial Gram spectrum; once an independently source-supplied positive metric `M` is present, the correct intrinsic object is the Hermitian-definite pencil `(G,M)`, whose generalized spectrum and target-relative Picard tail are invariant under generator reparameterization. The repair is therefore known, but its burden is explicit: **the source must independently determine the metric and its normalization**. `VIS-044` supplies the corresponding aggregation warning: arithmetic pooling of individually first-order Markov panel laws can create a non-Markov residual equal to a covariance term. Apparent higher-order structure can therefore be manufactured by heterogeneity unless pooling direction and matched controls are frozen as part of the representation contract.

`WP-162`, `MC-087`, and `PF-171` expose the same issue in three very different architectures. The cyclotomic inward radial flux has total mass exactly `Lambda(n)` and is pointwise positive precisely on prime-power shells, yet any shellwise positive scalarization makes every shell nonzero and destroys the Mangoldt selector; positivity must come only after the signed cross-shell/finite-archimedean assembly. Sparse annular omission in the Möbius line remains target-equivalent throughout the RH-relevant omission regime, so merely deleting a small exceptional set does not weaken the logical burden. In Prime Flute, every Margulis-short central first resolvent block is already in `S_r` for every `r>1`, including their direct sum, leaving the uncut body/collar/interface assembly as the only route-specific obstruction. Local success is therefore not evidence for the global theorem unless the source-forced gluing is controlled.

`WI-166`--`WI-168` and `PL-174`--`PL-175` further narrow the space of cheap repairs. Arbitrary positive diagonal covers do not remove the Lamzouri four-point negative direction, and same-deficit source-quantized flag refinements have rank at most linear in their deficit; bounded or sublinear flag funding cannot absorb a many-negative-direction confluence. Prime Lattice meanwhile closes all fixed-harmonic zero phases and the low-order fixed-shift Liouville/Walsh channels through quadratic mixed moments: normalized fixed-frequency probes wash out, while complete low-order recombination collapses to boundary or summatory-square information. The surviving constructions must therefore obtain genuinely source-funded rank, growing complexity, mixed-prime/additive coupling, or another nonseparable carrier before scalarization.

`XF-048`--`XF-051` are the strongest positive example of what the new gate asks for. `XF-048` gives a genuine endpoint selector by separating the shrinking Xi memory frequency from the first prime-power Fourier line. `XF-049`--`XF-050` show that finite positive-frequency transport is Volterra-triangular even through complex roots and collisions. `XF-051` now removes the qualitative infinite-volume renormalization barrier: the horizontal logarithmic derivative on any fixed zero-free line gives a canonical half-line tempered distribution whose exact Burgers/Fourier evolution is one-sided Volterra and agrees with the finite carrier away from frequency zero. The remaining Xi obstruction is sharply quantitative rather than structural: as the memory band shrinks to `xi=0`, one must subtract the deterministic archimedean background and prove that low-to-low Volterra transfer and the endpoint singularity contribute only below the selector margin.

Analytic Frontier supplies a complementary finite-dimensional tightening. `ANF-057` improves the support-free unequal-height exclusion for the accepted Montgomery--Taylor profile to relative mismatch `q>0.1409` for any surviving zero/negative witness, while the exact hyperbolic condition simultaneously constrains it relative to horizontal separation. The remaining five-point decision problem is therefore an intermediate compact mismatch band, not a neighborhood of the equal-height diagonal. This remains a genuine `continue` line because the residual region is explicit and theorem-level rather than an open-ended search.

The program-level discriminator is consequently: **fix the source representation, metric, topology, pooling and completion rules; quotient the strongest matched controls; identify a selector before classicalization; preserve its sign/support through source-canonical aggregation and global assembly; and prove that its margin stays above all transport, boundary, and singular-background errors in the exact object consumed by the target theorem.**

## Cross-line structure

1. **A selector can be lost after it has already been found.** `WP-162` is the cleanest example: signed radial flux selects prime powers exactly, but local positive scalarization destroys that support. `AF-137` and `VIS-044` show the same danger one level earlier, where metric or pooling choices can respectively erase intrinsic spectral meaning or manufacture a residual. Selector discovery and selector-preserving assembly are separate proof obligations.

2. **Source-canonical geometry is now a constructive requirement, not only a no-go principle.** `AF-138` shows exactly how a source metric repairs the full generator gauge: use the generalized pencil `(G,M)` and transport `M` with coordinates. This supplies a reusable template for other lines: when a gauge or aggregation choice is unavoidable, the source must furnish the extra datum that makes the transported quantity intrinsic.

3. **Local simplification repeatedly pushes the hard part into global assembly.** `PF-171` makes every short central collar block benign for `r>1`; `MC-087` shows sparse deletion leaves the global Mertens burden; `WI-166`--`WI-168` show regular positive or low-rank repairs cannot absorb the confluence direction. The unresolved information lives in the coupling that remains after the easy local pieces have been exhausted.

4. **Xi has crossed from qualitative transport to a singular quantitative boundary problem.** `XF-051` canonically defines the infinite positive-frequency carrier and proves exact one-sided Volterra evolution without assuming root reality or simplicity. The only surviving transport threat is input from lower positive frequencies and the zero-frequency/archimedean background as the selector band collapses toward `0`.

5. **Fixed-complexity classical probes continue to saturate.** `PL-174`--`PL-175` remove fixed harmonics and low-order fixed-shift Walsh statistics, while `ANF-057` shows how exact retained structure can still shrink a finite danger set when the source profile is kept intact. The common lesson is to spend complexity only where the source forces a relation that survives complete matched controls.
