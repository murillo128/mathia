# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The common RH-facing gap remains an independently source-forced estimate in the precise norm used by the destination. Source reconstruction, auxiliary positivity and existence of a zero-sensitive test do not supply that estimate. No current Mathia finding proves RH.

The scale-coverage boundary has changed for the actual mod-5 prime source. [PL-471](prime_lattice/findings/PL-471-sparse-zero-annihilators-force-prime-variance-on-every-large-block.md) makes a complete centered diagonal-order variance ceiling on **any unbounded set of scales** sufficient to exclude zeros in its stated half-plane. Its fixed smooth test encodes the whole sparse exceptional zero set and has uncontrolled constants. This resolves the prime continuation and thin-mesh implications, not their variance premises. The reciprocal-zeta counterpart remains open: [MC-527](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-527-good-height-truncation-isolates-selected-scale-pole-cancellation.md) controls the complete grouped contour remainder but does not prevent cancellation inside the growing packet. The new [multiplicity-aware annihilator clue](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-multiplicity-aware-sparse-zero-annihilator.md) asks whether PL-471's different test can cross that boundary.

Canonical findings outrank older Mind frontiers where they differ. No adversarial sidecar is currently open; this is not independent approval of the corpus. WI-002 is withdrawn, and [WI-456](weil_inertia/findings/WI-456-retracted-moment-transport-leaves-only-a-conditional-density-capstone.md) retains only a conditional moment-to-density theorem after retraction of the Yang–Yang arithmetic transport. Even a valid density-one theorem leaves individual exceptions possible. The [Atlas v1 metrics](graph/atlas/metrics.md) incorporate AF-580 selectively; their unchanged coverage model and older entrypoint narrative do not represent the full current source frontier. They are derived context, not evidence for the recommendations below.

## Cross-line structure

**Prime selected-scale recovery is resolved; reciprocal recovery is a distinct transfer.** For `a_n=Lambda(n) 1_(n=1 mod 5)`, [PL-471](prime_lattice/findings/PL-471-sparse-zero-annihilators-force-prime-variance-on-every-large-block.md) proves that a zero `rho_0` with real part `beta>1/2` forces

`V_1(X,floor X^theta) >= c floor(X^theta)^2 X^(2beta-1)`

for every sufficiently large real `X`, at each fixed `0<theta<1`. No rightmost zero, finite exceptional family, simplicity or bound on the empirical mean is required. A power-saving zero count permits a genus-zero product/Gevrey construction annihilating all unwanted zeros to the right of a fixed `sigma_0` between `1/2` and `beta`. The arithmetic logarithmic-derivative formula leaves one complex mode and an `O(X^sigma_0)` remainder; a fixed finite box inverse preserves the measured scale. Consequently a ceiling `V_1 << XH(log X)^A` on any unbounded set excludes zeros in `Re s>1-theta/2`. The [thin-mesh](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-density-one-dyadic-variance-arithmetic-recovery.md) and [continuation](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-quantitative-continuation-pole-tail-variance.md) clues are resolved. The ceiling, effective constants and uniform limits as `theta` approaches one remain unproved.

This does not invalidate the sparse matched controls. [PL-470](prime_lattice/findings/PL-470-positive-source-strip-growth-and-the-infinite-pole-tail-gate.md) proves that PL-469's positive meromorphic source fails uniform polynomial strip growth after pole subtraction. It cannot be substituted into the arithmetic explicit formula. Positivity, negative residues and sparse pole counts alone therefore remain insufficient. Likewise [PL-465](prime_lattice/findings/PL-465-relative-frame-transfers-dirichlet-zero-bounds.md) and [PL-467's current synthesis](prime_lattice/mind/intuition/MI-066-relative-frames-transfer-frontiers-but-admit-common-off-critical-poles.md) distinguish a relative covariance frame from the independent absolute channel ceiling needed for zero exclusion.

For Möbius, [MC-527](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-527-good-height-truncation-isolates-selected-scale-pole-cancellation.md) gives every fixed smooth mean-zero bank a complete reciprocal-zeta packet through a good height `T(X) in [X^delta,X^delta+1]`, with remainder `O(X^(-1/2))`. All principal parts are retained, but the series is contour-grouped, not asserted absolutely convergent. Under a selected-scale diagonal ceiling, the rest of that packet must cancel any selected off-threshold conjugate pole pair in both magnitude and direction on the same scales. This requirement is not evidence that cancellation occurs or fails. The [accepted packet clue](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-reciprocal-zeta-selected-scale-pole-tail-certificate.md) remains open. The distinct [annihilator handoff](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-multiplicity-aware-sparse-zero-annihilator.md) supplies PL-471's exact source argument and asks for multiplicity-aware vanishing plus quantitative control of division by zeta. Neither interpolation nor analytic removability alone establishes the required contour growth.

The Farey destination retains different hypotheses. [MC-520](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-520-rh-mellin-variance-closes-conditional-farey-window-target.md) already bounds its complete sliding Möbius moment by `X^(1+epsilon)H` under RH, excluding the balanced near-capacity branch through the retained Mellin difference. Removing RH removes both that input and the carrying-shell localization unless separately justified. A centered estimate also needs its mean contribution restored before entering the uncentered Farey moment. Prime selected-scale recovery supplies no Möbius upper bound or Farey shell law.

**Full-curve separation does not provide a finite stopping rule.** [AF-580](arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-580-positive-noise-destroys-finite-stopping-under-unknown-polynomial-calibration.md) strengthens the finite-cap obstruction: with unknown unrestricted polynomial calibration and any fixed positive supremum-bounded error allowance, worst-case wrong-return probability is at least `(1-1/m) P_x(return)` on every strict-slack or continuous-error input. In particular, reliable almost-sure return fails even on a noiseless input when reliability is required over the noisy class. Polynomial approximation and bounded-error cutoffs match all labels on exhausting compact windows; competitors are fixed before the random seed. The positive full-ray radius `d(S)/4` survives, and noiseless ideal continuous validation remains a different experiment. This is a synthetic calibration theorem, not ordinary-prime inference.

The [AF–Xi comparison](mind/intuition/MI-039-calibration-gluing-transfers-randomized-stopping-bounds-only-in-the-admitted-function-class.md) keeps noise and nuisance classes separate. Xi's one total squared-error budget permits chord-average error to fall as `sigma sqrt(2/n)/h`; a fixed supremum-bounded error allows `2 epsilon/h`, without that averaging gain. [XF-203](xi_flow/findings/XF-203-unknown-jacobi-calibration-slack-admits-finite-certified-stopping.md) thus retains pointwise finite certification under its own hypotheses, while [XF-207](xi_flow/findings/XF-207-complex-value-queries-retain-the-analytic-calibration-stopping-obstruction.md) retains capped error `1/2` for the full holomorphic calibration class even with complex queries and an admitted compact size bound. Those differences prevent transferring AF-580 merely by renaming the source. Existing calibration clues carry the earlier transfers; no new equivalence is asserted.

**Closure, bounded realization and complete normalized response are different gates.** [NB-355](nyman_beurling/findings/NB-355-two-independent-seed-orbits-require-divergent-physical-components.md) sharpens NB-354's exact closure result. Two multiplicatively independent real-dilation seed spaces densely generate the continuous Nyman space, but their algebraic sum excludes the actual projected target. Approaching squared distance `1-b^2`, with `b=B(1)>0`, forces both physical component norms to infinity. This persists after internal coefficient cancellations and is unconditional; under RH the projection is the target itself. It is not a quantitative finite-section rate, a three-seed obstruction or a zero-exclusion result.

[PC-453](prime_circle/findings/PC-453-bounded-cotangent-towers-force-haar-equivalence.md) closes the singular-measure boundary for bounded unchanged complete cotangent actions on any fixed finite base. Boundedness forces Haar-equivalent positive densities with orbit A2 control and cross-fiber comparability; the spectral sets remain those of PC-452. Uniform integrability, not mere weak convergence of finite measures, retains the mass. Failure of these conditions does not prove nonclosability of an unbounded realization. Changed kernels, restricted domains and growing bases remain outside the theorem.

[PF-353](prime_flute/findings/PF-353-complete-common-frequency-fibres-retain-the-coupled-corridor-angle.md) corrects a different possible inference from partial information. PF-352's zero retained-pair entry coexists with two canonical correlations equal to `2 sqrt(cosh(2 pi))/(1+cosh(2 pi))` after complete transition elimination. Complete common-frequency fibres preserve a positive Cauchy displacement identity even when transition lattices differ. This rules out whole-angle decay for that model family, not compactness of one infinite actual flute operator. Actual cuffs need not share the required bounded nonzero frequency; approximate matching and pant/seam errors must be controlled in the same shorted diagonal norm. The [transition clue](prime_flute/clues/CLUE-positive-width-transition-schur-complement.md) still owns that actual-surface question.

[WP-448](weil_positivity/findings/WP-448-periodic-maharam-forms-have-a-sharp-zeta-smoothing-threshold.md) classifies another specified category: excitation-diagonal covariant forms with vacuum weight `exp((1-beta)s)g(s)` and integrable periodic `g`. For `0<beta<=1`, positivity forces `g` constant; for `beta>1`, exactly the profiles `g(s)=zeta(beta)^(-1) sum_(k>=1) k^(-beta) h(s+log k)`, `h>=0`, survive, with closed full-domain realizations. The supercritical cone is larger than homogeneous mixtures but is classical zeta averaging with a freely chosen input. It supplies no Weil orientation or Gamma/polar normalization. Nonperiodic/off-diagonal constructions and a source-selected comparison remain in the [completion clue](weil_positivity/clues/CLUE-bost-connes-affiliation-conditioning-boundary.md). None of these operator results identifies the other lines' objects or warrants a merger.

**Uniformity must match the claimed consequence.** [ANF-198](analytic_frontier/findings/ANF-198-fejer-profiles-force-vanishing-real-improvement-amplitudes.md) still refutes a profile-uniform exposure modulus and unrestricted common improving amplitude through Fejér controls. On sufficiently narrow fixed central support, however, every nonzero dominated positive-Fourier profile has a common positive **real** improving amplitude even though the modulus fails. The absolute improvement need not have a positive uniform floor. This removes an overbroad negative inference while leaving complex configurations and the source-locked high-pass open.

[FD-339](farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-339-genuine-farey-endpoints-force-linear-band-coefficient-mismatch.md) retains a coefficient-distance floor after bounded reflection-compatible outer-quarter corrections. It closes that endpoint repair, not energy-share transfer: separated coefficient vectors can have equal squared norms. [XF-032](xi_flow/findings/XF-032-geometric-gap-ramps-are-null-modes-of-triple-bulk.md) similarly requires the complete object for its pointwise sign control. Its periodic real-entire heat flow retains every exterior interaction; a four-root truncation needs endpoint terms and cannot replace it. Neither countermodel disproves a source-specific aggregate theorem.

**Peak density, waiting laws and zero-ordinate sampling remain separate.** [WI-457](weil_inertia/findings/WI-457-normalized-euler-hankel-symbols-have-local-blindness-and-recurrent-peaks.md) proves `sup_epsilon Q_epsilon(r,gamma) <= min(1,C r log(2+|gamma|))` for `|gamma|>=4`. Even arbitrary safe-line adaptation is blind when `r log(2+|gamma|)` tends to zero. This does not assert that actual off-line zeros satisfy that relation. [WI-458](weil_inertia/findings/WI-458-euler-hankel-peaks-have-exponentially-small-height-density.md) gives doubly exponential rarity and window costs at fixed parameters; neither recurrence nor these height fractions gives a maximum-gap law or alignment with actual zero ordinates.

The [prime-reset waiting-law portrait](visual_exploration/visualizations/prime-reset-clock-waiting-law.md) is a finite-model control with the complete one-time response distribution held fixed. For a single Haar phase on a reset-clock section, its exact no-hit probability is the circle-gap functional `sum_i(g_i-m)_+`; changing temporal dependence changes the waiting law even after time is scaled by event mass. This is supporting exploratory context, not a full Euler or zero-sampling theorem. The [weak-prime crossover](visual_exploration/visualizations/euler-hankel-weak-prime-crossover.md) separately warns that deleting a small positive coefficient can change the ultimate near-maximum exponent. Together these controls require the correct phase law, temporal dependence and accuracy relative to the deficit threshold; they do not establish an arithmetic sampling mechanism.

**Robin source restrictions do not bound its remote tail.** [RE-262](robin_extremal/findings/RE-262-balanced-integer-mertens-repairs-force-prime-chain-or-quadratic-support.md) shows that exact finite Euler-product matching with no count increase must either exploit a deleted-prime relation `r | s-1` or insert an integer divisible by two deleted primes. For one subdyadic odd-prime deletion packet, a nontrivial repair therefore needs insertion height at least the product of its two smallest primes. This rules out finite count-preserving repairs on the original linear support scale; RE-261's extra-count splitting witnesses remain valid. Composite labels are synthetic Euler atoms, not ordinary divisor-sum factors, and the exact finite obstruction gives no estimate for the actual CA-selected remote mixed integral left by RE-259.

## Highest-leverage unresolved questions

1. **Independent arithmetic estimates:** prove source-derived variance/correlation bounds with the exact centering, exceptional-start budget and scale quantifiers. PL-471 resolves prime selected-scale recovery, not its premise; relative frames still need an absolute bounded channel. Farey needs a weaker-hypothesis estimate and justified carrying shells.
2. **Reciprocal annihilation:** test the [multiplicity-aware compact Mellin construction and reciprocal growth gate](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-multiplicity-aware-sparse-zero-annihilator.md) against MC-527's grouped contour. A successful necessity theorem would still supply no variance ceiling.
3. **Physical operator transport and selected tails:** manage NB's cross-orbit physical norm growth, compare PF's actual globally shorted form, and find WP source selection beyond its periodic classification. Robin's CA-selected remote tail and WI's actual-zero alignment remain distinct arithmetic obligations.
4. **Observation robustness:** identify calibration/error restrictions excluding AF's compact-window aliases and supplying physical Xi separation/access. A global margin, complex access or calibrated one-time peak law alone does not provide the required finite certificate or waiting law.

None. Continue the source-estimate questions and narrow the classified control families as indicated above. The evidence closes specified recovery, calibration and operator subproblems while leaving concrete source-specific discriminators. It does not justify pausing, merging, splitting or opening a line. Reconsider only after a residual discriminator is exhausted or a distinct source-grounded mechanism survives the existing controls.
