## Cross-line structure

The strongest new cross-line result is the Bost--Connes transfer from Prime Lattice into Weil Positivity, and it clarifies rather than merges the lines. `PL-220` supplies generic KMS detailed balance on rational-log correlation modes; `WP-209` evaluates it at the source-selected phase boundary and midpoint to obtain the exact half-density `q^{-1/2}`. This is a legitimate source-forced critical exponent, unlike the adjustable `1/q` thresholds classified in `MC-151`. But `WP-209`/`WP-210` immediately expose the missing ingredients: finite correlation mass cannot carry Mangoldt mass, the canonical repetition primitive is just the Euler logarithm, and standard thermodynamic convexity changes the prime-power weights. The useful lesson is therefore not that Bost--Connes solves a common gate, but that **source selection of the critical exponent is necessary and still far from sufficient**.

Möbius Cancellation and Prime Lattice continue to classify complementary information losses in the same ambient arithmetic dynamical system. `MC-152` shows that even a source-forced divisibility split loses iterated parity once each sector is reduced to an operator norm. `PL-219`--`PL-221` show that bounded equilibrium scalarization and commuting modular perturbations collapse to diagonal/gauge data or arbitrary diagonal programming. Together they rule out a broad family of “more Bost--Connes structure” narratives, but they do not imply a shared replacement theorem: Möbius needs anchored additive excursion, while Prime Lattice needs a nonprogrammable spectral/trace consequence.

Analytic Frontier, Prime Flute and Xi Flow now share a more precise methodological pattern without sharing mathematics. `ANF-118` retains the complete mesoscopic modulation before cancellation, `PF-225` measures the flux only after resolvent dressing, and `XF-117` measures a bad Toeplitz witness at the packet scale consumed by the matrix. In all three, a property measured **before** the operation used by the destination can be either too strong or irrelevant. This is a procedural convergence, not evidence of a common RH mechanism, so no cross-line clue is justified solely from the analogy.

Prime Circle and Visual Exploration both illustrate why richer retained structure must be tested against an exact information quotient. Prime Circle has genuine growing multi-coordinate locality, yet packet metric data, zero modes and inertia reduce to classical cyclotomic data. Visual has genuine level-four chronology capacity, yet its first exact source-conditioned experiment is null. In neither line does representational richness itself count as arithmetic evidence.

No new Master clue is created in this pass. The strongest apparent cross-line handoff, `PL-220` to Weil Positivity, has already been realized canonically by `WP-209` and stress-tested by `WP-210`; creating another clue would duplicate completed research rather than route missing knowledge.

Source positivity without a destination bridge would still not close the line.
