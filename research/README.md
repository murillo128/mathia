## Cross-line structure

**Few almost-integral defects do not control global spectral transport.** FD-319 and FD-322 both preserve every directed Farey edge and have exceptional indices tending to the *positive* integer one, yet retain macroscopic energy in fixed modes; FD-322 reduces to three breaks only by **abandoning reflection**. FD-321 is a weaker-positivity unreflected comparator, not the best three-break benchmark. FD-320 identifies cumulative excursion transport instead of break count and supplies *absolute* full-spectrum bounds. VIS-032/VIS-034's even-mode `Q_q=A S_q` is reflection-specific, so [[research/visual_exploration/clues/CLUE-farey-six-stitch-fixed-mode-null]] must compare reflection-preserving and unreflected controls separately, include odd modes in the latter, and recompute each null's normalization. Absolute spectral error cannot be divided by vanishing total energy without a separate relative bound. None proves a genuine-Farey ordering estimate.

**Low-order statistical equality is not equality of a hitting law.** VIS-441–VIS-443 show that equal mass/crossing flux, full two-point autocorrelation and even the entire triple correlation do not determine avoidance. VIS-444 proves the full occupation-moment hierarchy does recover Haar zero-occupation and exhibits an exact integrated order-four distinction. MC-514 supplies a *different* deterministic rectangle/cycle dependence after pairwise residue agreement. These are compatible warnings about downstream joint information, not an established equivalence between Wang and Möbius models or an arithmetic selector.

**Every apparent improvement must survive its destination topology.** AF-558's signed endpoint integral is not generally controlled sharply by absolute variation, and AF-559's exact edit distinction costs shrinking observation precision. PC-431's smooth-vector/Gram limit is not projection-norm convergence; XF-187's normalized frame is not stable in physical displacement coordinates. PL-446's infinite vertical mean erases off-diagonal arithmetic coherence and creates universal positivity. PF-345's raw Schur trace does not establish a normalized angle, while WP-435's compact Schatten regimes do not alter essential spectral sign.

**Population and source-side control cannot replace individual physical selection.** ANF-190 permits exceptional physical twists despite average control; WI-442/WI-443/WI-444 can count many safe zeros without excluding isolated off-line ones; NB-342 detects a source direction, not the Ford target's occupation. All remain below an RH-complete individual-defect estimate.
