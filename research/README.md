The strongest program-level refinement is a **two-sided resolving-budget requirement**. An observer must have enough resolution to see the endpoint discriminator, but substantially less stable capacity than is needed to reconstruct the source. `FD-142` makes the upper anti-circularity boundary unexpectedly low: on squarefree `n<=T`, the single additive coordinate `log rad(n)=log n` remains injective after quantization at inverse-polynomial scale, using only `log_2 T+log_2 log T+O(1)` bits, essentially the source identity budget. Thus polynomial precision, one scalar coordinate, or sublinear formal dimension are not anti-circularity certificates. `PC-305` gives the same warning for sparse moment reconstruction.

Arithmetic Fidelity supplies the opposite lower boundary. `AF-359` proves that the Riesz endpoint defect first occupies an order-one fraction of the critical zero energy at spectral height `T=exp(Theta(1/delta))`; any subexponential bandwidth in `1/delta` misses it. `AF-360` then closes the natural phase-shape escape: on every fixed-ratio dyadic shell the small-order Riesz multiplier is asymptotically a common scalar, so normalized projective shape and intra-shell phase geometry converge to the unsmoothed endpoint even far beyond the amplitude transition. An order-one discriminator therefore needs exponentially separated cross-scale amplitude information, a diverging-condition observer, or source information injected before Riesz compression. The useful corridor is not simply “more resolution”: it is enough endpoint resolution without reaching source-identifying capacity.

Prime Lattice now has an exact generic topology threshold. `PL-316` shows that arbitrarily small critical zero-extension `H^{1/2}` norm can still create order-one response on the moving Green diagonal, while `PL-317` proves that every fixed supercritical gain `H^{1/2+eta}` forces exponential tail decay, hence `Qm(Q)->0` and the required moving-sample suppression. Generic Sobolev refinements are therefore exhausted in both directions. The live theorem is source-specific: derive any genuine supercritical gain, equivalent boundary modulus, or direct moving-diagonal decay from the completed-Weil equation itself.

Nyman--Beurling and Robin Extremal independently sharpen `MI-017`'s sparse-exceptional-geometry warning. `NB-141` shows that Selberg density makes logarithmically crowded polynomial confluence cells power-law rare on average, yet the current zero-count, density, and zero-free envelopes still permit a sparse sequence of target-poor cells in a formal zeta-symmetric divisor. `RE-142` gives the corresponding Robin obstruction: even with negligible strict-subedge effective complexity, the same class of upper envelopes permits attained-edge gain `T^{-2+o(1)}` along a lacunary sequence. Average population control can therefore make bad packets rare without excluding the exact packet that dominates the endpoint. Both lines now need packet-sensitive actual-zeta input: worst-cell occupancy, amplitude/height anti-dominance, phase coupling, signed tail structure, or a direct target-projection law.

Möbius Cancellation remains the most concrete source-incidence program, but `MC-313` marks the boundary of generic coding-theory pressure. `MC-312` still proves the real arithmetic tradeoff `(n-R+O(log R))log Z >= (1-o(1))log y`; however generalized Griesmer collapses exactly to generalized Singleton in the live regime, and generic full-length rate-one-half codes can simultaneously realize the required coarse higher-weight profile `d_t>=R-O(R/t)`. Thus `n>=2R-o(R)` is a genuine source payment, but ordinary binary-code geometry does not make it impossible. The next coercive theorem must exploit arithmetic realizability of the Legendre incidence columns, a sharper second-order source-scale budget, joint labeled weight information, or another constraint absent from a random binary code.

Weil Inertia remains the clearest verified positive branch. `WI-301` turns dyadic screening into a uniform archimedean reserve `8.9e-18||f||_2^2`; complete one-signed prime-power screening is impossible for `Q_W(f)<=0` at any aperture. Weil Positivity moves further toward route exhaustion: `WP-317` shows that every bounded finite cluster of positive scalar Herglotz atoms is reduced by a regular Julia step from `N` atoms to `N-1` positive interlacing atoms, and repeated reduction ends at a real constant. A fixed finite multipole therefore supplies no persistent arithmetic sign mechanism; the surviving scalar route must use genuinely growing local multiplicity/continuous background, or the program must move to operator-valued, scattering, determinant, or nonseparable finite--archimedean structure.

Consequently `T_2=sum_j(N_j-2)_+`, and the endpoint-matched first-order transition table has a singleton realization. ## Cross-line structure

The first common constraint is **endpoint resolution versus source identity**. `FD-142` and `PC-305` show that nominally tiny observers can already be source-complete when precision or sparsity is counted correctly. `AF-359--AF-360` show from the other side that a legitimate endpoint observer may need exponential scale separation before it sees the relevant defect at all. A useful intermediate representation therefore needs two quantitative certificates: a lower bound showing it resolves the target-bearing endpoint feature, and an upper bound showing its stable distinguishability remains below source reconstruction. Dimension, tensor order, polynomial precision, and sample count are only proxies until those two budgets are priced.

The second common constraint is **endpoint topology rather than generic regularity**. Prime Lattice now closes this generically: critical `H^{1/2}` can miss the moving diagonal, every fixed supercritical gain controls it, and the only live question is whether the actual source equation supplies that gain. Arithmetic Fidelity gives an analogous moving-band obstruction, while Nyman shows that coordinate conditioning and average zero distribution can remain good while target occupation vanishes in rare local packets. The next useful norms must be chosen because the endpoint consumes them, not because they are stronger in an abstract hierarchy.

A third constraint is **rare exceptional geometry versus upper population control**. `NB-141` and `RE-142` give two distinct formal matched controls in which global density/count/zero-free information is respected while sparse endpoint-dominating packets survive. This makes `MI-017` operational: proving that bad configurations are rare in height, small in average density, or low in total population is insufficient when the theorem is defeated by one exceptional packet. The missing inputs must be worst-packet or coupling laws for actual zeta zeros.

The positive source-payment lines also separate generic structure from arithmetic realizability. Weil Inertia forces a fixed odd-prime repayment after dyadic screening. Möbius proves a real redundancy/scale payment but `MC-313` shows that generic binary coding can absorb it; the decisive information must therefore lie in the fact that code columns are labeled by actual lower primes and jointly realize Legendre characters on one shell. Robin similarly shows that a generic rightmost-transfer exponent is sharp under upper envelopes, so any improvement has to use actual-zero structure rather than another population bound.

Several representation families have reached clean exhaustion points without implying impossibility of the parent program. `WP-317` removes bounded finite scalar multipoles but leaves unbounded collective/operator geometry; `VIS-236` removes arbitrary fixed-type label randomization but leaves monotone gap/occupancy models; `MC-313` removes generic generalized-weight refinements but leaves arithmetic incidence; and `AF-360` removes intra-band projective phase as a Riesz escape but leaves genuinely cross-scale/source-native observables. These are carrier changes, not evidence against every transform-based route.

No new Master clue is justified. The actionable questions are already owned by their destination lines or have been incorporated into existing local clues, and the cross-line synthesis does not expose a distinct missing claim that needs separate bounded source-reading permission. No merge, split, or new-line proposal is warranted.

Arithmetic Fidelity supplies an endpoint lower bound through exponential cross-scale separation, while Farey and Prime Circle supply surprisingly low source-reconstruction ceilings. Identify an observer with a provable target-bearing margin whose stable capacity lies strictly between them.
