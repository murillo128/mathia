## Cross-line structure

The Farey/Nyman contrast now falsifies an overly broad version of the same-source heuristic. Farey first showed that reusing one source across nearby horizons can create incompatible equality rays, but `FD-125--FD-126` show that sufficiently sparse repeated-square horizons restore saturation even under a global square-root envelope and the exact squarefree/unit increment alphabet. Nyman's nested-cutoff coherence was already algebraically redundant; its progress begins only when `NB-123--NB-124` import a source-native invariant of actual zero packets. The reusable principle is therefore narrower: **cross-scale coherence matters only when the views impose nonredundant constraints that the actual source class cannot simultaneously saturate**.

Arithmetic Fidelity and Mobius sharpen the corresponding cost accounting. `AF-344` proves that ordered sign variation is not an invariant currency: a recoding can trade it for support sparsity and amplitude while keeping the analytic pole discriminator. `MC-296` shows that actual Page-theoretic arithmetic can indeed force many endpoint directions to become expensive, but source cost alone is not yet endpoint exclusion because an expensive subspace may still have enough capacity. Future certificates should track a vector of coupled costs—support, amplitude, conductor/source cost, multiplicity, and destination mass—rather than optimizing one proxy in isolation.

Prime Circle and Visual provide the cleanest destination-side comparison. `PC-295` shows that an apparently complete spectral object can classicalize after source averaging: even the full absolute singular spectrum forgets the prime source. `VIS-223`, by contrast, removes a representational artifact without weakening the live objective: midpoint centering gauges away the universal taper phase, leaving a real signed kernel, while `VIS-222` keeps the SDP and exact torus scales universally equivalent. The strategic test is not richness of representation but whether the exact statistic preserves the source-sensitive interaction at the normalization consumed by the theorem.

The remaining thin-endpoint lines are increasingly localized rather than diffuse. Prime Lattice has no hidden regular Volterra range obstruction beyond endpoint cancellation; Robin forces strong-atom cancellation into a reciprocal zero-spacing geometry; Weil Inertia confines complete screening to a polynomially tiny family of large-gap hosts; Weil Positivity finds the first Riemann-specific subleading curvature already wrong for its canonical reflected Herglotz tail. These reductions are useful precisely because each identifies the next operation that must do genuine mathematical work rather than merely reparameterize the same source.

The existing global fidelity/realization clue and `MI-009`, `MI-013`, and `MI-015` already cover the reusable program-level lessons about source realization, residual currencies, thin endpoints, and endpoint-faithful relaxations. The present delta sharpens those principles but does not expose a new destination-specific cross-line transfer with a distinct cheapest decisive test, so no new Master clue is justified.
