For every fixed `1/2<gamma<beta`, the probabilities of `max_i V_i>X^(2gamma)` are bounded below by constant multiples of `X^(-(1-beta)/2)` for Möbius and `X^(-(1-beta)/2)/sqrt(log(3X))` for primes.
