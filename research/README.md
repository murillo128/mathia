## Cross-line structure

**Source structure must survive the exact readout that consumes it.** Prime Lattice now supplies perhaps the cleanest negative control: class-field theory canonically couples prime rows by Frobenius monodromy, yet the trivial representation that produces ordinary Riemann zeta sends every monodromy to `1`. Weil Positivity supplies the complementary phenomenon: the real-place Gamma jump and finite Bost--Connes prime ray agree exactly at critical half-density, but imposing ordinary positivity after this local match reinstates the divergent diagonal and the one-dimensional archimedean distance fails on incomparable mixed-prime directions. These results reinforce the already-active non-character/finite--archimedean clues; they do not justify merging the lines, because Prime Lattice asks which source relation survives principal extraction while Weil Positivity still owes an independent sign theorem on the surviving completed form.

**Once a free relaxation becomes expressive enough, the next theorem must spend the discarded source law.** `ANF-153` makes the destination kernel universal and places all bow specificity in the demodulated arithmetic vector. `MC-184` shows that unsigned rough and squarefree support are already controllable, leaving Möbius orientation. `NB-017` shows that no amount of source-independent multiplicative depth removes reciprocal blind modes. `FD-016` shows that unrestricted real sources can defeat arbitrarily long sparse horizon families. These are mathematically different routes, but all rule out continuing to optimize the same source-blind surrogate after its expressive boundary has been reached.

**Stable transport and exact recoverability remain different currencies.** Prime Flute gives a positive example: two-derivative spectral locality survives both the physical hypercycle map and the actual variable-corridor scale, leaving a single intrinsic polar-transport gate. Xi gives the negative counterpart: even very rich real/complex observation can remain exponentially blind to the transition until a quantified geometric reach is crossed, and `XF-153` shows positive heat does not lower that near-antipodal threshold for the matched packet. This similarity is methodological rather than a shared lemma and does not support a merge or global clue.

**Target strength can change the apparent information bill without solving the source problem.** Arithmetic Fidelity's Li quotient deliberately forgets every subexponential perturbation while preserving the binary RH discriminator. That is a legitimate weaker endpoint, but its value depends entirely on proving a source-side remainder estimate without using the same off-critical-zero exclusion encoded by the quotient. The same discipline applies to Nyman's visibility budget and Robin's selected race: a local-looking estimate is useful only after its exact cost to the final theorem is written down.
