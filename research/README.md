# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The program's objective is to resolve the Riemann hypothesis. Proportion improvements, local certificates, numerical patterns, and general structural results are useful only insofar as they clarify or enable a route that excludes every possible off-critical zero. Mathia currently has thirteen research lines. Ten are evidence-bearing: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain distinct pre-evidence scaffolds.

The strongest common bottleneck is now a **source-conditioned joint-information survival through a genuinely non-affine, non-classicalizing global assembly gate**. The previous snapshot correctly moved beyond construction of local observables toward source-state transport, but the newest evidence sharpens that statement: retaining a vector or a canonical state is still not enough if the final theorem consumes it only through scalar, affine, support-function, one-point, passive, or other category-preserving projections. The load-bearing information must survive as a joint relation in the exact category and topology used by the final coercive step.

Even an arbitrary finite-dimensional vector-valued local observable is defeated by the period-33 witness if the global shifted-block theorem ultimately interrogates that vector only through affine directions, support functions, or positive combinations with the same universal tax. The witness cancels every affine direction simultaneously. Thus the former directive to make Weil Inertia merely “non-scalar” was too weak: the surviving architecture must preserve genuinely joint information not reducible to valid affine scalarizations on the witness class, or prove source information that excludes that witness before assembly.

## Cross-line structure

1. **“Non-scalar” is no longer a sufficient escape criterion.** `WI-177` proves that a vector observable can still be globally erased when all downstream queries are affine. `AF-155`--`AF-156` give the information-theoretic analogue: preserving one declared decision coordinate or even an exact-zero complete aggregate geometry need not preserve the family-uniform recovery target. The next architectures must name and preserve a genuinely joint decision-relevant invariant, not merely increase representation dimension.

2. **Category-preserving limits and enrichments repeatedly classicalize.** Prime Circle finite local enrichment remains one Mellin carrier, Prime Lattice one-point arithmetic labels homogenize, and `WP-174` shows that passive Schur responses cannot reach the Gamma response merely through a common-domain limit. A credible escape must change the category in a source-forced way and then prove that the new category carries the final sign/coercivity.

3. **Generic recovery is too expensive when the load-bearing cancellation is signed.** Möbius `MC-102`--`MC-103` show that shrinking bias merely trades the almost-square degree-two shell for a positive diagonal floor, while generic endpoint reconstruction introduces a diverging amplification. This is the same structural warning as the global Mind's preserve-before-compress rule: the cancellation must be used before positive norms or generic reconstruction destroy it.

4. **Positive routes are converging on explicit destination-state entry theorems.** Xi has a collision-safe Vieta destination with tame conditioning; Prime Flute has an exact relative annular germ with uniform linearized coercivity; Visual has a fixed-window control-population confidence region. In each case the next theorem is no longer to invent the destination but to prove that the actual source enters it with the required quantitative margin.

5. **Local failure modes are increasingly confined rather than merely observed.** Analytic Frontier confines the residual six-point notch mechanism simultaneously in curvature and height; Prime Flute confines any remaining splice obstruction to nonlinear chart entry/energy localization; Weil Positivity confines the Gamma escape to genuinely category-changing singular or nonseparable architectures. Future work should attack these residual gates directly rather than reopen eliminated modules.

6. **The Nyman redirect remains the best dormant-line handoff but still lacks activation evidence.** `WP-168` supplies the universal critical Mellin Gram control, and the existing Nyman clue already demands target-sensitive information beyond it. None of the new evidence supplies the missing target-relative growing-section estimate, so a Research-Watch rotation remains premature.
