## Cross-line structure

**Target-relative margin is a separate currency from source recovery.** Arithmetic Fidelity makes this exact: the source coefficients can be stably observable while the zero-count discriminant distance collapses. Prime Flute gives the operator analogue: even after the scalar Green reservoir is fully controlled, ideal decay must enter through the physical conversion vertices. These are not the same theorem, but both reject the inference that a well-conditioned intermediate representation automatically gives a robust endpoint observable.

**Local consistency is weaker than global continuation.** Nyman's boundary-chain counterexample proves this directly, while Analytic Frontier's matched late packet and Xi Flow's unknown-support/global-assembly gate show the same structural risk in different categories. The next useful results must constrain how a source can continue across growing scales, not merely identify every finite piece.

**A source feature can survive one quotient and disappear at the next.** Robin's endpoint prime labels and direct timing workload are compressed, while selector geometry survives before the final readout. Farey can now preserve a numerator spike at arbitrary later horizons but still lacks denominator control. Weil Inertia's exact sliding profile contains the real autocorrelation, yet firstness plus the scalar null equation erases the first small-aperture information one might try to use. Any cross-line transfer must therefore name the exact stage at which the proposed invariant is still visible.

**Several tempting regularizations are now calibrated controls rather than progress mechanisms.** Möbius bounded-support blind modes disappear but leave a high-support signed-amplitude problem; Visual nulls every subpolynomial near-diagonal band but leaves polynomial or nonquadratic regimes; Weil Positivity can cancel the critical exponential density with the canonical pole term, but ordinary temperedness of the result is already RH-equivalent. These results sharpen where genuinely new arithmetic structure must enter without licensing a transfer of their operators or normalizations.

## Highest-leverage unresolved questions

1. **Weil Inertia beyond null-equation redundancy.** Extract a medium-radius or operator-level relation for the same first crossing that survives prime-power threshold changes and is not determined by `Q_W(v)=0`; in parallel, keep the odd-sector signed radial flux as the clean source-specific coercivity target.
2. **Prime Flute normalized conversion vertices.** Derive the physical `F_N` and `R_N` and decide whether their killing-normalized forms carry the compactness or weak/Schatten decay required by the endpoint target. Further estimates of the scalar Green middle cannot answer this.
3. **Analytic Frontier source continuation at the sharp aperture.** Prove that the actual prime/rough placement cannot realize the matched packet through `Theta(X/a_X)` physical aperture, while keeping non-excisability as a logically separate final step.
4. **Nyman global continuation cost.** Bound the finite-horizon Schur certificates for the actual source on an unbounded horizon family, or derive an arithmetic property that rules out the stable boundary-chain counterexample mechanism.
5. **Farey same-horizon normalization.** Use the persistent `FD-069` numerator capture to control the denominator on the same horizons and obtain positive normalized occupation; more host-selection work is no longer the leverage point.
6. **Source structure beyond calibrated controls.** Möbius needs high-support signed amplitude, Visual needs polynomial-width or richer observables, Robin needs adaptive-interval prime-density control, and Weil Positivity needs a noncircular large-category finite–archimedean orientation. In each case, the next theorem must add source structure rather than relax the representation alone.
