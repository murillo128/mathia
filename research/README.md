## Cross-line structure

1. **Uniformity is part of the information, not a technical afterthought.** `AF-105`--`AF-106`, `MC-055`, and `WI-142` expose the same quantifier failure in three categories. Every finite stage may look correct, every frozen comparator may have the desired exponent, or every detector may sharpen with size, yet the family can escape through weak compactness, conductor-dependent constants, or a still-smaller confluence scale. A useful theorem must fix the admissible resource law from the source before the adversarial witness is chosen.

2. **A genuine local success can still be completely universal.** `WP-142`--`WP-143` produce real positive geometry and the correct logarithmic scale after the earlier normalization objection is removed, but matched composite stars reproduce it exactly. `VIS-021`--`VIS-023` similarly show that visually/statistically rich diagnostics can be deterministic quotients of one counting channel or a classical model-order statistic with a built-in null floor. `PL-147`--`PL-148` show that a sophisticated scalar/Hankel representation can be mathematically exact yet already be a completed RH-equivalent encoding. The discriminator is what survives the strongest matched control, not how rich the representation looks.

3. **Changing randomness or regularization counts only if the singular boundary also changes.** `ANF-025` trades Bragg persistence for the wrong infrared exponent, and `ANF-026` shows that the zero-Hurst boundary returns to the iid shuffled-lattice class and still fails after scale convexification. `WI-142` gives the operator analogue: shrinking a continuous regularization scale does not help if the source supplies no lower scale that the defect cannot outrun. Singular limits and quantifier order must be audited before a carrier enlargement is credited.

4. **Global assembly is often where the source information either appears or disappears.** `AF-105`--`AF-106` make original-range/admissibility assembly explicit; `PL-148` shows that the finite Suzuki moments exist only after globally canceling separately divergent prime and main terms; `WP-143` shows that a local intrinsic metric still lacks the global Weil form; and Xi Flow has already reduced its problem to near-buffer/mean assembly after the remote tail is controlled. A promising intermediate invariant is useful only if its final assembly theorem is source-forced and category-correct.

5. **Exact discrete/type information still needs a source anti-degeneracy law before it yields density-scale force.** Negative inertia in `weil_inertia` is the clearest example, while the vanishing Suzuki checkpoint reserve in `prime_lattice` is the complementary scalar warning. The program should not demand a universal positive margin where the target itself permits vanishing margins; it should instead prove the exact no-crossing, lower-gap, interaction, or transfer statement required by the destination theorem.
