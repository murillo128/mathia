# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia currently has thirteen research lines. Ten carry canonical evidence: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain pre-evidence scaffolds.

The evidence still does **not** establish one common positive mechanism or one mathematically meaningful global bottleneck shared by the program. The current delta instead reinforces a stricter way to measure progress: perform the source-admissible optimization, quotient, shorting, or scale transition first, and then ask whether the quantity needed by the destination theorem survives. This is a methodological synthesis across several lines, not a shared RH mechanism.

**Analytic Frontier has the clearest new positive movement of a live proof interface.** `ANF-111` exposed a real flaw in the naive recursive height-layer argument: a newly extracted layer could polarize against previously extracted mass rather than force genuinely new mass. `ANF-112` repairs that bookkeeping at the aggregate-packet level. If the accumulated sparse endpoint packet has negligible Montgomery--Taylor source norm it can be excised without changing near-extremality or fixed-notch exposure; if it retains macroscopic source norm, the global floor forces macroscopic negative polarization against the unused remainder, which in turn forces new disjoint endpoint mass. The remaining implication is still substantial but now precise: each newly forced layer must re-enter a sparse matched-critical chamber, or the alternative bulk/square-root/supercritical-height chambers must themselves be closed, before a strict fixed-notch gap follows.

**Prime Flute remains live, but its newest evidence is route narrowing rather than another positive locality estimate.** `PF-214` identifies the simultaneous normalized seam precision as block Jacobi. `PF-215` rules out uniformly bounded adjacent normalized hopping, `PF-217` shows that tangential boundary screening destroys every fixed positive fraction of the raw `PF-216` constant-mode mass floor after the correct variational shorting, and `PF-218` passes that obstruction through the exact inverse: the low Schur compression cannot simultaneously acquire raw `q_n^{1/2}` endpoint damping and an `ell^1` module-transport envelope. This does not refute `PF-213` locality. It changes the first unsupported smooth-route theorem to a coefficient-weighted estimate for the **actually screened inverse**, with the physical seam coefficient retained inside the recoupling word. The unsmoothed `PF-204` route and the independent true-short-collar splice remain separate alternatives.

**Xi Flow has moved from a failed raw-feasibility test to a sharper source-side obstruction, but not to transition coercivity.** `XF-106`--`XF-109` replace the raw-`l1` realization margin by the mass-preserving q-scale Toeplitz source. The strongest result, `XF-109`, proves that every nonpositive q-scale witness must place a fixed positive fraction of its coefficient-Fourier `L^2` mass outside a PNT-flat central window whose physical frequency grows like `exp(c sqrt(log q))`. Thus a vanishing amount of arbitrarily high-frequency contamination cannot fake the obstruction. What is still missing is the destination bridge: prove that any witness capable of representing the relevant Xi transition is too spectrally concentrated to satisfy this roughness requirement, prove an Xi-specific source prohibition on such rough witnesses, or replace the destination state. The previous autonomous Vieta-prefix nonidentifiability results therefore remain decisive.

**The Bost--Connes branch is more tightly classified on both the Möbius and Prime-Lattice sides.** `MC-141`--`MC-143` extend the Möbius obstruction from finite cyclotomic tests to the norm-completed coefficient algebra: `MC-143` proves that no continuous function on `hat Z` can realize the square-free Möbius orientation, and that its best uniform approximation error there is exactly one. On the Prime-Lattice side, `PL-215` shows that canonical Bost--Connes equilibrium states at distinct temperatures are disjoint once a high-temperature type-III sector is involved, while the canonical prime-product measure-class transition occurs at `beta=1`, not at `beta=1/2`. These results do not merge the two lines or close the full crossed product; they remove two natural ways of assigning half-critical significance to coefficient continuity or cross-temperature modular comparison.

**Arithmetic Fidelity, Prime Circle, Visual Exploration, and Weil Positivity mostly strengthen matched controls.** `AF-195`--`AF-197` show that once node geometry is optimized, equispaced parity stencils cease to be extremal even at fixed moment order and Wasserstein separation, so recovery lower bounds cannot treat one convenient stencil as the sharp control. `PC-213` moves nonlinear prime-power mask incidence before scalar contraction but the resulting two-projection spectrum still factorizes prime-locally. `VIS-093`--`VIS-095` show that fixed-window persistence, static temporal-edge graphs, and the unaugmented step-two signature retain at most fixed local-block inventories plus boundary data rather than genuinely nonlocal order. `WP-201`--`WP-202` classify the centered diagonal-bath escape exactly: universal stabilization of arbitrary finite positive payloads is equivalent to a reciprocal high-frequency Fourier floor, and that floor is itself nonarithmetic. These are useful boundary theorems, not evidence that their replacement categories contain an RH mechanism.

**Weil Inertia retains the same direct source-specific target.** `WI-195` fixes the exact multiplicative short-interval `L^2` quantity consumed by the bow, while `ANF-102`, `WI-196`, and `WI-197` rule out uniform twist saving, generic positivity/long-average de-exceptionalization, and the plan of merely strengthening the one-variable input inside the published Type-II transfer. The first unsupported implication remains a source-selected norm-level cancellation or a second source-fixed observable that excludes the same exceptional near-null direction.

The Riemann Atlas remains historical/structural navigation only. Its declared `source_revision` is still far behind the evidence reconciled here, so its percentages and frontier telemetry are stale derived data and are not used for portfolio decisions.

## Cross-line structure

The strongest common lesson in the current evidence is **not** a common mathematical obstruction; it is an order-of-operations discipline. `AF-195`--`AF-197` optimize the matched source geometry before declaring an extremal recovery barrier. `PF-217`--`PF-218` show that raw coercivity can disappear after the exact variational shorting and that the corresponding inverse weight can then be impossible. `ANF-112` replaces pairwise layer bookkeeping by the aggregate packet actually seen by the source norm. `XF-109` strengthens a pointwise roughness requirement to a fixed Fourier-mass requirement after passing to the q-scale Toeplitz source. Across these lines, claims of coercivity or fidelity should be made only after the admissible source optimization/elimination consumed downstream. This does not create a transferable theorem between the lines.

The **Prime Lattice / Möbius Bost--Connes overlap is converging negatively rather than toward a shared mechanism.** Möbius orientation is discontinuous on the continuous profinite coefficient spectrum (`MC-143`), while canonical cross-temperature comparison in Prime Lattice leaves the common normal high-temperature sector and sees a measure-class transition at `beta=1` (`PL-215`). Together they remove coefficient completion and temperature interpolation as reasons to treat the appearance of `1/2` as arithmetic evidence. The residual questions are different: Möbius needs an orientation-sensitive aggregate cheaper than Mertens, while Prime Lattice needs a source-forced relation among otherwise programmable channels or within a legitimate same-sector/global construction.

Prime Flute and Analytic Frontier remain the two lines where the proof architecture is most explicit, but the newest evidence moves them differently. Analytic Frontier repairs a genuine recursion defect and preserves a route to a finite contradiction if all chambers can be closed. Prime Flute has instead falsified two natural smooth-route coercivity completions and now has to work in the weaker physical coefficient currency or switch to the unsmoothed route. Neither pattern should be exported rhetorically to unrelated lines.

Weil Positivity and Arithmetic Fidelity reinforce the role of strong matched controls. `WP-202` gives an exact nonarithmetic mechanism that can manufacture the desired sign property, while `AF-197` shows that optimizing the geometry of the control can make a recovery obstruction stronger than the convenient equispaced model suggests. Both results raise the standard for source-specific claims, but they do not supply a common positive construction.

## Highest-leverage unresolved questions

1. **Analytic-Frontier packet cascade closure.** After `ANF-112`, can every newly forced disjoint source-heavy layer be returned to the sparse matched-critical regime, or can the bulk/square-root and supercritical-height alternatives be closed strongly enough to force a strict fixed-notch exposure gap? A physical near-extremizer in any surviving chamber would instead falsify the route.

2. **Prime-Flute coefficient-weighted screened transport.** After `PF-217`--`PF-218`, does the actual screened Schur inverse satisfy a PF-213-compatible transport estimate once the physical `d_n` seam coefficient is kept inside the operator word, or does a concrete low-data family violate every such weighted envelope? Independently, the `PF-204` unsmoothed critical transport and the true-short-collar splice remain decisive alternatives.

3. **Bow distinguished-twist coupled norm.** Can one prove a source-specific second-moment/dispersion theorem at the exact bow twist in the `WI-195` signed norm, preserving the required cancellation before the losses isolated by `WI-197`, or produce a second source-fixed observable that rules out the exceptional near-null direction?

4. **Xi q-scale roughness versus transition coercivity.** Does the destination collision/transition mechanism force witnesses into a Fourier-concentrated class incompatible with `XF-109`, or can the actual Xi source realize the required macroscopic rough witnesses? Either outcome would materially classify the surviving q-scale route; merely extending the old low-band transport does not answer it.
