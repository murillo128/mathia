# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia has thirteen research lines in the current portfolio. Ten are evidence-bearing: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain distinct pre-evidence scaffolds.

The strongest common bottleneck is now a **source-forced residual margin above the complete matched-control resolution floor**. The earlier quotient, gauge, topology, non-escape, and assembly gates remain necessary, but the newest evidence makes the quantitative part sharper. An exact residual can still be unusable because the admissible witness class sees it only through a weakened quotient norm, the observation horizon aliases it, finite sampling creates the same apparent signal, a universal dynamics contracts it too slowly, or the only positive completion erases the mixed arithmetic directions. Progress therefore requires the source to fix the representation and control category first, and then force a discriminator whose margin remains above the resolution/error floor in the exact norm, scale, time horizon, or topology consumed downstream.

`AF-129`--`AF-130` give the clean abstract model. Restricting admissible decision witnesses changes Le Cam recovery from the full total-variation geometry to the support gauge of the allowed witness body; for metric-local witnesses this becomes diameter-normalized Wasserstein-1 recovery, with a sharp quantitative loss controlled by the metric aspect ratio. Zero-error completeness can therefore coexist with a substantially weaker margin. `PL-167` gives the finite-horizon analogue: sufficiently low signed prime-log frequencies are indistinguishable from the constant character on the whole height interval and simultaneously lose sensitivity to `beta-1/2`. `VIS-036`--`VIS-038` give the empirical analogue: the Pearson/CA and likelihood-ratio/CMI summaries are nested functionals of the same fitted three-gap residual, with a nonzero sampling floor and a signed cubic correction rather than independent evidence channels.

The same threshold structure is now visible in the arithmetic and dynamical lines. `MC-076`--`MC-077` show that making one Dirichlet factor small by moving toward the convolution identity leaves the companion Mertens-equivalent at exactly the manufactured power scale; `MC-078` shows that even a source-natural fixed residue-class prime partition only produces Selberg--Delange logarithmic cancellation. `XF-043` suppresses remote replenishment of a memory-scale gap block to a relative `O(1/R)` floor after a super-mesoscopic buffer, while `XF-044` proves that the slow Cauchy mode needs heat time of order `log R` to gain the additional factor required by the inverse-buffer flux threshold. The missing Xi input is therefore localized and quantitatively calibrated rather than a generic request for more smoothing.

Several lines also close important representation-level escapes. `ANF-046` proves that the central-notch separator survives the exact minimum elementary affine slack, while `ANF-047`--`ANF-048` remove a phase search from a sufficient five-point safety test and exclude the whole support-one region `|x_1-x_2|<=1/3`; the remaining scalar obstruction is now a genuinely finite, non-small-separation compact coherence problem. `PL-168` shows that the cheapest fixed-order higher-correlation escape classicalizes in the Rudnick--Sarnak low-support regime to universal GUE pairings. `WP-155` shows that canonical flag or Cartesian completions of the resultant carrier either omit mixed-prime two-cells or make their curvature exactly zero, while the surviving same-prime curvature remains non-`ell^2` even after spectator quotient. `WI-161` repairs a source-interface issue by recovering the split dyadic BGSTB spike error from prefix subtraction, so the regular changing-test gate is valid on the canonical dyadic window; the singular boundary isolated by `WI-158`--`WI-159` remains the genuine unresolved arithmetic regime.

The program-level discriminator is therefore: **let the source fix the gauge, observation geometry, admissible witness class, conditioning, time horizon, and assembly topology; quotient the strongest matched control; compute the resulting resolution/error floor; then require a source-forced residual whose quantitative margin survives above that floor all the way to the target theorem.** Exact nonzero structure, zero-error completeness, a natural gauge, or a positive local completion is not enough when its usable margin collapses in the destination category.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Exact detectability and usable quantitative fidelity are now cleanly separated.** `AF-129`--`AF-130` show that an admissible witness class can be zero-error complete while shrinking the recovery margin by a sharp geometric factor. `VIS-036`--`VIS-038` show the empirical counterpart: several apparently different statistics may be deterministic coordinates of the same residual and share a finite-sample floor. A positive mechanism must state the norm or decision geometry in which its residual is quantitatively large, not merely nonzero.

2. **Every observation mechanism carries an endogenous resolution floor.** `PL-167` identifies the inverse-height frequency floor of a finite Kronecker orbit; `XF-043`--`XF-044` identify a buffer floor and a dynamical precision clock; `WI-161` together with `WI-158`--`WI-159` separates the regular dyadic error regime from the singular changing-test boundary; and `MC-076`--`MC-077` show a gauge perturbation whose apparent gain and loss of Mertens fidelity occur at the same power scale. The next source theorem has to beat the floor in the exact destination norm.

3. **Natural structure is not enough when completion universalizes the surviving directions.** `MC-078` uses an entirely arithmetic residue-class prime allocation yet still lands on classical Selberg--Delange behavior. `PL-168` turns low-support higher correlations into universal pairings. `WP-155` shows that canonical cell completions cannot create mixed-prime curvature absent from the source incidence. Together with the existing Prime Circle and Prime Flute controls, this reinforces the rule that provenance-sensitive coupling must be present before the universal completion step.

4. **The best current positive reductions shrink the unresolved domain instead of adding another representation.** `ANF-046`--`ANF-048` turn the last universal scalar question into a compact, non-small-separation coherence test. `XF-043` localizes any persistent memory obstruction to the near buffer. `WI-161` removes a false dyadic-interface obstacle and leaves the true singular norm boundary. These are high-value reductions because each names a smaller source theorem whose success or failure would be decisive.

5. **The common target is a source-calibrated margin theorem.** Across fidelity, phase sampling, Möbius factorization, changing tests, positive completion, and Xi dynamics, the remaining question is no longer whether a residual can be written down. It is whether the arithmetic source forces that residual to stay separated from its strongest matched control by more than the representation's own aliasing, sampling, error, conditioning, or dynamical floor through the complete assembly used by the final theorem.
