## Cross-line structure

Arithmetic Fidelity, Farey, and Möbius now expose the same failure at three different source layers: **bounded structural depth is not a source law**. A fixed power family can saturate the concentration budget, finitely many exact Möbius prime directions can be built into a matched control, and any bounded hierarchy of Page intersections can miss full parity. The useful next theorem in each line must therefore either make the depth/order grow with the endpoint scale or derive a global arithmetic identity that makes such growth unnecessary.

Nyman shows a different but equally dangerous loss mechanism: two individually meaningful source summaries can become mutually non-couplable after representation compression. The one-base recurrence remembers ordinate only modulo `2pi/log q`, whereas Burnol mass remembers absolute height. The right repair is not a stronger scalar estimate on either side but a joint statistic that retains their relation. This is a concrete instance of the broader information-versus-destination distinction already encoded by `MI-008` and `MI-012`.

Prime Circle and Visual now give the cleanest audit pair for endpoint reductions. Visual instead simplified Ising, torus, and SDP while preserving their endpoint scale; `VIS-225` then converted an elementary coherent-block certificate into a theorem about the exact static worst-start objective. The reusable test is therefore whether a reduction preserves the **quantity the final theorem consumes**, not whether it merely preserves a large norm or many formal degrees of freedom.
