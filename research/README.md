# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia has thirteen research lines in the current portfolio. Ten are evidence-bearing and active: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain distinct pre-evidence scaffolds.

The strongest common bottleneck is now a **source-forced exceptional-regime closure gate after complete matched control**. The previous profile-complexity and non-escape principles remain necessary, but the newest results repeatedly push broad generic escape families into thin, explicitly characterized source-specific leftovers. `ANF-041` closes the zero-margin small-height five-point boundary by positive-definite curvature Gram coupling; `MC-069` reduces positive quadratic feedback below a quasi-subpower threshold to at most one Landau--Page exceptional conductor; `PL-160`--`PL-161` force any fixed-step one-sided Suzuki counterexample under RH failure into an unattained, phase-pinned horizontal frontier; `WI-157` leaves only `O((log T)^-2)` deterministic headroom for regular `T`-dependent support-one scalar families; and `XF-036` shows that the full translated Xi counting law eliminates the borderline macroscopic flux fold that survives two-span controls.

The program-level task is therefore sharper than merely finding a carrier that retains information. After deterministic, statistical, sampling, refinement, positivity, and finite-information controls are removed, a live mechanism must prove that the **actual arithmetic source excludes the remaining exceptional geometry or forces a quantitative margin on it in the exact destination topology**. Generic flexibility is increasingly classified; the unresolved work is concentrated in source-specific anti-aliasing, anti-confluence, exceptional-family exclusion, sign production, and coherent global assembly.

Several lines now expose complementary versions of this gate. Arithmetic Fidelity adds an information-rate formulation: `AF-120` identifies Rényi information dimension with the Cesàro innovation rate of a coherent dyadic hierarchy, while `AF-121` proves that uniformly fiberwise Lipschitz recovery cannot increase conditional information dimension. A proposed repair must therefore pay not only a finite profile-count budget but also the fine-scale innovation dimension required by the discriminator. Prime Circle and Weil Positivity close the corresponding architectural shortcuts: `PC-171` collapses strongly refinement-covariant self-adjoint Robin operators to a four-real-parameter first-order family, while `WP-150` proves that every vertex-local positive renormalization that keeps fixed shell energies finite erases each fixed resultant arithmetic edge.

`xi_flow` remains the strongest constructive counterpoint. `XF-032`--`XF-034` identify geometric gap ramps as universal finite-range shape null modes and then use Xi counting to flatten coherent super-mesoscopic ramps. `XF-035` isolates the inverse-buffer flux-variation scale, and `XF-036` strengthens the source side decisively: once translated counting constraints are retained, `M V_M=O(1)` already forces every gap in the full buffer to converge uniformly to the source mean spacing. What remains is a genuine coercivity theorem linking adverse or near-null shape force to that rigid source family strongly enough to obtain fixed backward-time control.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Broad generic escapes are collapsing to thin source-specific exceptions.** `MC-069` leaves at most one exceptional conductor in a large range; `PL-160`--`PL-161` leave only an unattained phase-pinned zero frontier for fixed-ray lower boundedness; `XF-036` eliminates the macroscopic borderline fold once the full Xi counting source is used. The next gain is likely to come from proving that the source excludes the residual exceptional set, not from enlarging the generic test class again.

2. **Complete control must include information rate, not just support or profile count.** `AF-120`--`AF-121` show that coherent fine-scale recovery has an innovation-dimension cost that Lipschitz repair cannot compress away. This complements finite-profile non-escape: a carrier can use only finitely many macroscopic profiles and still demand unbounded new microscopic information.

3. **Positive/local/refinement-compatible completion is repeatedly selector-destructive.** `PC-171` leaves only universal first-order boundary structure under strong refinement covariance, while `WP-150` shows that local finite-energy renormalization deletes fixed arithmetic resultant edges. Source provenance must be coupled before these universalizations, or carried by a genuinely nonlocal/global object.

4. **Support-one scalar improvement beyond Montgomery--Taylor now needs an actual carrier change.** `ANF-041` shows that even the first coupled five-point complex boundary has a rigid curvature-Gram control near the real axis; `WI-153`--`WI-157` independently close bounded-depth, PSD-matrix, finite-portfolio, and regular growing-family scalar escapes. The remaining plausible information is genuinely joint, sign-indefinite, higher-order, wider-support, or source-restricted.

5. **Matched controls can themselves become arithmetic if allowed to grow.** `VIS-030` is the clearest example: a bounded Farey endpoint subtraction is an explicit geometric control, but the full growing endpoint hierarchy is a totient/Möbius Riesz object. Null models and nuisance subtraction must therefore be fixed independently of the target arithmetic scale or their information content explicitly audited.
