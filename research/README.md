# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia has thirteen research lines in the current portfolio. Ten are evidence-bearing and active: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain distinct pre-evidence scaffolds.

The strongest common bottleneck is now a **source-forced coupled scale-transfer and assembly gate after complete matched control**. The previous exceptional-regime and quantitative non-escape requirements remain necessary, but the newest results show a further failure mode: once broad generic escapes have been removed, isolating the remaining object into a standalone local estimate, normalized limit, or independently controlled component can itself destroy the only usable coupling or silently import the target theorem.

This appears in several mathematically different forms. `AF-123`--`AF-124` replace worst-case uniform recovery by an exact probabilistic scale-transfer budget: first-order information can survive highly nonuniform fibers, but only according to the input resolution depth required to resolve the target scale. `MC-070` closes the Landau--Page positive-feedback survivor below the square-root conductor horizon, while `MC-071`--`MC-072` show that standalone cancellation of the signed inverse kernel, or even its first dyadic reciprocal block, already carries zeta and quadratic-Dirichlet zero-free information. `PL-162` shows that simultaneous phase pinning on every fixed finite prime set is vertically generic among zeta zeros, and `PL-163` shows why the canonical positive Landau--Gonek/Fejér repair loses exactly the frontier resolution it would need. `WP-151` proves that normalized resultant mass escape depends on the chosen exhaustion, and `XF-039` proves that translated counting, vanishing total log-gap variation, and asymptotic Cauchy rigidity still do not recover the microscopic inverse-buffer flux budget.

The program-level task is therefore sharper than excluding a thin exceptional family. A viable route must derive from the arithmetic source **which scales, components, regularization/exhaustion, and couplings are admissible before compression**, preserve the cross-component sign or correlation needed by the destination theorem, and prove a quantitative margin without first assigning RH-strength control to an isolated subobject. Source provenance, scale transfer, topology, and assembly are now part of one proof obligation rather than separable implementation choices.

Two current boundaries make this especially concrete. `ANF-042`, after adversarial correction of only the degenerate central-frequency quantifier, gives the full two-pair five-point defect a sharp global one-self-energy descent floor and confines pointwise negativity to an explicit amplitude-mismatch danger band; the unresolved issue is coherent spectral mass and phase alignment across that band, not unbounded vertical amplification. `WI-158` shows that the singular support-one scalar boundary is genuine: the published pointwise BGSTB error is not uniform enough on near-Montgomery--Taylor extremizers at the natural `L^1=Theta(sqrt(log T))` scale, so any scalar escape now needs new frequency-sensitive or averaged arithmetic error information rather than another regular test family.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Componentwise localization is increasingly the wrong proof interface.** `MC-071`--`MC-072` show that controlling an isolated inverse component already imports zero-free information; `PL-163` shows that localizing a positive detector to the hypothetical frontier destroys the available arithmetic error control; and `XF-039` shows that strong coarse/static consequences do not reconstruct the microscopic flux that generated them. The live information is in the coupled weighted sum, source-conditioned moment, or dynamics, not in an independently bounded piece.

2. **Scale transfer and regularity must be source-forced rather than worst-case conventions.** `AF-123`--`AF-124` make this exact: a wildly nonuniform decoder can preserve first-order information if its required resolution depth is small in probability, while finer profile control demands stronger tail information. `WI-158` supplies the arithmetic analogue from the opposite side: pointwise error control that is adequate for regular tests can fail exactly on singular near-extremizers. The relevant theorem must match the scale distribution of the test family actually consumed downstream.

3. **A moving limit is not evidence until the source canonically chooses the movement.** `WP-151` makes normalized mass escape exhaustion-dependent, while `VIS-030` shows that a growing endpoint subtraction eventually becomes an arithmetic Möbius/totient channel. `VIS-031`--`VIS-032` provide the positive model: choose a pre-fixed asymptotic band whose endpoint null and matched-null baseline can both be proved before inspecting the target signal.

4. **Source rigidity now often eliminates macroscopic or generic escapes but leaves a coupled exceptional remainder.** `ANF-042` confines two-pair descent to a bounded danger band and one-self-energy budget; `PL-162` leaves only horizontal-conditioned phase recurrence rather than ordinary finite-prime recurrence; `XF-039` leaves microscopic dynamic flux rather than macroscopic span geometry. The next theorem must address those residual couplings directly rather than enlarge the generic test class.

5. **Positive/local/refinement-compatible completion continues to trade away the selector or the needed resolution.** `PC-173`--`PC-174` collapse fixed distributional and compact weak-covariant boundary repairs to universal/classical operator structure; `WP-151` removes noncanonical normalized mass as a rescue; and `PL-163` shows that positive harmonic localization loses interior frontier resolution. A positive route must couple provenance and topology before these universalizations, not attempt to recover them afterward.
