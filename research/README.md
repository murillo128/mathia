# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The program's objective is to resolve the Riemann hypothesis. Mathia currently has thirteen research lines. Ten are evidence-bearing: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain distinct pre-evidence scaffolds.

The current evidence does **not** establish one common positive mechanism or one mathematically meaningful global bottleneck shared by the active lines. Phrases such as "source-conditioned critical closure" are too broad to count as research progress unless they identify a concrete implication that has actually moved. The useful program-level distinction is instead between **proof-interface progress**, where a previously unsupported implication is replaced by a strictly narrower one, and **representation/classification progress**, where a construction is better understood or a false route is closed but the arithmetic bridge is unchanged.

Prime Flute currently supplies the clearest proof-interface progress. `PF-199` proves that exact finite-color localization/reconstruction in weak trace class does not pay a factor equal to the number of orthogonal colors. `PF-200` then shows that on the regular Lambert body the principal diagonal Cwikel constants can be made uniform in prime and window depth. Together these remove two specific candidate losses. The first unsupported implication has genuinely moved to the **full localized block remainder**: nonprincipal coefficients, off-diagonal matrix terms, cutoff commutators, window interfaces, coordinate/time-change errors, and the critical core must be bounded uniformly or with a prime-summable loss strongly enough to assemble the global weak-`S_1` object. That statement is narrower than the previous generic family-uniform reassembly problem and is the strongest current reason to keep the line in `continue`.

Xi Flow has a mixed but informative update. `XF-090` shows that subtracting the source-fixed endpoint pole/cusp does not make the relative Volterra problem perturbative: for the Xi reference the residual equation acquires a source-derived forcing with logarithmic-square size in the current guarded scale. `XF-091` simultaneously shows that this positive-time logarithmic cusp creates no additional local lattice counterterm: the first-cell trapezoidal defect is lower order. Thus the discretization singularity itself is not the remaining problem, but the source-reference forcing is. The live implication is to absorb or subtract that forcing through a source-fixed nonlinear reference response or a renormalized relative energy, then prove the actual Xi source enters the guarded state and, separately, that a hypothetical positive `Lambda` transition forces order-one mass in the same state.

Visual Exploration obtains an exact reduction rather than an asymptotic theorem. `VIS-079` sums the complete signed growing-support resonance energy into a one-variable triangular autocorrelation mean of a finite Euler product on `Re(s)=1`. This is valuable because it replaces a high-dimensional resonance census by one frozen theorem surface, but no current finding determines the joint-limit behavior under `X=X(L)`. Progress on this line now means proving an upper bound, lower bound, or asymptotic for that exact triangular mean; divergence of reciprocal-frequency certificates or visually strong beats is not evidence.

Most other new results are rigorous **negative or calibration results**, and should be valued as such rather than described as movement toward RH. `ANF-092` proves that even near-extremal safe separable configurations can have exponentially bad raw conditioning, so any stability theorem must quotient the exact separable directions before measuring conditioning. `AF-178` identifies the exact simple-Blaschke local recovery constant and `AF-179` identifies the sharp Hölder loss at root collisions; these are useful recovery calibrations but do not yet provide an arithmetic source bridge. `MC-125` proves that even the complete translation-invariant factor histogram through sub-square-root growing memory can forget the order information needed for first-absolute excursions, but its controls are not Möbius-multiplicative. `MC-126` closes a different finite-memory repair: a fixed finite additive accumulator such as `M mod q` has only a bounded potential, one circulation proportional to the ordinary Mertens endpoint, and reversal-even occupation, so it creates no independent oriented excursion carrier. `PC-203` and `PC-204` classicalize the canonical anchored Poisson/heat/fractional homogeneous scalar repairs back into the finite Haar-cone packet class. `PL-198` shows that one exact common dense-range intertwiner cannot create curved prime transport downstream of the canonical commuting prime shifts. `WP-191` identifies Burnol's genuine adelic Lax--Phillips globalization as an RH-equivalent causality representation, not an independent positivity theorem.

The program therefore has **real mathematical accumulation but uneven solution-directed progress**. A finding counts as movement of a line's proof frontier only when it changes the first unsupported implication or supplies a reusable estimate that acts on that implication. Exact identities, prior-art redirects, classicalizations, and matched-control obstructions remain valuable because they prevent repeated work and kill false mechanisms, but they must not be promoted into a common narrative of convergence unless an explicit theorem transfers between lines. No such cross-line positive theorem is established by the current delta.

## Cross-line structure

1. **The strongest shared lesson is methodological, not a common RH mechanism.** Prime Circle, Prime Lattice, Weil Positivity, Analytic Frontier, and Arithmetic Fidelity all show ways in which an apparently richer representation can collapse, be ill-conditioned, or move the arithmetic statement into an equivalent condition. These results should prevent repeated false starts, but no theorem presently transfers their obstruction into a positive estimate for another line.

2. **A useful reduction must change the next theorem, not merely rename it.** Prime Flute passes this test: the principal local endpoint and finite-color counting losses have been removed, leaving a specific remainder estimate. Visual Exploration partially passes it: the high-dimensional signed energy is now exactly one triangular mean, so the next calculation is unambiguous even though its difficulty is not yet known. Xi Flow passes it in a negative direction: the endpoint issue has split into a harmless local discretization singularity and a still-large reference forcing.

3. **Quotient before measuring difficulty.** `ANF-092` shows that raw conditioning can be exponentially bad on configurations already inside the safe separable cone; `AF-178`--`AF-179` show the analogous dependence of recovery on separation and collision multiplicity. The actionable cross-line principle is limited but precise: any stability claim must state the quotient and target metric before a condition number is treated as an obstruction or a resource.

4. **Order and relation survive where local summaries do not.** `MC-125` shows that sub-square-root local factor histograms can forget global excursion order, while `MC-126` shows that simply adding a fixed cumulative modular state recovers no independent oriented carrier beyond the Mertens endpoint. `PC-203`--`PC-204` and `PL-198` give different algebraic examples of relational structure being erased by exact contraction or intertwining. This is not one common theorem; it constrains future candidates to state exactly what nonlocal or source-specific information survives.

5. **Prior-art equivalence is a closure result, not a positivity result.** `WP-191` is the clearest current example: a genuine adelic scattering assembly exists, but its missing causal property is exactly RH-equivalent. The line learns that this canonical globalization cannot supply independent Weil positivity, not that global scattering itself has advanced toward a proof.

## Highest-leverage unresolved questions

1. **Prime-Flute full-block remainder.** Can one prove a uniform or prime-summable weak-`S_1` bound for each localized full block minus the principal Cwikel model, including all off-diagonal, cutoff, interface, coordinate/time-change, and critical-core errors, and then assemble those bounds without losing the shrinking-cross-section gain?

2. **Xi relative source-energy theorem.** Can the source-fixed reference forcing exposed by `XF-090` be absorbed by a canonical nonlinear reference response or a renormalized relative energy with a margin strong enough for guarded continuum-to-grid comparison? If so, can actual Xi data be shown to enter that state and can every positive-`Lambda` transition be forced to create order-one guarded mass?

3. **Weil-Inertia coefficient theorem.** Can the hyperbolically aggregated von-Mangoldt coefficient `B_n` be estimated at the exact structured alias scales required by the bow, rather than relying on the already-classical Atkinson phase geometry?

4. **Visual exact triangular mean.** For one frozen joint law `X=X(L)`, what is the asymptotic behavior of `(2/L^2) integral_0^L (L-u)[|P_X(1,u)|^2/P_X(2,0)-1] du`? A theorem here would either give the signed visual route actual evidence or close it cleanly.

5. **Möbius ordered-information transfer.** What source-natural Möbius statistic retains enough nonlocal ordering, growing/non-additive state, multiplicative placement, or controlled occupation information to imply RH-scale `D_M(X_j)` on some unbounded sequence, and can that implication be proved without inserting an RH-equivalent coarse mode into the hypothesis?
