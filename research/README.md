## Cross-line structure

**Propagation, abundance, and overlap are now cleanly separated.** `WI-267` forces support propagation without yet forcing autocorrelation; `FD-086` forces coherent occupancy inside a whole block without transporting it to the next block; `NB-085` exposes a residual prediction floor even after arbitrarily deep same-ray completion. These are not one common theorem, but they prevent the portfolio from counting geometric presence as if it were already the quantity consumed by the target.

**Destination-scale averaging can be strictly more relevant than worst-case local control.** `PF-318` is the clearest positive example: weak trace closure follows from logarithmic-rank Hilbert--Schmidt leakage even though the stronger pointwise operator-norm target is unavailable. `VIS-200` identifies the corresponding arithmetic limit of the present averaging technology: the four-form family average reaches just beyond logarithmic windows and then runs out of logarithmic savings. Further work should improve the aggregate quantity the destination actually sees, not optimize a stronger proxy.

**Qualitative source richness is no longer scarce; composability is.** Farey has long coherent blocks, Robin has abundant anchored-gap candidates, and Möbius retains an exactly normalized blind projector after conductor descent. None of those facts decides the target because the missing operation is respectively inter-block composition, selected second-layer geometry, or active-shell cancellation/rank. More instances of the same source event do not substitute for a mechanism that combines them in the target geometry.

**Universal limiting structure must be factored out before it is treated as evidence.** `WP-279`--`WP-280` make the leading critical Mangoldt tangent essentially PNT-level and push Gamma to the first correction. `PC-277` similarly confines the Li-grid signal to a shrinking near-resonance window, while fixed phase normalizes away. `AF-308` supplies the finite-dimensional analogue: exact zero incidence can be exceptional even when arbitrarily small stable margin is unavoidable. The live question in each line is what survives beyond the universal baseline.

The existing proposed `CLUE-global-cross-line-fidelity-realization-separation` still covers the program-level separation between source fidelity, target transport, destination realization, and assembly. The new results sharpen propagation, averaging, and destination-screening gates but do not provide one common source family, operator, normalization, and target from which an exact transfer theorem follows. No additional Master clue is warranted. The line owners have already retargeted Weil Inertia to activity-to-overlap, Prime Flute to endpoint-complete mean-square leakage, and Visual to averaged four-form local-factor cancellation.
