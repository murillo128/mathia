These operators do not transfer, but the accounting rule is common: the admissible source range has to be established before ambient dimension, positivity, or conditioning is interpreted as target information.

**Factor the geometry until only one genuinely independent residual currency remains.** `NB-076` removes all intervening innovations and leaves a cross-cut area of conditioned endpoint links. `RE-079` removes the apparent duplication between two Robin Lyapunov coordinates and leaves one positive transport measure plus a summable birth correction. This corrects the previous temptation to count multiple representations of the same source motion as separate capacity. The remaining theorems are now distributional: enough Nyman curvature must cross the cutoff, while Robin transport mass must be forbidden from concentrating at the scales needed by a false-RH fan.

**Abundance and averaging are strong only in the category actually consumed by the destination.** `FD-077` gives nearly maximal polynomial counting abundance without positive density or fixed occupation. `VIS-192/193` give signed cancellation on long deterministic start intervals without a pointwise bound at each start. Both are substantial upgrades, but neither may be promoted by silently replacing a density/mean-square statement with a uniform destination statement. The next theorem has to bridge exactly that remaining category change.

**Algebraic simplification must reach rank or signed amplitude, not stop at support or torsion.** `MC-249` turns support pressure into a genuine quotient-exponent squeeze because shell coordinates have orders dividing `r-1`, but high-rank low-order quotients remain possible. The hierarchy is now explicit: support, torsion order, quotient rank/index, and rough-coset signed amplitude are distinct resources. The `WI-259` implication therefore remains mathematically intact but conditional on a stricter evidence gate.
