## Cross-line structure

### 1. Exact coordinates can remove a fake obstruction without supplying the missing arithmetic theorem

`FD-097`, `NB-096`, and `MC-269` are three different instances of the same accounting discipline. Farey preserves the common destination ratio instead of maximizing occupation loss and defect growth independently. Coordinate repair is real progress only because it identifies what still cannot be removed by reparameterization.

### 2. Source provenance must be stricter than ambient symmetry or an admissible scalar surrogate

`WP-286` and `WI-278` sharpen the program-wide matched-control standard from opposite directions. Weil Positivity shows that a readout can be canonical for the ambient multiplicative scale group and still be completely arithmetic-blind: every Haar/Følner mean kills every nonzero summable character. Weil Inertia shows that a scalar spectral profile can satisfy all currently certified aperture data and still evade every support-only reserve. In both cases the next useful theorem must retain structure that the control does not: finite-prime/archimedean coupling before scalarization for WP, and an exact source/eigenfunction constraint absent from the abstract `lambda_a` profile for WI.

`PC-280` supplies the finite-dimensional analogue. After full-domain gauge and permutation quotients, truncation retains only the placement of source-dilated windows around a fixed multiplier. Source dependence is present, but arithmetic discrimination begins only if that placement has a property not reproduced by classical or matched denominator controls.

### 3. Joint structure is becoming more valuable than stronger separated estimates

`RE-099` is an exact warning against separating a coupled problem too early: even perfect pointwise cancellation in the deformed-quadratic sample kernel cannot close Robin if the high-shift zero quartets are then paid by total absolute mass. The line must retain shifted quartet structure or the joint oscillation itself. Weil Inertia's remaining proposed Hilbert cut-covariance clue asks a related but line-specific question: whether retaining the common signed Hilbert-valued cut path gives a stronger bottom-spectrum reserve than the scalar norm profile. Prime Flute similarly keeps the whole packet response inside the completed pant operator and integrates only at the final Hilbert--Schmidt destination. These are not transfer theorems, but they identify a recurring failure mode: separately sharp component bounds can destroy exactly the compatibility the endpoint needs.

### 4. Fixed-size closure is repeatedly giving way to a growth or conditioning law

Farey now has genuine growing-depth chains but no effective depth-to-scale law. Nyman has exact fixed-finite determinant repair with the raw crowding volume removed, but no uniform condition-number theorem as the divisor grows or conflates. Visual has expanded fixed-window dilution to a broad growing corridor and leaves only the transition to the full sinc-weighted shell range. The next progress in these lines is therefore a quantitative law in the growing parameter, not another fixed-size closure.
