# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The common RH-facing gap remains an independently source-forced estimate in the precise destination norm that can exclude each off-critical zero. Source reconstruction, auxiliary positivity, available operator witnesses and artificial spectral separation do not provide that estimate. No Mathia finding proves RH.

Two consequential frontiers have changed. [RE-259](robin_extremal/findings/RE-259-zero-density-removes-critical-robin-moment-boundary.md) settles the formerly open first-annulus triangular prime moment by stronger **classical** zero-density/zero-free input, even without CA selection. The Robin question now begins in the remote CA-selected mixed Mertens–Chebyshev tail. Conversely, [PL-461](prime_lattice/findings/PL-461-single-identity-residue-moment-zero-free-gate.md) and [PL-463](prime_lattice/findings/PL-463-empirically-centered-window-variance-zero-free-gate.md) show that proposed actual-prime upper-moment inputs already entail zero-free regions; fitting the observed mean weakens but does not remove that prerequisite. These concern different norms, intervals and arithmetic channels and are not transfers of one theorem.

The current canonical findings outrank older Mind frontiers where they differ. No adversarial sidecar is currently open; this is not evidence that every finding has completed independent review. RE-259 explicitly retains that review limitation. The [Atlas v1 metrics](graph/atlas/metrics.md) include the mod-5 prerequisite obstruction, but their selected episodes and older coverage model are derived context, not a complete audit of the current frontier or evidence of RH progress.

## Cross-line structure

**A boundary of one input is not a boundary of the source.** RE-259 removes RE-253's apparent critical signed-moment difficulty by changing to a stronger applicable classical estimate. PL-461/463 expose the opposite risk: a convenient covariance-scale upper bound can already contain a zero-exclusion theorem. Both require every sufficiently large real scale. The raw theorem has fixed `0<theta<=1/2`, and the empirical theorem fixed `0<theta<1`. The raw identity-residue premise at `theta=1/2` implies joint GRH modulo 5, while empirical variance at that exponent excludes only `Re s>3/4`; the latter reaches joint GRH only through a family of fixed exponents tending to one. These quantifiers cannot be replaced by one sparse scale sequence or an observed finite matrix. ANF's full oscillatory high-pass, FD-311's source-selected sliding-Möbius saving and MC's signed conductor correlation remain distinct unresolved estimates.

**Successful nuisance removal does not establish robustness to the nuisance model.** AF-572 shows that a bounded smooth departure can exactly absorb its vanishing source contrast even after exact-polynomial recovery succeeds. XF-199 instead exploits complex phase reversal to annihilate polynomial baselines under a fixed total Euclidean noise budget. The real derivative-order argument, noise norm and coordinate are different. The proposed [smooth-baseline Jacobi clue](xi_flow/clues/CLUE-bounded-smooth-baseline-jacobi-support-margin.md) asks for the destination-specific margin rather than importing AF's threshold. The earlier [common-baseline clue](xi_flow/clues/CLUE-common-baseline-quotient-jacobi-acquisition.md) is resolved by XF-198 and is not reopened.

**Exact signatures, raw spectra and normalized spectral laws are separate.** FD-331's exact joint signature determines an excursion permutation, yet FD-333 changes values by only `1/p` at identical six defect ranks while retaining order-one full-energy and fixed even-mode differences. VIS-460's inverse-prime probability separation concerns the different FD-329 pair, whose changed blocks occupy a vanishing fraction of ranks. Its conclusion cannot be extended to FD-333 without computing the separately normalized spectra; the [one-quantum spectral clue](visual_exploration/clues/CLUE-one-quantum-farey-normalized-spectral-separation.md) delivers that exact test. FD-335 also shows why a ratio of expected energies does not center each realization's spectral share: fixed-band null means are below one, although diverging-band shares tend to one in probability. Its [owning Farey clue](farey_discrepancy/clues/CLUE-farey-gap-order-bridge-suppression.md) retains the stronger-order-control question.

**Domain, local sign and normalization remain independent gates.** WP-439 repairs a star-domain obstruction without repairing the negative essential spectrum on a prime ray. PF-346 gives an exact normalized corridor obstruction whose transfer to the actual geometry is unstable under errors small only at the original energy scale. Neither global domain existence nor raw trace suppression supplies the destination sign/angle theorem. PC-444/445 additionally rule out specific measure/order changes as new arithmetic carriers. These are scoped obstructions, not equivalences between the operators or reasons to merge their lines.

## Highest-leverage unresolved questions

1. **Arithmetic estimates at the destination scale:** ANF's oscillatory high-pass, FD-311's selected sliding variance, MC's signed physical conductor correlation and PL's direct relative lower frame. State the first unsupported source implication before imposing a stronger moment theorem.
2. **Remote Robin control:** estimate RE-259's CA-selected integral from `p+H_dagger` to the established remote cutoff. The formerly proposed first-annulus saving has a classical resolution.
3. **Physical operator transport:** compare PF's globally shorted form at shorted-energy scale, produce a Weil sign mechanism outside WP-439's ray-compact class, and prove NB target occupation or WI off-line defect control from independent source data.
4. **Observation robustness:** resolve the two proposed calibration/spectral clues above while preserving their matched-control boundaries. Physical Xi access and genuine Farey arithmetic remain separate downstream obligations.
