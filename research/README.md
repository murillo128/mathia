# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The common RH-facing gap remains an independently source-forced estimate in the precise destination norm that can exclude each off-critical zero. Source reconstruction, auxiliary positivity, available operator witnesses and artificial spectral separation do not provide that estimate. No Mathia finding proves RH.

[MC-522](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-522-centered-dyadic-variance-recovers-all-reciprocal-zeta-poles.md) recovers every reciprocal-zeta pole from complete centered variance at every large dyadic block. Sparse coverage has a sharper current boundary: [MC-526](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-526-selected-mobius-variance-excludes-finite-exceptional-zero-clusters.md) excludes a finite nonempty exceptional zero family from the diagonal-order centered ceiling on **any unbounded scale set**, without a separately assumed reciprocal-growth bound or simple zeros. Infinite sparse exceptional families remain possible under that conclusion. [PL-470](prime_lattice/findings/PL-470-positive-source-strip-growth-and-the-infinite-pole-tail-gate.md) proves that the positive sparse control PL-469 necessarily fails uniform polynomial strip growth, and exposes the actual prime source's complete packet/tail certificate. Neither zero counting nor same-sign logarithmic-derivative residues supplies that certificate for an infinite family. The balanced Farey variance target remains **classically resolved under RH** by MC-520; independent continuation needs weaker hypotheses and justified carrying windows. RE-259 removes Robin's first annulus but leaves the CA-selected remote mixed tail open.

The current canonical findings outrank older Mind frontiers where they differ. No adversarial sidecar is currently open; this is not independent approval of the corpus. WI-002 has been withdrawn. [WI-456](weil_inertia/findings/WI-456-retracted-moment-transport-leaves-only-a-conditional-density-capstone.md) replaces its obsolete live-candidate assessment with a precise conditional moment-to-density theorem. The Yang–Yang arithmetic transport is retracted; neither model certificates nor the surviving conditional theorem restore it. Even a valid density-one theorem leaves individual exceptional zeros possible. The [Atlas v1 metrics](graph/atlas/metrics.md) include the AF-579/XF-207 query-domain extensions of the calibration boundaries in their selective telemetry. Their unchanged coverage model and older entrypoint narrative do not cover the whole current source frontier; portfolio judgments below rest on canonical evidence.

## Cross-line structure

**Selected variance excludes finite exceptions; infinite tails remain a separate arithmetic burden.** [MC-520](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-520-rh-mellin-variance-closes-conditional-farey-window-target.md) bounds the complete sliding Möbius moment by `X^(1+epsilon)H` under RH. Keeping the Mellin difference multiplier beats FD-311's balanced Farey lower bound, unlike separate endpoint estimates. The [resolved Farey handoff](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-farey-selected-sliding-mertens-variance.md) records this classical conditional result. Removing RH removes both that input and its carrying-shell localization unless separately justified. A centered estimate also needs its mean contribution restored before the uncentered Farey destination can use it.

[MC-522](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-522-centered-dyadic-variance-recovers-all-reciprocal-zeta-poles.md) and [PL-466](prime_lattice/findings/PL-466-dyadic-seam-weights-remove-the-variance-endpoint-loss.md) recover zeros in `Re s>1-theta/2` from complete centered diagonal-order variance at every sufficiently large dyadic block, for fixed `0<theta<1`. Seam filters and arithmetic pole sparsity restore the information lost by empirical centering; Möbius requires whole Laurent principal parts, including multiple poles with zero ordinary residue. The [Möbius transfer](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-dyadic-centered-mobius-pole-recovery.md) is resolved. Neither result supplies the variance estimate or permits arbitrary selected subsequences.

The sparse controls separate coverage from continuation. MC-523 passes a retained mesh with only `O(log J)` omitted indices through `J` but supplies no arithmetic continuation. PL-468's fixed-profile positive construction has a natural boundary. [MC-524](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-524-density-one-window-control-coexists-with-prescribed-sparse-poles.md) and [PL-469](prime_lattice/findings/PL-469-positive-sparse-block-sources-retain-negative-pole-residues.md) instead retain prescribed sparse poles on the more frequently omitted mesh `j_k=floor(k log k)`. PL-469 is positive and has negative unit residues, but lacks prime support, an Euler product, a completed functional equation and quantitative vertical control. Its [moderate-mesh handoff](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-variable-profile-sparse-poles-on-moderate-mesh.md) is resolved; the accepted [thin-mesh question](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-density-one-dyadic-variance-arithmetic-recovery.md) is distinct and open.

[MC-526](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-526-selected-mobius-variance-excludes-finite-exceptional-zero-clusters.md) removes the separate growth assumption for a **finite exceptional zeta family**. If finitely many but at least one zero lie in `Re s>sigma_*>1/2`, let `beta` be their largest real part and `m` the largest multiplicity on that line. Zero-free high disks give polynomial reciprocal growth; a mean-zero covariance bank gives `V_mu(X,H)>=c H^2 X^(2beta-1)(log X)^(2m-2)` for every sufficiently large real `X` and integer `1<=H<=h_0X`. Consequently, a diagonal-order epsilon-loss ceiling at `H=floor X^theta` on **any unbounded set** forces every half-plane `Re s>sigma>1-theta/2` to have either no zeros or infinitely many. The constants are not uniform in a growing packet, and infinite sparse exceptions remain unexcluded.

[PL-470](prime_lattice/findings/PL-470-positive-source-strip-growth-and-the-infinite-pole-tail-gate.md) proves that PL-469's pole-subtracted remainder violates every polynomial bound uniformly on each closed strip from `beta-1<sigma_0<beta` to `2`. For actual identity-residue primes, it supplies an absolutely convergent smoothed zero sum, with multiplicities, the trivial zero at 0 and the factor at 5 explicit. A finite packet's normalized singular-value margin must dominate the **complete residual on the same measured scales**. The fixed-test absolute tail is only `C_(N,f) X T^(1-N)log(2T)`; increasing the packet changes its Gram margin, derivative costs and test norms. The [accepted prime continuation clue](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-quantitative-continuation-pole-tail-variance.md) retains this unresolved estimate. The proposed [reciprocal-zeta handoff](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-reciprocal-zeta-selected-scale-pole-tail-certificate.md) asks which part transfers to Möbius, where full Laurent coefficients replace logarithmic-derivative multiplicities. No infinite-family recovery or actual tail cancellation is established.

**Closure can remove a source restriction without removing the RH obstruction.** [NB-354](nyman_beurling/findings/NB-354-finite-seed-real-dilation-spaces-have-an-exact-target-loss-classification.md) classifies the unrestricted real-dilation closure by classical Burnol/Beurling–Lax factorization. With `b=B(1)>0` for the Blaschke product of zeta zeros in `Re s>1/2`, two multiplicatively independent seeds generate the full continuous Nyman space, at squared target distance `1-b^2`. **Rare peaks need a window theorem and an arithmetic sampling theorem.** [WI-458](weil_inertia/findings/WI-458-euler-hankel-peaks-have-exponentially-small-height-density.md) brackets fixed submaximum Euler-Hankel peak densities on the scale `exp[-exp(C/epsilon)]`, taking height to infinity first. Tensorization prices frequencies up to `X^m`. At a fixed strict submaximum threshold, recurrent peak width gives `F_epsilon(H)>=min(1,b epsilon/H)`, making the same broad doubly exponential window scale necessary for an upper bound of this strength. This is not a finite-window lower-density law, a sharp-constant result, a maximum-gap estimate or control at actual zero ordinates. The separate mean bound is `mu_epsilon(r)+O(1/H)` on every window.

The [weak-prime crossover](visual_exploration/visualizations/euler-hankel-weak-prime-crossover.md) supplies a finite-model truncation warning under the correct shared-prime phase law. On `{2,4,3}`, attenuating the third coefficient by `eta` changes mean and variance only by `O(eta^2)`, but any fixed positive `eta` has near-maximum probability of order `t`, versus `sqrt(t)` when deleted. Its explicit crossover occurs at deficit `t` of order `eta`. This supporting portrait supplies no full-Euler shrinking-threshold law; it requires accuracy relative to the deficit threshold. The prior matched-moment and phase-fiber controls remain distinct checks on the phase law. Neither finite models nor Lebesgue-height rarity locate exceptional zeta zeros.
