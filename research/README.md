## Cross-line structure

**Sparse local defects can carry macroscopic spectral information.** FD-318 is stronger than a low-density heuristic: six nonintegrable index stitches preserve the *entire* directed Farey edge inventory yet alter quadratic discrepancy by order one and produce nonvanishing fixed even modes. Its artificial grids cannot disprove a theorem about genuine Farey sequences. However, VIS-032/VIS-034's reflection-and-gap null must be strengthened carefully: a low-mode residual cannot be credited to genuine arithmetic ordering while a stricter edge-preserving, six-stitch control produces it. The proposed destination-local Visual clue [[research/visual_exploration/clues/CLUE-farey-six-stitch-fixed-mode-null]] routes that precise comparison; it is a question, not transferred evidence.

**The destination's norm and observational quotient determine the price of information.** AF-557 separates edit detection from neighboring-prime identification despite exact calibrated inversion. PF-344 lowers the raw matrix scale without controlling the simultaneously shrinking shorted diagonals. PL-444 shows that new character-sector coordinates may simply repackage a correlation-gauge inequality. XF-186 demonstrates why one zero-frequency normalization row can invalidate a claimed unrestricted raw-noise transfer despite a sound normalized chart. These are distinct mathematical examples of an audit discipline, not one common theorem.

**Cancellation has to survive the next operation.** RE-240's coefficient-phase Wiener discount is real, but RE-243 prices its prediction error at the moving arc edge. MC-513's state participation is exact but leaves cross-state phase collisions and signed conductor alignment unresolved. WP-432's formal positive quadratic field cannot be interpreted as an RH-facing positive operator without closability. The program must test cancellation or positivity *after* the actual composition, normalization, domain and source-transport gates.

**Almost-all, asymptotic proportion and individual exclusion are different currencies.** ANF-190's height-exception set can contain every prescribed physical twist. WI-443/WI-444's global multiplicity proportions are compatible with isolated exceptional zeros. VIS-440's Haar directional crossing bound constrains a matched-phase quiet tail, not the arithmetic Gram selector. None supplies the pointwise, source-fixed witness needed for RH.
