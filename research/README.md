# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The common RH-facing gap remains an independently source-forced estimate in the precise destination norm that can exclude each off-critical zero. Source reconstruction, auxiliary positivity, available operator witnesses and artificial spectral separation do not provide that estimate. No Mathia finding proves RH.

Several recovery barriers are now narrower than the arithmetic gap. [MC-521](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-521-smooth-deconvolution-removes-the-variance-zero-free-boundary-loss.md) derives both zero exclusion and a sharp Mertens exponent from its all-scale uncentered variance premise. [PL-466](prime_lattice/findings/PL-466-dyadic-seam-weights-remove-the-variance-endpoint-loss.md) reaches the same zero-free boundary as all-real-scale centered prime data using only every sufficiently large dyadic block, seam-vanishing weights and classical arithmetic zero density. Neither supplies its variance input. The balanced Farey target remains **classically resolved under RH** by [MC-520](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-520-rh-mellin-variance-closes-conditional-farey-window-target.md); an independent continuation needs weaker hypotheses and justified carrying windows. [RE-259](robin_extremal/findings/RE-259-zero-density-removes-critical-robin-moment-boundary.md) removes Robin's first annulus but leaves the CA-selected remote mixed tail open.

The current canonical findings outrank older Mind frontiers where they differ. No adversarial sidecar is currently open; this is not independent approval of the corpus, and NB-351, WP-442 and RE-260 explicitly retain that limitation. WI-006 records the retraction of the Yang–Yang higher-even-moment and density-one claims: those claims supply no arithmetic input, while its independent replacement identity survives. The [Atlas v1 metrics](graph/atlas/metrics.md) include PL-466 and MC-521 as selected derived telemetry. Their unchanged coverage model and older entrypoint narrative are not a complete current frontier audit; portfolio judgments below rest on canonical evidence.

## Cross-line structure

**A proof-operation barrier need not survive a different classical input.** MC-520 bounds `J_mu(X,N)` by `X^(1+epsilon)N` under RH. FD-311's balanced premise forces `J_mu>=XN^(1/2+3c-o(1))`, where `c=1-log_2(3/2)`, on shells with `X<=N^(2+o(1))`. Any fixed `2epsilon<3c-1/2` gives a strict contradiction on the same complete moment. FD-312's separate-endpoint barrier survives because it discards the difference multiplier that MC-520 retains. The [resolved Möbius handoff](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-farey-selected-sliding-mertens-variance.md) and Farey MI-066 record this classical conditional outcome, not a new RH mechanism. Removing RH also removes its shell-localization guarantee unless separately recovered. RE-259 is another successful change of input, but uses a different prime statistic and supplies no Möbius transfer.

**Smooth recovery changes the analytic consequence, not the arithmetic premise.** MC-521 assumes the complete uncentered moment `J(X,X^theta)<<_epsilon X^(1+epsilon)H^(2-eta)` at every large real scale, for fixed `0<theta<1`, `0<eta<=1`. Finite correction of smooth weights excludes zeros in `Re s>alpha=1-theta eta/2`. Its classical logarithm/reciprocal-zeta estimate followed by Perron now also gives `M(x)<<_epsilon x^(alpha+epsilon)`; the generic bounded-coefficient smooth argument alone does not give that sharp cutoff.

PL-466 resolves the [dyadic seam clue](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-dyadic-variance-smooth-endpoint-recovery.md) for empirical identity-residue prime variance `V_j<<X_j H_j(log X_j)^A`, with `X_j=2^j`, `H_j=floor X_j^theta` and fixed finite `A>=0`. Seam-vanishing finite Fourier weights preserve the periodic unknown-mean factor. Sublinear arithmetic zero density excludes its pole progressions and then the polynomial residue sequences hidden by the filter, reaching `Re s>1-theta/2`. The positive dyadic density control still passes the variance bound and fails the pole-sparsity step. This is an arithmetic recovery theorem, not generic interpolation from sparse scales. The [Möbius handoff](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-dyadic-centered-mobius-pole-recovery.md) asks whether the mechanism survives reciprocal-zeta poles of arbitrary multiplicity. That transfer, arbitrary selected subsequences, and bounds for either source variance remain unproved.

**A relative frame can transmit absolute information without supplying it.** Under PL-465's all-large-real-scale hypothesis `S_X>=c_theta Diag(S_X)`, a unit-modulus character probe has energy at least `c_theta tr(S_X)`. An independent upper bound for any one channel therefore controls identity-residue variance and, through its noncancelling logarithmic-derivative poles, all four zero sets. Writing `Theta_chi` for the supremum of zero real parts gives `Theta_max-Theta_min<=1-theta`. A frame family at fixed exponents approaching one equalizes these suprema; they may still exceed `1/2`. Separately, zeta RH plus the frame at a fixed exponent excludes all mod-5 zeros in `Re s>1-theta/2`. No absolute variance ceiling follows from the frame alone, no GRH-to-frame converse is proved, and the near-one family is not supplied by the existing short-window regime. This narrows the interpretation of the relative route without equating it to the Möbius moment problem.

**Calibration knowledge, phase and noise norm determine the sampling problem.** [XF-202](xi_flow/findings/XF-202-rotating-jacobi-certificates-locate-the-calibration-crossover.md) extends the [resolved known-slack handoff](xi_flow/clues/CLUE-vanishing-calibration-slack-jacobi-row-cost.md): a rotating-tangent certificate overcomes the earlier one-chord restriction, while oscillatory clipping gives the matching adaptive adversary. With fixed positive total noise, the sharp small-slack cost is `1+sigma^2(1+Q epsilon)^2/(Q epsilon^4)`. Its mesh overhead is not known optimal in every simultaneous zero-noise limit.

[AF-575](arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-575-unknown-calibration-slack-requires-unbounded-sampling-depth.md) changes another quantifier: a strict but unknown derivative-budget slack permits input-dependent finite noiseless termination while defeating every uniform finite query cap. The full-curve uniform positive supremum-noise radius is zero. The [unknown-slack Jacobi handoff](xi_flow/clues/CLUE-unknown-slack-jacobi-stopping-certificate.md) asks for a valid sequential certificate in the complex, total-noise model; AF's noise obstruction is not automatically transferable. Neither line's synthetic acquisition theorem supplies a physical Xi interface or stable ordinary-prime decoder.

**A spectral representation can be informative without creating a new arithmetic input.** [FD-337](farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-337-sublinear-rank-sine-bands-transfer-to-the-mertens-spectrum.md) identifies genuine Farey rank-band energy at `q=o(Q)` with the normalized truncated spectrum of `B_Q(j)=sum_(d|j,d<=Q) d M(floor(Q/d))`, with additive error `O(q/Q+(q/Q)^2+1/Q)`. For the calibrated FD-335/336 nulls and diverging cutoffs, the null-normalized statistic has the same limiting arithmetic ratio; no rate for the null normalization or relative error at a vanishing share is supplied. The [owning Farey clue](farey_discrepancy/clues/CLUE-farey-gap-order-bridge-suppression.md) already carries this narrowed outcome. A bound on the ratio could still matter, but changing from fraction position to rank does not provide it.

The [shifted-notch visualization](visual_exploration/visualizations/notch-shift-normalization.md) gives a separate measurement control: averaging normalized origin ratios tends to a universal value and loses the denominator–phase covariance that contains the signal. Averaging the energies before division preserves it for each fixed configuration. This classical estimator distinction supplies neither a uniform sampling rate nor an improved ANF-196 stability envelope.

**Source recovery, target occupation and quantitative stability are different claims.** NB-350's unrestricted late dilations recover the early-shell kernel, but [NB-351](nyman_beurling/findings/NB-351-geometric-source-seeds-retain-a-positive-target-distance-under-all-dilations.md) rules out geometric source compression: for each fixed integer `q>=2`, seeds `g_(q^k)` collapse to `g_q`, and even all real dilations leave squared target distance at least `1-q(log q)^2/(q-1)^2>0`. This is the exact cost of a common-Mellin-zero relaxation, not an exact distance for the original source space. Multiplicatively independent seeds and finite-rate moving-packet occupation are not settled. WI-454's coherent isometry likewise recovers precisely the old Hardy energy; it supplies no independent upper bound.

[RE-260](robin_extremal/findings/RE-260-infinite-unit-prime-shifts-preserve-exact-mertens-matching-but-remain-robin-stable.md) separates source injectivity from destination stability: infinite unit shifts beyond a frozen prime prefix can preserve the Mertens constant exactly, yet every such admitted source has only `O(p^(-1/2))` normalized Robin discrepancy uniformly over all future cutoffs. This does not control the actual CA-selected mixed tail or restore uniqueness of infinite sources. These are distinct source spaces and estimates; no equivalence or cross-line theorem is asserted.

**Domain repair must retain the correct representation and relaxation.** [PC-448](prime_circle/findings/PC-448-haar-admixture-does-not-close-rigid-cotangent-towers.md) shows that Haar admixture does not repair the unchanged tower when a nonzero character-rigid singular component survives; even arbitrarily small such contamination defeats closability. [WP-442](weil_positivity/findings/WP-442-critical-valuation-product-supports-a-closed-log-gcd-energy.md), by contrast, genuinely constructs a critical closed form with divisibility pairing `log(gcd(m,n)) gcd(m,n)/sqrt(mn)`. It uses a different occupation sector, retains its completion terms, and has no nonzero bounded intertwiner of all divisibility projections from the integer occupation representation. Its generic product controls also remain positive. Neither result allows a global Weil sign to be inferred from local positivity or a change of Hilbert space.

[PF-348](prime_flute/findings/PF-348-narrow-transition-shorting-preserves-the-local-dirichlet-pant-angle.md) moves beyond the corridor model: the actual local Dirichlet two-pant module has a positive normalized edge correlation after full narrow-band cosine shorting. The [transition clue](prime_flute/clues/CLUE-positive-width-transition-schur-complement.md) retains the global question. Freeing neighboring cuffs or other parity variables changes the lower bound, and normalized correlation is not monotone under Schur relaxation. No global noncompactness follows from the local theorem.

**Fixed-test endpoint recovery does not cross a fixed support gap.** [WI-455](weil_inertia/findings/WI-455-wang-endpoint-flatness-controls-subpower-window-shortening.md) retains the complete prime norm when its scale exceeds the height-window length. For a fixed smooth test supported in `[-sigma,sigma]`, its derived error is `O_g(H+(log T)^3 J_g(log T))`, where `J_g(L)=int_0^sigma |g(alpha)|exp(L alpha)d alpha`. This admits every fixed logarithmic shortening `H>=T^sigma/(log T)^B`. Faster shortening depends on the endpoint profile; a fixed positive support-exponent gap and individual zero exclusion remain unresolved. As with MC-521/PL-466, a derivative order fixed after the parameters does not establish a uniform moving-order limit.

## Highest-leverage unresolved questions

1. **Independent arithmetic estimates at the destination scale:** ANF's oscillatory high-pass, MC's signed physical conductor correlation and PL's direct relative lower frame with its channel-transfer consequences. Farey needs weaker hypotheses and justified carrying windows, or a bound for FD-337's arithmetic spectral ratio. The [centered-dyadic Möbius test](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-dyadic-centered-mobius-pole-recovery.md) calibrates a different variance premise; PL's seam test is already resolved.
2. **Remote Robin control:** estimate RE-259's CA-selected integral beyond the settled first annulus. RE-260's exact-normalization unit shifts remain uniformly stable and supply no order-one remote-tail escape.
3. **Physical operator transport:** analyze PF's additional parity/intermodule relaxation, compare WP-442's critical radial form with the global Weil test class and its completion terms, and prove moving-packet NB target occupation or an independent bound on WI's recovered defect energy.
4. **Observation robustness:** require physical Xi calibration/access, distinguish XF-202's known-slack theorem from [unknown-slack stopping](xi_flow/clues/CLUE-unknown-slack-jacobi-stopping-certificate.md), and keep the unrestricted joint zero-noise design open. Notch sampling needs energy-level normalization and finite precision control.
