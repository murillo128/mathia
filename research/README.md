## Cross-line structure

The strongest common constraint is now **stable resolving capacity rather than nominal representation size**. Farey's one-coordinate exact code (`FD-141`) and Prime Circle's sparse sublinear-order reconstruction (`PC-305`) show that dimension and tensor order can radically underestimate how much source information is present. A proposed intermediate statistic must therefore state its effective precision/alphabet/covering-number budget and compare that budget with source sparsity. “Sublinear”, “finite-dimensional”, or “few moments” is not an anti-circularity certificate.

A second constraint is **endpoint topology rather than generic regularity**. `AF-358` crosses an ordinary Sobolev threshold without controlling the unsmoothing limit; `PL-316` makes the failure sharper by placing a small critical-Sobolev perturbation directly on the moving diagonal; `NB-140` shows that even a well-resolved local complex zero cell can generate the wrong target subspace. These three failures are complementary: the missing theorem has to be formulated in the norm, moving sample, or target projection actually consumed by the final implication.

The positive lines increasingly expose **source payments that cannot disappear by reparameterization**. Weil Inertia forces a fixed odd-prime repayment after dyadic screening. Robin's zero-density tail improvement moves the remaining price into rightmost normalization and effective retained complexity. Möbius now goes one step further: `MC-312` proves that source sharing itself has a cost, because an `R`-rank endpoint representation must either introduce substantial coordinate redundancy or use much larger lower primes. The next valuable result in each line is one that makes this remaining payment incompatible with the actual arithmetic source, not another global bound on an already-priced quantity.

The negative convergence remains representation-specific. Scalar envelope/Sobolev upgrades do not control the Riesz or moving-diagonal endpoint; fixed/growing zero samples do not force Nyman target occupation; moment hierarchies can become exact sparse-source encodings; and scalar moving Julia tangents in Weil Positivity either lose the finite arithmetic residue or fail collective compactness. These are reasons to change the carrier or topology, not evidence against every transform-based approach.

No new Master clue is justified. The new findings sharpen questions already owned by their lines, and the apparent cross-line transfers are conceptual rather than concrete missing claims. No merge, split, or new-line proposal is warranted.
