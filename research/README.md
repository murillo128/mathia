## Cross-line structure

Arithmetic Fidelity, Farey, Möbius, and Nyman now expose four versions of the same accounting failure. A source property can be real but useless because the destination smooths it away (`AF-347`), because its global cardinality hides insufficient active-scale density (`FD-128`), because its interaction order stays bounded while the endpoint parity order grows (`MC-298`), or because exact finite-dimensional identifiability has an arbitrarily bad inverse modulus (`NB-127`). The common gate is no longer simply “more source structure”: the resource must scale at the endpoint's order/density/resolution and remain visible after composition.

Prime Circle and Visual remain the cleanest audit pair for representation fidelity. `PC-298` proves that one-profile diffuse compression is universal even through a quantitative range of shrinking pseudospectral resolutions; pushing another statistic through the same compression is unlikely to recover information above its explicit defect scale. `VIS-226` closes the opposite kind of reduction: an elementary binary certificate, the exact torus objective and the SDP all have the same asymptotic order. The useful simplification is the one that preserves the destination quantity strongly enough that a positive or negative conclusion transfers back without ambiguity.

Prime Lattice is now the clearest positive endpoint-localization advance. `PL-311--PL-313` identify where the missing small-`s` information lives, prove the limiting outer cokernel is one-dimensional, and show that the physical corner reaches exactly that direction through one weighted scalar at the same first nontrivial scale as the completed source. The remaining gap is therefore a real matched-asymptotic/source theorem, not another search for hidden outer Fredholm conditions. Robin supplies a complementary negative localization: its strong-atom escape has been reduced to a very specific actual-zero pair geometry, leaving only that geometry or the diffuse-cloud alternative.

Möbius also sharpens the distinction between **individual source cost** and **collective realizability**. `MC-299` makes the cheap sector much smaller if an exceptional mode exists, but it does not bound the dimension or occupancy of the expensive quotient. That is exactly the sort of step that should not be mistaken for endpoint closure: pushing every dangerous direction past a cost threshold matters only if the source cannot collectively pay that threshold often enough.

The existing global fidelity/realization clue and `MI-008`, `MI-009`, `MI-012`, `MI-013`, `MI-015`, and `MI-018` already cover the reusable program-level lessons about stable transport, source realization, destination sensitivity, endpoint-faithful relaxations, and source complexity surviving composition.
