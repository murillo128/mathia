## Global picture

[FD-346](farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-346-classical-gap-correlations-separate-high-band-farey-transport.md) closes the projected high-band Farey comparator through classical fixed-gap correlations; its alternating-sector split remains open. Other gains have different destinations: NB-367 measures exceptional third-seed failures under a stronger Diophantine hypothesis; PC-465 classifies moving-center operator limits; PF-363 supplies a coupled actual-pant lower certificate. WP-459 closes correlated sign-oriented repairs on domains containing the natural orbit, while leaving domains omitting it and weaker margins outside the theorem. These results clarify independent proof obligations rather than furnish a common missing RH theorem.

WI-002 remains withdrawn, and [WI-456](weil_inertia/findings/WI-456-retracted-moment-transport-leaves-only-a-conditional-density-capstone.md) retains only a conditional moment-to-density theorem after retraction of the Yang–Yang arithmetic transport. Their selected episode rates and older Atlas/frontier projections are stale as a whole-portfolio assessment; they are not used to infer saturation or allocation. ## Cross-line structure

**Observation design and arithmetic strength remain separate.** The common recovery operator does not identify the Möbius and mod-5 zero sets. MC-533/PL-478 use deterministic square-root grids; MC-536/PL-481 use cube-root stratification. [MC-537](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-537-small-sampled-energy-permits-quarter-power-mobius-observations.md) and [PL-482](prime_lattice/findings/PL-482-small-empirical-energy-permits-quarter-power-prime-observations.md) instead exploit small observed energy with `2 ceil(X^(1/4))` independent uniform draws with replacement per channel. With `V` the average squared deviation from each channel's sample mean, retaining repeated draws, pairwise differences and sampled extrema bound population mean absolute dispersion by `O(sqrt(V)+sqrt(X)(log X)^2)` for Möbius and `O(sqrt(V)+sqrt(X)(log X)^3)` for primes. The prime logarithm is the jump price `log(3X)`.

Both probability-one events control every bounded mean-zero pairing simultaneously at all sufficiently large integer scales. For lengths `floor(X/20)` and its rounded `sqrt(2)` multiple, existence of one common unbounded scale set, fixed before epsilon, with `V_i<<_epsilon X^(1+epsilon)` is equivalent to the respective zero hypothesis. The set and constants may depend on the outcome; the source precedes the draws. Prime logarithmic-derivative pole weights stay positive and carry no reciprocal-zeta multiplicity logarithm; the prime proof also retains its `O(log X)` rounding error and separate pointwise GRH converse. The [quarter-power handoff](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-small-energy-quarter-power-prime-sampling.md) is resolved.

[MC-538](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-538-one-fixed-multiplicative-source-defeats-subquarter-random-observations.md) fixes a deterministic multiplicative `f`, `|f|=mu^2`, before sampling. For `0<=alpha<1/4` and `3/4<kappa<1-alpha`, its lacunary-scale sampled energies satisfy square-root-scale ceilings almost surely while a fixed smooth target has size at least `X^(2kappa-1)/log^2 X`. [PL-483](prime_lattice/findings/PL-483-fixed-prime-supported-sources-evade-subquarter-uniform-observations.md) instead fixes two positive sources between one-half and three-halves of the canonical mod-5 Mangoldt source, modifying only actual primes and preserving PNT. Both constructions hide primitive area from window-endpoint observations; their source classes and conclusions are different. Neither retains the actual arithmetic pole identity or bounds an estimator accumulating other scales.

[MC-539](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-539-two-random-starts-detect-mobius-poles-on-fourth-power-scales.md) changes the scale quantifier.
