# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The common RH-facing gap remains an independently source-forced estimate in the precise norm and parameter regime used by the destination. Reconstruction, auxiliary positivity and existence of a zero-sensitive test do not supply that estimate. No current Mathia finding proves RH.

The current evidence separates three questions that finite constructions can obscure: whether a target is approximable, what resources actual approximants require, and whether an approximation retains the claimed arithmetic property. Nyman--Beurling now has independent necessary column, coefficient and range budgets. Analytic Frontier has a positive width-uniform finite-cardinality approximation theorem for its real cubic-notch floor. Prime Circle has an exact compatible-selection obstruction invisible to every finite hole test. Prime Flute's known scalar escape fails after normalization by actual positive-shift energy. These are distinct results, not a universal obstruction to finite methods.

WI-002 remains withdrawn; [WI-456](weil_inertia/findings/WI-456-retracted-moment-transport-leaves-only-a-conditional-density-capstone.md) retains only a conditional moment-to-density theorem after retraction of the proposed arithmetic transport. No review sidecar is currently open, which is not independent approval of the findings. The [Atlas v1 metrics](graph/atlas/metrics.md) and [frontier telemetry](graph/atlas/telemetry/frontier-summary.md) include the specified PF-370 energy obstruction, but their Nyman range interpretation still gives only the weaker power certificate and does not incorporate NB-373's separate count bound. The Atlas entrypoint also has an older source window. Treat those projections as incomplete for this synthesis; their selective episode rates do not establish whole-portfolio saturation. Canonical evidence controls the recommendations below.

## Cross-line structure

**Observation design and a full-source signal remain separate.** [MC-539](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-539-two-random-starts-detect-mobius-poles-on-fourth-power-scales.md) and [PL-484](prime_lattice/findings/PL-484-two-random-starts-detect-prime-poles-on-fourth-power-scales.md) use two random starts per channel at every sufficiently late prescribed fourth-power scale, with independence across scales. The respective energy ceilings are RH- and joint mod-5 GRH-equivalent; neither is established. [PL-485](prime_lattice/findings/PL-485-prime-supported-perturbations-hide-every-scale-dispersion.md) constructs positive prime-supported reweightings with leading PNT, large every-block **perturbation** dispersion and eventually baseline-identical samples on faster schedules. The baseline energy is unknown. [MC-544](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-544-fixed-character-backgrounds-cancel-balanced-prime-replicas.md) instead cancels even narrow selected-prime targets inside a fixed square-free multiplicative character source whose full prefix is `O(sqrt(x))`.

[MC-545](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-545-balanced-character-swaps-force-a-full-source-singularity.md) changes those prime signs on balanced packets at every large dyadic scale, for fixed `3/4<kappa<1`. Its exact Euler quotient includes all composites and has a nonmeromorphic real singularity at `beta=2kappa-1`, with a fractional radial exponent. Thus its full prefix is not `O(x^(beta-delta))` for any `delta>0`, despite finite ordinary pretentious distance from the character control. This is classical-mechanism, construction-specific evidence. A failure of a global power bound supplies neither every-block window dispersion nor sparse-sample upper tails; the isolated meromorphic-pole certificate cannot simply be reused. The [accepted dense multiplicative clue](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-dense-multiplicative-full-source-dispersion.md) already carries the prime-to-multiplicative transfer and this exact residual. Farey's complete **uncentered** moment and justified carrying-shell range remain separate obligations, as [MC-520](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-520-rh-mellin-variance-closes-conditional-farey-window-target.md) makes explicit.

**Physical synthesis is not the same as selecting abstract components.** [NB-370](nyman_beurling/findings/NB-370-adaptive-lattice-selection-removes-the-three-orbit-cutoff-logarithm.md) removes the logarithm from the adaptive lattice component cost under its simultaneous bad-approximation hypothesis. Its vectors still live in full closed orbits. For actual sums of `J_M` generators `A_a g_v` approximating the projected target at error `O(M^(-1/2))`, NB-371--373 require

`J_M=Omega(M),  sum|c_nu|=Omega_rho(M^m),  log max(a_nu v_nu)=Omega(M)`.

Here `m` is the multiplicity of any fixed critical-line zero. The count bound permits unrestricted coefficients and frequencies: the Mellin numerator has at most `2J_M` exponential terms, and the classical interior norm inequality prevents compensation of the local zeta zero at squared error smaller than `c/J_M`. The range bound transfers classical Burnol duals to the projected target. These are necessary restrictions, not a jointly attainable rate. They bound excess above the Burnol floor without RH and do not identify physical range with the `M log M` lattice search horizon.

Prime Flute supplies a complementary normalization audit. [PF-369](prime_flute/findings/PF-369-nearly-equal-cuff-lattices-hide-broad-band-residual-mass-beyond-actual-windows.md) has two exact cuff traces whose difference escapes the prescribed window despite individual concentration. For that specified pair, [PF-370](prime_flute/findings/PF-370-positive-shift-trace-cost-dominates-the-two-cuff-escape-construction.md) proves `E_pant>=c sL^2/delta^2` and `H[f'-f]<=C L^2 exp(-L/2)`, where `s~4 exp(-L/2)` and `0<|delta|=o(L^2 exp(-L/2))`. Consequently `s^(-1)H[f'-f]/E_pant ->0`. The classical positive-shift interval DtN identity charges the large common amplitude even where the difference cancels. This also holds for both real components and conditionally on prime realization of the periods. It closes this witness's scalar-to-energy transfer, not the arbitrary-trace inequality or completed angle. The [accepted transition clue](prime_flute/clues/CLUE-positive-width-transition-schur-complement.md) and [current local frontier](prime_flute/mind/RESEARCH_LINES.md) already own those tests. [PF-366](prime_flute/findings/PF-366-shorting-a-small-positive-width-low-band-leaves-essential-angle-one.md)'s sufficiently narrow-band actual-angle obstruction remains intact. NB's fixed-target count theorem is not a theorem about this homogeneous ratio.

**Finite approximation can succeed without exact attainment or exact selection.** For fixed `lambda>0`, `a=lambda eta^3`, [ANF-214](analytic_frontier/findings/ANF-214-cubic-notch-floors-have-width-uniform-finite-cardinality-approximations.md) gives a real configuration of cardinality `O_lambda(R)` and span at most `R/eta` within `O_lambda(eta^4(1+log R)/R)` of the unrestricted floor, uniformly at sufficiently small widths. Its excess-paid occupation bound and one-sided localization use real pair positivity; simple/double multiplicities and the local occupation cap from [ANF-213](analytic_frontier/findings/ANF-213-small-real-notch-minima-reduce-exactly-to-simple-and-double-sites.md) can be retained. Cardinality escape therefore cannot hide a fixed normalized improvement from all sufficiently large finite tests. The cap grows with accuracy, and neither a limit coefficient, exact finite attainment, complex-site theorem nor source-locked estimate follows.

By contrast, [PC-473](prime_circle/findings/PC-473-compatible-root-selection-has-no-finite-hole-certificate.md) constructs a rooted admissible support for which every finite set of holes can be excluded while preserving the entire support, yet no phase excludes all holes. One hole with infinitely many available primes conflicts with countably many forced choices. Compatible supersets converge pointwise and their Cauchy operators converge strongly. If every hole has a finite available-prime set, finite consistency **does** suffice by compactness; if all have infinite sets, the earlier Baire sufficient condition applies. The mixed case is the remaining exact-image problem. Neither topology selects a canonical arithmetic phase. ANF's quantitative approximation theorem and PC's exact-selection failure do not contradict one another or justify a shared compactness claim.

**Farey sampling must retain rank values across separated cells.** [FD-348](farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-348-small-denominator-half-period-pairs-are-exact-rational-translates.md) reduces small-denominator pairs, under its strict small-product condition, to the triangular rank count `sum_u max(0,2-|r-D_Q(u)|)` on antipodal rational probes. [FD-350](farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-350-rational-probe-plateaus-have-a-sharp-spatial-sampling-cost.md) proves that their full genuine plateaus have total length asymptotic to `6t/(pi^2 Q)` and optimal positive sampling constant asymptotic to `Qt/2`, for `t->infinity`, `t=o(Q)`. On admissible small-product panels, the union indicator has spatial mass `O(1/t)` but discrepancy `Theta(t^2)`. It preserves the partition and symmetries while discarding equal-value linkage between cells with the same rank defect. It therefore refutes that relaxation, not the actual triangular test.

The [endpoint-pairing visualization](visual_exploration/visualizations/farey-quadrature-endpoint-pairing.md) retains the actual rank levels and exactly groups discrepancy as `2 sum_I(k_I-n|I|)`. Its finite panels show that pairing each interval's endpoints removes most of the absolute-jump cost; the equal-grid control also shows this effect. The bound still contains the unknown probe tally. Reflection pairs reinforce rather than cancel the signed error. This supporting exploration supplies no uniform occupancy estimate. The [accepted sampling clue](farey_discrepancy/clues/CLUE-half-arc-rational-probe-sampling-discrepancy.md) already records the partition obstruction and owns the surviving actual-value test. [FD-349](farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-349-denominator-parity-forces-a-half-period-gap-correlation-deficit.md)'s strict correlation deficit is genuine-source information, but does not prove decorrelation or sector equipartition.

**Sparse completion and fine-scale sampling have different limits.** [WP-459](weil_positivity/findings/WP-459-analytic-exponential-separators-rule-out-orbit-domain-block-orientation.md) excludes nonzero closable covariant forms with uniform variance-scale signed margins on every late block when the domain contains the natural vacuum orbit. [WP-460](weil_positivity/findings/WP-460-sparse-closed-diagonal-traces-admit-summable-prime-block-repairs.md) does not contradict it: a chosen unbounded closed diagonal trace on a sparse prime panel makes block norms `O(j 2^(-j/2))`, with arbitrarily small total scalar repair. The sparse panel has finite reciprocal-prime mass, enabling a nonzero common wandering space; all-prime covariance does not supply estimates on omitted blocks. Each uncorrected block still has both signs, and the phase correlations are chosen rather than source-forced. The [completion clue](weil_positivity/clues/CLUE-bost-connes-affiliation-conditioning-boundary.md) retains exact sign, domain and arithmetic-provenance gates.

For Weil Inertia at fixed safe parameters `sigma>1,r>0`, [WI-470](weil_inertia/findings/WI-470-three-prime-curvature-gives-finite-euler-correction-at-every-safe-line.md)'s finite signed correction measure and WI-472's fixed Gevrey formula coexist with WI-473's exact residual total variation `2 log T+O(1)`. Atomic-versus-diffuse sampling precludes the first correction for all continuous tests but does not refute a specified threshold/BV test. [WI-471](weil_inertia/findings/WI-471-full-euler-response-has-no-unrestricted-smooth-wiener-calculus.md)'s separate smooth Wiener obstruction concerns coefficient certification. None of these results turns density-one control into elimination of an individual off-line defect.

**Rational flexibility does not supply integer prime realizations.** [RE-276](robin_extremal/findings/RE-276-affine-direction-count-forbids-all-balanced-polynomial-euler-repairs.md) forbids nontrivial balanced universal polynomial Euler repairs of nonparallel affine deletion forms. [RE-277](robin_extremal/findings/RE-277-fixed-rational-balanced-repairs-have-sparse-integral-parameter-sets.md) shows that each fixed nontrivial rational repair has only `O_epsilon(N^(d-1+epsilon))` integral parameters in a `d`-dimensional box; in one dimension there are finitely many. This loses almost a power of the ambient affine prime-tuple count, but the two-variable control can be infinite and Zariski dense, and the upper bound still exceeds a Robin-critical repair budget. Sparse divisibility-conditioned prime supply remains a meaningful question. Changing formulas with scale, parallel forms and pointwise repairs are outside the fixed-form theorem.

AF-593's rational finite-jet approximation neither supplies that integer realization nor solves its own exact rational first-jet question. Robin's Euler-factor identity and Arithmetic Fidelity's reciprocal mass/logarithmic moment are different constraints. AF's existing [exact-rational handoff](arithmetic_fidelity/clues/CLUE-rational-unit-weight-first-jet-rigidity.md) must not be replaced by an approximate-matching assertion. Neither synthetic source construction supplies an actual CA-selected signed-tail estimate.
