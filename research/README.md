## Cross-line structure

**The main losses now occur after local success.** Arithmetic Fidelity can acquire a finite prefix with linear sample count yet retain a clock gauge; Nyman has full finite-window rank yet target leakage can ride a finite-rank forward-memory channel; Prime Lattice can certify every strictly positive fixed window in principle yet still lacks an aperture-uniform source margin. “We can reconstruct/certify the finite object” is therefore increasingly a solved subproblem, not evidence that the target survives the limit.

**The signed quantity must survive the exact transformation that precedes the target.** `MC-236` shows that a dense model can preserve each sign-specific mass while the one-sided transference machinery discards the comparison needed for near-balance. `WI-251` consumes a different signed functional—the radial von-Mangoldt flux of one forced first mode—and `WI-252` prevents replacing that problem by generic positivity. These source families are not identified, but both lines now demand a signed estimate in the exact target-selected family rather than qualitative sign information upstream.

**Classical geometry continues to absorb more elaborate representation effects.** `PC-271` upgrades the matched control from orbit closure to dual-lattice resolution geometry; `VIS-180` upgrades it again at the linear-support boundary by requiring local frequency crowding to be matched. The useful question is no longer whether an observable stops looking Haar at some complexity threshold, but whether the residual remains after matching the geometry that caused the failure of Haar approximation.

**Local abundance and capacity are no substitute for global assembly.** Farey can create polynomial packets yet lose the gain to the physical horizon. Robin can nearly exhaust bounded quiet architectures yet still needs the aggregate structure of unavoidable nonquiet interruptions. Prime Flute can price arbitrary scalar attenuation exactly while the complete Schur return may redistribute it. Future advances in these lines must alter the global denominator/assembly/return law rather than add another local witness already known to exist.

**Critical topology can erase the arithmetic channel itself.** `WP-267` is stronger than a large condition number: the standard closed-linear-relation completion makes the first prime layer pure multivalued freedom, so its canonical regular part loses both the critical first-layer norm and mixed-prime coupling. Together with `WP-266`, this makes topology part of the source claim, not a technical cleanup after the arithmetic has been encoded.
