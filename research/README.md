# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia has seven source-backed research lines: `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `weil_inertia`, and `weil_positivity`. The strongest common audit remains

`retained source information -> equivalence-compatible target/provenance -> quantitative information budget -> matched-control/source specificity -> stable realization -> global/sign assembly`.

The newest evidence makes the separations in that chain sharper. Arithmetic Fidelity shows that global safe-envelope existence can be trivial while a provenance-constrained lift is unstable under arbitrarily small ambient renorming (`AF-060`), so the admissible fiber/base/marking is part of the claim rather than presentation metadata. Möbius Cancellation still lacks a polynomial local-to-global bridge: `MC-009` records a potentially weaker mean-absolute Mertens endpoint, but `MC-010`--`MC-011` repair only specific defects in the external argument and do not complete its audit. Prime Circle's finite resultant deformation route now closes through global projective motion, finite differential response, and independent vertex motion: `PC-126`--`PC-128` reduce these respectively to collision hyperplanes, a classical arrangement master function, and the already studied inverse-square chord Laplacian. Prime Flute's canonical connected bottom Ruelle product reaches `s=0` with a finite nonzero value and a logarithmic cusp rather than a zero or pole (`PF-161`).

Prime Lattice now separates three different phenomena: the concrete regularized prime-shift Hamiltonian remains a scalar Fourier multiplier without the required discrete spectral mechanism (`PL-107`); a source-natural lattice coherence observable can genuinely force zeta zeros, but near the Euler/pole edge and through the scalar energy `log n` (`PL-108`); and classical prime-axis cancellation criteria already give RH-equivalent readouts, including Beurling-generalized analogues, without mixed exponent-lattice geometry (`PL-109`). Weil Inertia closes the generic prime-pair rank branch much further: extensive defects have vanishing dyadic cumulative coherence (`WI-094`), are confined to low-denominator boundary resonances (`WI-095`), and the residual defect is exactly the free-cycle count minus the zero-mean relation (`WI-096`). Weil Positivity shows that nonlinear selection is algebraically possible but does not solve the sign problem: Gibbs exponentiation gives an exact sparse logarithmic selector while critical entropy/flow energies diverge (`WP-103`), and the natural cover-Dirichlet Jensen route yields either the wrong extensive harmonic trace density (`WP-104`) or exact `log n` only at the previously known singular endpoint (`WP-105`).

The current canonical corpus excludes withdrawn `AF-058` and `WI-067`; the portfolio uses their surviving corrected successors, including `AF-060` and `WI-068`. `PL-107` remains current after its review closure. No unresolved review sidecar currently blocks use of the canonical findings summarized here. Graph/Atlas structure is used only for navigation, and prior-art coverage only for contextual saturation; neither is treated as theorem evidence.

The strategic pattern is therefore narrower than a shared mathematical mechanism. Exact representations, RH-equivalent readouts, positive carriers, large algebraic defects, and familiar spectral boundaries can all be real while failing at a later and different gate. The highest-value work is at the first source-specific residual left after the strongest matched control, with target semantics, quantitative transfer, realization category, and final global sign fixed independently of the desired zero set.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Representation or readout success is not mechanism success.** `AF-060` distinguishes global existence from provenance-constrained realization; `PL-108`--`PL-109` distinguish genuine zero forcing or RH-equivalent readout from a critical-line-generating geometry; `WP-103` distinguishes an exact sparse nonlinear selector from an independent sign theorem.

2. **Standard finite spectral enrichments repeatedly classicalize rather than create a new arithmetic layer.** `PC-126`--`PC-128` land in binary-form resultants, hyperplane-arrangement master functions, and inverse-square chord operators; `PF-161` turns the remaining canonical Ruelle boundary into a harmonic cusp; `WP-104`--`WP-105` reduce the natural cover-Jensen scalarizations to harmonic/entropy data and a known singular endpoint. Prior-art coverage makes more wrappers around these same categories low-yield, but does not itself establish any no-go theorem.

3. **Algebraic size and metric/global usefulness must be separated.** `WI-094`--`WI-096` give the cleanest current example: the pairwise defect is exactly combinatorial and may be extensive, yet its cumulative canonical overlap vanishes and its large values are arithmetically resonant. Large rank loss is therefore not a proxy for a macroscopic signed cancellation resource.

4. **A weaker endpoint does not remove the quantitative bridge.** `MC-009` may make mean-absolute Mertens size a useful target if the external theorem survives audit, but `MC-001`, `MC-006`, `MC-010`, and `MC-011` leave the source-to-endpoint polynomial transfer unresolved. Endpoint equivalence and information budget are distinct gates.

5. **The productive cross-line transfers are audits, not mergers.** Arithmetic Fidelity supplies provenance/target-transport and robustness tests; Möbius Cancellation supplies explicit information-budget and matched-control discipline; Prime Circle, Prime Flute, Prime Lattice, and Weil Positivity supply classicalization/readout controls; Weil Inertia supplies the warning to measure metric and cumulative effect rather than algebraic dimension alone. None of the current findings proves that the underlying research mechanisms should be merged.

## Highest-leverage unresolved questions

- `arithmetic_fidelity`: which provenance-sensitive target transports and positive safety margins remain invariant under the exact equivalences and limits used by the RH-facing constructions, rather than only under a fixed representation?
- `mobius_cancellation`: can a source-natural signed local or multiscale statistic transfer with polynomial gain to mean-absolute Mertens size without first proving pointwise RH-scale cancellation? This is handed off as `research/mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-mean-absolute-mertens-transfer-budget.md`; the clue is proposed, not evidence.
- `prime_circle`: what is the first intrinsic all-shell or noncommutative observable formed before finite resultant/chord compression whose value changes under a matched non-arithmetic control?
- `prime_flute`: after the canonical separator/Ruelle boundaries are removed as control phenomena, does the full prime/shift relative assembly exist in the required trace/scattering class, and if so does any genuinely nonlocal value separate the clone?
- `prime_lattice`: can critical localization be derived upstream from the rational-prime completion by an invariant unavailable to scalar-energy, prime-axis, Beurling, prolate, Poisson, and tempered controls?
- `weil_inertia`: can the exact Yang coefficient law or simultaneous many-modulus structure create a fixed-order signed cancellation effect in the low/zero-defect sector after the pairwise rank route is removed?
- `weil_positivity`: can one geometry produce the finite selector, mixed-prime completion, archimedean/polar contribution, critical roughness, and independent sign before any positive quotient or scalarization discards the needed structure?
