# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The common RH-facing gap remains an independently source-forced estimate in the precise norm used by the destination. Reconstruction, auxiliary positivity and existence of a zero-sensitive test do not supply that estimate. No current Mathia finding proves RH.

Two distinct recovery losses now have concrete repairs. [PL-475](prime_lattice/findings/PL-475-two-box-lengths-force-paired-macroscopic-prime-variance.md) uses two box lengths to recover a smooth prime test through macroscopic windows; [MC-531](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-531-bounded-jumps-recover-trimmed-mobius-variance.md) uses bounded jumps to recover a complete Möbius test after quantitatively bounded deletion of starts. Their proposed composition is [conditional Mind synthesis](mind/intuition/MI-040-paired-box-inversion-transports-trimmed-mobius-pole-tests.md), now routed through a [destination-local clue](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-paired-box-trimmed-mobius-recovery.md). Neither repair supplies the joint arithmetic upper bounds it would consume.

Canonical findings outrank older Mind frontiers where they differ. No adversarial sidecar is currently open; this is not independent approval of the corpus. WI-002 is withdrawn, and [WI-456](weil_inertia/findings/WI-456-retracted-moment-transport-leaves-only-a-conditional-density-capstone.md) retains only a conditional moment-to-density theorem after retraction of the Yang–Yang arithmetic transport. Even a valid density-one theorem leaves individual exceptions possible. The [Atlas v1 metrics](graph/atlas/metrics.md) selectively incorporate paired recovery and missing-start recovery, while their entrypoint narrative and some other frontier descriptions lag current sources. The unchanged coverage model and selective episode rates are derived context; they do not establish saturation or justify the recommendations below.

## Cross-line structure

**Prime and reciprocal recovery share a smooth-test interface, with different analytic inputs.** [PL-471](prime_lattice/findings/PL-471-sparse-zero-annihilators-force-prime-variance-on-every-large-block.md) isolates a selected mod-5 zero using sparse zero counts and noncancelling logarithmic-derivative residues. [MC-528](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-528-multiplicity-aware-annihilation-forces-mobius-variance-on-every-block.md) reconstructs the test for reciprocal zeta, cancelling every unwanted pole with full multiplicity and proving the needed local division bound. Its retained order-`m` pole contributes `X^rho Q_(m-1)(log X)` with nonzero leading coefficient even when the ordinary residue vanishes. The complex test avoids selected-scale cosine zeros. Neither result needs a rightmost zero or finite exceptional family; both retain full-zero-set dependence and non-effective onset.

For one box, [PL-474](prime_lattice/findings/PL-474-nonquasianalytic-tests-close-the-iterated-log-window-boundary.md) and [MC-530](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-530-nonquasianalytic-tests-reach-logarithmic-mobius-windows.md) reach every integer `1<=H<=X/[log X (loglog X)^r]`, for each fixed `r>1`, on every sufficiently large real block. [PL-473](prime_lattice/findings/PL-473-box-fourier-zeros-obstruct-logarithmic-window-test-inversion.md) excludes the same fixed-target, all-scale power-saving inverse certificate for `0<=r<=1`. This is a boundary of that approximation method, not of variance itself. [MC-529](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-529-single-near-linear-centered-variance-law-is-equivalent-to-rh.md) already makes its epsilon-loss near-linear ceiling on any unbounded scale set RH-equivalent.

PL-475 changes the operator rather than the target's regularity. Its classical [compact-distribution two-channel inverse](prior_art/incremental/PA-multichannel-compact-distribution-deconvolution.md) retains bounded localized mean-zero tests after dilation. With `K=floor(sqrt(2)H)`, integer rounding costs `O(1/X)` in target sup norm and `O(log X)` against the prime coefficients. A hypothetical mod-5 zero of real part `beta>1/2` therefore forces

`V_1(X,H)/H^2 + V_1(X,K)/K^2 >= c X^(2beta-1)`

for all integer `1<=H<=X/20` on every sufficiently large real block. Both variances have their own empirical means on the same base block. Both ceilings are required for the resulting joint-GRH implication at macroscopic lengths; one favorable channel is insufficient. Rational common zeros and Liouville small divisors exclude arbitrary choices of the second kernel. This is finite-derivative continuity on smooth tests, not a bounded inverse on arbitrary L2 data.

MC-531 addresses a separate loss. For deleted integer starts `E` of cardinality `B<X/2`, retained energy `V_E(c)` around any single constant `c`, and its existing single-box window range, it proves

`sqrt(X V_E(c)) + B^2 >= C H X^beta (log X)^(m-1)`.

The exact jump bound of two recovers the complete linear pairing without cutting its mean-zero analytic test. At the near-linear window, simultaneous bounds `B <<_epsilon X^(3/4+epsilon)` and `V_E(c) <<_epsilon X^(1+epsilon)H` on any unbounded integer scale set are equivalent to RH, with the families fixed before the epsilon quantifier. Qualitative density one is insufficient. Generic bounded-coefficient tent controls make quadratic absolute-mass recovery sharp; they do not prove the optimal deletion threshold for signed Möbius tests.

[MI-040](mind/intuition/MI-040-paired-box-inversion-transports-trimmed-mobius-pole-tests.md) composes these operators conditionally: the reciprocal smooth target fits PL-475's inverse, whose rounding bill becomes `O(1)` for bounded Möbius coefficients, while MC-531 can recover each complete pairing. The [paired-box clue](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-paired-box-trimmed-mobius-recovery.md) delivers the exact source packet and asks the owner to validate the macroscopic trimmed criterion. This is a proposed transfer, not another canonical theorem or an arithmetic upper estimate.

**Calibration restrictions, range and precision are separate resources.** [AF-580](arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-580-positive-noise-destroys-finite-stopping-under-unknown-polynomial-calibration.md) rules out a uniformly reliable almost-sure finite return even at a noiseless input when reliability is required over unknown unrestricted polynomial calibration and fixed positive supremum-bounded error, despite a positive full-ray margin. [AF-584](arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-584-growing-calibration-degree-has-sharp-quadratic-range-cost.md) closes the growing-degree range order. For the binary profile `x exp(1-x)` on `(0,B]`, writing `nu=r^2/B`, the lost margin is `Theta(nu^2 log(1/nu))` when `nu` is small, uniformly in `r>=2`; it tends to zero exactly when `r^2/B -> 0`. Retaining fraction `1-delta` needs range `Theta(r^2 sqrt(log(1/delta)/delta))`. The uniform prime-edit profile error has no degree amplification, and `delta log p -> infinity` suffices for quantitative transfer. Sharp higher-degree constants, a common multiway design and absolute precision remain open. These are generalized-prime controls, not ordinary-prime discrimination.

The [AF–Xi comparison](mind/intuition/MI-039-calibration-gluing-transfers-randomized-stopping-bounds-only-in-the-admitted-function-class.md) prevents a false transfer: Xi's total squared-error budget permits chord-average error `sigma sqrt(2/n)/h`, whereas fixed supremum error permits `2 epsilon/h` without averaging gain. **Physical assembly, normalization and destination sign need different theorems.** [NB-356](nyman_beurling/findings/NB-356-finite-orbit-target-assembly-is-equivalent-to-a-reciprocal-phase-series.md) gives the finite-energy reciprocal-phase criterion for algebraic assembly from complete orbit spaces; specified higher-rank integer families remain undecided. [NB-358](nyman_beurling/findings/NB-358-phase-filtering-characterizes-polynomial-two-orbit-energy.md) closes the two-orbit polynomial-exponent gap by filtering phase denominators instead of truncating indices. For irrational `alpha=log p/log q` and squared component energy `E(epsilon)`, each `kappa>0` satisfies

`E(epsilon)=O(epsilon^(-kappa)) iff inf_(n>=1) n^(1+kappa/2)||n alpha|| > 0`.

At bounded type the sharp order is logarithmic, not bounded energy. The limsup power is `2(mu(alpha)-2)`, without asserting an endpoint power bound. Fixed multiplicatively independent integer seed pairs have some polynomial bound from linear forms in logarithms. The target remains projected, the Burnol floor remains, and complete real-dilation orbit vectors may need infinitely many generators; no discrete acquisition rate follows.

3. **Individual defects and physical sampling:** establish independent coupling at the actual depth of an off-line block. WI's controlled shrinking-scale overlap and VIS's growing-shell calibration are all-zero statements. The physical Wang coefficient/separation regime and any selector residual must be checked against the resolved null before it can motivate a new mechanism.
4. **Source constraints beyond matched controls:** quantify AF multiway range and absolute precision, preserve Xi's distinct noise geometry, and bound Robin's actual CA-selected signed remote tail. Another calibration profile or balanced synthetic construction alone does not supply those inputs.
