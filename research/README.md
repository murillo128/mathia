## Cross-line structure

The first strong convergence is now between **source breadth and stable transport**. Arithmetic Fidelity proves that full prime breadth can in principle be recovered from an infinite observation channel: either real parts escaping to infinity or a fixed vertical line with unbounded phase-time. This corrects an overly pessimistic reading of the finite-observation no-go. But the recovery formulas immediately expose conditioning and observation-time costs. Weil Positivity reaches the complementary failure: even after exact coefficient sourcing and genuine cross-prime coherence have been engineered, the critical boundary map can be nonclosable. Prime Flute sits between these extremes: the local source map is stable in a positive Sobolev topology as long as its weighted source occupation remains controlled, so any loss of compactness must be paid by global coefficient growth or a later residual operator. Nyman supplies the finite-section analogue: raw compression can be excellent while normalized target orientation remains uncontrolled.

A second convergence concerns **selected/common modes versus generic averaging**. Möbius Cancellation now has an exact CRT-common block shared by every selected first-shell modulus; generic BDH and polynomial pseudorandomness can be arbitrarily strong logarithmically while still discarding the coherent sign needed on that block. Farey has the spatial version of the same issue: the arithmetic mechanism chooses reciprocal-floor endpoints rather than generic interval starts. Weil Inertia has the spectral version: the entire odd window contains enormous recurrent source quasimodes, but RH failure supplies one much smaller candidate class, the first odd zero mode. In all three lines, the next theorem must condition on the source-selected coordinate rather than strengthen a norm or average over a larger ambient family.

A third convergence concerns **exact source identity before global coherence**. Robin Extremal now has a sharp local primality certificate: the ordinary divisor-sum increment detects the absence of internal divisors and destroys the generalized composite-label control at square-root scale. Arithmetic Fidelity similarly separates local Euler degree from global prime breadth. These are encouraging because they identify source properties that matched controls cannot fake, but they also expose the remaining work: local identity has to be assembled into the global relation consumed by the endgame. Prime Lattice makes the same warning spectrally: source recovery can be exact while positivity remains generic only after a noncompact completion, leaving the arithmetic question in a finite residual sector.
