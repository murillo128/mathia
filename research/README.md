## Cross-line structure

The Farey/Nyman comparison now gives a sharper rule for multiscale arguments. **Coherence is useful only when it is nonredundant, accumulates, and can be localized.** Nyman fails at the first step: nested projections and source sections compose exactly, so even the entire prefix up to `R` is just the maximal-section problem in disguise. Farey passes the first two steps: repeated source reuse creates incompatible Möbius equality rays, and `FD-123--FD-124` show that a polynomial-growth source cannot keep the Fourier skeleton near-saturated on any horizon hierarchy of finite average logarithmic growth. What remains is not more multiscale consistency in the abstract but a localization theorem forcing that distributed loss into the particular critical horizon consumed by the endpoint.

Visual now supplies the cleanest current example of **proxy faithfulness**. The magnetic and simple-cycle certificates of `VIS-218` and `VIS-220` failed because those particular relaxations were too weak. The full correlation SDP is qualitatively different after `VIS-222`: symmetric Grothendieck bounds its objective within a universal factor of the exact torus optimum for every finite Hermitian matrix. The open canonical-prime SDP asymptotic therefore cannot be dismissed as a certificate artifact. A bounded SDP/RMS ratio would prove an RMS-scale exact static worst-start bound; a divergent ratio would prove that the exact torus endpoint itself diverges relative to RMS.

The Möbius/Robin/Weil-Inertia cluster shows a complementary trend: once broad ambient escapes are excluded, the remaining obstruction acquires a **small geometric or arithmetic support**. Möbius concentrates it into a Page-cheap exceptional quotient plus a high-cost remainder; Robin confines cancellation of a strong atom to a very narrow ordinate neighborhood or a genuinely diffuse packet; Weil Inertia requires incidence with sparse giant prime-gap hosts. These do not yet combine into a theorem, but they replace generic requests for cancellation with concrete configurations whose frequency, rank, clustering, or incidence can be attacked directly.

The existing global fidelity/realization clue and `MI-009`, `MI-014`, and `MI-015` already cover the program-level lessons: local correctness need not globalize, componentwise coercivity needs a switching/compatibility cost, and a relaxation is useful only if it faithfully reflects the endpoint.
