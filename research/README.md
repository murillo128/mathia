The program's objective is to resolve the Riemann hypothesis. Proportion improvements, local certificates, numerical patterns, and general structural results are useful only insofar as they clarify or enable a route that excludes every possible off-critical zero. The surviving theorem must aggregate the source-specific residual with constants that do not disappear under family-size dilution, frequency spreading, quotient collisions, confluence, or global geometric redistribution.
