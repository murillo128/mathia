# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia has thirteen valid research lines. Ten are evidence-bearing and active: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain distinct pre-evidence scaffolds.

The strongest common bottleneck is now a **source-forced profile-complexity, complete-control, and sign/assembly gate**. The earlier non-escape principle remains necessary, but `AF-114`--`AF-115` show that “the right scale” is itself too coarse a notion. A normalized resource cloud is repairable by one moving scalar scale exactly when its pairwise log-ratio law is tight, and it is repairable by at most `k` moving scales exactly when `k+1` independent resource samples cannot remain mutually separated with positive probability at arbitrarily large resolution. The source must therefore constrain not only the critical homogeneity and topology, but also the **number and movement of profiles/scales** that a valid realization is allowed to use. A candidate that continually opens new spectral, conductor, geometric, or auxiliary scales has not established fidelity until that profile complexity is source-forced and uniformly controlled.

Several RH-facing lines now realize different sides of that gate. `ANF-033` overturns the previous finite-real obstruction: the complete finite-real diffraction closure is disjoint from the sharp Montgomery--Taylor budget, and `ANF-034` makes the separation constructive along an explicit central-notch perturbation of the sharp spectrum. `ANF-035` then shows that common conjugation-symmetric vertical fibers add no new obstruction beyond real multiplicity tests. The scalar frontier has therefore moved to the **actual heterogeneous complex/conjugation-invariant zero geometry and full zeta affine inequality**, not to a richer real surrogate. In `mobius_cancellation`, `MC-062`--`MC-063` make moving complexity arithmetically expensive: under a fixed subquadratic quadratic-character budget, genuinely new certificates must occur at power-separated scales and can appear only on an `O(log log T)` scale skeleton. `MC-064` then identifies an independent transfer floor: the direct classical squarefree-character/Burgess architecture cannot certify a Mertens exponent below `11/16`, even if the local character-fit defect is made tiny.

Two sign mechanisms sharpen the destination side. `WI-150`--`WI-151` close the remaining regular scalar-screening escape much further: full bounded-depth universal scalar validity forces exact lattice-alias constraints and, after a positive phase mask removes the aliases, a pointwise spectral floor `phi(a) >= -2 phi(0) sech^2(2 pi B a)`. The surviving Weil route must therefore be source-rigid and genuinely joint/sign-indefinite rather than another universal scalar window. Conversely, `WP-146`--`WP-147` show that preserving the exact critical cyclotomic resultant coefficients does not automatically produce the desired sign: mixed-prime chains have both conditional signs, and disjoint arithmetic copies force unbounded positive and negative primitive inertia, defeating every fixed-codimension restriction and fixed-rank auxiliary correction. Arithmetic selection and positivity must be coupled before the finite block reaches this indefinite regime.

`xi_flow` remains the constructive counterpoint. `XF-029` shows at lattice quadratic order that a slowly varying triple-discriminant taper localizes through the Cauchy commutator `La`, paying `O(1/M)` rather than the full taper mass, which is compatible with the existing super-mesoscopic Xi buffer scale. `XF-030` then removes the linearization from the local three-root shape dynamics: the normalized triple discriminant is a strictly concave one-dimensional function of log-gap contrast, and its full exterior coupling is an exact signed cubic divided-difference flux with absolute Xi-tail convergence. The live problem is no longer to guess a collision-safe local coordinate, but to prove that the source controls the aggregate signed left/right flux and taper edges strongly enough to obtain fixed-time global coercivity.

### `arithmetic_fidelity` — `narrow`

`AF-114`--`AF-115` turn relative-scale tightness into an exact finite-profile hierarchy. One-scale repairability is equivalent to tight pairwise log-ratios; `k`-scale repairability is equivalent to the disappearance of `(k+1)` mutually separated resource samples at coarse resolution. This is stronger than choosing a clever normalization after seeing the spectrum: it decides intrinsically whether any bounded number of moving scales can retain the mass. Continue only by instantiating this hierarchy in a concrete arithmetic carrier and deriving from the source the admissible profile count, movement law, topology, ideal budget, and final assembly margin. Merely allowing more adaptive scales is now a classified relaxation, not a repair theorem.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Nonescape is a profile-count problem, not only a scale problem.** `AF-114`--`AF-115` provide an exact concentration-compactness hierarchy for one, two, or finitely many moving scales. `MC-062`--`MC-063` give an arithmetic realization: allowing a fresh comparator profile is possible only by moving to power-separated scales under a subquadratic budget. Future source claims should state how many adaptive profiles are allowed and why that number remains uniformly bounded.

2. **The complete control must live in the actual source category.** `ANF-033` is the clearest reversal: an apparently complete finite-real control family turns out not to impose the expected ceiling. `ANF-035` removes common vertical fibers but leaves heterogeneous complex zero geometry untouched. Matched controls should therefore be strengthened toward the exact category consumed by the destination theorem, not merely enlarged within an easier surrogate category.

3. **Moving complexity and destination transfer are independent costs.** Even after `MC-063` makes distinct quadratic certificates sparse, `MC-064` shows that the direct squarefree-character transfer still stops at `11/16`. Likewise a spectral profile may be finitely multiscale yet fail the required operator/trace assembly. Controlling how a witness moves does not prove that the destination can use it.

4. **Arithmetic selection and positive localization can be antagonistic.** Prime Lattice shows that a completed RH-equivalent sign can already be tested on a tiny dense sampling carrier, while Weil Positivity shows that the exact sparse critical resultant coefficients generate unbounded two-sided primitive inertia. A successful positivity mechanism must explain where the sign is created without first discarding the arithmetic selector or merely importing a completed RH-equivalent object.

5. **Relational carrier changes can expose the correct flux but still need source coercivity.** Xi Flow's triple contrast removes affine exterior fields and produces an exact cubic left/right flux; Farey's same-gap permutation bridge removes the entire one-point gap multiset and isolates ordering. These are useful because they identify what information remains after a complete lower-order quotient. Neither becomes RH leverage until the source supplies a quantitative global law for that residual.
