The corresponding positive prime-supported question is now a [destination-local clue](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-prime-supported-every-scale-hidden-dispersion.md), not an established transfer.

Other resolved interfaces leave different obligations. NB-368 supplies explicit real-seed completion of the projected target, PC-466 identifies the full admissible-pattern spectral set, and PF-364 closes the sufficiently narrow constant-retaining transition repair in the actual seam-energy completion. WI-002 remains withdrawn, and [WI-456](weil_inertia/findings/WI-456-retracted-moment-transport-leaves-only-a-conditional-density-capstone.md) retains only a conditional moment-to-density theorem after retraction of the Yang–Yang arithmetic transport. ## Cross-line structure

**Observation design does not supply arithmetic cancellation.** For the actual Möbius and identity-residue mod-5 Mangoldt sources, use lengths `L_1=floor(X/20)` and `L_2=floor(sqrt(2)L_1)`. Repeated draws count, the source precedes sampling, and the separate arithmetic pole formulas and pointwise converses remain essential. The shared recovery operator does not equate RH with joint mod-5 GRH.

The finite pair-tail lemma is `D<=2T+AX p_T^2+A p_T`, where `D` is population mean absolute pair dispersion, jumps are bounded by `A`, and `p_T` is the probability that two independent uniform values differ by more than `T`. A hypothetical pole at real part `beta>1/2` forces `D_1+D_2 >> X^beta` on every sufficiently large block. For every fixed `1/2<gamma<beta`, the probabilities of `max_i V_i>X^(2gamma)` are bounded below by constant multiples of `X^(-(1-beta)/2)` for Möbius and `X^(-(1-beta)/2)/sqrt(log(3X))` for primes. Both have divergent sums on `X_k=k^4`; independent repetitions then give infinitely many excessive pair energies. A favorable post-selected subsequence cannot replace this coverage.
