## Cross-line structure

### Finite identifiability and stable global recovery are now cleanly separated

Arithmetic Fidelity supplies the clearest quantitative example. `AF-282` says the two-prime labels are not exponentially colliding, so poor finite-prefix behavior cannot automatically be blamed on phase-node geometry. The missing theorem is collective inversion with an explicit breadth/horizon/noise budget. Xi provides the complementary local positive case: a known finite packet can be reconstructed with controlled conditioning, yet unknown support and global assembly remain untouched. Nyman gives the same separation in Hilbert geometry: the late quotient is identified exactly, but identifying the quotient does not decide whether the canonical residual can be approximated by the finite-time defect sector. These are analogous information boundaries, not one transferable theorem.

### Signed selectivity and canonical normalization are different gates

`MC-232`, `WI-250`, and `WP-264` clarify three distinct ways a sign argument can fail or succeed. Möbius now has a clean source-selected affine family but still needs a theorem that preserves parity. Weil Inertia has already preserved the signed von-Mangoldt coordinate by conditioning on the forced first mode; its problem is quantitative coercivity. Weil Positivity obtains an index-one sign theorem, but the sign survives an entire half-line of finite-part choices and therefore does not determine the arithmetic normalization. A viable global positivity argument must therefore be both sign-producing **and** source-selective at the normalization used by the target.

### Local composition is becoming exact, but global propagation remains the bill

Robin, Farey, Prime Flute, and Prime Lattice all now have sharper local or finite-window structure without a completed global implication. `RE-061` classifies bounded switch-packet cancellation. `FD-060` amplifies one zero-frontier spike into a growing packet but does not control the rest of the denominator. Prime Flute has ruled out the obvious uniform local discounts and must derive the full return map. Prime Lattice knows that a first negative crossing is irreversible with aperture. The strategic lesson is limited but useful: further local refinement is valuable only when it changes the global composition law, denominator, return operator, or crossing-prevention inequality actually consumed downstream.

### Matched controls continue to eliminate fixed/subcritical representation complexity

Analytic Frontier, Prime Circle, and Visual Exploration give complementary controls. `ANF-183` places the matched endpoint packet at a precise reciprocal-width aperture; Prime Circle has reduced bounded-depth and total-counting spectral observables to classical torus/IDS data; `VIS-177` extends the continuous additive prime-torus null throughout subpolynomial support in the Lindeberg phase. The next useful observables must cross those declared boundaries or use source information the controls cannot reproduce. Repackaging the same fixed-complexity representation is not a new route.
