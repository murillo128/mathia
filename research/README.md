## Cross-line structure

The strongest current positive boundary is **source-faithful compression can be useful, but only if the remaining target coordinate is then controlled rather than merely represented more economically**. Nyman now has an unusually concrete example: `NB-034` compresses the full section to a near-logarithmic-dimensional, polynomially conditioned skeleton while preserving both relative target distance and the low Möbius core. Prime Circle gives the opposite control: enlarging a fixed finite packet system without changing the underlying relational state remains finite CRT/Dirichlet-character information. Möbius makes the information-loss boundary exact: once the principal residue mode is centered away, no later nonlinear processing can reconstruct it; retaining the support field beforehand creates a quadratic decoder, but its one remaining uncentered source aggregate must still be bounded.

A second convergence is **absolute persistence versus increment control**. `ANF-166` shows that excellent interval-increment bounds do not control an endpoint primitive that was charged earlier and then held constant. Robin's `RE-030`--`RE-031` supplies a phase analogue: a qualitative shrinking zero-crossing lock is not useful until the source asymptotic locates it with enough absolute resolution to compare with event spacing. Farey supplies a projective analogue: a large absolute Schur distance does not give a useful angular defect when the physical tail energy itself diverges faster than linearly. Across all three, the next theorem must control the normalized/absolute quantity actually entering the endgame rather than a nearby relative statistic.

A third boundary is **moment observability is weaker than form observability**. `PL-259` explains why first/second moments can certify a positive proportion while missing `o(N)` exceptions; `PL-260` shows that Bombieri's full cofinal Weil truncations nevertheless detect every finite-total off-line defect. `WI-232`--`WI-235` independently make large local screened populations expensive. The resulting cross-line opportunity is the newly proposed `research/weil_inertia/clues/CLUE-bombieri-cofinal-negative-index-sparse-defect-bridge.md`: test whether the finite negative-index mechanism has a quantitative persistence margin against an infinite sparse remote tail, with any attempted neutralization priced in the existing WI amplitude ledger. This is narrower than demanding a new Hilbert--Pólya operator and stronger than taking more normalized moments.

Prime Flute and Visual Exploration reinforce the global Mind principle that the order of operations matters. Prime Flute only needs to control leakage that remains dangerous **after** deterministic propagation; demanding bare seam smoothing overprices sectors the propagator already kills. Visual has the dual problem: calibration and moment-null projection can erase both null and source terms before the statistic reaches the final detector. The correct currency in each case is therefore measured after the exact transport/calibration map, not on a more convenient upstream representation.

Finally, the automorphic/positivity lines continue to separate globality from non-scalar arithmetic. Maaß--Selberg gives genuine global Hilbert positivity, and congruence/higher-rank scattering supplies many channels, but `WP-242`--`WP-244` show that the Riemann-bearing arithmetic can remain a common scalar or orthogonal scalar `L` package. More geometry after that factorization does not recover the missing Weil orientation. A viable continuation must make the source-forced coupling and sign meet **before** the completed arithmetic is scalarized away.
