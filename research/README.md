## Cross-line structure

**Farey to Möbius is an exact handoff of a question, not an established estimate.** `FD-311` specifies the sliding second moment and strict power threshold; `FD-312` excludes the separate-endpoint shortcut. `MC-176--MC-177` already price fixed-power short-interval variance as a substantial Möbius resource. [[research/mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-farey-selected-sliding-mertens-variance|The proposed local clue]] grants bounded reading of those two Farey findings. It requires uniformity over the selected windows and payment of exceptional-set energy, not just an almost-all or logarithmic saving. This does not merge the lines: Farey's zero-packet and divisor geometry remain distinct.

**Canonical representation does not guarantee an attainable certificate.** `PL-442` fixes a legitimate Reynolds map, but the full-group norm may charge an artificial coordinate that the four-residue target never uses. [[research/prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-reynolds-certificate-vacuity|The proposed feasibility audit]] distinguishes the exact identity from a useful sufficient condition. The candidate compressed residual already has a nonempty rational control, but neither that calculation nor Reynolds positivity proves anything about actual prime covariance.

**Exact information, diagnostic factorization and arithmetic control are separate.** `AF-549` retains exact collision invariants without a robust calibration margin. `MC-512` separates two sources of participation loss without proving either source factor large. `PC-424` preserves finite algebraic information under Möbius re-basing while retaining the same zero-free matched selectors. These are useful restrictions on what the next proof may assume; they are not a common mathematical mechanism towards RH and do not prohibit source-sensitive invariants merely because a representation is invertible or classical.

**Local scale and witness occupation remain independent obligations.** Weil Inertia needs variation at the shallow defect scale, not global noncompactness alone. Nyman needs the actual Ford datum to occupy a direction that survives optimization, not merely the existence of a favorable direction. Visual needs a residual after the exact shell null and rarity tariff. The objects and normalizations differ, so no additional cross-line theorem is claimed.

## Portfolio changes proposed

No promotion is currently adopted. In particular, **Prime Lattice remains `narrow`**, rather than promoting it on the strength of the untested full-group Frobenius budget. The strongest argument for promotion is still that `PL-441` exposes a finite exact covariance region and `PL-442` provides canonical null data. The contrary evidence is the proposed universal error-floor obstruction and the absence of an arithmetic covariance estimate. Reconsider `continue` after the owner verifies a nonvacuous destination-normalized certificate and identifies an actual-source estimate that can decide the branch without importing the target as a premise.

No merge, split or new line is justified. Prime Flute remains the sole pause-candidate; that recommendation would reverse only after a changed split or endpoint demonstrably avoids the `PF-342` adjacent-mode obstruction. Farey retains its independent geometric questions despite the specific Möbius handoff. Xi remains narrow: its open raw-transfer objection is localized and does not erase the normalized chart, but it cannot support a stronger recommendation. The open `RE-164` equality issue is excluded from the present Robin rationale.
