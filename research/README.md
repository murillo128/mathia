# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia currently has thirteen research lines. Ten carry canonical evidence: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain pre-evidence scaffolds.

The evidence still does **not** establish one common positive mechanism or one mathematically meaningful global bottleneck shared by the program. The useful common discipline is narrower: optimize or quotient the admissible source first, preserve the exact operator/readout topology consumed downstream, and distinguish a representation theorem from a source-specific implication. Recent progress is therefore assessed by whether the first unsupported implication has become strictly more discriminating, not by whether another auxiliary representation has been built.

**Analytic Frontier has materially narrowed its surviving escape chambers.** `ANF-112` now proves that every source-heavy endpoint-localized sublinear packet forces fresh disjoint source-heavy height mass even if the packet source norm itself diverges. `ANF-113` weakens the required localization to localization relative to the packet's own source norm and shows that even multiplicatively supercritical sparse packets restart the cascade whenever they are only subexponentially below the universal height-capacity envelope. A sparse survivor must therefore become exponentially source-unsaturated, leave the sub-square-root regime, or keep spawning disjoint high mass. `ANF-114` separately makes the all-real Montgomery--Taylor triple gap effective, with `kappa_L \asymp L^{-4}` and the sharp real defect-to-span law `diameter \gtrsim N Delta^{-1/4}`. The live implication is no longer a generic height-cascade request: either extend an effective modulus to the complex/conjugation-invariant geometry strongly enough to constrain the source-unsaturated chamber, or close the remaining square-root/bulk and exponentially inefficient sparse alternatives before claiming a fixed-notch contradiction.

**Prime Flute also moves its first unsupported theorem, despite another negative locality result.** `PF-219` shows that no fixed polylogarithmic weakening of the raw low-Schur endpoint weight restores summable scalar transport. `PF-220` then proves that this obstruction was too coarse for the actual recoupling word: all coefficient-adjacent low/low pieces are already in weak `S_1` by physical `d_n` rank/norm counting, and source-weighted low/high leakage is automatically weak `S_2` for a positive Schur contraction. The smooth route is therefore reduced to the exact **coefficient-side cross-frequency leakage** terms containing `PDH` or `HDP`, with the physical coefficient kept on the module reached after transport. Scalar regularization of `PDP` is no longer a useful target. The unsmoothed `PF-204` route and the true-short-collar splice remain independent gates.

**Weil Inertia receives a genuine classical reduction of its adversarial bow regime.** `WI-198` combines Karatsuba's every-interval critical-line zero theorem with the exact bow/mirror count bookkeeping and proves that a count-saturating Maynard--Pratt bow is impossible whenever its power exponent satisfies `theta>27/82`: the bow interval must contain a complementary population of `Omega(m)` odd-order critical-line zeros. The difficult source-selected covariance problem therefore survives for count-saturating bows only at `0<theta<=27/82`; together with the existing `1/4` scale boundary, the interval below `1/4` still structurally requires cross-shift information while `1/4<theta<=27/82` is the narrower range where a genuinely uniform fixed-shift square-root theorem could already be scale-sufficient. No such theorem is supplied by `WI-198`.

**Xi Flow has turned its q-scale source obstruction into a phase-space bottleneck, without repairing the destination failure.** `XF-110`--`XF-112` show that the q-scale Toeplitz source has vanishing normalized off-diagonal `ell^2` mass; only a vanishing spectral fraction can be bad, every nonpositive Rayleigh witness needs `Omega(q/(log q)^2)` coefficient coordinates, and every such witness must place a fixed fraction of Fourier energy on one source-determined low-symbol set whose angular measure is `O((log q)^2/q)` and which lies outside the PNT-flat central arc. This is a much sharper source-side target than generic roughness. It still does not overcome `XF-101`--`XF-104` transition nonidentifiability: the next decisive result must control the depth/geometry of that rare symbol set or prove that a destination-relevant Xi witness cannot concentrate there.

Several `narrow` lines now contain sharper positive carriers without yet acquiring source-specific bridges. `PC-215` proves that squarefree mixed-composite cyclotomic mask compression has exact interaction locality `omega(N)-1`, so the binary Kronecker-sum collapse does not persist and genuine growing mixed-coordinate incidence exists; the support pattern itself is nevertheless reproducible by matched masks, so the unresolved question is whether the actual cyclotomic coefficients possess a stable source-specific invariant. `VIS-097` proves that three-dimensional delay signatures at level four can distinguish different assemblies of the same local edge inventory, making the information boundary of `VIS-096` sharp; the next test is arithmetic discrimination against matched local-block and spectral controls, not whether higher signatures can remember order in principle. `WP-203`--`WP-205` similarly show that the critical rough Gamma bath can be realized by positive fractional-extension geometry, but exact Gamma determinant data and the extension theorem leave an infinite `n=4k` order/channel ladder and do not generate the finite Mangoldt/global coupling.

The Bost--Connes branches are more tightly classified rather than closer to a positive mechanism. `MC-144`--`MC-146` extend the Möbius profinite obstruction from the coefficient algebra to every bounded canonical number-state diagonal, every fixed rational off-diagonal band, and even the full countable fixed-rational profile followed by continuous scalarization; a survivor must use genuinely singular/adaptive cross-scale information, another state/readout geometry, or an aggregate transfer that does not reconstruct Möbius pointwise. `PL-216` shows that the canonical temperature direction has infinite diagonal Fisher length throughout `0<beta<=1`, with the threshold again at `beta=1`, while `PL-217` shows that one-prime finite algebraic observables can interpolate arbitrary finite temperature samples and uniformly approximate arbitrary continuous scalar profiles on compact high-temperature intervals. Temperature-profile shape by itself is therefore programmable and cannot select `beta=1/2`.

Arithmetic Fidelity has also become better organized rather than more RH-specific. `AF-198` proves that the quartic symmetric free-node optimizer is a strict unrestricted local optimum, while `AF-199` identifies the whole minimal-support moment-flat geometry problem with a normalized Peano B-spline derivative-norm extremal. This replaces order-by-order stencil algebra by one classical variable-knot extremal problem, but the current proposed full-Euler localization clue correctly remains unproved: the homogeneous leading-moment optimizer does not yet control degenerating source shapes or the full arithmetic observable uniformly.

The Riemann Atlas remains historical/structural navigation only. Its declared `source_revision` is still far behind the canonical evidence reconciled here, so its percentages and frontier telemetry are stale derived data and are not used for portfolio decisions.

## Cross-line structure

The strongest current cross-line lesson remains methodological rather than a shared RH mechanism. `ANF-113` measures packet localization relative to the packet's actual source mass, `AF-199` replaces a convenient stencil by the optimized source-shape problem, and `PF-220` keeps the physical coefficient in its true noncommutative position instead of assigning damping to a scalar Schur compression. `XF-112` similarly identifies the exact source-determined phase-space set on which a bad witness must concentrate. These results all warn against measuring a resource before the optimization, quotient or transport that the destination actually consumes, but they do not furnish a theorem transferable between the lines.

The Bost--Connes overlap remains a negative classification, not a merger candidate. Möbius loses orientation under bounded continuous canonical scalar readouts even when all fixed rational bands are retained, while Prime Lattice shows that temperature variation and scalar KMS profiles are either singular at the thermodynamic threshold or programmable by one-prime controls. The two residual problems remain mathematically distinct: Möbius needs a cheaper excursion-coupled signed aggregate; Prime Lattice needs a nonprogrammable relation among channels or states.

There are two newly visible but still line-local category exits. Prime Circle now has a genuinely growing mixed-coordinate carrier after `PC-215`, but has not separated its cyclotomic coefficient law from matched support controls. Visual Exploration has a genuine chronology carrier after `VIS-097`, but has not separated an arithmetic delay-path statistic from matched local-block/spectral controls. Neither meets the threshold for a cross-line clue or a portfolio promotion yet.

Weil Positivity supplies a complementary caution: a source-looking geometric realization can still be nonselective. The Gamma fractional extension is canonical enough to realize the required rough bath, yet it leaves infinitely many admissible mod-four orders and still lacks the finite-prime signed sector. Geometric source provenance therefore counts only when it fixes the degree of freedom that the final sign theorem consumes.

## Highest-leverage unresolved questions

1. **Analytic-Frontier residual chamber closure.** Can an effective complex/conjugation-invariant analogue of `ANF-114`, or another source-capacity theorem, rule out the exponentially source-unsaturated sparse packets and the square-root/bulk alternatives left by `ANF-113` strongly enough to force a strict fixed-notch gap?

2. **Prime-Flute coefficient-side cross-frequency leakage.** Do the exact `PDH/HDP` terms in the PF-211/PF-212 recoupling, with the physical `d_n` coefficient left on the reached module, satisfy the required weak endpoint once the available high/Dirichlet smoothing is retained? A PF-compatible counterexample would decisively kill the remaining smooth route without reopening already closed `P/P` sectors.

3. **Weil-Inertia short-bow source theorem.** In the only count-saturating power range still alive, can the distinguished multiplicative short-interval norm be controlled directly? Below `theta=1/4` the architecture needs genuine cross-shift cancellation; in `1/4<theta<=27/82` a uniform fixed-shift square-root theorem would be scale-sufficient. Failure of both should end the present count-saturating bow mechanism rather than trigger another averaged surrogate.

4. **Xi rare-symbol-set versus transition coercivity.** Can the source-determined low-symbol set of `XF-112` be shown empty/shallow enough for eventual Toeplitz positivity, or can one prove that every destination-relevant Xi transition witness is unable to place fixed Fourier mass on a set of relative measure `O((log q)^2/q)` outside the PNT-flat arc? Without one of these bridges, the sharper source geometry does not repair transition nonidentifiability.
