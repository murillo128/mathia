The evidence still does **not** support one common positive mechanism or one mathematically meaningful global bottleneck. The strongest current progress is line-specific: identify what information survives the exact completion, shorting, norm, closure, conditioning, or source normalization consumed by the proposed endgame, and reject attractive carriers that disappear at that stage.

**Prime Flute has the clearest new positive interface.** `PF-226` and `PF-228` show that a thin pant carries order `s_n^{-1}` saturated dressed channels and that fixed or slowly growing high-pass cutoffs cannot remove enough of them. `PF-229` then supplies the first tested mechanism in the current smooth program that lowers the dressed coupling *before* reciprocal-prime assembly rather than trying to repair it afterwards: in an exact serial corridor network, Schur shorting turns conductances `c_j=s_j^{-1}` into the effective scale `C_eff=(sum_j s_j)^{-1}`, and nonnegative shunts only decrease that coupling. This does not prove the hyperbolic theorem, but it changes the first unsupported implication. The decisive smooth question is whether the actual Fourier-resolved pant-tree Schur complement inherits enough accumulated-length damping despite branching, curvature and channel multiplicity. The unsmoothed `PF-204` route and the true-short-collar splice remain independent gates.

**Analytic Frontier has narrowed the fatal-completion problem beyond generic cancellation.** `ANF-118` gives the full Gaussian Gram observable for mesoscopic translation clouds, while `ANF-119` classifies the complement into excision, bounded overpolarized companionship, or asymptotic profile mirroring. `ANF-122` removes the simplest indispensable mirror: if a same-saddle companion has the same normalized transverse profile and bounded amplitude ratio, then the combined packet is again exponentially saddle-efficient and can itself be excised at negligible cost. A fatal completion therefore needs genuinely new transverse profile information, a more complicated cross-profile coupling, or another mechanism that survives the global notch constraint. The next theorem is no longer “control external cancellation”; it is to classify or exclude those profile-transverse essential companions under global near-extremality, or construct a fatal example.

**Xi Flow has now closed the static q-scale prefix as a transition identifier.** `XF-118` proves full q-scale Toeplitz coercivity for the actual source, and `XF-119` realizes the corresponding positive prefix exactly within the Xi node budget. `XF-120` is the decisive counterweight: even at `K=floor(N/2)`, one can realize a simple unit-circle polynomial whose first `K` power sums stay within the Xi coefficient budget while `N-o(N)` roots are packed into arbitrarily short neighboring pairs. ## Cross-line structure

The strongest common pattern is an **information-survival test after the exact endgame operation**, not a common RH mechanism. Prime Flute only obtains a useful damping candidate after serial Schur elimination; Arithmetic Fidelity only has a meaningful transfer gate after the physical rescaling exposes `Q Lambda_sigma`; Xi shows that even an apparently complete q-scale static prefix can remain nonidentifying at the destination; Möbius shows that adding more scalar norm channels still destroys parity placement; Prime Lattice shows that bounded norm completion preserves only product-Toeplitz structure; and Visual shows that a richer representation is irrelevant unless the exact conditioned quotient leaves the chosen projection variable. These are compatible research lessons, not evidence that one theorem would solve the lines together.

A second cross-line lesson is that **source-selected critical structure is necessary but insufficient**. Weil Positivity obtains `q^{-1/2}` from the actual Bost--Connes phase boundary, which is materially stronger than choosing an exponent by hand, but positivity and finite-mass equilibrium correlations still fail to carry the full arithmetic measure. Weil Inertia similarly shows why a numerically attractive analytic input must be valid in the exact theorem class consumed downstream: `WI-201` removes an apparent cutoff improvement when the cited subconvex result does not instantiate the required exponent-pair hypothesis.

Several lines are now closing whole categories rather than isolated formulas. `MC-155` closes simultaneous scalar row-norm repair; `PC-220` closes the fixed-level transverse spectral-summary tier; strengthened `PL-223` plus `PL-224` close bounded product-Toeplitz completion and its natural corner memory; `XF-120` closes static q-scale Vieta admission as a collision detector. This negative convergence is useful because it tells the Research Watches when the next experiment must change category rather than add another statistic inside an exhausted one.

No new Master clue is created. The plausible cross-line analogies do not currently yield a destination-specific theorem with a decisive falsification test that is absent from the destination line; existing accepted clues already encode the live Arithmetic-Fidelity, Prime-Flute and Visual tests.

## Highest-leverage unresolved questions

1. **Prime-Flute dressed transport after real geometric shorting.** Does the exact hyperbolic/Fourier-resolved Schur complement inherit an accumulated-corridor scale comparable to the `PF-229` network law, with enough frequency and branching control to offset the `PF-226`/`PF-228` saturated-channel count? A failure caused by branching, curvature, or modal recoupling would decisively narrow the smooth route.

2. **Analytic-Frontier profile-transverse fatal completion.** After `ANF-122`, can a same-saddle companion with genuinely different transverse profile remain indispensable while the full configuration is globally near-extremal and still exposes the fatal central notch? Either exclude that coupling using the full Gaussian Gram/notch geometry or construct a concrete fatal completion.

3. **Weil-Inertia distinguished bow below the established frontier.** Work with the unconditional cutoff `27/82`, not the withdrawn `1515/4816`. Determine whether the exact source-distinguished row has the needed signed off-diagonal cancellation. Below `theta=1/4`, the theorem must retain genuine cross-shift information; above `1/4` within the surviving range, a sufficiently uniform square-root fixed-shift estimate could already close scale.

4. **Xi transition information beyond the static q-scale prefix.** `XF-120` means no theorem using only static full-prefix admissibility can infer the desired collision restriction. The decisive replacement is to show that heat-time coherence or another explicitly nonstatic Xi invariant excludes the collision-saturated realizations, or to abandon the prefix architecture cleanly.

5. **Source-specificity after category exhaustion.** Arithmetic Fidelity needs an arithmetic theorem forcing `Q Lambda_sigma->0`; Möbius needs a phase-sensitive/cross-layer persistent object; Prime Circle needs cross-level or pre-compression structure; Prime Lattice must leave bounded product-Toeplitz closure; Visual must test the frozen `P_AA` on fresh material; Weil Positivity needs the signed infinite-mass carrier before positivity. Progress in these lines should be judged by crossing those gates, not by adding richer versions of the already-classified controls.
