# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia currently has eight source-backed research lines: `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, and `weil_positivity`. `visual_exploration` is transversal rather than a standalone RH attack, but its canonical findings make it part of the dynamically discovered Master portfolio.

The strongest common audit is now

`retained source/provenance -> target-compatible representation -> topology-matched quantitative nonescape -> source-specific signed or quantitative gain -> matched-control specificity -> non-scalar global coupling -> independent positivity/zero-selection compatibility`.

The central bottleneck has sharpened from retaining information to proving that the retained arithmetic carrier **does not escape in the topology actually required by the final claim**. Arithmetic Fidelity makes this exact: `AF-075` characterizes compact-witness fidelity by Kolmogorov-width decay, `AF-076` characterizes a common compact envelope across a family by null-sequence synthesis, and `AF-077` supplies a canonical compact disk realizing such a common envelope. Weil Inertia gives the dual obstruction: `WI-109`--`WI-112` show that full rank or recoverable rank can coexist with a large near-null sector, and that linear rescue must pay in conditioning, transmission, or source dimension; the newest obstruction reaches essentially square-root dimension up to polylogarithms. Prime Circle and Visual Exploration separate average spectral capture from uniform control: `PC-143` and `VIS-005` allow Hilbert--Schmidt/trace-style locking while a fixed operator-norm leakage survives. Prime Flute adds the operator-ideal version: `PF-148` shows that square-resolvent trace class alone cannot force the first resolvent into `S_r` for any `r<2`. Compactness, coercivity, operator-norm locking, and the needed Schatten class are therefore distinct obligations rather than interchangeable notions of survival.

A second common constraint is that exact or universal structure is not yet an arithmetic selector. `PL-124` gives the exact Poisson--Newton lattice/divisor trace in a form universal for Dirichlet series, so exact trace realization plus half-centering does not itself force RH. `PC-141` classicalizes every fixed edge-Fourier window, `PF-166` makes the complete tail marked-length datum asymptotically indistinguishable from an all-composite shift clone class by class, and `MC-026`--`MC-028` show that generalized Möbius families, exponent-neutral scale doubling, and cutoff reflection do not manufacture the missing power gain. The surviving constructions must therefore combine topology-matched nonescape with a source-specific coupling or polarization that matched controls cannot reproduce.

No current `.review.md` sidecar is open in the eight research lines. The graph and Atlas remain useful navigation, but the current Atlas projection trails newer canonical findings and is treated as stale rather than used as portfolio evidence.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Topology-matched nonescape is now a shared hard gate.** `AF-075`--`AF-077` give the constructive compactness side; `WI-109`--`WI-112` show that rank can survive while coercivity escapes through a large near-null sector; `PC-143` and `VIS-005` show that average spectral agreement can coexist with fixed operator-norm leakage; and `PF-148` shows that membership at one resolvent/Schatten level does not automatically transfer to the stronger level a later construction may require. A carrier can therefore survive one topology while disappearing in the one that matters.

2. **Exactness and universality do not supply source specificity.** `PL-124` provides an exact but universal Poisson--Newton trace, `PC-141` classicalizes fixed Fourier windows, `PF-166` supplies an all-composite tail clone, and `MC-026`--`MC-028` keep natural analytic transformations boundary-neutral. Matched controls and classicalization repeatedly remove apparent arithmetic novelty after an exact upstream construction has already been obtained.

3. **Prime Lattice and Weil Positivity expose complementary halves of the same bridge but should remain separate.** Prime Lattice has exact arithmetic/zero realization without the required positive source-specific polarization. Weil Positivity has rich positive geometry but its local finite--archimedean completions retain the wrong critical summability behavior. The missing bridge is a non-circular arithmetic polarization whose topology and energy budget are compatible with both sides, not a reason to merge their distinct source programs.

4. **Postprocessing cannot create missing gain for free.** Möbius iteration/factorization remains exponent-neutral, Weil Inertia moves the deficit into conditioning or transmission, and Weil Positivity moves it into endpoint divergence or a geometry that would have to track the shell being tested. Across these lines, a successful rescue has to add new source information or a new coupling category before the lossy compression step, rather than decorate the same carrier afterwards.

The accepted global `CLUE-cross-line-fidelity-realization-separation` already captures the need to distinguish preservation from target realization, and the accepted line-local clues cover the current Prime Circle, Prime Flute, and Weil Positivity decisive tests. The new findings sharpen those tests but do not justify a duplicate Master clue.
