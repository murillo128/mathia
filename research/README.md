## Cross-line structure

The strongest reusable lesson is still methodological rather than a common RH mechanism: **information must survive the operation actually consumed by the endgame, with the conditioning of that operation measured in the same norm.** Prime Flute is a positive example: the complete seam and matched seam are not globally equivalent, but the one-sided order that survives after exposing the inverse-corridor block is exactly enough. Xi is the complementary negative example: exact one-trace invertibility survives algebraically while its temporal inverse develops exponentially small directions. Arithmetic Fidelity makes the same distinction between exact detection and quantitative recovery, and Visual Exploration separates representation capacity from source-specific effect size.

A second recurring boundary is **source selection versus destination orientation**. Weil Positivity now has a genuinely source-forced critical Bost--Connes Gram, yet the Weil-oriented defect has negative essential spectrum and the natural log-density score simply returns an already known signed Poisson tangent. Prime Lattice supplies a useful matched-control catalogue here: canonical KMS diagonal and ordinary off-diagonal data can be completely source-forced while still being too rigid, too positive, or reducible to freely chosen boundary-state information. This is evidence that the sign theorem is an additional obligation, not evidence that every noncommutative route fails.

A third boundary is **relational structure versus target re-encoding**. Möbius Cancellation shows that making the operator algebra noncommutative does not help when every finite word either removes the Möbius phase or carries it linearly. Prime Circle shows the dual failure mode: keeping a much larger mixed-conductor codomain does not help if the destination positive square immediately scalarizes it to fiber variance. A genuine category exit in either line must specify the new relation before assigning arithmetic significance and must demonstrate information or quantitative gain against controls preserving the classified lower-order data.

No current cross-line observation crosses the clue threshold beyond the already active Bost--Connes completion handoff. The recent analogies above are useful for portfolio reasoning, but they do not yet supply a destination-local falsifiable theorem that is both new and stronger than the lines' existing clues. No new Master clue is therefore created in this snapshot.
