## Cross-line synthesis

The strongest new cross-line distinction is that **global coupling and sign-preserving target extraction are separate gates**. `WP-236` proves that no bounded order of critical prime marginals determines the all-prime coupling. `PL-253`, however, supplies a classical object where the global automorphic coupling, analytic continuation and an independent Hilbert-space positivity are already present; the failure occurs one step later, when positivity of the whole Maaß--Selberg distribution is projected onto the Weil zero-divisor summand and leaves a signed complement. The current program therefore cannot treat either “find global coupling” or “find global positivity” as sufficient by itself. A proposed local Weil Positivity clue, `CLUE-maass-selberg-remainder-sign-interface`, routes the precise remainder-sign/cancellation test without asserting that it succeeds.

A second recurring boundary is **pointwise or fixed-parameter control versus uniform transport through the family actually consumed by the target**. Prime Flute now knows the exact fixed-mass Green asymptotic and even its universal leading coefficient, but the theorem needs the mass and both mode indices to move simultaneously before the odd-mass sum is formed. Xi Flow supplies the information-theoretic analogue: the exact single-sensor full-future exponent persists for any subexponential bank unless the bank itself pays exponential sensing entropy. These are not the same theorem, but both reject upgrading a family from individually favorable constituents without pricing the joint parameter range.

A third convergence is that several free geometries are becoming fully classified, so further progress has to spend **source-specific complexity** rather than improve the same relaxation. `FD-021` shows that even the weak source condition of polynomial growth breaks unrestricted bounded-ratio GCD saturation; `NB-022` makes one source discrepancy control both high-section gain and deep visibility; `MC-190` proves symmetry prevents transverse character energy from forcing the missing principal mode; and `WI-228` identifies the exact local population normalization absent from global density theorems. In each case the next useful theorem must convert a property of the actual arithmetic source into the final norm/count/sign currency.

Visual Exploration now supplies a useful stochastic counterpart to the same discipline. Once the source window, mean, diagonal mode and finite-CUE covariance are fixed, `VIS-135` shows that the remaining single-window fluctuation cannot be wished away by an unknown fourth cumulant: it is asymptotically Gaussian with an order-one intensity-noise floor. Any `1/L` arithmetic residual therefore needs an explicit averaging and transfer budget rather than another refinement of the same one-window null.
