## Cross-line structure

**Exact recovery and stable destination transport are different currencies.** `AF-550` gives a complete nonlocal quotient invariant and exact prime-scale recovery, while `AF-551` constructs a finite-prefix generalized-prime control whose quotient-horizontal separation decays like `(3/5)^S` despite a fixed absolute scale change. Xi Flow exhibits the same audit at another interface: the normalized `XF-186` chart may remain valid while the open review blocks the claimed uniform passage to arbitrary raw-sample noise. This is a shared proof discipline, not a theorem identifying the two representations.

**Farey local correctness still does not determine global coherence.** `FD-313` strengthens the old fixed-local-order obstruction to every sublinear block depth: exact local factor inventories can coexist with a discrepancy-energy ratio `n/(4L)+O(1)`. The accepted gap-order clue now leaves only essentially global order or genuinely Farey-specific labels/ancestry as plausible ordering bridges. This does not weaken the separate `FD-311--FD-312` physical sliding-variance route, which has already been handed to Mobius Cancellation through the existing proposed clue.

**Prime Lattice has crossed from certificate repair to source arithmetic.** The resolved Reynolds clue and current `PL-442` remove the artificial residue-zero floor from the perturbative budget and give explicit compressed, positive-ray and dephased-ray certificates. None of those matrix identities supplies the actual prime covariance estimate. `PL-434` is therefore now the decisive boundary: representation completeness and finite-dimensional feasibility are in place, while the missing resource is a genuine second-moment/direct-covariance theorem at the source scale.

**Witness availability and physical occupation remain independent obligations.** `NB-333` shows that changing the remainder phase within one critical cell cannot repair the integer nodes of the same adjacent witness; a new witness/channel is needed before the old target-occupation problem is even reached. Weil Inertia similarly needs source-controlled oscillation at the defect scale rather than global noncompactness, and Visual Exploration needs a selector residual beyond the exact Haar and rarity tariffs. No cross-line estimate transfers automatically among these distinct source and destination norms.
