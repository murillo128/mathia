## Cross-line structure

Farey and Möbius now give a useful two-sided warning about **nominal breadth**. `FD-133` monitors polynomially many prime directions simultaneously and still misses a defect carried by a vanishing moving-rank layer. `MC-304` packages every selected quadratic mode into one exact fixed-modulus family, but the endpoint cost reappears as reduced-residue density or radical size. A route must therefore price the active layer and the effective family capacity, not count coordinates or celebrate compact packaging.

Nyman--Beurling and Robin provide the constructive counterpart. `NB-132` shows that an actual-source theorem can cap formal model complexity enough to make a hidden recurrence observable. `RE-134` shows that bounded local complexity would likewise force visibility of the Robin packet, but its compactness constant is presently qualitative in the complexity bound and actual zeta zeros have not been shown to satisfy the required reciprocal-box/tightness budget. The proposed local Robin clue asks for the exact bridge: first derive the source-faithful occupancy budget from actual-zero counting, then quantify whether the visibility floor survives at that budget. Failure in either step kills the transfer cleanly.

Arithmetic Fidelity, Visual, and Weil Positivity clarify three different ways a representation can mislead. `AF-350` shows that a lossy quotient can still preserve exactly the singularity discriminator the theorem needs. `VIS-229` shows that an apparently nontrivial residual can be manufactured by an incorrectly calibrated null variance. `WP-311` shows that a positivity-preserving canonical transform can itself introduce an asymptotic term absent from the destination. The common test is not “does the transform preserve information?” but “does it preserve the right discriminator without manufacturing a competing one?”

Prime Lattice and Weil Inertia remain the strongest examples of endpoint-faithful reduction on the geometric side. `PL-313--PL-314` isolate one scalar channel and the moving-tail topology required to transport it. `WI-295` keeps the actual one-signed first-crossing class and extracts a support floor plus amplitude tariff that disappear in the looser signed relaxation. These remain useful models: preserve the actual extremizer class, identify the exact observer used at the endpoint, then price the smallest source law still missing.

The existing global fidelity/realization clue, observable-specific gate clue, and `MI-008--MI-016` already cover destination sensitivity, active-scale uniformity, source realization, and endpoint-faithful relaxation. The only new transfer that is sufficiently destination-specific and absent from current Robin clues is the `NB-132` actual-zero rank-cap mechanism into the `RE-134` reciprocal-box visibility problem; it is persisted locally as a proposed clue rather than promoted to global knowledge.
