## Cross-line structure

The first reusable lesson is **preserve the discriminating coordinate through every exact operation that the proof actually performs**. Möbius shows both failure modes sharply: cross-`lcm` synchronization is erased by full recombination, while within a fixed coprime-shift `lcm` slice the beta-sieve sign is already maximally coherent. Visual Exploration gives the operator analogue: extra commuting Hermitian data may refine degenerate spectral sectors, but simultaneous-equivariant positive metrics still commute with the Gram and remain spectral preconditioners. A useful source feature must therefore survive completion, recombination, quotient and normalization rather than merely appearing at an intermediate representation.

The second lesson is **legal transport is an independent theorem, not bookkeeping**. Nyman has an exact algebraic bridge from the reciprocal-zeta source to the Ford current, but the bridge differentiates the source and applies an unbounded multiplier. `NB-277` shows that the obvious Abel regularization cannot simultaneously preserve Ford depth and lower that cost. Arithmetic Fidelity expresses the same issue as a resolution law: more samples inside the same critical-noise horizon do not buy more stable generator resolution. In both cases, exact ancestry is weaker than stable destination-scale observability.

The third lesson is **survival is still not source specificity**. Prime Circle's subcritical contrast survives on long polynomial windows and can even grow, yet a deterministic matched cloud with the same continuum density can reproduce the scaling. Prime Lattice's raw `J`-positivity and Weil Positivity's fixed-shadow positivity fail for the same reason in different language: the surviving structure is ambient or order-theoretic rather than a proper arithmetic discriminator. The next positive result must survive a matched null after the leading generic mode has been removed.

Representation richness is therefore not itself progress. The operational gate is now three-part: retain a source-native coordinate after exact reduction, transport it with a controlled signal floor in the destination topology, and prove a residual/coercive inequality unavailable to a matched control. This explains why the current portfolio can absorb substantial structural progress without changing its status allocation.
