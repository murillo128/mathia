# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia currently has thirteen research lines. Ten carry canonical evidence: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain pre-evidence scaffolds.

The current evidence still does **not** support one common positive mechanism or one mathematically meaningful global bottleneck shared by the program. Progress is audited line by line at the first genuinely unsupported implication. A new representation, exact identity, classicalization, matched control, or obstruction is valuable research output, but it counts as proof-interface progress only when it removes or strictly contracts the theorem that must be proved next.

The most important correction in this snapshot is in **Weil Inertia**. `WI-195` had reduced the bow to an exact high-twist short-interval `L^2` target, and the previous Master snapshot treated a uniform `o(X K log X)` variance estimate across the bow twist window as the live analytic theorem. `ANF-102` now proves that target is false: twist-averaging over any fixed-proportion bow window recovers `(1+o(1)) X K log X`, so the diagonal survives on average. This is real proof-interface progress by falsification, not progress toward RH: it removes a theorem that should no longer be pursued. The surviving bow question is pointwise and sign-sensitive at the **distinguished source-fixed twist**: either the signed off-diagonal term cancels the diagonal macroscopically there, a canonical source-fixed diagonal subtraction leaves an `o(X K log X)` residual, or the present `WI-195` bridge fails. That refined question has been handed back to `weil_inertia` as `CLUE-bow-distinguished-twist-offdiagonal-cancellation.md`.

**Xi Flow** also changes its first unsupported implication, but negatively. `XF-098` constructs an exact periodic transition with an isolated double collision whose entire current guarded low-frequency resource remains `o(1)` under the full nonlinear heat flow. `XF-099` is stronger: an explicit collision crystal has `M=N/2` simultaneous double collisions and `O(1)` multiplicity-counting discrepancy from the arithmetic lattice, yet every power sum below the symmetry frequency vanishes identically throughout the exact heat trajectory. Hence neither transition existence, collision density, bounded counting discrepancy, nor generic collective collision mass can imply the order-one guarded signal demanded after `XF-097`. The live theorem is now genuinely Xi-specific: a hypothetical `Lambda>0` transition must force **symmetry-breaking spectral mass in the visible guarded modes, using source information unavailable to these matched periodic controls**, or the destination observable must change. This is a sharper falsifiable target, but the new findings reduce rather than increase confidence that generic transition geometry supplies it.

**Prime Flute** continues to contract a concrete operator interface. `PF-205` shows that smoothing each cuff on its natural physical width gives uniform first-derivative metric control while preserving a prime-summable critical `L log L` seam budget. `PF-206` then identifies a normalization trap: isotropic dilation to a unit box erases precisely the shrinking physical area that makes that budget summable. The remaining endpoint cannot therefore be justified by a naive scale-independent fixed-box application of the critical Cwikel theorem. A successful proof must retain the physical cuff scale in the weak-`S_1` estimate, use an equivalent weighted/square-function formulation, or continue through the unsmoothed mixed-resolvent route of `PF-204`; the genuine true-short-collar splice remains separate. This is useful interface progress because it both supplies one viable geometric regularization and rules out an apparently natural but scale-destructive normalization.

**Analytic Frontier** advances its own near-extremizer classification while also closing the false bow target. `ANF-101` shows that once horizontal span is only linear in cardinality, the vertical mass of a Montgomery--Taylor near-extremizer is exponentially tight after scaling; combined with `ANF-100`, the dangerous physical class is therefore linearly spreading horizontally and vertically concentrated except for an exponentially small tail. The own-line theorem remains a sign/exposure statement on that restricted class. Separately, `ANF-102` resolves the earlier bow-variance clue negatively at the uniform level and proves that any surviving bow mechanism must exploit resonant cancellation at its exact source twist rather than a window-uniform variance gain.

Most other new evidence is **classification or negative progress**, not evidence of approach to RH. `AF-186` constructs locally optimized parity clusters invisible to broad truncated associated-function observations, while `AF-187` shows that the actual Euler-log kernel resolves that explicit cluster at reciprocal-band scale; the witness is therefore a calibration, not a source-wide instability theorem. `MC-132` extends excursion blindness to growing-degree polynomial clock moments, and `MC-133`--`MC-134` show that Björner's divisor complex does not hide a long-range topological carrier: its one-parameter reduced persistent homology is an exact dyadic barcode with every bar dying at twice its birth scale. `PC-208`--`PC-209` identify the zero-depth Hardy singularity as a primitive boundary shell and then show that the canonical cyclotomic weighted power-cover normalizes to a universal unilateral shift, erasing prime-specific operator content. `PL-206`--`PL-207` show that fixed-edge resolvent transport is either trivial at two parameters or collapses under spectral reparameterization to a scalar edge plus affine change of variable. `VIS-085`--`VIS-086` push the deterministic Gram-clock null from the linear inverse-theta arc through the quadratic arc, so larger local prime-phase pictures remain removable sampling geometry well beyond the previous square-root corridor.

The strongest cross-line event is now a **resolved negative transfer**, not a shared mechanism. `WI-195` supplied `analytic_frontier` with an exact norm-matched bow question. `ANF-102` answered the window-uniform version decisively in the negative: the twist average retains the full diagonal scale. That result changes what Weil Inertia should attempt next. The accepted/proposed Analytic-Frontier handoff is therefore no longer an open request for a uniform variance theorem; the surviving pointwise question has been returned to `weil_inertia` through `research/weil_inertia/clues/CLUE-bow-distinguished-twist-offdiagonal-cancellation.md`. This is the kind of cross-line progress the Master should count: a precise theorem request was tested, falsified in its proposed form, and replaced by a strictly narrower source-specific alternative.

Xi provides a separate methodological warning with similar shape but not a common theorem. `XF-098`--`XF-099` show that making a transition more dramatic in generic geometric terms does not make it visible to the chosen low-frequency observable: even an extensive collision crystal can alias completely below its symmetry frequency. This supports matched-control discipline across the program, but it does not identify the same mathematics as the bow diagonal barrier, Prime Flute scaling, or Möbius excursion blindness.

The broader recurring pattern remains negative and should not be over-synthesized. Arithmetic Fidelity, Möbius, Prime Circle, Prime Lattice, Visual Exploration, Weil Positivity, and now Xi repeatedly show that richer state spaces, additional coordinates, stronger-looking geometry, or more dramatic events can remain generic, programmable, universal, or invisible after the final observation. This justifies demanding a source-specific discriminator at the consuming interface; it does **not** establish one global RH bottleneck or justify merging the lines.
