# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia has eight source-backed research lines: `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, and `weil_positivity`. `visual_exploration` is transversal rather than a standalone RH attack, but its canonical findings make it part of the dynamically discovered Master portfolio.

The strongest common audit is now

`retained source/provenance -> target-compatible nonescaping representation -> quantitative or signed gain -> matched-control/source specificity -> non-scalar global coupling -> independent positivity/zero-selection compatibility`.

The important shift is that **retaining structure is necessary but no longer the main shared uncertainty**. Arithmetic Fidelity now gives exact compactness/coherence criteria for finite-witness fidelity (`AF-070`, `AF-071`, `AF-074`). Across the RH-facing lines, the harder failures occur later: the Möbius Huxley--Watt endpoint survives as a bounded signed kernel but finite jets, fixed-radius analytic norms, and higher-degree factorizations are exponent-neutral (`MC-021`, `MC-023`--`MC-025`); Prime Circle keeps mesoscopic/macroscopic nonbulk modes even while natural scalar spectral summaries classicalize (`PC-136`--`PC-140`); Prime Flute's selected connected cusp scalar is reference-dependent strongly enough to defeat nonconstant real-analytic scalar normalization (`PF-164`, `PF-165`, `VIS-002`); Prime Lattice repeatedly obtains spectral, Hilbert, local-Frobenius, or critical-line structure without an independent compatible polarization/positivity theorem (`PL-116`--`PL-123`); Weil Inertia shows that neither scalar reweighting nor arbitrary target-local preconditioning restores coercivity to the full-packed multitarget defect interface (`WI-104`--`WI-108`); and Weil Positivity shows that natural Gamma/Schoenberg positive completions, parity projections, and affine shell recentering retain the sharp critical summability barrier (`WP-117`--`WP-119`, `VIS-004`).

The resulting program-level bottleneck is therefore not a theorem that these mechanisms are identical. It is a shared **compatibility obligation**: a source-specific discriminator must survive the required quotient/limit, produce quantitative cancellation or sign through a genuinely coupled global operation, and reach positivity or zero selection without that operation being chosen from the target divisor itself. Exact representations, large defect spaces, critical-axis geometry, positive Hilbert spaces, and RH-equivalent scalar readouts are all weaker than that obligation.

No current `.review.md` sidecar is open in the eight research lines. Graph and prior-art projections remain useful navigation, but the current Atlas projection trails newer canonical findings and is therefore treated as stale for portfolio allocation rather than recomputed or relied upon here.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Fidelity before compression is necessary but not sufficient.** Arithmetic Fidelity now gives exact language for nonescaping coherent witness families (`AF-070`--`AF-074`), while Prime Flute and Weil Inertia show two later failures: a retained scalar can remain non-intrinsic under the admissible reference action, and a retained family of defect spaces can remain non-coercive under every target-local conditioning. The downstream category and coupling operation are part of the mathematical claim.

2. **Scalarization and classicalization are recurrent second-stage failure modes.** Prime Circle's natural bulk/trace summaries collapse to universal or Nicolas/Artin-totient data (`PC-136`, `PC-137`, `PC-140`); Prime Lattice repeatedly reaches scalar/central L-function or known spectral realizations without the missing positivity compatibility; `VIS-003` identifies the leading Möbius endpoint oscillation with Watt's classical kernel. A real upstream structure can therefore survive while its canonical summary carries no new selector.

3. **Algebraic richness, local sign freedom, and geometric dimension are not quantitative gain.** Weil Inertia has large defect spaces with weak cumulative/coercive effect; `VIS-004` creates negative cross-prime Gram entries without changing the critical exponent; `PC-139` shows that nonbulk modes can remain numerous even after their count and simple scalar summaries classicalize. The useful discriminator must measure the global effect of the surviving relational organization, not its raw size.

4. **Generic analytic machinery is repeatedly exponent-neutral at the critical bridge.** `MC-023`--`MC-025` show this for jets, analytic norms, and higher-degree Huxley--Watt factorizations. `WP-117`--`WP-119` show the positive-geometric analogue: canonical CND/Hilbert/parity constructions can be exact and natural while critical prime assembly still diverges. In both cases the missing improvement must come from source-specific coupling, not a more elaborate wrapper around the same carrier.

5. **Prime Lattice and Weil Positivity meet at a compatibility problem but should remain separate.** Prime Lattice asks for an arithmetic/global mechanism making a spectral or cohomological realization select the divisor with the right polarization; Weil Positivity asks for a positive construction whose finite--archimedean coupling creates the required sign without circular normalization. Their constraints inform one another, but their source objects and decisive tests are not redundant enough for a merge recommendation.
