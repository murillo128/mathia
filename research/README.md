# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia has thirteen valid research lines. Ten are evidence-bearing and active: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain distinct pre-evidence scaffolds.

The strongest common bottleneck has sharpened from **carrier change plus source-valid margin** into a **source-rigid global margin gate**. A candidate carrier must retain a positive quantitative separation not only after obvious quotienting, but after the strongest admissible finite-configuration, boundary-concentration, category, matched-control, and source-realizability tests that the final theorem actually permits. The new evidence repeatedly shows that a signal can survive a thermodynamic limit, a positive representation, an exact symmetry, a local finite sample, or even a stronger information metric and still fail at the next global interface.

`analytic_frontier` gives the cleanest finite-configuration example. `ANF-014`--`ANF-015` show that the long-lattice periodization problem has a strict arithmetic stability cost above the first Mellin floor, but `ANF-016` constructs an explicit nonnegative cubic spectrum with `C(J)/p(J)=53/40<C_MT`; thermodynamic lattice constraints are therefore too weak to recover the Montgomery--Taylor ceiling. `ANF-017` then kills that entire amplitude ray using only a 15-point real support with two detuned edge gaps. The residual scalar functional is no longer `C(J)/p(J)` but `C(J)/q_real(J)`, where `q_real` is the infimum over all finite real configurations. Boundary-sensitive finite geometry is information that the infinite lattice limit erased.

`arithmetic_fidelity` supplies the abstract counterpart. `AF-096` closes the tempting predual-normal witness route: declaring the coefficient to be a genuine dual Banach space makes it an ultrasummand, forcing every barycentric fiber operator to extend and collapsing the very nonlinear-repair defect one hoped to witness. `AF-097` closes the opposite finite-local escape: even when a norm-robust global extension defect exists, every finite-dimensional pointwise observation can be matched exactly by an extendable operator. The live discriminator must therefore be globally coherent and source-natural in a category that neither trivializes the defect nor reduces to finite point samples.

`weil_inertia` now shows the same distinction inside Lamzouri's Hilbert geometry. The challenged `WI-129` claim has been withdrawn and is no longer portfolio evidence; `WI-131` replaces the blanket real-projection Riesz equivalence with the narrower valid boundary. `WI-132` identifies a normalized odd Schur complement as a task-aligned horizontal carrier, but `WI-133` proves that its global minimum eigenvalue can collapse like `O(1/M)` on a fixed-depth conjugate lattice while the actual horizontal remainder stays extensive. The right target is therefore not full-rank restoration or one worst collective mode, but a depth-weighted diagonal/average, lower spectral quantile, or localized frame quantity that retains extensive horizontal charge and can be bounded from the actual zeta source.

Several other lines reinforce the same gate. `MC-045`--`MC-046` build exact-support multiplicative terminal-prime controls whose ordinary pretentious distance and nearby endpoint-vanishing smoothings remain blind to a super-square-root endpoint discrepancy, while `MC-047` shows that strong power-aware pretentiousness does detect that discrepancy at exactly the target normalization. This repairs the information loss but not the theorem: fixed sparse twists in the strong critical regime inherit the same RH-scale obstruction. `PC-160` proves that the universal Bloch pencil suggested by the exact `t<->1-t` symmetry is strictly accretive and zero-free throughout the open unit strip, so its Riemann-like reflection is not merely non-specific but spectrally opposite to a zero-localizing mechanism. `PL-142`--`PL-143` similarly separate native positivity from completed arithmetic content: the native zeta Hardy kernel fails complete Pick at the first two-prime face, yet all positive kernel powers are unconditionally positive and infinitely divisible; the RH-sensitive Lévy positivity appears only after global completion and explicit-formula dualization, where it is already an RH-equivalent criterion.

The portfolio-level target is consequently more precise than “find a richer representation.” A serious advance should produce a **source-forced residual whose quantitative margin survives the complete admissible control class and whose aggregation is already strong enough for the destination theorem**. Detection, positivity, symmetry, rank, exact reconstruction, and even stronger metrics are useful only when the margin survives this final global test.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Finite and boundary controls are now first-class admissibility tests.** The `ANF-016` thermodynamic survivor dies under two detuned boundary gaps in `ANF-017`; `WI-133` creates a harmless collective near-null mode from an endpoint taper while preserving extensive diagonal charge; `MC-045`--`MC-046` hide endpoint mass from ordinary and nearby smoothed carriers; `WP-139` removes an apparent singular logarithm after the intrinsic normalization; and Xi Flow's remaining obstruction sits exactly in external boundary flux. Any asymptotic or bulk survivor must be retested against the finite/boundary degrees of freedom discarded by its limit.

2. **Global coherence can be invisible at every finite local observation.** `AF-097` proves this exactly for operator-extension defects, while `ANF-017` shows the complementary phenomenon that a finite global support can detect information erased by a thermodynamic average. The relevant topology and aggregation law are therefore part of the mathematical claim, not presentation choices.

3. **Native positivity, symmetry, or stronger metrics can repair one interface while leaving the target theorem untouched.** `MC-047` repairs the terminal-slab blindness but transfers the critical obstruction with it. `PC-160` turns a beautiful universal half-reflection into a zero-free strip. `PL-143` gives unconditional native infinite divisibility, while only the completed explicit-formula object carries RH-sensitive Lévy positivity. `WP-138`--`WP-139` retain positive puncture structure but miss the exact Mangoldt scale. The next step must explain why the strengthened structure is source-specific and why its margin survives to the target.

4. **Category changes must be audited before witness optimization.** `AF-096` shows that selecting a dual/predual category can annihilate the defect before any normal witness is chosen. Prime Lattice's complete-Pick repairs similarly alter the coefficient geometry, and Weil Positivity's regularizations can manufacture universal rather than prime-specific logarithms. A convenient analytic category is not neutral unless the source map and target property survive it faithfully.

5. **The useful invariant is an extensive or quantitatively stable residual after quotienting, not raw rank or nonzero structure.** Weil Inertia now needs averaged/depth-weighted Schur charge rather than `lambda_min`; Arithmetic Fidelity needs a norm-stable globally admissible defect; Analytic Frontier needs the finite-real energy floor rather than long-lattice periodization; Möbius needs a power-aware carrier plus an independently nontrivial transfer; Xi Flow needs source-controlled bulk-minus-boundary dissipation. This is the current common form of the source-rigid global margin gate.
