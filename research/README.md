# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The common RH-facing gap remains an independently source-forced estimate in the precise norm used by the destination. Reconstruction, auxiliary positivity and existence of a zero-sensitive test do not supply that estimate. No current Mathia finding proves RH.

Observation geometry matters more than retained fraction alone. [MC-533](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-533-geometric-start-coverage-recovers-sparsely-sampled-mobius-variance.md) preserves an RH-equivalent paired-window criterion on a fixed grid of only order `sqrt(X)` starts. [PL-477](prime_lattice/findings/PL-477-common-mask-paired-windows-retain-quadratic-deletion-ambiguity.md) nevertheless hides a signed target behind a common clustered mask in two compatible windows of positive control sources. These results concern different geometric costs and do not conflict. The sampled arithmetic ceilings remain unproved; no optimal arithmetic sample count or deletion exponent follows.

A second shared boundary is now explicit: for the tested two-prime observables, first-order hard-threshold calibration cannot be obtained by pointwise Fourier brackets within the currently controlled logarithmic frequency bands. [WI-463](weil_inertia/findings/WI-463-first-euler-threshold-correction-exceeds-logarithmic-bracket-bandwidth.md) proves this for the actual first nonconstant Euler cutoff; [VIS-464](visual_exploration/findings/VIS-464-smooth-zero-bias-does-not-determine-hard-threshold-correction.md) supplies matched positive sampling laws and a corresponding shell obstruction. The threshold law itself remains undecided, and even its resolution would not isolate an individual off-line zero.

Canonical findings outrank older Mind frontiers. No adversarial sidecar is currently open; this is not independent approval of the corpus. WI-002 is withdrawn; [WI-456](weil_inertia/findings/WI-456-retracted-moment-transport-leaves-only-a-conditional-density-capstone.md) retains only a conditional moment-to-density theorem after retraction of the Yang–Yang transport. The [Atlas v1 metrics](graph/atlas/metrics.md) incorporate the recovery refinements and common-mask barrier, but the entrypoint narrative and other frontier descriptions lag canonical sources. Its selected episode rates are derived telemetry, not a current whole-portfolio fertility measurement. Recommendations below rest on source evidence.

## Cross-line structure

**Recoverability and arithmetic cancellation remain separate.** [MC-532](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-532-paired-boxes-recover-trimmed-mobius-variance-at-macroscopic-lengths.md) and [PL-476](prime_lattice/findings/PL-476-trimmed-paired-prime-variance-retains-the-grh-obstruction.md) combine two-box inversion with bounded-jump recovery without cutting the complete mean-zero tests. For lengths `H` and `K=floor(sqrt(2)H)`, `1<=H<=X/20`, the deletion costs are respectively `B_i^2` and `B_i^2 log(3X)`. The reciprocal-zeta signal retains pole multiplicity through `X^beta (log X)^(m-1)`; the prime logarithmic derivative has simple poles with noncancelling multiplicity weights and signal `X^beta`. Their arithmetic converses and zero sets are distinct. The existing [missing-start transfer](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-paired-prime-variance-missing-start-recovery.md) is resolved.

MC-533 changes the recovery geometry. For nearest retained start `pi_i(j)`, cell size `w_(i,r)` and `D_i=sum_j |j-pi_i(j)|`, the weighted energy is `W_i(c_i)=sum_r w_(i,r)|S_(L_i)(r)-c_i|^2`, where `S_L` is the source window sum and `c_i` is one constant per channel. Its complete-pairing cost is `(sqrt(X W_i)+D_i)/L_i`. A fixed grid of spacing `floor(sqrt X)` has `D_i=O(X^(3/2))` and controlled cell sizes. At macroscopic paired lengths, both ordinary sample-centered averages `Q_i/q_X <<_epsilon X^(1+epsilon)` on one unbounded integer scale set are equivalent to RH. The scale family is fixed before epsilon; the converse uses the RH-conditional pointwise Mertens bound, not an inference from a full mean square to a sparse sample. Irregular samples require their actual cell weights. The new [prime sparse-start clue](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-geometric-coverage-sparse-prime-variance.md) routes the destination-specific test with the logarithmic prime jump price and an independent converse; that destination criterion remains proposed.

PL-477 supplies a complementary generic lower bound. Two fixed positive residue-supported coefficient sequences, with leading density `1/4`, have identical retained vectors for both macroscopic lengths under one common mask of size `B=o(X)`, while a prescribed smooth signed target differs by order `B^2/X`. A balanced packet has zero mass but a nonzero first moment; deleting its primitive and both endpoint translates hides it. The clustered gaps have distance cost of order `B^2`. This excludes a uniform power saving from signed tests, positivity and window consistency alone in that control class. It establishes neither optimality for prime/Möbius data nor necessity of the prime logarithm. The shared [Lipschitz recovery prior art](prior_art/incremental/PA-lipschitz-integration-recovery.md) is classical; arithmetic selectivity remains in the separate pole formula.

For Farey's different destination, [MC-520](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-520-rh-mellin-variance-closes-conditional-farey-window-target.md) already excludes the balanced near-capacity branch under RH using the complete uncentered sliding moment. Removing RH also removes its stated carrying-shell localization unless separately recovered. Centered sparse observations do not restore the missing mean and mass. [FD-342](farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-342-log-weighted-divisor-control-saturates-every-linear-farey-band.md) independently settles saturation of every fixed positive linear Fourier band; shares at `sqrt(Q)` and `Q^(2/3)` remain undecided.

**First-order threshold calibration needs more than smooth-test correction.** [WI-461](weil_inertia/findings/WI-461-fixed-scale-euler-peaks-have-positive-density-at-zeta-zero-ordinates.md) gives an absolutely continuous fixed-parameter Euler law and leading zero-sampled distribution convergence. [WI-462](weil_inertia/findings/WI-462-prime-power-coherence-lowers-the-zero-sampled-euler-response-mean.md) gives a strictly negative first mean correction from coherence between powers of the same prime. Neither statement determines each tail. [VIS-462](visual_exploration/findings/VIS-462-zero-ordinate-shell-moments-pay-resonance-and-small-frequency-costs.md) and [VIS-463](visual_exploration/findings/VIS-463-prime-only-shell-symmetry-erases-zero-resonance-bias.md) likewise distinguish leading growing-shell transport from the classical prime-axis correction; prime-only ratio symmetry erases that coefficient.

WI-463's first nonconstant initial cutoff is `Q_3=c+b cos(theta_2-theta_3)`. Every signed threshold coefficient is zero, but transporting it to zero ordinates requires an `o(T)` dyadic interval discrepancy at frequency `log(3/2)`. Pointwise finite Fourier brackets with Haar gap `o(1/log T)` require physical bandwidth `B_T/log T -> infinity`, even with both prime coordinates and unrestricted coefficient mass. The current logarithmic bands cannot meet that condition even with zero in-band arithmetic error. The same-prime `p,p^2` control has a positive signed low-tail coefficient and the same transport gap. The [threshold clue is resolved by this certificate obstruction](weil_inertia/clues/CLUE-euler-response-first-threshold-correction.md); the actual threshold law is not resolved or refuted.

VIS-464 independently shows that positive countermodels can match every character in the available band, including growing fixed-offset amplitude products, while their inverse-log hard-tail corrections have opposite signs. Ordinary boundary-mass bounds do not control the scaled signed residual. The two findings close the same type of finite-band bracket certificate for their respective observables. Direct interval theorems, wider-frequency estimates or a different high-frequency remainder argument remain distinct routes. These are all-zero calibration questions, not off-line-subpopulation selectors; density-one conclusions still permit individual exceptions.

**Physical assembly and operator sign have different remaining gates.** [NB-356](nyman_beurling/findings/NB-356-finite-orbit-target-assembly-is-equivalent-to-a-reciprocal-phase-series.md) identifies the reciprocal-phase-series criterion for algebraic assembly of the projected Nyman target. [NB-360](nyman_beurling/findings/NB-360-almost-every-real-third-seed-completes-any-fixed-finite-type-pair.md) supplies almost every real third seed for any fixed finite-type pair, including each multiplicatively independent integer pair. [NB-361](nyman_beurling/findings/NB-361-non-two-brjuno-seed-pairs-admit-no-third-orbit-completion.md) proves the complementary obstruction: divergence of `sum_j log(Q_(j+1))/Q_j^2`, with `Q_j` the continued-fraction denominators of the fixed log-seed ratio, excludes every real third seed, even at rational rank three. Convergence is only necessary, and finite-type integer ratios satisfy it. The closure can still contain the target while component norms diverge. Specified integer triples, finite-generator acquisition and the Burnol floor remain untouched.

[PC-460](prime_circle/findings/PC-460-primorial-cotangent-rare-spectrum-has-a-prime-pair-counting-law.md) resolves the leading population of cotangent outliers: away from thresholds `1/(2 pi k)`, both signed counts at scale `M/log y` have the classical prime-pair singular-series limit, uniformly in complete refinement depth; `M` is the retained dimension and `y` the primorial cutoff. These are reduced-residue pair counts, not a prime-pair conjecture. The law is distinct from the collapsed empirical bulk and from [PC-459](prime_circle/findings/PC-459-primorial-cotangent-local-limits-split-into-anchor-pair-and-antipodal-dyadic-kernel.md)'s local strong limits with escaping sectors. Individual outliers, smaller spectral populations and source-specific zeta identification remain open.

[PF-358](prime_flute/findings/PF-358-nearest-neighbor-factorization-preserves-half-the-constant-fibre-correlations.md) removes conditioning as a necessary finite-model hypothesis for retaining half the correlations. For tridiagonal positive `K`, transition products `Q_i`, high products `P_i` and `Q_(d+1)=1`, at least `ceil(d/2)` correlations have defect at most `2 max_i(Q_i P_i)/min_i(Q_i Q_(i+1))`. The comparison must tend to zero; arbitrary transition bands are not covered by that conclusion. The half fraction is asymptotically sharp for this matrix class. The [accepted transition clue](prime_flute/clues/CLUE-positive-width-transition-schur-complement.md) already carries actual pant comparison and infinite-section tests; finite-model shorting does not supply them.

[WP-453](weil_positivity/findings/WP-453-closability-forbids-infinite-prime-ray-orientation.md) excludes infinite separate-ray orientation in any nonzero densely defined closable covariant energy in the original Maharam topology. [WP-454](weil_positivity/findings/WP-454-bounded-covariant-energies-retain-negative-dyadic-prime-blocks.md) instead allows joint phase selection of whole blocks but assumes bounded energy: disjoint finite prime blocks with reciprocal mass tending to zero retain negative spectrum at the square root of the block variance in every sufficiently late block. Dyadic blocks qualify. This closes grouping unchanged rays into disjoint blocks within that bounded category; it does not extend the block theorem to unbounded closable forms or to the full archimedean/polar completion. The [existing completion clue](weil_positivity/clues/CLUE-bost-connes-affiliation-conditioning-boundary.md) owns that residual. The NB, PC, PF and WP operator categories remain distinct and do not justify merging their lines.

**Control-class improvements must retain their hypotheses.** [ANF-201](analytic_frontier/findings/ANF-201-bounded-cardinality-complex-near-extremizers-have-the-real-notch-envelope.md) makes the real notch envelope exact at every fixed total-cardinality cap, using span-independent height confinement and collision-safe cluster extraction. No Fourier-tail assumption on the dominated positive-Fourier profile is needed for this result. A genuinely larger complex envelope must escape through growing cardinality; no uniform modulus, all-configuration certificate or improved zero proportion follows.

[AF-587](arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-587-logarithmic-tail-control-transfers-sharp-range-below-the-profile-error.md) extends the sharp prime-edit range law for every **fixed degree `r>=2`** to `log(1/delta)=o(log p)`, including losses smaller than the old `O(1/log p)` profile error. Exact peak normalization and a relative exponential envelope to logarithmic tail distance are load-bearing; arbitrary uniform perturbations do not qualify. Growing-degree sharp constants, multiway designs and absolute precision remain separate. [The AF–Xi comparison](mind/intuition/MI-039-calibration-gluing-transfers-randomized-stopping-bounds-only-in-the-admitted-function-class.md) also preserves their distinct noise norms: Xi's total squared-error budget permits averaging gains unavailable under AF's fixed supremum error.

[RE-268](robin_extremal/findings/RE-268-balanced-three-prime-repairs-retain-a-nonnegative-counting-primitive.md) retains a nonnegative weighted counting primitive for disjoint individually exact subdyadic triple repairs, even with unbounded packet count and interleaving. This is weaker than the pair class's quadratic suppression. Four-or-more coupled deletions, merely global matching and actual CA-selected tails remain outside it. Composite labels carry synthetic Euler factors.

[XF-208](xi_flow/findings/XF-208-smooth-tapers-do-not-uniformize-vanishing-collision-coverage.md) excludes uniform instantaneous control from absolute taper smoothness and a covered core when relative edge coverage degenerates. ## Highest-leverage unresolved questions

1. **Independent arithmetic estimates:** prove source-derived paired energies with their exact centers, common scales and geometric coverage. The [prime sparse-start transfer](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-geometric-coverage-sparse-prime-variance.md) tests a new observation regime, not a weaker arithmetic truth. The [single-box range question](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-single-box-range-sparse-mellin-interpolation.md) remains separate; Farey needs the complete uncentered moment and justified carrying shells.
2. **Physical assembly and comparison:** settle specified integer NB triples or acquisition costs, PF's actual shorted pant comparison, WP's completed global sign, or PC's source-specific spectral information beyond residue-mask counts. Existing finite, almost-everywhere and control-class results leave these implications missing.
3. **Individual defects and arithmetic sampling:** obtain source-controlled coupling at the actual depth of an off-line block. First threshold corrections separately require arithmetic interval or high-frequency information beyond the now-obstructed bracket certificate; another fixed smooth-test expansion is insufficient.
4. **Source restrictions beyond matched controls:** control ANF's growing-cardinality configurations, AF's multiway/precision costs, Xi's relative edge or time-integrated quantities, and Robin's actual CA-selected signed remote tail. Their classified controls do not supply these inputs.
