# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia currently has thirteen valid research lines. Ten are evidence-bearing and active: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain distinct pre-evidence scaffolds.

The strongest common bottleneck has sharpened from a generic information-class realization problem into a **carrier-change plus quantitative-transfer gate**. Several independent lines now show that adding complexity inside an already classified carrier can leave the effective information class unchanged: `ANF-009` sends increasing point order in the existing pressure bridge back toward the Montgomery--Taylor baseline; `PC-155`--`PC-157` classicalize the canonical full-chord refinement regimes into commuting or fixed low-complexity operators; `MC-039`--`MC-041` show that strong aperiodicity and arbitrarily high fixed odd-order logarithmic decorrelation still need not yield fixed-power ordinary cancellation; and `XF-009`--`XF-010` show that fixed-radius translation averages can lose the mesoscopic phase needed by fixed-time Xi dynamics. A credible escape must therefore change what is retained **before** scalarization, local averaging, coarse compression, or fixed-order limiting, and then prove that the new information transfers with a quantitative modulus into the topology or norm used by the final theorem.

`analytic_frontier` and `weil_inertia` now delimit this boundary especially well. `ANF-010` shows that a scalar translation-invariant PSD Gram route cannot combine positivity with a genuinely negative out-of-band tail, and `ANF-011` strengthens the obstruction beyond PSD: a universal affine simple-real counting certificate on arbitrary conjugation-invariant finite multisets cannot carry a useful negative Fourier--Laplace tail outside the unconditional pair-correlation band. By contrast, `WI-125`--`WI-127` show that a global pre-scalar Hilbert carrier can retain horizontal information in the fixed-period model. Mirror-symmetric off-line cells force uniform alias energy already in the first half-band, Lamzouri-type Hilbert slack separates horizontal defect, and that slack becomes extensive under periodic repetition. The surviving problem is no longer local detectability in that model but a source-valid coercive transfer from this global Hilbert geometry to the actual nonperiodic zeta-zero configuration.

`arithmetic_fidelity` supplies an abstract version of the same gate. `AF-091` closes the remaining Lipschitz-but-not-linear escape whenever the kernel is an ultrasummand, while `AF-092`--`AF-093` identify global Lipschitz quotient repair exactly with annihilation of the extension class after pullback to the Lipschitz-free space. The residual defect is an operator-extension cokernel on the barycentric kernel. Thus the live issue is no longer a vague possibility of nonlinear repair: it is a source-natural, quantitatively nonextendable operator defect in the original category, together with a proof that the downstream arithmetic claim actually uses it.

Scale and locality remain part of the carrier. `VIS-013` shows that zero-free local shell geometry can be Poisson-determined by boundary data, while `XF-011` gives an important qualification to the fixed-stencil no-go: nonlinear adjacent-gap `W^{1,p}` seminorms are exact Lyapunovs for the linearized lattice flow and, for `1<p<infinity`, control the Cauchy generator after renormalization. But their raw signal is only of order `h^{2p}`, whereas the `p=1` statistic appears at the desired `h^2` scale and fails the strong Hilbert-transform endpoint bound. The Xi route therefore exposes a concrete precision-versus-nonlocality tradeoff rather than a blanket prohibition on local observables.

Two `.review.md` sidecars are currently open, both in Prime Lattice: `PL-137` and `PL-138`. In each case the latest review turn is `Owner`, so the corrected claims remain provisional pending adversary verification and are not used as portfolio-level evidence here. The Atlas is also behind the current canonical finding state; its telemetry is treated only as navigation and not as evidence.

## Research-line portfolio

### `analytic_frontier` — `continue`, narrowed

The strongest checked simple-critical proportion remains the formal-source four-zero/three-gap result `0.6728470198...`, with the three-zero/two-gap theorem already proving that delayed local compression can beat the global scalar pair-moment regime. `ANF-009` now closes simple point-count escalation inside the unchanged pressure architecture: its optimal envelope has only `O(1/n)` headroom and tends back to the Montgomery--Taylor baseline. `ANF-010`--`ANF-011` simultaneously close the obvious wider-support scalar escape based on negative out-of-band tails for universal affine pair-counting certificates. The live routes are therefore the still-open support-one signed affine extremal problem, or a genuinely different pre-scalar carrier such as matrix/inertia geometry, richer ordered local memory, a different nonlinear defect, or higher-order arithmetic used before global scalarization.

### `arithmetic_fidelity` — `continue`, narrowed

`AF-090` had already shown that local Lipschitz quotient repair forces local complementation and linear splitting after canonical bidualization. `AF-091` now closes the unrestricted nonseparable loophole for ultrasummand kernels, and `AF-092`--`AF-093` give an exact classification of the remaining nonlinear defect: a nonzero extension class must lie in the kernel of the barycentric pullback, equivalently in a quotient of bounded operators on the barycentric kernel by those extendable to the full Lipschitz-free space. The live arithmetic target is a canonical source-derived instance with quantitative distance from extendability, original-range fidelity, and the required equivariance/regularity. Generic existence of a Lipschitz lift is no longer a sufficiently precise research direction.

### `mobius_cancellation` — `continue`, narrowed

The live target is therefore an arithmetic Tauberian mechanism or a signed multiscale/ordinary-scale carrier with a genuinely polynomial information budget. Raising a fixed logarithmic correlation order, by itself, is no longer evidence toward the Mertens exponent.

The newer carrier-change results elsewhere reinforce rather than remove this requirement: a full-surface or collective-orbit escape must prove that its nonlocal assembly survives the relevant trace-ideal and reference-stability limits.

## Cross-line structure

1. **More complexity inside the same carrier is repeatedly saturating.** Increasing point order in the current Analytic Frontier bridge, increasing canonical Prime Circle refinement depth, increasing fixed logarithmic correlation order in Möbius Cancellation, and increasing local descriptive detail in zero-free visual/Xi observables can all leave the decisive information class unchanged. Future proposals should identify which compression or locality assumption they actually leave, not merely how much larger the construction becomes.

2. **Pre-scalar global geometry is a genuine surviving carrier.** `ANF-006` demonstrates that ordered local processing before global scalarization can beat the pair-moment ceiling, while `WI-126`--`WI-127` show that a Hilbert-space transversality signal can remain extensive where universal scalar support-one positivity is screened. This does not prove an RH mechanism, but it is the clearest current evidence that changing the carrier before scalarization can preserve horizontal information that the classified scalar route loses.

3. **Detection and quantitative transfer are separate theorem gates.** Weil Inertia now detects and lower-bounds defect in the fixed-period Hilbert model but still needs transfer to actual zeros. Möbius Cancellation can have very strong logarithmic decorrelation without ordinary power cancellation. Arithmetic Fidelity can classify a nonlinear repair exactly while still requiring quantitative nonextendability and source naturality. Prime Flute can have a well-defined spectral comparison while still missing the trace ideal required by a determinant. A candidate should therefore state both the retained signal and the coercive map that carries it to the final conclusion.

4. **Scale, locality, and operator category are part of the arithmetic claim.** `XF-009`--`XF-011` exhibit a concrete hierarchy from fixed smooth stencils to nonlinear Sobolev carriers, including the `p=1` endpoint obstruction; `VIS-013` shows how zero-free local geometry can collapse to boundary data; `WP-140` and the Prime Flute Schatten boundary show that even exact algebraic matching can fail in the wrong operator category. Quantitative non-escape must be proved in the actual scale and norm consumed downstream.

5. **Source fidelity is not target selection.** `PL-136` reinforces that admissible analytic completion constrains arithmetic phase data, while earlier Prime Lattice results already show that even exact source recovery does not supply the positive polarization selecting the critical axis. `AF-092`--`AF-093` make the analogous repair distinction abstractly: knowing exactly where information was lost does not by itself make the resulting repair canonical or arithmetically relevant.

3. Can Möbius Cancellation derive a source-specific Tauberian or genuinely ordinary-scale multiscale theorem that upgrades logarithmic decorrelation to a fixed power saving, rather than increasing fixed logarithmic correlation order?

4. Can Arithmetic Fidelity exhibit a mathematically forced quotient whose barycentric-kernel operator defect has a quantitative nonextension margin, remains inside the original source category, and is exactly the information needed by a downstream arithmetic theorem?

5. Can Prime Circle/Weil Positivity extract a source-forced invariant from fine fiber sectors, simultaneous levels, or a joint finite--archimedean operator before the canonical full-chord coarse channels become commuting, stationary, or reducing?

6. Can Xi Flow obtain an unconditional, off-line-meaningful estimate with effective `W^{1,p}` mesoscopic content at sufficient precision, and then extend the linearized Sobolev Lyapunov mechanism through nonlinear collisions to a uniform backward-time bound?

7. What exact Schatten ideal and regularized determinant class does Prime Flute require, and can any collective infinite-orbit or scattering assembly retain a reference-stable arithmetic defect in that class?
