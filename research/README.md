# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia currently has thirteen valid research lines. Ten are evidence-bearing and active: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain distinct pre-evidence scaffolds.

The strongest common bottleneck is now an **information-class realization gate**. A candidate mechanism must do more than encode the target faithfully or display structure: it must preserve a relation, scale, arithmetic source, or spectral/operator type that the known scalarizations and controls cannot reproduce, and it must realize that information in the topology, regularity class, positivity class, or operator ideal actually consumed by the final theorem. The main negative results across Mathia increasingly identify proposals that merely reparameterize a classified information source.

The clearest positive counterexample to excessive pessimism is `analytic_frontier`. `ANF-003`--`ANF-005` show that common-character vector channels and finite affine/convex pair-moment lifts can collapse back to one scalar signed support-one witness, while `ANF-006`/`ANF-007` show that relational depth can genuinely matter: a one-gap certificate gives no gain, the three-zero/two-successive-gap theorem reaches `0.6727373345...`, and the fully machine-checked four-zero/three-gap theorem improves this further to `0.6728470198...`. These numerical bounds are formal-source/prior-art results rather than Mathia novelty. Their strategic significance is that ordered local configuration retains unconditional information that global scalar pair-moment compression loses.

Three other developments sharpen the realization gate. `AF-088` gives exact scalar Lipschitz repair on weak-star compact dual fibers under the appropriate fiber and metric hypotheses, and `AF-090` then shows that a local Lipschitz right inverse for a Banach quotient already forces local complementation and bounded linear splitting after canonical bidualization while preserving exact metric distortion in the bidual. The remaining unrestricted nonseparable loophole is therefore original-range/category fidelity rather than absence of linear structure after completion; in the separable quotient setting the local Lipschitz route collapses back to an original-space linear section. `WP-140` closes the distribution-valued loophole for equivariant compact radial-solenoid Gamma intertwiners: reciprocal compact dynamics still force modulus one and therefore cannot carry genuine Gamma growth/decay. `PC-154` identifies the full fixed-order linked trace/logdet cluster hierarchy with prime-tuple singular-series data. Prime Circle therefore does retain arithmetic information, but fixed linked clusters reorganize an already classified arithmetic source rather than automatically creating a new one.

Scale is part of the same gate. Fixed or convergent Mellin modes in Möbius Cancellation, finite local residual jets in `VIS-012`, finite Xi-flow collision jets in `XF-007`, and increasingly broad fixed/periodic correlation screens in Weil Inertia all become reducible to moments, aliasable, or screenable once the adversarial scale is exposed. `WI-124` strengthens the periodic obstruction sharply: an exactly zeta-mirror-symmetric finite-period off-line cell must leak a reciprocal alias already at some `0<alpha<=1/2`; the unresolved issue is quantitative amplitude and protection from cancellation, not finding a support-one frequency. Across these lines the credible escape routes are mesoscopic, multiscale, nonperiodic, globally coupled, or non-fixed-order rather than merely higher-dimensional at one fixed scale.

No current `.review.md` sidecar is open. The Atlas remains useful for navigation but is treated as a derived projection rather than evidence when it lags canonical findings.

## Research-line portfolio

### `analytic_frontier` — `continue`, narrowed

`ANF-006` is the strongest positive portfolio update: the fully machine-checked four-zero/three-gap certificate gives the unconditional lower bound `0.6728470198...`. `ANF-007` additionally isolates three zeros/two successive normalized gaps as the minimum local configuration depth at which this bridge can improve the one-gap scalar regime; the corresponding three-point theorem gives `0.6727373345...`, while two points cannot beat Montgomery--Taylor. The live target is now the strongest theorem-grade higher-configuration inequality and the exact ceiling of ordered local pair-correlation information before genuinely higher-order arithmetic, wider support, or a different source becomes necessary. More common-character vector features or globally summed finite pair moments are not progress unless they escape `ANF-003`--`ANF-005` scalarization.

### `arithmetic_fidelity` — `continue`, narrowed

`AF-081`--`AF-090` now separate exact recovery, bounded-scale uniform repair, global Lipschitz repair, linear/tangent splitting, weak-star compact-fiber repair, local complementation, bidual splitting, and naturality. `AF-090` is the sharp current boundary: one local Lipschitz quotient section globalizes with the same metric constant after canonical bidual relaxation and forces a bounded linear section of the full bidual quotient; with a separable quotient target it collapses all the way to an original-space bounded linear section. In the unrestricted nonseparable case the remaining escape is whether representatives can stay inside the original source with the required quantitative fidelity. The live arithmetic question is therefore source-natural original-category repair: equivariance, canonicity, higher regularity, admissible topology, and a modulus strong enough for the downstream theorem.

### `farey_discrepancy` — pre-evidence

Keep dormant until a canonical theorem-grade finding distinguishes a genuinely local or multiscale Farey invariant from the classical Franel--Landau/Mobius discrepancy and from matched reduced-rational controls. A complete scalar reduction should be handed to `mobius_cancellation` rather than duplicated here.

### `mobius_cancellation` — `continue`, narrowed

The fixed-frequency frontier is now largely classified. Fixed Mellin modes expose their boundary directly, convergent or asymptotically fixed Mellin-frequency escapes close, and product-fiber analyses do not hide cancellation inside a fixed quotient. The accepted joint dyadic covariance diagnostic preserves a genuinely different multiscale relation. Highest leverage is to convert that joint covariance, harmonic leakage, or another cross-scale statistic into a theorem-grade bound that cannot be reconstructed from fixed or convergent scalar Mellin transforms.

### `nyman_beurling` — pre-evidence

Keep dormant until the line produces a theorem-grade discriminator in target-sensitive approximation geometry: a rate, conditioning obstruction, or dual certificate that survives equivalent basis changes and synthetic Gram controls without importing an RH-equivalent Mobius estimate.

### `prime_circle` — `continue`, narrowed

Finite-radius chord algebra already localizes the top Fourier band in operator norm, and canonical dominant cross-level transport is CRT-flat. The newer fixed-moment/logdet analysis now identifies the arithmetic source exactly: fixed polynomial moments and fixed linked trace/logdet clusters are prime-tuple singular-series correlation data, culminating in `PC-154`. This is positive arithmetic retention but also a closure result for the fixed hierarchy. A live escape must use non-fixed order, critical/mesoscopic scaling, a non-polynomial statistic, residual cross-level organization, or another operator mechanism that provably extracts information beyond the classified fixed prime-tuple hierarchy.

### `prime_flute` — `continue`, narrowed

Schatten bookkeeping remains the decisive theorem gate. The singular-value ladder can be Hilbert--Schmidt while the same resolvent square misses every `S_r` endpoint with `r<2`; determinant or trace arguments therefore need the exact operator ideal and regularized determinant class established rather than inferred from `S_2`. No newer cross-line result removes this requirement.

### `prime_lattice` — `continue`, narrowed

Finite phase samples can separate integer prefixes, but injective multiplicative phase fingerprints do not manufacture modular duality. The current findings isolate the modular input behind the relevant functional equations and delimit Boolean/Walsh enrichments that still fail to supply it. The live target is a genuinely modular or arithmetic source for the functional equation and the missing positive polarization, not a richer encoding of the same multiplicative scaffold.

### `robin_extremal` — pre-evidence

Keep dormant until a canonical result gives a theorem-level restriction on extremal exponent transitions or a controlled discrete-versus-continuous variational defect that survives synthetic prime-like controls without assuming RH-strength prime-distribution errors.

### `visual_exploration` — `continue`

`VIS-012` makes the local-information boundary precise: a finite residual derivative jet around a repeated zero is a polynomial function of finitely many zero-centered power sums of the nearby normalized zero positions. Such jets add no information beyond finite local moments, with symmetry able to erase some odd components. Visual Exploration remains useful as a transversal generator of exact diagnostics, but new evidence should be tail-sensitive, mesoscopic, or explicitly nonlocal rather than a prettier finite local jet.

### `weil_inertia` — `continue`, narrowed

Fixed-order bandlimited correlation channels, regular moving-edge screens, and periodic compensators now face increasingly explicit no-go results. `WI-124` uses the actual same-ordinate zeta mirror symmetry to show that any density-one finite-period off-line cell must have a nonzero reciprocal harmonic at some `alpha<=1/2`, eliminating the idea that exact periodic compensation can hide all deterministic leakage near the support-one edge. What remains open is quantitative and global: lower-bound enough first-half alias mass in terms of horizontal defect and prevent cancellation by the complementary zeta amplitude. Aperiodic or growing-period screens remain viable only if they make these aliases quantitatively negligible while preserving the other constraints.

### `weil_positivity` — `continue`, narrowed

The scalar Gamma route is now closed in two complementary senses. Arbitrary scalar CND warps after de-subordination are noncanonical reparameterizations, while bounded and now distribution-valued equivariant intertwiners on the compact radial solenoid still force modulus one. `WP-140` therefore closes a natural compact-distributional loophole without ruling out noncompact/infinite-register, non-equivariant, operator-valued, or genuinely global spectral constructions. A viable bridge must carry real Gamma growth/decay and have its positivity forced by the source rather than inserted by scalar functional calculus.

### `xi_flow` — `continue`, narrowed

Collision-safe symmetric coordinates remain correct locally, but `XF-007` shows that finite collision jets collapse to finitely many local reciprocal-gap moments. Together with the failure of absolute-gap bootstraps, this places the needed fixed-time lattice memory at mesoscopic rather than finite-jet scale. The live target is a scale-free or mesoscopic configuration observable that remains regular through collisions and participates in a global monotonicity or positivity theorem rather than only recording universal local heat-flow geometry.

The thirteen lines remain mathematically distinct. The ten evidence-bearing programs should stay active at the narrowed frontiers above; current evidence does not justify a merge, pause, split, or new research line.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Relational depth matters more than channel count.** `ANF-003`--`ANF-005` show that many coordinates can still scalarize, while `ANF-006` shows that preserving the ordered relation among three consecutive zeros can create a genuine gain that one-gap data cannot. This is the cleanest current demonstration that a proposal should name the relation it preserves, not merely the number of observables it adds.

2. **A fixed hierarchy can retain arithmetic and still be informationally closed.** `PC-154` identifies fixed linked logdet clusters with prime-tuple singular-series data rather than declaring them information-free. Prime Lattice supplies the complementary warning: an injective phase fingerprint can preserve its source perfectly and still lack the modular input required by the target theorem. The audit question is therefore whether a new construction changes arithmetic provenance, not merely whether it remembers its input.

3. **Scale is an information class.** Möbius fixed/convergent Mellin modes, `VIS-012` finite residual jets, `XF-007` finite collision jets, and Weil Inertia's fixed/periodic screens all become classifiable at a fixed local scale. The surviving routes are multiscale, mesoscopic, aperiodic, or globally coupled, and must come with a theorem showing that the relevant information survives the limit rather than leaking into near-null or cancelable directions.

4. **Local repair already carries hidden linear structure.** `AF-090` shows that a local Lipschitz right inverse is not an arbitrarily nonlinear accident: it forces local complementation, exact metric globalization in the canonical bidual, and linear splitting of the full bidual quotient. In separable quotient settings it forces an original-space linear section. The genuine remaining gate is range/category fidelity—whether the repair stays in the original source—and then naturality, equivariance, higher regularity, and quantitative compatibility with the arithmetic theorem.

5. **Spectral/operator type is part of realization.** `WP-140` shows that even distributional compact radial-solenoid intertwiners cannot carry non-unimodular Gamma amplitude; Prime Flute shows that `S_2` membership does not imply the stronger Schatten endpoint a determinant argument may need; Weil Inertia shows that an observable can exist while its signal remains cancelable or quantitatively inaccessible. Matching data without matching the conclusion's operator class is not enough.

## Highest-leverage unresolved questions

1. How far can the `ANF-006` ordered three-zero/two-gap gain be strengthened by a theorem-grade three- or four-zero configuration certificate, and where is the exact ceiling before higher-order arithmetic or wider support is unavoidable?

2. Can Möbius Cancellation turn the accepted joint dyadic covariance signal into a quantitative multiscale theorem that is not reconstructible from fixed, convergent, or asymptotically fixed Mellin frequencies?

3. Can Prime Circle find a non-fixed-order, critical-scale, or non-polynomial operator statistic that escapes the `PC-154` fixed prime-tuple singular-series hierarchy, and can Prime Lattice supply a genuinely modular source rather than another injective phase encoding?

4. Which concrete Arithmetic Fidelity quotient can force source-natural representatives to remain in the original source category with the required equivariance, regularity, and quantitative fidelity, rather than only in the canonical bidual allowed by `AF-090`?

5. Can Weil Inertia quantitatively convert horizontal defect into protected lower-half alias mass beyond `WI-124`, or can an aperiodic/growing-period construction make every such forced harmonic negligible? Can Weil Positivity build a noncompact or operator-valued finite--archimedean bridge carrying genuine Gamma modulus with source-forced positivity?

6. Can Xi Flow construct a collision-robust mesoscopic fixed-time memory observable that survives the finite-jet collapse and enters a global monotonicity/positivity theorem?

7. What exact Schatten ideal and regularized determinant class does Prime Flute require, including the lowest power at which the needed trace structure actually holds?

## Portfolio changes proposed

No pause, merge, split, or new-line action is justified by the live evidence. The ten evidence-bearing lines should remain active with the narrowed targets above, while Farey Discrepancy, Nyman--Beurling, and Robin Extremal remain pre-evidence scaffolds. No new Master clue is needed: existing accepted or proposed clues already cover the live multiscale, configuration-level, modular, and operator-valued tests. The next portfolio-level discriminator is whether a line can demonstrate a genuine increase in the information class available to the final theorem—new relational depth, scale, arithmetic provenance, or spectral/operator type—and then realize it with quantitative non-escape in the required category.
