## Cross-line structure

**Observation design does not supply arithmetic cancellation.** For the actual Möbius and identity-residue mod-5 Mangoldt sources, use lengths `L_1=floor(X/20)` and `L_2=floor(sqrt(2)L_1)`. MC-537/PL-482 use quarter-power-many independent uniform observations per channel and permit one common unbounded scale set fixed before epsilon. MC-539/PL-484 instead use two starts per channel, with `V_i=|S_(L_i)(R_1)-S_(L_i)(R_2)|^2/4`, and require `V_i <<_(epsilon,omega) X^(1+epsilon)` for every `epsilon>0` on every sufficiently late prescribed scale. Repeated draws count, the source precedes sampling, and the separate arithmetic pole formulas and pointwise converses remain essential. The shared recovery operator does not equate RH with joint mod-5 GRH.

The finite pair-tail lemma is `D<=2T+AX p_T^2+A p_T`, where `D` is population mean absolute pair dispersion, jumps are bounded by `A`, and `p_T` is the probability that two independent uniform values differ by more than `T`. A hypothetical pole at real part `beta>1/2` forces `D_1+D_2 >> X^beta` on every sufficiently large block. For every fixed `1/2<gamma<beta`, the probabilities of `max_i V_i>X^(2gamma)` are bounded below by constant multiples of `X^(-(1-beta)/2)` for Möbius and `X^(-(1-beta)/2)/sqrt(log(3X))` for primes. Both have divergent sums on `X_k=k^4`; independent repetitions then give infinitely many excessive pair energies. A favorable post-selected subsequence cannot replace this coverage. The [prime transfer clue](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-two-start-prime-detection-prescribed-scales.md) is resolved by PL-484.
