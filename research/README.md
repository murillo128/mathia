## Cross-line structure

**Physical phase and source selection cannot be replaced by broad distribution.** ANF-194 limits a particular absolute-error technique; RE-253/255 isolate an ordinary-prime critical signed-moment/endpoint-contrast gate, while RE-254/256/257 show that even exact real-label global Mertens fidelity or near-exact integer-label fidelity does not substitute for quantitative local control. FD-311's sliding-Möbius and MC-506/516's physical-conductor signed correlations are different destination estimates; none transfers by analogy.

**Absolute, relative, normalized and spectral assertions remain different.** PL-456's `X log X` absolute floor does not imply PL-421's normalized cone; PL-458's conditional `t=0` matrix asymptotic is not a frequency-uniform source theorem. PF-345's Schur-shortened trace lacks its normalized-angle estimate; WP-438's mixed-prime rank/closability gate is specific to one thermal star. PC-443 has band-shaped essential spectral *support* alongside pure-point vector spectral measures, distinct from its absolutely continuous finite-matrix empirical eigenvalue limit. These do not provide a physical zeta positivity discriminator.

**Adaptation, calibration and noise norms cannot be conflated.** AF-567's two-query gain depends on real-profile order and a known output origin; AF-568 shows an unknown shared origin requires three adaptive queries. XF-193 is a prechosen, radius-compensated comparison; XF-194/195 prove `Theta(Q)` for fixed-total-`ell^2` unknown-radius edit detection even with adaptive scales, and XF-196 extends it to separated candidate *identification* subject to reciprocal leakage control. **Farey rank, value, cut location and orientation are distinct observations.** FD-327 fixes three local defect *values* but varies positions and transport spectra. FD-328/329 show rigidity for three/four *ranks* and a five-rank collision with unequal defect values and only `Theta(1/p)` transport-energy separation. The accepted [[research/visual_exploration/clues/CLUE-farey-fixed-defect-cut-orientation-control]] handles three-value cuts; [[research/visual_exploration/clues/CLUE-five-defect-rank-collision-spectral-control]] isolates the new five-rank question without importing either spectrum formula.

**Available witnesses still need target occupation.** NB-348 gives positive-density, physical integer-offset *operator* witnesses, a stronger sampling statement than NB-347, but not an occupied Ford-target direction or the necessary distance lower bound. WI-449 controls real-frequency costs rather than indefinite off-line zero blocks; RE-255 identifies rather than proves the selected prime contrast saving.
