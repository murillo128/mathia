# Cross-line structure

The dominant distinction is now **information equivalence versus proof-theoretic usefulness versus endpoint coercivity**. Farey supplies the clean reversible case: adding the whole discrepancy field or extra Fourier bandwidth does not add arithmetic information, but an equivalent representation could still matter if it proves a stronger estimate. Weil Positivity supplies the opposite failure: a rich noncommutative positive model can contain more ambient structure, yet the chosen frustration-free zero-mode readout collapses it to pairwise common kernels and deletes nonzero archimedean spectrum. Visual supplies a quantitative relaxation failure: even genuine holonomy cannot make the normalized magnetic certificate reach RMS scale when the effective edge dimension is too large. These are different obstructions and should not be merged into one generic notion of “information loss.”

A second common pattern is that matched-control frontiers are becoming quantitative. Arithmetic Fidelity knows the finite sign order that a given half-plane depth can support; Prime Circle knows that all sub-square-root positional resolution is reproducible once total count is matched; Visual knows that the entire strictly subcritical magnetic spectral route has the wrong dimension scaling; Weil Inertia knows that the surviving screening problem is cross-scale incidence, not window scarcity. Future work should target the first scale or category where these controls actually fail instead of producing another subcritical refinement.

A third pattern is that endpoint currencies are increasingly exact. Analytic Frontier has a physical activation clock but still needs a source-energy theorem. Nyman has an exact weighted interpolation metric but needs a zero-specific approximation obstruction. Prime Lattice has a scalar virial/stress identity but still needs state-side regularity and an arithmetic sign. Möbius has exact odd-moment constraints but needs arithmetic control of occupancy, conductors, and exceptional characters. The existing global fidelity/realization clue and `MI-016` already organize these gates; the current evidence does not justify a new Master clue.

1. **Analytic Frontier:** prove or refute source-energy non-excisability for the physically activated same-profile cloud, and quantify the stage dependence strongly enough to decide whether the sharp `A asymp ell^2` clock can be iterated at growing depth.

Weil Positivity must leave fixed-kernel frustration-free zero-mode readout; Prime Lattice must justify Pohozaev/virial regularity and then obtain a rational-prime-specific sign.
4. **Prime Circle / Visual / Weil Inertia:** work at the first unresolved critical interface rather than below it: `B~sqrt(q)` for Prime Circle, stronger-than-normalized-spectral torus/cycle certificates or a new scaling for Visual, and cross-scale prime-gap incidence or null-mode amplitudes for Weil Inertia.
