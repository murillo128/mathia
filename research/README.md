## Cross-line structure

Möbius Cancellation and Farey Discrepancy now expose two sides of the same strategic requirement without becoming the same line. Möbius shows that endpoint information must survive coordinate projection and source cuts: a large global radical can be assembled from cheap pieces unless the hereditary restriction profile is audited. Farey shows that physical-scale localization alone is still a marginalization: the active witness disappears only when scale and multiplicative rank are resolved jointly. The common lesson is not a new theorem but a stricter gate for future claims: the source constraint must remain coercive under the restrictions that preserve the endpoint and must charge the coordinate in which the matched control concentrates.

Weil Inertia and Robin Extremal now separate two superficially similar “mass can escape to infinity” scenarios. In the screened one-signed Weil branch, rearrangement is monotone for the relevant prime-deleted form and collapses the tail geometry to one finite aperture, so a propagation theorem is unnecessary. In Robin, fixed-box rearrangement/compactness already gives local visibility and the surviving matched controls use growing reciprocal radius; `RE-136` proves that this exterior escape has a sharp exponential price. A future tightness argument should therefore first ask whether a monotone compression exists; only when it does not should global tail control be treated as the missing source theorem.

Nyman--Beurling, Prime Circle, and Weil Positivity now form a useful representation-exhaustion triad. Nyman shows that finite full recurrences and even the full natural prefix can remain catastrophically conditioned without an actual-zero arrangement law. Prime Circle shows that every fixed finite oriented-frame tensor is already classical joint Fourier data. Weil Positivity shows that infinite fixed-node scalar reductions and the canonical empty-side moving-node boundary layer either retain the wrong asymptotic defect or universalize away the arithmetic remainder. Increasing the size, order, or stage count of a representation is not a new information channel unless one can identify the source-specific discriminator that appears only after that increase.

Arithmetic Fidelity and Visual Exploration make the corresponding statistical/parameter warning quantitative. A positive fixed Riesz order improves critical summability, but `AF-354` shows that this advantage disappears when the order approaches zero too quickly. A fully calibrated collision statistic can be nondegenerate under a stochastic null, but `VIS-232` shows that a stronger confirmation-sample control can instead condition away the statistic itself. Uniformity in the parameter and nondegeneracy under the admitted control are both part of endpoint fidelity; fixed-parameter success or a more restrictive null is not automatically stronger evidence.

No new global clue is justified. The durable fidelity, source-realization, destination-sensitivity, conditioning, and localization machinery already covers the program-level abstraction, while the new findings sharpen line-local decisive tests rather than exposing one shared unowned lemma. The accepted Robin occupancy clue has already been consumed by `RE-135`; its surviving exterior-tightness question is canonical line work and should not be reopened as another Master clue.
