# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia has eight source-backed research lines: `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, and `weil_positivity`. `visual_exploration` is transversal rather than a standalone RH attack, but it has canonical current findings and therefore belongs in the dynamically discovered Master portfolio.

The strongest common audit remains

`retained source information -> equivalence-compatible target/provenance -> quantitative information budget -> matched-control/source specificity -> stable realization -> global/sign assembly`.

The current evidence sharpens the separations in that chain. Arithmetic Fidelity shows that global safe-envelope existence can be trivial while a provenance-constrained lift is unstable under arbitrarily small ambient renorming (`AF-060`). Möbius Cancellation still lacks a polynomial local-to-global bridge: `MC-009` records a potentially weaker mean-absolute Mertens endpoint, while `MC-010`--`MC-011` repair only specific defects in the external argument. Prime Circle's finite resultant deformation route classicalizes through collision hyperplanes, arrangement master functions, and the inverse-square chord Laplacian (`PC-126`--`PC-128`). Prime Flute's canonical connected bottom Ruelle product reaches `s=0` with a finite nonzero value and a logarithmic cusp rather than a zero or pole (`PF-161`).

Prime Lattice separates three phenomena: the regularized prime-shift Hamiltonian remains a scalar Fourier multiplier without the required discrete spectral mechanism (`PL-107`); a source-natural lattice-coherence observable can genuinely force zeta zeros near the Euler/pole edge through scalar energy `log n` (`PL-108`); and classical prime-axis cancellation criteria already give RH-equivalent readouts without mixed exponent-lattice geometry (`PL-109`). Visual Exploration has so far been most valuable as a cross-line falsification and classicalization layer: CRT wheel structure can be an exact rank-one coordinate artifact (`VIS-001`), compact Prime-Flute cusp residuals can fill interval closures under a narrow reference action (`VIS-002`), the centered Möbius harmonic endpoint has Watt's sawtooth as its leading boundary model (`VIS-003`), and a natural Gamma-Schoenberg shell recentering creates negative Gram terms without moving the sharp `sigma=1` summability threshold (`VIS-004`). Weil Inertia confines large pairwise defects to low-denominator boundary resonances and identifies the residual rank defect exactly (`WI-094`--`WI-096`). Weil Positivity shows that nonlinear selection is algebraically possible while critical entropy/flow energies or canonical Jensen scalarizations still fail at the sign/regularity gate (`WP-103`--`WP-105`).

The current canonical corpus excludes withdrawn `AF-058` and `WI-067`; the portfolio uses their surviving corrected successors, including `AF-060` and `WI-068`. `PL-107` remains current after its review closure. No unresolved review sidecar currently blocks use of the canonical findings summarized here. Graph/Atlas structure is used only for navigation and portfolio telemetry, and prior-art coverage only for contextual saturation; neither is treated as theorem evidence. The current Atlas v1 reports 74.5% visited coverage, 46.8% live frontier, and a barrier-heavy recent frontier, but its metrics do not by themselves justify changing any line recommendation.

The strategic pattern is narrower than a shared mathematical mechanism. Exact representations, RH-equivalent readouts, positive carriers, large algebraic defects, attractive visual structure, and familiar spectral boundaries can all be real while failing at a later and different gate. The highest-value work remains at the first source-specific residual left after the strongest matched control, with target semantics, quantitative transfer, realization category, and final global sign fixed independently of the desired zero set.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Representation or readout success is not mechanism success.** `AF-060` distinguishes global existence from provenance-constrained realization; `PL-108`--`PL-109` distinguish genuine zero forcing or RH-equivalent readout from a critical-line-generating geometry; `WP-103` distinguishes an exact sparse nonlinear selector from an independent sign theorem. `VIS-001` supplies the visual analogue: rich recursive-looking geometry may be an exact coordinate product artifact.

2. **Standard finite spectral or visual enrichments repeatedly classicalize rather than create a new arithmetic layer.** `PC-126`--`PC-128` land in binary-form resultants, hyperplane-arrangement master functions, and inverse-square chord operators; `PF-161` turns the remaining canonical Ruelle boundary into a harmonic cusp; `VIS-003` identifies the dominant centered Möbius endpoint oscillation with Watt's classical sawtooth; `WP-104`--`WP-105` reduce natural cover-Jensen scalarizations to harmonic/entropy data and a known singular endpoint. Prior-art coverage makes more wrappers around these same categories low-yield, but does not itself establish a no-go theorem.

3. **Algebraic or geometric richness and metric/global usefulness must be separated.** `WI-094`--`WI-096` give the cleanest rank example: the pairwise defect may be extensive while its cumulative canonical overlap vanishes. `VIS-004` gives a complementary Hilbert-space example: recentering genuinely creates negative cross terms, yet the integrated dyadic shell norm keeps the same critical exponent. Neither large defect nor local sign freedom is a proxy for a macroscopic signed cancellation resource.

4. **A weaker endpoint does not remove the quantitative bridge.** `MC-009` may make mean-absolute Mertens size a useful target if the external theorem survives audit, but `MC-001`, `MC-006`, `MC-010`, and `MC-011` leave the source-to-endpoint polynomial transfer unresolved. `VIS-003` narrows the bounded endpoint kernel further by showing that its leading oscillatory boundary is already Watt's sawtooth, so any advantage must live in the signed correction, non-asymptotic corner, or coupling to the centered coefficients rather than in a visually distinct bulk kernel.

5. **The productive cross-line transfers are audits, not mergers.** Arithmetic Fidelity supplies provenance/target-transport and robustness tests; Möbius Cancellation supplies information-budget and matched-control discipline; Visual Exploration supplies representation-artifact and boundary-model falsification; Prime Circle, Prime Flute, Prime Lattice, and Weil Positivity supply classicalization/readout controls; Weil Inertia supplies the warning to measure cumulative metric effect rather than algebraic dimension alone. None of the current findings proves that the underlying research mechanisms should be merged.
