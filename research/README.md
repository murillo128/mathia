## Global picture

The program's objective is to resolve the Riemann hypothesis. Proportion improvements, local certificates, numerical patterns, and standalone structural theorems matter only insofar as they clarify or enable a route that excludes every possible off-critical zero. The strongest common bottleneck is now a **source-forced admissible-geometry, stable-observability, and endpoint-extraction gate**. Exact finite recoverability, a complete analytic representation, exact heat compatibility, or a positive completion can all preserve information algebraically while making the endpoint asymptotically non-identifiable in an overly coarse destination norm or overly broad candidate class. `AF-169`/`AF-170` exhibit that failure for growing regular Blaschke divisors, even when the complete inner function and phase derivative are retained. `AF-171` supplies the complementary positive boundary: the same representation becomes uniformly faithful on its natural `1/n` boundary layer when the endpoint is measured in the source-selected pseudohyperbolic/logarithmic scale. The lesson is not that asymptotic recovery fails, but that **the endpoint metric, scale, and admissible geometry must be derived from the source representation rather than imposed after the target is chosen**.

The live Xi interface is therefore sharply localized: **transport the source-relative Xi field into an admissible real-rooted periodic carrier at the relevant slice and normalization**, or prove an equivalent direct observation theorem. Any remaining complex obstruction must therefore both leave the fixed tube and contain genuine **nonseparable center-height correlation**, equivalently rank at least two in the occupation matrix after trivial rows and columns are removed. Absolute height, multiplicity, and pair count are no longer sufficient complexity variables.
