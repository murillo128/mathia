## Cross-line structure

**Finite acquisition and finite rank are becoming cheap; stable target transport is not.** `AF-285` closes the known-prefix raw sample-count order at `Theta(N)`, Xi already has a linear-size local packet bank, and `NB-062` closes finite-window algebraic rank loss with an exact one-innovation-per-cell flag. None of these produces the infinite or target-oriented estimate by itself. The remaining currencies are explicit source-compatible access, time/precision/support uncertainty, Gram or inverse conditioning, target projection, non-escape, and topology. This is the concrete form of the global distinction between exact recovery and stable transport.

**Qualitative sign information is much weaker than the signed functional consumed by the target.** `MC-235` shows that both Möbius signs can already occupy the full exponent of the hard progression support while their difference remains uncontrolled at the leading power. `WI-252` independently shows that the first odd Weil mode cannot be made one-signed by generic order theory once prime reflections are active. These are not transferable cancellation theorems—the source families are different—but they reinforce the same discipline: prove the signed amplitude or phase in the exact family selected by the endpoint, rather than upgrading sign existence into cancellation by language alone.

**Local abundance and rigidity keep failing to settle global assembly.** `FD-063` removes Farey host scarcity across a whole corridor, but the global denominator remains. `RE-064` nearly exhausts bounded Robin cancellation architectures, but deep fringe-free or unbounded-complexity escape remains. `PF-300` prices scalar loss exactly, but the full `P/H` Schur return can still redistribute that loss. The next useful theorems in these lines must change the global denominator, full return law or adaptive fan behavior, not add another local witness of a type already known to exist abundantly.

**Fixed-complexity representation tests continue to classicalize.** `PC-270` absorbs fixed exact resonances into a periodic-Haar bulk model, `VIS-179` extends the continuous quadratic null all the way to `y=o(H)`, and Analytic Frontier already identifies a scale-dependent complexity aperture for its matched packet. The common opportunity is therefore not “try a slightly richer finite statistic,” but make complexity grow with the resolution that hides the source, use microscopic/rare/discrete information, or invoke a source identity that the matched representation cannot carry.

**Canonical source identity and usable critical topology remain separate.** `WP-266` shows that even after a source-defined centering fixes the tested regulator ambiguity, the exact critical Mangoldt transport can be nonclosable. This is why proximity between Weil Positivity, Weil Inertia and Prime Lattice is not yet a valid merge or clue transfer: their operators, normalizations and target topologies have not been connected by an exact admissible map. The same caution applies to the broad global proposed clues: they remain useful questions, not evidence that the current line-specific gates have unified.
