## Cross-line structure

The first reusable lesson is **complete the exact reduction before crediting information**. Möbius is now the clearest example: translated-character phases and cross-lcm frequency alignment are genuine intermediate structures, but complete conductor blocks or full lcm recombination can erase exactly the feature one hoped to exploit. Weil Positivity reaches the same conclusion through quotient order, while Visual Exploration shows the metric analogue: if the metric is chosen without new source information, preconditioning can change conditioning or spectrum without changing arithmetic content. A future mechanism should identify the source coordinate that remains after every completion, symmetry, quotient, recombination and normalization actually used downstream.

The second lesson is **exact identifiability and stable transport are different resources**. Arithmetic Fidelity can recover an ideal Dirichlet source from sufficiently far-right exact data, yet at critical noise its local resolution is controlled by the farthest observed horizon, not by how much history is retained. Nyman now exhibits an even sharper form of the distinction: the Ford current is exactly derived from the canonical reciprocal-zeta source, but the derivative/s-multiplier bridge is unbounded in the Hilbert geometry where the target lives. Exact algebraic ancestry therefore counts only after the cost of transporting it through the destination norm has been paid.

The third lesson is **proper coercivity must remain after the natural null directions are removed**. Prime Lattice's raw `J`-positivity is automatic, so the meaningful target is model-subtracted matrix Loewner coercivity or an equivalent signed-sector inequality. Weil Inertia similarly shows that exact source-zero constraints alone do not create a gap when logarithmic spectral compactness still forces recurrence. Farey and Robin phrase the same demand in different coordinates: actual source slack or phase interference must beat a matched control by a quantitative margin, not merely introduce additional structure.

Representation richness is therefore no longer a useful progress metric by itself. A line gets credit only when the new representation preserves a source-native discriminator at the exact destination scale, with a stable signal floor and a coercive inequality unavailable to the matched control. This criterion explains why several mathematically richer constructions remain `narrow`, while Nyman, Möbius, Robin, Analytic Frontier and Weil Inertia retain sharply falsifiable source-specific gates.
