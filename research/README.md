## Cross-line structure

### 1. Representation can retain the local bit while aggregation destroys the useful margin

`MC-277` is the clearest new example. The low-degree Christoffel scalar preserves blind versus nonblind row states with an explicit margin; the hard resolution only appears when many rows are aggregated and row identity is forgotten. `AF-327` makes a related distinction: the full lacunary Gram has a quantitative finite-support margin, but the real issue is whether the centered singular incidence and downstream metric preserve enough of it. `NB-104` likewise shows that absolute ordinate information re-enters finite-window volume only relative to the chosen cell scale. The program-level lesson is more precise than “avoid compression”: identify **where** the source margin is still present and do not quotient it out before the target consumes it.

### 2. The endpoint norm or functional can remove a generic obstruction

Prime Flute now supplies the cleanest case. `PF-333` finds logarithmic operator-norm sensitivity for generic positive relative square roots, but `PF-334` proves dimension-free Hilbert--Schmidt transfer, which is much closer to the actual endpoint budget. Nyman previously showed the analogous separation between conditioning and determinant volume. A barrier in a stronger ambient norm should not be promoted to a route-level obstruction until it survives the exact destination currency.

### 3. Strong local coercivity increasingly leaves a compatibility or recurrence theorem

`FD-105` keeps logarithmic-depth transport at polynomial source scale but still needs branch-sensitive coupling between source descent and mass loss. `RE-107` closes the last intra-fan reset and leaves inter-block recurrence. `WI-282` shows that even all scalar screening constraints plus qualitative span can retain the extremal packing profile, so only a quantitative null/source compatibility condition can move the budget. These are different operators, but the same failure mode is visible: local admissibility and local coercivity do not globalize until switching, recurrence or exact source compatibility is charged.

### 4. Finite-horizon and source-specific frontiers are replacing qualitative averaging claims

`VIS-212` upgrades “long-start cancellation” to a uniform deterministic horizon `L~y^2` and identifies small semiprime cross-determinants as the only route to a shorter scale. `AF-327` similarly supplies an explicit height/time lower envelope instead of a fixed-support nondegeneracy statement. `NB-104` prices finite observation depth through an ordinate delay. These refinements are valuable because they expose the exact quantitative resource a target must pay rather than treating asymptotic observability as binary.
