# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia has thirteen research lines. Ten carry canonical evidence: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain pre-evidence scaffolds.

The evidence still does **not** support one common positive mechanism or one mathematically meaningful global bottleneck. The useful program-level discipline is to locate the first unsupported implication after the exact quotient, optimization, transport, resolution, or source normalization consumed by the proposed proof. Several new findings are valuable precisely because they falsify apparently strong intermediate conditions while leaving a smaller, more discriminating question.

**Analytic Frontier has lost two previously plausible packet-local closure routes.** `ANF-115` constructs explicit distinct conjugation-invariant sparse packets that are exponentially source-unsaturated and concentrate their Montgomery--Taylor source mass at a stable interior spectral saddle; they can remain fixed-notch negligible even after crossing the earlier max-height visibility threshold. `ANF-116` then shows that global near-extremality does not exclude such packets componentwise: three translated copies can realize pairwise `-1/2` source polarization, cancel to a source-negligible aggregate, and embed harmlessly in a genuine near-extremizing sequence. The live implication is therefore no longer to prove that exponential source unsaturation or the required anti-alignment cannot occur. It is an **aggregate saddle-phase** question: every non-excisable source-heavy aggregate in a fatal near-extremizer must either retain a phase defect that the unused remainder cannot cancel, move into another iterable population, or lose the fixed-notch advantage. A genuine obstruction would be a non-excisable aggregate whose near-extremal completion actually raises the fixed-notch exposure.

**Prime Flute makes the clearest positive endpoint contraction.** `PF-221` moves the physical reciprocal-prime coefficient through the cross-frequency Schur factor exactly, reducing the unresolved smooth coefficient-transfer term to `P[D,Omega]H` and its adjoint. `PF-222` proves that this commutator is compact by an exact reciprocal-prime prefix decomposition and one-seam decoupling: each fixed cut flux is smoothing/trace-class, and the full commutator is their norm-convergent weighted limit. Noncompactness is therefore no longer a possible obstruction. The first unsupported smooth theorem is quantitative: obtain the singular-value counting needed for weak `S_1` for the weighted cut-flux series in the actual low/high word. The unsmoothed `PF-204` route and the true-short-collar conservative splice remain independent gates.

**Weil Inertia receives a substantial prior-art reduction without solving its source theorem.** The audited exponent-pair theorems used in `WI-200` lower the every-interval bow cutoff from `27/82` to `1515/4816`. Above that exponent, a mirror-closed count-saturating Maynard--Pratt bow is excluded by a linear reservoir of odd-order critical-line zeros, and the explicit fixed-depth deep plateau is excluded even without count saturation. The hard count-saturating bow regime is therefore confined to `theta <= 1515/4816`. The distinguished multiplicative short-interval `L^2` covariance from `WI-195` remains unresolved inside that range, and the newer density theorem does not reach the `O(1/log T)` screening layer.

**Xi Flow now has both a sharp matched control and a genuine Xi-specific source restriction at the correct averaging scale.** `XF-113` proves that all coarse q-scale conditions accumulated through `XF-112` can coexist with a uniformly negative Toeplitz direction: a vanishing bad-set measure is still large compared with finite-section resolution when `K epsilon -> infinity`. ## Cross-line structure

The strongest commonality remains a **failure-mode discipline**, not a shared RH mechanism. `ANF-115`/`ANF-116` show that vertical capacity and componentwise anti-alignment do not control phase-organized aggregates; `AF-200` shows that fixed transport distance does not prevent Peano-carrier escape; and `XF-113` shows that vanishing bad-set measure does not control a finite Toeplitz section when the set is large at Fejer resolution. In each case, a coarse scalar resource survives while the object consumed by the destination escapes in an unresolved variable. This similarity does not itself provide a theorem transferable across the lines.

The Bost--Connes overlap is now more strongly negative but still not a merge candidate. Möbius has classified norm/profinite, weak-completion, time-flow and raw-semigroup regularity extremes, while Prime Lattice has classified temperature-sector geometry and increasingly strong scalar KMS programmability. Both say that “more operator structure” is not evidence unless a source-forced relation survives the relevant topology, but their destination theorems remain different: first-absolute Mertens excursion versus a nonprogrammable zeta/zero selector.

Prime Flute and Weil Positivity illustrate a complementary operator distinction. `PF-222` shows that proving compactness of the exact transported remainder can be useful because the desired conclusion is itself a quantitative ideal statement and the next missing exponent is explicit. `WP-206` shows that obtaining the desired positive roughness category is not enough when the arithmetic source does not select the geometry/order. Compactness, roughness, positivity, or noncommutativity count as progress only when they sit in the exact implication chain consumed by the final argument.

Visual Exploration and Farey Discrepancy both study ordered information lost by scalar/local quotients, but there is not yet an exact transfer theorem between their objects. The Farey Pluecker clue remains proposed and kill-first; `VIS-099` supplies an exact conditional null only after a declared finite-alphabet quotient. That resemblance is not sufficient for another Master clue or for activating Farey ahead of an active line.

3. **Weil-Inertia source theorem below the exponent-pair cutoff.** For `theta <= 1515/4816`, can one prove diagonal-scale cancellation directly for the distinguished multiplicative short-interval `L^2` norm, or obtain a stronger every-interval theorem that removes more of the adversarial bow before covariance is needed? 4. **Xi near-Nyquist Fejer/source interface.** `XF-114` excludes Fejer-visible wells from a fixed central mesh-angle arc, while `XF-113` shows that coarse global restrictions still permit them elsewhere. Can the actual discrete prime-power deposition be controlled at order-one mesh angle, or can destination-relevant transition witnesses be shown unable to exploit that sector? Without this, source progress and transition coercivity remain separate.
