The strongest common audit is now

`retained source/provenance -> target-compatible representation -> category-correct quantitative nonescape -> source-forced repair/coupling with an admissible modulus -> matched-control specificity -> signed/non-scalar global mechanism -> independent positivity/zero-selection theorem`.

Arithmetic Fidelity has refined the repair gate into a category-and-scale hierarchy. `AF-081` shows that intrinsic metric sections can exist beyond linear splitting under strong Banach geometry; `AF-082` and `AF-084` show that global Lipschitz repair or a genuine bounded-linear tangent at the homogeneity apex collapses back to bounded linear splitting; and `AF-083` places bounded-ball uniform repair strictly between continuity and global Lipschitz, with the exact renormalization law `omega_R(t)=R omega_1(t/R)` and unavoidable non-Lipschitz small-scale slope in nonsplitting examples. Escaping a linear obstruction by nonlinear repair is therefore meaningful only when the downstream arithmetic theorem tolerates that weaker category and the representative is source-forced rather than target-programmed.

The most important positive cross-line change is the new `analytic_frontier` / `weil_inertia` boundary. `WI-115`--`WI-118` show that support-one pair arguments based on universal termwise positivity are screened at the critical-lattice scale: positivity forces endpoint taper and the mirror-pair-versus-double defect is asymptotically negligible. `ANF-002` demonstrates a genuine escape from that exact abstraction. Lamzouri's global conjugation-aware Hilbert inequality extracts horizontal information directly from unconditional pair correlation and recovers the Alpöge--Furman simple-critical proportion without an a priori narrow box, even though individual pair contributions need not be nonnegative. This does not improve the numerical proportion, but it identifies global signed semidefinite/Hilbert geometry—not termwise positive kernels—as the current pair-correlation frontier. The proposed `analytic_frontier` semidefinite-lift clue already states the decisive next test.

Other recent results sharpen what nonlocality and source specificity can still mean. `PC-145`--`PC-147` show that the Prime-Circle top-band projector is uniformly finite-chord local, and `PC-148` proves that canonical cross-level transport of the dominant gap-two matching skeleton is exactly CRT-flat. `PL-129` shows that the scalar Grosswald--Schnitzer reflection-phase slope has exact arbitrary-tail aliases. `MC-033` removes hidden cancellation inside Huxley--Watt product fibers, while the new prime-log-slab clue isolates the remaining cross-fiber phase question without claiming evidence. `WP-129` shows that arbitrary scalar CND warps of the Gamma symbol are tautological after de-subordination, so any useful positivity warp must be independently forced. `XF-001`--`XF-002` replace false analytic root labelling at Xi-flow collisions by collision-safe symmetric coordinates: the roots branch with square-root speed, while their squared separation is analytic with universal slope eight.

No current `.review.md` sidecar is open in the portfolio. The graph and Atlas remain useful navigation, but their current projection predates several of the findings above and is treated as stale rather than used as portfolio evidence.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Global Hilbert positivity can bypass termwise positivity without bypassing information gates.** `WI-118` closes universal termwise-positive support-one kernels, while `ANF-002` shows that global conjugation-aware Hilbert geometry can still extract horizontal information from the same second-order source. This is a real category change, not a contradiction. The next question is whether multi-feature PSD structure adds genuinely new evaluated information or merely repackages the one-feature extremizer.

2. **Nonlinear repair is useful only with a downstream-compatible modulus and naturality.** `AF-081` and `AF-083` show nonlinear bounded-scale repair can exist beyond splitting; `AF-082` and `AF-084` show that too much global or first-order regularity collapses back to linear splitting. A cross-line rescue must state exactly which repair category the final theorem consumes.

3. **Finite-local and cross-level-flat does not mean information-free, but it removes fake nonlocality.** `PC-147` and `PC-148` leave only residual subspace organization or a different correspondence as possible Prime-Circle carriers. `MC-033` makes the analogous move for Mobius cancellation by pushing any gain outside individual product fibers.

4. **Faithful scalar diagnostics are not reconstructive selectors.** `PL-129` gives exact tail aliases for a useful scalar phase diagnostic; `WP-129` shows arbitrary scalar Gamma warps are noncanonical; and current visual baselines show how symmetry can force apparently distinctive scalar geometry. A selector needs a source-forced coupling that matched controls cannot reproduce.

5. **Critical-line selection remains the final bridge.** Prime Lattice has exact zero realization and phase rigidity without the required positive arithmetic polarization. Weil Positivity has positive structures without a canonical finite-energy arithmetic bridge. Xi Flow now has collision-safe local coordinates but no global selector from them. These lines are complementary rather than merge candidates.
