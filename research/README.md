# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The program's objective is to resolve the Riemann hypothesis. Mathia currently has thirteen research lines. Ten are evidence-bearing: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain distinct pre-evidence scaffolds.

The strongest common bottleneck is now a **source-conditioned critical-closure gate**. Several lines have moved past the question of whether a useful local representation exists. The remaining issue is whether the arithmetic source supplies the regularity, cancellation, conditioning, or family-uniform control needed for that representation to survive the exact limiting operation consumed by the target theorem. Apparent complexity before this gate is repeatedly classicalized: `PC-202` shows that every fixed finite scalar same-angle Haar fusion, even with several holomorphic and antiholomorphic outputs, reduces after Mellin transform to colored rational-cone multiple Dirichlet data; `PL-196` closes the arbitrary-Hilbert-fiber exact-unitary repair by forcing the prime-shift unitaries to be trivial and leaving only inert internal multiplicity. Higher valence, larger fibers, or more indices therefore do not constitute an escape unless a source-forced relation survives the final algebraic contraction.

The same distinction now appears quantitatively in growing families. `VIS-076` proves fixed-support collapse for the first natural signed Euler-product witness, but `VIS-077` shows that the absolute reciprocal-frequency certificate used there must itself grow: neighboring-prime modes force the critical-line `1/L` certificate coefficient to be at least order `X/log X`. This does **not** prove persistence of a visual signal; it proves that coefficientwise absoluteization has become the wrong proof topology. The surviving growing-support question is cancellation-sensitive: control the actual sinc-filtered signed spectrum or a comparable mean-square object rather than demanding a dimension-uniform `l^1` small-divisor norm.

Prime Flute supplies the positive counterpart and materially strengthens the portfolio. `PF-197` identifies an available matrix-valued critical Cwikel theorem for each localized vector-gradient resolvent form, and `PF-198` proves that a fixed-overlap Lambert localization preserves the prime-summable `L log L` budget instead of multiplying it by the number of windows. The local critical endpoint and coefficient-localization currency are therefore no longer the dominant obstruction. The remaining theorem is a source-compatible **family-uniform global reassembly bound**: polynomially controlled pseudodifferential constants, prime-summable off-diagonal/interface errors, and weak-`S_1` assembly without forgetting the shrinking cross-section.

Arithmetic Fidelity shows why such family-uniform control should be stated through source geometry rather than raw complexity. `AF-174` gives the sharp global fixed-degree `1/n` recovery exponent, while `AF-175` shows that a degree-independent interpolation/separation constant restores degree-uniform local Lipschitz recovery. Degree growth is not intrinsically destructive; uncontrolled collision geometry is. Across Mathia, a viable bridge should therefore name the source-derived conditioning parameter that replaces a generic worst-case complexity bound.

Möbius Cancellation removes a different generic burden. `MC-121`, using the audited Pintz zero-to-mean lower bound for the actual Möbius/zeta pair, shows that an RH-scale bound for the mean absolute Mertens function on **any unbounded checkpoint sequence** already implies RH. Dense final-scale coverage is not required. The source-side problem is consequently sharper: obtain the first-absolute-moment bound for the actual Möbius function on some unbounded family from signed, bilinear, multiplicative, correlation, or other excursion-sensitive information. Generic sub-`L^1` moment control remains insufficient even when imposed coherently on a fixed multiplicative comparator.

Xi Flow and Weil Inertia likewise reduce their remaining interfaces to source-specific closure theorems. `XF-087` proves that once the visible moments are realized, their periodic heat evolution is exactly the trapezoidal frequency discretization of the canonical Volterra/Burgers equation; static real-divisor feasibility and low-mode periodic dynamics are no longer separate existence problems. Xi now needs actual-source moment extraction with controlled guarded resource, source-faithful renormalization of the singular endpoint/background so that continuum and grid remain close, and separately a positive-`Lambda` transition-mass theorem in the same quotient. In Weil Inertia, `WI-191` identifies the stationary dual geometry exactly with the classical Atkinson phase and Jacobian. The phase is therefore not the new arithmetic mechanism: the unresolved object is the hyperbolically aggregated von-Mangoldt coefficient `B_n`, together with the activation and visibility conditions already required downstream.

The program-level discriminator is therefore: **after all exact quotients, classical packet reductions, matched controls, and target-specific converses are applied, identify the source parameter that controls the remaining critical interface and prove a family-uniform or cancellation-sensitive coercive estimate in exactly the topology consumed by the final theorem.** A category exit counts only when it comes with this replacement theorem. More coordinates, signed modes, larger support, matrix fibers, higher valence, or an exact local inverse are not progress by themselves.

## Cross-line structure

1. **Classicalization now reaches surprisingly rich finite enrichments.** Prime Circle closes arbitrary fixed finite scalar multi-output Haar fusion as rational-cone Dirichlet data; Prime Lattice closes arbitrary exact-unitary Hilbert fibers as gauge/inert multiplicity; Weil Positivity closes several finite positive completions. A useful category exit must therefore be defined by what survives the exact contraction, not by the apparent dimension of the intermediate representation.

2. **Growing complexity is viable only with source-conditioned stability.** Arithmetic Fidelity supplies the clean model: unrestricted degree growth has a sharp deterioration, but a uniform interpolation constant restores degree-independent local stability. Analytic Frontier asks the analogous question for Gram/Schur conditioning, and Prime Flute now asks it for module-uniform pseudodifferential and reassembly constants. The common target is a source-derived regularity modulus, not a generic worst-case bound.

3. **Signed structure must survive without premature absoluteization.** Visual Exploration now has actual signed small divisors, yet the `l^1` reciprocal-frequency proof becomes expensive as support grows; Möbius cancellation likewise disappears when cross-degree structure is converted too early into positive shell mass or sub-`L^1` statistics. In both cases the live theorem must retain cancellation until the target-relevant endpoint is reached.

4. **Target-specific converse theorems can delete generic transport burdens.** `MC-121` removes final checkpoint density entirely once the actual Möbius first-absolute mean reaches the RH exponent. Xi's exact trapezoid identity removes ambiguity in the finite low-mode dynamics once the target moments are known. Research effort should be concentrated on the remaining source-entry and critical-interface theorem, not on generic coverage that the downstream theorem no longer needs.

5. **Exact classical geometry is increasingly a control, not a mechanism.** `WI-191` identifies the bow's dual phase with Atkinson geometry; `PC-202` identifies finite fusion with rational-cone Dirichlet data; `WP-187` identifies canonical Hardy leakage as universal positive defects whose signed difference remains indefinite. The arithmetic content, if any, must live in the coefficient, incidence, orientation, or source-forced coupling left after those geometries are factored out.
