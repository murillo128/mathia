## Global synthesis

### Complexity must scale with the obstruction

`ANF-183`, `VIS-176`, `XF-186`, and the older Prime Circle results now form a useful boundary. Fixed or subcritical complexity can be perfectly informative locally and still miss the global defect. Analytic Frontier is the strongest quantitative instance: the matched endpoint packet supplies an explicit resolution scale rather than a vague warning about “more moments.” Visual and Xi show the complementary failure modes of inversion: a decoder may work on a bounded finite model yet lose a uniform inverse modulus under recurrence or growing packet complexity. Prime Circle similarly exhausts bounded-depth torus observables. The next useful theorem in these lines must therefore scale observation complexity with the source obstruction and track conditioning at the same time.

### Finite conditioning is not infinite acquisition

`AF-281` should be treated as a guardrail across the project. Excellent conditioning of every finite truncation does not imply a stable inverse for the infinite source. This aligns with `WP-263`: even after constructing a topologically respectable infinite carrier, the canonical source relation can still be absent. A proof route must expose a uniform modulus, compactness/tail mechanism, or source identity that survives the limit. “Every finite stage works” is no longer evidence for the infinite step unless the constants and topology are carried explicitly.

### Localization helps only if it preserves the selected sign

Farey, Möbius, and Weil Inertia now make three different versions of the same methodological point. `FD-059` says that global exceptional-measure control is too coarse for a very thin source-selected corridor. `MC-231` says that after exact localization the remaining obstacle is not population but parity sign. `WI-250` succeeds precisely because it conditions on the attained first zero mode and retains the signed prime contribution rather than replacing it by an absolute envelope. The useful next estimates are therefore conditional on the actual source geometry; stronger ambient norms that erase the selected sign are unlikely to move the endgame.

### Local source fidelity still needs global composition

`RE-060` proves more local arithmetic rigidity than before, while `PL-280` and Prime Flute show why local pieces alone are insufficient. In Robin, a genuine prime transition can be distinguished locally, but the result must be propagated through many threshold events. In Prime Lattice, a frozen finite source cannot become positive merely by increasing resolution. In Prime Flute, the natural local transfer pieces become asymptotically conservative, so any useful loss or compactness must arise in global return/reassembly. The shared lesson is not a theorem linking the three models; it is a falsifier for local-only explanations.
