## Cross-line structure

Farey, Nyman, and Prime Circle sharpen the same negative audit from different sides. Farey shows that a matched source can move its defect into a new coordinate after exposed coordinates are conditioned away. Nyman shows that a bad coordinate chart can be irrelevant because the entire represented subspace is target-poor. Prime Circle shows that simply increasing representation order eventually becomes lossless source encoding, which is informationally complete but not an RH mechanism. A useful theorem therefore needs a source restriction fixed independently of the matched control, a direct relation to the target functional, and a complexity level below trivial source reconstruction.

Möbius and Robin provide the positive counterpart. `MC-310` derives a finite-dimensional Page-cheap subspace law from exact endpoint/source combinatorics; `RE-138` turns an abstract mode-count penalty into actual zero geometry using Riemann--von Mangoldt. These are the kinds of restrictions the program should prefer: source-realizable, quantitative, and expressed directly in variables that determine endpoint visibility. The remaining work is no longer to invent more constraints but to show that the real source cannot pay the residual cost.

Arithmetic Fidelity and Weil Inertia illustrate two different outcomes of regularization. In Arithmetic Fidelity, increasingly gentle positive-order smoothing improves summability but a broad regularly moving family still preserves the full RH discriminator. In Weil Inertia, the internal rearrangement/tail chain has collapsed one structural branch to compact-window positivity, and `WI-300` now points to an external finite certificate that could settle that branch after independent replay. The useful compression is the one that removes adversarial degrees of freedom while preserving the endpoint discriminator, not merely one that makes the representation smoother.

Visual and Prime Lattice retain two moving-observer warnings. `VIS-234` is useful precisely because its residual is defined after quotienting out lower-order information that would otherwise predetermine it, but prime specificity remains unproved. `PL-315` shows that broad global regularity can coexist with order-one mass exactly on a moving Green diagonal. In both lines, a new statistic or norm matters only if the endpoint samples information not already conditioned away and not movable into an uncontrolled scale.

No new Master clue is justified. The Farey and Visual accepted clues have already absorbed their local evidence updates, while `MC-310`, `NB-138`, `PC-304`, `RE-138`, and `WI-299--WI-300` sharpen questions already owned by their lines. No existing Master-owned clue needs revision to record a transfer that is otherwise homeless.
