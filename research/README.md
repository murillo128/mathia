# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The common RH-facing gap remains an independently source-forced estimate in the precise destination norm that can exclude each off-critical zero. Source reconstruction, auxiliary positivity, available operator witnesses and artificial spectral separation do not provide that estimate. No Mathia finding proves RH.

[MC-522](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-522-centered-dyadic-variance-recovers-all-reciprocal-zeta-poles.md) completes the centered-dyadic Möbius transfer, including arbitrary multiple reciprocal-zeta poles. [XF-203](xi_flow/findings/XF-203-unknown-jacobi-calibration-slack-admits-finite-certified-stopping.md) supplies an unknown-slack finite stopping certificate even with fixed total noise. Neither supplies the independent source estimate or physical access needed for an RH implication. The balanced Farey variance target remains **classically resolved under RH** by [MC-520](mobius_cancellation/findings/MC-520-rh-mellin-variance-closes-conditional-farey-window-target.md); independent continuation needs weaker hypotheses and justified carrying windows. [RE-259](robin_extremal/findings/RE-259-zero-density-removes-critical-robin-moment-boundary.md) removes Robin's first annulus but leaves the CA-selected remote mixed tail open.

The current canonical findings outrank older Mind frontiers where they differ. No adversarial sidecar is currently open; this is not independent approval of the corpus. WI-002 has been withdrawn. [WI-456](weil_inertia/findings/WI-456-retracted-moment-transport-leaves-only-a-conditional-density-capstone.md) replaces its obsolete live-candidate assessment with a precise conditional moment-to-density theorem. The Yang–Yang arithmetic transport is retracted; neither model certificates nor the surviving conditional theorem restore it. Even a valid density-one theorem leaves individual exceptional zeros possible. The [Atlas v1 metrics](graph/atlas/metrics.md) reconcile WI-456 and retain selected PL-466/MC-521 telemetry. Their unchanged coverage model and older entrypoint narrative do not cover the whole current source frontier; portfolio judgments below rest on canonical evidence.

## Cross-line structure

**A proof-operation barrier need not survive a different classical input.** MC-520 bounds `J_mu(X,N)` by `X^(1+epsilon)N` under RH. FD-311's balanced premise forces `J_mu>=XN^(1/2+3c-o(1))`, where `c=1-log_2(3/2)`, on shells with `X<=N^(2+o(1))`. Any fixed `2epsilon<3c-1/2` gives a strict contradiction on the same complete moment. FD-312's separate-endpoint barrier survives because it discards the difference multiplier that MC-520 retains. The [resolved Möbius handoff](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-farey-selected-sliding-mertens-variance.md) and Farey MI-066 record this classical conditional outcome, not a new RH mechanism. Removing RH also removes its shell-localization guarantee unless separately recovered. RE-259 is another successful change of input, but uses a different prime statistic and supplies no Möbius transfer.

**Smooth recovery changes the analytic consequence, not the arithmetic premise.** MC-521 assumes the complete uncentered moment `J(X,X^theta)<<_epsilon X^(1+epsilon)H^(2-eta)` at every large real scale, for fixed `0<theta<1`, `0<eta<=1`. Finite correction of smooth weights excludes zeros in `Re s>alpha=1-theta eta/2`. Its classical logarithm/reciprocal-zeta estimate followed by Perron now also gives `M(x)<<_epsilon x^(alpha+epsilon)`; the generic bounded-coefficient smooth argument alone does not give that sharp cutoff.

PL-466 resolves the [dyadic seam clue](prime_lattice/clues/CLUE-dyadic-variance-smooth-endpoint-recovery.md) for empirical identity-residue prime variance `V_j<<X_j H_j(log X_j)^A`, with `X_j=2^j`, `H_j=floor X_j^theta` and fixed finite `A>=0`. Seam-vanishing finite Fourier weights preserve the periodic unknown-mean factor. Sublinear arithmetic zero density excludes its pole progressions and then the polynomial residue sequences hidden by the filter, reaching `Re s>1-theta/2`. The positive dyadic density control still passes the variance bound and fails the pole-sparsity step. This is an arithmetic recovery theorem, not generic interpolation from sparse scales. The [Möbius handoff](mobius_cancellation/clues/CLUE-dyadic-centered-mobius-pole-recovery.md) is resolved by MC-522: under `V_j<<_epsilon X_j^(1+epsilon)H_j`, the finite recurrence acts on whole Laurent principal parts, so it detects multiple poles even when their residues vanish. It gives the same zero-free boundary and, using the classical reciprocal-zeta growth step, `M(x)<<_epsilon x^(1-theta/2+epsilon)`. Every sufficiently large dyadic block is required. Arbitrary selected subsequences and independent bounds for either source variance remain open. A centered bound on a Farey carrying shell would also need its mean contribution restored before the uncentered FD-311 destination can use it.

**A relative frame can transmit absolute information without supplying it.** Under PL-465's all-large-real-scale hypothesis `S_X>=c_theta Diag(S_X)`, a unit-modulus character probe has energy at least `c_theta tr(S_X)`. An independent upper bound for any one channel therefore controls identity-residue variance and, through its noncancelling logarithmic-derivative poles, all four zero sets. Writing `Theta_chi` for the supremum of zero real parts gives `Theta_max-Theta_min<=1-theta`. A frame family at fixed exponents approaching one equalizes these suprema; they may still exceed `1/2`. Separately, zeta RH plus the frame at a fixed exponent excludes all mod-5 zeros in `Re s>1-theta/2`. No absolute variance ceiling follows from the frame alone, no GRH-to-frame converse is proved, and the near-one family is not supplied by the existing short-window regime. PL-467 makes that distinction concrete: one bounded positive integer-supported control has the `1/4` relative frame at all sufficiently large real scales for each fixed `1-beta<theta<1`, while every character has a finite symmetric pole set with common right frontier `beta>1/2`. Its centered variance is `asymp H^2 X^(2beta-1)`, exceeding the absolute ceiling when `beta>1-theta/2`. It lacks prime support, an Euler product and a completed functional equation. Pole sparsity repairs lost information after an absolute estimate; it does not turn a relative floor into that estimate.

**Calibration knowledge, phase and noise norm determine the sampling problem.** [XF-202](xi_flow/findings/XF-202-rotating-jacobi-certificates-locate-the-calibration-crossover.md) extends the [resolved known-slack handoff](xi_flow/clues/CLUE-vanishing-calibration-slack-jacobi-row-cost.md): a rotating-tangent certificate overcomes the earlier one-chord restriction, while oscillatory clipping gives the matching adaptive adversary. With fixed positive total noise, the sharp small-slack cost is `1+sigma^2(1+Q epsilon)^2/(Q epsilon^4)`. Its mesh overhead is not known optimal in every simultaneous zero-noise limit.

[AF-575](arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-575-unknown-calibration-slack-requires-unbounded-sampling-depth.md) changes another quantifier: a strict but unknown derivative-budget slack permits input-dependent finite noiseless termination while defeating every uniform finite query cap. The full-curve uniform positive supremum-noise radius is zero. The [unknown-slack Jacobi handoff](xi_flow/clues/CLUE-unknown-slack-jacobi-stopping-certificate.md) is resolved by XF-203. Endpoint chord contrasts and increasingly populated distinct-row clusters certify finite stopping for each strict-slack input under one known total squared-noise budget. At fixed source and positive noise, the vanishing-slack quartic cost order survives; arbitrary coordinate precision and certified constants remain necessary. AF's uniform supremum-noise obstruction does not transfer to that norm.

[AF-576](arithmetic_fidelity/findings/AF-576-randomized-calibration-recovery-has-a-sharp-error-stopping-tail.md) separately fixes the randomized quantifier: its two nuisance inputs are fixed independently of the realized seed. Every finite binary query cap has worst-case error `1/2`; error at most `alpha<1/2` forces worst-case stopping tail at least `1-2alpha` for each cap, sharply. XF-203's finite-prefix interpolant proves only its deterministic lower bound. The [randomized Jacobi clue](xi_flow/clues/CLUE-randomized-jacobi-unknown-slack-stopping-tail.md) tests the stronger statement while retaining complex phase and access to the endpoint itself. Neither acquisition line supplies a physical Xi interface or stable ordinary-prime decoder.

**A spectral representation can be informative without creating a new arithmetic input.** [FD-337](farey_discrepancy/findings/FD-337-sublinear-rank-sine-bands-transfer-to-the-mertens-spectrum.md) identifies genuine Farey rank-band energy at `q=o(Q)` with the normalized truncated spectrum of `B_Q(j)=sum_(d|j,d<=Q) d M(floor(Q/d))`, with additive error `O(q/Q+(q/Q)^2+1/Q)`. For the calibrated FD-335/336 nulls and diverging cutoffs, the null-normalized statistic has the same limiting arithmetic ratio; no rate for the null normalization or relative error at a vanishing share is supplied. The [owning Farey clue](farey_discrepancy/clues/CLUE-farey-gap-order-bridge-suppression.md) already carries this narrowed outcome. A bound on the ratio could still matter, but changing from fraction position to rank does not provide it.

The [shifted-notch visualization](visual_exploration/visualizations/notch-shift-normalization.md) gives a separate measurement control: averaging normalized origin ratios tends to a universal value and loses the denominator–phase covariance that contains the signal. Averaging the energies before division preserves it for each fixed configuration. This classical estimator distinction supplies neither a uniform sampling rate nor an improved ANF-196 stability envelope.

**Source recovery, target occupation and quantitative stability are different claims.** NB-350's unrestricted late dilations recover the early-shell kernel, but [NB-351](nyman_beurling/findings/NB-351-geometric-source-seeds-retain-a-positive-target-distance-under-all-dilations.md) rules out geometric source compression: for each fixed integer `q>=2`, seeds `g_(q^k)` collapse to `g_q`, and even all real dilations leave squared target distance at least `1-q(log q)^2/(q-1)^2>0`. This is the exact cost of a common-Mellin-zero relaxation, not an exact distance for the original source space. [NB-352](nyman_beurling/findings/NB-352-finite-nyman-patches-cannot-remove-the-geometric-target-obstruction.md) also excludes every fixed finite patch of Nyman columns: simultaneous returns on the geometric Mellin lattice and slow zeta growth give a finite normed dual certificate. Its constant depends on the patch; a growing patch or all dilations of a multiplicatively independent seed is not covered. Finite-rate moving-packet occupation remains separate. WI-454's coherent isometry likewise recovers precisely the old Hardy energy; it supplies no independent upper bound.

[RE-260](robin_extremal/findings/RE-260-infinite-unit-prime-shifts-preserve-exact-mertens-matching-but-remain-robin-stable.md) separates source injectivity from destination stability: infinite unit shifts beyond a frozen prime prefix can preserve the Mertens constant exactly, yet every such admitted source has only `O(p^(-1/2))` normalized Robin discrepancy uniformly over all future cutoffs. This does not control the actual CA-selected mixed tail or restore uniqueness of infinite sources. [RE-261](robin_extremal/findings/RE-261-exact-mertens-matching-has-a-sharp-counting-discrepancy-robin-threshold.md) identifies the larger real-label class's exact scale: counting discrepancy `|Pi_Q-pi|<=K`, exact Mertens matching and `K=o(p/log p)` give worst-case normalized transient size `(1/2+o(1))K log(p)/sqrt(p)`. Finite cardinality-preserving transports attain it, although their full tail difference is zero. Thus exact normalization and a frozen prefix `p+o(p)` do not prevent a transient excursion at `K asymp sqrt(p)/log p`. This is a synthetic minimax statement, not a lower bound on the actual CA-selected tail.

**Domain repair must retain the correct representation and relaxation.** [PC-448](prime_circle/findings/PC-448-haar-admixture-does-not-close-rigid-cotangent-towers.md) shows that Haar admixture does not repair the unchanged tower when a nonzero character-rigid singular component survives; even arbitrarily small such contamination defeats closability. [WP-442](weil_positivity/findings/WP-442-critical-valuation-product-supports-a-closed-log-gcd-energy.md), by contrast, genuinely constructs a critical closed form with divisibility pairing `log(gcd(m,n)) gcd(m,n)/sqrt(mn)`. It uses a different occupation sector, retains its completion terms, and has no nonzero bounded intertwiner of all divisibility projections from the integer occupation representation. Its generic product controls also remain positive. [WP-443](weil_positivity/findings/WP-443-critical-valuation-energy-has-an-explicit-hierarchical-spectrum.md) classifies the latter operator completely: its eigenvalues are `eta_n=log n+sum_(p|n) log(p)/(p-1)`, with simple spectrum and compact resolvent; the heat trace is finite exactly for `t>1`. Its unitary diagonalization does not intertwine the source divisibility projections with the integer ones. The [valuation-tail control](visual_exploration/visualizations/valuation-tail-observable-angle.md) makes this concrete at one coordinate: for fixed geometric ratio `r`, the projection distance stays `sqrt(r)` at every depth even as relative-energy concentration sharpens. This supports the existing representation boundary; neither result provides a global Weil sign.

[PF-349](prime_flute/findings/PF-349-local-full-parity-transition-shorting-retains-the-narrow-band-angle.md) now includes both face parities and all local sine/cosine transition modes. Relative form comparison with PF-348 preserves normalized correlation at least `1/4` for sufficiently narrow fixed bands. The [transition clue](prime_flute/clues/CLUE-positive-width-transition-schur-complement.md) retains neighboring-module transmission as the unresolved freedom: other cuffs are still held at zero, and normalized correlation is not monotone under further Schur relaxation. No global noncompactness follows from the local theorem.

**Fixed-test endpoint recovery does not cross a fixed support gap.** [WI-455](weil_inertia/findings/WI-455-wang-endpoint-flatness-controls-subpower-window-shortening.md) retains the complete prime norm when its scale exceeds the height-window length. For a fixed smooth test supported in `[-sigma,sigma]`, its derived error is `O_g(H+(log T)^3 J_g(log T))`, where `J_g(L)=int_0^sigma |g(alpha)|exp(L alpha)d alpha`. This admits every fixed logarithmic shortening `H>=T^sigma/(log T)^B`. Faster shortening depends on the endpoint profile; a fixed positive support-exponent gap and individual zero exclusion remain unresolved. As with MC-521/PL-466, a derivative order fixed after the parameters does not establish a uniform moving-order limit.

**Normalized density control cannot exclude each exceptional block.** WI-456 needs every fixed arithmetic matrix moment to converge to a Hamburger-determinate law with no nonpositive mass, plus the actual positive-index counting inequality and its tail error. Those premises would yield density one without uniformity in moment order. The control `diag(-1,1,...,1)` still retains one negative direction while meeting them. The retracted arithmetic transport and the individual-block detector are therefore separate missing inputs; the [higher-correlation clue](weil_inertia/clues/CLUE-higher-zero-correlations-horizontal-rigidity.md) pursues a signed-reservoir or horizontally coupled statistic, but its density-scale target does not itself provide an individual-block detector.

## Highest-leverage unresolved questions

1. **Independent arithmetic estimates at the destination scale:** ANF's oscillatory high-pass, MC's signed physical conductor correlation and PL's direct relative lower frame with its channel-transfer consequences. Farey needs weaker hypotheses and justified carrying windows, or a bound for FD-337's arithmetic spectral ratio. Both centered-dyadic recovery tests are resolved conditionally; a selected-shell estimate must state its center, mean and scale coverage rather than silently importing either complete-scale premise.
2. **Remote Robin control:** estimate RE-259's CA-selected integral beyond the settled first annulus. RE-261 locates the synthetic counting threshold; actual-prime fidelity or a signed restriction excluding its extremizers is still missing.
3. **Physical operator transport:** analyze PF's intermodule relaxation, compare WP-443's actual source observables and completion terms with the global Weil class, and prove moving-packet NB target occupation or independent individual-defect control in WI. Conditional moment-density control cannot replace the last requirement.
4. **Observation robustness:** require physical Xi calibration/access, retain XF-203's certified unknown-slack stopping assumptions, test the [randomized stopping-tail transfer](xi_flow/clues/CLUE-randomized-jacobi-unknown-slack-stopping-tail.md), and keep the unrestricted joint zero-noise design open. Notch sampling needs energy-level normalization and finite precision control.
