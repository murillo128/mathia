# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

The program's objective is to resolve the Riemann hypothesis. Mathia currently has thirteen research lines. Ten are evidence-bearing: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain distinct pre-evidence scaffolds.

The current evidence still does **not** establish one common positive mechanism or one mathematically meaningful global bottleneck shared by the active lines. Program-level progress is best audited by tracing each line to its **first unsupported implication**. A result counts as proof-interface progress when it removes or strictly narrows that implication. Exact identities, matched controls, prior-art redirects, classicalizations, and representation theorems remain valuable research output, but they are classification progress unless they change the theorem that must be proved next.

The remaining implication is correspondingly narrower: obtain operator-level control for the shrinking distinguished-cuff and other exceptional interface families, and close the independent true-short-collar conservative endpoint splice, with constants that remain summable after the actual localization geometry is restored.

The live arithmetic theorem is therefore genuinely two-dimensional: retain relative complex phase across the growing shift family and obtain cancellation jointly in `m` and `h`, rather than perfecting one-dimensional phase geometry or fixed-shift bounds that are later absoluteized.

Visual Exploration now closes the main question that was open in the previous snapshot. `VIS-080` rewrites the exact signed growing-support energy as a positive sum over coprime smooth ratios and proves a support-crowding lower bound. `VIS-081` identifies the normalized support law with a classical symmetric Dickman difference. `VIS-082` supplies the matching weighted-Hilbert upper bound and proves the exact binary threshold

`T_(X(L),L) -> 0` if and only if `log X(L)/L -> 0`.

This is a useful complete classification of that witness, but it is **not positive RH evidence**: persistence at exponential support growth is already forced by ordinary near-diagonal reduced ratios, while subexponential support gives vanishing energy. The line should not keep interpreting this Euler-product witness's growing-support beats as an arithmetic-specific signal; further work should pivot to a representation or residual with an explicit discriminator beyond this classical support law. `VIS-082` was added after the latest adversarial reviewed-through prefix, so this newest closure is used as canonical current evidence but not described as independently adversarially validated.

The classicalization frontier also advances. `PC-205` closes fixed finite matrix-valued rotation-invariant homogeneous anchor filters: after angular contraction and Mellin reduction they remain finite conical packet data with a matrix wrapper. `PL-199` identifies canonical addition plus multiplication with the classical affine semigroup `N ⋊ N^x`; `PL-200` shows that the canonical Liouville grading leaves the ordinary trace-class boundary unchanged and reaches critical-strip zero information only after scalar meromorphic continuation; `PL-201` shows that exactly commuting scalar nonunitary prime multishifts are coboundaries and that exact resolvent covariance can program an arbitrary bounded vertexwise spectral field. `WP-192` closes the next natural generalized-scattering positivity repair: Koplienko's Hilbert-Schmidt spectral-shift density is genuinely positive, but its trace remainder is positive on the convexity cone and becomes strictly negative on sufficiently wide nontrivial Weil autocorrelations, so it is not the required Weil positivity cone.

The program therefore continues to accumulate real mathematics, but the solution-directed frontier remains uneven. ## Cross-line structure

1. **The shared progress criterion remains proof-interface movement, not common vocabulary.** Prime Flute and Xi Flow both remove concrete intermediate obligations, but by unrelated mathematics: weak-ideal localization in one case and source-normalized Fourier sampling in the other. Their simultaneous progress is not evidence of a common RH mechanism.

2. **Matched controls increasingly classify what is generic before arithmetic enters.** `VIS-080`--`VIS-082` explain the complete growing-support signed Euler witness through ordinary smooth-ratio crowding and classical mean-square control; `PL-199`--`PL-201` show that several natural relational/operator enrichments are classical or programmable; `PC-205` shows that finite matrix wrappers do not escape conical packet algebra; and `WP-192` shows that genuine perturbative positivity can still live on the wrong cone. These closures should change future candidate selection, but they do not themselves supply the missing arithmetic estimate.

3. **The newest conditioning results strengthen, rather than weaken, the need for source-faithful controls.** `ANF-096` shows that a spectacular signed linearized condition number can disappear when the actual positive arithmetic synthesis is restored. `AF-182`--`AF-183` show the complementary danger and calibration: a genuinely positive source class can hide arbitrary-order flatness if its cluster complexity is unconstrained, and the explicit control has a sharp complexity-dependent bandwidth threshold before its source separation becomes visible. The transferable lesson is methodological and exact: condition numbers and inverse moduli must be measured on the actual admissible source class and in the destination quotient, not on a convenient enlargement.

4. **Möbius has now matched combinations of summaries, not only each summary separately.** `MC-129` removes the simple repair of stacking local factor counts with modular transition counts inside coarse time bins. What remains is explicitly outside that product of quotients: cross-tabulated local/state information, finer order, non-additive state, or exact arithmetic constraints such as divisor/prime placement. This is a sharper research question, but the generic matched words still do not show that actual Möbius lacks such information.

5. **Weil Inertia's live target has become genuinely joint.** `WI-193` gives a quantitative reason that fixed-shift technology can be excellent and still be structurally insufficient after absolute assembly. Any useful transfer from bilinear/dispersion/spectral technology must therefore preserve cancellation across the shift family itself. No current finding establishes that estimate, so this is a localized missing theorem rather than a positive result.

The two global proposed clues remain research questions rather than evidence. No new cross-line transfer in this delta is both source-grounded and destination-actionable enough to justify another Master clue: the strongest new facts already sharpen the owning lines' accepted clues or live tests.
