# Mathia Research State

## Global picture

Mathia currently has thirteen research lines. Ten carry canonical evidence: `analytic_frontier`, `arithmetic_fidelity`, `mobius_cancellation`, `prime_circle`, `prime_flute`, `prime_lattice`, `visual_exploration`, `weil_inertia`, `weil_positivity`, and `xi_flow`. `farey_discrepancy`, `nyman_beurling`, and `robin_extremal` remain pre-evidence scaffolds.

The current evidence still does **not** support one common positive mechanism or one mathematically meaningful global bottleneck shared by the program. Progress is therefore audited line by line at the first genuinely unsupported implication. A new representation, exact identity, classicalization, matched control, or obstruction is valuable research output, but it counts as proof-interface progress only when it removes or strictly contracts the theorem that must be proved next.

The clearest movement in the present snapshot is in **Xi Flow**. `XF-095` removes the remaining deterministic dynamic endpoint forcing with a source-fixed logarithmic-square correction; `XF-096` then supplies the corrected endpoint `C^1` control needed to make the canonical mesh-sampling error negligible in the guarded resource; and `XF-097` lifts the whole guarded prefix to an exact real carrier with a quantitative Volterra/trapezoid shadow. Taken together, these results materially contract the source-side chain that previously stopped at mesh-scale anti-concentration. The live burden is no longer another reference subtraction or generic sampling theorem: the decisive unsupported implication is now the destination statement that a hypothetical `Lambda>0` transition produces an order-one event or mass in the **same corrected guarded slow band**, with the remaining carrier/continuum errors below that margin. This is real proof-interface progress, but it is not evidence that the positive-transition theorem itself is true.

**Prime Flute** also continues to narrow its first unsupported operator statement. `PF-203` removes the need to smooth decomposition cuffs at form level: the pantwise exact-area maps already glue to a globally bi-Lipschitz area-preserving identification with no extra cuff surface term. `PF-204` then removes another artificial burden by rewriting the relative resolvent through the two native smooth hyperbolic resolvents, isolating the nonsmooth geometry in a bounded cotangent transport. The remaining theorem is correspondingly specific: prove the critical weak-`S_1` estimate for the mixed product with that piecewise transport, with uniform or prime-summable constants, and independently close the genuine short-collar conservative splice. Neither boundedness of the transport nor the exact factorization proves the endpoint ideal estimate.

**Weil Inertia** makes a useful interface localization rather than a positive estimate. `WI-195` rewrites the whole localized bow covariance, before absolute assembly, as an exact multiplicative short-interval `L^2` norm. In the difficult `epsilon<1/4` regime the currently matched 2026 almost-all major-arc theorem has the right interval and Archimedean-twist ranges but the wrong norm strength: even granting the same pointwise amplitude on every interval misses the required diagonal-scale variance by a polynomial factor. The live analytic target is now explicit: obtain a smooth-weighted variance of scale `o(X K log X)` for the `Lambda-Lambda^sharp` high-twist short sums and control the structured `Lambda^sharp` contribution in the same norm. This target has been routed to `analytic_frontier` as `CLUE-bow-multiplicative-gallagher-variance.md` rather than left as an implicit cross-line observation.

**Analytic Frontier** also contracts one branch of its near-extremizer problem. `ANF-098` shows that convex relaxation destroys the nonseparability the argument needs, so the comparator-selection problem is genuinely nonconvex. `ANF-099` turns the fixed-notch improvement question into a near-extremizer exposure bound, and `ANF-100` proves that any growing physical Montgomery--Taylor near-extremizer must have horizontal diameter at least linear in its cardinality. This rules out bounded or sublinear horizontal concentration, including large vertical stacks, as the hidden obstruction. The first unsupported implication is still substantial but more concrete: control notch exposure for linearly spreading positive multisets, or construct a physical linearly spreading near-extremizer that reaches the fatal exposure envelope.

Most other changes are **classification or negative progress**, not evidence of approach to RH. `AF-184`--`AF-185` generalize adaptive positive-cluster invisibility into a Gevrey derivative-budget obstruction, sharpening when family-wide power-law recovery cannot hold without a source-complexity restriction. `MC-130`--`MC-131` show that finite-horizon product-state folding and a growing periodic clock still do not recover excursion order, so another finite-state/time-coordinate enrichment is not the missing Möbius mechanism. `PC-206`--`PC-207` close both the total mismatch regular representation and the canonical fixed-finite individual-Fourier-leg Hardy lift as scalar/rational separable operator data. `PL-203`--`PL-205` show that dense-range noninvertibility remains programmable while exact self-adjoint resolvent covariance itself forbids a cokernel/closed-range unilateral defect; the surviving singular route must therefore make a kernel, domain, approximation, or non-fiberwise coupling source-forced and load-bearing. `VIS-083`--`VIS-084` show that fixed finite prime phases at Gram points are globally governed by classical equidistribution and locally freeze on sublogarithmic Gram blocks, closing those visual patterns as independent arithmetic evidence.

The current Riemann Atlas remains useful only as historical/structural navigation in this pass: its declared source revision `4bc527123c549c73b4ce4f04512cb291b342311d` is far behind the present evidence, so its percentages and fertility vector are treated as stale derived telemetry and do not influence the portfolio decision.

## Cross-line structure

The most important cross-line conclusion remains negative: **there is no established common RH mechanism here**. Xi's progress comes from source-fixed endpoint normalization and discretization, Prime Flute's from critical operator factorization/localization, Weil Inertia's from an exact arithmetic `L^2` representation, and Analytic Frontier's from positive-multiset geometry. Their simultaneous narrowing is not evidence that one abstract “source-conditioned” or “local-to-global” gate controls all four.

There is, however, one concrete productive transfer. `WI-195` identifies an exact high-twist short-interval variance target that lies squarely inside `analytic_frontier`'s analytic-number-theory mandate. Because line-specific watches are cross-line blind, the target has been handed off explicitly through `research/analytic_frontier/clues/CLUE-bow-multiplicative-gallagher-variance.md`. The clue asks for either the required diagonal-scale variance with the actual source weights or a rigorous matched obstruction showing why the tested analytic machinery cannot reach it.

A second recurring pattern is methodological rather than a shared theorem. Arithmetic Fidelity, Möbius, Prime Circle, Prime Lattice, Visual Exploration, and Weil Positivity repeatedly show that richer state spaces, extra coordinates, positive representations, or visually stronger structure can remain generic, programmable, separable, or classical after the final observation. This supports using matched controls before interpreting representation richness as arithmetic information, but it does **not** justify merging those lines: their source objects and decisive escape tests remain mathematically different.
