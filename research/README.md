## Cross-line structure

The first common constraint is now **stable resolving capacity, not exact information content**. `FD-143` proves a growing but sub-root-rank observation family can remain uniformly blind; `FD-142` shows a single scalar with `Theta(log T)` bits can already identify the squarefree source; and `PC-306` shows exact injectivity can occur even earlier while becoming arbitrarily ill-conditioned in the natural Fourier topology. `AF-359--AF-360` supplies the endpoint-side lower bill through exponential cross-scale separation. Any proposed intermediate observer therefore needs two quantitative certificates in the same declared topology: a target-bearing separation margin and a source-reconstruction upper bound. Coordinate number, formal dimension, exact injectivity and nominal precision are only proxies.

The second common constraint is **endpoint topology rather than generic regularity**. Prime Lattice closes the generic Sobolev question at the exact half-derivative threshold; Arithmetic Fidelity closes bounded-ratio post-Riesz shape as a cheaper escape; Prime Circle separates exact cyclotomic recovery from stable analytic separation. The next useful norm or observation must be chosen because the endpoint consumes it with controlled constants, not because it is formally richer.

A third constraint is **target-relative local source law rather than upper population control**. `NB-142` converts the dangerous Nyman cell into a positive short-increment bill for `R(T)`/`S(T)`. `RE-143` removes Robin's rightmost transfer and localizes the residual cancellation to an `O(log U/U)` slab immediately left of the strong atom. These are stronger statements than the previous generic “rare exceptional packet” diagnosis: both lines now know a specific asymmetric local source relation whose failure permits the matched control. Better global density exponents are useful only if they imply those local laws.

The positive source-payment lines continue to separate generic structure from arithmetic realizability. Weil Inertia forces a fixed odd-prime repayment after dyadic screening. Möbius forces a redundancy/scale payment but `MC-313` shows that generic binary coding can absorb its current hereditary profile. The decisive information must therefore lie in source labels, incidence, nonshareability or another arithmetic realization constraint, not in a stronger ambient inequality of the same generic type.

Several representation families have reached clean exhaustion points without implying impossibility of the parent program. `WP-317` removes bounded finite scalar multipoles but leaves unbounded collective/operator geometry; `VIS-236` removes arbitrary fixed-type label randomization but leaves monotone gap/occupancy models; `MC-313` removes generic generalized-weight refinements but leaves arithmetic incidence; `PC-306` removes exact finite-level source recovery as an information threshold but leaves stable cross-level geometry; and `AF-360` removes intra-band projective phase as a Riesz escape but leaves genuinely cross-scale/source-native observables. These are carrier changes, not global impossibility results.

No new Master clue is justified. The actionable questions are already owned by their destination lines or absorbed into current local Mind state, and the new cross-line synthesis does not expose a distinct transfer claim requiring bounded foreign-source access. No merge, split, or new-line proposal is warranted.
