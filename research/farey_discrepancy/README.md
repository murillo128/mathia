# Farey Discrepancy

## Research mandate

### Primary object

The line studies Franel–Landau-type reformulations of the Riemann hypothesis through the discrepancy between ordered Farey fractions and a uniform grid, together with equivalent reduced-rational discrepancy aggregates and exact transforms linking them to Möbius-weighted arithmetic.

The intrinsic data are the ordered reduced fractions of bounded denominator, their rank positions, local gaps, and global discrepancy norms after the normalization required by the classical RH-equivalent criteria.

### Objective

Determine whether the Farey reformulation contains geometric or multiscale information that is not lost when one collapses it to the classical scalar discrepancy criterion, and whether that additional structure yields a new exact bound, cancellation mechanism, or obstruction relevant to RH.

The route to RH requires a new estimate or usable structure in the discrepancy problem. Additional coordinates are useful only through what they reveal or enable; an exact gain in the original scalar criterion can also be substantive.

### Priority questions

- Is there a multiscale or local-to-global discrepancy decomposition whose cross-scale coupling retains information erased by the standard aggregate norm?
- Can neighboring Farey intervals, mediants, or denominator strata yield a signed invariant that is not equivalent to a familiar Möbius summatory estimate?
- Are there two-dimensional or graph representations of Farey adjacency whose non-scalar spectrum controls the RH-equivalent discrepancy more sharply than the one-dimensional ordering alone?
- Can exact reciprocity or Dedekind/Vasyunin-type correlations distinguish arithmetic Farey configurations from matched reduced-rational controls while contributing at the RH-critical scale?
- Which visually compelling Farey patterns disappear after normalization or reduce exactly to known discrepancy identities?

### Scope and exclusions

This line owns the Farey/Franel–Landau discrepancy representation and any genuinely additional structure intrinsic to it. It does not own generic Möbius cancellation once the Farey geometry has been eliminated, nor arbitrary Ford-circle or tessellation pictures without an exact quantitative bridge to the discrepancy criterion.

Do not count the classical RH equivalence, a new plot of Farey fractions, or a coordinate transformation of the same scalar discrepancy as progress.

### Line-specific falsification controls

For every proposed geometric invariant, derive its relation to the classical Farey discrepancy and Möbius summatory functions explicitly. Telescoping or recovery from the existing scalar criterion rules out a claimed additional information channel; it does not rule out a genuinely stronger estimate of that same quantity. A matched rational-point control must preserve the assumed Farey relations and change the transported target before equality can refute the proposed implication.

Separate exact finite Farey identities from asymptotic statements at the RH-critical scale. If a claimed gain comes from a norm change, prove that the new norm is not simply equivalent to the old one with constants too weak to affect the critical exponent.

### Prior-art domains

- Franel–Landau Farey discrepancy criteria;
- Farey sequences, Ford circles, and Stern–Brocot/Farey adjacency;
- Möbius inversion and summatory Möbius relations to Farey counts;
- discrepancy theory and equidistribution;
- Dedekind and Vasyunin sums, reciprocity, and Farey correlations;
- transfer/operator formulations only when intrinsically generated by the Farey structure.

### Relationship to other lines

`mobius_cancellation` is the main collapse boundary: any Farey mechanism that reduces entirely to a Möbius summatory estimate should be handed there rather than duplicated. `visual_exploration` may search for multiscale Farey patterns, but a persistent finding requires an exact invariant or falsifiable quantitative statement owned by this line.

`prime_circle` may share roots-of-unity or rational-angle representations, but geometric similarity alone is not a bridge.
