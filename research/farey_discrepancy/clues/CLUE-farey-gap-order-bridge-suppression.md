# Does Farey ordering suppress discrepancy beyond fixed gaps, reflection, and the full fixed-`nx` endpoint hierarchy?

## Observation

`VIS-026` gives an exact same-gap permutation control: uniformly permuting a fixed gap multiset makes the cumulative rank discrepancy a finite-population bridge with exact mean squared energy `sigma_g^2 N(N+1)/6`. The actual Farey discrepancy is far smaller at tested orders, so gap ordering contains information beyond the one-point gap multiset.

`VIS-027` removes an exact symmetry artifact. Farey reflection makes the discrepancy path antisymmetric, annihilating every odd Dirichlet sine mode. Even after conditioning the same-gap permutation null on that reflection, the finite Farey energy remains strongly suppressed.

`VIS-028` then shows that the deterministic unit-fraction endpoint fan already forces an `r=Theta(n)` even-mode spectral scale. But `VIS-029` strengthens that control: the fan is only the first layer of a whole deterministic fixed-`nx` endpoint profile. For every fixed `Y`, the rank below `y/n` has the limit

`R_n(y)/n -> K(y)=sum_(a<=y) phi(a)(1/a-1/y)`,

and the scaled local discrepancy tends to

`H(y)=y-(pi^2/3)K(y)`.

The reflected endpoint window through `Y/n` has `nE_(n,Y)->2 integral_1^Y H(y)^2 dK(y)` and still occupies `Theta(n)` ranks, so it produces the same `r=Theta(n)` spectral scale. Numerators `2,3,...` therefore keep deterministic endpoint structure alive after the `Y=2` unit-fraction fan is subtracted.

The **existence or persistence of an `r/n` modal collapse after subtracting only the unit-fraction fan is not a discriminating signal** for an interior Farey mechanism.

## Research question

After controlling a pre-fixed family of fixed-`nx` endpoint layers — not just the first unit-fraction fan — does the remaining Farey ordering exhibit a stable multiscale spectral organization that cannot be reduced to the classical Franel–Landau scalar discrepancy, familiar Möbius summatory quantities, fixed gap statistics, reflection symmetry, or bounded local structure?

If a residual survives, can it be localized to genuinely interior information such as denominator strata, mediant/Farey-parent ancestry, long-range gap order, or a cross-band coupling whose endpoint contribution is provably negligible?

## Why it may matter

The Farey mandate asks whether geometry or multiscale organization survives after classical scalar discrepancy information is accounted for. The visual branch has now removed three increasingly strong false positives: the gap multiset alone, exact reflection parity, and the apparent `r/n` scale generated by deterministic endpoint geometry.

This makes the next positive result much more valuable but also raises the bar. A stable bulk signal that survives an enlarging pre-fixed endpoint control would isolate information not explained by bounded-numerator boundary layers. Failure would close a visually attractive branch without confusing endpoint rank geometry with long-range arithmetic organization.

## Decisive test

Choose endpoint cutoffs `Y_1<...<Y_J` **before inspecting the residual spectra**. For each `Y_j`, remove the exact ranks with `x<=Y_j/n` and their Farey reflections, or use a matched ensemble that preserves those endpoints together with the complete gap multiset and reflection symmetry. Form the even Dirichlet spectral measure of the remaining discrepancy in the fixed coordinates of `VIS-027`.

A candidate bulk signal must be stable as `Y_j` increases across the pre-fixed family and across substantially larger Farey orders. Better still, derive a statistic or band whose fixed-`nx` endpoint contribution is asymptotically negligible. Only after that gate should stronger controls preserve adjacent gap pairs, bounded-depth blocks, denominator strata, and mediant/Farey-parent relations.

Kill the route if the apparent `r/n` profile drifts materially with the endpoint cutoff, is recreated by endpoint-preserving controls, collapses to known Farey/Möbius discrepancy asymptotics, or is determined by admitted bounded local statistics.

## Evidence boundary

`VIS-026` establishes the fixed-gap bridge control. `VIS-027` establishes reflection parity and a reflection-preserving same-gap baseline. `VIS-028` establishes the first endpoint fan and its `r=Theta(n)` scale. `VIS-029` establishes that every fixed `nx` endpoint window contains further deterministic totient layers on the same scale.

None of these results explains the complete Farey spectral profile or yields a stronger RH criterion. They only show that the current endpoint null must be broader than the unit-fraction fan. This file remains a `status: proposed` clue, not mathematical evidence.
