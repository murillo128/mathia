# FD-007 — squarefree-supported ternary increment freedom still does not improve the leading Franel--Mertens dual constant

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED + SQUAREFREE-SUPPORTED-TERNARY-RELAXATION + DISCRETE-SIGN-CONSTRUCTION + SHARP-ASYMPTOTIC-DUALITY + NEGATIVE/OBSTRUCTION`. `FD-003` identifies the quadratic Franel discrepancy with the normalized square-GCD form

\[
K_N(d,e)=\frac{\gcd(d,e)^2}{de}
\]

on the divisor-scale Mertens staircase. `FD-004`--`FD-006` show that equal-floor block structure and every fixed finite initial segment of the exact Jordan-moment hierarchy still leave the leading scalar-dual constant equal to `zeta(2)`. A different source restriction survives those results: the staircase is generated by one increment sequence, and the actual increments satisfy

\[
\mu(1)=1,\qquad
\mu(n)=0\ \text{on nonsquarefree }n,\qquad
\mu(n)\in\{-1,+1\}\ \text{on squarefree }n.
\]

Even **that exact support-and-amplitude information is insufficient if the signs on squarefree integers are allowed to vary independently**. Let `q_d=floor(N/d)`. For a sequence `a=(a_n)_(1<=n<=N)` define

\[
A_a(x)=\sum_{n\le x}a_n,
\qquad
m_d(a)=A_a(q_d).
\tag{1}
\]

Let `mathcal U_N` be the finite class of all sign sequences satisfying

\[
a_1=1,
\qquad
a_n=0\ \text{if }n\text{ is nonsquarefree},
\qquad
a_n\in\{-1,+1\}\ \text{if }n\text{ is squarefree},
\tag{2}
\]

and define the sharp coordinate constant on their induced staircases by

\[
\Theta_N
:=\max_{a\in\mathcal U_N}
\frac{m_1(a)^2}{m(a)^TK_Nm(a)}.
\tag{3}
\]

The actual Möbius sequence belongs to `mathcal U_N`. Nevertheless

\[
\boxed{
\Theta_N\longrightarrow\zeta(2)=\frac{\pi^2}{6}.
}
\tag{4}
\]

In fact the construction below gives the explicit one-sided rate

\[
\boxed{
0\le\zeta(2)-\Theta_N\ll N^{-1/10}.
}
\tag{5}
\]

Consequently, after the Farey discrepancy has been compressed to the `FD-003` GCD energy, the facts that Möbius increments are ternary, vanish exactly on nonsquarefree integers, have unit modulus on every squarefree integer, and start with `mu(1)=1` still do not by themselves improve the RH-facing leading scalar coefficient. The remaining cumulative source information must use the **specific correlated sign law** `mu(n)=(-1)^{omega(n)}` on squarefree integers, multiplicativity/divisor compatibility tying those signs together, an interaction of that law with unbounded-depth identities, or another genuinely nonlocal restriction. Ordered pre-cumulative Farey information remains a separate route.

## 1. The unrestricted principal-block extremizer has uniformly bounded coordinates

Retain the `FD-003` notation

\[
C_R=e_1^TK_R^{-1}e_1
=\sum_{r\le R}\frac{\mu(r)^2}{J_2(r)},
\qquad C_R\uparrow\zeta(2).
\tag{6}
\]

Let

\[
u^{(R)}:=K_R^{-1}e_1.
\tag{7}
\]

Then

\[
(u^{(R)})^TK_Ru^{(R)}=C_R,
\qquad u^{(R)}_1=C_R.
\tag{8}
\]

The inverse Smith/Jordan factorization of `FD-003` gives, for `1<=d<=R`,

\[
u^{(R)}_d
=d\sum_{\substack{r\le R\\d\mid r}}
\frac{\mu(r/d)\mu(r)}{J_2(r)}.
\tag{9}
\]

Since

\[
J_2(r)
=r^2\prod_{p\mid r}(1-p^{-2})
\ge\frac{r^2}{\zeta(2)},
\]

one obtains the uniform bound

\[
\begin{aligned}
|u^{(R)}_d|
&\le d\sum_{k\le R/d}\frac1{J_2(dk)}\\
&\le\frac{\zeta(2)}d
\sum_{k\ge1}\frac1{k^2}
=\frac{\zeta(2)^2}{d}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{10}
\]

In particular

\[
|u^{(R)}_d-u^{(R)}_{d+1}|\le2\zeta(2)^2,
\tag{11}
\]

with the convention `u^(R)_(R+1)=0`. No assertion is made that this vector is itself a Mertens staircase. The point is only that its coordinate oscillation is bounded strongly enough to be encoded by many squarefree sign choices on the hyperbola intervals.

## 2. The first `N^(1/5)` hyperbola intervals contain a positive fraction of squarefree integers

Put

\[
R=R(N):=\lfloor N^{1/5}\rfloor,
\qquad
q_d:=\left\lfloor\frac Nd\right\rfloor,
\qquad
I_d:=(q_{d+1},q_d]\cap\mathbb Z
\quad(1\le d\le R).
\tag{12}
\]

For large `N`, `R(R+1)<N`, so the values `q_1,...,q_(R+1)` are strictly decreasing. Let

\[
L_d:=|I_d|=q_d-q_{d+1}.
\]

The elementary floor estimate gives

\[
L_d\ge\frac{N}{d(d+1)}-1
\ge\frac{N}{R(R+1)}-1
\asymp N^{3/5}.
\tag{13}
\]

Let `S_d` be the number of squarefree integers in `I_d`. A nonsquarefree integer is divisible by `k^2` for some `k>=2`. A union bound therefore gives, for any interval of length `L_d` contained in `[1,N]`,

\[
L_d-S_d
\le
\sum_{2\le k\le\sqrt N}
\left(\frac{L_d}{k^2}+1\right)
\le
(\zeta(2)-1)L_d+\sqrt N.
\tag{14}
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
S_d\ge(2-\zeta(2))L_d-\sqrt N.
}
\tag{15}
\]

Because `L_d\gg N^(3/5)`, there is an absolute constant `c_0>0` such that, uniformly for `d<=R` and all sufficiently large `N`,

\[
\boxed{S_d\ge c_0L_d.}
\tag{16}
\]

This deliberately uses only the elementary squarefree support property. No prime-number theorem, zero-free region, or estimate for the actual Möbius signs is imported.

## 3. Any bounded principal extremizer can be encoded by actual `+-1` squarefree signs up to unit error

Let

\[
L_*:=\min_{1\le d\le R}L_d,
\qquad
t_N:=\frac{c_0L_*}{8\zeta(2)^2}.
\tag{17}
\]

Then

\[
t_N\asymp\frac{N}{R^2}\asymp N^{3/5}.
\tag{18}
\]

Let

\[
Q(x):=\#\{n\le x:n\text{ squarefree}\}.
\]

For each `1<=d<=R`, choose an integer `y_d` of the required parity

\[
y_d\equiv Q(q_d)\pmod2
\tag{19}
\]

such that

\[
|y_d-t_Nu^{(R)}_d|\le1.
\tag{20}
\]

Such an integer always exists because integers of a fixed parity have spacing two. Also choose

\[
y_{R+1}\in\{0,1\},
\qquad
y_{R+1}\equiv Q(q_{R+1})\pmod2.
\tag{21}
\]

Set

\[
\Delta_d:=y_d-y_{d+1}.
\tag{22}
\]

The parity condition is exactly the one required for `Delta_d` to be the sum of `S_d` signs:

\[
\Delta_d
\equiv Q(q_d)-Q(q_{d+1})
\equiv S_d\pmod2.
\tag{23}
\]

Moreover (11), (17), and (20) imply

\[
|\Delta_d|
\le2\zeta(2)^2t_N+2
\le\frac{c_0L_*}{4}+2
\le S_d
\tag{24}
\]

for all sufficiently large `N`. Therefore the `S_d` squarefree integers in `I_d` can be assigned signs `+-1` with total sum exactly `Delta_d`: take `(S_d+Delta_d)/2` positive signs and the rest negative.

It remains to assign the squarefree signs below `q_(R+1)`. Enumerate those squarefree integers increasingly and alternate the signs `+1,-1,+1,-1,...`, starting with `a_1=+1`. Then

\[
\left|\sum_{\substack{n\le x\\n\text{ squarefree}}}a_n\right|\le1
\qquad(1\le x\le q_{R+1}),
\tag{25}
\]

and the value at `q_(R+1)` is exactly the parity-compatible value (21). Set `a_n=0` on every nonsquarefree integer. On each `I_d`, use the sign assignment from (24). This defines one `a in mathcal U_N` on **every** integer `1<=n<=N`; no squarefree position is left at amplitude zero.

Telescoping (22) now gives

\[
A_a(q_d)=y_d
\qquad(1\le d\le R),
\tag{26}
\]

while (25) gives

\[
|A_a(q_d)|\le1
\qquad(d>R).
\tag{27}
\]

Thus the induced staircase can be written

\[
\boxed{
m(a)=t_Nw^{(N)}+z^{(N)},
}
\tag{28}
\]

where `w^(N)` is `u^(R)` padded by zeros to length `N`, and

\[
\boxed{|z_d^{(N)}|\le1\quad(1\le d\le N).}
\tag{29}
\]

This is the decisive source-side construction. The principal GCD extremizer need not itself have ternary increments. It is enough that, after a growing amplification `t_N`, its required hyperbola-block differences can be realized as totals of the many physical squarefree sign slots in each interval, with only a uniformly bounded cumulative rounding error.

## 4. The bounded rounding tail has only `O(N)` GCD energy

Because every entry of `K_N` is nonnegative, (29) gives

\[
(z^{(N)})^TK_Nz^{(N)}
\le\mathbf1^TK_N\mathbf1.
\tag{30}
\]

The Smith/Jordan square decomposition from `FD-003` evaluates the right side as

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf1^TK_N\mathbf1
&=\sum_{r\le N}J_2(r)
\left(\sum_{\substack{d\le N\\r\mid d}}\frac1d\right)^2\\
&=\sum_{r\le N}\frac{J_2(r)}{r^2}
H_{\lfloor N/r\rfloor}^2\\
&\le\sum_{r\le N}H_{\lfloor N/r\rfloor}^2
\ll N,
\end{aligned}
\tag{31}
\]

where `H_m=sum_(j<=m)1/j`. The final bound is elementary: `H_m<=1+log m`, and comparison with

\[
N\int_0^\infty(1+u)^2e^{-u}\,du
\]

controls the corresponding sum. Hence

\[
\boxed{\|z^{(N)}\|_{K_N}=O(\sqrt N).}
\tag{32}
\]

On the other hand, by (8),

\[
\|t_Nw^{(N)}\|_{K_N}
=t_N\sqrt{C_R}
\asymp N^{3/5}.
\tag{33}
\]

The perturbation is therefore norm-negligible:

\[
\boxed{
\frac{\|z^{(N)}\|_{K_N}}
{t_N\|w^{(N)}\|_{K_N}}
=O(N^{-1/10}).
}
\tag{34}
\]

The same construction gives

\[
m_1(a)=t_NC_R+O(1).
\tag{35}
\]

Cauchy--Schwarz in the `K_N` inner product applied to (28) now yields

\[
\frac{m_1(a)^2}{m(a)^TK_Nm(a)}
=C_R+O(N^{-1/10}).
\tag{36}
\]

More precisely, the error in (36) can have either sign; what matters is the lower estimate

\[
\Theta_N\ge C_R-O(N^{-1/10}).
\tag{37}
\]

Since `R=floor(N^(1/5))`, `FD-003` gives

\[
0\le\zeta(2)-C_R\le\frac{\zeta(2)}R
=O(N^{-1/5}).
\tag{38}
\]

Combining (37)--(38),

\[
\Theta_N\ge\zeta(2)-O(N^{-1/10}).
\tag{39}
\]

The opposite inequality is immediate because every induced staircase is an ordinary real vector and `FD-003` gives the unrestricted sharp constant `C_N<=zeta(2)`:

\[
\Theta_N\le C_N\le\zeta(2).
\tag{40}
\]

Equations (39)--(40) prove (4)--(5).

## 5. What this rules out and what remains genuinely arithmetic

The class `mathcal U_N` is much smaller than the block-constant space of `FD-004`. Every member is generated by **one discrete increment sequence**, every nonsquarefree increment is forced to zero, every squarefree increment has exactly unit modulus, and the initial value `a_1=1` is fixed. Nevertheless the projective scalar-dual obstruction survives asymptotically unchanged.

What has been relaxed is precisely the sign correlation. For the actual Möbius function,

\[
\mu(n)=(-1)^{\omega(n)}
\qquad(n\text{ squarefree}),
\tag{41}
\]

so the signs are not independently selectable: they are forced by prime-factor parity and multiplicativity. The construction in Section 3 deliberately violates that source law in general. Therefore this finding does **not** show that the true Mertens staircase is close to the GCD dual extremizer, nor that Möbius multiplicativity is irrelevant. It proves the opposite boundary statement: amplitude, support, and ternary increment geometry alone are too weak, even when imposed exactly.

This result is also logically separate from `FD-005`--`FD-006`. The artificial sign assignments constructed here do not generally satisfy the exact total-sum or Jordan-moment right sides of the Möbius sequence. Thus one cannot combine the negative conclusions by intersection and claim that squarefree ternary support **together with** an unbounded hierarchy is harmless. A viable cumulative route may still come from coupling the exact sign law to those identities. The surviving question is whether that coupled arithmetic compatibility excludes the near-extremal directions, not whether one more independent relaxation does.

A useful falsification criterion for future cumulative claims follows immediately. If a proposed improvement uses only inequalities that are valid for every sequence with `|a_n|=mu(n)^2` and `a_1=1`, then it cannot improve the leading homogeneous Franel--Mertens scalar constant: the explicit class above already contains asymptotically sharp witnesses. A stronger theorem must fail on some of these arbitrary squarefree sign assignments for a reason traceable to the actual Möbius sign law.

## 6. Prior art and audit boundary

The squarefree-support identity `|mu(n)|=mu(n)^2`, the representation of nonsquarefree integers as multiples of a square, the elementary interval count (14), and the Smith/Jordan GCD-matrix factorization are classical ingredients. No novelty is claimed for any of them individually. The GCD-matrix input and its sharp coordinate constant are already anchored in `FD-003`.

A targeted literature search across Franel--Landau discrepancy, Mertens floor-quotient formulas, squarefree-supported coefficient variants, and GCD-matrix coordinate inequalities found neighboring work on Farey fractions with squarefree **denominators** and on classical Farey/Mertens discrepancy, but not an equivalent theorem for the present object. Those squarefree-denominator variants alter the Farey sample itself; here the Farey/Franel kernel is unchanged and only the hidden Möbius sign source is relaxed. This search boundary is not evidence of historical novelty, and no external theorem is load-bearing in (4).

The main adversarial checks are structural. The exponent `1/5` is not asserted to be optimal; it is chosen only so that the hyperbola intervals are simultaneously much longer than `sqrt(N)` and the amplification `t_N~N/R^2` dominates the `O(sqrt N)` energy of the rounding tail. The exact sign realization uses the parity condition (23); allowing real increments would make the result easier but weaker. The tail below `q_(R+1)` is not discarded: alternating all of its squarefree signs keeps every relevant cumulative value bounded by one and its full GCD energy is included in (31). Finally, the result concerns a homogeneous coordinate extraction from the exact `FD-003` energy. It does not give a bound for the actual Mertens function, prove any Farey spectral-allocation statement, or address a nonlinear output other than the scalar coordinate `M(N)`.

No RH improvement is established. The durable consequence is a sharper no-free-gain boundary on the cumulative route: **even the complete squarefree zero pattern and exact ternary amplitude of Möbius increments leave the leading Franel--Mertens dual constant unchanged unless their arithmetic signs are coupled in the specifically Möbius way.**
