# FD-011 — raw increment Plücker field collapses to the Farey-index algebra

**Status:** `LITERATURE+CLASSICAL-EXACT + EXACT-DERIVED + ORDERED-INFORMATION-BOUNDARY + NEGATIVE/OBSTRUCTION`. The live ordered branch asks whether orientation-sensitive data can survive before cumulative Farey discrepancy collapses to Mertens energy. A natural raw candidate is to lift consecutive Farey fractions to primitive homogeneous vectors and take determinants of consecutive increment vectors. That candidate does retain order, but it is not a new carrier: its nearest-neighbor Plücker field is exactly the Hall--Shiu Farey index minus two, and every fixed-lag increment minor is a fixed continuant expression in a finite window of the same classical index sequence.

With the standard periodic positive Farey convention of Hall--Shiu and Haynes, let

\[
\mathcal F_Q=\{\gamma_i=p_i/q_i:1\le i\le N(Q)\},
\qquad
\gamma_{i+N(Q)}=\gamma_i+1,
\tag{1}
\]

where `1/Q=gamma_1<...<gamma_N=1`, and put

\[
v_i:=\binom{p_i}{q_i}\in\mathbb Z^2,
\qquad
u_i:=\frac{q_{i-1}+q_{i+1}}{q_i}.
\tag{2}
\]

Consecutive Farey neighbors satisfy

\[
p_{i+1}q_i-p_iq_{i+1}=1,
\tag{3}
\]

and the next-term recurrence is

\[
\boxed{v_{i+1}=\nu_i v_i-v_{i-1}.}
\tag{4}
\]

Define the raw homogeneous increment

\[
u_i^{\rm raw}:=v_{i+1}-v_i
\tag{5}
\]

and its consecutive oriented Plücker minor

\[
P_i:={\det}(u_i^{\rm raw},u_{i+1}^{\rm raw}).
\tag{6}
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{P_i=\nu_{i+1}-2.}
\tag{7}
\]

Consequently Hall--Shiu's exact first-moment identity gives

\[
\boxed{
\sum_{i=1}^{N(Q)}P_i=N(Q)-1.
}
\tag{8}
\]

Thus the most direct ordered antisymmetric area field has a deterministic complete aggregate. More generally, every fixed-lag raw increment determinant is already contained in the generalized Farey-index/continuant algebra studied classically by Haynes. This closes the raw homogeneous-vector version of a Plücker escape, but it does **not** close a genuinely controlled discrepancy/refinement increment field whose coordinates are defined after an independent subtraction or quotient.

## 1. The nearest-neighbor minor is exactly the index minus two

Write the determinant bracket as

\[
[x,y]:=x_1y_2-x_2y_1.
\tag{9}
\]

Equation (3) is

\[
[v_i,v_{i+1}]=-1.
\tag{10}
\]

From (5), bilinearity gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
P_i
&=[v_{i+1}-v_i,v_{i+2}-v_{i+1}]\\
&=[v_{i+1},v_{i+2}]+[v_i,v_{i+1}]-[v_i,v_{i+2}]\\
&=-2-[v_i,v_{i+2}].
\end{aligned}
\tag{11}
\]

Using (4) at the middle index,

\[
v_{i+2}=\nu_{i+1}v_{i+1}-v_i,
\]

so

\[
[v_i,v_{i+2}]
=\nu_{i+1}[v_i,v_{i+1}]
=-\nu_{i+1}.
\tag{12}
\]

Substitution into (11) proves (7). No asymptotic statement, averaging, or denominator estimate is used.

Hall and Shiu proved in the same periodic convention that

\[
\sum_{i=1}^{N(Q)}\nu_i=3N(Q)-1.
\tag{13}
\]

Periodicity shifts the index harmlessly inside the sum, and (8) follows immediately:

\[
\sum_iP_i=(3N(Q)-1)-2N(Q)=N(Q)-1.
\]

Therefore the unweighted total signed area of successive raw homogeneous increments cannot carry an RH-sensitive discrepancy fluctuation: it is fixed exactly once the Farey order size is known.

## 2. Every fixed-lag raw increment minor is a generalized index expression

For `r>=0`, define the positive nonconsecutive Farey determinant

\[
D_{i,r}:=p_{i+r}q_i-p_iq_{i+r}=-[v_i,v_{i+r}],
\tag{14}
\]

so

\[
D_{i,0}=0,
\qquad
D_{i,1}=1.
\tag{15}
\]

Applying (4) at `i+r` gives the exact recurrence

\[
\boxed{
D_{i,r+1}=\nu_{i+r}D_{i,r}-D_{i,r-1}.
}
\tag{16}
\]

Thus, for each fixed `r`, `D_(i,r)` is a continuant polynomial in the finite window

\[
\nu_{i+1},\ldots,\nu_{i+r-1}.
\tag{17}
\]

For example,

\[
D_{i,2}=\nu_{i+1},
\qquad
D_{i,3}=\nu_{i+1}\nu_{i+2}-1.
\tag{18}
\]

Up to the harmless shift of indexing conventions, these `D_(i,r)` are precisely the generalized `k`-indices defined by Haynes as numerators of differences of nonconsecutive Farey fractions. Haynes proves the corresponding convergent-polynomial representation and asymptotic first moments for every fixed separation.

Now fix `h>=1` and define the fixed-lag raw increment minor

\[
P_{i,h}:=[u_i^{\rm raw},u_{i+h}^{\rm raw}].
\tag{19}
\]

Expanding the two differences gives

\[
\boxed{
P_{i,h}
=D_{i,h+1}-D_{i,h}
+D_{i+1,h-1}-D_{i+1,h}.
}
\tag{20}
\]

Every term on the right is a fixed-window continuant in the classical index sequence. Hence for every fixed `h`, the entire field `P_(i,h)` is algebraically generated by finitely many adjacent Hall--Shiu indices. Equation (7) is simply the case `h=1`.

This also gives a direct falsification rule for proposed raw exterior observables: taking more finite separations of the same homogeneous Farey vectors does not leave the generalized-index algebra. A genuinely different Plücker carrier must change the retained vectors, the scale with `Q`, or the source-conditioned relation before this reduction.

## 3. What this does and does not remove from the ordered frontier

The result is stronger than observing the elementary unimodularity

\[
p_{i+1}q_i-p_iq_{i+1}=1.
\]

Even after passing from the fractions themselves to **increment vectors** and taking their antisymmetric area, the nearest-neighbor field is still only `nu-2`. At every fixed lag, (20) keeps the construction inside finite index windows. Thus raw determinant geometry, raw consecutive homogeneous increments, and fixed-step numerator-of-difference refinements should be treated as classical controls rather than automatically as new Farey-discrepancy information.

This does not imply that the Farey index sequence is information-free. It encodes genuine order and its correlations have substantial classical arithmetic structure. It also does not prove that every function of a growing index window is useless for RH. The negative conclusion is narrower: **the raw Plücker operation itself does not manufacture an information channel outside the existing Farey-index dynamics.**

In particular, `CLUE-ordered-plucker-refinement-transfer.md` explicitly proposes a different object: controlled refinement/discrepancy increment vectors after a predeclared subtraction, not the raw homogeneous vectors `v_i` or their differences (5). The present finding supplies a mandatory control for that clue. If its controlled vectors are later shown to differ from the raw construction only by an invertible local change of coordinates or a coboundary that leaves (20) unchanged, the route classicalizes. If the control subtraction genuinely changes the retained quotient and the resulting minors survive the clue's scalarization and matched-control tests, (7)--(20) do not refute them.

Likewise, a moving separation `h=h(Q)`, a nonlocal aggregation, or an ancestry-dependent coordinate system is outside the fixed-lag theorem. Such extensions must still show an RH-facing gain rather than merely recover a larger portion of the ordered Farey sequence.

## 4. Prior art and evidence boundary

The index

\[
\nu_i=(q_{i-1}+q_{i+1})/q_i
\]

and the exact sum (13) are classical results of R. R. Hall and P. Shiu, *The index of a Farey sequence*, Michigan Mathematical Journal **51**(1) (2003), 209--223, DOI `10.1307/mmj/1049832901`. Alan K. Haynes, *Numerators of differences of nonconsecutive Farey fractions*, International Journal of Number Theory **6**(3) (2010), 655--666, DOI `10.1142/S1793042110003113`, defines generalized indices by nonconsecutive Farey determinants and proves their convergent-polynomial representation and fixed-separation average asymptotics. The recurrence (16) and increment identity (20) are derived here directly from the elementary Farey next-term relation; no novelty is claimed for continuants, generalized Farey indices, or their classical distribution theory.

The Mathia-specific durable content is the boundary relevant to the current ordered-discrepancy program: the naive antisymmetric area of successive homogeneous increments, and indeed every fixed-lag version of it, is not a new pre-cumulative carrier but a fixed algebraic transform of the already-studied Farey-index process. This is a negative classification of one natural Plücker route.

No estimate for Franel--Landau discrepancy, Möbius cancellation, or RH follows from (7)--(20). The finding does not settle the proposed controlled refinement Plücker field, the spectral-allocation clue, growing-lag index statistics, or any nonlocal source-conditioned relation.
