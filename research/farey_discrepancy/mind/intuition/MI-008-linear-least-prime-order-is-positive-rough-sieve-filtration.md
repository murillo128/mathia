# MI-008 — Linear least-prime order is a positive commuting rough-sieve filtration

**Evidence level:** exact route restriction from [FD-110](../../findings/FD-110-least-prime-factor-order-remains-a-commuting-rough-sieve-filtration.md), extending the commuting incidence algebra of FD-109. No nonlinear least-prime theorem or RH consequence is claimed.

For the physical coefficient law `a(n)=mu(rad n) phi(rad n)/n`, excluding all primes below `p` does not create a signed ordered interaction. Inclusion-exclusion cancels against the physical prime signs and gives

`B_p = (prod_(q<p) A_q) H`, with `A_q = I + (1-q^(-1)) R_q`,

where the dilation resolvents `R_q` and all `A_q` commute. Each `A_q` has a nonnegative dilation expansion.

The least-prime packet is therefore

`L_p = a(p) R_p (prod_(q<p) A_q) H`

and expands as a positive mixture of deeper samples `H(floor(X/r))` with `P^+(r)=p`. Every such denominator satisfies `r>=p`; extra prime-prefix information only moves farther down the source.

More generally, every finite linear statistic of finitely many divisibility bits is a polynomial in the same commuting resolvents. The order encoded by finite Boolean prime labels changes coefficients and which product depths appear, but does not manufacture a noncommutative exterior defect or a new Cartesian family of same-scale source-rich constraints.

At the terminal cutoff, `p>P_*(X)` already forces **all** samples in `L_p(X)` below the source-rich range. Thus linear least-prime-factor ordering does not extend the one-prime terminal fan beyond the product-depth budget isolated by FD-109.

The live escape must change the operation, not just the label: nonlinear products before summation, cross-horizon persistence/global descent, or refinement geometry not reducible to finite divisibility bits can still carry information outside this linear rough-sieve filtration.

**Boundary.** The result does not rule out nonlinear functionals of the growing family `{L_p}`, unbounded-prefix phenomena, cross-horizon correlations, or another ordered observable whose algebra is not a finite linear Boolean divisibility statistic.
