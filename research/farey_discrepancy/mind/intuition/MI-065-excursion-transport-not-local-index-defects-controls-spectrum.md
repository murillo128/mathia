# MI-065 — Excursion permutation spectra are controlled by cumulative length transport, not local Farey-index defects

**Evidence level:** exact permutation identity and uniform prime-order asymptotic from [FD-319](../../findings/FD-319-four-vanishing-farey-index-defects-carry-macroscopic-energy.md) and [FD-320](../../findings/FD-320-all-prime-farey-excursion-permutations-have-an-exact-transport-spectrum.md).

For prime-order Farey excursions `E_j` of geometric width `1/p` and gap count `ell_j`, set `w_j=ell_j/N_p-1/p`, `U_j=sum_(i<j)w_i`, and `U_t^sigma=sum_(s<t)w_(sigma(s))`. Reassemble any permutation `sigma` while retaining each directed denominator-edge gap. The full rank discrepancy vector satisfies the **exact** transport identity
`D^sigma=P_sigma D+v^sigma` with `||v^sigma||_2^2=T_p(sigma)=sum_t ell_(sigma(t))(U_(sigma(t))-U_t^sigma)^2`.
As the true Farey `||D||_2=o(1)`, uniformly in permutations,
`|sqrt(E_p^sigma)-sqrt(T_p(sigma))|<=sqrt(E_p)=o(1)`.
The discrete sine transform carries the same uniform `l^2` approximation over *all modes*.

The local Hall--Shiu defect count is instead the number of nonconsecutive boundaries in the excursion permutation. FD-319 exhibits a reflection-symmetric permutation with **four** defects, each distance `1/p` from integer index one, but `T_p->3/(4pi^2)`; FD-320 also builds reflection-symmetric permutations with diverging defect counts and `T_p->0`. Neither total index distance nor defect count controls the transported spectral energy in this class.

[FD-327](../../findings/FD-327-same-prime-three-defect-profiles-have-unbounded-energy-ratios.md) sharpens the value-only failure: two reassemblies of the *same* prime-order excursion list retain identical **ordered values** of three scaled local index defects and the entire directed-edge inventory, while their limiting transport energies can have an arbitrarily large ratio. Their defect **ranks** differ. In contrast, [FD-328](../../findings/FD-328-three-farey-index-defect-ranks-reconstruct-the-excursion-permutation.md) proves that the exact three defective rank positions determine the two swapped contiguous blocks: with original strictly increasing cumulative gap counts `S_j`, the three cut labels are `1+S^{-1}(r_1-1)`, `1+S^{-1}(r_1+r_3-r_2-1)`, and `1+S^{-1}(r_3-1)`. This is exact for a fixed prime, not a stability estimate.

[FD-329](../../findings/FD-329-four-defect-ranks-rigid-five-admit-transport-ambiguity.md) extends rank-only reconstruction to **four** defects (internal block order `CBA`) and breaks it at **five**. The original five-break pair `BDACR` versus `DBACR` has identical exact defective ranks and a nonzero transport difference of order `1/p`, without matching defective index values. [FD-330](../../findings/FD-330-five-identical-defect-ranks-permit-macroscopic-farey-energy-separation.md) strengthens this from a vanishing to a **macroscopic** discrepancy: for fixed integers `u>v>=3` and primes `p ≡ -1 (mod lcm(u,v))`, symmetric positive-proportion blocks give the same five ranks for the two words, while
`T_p(BDACR)-T_p(DBACR) -> (6/pi^2)(1/v-1/u)^2(1/u+1/v)>0`.
Their true reconstructed Franel-energy difference has the same limit, using the uniform `|sqrt(E_p)-sqrt(T_p)|=o(1)` transport approximation. Exactly equal **rank positions** therefore do not even imply asymptotically equal energy at five defects in this labeled-edge reassembly class; the **defect index values are not matched**, so joint rank-and-value sufficiency remains open.

The exact destination-side condition for asymptotically vanishing energy is `T_p(sigma)->0`, not approximate local index integrality. Nothing here turns an artificial reassembled grid into another canonical Farey sequence or changes the true Farey identity; the gap is whether natural source restrictions enforce small **weighted cumulative** excursion displacement.
