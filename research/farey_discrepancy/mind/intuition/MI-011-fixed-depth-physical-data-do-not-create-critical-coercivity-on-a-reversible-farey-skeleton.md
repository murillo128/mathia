# MI-011 — Even infinite sparse Möbius ancestry can remain noncoercive on sparse horizons

**Evidence level:** exact for the floor-quotient Fourier skeleton through [FD-117](../../findings/FD-117-full-farey-discrepancy-field-is-invertibly-equivalent-to-the-quotient-mertens-staircase.md)--[FD-128](../../findings/FD-128-infinite-sparse-mobius-ancestry-still-saturates-the-common-source-fourier-skeleton.md). The matched controls are constructed sources, not the Möbius function, and no RH consequence is claimed.

FD-117--FD-120 show that rich same-horizon physical-looking constraints remain reversible enough to admit critical equality-ray witnesses. FD-121--FD-124 identify a genuine common-source cost: a polynomially bounded source cannot Cesàro-saturate along horizon schedules with finite average logarithmic growth. FD-125--FD-126 then realize the remaining superexponential escape with one permanent squarefree unit-sign source.

FD-127 proves that exact Möbius ancestry in any fixed finite set of prime directions is still cheap. FD-128 removes the next tempting threshold. One can choose an **infinite** but sufficiently sparse prime set `S` and one source satisfying the exact identities

`a_(pn)=-a_n` for every `p in S` with `p` not dividing `n`,

while keeping `|Y(x)|<<_S sqrt(x)` and asymptotically saturating the floor-quotient skeleton on sufficiently sparse horizons. The construction factors the ancestry through an `S`-free squarefree core; summable semigroup weights keep both the source envelope and the triangular inverse finite.

The surviving arithmetic resource is therefore not finite versus infinite multiplicative rank. What matters is **how much rank is visible at the active scale**. An infinite family placed so sparsely that its reciprocal/semigroup costs remain summable leaves enough core capacity to interpolate the equality prefix.

A coercive theorem must price a scale-dependent breadth: density or product of active primes below the relevant horizon, growth of the multiplicative semigroup inverse, full coprime multiplicativity, or an equivalent expanding divisor/cross-horizon identity family. The decisive threshold should say when those costs become comparable to the available core capacity and force a nonzero critical energy gap.

**Boundary.** FD-128 does not show that every infinite prime family is cheap, determine the sharp breadth threshold, or realize the actual Möbius source. Its conclusion is narrower and reusable: global cardinality of exact arithmetic constraints is not a coercivity measure; the destination only feels the constraints that accumulate quantitatively at its working scale.
