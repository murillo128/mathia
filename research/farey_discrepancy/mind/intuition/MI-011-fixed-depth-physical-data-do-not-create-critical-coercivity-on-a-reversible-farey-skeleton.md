# MI-011 — Same-horizon physical laws can be cheap, while bounded-scale source coherence forces a Cesàro gap

**Evidence level:** exact for the floor-quotient Fourier skeleton through [FD-117](../../findings/FD-117-full-farey-discrepancy-field-is-invertibly-equivalent-to-the-quotient-mertens-staircase.md), [FD-118](../../findings/FD-118-floor-quotient-frequencies-form-a-minimal-linear-fourier-skeleton.md), [FD-119](../../findings/FD-119-fixed-mertens-endpoint-data-cost-only-bounded-energy-on-the-floor-quotient-fourier-skeleton.md), [FD-120](../../findings/FD-120-squarefree-unit-increments-and-one-divisor-identity-still-saturate-the-fourier-skeleton.md), [FD-121](../../findings/FD-121-coherent-dyadic-horizons-force-a-uniform-fourier-skeleton-energy-gap.md), [FD-122](../../findings/FD-122-squarefree-dilations-force-fourier-skeleton-antichain-gaps.md), and [FD-123](../../findings/FD-123-polynomial-growth-forces-a-cesaro-fourier-skeleton-gap-on-bounded-ratio-chains.md). The same-horizon countermodels are synthetic quotient sources; the multihorizon inequalities are exact for any common source satisfying their stated hypotheses.

FD-117 shows that the complete cumulative Farey discrepancy field is invertibly equivalent to the quotient-Mertens staircase. FD-118 compresses its same-horizon Fourier data to the divisor-closed floor-quotient skeleton `Q_N`, of size `2 sqrt(N)+O(1)`, without losing that source.

FD-119 identifies the coercivity obstruction inside this reversible carrier. In reindexed source coordinates the skeleton has the exact Möbius equality ray `x_k=m mu(k)/k`, for which every Fourier mode except the first vanishes and the normalized energy is exactly `m^2`. Any fixed true Mertens prefix plus the exact identity `sum_(d<=N)y_floor(N/d)=1` can be grafted onto that ray with only `O_R(1)` additional energy. At the RH-critical amplitude `m_N~sqrt(N)`, fixed-depth truth therefore has vanishing relative cost.

FD-120 shows that **depth by itself is not the missing resource**. At each sufficiently large horizon one can impose the complete squarefree/nonsquarefree increment alphabet, `a_N(1)=1`, the exact single-horizon divisor identity and terminal size `sqrt(N)+O(1)`, yet still obtain `E_N^Q(Y_N)=Y_N(N)^2+o(N)`. The construction is allowed to reprogram its squarefree signs and witness as the horizon changes.

FD-121 isolates that freedom quantitatively. For one common source `Y`, with `Delta_H=E_H^Q(Y)-|Y(H)|^2`, every `N>=6` satisfies

`Delta_N + Delta_2N >= c_0 (|Y(N)|^2+|Y(2N)|^2)`,

where `c_0=(41-sqrt(1553))/64>0`. Thus one source cannot be a critical relative near-extremizer at two consecutive dyadic horizons. FD-122 shows that doubling is not special: for every fixed squarefree `r>1`, there is `c_r>0` with

`Delta_N + Delta_(rN) >= c_r (|Y(N)|^2+|Y(rN)|^2)`

for all sufficiently large `N`. In a cube generated by any fixed finite prime family, sufficiently cheap endpoints form a Sperner antichain.

FD-123 closes the corresponding **bounded-ratio average escape**, including nonsquarefree transitions. Put `S_H=|Y(H)|^2/E_H^Q(Y)`. Along any integer-ratio chain `H_j=q_jH_(j-1)` with `2<=q_j<=Q`, every polynomially bounded source `|Y(n)|<=Cn^alpha` satisfies

`liminf_(n->infinity) (1/(n+1)) sum_(j=0)^n (1-S_(H_j)) >= Gamma_(alpha,Q) > 0`.

The mechanism is exhaustive at bounded ratio. Squarefree steps pay the FD-122 pair gap. If a current step is nonsquarefree, then for every earlier horizon `H_i` the quotient `H_j/H_i` is an exact visible floor-quotient coordinate with Möbius value zero. FD-122 coordinate stability then makes high saturation at `H_j` force large endpoint amplification relative to `H_i`; a positive density of such highly saturated nonsquarefree stages would outrun the polynomial envelope. For the physical source `Y=M`, `|M(n)|<=n` supplies this hypothesis unconditionally.

The reusable distinction is now three-way. Same-horizon admissibility can be asymptotically invisible. Nonredundant multihorizon compatibility can force a critical-scale cost. With only polynomial source growth, that cost already has positive **Cesàro density** on every bounded integer-ratio schedule. But none of these statements forces a prescribed horizon to pay the cost.

**Boundary.** FD-123 does not prove a pointwise Mertens/Farey estimate, improve the Franel--Landau exponent, control unbounded-ratio or supermultiplicatively sparse schedules, or give constants uniform as the ratio ceiling grows. Sparse near-equality horizons may remain. The surviving theorem must convert bounded-scale average incompatibility into designated-endpoint coercivity, control the unbounded-scale escape, or add a genuinely new arithmetic source law rather than another representation of cumulative discrepancy.
