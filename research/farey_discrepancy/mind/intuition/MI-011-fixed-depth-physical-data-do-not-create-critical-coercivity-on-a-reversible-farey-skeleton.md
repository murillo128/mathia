# MI-011 — Scale-visible Möbius ancestry is the resource, but complete-prefix critical control is already the target

**Evidence level:** exact for the floor-quotient Fourier skeleton through [FD-117](../../findings/FD-117-full-farey-discrepancy-field-is-invertibly-equivalent-to-the-quotient-mertens-staircase.md)--[FD-129](../../findings/FD-129-growing-complete-prime-prefix-ancestry-transfers-every-uniform-prefix-envelope-to-mertens.md). The matched controls are constructed sources, not the Möbius function, except on prefixes where exact ancestry forces equality. No new RH consequence is claimed beyond the classical Mertens criterion used in FD-129.

FD-117--FD-120 show that rich same-horizon physical-looking constraints remain reversible enough to admit critical equality-ray witnesses. FD-121--FD-124 identify a genuine common-source cost; FD-125--FD-126 realize the remaining superexponential escape with one permanent squarefree unit-sign source.

FD-127 proves that exact Möbius ancestry in any fixed finite set of prime directions is still cheap. FD-128 removes “infinite rank” as a threshold: one can choose an infinite but sufficiently sparse prime set `S`, retain `|Y(x)|<<_S sqrt(x)`, and still asymptotically saturate the skeleton on sufficiently sparse horizons. What matters is how much arithmetic rank is visible at the working scale.

FD-129 supplies the opposite boundary. If the ancestry set at stage `j` contains every prime `p<=Y_j`, then unique factorization forces `a_n^(j)=mu(n)` and `A_j(x)=M(x)` for every `x<=Y_j`. Once `Y_j->infinity`, any **stage-uniform** envelope on those prefixes transfers to `M(x)` pointwise. In particular, demanding `|A_j(x)|<=C_epsilon x^(1/2+epsilon)` uniformly on `x<=Y_j` is RH-equivalent; changing the synthetic source with the horizon does not evade the recovery.

The surviving arithmetic resource is therefore a **noncircular scale-dependent breadth**. It must constrain enough prime/divisor directions to reduce the free-core capacity, but it cannot be counted as progress if the same hypothesis also reconstructs the physical prefix and imports the desired Mertens envelope there. Approximate/aggregate ancestry, free-region estimates, semigroup inverse growth or a direct skeleton energy theorem remain possible currencies precisely because they need not contain that recovered-prefix target.

**Boundary.** FD-129 does not show that broad ancestry is useless, nor that every uniform source estimate is circular. The transfer uses complete initial-prime coverage, exact ancestry and an envelope imposed on the recovered prefix with constants uniform in the stage. Dropping any of those features can leave a genuinely synthetic comparison problem.
