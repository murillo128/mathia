# MI-011 — Same-horizon laws are cheap; even fixed finite Möbius ancestry remains noncoercive on repeated squares

**Evidence level:** exact for the floor-quotient Fourier skeleton through [FD-117](../../findings/FD-117-full-farey-discrepancy-field-is-invertibly-equivalent-to-the-quotient-mertens-staircase.md)--[FD-127](../../findings/FD-127-fixed-prime-mobius-ancestry-still-saturates-the-repeated-square-fourier-skeleton.md). FD-127 preserves one permanent squarefree unit-sign source and imposes the true Möbius prime-step law in any prescribed fixed finite prime set. It does not impose full multiplicativity or a prime family growing with the horizon. No RH consequence is claimed.

FD-117--FD-120 show that the complete cumulative Farey field and its same-horizon Fourier data remain reversible enough to admit critical equality-ray witnesses even after rich single-horizon source constraints are imposed. FD-121--FD-124 identify the first genuine common-source cost: a polynomially bounded source cannot Cesàro-saturate along horizon schedules with finite average logarithmic growth. Any saturating schedule must be superexponentially sparse.

FD-125 shows that repeated squaring realizes that escape. FD-126 strengthens the control to one infinite source with exact squarefree support, exact unit amplitudes and global `O(sqrt n)` cumulative size while the quotient energy still tends to one on `H_(j+1)=H_j^2`.

FD-127 now removes the next tempting boundary. Fix any finite set of primes `P`. The same kind of permanent source can additionally satisfy the exact Möbius ancestry law

`a(pn)=-a(n)` for every `p in P` with `p` not dividing `n`,

while retaining the square-root envelope and repeated-square near-saturation. The finite prime directions can be factored out; the remaining `P`-free squarefree core still has enough triangular freedom to fit the low quotient coordinates at each sparse stage.

Thus the surviving arithmetic resource is not merely “some multiplicativity.” **Every fixed finite breadth of the true prime sign law remains endpoint-cheap in this representation.** The first plausible coercive variable is growing arithmetic rank: a prime family `P(H)` whose size/product/density grows with the horizon, full coprime multiplicativity, or an equivalent family of exact cross-horizon divisor constraints that eventually exhausts the triangular freedom.

A useful next theorem should quantify the FD-127 inverse as `P=P(H)` grows and identify the threshold at which the product/density/inverse constants force a critical energy deficit. Adding one more fixed prime direction, stronger support information or another fixed-depth local law cannot advance the line.

**Boundary.** FD-127 does not construct the Möbius function, impose all prime directions simultaneously, prove that an expanding family is coercive, or determine the sharp growth threshold. The durable conclusion is a rank statement: finite multiplicative ancestry can be absorbed; any source law capable of breaking the repeated-square matched control must grow in arithmetic breadth with scale.
