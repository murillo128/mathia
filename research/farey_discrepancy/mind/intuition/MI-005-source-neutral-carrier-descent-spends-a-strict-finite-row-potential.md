# MI-005 — Source-neutral carrier descent spends a strict finite row potential

**Evidence level:** proved for the tagged non-head carrier genealogies covered by FD-091, FD-093, FD-100 and [FD-101](../../findings/FD-101-one-shell-exactification-makes-tagged-nonhead-source-descent-impossible.md). No theorem yet lifts the charge to the adaptive energy packets used by the full predecessor argument.

FD-099 shows that physical horizon descent can be arbitrarily deep while the sampled source quotient stays fixed: repeated-square inventory can prepay many genuine contractions. Taken alone, that makes multiplicative horizon motion a poor proxy for source progress.

FD-100 identifies the finite resource on exact carriers. On `H=rX` with nonsquarefree `r>=4`, every currently available source-neutral non-head repair replaces `r` by a nonsquarefree `r'` satisfying `4 <= r' <= r/2`. Therefore

`I(r)=log_2(r/4)`

is a strict row potential: each source-neutral repair spends at least one unit, and the genealogy reaches the four-adic head after at most `ceil(log_2(r_0/4))` steps.

FD-101 shows that exact divisibility is not the essential boundary. For an arbitrary tagged state, put `x=floor(H/r)`. The first canonical compression has new source quotient only `x` or `x+1`. If it jumps to `x+1`, the child lands exactly on its new carrier row; all later non-head repairs then preserve that quotient by FD-100. Hence along the whole tagged non-head genealogy

`x_j in {x_0,x_0+1}`

and the quotient can change at most once. The same row potential still drains to the head. On the frontier core, where `x_0` grows, this single-shell correction is even relatively negligible.

This removes cumulative rounding as a possible source of fresh carrier inventory. A tagged lineage cannot repeatedly descend in physical horizon, drift through many quotient shells, and thereby reset its source-neutral budget. Its only outcomes before the head are finite row-potential expenditure plus at most one exactifying upward shell correction.

The remaining obstruction is therefore **retagging**. The adaptive predecessor theorem selects packet energy, not a fixed coordinate genealogy, and may reselect a different row after each step. A useful packet invariant must couple projective quality to the remaining row potential while charging switches of carrier identity; otherwise each new tag can import a fresh coordinate-level budget without contradicting the tagged termination theorem. If enough mass instead remains genealogically coherent, FD-100--FD-101 force that mass to the head without genuine source descent.

**Boundary.** The result controls tagged non-head ancestry, not how much energy follows one tag, how expensive retagging is, or whether repeated source-moving head events accumulate fast enough for an RH contradiction. The one-shell law is source non-descent, not packet-level cancellation.
