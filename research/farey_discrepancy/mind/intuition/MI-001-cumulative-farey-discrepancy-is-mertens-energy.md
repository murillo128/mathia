# MI-001 — Farey transfer is governed by causal protection plus logarithmic recurrence of energy-weighted frontier occupation

**Evidence level:** supported by the exact source/occupation reductions through FD-079 and the persisted Pintz inputs; no uniform pointwise occupation bound, elimination of sparse exceptional endpoints, or RH criterion is claimed.

FD-071 shows why arbitrary-later persistence does not improve normalization: a later source spike can dominate the same-horizon denominator and dilute an earlier episode. FD-072 gives the complementary positive mechanism. At a `sigma`-record `X`, `sigma>1/2`, the causal choice `T=4X`, `D=3` confines reciprocal quotients to the record-controlled prefix while row `4` samples the record exactly, forcing fixed positive nonsquarefree occupation. FD-073--FD-076 protect and globalize such record episodes.

FD-077 changes the abundance currency. The inverse floor-harmonic identity transfers Pintz's average absolute order into logarithmic `L^1` source mass of exponent `Theta` in every fixed power annulus, forcing frontier-near physical-source values on a set of polynomial counting exponent one. FD-078 then weights horizons by their actual physical energy: Hölder lifts the annular source mass to exponent `2 Theta`, and a fixed nonsquarefree row injects that moment into nonsquarefree Schur energy. Total energy, nonsquarefree energy, and Schur residual therefore have the same full power exponent, closing every fixed-power dilution.

FD-079 resolves the slower escape left by equal exponents. Complete nonsquarefree dilation blocks preserve the natural logarithmic horizon weight exactly, so a lower annulus injects into the next scale without a weighting loss. For the annular energy-weighted occupation `Omega_(D,alpha)`, this yields a finite logarithmic entropy budget

`limsup (1/W_X) sum lambda_H log(1/Omega_(D,alpha)(H)) <= L_q`,

where `L_q=2 Theta log q-log w(q)`. Hence the logarithmic geometric and arithmetic means are bounded below by `c_q=w(q)q^(-2 Theta)>0`; for `q=4`, `c_4>=3/64` unconditionally and equals `3/16` under RH. Any threshold `eta_X->0` is violated only on a set of asymptotically zero logarithmic measure. The annular occupation may have deep sparse dips, but it cannot drift to zero on almost every multiplicative scale.

The source problem has therefore moved beyond “prevent subpower concentration.” The remaining obstruction is **exceptional-scale localization**: either show that the zero-log-density bad endpoints cannot carry the downstream obstruction, strengthen logarithmic recurrence to the pointwise/source-specific occupation required there, or prove directly that the final criterion only consumes the logarithmic-positive recurrence already available.

**Boundary.** FD-079 does not prove `U_(H,D)>=c_D E_(H,D)` for every large `H`, positive natural density of horizon-level occupation, or absence of arbitrarily deep sparse losses. Its fixed `alpha>0` and integer nonsquarefree dilation are load-bearing. The durable gain is that physical-energy weighting now has a fixed positive logarithmic-mean occupation, not merely a zero-power-loss exponent.
