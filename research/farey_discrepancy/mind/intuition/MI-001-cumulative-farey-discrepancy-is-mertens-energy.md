# MI-001 — Farey transfer is governed by source coherence, reciprocal capture, and destination normalization

**Evidence level:** supported by the exact source/occupation reductions through FD-070 plus the cited short-interval Möbius input; no normalized RH obstruction at an adaptively selected zero-frontier spike is claimed.

The cumulative Farey obstruction is controlled by the physical shell source. Earlier results separate deterministic transfer, short-interval cancellation, host placement, packet multiplicity, horizon normalization, and Schur-head leverage. FD-066 identifies the direct coherent-packet threshold: a frontier spike coherent on a sufficiently long source window can beat the terminal mechanism, while shorter coherence cannot.

FD-067--FD-068 add a different multiplicative recurrence statement: very low normalized nonsquarefree occupation cannot dominate logarithmic scale, but a sparse adaptive spike sequence can still evade a density theorem. FD-069 then removes host selection as the missing pointwise map. A source spike of height `A_X` is detected by a nonsquarefree reciprocal row on every sufficiently late admissible horizon once `T` reaches order `X^2/A_X`.

FD-070 shows that the physical one-Lipschitz law automatically promotes that row into a growing packet. A spike of height `A_X` produces a source interval of width comparable with `A_X`, which becomes about `T A_X/X^2` reciprocal rows. After the packet has genuinely grown, its forced nonsquarefree energy is of order `T A_X^3/X^2`.

This is a strong quantifier improvement but not a normalized breakthrough. On a zero-frontier spike, the generic same-horizon denominator grows fast enough that the best scalar lower bound from the cubic packet occurs at the **earliest** packet onset. Waiting longer only weakens the power bound. The resulting exponent crosses the terminal exponent at exactly `Theta=2/3`, so persistent packet growth reproduces rather than removes the old direct-packet boundary.

The decisive currency is therefore the ratio between captured source energy and the denominator generated on the **same horizon**. Source coherence, recurrence, and reciprocal sampling already guarantee a substantial numerator. A successful next theorem must prevent `E_(T,D)` from diluting that packet at the zero-frontier scale, or replace the scalar occupation ratio by a target whose normalization preserves the forced source mass.

**Boundary.** FD-070 is an exact packet lower bound plus a negative normalization result for this mechanism. It does not prove a lower bound for normalized occupation, prescribe a density of frontier spikes, or establish a zero-free region.
