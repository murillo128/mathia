# MI-001 — Farey transfer is governed by causal protection plus the abundance and energy geometry of frontier-sized source values

**Evidence level:** supported by the exact source/occupation reductions through FD-078 and the persisted Pintz inputs; no positive natural/logarithmic density, fixed positive occupation, positive limiting energy ratio, or RH criterion is claimed.

FD-071 shows why arbitrary-later persistence does not improve normalization: a later source spike can dominate the same-horizon denominator and dilute an earlier episode. FD-072 gives the complementary positive mechanism. At a `sigma`-record `X`, `sigma>1/2`, the causal choice `T=4X`, `D=3` confines every reciprocal quotient to the record-controlled prefix while row `4` samples the record exactly, forcing fixed positive nonsquarefree occupation. FD-073--FD-076 protect and globalize such record episodes.

FD-077 changes the abundance currency. The inverse floor-harmonic identity transfers Pintz's average absolute order into logarithmic `L^1` source mass of exponent `Theta` in every fixed power annulus. Comparing this mass with the pointwise envelope forces frontier-near physical-source values on a set of polynomial counting exponent one. A fixed nonsquarefree row transports a full-counting-exponent subfamily to causal horizons whose occupation and normalized Schur defect lose at most an arbitrarily small fixed power.

FD-078 then tests whether the **physical energy itself** could hide on a much thinner set of badly occupied horizons. Hölder lifts the annular source `L^1` exponent to `p Theta`; at `p=2`, one fixed nonsquarefree row injects that moment into the nonsquarefree Schur energy. The logarithmically aggregated total energy `E`, nonsquarefree energy `U`, and Schur residual `rho^2` therefore all have exponent `2 Theta` in every fixed power annulus.

Consequently the energy-weighted ratios `U/E` and `rho^2/E` may still tend to zero, but only subpolynomially: for every fixed `eta>0` they are eventually at least `X^(-eta)` at the annular aggregate level. This closes polynomial energy dilution even after horizons are weighted adversarially by their actual physical energy.

The remaining gap is now sharper. The record mechanism gives fixed occupation but may be sparse; the average-order mechanism gives near-maximal counting abundance; the energy-moment mechanism says even physical-energy weighting cannot lose a fixed power. A global conclusion still needs a **nonvanishing aggregate occupation**, a source-specific no-concentration theorem ruling out the residual subpower loss, or another exact bridge from these annular moments to the downstream criterion. Positive density is sufficient-looking but is no longer the only conceivable currency.

**Boundary.** FD-078 does not prove positive density, a fixed pointwise lower bound for `U/E`, or a positive limiting aggregate ratio. Equal power exponents do not imply comparable constants. At `Theta=1/2` the result also gives no supercritical polynomial separation. Its durable contribution is that both counting abundance and energy-weighted occupation have now been pushed to the zero-power-loss boundary.
