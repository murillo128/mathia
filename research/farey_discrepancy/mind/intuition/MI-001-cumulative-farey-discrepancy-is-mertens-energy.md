# MI-001 — The square-root free-model boundary survives every sublinear Möbius ancestry prefix

**Evidence level:** exact sparse-horizon saturation, rough-core reduction, and linear-prefix Schur-complement geometry through FD-031

## Core intuition

The GCD-duality/Farey route now has a much sharper arithmetic boundary. Exact squarefree support, ternary values, and Möbius ancestry along not only sparse sets but every unbounded sublinear initial prime prefix can still coexist with square-root free-model saturation.

The first structurally different regime is linear prime breadth. There the remaining discrepancy is no longer an unspecified sign freedom: it becomes an exact angular defect of the physical Mertens tail against a distinguished Schur-complement direction.

## Strongest justified principle

FD-024--FD-026 establish the square-root saturation threshold and preserve it under exact Möbius support/value constraints. FD-027--FD-029 progressively impose prime-direction ancestry and identify a rough-core parametrization after the constrained prime factors are removed.

FD-030 proves the decisive breadth statement: for every `Y_H=o(H)`, even with `Y_H` arbitrarily close to linear on a sublinear scale, enough rough-core freedom remains to attain the same GCD-duality saturation. FD-031 then analyzes `Y` at linear scale and writes the relaxation loss as an exact Mertens-tail angle term. The physical source fails to saturate precisely to the extent that its remaining tail stays away from the distinguished extremizing direction; when the prefix reaches the endpoint-access regime, this geometry becomes still more constrained.

## Program consequence

Stop searching for a merely growing ancestry threshold below linear scale. Attack the linear-prefix angle directly: prove a scale-uniform lower bound on the Mertens-tail angle, an equivalent Schur-complement deficit, or an endpoint-reachability obstruction that the true Möbius source must satisfy. This is the first current condition that the sublinear free controls cannot reproduce by construction.

## Counterevidence / boundary

FD-031 does not yet prove a positive angular gap for the actual Möbius tail. A linear ancestry prefix could in principle align closely with the distinguished Schur direction, and the exact size of the first coercive linear fraction is not determined. The results classify the free boundary; they do not themselves improve Mertens cancellation.

## Epistemic status

**Exact negative boundary for all sublinear prime-prefix ancestry, together with an exact linear-scale Mertens-tail angular discriminator; proving that the true source stays quantitatively away from the extremizing direction remains open.**

## Falsification criterion

Construct a sublinear-prefix ancestry model that violates FD-030's saturation conclusion under the same assumptions, or show that the actual linear-prefix Mertens tail can approach the FD-031 extremizing Schur direction closely enough to erase every uniform angular defect.
