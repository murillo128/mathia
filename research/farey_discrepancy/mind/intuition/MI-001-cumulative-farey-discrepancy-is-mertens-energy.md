# MI-001 — Farey frontier blocks are projectively coherent, while small common anchors live in vanishing terminal energy

**Evidence level:** supported by the exact physical occupation reductions through FD-084, the exact matched geometric control FD-085, the divisor-supported transport inequality FD-086, and the exact dilation/terminal-sector bounds FD-087. No theorem yet composes separated frontier blocks into the finite-entropy multiplicative transport needed by the current accumulation route.

FD-071--FD-084 show that every sufficiently late fixed-power future window contains a constant-strength occupied block of length `H^(Theta-delta)`. FD-085 proves that abundance and even linear additive blocks do not by themselves imply a bounded-ratio integer chain: a synthetic set can satisfy stronger coarse geometry while every infinite integer-ratio chain has superexponential logarithmic growth.

FD-086 identifies source information missing from that control. In the common Jordan Hilbert space, moving the horizon by `h` changes only divisor-supported rows, and against the near-frontier energy this displacement is negligible throughout the full occupied block. The normalized physical Jordan vectors, Schur equality ray and transverse defect are therefore projectively coherent within each good block.

FD-087 then tests the most natural exact inter-block anchor. Integer dilation is not ill-conditioned: every state at horizon `H` appears as an exact coordinate copy inside the state at `qH`, with conditioning bounded uniformly by `sqrt(zeta(2))`, regardless of `q`. A huge least common multiple therefore does not itself destroy the lower-horizon Jordan state.

The obstruction is instead **where additive block thickness can place the anchor**. A block of length `L_H=H^(Theta-delta)` can force shared divisors only on that scale, and every exact dilation copy coming from any anchor `a<=L_H` is contained in one terminal quotient sector. FD-087 proves that the normalized energy of the entire sector tends to zero on the near-frontier blocks. Taking the union of all such small anchors does not recover bulk frontier mass.

The remaining inter-block theorem must therefore do something qualitatively different: produce anchors substantially larger than the additive block length, use a signed/bulk observable outside the terminal quotient sector, obtain a destination-energy saving that changes the normalization, or construct a multihorizon composition law that does not depend on small common divisors.

**Boundary.** FD-087 does not say exact dilation copies are information-destroying; they are uniformly recoverable on their range. It says the anchors forced by current additive thickness occupy asymptotically negligible normalized frontier energy. Relative direction between separated blocks and any viable large-anchor/bulk composition remain open.
