# MI-001 — Farey frontier blocks are projectively coherent in Jordan space, but inter-block composition remains open

**Evidence level:** supported by the exact physical occupation reductions through FD-084, the exact matched geometric control FD-085, and the exact divisor-supported transport inequality FD-086. No theorem yet composes separated frontier blocks into the finite-entropy multiplicative transport needed by the current accumulation route.

FD-071--FD-084 show that every sufficiently late fixed-power future window contains a constant-strength occupied block of length `H^(Theta-delta)`. FD-085 proves that abundance and even linear additive blocks do not by themselves imply a bounded-ratio integer chain: a synthetic set can satisfy stronger coarse geometry while every infinite integer-ratio chain has superexponential logarithmic growth.

FD-086 identifies the source information missing from that control. In the common Jordan Hilbert space, moving the horizon by `h` changes only rows whose divisors occur in `(H,H+h]`, giving `||V_(H+h,D)-V_(H,D)||_2^2 << h^2+h(H+h)^gamma`. Against the near-frontier energy of FD-084, this displacement is negligible throughout the whole occupied block. The normalized physical Jordan vectors therefore converge to one projective direction across each block; the Schur equality ray and transverse defect are coherent there as well.

So additive thickness and additive **source transport** are now different from multiplicative composability. The local problem is solved more strongly than set occupation suggested: each good block behaves like one signed Jordan packet. The remaining theorem must relate packets at separated scales or find a multihorizon accumulation functional that composes them directly.

**Boundary.** FD-086 gives no control on the relative direction or placement of different frontier blocks. A sequence of internally coherent blocks may still be superexponentially separated or rotate between blocks, so the existing multiplicative-chain endpoint does not follow.
