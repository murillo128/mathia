# MI-001 — Farey occupation is additively thick, but coarse set geometry cannot supply the required finite-entropy multiplicative transport

**Evidence level:** supported by the exact physical occupation reductions through FD-084 and the exact matched geometric control FD-085. Full counting exponent and polynomial-length consecutive additive blocks are proved for the physical occupation set. No compatible multiplicative chain, additive transport inequality or RH criterion is proved.

FD-071--FD-084 build a strict ladder from protected records and physical-energy weighting through annular recurrence, full-counting occupation and finally consecutive occupied blocks. Every sufficiently late fixed-power future window contains a constant-strength occupied block of length `H^(Theta-delta)` for every fixed `delta>0`. The obstruction is therefore not a shortage of good horizons.

FD-085 identifies what this geometry still does not buy. Its logarithmically accelerating band set has full counting exponent in every fixed-power future window and even linear consecutive blocks, yet every bounded integer dilation eventually misses the set. Consequently every infinite integer-ratio chain inside the control has ratios tending to infinity and superexponential logarithmic growth per retained scale.

This is the exact matched-control boundary for the current accumulation route. **No theorem using only power-window abundance and additive block length can manufacture the finite-logarithmic-entropy integer-ratio chain required by FD-022--FD-023.** Additive thickness and multiplicative composability are genuinely different resources even at maximal coarse counting strength.

The next theorem must therefore retain source-specific information that FD-085 deliberately throws away: the quotient-shell/Jordan-energy increment identities, a restriction on the relative placement of successive physical blocks, or a transport estimate that accumulates gain directly under additive/nonlocal horizon motion. The alternative is to replace integer-ratio chaining by a destination mechanism naturally adapted to the additive blocks already proved.

**Boundary.** FD-085 is a synthetic control, not a model of the actual Farey/Mertens occupied set. It does not prove that the physical source realizes the superexponential escape. It proves only that excluding that escape requires arithmetic or transport structure beyond the coarse set-theoretic conclusions of FD-083--FD-084.
