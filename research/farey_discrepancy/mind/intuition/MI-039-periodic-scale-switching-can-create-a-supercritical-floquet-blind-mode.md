# MI-039 — Switched blindness has macroscopic signed directions, but positivity is the load-bearing physical constraint

**Evidence level:** exact/literature-backed synthesis from FD-212--[FD-231](../../findings/FD-231-signed-linear-capacity-profiles-can-be-uniformly-switched-invisible.md), interpreted against the source-identification boundary of FD-224.

The periodic schedule `q_j=(0,3,3)` has a physical super-square-root Floquet blind mode on non-exhaustive source windows, while horizon-relative exact Euler compatibility eventually identifies a permanent source. FD-225 then removes the earlier switched-matrix conditioning: the complete divisor-reservoir response has an exact integer inverse. FD-226 removes one power of prime-resolution loss with scale-adapted exact floor cells.

Those representation repairs expose capacity as the real physical constraint. FD-227 shows that smooth positive power-law occupancy creates a visible Jordan-totient main mode. FD-228--FD-230 strengthen this from one smooth ansatz to a positivity theorem: periodic band oscillation, bounded bandwise cushions and arbitrary nonnegative within-band redistribution cannot preserve a nonvanishing fraction of natural linear capacity while every required switched row stays subquadratic.

FD-231 proves that the conclusion changes immediately when signs are allowed. A greedy totient construction gives integers `b_n` with `|b_n|<=n`, macroscopic positive and negative mass on every late dyadic band, and divisor response `|A b(m)|<=m` for every integer `m`. Hence every fixed switched root row is only first order although the signed profile has quadratic aggregate capacity.

The reusable distinction is now **source coverage -> exact algebraic inversion -> prime resolution -> signed blind space -> one-sided source realizability**. Positivity is not a cosmetic assumption used to simplify a coercive proof; it is exactly what turns immediately preceding capacity into a detectable mass term. Once signs are admitted, natural norm coercivity fails badly.

The remaining blind-route question is therefore not to find another oscillatory signed null vector. Such vectors exist. It is to realize the signed correction demanded by the physical Mertens baseline inside one nonnegative prime-reservoir occupancy without adding an offset whose own switched response restores the obstruction. Equivalently, a negative theorem should lower-bound the one-sided slack cost of every such realization.

**Boundary.** FD-231 produces a signed divisor-cell direction and can be interpreted as the difference of two feasible positive profiles after scaling; it is not itself one physical prime-flip occupancy and is not a new Farey or Möbius countermodel. FD-230 remains the relevant positive coercivity theorem. None of these results proves or refutes RH.
