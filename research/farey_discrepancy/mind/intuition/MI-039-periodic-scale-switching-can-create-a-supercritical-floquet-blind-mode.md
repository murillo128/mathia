# MI-039 — The fixed-profile switched quotient kills the block-Mertens repair; the live positivity problem is triangular

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from FD-230--[FD-235](../../findings/FD-235-fixed-horizon-block-mertens-repair-collapses-inside-the-switched-blind-quotient.md). No Mertens or RH conclusion is claimed.

FD-232--FD-233 identify the one-sided coefficient criterion inside any genuinely nontrivial switched-response class: a switched-preserving nonnegative lift exists exactly when the negative aggregate capacity of a signed representative is asymptotically negligible. FD-234 then removes nearest-integer bookkeeping from the source-forced correction by writing the canonical real representative as `tilde c_N=-(1/2)(1*U_N)`, with `U_N(d)=M(dN)-M((d-1)N)`, and showing that rounding contributes only a capacity-negligible switched-blind perturbation.

FD-235 corrects the quantifier order of the resulting affine problem. For every fixed `N`, the canonical block-Mertens repair itself belongs to the persisted switched-blind space: `R_j(tilde c_N)=-(1/2)(E-I)^{r_j}M(2^jN)=O_N(2^j)=o(4^j)`. Hence `tilde c_N+N_sw=N_sw`, the zero profile is an exact representative, and the fixed-`N` negative-capacity infimum is identically zero. The old question of optimizing the fixed profile modulo `N_sw` is therefore vacuous for this repair.

The growing reservoir construction has the opposite quantifier order. It uses only rows `0<=j<J(N)` with `2^{J(N)} asymp D_N=N^{o(1)}`, long before the fixed-`N` estimate becomes small merely because `j` has outrun `log N`. A meaningful quotient must therefore be a normalized triangular family coupling `N`, the available depth `J(N)` and the source-side capacity scale. A condition of the form `max_{j<J(N)} |R_j(h_N/L_N)|/4^j -> 0` illustrates the required uniformity, but FD-235 does not determine the canonical normalization `L_N`.

The reusable point is the order-of-quantifiers gate: a null space defined after freezing the source parameter can erase the very family that a growing-scale obstruction is supposed to measure. The coefficient-level positive-lift theorem remains exact, but it becomes informative for the block-Mertens repair only after the blind class is defined on the same coupled family and normalization as the reservoir construction.

**Boundary.** FD-235 does not construct the triangular quotient, prove a positive lower bound in it, or produce an admissible prime occupancy. It only proves that the fixed-profile affine formulation cannot supply the missing obstruction. The next durable step needs a source-derived family normalization and a uniform blind condition on the rows actually used; only then is one-sided negative-capacity distance a nontrivial arithmetic question.
