# MI-039 — Every fixed switched block remains blind until literal Euler-prefix coverage; only growing coherence can differ

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [FD-212](../../findings/FD-212-period-three-diagonal-root-depth-creates-a-source-realizable-supercritical-floquet-blind-ray.md) through [FD-221](../../findings/FD-221-every-fixed-switched-block-remains-blind-until-literal-euler-prefix-coverage.md), interpreted against the fixed-family dichotomy of FD-210--FD-211.

The periodic depth schedule `q_j=(0,3,3)` has a physical super-square-root Floquet blind mode even though every instantaneous annihilator has only the harmless root `1`. FD-212--FD-220 progressively show that exact Möbius Euler laws, bounded scheduled observations and a macroscopic earlier-prefix defect can coexist for one persistent comparison source across one, two, three and then four dyadic horizons. The apparent `1/2,1/4,1/8` pattern is source-overlap geometry: the first rigid point is when the latest Euler cutoff literally reaches every prime that can affect the original prefix.

FD-221 proves that this is not an accident of the first few blocks. For every **fixed** horizon count `J` and every fixed `theta<2^{-(J-1)}`, one squarefree `{-1,0,1}` source can satisfy all exact Euler laws required at the horizons `N,2N,...,2^{J-1}N`, keep every scheduled switched observation `O_J(1)`, and still have `X_N(N) asymp_{J,theta} N/log N`. At `theta>=2^{-(J-1)}`, the final Euler window covers all primes up to `N` and the earlier prefix is forced to be Möbius.

The uniform finite-block mechanism is a **positive null cycle plus triangular repair**. Equal reservoir occupancy creates an exact constant response at all sampled dyadic points; every positive-order difference filter annihilates that response. Remaining observations are then corrected by reservoir pivots with triangular positive geometry. The finite observation panel can therefore grow arbitrarily large *before the source is fixed*, without becoming coercive on the earlier prefix.

The reusable distinction is now sharper than a generic overlap audit. **Every fixed finite block is a local quantifier. Cofinal source identity is a different quantifier.** A proof that hopes to recover Möbius cancellation from the switched observations must use a constraint that couples an unbounded sequence of horizons, or obtain estimates uniform enough in a growing `J=J(N)` that the finite reservoir construction loses capacity before literal prefix coverage.

This also explains why adding another fixed filter, another bounded-depth period, or another finite collection of exact Euler laws is not automatically progress. Such additions may increase the finite linear system while leaving the same positive null-cycle mechanism intact. The relevant question is whether the source must satisfy the whole growing family simultaneously with constants/capacity that no longer scale freely with `J`.

**Boundary.** FD-221 is nonuniform in `J`. It does not construct one fixed source serving infinitely many horizons, does not handle an arbitrary growing `J(N)`, and does not prove that approximate Euler relations have the same sharp coverage threshold. The comparison sources are not counterexamples to Möbius cancellation or RH. The exact conclusion is only that no fixed finite switched block of the audited periodic schedule can force the earlier prefix before literal Euler-prefix coverage.
