# MI-039 — Periodic scale switching creates a supercritical blind mode until cross-horizon prime coverage reaches the earlier prefix

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from [FD-212](../../findings/FD-212-period-three-diagonal-root-depth-creates-a-source-realizable-supercritical-floquet-blind-ray.md) through [FD-218](../../findings/FD-218-two-horizon-fractional-euler-coherence-has-a-sharp-half-scale-prefix-rigidity-threshold.md), interpreted against the fixed-family dichotomy of FD-210--FD-211.

Let `Delta_A(n)=A(2n)-A(n)` and observe one dyadic root filter at each scale. FD-212 takes the periodic depth schedule `q_j=(0,3,3)`. Each instantaneous annihilator has only the harmless root `1`, yet the ordered period-three transfer has multiplier `lambda=-(7+3sqrt(5))/2=-phi^4`, giving a physical super-square-root Floquet mode at bounded filter depth. FD-213--FD-216 lift the mode to all integers and show that squarefree ternary amplitudes plus growing families of exact Möbius Euler sign laws can coexist with blindness.

FD-217 shows that one isolated horizon remains extremely permissive: for every fixed `0<theta<1`, a target-dependent source can obey the exact Euler law for every prime `p<=theta N`, keep the switched observation bounded at `N`, and still have `X_N(N) asymp_theta N/log N`. Full coverage `theta=1` instead identifies Möbius on the target prefix.

FD-218 turns the vague persistence requirement into a geometric overlap threshold. One **same deterministic source** can simultaneously serve horizons `N` and `2N`, satisfy all exact Euler laws through `p<=theta H` at each horizon, and retain a macroscopic summatory defect at the earlier horizon for every fixed `theta<1/2`. At `theta>=1/2`, the later cutoff reaches every prime `p<=N`; that is enough to force the earlier squarefree prefix to equal Möbius. The first-crossing scale is therefore not “two horizons” by itself but whether the later horizon's exact-prime window covers the earlier prefix.

The reusable mechanism is an **overlap audit**. Relation count, local cutoff fraction and persistence are distinct currencies. A persistent source can still remain blind while successive constraint windows leave an unconstrained reservoir; rigidity appears when cross-horizon coverage actually closes that reservoir on the prefix being tested.

The live question is now how this first-crossing principle composes for more general horizon ratios, multiple horizons, shrinking cutoffs or observables whose windows overlap without literal prime-prefix coverage. A useful theorem must quantify that overlap rather than merely increase the number of prime relations at each isolated scale.

**Boundary.** FD-218 treats the explicit two-horizon `N,2N` construction and its fractional cutoff. It does not classify arbitrary horizon schedules or prove a Möbius theorem from approximate relations. The source constructions are comparison objects, not counterexamples to Möbius cancellation or RH.
