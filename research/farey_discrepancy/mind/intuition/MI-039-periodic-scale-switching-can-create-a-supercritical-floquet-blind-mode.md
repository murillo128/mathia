# MI-039 — Positive liftability is one-sided sparsity in the switched-blind affine class, and rounding is quotient-negligible

**Evidence level:** exact synthesis from FD-230--[FD-234](../../findings/FD-234-nearest-integer-repair-is-a-capacity-negligible-switched-blind-perturbation-of-block-mertens-inversion.md). No Mertens or RH conclusion is claimed.

FD-232 proved the necessary one-sided obstruction: if a signed representative has a fixed positive fraction of negative aggregate capacity on late dyadic blocks, every nonnegative lift requires comparable slack and cannot preserve a subquadratic switched response. FD-233 proved the converse. For any signed linear-envelope profile `b`, a switched-preserving positive lift exists iff `sum_{d in B_k} b_d^- / C_k -> 0`; the pointwise-minimal lift `a=b^+` already preserves the required switched scale.

FD-234 identifies a canonical representative for the source-forced correction. If `U_N(d)=M(dN)-M((d-1)N)`, then the exact real inversion is `tilde c_N(n)=-(1/2) sum_{d|n} U_N(d)=-(1/2)(1*U_N)(n)`. The rounded FD-225 correction satisfies `c_N=tilde c_N+r_N`, where `|r_N(n)|<=tau(n)`, every relevant switched row obeys `|R_j(r_N)|<=8`, and `sum_{n in B_k}|r_N(n)|/C_k = O(k/2^k)`. Thus `r_N` is both switched-blind at the target scale and asymptotically negligible in the one-sided capacity metric. The rounded and real corrections lie in the same FD-233 affine class for the positivity question.

The reusable object is therefore explicit: the affine class of `-(1/2)(1*U_N)` modulo legitimate switched-blind directions, measured by late-band negative-capacity density. Nearest-integer bookkeeping cannot decide the obstruction. A positive lower bound on the best density is an intrinsic coefficient-level obstruction; one representative with vanishing density removes that obstruction automatically.

**Boundary.** FD-234 does not determine which side of this affine criterion the block-Mertens correction occupies. Even a successful coefficient lift does not construct an admissible prime occupancy: integer multiplicities, finite reservoir capacities and exact source constraints remain separate gates.
