# MI-006 — Generic coherent shell structure does not beat the terminal harmonic fan

**Evidence level:** exact countermodel boundary from [FD-107](../../findings/FD-107-coherent-one-lipschitz-terminal-fans-still-saturate-the-harmonic-endpoint.md), refining the terminal-fan endpoint of FD-106. The countermodel is synthetic and does not satisfy the physical Möbius/Jordan increment law.

FD-106 leaves one scale-dependent loss at critical excess: the repaired dyadic-terminal prime fan with `C_p/H~1/p`. A plausible escape was that its matched harmonic packet allocation was too relaxed because the actual packets come from one common quotient-shell source rather than from independent masses.

FD-107 closes that generic-coherence escape. It constructs one fixed nonnegative one-Lipschitz source `F`, with sublinear envelope `F(n)<<n^(3/4)`, and horizons `H_j` for which the parent mass is polynomially high, `U^F_(H_j)=H_j^(6/5+o(1))`, almost all current mass lies in the exact terminal rows `4p`, every selected source coordinate lies far above the adaptive cutoff, and yet

`sup_p (U^F_(C_p)/C_p)/(U^F_(H_j)/H_j) -> 0`.

The construction realizes the harmonic `1/p` packet profile inside one coherent source and controls the **complete** repaired child state, not merely the transported packet. Common source values, bounded increments, sublinear growth, high mass and the exact least-prime-factor partition therefore do not supply the missing endpoint gain.

What the synthetic source deliberately lacks is the physical coefficient law

`Delta H(n)=mu(rad n) phi(rad n)/n`.

The live distinction is thus no longer independent packets versus coherent packets; it is **generic shell coherence versus arithmetic coefficient coherence**. To remove the `epsilon` loss through the terminal fan, one must derive a quantitative consequence of the actual increment law, or another source restriction not implied by one-Lipschitz coherent shell geometry, that forbids or depletes the harmonic allocation. The alternative remains to make repeated source descents/head events pay enough of the selection tax globally.

**Boundary.** FD-107 is a synthetic obstruction, not a model of the physical source. It proves only that the named generic regularity properties cannot by themselves remove the endpoint loss; it does not show that the physical coefficient law fails to do so.
