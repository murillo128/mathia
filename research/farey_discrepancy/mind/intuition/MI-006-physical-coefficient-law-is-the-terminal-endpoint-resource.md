# MI-006 — First-order physical coefficient coherence is still power-critical at the terminal harmonic fan

**Evidence level:** exact countermodel boundary from [FD-107](../../findings/FD-107-coherent-one-lipschitz-terminal-fans-still-saturate-the-harmonic-endpoint.md), sharpened by the exact physical prime-divisibility covariance and large-sieve budget in [FD-108](../../findings/FD-108-physical-prime-divisibility-large-sieve-is-power-critical-at-the-terminal-fan-scale.md). No endpoint gain or RH consequence is claimed.

FD-106 leaves one scale-dependent loss at critical excess: the repaired dyadic-terminal prime fan with `C_p/H~1/p`. FD-107 closes the generic-coherence escape by constructing one fixed nonnegative one-Lipschitz sublinear source whose complete repaired children realize the harmonic matched allocation at polynomial high mass. Common source provenance, bounded increments and sublinear growth are therefore insufficient.

FD-108 restores the actual physical increment law

`Delta H(n)=mu(rad n) phi(rad n)/n`

and extracts a genuine arithmetic consequence: reciprocal-prime samples satisfy an exact prime-divisibility covariance and a weighted additive large-sieve estimate. This is information the synthetic FD-107 source does not have.

The crucial point is quantitative rather than qualitative. At the terminal source-rich scale, the harmonic endpoint profile consumes only `o(X^2)` of an `X^2` large-sieve budget. The first physical `L^2` constraint is therefore compatible with the same power-critical allocation it was meant to exclude. Introducing arithmetic structure is not enough; it must be arithmetic structure whose budget is *strictly smaller than the endpoint profile it controls*.

The live distinction is now **first-order prime covariance versus higher-order multiplicative coherence**. To remove the `epsilon` loss directly, one needs a consequence of the physical source that couples several prime divisibility events, their signs, or the least-prime-factor tree strongly enough to forbid the harmonic allocation. The alternative remains to make repeated source descents/head events pay the selection tax globally.

**Boundary.** FD-108 does not show that the physical source actually saturates the large-sieve bound; it shows that this information class cannot by itself rule out the terminal allocation at the required power scale. Higher-order incidence, signed correlations or a different source identity remain open and may be stronger.
