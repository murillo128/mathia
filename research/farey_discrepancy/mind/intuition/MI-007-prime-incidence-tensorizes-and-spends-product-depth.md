# MI-007 — Raw higher-order prime incidence is flat and spends a product-depth source budget

**Evidence level:** exact route restriction from [FD-109](../../findings/FD-109-physical-prime-cubes-have-zero-plucker-curvature-and-divisibility-incidence-tensorizes.md), on top of the first-order physical endpoint boundary in [FD-108](../../findings/FD-108-physical-prime-divisibility-large-sieve-is-power-critical-at-the-terminal-fan-scale.md). No endpoint gain or RH consequence is claimed.

The first natural response to FD-108 is to replace one-prime divisibility by two-, three- or higher-prime incidence. FD-109 shows that the raw physical coefficient law does not create a new local interaction this way. For distinct prime directions, the coefficient cube factorizes pointwise as a decomposable tensor, so every two-prime Plücker minor vanishes exactly.

Summation creates structure, but of a very specific kind. The simultaneous-divisibility projection for a squarefree product `q` is a commuting product of the one-prime dilation resolvents, and finite differences recover one source value at the deeper scale `X/q`. Thus higher incidence adds **product depth**, not an independent same-scale coupling between the terminal samples `H(X/p)`.

The adaptive source cutoff makes that distinction quantitative. A `k`-prime projection remains source-rich only while the product of its prime labels satisfies `q lesssim P_*(X)`. The admissible labels therefore live in a logarithmic simplex, not a Cartesian product of the full terminal prime fan. More labels spend the same source budget faster.

The reusable lesson is that “higher order” must be defined by the information it creates, not by the number of indices. Linear simultaneous-divisibility projections remain inside the same flat commuting algebra. A genuinely stronger endpoint resource must break that reduction through nonlinear products before summation, least-prime-factor order, cross-horizon correlations, signed couplings, or refinement vectors whose exterior minors do not vanish identically.

**Boundary.** FD-109 does not prove that product-depth samples are useless when combined nonlinearly, nor that every higher-order arithmetic statistic tensorizes. It closes only the raw coefficient cube and its linear cumulative divisibility hierarchy.
