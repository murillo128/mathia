## Determine whether power-syndetic pointwise occupation is already enough for the Farey/RH criterion

**Linked intuition:** `MI-001-cumulative-farey-discrepancy-is-mertens-energy`.

FD-072--FD-076 give constant-strength protection around source-selected records. FD-077 supplies near-maximal counting abundance. FD-078 proves that physical-energy weighting creates no fixed-power loss; FD-079 gives fixed positive logarithmic-mean annular occupation; and FD-080 upgrades this to subpower first passage for annular occupation along exact nonsquarefree dilation successors.

FD-081 now converts that annular currency to an actual physical horizon. The annular ratio `Omega_(D,alpha)(Y)` is exactly a positive energy-weighted average of the pointwise ratios `nu_(H,D)=U_(H,D)/E_(H,D)`. By choosing the annulus lower edge just beyond an arbitrary `X` and applying FD-080, for every fixed `eta<c_q` and every `epsilon>0`, all sufficiently large `X` admit

`X < H <= X^(1+epsilon)` with `nu_(H,D)>eta`.

Equivalently the first good pointwise horizon satisfies `log sigma_eta(X)/log X -> 1`. For `q=4`, every `eta<3/64` is unconditional and, under RH, every `eta<3/16` is available.

The live gap is therefore no longer annular-to-pointwise transfer. It is whether the downstream Farey/RH obstruction can consume this **power-syndetic pointwise** sequence, or whether the still-allowed `X^(o(1))` bad gaps can carry all dangerous near-saturation behavior. A stronger theorem would need either stability of the criterion under subpower enlargement, quantitative control of the residual bad gaps, or eventual pointwise occupation.
