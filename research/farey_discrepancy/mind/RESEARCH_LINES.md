# Farey-discrepancy research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Farey-discrepancy intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Quantify physical descent as a function of growing predecessor depth

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-cumulative-farey-discrepancy-is-mertens-energy`, `MI-002-odd-prime-square-reembedding-localizes-recursive-failure-to-dyadic-channel`, `MI-003-normalized-record-defect-is-the-composition-loss`.

FD-094--FD-096 close one-step representation, the source-head branch and the existence of infinitely many sharp occupied bounded-defect entry states under false RH. FD-097 then removes the apparent superexponential multistep loss: if `eta` is an occupation lower bound and `K` a backward-record-defect upper bound, the projective quality `q=eta/K` loses only a fixed branch factor because both quantities inherit the same destination ratio `F_sigma(H)/F_sigma(C)`.

Thus an `m`-step chain has `q_m>=q_0 b_sigma^m` for fixed `b_sigma>0`, and a diagonal sequence of entry records gives depths tending to infinity with only subpower occupation/defect loss at every visited scale. The old question “can the constants survive beyond fixed depth?” is no longer the right boundary.

The live theorem is effective **physical descent versus depth**. Relate the number and types of predecessor steps to the actual decrease of the horizon, control the accumulated asymptotic transport errors, and show that a sufficiently long admissible chain reaches a scale incompatible with the assumed false-RH frontier. The all-head model is the negative control: current projective inequalities alone permit only `O(H^Theta)` guaranteed additive progress from scale `H`.

## Find a scale-sensitive invariant beyond projective certificate quality

The projective certificate deliberately quotients the common normalized-energy factor and therefore controls composition quality rather than absolute descent. A final invariant may need to combine `q`, horizon loss, branch type and transport error in one monotone quantity, or prove recurrent returns to source records that reset the absolute scale ledger.

A useful strengthening must beat the all-head control and produce an effective depth-to-scale law. Improving the already-exponential projective loss without changing physical progress is not enough.

## Keep entry existence, projective composition and physical descent distinct

Source-record slowdown supplies the entry family; FD-094--FD-095 supply exact one-step closure; FD-097 supplies the correct projective multistep accounting. None of these by itself gives the rate at which a growing-depth chain reaches small physical scales. Future synthesis should not reopen bounded-defect entry or independent occupation/defect bookkeeping after those gates have been closed.
