# Farey-discrepancy research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable farey-discrepancy intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Prevent physical hyperbola-sampling amplification with source-specific energy regularity or coercivity

**Linked intuition:** `MI-001-cumulative-farey-discrepancy-is-mertens-energy`.

FD-024--FD-040 show that terminal nonsquarefree occupation is universally positive and every fixed finite supercritical Mellin packet is coercively sampled, while generic bounded-increment shell profiles can still evade by concentrating energy on sparse low rows. The unresolved regime was therefore already source-specific rather than a density problem.

FD-041 sharpens the scalar side. For the untruncated Jordan energy, squarefree versus nonsquarefree occupation is an exact powerful-number dilation sieve of the **single total-energy curve**. There is no independent overlap datum to recover, and any dominated multiplicative scaling law with index above `1/2` forces a positive nonsquarefree fraction. A physical vanishing-occupation mechanism must therefore make the scalar energy multiplicatively irregular as well as spectrally complicated.

FD-042--FD-043 sharpen the spectral side at the critical exponent. Every fixed finite critical Mellin packet has the universal occupation ratio `delta_ns`, and centering by that density cancels the critical pole before Fourier analysis. For growing finite packets the occupation error is controlled by coefficient conditioning and frequency diameter with no inverse minimum-gap singularity. Merely adding many or close frequencies is not an escape.

The live theorem is to show that the physical quotient-shell source cannot simultaneously exhibit the multiplicative energy irregularity and critical packet ill-conditioning/remainder size needed to evade these controls. A viable proof should exploit the exact coefficient law or derive a uniform coercivity/approximation statement for the growing effective spectral complexity of the physical source. Another generic Lipschitz, density, or fixed-packet estimate is already below the frontier.

## Treat terminal density, scalar scaling, and finite/growing packet coercivity as matched controls

Terminal Jordan blocks, regular multiplicative total-energy growth, fixed critical packets, and sufficiently well-conditioned growing critical packets all force positive nonsquarefree occupation. Generic shell regularity alone does not. Any proposed physical escape or proof must identify which source-specific mechanism defeats or enforces these controls rather than infer pointwise rigidity from one of them in isolation.
