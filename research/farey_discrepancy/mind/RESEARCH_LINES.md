## Localize quadratic transfer on the sparse reciprocal-floor host set

**Linked intuition:** `MI-001-cumulative-farey-discrepancy-is-mertens-energy`.

FD-049--FD-054 reduce any residual occupation collapse to a low-row problem and give the first positive additive transfer from a squarefree host to a nearby nonsquarefree quadratic twin.

FD-055 shows that the physical Möbius source is smoother than the bare one-Lipschitz bound: all-short-interval cancellation penetrates the host threshold by every fixed logarithmic factor and closes the zero-frontier power endpoint whenever `Theta>0.55`.

The unresolved near-critical band is now a localization problem rather than an interval-length problem. FD-056 shows that even an almost-all theorem needs a near-unit exceptional-set exponent if one pretends every point in the additive corridor is selectable. FD-057 restores the exact reciprocal-floor host geometry: the realizable starts have cardinality only `X/R`, so exceptional-set cardinality alone must beat the inverse-length scale `c=1`.

The live theorem must therefore prove structured nonconcentration/cancellation **on the reciprocal-floor realizable starts themselves**, or replace the almost-all route by another source-coupled mechanism. A generic density-zero or power-saving exceptional set over all interval starts is not enough.

## Treat signed multiplicative cancellation, additive localization, and quadratic physical occupation as different currencies

A complete Jordan ray may be almost perfectly signed-cancelled while one nearby nonsquarefree row carries comparable quadratic energy. Conversely, strong almost-all cancellation over arbitrary starts may miss the sparse starts generated by admissible hosts. Future arguments should be formulated in the target quadratic statistic and preserve the exact reciprocal-floor placement law.
