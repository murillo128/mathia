# Farey-discrepancy research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Farey-discrepancy intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Locate a nonreconstructive overlap regime where multi-horizon coherence remains coercive

**Linked intuition:** `MI-001-cumulative-farey-discrepancy-is-mertens-energy`.

FD-014--FD-015 show that nearby multiplicatively related horizons are not independent: squarefree divisibility collisions force strict two-horizon gaps from simultaneous GCD-duality saturation, with repeated-prime collisions giving the strongest current elementary deficit. But FD-016--FD-017 show the opposite boundary. Sufficiently separated multiplicative horizons can plant their equality-ray prefixes independently, and along supermultiplicatively sparse schedules one unrestricted common source can asymptotically saturate every horizon.

The live regime is therefore between two failures: dense hierarchical sampling risks reconstructing the source, while overly sparse horizons are interpolable and noncoercive. Seek a growing family of horizons with persistent overlap/collision structure strong enough to accumulate a quantitative common-source gap, yet weak enough not to amount to source reconstruction.

## Treat isolated two-horizon gaps and supermultiplicative schedules as controls

A strict finite GCD-duality gap is not enough if it decays too quickly with breadth. Conversely, adding more horizons is not automatically stronger when their fresh coordinate blocks decouple. Any proposed multi-horizon theorem must state the overlap geometry and show that its common gap survives at the normalization needed by the cumulative Farey/Mertens target.
