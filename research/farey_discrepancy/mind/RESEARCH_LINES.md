## Turn frontier-normalized predecessor transport into recursively eligible recurrence

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-cumulative-farey-discrepancy-is-mertens-energy`, `MI-002-odd-prime-square-reembedding-localizes-recursive-failure-to-dyadic-channel`.

FD-072--FD-084 push the physical occupation geometry from recurrence to full counting exponent and then to consecutive occupied blocks of length `H^(Theta-delta)` inside every sufficiently late fixed-power future window. FD-085 shows that this set geometry alone cannot supply the finite-entropy multiplicative chain required by the existing GCD-duality accumulation mechanism.

FD-086 restores source information before scalarization. The complete physical Jordan vector `V_(H,D)` has divisor-supported horizon increments, making every horizon in the whole `H^(Theta-delta)` block asymptotically collinear in normalized Jordan space; the normalized Schur equality direction and transverse defect are coherent on the same block.

FD-087 resolves the first exact common-multiple idea. Integer dilation embeds `V_(H,D)` inside `V_(qH,D)` with uniform condition number at most `sqrt(zeta(2))`, but every exact copy arising from an anchor `a<=L_H` lies in a terminal quotient sector carrying only `o(1)` normalized frontier energy. Additive block thickness alone therefore cannot transport the bulk packet through one small common divisor.

FD-088 finds the missing large-anchor carrier. On a false-RH near-frontier block, the occupied nonsquarefree core can be stitched at rowwise next multiples inside the same block with only `o(sqrt(E_H))` distortion. Repeated-prime deflation preserves the Jordan weight exactly and represents a fixed positive share of the `D=1` frontier energy by exact lower anchors satisfying uniformly `a_r >> sqrt(H L_H) >> L_H`.

FD-089 shows that the row adaptivity is not intrinsically high entropy. For every fixed small `alpha>0`, essentially all of that packet is carried by canonical prime contractions `A_p=ceil(H/p)` with `p<=H^alpha`. Its unweighted pigeonhole still loses the number of predecessors, so diagonalizing `alpha->0` was used there to recover the full frontier exponent at an `H^(1-o(1))` lower scale.

FD-090 removes that predecessor-count loss at the natural metric. Weight each exact lower mask by `A_p^(-2 sigma)`. Because `A_p/H~1/p` and `sum_p p^(-2 sigma)<infinity` for `sigma>1/2`, the whole predecessor family has bounded effective mass after frontier normalization. Under false RH, taking `sigma=Theta` gives one canonical predecessor whose masked and total lower-state energies both have the full `2Theta` frontier exponent for every fixed admissible `alpha`; no `H^-alpha` counting penalty remains.

FD-091 then localizes the remaining eligibility defect. In every odd repeated-prime channel, strip the second copy of `p` and reembed `s=pt` as `4t` at `C_p=4 floor(A_p/p)~4H/p^2`. The floor quotient is preserved exactly, the Jordan weight changes only by a universal `zeta(2)` factor, and the new mask is entirely divisible by `4`, hence nonsquarefree. For `p>=3` this is a genuine descent with `C_p<=(4/9+o(1))H`; for `p=2` the same universal square reembedding gives `H+O(1)` and loses all multiplicative descent. The odd normalized family now carries weight `p^(-4 sigma)`, summable already for `sigma>1/4`, so the critical `sigma=1/2` normalization has no predecessor-entropy loss.

The remaining obstruction is therefore sharply split. On the odd branch, the reembedded nonsquarefree mask has the full false-RH frontier exponent but is not yet known to occupy a fixed fraction of the lower state. On the dyadic branch, universal nonsquarefree reembedding is scale-critical. A live theorem must upgrade the odd full-exponent mask to fixed-strength recursive eligibility, compose normalized carriers without that fixed raw fraction, or extract a source-compatible contradiction from the dyadic terminal channel.

## Keep set occupation, within-block coherence, exact embedding, normalized capacity, predecessor entropy and recursive eligibility separate

The established hierarchy is now `recurrence -> full-counting occupation -> polynomial additive blocks -> source-specific projective coherence -> uniformly conditioned exact dilation -> positive-mass row-adaptive anchor packet -> canonical prime-dilation predecessors -> fixed-strength frontier-normalized predecessor transport -> odd-channel nonsquarefree reembedding with a dyadic scale-critical exception`. None of these by itself proves an indefinitely iterable fixed-strength descent.

FD-085 proves that coarse set geometry cannot imply multiplicative composability; FD-086 proves local vector coherence; FD-087 proves both that dilation itself is well conditioned and that small-anchor capacity vanishes; FD-088 proves that row-adaptive repeated-prime deflation recovers positive bulk mass at anchors much larger than the block length; FD-089 compresses those anchors to a subpower family; FD-090 proves that the family cardinality causes no additional asymptotic loss once the physical scale change is normalized correctly; FD-091 proves that every odd channel can restore nonsquarefree eligibility at a lower scale and isolates `p=2` as the channel where that universal repair consumes the whole contraction. The next theorem must control **fixed mask fraction/composition in the odd branch or the dyadic terminal carrier**, not merely existence, conditioning, predecessor count, frontier exponent or nonsquarefree support.
