## Separate source-side rigidity from the exact observation and recovery geometry

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-cumulative-farey-discrepancy-is-mertens-energy` through `MI-039-periodic-scale-switching-can-create-a-supercritical-floquet-blind-mode`.

FD-187--FD-206 separate source information, observation geometry and destination completeness. Prime-extension energy is a genuine Möbius relation currency; raw dyadic observation has a sharp geometric recovery threshold; adjacent-prime differences and lowbit-tree coordinates improve overlap and inverse geometry. But FD-206 shows that a favorable positive representation can still contain a standalone RH-complete scalar anchor.

FD-207--FD-211 then classify fixed signed repairs. Every fixed depth and every common sublogarithmic depth controlled on square-root windows remains destination-complete at subpower inverse cost. For a fixed finite polynomial-filter family, a common characteristic root in `sqrt(2)<|rho|<=2` gives a bounded-source super-square-root blind mode; if the gcd has no such root, pointwise square-root-plus-epsilon control reconstructs the destination for every bounded source.

FD-212 shows that diagonal scale variation is genuinely different: the period-three depth schedule `q_j=(0,3,3)` has a supercritical Floquet multiplier although each instantaneous filter is annulus-free and the fixed family has gcd `1`. FD-213 lifts that mode from one dyadic ray to every integer by separating dyadic scale from mantissa. FD-214 then shows that exact squarefree support and exact unit amplitudes still do not remove the obstruction: one coherent ternary source can satisfy the selected all-integer filter at RH scale while its summatory function grows like `N^alpha`, `alpha≈0.9256558848>1/2`.

FD-215 strengthens the arithmetic audit from local source fidelity to multiplicative fidelity. For **every fixed finite set of primes**, the countermodel can be chosen with `xi_n^2=mu(n)^2` and the exact Möbius Euler relations `xi_(pn)=-xi_n` whenever `p` is selected and `p` does not divide `n`, while retaining the same all-integer critical switched-filter bound and super-square-root summatory growth. Finite odd-prime constraints do mix dyadic mantissas, but a fixed collection can be absorbed by a finite Euler-product Green operator before squarefree projection and rounding.

The live signed-filter question is therefore no longer whether support, amplitude, all-integer additive coherence, or a fixed amount of multiplicative coherence kills the Floquet mode. **None of them does.** The next discriminator must use genuinely global or scale-growing Möbius multiplicativity, another exact arithmetic relation whose complexity grows with the observation scale, or an observation law that mixes dyadic fibres in a way that cannot be preconditioned by a fixed finite Euler operator.

## Apply information budgets only after fixing source class, observation family and destination norm

The same representation can be complete, blind or cheaply invertible depending on the source class and destination. The current sequence separates quotient coverage, raw inverse conditioning, positive coercivity, source-overlap cost, graph resistance, scalar destination anchors, fixed-family spectral roots, nonautonomous monodromy, dyadic-mantissa profile lifting, squarefree ternary fidelity and now finite-prime Euler coherence.

FD-213--FD-215 identify the surviving representation boundary sharply. Dilation preserves the mantissa coordinate, and even finitely many exact odd-prime Möbius relations can be compensated by a finite-prime inverse before constructing the source. The useful audit is therefore to ask whether the physical source law couples the `(scale, mantissa)` fibres through an **unbounded family** of arithmetic constraints. A proof that invokes only finitely many Euler factors has not yet reached the part of Möbius multiplicativity that the countermodel cannot absorb.

The dependence on the selected finite prime set is essential: FD-215 is not uniform as that set grows. The global infinite-prime limit is exactly where the finite Green-operator construction stops providing a countermodel for free, so that limit is a genuine remaining frontier rather than a cosmetic strengthening of the same relaxation.

## Keep source coercivity, coordinate inversion and the Franel--Landau destination separate

Stable source recovery and a critical random benchmark still do not imply an RH-critical discrepancy theorem. Positive coordinates can already be RH-complete, while signed nonautonomous coordinates can be exactly or critically blind on source classes that satisfy increasingly faithful finite pieces of the Möbius law.

The high-value frontier is now narrow: determine whether **global or scale-growing multiplicative compatibility** of the Möbius source forbids the FD-213 profile-lifted mode, or construct a source-independent sensor whose dynamics mixes the preserved fibres strongly enough that no finite Euler preconditioning can restore the blind mode. Another fixed filter, another bandwise depth schedule, exact squarefree amplitudes, or any fixed finite collection of prime Euler relations will not address the surviving obstruction.
