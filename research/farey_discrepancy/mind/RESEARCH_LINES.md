# Farey-discrepancy research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Farey-discrepancy intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Turn squarefree source-coherence coercivity into a pointwise endpoint gain, or leave cumulative Farey discrepancy

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-cumulative-farey-discrepancy-is-mertens-energy`, `MI-002-odd-prime-square-reembedding-localizes-recursive-failure-to-dyadic-channel`, `MI-003-normalized-record-defect-is-the-composition-loss`, `MI-004-multiplicative-horizon-descent-can-preserve-the-source-quotient`, `MI-005-source-neutral-carrier-descent-spends-a-strict-finite-row-potential`, `MI-006-physical-coefficient-law-is-the-terminal-endpoint-resource`, `MI-007-prime-incidence-tensorizes-and-spends-product-depth`, `MI-008-linear-least-prime-order-is-positive-rough-sieve-filtration`, `MI-009-finite-local-polynomial-nonlinearity-splits-by-parity`, `MI-010-fixed-rational-anchor-horizon-is-a-periodic-mobius-filter`, `MI-011-fixed-depth-physical-data-do-not-create-critical-coercivity-on-a-reversible-farey-skeleton`.

FD-102--FD-115 close the fixed local/refinement algebra: generic coherence, fixed-order prime incidence, finite prime ordering, fixed local polynomial nonlinearities, finite descendant geometry and centered discrepancy do not create a new source coordinate. FD-116 classifies fixed rational anchor histories as periodic Möbius filters.

FD-117 makes the entire cumulative discrepancy field an invertible affine image of the quotient-Mertens staircase. FD-118 then compresses its same-horizon Fourier data to the divisor-closed floor-quotient skeleton `Q_N` of size `2 sqrt(N)+O(1)`, with every other mode algebraically determined by that carrier. Enlarging the same discrepancy representation therefore does not add source information.

FD-119 identifies an exact Möbius equality ray on the skeleton and shows that any fixed true Mertens prefix plus the global identity `sum_(d<=N)M(floor(N/d))=1` can be imposed at only `O_R(1)` energy cost. FD-120 goes substantially further: even a full single-horizon increment law—zero on nonsquarefree integers and unit signs on every squarefree integer—together with `a_1=1`, the exact divisor identity and terminal size `sqrt(N)+O(1)` still admits horizon-dependent synthetic witnesses with `E_N^Q(Y_N)=Y_N(N)^2+o(N)`.

FD-121 shows that the horizon dependence of those witnesses is genuinely load-bearing. If one common source `Y` is used at `N` and `2N`, then for `N>=6`

`Delta_N(Y)+Delta_2N(Y) >= c_0 (|Y(N)|^2+|Y(2N)|^2)`,

with `Delta_H=E_H^Q-|Y(H)|^2` and `c_0=(41-sqrt(1553))/64>0`. Only the modes `2` at `N` and `2,4` at `2N` are needed. Thus two adjacent dyadic horizons cannot both track their separate equality rays at critical relative accuracy: cross-horizon source coherence itself costs `Theta(N)` total energy when the endpoints are of square-root size.

FD-122 extends that obstruction from the dyadic edge to **every fixed squarefree dilation**. Möbius inversion on the divisor-closed skeleton makes small `Delta_H` control each quotient coordinate. For squarefree `r` and a prime `p|r`, the coordinates `p` at `N` and `r,rp` at `rN` are then incompatible with simultaneous equality-ray tracking because `mu(r) ne 0` while `mu(rp)=0`. Hence for each fixed squarefree `r>1` there is `c_r>0` with

`Delta_N(Y)+Delta_rN(Y) >= c_r (|Y(N)|^2+|Y(rN)|^2)`

for all sufficiently large `N`. On any fixed finite prime cube, relative near-extremizers below one common threshold form a Sperner antichain. The squarefree/Sperner pattern was already present in the older GCD-duality geometry of FD-015; the new content is that it survives quantitatively on the current minimal Fourier skeleton.

The remaining problem is no longer to establish that coherence can matter, nor merely to stop dyadic alternation. It is to make this **squarefree network obstruction pointwise**. The present constants deteriorate as the finite prime family grows, and the repeated-prime collision does not control nonsquarefree dilation ratios. A successful coercivity theorem must close that escape, propagate the finite-network excess to a prescribed endpoint, or use genuine Möbius correlation/multiplicativity to determine which critical-amplitude endpoints pay a positive relative cost. If that cannot be done, this line must leave cumulative Farey discrepancy as its source object and introduce genuinely additional arithmetic data rather than another transform of `D_N`.

## Locate the exact boundary of the high-mass transport mechanism

The adaptive source threshold remains intrinsic: the source-rich tag grows while `U_H/H` does. Near the linear floor, neither the current source core nor its error normalization supplies a growing tag. A continuation must either keep a false-RH chain a fixed power above that floor long enough, or introduce an observable whose compression error remains negligible at near-linear mass.

FD-107--FD-122 now rule out an increasingly broad family of substitutes for that missing information and identify one resource that is quantitatively non-neutral: generic same-horizon coherence, first-order arithmetic covariance, fixed-order prime incidence, finite prime ordering, fixed local polynomial nonlinearity, finite local refinement geometry, canonical moving ancestry, centered discrepancy, fixed anchors, the full cumulative discrepancy field, redundant full-spectrum access, fixed-depth physical endpoint constraints, and even the complete single-horizon squarefree/unit-amplitude increment law plus one exact divisor identity do not suffice; exact reuse of one source across fixed squarefree dilation edges does force a uniform skeleton-energy penalty.

The open transport question is whether that squarefree-dilation penalty can be made to follow the large endpoint rather than merely one member of each pair, and whether the repeated-prime/nonsquarefree transition escape can be controlled without importing a genuinely new source coordinate.

## Keep information equivalence, proof geometry and source-correlation cost separate

FD-117--FD-118 are information/representation-classification theorems, not smallness theorems. A reversible transform can still expose a useful inequality even though it carries no new source bits. FD-119 adds that true physical constraints can be too cheap in the chosen proof geometry. FD-120 shows that the issue is not merely the number or depth of constraints: a full horizon of exact pointwise admissibility can remain cheap when the relational law between signs and between horizons is absent.

FD-121 supplies the first complementary positive control: no new source coordinate is introduced, but exact cross-horizon reuse of the existing source makes the separate same-horizon equality rays incompatible by a uniform amount. FD-122 shows that this is a squarefree-dilation network phenomenon rather than a dyadic accident and converts finite-prime near-saturation sets into antichains. Future arguments must therefore distinguish four questions: whether they add arithmetic information, whether they only reorganize the Mertens-equivalent field, whether they impose genuine source coherence, and whether that coherence forces the **designated** RH-relevant endpoint away from saturation. The last step, now with the nonsquarefree transition escape made explicit, is the active bottleneck.
