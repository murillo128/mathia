## Push Möbius sign correlation beyond every fixed finite prime set

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-cumulative-farey-discrepancy-is-mertens-energy`, `MI-002-odd-prime-square-reembedding-localizes-recursive-failure-to-dyadic-channel`, `MI-003-normalized-record-defect-is-the-composition-loss`, `MI-004-multiplicative-horizon-descent-can-preserve-the-source-quotient`, `MI-005-source-neutral-carrier-descent-spends-a-strict-finite-row-potential`, `MI-006-physical-coefficient-law-is-the-terminal-endpoint-resource`, `MI-007-prime-incidence-tensorizes-and-spends-product-depth`, `MI-008-linear-least-prime-order-is-positive-rough-sieve-filtration`, `MI-009-finite-local-polynomial-nonlinearity-splits-by-parity`, `MI-010-fixed-rational-anchor-horizon-is-a-periodic-mobius-filter`, `MI-011-fixed-depth-physical-data-do-not-create-critical-coercivity-on-a-reversible-farey-skeleton`.

FD-102--FD-120 close the fixed local/refinement and same-horizon algebra. The complete cumulative discrepancy field is an invertible affine image of the quotient-Mertens staircase; its same-horizon Fourier data compress to the divisor-closed floor-quotient skeleton `Q_N` of size `2 sqrt(N)+O(1)`. Even a full single-horizon squarefree/unit-amplitude increment law plus the exact divisor identity still admits horizon-dependent synthetic witnesses with critical near-saturation.

FD-121--FD-124 show that reusing one polynomially bounded source across horizons creates a real average cost. Fixed squarefree dilation edges force pair gaps; bounded-ratio chains have a positive Cesàro deficit; and finite average logarithmic horizon growth is incompatible with Cesàro saturation. Thus any common-source near-saturating schedule must have `log(H_n/H_0)/n->infinity`.

FD-125 proves that this remaining superexponential escape is real: on the repeated-square chain `H_(j+1)=H_j^2` there is one horizon-independent square-root-bounded source that asymptotically saturates the skeleton. FD-126 strengthens that matched control to one infinite sequence with exact squarefree support and exact unit amplitudes. FD-127 now adds genuine Möbius multiplicative ancestry in an arbitrary fixed finite set of prime directions: for every fixed finite `P`, the same source can satisfy

`a(pn)=-a(n)` for every `p in P` and `p` not dividing `n`,

while retaining `|Y(n)|<<_P sqrt(n)` and

`E_(H_j)^Q(Y)=|Y(H_j)|^2+O_P(H_j^(3/4)(log H_j)^2)`

with `|Y(H_j)|=sqrt(H_j)+O(1)`.

Therefore neither physical discreteness nor **any fixed finite prime breadth of the true multiplicative sign law** charges the repeated-square equality geometry at critical scale. The remaining arithmetic distinction must grow with scale: an expanding prime family, full coprime multiplicativity, a growing exact divisor/cross-horizon identity family, or another global correlation law whose rank cannot be absorbed by the finite triangular inverse behind FD-127.

The next theorem should test the first genuinely growing version of this input. A useful target is to quantify how large a prime set `P(H)` the FD-127 construction can still absorb while preserving the `H^(3/4)` tail budget, and to identify the threshold at which the product/density/inverse constants force a critical energy cost. Merely adding another fixed prime direction cannot advance the line.

## Locate the first growing Möbius sign law that breaks the repeated-square matched control

The adaptive source threshold remains intrinsic: the source-rich tag grows while `U_H/H` does. Near the linear floor, neither the old source core nor its error normalization supplies a growing tag. FD-107--FD-124 rule out generic same-horizon coherence, fixed-order prime incidence, finite prime ordering, local polynomial nonlinearities, nested geometry, rational anchors, full-spectrum redundancy and finite-entropy scheduling as substitutes.

FD-126 was the first fully discrete repeated-square matched control. FD-127 shows that even exact prime-by-prime Möbius ancestry is still noncoercive when the number of prescribed prime directions is fixed. The finite-prime factorization moves all remaining freedom to a positive-density `P`-free squarefree core, and the equality-prefix conditions become a finite triangular difference problem. That mechanism is exactly what any next source law must defeat.

Candidate input must therefore have **growing arithmetic rank** under the floor-quotient representation. It should couple signs across enough prime directions or horizons that the stagewise target assignment

`Y(floor(H/k)) approx Y(H) mu(k)/k`

can no longer be solved with `O(H^(3/4))` prefix mass. Full Möbius multiplicativity is certainly sufficient to determine the signs once prime values are fixed, but using it wholesale would hide the threshold. The sharper question is where the finite-prime inverse ceases to be cheap as `P=P(H)` expands.

## Keep information equivalence, average coherence cost, sparse scheduling, support/amplitude admissibility and sign-correlation rank separate

FD-117--FD-118 classify information, not smallness. FD-119--FD-120 show rich same-horizon physical-looking constraints can remain cheap. FD-121--FD-124 prove a genuine network cost whenever the horizon schedule has finite logarithmic entropy per step. FD-125 shows that superexponential scheduling evades that generic cost with a square-root common source. FD-126 shows that the escape survives the full squarefree support/unit-amplitude alphabet. FD-127 shows that it also survives every **fixed finite** set of exact Möbius prime directions.

The surviving variable is now not whether sign correlation exists, but how its arithmetic rank scales with the horizon. Future work should price that rank directly rather than repackage fixed-depth multiplicativity, support, or horizon coherence already absorbed by the matched control.
