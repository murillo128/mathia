FD-130 rules out one aggregate middle ground. FD-131 closes the immediate rank-visible repair. Taking the supremum over multiplicative ranks of the **rankwise mean** defect still does not coerce the source. The defect no longer hides between ranks; it dilutes **within every rank across prime directions**.

FD-132 closes pointwise fixed-prime control as the next repair. A single central-rank gauge agrees with Möbius through every prescribed fixed rank and differs on asymptotically half of the squarefree integers, yet for every fixed prime `p` its primewise ancestry defect tends to zero. The same witness defeats every fixed finite family of prime directions and every fixed summable weighting of the primewise defects.

The active source law must therefore be noncircular and visible at the **active scale in both multiplicative rank and prime direction**. Neither averaging over directions nor taking the quantifier “for each fixed prime” retains a defect whose prime-coordinate location migrates with the scale.
