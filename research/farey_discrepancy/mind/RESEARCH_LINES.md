# Farey-discrepancy research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Farey-discrepancy intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Find a source-visible ancestry law that cannot rotate its hidden wall into another additive coordinate

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-cumulative-farey-discrepancy-is-mertens-energy` through `MI-017-finite-rank-localization-can-miss-an-orthogonal-additive-prime-factor-coordinate`.

FD-117--FD-129 classify the reversible Farey skeleton, common-source horizon coherence, sparse multiplicative escape and the circular reconstruction boundary. FD-130--FD-136 then show that counting means, rankwise means, fixed-prime convergence and harmonic physical-scale balancing can all miss a macroscopic source defect.

FD-137 proves that the surviving FD-132 witness is not hiding across physical scales. Inside every factor-two shell its defects occupy an `o(sqrt(loglog T))` central `omega(n)` strip, so mean ancestry still vanishes over a broad growing prime family. Exact conditioning on the exceptional rank, however, exposes a maximal defect wall. This identifies the hidden coordinate of that witness rather than proving a general coercive law.

FD-138 supplies the next matched obstruction. A new common source moves the sign wall into the additive prime-color coordinate `D(n)=sum_(q|n) chi_4(q)`. Even after restricting to one factor-two shell and a fixed positive-Gaussian-mass central rank window, the mean defect tends to zero uniformly over all primes while coefficient disagreement stays positive-density. Physical scale plus one CLT-scale rank coordinate is therefore still noncoercive.

The live theorem must now use **source-native multidimensional structure** rather than another arbitrary marginal. Can the admissible Farey/Möbius source be shown to forbid orthogonal additive half-space walls, or can one identify a finite family of prime-factor coordinates whose joint localization is coercive without already reconstructing the Möbius prefix? Any proposed law must survive the FD-138 rotation test and stay below the exact rough-core reconstruction threshold of FD-135.

## Distinguish exact rank exposure from mesoscopic rank localization

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-015-local-ancestry-has-an-exact-rough-core-rigidity-threshold`, `MI-016-harmonic-scale-balancing-still-misses-the-moving-rank-interface`, `MI-017-finite-rank-localization-can-miss-an-orthogonal-additive-prime-factor-coordinate`.

FD-137 shows that exact conditioning on the moving exceptional rank kills the old witness maximally. FD-138 shows that retaining only a broad CLT-scale rank window does not: another additive coordinate can carry the transition while `omega(n)` remains typical. Thus “rank-aware” is not one resource. The width of the retained rank cell and the residual source coordinates inside it matter separately.

A useful interpolation must preserve enough local source geometry to prevent a hidden wall from having vanishing conditional mass, while leaving enough source freedom that the hypothesis is not equivalent to exact ancestry. A destination-side Fourier-skeleton coercivity theorem remains a distinct possibility because it could bypass coefficient recovery entirely; it must be tested on the repeated-square target rather than inferred from ancestry fidelity.

## Keep information equivalence, horizon entropy, scale visibility, coordinate visibility, local rigidity and target recovery separate

The current obstruction chain shows that “more arithmetic breadth” is not a scalar resource. A coercive law must specify which prime coordinates and additive factor statistics remain visible after normalization, how physical scales are weighted, how narrow the conditioning cells are, and how much source freedom remains before the condition simply reconstructs Möbius.

FD-138 adds the decisive warning: after one hidden coordinate is exposed, a matched source may move its transition into another additive direction. The next condition must therefore be justified from the actual source geometry, not chosen merely because it kills the previous counterexample.
