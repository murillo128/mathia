## Separate source-side rigidity from the exact observation and recovery geometry

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-cumulative-farey-discrepancy-is-mertens-energy` through `MI-039-periodic-scale-switching-can-create-a-supercritical-floquet-blind-mode`.

FD-187--FD-206 separate source information, observation geometry and destination completeness. Prime-extension energy is a genuine Möbius relation currency; raw dyadic observation has a sharp geometric recovery threshold; adjacent-prime differences and lowbit-tree coordinates improve overlap and inverse geometry. But FD-206 shows that a favorable positive representation can still contain a standalone RH-complete scalar anchor.

FD-207--FD-211 then classify fixed signed repairs. Every fixed depth and every common sublogarithmic depth controlled on square-root windows remains destination-complete at subpower inverse cost. For a fixed finite polynomial-filter family, a common characteristic root in `sqrt(2)<|rho|<=2` gives a bounded-source super-square-root blind mode; if the gcd has no such root, pointwise square-root-plus-epsilon control reconstructs the destination for every bounded source.

FD-212 shows that diagonal scale variation is genuinely different: the period-three depth schedule `q_j=(0,3,3)` has a supercritical Floquet multiplier although each instantaneous filter is annulus-free and the fixed family has gcd `1`. FD-213 lifts that mode from one dyadic ray to every integer by separating dyadic scale from mantissa. FD-214 then shows that exact squarefree support and exact unit amplitudes still do not remove the obstruction: one coherent ternary source can satisfy the selected all-integer filter at RH scale while its summatory function grows like `N^alpha`, `alpha≈0.9256558848>1/2`.

FD-215 strengthens the arithmetic audit from local source fidelity to multiplicative fidelity: every fixed finite set of prime Euler sign relations can be absorbed. FD-216 makes the remaining quantifier boundary quantitative. At a target horizon `N`, the first `k=floor(c log N)` exact Euler relations can still be imposed for every fixed `c<1/(2 log 2)`: a deterministic horizon-indexed squarefree ternary source has switched-filter size `N^(gamma_c+o(1))` with `gamma_c=max(alpha-1/2,c log 2)<1/2`, while its summatory value at the blind point is `N^(alpha-o(1))`. The proof exposes the complete error ledger: the Euler-product terms are `N^o(1)` for the first `Theta(log N)` primes, while deterministic prefix rounding and the finite Euler cube cost `2^k`.

The live signed-filter question is therefore no longer whether the number of enforced Euler factors merely tends to infinity. **Even logarithmically many horizon-local exact factors can remain blind.** The unresolved discriminator is compatibility across horizons on one persistent source, or another source law/observation that couples the preserved dyadic fibres in a way that cannot be rebuilt independently at each target scale.

## Apply information budgets only after fixing source class, observation family and destination norm

The same representation can be complete, blind or cheaply invertible depending on the source class and destination. The current sequence separates quotient coverage, raw inverse conditioning, positive coercivity, source-overlap cost, graph resistance, scalar destination anchors, fixed-family spectral roots, nonautonomous monodromy, dyadic-mantissa profile lifting, squarefree ternary fidelity, finite-prime Euler coherence and now horizon-growing finite Euler coherence.

FD-213--FD-216 identify the surviving representation boundary sharply. Dilation preserves the mantissa coordinate, and a finite Euler inverse can compensate selected multiplicative relations before the source is rounded. FD-216 shows that the compensation remains cheap enough for the first `Theta(log N)` primes at each horizon. Thus `|P_N|->infinity` is not itself an information threshold.

The quantifier that does change the class is **same-source global coherence**. FD-216 proves that if one fixed squarefree ternary source with `xi_1=1` obeys nested exact Euler laws whose union contains every prime, then it is exactly Möbius. The horizon-indexed countermodels therefore cannot be diagonalized into a permanent source by rhetoric: their dependence on `N` is the mathematical escape hatch.

The useful audit is now to ask which cross-scale compatibility can be read by the physical Farey observable before imposing enough exact Euler data to identify Möbius tautologically. Improving the sufficient constant `1/(2 log 2)` is secondary unless it changes that same-source boundary.

## Keep source coercivity, coordinate inversion and the Franel--Landau destination separate

Stable source recovery and a critical random benchmark still do not imply an RH-critical discrepancy theorem. Positive coordinates can already be RH-complete, while signed nonautonomous coordinates can be critically blind on source classes satisfying increasingly large finite pieces of the Möbius law.

The high-value frontier is now narrow: determine whether **global multiplicative compatibility across scales** forbids the FD-213 profile-lifted mode with a quantitative estimate usable before full source identification, or construct a source-independent sensor whose dynamics mixes the preserved fibres strongly enough that no horizon-local finite Euler preconditioning can restore blindness. Another fixed filter, exact squarefree amplitudes, any fixed finite prime set, or merely an unbounded count of independently reselected prime constraints does not address the surviving obstruction.
