This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Farey-discrepancy intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Find a quantitatively coercive reorganization of the Mertens-equivalent discrepancy field, or leave cumulative Farey discrepancy

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-cumulative-farey-discrepancy-is-mertens-energy`, `MI-002-odd-prime-square-reembedding-localizes-recursive-failure-to-dyadic-channel`, `MI-003-normalized-record-defect-is-the-composition-loss`, `MI-004-multiplicative-horizon-descent-can-preserve-the-source-quotient`, `MI-005-source-neutral-carrier-descent-spends-a-strict-finite-row-potential`, `MI-006-physical-coefficient-law-is-the-terminal-endpoint-resource`, `MI-007-prime-incidence-tensorizes-and-spends-product-depth`, `MI-008-linear-least-prime-order-is-positive-rough-sieve-filtration`, `MI-009-finite-local-polynomial-nonlinearity-splits-by-parity`, `MI-010-fixed-rational-anchor-horizon-is-a-periodic-mobius-filter`.

FD-102--FD-115 close the fixed local/refinement algebra: generic coherence, fixed-order prime incidence, finite prime ordering, fixed local polynomial nonlinearities, finite descendant geometry and centered discrepancy do not create a new source coordinate. FD-116 then classifies fixed rational anchor histories as periodic Möbius filters.

FD-117 closes the broader moving-anchor escape. For every horizon, the **entire** cumulative discrepancy field is an explicit affine image of the quotient-Mertens staircase and is invertible back to that staircase. FD-118 then shows that the first `N` modes used in that inverse are highly redundant: the divisor-closed floor-quotient frequencies `Q_N` already form a square invertible carrier of size only `2 sqrt(N)+O(1)`, and every other Fourier mode is algebraically determined by it. Hence moving endpoints, growing denominators, growing endpoint families, adaptive same-field selection, complete cross-horizon histories and extra same-horizon Fourier bandwidth remain information-equivalent to Mertens data.

The live question is no longer how to manufacture an independent source by enlarging the discrepancy representation or its spectrum. It is whether the `Q_N` skeleton, or another reversible Farey/refinement/exterior representation of the same source, exposes a **quantitatively stronger cancellation or coercivity theorem** at the Franel--Landau/RH-critical scale. A nonzero minor, extra dimension, moving selector or wider Fourier band is not evidence by itself.

If no such quantitative gain can be isolated, the research line must leave cumulative Farey discrepancy as its source object and introduce genuinely additional arithmetic data rather than another transform of `D_N`.

## Locate the exact boundary of the high-mass transport mechanism

The adaptive source threshold remains intrinsic: the source-rich tag grows while `U_H/H` does. Near the linear floor, neither the current source core nor its error normalization supplies a growing tag. A continuation must either keep a false-RH chain a fixed power above that floor long enough, or introduce an observable whose compression error remains negligible at near-linear mass.

FD-107--FD-118 now rule out an increasingly broad family of substitutes for that missing information: generic coherence, first-order arithmetic covariance, fixed-order prime incidence, finite prime ordering, fixed local polynomial nonlinearity, finite local refinement geometry, canonical moving ancestry, centered discrepancy, fixed anchors, the full cumulative discrepancy field and redundant full-spectrum access to that field.

## Keep information equivalence, proof geometry and quantitative cancellation separate

FD-117--FD-118 are information/representation-classification theorems, not smallness theorems. A reversible transform or a redundant measurement family can still expose a useful inequality even though it carries no new source bits. Future arguments must state whether they introduce genuinely new arithmetic information or merely reorganize the Mertens-equivalent field, and in the latter case identify the exact quantitative estimate that the new geometry makes accessible.
