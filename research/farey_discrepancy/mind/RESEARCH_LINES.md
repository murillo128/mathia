This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable farey-discrepancy intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Upgrade zero-power-loss energy occupation to a nonvanishing aggregate or source-specific concentration theorem

**Linked intuition:** `MI-001-cumulative-farey-discrepancy-is-mertens-energy`.

FD-072 supplies a source-selected positive mechanism: at a `sigma`-power record with `sigma>1/2`, the causal horizon `T=4X`, `D=3` confines the complete denominator to the record-controlled prefix and forces fixed positive nonsquarefree occupation and Schur defect. FD-073--FD-076 protect those episodes and make frontier records power-dense.

FD-077 no longer needs records for abundance. Pintz's average absolute order, transferred through the exact inverse floor-harmonic identity, gives logarithmic `L^1` source mass of exponent `Theta` in every fixed power annulus and forces frontier-near physical-source values on a set of full polynomial counting exponent.

FD-078 closes a stronger dilution escape. Weighted Hölder lifts the source mass to full logarithmic `L^p` exponent `p Theta`; a single fixed nonsquarefree Jordan row injects the `p=2` moment into physical Schur energy. In every fixed power annulus the total energy, nonsquarefree energy, and Schur residual all have exponent `2 Theta`. Consequently the **energy-weighted** nonsquarefree occupation and normalized projective defect may decay, but not by any fixed positive power.

The remaining theorem is now genuinely zero-power-loss. One must upgrade the subpower aggregate ratio to a nonvanishing constant or another quantitatively usable lower bound, prove that total Schur energy cannot concentrate by even a subpower factor on the residual low-occupation set, or supply a source-specific aggregation principle that converts the annular energy statement into the recurrence/occupation strength required downstream. Positive natural/logarithmic density of individual horizons remains a different possible route, not something implied by the energy moment theorem.

## Keep record protection, counting abundance, energy weighting, occupation strength and density separate

FD-072--FD-076 give constant-strength protection around source-selected records. FD-077 gives frontier-near values on a full-counting-exponent set with only arbitrarily small polynomial occupation loss. FD-078 is stronger in a different direction: after weighting horizons by their actual physical energy, aggregate occupation still loses no fixed power. None of these implies positive natural/logarithmic density, fixed pointwise occupation, or a positive limiting energy-weighted occupation ratio. Those are separate currencies.
