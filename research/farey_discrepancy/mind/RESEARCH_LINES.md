# Farey-discrepancy research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Farey-discrepancy intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Bridge positive anchor exposure to actual Möbius orientation across a genuinely informative horizon hierarchy

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-cumulative-farey-discrepancy-is-mertens-energy` through `MI-022-polylogarithmic-horizon-bundles-leave-dense-orientation-kernels`.

FD-145--FD-162 reduce finite-`L^q` binary ancestry recovery to anchored cut exposure and show that extra parents, finite auxiliary vocabularies, sparse holes, terminal-core migration and high-prime perturbations of an exhausting prime anchor do not remove the hidden-fibre cut after intrinsic divisor/principal closure. The first omitted anchor prime controls a growing squarefree closure volume.

FD-163 closes the remaining low-prime-hole geometric escape under bounded architecture resources. If anchored expansion stays uniformly positive, parent distortion and normalized leakage stay bounded, and a nontrivial auxiliary core persists, then only finitely many anchor primes can be relevant below the principal-closure cutoff. The corresponding prime-avoidance anchor therefore has a uniform positive density. A coercive bounded-resource ancestry architecture cannot make the anchor exposure vanish merely by keeping one fixed small prime outside the anchor.

FD-164 shows why that geometric closure is still not source justification. At a single Farey horizon `T`, the complete discrepancy field is exactly equivalent to the floor-quotient Mertens staircase. Within the exact squarefree-unit coefficient class one can flip paired Möbius signs inside finitely many macroscopic quotient shells, preserve every floor-quotient cumulative value and hence preserve the entire same-horizon Farey discrepancy function exactly, while changing an arbitrarily large fraction of any positive-density squarefree family.

FD-165 now makes the cross-horizon boundary quantitative. For an arbitrary horizon family `H_T` of size `J_T`, one common squarefree-unit source `a_T` can match the physical Mertens values at every floor quotient contributed by every horizon in the family while changing a fraction

`1 - beta - O_beta(J_T M_beta(T)/T) - o_beta(1)`

of the squarefree orientations, where `M_beta(T)=max_(beta T <= x <= T)|M(x)|`. Thus the exact sufficient regime for dense ambiguity is `J_T M_beta(T)=o(T)`. Davenport's unconditional log-power cancellation makes **every polylogarithmically large arbitrary horizon bundle** fall inside that regime. The FD-163 positive-density anchor therefore remains almost completely unoriented even after any prescribed polylogarithmic number of complete Farey fields is added.

The live source bridge is narrower than “use several horizons.” A successful route must exploit a denser hierarchy whose breakpoint budget is large enough to leave the FD-165 regime, one permanent source constrained coherently across an unbounded nested family of horizons, multiplicativity/divisor identities, or another specifically Möbius law. FD-009 shows the opposite endpoint: the full consecutive physical divisor-horizon hierarchy is lossless by triangular Möbius inversion. The unresolved theorem lies between these extremes and must orient the exposed face without merely disguising complete source reconstruction.

The strongest immediate question is therefore to quantify **permanent-source cross-horizon rigidity**. FD-165 uses one source common to the entire finite bundle but allows that source to depend on `T`; it does not construct one infinite non-Möbius source that survives an unbounded nested hierarchy. Determine how sparse such a hierarchy can be before common-source consistency, rather than raw horizon count, begins to destroy the dense orientation kernel.

## Apply the information-budget gate only after orientation survives the horizon kernel

FD-144 remains a later lower bound: even after anchoring and quantitative cut exposure are solved, `o(r_T)` arbitrary source-independent normalized ancestry tests can still be fooled along a subsequence. FD-164--FD-165 sharpen the ordering of obligations. Before counting source bits, first quotient out the exact kernel of the **entire declared horizon family**. Positive exposure, recipient population, principal-closure volume and even many complete discrepancy fields are not orientation information while balanced squarefree-unit perturbations remain invisible to all of them.

Audit future ancestry proposals in the order **anchor geometry and mass -> parent distortion/leakage -> intrinsic closure -> positive exposure -> horizon-family orientation kernel -> permanent-source/arithmetic coherence -> information and precision budget -> Franel--Landau destination**.

## Keep coefficient coercivity and nonlocal destination coercivity separate

The current line has a sharp architecture boundary but not an RH proof. FD-163 gives a positive-density coefficient surface under bounded resources; FD-164--FD-165 prove that one horizon and even arbitrary polylogarithmic bundles do not orient that surface. Any surviving route must add nonredundant source coherence and then still show that the resulting coefficient information controls the RH-equivalent discrepancy norm. Neither step follows from the other.
