## Find a growing-depth coercive restriction on the Mertens-equivalent Fourier skeleton, or leave cumulative Farey discrepancy

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-cumulative-farey-discrepancy-is-mertens-energy`, `MI-002-odd-prime-square-reembedding-localizes-recursive-failure-to-dyadic-channel`, `MI-003-normalized-record-defect-is-the-composition-loss`, `MI-004-multiplicative-horizon-descent-can-preserve-the-source-quotient`, `MI-005-source-neutral-carrier-descent-spends-a-strict-finite-row-potential`, `MI-006-physical-coefficient-law-is-the-terminal-endpoint-resource`, `MI-007-prime-incidence-tensorizes-and-spends-product-depth`, `MI-008-linear-least-prime-order-is-positive-rough-sieve-filtration`, `MI-009-finite-local-polynomial-nonlinearity-splits-by-parity`, `MI-010-fixed-rational-anchor-horizon-is-a-periodic-mobius-filter`, `MI-011-fixed-depth-physical-data-do-not-create-critical-coercivity-on-a-reversible-farey-skeleton`.

FD-102--FD-115 close the fixed local/refinement algebra: generic coherence, fixed-order prime incidence, finite prime ordering, fixed local polynomial nonlinearities, finite descendant geometry and centered discrepancy do not create a new source coordinate. FD-116 then classifies fixed rational anchor histories as periodic Möbius filters.

FD-117 closes the broader moving-anchor escape. For every horizon, the **entire** cumulative discrepancy field is an explicit affine image of the quotient-Mertens staircase and is invertible back to that staircase. FD-118 then shows that the first `N` modes used in that inverse are highly redundant: the divisor-closed floor-quotient frequencies `Q_N` already form a square invertible carrier of size only `2 sqrt(N)+O(1)`, and every other Fourier mode is algebraically determined by it. Hence moving endpoints, growing denominators, growing endpoint families, adaptive same-field selection, complete cross-horizon histories and extra same-horizon Fourier bandwidth remain information-equivalent to Mertens data.

FD-119 now falsifies the first fixed-depth coercivity hope on that minimal carrier. The skeleton has an exact Möbius equality ray with energy `m_N^2`. Any fixed prefix `M(1),...,M(R)` and the exact identity `sum_(d<=N) M(floor(N/d))=1` can be imposed on a formal quotient source by changing only finitely many high-index coordinates, at total normalized energy cost `O_R(1)`. For `m_N~sqrt(N)` the relative energy gap is therefore only `O_R(1/N)`.

The live question is consequently narrower than “find a useful norm on the skeleton.” A successful coercivity theorem must use a **growing-depth physical restriction** whose cost cannot be repaired at `o(N)` energy: growing ranges of the Mertens increment law, squarefree/multiplicative sign structure, growing divisor compatibility, cross-horizon coherence, or another exact arithmetic constraint with critical-scale force. If no such quantitative gain can be isolated, the research line must leave cumulative Farey discrepancy as its source object and introduce genuinely additional arithmetic data rather than another transform of `D_N`.

## Locate the exact boundary of the high-mass transport mechanism

The adaptive source threshold remains intrinsic: the source-rich tag grows while `U_H/H` does. Near the linear floor, neither the current source core nor its error normalization supplies a growing tag. A continuation must either keep a false-RH chain a fixed power above that floor long enough, or introduce an observable whose compression error remains negligible at near-linear mass.

FD-107--FD-119 now rule out an increasingly broad family of substitutes for that missing information: generic coherence, first-order arithmetic covariance, fixed-order prime incidence, finite prime ordering, fixed local polynomial nonlinearity, finite local refinement geometry, canonical moving ancestry, centered discrepancy, fixed anchors, the full cumulative discrepancy field, redundant full-spectrum access to that field, and fixed-depth physical endpoint constraints on its minimal Fourier skeleton.

## Keep information equivalence, proof geometry and critical-scale source cost separate

FD-117--FD-118 are information/representation-classification theorems, not smallness theorems. A reversible transform or a redundant measurement family can still expose a useful inequality even though it carries no new source bits. FD-119 adds the next gate: **even true physical constraints can be too cheap in the chosen proof geometry**. A fixed amount of endpoint truth costs only bounded energy on the skeleton equality ray and therefore cannot create a fixed relative gap at square-root amplitude.

Future arguments must state separately whether they introduce genuinely new arithmetic information, reorganize the same Mertens-equivalent field, and exclude the relevant extremizer at the energy scale consumed by the RH endpoint. A quantitative gain requires the third property even when the first two are already understood.
