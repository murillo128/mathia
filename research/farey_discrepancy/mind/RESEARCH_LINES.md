## Separate source-side rigidity from the exact observation and recovery geometry

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-cumulative-farey-discrepancy-is-mertens-energy` through `MI-039-periodic-scale-switching-can-create-a-supercritical-floquet-blind-mode`.

FD-187--FD-206 separate source information, observation geometry and destination completeness. Prime-extension energy is a genuine Möbius relation currency; raw dyadic observation has a sharp geometric recovery threshold; adjacent-prime differences and lowbit-tree coordinates improve overlap and inverse geometry. But FD-206 shows that a favorable positive representation can still contain a standalone RH-complete scalar anchor.

FD-207--FD-211 then classify fixed signed repairs. Every fixed depth and every common sublogarithmic depth controlled on square-root windows remains destination-complete at subpower inverse cost. For a fixed finite polynomial-filter family, a common characteristic root in `sqrt(2)<|rho|<=2` gives a bounded-source super-square-root blind mode; if the gcd has no such root, pointwise square-root-plus-epsilon control reconstructs the destination for every bounded source.

FD-212 shows that diagonal scale variation is genuinely different: the period-three depth schedule `q_j=(0,3,3)` has a supercritical Floquet multiplier although each instantaneous filter is annulus-free and the fixed family has gcd `1`. FD-213 removes the remaining additive all-integer coherence escape. Dyadic dilation preserves the band mantissa, so the scale-index Floquet mode can be multiplied by a Lipschitz profile vanishing at dyadic endpoints. The endpoint zeros stitch adjacent bands with uniformly bounded increments, while the switched recurrence remains exact on **every integer**. Hence there is a bounded source with

`C_(q(n))^A(n)=0` for every `n>=1`

but `|A(N_m)| asymp N_m^alpha` along explicit interior band points, with `alpha≈0.9256558848>1/2`.

The live signed-filter question is therefore no longer cross-ray additive coherence. **Bounded increments plus all-integer diagonal observation are insufficient.** The next discriminator must use source structure absent from the FD-213 countermodel: squarefree support, multiplicativity, another exact arithmetic relation among dyadic mantissas, or an observation law that actually mixes those mantissas instead of acting fibrewise under dilation. Any proposed Möbius criterion should identify precisely which such property kills the profile-lifted Floquet mode.

## Apply information budgets only after fixing source class, observation family and destination norm

The same representation can be complete, blind or cheaply invertible depending on the source class and destination. The current sequence separates quotient coverage, raw inverse conditioning, positive coercivity, source-overlap cost, graph resistance, scalar destination anchors, fixed-family spectral roots, nonautonomous monodromy and now dyadic-mantissa profile lifting.

FD-213 is the decisive new boundary: passing from one dyadic ray to all integers does not by itself add the missing information because dilation preserves the mantissa coordinate exactly. The useful audit is therefore to write the switched inverse in `(scale index, mantissa)` coordinates and ask whether the physical source law couples those coordinates. If it does not, all-integer sampling can still be only a fibrewise replication of the same blind mode.

## Keep source coercivity, coordinate inversion and the Franel--Landau destination separate

Stable source recovery and a critical random benchmark still do not imply an RH-critical discrepancy theorem. Positive coordinates can already be RH-complete, while signed nonautonomous coordinates can be exactly blind on a broad bounded-source class.

The high-value frontier is now narrower: determine whether the additional arithmetic constraints of the Möbius source forbid the FD-213 profile-lifted mode, or construct a source-independent sensor whose dilation dynamics mixes the preserved mantissa fibres strongly enough that its nullspace cannot contain such a mode. Another fixed filter, another common depth schedule, or an argument based only on bounded increments and all-integer sampling will not address the surviving obstruction.
