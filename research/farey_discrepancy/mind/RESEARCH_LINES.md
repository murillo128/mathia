## Lift the finite carrier-inventory charge to adaptive energy packets

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-cumulative-farey-discrepancy-is-mertens-energy`, `MI-002-odd-prime-square-reembedding-localizes-recursive-failure-to-dyadic-channel`, `MI-003-normalized-record-defect-is-the-composition-loss`, `MI-004-multiplicative-horizon-descent-can-preserve-the-source-quotient`, `MI-005-source-neutral-carrier-descent-spends-a-strict-finite-row-potential`.

FD-094--FD-098 close one-step representation, projective composition loss and the strongest current uniform short-interval regularity gain. FD-099 then gives the decisive matched obstruction: for every fixed depth, false-RH source records support genuine multiplicative predecessor chains whose physical horizon contracts while the sampled quotient `H/r` remains unchanged.

FD-100 shows that this source-neutral inventory is finite on an exact carrier: every non-head repair sends the nonsquarefree row to `r'<=r/2`, so `I(r)=log_2(r/4)` drains by at least one until the four-adic head is reached.

FD-101 removes the exact-divisibility escape. For an arbitrary tagged nonsquarefree coordinate, the first canonical compression changes the physical source quotient from `x=floor(H/r)` to only `x` or `x+1`. If the `+1` case occurs, the child is automatically an exact carrier; from then on FD-100 freezes the quotient. Consequently an entire tagged non-head genealogy changes source shell at most once, reaches the head after the same finite row-potential charge, and ends with source `x_0` or `x_0+1`. Floor/ceiling errors cannot accumulate into genuine source descent.

The live theorem is therefore an **adaptive packet/retagging theorem**. The predecessor-energy selection may abandon one tagged genealogy and reselect a different row whose source quotient differs. Either show that enough mass remains genealogically coherent for the finite inventory charge to bite, or assign a quantitative cost to carrier switching that is incompatible with preserving frontier-sharp occupation/defect quality indefinitely. Once a lineage reaches the head, genuinely source-moving steps still have to accumulate.

## Find a scale-sensitive invariant that couples packet quality, carrier inventory and retagging

The projective certificate controls composition quality but intentionally quotients out a common normalized-energy factor. FD-099 shows that this can hide source-neutral contraction; FD-100 shows that the hidden row resource is finite on exact carriers; FD-101 shows that ordinary rounding cannot replenish it along a tagged physical genealogy.

A decisive invariant should therefore carry three currencies: projective quality, remaining source-neutral row potential, and enough carrier identity to charge a switch of genealogy. A retag that resets the row potential should have to pay through changed source scale, lost packet quality, or another explicitly controlled quantity. Merely improving the branch factor, counting contractive branch types, bounding per-step rounding, or proving physical horizon descent cannot distinguish repeated factorization transport from genuine movement through the source hierarchy.
