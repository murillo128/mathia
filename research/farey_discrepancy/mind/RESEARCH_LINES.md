# Farey-discrepancy research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Farey-discrepancy intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history.

## Turn bounded-scale Cesàro coercivity into a pointwise endpoint gain, or leave cumulative Farey discrepancy

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-cumulative-farey-discrepancy-is-mertens-energy`, `MI-002-odd-prime-square-reembedding-localizes-recursive-failure-to-dyadic-channel`, `MI-003-normalized-record-defect-is-the-composition-loss`, `MI-004-multiplicative-horizon-descent-can-preserve-the-source-quotient`, `MI-005-source-neutral-carrier-descent-spends-a-strict-finite-row-potential`, `MI-006-physical-coefficient-law-is-the-terminal-endpoint-resource`, `MI-007-prime-incidence-tensorizes-and-spends-product-depth`, `MI-008-linear-least-prime-order-is-positive-rough-sieve-filtration`, `MI-009-finite-local-polynomial-nonlinearity-splits-by-parity`, `MI-010-fixed-rational-anchor-horizon-is-a-periodic-mobius-filter`, `MI-011-fixed-depth-physical-data-do-not-create-critical-coercivity-on-a-reversible-farey-skeleton`.

FD-102--FD-116 close the fixed local/refinement algebra: generic coherence, fixed-order prime incidence, finite prime ordering, fixed local polynomial nonlinearities, finite descendant geometry and centered discrepancy do not create a new source coordinate. FD-116 classifies fixed rational anchor histories as periodic Möbius filters.

FD-117 makes the entire cumulative discrepancy field an invertible affine image of the quotient-Mertens staircase. FD-118 then compresses its same-horizon Fourier data to the divisor-closed floor-quotient skeleton `Q_N` of size `2 sqrt(N)+O(1)`, with every other mode algebraically determined by that carrier. Enlarging the same discrepancy representation therefore does not add source information.

FD-119 identifies an exact Möbius equality ray on the skeleton and shows that any fixed true Mertens prefix plus the global identity `sum_(d<=N)M(floor(N/d))=1` can be imposed at only `O_R(1)` energy cost. FD-120 goes substantially further: even a full single-horizon increment law—zero on nonsquarefree integers and unit signs on every squarefree integer—together with `a_1=1`, the exact divisor identity and terminal size `sqrt(N)+O(1)` still admits horizon-dependent synthetic witnesses with `E_N^Q(Y_N)=Y_N(N)^2+o(N)`.

FD-121 shows that horizon dependence is load-bearing: one common source cannot track the separate equality rays at `N` and `2N` with critical relative accuracy. FD-122 extends that obstruction to every fixed squarefree dilation and makes sufficiently cheap horizons inside every fixed finite prime cube form a Sperner antichain. The squarefree/Sperner pattern was already present in the older GCD-duality geometry of FD-015; the current content is that the obstruction survives quantitatively on the minimal Fourier skeleton.

FD-123 closes the simplest repeated-prime escape **in bounded-scale Cesàro mean**. For any integer-ratio chain `H_j=q_jH_(j-1)` with `2<=q_j<=Q` and any common source satisfying `|Y(n)|<=Cn^alpha`, the saturation ratios `S_H=|Y(H)|^2/E_H^Q(Y)` obey

`liminf_(n->infinity) (1/(n+1)) sum_(j=0)^n (1-S_(H_j)) >= Gamma_(alpha,Q) > 0`.

Squarefree transitions contribute the FD-122 pair gap. At a nonsquarefree transition, the exact quotient coordinate `H_j/H_i` has Möbius value zero, so high saturation at the later horizon forces multiplicative growth of every earlier endpoint; too many such stages contradict the polynomial envelope. For the physical source `Y=M`, the trivial bound `|M(n)|<=n` gives the theorem unconditionally with `alpha=1`.

The remaining problem is therefore sharper than “make coherence matter.” Bounded integer-ratio schedules already carry a fixed positive average deficit. The unresolved RH-relevant step is **pointwise globalization**: force the designated large endpoint to pay a positive relative cost, obtain control stable as the ratio ceiling/dilation family grows, or introduce a genuinely additional arithmetic law that excludes sparse near-saturation and unbounded-ratio escape. If that cannot be done, this line must leave cumulative Farey discrepancy as its source object rather than apply another transform to the same reversible carrier.

## Locate the exact boundary of the high-mass transport mechanism

The adaptive source threshold remains intrinsic: the source-rich tag grows while `U_H/H` does. Near the linear floor, neither the current source core nor its error normalization supplies a growing tag. A continuation must either keep a false-RH chain a fixed power above that floor long enough, or introduce an observable whose compression error remains negligible at near-linear mass.

FD-107--FD-123 now rule out an increasingly broad family of substitutes for that missing information. Generic same-horizon coherence, first-order arithmetic covariance, fixed-order prime incidence, finite prime ordering, fixed local polynomial nonlinearity, finite local refinement geometry, canonical moving ancestry, centered discrepancy, fixed anchors, the full cumulative discrepancy field, redundant full-spectrum access, fixed-depth physical endpoint constraints, and even the complete single-horizon squarefree/unit-amplitude increment law plus one exact divisor identity do not suffice. Exact reuse of one source across fixed squarefree dilation edges forces a uniform penalty, and polynomial source growth upgrades the squarefree/nonsquarefree alternative to a positive Cesàro deficit on every bounded integer-ratio chain.

The open transport question is whether that average bounded-scale penalty can be made to follow the **prescribed** RH-relevant endpoint. Sparse exceptional horizons, constants deteriorating with the scale-ratio family, and genuinely unbounded/supermultiplicative scale jumps remain outside FD-123. Any source theorem closing the line must act on those surviving degrees of freedom rather than reprove average bounded-scale incompatibility.

## Keep information equivalence, proof geometry, average coherence cost and pointwise source control separate

FD-117--FD-118 are information/representation-classification theorems, not smallness theorems. A reversible transform can still expose a useful inequality even though it carries no new source bits. FD-119--FD-120 show that very rich same-horizon physical constraints can remain cheap in the chosen proof geometry.

FD-121--FD-122 supply the complementary network phenomenon: exact cross-horizon reuse of one source makes distinct same-horizon equality rays incompatible. FD-123 then shows that, under only polynomial source growth, the squarefree pair gaps and nonsquarefree long-range memory together prevent Cesàro saturation on every bounded-ratio chain. This is stronger than a finite-network obstruction but still weaker than pointwise endpoint coercivity.

Future arguments must therefore distinguish four questions: whether they add arithmetic information, whether they only reorganize the Mertens-equivalent field, whether they impose genuinely nonredundant source coherence, and whether the resulting coherence controls the **designated** endpoint rather than merely an average or one member of each incompatible family.
