## Prevent physical hyperbola-sampling amplification with arithmetic source structure

**Linked intuition:** `MI-001-cumulative-farey-discrepancy-is-mertens-energy`.

FD-024--FD-034 show that sublinear ancestry can coexist with square-root GCD-duality saturation while linear ancestry exposes a projective equality ray and square-divisor Jordan modes force a positive normalized defect on average along every complete `4`-adic chain. FD-035--FD-037 show that multiplicative renormalization and bounded increments alone do not upgrade this to a universal pointwise gap.

FD-038 sharpens the positive side: every sufficiently long terminal quotient range has a universal positive nonsquarefree Jordan occupation fraction, independently of the shell profile. But FD-039 proves the remaining escape is real for generic sources. A one-Lipschitz shell profile with divergent critical energy can build sparse low-row spikes whose hyperbola samples overwhelm that universal terminal bulk and make the nonsquarefree occupation ratio tend to zero along selected horizons.

FD-040 excludes a large class of such escapes. Nonsquarefree rows form a coercive frame for every finite Mellin-frequency packet at fixed exponent `beta>1/2`, so no finite dominant supercritical spectral packet can realize the FD-039 concentration mechanism. The unresolved regime is therefore genuinely source-specific: infinite or non-separated Mellin complexity, growing packet dimension with collapsing frame constants, or the exact multiplicative/divisibility constraints encoded by `Delta mathcal H(n)=mu(rad n) phi(rad n)/n`.

The live theorem is to prove that the physical quotient-shell source cannot amplify sparse low-row hyperbola samples enough to dominate the universal terminal bulk. A viable proof should exploit the exact coefficient law or obtain a uniform frame/coercivity theorem for the growing spectral complexity actually admitted by the physical source. Another generic Lipschitz or density estimate is already ruled out by FD-039.

## Treat terminal occupation, generic shell regularity, and finite spectral packets as matched controls

Terminal Jordan blocks have universal positive nonsquarefree occupation, but generic bounded-increment sources can still evade globally by concentrating on exceptional low rows. Conversely every fixed finite supercritical Mellin packet is already coerced by the nonsquarefree rows. Any proposed physical escape or proof must therefore explain what happens in the gap between these controls rather than infer pointwise rigidity from either terminal density or finite spectral structure alone.
