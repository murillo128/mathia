## Beat the terminal harmonic fan or make source descent pay the remaining selection tax

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-cumulative-farey-discrepancy-is-mertens-energy`, `MI-002-odd-prime-square-reembedding-localizes-recursive-failure-to-dyadic-channel`, `MI-003-normalized-record-defect-is-the-composition-loss`, `MI-004-multiplicative-horizon-descent-can-preserve-the-source-quotient`, `MI-005-source-neutral-carrier-descent-spends-a-strict-finite-row-potential`.

FD-102 gives the sharp carrier-free source-neutral potential

`A(H,x)=log_2((H-1)/(8x-1))`,

and FD-103 shows that actual false-RH source-record ladders saturate its logarithmic coefficient. Deterministic floor geometry therefore cannot shorten the source-neutral window.

FD-104--FD-105 then remove the old survival loopholes above the linear nonsquarefree-mass floor: direct single-state compression transports a fixed-power normalized mass, the adaptive cutoff `L_H=floor(U_H/(H log H))` keeps the selected source quotient large, and a guaranteed logarithmic predecessor chain cannot collapse to bounded physical or source scale.

FD-106 now resolves most of the missing branch-sensitive amortization at the critical excess scale `R_H=U_H/H`. For every fixed `epsilon>0`, a high-mass state has a source-tagged predecessor satisfying

`R_C >= a_epsilon (C/H)^epsilon R_H`.

The `epsilon` loss is needed only in the repaired dyadic-terminal nonhead family, where `C_p/H~1/p`. Odd repeated-square branches with `C_p/H~4/p^2`, the dyadic half-scale branch and source-head retreat are already **critical-lossless**: they admit `epsilon=0`. Moreover the exact FD-102 potential charges a source-neutral contraction by the full logarithm `log(H/C)`, while critical excess mass pays only `epsilon log(H/C)`. Horizon contraction is therefore no longer an unidentified independent loss.

The remaining endpoint is the terminal prime fan. Its harmonic contraction law makes `sum_p C_p/H` diverge, and FD-106 gives a matched nonnegative packet allocation showing that total packet mass plus `C_p~H/p` cannot yield a uniform `epsilon=0` theorem. Any improvement must use the information discarded by that relaxation: the least-prime-factor partition of the complete odd-squarefree terminal prefix and the common quotient-shell source coherence. The alternative is to combine the `epsilon`-amortized law with actual source descents/head events strongly enough that the fixed packet-selection factor `a_epsilon^m` cannot survive a full false-RH chain.

## Locate the exact boundary of the high-mass mechanism

The adaptive source threshold is intrinsic: `L_H` tends to infinity exactly when `U_H/H` does. At the linear floor `U_H=H^(1+o(1))`, neither the source-rich core nor the current error normalization gives a growing source tag. A continuation that reaches this boundary must either prove the false-RH chain stays a fixed power above the floor long enough, or introduce a different observable whose compression error is still negligible at near-linear mass.

FD-106 does not remove this boundary. It shows instead that within the fixed-power high-mass regime the only scale-dependent critical transport loss comes from the terminal harmonic fan; projective quality, generic horizon contraction and the other predecessor families are no longer the limiting currencies.

## Keep deterministic budget, transport mass, fan entropy and source progress distinct

Source records provide recurrent entry states; FD-102 supplies an index-free deterministic charge; FD-103 proves that charge sharp; FD-104 transports actual normalized mass; FD-105 transports an adaptive source tag; and FD-106 couples the exact contraction currency to critical excess transport while localizing the unresolved entropy to `C_p~H/p`. None of these alone proves that the terminal least-prime/source coherence beats the harmonic matched allocation or that source descent occurs often enough to kill the repeated selection tax. Future arguments should preserve those currencies jointly instead of replacing them by another worst-branch scalar constant.
