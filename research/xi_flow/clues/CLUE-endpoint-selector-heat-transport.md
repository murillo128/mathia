# Can the endpoint Xi selector be transported through the heat-flow interval needed for an upper bound on Lambda?

## Observation

XF-048 supplies an unconditional `t=0` selector: the prime-free Fourier gap in the explicit formula excludes the coherent critical memory wave, including a localized insertion, so the earlier local-counting obstruction is no longer the missing source input. The resolved parent clue states the remaining gap explicitly: the endpoint exclusion does not yet control arbitrary broadband or compensating forcing, nor transport the filtered coefficient through the real-simple heat-flow interval needed by an upper-bound argument.

## Research question

Can the same source-faithful filtered zero statistic, or a rigorously related quantity, be propagated over the legitimate real-simple time interval with enough quantitative stability to rule out the forcing/cancellation scenarios required by a hypothetical `Lambda>0`?

## Why it may matter

This is the bridge from a genuine Xi-specific endpoint discriminator to the direction of implication that RH needs. Without transport, the endpoint theorem remains a local exclusion at `t=0`; with it, the selector could constrain the collision scenario underlying a positive transition time.

## Decisive test

Fix the XF-048 filter, normalization, source buffer, and admissible time interval before estimating. Derive its evolution from valid zero-motion or heat-flow identities only where the relevant zeros are known real and simple. Bound near-buffer exchange, compensating phases, broadband forcing, and localization errors in the same norm used by the endpoint selector.

Either prove a quantitative transport inequality that preserves a contradiction-sized margin for every admissible forcing scenario, or construct a source-compatible real-simple evolution that satisfies the endpoint constraint while rebuilding the adverse local statistic before the required time. An artificial periodic continuation, a single coherent wave, or an argument that assumes real-rootedness beyond the legitimate regime is not decisive.

## Evidence boundary

XF-048 establishes the endpoint phase-sensitive exclusion only at `t=0`; it does not prove dynamic transport or an upper bound on `Lambda`. The accepted taper/discriminant clue controls a different localization mechanism and does not by itself propagate the explicit-formula selector. No broadband exclusion or RH consequence is asserted here.
