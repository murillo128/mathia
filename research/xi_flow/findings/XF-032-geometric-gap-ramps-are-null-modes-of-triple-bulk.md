# XF-032 — geometric gap ramps are exact null modes of the triple-discriminant bulk

**Status:** `EXACT-DERIVED` + `NEGATIVE/OBSTRUCTION` + `STRUCTURAL/BOUNDARY`. XF-031 rewrites the finite-gap triple-discriminant route in terms of two positive-conductance operators acting on reciprocal gaps. This exposes a further exact boundary: the nearest-neighbor shape operator has a complete nonlinear null family. It detects **variation of logarithmic contrast**, but not the mean logarithmic contrast itself.

Let
\[
y_j=\log g_j,
\qquad
d_j=y_{j+1}-y_j,
\qquad
h_j=\frac1{g_j},
\]
and let `F` be the normalized three-root discriminant from XF-030, with
\[
\phi_j:=F'(d_j).
\]
XF-031 gives
\[
(L_\lambda h)_i=\phi_{i-1}-\phi_i,
\tag{1}
\]
where `L_lambda` is the positive nearest-neighbor conductance operator generated by the triple shape. Since XF-030 proves
\[
F''(d)<0\qquad(d\in\mathbb R),
\tag{2}
\]
`F'` is strictly decreasing and therefore injective. It follows immediately that on any consecutive interval
\[
\boxed{
(L_\lambda h)_i=0\ \text{at every interior site}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
d_j\equiv d
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
g_j=C r^j
}
\tag{3}
\]
for some `C>0` and `r=e^d>0`.

Thus **geometric gap progressions are exactly the nonlinear bulk null modes** of the triple-discriminant assembly. In log-gap coordinates they are the affine profiles `y_j=A+Bj`. The arithmetic lattice is only the special case `B=0`.

For a constant-weight block of triple starts `j=m,...,n`, this null mode collapses the full derivative to an endpoint flux:
\[
\boxed{
\left(\sum_{j=m}^n \mathcal J_j\right)'
=\phi(d)\bigl(y_{n+1}'-y_m'\bigr).
}
\tag{4}
\]
More generally, for any finitely supported taper `a_j` whose active triples have the same contrast `d`, the XF-031 product rule becomes
\[
\boxed{
\mathcal K_a'
=\phi(d)\sum_i(a_{i-1}-a_i)y_i'.
}
\tag{5}
\]
There is then no locally constant-weight bulk contribution at all: the derivative is supported entirely where the taper changes.

The missing scalar is explicit. Over a block,
\[
\boxed{
\sum_{j=m}^n d_j
=y_{n+1}-y_m
=\log\frac{g_{n+1}}{g_m}.
}
\tag{6}
\]
Therefore any coercive continuation of the present triple route must split logarithmic contrast into a mean component, fixed by an endpoint gap ratio, and fluctuations about that mean. The `L_lambda` bulk can constrain the fluctuations but is blind to the mean. A universal estimate that attempts to control absolute contrast, total triple shape deficit, or the sum of the individual internal square productions from the bulk term alone cannot be coercive without an endpoint/source anchor or an additional observable that sees affine `y`.

This does **not** refute the overlap/taper route. It identifies the exact missing mode. A long fixed-ratio geometric ramp may require an enormous dynamic range of gaps and need not be source-admissible for Xi. A positive Xi theorem may still succeed if source-valid information controls the endpoint ratio in (6), or if the endpoint flux in (4) has a favorable aggregate sign. The result instead rules out treating positivity of the two conductance operators in XF-031 as sufficient bulk coercivity.

## 1. Exact classification of the bulk kernel

For adjacent gaps `p=g_i` and `q=g_{i+1}`, XF-031 factors the triple contrast flux as
\[
\phi\!\left(\frac qp\right)
=\lambda(p,q)\left(\frac1q-\frac1p\right),
\qquad
\lambda(p,q)>0,
\tag{7}
\]
and consequently
\[
(L_\lambda h)_i
=\lambda_{i-1}(h_i-h_{i-1})
+\lambda_i(h_i-h_{i+1})
=\phi_{i-1}-\phi_i.
\tag{8}
\]
Suppose (8) vanishes for every interior site `i=m+1,...,n`. Then
\[
\phi_m=\phi_{m+1}=\cdots=\phi_n.
\tag{9}
\]
By the strict concavity (2), `phi=F'` is injective, so
\[
d_m=d_{m+1}=\cdots=d_n=d.
\tag{10}
\]
Exponentiating `d_j=log(g_{j+1}/g_j)` gives
\[
g_{j+1}=r g_j,
\qquad r=e^d,
\]
and hence `g_j=C r^j` after absorbing the starting index into `C`. The converse is immediate: constant `d_j` makes every difference `phi_{i-1}-phi_i` vanish. This proves (3) with no approximation and no assumption that the gaps are close to an arithmetic lattice.

The operator therefore has the nonlinear analogue of an affine kernel. Near the arithmetic lattice, `phi(d)=-3d/2+O(d^3)`, so `L_lambda h=0` linearizes to constant first difference of `y`, exactly as the Cauchy-lattice picture suggests. Equation (3) shows that this is not an artifact of linearization.

## 2. Constant contrast makes the assembled derivative endpoint-only

For the triple beginning at `j`, XF-030 gives
\[
\mathcal J_j=F(d_j).
\tag{11}
\]
At any configuration for which `d_j=d` on `j=m,...,n`, differentiation at that time gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\left(\sum_{j=m}^n\mathcal J_j\right)'
&=\phi(d)\sum_{j=m}^n d_j'\\
&=\phi(d)\sum_{j=m}^n(y_{j+1}'-y_j')\\
&=\phi(d)(y_{n+1}'-y_m'),
\end{aligned}
\tag{12}
\]
which is (4). No assumption is made that the flow preserves the geometric progression; (12) is an exact instantaneous identity on the geometric-ramp configuration.

The same conclusion follows directly from the nonlinear product rule of XF-031,
\[
\mathcal K_a'
=\sum_i(a_{i-1}\phi_{i-1}-a_i\phi_i)y_i'.
\tag{13}
\]
If all active contrasts equal `d`, then `phi_i=phi(d)` and (13) reduces to (5). A plateau of constant taper therefore contributes nothing from its interior. Only the taper transition layers remain.

This cancellation is not the statement that each triple has zero production. For `r\ne1`, XF-030 gives a strictly positive internal square term
\[
4\|q^{(j)}\|_2^2
=\frac{2\phi(r)^2(g_j^2+g_jg_{j+1}+g_{j+1}^2)}{g_j^2g_{j+1}^2}>0.
\tag{14}
\]
The endpoint collapse shows that those local positive productions can be cancelled in the assembled derivative by the remaining interactions. Overlap removes collision-wall singularities and localizes explicit taper defects, but it does not turn the sum of blockwise internal productions into an independently coercive bulk quantity.

As a concrete exact normalization, take `r=1/2`. Then
\[
\phi(1/2)=\frac{20}{21},
\tag{15}
\]
so every triple has nonzero positive internal production while an entire constant-weight geometric block satisfies
\[
\left(\sum_{j=m}^n\mathcal J_j\right)'
=\frac{20}{21}(y_{n+1}'-y_m').
\tag{16}
\]
The interior cancellation is exact at every block length.

## 3. The missing mode is the mean logarithmic contrast

Let a block contain `M=n-m+1` contrasts and define
\[
\bar d=\frac1M\sum_{j=m}^n d_j.
\tag{17}
\]
Equation (6) gives
\[
\bar d
=\frac1M\log\frac{g_{n+1}}{g_m}.
\tag{18}
\]
The null family is precisely the case in which every fluctuation `d_j-bar d` vanishes while `bar d` may be nonzero. Thus the current bulk mechanism has the same structural limitation as a derivative seminorm: it can measure nonuniformity of the contrast field but cannot determine its average without boundary data.

This is the exact nonlinear version of the mean-removal problem that motivated XF-021--XF-025, but it occurs in a different and collision-safe category. The earlier compact centered functionals failed because they reintroduced singular positive boundary spikes. The normalized-discriminant route avoids those poles and reaches a better boundary: **mean removal has become an endpoint mode rather than a collision singularity**.

That distinction matters for the Xi program. XF-020 supplies enough spatial room to make the remote exterior negligible, but zero counting alone controls the number of zeros in the buffer, not the endpoint gap ratio appearing in (18). A future source-valid theorem must either control this ratio/mean contrast at the relevant scale, show that its endpoint flux is favorable, or augment the observable so that affine log-gap profiles acquire bulk cost.

## 4. Pointwise conductance alignment is also false in a complete periodic control

Even away from an exact geometric ramp, the two positive-conductance
Laplacians in XF-031 can have opposite pointwise signs. This requires a
complete zero configuration: restricting the infinite gap equation to the
gaps of a finite root set drops endpoint terms.

Take the bi-infinite ordered configuration
\[
x_{4m+j}=3m+a_j,\qquad
(a_0,a_1,a_2,a_3)=\left(0,2,\frac52,\frac{11}4\right),
\quad m\in\mathbb Z.
\]
Its periodic gaps and reciprocal gaps are
\[
(g_0,g_1,g_2,g_3)=\left(2,\frac12,\frac14,\frac14\right),
\qquad
(h_0,h_1,h_2,h_3)=\left(\frac12,2,4,4\right).
\tag{19}
\]
This is the full simple real zero set of the real-entire function
\[
f(z)=\prod_{j=0}^3\sin\frac{\pi(z-a_j)}3.
\]
The finite Fourier sum \(f_t=e^{-t\partial_z^2}f\) supplies an actual
heat-flow realization. At \(t=0\), differentiation at a simple root gives
\(x_i'=f''(x_i)/f'(x_i)\). All four roots per period continue as simple
real roots for sufficiently small real \(|t|\), by the implicit function
theorem. There are no additional roots: after multiplication by
\(e^{4\pi iz/3}\), the Fourier sum is a degree-four polynomial in
\(e^{2\pi iz/3}\) with nonzero constant and leading coefficients, and
these four continued roots account for its full degree.

At the middle gap, the nearest-neighbor triple conductances are
\[
\lambda\!\left(2,\frac12\right)=\frac{36}{35},
\qquad
\lambda\!\left(\frac12,\frac14\right)=\frac{10}{21}.
\tag{20}
\]
Consequently
\[
(L_\lambda h)_1
=\frac{36}{35}\left(2-\frac12\right)
+\frac{10}{21}(2-4)
=\frac{62}{105}>0.
\tag{21}
\]

The full periodic velocity, including every image, is
\[
x_j'=\frac{2\pi}{3}\sum_{\substack{0\le k<4\\k\ne j}}
\cot\frac{\pi(a_j-a_k)}3.
\tag{22}
\]
This follows directly from the sine product, or from the classical
cotangent partial-fraction expansion
\(\operatorname{PV}\sum_{m\in\mathbb Z}(z-3m)^{-1}
=(\pi/3)\cot(\pi z/3)\). Self-images cancel in symmetric pairs.
Using \(\cot(\pi/12)=2+\sqrt3\) and the elementary special angles,
\[
 x_1'=\frac{2\pi}{3}\left(-1-\frac4{\sqrt3}\right),
 \qquad
 x_2'=\frac{2\pi}{3}(-2-\sqrt3).
\]
Hence
\[
y_1'=\frac{x_2'-x_1'}{g_1}
=\frac{4\pi}{3}\left(\frac1{\sqrt3}-1\right)<0.
\]
For this complete periodic configuration the XF-014/XF-031 gap identity
is valid: reindexing the principal-value sums has vanishing boundary
terms because \(|x_k|\asymp |k|\), and the resulting conductance sum is
absolutely convergent because the gaps are bounded and its coefficients
are \(O(|k|^{-2})\). Thus \(y_1'=2h_1(L_wh)_1\), and
\[
\boxed{
2h_1(L_\lambda h)_1(L_wh)_1
=\frac{248\pi}{315}\left(\frac1{\sqrt3}-1\right)<0.
}
\tag{23}
\]
This is the actual instantaneous locally constant-weight bulk density,
with every exterior interaction retained.

The distinction from a finite truncation is material. If only
\(0,2,5/2,11/4\) are kept and evolved by the finite polynomial zero law
\(x_i'=2\sum_{k\ne i}(x_i-x_k)^{-1}\), then
\(x_1'=-17/3\), \(x_2'=-16/5\), and \(y_1'=74/15>0\).
The two adjacent cross-ratio weights being one does not make them the
whole finite-system gap velocity. For finite roots \(x_0<\cdots<x_n\),
the precise endpoint correction is
\[
\frac{g_i'}2
=\sum_{\substack{0\le k<n\\k\ne i}}c_{ik}(g_k-g_i)
 +\frac1{x_{i+1}-x_0}-\frac1{x_i-x_n},
\qquad 0\le i<n.
\]
Here \(c_{ik}=[(x_i-x_k)(x_{i+1}-x_{k+1})]^{-1}\).
The identity follows by shifting the first velocity sum by one index;
the two unmatched endpoints must be retained. The periodic construction avoids
this finite-boundary substitution.

The control disproves universal **pointwise** conductance alignment.
In fact it cannot produce a negative derivative of any fixed,
nonnegative tapered triple sum at this instant. To see the distinction,
put \(c=2\pi/3\). Equation (22) gives, in cyclic order,
\[
\frac{(x_0',x_1',x_2',x_3')}{c}
=\left(2+\frac7{\sqrt3},-1-\frac4{\sqrt3},-2-\sqrt3,1\right),
\]
and hence
\[
\frac{(y_0',y_1',y_2',y_3')}{c}
=\left(-\frac32-\frac{11\sqrt3}6,
        -2+\frac{2\sqrt3}3,
        12+4\sqrt3,
        4+\frac{28\sqrt3}3\right).
\]
The four contrast fluxes are
\[
(\phi_0,\phi_1,\phi_2,\phi_3)
=\left(\frac{54}{35},\frac{20}{21},0,-\frac{1190}{657}\right).
\]
Using the unregrouped identity
\(\mathcal J_j'=\phi_j(y_{j+1}'-y_j')\) therefore yields
\[
\boxed{
\frac{(\mathcal J_0',\mathcal J_1',\mathcal J_2',\mathcal J_3')}{c}
=\left(
\frac{27(5\sqrt3-1)}{35},
\frac{40(21+5\sqrt3)}{63},
0,
\frac{595(33+67\sqrt3)}{1971}
\right).
}
\tag{24}
\]
Every entry is nonnegative. Periodicity now proves
\[
\mathcal K_a'(0)=\sum_j a_j\mathcal J_j'(0)\ge0
\tag{25}
\]
for **every** finitely supported, time-independent \(a_j\ge0\),
without a slow-taper hypothesis. It is strictly positive whenever the
taper gives positive weight to a triple whose start is not congruent
to \(2\pmod4\). Thus repeating this particular control across a growing
buffer cannot turn (23) into the adverse aggregate sought by the
overlap/taper clue.

The negative density in (23) and the nonnegative densities in (24)
are compatible: discrete summation by parts redistributes the local
contributions. For a nonconstant taper the taper-difference term in
XF-031 must accompany the regrouped bulk term. The signs of these
two local representations are not interchangeable. This calculation
does not prove aggregate positivity for other configurations, for
time-dependent weights, or at later times of this trajectory; it
does not supply an Xi-specific restriction either.

Exact arithmetic in \(\mathbb Q(\sqrt3)\) checks the cotangent sums,
the rational fluxes, and both cyclic expressions
\(\sum_j\phi_j(y_{j+1}'-y_j')
=\sum_j(\phi_{j-1}-\phi_j)y_j'\).
The displayed equations, rather than a numerical sign test, prove
the result.

## 5. Boundary conditions and stress tests

The case `r=1` is the arithmetic lattice. Then `d=0`, `phi=0`, and the null mode is trivial. XF-029 studies perturbations away from precisely this member; its quadratic Cauchy coercivity controls variations of the contrast and is consistent with the exact nonlinear kernel (3).

For fixed `r\ne1`, a block of length `M` has endpoint ratio `g_{n+1}/g_m=r^M`, which becomes exponentially large or small. Such a long ramp need not satisfy any source-relevant Xi gap envelope. One may instead take `d=d_M\to0` so that the dynamic range stays bounded, but then the absolute per-triple shape defect also shrinks. Therefore (3) by itself is **not** a source-admissible growing-buffer counterexample and does not establish a positive-`Lambda` scenario.

The pointwise example (19)--(23) is a complete periodic real-entire heat-flow control, not an Xi configuration. It is not asserted to have Xi's even symmetry or arithmetic source law. Its role is falsification: any successful theorem must use aggregation, endpoint information, collision coverage, or genuinely Xi-specific input rather than infer pointwise sign from the two conductance factorizations.

Finally, (3) depends on strict injectivity of `F'`, supplied here by the exact `F''<0` theorem of XF-030. For a different block observable whose shape derivative is not injective, the null family could be larger. The classification is exact for the active normalized three-root discriminant route, not for arbitrary shape energies.

## 6. Prior-art and novelty boundary

Affine kernels of derivative operators, discrete summation by parts, geometric progressions as affine profiles after taking logarithms, and positive-conductance graph Laplacians are classical structures. The relevant one-dimensional log-gas and nonlocal-diffusion prior-art boundaries are already anchored in `SOURCES.md`. The periodic summation uses the classical cotangent partial fraction [NIST DLMF, Eq. 4.22.3](https://dlmf.nist.gov/4.22.E3); the same velocity is derived directly from the finite sine product. Neither that identity nor periodic logarithmic repulsion is new. The explicit complete-control sign check supplies the pointwise obstruction without importing a finite truncation of the infinite gap law.

The line-specific content is the exact identification of the nonlinear kernel created by the XF-030/XF-031 normalized-triple construction and its consequence for the active Xi taper strategy: **the bulk operator annihilates precisely constant log-gap contrast, so mean contrast survives only as an endpoint gap-ratio mode**. This is not claimed as a new general theorem about log gases.

## 7. Consequence for `xi_flow`

The accepted overlap-discriminant taper clue remains open, but its decisive inequality is now more constrained. The algebraic localization question is solved by XF-031; XF-032 shows that the remaining bulk-alignment theorem cannot be a coercive estimate on absolute triple shape without an additional term controlling the null mode.

A viable positive continuation should therefore decompose
\[
d_j=\bar d+(d_j-\bar d),
\]
use the `L_lambda`/`L_w` assembly to control the fluctuating component, and supply an independent source-valid estimate for
\[
\bar d=\frac1M\log\frac{g_{n+1}}{g_m},
\]
or an endpoint-flux identity that makes this mode harmless. Alternatively, a larger-block or different symmetric observable could be sought whose collision-safe bulk sees affine log-gap profiles as well as their curvature.

A decisive negative continuation would have to do more than exhibit a fixed geometric ramp: it should produce a growing-buffer, source-compatible family in which the endpoint mode remains quantitatively adverse after the Xi counting, collision-coverage, and taper constraints are imposed. Until that step, the correct conclusion is a structural coercivity boundary, not failure of the taper program.
