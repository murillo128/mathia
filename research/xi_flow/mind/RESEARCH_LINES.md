## Treat the natural q-scale source history as collision-nonidentifying, not merely statically admissible

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-endpoint-prime-free-fourier-sector-is-volterra-triangular`, `MI-006-periodic-vieta-coordinates-diagonalize-the-nonlinear-heat-flow`, `MI-007-ultra-infrared-vieta-modes-should-be-quotiented-by-destination-energy`, `MI-008-gaussian-reference-localization-is-relatively-exact-but-not-global`, `MI-009-guarded-energy-can-enforce-static-real-divisor-feasibility`, `MI-010-endpoint-renormalization-is-source-fixed-before-discretization`.

XF-114--XF-119 close the natural q-scale source and static-realization gates: the full Toeplitz section is asymptotically the identity and the exact Xi prefix has equal-weight real-divisor realizations at the prescribed node budget. XF-120 shows that this strong source theorem has essentially no static collision margin: the same complete q-scale prefix can be realized while `1-o(1)` of all root slots lie in simple double collisions.

XF-121--XF-123 push the obstruction through heat time. The source Toeplitz near-isometry propagates uniformly under positive periodic heat, and for every sequence of finite target times the canonical Xi q-scale history admits exact matched controls whose periodic Newman threshold is that target time and whose collision density tends to one. The q-scale history is therefore not merely statically nonidentifying; within the declared periodic matched-control interface it cannot determine the transition time even when used coherently through positive heat.

XF-124--XF-125 close the sparse cross-scale repair. Sublinear moment sampling and even the complete heat histories of any sublinear collection of proper harmonics can remain identically blind to asymptotically full collision density. The failure is structural: the controls avoid the additive support semigroup generated by a large collision crystal and a vanishing filler.

XF-126 identifies a genuine global boundary. The terminal history `P_N(s)` recovers the first active Vieta shell and its magnitude, so the terminal harmonic is not just another sparse coordinate. But XF-127--XF-128 then show that this information still does not determine collision geometry: colliding and simple targets can share the full terminal history, and a consecutive terminal window containing a linear fraction approaching one half of all harmonics can remain exactly collision-blind.

The live upper-bound route must therefore use an **Xi-specific global/cross-scale relation not captured by the natural q-scale history, sublinear proper-harmonic histories, or a merely terminal high-frequency window**. A candidate may require a denser structured family crossing the active-shell boundary, an observable coupling low and high Vieta shells in a way the phase fibers cannot preserve, or a deliberate move to the prime band. In every case the first theorem should be an exact collision/transition identifiability statement against the current matched controls, not another source-admission result.

## Decide the q-scale versus prime-band fork explicitly

The q-scale source problem is now stronger than solved positivity: exact realizability and uniform heat-history matching coexist with arbitrarily prescribed matched transition times. A q-scale continuation must therefore introduce information genuinely absent from the first `K` moment history or from the classified sparse/terminal harmonic observations.

Moving to `xi=Theta(1)` beginning at `lambda_2` remains a separate route. It must handle distributional prime-power atoms and replace the perturbative point-sampling/shadow interface. Q-scale Toeplitz coercivity, exact realization, and collision controls must not be transferred to the prime band without a new source-to-destination theorem.

## Keep source coercivity, exact realization, history identifiability, and transition coercivity separate

A positive source cone can be exactly realizable and still contain fibers with radically different collision geometry. A global harmonic can reveal nontrivial Vieta structure and still fail to determine the discriminant. Future results should state whether they change source admission, node realization, heat-history identifiability, observable density/scale, prime-band access, or the actual transition inequality.
