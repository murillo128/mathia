This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Xi-flow intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they separate genuinely different mechanisms.

## Use collision-safe variables while excluding every fixed local jet

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-collision-safe-coordinates-separate-singularity-from-source-information` and `MI-003-fixed-time-memory-is-mesoscopic-not-finite-jet`.

Individual roots have square-root singularities at a simple double collision, while the discriminant/squared gap is analytic. XF-006 shows that no robust finite collision jet is Xi-specific because real-rooted polynomial heat controls can reproduce it with an independently movable transition time. A live collision observable must therefore carry information not compressible to any fixed local jet.

## Derive an Xi-specific coercive law in a scale-matched fractional norm

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-scale-free-gap-dynamics-still-need-an-xi-specific-law` and `MI-003-fixed-time-memory-is-mesoscopic-not-finite-jet`.

Fixed-time perturbative memory requires `Theta(log^2 T)` gaps and converges after rescaling to a Cauchy/half-Laplacian flow. XF-009--XF-010 show that fixed-radius translation averages lose mesoscopic ordering at leading order; smooth stencils see it only at `O(log^-4 T)` with a local `k^2` response.

XF-011 identifies the sharper live boundary. Renormalized adjacent-gap `W^{1,p}` seminorms for `1<p<infinity` control the fractional generator but their raw signal is `h^{2p}`. Total variation reaches the desired `h^2` scale but fails to control `|D|` strongly in `L^1`. A decisive Xi theorem should either derive source-specific coercivity in a `p>1` scale with sufficient precision, exploit an endpoint weak-type/cancellation mechanism, or introduce another norm naturally adapted to the Cauchy generator.

## Match analytic-frontier inputs to mesoscopic width and precision before importing them

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-003-fixed-time-memory-is-mesoscopic-not-finite-jet`.

A fixed finite ordered-gap certificate can improve a simple-zero proportion while still discarding the low-frequency ordering needed by Xi dynamics. Any cross-line input must therefore retain block width comparable to `log^2 T` or provide a subleading/nonlocal statistic at the `log^-2 T` dynamical scale (or the sharper scale required by its regularity class). Better limiting constants alone are not fixed-time dynamical information.
