## Find a transition-specific modulus beyond exact real-axis state identification

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-endpoint-prime-free-fourier-sector-is-volterra-triangular`, `MI-006-periodic-vieta-coordinates-diagonalize-the-nonlinear-heat-flow`, `MI-007-ultra-infrared-vieta-modes-should-be-quotiented-by-destination-energy`, `MI-008-gaussian-reference-localization-is-relatively-exact-but-not-global`, `MI-009-guarded-energy-can-enforce-static-real-divisor-feasibility`, `MI-010-endpoint-renormalization-is-source-fixed-before-discretization`, `MI-011-cross-scale-observability-can-compress-to-one-off-circle-heat-trace`.

XF-124--XF-142 show that complete future/local-patch tomography, zero-delay local data, full linear-depth spatial jets at fixed exterior height, and mixed instantaneous space-time jets can remain transition-blind; after a universal gauge the local trace obeys the one-dimensional heat equation, so same-slice time derivatives add no independent information.

XF-143--XF-144 close an apparent algebraic escape positively. Two real heat traces separated by half a mean root spacing recover the full polynomial state, and even a single real probe moving at any nonzero constant speed is exactly identifying over any nonempty time interval. There is no positive spatial-travel threshold for exact recovery.

XF-145 shows why this still does not solve the Xi problem. Transition-sharp matched controls remain **superexponentially invisible** to every root-spacing real scan throughout heat windows `o(1/N)`; the full root-spacing tube has condition number at least `exp(Omega(N log N))` for the transition functional in polynomial sub-inverse-degree windows. The live theorem must therefore improve the transition modulus itself: use heat time at a genuinely independent scale, a transition-specific quotient, a source theorem excluding the central packets, or nonlocal geometry not reducible to a short real scan.

## Separate state identifiability, scan conditioning, and transition conditioning

A moving probe can be injective for arbitrarily small travel while its nearest reflected pair is badly conditioned, and the transition functional can be vastly more ill-conditioned still because of the central packets. Exact full-state reconstruction is therefore not the relevant success criterion.

Future results should state the observation scale, source class, and modulus for `Lambda_per` explicitly. An injective Vandermonde representation or a continuum root-spacing tube is a control if matched states with order-one transition separation remain superexponentially close.

## Keep q-scale and prime-band routes separate

The q-scale source cone can be coercive and realizable while target observability remains unstable. A prime-band continuation begins in a different source regime with distributional prime-power atoms and must derive a new source-to-destination interface rather than importing q-scale controls.
