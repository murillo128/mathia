## Prove or refute nonlinear bulk alignment for overlapping triple discriminants

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-collision-safe-coordinates-separate-singularity-from-source-information` and `MI-003-fixed-time-memory-is-mesoscopic-not-finite-jet`.

XF-026--XF-031 now resolve most of the localization algebra for three-root blocks. Normalized discriminants have square production and affine exterior cancellation; positive overlap protects every covered collision; lattice-order taper loss is a Cauchy commutator of size `O(1/M)`; the full triple shape reduces to one logarithmic contrast with an exact signed cubic exterior flux; and the nonlinear tapered sum has an exact product rule in which explicit localization enters only through first differences of the taper.

The unresolved bulk term is precise. Triple contrast produces a nearest-neighbor positive-conductance Laplacian `L_lambda` on reciprocal gaps, while the exact Xi log-gap flow supplies a long-range positive-conductance Laplacian `L_w` on the same field. The local bulk density contains `h_i(L_lambda h)_i(L_w h)_i`, whose aggregate sign is not implied by positivity of the two operators separately. A decisive theorem should prove a mesoscopic coercive comparison or exhibit growing-buffer misalignment that survives vanishing taper variation.

## Combine taper-difference localization with collision coverage and source-valid buffers

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-collision-safe-coordinates-separate-singularity-from-source-information`, `MI-002-scale-free-gap-dynamics-still-need-an-xi-specific-law`, and `MI-003-fixed-time-memory-is-mesoscopic-not-finite-jet`.

The fixed-time core has `Theta(log^2 T)` gaps and physical span `Theta(log T)`, while source-valid super-mesoscopic buffers provide parametrically more gap indices. XF-029 shows this width is sufficient at lattice order, and XF-031 shows the same taper difference is the exact nonlinear localization coefficient away from collision. Near collision, however, absolute-value estimates of log-gap velocity are singular; the proof must splice in the positive pair-coverage asymptotic of XF-028 rather than discard its sign.

A positive should obtain one height-uniform aggregate bound across lattice, intermediate finite-gap, and collision regimes. A negative should build source-compatible configurations whose adverse bulk or taper term remains order one throughout the growing buffer.
