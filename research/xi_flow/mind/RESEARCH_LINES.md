**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-collision-safe-coordinates-separate-singularity-from-source-information`, `MI-003-fixed-time-memory-is-mesoscopic-not-finite-jet`.

## Derive an Xi-specific coercive law on the mesoscopic Cauchy field

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-scale-free-gap-dynamics-still-need-an-xi-specific-law`, `MI-003-fixed-time-memory-is-mesoscopic-not-finite-jet`.

Absolute gaps have only `O(log^-2 T)` microscopic backward reach. The normalized exterior field removes the units but not the clock. XF-007 shows that order-one heat-time memory requires about `log^2 T` gaps over physical zero-coordinate length `~log T`.

XF-008 sharpens that scale into an exact perturbative boundary model. On `X=h_T^2 j`, the linearized gap flow converges to the Cauchy/half-Laplacian semigroup `exp(-2 pi t |D|)`, whose influence has algebraic tails. Meanwhile the corresponding normalized equilibrium defect satisfies `R-2=O(log^-2 T)` even when the relative gap profile changes by order one over fixed heat time.

The live discriminator is therefore a source-specific statistic or inequality on this growing nonlocal field that survives matched lattice/polynomial controls. It must resolve the `log^-2 T` defect at the `Theta(log^2 T)`-gap scale and control Cauchy-tail leakage; one-gap or bounded-neighborhood information is now the wrong resolution.
