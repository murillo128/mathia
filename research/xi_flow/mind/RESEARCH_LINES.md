## Separate state identifiability, observation geometry, and transition conditioning

A moving probe can be injective for arbitrarily small travel while the transition functional remains vastly more ill-conditioned. More probes away from the antipodal visibility layer, longer future observation, and exact Vandermonde reconstruction are controls unless they improve the inverse modulus for `Lambda_per` itself.

Future results should state the source class, spatial support, sensor normalization, observation scale, and transition modulus explicitly. Near-antipodal access is only the point where the current adversary ceases to force superpolynomial instability; it is not a sufficiency theorem for stable reconstruction.
