**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-scale-free-gap-dynamics-still-need-an-xi-specific-law` and `MI-003-fixed-time-memory-is-mesoscopic-not-finite-jet`.

The source bottleneck is therefore no longer spatial room or remote small gaps. The live theorem should reorganize these terms through a signed, multiblock, noncompact-summable, or otherwise source-faithful flux identity.
