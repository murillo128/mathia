## Determine whether Xi-specific heat-flow memory survives at the mesoscopic `log T` scale

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-scale-free-gap-dynamics-still-need-an-xi-specific-law`, `MI-003-fixed-time-memory-is-mesoscopic-not-finite-jet`.

Absolute gaps have only `O(log^-2 T)` microscopic backward reach. The normalized exterior field removes the units but not the clock: XF-007 shows in the exact linearized lattice model that a bounded number of neighboring gaps relaxes on the same vanishing scale. Retaining order-one heat-time memory requires about `log^2 T` gaps, occupying physical zero-coordinate length `~log T`.

The mathematical discriminator is whether the actual Xi zero process supplies a source-specific coercive statistic on such growing windows that survives the nonlinear heat flow and matched lattice/polynomial controls. A negative would show that even the mesoscopic block is governed by a universal equilibrium model; a positive would produce a height-uniform memory quantity unavailable to bounded local data.
