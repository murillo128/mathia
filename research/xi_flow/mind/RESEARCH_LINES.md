# Xi-flow research lines

This file holds the current mathematical questions suggested by the durable Xi-flow intuitions. It is not a roadmap, task queue, status page, or history. Lines should survive only while they separate genuinely different mechanisms.

## Use collision-safe symmetric coordinates while subtracting the universal heat normal form

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-collision-safe-coordinates-separate-singularity-from-source-information`.

Individual zero branches have square-root singularities at a simple double collision, so bounded-speed root dynamics are the wrong coordinates. The local Weierstrass discriminant and squared gap are analytic and have a universal first slope fixed by the heat equation.

A decisive continuation should therefore formulate collision propagation in symmetric variables and isolate the first coefficient that depends on the exterior Xi zero configuration rather than on the universal double-collision normal form.

## Derive an Xi-specific scale-free law for the normalized exterior field

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-scale-free-gap-dynamics-still-need-an-xi-specific-law`.

Absolute gaps shrink like `1/log T`, so any continuation budget based on an unnormalized minimum gap gives at most `O(1/log^2 T)` backward time at high ordinates. The scale-invariant coordinate `R=qS` avoids that degeneration and exactly controls whether a neighboring pair opens or contracts, but the same law holds for general real-zero backward-heat systems.

A decisive theorem must derive an Xi-specific bound, monotonicity, averaged deficit, or coupling for `R` (or a stronger normalized field) that survives matched heat-flow controls and can be integrated to a height-uniform backward-time gain.

## Bridge local collision control to a global bound for the de Bruijn--Newman constant

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-001-collision-safe-coordinates-separate-singularity-from-source-information` and `MI-002-scale-free-gap-dynamics-still-need-an-xi-specific-law`.

Local collision regularization and normalized gap dynamics are not yet a global continuation theorem. A viable bridge must remain uniform across increasing height, multiple neighboring pairs, and possible collision cascades, with all error terms expressed in scale-free variables.

A positive should yield a fixed backward interval from independently controlled Xi data; a negative should show that the normalized exterior-field budget can still escape through collective configurations compatible with the known zero distribution.
