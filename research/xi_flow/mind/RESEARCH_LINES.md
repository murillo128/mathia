## Move from collision-nonidentifying q-scale histories to source-justified cross-scale observability

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-004-endpoint-prime-free-fourier-sector-is-volterra-triangular`, `MI-006-periodic-vieta-coordinates-diagonalize-the-nonlinear-heat-flow`, `MI-007-ultra-infrared-vieta-modes-should-be-quotiented-by-destination-energy`, `MI-008-gaussian-reference-localization-is-relatively-exact-but-not-global`, `MI-009-guarded-energy-can-enforce-static-real-divisor-feasibility`, `MI-010-endpoint-renormalization-is-source-fixed-before-discretization`, `MI-011-cross-scale-observability-can-compress-to-one-off-circle-heat-trace`.

XF-114--XF-125 close the natural q-scale source and sparse-history gates. The full Toeplitz section is asymptotically the identity and the exact Xi prefix has equal-weight real-divisor realizations at the prescribed node budget, yet the same q-scale prefix and its positive-heat history can be matched by collision-saturated controls with arbitrarily prescribed periodic transition times. Sublinear moment sampling and complete histories of any sublinear collection of proper harmonics can remain exactly blind to asymptotically full collision density.

XF-126--XF-128 locate the first global boundary but do not yet cross it. The terminal history recovers the first active Vieta shell and its magnitude, while a consecutive terminal window containing a linear fraction approaching one half of all harmonics can still remain collision-blind.

XF-129 proves that the half-spectrum threshold is real. The complete histories of all harmonics `m>N/2` recover the full unit-circle divisor when `N` is odd and recover it up to one harmless central rotation when `N` is even. Hence they determine collision geometry and the periodic Newman transition time. This is asymptotically sharp against the XF-128 terminal-window controls.

XF-130 compresses that identifying family dramatically. One complex scalar heat trace `Q_zeta(s)=E(zeta,s)` at any fixed nonzero point with `|zeta|!=1` determines every Vieta coefficient. Choosing `|zeta_N|=exp(sigma/N)` keeps the reflected-shell inversion only polynomially ill-conditioned, so a displacement of one root-spacing scale off the real circle already mixes enough low/high Vieta information for exact periodic collision observability.

The periodic **identifiability** problem therefore has a concrete positive solution. The live theorem has moved to source access and transport: derive such an off-circle/cross-scale trace, or an equivalent identifying relation, from the actual Xi source with controlled nonperiodic error and normalization. Another q-scale admission theorem is not enough. A negative result should show that the source-compatible interface cannot access the needed reflected-shell asymmetry or that its conditioning is destroyed when transferred back from the periodic model.

## Decide the q-scale versus prime-band fork explicitly

The q-scale source problem is stronger than solved positivity: exact realizability and uniform heat-history matching coexist with arbitrary matched transition times, but the periodic target also has exact cross-scale observables that determine the transition. A q-scale continuation must therefore connect the **actual source** to one of those identifying observables rather than search indefinitely for more static q-scale coercivity.

Moving to `xi=Theta(1)` beginning at `lambda_2` remains a separate route. It must handle distributional prime-power atoms and replace the perturbative point-sampling/shadow interface. Q-scale Toeplitz coercivity, exact realization, collision controls, and periodic off-circle observability must not be transferred to the prime band without a new source-to-destination theorem.

## Keep source coercivity, exact realization, history identifiability, source accessibility, and transition coercivity separate

A positive source cone can be exactly realizable and still contain fibers with radically different collision geometry. Conversely, the full unit-circle divisor can be identifiable from a surprisingly small target-side observable that the source may not control. Future results should state whether they change source admission, node realization, periodic identifiability, source access to the identifying observable, conditioning at one-root-spacing displacement, prime-band access, or the actual transition inequality.
