## Transfer Xi through the center-local Gaussian quotient, but control Vieta conditioning rather than mode count alone

XF-078 proves that this bandwidth obstruction is genuinely global. On the safe center rectangle the same matched Gaussian quotient has an explicit entire trigonometric approximation with the ordinary `N+1` Vieta-sized mode budget and super-polynomial error. XF-079 then shows that the exact weighted selector resource of XF-070 is pointwise in the translated center because the chosen Fourier sidebands are disjoint. A one-center source interface is therefore sufficient; neither full-period seam crossing nor center averaging is forced by the destination norm.

XF-080 identifies the new bottleneck. The explicit accurate XF-078 surrogate is **Vieta-ill-conditioned**: after either outer normalization its first root-power mode is `Theta(N)`, and its terminal Vieta coefficient is not unimodular, so it cannot represent a bounded-displacement real periodic zero carrier. Local Fourier accuracy plus matching mode count does not control the normalized divisor coordinates consumed by the nonlinear log-Vieta flow.

The live source bridge is therefore an object/dictionary theorem: either construct a center-local periodic surrogate with controlled outer coefficient and bounded low log-Vieta resource, or map the Gaussian quotient/logarithmic derivative directly to the XF-079 one-center weighted selector without promoting the Fourier extension to a zero polynomial.

## Match the surrogate norm **and conditioning** to the weighted destination before declaring the local branch solved

XF-079 removes the center-domain mismatch, but XF-080 shows that the remaining norm match is not only a function-approximation problem. The inverse map from local trigonometric approximation to divisor/log-Vieta state is therefore not uniformly conditioned.

A useful interface theorem must control the exact `X(B)` weighted resource together with the normalization/divisor parameters that keep the low log-Vieta modes in the bounded-displacement regime of XF-070--XF-071. Generic `C^J` center accuracy or an `O(N)` mode count is insufficient.
