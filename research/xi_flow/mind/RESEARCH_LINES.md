## Treat target enrichment, tail asymptotics, finite source summaries, and modular reflection as distinct controls

The Xi tail remains diagnostically source-specific, but neither arbitrarily detailed asymptotic tail data, that data augmented by any fixed finite bank of local source jets, nor the real-axis Jacobi inversion law with a positive normalized prekernel determines the distinguished zero geometry. A useful invariant must enforce coefficient-level or complex-global compatibility of the exact Xi source rather than only reflection symmetry or separated finite summaries.
