## Derive Xi-specific mesoscopic envelope, span, and endpoint-flux control

**Linked intuitions:** `MI-002-scale-free-gap-dynamics-still-need-an-xi-specific-law` and `MI-003-fixed-time-memory-is-mesoscopic-not-finite-jet`.

The fixed-time core has `Theta(log^2 T)` gaps and a scale-matched `L log L`/Cauchy carrier. XF-017 shows that a diverging logarithmic-capacity buffer can make pure fractional cutoff leakage `o(1)`. XF-018 further shows that uncentered nonlinear leakage is governed by a bounded cross-ratio kernel with telescoping far tails, replacing uniform remote lower-gap control by cumulative-span non-domination.

The live source theorem should control large endpoint gaps relative to cumulative physical spans and the endpoint span/velocity term needed to remove the neutral mean mode. These are now more precise targets than a blanket two-sided gap envelope.
