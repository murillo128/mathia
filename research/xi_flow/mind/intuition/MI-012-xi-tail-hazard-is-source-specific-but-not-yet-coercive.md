# MI-012 — Xi source rigidity must reach beyond real-axis modular reflection

**Evidence level:** exact theta-tail discrimination, Gaussian heat-origin covariance, analytic beyond-all-orders splicing, finite-jet non-identifiability, and real-axis Jacobi-reflection non-identifiability through XF-166

## Core intuition

The Xi theta tail is genuinely distinctive, but asymptotic distinctiveness is not coercivity. The strongest current controls can preserve the Xi tail beyond every fixed theta-exponential scale and match any prescribed finite bank of exact local source jets while their bounded heat orbit approaches a negative-time Xi slice with nonreal zeros at the distinguished `t=0` time.

XF-166 shows that even an exact global-looking symmetry can still be too weak: every even sub-Xi source can be lifted by a Green operator to a positive normalized prekernel obeying the same real-axis Jacobi inversion law as the true theta prekernel. The missing source datum must therefore constrain the **arithmetic realization** of that symmetry, not merely the symmetry itself.

## Strongest justified principle

XF-160--XF-162 separate the actual Xi source from positive analytic maximal-contact controls by its normalized double-exponential tail action, hazard, curvature, and all-time heat admissibility. XF-163 shows that those normalized tail quantities are invariant under the exact Gaussian source tilt `Phi -> e^(-tau u^2) Phi`, while the heat orbit is translated by `tau`. Any invariant blind to that gauge therefore cannot control absolute transition time.

XF-164 removes the obvious repair of keeping more tail terms. A strictly positive analytic source can agree with Xi beyond every fixed theta-exponential asymptotic scale yet converge on every bounded spacetime window to the shifted Xi orbit, so a nonreal negative-time zero persists at `t=0` by Rouche stability. XF-165 removes the next finite-dimensional repair: analytic notch factors can simultaneously preserve any fixed collection of Xi source jets exactly while leaving the same shifted-orbit limit and beyond-all-orders tail agreement.

XF-166 then factors the true source as `Phi=(S''-S)/8`, where the true theta prekernel obeys `S(u)-S(-u)=-sinh u`. The same reflection law can be synthesized from any even source by an explicit Green inverse of `D^2-1`; for every current control with `0<rho<=Phi`, the reconstructed prekernel is still positive and normalized. Thus real-axis Jacobi inversion, source evenness, prekernel positivity, and the differential extraction do not fix the heat origin either.

## Program consequence

Search for source laws at the coefficient or complex-modular level. The exact Xi prekernel `F(x)=sum_(n>=1) exp(-pi n^2 x)` carries a square-lattice Laplace spectrum that the Green reconstruction does not impose. The cheapest next test is whether the reconstructed prekernel of the Gaussian tilt already violates complete monotonicity; if not, inspect its inverse-Laplace support for off-square mass.

Any successful discriminator must then be propagated into the collision/transition variable. Being globally Xi-specific is necessary but not sufficient for RH; a coefficient law that merely identifies Xi without controlling transition-forming mesoscopic mass is not yet a closing theorem.

## Counterevidence / boundary

XF-166 does not preserve the full complex modular-form structure of Jacobi theta, its `tau -> tau+1` law, or its discrete square-lattice `q`-expansion. Those remain viable sources of rigidity. It also does not prove whether complete monotonicity survives the Gaussian tilt.

XF-163--XF-165 likewise do not preserve the complete analytic germ of Xi at a point or an exact open-interval identity with Xi. The negative boundary is therefore broad but not universal: tail data, finite local anchors, and real-axis modular reflection are defeated; coefficient-level and genuinely complex-global identities remain open.

## Epistemic status

**Exact negative boundary: Xi tail asymptotics, arbitrary fixed finite local source jets, and even the positive normalized real-axis Jacobi-reflection lift do not determine the distinguished heat origin. Surviving coercivity must use coefficient-level or complex-global theta structure and still transport it to zero geometry.**

## Falsification criterion

Produce a criterion built only from the defeated tail/finite-jet/real-axis-reflection data that forces real-rootedness at `t=0` despite the XF-163--XF-166 controls, or exhibit a stronger exact Xi coefficient/modular property that fails on those controls and quantitatively constrains the transition-forming mesoscopic mass.
