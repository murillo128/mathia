# MI-005 — Slow-frequency aggregation is not transition coercivity without spatial/multiscale coverage

**Evidence level:** exact Xi transport and matched-control hierarchy through XF-061

## Core intuition

The source-to-transition gap survives successive repairs of the selector norm. Pointwise control loses frequency-spread flux; unweighted square aggregation loses derivative calibration; derivative weighting fixes that scale mismatch but still misses a sparse local defect whose spectral mass lies outside the shrinking slow cone. The missing theorem is therefore not merely a better slow-frequency weight: it must match both the derivative order and the spatial/spectral support of the transition variation.

## Strongest justified principle

XF-057 gives a chirp with critical flux and vanishing pointwise coefficients. XF-058 shows unweighted square energy detects it. XF-059 extends the actual Xi source estimate to the infrared. XF-060 constructs a high-slow pure wave with critical flux but vanishing unweighted square energy and shows the derivative weight `(M theta^2)^2` repairs that family.

XF-061 then moves one root by `kappa/M`. The perturbation is source-compatible by a wide margin and has exact normalized triple-flux variation tending to `12 kappa`, yet its complete derivative-weighted selector energy over the XF-059 slow cone tends to zero. The failure is intrinsic to restricting attention to that shrinking slow-frequency sector: compact defects place most of their transition information elsewhere.

## What remains possible

A multiscale phase-space norm, a direct localized defect estimate, or a source theorem excluding sparse root defects may still close the bridge. Any candidate must detect both distributed chirps and sparse defects while staying quantitatively small on the actual Xi carrier.

## Status / novelty

The harmonic-analysis mechanisms are classical. The durable synthesis is line-specific: **coercivity requires destination-matched derivative scaling and enough spatial/spectral coverage; no weighting of the present shrinking slow cone alone controls arbitrary critical transition geometry**.

## Falsification criterion

Show that the XF-061 defect has nonvanishing derivative-weighted slow-cone energy, that its exact flux is not critical at the stated amplitude, or prove an Xi source constraint that excludes the defect before invoking the selector norm.
