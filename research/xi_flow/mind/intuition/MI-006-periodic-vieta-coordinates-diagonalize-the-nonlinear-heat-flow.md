# MI-006 — Periodic Vieta coordinates diagonalize the nonlinear zero heat flow

**Evidence level:** exact periodic nonlinear theorem through XF-067, with finite-displacement measurement support through XF-066

## Core intuition

The Cauchy multiplier seen in the arithmetic-lattice tangent flow is not merely a linear artifact. On a periodic zero system, symmetric Vieta coordinates of the unit-circle root variables diagonalize the full backward heat evolution exactly. These coordinates remain regular when roots collide or leave the real axis, so they separate the real nonlinear dynamics from the coordinate singularities of labelled roots.

## Strongest justified principle

XF-064--XF-066 first show that the destination measurement no longer requires infinitesimal dynamics: after quotienting translation, sufficiently slow gap distortion and local finite-difference control make the exact moved-point selector a perturbation of the established lower frame.

XF-067 supplies the nonlinear state representation. Each periodic Vieta coefficient evolves autonomously with decay rate `4 pi^2 k(N-k)/L^2`; at the arithmetic lattice its first variation is exactly one discrete root-displacement Fourier mode, recovering the XF-062 tangent semigroup. Newton identities also give a triangular bridge from low positive-frequency root sums to low Vieta coordinates. Thus collision crossing and nonlinear mode-to-mode replenishment are solved inside the periodic model.

## What remains possible

The hard issue is now localization and conditioning rather than periodic nonlinear algebra. The real Xi window is not globally periodic, the source controls moving-line/Fourier information rather than Vieta coefficients directly, and the growing Newton map may amplify errors. One must quantify those losses and show that a nontrivial transition quantity survives to a state satisfying the XF-065--XF-066 destination criteria.

## Status / novelty

Fourier heat evolution, Vieta/Newton identities, and symmetric collision-safe coordinates are classical ingredients. The durable line-specific synthesis is: **the periodic nonlinear zero flow has an exact collision-safe diagonal state space whose differential is the previously derived tangent Cauchy dynamics**.

## Falsification criterion

Invalidate the exact Vieta heat rates or their equivalence with the periodic zero-motion law, show that the coordinates fail analytically at multiple/complex roots, or find a mismatch between their lattice linearization and the XF-062 multiplier. Failure to localize this theorem to Xi would narrow its usefulness but would not falsify the periodic result.
